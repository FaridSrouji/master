DONALD P. CARSON ELECTED TO THE ROLLINS, INC. BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ATLANTA, MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF DONALD P. CARSON TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2021.
