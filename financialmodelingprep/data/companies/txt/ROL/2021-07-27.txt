ROLLINS, INC. ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. ATLANTA, JULY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $0.08 PER SHARE PAYABLE SEPTEMBER 10, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 10, 2021. ROLLINS, INC. IS A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY.
