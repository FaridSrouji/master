ROLLINS, INC. SCHEDULES DATE FOR RELEASE OF FOURTH QUARTER AND TWELVE MONTHS 2021 RESULTS. ATLANTA, JAN. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL RELEASE ITS UNAUDITED FOURTH QUARTER AND TWELVE MONTHS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2021 ON WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 26, 2022.  IN CONJUNCTION WITH ITS RELEASE, THE COMPANY WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL TO REVIEW THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS ON WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 26, 2022 AT 10:00 A.M.
