ROLLINS, INC. ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. ATLANTA, JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE:ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $0.10 PER SHARE PAYABLE MARCH 10, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 10, 2022. ABOUT ROLLINSROLLINS, INC. IS A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY.
