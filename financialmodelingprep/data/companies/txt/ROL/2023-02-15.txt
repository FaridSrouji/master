ROLLINS, INC. REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS. STRONG REVENUE GROWTH DRIVES 26.1% INCREASE IN QUARTERLY NET INCOME ATLANTA , FEB. 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE:ROL) ("ROLLINS" OR THE "COMPANY"), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, REPORTED UNAUDITED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER AND TWELVE MONTHS ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2022. QUARTERLY HIGHLIGHTS FOURTH QUARTER REVENUES WERE $661.4 MILLION, AN INCREASE OF 10.2% OVER THE FOURTH QUARTER 2021 WITH ORGANIC REVENUES* INCREASING 6.9%.
