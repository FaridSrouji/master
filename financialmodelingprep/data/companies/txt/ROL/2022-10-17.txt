CHICAGO TOPS ORKIN'S RATTIEST CITIES LIST FOR EIGHTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. NEW YORK RISES TO SECOND SPOT, TWO INDIANA CITIES BREAK INTO THE TOP 50 ATLANTA , OCT. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHICAGO MAY SOON NEED TO CHANGE ITS NAME FROM "THE WINDY CITY" TO THE "RATTIEST CITY," AFTER TOPPING ORKIN'S TOP 50 RATTIEST CITIES LIST, YET AGAIN. ORKIN RELEASED THE LIST TODAY, AND FOR THE EIGHTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, THE MIDWEST CITY TAKES THE TOP SPOT.
