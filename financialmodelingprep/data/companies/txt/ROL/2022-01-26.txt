ROLLINS, INC. REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. ATLANTA, JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL) ("ROLLINS" OR THE "COMPANY"), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, REPORTED STRONG UNAUDITED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER AND YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2021. THE COMPANY RECORDED FOURTH QUARTER REVENUES OF $600.3 MILLION, AN INCREASE OF 11.9% OVER THE PRIOR YEAR'S FOURTH QUARTER REVENUE OF $536.3 MILLION.
