ROLLINS, INC. RECEIVES SIXTH-CONSECUTIVE TOP WORKPLACES AWARD FROM THE ATLANTA JOURNAL-CONSTITUTION. ATLANTA, MARCH 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, HAS BEEN AWARDED A 2022 TOP WORKPLACES AWARD BY THE ATLANTA JOURNAL-CONSTITUTION. ROLLINS RANKED 17TH IN THE LARGE BUSINESS CATEGORY, AND THIS IS THE SIXTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR ROLLINS HAS RECEIVED THE ILLUSTRIOUS AWARD.
