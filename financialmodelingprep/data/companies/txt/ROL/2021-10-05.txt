ROLLINS, INC. SCHEDULES DATE FOR RELEASE OF THIRD QUARTER AND NINE MONTHS 2021 RESULTS. ATLANTA, OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL RELEASE ITS UNAUDITED THIRD QUARTER  AND NINE MONTHS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2021 ON WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 27, 2021.  IN CONJUNCTION WITH ITS RELEASE, THE COMPANY WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL TO REVIEW THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS ON WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 27, 2021 AT 10:00 A.M.
