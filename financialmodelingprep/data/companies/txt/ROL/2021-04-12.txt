ROLLINS, INC. BOARD DIRECTOR HENRY B. TIPPIE TO RETIRE. ATLANTA, APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE:ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, ANNOUNCED THE RETIREMENT OF HENRY B. TIPPIE, WHO JOINED THE COMPANY IN 1953.
