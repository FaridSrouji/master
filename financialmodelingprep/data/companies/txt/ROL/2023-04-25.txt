ROLLINS, INC. ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. ATLANTA, APRIL 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE:ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $0.13 PER SHARE PAYABLE JUNE 9, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 10, 2023.  THIS REPRESENTS AN INCREASE OF 30 PERCENT FROM THE SAME QUARTER A YEAR AGO.
