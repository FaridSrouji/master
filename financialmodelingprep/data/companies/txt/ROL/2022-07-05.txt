ROLLINS, INC. SCHEDULES DATE FOR RELEASE OF SECOND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. ATLANTA , JULY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL RELEASE ITS UNAUDITED SECOND QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED JUNE 30, 2022 ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 27, 2022.  IN CONJUNCTION WITH ITS RELEASE, THE COMPANY WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL TO REVIEW THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 27, 2022 AT 10:00 A.M.
