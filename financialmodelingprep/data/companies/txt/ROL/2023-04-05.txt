ROLLINS, INC. COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF FOX PEST CONTROL, A LEADING PEST MANAGEMENT COMPANY. ACQUIRED THROUGH THE HOMETEAM PEST DEFENSE BRAND HIGHLY COMPLEMENTARY END MARKET EXPOSURE THAT PROVIDES STRATEGIC GROWTH OPPORTUNITIES EXPECTED TO BE ACCRETIVE TO EARNINGS AND CASH FLOW IN THE FIRST FULL YEAR OF OWNERSHIP TRANSACTION FINANCED THROUGH A COMBINATION OF CASH ON HAND AND EXISTING CREDIT FACILITIES ATLANTA , APRIL 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE:ROL) ("ROLLINS"), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED FPC HOLDINGS, LLC ("FOX PEST CONTROL" OR THE "COMPANY"). FOX PEST CONTROL EMPLOYS MORE THAN 1,300 ASSOCIATES AND IS HEADQUARTERED IN LOGAN, UTAH.
