ROLLINS, INC. SCHEDULES DATE FOR RELEASE OF FIRST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS. ATLANTA , APRIL 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL RELEASE ITS FIRST QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED MARCH 31, 2023 AFTER THE MARKET CLOSES ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 26, 2023.  IN CONJUNCTION WITH ITS RELEASE, THE COMPANY WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL TO REVIEW THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS ON THURSDAY, APRIL 27, 2023 AT 8:30 A.M.
