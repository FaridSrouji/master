THE RAT PACK: FORMER TOP FIVE CITIES REMAIN LEADERS ON ORKIN'S 2021 RATTIEST CITIES LIST. ATLANTA, OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SPOOKY SEASON IS HERE, CUE THE RODENTS ORKIN RELEASED ITS TOP 50 RATTIEST CITIES LIST TODAY, AND THE TOP FIVE CITIES DIDN'T BUDGE, WITH CHICAGO TAKING THE TOP SPOT FOR THE SEVENTH CONSECUTIVE TIME. BREAKING INTO THE TOP 10 THIS YEAR IS CLEVELAND, TAKING THE TENTH SPOT, AND BALTIMORE INCHES CLOSER TO THE TOP FIVE, MOVING UP TWO SPOTS TO #6.
