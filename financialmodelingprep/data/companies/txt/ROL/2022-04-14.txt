NBC ENVIRONMENT ACQUIRED BY ORKIN. ATLANTA , APRIL 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE:ROL), BEST KNOWN FOR ITS SUBSIDIARY PEST CONTROL BRAND, ORKIN, FINALIZED THE ACQUISITION OF NBC ENVIRONMENT HEADQUARTERED AT BANHAM, NORFOLK, UK IN EARLY APRIL 2022. WITH A £500 PRINCE'S TRUST GRANT, JOHN DICKSON, MANAGING DIRECTOR, FOUNDED NBC ENVIRONMENT IN 1993.
