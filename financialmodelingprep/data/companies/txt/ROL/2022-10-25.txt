ROLLINS, INC. ANNOUNCES 30 PERCENT INCREASE IN REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA , OCT. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE:ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $0.13 PER SHARE PAYABLE DECEMBER 9, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 10, 2022, REPRESENTING A 30 PERCENT INCREASE IN THE REGULAR DIVIDEND.  "ROLLINS HAS A LONG-STANDING TRACK RECORD OF INCREASING OUR DIVIDEND AS WE GROW OUR BUSINESS," COMMENTED MR.
