DANAHER ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS. WASHINGTON, SEPT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DANAHER CORPORATION (NYSE: DHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.21 PER SHARE OF ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 29, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2021.
