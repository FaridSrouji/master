DANAHER ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS. WASHINGTON , SEPT. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DANAHER CORPORATION (NYSE: DHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.25 PER SHARE OF ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 28, 2022 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2022.
