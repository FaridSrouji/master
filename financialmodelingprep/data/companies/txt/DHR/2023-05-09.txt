DANAHER ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. WASHINGTON , MAY 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DANAHER CORPORATION (NYSE: DHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.27 PER SHARE OF ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JULY 28, 2023 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 30, 2023. ABOUT DANAHER DANAHER IS A GLOBAL SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY INNOVATOR COMMITTED TO HELPING ITS CUSTOMERS SOLVE COMPLEX CHALLENGES AND IMPROVING QUALITY OF LIFE AROUND THE WORLD.
