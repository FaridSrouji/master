DANAHER ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS. WASHINGTON , DEC. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DANAHER CORPORATION (NYSE: DHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.25 PER SHARE OF ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 27, 2023 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON DECEMBER 30, 2022. IN ADDITION, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $12.50 PER SHARE OF ITS 5.00% SERIES B MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED STOCK, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 15, 2023 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON DECEMBER 31, 2022.
