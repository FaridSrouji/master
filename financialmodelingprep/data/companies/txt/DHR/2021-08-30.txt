DANAHER COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF ALDEVRON. WASHINGTON , AUG. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DANAHER CORPORATION (NYSE: DHR) ("DANAHER") ANNOUNCED TODAY THE COMPLETION OF ITS ACQUISITION OF ALDEVRON. AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED, ALDEVRON WILL OPERATE AS A STANDALONE OPERATING COMPANY AND BRAND WITHIN DANAHER'S LIFE SCIENCES SEGMENT.
