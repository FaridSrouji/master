DANAHER ANNOUNCES INTENTION TO SEPARATE ENVIRONMENTAL & APPLIED SOLUTIONS SEGMENT TO CREATE AN INDEPENDENT, PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANY. WASHINGTON , SEPT. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DANAHER CORPORATION (NYSE: DHR) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INTENTION TO SEPARATE ITS ENVIRONMENTAL & APPLIED SOLUTIONS SEGMENT TO CREATE AN INDEPENDENT, PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANY.
