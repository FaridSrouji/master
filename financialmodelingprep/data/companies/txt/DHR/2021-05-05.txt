DANAHER ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS. WASHINGTON, MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DANAHER CORPORATION (NYSE: DHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.21 PER SHARE OF ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JULY 30, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 25, 2021. IN ADDITION, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $11.875 PER SHARE OF ITS 4.75% SERIES A MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED STOCK, PAYABLE ON JULY 15, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 30, 2021.
