CONSUMERS ENERGY ANNOUNCES MAJOR NEW INVESTMENT IN NATURAL GAS SYSTEM, IMPROVES RELIABILITY FOR CUSTOMERS. JACKSON, MICH. , JULY 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY RECEIVED APPROVAL TODAY ON A $170 MILLION INVESTMENT TO MODERNIZE MICHIGAN'S NATURAL GAS SYSTEM, MAKING IT EVEN MORE SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE AND AFFORDABLE FOR CUSTOMERS.
