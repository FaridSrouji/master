CONSUMERS ENERGY TO STUDY IMPACTS OF ITS DAMS ON LOCAL COMMUNITIES. JACKSON, MICH. , DEC. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY IS FUNDING AN INDEPENDENT STUDY DURING THE FIRST HALF OF 2023 TO DETERMINE THE CONTRIBUTION OF ITS 13 RIVER HYDRO FACILITIES TO LOCAL COMMUNITIES NEAR THOSE DAMS ACROSS MICHIGAN.
