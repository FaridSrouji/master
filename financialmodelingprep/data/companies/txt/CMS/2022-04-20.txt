CONSUMERS ENERGY AND KEY STAKEHOLDERS REACH LANDMARK AGREEMENT TO CREATE CLEANER ENERGY FUTURE FOR MICHIGAN. JACKSON, MICH. , APRIL 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY AND KEY STAKEHOLDERS THROUGHOUT MICHIGAN HAVE AGREED ON A SETTLEMENT RELATED TO THE COMPANY'S CLEAN ENERGY PLAN, A SWEEPING PROPOSAL TO STOP USING COAL AS A FUEL SOURCE FOR ELECTRIC GENERATION BY 2025, MAKING THE COMPANY ONE OF THE FIRST IN THE NATION TO GO COAL-FREE.
