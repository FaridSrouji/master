CONSUMERS ENERGY STARTS OPERATING COVERT POWER PLANT, RELIABLY MEETING MICHIGAN'S ENERGY NEEDS. COVERT TOWNSHIP, MICH., JUNE 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY IT IS NOW OPERATING THE COVERT GENERATING STATION IN SOUTHWEST MICHIGAN'S VAN BUREN COUNTY, AN IMPORTANT PART OF ITS INDUSTRY-LEADING CLEAN ENERGY PLAN TO MEET MICHIGAN'S ENERGY NEEDS THIS SUMMER AND FOR YEARS TO COME.
