CONSUMERS ENERGY FOUNDATION PROVIDES $500,000 TO THREE PROJECTS PROTECTING MICHIGAN'S ENVIRONMENT. PLANET AWARDS SUPPORT PROJECTS IN SOUTHEAST MICHIGAN AND KALAMAZOO AREA   JACKSON, MICH. , APRIL 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CONSUMERS ENERGY FOUNDATION CELEBRATED EARTH MONTH TODAY BY PROVIDING $500,000 FOR THREE PROJECTS THAT WILL MAKE A SIGNIFICANT IMPACT ON MICHIGAN'S ENVIRONMENT – PRESERVING FRESHWATER RESOURCES, PLANTING 1,000 TREES AND PROTECTING HABITAT FOR MICHIGAN'S WILDLIFE.
