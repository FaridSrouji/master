CONSUMERS ENERGY PROPOSES INVESTMENT TO MODERNIZE GRID, IMPROVE CUSTOMER RELIABILITY AND LEAD CLEAN ENERGY TRANSFORMATION. JACKSON, MICH. , APRIL 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY PROPOSED A $272 MILLION INVESTMENT IN MICHIGAN'S ENERGY FUTURE TO SUPPORT A MORE RELIABLE, RESILIENT, AND INCREASINGLY CLEAN ENERGY SYSTEM FOR ITS CUSTOMERS.
