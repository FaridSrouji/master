CMS ENERGY ANNOUNCES STRONG SECOND QUARTER PERFORMANCE WITH CONFIDENCE TOWARD THE HIGH END OF 2021 EPS GUIDANCE. JACKSON, MICH., JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CMS ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY REPORTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $0.61 FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021, COMPARED TO $0.48 PER SHARE FOR THE SAME QUARTER IN 2020.
