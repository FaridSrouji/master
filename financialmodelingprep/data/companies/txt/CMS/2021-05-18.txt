CONSUMERS ENERGY INTRODUCES NEW SUMMER PEAK RATE, GIVING HOUSEHOLDS MORE CONTROL OVER ENERGY COSTS. JACKSON, MICH., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY IS GIVING MICHIGAN HOUSEHOLDS THE ABILITY TO LOWER ENERGY BILLS AND TAKE MEANINGFUL ACTIONS TO PROTECT THE PLANET WITH A NEW STANDARD SUMMER PEAK RATE THAT WILL GO INTO EFFECT JUNE 1 FOR 1.6 MILLION RESIDENTIAL ELECTRIC CUSTOMERS.
