CONSUMERS ENERGY FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES $500,000 IN GRANTS FOR BASIC NEEDS ASSISTANCE TO FOOD BANK COUNCIL OF MICHIGAN, MICHIGAN ASSOCIATION OF UNITED WAYS. JACKSON, MICH. , NOV. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CONSUMERS ENERGY FOUNDATION ANNOUNCED TODAY $500,000 IN GRANT FUNDING TO HELP TWO STATEWIDE ORGANIZATIONS — THE FOOD BANK COUNCIL OF MICHIGAN (FBCM) AND THE MICHIGAN ASSOCIATION OF UNITED WAYS (MAUW) — TO PROVIDE DIRECT ASSISTANCE AND NECESSITIES TO MICHIGANDERS.
