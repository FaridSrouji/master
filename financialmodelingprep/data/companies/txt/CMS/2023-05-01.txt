CMS ENERGY ANNOUNCES PROPOSED OFFERING OF $650 MILLION OF CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028. JACKSON, MICH. , MAY 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CMS ENERGY CORPORATION ("CMS ENERGY") (NYSE: CMS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT INTENDS TO OFFER, SUBJECT TO MARKET AND OTHER CONDITIONS, $650 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (THE "CONVERTIBLE NOTES") IN A PRIVATE PLACEMENT UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED (THE "SECURITIES ACT").
