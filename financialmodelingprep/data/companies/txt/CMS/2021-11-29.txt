CONSUMERS ENERGY AGAIN PROVIDES HOLIDAY CHEER WITH $750,000 FOR GIFT CARDS IN OVER 60 MICHIGAN COMMUNITIES. JACKSON, MICH., NOV. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY IS AGAIN CELEBRATING THE HOLIDAYS BY HELPING SHOPPERS AND MICHIGAN'S LOCAL BUSINESSES, PROVIDING $750,000 IN MATCHING GIFT CARDS IN 63 COMMUNITIES THROUGH ITS "OUR TOWN" EFFORT.
