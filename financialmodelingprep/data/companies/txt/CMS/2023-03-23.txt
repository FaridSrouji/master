SEVEN DOMINO'S PIZZA LOCATIONS JOIN CONSUMERS ENERGY IN POWERING ELECTRIC VEHICLE COMMITMENT IN MICHIGAN. JACKSON, MICH. , MARCH 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY IS PROVIDING REBATES FOR ELECTRIC VEHICLE (EV) CHARGERS AT SEVEN DOMINO'S PIZZA LOCATIONS IN WEST AND MID-MICHIGAN THAT WILL SERVE CUSTOMERS WITH ELECTRIC VEHICLES.
