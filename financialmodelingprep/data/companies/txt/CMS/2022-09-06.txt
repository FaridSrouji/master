CMS ENERGY ANNOUNCES NEW EXECUTIVE TEAM MEMBERS. GUY PACKARD, VP OF ELECTRIC OPS, TO RETIRE AFTER 37 YEARS JACKSON, MICH. , SEPT. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CMS ENERGY AND ITS PRINCIPAL SUBSIDIARY CONSUMERS ENERGY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT GUY PACKARD, VICE PRESIDENT OF ELECTRIC OPERATIONS WILL RETIRE AFTER 37 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY AND CHRIS LAIRD WILL BE NAMED VICE PRESIDENT OF ELECTRIC OPERATIONS EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 16.
