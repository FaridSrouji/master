CONSUMERS ENERGY WARNS MICHIGAN CUSTOMERS TO WATCH OUT FOR NEW ONLINE SCAMS. JACKSON, MICH. , JUNE 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY IS WARNING CUSTOMERS TO BE AWARE OF ONLINE SCAMMERS WHO AIM TO TRICK PEOPLE INTO CALLING BOGUS PHONE NUMBERS AND OBTAIN ACCESS TO CREDIT CARDS, BANK ACCOUNTS OR OTHER PERSONAL INFORMATION.
