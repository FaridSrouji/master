CONSUMERS ENERGY FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES $500,000 AWARDED TO CRISTO REY COMMUNITY CENTER, ROSCOMMON COUNTY UNITED WAY. JACKSON, MICH. , OCT. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CONSUMERS ENERGY FOUNDATION ANNOUNCED TWO GRANTS TODAY TOTALING $500,000 FOR TWO ORGANIZATIONS — CRISTO REY COMMUNITY CENTER IN LANSING AND ROSCOMMON COUNTY UNITED WAY — IN ITS THIRD YEAR OF PEOPLE AWARDS DISBURSEMENTS.
