CMS ENERGY ANNOUNCES HOLLY BOWERS AS VICE PRESIDENT OF GAS ENGINEERING AND SUPPLY. DENNIS DOBBS TO RETIRE AFTER 20 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY JACKSON, MICH., JUNE 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CMS ENERGY AND ITS PRINCIPAL SUBSIDIARY CONSUMERS ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DENNIS DOBBS, VICE PRESIDENT OF GAS ENGINEERING AND SUPPLY WILL RETIRE AFTER 20 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY WITH HOLLY BOWERS BEING NAMED VICE PRESIDENT OF GAS ENGINEERING AND SUPPLY, EFFECTIVE JUNE 16.
