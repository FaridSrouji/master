CMS ENERGY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK TO 48.75 CENTS PER SHARE. JACKSON, MICH. , FEB. 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CMS ENERGY TODAY INCREASED THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK TO 48.75 CENTS PER SHARE, UP FROM 46 CENTS PER SHARE.
CMS ENERGY ANNOUNCES STRONG 2022 RESULTS FOR THE 20TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR AND RAISES 2023 ADJUSTED EPS GUIDANCE. JACKSON, MICH. , FEB. 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CMS ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY REPORTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $2.85 FOR 2022, COMPARED TO $4.66 PER SHARE FOR 2021.
