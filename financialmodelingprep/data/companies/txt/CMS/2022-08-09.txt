CONSUMERS ENERGY GATHERING OPINIONS TO GUIDE PLANNING THEIR DAMS' FUTURES. COLLECTING FEEDBACK FROM COMMUNITY MEETINGS AND SURVEYS JACKSON, MICH. , AUG. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY IS STARTING THIS MONTH TO GATHER OPINIONS FROM THE PUBLIC ABOUT THE 13 DAMS THEY OWN AND OPERATE ON FIVE MICHIGAN RIVERS TO HELP GUIDE THEIR DECISIONS ABOUT THE FUTURE OF THOSE ELECTRIC GENERATING FACILITIES.
