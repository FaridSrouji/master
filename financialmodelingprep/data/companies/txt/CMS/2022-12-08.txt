CONSUMERS ENERGY PROVIDES $7.25 MILLION TO HELP MICHIGANDERS WITH WINTER HEATING BILLS. JACKSON, MICH. , DEC. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY TODAY IS PROVIDING $7.25 MILLION TO HELP CUSTOMERS PAY HEATING BILLS, PROVIDING A LIFELINE TO THOUSANDS OF FAMILIES JUST BEFORE THE START OF ANOTHER COLD MICHIGAN WINTER.
