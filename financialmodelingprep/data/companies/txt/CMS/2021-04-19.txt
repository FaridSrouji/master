CONSUMERS ENERGY LEADS MICHIGAN'S ELECTRIC VEHICLE TRANSFORMATION, PLANS 200 FAST CHARGING STATIONS BY 2024. JACKSON, MICH., APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY IS CELEBRATING EARTH WEEK BY ANNOUNCING IT HAS NOW BUILT THE BACKBONE FOR THE STILL-GROWING FAST CHARGING NETWORK FOR ELECTRIC VEHICLES ACROSS MICHIGAN.
