CONSUMERS ENERGY FOUNDATION PROVIDES $500,000 TO FOUR PROJECTS PROTECTING MICHIGAN'S ENVIRONMENT. JACKSON, MICH., MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CONSUMERS ENERGY FOUNDATION CELEBRATED EARTH MONTH BY PROVIDING $500,000 TO FOUR PROJECTS THAT WILL HELP PROTECT AND RESTORE MICHIGAN'S LAND, WATER AND AIR.
