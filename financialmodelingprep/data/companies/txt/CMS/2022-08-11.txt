CONSUMERS ENERGY, STATE OF MICHIGAN AGREE TO POWER OVER 1,200 PUBLIC BUILDINGS WITH 100% CLEAN ENERGY. JACKSON, MICH. , AUG. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY AND THE STATE OF MICHIGAN TODAY ANNOUNCED A PLEDGE TO POWER 1,274 PUBLICLY OWNED GOVERNMENT BUILDINGS EXCLUSIVELY WITH CLEAN ENERGY, A VISIBLE COMMITMENT BY THE STATE'S GOVERNMENT AND ITS LARGEST ENERGY PROVIDER TO PROTECT THE PLANET.
