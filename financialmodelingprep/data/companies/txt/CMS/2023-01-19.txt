CONSUMERS ENERGY MAKES NEW $25 MILLION CONTRIBUTION TO HELP MICHIGAN HOMES AND BUSINESSES WITH BILLS. JACKSON, MICH. , JAN. 19, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY TODAY IS ANNOUNCING IT WILL PROVIDE $25 MILLION TO HOMES AND BUSINESSES, A MEANINGFUL EFFORT TO HELP CUSTOMERS WHO ARE FACING CHALLENGES WITH THEIR ENERGY BILLS.
