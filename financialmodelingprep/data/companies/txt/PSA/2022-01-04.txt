PUBLIC STORAGE ANNOUNCES PRICING OF 4.100% CUMULATIVE PREFERRED SHARES OF BENEFICIAL INTEREST, SERIES S. GLENDALE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TOM BOYLE, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER OF PUBLIC STORAGE (NYSE:PSA, THE “COMPANY”), ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF 10,000,000 DEPOSITARY SHARES AT $25.00 PER DEPOSITARY SHARE, WITH EACH DEPOSITARY SHARE REPRESENTING 1/1,000 OF A 4.100% CUMULATIVE PREFERRED SHARE OF BENEFICIAL INTEREST, SERIES S (THE “SERIES S PREFERRED SHARES”). THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO RESULT IN $250 MILLION OF GROSS PROCEEDS (ASSUMING NO EXERCISE OF THE UNDERWRITER
