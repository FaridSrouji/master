SANDS ANNOUNCES NEW SANDS CARES ACCELERATOR GRADUATE. NEVADA PARTNERSHIP FOR HOMELESS YOUTH COMPLETES THE THREE-YEAR FLAGSHIP PROGRAM DEVELOPED BY SANDS TO HELP SELECT NONPROFITS REACH NEW HEIGHTS LAS VEGAS , MARCH 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN A VIRTUAL GLOBAL CELEBRATION HELD LAST EVENING, LAS VEGAS SANDS (NYSE: LVS) HONORED NEVADA PARTNERSHIP FOR HOMELESS YOUTH (NPHY) AS THE SECOND GRADUATE OF THE SANDS CARES ACCELERATOR, AN EXCLUSIVE THREE-YEAR NONPROFIT MEMBERSHIP PROGRAM DESIGNED TO HELP FAST-TRACK RISING ORGANIZATIONS ON THE TIPPING POINT OF MAKING A LEAP IN COMMUNITY IMPACT. NPHY JOINED THE SANDS CARES ACCELERATOR IN 2018 TO FOCUS ON BUILDING THE ORGANIZATION'S STRATEGIC VISION FOR THE NEXT STAGE IN ITS EVOLUTION AS THE MOST COMPREHENSIVE SERVICE PROVIDER FOR THE THOUSANDS OF YOUNG PEOPLE EXPERIENCING HOMELESSNESS IN SOUTHERN NEVADA.
