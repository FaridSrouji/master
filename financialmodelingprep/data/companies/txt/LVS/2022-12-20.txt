LAS VEGAS SANDS NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. SUBSIDIARY SANDS CHINA LTD. JOINS THE DJSI WORLD INDEX LAS VEGAS , DEC. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LAS VEGAS SANDS (NYSE: LVS) HAS ONCE AGAIN BEEN RECOGNIZED ON THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDICES (DJSI), WITH PLACEMENT ON DJSI WORLD INDEX FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR AND ON THE DJSI NORTH AMERICA INDEX FOR THE SEVENTH TIME.
