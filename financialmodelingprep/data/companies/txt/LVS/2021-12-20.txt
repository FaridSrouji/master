LAS VEGAS SANDS NAMED TO NEWSWEEK'S AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES 2022. LAS VEGAS, DEC. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LAS VEGAS SANDS (NYSE: LVS) HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY NEWSWEEK AS ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES FOR 2022. THE COMPANY RANKED 136TH OUT OF 500 TOTAL COMPANIES INCLUDED AND WAS THE HIGHEST-RANKING HOSPITALITY AND GAMING COMPANY NAMED TO THE LIST.
