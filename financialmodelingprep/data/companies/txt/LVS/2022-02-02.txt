LAS VEGAS SANDS EARNS SIXTH STRAIGHT APPEARANCE ON FORTUNE'S "WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES" LIST. LAS VEGAS, FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LAS VEGAS SANDS (NYSE: LVS) HAS AGAIN BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE "WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES" IN FORTUNE'S ANNUAL LIST OF THE MOST RESPECTED AND REPUTABLE BUSINESSES. SANDS' 2022 APPEARANCE ON THE LIST IS ITS SIXTH STRAIGHT AND NINTH IN THE PAST DECADE.
