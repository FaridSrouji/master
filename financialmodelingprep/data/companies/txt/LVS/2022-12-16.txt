SANDS CHINA SIGNS 10-YEAR GAMING CONCESSION CONTRACT. MACAO , DEC. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SANDS CHINA GROUP SIGNED ITS FINALISED 10-YEAR GAMING CONCESSION CONTRACT FRIDAY AT A SIGNING CEREMONY, AFTER HAVING BEEN AWARDED A PROVISIONAL CONCESSION BY THE MACAO SAR GOVERNMENT ON NOV. 26.
