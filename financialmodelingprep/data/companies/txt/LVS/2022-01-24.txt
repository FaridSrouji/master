SANDS CHINA HOSTS 9TH SANDS SUPPLIER EXCELLENCE AWARDS AND 10TH SANDS PROCUREMENT ACADEMY GRADUATION. MACAO, JAN. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SANDS CHINA LTD. HELD A JOINT EVENT FRIDAY AT THE LONDONER® MACAO, WHERE THE COMPANY HONOURED SOME OF ITS MOST OUTSTANDING SUPPLIERS, RECOGNISED LOCAL SMES GRADUATING FROM ITS PROCUREMENT TRAINING PROGRAMME, AND LAUNCHED A BOOK HIGHLIGHTING THE SUCCESS STORIES OF ITS LOCAL SME SUPPLIERS.
