WTW APPOINTS WENDY CROSLEY GLOBAL DIRECTOR OF UNDERWRITING TRANSFORMATION AND AUTOMATION. NEW YORK, FEB. 13, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WTW (NASDAQ: WTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF WENDY CROSLEY AS THE GLOBAL DIRECTOR OF UNDERWRITING TRANSFORMATION AND AUTOMATION, WITHIN THE COMPANY'S INSURANCE CONSULTING AND TECHNOLOGY (ICT) BUSINESS.
