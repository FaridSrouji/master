WTW INNOVATIVE RISK FINANCE PROJECT TO SAFEGUARD MELANESIAN FISHING COMMUNITIES AGAINST CLIMATE THREATS. LONDON, MAY 09, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WTW (WILLIS TOWERS WATSON, NASDAQ: WTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING, AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A NEW FINANCIAL INITIATIVE TO HELP VULNERABLE FISHING COMMUNITIES IN FIJI AND PAPUA NEW GUINEA (PNG) TO BE MORE RESILIENT TO THE ADVERSE IMPACTS OF CLIMATE CHANGE.
