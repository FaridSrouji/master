WTW APPOINTS TONY YEN AS HEAD OF TAIWAN. TAIPEI, TAIWAN, JAN. 16, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WTW (NASDAQ: WTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF TONY YEN AS HEAD OF TAIWAN, EFFECTIVE 1 JANUARY 2023. TONY SUCCEEDS CHARLES WANG WHO RETIRED IN DECEMBER 2022.
