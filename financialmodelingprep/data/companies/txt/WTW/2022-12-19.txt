CORPORATE RISK AND BROKING NORTH AMERICA LAUNCHES INNOVATIVE NEW APPROACH TO INDUSTRY SPECIALIZATION BASED ON EXTENSIVE CLIENT INPUT. NEW YORK, DEC. 19, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WTW (WILLIS TOWERS WATSON, NASDAQ: WTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING, AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS NORTH AMERICAN CORPORATE RISK AND BROKING (CRB) BUSINESS HAS LAUNCHED A NEW STRATEGY WHICH ENHANCES ITS CLIENT PROPOSITION THROUGH ALIGNING THE ENTIRE VALUE-CHAIN OF INTERNAL RESOURCES THROUGH INDUSTRY SPECIALIZATION.
