WTW INDIA RECEIVES IRDAI APPROVAL FOR FULL OWNERSHIP AND APPOINTS VIVEK NATH AS HEAD OF INDIA. SINGAPORE, APRIL 10, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WTW (WILLIS TOWERS WATSON, NASDAQ: WTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED APPROVAL FROM THE INSURANCE REGULATORY AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY OF INDIA (IRDAI) AND HAS CLOSED THE TRANSACTION ON THE ACQUISITION OF THE REMAINING 51% SHARES IN WILLIS TOWERS WATSON INDIA INSURANCE BROKERS PRIVATE LIMITED (WTW INDIA). EFFECTIVE 11 APRIL, VIVEK NATH IS APPOINTED AS WTW'S HEAD OF INDIA.
