INVESTORS HIGHLIGHT LIMITS OF CARBON METRICS IN MANAGING RISKS AND OPPORTUNITIES OF NET ZERO TRANSITION, WTW POLL REVEALS. ARLINGTON, VA., OCT. 06, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CARBON METRICS ON THEIR OWN LACK THE FORWARD-LOOKING INSIGHTS NEEDED TO ASSESS THE FINANCIAL RISKS AND OPPORTUNITIES OF TRANSITIONING TO A LOW-CARBON ECONOMY, ACCORDING TO A SURVEY OF LEADING INVESTMENT PROFESSIONALS CONDUCTED BY WTW (NASDAQ: WTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY.
