WTW RESEARCH SHOWS DIVERSE INVESTMENT TEAMS OUTPERFORM. ARLINGTON, VA., MARCH 07, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NEW ANALYSIS RELEASED BY WTW HAS REVEALED THAT AN INVESTMENT TEAM WITH A GREATER LEVEL OF DIVERSITY LEADS TO BETTER INVESTMENT OUTCOMES. IN THE COMPANY'S NEW PAPER, “DIVERSITY IN THE ASSET MANAGEMENT INDUSTRY: ON THE RIGHT TRACK BUT AT THE WRONG PACE,” DATA SHOW THAT INVESTMENT TEAMS IN THE TOP QUARTILE OF GENDER DIVERSITY OUTPERFORM THE BOTTOM QUARTILE BY 45 BASIS POINTS YEARLY IN TERMS OF NET EXCESS RETURNS.
