WTW LAUNCHES GEOPOLITICAL RISK RESEARCH PROGRAMME TO BRING TOGETHER WORLD EXPERTS AND BUILD RESILIENCE AGAINST ESCALATING THREATS. THE THREE-YEAR PROGRAMME WILL APPLY WAR-GAMING AND SCENARIO PLANNING TO TEST THE VULNERABILITY OF ORGANISATIONS TO CHANGING GEOPOLITICAL DRIVERS OF RISK, IDENTIFY THEIR INSURANCE GAPS AND IMPROVE RESILIENCE.
