ESG MEASURES IN EXECUTIVE INCENTIVE PLANS GAINING MOMENTUM, WTW STUDY FINDS. ARLINGTON, VA., DEC. 15, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FACED WITH MOUNTING PRESSURE FROM INVESTORS, CUSTOMERS, REGULATORS AND EMPLOYEES, MORE U.S. PUBLIC COMPANIES ARE INCORPORATING ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) MEASURES IN THEIR EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION PROGRAMS, ACCORDING TO A NEW GLOBAL STUDY BY WTW (NASDAQ: WTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY. SIMILAR TRENDS ARE OCCURRING AMONG EUROPEAN AND CANADIAN COMPANIES, THE STUDY FOUND.
