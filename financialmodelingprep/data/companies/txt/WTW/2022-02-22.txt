WTW TAKES THE TOP SPOT IN ADVISEN'S ANNUAL INNOVATION PACESETTER INDEX. ARLINGTON, VA., FEB. 20, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WTW (WILLIS TOWERS WATSON, NASDAQ: WTW) HAS BEEN AWARDED THE NUMBER ONE SPOT IN ADVISEN'S ANNUAL INNOVATION PACESETTER INDEX. THE COMPANY TOPPED THE INDEX HAVING LAUNCHED 16 NEW PRODUCTS AND SERVICES IN 2021.
