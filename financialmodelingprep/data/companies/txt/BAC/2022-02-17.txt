DIGITAL ENGAGEMENT SOARS AT BANK OF AMERICA TO MORE THAN 10 BILLION LOGINS, UP 15% YEAR-OVER-YEAR. CHARLOTTE, N.C., FEB. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA ADDED MORE THAN 2 MILLION ACTIVE DIGITAL CLIENTS LAST YEAR, A SINGLE-YEAR RECORD, WITH ITS TOTAL NUMBER OF VERIFIED DIGITAL USERS REACHING MORE THAN 54 MILLION.
