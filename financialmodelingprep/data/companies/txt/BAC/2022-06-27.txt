BANK OF AMERICA COMMENTS ON STRESS TEST RESULTS; PLANS TO INCREASE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO $0.22 PER SHARE. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , JUNE 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA TODAY COMMENTED ON THE RESULTS OF THE FEDERAL RESERVE'S 2022 COMPREHENSIVE CAPITAL ANALYSIS AND REVIEW (CCAR) AND ANNOUNCED PLANS TO INCREASE ITS QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND TO $0.22 PER SHARE BEGINNING IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022.
