BANK OF AMERICA DONATES $1 MILLION TOWARD KIRK KERKORIAN SCHOOL OF MEDICINE AT UNLV. LAS VEGAS, DEC. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BUDDING 135,000 SQUARE-FOOT KIRK KERKORIAN SCHOOL OF MEDICINE AT UNLV HAS RECEIVED A $1 MILLION DONATION TOWARD ITS CONSTRUCTION FROM BANK OF AMERICA. THE CORPORATE DONATION IS THE VERY FIRST OF ITS KIND BENEFITTING THE PROJECT, WHICH BROKE GROUND IN OCTOBER 2020 AND IS SLATED FOR COMPLETION IN 2022.
