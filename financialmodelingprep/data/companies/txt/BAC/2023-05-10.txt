BANK OF AMERICA OPENS LUXEMBOURG BRANCH. IN-COUNTRY TRANSACTION BANKING OPERATIONS LAUNCHED IN KEY FINANCIAL HUB LONDON , MAY 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS OPENED A NEW BRANCH IN LUXEMBOURG, FURTHER STRENGTHENING ITS CAPABILITIES AND THE VALUE IT OFFERS TO CLIENTS IN CORE MARKETS. THE LAUNCH MEANS THE BANK CAN SUPPORT CLIENTS – CORPORATE, COMMERCIAL AND NBFIS (NON-BANK FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS) – WITH SETTING UP LOCAL BANK ACCOUNTS AND PROVIDE IN-COUNTRY TRANSACTION BANKING PRODUCTS AND SERVICES.
