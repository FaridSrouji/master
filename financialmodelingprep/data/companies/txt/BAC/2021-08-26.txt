BANK OF AMERICA ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF ANNE FINUCANE, VICE CHAIRMAN AND THOMAS MONTAG, CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER AND PRESIDENT OF GLOBAL BANKING AND MARKETS. CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BANK OF AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RETIREMENT OF ANNE FINUCANE, VICE CHAIRMAN, AND THOMAS MONTAG, CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER AND PRESIDENT OF GLOBAL BANKING AND MARKETS, AFTER MANY YEARS OF DISTINGUISHED SERVICE. BOTH EXECUTIVES ARE MEMBERS OF THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE MANAGEMENT TEAM AND REPORT TO CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER BRIAN MOYNIHAN.
