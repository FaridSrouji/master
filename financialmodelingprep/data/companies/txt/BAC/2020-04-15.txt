BANK OF AMERICA REPORTS Q1 EPS OF $0.40. BANK OF AMERICA REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER 2020 FINANCIAL RESULTS.QUARTERLY EARNINGS OF $4.0 BILLION, EPS OF $0.40.QUARTERLY PROVISION FOR CREDIT LOSSES OF $4.8 BILLION INCLUDES A $3.6 BILLION RESERVE BUILD.QUARTERLY REVENUE, NET OF INTEREST EXPENSE, DECREASED 1% TO $22.8 BILLION.QUARTERLY NET INCOME OF $4.0 BILLION, OR $0.40 PER DILUTED SHARE, INCLUDES HIGHER PROVISION EXPENSE FOR COVID-19 RELATED RESERVE BUILD.RECEIVED 279,000 SMALL BUSINESS LOAN APPLICATIONS THROUGH APRIL 8 UNDER THE PAYCHECK PROTECTION PROGRAM, TOTALING $43 BILLION.BANK OF AMERICA -ENDS QUARTER WITH $265 BILLION IN SHAREHOLDERS' EQUITY AND NEARLY $700 BILLION IN GLOBAL LIQUIDITY SOURCES.QUARTERLY FICC REVENUE INCREASED 13% TO $2.7 BILLION.QUARTERLY EQUITIES REVENUE INCREASED 39% TO $1.7 BILLION.QUARTERLY AVERAGE LOAN AND LEASE BALANCES IN THE BUSINESS SEGMENTS ROSE $57 BILLION, OR 6%, YOY TO $954 BILLION.QUARTERLY NET INTEREST INCOME $12,130 MILLION VERSUS $12,375 MILLION.QUARTERLY NET CHARGE-OFF RATIO IMPROVED TO 1.22%, COMPARED TO 1.28%.QUARTERLY NONINTEREST INCOME $10,637 MILLION VERSUS $10,629 MILLION.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $0.46, REVENUE VIEW $22.91 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
