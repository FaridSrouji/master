BANK OF AMERICA IS FIRST IN INDUSTRY TO LAUNCH VIRTUAL REALITY TRAINING PROGRAM IN NEARLY 4,300 FINANCIAL CENTERS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., OCT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA IS THE FIRST FINANCIAL SERVICES FIRM TO LAUNCH VIRTUAL REALITY (VR) TRAINING IN NEARLY 4,300 FINANCIAL CENTERS NATIONWIDE.
