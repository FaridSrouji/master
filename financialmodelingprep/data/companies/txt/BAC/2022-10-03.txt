CHICAGO'S PREMIER RACING PORTFOLIO RETURNS IN 2023 WITH THE DEBUT OF THE BANK OF AMERICA CHICAGO DISTANCE SERIES. REGISTRATION FOR THE 2023 BANK OF AMERICA CHICAGO MARATHON, CHICAGO 13.1 AND SHAMROCK SHUFFLE OPENS THIS MONTH CHICAGO , OCT. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY THE TEAMS BEHIND CHICAGO'S PREMIER RACING PORTFOLIO –  THE BANK OF AMERICA CHICAGO MARATHON, CHICAGO 13.1 AND SHAMROCK SHUFFLE – ANNOUNCED THE DEBUT OF THE BANK OF AMERICA CHICAGO DISTANCE SERIES. THE DISTANCE SERIES CONNECTS THE THREE UNIQUE EVENTS THAT SPAN THE OUTDOOR RUNNING SEASON, FROM AN APPROACHABLE 8K TO A WORLD-CLASS MARATHON.
