NOMAD HEALTH APPOINTS FORMER BANK OF AMERICA EXECUTIVE ANNE WALKER AS NEW CFO. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NOMAD HEALTH, THE LEADING DIGITAL MARKETPLACE FOR TEMPORARY HEALTHCARE JOBS, ANNOUNCED THAT ANNE WALKER HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS ITS NEW CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO). RESPONSIBLE FOR MANAGING NOMAD HEALTH'S FINANCIAL STRATEGY AS IT CONTINUES MODERNIZING THE HEALTHCARE STAFFING INDUSTRY, WALKER HAS OVER 20 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE TRANSFORMING ORGANIZATIONS AND OVERSEEING STRATEGIC GROWTH AT COMPANIES FROM EARLY STAGE TO FORTUNE 500.
