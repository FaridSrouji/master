BOFA ADDS ACH POSITIVE PAY ON THE CASHPRO® APP, GIVING CLIENTS GREATER CONTROL AND PEACE OF MIND. NEW YORK , MARCH 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA'S BUSINESS CLIENTS ARE INCREASINGLY UTILIZING MOBILE DEVICES TO MANAGE PAYMENTS AND TREASURY OPERATIONS, AND IN SUPPORT OF THIS TREND, THE BANK TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ACH POSITIVE PAY ON ITS AWARD-WINNING CASHPRO APP. ACH POSITIVE PAY, WHICH IS ALREADY AVAILABLE TO BUSINESS CLIENTS ON THE BANK'S ONLINE VERSION OF CASHPRO, LETS CLIENTS REVIEW AND EITHER APPROVE OR REJECT INCOMING TRANSACTION REQUESTS ("EXCEPTIONS") PROCESSED THROUGH THE U.S. AUTOMATED CLEARING HOUSE, OR ACH.
