BANK OF AMERICA ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF €2.0 BILLION OF FLOATING RATE SENIOR NOTES, DUE APRIL 25, 2024. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , APRIL 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA CORPORATION ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL REDEEM ON APRIL 25, 2023 ALL €2,000,000,000 PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OUTSTANDING OF ITS FLOATING RATE SENIOR NOTES, DUE APRIL 25, 2024 (ISIN: XS1811433983; COMMON CODE: 181143398) (THE " NOTES").
