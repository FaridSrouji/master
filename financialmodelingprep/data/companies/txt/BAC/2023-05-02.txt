BANK OF AMERICA AWARDS NEGRO LEAGUES BASEBALL MUSEUM $1M GRANT FOR NEW FACILITY. NEW MUSEUM WILL FURTHER TELL THE POWERFUL STORIES OF THE NEGRO LEAGUES BASEBALL PLAYERS KANSAS CITY, MO. , MAY 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE NEGRO LEAGUES BASEBALL MUSEUM (NLBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN AWARDED A $1 MILLION GRANT FROM BANK OF AMERICA IN SUPPORT OF THE MUSEUM'S $25 MILLION CAPITAL CAMPAIGN TO BUILD A NEW 30,000 SQUARE-FOOT FACILITY.
