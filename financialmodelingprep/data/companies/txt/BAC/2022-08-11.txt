FIVE NEW JERSEY TEENS SELECTED AS BANK OF AMERICA STUDENT LEADERS. PAID INTERNSHIPS CONNECT NEW JERSEY YOUTH TO CAREER BUILDING OPPORTUNITIES TRENTON, N.J. , AUG. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED FIVE NORTHERN NEW JERSEY HIGH SCHOOL STUDENTS HAVE BEEN SELECTED AS STUDENT LEADERS®  (#BOFASTUDENTLEADERS), AN EIGHT-WEEK PAID INTERNSHIP PROVIDING STUDENTS WITH FIRST-HAND EXPERIENCE IN SERVING THEIR COMMUNITIES.
