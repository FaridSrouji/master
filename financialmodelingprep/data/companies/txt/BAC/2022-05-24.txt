BANK OF AMERICA TO MORE THAN DOUBLE ELECTRIFY AMERICA ULTRA-FAST CHARGING STATIONS AT FINANCIAL CENTERS. OVER 90 FINANCIAL CENTER LOCATIONS WILL OFFER ELECTRIC VEHICLE (EV) CHARGING BY THE END OF 2023 CHARLOTTE, N.C. , MAY 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA AND ELECTRIFY AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO MORE THAN DOUBLE THE NUMBER OF FINANCIAL CENTERS EQUIPPED WITH ELECTRIC-VEHICLE (EV) CHARGING STATIONS BY THE END OF 2023.
