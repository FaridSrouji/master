BANK OF AMERICA CORP. HIRES MICHAEL CAHILL AS ITS GLOBAL CORPORATE AND INVESTMENT BANKING COO FOR AUSTRALIA. BANK OF AMERICA CORP. HAS HIRED GOLDMAN SACHS' EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR MICHAEL CAHILL AS ITS GLOBAL CORPORATE AND INVESTMENT BANKING COO FOR AUSTRALIA. CAHILL HAD BEEN WITH GOLDMAN SACHS FOR THE PAST 14 YEARS. IN THE NEW ROLE, HE IS TO REPORT TO BANK OF AMERICA CHIEF EXECUTIVE JOE FAYYAD AND GROW THE COMPANY'S CORPORATE AND INVESTMENT BANKING PLATFORM IN AUSTRALIA.
