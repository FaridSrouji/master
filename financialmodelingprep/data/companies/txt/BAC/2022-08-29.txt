SAINT ELIZABETH UNIVERSITY RECEIVES GRANT TO SUPPORT HISPANIC STUDENTS PURSUING HEALTHCARE CAREERS. PROGRESANDO INITIATIVE PROMOTES THE ECONOMIC AND SOCIAL MOBILITY OF HISPANIC ADULTS MORRISTOWN, N.J. , AUG. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SAINT ELIZABETH UNIVERSITY (SEU) WAS AWARDED A TWO-YEAR $200,000 GRANT FROM BANK OF AMERICA TO SUPPORT HISPANIC-LATINO STUDENTS PURSUING CAREERS IN HEALTHCARE THROUGH ITS PROGRESANDO INITIATIVE.
