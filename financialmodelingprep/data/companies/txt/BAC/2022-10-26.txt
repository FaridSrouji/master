UNIVERSITY OF CENTRAL FLORIDA RECEIVES GRANT TO INCREASE HISPANIC/LATINA/O/X HEALTH CARE GRADUATES, DECREASE GAP IN REPRESENTATION. PROGRESANDO INITIATIVE EMPOWERS HISPANIC/LATINA/O/X ADULTS TO ACHIEVE ECONOMIC AND SOCIAL MOBILITY THROUGH HIGH-IMPACT CAREERS ORLANDO, FLA. , OCT. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNIVERSITY OF CENTRAL FLORIDA (UCF) HAS BEEN AWARDED A TWO-YEAR $250,000 GRANT FROM BANK OF AMERICA TO SUPPORT HISPANIC/LATINA/O/X STUDENTS PURSUING CAREERS IN HEALTH CARE THROUGH ITS PROGRESANDO INITIATIVE.
