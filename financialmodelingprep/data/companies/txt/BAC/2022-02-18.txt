WING LUKE MUSEUM ADVANCES COMMUNITY REVITALIZATION EFFORTS WITH BANK OF AMERICA GRANT. SEATTLE, FEB. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS CELEBRATIONS FOR THE LUNAR NEW YEAR 2022 CONTINUE, THE WING LUKE MUSEUM OF THE ASIAN PACIFIC AMERICAN EXPERIENCE IS ANNOUNCING NEW SUPPORT FROM BANK OF AMERICA THROUGH A $1 MILLION GRANT TOWARDS THE ORGANIZATION'S WORK TO ENRICH THE RESIDENTIAL AND BUSINESS COMMUNITIES IN SEATTLE'S CHINATOWN-INTERNATIONAL DISTRICT (CID). FUNDING FROM THE BANK WILL SUPPORT FORTHCOMING CAPITAL BUILDING RENOVATIONS, SAFETY UPGRADES, AND TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM ENHANCEMENTS AT THE MUSEUM.
