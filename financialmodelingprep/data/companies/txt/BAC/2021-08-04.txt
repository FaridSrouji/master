BOFA TAKES STRESS OUT OF BUSINESS TRAVEL WITH EXECUTIVE EXPLORER CREDIT CARD AND NEW CARDHOLDER APP. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BANK OF AMERICA HAS LAUNCHED A NEW COMMERCIAL CREDIT CARD THAT INCLUDES PREMIUM BENEFITS FOR FREQUENT BUSINESS TRAVELERS AND EXECUTIVES CALLED EXECUTIVE EXPLORER1. THE CARD PROGRAM OFFERS GENEROUS STATEMENT CREDITS FOR TRAVEL PERKS AND ENHANCED INSURANCE COVERAGE2 THAT CAN HELP MAKE TRAVELING MORE ENJOYABLE.
