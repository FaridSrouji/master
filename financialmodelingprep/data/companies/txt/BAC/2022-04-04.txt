BANK OF AMERICA MOBILIZED AND DEPLOYED $250 BILLION IN SUSTAINABLE FINANCE CAPITAL IN 2021. COMPANY IS ACCELERATING ITS CAPITAL MOBILIZATION AND DEPLOYMENT AS PART OF ITS $1.5 TRILLION BY 2030 SUSTAINABLE FINANCE GOAL THAT IS COMMITTED TO ENVIRONMENTAL TRANSITION AND INCLUSIVE SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT  CHARLOTTE, N.C. , APRIL 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT MOBILIZED AND DEPLOYED APPROXIMATELY $250 BILLION IN SUSTAINABLE FINANCE ACTIVITY FOR 2021, AN ALL-TIME HIGH AND A SIGNIFICANT INCREASE OVER 2020 LEVELS.
