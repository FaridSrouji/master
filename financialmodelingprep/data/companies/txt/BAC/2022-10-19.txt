BANK OF AMERICA DECLARES FOURTH-QUARTER 2022 STOCK DIVIDENDS. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , OCT. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA CORPORATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON BANK OF AMERICA COMMON STOCK OF $0.22 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 30, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 2, 2022.
