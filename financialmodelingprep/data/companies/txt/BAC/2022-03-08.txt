BANK OF AMERICA CONSUMER CLIENTS MAKE $294 BILLION IN PAYMENTS IN FEBRUARY, UP 16% YEAR-OVER-YEAR. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MARCH 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED THEIR APPROXIMATELY 67 MILLION CLIENTS MADE $294 BILLION IN TOTAL PAYMENTS DURING FEBRUARY, A 16% INCREASE OVER FEBRUARY 2021.
