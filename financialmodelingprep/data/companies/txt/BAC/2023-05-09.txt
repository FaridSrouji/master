BOFA FINDS YOUNGER HOUSE-HUNTERS UNDETERRED BY WHAT MANY BELIEVE IS A SELLER'S MARKET. WHETHER BIDDING, BUYING, OR JUST LOOKING, HOMEBUYERS SHOW A BIAS TOWARD ACTION CHARLOTTE, N.C. , MAY 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MANY HOPEFUL HOMEBUYERS – ESPECIALLY THOSE IN THEIR 40S AND YOUNGER – ARE FORGING AHEAD WITH PLANS TO BUY HOMES DESPITE BELIEVING THE MARKET FAVORS SELLERS, ACCORDING TO BANK OF AMERICA'S 2023 HOMEBUYER INSIGHTS REPORT.
