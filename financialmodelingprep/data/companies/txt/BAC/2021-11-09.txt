BANK OF AMERICA NAMES AL MCRAE PRESIDENT OF ATLANTA. ATLANTA, NOV. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA HAS NAMED AL MCRAE AS PRESIDENT OF BANK OF AMERICA ATLANTA. MCRAE SUCCEEDS WENDY STEWART, WHO WAS RECENTLY NAMED A MEMBER OF THE COMPANY'S SENIOR MANAGEMENT TEAM AND THE PRESIDENT OF GLOBAL COMMERCIAL BANKING.
