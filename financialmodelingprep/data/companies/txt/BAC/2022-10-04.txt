BANK OF AMERICA DOUBLES MDI DEPOSITS WITH ADDITIONAL $100 MILLION TO SUPPORT MINORITY SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS, COMMUNITIES. NEW YORK , OCT. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED AN ADDITIONAL $100 MILLION IN LOW-COST DEPOSITS TO MINORITY DEPOSITORY INSTITUTIONS (MDIS), WHICH WILL FACILITATE LENDING, HOUSING, NEIGHBORHOOD REVITALIZATION AND OTHER BANKING SERVICES IN MINORITY AND LOW- TO MODERATE-INCOME COMMUNITIES. THIS PROGRAM WILL DOUBLE BANK OF AMERICA'S EXISTING $100 MILLION IN DEPOSITS IN MDIS, PART OF THE COMPANY'S INDUSTRY LEADING $2 BILLION PORTFOLIO OF LOANS, DEPOSITS AND INVESTMENTS IN COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS (CDFIS) AND MDIS.
