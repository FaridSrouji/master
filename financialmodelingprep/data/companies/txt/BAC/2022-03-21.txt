VIRGINIA UNION UNIVERSITY RECEIVES $1M GRANT FROM BANK OF AMERICA. FUNDS WILL BE USED TO PREPARE STUDENTS FOR CAREERS IN THE FINANCIAL INDUSTRY RICHMOND, VA., MARCH 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VIRGINIA UNION UNIVERSITY  (VUU) HAS ANNOUNCED IT IS THE RECIPIENT OF A $1 MILLION GRANT FROM BANK OF AMERICA FOR ITS INNOVATIVE APPROACH TO PREPARING STUDENTS FOR CAREERS IN THE FINANCIAL INDUSTRY.
