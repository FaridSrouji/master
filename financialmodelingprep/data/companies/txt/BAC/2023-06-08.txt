BANK OF AMERICA ANNOUNCES 2024 FINANCIAL REPORTING DATES. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , JUNE 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FOLLOWING REPORTING DATES FOR QUARTERLY FINANCIAL RESULTS: FOURTH QUARTER 2023 – FRIDAY, JANUARY 12, 2024 FIRST QUARTER 2024 – TUESDAY, APRIL 16, 2024 SECOND QUARTER 2024 – TUESDAY, JULY 16, 2024 THIRD QUARTER 2024 – TUESDAY, OCTOBER 15, 2024 THE RESULTS WILL BE RELEASED AT APPROXIMATELY 6:45 A.M.
