BOFA PROVIDES NEARLY $19 MILLION TO LOCAL HUNGER ORGANIZATIONS FROM EMPLOYEE BOOSTER CAMPAIGN. MORE THAN $150M DISTRIBUTED TO HELP FIGHT FOOD INSECURITY SINCE 2015 NEW YORK , DEC. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BANK OF AMERICA ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WILL DONATE AN ADDITIONAL $8 MILLION TO LOCAL HUNGER ORGANIZATIONS FROM ITS SECOND EMPLOYEE BOOSTER CAMPAIGN.  AS A RESULT, NEARLY $19 MILLION WILL BE DISTRIBUTED TO HUNGER RELIEF ORGANIZATIONS IN LOCAL COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE U.S. AND PUERTO RICO.
