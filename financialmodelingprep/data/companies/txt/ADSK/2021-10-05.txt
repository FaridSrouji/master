AUTODESK PRICES FIRST SUSTAINABILITY BOND OFFERING. SAN FRANCISCO, OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS FIRST SUSTAINABILITY  BOND OFFERING OF AN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $1 BILLION IN 2.4% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031. THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON OCTOBER 7, 2021, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
POWERFUL NEW ADVANCEMENTS STRENGTHEN AUTODESK CONSTRUCTION CLOUD. SAN FRANCISCO, CALIF., OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTODESK UNIVERSITY – AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) HAS ANNOUNCED POWERFUL NEW CAPABILITIES ACROSS AUTODESK CONSTRUCTION CLOUD AND AN EXPANSION OF ITS PARTNER ECOSYSTEM, DELIVERING NEW WAYS FOR CONSTRUCTION TEAMS TO CONNECT THEIR WORKFLOWS ON ONE PLATFORM.
