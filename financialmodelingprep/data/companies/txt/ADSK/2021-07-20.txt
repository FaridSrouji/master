AUTODESK TERMINATES ACQUISITION DISCUSSIONS WITH ALTIUM. SAN FRANCISCO, JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS TERMINATED DISCUSSIONS TO ACQUIRE ALTIUM LIMITED (ASX: ALU). "AUTODESK HAS A LONG TRACK RECORD OF DISCIPLINED STRATEGIC ACQUISITIONS," SAID ANDREW ANAGNOST, CEO OF AUTODESK.
