NEW AUTODESK FUSION 360 APP FROM SNAPEDA MAKES PCB PART LIBRARIES A SNAP. SAN FRANCISCO, CA / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 11, 2021 / TODAY, SNAPEDA, CREATOR OF THE FIRST SEARCH ENGINE FOR ELECTRONICS DESIGN, IS RELEASING THE SNAPEDA FOR AUTODESK ® FUSION 360 ® APP IN COLLABORATION WITH AUTODESK (NASDAQ:ADSK). THIS NEW APP INTEGRATES SNAPEDA'S DATABASE DIRECTLY WITHIN FUSION 360, ENABLING ELECTRONICS ENGINEERS AND DESIGNERS TO SEARCH AND PLACE MILLIONS OF ELECTRONIC COMPONENT MODELS - INCLUDING SYMBOLS, FOOTPRINTS, AND 3D MODELS - DIRECTLY WITHIN THE FUSION 360 ENVIRONMENT.
XOMETRY LAUNCHES AUTODESK FUSION 360 APP. ROCKVILLE, MD., MARCH 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- XOMETRY, THE LARGEST U.S. MARKETPLACE FOR ON DEMAND MANUFACTURING, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE LAUNCH OF AN APP FOR AUTODESK ®  FUSION 360 ® IN COLLABORATION WITH AUTODESK (NASDAQ: ADSK).
