AUTODESK ANNOUNCES EXECUTIVE CHANGE. SAN RAFAEL, CALIF., NOV. 12, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE DEPARTURE OF SCOTT HERREN, THE COMPANY'S SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. AFTER MORE THAN SIX YEARS AT AUTODESK, HERREN IS LEAVING THE DESIGN AND ENGINEERING SOFTWARE...
