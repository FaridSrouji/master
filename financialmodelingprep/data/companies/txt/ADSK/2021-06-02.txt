AUTODESK CONSTRUCTION CLOUD GLOBAL FOCUS EXPANDS WITH WORKFLOWS THAT SUPPORT ISO 19650. SAN FRANCISCO, JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RELEASED A NEW SERIES OF WORKFLOWS FOR AUTODESK CONSTRUCTION CLOUD THAT CAN HELP CUSTOMERS COMPLY WITH ISO 19650, A GLOBAL DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION INFORMATION MANAGEMENT STANDARD. THESE NEW WORKFLOWS OFFER A ROBUST SET OF TOOLS TO DEFINE A STRUCTURED AND STANDARDIZED PROCESS FOR PUBLISHING, SHARING AND STORING PROJECT INFORMATION.
