NETDRAGON REACHES STRATEGIC COOPERATION WITH AUTODESK (CHINA) TO EXPLORE NEW PATH OF DIGITAL EDUCATION. HONG KONG, JULY 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NETDRAGON WEBSOFT HOLDINGS LIMITED ("NETDRAGON" OR THE "COMPANY"; HONG KONG STOCK CODE: 777), A GLOBAL LEADER IN BUILDING INTERNET COMMUNITIES, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT THE COMPANY HAS SIGNED A STRATEGIC MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING ("MOU") WITH AUTODESK SOFTWARE (CHINA) LIMITED ("AUTODESK (CHINA)") IN THE DIGITAL EDUCATION TOWN IN CHANGLE, FUZHOU. BOTH PARTIES INTEND TO ESTABLISH A LONG-TERM COMMUNICATION MECHANISM THROUGH UTILIZING THEIR CORE TECHNOLOGY ADVANTAGES, AND WORK TOGETHER TO PROMOTE THE APPLICATION OF DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY IN EDUCATION.
