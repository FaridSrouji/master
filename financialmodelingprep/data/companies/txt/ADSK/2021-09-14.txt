(NASDAQ: ADSK) AND MANAGEMENT CONSULTING FIRM FMI CORPORATION TODAY RELEASED THE RESULTS FROM A NEW STUDY, "HARNESSING THE DATA ADVANTAGE IN CONSTRUCTION," WHICH SURVEYED OVER 3,900 PROFESSIONALS ACROSS THE GLOBAL CONSTRUCTION INDUSTRY ON THEIR DATA PRACTICES IN 2020.
