OVER 350,000 PROJECTS TURN TO AUTODESK CONSTRUCTION CLOUD FOR PRECONSTRUCTION WORKFLOWS. SAN FRANCISCO, SEPT. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MORE THAN 350,000 PROJECTS ACROSS THE GLOBE ARE USING AUTODESK CONSTRUCTION CLOUD TO POWER MORE EFFECTIVE PRECONSTRUCTION WORKFLOWS, INCLUDING DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT, BID MANAGEMENT, QUANTIFICATION, MODEL COORDINATION AND DESIGN COLLABORATION.
