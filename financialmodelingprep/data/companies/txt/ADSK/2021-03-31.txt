AUTODESK COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF INNOVYZE, PROVIDER OF SMART WATER INFRASTRUCTURE MODELING, SIMULATION, AND PREDICTIVE TECHNOLOGIES. SAN FRANCISCO, MARCH 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) HAS ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF ITS ACQUISITION OF INNOVYZE, INC. FOR $1 BILLION NET OF CASH SUBJECT TO WORKING CAPITAL AND TAX CLOSING ADJUSTMENTS. THE ACQUISITION POSITIONS AUTODESK AS A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF END-TO-END DIGITAL SOLUTIONS FROM DESIGN TO OPERATIONS OF WATER INFRASTRUCTURE, ACCELERATES AUTODESK'S DIGITAL TWIN STRATEGY, AND CREATES A CLEARER PATH TO A MORE SUSTAINABLE AND DIGITIZED WATER INDUSTRY.
