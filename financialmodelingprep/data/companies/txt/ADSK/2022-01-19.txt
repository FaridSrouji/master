AUTODESK ACQUIRES MOXION CLOUD SOLUTION FOR DIGITAL DAILIES. SAN FRANCISCO, JAN. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY AUTODESK (NASDAQ: ADSK) ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF MOXION, THE NEW ZEALAND-BASED DEVELOPER OF A POWERFUL, CLOUD-BASED PLATFORM FOR DIGITAL DAILIES USED BY LEADING FILMMAKERS ON SOME OF THE WORLD'S MOST COMPLEX AND CHALLENGING PRODUCTIONS, INCLUDING "THE MIDNIGHT SKY," "THE MARVELOUS MRS. MAISEL," AND "THE MATRIX RESURRECTIONS.
