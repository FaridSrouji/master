AUTODESK APPOINTS SEBASTIAN GOODWIN AS CHIEF TRUST OFFICER. SAN FRANCISCO , MARCH 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF SEBASTIAN GOODWIN AS CHIEF TRUST OFFICER, STARTING MARCH 27, 2023. AS CHIEF TRUST OFFICER, GOODWIN WILL OVERSEE THE FULFILLMENT OF AUTODESK'S COMMITMENTS TO ITS CUSTOMERS AND STAKEHOLDERS ON SECURITY, PRIVACY, AND DATA ETHICS.
