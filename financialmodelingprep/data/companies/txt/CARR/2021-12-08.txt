CARRIER BOARD OF DIRECTORS INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 25%. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA., DEC. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE:CARR), THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.15 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF CARRIER COMMON STOCK, WHICH REPRESENTS A 25% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
