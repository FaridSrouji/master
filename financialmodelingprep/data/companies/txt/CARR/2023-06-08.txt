CARRIER BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA. , JUNE 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE:CARR), GLOBAL LEADER IN INTELLIGENT CLIMATE AND ENERGY SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.185 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF CARRIER COMMON STOCK.
