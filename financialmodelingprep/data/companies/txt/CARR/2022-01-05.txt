AUTOMATED LOGIC ACQUIRES INTEGRATED CONTROL SYSTEMS INC. KENNESAW, GA., JAN. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTOMATED LOGIC CONTRACTING SERVICES INC (AUTOMATED LOGIC), A LEADING PROVIDER OF INNOVATIVE BUILDING-MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS, HAS ACQUIRED THE BUSINESS OF INTEGRATED CONTROL SYSTEMS INC (ICSI), AN INDEPENDENT AUTOMATED LOGIC CONTROLS CONTRACTOR WITH OFFICES IN ARIZONA, COLORADO AND NEW MEXICO.
