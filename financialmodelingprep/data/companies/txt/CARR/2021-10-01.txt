ON NATIONAL FIRE PUP DAY, KIDDE ANNOUNCES ITS FIRST FIRE SERVICE DOG OF THE YEAR. MEBANE, N.C., OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR CENTURIES, FIREHOUSE DOGS HAVE BEEN LOYAL COMPANIONS — ASSISTING FIREFIGHTERS IN LIFESAVING RESCUES, PROVIDING CAMARADERIE IN FIREHOUSES AND BRINGING SMILES TO HEROIC FIRST RESPONDERS.
