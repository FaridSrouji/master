CALIFORNIA ENERGY COMMISSION SELECTS NORESCO TO DEVELOP NEW ENERGY EFFICIENCY STANDARDS IN SUPPORT OF STATE'S DECARBONIZATION GOALS. OAKLAND, CALIF. , JULY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORESCO, A PART OF CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE: CARR), IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE IT HAS BEEN AWARDED A NEW, THREE-YEAR CONTRACT TO HELP THE CALIFORNIA ENERGY COMMISSION (CEC) DEVELOP AND ADOPT THE STATE'S BUILDING ENERGY CODE FOR 2025.
