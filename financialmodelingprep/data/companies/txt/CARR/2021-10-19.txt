CARRIER ACQUIRES CAVIUS, AN INNOVATIVE RESIDENTIAL ALARM COMPANY. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA., OCT. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE:CARR) THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, HAS ACQUIRED CAVIUS, AN INNOVATIVE DANISH RESIDENTIAL ALARM COMPANY THAT PROVIDES A COMPLETE RANGE OF SMOKE, HEAT, FLOOD AND CARBON MONOXIDE ALARMS.
