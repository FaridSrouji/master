PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA. , FEB. 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE: CARR), THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO REDUCE ITS GREENHOUSE GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS IN LINE WITH THE SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE (SBTI) TO LIMIT GLOBAL WARMING TO 1.5⁰ C.
