CARRIER'S INDIANAPOLIS SITE ACHIEVES ZERO WASTE TO LANDFILL CERTIFICATION. INDIANAPOLIS, DEC. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS INDIANAPOLIS MANUFACTURING SITE, HOME TO ITS RESIDENTIAL GAS FURNACE PLANT, HAS ACHIEVED ZERO WASTE TO LANDFILL CERTIFICATION, WHICH MEANS WASTE WILL BE CONVERTED TO ENERGY AND OTHER USES. WITH THIS MILESTONE, THE INDIANAPOLIS FACILITY BECOMES THE FIRST SITE OF PARENT COMPANY CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE: CARR), THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, TO ACHIEVE THIS DESIGNATION.
