CARRIER ADVANCES DIVERSITY & INCLUSION STRATEGY WITH SIGNING OF HISPANIC PROMISE PLEDGE. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA., SEPT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN CELEBRATION OF HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH AND IN SUPPORT OF ITS COMMITMENT TO CREATE A MORE DIVERSE AND INCLUSIVE WORKPLACE, CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE:CARR) THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, SIGNED A NATIONAL PLEDGE TO HIRE, RETAIN AND DEVELOP HISPANIC EMPLOYEES.
