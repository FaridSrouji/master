ADRF LAUNCHES NEW LONG RANGE ANNUNCIATOR FOR ITS PUBLIC SAFETY REPEATERS AT IWCE. BURBANK, CALIF., SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ADVANCED RF TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (ADRF), THE LARGEST PURE-PLAY IN-BUILDING DISTRIBUTED ANTENNA SYSTEM (DAS) PROVIDER FOR PUBLIC SAFETY AND COMMERCIAL RADIO FREQUENCIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW LONG RANGE ANNUNCIATOR, ANN-L, FOR ITS PUBLIC SAFETY REPEATERS AT THIS YEAR'S INTERNATIONAL WIRELESS COMMUNICATIONS EXPO (IWCE).
