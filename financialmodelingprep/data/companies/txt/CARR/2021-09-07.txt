CARRIER FINALIZES ACQUISITION OF GUANGDONG GIWEE GROUP, CHINA-BASED HVAC MANUFACTURER. CHARLOTTE, N.C., SEPT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF THE REMAINDER OF GUANGDONG GIWEE GROUP (GIWEE GROUP) AND ITS SUBSIDIARIES, INCLUDING GUANGDONG CHIGO HEATING & VENTILATION EQUIPMENT CO., LTD.
