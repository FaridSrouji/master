CARRIER HEALTHY BUILDINGS AND HOMES SOLUTIONS HELP PROTECT INDOOR AIR QUALITY FROM WILDFIRE SMOKE. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA. , JUNE 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF ITS COMMITMENT TO HEALTHY BUILDINGS AND HEALTHY HOMES, CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE:CARR), GLOBAL LEADER IN INTELLIGENT CLIMATE AND ENERGY SOLUTIONS, OFFERS A SUITE OF SOLUTIONS TO HELP KEEP PEOPLE SAFE FROM POOR OUTDOOR AIR QUALITY IN THE WAKE OF THE CANADIAN WILDFIRES.
