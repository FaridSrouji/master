CARRIER RANKS NO. 9 ON BARRON'S 100 MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES LIST. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA., FEB. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE: CARR), THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, HAS RANKED NINTH ON BARRON'S 2022 LIST OF THE 100 MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES IN THE U.S. "OUR MISSION AT CARRIER IS TO CREATE SOLUTIONS THAT MATTER FOR PEOPLE AND OUR PLANET, AND WE ARE VERY PROUD TO BE NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES," SAID CARRIER CHAIRMAN & CEO DAVE GITLIN.
