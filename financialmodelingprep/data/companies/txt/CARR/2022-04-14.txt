CARRIER BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA. , APRIL 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE:CARR), THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.15 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF CARRIER COMMON STOCK.
