CARRIER LAUNCHES CORPORATE VENTURE CAPITAL GROUP TO ACCELERATE SUSTAINABLE INNOVATION AND DISRUPTIVE BUILDING & COLD CHAIN TECHNOLOGY. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA., FEB. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE: CARR), THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, HAS CREATED CARRIER VENTURES, A NEW GLOBAL VENTURE CAPITAL GROUP FOCUSED ON INVESTMENTS TO ACCELERATE THE DEVELOPMENT OF SUSTAINABLE INNOVATIONS AND DISRUPTIVE TECHNOLOGIES ENABLING THE FUTURE OF BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN MANAGEMENT.
