CARRIER ADVANCES COLD CHAIN DEVELOPMENT ACROSS AFRICA THROUGH COLLABORATIONS WITH LEADING INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA. , NOV. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE: CARR), THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, RECENTLY FORGED INNOVATIVE AGREEMENTS WITH THE UNITED NATIONS WORLD FOOD PROGRAMME (WFP) AND THE AFRICAN CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE FOR SUSTAINABLE COOLING AND COLD-CHAIN (ACES) TO HELP ADVANCE COLD CHAIN DEVELOPMENT AND TRAINING IN AFRICA.
