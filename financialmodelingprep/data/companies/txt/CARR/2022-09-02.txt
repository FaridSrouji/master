RUSSELL WILSON TO PARTNER WITH CARRIER TO INTRODUCE SOLUTIONS FOR HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE INDOOR ENVIRONMENTS. NEW PARTNERSHIP WILL FOCUS ON CARRIER'S ABOUND VENTURES™, CO-FOUNDED BY WILSON, AND LINE OF AIR PURIFIERS TO SUSTAINABLY IMPROVE INDOOR AIR QUALITY PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA. , SEPT. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NFL SUPER BOWL-WINNING QUARTERBACK RUSSELL WILSON IS PARTNERING WITH CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE:CARR), THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, TO HELP LAUNCH SOLUTIONS CRITICAL TO HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS AND HOMES.
