LINK LOGISTICS AND CARRIER COLLABORATE TO DRIVE VALUE THROUGH ENERGY EFFICIENCY. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINK LOGISTICS (“LINK”), A LEADING NATIONAL LAST-MILE LOGISTICS REAL ESTATE COMPANY DESIGNED TO MEET THE NEEDS OF THE MODERN SUPPLY CHAIN, AND CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE: CARR), THE LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HEALTHY, SAFE, SUSTAINABLE AND INTELLIGENT BUILDING AND COLD CHAIN SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION TO DELIVER ENERGY MANAGEMENT AND ANALYTICS ACROSS LINK'S ENTERPRISE PORTFOLIO.
