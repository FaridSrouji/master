HORSESHOE HAMMOND REMAINS PART OF CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT. HAMMOND, IND., JUNE 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, THE INDIANA GAMING COMMISSION (IGC) AMENDED ITS ORDER REQUIRING CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC (NASDAQ: CZR) ("CAESARS" OR "THE COMPANY") TO SELL A THIRD CASINO ASSET IN THE STATE.
