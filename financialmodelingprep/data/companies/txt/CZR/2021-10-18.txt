BOBBY'S BURGERS BY BOBBY FLAY EXPANDS TO TWO NEW LAS VEGAS LOCATIONS. LAS VEGAS, OCT. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ACCLAIMED CHEF BOBBY FLAY AND CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO EXPAND FLAY'S FAST-CASUAL BRAND, BOBBY'S BURGERS, TO TWO NEW LOCATIONS ON THE LAS VEGAS STRIP AT HARRAH'S LAS VEGAS AND PARIS LAS VEGAS. BOBBY'S BURGERS IS INSPIRED BY FLAY'S LOVE OF THE GRILL AND THE TRADITIONAL BURGER, FRIES AND SHAKE COMBO.
