CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT AND SL GREEN ANNOUNCE EXPANSION OF CAESARS REWARDS® PARTNERSHIP TO NEW YORK BUSINESSES AS PART OF TIMES SQUARE GAMING LICENSE BID. NEW YORK , FEB. 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT ("CAESARS") AND SL GREEN REALTY CORP ("SL GREEN") TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW CAESARS REWARDS® PARTNERSHIP FOR LOCAL MERCHANTS IN GREATER NEW YORK CITY AS A PART OF THEIR PURSUIT OF A GAMING LICENSE IN TIMES SQUARE. WHEN CAESARS PALACE TIMES SQUARE OPENS IN MANHATTAN, CAESARS REWARDS MEMBERS WILL BE ABLE TO USE THEIR REWARD CREDITS BY CONVERTING THEM TO GIFT CARDS, VALID ACROSS THE CAESARS REWARDS GIFT CARD NETWORK, INCLUDING PARTICIPATING HOTELS, RESTAURANTS, RETAILERS, COMEDY CLUBS, ENTERTAINMENT VENUES AND MORE ACROSS NEW YORK.
