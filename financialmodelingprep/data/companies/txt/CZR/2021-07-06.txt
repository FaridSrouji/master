CAESARS PALACE UNVEILS PLANS FOR MULTIMILLION-DOLLAR MAIN ENTRANCE RENOVATION. LAS VEGAS, JULY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALREADY ONE OF THE MOST ICONIC RESORTS IN THE WORLD, CAESARS PALACE IS NOT RESTING ON ITS LAURELS. INSTEAD, IT EMBARKS ON A MULTIMILLION-DOLLAR RENOVATION OF THE ORIGINAL MAIN ENTRANCE, MAIN CASINO AND PORTE-COCHÈRE TO CREATE AN EXTRAVAGANT ENTRYWAY, REDESIGNED GAMING AREAS AND A NEW LOBBY BAR.
