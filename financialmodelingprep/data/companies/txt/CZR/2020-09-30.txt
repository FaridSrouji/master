CAESARS ANNOUNCES RECOMMENDED CASH ACQUISITION OF WILLIAM HILL. CAESARS ENTRTNMNT WILLIAM HILL PLC  - RECOMMENDED CASH ACQUISITION OF WILLIAM HILL PLC.WILLIAM HILL - CO, CAESARS AND CAESARS UK BIDCO ARE PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT THEY HAVE REACHED AGREEMENT ON TERMS OF A RECOMMENDED CASH ACQUISITION.WILLIAM HILL - ACQUISITION VALUES ENTIRE ISSUED AND TO BE ISSUED SHARE CAPITAL OF WILLIAM HILL AT APPROXIMATELY £2.9 BILLION..WILLIAM HILL - INTEND TO RECOMMEND UNANIMOUSLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY THAT WILLIAM HILL SHAREHOLDERS VOTE IN FAVOUR OF SCHEME AT COURT MEETING.WILLIAM HILL - CAESARS INTENDS TO INTEGRATE WHOLE OF WILLIAM HILL'S US BUSINESS INTO CAESARS GROUP WITH MINIMAL, IF ANY, IMPACT ON EMPLOYEE HEADCOUNT.WILLIAM HILL - INTENDED THAT, UPON COMPLETION OF ACQUISITION, EACH OF NON-EXECUTIVE MEMBERS OF WILLIAM HILL BOARD SHALL RESIGN FROM THEIR OFFICE.WILLIAM HILL - ACQUISITION IS NOT EXPECTED TO NOT HAVE ANY IMPACT ON EXISTING BUSINESS AND EMPLOYEES OF CAESARS.
