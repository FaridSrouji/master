COLUMBUS EXPOSITION AND RACING CONTRACTS WITH CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT TO DEVELOP CASINO AND RACETRACK IN NEBRASKA. COLUMBUS, NEB., JULY 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COLUMBUS EXPOSITION AND RACING (CER) AND CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. (NASDAQ: CZR) TODAY ANNOUNCED CER'S SELECTION OF CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT TO BUILD AND OPERATE A HARRAH'S CASINO AND RACETRACK IN COLUMBUS, NEBRASKA.
