WORLD SERIES OF POKER ANNOUNCES 2022 DAILY EVENT SCHEDULE. LAS VEGAS, FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE 53RD WORLD SERIES OF POKER® (WSOP®) – THE RICHEST, MOST PRESTIGIOUS AND LONGEST-RUNNING POKER SERIES – TODAY ANNOUNCED THE DAILY EVENT SCHEDULE FOR 2022. FOLLOWING A SUCCESSFUL 17-YEAR RUN AT THE RIO ALL-SUITE HOTEL & CASINO, THE WSOP MOVES TO THE LAS VEGAS STRIP FOR THE VERY FIRST TIME AT BALLY'S, THE FUTURE HORSESHOE, AND PARIS LAS VEGAS HOTEL & CASINO, AND WILL FEATURE THE WSOP'S INAUGURAL MASTER OF CEREMONIES, VINCE VAUGHN.
