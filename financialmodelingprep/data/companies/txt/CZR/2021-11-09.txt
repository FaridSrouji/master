CAESARS SPORTSBOOK ADDS THE MANNINGS TO THE EMPIRE. LAS VEGAS, NOV. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAESARS SPORTSBOOK TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF THE MANNINGS IN A HISTORIC FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN A SPORTS BETTING COMPANY AND AN ICONIC SPORTS FAMILY. PART OF CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. (NASDAQ: CZR) ("CAESARS"), CAESARS SPORTSBOOK WELCOMES ARCHIE, PEYTON, ELI, AND COOPER MANNING TO JOIN THE EMPIRE AS CAESARS SPORTSBOOK EXPANDS ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
