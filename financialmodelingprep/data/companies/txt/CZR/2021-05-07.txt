BACCHANAL BUFFET TO REOPEN AT CAESARS PALACE ON MAY 20. LAS VEGAS, MAY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BACCHANAL BUFFET AT CAESARS PALACE REDEFINED THE BUFFET EXPERIENCE WHEN IT OPENED IN 2012. NOW, FOLLOWING MORE THAN A YEAR OF RENOVATION, THE AWARD-WINNING BUFFET WILL REOPEN ON THURSDAY, MAY 20, SHOWCASING A MULTIMILLION-DOLLAR ENHANCEMENT.
