FIESTA BOWL AND CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT CREATE BOWL SEASON'S FIRST-EVER EXCLUSIVE SPORTS BETTING AND FANTASY GAMING PARTNERSHIP. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ. AND LAS VEGAS, AUG. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE FIESTA BOWL ORGANIZATION TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS JOINED FORCES WITH CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. (NASDAQ: CZR) ("CAESARS") IN A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND SPORTS BETTING AND FANTASY GAMING PARTNERSHIP FOR A COLLEGE FOOTBALL BOWL GAME.
