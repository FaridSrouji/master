CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. COMPLETES PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SALE OF TROPICANA EVANSVILLE TO GAMING & LEISURE PROPERTIES AND BALLY'S CORPORATION. LAS VEGAS AND RENO, NEV., JUNE 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. (NASDAQ: CZR) ("CAESARS") TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CLOSING OF THE SALE OF TROPICANA EVANSVILLE TO GAMING & LEISURE PROPERTIES AND BALLY'S CORPORATION FOR AGGREGATE CONSIDERATION OF $480 MILLION IN CASH, SUBJECT TO A CUSTOMARY WORKING CAPITAL ADJUSTMENT.
