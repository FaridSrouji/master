2021 WORLD SERIES OF POKER DAILY EVENT SCHEDULE FINALIZED. LAS VEGAS, JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE WORLD SERIES OF POKER® (WSOP®) – THE RICHEST, MOST PRESTIGIOUS AND LONGEST-RUNNING POKER SERIES – HAS FINALIZED ITS DAILY EVENT SCHEDULE FOR 2021. DOORS FOR THE EVENT OPEN ON THURSDAY, SEPT.
