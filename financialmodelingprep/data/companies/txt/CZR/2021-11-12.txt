CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT UNVEILS REIMAGINED TEAM MEMBER WELLNESS CENTER OPERATED BY PREMISE HEALTH ON THE LAS VEGAS STRIP. BRENTWOOD, TENN. AND LAS VEGAS, NOV. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, THE LARGEST CASINO-ENTERTAINMENT COMPANY IN THE U.S., AND PREMISE HEALTH, THE LARGEST DIRECT HEALTH CARE PROVIDER AND AN OMERS PORTFOLIO COMPANY, HAS OPENED A NEW WELLNESS CENTER ON THE LAS VEGAS STRIP, DELIVERING IN-PERSON AND DIGITAL CARE TO ALL ELIGIBLE CAESARS TEAM MEMBERS.
