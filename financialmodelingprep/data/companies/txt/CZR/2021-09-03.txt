CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. COMPLETES PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SALE OF CAESARS SOUTHERN INDIANA OPERATIONS TO THE EASTERN BAND OF CHEROKEE INDIANS. LAS VEGAS AND RENO, NEV., SEPT. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. (NASDAQ: CZR) ("CAESARS") TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CLOSING OF THE SALE OF THE OPERATIONS OF CAESARS SOUTHERN INDIANA TO THE EASTERN BAND OF CHEROKEE INDIANS ("EBCI") FOR APPROXIMATELY $250 MILLION.
