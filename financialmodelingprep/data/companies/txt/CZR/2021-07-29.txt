LISA VANDERPUMP TO OPEN SECOND LAS VEGAS VENUE, VANDERPUMP À PARIS, AT PARIS LAS VEGAS. LAS VEGAS, JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RESTAURATEUR, TELEVISION STAR AND PHILANTHROPIST LISA VANDERPUMP IS SET TO BRING HER SIGNATURE STUNNING AND INNOVATIVE DESIGN AESTHETIC TO PARIS LAS VEGAS FOR HER SECOND LAS VEGAS VENUE, VANDERPUMP À PARIS. LIKE HER HIGHLY SUCCESSFUL FIRST VEGAS LOCATION, VANDERPUMP COCKTAIL GARDEN AT CAESARS PALACE, VANDERPUMP À PARIS WILL FEATURE DELECTABLE DISHES, WHIMSICAL AND UNIQUE COCKTAILS, AND A LUSH, ELABORATE ATMOSPHERE WITH ECLECTIC, PARISIAN-INSPIRED DESIGN DETAILS.
