CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT'S WORLD SERIES OF POKER® RETURNS FOR ITS 54TH ANNUAL TOURNAMENT MAY 30 - JULY 18. POKER'S MOST POPULAR EVENT CONFIRMS SUMMER 2023 DATES, SPECIAL ROOM RATES AND MORE JUST IN TIME FOR THE HOLIDAYS    "THE MAIN EVENT," POKER'S UNDISPUTED WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP, RETURNS JULY 3-17 LAS VEGAS , DEC. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- POKER PLAYERS AND ENTHUSIASTS CAN OFFICIALLY SAVE THE DATE(S) FOR THE 54TH ANNUAL WORLD SERIES OF POKER® (WSOP®). CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT ("CAESARS") (NASDAQ: CZR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE RICHEST, MOST PRESTIGIOUS, AND LONGEST-RUNNING POKER SERIES WILL RETURN TO THE LAS VEGAS STRIP AT PARIS LAS VEGAS, AND THE SOON-TO-BE HORSESHOE ON MAY 30 – JULY 18, 2023, FOLLOWING A 2022 TOURNAMENT THAT SAW 197,626 ENTRANTS FROM MORE THAN 100 COUNTRIES WITH A RECORD-BREAKING $347.9 MILLION TOTAL PRIZE POOL.
