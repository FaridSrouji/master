NOBU HOTEL CAESARS PALACE BEGINS MULTIMILLION-DOLLAR REFRESH PROJECT. LAS VEGAS, OCT. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS THE WORLD'S FIRST NOBU HOTEL CAESARS PALACE COMMEMORATES ITS EIGHTH ANNIVERSARY, THE LUXURY BOUTIQUE HOTEL WITHIN THE ICONIC LAS VEGAS RESORT EMBARKS ON A MULTIMILLION-DOLLAR REFRESH OF ALL 182 GUEST ROOMS AND PUBLIC AREAS. CONSTRUCTION IS SET TO BE COMPLETE BY THE END OF 2021.
