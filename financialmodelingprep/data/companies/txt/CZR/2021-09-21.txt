CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT AND NOBU HOSPITALITY STRENGTHEN PARTNERSHIP WITH THREE NEW LOCATIONS ACROSS THE US. LAS VEGAS, SEPT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. AND NOBU HOSPITALITY – THE GLOBAL LIFESTYLE BRAND FOUNDED BY NOBU MATSUHISA, ROBERT DE NIRO AND MEIR TEPER – TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF ITS LONGSTANDING PARTNERSHIP WITH TWO NEW NOBU HOTELS AND RESTAURANTS AT HARRAH'S NEW ORLEANS AND CAESARS ATLANTIC CITY, A NOBU RESTAURANT AT PARIS LAS VEGAS AND A REFRESH OF THE FIRST NOBU HOTEL AT CAESARS PALACE LAS VEGAS.
