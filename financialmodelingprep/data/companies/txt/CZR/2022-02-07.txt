LUMIÈRE PLACE CASINO & HOTEL TO BECOME HORSESHOE SAINT LOUIS. SAINT LOUIS, FEB. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEGENDARY CASINO BRAND HORSESHOE IS COMING TO SAINT LOUIS AS LUMIÈRE PLACE CASINO & HOTEL BEGINS A TRANSFORMATION INTO HORSESHOE SAINT LOUIS. THE PROPERTY EVOLUTION IS EXPECTED TO BE FINALIZED IN SPRING 2022.
