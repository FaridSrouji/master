TAVR WITH SAPIEN 3 DEMONSTRATED AS ECONOMICALLY DOMINANT TREATMENT STRATEGY COMPARED TO SURGERY IN PARTNER 3 ANALYSIS. IRVINE, CALIF., NOV. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES (NYSE: EW) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT A COST-EFFECTIVENESS ANALYSIS COMPARING TRANSCATHETER AORTIC VALVE REPLACEMENT (TAVR) TO SURGERY DEMONSTRATED THAT TAVR WITH SAPIEN 3 IS AN ECONOMICALLY DOMINANT TREATMENT STRATEGY, OFFERING IMPROVED OUTCOMES AND REDUCED COST.
