EDWARDS ANNOUNCES ONE-YEAR DATA ON TRANSFEMORAL TRANSCATHETER TRICUSPID VALVE REPLACEMENT. LONDON , NOV. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES CORPORATION (NYSE: EW) ANNOUNCED THAT ONE-YEAR RESULTS ON PATIENTS TREATED IN THE SINGLE-ARM, PROSPECTIVE, GLOBAL, MULTI-CENTER TRISCEND STUDY OF THE COMPANY'S EVOQUE TRANSCATHETER TRICUSPID VALVE REPLACEMENT SYSTEM DEMONSTRATED FAVORABLE SAFETY, EFFICACY AND QUALITY-OF-LIFE OUTCOMES. THE DATA WERE PRESENTED DURING THE LATE-BREAKING TRIALS SESSION AT PCR LONDON VALVES 2022.
