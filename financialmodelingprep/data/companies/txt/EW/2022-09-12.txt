EDWARDS LAUNCHES SAPIEN 3 ULTRA RESILIA VALVE FOLLOWING FDA APPROVAL. IRVINE, CALIF. , SEPT. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES (NYSE: EW) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE SAPIEN 3 ULTRA RESILIA VALVE, WHICH INCORPORATES EDWARDS' BREAKTHROUGH RESILIA TISSUE TECHNOLOGY WITH THE INDUSTRY-LEADING SAPIEN 3 ULTRA TRANSCATHETER AORTIC HEART VALVE.
