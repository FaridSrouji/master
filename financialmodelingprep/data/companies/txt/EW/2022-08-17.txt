EDWARDS PASCAL PRECISION TRANSCATHETER MITRAL AND TRICUSPID VALVE REPAIR SYSTEM RECEIVES CE MARK. IRVINE, CALIF. , AUG. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES CORPORATION (NYSE: EW), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY'S PASCAL PRECISION TRANSCATHETER VALVE REPAIR SYSTEM RECEIVED CE MARK FOR THE TREATMENT OF MITRAL AND TRICUSPID REGURGITATION (MR AND TR).
