EDWARDS ANNOUNCES SIX-MONTH DATA CONFIRMING TEER AS SAFE AND EFFECTIVE FOR DMR IN FIRST HEAD-TO-HEAD TRIAL. BOSTON , SEPT. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES CORPORATION (NYSE: EW) ANNOUNCED RESULTS FROM CLASP IID, THE FIRST RANDOMIZED CONTROLLED TRIAL THAT DIRECTLY COMPARES TWO CONTEMPORARY TRANSCATHETER EDGE-TO-EDGE REPAIR (TEER) THERAPIES.
