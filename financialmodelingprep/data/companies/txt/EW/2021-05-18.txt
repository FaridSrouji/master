CLINICAL DATA UPDATES ON EDWARDS' TRANSCATHETER MITRAL, TRICUSPID PROGRAMS PRESENTED AT EUROPCR. IRVINE, CALIF., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES (NYSE: EW) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CLINICAL RESULTS FROM THE COMPANY'S TRANSCATHETER MITRAL AND TRICUSPID PROGRAMS WERE PRESENTED AS PART OF THE LATE-BREAKING CLINICAL TRIALS AT EUROPCR 2021, DEMONSTRATING POSITIVE OUTCOMES FOR PATIENTS IMPACTED BY MITRAL OR TRICUSPID HEART VALVE DISEASE.
