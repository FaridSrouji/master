EDWARDS RECEIVES CLEARANCE FOR HYPOTENSION PREDICTION INDEX SOFTWARE FOR NONINVASIVE ACUMEN IQ CUFF. IRVINE, CALIF., JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES (NYSE: EW) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ACUMEN HYPOTENSION PREDICTION INDEX (HPI) SOFTWARE WITH THE ACUMEN IQ FINGER CUFF HAS RECEIVED U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION CLEARANCE.
