EDWARDS ANNOUNCES KEY EVENTS FOR EUROPCR 2022. PARIS , MAY 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES CORPORATION (NYSE: EW) ANNOUNCED KEY EVENTS FOR THE COMPANY DURING EUROPCR 2022, THE ANNUAL MEETING OF THE EUROPEAN ASSOCIATION OF PERCUTANEOUS CARDIOVASCULAR INTERVENTIONS (EAPCI). THE EVENT TAKES PLACE MAY 17 TO 20 AT THE PALAIS DES CONGRÈS IN PARIS.
