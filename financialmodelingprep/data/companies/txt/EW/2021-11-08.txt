EDWARDS ANNOUNCES SIX-MONTH DATA FROM TRANSCATHETER TRICUSPID REPLACEMENT PROGRAM. IRVINE, CALIF., NOV. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES CORPORATION (NYSE: EW) ANNOUNCED THAT RESULTS FROM A CLINICAL TRIAL OF THE COMPANY'S EVOQUE TRANSCATHETER TRICUSPID VALVE REPLACEMENT SYSTEM DEMONSTRATED THAT FAVORABLE PATIENT OUTCOMES WERE SUSTAINED AT SIX MONTHS.
