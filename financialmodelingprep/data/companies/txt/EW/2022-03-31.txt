EDWARDS MITRIS RESILIA VALVE RECEIVES FDA APPROVAL FOR MITRAL REPLACEMENT SURGERIES. IRVINE, CALIF. , MARCH 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES (NYSE: EW) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT RECEIVED APPROVAL FROM THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR THE MITRIS RESILIA VALVE, A TISSUE VALVE REPLACEMENT SPECIFICALLY DESIGNED FOR THE HEART'S MITRAL POSITION.
