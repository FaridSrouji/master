MERIT JANOW NOMINATED FOR ELECTION TO APTIV'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. DUBLIN, MARCH 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- APTIV PLC (NYSE: APTV), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY ENABLING THE FUTURE OF MOBILITY, TODAY ANNOUNCED MERIT E. JANOW WILL BE STANDING FOR ELECTION TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS IN CONNECTION WITH APTIV'S ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS, SCHEDULED FOR APRIL 30, 2021.
