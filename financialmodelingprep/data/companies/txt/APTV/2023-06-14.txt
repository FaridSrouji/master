APTIV ANNOUNCES CONVERSION DATE AND CONVERSION RATE FOR SERIES A MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED SHARES. DUBLIN , JUNE 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- APTIV PLC (NYSE: APTV) (THE "COMPANY")  A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY FOCUSED ON MAKING MOBILITY SAFER, GREENER AND MORE CONNECTED, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS 5.50% SERIES A MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED SHARES (THE "PREFERRED SHARES"), WILL AUTOMATICALLY CONVERT INTO ORDINARY SHARES OF THE COMPANY ON JUNE 15, 2023 (THE "CONVERSION DATE"). THE CONVERSION RATE FOR EACH PREFERRED SHARE WILL BE 1.0754 ORDINARY SHARES OF THE COMPANY.
