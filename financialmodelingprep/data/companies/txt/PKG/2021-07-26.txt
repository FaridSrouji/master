PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. LAKE FOREST, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA (NYSE: PKG) TODAY REPORTED SECOND QUARTER 2021 NET INCOME OF $207 MILLION, OR $2.17 PER SHARE, AND NET INCOME OF $207 MILLION, OR $2.17 PER SHARE, EXCLUDING SPECIAL ITEMS. SECOND QUARTER NET SALES WERE $1.9 BILLION IN 2021 AND $1.5 BILLION IN 2020.
