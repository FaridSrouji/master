PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA ANNOUNCES 25% DIVIDEND INCREASE. LAKE FOREST, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA (NYSE: PKG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT INTENDS TO INCREASE THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK TO AN ANNUAL PAYOUT OF $5.00 PER SHARE FROM $4.00 PER SHARE, A 25% INCREASE. THE FIRST QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.25 PER SHARE WILL BE PAID TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 15, 2022 WITH A PAYMENT DATE OF JULY 15, 2022.
