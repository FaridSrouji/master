PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. LAKE FOREST, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA (NYSE: PKG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.25 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.25 PER SHARE WILL BE PAID TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF OCTOBER 3, 2022 WITH A PAYMENT DATE OF OCTOBER 17, 2022. FUTURE DECLARATION OF QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS AND THE ESTABLISHMENT OF FUTURE RECORD AND PAYMENT DATES ARE SUBJECT TO THE FINAL DETERMINATION BY PCA'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS
