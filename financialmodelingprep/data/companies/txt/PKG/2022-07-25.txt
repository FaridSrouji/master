PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. LAKE FOREST, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA (NYSE: PKG) TODAY REPORTED SECOND QUARTER 2022 NET INCOME OF $301 MILLION, OR $3.20 PER SHARE, AND NET INCOME OF $304 MILLION, OR $3.23 PER SHARE, EXCLUDING SPECIAL ITEMS. SECOND QUARTER NET SALES WERE $2.2 BILLION IN 2022 AND $1.9 BILLION IN 2021.
