PACKAGING CORP OF AMERICA SAYS APPROVED PLAN FOR CLOSURE OF CORRUGATED PRODUCTS PRODUCTION FACILITY LOCATED IN SAN LORENZO, CALIFORNIA. PACKAGING CORP OF AMERICA - ON APRIL 27, APPROVED PLAN FOR CLOSURE OF CORRUGATED PRODUCTS PRODUCTION FACILITY LOCATED IN SAN LORENZO, CALIFORNIA.PACKAGING CORP OF AMERICA - LAST DATE OF PRODUCTION AT SAN LORENZO PLANT IS EXPECTED TO BE IN JUNE 2020.PACKAGING CORP OF AMERICA - EXPECTS TO RECORD $21 TO $25 MILLION OF CHARGES IN Q2 OF 2020 RELATED TO PLANT CLOSURE.PACKAGING CORP OF AMERICA - ABOUT 20% OF SAN LORENZO CUSTOMER BUSINESS WILL BE ABSORBED INTO OTHER PCA PLANTS.
