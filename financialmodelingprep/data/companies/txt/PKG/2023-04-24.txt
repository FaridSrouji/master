PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS. LAKE FOREST, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA (NYSE: PKG) TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2023 NET INCOME OF $190 MILLION, OR $2.11 PER SHARE, AND NET INCOME OF $198 MILLION, OR $2.20 PER SHARE, EXCLUDING SPECIAL ITEMS. FIRST QUARTER NET SALES WERE $2.0 BILLION IN 2023 AND $2.1 BILLION IN 2022.
