FREEPORT-MCMORAN REPORTS FOURTH-QUARTER AND YEAR ENDED 2019 RESULTS.Q4 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.02.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $-0.00 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FREEPORT-MCMORAN- CONSOLIDATED SALES FOR 2020 ARE EXPECTED TO APPROXIMATE 3.5 BILLION POUNDS OF COPPER, 0.8 MILLION OUNCES OF GOLD, 88 MILLION POUNDS OF MOLYBDENUM.QTRLY REVENUE $3.91 BILLION VERSUS $3.68 BILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.Q4 REVENUE VIEW $3.70 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.AT DEC 31, 2019, CONSOLIDATED DEBT TOTALED $9.8 BILLION AND CONSOLIDATED CASH TOTALED $2.0 BILLION.QTRLY AVERAGE REALIZED PRICE PER POUND FOR COPPER $2.74 VERSUS $2.75.CAPITAL EXPENDITURES TOTALED $0.7 BILLION (INCLUDING ABOUT $0.4 BILLION FOR MAJOR PROJECTS) IN Q4 2019.Q4 COPPER SALES OF 906 MILLION POUNDS WERE 4 PERCENT HIGHER THAN OCTOBER 2019 ESTIMATE OF 870 MILLION POUNDS.QTRLY AVERAGE REALIZED PRICE PER OUNCE FOR GOLD $1,491 VERSUS $1,255.Q4 GOLD SALES OF 317 THOUSAND OUNCES WERE 117 THOUSAND OUNCES HIGHER THAN OCTOBER 2019 ESTIMATE OF 200 THOUSAND OUNCES.Q4 2019 MOLYBDENUM SALES OF 22 MILLION POUNDS WERE SLIGHTLY LOWER THAN BOTH OCTOBER 2019 ESTIMATE.ON SCHEDULE TO ESTABLISH LARGE-SCALE PRODUCTION FROM CO'S UNDERGROUND ORE BODIES AT GRASBERG.CAPITAL EXPENDITURES FOR YEAR 2020 ARE EXPECTED TO APPROXIMATE $2.8 BILLION.DURING Q4, PT-FI COMPLETED MINING IN THE GRASBERG OPEN PIT.PT-FI HAS COMMENCED EXTRACTION OF ORE FROM GRASBERG BLOCK CAVE UNDERGROUND MINE.
