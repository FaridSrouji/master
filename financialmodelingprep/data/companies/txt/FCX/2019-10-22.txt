FREEPORT-MCMORAN INC. REPORTS PRODUCTION RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER AND NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019; PROVIDES CONSOLIDATED SALES GUIDANCE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR OF 2019. FREEPORT-MCMORAN INC. REPORTED PRODUCTION RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER AND NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE QUARTER, THE COMPANY REPORTED COPPER PRODUCTION OF 864 MILLION OF RECOVERABLE POUNDS AGAINST 1,006 MILLION OF RECOVERABLE POUNDS A YEAR AGO. GOLD PRODUCTION WAS 333,000 OF RECOVERABLE OUNCES AGAINST 760,000 OF RECOVERABLE OUNCES A YEAR AGO. MOLYBDENUM PRODUCTION WAS 21 MILLION OF RECOVERABLE POUNDS AGAINST 23 MILLION OF RECOVERABLE POUNDS A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, THE COMPANY REPORTED COPPER PRODUCTION OF 2,420 MILLION OF RECOVERABLE POUNDS AGAINST 2,972 MILLION OF RECOVERABLE POUNDS A YEAR AGO. GOLD PRODUCTION WAS 659,000 OF RECOVERABLE OUNCES AGAINST 2,105,000 OF RECOVERABLE OUNCES A YEAR AGO. MOLYBDENUM PRODUCTION WAS 69 MILLION OF RECOVERABLE POUNDS AGAINST 69 MILLION OF RECOVERABLE POUNDS A YEAR AGO.FULL YEAR CONSOLIDATED SALES GUIDANCE IS SIMILAR TO PRIOR ESTIMATES, WITH CONSOLIDATED SALES EXPECTED TO APPROXIMATE 3.3 BILLION POUNDS OF COPPER, 874,000 OUNCES OF GOLD AND 92 MILLION POUNDS OF MOLYBDENUM FOR THE YEAR 2019, INCLUDING 870 MILLION POUNDS OF COPPER, 200,000 OUNCES OF GOLD AND 24 MILLION POUNDS OF MOLYBDENUM IN FOURTH-QUARTER 2019.
