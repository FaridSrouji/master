JAPAN BECOMES FIRST COUNTRY TO APPROVE REGENERON ANTIBODY COCKTAIL (CASIRIVIMAB AND IMDEVIMAB) FOR THE TREATMENT OF MILD TO MODERATE COVID-19. TARRYTOWN, N.Y., JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JAPAN'S MINISTRY OF HEALTH, LABOUR AND WELFARE (MHLW) HAS APPROVED REGENERON'S CASIRIVIMAB AND IMDEVIMAB ANTIBODY COCKTAIL TO TREAT PATIENTS WITH MILD TO MODERATE COVID-19.
