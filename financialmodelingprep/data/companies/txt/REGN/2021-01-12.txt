REGENERON ANNOUNCES U.S. GOVERNMENT AGREEMENT TO PURCHASE ADDITIONAL COVID-19 ANTIBODY COCKTAIL DOSES. TARRYTOWN, N.Y., JAN. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEW AGREEMENT COVERS 1.25 MILLION ADDITIONAL DOSES OF THE CASIRIVIMAB AND IMDEVIMAB ANTIBODY COCKTAIL, BRINGING TOTAL POTENTIAL U.S. SUPPLY TO OVER 1.5 MILLION DOSES DOSES ARE BEING SUPPLIED FOR USE PURSUANT TO THE FDA EMERGENCY USE...
