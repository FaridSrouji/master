EVKEEZA® (EVINACUMAB) PHASE 3 TRIAL DEMONSTRATES 48% LDL-C REDUCTION IN CHILDREN WITH ULTRA-RARE FORM OF HIGH CHOLESTEROL. TARRYTOWN, N.Y. , MAY 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHILDREN ALREADY ON OTHER LIPID-LOWERING THERAPIES ENTERED THE TRIAL WITH DANGEROUSLY HIGH LDL-C (264 MG/DL ON AVERAGE), AND 79% SAW THEIR LDL-C REDUCED BY AT LEAST HALF AT 24 WEEKS FDA SUBMISSION PLANNED BY END OF 2022 REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED POSITIVE RESULTS FROM A PHASE 3 TRIAL EVALUATING EVKEEZA® (EVINACUMAB) IN CHILDREN AGED 5 TO 11 WITH HOMOZYGOUS FAMILIAL HYPERCHOLESTEROLEMIA (HOFH).
