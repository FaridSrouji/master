LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB) RECEIVES POSITIVE CHMP OPINION FOR THE TREATMENT IN EUROPE OF TWO ADVANCED CANCERS. TARRYTOWN, N.Y. AND PARIS, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LIBTAYO RECOMMENDED FOR APPROVAL IN THE FIRST-LINE TREATMENT OF CERTAIN PATIENTS WITH ADVANCED NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER WHOSE TUMORS HAVE ≥50% PD-L1 EXPRESSION LIBTAYO ALSO RECOMMENDED FOR APPROVAL IN PATIENTS WITH ADVANCED BASAL CELL CARCINOMA WHO HAVE PROGRESSED ON OR ARE INTOLERANT TO A HEDGEHOG PATHWAY INHIBITOR CHMP HAS NOW ISSUED THREE POSITIVE OPINIONS FOR LIBTAYO IN ADVANCED CANCERS REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) AND SANOFI TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EUROPEAN MEDICINES AGENCY'S (EMA) COMMITTEE FOR MEDICINAL PRODUCTS FOR HUMAN USE (CHMP) HAS ADOPTED POSITIVE OPINIONS FOR LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB) AS MONOTHERAPY IN TWO ADVANCED CANCERS.
