NEW ENGLAND JOURNAL OF MEDICINE PUBLISHES POSITIVE PHASE 3 TRIAL RESULTS FOR REGEN-COV™ (CASIRIVIMAB AND IMDEVIMAB) TO PREVENT SARS-COV-2 INFECTION. TARRYTOWN, N.Y., AUG. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE NEW ENGLAND JOURNAL OF MEDICINE (NEJM) PUBLISHED POSITIVE DETAILED RESULTS FROM A PHASE 3 TRIAL THAT ASSESSED THE ABILITY OF REGEN-COV™ (CASIRIVIMAB AND IMDEVIMAB) TO PREVENT COVID-19 INFECTION AMONG HOUSEHOLD CONTACTS OF SARS-COV-2 INFECTED INDIVIDUALS.
