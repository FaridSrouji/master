TEEN SCIENTISTS AND ENGINEERS WIN $5 MILLION AT LARGEST GLOBAL HIGH SCHOOL STEM COMPETITION. TARRYTOWN, N.Y. AND WASHINGTON, MAY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- $75,000 TOP AWARD GOES TO 16-YEAR-OLD MICHELLE HUA FOR NOVEL HUMAN ACTION RECOGNITION ALGORITHM IN REGENERON INTERNATIONAL SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING FAIR 2021 REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC.  (NASDAQ: REGN) AND SOCIETY FOR SCIENCE  (THE SOCIETY) ANNOUNCED THAT MICHELLE HUA, 16, OF TROY, MICHIGAN, WON THE $75,000 TOP AWARD IN THE 2021 REGENERON INTERNATIONAL SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING FAIR (REGENERON ISEF), THE WORLD'S LARGEST GLOBAL HIGH SCHOOL COMPETITION.
