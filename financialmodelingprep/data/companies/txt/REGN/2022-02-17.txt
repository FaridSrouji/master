UPDATE ON ONGOING DUPIXENT® (DUPILUMAB) CHRONIC SPONTANEOUS URTICARIA PHASE 3 PROGRAM. UPDATE ON ONGOING DUPIXENT ® (DUPILUMAB) CHRONIC SPONTANEOUS URTICARIA PHASE 3 PROGRAM
REGENERON AND SANOFI PROVIDE UPDATE ON ONGOING DUPIXENT® (DUPILUMAB) CHRONIC SPONTANEOUS URTICARIA PHASE 3 PROGRAM. TARRYTOWN, N.Y. AND PARIS, FEB. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN A PHASE 3 TRIAL IN PATIENTS REFRACTORY TO OMALIZUMAB, DUPIXENT DID NOT REACH STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE IN INTERIM ANALYSIS DESPITE NUMERIC IMPROVEMENTS OBSERVED ACROSS KEY ENDPOINTS; TRIAL WILL BE STOPPED DUE TO FUTILITY POSITIVE RESULTS FROM A PREVIOUS PHASE 3 TRIAL IN BIOLOGIC-NAÏVE PATIENTS SHOWED DUPIXENT SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCED ITCH AND HIVES COMPARED TO STANDARD-OF-CARE ANTIHISTAMINES ALONE COMPANIES REMAIN COMMITTED TO ADVANCING DUPIXENT FOR PATIENTS WITH CHRONIC SPONTANEOUS URTICARIA UNCONTROLLED ON ANTIHISTAMINES AND ARE EVALUATING NEXT STEPS REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) AND SANOFI TODAY ANNOUNCED A PHASE 3 TRIAL (CUPID STUDY B) EVALUATING DUPIXENT® (DUPILUMAB) IN PATIENTS WITH CHRONIC SPONTANEOUS URTICARIA (CSU) WILL STOP DUE TO FUTILITY, BASED ON A PRE-SPECIFIED INTERIM ANALYSIS.
