SANOFI: FDA GRANTS DUPIXENT® (DUPILUMAB) BREAKTHROUGH THERAPY DESIGNATION FOR EOSINOPHILIC ESOPHAGITIS. FDA GRANTS DUPIXENT® (DUPILUMAB) BREAKTHROUGH THERAPY DESIGNATION FOR EOSINOPHILIC ESOPHAGITIS.DESIGNATION BASED ON POSITIVE RESULTS FROM PART A OF PIVOTAL PHASE 3 TRIAL.DUPIXENT IS FIRST AND ONLY BIOLOGIC TO SHOW POSITIVE AND CLINICALLY-MEANINGFUL PHASE 3 RESULTS IN PATIENTS 12 YEARS AND OLDER WITH EOSINOPHILIC ESOPHAGITIS (EOE).
