LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB) APPROVED BY THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION AS THE FIRST IMMUNOTHERAPY IN SECOND LINE RECURRENT OR METASTATIC CERVICAL CANCER IRRESPECTIVE OF PD-L1 EXPRESSION LEVEL OR TUMOR HISTOLOGY. APPROVAL BASED ON A PHASE 3 TRIAL THAT DEMONSTRATED SIGNIFICANT SURVIVAL BENEFIT IN PATIENTS WITH RECURRENT OR METASTATIC CERVICAL CANCER, WITH LIBTAYO REDUCING THE RISK OF DEATH BY 31% COMPARED TO CHEMOTHERAPY DURING THE STUDY LIBTAYO NOW APPROVED TO TREAT FOUR CANCER TYPES IN THE EUROPEAN UNION TARRYTOWN, N.Y. , NOV. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION (EC) APPROVED LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB) AS MONOTHERAPY FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULT PATIENTS WITH RECURRENT OR METASTATIC CERVICAL CANCER AND DISEASE PROGRESSION ON OR AFTER PLATINUM-BASED CHEMOTHERAPY.
