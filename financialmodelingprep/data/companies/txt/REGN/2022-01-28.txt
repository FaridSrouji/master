REGENERON AND SANOFI PROVIDE REGULATORY UPDATE ON LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB-RWLC) IN ADVANCED CERVICAL CANCER. TARRYTOWN, N.Y. AND PARIS, JAN. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) AND SANOFI TODAY ANNOUNCED THE VOLUNTARY WITHDRAWAL OF THE SUPPLEMENTAL BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (SBLA) FOR LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB-RWLC) AS A SECOND-LINE TREATMENT FOR PATIENTS WITH ADVANCED CERVICAL CANCER.
