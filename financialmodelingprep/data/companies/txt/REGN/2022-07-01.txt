REGENERON COMPLETES PURCHASE OF SANOFI'S STAKE IN LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB). COMPANY PROVIDES ANTICIPATED IMPACT TO SECOND QUARTER 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS DUE TO RECENTLY COMPLETED BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT TRANSACTIONS TARRYTOWN, N.Y. , JULY 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF SANOFI'S STAKE IN LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB), PROVIDING REGENERON WITH EXCLUSIVE WORLDWIDE DEVELOPMENT, COMMERCIALIZATION, AND MANUFACTURING RIGHTS TO THE MEDICINE ORIGINALLY DISCOVERED IN REGENERON'S LABORATORIES.
