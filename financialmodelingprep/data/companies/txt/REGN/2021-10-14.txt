FDA ACCEPTS REGEN-COV® (CASIRIVIMAB AND IMDEVIMAB) FOR PRIORITY REVIEW FOR TREATMENT AND PROPHYLAXIS OF COVID-19. TARRYTOWN, N.Y., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ACCEPTED FOR PRIORITY REVIEW A BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) FOR REGEN-COV® (CASIRIVIMAB AND IMDEVIMAB) TO TREAT COVID-19 IN NON-HOSPITALIZED PATIENTS AND AS PROPHYLAXIS IN CERTAIN INDIVIDUALS.
