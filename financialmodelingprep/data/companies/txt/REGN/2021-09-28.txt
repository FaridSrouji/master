FDA ACCEPTS LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB-RWLC) FOR PRIORITY REVIEW FOR ADVANCED CERVICAL CANCER. TARRYTOWN, N.Y., SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EUROPEAN UNION REGULATORY SUBMISSION PLANNED BY END OF 2021 REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ACCEPTED FOR PRIORITY REVIEW THE SUPPLEMENTAL BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (SBLA) FOR PD-1 INHIBITOR LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB-RWLC) TO TREAT PATIENTS WITH RECURRENT OR METASTATIC CERVICAL CANCER WHOSE DISEASE PROGRESSED ON OR AFTER CHEMOTHERAPY.
