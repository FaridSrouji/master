REGENERON ANNOUNCES THE 2022 WINNERS OF THE REGENERON PRIZE FOR CREATIVE INNOVATION. TARRYTOWN, N.Y. , JULY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE WINNERS OF THE 10TH ANNUAL REGENERON PRIZE FOR CREATIVE INNOVATION, A COMPETITION DESIGNED TO RECOGNIZE EXCELLENCE AND CREATIVITY IN BIOMEDICAL RESEARCH CONDUCTED BY POSTDOCTORAL FELLOWS AND GRADUATE STUDENTS.
