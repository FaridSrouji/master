REGENERON PHARMA SAYS FDA ACCEPTS EVINACUMAB BLA FOR PRIORITY REVIEW TO TREAT HIGH CHOLESTEROL. FDA ACCEPTS EVINACUMAB BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION FOR PRIORITY REVIEW AS A TREATMENT FOR PATIENTS WITH HOFH, AN ULTRA-RARE INHERITED FORM OF HIGH CHOLESTEROL.REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS INC - TARGET ACTION DATE FOR FDA DECISION ON EVINACUMAB IS FEBRUARY 11, 2021.
