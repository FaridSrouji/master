FDA EXPANDS APPROVAL OF DUPIXENT® (DUPILUMAB) TO INCLUDE CHILDREN AGED 6 TO 11 YEARS WITH MODERATE-TO-SEVERE ASTHMA. TARRYTOWN, N.Y. AND PARIS, OCT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  DUPIXENT IS THE ONLY BIOLOGIC MEDICINE TO IMPROVE LUNG FUNCTION IN CHILDREN AGED 6 TO 11 YEARS IN A RANDOMIZED PHASE 3 TRIAL, SUPPORTING POTENTIAL AS A BEST-IN-CLASS OPTION ONLY BIOLOGIC MEDICINE APPROVED FOR CHILDREN WITH ORAL CORTICOSTEROID-DEPENDENT ASTHMA REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) AND SANOFI TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED DUPIXENT® (DUPILUMAB) AS AN ADD-ON MAINTENANCE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS AGED 6 TO 11 YEARS WITH MODERATE-TO-SEVERE ASTHMA CHARACTERIZED BY AN EOSINOPHILIC PHENOTYPE OR WITH ORAL CORTICOSTEROID-DEPENDENT ASTHMA.
