EVERSOURCE AGAIN RECOGNIZED BY BARRON'S AS ONE OF AMERICA'S “MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES”. BOSTON & HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FOR THE SECOND YEAR IN A ROW, EVERSOURCE ENERGY HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY BARRON'S ON ITS LIST OF AMERICA'S MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES.
