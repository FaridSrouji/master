EVERSOURCE AGAIN NATIONALLY RECOGNIZED AMONG AMERICA'S “MOST JUST” COMPANIES BY JUST CAPITAL AND CNBC. HARTFORD, CONN. & BOSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WITH THE RELEASE TODAY OF THIS YEAR'S RANKINGS OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES BY JUST CAPITAL AND CNBC, EVERSOURCE ENERGY (NYSE: ES) IS BEING NATIONALLY CELEBRATED AS A LEADER FOR CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY. THE RECOGNITION MARKS EVERSOURCE'S FOURTH CONSECUTIVE APPEARANCE IN THE TOP-100 OF THE ANNUAL LIST, RECOGNIZING THE COMPANY'S CONSISTENT AND UNRELENTING COMMITMENT TO ITS CUSTOMERS, COMMUNITIES, EMPLOYEES, AND THE ENVIRONMENT IN PROVIDING SAFE, RELIABLE ENERGY SERVICE.
