EVERSOURCE ENERGY REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. HARTFORD, CONN. & BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EVERSOURCE ENERGY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS OF $366.1 MILLION COMPARED WITH EARNINGS OF $334.8 MILLION IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020.
