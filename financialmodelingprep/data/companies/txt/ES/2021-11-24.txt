SOUTH FORK WIND RECEIVES FEDERAL RECORD OF DECISION, SETTING STAGE FOR NEW YORK'S FIRST OFFSHORE WIND FARM TO BEGIN ONSHORE CONSTRUCTION IN EARLY 2022. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEW YORK'S FIRST OFFSHORE WIND FARM, HAS RECEIVED ITS RECORD OF DECISION FROM THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR'S BUREAU OF OCEAN ENERGY MANAGEMENT.
