CORNING ANNOUNCES INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CORNING, N.Y., FEB. 02, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORNING INCORPORATED'S (NYSE: GLW) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.27 PER SHARE, A 12.5% INCREASE VERSUS THE COMPANY'S PREVIOUS QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.24. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON MARCH 30, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEB. 28, 2022.
