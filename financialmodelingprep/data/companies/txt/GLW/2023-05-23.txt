CORNING ANNOUNCES PRICE INCREASE FOR DISPLAY GLASS PRODUCTS. CORNING, N.Y., MAY 23, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORNING INCORPORATED (NYSE: GLW) IS ANNOUNCING A 20% INCREASE TO ITS DISPLAY GLASS SUBSTRATE PRICES. THE GLOBAL PRICE INCREASE WILL IMPACT ALL REGIONS, COMPOSITIONS, AND GLASS GEN SIZES BEGINNING IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2023.
