CORNING ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CORNING, N.Y. , JUNE 30, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORNING INCORPORATED'S (NYSE: GLW) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON SEPT. 29, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUG. 31, 2021.
