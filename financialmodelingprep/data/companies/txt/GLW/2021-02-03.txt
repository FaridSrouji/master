ROGER W. FERGUSON JR., PRESIDENT AND CEO OF TIAA, NAMED TO CORNING BOARD OF DIRECTORS. CORNING, N.Y., FEB. 03, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORNING INCORPORATED (NYSE: GLW) TODAY APPOINTED ROGER W. FERGUSON JR., PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF TIAA, TO CORNING'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1, 2021.
CORNING ANNOUNCES INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CORNING, N.Y. , FEB. 03, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORNING INCORPORATED'S (NYSE: GLW) BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED ON WEDNESDAY A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER COMMON SHARE, A 9% INCREASE VERSUS THE COMPANY'S PREVIOUS QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.22. THE 2021 FIRST-QUARTER DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON MARCH 30, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEB. 26, 2021.
