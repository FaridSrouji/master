CORNING APPOINTS THOMAS D. FRENCH TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. CORNING, N.Y., JUNE 20, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORNING INCORPORATED'S (NYSE: GLW) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THOMAS D. FRENCH, FORMER SENIOR PARTNER AND CURRENT SENIOR PARTNER EMERITUS WITH MCKINSEY & COMPANY, HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO CORNING'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JUNE 20, 2023.
