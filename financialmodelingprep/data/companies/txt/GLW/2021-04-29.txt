CORNING ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CORNING, N.Y., APRIL 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORNING INCORPORATED'S (NYSE: GLW) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON JUNE 29, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 28, 2021.
