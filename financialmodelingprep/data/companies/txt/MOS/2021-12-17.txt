MOSAIC ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.1125 PER SHARE. TAMPA, FL / ACCESSWIRE / DECEMBER 17, 2021 / THE MOSAIC COMPANY (NYSE:MOS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.1125 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON MARCH 17, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 3, 2022.
