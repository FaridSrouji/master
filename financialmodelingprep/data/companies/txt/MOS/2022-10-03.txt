THE MOSAIC COMPANY ANNOUNCES HURRICANE IAN IMPACTS. TAMPA, FL / ACCESSWIRE / OCTOBER 3, 2022 / THE MOSAIC COMPANY (NYSE:MOS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT NORTH AMERICAN PHOSPHATES WAS NEGATIVELY IMPACTED BY DAMAGE CAUSED BY HURRICANE IAN. SIGNIFICANT FLOODING AND HIGH WINDS WERE EXPERIENCED THROUGHOUT CENTRAL FLORIDA DURING THE STORM, AND THIS CAUSED MODEST DAMAGE TO OUR FACILITIES AND TO SUPPORTING INFRASTRUCTURE.
