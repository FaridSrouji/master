THE MOSAIC COMPANY DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 19, 2019. THE MOSAIC COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.05 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY’S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON DECEMBER 19, 2019, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 5, 2019.
MOSAIC UNIT ISSUED IMMINENT DANGER ORDER AT ITS CARLSBAD MINE. MOSAIC - UNIT WAS ISSUED IMMINENT DANGER ORDER BY U.S. DEPARTMENT OF LABOR, MINE SAFETY & HEALTH ADMINISTRATION AT ITS MINE AT CARLSBAD, NEW MEXICO.MOSAIC - ORDER WAS ISSUED UPON OBSERVING A MECHANIC WORKING UNDER RAISED FORKS OF A CAT FORKLIFT.MOSAIC - NO ONE WAS INJURED AS A RESULT OF THE INCIDENT AT CARLSBAD MINE, NEW MEXICO.MOSAIC - IMMINENT DANGER AS A RESULT OF THE INCIDENT AT CARLSBAD MINE, NEW MEXICO ,HAS BEEN ADDRESSED.
