MOSAIC ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.15 PER SHARE. TAMPA, FL / ACCESSWIRE / MAY 19, 2022 / THE MOSAIC COMPANY (NYSE:MOS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.15 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JUNE 16, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 2, 2022.
