LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND. MOORESVILLE, N.C., NOV. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS FOR LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF EIGHTY CENTS ($0.80) PER SHARE, PAYABLE FEBRUARY 2, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JANUARY 19, 2022.
