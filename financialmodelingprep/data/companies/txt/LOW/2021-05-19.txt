LOWE'S REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2021 SALES AND EARNINGS RESULTS. MOORESVILLE, N.C., MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) TODAY REPORTED NET EARNINGS OF $2.3 BILLION AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) OF $3.21 FOR THE QUARTER ENDED APRIL 30, 2021 COMPARED TO NET EARNINGS OF $1.3 BILLION AND DILUTED EPS OF $1.76 IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020.
