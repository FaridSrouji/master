LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND. MOORESVILLE, N.C., AUG. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS FOR LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF EIGHTY CENTS ($0.80) PER SHARE, PAYABLE NOVEMBER 3, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF OCTOBER 20, 2021.
