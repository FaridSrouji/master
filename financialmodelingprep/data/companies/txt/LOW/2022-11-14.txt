LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND. MOORESVILLE, N.C. , NOV. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND FIVE CENTS ($1.05) PER SHARE, PAYABLE FEB. 8, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JAN. 25, 2023.
