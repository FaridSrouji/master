LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. PROVIDES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDING JAN. 31, 2020. LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. PROVIDED EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDING JAN. 31, 2020. FOR THE FULL YEAR OF 2019, TOTAL SALES ARE EXPECTED TO INCREASE APPROXIMATELY 2%. COMPARABLE SALES ARE EXPECTED TO INCREASE APPROXIMATELY 3%. OPERATING INCOME AS A PERCENTAGE OF SALES (OPERATING MARGIN) IS EXPECTED TO INCREASE 290 TO 320 BASIS POINTS. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $5.35 TO $5.47 ARE EXPECTED FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDING JAN. 31, 2020.
