LOWE'S, PETCO TO HARNESS THE POWER OF HOME AND PETS WITH NEW STORE-IN-STORE CONCEPT. MOORESVILLE, N.C., JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S (NYSE: LOW) AND PETCO HEALTH AND WELLNESS COMPANY, INC. (NASDAQ: WOOF) TODAY ANNOUNCED A PILOT STORE-IN-STORE PROGRAM THAT BRINGS TRUSTED PRODUCTS, SERVICES AND EXPERTISE – FOR BOTH HOME AND PETS – INTO ONE, CONVENIENT STOP AT SELECT LOWE'S LOCATIONS.
