LOWE'S COMPLETES SALE OF CANADIAN RETAIL BUSINESS TO SYCAMORE PARTNERS. MOORESVILLE, N.C. , FEB. 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE SALE OF ITS CANADIAN RETAIL BUSINESS TO SYCAMORE PARTNERS, A PRIVATE EQUITY FIRM SPECIALIZING IN RETAIL, CONSUMER AND DISTRIBUTION-RELATED INVESTMENTS.
