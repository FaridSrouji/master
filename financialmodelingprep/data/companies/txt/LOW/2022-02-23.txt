LOWE'S REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER 2021 SALES AND EARNINGS RESULTS. MOORESVILLE, N.C., FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) TODAY REPORTED NET EARNINGS OF $1.2 BILLION AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) OF $1.78 FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JANUARY 28, 2022 COMPARED TO NET EARNINGS OF $978 MILLION AND DILUTED EPS OF $1.32 IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020.
