LOWE'S SELECTS FIRST 100 LOWE'S HOMETOWNS COMMUNITY IMPACT PROJECTS AS PART OF NEW FIVE-YEAR, $100 MILLION COMMITMENT TO COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE U.S.. LOWE'S HOMETOWNS AMBASSADOR BOBBY BERK AND THE LOWE'S HOME TEAM – INCLUDING NFL LEGEND DREW BREES AND OTHER STARS SUCH AS JUSTIN HERBERT, CHRISTIAN MCCAFFREY AND TRAVIS KELCE – TO HELP MAKE COMMUNITIES BETTER FOR ALL MOORESVILLE, N.C. , JUNE 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S TODAY REVEALED THE 100 COMMUNITY-NOMINATED IMPACT PROJECTS THAT HAVE BEEN SELECTED AS PART OF THIS YEAR'S LOWE'S HOMETOWNS PROGRAM, WITH 2022 SERVING AS THE FIRST OF A FIVE-YEAR, $100 MILLION COMMITMENT TO THE COMMUNITIES IT SERVES.
