LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND. MOORESVILLE, N.C., MARCH 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS FOR LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF SIXTY CENTS ($0.60) PER SHARE, PAYABLE MAY 5, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 21, 2021.
