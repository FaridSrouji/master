DAILY TO ENSURE ADDITIONAL TIME TO REPLENISH ESSENTIAL PRODUCTS.LOWE'S COMPANIES - TEMPORARILY RAISING WAGES BY $2 AN HOUR FOR EVERY FULL-TIME, PART-TIME & SEASONAL HOURLY STORE.LOWE'S COMPANIES - EXTENDED EMERGENCY PAID LEAVE UP TO A TOTAL OF FOUR WEEKS FOR THOSE AT A HIGHER RISK FOR SEVERE ILLNESS FROM COVID-19.LOWE'S COMPANIES - TEMPORARILY RAISING WAGES BY $2 AN HOUR FOR EVERY CONTACT CENTER & SUPPLY CHAIN ASSOCIATES.LOWE'S COMPANIES - ACTIVELY HIRING 30,000 ASSOCIATES IN STORES ACROSS U.S..LOWE'S COMPANIES INC - SHIPPED AN ADDITIONAL 10,000 TRUCKLOADS OF ESSENTIAL PRODUCTS TO REPLENISH STORES.LOWE'S COMPANIES - TEMPORARILY INCREASING WAGES FOR ASSOCIATES BY $2 AN HOUR FOR HOURS THEY WORK THROUGHOUT MONTH OF APRIL IN U.S. AND CANADA.
