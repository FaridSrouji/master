LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. ANNOUNCES THE RETIREMENT OF MATTHEW V. HOLLIFIELD AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER. ON OCTOBER 23, 2019, MATTHEW V. HOLLIFIELD ANNOUNCED HIS INTENTION TO RETIRE AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER OF LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. MR. HOLLIFIELD WILL REMAIN EMPLOYED WITH THE COMPANY UNTIL DECEMBER 13, 2019 AND WILL THEREAFTER PROVIDE CONSULTING SERVICES TO THE COMPANY THROUGH FEBRUARY 2020.
