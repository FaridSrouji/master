LOWE'S COMMITS MORE THAN $15 MILLION TO SUPPORT NATIONAL NONPROFITS IN 2022. COMMITMENTS TO MILITARY AND OTHER NONPROFITS TO INCLUDE MORE THAN $9 MILLION TO CREATE SAFE, AFFORDABLE HOUSING AND $4 MILLION TO SUPPORT SKILLED TRADES WITH GENERATION T PARTNERS MOORESVILLE, N.C., MAY 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S TODAY ANNOUNCED A COMMITMENT OF MORE THAN $15 MILLION TO SUPPORT NATIONAL NONPROFIT PARTNERS IN 2022, ADDRESSING SOME OF THE MOST CRITICAL CHALLENGES FACING COMMUNITIES TODAY.
