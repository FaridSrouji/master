LOWE'S REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 SALES AND EARNINGS RESULTS. MOORESVILLE, N.C., AUG. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) TODAY REPORTED NET EARNINGS OF $3.0 BILLION AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) OF $4.25 FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JULY 30, 2021 COMPARED TO NET EARNINGS OF $2.8 BILLION AND DILUTED EPS OF $3.74 IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020.
