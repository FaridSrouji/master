LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. ANNOUNCES 31 PERCENT INCREASE TO QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. MOORESVILLE, N.C. , MAY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND FIVE CENTS ($1.05) PER SHARE, PAYABLE AUGUST 3, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 20, 2022.
