LOWE'S DONATING $1 MILLION TO PROVIDE IMMEDIATE RELIEF IN WAKE OF TORNADO DEVASTATION ACROSS U.S.. MOORESVILLE, N.C., DEC. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL DONATE $1 MILLION TO SUPPORT IMMEDIATE RELIEF AND RECOVERY EFFORTS AFTER TORNADOES CUT THROUGH KENTUCKY AND THE NATION'S HEARTLAND, CAUSING CATASTROPHIC AND UNPRECEDENTED DAMAGE.
