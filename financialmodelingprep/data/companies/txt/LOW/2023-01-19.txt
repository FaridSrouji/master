LOWE'S PILOTS IN-STORE BIRTHDAY PARTIES TO INSPIRE THE NEXT GENERATION OF BUILDERS. EXPANDING UPON THE SUCCESS OF KID'S WORKSHOPS, A NEW, HANDS-ON BIRTHDAY PARTY EXPERIENCE IS LAUNCHING AT SELECT LOWE'S STORES, AS A FUN WAY TO SPARK CREATIVITY IN FUTURE BUILDERS MOORESVILLE, N.C. , JAN. 19, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S (NYSE: LOW) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN IN-STORE KIDS' BIRTHDAY PARTY PROGRAM THAT AIMS TO INSPIRE THE NEXT GENERATION OF BUILDERS WHILE GIVING PARENTS A COMPREHENSIVE ONE-STOP PARTY SOLUTION.
