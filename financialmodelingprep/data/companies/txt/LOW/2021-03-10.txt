HABITAT FOR HUMANITY INTERNATIONAL AND LOWE'S KICK OFF MOST AMBITIOUS INTERNATIONAL WOMEN BUILD WEEK EVER WITH MORE THAN 300 PROJECTS ACROSS THE UNITED STATES, CANADA AND INDIA. ATLANTA, MARCH 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HABITAT FOR HUMANITY INTERNATIONAL (HABITAT) AND LOWE'S KICK OFF THEIR THIRD ANNUAL HABITAT FOR HUMANITY INTERNATIONAL WOMEN BUILD WEEK (MARCH 8-15) BY EMPHASIZING THE GLOBAL NEED FOR WOMEN'S ACCESS TO SAFE AND AFFORDABLE HOUSING. THIS YEAR, THE PARTNERSHIP WILL LAUNCH 300 WOMEN BUILD PROJECTS ACROSS THE UNITED STATES, CANADA AND INDIA, AN INCREASE DESPITE THE NEED FOR STRICT ON-SITE COVID-19 PROTOCOLS AND FEWER PARTICIPANTS ABLE TO VOLUNTEER.
