LOWE'S MAKES HOME THE DESTINATION THIS SPRING. MOORESVILLE, N.C., MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS INSPIRING PEOPLE TO TRANSFORM THEIR HOMES INTO THE DESTINATION THIS SPRING WITH A MONTH-LONG "SPRINGFEST" CELEBRATION FEATURING FREE, CURBSIDE FAMILY PROJECT KITS, AS WELL AS PROJECT INSPIRATION THAT WILL TRANSPORT HOUSEHOLDS TO THE DESTINATION OF THEIR CHOICE WITHOUT EVER HAVING TO LEAVE HOME.
