'100 HOMETOWNS' REVEALED: LOWE'S ANNOUNCES 100 COMMUNITY IMPACT PROJECTS AS PART OF 100TH BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION. MOORESVILLE, N.C., JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AFTER 100 YEARS OF SERVING COMMUNITIES, LOWE'S TODAY REVEALED THE 100 IMPACT PROJECTS OUT OF MORE THAN 2,200 SUBMISSIONS THAT ARE THE CORNERSTONE OF 100 HOMETOWNS, AN INITIATIVE TO CELEBRATE LOWE'S CENTENNIAL.
