LOWE'S ANNOUNCES 5-YEAR, $100M COMMITMENT TO COMMUNITY IMPACT PROJECTS ACROSS AMERICA. MOORESVILLE, N.C., MARCH 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S TODAY ANNOUNCED A FIVE-YEAR, $100 MILLION INVESTMENT IN THE COMMUNITIES IT SERVES – THE COMPANY'S LARGEST-EVER COMMITMENT OF ITS KIND – WITH A NEW COMMUNITY IMPACT PROGRAM CALLED LOWE'S HOMETOWNS.
