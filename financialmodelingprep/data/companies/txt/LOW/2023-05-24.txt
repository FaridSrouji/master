LOWE'S BROADENS RURAL FOOTPRINT WITH FARM AND RANCH OFFERINGS IN UP TO 300 STORES. LOWE'S DEEPENS AFFINITY WITH RURAL CUSTOMER BASE, PET OWNERS WITH NEW OUTDOOR PRODUCT ASSORTMENT FOR WORK AND PLAY MOORESVILLE, N.C. , MAY 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S ANNOUNCED A NEW ONE-STOP SHOP CONCEPT DESIGNED TO BRING THOSE CUSTOMERS LOCATED IN RURAL AREAS ACROSS THE COUNTRY EVERYTHING NEEDED FOR FARM AND HOME WITH A WIDER OFFERING OF FARM, RANCH AND OUTDOOR PRODUCTS IN UP TO 300 ADDITIONAL LOWE'S STORES BY YEAR END.
