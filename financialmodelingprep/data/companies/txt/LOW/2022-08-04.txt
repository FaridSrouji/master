LOWE'S DONATING $500,000 TO PROVIDE IMMEDIATE RELIEF IN WAKE OF CATASTROPHIC FLOODING IN EASTERN KENTUCKY. MOORESVILLE, N.C. , AUG. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOWE'S TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL DONATE $500,000 TO SUPPORT DISASTER RELIEF EFFORTS FOR ASSOCIATES, CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES IN DIRECT RESPONSE TO SEVERE FLOODING IN EASTERN KENTUCKY.
