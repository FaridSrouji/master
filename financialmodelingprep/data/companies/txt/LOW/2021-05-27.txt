LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. ANNOUNCES 33 PERCENT INCREASE TO QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. MOORESVILLE, N.C., MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS FOR LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF EIGHTY CENTS ($0.80) PER SHARE, PAYABLE AUGUST 4, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 21, 2021.
