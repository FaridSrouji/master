TAPESTRY, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 30, 2019. TAPESTRY, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.3375 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 30, 2019 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 6, 2019.
