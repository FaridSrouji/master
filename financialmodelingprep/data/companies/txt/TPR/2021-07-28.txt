TAPESTRY TAKES ACTION TO DRIVE POSITIVE CHANGE FOR PEOPLE, PLANET AND COMMUNITY. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TAPESTRY, INC. (NYSE: TPR), A LEADING NEW YORK-BASED HOUSE OF MODERN LUXURY ACCESSORIES AND LIFESTYLE BRANDS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ACTIONS TO DRIVE ITS PEOPLE-CENTERED, PURPOSE-LED STRATEGY. FIRST, THE COMPANY HAS FORMED A NEW TAPESTRY FOUNDATION TO ADVANCE ACCESS AND EQUITY INITIATIVES AND TO COMBAT CLIMATE CHANGE. IN ADDITION, TAPESTRY IS TAKING FURTHER ACTION TO ACCELERATE AND AMPLIFY ITS OUR SOCIAL FABRIC CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY AGENDA TO EFFECT POSITIVE CHANGE. TAPES
