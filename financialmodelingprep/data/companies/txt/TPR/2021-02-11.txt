INVESTOR ALERT: SCOTT+SCOTT ATTORNEYS AT LAW LLP CONTINUES TO INVESTIGATE TAPESTRY, INC.'S DIRECTORS AND OFFICERS FOR BREACH OF FIDUCIARY DUTIES – TPR. NEW YORK, FEB. 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SCOTT+SCOTT ATTORNEYS AT LAW LLP (“SCOTT+SCOTT”), AN INTERNATIONAL SECURITIES AND CONSUMER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, CONTINUES TO INVESTIGATE CERTAIN DIRECTORS AND OFFICERS OF TAPESTRY, INC. (“TAPESTRY”) (NYSE: TPR) FOR BREACHING THEIR FIDUCIARY DUTIES TO TAPESTRY AND ITS SHAREHOLDERS. IF YOU ARE A TAPESTRY SHAREHOLDER, YOU MAY CONTACT ATTORNEY JOE PETTIGREW FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION TOLL-FREE AT 844-818-6982 OR JPETTIGREW@SCOTT-SCOTT.COM .
