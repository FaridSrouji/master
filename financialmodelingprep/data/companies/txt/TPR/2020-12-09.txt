PAM LIFFORD AND THOMAS R. GRECO JOIN TAPESTRY, INC. BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TAPESTRY, INC. (NYSE: TPR), A LEADING NEW YORK-BASED HOUSE OF MODERN LUXURY ACCESSORIES AND LIFESTYLE BRANDS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PAM LIFFORD AND TOM GRECO HAVE BEEN APPOINTED TO TAPESTRY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. THE APPOINTMENTS OF MS. LIFFORD AND MR. GRECO TO THE BOARD BRING THE MEMBERSHIP TO TEN, INCLUDING NINE INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS.
