APPLE REPORTS FIRST QUARTER RESULTS. CUPERTINO, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--APPLE® TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FISCAL 2023 FIRST QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2022. THE COMPANY POSTED QUARTERLY REVENUE OF $117.2 BILLION, DOWN 5 PERCENT YEAR OVER YEAR, AND QUARTERLY EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE OF $1.88.
