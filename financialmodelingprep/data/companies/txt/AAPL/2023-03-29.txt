APPLE'S WORLDWIDE DEVELOPERS CONFERENCE RETURNS JUNE 5, 2023. CUPERTINO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--APPLE® TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL HOST ITS ANNUAL WORLDWIDE DEVELOPERS CONFERENCE (WWDC) IN AN ONLINE FORMAT FROM JUNE 5 THROUGH 9, 2023, WITH AN OPPORTUNITY FOR DEVELOPERS AND STUDENTS TO CELEBRATE IN PERSON AT A SPECIAL EXPERIENCE AT APPLE PARK ON OPENING DAY. FREE FOR ALL DEVELOPERS, WWDC23 WILL SPOTLIGHT THE LATEST IOS, IPADOS®, MACOS®, WATCHOS®, AND TVOS® ADVANCEMENTS.
