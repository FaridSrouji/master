MONICA LOZANO JOINS APPLE'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. CUPERTINO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--APPLE® TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MONICA LOZANO, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF COLLEGE FUTURES FOUNDATION, HAS BEEN ELECTED TO APPLE'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. LOZANO BRINGS WITH HER A BROAD RANGE OF LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCE IN THE PUBLIC AND PRIVATE SECTORS, AS WELL AS A LONG AND STORIED TRACK RECORD AS A CHAMPION FOR EQUITY, OPPORTUNITY, AND REPRESENTATION.
