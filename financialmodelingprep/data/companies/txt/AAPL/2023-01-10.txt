JUNIPER RESEARCH: ESIM MARKET TO BE WORTH OVER $4 BILLION GLOBALLY IN 2023, AS APPLE INNOVATION DISRUPTS SMARTPHONE SECTOR. BASINGSTOKE, ENGLAND--( BUSINESS WIRE )--A NEW JUNIPER RESEARCH STUDY HAS FOUND THE VALUE OF THE GLOBAL ESIM MARKET WILL INCREASE FROM $4.7 BILLION IN 2023, TO $16.3 BILLION BY 2027. THE NEW REPORT OFFERS THE MOST COMPREHENSIVE ANALYSIS OF THE ESIMS (EMBEDDED SIMS) MARKET AVAILABLE TODAY.
