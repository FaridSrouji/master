APPLE BANK PROVIDES FINANCIAL SERVICES TO UKRAINIAN NATIONALS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--APPLE BANK ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT UKRAINIAN NATIONALS WHO HAVE RECENTLY ARRIVED IN THE NEW YORK CITY AREA AND HOPE TO ACCESS FINANCIAL SERVICES ARE NOW ELIGIBLE TO OPEN AN ACCOUNT WITH THE BANK BY VISITING ANY OF ITS 82 BRANCH LOCATIONS. THIS PROVIDES THEM WITH ACCESS TO THE FUNDAMENTAL BANKING SERVICES NEEDED TO PAY BILLS, MAKE PAYMENTS, WITHDRAW MONEY AND ESTABLISH PAYROLL DIRECT DEPOSIT.
