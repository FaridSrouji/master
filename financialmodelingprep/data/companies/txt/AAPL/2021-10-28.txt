APPLE REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER RESULTS. CUPERTINO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--APPLE® TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FISCAL 2021 FOURTH QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 25, 2021. THE COMPANY POSTED A SEPTEMBER QUARTER REVENUE RECORD OF $83.4 BILLION, UP 29 PERCENT YEAR OVER YEAR, AND QUARTERLY EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE OF $1.24. “THIS YEAR WE LAUNCHED OUR MOST POWERFUL PRODUCTS EVER, FROM M1-POWERED MACS TO AN IPHONE 13 LINEUP THAT IS SETTING A NEW STANDARD FOR PERFORMANCE AND EMPOWERING OUR CUSTOMERS TO CREATE AND CONNECT IN NEW WAY
