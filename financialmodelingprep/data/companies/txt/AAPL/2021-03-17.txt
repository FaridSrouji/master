APPLE'S $4.7 BILLION GREEN BOND SPEND IS HELPING TO CREATE 1.2 GIGAWATTS OF CLEAN POWER. CUPERTINO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--APPLE®'S NEWLY COMPLETED RENEWABLE PROJECTS, PART OF THE COMPANY'S PLANNED $4.7 BILLION GREEN BOND SPEND, ARE BRINGING CLEAN ENERGY TO LOCAL COMMUNITIES WHILE REDUCING CARBON EMISSIONS. IN 2020, APPLE FUNDED 17 GREEN BOND PROJECTS THAT WILL AVOID AN AVERAGE OF 921,000 METRIC TONS OF CARBON EMISSIONS ANNUALLY, WHICH IS EQUIVALENT TO REMOVING NEARLY 200,000 CARS FROM THE ROAD.
