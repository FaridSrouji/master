ALIPAY NOW SUPPORTS USERS TO LINK ALIPAY TO THEIR APPLE ID IN A ONE-STOP WAY WITHIN ITS APP. ALIPAY APP IS THE WORLD'S FIRST THIRD-PARTY PAYMENT APP TO LAUNCH THIS NEW FEATURE TO GREATLY OPTIMIZE IPHONE CUSTOMERS' SHOPPING EXPERIENCE.
