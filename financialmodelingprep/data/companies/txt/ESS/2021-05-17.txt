ESSEX PROPERTY TRUST PRICES $300 MILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ESSEX PROPERTY TRUST, INC. (NYSE:ESS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS OPERATING PARTNERSHIP, ESSEX PORTFOLIO, L.P. (THE “ISSUER”), PRICED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF $300 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.550% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE “NOTES”). THE NOTES WERE PRICED AT 99.367% OF PAR VALUE WITH A YIELD TO MATURITY OF 2.622%.
