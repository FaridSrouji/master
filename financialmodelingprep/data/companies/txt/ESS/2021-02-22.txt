ESSEX PROPERTY TRUST PRICES $450 MILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ESSEX PROPERTY TRUST, INC. (NYSE:ESS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS OPERATING PARTNERSHIP, ESSEX PORTFOLIO, L.P. (THE “ISSUER”), PRICED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF $450 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 1.700% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (THE “NOTES”). THE NOTES WERE PRICED AT 99.423% OF PAR VALUE WITH A YIELD TO MATURITY OF 1.788%.
