ESSEX ANNOUNCES ITS 27TH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASE. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ESSEX PROPERTY TRUST, INC. (NYSE:ESS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A $0.05 PER SHARE INCREASE TO ITS ANNUAL CASH DIVIDEND. THIS REPRESENTS THE 27TH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASE. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A FIRST QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $2.09 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON APRIL 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MARCH 31, 2021.
