121ST COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND INCREASE DECLARED BY REALTY INCOME. SAN DIEGO , JUNE 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED AN INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK MONTHLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.2555 PER SHARE FROM $0.2550 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JULY 14, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 3, 2023.
