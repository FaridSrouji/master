REALTY INCOME ANNOUNCES EXPANSION INTO CONTINENTAL EUROPE: STRATEGIC SALE-LEASEBACK TRANSACTION IN SPAIN. SAN DIEGO, SEPT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS CLOSED ON A €93 MILLION SALE-LEASEBACK TRANSACTION IN SPAIN, CONSISTING OF SEVEN PROPERTIES UNDER LONG-TERM NET LEASE AGREEMENTS WITH CARREFOUR AND ITS REAL ESTATE SUBSIDIARY CARREFOUR PROPERTY.
