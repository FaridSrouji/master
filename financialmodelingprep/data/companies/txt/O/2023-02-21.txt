REALTY INCOME AND PLENTY ANNOUNCE STRATEGIC ALLIANCE FOR UP TO $1 BILLION OF VERTICAL FARM DEVELOPMENT. INITIAL INVESTMENT WILL SUPPORT PLENTY'S DEVELOPMENT OF THE WORLD'S LARGEST INDOOR VERTICAL FARM CAMPUS SAN DIEGO AND SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, CALIF. , FEB. 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, AND PLENTY UNLIMITED INC. ("PLENTY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE ENTERED INTO A STRATEGIC REAL ESTATE ALLIANCE TO SUPPORT THE DEVELOPMENT OF PLENTY'S INDOOR VERTICAL FARMS.
