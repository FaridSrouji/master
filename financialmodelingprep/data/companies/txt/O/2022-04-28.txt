REALTY INCOME ANNOUNCES EXPANDED $4.25 BILLION UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY. SAN DIEGO, APRIL 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS CLOSED ON AN AMENDED $4.25 BILLION MULTICURRENCY UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY, WHICH REPLACES THE COMPANY'S EXISTING $3.0 BILLION UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY. THE CAPACITY OF THE REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY CAN BE INCREASED TO $5.25 BILLION WITH AN ACCORDION EXPANSION FEATURE, WHICH IS SUBJECT TO OBTAINING LENDER COMMITMENTS.
