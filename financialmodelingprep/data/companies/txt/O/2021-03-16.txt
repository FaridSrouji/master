110TH COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND INCREASE DECLARED BY REALTY INCOME. SAN DIEGO, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED AN INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK MONTHLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.235 PER SHARE FROM $0.2345 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON APRIL 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 1, 2021.
