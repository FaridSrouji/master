REALTY INCOME PUBLISHES SECOND ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. SAN DIEGO , APRIL 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT PUBLISHED ITS SECOND ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT WHICH COVERS ITS 2021 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE ("ESG") INITIATIVES AND PROGRESS. THE REPORT IS PREPARED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE GRI STANDARDS AND BUILT TO ALIGN WITH OTHER DISCLOSURE EFFORTS, INVESTOR FEEDBACK, ANNUAL RATINGS AND RANKINGS SUBMISSIONS, AND THE UNITED NATIONS SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS.
