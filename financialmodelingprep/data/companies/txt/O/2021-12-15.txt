114TH COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND INCREASE DECLARED BY REALTY INCOME. SAN DIEGO, DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED AN INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK MONTHLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.2465 PER SHARE FROM $0.246 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 14, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JANUARY 3, 2022.
