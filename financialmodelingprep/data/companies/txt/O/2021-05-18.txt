611TH CONSECUTIVE COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND DECLARED BY REALTY INCOME. SAN DIEGO, MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED THE 611TH CONSECUTIVE COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND. THE DIVIDEND AMOUNT OF $0.235 PER SHARE, REPRESENTING AN ANNUALIZED AMOUNT OF $2.82 PER SHARE, IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 1, 2021.
