626TH CONSECUTIVE COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND DECLARED BY REALTY INCOME. SAN DIEGO , AUG. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED THE 626TH CONSECUTIVE COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND. THE DIVIDEND AMOUNT OF $0.2475 PER SHARE, REPRESENTING AN ANNUALIZED AMOUNT OF $2.97 PER SHARE, IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF SEPTEMBER 1, 2022.
