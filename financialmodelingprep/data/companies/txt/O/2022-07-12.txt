625TH CONSECUTIVE COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND DECLARED BY REALTY INCOME. SAN DIEGO , JULY 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED THE 625TH CONSECUTIVE COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND. THE DIVIDEND AMOUNT OF $0.2475 PER SHARE, REPRESENTING AN ANNUALIZED AMOUNT OF $2.97 PER SHARE, IS PAYABLE ON AUGUST 15, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUGUST 1, 2022.
