REALTY INCOME PRICES DEBUT €1.1 BILLION DUAL-TRANCHE OFFERING OF EURO-DENOMINATED SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES. SAN DIEGO , JUNE 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF A PUBLIC OFFERING OF €550 MILLION OF 4.875% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE JULY 6, 2030 (THE "2030 NOTES"), AND €550 MILLION OF 5.125% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE JULY 6, 2034 (THE "2034 NOTES"). THE PUBLIC OFFERING PRICE FOR THE 2030 NOTES WAS 99.421% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT FOR AN EFFECTIVE ANNUAL YIELD TO MATURITY OF 4.975%, AND THE PUBLIC OFFERING PRICE FOR THE 2034 NOTES WAS 99.506% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT FOR AN EFFECTIVE ANNUAL YIELD TO MATURITY OF 5.185%.
