628TH CONSECUTIVE COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND DECLARED BY REALTY INCOME. SAN DIEGO , OCT. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED THE 628TH CONSECUTIVE COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND. THE DIVIDEND AMOUNT OF $0.248 PER SHARE, REPRESENTING AN ANNUALIZED AMOUNT OF $2.976 PER SHARE, IS PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 15, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF NOVEMBER 1, 2022.
