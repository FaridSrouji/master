116TH COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND INCREASE DECLARED BY REALTY INCOME. SAN DIEGO , JUNE 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED AN INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK MONTHLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.2475 PER SHARE FROM $0.247 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JULY 15, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 1, 2022.
