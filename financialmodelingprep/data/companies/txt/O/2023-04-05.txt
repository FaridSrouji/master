REALTY INCOME PRICES $1.0 BILLION OFFERING OF DUAL-TRANCHE SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES. SAN DIEGO , APRIL 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $400 MILLION OF 4.70% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE DECEMBER 15, 2028 (THE "2028 NOTES") AND $600 MILLION OF 4.90% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE JULY 15, 2033 (THE "2033 NOTES"). THE PUBLIC OFFERING PRICE FOR THE 2028 NOTES WAS 98.949% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT FOR AN EFFECTIVE SEMI-ANNUAL YIELD TO MATURITY OF 4.912% AND THE PUBLIC OFFERING PRICE FOR THE 2033 NOTES WAS 98.020% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT FOR AN EFFECTIVE SEMI-ANNUAL YIELD TO MATURITY OF 5.148%.
