FANNIE MAE ANNOUNCES PRISCILLA ALMODOVAR AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. FINANCIAL SERVICES VETERAN BRINGS MORE THAN 30 YEARS OF FINANCE, REAL ESTATE, AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT EXPERTISE AND A STRONG COMMITMENT TO AFFORDABLE HOUSING WASHINGTON, SEPT. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FANNIE MAE (OTCQB: FNMA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS APPOINTED PRISCILLA ALMODOVAR AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER (CEO) AND MEMBER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 5, 2022.
