112TH COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND INCREASE DECLARED BY REALTY INCOME. SAN DIEGO, SEPT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED AN INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK MONTHLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.236 PER SHARE FROM $0.2355 PER SHARE.
