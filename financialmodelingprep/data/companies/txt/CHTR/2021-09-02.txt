CHARTER AWARDS $1 MILLION 2021 SPECTRUM DIGITAL EDUCATION GRANTS TO 49 NONPROFITS SUPPORTING BROADBAND LITERACY. STAMFORD, CONN., SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHARTER COMMUNICATIONS, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RECIPIENTS OF ITS 2021 SPECTRUM DIGITAL EDUCATION GRANTS, AWARDING A TOTAL OF $1 MILLION TO 49 NONPROFITS IN 16 STATES AND WASHINGTON, D.C.
