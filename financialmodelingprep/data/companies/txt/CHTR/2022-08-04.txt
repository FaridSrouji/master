CHARTER PRICES $1.5 BILLION SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES. STAMFORD, CONN. , AUG. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHARTER COMMUNICATIONS, INC. (NASDAQ: CHTR) (ALONG WITH ITS SUBSIDIARIES, "CHARTER") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS SUBSIDIARIES, CCO HOLDINGS, LLC AND CCO HOLDINGS CAPITAL CORP. (COLLECTIVELY, THE "ISSUERS"), HAVE PRICED $1.5 BILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2029 (THE "NOTES").
