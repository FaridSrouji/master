CHARTER COMMUNICATIONS APPOINTS JOHN BICKHAM VICE CHAIRMAN. STAMFORD, CONN., OCT. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHARTER COMMUNICATIONS, INC  (NASDAQ: CHTR)  TODAY ANNOUNCED A SERIES OF EXECUTIVE PROMOTIONS AND APPOINTMENTS TO ITS LEADERSHIP TEAM WITH THE NEWS THAT VETERAN CABLE EXECUTIVE JOHN BICKHAM WAS NAMED VICE CHAIRMAN AHEAD OF HIS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED RETIREMENT AT THE END OF 2022.
