CHARTER PRICES $2.0 BILLION SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES. STAMFORD, CONN., AUG. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHARTER COMMUNICATIONS, INC. (NASDAQ: CHTR) (ALONG WITH ITS SUBSIDIARIES, "CHARTER") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS SUBSIDIARIES, CCO HOLDINGS, LLC AND CCO HOLDINGS CAPITAL CORP. (COLLECTIVELY, THE "ISSUERS"), HAVE PRICED $2.0 BILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2034 (THE "NOTES").
