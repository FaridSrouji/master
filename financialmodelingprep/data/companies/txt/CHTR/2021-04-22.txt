CHARTER CLOSES $1.0 BILLION SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES. STAMFORD, CONN., APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHARTER COMMUNICATIONS, INC. (NASDAQ: CHTR) (ALONG WITH ITS SUBSIDIARIES, "CHARTER") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS SUBSIDIARIES, CCO HOLDINGS, LLC AND CCO HOLDINGS CAPITAL CORP., HAVE CLOSED ON $1.0 BILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2033 (THE "NOTES").
