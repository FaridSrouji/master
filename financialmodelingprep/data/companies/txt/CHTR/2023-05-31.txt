SPECTRUM PARTNERS WITH OVATION TV TO SUPPORT DULUTH ART INSTITUTE WITH $10,000 STAND FOR THE ARTS AWARD. DULUTH, MINN. , MAY 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SPECTRUM IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE THE DULUTH ART INSTITUTE IS THE RECIPIENT OF $10,000 THROUGH THE 2022-2023 STAND FOR THE ARTS AWARDS IN PARTNERSHIP WITH OVATION TV THAT RECOGNIZES LOCAL ARTS, CULTURAL AND EDUCATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS AND PROGRAMS.
