AMAZON SUES ‘FAKE REVIEW BROKERS' WHO ATTEMPT TO PROFIT FROM GENERATING MISLEADING AND FRAUDULENT REVIEWS. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMAZON (NASDAQ: AMZN) HAS FILED LAWSUITS AGAINST FAKE REVIEW BROKERS WHO ORCHESTRATE THE POSTING OF INCENTIVIZED AND MISLEADING PRODUCT REVIEWS, IN EXCHANGE FOR MONEY OR FREE PRODUCTS. THE LAWSUITS AIM TO SHUT DOWN TWO MAJOR FAKE REVIEW BROKERS, APPSALLY AND REBATEST, WHO HELPED MISLEAD SHOPPERS BY HAVING THEIR MEMBERS TRY TO POST FAKE REVIEWS IN STORES SUCH AS AMAZON, EBAY, WALMART, AND ETSY. THIS LEGAL ACTION IS ONE PART OF AMAZON'S COMPREHENSIVE AND PROACTIVE EFFORT
