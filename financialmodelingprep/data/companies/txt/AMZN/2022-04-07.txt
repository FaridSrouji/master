FOR LOVE & COUNTRY DOCUMENTARY BY AMAZON MUSIC OUT TODAY. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMAZON MUSIC ANNOUNCED TODAY THE RELEASE OF FOR LOVE & COUNTRY, A NEW AMAZON MUSIC ORIGINAL FEATURE-LENGTH DOCUMENTARY, WHICH PROCLAIMS COUNTRY MUSIC HAS ALWAYS BEEN BLACK MUSIC. FOR LOVE & COUNTRY OBSERVES COUNTRY MUSIC'S COMPLICATED PAST AND HOW THE MUSIC INDUSTRY ITSELF HELPED TO ENSHRINE A SKEWED VERSION OF THE GENRE, ITS ARTISTS, AND FANS.
