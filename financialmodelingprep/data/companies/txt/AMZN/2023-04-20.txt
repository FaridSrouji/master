AMAZON ANNOUNCES ANTI-COUNTERFEITING EXCHANGE TO HELP ELIMINATE COUNTERFEITS ACROSS THE RETAIL INDUSTRY. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMAZON (NASDAQ: AMZN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ANTI-COUNTERFEITING EXCHANGE (ACX), AN INDUSTRY COLLABORATION DESIGNED TO MAKE IT SAFER TO SHOP ONLINE AND MORE DIFFICULT FOR COUNTERFEITERS TO MOVE AMONG DIFFERENT STORES TO ATTEMPT TO SELL THEIR COUNTERFEIT GOODS. ACX ALLOWS PARTICIPATING STORES TO SHARE INFORMATION ABOUT CONFIRMED COUNTERFEITERS WHO ATTEMPTED TO USE THEIR SERVICES TO TRY TO SELL COUNTERFEIT PRODUCTS.
