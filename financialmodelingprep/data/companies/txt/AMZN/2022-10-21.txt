HAWAIIAN AIRLINES ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT WITH AMAZON TO OPERATE FREIGHTER AIRCRAFT. TO HOST INVESTOR CONFERENCE CALL HONOLULU , OCT. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HAWAIIAN HOLDINGS, INC. (NASDAQ: HA) (THE "COMPANY"), THE PARENT COMPANY OF HAWAIIAN AIRLINES, INC. ("HAWAIIAN"), TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT WITH AMAZON.COM, INC. AND ITS SUBSIDIARIES (NASDAQ: AMZN) ("AMAZON") TO OPERATE AND MAINTAIN AN INITIAL FLEET OF 10 AIRBUS A330-300 FREIGHTERS STARTING IN THE FALL OF 2023. HAWAIIAN WILL MAINTAIN AND FLY AMAZON'S A330S UNDER HAWAIIAN'S FAA AIR CARRIER CERTIFICATE TO MOVE CARGO BETWEEN AIRPORTS NEAR THE ONLINE RETAILER'S OPERATIONS FACILITIES.
