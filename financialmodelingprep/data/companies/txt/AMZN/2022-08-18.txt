AMZN LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES AMAZON.COM, INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , AUG. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN AMAZON.COM, INC. ("AMAZON" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: AMZN) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF AMAZON INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD.
