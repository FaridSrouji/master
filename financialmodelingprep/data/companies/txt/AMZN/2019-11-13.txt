AMAZON EXPANDS IN LAS VEGAS AND ANNOUNCES PLANS TO CREATE 125 NEW CORPORATE JOBS. AMAZON ANNOUNCED AN EXPANSION OF THE COMPANY’S WORKFORCE IN LAS VEGAS WITH PLANS TO CREATE 125 ADDITIONAL CORPORATE JOBS IN THE CITY. TO ACCOMMODATE THIS GROWTH, THE COMPANY RECENTLY OPENED A NEW 30,000 SQUARE FOOT OFFICE IN TOWN SQUARE IN DOWNTOWN LAS VEGAS. THIS EXPANSION WILL ALLOW AMAZON TO MORE THAN DOUBLE ITS CORPORATE WORKFORCE IN THE AREA – WITH TEAMS BASED IN THE NEW LAS VEGAS OFFICE SUPPORTING THE COMPANY’S HUMAN RESOURCES ORGANIZATION.
