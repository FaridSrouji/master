AMAZON TO BRING LOW CARBON ELECTROFUELS TO ITS TRUCKING FLEET. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMAZON (NASDAQ: AMZN) HAS SIGNED AN AGREEMENT WITH RENEWABLE FUELS TECHNOLOGY COMPANY INFINIUM TO BEGIN POWERING AMAZON'S TRANSPORTATION FLEET WITH ULTRA-LOW CARBON ELECTROFUELS BEGINNING IN 2023. INFINIUM IS EXPECTED TO INITIALLY SUPPLY ENOUGH ELECTROFUELS, WHICH ARE A FOSSIL-BASED FUEL ALTERNATIVE CREATED WITH CARBON WASTE AND RENEWABLE POWER, TO BEGIN POWERING AMAZON TRUCKS IN LIEU OF DIESEL FUEL FOR APPROXIMATELY 5 MILLION MILES OF TRAVEL PER YEAR.
