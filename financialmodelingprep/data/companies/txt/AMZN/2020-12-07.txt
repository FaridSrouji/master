AWS ANNOUNCES PLANS TO OPEN SECOND REGION IN AUSTRALIA. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMAZON WEB SERVICES, INC. (AWS), AN AMAZON.COM COMPANY (NASDAQ: AMZN), TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO OPEN A SECOND INFRASTRUCTURE REGION IN AUSTRALIA IN THE SECOND HALF OF 2022. THE NEW AWS ASIA PACIFIC (MELBOURNE) REGION, WHICH WILL CONSIST OF THREE AVAILABILITY ZONES (AZS) AT LAUNCH, WILL JOIN THE EXISTING 25 AVAILABILITY ZONES IN EIGHT AWS REGIONS ACROSS ASIA PACIFIC IN AUSTRALIA, CHINA, INDIA, JAPAN, KOREA, AND SINGAPORE.
