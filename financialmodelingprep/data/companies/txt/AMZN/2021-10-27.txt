DATA VAULT HOLDINGS INC. APPOINTS ED CUSHING CHAIRMAN TO THE ADVISORY BOARD. NEW YORK, OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DATA VAULT HOLDINGS INC. ANNOUNCES THE FORMATION OF ITS ADVISORY BOARD AND APPOINTED ED CUSHING, GLOBAL ACCOUNT MANAGER AT AMAZON WEB SERVICES (AWS), A DIVISION OF AMAZON.COM, INC. (NASDAQ: AMZN), AS ITS INAUGURAL MEMBER. CUSHING WILL OFFER STRATEGIC ADVICE AND CONTINUE TO GROW THE TECHNOLOGY COMPANY'S ADVISORY BOARD BY IDENTIFYING A DIVERSE GROUP OF INNOVATIVE PROFESSIONALS TO WORK CLOSELY WITH DATA VAULT HOLDING'S MANAGEMENT TEAM TO ACCELERATE MARKETING DEVELOPMENT AND BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES.
