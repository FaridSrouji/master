AMAZON PRIME VIDEO MAKES HISTORY AS THE FIRST STREAMING SERVICE TO SECURE AN EXCLUSIVE NATIONAL BROADCAST PACKAGE FROM THE NFL, BECOMING THE HOME FOR THURSDAY NIGHT FOOTBALL BEGINNING IN 2023. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMAZON (NASDAQ: AMZN) AND THE NATIONAL FOOTBALL LEAGUE (NFL) MADE HISTORY TODAY BY ANNOUNCING A 10-YEAR AGREEMENT TO EXCLUSIVELY BROADCAST 15 THURSDAY NIGHT FOOTBALL GAMES AND ONE PRE-SEASON GAME PER YEAR ON PRIME VIDEO IN THE UNITED STATES. THE PRIME VIDEO DEAL BEGINS IN 2023 AND IS THE NFL'S FIRST EXCLUSIVE NATIONAL BROADCAST PACKAGE WITH A DIGITAL STREAMING SERVICE.
