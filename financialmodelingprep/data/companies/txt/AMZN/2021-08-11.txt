WYNDHAM HOTELS & RESORTS SELECTS AWS AS ITS PREFERRED CLOUD PROVIDER TO ENHANCE GUEST EXPERIENCES. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, AMAZON WEB SERVICES, INC. (AWS), AN AMAZON.COM, INC. COMPANY (NASDAQ: AMZN), ANNOUNCED A GLOBAL COLLABORATION WITH WYNDHAM HOTELS & RESORTS, INC.
