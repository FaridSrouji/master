AMAZON.COM SAYS FILED 3 LAWSUITS IN GEORGIA, MICHIGAN, AND TEXAS TO STOP FRAUDULENT AFFILIATE MARKETING SCHEMES. AMAZON.COM - FILED 3 LAWSUITS IN GEORGIA, MICHIGAN, AND TEXAS TO STOP FRAUDULENT AFFILIATE MARKETING SCHEMES.AMAZON.COM - SCHEMES IDENTIFIED IN LAWSUITS SPAMMED EMAIL ADDRESSES WITH AMAZON BRANDED-EMAILS TO ENTICE THOSE RECEIVING EMAILS TO CLICK ON LINKS.AMAZON.COM - IN A SEPARATE SCHEME, STOPPED FAKE AMAZON-BRANDED EMAIL CAMPAIGNS RUN BY COS, INDIVIDUALS IN COLORADO & CALIFORNIA.
BHARTI AIRTEL, ON REPORT AMAZON IN TALKS TO BUY STAKE, SAYS NO SUCH PROPOSAL IN CONSIDERATION AT THIS STAGE. BHARTI AIRTEL CLARIFIES ON REPORT AMAZON IN TALKS TO BUY STAKE, SAYS AT THIS STAGE, NO SUCH PROPOSAL IN CONSIDERATION.
