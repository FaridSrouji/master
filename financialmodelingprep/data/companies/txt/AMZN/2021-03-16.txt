NEW IPSOS STUDY FINDS 8 IN 10 AMERICANS THINK THE FEDERAL MINIMUM WAGE IS TOO LOW AND TWO-THIRDS SUPPORT INCREASING MINIMUM WAGE TO $15 PER HOUR. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, GLOBAL MARKET RESEARCH FIRM IPSOS RELEASED A NEW, COMPREHENSIVE FEDERAL MINIMUM WAGE STUDY, WHICH POLLED MORE THAN 6,000 AMERICANS ON THEIR KNOWLEDGE OF AND OPINIONS ON THE CURRENT FEDERAL MINIMUM WAGE OF $7.25 AN HOUR. THE STUDY CONFIRMED THAT THE VAST MAJORITY OF AMERICANS AGREE THAT THE FEDERAL MINIMUM WAGE IS TOO LOW AND SHOULD BE INCREASED TO AT LEAST $15 PER HOUR.
