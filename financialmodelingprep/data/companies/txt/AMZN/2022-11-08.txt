WINNERS ANNOUNCED AT THE 2022 CFDA FASHION AWARDS. CATHERINE HOLSTEIN OF KHAITE NAMED AMERICAN WOMENSWEAR DESIGNER OF THE YEAR AND EMILY ADAMS BODE AUJLA OF BODE NAMED AMERICAN MENSWEAR DESIGNER OF THE YEAR NEW YORK , NOV. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COUNCIL OF FASHION DESIGNERS OF AMERICA [CFDA] CELEBRATED THE WINNERS AND HONOREES OF THE 2022 CFDA FASHION AWARDS IN PARTNERSHIP WITH AMAZON FASHION AT CIPRIANI SOUTH STREET AT CASA CIPRIANI IN MANHATTAN. NATASHA LYONNE SERVED AS THE EVENING'S HOST.
