DEVON ENERGY APPOINTS MICHAEL MEARS AND GENNIFER KELLY TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. OKLAHOMA CITY, JAN. 03, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY CORP. (NYSE: DVN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MICHAEL MEARS AND GENNIFER KELLY HAVE JOINED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MICHAEL MEARS IS THE FORMER CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF MAGELLAN MIDSTREAM PARTNERS. GENNIFER KELLY PREVIOUSLY HELD THE ROLE OF CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER AND SVP OF WESTERN MIDSTREAM PARTNERS.
