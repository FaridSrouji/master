DEVON ENERGY COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF WILLISTON BASIN BOLT-ON. OKLAHOMA CITY, JULY 21, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY CORP. (NYSE: DVN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF THE LEASEHOLD INTEREST AND RELATED ASSETS OF RIMROCK OIL AND GAS, LP IN THE WILLISTON BASIN FOR A TOTAL CASH CONSIDERATION OF $865 MILLION, LESS PURCHASE PRICE ADJUSTMENTS.
