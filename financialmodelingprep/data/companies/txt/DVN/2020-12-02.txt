DEVON ENERGY ANNOUNCES FIRST-QUARTER 2021 CASH DIVIDEND FOR COMMON STOCKHOLDERS. OKLAHOMA CITY, DEC. 02, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY CORP. (NYSE: DVN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON DEVON'S COMMON STOCK FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 31, 2021, AT A RATE OF $0.11 PER SHARE BASED ON A RECORD DATE OF MARCH 15, 2021.
