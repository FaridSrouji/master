DEVON ENERGY ANNOUNCES SECOND-QUARTER DIVIDEND FOR COMMON STOCKHOLDERS. OKLAHOMA CITY, AUG. 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY CORP. (NYSE: DVN) ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A FIXED-PLUS-VARIABLE DIVIDEND OF $1.55 PER SHARE BASED ON THE COMPANY'S SECOND-QUARTER FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE. THIS RECORD PAYOUT REPRESENTS A 22 PERCENT INCREASE FROM THE PREVIOUS QUARTER.
