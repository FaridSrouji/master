DEVON ENERGY ANNOUNCES EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN RETIREMENT AND SUCCESSION PLAN. OKLAHOMA CITY, SEPT. 15, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY CORP. (NYSE: DVN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS EXPECTS TO APPOINT BARBARA BAUMANN AS CHAIR OF THE BOARD, SUCCEEDING DAVE HAGER, WHO HAS NOTIFIED THE BOARD OF HIS PLANS TO RETIRE FROM THE BOARD IN JANUARY 2023. THIS TRANSITION ALIGNS WITH DEVON'S CONTINUING FOCUS ON STRONG CORPORATE VALUES, GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES, AND STRATEGIC VISION.
