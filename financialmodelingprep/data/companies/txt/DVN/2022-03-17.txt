DEVON ENERGY PLEDGES UP TO $20 MILLION TO AID HUMANITARIAN EFFORTS IN UKRAINE. OKLAHOMA CITY, MARCH 17, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY A PLEDGE OF UP TO $20 MILLION TO SUPPORT HUMANITARIAN EFFORTS IN UKRAINE AND NEIGHBORING COUNTRIES SUPPORTING UKRAINIAN REFUGEES. THESE DONATIONS WILL BE SPLIT AMONG SEVERAL ORGANIZATIONS PROVIDING EMERGENCY MEDICAL CARE, FOOD SUPPLIES, FINANCIAL SUPPORT, AND HOUSING FOR DISPLACED CIVILIANS AND REFUGEES IN THE REGION.
