DEVON ENERGY REPORTS FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2022 RESULTS; DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AND RAISES FIXED DIVIDEND BY 11 PERCENT IN 2023. OKLAHOMA CITY, FEB. 14, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY CORP. (NYSE: DVN) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2022. THE COMPANY ALSO DECLARED ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DEVON'S EARNINGS RELEASE, SUPPLEMENTAL FINANCIAL TABLES, GUIDANCE AND RELATED EARNINGS PRESENTATION CAN BE ACCESSED VIA THE INVESTOR RELATIONS SECTION OF DEVON'S WEBSITE, WWW.DEVONENERGY.COM.
