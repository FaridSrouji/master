DEVON ENERGY REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER 2020 FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL RESULTS.DEVON ENERGY CORP - 2020 CAPITAL INVESTMENT REDUCED 45 PERCENT FROM ORIGINAL PLAN TO $1 BILLION.DEVON ENERGY CORP - 2020 OIL PRODUCTION EXPECTED TO BE ESSENTIALLY FLAT COMPARED TO 2019.DEVON ENERGY CORP - 2020 GUIDANCE ASSUMES 10,000 BARRELS PER DAY OF OIL CURTAILMENTS IN THE SECOND QUARTER.DEVON ENERGY CORP- QTRLY LOSS PER SHARE  $4.82.DEVON ENERGY CORP - 2020 HEDGES PROTECT APPROXIMATELY 90 PERCENT OF OIL VOLUMES AT $42 PER BARREL.DEVON ENERGY CORP - 2020 FULL-YEAR COST OUTLOOK IMPROVED BY $250 MILLION, INCLUDES EXECUTIVE PAY REDUCTIONS.DEVON ENERGY CORP - QTRLY ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.13.DEVON ENERGY CORP - BARNETT SHALE ASSET SALE PROCESS ON TRACK TO CLOSE BY YEAR-END.DEVON ENERGY - QTRLY LOSS WAS ATTRIBUTABLE TO $2.8 BILLION OF NON-CASH IMPAIRMENT CHARGES DUE TO ASSET EVALUATIONS.DEVON ENERGY CORP - FOR SECOND QUARTER OF 2020, DEVON EXPECTS TO INVEST APPROXIMATELY $200 MILLION TO $250 MILLION OF CAPITAL.DEVON ENERGY - WITH REVISED CAPITAL PROGRAM, EXPECTS Q2 OIL PRODUCTION TO AVERAGE 145,000 TO 155,000 BARRELS PER DAY.DEVON ENERGY - . INCLUDED WITHIN CO’S Q2 PRODUCTION OUTLOOK IS THE ELECTION TO CURTAIL 10,000 BARRELS PER DAY DUE TO LOW COMMODITY PRICES.DEVON ENERGY - GIVEN THE ONGOING PRICE VOLATILITY, CURTAILMENT DECISIONS ARE EXPECTED TO BE MADE ON A MONTH-TO-MONTH BASIS.
