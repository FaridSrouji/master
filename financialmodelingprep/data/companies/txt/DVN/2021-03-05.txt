DEVON ENERGY ANNOUNCES BOARD CHANGE. OKLAHOMA CITY, MARCH 05, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY CORP. (NYSE: DVN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT D. MARTIN PHILLIPS, A MANAGING PARTNER OF THE ENCAP INVESTMENTS, L.P. (ENCAP), WILL RESIGN FROM THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. THE RESIGNATION IS FOLLOWING A REDUCTION IN ENCAP'S HOLDINGS IN DEVON THROUGH DISPOSITION TRANSACTIONS THAT OCCURRED ON FEB. 22, 2021.
