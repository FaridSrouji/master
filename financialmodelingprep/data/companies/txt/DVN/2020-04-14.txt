DEVON ENERGY SAYS AGREED TO SELL BARNETT SHALE ASSETS TO BKV FOR UP TO $830 MLN OF TOTAL PROCEEDS. DEVON ENERGY ANNOUNCES AMENDED BARNETT SHALE ASSET SALE AGREEMENT.DEVON ENERGY - AGREED TO SELL BARNETT SHALE ASSETS TO BKV FOR UP TO $830 MILLION OF TOTAL PROCEEDS.DEVON ENERGY CORP -  WITH AMENDED TERMS, DEVON WILL RECEIVE AN INCREASED DEPOSIT OF $170 MILLION FROM BKV.DEVON ENERGY - AGREEMENT ALSO PROVIDES FOR CONTINGENT CASH PAYMENTS OF UP TO $260 MILLION BASED UPON FUTURE COMMODITY PRICES.DEVON ENERGY - CONTINGENT PAYMENT PERIOD COMMENCES ON JAN. 1, 2021 AND HAS A TERM OF 4 YEARS.
