THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION SELECTED THE EU DIGITAL IDENTITY WALLET CONSORTIUM FOR THE EU DIGITAL IDENTITY WALLET PILOTS. AVAST WILL CONTRIBUTE TO THIS INITIATIVE, POWERING DIGITAL FREEDOM FOR ONLINE CITIZENS PRAGUE AND TEMPE, ARIZ. , DEC. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE EU DIGITAL IDENTITY WALLET CONSORTIUM (EWC), WAS SELECTED BY THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION TO RUN ONE OF THE LARGE SCALE PILOTS FOR THE NEW EU DIGITAL IDENTITY WALLET (EUDI) ECOSYSTEM.
