‘VIALS OF HOPE' ARRIVE AT GENESIS HEALTHCARE-AFFILIATED RESERVOIR CENTER AS NURSING HOME RESIDENTS AND HEALTHCARE WORKERS BEGIN COVID-19 VACCINATIONS IN CONNECTICUT. KENNETT SQUARE, PA., DEC. 18, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RESIDENTS AND HEALTHCARE WORKERS AT THE RESERVOIR CENTER, A GENESIS HEALTHCARE- (NYSE:GEN) (GENESIS OR THE COMPANY) AFFILIATED NURSING HOME, BECAME THE FIRST LONG-TERM CARE FACILITY IN CONNECTICUT AND AMONG THE FIRST IN THE U.S. TO RECEIVE THE COVID-19 VACCINATION TODAY. GENESIS HEALTHCARE IS ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST PROVIDERS OF POST-ACUTE CARE.
