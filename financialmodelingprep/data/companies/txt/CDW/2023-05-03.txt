CDW DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.59 PER SHARE. LINCOLNSHIRE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CDW CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CDW), A LEADING MULTI-BRAND PROVIDER OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS TO BUSINESS, GOVERNMENT, EDUCATION AND HEALTHCARE CUSTOMERS IN THE UNITED STATES, THE UNITED KINGDOM AND CANADA, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.59 PER COMMON SHARE TO BE PAID ON JUNE 13, 2023 TO ALL STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 25, 2023. FUTURE DIVIDENDS WILL BE SUBJECT TO BOA.
