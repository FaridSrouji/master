CDW WELCOMES NEW CHIEF MARKETING AND DIGITAL OFFICER AND SVP OF STRATEGY AND CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT. LINCOLNSHIRE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CDW (NASDAQ: CDW), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ADDITION OF TWO NEW STRATEGY AND MARKETING LEADERS. ANAND RAO IS JOINING THE ORGANIZATION AS CHIEF MARKETING AND DIGITAL OFFICER WHILE MICHAEL DRORY HAS JOINED AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF STRATEGY AND CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT. BOTH LEADERS WILL SERVE ON THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE AND REPORT TO SONA CHAWLA, CHIEF GROWTH AND INNOVATION OFFICER.
