CDW WELCOMES STEVE O'BRIEN, VICE PRESIDENT, INVESTOR RELATIONS. LINCOLNSHIRE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CDW (NASDAQ: CDW), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT STEVE O'BRIEN HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS VICE PRESIDENT, INVESTOR RELATIONS. O'BRIEN JOINS CDW FROM ARROW ELECTRONICS, AN ELECTRONIC COMPONENTS AND TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS DISTRIBUTOR, WHERE HE LED INVESTOR RELATIONS FOR THE PAST EIGHT YEARS.
