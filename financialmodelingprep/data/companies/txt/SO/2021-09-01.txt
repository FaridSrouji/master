GEORGIA POWER ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS TO TAKE ACTION DURING NATIONAL PREPAREDNESS MONTH. ATLANTA, SEPT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS GEORGIA POWER'S STORM TEAM OF MORE THAN 500 WORKERS MOVE FROM MISSISSIPPI TO LOUISIANA TO CONTINUE ASSISTING WITH POWER RESTORATION FOLLOWING HURRICANE IDA, THE COMPANY IS ENCOURAGING ITS 2.6 MILLION CUSTOMERS TO PREPARE FOR POTENTIAL EMERGENCIES HERE AT HOME.
