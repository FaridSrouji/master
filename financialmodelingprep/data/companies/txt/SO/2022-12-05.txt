VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS PROCURES ADDITIONAL NEXT GENERATION NATURAL GAS AS PART OF ITS COMMITMENT TO REDUCE GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS. VIRGINIA BEACH, VA. , DEC. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH A CONTINUED COMMITMENT TO REDUCING GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS ACROSS THE NATURAL GAS VALUE CHAIN, VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS (VNG) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WILL INCREASE THE AMOUNT OF ITS NATURAL GAS SUPPLY THAT IS PROCURED, TRANSPORTED OR DELIVERED BY COMPANIES COMMITTED TO LOWERING METHANE EMISSIONS – VNG REFERS TO THIS NATURAL GAS AS "NEXT GENERATION NATURAL GAS.
