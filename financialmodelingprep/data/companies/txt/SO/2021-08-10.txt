GEORGIA POWER, ELECTRIC POWER RESEARCH INSTITUTE OPEN ASH BENEFICIAL USE CENTER. ATLANTA, AUG. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER, IN COLLABORATION WITH THE ELECTRIC POWER RESEARCH INSTITUTE (EPRI) AND SOUTHERN COMPANY, MARKED THE OPENING EARLIER THIS YEAR OF THE FIRST ASH BENEFICIAL USE CENTER (ABUC). THE ABUC WILL HOST PILOT PROJECTS AND LEAD CONTINUED TESTING OF TECHNOLOGIES TO POTENTIALLY FURTHER DEVELOP USEFUL PRODUCTS FROM RECYCLED COAL COMBUSTION PRODUCTS (CCPS) SUCH AS COAL ASH.
