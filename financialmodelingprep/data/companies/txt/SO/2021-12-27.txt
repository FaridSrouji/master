GEORGIA POWER AND MICROSOFT PARTNER TO PROVIDE MORE THAN $200,000 IN FUNDING TO SUPPORT CLARK ATLANTA UNIVERSITY'S SOLAR POWERED GENERATOR STUDENT VENTURE. ATLANTA , DEC. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER AND MICROSOFT TODAY ANNOUNCED MORE THAN A $200,000 INVESTMENT IN SUPPORT OF CLARK ATLANTA UNIVERSITY'S (CAU) MAKERSPACE AND ADVANCED MANUFACTURING LAB. THE LAB IS DEVELOPED TO GIVE ENTREPRENEURIAL UNDERGRADUATE STUDENTS THE OPPORTUNITY TO EXPAND THEIR KNOWLEDGE, SKILL SET, AND MARKET COMPETITIVENESS IN THE RENEWABLE ENERGY SPACE.
