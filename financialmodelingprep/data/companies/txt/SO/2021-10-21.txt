GEORGIA POWER ADJUSTS SCHEDULE FOR VOGTLE UNITS 3 & 4. ATLANTA, OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER TODAY ANNOUNCED A REVISED SCHEDULE FOR THE VOGTLE 3 & 4 NUCLEAR EXPANSION PROJECT. THE COMPANY CURRENTLY PROJECTS A UNIT 3 IN-SERVICE DATE IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022 AND A UNIT 4 IN-SERVICE DATE IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2023, REPRESENTING A THREE-MONTH SHIFT FOR EACH UNIT.
