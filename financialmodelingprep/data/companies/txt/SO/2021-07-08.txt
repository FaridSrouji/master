GEORGIA POWER CONTINUES TO ASSESS DAMAGE AND RESTORE POWER FOLLOWING TROPICAL STORM ELSA. ATLANTA, JULY 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A WORKFORCE INVOLVING HUNDREDS OF GEORGIA POWER PERSONNEL IS ASSESSING DAMAGE AND WORKING TO RESTORE POWER AS QUICKLY AND SAFELY AS POSSIBLE TO CUSTOMERS IMPACTED BY TROPICAL STORM ELSA. HIGH WINDS, HEAVY RAIN AND REPORTED TORNADOES HAVE LED TO FALLEN TREES AND DOWNED POWER LINES ACROSS COASTAL GEORGIA.
