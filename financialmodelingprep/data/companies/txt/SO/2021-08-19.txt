GEORGIA POWER ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS TO REVIEW THEIR CURRENT RATE PLAN AS SUMMER TEMPERATURES CONTINUE TO CLIMB. ATLANTA, AUG. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS SUMMER TEMPERATURES ACROSS GEORGIA CONTINUE TO CLIMB, GEORGIA POWER IS REMINDING CUSTOMERS TO REVIEW THEIR CURRENT RATE PLAN AND SELECT THE ONE THAT BEST FITS THEIR LIFESTYLE. THE COMPANY IS COMMITTED TO PROVIDING OPTIONS AND INFORMATION THAT PUTS CUSTOMERS IN CONTROL OF THEIR BUDGET AND POWER USAGE.
