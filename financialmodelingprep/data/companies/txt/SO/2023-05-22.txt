SOUTHERN COMPANY AND NATIONAL FISH AND WILDLIFE FOUNDATION ANNOUNCE FIVE-YEAR PARTNERSHIP EXTENSION. AGREEMENT CONTINUES SOUTHERN COMPANY'S LONGSTANDING COMMITMENT TO CONSERVATION ATLANTA , MAY 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY AND THE NATIONAL FISH AND WILDLIFE FOUNDATION (NFWF) HAVE SIGNED A FIVE-YEAR PARTNERSHIP EXTENSION TO KEEP SOUTHERN COMPANY ACTIVE IN VOLUNTARY WILDLIFE CONSERVATION WORK UNTIL 2028. THE NEW AGREEMENT WILL MARK 25 YEARS THAT SOUTHERN COMPANY HAS PARTNERED WITH NFWF AND DEMONSTRATES A CORPORATE COMMITMENT TO CONSERVATION THAT IS ONE OF THE MOST SUSTAINED IN THE HISTORY OF THE FOUNDATION.
