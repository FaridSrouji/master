GEORGIA POWER HIGHLIGHTS HABITAT CONSERVATION EFFORTS FOR LAST WEEK OF ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS MONTH. ATLANTA, SEPT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN HONOR OF THE FOURTH WEEK OF ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS MONTH THIS SEPTEMBER, GEORGIA POWER IS HIGHLIGHTING ITS EFFORTS AROUND PROTECTING AND MANAGING HABITATS THROUGHOUT THE STATE, SUCH AS THE COMPANY'S RIGHT-OF-WAY MANAGEMENT, INCLUDING SPECIAL MANAGEMENT AREAS FOR RARE PLANTS LIKE THE GEORGIA ASTER AND THE SMOOTH PURPLE CONEFLOWER.
