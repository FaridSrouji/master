GEORGIA POWER LAUNCHES NEW EDUCATION EQUITY INITIATIVE WITH SCHOOL DISTRICTS IN ALBANY, ATLANTA, AUGUSTA AND SAVANNAH. $3 MILLION INVESTMENT TO SUPPORT IMPACTFUL LOCAL PROGRAMS TO FACILITATE HOLISTIC GENERATIONAL CHANGE ATLANTA, MARCH 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER ANNOUNCED TODAY A NEW EDUCATION EQUITY INITIATIVE IN PARTNERSHIP WITH FOUR GEORGIA SCHOOL DISTRICTS, DESIGNED TO SUPPORT IMPACTFUL LOCAL PROGRAMS IN COMMUNITIES OF COLOR AND FACILITATE HOLISTIC GENERATIONAL CHANGE. THE PROGRAM REPRESENTS A $3 MILLION INVESTMENT THAT WILL ADDRESS SPECIFIC NEEDS AND OPPORTUNITIES IDENTIFIED BY LOCAL SCHOOL DISTRICTS FOCUSED ON IMPROVING CAREER READINESS AND MULTIGENERATIONAL ECONOMIC STABILITY.
