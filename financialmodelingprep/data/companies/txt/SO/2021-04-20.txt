SOUTHERN POWER ACQUIRES GLASS SANDS WIND FACILITY. ATLANTA, APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN POWER, A LEADING U.S. WHOLESALE ENERGY PROVIDER AND SUBSIDIARY OF SOUTHERN COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF ITS 15TH WIND PROJECT — THE 118-MEGAWATT (MW) GLASS SANDS WIND FACILITY — FROM STEELHEAD AMERICAS, VESTAS' DEVELOPMENT ARM IN NORTH AMERICA. "WE ARE PROUD TO ANNOUNCE THIS NEW ADDITION TO OUR GENERATION PORTFOLIO," SAID SOUTHERN POWER PRESIDENT BILL GRANTHAM.
