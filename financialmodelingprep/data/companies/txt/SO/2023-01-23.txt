SOUTHERN COMPANY TO BECOME OFFICIAL DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION PARTNER OF THE AJGA. THE PARTNERSHIP AIMS TO ADVANCE THE AJGA DEI PLATFORMS AND FURTHER CLOSE DIVERSITY GAPS. ATLANTA , JAN. 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY AND THE AMERICAN JUNIOR GOLF ASSOCIATION (AJGA) ARE PROUD TO ANNOUNCE SOUTHERN COMPANY AS THE OFFICIAL DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION PARTNER OF THE AJGA.
