GEORGIA POWER REMINDS CUSTOMERS TO BE VIGILANT, FOLLOW SIMPLE TIPS TO AVOID COMMON SCAMS. ATLANTA, AUG. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH A RECENT INCREASE IN REPORTS REGARDING SCAMS AND FRAUDULENT PHONE CALLS BY CRIMINALS POSING AS GEORGIA POWER EMPLOYEES, THE COMPANY IS REMINDING CUSTOMERS TO BE AWARE AND FOLLOW SIMPLE TIPS TO AVOID BEING A TARGET. GEORGIA POWER URGES CUSTOMERS TO BE CAUTIOUS WHEN CONTACTED BY AN UNVERIFIED PERSON CLAIMING TO BE A GEORGIA POWER REPRESENTATIVE.
