NATIONAL SOLAR APPRECIATION DAY A GREAT TIME FOR GEORGIA POWER CUSTOMERS TO EXPLORE SOLAR OFFERINGS. ATLANTA, MARCH 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO CELEBRATE NATIONAL SOLAR APPRECIATION DAY THIS YEAR, GEORGIA POWER IS REMINDING CUSTOMERS OF THE RESOURCES AND PROGRAMS AVAILABLE TO HELP THEM CONNECT WITH THE BENEFITS OF RENEWABLE AND SOLAR ENERGY – EVEN IF THEY ARE UNABLE TO INSTALL SOLAR. THE COMPANY ALREADY HAS ONE OF THE LARGEST VOLUNTARY RENEWABLE PORTFOLIOS IN THE COUNTRY AND EXPECTS TO CONTINUE AS A LEADER IN SOLAR ENERGY PRODUCTION BY GROWING ITS RENEWABLE GENERATION BY MORE THAN 72% BY 2025, WHICH WILL INCREASE THE COMPANY'S TOTAL RENEWABLE RESOURCES TO 22 % OF ITS ALREADY DIVERSE PORTFOLIO.
