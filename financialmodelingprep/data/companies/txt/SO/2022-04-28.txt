SOUTHERN COMPANY REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER 2022 EARNINGS. ATLANTA , APRIL 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY TODAY REPORTED FIRST-QUARTER 2022 EARNINGS OF $1.03 BILLION, OR 97 CENTS PER SHARE, COMPARED WITH EARNINGS OF $1.14 BILLION, OR $1.07 PER SHARE, IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021.  EXCLUDING THE ITEMS DESCRIBED UNDER "NET INCOME – EXCLUDING ITEMS" IN THE TABLE BELOW, SOUTHERN COMPANY EARNED $1.03 BILLION, OR 97 CENTS PER SHARE, DURING THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022, COMPARED WITH $1.04 BILLION, OR 98 CENTS PER SHARE, DURING THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021.
