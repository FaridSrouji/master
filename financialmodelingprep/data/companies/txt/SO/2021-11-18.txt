SOUTHERN COMPANY SIGNS AGREEMENT WITH U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY TO DEMONSTRATE WORLD'S FIRST FAST-SPECTRUM SALT REACTOR IN COLLABORATION WITH TERRAPOWER AND IDAHO NATIONAL LABORATORY. ATLANTA, NOV. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (DOE) HAVE ESTABLISHED A COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT TO DESIGN, CONSTRUCT AND OPERATE THE MOLTEN CHLORIDE REACTOR EXPERIMENT – THE WORLD'S FIRST CRITICAL FAST-SPECTRUM SALT REACTOR. SELECTED FOR FUNDING UNDER DOE'S ADVANCED REACTOR DEMONSTRATION PROGRAM (ARDP), THE SMALL REACTOR EXPERIMENT WILL ADVANCE TERRAPOWER'S MOLTEN CHLORIDE FAST REACTOR (MCFR) — A TECHNOLOGY IMPORTANT TO A SUSTAINABLE CLEAN ENERGY FUTURE.
