GEORGIA POWER FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES $500,000 TO SUPPORT UNCF DURING ANNUAL MAYOR'S MASKED BALL. ATLANTA, DEC. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THIS WEEKEND, LEADERS FROM GEORGIA POWER ANNOUNCED A SERIES OF HISTORIC GRANTS FROM THE GEORGIA POWER FOUNDATION TO UNCF TO SUPPORT HBCUS ACROSS THE STATE. OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS, THE FOUNDATION WILL AWARD $500,000 IN GRANTS TO ASSIST THE UNCF IN ITS MISSION TO PROVIDE EQUAL ACCESS TO COLLEGE EDUCATION THROUGH A ROBUST AND NATIONALLY-RECOGNIZED PIPELINE OF UNDER-REPRESENTED STUDENTS.
