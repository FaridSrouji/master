GEORGIA NATURAL GAS (GNG) FOUNDATION TEAMS UP WITH HOUSEPROUD ATLANTA FOR THE HOLIDAYS. GNG FOUNDATION DONATES $120,000 TO ATLANTA NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION ENABLING MINORITY SENIORS TO STAY IN THEIR HOMES ATLANTA , DEC. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HOME MAY BE THE HEART OF THE HOLIDAYS, BUT FOR MANY LOW-INCOME SENIORS, IT REPRESENTS A YEAR-ROUND STRUGGLE TO KEEP UP WITH NEEDED REPAIRS. THIS YEAR, THE GEORGIA NATURAL GAS FOUNDATION IS AWARDING $120,000 TO HOUSEPROUD ATLANTA TO SUPPORT ITS MISSION TO PROVIDE NO-COST HOME REPAIRS TO LOW-INCOME SENIOR, VETERAN, AND DISABLED HOMEOWNERS.
