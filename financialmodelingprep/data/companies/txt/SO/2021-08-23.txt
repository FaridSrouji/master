GEORGIA POWER NAMES AARON ABRAMOVITZ EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND TREASURER. ATLANTA, AUG. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED AARON ABRAMOVITZ, SOUTHERN NUCLEAR VICE PRESIDENT OF BUSINESS OPERATIONS FOR VOGTLE 3 & 4, AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND TREASURER OF GEORGIA POWER, EFFECTIVE SEPT. 1, 2021. IN HIS NEW ROLE, ABRAMOVITZ WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR OVERSEEING THE COMPANY'S ACCOUNTING AND FINANCIAL FUNCTIONS.
SOUTHERN COMPANY ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER ANDREW W. EVANS; DANIEL S.. ATLANTA, AUG. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ANDREW W. EVANS, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, ANNOUNCED HIS PLANS TO RETIRE.
GEORGIA POWER CREWS MOVE NORTH TO ASSIST WITH HURRICANE HENRI RECOVERY. ATLANTA, AUG. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS HURRICANE HENRI THREATENS THE NORTHEAST COAST, GEORGIA POWER IS RESPONDING TO SUPPORT AFFECTED UTILITIES AS PART OF THE MUTUAL ASSISTANCE NETWORK. MORE THAN 100 GEORGIA POWER PERSONNEL ARE TRAVELING NORTH TOWARDS HARTFORD, CONNECTICUT.
