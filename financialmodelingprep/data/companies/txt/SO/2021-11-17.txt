SOUTHERN COMPANY GAS NAMES JOANNE MELLO VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY. ATLANTA, NOV. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY GAS HAS NAMED JOANNE MELLO VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY. IN THIS ROLE, MELLO WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR DEVELOPING AND LEADING SOUTHERN COMPANY GAS' OVERALL SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY — INCORPORATING ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY AND GOVERNANCE STAKEHOLDER INTERESTS.
