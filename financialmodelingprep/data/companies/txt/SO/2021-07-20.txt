GEORGIA POWER LAUNCHES NEW SERIES OF INTERACTIVE STEM-BASED VIDEOS. ATLANTA, JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER'S LEARNING POWER TEAM CONTINUES TO SUPPORT SUMMER LEARNING AND STEM ENRICHMENT FOR STUDENTS BY ROLLING OUT NEW "EXPERIMENT" VIDEOS. THE EXPERIMENTS COVER A VARIETY OF STEM AND ENERGY EFFICIENCY TOPICS WITH HANDS-ON ACTIVITIES REQUIRING ONLY A FEW BASIC SUPPLIES, MOST OF WHICH CAN BE FOUND AROUND THE HOUSE.
