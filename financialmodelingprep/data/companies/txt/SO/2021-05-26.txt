CHRIS WOMACK NAMED CHAIRMAN AND CEO OF GEORGIA POWER. ATLANTA, MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHRIS WOMACK WILL ASSUME THE ROLES OF CHAIRMAN AND CEO, EFFECTIVE JUNE 1. HE WILL NOW OFFICIALLY LEAD THE COMPANY AS CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CEO.
