GEORGIA POWER, GEORGIA 811 TEAM-UP TO RAISE AWARENESS AROUND NATIONAL 811 DAY. ATLANTA, AUG. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER AND GEORGIA 811 CONTINUE TO WORK TOGETHER TO RAISE AWARENESS FOR NATIONAL 811 DAY ON AUGUST 11 ("8-11").  GEORGIA POWER WANTS TO REMIND CUSTOMERS TO DO THEIR PART TO PREVENT DANGEROUS AND DISRUPTIVE ACCIDENTS BY CONTACTING 811 BEFORE DIGGING.
