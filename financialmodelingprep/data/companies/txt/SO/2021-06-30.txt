GEORGIA POWER FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES $500,000 INVESTMENT IN GEORGIA JUSTICE PROJECT INITIATIVE. ATLANTA, JUNE 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER TODAY ANNOUNCED AN INVESTMENT OF $500,000 BY THE GEORGIA POWER FOUNDATION, TO THE GEORGIA JUSTICE PROJECT (GJP). THE FUNDING WILL HELP REMOVE BARRIERS TO EMPLOYMENT AND HOUSING AND OPEN OTHER OPPORTUNITIES FOR THOSE WITH PAST RECORDS WHO ARE WORKING TO BE PRODUCTIVE CITIZENS.
