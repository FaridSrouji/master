SOUTHERN COMPANY GAS EARNS TOP INDUSTRY RECOGNITION FOR EFFORTS TO PROMOTE SUSTAINABILITY. SOUTHERN GAS ASSOCIATION AWARDS COMPANY HIGHEST HONOR IN ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL & GOVERNANCE CATEGORY ATLANTA , JUNE 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN RECOGNITION OF ITS EFFORTS TO ENCOURAGE THE EVOLUTION WITHIN THE NATURAL GAS INDUSTRY TOWARD A SUSTAINABLE FUTURE, SOUTHERN COMPANY GAS HAS BEEN HONORED WITH THE 2022 SOUTHERN GAS ASSOCIATION ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL & GOVERNANCE AWARD. SOUTHERN COMPANY GAS' NEXT GENERATION NATURAL GAS INITIATIVE LEVERAGES THE COMPANY'S LEADERSHIP VOICE, COLLABORATIVE SPIRIT, AND LOW-CARBON AMBITIONS TO RALLY INDUSTRY PEERS TO WORK TOGETHER TO ACCELERATE MEANINGFUL EMISSIONS REDUCTIONS ACROSS THE NATURAL GAS VALUE CHAIN.
