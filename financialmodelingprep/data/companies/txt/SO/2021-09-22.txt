GEORGIA POWER HIGHLIGHTS WATERWAY CONSERVATION EFFORTS FOR THIRD WEEK OF ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS MONTH. ATLANTA, SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN HONOR OF THE THIRD WEEK OF ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS MONTH THIS SEPTEMBER, GEORGIA POWER IS HIGHLIGHTING ITS EFFORTS AROUND IMPROVING AND PROTECTING GEORGIA'S WATERWAYS AND THE SPECIES THAT LIVE IN THEM, SUCH AS SHOAL BASS.
