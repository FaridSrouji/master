SOUTHERN COMPANY ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA, OCT. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 66 CENTS PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE DEC. 6, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF NOV. 15, 2021. EVERY QUARTER FOR MORE THAN 70 YEARS SOUTHERN COMPANY HAS PAID A DIVIDEND TO ITS SHAREHOLDERS THAT IS EQUAL TO OR GREATER THAN THE PREVIOUS QUARTER.
GEORGIA POWER CELEBRATES CAREERS IN ENERGY WEEK. ATLANTA, OCT. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER IS JOINING ELECTRICAL UTILITIES ACROSS THE COUNTRY TO HIGHLIGHT CAREERS IN ENERGY WEEK, OCTOBER 18-22. CAREERS IN ENERGY WEEK IS DEDICATED TO CELEBRATING AND RAISING AWARENESS OF ENERGY CAREERS AND THEIR IMPORTANCE TO OUR COMMUNITIES, STATE AND NATION.
NICOR GAS ANNOUNCES NORTHERN ILLINOIS COMMUNITY INITIATIVES. NAPERVILLE, ILL., OCT. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NICOR GAS ANNOUNCES NORTHERN ILLINOIS COMMUNITY INITIATIVES, A FOUNDATION CREATED TO SPEARHEAD BOLD ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT STRATEGIES AND TO STIMULATE INVESTMENT IN REAL ESTATE DEVELOPMENT, WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP IN SEVERELY UNDER-RESOURCED COMMUNITIES IT SERVES IN NORTHERN ILLINOIS.
FORMER U.S. NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION CHAIRMAN KRISTINE L. SVINICKI TO JOIN SOUTHERN COMPANY BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ATLANTA, OCT. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF SOUTHERN COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF KRISTINE L. SVINICKI AS AN INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR, EFFECTIVE OCT. 17, 2021.
