ATLANTA GAS LIGHT REMINDS CUSTOMERS TO PROTECT THEMSELVES AGAINST UTILITY SCAMS. ATLANTA, NOV. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH UTILITY SCAMS ON THE RISE, IT IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN EVER FOR CUSTOMERS TO BE VIGILANT AND KNOW WHAT STEPS TO TAKE TO PREVENT THEMSELVES OR THEIR FAMILIES FROM FALLING VICTIM. ATLANTA GAS LIGHT IS JOINING 150 U.S. AND CANADIAN ENERGY COMPANIES NOV. 15-19 FOR UTILITY SCAM AWARENESS WEEK, A WEEKLONG ANNUAL CAMPAIGN CREATED BY UTILITIES UNITED AGAINST SCAMS, TO RAISE AWARENESS OF AND HELP EDUCATE CUSTOMERS ON THE TACTICS USED BY SCAMMERS.
