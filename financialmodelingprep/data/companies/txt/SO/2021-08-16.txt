GEORGIA POWER OFFERS TIPS AND OUTAGE RESOURCES FOR CUSTOMERS AHEAD OF EXPECTED IMPACTS FROM TROPICAL STORM FRED. ATLANTA, AUG. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TROPICAL STORM FRED IS EXPECTED TO CROSS INTO GEORGIA ON TUESDAY BRINGING WITH IT THE POTENTIAL FOR HEAVY RAINS AND GUSTY WINDS. GEORGIA POWER ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS TO MONITOR LOCAL WEATHER CONDITIONS AND KEEP SAFETY FIRST AS INCLEMENT WEATHER MOVES IN.
