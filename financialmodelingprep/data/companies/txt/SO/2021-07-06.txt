GEORGIA POWER OFFERS TIPS AND OUTAGE RESOURCES FOR CUSTOMERS AHEAD OF EXPECTED IMPACTS FROM TROPICAL STORM ELSA. ATLANTA, JULY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TROPICAL STORM ELSA IS EXPECTED TO CROSS INTO GEORGIA LATER THIS WEEK, BRINGING HEAVY RAINS AND GUSTY WINDS TO THE SOUTHERN AND COASTAL PARTS OF THE STATE. GEORGIA POWER ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS TO MONITOR LOCAL WEATHER CONDITIONS AND KEEP SAFETY FIRST AS INCLEMENT WEATHER MOVES IN.
