MISSISSIPPI POWER, FORREST COUNTY AGRICULTURAL HIGH SCHOOL CELEBRATE STATE'S FIRST ELECTRIC SCHOOL BUS IN OPERATION. GULFPORT, MISS. , DEC. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPRESENTATIVES FROM MISSISSIPPI POWER AND FORREST COUNTY AGRICULTURAL HIGH SCHOOL HELD A RIBBON-CUTTING CEREMONY AS THE FIRST ELECTRIC SCHOOL BUS IN THE STATE OF MISSISSIPPI BEGAN OPERATING TODAY IN BROOKLYN.
