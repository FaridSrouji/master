GEORGIA POWER NAMES FRAN FOREHAND SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF POWER DELIVERY. ATLANTA, MARCH 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER HAS NAMED FRAN FOREHAND AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF POWER DELIVERY FOR GEORGIA POWER, EFFECTIVE APRIL 2, 2021. IN HER NEW ROLE, FOREHAND WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE PLANNING, DESIGN, OPERATIONS, CONSTRUCTION AND MAINTENANCE OF GEORGIA POWER'S DISTRIBUTION AND TRANSMISSION SYSTEMS.
