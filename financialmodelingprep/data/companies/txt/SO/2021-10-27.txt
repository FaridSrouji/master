ATLANTA GAS LIGHT FOUNDATION INVESTS IN WORKFORCE TRAINING AT TECHNICAL COLLEGE SYSTEM OF GEORGIA. ATLANTA, OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE ATLANTA GAS LIGHT FOUNDATION IS PARTNERING WITH THE TECHNICAL COLLEGE SYSTEM OF GEORGIA (TCSG) TO HELP DUAL ENROLLMENT AND TRADITIONAL STUDENTS THROUGHOUT GEORGIA DEVELOP THE TECHNICAL SKILLS THEY NEED TO SUCCEED IN THE WORKFORCE. THE ATLANTA GAS LIGHT FOUNDATION PRESENTED A $150,000 GIFT TO TCSG TUESDAY AFTERNOON.
