SOUTHERN COMPANY FIRST-QUARTER EARNINGS TO BE RELEASED APRIL 29. ATLANTA, APRIL 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY PLANS TO RELEASE ITS EARNINGS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021 BY 7:30 A.M. EDT ON THURSDAY, APRIL 29, 2021.
SOUTHERN COMPANY MAKING ADDITIONAL EXECUTIVE MOVES IN ADVANCE OF MARK LANTRIP'S PLANNED RETIREMENT AFTER 40 YEARS WITH THE ORGANIZATION. ATLANTA, APRIL 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED ADDITIONAL PERSONNEL MOVES IN ADVANCE OF THE RETIREMENT OF MARK LANTRIP AFTER 40 YEARS OF SERVICE. LANTRIP, WHO WILL BE RETIRING THIS MONTH, MOST RECENTLY SERVED AS THE EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT FOR SOUTHERN COMPANY AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF SOUTHERN COMPANY SERVICES, INC. (SCS), THE SOUTHERN COMPANY SYSTEM SERVICE COMPANY AND A WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF SOUTHERN COMPANY.
