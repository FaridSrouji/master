VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS INVESTS IN NEW TECHNOLOGY TO REDUCE EMISSIONS DURING PIPELINE INSPECTION AND REPAIRS. VIRGINIA BEACH, VA. , OCT. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH A FOCUS ON THE ENVIRONMENT AND SUSTAINABILITY, VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS (VNG) IS UTILIZING INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGY TO REDUCE EMISSIONS WHILE SUPPORTING EFFORTS BY ITS PARENT SOUTHERN COMPANY, TO REACH NET-ZERO DIRECT GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS BY 2050.
