GEORGIA POWER HIGHLIGHTS SENSITIVE SPECIES CONSERVATION EFFORTS FOR SECOND WEEK OF ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS MONTH. ATLANTA, SEPT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN HONOR OF THE SECOND WEEK OF ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS MONTH THIS SEPTEMBER, GEORGIA POWER IS HIGHLIGHTING ITS EFFORTS AROUND PROTECTING SENSITIVES SPECIES SUCH AS THE GOPHER TORTOISE, WHICH IS GEORGIA'S STATE REPTILE AND CONSIDERED A KEYSTONE SPECIES.
