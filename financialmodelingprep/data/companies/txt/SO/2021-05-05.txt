SOUTHERN COMPANY GAS ASSUMES CONTROL OF MEADOW BRANCH RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS FACILITY, LAUNCHES NEW SUBSIDIARY TO INCREASE ACCESS TO SUSTAINABLE FUEL. ATHENS, TENN., MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY GAS ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS TAKEN OWNERSHIP OF THE MEADOW BRANCH LANDFILL METHANE RECOVERY FACILITY, THE RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS FACILITY LOCATED AT THE MEADOW BRANCH LANDFILL IN ATHENS, TENN.
