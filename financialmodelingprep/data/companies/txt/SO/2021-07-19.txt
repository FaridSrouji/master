SOUTHERN COMPANY ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA, JULY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 66 CENTS PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE SEPT. 7, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUG. 16, 2021.
