VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS INCORPORATES ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TO HELP PROTECT CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE. VIRGINIA BEACH, VA. , APRIL 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OVER THE PAST DECADE, VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS (VNG) HAS CONTINUOUSLY WORKED TO MODERNIZE ITS PIPELINE INFRASTRUCTURE AND HAS COORDINATED EFFORTS WITH THE VIRGINIA STATE CORPORATION COMMISSION, LOCAL GOVERNMENTS, EXCAVATORS AND VIRGINIA 811 (VA811) TO PROMOTE SAFE DIGGING AND BUILD AWARENESS OF THE DAMAGE PREVENTION LAWS THAT KEEP CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES SAFE.
