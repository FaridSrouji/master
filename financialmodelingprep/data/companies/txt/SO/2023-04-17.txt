SOUTHERN LINC ANNOUNCES LTE-TO-LMR INTEROPERABILITY FOR PUBLIC SAFETY CUSTOMERS WITH L3HARRIS XL SERIES RADIOS. ATLANTA, APRIL 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN LINC, THE WIRELESS SUBSIDIARY OF SOUTHERN COMPANY (SO), HAS ANNOUNCED THE AVAILABILITY OF L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES RADIOS, PROVIDING COMMUNICATIONS ACROSS LTE AND P25 LAND MOBILE RADIO (LMR) NETWORKS. THE COMPANY WILL OFFER THE XL CONVERGE™ AND XL ONBOARD™ SERIES OF PORTABLE AND MOBILE UNITS FROM L3HARRIS.
