GEORGIA POWER FOUNDATION HIGHLIGHTS $1 MILLION INVESTMENT IN WATER GRANTS THIS WORLD WATER DAY. ATLANTA, MARCH 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE GEORGIA POWER FOUNDATION TODAY IN HONOR OF WORLD WATER DAY IS HIGHLIGHTING $1 MILLION IN ENVIRONMENTAL GRANTS AWARDED TO EIGHT ORGANIZATIONS THROUGHOUT THE STATE. WORLD WATER DAY IS ABOUT RECOGNIZING THE TRUE VALUE OF WATER AND PROTECTING THIS VITAL RESOURCE.
