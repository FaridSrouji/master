GEORGIA NATURAL GAS CUSTOMERS OFFSET MORE THAN 100 MILLION POUNDS OF CARBON THROUGH GREENER LIFE® PROGRAM. NATURAL GAS MARKETER PURCHASES AN ADDITIONAL 10 MILLION POUNDS OF CARBON OFFSETS TO MARK WORLD ENVIRONMENT DAY, JUNE 5, 2022 ATLANTA , JUNE 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA NATURAL GAS® (GNG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS GREENER LIFE® PROGRAM WHICH HELPS CUSTOMERS MAKE THEIR NATURAL GAS USE CARBON NEUTRAL, REACHED A PROGRAM MILESTONE OF 100 MILLION POUNDS OF CARBON EMISSIONS OFFSET FROM THE ENVIRONMENT. THAT AMOUNT IS THE EQUIVALENT TO OVER 110 MILLION MILES DRIVEN BY AN AVERAGE GASOLINE-POWERED PASSENGER VEHICLE.
