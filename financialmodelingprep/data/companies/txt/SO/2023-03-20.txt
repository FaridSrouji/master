VOGTLE UNIT 4 BEGINS HOT FUNCTIONAL TESTING. LAST SERIES OF MAJOR TESTS UNDERWAY FOR UNIT 4 AHEAD OF INITIAL FUEL LOAD ATLANTA , MARCH 20, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT HOT FUNCTIONAL TESTING HAS BEGUN FOR VOGTLE UNIT 4. HOT FUNCTIONAL TESTING MARKS THE LAST SERIES OF MAJOR TESTS UNDERWAY FOR THE NEW NUCLEAR UNIT AHEAD OF INITIAL FUEL LOAD.
