VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS AWARDS $100,000 TO PHARRELL WILLIAMS' YELLOWHAB SCHOOL TO FUEL STUDENTS WITH HEALTHY MEALS. VIRGINIA BEACH, VA., FEB. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS (VNG) IS PARTNERING WITH A NATIONAL RECORDING ARTIST WITH LOCAL ROOTS TO HELP NOURISH STUDENTS AT A NEW, ONE-OF-A-KIND SCHOOL IN NORFOLK, VIRGINIA.
