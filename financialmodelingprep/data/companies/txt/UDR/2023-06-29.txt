UDR ANNOUNCES FORMATION OF $510 MILLION JOINT VENTURE. DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UDR, INC. (THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: UDR), A LEADING MULTIFAMILY REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST AND GRESB 5 STAR RATED COMPANY FOR ITS SUSTAINABILITY LEADERSHIP, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS CLOSED A $510 MILLION JOINT VENTURE WITH LASALLE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (THE “PARTNER”). THE COMPANY AND THE PARTNER INTEND TO GROW THE JOINT VENTURE OVER TIME THROUGH THE ACQUISITION OF HIGH-QUALITY, WELL-LOCATED COMMUNITIES WITH OPERATIONAL UPSIDE THAT CAN BE RECOGNIZED THROUGH THE IMPLEM.
