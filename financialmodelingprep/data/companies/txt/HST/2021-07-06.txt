HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. ACQUIRES BAKER'S CAY RESORT KEY LARGO FOR $200 MILLION. BETHESDA, MD., JULY 06, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. (NASDAQ: HST), THE NATION'S LARGEST LODGING REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST (THE "COMPANY"), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED THE FEE SIMPLE INTEREST IN THE 200-ROOM BAKER'S CAY RESORT KEY LARGO, CURIO COLLECTION FOR APPROXIMATELY $200 MILLION IN CASH. THE PURCHASE PRICE REPRESENTS AN ESTIMATED 6.2% CAPITALIZATION RATE AND A 14.5X EBITDA MULTIPLE BASED ON 2021 PROPERTY FORECASTS.( 1)
