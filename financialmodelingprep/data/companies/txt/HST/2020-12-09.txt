HOST HOTELS & RESORTS NAMED TO CDP'S A LIST FOR LEADING EFFORT AGAINST CLIMATE CHANGE. BETHESDA, MD., DEC. 09, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. (NASDAQ: HST), THE NATION'S LARGEST LODGING REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST (THE “COMPANY”), HAS ONCE AGAIN BEEN RECOGNIZED FOR ITS CLIMATE ACTION. THE COMPANY ACHIEVED A PLACE ON THE GLOBAL ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT NONPROFIT CDP'S PRESTIGIOUS A LIST, WHICH NAMES THE WORLD'S MOST PIONEERING COMPANIES LEADING ON ENVIRONMENTAL PERFORMANCE ANNUALLY.
