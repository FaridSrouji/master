HOST HOTELS & RESORTS DISPOSES OF SHERATON NEW YORK TIMES SQUARE HOTEL. BETHESDA, MD., APRIL 20, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. (NASDAQ: HST), THE NATION'S LARGEST LODGING REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST (THE "COMPANY"), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SOLD THE 1,780-ROOM SHERATON NEW YORK TIMES SQUARE HOTEL FOR APPROXIMATELY $373 MILLION, WHICH REPRESENTS A 28.0X EBITDA MULTIPLE1 ON 2019 EBITDA. THE EBITDA MULTIPLE INCLUDES APPROXIMATELY $136 MILLION OF ESTIMATED FOREGONE CAPITAL EXPENDITURES OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS. IN CONNECTION WITH THE SALE, THE COMPANY IS PROVIDING A $250 MILLION BRIDGE LOAN TO THE PURCHASER.
