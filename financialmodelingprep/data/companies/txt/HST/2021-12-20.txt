HOST HOTELS & RESORTS ACQUIRES THE ALIDA, SAVANNAH AND DISPOSES OF W HOLLYWOOD. BETHESDA, MD., DEC. 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. (NASDAQ: HST), THE NATION'S LARGEST LODGING REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST (THE "COMPANY"), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED THE FEE SIMPLE INTEREST IN THE ALIDA, SAVANNAH, A 173-ROOM BOUTIQUE HOTEL, FOR APPROXIMATELY $103 MILLION IN CASH.
