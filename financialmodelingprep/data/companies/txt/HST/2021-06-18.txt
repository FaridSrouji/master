HERMAN E. BULLS APPOINTED TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC.. BETHESDA, MD., JUNE 18, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. (NASDAQ: HST), THE NATION'S LARGEST LODGING REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST (THE "COMPANY"), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF HERMAN E. BULLS TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. WITH THE ADDITION OF MR. BULLS, THE COMPANY'S BOARD WILL HAVE NINE DIRECTORS, SEVEN OF WHOM ARE INDEPENDENT. MR. BULLS WAS ELECTED TO SERVE ON THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS UNTIL THE NEXT ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS OF THE COMPANY AND WAS ALSO APPOINTED TO THE NOMINATING AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE COMMITTEE.
