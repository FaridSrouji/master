DIANA M. LAING APPOINTED TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC.. BETHESDA, MD., OCT. 12, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. (NASDAQ: HST) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DIANA M. LAING TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 11, 2022. MS. LAING HAS ALSO BEEN APPOINTED TO SERVE ON THE AUDIT COMMITTEE, EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 7, 2022. WITH THE ADDITION OF MS. LAING, THE COMPANY'S BOARD WILL HAVE NINE DIRECTORS, SEVEN OF WHOM ARE INDEPENDENT; AND THREE WOMEN DIRECTORS, WHICH IS MORE THAN 30 PERCENT OF THE BOARD.
