HOST HOTELS & RESORTS TO LIVE STREAM 2023 INVESTOR DAY. BETHESDA, MD., MAY 17, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. (NASDAQ: HST), THE NATION'S LARGEST LODGING REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST (THE "COMPANY"), TODAY ANNOUNCED ADDITIONAL DETAILS FOR ITS INVESTOR DAY ON TUESDAY, MAY 23, 2023. THE EVENT WILL BE STREAMED LIVE FROM THE NEWLY TRANSFORMED ORLANDO WORLD CENTER MARRIOTT, WHICH WAS PART OF THE MARRIOTT TRANSFORMATIONAL CAPITAL PROGRAM, AND WILL INCLUDE INVESTORS AND ANALYSTS.
