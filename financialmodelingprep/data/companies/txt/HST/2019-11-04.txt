HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. UPDATES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE FULL YEAR 2019; ANNOUNCES IMPAIRMENT CHARGES FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. UPDATED EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE FULL YEAR 2019. FOR THE YEAR, THE COMPANY REVISED NET INCOME GUIDANCE RANGE FROM $956 MILLION TO $993 MILLION TO $912 MILLION TO $935 MILLION. DILUTED EARNINGS PER COMMON SHARE GUIDANCE RANGE HAS BEEN REVISED FROM $1.28 TO $1.33 TO $1.23 TO $1.26. THE COMPANY REPORTED NON-CASH IMPAIRMENT EXPENSE OF $6 MILLION AGAINST $239 MILLION A YEAR AGO.
