PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (PMI) HOLDS 2023 VIRTUAL ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS. STAMFORD, CT--(BUSINESS WIRE)--REGULATORY NEWS: PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE: PM) HELD ITS 2023 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS TODAY. ANDRÉ CALANTZOPOULOS, EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, ADDRESSED SHAREHOLDERS AND ANSWERED QUESTIONS. JACEK OLCZAK, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, GAVE A BUSINESS PRESENTATION, WHICH INCLUDED AN OVERVIEW OF PMI'S: STRONG PERFORMANCE IN 2022 AND ENCOURAGING START TO 2023; ROBUST BUSINESS FUNDAMENTALS AND RAPID PROGRESS ON ITS SMOKE-FREE TRANSFORMATION; EXCELLE.
