PMI RECOGNIZED IN 2023 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. LAUSANNE, SWITZERLAND--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (PMI) (NYSE: PM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE 2023 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI) FOR THE THIRD YEAR RUNNING. WITH AN OVERALL SCORE OF 80.6%, SURPASSING THE INDEX AVERAGE SCORE OF 73%, PMI JOINS AN A-LIST OF GLOBAL COMPANIES COMMITTED TO TRANSPARENCY IN GENDER REPORTING AND ADVANCING EQUALITY IN THE WORKPLACE.
