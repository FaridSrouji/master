SWEDISH MATCH CONFIRMS DISCUSSIONS REGARDING A POSSIBLE PUBLIC TAKEOVER OFFER FOR SWEDISH MATCH. STOCKHOLM , MAY 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF SWEDISH MATCH AB HAS NOTED THE RECENT SPECULATION AND CONFIRMS THAT DISCUSSIONS WITH PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. REGARDING A POSSIBLE PUBLIC TAKEOVER OFFER FOR SWEDISH MATCH ARE ONGOING. THERE CAN BE NO CERTAINTY THAT AN OFFER WILL BE MADE, NOR AS TO THE TERMS OF ANY SUCH POTENTIAL OFFER.
PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. CONFIRMS DISCUSSIONS WITH SWEDISH MATCH AB REGARDING POTENTIAL OFFER. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (PMI) (NYSE: PM) TODAY CONFIRMED, IN RESPONSE TO RECENT MARKET SPECULATION, THAT DISCUSSIONS ARE ONGOING BETWEEN PMI AND SWEDISH MATCH AB (STO: SWMA) REGARDING A POTENTIAL OFFER FOR THE COMPANY. THE DISCUSSIONS ARE IN PROGRESS AND IT IS UNCERTAIN WHETHER AN OFFER WILL BE MADE. PMI INTENDS TO MAKE NO FURTHER COMMENT REGARDING THE DISCUSSIONS UNLESS AND UNTIL IT IS APPROPRIATE TO DO SO.
