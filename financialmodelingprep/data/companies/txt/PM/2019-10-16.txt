PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. REPORTS CONSOLIDATED UNAUDITED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. ANNOUNCED CONSOLIDATED UNAUDITED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 20,380 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 20,439 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 2,788 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 3,156 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 1,896 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,247 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.22 COMPARED TO USD 1.44 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 58,072 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 59,965 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 8,025 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 8,675 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 5,569 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 6,001 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 3.57 COMPARED TO USD 3.85 A YEAR AGO.
