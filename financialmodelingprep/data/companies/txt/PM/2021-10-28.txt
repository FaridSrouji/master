PMI TO COMMIT ADDITIONAL USD 200 MILLION TO CORPORATE VENTURE CAPITAL ACTIVITIES OF PM EQUITY PARTNER. LAUSANNE, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (PMI) (NYSE: PM) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS PLANS TO DEDICATE A FURTHER USD 200 MILLION TO MINORITY INVESTMENTS IN EARLY AND GROWTH-STAGE COMPANIES THROUGH PM EQUITY PARTNER (PMEP), PMI'S CORPORATE VENTURE CAPITAL ARM. THIS ALLOCATION FOLLOWS A 2016 COMMITMENT OF USD 150 MILLION THAT PMEP HAS SINCE FULLY INVESTED AND IS INTENDED TO SUPPORT PMI'S SMOKE-FREE AND BEYOND NICOTINE AMBITIONS.
