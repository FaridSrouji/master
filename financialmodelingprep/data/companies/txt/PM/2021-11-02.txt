PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL APPLAUDS U.K. GOVERNMENT'S APPROACH TO ACHIEVE ITS SMOKE-FREE AMBITION. LAUSANNE, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (PMI) (NYSE: PM) ANNOUNCED ITS SUPPORT OF THE U.K. GOVERNMENT'S PLAN TO SIMPLIFY THE PATHWAY TO LICENSE ELECTRONIC CIGARETTES AND OTHER INHALED NICOTINE-CONTAINING PRODUCTS (NCPS) AS MEDICINES IN ENGLAND. THE U.K. ALREADY HAS ONE OF EUROPE'S LOWEST SMOKING RATES, SUPPORTED BY A HIGH RATE OF SMOKERS WHO HAVE SWITCHED TO BETTER ALTERNATIVES.
