PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL REPORTS QTRLY ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS OF $1.21. QTRLY ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS OF $1.21, UP BY 11.0%; UP BY 30.1% ON A LIKE-FOR-LIKE BASIS, EXCLUDING CURRENCY.WITHDRAWS 2020 FULL-YEAR REPORTED DILUTED EPS FORECAST DUE SOLELY TO UNCERTAINTY RELATED TO COVID-19 PANDEMIC.BUSINESS CONTINUITY MEASURES ARE OPERATING EFFECTIVELY.EXPERIENCED A LIMITED IMPACT ON PERFORMANCE FROM THE EARLY STAGES OF THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.PHILIP MORRIS - HAVE TO ASSUME THAT, IN SOME MARKETS, UNEMPLOYMENT, RELATED CUT IN DISPOSABLE INCOME WILL HAVE TEMPORARY IMPACT ON MARKET DYNAMICS.PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL - HAS A ROBUST FINANCIAL POSITION.PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL - LARGE MAJORITY OF CO'S MANUFACTURING FACILITIES GLOBALLY ARE OPERATIONAL, INCLUDING ALL HEATED TOBACCO UNIT FACTORIES.PHILIP MORRIS - CIGARETTE PRODUCTION FACILITIES TEMPORARILY IMPACTED BY SHUTDOWNS OR LIMITATIONS ACCOUNT FOR ABOUT 20% OF CO'S TOTAL CAPACITY.PHILIP MORRIS - BASED ON CURRENT SALES TRENDS, THERE ARE ADEQUATE INVENTORIES OF OVER TWO MONTHS FOR HEATED TOBACCO UNITS.PHILIP MORRIS - BASED ON CURRENT SALES TRENDS, THERE ARE ADEQUATE INVENTORIES OF OVER 3 MONTHS FOR TOBACCO HEATING DEVICES.PHILIP MORRIS - BASED ON CURRENT SALES TRENDS, THERE ARE ADEQUATE INVENTORIES OF OVER ONE AND A HALF MONTHS FOR CIGARETTES.PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL - DOES NOT CURRENTLY ANTICIPATE OUT-OF-STOCK SITUATIONS IN ANY MAJOR OPERATING INCOME MARKETS.
