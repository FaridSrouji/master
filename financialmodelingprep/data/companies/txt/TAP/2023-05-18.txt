MOLSON COORS BEVERAGE COMPANY ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. GOLDEN, COLO. & MONTREAL--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MOLSON COORS BEVERAGE COMPANY (NYSE: TAP, TAP.A) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK OF US$0.41 PER SHARE, PAYABLE JUNE 15, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 2, 2023. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE TO HOLDERS OF CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK OF MOLSON COORS BEVERAGE COMPANY.
