ERIC GUNNING APPOINTED CORPORATE SECRETARY OF MOLSON COORS BEVERAGE COMPANY. GOLDEN, COLO. & MONTREAL--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOLSON COORS BEVERAGE COMPANY ANNOUNCED ERIC GUNNING HAS BEEN APPOINTED CORPORATE SECRETARY, IN ADDITION TO HIS ROLE AS DEPUTY CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER.
