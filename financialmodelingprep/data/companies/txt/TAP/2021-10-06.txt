TRUSS CBD USA, A MOLSON COORS AND HEXO CORP JOINT VENTURE, EXPANDS VERYVELL™ CBD BEVERAGES SEMI-NATIONALLY. DENVER, OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUSS CBD USA, A JOINT VENTURE BY MOLSON COORS BEVERAGE COMPANY (NYSE: TAP) AND HEXO USA INC, A SUBSIDIARY OF HEXO CORP (NASDAQ: HEXO), TODAY ANNOUNCES THE EXPANSION OF VERYVELL, A LINE OF HEMP-DERIVED CBD AND ADAPTOGEN BEVERAGES. FOLLOWING A SUCCESSFUL COLORADO LAUNCH IN 2020, TRUSS CBD USA NOW EXPANDS VERYVELL SEMI-NATIONALLY TO BE AVAILABLE IN 17 STATES.
