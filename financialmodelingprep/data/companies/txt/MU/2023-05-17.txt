MICRON TO BRING EUV TECHNOLOGY TO JAPAN, ADVANCING NEXT-GENERATION MEMORY MANUFACTURING. HIROSHIMA, JAPAN, MAY 17, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MICRON TECHNOLOGY, INC. (NASDAQ: MU) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WILL BE INTRODUCING EXTREME ULTRAVIOLET (EUV) TECHNOLOGY TO JAPAN, TAPPING THIS SOPHISTICATED PATTERNING TECHNOLOGY TO MANUFACTURE ITS NEXT GENERATION OF DRAM, THE 1-GAMMA (1Γ) NODE. MICRON WILL BE THE FIRST SEMICONDUCTOR COMPANY TO BRING EUV TECHNOLOGY TO JAPAN FOR PRODUCTION, WITH ITS HIROSHIMA FAB PLAYING A CRITICAL ROLE IN THE COMPANY'S DEVELOPMENT OF THE 1-GAMMA NODE.
