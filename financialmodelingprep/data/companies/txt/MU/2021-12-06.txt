MICRON TO OPEN STATE-OF-THE-ART MEMORY DESIGN CENTER IN ATLANTA. ATLANTA, DEC. 06, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MICRON TECHNOLOGY, INC. (NASDAQ: MU), ONE OF THE WORLD'S LARGEST SEMICONDUCTOR MANUFACTURERS AND THE ONLY U.S.-BASED MANUFACTURER OF MEMORY, TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS FOR ITS NEW MEMORY DESIGN CENTER IN MIDTOWN ATLANTA, EXPANDING THE COMPANY'S REACH INTO THE SOUTHEAST.
