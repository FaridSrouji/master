MICRON NAMES COURTNEY GEDULDIG CORPORATE VICE PRESIDENT FOR PUBLIC AFFAIRS. BOISE, IDAHO, APRIL 07, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MICRON TECHNOLOGY, INC. (NASDAQ: MU), AN INDUSTRY LEADER IN INNOVATIVE MEMORY AND STORAGE SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF COURTNEY GEDULDIG TO THE ROLE OF CORPORATE VICE PRESIDENT FOR PUBLIC AFFAIRS.
