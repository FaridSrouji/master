MICRON CLOSES SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED CREDIT FACILITIES TOTALING NEARLY $3.7 BILLION. BOISE, IDAHO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MICRON TECHNOLOGY, INC. (NASDAQ: MU), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SUCCESSFUL CLOSING OF NEARLY $3.7 BILLION INAUGURAL SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED CREDIT FACILITIES. THE FACILITIES, WHICH CREATE ADDITIONAL LONG-TERM VALUE FOR MICRON'S STAKEHOLDERS, REINFORCE THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABILITY AND 2030 ENVIRONMENTAL GOALS.
