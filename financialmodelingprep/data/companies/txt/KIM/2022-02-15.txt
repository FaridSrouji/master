KIMCO REALTY® ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION IN FULL OF ITS 3.400% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2022. JERICHO, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KIMCO REALTY® (NYSE: KIM) (“KIMCO REALTY” OR THE “COMPANY”), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL REDEEM $500.0 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 3.400% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2022 (CUSIP NO. 49446R AN9) (THE “SENIOR NOTES”), REPRESENTING ALL OF THE OUTSTANDING SENIOR NOTES, ON MARCH 2, 2022 (THE “REDEMPTION DATE”).
