KIMCO REALTY'S TENANT ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (TAP) WILL HELP SMALL BUSINESSES ACCESS LATEST ROUND OF PPP FUNDING. JERICHO, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KIMCO REALTY CORP. (NYSE: KIM), ONE OF NORTH AMERICA'S LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED OWNERS AND OPERATORS OF OPEN-AIR, GROCERY-ANCHORED SHOPPING CENTERS AND MIXED-USE ASSETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS ONCE AGAIN DEPLOYED ITS TENANT ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (TAP) TO ASSIST SMALL BUSINESS TENANTS IN APPLYING FOR A SHARE OF THE $284 BILLION IN PAYCHECK PROTECTION PROGRAM (PPP) FUNDS RECENTLY APPROVED UNDER THE LATEST FEDERAL COVID-19 RELIEF BILL.
