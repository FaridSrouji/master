KIMCO REALTY® RECOGNIZED AS NAREIT'S 2022 RETAIL LEADER IN THE LIGHT FOR OUTSTANDING SUSTAINABILITY PRACTICES. JERICHO, N.Y.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--KIMCO REALTY® (NYSE: KIM), NORTH AMERICA'S LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED OWNER AND OPERATOR OF OPEN-AIR, GROCERY-ANCHORED SHOPPING CENTERS, AND A GROWING PORTFOLIO OF MIXED-USE ASSETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN AWARDED NAREIT'S LEADER IN THE LIGHT AWARD FOR THE RETAIL REIT SECTOR, MARKING THE THIRD TIME THE COMPANY HAS RECEIVED THIS PRESTIGIOUS RECOGNITION FOR ITS SUSTAINABILITY INITIATIVES.
