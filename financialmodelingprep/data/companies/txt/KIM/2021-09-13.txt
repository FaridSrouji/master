(NYSE: KIM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS PUBLIC OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.250% NOTES DUE 2031 (THE “NOTES”) WITH AN EFFECTIVE YIELD OF 2.301%, MATURING DECEMBER 1, 2031. THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO SETTLE ON SEPTEMBER 22, 2021, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
