SHAREHOLDER ALERT: MONTEVERDE & ASSOCIATES PC ANNOUNCES AN INVESTIGATION OF KIMCO REALTY CORP. - KIM. NEW YORK, APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JUAN MONTEVERDE , FOUNDER AND MANAGING PARTNER AT MONTEVERDE & ASSOCIATES PC, A NATIONAL SECURITIES FIRM RATED TOP 50 IN THE 2018-2020 ISS SECURITIES CLASS ACTION SERVICES REPORT AND HEADQUARTERED AT THE EMPIRE STATE BUILDING IN NEW YORK CITY, IS INVESTIGATING KIMCO REALTY CORP. ("KIM" OR THE "COMPANY") ( KIM ) RELATING TO ITS PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF WEINGARTEN REALTY INVESTORS (WRI). UNDER THE TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT, WRI SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE 1.408 SHARES OF KIMCO AND $2.89 IN CASH PER SHARE.
