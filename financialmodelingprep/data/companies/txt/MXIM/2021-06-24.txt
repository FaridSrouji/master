MAXIM INTEGRATED AND SICK AG TEAM UP FOR INDUSTRY'S SMALLEST LIDAR SAFETY LASER SCANNER. SAN JOSE, CALIF., JUNE 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MAXIM INTEGRATED PRODUCTS, INC. (NASDAQ: MXIM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS SOFTWARE-CONFIGURABLE DIGITAL IO PRODUCTS HELPED ENABLE A 50 PERCENT SIZE REDUCTION FOR THE MICROSCAN3 CORE I/O LIDAR-BASED SAFETY LASER SCANNER FROM SICK AG, A LEADER IN THE DESIGN AND MANUFACTURING OF INDUSTRIAL SENSOR-BASED SOLUTIONS.
