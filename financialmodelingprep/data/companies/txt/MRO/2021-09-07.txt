MARATHON OIL CORPORATION ANNOUNCES FULL REDEMPTION OF $900 MILLION 3.85% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025. HOUSTON, SEPT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  MARATHON OIL CORPORATION (NYSE: MRO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY FULLY REDEEMED ITS $900 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 3.85% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025, ACCELERATING THE REALIZATION OF MARATHON OIL'S PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED GROSS DEBT OBJECTIVE OF APPROXIMATELY $4.0 BILLION.
