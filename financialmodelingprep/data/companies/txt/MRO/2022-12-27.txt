MARATHON OIL COMPLETES EAGLE FORD ACQUISITION. HOUSTON, DEC. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON OIL CORPORATION (NYSE: MRO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF ITS ACQUISITION OF THE EAGLE FORD ASSETS OF ENSIGN NATURAL RESOURCES FOR A TOTAL CASH CONSIDERATION OF $3.0 BILLION AFTER TAKING INTO ACCOUNT CLOSING ADJUSTMENTS. THE ACQUISITION WAS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ON NOVEMBER 2, 2022.
