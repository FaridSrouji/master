MARATHON OIL PROVIDES PRELIMINARY FIRST QUARTER 2021 UPDATE. HOUSTON, APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON OIL CORPORATION (NYSE: MRO) TODAY PROVIDED PRELIMINARY INFORMATION REGARDING CERTAIN FIRST QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL ESTIMATES IN LIGHT OF UNCERTAINTY FROM WINTER STORM URI. THE COMPANY WILL REPORT FINAL FIRST QUARTER RESULTS ON WEDNESDAY, MAY 5, AFTER THE CLOSE OF U.S. FINANCIAL MARKETS.
