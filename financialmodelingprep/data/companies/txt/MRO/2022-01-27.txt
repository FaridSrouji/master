MARATHON OIL PROVIDES ESG UPDATE. HOUSTON, JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON OIL CORPORATION (NYSE: MRO) TODAY PROVIDED AN UPDATE REGARDING ITS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PERFORMANCE, INCLUDING AN ANNOUNCEMENT OF NEW QUANTITATIVE ENVIRONMENTAL OBJECTIVES. THE COMPANY BELIEVES CONTINUOUSLY IMPROVING ALL ELEMENTS OF ITS ESG PERFORMANCE IS FOUNDATIONAL TO MAXIMIZING LONG-TERM SHAREHOLDER VALUE.
