MARATHON OIL CORPORATION DECLARES SECOND QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. HOUSTON , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON OIL CORPORATION (NYSE: MRO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 8 CENTS PER SHARE ON MARATHON OIL CORPORATION COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 12, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 17, 2022.
