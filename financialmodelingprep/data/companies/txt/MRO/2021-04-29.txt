MARATHON OIL REDUCES GROSS DEBT AND RAISES BASE DIVIDEND. HOUSTON, APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON OIL CORPORATION (NYSE: MRO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY FULLY REDEEMED ITS $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.8% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2022. THIS TRANSACTION WILL REDUCE ANNUAL CASH INTEREST EXPENSE BY $14 MILLION.
