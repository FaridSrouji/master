MARATHON OIL CORPORATION DECLARES THIRD QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. HOUSTON, OCT. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON OIL CORPORATION (NYSE: MRO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 9 CENTS PER SHARE ON MARATHON OIL CORPORATION COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DEC. 12, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOV. 16, 2022.
