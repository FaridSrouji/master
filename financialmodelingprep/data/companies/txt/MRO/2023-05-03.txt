MARATHON OIL REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS. RETURNED $400 MILLION TO SHAREHOLDERS AND COMPLETED ENSIGN INTEGRATION HOUSTON,, MAY 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON OIL CORPORATION (NYSE: MRO) REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2023 NET INCOME OF $417 MILLION OR $0.66 PER DILUTED SHARE, WHICH INCLUDES THE IMPACT OF CERTAIN ITEMS NOT TYPICALLY REPRESENTED IN ANALYSTS' EARNINGS ESTIMATES AND THAT WOULD OTHERWISE AFFECT COMPARABILITY OF RESULTS. ADJUSTED NET INCOME WAS $420 MILLION OR $0.67 PER DILUTED SHARE.
