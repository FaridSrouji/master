MARATHON OIL CORPORATION DECLARES THIRD QUARTER 2021 DIVIDEND. HOUSTON, OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON OIL CORPORATION (NYSE: MRO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 6 CENTS PER SHARE ON MARATHON OIL CORPORATION COMMON STOCK. THIS REPRESENTS A 20% INCREASE FROM THE COMPANY'S LAST QUARTERLY BASE DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF 5 CENTS PER SHARE.
