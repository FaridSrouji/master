JOHN DEERE RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF THE 50 MOST COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN THE UNITED STATES IN 2023. MOLINE, ILL. , MAY 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR THE SECOND YEAR IN A ROW, JOHN DEERE (NYSE: DE) HAS BEEN NAMED AN HONOREE OF THE CIVIC 50, WHICH RECOGNIZES THE 50 MOST COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN THE NATION EACH YEAR ACCORDING TO AN ANNUAL SURVEY.
