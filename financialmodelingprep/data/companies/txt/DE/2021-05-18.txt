JOHN DEERE COMMITS $2.7 MILLION TO ASSIST INDIA IN FIGHT AGAINST COVID-19. MOLINE, ILL., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHN DEERE (NYSE: DE) ANNOUNCED TODAY COMMITMENTS OF OVER $2.7 MILLION (USD) TO PROVIDE URGENT MEDICAL RESOURCES AND ONGOING STATE SUPPORT TO ASSIST INDIA IN ITS FIGHT AGAINST COVID-19.
