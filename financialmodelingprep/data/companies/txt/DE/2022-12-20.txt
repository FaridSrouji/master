JOHN DEERE REPORTS RECORD COMMUNITY INVESTMENTS IN 2022. DIRECT GRANTS FROM THE JOHN DEERE FOUNDATION FOCUS ON RISING RATES OF HUNGER AND FOOD INSECURITY GLOBALLY MOLINE, ILL. , DEC. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHN DEERE (NYSE: DE) ANNOUNCED COMMUNITY INVESTMENTS TOTALING $55.5 MILLION IN 2022, A 30 PERCENT INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR YEAR.
