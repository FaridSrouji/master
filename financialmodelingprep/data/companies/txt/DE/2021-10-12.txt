DEERE EMPLOYEES REJECT TENTATIVE AGREEMENTS. MOLINE, ILL., OCT. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE INTERNATIONAL UNION, UNITED AUTOMOBILE, AEROSPACE AND AGRICULTURAL IMPLEMENT WORKERS OF AMERICA (UAW) HAS INFORMED DEERE & COMPANY (NYSE: DE) THAT ITS PRODUCTION AND MAINTENANCE EMPLOYEES HAVE VOTED TO REJECT THE TENTATIVE COLLECTIVE BARGAINING AGREEMENTS THAT WOULD HAVE COVERED MORE THAN 10,000 WORKERS AT 14 FACILITIES ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
