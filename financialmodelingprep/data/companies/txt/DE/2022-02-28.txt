JOHN DEERE ACQUIRES DEERE-HITACHI FACTORIES, BEGINS NEW LICENSE AND SUPPLY AGREEMENTS WITH HITACHI. MOLINE, ILL., FEB. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHN DEERE (NYSE: DE) HAS ACQUIRED FULL OWNERSHIP OF THREE DEERE-HITACHI JOINT VENTURE FACTORIES AND HAS BEGUN NEW LICENSE AND SUPPLY AGREEMENTS WITH HITACHI CONSTRUCTION MACHINERY.
