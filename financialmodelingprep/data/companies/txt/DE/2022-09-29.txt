JOHN DEERE ANNOUNCES REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS FOR SATELLITE COMMUNICATIONS OPPORTUNITY. MOLINE, ILL. , SEPT. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DEERE & COMPANY (NYSE: DE) ANNOUNCED IT HAS ISSUED A SATELLITE COMMUNICATIONS (SATCOM)-FOCUSED REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS TO SECURE A CUTTING-EDGE SOLUTION THAT WILL FURTHER CONNECT ITS FLEET OF INTELLIGENT MACHINES.
