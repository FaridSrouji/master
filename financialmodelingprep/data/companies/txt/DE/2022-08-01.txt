FELECIA PRYOR JOINS DEERE & COMPANY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF PEOPLE OFFICER. MOLINE, ILL. , AUG. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DEERE & COMPANY (NYSE:DE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FORMER BORGWARNER AND FORD MOTOR COMPANY EXECUTIVE, FELECIA PRYOR WILL JOIN ITS SENIOR LEADERSHIP TEAM AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF PEOPLE OFFICER EFFECTIVE AUGUST 15.
