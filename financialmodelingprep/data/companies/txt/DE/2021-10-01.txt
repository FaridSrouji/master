DEERE, UAW REACH TENTATIVE AGREEMENT. MOLINE, ILL., OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DEERE & COMPANY (NYSE: DE) AND THE INTERNATIONAL UNION, UNITED AUTOMOBILE, AEROSPACE AND AGRICULTURAL IMPLEMENT WORKERS OF AMERICA (UAW) HAVE REACHED A TENTATIVE AGREEMENT ON A NEW SIX-YEAR LABOR AGREEMENT COVERING APPROXIMATELY 10,100 PRODUCTION AND MAINTENANCE EMPLOYEES AT 12 FACILITIES IN IOWA, ILLINOIS, AND KANSAS.
