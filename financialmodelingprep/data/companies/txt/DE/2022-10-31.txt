JOHN DEERE ENTERS PARTNERSHIP WITH ADVANTAGE CAPITAL TO INCREASE INVESTMENT IN SUPPORTING UNDERREPRESENTED ENTREPRENEURS AND BUSINESS OWNERS. MOLINE, ILL. , OCT. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DEERE & COMPANY (NYSE: DE) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL BE ENTERING A PARTNERSHIP AS A LIMITED INVESTOR IN ADVANTAGE CAPITAL'S EMPOWER THE CHANGE (EPC) GROWTH FUND, WHICH PROVIDES ENTREPRENEURS OF COLOR ACCESS TO CAPITAL IN ORDER TO GROW THEIR BUSINESSES AND CREATE COMMUNITY WEALTH.
