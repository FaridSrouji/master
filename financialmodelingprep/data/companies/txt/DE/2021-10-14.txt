DEERE REMAINS COMMITTED TO REACHING NEW UAW AGREEMENT. MOLINE, ILL., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE INTERNATIONAL UNION, UNITED AUTOMOBILE, AEROSPACE AND AGRICULTURAL IMPLEMENT WORKERS OF AMERICA (UAW) HAS CALLED A STRIKE AGAINST DEERE & COMPANY (NYSE: DE), AFFECTING MORE THAN 10,000 WORKERS AT 14 FACILITIES ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
