JOHN DEERE AND IOWA STATE UNIVERSITY LAUNCH NEW DEMONSTRATION FARM TO HELP FARMERS FEED AND FUEL THE WORLD SUSTAINABLY. MOLINE, ILL. , OCT. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DEERE & COMPANY (NYSE: DE) ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH IOWA STATE UNIVERSITY (ISU) TO ESTABLISH A DEMONSTRATION SITE CONSISTING OF FOUR DIFFERENT FIELDS AND PROCESSES THAT WILL ENABLE DEERE TO TEST SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS FOR LARGE GRAIN PRODUCTION SYSTEMS IN REAL-WORLD SCENARIOS.
