WYNN RESORTS MACAU SIGNS 10-YEAR GAMING CONCESSION AGREEMENT. LAS VEGAS , DEC. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  WYNN RESORTS IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT WYNN RESORTS (MACAU) S.A. HAS ENTERED INTO A 10-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH THE MACAU GOVERNMENT FOR THE RENEWAL OF ITS GAMING CONCESSION, COVERING WYNN MACAU AND WYNN PALACE COTAI.
