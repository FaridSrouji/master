WYNN LAS VEGAS UPDATES MASK POLICY TO COMPLY WITH CDC GUIDELINES. LAS VEGAS, MAY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BEGINNING THIS EVENING, WYNN LAS VEGAS (NASDAQ: WYNN) AND ENCORE WILL COMMUNICATE TO GUESTS THAT THEY ARE NOT REQUIRED TO WEAR A MASK IF THEY ARE FULLY VACCINATED AND ARE REQUIRED TO WEAR A MASK IF THEY ARE NOT.  THE RESORT TRUSTS GUESTS TO TAKE THE APPROPRIATE PRECAUTIONS BASED ON THEIR PERSONAL VACCINATION STATUS.
