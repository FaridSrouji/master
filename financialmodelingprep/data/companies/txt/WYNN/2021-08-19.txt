WYNNBET INTEGRATING WYNN REWARDS INTO ONLINE SPORTS BETTING & IGAMING PLATFORMS. LAS VEGAS, AUG. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WYNNBET, THE PREMIER ONLINE SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING APP FROM THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LUXURY HOSPITALITY, WYNN RESORTS, ANNOUNCED ON THURSDAY THAT THE COMPREHENSIVE WYNN REWARDS PROGRAM IS BEING INTEGRATED INTO ITS ONLINE PLATFORMS. THROUGH THIS CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE DRIVEN PROGRAM, WYNNBET AND WYNN RESORTS PATRONS WILL BE ABLE TO EARN REWARDS THROUGH MOBILE, DESKTOP AND ON-PROPERTY PLAY.
