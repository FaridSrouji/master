WYNN RESORTS INCLUDED ON LIST OF 50 MOST COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN AMERICA. THE COMPANY RECEIVES RECOGNITION FROM POINTS OF LIGHT FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR  LAS VEGAS , MAY 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WYNN RESORTS (NASDAQ: WYNN) HAS BEEN NAMED A 2023 HONOREE ON THE CIVIC 50 LIST BY POINTS OF LIGHT, WHICH RECOGNIZES THE TOP 50 MOST COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN THE UNITED STATES. FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR, THE COMPANY WAS RECOGNIZED FOR USING TIME, TALENT AND RESOURCES TO DRIVE SOCIAL IMPACT IN BUSINESS AND IN THE COMMUNITY.
