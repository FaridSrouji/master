WYNNBET APPOINTS SPORTS BETTING & IGAMING INDUSTRY VETERAN IAN WILLIAMS AS CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER. LAS VEGAS, OCT. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WYNNBET, THE PREMIER ONLINE SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING APP FROM THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LUXURY HOSPITALITY, WYNN RESORTS, ANNOUNCED ON MONDAY IT HAS APPOINTED 20-YEAR GAMING INDUSTRY VETERAN IAN WILLIAMS AS CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER. IN THIS ROLE, WILLIAMS WILL OVERSEE WYNNBET'S TRADING, MARKETING, CUSTOMER SERVICE, PAYMENTS AND DATA SCIENCE TEAMS AND REPORT DIRECTLY TO WYNN INTERACTIVE CEO CRAIG BILLINGS.
