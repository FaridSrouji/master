WYNNBET NOW LIVE IN TENNESSEE, ADDING SIXTH STATE TO GROWING ROSTER. JERSEY CITY, N.J., APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WYNNBET, THE PREMIER CASINO AND SPORTS BETTING APP FROM THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LUXURY HOSPITALITY, WYNN RESORTS, OPENED ITS MOBILE SPORTS BOOK IN TENNESSEE TODAY FOLLOWING APPROVAL FROM THE SPORTS WAGERING COMMITTEE OF THE TENNESSEE EDUCATION LOTTERY CORPORATION'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
