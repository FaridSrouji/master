WYNN RESORTS NAMED TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S 2022 WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES LIST. LAS VEGAS, FEB. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WYNN RESORTS WAS AGAIN HONORED ON FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S 2022 WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES LIST IN THE HOTEL, CASINO AND RESORT CATEGORY. THE COMPANY PLACED FIRST OVERALL IN THE GROUP FOR QUALITY OF PRODUCTS/SERVICES.
