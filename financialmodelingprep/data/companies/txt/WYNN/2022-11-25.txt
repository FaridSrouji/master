WYNN RESORTS MACAU RECEIVES GAMING CONCESSION RENEWAL. LAS VEGAS , NOV. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WYNN RESORTS (NASDAQ: WYNN) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT WYNN RESORTS MACAU RECEIVED A PROVISIONAL AWARD OF A GAMING CONCESSION FROM THE MACAU GOVERNMENT. THE ULTIMATE AWARD OF THE GAMING CONCESSION CONTRACT REMAINS SUBJECT TO THE FINAL DOCUMENTATION OF ITS EXACT TERMS AND CONDITIONS WITH THE MACAU GOVERNMENT.
