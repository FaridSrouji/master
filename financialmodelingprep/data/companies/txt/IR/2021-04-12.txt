PLATINUM EQUITY TO ACQUIRE CLUB CAR FROM INGERSOLL RAND FOR $1.7 BILLION. LOS ANGELES, APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PLATINUM EQUITY ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE CLUB CAR FROM INGERSOLL RAND (NYSE:IR) IN A TRANSACTION VALUED AT APPROXIMATELY $1.7 BILLION. THE ACQUISITION, SUBJECT TO STANDARD CLOSING CONDITIONS, IS EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED BY THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021.
