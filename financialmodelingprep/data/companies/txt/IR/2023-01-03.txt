INGERSOLL RAND COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF SPX FLOW'S AIR TREATMENT BUSINESS. DAVIDSON, N.C.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--INGERSOLL RAND INC. (NYSE:IR), A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF MISSION-CRITICAL FLOW CREATION AND INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS, HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF SPX FLOW'S AIR TREATMENT BUSINESS IN AN ALL-CASH TRANSACTION OF APPROXIMATELY $525 MILLION.
