INGERSOLL RAND NAMES ENRIQUE MINARRO VISERAS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER, GLOBAL PRECISION & SCIENCE TECHNOLOGIES. DAVIDSON, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INGERSOLL RAND INC. (NYSE: IR) HAS APPOINTED ENRIQUE MINARRO VISERAS AS ITS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER OF THE GLOBAL PRECISION & SCIENCE TECHNOLOGIES (PST) SEGMENT, REPORTING TO VICENTE REYNAL, CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
