CUMMINS TO SUPPLY 35MW ELECTROLYZER SYSTEM TO LINDE FOR GREEN HYDROGEN PRODUCTION IN NIAGARA FALLS, NEW YORK. COLUMBUS, IND.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) WILL SUPPLY A 35-MEGAWATT (MW) PROTON EXCHANGE MEMBRANE (PEM) ELECTROLYZER SYSTEM FOR LINDE'S NEW HYDROGEN PRODUCTION PLANT IN NIAGARA FALLS, NEW YORK. ONCE COMMISSIONED, CUMMINS' ELECTROLYZER SYSTEM WILL POWER LINDE'S LARGEST GREEN HYDROGEN PLANT IN THE U.S., MARKING SIGNIFICANT PROGRESS IN MOVING THE GREEN HYDROGEN ECONOMY FORWARD.
