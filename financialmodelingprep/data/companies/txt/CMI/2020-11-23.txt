CUMMINS INC.: MOMENTUM FOR HYDROGEN TRANSIT GROWS AS EUROPEAN CONSUMERS PRIORITISE CLIMATE BEFORE COST WHEN IT COMES TO TRAVEL. GLADBECK, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN CONJUNCTION WITH EUROPEAN HYDROGEN WEEK, GLOBAL POWER LEADER CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) TODAY SHARED NEW RESEARCH REVEALING THAT MORE THAN HALF OF THE UNITED KINGDOM (UK) COMMUTERS WOULD BE WILLING TO TRAVEL TO WORK ON A TRAIN OR BUS POWERED BY HYDROGEN TO LOWER THEIR CARBON FOOTPRINT AND REDUCE EMISSIONS AND NEARLY HALF OF COMMUTERS IN BELGIUM AND GERMANY EXPRESSED THE SAME VIEW.
