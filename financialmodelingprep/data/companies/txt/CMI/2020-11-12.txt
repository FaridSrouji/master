CUMMINS TO OPEN NEW FUEL CELL SYSTEMS PRODUCTION FACILITY IN GERMANY, STRENGTHENING ITS COMMITMENT TO HYDROGEN IN EUROPE. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL OPEN A NEW FACILITY IN HERTEN, GERMANY, WHICH WILL INITIALLY FOCUS ON THE ASSEMBLY OF FUEL CELL SYSTEMS FOR GLOBAL TRANSPORTATION LEADER ALSTOM'S HYDROGEN TRAINS.
