CUMMINS ANNOUNCES FILING OF IPO REGISTRATION STATEMENT FOR FILTRATION BUSINESS. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS FILTRATION BUSINESS, ATMUS FILTRATION TECHNOLOGIES, HAS FILED A REGISTRATION STATEMENT ON FORM S-1 WITH THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION (THE "SEC") FOR A PROPOSED UNDERWRITTEN INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING (IPO) OF NEWLY ISSUED COMMON STOCK. ATMUS FILTRATION TECHNOLOGIES INTENDS TO APPLY TO HAVE ITS COMMON STOCK LISTED ON THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE (NYSE) UNDER THE SYMBOL ATMU.
