ISUZU AND CUMMINS ANNOUNCE BATTERY ELECTRIC TRUCK COLLABORATION. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) AND ISUZU MOTORS LIMITED ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO CREATE A PROTOTYPE MEDIUM-DUTY, BATTERY ELECTRIC TRUCK TO DEMONSTRATE IN NORTH AMERICA. THIS TRUCK REPRESENTS THE FIRST ZERO-EMISSIONS SOLUTION FACILITATED BY THE ISUZU CUMMINS POWERTRAIN PARTNERSHIP (ICPP) FORMED IN MAY 2019. THROUGH THIS ARRANGEMENT, CUMMINS WILL INTEGRATE THE CUMMINS POWERDRIVE6000 INTO ISUZU'S F-SERIES TRUCK AND WILL PILOT THE TRUCK WITH PROMINENT NORTH AMERICAN FLEE
