DAIMLER TRUCK AG AND CUMMINS INC. HAVE SIGNED GLOBAL FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT FOR COOPERATION IN MEDIUM-DUTY COMMERCIAL VEHICLE ENGINES. STUTTGART, GERMANY & COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DAIMLER TRUCK AG AND U.S.-BASED GLOBAL POWER LEADER AND ENGINE MANUFACTURER CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) ANNOUNCED IN FEBRUARY THAT THEY WOULD ENTER INTO A GLOBAL STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP FOR MEDIUM-DUTY ENGINES. THE DETAILS OF THE AGREEMENT HAVE BEEN ESTABLISHED AND THE FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT HAS BEEN SIGNED BY BOTH COMPANIES.
