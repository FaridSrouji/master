ATMUS FILTRATION TECHNOLOGIES ANNOUNCES PRICING OF INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ATMUS FILTRATION TECHNOLOGIES INC. (ATMUS), THE FILTRATION BUSINESS UNIT OF CUMMINS INC. (CUMMINS; NYSE: CMI), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING (IPO) OF 14,124,409 SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK OWNED BY CUMMINS AT A PRICE TO THE PUBLIC OF $19.50 PER SHARE. THE SHARES ARE EXPECTED TO BEGIN TRADING ON THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE (NYSE) ON MAY 26, 2023 UNDER THE TICKER SYMBOL “ATMU.” ATMUS IS NOT SELLING ANY SHARES AND WILL NOT RECEIVE ANY.
