CUMMINS' FILTRATION BUSINESS SUBMITS CONFIDENTIAL REGISTRATION STATEMENT FOR PROPOSED INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS FILTRATION BUSINESS HAS CONFIDENTIALLY SUBMITTED A DRAFT REGISTRATION STATEMENT ON FORM S-1 WITH THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION (THE "SEC") FOR A PROPOSED UNDERWRITTEN INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING (THE "IPO") OF NEWLY ISSUED COMMON STOCK OF THE BUSINESS IN THE UNITED STATES. THE NUMBER OF SHARES TO BE OFFERED AND THE PRICE RANGE FOR THE IPO HAVE NOT YET BEEN DETERMINED. THE IPO IS EXPECTED TO COMMENCE AFT
