CUMMINS INC. AND TATA MOTORS STRENGTHEN THEIR 30-YEAR ALLIANCE TO POWER A CLEANER INDIA WITH LOW- TO ZERO-EMISSIONS TECHNOLOGIES FOR MOBILITY SOLUTIONS. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GLOBAL POWER LEADER CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT WITH TATA MOTORS LIMITED (TML), TO MANUFACTURE A RANGE OF LOW- TO ZERO-EMISSIONS TECHNOLOGY PRODUCTS IN INDIA OVER THE NEXT FEW YEARS. THE DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT WAS SIGNED BY JENNIFER RUMSEY, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, CUMMINS INC., AND GIRISH WAGH, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR, TATA MOTORS, AT CUMMINS INC. HEADQUARTERS IN COLUMBUS, INDIANA, USA. SENIOR OFFICI.
