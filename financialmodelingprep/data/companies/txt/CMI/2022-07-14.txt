JENNIFER RUMSEY PROMOTED TO PRESIDENT AND CEO OF CUMMINS. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) ANNOUNCED THAT, EFFECTIVE AUGUST 1, 2022, TOM LINEBARGER WILL END HIS TERM AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER (CEO) AND JENNIFER RUMSEY, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER (COO), WILL ASSUME THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT AND CEO. RUMSEY WILL BE THE SEVENTH CEO AND FIRST WOMAN TO LEAD THE COMPANY SINCE IT WAS FOUNDED IN 1919.
