CUMMINS INC. INCREASES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) TODAY APPROVED AN INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK OF APPROXIMATELY 8 PERCENT TO 1.57 DOLLARS PER SHARE FROM 1.45 DOLLARS PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 1, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 19, 2022. ABOUT CUMMINS INC.
