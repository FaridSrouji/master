CUMMINS ANNOUNCES MARY CHANDLER RETIRING AFTER SERVING CUMMINS AND ITS GLOBAL COMMUNITIES SINCE 2011. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #CUMMINS--TODAY, CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) ANNOUNCED THAT MARY CHANDLER, VICE PRESIDENT – CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY AND COMMUNITY RELATIONS AND CEO OF THE CUMMINS FOUNDATION, IS RETIRING LATER THIS SPRING AND JIM SCHACHT, CURRENTLY A MEMBER OF THE CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY LEADERSHIP TEAM, WILL SUCCEED MARY AND BE PROMOTED TO VICE PRESIDENT OF CUMMINS.
