CUMMINS REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021. SECOND QUARTER REVENUES OF $6.1 BILLION INCREASED 59 PERCENT FROM THE SAME QUARTER IN 2020. SALES IN NORTH AMERICA INCREASED 74 PERCENT WHILE INTERNATIONAL REVENUES INCREASED 42 PERCENT DRIVEN BY STRONG DEMAND ACROSS ALL GLOBAL MARKETS COMPARED TO THE SAME QUARTER IN 2020, WHICH WAS IMPACTED SIGNIFICANTLY BY THE PANDEMIC. CURRENCY POSITIVELY IMPACTED SALES BY 3 PERCENT PRIMARILY DUE
