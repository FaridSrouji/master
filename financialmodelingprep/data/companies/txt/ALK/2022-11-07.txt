ALASKA AIRLINES NAMES NEW VICE PRESIDENT OF FLIGHT OPERATIONS. 23-YEAR VETERAN OF THE AIRLINE TO LEAD 3,500-STRONG PILOT GROUP SEATTLE , NOV. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES' BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED CAPTAIN DAVE METS AS VICE PRESIDENT OF FLIGHT OPERATIONS. IN THIS ROLE, METS WILL OVERSEE THE DAY-TO-DAY OPERATIONS AND FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE OF ALASKA'S FLIGHT OPERATIONS DIVISION, WHICH INCLUDES ALASKA'S 3,500 PILOTS.
