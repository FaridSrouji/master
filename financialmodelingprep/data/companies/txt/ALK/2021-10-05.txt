ALASKA AIRLINES LAUNCHES NEW CODESHARE AGREEMENT WITH IBERIA AIRLINES. SEATTLE, OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES FURTHER EXPANDED ITS GLOBAL REACH TODAY WITH THE ANNOUNCEMENT OF A NEW CODESHARE AGREEMENT WITH IBERIA AIRLINES, A FELLOW MEMBER OF THE ONEWORLD ALLIANCE . THE AGREEMENT FURTHER STRENGTHENS THE EXISTING PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN THE TWO AIRLINES BY PROVIDING FLYERS WITH EXCITING AND CONVENIENT TRAVEL OPTIONS.
