ALASKA AIR GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCES CONSOLIDATED OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE MONTH AND YEAR TO DATE ENDED SEPTEMBER 2019. ALASKA AIR GROUP, INC. PASSENGER LOAD FACTOR WAS 83.0% COMPARED TO 80.8% A YEAR AGO. FOR THE YEAR TO DATE, THE COMPANY REPORTED REVENUE PASSENGERS OF 35,018,000 COMPARED TO 34,685,000 A YEAR AGO. REVENUE PASSENGER MILES WERE 42,113,000,000 COMPARED TO 41,272,000,000 A YEAR AGO. AVAILABLE SEAT MILES WERE 50,006,000,000 COMPARED TO 49,256,000,000 A YEAR AGO. PASSENGER LOAD FACTOR WAS 84.2% COMPARED TO 83.8% A YEAR AGO.
