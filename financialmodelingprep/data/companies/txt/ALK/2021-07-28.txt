ALASKA AIRLINES APPOINTS 34-YEAR AIRLINE VETERAN TO LEAD SALES. SEATTLE, JULY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES HAS NAMED 34-YEAR AIRLINE VETERAN TONI FREEBERG MANAGING DIRECTOR OF SALES EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. FREEBERG ASSUMES THE ROLE CURRENTLY HELD BY MARK BOCCHI, WHO WILL RETIRE FROM ALASKA AIRLINES IN OCTOBER AFTER 40 YEARS.
