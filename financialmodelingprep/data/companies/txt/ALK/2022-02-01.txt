ALASKA AIRLINES EXPANDS FINNAIR CODESHARE AGREEMENT FOR DEEPER GLOBAL REACH. SEATTLE, FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES HAS ANNOUNCED TODAY A SIGNIFICANT CODESHARE EXPANSION WITH OUR EUROPEAN ONEWORLD PARTNER FINNAIR, THE LATEST IN A SERIES OF PARTNERSHIP EXPANSIONS FOR ALASKA SINCE JOINING THE GLOBAL ALLIANCE IN MARCH 2021. BEGINNING NOW, FINNAIR PASSENGERS CAN BOOK CONNECTING FLIGHTS THROUGH SEATTLE – OUR HOMETOWN AIRPORT – TO MORE THAN 60 DESTINATIONS THROUGHOUT ALASKA'S NETWORK FOR TRAVEL BEGINNING JUNE 1, 2022.
