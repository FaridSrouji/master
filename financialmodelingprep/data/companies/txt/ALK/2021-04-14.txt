ALASKA AIRLINES AND SKYNRG SIGN PARTNERSHIP TO ADVANCE SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL MADE FROM MUNICIPAL SOLID WASTE. SEATTLE, APRIL 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES AND SKYNRG AMERICAS TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) COMMITTING TO INCREASED INVESTMENT IN SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL (SAF). THE MOU BUILDS ON A LONG HISTORY OF ALASKA LEADERSHIP ADVANCING SAF AND PARTNERS THE AIRLINE WITH THE GLOBAL SAF PIONEERS AT SKYNRG AMERICAS.
