ALASKA AIRLINES ADDS NEW NONSTOP SERVICE BETWEEN ANCHORAGE AND SALT LAKE CITY. SEATTLE, OCT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES ANNOUNCED TODAY THE START OF NEW NONSTOP SERVICE BETWEEN ANCHORAGE, OUR KEY HUB IN THE STATE OF ALASKA, AND SALT LAKE CITY. WITH THIS FLIGHT – AND OUR NEW ONE BETWEEN ANCHORAGE AND MINNEAPOLIS-ST. PAUL – WE WILL OFFER NONSTOP SERVICE CONNECTING ALASKA TO THE TOP 10 TRAVEL DESTINATIONS IN THE CONTINENTAL U.S. THIS SUMMER.
