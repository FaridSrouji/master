TRAINING THE NEXT GENERATION OF PILOTS: ALASKA AIRLINES AND HORIZON AIR LAUNCH ASCEND PILOT ACADEMY. PORTLAND, ORE., MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES AND ITS REGIONAL PARTNER HORIZON AIR ARE TEAMING UP WITH HILLSBORO AERO ACADEMY, A PREMIER FLIGHT SCHOOL IN THE PACIFIC NORTHWEST, TO LAUNCH THE ASCEND PILOT ACADEMY (APA).
