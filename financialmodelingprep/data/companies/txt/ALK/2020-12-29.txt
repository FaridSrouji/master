ALASKA AIRLINES ANNOUNCES REVISIONS TO ITS SERVICE ANIMAL POLICY. SEATTLE, DEC. 29, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOLLOWING RECENT CHANGES TO U.S. DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION'S (DOT) RULES, ALASKA AIRLINES WILL NO LONGER ACCEPT EMOTIONAL SUPPORT ANIMALS ON ITS FLIGHTS. EFFECTIVE JAN. 11, 2021, ALASKA WILL ONLY TRANSPORT SERVICE DOGS, WHICH ARE SPECIALLY TRAINED...
