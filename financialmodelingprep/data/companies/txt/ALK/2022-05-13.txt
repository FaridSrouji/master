AIRCRAFT TECHNICIANS AT HORIZON AIR RATIFY NEW TWO-YEAR CONTRACT. SEATTLE, MAY 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORIZON AIR AIRCRAFT TECHNICIANS AND FLEET SERVICE AGENTS, WHO ARE REPRESENTED BY THE AIRCRAFT MECHANICS FRATERNAL ASSOCIATION (AMFA), HAVE RATIFIED A NEW TWO-YEAR CONTRACT. THE CONTRACT WAS APPROVED BY 91% OF THOSE EMPLOYEES WHO VOTED.
