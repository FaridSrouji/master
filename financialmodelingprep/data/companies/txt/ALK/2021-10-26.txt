ALASKA AIR GROUP COLLABORATING WITH ZEROAVIA TO DEVELOP HYDROGEN POWERTRAIN FOR 76-SEAT ZERO-EMISSION AIRCRAFT. - ZEROAVIA WILL INCORPORATE A 3MW+ HYDROGEN-ELECTRIC POWERTRAIN SYSTEM INTO A DE HAVILLAND DHC-8-400 (Q400) AIRCRAFT. - JOINT DEVELOPMENT COLLABORATION ALSO ADDS ALASKA AIRLINES TO ZEROAVIA'S LIST OF INVESTORS.
