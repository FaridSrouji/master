ALASKA AIR GROUP SAYS AUGUST CASH BURN WAS ABOUT $80 MILLION. ALASKA AIR GROUP INC - AUGUST CASH BURN WAS APPROXIMATELY $80 MILLION, WHICH WAS LOWER THAN CASH BURN IN JULY.ALASKA AIR GROUP - EXPECT SEPTEMBER CASH BURN WILL BE APPROXIMATELY $150 MILLION.ALASKA AIR GROUP - AS OF SEPTEMBER 8,  AIR GROUP HAD CASH AND SHORT-TERM INVESTMENTS OF APPROXIMATELY $3.6 BILLION.ALASKA AIR GROUP INC - SEES SEPT REVENUE PASSENGERS YEAR-OVER-YEAR DOWN 65%-70%.ALASKA AIR GROUP - PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS ARE FOR Q3 CAPACITY TO BE DOWN APPROXIMATELY 55%.ALASKA AIR GROUP INC - SEES SEPT TOTAL REVENUE YEAR-OVER-YEAR DOWN ABOUT 70-75%.
