ALASKA AIRLINES NAMES NEW VICE PRESIDENT OF MAINTENANCE AND ENGINEERING. SEATTLE, AUG. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES' BOARD OF DIRECTORS ELECTED AVIATION VETERAN DONALD WRIGHT VICE PRESIDENT OF MAINTENANCE AND ENGINEERING, EFFECTIVE AUG. 23, 2021. WRIGHT WILL ASSUME THE ROLE FORMERLY HELD BY CONSTANCE VON MUEHLEN WHO WAS APPOINTED CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER ON APRIL 3.
