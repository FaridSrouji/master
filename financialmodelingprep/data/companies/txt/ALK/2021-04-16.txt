ALASKA AIRLINES EXPANDS SERVICE AND PRESENCE AT SANTA ROSA/SONOMA COUNTY. SEATTLE, APRIL 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES IS INCREASING ITS SERVICE AND COMMITMENT TO SANTA ROSA/SONOMA COUNTY WITH MORE NONSTOP FLIGHTS BETWEEN SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA AND THE HEART OF WINE COUNTRY. STARTING JUNE 1, THE AIRLINE WILL OFFER DAILY NONSTOP SERVICE BETWEEN SANTA ROSA/SONOMA COUNTY AND BURBANK.
