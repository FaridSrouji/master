FROM CUSTOMER SERVICE AGENT TO VP: CELLEY BUCHANAN ELECTED VICE PRESIDENT OF AIRPORT OPERATIONS AND CUSTOMER SERVICE AT ALASKA AIRLINES. SEATTLE , JUNE 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES' BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED CELLEY BUCHANAN VICE PRESIDENT OF AIRPORT OPERATIONS AND CUSTOMER SERVICE. IN THIS ROLE SHE IS RESPONSIBLE FOR ALL ASPECTS OF THE AIRLINE'S AIRPORT OPERATIONS ACROSS ITS BROAD NETWORK, EXCLUDING SEATTLE.
