METLIFE ANNOUNCES LEGAL PLAN OFFERING FOR THE FEDERAL FAMILY. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--METLIFE ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE METLIFE FEDERAL LEGAL PLAN IS NOW AVAILABLE TO 9 MILLION FEDERAL EMPLOYEES, RETIREES, AND CERTAIN MEMBERS OF THE UNIFORMED SERVICES. UNDER THE METLIFE FEDERAL LEGAL PLAN, PARTICIPANTS AND THEIR DEPENDENTS GAIN ACCESS TO A NETWORK OF OVER 18,000 HIGHLY QUALIFIED ATTORNEYS AND COVERAGE FOR A BROAD RANGE OF PERSONAL LEGAL MATTERS.
