METLIFE HONORED AS A MEMBER OF THE 2023 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FOR THE EIGHTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI), WHICH TRACKS THE PERFORMANCE OF PUBLIC COMPANIES THAT REPORT GENDER-RELATED DATA.
