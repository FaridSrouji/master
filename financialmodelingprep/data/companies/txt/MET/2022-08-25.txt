METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT ORIGINATES A RECORD $9.2 BILLION IN PRIVATE PLACEMENT DEBT AND PRIVATE STRUCTURED CREDIT FOR 1H 2022. WHIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (MIM), THE INSTITUTIONAL ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT ORIGINATED $9.2 BILLION IN PRIVATE PLACEMENT DEBT AND PRIVATE STRUCTURED CREDIT FOR THE FIRST HALF OF 2022 ACROSS 137 TRANSACTIONS. THIS WAS A RECORD FOR MIM AND INCLUDED $2.3 BILLION OF INVESTMENTS ORIGINATED ON BEHALF OF UNAFFILIATED INSTITUTIONAL CLIENTS. MIM'S TOTAL PRIVATE PLACEMENT ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT STOOD AT $90.5 BILLION
