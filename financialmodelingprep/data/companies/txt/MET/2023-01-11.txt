METLIFE ANNOUNCES FULL REDEMPTION OF 4.368% SERIES D SENIOR DEBENTURES. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL REDEEM ALL OF ITS OUTSTANDING 4.368% SERIES D SENIOR DEBENTURES (THE "NOTES"), PURSUANT TO THE TERMS OF THE NOTES. THE FULL $1 BILLION OUTSTANDING PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF THE NOTES WILL BE REDEEMED ON FEBRUARY 10, 2023 (THE “REDEMPTION DATE”).
