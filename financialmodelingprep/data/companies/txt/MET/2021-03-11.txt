METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT ORIGINATES $15.7 BILLION IN PRIVATE PLACEMENT DEBT AND PRIVATE STRUCTURED CREDIT IN 2020. WHIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (MIM), THE INSTITUTIONAL ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT ORIGINATED $15.7 BILLION IN PRIVATE PLACEMENT DEBT AND PRIVATE STRUCTURED CREDIT ACROSS 215 TRANSACTIONS IN 2020. THIS INCLUDED $3.6 BILLION OF INVESTMENTS ORIGINATED ON BEHALF OF THIRD PARTY INSTITUTIONAL CLIENTS.
