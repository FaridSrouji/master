METLIFE ANNOUNCES NEW DENTAL WELLNESS INCENTIVE TO REWARD HEALTHY BEHAVIORS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #FINANCE--METLIFE ANNOUNCED TODAY THE LAUNCH OF A NEW DENTAL WELLNESS INCENTIVE, DESIGNED TO HELP EMPLOYEES MAKE THE MOST OF THEIR DENTAL BENEFITS BY ENCOURAGING PREVENTIVE CARE, HELPING REDUCE OUT-OF-POCKET COSTS, AND PROMOTING WHOLE-PERSON HEALTH. THE PROVISION REWARDS EMPLOYEES FOR POSITIVE ORAL HEALTH BEHAVIOR - SUCH AS ROUTINE CLEANINGS AND EXAMS - BY INCREASING A MEMBER'S ANNUAL MAXIMUM AND/OR COINSURANCE PERCENTAGES, AND/OR BY REDUCING PLAN DEDUCTIBLES.
