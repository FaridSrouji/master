METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT BROADENS INVESTOR ACCESS TO PUBLIC FIXED INCOME STRATEGIES. HONG KONG--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (MIM), THE INSTITUTIONAL ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET), ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED THE FIRST PHASE OF A COLLECTIVE INVESTMENT TRUST (CIT) OFFERING WITH SEI TRUST COMPANY (STC) AS THE CIT TRUSTEE. MIM'S SUITE OF CITS WILL EXPAND INVESTOR ACCESS TO ITS PUBLIC FIXED INCOME STRATEGIES AND CAPABILITIES, PARTICULARLY FOR THE DEFINED CONTRIBUTION MARKET. JUDE DRISCOLL, HEAD OF PUBLIC FIXED INCOME FOR METLIFE INVESTMENT
