METLIFE NAMES KATANYA MOORE HEAD OF REGULATORY POLICY. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KATANYA MOORE WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS VICE PRESIDENT AND HEAD OF REGULATORY POLICY, EFFECTIVE JUNE 8, 2021. SHE WILL OVERSEE REGULATORY ANALYSIS AND ADVOCACY FOR ISSUES AT THE STATE, FEDERAL AND INTERNATIONAL LEVEL. MOORE WILL REPORT TO SUSAN GREENWELL, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND HEAD OF GLOBAL GOVERNMENT RELATIONS. “KATANYA BRINGS MANY YEARS OF EXPERIENCE DEVELOPING AND IMPLEMENTING REGULATORY STRATEGIES FOR A WIDE RANGE
