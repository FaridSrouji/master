METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT PROVIDES GBP 122.5 MILLION IN FINANCING FOR TRIPLE POINT SOCIAL HOUSING REIT PLC. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (MIM), THE INSTITUTIONAL ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ARRANGED GBP 122.5 MILLION IN LOAN NOTES FOR TRIPLE POINT SOCIAL HOUSING REIT PLC (TRIPLE POINT) (LSE: SOHO) TO REFINANCE EXISTING DEBT AND SUPPORT THE FUTURE GROWTH OF THE COMPANY.
