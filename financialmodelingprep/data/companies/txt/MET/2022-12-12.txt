METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT NAMED A BEST PLACE TO WORK IN MONEY MANAGEMENT FOR SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR. WHIPPANY, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (MIM), THE INSTITUTIONAL ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED A “2022 BEST PLACE TO WORK IN MONEY MANAGEMENT” BY PENSIONS & INVESTMENTS. THIS MARKS MIM'S SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR ON THE LIST.
