METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT PARTNERSHIPS WITH NORGES BANK INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT AND NEW YORK STATE COMMON RETIREMENT FUND RECEIVE 5 STAR RATING FROM GLOBAL REAL ESTATE SUSTAINABILITY BENCHMARK. WHIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (MIM), THE INSTITUTIONAL ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT TWO REAL ESTATE PARTNERSHIPS WITH NORGES BANK INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT AND NEW YORK STATE COMMON RETIREMENT FUND (“MCPP PORTFOLIO”) HAVE RECEIVED 5 STAR RATINGS FROM GLOBAL REAL ESTATE SUSTAINABILITY BENCHMARK (GRESB), WHICH ALSO RECOGNIZED THE PARTNERSHIPS AS “SECTOR LEADERS”. A THIRD MIM CORE REAL ESTATE PORTFOLIO WAS ALSO RECOGNIZED
