(NYSE: MET) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MICHEL KHALAF, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AND JOHN MCCALLION, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A FIRESIDE CHAT AT THE GOLDMAN SACHS 2022 US FINANCIAL SERVICES CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 7, 2022, BEGINNING AT APPROXIMATELY 8:40 A.M. (ET).
