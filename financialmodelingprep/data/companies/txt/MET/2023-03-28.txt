METLIFE ONCE AGAIN EARNS 2023 ENERGY STAR® PARTNER OF THE YEAR ‘SUSTAINED EXCELLENCE' AWARD. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED THE 2023 ENERGY STAR® PARTNER OF THE YEAR SUSTAINED EXCELLENCE AWARD FROM THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (EPA) AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY.
