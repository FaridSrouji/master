METLIFE FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES STRATEGY TO DRIVE INCLUSIVE ECONOMIC MOBILITY. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGY AIMED AT DRIVING INCLUSIVE ECONOMIC MOBILITY BY ADDRESSING THE NEEDS OF UNDERSERVED AND UNDERREPRESENTED COMMUNITIES AROUND THE GLOBE. THIS MULTI-PRONGED STRATEGY BUILDS UPON METLIFE'S 2030 DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION COMMITMENTS, THROUGH WHICH THE FOUNDATION PLEDGED $150 MILLION IN FUNDING AND METLIFE PLEDGED 800,000 EMPLOYEE VOLUNTEER HOURS IN SUPPORT OF HISTORICALLY MARGINALIZED INDIVIDUALS AND THEIR FAMILIES BETWEE
