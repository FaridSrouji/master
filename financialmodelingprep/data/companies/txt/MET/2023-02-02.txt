METLIFE AMONG THE WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES, ACCORDING TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE. HONG KONG--( BUSINESS WIRE )--METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT ONCE AGAIN HAS BEEN NAMED TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S 2023 LIST OF THE “WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES.” EIGHT LIFE INSURERS WERE INCLUDED IN THE ANNUAL REPORT CARD OF THE BEST-REGARDED COMPANIES.
