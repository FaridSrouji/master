HENRY SCHEIN MAKES INVESTMENT TO FORM STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH BIOTECH DENTAL GROUP. MELVILLE, N.Y.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--HENRY SCHEIN, INC. (NASDAQ: HSIC), THE WORLD'S LARGEST PROVIDER OF HEALTH CARE SOLUTIONS TO OFFICE-BASED DENTAL AND MEDICAL PRACTITIONERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT WITH THE SHAREHOLDERS OF BIOTECH DENTAL S.A.S., A RAPIDLY GROWING PROVIDER OF INNOVATIVE CLINICAL SOFTWARE AND ORAL SURGERY AND ORTHODONTIC PRODUCTS BASED IN SALON-DE-PROVENCE, FRANCE, TO ACQUIRE A MAJORITY OWNERSHIP STAKE IN THE COMPANY.
