HENRY SCHEIN ANNOUNCES DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT WITH CARRIZO DENTAL TO PROVIDE ORAL HEALTH SOLUTIONS FOR DENTAL PRACTITIONERS IN ARGENTINA. MELVILLE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HENRY SCHEIN, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT WITH CARRIZO DENTAL, A LEADING CÓRDOBA-BASED DISTRIBUTOR OF DENTAL SUPPLIES AND EQUIPMENT, TO DISTRIBUTE THE COMPANY'S HENRY SCHEIN-BRAND PRODUCT PORTFOLIO TO DENTAL PROFESSIONALS THROUGHOUT ARGENTINA. AS A RESULT, DENTAL PRACTITIONERS OPERATING IN ARGENTINA WILL HAVE A LOCAL SALES CHANNEL TO PURCHASE HENRY SCHEIN-BRAND PRODUCTS, HELPING TO ENSURE A CONVENIENT, FAST, AND SEAMLESS PROCESS FOR
