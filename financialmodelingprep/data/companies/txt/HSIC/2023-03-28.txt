HENRY SCHEIN ANNOUNCES PRESENCE AT THE 2023 ASSOCIATION OF DENTAL SUPPORT ORGANIZATIONS (ADSO) SUMMIT. MELVILLE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HENRY SCHEIN, INC. (NASDAQ: HSIC) ANNOUNCED ITS PRESENCE AT THE 2023 ADSO ANNUAL SUMMIT, INCLUDING THOUGHT-LEADERSHIP PRESENTATIONS ON THE FUTURE OF DENTISTRY AND INTRAPRENEURSHIP AS WELL AS ACTIVITIES TO SUPPORT COMMUNITIES IN NEED.
