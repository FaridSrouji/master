HENRY SCHEIN CELEBRATES 25 YEARS OF ITS ‘BACK TO SCHOOL' PROGRAM. MELVILLE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HENRY SCHEIN, INC. (NASDAQ: HSIC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF ITS “BACK TO SCHOOL” PROGRAM, WHICH PROVIDES BACKPACKS, SCHOOL SUPPLIES, AND MORE TO SUPPORT THE BACK-TO-SCHOOL NEEDS OF UNDERSERVED CHILDREN AND THEIR FAMILIES. SINCE THE PROGRAM'S INCEPTION IN 1998, THE COMPANY HAS HELPED MORE THAN 65,000 CHILDREN START A NEW SCHOOL YEAR WITH CONFIDENCE.
