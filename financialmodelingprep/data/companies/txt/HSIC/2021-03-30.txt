HENRY SCHEIN STRENGTHENS ITS COMMITMENT TO AMBULATORY SURGERY MARKET WITH A MAJORITY INVESTMENT IN STRADIS HEALTHCARE. MELVILLE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HENRY SCHEIN, INC. ANNOUNCED A MAJORITY INVESTMENT IN STRADIS MEDICAL, LLC TO ADVANCE ITS COMMITMENT TO THE GROWING AMBULATORY SURGERY MARKET.
