HENRY SCHEIN TO HOST PRE-CONFERENCE PROGRAM AT THE 2023 ADSO ANNUAL SUMMIT. MELVILLE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN COLLABORATION WITH THE ASSOCIATION OF DENTAL SUPPORT ORGANIZATIONS (ADSO), HENRY SCHEIN, INC. (NASDAQ: HSIC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL PRESENT A PRE-CONFERENCE PROGRAM ON TUESDAY, MARCH 28 FROM 2:30 – 5:30 PM AT THE 2023 ADSO ANNUAL SUMMIT.
