HENRY SCHEIN ANNOUNCES SUPPORT FOR MISSION OF MERCY EVENTS. MELVILLE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HENRY SCHEIN, INC. (NASDAQ: HSIC) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS SUPPORT FOR TWO MISSION OF MERCY EVENTS SCHEDULED THROUGH 2021, PART OF THE COMPANY'S THREE-YEAR COMMITMENT TO AMERICA'S DENTISTS CARE FOUNDATION (ADCF). HENRY SCHEIN'S SUPPORT INCLUDES DONATIONS UP TO $600,000 IN HEALTH CARE PRODUCTS TO ASSIST IN THE DELIVERY OF FREE ORAL CARE TO UNDERSERVED AND UNDERREPRESENTED COMMUNITIES.
