ZIMBIS ANNOUNCES NEW PARTNERSHIP AND LAUNCH OF ORDER INTEGRATION WITH SOUTHERN ANESTHESIA & SURGICAL, INC.. PHOENIX, NOV. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ZIMBIS, A DIVISION OF CUBEX LLC AND PROVIDER OF MEDICATION AND SUPPLY MANAGEMENT TECHNOLOGY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE LAUNCH OF A PARTNERSHIP WITH SOUTHERN ANESTHESIA & SURGICAL, INC. (SAS), A LEADING U.S. DISTRIBUTOR OF ANESTHESIA AND SURGICAL SUPPLIES TO ORAL SURGEONS, DENTAL ANESTHESIOLOGISTS, AND PERIODONTISTS. THE PARTNERSHIP WITH SAS SUPPORTS ZIMBIS' MISSION TO PROVIDE CLIENTS WITH SOLUTIONS AND WORKFLOWS THAT ADDRESS THEIR SPECIFIC MEDICATION AND SUPPLY REQUIREMENTS.
