HENRY SCHEIN PARTNERS WITH NGOS TO PROVIDE HEALTH CARE PRODUCTS TO AID HAITIAN EARTHQUAKE VICTIMS AND OPENS DISASTER RELIEF FUND. MELVILLE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HENRY SCHEIN IS PARTNERING WITH NGOS TO PROVIDE HEALTH CARE PRODUCTS TO AID HAITIAN EARTHQUAKE VICTIMS AND IS OPENING ITS DISASTER RELIEF FUND.
