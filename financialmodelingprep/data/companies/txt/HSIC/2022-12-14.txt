HENRY SCHEIN'S 24TH ANNUAL ‘HOLIDAY CHEER FOR CHILDREN' HELPS SPREAD JOY AROUND THE WORLD. MELVILLE, N.Y.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--HENRY SCHEIN, INC. (NASDAQ: HSIC) AND TEAM SCHEIN MEMBERS (TSMS) FROM AROUND THE WORLD ARE CELEBRATING THE COMPANY'S 24TH ANNUAL “HOLIDAY CHEER FOR CHILDREN” PROGRAM, A FLAGSHIP CORPORATE INITIATIVE THAT HELPS UNDERSERVED CHILDREN AND THEIR FAMILIES ENJOY A FUN AND FESTIVE HOLIDAY SEASON.
