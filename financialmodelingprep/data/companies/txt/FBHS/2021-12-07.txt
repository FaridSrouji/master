FORTUNE BRANDS GLOBAL PLUMBING GROUP ANNOUNCES NEW CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER. NORTH OLMSTED, OHIO, DEC. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FORTUNE BRANDS GLOBAL PLUMBING GROUP (GPG), A DIVISION OF FORTUNE BRANDS HOME & SECURITY, INC. (NYSE: FBHS), AN INDUSTRY-LEADING HOME AND SECURITY PRODUCTS COMPANY, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE TWO NEW ADDITIONS TO ITS LEADERSHIP TEAM. ASHLEY GEORGE, PREVIOUSLY VICE PRESIDENT, FINANCE, FOR GPG HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO) AND SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL FINANCE; AND CHRIS FALLON JOINS GPG AS ITS NEW CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER (CIO).
