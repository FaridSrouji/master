LEADING AUSTRALIAN WEALTH FIRM SELECTS BROADRIDGE TO GROW SECURITIES FINANCE BUSINESS. LONDON, SYDNEY AND NEW YORK, FEB. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO ASSIST IN OPTIMISING THEIR SECURITIES FINANCE OFFERING,  FINCLEAR , THE LEADING WEALTH MANAGEMENT INFRASTRUCTURE PROVIDER IN AUSTRALIA, HAS SELECTED BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER. BROADRIDGE'S SECURITIES FINANCE AND COLLATERAL MANAGEMENT (SFCM) FASTSTART SOLUTION PROVIDES FINCLEAR AND ITS CLIENTS WITH ENHANCED FUNCTIONALITIES IN THIS AREA OF THE BUSINESS.
