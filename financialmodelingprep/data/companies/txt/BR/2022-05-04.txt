BROADRIDGE PARTNERS WITH USROWING TO SUPPORT THE U.S. WOMEN'S NATIONAL ROWING TEAM. NEW YORK , MAY 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- USROWING IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS INC. (NYSE: BR) HAS SIGNED ON AS AN OFFICIAL USROWING PARTNER, SUPPORTING THE ORGANIZATION AND THE U.S. WOMEN'S NATIONAL TEAM. WORKING WITH USROWING, BROADRIDGE WILL LAUNCH AN ELITE ATHLETE MANAGEMENT PROGRAM, PROVIDING A POSITION FOR UP TO FIVE WOMEN U.S. ROWING NATIONAL TEAM HOPEFULS.
