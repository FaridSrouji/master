PASS-THROUGH VOTING ENABLING DEMOCRATIZATION OF INVESTING NEW BROADRIDGE REPORT SHOWS. GIVING INVESTORS A VOICE CAN BE A KEY DIFFERENTIATOR FOR ASSET MANAGEMENT FIRMS WORLDWIDE NEW YORK , MAY 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE RISE IN DEMAND FOR PASS-THROUGH VOTING FROM INVESTORS, COMBINED WITH SUPPORT FROM REGULATORS, IS FOSTERING THE DEMOCRATIZATION OF INVESTING IN A MAJOR WAY, ACCORDING TO A NEW REPORT BY GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR).  "PASS-THROUGH VOTING GIVES A VOICE TO INVESTMENT FUND HOLDERS," SAID DEMI DEREM, GENERAL MANAGER, INTERNATIONAL INVESTOR COMMUNICATION SOLUTIONS AT BROADRIDGE.
