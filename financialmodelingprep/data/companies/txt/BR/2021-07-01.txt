BROADRIDGE ACQUIRES INNOVATIVE COMPLIANCE AND SURVEILLANCE SOLUTION FOR BROKER-DEALERS. NEW YORK, JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS FURTHER ENHANCING ITS REGULATORY COMPLIANCE CAPABILITIES FOR BROKER-DEALERS WITH THE ACQUISITION OF THE CLOUD-BASED EXECUTION COMPLIANCE AND SURVEILLANCE SERVICE (ECS) ASSETS FROM JORDAN & JORDAN. THE SOLUTION PROVIDES A COMBINATION OF SURVEILLANCE AND REGULATORY REPORTING AS WELL AS COMPLIANCE CONSULTING CAPABILITIES FOR US REGULATIONS.
