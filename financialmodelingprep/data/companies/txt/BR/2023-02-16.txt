BROADRIDGE DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.725 PER SHARE. NEW YORK , FEB. 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --- BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.725 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON APRIL 5, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 15, 2023.
