BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 3, 2020. BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.54 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 3, 2020 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 13, 2019.
