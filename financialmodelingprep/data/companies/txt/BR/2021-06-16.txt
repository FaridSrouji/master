� BROADRIDGE FURTHER STRENGTHENS ITS CAPITAL MARKETS TEAM WITH THE ADDITION OF RAY TIERNEY. NEW YORK, JUNE 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONTINUING TO INVEST IN ITS CAPITAL MARKETS BUSINESS, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS FURTHER STRENGTHENED ITS LEADERSHIP TEAM WITH THE APPOINTMENT OF FINTECH AND FINANCIAL SERVICES VETERAN RAY TIERNEY AS PRESIDENT OF ITIVITI. WITH EXTENSIVE EXPERIENCE ON BOTH THE SELL-SIDE AND BUY-SIDE, TIERNEY WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE MANAGEMENT AND GROWTH OF BROADRIDGE'S FRONT-OFFICE OEMS AND CONNECTIVITY SOLUTIONS WITH ITS RECENTLY COMPLETED ACQUISITION OF ITIVITI.
