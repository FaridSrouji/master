THIS NEW SERVICE DELIVERS A TURNKEY MIDDLE AND BACK-OFFICE OPERATIONS SOLUTION IN TRADE FINANCE COMBINING THE MOST ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY OF LIQUIDX AND THE WORLD CLASS OUTSOURCING CAPABILITIES OF BROADRIDGE.
