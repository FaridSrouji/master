DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION BEING PUSHED AT FASTER RATE BY FINANCIAL SERVICES FIRMS, NEW BROADRIDGE SURVEY FINDS. NEW YORK, JAN. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FINANCIAL SERVICES FIRMS WHO INVESTED IN NEXT-GEN TECHNOLOGIES AND CONTINUED THEIR DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION PUSH IN 2021 ARE ALREADY EXPERIENCING THE POSITIVE EFFECTS OF THESE INVESTMENTS, ACCORDING TO A NEW GLOBAL SURVEY OF 750 FINANCIAL SERVICE FIRMS, RELEASED TODAY BY GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS (NYSE: BR). BROADRIDGE'S NEXT-GEN TECHNOLOGY SURVEY SCORED FIRMS ON PROGRESS IN THREE KEY AREAS OF DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION: EXPERIENCES, WORKFLOWS AND ANALYTICS.
