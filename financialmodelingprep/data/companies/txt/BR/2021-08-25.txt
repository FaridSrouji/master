BROADRIDGE STUDY: 2 IN 3 MILLENNIALS LIKELY TO BEGIN WORKING WITH FAS. NEW YORK, AUG. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AN OVERWHELMING MAJORITY (96%) OF INVESTORS WHO WORK WITH A FINANCIAL ADVISOR ARE SATISFIED (VERY OR SOMEWHAT) WITH THEIR ADVISOR, ACCORDING TO A NEW SURVEY OF 1,000 U.S. INVESTORS RELEASED TODAY FROM BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER. OF THOSE WHO DO NOT WORK WITH A FINANCIAL ADVISOR, 44% OF ALL INVESTORS AND 65% OF THE MILLENNIAL INVESTORS SAY THAT THEY ARE LIKELY (VERY OR SOMEWHAT) TO BEGIN WORKING WITH AN ADVISOR OVER THE NEXT TWO YEARS.
