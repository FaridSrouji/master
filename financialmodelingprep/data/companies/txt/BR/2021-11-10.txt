NYFIX ORDER ROUTING NETWORK WINS "BEST LOW LATENCY" PRIZE FROM WATERSTECHNOLOGY'S BUY-SIDE TECHNOLOGY AWARDS 2021. NEW YORK, NOV. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC.  (NYSE: BR), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS NYFIX ORDER ROUTING NETWORK FROM ITIVITI WAS NAMED "BEST LOW LATENCY TRADING NETWORK" FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR AT THE WATERS TECHNOLOGY'S BUY-SIDE TECHNOLOGY (BST) AWARDS 2021. THIS WIN MARKS THE FIFTH TIME IN THE PAST SIX YEARS NYFIX FROM ITIVITI HAS TAKEN HOME A PRESTIGIOUS BST AWARD.
