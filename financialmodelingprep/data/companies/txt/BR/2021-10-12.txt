BROADRIDGE EXTENDS INTELLIGENT AUTOMATION SUITE WITH NEW AI-POWERED ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING SOLUTION. NEW YORK, OCT. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO BETTER SERVE CLIENTS AND THE FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE BROADRIDGE ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING SOLUTION (AMLS), BRINGING NEW, LEADING EDGE CAPABILITIES TO BROADRIDGE'S EXISTING INTELLIGENT AUTOMATION SUITE. THE NEW SOLUTION DELIVERS AN END-TO-END MACHINE LEARNING (ML) POWERED ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING PLATFORM COVERING TRANSACTION MONITORING, NAME SCREENING, ALERT PRIORITIZATION, AND CUSTOMER RISK SCORING.
