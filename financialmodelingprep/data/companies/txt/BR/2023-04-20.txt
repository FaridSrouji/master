CAT FINANCIAL PRODUCTS SELECTS BROADRIDGE FOR INTERNATIONAL POST-TRADE PROCESSING OF STRUCTURED PRODUCTS. NEW YORK AND LONDON AND ZURICH , APRIL 20, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAT FINANCIAL PRODUCTS LTD. (CATFP), ONE OF THE LEADING INDEPENDENT SWISS PROVIDERS OF SECURITIES AND INVESTMENT SERVICES FOR STRUCTURED PRODUCTS, HAS TRANSFORMED ITS FRONT TO BACK OFFICE PROCESSING EFFICIENCY BY IMPLEMENTING THE INTERNATIONAL MULTI-ASSET POST-TRADE PROCESSING SOLUTION FROM GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR).
