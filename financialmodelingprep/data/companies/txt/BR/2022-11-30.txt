BROADRIDGE'S LTX® ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP TRANSITIONS, JIM KWIATKOWSKI NAMED CEO OF LTX. NEW YORK , NOV. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LTX, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC.'S [BR: NYSE] ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI)-DRIVEN DIGITAL TRADING BUSINESS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JIM KWIATKOWSKI HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO THE ROLE OF CEO OF LTX. JIM TOFFEY, CO-FOUNDER OF LTX, WILL SUCCEED ART CERTOSIMO AS CHAIRMAN OF THE LTX BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
