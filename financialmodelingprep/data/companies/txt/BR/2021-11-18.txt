BROADRIDGE DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.64 PER SHARE. NEW YORK, NOV. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.64 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 5, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 15, 2021.
