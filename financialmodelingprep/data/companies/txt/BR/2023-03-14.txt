CQG ANNOUNCES STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH BROADRIDGE TO INTEGRATE OMS AND EMS TECHNOLOGIES. CHICAGO AND NEW YORK AND BOCA RATON, FLA. , MARCH 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CQG, A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HIGH-PERFORMANCE TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS FOR MARKET MAKERS, TRADERS, BROKERS, COMMERCIAL HEDGERS AND EXCHANGES, TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS INC. (NYSE: BR) THAT WILL INTEGRATE THE TWO FIRMS' EXECUTION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (EMS) AND ORDER MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (OMS) RESPECTIVELY.
