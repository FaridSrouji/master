NEW ENHANCEMENTS TO VIRTUAL SHAREHOLDER MEETING PLATFORM BY BROADRIDGE DESIGNED TO ENABLE MORE TRANSPARENCY, ENGAGEMENT AND VOTING. NEW YORK, MARCH 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO ENABLE MORE INVESTORS TO PARTICIPATE IN THE CORPORATE GOVERNANCE PROCESS AND HELP CORPORATE ISSUERS ENGAGE WITH THEIR SHAREHOLDERS WITH GREATER EFFICIENCY, GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS INC. (NYSE: BR) HAS INTRODUCED NEW ENHANCEMENTS TO ITS VIRTUAL SHAREHOLDER MEETING PLATFORM FOR 2022. BUILDING ON LAST YEARS' EXPERIENCE OF HOSTING CLOSE TO 2,400 VIRTUAL SHAREHOLDER MEETINGS (VSMS), BROADRIDGE HAS SIMPLIFIED AND REFRESHED THE PLATFORM'S USER INTERFACE AND ENHANCED THE Q&A FUNCTIONALITY.
