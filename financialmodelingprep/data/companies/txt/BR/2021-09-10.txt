WELCOME TO THE TRI "PARTY": BROADRIDGE LAUNCHES SPONSORED GENERAL COLLATERAL SERVICE. NEW YORK, SEPT. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE FIRST FIXED INCOME TRADES FOR SPONSORED GENERAL COLLATERAL (GC) SERVICE WERE COMPLETED VIA BROADRIDGE'S POST-TRADE PROCESSING SOLUTION, IN CONJUNCTION WITH DTCC AND BNY MELLON.
