BROADRIDGE DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.725 PER SHARE. NEW YORK , MAY 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.725 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 6, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 15, 2023.
