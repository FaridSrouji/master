SBI SECURITIES CENTRALISES SETTLEMENT OPERATIONS WITH BROADRIDGE. TOKYO, OCT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO ENHANCE SERVICES TO ITS CLIENTS AND FURTHER AUTOMATE OPERATIONS ACROSS BUSINESS LINES, SBI SECURITIES CO., LTD., ONE OF JAPAN'S LARGEST ONLINE SECURITIES COMPANIES, HAS EXPANDED ITS BUSINESS RELATIONSHIP WITH BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER.
