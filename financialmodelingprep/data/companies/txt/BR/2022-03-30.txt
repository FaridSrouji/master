SHAREHOLDER VOTING MADE EVEN EASIER WITH THE UPDATED ENHANCED PROXYVOTE APP. NEW YORK , MARCH 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN ITS CONTINUED EFFORTS TO ENGAGE ALL SHAREHOLDERS IN THE CORPORATE GOVERNANCE PROCESS, GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: BR), ANNOUNCED NEW ENHANCEMENTS TO ITS PROXYVOTE APP. THE ENHANCEMENTS MAKE IT EASIER THAN EVER FOR INVESTORS TO ACCESS AND REVIEW PROXY INFORMATION IN UPCOMING ANNUAL MEETINGS OF THE COMPANIES AND MUTUAL FUNDS THEY OWN, AND TO CAST THEIR VOTES THIS PROXY SEASON.
