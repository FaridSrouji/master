DATA MANAGEMENT AND AI AUTOMATION TOOLS ARE THE TOP INVESTMENT FOR FINANCIAL FIRMS, BROADRIDGE SURVEY FINDS. 57% OF RESPONDENTS CONFIRM FINANCIAL FIRMS STILL HAVE PROGRESS TO MAKE, BEFORE THEY REACH THE ADVANCED STAGES OF INNOVATION AND TECHNOLOGY EFFORTS NEW YORK , JULY 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESTING IN TECHNOLOGY TO BETTER OPTIMIZE DATA AND MODERNIZE DATA MANAGEMENT ARE THE TOP PRIORITIES FOR FINANCIAL SERVICES FIRMS ACCORDING TO NEW RESEARCH BY GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: BR). THE RESEARCH WAS COMPILED FROM A SURVEY OF 200 FINANCIAL SERVICES PROFESSIONALS, CONDUCTED AT THE RECENT SIFMA OPERATIONS CONFERENCE IN MAY 2022.
