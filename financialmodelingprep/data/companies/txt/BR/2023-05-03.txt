BROADRIDGE DATA AND ANALYTICS AND AIVIQ ENHANCE DATA INTEGRATION AND TRANSPARENCY FOR ASSET MANAGERS. LONDON AND NEW YORK , MAY 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIVIQ, THE LEADING PROVIDER OF CLIENT-LEVEL AUM AND ASSET FLOW REPORTING FOR GLOBAL ASSET MANAGERS, HAS COLLABORATED WITH GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: BR) TO IMPROVE THE QUALITY AND TRANSPARENCY OF BOTH INTERNAL SALES REPORTING DATA AND EXTERNAL MARKET INTELLIGENCE. "THE STRATEGIC RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN AIVIQ AND BROADRIDGE WILL PROVIDE ASSET MANAGEMENT FIRMS WITH A BETTER VIEW OF CLIENT AUM AND ASSET FLOW DATA FOR THEIR GLOBAL MARKET INTELLIGENCE," SAID GREG GLASS, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF AIVIQ.
