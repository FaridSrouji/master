BROADRIDGE PARTNERS WITH ACCESSFINTECH TO TRANSFORM RESOLUTION OF SETTLEMENT FAILS. NEW YORK, MARCH 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, HAS PARTNERED WITH ACCESSFINTECH TO DELIVER A NEW STRATEGIC GATEWAY FOR SETTLEMENT WORKFLOW THAT OFFERS EFFICIENCIES IN RESOLVING MULTI-PARTY SETTLEMENT FAILS. THE SOLUTION IS POWERED BY BROADRIDGE'S INDUSTRY-LEADING POST-TRADE PLATFORMS AND DATA, AND INCORPORATES ACCESSFINTECH'S CLOUD-BASED OPERATIONS WORKFLOW MODEL.
