NEW BROADRIDGE ESG DASHBOARD ENABLES COMPANIES TO MAKE BETTER SENSE OF ESG RATINGS. NEW YORK, NOV. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A COMPANY'S ESG RATINGS HAS BECOME INCREASINGLY IMPORTANT TO BOTH INVESTORS AND SHAREHOLDERS ALIKE AND ACHIEVING AN ACCURATE COMPREHENSIVE UNDERSTANDING OF THOSE RATINGS HAS GROWN MORE COMPLEX AND TIME CONSUMING. TO HELP COMPANIES BETTER UNDERSTAND AND MONITOR THEIR ESG RATINGS, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS (NYSE: BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, HAS LAUNCHED THE BROADRIDGE ESG PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD.
