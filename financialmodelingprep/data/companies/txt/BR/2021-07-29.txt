RBC WEALTH MANAGEMENT-U.S. ACCELERATES DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION WITH SELECTION OF NEW BROADRIDGE PLATFORM. MINNEAPOLIS AND NEW YORK, JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RBC WEALTH MANAGEMENT-U.S. (RBCWM) AND BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT RBCWM WILL UTILIZE THE BROADRIDGE WEALTH MANAGEMENT PLATFORM TO POWER ITS GROWING U.S. BUSINESS. BROADRIDGE'S NEXT-GENERATION WEALTH MANAGEMENT PLATFORM IS AN OPEN, INDUSTRY-LEVEL PLATFORM OFFERING AN INTEGRATED AND MODERN FRONT-TO-BACK-OFFICE SOLUTION, LEVERAGING BEST-IN-CLASS TECHNOLOGY.
