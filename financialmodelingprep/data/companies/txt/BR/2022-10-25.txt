BROADRIDGE FULLY AUTOMATES CORPORATE ACTION AND DIVIDEND EVENTS, MANAGED THROUGH EUROCLEAR CREST. NEW YORK , OCT. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL ENABLE CLIENTS TO BETTER AUTOMATE THE PROCESSING OF CORPORATE ACTION AND DIVIDENDS EVENTS MANAGED THROUGH THE EUROCLEAR CREST PLATFORM. THE NEW SOLUTION CAPABILITIES FURTHER ENABLE FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS TO SIMPLIFY AND AUTOMATE THEIR CORPORATE ACTIONS PROCESSING WHILE REDUCING THE RISK AND COMPLEXITY ACROSS THEIR BUSINESS.
