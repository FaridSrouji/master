BROADRIDGE COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF ITIVITI, EXTENDING CAPITAL MARKETS FRANCHISE. NEW YORK, MAY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, HAS COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF ITIVITI HOLDING AB ("ITIVITI"), A LEADING PROVIDER OF TRADING AND CONNECTIVITY TECHNOLOGY TO THE CAPITAL MARKETS INDUSTRY. THE ACQUISITION ENHANCES BROADRIDGE'S POSITION AS A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER AND SIGNIFICANTLY STRENGTHENS BROADRIDGE'S CAPITAL MARKETS FRANCHISE.
