BROADRIDGE TEAMS WITH OPENFIN TO OFFER ENHANCED DIGITAL WORKSPACE FOR PORTFOLIO MANAGERS AND TRADERS. NEW YORK AND LONDON, JULY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENHANCING THE PRODUCTIVITY AND EFFICIENCY OF ITS TRADING AND PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT SOLUTION FOR ITS ASSET MANAGEMENT CLIENTS GLOBALLY, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL UTILIZE OPENFIN'S OPERATING SYSTEM FOR FINANCIAL DESKTOPS. WITH THIS NEW DIGITAL WORKSPACE SOLUTION, ASSET MANAGERS USING BROADRIDGE'S NEXT GENERATION PORTFOLIO AND ORDER MANAGEMENT SYSTEM WILL BENEFIT FROM COMPONENTIZED APPS WITH HIGHLY CONFIGURABLE LAYOUTS AND INTUITIVE WORKFLOWS.
