BROADRIDGE AMONG FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S 2022 'WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES'. NEW YORK, FEB. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FORTUNE® MAGAZINE HAS AGAIN NAMED BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: BR) TO ITS LIST OF THE WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES IN THE FINANCIAL DATA SERVICES INDUSTRY. THE FORTUNE LIST, CONSIDERED ONE OF THE LEADING MEASURES OF CORPORATE REPUTATION AMONG THE WORLD'S LARGEST COMPANIES, RATES FIRMS ON A RANGE OF CRITERIA FROM INVESTMENT VALUE TO GLOBAL COMPETITIVENESS AND INNOVATION.
