SYNDIO PARTNERS WITH BROADRIDGE FOR PAY EQUITY AS FINANCIAL SERVICES SECTOR LOOKS TO DELIVER ON THE "S" IN ESG. GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER WILL EMPLOY SYNDIO'S ANALYTICS PLATFORM TO BETTER MEASURE, ACHIEVE AND PROVE MEANINGFUL AND LASTING EQUITY IN THE WORKPLACE SEATTLE , JAN. 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNDIO, THE WORLD'S LEADING WORKPLACE EQUITY ANALYTICS PLATFORM, ANNOUNCED A NEW PARTNERSHIP TODAY WITH GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS (NYSE: BR). THE COMPANY IS EMPLOYING SYNDIO'S TECHNOLOGY TO BETTER ENABLE THE CRITICAL STEPS OF MEASURING AND DEEPENING THEIR COMMITMENT TO PAY EQUITY.
