UBS EXECUTES FIRST CROSS-BORDER INTRADAY REPO TRADE ON BROADRIDGE DISTRIBUTED LEDGER REPO PLATFORM. NEW YORK , APRIL 3, 2023  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT UBS (NYSE:UBS) AND A GLOBAL ASIAN BANK HAVE SUCCESSFULLY EXECUTED THE FIRST CROSS-BORDER INTRADAY REPO TRANSACTION ON BROADRIDGE'S BLOCKCHAIN ENABLED DISTRIBUTED LEDGER REPO (DLR) PLATFORM. THIS GROUNDBREAKING INTRADAY TRADE MARKS THE LAUNCH OF THE NEXT PHASE IN THE ROLLOUT OF BROADRIDGE'S DLR PLATFORM.
