BROADRIDGE RECOGNIZED FOR LEADERSHIP IN FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY AT SIFMA'S 50TH ANNUAL OPERATIONS CONFERENCE. NEW YORK , MAY 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SECURITIES INDUSTRY AND FINANCIAL MARKETS ASSOCIATION (SIFMA) TODAY RECOGNIZED GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC.  (NYSE:BR), WITH ITS EXCELLENCE IN OPERATIONS AWARD . THIS AWARD HIGHLIGHTS BROADRIDGE'S EXEMPLARY LEADERSHIP AND CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY.
