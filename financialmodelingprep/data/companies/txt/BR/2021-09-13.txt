BROADRIDGE NAMED TO IDC FINTECH RANKINGS FOR SEVENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. NEW YORK, SEPT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE:BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS PLACED 14 OUT OF 100 ON THE 2021 IDC FINTECH RANKINGS, RISING THREE PLACES FROM ITS 2020 RANKING.
