HCA HEALTHCARE TO SELL NORTH GEORGIA HOSPITAL TO ADVENTHEALTH. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HCA HEALTHCARE, INC. (NYSE:HCA), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL REDMOND REGIONAL MEDICAL CENTER, A 230 BED HOSPITAL IN ROME, GEORGIA, TO ADVENTHEALTH FOR APPROXIMATELY $635 MILLION. THE SALE COMPLETES THE COMPANY'S DIVESTITURE OF HOSPITALS IN THE SURROUNDING AREAS OF ATLANTA.
