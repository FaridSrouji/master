BRENTWOOD ASSOCIATES ANNOUNCES SALE OF MD NOW URGENT CARE. LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BRENTWOOD ASSOCIATES ("BRENTWOOD"), A LOS ANGELES-BASED PRIVATE EQUITY INVESTMENT FIRM, ANNOUNCED IT HAS SOLD MD NOW URGENT CARE (“MD NOW”) TO HCA HEALTHCARE (NYSE: HCA). THE TRANSACTION WAS COMPLETED AT THE END OF 2021. THE TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION WERE NOT DISCLOSED.
