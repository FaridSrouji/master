HCA HEALTHCARE TO GIVE $10 MILLION TO HBCUS AND HSIS. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HCA HEALTHCARE, INC. (NYSE:HCA), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL GIVE $10 MILLION OVER THE NEXT THREE YEARS TO HISTORICALLY BLACK COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES (HBCUS) AND HISPANIC-SERVING INSTITUTIONS (HSIS) IN COMMUNITIES NEAR ITS HOSPITALS IN ORDER TO ADVANCE DIVERSITY IN HEALTHCARE. “HCA HEALTHCARE SERVES DIVERSE COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE COUNTRY, AND WE KNOW OUR ABILITY TO CONTINUE TO DO THAT MOST EFFECTIVELY DEPEND
