KEITH ZIMMERMAN APPOINTED PRESIDENT OF HCA HEALTHCARE'S MIDAMERICA DIVISION. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HCA HEALTHCARE, INC. (NYSE: HCA), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS APPOINTED KEITH ZIMMERMAN AS PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY'S KANSAS CITY-BASED MIDAMERICA DIVISION, EFFECTIVE JUNE 13. AS MIDAMERICA DIVISION PRESIDENT, ZIMMERMAN WILL HAVE RESPONSIBILITY FOR SEVEN HOSPITALS AND NUMEROUS OTHER SITES OF CARE SERVING THE GREATER KANSAS CITY AREA.
