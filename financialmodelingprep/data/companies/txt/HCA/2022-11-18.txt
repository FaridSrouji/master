KAIN CAPITAL ANNOUNCES FORMATION OF NEW HOSPICE HOLDING COMPANY AND ITS FIRST ACQUISITION. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--KAIN CAPITAL LLC, A LEADING PRIVATE EQUITY FIRM FOCUSED ON THE TECHNOLOGY ENABLEMENT OF HEALTHCARE SERVICES COMPANIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED BOTH THE FORMATION OF A NEW HOLDING COMPANY FOR HOSPICE INVESTMENTS, PERA HOLDINGS, AND THE PLATFORM'S FIRST ACQUISITION.
