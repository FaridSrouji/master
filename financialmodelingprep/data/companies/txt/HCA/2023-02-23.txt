HCA HEALTHCARE AND THE HCA HEALTHCARE FOUNDATION ANNOUNCE $44 MILLION IN CONTRIBUTIONS TO COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS IN 2022. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HCA HEALTHCARE, INC. (NYSE:HCA), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY AND THE HCA HEALTHCARE FOUNDATION GAVE A TOTAL OF MORE THAN $44 MILLION TO COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS IN 2022, EXCEEDING THE $41 MILLION GIVEN IN 2021.
