EPAM STRENGTHENS POSITION AS A CONSULTANCY LEADER IN THE DACH REGION WITH ACQUISITION OF CORE SE. BERLIN, JULY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EPAM SYSTEMS, INC.  (NYSE: EPAM), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DIGITAL PLATFORM ENGINEERING AND DEVELOPMENT SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS ACQUISITION OF CORE SE, A PROFESSIONAL SERVICE PROVIDER SPECIALIZING IN IT STRATEGY AND TECHNOLOGY-DRIVEN TRANSFORMATIONS. FOLLOWING THE RECENT ANNOUNCEMENT OF A NEW BERLIN OFFICE, EPAM'S ACQUISITION OF CORE SE WILL FURTHER EXPAND ITS WESTERN EUROPEAN FOOTPRINT WITHIN THE DACH REGION.
