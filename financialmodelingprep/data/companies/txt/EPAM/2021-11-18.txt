EPAM WINS LINKEDIN'S BEST CULTURE OF LEARNING TALENT AWARD. NEWTOWN, PA., NOV. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EPAM SYSTEMS, INC. (NYSE: EPAM), A LEADING DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION SERVICES AND PRODUCT ENGINEERING COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WON THE 'BEST CULTURE OF LEARNING' AWARD AT THE 2021 LINKEDIN TALENT AWARDS.
