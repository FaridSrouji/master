(NYSE: EPAM), HAS ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC DIGITAL PARTNERSHIP WITH INEOS AUTOMOTIVE, A SUBSIDIARY OF INEOS GROUP, FORMED TO DEVELOP BEST-IN-CLASS OFF-ROAD VEHICLES.
