EPAM LAUNCHES UKRAINE ASSISTANCE FUND. ADVANCING EPAM'S ONGOING HUMANITARIAN EFFORTS IN UKRAINE NEWTOWN, PA. , APRIL 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EPAM SYSTEMS, INC. (NYSE: EPAM), A LEADING DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION SERVICES AND PRODUCT ENGINEERING COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ESTABLISHED THE EPAM UKRAINE ASSISTANCE FUND (THE 'ASSISTANCE FUND') TO SUPPORT CHARITABLE AID ORGANIZATIONS THAT PROVIDE DIRECT RELIEF TO THOSE IN VULNERABLE SITUATIONS ACROSS UKRAINE.
