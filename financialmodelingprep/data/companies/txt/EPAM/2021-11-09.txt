EPAM ACQUIRES EMAKINA GROUP, BRINGING NEW LINES OF HIGH-PERFORMANCE MARKETING & CREATIVE SERVICES TO EMEA MARKETS. NEWTOWN, PA., NOV. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EPAM SYSTEMS, INC. (NYSE: EPAM), A LEADING DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION SERVICES AND PRODUCT ENGINEERING COMPANY, ANNOUNCES THE ACQUISITION OF EMAKINA GROUP (EURONEXT: ALEMK), A MULTI-AWARD-WINNING DIGITAL AGENCY.
