SHAREHOLDER ACTION ALERT: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN META PLATFORMS, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $1,000,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - APRIL 5, 2022) - THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST META PLATFORMS, INC. ("META" OR "THE COMPANY") (NASDAQ: FB) FOR VIOLATIONS OF §§10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION.
FB ALERT: THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF MAY 9, 2022 IN THE CLASS ACTION FILED ON BEHALF OF META PLATFORMS, INC. SHAREHOLDERS. NEW YORK , APRIL 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION COMPLAINT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF META PLATFORMS, INC. (NASDAQ: FB) ALLEGING THAT THE COMPANY VIOLATED FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS. CLASS PERIOD: MARCH 2, 2021 TO FEBRUARY 2, 2022 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE: MAY 9, 2022 NO OBLIGATION OR COST TO YOU.
