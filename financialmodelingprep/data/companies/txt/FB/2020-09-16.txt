FACEBOOK AND ESSILORLUXOTTICA ANNOUNCE COLLABORATION FOR SMART GLASSES.MULTIYEAR COLLABORATION TO DEVELOP NEXT GENERATION OF SMART GLASSES.FIRST PRODUCT WILL BE BRANDED RAY-BAN, WORLD'S MOST POPULAR EYEWEAR BRAND, AND IS SCHEDULED TO LAUNCH IN 2021.PRODUCT NAME, SPECS, SOFTWARE CAPABILITIES, PRICING, AND OTHER DETAILS WILL BE SHARED CLOSER TO LAUNCH IN 2021.
