CHARLES SCHWAB SET TO JOIN S&P 100; BELLRING BRANDS TO JOIN S&P MIDCAP 400; CERENCE TO JOIN S&P SMALLCAP 600. NEW YORK, MARCH 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES WILL MAKE THE FOLLOWING CHANGES TO THE S&P 100, S&P MIDCAP 400 AND S&P SMALLCAP 600: S&P 500 CONSTITUENT THE CHARLES SCHWAB CORP. (NYSE: SCHW) WILL REPLACE BIOGEN INC. (NASD: BIIB) IN THE S&P 100 EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPENING OF TRADING ON MONDAY, MARCH 21 TO COINCIDE WITH THE QUARTERLY REBALANCE. BIOGEN WILL REMAIN IN THE S&P 500.
