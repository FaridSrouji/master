INVESTIGATION REMINDER: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN THE CHARLES SCHWAB CORPORATION WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - JULY 27, 2022) - THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, ANNOUNCES THAT IT IS INVESTIGATING CLAIMS ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS OF THE CHARLES SCHWAB CORPORATION (NYSE: SCHW) ("CHARLES SCHWAB" OR "THE COMPANY") FOR VIOLATIONS OF THE SECURITIES LAWS.THE INVESTIGATION FOCUSES ON WHETHER THE COMPANY ISSUED FALSE AND/OR MISLEADING STATEMENTS AND/OR FAILED TO DISCLOSE INFORMATION PERTINENT TO INVESTORS.
