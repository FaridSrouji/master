SCHWAB TO LAUNCH THE SCHWAB HIGH YIELD BOND ETF. WESTLAKE, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SCHWAB ASSET MANAGEMENT™, THE ASSET MANAGEMENT ARM OF THE CHARLES SCHWAB CORPORATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE SCHWAB HIGH YIELD BOND ETF (NYSE ARCA: SCYB). THE FIRST DAY OF TRADING IS EXPECTED TO BE ON OR ABOUT JULY 11. WITH AN EXPENSE RATIO OF 0.10%, THE SCHWAB HIGH YIELD BOND ETF IS PRICED IN LINE WITH THE LOWEST FEE ETFS IN THE MORNINGSTAR HIGH YIELD BOND CATEGORY. THE ETF WILL PROVIDE SIMPLE ACCESS TO THE U.S. DOLLAR DENOMINATED HIGH YIELD CORPORA.
