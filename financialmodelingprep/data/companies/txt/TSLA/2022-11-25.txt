GLOBAL NEV SALES REACHED 2.87 MILLION UNITS FOR 3Q22 AS BYD TRAILED CLOSELY BEHIND TESLA IN BEV SEGMENT, SAYS TRENDFORCE. TAIPEI, TAIWAN--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TRENDFORCE'S LATEST RESEARCH FINDS THAT GLOBAL SALES OF NEW ENERGY VEHICLES (NEVS), WHICH ENCOMPASS BATTERY-ELECTRIC VEHICLES (BEVS), PLUG-IN HYBRID ELECTRIC VEHICLES (PHEVS), AND FUEL-CELL VEHICLES (FCVS), ROSE BY 70% YOY TO 2.87 MILLION UNITS FOR 3Q22. OF THE QUARTERLY TOTAL, BEV SALES ACCOUNTED FOR 2.147 MILLION UNITS AND REGISTERED A YOY GROWTH OF 75%, WHEREAS PHEV SALES ACCOUNTED FOR 714,000 UNITS AND REGISTERED A YOY GROWTH OF 57%.
