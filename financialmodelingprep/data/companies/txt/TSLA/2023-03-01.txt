NEW YORK , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ATTORNEY ADVERTISING-- BRONSTEIN, GEWIRTZ & GROSSMAN, LLC NOTIFIES INVESTORS THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED AGAINST TESLA, INC. ("TESLA" OR "THE COMPANY") (NASDAQ: TSLA) AND CERTAIN OF ITS OFFICERS, ON BEHALF OF ALL PERSONS AND ENTITIES THAT PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED TESLA COMMON STOCK BETWEEN FEBRUARY 19, 2019 AND FEBRUARY 17, 2023, (THE "CLASS PERIOD"). SUCH INVESTORS ARE ENCOURAGED TO JOIN THIS CASE BY VISITING THE FIRM'S SITE: WWW.BGANDG.COM/TSLA.
