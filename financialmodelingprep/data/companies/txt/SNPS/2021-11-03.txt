TSMC RECOGNIZES LONG-STANDING COLLABORATION WITH SYNOPSYS ON SEMICONDUCTOR INNOVATION WITH MULTIPLE OIP PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARDS. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF., NOV. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FOR THE ELEVENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, IT HAS BEEN SELECTED AS A TSMC OPEN INNOVATION PLATFORM® (OIP) PARTNER OF THE YEAR, SPOTLIGHTING A LONG-STANDING COLLABORATION TO ADVANCE NEXT-GENERATION SYSTEM-ON-CHIP (SOC) AND 3DIC DESIGN ENABLEMENT.
