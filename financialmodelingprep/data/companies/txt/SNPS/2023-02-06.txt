SYNOPSYS CHANNEL AND ALLIANCES LEADER TOM HERRMANN RECOGNIZED AS 2023 CRN CHANNEL CHIEF. SYNOPSYS EXECUTIVE EARNS CRN'S CHANNEL CHIEF HONOR FOR SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR, DEMONSTRATING EXPERTISE, INFLUENCE AND INNOVATION IN CHANNEL LEADERSHIP MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF. , FEB. 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CRN®, A BRAND OF THE CHANNEL COMPANY , HAS RECOGNIZED TOM HERRMANN, VICE PRESIDENT OF GLOBAL CHANNELS AND ALLIANCES FOR THE SYNOPSYS SOFTWARE INTEGRITY GROUP, ON ITS 2023 CHANNEL CHIEFS LIST.
