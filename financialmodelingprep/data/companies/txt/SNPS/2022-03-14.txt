SYNOPSYS STREAMLINES OPTICAL PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT WITH NEW INTEROPERABILITY BETWEEN CODE V AND LIGHTTOOLS. NEW CAPABILITIES LINK INDUSTRY-LEADING IMAGING AND ILLUMINATION DESIGN TOOLS FOR FASTER MULTI-DOMAIN OPTICAL SIMULATIONS  MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF. , MARCH 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE AVAILABILITY OF NEW AND IMPROVED INTEROPERABILITY FEATURES BETWEEN CODE V® AND LIGHTTOOLS®, WHICH ENABLE DESIGNERS TO EASILY SIMULATE OPTICAL SYSTEMS THAT CONTAIN IMAGING AND NON-IMAGING COMPONENTS, THEREBY REDUCING PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT TIME.
