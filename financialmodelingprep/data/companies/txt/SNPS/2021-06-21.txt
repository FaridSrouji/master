SYNOPSYS TO ACQUIRE SEMICONDUCTOR AND FLAT PANEL DISPLAY SOLUTIONS FROM BISTEL. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF., JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE THE SEMICONDUCTOR AND FLAT PANEL DISPLAY SOLUTIONS FROM BISTEL, A LEADER IN ENGINEERING EQUIPMENT SYSTEMS AND AI APPLICATIONS FOR SEMICONDUCTOR SMART MANUFACTURING, HEADQUARTERED IN SOUTH KOREA.
