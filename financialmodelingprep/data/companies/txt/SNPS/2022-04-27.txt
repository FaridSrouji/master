SYNOPSYS TO ACQUIRE WHITEHAT SECURITY FROM NTT. ACQUISITION EXPANDS APPLICATION SECURITY SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE CAPABILITIES MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF. , APRIL 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE WHITEHAT SECURITY, A LEADING PROVIDER OF APPLICATION SECURITY SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE (SAAS).
