SYNOPSYS INITIATES $175 MILLION ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF., AUG. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT (ASR) WITH HSBC BANK USA, NATIONAL ASSOCIATION TO REPURCHASE AN AGGREGATE OF $175 MILLION OF SYNOPSYS STOCK.
