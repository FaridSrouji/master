SYNOPSYS APPROVES STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM WITH AUTHORIZATION UP TO $1.5 BILLION. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF. , SEPT. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS REPLENISHED ITS STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM WITH AN INCREASED AUTHORIZATION TO PURCHASE UP TO $1.5 BILLION OF COMMON STOCK.
