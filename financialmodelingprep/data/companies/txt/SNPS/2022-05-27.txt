SYNOPSYS INITIATES $200 MILLION ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF. , MAY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT (ASR) WITH WELLS FARGO BANK, NA TO REPURCHASE AN AGGREGATE OF $200 MILLION OF SYNOPSYS STOCK.
