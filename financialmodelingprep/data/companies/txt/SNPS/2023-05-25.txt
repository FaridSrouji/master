SYNOPSYS INITIATES $300 MILLION ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF. , MAY 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT (ASR) WITH MIZUHO MARKETS AMERICAS LLC TO REPURCHASE AN AGGREGATE OF $300 MILLION OF SYNOPSYS STOCK.
