SYNOPSYS CHOSEN BY JUNIPER NETWORKS TO ACCELERATE DEVELOPMENT OF PHOTONIC ICS FOR NEXT-GEN DATA CENTERS. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF., DEC. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JUNIPER NETWORKS, A LEADER IN SECURE, AI-DRIVEN NETWORKS, HAS ADOPTED THE SYNOPSYS OPTOCOMPILER™ PLATFORM, INCLUDING THE OPTSIM™ AND PRIMESIM™ HSPICE® SIMULATION SOLUTIONS, TO ACCELERATE THE DEVELOPMENT OF PHOTONIC-ENABLED CHIPS FOR THE NEXT GENERATION OF OPTICAL COMMUNICATIONS.
