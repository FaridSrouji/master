SYNOPSYS TO EXPAND DESIGNWARE ETHERNET IP PORTFOLIO WITH ACQUISITION OF MORETHANIP. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF., APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE MORETHANIP, A PROVIDER OF ETHERNET DIGITAL CONTROLLER IP SUPPORTING DATA RATES FROM 10G TO 800G.
