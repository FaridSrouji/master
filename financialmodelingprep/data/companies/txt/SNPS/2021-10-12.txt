SYNOPSYS AND DASSAULT SYSTÈMES PARTNER TO ENABLE INDUSTRY'S FIRST HOLISTIC LIGHTING DESIGN PLATFORM. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF. AND VELIZY-VILLACOUBLAY, FRANCE, OCT. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) AND DASSAULT SYSTÈMES (EURONEXT PARIS: FR0014003TT8, DSY.PA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THEIR PARTNERSHIP TO INTEGRATE SYNOPSYS OPTICAL DESIGN SOLUTIONS INTO THE DASSAULT SYSTÈMES 3DEXPERIENCE PLATFORM TO FACILITATE THE DEVELOPMENT OF SAFER, SMARTER VEHICLES.
