SYNOPSYS APPROVES STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM WITH AUTHORIZATION UP TO $1 BILLION. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF., DEC. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A NEW STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM WITH AUTHORIZATION TO PURCHASE UP TO $1 BILLION OF COMMON STOCK.
