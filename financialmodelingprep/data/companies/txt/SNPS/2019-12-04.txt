SYNOPSYS COLLABORATES WITH GOOGLE CLOUD TO BROADLY SCALE CLOUD-BASED FUNCTIONAL VERIFICATION. SYNOPSYS, INC. ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION WITH GOOGLE CLOUD TO PROVIDE A FULL END-TO-END SOLUTION TO PERFORM FUNCTIONAL VERIFICATION WORKLOADS IN THE CLOUD. AN INCREASING NUMBER OF USERS ARE SEEKING SOLUTIONS THAT TAKE ADVANTAGE OF THE ELASTIC HARDWARE SCALABILITY OFFERED IN THE CLOUD TO ADDRESS PEAK/BURST SIMULATION CAPACITY REQUIREMENTS, ENABLING A HIGHER QUALITY VERIFICATION ON A PREDICTABLE SCHEDULE. THIS COLLABORATION PROVIDES A PROVEN AND SCALABLE SOLUTION TO ENABLE SYNOPSYS VCS® SIMULATION WORKLOADS ON GOOGLE CLOUD. MOVING VERIFICATION WORKLOADS TO THE CLOUD BRINGS A UNIQUE SET OF CHALLENGES, SUCH AS HARDWARE INSTANCE SELECTION, FILE SYSTEM CONFIGURATION, AND JOB ORCHESTRATION. USING PROVEN INFRASTRUCTURE TO ENSURE EFFICIENT UTILIZATION OF RESOURCES AND NATIVE GOOGLE CLOUD NETAPP INTEGRATION TO BRIDGE THE ON-PREMISES AND CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURES, TOGETHER WITH TIGHT INTEGRATION BETWEEN VCS AND GOOGLE CLOUD PREEMPTIBLE VM INSTANCES, ENABLES VCS USERS TO QUICKLY TAKE ADVANTAGE OF THE ELASTIC RESOURCES AVAILABLE IN THE CLOUD.
SYNOPSYS POSTS QUARTERLY GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $1.04. SYNOPSYS POSTS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL YEAR 2019.Q4 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $4.56.Q4 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.04.Q4 REVENUE $851.1 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $847.3 MILLION.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.13 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
