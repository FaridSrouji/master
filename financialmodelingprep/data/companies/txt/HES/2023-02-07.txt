HESS RECOGNIZED FOR SUSTAINABILITY PERFORMANCE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HESS CORPORATION (NYSE: HES) HAS ONCE AGAIN RECEIVED A AAA RATING IN THE LATEST MSCI ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) RATING ASSESSMENT. AAA, WHICH IS MSCI ESG'S HIGHEST RATING, DESIGNATES HESS AS A LEADER IN MANAGING INDUSTRY SPECIFIC ESG RISKS RELATIVE TO PEERS. HESS IS ONE OF ONLY TWO U.S. OIL AND GAS COMPANIES THAT CURRENTLY HOLD A AAA RATING FROM MSCI ESG.
