HESS CEO DISCUSSES CLIMATE AND ENERGY IN CERAWEEK CONVERSATIONS SERIES. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JOHN HESS, CEO OF HESS CORPORATION (NYSE: HES), SHARES HIS PERSPECTIVE ON A RANGE OF ENERGY ISSUES IN THE LATEST EDITION OF THE CERAWEEK CONVERSATIONS SERIES. IN A DISCUSSION WITH IHS MARKIT VICE CHAIRMAN DANIEL YERGIN, HESS DESCRIBES THE NEED FOR CLIMATE AND ENERGY LITERACY IN ORDER TO TACKLE THE CRITICAL CHALLENGE OF CLIMATE CHANGE, THE VITAL IMPORTANCE OF THE OIL AND GAS INDUSTRY TO THE U.S. ECONOMY, AND THE COMPANY'S STRATEGIC FOCUS.
