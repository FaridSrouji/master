HESS ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF HESS CORPORATION (NYSE: HES) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 37.5 CENTS PER SHARE PAYABLE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE CORPORATION ON DECEMBER 29, 2022 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 19, 2022.
