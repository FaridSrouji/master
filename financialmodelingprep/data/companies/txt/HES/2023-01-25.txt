HESS REPORTS ESTIMATED RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--   HESS CORPORATION (NYSE: HES) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $624 MILLION, OR $2.03 PER COMMON SHARE, IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022 COMPARED WITH NET INCOME OF $265 MILLION, OR $0.85 PER COMMON SHARE, IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021. ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS, THE CORPORATION HAD NET INCOME OF $548 MILLION OR $1.78 PER COMMON SHARE IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022. THE IMPROVEMENT IN ADJUSTED AFTER-TAX EARNINGS COMPARED WITH THE PRIOR-YEAR PERIOD WAS PRIMARILY DUE TO INCREASED SALES VOLUMES IN GUYANA IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022.
