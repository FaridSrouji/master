HESS REPORTS ESTIMATED RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HESS CORPORATION (NYSE: HES) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $417 MILLION, OR $1.34 PER SHARE, IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022, COMPARED WITH NET INCOME OF $252 MILLION, OR $0.82 PER SHARE, IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS, THE CORPORATION REPORTED NET INCOME OF $404 MILLION, OR $1.30 PER SHARE, IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022.
