TWITTER, INC. ANNOUNCES $2 BILLION ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENTS. SAN FRANCISCO, FEB. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TWITTER, INC. ("TWITTER" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: TWTR) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENTS (ASRS) WITH MORGAN STANLEY & CO. LLC AND WELLS FARGO BANK, NATIONAL ASSOCIATION (COLLECTIVELY, THE "DEALERS") TO REPURCHASE $2 BILLION OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK.  THE ASRS WERE ENTERED INTO PURSUANT TO TWITTER'S RECENTLY ANNOUNCED SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM, UNDER WHICH APPROXIMATELY $2 BILLION OF SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION WILL REMAIN AVAILABLE UPON COMPLETION OF THE ASRS.
