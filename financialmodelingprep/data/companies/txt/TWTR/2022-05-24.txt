ONGOING INVESTIGATION REMINDER: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN TWITTER, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - MAY 24, 2022) - THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, ANNOUNCES THAT IT IS INVESTIGATING CLAIMS ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS OF TWITTER, INC. ("TWITTER" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE: TWTR) FOR VIOLATIONS OF THE SECURITIES LAWS.THE INVESTIGATION FOCUSES ON WHETHER THE COMPANY ISSUED FALSE AND/OR MISLEADING STATEMENTS AND/OR FAILED TO DISCLOSE INFORMATION PERTINENT TO INVESTORS.
