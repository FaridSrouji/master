TWITTER SAYS ATTACK ON JULY 15 TARGETED SMALL NUMBER OF EMPLOYEES THROUGH PHONE SPEAR PHISHING ATTACK. ATTACK ON JULY 15, 2020, TARGETED A SMALL NUMBER OF EMPLOYEES THROUGH A PHONE SPEAR PHISHING ATTACK.SIGNIFICANTLY LIMITED ACCESS TO INTERNAL TOOLS AND SYSTEMS.ATTACK RELIED ON SIGNIFICANT & CONCERTED ATTEMPT TO MISLEAD CERTAIN EMPLOYEES,EXPLOIT HUMAN VULNERABILITIES TO GAIN ACCESS TO INTERNAL SYSTEMS."UNTIL WE CAN SAFELY RESUME NORMAL OPERATIONS, OUR RESPONSE TIMES TO SOME SUPPORT NEEDS AND REPORTS WILL BE SLOWER".ATTACK ON JULY 15 TARGETED 130 TWITTER ACCOUNTS - TWEETING FROM 45, ACCESSING THE DM INBOX OF 36, AND DOWNLOADING THE TWITTER DATA OF 7.BY OBTAINING EMPLOYEE CREDENTIALS, ATTACKERS WERE ABLE TO TARGET SPECIFIC EMPLOYEES WHO HAD ACCESS TO ACCOUNT SUPPORT TOOLS.CO IS ACCELERATING SEVERAL OF ITS PRE-EXISTING SECURITY WORKSTREAMS AND IMPROVEMENTS TO ITS TOOLS.IMPROVING METHODS FOR DETECTING AND PREVENTING INAPPROPRIATE ACCESS TO INTERNAL SYSTEMS AND PRIORITIZING SECURITY WORK ACROSS MANY TEAMS.
