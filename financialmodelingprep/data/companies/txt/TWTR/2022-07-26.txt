NEW TALKWALKER REPORT REVEALS YOUNG PEOPLE ON TWITTER ARE MOST CONCERNED BY THE RISING COST OF LIVING. TALKWALKER, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH TWITTER, ANALYZED OVER 16.2 MILLION COST-OF-LIVING CONVERSATIONS TO REVEAL THE IMPACT OF RAPIDLY RISING COSTS ON GLOBAL CONSUMERS PEOPLE ON TWITTER AGED 25-34, SAW THE SHARPEST INCREASE IN COST OF LIVING CONVERSATIONS, HIGHLIGHTING THEIR INCREASED CONCERNS ABOUT THE CRISIS. CONSUMERS ARE MOST ANXIOUS ABOUT THE RISING COST OF GAS AND HEATING.
