TWITTER SAYS INTRODUCING NEW LABELS, WARNINGS TO PROVIDE ADDITIONAL DATA ON TWEETS CONTAINING DISPUTED/ MISLEADING INFORMATION RELATED TO COVID-19. TWITTER - INTRODUCING NEW LABELS, WARNINGS TO PROVIDE ADDITIONAL DATA ON TWEETS CONTAINING DISPUTED/ MISLEADING INFORMATION RELATED TO COVID-19.TWITTER -  DEPENDING ON PROPENSITY FOR HARM AND TYPE OF MISLEADING INFORMATION, WARNINGS MAY ALSO BE APPLIED TO A COVID-19 RELATED TWEET.TWITTER - NEW LABELS WILL LINK TO TWITTER-CURATED PAGE OR EXTERNAL TRUSTED SOURCE CONTAINING ADDITIONAL COVID-RELATED INFORMATION ON THE CLAIMS MADE WITHIN TWEET.TWITTER - WARNINGS WILL INFORM PEOPLE THAT THE INFORMATION IN THE COVID-19 RELATED TWEET CONFLICTS WITH PUBLIC HEALTH EXPERTS’ GUIDANCE BEFORE THEY VIEW IT.TWITTER - WILL PRIORITIZE REVIEW & LABELING OF COVID RELATED CONTENT THAT COULD LEAD TO INCREASED EXPOSURE OR TRANSMISSION.
