SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ANNOUNCES BOARD OF DIRECTORS TRANSITIONS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., FEB. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP (SVB), FINANCIAL PARTNER TO THE INNOVATION ECONOMY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ROGER DUNBAR WILL RETIRE FROM HIS ROLE AS THE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD ON APRIL 21, 2022, AFTER A DECADE IN THE POSITION.
