SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $350 MILLION 4.345% AND $450 MILLION 4.570% SENIOR FIXED RATE/FLOATING RATE NOTES. SANTA CLARA, CALIF. , APRIL 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ("SVB") (NASDAQ: SIVB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF AN UNDERWRITTEN OFFERING OF $350 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 4.345 % SENIOR FIXED RATE/FLOATING RATE NOTES DUE 2028 AND $450 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 4.570% SENIOR FIXED RATE/FLOATING RATE NOTES DUE 2033.
