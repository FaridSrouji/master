DELIVERING COMPREHENSIVE FINANCIAL SERVICES TO THE INNOVATION ECONOMY, SVB FINANCIAL GROUP REBRANDS TO REFLECT GROWTH. "SVB" TO REPRESENT THE COMPANY'S FULL OFFERING: SILICON VALLEY BANK, SVB CAPITAL, SVB PRIVATE, AND SVB SECURITIES SANTA CLARA, CALIF. , APRIL 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP (SVB), THE FINANCIAL PARTNER OF THE INNOVATION ECONOMY, TODAY ANNOUNCED A REBRAND OF ITS FOUR PRIMARY BUSINESSES — SILICON VALLEY BANK, SVB CAPITAL, SVB PRIVATE AND SVB SECURITIES — UNDER THE BRAND NAME SVB.
