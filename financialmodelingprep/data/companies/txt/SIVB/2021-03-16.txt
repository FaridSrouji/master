SILICON VALLEY BANK AND BLACK TALENT PLATFORM VALENCE TEAM UP TO INCREASE BLACK REPRESENTATION IN THE INNOVATION ECONOMY. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SILICON VALLEY BANK  (SVB), THE BANK OF THE WORLD'S MOST INNOVATIVE COMPANIES AND THEIR INVESTORS, AND  VALENCE , THE LEADING COMMUNITY TO CONNECT AND EMPOWER BLACK PROFESSIONALS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATIVE EFFORT TO CONNECT INNOVATIVE COMPANIES WITH BLACK TALENT.
