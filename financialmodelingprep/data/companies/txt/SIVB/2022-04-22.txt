KAY MATTHEWS BECOMES CHAIR OF SVB BOARD OF DIRECTORS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF. , APRIL 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP (SVB), THE FINANCIAL PARTNER OF THE INNOVATION ECONOMY AND PARENT OF SILICON VALLEY BANK, TODAY ANNOUNCED KAY MATTHEWS BECAME CHAIR OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS FOLLOWING THE COMPANY'S ANNUAL STOCKHOLDER MEETING ON APRIL 21, 2022.
