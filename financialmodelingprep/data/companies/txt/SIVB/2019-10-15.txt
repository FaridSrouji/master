CIRCULAR BOARD, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED $6.271344 MILLION IN FUNDING FROM SVB FINANCIAL GROUP AND OTHER INVESTORS. ON OCTOBER 16, 2019 CIRCULAR BOARD, INC. CLOSED THE TRANSACTION.
