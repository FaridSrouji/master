SVB LEERINK LLC ANNOUNCES COMPANY NAME CHANGE TO SVB SECURITIES LLC. BOSTON, FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB LEERINK, A LEADING INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN HEALTHCARE AND TECHNOLOGY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY WILL BEGIN OPERATING UNDER A NEW NAME AS SVB SECURITIES LLC. THE NEW CORPORATE NAME ALIGNS WITH THE FIRM'S STRATEGY TO PROVIDE CLIENTS AND INVESTORS ACROSS THE INNOVATION ECONOMY WITH THE EXCEPTIONAL FINANCIAL ADVISORY SERVICES THEY NEED TO SUPPORT THEIR GROWTH AND INVESTMENT OBJECTIVES.
