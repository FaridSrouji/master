SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ANNOUNCES FULL-RIDE UNDERGRADUATE UNIVERSITY SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MAY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP (SVB), PARENT COMPANY OF SILICON VALLEY BANK, THE BANK OF THE WORLD'S MOST INNOVATIVE COMPANIES AND THEIR INVESTORS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW UNIVERSITY SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM, WHICH WILL FUND APPROXIMATELY 25 FULL-RIDE SCHOLARSHIPS.
