SILICON VALLEY BANK'S HEALTHCARE REPORT SHOWS RECORD VENTURE INVESTMENT IN 2021. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., JAN. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VENTURE INVESTMENT IN HEALTHCARE REACHED NEW HEIGHTS IN 2021, WITH MORE THAN $80 BILLION INVESTED IN BIOPHARMA, MEDICAL DEVICE, DIAGNOSTICS/TOOLS (DX/TOOLS) AND HEALTHTECH COMPANIES, ACCORDING TO SILICON VALLEY BANK'S ANNUAL HEALTHCARE INVESTMENTS AND EXITS REPORT.
