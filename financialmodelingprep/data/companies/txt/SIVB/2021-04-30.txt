SVB FINANCIAL GROUP REITERATES THAT IT WILL NOT INCREASE PURCHASE PRICE FOR BOSTON PRIVATE. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ("SVB") (NASDAQ: SIVB) REMAINS FULLY COMMITTED TO THE MERGER WITH BOSTON PRIVATE FINANCIAL HOLDINGS, INC. ("BOSTON PRIVATE") (NASDAQ: BPFH) AND LOOKS FORWARD TO COMPLETING THE TRANSACTION.
