SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ANNOUNCES PRICING OF OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ("SVB") (NASDAQ: SIVB), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF ITS UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 2,227,000 SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK AT A PRICE TO THE PUBLIC OF $564.00 PER SHARE FOR EXPECTED GROSS PROCEEDS OF APPROXIMATELY $1.25 BILLION BEFORE UNDERWRITING DISCOUNTS AND COMMISSIONS AND OFFERING EXPENSES.
SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ANNOUNCES PROPOSED OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ("SVB") (NASDAQ: SIVB), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT INTENDS TO OFFER 2,227,000 SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK IN AN UNDERWRITTEN REGISTERED PUBLIC OFFERING.
