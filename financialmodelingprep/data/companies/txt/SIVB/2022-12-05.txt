SVB APPOINTS NEW LEADERSHIP FOR SVB PRIVATE AND SILICON VALLEY BANK UK. SANTA CLARA, CALIF. , DEC. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP (SVB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ERIN PLATTS HAS BEEN APPOINTED PRESIDENT OF SVB PRIVATE AND DAVID SABOW HAS BEEN NAMED CEO OF SILICON VALLEY BANK UK AND HEAD OF EMEA, PENDING UK REGULATORY APPROVAL.
