NEW ENERGY EQUITY CLOSES $50 MILLION UNITRANCHE REVOLVER LED BY SVB. ANNAPOLIS, MD., SEPT. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEW ENERGY EQUITY, A DEVELOPER AND FINANCIER OF COMMUNITY AND COMMERCIAL SOLAR PROJECTS THROUGHOUT THE UNITED STATES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CLOSING OF A $50 MILLION FACILITY, WITH $41 MILLION LED BY SILICON VALLEY BANK AND JOINED BY SVB CAPITAL, TWO INNOVATIVE FINANCIAL PARTNERS IN THE RENEWABLE ENERGY AND TECHNOLOGY SECTOR.
