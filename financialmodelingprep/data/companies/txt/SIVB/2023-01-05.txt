SVB CONFIRMS DATE AND TIME OF QUARTERLY EARNINGS CALL. SANTA CLARA, CALIF. , JAN. 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB (NASDAQ: SIVB), THE FINANCIAL PARTNER FOR THE INNOVATION ECONOMY, WILL REPORT ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2022, ON THURSDAY, JANUARY 19, 2023, AFTER THE CLOSE OF THE STOCK MARKET.
