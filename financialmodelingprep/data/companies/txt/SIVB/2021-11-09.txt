SVB FINANCIAL GROUP APPOINTS ELIZABETH BURR TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., NOV. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP (SVB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ELIZABETH "BUSY" BURR, FORMER PRESIDENT AND CHIEF COMMERCIAL OFFICER OF CARROT, INC., WHO HAS ALSO LED INNOVATION EFFORTS AT CITIGROUP AND HUMANA, TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 8, 2021.
