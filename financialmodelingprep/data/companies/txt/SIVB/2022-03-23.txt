SVB SECURITIES STRENGTHENS TECH INVESTMENT BANKING TEAM WITH THE ADDITION OF GEORGI BALINOV. BOSTON, MARCH 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB SECURITIES, A LEADING INVESTMENT BANK SERVING THE INNOVATION ECONOMY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT GEORGI BALINOV HAS JOINED THE FIRM'S TECH INVESTMENT BANKING TEAM AS A SENIOR MANAGING DIRECTOR. HE WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR SVB SECURITIES' EFFORTS TO DELIVER ADVISORY AND CAPITAL SOLUTIONS ACROSS THE RAPIDLY GROWING FINTECH VERTICAL.
