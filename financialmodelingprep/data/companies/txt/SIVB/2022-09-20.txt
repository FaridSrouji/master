SVB TO CONDUCT EQUITY AUDIT. THIRD-PARTY WILL REVIEW SVB'S ACCESS TO INNOVATION PROGRAMS, WHICH FOCUS ON INCREASING OPPORTUNITIES AND FUNDING FOR UNDERREPRESENTED PEOPLE IN THE INNOVATION ECONOMY SANTA CLARA, CALIF. , SEPT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB, THE FINANCIAL PARTNER OF THE INNOVATION ECONOMY AND PARENT OF SILICON VALLEY BANK, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL AUDIT ITS ACCESS TO INNOVATION PLATFORM, A FAMILY OF PROGRAMS FIRST ANNOUNCED IN 2019 FOCUSED ON ADVANCING WOMEN AND BLACK AND LATINX INDIVIDUALS TO POSITIONS OF INFLUENCE IN THE INNOVATION ECONOMY.
