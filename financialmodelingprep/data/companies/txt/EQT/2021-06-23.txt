EQT STRENGTHENS COMMITMENT TO REDUCING METHANE EMISSIONS, JOINS OGMP 2.0 INITIATIVE. PITTSBURGH, JUNE 23, 2021  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQT CORPORATION (NYSE: EQT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENHANCED ITS COMMITMENT AND LEADERSHIP IN REDUCING METHANE EMISSIONS BY JOINING THE OIL & GAS METHANE PARTNERSHIP 2.0 INITIATIVE. "REDUCING METHANE EMISSIONS IS A FOUNDATIONAL COMPONENT OF OUR MISSION TO BE THE OPERATOR OF CHOICE FOR ALL OF OUR STAKEHOLDERS, A RESPONSIBILITY WE EMBRACE AS THE COUNTRY'S LARGEST PRODUCER OF NATURAL GAS," SAID TOBY Z.
