EQT CORPORATION ANNOUNCES IMPAIRMENT CHARGES FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019; PROVIDES SALES GUIDANCE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER, FULL YEAR OF 2019 AND FULL YEAR OF 2020; ANNOUNCES OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER AND NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. EQT CORPORATION ANNOUNCED IMPAIRMENT CHARGES FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE QUARTER, THE COMPANY REPORTED IMPAIRMENT OF INTANGIBLE ASSETS OF $15,411,000. THE COMPANY ALSO REPORTED IMPAIRMENT/LOSS ON SALE/EXCHANGE OF LONG-LIVED ASSETS OF $13,935,000 AGAINST $259,279,000 A YEAR AGO.FOR THE QUARTER, THE COMPANY REPORTED TOTAL PRODUCTION SALES VOLUMES OF 380,823 MMCFE COMPARED TO 374,237 MMCFE FOR THE SAME PERIOD A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, THE COMPANY REPORTED TOTAL PRODUCTION SALES VOLUMES OF 1,134,407 MMCFE COMPARED TO 1,093,782 MMCFE FOR THE SAME PERIOD A YEAR AGO.FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER, THE COMPANY EXPECTS TOTAL SALES VOLUME BETWEEN 355 BCFE – 375 BCFE.FOR THE FULL YEAR 2019, THE COMPANY EXPECTS TOTAL SALES VOLUME BETWEEN 1,490 BCFE – 1,510 BCFE.FOR THE FULL YEAR 2020, THE COMPANY EXPECTS TOTAL SALES VOLUME BETWEEN 1,450 BCFE – 1,500 BCFE, ROUGHLY FLAT TO 2019 EXPECTED SALES VOLUMES.
