EQT CORPORATION ANNOUNCES RESIGNATION OF DONALD M. JENKINS AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF COMMERCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 15, 2019. ON NOVEMBER 15, 2019, EQT CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT DONALD M. JENKINS, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF COMMERCIAL OFFICER OF THE COMPANY, NOTIFIED THE COMPANY OF HIS INTENT TO RESIGN FROM HIS POSITION WITH THE COMPANY, EFFECTIVE ON DECEMBER 15, 2019.
