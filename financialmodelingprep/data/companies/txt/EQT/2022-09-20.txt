EQT ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $1.0 BILLION OF NEW SENIOR NOTES. PITTSBURGH , SEPT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQT CORPORATION (NYSE: EQT) ("EQT") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS PRICED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING (THE "OFFERING") OF $1.0 BILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR NOTES CONSISTING OF $500 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.678% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025 AND $500 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.700% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (TOGETHER, THE "NOTES").
