EQT AND WHEELING PARK COMMISSION LAUNCH FORESTRY MANAGEMENT PROGRAM AT OGLEBAY. PITTSBURGH , APRIL 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQT CORPORATION (NYSE: EQT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY'S FIRST NATURE-BASED CARBON OFFSET INITIATIVE. EQT HAS PARTNERED WITH THE WHEELING PARK COMMISSION, TERALYTIC, A SOIL ANALYTICS COMPANY, AND CLIMATE SMART ENVIRONMENTAL CONSULTING, LLC, TO IMPLEMENT FOREST MANAGEMENT PROJECTS THAT WILL GENERATE CARBON OFFSETS.
