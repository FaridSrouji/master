EQT ANNOUNCES EARLY RESULTS AND UPSIZING OF ITS TENDER OFFER FOR 6.125% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025. PITTSBURGH , JUNE 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQT CORPORATION (NYSE: EQT) ("EQT") TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EARLY RESULTS AND UPSIZING OF ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED TENDER OFFER (THE "TENDER OFFER") TO PURCHASE FOR CASH UP TO AN AMENDED MAXIMUM TENDER AMOUNT (AS DEFINED BELOW) OF ITS OUTSTANDING 6.125% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025 (THE "NOTES"). EQT HAS AMENDED THE TENDER OFFER TO INCREASE THE AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF NOTES SUBJECT TO THE TENDER OFFER (THE "MAXIMUM TENDER AMOUNT") FROM $250.0 MILLION TO $300.0 MILLION.
