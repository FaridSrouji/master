EQT DONATES PRODUCTION EQUIPMENT TO UKRAINE. DONATION WILL HELP JSC UKRGASVYDOBUVANNYA RESTORE DAMAGED FACILITIES AND INCREASE PRODUCTION  PITTSBURGH , FEB. 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQT CORPORATION (NYSE: EQT), THE LARGEST PRODUCER OF NATURAL GAS IN THE UNITED STATES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY DONATED OIL AND GAS PRODUCTION EQUIPMENT TO JSC UKRGASVYDOBUVANNYA, UKRAINE'S LARGEST GAS PRODUCER. THE EQUIPMENT WILL BE USED TO RESTORE DAMAGED OIL AND GAS PRODUCTION FACILITIES AND REVIVE CRITICAL PRODUCTION IN WARTIME CONDITIONS.
