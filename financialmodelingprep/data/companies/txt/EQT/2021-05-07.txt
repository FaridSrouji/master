SHAREHOLDER ALERT: MONTEVERDE & ASSOCIATES PC ANNOUNCES AN INVESTIGATION OF EQT CORP. - EQT. NEW YORK, MAY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JUAN MONTEVERDE , FOUNDER AND MANAGING PARTNER AT MONTEVERDE & ASSOCIATES PC, A NATIONAL SECURITIES FIRM RATED TOP 50 IN THE 2018-2020 ISS SECURITIES CLASS ACTION SERVICES REPORT AND HEADQUARTERED AT THE EMPIRE STATE BUILDING IN NEW YORK CITY, IS INVESTIGATING EQT CORP. ("EQT" OR THE "COMPANY") (EQT) RELATING TO ITS PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF ALTA RESOURCES DEVELOPMENT, LLC. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT, EQT WILL ACQUIRE ALL OF THE MEMBERSHIP INTERESTS IN ALTA'S SUBSIDIARIES FOR APPROXIMATELY $2.925 BILLION, COMPRISED OF APPROXIMATELY $1.0 BILLION IN CASH AND 105 MILLION SHARES OF EQT COMMON STOCK ISSUED TO ALTA'S SHAREHOLDERS.
