CBRE ANNOUNCES INDUSTRY-LEADING SCIENCE-BASED GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSION TARGET. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CBRE GROUP, INC. (NYSE:CBRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE (SBTI) HAS APPROVED THE COMPANY'S SCIENCE-BASED TARGET TO SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCE GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS (GHG) FROM BOTH CBRE'S OWN OPERATIONS AND THE PROPERTIES IT MANAGES FOR INVESTORS AND OCCUPIERS.
