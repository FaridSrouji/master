MINT ECO CAR WASH ACQUIRES TENTH SITE, BECOMES THE LARGEST CAR WASH IN PALM BEACH COUNTY. WEST PALM BEACH, FLA. , DEC. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MINT ECO CAR WASH HAS ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF ITS TENTH SITE IN PALM BEACH COUNTY, OFFICIALLY MAKING THE AWARD-WINNING, LOCAL BRAND THE LARGEST CAR WASH BUSINESS IN THE COUNTY.
