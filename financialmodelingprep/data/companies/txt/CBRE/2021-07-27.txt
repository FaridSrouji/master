CBRE TO ACQUIRE MAJORITY INTEREST IN AND FORM STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH TURNER & TOWNSEND. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CBRE GROUP, INC. (NYSE:CBRE) AND TURNER & TOWNSEND HOLDINGS LIMITED TODAY ANNOUNCED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT WHEREBY CBRE WILL ACQUIRE A 60% OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN, AND ENTER INTO A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH TURNER & TOWNSEND, A UNITED KINGDOM-BASED GLOBAL LEADER IN PROGRAM, PROJECT AND COST MANAGEMENT. CBRE WILL ACQUIRE A 60% STAKE FOR APPROXIMATELY £960 MILLION ($1.3 BILLION) IN CASH, WITH 55% TO BE PAID UPON CLOSING. THE TRANSACTION PRESERVES TURNER & TOWNS
