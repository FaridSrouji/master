CBRE RECOGNIZED ON DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX FOUR YEARS IN A ROW. DALLAS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CBRE GROUP, INC. (NYSE: CBRE) HAS AGAIN JOINED THE TOP CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY LEADERS ON THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX (DJSI) FOR THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. DJSI IDENTIFIES COMPANIES USING S&P GLOBAL'S CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY ASSESSMENT (CSA) FOCUSED ON ECONOMIC, ENVIRONMENTAL AND SOCIAL CRITERIA.
