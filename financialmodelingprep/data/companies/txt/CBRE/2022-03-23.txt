NATE PAUL'S WORLD CLASS SELLS 64-PROPERTY SELF-STORAGE PORTFOLIO ACROSS 10 STATES FOR $588 MILLION. AUSTIN, TEXAS, MARCH 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NATE PAUL'S WORLD CLASS HOLDINGS HAS SOLD A 64-PROPERTY SELF-STORAGE PORTFOLIO ACROSS 10 STATES TO GLOBAL REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT FIRM CBRE (NYSE: CBRE) AND FACILITY OPERATOR WILLIAM WARREN GROUP/STORQUEST FOR $588 MILLION. THE PORTFOLIO COMPRISES OVER 4.1 MILLION RENTABLE SQUARE FEET IN TEXAS, OHIO, ILLINOIS, COLORADO, MISSOURI, MISSISSIPPI, TENNESSEE, INDIANA, NEW YORK, AND NEVADA.
