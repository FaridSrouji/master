CBRE ARRANGES EXPANSION LEASE FOR COUR PHARMACEUTICALS. CHICAGO, NOV. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO MEET THE INCREASING DEMAND FOR MEDICAL RESEARCH SPACE IN THE CHICAGOLAND AREA, CBRE IS EXPANDING A 14,000 SQ. FT LEASE TO EXPAND COUR PHARMACEUTICALS' LIFE SCIENCE AND EMERGENCY BIOTECHNOLOGY FOOTPRINT WITHIN THE ILLINOIS SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY PARK IN SKOKIE, ILL.
