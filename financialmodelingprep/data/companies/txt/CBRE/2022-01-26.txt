CBRE EARNS PLACE IN BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CBRE GROUP, INC.'S CONTINUED FOCUS ON DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION EARNED THE COMPANY A PLACE IN THE 2022 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI) FOR THE THIRD YEAR IN A ROW. THE BLOOMBERG GEI BRINGS TRANSPARENCY TO GENDER-RELATED PRACTICES AND POLICIES AT PUBLICLY LISTED COMPANIES, INCREASING THE BREADTH OF ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, GOVERNANCE (ESG) DATA AVAILABLE TO INVESTORS.
