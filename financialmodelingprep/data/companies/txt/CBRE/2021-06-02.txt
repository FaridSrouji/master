CBRE GROUP, INC. RISES TO #122 ON THE FORTUNE 500. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CBRE GROUP, INC. (NYSE: CBRE) HAS RISEN TO #122 ON THE 2021 FORTUNE 500 LIST OF THE LARGEST U.S.-BASED COMPANIES—UP FROM #128 IN 2020. “COVID-19 POSED UNPRECEDENTED CHALLENGES THROUGHOUT 2020,” SAID BOB SULENTIC, CBRE'S PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. “OUR CONTINUED RISE IN THE FORTUNE RANKINGS IN A YEAR OF SUCH CHALLENGES REFLECTS OUR PEOPLE'S PASSION FOR DELIVERING GREAT OUTCOMES FOR OUR CLIENTS WHILE DEALING WITH SIGNIFICANT UPHEAVAL IN THEIR PERSONAL LIVES.
