NEW YORK LIFE INVESTMENTS PARTNERS WITH CBRE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT TO LAUNCH MAINSTAY CBRE GLOBAL INFRASTRUCTURE MEGATRENDS FUND. NEW YORK, OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEW YORK LIFE INVESTMENTS TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE MAINSTAY CBRE GLOBAL INFRASTRUCTURE MEGATRENDS FUND (THE "FUND") THROUGH AN ONGOING STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH CBRE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT, A LEADING GLOBAL REAL ASSETS INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT FIRM. NEW YORK LIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT LLC SERVES AS THE MANAGER OF THE FUND AND CBRE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT LISTED REAL ASSETS LLC SERVES AS THE SUBADVISOR.
