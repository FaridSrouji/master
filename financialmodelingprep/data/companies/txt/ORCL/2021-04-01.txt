ORACLE STATEMENT: RIMINI STREET DISTORTS COURT RULING, SAYS UP IS DOWN. AUSTIN, TEXAS, APRIL 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RIMINI STREET TODAY TOOK ITS DECEPTIVE BUSINESS PRACTICES DESCRIBED IN ORACLE'S LITIGATION AGAINST RIMINI STREET TO NEW HEIGHTS, PROCLAIMING THAT ITS UNEQUIVOCAL LOSS IN US DISTRICT COURT WAS ACTUALLY SOMEHOW A VICTORY. ITS RECENT STATEMENT IS NOTHING MORE THAN A CONVOLUTED ATTEMPT AT PUBLIC RELATIONS DAMAGE CONTROL.
