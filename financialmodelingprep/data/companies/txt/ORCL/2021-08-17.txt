ORACLE CLOUD ON THE MENU FOR BURGER KING AND CHURCH'S TEXAS CHICKEN IN MEXICO. CAMPACHE, MEXICO AND AUSTIN, TEXAS, AUG. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CORPORATIVO GES OPERATES MORE THAN 30 BURGER KING AND CHURCH'S TEXAS CHICKEN FRANCHISES IN MEXICO. WITH THE PANDEMIC PUTTING A STRAIN ON RESTAURANTS, THE FAST-GROWING OPERATOR KNEW IT NEEDED TO MOVE TO THE CLOUD TO IMPROVE THE EFFICIENCY OF ITS OPERATIONS.
