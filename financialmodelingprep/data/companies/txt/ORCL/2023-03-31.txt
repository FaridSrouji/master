ORACLE NAMED A LEADER IN THE 2023 GARTNER® MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR TRANSPORTATION MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS. ORACLE POSITIONED AS HIGHEST IN "ABILITY TO EXECUTE" AND FURTHEST ON "COMPLETENESS OF VISION" AUSTIN, TEXAS , MARCH 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE HAS BEEN NAMED A LEADER IN THE 2023 GARTNER MAGIC QUADRANT FOR TRANSPORTATION MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS (TMS) FOR ORACLE FUSION CLOUD TRANSPORTATION MANAGEMENT. OF THE 15 COMPANIES EVALUATED, ORACLE WAS POSITIONED HIGHEST ON THE "ABILITY TO EXECUTE" AND FURTHEST RIGHT ON THE "COMPLETENESS OF VISION" AXES.
