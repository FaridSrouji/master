CDC STUDY UNDERSCORES THE CRITICAL NEED FOR REAL-WORLD DATA IN MEDICINE. AUSTIN, TEXAS, MAY 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE NEW ENGLAND JOURNAL OF MEDICINE RECENTLY PUBLISHED A STUDY ON THE IMPACT OF COVID-19 VACCINATIONS ON PREGNANT PEOPLE. AT THE HEART OF THE STUDY WAS THE DATA THAT EXPECTANT INDIVIDUALS PROVIDED TO THE CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL (CDC) USING THE V-SAFE AFTER VACCINATION HEALTH CHECKER.
