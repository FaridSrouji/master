ORACLE NAMED A LEADER IN THE GARTNER MAGIC QUADRANT FOR CLOUD HCM SUITES FOR 1,000+ EMPLOYEE ENTERPRISES. AUSTIN, TEXAS, OCT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED A LEADER FOR THE SIXTH SUCCESSIVE YEAR IN THE 2021 GARTNER® MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR CLOUD HCM SUITES FOR 1,000+ EMPLOYEE ENTERPRISES FOR ORACLE FUSION CLOUD HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM). THE REPORT EVALUATES VENDORS BASED ON ABILITY TO EXECUTE AND COMPLETENESS OF VISION.
