ORACLE SETS THE DATE FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2021 EARNINGS ANNOUNCEMENT. AUSTIN, TEXAS, JUNE 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE CORPORATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS FOURTH QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2021 RESULTS WILL BE RELEASED ON TUESDAY, JUNE 15TH, AFTER THE CLOSE OF THE MARKET.  ORACLE WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL AND LIVE WEBCAST AT 4:00 P.M.
