ORACLE OPENS FIRST CLOUD REGION IN AFRICA. JOHANNESBURG, JAN. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE AVAILABILITY OF ITS FIRST CLOUD REGION IN AFRICA TO MEET THE RAPIDLY GROWING DEMAND FOR ENTERPRISE CLOUD SERVICES ON THE CONTINENT. THE ORACLE CLOUD JOHANNESBURG REGION WILL BOOST CLOUD ADOPTION ACROSS AFRICA WHILE ALSO HELPING BUSINESSES ACHIEVE BETTER PERFORMANCE AND DRIVE CONTINUOUS INNOVATION.
