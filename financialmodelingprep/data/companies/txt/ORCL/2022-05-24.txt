ORACLE ENHANCES ITS COMPREHENSIVE CLOUD SECURITY CAPABILITIES WITH INTEGRATED THREAT MANAGEMENT. NEW CAPABILITIES FORTIFY DEFENSES ACROSS ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE (OCI) AND THE ORACLE APPLICATIONS STACK TO HELP CUSTOMERS PROTECT THEIR APPLICATIONS AND DATA AUSTIN, TEXAS , MAY 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE IS EXPANDING THE BUILT-IN SECURITY SERVICES AND CAPABILITIES OF ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE (OCI) TO HELP CUSTOMERS PROTECT THEIR CLOUD APPLICATIONS AND DATA AGAINST EMERGING THREATS. FIVE NEW CAPABILITIES ROUND OUT OCI'S ALREADY COMPREHENSIVE SECURITY OFFERING, INCLUDING A NEW BUILT-IN AND CLOUD-NATIVE FIREWALL SERVICE AND ENHANCEMENTS TO ORACLE CLOUD GUARD AND ORACLE SECURITY ZONES.
