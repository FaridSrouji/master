ORACLE ANNOUNCES FISCAL 2021 FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL FULL YEAR FINANCIAL RESULTS. AUSTIN, TEXAS, JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ORCL) TODAY ANNOUNCED FISCAL 2021 Q4 RESULTS. TOTAL QUARTERLY REVENUES WERE UP 8% YEAR-OVER-YEAR TO $11.2 BILLION.
