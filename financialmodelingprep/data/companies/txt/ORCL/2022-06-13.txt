ORACLE ANNOUNCES FISCAL 2022 FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL FULL YEAR FINANCIAL RESULTS. Q4 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.16, NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.54 TOTAL Q4 REVENUE $11.8 BILLION, UP 5% IN USD, UP 10% IN CONSTANT CURRENCY TOTAL Q4 CLOUD REVENUE (IAAS PLUS SAAS) $2.9 BILLION, UP 19% IN USD, UP 22% IN CONSTANT CURRENCY INFRASTRUCTURE CLOUD REVENUE (IAAS) UP 36% IN USD, UP 39% IN CONSTANT CURRENCY FUSION ERP CLOUD REVENUE (SAAS) UP 20% IN USD, UP 23% IN CONSTANT CURRENCY NETSUITE ERP CLOUD REVENUE (SAAS) UP 27% IN USD, UP 30% IN CONSTANT CURRENCY AUSTIN, TEXAS , JUNE 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ORCL) TODAY ANNOUNCED FISCAL 2022 Q4 RESULTS. TOTAL QUARTERLY REVENUES WERE UP 5% YEAR-OVER-YEAR IN USD AND UP 10% IN CONSTANT CURRENCY TO $11.8 BILLION.
