ORACLE AND RED BULL RACING LAUNCH A NEW CHAPTER OF INNOVATION IN FORMULA ONE. AUSTIN, TEXAS, AND MILTON KEYNES, UK, FEB. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE AND RED BULL RACING HAVE ENHANCED THEIR PARTNERSHIP AND ARE INTRODUCING A NEW TEAM NAME, ORACLE RED BULL RACING, AS WELL AS THE TEAM'S NEW CAR FOR THE UPCOMING SEASON, THE RB18. BUILDING ON THE SUCCESS OF LAST YEAR'S F1 DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP, THE TEAM'S 2022 SEASON WILL FEATURE EXPANDED USE OF ORACLE CLOUD ACROSS ITS MOST SIGNIFICANT AREAS OF OPERATION INCLUDING ANALYTICS-BASED RACE STRATEGY, OPTIMIZED ENGINE DEVELOPMENT, AI AND MACHINE LEARNING-POWERED DRIVER TRAINING AND A TAILORED FAN EXPERIENCE.
