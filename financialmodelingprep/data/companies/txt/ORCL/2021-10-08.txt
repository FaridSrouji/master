ORACLE, TIM (TELECOM ITALIA) AND NOOVLE ANNOUNCE JOINT PLAN TO OFFER MULTICLOUD SERVICES IN ITALY. ROME AND AUSTIN, TEXAS, OCT. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE, TIM (TELECOM ITALIA) AND NOOVLE, TIM GROUP'S CLOUD COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE SIGNED A COLLABORATION AGREEMENT AS PART OF A PLAN TO OFFER MULTICLOUD SERVICES FOR ENTERPRISES AND PUBLIC SECTOR ORGANIZATIONS IN ITALY. UNDER THE AGREEMENT, TIM GROUP PLANS TO UTILIZE ADVANCED CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE TECHNOLOGIES TO SUPPORT ITS GOAL OF ADVANCING ITALY'S DIGITAL MODERNIZATION AND ESTABLISHING ITS POSITION, THROUGH NOOVLE, AS THE MARKET REFERENCE POINT FOR ENTERPRISE MULTICLOUD SERVICES IN THE COUNTRY.
