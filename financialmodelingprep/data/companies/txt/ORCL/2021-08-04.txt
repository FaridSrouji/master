W1TTY TAPS ORACLE TO PROVIDE A BETTER DIGITAL BANKING EXPERIENCE TO CUSTOMERS. LONDON AND AUSTIN, TEXAS, AUG. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- W1TTY, A SMART FINANCE INNOVATOR SET TO LAUNCH THIS FALL, AIMS TO OFFER CUSTOMERS A CLEAR CHOICE IN HOW THEY MANAGE THEIR MONEY WITH HYPER-PERSONALIZED PRODUCTS AND SERVICES. RUNNING ON ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE (OCI), W1TTY WILL USE ORACLE BANKING SUITE TO DELIVER AN EASIER AND MORE REWARDING FINANCIAL EXPERIENCE FOR CUSTOMERS.
