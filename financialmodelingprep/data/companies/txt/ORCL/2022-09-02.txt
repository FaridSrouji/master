ORACLE SETS THE DATE FOR ITS FIRST QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2023 EARNINGS ANNOUNCEMENT. EARNINGS RESULTS TO BE RELEASED ON SEPTEMBER 12, 2022, AFTER THE CLOSE OF THE MARKET AUSTIN, TEXAS , SEPT. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE CORPORATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS FIRST QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2023 RESULTS WILL BE RELEASED ON MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 12TH, AFTER THE CLOSE OF THE MARKET.
