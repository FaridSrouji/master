TELEFONICA ESPAÑA AND ORACLE ANNOUNCE COLLABORATION TO ACCELERATE CLOUD ADOPTION. MADRID AND AUSTIN, TEXAS, SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE AND TELEFONICA ESPAÑA TODAY ANNOUNCED A MULTI-YEAR COLLABORATION TO ACCELERATE THE TELECOMS COMPANY'S CLOUD ADOPTION AND THE DEVELOPMENT OF NEW COMMUNICATION SERVICES FOR CONSUMERS AND BUSINESSES.
