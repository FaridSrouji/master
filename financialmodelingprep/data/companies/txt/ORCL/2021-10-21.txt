RESEARCH SHOWS CUSTOMER ACTION KEY TO REACHING UTILITY DECARBONIZATION GOALS. AUSTIN, TEXAS, OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN THE RACE TO MEET AGGRESSIVE DECARBONIZATION AND NET-ZERO CARBON GOALS AND REGULATIONS, A NEW STUDY SHOWS THAT UTILITIES CANNOT AFFORD TO JUST FOCUS ON CLEAN ENERGY SUPPLY INVESTMENTS. THE REPORT, BY THE BRATTLE GROUP AND ORACLE UTILITIES, FOUND THAT BY 2040 ACTIONS BY UTILITY CUSTOMERS CAN REDUCE NEARLY TWO TIMES MORE GREENHOUSE GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS FROM THE RESIDENTIAL AND LIGHT-DUTY VEHICLE SECTORS THAN WOULD RESULT FROM CURRENT POLICIES TO PROMOTE INVESTMENTS IN CLEAN ENERGY SUPPLY ALONE.
