THOMSON REUTERS ROLLS OUT ONESOURCE INDIRECT TAX ON ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE WITH ORACLE AUTONOMOUS DATABASE. AUSTIN, TEXAS, JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THOMSON REUTERS, A LEADING PROVIDER OF BUSINESS INFORMATION SERVICES, TURNED TO ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE (OCI) AS PART OF ITS MULTI-CLOUD STRATEGY TO MAKE ITS INDIRECT TAX SOFTWARE AVAILABLE TO CUSTOMERS WORLDWIDE. IN ADDITION, THOMSON REUTERS WILL BUILD OUT THE APPLICATION ON ORACLE AUTONOMOUS DATABASE TO HELP SIMPLIFY PROCESSES AND ELEVATE PERFORMANCE WITH A CLOUD-FIRST SOLUTION.
