AT&T MEXICO TRANSFORMS ITS TECHNOLOGY STRATEGY WITH ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE. RELATIONSHIP BRINGS CONNECTIVITY AND COMMUNICATION SERVICES TO OVER 21 MILLION USERS NATIONWIDE BARCELONA, SPAIN AND MEXICO CITY , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MOBILE WORLD CONGRESS -- AT&T MEXICO IS MOVING CRITICAL IT AND BUSINESS PROCESSES TO ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE (OCI) TO EXPAND THE BENEFITS OF MOBILE INTERNET TO MORE THAN 21 MILLION SUBSCRIBERS AND BUSINESS CUSTOMERS IN INDUSTRIES SUCH AS EDUCATION, HEALTH AND BANKING NATIONWIDE. WITH OCI, THE COMPANY WILL BE ABLE TO MANAGE OSS/BSS WORKLOADS, ANALYTICS, AND DATABASES MORE EFFICIENTLY IN THE CLOUD.
