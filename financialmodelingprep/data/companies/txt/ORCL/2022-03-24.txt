ORACLE NAMES AWO ABLO TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS. AUSTIN, TEXAS , MARCH 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ORCL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT UNANIMOUSLY ELECTED AWO ABLO TO ORACLE'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND INCREASED THE SIZE OF THE BOARD TO 15. THE ELECTION IS EFFECTIVE AS OF MARCH 22, 2022.
