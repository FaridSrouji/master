ORACLE SETS THE DATE FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2023 EARNINGS ANNOUNCEMENT. EARNINGS RESULTS TO BE RELEASED ON JUNE 12, 2023, AFTER THE CLOSE OF THE MARKET AUSTIN, TEXAS , JUNE 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE CORPORATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS FOURTH QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2023 RESULTS WILL BE RELEASED ON MONDAY, JUNE 12TH, AFTER THE CLOSE OF THE MARKET.  ORACLE WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL AND LIVE WEBCAST AT 4:00 P.M.
