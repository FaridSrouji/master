STADIUM GOERS ROOT FOR HIGH-TECH OUTINGS. NEW ORACLE SURVEY SHOWS FANS WANT TECH TO CUT WAITS AND BOOST INTERACTIONS WITH FAVORITE TEAMS AND ARTISTS MANY FANS DRAW THE TECH LINE AT ROBOT UMPIRES AND REFEREES AUSTIN, TEXAS , AUG. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FROM THEIR MOBILE DEVICES TO THE METAVERSE, CONSUMERS ARE LOOKING TO TECH TO LEVEL-UP THEIR STADIUM OUTINGS, ACCORDING TO A NEW ORACLE FOOD AND BEVERAGE SURVEY. FOR EXAMPLE, 57% OF STADIUM GOERS WOULD LOVE TO PRE-ORDER FOOD AND DRINKS TO AVOID THE LINES AND 53% THINK IT WOULD BE AMAZING TO ENGAGE WITH THEIR FAVORITE TEAMS AND ARTISTS IN THE METAVERSE.
