SYNTAX TAPS ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE AS STRATEGIC PLATFORM FOR MULTICLOUD MANAGED SERVICES. AUSTIN, TEXAS, JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNTAX, A LEADING PROVIDER OF MULTICLOUD AND MISSION-CRITICAL APPLICATION MANAGED SERVICES, HAS INCREASED ITS INVESTMENT IN ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE (OCI) AS PART OF ITS MULTICLOUD STRATEGY AND WILL ACCELERATE THE TRANSFORMATION OF ORACLE E-BUSINESS SUITE TO THE CLOUD. AS PART OF THIS PARTNERSHIP, ORACLE AND SYNTAX WILL JOINTLY ENABLE ON-PREMISES ORACLE E-BUSINESS SUITE CUSTOMERS TO MIGRATE OR EXTEND THEIR SOLUTIONS BY TAKING ADVANTAGE OF OCI'S LOWER COSTS, BETTER PERFORMANCE, IMPROVED SCALABILITY, AND A WIDE ARRAY OF PLATFORM SERVICES.
