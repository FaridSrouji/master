ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE HELPS TRANSFORM HANA FINANCIAL GROUP'S 'HANA MEMBERS,' KOREA'S LARGEST DIGITAL FINANCIAL LIFESTYLE PLATFORM WITH 15 MILLION USERS. SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA, APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING THE WAY WITH AN INDUSTRY FIRST IN KOREA, HANA FINANCIAL GROUP (HFG) HAS COMPLETED A LARGE-SCALE MIGRATION OF ITS MISSION-CRITICAL APPLICATIONS TO THE PUBLIC CLOUD WORKING WITH ORACLE. THE MIGRATION IS PART OF HFG'S DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION OF ITS OPERATIONS.
