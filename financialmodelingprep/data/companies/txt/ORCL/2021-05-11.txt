JAPANESE MEGABANK WORKS WITH ORACLE TO MODERNIZE ACCOUNTING SYSTEMS. AUSTIN, TEXAS, MAY 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SUMITOMO MITSUI FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. (SMBC GROUP) HAS SELECTED ORACLE FUSION CLOUD ENTERPRISE RESOURCE PLANNING (ERP) TO CONSOLIDATE ACCOUNTING FUNCTIONS AS PART OF ITS EFFORTS TO STREAMLINE BUSINESS PROCESSES. SMBC GROUP WILL IMPLEMENT ITS SHARED SERVICES ACROSS ITS GROUP COMPANIES.
