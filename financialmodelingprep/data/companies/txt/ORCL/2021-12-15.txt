ORACLE OPENS FIRST CLOUD REGION IN ITALY. MILAN, DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF ITS FIRST CLOUD REGION IN MILAN, ITALY, TO SUPPORT THE INCREASING CLOUD COMPUTING DEMANDS OF PRIVATE AND PUBLIC SECTOR ORGANIZATIONS. THE ORACLE CLOUD MILAN REGION WILL PROVIDE CUSTOMERS AND PARTNERS ACCESS TO ORACLE CLOUD SERVICES TO DRIVE BUSINESS GROWTH.
