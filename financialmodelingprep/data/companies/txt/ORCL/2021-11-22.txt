SUPPLY CHAIN WORRIES REMAIN, BUT CONSUMERS SLOW TO HOLIDAY SHOP. AUSTIN, TEXAS, NOV. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A NEW ORACLE RETAIL SURVEY SHOWS THAT CONSUMERS HAVE BARELY MADE A DENT IN THEIR HOLIDAY SHOPPING. WHILE A SEPTEMBER SURVEY1 SHOWED THAT 52% OF AMERICANS HAD ALREADY STARTED HOLIDAY SHOPPING OR PLAN TO SHOP EARLIER THAN USUAL, WHEN ASKED AGAIN LAST WEEK, 66% SAID THEY ARE LESS THAN HALFWAY DONE WITH THEIR HOLIDAY SHOPPING.
SUPPLY CHAIN WOES HAVE CONSUMERS GOBBLING UP THANKSGIVING FIXINGS EARLY. AUSTIN, TEXAS, NOV. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICANS ARE WORRIED ABOUT THE IMPACT SUPPLY CHAIN ISSUES AND HIGH PRICES WILL HAVE ON THANKSGIVING. AS A RESULT, NEARLY A QUARTER (23%) OF THOSE SURVEYED IN THE PAST WEEK HAVE ALREADY SHOPPED FOR THEIR NON-PERISHABLE ITEMS AND 35% PLAN TO SHOP EARLIER THAN NORMAL FOR THANKSGIVING GROCERIES.
