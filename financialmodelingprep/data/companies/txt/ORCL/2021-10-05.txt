ORACLE CLOUD HELPS K-12 SCHOOLS BETTER SUPPORT STUDENTS AND STAFF. AUSTIN, TEXAS, OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- K-12 SCHOOL SYSTEMS ACROSS THE U.S. ARE ADOPTING ORACLE CLOUD TO SIMPLIFY BUSINESS PROCESSES, MAXIMIZE RESOURCES, AND BETTER MANAGE AND SUPPORT STUDENTS AND STAFF. WITH FLUCTUATING BUDGETS AND FEDERAL AND STATE-WIDE GUIDELINES, INSTITUTIONS ARE SEEKING WAYS TO DO MORE WITH LESS WITHOUT JEOPARDIZING THE QUALITY OF THEIR EDUCATIONAL OFFERINGS.
