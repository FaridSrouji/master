RIMINI STREET HELD IN CONTEMPT OF COURT. AUSTIN, TEXAS, JAN. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, IN RIMINI STREET, INC. V. ORACLE INTERNATIONAL, THE DISTRICT COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF NEVADA HELD RIMINI IN CONTEMPT OF COURT FOR WILLFULLY AND REPEATEDLY VIOLATING THE COURT'S 2018 PERMANENT INJUNCTION TO STOP VIOLATING ORACLE'S INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS.
