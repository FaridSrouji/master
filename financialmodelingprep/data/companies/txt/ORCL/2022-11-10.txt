ORACLE APPOINTS STUART LEVEY EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER. AUSTIN, TEXAS , NOV. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT STUART LEVEY HAS BEEN NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER. "STUART HAS ONE OF THE SHARPEST LEGAL MINDS IN THE COUNTRY," SAID ORACLE CEO, SAFRA CATZ.
