ORACLE BUYS CERNER. AUSTIN, TEXAS AND KANSAS CITY, MO., DEC. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ORCL) AND CERNER CORPORATION TODAY JOINTLY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT FOR ORACLE TO ACQUIRE CERNER THROUGH AN ALL-CASH TENDER OFFER FOR $95.00 PER SHARE, OR APPROXIMATELY $28.3 BILLION IN EQUITY VALUE.
