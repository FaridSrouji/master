HALF OF US COLLEGES SURVEYED HAVE NOT STARTED PREPARATIONS FOR NEW STUDENT AID REQUIREMENTS. AUSTIN, TEXAS, NOV. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A NEW SURVEY SHOWS THAT MORE THAN HALF (52%) OF RESPONDENTS AT US HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS HAVE NOT YET STARTED TO PREPARE FOR THE SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO THE FREE APPLICATION FOR FEDERAL STUDENT AID (FAFSA) THAT WILL GO INTO EFFECT IN THE 2024-25 SCHOOL YEAR. ALL COLLEGE STUDENTS SEEKING FEDERAL FINANCIAL AID IN THE US FILL OUT THE APPLICATION.
