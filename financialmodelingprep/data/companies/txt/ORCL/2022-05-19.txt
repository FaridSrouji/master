ORACLE AND NIH NETWORKS COLLABORATE TO HELP END HIV. ORACLE EXTENDS TECHNOLOGY FIRST BUILT TO FIGHT COVID-19 TO ACCELERATE EFFORTS TO GET A SAFE AND EFFECTIVE HIV VACCINE TO MARKET AUSTIN, TEXAS , MAY 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HIV VACCINE TRIALS NETWORK, FUNDED BY THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF ALLERGY AND INFECTIOUS DISEASES (NIAID), A PART OF THE NIH, TODAY LAUNCHED A NEW HIV AWARENESS CAMPAIGN AND THE RED RIBBON REGISTRY WITH THE GOAL OF HELPING END HIV. BUILT BY ORACLE, THE UNIQUE CLOUD-BASED REGISTRY MAKES IT EASY FOR ANYONE TO VOLUNTEER TO BE CONSIDERED FOR AN HIV CLINICAL STUDY – GETTING US ONE STEP CLOSER TO A CURE.
