WILLIAM A. VON HOENE JR., SENIOR EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF STRATEGY OFFICER, TO DEPART EXELON ON MAR. 31. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EXELON CORP. (NASDAQ: EXC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT WILLIAM (BILL) VON HOENE JR., SENIOR EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF STRATEGY OFFICER, IS LEAVING EXELON ON MARCH 31, BRINGING AN ACCOMPLISHED 19-YEAR CAREER AT THE COMPANY TO A CLOSE. “BILL'S UNPARALLELED LEGAL MIND, OUTSTANDING STRATEGIC CAPABILITIES AND KEEN EMOTIONAL INTELLIGENCE HAVE BEEN INTEGRAL TO THE SUCCESS OF EXELON, AND THE IMPACT OF HIS WORK CANNOT BE OVERSTATED,” SAID CHRIS CRANE, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF EXE
