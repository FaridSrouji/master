EXELON JOINS ELECTRIC HIGHWAY COALITION; ENCOURAGES EV ADOPTION. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EXELON HAS JOINED THE ELECTRIC HIGHWAY COALITION WITH 14 U.S. UTILITIES TO CREATE A NETWORK OF EV CHARGING STATIONS CONNECTING MAJOR HIGHWAY SYSTEMS.
