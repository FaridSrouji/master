COMED DONATES 3,300 TREES TO 255 NORTHERN ILLINOIS COMMUNITIES TO ENHANCE GREEN SPACE. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN HONOR OF ARBOR DAY ON APRIL 29, COMED TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL DONATE OVER 3,300 TREES AND SHRUBS TO COMMUNITIES ACROSS NORTHERN ILLINOIS TO EXPAND GREEN SPACES ACROSS ITS SERVICE TERRITORY. A COLLABORATION WITH THE ARBOR DAY FOUNDATION'S ENERGY-SAVING TREES PROGRAM, COMED RECOGNIZES THE IMPORTANT ROLE THAT TREES PLAY IN MAINTAINING CLEAN AIR AND COMMUNITY SPACES, AS WELL AS THE POSITIVE IMPACT ON ENERGY EFFICIENCY FOR CUSTOMERS.
