EXELON ANNOUNCES EXTENSION OF EXCHANGE OFFERS. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--EXELON CORPORATION (EXELON) (NASDAQ: EXC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS EXTENDED THE EXPIRATION DATE FOR EACH OF ITS OFFERS TO EXCHANGE ANY AND ALL OF ITS OUTSTANDING UNREGISTERED NOTES LISTED BELOW THAT WERE ORIGINALLY ISSUED IN PRIVATE OFFERINGS FOR EQUAL PRINCIPAL AMOUNTS OF NEW ISSUES OF NOTES REGISTERED UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED.
