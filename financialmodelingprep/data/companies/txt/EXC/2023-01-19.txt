EXELON ANNOUNCES JANE S. PARK AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF FEDERAL GOVERNMENTAL AND REGULATORY AFFAIRS, AND PUBLIC POLICY. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--EXELON (NASDAQ: EXC) ANNOUNCES THAT JANE S. PARK HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE ROLE OF SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF FEDERAL GOVERNMENTAL AND REGULATORY AFFAIRS, AND PUBLIC POLICY FOR THE COMPANY.
