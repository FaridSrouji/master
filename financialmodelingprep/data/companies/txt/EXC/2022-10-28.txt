COMED ANNOUNCES $250,000 IN GRANTS TO BRING EVS INTO DRIVER EDUCATION. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STUDENTS AT FIVE AREA PUBLIC HIGH SCHOOLS WILL GET BEHIND THE WHEEL OF AN ELECTRIC VEHICLES (EV) IN DRIVER EDUCATION CLASS THANKS TO A $250,000 INVESTMENT COMED ANNOUNCED TODAY WITH CHICAGO PUBLIC SCHOOLS (CPS) AND EDUCATORS FROM ACROSS THE REGION.
