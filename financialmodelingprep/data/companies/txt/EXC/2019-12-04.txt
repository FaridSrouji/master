EXELON CORPORATION ANNOUNCES STEPHEN STEINOUR, DIRECTOR OF COMPANY NOT STANDING FOR ELECTION. ON DECEMBER 3, 2019, EXELON CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT MR. STEPHEN STEINOUR, A DIRECTOR OF COMPANY, INFORMED THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS THAT HE WILL NOT STAND FOR ELECTION AS A DIRECTOR OF EXELON AT THE 2020 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS.
