NEW YORK PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION APPROVES EXELON SEPARATION. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EXELON CORP. (NASDAQ: EXC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE NEW YORK STATE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION HAS APPROVED A UNANIMOUS SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT THAT ALLOWS EXELON TO MOVE FORWARD WITH ITS PLAN TO SEPARATE INTO TWO COMPANIES IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. IN APPROVING THE SETTLEMENT, THE COMMISSION AUTHORIZED THE TRANSFER OF EXELON'S NEW YORK NUCLEAR FACILITIES TO THE NEW CONSTELLATION COMPANY, WHICH WILL OCCUR AT SEPARATION.
