COMED, METROPOLITAN MAYORS CAUCUS OFFER GRANTS TO ILLUMINATE COMMUNITY LIGHT EVENTS THIS HOLIDAY SEASON. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EVEN DURING THE HEIGHT OF THE SUMMER HEAT, IT'S NEVER TOO EARLY TO PLAN FOR THE WINTER HOLIDAY SEASON.
