EXELON FOUNDATION RECOGNIZES EMPLOYEES DURING NATIONAL VOLUNTEER MONTH FOR YEAR-ROUND VOLUNTEERING EFFORTS. IN 2022 ALONE, VOLUNTEERS GAVE 126,529 HOURS OF THEIR TIME TO LOCAL NONPROFITS.
