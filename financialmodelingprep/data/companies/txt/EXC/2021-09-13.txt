PASSAGE OF ILLINOIS ENERGY LEGISLATION PRESERVES NUCLEAR PLANTS AND STRENGTHENS STATE'S CLEAN ENERGY LEADERSHIP. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EXELON GENERATION SAID TODAY THAT IT IS PREPARING TO REFUEL ITS BYRON AND DRESDEN NUCLEAR PLANTS AS A RESULT OF THE ACTION TAKEN BY THE ILLINOIS LEGISLATURE TO ENACT A COMPREHENSIVE ENERGY BILL.
