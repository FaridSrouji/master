COMED ANNOUNCES $450,000 TO LAUNCH SKILLED TRADES PROGRAM IN PARTNERSHIP WITH FOUR CHICAGO CATHOLIC HIGH SCHOOLS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMED, IN COLLABORATION WITH BIG SHOULDERS FUND AND UNITED WAY METRO CHICAGO, TODAY LAUNCHED POWERING OUR FUTURE – A NEW INITIATIVE THAT WILL EXPAND SKILLED TRADES AND STEM TRAINING AT FOUR CHICAGO CATHOLIC HIGH SCHOOLS. TO REMOVE BARRIERS TO ENTRY TO ENERGY AND SKILLED TRADES CAREERS, A NEW $450,000 INVESTMENT BY COMED AND PARENT COMPANY EXELON WILL PILOT THE PROGRAM IN SCHOOLS LOCATED IN HISTORICALLY UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES.
