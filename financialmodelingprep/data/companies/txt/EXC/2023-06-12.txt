CLIMATE CHANGE INVESTMENT INITIATIVE PAVES THE WAY FOR INNOVATION IN BALTIMORE. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STARTUPS WHOSE PRODUCTS, SERVICES OR TECHNOLOGIES ARE FOCUSED ON FIGHTING CLIMATE CHANGE AND IMPROVING THE QUALITY OF LIFE FOR RESIDENTS OF THE BALTIMORE REGION AND BEYOND, ARE ENCOURAGED TO APPLY FOR AN EQUITY INVESTMENT FROM 2C2I, A JOINT EFFORT OF THE EXELON FOUNDATION AND EXELON CORPORATION (NASDAQ: EXC). EACH YEAR, EXELON PROVIDES EQUITY INVESTMENTS TO STARTUPS WHOSE WORK BENEFITS RESIDENTS OF A REGION SERVED BY THE COMPANY. IN ADDITION TO THE EXELON FOUNDATION'S.
