COMED AWARDED FOR TWO PROGRAMS THAT MAKE ENERGY EFFICIENCY BENEFITS MORE ACCESSIBLE TO FAMILIES AND BUSINESSES. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE MIDWEST ENERGY EFFICIENCY ALLIANCE (MEEA) HAS RECOGNIZED COMED WITH A PAIR OF INSPIRING EFFICIENCY AWARDS. THE RECOGNITION IS FOR THE ELECTRIC COMPANY'S EFFORTS TO HELP PROPERTY OWNERS AND TENANTS SAVE MONEY ON ELECTRIC BILLS, AS WELL AS AN EDUCATION AND TRAINING THAT HELPS DIVERSE CONTRACTORS BUILD SUCCESSFUL BUSINESSES IN THE ENERGY EFFICIENCY FIELD.
