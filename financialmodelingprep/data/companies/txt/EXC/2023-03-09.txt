EXELON ENERGY TRANSFORMATION CENTERED ON DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS JUST ONE PART OF ITS COMMITMENT TO ENSURE THE CLEAN ENERGY FUTURE IS ACCESSIBLE TO ALL COMMUNITIES, EXELON (NASDAQ: EXC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT SPENT MORE THAN $2.88 BILLION WITH DIVERSE SUPPLIERS IN 2022, REPRESENTING 39 PERCENT OF TOTAL ENTERPRISE-WIDE SPEND. OVER THE PAST FIVE YEARS, EXELON'S SPENDING WITH BUSINESSES OWNED BY WOMEN, PEOPLE OF COLOR, VETERANS AND LGBT PERSONS TOTALED MORE THAN $13 BILLION.
