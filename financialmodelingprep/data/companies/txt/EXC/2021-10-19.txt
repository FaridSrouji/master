10 HOLIDAY LIGHTING EVENTS AWARDED GRANTS FROM COMED AND METROPOLITAN MAYORS CAUCUS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--RECOGNIZING THE IMPORTANCE OF CULTURAL HOLIDAY CELEBRATIONS AND HOW THEY CAN POSITIVELY IMPACT COMMUNITIES, COMED AND THE METROPOLITAN MAYORS CAUCUS TODAY ANNOUNCE GRANTS OF UP TO $2,500 TO 10 NORTHERN ILLINOIS COMMUNITIES AS PART OF THE INAUGURAL COMED POWERING THE HOLIDAYS PROGRAM.
