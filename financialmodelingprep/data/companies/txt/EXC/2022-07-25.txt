COMED SEEKING FOUR NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS TO RECEIVE $1.8 MILLION IN CLIMATE-FRIENDLY GRANTS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TO HELP BRING MORE CLIMATE-FRIENDLY ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS TO NORTHERN ILLINOIS FAMILIES AND BUSINESSES, COMED TODAY ANNOUNCED A $1.8 MILLION COMPETITIVE GRANT PROGRAM TO SUPPORT NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS IN OFFERING ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS FOCUSED ON CLEAN ENERGY TO LIMITED-INCOME CUSTOMERS IN THE COMMUNITIES THE ENERGY COMPANY SERVES.
