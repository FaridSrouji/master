HOUSING INITIATIVE PARTNERSHIP, PEPCO AND EMERA TECHNOLOGIES AWARDED $200,000 GRANT BY THE MARYLAND ENERGY ADMINISTRATION FOR INNOVATIVE MICROGRID COMMUNITY IN FAIRMOUNT HEIGHTS. WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HOUSING INITIATIVE PARTNERSHIP (HIP), A GREEN NONPROFIT AFFORDABLE HOUSING DEVELOPER BASED IN PRINCE GEORGE'S COUNTY, ALONG WITH PEPCO AND EMERA TECHNOLOGIES, HAVE BEEN AWARDED A $200,000 GRANT FROM THE MARYLAND ENERGY ADMINISTRATION (MEA) TO CONSTRUCT A NEW RESIDENTIAL MICROGRID TO POWER A SMALL SUBDIVISION OF HIGHLY ENERGY EFFICIENT SINGLE FAMILY HOMES IN FAIRMOUNT HEIGHTS, MARYLAND.
