CONSUMER ALERT: COMED, BETTER BUSINESS BUREAU REPORT ANOTHER RISE IN MOBILE PAYMENT APP SCAMS FOLLOWING SUMMER HEAT WAVE. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WHEN TEMPERATURES RISE OR FALL TO EXTREMES, AND ELECTRICITY USE INCREASES, COMED AND THE BETTER BUSINESS BUREAU (BBB) HAVE OBSERVED SCAMMERS TAKING ADVANTAGE OF THESE CONDITIONS TO BILK CUSTOMERS OUT OF THEIR HARD-EARNED MONEY. IN JULY 2022, FOLLOWING A STRETCH OF EXTREME HEAT DURING THE FIRST FIVE WEEKS OF SUMMER, COMED RECEIVED NEARLY 280 REPORTS OF SCAMS FROM CUSTOMERS, WHICH IS SIGNIFICANTLY HIGHER THAN THE 80 REPORTS RECEIVED IN JULY 2021.
