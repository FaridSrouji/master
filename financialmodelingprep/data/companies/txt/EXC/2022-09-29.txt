COMED TOOLKIT HELPS CUSTOMERS NAVIGATE PURCHASING AN ELECTRIC VEHICLE. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BECAUSE NAVIGATING THE EMERGENT ELECTRIC VEHICLE (EV) MARKET CAN BE INTIMIDATING, COMED HAS CREATED A ONE-STOP-SHOP FOR THOSE CONSIDERING AN EV PURCHASE. THE COMED EV TOOLKIT PROVIDES INFORMATION ON SAVINGS, BENEFITS AND INCENTIVES FOR THE PURCHASE OF EVS, ALONG WITH AN OVERVIEW OF EV BRANDS AND MODELS, RATE OPTIONS, EV CHARGERS AND A PUBLIC CHARGING STATION LOCATOR.
