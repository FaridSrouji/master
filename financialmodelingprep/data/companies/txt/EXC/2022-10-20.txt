COMED, NORTH CAROLINA STATE UNIVERSITY RECEIVE FEDERAL GRANT TO DEVELOP, DEMONSTRATE EXTREME FAST CHARGING FOR ELECTRIC VEHICLES. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMED, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH NORTH CAROLINA STATE UNIVERSITY'S FREEDM SYSTEMS CENTER, HAS BEEN AWARDED A $200,000 FEDERAL RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT GRANT FROM THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (DOE) TO HELP FUND A $5 MILLION RESEARCH PROJECT FOCUSED ON IMPROVING THE EFFICIENCY OF, AND REDUCING THE COST OF, EXTREME FAST CHARGING (XFC) FOR ELECTRIC VEHICLES (EVS).
