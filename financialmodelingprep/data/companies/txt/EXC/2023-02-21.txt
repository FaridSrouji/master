YOUNG WOMEN IN ILLINOIS CAN APPLY TO BUILD, RACE ELECTRIC-POWERED RACECARS IN COMED EV RALLY. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WITH WOMEN'S HISTORY MONTH JUST AROUND THE CORNER, COMED IS EXCITED TO ANNOUNCE THE RETURN OF ITS SIGNATURE SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, ENGINEERING AND MATH (STEM) PROGRAM FOR FUTURE WOMEN'S HISTORY-MAKERS, THE COMED EV RALLY. YOUNG WOMEN IN ILLINOIS CAN NOW APPLY TO PARTICIPATE IN THE ANNUAL SUMMER EVENT, A COMPETITION THAT CHALLENGES TEEN GIRLS TO BUILD AND RACE HIGH-TECH, ELECTRIC-POWERED GO-CARTS.
