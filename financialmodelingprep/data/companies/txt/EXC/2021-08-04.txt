EXELON UTILITIES ANNOUNCES GOAL TO ACHIEVE NET-ZERO EMISSIONS BY 2050. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EXELON UTILITIES TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL REDUCE ITS OPERATIONS-DRIVEN EMISSIONS 50 PERCENT BY 2030* AND ULTIMATELY TO NET-ZERO BY 2050 AS PART OF ITS CONTINUING EFFORTS TO ADDRESS THE CLIMATE CRISIS. A DIVISION OF EXELON CORPORATION, EXELON UTILITIES IS COMPOSED OF ATLANTIC CITY ELECTRIC, BGE, COMED, DELMARVA POWER, PECO AND PEPCO.
