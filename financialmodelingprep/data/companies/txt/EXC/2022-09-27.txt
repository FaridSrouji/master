COMED PILOTING HERBICIDE DRONE PROGRAM TO PREVENT POWER OUTAGES THROUGH VEGETATION MANAGEMENT. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMED HAS BEGUN PILOTING AN INNOVATIVE APPROACH TO VEGETATION MANAGEMENT NEAR POWERLINES WITH THE HELP OF SPECIALIZED DRONES. THE FIRST DEPLOYMENT OF DRONE-DELIVERED HERBICIDE TOOK PLACE ON TWO-ACRES OF COMED RIGHT-OF-WAY LAND IN FOREST VIEW, ILL. IN LATE AUGUST. THE DRONE DEPLOYMENT PROCESS TOOK 45 MINUTES AND 0.75 GALLONS OF HERBICIDE TO COMPLETE WHAT WOULD HAVE TAKEN A HUMAN CREW APPROXIMATELY TWO DAYS OF WORK AND 8 GALLONS OF HERBICIDE SOLUTION.
