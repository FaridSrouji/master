MONOLITHIC POWER SYSTEMS ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND. KIRKLAND, WASH., DEC. 15, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONOLITHIC POWER SYSTEMS, INC. (MPS) (NASDAQ: MPWR), A LEADING COMPANY IN HIGH-PERFORMANCE ANALOG SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.50 PER COMMON SHARE TO ALL STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 31, 2020. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID TO STOCKHOLDERS ON JANUARY 15, 2021.
