MONOLITHIC POWER SYSTEMS RECEIVES 3RD PARTY ISO26262 PROCESS CERTIFICATION FOR AUTOMOTIVE FUNCTIONAL SAFETY. KIRKLAND, WASH., APRIL 19, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  MONOLITHIC POWER SYSTEMS, INC. (MPS) (NASDAQ: MPWR), A GLOBAL COMPANY THAT PROVIDES HIGH-PERFORMANCE, SEMICONDUCTOR-BASED POWER ELECTRONICS SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACHIEVEMENT OF INDEPENDENT THIRD-PARTY CERTIFICATION OF ITS ISO26262 FUNCTIONAL SAFETY PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PROCESS. THE ISO26262 INTERNATIONAL STANDARD HAS BECOME A CORE REQUIREMENT TO ADDRESS THE RISKS OF FAILURE IN ELECTRICAL AND ELECTRONIC SYSTEMS AS THE AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRY PURSUES INCREASINGLY AUTONOMOUS, CONNECTED, AND ELECTRIFIED CARS.
