NIKE, INC. ANNOUNCES 11 PERCENT INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BEAVERTON, ORE.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NIKE, INC. (NYSE: NKE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.340 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK. THIS REPRESENTS AN INCREASE OF 11 PERCENT VERSUS THE PRIOR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE OF $0.305 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND DECLARED TODAY IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 28, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS DECEMBER 5, 2022.
