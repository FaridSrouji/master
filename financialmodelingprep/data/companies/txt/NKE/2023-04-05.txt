NIKE, INC. NAMES KEJUAN WILKINS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER. BEAVERTON, ORE.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NIKE, INC. (NYSE: NKE) ANNOUNCED A CHANGE IN ITS SENIOR EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM (ELT) TODAY. KEJUAN WILKINS, VICE PRESIDENT OF ENTERPRISE COMMUNICATIONS, WILL BECOME THE COMPANY'S NEW EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER (CCO). IN HIS NEW ROLE, WILKINS WILL LEAD ALL GLOBAL CORPORATE, CONSUMER, AND EMPLOYEE COMMUNICATIONS FOR NIKE, INC.
