(NYSE: NKE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.275 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON APRIL 1, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS MARCH 1, 2021.
