NIKE, INC. REPORTS FISCAL 2021 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS. BEAVERTON, ORE.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NIKE, INC. (NYSE:NKE) TODAY REPORTED FISCAL 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS SECOND QUARTER ENDED NOVEMBER 30, 2020.
