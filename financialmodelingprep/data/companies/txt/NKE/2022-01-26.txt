NIKE, INC. NAMES ANN MILLER EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & GENERAL COUNSEL. BEAVERTON, ORE.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NIKE, INC. (NYSE: NKE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ANN MILLER, A 14-YEAR NIKE VETERAN WITH MORE THAN TWO DECADES OF LEGAL EXPERIENCE, WILL BECOME EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & GENERAL COUNSEL, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 17, 2022. MILLER SUCCEEDS HILARY KRANE, EVP, CAO & GENERAL COUNSEL, NIKE, INC., WHO HAS DECIDED TO RETIRE IN FEBRUARY 2022, AFTER 12 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY. “FOR MORE THAN 14 YEARS, ANN HAS BEEN A PROVEN NIKE LEADER WHO HAS MADE SIGNIFICANT CONTRIBUTIONS
