REPORTS FISCAL 2020 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.70.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.57 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q2 REVENUE $10.3 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $10.07 BILLION.AT QUARTER-END, INVENTORIES FOR CO WERE $6.2 BILLION, UP 15 PERCENT COMPARED TO PRIOR YEAR PERIOD.QTRLY REVENUES FOR NIKE BRAND WERE $9.8 BILLION, UP 12 PERCENT ON A CURRENCY-NEUTRAL BASIS.QTRLY NORTH AMERICA TOTAL REVENUE WAS $3,982 MILLION VERSUS $3,782 MILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.QTRLY GROSS MARGIN INCREASED 20 BASIS POINTS TO 44.0 PERCENT.QTRLY SELLING AND ADMINISTRATIVE EXPENSE INCREASED 6 PERCENT TO $3.3 BILLION.QTRLY EUROPE, MIDDLE EAST & AFRICA TOTAL REVENUE WAS $2,537 MILLION VERSUS $2,313 MILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.QTRLY GREATER CHINA TOTAL REVENUE WAS $1,847 MILLION VERSUS $1,544 MILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.QTRLY REVENUE UP 10% ON REPORTED BASIS, UP 13% ON A CURRENCY-NEUTRAL BASIS, DRIVEN BY STRONG GROWTH ACROSS ALL GEOGRAPHIES.QTRLY GROSS MARGIN PARTIALLY OFFSET BY IMPACTS FROM HIGHER PRODUCT COSTS, PRIMARILY DUE TO INCREMENTAL TARIFFS IN NORTH AMERICA.
