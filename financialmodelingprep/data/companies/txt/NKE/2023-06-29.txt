NIKE, INC. REPORTS FISCAL 2023 FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR RESULTS. BEAVERTON, ORE.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NIKE, INC. (NYSE:NKE) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FISCAL 2023 FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR ENDED MAY 31, 2023. FULL YEAR REPORTED REVENUES WERE $51.2 BILLION, UP 10 PERCENT COMPARED TO PRIOR YEAR AND UP 16 PERCENT ON A CURRENCY-NEUTRAL BASIS* FOURTH QUARTER REPORTED REVENUES WERE $12.8 BILLION, UP 5 PERCENT COMPARED TO PRIOR YEAR AND UP 8 PERCENT ON A CURRENCY-NEUTRAL BASIS NIKE DIRECT REPORTED REVENUES FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER WERE $5.5 BILLION, UP.
