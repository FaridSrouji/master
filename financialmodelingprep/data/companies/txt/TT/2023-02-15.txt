FORBES NAMES TRANE TECHNOLOGIES ONE OF AMERICA'S BEST LARGE EMPLOYERS. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRANE TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S BEST LARGE EMPLOYERS BY FORBES. TO COMPILE THIS YEAR'S LIST, FORBES PARTNERED WITH MARKET RESEARCH COMPANY STATISTA TO SURVEY 45,000 WORKERS FROM SOME OF THE LARGEST COMPANIES IN THE UNITED STATES.
