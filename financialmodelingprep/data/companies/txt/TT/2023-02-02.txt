TRANE TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS STRONG FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2022 RESULTS; ROBUST BACKLOG PROVIDES STRONG VISIBILITY ENTERING 2023. SWORDS, IRELAND--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TRANE TECHNOLOGIES PLC (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, TODAY REPORTED DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $1.91 FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022. ADJUSTED CONTINUING EPS WAS $1.82, UP 34 PERCENT, WHICH EXCLUDES $22.5 MILLION OF NET FAVORABLE PRE-TAX NON-GAAP ADJUSTMENTS.
