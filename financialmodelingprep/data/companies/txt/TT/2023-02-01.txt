TRANE TECHNOLOGIES NAMED TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES LIST FOR ELEVENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. SWORDS, IRELAND--( BUSINESS WIRE )--FORTUNE MAGAZINE HAS NAMED TRANE TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, TO ITS WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES LIST FOR THE ELEVENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
