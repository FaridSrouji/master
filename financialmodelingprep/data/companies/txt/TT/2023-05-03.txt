TRANE TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS STRONG FIRST-QUARTER 2023 RESULTS; RAISES 2023 GUIDANCE. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRANE TECHNOLOGIES PLC (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, TODAY REPORTED DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $1.35 FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023. ADJUSTED CONTINUING EPS WAS $1.41, UP 26 PERCENT, EXCLUDING $15.6 MILLION OF PRE-TAX NON-GAAP ADJUSTMENTS.
