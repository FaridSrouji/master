TRANE TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS STRONG SECOND-QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRANE TECHNOLOGIES PLC (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, TODAY REPORTED DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $1.91 FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021. ADJUSTED CONTINUING EPS WAS $1.92, UP 51 PERCENT, WHICH EXCLUDES $5.3 MILLION RELATED TO PLANNED RESTRUCTURING AND TRANSFORMATION COSTS.
