TRANE TECHNOLOGIES DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TRANE TECHNOLOGIES PLC (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.59 PER ORDINARY SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 30, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 4, 2021. TRANE TECHNOLOGIES HAS PAID CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDENDS ON ITS COMMON SHARES SINCE 1919 AND ANNUAL DIVIDENDS SINCE 1910.
