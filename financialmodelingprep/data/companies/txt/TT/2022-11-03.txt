TRANE TECHNOLOGIES SURPASSES U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY REQUIREMENTS FOR HIGH-EFFICIENCY, COLD CLIMATE HEAT PUMP. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRANE TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, HAS SURPASSED U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (DOE) REQUIREMENTS FOR THE RESIDENTIAL COLD CLIMATE HEAT PUMP (CCHP) CHALLENGE FOR HIGH-EFFICIENCY HEATING IN FREEZING TEMPERATURES.
