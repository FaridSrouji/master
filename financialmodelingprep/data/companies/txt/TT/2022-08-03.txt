TRANE TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS RECORD SECOND-QUARTER 2022 RESULTS; RAISES FULL-YEAR REVENUE AND EPS GUIDANCE. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRANE TECHNOLOGIES PLC (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, TODAY REPORTED DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $2.17 FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022. ADJUSTED CONTINUING EPS WAS $2.16, UP 13 PERCENT, WHICH EXCLUDES A NET GAIN OF $3.3 MILLION RELATED PRIMARILY TO NON-CASH ADJUSTMENTS, TRANSFORMATION AND OTHER RESTRUCTURING COSTS.
