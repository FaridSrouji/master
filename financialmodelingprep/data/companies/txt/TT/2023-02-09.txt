TRANE TECHNOLOGIES INCREASES ANNUAL DIVIDEND BY 12 PERCENT. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TRANE TECHNOLOGIES PLC (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL INCREASE ITS DIVIDEND BY 12%, FROM $0.67 TO $0.75 PER SHARE QUARTERLY AND FROM $2.68 TO $3.00 PER SHARE ANNUALIZED. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 31, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MARCH 3, 2023.
