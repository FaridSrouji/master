TRANE TECHNOLOGIES AND NEXII JOIN FORCES TO CREATE SUSTAINABLE HIGH-PERFORMANCE BUILDINGS OF THE FUTURE. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRANE® BY TRANE TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, AND NEXII BUILDING SOLUTIONS INC. (NEXII) ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE JOINED FORCES TO CREATE SUSTAINABLE COMMERCIAL BUILDINGS WITH SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCED CARBON FOOTPRINTS. THE COLLABORATION COMBINES NEXII'S EXPERTISE IN HIGH-PERFORMANCE AND EFFICIENT BUILDING CONSTRUCTION WITH TRANE'S DIGITALLY ENABLED ECOWISE HIGH-EFFICIENCY CLIMATE COMFORT SYSTEMS TO REDUCE ENERGY USAGE AND CARBON EMISSIONS.
