TRANE TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS RECORD FIRST-QUARTER 2022 RESULTS, UPDATES 2022 GUIDANCE. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRANE TECHNOLOGIES PLC (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, TODAY REPORTED DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $1.13 FOR THE FIRST-QUARTER OF 2022. ADJUSTED CONTINUING EPS WAS $1.12, UP 11 PERCENT, WHICH EXCLUDES A NET GAIN OF $3.3 MILLION RELATED PRIMARILY TO NON-CASH ADJUSTMENTS, TRANSFORMATION AND OTHER RESTRUCTURING COSTS.
