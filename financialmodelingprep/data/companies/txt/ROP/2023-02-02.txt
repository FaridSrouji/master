ADERANT BOLSTERS STRENGTH OF EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM. ATLANTA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ADERANT ®, A LEADING GLOBAL BUSINESS MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE PROVIDER FOR LAW FIRMS, HAS STRENGTHENED ITS EXECUTIVE FIREPOWER WITH TWO NEW C-LEVEL HIRES, DOUG MATTHEWS AS CHIEF PRODUCT OFFICER (CPO) AND ANDREW HOYT AS CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER (CTO). IN ADDITION, THE COMPANY HAS PROMOTED JESSICA KATTMAN TO VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL MARKETING AND COMMUNICATIONS. ADERANT CONTINUES TO MAKE BOLD STRIDES FORWARD TO EXPAND ITS TEAM AND TALENT BASE.
