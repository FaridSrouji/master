ROPER TECHNOLOGIES SCHEDULES FIRST QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS CONFERENCE CALL. SARASOTA, FLA., APRIL 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ROPER TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: ROP) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021, ENDED MARCH 31, 2021, WILL BE RELEASED BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS ON TUESDAY, APRIL 27, 2021. A CONFERENCE CALL TO DISCUSS THESE RESULTS HAS BEEN SCHEDULED FOR 7:30 AM ET ON TUESDAY, APRIL 27, 2021. THE CALL CAN BE ACCESSED VIA WEBCAST OR BY DIALING +1 844-750-4898 (US/CANADA) OR +1 412-317-5294, USING ROPER TECHNOLOGIES AS THE CONFERENCE REFERENCE. WEBCAST INFORMATION AND CONFERENCE CALL MATERIALS WILL BE MADE AVAILABLE IN THE INVESTORS SECTION OF ROPER'S WEBSITE PRIOR TO THE START OF THE CALL.
