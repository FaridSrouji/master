ROPER TECHNOLOGIES ANNOUNCES DIVIDEND. SARASOTA, FLA., JUNE 10, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ROPER TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: ROP) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A DIVIDEND OF $0.5625 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON JULY 23, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JULY 8, 2021.
