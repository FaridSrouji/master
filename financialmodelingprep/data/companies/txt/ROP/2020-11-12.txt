ROPER TECHNOLOGIES INCREASES DIVIDEND 10% - ITS 28TH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASE. SARASOTA, FLA., NOV. 12, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ROPER TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: ROP) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.5625 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 22, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JANUARY 8, 2021. THIS REPRESENTS AN INCREASE OF 10% OVER THE DIVIDEND PAID IN EACH QUARTER OF 2020, OR AN EXPECTED $0.20 INCREASE ON AN ANNUAL BASIS ($0.05 ON A QUARTERLY BASIS).  THIS IS THE TWENTY-EIGHTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR IN WHICH ROPER HAS INCREASED ITS DIVIDEND.
