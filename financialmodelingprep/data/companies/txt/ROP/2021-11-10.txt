ROPER TECHNOLOGIES INCREASES DIVIDEND 10% - ITS 29TH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASE. SARASOTA, FLA., NOV. 10, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ROPER TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: ROP) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.62 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 24, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JANUARY 10, 2022. THIS REPRESENTS AN INCREASE OF 10% OVER THE DIVIDEND PAID IN EACH QUARTER OF 2021, OR AN EXPECTED $0.23 INCREASE ON AN ANNUAL BASIS ($0.0575 ON A QUARTERLY BASIS).  THIS IS THE TWENTY-NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR IN WHICH ROPER HAS INCREASED ITS DIVIDEND.
