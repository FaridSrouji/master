THOMA BRAVO ENTERS INTO AGREEMENT TO SELL FRONTLINE EDUCATION TO ROPER TECHNOLOGIES. SAN FRANCISCO AND MALVERN, PA. , AUG. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THOMA BRAVO, A LEADING SOFTWARE INVESTMENT FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL FRONTLINE EDUCATION ("FRONTLINE"), A LEADING PROVIDER OF ADMINISTRATION SOFTWARE PURPOSE-BUILT FOR EDUCATORS IN K-12, TO ROPER TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: ROP) IN AN ALL-CASH TRANSACTION THAT VALUES FRONTLINE AT APPROXIMATELY $3.725 BILLION.
