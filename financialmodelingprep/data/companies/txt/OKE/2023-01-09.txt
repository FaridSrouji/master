ONEOK REACHES AGREEMENT ON MEDFORD NGL FACILITY INSURANCE CLAIM FOR $930 MILLION. ANNOUNCES PLANS TO BUILD NGL FRACTIONATOR IN MONT BELVIEU PRESERVING MEDFORD EQUIPMENT FOR POTENTIAL FUTURE USE TULSA, OKLA. , JAN. 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ONEOK, INC. (NYSE: OKE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS REACHED AN AGREEMENT WITH ITS INSURERS TO SETTLE ALL CLAIMS FOR PHYSICAL DAMAGE AND BUSINESS INTERRUPTION RELATED TO THE MEDFORD INCIDENT THAT OCCURRED IN JULY 2022.
