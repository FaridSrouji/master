ONEOK SCHEDULES 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS; SETS RECORD DATE. TULSA, OKLA., MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ONEOK, INC. (NYSE: OKE) WILL HOLD ITS 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS AS A VIRTUAL MEETING ONLY AS THE COMPANY CONTINUES TO TAKE PRECAUTIONS RELATED TO COVID-19.
