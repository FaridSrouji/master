ONEOK ANNOUNCES $500 MILLION DECREASE IN 2020 CAPITAL SPENDING. ONEOK ANNOUNCES $500 MILLION DECREASE IN 2020 CAPITAL SPENDING.ONEOK INC - A DECREASE IN ITS 2020 GROWTH CAPITAL GUIDANCE DUE TO CURRENT COMMODITY PRICE ENVIRONMENT.ONEOK INC - NOW EXPECTS FY CAPITAL-GROWTH EXPENDITURES IN RANGE OF $1.60 BILLION TO $2.40 BILLION.ONEOK INC - UPDATED RANGE PROVIDES ONEOK WITH FLEXIBILITY TO ADJUST ACCORDINGLY BASED ON EXPECTED PRODUCER ACTIVITY.ONEOK INC - EXPECT 2020 RESULTS TO BE WITHIN PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED GUIDANCE RANGES.ONEOK INC - WORKING WITH PRODUCERS ON ANY UPDATES TO THEIR DRILLING PLANS.ONEOK - FINANCIAL FLEXIBILITY, SIGNIFICANT DIVIDEND COVERAGE POSITION ONEOK WELL TO WEATHER CHALLENGING MARKET CONDITIONS.ONEOK INC - SUSPENDING 100,000 BARREL PER DAY ADDITIONAL EXPANSION OF WEST TEXAS LPG PIPELINE IN PERMIAN BASIN PROJECT.ONEOK INC - SUSPENDING 200 MILLION CUBIC FEET PER DAY (MMCF/D) EXPANSION OF DEMICKS LAKE NATURAL GAS PROCESSING FACILITY.ONEOK - SCOPE OF ELK CREEK PIPELINE EXPANSION WILL BE REDUCED.
