TRUIST DECLARES COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , OCT. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.52 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON DEC. 1, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOV. 10, 2022.
