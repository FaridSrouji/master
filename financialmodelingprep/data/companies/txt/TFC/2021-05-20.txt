TRUIST ADDS INDUSTRY SPECIALTIES TO GROWING COMMERCIAL BANKING TEAM. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE EXPANSION OF THE TRUIST COMMERCIAL COMMUNITY BANK'S INDUSTRY CONSULTING TEAM, ADDING WASTE AND ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES AND BUILDING PRODUCTS TO ITS GROWING LIST OF INDUSTRY SPECIALTIES.
