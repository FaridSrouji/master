TRUIST RECEIVES "OUTSTANDING" COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT ACT RATING. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST RECEIVED THE HIGHEST POSSIBLE OVERALL RATING OF "OUTSTANDING" FROM THE FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION FOR ITS MOST RECENT COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT ACT (CRA) EXAMINATION PERIOD.
