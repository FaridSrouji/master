TRUIST ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER DARYL BIBLE. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , MAY 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER DARYL BIBLE HAS MADE THE DECISION TO RETIRE FROM TRUIST AFTER A DISTINGUISHED 38-YEAR CAREER AND MORE THAN 14 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY.
