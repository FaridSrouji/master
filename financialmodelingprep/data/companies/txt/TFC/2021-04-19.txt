TRUIST ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF FLOATING RATE SENIOR NOTES DUE JUNE 2021. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL REDEEM ALL $250,000,000 PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OUTSTANDING OF ITS FLOATING RATE SENIOR NOTES DUE JUNE 1, 2021 (CUSIP 05531FBC6) ON THE REDEMPTION DATE OF MAY 1, 2021.
