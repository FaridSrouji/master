TRUIST ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF CALLABLE FIXED RATE NOTES DUE NOVEMBER 2026. CHARLOTTE, N.C., SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL REDEEM ALL $17,200,000 PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OUTSTANDING OF ITS CALLABLE FIXED RATE NOTES DUE NOV. 2, 2026 (CUSIP 86802WBC1) ON THE REDEMPTION DATE OF OCT. 31, 2021.
