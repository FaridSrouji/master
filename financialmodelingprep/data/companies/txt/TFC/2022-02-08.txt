TRUIST INSURANCE HOLDINGS TO ACQUIRE KENSINGTON VANGUARD NATIONAL LAND SERVICES. CHARLOTTE, N.C., FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST INSURANCE HOLDINGS, INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION AND THE SIXTH-LARGEST INSURANCE BROKERAGE IN THE U.S., ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE KENSINGTON VANGUARD NATIONAL LAND SERVICES, ONE OF THE COUNTRY'S LARGEST INDEPENDENT FULL-SERVICE NATIONAL TITLE INSURANCE AGENCIES.
