TRUIST FOUNDATION PARTNERS WITH CONNECT HUMANITY TO ADVANCE THE INTERNET CONNECTIVITY SPACE AND ESTABLISH DIGITAL EQUITY. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FOUNDATION ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS AWARDED A $10 MILLION GRANT TO CONNECT HUMANITY, A NONPROFIT FOCUSED ON ADVANCING DIGITAL EQUITY AMONG HISTORICALLY MARGINALIZED COMMUNITIES.
