TRUIST DECLARES COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , JAN. 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.52 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON MAR. 1, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEB. 10, 2023.
