TRUIST NAMES DOMINICA GROOM WILLIAMS HEAD OF DEI. INDUSTRY VETERAN TAPPED TO LEAD BANK-WIDE STRATEGIC INCLUSION AND DIVERSITY INITIATIVES CHARLOTTE, N.C. , JULY 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DOMINICA GROOM WILLIAMS WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS HEAD OF DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION (DEI), EFFECTIVE TODAY.
