TRUIST DECLARES COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.48 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON MAR. 1, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEB. 11, 2022.
