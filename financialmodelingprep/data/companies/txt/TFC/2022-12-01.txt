TRUIST AND TRUIST FOUNDATION ANNOUNCE $4.5 MILLION IN GRANTS TO STRENGTHEN TEXAS' RACIALLY DIVERSE SMALL BUSINESS ECOSYSTEM. HOUSTON , DEC. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED A $3.5 MILLION GRANT TO PEOPLEFUND, A TEXAS-BASED COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT FINANCIAL INSTITUTION (CDFI) AND NONPROFIT 501(C)(3) THAT STRIVES TO CREATE ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY AND FINANCIAL STABILITY FOR UNDERSERVED PEOPLE BY PROVIDING ACCESS TO CAPITAL, EDUCATION AND OTHER RESOURCES TO BUILD HEALTHY SMALL BUSINESSES. TRUIST FOUNDATION'S GRANT PROVIDES LONG-TERM SUSTAINABILITY FOR PEOPLEFUND'S BLACK, INDIGENOUS, PERSON OF COLOR (BIPOC) SMALL BUSINESS ACCELERATOR, A PROGRAM THAT PROVIDES BUSINESS OWNERS THE CAPITAL, RESOURCES AND NETWORK TO START AND GROW THEIR BUSINESSES AND ATTAIN ECONOMIC MOBILITY.
