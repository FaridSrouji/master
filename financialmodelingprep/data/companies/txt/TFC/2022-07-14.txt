TRUIST FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES $1.25 MILLION GRANT TO NASHVILLE BUSINESS INCUBATION CENTER. GRANT PROVIDES WRAPAROUND SUPPORT SERVICES FOR LOCAL WOMEN ENTREPRENEURS THROUGH TOOLS, TRAINING, MENTORSHIPS AND RESULTS-DRIVEN CURRICULUM NASHVILLE, TENN. , JULY 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED A $1.25 MILLION COMMITMENT TO THE NASHVILLE BUSINESS INCUBATION CENTER (NBIC), A NONPROFIT FOCUSED ON CULTIVATING THE GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT OF LOCAL WOMEN-, RACIALLY AND ETHNICALLY DIVERSE-, AND VETERAN-OWNED SMALL BUSINESSES.
