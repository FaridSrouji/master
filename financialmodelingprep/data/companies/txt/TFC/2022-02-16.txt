TRUIST FOUNDATION LAUNCHES INSPIRE AWARDS IN PARTNERSHIP WITH MIT SOLVE TO SUPPORT NONPROFITS HELPING BIPOC AND WOMEN-OWNED SMALL BUSINESSES THRIVE. CHARLOTTE, N.C., FEB. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS INSPIRE AWARDS, A PITCH COMPETITION OPEN TO NONPROFITS SUPPORTING BLACK, INDIGENOUS, AND PEOPLE OF COLOR (BIPOC) AND WOMEN-OWNED SMALL BUSINESSES TO PLAN, MARKET, AND GROW THEIR BUSINESSES.
