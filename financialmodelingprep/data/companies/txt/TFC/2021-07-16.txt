TRUIST BANK ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF SENIOR BANK NOTES DUE AUGUST 2022. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JULY 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST BANK TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL REDEEM ALL $500,000,000 PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OUTSTANDING OF ITS 3.502% FIXED-TO-FLOATING RATE SENIOR BANK NOTES DUE AUG. 2, 2022 (CUSIP 86787EAX5) AND ALL $300,000,000 PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OUTSTANDING OF ITS FLOATING RATE SENIOR BANK NOTES DUE AUG. 2, 2022 (CUSIP 86787EAW7) ON THE REDEMPTION DATE OF AUG. 2, 2021.
