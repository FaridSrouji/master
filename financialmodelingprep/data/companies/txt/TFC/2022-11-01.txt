TRUIST UPDATES PRESENTATION TIME AT THE BANCANALYSTS ASSOCIATION OF BOSTON CONFERENCE. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , NOV. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER MIKE MAGUIRE AND CHIEF INSURANCE OFFICER JOHN HOWARD WILL PRESENT AT THE BANCANALYSTS ASSOCIATION OF BOSTON CONFERENCE ON FRIDAY, NOV. 4, 2022, AT 10:35 A.M.
