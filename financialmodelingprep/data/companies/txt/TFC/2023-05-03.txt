MORE THAN HALF OF BUSINESS OWNERS FEEL THAT BUSINESS CONDITIONS HAVE IMPROVED IN THE LAST YEAR, DESPITE ECONOMIC UNCERTAINTY, ACCORDING TO A TRUIST SURVEY. TRUIST'S SMALL BUSINESS PULSE SURVEY INDICATES THAT WHILE SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS CONTINUE TO NAVIGATE INFLATIONARY CONCERNS, THEY ARE FINDING NEW WAYS TO GROW THEIR BUSINESS THROUGH INVESTMENTS IN EMPLOYEES, TECHNOLOGY, AND SALES PRACTICES CHARLOTTE, N.C. , MAY 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHILE SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS CONTINUE TO BE CONCERNED ABOUT INFLATION, THEY FEEL THE FUTURE IS BRIGHT AND THEY ARE MAKING SMART INVESTMENTS IN THEIR COMPANIES, EMPLOYEES, AND THEMSELVES, ACCORDING TO TRUIST'S ANNUAL SMALL BUSINESS PULSE SURVEY  1.
