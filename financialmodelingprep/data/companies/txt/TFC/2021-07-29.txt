TRUIST HIGHLIGHTS ESG AND DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION PROGRESS IN 2020 CSR AND ESG REPORT. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) TODAY RELEASED ITS 2020 CSR AND ESG REPORT, HIGHLIGHTING THE COMPANY'S MEANINGFUL PROGRESS ON ESG PERFORMANCE AND DISCLOSURE AND A CONTINUED, INTENSE FOCUS ON COMMUNITY INVOLVEMENT AND FINANCIAL INCLUSION AS THE COMPANY CONTINUES TO HELP PEOPLE AND COMPANIES EMERGE FROM THE IMPACTS OF THE GLOBAL COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
