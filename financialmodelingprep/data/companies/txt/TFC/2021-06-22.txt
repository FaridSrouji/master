TRUIST ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF CALLABLE FIXED RATE NOTES DUE OCTOBER 2022. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL REDEEM ALL $9,436,000 PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OUTSTANDING OF ITS 2.60% CALLABLE NOTES DUE OCT. 30, 2022 (CUSIP 86802WBD9) ON THE REDEMPTION DATE OF JULY 30, 2021.
