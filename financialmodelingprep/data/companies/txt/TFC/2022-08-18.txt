TRUIST COMMITS $1.45 MILLION TO JUNIOR ACHIEVEMENT OF GREATER WASHINGTON IN SUPPORT OF FINANCIAL LITERACY. FUNDING WILL SUPPORT PROGRAMMING ACROSS FAIRFAX AND PRINCE GEORGE'S COUNTIES CHARLOTTE, N.C. , AUG. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: TFC), TOGETHER WITH TRUIST FOUNDATION AND TRUIST CHARITABLE FUND, TODAY ANNOUNCED A $1.45 MILLION COMMITMENT TO JUNIOR ACHIEVEMENT OF GREATER WASHINGTON (JAGW).
