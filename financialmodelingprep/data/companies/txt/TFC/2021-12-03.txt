TRUIST BANK ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF FIXED RATE SENIOR NOTES DUE JANUARY 2022. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , DEC. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST BANK TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL REDEEM ALL $1,000,000,000 PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OUTSTANDING OF ITS FIXED RATE SENIOR NOTES DUE JAN. 15, 2022 (CUSIP 07330NAQ8) ON THE REDEMPTION DATE OF DEC. 15, 2021.
