SHEFFIELD FINANCIAL AND SUZUKI MARINE USA LLC ANNOUNCE A RETAIL FINANCING PROGRAM AGREEMENT. WINSTON-SALEM, N.C., SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SHEFFIELD FINANCIAL, A DIVISION OF TRUIST BANK, MEMBER FDIC, AND SUZUKI MARINE USA LLC (SUZUKI MARINE), RECENTLY AGREED TO A FINANCING SERVICES PROGRAM AGREEMENT.
