FACEBOOK, INC. COMMITS $2 MILLION DONATION TO MCBRIDE SISTERS COLLECTION'S SHE CAN FUND. OAKLAND, CALIF., SEPT. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCBRIDE SISTERS COLLECTION, INC., THE LARGEST BLACK-OWNED WINE COMPANY IN THE U.S., TODAY ANNOUNCES THAT IT HAS RECEIVED A $2 MILLION DONATION FROM FACEBOOK, INC. (NASDAQ: FB) FOR ITS 501 (C)(3), MCBRIDE SISTERS SHE CAN FUND.
