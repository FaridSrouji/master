DUKE REALTY ANNOUNCES PRICING TERMS FOR $450 MILLION SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES OFFERING. INDIANAPOLIS, JAN. 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DUKE REALTY CORPORATION (NYSE: DRE), A LEADING INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY REIT, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS OPERATING PARTNERSHIP, DUKE REALTY LIMITED PARTNERSHIP (THE “OPERATING PARTNERSHIP”), HAS PRICED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF $450 MILLION OF ITS 1.75% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE FEBRUARY 1, 2031. THE NOTES WERE PRICED AT 99.252% OF THEIR FACE AMOUNT TO YIELD 1.832% TO MATURITY. THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON JANUARY 21, 2021, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
