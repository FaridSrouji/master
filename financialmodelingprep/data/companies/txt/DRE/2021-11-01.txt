DUKE REALTY ANNOUNCES PRICING TERMS FOR $500 MILLION SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES OFFERING. INDIANAPOLIS, NOV. 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DUKE REALTY CORPORATION (NYSE: DRE), A LEADING INDUSTRIAL PROPERTY REIT, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS OPERATING PARTNERSHIP, DUKE REALTY LIMITED PARTNERSHIP (THE “OPERATING PARTNERSHIP”), HAS PRICED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF $500 MILLION OF ITS 2.250% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE JANUARY 15, 2032. THE NOTES WERE PRICED AT 98.823% OF THEIR FACE AMOUNT TO YIELD 2.381% TO MATURITY. THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON NOVEMBER 10, 2021, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
