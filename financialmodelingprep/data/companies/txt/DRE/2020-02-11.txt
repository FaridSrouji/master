DUKE REALTY ANNOUNCES PRICING TERMS FOR $325 MLN SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES OFFERING. DUKE REALTY ANNOUNCES PRICING TERMS FOR $325 MILLION SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES OFFERING.PRICED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF $325 MILLION OF ITS 3.05% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE MARCH 1, 2050.NOTES WERE PRICED AT 97.345% OF THEIR FACE AMOUNT TO YIELD 3.188% TO MATURITY.
