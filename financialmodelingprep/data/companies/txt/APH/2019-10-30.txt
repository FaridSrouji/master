AMPHENOL CORPORATION APPROVES DIVIDEND FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2019, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 8, 2020. AMPHENOL CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED THE FOURTH QUARTER 2019 DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK IN THE AMOUNT OF $0.25 PER SHARE AT ITS MEETING HELD ON OCTOBER 30, 2019. THE COMPANY WILL PAY THIS FOURTH QUARTER 2019 DIVIDEND ON JANUARY 8, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 17, 2019.
