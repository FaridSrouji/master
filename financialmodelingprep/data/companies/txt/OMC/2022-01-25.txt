KETCHUM DOUBLES DOWN ON BOOMING INFLUENCER MARKETING SPECIALTY WITH DYNAMIC DUO SENIOR APPOINTMENTS. NEW YORK, JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING GLOBAL COMMUNICATIONS CONSULTANCY KETCHUM TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ADDITION OF TWO MAJOR PLAYERS TO ITS HIGH-PERFORMING INFLUENCER MARKETING TEAM. JENNY HEINRICH JOINS KETCHUM AS EVP, INFLUENCER MARKETING, AND HEATHER GREEN HAS BEEN APPOINTED EVP, INFLUENCER MARKETING AND PERFORMANCE.
