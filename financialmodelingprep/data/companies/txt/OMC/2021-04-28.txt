OMNICOM PRICES $800 MILLION SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. NEW YORK, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS PUBLIC OFFERING OF $800 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.600% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE "NOTES"). THE NOTES WILL MATURE ON AUGUST 1, 2031.
