NANCY REYES ELEVATED TO CEO OF TBWA\CHIAT\DAY NEW YORK. NEW YORK, APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TBWA TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT NANCY REYES IS BEING ELEVATED TO CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF TBWA\CHIAT\DAY NEW YORK. WITH THIS PROMOTION, REYES EXPANDS HER ROLE AND TAKES OVER FULL LEADERSHIP OF THE AGENCY FROM ROB SCHWARTZ, WHO HAS BEEN CEO SINCE 2015.
