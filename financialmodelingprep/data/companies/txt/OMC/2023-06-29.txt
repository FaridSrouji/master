DOREMUS+CO NAMES SARAH LENT AS PRESIDENT. NEW YORK , JUNE 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SARAH LENT HAS JOINED DOREMUS+CO, A GLOBAL LEADER IN B2B MARKETING AND COMMUNICATIONS, AS PRESIDENT. SHE FILLS A ROLE THAT HAD PREVIOUSLY BEEN HANDLED BY PAUL HIRSCH, WHO HAS BEEN ELEVATED TO CEO AND RETAINS THE TITLE OF CHIEF CREATIVE OFFICER.
