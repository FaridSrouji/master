OMNICOM NAMES GREGORY LUNDBERG SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF INVESTOR RELATIONS. NEW YORK, AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED GREGORY LUNDBERG WILL JOIN THE GROUP AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF INVESTOR RELATIONS. IN THIS ROLE, LUNDBERG WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR MANAGING ALL OMNICOM INVESTOR RELATIONS ACTIVITIES WITH THE FINANCIAL AND INVESTMENT COMMUNITIES.
