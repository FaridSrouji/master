OMNICOM GROUP APPOINTS PATRICIA SALAS PINEDA AND MARK D. GERSTEIN TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW YORK, FEB. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED TWO NEW MEMBERS OF ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. PATRICIA SALAS PINEDA HAS BEEN APPOINTED AN INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR AND MEMBER OF THE GOVERNANCE COMMITTEE, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.
