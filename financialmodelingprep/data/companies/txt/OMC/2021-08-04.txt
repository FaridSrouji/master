OMNICOM GROUP ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENTS AT THE DAS GROUP OF COMPANIES (DAS). NEW YORK, AUG. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED TWO LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENTS AT THE DAS GROUP OF COMPANIES (DAS). MICHAEL LARSON HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO CEO OF DAS AND JOHN DOOLITTLE HAS BEEN NAMED CEO OF THE NEWLY FORMED COMMUNICATIONS CONSULTANCY NETWORK (CCN).
