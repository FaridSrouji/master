OMNICOM PRICES £325 MILLION SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. NEW YORK, NOV. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) AND OMNICOM CAPITAL HOLDINGS PLC (THE "ISSUER"), A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF OMNICOM GROUP INC. ("OMNICOM GROUP"), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF THE ISSUER'S PUBLIC OFFERING OF £325 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.250% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2033 (THE "NOTES"). THE NOTES WILL MATURE ON NOVEMBER 22, 2033.
