KETCHUM DEEPENS BENCH IN RAPIDLY EXPANDING AREAS OF HEALTH, MEDIA; ANNOUNCES KEY SENIOR-LEVEL PROMOTIONS. NEW YORK , APRIL 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING GLOBAL COMMUNICATIONS CONSULTANCY KETCHUM TODAY ANNOUNCED TWO APPOINTMENTS OF TOP INDUSTRY TALENT AND THREE RECENT PROMOTIONS OF KEY SENIOR LEADERS IN AREAS OF HIGH GROWTH THAT ARE SEEING SIGNIFICANT CLIENT DEMAND, INCLUDING HEALTH SERVICES; MEDIA; RETAIL; FINANCIAL & PROFESSIONAL SERVICES; AUTOMOTIVE, ENERGY & MANUFACTURING; AND TRAVEL, HOSPITALITY & LEISURE. NEW SENIOR HIRES TO LEAD KEY INDUSTRIES AND SPECIALTIES INCLUDE: JEN FUHRMAN-KESTLER, WHO JOINED KETCHUM IN JANUARY AS EVP AND MANAGING DIRECTOR, HEALTH SERVICES.
