KETCHUM APPOINTS CONOR NASH AS GLOBAL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER. NEW YORK , JAN. 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING GLOBAL COMMUNICATIONS CONSULTANCY KETCHUM HAS ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CONOR NASH AS GLOBAL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER. WITHIN HIS NEW ROLE, NASH WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR DRIVING GROWTH FOR THE FIRM, BOTH OPERATIONALLY AND FINANCIALLY.
