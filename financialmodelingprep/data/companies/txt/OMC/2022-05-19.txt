KETCHUM ANNOUNCES KEY GLOBAL LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENTS. NEW YORK , MAY 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING COMMUNICATIONS CONSULTANCY KETCHUM TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ELEVATION OF THREE SENIOR LEADERS TO EXPANDED GLOBAL LEADERSHIP ROLES: JO-ANN ROBERTSON, CEO, UK, ADDS THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT, GLOBAL MARKETS TO HER RESPONSIBILITIES; KELLY SAUTER, PREVIOUSLY SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND DIRECTOR OF BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT, ASSUMES THE ROLE OF EXECUTIVE VICE PRESENT AND MANAGING DIRECTOR, GLOBAL GROWTH; AND MICHAEL O'BRIEN, PREVIOUSLY PRESIDENT OF CLIENT EXPERIENCE, ASSUMES THE ROLE OF CHIEF STRATEGY OFFICER. ALL APPOINTMENTS ARE EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.
