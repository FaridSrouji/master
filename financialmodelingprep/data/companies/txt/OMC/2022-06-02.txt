OMNICOM NAMES MATT MCNALLY CEO OF OMNICOM HEALTH GROUP. NEW YORK , JUNE 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF MATT MCNALLY AS CEO OF OMNICOM HEALTH GROUP (OHG), THE LARGEST HEALTHCARE MARKETING AND COMMUNICATIONS NETWORK IN THE WORLD AND PART OF OMNICOM'S DAS GROUP OF COMPANIES (DAS). HE SUCCEEDS ED WISE, WHO HELD THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE ROLE SINCE OHG'S FORMATION IN 2016 AND RECENTLY ANNOUNCED HIS RETIREMENT.
