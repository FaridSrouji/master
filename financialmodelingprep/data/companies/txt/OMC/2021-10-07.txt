BEN WILLIAMS JOINS TBWA\WORLDWIDE AS CHIEF CREATIVE EXPERIENCE OFFICER. NEW YORK, OCT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TBWA\WORLDWIDE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF BEN WILLIAMS AS GLOBAL CHIEF CREATIVE EXPERIENCE OFFICER. WILLIAMS JOINS THE TBWA COLLECTIVE TO SET THE VISION FOR TBWA'S GLOBAL CREATIVE PRODUCT, UNLOCKING THE POWER OF CREATIVITY TO PUSH THE BOUNDARIES OF BRAND EXPERIENCE.
