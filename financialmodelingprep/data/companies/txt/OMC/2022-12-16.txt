TBWA\CHIAT\DAY NEW YORK HIRES GABRIEL CHEUNG AS GLOBAL ECD AND AMANDA DAVIS AS HEAD OF CONTENT. NEW YORK , DEC. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TBWA\CHIAT\DAY NEW YORK TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF TWO STRATEGIC HIRES TO FURTHER EXPAND THE AGENCY'S CREATIVE CHOPS, GABRIEL CHEUNG AND AMANDA DAVIS. BOTH HIRES SIGNAL TBWA'S COMMITMENT TO FURTHER OPENING THE APERTURE OF CREATIVITY, WITH A FOCUS ON BUILDING DISRUPTIVE BRAND EXPERIENCES FOR ITS CLIENTS.
