OMNICOM GROUP INC. DECLARES DIVIDEND. NEW YORK , JULY 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 70 CENTS PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 12, 2022 TO OMNICOM GROUP COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 21, 2022.
