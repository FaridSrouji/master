OMNICOM APPOINTS BRIAN CLAYTON AS CHIEF DATA PRIVACY OFFICER. NEW YORK , MAY 24, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF BRIAN CLAYTON, CHIEF DATA PRIVACY OFFICER. IN THIS NEW ROLE, CLAYTON WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR DRIVING OMNICOM'S PRIVACY FIRST APPROACH WHILE ENSURING BRANDS AND THE COMPANY COMPLY WITH THE INCREASINGLY COMPLEX DATA PRIVACY LAWS AND REGULATIONS AROUND THE GLOBE.
