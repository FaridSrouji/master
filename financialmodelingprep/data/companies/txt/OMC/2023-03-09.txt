TBWA HIRES BEN MYERS AS FIRST NORTH AMERICAN CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER. NEW YORK , MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TBWA HAS TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF BEN MYERS AS NORTH AMERICAN CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER, RESPONSIBLE FOR DRIVING CONTINUED GROWTH ACROSS ITS OFFICES IN THE US AND CANADA. FROM EAST COAST TO WEST, AS THE KEY NEW BUSINESS LEAD MYERS WILL PARTNER CLOSELY WITH CEOS NANCY REYES AND ERIN RILEY IN THE US, ALONGSIDE EVE RÉMILLARD-LAROSE IN CANADA.
