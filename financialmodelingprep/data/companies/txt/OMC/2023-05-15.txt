MICHAEL SOLOMON NAMED CEO OF PHD USA. NEW YORK , MAY 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM MEDIA GROUP (OMG), THE MEDIA SERVICES DIVISION OF OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) HAS NAMED MICHAEL SOLOMON CEO OF PHD USA. SOLOMON, WHO TAKES ON THE NEW ROLE FOLLOWING THREE YEARS AS THE AGENCY'S COO, SUCCEEDS CATHERINE SULLIVAN, THE FORMER NETWORK AD SALES EXECUTIVE WHO HAS HELD THE TITLE SINCE 2020, AND IS STEPPING DOWN TO RETURN TO THE SALES SIDE OF THE BUSINESS.
