OMNICOM SCORES BIG IN USA TODAY'S 34TH ANNUAL AD METER COMPETITION FOR SUPER BOWL LVI ADS. NEW YORK , FEB. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP INC.
