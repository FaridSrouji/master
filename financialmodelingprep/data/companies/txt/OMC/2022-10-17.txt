OMNICOM ELEVATES ANDREA LENNON TO CHIEF CLIENT OFFICER. LENNON PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS PRESIDENT OF CRITICAL MASS NEW YORK , OCT. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ANDREA LENNON TO THE NEW ROLE OF CHIEF CLIENT OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. LENNON WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR LEADING OMNICOM'S MULTI-DISCIPLINARY SOLUTIONS FOR ENTERPRISE CLIENTS, INCLUDING THE COMPANY'S GLOBAL CLIENT LEADERS (GCL) GROUP THAT MANAGES AND ORCHESTRATES CLIENT SOLUTIONS ACROSS OMNICOM'S LEADING AGENCIES AND PRACTICE AREAS.
