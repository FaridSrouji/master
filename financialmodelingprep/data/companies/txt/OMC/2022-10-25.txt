KATHLEEN SAXTON JOINS OMNICOM GROUP AS CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER. NEW YORK , OCT. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF KATHLEEN SAXTON AS CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1. IN THIS NEW POSITION, SAXTON WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR MARKETING OMNICOM'S AWARD-WINNING CAPABILITIES AND ITS OMNI-CHANNEL SOLUTIONS FOR GLOBAL ENTERPRISE CLIENTS, BUILDING ON THE COMPANY'S SUCCESS IN THIS AREA.
