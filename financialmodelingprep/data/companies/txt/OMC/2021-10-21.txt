EDDI YANG RETURNS TO KETCHUM AS CHIEF CLIENT AND OPERATING OFFICER FOR MAINLAND CHINA. SHANGHAI, OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING GLOBAL COMMUNICATIONS CONSULTANCY KETCHUM  TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF EDDI YANG AS CHIEF CLIENT AND OPERATING OFFICER, CHINA. YANG, A RESPECTED SENIOR LEADER WITH MORE THAN 20 YEARS OF MARKETING AND COMMUNICATIONS EXPERIENCE, REJOINS KETCHUM WITH RESPONSIBILITY FOR BUSINESS GROWTH, CLIENT RELATIONSHIPS AND TALENT DEVELOPMENT.
