AMY FERGUSON ELEVATED TO CCO OF TBWA\CHIAT\DAY NEW YORK. NEW YORK, DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, TBWA\CHIAT\DAY NEW YORK ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF AMY FERGUSON AS CHIEF CREATIVE OFFICER. FOR FERGUSON, WHO BEGAN HER CAREER AT THE AGENCY, THE ELEVATION IS THE CULMINATION OF A 17-YEAR ASCENT WITHIN THE INDUSTRY TO ASSUME CREATIVE LEADERSHIP OF THE AGENCY'S FULL ROSTER OF CLIENTS, WHICH INCLUDES ABBOTT, HILTON, MAYO CLINIC, MTN DEW, NISSAN, AND TRAVELERS INSURANCE, AMONG OTHERS.
