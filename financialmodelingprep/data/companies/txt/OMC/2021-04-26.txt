FLEISHMANHILLARD HIRES INCLUSION INNOVATOR, ADRIANNE C. SMITH, TO LEAD THE AGENCY'S DE&I STRATEGY AS CHIEF DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION OFFICER. ST. LOUIS, APRIL 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FLEISHMANHILLARD TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ADRIANNE C. SMITH (SHE/HER) TO CHIEF DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION OFFICER, FROM WPP WHERE SHE PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS ITS FIRST GLOBAL DIRECTOR OF INCLUSION AND DIVERSITY.
