OMD WORLDWIDE APPOINTS GEORGE MANAS AS CEO. NEW YORK, NOV. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM MEDIA GROUP'S OMD WORLDWIDE TODAY ANNOUNCED GEORGE MANAS AS ITS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MANAS SUCCEEDS FLORIAN ADAMSKI, WHO WAS ELEVATED TO CEO OF OMNICOM MEDIA GROUP.
