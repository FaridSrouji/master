FLEISHMANHILLARD UNVEILS CULTURE UNIT TO KEEP PULSE ON GLOBAL CONSUMER BEHAVIORS AND INSIGHTS. ST. LOUIS, FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FLEISHMANHILLARD TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE CULTURE UNIT, A TEAM WITHIN THE BRAND MARKETING PRACTICE DEDICATED TO ANALYZING CONSUMER BEHAVIORAL TRENDS AND INSIGHTS. THE INFORMATION IS THEN USED TO COUNSEL CLIENTS ON HOW TO POSITION A BRAND, PRODUCT OR ORGANIZATION IN RELATION TO ANY DEFINED PROFILES.
