OMNICOM NAMES CHRIS FOSTER CEO OF OMNICOM PUBLIC RELATIONS GROUP. NEW YORK, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CHRIS FOSTER AS CEO OF OMNICOM PUBLIC RELATIONS GROUP (OPRG) EFFECTIVE JUNE 28. FOSTER WILL OVERSEE THE PUBLIC RELATIONS PORTFOLIO WITHIN OMNICOM, FOCUSING ON TALENT, INNOVATION AND CROSS-AGENCY COLLABORATION TO DRIVE GROWTH.
THE INTEGER GROUP® LAUNCHES GROWTH SCIENCES PRACTICE FUELED BY PROPRIETARY INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM.
