OMNICOM HEALTH GROUP ACQUIRES ARCHBOW CONSULTING. NEW YORK, APRIL 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM HEALTH GROUP, THE LARGEST HEALTHCARE MARKETING AND COMMUNICATIONS GROUP IN THE WORLD, AND PART OF OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED ARCHBOW CONSULTING. ESTABLISHED IN 2017 BY INDUSTRY VETERANS DOUGLAS BOCK, KEVIN CAST, AND DEWAYNE MANNING, ARCHBOW HELPS PHARMACEUTICAL AND BIOTECH COMPANIES DESIGN, BUILD, AND OPTIMIZE MARKET ACCESS OPERATIONS, PRODUCT DISTRIBUTION, AND PATIENT ACCESS HUBS.
