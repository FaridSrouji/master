OMD'S GUY MARKS NAMED CEO OF OMNICOM MEDIA GROUP EMEA. NEW YORK, JAN. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM MEDIA GROUP, THE MEDIA SERVICES DIVISION OF OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) HAS NAMED GUY MARKS CEO OF OMNICOM MEDIA GROUP EMEA.  IN HIS NEW ROLE, MARKS, PREVIOUSLY LEADER OF OMD EMEA, WILL OVERSEE STRATEGY, OPERATIONS AND THE DEVELOPMENT AND ADOPTION OF TRANSFORMATIVE CAPABILITIES TO SUPPORT OMNICOM MEDIA GROUP (OMG) AGENCIES - INCLUDING OMD, PHD, HEARTS & SCIENCE, AND ITS ANNALECT DATA AND ANALYTICS DIVISION - ACROSS EMEA.
