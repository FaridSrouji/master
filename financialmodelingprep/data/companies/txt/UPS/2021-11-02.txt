UPS AIMS TO HIRE 60,000 AT ‘UPS BROWN FRIDAY' EVENTS NATIONWIDE. ATLANTA, NOV. 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- UPS (NYSE: UPS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT EXPECTS TO HIRE AT LEAST 60,000 SEASONAL EMPLOYEES THIS THURSDAY THROUGH SATURDAY, IN ITS ANNUAL ‘UPS BROWN FRIDAY' EVENTS AT COMPANY LOCATIONS ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
