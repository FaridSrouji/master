UPS INVESTS MILLIONS IN ADVANCED TRAINING FOR ON-THE-ROAD DRIVERS. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UPS (NYSE:UPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED ADDITIONAL INVESTMENTS IN SAFE DRIVING EDUCATION AND TRAINING, INCLUDING THE ROLLOUT OF 20 ADDITIONAL DRIVING AND DELIVERY SIMULATORS ACROSS THE COUNTRY, NEARLY DOUBLING THE NUMBER OF DRIVING SIMULATORS IN UPS FACILITIES SINCE FIRST LAUNCHING IN 2020.
