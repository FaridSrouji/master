UPS RELEASES 3Q 2022 EARNINGS. ATLANTA, OCT. 25, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- UPS (NYSE:UPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THIRD-QUARTER 2022 CONSOLIDATED REVENUES OF $24.2 BILLION, A 4.2% INCREASE OVER THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021. CONSOLIDATED OPERATING PROFIT WAS $3.1 BILLION, UP 7.5% COMPARED TO THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021, AND UP 6.0% ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $2.96 FOR THE QUARTER; ADJUSTED DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $2.99 WERE 10.3% ABOVE THE SAME PERIOD IN 2021.
