UPS AIMS TO SIGN UP 50,000 AT ‘UPS BROWN FRIDAY' HIRING EVENTS NATIONWIDE. ATLANTA, OCT. 27, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- UPS (NYSE: UPS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT EXPECTS TO SIGN-UP AT LEAST 50,000 SEASONAL EMPLOYEES THIS FRIDAY, OCTOBER 30, IN ITS ANNUAL ONE-DAY ‘UPS BROWN FRIDAY' HIRING BLITZ AT COMPANY LOCATIONS ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
