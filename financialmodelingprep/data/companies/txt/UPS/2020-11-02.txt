UPS BOARD ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA, NOV. 02, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE UPS (NYSE: UPS) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.01 PER SHARE ON ALL OUTSTANDING CLASS A AND CLASS B SHARES.
