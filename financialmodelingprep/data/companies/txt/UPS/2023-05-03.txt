UPS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UPS (NYSE: UPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.62 PER SHARE ON ALL OUTSTANDING CLASS A AND CLASS B SHARES. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 1, 2023, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD ON MAY 15, 2023. COMMITMENT TO THE DIVIDEND IS ONE OF UPS'S CORE PRINCIPLES AND A HALLMARK OF THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL STRENGTH.
