BETTERINVESTING™ MAGAZINE UPDATE ON UNITED PARCEL SERVICE INC. AND TRUIST FINANCIAL CORP.. TROY, MICH. , APRIL 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE EDITORIAL ADVISORY AND SECURITIES REVIEW COMMITTEE OF BETTERINVESTING MAGAZINE TODAY ANNOUNCED UNITED PARCEL SERVICE INC. (NYSE: UPS) AS ITS "STOCK TO STUDY" AND TRUIST FINANCIAL CORP. (NYSE: TFC) AS ITS "UNDERVALUED STOCK" IN THE JUNE 2023 ISSUE FOR INVESTORS' INFORMATIONAL AND EDUCATIONAL USE.
