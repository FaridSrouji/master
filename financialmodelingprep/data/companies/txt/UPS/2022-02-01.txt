UPS RELEASES 4Q 2021 EARNINGS. ATLANTA, FEB. 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- UPS (NYSE:UPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED FOURTH-QUARTER 2021 CONSOLIDATED REVENUE OF $27.8 BILLION, AN 11.5% INCREASE OVER THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020. CONSOLIDATED OPERATING PROFIT WAS $3.9 BILLION, UP 91.0% COMPARED TO THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020, AND UP 37.7% ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $3.52 FOR THE QUARTER; ADJUSTED DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE 35.0% ABOVE THE SAME PERIOD IN 2020.
