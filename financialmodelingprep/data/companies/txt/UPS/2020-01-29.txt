UNITED PARCEL SERVICE INVESTS IN ARRIVAL. UPS INVESTS IN ARRIVAL, ACCELERATES FLEET ELECTRIFICATION WITH ORDER OF 10,000 ELECTRIC DELIVERY VEHICLES.UPS - BOTH COMPANIES ARE CO-DEVELOPING ELECTRIC DELIVERY VEHICLES FOR UPS.UPS - EQUITY INVESTMENT GIVES UPS EARLY ACCESS TO ARRIVAL’S VEHICLES.UPS - VENTURE CAPITAL ARM, UPS VENTURES, HAS COMPLETED A MINORITY INVESTMENT IN ARRIVAL.UPS - WILL COLLABORATE WITH ARRIVAL TO DEVELOP A WIDE RANGE OF ELECTRIC VEHICLES WITH ADVANCED DRIVER-ASSISTANCE SYSTEMS.UPS - UPS WILL INITIATE TESTING ADAS FEATURES LATER IN 2020..
UPS AND WAYMO PARTNER TO BEGIN SELF-DRIVING PACKAGE PICKUP IN ARIZONA. UPS AND WAYMO PARTNER TO BEGIN SELF-DRIVING PACKAGE PICKUP IN ARIZONA.UPS - WAYMO'S SELF-DRIVING VEHICLES WILL SHUTTLE PACKAGES FROM UPS STORE LOCATIONS IN METRO PHOENIX TO UPS FACILITY.
UPS ANNOUNCES FACILITY ENHANCEMENTS IN PENNSYLVANIA. UPS - ANNOUNCED COMMITMENT OF ABOUT $1.4 BILLION TO SIGNIFICANT FACILITY ENHANCEMENTS IN PENNSYLVANIA, AS PART OF CO’S MAJOR CAPACITY EXPANSIONS IN 2020.UPS - TO OPEN NEW “SUPER HUB” IN HARRISBURG, ALONG WITH PACKAGE OPERATIONS IN CARLISLE, LEHIGH VALLEY & PHILADELPHIA, CREATING MORE THAN 1,700 JOBS.
