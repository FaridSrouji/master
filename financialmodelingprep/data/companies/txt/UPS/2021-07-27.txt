UPS RELEASES 2Q 2021 EARNINGS. ATLANTA, JULY 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- UPS (NYSE:UPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED SECOND-QUARTER 2021 CONSOLIDATED REVENUE OF $23.4 BILLION, A 14.5% INCREASE OVER THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020. CONSOLIDATED OPERATING PROFIT WAS $3.3 BILLION, UP 47.3% COMPARED TO THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020, AND UP 40.8% ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $3.05 FOR THE QUARTER, 50.2% ABOVE THE SAME PERIOD IN 2020, AND UP 43.7% ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS.
