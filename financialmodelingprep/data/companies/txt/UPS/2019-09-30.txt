UPS FLIGHT FORWARD ATTAINS FAA’S FIRST FULL APPROVAL FOR DRONE AIRLINE. OCT 1  - UNITED PARCEL SERVICE INC <UPS.N>::UPS FLIGHT FORWARD ATTAINS FAA’S FIRST FULL APPROVAL FOR DRONE AIRLINE.UPS -  UPS FLIGHT FORWARD WILL INITIALLY EXPAND ITS DRONE DELIVERY SERVICE FURTHER TO SUPPORT HOSPITAL CAMPUSES AROUND COUNTRY.UPS - UPS FLIGHT FORWARD PLANS IN FUTURE TO TRANSPORT A VARIETY OF ITEMS FOR CUSTOMERS IN MANY INDUSTRIES.UPS -  UPS FLIGHT FORWARD PLANS TO "REGULARLY FLY DRONES BEYOND OPERATORS' VISUAL LINE OF SIGHT".
