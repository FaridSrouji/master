ADVANCE AUTO PARTS NAMED OFFICIAL SPONSOR OF CHECKERED FLAG FOR NTT INDYCAR® SERIES. RALEIGH, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADVANCE AUTO PARTS (NYSE: AAP), A LEADING AUTOMOTIVE AFTERMARKET PARTS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED A MULTIYEAR AGREEMENT TO BECOME THE OFFICIAL SPONSOR OF INDYCAR'S CHECKERED FLAG USED AT EACH NTT INDYCAR SERIES RACE THROUGH THE 2025 SEASON. THE PARTNERSHIP CONNECTS ADVANCE'S DISTINCTIVE LOGO, WHICH FEATURES THE CHECKERED FLAG ICON WITH THE ICONIC FLAG NTT INDYCAR SERIES DRIVERS ASPIRE TO SEE FIRST AT THE END OF EACH RACE.
