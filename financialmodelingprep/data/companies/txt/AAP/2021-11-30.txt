ADVANCE AUTO PARTS RAISES NEARLY $1.7 MILLION IN SUPPORT OF THE AMERICAN HEART ASSOCIATION®. RALEIGH, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $AAP--ADVANCE AUTO PARTS, INC. (NYSE: AAP), A LEADING AUTOMOTIVE AFTERMARKET PARTS RETAILER, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RAISED NEARLY $1.7 MILLION IN SUPPORT OF THE AMERICAN HEART ASSOCIATION'S “LIFE IS WHY” CAMPAIGN. THE ACHIEVEMENT MAKES ADVANCE AUTO PARTS ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST CORPORATE FUNDRAISERS FOR THE AMERICAN HEART ASSOCIATION. THE RECORD-SETTING AMOUNT REPRESENTS A 21% INCREASE OVER 2020.
