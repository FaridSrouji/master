ADVANCE AUTO PARTS REPORTS RECORD FIRST QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. RALEIGH, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $AAP #AAP--ADVANCE AUTO PARTS, INC. (NYSE: AAP), A LEADING AUTOMOTIVE AFTERMARKET PARTS PROVIDER IN NORTH AMERICA, THAT SERVES BOTH PROFESSIONAL INSTALLER AND DO-IT-YOURSELF CUSTOMERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED APRIL 24, 2021.
