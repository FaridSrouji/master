ADVANCE AUTO PARTS PROVIDES COVID-19 BUSINESS UPDATE. ADVANCE AUTO PARTS PROVIDES COVID-19 BUSINESS UPDATE.IN AN EFFORT TO PRESERVE CASH,  HAVE DECREASED REMAINING CAPITAL SPEND FOR 2020 BY OVER 50%.QUARTER TO DATE COMPARABLE STORE SALES FOR TWELVE WEEKS ENDED MARCH 21, 2020 WERE DOWN 3.2% COMPARED TO PRIOR YEAR.ADVANCE AUTO PARTS - REDUCED STORE HOURS OF OPERATION AND STRATEGICALLY CLOSED STORES TEMPORARILY WHERE CAN SERVICE DEMAND FROM ALTERNATIVE LOCATION.AS OF APRIL 4, 2020, COMPANY'S CASH ON HAND WAS APPROXIMATELY $744 MILLION.CO IS WITHDRAWING FULL YEAR FINANCIAL GUIDANCE IT PROVIDED ON FEBRUARY 18, 2020.CO HAS SUSPENDED ITS SHARE REPURCHASE ACTIVITY TO FURTHER PRESERVE CASH.
