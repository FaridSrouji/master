WELLS FARGO AND HSBC ESTABLISH BILATERAL AGREEMENT TO SETTLE FX TRANSACTIONS THROUGH A BLOCKCHAIN-BASED SOLUTION. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) AND HSBC BANK PLC TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO USE A BLOCKCHAIN-BASED SOLUTION FOR THE NETTING AND SETTLEMENT OF MATCHED FOREIGN EXCHANGE (FX) TRANSACTIONS. THROUGH THIS AGREEMENT, WELLS FARGO AND HSBC WILL JOINTLY USE A SHARED SETTLEMENT LEDGER TO PROCESS US DOLLAR, CANADIAN DOLLAR, BRITISH POUND STERLING AND EURO TRANSACTIONS WITH PLANS TO EXTEND THE PLATFORM TO SETTLE ADDITIONAL CURRENCIES IN THE NEAR FUTURE.
