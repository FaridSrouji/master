NEW YORK , JULY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- POMERANTZ LLP ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED AGAINST WELLS FARGO & COMPANY ("WELLS FARGO" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: WFC) AND CERTAIN OF ITS OFFICERS. THE CLASS ACTION, FILED IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA, AND DOCKETED UNDER 22-CV-03811, IS ON BEHALF OF A CLASS CONSISTING OF ALL PERSONS AND ENTITIES OTHER THAN DEFENDANTS THAT PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED WELLS FARGO COMMON STOCK BETWEEN FEBRUARY 24, 2021 AND JUNE 9, 2022, BOTH DATES INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD"), SEEKING TO RECOVER DAMAGES CAUSED BY DEFENDANTS' VIOLATIONS OF THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS AND TO PURSUE REMEDIES UNDER SECTIONS 10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 (THE "EXCHANGE ACT") AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER, AGAINST THE COMPANY AND CERTAIN OF ITS TOP OFFICIALS.
