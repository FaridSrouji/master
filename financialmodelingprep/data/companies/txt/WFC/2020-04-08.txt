WELLS FARGO TO EXPAND PARTICIPATION IN THE PAYCHECK PROTECTION PROGRAM. WELLS FARGO TO EXPAND PARTICIPATION IN THE PAYCHECK PROTECTION PROGRAM (PPP).WELLS FARGO & CO - WILL OFFER LOANS TO A BROADER SET OF ITS SMALL BUSINESS AND NONPROFIT CUSTOMERS SUBJECT TO TERMS OF PAYCHECK PROTECTION PROGRAM.WELLS FARGO & CO - WILL CONTINUE TO ACTIVELY MAKE DECISIONS ON HOW TO ALLOCATE BALANCE SHEET TO SUPPORT NEEDS OF CUSTOMERS UNDER EXISTING ASSET CAP.WELLS FARGO & CO - REITERATING CO'S POSITION ON ASSET CAP.WELLS FARGO & CO - BEGINNING IMMEDIATELY, IN RESPONSE TO ACTIONS BY FEDERAL RESERVE, IT WILL EXPAND ITS PARTICIPATION IN PAYCHECK PROTECTION PROGRAM.
