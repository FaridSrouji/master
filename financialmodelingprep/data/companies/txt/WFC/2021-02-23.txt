SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL WELLS FARGO ASSET MANAGEMENT (WFAM) TO GTCR LLC AND REVERENCE CAPITAL PARTNERS, L.P.
