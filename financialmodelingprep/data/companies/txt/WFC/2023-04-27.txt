JAKES GROUP, WHICH INCLUDES T.D. JAKES REAL ESTATE VENTURES, LLC (TDJREV), TDJ ENTERPRISES (TDJE) AND T.D. JAKES FOUNDATION (TDJF), TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO DRIVE ECONOMIC VITALITY AND INCLUSIVITY IN COMMUNITIES ACROSS AMERICA.
