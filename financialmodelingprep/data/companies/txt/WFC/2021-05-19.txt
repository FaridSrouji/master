WELLS FARGO ISSUES INCLUSIVE COMMUNITIES AND CLIMATE BOND. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #ESG--WELLS FARGO TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ISSUANCE OF AN INCLUSIVE COMMUNITIES AND CLIMATE BOND, ITS FIRST SUSTAINABILITY BOND, WHICH WILL FUND PROJECTS AND PROGRAMS THAT SUPPORT HOUSING AFFORDABILITY, SOCIOECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY, AND RENEWABLE ENERGY. THE TRANSACTION BUILDS ON WELLS FARGO'S EXPERTISE IN AND HISTORY OF UNDERWRITING GREEN, SOCIAL, AND SUSTAINABILITY BONDS, AND ADVANCES THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO ACCELERATE A MORE INCLUSIVE AND SUSTAINABLE ECONOMY.
