PAUL CAMP NAMED HEAD OF NEW GLOBAL TREASURY MANAGEMENT GROUP AT WELLS FARGO. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PAUL CAMP HAS BEEN NAMED HEAD OF GLOBAL TREASURY MANAGEMENT. MOST RECENTLY CEO OF TREASURY SERVICES AT BNY MELLON, HE WILL JOIN THE COMPANY IN NOVEMBER, AND WILL DUALLY REPORT TO PERRY PELOS, CEO OF WELLS FARGO COMMERCIAL BANKING, AND JON WEISS, CEO OF CORPORATE & INVESTMENT BANKING.
