EVAN SAVARICK PROMOTED TO MANAGING DIRECTOR OF INVESTMENTS. NEW YORK , OCT. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WELLS FARGO ADVISORS ANNOUNCED SENIOR PORTFOLIO MANAGER, EVAN SAVARICK, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO MANAGING DIRECTOR – INVESTMENTS. THIS PRESTIGIOUS DISTINCTION IS IN RECOGNITION OF HIS DECADE LONG EXPERIENCE AND PERFORMANCE IN THE FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY, INCLUDING HIS PRACTICE'S IMPRESSIVE 300%+ GROWTH ACROSS THE SAVARICK GROUP'S NEW YORK CITY & SOUTH FLORIDA OFFICES OVER THE PAST 3 YEARS.
