WELLS FARGO NAMES PAUL RICCI AS CHIEF AUDITOR. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PAUL RICCI HAS BEEN NAMED CHIEF AUDITOR, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. RICCI WILL REPORT TO THE AUDIT COMMITTEE OF THE WELLS FARGO BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND ADMINISTRATIVELY TO CEO CHARLIE SCHARF. HE ALSO WILL JOIN THE WELLS FARGO OPERATING COMMITTEE.
