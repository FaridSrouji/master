WELLS FARGO NAMED A GLOBAL BEST IN SERVICE WINNER BY EUROMONEY MAGAZINE. SAN FRANCISCO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WELLS FARGO HAS BEEN NAMED GLOBAL BEST IN SERVICE FOR U.S. DOLLAR CASH MANAGEMENT FOR FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS BY EUROMONEY MAGAZINE IN THEIR 2022 CASH MANAGEMENT FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS SURVEY. IN ITS 21ST YEAR, THE ANNUAL SURVEY ASKS CASH MANAGERS, TREASURERS, AND FINANCIAL OFFICERS WORLDWIDE TO RANK AND ASSESS THEIR TOP PROVIDERS OF CASH MANAGEMENT SERVICES.
