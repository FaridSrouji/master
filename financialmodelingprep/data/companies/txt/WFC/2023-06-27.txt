AEQUUM CAPITAL EXPANDS EXISTING WAREHOUSE LINE WITH THE ADDITION OF WELLS FARGO & COMPANY. CHICAGO , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AEQUUM CAPITAL RECENTLY EXPANDED ITS REVOLVING LINE OF CREDIT LED BY TEXAS CAPITAL BANK TO $60 MILLION BY ADDING WELLS FARGO & COMPANY TO THE BANK LENDING GROUP.  THIS NEW FINANCING WILL FURTHER AUGMENT AEQUUM'S SIGNIFICANT EQUITY BASE LED BY CASTLELAKE, LP.
