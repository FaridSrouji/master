WELLS FARGO BANK INCREASES PRIME RATE TO 7.75 PERCENT. SAN FRANCISCO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WELLS FARGO BANK, N.A., SAID TODAY IT IS INCREASING ITS PRIME RATE TO 7.75 PERCENT FROM 7.50 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE TOMORROW, FEB. 2, 2023.
WELLS FARGO ANNOUNCES $50 MILLION GRANT TO NAACP. SAN FRANCISCO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TODAY, WELLS FARGO ANNOUNCED A $50 MILLION GRANT TO SUPPORT THE NATION'S LARGEST CIVIL RIGHTS AND SOCIAL JUSTICE ORGANIZATION IN ITS CONTINUED EFFORTS TO ADVANCE RACIAL EQUITY IN AMERICA.
