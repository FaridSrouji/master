WELLS FARGO & COMPANY ANNOUNCES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE, PAYABLE JUNE 1, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 5, 2023. ABOUT WELLS FARGO WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) IS A LEADING FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANY THAT HAS APPROXIMATELY $1.9 TRILLION IN ASSETS, PROUDLY SERVES ONE IN THREE U.S. HOUSEHOLDS AND MORE THAN 10% OF SMALL BUSINESSES IN THE U.S., AND IS A LEADING MIDDLE.
