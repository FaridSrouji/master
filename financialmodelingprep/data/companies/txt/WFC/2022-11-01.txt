WELLS FARGO NAMES KRISTY FERCHO AS HEAD OF DIVERSE SEGMENTS, REPRESENTATION AND INCLUSION. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO (NYSE: WFC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT KRISTY FERCHO, HEAD OF WELLS FARGO HOME LENDING, HAS BEEN NAMED THE COMPANY'S NEW HEAD OF DIVERSE SEGMENTS, REPRESENTATION AND INCLUSION (DSRI).
