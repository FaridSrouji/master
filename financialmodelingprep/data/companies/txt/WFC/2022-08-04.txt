WFC LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES WELLS FARGO & COMPANY INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , AUG. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN WELLS FARGO & COMPANY ("WELLS FARGO" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: WFC) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF WELLS FARGO INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD.
