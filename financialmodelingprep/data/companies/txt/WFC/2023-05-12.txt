WELLS FARGO ANNOUNCES TRANSITION INFORMATION FOR OUTSTANDING U.S. DOLLAR LIBOR-LINKED INSTRUMENTS. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) (“WELLS FARGO”) AND CERTAIN OF ITS CONSOLIDATED SUBSIDIARIES HAVE ISSUED DEBT SECURITIES, CERTIFICATES OF DEPOSIT, TRUST PREFERRED SECURITIES AND PREFERRED STOCK AND RELATED DEPOSITARY SHARES THAT REFERENCE THE LONDON INTERBANK OFFERED RATE (LIBOR) FOR DEPOSITS IN U.S. DOLLARS FOR A THREE-MONTH TENOR. THE INSTRUMENTS ADDRESSED IN THIS PRESS RELEASE ARE GOVERNED BY U.S. LAW OR THE LAWS OF A U.S. STATE. DEBT SECURITIES, CERTIFI.
