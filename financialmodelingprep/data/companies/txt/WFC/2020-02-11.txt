WELLS FARGO ANNOUNCES ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGES. WELLS FARGO ANNOUNCES ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGES.WELLS FARGO & CO - NEW MODEL HAS FIVE LINE OF BUSINESS CEOS.WELLS FARGO & CO -  MARY MACK, WHO MOST RECENTLY LED CONSUMER BANKING, BECOMES CEO OF CONSUMER AND SMALL BUSINESS BANKING.WELLS FARGO & CO - WILL CONDUCT A SEARCH FOR A NEW CEO OF WEALTH & INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT.WELLS FARGO & CO - JON WEISS, WHO MOST RECENTLY LED WEALTH & INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT, TRANSITIONS TO A NEW ROLE AS CEO OF CORPORATE & INVESTMENT BANKING.WELLS FARGO & CO - PERRY PELOS TRANSITIONS TO NEW ROLE AS CEO OF COMMERCIAL BANKING.WELLS FARGO - MIKE WEINBACH, WHO MOST RECENTLY SERVED AS CEO OF CHASE HOME LENDING AT JPMORGAN CHASE, WILL JOIN IN MAY AS CEO OF CONSUMER LENDING.WELLS FARGO & CO - BUSINESS CEOS WILL REPORT TO WELLS FARGO CEO CHARLIE SCHARF AND WILL BE REPRESENTED ON OPERATING COMMITTEE.WELLS FARGO & CO - SEPARATELY, COMPANY IS CREATING A NEW STRATEGY, DIGITAL PLATFORM & INNOVATION GROUP, REPORTING TO CEO CHARLIE SCHARF.WELLS FARGO & CO - SCOTT POWELL IS CREATING A SALES PRACTICES OVERSIGHT AND MANAGEMENT ROLE.WELLS FARGO & CO - POWELL IS LEADING STRATEGY, DIGITAL PLATFORM & INOVATION GROUP ON AN INTERIM BASIS WHILE COMPANY CONDUCTS A SEARCH FOR LEADER.WELLS FARGO & CO - MICHAEL CLEARY IS FILLING IN THE SALES PRACTICES OVERSIGHT AND MANAGEMENT ROLE.WELLS FARGO - MICHAEL CLEARY JOINED CO ON FEB. 4 FROM SANTANDER US, WHERE HE WAS CO-PRESIDENT OF SANTANDER BANK.
