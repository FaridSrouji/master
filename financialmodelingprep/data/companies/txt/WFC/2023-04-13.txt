SAUL VAN BEURDEN NAMED CEO OF CONSUMER & SMALL BUSINESS BANKING FOR WELLS FARGO; MARY MACK TO RETIRE; TRACY KERRINS NAMED HEAD OF TECHNOLOGY. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MARY MACK WILL RETIRE FROM THE COMPANY THIS SUMMER AND SAUL VAN BEURDEN, HEAD OF TECHNOLOGY AT WELLS FARGO, WILL SUCCEED MACK AS CEO OF CONSUMER & SMALL BUSINESS BANKING (CSBB), EFFECTIVE MAY 15. VAN BEURDEN WILL CONTINUE TO REPORT TO CEO CHARLIE SCHARF AND WILL CONTINUE TO SERVE ON THE COMPANY'S OPERATING COMMITTEE.
