WELLS FARGO & COMPANY ANNOUNCES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. SAN FRANCISCO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE, PAYABLE MARCH 1, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEB. 3, 2023, AS APPROVED TODAY BY THE WELLS FARGO BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
