RIGHT TO BE HOSTS BYSTANDER INTERVENTION WORKSHOPS TO COMBAT RISE IN HARASSMENT AND DISCRIMINATION. HOSTED ALONGSIDE WELLS FARGO AND NBC NEWS/MSNBC ANCHOR RICHARD LUI; SEMINARS OPEN TO COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS WILL TAKE PLACE AT AMC THEATRES IN NEW YORK AND SAN FRANCISCO IN MARCH NEW YORK , MARCH 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN RESPONSE TO THE ALARMING RISE IN HATE INCIDENTS ACROSS MAJOR U.S. CITIES, RIGHT TO BE  (FORMERLY HOLLABACK!), A NONPROFIT WORKING TO END HARASSMENT IN ALL ITS FORMS, AND  WELLS FARGO  HAVE JOINED FORCES TO HOLD BYSTANDER INTERVENTION WORKSHOPS AT AMC THEATRES IN NEW YORK CITY (MARCH 9) AND SAN FRANCISCO (MARCH 15).
