WELLS FARGO & COMPANY ANNOUNCES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE, PAYABLE DEC. 1, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOV. 4, 2022, AS APPROVED TODAY BY THE WELLS FARGO BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
