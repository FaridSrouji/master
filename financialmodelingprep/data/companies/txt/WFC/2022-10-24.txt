WELLS FARGO'S NEW VIRTUAL ASSISTANT, FARGO, TO BE POWERED BY GOOGLE CLOUD AI. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FARGO, A NEW VIRTUAL ASSISTANT SET TO ROLL OUT TO CUSTOMERS IN THE COMING MONTHS, WILL LEVERAGE GOOGLE CLOUD'S ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) TO PROVIDE A MORE PERSONALIZED, CONVENIENT, AND SIMPLE BANKING EXPERIENCE.
