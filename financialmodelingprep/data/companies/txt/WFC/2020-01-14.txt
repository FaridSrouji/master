WELLS FARGO & CO REPORTS QTRLY REVENUE OF $19.9 BLN. WELLS FARGO REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER 2019 NET INCOME OF $2.9 BILLION.QTRLY OPERATING LOSSES OF $1.9 BILLION, DRIVEN BY $1.5 BILLION, OR ($0.33) PER SHARE, OF LITIGATION ACCRUALS FOR A VARIETY OF MATTERS.QTRLY REVENUE OF $19.9 BILLION, DOWN FROM $21.0 BILLION IN FOURTH QUARTER 2018.QTRLY NET INTEREST INCOME OF $11.2 BILLION, DOWN $1.4 BILLION.QTRLY NONINTEREST EXPENSE OF $15.6 BILLION, UP $2.3 BILLION PRIMARILY DUE TO HIGHER OPERATING LOSSES.COMMON EQUITY TIER 1 RATIO WAS 11.1% AT QUARTER END.QTRLY DILUTED EPS OF $0.60 INCLUDED THE IMPACT OF LITIGATION ACCRUALS OF LOSS OF $0.33 PER SHARE.QTRLY NET INTEREST MARGIN WAS 2.53%, DOWN 13 BASIS POINTS FROM THE PRIOR QUARTER.QTRLY AVERAGE LOANS OF $956.5 BILLION, UP $10.2 BILLION, OR 1%.QTRLY MORTGAGE BANKING INCOME WAS $783 MILLION, UP FROM $466 MILLION IN THIRD QUARTER 2019.QTRLY EFFICIENCY RATIO 78.6 % VERSUS 63.6%.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.12, REVENUE VIEW $20.14 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY NONPERFORMING ASSETS DECREASED $333 MILLION, OR 6%, FROM THIRD QUARTER 2019 TO $5.6 BILLION.WELLS FARGO CEO - DURING MY FIRST 3 MONTHS, PRIMARY FOCUS HAS BEEN ON ADVANCING REQUIRED REGULATORY WORK WITH A DIFFERENT SENSE OF URGENCY, RESOLVE.QTRLY RETURN ON EQUITY 5.91% VERSUS 12.89% REPORTED LAST YEAR.WELLS FARGO CEO SAYS "OUR COST STRUCTURE IS TOO HIGH, AND I BELIEVE THERE ARE MANY AREAS WHERE WE WILL BE ABLE TO INCREASE OUR RATE OF GROWTH".
