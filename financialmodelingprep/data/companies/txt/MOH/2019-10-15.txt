MOLINA HEALTHCARE, INC. (NYSE:MOH) ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE CERTAIN ASSETS OF YOURCARE HEALTH PLAN, INC. FOR APPROXIMATELY $40 MILLION.. MOLINA HEALTHCARE, INC. (NYSE:MOH) ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE CERTAIN ASSETS OF YOURCARE HEALTH PLAN, INC. FOR APPROXIMATELY $40 MILLION ON OCTOBER 10, 2019. AS A PART OF THE TRANSACTION, MOLINA WILL ASSUME THE RIGHT TO SERVE APPROXIMATELY 46,000 MEDICAID MEMBERS IN SEVEN COUNTIES IN THE WESTERN NEW YORK AND FINGER LAKES REGIONS. THE PURCHASE PRICE WILL BE FUNDED THROUGH MOLINA’S AVAILABLE CASH. MONROE AND ITS AFFILIATE MP CARESOLUTIONS WILL CONTINUE TO PROVIDE CERTAIN MANAGEMENT AND ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES RELATED TO MEMBER CARE AND PROVIDER RELATIONS. THE TRANSACTION IS SUBJECT TO THE RECEIPT OF REGULATORY APPROVALS AND THE SATISFACTION OF OTHER CUSTOMARY CONDITIONS, AND THE CLOSING OF THE TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO OCCUR IN EARLY 2020.
