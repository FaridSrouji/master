MOLINA HEALTHCARE PROJECTS ITS CALIFORNIA MEDICAID REVENUE WILL DOUBLE UNDER REVISED MEDI-CAL CONTRACT AWARDS. LONG BEACH, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MOLINA HEALTHCARE, INC. (NYSE: MOH) (“MOLINA”) CONFIRMED TODAY THAT THE CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH CARE SERVICES (“DHCS”) HAS ANNOUNCED THE AWARDS OF MEDI-CAL CONTRACTS TO MOLINA HEALTHCARE OF CALIFORNIA COMMENCING ON JANUARY 1, 2024.
