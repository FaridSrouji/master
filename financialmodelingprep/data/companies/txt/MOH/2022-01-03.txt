MOLINA HEALTHCARE CLOSES ACQUISITION OF CIGNA'S TEXAS MEDICAID CONTRACTS. LONG BEACH, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $MOH--MOLINA HEALTHCARE, INC. (NYSE: MOH) (THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS ACQUISITION OF CIGNA'S TEXAS MEDICAID CONTRACTS CLOSED ON JANUARY 1, 2022. AS OF NOVEMBER 30, 2021, CIGNA'S TEXAS MEDICAID BUSINESS SERVED APPROXIMATELY 50,000 MEMBERS. ABOUT MOLINA HEALTHCARE MOLINA HEALTHCARE, INC., A FORTUNE 500 COMPANY, PROVIDES MANAGED HEALTHCARE SERVICES UNDER THE MEDICAID AND MEDICARE PROGRAMS AND THROUGH THE STATE INSURANCE MARKETPLACES.
