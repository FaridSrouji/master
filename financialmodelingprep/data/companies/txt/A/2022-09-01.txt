AGILENT COLLABORATES WITH METTLER TOLEDO TO REDUCE ERRORS IN SAMPLE PREPARATION WORKFLOWS FOR BETTER CHROMATOGRAPHIC RESULTS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC. (NYSE: A) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS COLLABORATING WITH METTLER TOLEDO TO ADDRESS ONE OF THE BIGGEST CONCERNS IN ANY LABORATORY—ERROR-PRONE SAMPLE PREPARATION. THIS INTEGRATED SOLUTION ALLOWS THE AUTOMATIC AND SEAMLESS TRANSFER OF WEIGHING RESULTS AND THE ASSOCIATED METADATA FROM METTLER TOLEDO LABX™ BALANCE SOFTWARE TO AGILENT OPENLAB SOFTWARE.
