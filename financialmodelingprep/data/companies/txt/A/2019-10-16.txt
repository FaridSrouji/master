SEPARATIVE CHROMATOGRAPHIE MULTICAPILLAIRE ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED €0.78 MILLION IN FUNDING FROM AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES, INC., SODIPRO, AND OTHER INVESTORS. SEPARATIVE CHROMATOGRAPHIE MULTICAPILLAIRE ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED €780,000 IN A ROUND OF FUNDING ON OCTOBER 17, 2019. THE TRANSACTION INCLUDED PARTICIPATION FROM RETURNING INVESTOR AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE:A), NEW INVESTOR SODIPRO, FOUNDERS OF THE COMPANY, AND BUSINESS ANGELS.
