AGILENT RESOLUTION CTDX FIRST RECEIVES FDA APPROVAL AS A LIQUID BIOPSY COMPANION DIAGNOSTIC TEST FOR ADVANCED NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC. (NYSE: A) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED AGILENT RESOLUTION CTDX FIRST AS A COMPANION DIAGNOSTIC (CDX) TO IDENTIFY ADVANCED NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (NSCLC) PATIENTS WITH KRAS G12C MUTATIONS WHO MAY BENEFIT FROM TREATMENT WITH KRAZATI TM (ADAGRASIB).
