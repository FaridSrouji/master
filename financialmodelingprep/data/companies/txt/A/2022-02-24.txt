AGILENT AND UNIVERSITY OF VERMONT ANNOUNCE THE AGILENT LABORATORY FOR CHEMICAL ANALYSIS (ALCA). SANTA CLARA, CALIF. & BURLINGTON, VT.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC. (NYSE: A) AND THE UNIVERSITY OF VERMONT (UVM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE AGILENT LABORATORY FOR CHEMICAL ANALYSIS (ALCA).
