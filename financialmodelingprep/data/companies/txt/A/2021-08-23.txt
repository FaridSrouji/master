AGILENT COMPANION DIAGNOSTIC EXPANDS CE-IVD MARK IN EUROPE TO INCLUDE ESOPHAGEAL CANCER. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC. (NYSE: A) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S PD-L1 IHC 22C3 PHARMDX ASSAY HAS EXPANDED ITS USE IN EUROPE. THE ASSAY IS NOW CE-IVD MARKED FOR USE AS AN AID IN IDENTIFYING ESOPHAGEAL CANCER PATIENTS FOR TREATMENT WITH KEYTRUDA USING COMBINED POSITIVE SCORE (CPS) ≥ 10.1 IN EUROPE, KEYTRUDA IS APPROVED IN COMBINATION WITH PLATINUM- AND FLUOROPYRIMIDINE-BASED CHEMOTHERAPY FOR PATIENTS WITH LOCALLY ADVANCED UNRESECTABLE OR METASTATIC ESO
