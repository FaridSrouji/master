AGILENT INCLUDED IN JUST CAPITAL'S 2023 LIST OF MOST JUST COMPANIES. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC. (NYSE: A) IS RANKED 35 AMONG THE TOP 100 MOST JUST COMPANIES, THE FIFTH YEAR THE COMPANY HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE ANNUAL LIST BY JUST CAPITAL AND CNBC. AGILENT ALSO EARNED THE TOP RANKING IN ITS SECTOR FOR INDUSTRY-LEADING PERFORMANCE REGARDING TREATMENT OF EMPLOYEES, CUSTOMERS AND THE ENVIRONMENT.
