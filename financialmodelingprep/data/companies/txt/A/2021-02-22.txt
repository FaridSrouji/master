AGILENT PD-L1 IHC 22C3 PHARMDX RECEIVES EXPANDED FDA APPROVAL IN NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (NSCLC). SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC. (NYSE: A) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED THE COMPANY'S PD-L1 IHC 22C3 PHARMDX ASSAY FOR EXPANDED USE IN PATIENTS WITH NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (NSCLC). PD-L1 IHC 22C3 PHARMDX CAN NOW BE USED AS AN AID IN IDENTIFYING NSCLC PATIENTS WITH TUMOR PD-L1 EXPRESSION OF TUMOR PROPORTION SCORE (TPS) ≥ 50% FOR TREATMENT WITH LIBTAYO® (CEMIPLIMAB-RWLC).
