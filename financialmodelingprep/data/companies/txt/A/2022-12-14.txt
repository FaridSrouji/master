BLUE STAR HELIUM LTD ACCELERATES COLORADO DRILLING DEVELOPMENT. LAS ANIMAS, CO / ACCESSWIRE / DECEMBER 14, 2022 / FOLLOWING SIGNIFICANT LAS ANIMAS HELIUM GAS DISCOVERIES IN 2022, BLUE STAR HELIUM LTD (OTCQB:BSNLF)(ASX:BNL) IS APPROACHING APPROVAL OF ITS FIRST FOUR DEVELOPMENT WELLS, NEARBY THE MODEL DOME HELIUM GAS FIELD. A FURTHER 13 WELLS ARE IN THE PIPELINE FOR APPROVAL FROM DECEMBER 2022 TO MAY 2023.
