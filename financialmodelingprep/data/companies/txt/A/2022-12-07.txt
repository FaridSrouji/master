AGILENT AGAIN NAMED TO NEWSWEEK'S LIST OF AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC. (NYSE: A) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY IS RANKED 101 IN NEWSWEEK'S 2023 LIST OF AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES, UP 28 PLACES FROM 2022. IT MARKS AGILENT'S FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR ON THE PRESTIGIOUS LIST, WHICH INCLUDES THE BEST-PERFORMING COMPANIES BASED ON THEIR ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) ACTIVITIES. AGILENT ALSO RANKED 10 AMONG HEALTHCARE AND LIFE SCIENCE COMPANIES, UP FROM NUMBER 16.
