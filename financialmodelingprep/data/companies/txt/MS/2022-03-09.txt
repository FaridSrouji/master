MORGAN STANLEY AT WORK TO HOST SECOND ANNUAL VIRTUAL FINANCIAL WELLNESS RETREAT. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY AT WORK ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT IS HOSTING ITS SECOND ANNUAL FINANCIAL WELLNESS RETREAT ON MARCH 16 -17, 2022. THIS EXCLUSIVE EVENT FOR PARTICIPANTS OF MORGAN STANLEY AT WORK'S CORPORATE CLIENTS WILL PROVIDE FINANCIAL EDUCATION, TOOLS AND INSIGHTS TO HELP THEM TAKE CONTROL OF THEIR FINANCIAL WELL-BEING.
