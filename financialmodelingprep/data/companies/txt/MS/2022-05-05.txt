MORGAN STANLEY INTRODUCES THE SMALL BUSINESS ACADEMY TO PROMOTE EQUITY AND EMPOWER DIVERSE-OWNED SMALL-BUSINESS LEADERS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY'S INSTITUTE FOR INCLUSION (IFI) AND THE MORGAN STANLEY SUPPLIER DIVERSITY PROGRAM ARE ANNOUNCING THE INAUGURAL CLASS OF THE MORGAN STANLEY SMALL BUSINESS ACADEMY (THE ACADEMY). ESTABLISHED TO PROMOTE EQUITY AND EMPOWER DIVERSE-OWNED SMALL-BUSINESS LEADERS, THIS EDUCATIONAL INITIATIVE ENABLES SMALL BUSINESSES TO DIFFERENTIATE THEMSELVES IN THE PROCUREMENT PROCESS, STRENGTHEN THEIR BUSINESS ACUMEN AND EXPAND THEIR PROFESSIONAL NETWORKS.
