MORGAN STANLEY GLOBAL SPORTS & ENTERTAINMENT AND ESPORTS TRADE ASSOCIATION LAUNCH INAUGURAL ESPORTS BUSINESS SYMPOSIUM TO UNITE KEY INDUSTRY STAKEHOLDERS. NEW YORK & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY GLOBAL SPORTS & ENTERTAINMENT (GSE) AND THE ESPORTS TRADE ASSOCIATION (ESTA) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE LAUNCH OF THEIR INAUGURAL ESPORTS BUSINESS SYMPOSIUM. THE VIRTUAL EXPERIENCE WILL FEATURE A SERIES OF PROGRAMS AND CONVERSATIONS SPANNING MYRIAD THEMES AT THE FOREFRONT OF GAMING AND ESPORTS. IT WILL BEGIN ON THURSDAY, MAY 19, 2022, KICKING OFF PROMPTLY AT 11 A.M. ET. THE EVENT WILL EXPLORE THE GLOBAL PHENOMENON OF ESPORTS AND PROVIDE ATTENDEES
