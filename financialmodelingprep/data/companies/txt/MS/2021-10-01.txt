MORGAN STANLEY CAPITAL PARTNERS COMPLETES THE SALE OF PPC FLEXIBLE PACKAGING. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVESTMENT FUNDS MANAGED BY MORGAN STANLEY CAPITAL PARTNERS (“MSCP”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SALE OF PPC FLEXIBLE PACKAGING (“PPC” OR THE “COMPANY”) TO GTCR. AS PART OF THE TRANSACTION, PPC MANAGEMENT WILL CONTINUE TO LEAD THE COMPANY AND RETAIN A SIGNIFICANT MINORITY STAKE. FINANCIAL TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION WERE NOT DISCLOSED. HEADQUARTERED IN BUFFALO GROVE, IL, PPC IS A LEADING FLEXIBLE PACKAGING PROVIDER OPERATING ACROSS TEN MANUFACTURING LOCATIONS IN THE UNITED STATE
