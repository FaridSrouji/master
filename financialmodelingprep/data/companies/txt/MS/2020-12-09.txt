MORGAN STANLEY AND ROBIN HOOD LAUNCH CAMPAIGN TO SUPPORT NEW YORK CITY STREET VENDORS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY AND ROBIN HOOD TODAY ANNOUNCED A COMMITMENT TO PROVIDE FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO 2,000 STREET VENDORS ACROSS NEW YORK CITY IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE STREET VENDOR PROJECT. STREET VENDORS HAVE REPORTED LOSSES OF 70-90% OF THEIR EARNINGS DURING COVID, AND MANY HAVE BEEN EXCLUDED FROM RELIEF PROGRAMS.1 TO ADDRESS THIS GAP AND HELP VENDORS PAY RENT, AFFORD UTILITIES AND PURCHASE FOOD, THIS FUNDING WILL PROVIDE MORE THAN 2,000 NYC VENDORS UPWARDS OF $1,000 EACH
