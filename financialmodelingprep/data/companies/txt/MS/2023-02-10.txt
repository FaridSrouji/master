E*TRADE FROM MORGAN STANLEY SAYS ‘I DO' TO SUPER BOWL LVII. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--E*TRADE FROM MORGAN STANLEY ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WILL ONCE AGAIN FEATURE IN THE SUPER BOWL WITH A 30-SECOND SPOT DURING THE FIRST QUARTER OF THE GAME, TITLED ‘WEDDING.' AFTER COMING OUT OF RETIREMENT DURING SUPER BOWL LVI 2022, THE ICONIC E*TRADE BABY IS BACK TO HELP PREPARE INVESTORS FOR LIFE'S BIGGEST FINANCIAL MOMENTS.
