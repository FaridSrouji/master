MORGAN STANLEY AT WORK SHARES INSIGHTS FROM SECOND ANNUAL FINANCIAL WELLNESS RETREAT. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY AT WORK TODAY RELEASED INSIGHTS AND LEARNINGS FROM ITS EXCLUSIVE SECOND ANNUAL FINANCIAL WELLNESS RETREAT, HELD ON MARCH 16 –17, FOR PLAN PARTICIPANTS OF ITS CORPORATE CLIENTS. THIS YEAR'S RETREAT HOSTED OVER 11,000 PLAN PARTICIPANTS, DELIVERING 30 SESSIONS ACROSS FOUR TRACKS: PERSONAL FINANCE, EQUITY COMPENSATION, RETIREMENT PLANNING AND EXECUTIVE EDUCATION.
