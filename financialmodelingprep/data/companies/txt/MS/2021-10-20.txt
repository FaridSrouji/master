MORGAN STANLEY SURVEY: PENSION FUNDS ARE LEADING THE WAY IN DIVERSITY-BASED INVESTMENTS, YET PERCEIVED FINANCIAL TRADE-OFF CREATES HURDLES FOR MANY ASSET OWNERS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ACCORDING TO A NEW REPORT AND SURVEY RELEASED BY MORGAN STANLEY, WHILE A LARGE MAJORITY OF PENSION FUNDS SAY INCORPORATING GENDER AND RACIAL DIVERSITY INTO INVESTMENT DECISIONS CAN BE FINANCIALLY BENEFICIAL, MORE THAN HALF OF ASSET OWNERS BROADLY SAY THEY BELIEVE THEY MUST CHOOSE BETWEEN FINANCIAL GAINS AND A DIVERSITY-BASED APPROACH.
