MORGAN STANLEY SURVEY REVEALS NEARLY HALF OF U.S. TEENS ARE CONCERNED ABOUT EXPERIENCING ANXIETY IN RETURNING TO REGULAR ACTIVITIES. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEARLY HALF (48%) OF U.S. TEENS ARE CONCERNED ABOUT EXPERIENCING SOCIAL ANXIETY IN TRANSITIONING BACK TO “NORMAL” LIFE, ACCORDING TO A NEW SURVEY RELEASED TODAY BY THE MORGAN STANLEY ALLIANCE FOR CHILDREN'S MENTAL HEALTH (THE “ALLIANCE”). THE SURVEY POLLED A NATIONALLY REPRESENTATIVE GROUP OF 516 U.S. TEENS AGES 15-19 TO GATHER INSIGHTS REGARDING THE IMPACT OF THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC ON MENTAL HEALTH. FOLLOWING A YEAR THAT INCLUDED REMOTE LEARNING AND DISRUPTION OF DAIL
