AT $2.5 BILLION, MORGAN STANLEY INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT RAISES ONE OF THE LARGEST FUNDS FOCUSED ON SINGLE ASSET GP-LED CONTINUATION VEHICLES. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MORGAN STANLEY INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (MSIM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS HELD THE FINAL CLOSE OF ASHBRIDGE TRANSFORMATIONAL SECONDARIES FUND II (ASHBRIDGE II) AT $2.5 BILLION OF TOTAL CAPITAL COMMITMENTS. THE AMOUNT REPRESENTS NEARLY FOUR TIMES OF THAT RAISED IN THE PREDECESSOR FUND, ASHBRIDGE TRANSFORMATIONAL SECONDARIES FUND I (ASHBRIDGE I), WHICH CLOSED IN 2018 AT $675 MILLION. ASHBRIDGE II IS ONE OF THE LARGEST FUNDS RAISED TO DATE THAT IS FOCUSED EXCLUSIVELY ON SINGLE ASSET GP-LED CONTINUATION VEHICLES. INVESTORS INCLUDE A SOPHISTICATED GROUP OF PUBLIC AND PRIVATE PENSION FUNDS, SOVEREIGN WEALTH FUNDS, NON-PROFITS, FUND OF FUNDS, FAMILY OFFICES AND HIGH NET WORTH INDIVIDUALS FROM AROUND THE WORLD.
