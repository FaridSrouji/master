MORGAN STANLEY FIRST QUARTER 2023 EARNINGS RESULTS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY (NYSE: MS) TODAY REPORTED NET REVENUES OF $14.5 BILLION FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2023 COMPARED WITH $14.8 BILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME APPLICABLE TO MORGAN STANLEY WAS $3.0 BILLION, OR $1.70 PER DILUTED SHARE,1 COMPARED WITH NET INCOME OF $3.7 BILLION, OR $2.02 PER DILUTED SHARE,1 FOR THE SAME PERIOD A YEAR AGO. JAMES P.
