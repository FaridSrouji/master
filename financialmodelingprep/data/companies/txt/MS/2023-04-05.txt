MORGAN STANLEY'S PORTFOLIO RISK PLATFORM WINS CELENT MODEL WEALTH MANAGER 2023 AWARD FOR DATA & ANALYTICS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY (NYSE: MS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS PORTFOLIO RISK PLATFORM HAS WON CELENT'S MODEL WEALTH MANAGER 2023 AWARD IN THE DATA AND ANALYTICS CATEGORY, HONORED FOR BEST PRACTICES OF TECHNOLOGY USAGE IN DIFFERENT AREAS CRITICAL TO SUCCESS IN WEALTH MANAGEMENT. CELENT, A GLOBAL RESEARCH AND ADVISORY FIRM FOR THE FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY, RECOGNIZES FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS AS ‘MODEL WEALTH MANAGERS' FOR THEIR OUTSTANDING TECHNOLOGY INITIATIVES.
