MORGAN STANLEY SURVEY REVEALS NEARLY HALF OF U.S. TEENS ARE CONCERNED ABOUT EXPERIENCING ANXIETY IN RETURNING TO REGULAR ACTIVITIES. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEARLY HALF (48%) OF U.S. TEENS ARE CONCERNED ABOUT EXPERIENCING SOCIAL ANXIETY IN TRANSITIONING BACK TO “NORMAL” LIFE, ACCORDING TO A NEW SURVEY RELEASED TODAY BY THE MORGAN STANLEY ALLIANCE FOR CHILDREN'S MENTAL HEALTH (THE “ALLIANCE”). THE SURVEY POLLED A NATIONALLY REPRESENTATIVE GROUP OF 516 U.S. TEENS AGES 15-19 TO GATHER INSIGHTS REGARDING THE IMPACT OF THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC ON MENTAL HEALTH. MORGAN STANLEY SELECTS 8 STARTUPS FOR 6TH COHORT OF MULTICULTURAL INNOVATION LAB SUPPORTING DIVERSE FOUNDERS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SIXTH COHORT OF THE MULTICULTURAL INNOVATION LAB, AN ACCELERATOR PROGRAM FOR TECHNOLOGY AND TECHNOLOGY-ENABLED STARTUPS IN THE POST‐SEED TO SERIES B FUNDING ROUNDS. THE PROGRAM, NOW IN ITS FIFTH YEAR AND WITH 51 TOTAL PARTICIPATING COMPANIES TO DATE, TARGETS STARTUPS WITH A MULTICULTURAL OR WOMAN FOUNDER, CO‐FOUNDER, CTO OR OTHER C-SUITE MEMBER THAT IS DEVELOPING INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS ACROSS SECTORS.
