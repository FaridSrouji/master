MORGAN STANLEY GLOBAL SPORTS & ENTERTAINMENT RETURNS TO MOBILE, AL AS THE EXCLUSIVE FINANCIAL EDUCATION SPONSOR OF THE REESE'S SENIOR BOWL. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MORGAN STANLEY GLOBAL SPORTS & ENTERTAINMENT (GSE), A DIVISION OF MORGAN STANLEY WEALTH MANAGEMENT, REMAINS THE EXCLUSIVE FINANCIAL EDUCATION SPONSOR OF THE REESE'S SENIOR BOWL FOR THE NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. AFTER BEING LIMITED TO VIRTUAL ENGAGEMENTS FOR THE PAST 2 YEARS DUE TO CONCERNS AND PRECAUTIONS FROM COVID-19, MORGAN STANLEY GSE RETURNS TO MOBILE, AL IN-PERSON TO PARTICIPATE IN THIS YEAR'S WEEK OF FESTIVITIES AND ENGAGEMENTS.
