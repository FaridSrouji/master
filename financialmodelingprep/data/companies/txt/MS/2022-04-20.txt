MORGAN STANLEY APPOINTS MONTRÉAL SENIOR EXECUTIVE SOPHIA BENNACEUR TO LEAD ITS MONTRÉAL OFFICE. MONTRÉAL--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MONTRÉAL SENIOR EXECUTIVE SOPHIA BENNACEUR WILL ASSUME THE ROLE OF REGIONAL HEAD OF MORGAN STANLEY MONTRÉAL AND TECHNOLOGY MONTRÉAL. SOPHIA HAS LIVED AND WORKED IN MONTRÉAL FOR 20 YEARS AND IS A NATIVE FRENCH SPEAKER. SHE WILL REPLACE ALAN VESPRINI, WHO RETIRES AFTER 12 YEARS OF SUCCESS WITH THE FIRM.
