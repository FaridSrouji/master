MESA WEST CAPITAL RAISES $1.37 BILLION FOR FIFTH VALUE-ADD REAL ESTATE DEBT FUND. LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MESA WEST CAPITAL (“MESA WEST”), THE PRIVATE U.S. REAL ESTATE CREDIT ARM OF MORGAN STANLEY INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RAISED ~$1.37 BILLION FOR MESA WEST REAL ESTATE INCOME FUND V, L.P. (“FUND V” OR THE “FUND”), EXCEEDING MESA WEST'S ORIGINAL $1.0 BILLION FUNDRAISING TARGET FOR THE FUND.
