EXACT CAPITAL GROUP RECEIVES EQUITY INVESTMENT FROM TRUFUND'S IMPACT DEVELOPERS FUND TO ACCELERATE GROWTH. NEW YORK, OCT. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUFUND FINANCIAL SERVICES ("TRUFUND"), A COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT FINANCIAL INSTITUTION LOCATED IN NEW YORK CITY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS MADE AN EQUITY INVESTMENT IN EXACT CAPITAL GROUP, LLC, A MAJORITY BLACK-OWNED REAL ESTATE DEVELOPMENT FIRM HEADQUARTERED IN NEW YORK, NY THAT SPECIALIZES IN THE DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT OF AFFORDABLE AND MIXED-USE, RESIDENTIAL, AND COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE. THE PREFERRED EQUITY INVESTMENT WILL SUPPORT CRITICAL ASPECTS OF EXACT CAPITAL'S STRATEGIC GROWTH PLAN, SPECIFICALLY ALLOWING THE FIRM TO CONTINUE WITH THE DEVELOPMENT OF AFFORDABLE HOUSING FOR LOW AND MODERATE INCOME RESIDENTS, LEVERAGE INCREASED LIQUIDITY, AND ENABLE IT TO GROW INTERNAL CAPACITY.
