MORGAN STANLEY ANNOUNCES KEY LEADERSHIP CHANGES. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY (NYSE: MS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT TED PICK AND ANDY SAPERSTEIN HAVE BEEN NAMED CO-PRESIDENTS OF MORGAN STANLEY. BOTH EXECUTIVES ARE LONG-SERVING EMPLOYEES IN KEY MANAGEMENT POSITIONS, AND BOTH HEAD WORLD-CLASS BUSINESSES WITH MARKET LEADERSHIP POSITIONS. MR. PICK LEADS MORGAN STANLEY'S INSTITUTIONAL SECURITIES BUSINESS AND WILL NOW ALSO BE RESPONSIBLE FOR MORGAN STANLEY'S INTERNATIONAL OPERATIONS AND BECOME THE CO-HEAD OF FIRM STRATEGY AND EXECUTION. MR.
