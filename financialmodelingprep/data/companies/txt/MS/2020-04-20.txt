ALTICE EUROPE ANOUNCES CREATION ALTICE PORTUGAL FTTH. CREATION OF ALTICE PORTUGAL FTTH AND PARTNERSHIP WITH MORGAN STANLEY INFRASTRUCTURE PARTNERS.TRANSACTION VALUES FASTFIBER AT EUR 4.6 BILLION ON A 100% BASIS REPRESENTING AN EBITDA MULTIPLE OF 20X.FINAL CASH CONSIDERATION RECEIVED AT CLOSING WAS EUR 1,573 MILLION FOR SALE OF 49.99% IN FASTFIBER.FURTHER PAYMENTS: EUR 375 MILLION IN DECEMBER 2021 AND EUR 375 MILLION IN DECEMBER 2026 SUBJECT TO SOME PERFORMANCE RATCHETS.
