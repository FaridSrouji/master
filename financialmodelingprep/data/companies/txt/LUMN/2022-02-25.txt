LUMEN NAMED A LEADER IN THE 2022 GARTNER® MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR NETWORK SERVICES, GLOBAL. DENVER, FEB. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) HAS BEEN NAMED A LEADER IN THE FEBRUARY 2022 GARTNER MAGIC QUADRANT FOR NETWORK SERVICES, GLOBAL. THIS IS THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR LUMEN HAS BEEN POSITIONED IN THE LEADER'S QUADRANT.
