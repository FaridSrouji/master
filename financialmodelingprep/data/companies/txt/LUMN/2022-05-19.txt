LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. DENVER , MAY 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS VOTED TO DECLARE A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.25 CENTS PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE FRIDAY JUNE 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON TUESDAY MAY 31, 2022.
