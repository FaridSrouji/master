BRIGHTSPEED ANNOUNCES INITIAL FIBER BUILD MARKETS FOR NORTH CAROLINA. COMPANY'S STATE-OF-THE-ART NETWORK TO REACH OVER 300,000 POTENTIAL CUSTOMERS IN NORTH CAROLINA BY END OF 2023 CHARLOTTE, N.C. , JUNE 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BRIGHTSPEED TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL BUILD A SIGNIFICANT PORTION OF ITS PLANNED FIBER OPTICS NETWORK IN NORTH CAROLINA, WHERE IT EXPECTS TO DELIVER, BY END OF 2023, OVER 300,000 NEW FIBER PASSINGS IN PORTIONS OF MORE THAN 30 COUNTIES IN THE FIRST PHASE OF CONSTRUCTION IN THE STATE.
