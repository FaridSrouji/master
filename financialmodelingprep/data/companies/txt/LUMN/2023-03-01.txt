LUMN INVESTOR ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP REMINDS INVESTORS OF AN INVESTIGATION INVOLVING POSSIBLE SECURITIES FRAUD VIOLATIONS BY CERTAIN CURRENT AND FORMER OFFICERS OF LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES, INC.. NEW YORK , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES INVESTORS THAT IT HAS COMMENCED AN INVESTIGATION OF LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES, INC. ("LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: LUMN) CONCERNING POSSIBLE VIOLATIONS OF FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS. AFTER THE MARKET CLOSED ON FEBRUARY 7, 2023, LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES ANNOUNCED RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2022, INCLUDING THAT FREE CASH FLOW WAS $126 MILLION IN THE FOURTH QUARTER 2022, COMPARED TO $776 MILLION IN THE FOURTH QUARTER 2021.
