LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. DENVER, MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: LUMN) REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021. "WE GENERATED SOLID ADJUSTED EBITDA AND FREE CASH FLOW IN THE FIRST QUARTER, AND WE REMAIN ON TRACK TO ACHIEVING OUR FINANCIAL TARGETS FOR THE YEAR," SAID JEFF STOREY, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF LUMEN.
