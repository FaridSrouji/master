LUMEN SELECTS ALIANZA TO POWER COMMUNICATIONS NETWORK TRANSFORMATION FROM THE CLOUD. PLEASANT GROVE, UTAH AND DENVER , APRIL 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALIANZA, INC.  AND LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT LUMEN WILL USE ALIANZA'S CLOUD COMMUNICATIONS PLATFORM TO STRENGTHEN ITS VOICE SERVICES PORTFOLIO AND PROVIDE CUSTOMERS WITH ROBUST, ANYWHERE BUSINESS COMMUNICATION FUNCTIONALITY. BUILT FOR SERVICE PROVIDERS, ALIANZA'S CLOUD COMMUNICATIONS PLATFORM IS A PREMIER CLOUD-NATIVE, CARRIER-GRADE PLATFORM THAT ADDRESSES THE CRITICAL NEEDS OF LUMEN'S CORE VOICE PRODUCTS AND CUSTOMER SEGMENTS.
