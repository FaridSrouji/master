LUMEN ANNOUNCES ISSUANCE OF NOTICES TO REDEEM QWEST CORPORATION NOTES. DENVER, FEB. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT QWEST CORPORATION, ITS INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY ("QWEST"), ISSUED NOTICES TO REDEEM ALL $235 MILLION OUTSTANDING AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 7.00% NOTES DUE 2056 (THE "QWEST NOTES")....
LUMEN AND SAP® PARTNER TO OFFER SAP HANA® FOR SAP BUSINESS ONE® ON THE LUMEN PLATFORM. DENVER, FEB. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO HELP SIMPLIFY CLOUD SERVICES FOR SMALL AND MEDIUM-SIZED ENTERPRISES, LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) TODAY ANNOUNCES A GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP WITH SAP® (NYSE: SAP) TO INTEGRATE SAP HANA® INTO THE LUMEN PLATFORM FOR BUSINESSES USING SAP BUSINESS ONE®....
