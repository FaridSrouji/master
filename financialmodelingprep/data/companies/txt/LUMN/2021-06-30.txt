AWARD RECOGNIZES LUMEN NEXTGEN911 PLATFORM FOR IMPROVING EMERGENCY CALL DELIVERY. DENVER, JUNE 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS NEXT GENERATION 911 PLATFORM IS THE FIRST RECIPIENT OF FROST & SULLIVAN'S 2021 ENABLING PLATFORM LEADERSHIP AWARD IN THE NG911 MARKET. THE LUMEN NG911 PLATFORM IMPROVES THE DELIVERY OF EMERGENCY CALLS AND ENABLES RESIDENTS TO CONTACT 911 NOT ONLY BY MAKING A VOICE CALL—IT ALSO LAYS THE FOUNDATION FOR PICTURES AND VIDEOS IN THE FUTURE.
