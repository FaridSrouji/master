LUMEN STOPS 1.06 TBPS DDOS ATTACK IN THE COMPANY'S LARGEST MITIGATION TO DATE. INTENDED VICTIM EXPERIENCED NO DOWNTIME DESPITE ATTACKER'S PERSISTENCE DENVER , AUG. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN ITS QUARTERLY REPORT ON DISTRIBUTED DENIAL OF SERVICE (DDOS) ATTACKS, LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) REVEALED THE COMPANY MITIGATED ONE OF ITS LARGEST EVER – A 1.06 TERABITS PER SECOND (TBPS) ATTACK THAT WAS PART OF A LARGER CAMPAIGN TARGETING A SINGLE VICTIM. DESPITE THE SIZE AND COMPLEXITY OF THE ATTEMPTED ATTACK, THE TARGET EXPERIENCED NO DOWNTIME.
