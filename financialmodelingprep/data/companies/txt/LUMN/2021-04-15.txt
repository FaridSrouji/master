LUMEN WELCOMES JASON LISH AS CHIEF SECURITY OFFICER. DENVER, APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JASON LISH HAS JOINED THE COMPANY'S SENIOR LEADERSHIP TEAM AS CHIEF SECURITY OFFICER. IN THIS ROLE, JASON WILL PROVIDE LEADERSHIP, STRATEGIC DIRECTION AND OVERSIGHT FOR ALL CORPORATE SECURITY FUNCTIONS, INCLUDING PHYSICAL SECURITY, EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, FRAUD MANAGEMENT, GOVERNMENT SECURITY SERVICES, AND CORPORATE CYBERSECURITY.
