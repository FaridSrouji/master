LUMEN ANNOUNCES CASH TENDER OFFERS AND CONSENT SOLICITATIONS. DENVER , JULY 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: LUMN) (THE "COMPANY" OR "LUMEN") AND ITS INDIRECT, WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARIES LEVEL 3 FINANCING, INC. ("LEVEL 3 FINANCING") AND EMBARQ FLORIDA, INC. THERE IS NO AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT SUBLIMIT APPLICABLE TO ANY OTHER SERIES OF NOTES.
