LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. DENVER, AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: LUMN) REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021. "LUMEN DELIVERED SOLID ADJUSTED EBITDA AND FREE CASH FLOW IN THE SECOND QUARTER, AND WE ARE EXECUTING ON OUR STRATEGY TO OPTIMIZE THE LUMEN ASSET PORTFOLIO, POSITIONING THE COMPANY FOR FUTURE GROWTH," SAID JEFF STOREY, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF LUMEN.
