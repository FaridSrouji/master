THEORY CONFIRMED: LUMEN BLACK LOTUS LABS DISCOVERS LINUX EXECUTABLE FILES HAVE BEEN DEPLOYED AS STEALTH WINDOWS LOADERS. DENVER, SEPT. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BLACK LOTUS LABS, THE THREAT INTELLIGENCE ARM OF LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN), HAS PROVEN WHAT WAS PREVIOUSLY JUST A THEORY: THREAT ACTORS CAN USE A LINUX BINARY AS A LOADER DESIGNED FOR WINDOWS SUBSYSTEM FOR LINUX (WSL) TO INJECT MALICIOUS FILES INTO A WINDOWS RUNNING PROCESS.
