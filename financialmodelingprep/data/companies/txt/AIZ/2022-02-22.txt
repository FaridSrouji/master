MOBILE DEVICE TRADE-IN AND UPGRADE PROGRAMS RETURN MORE THAN $3 BILLION TO US CONSUMERS IN 2021. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ASSURANT, INC. (NYSE: AIZ), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF LIFESTYLE AND HOUSING SOLUTIONS THAT SUPPORT, PROTECT AND CONNECT MAJOR CONSUMER PURCHASES, TODAY REVEALS THAT $3.1 BILLION WAS RETURNED TO U.S. CONSUMERS IN 2021 THROUGH MOBILE DEVICE TRADE-IN AND UPGRADE PROGRAMS. THIS IS ALMOST $1 BILLION MORE THAN THE $2.1 BILLION RETURNED IN 2020, AND EVEN EXCEEDS PRE-PANDEMIC LEVELS OF $2.4 BILLION IN 2019, ACCORDING TO ASSURANT'S 2021 ANNUAL TRADE-IN AND UPGRADE INDUSTRY
