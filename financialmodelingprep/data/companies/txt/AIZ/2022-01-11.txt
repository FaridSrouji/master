ASSURANT BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER COMMON SHARE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ASSURANT, INC. (NYSE: AIZ), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF LIFESTYLE AND HOUSING SOLUTIONS THAT SUPPORT, PROTECT AND CONNECT MAJOR CONSUMER PURCHASES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON MARCH 21, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 28, 2022.
