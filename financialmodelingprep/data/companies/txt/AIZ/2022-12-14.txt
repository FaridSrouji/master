ASSURANT AIMS TO REDUCE GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS BY 40 PERCENT BY 2030. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ASSURANT, INC. (NYSE: AIZ), A LEADING GLOBAL BUSINESS SERVICES COMPANY THAT SUPPORTS, PROTECTS AND CONNECTS MAJOR CONSUMER PURCHASES, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS GOAL TO REDUCE GREENHOUSE GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS BY 40 PERCENT BY 2030. THE TARGET, WHICH ALIGNS WITH THE PARIS AGREEMENT AND THE SCIENCE-BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE (SBTI), WAS SET AS PART OF THE COMPANY'S ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) GUIDING PRINCIPLE TO ENSURE ITS FACILITIES AROUND THE WORLD ADHERE TO SUSTAINABILITY PRACTICES.
