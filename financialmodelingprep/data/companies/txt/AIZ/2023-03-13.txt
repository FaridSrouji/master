ASSURANT APPOINTS RAJIV BASU TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ASSURANT, INC. (NYSE: AIZ), A LEADING GLOBAL BUSINESS SERVICES COMPANY THAT SUPPORTS, PROTECTS AND CONNECTS MAJOR CONSUMER PURCHASES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF RAJIV BASU, FORMER SENIOR PARTNER IN DELOITTE'S INSURANCE PRACTICE, TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.
