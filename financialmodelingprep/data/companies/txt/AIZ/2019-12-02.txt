ASSURANT, INC. ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF CHARLES J. KOCH AS DIRECTOR. ON NOVEMBER 27, 2019, CHARLES J. KOCH NOTIFIED THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE “BOARD”) OF ASSURANT, INC. (THE “COMPANY”) OF HIS DECISION TO RETIRE AS A DIRECTOR OF THE COMPANY AND NOT STAND FOR RE-ELECTION AT THE COMPANY’S 2020 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS (THE “2020 ANNUAL MEETING”). MR. KOCH WILL SERVE FOR THE REMAINDER OF HIS TERM AS A DIRECTOR, WHICH EXPIRES IMMEDIATELY PRIOR TO THE 2020 ANNUAL MEETING.
DANIEL A. PACICCO, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER AND CONTROLLER TO RESIGN FROM ASSURANT, INC. ON NOVEMBER 26, 2019, DANIEL A. PACICCO, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER AND CONTROLLER, NOTIFIED ASSURANT, INC. OF HIS DECISION TO RESIGN FROM HIS POSITION FOLLOWING THE FILING OF THE COMPANY’S FORM 10-K FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2019, TO ACCEPT THE ROLE OF CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AT A PRIVATE COMPANY.
