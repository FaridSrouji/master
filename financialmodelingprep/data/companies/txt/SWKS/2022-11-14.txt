THIS 5G NEW RADIO SKY5A RF FRONT-END SOLUTION WILL ACCELERATE THE DEPLOYMENT OF THIS CUTTING-EDGE PROTOCOL ACROSS AN ARRAY OF AUTOMOTIVE OEM AND CONSUMER SERVICE OFFERINGS.
