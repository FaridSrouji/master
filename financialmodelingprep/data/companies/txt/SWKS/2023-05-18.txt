BETTERINVESTING™ MAGAZINE UPDATE ON BOOKING HOLDINGS INC. AND SKYWORKS SOLUTIONS INC.. TROY, MICH. , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE EDITORIAL ADVISORY AND SECURITIES REVIEW COMMITTEE OF BETTERINVESTING MAGAZINE TODAY ANNOUNCED BOOKING HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: BKNG) AS ITS "STOCK TO STUDY" AND SKYWORKS SOLUTIONS INC. (NASDAQ: SWKS) AS ITS "UNDERVALUED STOCK" IN THE AUGUST 2023 ISSUE FOR INVESTORS' INFORMATIONAL AND EDUCATIONAL USE.
