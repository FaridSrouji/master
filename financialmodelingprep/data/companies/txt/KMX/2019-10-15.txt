CARMAX ROLLS INTO NEW ENGLAND WITH NEW RETAIL CONCEPT. CARMAX, INC. ANNOUNCED ITS NEW PERSONALIZED CAR BUYING EXPERIENCE IS NOW AVAILABLE TO CUSTOMERS IN NEW ENGLAND. CUSTOMERS IN MASSACHUSETTS AND CONNECTICUT CAN NOW CHOOSE TO COMPLETE THE ENTIRE CAR-BUYING EXPERIENCE FROM HOME AND HAVE THE CAR DELIVERED, INCLUDING THE ABILITY TO TEST DRIVE, PRIOR TO PURCHASE. THE NEW OFFERING ALSO GIVES CUSTOMERS THROUGHOUT NEW ENGLAND THE FLEXIBILITY TO DO MORE OF THE CAR BUYING PROCESS FROM HOME, AND THEN HEAD TO THEIR LOCAL CARMAX FOR EXPEDITED PICK UP OF THE VEHICLE.
