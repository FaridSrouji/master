CARMAX PARTNERS WITH AI TECHNOLOGY COMPANY UVEYE ON VEHICLE ASSESSMENT TECHNOLOGY FOR WHOLESALE VEHICLES. RICHMOND, VA, JAN. 24, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CARMAX, THE NATION'S LARGEST RETAILER OF USED CARS AND ONE OF THE LARGEST WHOLESALERS OF USED CARS, ANNOUNCES IT IS PARTNERING WITH UVEYE ON AUTOMATED VEHICLE ASSESSMENT TECHNOLOGY THROUGH AI-ENHANCED CONDITION REPORTS FOR WHOLESALE BUYERS OF VEHICLES SOLD AT AUCTION. UVEYE IS A COMPUTER VISION TECH COMPANY THAT DEVELOPS AUTOMATED INSPECTION SYSTEMS FOR VEHICLES, POWERED BY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND PROPRIETARY HARDWARE.
