BRIDGE GROWTH PARTNERS ANNOUNCES SALE OF ACCEDIAN TO CISCO. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BRIDGE GROWTH PARTNERS, LLC, A LEADING TECHNOLOGY INVESTMENT FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL ACCEDIAN, A LEADER IN PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS AND END-USER EXPERIENCE SOLUTIONS, TO CISCO. FINANCIAL TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION WERE NOT DISCLOSED. ACCEDIAN, A LEADING PROVIDER OF NETWORK AND APPLICATION PERFORMANCE MONITORING SOLUTIONS PRIMARILY USED BY TELECOMMUNICATIONS COMPANIES, WAS ACQUIRED BY BRIDGE GROWTH IN 2017. SINCE THAT TIME, ACCEDIA.
