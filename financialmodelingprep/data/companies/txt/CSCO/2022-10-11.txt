CONSUMERS WANT MORE TRANSPARENCY ON HOW BUSINESSES HANDLE THEIR DATA, NEW CISCO SURVEY SHOWS. NEWS SUMMARY: THE CISCO 2022 CONSUMER PRIVACY SURVEY REVEALS THAT CONSUMERS WANT MORE TRANSPARENCY ON HOW THEIR PERSONAL DATA IS USED AND PROTECTED. CONSUMERS ARE SUPPORTIVE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) BUT ARE CONCERNED ABOUT HOW BUSINESSES USE AI; 65 PERCENT HAVE LOST TRUST IN ORGANIZATIONS DUE TO THEIR AI USE.
