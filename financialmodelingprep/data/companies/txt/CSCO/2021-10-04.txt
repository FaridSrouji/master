TATA COMMUNICATIONS AND CISCO SYSTEMS EXPAND GLOBAL STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP. MUMBAI, INDIA AND SAN JOSE, CALIF.  , OCT. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  TATA COMMUNICATIONS, A GLOBAL DIGITAL ECOSYSTEM ENABLER, AND CISCO SYSTEMS, THE WORLDWIDE LEADER IN TECHNOLOGY THAT POWERS THE INTERNET, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF THEIR TWO-DECADES-LONG STRATEGIC GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP.
