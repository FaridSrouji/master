DOJONETWORKS REPLACES CISCO WITH IP INFUSION. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #ARCHITECHS--IP INFUSION, MARKET LEADER IN CARRIER-GRADE NETWORKING SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DOJONETWORKS, A RAPIDLY GROWING INTERNET SERVICE PROVIDER FOR MULTI-DWELLING UNITS (MDUS), HAS CHOSEN AN OPEN NETWORKING STRATEGY TO UPGRADE ITS TRANSPORT NETWORK INFRASTRUCTURE. DOJO WILL BE USING A COMBINATION OF IP INFUSION OCNOS® SOFTWARE WITH EDGECORE NETWORKS OPEN NETWORKING HARDWARE TO UPGRADE AND INTEROPERATE WITH ITS EXISTING CISCO AND JUNIPER EQUIPMENT.
