ONX CANADA ACHIEVES CISCO PARTNER ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY SPECIALIZATION. TORONTO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ONX CANADA ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ACHIEVED THE ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY SPECIALIZATION THROUGH THE CISCO PARTNER PROGRAM. THE DISTINCTION INDICATES THAT THE COMPANY HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED EXAMS AND COURSES CREATED TO HELP PARTNERS OPERATIONALIZE ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY PRACTICES.
