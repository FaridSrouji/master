CISCO DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SAN JOSE, CALIF., DEC. 9, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CISCO (NASDAQ: CSCO) ANNOUNCED THAT EARLIER TODAY ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.36 PER COMMON SHARE TO BE PAID ON JANUARY 20, 2021, TO ALL SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 5, 2021....
