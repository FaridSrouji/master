CISCO COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF SOCIO LABS. SAN JOSE, CALIF., JULY 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CISCO (NASDAQ: CSCO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF THE ACQUISITION OF PRIVATELY-HELD, U.S.-BASED SOCIO LABS, INC. SOCIO IS A MODERN EVENT TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM THAT MANAGES THE FULL LIFECYCLE OF MULTI-SESSION, MULTI-TRACK VIRTUAL, IN-PERSON AND HYBRID CONFERENCES – FROM REGISTRATION TO POST-EVENT ANALYTICS.
