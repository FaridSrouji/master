CISCO AND EMTRAIN ANNOUNCE PARTNERSHIP TO BUILD A CONSCIOUS WORKPLACE CULTURE FOR HYBRID WORK. PROJECT FOCUSES ON DELIVERING IN-APP TRAINING TO CREATE INCLUSIVE WORKPLACES ON WEBEX SAN FRANCISCO , JUNE 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EMTRAIN , A COMPANY PROVIDING ELEARNING AND ANALYTICS TO CREATE INCLUSIVE WORKPLACES BY DEVELOPING PRO-SOCIAL SKILLS, AND CISCO ARE ANNOUNCING A PARTNERSHIP TO INTEGRATE EMTRAIN'S TRAINING MODULES THROUGH WEBEX'S COLLABORATION SOLUTIONS. THIS IS ONE OF THE FIRST PROJECTS OF A BROADER PARTNERSHIP WITH CISCO TO SUPPORT WORKPLACE CULTURE IN A CHANGING HYBRID WORKPLACE.
