CISCO DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SAN JOSE, CALIF., SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEWS SUMMARY: CISCO BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND $0.37 PER COMMON SHARE TO BE PAID ON OCTOBER 27, 2021 TO ALL STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF OCTOBER 5, 2021 PREVIOUS DIVIDEND OF $0.37 PER COMMON SHARE PAID ON JULY 28, 2021 CISCO (NASDAQ: CSCO) ANNOUNCED THAT EARLIER TODAY ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.37 PER COMMON SHARE TO BE PAID ON OCTOBER 27, 2021, TO ALL STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON OCTOBER 5, 2021.
