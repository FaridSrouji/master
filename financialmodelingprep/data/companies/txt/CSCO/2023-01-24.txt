NINETY-TWO PERCENT OF ORGANIZATIONS THINK THEY NEED TO DO MORE TO REASSURE CUSTOMERS ABOUT HOW THEIR DATA IS USED IN AI, NEW CISCO RESEARCH FINDS. NEWS SUMMARY CISCO'S 2023 DATA PRIVACY BENCHMARK STUDY REVEALS THAT TO BUILD TRUST, ORGANIZATIONS FOCUS PRIMARILY ON COMPLIANCE, WHEREAS CONSUMERS VALUE TRANSPARENCY MOST. 92% OF RESPONDENTS SAY THEIR ORGANIZATION NEEDS TO DO MORE TO REASSURE CUSTOMERS ABOUT HOW THEIR DATA IS USED IN AI.
