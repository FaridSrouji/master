MSCI INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2019, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 27, 2019. ON OCTOBER 29, 2019, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS MSCI INC. DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER SHARE FOR FOURTH QUARTER 2019. THE FOURTH QUARTER 2019 DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 27, 2019 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF TRADING ON NOVEMBER 15, 2019.
