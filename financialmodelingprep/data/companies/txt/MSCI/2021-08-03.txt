MSCI LAUNCHES PRIVATE OFFERING OF $700 MILLION SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2033; TO REDEEM 5.375% NOTES DUE 2027. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MSCI INC.
