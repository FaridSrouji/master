3M JOINS WATER RESILIENCE COALITION, CEO WATER MANDATE. ST. PAUL, MINN., AUG. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- 3M (NYSE: MMM) ANNOUNCED IT IS JOINING THE WATER RESILIENCE COALITION (WRC) TODAY, TAKING ONE MORE STEP FORWARD IN THE COMPANY'S ONGOING COMMITMENT TO HELP THE NEARLY TWO BILLION PEOPLE WORLDWIDE LIVING IN WATER-STRESSED AND WATER-SCARCE COMMUNITIES.
