THIS EARNINGS RANGE INCLUDES AN ESTIMATED $0.15 PER SHARE NEGATIVE IMPACT FROM THE RECENTLY CLOSED ACQUISITION OF ACELITY INC., WHICH WAS PREVIOUSLY NOT INCLUDED IN GUIDANCE.
