3M TO INVEST NEARLY $150 MILLION TO ADVANCE CAPABILITIES IN BIOPHARMA FILTRATION TECHNOLOGY. ST. PAUL, MINN. , MAY 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- APPLYING ITS LEADERSHIP IN BIOPHARMA FILTRATION TECHNOLOGY, 3M (NYSE: MMM) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL INVEST $146 MILLION DOLLARS TO EXPAND ITS CAPABILITIES TO FURTHER SUPPORT BIOTECH MANUFACTURING, A FAST-GROWING AREA OF HEALTH CARE WHICH IS BRINGING BREAKTHROUGH THERAPIES TO PATIENTS.
