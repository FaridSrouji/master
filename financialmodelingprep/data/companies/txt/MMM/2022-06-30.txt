3M TO SELL RIGHTS TO NEOPLAST™ AND NEOBUN™ BRANDS AND RELATED ASSETS IN THAILAND AND CERTAIN OTHER SOUTHEAST ASIA COUNTRIES. ST. PAUL, MINN., JUNE 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- 3M (NYSE: MMM) HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS RIGHTS TO THE NEOPLAST™ AND NEOBUN™ BRANDS AND RELATED ASSETS IN THAILAND AND CERTAIN OTHER SOUTHEAST ASIA COUNTRIES, INCLUDING THE MANUFACTURING ASSETS OF ITS LADLUMKAEW, THAILAND, FACILITY TO SELIC CORP PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED (SELIC), A COMPANY FOCUSING ON BONDING INNOVATION SERVING VARIOUS INDUSTRIES.
