3M ANNOUNCES FINAL PRORATION FACTOR OF 7.346065 PERCENT FOR SHARES TENDERED IN SPLIT-OFF EXCHANGE OFFER. ST. PAUL, MINN. , SEPT. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- 3M (NYSE: MMM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE FINAL PRORATION FACTOR OF 7.346065 PERCENT IN ITS SPLIT-OFF EXCHANGE OFFER TO 3M STOCKHOLDERS IN CONNECTION WITH THE SEPARATION OF 3M'S FOOD SAFETY BUSINESS AND MERGER OF GARDEN SPINCO CORPORATION ("SPINCO"), THE 3M SUBSIDIARY HOLDING THE FOOD SAFETY BUSINESS, WITH A SUBSIDIARY OF NEOGEN CORPORATION.
