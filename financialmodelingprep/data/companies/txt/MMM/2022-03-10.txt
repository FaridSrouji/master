NEOGEN INVESTOR ALERT BY THE FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA: KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC INVESTIGATES MERGER OF NEOGEN CORPORATION - NEOG. NEW ORLEANS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA CHARLES C. FOTI, JR., ESQ. AND THE LAW FIRM OF KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC (“KSF”) ARE INVESTIGATING THE PROPOSED MERGER OF NEOGEN CORPORATION (“THE COMPANY”) (NASDAQGS: NEOG) WITH 3M (NYSE: MMM) PURSUANT TO WHICH SHAREHOLDERS OF NEOGEN WILL OWN ONLY APPROXIMATELY 49.9% OF THE COMBINED COMPANY. KSF IS SEEKING TO DETERMINE WHETHER THE MERGER AND THE PROCESS THAT LED TO IT ARE ADEQUATE, OR WHETHER THE MERGER UNDERVALUES THE COMPANY
