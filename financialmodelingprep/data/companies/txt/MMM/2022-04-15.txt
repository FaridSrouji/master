3M AND THE NATIONAL COALITION OF CERTIFICATION CENTERS AWARD NATIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS. ST. PAUL, MINN. , APRIL 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- 3M'S SAFETY AND INDUSTRIAL BUSINESS GROUP AND THE NATIONAL COALITION OF CERTIFICATION CENTERS (NC3) HAVE ANNOUNCED THE WINNERS FOR THE SKILLED TRADES - 3M TRANSFORMATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP FOR STUDENTS WITH A FOCUS IN SKILLED TRADES.
