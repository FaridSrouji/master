3M AND GUARDHAT ANNOUNCE COLLABORATION ON CONNECTED SAFETY. GUARDHAT WILL BRING 3M'S SAFETY INSPECTION MANAGER (SIM) INTO ITS INDUSTRIAL INTERNET OF PEOPLE (IIOP) PLATFORM TO ACCELERATE PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT. THIS COLLABORATION IS EXPECTED TO ACCELERATE SIM ENHANCEMENTS WHILE ALSO EVALUATING NEW CONNECTED SAFETY PERSONAL PROTECTION EQUIPMENT (PPE) THAT AIMS TO KEEP MORE FRONTLINE WORKERS SAFE.
