SLB INTRODUCES LOW-CARBON ALTERNATIVE FOR WELL CONSTRUCTION CEMENT. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--REGULATORY NEWS: SLB (NYSE: SLB) TODAY INTRODUCED THE ECOSHIELD™ GEOPOLYMER CEMENT-FREE SYSTEM THAT MINIMIZES THE CO2 FOOTPRINT OF A WELL'S CONSTRUCTION. THIS INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGY ELIMINATES UP TO 85 PERCENT OF EMBODIED CO2 EMISSIONS COMPARED WITH CONVENTIONAL WELL CEMENTING SYSTEMS, WHICH INCLUDE PORTLAND CEMENT. THE ECOSHIELD SYSTEM HAS THE POTENTIAL TO AVOID UP TO 5 MILLION METRIC TONS OF CO2 EMISSIONS ANNUALLY—THE EQUIVALENT OF REMOVING 1.
