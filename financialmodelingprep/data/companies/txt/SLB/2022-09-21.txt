SCHLUMBERGER LAUNCHES ENTERPRISE DATA SOLUTION. LUCERNE, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SCHLUMBERGER TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMMERCIAL RELEASE OF THE SCHLUMBERGER ENTERPRISE DATA SOLUTION, WHICH IS POWERED BY MICROSOFT ENERGY DATA SERVICES. DEVELOPED TO DELIVER THE MOST COMPREHENSIVE CAPABILITIES FOR SUBSURFACE DATA—IN ALIGNMENT WITH THE EMERGING REQUIREMENTS OF THE OSDU™ TECHNICAL STANDARD, A NEW OPEN INDUSTRY STANDARD FOR ENERGY DATA—THE ENTERPRISE DATA SOLUTION MAKES DATA ACCESSIBLE ON AN UNPRECEDENTED SCALE FOR THE GLOBAL ENERGY INDUSTRY.
