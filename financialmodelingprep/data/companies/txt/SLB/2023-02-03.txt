SLB COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF GYRODATA INCORPORATED. HOUSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--SLB (NYSE: SLB) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF GYRODATA INCORPORATED, A GLOBAL COMPANY SPECIALIZING IN GYROSCOPIC WELLBORE POSITIONING AND SURVEY TECHNOLOGY. THE TRANSACTION WILL INCORPORATE GYRODATA'S WELLBORE PLACEMENT AND SURVEYING TECHNOLOGIES WITHIN SLB'S WELL CONSTRUCTION BUSINESS, BRINGING CUSTOMERS INNOVATIVE DRILLING SOLUTIONS.
