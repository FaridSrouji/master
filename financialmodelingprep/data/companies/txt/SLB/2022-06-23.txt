HYZON MOTORS TO COLLABORATE WITH SCHLUMBERGER IN DECARBONIZING OIL & GAS FIELD OPERATIONS WITH HIGH-POWER FUEL CELLS. ROCHESTER, N.Y. , JUNE 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HYZON MOTORS (NASDAQ: HYZN) IS TEAMING UP WITH SCHLUMBERGER LIMITED (NYSE: SLB) TO REDUCE EMISSIONS IN UPSTREAM OIL & GAS OPERATIONS WITH POWER GENERATED BY ITS HEAVY DUTY FUEL CELL SYSTEMS.
