STRATEGIC BIOFUELS SELECTS SLB FOR CARBON SEQUESTRATION SERVICES ON CARBON-NEGATIVE FUELS PROJECT IN LOUISIANA. COLUMBIA, LA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STRATEGIC BIOFUELS, THE LEADER IN DEVELOPING NEGATIVE CARBON FOOTPRINT BIOFUELS PLANTS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH SLB, A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TO PROVIDE CARBON SEQUESTRATION SERVICES FOR STRATEGIC BIOFUEL'S LOUISIANA GREEN FUELS (LGF) PROJECT, SUPPORTING THE PRODUCTION OF DEEPLY CARBON-NEGATIVE FUELS. IN THE PAST 20 YEARS, SLB HAS BEEN INVOLVED IN OVER 100 CARBON SEQUESTRATION PROJECTS AROUND THE WORLD IN VARIOUS INDUSTRY SE.
