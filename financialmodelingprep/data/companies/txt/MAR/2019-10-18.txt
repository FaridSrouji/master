MARRIOT INTERNATIONAL TO ACQUIRE ELEGANT HOTELS GROUP FUELING GROWTH OF ALL-INCLUSIVE PLATFORM.OFFER VALUES ENTIRE ISSUED AND TO BE ISSUED ORDINARY SHARE CAPITAL OF ELEGANT AT APPROXIMATELY £100.8 MILLION.UNDER TERMS OF OFFER, EACH ELEGANT SHAREHOLDER WOULD BE ENTITLED TO RECEIVE 110 PENCE PER ELEGANT SHARE.UPON COMPLETION OF PROPERTY RENOVATIONS, CO TO OPERATE ELEGANT HOTELS AS ALL-INCLUSIVE RESORTS UNDER ONE OR MORE OF MARRIOTT'S COLLECTION BRANDS.
