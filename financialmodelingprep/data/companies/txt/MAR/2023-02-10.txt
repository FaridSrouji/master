MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND; APPOINTS CURRENT CEO ANTHONY CAPUANO AS PRESIDENT AND CEO. BETHESDA, MD. , FEB. 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL (NASDAQ: MAR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 40 CENTS PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.
