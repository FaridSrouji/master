MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL DECLARES AN INCREASE IN QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND AND INCREASES SHARE BUYBACK AUTHORIZATION. BETHESDA, MD. , NOV. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL, INC., (NASDAQ: MAR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 40 CENTS PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, WHICH REPRESENTS A 33 PERCENT INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AMOUNT OF 30 CENTS PER SHARE AND REFLECTS THE COMPANY'S EARNINGS GROWTH AND STRONG CASH GENERATION.
