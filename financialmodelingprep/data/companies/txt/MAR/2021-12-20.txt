THE LUXURY COLLECTION DEBUTS IN AUSTRALIA WITH THE OPENING OF THE TASMAN, A LUXURY COLLECTION HOTEL, HOBART. HOBART, AUSTRALIA, DEC. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LUXURY COLLECTION HOTELS & RESORTS, PART OF MARRIOTT BONVOY'S PORTFOLIO OF 30 EXTRAORDINARY HOTEL BRANDS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF THE TASMAN, A LUXURY COLLECTION HOTEL, HOBART, MARKING THE DEBUT OF THE LUXURY COLLECTION BRAND IN AUSTRALIA. THE TASMAN SHOWCASES HOBART, A DESTINATION STEEPED IN RICH HISTORY AND AUSTRALIA'S SECOND OLDEST CITY.
