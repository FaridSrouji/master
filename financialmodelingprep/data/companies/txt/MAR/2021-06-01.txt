UNPARALLELED LUXURY EMBRACES THE CIRCLE OF ISLAND LIFE WITH THE DEBUT OF THE RITZ-CARLTON MALDIVES, FARI ISLANDS. NORTH MALÉ ATOLL, MALDIVES, JUNE 1, 2021   /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE RITZ-CARLTON HOTEL COMPANY, L.L.C TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS DEBUT IN THE MALDIVES, WITH THE OPENING OF THE RITZ-CARLTON MALDIVES, FARI ISLANDS. LOCATED ON THE FARI ISLANDS, AN ARCHIPELAGO ON THE NORTH-EASTERN TIP OF NORTH MALÉ ATOLL, THE AREA IS FAMOUS FOR ITS UNTOUCHED WHITE-SAND BEACHES AND BRILLIANT AZURE WATERS TEEMING WITH MARINE LIFE.
