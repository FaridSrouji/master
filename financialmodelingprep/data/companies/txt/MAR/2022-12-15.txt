MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL CELEBRATES THE POWER OF "ONE IN A MILLION" IN ANTI-TRAFFICKING MILESTONE. ONE MILLION MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATES GLOBALLY HAVE BEEN TRAINED TO RECOGNIZE AND RESPOND TO SIGNS OF HUMAN TRAFFICKING BETHESDA, MD. , DEC. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH THE UNITED NATIONS GENERAL ASSEMBLY RECOGNITION OF HUMAN RIGHTS DAY ON DECEMBER 10, 2022, MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY TRAINED ONE MILLION ASSOCIATES TO SPOT THE SIGNS OF AND RESPOND TO HUMAN TRAFFICKING.
