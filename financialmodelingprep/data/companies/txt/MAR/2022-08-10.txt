MARRIOTT HOTELS DEBUTS IN ONE OF COLOMBIA'S MOST VIBRANT DESTINATIONS WITH THE OPENING OF BARRANQUILLA MARRIOTT HOTEL. INSPIRED BY THE SPIRIT OF THE COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN, THE 191-ROOM HOTEL INVITES GUESTS TO IMMERSE THEMSELVES IN LOCAL CULTURE WHEN VISITING BARRANQUILLA BETHESDA, MD. , AUG. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARRIOTT HOTELS , THE SIGNATURE FLAG OF MARRIOTT BONVOY'S 30 EXTRAORDINARY HOTEL BRANDS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF THE HIGHLY-ANTICIPATED BARRANQUILLA MARRIOTT HOTEL , A TRIBUTE TO THE VIBRANT CITY OF BARRANQUILLA KNOWN AS THE "GOLDEN GATE OF COLOMBIA," SURROUNDED BY LUSH TROPICAL LANDSCAPES AND THE COLORS OF THE COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN.
