MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL RECEIVES REGULATORY APPROVAL TO COMPLETE CITY EXPRESS BRAND ACQUISITION. BETHESDA, MD. , MARCH 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MAR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED APPROVAL FROM MEXICO'S FEDERAL ECONOMIC COMPETITION COMMISSION, COFECE, FOR THE ACQUISITION OF THE CITY EXPRESS BRAND PORTFOLIO FROM HOTELES CITY EXPRESS, S.A.B.
