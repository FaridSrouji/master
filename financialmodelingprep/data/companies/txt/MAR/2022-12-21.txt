UNDER ARMOUR ANNOUNCES STEPHANIE LINNARTZ AS PRESIDENT AND CEO. BALTIMORE , DEC. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNDER ARMOUR, INC. (NYSE: UA, UAA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT STEPHANIE LINNARTZ WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS PRESIDENT, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AND MEMBER OF ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 27, 2023. HER APPOINTMENT FOLLOWS A THOROUGH AND DELIBERATE SEARCH LED BY A COMMITTEE OF INDEPENDENT MEMBERS OF UNDER ARMOUR'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
