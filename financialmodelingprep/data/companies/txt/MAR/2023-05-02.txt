MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS AND RAISES FULL YEAR OUTLOOK. FIRST QUARTER 2023 COMPARABLE SYSTEMWIDE CONSTANT DOLLAR REVPAR INCREASED 34.3   PERCENT WORLDWIDE, 25.6 PERCENT IN THE U.S. & CANADA, AND 63.1 PERCENT IN INTERNATIONAL MARKETS, COMPARED TO THE 2022 FIRST QUARTER;  FIRST QUARTER REPORTED DILUTED EPS TOTALED $2.43, COMPARED TO REPORTED DILUTED EPS OF $1.14 IN THE YEAR-AGO QUARTER.  FIRST QUARTER ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS TOTALED $2.09, COMPARED TO FIRST QUARTER 2022 ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS OF $1.25; FIRST QUARTER REPORTED NET INCOME TOTALED $757 MILLION, COMPARED TO REPORTED NET INCOME OF $377 MILLION IN THE YEAR-AGO QUARTER.
