MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL SIGNS AGREEMENT WITH BAIN CAPITAL CREDIT AND OMNAM GROUP TO BRING EDITION HOTELS BRAND TO ITALY'S LAKE COMO. LAKE COMO, ITALY , SEPT. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL, INC., TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED AN AGREEMENT WITH BAIN CAPITAL CREDIT AND OMNAM GROUP TO BRING ITS EDITION HOTELS BRAND TO LAKE COMO.
