THE LUXURY COLLECTION ANNOUNCES NEW GLOBAL EXPLORER CAROLINE DE MAIGRET. NEW YORK, DEC. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LUXURY COLLECTION, PART OF MARRIOTT BONVOY'S PORTFOLIO OF 30 EXTRAORDINARY HOTEL BRANDS, ANNOUNCES CAROLINE DE MAIGRET AS THE BRAND'S NEWEST GLOBAL EXPLORER. IN HER ROLE AS A BRAND AMBASSADOR, DE MAIGRET HAS COLLABORATED WITH THE LUXURY COLLECTION TO PRODUCE A NIGHT IN SEVILLE AND THE MORNING AFTER  -- A GUIDE TO HER FAVORITE PLACES TO STAY, DINE AND DISCOVER PUBLISHED BY ASSOULINE.
