MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL DECLARES AN INCREASE IN QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. BETHESDA, MD. , MAY 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MAR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 52 CENTS PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, WHICH REPRESENTS A 30 PERCENT INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AMOUNT OF 40 CENTS PER SHARE AND REFLECTS THE COMPANY'S EARNINGS GROWTH AND STRONG CASH GENERATION.
