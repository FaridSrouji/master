MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL EXPANDS INTO AFFORDABLE MIDSCALE SEGMENT WITH ACQUISITION OF THE CITY EXPRESS BRAND PORTFOLIO. TRANSACTION MAKES MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL THE LARGEST HOTEL COMPANY IN THE CARIBBEAN AND LATIN AMERICA BETHESDA, MD. , MAY 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MAR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF THE CITY EXPRESS BRAND PORTFOLIO FROM HOTELES CITY EXPRESS, S.A.B.
