AMERICAN EXPRESS TO OPEN A NEW CENTURION® LOUNGE AT HARTSFIELD-JACKSON ATLANTA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN EXPRESS (NYSE: AXP) TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO OPEN A NEW CENTURION LOUNGE AT THE HARTSFIELD-JACKSON ATLANTA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (ATL). THE NEW LOUNGE, WHICH IS EXPECTED TO OPEN IN 2023, WILL BE THE LARGEST IN THE CENTURION NETWORK WITH APPROXIMATELY 26,000 SQUARE FEET AND WILL FEATURE AN ARRAY OF NEW AMENITIES.
