AMERICAN EXPRESS PLEDGES $2 MILLION TO THE UNITED NEGRO COLLEGE FUND TO HELP ADVANCE OPPORTUNITIES FOR STUDENTS IN UNDERREPRESENTED COMMUNITIES. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN EXPRESS (NYSE: AXP) AMERICAN EXPRESS TODAY ANNOUNCED A $2 MILLION PLEDGE FROM THE AMERICAN EXPRESS FOUNDATION TO UNCF (UNITED NEGRO COLLEGE FUND) TO HELP ADVANCE OPPORTUNITIES FOR STUDENTS IN UNDERREPRESENTED COMMUNITIES.
