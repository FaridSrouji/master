AMERICAN EXPRESS CHAIRMAN AND CEO STEPHEN J. SQUERI TO PARTICIPATE IN BERNSTEIN 39TH ANNUAL STRATEGIC DECISIONS CONFERENCE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN EXPRESS COMPANY (NYSE: AXP) CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER STEPHEN J. SQUERI WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE BERNSTEIN 39TH ANNUAL STRATEGIC DECISIONS CONFERENCE ON THURSDAY, JUNE 1, AT 11 A.M. EASTERN. MR. SQUERI WILL PARTICIPATE IN A FIRESIDE CHAT RELATING TO THE COMPANY'S BUSINESS STRATEGY AND FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE.
