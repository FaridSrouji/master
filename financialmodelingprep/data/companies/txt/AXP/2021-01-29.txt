AMERICAN EXPRESS CONTINUES TO EXPAND THE CENTURION® LOUNGE NETWORK WITH THE OPENING OF ITS 14TH LOCATION AT DENVER INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, AMERICAN EXPRESS (NYSE: AXP) ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL OPEN ITS 14TH CENTURION LOUNGE AT DENVER INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (DEN) ON FEBRUARY 1, 2021.
