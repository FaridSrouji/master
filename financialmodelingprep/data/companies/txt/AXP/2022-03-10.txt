AMERICAN EXPRESS BOARD AUTHORIZES 20 PERCENT DIVIDEND INCREASE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMERICAN EXPRESS COMPANY (NYSE: AXP) APPROVED A $0.09 – OR APPROXIMATELY 20 PERCENT – INCREASE IN THE REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WAS RAISED TO $0.52 PER COMMON SHARE, FROM $0.43 CENTS, PAYABLE ON MAY 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON APRIL 8, 2022.
