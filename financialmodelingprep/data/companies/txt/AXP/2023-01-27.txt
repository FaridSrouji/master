AMERICAN EXPRESS DELIVERS ON 2022 GROWTH PLAN WITH FULL-YEAR REVENUE GROWTH OF 25% AND EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $9.85. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMERICAN EXPRESS COMPANY (NYSE: AXP) TODAY REPORTED FULL-YEAR NET INCOME OF $7.5 BILLION, OR $9.85 PER SHARE, COMPARED WITH NET INCOME OF $8.1 BILLION, OR $10.02 PER SHARE, A YEAR AGO.
