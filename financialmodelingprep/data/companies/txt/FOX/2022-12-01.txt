FOX CORPORATION CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER STEVE TOMSIC TO PARTICIPATE IN UPCOMING UBS GLOBAL TMT CONFERENCE. NEW YORK AND LOS ANGELES , DECEMBER 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: FOXA, FOX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER STEVE TOMSIC WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE UPCOMING UBS GLOBAL TMT CONFERENCE ON MONDAY, DECEMBER 5 AT APPROXIMATELY 10:00AM (EASTERN), 7:00AM (PACIFIC). A LIVE AND ARCHIVED WEBCAST OF THE PRESENTATION WILL BE AVAILABLE AT INVESTOR.FOXCORPORATION.COM.
