FOX NEWS DIGITAL CLAIMS NUMBER ONE SPOT IN MULTIPLATFORM MINUTES AND MULTIPLATFORM VIEWS DURING SECOND QUARTER. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FOX NEWS DIGITAL CLOSED OUT THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021 EARNING THE TOP SPOT IN BOTH MULTIPLATFORM MINUTES AND MULTIPLATFORM VIEWS, REACHING OVER 4.7 BILLION MULTIPLATFORM VIEWS AND NEARLY 9.9 BILLION MULTIPLATFORM TOTAL MINUTES, ACCORDING TO COMSCORE. IN THE MOST RECENT MONTH OF JUNE, THE DIGITAL NETWORK SURPASSED CNN.COM IN TOTAL MULTIPLATFORM MINUTES FOR THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE MONTH AND OUTPERFORMED ALL OTHER NEWS BRANDS IN THE NEWS COMPETITIVE SET, WITH 2.9 BILLION
