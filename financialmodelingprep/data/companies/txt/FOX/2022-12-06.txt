FOX'S SPECIAL COMMITTEE OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS PROVIDES UPDATE. NEW YORK AND LOS ANGELES , DEC. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SPECIAL COMMITTEE OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE "SPECIAL COMMITTEE") OF FOX CORPORATION ("FOX" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: FOX, FOXA) FORMED TO EXPLORE A POTENTIAL COMBINATION WITH NEWS CORPORATION (THE "POTENTIAL TRANSACTION") FOLLOWING RECEIPT OF LETTERS FROM K. RUPERT MURDOCH AND THE MURDOCH FAMILY TRUST PROVIDED AN UPDATE ON THE PROCESS IN RESPONSE TO RECENT INQUIRIES.
