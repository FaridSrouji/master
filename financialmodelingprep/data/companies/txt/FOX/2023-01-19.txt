OUTKICK FINISHES 2022 AS THE FASTEST GROWING SPORTS SITE IN THE FOURTH QUARTER WITH UNIQUE VISITORS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--OUTKICK, THE FASTEST GROWING NATIONAL MULTIMEDIA PLATFORM, FOUNDED BY CLAY TRAVIS, SAW A MASSIVE INCREASE AMONGST ITS CUSTOM SPORTS COMPETITIVE SET IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022 WITH MULTIPLATFORM UNIQUE VISITORS. OUTKICK CLOSED OUT THE FOURTH QUARTER WITH AN AVERAGE OF 9.1 MILLION MULTIPLATFORM UNIQUE VISITORS, UP 235% FROM THE FOURTH QUARTER IN 2021, ACCORDING TO COMSCORE.
