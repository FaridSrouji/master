INTERPUBLIC DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, NY, MAY 25, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- INTERPUBLIC GROUP (NYSE: IPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON IPG COMMON STOCK OF $0.31 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JUNE 20, 2023, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 6, 2023.
