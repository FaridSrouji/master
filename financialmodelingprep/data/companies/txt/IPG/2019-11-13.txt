MICHAEL KAPFER AND DIETER PIVRNEC ACQUIRED 80% STAKE IN LOWE GGK WERBEAGENTUR GMBH FROM THE INTERPUBLIC GROUP OF COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE:IPG) ON NOVEMBER 14, 2019. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION, MICHAEL KAPFER AND DIETER PIVRNEC WILL ACQUIRE 40% STAKE EACH AND THE REMAINING THE INTERPUBLIC GROUP OF COMPANIES WILL RETAIN THE REMAINING 20%. UPON COMPLETION OF THE TRANSACTION, THE THREE COMPANY BRANDS WILL BE PRESERVED. GGK MULLENLOWE REPORTED A REVENUE OF €6.4 MILLION IN 2018.
