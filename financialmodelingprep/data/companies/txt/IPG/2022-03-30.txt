MCCANN NAMES MICHELLE TANG CHIEF GROWTH OFFICER, NORTH AMERICA. NEW YORK , MARCH 30, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCANN TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MICHELLE TANG HAS BEEN NAMED CHIEF GROWTH OFFICER FOR THE AGENCY'S NORTH AMERICAN OPERATIONS. SHE WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO ALEX LUBAR, PRESIDENT, MCCANN NORTH AMERICA.
