PRIMIS APPOINTS AMIR RUDNER AS VP OF R&D. TEL AVIV, ISRAEL , AUG. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PRIMIS, THE LEADING VIDEO DISCOVERY PLATFORM FOR GLOBAL PUBLISHERS AND FOUNDERS OF SELLERS.GUIDE, ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF AMIR RUDNER AS THE COMPANY'S NEW VP OF R&D. AMIR STARTED HIS CAREER IN THE R&D DEPARTMENT AT PRIMIS 6 AND A HALF YEARS AGO.
