MCCANN WORLDGROUP NAMES NICOLINA O'RORKE GLOBAL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. NEW YORK, NOV. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCANN WORLDGROUP TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT NICOLINA O'RORKE HAS BEEN NAMED GLOBAL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. O'RORKE STEPS INTO THE ROLE VACATED BY GARY LEE, WHO ANNOUNCED HIS RETIREMENT EARLIER THIS YEAR AND WILL SERVE IN A TRANSITIONAL ROLE OVER THE NEXT FEW MONTHS.
