INTERPUBLIC DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, NY, OCT. 31, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- INTERPUBLIC GROUP (NYSE: IPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON IPG COMMON STOCK OF $0.29 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2022, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 1, 2022.
