MRM BOOSTS COMMERCE PRACTICE LEADERSHIP, APPOINTS ED KIM AS EVP OF TOTAL COMMERCE EXPERIENCES. NEW YORK, JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MRM, THE LEADING MARKETING AGENCY THAT IS PART OF MCCANN WORLDGROUP, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE APPOINTMENT OF ED KIM AS EVP OF TOTAL COMMERCE EXPERIENCES AND COMMERCE PRACTICE LEAD. IN THIS ROLE, KIM WILL LEAD ALL EXISTING AND NEW COMMERCE PROPOSITIONS AND SERVICE LINES GLOBALLY, WITH A FOCUS ON SUSTAINABLE GROWTH AT SCALE.
