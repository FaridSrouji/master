INTERPUBLIC DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, NOV. 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --   INTERPUBLIC GROUP (NYSE: IPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON IPG COMMON STOCK OF $0.27 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 1, 2021.
