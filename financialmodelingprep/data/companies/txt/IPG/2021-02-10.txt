INTERPUBLIC INCREASES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND FOR NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. NEW YORK, FEB. 10, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --   INTERPUBLIC GROUP (NYSE: IPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON IPG COMMON STOCK OF $0.27 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON MARCH 15, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 1, 2021. THE INCREASE IN THE DIVIDEND FROM $0.255 TO $0.27 PER SHARE REPRESENTS A 6 PERCENT INCREASE TO THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
