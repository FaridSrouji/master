INTERPUBLIC GROUP DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 16, 2019. INTERPUBLIC GROUP ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY’S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON IPG COMMON STOCK OF $0.235 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 16, 2019, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 2, 2019.
