IPG MEDIABRANDS AND TIKTOK BUILD EXCLUSIVE CREATOR PROGRAMS TO CONNECT BRANDS WITH CULTURE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, IPG MEDIABRANDS AND TIKTOK ARE JOINING FORCES TO SPEARHEAD A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND CREATOR AND CONTENT ACCELERATOR INITIATIVE DESIGNED TO DRIVE BRANDS TO THE CENTER OF CULTURE. IPG MEDIABRANDS AND TIKTOK WILL CO-CREATE A SERIES OF CUSTOM PROGRAMS FOR IPG MEDIABRANDS' CLIENTS, HELPING THEM BUILD AN AUTHENTIC PRESENCE ON TIKTOK BY TAPPING INTO THE PLATFORM'S COMMUNITY OF CREATORS.
