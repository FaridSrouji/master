HUGE BROOKLYN APPOINTED AS AGENCY OF RECORD TO WORK ON TEZOS. BROOKLYN, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, GLOBAL EXPERIENCE AGENCY HUGE (NYSE: IPG) ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED AGENCY OF RECORD FOR THE TEZOS FOUNDATION. IN A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND APPOINTMENT, HUGE WILL WORK WITH THE TEZOS ECOSYSTEM TO HELP DEFINE GO TO MARKET STRATEGIES, BRAND EXPRESSIONS, DEVELOPER ENGAGEMENT, AND CREATE ENGAGING EXPERIENCES FOR COMMUNITY MEMBERS, DEVELOPERS, BUILDERS AND MEDIA.
