H&R BLOCK SELECTS CARMICHAEL LYNCH AS LEAD BRAND AGENCY. MINNEAPOLIS, AUG. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARMICHAEL LYNCH ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED THE STRATEGY AND CREATIVE LEAD FOR H&R BLOCK, THE ICONIC BRAND FOUNDED BY BROTHERS HENRY AND RICHARD BLOCH IN 1955. THE AGENCY WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE BRAND'S STRATEGY, CREATIVE AND SOCIAL MEDIA.
