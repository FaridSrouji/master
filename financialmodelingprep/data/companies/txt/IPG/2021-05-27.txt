INTERPUBLIC DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, MAY 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --   INTERPUBLIC GROUP (NYSE: IPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON IPG COMMON STOCK OF $0.27 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JUNE 21, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 7, 2021.
