BLACK & ABROAD LAUNCHES DATA-DRIVEN FOLLOW-UP TO ITS CELEBRATED "GO BACK TO AFRICA" CAMPAIGN.. NEW YORK, FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TWO YEARS AFTER ITS PROVOCATIVE AND ACCLAIMED PAN-AFRICA TOURISM CAMPAIGN "GO BACK TO AFRICA," TRAVEL BRAND BLACK & ABROAD HAS LAUNCHED A POWERFUL, DATA-DRIVEN DOMESTIC TRAVEL PLATFORM: "THE BLACK ELEVATION MAP". RESIDING AT BLACKELEVATIONMAP.COM , THE PLATFORM TAKES CULTURAL DATA, INCLUDING BLACK POPULATION DATA, HISTORICAL MARKERS, BLACK-OWNED BUSINESSES AND SOCIAL MEDIA ACTIVITY, AND VISUALIZES IT AS POINTS OF INTEREST ON A DYNAMIC, SEARCHABLE ELEVATION MAP OF THE UNITED STATES.
