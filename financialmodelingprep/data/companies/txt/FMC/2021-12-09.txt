FMC CORPORATION SIGNS AGREEMENTS WITH CORTEVA AGRISCIENCE TO PROVIDE GROWERS SEED TREATMENT INSECTICIDES. PHILADELPHIA, DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC), A LEADING GLOBAL AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY, ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENTS WITH CORTEVA AGRISCIENCE TO CONTINUE TO SUPPLY RYNAXYPYR® AND CYAZYPYR® ACTIVES TO CORTEVA FOR SEED TREATMENT PRODUCTS. THESE MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENTS EXTEND THE EXISTING GLOBAL COLLABORATION BETWEEN THE TWO COMPANIES.
