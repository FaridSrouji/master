FMC CORPORATION ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGE IN ASIA PACIFIC REGION. PHILADELPHIA , MAY 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC) HAS ANNOUNCED THAT BETHWYN TODD, FMC VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT OF FMC ASIA PACIFIC, HAS DECIDED TO LEAVE THE COMPANY FOR HEALTH REASONS.  PRAMOD THOTA, PRESIDENT OF FMC U.S.A.
