FMC CORPORATION BOARD ELECTS DR. BÉNÉDICTE FLAMBARD AS VICE PRESIDENT, PLANT HEALTH. PHILADELPHIA , OCT. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC), A LEADING GLOBAL AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY, HAS ELECTED DR. BÉNÉDICTE FLAMBARD AN FMC VICE PRESIDENT. SHE WILL JOIN THE COMPANY'S SENIOR MANAGEMENT OPERATING COMMITTEE AND WILL CONTINUE TO LEAD FMC'S PLANT HEALTH BUSINESS REPORTING TO RONALDO PEREIRA, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PRESIDENT OF FMC AMERICAS.
