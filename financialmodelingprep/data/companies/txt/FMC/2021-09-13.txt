COURT RULES IN FMC CORPORATION'S FAVOR IN PATENT INFRINGEMENT CASE AGAINST SHANDONG WEIFANG RAINBOW CHEMICAL CO. LTD.. PHILADELPHIA, SEPT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC) ANNOUNCED THAT QINGDAO INTERMEDIATE COURT IN CHINA HAS RULED IN ITS FAVOR IN THE CHLORANTRANILIPROLE PATENT INFRINGEMENT SUIT AGAINST SHANDONG WEIFANG RAINBOW CHEMICAL CO. LTD.
