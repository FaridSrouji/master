FMC CORPORATION OBTAINS PRE-SUIT INJUNCTION IN CHINA FOR PATENT INFRINGEMENT. PHILADELPHIA , MAY 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC), A LEADING GLOBAL AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY, ANNOUNCED THE NINGBO INTERMEDIATE PEOPLE'S COURT IN ZHEJIANG PROVINCE, CHINA, GRANTED FMC AGRO SINGAPORE PTE. LTD A PRE-SUIT PRELIMINARY INJUNCTION TO STOP INFRINGEMENT OF A CHLORANTRANILIPROLE PATENT.
