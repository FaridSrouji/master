FMC CORPORATION COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF BIOPHERO APS. PHILADELPHIA , JULY 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC), AN AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE CLOSING OF ITS ACQUISITION OF BIOPHERO APS, A DENMARK-BASED PHEROMONE RESEARCH AND PRODUCTION COMPANY. THE COMPANIES ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF A DEFINITIVE ACQUISITION AGREEMENT ON JUNE 29, 2022, AND HAVE NOW SATISFIED ALL NECESSARY CONDITIONS AND REGULATORY APPROVALS.
