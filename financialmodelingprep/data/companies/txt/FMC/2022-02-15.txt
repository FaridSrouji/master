FMC CORPORATION NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES BY BARRON'S. PHILADELPHIA, FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC), A LEADING GLOBAL AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY, HAS MADE BARRON'S ANNUAL LIST OF AMERICA'S MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES FOR THE FIRST TIME, AND IS THE ONLY AGRICULTURAL COMPANY INCLUDED ON THE LIST.  "SUSTAINABILITY HAS BECOME A KEY INDICATOR OF COMPANY PERFORMANCE, NOT JUST FOR FMC BUT FOR ANY COMPANY LOOKING TO COMPETE IN THE GLOBAL MARKETPLACE," SAID MARK DOUGLAS, PRESIDENT AND CEO.
