FMC RECOGNIZED BY CDP FOR LEADERSHIP IN CLIMATE ACTION AND WATER STEWARDSHIP. PHILADELPHIA , DEC. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC) FMC HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY CDP, THE GOLD STANDARD OF ENVIRONMENTAL REPORTING, AS A LEADER IN ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY, EARNING AN A- FOR CLIMATE CHANGE AND WATER SECURITY. THE SCORES REFLECT FMC'S AMBITIOUS ENVIRONMENTAL GOALS AS WELL AS THE COMPANY'S TRANSPARENCY AND MANAGEMENT OF CLIMATE-RELATED RISKS.
