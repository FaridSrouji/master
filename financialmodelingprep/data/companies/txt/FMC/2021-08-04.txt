FMC CORPORATION ANNOUNCES NET ZERO GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS BY 2035. PHILADELPHIA, AUG. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC), A LEADING GLOBAL AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY, ANNOUNCED ITS GOAL TO ACHIEVE NET-ZERO GREENHOUSE GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS BY 2035.  THE COMPANY WILL USE SCIENCE-BASED TARGETS ALIGNED WITH KEEPING THE GLOBAL TEMPERATURE AT 1.5°C ABOVE PRE-INDUSTRIAL TIMES.
