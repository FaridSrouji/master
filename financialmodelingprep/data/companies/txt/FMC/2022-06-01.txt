FMC CORPORATION PUBLISHES 11TH ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. FOR GENERATIONS TO COME PRESENTS THE COMPANY'S PROGRESS ON KEY GLOBAL COMMITMENTS PHILADELPHIA , JUNE 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC) TODAY PUBLISHED ITS 2021 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT, FOR GENERATIONS TO COME. IT PRESENTS THE COMPANY'S PROGRESS ON KEY GLOBAL COMMITMENTS INCLUDING NET-ZERO EMISSIONS, SUSTAINABLE INNOVATION, WORKFORCE DIVERSITY AND SAFETY.
