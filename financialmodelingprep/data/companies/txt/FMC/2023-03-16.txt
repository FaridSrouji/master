FMC CORPORATION AMONG JUST SIX COMPANIES IN THE WORLD TO HAVE ITS NET-ZERO TARGET BY 2035 VERIFIED BY THE SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE. WITH THIS APPROVAL FMC BECOMES: THE FIRST U.S. COMPANY TO HAVE AN APPROVED NET-ZERO TARGET BY 2035 THE FIRST CROP PROTECTION COMPANY GLOBALLY WITH AN APPROVED NET-ZERO TARGET ONE OF ONLY THREE CHEMICAL COMPANIES GLOBALLY WITH AN APPROVED NET-ZERO TARGET ALIGNED WITH 1.5°C PHILADELPHIA , MARCH 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC), A LEADING AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY, HAS HAD ITS NET-ZERO SCIENCE-BASED TARGET VERIFIED BY THE SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE (SBTI), MAKING IT THE FIRST U.S. COMPANY—AND ONE OF ONLY SIX COMPANIES IN THE WORLD—TO HAVE AN APPROVED TARGET TO BE ACHIEVED BY 2035. "FMC'S EMISSIONS REDUCTION TARGET IS AMONG THE MOST AGGRESSIVE TO BE VERIFIED BY THE SBTI," SAID MARK DOUGLAS, FMC PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
