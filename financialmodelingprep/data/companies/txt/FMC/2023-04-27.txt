FMC CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. PHILADELPHIA , APRIL 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 58 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JULY 20, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 30, 2023.   ABOUT FMC  FMC CORPORATION IS A GLOBAL AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY DEDICATED TO HELPING GROWERS PRODUCE FOOD, FEED, FIBER AND FUEL FOR AN EXPANDING WORLD POPULATION WHILE ADAPTING TO A CHANGING ENVIRONMENT.
