FMC CORPORATION MOST RESPONSIBLE CROP PROTECTION COMPANY IN THE U.S. ACCORDING TO NEWSWEEK. PHILADELPHIA, DEC. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC) WAS RECOGNIZED FOR ITS EXCELLENCE IN SUSTAINABILITY, RANKING HIGHER THAN ANY OTHER U.S. CROP PROTECTION COMPANY ON NEWSWEEK'S 2021 MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES LIST. "SUSTAINABILITY HAS LONG BEEN A CORE VALUE FOR FMC, BUT OVER THE PAST YEAR WE HAVE TAKEN GIANT STEPS TOWARD DEFINING A CLEAR STRATEGY TO ADDRESS MAJOR GLOBAL THREATS LIKE CLIMATE CHANGE, HUNGER AND MALNUTRITION," SAID KAREN TOTLAND, VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER FOR FMC.
