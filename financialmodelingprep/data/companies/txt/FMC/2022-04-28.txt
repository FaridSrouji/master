FMC CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. PHILADELPHIA , APRIL 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 53 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JULY 21, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 30, 2022. ABOUT FMC FMC CORPORATION IS A GLOBAL AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY DEDICATED TO HELPING GROWERS PRODUCE FOOD, FEED, FIBER AND FUEL FOR AN EXPANDING WORLD POPULATION WHILE ADAPTING TO A CHANGING ENVIRONMENT.
