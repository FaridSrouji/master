FMC CORP. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FMC CORP. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 0.85 COMPARED TO USD 0.36 A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.69 COMPARED TO USD 0.54 A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 3.88 COMPARED TO USD 2.76 A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 3.66 COMPARED TO USD 3.47 A YEAR AGO.
