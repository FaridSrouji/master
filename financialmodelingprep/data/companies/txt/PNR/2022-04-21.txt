PENTAIR REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENTAIR PLC (NYSE: PNR) TODAY ANNOUNCED FIRST QUARTER 2022 SALES OF $1.0 BILLION. SALES WERE UP 15 PERCENT COMPARED TO SALES FOR THE SAME PERIOD LAST YEAR. EXCLUDING CURRENCY TRANSLATION, ACQUISITIONS AND DIVESTITURES, CORE SALES GREW 12 PERCENT IN THE FIRST QUARTER. FIRST QUARTER 2022 EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS (“EPS”) WERE $0.71 COMPARED TO $0.78 IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021.
