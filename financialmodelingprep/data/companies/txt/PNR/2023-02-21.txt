PENTAIR ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENTAIR PLC (NYSE: PNR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL PAY A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.22 PER SHARE ON MAY 5, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 21, 2023. 2023 MARKS THE 47TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR THAT PENTAIR HAS INCREASED ITS DIVIDEND.
