PENTAIR NAMES TANYA HOOPER AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER. LONDON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PENTAIR PLC (NYSE: PNR), A LEADING PROVIDER OF WATER TREATMENT AND SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS NAMED TANYA HOOPER TO ITS EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2023. HOOPER WILL REPORT TO JOHN STAUCH, PENTAIR PRESIDENT AND CEO.
