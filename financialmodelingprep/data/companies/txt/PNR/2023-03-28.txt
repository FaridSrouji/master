PENTAIR EARNS 2023 ENERGY STAR® PARTNER OF THE YEAR – SUSTAINED EXCELLENCE AWARD. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENTAIR (NYSE: PNR), A LEADING PROVIDER OF WATER TREATMENT AND SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS, IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE IT RECEIVED THE 2023 ENERGY STAR® PARTNER OF THE YEAR – SUSTAINED EXCELLENCE AWARD FROM THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (EPA) AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY.
