SOCALGAS TO ASSIST LOCAL FLEET OWNERS IN SECURING INCENTIVE FUNDS TO TRANSITION TO CLEANER TECHNOLOGIES. LOS ANGELES, MARCH 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY OPENED ITS ANNUAL INITIATIVE TO ASSIST FLEET OWNERS OPERATING WITHIN THE SOUTH COAST AIR QUALITY MANAGEMENT DISTRICT (SCAQMD) APPLY FOR INCENTIVE FUNDING TO REPLACE DIESEL TRUCKS WITH NEW NEAR-ZERO EMISSIONS NATURAL GAS TRUCKS THROUGH THE CARL MOYER PROGRAM. APPROXIMATELY $36 MILLION IS AVAILABLE FOR ELIGIBLE CLEAN AIR PROJECTS WITHIN THE SCAQMD'S TERRITORY.
