SOCALGAS DECLARES PREFERRED DIVIDENDS. LOS ANGELES, AUG. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) HAS DECLARED REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS FOR THE PREFERRED SERIES STOCK OF THE COMPANY AS FOLLOWS: SOCALGAS: PREFERRED STOCK $0.375 PER SHARE PREFERRED STOCK, SERIES A $0.375 PER SHARE THE DIVIDENDS ARE PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON SEPTEMBER 10, 2021. ABOUT SOCALGAS HEADQUARTERED IN LOS ANGELES, SOCALGAS® IS THE LARGEST GAS DISTRIBUTION UTILITY IN THE UNITED STATES.
