SOCALGAS NAMES 2021 CLIMATE CHAMPIONS INITIATIVE GRANT RECIPIENTS. LOS ANGELES, FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO.  (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE WINNERS OF ITS 2021 CLIMATE CHAMPIONS INITIATIVE, WHICH AWARDS GRANTS OF UP TO $50,000 TO NONPROFITS THAT ENCOURAGE AND FOSTER CLEAN, SAFE, AND INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS TOWARDS A CLEAN ENERGY FUTURE. IN ALL, 10 NONPROFITS WERE SELECTED TO RECEIVE A TOTAL OF $400,000 IN FUNDING.
