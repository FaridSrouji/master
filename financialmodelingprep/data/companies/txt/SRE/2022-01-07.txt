SOCALGAS ANNOUNCES PARTNERSHIP WITH DOOSAN MOBILITY INNOVATION AND GTI TO LAUNCH HYDROGEN DRONE DEMONSTRATION. LOS ANGELES, JAN. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY JOINED DOOSAN MOBILITY INNOVATION (DMI) AND GTI AT THE CES LAS VEGAS SUMMIT TO ANNOUNCE THEIR PARTNERSHIP IN LAUNCHING DMI'S FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND HYDROGEN DRONE TECHNOLOGY. WITH THE SUPPORT OF SOCALGAS AND GTI, DMI WILL BE DEMONSTRATING THEIR DS30 DRONE SYSTEM, WHICH OFFERS THE BEST FUEL CELL POWERPACK PERFORMANCE WITH UP TO 120 MINUTES OF FLIGHT TIME AND UP TO 11 POUNDS MAXIMUM PAYLOAD.
