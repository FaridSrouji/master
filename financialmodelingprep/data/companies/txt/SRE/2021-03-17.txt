INDUSTRY-LEADING SOCALGAS ENERGY EFFICIENCY PROGRAMS SAVE 40 MILLION THERMS IN 2020. LOS ANGELES, MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT LAST YEAR, THE COMPANY'S ENERGY EFFICIENCY PROGRAMS HELPED CONSERVE MORE ENERGY THAN ANY OTHER NATURAL GAS UTILITY IN THE U.S. THE UTILITY INVESTS MORE IN ENERGY EFFICIENCY THAN ANY OTHER NATURAL GAS LOCAL DISTRIBUTION COMPANY IN THE COUNTRY AND CURRENTLY OPERATES THE LARGEST NATURAL GAS ENERGY EFFICIENCY PROGRAM. IN 2020, SOCALGAS' ENERGY EFFICIENCY PROGRAMS SAVED ENOUGH ENERGY TO POWER 100,000 HOMES IN SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA FOR ONE YEAR.
