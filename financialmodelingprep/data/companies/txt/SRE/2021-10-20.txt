SOCALGAS LAUNCHES 2021 SOCAL CLIMATE CHAMPIONS GRANT PROGRAM. LOS ANGELES, OCT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPLICATION WINDOW FOR ITS 2021 SOCAL CLIMATE CHAMPIONS GRANT PROGRAM IS NOW OPEN. THE PROGRAM PROVIDES GRANTS TO NON-PROFITS ORGANIZATIONS WORKING TO ADVANCING CLIMATE SOLUTIONS IN SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA.
