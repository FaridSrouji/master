SOCALGAS PARTNERS WITH THE RETIREMENT HOUSING FOUNDATION ON ENERGY EFFICIENCY UPGRADES AT SENIOR HOUSING COMPLEX, THE CONCORD. INCENTIVE FUNDING USED TO UPGRADE CENTRAL HOT WATER SYSTEM, SAVING OVER 4,000 THERMS OF ENERGY ANNUALLY LOS ANGELES , JUNE 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF ENERGY EFFICIENCY UPGRADES IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE RETIREMENT HOUSING FOUNDATION (RHF) AT THE CONCORD AFFORDABLE SENIOR LIVING COMMUNITY IN PASADENA, CALIF. THE PROJECT WAS MADE POSSIBLE THROUGH SOCALGAS' WHOLE BUILDING PROGRAM.
