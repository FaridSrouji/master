SOCALGAS TRIPLES CONTRIBUTION TO GAS ASSISTANCE FUND TO HELP CUSTOMERS IMPACTED BY HISTORICALLY HIGH NATURAL GAS PRICES. COMPANY ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS TO TAKE ADVANTAGE OF CONSERVATION TIPS AND PROGRAMS TO HELP REDUCE THEIR BILLS LOS ANGELES , JAN. 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED A $1 MILLION CONTRIBUTION TO THE GAS ASSISTANCE FUND, A PROGRAM THAT HELPS INCOME-QUALIFIED CUSTOMERS PAY THEIR NATURAL GAS BILLS. CUSTOMERS WILL BE FACING BILLS THAT COULD BE DOUBLE OR HIGHER COMPARED TO LAST YEAR'S WINTER BILLS.
