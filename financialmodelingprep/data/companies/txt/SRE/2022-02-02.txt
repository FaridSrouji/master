SEMPRA HONORED BY FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES LIST. SAN DIEGO, FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED AS ONE OF FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES FOR 2022. THIS IS THE 12TH TIME THAT SEMPRA HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY THIS ANNUAL LIST, WHICH HONORS GLOBAL BUSINESSES WITH STRONG CORPORATE REPUTATIONS ACROSS NINE KEY ATTRIBUTES, INCLUDING FINANCIAL SOUNDNESS, INVESTMENT VALUE, INNOVATION, SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY AND TALENT.
