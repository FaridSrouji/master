SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT WITH ENGIE FOR SUPPLY OF U.S. LNG FROM PORT ARTHUR LNG PHASE 1. HOUSTON , DEC. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE, A SUBSIDIARY OF SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO A LONG-TERM SALE AND PURCHASE AGREEMENT (SPA) WITH ENGIE S.A. FOR THE SUPPLY OF LIQUEFIED NATURAL GAS (LNG) FROM PHASE 1 OF ITS PROPOSED PORT ARTHUR LNG PROJECT UNDER DEVELOPMENT IN JEFFERSON COUNTY, TEXAS.
