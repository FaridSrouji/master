SEMPRA ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO SELL 10% INTEREST IN SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE PARTNERS. SAN DIEGO, DEC. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL A NON-CONTROLLING 10% INTEREST IN SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE PARTNERS (SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE) TO A SUBSIDIARY OF THE ABU DHABI INVESTMENT AUTHORITY (ADIA) FOR $1.785 BILLION IN CASH, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING ADJUSTMENTS. THIS TRANSACTION IMPLIES AN ENTERPRISE VALUE FOR SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE OF $26.5 BILLION, INCLUDING ASSET-RELATED DEBT OF APPROXIMATELY $8.6 BILLION.
