SEMPRA NAMED TO DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY NORTH AMERICA INDEX FOR 12TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. SAN DIEGO , DEC. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY NORTH AMERICA INDEX (DJSI NORTH AMERICA) FOR THE 12TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. THE COMPANY WAS RECOGNIZED AS A HIGH PERFORMER IN THE UTILITIES SECTOR AND ACHIEVED 100TH PERCENTILE SCORES IN THE POLICY INFLUENCE, INFORMATION SECURITY AND/OR CYBERSECURITY AND WATER-RELATED RISK CATEGORIES.
SDG&E WELL ON ITS WAY TO ACHIEVING A FULLY ZERO-EMISSIONS FLEET. SAN DIEGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TO ADVANCE ITS OWN AND CALIFORNIA'S GOALS TO REACH NET ZERO EMISSIONS BY 2045, SAN DIEGO GAS & ELECTRIC (SDG&E) HAS ELECTRIFIED MORE THAN 20% OF ITS OVER-THE-ROAD FLEET AND IS ON TRACK TO REACH 100% ELECTRIFICATION OF ITS PASSENGER CARS, PICKUP TRUCKS AND SPORT UTILITY VEHICLES BY 2030. THIS PUTS SDG&E WELL ON ITS WAY TO ACHIEVE ITS GOAL OF OPERATING A FULLY ZERO-EMISSIONS FLEET BY 2035, AHEAD OF STATE MANDATES.
