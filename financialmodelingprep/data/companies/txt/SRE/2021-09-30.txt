SOCALGAS AMONG FIRST IN THE NATION TO TEST HYDROGEN BLENDING IN REAL-WORLD INFRASTRUCTURE AND APPLIANCES IN CLOSED LOOP SYSTEM. LOS ANGELES, SEPT. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS BLENDING HYDROGEN TO FUEL A HOUSEHOLD SYSTEM AND APPLIANCES AT ITS ENGINEERING ANALYSIS CENTER AND CENTRALIZED TRAINING FACILITY.
