SOCALGAS RECEIVES SUSTAINABLE INNOVATION AWARD FROM U.S. GREEN BUILDING COUNCIL - LOS ANGELES FOR [H2] INNOVATION EXPERIENCE PROJECT. AWARD RECOGNIZES SOCALGAS' COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABILITY THROUGH PROJECTS THAT DEMONSTRATE EXEMPLARY PERFORMANCE. LOS ANGELES , DEC. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) HAS BEEN AWARDED THE U.S. GREEN BUILDING COUNCIL - LOS ANGELES' (USGBC-LA) SUSTAINABLE INNOVATION AWARD IN THE CATEGORY OF "ENERGY AND OPERATIONAL CARBON" FOR ITS SOON-TO-BE-COMPLETED [H2] INNOVATION EXPERIENCE MICROGRID DEMONSTRATION PROJECT IN DOWNEY, CALIFORNIA.
