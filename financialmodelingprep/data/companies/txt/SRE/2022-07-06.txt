NEW ELIGIBILITY GUIDELINES OPEN SOCALGAS ENERGY SAVINGS ASSISTANCE PROGRAM TO MORE CUSTOMERS. RECENTLY ENACTED CALIFORNIA LAW INCREASES CUSTOMER ACCESS TO NO-COST ENERGY-SAVING HOME IMPROVEMENTS WHICH SUPPORT SOCALGAS' EFFORTS TO LEAD THE TRANSITION TO A CLEAN ENERGY SYSTEM LOS ANGELES , JULY 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) ANNOUNCED THAT AS OF JULY 1, 2022, MORE CUSTOMERS WILL NOW BE ELIGIBLE FOR NO-COST ENERGY-SAVING HOME IMPROVEMENTS THROUGH THE ENERGY SAVINGS ASSISTANCE PROGRAM. THE NEW GUIDELINES EXPAND ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR CUSTOMERS WHOSE INCOME IS UP TO 250% OF FEDERAL POVERTY GUIDELINES, COMPARED TO THE PREVIOUS CRITERIA OF UP TO 200%.
