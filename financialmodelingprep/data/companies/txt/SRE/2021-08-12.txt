SEMPRA LAUNCHES CASH TENDER OFFER FOR REMAINING PUBLICLY HELD SHARES OF IENOVA. SAN DIEGO, AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS LAUNCHED A TENDER OFFER TO PURCHASE FOR CASH THE 3.6% OF THE OUTSTANDING SHARES OF IENOVA (INFRAESTRUCTURA ENERGÉTICA NOVA, S.A.B DE C.V.) THAT ARE NOT OWNED BY SEMPRA.
