SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE'S MEXICO SUBSIDIARY CREDIT RATING UPGRADED BY FITCH. SAN DIEGO, MARCH 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE, A MAJORITY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FITCH RATINGS ("FITCH") HAS UPGRADED ITS MEXICO SUBSIDIARY'S LONG-TERM FOREIGN AND LOCAL CURRENCY ISSUER DEFAULT RATING AS WELL AS ITS SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES TO 'BBB+' FROM 'BBB'. THE OUTLOOK REMAINS STABLE.
