SEMPRA RECOGNIZED AMONG WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES BY FORTUNE MAGAZINE. SAN DIEGO , FEB. 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES FOR 2023 BY FORTUNE MAGAZINE, MARKING THE 13TH YEAR THE COMPANY IS ON THIS PRESTIGIOUS LIST. FORTUNE'S ANNUAL LIST OF WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES RECOGNIZES GLOBAL BUSINESSES WITH STRONG CORPORATE REPUTATIONS.
