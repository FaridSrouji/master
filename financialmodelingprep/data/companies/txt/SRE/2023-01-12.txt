SEMPRA NAMED TO JUST 100 LIST FOR AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES OF 2023. SAN DIEGO , JAN. 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE PRESTIGIOUS JUST 100 LIST BY JUST CAPITAL, AN INDEPENDENT NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO MEASURING AND IMPROVING CORPORATE STAKEHOLDER PERFORMANCE. THE RANKINGS ARE INTENDED TO BE A COMPREHENSIVE EVALUATION OF HOW CORPORATIONS PERFORM ON ISSUES THAT MATTER MOST TO THE AMERICAN PUBLIC, SUCH AS JOB CREATION AND PAYING A FAIR, LIVING WAGE, ACTING ETHICALLY, LEADERSHIP INTEGRITY, ACCOUNTABILITY, WORKFORCE HEALTH AND SAFETY AND WORK-LIFE BALANCE.
