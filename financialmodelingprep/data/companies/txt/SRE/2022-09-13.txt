SOCALGAS AND LANDI RENZO USA CONVERT 200 ADDITIONAL FIELD SERVICE TRUCKS TO RUN ON RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS. APPROXIMATELY 44% OF THE UTILITY'S FLEET WILL RUN ON CLEAN FUELS LOS ANGELES , SEPT. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN ADDITIONAL MULTI-MILLION-DOLLAR COLLABORATION WITH LANDI RENZO USA FOR 200 NEW FORD F-250 SERVICE PICKUP TRUCKS TO JOIN THE UTILITIES FLEET.
