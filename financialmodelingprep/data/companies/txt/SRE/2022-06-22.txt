SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE AND INEOS ENERGY TRADING SIGN HEADS OF AGREEMENT FOR LNG SUPPLY. HOUSTON , JUNE 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE, A SUBSIDIARY OF SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE), AND INEOS ENERGY TRADING LTD., A SUBSIDIARY OF INEOS, A GLOBAL CHEMICAL PRODUCTS MANUFACTURER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE ENTERED INTO A HEADS OF AGREEMENT (HOA) FOR THE LONG-TERM SUPPLY OF LIQUEFIED NATURAL GAS (LNG) FROM SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE'S GULF COAST LNG PORTFOLIO OF PROJECTS UNDER DEVELOPMENT IN NORTH AMERICA.
