SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE ANNOUNCES SALE AND PURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH RWE FOR PORT ARTHUR LNG. HOUSTON , DEC. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE, A SUBSIDIARY OF SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO A LONG-TERM SALE AND PURCHASE AGREEMENT (SPA) WITH RWE SUPPLY & TRADING, A SUBSIDIARY OF RWE (RWE: AG), FOR THE SUPPLY OF APPROXIMATELY 2.25 MILLION TONNES PER ANNUM (MTPA) OF LIQUEFIED NATURAL GAS (LNG) FROM THE PORT ARTHUR LNG PHASE 1 PROJECT UNDER DEVELOPMENT IN JEFFERSON COUNTY, TEXAS. THE LNG WILL BE DELIVERED ON A FREE-ON-BOARD BASIS FOR 15 YEARS.
