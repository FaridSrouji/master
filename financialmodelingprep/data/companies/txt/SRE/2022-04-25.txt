SOCALGAS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER SCOTT DRURY JOINS PRESTIGIOUS WALL STREET JOURNAL CEO COUNCIL. DRURY JOINS INFLUENTIAL LEADERS OF COMPANIES FROM 25 DIFFERENT COUNTRIES LOS ANGELES , APRIL 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, SCOTT DRURY, WAS RECENTLY SELECTED TO JOIN THE WALL STREET JOURNAL'S CEO COUNCIL. THE WALL STREET JOURNAL CEO COUNCIL BRINGS TOGETHER THE MOST INFLUENTIAL DECISION MAKERS FROM AROUND THE WORLD.
