SEMPRA FOUNDATION DONATES MORE THAN $350,000 TO GRID ALTERNATIVES TO HELP ADVANCE ENERGY ACCESS IN TRIBAL COMMUNITIES. SAN DIEGO AND OAKLAND, CALIF., DEC. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA FOUNDATION, FOUNDED BY SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE), HAS DONATED MORE THAN $350,000 TO GRID ALTERNATIVES HTTPS://MOZCALTI.ORG/ES/ TO HELP ADVANCE ENERGY ACCESS IN CALIFORNIA TRIBAL COMMUNITIES BY DEVELOPING, FINANCING AND IMPLEMENTING SOLAR POWER PROJECTS.
