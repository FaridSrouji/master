SOCALGAS PARTNERS WITH PEOPLE ASSISTING THE HOMELESS (PATH) AND LA FAMILY HOUSING ON PROJECT HOMEKEY INITIATIVE. LOS ANGELES, NOV. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) PARTNERS WITHPEOPLE ASSISTING THE HOMELESS (PATH) AND LA FAMILY HOUSING (LAFH) IN A CONTINUOUS EFFORT TO SUPPORT PROJECT HOMEKEY, A STATEWIDE EFFORT BETWEEN LOS ANGELES COUNTY AND THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA TO RELOCATE UNHOUSED INDIVIDUALS INTO PERMANENT HOUSING. THE UTILITY HAS PROVIDED OVER $300,000 IN ASSISTANCE TOWARDS ENERGY EFFICIENCY PROGRAMS AND EQUIPMENT INSTALLATION IN OVER 650 PROJECT HOMEKEY UNITS.
