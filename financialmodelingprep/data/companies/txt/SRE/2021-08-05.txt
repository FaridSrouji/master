ONCOR REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. DALLAS, AUG. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ONCOR ELECTRIC DELIVERY COMPANY LLC ("ONCOR") TODAY REPORTED THREE MONTHS ENDED JUNE 30, 2021 NET INCOME OF $169 MILLION COMPARED TO NET INCOME OF $176 MILLION IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020. THE $7 MILLION QUARTER-OVER-QUARTER DECREASE IN NET INCOME WAS DRIVEN BY INCREASES IN OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE EXPENSE, COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH ADDITIONAL INVESTMENT (PRIMARILY DEPRECIATION AND AMORTIZATION AND PROPERTY TAXES), PARTIALLY OFFSET BY A NET INCREASE IN REVENUES CONTRIBUTING TO EARNINGS PRIMARILY FROM INCREASES IN BASE TRANSMISSION AND DISTRIBUTION RATES AND CUSTOMER GROWTH, NET OF LOWER CONSUMPTION PRIMARILY ATTRIBUTABLE TO MILDER WEATHER.
SEMPRA REPORTS SECOND-QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS RESULTS. SAN DIEGO, AUG. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED SECOND-QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS OF $424 MILLION, OR $1.37 PER DILUTED SHARE, COMPARED TO SECOND-QUARTER 2020 EARNINGS OF $2.239 BILLION, OR $7.61 PER DILUTED SHARE. ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS, THE COMPANY'S SECOND-QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS WERE $504 MILLION, OR $1.63 PER DILUTED SHARE, COMPARED TO $501 MILLION, OR $1.71 PER DILUTED SHARE, IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020.
