SOCALGAS BREAKS GROUND ON FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND TECHNOLOGY TO PRODUCE CLEAN HYDROGEN FOR SUNLINE TRANSIT AGENCY'S HYDROGEN FUEL CELL ELECTRIC BUSES. THE DEMONSTRATION RESEARCH PROJECT WILL PRODUCE HYDROGEN FROM RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS FOR FUEL CELL BUSES TO HELP REDUCE GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS LOS ANGELES , AUG. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OFFICIAL GROUNDBREAKING ON CONSTRUCTION OF A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND ADVANCED HYDROGEN GENERATION SYSTEM AT SUNLINE TRANSIT AGENCY IN THOUSAND PALMS, CALIFORNIA. THE PROJECT, CALLED H2 SILVERSTARS, WILL PRODUCE HYDROGEN FROM RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS (RNG) AND HELP FUEL SUNLINE'S FLEET OF 17 HYDROGEN FUEL CELL ELECTRIC BUSES.
