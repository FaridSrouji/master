SOCALGAS AMONG FIRST UTILITIES IN THE NATION TO TRANSITION ITS OVER-THE-ROAD FLEET WITH HYDROGEN FUEL CELL ELECTRIC VEHICLES. LOS ANGELES, JAN. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS COMPANY (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT TOOK DELIVERY OF 23 TOYOTA MIRAI HYDROGEN FUEL CELL ELECTRIC VEHICLES (HFCEV), MARKING THE COMPANY'S FIRST PURCHASE OF HYDROGEN-POWERED VEHICLES. THE COMPANY PLANS TO EXPAND ITS FLEET OF HFCEVS TO 50 NEXT MONTH, MAKING SOCALGAS AMONG THE FIRST UTILITIES IN THE NATION TO START TRANSITIONING TO HYDROGEN.
