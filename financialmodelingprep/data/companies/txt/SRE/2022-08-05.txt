SOCALGAS BREAKS GROUND ON MOBILEHOME PARK UTILITY CONVERSION PROJECT IN INDIO. PROGRAM PROVIDES MOBILE HOME PARK RESIDENTS WITH ENHANCED SAFETY, RELIABILITY, AND COST SAVINGS BY REPLACING PRIVATELY OWNED GAS SYSTEMS WITH DIRECT UTILITY SERVICE INDIO, CALIF. , AUG. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE BEGINNING OF CONSTRUCTION AT ARABIAN GARDENS MOBILE HOME PARK IN THE CITY OF INDIO TO PROVIDE SAFETY AND SERVICE UPGRADES THROUGH THE MOBILEHOME PARK UTILITY CONVERSION PROGRAM.
