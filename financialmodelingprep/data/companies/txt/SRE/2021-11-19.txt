SEMPRA NAMED TO DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX FOR FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. SAN DIEGO, NOV. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS AGAIN THE ONLY NORTH AMERICAN UTILITY SECTOR COMPANY TO BE NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX (DJSI WORLD), ONE OF THE MOST PRESTIGIOUS CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY RANKINGS. THIS IS THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR THAT THE COMPANY HAS BEEN LISTED ON THE DJSI WORLD INDEX.
