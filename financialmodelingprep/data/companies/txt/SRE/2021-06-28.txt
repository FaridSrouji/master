SOCALGAS AWARDS MORE THAN $235,000 IN SCHOLARSHIPS TO CENTRAL AND SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA STUDENTS. LOS ANGELES, JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE 2021 SOCALGAS SCHOLARSHIP RECIPIENTS. THIS YEAR, 56 STUDENTS FROM CENTRAL AND SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA WILL RECEIVE A SHARE OF THE MORE THAN $235,000 IN COLLEGE SCHOLARSHIPS.
