SEMPRA DECLARES COMMON DIVIDEND. SAN DIEGO , MAY 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A $1.19 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, WHICH IS PAYABLE JUL. 15, 2023, TO COMMON STOCK SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUL. 5, 2023. ABOUT SEMPRA SEMPRA IS A LEADING NORTH AMERICAN ENERGY INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY THAT HELPS MEET THE DAILY ENERGY NEEDS OF NEARLY 40 MILLION CONSUMERS.
