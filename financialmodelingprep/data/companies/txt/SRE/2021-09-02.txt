SEMPRA DECLARES COMMON AND PREFERRED DIVIDENDS. SAN DIEGO, SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A $1.10 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, WHICH IS PAYABLE OCT. 15, 2021, TO COMMON STOCK SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPT.
