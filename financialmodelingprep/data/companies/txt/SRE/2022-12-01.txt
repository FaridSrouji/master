SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE ANNOUNCES SALE AND PURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH INEOS FOR PORT ARTHUR LNG PHASE 1. HOUSTON , DEC. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE, A SUBSIDIARY OF SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO A LONG-TERM SALE AND PURCHASE AGREEMENT (SPA) WITH INEOS, FOR THE SUPPLY OF LIQUEFIED NATURAL GAS (LNG) FROM PHASE 1 OF ITS PORT ARTHUR LNG PROJECT UNDER DEVELOPMENT IN JEFFERSON COUNTY, TEXAS. UNDER THE SPA, INEOS HAS AGREED TO PURCHASE APPROXIMATELY 1.4 MILLION TONNES PER ANNUM (MTPA) OF LNG DELIVERED FREE-ON-BOARD FROM THE PROPOSED PHASE 1 LIQUEFACTION PROJECT FOR A TERM OF 20 YEARS.
