SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE ANNOUNCES FINAL EPC CONTRACT WITH BECHTEL FOR PORT ARTHUR LNG. HOUSTON , OCT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA INFRASTRUCTURE, A SUBSIDIARY OF SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PORT ARTHUR LNG AND BECHTEL ENERGY HAVE AMENDED AND RESTATED THE FIXED-PRICE ENGINEERING, PROCUREMENT AND CONSTRUCTION (EPC) CONTRACT, FOR THE PROPOSED PHASE 1 LIQUEFACTION PROJECT UNDER DEVELOPMENT IN JEFFERSON COUNTY, TEXAS. THE AMENDED CONTRACT INCLUDES AN UPDATED PRICE OF APPROXIMATELY $10.5 BILLION.
