SEMPRA RANKED TOP UTILITY ON WALL STREET JOURNAL'S BEST-MANAGED COMPANIES FOR 2022. THE MANAGEMENT TOP 250 IS ONE OF THE MOST PRESTIGIOUS RANKING EFFORTS DEDICATED TO MEASURING CORPORATE EFFECTIVENESS.
