SDG&E HYDROGEN PILOT PROJECT AIMS TO ADVANCE CA'S CLEAN ENERGY GOALS. SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS ANOTHER STEP TOWARD FULFILLING REGIONAL AND STATE CLIMATE GOALS AND ITS OWN NET ZERO ROADMAP, SAN DIEGO GAS & ELECTRIC COMPANY (SDG&E) SUBMITTED A PROPOSAL WITH THE CALIFORNIA PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION (CPUC) ON SEPT. 8 FOR A DEMONSTRATION PROJECT ON THE UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA SAN DIEGO (UC SAN DIEGO) CAMPUS TO STUDY HOW BLENDING HYDROGEN WITH NATURAL GAS IN THE EXISTING GAS SYSTEM COULD HELP ACHIEVE A SUCCESSFUL ENERGY TRANSITION FOR ALL CALIFORNIANS.
