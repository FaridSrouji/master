RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS PRODUCED BY ANAERGIA FROM DIVERTED LANDFILL WASTE NOW FLOWING THROUGH SOCALGAS SYSTEM. RIALTO, CALIF., MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) AND ANAERGIA, INC. (ANAERGIA) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS (RNG) PRODUCED FROM HOUSEHOLD WASTE IS NOW FLOWING INTO SOCALGAS' PIPELINE SYSTEM FROM THE RIALTO BIOENERGY FACILITY (FACILITY).
