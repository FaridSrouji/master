SEMPRA DECLARES COMMON DIVIDEND. SAN DIEGO , MAY 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA (NYSE: SRE) (BMV: SRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A $1.145 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, WHICH IS PAYABLE JULY 15, 2022, TO COMMON STOCK SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 7, 2022. ABOUT SEMPRA SEMPRA'S MISSION IS TO BE NORTH AMERICA'S PREMIER ENERGY INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY.
