SOCALGAS ANNOUNCES PLANS TO CONVERT 200 NEW FIELD SERVICE TRUCKS TO RUN ON RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS. LOS ANGELES, APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL CONVERT 200 NEW FORD F-250 SERVICE PICKUP TRUCKS TO RUN ON RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS (RNG) - A RENEWABLE FORM OF ENERGY PRODUCED FROM A VARIETY OF WASTE STREAMS. THE SERVICE TRUCKS WILL BE OUTFITTED WITH THE NEWEST LANDI RENZO ECO READY™ EQUIPMENT, A CALIFORNIA AIR RESOURCE BOARD CERTIFIED ULTRA-LOW EMISSIONS VEHICLES SYSTEM.
