LYONDELLBASELL ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON AND LONDON, NOV. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $1.13 PER SHARE, TO BE PAID DECEMBER 6, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD NOVEMBER 29, 2021, WITH AN EX-DIVIDEND DATE OF NOVEMBER 26, 2021. ABOUT LYONDELLBASELL LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) IS ONE OF THE LARGEST PLASTICS, CHEMICALS AND REFINING COMPANIES IN THE WORLD.
