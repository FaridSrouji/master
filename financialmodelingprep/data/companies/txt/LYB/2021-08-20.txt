LYONDELLBASELL ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON AND LONDON, AUG. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB), ONE OF THE LARGEST PLASTICS, CHEMICALS AND REFINING COMPANIES IN THE WORLD, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $1.13 PER SHARE, TO BE PAID SEPTEMBER 7, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AUGUST 30, 2021, WITH AN EX-DIVIDEND DATE OF AUGUST 27, 2021.
