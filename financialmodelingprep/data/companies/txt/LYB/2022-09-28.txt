LYONDELLBASELL POSTPONES RESTART OF BERRE, FRANCE CRACKER UNTIL NEXT YEAR. HOUSTON AND BERRE, FRANCE , SEPT. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ETHYLENE CRACKER AT ITS INTEGRATED OLEFINS AND POLYOLEFINS PRODUCTION SITE IN BERRE, FRANCE WILL NOT RESTART UNTIL EARLY 2023.
