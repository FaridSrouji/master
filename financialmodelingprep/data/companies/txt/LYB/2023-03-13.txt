LYONDELLBASELL ANNOUNCES DECISION TO ACQUIRE MEPOL GROUP. FACILITIES IN POLAND AND ITALY WILL PRODUCE RECYCLED AND POLYMER COMPOUNDS FOR DIVERSE MARKETS ROTTERDAM, NETHERLANDS AND FERRARA, ITALY , MARCH 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) AND MEPOL GROUP TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT FOR LYONDELLBASELL TO ACQUIRE MEPOL GROUP, A MANUFACTURER OF RECYCLED, HIGH-PERFORMING TECHNICAL COMPOUNDS LOCATED IN ITALY AND POLAND, CONSISTING OF MEPOL S.R.L. AND ITS SUBSIDIARIES POLAR S.R.L.
