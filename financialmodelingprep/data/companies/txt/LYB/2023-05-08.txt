LYONDELLBASELL INTRODUCES GREEN FINANCING FRAMEWORK TO HELP FURTHER SUSTAINABILITY GOALS. HOUSTON , MAY 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS PUBLISHED ITS INAUGURAL GREEN FINANCING FRAMEWORK TO HELP ADVANCE ITS LONG-TERM SUSTAINABILITY GOALS. "OUR GREEN FINANCING FRAMEWORK REFLECTS THE DECISIVE STEPS WE ARE TAKING TO ALLOCATE CAPITAL TOWARD INITIATIVES ALIGNED WITH OUR SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY," SAID PETER VANACKER, LYONDELLBASELL CEO.
