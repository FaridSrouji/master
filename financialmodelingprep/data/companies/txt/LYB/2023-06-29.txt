LYONDELLBASELL INVESTS IN CARBON NEUTRALITY VENTURE FUND. ROTTERDAM, NETHERLANDS , JUNE 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INVESTMENT IN A NEW CARBON NEUTRALITY FUND LED BY CHRYSALIX VENTURE CAPITAL. THE FUND WILL SUPPORT EMERGING COMPANIES DEVELOPING AND DEPLOYING NEW LOW-CARBON TECHNOLOGIES, FOCUSING ON HARD-TO-ABATE SECTORS, SUCH AS THE CHEMICALS INDUSTRY.
