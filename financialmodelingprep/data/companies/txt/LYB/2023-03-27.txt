LYONDELLBASELL AND EEW SIGN LOI FOR POTENTIAL ADVANCED WASTE SORTING. ROTTERDAM, NETHERLANDS AND HELMSTEDT, GERMANY , MARCH 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL, A GLOBAL LEADER IN THE CHEMICAL INDUSTRY, AND EEW ENERGY FROM WASTE, A LEADING COMPANY IN THERMAL WASTE TREATMENT IN EUROPE RECENTLY SIGNED A LETTER OF INTENT (LOI). THE LOI INCLUDES EXPLORING A POTENTIAL LONG-TERM STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO EXTRACT AND RECYCLE PLASTICS OUT OF INCINERATION WASTE STREAMS.
