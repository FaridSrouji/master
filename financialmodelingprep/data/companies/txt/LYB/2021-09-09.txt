LYONDELLBASELL GAINS ISCC PLUS CERTIFICATION TO EXTEND CIRCULEN PRODUCT LINE TO NORTH AMERICA. HOUSTON, SEPT. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY HAS OBTAINED THE INTERNATIONAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CARBON CERTIFICATION (ISCC) PLUS CERTIFICATION FOR CERTAIN GRADES OF POLYETHYLENE AND POLYPROPYLENE PRODUCED AT FOUR OF ITS U.S. MANUFACTURING SITES.
