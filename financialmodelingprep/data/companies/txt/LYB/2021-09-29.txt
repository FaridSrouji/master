LYONDELLBASELL ANNOUNCES GOAL OF ACHIEVING NET ZERO EMISSIONS BY 2050. COMPANY ANNOUNCES A 30 PERCENT ABSOLUTE REDUCTION IN EMISSIONS AND A GOAL OF 50 PERCENT OF ELECTRICITY FROM RENEWABLE ENERGY BY 2030 HOUSTON AND LONDON, SEPT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS AMBITION OF, AND APPROACH TO, ACHIEVING NET ZERO EMISSIONS FROM GLOBAL OPERATIONS BY 2050.
