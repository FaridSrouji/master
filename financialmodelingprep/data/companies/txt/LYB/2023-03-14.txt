LYONDELLBASELL COMMISSIONS WORLD'S LARGEST PO/TBA UNIT. HOUSTON , MARCH 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) TODAY ANNOUNCES IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY STARTED UP THE WORLD'S LARGEST PROPYLENE OXIDE (PO) AND TERTIARY BUTYL ALCOHOL (TBA) UNIT IN TEXAS. THESE NEW ASSETS ON THE U.S. GULF COAST HAVE AN ANNUAL CAPACITY OF 470 THOUSAND METRIC TONS OF PO AND ONE MILLION METRIC TONS OF TBA AND ITS DERIVATIVES.
