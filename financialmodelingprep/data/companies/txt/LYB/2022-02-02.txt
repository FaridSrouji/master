LYONDELLBASELL NAMED TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S "WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES" LIST FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. HOUSTON, FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S 2022 LIST OF THE "WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES." LYONDELLBASELL HAS RECEIVED THIS DISTINCTION FOR FIVE CONSECUTIVE YEARS.
