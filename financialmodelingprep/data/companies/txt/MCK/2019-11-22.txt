ADAPTHEALTH SIGNS DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE PATIENT CARE SOLUTIONS BUSINESS FROM MCKESSON CORPORATION. ADAPTHEALTH SIGNS DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE PATIENT CARE SOLUTIONS BUSINESS FROM MCKESSON CORPORATION.ADAPTHEALTH CORP - FOR TRAILING TWELVE MONTHS ENDED OCTOBER 31, 2019, PCS GENERATED NET REVENUES OF APPROXIMATELY $134 MILLION.ADAPTHEALTH CORP - WILL ENTER INTO A SUPPLY AND DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT WITH MCKESSON CORPORATION WITH RESPECT TO PCS'S HME SERVICE LINES.ADAPTHEALTH - TOTAL INVESTMENT FOR TRANSACTION TO ACQUIRE PATIENT CARE SOLUTIONS BUSINESS TO BE ABOUT $30 MILLION.
