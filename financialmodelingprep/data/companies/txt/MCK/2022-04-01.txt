MCKESSON'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS ELECTS W. ROY DUNBAR AS NEW DIRECTOR. IRVING, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MCKESSON CORPORATION (NYSE: MCK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE MCKESSON BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED W. ROY DUNBAR AS DIRECTOR AND MEMBER OF THE AUDIT AND GOVERNANCE COMMITTEES, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1, 2022.
