CAMPBELL REPORTS THIRD-QUARTER FISCAL 2023 RESULTS. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE:CPB) TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR ITS THIRD-QUARTER FISCAL 2023 ENDED APRIL 30, 2023. CEO COMMENTS “OUR THIRD-QUARTER RESULTS WERE IN LINE WITH OUR EXPECTATIONS AND WERE DRIVEN BY IN-MARKET MOMENTUM, CONTINUED BEST-IN-CLASS SUPPLY CHAIN EXECUTION AND FAVORABLE INFLATION-DRIVEN NET PRICE REALIZATION, ALL DESPITE THE ANTICIPATED CHALLENGING COMPARISON FROM THE PRIOR YEAR'S RETAILER INVENTORY REBUILD,” SAID CAMPBELL'S PRESIDENT AND CEO, MARK.
