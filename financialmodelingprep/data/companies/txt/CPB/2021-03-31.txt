SUN-MAID GROWERS OF CALIFORNIA ACQUIRES PLUM ORGANICS FROM CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY. TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION WERE NOT DISCLOSED. PLUM OFFERS A DIVERSE PORTFOLIO OF ORGANIC FOODS AND SNACKS TO MEET THE NUTRITIONAL NEEDS OF BABIES, TOTS AND KIDS.
