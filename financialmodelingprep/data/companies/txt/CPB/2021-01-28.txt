CAMPBELL BOARD ELECTS GRANT H. HILL AS DIRECTOR. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE:CPB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED GRANT H. HILL AS A MEMBER OF THE BOARD. HILL, 48, IS A CO-OWNER AND VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE ATLANTA HAWKS PROFESSIONAL BASKETBALL TEAM. “WE ARE EXCITED TO WELCOME GRANT TO CAMPBELL'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS,” SAID KEITH MCLOUGHLIN, CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD. “GRANT HAS ACHIEVED SUCCESS AT EVERY LEVEL. HIS UNIQUE SKILL SET IN BUILDING SUCCESSFUL BRANDS AND BUSINESSES, LEADING INC
