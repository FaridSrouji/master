CAMPBELL DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CAMDEN, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE:CPB) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON CAMPBELL'S CAPITAL STOCK OF $0.37 PER SHARE. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JAN. 30, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS AS OF JAN. 5, 2023.
