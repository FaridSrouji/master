CAMPBELL SOUP SAYS EXTENDING PREMIUM PAYMENTS TO ITS ABOUT 11,000 FRONT-LINE TEAM MEMBERS. CAMPBELL SOUP - EXTENDING PREMIUM PAYMENTS TO ITS ABOUT 11,000 FRONT-LINE TEAM MEMBERS WHO MAKE, SELL, & DISTRIBUTE PRODUCTS TO RETAIL CUSTOMERS.CAMPBELL SOUP CO - THE PREMIUM PAYMENTS WILL BE EXTENDED THROUGH MAY 16/17.CAMPBELL SOUP CO - PAYMENTS INCLUDE A $2 PER-HOUR PREMIUM PAYMENT FOR HOURLY EMPLOYEES AND TEMP AGENCY EMPLOYEES.CAMPBELL SOUP CO - PAYMENTS INCLUDE A $100 PER-WEEK PREMIUM PAYMENT FOR FRONT-LINE SUPERVISORS, CERTAIN SITE STAFF AND DEPOT MANAGERS.CAMPBELL SOUP CO - FOR FOUR WEEKS ENDING MARCH 29 CAMPBELL'S CONDENSED SOUP GAINED 5.2 MILLION NEW HOUSEHOLDS.CAMPBELL SOUP CO - TOTAL MEALS & BEVERAGES SEGMENT INCREASED 34.4% FOR FOUR WEEKS, ENDING APRIL 19.
