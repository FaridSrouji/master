INSTACART AND CAMPBELL PARTNER TO RECOGNIZE INSTACART SHOPPER COMMUNITY. SAN FRANCISCO, DEC. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INSTACART, THE LEADING ONLINE GROCERY PLATFORM IN NORTH AMERICA, IS PARTNERING WITH CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY TO CELEBRATE THE INSTACART SHOPPER COMMUNITY DURING THE HOLIDAY SEASON. IN APPRECIATION OF THE SHOPPER COMMUNITY'S EFFORTS TO DELIVER GROCERIES AND EVERYDAY ESSENTIALS TO FAMILIES AND HOUSEHOLDS ACROSS THE COUNTRY, CAMPBELL IS PROVIDING $50,000 IN PRIZES FOR INSTACART SHOPPERS AS PART OF THE FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND SOUPER SHOPPER SWEEPSTAKES.
