CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY LAUNCHES PROGRAM TO ADVANCE SCHOOL NUTRITION. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE: CPB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF FULL FUTURES, A PROGRAM TO FOSTER A SCHOOL NUTRITION ENVIRONMENT THAT ENSURES ALL STUDENTS ARE WELL-NOURISHED AND READY TO THRIVE AT SCHOOL AND IN LIFE. CAMPBELL PLANS TO INVEST $5 MILLION OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS AND IS JOINED BY A NUMBER OF NON-PROFIT AND CORPORATE PARTNERS.
