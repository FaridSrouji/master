CAMPBELL DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE:CPB) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON CAMPBELL'S CAPITAL STOCK OF $0.37 PER SHARE. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 31, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS AS OF JULY 6, 2023.
