CAMPBELL NAMES CHARLES NEVIN CHIEF CUSTOMER OFFICER OF MEALS & BEVERAGES. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE:CPB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CHARLES NEVIN AS CHIEF CUSTOMER OFFICER, MEALS & BEVERAGES, EFFECTIVE NOV. 29, 2021. NEVIN SUCCEEDS PIERSON, WHO WAS PROMOTED FROM SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, SALES, MEALS & BEVERAGES TO CHIEF CUSTOMER OFFICER OF THE SNACKS DIVISION IN AUGUST 2021.
