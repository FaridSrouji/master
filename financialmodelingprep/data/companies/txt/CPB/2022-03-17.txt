CAMPBELL APPOINTS KATE BARRETT TO LEAD COMMUNITY AFFAIRS. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE:CPB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF KATE BARRETT AS DIRECTOR, COMMUNITY AFFAIRS, EFFECTIVE MAY 6, 2022. BARRETT WILL SUCCEED KIM FORTUNATO WHO LAST YEAR INFORMED THE COMPANY OF HER INTENT TO RETIRE. FORTUNATO WILL REMAIN WITH CAMPBELL UNTIL MAY 6, 2022 TO ENSURE A SMOOTH TRANSITION.
