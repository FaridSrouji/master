CAMPBELL ANNOUNCES NEW ROUND OF GRANTS, BRINGING FISCAL 2022 TOTAL TO OVER $2 MILLION. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE:CPB) AND THE CAMPBELL SOUP FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW ROUND OF 42 COMMUNITY IMPACT GRANTS TOTALING NEARLY $1 MILLION TO ORGANIZATIONS MAKING AN IMPACT IN THE COMMUNITIES WHERE CAMPBELL HAS OPERATIONS. THE GRANTS PROVIDE SUPPORT TO NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS OPERATING IN CAMPBELL COMMUNITIES WHOSE WORK ALIGNS WITH ONE OR MORE OF THE FOCUS AREAS OF THE FOUNDATION: INCREASING FOOD ACCESS, ENCOURAGING HEALTHY LIVING AND NURTURING CAMPBEL
