CAMPBELL APPOINTS STEWART LINDSAY VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY AND SUSTAINABILITY. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE:CPB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF STEWART LINDSAY AS VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY AND SUSTAINABILITY, EFFECTIVE DEC. 6, 2021. HE JOINS CAMPBELL FROM THE NATURE CONSERVANCY. LINDSAY WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE COMPANY'S CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY AND SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY AND PROGRAMS.
