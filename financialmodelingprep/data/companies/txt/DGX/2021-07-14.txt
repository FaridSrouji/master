QUEST DIAGNOSTICS NAMED A "BEST PLACE TO WORK FOR DISABILITY INCLUSION" FOR FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. SECAUCUS, N.J., JULY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE 2021 DISABILITY EQUALITY INDEX® (DEI) BEST PLACES TO WORK FOR DISABILITY INCLUSION BY DISABILITY: IN AND THE AMERICAN ASSOCIATION OF PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES (AAPD).
