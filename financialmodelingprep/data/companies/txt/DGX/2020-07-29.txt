FDA AUTHORIZES NEW QUEST DIAGNOSTICS LAB METHOD DESIGNED TO INCREASE COVID-19 MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTICS CAPACITY. FDA AUTHORIZES NEW QUEST DIAGNOSTICS LAB METHOD DESIGNED TO INCREASE COVID-19 MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTICS CAPACITY.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS INC - CURRENTLY HAS CAPACITY TO PERFORM 135,000 COVID-19 MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTIC TESTS A DAY.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS INC - NEW METHOD IS EXPECTED TO ADD AN ADDITIONAL 35,000 TESTS A DAY IN OVERALL CAPACITY OVER NEXT SEVERAL WEEKS.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS INC - NOW EXPECTS TO HAVE CAPACITY TO PERFORM 150,000 TESTS PER DAY BY NEXT WEEK.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS INC - NOW EXPECTS TO CONTINUE TO BUILD ADDITIONAL CAPACITY BEYOND THAT TO 185,000 TESTS PER DAY BY LABOR DAY.
