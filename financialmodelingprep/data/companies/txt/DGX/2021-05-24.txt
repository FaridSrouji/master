SOCIAL & HEALTH RESEARCH CENTER AND QUEST DIAGNOSTICS TEAM UP TO ADDRESS CHILDHOOD HUNGER AND OBESITY EXACERBATED BY COVID-19 IN UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES. SAN ANTONIO AND SECAUCUS, N.J., MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHILDHOOD OBESITY HAS DISPROPORTIONATELY AFFECTED HISPANIC AND BLACK COMMUNITIES AS A RESULT OF NEGATIVE SOCIAL DETERMINANTS OF HEALTH FOR YEARS.
