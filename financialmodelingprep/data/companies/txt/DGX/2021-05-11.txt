QUEST DIAGNOSTICS EXPANDS ELIGIBILITY FOR $0 OUT-OF-POCKET COVID-19 TESTING THROUGH QUESTDIRECT™ TO ASSIST IN COMBATTING THE PANDEMIC AND HELP SUPPORT SAFER TRAVEL, EVENTS AND GATHERINGS NATIONWIDE. SECAUCUS, N.J., MAY 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, TODAY UNVEILED SEVERAL NEW ENHANCEMENTS TO ITS COVID-19 TESTING SERVICES, WITH THE GOAL TO HELP COMBAT COVID-19 WHILE EMPOWERING MORE INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES TO RESUME "BACK TO LIFE" ACTIVITIES WITH GREATER SAFETY THIS SPRING, SUMMER AND BEYOND.
