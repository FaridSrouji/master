QUEST DIAGNOSTICS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SECAUCUS, N.J., AUG. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.62 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 20, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD OF QUEST DIAGNOSTICS COMMON STOCK ON OCTOBER 5, 2021.
