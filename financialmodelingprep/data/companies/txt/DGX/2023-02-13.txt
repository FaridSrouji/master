QUEST DIAGNOSTICS TO HOST INVESTOR DAY ON MARCH 16, 2023. SECAUCUS, N.J. , FEB. 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS  (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, WILL HOST AN INVESTOR DAY FOR INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS AND FINANCIAL ANALYSTS IN NEW YORK CITY ON THURSDAY, MARCH 16, 2023 AT THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE.
