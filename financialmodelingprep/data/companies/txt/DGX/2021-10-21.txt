QUEST DIAGNOSTICS REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS, RAISES OUTLOOK FOR FULL YEAR 2021. SECAUCUS, N.J., OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS INCORPORATED (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, ANNOUNCED TODAY FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2021.
