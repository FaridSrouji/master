QUEST DIAGNOSTICS COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF NORTHERN LIGHT LABORATORY IN MAINE. SECAUCUS, N.J. , MARCH 20, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE NATION'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF SELECT ASSETS OF NORTHERN LIGHT LABORATORY, THE OUTREACH LABORATORY SERVICES BUSINESS OF NORTHERN LIGHT HEALTH, A LARGE INTEGRATED HEALTHCARE SYSTEM IN MAINE.
