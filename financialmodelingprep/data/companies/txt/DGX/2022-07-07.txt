QUEST DIAGNOSTICS PARTNERS WITH CDC TO EXTEND $0 OUT-OF-POCKET COVID-19 DIAGNOSTIC TESTING TO UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES. QUALIFIED UNINSURED INDIVIDUALS NOW HAVE ACCESS TO THE COMPANY'S COVID-19 MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTIC TESTING AT APPROXIMATELY 1,200 QUEST DIAGNOSTICS PATIENT SERVICE CENTERS SECAUCUS, N.J. , JULY 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS NOW A PARTICIPATING PARTNER IN THE CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION'S (CDC) INCREASING COMMUNITY ACCESS TO TESTING (ICATT) FOR COVID-19 PROGRAM, WHICH IS DESIGNED TO SUPPORT FREE COVID-19 LABORATORY TESTING FOR PATIENTS IN U.S. COMMUNITIES THAT HAVE BEEN DISPROPORTIONATELY AFFECTED BY THE PANDEMIC.
