QUEST DIAGNOSTICS TO ACQUIRE OUTREACH LAB SERVICES BUSINESS OF SUMMA HEALTH. TRANSACTION TO BROADEN ACCESS TO INNOVATIVE, QUALITY DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES POWERING AFFORDABLE CARE IN OHIO SECAUCUS, N.J., AND AKRON, OHIO , OCT. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, AND SUMMA HEALTH, A LARGE INTEGRATED HEALTHCARE DELIVERY SYSTEM IN OHIO, TODAY ANNOUNCED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT FOR QUEST TO ACQUIRE SELECT ASSETS OF SUMMA HEALTH'S OUTREACH LABORATORY SERVICES BUSINESS, REFERRED TO AS LABCARE PLUS, IN AN ALL-CASH TRANSACTION.
