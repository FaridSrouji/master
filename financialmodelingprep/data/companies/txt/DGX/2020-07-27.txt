DURING THE PAST WEEK SURPASSED 9.2 MILLION COVID-19 MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTIC TEST RESULTS DELIVERED TO DATE.CURRENTLY HAVE CAPACITY TO PERFORM AS MANY AS 135,000 MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTIC TESTS A DAY.WORKING TO INCREASE CAPACITY TO 150,000 COVID-19 MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTIC TESTS A DAY BY NEXT WEEK.DURING THE PAST WEEK, SAW TEST CAPACITY SLIGHTLY EXCEED DEMAND FOR THE FIRST TIME SINCE EARLY JUNE.MOST SIGNIFICANT HURDLE CO FACES NOW IS LIMITS TO COMPLEX TESTING PLATFORMS & CHEMICAL REAGENTS REQUIRED TO PERFORM TESTING.EXPECT AS CAPACITY GROWS, WILL BE ABLE TO RETURN TO AVERAGE TURNAROUND TIMES IN RANGE OF 1 DAY FOR PRIORITY 1 PATIENTS.AVERAGE TURNAROUND TIME FOR REPORTING TEST RESULTS IS NOW OVER TWO DAYS FOR PRIORITY 1 PATIENTS & 7 DAYS FOR ALL OTHER PATIENTS.RATE OF INCOMING SPECIMENS INTO OUR LABORATORIES HAS HELD STEADY OVER APPROXIMATELY PAST 2 WEEKS.EXPECT AS CAPACITY GROWS, WILL BE ABLE TO RETURN TO AVERAGE TURNAROUND TIME OF ABOUT 3 DAYS FOR MOST NON-PRIORITY 1 PATIENTS.HAVE STEADILY ADDED CAPACITY SINCE WE BEGAN TO PROVIDE SERVICES IN EARLY MARCH, BUT IT WILL TAKE TIME TO ADD MORE.
