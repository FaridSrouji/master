QUEST DIAGNOSTICS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SECAUCUS, N.J. , AUG. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.66 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 19, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD OF QUEST DIAGNOSTICS COMMON STOCK ON OCTOBER 4, 2022.
