QUEST DIAGNOSTICS NAMES SAM SAMAD EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. SECAUCUS, N.J. , JUNE 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT SAM SAMAD WILL BECOME EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.
