FORTUNE MAGAZINE NAMES QUEST DIAGNOSTICS ONE OF 2023 "WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES" FOR NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. SECAUCUS, N.J. , FEB. 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN SELECTED AS ONE OF FORTUNE'S WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES IN 2023 FOR THE NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
