QUEST DIAGNOSTICS RECEIVES PERFECT SCORE ON HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION'S CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX FOR SIXTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. SECAUCUS, N.J., JAN. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED A SCORE OF 100% ON THE HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION'S 2022 CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX (CEI), THE NATION'S FOREMOST BENCHMARKING SURVEY AND REPORT MEASURING CORPORATE POLICIES AND PRACTICES RELATED TO LGBTQ+ WORKPLACE EQUALITY.
