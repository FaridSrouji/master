DUPONT UNVEILS CUTTING-EDGE SEALING SOLUTIONS FOR OIL & GAS INDUSTRY AT CIPPE 2023. BEIJING , MAY 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD), A PREMIER MULTI-INDUSTRIAL COMPANY, IS EXCITED TO ANNOUNCE ITS PARTICIPATION IN THE 23RD CHINA INTERNATIONAL PETROLEUM & PETROCHEMICAL TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT EXHIBITION (CIPPE). THIS MARKS THE FIRST TIME THAT DUPONT WILL EXHIBIT AT THE EVENT WHERE IT WILL INTRODUCE ITS CUTTING-EDGE SOLUTIONS, INCLUDING DUPONT™ KALREZ® PERFLUOROELASTOMER PARTS AND DUPONT™ VESPEL® PARTS AND SHAPES FOR THE OIL AND GAS INDUSTRY.
