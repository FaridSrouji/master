BROADPEAK GLOBAL LP ANNOUNCES CHANGE OF NAME; REBRANDED AS BPGC MANAGEMENT LP. NEW YORK, JAN. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADPEAK GLOBAL LP TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS REBRANDING TO BPGC MANAGEMENT LP ("BPGC"), WITH THE CHANGE OF NAME EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.  THE FIRM WAS ESTABLISHED IN MAY 2020 BY CO-FOUNDERS STEPHEN TOY AND NADIM QURESHI, AND INCLUDES PRINCIPALS ADEMOLA ADEWALE-SADIK AND NICK PETERSON.
