DUPONT INTRODUCES SOLDERON™ BP TS 7000 CHEMISTRY AS NEWEST ADDITION TO THE TIN-SILVER PLATING BATH FAMILY. NEWLY REDESIGNED LEAD-FREE FORMULATION DELIVERS INDUSTRY-LEADING PLATING RATES, ENHANCED PLATING PERFORMANCE, BATH STABILITY AND GREATER PROCESS FLEXIBILITY FOR ADVANCED PACKAGING APPLICATIONS WILMINGTON, DEL. , MAY 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT ELECTRONICS & INDUSTRIAL TODAY INTRODUCED DUPONT™ SOLDERON™ BP TS 7000, THE LATEST INNOVATION IN TIN-SILVER PLATING CHEMISTRY, AT THE IEEE ELECTRONIC COMPONENTS AND TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE (ECTC) IN ORLANDO, FLORIDA.
