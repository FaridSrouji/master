DUPONT COMPLETES DIVESTITURE OF BIOMATERIALS BUSINESS. WILMINGTON, DEL. , JUNE 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED COMPLETION OF THE PREVIOUSLY-ANNOUNCED SALE OF ITS BIOMATERIALS BUSINESS UNIT, EFFECTIVE MAY 31, 2022, TO THE HUAFON GROUP FOR A PURCHASE PRICE OF APPROXIMATELY $240 MILLION.
