DUPONT ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. WILMINGTON, DEL. , APRIL 19, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A SECOND QUARTER DIVIDEND OF THIRTY-SIX CENTS ($0.36) PER SHARE ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY (PAR VALUE $0.01 PER SHARE) PAYABLE ON JUNE 15, 2023, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD OF SAID STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 31, 2023.
