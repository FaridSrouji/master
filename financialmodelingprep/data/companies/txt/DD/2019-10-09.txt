DUPONT DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 13, 2019. DUPONT'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 13, 2019, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD OF SAID STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 29, 2019.
