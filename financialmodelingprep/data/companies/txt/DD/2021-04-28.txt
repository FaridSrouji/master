DUPONT ANNOUNCES $20 MILLION INVESTMENT IN LISC'S BLACK ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT FUND. WILMINGTON, DEL., APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMMITTED TO INVEST $20 MILLION IN THE BLACK ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT FUND (BEDF), MANAGED BY THE LOCAL INITIATIVES SUPPORT CORPORATION (LISC).
DUPONT ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. WILMINGTON, DEL., APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A SECOND QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY (PAR VALUE $0.01 PER SHARE) PAYABLE JUNE 15, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD OF SAID STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 28, 2021.
