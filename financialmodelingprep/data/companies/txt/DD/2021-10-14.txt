DUPONT ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. WILMINGTON, DEL., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY (PAR VALUE $0.01 PER SHARE) PAYABLE DECEMBER 15, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD OF SAID STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 30, 2021.
