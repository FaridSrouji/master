DUPONT NAMES 2021 LAVOISIER AND PEDERSEN AWARD MEDALISTS. WILMINGTON, DEL., JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE:DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS NAMED SEVEN DUPONT SCIENTISTS AND ENGINEERS AS RECIPIENTS OF THE COMPANY'S 2021 LAVOISIER MEDAL FOR LIFETIME TECHNICAL ACHIEVEMENT AND THE PRESTIGIOUS PEDERSEN AWARD MEDAL.
