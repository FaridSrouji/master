DUPONT WINS FOUR 2022 EDISON AWARDS, HIGHLIGHTING INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGIES. COMPANY RECEIVED THREE GOLD, ONE SILVER AWARD WILMINGTON, DEL. , APRIL 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE:DD) IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE THAT FOUR OF ITS INNOVATIVE, BREAKTHROUGH TECHNOLOGIES WERE RECOGNIZED WITH THE PRESTIGIOUS 2022 EDISON AWARD.
