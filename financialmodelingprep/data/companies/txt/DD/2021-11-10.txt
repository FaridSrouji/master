DUPONT INTERCONNECT SOLUTIONS ACHIEVES 95 PERCENT RENEWABLE ELECTRICITY MILESTONE. WILMINGTON, DEL., NOV. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) INTERCONNECT SOLUTIONS (ICS) — A PART OF THE ELECTRONICS & INDUSTRIAL (E&I) BUSINESS SEGMENT — HAS SET A BUSINESS AMBITION OF ZERO BY 2030, WITH THE GOAL OF REACHING CARBON NEUTRAL OPERATIONS FOR THE GLOBAL ICS BUSINESS SEGMENT BY 2030.
