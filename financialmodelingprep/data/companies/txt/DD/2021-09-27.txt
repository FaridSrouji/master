DUPONT JOINS RE100 GLOBAL INITIATIVE. WILMINGTON, DEL., SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS JOINED RE100, A GLOBAL ENVIRONMENTAL INITIATIVE LED BY THE CLIMATE GROUP IN PARTNERSHIP WITH CDP, WHICH BRINGS TOGETHER COMPANIES COMMITTED TO SHIFTING THE ELECTRICITY USED GLOBALLY IN ITS OPERATIONS TO 100% RENEWABLE ENERGY.
