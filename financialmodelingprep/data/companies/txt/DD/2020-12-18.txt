DUPONT BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVES THE SEPARATION OF DUPONT'S NUTRITION & BIOSCIENCES BUSINESS THROUGH AN EXCHANGE OFFER. WILMINGTON, DEL., DEC. 18, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED THE SEPARATION OF DUPONT'S NUTRITION & BIOSCIENCES BUSINESS THROUGH AN EXCHANGE OFFER (SPLIT-OFF) WHEREBY DUPONT STOCKHOLDERS CAN ELECT TO TENDER SHARES OF DUPONT...
