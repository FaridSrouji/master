DUPONT WINS EDIE AWARD FOR SUSTAINABILITY BENEFITS OF CLOSED CIRCUIT REVERSE OSMOSIS TECHNOLOGY. RECOGNITION CELEBRATES CCRO FOR INNOVATION IN  WATER, WASTE AND RESOURCE RECOVERY WILMINGTON, DEL. , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE:DD) RECENTLY RECEIVED AN EDIE AWARD HONORING THE BENEFITS OF ITS CLOSED CIRCUIT REVERSE OSMOSIS (CCRO) TECHNOLOGY IN SUPPORTING INDUSTRIAL AND COMMERCIAL CUSTOMERS' SUSTAINABILITY GOALS.
