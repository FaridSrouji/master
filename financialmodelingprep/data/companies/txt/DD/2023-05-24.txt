DUPONT TO LAUNCH TEDLAR® FRONTSHEET SOLUTION AT 2023 SNEC PV POWER EXPO. SHANGHAI , MAY 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL INTRODUCE ITS LATEST TEDLAR® FRONTSHEET MATERIALS AT THE 2023 SNEC INTERNATIONAL PHOTOVOLTAIC POWER GENERATION AND SMART ENERGY EXHIBITION. DUPONT WILL BE LOCATED AT BOOTH W4-555 AT SHANGHAI NEW INTERNATIONAL EXPO CENTER.
