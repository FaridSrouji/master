DISH NETWORK PLACES OFFERING OF $2,000,000,000 IN SENIOR SECURED NOTES. ENGLEWOOD, COLO. , NOV. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DISH NETWORK CORPORATION ("DISH NETWORK") (NASDAQ: DISH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT PRICED AN OFFERING OF $2,000,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 11.750% SENIOR SECURED NOTES DUE 2027 (THE "NOTES").
