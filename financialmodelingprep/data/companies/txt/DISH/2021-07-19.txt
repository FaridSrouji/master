DISH AND AT&T SIGN STRATEGIC NETWORK SERVICES AGREEMENT. LITTLETON, COLO. AND DALLAS, JULY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DISH (NASDAQ:DISH), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SIGNING OF A TRANSFORMATIVE, LONG-TERM STRATEGIC NETWORK SERVICES AGREEMENT (NSA) WITH AT&T, MAKING AT&T THE PRIMARY NETWORK SERVICES PARTNER FOR DISH MVNO CUSTOMERS.
