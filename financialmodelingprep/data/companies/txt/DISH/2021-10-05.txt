DISH FIGHTS FOR A FAIR DEAL, TEGNA PRIORITIZES GREED ABOVE CONSUMERS. ENGLEWOOD, COLO., OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS THE CONTRACT EXPIRATION WITH DISH APPROACHES, TEGNA INC., ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST LOCAL STATION OWNERS, IS DEMANDING A MASSIVE INCREASE TO NEARLY A BILLION DOLLARS IN FEES FOR ITS PROGRAMMING AND IS USING VIEWERS AS A BARGAINING CHIP IN THEIR NEGOTIATIONS.
