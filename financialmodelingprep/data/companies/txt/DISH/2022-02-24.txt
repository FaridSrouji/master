DISH NETWORK REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER, YEAR-END 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. ENGLEWOOD, COLO., FEB. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DISH NETWORK CORPORATION (NASDAQ: DISH) REPORTED REVENUE TOTALING $4.45 BILLION FOR THE QUARTER ENDING DECEMBER 31, 2021, COMPARED TO $4.56 BILLION FOR THE CORRESPONDING PERIOD IN 2020.
