DISH NETWORK ANNOUNCES SECURED DEBT OFFERING. ENGLEWOOD, COLO., NOV. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DISH NETWORK CORPORATION ("DISH NETWORK") (NASDAQ: DISH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS SUBSIDIARY, DISH DBS CORPORATION ("DISH DBS"), PLANS TO OFFER, SUBJECT TO MARKET AND OTHER CONDITIONS, APPROXIMATELY $4 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS SENIOR SECURED NOTES.
