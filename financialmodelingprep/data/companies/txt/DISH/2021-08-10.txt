SINCLAIR THREATENS TO REMOVE LOCAL CHANNELS FROM DISH CUSTOMERS. ENGLEWOOD, COLO., AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SINCLAIR BROADCAST GROUP, THE COUNTRY'S THIRD LARGEST LOCAL CHANNEL OWNER, IS THREATENING TO BLACK OUT DISH CUSTOMERS' ACCESS TO 144 LOCAL CHANNELS ACROSS 86 MARKETS NATIONWIDE.
