T-MOBILE US INC - ON JUNE 17, T-MOBILE, SPRINT AND DISH ENTERED INTO FIRST AMENDMENT TO ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT.T-MOBILE US INC - DOJ DETERMINED CO HAS COMPLIED WITH REQUIREMENT IN FINAL JUDGMENT ENTERED BY FEDERAL DISTRICT COURT IN WASHINGTON, D.C. ON APR 1.T-MOBILE US INC - BELIEVE ALL CONDITIONS TO CLOSING UNDER ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT HAVE BEEN SATISFIED.T-MOBILE US INC - PARTIES HAVE ALSO AGREED TO AMEND DEFINITION OF "TERMINATION DATE" IN ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT.T-MOBILE US INC - CLOSING OF PREPAID BUSINESS SALE WILL OCCUR ON JULY 1.T-MOBILE US INC - UNDER AMENDMENT EITHER PARTY MAY TERMINATE ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT IF CLOSING HAS NOT OCCURRED BEFORE END OF DAY ON JULY 1, 2020.T-MOBILE US INC - ALL OTHER TERMS & CONDITIONS IN ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT REMAIN UNCHANGED.
