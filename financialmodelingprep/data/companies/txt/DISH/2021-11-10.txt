DISH NETWORK PLACES OFFERING OF $5,250,000,000 IN SENIOR SECURED NOTES. ENGLEWOOD, COLO., NOV. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DISH NETWORK CORPORATION ("DISH NETWORK") (NASDAQ: DISH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS SUBSIDIARY, DISH DBS CORPORATION ("DISH DBS"), PRICED AN OFFERING OF $2,750,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.25% SENIOR SECURED NOTES DUE 2026 (THE "2026 NOTES") AND $2,500,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.75% SENIOR SECURED NOTES DUE 2028 (THE "2028 NOTES" AND, TOGETHER WITH THE 2026 NOTES, THE "NOTES").
