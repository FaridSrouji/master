DISH ADDS HBO MAX, HBO AND CINEMAX, BRINGS MORE CONTENT TO VIEWERS NATIONWIDE. ENGLEWOOD, COLO., JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DISH NETWORK ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE POPULAR HBO MAX STREAMING PLATFORM, AS WELL AS THE HBO AND CINEMAX PREMIUM NETWORKS ARE NOW AVAILABLE TO DISH TV CUSTOMERS NATIONWIDE THROUGH A NEW AGREEMENT WITH WARNERMEDIA.
