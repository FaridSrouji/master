KELLOGG COMPANY PUBLICLY ADDRESSES CEREAL CONTRACT NEGOTIATIONS AND STRIKE. BATTLE CREEK, MICH., OCT. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KELLOGG COMPANY TODAY IS PUBLICLY ADDRESSING THE TERMS OF ITS MASTER LABOR CONTRACT NEGOTIATIONS WITH THE BAKERY, CONFECTIONERY, TOBACCO WORKERS AND GRAIN MILLERS INTERNATIONAL UNION FOR FOUR OF ITS READY TO EAT CEREAL (RTEC) PLANTS.
