KELLOGG COMPANY DECLARES REGULAR DIVIDEND OF $0.58 PER SHARE. BATTLE CREEK, MICH., OCT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KELLOGG COMPANY (NYSE: K) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.58 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2021, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 1, 2021.
