KELLOGG COMPANY REACHES NEW TENTATIVE AGREEMENT WITH UNION. BATTLE CREEK, MICH., DEC. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KELLOGG COMPANY IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT IT HAS REACHED A RECOMMENDED TENTATIVE AGREEMENT FOR A FIVE-YEAR LABOR CONTRACT WITH THE BAKERY, CONFECTIONARY, TOBACCO WORKERS AND GRAIN MILLERS INTERNATIONAL UNION AND THE FOUR LOCAL UNIONS REPRESENTING 1,400 EMPLOYEES AT ITS U.S. CEREAL PLANTS IN BATTLE CREEK, MICH.
