KELLOGG CO. DONATES $75,000 TO ALBERTSONS COMPANIES FOUNDATION'S NOURISHING NEIGHBORS INITIATIVE. DONATION SUPPORTS FEBRUARY "FEED THE LOVE" HUNGER RELIEF CAMPAIGN BATTLE CREEK, MICH. , FEB. 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KELLOGG COMPANY AND ALBERTSONS COMPANIES, ONE OF THE LARGEST FOOD AND DRUG RETAILERS IN THE UNITED STATES, HAVE JOINED FORCES FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR TO TACKLE THE ONGOING ISSUE OF HUNGER IN THE COMMUNITIES THEY SERVE.
