KELLOGG COMPANY DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 16, 2019. KELLOGG COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.57 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 16, 2019, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 2, 2019. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS NOVEMBER 29, 2019.
