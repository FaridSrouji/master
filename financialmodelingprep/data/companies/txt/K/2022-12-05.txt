KELLOGG EMPLOYEES VOLUNTEER MORE THAN 2,400 HOURS IN COMMUNITIES AROUND THE WORLD. EFFORTS MARK NEARLY A DECADE OF HONORING WORLD FOOD DAY BATTLE CREEK, MICH. , DEC. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KELLOGG COMPANY ANNOUNCED TODAY – INTERNATIONAL VOLUNTEER DAY – THAT EMPLOYEES AROUND THE WORLD VOLUNTEERED MORE THAN 2,400 HOURS AS PART OF THE COMPANY'S VOLUNTEER PLEDGE TO HONOR WORLD FOOD DAY.
