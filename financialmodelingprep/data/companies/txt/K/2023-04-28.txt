KELLOGG COMPANY DECLARES REGULAR DIVIDEND OF $0.59 PER SHARE FOR SECOND QUARTER AND ANNOUNCES PLANS FOR DIVIDEND INCREASE IN THIRD QUARTER. BATTLE CREEK, MICH. , APRIL 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KELLOGG COMPANY (NYSE: K) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.59 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY, PAYABLE ON JUNE 15, 2023, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 1, 2023.
