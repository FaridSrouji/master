KELLOGG COMPANY ANNOUNCES SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION. BATTLE CREEK, MICH. , DEC. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KELLOGG COMPANY (NYSE: K) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION OF UP TO $1.5 BILLION, UNDER WHICH THE COMPANY MAY BUY BACK KELLOGG SHARES AT ITS DISCRETION FROM JANUARY 1, 2023, THROUGH DECEMBER 31, 2025.
