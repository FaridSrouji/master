KELLOGG COMPANY AND GROCER ALBERTSONS COMPANIES DRIVE AWARENESS FOR IN-STORE RECYCLING. BATTLE CREEK, MICH., APRIL 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KELLOGG AND ALBERTSONS COMPANIES, ONE OF THE LARGEST FOOD AND DRUG RETAILERS IN THE UNITED STATES, ARE TEAMING UP TO HELP CHANGE CUSTOMERS' RECYCLING HABITS IN HONOR OF EARTH DAY.
