KELLOGG CO. DONATES $70,000 TO OPERATION HOMEFRONT TO SUPPORT MILITARY FAMILIES. BATTLE CREEK, MICH., FEB. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KELLOGG COMPANY ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS DONATED $70,000 TO OPERATION HOMEFRONT, A NON-PROFIT WHOSE MISSION IS TO BUILD STRONG, STABLE AND SECURE MILITARY FAMILIES SO THEY CAN THRIVE – NOT SIMPLE STRUGGLE TO GET BY - IN THE COMMUNITIES THEY HAVE WORKED SO HARD TO PROTECT.
