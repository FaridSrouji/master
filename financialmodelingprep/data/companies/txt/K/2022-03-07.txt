KELLOGG COMPANY DONATION TO NO KID HUNGRY CAN HELP PROVIDE UP TO 2.5M BREAKFASTS TO KIDS IN NEED. BATTLE CREEK, MICH., MARCH 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO CELEBRATE NATIONAL CEREAL DAY, KELLOGG COMPANY ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BREAKFAST FOR ALL CAMPAIGN, WHICH FOCUSES PARTICULARLY ON THE ISSUE OF CHILDHOOD HUNGER, IS NOW UNDERWAY IN STORES ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
