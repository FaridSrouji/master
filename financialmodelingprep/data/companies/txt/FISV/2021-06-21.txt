DEUTSCHE BANK AND FISERV ANNOUNCE JOINT VENTURE FOR PAYMENT ACCEPTANCE IN GERMANY. FRANKFURT--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DEUTSCHE BANK AND FISERV TODAY ANNOUNCED A JOINT VENTURE TO CREATE A COMPREHENSIVE PROVIDER OF PAYMENT ACCEPTANCE AND BANKING SOLUTIONS.
