FISERV AND ADVANCED FRAUD SOLUTIONS HELP FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS FIGHT CHECK FRAUD. FISERV CLIENTS WILL BE ABLE TO MITIGATE FRAUD IN REAL TIME WITH ACCESS TO TRUECHECKS® FROM ADVANCED FRAUD SOLUTIONS HIGH POINT, N.C. , JUNE 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ADVANCED FRAUD SOLUTIONS (AFS), A LEADER IN PAYMENTS FRAUD DETECTION, ANNOUNCED TODAY AN AGREEMENT WITH FISERV, A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF PAYMENTS AND FINANCIAL SERVICES TECHNOLOGY, THAT WILL ALLOW FISERV FINANCIAL INSTITUTION CLIENTS TO AUTOMATE CHECK FRAUD DETECTION, RISK-BASED FUNDS AVAILABILITY AND DECISIONING PROCESSES, HELPING TO PREVENT LOSSES AND IDENTIFY FRAUDULENT DEPOSITS BEFORE THEY ARE ACCEPTED.
