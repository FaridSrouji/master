FISERV NAMED TO FORBES AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS 2023 LIST. BROOKFIELD, WIS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FISERV, INC. (NASDAQ: FISV), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF PAYMENTS AND FINANCIAL SERVICES TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS, HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY FORBES AS ONE OF AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS 2023, A PRESTIGIOUS ANNUAL RANKING COMPILED IN PARTNERSHIP WITH MARKET RESEARCH COMPANY STATISTA THAT HIGHLIGHTS THE LARGE EMPLOYERS AMERICAN WORKERS WOULD MOST RECOMMEND TO OTHERS.
