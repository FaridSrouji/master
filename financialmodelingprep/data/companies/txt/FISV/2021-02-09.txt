FISERV HELPS MORE BUSINESSES GET BACK2BUSINESS WITH EXPANDED COMMITMENT TO MINORITY-OWNED SMALL BUSINESS COMMUNITY. BROOKFIELD, WIS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FISERV ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS DESIGNATED $50 MILLION FOR INVESTMENT IN ITS BACK2BUSINESS PROGRAM AND ASSOCIATED SMALL BUSINESS INITIATIVES.
