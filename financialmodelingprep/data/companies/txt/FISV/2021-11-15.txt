BANK OF QUEENSLAND CONTINUES TECHNOLOGY MODERNISATION WITH MOVE TO CLOUD-BASED DEBIT CARD MANAGEMENT FROM FISERV. BRISBANE, AUSTRALIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #CLOUDMIGRATION--BOQ BRINGS AN ENHANCED SET OF CAPABILITIES TO DEBIT ITS CARD HOLDERS WITH THE MIGRATION OF ITS PORTFOLIO TO A CLOUD-BASED PLATFORM FROM FISERV.
