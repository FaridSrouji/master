FISERV INCLUDED IN 2023 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX. BROOKFIELD, WIS.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--FISERV, INC. (NASDAQ: FISV), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF PAYMENTS AND FINANCIAL SERVICES TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS, HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE 2023 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI), RECEIVING RECOGNITION AMONG COMPANIES FROM A VARIETY OF INDUSTRY SECTORS REPRESENTING 45 COUNTRIES AND REGIONS. THIS MARKS THE SEVENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR FISERV HAS BEEN REPRESENTED IN THE INDEX FOR ITS EFFORTS TO CULTIVATE AN INCLUSIVE AND WELCOMING WORK ENVIRONMENT WHERE EVERYONE CAN SUCCEED.
