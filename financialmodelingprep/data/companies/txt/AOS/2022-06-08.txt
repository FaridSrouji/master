A. O. SMITH ACQUIRES WATER TREATMENT SOLUTIONS PROVIDER ATLANTIC FILTER CORPORATION. MILWAUKEE , JUNE 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WATER TECHNOLOGY COMPANY A. O. SMITH CORPORATION (NYSE: AOS) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT ACQUIRED ATLANTIC FILTER CORPORATION, A FLORIDA-BASED WATER TREATMENT COMPANY IN AN ALL-CASH TRANSACTION.
