A. O. SMITH'S JIM STERN NAMED WATER QUALITY ASSOCIATION PRESIDENT. MILWAUKEE , MAY 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A. O. SMITH CORPORATION (NYSE: AOS), A LEADER IN WATER HEATING AND WATER TREATMENT SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCES JIM STERN, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GENERAL COUNSEL AND SECRETARY, AS THE NEW PRESIDENT OF THE WATER QUALITY ASSOCIATION (WQA).
