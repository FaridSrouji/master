A. O. SMITH CORP TERMINATES EMPLOYMENT OF WEI DING AFTER INTERNAL INVESTIGATION, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. A. O. SMITH CORP - ON FEB 18, CO TERMINATED EMPLOYMENT OF WEI DING AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, PRESIDENT OF A. O. SMITH CHINA, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.A. O. SMITH CORP - DING'S TERMINATION IS RELATED TO AN INTERNAL INVESTIGATION INITIATED IN Q4 OF 2019 BY COMPANY.A. O. SMITH CORP - INVESTIGATION CONCLUDED THAT DING VIOLATED COMPANY'S GUIDING PRINCIPLES AND CODE OF CONDUCT.
