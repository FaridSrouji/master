MASTERCARD SPENDINGPULSE: APRIL U.S. RETAIL SALES REMAIN STEADY, UP +7.2%* YEAR-OVER-YEAR. PURCHASE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FROM A CLEAN HOME TO A NEW WARDROBE, SPRING BRINGS FRESH STARTS AND BECKONS CONSUMERS OUTSIDE AND BACK INTO STORES. ACCORDING TO MASTERCARD SPENDINGPULSE™, WHICH MEASURES IN-STORE AND ONLINE RETAIL SALES ACROSS ALL FORMS OF PAYMENT, TOTAL RETAIL SALES EXCLUDING AUTO IN APRIL INCREASED +7.2% YEAR-OVER-YEAR (YOY) AND +15.3% COMPARED TO PRE-PANDEMIC SPENDING (2019), NOT ADJUSTED FOR INFLATION. THIS IS SIMILAR TO THE YOY MONTHLY GROWTH EXPERIENCED THUS FAR IN 2022.
