MASTERCARD BOARD OF DIRECTORS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AND $8 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM. MASTERCARD BOARD OF DIRECTORS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AND $8 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM.MASTERCARD INC - BOARD DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 40 CENTS PER SHARE, A 21 PERCENT INCREASE OVER PREVIOUS DIVIDEND OF 33 CENTS PER SHARE.MASTERCARD - NEW SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM WILL BECOME EFFECTIVE AT COMPLETION OF CO'S PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED $6.5 BILLION PROGRAM.
