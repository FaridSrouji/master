WITH FOCUS ON AFRICA, NEW DFC AND MASTERCARD COLLABORATION TO PROVIDE UP TO $50 MILLION IN POTENTIAL FINANCING TO ENABLE DIGITIZATION AND FINANCIAL INCLUSION. WASHINGTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AT THE CLOSE OF THE U.S.-AFRICA LEADERS SUMMIT, U.S. INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT FINANCE CORPORATION (DFC) AND MASTERCARD ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION TO INCREASE SUPPORT TO FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS, AGRICULTURAL AND TECHNOLOGY COMPANIES, AND OTHER BUSINESSES ENGAGED WITH MASTERCARD COMMUNITY PASS, A DIGITAL PLATFORM DESIGNED TO ADDRESS INFRASTRUCTURE CHALLENGES THAT ARISE IN DIGITIZING RURAL COMMUNITIES, SUCH AS UNRELIABLE CONNECTIVITY, LOW SMARTPHONE OWNERSHIP, AND LACK OF CONSISTENT IDENTIFICATION OR CREDENTIALS.
