MASTERCARD SPENDINGPULSE: U.S. RETAIL SALES GREW 7.6%* THIS HOLIDAY SEASON. PURCHASE, N.Y.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ACCORDING TO MASTERCARD SPENDINGPULSETM , U.S. RETAIL SALES EXCLUDING AUTOMOTIVE INCREASED 7.6% YEAR-OVER-YEAR THIS HOLIDAY SEASON, RUNNING FROM NOVEMBER 1 THROUGH DECEMBER 24. MASTERCARD SPENDINGPULSE MEASURES IN-STORE AND ONLINE RETAIL SALES ACROSS ALL FORMS OF PAYMENT AND IS NOT ADJUSTED FOR INFLATION.
