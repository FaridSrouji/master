MASTERCARD TO DONATE $10 MILLION TO ADDRESS INDIA'S COVID CRISIS. PURCHASE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MASTERCARD TODAY ANNOUNCED A US$10 MILLION (APPROXIMATELY 75 CRORE RUPEES) COMMITMENT TO DIRECTLY ADDRESS CRITICAL COVID-RELATED NEEDS IN INDIA.
