COTERRA ENERGY REPORTS FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2021 RESULTS, PROVIDES 2022 OUTLOOK AND UPDATES SHAREHOLDER RETURN STRATEGY. HOUSTON, FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COTERRA ENERGY INC. (NYSE: CTRA) ("COTERRA" OR THE "COMPANY") TODAY REPORTED FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2021 FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS. ON OCTOBER 1, 2021, COTERRA ANNOUNCED THAT THE MERGER INVOLVING THE COMPANY, WHICH WAS FORMERLY NAMED CABOT OIL & GAS CORPORATION ("CABOT"), AND CIMAREX ENERGY CO. ("CIMAREX"), WAS COMPLETED (THE "MERGER").
