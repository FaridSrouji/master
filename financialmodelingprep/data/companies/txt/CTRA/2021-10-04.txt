COTERRA ANNOUNCES SPECIAL DIVIDEND. HOUSTON, OCT. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COTERRA ENERGY INC. ("COTERRA" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: CTRA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A SPECIAL CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.50 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 22, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON OCTOBER 14, 2021.
