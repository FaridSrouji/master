SHAREHOLDER ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES SHAREHOLDERS OF HP INC. OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF JANUARY 4, 2021 - HPQ. NEW YORK, NEW YORK--(NEWSFILE CORP. - NOVEMBER 18, 2020) -  THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT IS BEING ISSUED BY LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP:TO: ALL PERSONS OR ENTITIES WHO PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED SECURITIES OF HP INC. ("HP") (NYSE: HPQ) BETWEEN NOVEMBER 6, 2015 AND JUNE 21, 2016. YOU ARE HEREBY NOTIFIED THAT A SECURITIES CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN COMMENCED IN THE THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA. TO GET MORE INFORMATION...
