HP INC. PLANS INCREASE IN QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2019, THE BOARD OF HP INC. AUTHORIZED AN INCREASE OF 10% IN HP’S PLANNED QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND AMOUNT PAYABLE TO HOLDERS OF RECORD OF ITS OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK, COMMENCING WITH THE DIVIDEND FOR THE FIRST FISCAL QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2020, WHICH WOULD RESULT IN A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.1762 PER SHARE. EACH FUTURE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND MUST BE DECLARED BY THE BOARD OUT OF LEGALLY AVAILABLE SOURCES PRIOR TO PAYMENT.
