XEROX ANNOUNCES TO OFFER MORE THAN $30 BILLION FOR HP. ON NOVEMBER 7, 2019, XEROX HOLDINGS CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS OFFERED TO BUY HP INC. FOR BETWEEN $22 AND $23 PER SHARE, OR ABOUT $33 BILLION IN CASH AND STOCK. XEROX HOLDINGS STATED THAT AN OFFER OF $22 PER COMPANY SHARE REPRESENTS ABOUT A 14% PREMIUM TO THE STOCK'S ON NOVEMBER 6, 2019 CLOSE.
