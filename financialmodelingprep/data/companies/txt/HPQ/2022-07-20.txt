FIRST LADY OF UKRAINE ACCEPTS COMPUTER DONATION FROM HP INC. FOR UKRAINIAN CHILDREN AND HEALTHCARE WORKERS. WASHINGTON, D.C., JULY 20, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FIRST LADY OF UKRAINE, OLENA ZELENSKA, MET WITH REPRESENTATIVES FROM HP INC. AND THE GLOBAL BUSINESS COALITION FOR EDUCATION, AT THE UKRAINE HOUSE CULTURAL CENTER IN WASHINGTON, D.C. THIS WEEK, TO ACCEPT A DONATION OF TECHNOLOGY AND LEARNING MATERIAL HEADED TO UKRAINE TO SUPPORT THOUSANDS OF STUDENTS, TEACHERS, AND HEALTHCARE PRACTITIONERS.
