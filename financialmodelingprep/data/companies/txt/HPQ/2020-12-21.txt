HPQ: PAWAR LAW GROUP ANNOUNCES A SECURITIES CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST HP INC. – HPQ. NEW YORK, DEC. 21, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PAWAR LAW GROUP ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS WHO PURCHASED SHARES OF HP INC. (NYSE: HPQ) FROM NOVEMBER 6, 2015 THROUGH JUNE 21, 2016, INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”). THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER DAMAGES FOR HP INC. INVESTORS UNDER THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
