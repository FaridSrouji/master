HP INC. NAMES KIM K.W. RUCKER TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. PALO ALTO, CALIF., NOV. 17, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HP INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF KIM K.W. RUCKER TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. RUCKER IS THE FORMER EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GENERAL COUNSEL AND SECRETARY OF ANDEAVOR, WHICH WAS ACQUIRED BY MARATHON PETROLEUM IN 2018.
