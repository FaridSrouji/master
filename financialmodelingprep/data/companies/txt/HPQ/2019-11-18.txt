HP PROVIDES INFORMATION TO SHAREHOLDERS. ON NOVEMBER 19, 2019, HP INC. ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS REJECTED $US33BN ($48.4BN) TAKEOVER OFFER FROM XEROX CORP AS TOO LOW, BUT IT WAS INTERESTED IN DISCUSSING A DEAL TO COMBINE WITH ITS SMALLER RIVAL. THE COMPANY STATED THAT IT RECOGNIZED THE BENEFITS OF CONSOLIDATION AND WAS ‘OPEN TO EXPLORING A POTENTIAL COMBINATION WITH XEROX’. IN ADDITION, THE COMPANY STATED IN ITS LETTER IT URGED XEROX TO LET IT PERFORM ADDITIONAL DUE DILIGENCE SO IT COULD BETTER UNDERSTAND XEROX'S RECENT REVENUE DECLINES AND ANALYZE POTENTIAL COST SAVINGS FROM A COMBINATION, WHICH XEROX HAS PEGGED AT $US2BN.
ICAHN CAPITAL PUSHES HP FOR MERGER WITH XEROX HOLDINGS. ON NOVEMBER 18, 2019, ICAHN CAPITAL LP ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS TAKEN A 4.24 PERCENT STAKE IN HP INC. AND IS PUSHING FOR ITS $33 BILLION MERGER WITH XEROX HOLDINGS CORP.
HP BOARD OF DIRECTORS UNANIMOUSLY REJECTS UNSOLICITED XEROX PROPOSAL. HP BOARD OF DIRECTORS UNANIMOUSLY REJECTS UNSOLICITED XEROX PROPOSAL.BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS UNANIMOUSLY REJECTED UNSOLICITED PROPOSAL FROM XEROX HOLDINGS CORPORATION TO ACQUIRE COMPANY.CONCLUDED THAT XEROX PROPOSAL SIGNIFICANTLY UNDERVALUES HP AND IS NOT IN BEST INTERESTS OF HP SHAREHOLDERS."WE ARE OPEN TO EXPLORING WHETHER THERE IS VALUE TO BE CREATED FOR HP SHAREHOLDERS THROUGH A POTENTIAL COMBINATION WITH XEROX".HAVE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTIONS THAT NEED TO BE ADDRESSED IN OUR DILIGENCE OF XEROX.BELIEVE IT IS CRITICAL TO ENGAGE IN A RIGOROUS ANALYSIS OF ACHIEVABLE SYNERGIES FROM A POTENTIAL COMBINATION.BELIEVE THAT WE CAN QUICKLY EVALUATE MERITS OF POTENTIAL TRANSACTION WITH SUBSTANTIVE ENGAGEMENT FROM XEROX MANAGEMENT.
HP OPENS TO EXPLORING BID FOR XEROX HOLDINGS. ON NOVEMBER 17, 2019, HP INC. ANNOUNCED THAT IT WAS OPEN TO EXPLORING A BID FOR XEROX HOLDINGS CORP. AFTER REBUFFING A US$33.5 BILLION CASH-AND-STOCK ACQUISITION OFFER FROM THE LATTER AS SIGNIFICANTLY UNDERVALUING THE PERSONAL COMPUTER MAKER.
