SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISS LAW INVESTIGATES PLANTRONICS, INC.. NEW YORK , MARCH 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISS LAW IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PLANTRONICS, INC. ("PLANTRONICS" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: POLY), IN CONNECTION WITH THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF THE COMPANY BY HP INC. ("HP") (NYSE: HPQ).  UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $40.00 IN CASH FOR EACH SHARE OF PLANTRONICS COMMON STOCK OWNED.
