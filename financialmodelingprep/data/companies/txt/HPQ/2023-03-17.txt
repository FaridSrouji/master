BROOK + WHITTLE TO INSTALL FIRST HP INDIGO V12 DIGITAL PRESS IN US. GUILFORD, CONN. , MARCH 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROOK + WHITTLE, A LEADER IN SUSTAINABLE PACKAGING, COMPLEX DECORATION, AND DIGITAL PRINTING, IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE THE INSTALLATION OF THE FIRST HP INDIGO V12 DIGITAL PRESS IN THE UNITED STATES.
