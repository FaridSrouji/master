XEROX HOLDINGS PROVIDES INFORMATION TO SHAREHOLDERS. ON NOVEMBER 27, 2019, XEROX HOLDINGS CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT IT WOULD ADDRESS ITS BID STRAIGHT TO THE SHAREHOLDERS OF HP INC. AFTER THE COMPANY REJECTED ITS DUE DILIGENCE REQUEST. XEROX STATED THAT IT HAD PREVIOUSLY GIVEN THE COMPANY A NOVEMBER 25 DEADLINE TO AGREE ON FRIENDLY DISCUSSIONS AS WELL AS ON MUTUAL DUE DILIGENCE AND IT DID NOT DISCLOSE FURTHER DETAILS ABOUT ITS NEXT MOVES AND THERE ARE VARIOUS POSSIBLE OPTIONS, INCLUDING A HOSTILE TENDER OFFER OR A PROXY FIGHT. XEROX STATED THAT IT ALSO REITERATED THE BENEFITS FROM A DEAL AND AGAIN REFUTED THE COMPANY’S CLAIMS THAT ITS PROPOSAL WAS ‘HIGHLY CONDITIONAL’ AND ‘UNCERTAIN’, ALSO HIGHLIGHTING THAT THERE WOULD BE NO FINANCING CONDITION TO THE COMPLETION OF THE ACQUISITION.
