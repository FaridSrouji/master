HP INC. INVESTIGATION CONTINUED BY FORMER LOUISIANA ATTORNEY GENERAL: KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC CONTINUES TO INVESTIGATE THE OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS OF HP INC. - HPQ. NEW ORLEANS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA, CHARLES C. FOTI, JR., ESQ., A PARTNER AT THE LAW FIRM OF KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC (“KSF”), ANNOUNCES THAT KSF CONTINUES ITS INVESTIGATION INTO HP INC. (NYSE: HPQ).
