GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP REMINDS INVESTORS OF LOOMING DEADLINE IN THE CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST HP INC. (HPQ). LOS ANGELES, DEC. 02, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP (“GPM”) REMINDS INVESTORS OF THE UPCOMING JANUARY 4, 2021  DEADLINE TO FILE A LEAD PLAINTIFF MOTION IN THE CLASS ACTION FILED ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED HP INC. (“HP” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: HPQ ) COMMON STOCK BETWEEN NOVEMBER 6, 2015 AND JUNE 21, 2016, INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”).
