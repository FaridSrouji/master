ANALOG DEVICES REPORTS RECORD FIRST QUARTER FISCAL 2023 RESULTS. WILMINGTON, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ANALOG DEVICES, INC. (NASDAQ: ADI), A GLOBAL SEMICONDUCTOR LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FIRST QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2023, WHICH ENDED JANUARY 28, 2023. “ADI CONTINUES TO EXECUTE EXCEPTIONALLY WELL WITH REVENUE GROWTH OF 21% YEAR-OVER-YEAR AND RECORD EARNINGS PER SHARE,” SAID VINCENT ROCHE, CEO AND CHAIR.
