ANALOG DEVICES APPOINTS ALAN LEE AS CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER. WILMINGTON, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ANALOG DEVICES, INC. (NASDAQ: ADI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ALAN LEE TO THE POSITION OF CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER (CTO). IN THIS ROLE, ALAN WILL IDENTIFY AND WORK TO ADVANCE THE NEXT-GENERATION TECHNOLOGIES THAT WILL DISRUPT AND SHAPE THE SEMICONDUCTOR INDUSTRY AND ITS MARKETS. HE AND HIS TEAM WILL COLLABORATE CLOSELY WITH ADI CUSTOMERS, UNIVERSITIES, RESEARCH ORGANIZATIONS, AND OTHER STRATEGIC PARTNERS TO INCUBATE NOVEL TECHNOLOGIES AND DEVELOP THE.
