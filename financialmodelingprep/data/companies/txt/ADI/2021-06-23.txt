ANALOG DEVICES ANNOUNCES $2.5 BILLION SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY. WILMINGTON, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ANALOG DEVICES, INC. (NASDAQ: ADI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY EXECUTED A $2.5 BILLION SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED SENIOR REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY (THE “FACILITY”), REPLACING ITS EXISTING $1.25 BILLION SENIOR REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY. HALF OF THE $2.5 BILLION FACILITY WILL BECOME AVAILABLE ONLY UPON THE CONSUMMATION OF ADI'S ACQUISITION OF MAXIM INTEGRATED PRODUCTS, INC. ADI IS ONE OF THE FIRST U.S. SEMICONDUCTOR COMPANIES TO ISSUE A SUSTAINABILITY-L
