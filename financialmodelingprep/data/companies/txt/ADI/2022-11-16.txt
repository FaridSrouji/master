THE NEW OFFERINGS FACILITATE POWERED, LAST-MILE CONNECTIVITY FOR FACTORY AND BUILDING AUTOMATION THROUGH REAL-TIME POWER MANAGEMENT, TELEMETRY, EXTREMELY LOW STANDBY POWER CONSUMPTION, AND EASE OF INSTALLATION.
