ANALOG DEVICES RAISES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 13%. WILMINGTON, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ANALOG DEVICES, INC. (NASDAQ: ADI), A GLOBAL SEMICONDUCTOR LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS VOTED TO INCREASE ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FROM $0.76 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO $0.86, WHICH REPRESENTS AN INCREASE OF 13% AND IS THE EQUIVALENT OF $3.44 ANNUALLY.
