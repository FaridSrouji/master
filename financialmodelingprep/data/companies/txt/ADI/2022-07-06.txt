IDEX BIOMETRICS – CFO TRANSITION. OSLO, NORWAY – 06 JULY 2022 – IDEX BIOMETRICS ASA, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JAMIE SIMMS WILL STEP DOWN FROM HIS POSITION AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER EFFECTIVE AUGUST 15, 2022. IDEX HAS INITIATED THE PROCESS OF FINDING HIS SUCCESSOR. THE COMPANY HAS APPOINTED EILEEN WYNNE, FORMER EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER AT ANALOG DEVICES (NASDAQ: ADI), AS INTERIM CFO EFFECTIVE AUGUST 15, 2022. MS. WYNN HAS BEEN SUPPORTING IDEX ON A CONSULTING BASIS SINCE DECEMBER 2020.
