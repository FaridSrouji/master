THE ANALOG DEVICES, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 17, 2019. THE ANALOG DEVICES, INC. BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.54 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON DECEMBER 17, 2019 TO ALL SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 6, 2019.
