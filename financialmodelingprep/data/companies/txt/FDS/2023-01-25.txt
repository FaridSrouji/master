ENTELLIGENT'S T-RISK GOES LIVE ON FACTSET TO SUPPORT INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS WORLDWIDE. BOULDER, COLO.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ENTELLIGENT, AN INDEPENDENT CLIMATE DATA AND ANALYTICS PROVIDER, AND FACTSET, A GLOBAL FINANCIAL DIGITAL PLATFORM AND ENTERPRISE SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ENTELLIGENT'S PATENTED CLIMATE TRANSITION RISK DATA, KNOWN AS T-RISK, ON FACTSET'S DIGITAL PLATFORM.
