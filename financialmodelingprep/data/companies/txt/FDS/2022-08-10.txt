FACTSET APPOINTS KATE STEPP AS CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER. NORWALK, CONN., AUG. 10, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FACTSET ("FACTSET" OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: FDS) (NASDAQ: FDS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF KATE STEPP AS CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER (CTO), EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 1, 2022. STEPP WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO FACTSET'S CEO, PHIL SNOW. AS A 14-YEAR VETERAN OF FACTSET, STEPP BRINGS HER SIGNIFICANT ENGINEERING, PRODUCT, AND ORGANIZATIONAL KNOWLEDGE TO THE ROLE OF LEADING THE COMPANY'S TECHNOLOGY ORGANIZATION AND OVERSEEING ITS DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION STRATEGY.
