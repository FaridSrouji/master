FACTSET APPOINTS ELISHA WIESEL TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NORWALK, CONN., MARCH 24, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FACTSET RESEARCH SYSTEMS INC. (NYSE: FDS | NASDAQ: FDS), A GLOBAL FINANCIAL DIGITAL PLATFORM AND ENTERPRISE SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ELISHA WIESEL TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
