FACTSET INCREASES DIVIDEND. NORWALK, CONN., MAY 04, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FACTSET (NYSE: FDS | NASDAQ: FDS), A GLOBAL FINANCIAL DIGITAL PLATFORM AND ENTERPRISE SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A 10% INCREASE IN THE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND FROM $0.89 PER SHARE TO $0.98 PER SHARE.
