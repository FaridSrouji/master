FACTSET RESEARCH SYSTEMS INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 19, 2019. FACTSET RESEARCH SYSTEMS INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.72 PER SHARE. THE CASH DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON DECEMBER 19, 2019 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD OF FACTSET’S COMMON STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 29, 2019.
