AM BEST UPGRADES ISSUER CREDIT RATINGS OF THE CIGNA GROUP AND MOST OF ITS SUBSIDIARIES. OLDWICK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #INSURANCE--AM BEST HAS UPGRADED MOST OF THE LONG-TERM ISSUER CREDIT RATINGS (LONG-TERM ICRS) TO “A+” (EXCELLENT) FROM “A” (EXCELLENT) AND AFFIRMED THE FINANCIAL STRENGTH RATING (FSR) OF A (EXCELLENT) OF THE KEY U.S. LIFE/HEALTH SUBSIDIARIES, HEALTH MAINTENANCE ORGANIZATIONS AND EUROPE-BASED INSURANCE COMPANIES OF THE CIGNA GROUP (CIGNA) (HEADQUARTERED IN BLOOMFIELD, CT) [NYSE: CI]. THE OUTLOOK OF THE LONG-TERM ICRS HAS BEEN REVISED TO STABLE FROM POSITIVE WHILE THE OUTLOOK.
