CIGNA AND HARTFORD HEALTHCARE REACH AGREEMENT TO KEEP QUALITY CARE AFFORDABLE. HARTFORD, CONN., SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CIGNA (NYSE: CI) AND HARTFORD HEALTHCARE HAVE REACHED A MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT THAT ENSURES CIGNA CUSTOMERS WILL HAVE CONTINUED ACCESS TO HARTFORD HEALTHCARE'S HOSPITALS, FACILITIES AND PROVIDERS FOR QUALITY CARE AT PREDICTABLE, AFFORDABLE RATES.
