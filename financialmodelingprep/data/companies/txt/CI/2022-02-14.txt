CIGNA CORPORATION ANNOUNCES APPEARANCE AT THE COWEN 42ND ANNUAL HEALTH CARE CONFERENCE. BLOOMFIELD, CONN., FEB. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL HEALTH SERVICES COMPANY CIGNA CORPORATION (NYSE:CI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ERIC PALMER, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF EVERNORTH, WILL PRESENT AT THE COWEN 42ND ANNUAL HEALTH CARE CONFERENCE ON MARCH 7, 2022 THROUGH A VIRTUAL WEBCAST.
