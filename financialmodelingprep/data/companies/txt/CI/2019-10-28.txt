CIGNA CORPORATION APPOINTS KIMBERLY A. ROSS TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JUNE 1, 2020. CIGNA CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT KIMBERLY A. ROSS HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO CIGNA'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. HER APPOINTMENT IS EFFECTIVE JUNE 1, 2020. MS. ROSS SERVED AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER OF BAKER HUGHES (SUPPLIER TO THE OIL AND GAS INDUSTRY) FROM SEPTEMBER 2014 UNTIL JULY 2017. SHE IS CURRENTLY A DIRECTOR OF NESTLÉ S.A. AND A DIRECTOR AND CHAIR OF THE AUDIT COMMITTEE OF PQ CORPORATION.
