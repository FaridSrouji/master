MERCY AND CIGNA EXTEND CONTRACT END DATE. MERCY MEDICAL CENTER AND CIGNA HAVE AGREED TO EXTEND THEIR CURRENT CONTRACT END DATE TO OCTOBER 18, 2019, AS RATE NEGOTIATIONS CONTINUE BETWEEN THE TRINITY HEALTH OF NEW ENGLAND PARTNER AND THE CONNECTICUT-BASED INSURER. THE FOUR-DAY EXTENSION MEANS THAT CIGNA COMMERCIAL HEALTH PLAN INSURANCE, SCHEDULED TO END ON OCTOBER 16, 2019, IF NEGOTIATIONS STALLED BETWEEN THE TWO, WILL CONTINUE TO BE ACCEPTED BY MERCY, AS WELL AS ITS AFFILIATE WELDON REHABILITATION HOSPITAL. PROVIDENCE BEHAVIORAL HEALTH IN HOLYOKE, ALSO A MERCY AFFILIATE, AS WELL AS TRINITY HEALTH OF NEW ENGLAND MEDICAL GROUP ARE NOT IMPACTED BY THE CURRENT NEGOTIATIONS.
CIGNA EXPANDS SOLUTIONS IN COLORADO, OFFERING MEDICARE ADVANTAGE PLANS FOR THE FIRST TIME. CIGNA ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS EXPANDING IN COLORADO, OFFERING FOR THE FIRST TIME MEDICARE ADVANTAGE (MA) PLANS IN THE DENVER AND BOULDER AREAS. THESE PLANS WILL FIRST BE AVAILABLE TO MEDICARE ELIGIBLE CUSTOMERS DURING MEDICARE’S ANNUAL ELECTION PERIOD (AEP), WHICH BEGINS OCT. 15 AND CONTINUES THROUGH DEC. 7, FOR PLANS THAT TAKE EFFECT JAN. 1, 2020. CIGNA-HEALTHSPRING PREFERRED HMO AND CIGNA-HEALTHSPRING TRUE CHOICE PPO WILL BE AVAILABLE IN ADAMS, ARAPAHOE, BOULDER, BROOMFIELD, DENVER, DOUGLAS AND JEFFERSON COUNTIES.
