CIGNA DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BLOOMFIELD, CONN., APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CIGNA CORPORATION (NYSE: CI) TODAY DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.00 PER SHARE OF CIGNA COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 23, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 8, 2021.
