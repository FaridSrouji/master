CIGNA CORPORATION ANNOUNCES CYNTHIA RYAN AS CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER AS LONGTIME CHRO JOHN MURABITO RETIRES. BLOOMFIELD, CONN., FEB. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CIGNA CORPORATION (NYSE: CI), A GLOBAL HEALTH SERVICE COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JOHN MURABITO, LONGTIME CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER (CHRO) AND CURRENT CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER (CAO), WILL RETIRE ON APRIL 1.
