CIGNA CORPORATION ANNOUNCES APPEARANCE AT THE BOFA SECURITIES 2022 HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE. BLOOMFIELD, CONN. , APRIL 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL HEALTH SERVICES COMPANY CIGNA CORPORATION (NYSE:CI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT BRIAN EVANKO, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL PRESENT AT THE BOFA SECURITIES 2022 HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE ON MAY 11, 2022 IN LAS VEGAS, NV.
