CIGNA FOUNDATION EXPANDS COMMITMENT TO HELP THOUSANDS OF STUDENTS, EDUCATORS, AND FAMILIES ACCESS HEALTHY FOOD, MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT. BLOOMFIELD, CONN., SEPT. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH CHILDREN AND FAMILIES FACING UNPRECEDENTED CHALLENGES DUE TO THE STRESS OF THE PANDEMIC, CIGNA FOUNDATION IS TEAMING UP WITH NONPROFITS ACROSS THE COUNTRY TO ENSURE UNDERSERVED STUDENTS AND FAMILIES HAVE ACCESS TO HEALTHY FOOD AND MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT IN SCHOOLS.
