CIGNA ANNOUNCES $2 BILLION ACCELERATED STOCK REPURCHASE AGREEMENTS. BLOOMFIELD, CONN., AUG. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL HEALTH SERVICES COMPANY CIGNA CORPORATION (NYSE: CI) WILL REPURCHASE $2.0 BILLION OF COMMON STOCK THROUGH ACCELERATED STOCK REPURCHASE AGREEMENTS (THE "ASR AGREEMENTS") WITH EACH OF MORGAN STANLEY & CO. LLC AND JPMORGAN CHASE BANK, N.A.
