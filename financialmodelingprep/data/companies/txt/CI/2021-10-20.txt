CIGNA ADDS IORA PRIMARY CARE TO MEDICARE ADVANTAGE NETWORK IN PHOENIX AREA. NASHVILLE, TENN., OCT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CIGNA CORPORATION, A GLOBAL HEALTH SERVICE COMPANY, HAS ADDED IORA PRIMARY CARE PROVIDERS TO ITS MEDICARE ADVANTAGE (MA) NETWORK IN ARIZONA'S MARICOPA COUNTY, WHICH INCLUDES PHOENIX.
