CHRIS DEROSA, PRESIDENT, U.S. GOVERNMENT, CIGNA HEALTHCARE ASSUMES LEADERSHIP OF MEDICARE AND INDIVIDUAL & FAMILY PLAN BUSINESSES. BLOOMFIELD, CONN. , JUNE 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CIGNA GROUP ANNOUNCED TODAY CHRIS DEROSA, PRESIDENT, U.S. GOVERNMENT, WILL ASSUME LEADERSHIP OF CIGNA HEALTHCARE'S MEDICARE AND INDIVIDUAL & FAMILY PLAN (IFP) BUSINESSES, AS CHUCK BERG, SENIOR ADVISOR, ANNOUNCES HIS DEPARTURE.
