CIGNA DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BLOOMFIELD, CONN. , APRIL 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CIGNA CORPORATION (NYSE: CI) TODAY DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.12 PER SHARE OF CIGNA COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 23, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 8, 2022.
