CIGNA CORPORATION ANNOUNCES APPEARANCE AT THE 43RD ANNUAL COWEN HEALTH CARE CONFERENCE. BLOOMFIELD, CONN. , FEB. 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL HEALTH SERVICES COMPANY CIGNA CORPORATION (NYSE:CI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT BRIAN EVANKO, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL PRESENT AT THE 43RD  ANNUAL COWEN HEALTH CARE CONFERENCE ON MARCH 6, 2023 IN BOSTON, MA.
