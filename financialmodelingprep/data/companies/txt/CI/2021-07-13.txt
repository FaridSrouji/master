BONNIE EVELYN NAMED CIGNA MARKET PRESIDENT FOR GEORGIA/ALABAMA. ATLANTA, JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CIGNA (NYSE:CI) HAS NAMED BONNIE EVELYN AS MARKET PRESIDENT FOR ITS HEALTH CARE AND RELATED BENEFITS PLANS IN GEORGIA/ALABAMA. SHE REPLACES FORMER MARKET PRESIDENT BRYAN HOLGERSON, WHO WAS PROMOTED TO CHIEF UNDERWRITING OFFICER FOR CIGNA U.S. COMMERCIAL.
