NEW YORK LIFE TO ACQUIRE CIGNA’S GROUP LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE BUSINESS.CIGNA CORP - NEW YORK LIFE WILL ACQUIRE CIGNA'S GROUP LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE BUSINESS FOR $6.3 BILLION.CIGNA - BOARD INCREASES SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORITY TO $4.0 BILLION.CIGNA CORP - TRANSACTION IS NOT SUBJECT TO A FINANCING CONDITION AT CLOSING.CIGNA CORP - CIGNA EXPECTS IMPACT OF TRANSACTION TO BE NEUTRAL TO EARNINGS PER SHARE IN 2020 AND MODESTLY ACCRETIVE TO EARNINGS PER SHARE IN 2021.CIGNA CORP - EXPECTS TO UTILIZE PROCEEDS OF TRANSACTION FOR SHARE REPURCHASE AND REPAYMENT OF DEBT IN 2020.CIGNA - CONTINUES TO EXPECT TO MEET ITS DELEVERAGING COMMITMENTS MADE FOLLOWING EXPRESS SCRIPTS COMBINATION.CIGNA CORP - EXPECTS TO REALIZE ABOUT $5.3 BILLION OF NET AFTER-TAX PROCEEDS FROM TRANSACTION.CIGNA CORP - CIGNA GROUP INSURANCE EMPLOYEES, AS WELL AS EMPLOYEES WHO PRIMARILY SUPPORT ACQUIRED BUSINESS, WILL TRANSFER TO NEW YORK LIFE.
