THE CIGNA GROUP DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BLOOMFIELD, CONN. , APRIL 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE CIGNA GROUP (NYSE: CI) TODAY DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.23 PER SHARE OF ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 22, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 7, 2023.
THE CIGNA GROUP RECOGNIZED AS TOP EMPLOYER FOR COMMITMENT TO EMPLOYEE WELL-BEING BY BUSINESS GROUP ON HEALTH. BLOOMFIELD, CONN. , APRIL 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL HEALTH COMPANY THE CIGNA GROUP (NYSE:CI) HAS BEEN NAMED AMONG THE BUSINESS GROUP ON HEALTH'S BEST EMPLOYERS FOR ITS EXCELLENCE IN HEALTH AND WELL-BEING.
