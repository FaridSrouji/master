CIGNA PROTECTS ITS WORKFORCE BY REQUIRING COVID-19 VACCINATIONS OR TESTING FOR EMPLOYEES ENTERING U.S. WORKSITES. BLOOMFIELD, CONN., AUG. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH CONFIRMED CASES OF COVID-19 ON THE RISE IN THE UNITED STATES, CIGNA HAS ESTABLISHED NEW REQUIREMENTS TO HELP PROTECT ITS WORKFORCE.
