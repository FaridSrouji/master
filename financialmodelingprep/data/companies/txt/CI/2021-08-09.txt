CIGNA LAUNCHES WORLD'S FIRST VOICE-ACTIVATED STRESS TEST. ' CIGNA STRESSWAVES TEST' POWERED BY ELLIPSIS HEALTH'S ADVANCED AI TECHNOLOGY FIRST AND ONLY STRESS ANALYSIS MODEL TO DETECT BOTH ACOUSTIC AND SEMANTIC PATTERNS IN VOICE AVAILABLE FREE-OF-CHARGE WITH VISUAL STRESS RESULTS IN 90-SECONDS HONG KONG, AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CIGNA INTERNATIONAL ANNOUNCES THE LAUNCH OF THE CIGNA STRESSWAVES TEST, A FREE ONLINE TOOL THAT ANALYSES ACOUSTIC AND SEMANTIC VOICE PATTERNS TO EVALUATE STRESS LEVELS. THE STRESSWAVES TEST WAS CREATED IN PARTNERSHIP WITH ELLIPSIS HEALTH, A MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPER AND LEADER IN AI HEALTH AND VOCAL BIOMARKERS.
