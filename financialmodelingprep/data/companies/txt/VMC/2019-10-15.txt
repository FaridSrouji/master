VULCAN MATERIALS COMPANY ELECTS MELISSA H. ANDERSON AS NEW DIRECTOR. VULCAN MATERIALS COMPANY ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF MELISSA H. ANDERSON TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MS. ANDERSON WILL SERVE ON THE COMPENSATION AND SAFETY, HEALTH AND ENVIRONMENTAL AFFAIRS COMMITTEES. MS. ANDERSON IS THE EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER OF DUKE ENERGY CORPORATION.
