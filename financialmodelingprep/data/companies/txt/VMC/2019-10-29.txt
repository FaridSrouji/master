VULCAN MATERIALS COMPANY (NYSE:VMC) COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF RAPPAHANNOCK CONCRETE & MASONRY.. VULCAN MATERIALS COMPANY (NYSE:VMC) ACQUIRED RAPPAHANNOCK CONCRETE & MASONRY ON OCTOBER 25, 2019. UNDER THE TRANSACTION, RAPPAHANNOCK’S MECHANICAL SHOP LOCATED AT SALUDA, DID NOT CONVEY IN THE SALE. FOLLOWING THE TRANSACTION, SCOTT FINNEY, FORMER PRESIDENT AND CARA RANDOLPH, FORMER VICE PRESIDENT OF RAPPAHANNOCK WILL REMAIN ON RETAINER AS CONSULTANTS FOR THE BUSINESS FOR A SHORT PERIOD OF TIME AND 99% OF THE CURRENT EMPLOYEES OF RAPPAHANNOCK WILL CONTINUE WITH VULCAN AND CURRENT MANAGEMENT WILL CONTINUE TO RUN OPERATIONS.
