VULCAN DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 5, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VULCAN MATERIALS COMPANY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF THIRTY-ONE CENTS PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 5, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 19, 2019.
