NASDAQ ANNOUNCES END-OF-MONTH OPEN SHORT INTEREST POSITIONS IN NASDAQ STOCKS AS OF SETTLEMENT DATE JULY 30, 2021. NEW YORK, AUG. 10, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF JULY 30, 2021, SHORT INTEREST IN 2,770 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKETSM SECURITIES TOTALLED 8,508,823,975 SHARES COMPARED WITH 8,537,253,537 SHARES IN 2,732 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF JULY 15, 2021.
