DILIGEND REBRANDS AS DASSETI, ANNOUNCES SERIES A FINANCING LED BY NASDAQ VENTURES. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--DILIGEND, THE AWARD-WINNING DIGITAL DUE DILIGENCE SOFTWARE COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE REBRAND OF ITS COMPANY, NOW TO BE KNOWN AS DASSETI. THE REBRAND COMES ALONGSIDE THE SUCCESSFUL CLOSE OF ITS SERIES A FINANCING LED BY NASDAQ VENTURES.
