NASDAQ ANNOUNCES MID-MONTH OPEN SHORT INTEREST POSITIONS IN NASDAQ STOCKS AS OF SETTLEMENT DATE DECEMBER 15, 2022. NEW YORK, DEC. 27, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF DECEMBER 15, 2022, SHORT INTEREST IN 3,476 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKETSM SECURITIES TOTALED 10,247,653,483 SHARES COMPARED WITH 10,154,992,155 SHARES IN 3,471 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF NOVEMBER 30, 2022. THE MID-DECEMBER SHORT INTEREST REPRESENTS 2.84 DAYS COMPARED WITH 3.21 DAYS FOR THE PRIOR REPORTING PERIOD.
