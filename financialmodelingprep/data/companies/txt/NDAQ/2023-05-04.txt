NASDAQ ANNOUNCES NEW CHAIR OF THE CANADIAN EXCHANGE BOARD. TORONTO, MAY 04, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ, INC. (NASDAQ:NDAQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF MARY ANNE WILEY AS THE CHAIR OF THE NASDAQ CXC LIMITED (“NASDAQ CANADA”) BOARD OF DIRECTORS, FINALIZING THE SLATE OF BOARD MEMBERS FOR 2023.
