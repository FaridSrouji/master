NASDAQ ANNOUNCES END-OF-MONTH OPEN SHORT INTEREST POSITIONS IN NASDAQ STOCKS AS OF SETTLEMENT DATE MARCH 31, 2022. NEW YORK, APRIL 11, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF MARCH 31, 2022, SHORT INTEREST IN 3,350 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKETSM SECURITIES TOTALED 10,049,631,674 SHARES COMPARED WITH 10,111,145,146 SHARES IN 3,348 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF MARCH 15, 2022. THE END-OF-MARCH SHORT INTEREST REPRESENT 2.63 DAYS AVERAGE DAILY NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKET SHARE VOLUME FOR THE REPORTING PERIOD, COMPARED WITH 2.55 DAYS FOR THE PRIOR REPORTING PERIOD.
