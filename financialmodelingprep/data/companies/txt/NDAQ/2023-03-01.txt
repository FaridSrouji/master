DELISTING OF SECURITIES OF EQONEX LIMITED; SCOPUS BIOPHARMA INC.; PEAK BIO, INC.; STATERA BIOPHARMA, INC.; TRICIDA, INC.; ARBOR RAPHA CAPITAL BIOHOLDINGS CORP. I; CALITHERA BIOSCIENCES, INC.; WAITR HOLDINGS INC.; ALLIED HEALTHCARE PRODUCTS, INC.; AND METACRINE, INC. FROM THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. NEW YORK, MARCH 01, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL DELIST THE ORDINARY SHARES OF EQONEX LIMITED. EQONEX LIMITED ORDINARY SHARES WERE SUSPENDED ON NOVEMBER 30, 2022, AND HAVE NOT TRADED ON NASDAQ SINCE THAT TIME.
