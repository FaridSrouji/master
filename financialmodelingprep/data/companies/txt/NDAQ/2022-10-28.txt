NASDAQ LAUNCHES NASDAQ CUSTOM BASKET CALCULATION SERVICE IN COLLABORATION WITH S&P GLOBAL MARKET INTELLIGENCE. STOCKHOLM, SWEDEN, OCT. 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF NASDAQ CUSTOM BASKET CALCULATION SERVICE (CBCS). THE INTRODUCTION OF CBCS TO THE MARKET IS DRIVEN BY THE INCREASING DEMAND FOR A SIMPLER AND MORE EFFICIENT WAY OF MANAGING PROPRIETARY INDEXES, THEMATIC BASKETS, AND ESG INVESTMENTS.
