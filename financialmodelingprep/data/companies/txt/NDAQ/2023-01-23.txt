DELISTING OF SECURITIES OF SCHMITT INDUSTRIES, INC.; VIVEVE MEDICAL, INC.; AND AMERICAN VIRTUAL CLOUD TECHNOLOGIES, INC. FROM THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. NEW YORK, JAN. 23, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL DELIST THE COMMON STOCK OF SCHMITT INDUSTRIES, INC. SCHMITT INDUSTRIES, INC.'S COMMON STOCK WAS SUSPENDED ON JANUARY 17, 2023, AND HAS NOT TRADED ON NASDAQ SINCE THAT TIME.
