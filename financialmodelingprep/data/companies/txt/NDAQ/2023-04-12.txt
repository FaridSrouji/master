NASDAQ ANNOUNCES END OF MONTH OPEN SHORT INTEREST POSITIONS IN NASDAQ STOCKS AS OF SETTLEMENT DATE MARCH 31, 2023. NEW YORK, APRIL 12, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF MARCH 31, 2023, SHORT INTEREST IN 3,429 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKETSM SECURITIES TOTALED 10,672,627,941 SHARES COMPARED WITH 10,373,177,802 SHARES IN 3,418 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF MARCH 15, 2023. THE END OF MARCH SHORT INTEREST REPRESENTS 2.68 DAYS AVERAGE DAILY NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKET SHARE VOLUME FOR THE REPORTING PERIOD, COMPARED WITH 2.72 DAYS FOR THE PRIOR REPORTING PERIOD.
