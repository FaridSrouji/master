DELISTING OF SECURITIES OF ALBERTON ACQUISITION CORPORATION; ZOSANO PHARMA CORPORATION; ELECTRIC LAST MILE SOLUTIONS, INC.; 4D PHARMA PLC; AND ENJOY TECHNOLOGY, INC. FROM THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. NEW YORK, JULY 18, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL DELIST THE ORDINARY SHARES, WARRANT, UNIT, AND RIGHTS OF ALBERTON ACQUISITION CORPORATION. ALBERTON ACQUISITION CORPORATION'S SECURITIES WERE SUSPENDED ON APRIL 26, 2022 AND HAVE NOT TRADED ON NASDAQ SINCE THAT TIME.
