CROWDSTRIKE HOLDINGS, INC. TO JOIN THE NASDAQ-100 INDEX BEGINNING AUGUST 26, 2021. NEW YORK, AUG. 23, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CROWDSTRIKE HOLDINGS, INC. (NASDAQ: CRWD), WILL BECOME A COMPONENT OF THE NASDAQ-100 INDEX® (NASDAQ: NDX), THE NASDAQ-100 EQUAL WEIGHTED INDEX (NASDAQ: NDXE) AND THE NASDAQ-100 TECHNOLOGY INDEX (NASDAQ: NDXT) PRIOR TO MARKET OPEN ON THURSDAY, AUGUST 26, 2021. CROWDSTRIKE HOLDINGS, INC. WILL REPLACE MAXIM INTEGRATED PRODUCTS, INC. (NASDAQ: MXIM) IN THE NASDAQ-100 INDEX®, THE NASDAQ-100 EQUAL WEIGHTED INDEX AND THE NASDAQ-100 TECHNOLOGY INDEX.
