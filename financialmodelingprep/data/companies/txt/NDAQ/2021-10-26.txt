NASDAQ ANNOUNCES MID-MONTH OPEN SHORT INTEREST POSITIONS IN NASDAQ STOCKS AS OF SETTLEMENT DATE OCTOBER 15, 2021. NEW YORK, OCT. 26, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF OCTOBER 15, 2021, SHORT INTEREST IN 2,878 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKETSM SECURITIES TOTALED 8,844,587,706 SHARES COMPARED WITH 8,738,351,082 SHARES IN 2,803 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF SEPTEMBER 30, 2021. THE MID-OCTOBER SHORT INTEREST REPRESENTS 3.20 DAYS COMPARED WITH 2.85 DAYS FOR THE PRIOR REPORTING PERIOD.
DELISTING OF SECURITIES OF TELIGENT, INC. FROM THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. NEW YORK, OCT. 26, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL DELIST THE COMMON STOCK OF TELIGENT, INC. TELIGENT, INC.'S COMMON STOCK WAS SUSPENDED ON OCTOBER 18, 2021 AND HAS NOT TRADED ON NASDAQ SINCE THAT TIME.
