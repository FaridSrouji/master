NASDAQ'S EUROPEAN DEBT MARKET SHOWED CONTINUED GROWTH IN 2020. STOCKHOLM, SWEDEN, FEB. 04, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) SAW ITS EUROPEAN DEBT MARKET GROW BY 8 PERCENT IN 2020. CONTINUED INTEREST TO LIST EURO MEDIUM TERM NOTE (EMTN) PROGRAMS AND STRONG DEMAND FOR ESG INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITIES HAVE FUELED THE EXPANSION. ISSUERS HAVE ALSO TAPPED THE DEBT CAPITAL MARKETS TO RAISE FUNDS TO SUPPORT COVID-19 PANDEMIC MITIGATION EFFORTS.
