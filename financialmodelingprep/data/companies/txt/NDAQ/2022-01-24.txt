DELISTING OF SECURITIES OF IFRESH INC. AND ODONATE THERAPEUTICS, INC. FROM THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. NEW YORK, JAN. 24, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL DELIST THE COMMON STOCK OF IFRESH INC. IFRESH INC.'S COMMON STOCK WAS SUSPENDED ON NOVEMBER 23, 2021 AND HAS NOT TRADED ON NASDAQ SINCE THAT TIME.
