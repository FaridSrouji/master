DELISTING OF SECURITIES OF SUPERCONDUCTOR TECHNOLOGIES INC.; GULFPORT ENERGY CORPORATION; APEX GLOBAL BRANDS INC.; PARETEUM CORPORATION; AND YOUNGEVITY INTERNATIONAL, INC. FROM THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. NEW YORK, FEB. 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL DELIST THE COMMON STOCK, OF SUPERCONDUCTOR TECHNOLOGIES INC. SUPERCONDUCTOR TECHNOLOGIES INC.'S COMMON STOCK WAS SUSPENDED ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2020 AND HAS NOT TRADED ON NASDAQ SINCE THAT TIME.
