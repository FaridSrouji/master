NASDAQ, INC. DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 27, 2019. NASDAQ, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.47 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 27, 2019 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 13, 2019.
