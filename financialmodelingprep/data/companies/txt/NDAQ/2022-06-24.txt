19TH CONSECUTIVE NASDAQ CLOSING CROSS SETS RECORD FOR NUMBER OF SHARES TRADED DURING 2022 RUSSELL US INDEXES RECONSTITUTION. NEW YORK, JUNE 24, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ, INC. (NASDAQ: NDAQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE NASDAQ CLOSING CROSS HAD A RECORD DAY AS IT WAS USED FOR THE 19TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR TO REBALANCE NASDAQ-LISTED SECURITIES IN THE ENTIRE FAMILY OF RUSSELL US INDEXES, PART OF LEADING GLOBAL INDEX PROVIDER FTSE RUSSELL, DURING ITS ANNUAL RECONSTITUTION.
