NASDAQ ANNOUNCES 3-FOR-1 STOCK SPLIT AND QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.20 PER SHARE. NEW YORK, JULY 20, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NASDAQ, INC. (NASDAQ: NDAQ) HAS APPROVED AND DECLARED BOTH A 3-FOR-1 STOCK SPLIT OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK IN THE FORM OF A STOCK DIVIDEND, ALONG WITH A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.20 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK, WHICH ON A SPLIT-ADJUSTED BASIS IS ECONOMICALLY EQUIVALENT TO THE PRE-SPLIT DIVIDEND AMOUNT OF $0.60 PER SHARE PAID IN THE PRECEDING QUARTER.
