NASDAQ ANNOUNCES END-OF-MONTH OPEN SHORT INTEREST POSITIONS IN NASDAQ STOCKS AS OF SETTLEMENT DATE JUNE 30, 2022. NEW YORK, JULY 12, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF JUNE 30, 2022, SHORT INTEREST IN 3,432 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKETSM SECURITIES TOTALED 10,675,154,725 SHARES COMPARED WITH 10,650,038,311 SHARES IN 3,411 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF JUNE 15, 2022. THE END-OF-JUNE SHORT INTEREST REPRESENTS 2.44 DAYS COMPARED WITH 2.92 DAYS FOR THE PRIOR REPORTING PERIOD.
