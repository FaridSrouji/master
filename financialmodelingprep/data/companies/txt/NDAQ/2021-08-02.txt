NASDAQ ACQUIRES MINORITY STAKE IN LEVEL ATS. NEW YORK AND BOSTON, AUG. 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) AND LEVEL ATS TODAY ANNOUNCED NASDAQ HAS ACQUIRED A SIGNIFICANT MINORITY STAKE IN LEVEL ATS, A LEADING, INDEPENDENTLY OPERATED US EQUITY DARK POOL TRADING VENUE. THROUGH THIS INVESTMENT, NASDAQ JOINS OWNERS INCLUDING BANK OF AMERICA, CITI, AND FIDELITY. THE TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION WERE NOT DISCLOSED.
