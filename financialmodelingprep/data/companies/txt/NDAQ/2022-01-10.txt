NASDAQ DECEMBER 2021 VOLUMES AND 4Q21 STATISTICS. NEW YORK, JAN. 10, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) TODAY REPORTED MONTHLY VOLUMES FOR DECEMBER 2021, AS WELL AS QUARTERLY VOLUMES, ESTIMATED REVENUE CAPTURE, NUMBER OF LISTINGS AND INDEX STATISTICS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2021 ON ITS INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE. A DATA SHEET SHOWING THE MONTHLY VOLUMES AND QUARTERLY CAPTURE RATES CAN BE FOUND AT: HTTP://IR.NASDAQ.COM/FINANCIALS/VOLUME-STATISTICS.
