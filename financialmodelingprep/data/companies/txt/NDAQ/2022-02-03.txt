CONSTELLATION ENERGY CORP. JOINED THE NASDAQ-100 INDEX ON FEBRUARY 2, 2022. NEW YORK, FEB. 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ON FEBRUARY 2, 2022, EXELON CORP. (NASDAQ: EXC) ANNOUNCED IT COMPLETED THE SEPARATION OF CONSTELLATION ENERGY CORP. (NASDAQ: CEG), EXELON'S FORMER POWER GENERATION AND COMPETITIVE ENERGY BUSINESS. UPON THE COMPLETION OF THIS SPIN-OFF TRANSACTION, CONSTELLATION ENERGY CORP. WAS ADDED TO THE NASDAQ-100 INDEX® (NASDAQ: NDX), THE NASDAQ-100 EQUAL WEIGHTED INDEX (NASDAQ: NDXE) AND THE NASDAQ-100 EX-TECHNOLOGY INDEX (NASDAQ: NDXX) ON FEBRUARY 2, 2022.
