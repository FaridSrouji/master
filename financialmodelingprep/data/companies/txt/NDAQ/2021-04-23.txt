DELISTING OF SECURITIES OF INTERPACE BIOSCIENCES, INC., FRANCESCA'S HOLDINGS CORPORATION, AND WINS FINANCE HOLDINGS INC. FROM THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. NEW YORK, APRIL 23, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL DELIST THE COMMON STOCK OF INTERPACE BIOSCIENCES, INC. INTERPACE BIOSCIENCES, INC.'S COMMON STOCK WAS SUSPENDED ON FEBRUARY 25, 2021 AND HAS NOT TRADED ON NASDAQ SINCE THAT TIME.
