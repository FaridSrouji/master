DELISTING OF SECURITIES OF TRITERRAS, INC. FROM THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. NEW YORK, MARCH 11, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL DELIST THE CLASS A ORDINARY SHARES AND WARRANTS OF TRITERRAS, INC. TRITERRAS, INC.'S SECURITIES WERE SUSPENDED ON FEBRUARY 3, 2022, AND HAVE NOT TRADED ON NASDAQ SINCE THAT TIME.
