NASDAQ NAMED TO 2023 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX. NEW YORK, JAN. 31, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INCLUSION IN THE 2023 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI) FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. THE GEI IS A MODIFIED MARKET CAPITALIZATION-WEIGHTED INDEX THAT TRACKS THE PERFORMANCE OF PUBLIC COMPANIES COMMITTED TO TRANSPARENCY IN GENDER-DATA REPORTING. NASDAQ IS ONE OF 484 COMPANIES ACROSS 45 COUNTRIES AND REGIONS TO BE NAMED TO THE 2023 INDEX.
