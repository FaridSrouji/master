ASTRAZENECA PLC – ADR TO JOIN THE NASDAQ-100 INDEX BEGINNING FEBRUARY 22, 2022. NEW YORK, FEB. 10, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ASTRAZENECA PLC – ADR (NASDAQ: AZN), WILL BECOME A COMPONENT OF THE NASDAQ-100 INDEX® (NASDAQ: NDX) AND THE NASDAQ-100 EQUAL WEIGHTED INDEX (NASDAQ: NDXE) PRIOR TO MARKET OPEN ON TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 22, 2022. ASTRAZENECA PLC – ADR WILL REPLACE XILINX, INC. (NASDAQ: XLNX) IN THE NASDAQ-100 INDEX® AND THE NASDAQ-100 EQUAL WEIGHTED INDEX.
