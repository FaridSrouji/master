KIATNAKIN PHATRA SECURITIES ADVANCES SURVEILLANCE & MARKET INTEGRITY EFFORTS IN THAILAND WITH NASDAQ TRADE SURVEILLANCE. NEW YORK AND BANGKOK, THAILAND, JAN. 18, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) AND KIATNAKIN PHATRA SECURITIES (KKPS), A LEADING SECURITIES COMPANY IN THAILAND, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT KKPS WILL LEVERAGE NASDAQ TRADE SURVEILLANCE TECHNOLOGY TO ADVANCE ITS SURVEILLANCE AND MARKET INTEGRITY EFFORTS. THE FIRST LOCAL BROKER IN THAILAND TO LEVERAGE NASDAQ TRADE SURVEILLANCE, KKPS WILL BENEFIT FROM RICH FUNCTIONALITY TO EFFECTIVELY MONITOR FOR MARKET ABUSE.
