NASDAQ ANNOUNCES MID-MONTH OPEN SHORT INTEREST POSITIONS IN NASDAQ STOCKS AS OF SETTLEMENT DATE SEPTEMBER 15, 2021. NEW YORK, SEPT. 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF SEPTEMBER 15, 2021, SHORT INTEREST IN 2,819 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKETSM SECURITIES TOTALED 8,628,581,482 SHARES COMPARED WITH 8,628,020,542 SHARES IN 2,803 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF AUGUST 31, 2021. THE MID-AUGUST SHORT INTEREST REPRESENTS 3.48 DAYS COMPARED WITH 3.22 DAYS FOR THE PRIOR REPORTING PERIOD.
