NASDAQ STOCKHOLM CELEBRATES ITS 160TH ANNIVERSARY. STOCKHOLM, SWEDEN, MARCH 20, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ STOCKHOLM CELEBRATES ITS 160TH ANNIVERSARY THIS YEAR, MARKING THE MILESTONE WITH THE LAUNCH OF A SERIES OF INTERNAL AND EXTERNAL EVENTS OVER THE COMING MONTHS IN RECOGNITION OF THE DEEP HISTORY AND HERITAGE OF THE EXCHANGE, INCLUDING A BELL CEREMONY DURING THE ALMEDALEN WEEK. FOUNDED IN 1863, NASDAQ STOCKHOLM HAS SUPPORTED JOB CREATION AND GROWTH ACROSS THE SWEDISH AND EUROPEAN ECONOMY. AS AN IMPORTANT PART OF THE SWEDISH INVESTMENT COMMUNITY, RETAIL CAPITAL FLOWS REPRESENTING MORE THAN A THIRD OF FIRST NORTH AND 15 PERCENT OF OUR MAIN MARKET.
