GE COMPLETES LATEST ADAPTIVE CYCLE ENGINE TESTS, SUCCESSFULLY CONCLUDES ADAPTIVE ENGINE TRANSITION PROGRAM EFFORTS. TULLAHOMA, TENN. , SEPT. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE U.S. AIR FORCE AND GE HAVE SUCCESSFULLY CONCLUDED TESTING ON GE'S SECOND XA100 ADAPTIVE CYCLE ENGINE AT THE AIR FORCE'S ARNOLD ENGINEERING DEVELOPMENT COMPLEX (AEDC).
