GE SIGNS AN EXCLUSIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL PART OF STEAM POWER'S NUCLEAR ACTIVITIES TO EDF. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GE (NYSE:GE) AND EDF ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THEY HAVE SIGNED AN EXCLUSIVE AGREEMENT FOR EDF TO ACQUIRE PART OF GE STEAM POWER'S NUCLEAR POWER ACTIVITIES. THE PROPOSED TRANSACTION WOULD BRING TOGETHER GE'S NUCLEAR STEAM TURBINE TECHNOLOGY AND SERVICES EXPERTISE WITH EDF STRENGTHENING ITS COMMITMENT TO THE NUCLEAR POWER SECTOR, CREATING AN INDUSTRY-LEADING GLOBAL STEAM TURBINE EQUIPMENT AND SERVICES PROVIDER WITHIN EDF GROUP.
