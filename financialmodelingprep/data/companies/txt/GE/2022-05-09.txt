CORRECTION OF PRESS RELEASE: MISSING MAR LABEL IN PREVIOUS PRESS RELEASE "RAYSEARCH COLLABORATES WITH GE HEALTHCARE TO IMPROVE RADIATION ONCOLOGY TREATMENT PLANNING". STOCKHOLM , MAY 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYSEARCH LABORATORIES AB (PUBL) ISSUES A CORRECTION OF THE PRESS RELEASE THAT WAS SENT OUT AT 10:00 MAY 9, 2022. THE CORRECTION REFERS TO THE FACT THAT THE PRESS RELEASE LACKED THE MAR LABEL WITH REFERENCE TO THE EU MARKET ABUSE REGULATION (MAR).
RAYSEARCH COLLABORATES WITH GE HEALTHCARE TO IMPROVE RADIATION ONCOLOGY TREATMENT PLANNING. STOCKHOLM , MAY 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYSEARCH LABORATORIES AB (PUBL) IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE AN AGREEMENT TO COLLABORATE WITH GE HEALTHCARE, A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY, DIAGNOSTICS, AND DIGITAL SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TO DEVELOP A NEW RADIATION THERAPY SIMULATION AND TREATMENT PLANNING WORKFLOW SOLUTION, DESIGNED TO MAKE USE OF THE LATEST ADVANCEMENTS IN TREATMENT PLANNING TECHNOLOGY. THE COMPANIES AIM TO COMBINE RAYSEARCH'S ADVANCED TREATMENT PLANNING SYSTEM RAYSTATION®* WITH GE HEALTHCARE'S LEADING MULTI-MODALITY (CT/MR/MOLECULAR IMAGING) SIMULATOR SYSTEMS TO MAKE CANCER TREATMENT FASTER AND MORE PRECISE.
