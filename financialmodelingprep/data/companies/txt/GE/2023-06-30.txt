GE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AUTHORIZES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GE (NYSE: GE) TODAY DECLARED A $0.08 PER SHARE DIVIDEND ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 25, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 11, 2023. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS JULY 10, 2023.
