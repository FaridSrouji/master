GE COMPLETES SEPARATION OF GE HEALTHCARE. BOSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--GE (NYSE: GE) ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE SEPARATION OF ITS HEALTHCARE BUSINESS, LAUNCHING GE HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGIES INC. (“GE HEALTHCARE”), A GLOBAL LEADER IN PRECISION CARE. STARTING TODAY, JANUARY 4, 2023, GE HEALTHCARE WILL TRADE ON NASDAQ UNDER THE TICKER SYMBOL “GEHC”.
