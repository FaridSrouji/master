TRANSCEND AIR ENTERS AGREEMENT WITH GE AVIATION AS ENGINE SUPPLIER FOR THE GROUNDBREAKING VY 400 HIGH SPEED VTOL AIRCRAFT. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #AAM--TRANSCEND AIR AGREES WITH GE AVIATION TO DEVELOP A VARIANT OF GE'S CLASS-LEADING CT7-8 ENGINE TO POWER THE VY 400 HIGH SPEED VTOL (HSVTOL) AIRCRAFT.
