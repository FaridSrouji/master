IDC MARKETSCAPE NAMES GE DIGITAL A LEADER IN FOUR WORLDWIDE ASSET PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT VENDOR ASSESSMENTS. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #APM--GE DIGITAL TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY IDC'S NEW WORLDWIDE ASSET PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT 2020-2021 MARKETSCAPES: OIL & GAS, UTILITIES, MINING, AND MANUFACTURING. GE DIGITAL'S ASSET PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT (APM) IS A SUITE OF SOFTWARE AND SERVICE SOLUTIONS DESIGNED TO HELP OPTIMIZE THE PERFORMANCE OF ASSETS FOR INDUSTRIAL COMPANIES.
