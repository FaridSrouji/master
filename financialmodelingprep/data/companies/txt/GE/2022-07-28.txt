REAGAN FOUNDATION AND GE ANNOUNCE 10 RECIPIENTS OF $40,000 SCHOLARSHIPS PROGRAM TOPS $8 MILLION AWARDED TO MORE THAN 200 STUDENTS SINCE 2011. SIMI VALLEY, CALIF. , JULY 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN A CELEBRATION AT THE REAGAN LIBRARY LAST MONTH, THE RONALD REAGAN PRESIDENTIAL FOUNDATION AND INSTITUTE IN PARTNERSHIP WITH GE RECOGNIZED THIS YEAR'S CLASS OF GE-REAGAN FOUNDATION SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM RECIPIENTS.
