AFFIDEA AND GE HEALTHCARE SIGN $100 MLN TECHNOLOGY PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT. GE HEALTHCARE - CO, AFFIDEA SIGN $100 MILLION TECHNOLOGY PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT FOR IMAGING FLEET, DIGITAL WORKFLOW SOLUTIONS AND CONTRAST MEDIA.GE HEALTHCARE - CO WILL INSTALL MORE THAN 200 NEW EQUIPMENT IN AFFIDEA’S NETWORK OF CENTERS ACROSS EUROPE.GE HEALTHCARE - AFFIDEA DEAL INCLUDES PROVISION OF 60 NEW MRIS, 50 ULTRASOUND DEVICES, 40 CT SCANNERS & 30 X-RAYS MACHINES IN NEXT 3 YRS.GE - IN ADDITION, AFFIDEA EXPANDS ITS COLLABORATION WITH GE HEALTHCARE IN PHARMACEUTICAL DIAGNOSTICS IN ALL ITS GEOGRAPHIES, TO INCLUDE CLARISCAN.

AND DECISIO HEALTH.GE HEALTHCARE - COLLABORATING WITH FORMLABS, A MASSACHUSETTS-BASED MANUFACTURER OF 3D-PRINTERS.GE HEALTHCARE - RECENTLY PARTICIPATED AS A MINORITY INVESTOR IN A $240 MILLION FUNDING ROUND FOR UK BASED CMR SURGICAL.GE HEALTHCARE - ALSO INVESTING IN DECISIO HEALTH, A HOUSTON, TEXAS-BASED SOFTWARE COMPANY THAT SPECIALIZES IN CLINICAL SURVEILLANCE.
