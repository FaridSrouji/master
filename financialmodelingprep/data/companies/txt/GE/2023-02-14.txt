AIR INDIA SELECTS GE AEROSPACE TO POWER RECORD AIRCRAFT ORDER. MUMBAI, INDIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AIR INDIA, PART OF TATA SONS, HAS SIGNED A FIRM ORDER FOR 40 GENX-1B AND 20 GE9X ENGINES, PLUS A MULTI-YEAR TRUECHOICE™ ENGINE SERVICES AGREEMENT. THE DEAL WAS SIGNED IN COORDINATION WITH THE AIRLINE'S FIRM ORDER FOR 20 BOEING 787 AND 10 BOEING 777X AIRCRAFT.
