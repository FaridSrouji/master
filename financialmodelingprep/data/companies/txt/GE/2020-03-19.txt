GE HEALTHCARE SAYS HIRING MORE MANUFACTURING EMPLOYEES AND MOVING CURRENT EMPLOYEES TO SUPPORT INCREASED DEMAND. STATEMENT FROM GE HEALTHCARE PRESIDENT & CEO ON EFFORTS TO ADDRESS THE CORONAVIRUS.GE HEALTHCARE - HIRING ADDITIONAL MANUFACTURING EMPLOYEES AND SHIFTING CURRENT EMPLOYEES TO SUPPORT INCREASED DEMAND IMMEDIATELY..GE HEALTHCARE - MANAGING SUPPLY CHAIN BY WORKING WITH SUPPLIERS TO MITIGATE SHORTAGES AND MINIMIZE IMPACT ON OUR CUSTOMERS GLOBALLY.GE HEALTHCARE SAYS IS ADDING MANUFACTURING LINES TO VENTILATOR PRODUCTION AND INCREASING NUMBER OF SHIFTS TO PRODUCE AROUND CLOCK.GE HEALTHCARE - INCREASED MANUFACTURING CAPACITY, OUTPUT OF EQUIPMENT - INCLUDING CTS, ULTRASOUND DEVICES, MOBILE X-RAY SYSTEMS, PATIENT MONITORS.
