GE AEROSPACE SIGNS F-35 AVIONICS AND POWER SERVICES AGREEMENT. EVENDALE, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GE AEROSPACE HAS SIGNED AN AGREEMENT WITH LOCKHEED MARTIN TO SUPPORT AVIONICS AND ELECTRICAL POWER SYSTEMS ON THE F-35 GLOBALLY. “THE F-35'S RELIABILITY AND MISSION READINESS REMAIN AN ESSENTIAL FOCUS,” SAID CHRIS NEWMAN, F-35 SENIOR PROGRAM MANAGER, GE AEROSPACE. “AS THE F-35 FLEET CONTINUES TO EXPAND, WE'VE TAKEN AGGRESSIVE ACTIONS TO STRENGTHEN LEAD TIME, SUPPLY AVAILABILITY AND DELIVERY TO OUR CUSTOMERS.
