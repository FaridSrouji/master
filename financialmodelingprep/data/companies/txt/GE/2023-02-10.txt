GE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AUTHORIZES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GE (NYSE: GE) TODAY DECLARED A $0.08 PER SHARE DIVIDEND ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE APRIL 25, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 7, 2023. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS MARCH 6, 2023. ABOUT GE GE (NYSE:GE) RISES TO THE CHALLENGE OF BUILDING A WORLD THAT WORKS.
