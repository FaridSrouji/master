GE HEALTHCARE TO ACQUIRE BK MEDICAL, EXPANDING ULTRASOUND PORTFOLIO INTO SURGICAL VISUALIZATION. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GE (NYSE:GE) HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE BK MEDICAL, A LEADER IN ADVANCED SURGICAL VISUALIZATION, FROM ALTARIS CAPITAL PARTNERS FOR A CASH PURCHASE PRICE OF $1.45 BILLION. BK MEDICAL IS AN INNOVATOR IN GLOBAL INTRAOPERATIVE IMAGING AND SURGICAL NAVIGATION, USED TO GUIDE CLINICIANS DURING MINIMALLY INVASIVE AND ROBOTIC SURGERIES AND TO VISUALIZE DEEP TISSUE DURING PROCEDURES IN NEURO AND ABDOMINAL SURGERY, AND IN ULTRASOUND UROLOGY.
