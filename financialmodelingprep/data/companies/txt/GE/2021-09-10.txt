GE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AUTHORIZES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GE (NYSE: GE) TODAY DECLARED A $0.08 PER SHARE DIVIDEND ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE OCTOBER 25, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 27, 2021. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS SEPTEMBER 24, 2021.
