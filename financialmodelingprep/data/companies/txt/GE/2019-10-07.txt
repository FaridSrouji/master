GENERAL ELECTRIC SECURES 132.5-MW WIND TURBINE ORDER FROM EDF IN BRAZIL. GENERAL ELECTRIC HAS WON A CONTRACT TO SUPPLY 132.5 MW OF WIND TURBINES FROM ITS CYPRESS PLATFORM TO EDF RENEWABLES IN BRAZIL. GE RENEWABLE ENERGY WILL DELIVER 25 OF ITS 5.3-MW MACHINES, ITS ONSHORE WIND TURBINE, AS PART OF THE COMPANY'S FIRST CONTRACT WITH THE RENEWABLES ARM OF FRANCE'S EDF SA IN THE LATIN AMERICAN COUNTRY. THE EQUIPMENT WILL BE INSTALLED AS PART OF THE VENTOS DA BAHIA WIND FARM, CURRENTLY OPERATING WITH A 182-MW CAPACITY NEAR THE CITIES OF BONITO AND MULUNGU DO MORRO, BAHIA STATE. GE WILL ALSO PROVIDE OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE (O&M) SERVICES FOR A 20-YEAR PERIOD. THE CYPRESS TURBINES, TOGETHER WITH THE BLADES, ARE TO BE MANUFACTURED LOCALLY AT FACILITIES IN CAMACARI AND PORT OF SUAPE, RESPECTIVELY.
