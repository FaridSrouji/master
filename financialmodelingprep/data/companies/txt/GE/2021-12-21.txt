GE HEALTHCARE COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF BK MEDICAL. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GE (NYSE:GE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF BK MEDICAL, A LEADER IN ADVANCED SURGICAL VISUALIZATION, FROM ALTARIS CAPITAL PARTNERS, LLC. THE COMPANIES ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT FOR GE TO PURCHASE BK MEDICAL FOR A CASH PURCHASE PRICE OF $1.45 BILLION ON SEPTEMBER 22, 2021.
