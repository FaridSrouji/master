HUMANA PARTNERS WITH NOWPOW TO ADDRESS SOCIAL NEEDS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HEALTH CARE COMPANY HUMANA INC. (NYSE: HUM) IS PARTNERING WITH NOWPOW TO IMPROVE THE HEALTH OF COMMUNITIES ACROSS CHICAGO BY IDENTIFYING AND ADDRESSING ROOT CAUSES OF POOR HEALTH. HUMANA WILL LEVERAGE NOWPOW'S PERSONALIZED COMMUNITY REFERRAL PLATFORM TO HELP EXTEND THE DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE FOR ITS POPULATION HEALTH STRATEGY SUPPORTING HUMANA MEDICAID AND MEDICARE MEMBERS IN CHICAGO. THIS EFFORT AIMS TO IMPROVE MEMBERS' HEALTH BY MEETING NEEDS LIKE FOOD INSECURITY, LO
