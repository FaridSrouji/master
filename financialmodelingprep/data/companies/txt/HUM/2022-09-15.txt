HUMANA ELECTS BRAD D. SMITH TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HUMANA INC. (NYSE: HUM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT BRAD D. SMITH, FORMER EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF INTUIT INC., HAS BEEN ELECTED AS A MEMBER OF THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. HE CURRENTLY SERVES AS THE PRESIDENT OF MARSHALL UNIVERSITY, HAVING ASSUMED THE ROLE OF LEADING HIS ALMA MATER IN JANUARY 2022.
