HUMANA'S CENTERWELL SENIOR PRIMARY CARE AND WELSH, CARSON, ANDERSON & STOWE ANNOUNCE SECOND JOINT VENTURE TO DEVELOP AND OPERATE VALUE-BASED PRIMARY CARE CLINICS FOR MEDICARE PATIENTS. LOUISVILLE, KY. & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HUMANA INC.'S (NYSE: HUM) CENTERWELL SENIOR PRIMARY CARE AND WELSH, CARSON, ANDERSON & STOWE (“WCAS”) HAVE ESTABLISHED A SECOND JOINT VENTURE (“JV”) TO FURTHER EXPAND ACCESS TO VALUE-BASED PRIMARY CARE CLINICS FOR MEDICARE PATIENTS. THE NEW JV WILL DEPLOY UP TO $1.2 BILLION OF COMMITTED CAPITAL TO DEVELOP APPROXIMATELY 100 NEW SENIOR-FOCUSED, PAYER-AGNOSTIC PRIMARY CARE CLINICS BETWEEN 2023 AND 2025 AND OPERATE THEM TO PROFITABILITY.
