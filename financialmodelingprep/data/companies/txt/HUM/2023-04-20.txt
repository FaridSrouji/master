HUMANA BOARD DECLARES PAYMENT OF QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO STOCKHOLDERS. LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HUMANA INC. (NYSE: HUM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND TO STOCKHOLDERS OF $0.885 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON JULY 28, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 30, 2023.
