HUMANA’S PARTNERS IN PRIMARY CARE AND WELSH, CARSON, ANDERSON & STOWE FORM JOINT VENTURE. HUMANA’S PARTNERS IN PRIMARY CARE AND WELSH, CARSON, ANDERSON & STOWE FORM JOINT VENTURE TO EXPAND THE NATION’S SYSTEM OF VALUE-BASED PRIMARY CARE CENTERS FOCUSED ON SERVING MEDICARE PATIENTS.HUMANA INC - WCAS AND HUMANA COMMIT ABOUT $600 MILLION TO NEW JOINT VENTURE.HUMANA INC - TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO HAVE AN IMMATERIAL IMPACT TO HUMANA'S EARNINGS IN 2020.HUMANA INC - WCAS WILL HAVE MAJORITY OWNERSHIP IN NEW CO; HUMANA WILL OWN A SMALL MINORITY STAKE.HUMANA - PARTNERS IN PRIMARY CARE WILL RECEIVE MANAGEMENT FEE, INCLUDING PERFORMANCE-BASED INCENTIVES, FOR MANAGEMENT OF ALL JV CENTERS.HUMANA INC - DEAL INCLUDES SERIES OF PUT, CALL OPTIONS THROUGH WHICH PARTNERS IN PRIMARY CARE MAY ACQUIRE WCAS'S INTEREST IN JV.HUMANA INC - THROUGH PUT, CALL OPTIONS WCAS MAY REQUIRE PARTNERS IN PRIMARY CARE TO PURCHASE ITS INTEREST IN JV, IN STAGES OVER NEXT 5-10 YEARS.
