HUMANA'S ECONOMIC IMPACT IN FLORIDA TOTALS $48 BILLION. TAMPA, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AN INDEPENDENT ECONOMIC RESEARCH ANALYSIS ESTIMATED THAT LEADING HEALTH AND WELL-BEING COMPANY HUMANA INC. (NYSE: HUM) WOULD CONTRIBUTE A TOTAL OF $48 BILLION IN OVERALL ECONOMIC IMPACT IN THE STATE OF FLORIDA IN 2022. IN ADDITION, FLORIDA IS HOME TO MORE HUMANA EMPLOYEES AND HEALTH PLAN MEMBERS THAN ANY OTHER STATE.
