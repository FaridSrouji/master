HUMANA TO HOST VIRTUAL INVESTOR UPDATE ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2022. LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HUMANA INC. (NYSE: HUM) WILL HOST A VIRTUAL INVESTOR UPDATE ON THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 15, 2022. THE INVESTOR UPDATE WILL INCLUDE PRESENTATIONS BY COMPANY LEADERS FOCUSING ON HUMANA'S STRATEGIC DIRECTION, OPERATIONAL AND FINANCIAL PROGRESS AS WELL AS EXPECTATIONS FOR FUTURE PERFORMANCE. PRESENTATIONS WILL BEGIN AT 8:30 A.M. EASTERN. THE INVESTOR UPDATE WEBCAST AND VIRTUAL PRESENTATION MAY BE ACCESSED VIA HUMANA'S INVESTOR RELATIONS PAGE AT HTTPS://HUMANA.GCS-WEB.CO
