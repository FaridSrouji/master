HUMANA FOUNDATION INVESTS $1 MILLION TO SUPPORT LOUISVILLE IN WAKE OF SHOOTINGS. THE HUMANA FOUNDATION, PHILANTHROPIC ARM OF HUMANA INC. (NYSE: HUM), IS INVESTING $1 MILLION TO BOLSTER MENTAL HEALTH RESOURCES IN ITS HOMETOWN OF LOUISVILLE, KY., FOLLOWING THE AFTERMATH OF MONDAY'S MASS SHOOTING IN DOWNTOWN LOUISVILLE.
