HUMANA PRICES $1.25 BILLION DEBT OFFERING. LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HUMANA INC. (THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: HUM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $1.25 BILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR NOTES. THESE SENIOR NOTES ARE COMPRISED OF $500 MILLION OF THE COMPANY'S 5.700 PERCENT SENIOR NOTES, DUE 2026, AT 99.984 PERCENT OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT AND $750 MILLION OF THE COMPANY'S 5.500 PERCENT SENIOR NOTES, DUE 2053, AT 96.431 PERCENT OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT (COLLECTIVELY, THE “SENIOR NOTES OFFERINGS”).
