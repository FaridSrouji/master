(THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: HUM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE COMPLETION OF ITS PUBLIC OFFERING OF $750 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF THE COMPANY'S 3.700 PERCENT SENIOR NOTES, DUE 2029 (THE “SENIOR NOTES OFFERING”).
