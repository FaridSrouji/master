HUMANA AND ALEDADE ANNOUNCE 10 YEAR COLLABORATION. LOUISVILLE, KY. & BETHESDA, MD.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALEDADE, THE NATION'S LARGEST NETWORK OF INDEPENDENT PRIMARY CARE, AND HUMANA INC. (NYSE: HUM), LEADING HEALTH INSURER AND HEALTH CARE SERVICES COMPANY, HAVE ANNOUNCED A 10-YEAR COLLABORATION TO PROVIDE VALUE-BASED PRIMARY CARE FOR HUMANA'S MEDICARE ADVANTAGE MEMBERS FROM IN-NETWORK ALEDADE-ENABLED PHYSICIANS.
