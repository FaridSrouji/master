PULTEGROUP ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.16 PER SHARE. ATLANTA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PULTEGROUP, INC. (NYSE: PHM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.16 PER COMMON SHARE PAYABLE APRIL 4, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 20, 2023.
