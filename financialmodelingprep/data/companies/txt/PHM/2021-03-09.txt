PULTEGROUP'S BUILT TO HONOR PROGRAM SURPRISES PURPLE HEART RECIPIENT WITH MORTGAGE-FREE HOME IN MARYLAND. WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--U.S. ARMY SERGEANT BRYAN EDWARDS RECEIVED THE SURPRISE OF A LIFETIME: HE WILL BE THE RECIPIENT OF A BRAND-NEW MORTGAGE-FREE HOME THROUGH PULTEGROUP'S BUILT TO HONOR PROGRAM. THE SURPRISE TOOK PLACE AT PULTE HOMES' WATERSHED COMMUNITY IN LAUREL, MARYLAND, WHERE SGT. EDWARDS ARRIVED FOR WHAT HE THOUGHT WOULD BE HIS FINAL INTERVIEW IN THE APPLICATION PROCESS.
