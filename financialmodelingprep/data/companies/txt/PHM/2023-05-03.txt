PULTEGROUP ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.16 PER SHARE. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PULTEGROUP, INC. (NYSE: PHM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.16 PER COMMON SHARE PAYABLE JULY 5, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 20, 2023. ABOUT PULTEGROUP PULTEGROUP, INC. (NYSE: PHM), BASED IN ATLANTA, GEORGIA, IS ONE OF AMERICA'S LARGEST HOMEBUILDING COMPANIES WITH OPERATIONS IN MORE THAN 40 MARKETS THROUGHOUT THE COUNTRY. THROUGH ITS BRAND PORTFOLIO THAT INCLUDES CENTEX, PULTE HOME.
