TWO UNITED STATES ARMY VETERANS AWARDED NEW MORTGAGE-FREE HOMES IN HOUSTON THROUGH PULTEGROUP'S BUILT TO HONOR® PROGRAM. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN A CELEBRATORY DEDICATION EVENT, U.S. ARMY SERGEANT JAMES FORD AND U.S. ARMY SPECIALIST KISHA DORSEY WERE PRESENTED WITH THE KEYS TO THEIR BRAND-NEW, MORTGAGE-FREE PULTE HOMES IN THE WINDROW COMMUNITY LOCATED IN HOCKLEY, TEXAS. THE TWO HOMES WERE AWARDED THROUGH PULTEGROUP'S BUILT TO HONOR® PROGRAM, WHICH PROVIDES THE GIFT OF A NEW HOME TO DESERVING VETERANS AND THEIR FAMILIES.
