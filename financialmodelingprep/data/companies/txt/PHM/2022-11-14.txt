PULTEGROUP'S BUILT TO HONOR® PROGRAM DEDICATING 75TH MORTGAGE-FREE HOME ON VETERANS DAY. DALLAS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THIS VETERANS DAY, NATIONAL HOMEBUILDER PULTEGROUP WILL DEDICATE ITS MILESTONE 75TH MORTGAGE-FREE HOME THROUGH ITS BUILT TO HONOR® PROGRAM, WHICH SUPPORTS WOUNDED VETERANS AND THEIR FAMILIES BY PROVIDING MORTGAGE-FREE HOMES. ON FRIDAY, THE COMPANY WILL PRESENT UNITED STATES ARMY SERGEANT DAVID SPEIGHTS WITH THE KEYS TO HIS BRAND-NEW HOME AT PULTE'S WHITEWING TRAILS COMMUNITY IN PRINCETON, TEXAS.
