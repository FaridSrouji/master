PULTE HOMES OPENS NEW COMMUNITY OF AFFORDABLY PRICED HOME DESIGNS IN LOUISVILLE. LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PULTE HOMES ANNOUNCES THE OPENING OF ITS NEW COMMUNITY IN LOUISVILLE, THE TRAILS AT BELMOND. COMBINING SPACIOUS AND AFFORDABLY PRICED FLOOR PLANS WITH A GREAT LOCATION JUST MINUTES AWAY FROM OUTDOOR RECREATION AND SHOPPING AND DINING OPTIONS, THIS COMMUNITY IS AN IDEAL PLACE FOR GROWING FAMILIES TO CALL HOME.
