PULTEGROUP GIFTS NEW MORTGAGE-FREE HOME TO WOUNDED ARMY VETERAN IN TENNESSEE. NASHVILLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITED STATES ARMY SPECIALIST CHRISTOPHER LEWIS WAS PRESENTED WITH THE KEYS TO HIS NEW, MORTGAGE-FREE CENTEX HOME DURING A SPECIAL DEDICATION CEREMONY LAST MONTH.
