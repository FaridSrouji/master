PULTEGROUP INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY 7% TO $0.16 PER SHARE. ATLANTA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PULTEGROUP, INC. (NYSE: PHM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS VOTED TO APPROVE A 7% INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.16 PER COMMON SHARE. THE INCREASE WILL BE EFFECTIVE WITH THE COMPANY'S NEXT SCHEDULED DIVIDEND, WHICH IS PAYABLE JANUARY 3, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 14, 2022.
