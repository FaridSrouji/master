DEL WEBB ANNOUNCES NEW ACTIVE ADULT COMMUNITY IN MICHIGAN. DETROIT--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DEL WEBB, THE NATION'S LEADING BUILDER OF ACTIVE ADULT COMMUNITIES FOR THOSE 55 AND OLDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS NEWEST COMMUNITY IN SOUTHEAST MICHIGAN, KENSINGTON RIDGE BY DEL WEBB. GRAND OPENING IN SPRING 2023, THIS AGE-RESTRICTED COMMUNITY WILL OFFER RESORT-STYLE AMENITIES AND NEW RANCH HOME DESIGNS FROM DEL WEBB'S GENYOU™ COLLECTION IN MILFORD TOWNSHIP.
