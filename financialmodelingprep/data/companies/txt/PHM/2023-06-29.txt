PULTEGROUP GRAND OPENS FIRST COMMUNITIES IN GREENVILLE AND COLUMBIA, SOUTH CAROLINA. GREENVILLE, S.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PULTEGROUP, INC. (NYSE: PHM) ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST HOMEBUILDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF ITS FIRST COMMUNITIES IN GREENVILLE AND COLUMBIA, SOUTH CAROLINA. THE COMPANY PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ITS ENTRY INTO GREENVILLE IN MARCH, AND COLUMBIA LAST AUGUST. UNDER ITS PULTE HOMES BRAND, INDIGO PARK IS NOW OPEN IN EASLEY, SOUTH CAROLINA. THIS NEW CONSTRUCTION ENCLAVE OF 62 SINGLE-FAMILY HOMES MARKS THE COMPANY'S FIRST COMMUNITY IN THE GREENVILLE MARKET. IND.
