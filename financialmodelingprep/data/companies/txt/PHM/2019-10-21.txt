PULTEGROUP, INC. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. PULTEGROUP, INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 2,710.365 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,649.366 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 273.104 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 289.535 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.99 COMPARED TO USD 1.01 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 7,196.044 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 7,189.181 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 680.902 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 784.375 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.44 COMPARED TO USD 2.72 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.44 COMPARED TO USD 2.71 A YEAR AGO.
