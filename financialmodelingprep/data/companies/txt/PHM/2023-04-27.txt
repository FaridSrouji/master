PULTEGROUP ANNOUNCES NATIONAL PARTNERSHIP WITH BOYS & GIRLS CLUBS OF AMERICA. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PULTEGROUP, INC. (NYSE: PHM), ONE OF AMERICA'S LARGEST HOMEBUILDERS, HAS LAUNCHED A NATIONWIDE PARTNERSHIP WITH BOYS & GIRLS CLUBS OF AMERICA, A LEADING YOUTH-DEVELOPMENT ORGANIZATION THAT PROVIDES SAFE PLACES, CARING MENTORS AND LIFE-ENHANCING EXPERIENCES FOR YOUNG PEOPLE ACROSS THE NATION. THE PARTNERSHIP WILL FOCUS ON INSPIRING KIDS AND TEENS TO ACHIEVE ACADEMIC SUCCESS AND LEARN ABOUT THE VARIOUS CAREER OPPORTUNITIES AVAILABLE IN THE HOMEBUILDING INDUSTRY. PULT.
