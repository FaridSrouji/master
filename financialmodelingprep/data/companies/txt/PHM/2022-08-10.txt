KIMBLEWICK BY DEL WEBB'S HIGHLY ANTICIPATED AMENITY CENTER SET TO OPEN SUMMER 2023. INDIANAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DEL WEBB, THE NATION'S LEADING BUILDER OF ACTIVE ADULT COMMUNITIES FOR THOSE 55 AND OLDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED KIMBLEWICK BY DEL WEBB'S HIGHLY ANTICIPATED CLUBHOUSE AND AMENITY CENTER, KNOWN AS THE CARRIAGE HOUSE, WILL GRAND OPEN AT THE INDIANAPOLIS-AREA COMMUNITY IN SUMMER 2023. “THE CARRIAGE HOUSE WILL HAVE THE PERFECT AMOUNT OF FUNCTIONAL SPACE COMBINED WITH RESORT-INSPIRED INDOOR AND OUTDOOR AMENITIES TO MAKE IT THE HEART OF KIMBLEWICK BY DEL WEBB,” SAID SCOTT MAI
