PULTEGROUP INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY 17% TO $0.14 PER SHARE. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PULTEGROUP, INC. (NYSE: PHM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS VOTED TO APPROVE A 17% INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.14 PER COMMON SHARE. THE INCREASE IS EFFECTIVE WITH THE COMPANY'S NEXT DIVIDEND WHICH IS PAYABLE JANUARY 5, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 16, 2020. “PULTEGROUP'S STRONG OPERATING AND FINANCIAL RESULTS HAVE AGAIN PUT US IN POSITION TO RAISE OUR DIVIDEND,” SAID RYAN MARSHALL, PUL
