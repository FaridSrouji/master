PULTEGROUP INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY 7% TO $0.15 PER SHARE. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PULTEGROUP, INC. (NYSE: PHM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS VOTED TO APPROVE A 7% INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.15 PER COMMON SHARE.
