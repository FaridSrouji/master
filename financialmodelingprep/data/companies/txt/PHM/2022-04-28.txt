PULTEGROUP, INC. REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PULTEGROUP, INC. (NYSE: PHM) ANNOUNCED TODAY FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2022. FOR THE QUARTER, THE COMPANY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $455 MILLION, OR $1.83 PER SHARE. IN THE PRIOR YEAR, THE COMPANY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $304 MILLION, OR $1.13 PER SHARE, AND ADJUSTED NET INCOME OF $343 MILLION, OR $1.28 PER SHARE.
