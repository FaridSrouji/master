INTERNATIONAL PAPER ANNOUNCES PROPOSED SENIOR NOTES OFFERING BY SYLVAMO CORPORATION. MEMPHIS, TENN., AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL PAPER (NYSE: IP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY, SYLVAMO CORPORATION ("SYLVAMO"), INTENDS TO OFFER $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2029 IN CONNECTION WITH THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SPIN-OFF OF INTERNATIONAL PAPER'S PRINTING PAPERS BUSINESS.
