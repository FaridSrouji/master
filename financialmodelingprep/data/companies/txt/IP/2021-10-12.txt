INTERNATIONAL PAPER ANNOUNCES $2 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM; DECREASES CASH DIVIDEND 9.8% TO $1.85 PER SHARE ANNUALLY. MEMPHIS, TENN., OCT. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL PAPER TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED A SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM TO ACQUIRE UP TO $2 BILLION OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK.
