INTERNATIONAL PAPER ANNOUNCES THE RECORD DATE AND DISTRIBUTION DATE IN CONNECTION WITH THE SPIN-OFF OF ITS PRINTING PAPERS BUSINESS. MEMPHIS, TENN., AUG. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL PAPER (NYSE: IP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE TIMING AND ADDITIONAL DETAILS REGARDING THE SPINOFF OF ITS PRINTING PAPER BUSINESS, WHICH WILL BE KNOWN AS SYLVAMO CORPORATION ("SYLVAMO").
