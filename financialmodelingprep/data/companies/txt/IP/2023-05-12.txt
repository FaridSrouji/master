INTERNATIONAL PAPER RECOMMENDS SHAREHOLDERS REJECT MINI-TENDER OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT CORPORATION. MEMPHIS, TENN. , MAY 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL PAPER (NYSE: IP) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS RECEIVED NOTICE OF AN UNSOLICITED MINI-TENDER OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT CORPORATION ("TRC CAPITAL") TO PURCHASE UP TO 3,000,000 SHARES OF INTERNATIONAL PAPER COMPANY ("INTERNATIONAL PAPER") COMMON STOCK FROM INTERNATIONAL PAPER SHAREHOLDERS.
