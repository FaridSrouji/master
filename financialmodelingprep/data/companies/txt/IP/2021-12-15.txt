INTERNATIONAL PAPER TO BUILD STATE-OF-THE-ART CORRUGATED PACKAGING PLANT IN ATGLEN, PENNSYLVANIA TO MEET GROWING CUSTOMER DEMAND IN THE REGION. MEMPHIS, TENN., DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL PAPER (NYSE: IP) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL BUILD A STATE-OF-THE-ART CORRUGATED PACKAGING PLANT IN ATGLEN, PENNSYLVANIA.
