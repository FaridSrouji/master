IDG INSIDER PRO AND COMPUTERWORLD NAME INTERNATIONAL PAPER TO 2021 LIST OF 100 BEST PLACES TO WORK IN IT. MEMPHIS, TENN., JULY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IDG'S INSIDER PRO (HTTPS://WWW.IDGINSIDERPRO.COM) AND COMPUTERWORLD (HTTP://WWW.COMPUTERWORLD.COM) HAVE ANNOUNCED INTERNATIONAL PAPER AS ONE OF THE 2021 BEST PLACES TO WORK IN IT.
