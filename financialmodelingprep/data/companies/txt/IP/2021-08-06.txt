INTERNATIONAL PAPER COMPLETES THE SALE OF ITS KWIDZYN MILL IN POLAND. MEMPHIS, TENN., AUG. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL PAPER (NYSE: IP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED SALE OF ITS KWIDZYN PULP AND PAPER MILL AND SUPPORTING OPERATIONS TO MAYR-MELNHOF GROUP.
