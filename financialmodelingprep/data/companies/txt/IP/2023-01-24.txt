INTERNATIONAL PAPER REACHES AGREEMENT TO SELL OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN ILIM JOINT VENTURE. MEMPHIS, TENN. , JAN. 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL PAPER (NYSE: IP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS 50% INTEREST IN ILIM SA, THE HOLDING COMPANY FOR ITS ILIM JOINT VENTURE (JV), TO ITS JV PARTNERS FOR $484MM (USD) EQUITY VALUE.
