INTERNATIONAL PAPER ANNOUNCES PRICING OF SENIOR NOTES OFFERING BY SYLVAMO CORPORATION. MEMPHIS, TENN., AUG. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL PAPER COMPANY (NYSE: IP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY, SYLVAMO CORPORATION ("SYLVAMO"), HAS PRICED $450 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 7.000% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2029.
