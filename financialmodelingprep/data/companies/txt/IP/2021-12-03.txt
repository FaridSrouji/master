INTERNATIONAL PAPER RECEIVES SBTI APPROVAL FOR ITS SCIENCE-BASED CARBON REDUCTION TARGET. MEMPHIS, TENN., DEC. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL PAPER (NYSE: IP) TODAY ANNOUNCED OUR 35% GREENHOUSE GAS REDUCTION TARGET HAS BEEN APPROVED BY SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE (SBTI) AS CONSISTENT WITH LEVELS REQUIRED TO MEET THE GOALS OF THE PARIS AGREEMENT.
