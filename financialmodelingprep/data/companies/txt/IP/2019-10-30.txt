INTERNATIONAL PAPER COMPANY REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. INTERNATIONAL PAPER COMPANY ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 5,568 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 5,901 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 344 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 562 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.88 COMPARED TO USD 1.38 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.87 COMPARED TO USD 1.37 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 16,878 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 17,355 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 1,060 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,696 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 2.67 COMPARED TO USD 3.28 A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.67 COMPARED TO USD 4.12 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 2.65 COMPARED TO USD 3.25 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.65 COMPARED TO USD 4.08 A YEAR AGO.
