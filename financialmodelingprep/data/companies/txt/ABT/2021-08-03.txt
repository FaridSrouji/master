ABBOTT RECEIVES FDA CLEARANCE FOR ITS IMAGING TECHNOLOGY USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR VESSELS IN THE HEART. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) CLEARANCE FOR ITS LATEST OPTICAL COHERENCE TOMOGRAPHY (OCT) IMAGING PLATFORM POWERED BY THE COMPANY'S NEW ULTREON SOFTWARE.
