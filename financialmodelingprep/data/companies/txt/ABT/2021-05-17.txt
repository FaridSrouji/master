ABBOTT RECEIVES CE MARK FOR NAVITOR™, THE LATEST-GENERATION TRANSCATHETER AORTIC VALVE IMPLANTATION (TAVI) SYSTEM TO TREAT AORTIC STENOSIS. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., MAY 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED CE MARK FOR ITS LATEST-GENERATION TRANSCATHETER AORTIC VALVE IMPLANTATION (TAVI) SYSTEM, NAVITOR™, MAKING THE MINIMALLY INVASIVE DEVICE AVAILABLE FOR PEOPLE IN EUROPE WITH SEVERE AORTIC STENOSIS WHO ARE AT HIGH OR EXTREME SURGICAL RISK.
