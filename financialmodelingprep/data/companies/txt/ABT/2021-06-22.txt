ABBOTT EARNS FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND RECOMMENDATION FOR HIGH SCHOOL STEM INTERNS TO RECEIVE COLLEGE CREDIT. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HIGH SCHOOL STUDENTS WHO COMPLETE ABBOTT'S AWARD-WINNING SUMMER INTERNSHIP PROGRAM ARE NOW ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE COLLEGE CREDIT FOR THE EXPERIENCE IN A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND EFFORT THAT CAN SERVE AS A MODEL FOR OTHER COMPANIES TO SUPPORT STEM EDUCATION.
