ABBOTT'S XIENCE™ STENT RECEIVES EUROPEAN APPROVAL FOR ONE-MONTH DUAL ANTI-PLATELET THERAPY (DAPT) FOR HIGH BLEEDING RISK PATIENTS. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., APRIL 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS XIENCE STENT HAS RECEIVED CE MARK IN EUROPE FOR SHORTER DURATION OF DUAL ANTI-PLATELET THERAPY (DAPT) – AS SHORT AS 28 DAYS, THE SHORTEST INDICATION AVAILABLE IN THE WORLD – FOR PATIENTS WITH HIGH BLEEDING RISK (HBR).
