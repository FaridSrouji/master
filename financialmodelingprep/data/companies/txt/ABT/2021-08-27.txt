GUIDE-HF STUDY DATA SHOW ABBOTT'S CARDIOMEMS™ DEVICE CAN IMPROVE CARE FOR MORE PATIENTS WITH HEART FAILURE. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., AUG. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY ANNOUNCED RESULTS OF THE LANDMARK GUIDE-HF CLINICAL TRIAL, A 1,000 PATIENT RANDOMIZED STUDY DESIGNED TO ASSESS THE BENEFITS OF THE CARDIOMEMS™ HF SYSTEM IN PEOPLE LIVING WITH NYHA CLASS II, III AND IV HEART FAILURE.
