ABT LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES ABBOTT LABORATORIES INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , OCT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN ABBOTT LABORATORIES ("ABBOTT" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: ABT) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF ABBOTT INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD.
