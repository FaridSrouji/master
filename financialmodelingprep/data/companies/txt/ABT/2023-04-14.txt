FDA CLEARS READER FOR ABBOTT'S FREESTYLE LIBRE® 3 SYSTEM. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., APRIL 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS CLEARED A READER FOR ITS FREESTYLE LIBRE® 3 INTEGRATED CONTINUOUS GLUCOSE MONITORING (ICGM) SYSTEM, WHICH FEATURES THE WORLD'S SMALLEST, THINNEST AND MOST DISCREET5 GLUCOSE SENSOR.
