ABBOTT RECEIVES FDA APPROVAL FOR MINIMALLY INVASIVE PORTICO™ WITH FLEXNAV™ TAVR SYSTEM TO TREAT PATIENTS WITH AORTIC VALVE DISEASE. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., SEPT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED THE COMPANY'S PORTICO™ WITH FLEXNAV™ TRANSCATHETER AORTIC VALVE REPLACEMENT (TAVR) SYSTEM TO TREAT PEOPLE WITH SYMPTOMATIC, SEVERE AORTIC STENOSIS WHO ARE AT HIGH OR EXTREME RISK FOR OPEN-HEART SURGERY.
