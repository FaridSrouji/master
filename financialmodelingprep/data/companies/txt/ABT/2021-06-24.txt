PEOPLE WHO EXERCISE INTENSELY COULD IMPROVE RECOVERY BY ADJUSTING THEIR HYDRATION HABITS. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., JUNE 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A NEW HARRIS POLL SURVEY FINDS THE MAJORITY OF ADULTS IN THE U.S. WHO EXERCISE INTENSELY UNDERSTAND THE IMPORTANCE OF MAINTAINING HYDRATION THROUGHOUT A WORKOUT (93%) AND THAT HYDRATION IS IMPORTANT FOR MUSCLE RECOVERY (89%), YET MANY HAVE MISCONCEPTIONS ABOUT THE TYPE OF HYDRATION THAT MAY IMPACT THEIR ABILITY TO PERFORM AND RECOVER OPTIMALLY.
