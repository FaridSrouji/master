ABBOTT EXPANDS PERIPHERAL VASCULAR OFFERINGS WITH ACQUISITION OF WALK VASCULAR, LLC. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED WALK VASCULAR, LLC, A COMMERCIAL-STAGE MEDICAL DEVICE COMPANY WITH A MINIMALLY INVASIVE MECHANICAL ASPIRATION THROMBECTOMY SYSTEM DESIGNED TO REMOVE PERIPHERAL BLOOD CLOTS.
