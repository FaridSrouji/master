ABBOTT COMMITS TO REDUCING MALNUTRITION GLOBALLY WITH LAUNCH OF ABBOTT CENTER FOR MALNUTRITION SOLUTIONS. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., SEPT. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE CREATION OF THE ABBOTT CENTER FOR MALNUTRITION SOLUTIONS, AN INNOVATION HUB FOCUSED ON REDUCING MALNUTRITION AROUND THE WORLD.
