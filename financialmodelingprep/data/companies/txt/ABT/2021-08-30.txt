LATE-BREAKING DATA SHOWS ABBOTT'S AMPLATZER™ AMULET™ OCCLUDER OFFERS SUPERIOR LEFT ATRIAL APPENDAGE CLOSURE COMPARED TO WATCHMAN‡ DEVICE FOR PEOPLE WITH ATRIAL FIBRILLATION AT RISK OF STROKE.
