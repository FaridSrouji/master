ABBOTT INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, MARKING 51 CONSECUTIVE YEARS OF DIVIDEND GROWTH. DIVIDEND INCREASED BY 8.5% REFLECTS ABBOTT'S LONGSTANDING COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABLE GROWTH AND SHAREHOLDER RETURNS ABBOTT PARK, ILL., DEC. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS INCREASED THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND TO 51 CENTS PER SHARE, AN INCREASE OF 8.5%.
