KAUTEX WINS FIRST ORDER FOR PENTATONIC SKID PLATE FOR BATTERY ELECTRIC VEHICLES. BONN, GERMANY--( BUSINESS WIRE )--KAUTEX TEXTRON GMBH & CO. KG (KAUTEX), A TEXTRON INC. (NYSE: TXT) COMPANY, ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED THE FIRST ORDER FROM AN AUTOMOTIVE OEM FOR A THERMOPLASTIC COMPOSITE UNDERBODY BATTERY PROTECTION SKID PLATE. THE SKID PLATE IS PART OF THE COMPANY'S NEW PENTATONIC BATTERY SYSTEM PRODUCT LINE SUPPORTING BATTERY ELECTRIC VEHICLE PRODUCTION.
