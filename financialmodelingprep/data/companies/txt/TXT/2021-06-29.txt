E-Z-GO® PROUD TO AGAIN SUPPORT "CAPITAL ONE'S THE MATCH". AUGUSTA, GA., JUNE 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- E-Z-GO WILL AGAIN BE THE RIDE OF CHOICE FOR "CAPITAL ONE'S THE MATCH," THE EMMY-WINNING SERIES OF TELEVISED LIVE GOLF COMPETITIONS THAT PIT GOLF ICONS AND PROFESSIONAL ATHLETES AGAINST EACH OTHER ON WORLD-RENOWNED COURSES TO SUPPORT CHARITIES AND COMMUNITIES NATIONWIDE.
