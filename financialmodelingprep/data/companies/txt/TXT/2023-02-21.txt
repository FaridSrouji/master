BELL 505 BECOMES WORLD'S FIRST SINGLE ENGINE HELICOPTER TO FLY USING 100% SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL. FORT WORTH, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BELL TEXTRON INC., A TEXTRON INC. (NYSE: TXT) COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE BELL 505 COMPLETED ITS FIRST FLIGHT FUELED SOLELY BY 100% SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL (SAF), MARKING THE FIRST-EVER SINGLE ENGINE HELICOPTER TO FLY WITH 100% SAF. BELL COLLABORATED WITH SAFRAN HELICOPTER ENGINES, NESTE, GKN AEROSPACE AND VIRENT INC. TO MAKE THIS BELL 505 FLIGHT POSSIBLE.
