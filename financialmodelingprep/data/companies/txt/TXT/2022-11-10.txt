KAUTEX TEXTRON GMBH & CO. KG RECEIVES INTERNATIONALE GESELLSCHAFT FÜR KUNSTSTOFFTECHNIK, SPE CENTRAL EUROPE AUTOMOTIVE AWARD. BONN, GERMANY--( BUSINESS WIRE )--KAUTEX TEXTRON GMBH & CO., KG (KAUTEX), A TEXTRON INC. (NYSE: TXT) COMPANY, ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED AN AUTOMOTIVE DIVISION AWARD FROM THE INTERNATIONALE GESELLSCHAFT FÜR KUNSTSTOFFTECHNIK, SPE CENTRAL EUROPE FOR ITS PENTATONIC BATTERY SYSTEM. THE AWARD WAS PRESENTED AT THE SOCIETY'S ANNUAL AWARDS EVENT IN DUSSELDORF, GERMANY.
