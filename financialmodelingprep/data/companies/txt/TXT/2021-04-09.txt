WHEELS UP AND BELL TEXTRON ANNOUNCE STRATEGIC INITIATIVE TO TRANSFORM THE DREAM OF URBAN MOBILITY INTO A REALITY. NEW YORK, APRIL 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHEELS UP, THE LEADING BRAND IN PRIVATE AVIATION, TODAY ANNOUNCES AN INITIATIVE WITH BELL TEXTRON, A TEXTRON INC. (NYSE: TXT) COMPANY, TO LEAD THE INDUSTRY BOTH IN SHORT- AND LONG-TERM SOLUTIONS WITH VERTICAL TAKE-OFF AND LANDING (VTOL). THE COLLABORATION BETWEEN THE INDUSTRY LEADERS WILL SET A NEW STANDARD IN ADDRESSING THE GROWING DEMAND FOR CONVENIENT INTRA-URBAN TRAVEL FROM/TO CITY-CENTERS AND REGIONAL AIRPORT HELIPADS.
