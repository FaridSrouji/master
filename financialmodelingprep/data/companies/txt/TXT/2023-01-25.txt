TEXTRON REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER 2022 RESULTS; ANNOUNCES 2023 FINANCIAL OUTLOOK. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TEXTRON INC. (NYSE: TXT) TODAY REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER 2022 INCOME FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $1.07 PER SHARE, COMPARED WITH $0.93, OR $0.94 PER SHARE OF ADJUSTED INCOME FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS, A NON-GAAP MEASURE THAT IS DEFINED AND RECONCILED TO GAAP IN AN ATTACHMENT TO THIS RELEASE, IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021.
