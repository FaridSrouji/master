TEXTRON INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 1, 2020. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TEXTRON INC. DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.02 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY’S COMMON STOCK. ALL DIVIDENDS WILL BE PAID ON JANUARY 1, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 13, 2019.
