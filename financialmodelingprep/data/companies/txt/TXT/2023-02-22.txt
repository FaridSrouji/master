TEXTRON DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TEXTRON INC. (NYSE:TXT) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.02 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. ALL DIVIDENDS WILL BE PAID ON APRIL 1, 2023 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 10, 2023. ABOUT TEXTRON INC. TEXTRON INC. IS A MULTI-INDUSTRY COMPANY THAT LEVERAGES ITS GLOBAL NETWORK OF AIRCRAFT, DEFENSE, INDUSTRIAL AND FINANCE BUSINESSES TO PROVIDE CUSTOMERS WITH INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS AND SERVICES.
