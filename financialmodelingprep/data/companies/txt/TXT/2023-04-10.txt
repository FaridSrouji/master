LISA ATHERTON NAMED PRESIDENT & CEO OF BELL. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TEXTRON INC. (NYSE: TXT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT LISA ATHERTON HAS BEEN NAMED PRESIDENT & CEO OF THE BELL SEGMENT, EFFECTIVE APRIL 28, 2023. ATHERTON, MOST RECENTLY THE CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER OF BELL, SUCCEEDS MITCH SNYDER, WHO WILL BE RETIRING AFTER MORE THAN SEVEN YEARS AS PRESIDENT & CEO OF BELL AND NEARLY 20 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY. ATHERTON SERVED AS PRESIDENT & CEO OF TEXTRON SYSTEMS FROM 2017 UNTIL JANUARY 2023 WHEN SHE WAS NAMED COO AT BELL.
