TEXTRON REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TEXTRON INC. (NYSE: TXT) TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2023 NET INCOME OF $0.92 PER SHARE, AS COMPARED TO $0.88 PER SHARE IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. ADJUSTED NET INCOME, A NON-GAAP MEASURE THAT IS DEFINED AND RECONCILED TO GAAP IN AN ATTACHMENT TO THIS RELEASE, WAS $1.05 PER SHARE FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023, COMPARED TO $0.97 PER SHARE IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. “IN THE QUARTER, WE SAW REVENUE GROWTH AT AVIATION, INDUSTRIAL AND SYSTEMS,” SAID TEXTRON C.
