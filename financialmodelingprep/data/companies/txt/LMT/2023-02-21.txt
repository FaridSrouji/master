LOCKHEED MARTIN DELIVERS FIRST C-130J-30 SUPER HERCULES TO THE INDONESIAN AIR FORCE. THE IDAF WILL RECEIVE A TOTAL OF FIVE SUPER HERCULES TACTICAL AIRLIFTERS MARIETTA, GA. , FEB. 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) DELIVERED THE FIRST OF FIVE C-130J-30 SUPER HERCULES TACTICAL AIRLIFTERS TO THE INDONESIAN AIR FORCE (IDAF) DURING A CEREMONY HERE TODAY, COMMEMORATING A NEW ERA IN HERCULES OPERATIONS FOR THIS LONGTIME C-130 OPERATOR.
