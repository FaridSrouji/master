CANADA ANNOUNCES THE PROCUREMENT OF THE F-35 LIGHTNING II. F-35 RANKED AS MOST CAPABLE, SURVIVABLE AND AFFORDABLE SOLUTION FORT WORTH, TEXAS , JAN. 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE GOVERNMENT OF CANADA ANNOUNCED TODAY IT IS PROCURING LOCKHEED MARTIN'S 5TH GENERATION F-35 LIGHTNING II AIRCRAFT AS A RESULT OF THE FUTURE FIGHTER CAPABILITY PROJECT COMPETITION. THE ROYAL CANADIAN AIR FORCE WILL RECEIVE 88 F-35A MULTIROLE STEALTH FIGHTERS, A SUSTAINMENT SOLUTION TAILORED TO CANADA'S SOVEREIGN REQUIREMENTS AND A COMPREHENSIVE TRAINING PROGRAM.
