PENTAGON AND LOCKHEED MARTIN AGREE TO F-35 SUSTAINMENT CONTRACTS. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, SEPT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE F-35 JOINT PROGRAM OFFICE AWARDED THE LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) INDUSTRY TEAM ANNUALIZED CONTRACTS COVERING FISCAL YEARS 2021-2023 TO SUPPORT OPERATIONS AND SUSTAINMENT OF THE GLOBAL F-35 FLEET, SUPPORTING MISSION READINESS AND FURTHER REDUCING COSTS.
