SIXTH GPS III SATELLITE BUILT BY LOCKHEED MARTIN LAUNCHES AS PART OF CONSTELLATION MODERNIZATION. LITTLETON, COLO. , JAN. 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SIXTH GLOBAL POSITIONING SYSTEM III (GPS III) SATELLITE DESIGNED AND BUILT BY LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) HAS BEEN LAUNCHED AND IS PROPELLING TO ITS OPERATIONAL ORBIT APPROXIMATELY 12,550 MILES ABOVE EARTH, WHERE IT WILL CONTRIBUTE TO THE ONGOING MODERNIZATION OF THE U.S. SPACE FORCE'S GPS CONSTELLATION.
