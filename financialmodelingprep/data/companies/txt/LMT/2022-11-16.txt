LOCKHEED MARTIN-BUILT ORION SPACECRAFT LAUNCHED TO THE MOON ON HISTORIC ARTEMIS I MISSION. KENNEDY SPACE CENTER, FLA. , NOV. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH THE BACKDROP OF A FLORIDA STARLIT SKY, NASA'S ARTEMIS I MISSION, WITH THE LOCKHEED MARTIN-BUILT [NYSE: LMT] ORION SPACECRAFT ON TOP, MAJESTICALLY LAUNCHED TO THE MOON AND PROPELLED THE WORLD INTO A NEW ERA OF HUMAN DEEP SPACE EXPLORATION.
