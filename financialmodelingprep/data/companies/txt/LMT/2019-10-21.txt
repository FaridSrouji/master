LOCKHEED MARTIN CORPORATION REVISES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019; PROVIDES NET SALES GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2020. LOCKHEED MARTIN CORPORATION REVISED EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019. FOR THE YEAR, THE COMPANY EXPECTS REVISED NET SALES OF $59,100 MILLION, OPERATING PROFIT OF $8,350 MILLION AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $21.55 COMPARED TO PREVIOUSLY EXPECT NET SALES TO BE IN THE RANGE OF $58,250 MILLION TO $59,750 MILLION, OPERATING PROFIT TO BE IN THE RANGE OF $8,260 MILLION TO $8,410 MILLION AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE TO BE IN THE RANGE OF $20.85 TO $21.15.THE CORPORATION EXPECTS ITS 2020 NET SALES TO INCREASE TO APPROXIMATELY $62.0 BILLION.
