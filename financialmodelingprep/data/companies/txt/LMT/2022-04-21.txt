LOCKHEED MARTIN FORECASTS JOB GROWTH IN PENNSYLVANIA AND FLORIDA IN 2022. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, APRIL 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE:LMT) IS ANNOUNCING THE GRAND OPENING OF A NEW FACILITY THAT EXPANDS ITS JOHNSTOWN, PENNSYLVANIA, OPERATIONS. THE JOHNSTOWN BUSINESS EXPECTS TO HIRE MORE THAN 80 MECHANICS AND TECHNICIANS IN THE NEXT YEAR TO SUPPORT COMPONENT PRODUCTION FOR THE NEWEST F-16S BEING MANUFACTURED IN GREENVILLE, SOUTH CAROLINA.
