SAP EXPANDS 25-YEAR PARTNERSHIP WITH LOCKHEED MARTIN. LOCKHEED MARTIN CHOOSES RISE WITH SAP AS THE SECURE SOLUTION TO FUEL 1LMX TRANSFORMATION BETHESDA, MD. , JAN. 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN CORPORATION (NYSE: LMT) AND SAP SE (NYSE: SAP) ANNOUNCED TODAY AN EXPANSION OF THEIR STRATEGIC RELATIONSHIP TO TRANSFORM THE MISSION-CRITICAL BUSINESS SYSTEMS THAT POWER LOCKHEED MARTIN'S ONELM TRANSFORMATION PROGRAM (1LMX).
