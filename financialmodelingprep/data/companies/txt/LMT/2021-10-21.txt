NANORACKS, VOYAGER SPACE, AND LOCKHEED MARTIN TEAMING TO DEVELOP COMMERCIAL SPACE STATION. DENVER, OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NANORACKS, IN COLLABORATION WITH VOYAGER SPACE AND LOCKHEED MARTIN [NYSE: LMT], HAS FORMED A TEAM TO DEVELOP THE FIRST-EVER FREE FLYING COMMERCIAL SPACE STATION. THE SPACE STATION, KNOWN AS STARLAB, WILL BE A CONTINUOUSLY CREWED COMMERCIAL PLATFORM, DEDICATED TO CONDUCTING CRITICAL RESEARCH, FOSTERING INDUSTRIAL ACTIVITY, AND ENSURING CONTINUED U.S. PRESENCE AND LEADERSHIP IN LOW-EARTH ORBIT.
