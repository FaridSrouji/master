LOCKHEED MARTIN ANNOUNCES NEW SKUNK WORKS® LEADER. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, MARCH 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE:LMT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JOHN CLARK HAS BEEN NAMED THE VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER OF ADVANCED DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS (ADP) – ALSO KNOWN AS SKUNK WORKS® – SUCCEEDING JEFF BABIONE WHO WILL RETIRE AT THE END OF THE MONTH. THIS CHANGE IS EFFECTIVE APRIL 4.
