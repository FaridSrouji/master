LOCKHEED MARTIN TO HELP NASA UNCOVER MYSTERIES OF VENUS WITH VERITAS AND DAVINCI+ SPACECRAFT. DENVER, JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO LEARN MORE ABOUT HOW TERRESTRIAL PLANETS EVOLVE OVER TIME, NASA SELECTED THE VERITAS AND DAVINCI+ MISSIONS FOR ITS DISCOVERY PROGRAM, BOTH BOUND FOR VENUS. LOCKHEED MARTIN WILL DESIGN, BUILD AND OPERATE BOTH SPACECRAFT.
