LOCKHEED MARTIN CUBESATS SUCCESSFULLY VALIDATE ESSENTIAL MANEUVERS FOR ON-ORBIT SERVICING. LM 50 SATELLITES DEMONSTRATE ADVANCED NAVIGATIONAL ALGORITHMS AND CLOUD-BASED OPERATIONS LITTLETON, COLO. , APRIL 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS IN-SPACE UPGRADE SATELLITE SYSTEM (LM LINUSS™) ACCOMPLISHED A SUCCESSFUL ON-ORBIT DEMONSTRATION, PROVING HOW SMALL SATELLITES CAN SERVE AN ESSENTIAL ROLE IN SUSTAINING CRITICAL SPACE ARCHITECTURES.
LOCKHEED MARTIN REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2023 FINANCIAL RESULTS. LOCKHEED MARTIN CORPORATION [NYSE: LMT] TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2023 NET SALES OF $15.1 BILLION, COMPARED TO $15.0 BILLION IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. NET EARNINGS IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023 WERE $1.7 BILLION, OR $6.61 PER SHARE, COMPARED TO $1.7 BILLION, OR $6.44 PER SHARE, IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. CASH FROM OPERATIONS WAS $1.6 BILLION IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023, COMPARED TO $1.4 BILLION IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. FREE CASH FLOW WAS $1.3 BILLION IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023, COMPARED TO $1.1 BILLION IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022.
