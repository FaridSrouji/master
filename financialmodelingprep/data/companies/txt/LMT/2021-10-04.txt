LOCKHEED MARTIN OPENS INTELLIGENT, ADVANCED HYPERSONIC STRIKE PRODUCTION FACILITY. COURTLAND, ALA., OCT. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) IS BREAKING BARRIERS IN HYPERSONIC TECHNOLOGY, ADDING TO ITS WORLD-CLASS PORTFOLIO ANOTHER MILESTONE WITH THE GRAND OPENING OF AN ADVANCED PRODUCTION FACILITY IN COURTLAND, ALABAMA, FOCUSED ON HYPERSONIC STRIKE PRODUCTION.
