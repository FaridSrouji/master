LOCKHEED MARTIN ON BLUE ORIGIN'S NATIONAL TEAM SELECTED TO DEVELOP HUMAN LUNAR LANDER. NASA'S SUSTAINABLE HUMAN LANDING SYSTEM WILL LAND CREW ON THE MOON FOR ARTEMIS V DENVER , MAY 19, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) IS ON THE TEAM THAT HAS WON A CONTRACT FROM NASA TO DEVELOP AND DEMONSTRATE A HUMAN LANDING SYSTEM FOR THE ARTEMIS PROGRAM UNDER THE AGENCY'S HUMAN LANDING SYSTEM PROGRAM. THE GOAL OF THE PROGRAM IS TO RAPIDLY DEVELOP A SUSTAINABLE HUMAN LUNAR LANDER AND PERFORM A CREWED DEMONSTRATION FLIGHT TO THE LUNAR SURFACE FOR ARTEMIS V.
