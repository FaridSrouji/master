LOCKHEED MARTIN'S FIRST LM 400 MULTI-MISSION SPACE VEHICLE COMPLETES DEMANDING TESTING MILESTONE. DENVER , APRIL 19, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE FIRST LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) LM 400, A VERSATILE, MID-SIZED SATELLITE WHICH CAN BE ADAPTED FOR MILITARY, CIVIL OR COMMERCIAL USES, HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED ELECTROMAGNETIC INTERFERENCE/ELECTROMAGNETIC COMPATIBILITY TESTING. THIS TRIAL IS CRUCIAL TO ENSURE THAT SIGNALS FROM THE SATELLITE BUS COMPONENTS WILL NOT INTERFERE WITH CRITICAL PAYLOADS DURING OPERATIONS.
