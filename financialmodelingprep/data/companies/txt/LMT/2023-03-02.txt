DELIVERY CELEBRATION CEREMONY FOR FIRST F-16 BLOCK 70 IN GREENVILLE, SOUTH CAROLINA, TO BE LIVESTREAMED. GREENVILLE, S.C. , MARCH 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN [NYSE: LMT] HAS ANNOUNCED SENIOR BAHRAINI, U.S. GOVERNMENT, MILITARY AND AEROSPACE INDUSTRY OFFICIALS WILL SOON CELEBRATE THE DELIVERY OF THE FIRST ROYAL BAHRAINI AIR FORCE F-16 BLOCK 70.
