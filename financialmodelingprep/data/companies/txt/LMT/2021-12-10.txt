FINLAND SELECTS F-35 LIGHTNING II AS ITS NEXT FIGHTER. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, DEC. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE FINNISH GOVERNMENT HAS ANNOUNCED LOCKHEED MARTIN'S 5TH GENERATION F-35 LIGHTNING II IS THE AIRCRAFT SELECTED FROM ITS HX FIGHTER PROGRAM. BY SELECTING THE F-35, FINLAND GAINS A SIGNIFICANT CAPABILITY TO ENSURE STABILITY IN THE REGION.
