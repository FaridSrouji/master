LOCKHEED MARTIN AWARDED $1.1 BILLION INITIAL CONTRACT TO PROVIDE NATION'S FIRST SEA-BASED HYPERSONIC STRIKE CAPABILITY. COMPANY WILL INTEGRATE WEAPON SYSTEM ONTO U.S. NAVY SURFACE SHIPS LITTLETON, COLO. , FEB. 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) IS PARTNERING WITH THE U.S. NAVY TO INTEGRATE HYPERSONIC STRIKE CAPABILITY ONTO SURFACE SHIPS.
