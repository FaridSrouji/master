F-35 EXPANDS GLOBAL PRESENCE IN 2021. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, JAN. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) COMPLETED ANOTHER SUCCESSFUL YEAR AS THE F-35 PROGRAM CONTINUED TO EXPAND ITS GLOBAL FOOTPRINT AND ENHANCE OPERATIONAL CAPABILITIES. IN 2021, TWO NEW COUNTRIES – SWITZERLAND AND FINLAND – SELECTED THE F-35 FOR THEIR NEW FIGHTER JET PROGRAMS.
