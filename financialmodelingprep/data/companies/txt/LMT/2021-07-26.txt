LOCKHEED MARTIN REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. BETHESDA, MD., JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN CORPORATION [NYSE: LMT] TODAY REPORTED SECOND QUARTER 2021 NET SALES OF $17.0 BILLION, COMPARED TO $16.2 BILLION IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020.
