LOCKHEED MARTIN REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS. NET SALES OF $15.4 BILLION NET EARNINGS OF $309 MILLION, OR $1.16 PER SHARE, INCLUSIVE OF NON-OPERATIONAL CHARGES OF $1.7 BILLION ($1.4 BILLION, OR $5.16 PER SHARE, AFTER-TAX) CASH FROM OPERATIONS OF $1.3 BILLION AND FREE CASH FLOW OF $1.0 BILLION RETURNED $1.1 BILLION OF CASH TO SHAREHOLDERS THROUGH SHARE REPURCHASES AND DIVIDENDS UPDATES 2022 OUTLOOK FOR SALES AND EARNINGS PER SHARE; MAINTAINS 2022 OUTLOOK FOR SEGMENT OPERATING PROFIT, CASH FROM OPERATIONS, AND FREE CASH FLOW BETHESDA, MD. , JULY 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN CORPORATION [NYSE: LMT] TODAY REPORTED SECOND QUARTER 2022 NET SALES OF $15.4 BILLION, COMPARED TO $17.0 BILLION IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021.
