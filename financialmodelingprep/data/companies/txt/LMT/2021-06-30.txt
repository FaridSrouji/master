SWITZERLAND SELECTS F-35 LIGHTNING II FOR FUTURE AIR DEFENSE REQUIREMENTS. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, JUNE 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SWISS FEDERAL COUNCIL ANNOUNCED LOCKHEED MARTIN'S F-35 LIGHTNING II IS THE AIRCRAFT SELECTED FROM ITS NEW FIGHTER AIRCRAFT COMPETITION. SWITZERLAND WILL BECOME THE 15TH NATION TO JOIN THE F-35 PROGRAM OF RECORD, JOINING SEVERAL EUROPEAN NATIONS.
