UNITED STATES ARMY AND SIKORSKY STRENGTHEN ARMY AVIATION FLEET WITH 10TH H-60 BLACK HAWK HELICOPTER CONTRACT. STRATFORD, CONN. , JUNE 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT AND SIKORSKY, A LOCKHEED MARTIN COMPANY (NYSE: LMT), SIGNED A FIVE-YEAR CONTRACT FOR A BASELINE OF 120 H-60M BLACK HAWK HELICOPTERS, WITH OPTIONS TO REACH A TOTAL OF 255 AIRCRAFT TO BE DELIVERED TO THE U.S. ARMY AND FOREIGN MILITARY SALES (FMS) CUSTOMERS.
