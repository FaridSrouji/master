F-35 BEST AND FINAL OFFER SUBMITTED TO FINNISH GOVERNMENT. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE U.S. GOVERNMENT AND LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) SUBMITTED AN F-35 BEST AND FINAL OFFER (BAFO) ON APRIL 29, 2021, TO THE FINNISH GOVERNMENT IN SUPPORT OF ITS COMPETITION TO REPLACE ITS CURRENT FIGHTER FLEET. THE 5TH GENERATION F-35 PROVIDES UNMATCHED CAPABILITIES, SECURITY OF SUPPLY AND INDUSTRIAL OPPORTUNITIES FOR FINLAND.
