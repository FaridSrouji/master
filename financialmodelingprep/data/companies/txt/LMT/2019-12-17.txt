GOMSPACE, LOCKHEED MARTIN SPACE, ORBITAL MICRO SYSTEMS TEAM UP FOR NEW MICROWAVE SENSING NANOSATELLITE PROJECT. GOMSPACE, LOCKHEED MARTIN SPACE AND ORBITAL MICRO SYSTEMS TEAM UP FOR NEW MICROWAVE SENSING NANOSATELLITE PROJECT.CONTRACT IS WORTH 17 MSEK, WILL BE FINANCED THROUGH AN INDUSTRIAL CORPORATION COMMITMENT MADE BY LOCKHEED MARTIN TO STATE OF DENMARK.LOCKHEED MARTIN WILL FINANCIALLY ASSIST GS TO DESIGN, DEVELOP AN OPTIMISED 6U NANOSATELLITE PLATFORM FOR OMS SENSOR.GS WILL DELIVER INTEGRATED 6U SATELLITE TO OMS BY END OF 2020 FOR EXPECTED LAUNCH AND EVALUATION IN 2021.LOCKHEED MARTIN TO PROVIDE TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE TO REVIEW AND IMPROVE GS QUALITY SYSTEMS AND ENHANCE NANOSATELLITE'S DESIGN LIFE.
