KEYCORP RAISES ITS PRIME LENDING RATE TO 3.50 PERCENT. CLEVELAND , MARCH 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEYCORP (NYSE: KEY) AND ITS BANKING AFFILIATES HAVE RAISED THEIR PRIME LENDING RATE TO 3.50 PERCENT FROM 3.25 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE TOMORROW, MARCH 17, 2022.
