KEYCORP RAISES ITS PRIME LENDING RATE TO 7.50 PERCENT. CLEVELAND , DEC. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEYCORP (NYSE: KEY) AND ITS BANKING AFFILIATES HAVE RAISED THEIR PRIME LENDING RATE TO 7.50 PERCENT FROM 7.00 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE TOMORROW, DECEMBER 15, 2022.
