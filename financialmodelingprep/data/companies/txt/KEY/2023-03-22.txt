KEYCORP RAISES ITS PRIME LENDING RATE TO 8.00 PERCENT. CLEVELAND , MARCH 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEYCORP (NYSE: KEY) AND ITS BANKING AFFILIATES HAVE RAISED THEIR PRIME LENDING RATE TO 8.00 PERCENT FROM 7.75 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE TOMORROW, MARCH 23, 2023.
