PANDEMIC DRIVES AMERICANS TO PURSUE FINANCIAL MOBILITY AND PERSONAL PRIORITIES, KEYBANK SURVEY FINDS. CLEVELAND, JAN. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KEYBANK 2022 FINANCIAL MOBILITY SURVEY, RELEASED TODAY, FINDS MANY AMERICANS ARE RECONSIDERING THEIR WORK AND FINANCIAL PRIORITIES, WITH TWO-THIRDS (62%) OF RESPONDENTS INDICATING THAT WORK-LIFE BALANCE IS MORE IMPORTANT TO THEM THAN A HIGH-PAYING SALARY (22%). WHILE THE DESIRE FOR GREATER WORK-LIFE BALANCE EXISTS, ONLY A QUARTER (25%) OF AMERICANS SAY THEY HAVE EXPERIENCED AN IMPROVEMENT IN THEIR STANDARD OF LIVING COMPARED TO 2020.
