KEYCORP ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, DONALD R. KIMBLE AND NAMES CHIEF STRATEGY OFFICER, CLARK H.I.. CLEVELAND , NOV. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEYCORP (NYSE:KEY) ANNOUNCED THAT DONALD R. KIMBLE, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO) AND CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER, WILL RETIRE ON MAY 1, 2023.
