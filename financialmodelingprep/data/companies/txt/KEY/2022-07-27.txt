KEYCORP RAISES ITS PRIME LENDING RATE TO 5.5 PERCENT. CLEVELAND , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEYCORP (NYSE: KEY) AND ITS BANKING AFFILIATES HAVE RAISED THEIR PRIME LENDING RATE TO 5.5 PERCENT FROM 4.75 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE TOMORROW, JULY 28, 2022.
