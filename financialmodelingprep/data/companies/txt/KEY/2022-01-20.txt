KEYCORP REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER 2021 NET INCOME OF $601 MILLION, OR $.64 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE. CLEVELAND, JAN. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEYCORP (NYSE: KEY) TODAY ANNOUNCED NET INCOME FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS ATTRIBUTABLE TO KEY COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $601 MILLION, OR $.64 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021. THIS COMPARED TO $616 MILLION, OR $.65 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021 AND $549 MILLION, OR $.56 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020.
