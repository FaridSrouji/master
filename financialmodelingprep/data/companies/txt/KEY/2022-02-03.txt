GOOGLE CLOUD ANNOUNCES CLOUD-FIRST PARTNERSHIP WITH KEYBANK. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., AND CLEVELAND, OHIO, FEB. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GOOGLE CLOUD, KEYBANK, AND DELOITTE TODAY ANNOUNCED AN EXPANDED, MULTI-YEAR STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO ACCELERATE KEYBANK'S COMMITMENT TO A CLOUD-FIRST APPROACH TO BANKING.
