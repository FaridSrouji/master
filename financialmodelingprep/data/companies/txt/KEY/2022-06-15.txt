KEYCORP RAISES ITS PRIME LENDING RATE TO 4.75 PERCENT. CLEVELAND , JUNE 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEYCORP (NYSE: KEY) AND ITS BANKING AFFILIATES HAVE RAISED THEIR PRIME LENDING RATE TO 4.75 PERCENT FROM 4.00 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE TOMORROW, JUNE 16, 2022.
