KEYCORP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2021 NET INCOME OF $616 MILLION, OR $.65 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE. CLEVELAND , OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEYCORP (NYSE: KEY) TODAY ANNOUNCED NET INCOME FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS ATTRIBUTABLE TO KEY COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $616 MILLION, OR $.65 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021. THIS COMPARED TO $698 MILLION, OR $.72 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021 AND $397 MILLION, OR $.41 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2020.
