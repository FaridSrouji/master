J. B. HUNT TRANSPORT SERVICES, INC. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. LOWELL, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--J.B. HUNT TRANSPORT SERVICES, INC. (NASDAQ:JBHT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $ 0.40 (FORTY CENTS) PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 13, 2022 AND WILL BE PAID ON MAY 27, 2022. ABOUT J.B. HUNT J.B.
