J.B. HUNT PUBLISHES SUSTAINABILITY REPORT DISCLOSING ESG PROGRESS IN 2020. LOWELL, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--J.B. HUNT TRANSPORT SERVICES INC. (NASDAQ: JBHT), ONE OF THE LARGEST SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTIONS PROVIDERS IN NORTH AMERICA, HAS PUBLISHED A REPORT FOR ITS SUBSIDIARY J.B. HUNT TRANSPORT INC. DETAILING THE COMPANY'S PROGRESS IN 2020 TOWARD ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE PERFORMANCE. THE 2020 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT, A FIRST OF ITS KIND FOR THE COMPANY, HIGHLIGHTS MANY ESG ACHIEVEMENTS FOR THE YEAR, INCLUDING: AVOIDING AN ESTIMATED 3.5 MILLION METRIC TONS
