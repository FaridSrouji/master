J.B. HUNT RECOGNIZES 116 DRIVERS FOR SAFELY DRIVING 2M-PLUS MILES AT 20TH ANNUAL COMPANY EVENT. LOWELL, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--J.B. HUNT TRANSPORT SERVICES INC. (NASDAQ: JBHT), ONE OF THE LARGEST SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTIONS PROVIDERS IN NORTH AMERICA, RECENTLY RECOGNIZED 116 COMPANY DRIVERS FOR ACHIEVING TWO, THREE, FOUR, AND – FOR THE FIRST TIME IN COMPANY HISTORY - FIVE MILLION MILES DRIVEN WITHOUT A PREVENTABLE ACCIDENT, AWARDING MORE THAN $1.6 MILLION IN SAFE DRIVER BONUSES.
