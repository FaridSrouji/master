MULESOFT UNVEILS NEXT MAJOR RELEASE OF ANYPOINT PLATFORM WITH DATAGRAPH, EMPOWERING DEVELOPERS TO CREATE COMPOSABLE BUSINESS CAPABILITIES FASTER. SAN FRANCISCO, MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MULESOFT, PROVIDER OF THE WORLD'S #1 INTEGRATION AND API PLATFORM, TODAY INTRODUCED THE NEXT MAJOR RELEASE OF ANYPOINT PLATFORM ™ WITH DATAGRAPH. DEVELOPERS CAN NOW INSTANTLY DISCOVER, ACCESS, AND SERVE DATA FROM MULTIPLE EXISTING APIS WITH A SINGLE QUERY, WITHOUT WRITING ANY ADDITIONAL CODE.
