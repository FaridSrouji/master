MULESOFT RECOGNIZED AS A LEADER IN GARTNER MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR INTEGRATION PLATFORM AS A SERVICE, WORLDWIDE (IPAAS). SAN FRANCISCO , JAN. 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALESFORCE [NYSE: CRM], THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT GARTNER® NAMED MULESOFT  A LEADER IN THE GARTNER® MAGIC QUADRANT FOR INTEGRATION PLATFORM AS A SERVICE, WORLDWIDE (IPAAS). THIS IS THE EIGHTH TIME THAT MULESOFT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED.
