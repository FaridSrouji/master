SALESFORCE RECOGNIZED AS A LEADER IN THE GARTNER® MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR SALES FORCE AUTOMATION PLATFORMS FOR 16TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. SAN FRANCISCO , OCT. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALESFORCE (NYSE: CRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CRM, HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY GARTNER AS A LEADER IN THE 2022 MAGIC QUADRANT FOR SALES FORCE AUTOMATION PLATFORMS. IT IS THE 16TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR THAT SALESFORCE HAS RECEIVED THIS RECOGNITION.
