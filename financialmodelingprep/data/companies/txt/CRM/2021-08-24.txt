TABLEAU INVITES DATA ENTHUSIASTS FROM AROUND THE WORLD TO ANNUAL CONFERENCE THIS FALL. SEATTLE , AUG. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, TABLEAU, THE WORLD'S LEADING ANALYTICS PLATFORM (NYSE: CRM), OPENED REGISTRATION FOR ITS ANNUAL TABLEAU CONFERENCE, WHICH WILL BE BROADCAST AROUND THE WORLD NOVEMBER 9-11, 2021. TABLEAU CONFERENCE WILL OFFER OPPORTUNITIES TO LEARN, NETWORK, SHARE BEST PRACTICES, AND BE INSPIRED BY DATA LEADERS AT A TIME WHEN CONNECTING PEOPLE TO DATA-DRIVEN INSIGHTS IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN EVER.
