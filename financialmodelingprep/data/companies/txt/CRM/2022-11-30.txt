BRET TAYLOR TO STEP DOWN AS SALESFORCE VICE CHAIR AND CO-CEO; MARC BENIOFF TO BE CHAIR AND CEO. SAN FRANCISCO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--SALESFORCE (NYSE: CRM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BRET TAYLOR WILL STEP DOWN AS VICE CHAIR AND CO-CEO OF SALESFORCE, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 31, 2023. AT THAT POINT, MARC BENIOFF WILL BE CHAIR AND CEO OF THE COMPANY.
