SALESFORCE GRANTS EQUITY AWARDS TO SLACK EMPLOYEES UNDER ITS INDUCEMENT EQUITY INCENTIVE PLAN. SAN FRANCISCO, AUG. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALESFORCE (NYSE: CRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS GRANTED EQUITY AWARDS UNDER ITS 2014 INDUCEMENT EQUITY INCENTIVE PLAN (THE "PLAN") TO THREE NEW EMPLOYEES WHO JOINED SALESFORCE IN CONNECTION WITH THE ACQUISITION OF SLACK. THE PLAN WAS ADOPTED BY THE SALESFORCE BOARD OF DIRECTORS IN JULY 2014, IN ACCORDANCE WITH NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE RULE 303A.08.
