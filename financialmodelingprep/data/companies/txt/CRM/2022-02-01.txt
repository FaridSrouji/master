SALESFORCE LAUNCHES SAFETY CLOUD TO HELP BUSINESSES AND COMMUNITIES GET TOGETHER SAFELY. SAN FRANCISCO, FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALESFORCE (NYSE: CRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED SAFETY CLOUD TO HELP PEOPLE GET TOGETHER SAFELY. BUILDING ON SALESFORCE'S OWN EVENT AND WORKPLACE EXPERIENCE — AND EXPANDING ON EXISTING HEALTH AND SAFETY INNOVATIONS ANNOUNCED IN DECEMBER — SAFETY CLOUD HELPS BUSINESSES AND COMMUNITIES BETTER MANAGE TESTING, HEALTH AND ENTRY PROTOCOLS TO CREATE SAFER IN-PERSON EXPERIENCES AT EVENTS AND IN THE WORKPLACE.
