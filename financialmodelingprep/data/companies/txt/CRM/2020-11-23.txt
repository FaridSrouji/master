SALESFORCE GRANTS EQUITY AWARDS TO MOBIFY EMPLOYEES UNDER ITS INDUCEMENT EQUITY INCENTIVE PLAN. SAN FRANCISCO, NOV. 23, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALESFORCE (NYSE: CRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS GRANTED EQUITY AWARDS UNDER ITS 2014 INDUCEMENT EQUITY INCENTIVE PLAN (THE "PLAN") TO NEW EMPLOYEES WHO JOINED SALESFORCE IN CONNECTION WITH THE ACQUISITION OF MOBIFY...
