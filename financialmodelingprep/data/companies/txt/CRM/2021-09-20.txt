SALESFORCE GIVES $19M TO SCHOOLS. SAN FRANCISCO, SEPT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALESFORCE (NYSE: CRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED $17.25 MILLION IN NEW GRANTS TO SUPPORT FIVE SCHOOL DISTRICTS ACROSS THE UNITED STATES – SAN FRANCISCO UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT, OAKLAND UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT, CHICAGO PUBLIC SCHOOLS, INDIANAPOLIS PUBLIC SCHOOLS, AND THE NEW YORK CITY DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION.
