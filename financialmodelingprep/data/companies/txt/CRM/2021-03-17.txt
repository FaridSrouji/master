BOYS & GIRLS CLUBS OF AMERICA SELECTS SALESFORCE TO STREAMLINE OPERATIONS, HELPING STAFF AND VOLUNTEERS SPEND MORE TIME WITH YOUNG MEMBERS. SAN FRANCISCO AND ATLANTA, MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALESFORCE (NYSE: CRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BOYS & GIRLS CLUBS OF AMERICA HAS SELECTED SALESFORCE TECHNOLOGY TO WORK MORE EFFICIENTLY AND BETTER ENGAGE WITH KIDS AND TEENS ACROSS ITS MORE THAN 4,700 CLUBS. THIS WILL HELP BOYS & GIRLS CLUBS TO DELIVER MORE PERSONALIZED EXPERIENCES AND PROGRAMS TO THE 4.6 MILLION YOUNG PEOPLE IT SERVES.
