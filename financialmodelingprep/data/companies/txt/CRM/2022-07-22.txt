SALESFORCE NAMED A LEADER IN 2022 GARTNER® MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR MULTICHANNEL MARKETING HUBS FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. SAN FRANCISCO , JULY 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALESFORCE (NYSE: CRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SALESFORCE HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY GARTNER AS A LEADER IN ITS 2022 MAGIC QUADRANT FOR MULTICHANNEL MARKETING HUBS. SALESFORCE WAS EVALUATED FOR ITS SALESFORCE MARKETING CLOUD.
