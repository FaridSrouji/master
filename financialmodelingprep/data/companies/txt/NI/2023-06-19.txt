EDP RENEWABLES AND NIPSCO ENTER INTO LONG-TERM AGREEMENT FOR THE 198-MEGAWATT CARPENTER WIND FARM IN INDIANA. THIS CONTRACT MARKS NIPSCO AND EDP RENEWABLES' FIFTH RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECT TO BRING TO INDIANA, TOTALING MORE THAN 1,000 MEGAWATTS OF CLEAN ENERGY GENERATION IN THE HOOSIER STATE.
