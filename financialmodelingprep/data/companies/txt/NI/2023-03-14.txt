NISOURCE DECLARES COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. MERRILLVILLE, IND. , MARCH 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF 25 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE MAY 19, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 28, 2023.
