NISOURCE INCREASES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND AND DECLARES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND. MERRILLVILLE, IND., JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF 23.5 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE FEBRUARY 18, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 8, 2022.
