NISOURCE ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGES IN LEGAL FUNCTION. MERRILLVILLE, IND., NOV. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ANNE-MARIE D'ANGELO, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GENERAL COUNSEL AND CORPORATE SECRETARY, IS LEAVING NISOURCE TO ASSUME THE POSITION OF CHIEF LEGAL AND GOVERNMENT AFFAIRS OFFICER AT MOLSON COORS BEVERAGE COMPANY (NYSE: TAP, TAP.A; TSX: TPX.B, TPX.A).
