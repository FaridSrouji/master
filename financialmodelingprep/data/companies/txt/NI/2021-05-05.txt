NISOURCE REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. MERRILLVILLE, IND., MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED, ON A GAAP BASIS, NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2021 OF $281.7 MILLION, OR $0.72 DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE, COMPARED TO A NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $61.8 MILLION, OR $0.16 DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE, FOR THE SAME PERIOD OF 2020.
