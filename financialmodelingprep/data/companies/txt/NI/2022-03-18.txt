COLUMBIA GAS OF PENNSYLVANIA PROPOSES FURTHER SAFETY INVESTMENTS THROUGH AGING INFRASTRUCTURE REPLACEMENTS, UPGRADES. REQUEST SUPPORTS ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT; RECOMMENDS NEW RESIDENTIAL ENERGY-EFFICIENCY PROGRAMS CANONSBURG, PA. , MARCH 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COLUMBIA GAS OF PENNSYLVANIA, INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI), HAS MADE A REQUEST TODAY WITH THE PENNSYLVANIA PUBLIC UTILITY COMMISSION (PA PUC) TO APPROVE REVISED RATES FOR FURTHER UPGRADING AND REPLACING PORTIONS OF THE COMPANY'S UNDERGROUND NATURAL GAS DISTRIBUTION PIPELINES TO BENEFIT ITS CUSTOMERS.
