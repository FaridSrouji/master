BRENT ARCHER TO RETIRE FROM NISOURCE. JENNIFER MONTAGUE APPOINTED PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, COLUMBIA GAS OF VIRGINIA MERRILLVILLE, IND. , MARCH 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF JENNIFER MONTAGUE TO THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, COLUMBIA GAS OF VIRGINIA, EFFECTIVE MAY 1, FOLLOWING THE RETIREMENT OF BRENT ARCHER.
