NIPSCO AND INVENERGY SIGN 250-MEGAWATT SOLAR ENERGY BUILD TRANSFER AGREEMENT. MERRILLVILLE, IND., MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORTHERN INDIANA PUBLIC SERVICE COMPANY, LLC (NIPSCO), A SUBSIDIARY OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI), AND INVENERGY, A LEADING DEVELOPER AND OPERATOR OF SUSTAINABLE ENERGY SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE SIGNED A BUILD TRANSFER AGREEMENT TO BRING 250 MEGAWATTS (MW) OF SOLAR ENERGY TO INDIANA WITH THE FAIRBANKS SOLAR PROJECT.
