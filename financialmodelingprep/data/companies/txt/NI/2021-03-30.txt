COLUMBIA GAS OF PENNSYLVANIA PROPOSES FURTHER INVESTMENTS IN SAFETY THROUGH REPLACING AND UPGRADING AGING INFRASTRUCTURE. CANONSBURG, PA., MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COLUMBIA GAS OF PENNSYLVANIA, INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI), HAS MADE A REQUEST TODAY WITH THE PENNSYLVANIA PUBLIC UTILITY COMMISSION (PA PUC) TO APPROVE REVISED RATES FOR FURTHER UPGRADING AND REPLACING PORTIONS OF THE COMPANY'S UNDERGROUND NATURAL GAS DISTRIBUTION PIPELINES.
