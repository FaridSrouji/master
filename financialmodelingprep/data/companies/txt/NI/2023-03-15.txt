NISOURCE ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGES AND ADDITIONS. MERRILLVILLE, IND. , MARCH 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF MICHAEL LUHRS TO THE NISOURCE EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM, ALONG WITH A RECONFIGURATION OF LEADERSHIP RESPONSIBILITIES FOR SEVERAL OTHERS ON THAT TEAM.
