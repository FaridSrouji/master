NISOURCE JOINS LOW-CARBON RESOURCES INITIATIVE, DEVELOPING KEY TECHNOLOGIES FOR A SUSTAINABLE FUTURE. MERRILLVILLE, IND., APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) HAS JOINED THE LOW-CARBON RESOURCES INITIATIVE (LCRI), A FIVE-YEAR INITIATIVE TO ACCELERATE THE DEVELOPMENT AND DEMONSTRATION OF LOW-CARBON ENERGY TECHNOLOGIES.
