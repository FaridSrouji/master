NISOURCE INC. ANNOUNCES EQUITY UNITS OFFERING. MERRILLVILLE, IND., APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) ("NISOURCE") ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS INTENTION TO OFFER TO SELL, SUBJECT TO MARKET AND OTHER CONDITIONS, 7,500,000 EQUITY UNITS, EACH WITH A STATED AMOUNT OF $100.
