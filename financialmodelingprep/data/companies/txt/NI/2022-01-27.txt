NISOURCE CEO JOE HAMROCK TO RETIRE FOLLOWING ACCOMPLISHED TEN-YEAR CAREER WITH THE COMPANY. MERRILLVILLE, IND., JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT, AS A RESULT OF A LONG-PLANNED SUCCESSION PROCESS, ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED LLOYD YATES AS THE COMPANY'S NEXT PRESIDENT AND CEO, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 14, 2022.
