NISOURCE RECOGNIZED FOR IMPLEMENTING INDUSTRY-LEADING SAFETY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM. MERRILLVILLE, IND. , SEPT. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI), ONE OF THE LARGEST FULLY REGULATED UTILITY COMPANIES IN THE UNITED STATES, HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY LRQA – A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY SERVICES – FOR ACHIEVING CONFORMANCE CERTIFICATION IN THE AMERICAN PETROLEUM INSTITUTE'S RECOMMENDED PRACTICE 1173 FOR ITS SAFETY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (SMS).
