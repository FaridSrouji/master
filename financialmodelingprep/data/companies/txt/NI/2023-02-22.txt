NISOURCE ANNOUNCES 2022 RESULTS, RAISES 2023 GUIDANCE. 2022 DILUTED NOEPS TOPS GUIDANCE RANGE; 2023 GUIDANCE RANGE INCREASED NIPSCO MINORITY INTEREST SALE LAUNCHED AND ON TRACK FOR 2023 MERRILLVILLE, IND. , FEB. 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED, ON A GAAP BASIS, 2022 NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS  WAS $749.0 MILLION, OR $1.70 DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE, COMPARED TO NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $529.8 MILLION, OR $1.27 DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE, FOR THE SAME PERIOD OF 2021.
