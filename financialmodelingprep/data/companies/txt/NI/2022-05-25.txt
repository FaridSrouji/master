NISOURCE ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGES AND ADDITIONS. MELODY BIRMINGHAM, WILLIAM (BILL) JEFFERSON AND MELANIE BERMAN JOIN EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM MERRILLVILLE, IND. , MAY 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENTS OF MELODY BIRMINGHAM, WILLIAM (BILL) JEFFERSON, AND MELANIE BERMAN TO THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM, ALONG WITH A RECONFIGURATION OF LEADERSHIP RESPONSIBILITIES FOR SEVERAL OTHERS ON THAT TEAM.
