NISOURCE INC. ANNOUNCES TWO NEW LEADERS IN EXPERIENCE ORGANIZATION. MERRILLVILLE, IND., JUNE 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MELANIE BERMAN HAS BEEN NAMED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER AND CARLOS AYALA HAS BEEN NAMED VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF DIVERSITY, EQUITY & INCLUSION OFFICER.
