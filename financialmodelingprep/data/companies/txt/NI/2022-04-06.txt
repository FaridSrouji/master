NISOURCE APPOINTS KIM CUCCIA AS GENERAL COUNSEL AND CORPORATE SECRETARY. MERRILLVILLE, IND. , APRIL 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED INTERIM GENERAL COUNSEL KIM CUCCIA HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO THE ROLE OF GENERAL COUNSEL AND CORPORATE SECRETARY, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, AFTER THE CONCLUSION OF A NATIONWIDE SEARCH.
