NISOURCE CLOSES ON INAUGURAL GREEN BOND OFFERING. MERRILLVILLE, IND. , JUNE 10, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) ("NISOURCE") CLOSED TODAY ON ITS FIRST EVER GREEN BOND ISSUANCE, RAISING $350 MILLION THAT WILL BE USED TO FINANCE THE ACQUISITION OF ELIGIBLE RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECTS LOCATED IN INDIANA.
