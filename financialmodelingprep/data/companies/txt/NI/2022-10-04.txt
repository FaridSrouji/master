EDP RENEWABLES CELEBRATES THE START OF CONSTRUCTION ON THE INDIANA CROSSROADS II WIND FARM. WITH 1,400 MEGAWATTS OF INSTALLED RENEWABLE ENERGY CAPACITY IN INDIANA, EDP RENEWABLES WILL STRENGTHEN ITS POSITION AS THE TOP WIND ENERGY PRODUCER IN THE STATE WITH THIS NEW 202-MEGAWATT WIND FARM.
