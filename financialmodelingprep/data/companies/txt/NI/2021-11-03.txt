NISOURCE REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. MERRILLVILLE, IND., NOV. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED, ON A GAAP BASIS, NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2021 OF $49.4 MILLION, OR $0.12 DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE, COMPARED TO NET LOSS AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $186.7 MILLION, OR $0.49 DILUTED LOSS PER SHARE, FOR THE SAME PERIOD OF 2020.
