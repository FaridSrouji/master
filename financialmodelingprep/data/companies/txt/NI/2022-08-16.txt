NISOURCE ANNOUNCES DEPARTURE OF PABLO VEGAS. MERRILLVILLE, IND. , AUG. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, NISOURCE, AND GROUP PRESIDENT, NISOURCE UTILITIES, PABLO VEGAS, IS LEAVING NISOURCE TO BECOME PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF THE ELECTRIC RELIABILITY COUNCIL OF TEXAS (ERCOT), EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 1.
