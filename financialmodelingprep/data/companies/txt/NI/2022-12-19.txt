NISOURCE NAMED TO DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX FOR NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. MERRILLVILLE, IND. , DEC. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NISOURCE WAS NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX (DJSI) - NORTH AMERICA FOR THE NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, IN RECOGNITION OF THE COMPANY'S LEADERSHIP IN SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES.
