NISOURCE DECLARES COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. MERRILLVILLE, IND. , MAY 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF 23.5 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE AUGUST 19, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 29, 2022.
