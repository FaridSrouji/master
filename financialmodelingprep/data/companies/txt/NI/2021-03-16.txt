NISOURCE DECLARES COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. MERRILLVILLE, IND., MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF 22 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE MAY 20, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 30, 2021.
