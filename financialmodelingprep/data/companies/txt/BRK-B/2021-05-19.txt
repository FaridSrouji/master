MITEK LAUNCHES MODULAR INITIATIVE WITH DANNY FORSTER & ARCHITECTURE PARTNERSHIP. ST. LOUIS, MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MITEK, A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF INNOVATIVE CONSTRUCTION-BASED SOFTWARE, SERVICES, AND ENGINEERED BUILDING SOLUTIONS THAT IS OWNED BY BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY, AND DANNY FORSTER & ARCHITECTURE (DF&A), A NEW YORK CITY-BASED ARCHITECTURE FIRM, ANNOUNCE THE FORMAL LAUNCH OF THEIR MODULAR INITIATIVE, WITH THE MISSION TO HELP THE CONSTRUCTION INDUSTRY ANSWER THE GLOBAL DEMAND FOR SMARTER, MORE AFFORDABLE, AND MORE SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS AND COMMUNITIES. AS PART OF THE AGREEMENT, DF&A WILL LEAD THE DESIGN, AND RESEARCH WORK ALONGSIDE MITEK'S R&D AND NEW MARKET GROWTH TEAM, AS MITEK INVESTS SIGNIFICANT RESOURCES IN TO DEVELOPING AND EVOLVING A MODULAR ENABLEMENT PLATFORM FOR DECADES TO COME.
