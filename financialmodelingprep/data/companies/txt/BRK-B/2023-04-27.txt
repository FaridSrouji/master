BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC. NEWS RELEASE. OMAHA, NEB.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--(BRK.A; BRK.B) – ON DECEMBER 14, 2007, AN INDIRECT SUBSIDIARY OF BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC. ACQUIRED THE EQUITY OF WHITTAKER, CLARK & DANIELS, INC., BRILLIANT NATIONAL SERVICES, INC., L.A. TERMINALS, INC., AND SOCO WEST, INC. THESE COMPANIES HAD CEASED THEIR OPERATIONS IN 2004 AND SOLD ALL THEIR OPERATING ASSETS PRIOR TO THE ACQUISITION, THOUGH THEY CONTINUED TO FACE LIABILITIES ARISING FROM ASBESTOS, TALC AND ENVIRONMENTAL CLAIMS. NO BERKSHIRE COMPANY EVER OPERATED, OR HAD ANY INVOLVEMENT IN, THE MANUFACTURING AND CHEMICAL OPERATIONS THAT GAVE RISE TO THE COMPANIES' LIABILITIES, AND NO BERKSHIRE INSURER ISSUED IT ANY INSURANCE IN CONNECTION WITH THE ACQUISITION. ON APRIL 26, 2023, THESE COMPANIES FILED VOLUNTARY PETITIONS UNDER CHAPTER 11 OF BANKRUPTCY CODE IN THE UNITED STATES BANKRUPTCY COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF NEW JERSEY.
