BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY TO ACQUIRE ALLEGHANY CORPORATION FOR $848.02 PER SHARE IN $11.6 BILLION TRANSACTION. OMAHA, NEB. & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY (NYSE:BRK.A; BRK.B) AND ALLEGHANY CORPORATION (NYSE:Y) TODAY JOINTLY ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY WILL ACQUIRE ALL OUTSTANDING ALLEGHANY SHARES FOR $848.02 PER SHARE IN CASH. THE TRANSACTION, WHICH WAS UNANIMOUSLY APPROVED BY BOTH BOARDS OF DIRECTORS, REPRESENTS A TOTAL EQUITY VALUE OF APPROXIMATELY $11.6 BILLION.
