KELLY SILVER JOINS BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY HOMESERVICES GEORGIA PROPERTIES AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF MARKETING. ROSWELL, GA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY HOMESERVICES GEORGIA PROPERTIES, ONE OF THE LARGEST AND MOST SUCCESSFUL REAL ESTATE ORGANIZATIONS IN THE COUNTRY, ANNOUNCES THAT KELLY SILVER HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF MARKETING. SHE WILL LEAD ALL BRAND STRATEGIES AND MARKETING EFFORTS AT THE AWARD-WINNING BROKERAGE.
