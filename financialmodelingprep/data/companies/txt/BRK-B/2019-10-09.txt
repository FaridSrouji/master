STEVE MENZIES AND QUADRANT MANAGEMENT INC. COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF APPLIED UNDERWRITERS, INC. FROM BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC. (NYSE:BRK.A) AND OTHERS FOR AN ENTERPRISE VALUE OF $920 MILLION.. AN UNKNOWN BUYER ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE APPLIED UNDERWRITERS, INC. FROM BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC. (NYSE:BRK.A) AND OTHERS ON FEBRUARY 25, 2019. THE TRANSACTION IS SUBJECT TO REGULATORY APPROVAL AND IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2019.
STEVE MENZIES AND QUADRANT MANAGEMENT INC. COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF APPLIED UNDERWRITERS, INC. FROM BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC. (NYSE:BRK.A) AND OTHERS FOR AN ENTERPRISE VALUE OF $920 MILLION ON OCTOBER 10, 2019. UNDER THE TRANSACTION, BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC. SOLD 81% STAKE IN APPLIED UNDERWRITERS, INC. QUADRANT MANAGEMENT INC. HAS ACQUIRED APPLIED UNDERWRITERS’ SERVICE COMPANIES AS PART OF THE DEAL.
