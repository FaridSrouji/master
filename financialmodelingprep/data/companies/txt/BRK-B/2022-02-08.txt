HELZBERG DIAMONDS AND BRITISH DESIGNER JENNY PACKHAM CREATE NEW HONEYBEE-INSPIRED BRIDAL JEWELRY COLLECTION. KANSAS CITY, MO., FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HELZBERG DIAMONDS, ONE OF THE JEWELRY INDUSTRY'S MOST INNOVATIVE RETAILERS, RECENTLY EXPANDED ITS PRESTIGIOUS PARTNERSHIP WITH BRITISH EVENINGWEAR AND BRIDAL DESIGNER JENNY PACKHAM.
