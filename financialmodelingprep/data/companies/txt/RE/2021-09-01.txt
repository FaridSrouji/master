EVEREST APPOINTS JOHN MODIN AS PRESIDENT OF MT. LOGAN RE. HAMILTON, BERMUDA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EVEREST RE GROUP, LTD. (“EVEREST” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE APPOINTMENT OF JOHN MODIN AS PRESIDENT OF MT. LOGAN RE, LTD. (“MT. LOGAN”), EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 30, 2021. MR. MODIN JOINS MT. LOGAN FROM CITIGROUP WHERE HE WAS A MANAGING DIRECTOR IN THE FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP AND HEAD OF INSURANCE SOLUTIONS. HE HAS OVER 30 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE IN THE FINANCIAL SERVICES, INSURANCE LINKED SECURITIES (“ILS”) AND INSURANCE MARKETS AND EARLIER IN HIS CAREER
