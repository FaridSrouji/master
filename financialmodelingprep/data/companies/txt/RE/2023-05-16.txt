EVEREST RE GROUP, LTD. ANNOUNCES PRICING OF PUBLIC OFFERING OF 3,600,000 COMMON SHARES. HAMILTON, BERMUDA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EVEREST RE GROUP, LTD. (NYSE: RE) (THE “COMPANY” OR “EVEREST”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 3,600,000 COMMON SHARES AT A PRICE TO THE PUBLIC OF $360.00 PER SHARE, BEFORE UNDERWRITING DISCOUNTS AND COMMISSIONS. THE UNDERWRITERS HAVE THE OPTION TO PURCHASE UP TO AN AGGREGATE OF 540,000 ADDITIONAL COMMON SHARES FROM THE COMPANY. THIS REFLECTS AN UPSIZING OF THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED OFFERING OF 3,000,000 SHARES AND 450,000 OPT.
EVEREST RE GROUP, LTD. ANNOUNCES PUBLIC OFFERING OF 3,000,000 COMMON SHARES. HAMILTON, BERMUDA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EVEREST RE GROUP, LTD. (NYSE: RE) (THE “COMPANY” OR “EVEREST”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMMENCED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 3,000,000 COMMON SHARES. THE UNDERWRITERS WILL HAVE THE OPTION TO PURCHASE UP TO AN AGGREGATE OF 450,000 ADDITIONAL COMMON SHARES FROM THE COMPANY. THE COMPANY INTENDS TO USE THE NET PROCEEDS FROM THIS OFFERING FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES, WHICH MAY INCLUDE EXPANDING ITS EXISTING BUSINESS LINES AND OPERATIONS. CITIGROUP GL.
