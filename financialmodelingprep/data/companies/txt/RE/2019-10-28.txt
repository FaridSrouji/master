EVEREST RE GROUP POSTS Q3 EPS OF $2.56. EVEREST RE GROUP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.QTRLY EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.56.QTRLY AFTER-TAX OPERATING INCOME $3.39 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE.GROSS WRITTEN PREMIUMS FOR QUARTER WERE $2.4 BILLION, UP 9%.QTRLY TOTAL REVENUES $2.04 BILLION VERSUS $1.94 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $2.21, REVENUE VIEW $1.92 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
EVEREST RE GROUP LTD. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. EVEREST RE GROUP LTD. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 2,042.520 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,936.463 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 104.398 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 198.381 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.56 COMPARED TO USD 4.87 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.56 COMPARED TO USD 4.84 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 6,017.083 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 5,569.919 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 791.817 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 474.354 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 19.44 COMPARED TO USD 11.61 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 19.38 COMPARED TO USD 11.55 A YEAR AGO.
