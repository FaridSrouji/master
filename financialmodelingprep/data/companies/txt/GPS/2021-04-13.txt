GAP INC. SIGNS CREDIT CARD PROGRAM AGREEMENTS WITH BARCLAYS AND MASTERCARD. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GAP INC. (NYSE: GPS), A COLLECTION OF PURPOSE-LED, LIFESTYLE BRANDS INCLUDING OLD NAVY, GAP, BANANA REPUBLIC AND ATHLETA, AND THE LARGEST SPECIALTY APPAREL COMPANY IN THE U.S., ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO NEW, LONG-TERM CREDIT CARD PROGRAM AGREEMENTS WITH BARCLAYS AND MASTERCARD. BARCLAYS WILL BECOME THE EXCLUSIVE ISSUER OF GAP INC.'S CO-BRANDED AND PRIVATE LABEL CREDIT CARD PROGRAM IN THE U.S. BEGINNING IN MAY 2022. IN ADDITION, GAP INC. AND BARCLAY
