ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES APPOINTS JOE WHITE AS CHIEF PRODUCT & SOLUTIONS OFFICER. LINCOLNSHIRE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION (NASDAQ: ZBRA), AN INNOVATOR AT THE FRONT LINE OF BUSINESS WITH SOLUTIONS AND PARTNERS THAT DELIVER A PERFORMANCE EDGE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JOE WHITE HAS BEEN NAMED CHIEF PRODUCT & SOLUTIONS OFFICER. WHITE MOVES INTO THE ROLE VACATED BY BILL BURNS WHO BECAME ZEBRA'S CEO EARLIER THIS MONTH.
