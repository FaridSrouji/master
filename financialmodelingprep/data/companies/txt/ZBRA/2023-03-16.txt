ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES APPOINTS ROB ARMSTRONG AS CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER. LINCOLNSHIRE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION (NASDAQ: ZBRA), AN INNOVATOR AT THE FRONT LINE OF BUSINESS WITH SOLUTIONS AND PARTNERS THAT DELIVER A PERFORMANCE EDGE, ANNOUNCED THAT ROB ARMSTRONG HAS BEEN NAMED CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER. ARMSTRONG STEPS INTO THE ROLE AS JEFF SCHMITZ, WHO MOST RECENTLY SERVED AS CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES AND MARKETING OFFICER, BECOMES ZEBRA'S CHIEF PEOPLE OFFICER.
