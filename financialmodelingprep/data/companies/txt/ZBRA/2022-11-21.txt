ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES APPOINTS TAMI FROESE AS CHIEF SUPPLY CHAIN OFFICER. LINCOLNSHIRE, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION (NASDAQ: ZBRA), AN INNOVATOR AT THE FRONT LINE OF BUSINESS WITH SOLUTIONS AND PARTNERS THAT DELIVER A PERFORMANCE EDGE, ANNOUNCED THAT TAMI FROESE HAS BEEN NAMED CHIEF SUPPLY CHAIN OFFICER.
