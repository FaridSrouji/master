TRANCHE UPDATE ON ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION (NASDAQGS:ZBRA)'S EQUITY BUYBACK PLAN ANNOUNCED ON JULY 30, 2019.. FROM JULY 26, 2019 TO SEPTEMBER 28, 2019, THE COMPANY HAS REPURCHASED 101,062 SHARES, REPRESENTING 0.19% FOR $19.9 MILLION. WITH THIS, THE COMPANY HAS COMPLETED THE REPURCHASE OF 101,062 SHARES, REPRESENTING 0.19% FOR $19.9 MILLION UNDER THE BUYBACK ANNOUNCED ON JULY 30, 2019.
UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE SELECTS ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES AS NEXT-GENERATION MOBILE DELIVERY DEVICE PROVIDER. UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE SELECTS ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES AS NEXT-GENERATION MOBILE DELIVERY DEVICE PROVIDER.ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES - U.S. POSTAL SERVICE AWARDED CO AN IDIQ CONTRACT TO SUPPLY TC77 TOUCH COMPUTERS TO SCAN, TRACK PACKAGES ACROSS ITS CARRIER NETWORK.ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES - USPS WILL START DEPLOYING 300,000 TC77 MOBILE DEVICES AS ITS NEXT-GENERATION MOBILE DELIVERY DEVICE IN FIRST HALF OF 2020.
ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS Q3 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.43. ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES ANNOUNCES THIRD-QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.Q3 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.43.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.50.Q3 SALES $1.13 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.14 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $3.28 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES Q4 2019 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.55 TO $3.75.SEES Q4 2019 SALES UP ABOUT 4 TO 6 PERCENT.ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES -QTRLY SALES GROWTH IN NORTH AMERICA AND EMEA WAS PARTIALLY OFFSET BY A SOFTER SPENDING ENVIRONMENT IN CHINA.ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES  - HAS BEGUN DIVERSIFYING SOURCING OF MOST OF U.S. VOLUMES OUT OF CHINA.ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES - NET IMPACT TO COST OF SALES FROM RECENTLY ENACTED LIST 4 TARIFFS IS EXPECTED TO PEAK AT $15-25 MILLION IN Q1 OF 2020.NET IMPACT TO COST OF SALES FROM RECENTLY ENACTED LIST 4 TARIFFS SHOULD MODERATE THROUGH MID-2020.ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES - ACTIONS TO MITIGATE TARIFFS EXPECTED TO RESULT IN UP TO $30 MILLION ONE-TIME PRE-TAX CHARGES THROUGH MID-2020.
