THIRD WAVE AUTOMATION ANNOUNCES STRATEGIC INVESTMENT FROM QUALCOMM VENTURES AND ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES. FUNDING TO DRIVE PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT AND MARKET EXPANSION AS TWA REACH FORKLIFT AUTOMATION SOLUTION EXPANDS PILOT PROGRAM  UNION CITY, CALIF. , FEB. 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  THIRD WAVE AUTOMATION  (TWA), PROVIDER OF HIGH-REACH AUTONOMOUS FORKLIFTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC INVESTMENT BY QUALCOMM VENTURES AND ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES, BRINGING TOTAL FUNDING TO $70 MILLION.
