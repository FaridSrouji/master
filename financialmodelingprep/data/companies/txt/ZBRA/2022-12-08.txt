ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES NAMES BILL BURNS AS NEXT CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. LINCOLNSHIRE, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION (NASDAQ: ZBRA), AN INNOVATOR AT THE FRONT LINE OF BUSINESS WITH SOLUTIONS AND PARTNERS THAT DELIVER A PERFORMANCE EDGE, TODAY ANNOUNCED ZEBRA'S CHIEF PRODUCT & SOLUTIONS OFFICER BILL BURNS WILL BECOME THE COMPANY'S NEXT CEO AND A MEMBER OF THE ZEBRA BOARD OF DIRECTORS; ANDERS GUSTAFSSON WILL TRANSITION FROM HIS ROLE AS CEO TO BECOME EXECUTIVE CHAIR OF THE ZEBRA BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
