BD LAUNCHES NEW 'TOGETHER WE ADVANCE' ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, GOVERNANCE STRATEGY; ANNOUNCES 2030+ GOALS TO ENHANCE STEWARDSHIP OF HUMAN HEALTH, COMMUNITIES AND THE PLANET. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., OCT. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW STRATEGY TO ADVANCE ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) INITIATIVES, AS WELL AS A SUITE OF GOALS FOR 2030 AND BEYOND THAT FOCUS ON ENHANCING STEWARDSHIP OF THE COMPANY, THE PLANET, COMMUNITY AND HUMAN HEALTH.
