CHRISTOPHER REIDY ANNOUNCES INTENT TO RETIRE FROM BD. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., AUG. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHRISTOPHER REIDY, 64, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO) AND CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER (CAO), HAS INFORMED THE COMPANY OF HIS INTENT TO RETIRE AS HE REACHES RETIREMENT ELIGIBILITY.
