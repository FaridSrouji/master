NEW HARRIS POLL REVEALS SIGNIFICANT GAPS IN WOMEN'S KNOWLEDGE ABOUT CERVICAL CANCER. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J. , JAN. 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY RELEASED FINDINGS FROM A SURVEY CONDUCTED BY THE HARRIS POLL, BETWEEN NOV. 14-16, 2022, AMONG 872 U.S. WOMEN AGES 18 TO 64 YEARS OLD, THAT INDICATE A SIGNIFICANT GAP IN WOMEN'S KNOWLEDGE ABOUT THE PRIMARY CAUSES OF CERVICAL CANCER AS WELL AS THE MOST EFFECTIVE MEANS OF PREVENTION.
