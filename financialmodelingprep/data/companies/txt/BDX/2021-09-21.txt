BD PARTNERS WITH WASHINGTON STATE IN BACK-TO-SCHOOL TESTING EFFORT. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., SEPT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN SELECTED BY THE STATE OF WASHINGTON AS A DIGITALLY READ, RAPID ANTIGEN TEST OPTION FOR COVID-19 TESTING AND SCREENING IN K-12 SCHOOLS ACROSS THE STATE.
