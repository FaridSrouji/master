BD ANNOUNCES 510(K) CLEARANCE OF EXPANDED INDICATIONS FOR THE ROTAREX™ ATHERECTOMY SYSTEM. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED 510(K) CLEARANCE FOR EXPANDED INDICATIONS FROM THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR THE ROTAREX™ ATHERECTOMY SYSTEM.
