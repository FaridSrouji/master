BD ANNOUNCES SECOND FISCAL QUARTER RESULTS; REAFFIRMS FISCAL 2021 GUIDANCE. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY REPORTED QUARTERLY REVENUES OF $4.9 BILLION FOR THE SECOND FISCAL QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021.
