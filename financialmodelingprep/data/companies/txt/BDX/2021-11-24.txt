BD NAMES FRANCESCA DEMARTINO AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF INVESTOR RELATIONS. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., NOV. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED FRANCESCA DEMARTINO HAS BEEN NAMED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF INVESTOR RELATIONS FOR THE COMPANY, EFFECTIVE NOV. 29.
