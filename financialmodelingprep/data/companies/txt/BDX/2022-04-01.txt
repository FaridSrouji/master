BD ANNOUNCES COMPLETION OF EMBECTA CORP. SPINOFF. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J. , APRIL 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS SPINOFF OF EMBECTA CORP. (EMBECTA) (NASDAQ: EMBC), WHICH HOLDS BD'S FORMER DIABETES CARE BUSINESS AND IS NOW ONE OF THE LARGEST PURE-PLAY DIABETES MANAGEMENT COMPANIES IN THE WORLD.
