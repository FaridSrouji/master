MICHELLE QUINN NAMED GENERAL COUNSEL FOR BD. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J. , APRIL 3, 2023  BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MICHELLE QUINN HAS BEEN NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL COUNSEL FOR THE COMPANY, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1.
