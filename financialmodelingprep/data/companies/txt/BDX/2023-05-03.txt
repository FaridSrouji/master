BD RECOGNIZED BY DIVERSITYINC AS A TOP U.S. COMPANY FOR DIVERSITY IN 2023 LIST. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J. , MAY 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED A "NOTEWORTHY COMPANY" IN DIVERSITYINC'S ANNUAL RANKING OF THE TOP U.S. COMPANIES FOR DIVERSITY.
