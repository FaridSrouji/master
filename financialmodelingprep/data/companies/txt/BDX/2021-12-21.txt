BD ACQUIRES SCANWELL HEALTH, INC. TO EXPAND AND SCALE DIGITAL AT-HOME TESTING. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., DEC. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF PRIVATELY HELD SCANWELL HEALTH INC., A LEADER IN SMARTPHONE-ENABLED AT-HOME MEDICAL TESTS.
