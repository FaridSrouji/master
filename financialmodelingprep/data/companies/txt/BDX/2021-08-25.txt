BD LAUNCHES FULLY AUTOMATED HIGH-THROUGHPUT MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTIC PLATFORM FOR U.S. LABORATORIES. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., AUG. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS LAUNCHED A NEW, FULLY AUTOMATED HIGH-THROUGHPUT DIAGNOSTIC SYSTEM USING ROBOTICS AND SAMPLE MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE ALGORITHMS TO SET A NEW STANDARD IN AUTOMATION FOR INFECTIOUS DISEASE MOLECULAR TESTING IN CORE LABORATORIES AND OTHER CENTRALIZED LABORATORIES IN THE UNITED STATES.
