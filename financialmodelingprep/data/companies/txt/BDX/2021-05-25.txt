BD LAUNCHES INDUSTRY FIRST READY-TO-USE IODINE-BASED SURGICAL IRRIGATION SYSTEM. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE AVAILABILITY OF BD SURGIPHOR™ STERILE WOUND IRRIGATION SYSTEM, THE FIRST AND ONLY READY-TO-USE AQUEOUS POVIDONE-IODINE (PVP-I) IRRIGATION SOLUTION THAT MECHANICALLY LOOSENS AND REMOVES WOUND DEBRIS.
