PINNACLE WEST CAPITAL CORPORATION INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 2, 2019. PINNACLE WEST CAPITAL CORPORATION’S BOARD OF DIRECTORS VOTED ON OCTOBER 23, 2019 TO RAISE THE COMPANY’S DIVIDEND BY 6.1%, OR 18 CENTS PER COMMON SHARE ANNUALLY. THIS ACTION WILL RESULT IN AN INDICATED ANNUAL DIVIDEND OF $3.13 PER SHARE – OR $0.7825 PER SHARE QUARTERLY. THE DECLARED QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DEC. 2, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOV. 4, 2019.
