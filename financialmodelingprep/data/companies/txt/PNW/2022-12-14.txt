PINNACLE WEST DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. PHOENIX--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PINNACLE WEST CAPITAL CORPORATION'S (NYSE: PNW) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.865 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MARCH 1, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEB. 1, 2023.
