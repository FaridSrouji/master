LINDE TO SUPPLY WORLD'S FIRST HYDROGEN-POWERED FERRY. GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 8, 2021 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN; FWB:LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN SELECTED BY NORWEGIAN FERRY OPERATOR NORLED TO SUPPLY LIQUID HYDROGEN AND RELATED INFRASTRUCTURE TO THE WORLD'S FIRST OPERATIONAL HYDROGEN-POWERED FERRY, WHICH WILL TRANSPORT BOTH CARS AND PASSENGERS. LINDE WILL PROVIDE A FULL-SERVICE SOLUTION TO THE MF HYDRA FERRY IN NORWAY.
