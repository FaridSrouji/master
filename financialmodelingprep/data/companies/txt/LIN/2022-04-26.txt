LINDE PLC: LINDE DECLARES DIVIDEND IN SECOND QUARTER 2022. WOKING, UK/ ACCESSWIRE / APRIL 26, 2022 / LINDE PLC (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.17 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 17, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 3, 2022.
