LINDE PLC: LINDE PREPARES FOR LAST DAY OF TRADING ON FRANKFURT STOCK EXCHANGE. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / FEBRUARY 23, 2023 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE IRISH HIGH COURT HEARINGS REGARDING ITS INTERCOMPANY REORGANIZATION ARE SCHEDULED FOR FEBRUARY 27, 2023. ONCE THE REORGANIZATION IS APPROVED BY THE IRISH HIGH COURT, LINDE EXPECTS ITS LAST TRADING DAY ON THE FRANKFURT STOCK EXCHANGE TO BE FEBRUARY 28, 2023.
