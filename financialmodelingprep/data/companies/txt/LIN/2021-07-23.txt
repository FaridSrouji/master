LINDE RECOGNIZED AS A LEADER IN 100 BEST CORPORATE CITIZENS OF 2021. GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / JULY 23, 2021 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE 100 BEST CORPORATE CITIZENS OF 2021, WHICH IDENTIFIES THE TOP COMPANIES FOR ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PRACTICES. THE 100 BEST CORPORATE CITIZENS OF 2021 WAS COMPILED BASED ON THOROUGH ANALYSIS OF THE PUBLIC DISCLOSURES, POLICIES AND PERFORMANCE OF THE 1,000 LARGEST, PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. COMPANIES.
