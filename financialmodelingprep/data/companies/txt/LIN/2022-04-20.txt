LINDE SIGNS NEW SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH MAJOR SPACE LAUNCH COMPANY. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / APRIL 20, 2022 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED ANOTHER LONG-TERM AGREEMENT WITH A MAJOR SPACE LAUNCH COMPANY IN FLORIDA FOR THE SUPPLY OF BULK INDUSTRIAL GASES. LINDE WILL SUPPLY THE CUSTOMER WITH LIQUID OXYGEN AND NITROGEN FROM ITS AIR SEPARATION PLANT AT MIMS, FLORIDA, WHICH IS CURRENTLY UNDERGOING A FURTHER EXPANSION TO INCREASE PRODUCTION CAPACITY BY UP TO 50%.
