LINDE TO EXPAND PRODUCTION CAPACITY IN FLORIDA TO MEET GROWING DEMAND. GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / SEPTEMBER 13, 2021 / LINDE (NYSE: LIN; FWB: LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO INCREASE PRODUCTION CAPACITY AT ITS AIR SEPARATION PLANT IN MIMS, FLORIDA, TO MEET RAPIDLY GROWING DEMAND FOR INDUSTRIAL GASES. THROUGH ITS INVESTMENT, LINDE WILL INCREASE CAPACITY AT MIMS BY ALMOST 50% TO SUPPLY ITS CONTRACTED CUSTOMERS ACROSS ALL END MARKETS, INCLUDING AEROSPACE, HEALTHCARE, MANUFACTURING, FOOD PROCESSING AND WATER TREATMENT.
