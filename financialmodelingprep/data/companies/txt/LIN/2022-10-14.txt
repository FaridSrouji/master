LINDE JOINS UNITED NATIONS GLOBAL COMPACT. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / OCTOBER 14, 2022 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BECOME A SIGNATORY TO THE UNITED NATIONS GLOBAL COMPACT (UNGC), THE WORLD'S LARGEST CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY INITIATIVE. THROUGH ITS PARTICIPATION, LINDE HAS COMMITTED TO ALIGN ITS STRATEGY AND ACTIVITIES WITH THE UNGCS TEN PRINCIPLES ACROSS HUMAN RIGHTS, LABOR, ENVIRONMENT AND ANTI-CORRUPTION.
