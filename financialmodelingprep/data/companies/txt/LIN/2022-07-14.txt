LINDE INCLUDED IN FTSE4GOOD INDEX FOR SEVENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / JULY 14, 2022 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN; FWB:LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE FTSE4GOOD INDEX FOR THE SEVENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. THE FTSE4GOOD INDEX SERIES IS A TOOL FOR INVESTORS SEEKING TO INVEST IN COMPANIES THAT DEMONSTRATE STRONG ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PRACTICES MEASURED AGAINST INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS.
