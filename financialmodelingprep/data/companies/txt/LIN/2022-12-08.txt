LINDE ACHIEVES INDUSTRY-LEADING SCORE IN S&P GLOBAL CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY ASSESSMENT. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / DECEMBER 8, 2022 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS ACHIEVED AN INDUSTRY-LEADING SCORE IN THE 2022 S&P GLOBAL CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY ASSESSMENT (CSA). THE S&P GLOBAL CSA IS A SUSTAINABILITY BENCHMARK, WHICH ALSO SERVES AS A BASIS FOR THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDICES (DJSI), S&P 500 ESG AND OTHER SUSTAINABILITY INDICES.
