GREAT LAKES CLEAN HYDROGEN COALITION ENCOURAGED TO SUBMIT FULL APPLICATION BY U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY. TOLEDO, OHIO , JAN. 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE GREAT LAKES CLEAN HYDROGEN COALITION (GLCH) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED ENCOURAGEMENT FROM THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (DOE) TO PROCEED WITH THE DEVELOPMENT AND SUBMITTAL OF THEIR FULL HYDROGEN HUB APPLICATION.  THE COALITION, WHICH IS LED BY LINDE (NYSE: LIN; FWB: LIN), ENERGY HARBOR, CLEVELAND-CLIFFS INC. (NYSE: CLF), GE AEROSPACE AND THE UNIVERSITY OF TOLEDO, ALONG WITH THE GLASS MANUFACTURING INDUSTRY COUNCIL (GMIC), WAS AMONG 79 ORGANIZATIONS SUBMITTING CONCEPT PAPERS TO THE DOE FOR DEVELOPMENT CONSIDERATION.
