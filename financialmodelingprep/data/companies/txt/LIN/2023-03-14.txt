S&P GLOBAL INCLUDES LINDE IN THE SUSTAINABILITY YEARBOOK FOR 20TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 14, 2023 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN S&P GLOBAL'S SUSTAINABILITY YEARBOOK 2023, ITS 20TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR OF INCLUSION. THE SUSTAINABILITY YEARBOOK RECOGNIZES THE WORLD'S LEADING COMPANIES FOR SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES.
