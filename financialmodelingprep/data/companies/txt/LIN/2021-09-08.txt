LINDE SIGNS LONG-TERM AGREEMENT TO SUPPLY NEW WORLD-CLASS SEMICONDUCTOR MANUFACTURING COMPLEX IN THE U.S.. GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / SEPTEMBER 8, 2021 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN; FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS SIGNED A LONG-TERM AGREEMENT WITH ONE OF THE WORLD'S LARGEST SEMICONDUCTOR MANUFACTURERS FOR THE SUPPLY OF INDUSTRIAL GASES TO A NEW MULTI-BILLION DOLLAR MANUFACTURING FACILITY IN PHOENIX, ARIZONA. LINDE WILL BUILD, OWN AND OPERATE A COMPLEX OF ON-SITE PLANTS TO SUPPLY ULTRA-HIGH-PURITY NITROGEN, OXYGEN AND ARGON TO THE CUSTOMER.
