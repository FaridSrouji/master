LINDE SIGNS RECORD SMALL ON-SITE CONTRACTS IN 2021. GUILFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / JANUARY 27, 2022 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS ACHIEVED ANOTHER RECORD YEAR FOR SMALL ON-SITE CONTRACTS, SIGNING 43 NEW PROJECTS DURING 2021, A 19% INCREASE COMPARED WITH 2020. LINDE SIGNED NEW AGREEMENTS TOTALING $170 MILLION IN 2021, WITH CUSTOMERS ACROSS 20 COUNTRIES AND END-MARKETS INCLUDING ENERGY, ELECTRONICS, MINING AND MANUFACTURING.
