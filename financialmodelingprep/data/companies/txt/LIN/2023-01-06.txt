LINDE BUYS OUT STAKE IN ONE OF THE LARGEST U.S. INDEPENDENT PACKAGED GAS DISTRIBUTORS. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / JANUARY 6, 2022 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS ACQUIRED THE REMAINING INTERESTS IN NEXAIR, LLC, ONE OF THE LARGEST INDEPENDENT PACKAGED GAS DISTRIBUTORS IN THE UNITED STATES WITH 2022 SALES OF APPROXIMATELY $400 MILLION. LINDE'S WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY LINDE GAS & EQUIPMENT INC. (LG&E) HAS HELD A MINORITY INTEREST IN NEXAIR SINCE 2012.
