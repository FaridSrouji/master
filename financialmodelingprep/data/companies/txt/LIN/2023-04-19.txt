LINDE TO INCREASE CAPACITY OF SAMSUNG DISPLAY ON-SITE FACILITY IN SOUTH KOREA. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / APRIL 19, 2023 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL EXPAND THE CAPACITY OF ITS ON-SITE FACILITY IN TANGJEONG, SOUTH KOREA, TO INCREASE ITS SUPPLY OF HIGH-PURITY INDUSTRIAL GASES TO SAMSUNG DISPLAY. THE INCREASED CAPACITY SUPPORTS SAMSUNG DISPLAY'S PLANNED MULTI-BILLION-DOLLAR INVESTMENT TO CONVERT ITS EXISTING LCD PRODUCTION LINE TO OLED, IN RESPONSE TO GLOBAL MARKET DEMAND.
