LINDE DECLARES DIVIDEND IN THIRD QUARTER 2021. GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / JULY 26, 2021 / LINDE PLC (NYSE:LIN; FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.06 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 17, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON SEPTEMBER 3, 2021.
