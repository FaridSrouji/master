DOW SELECTS LINDE AS CLEAN HYDROGEN AND NITROGEN PARTNER FOR ITS PROPOSED NET-ZERO CARBON EMISSIONS ETHYLENE AND DERIVATIVES COMPLEX IN CANADA. MIDLAND, MICH. , APRIL 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOW (NYSE: DOW) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS SELECTED LINDE (NYSE: LIN) AS ITS INDUSTRIAL GAS PARTNER FOR THE SUPPLY OF CLEAN HYDROGEN AND NITROGEN FOR ITS PROPOSED NET-ZERO CARBON EMISSIONS1 INTEGRATED ETHYLENE CRACKER AND DERIVATIVES SITE IN FORT SASKATCHEWAN, ALBERTA, CANADA.
