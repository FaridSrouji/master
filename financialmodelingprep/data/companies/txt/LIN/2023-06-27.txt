LINDE TO DE-CAPTIVATE MULTIPLE AIR SEPARATION UNITS AND EXPAND COOPERATION WITH WANHUA CHEMICAL GROUP. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / JUNE 27, 2023 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A SERIES OF CONTRACTS WITH WANHUA CHEMICAL GROUP, EXPANDING THE COMPANIES' COOPERATION ACROSS MULTIPLE KEY SITES IN CHINA. IN FUJIAN PROVINCE, LINDE WILL ACQUIRE THREE AIR SEPARATION UNITS (ASUS) FROM WANHUA, INCLUDING TWO ASUS THAT ARE CURRENTLY UNDER CONSTRUCTION AND EXPECTED TO START UP IN 2024 AND 2025, RESPECTIVELY.
