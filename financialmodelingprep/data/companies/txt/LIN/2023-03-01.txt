LINDE PLC: LINDE COMPLETES DELISTING FROM FRANKFURT STOCK EXCHANGE. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 1, 2023 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS INTERCOMPANY REORGANIZATION RESULTING IN THE DELISTING OF ITS SHARES FROM THE FRANKFURT STOCK EXCHANGE. AS OF MARCH 2, 2023, THE NEW HOLDING COMPANY, "LINDE PLC", WILL BE LISTED SOLELY ON THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE AND TRADE UNDER THE EXISTING TICKER "LIN".
