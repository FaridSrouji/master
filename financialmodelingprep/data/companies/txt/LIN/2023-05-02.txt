GREAT LAKES CLEAN HYDROGEN HUB COALITION SUBMITS APPLICATION FOR DOE FUNDING. TOLEDO, OHIO , MAY 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE GREAT LAKES CLEAN HYDROGEN HUB COALITION (GLCH) HAS SUBMITTED A FULL APPLICATION FOR FEDERAL FUNDING FROM THE $8 BILLION U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (DOE) PROGRAM TO SUPPORT THE CREATION OF REGIONAL CLEAN HYDROGEN HUBS UNDER THE INFRASTRUCTURE INVESTMENT AND JOBS ACT.  GREAT LAKES CLEAN HYDROGEN HUB COALITION AIMS TO HELP MAJOR INDUSTRIES ACROSS MIDWEST DECARBONIZE.
