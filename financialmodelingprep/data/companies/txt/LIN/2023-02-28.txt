LINDE PLC: LINDE DECLARES DIVIDEND INCREASE FOR THE 30TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / FEBRUARY 28, 2023 / LINDE PLC (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A 9% INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO $1.275 PER SHARE. THIS MARKS THE 30TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR OF QUARTERLY DIVIDEND INCREASES ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK.
