LINDE STARTS UP MAJOR NEW HYDROGEN FACILITY IN THE U.S. GULF COAST. GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / OCTOBER 21, 2021 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS STARTED UP A NEW WORLD-SCALE HYDROGEN PRODUCTION FACILITY IN TEXAS, BRINGING LINDE'S TOTAL U.S. GULF COAST HYDROGEN CAPACITY TO APPROXIMATELY 1.5 BILLION CUBIC FEET PER DAY. LINDE'S NEW PLANT HAS STARTED SUPPLYING HIGH-PURITY HYDROGEN TO THE PHILLIPS 66 SWEENY REFINERY IN OLD OCEAN, TEXAS, UNDER A LONG-TERM SUPPLY AGREEMENT.
