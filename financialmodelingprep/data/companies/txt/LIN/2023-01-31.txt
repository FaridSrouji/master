LINDE TO INCREASE GREEN HYDROGEN PRODUCTION IN CALIFORNIA. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / JANUARY 31, 2023 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY PLANS TO INCREASE ITS GREEN HYDROGEN PRODUCTION CAPACITY IN ONTARIO, CALIFORNIA, IN RESPONSE TO GROWING DEMAND FROM THE MOBILITY MARKET. LINDE WILL BUILD, OWN AND OPERATE THE FIRST OF SEVERAL PLANNED FIVE-MEGAWATT PEM (PROTON EXCHANGE MEMBRANE) ELECTROLYZERS TO INCREASE ITS HYDROGEN CAPACITY.
