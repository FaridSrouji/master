LINDE WINS CONTRACT FOR CRACKER UNIT OF SIBUR'S AMUR GAS CHEMICAL COMPLEX. IT HAS BEEN AWARDED A CONTRACT TO PROVIDE ENGINEERING, PROCUREMENT AND SITE SERVICES BASED ON ITS PROPRIETARY TECHNOLOGY FOR CRACKER UNIT OF SIBUR'S AMUR GAS CHEMICAL COMPLEX.SERVICES WILL BE PROVIDED AS PART OF A CONSORTIUM WITH NIPIGAS.
