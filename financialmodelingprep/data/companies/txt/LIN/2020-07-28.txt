LINDE TO START CONSTRUCTION OF HYDROGEN REFUELING STATION FOR PASSENGER TRAINS. TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL START CONSTRUCTION OF WORLD'S FIRST HYDROGEN REFUELING STATION FOR PASSENGER TRAINS IN BREMERVÖRDE, GERMANY, IN SEPTEMBER.LINDE WILL BUILD AND OPERATE HYDROGEN FILLING STATION, WHICH IS EXPECTED TO START SERVICE IN EARLY 2022.STATION WILL FUEL 14 HYDROGEN-POWERED PASSENGER TRAINS WHICH WILL BE SUPPLIED BY ALSTOM TO REGIONAL RAIL TRAFFIC PROVIDER, LNVG, AND WILL USE NETWORK OF REGIONAL RAILWAY COMPANY EVB.HYDROGEN REFUELING STATION WILL HAVE A CAPACITY OF AROUND 1,600 KG OF HYDROGEN PER DAY.
