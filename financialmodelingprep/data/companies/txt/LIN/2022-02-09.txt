S&P GLOBAL INCLUDES LINDE IN THE SUSTAINABILITY YEARBOOK 2022. GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / FEBRUARY 9, 2022 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN) (FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN S&P GLOBAL'S SUSTAINABILITY YEARBOOK 2022, IN RECOGNITION OF ITS STRONG SUSTAINABILITY TRACK RECORD. THE SUSTAINABILITY YEARBOOK IS ONE OF THE LARGEST AND MOST COMPREHENSIVE GLOBAL DATABASES ON CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY.
