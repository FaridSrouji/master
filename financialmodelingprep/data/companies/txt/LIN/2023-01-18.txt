LINDE SHAREHOLDERS APPROVE PROPOSAL TO DELIST FROM FRANKFURT STOCK EXCHANGE. WOKING, UK / ACCESSWIRE / JANUARY 18, 2023 / LINDE PLC (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS SHAREHOLDERS HAVE APPROVED THE COMPANY'S PROPOSAL FOR AN INTERCOMPANY REORGANIZATION THAT WILL RESULT IN THE DELISTING OF ITS ORDINARY SHARES FROM THE FRANKFURT STOCK EXCHANGE. PRELIMINARY VOTING RESULTS INDICATE THAT AT LEAST APPROXIMATELY 93% OF THE VOTES CAST ON EACH OF THE DELISTING PROPOSALS WERE CAST IN FAVOR AT THE COURT MEETING AND THE EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS HELD TODAY.
