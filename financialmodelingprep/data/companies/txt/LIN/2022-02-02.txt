THE HEALTH TRANSFORMATION ALLIANCE (HTA) RECOGNIZES MEMBERS LINDE AND WAYNE FARMS FOR EXCELLENCE IN EMPLOYEE HEALTHCARE IN 2021. WESTMONT, N.J., FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HEALTH TRANSFORMATION ALLIANCE (HTA), A COOPERATIVE OF LEADING EMPLOYERS ACROSS THE NATION UNITED IN THE COMMON CAUSE TO IMPROVE HEALTHCARE QUALITY AND AFFORDABILITY FOR EMPLOYEES AND THEIR FAMILIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FIRST ANNUAL MEMBER AWARDS FOR 2021.
