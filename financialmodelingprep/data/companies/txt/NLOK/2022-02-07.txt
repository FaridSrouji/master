NORTONLIFELOCK RECEIVES ANTITRUST CLEARANCE IN GERMANY FOR THE RECOMMENDED MERGER WITH AVAST PLC. TEMPE, ARIZ., FEB. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORTONLIFELOCK (NASDAQ: NLOK), A GLOBAL LEADER IN CONSUMER CYBER SAFETY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS RECOMMENDED MERGER (THE "MERGER") WITH AVAST PLC ("AVAST") HAS RECEIVED ANTITRUST CLEARANCE FROM GERMANY'S FEDERAL CARTEL OFFICE.
