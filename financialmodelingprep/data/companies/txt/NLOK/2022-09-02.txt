NORTONLIFELOCK'S ACQUISITION OF AVAST TO CLOSE SEPTEMBER 12, 2022. NORTONLIFELOCK RECEIVES APPROVAL FROM U.K. COMPETITION AND MARKETS AUTHORITY (CMA) TEMPE, ARIZ. , SEPT. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORTONLIFELOCK (NASDAQ: NLOK), A GLOBAL LEADER IN CONSUMER CYBER SAFETY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS MERGER WITH AVAST PLC ("AVAST" OR THE "COMPANY") IS NOW EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON SEPTEMBER 12, 2022, FOLLOWING THE ISSUANCE OF THE CMA'S FINAL REPORT CLEARING THE TRANSACTION.
