CHUBB SUPPORTS LANDMARK DEBT-FOR-NATURE RESTRUCTURING PROJECT IN BELIZE. ZURICH, DEC. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS SUPPORT FOR THE U.S. INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT FINANCE CORPORATION'S (DFC) PARTICIPATION IN A LANDMARK DEBT RESTRUCTURING AND OCEAN CONSERVATION PROJECT IN BELIZE.  CHUBB GLOBAL MARKETS AND SOVEREIGN RISK INSURANCE LIMITED, A CHUBB COMPANY, TOGETHER WITH OTHER PRIVATE INSURERS, PROVIDED $300 MILLION OF REINSURANCE TO DFC IN AN INNOVATIVE POLITICAL RISK INSURANCE TRANSACTION DESIGNED TO HELP BELIZE REDUCE ITS EXTERNAL GOVERNMENT DEBT AND FUND MARINE PROTECTION.
