CHUBB INTRODUCES NEW AND MODERNIZED PACKAGE PRODUCT FOR COMMERCIAL CLIENTS. WHITEHOUSE STATION, N.J., MAY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB HAS INTRODUCED A NEW INSURANCE PRODUCT, BENCHMARQ PACKAGE, FOR THE LOWER MIDDLE MARKET SEGMENT, SETTING THE STANDARD FOR PACKAGE POLICIES FOCUSED ON BROADER AND MORE SCALABLE COVERAGE AND EFFICIENCY.
