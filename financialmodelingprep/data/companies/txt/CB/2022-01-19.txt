REGIONAL MANAGEMENT CORP. APPOINTS PHILIP BANCROFT TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. GREENVILLE, S.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--REGIONAL MANAGEMENT CORP. (NYSE: RM), A DIVERSIFIED CONSUMER FINANCE COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF PHILIP BANCROFT TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MR. BANCROFT IS THE FORMER CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND CURRENT EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF CHUBB (NYSE: CB), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED PROPERTY AND CASUALTY INSURANCE COMPANY IN THE WORLD. MR.
