CHUBB LAUNCHES NEW EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES LIABILITY INSURANCE POLICY. WHITEHOUSE STATION, N.J., DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  CHUBB HAS LAUNCHED A NEW INSURANCE OFFERING TO HELP PROTECT COMPANIES FROM THE FINANCIAL COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH EMPLOYMENT-RELATED LAWSUITS FILED FOR A RANGE OF ISSUES, INCLUDING WRONGFUL TERMINATION, BREACH OF CONTRACT, RETALIATION AND OTHERWORK-RELATED ISSUES.
