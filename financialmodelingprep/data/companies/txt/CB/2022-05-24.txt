CHUBB APPOINTS JOHN THOMPSON VICE PRESIDENT, CHUBB GROUP AND DIVISION PRESIDENT OF INTERNATIONAL PERSONAL LINES; DANIELA HERNANDEZ NAMED DIVISION PRESIDENT OF INTERNATIONAL ACCIDENT & HEALTH. ZURICH , MAY 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB LIMITED (NYSE: CB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JOHN THOMPSON HAS BEEN APPOINTED VICE PRESIDENT, CHUBB GROUP AND DIVISION PRESIDENT OF INTERNATIONAL PERSONAL LINES. THE COMPANY ALSO NAMED DANIELA HERNANDEZ DIVISION PRESIDENT OF INTERNATIONAL ACCIDENT & HEALTH (A&H).
