CHUBB APPOINTS NATALIE CHIARAMONTE PRESIDENT OF SOVEREIGN RISK INSURANCE LIMITED. HAMILTON, BERMUDA, JULY 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT NATALIE CHIARAMONTE HAS BEEN NAMED DIVISION PRESIDENT OF SOVEREIGN RISK INSURANCE LIMITED, A CHUBB COMPANY AND LEADING UNDERWRITER OF POLITICAL RISK INSURANCE AND REINSURANCE.
