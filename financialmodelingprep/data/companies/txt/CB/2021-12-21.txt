CHUBB AND WHARTON STUDY EXAMINES ULTRA-HIGH-NET-WORTH INDIVIDUALS' PREFERENCES ON FINANCIAL ADVISING AND INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT. WHITEHOUSE STATION, N.J., DEC. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB AND THE WHARTON SCHOOL OF THE UNIVERSITY OF PENNSYLVANIA HAVE RELEASED A NEW RESEARCH REPORT THAT EXAMINES DIFFERENCES IN HOW WEALTH MANAGERS AND ULTRA-HIGH-NET-WORTH (UHNW) INDIVIDUALS EVALUATE ASSETS AND COORDINATE RISK MANAGEMENT ACTIVITIES.
