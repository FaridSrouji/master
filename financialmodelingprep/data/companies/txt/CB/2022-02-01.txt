CHUBB REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER NET INCOME PER SHARE OF $4.95 VERSUS $5.34 PRIOR YEAR, AND RECORD CORE OPERATING INCOME PER SHARE OF $3.81, UP 19.8%; COMMERCIAL LINES NET PREMIUMS WRITTEN UP 12.9%; P&C COMBINED RATIO OF 85.5%. ZURICH, FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB LIMITED (NYSE: CB) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME FOR THE QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2021 OF $2.14 BILLION, OR $4.95 PER SHARE, AND CORE OPERATING INCOME OF $1.65 BILLION, OR $3.81 PER SHARE. THE P&C COMBINED RATIO WAS 85.5% COMPARED TO 87.6% PRIOR YEAR, AND THE CURRENT ACCIDENT YEAR P&C COMBINED RATIO EXCLUDING CATASTROPHE LOSSES WAS 83.9% COMPARED TO 86.4% PRIOR YEAR.
