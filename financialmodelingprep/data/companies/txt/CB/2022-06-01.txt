CHUBB APPOINTS CHERYL KRAUSS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHUBB GROUP AND CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER. ZURICH , JUNE 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB LIMITED (NYSE: CB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHERYL KRAUSS HAS BEEN APPOINTED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHUBB GROUP AND CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JUNE 6.  MS. KRAUSS WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR CHUBB'S CORPORATE COMMUNICATIONS AND MARKETING FUNCTIONS GLOBALLY.
