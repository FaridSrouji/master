CHUBB APPOINTS FRAN O'BRIEN DEPUTY CHIEF RISK OFFICER; ANA ROBIC NAMED DIVISION PRESIDENT, NORTH AMERICA PERSONAL RISK SERVICES. ZURICH, DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB LIMITED (NYSE: CB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FRAN O'BRIEN HAS BEEN APPOINTED DEPUTY CHIEF RISK OFFICER. CURRENTLY, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHUBB GROUP AND DIVISION PRESIDENT, NORTH AMERICA PERSONAL RISK SERVICES, MS.
