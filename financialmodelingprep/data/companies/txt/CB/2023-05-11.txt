CHUBB NAMES ADAM MASON EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CASUALTY, FOR ITS INTERNATIONAL GENERAL INSURANCE OPERATIONS. SEASONED UNDERWRITER WITH SIGNIFICANT EXPERIENCE ACROSS ALL REGIONS NEW YORK , MAY 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ADAM MASON HAS BEEN APPOINTED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CASUALTY FOR OVERSEAS GENERAL INSURANCE, THE COMPANY'S GENERAL INSURANCE OPERATIONS. CURRENTLY, MASON SERVES AS DEPUTY HEAD OF CASUALTY FOR THE BUSINESS.
