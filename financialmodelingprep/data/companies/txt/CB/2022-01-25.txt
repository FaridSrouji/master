CHUBB NAMES MIRIAM CONNOLE CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER FOR ITS INTERNATIONAL GENERAL INSURANCE OPERATIONS; JOHN JONES TO RETIRE. NEW YORK, JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MIRIAM CONNOLE HAS BEEN APPOINTED CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER FOR OVERSEAS GENERAL INSURANCE, THE COMPANY'S INTERNATIONAL GENERAL INSURANCE OPERATIONS. CURRENTLY, SHE SERVES AS CFO FOR CHUBB'S EUROPE AND MIDDLE EAST & AFRICA REGIONS.
