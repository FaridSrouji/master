CHUBB RULE OF LAW FUND EXPANDS SUPPORT FOR PROJECTS THAT ADVANCE RACIAL JUSTICE. NEW YORK, MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS INNOVATIVE CHUBB RULE OF LAW FUND, SUPPORTED BY 15 LAW FIRMS AND THE CHUBB CHARITABLE FOUNDATION, HAS AWARDED FOUR NEW GRANTS, TOTALING $1.1 MILLION, TO PROJECTS THAT PROMOTE EQUITY AND ADVANCE RACIAL JUSTICE. SINCE IT WAS ESTABLISHED IN 2008, THE CHUBB RULE OF LAW FUND HAS MADE 66 GRANTS TO SUPPORT PROJECTS AROUND THE WORLD THAT PROMOTE THE PRESERVATION AND ADVANCEMENT OF THE RULE OF LAW.
