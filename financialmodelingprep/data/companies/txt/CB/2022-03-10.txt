CHUBB EXPANDS NORTH AMERICA LEADERSHIP TEAM WITH NEW APPOINTMENTS IN SMALL COMMERCIAL AND LOWER MIDDLE MARKET SEGMENTS. WHITEHOUSE STATION, N.J., MARCH 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB HAS EXPANDED ITS COMMERCIAL LEADERSHIP TEAM WITH THE APPOINTMENT OF KETAN VAIDYA AS DIVISION PRESIDENT OF NORTH AMERICA SMALL COMMERCIAL INSURANCE.
