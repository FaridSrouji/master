CHUBB ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENTS IN ITS ACCIDENT AND HEALTH BUSINESS. ALIGNMENT OF LEADERSHIP TO PROVIDE GREATER ACCESS TO RESOURCES AIMED AT DELIVERING A BROAD RANGE OF ACCIDENT AND HEALTH-RELATED RISK SOLUTIONS WHITEHOUSE STATION, N.J., APRIL 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB HAS ANNOUNCED TWO LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENTS IN ITS NORTH AMERICA ACCIDENT AND HEALTH (A&H) BUSINESS THAT ARE INTENDED TO PROVIDE DISTRIBUTION PARTNERS AND CLIENTS EVEN GREATER ACCESS TO A&H PRODUCT OFFERINGS AND RESOURCES.
CHUBB APPOINTS LYNDSEY CHRISTOFER CONSTRUCTION INDUSTRY PRACTICE LEADER. WHITEHOUSE STATION, N.J., APRIL 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB HAS APPOINTED LYNDSEY CHRISTOFER AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CONSTRUCTION INDUSTRY PRACTICE LEADER FOR ITS LARGE ACCOUNT SEGMENT.
