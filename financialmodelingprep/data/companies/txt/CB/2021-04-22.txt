CHUBB SURVEY FINDS BUSINESS TRAVELERS AROUND THE WORLD AGREE: JOB EFFECTIVENESS HAS SUFFERED FROM THE PANDEMIC AND TRAVEL RESTRICTIONS. NEW YORK, APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A NEW SURVEY RELEASED TODAY BY CHUBB FINDS THAT BUSINESS TRAVELERS AROUND THE WORLD BELIEVE THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC AND RELATED TRAVEL RESTRICTIONS HAVE NEGATIVELY IMPACTED THEIR EFFECTIVENESS ON THE JOB AS WELL AS THE ABILITY OF THEIR EMPLOYERS TO DEVELOP BUSINESS, SERVE CLIENTS AND MAINTAIN BUSINESS RELATIONSHIPS. AT THE SAME TIME, BUSINESS TRAVELERS REPORT THAT THEY HAVE PERSONALLY MISSED TRAVELING FOR WORK AND LEISURE, AND ARE EAGER TO GET BACK ON THE ROAD.
