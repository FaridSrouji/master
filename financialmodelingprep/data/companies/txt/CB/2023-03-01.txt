CHUBB TEMPEST RE APPOINTS WAYNE ASHLEY TO LEAD ITS INTERNATIONAL P&C REINSURANCE BUSINESS. LONDON, MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB TEMPEST RE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT WAYNE ASHLEY HAS BEEN APPOINTED DIVISION PRESIDENT, CHUBB TEMPEST RE INTERNATIONAL. CURRENTLY, HE SERVES AS DEPUTY HEAD OF THE BUSINESS.
