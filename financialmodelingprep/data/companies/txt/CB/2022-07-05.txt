CHUBB LIFE APPOINTS JACK CHANG TO LEAD NEWLY ACQUIRED LIFE INSURANCE OPERATIONS OF CIGNA IN TAIWAN. HONG KONG , JULY 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB LIFE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JACK CHANG HAS BEEN APPOINTED PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY'S LIFE INSURANCE OPERATIONS IN TAIWAN THAT WERE RECENTLY ACQUIRED FROM CIGNA. MR. CHANG IS CURRENTLY PRESIDENT OF CHUBB LIFE TAIWAN.

CHUBB LIFE ANNOUNCES GAIL COSTA WILL LEAD NEWLY ACQUIRED LIFE INSURANCE OPERATIONS OF CIGNA IN NEW ZEALAND.
