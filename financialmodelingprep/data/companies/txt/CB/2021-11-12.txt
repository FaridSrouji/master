NEW CHUBB SURVEY MEASURES AMERICANS' ATTITUDES TOWARD COLLECTING VALUABLES AND PURCHASING DECISIONS AMID THE PANDEMIC. WHITEHOUSE STATION, N.J., NOV. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A NEW SURVEY ON VALUABLE COLLECTIONS FROM CHUBB FINDS AMERICAN COLLECTORS HAVE CONTINUED TO EXPAND THEIR PERSONAL VALUABLE COLLECTIONS, AMID THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC AND ITS CHALLENGES.
