CHUBB APPOINTS MICHAEL O'DONNELL CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER OF GLOBAL REINSURANCE. ZURICH , JUNE 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB LIMITED (NYSE: CB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MICHAEL O'DONNELL HAS BEEN APPOINTED CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, GLOBAL REINSURANCE. CURRENTLY, HE SERVES AS DIVISION PRESIDENT OF CHUBB TEMPEST RE USA.
