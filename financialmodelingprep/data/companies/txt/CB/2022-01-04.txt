CHUBB TO APPOINT BRYCE JOHNS TO LEAD COMPANY'S INTERNATIONAL LIFE INSURANCE BUSINESS. ZURICH, JAN. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB LIMITED (NYSE: CB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BRYCE JOHNS WILL BE APPOINTED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHUBB GROUP AND PRESIDENT OF CHUBB LIFE.  HE WILL JOIN THE COMPANY FROM HSBC, WHERE HE SERVED MOST RECENTLY AS GLOBAL CEO OF HSBC LIFE AND INSURANCE PARTNERSHIPS.
