COVID-19 IS A TOP CAUSE OF DISABILITY FOR U.S. WORKERS. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., JUNE 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COVID-19 WAS THE THIRD-LEADING CAUSE OF SHORT-TERM DISABILITY FOR U.S. WORKERS IN 2020, ACCORDING TO INTERNAL DATA FROM UNUM (NYSE:UNM).
