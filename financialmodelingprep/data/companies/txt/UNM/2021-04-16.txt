UNUM GROUP DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.285 PER SHARE OF ITS COMMON STOCK. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., APRIL 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EFFECTIVE APRIL 16, 2021, THE UNUM GROUP (NYSE:UNM) BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.285 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON MAY 21, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON APRIL 30, 2021.
