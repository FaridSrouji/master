UNUM GROUP COMPLETES SENIOR NOTE OFFERING AND ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF 4.500% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., JUNE 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNUM GROUP (NYSE: UNM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED AN OFFERING OF $600 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR NOTES DUE IN 2051 WITH AN ANNUAL COUPON RATE OF 4.125 PERCENT.
