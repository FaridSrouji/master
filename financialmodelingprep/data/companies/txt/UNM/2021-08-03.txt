UNUM GROUP REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNUM GROUP (NYSE: UNM) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $182.9 MILLION ($0.89 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE) FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021, COMPARED TO NET INCOME OF $265.5 MILLION ($1.30 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE) FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020.
