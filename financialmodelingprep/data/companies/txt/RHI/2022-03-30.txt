PROTIVITI EXPANDS LEGAL CONSULTING CAPABILITIES TO MEET GROWING CLIENT NEEDS. GLOBAL BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI BROADENS ROBUST LEGAL SOLUTIONS FOR GENERAL COUNSELS, LAW FIRM PARTNERS AND OTHER LEGAL EXECUTIVES MENLO PARK, CALIF. , MARCH 30, 2022 TO MEET THE NEEDS OF CLIENTS' INCREASINGLY COMPLEX LEGAL MATTERS, PROTIVITI HAS EXPANDED ITS LEGAL CONSULTING CAPABILITIES WITH THE TRANSITION OF PARENT COMPANY ROBERT HALF'S LEGAL CONSULTING SOLUTIONS PRACTICE TO PROTIVITI.
