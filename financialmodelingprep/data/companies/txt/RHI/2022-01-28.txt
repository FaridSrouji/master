ROBERT HALF RECOGNIZED FOR ITS COMMITMENT TO DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION. MENLO PARK, CALIF., JAN. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF (NYSE: RHI) HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY THE HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION AND BLOOMBERG FOR ITS DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION (DEI) EFFORTS.
