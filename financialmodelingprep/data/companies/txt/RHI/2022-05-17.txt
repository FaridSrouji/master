ROBERT HALF'S ALFREDO MENDEZ NAMED TO STAFFING INDUSTRY ANALYSTS' 2022 DE&I INFLUENCERS LIST. MENLO PARK, CALIF. , MAY 17, 2022  ALFREDO MENDEZ, VICE PRESIDENT OF EXPERIENCE AND INCLUSION AT GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF, HAS BEEN NAMED TO STAFFING INDUSTRY ANALYSTS' 2022 DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION INFLUENCERS — U.S. AND CANADA  LIST.
