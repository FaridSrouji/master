ROBERT HALF TOPS FORBES' 2021 LIST OF AMERICA'S BEST PROFESSIONAL RECRUITING FIRMS. MENLO PARK, CALIF., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL STAFFING FIRM ROBERT HALF (NYSE: RHI) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS RANKED FIRST ON FORBES' ANNUAL LIST OF AMERICA'S BEST PROFESSIONAL RECRUITING FIRMS AND AMERICA'S BEST TEMPORARY STAFFING FIRMS.
MAJORITY OF INTERNAL AUDIT GROUPS SLOW TO ADOPT NEXT-GEN TECHNOLOGIES AND UPSKILL; AT RISK OF BECOMING OBSOLETE, ACCORDING TO PROTIVITI SURVEY. MENLO PARK, CALIF., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ACCORDING TO PROTIVITI'S 2021 NEXT-GENERATION INTERNAL AUDIT SURVEY, MOST OF THE CHIEF AUDIT EXECUTIVES (CAES) AND SENIOR INTERNAL AUDIT LEADERS SURVEYED REPORTED THAT THEIR TEAMS ARE STILL IN THE EARLY STAGES OF, OR HAVE YET TO EMBARK ON, THEIR NEXT-GEN INTERNAL AUDIT JOURNEYS.
