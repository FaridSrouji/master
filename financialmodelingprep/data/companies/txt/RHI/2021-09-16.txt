FORTUNE AND GREAT PLACE TO WORK® NAME PROTIVITI TO THE 2021 BEST WORKPLACES FOR WOMEN™ LIST. MENLO PARK, CALIF., SEPT. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE FORTUNE 2021 'BEST WORKPLACES FOR WOMEN' LIST, BASED ON THE ANALYSIS OF CONFIDENTIAL SURVEY RESPONSES FROM MORE THAN FIVE MILLION U.S. EMPLOYEES.
