PROTIVITI BRAND AMBASSADOR PRO GOLFER JENNIFER KUPCHO WINS HER FIRST LPGA TOURNAMENT AT THE CHEVRON CHAMPIONSHIP. VICTORY AT MISSION HILLS COUNTRY CLUB IN RANCHO MIRAGE ALSO MARKS KUPCHO'S FIRST MAJOR CHAMPIONSHIP WIN MENLO PARK, CALIF. , APRIL 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE THAT BRAND AMBASSADOR AND PROFESSIONAL GOLFER JENNIFER KUPCHO WON THE 2022 CHEVRON CHAMPIONSHIP IN RANCHO MIRAGE, CALIFORNIA LAST SUNDAY.
