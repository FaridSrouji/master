ROBERT HALF NAMES ROB HOSKING EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR FOR ADMINISTRATIVE AND CUSTOMER SUPPORT PRACTICE GROUP. MENLO PARK, CALIF. , MARCH 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF HAS APPOINTED ROB HOSKING AS EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF THE COMPANY'S ADMINISTRATIVE AND CUSTOMER SUPPORT PRACTICE GROUP.
