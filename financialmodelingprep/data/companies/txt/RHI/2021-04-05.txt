PROTIVITI JOINS CATALYST CEO CHAMPIONS FOR CHANGE. MENLO PARK, CALIF., APRIL 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI HAS JOINED THE CATALYST CEO CHAMPIONS FOR CHANGE INITIATIVE WITH PRESIDENT AND CEO JOSEPH TARANTINO SIGNING A PLEDGE TO INCREASE THE REPRESENTATION OF WOMEN, PARTICULARLY WOMEN OF COLOR, IN SENIOR LEVEL POSITIONS WITHIN THE FIRM.
