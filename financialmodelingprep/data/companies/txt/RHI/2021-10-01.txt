PROTIVITI'S 'BIRDIES FOR MEALS' CAMPAIGN WITH PROFESSIONAL GOLFERS MATT FITZPATRICK AND JENNIFER KUPCHO PROVIDES 151,500 MEALS FOR THE HUNGRY. MENLO PARK, CALIF., OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS A RESULT OF THE 2021 'BIRDIES FOR MEALS' CAMPAIGN, GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI IS DONATING 151,500 MEALS TO PEOPLE IN NEED.
