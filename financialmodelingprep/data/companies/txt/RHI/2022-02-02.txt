ROBERT HALF NAMED TO FORTUNE'S "MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES" LIST FOR 25TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. MENLO PARK, CALIF., FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF (NYSE: RHI) HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE FORTUNE "WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES®" LIST AND IS THE ONLY STAFFING FIRM INCLUDED FOR 2022.
