PROTIVITI LAUNCHES 2022 'BIRDIES FOR MEALS' CAMPAIGN WITH PROFESSIONAL GOLFERS MATT FITZPATRICK AND JENNIFER KUPCHO TO FIGHT HUNGER. PROTIVITI WILL DONATE 1,000 MEALS FOR EVERY BIRDIE MADE BY ITS BRAND AMBASSADORS FITZPATRICK AND KUPCHO FOR 2022 MAJOR CHAMPIONSHIPS MENLO PARK, CALIF. , MARCH 31, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI WILL AIM TO DONATE OVER 150,000 MEALS TO U.S. HUNGER, A NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION, BASED ON THE TOTAL NUMBER OF BIRDIES MADE BY GOLF PROS MATT FITZPATRICK AND JENNIFER KUPCHO DURING THE 2022 MAJOR CHAMPIONSHIPS.
