ROBERT HALF NAMED TO 2023 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX. MENLO PARK, CALIF. , JAN. 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF (NYSE: RHI), INCLUDING ITS SUBSIDIARY PROTIVITI, HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI).
