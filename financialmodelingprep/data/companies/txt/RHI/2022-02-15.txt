ROBERT HALF EXECUTIVES NAMED TO STAFFING INDUSTRY ANALYSTS' 2022 NORTH AMERICA STAFFING 100. MENLO PARK, CALIF., FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TWO EXECUTIVES FROM GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF (NYSE: RHI) HAVE BEEN NAMED TO STAFFING INDUSTRY ANALYSTS' 2022 NORTH AMERICA STAFFING 100 LIST.
