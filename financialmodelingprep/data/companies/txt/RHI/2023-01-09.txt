UNITED NATIONS EXECUTIVE SECRETARY TALKS WITH PROTIVITI: 'WE NEED PRIVATE SECTOR ENGAGEMENT, INVESTMENT TO SOLVE ENVIRONMENTAL CRISES'. IBRAHIM THIAW, EXECUTIVE SECRETARY OF THE UNITED NATIONS CONVENTION TO COMBAT DESERTIFICATION, ON CLIMATE CHANGE, BIODIVERSITY AND PRODUCTIVE LAND LOSS REQUIRING IMMEDIATE ACTION, FEATURED IN EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW WITH 'VISION BY PROTIVITI' MENLO PARK, CALIF. , JAN. 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN AN INTERVIEW WITH VISION BY PROTIVITI , THE UNITED NATIONS' (UN) EXECUTIVE SECRETARY OF THE UN CONVENTION TO COMBAT DESERTIFICATION CALLS ON GLOBAL BUSINESS LEADERS TO TAKE AN ACTIVE ROLE IN HELPING TO SOLVE THE PLANET'S BIGGEST PROBLEMS.
