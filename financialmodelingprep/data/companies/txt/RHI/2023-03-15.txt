ROBERT HALF NAMES RYAN SUTTON EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR FOR TECHNOLOGY PRACTICE GROUP. MENLO PARK, CALIF. , MARCH 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RYAN SUTTON, A 24-YEAR VETERAN OF GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF THE COMPANY'S TECHNOLOGY PRACTICE GROUP WHICH CONNECTS EMPLOYERS WITH HIGHLY SKILLED TECHNOLOGY PROFESSIONALS TO MEET THEIR IT HIRING NEEDS.
