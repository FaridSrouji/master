ROBERT HALF RAISES $300,000 FOR NONPROFITS DURING ANNUAL SUIT DRIVE. MENLO PARK, CALIF. , NOV. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF RECENTLY CONCLUDED ITS 20TH ANNUAL SUIT DRIVE, RAISING $300,000 FOR NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS FOCUSED ON EDUCATION AND EMPLOYMENT, AND PROVIDING SUPPORT FOR THOUSANDS OF PROFESSIONALS ENTERING OR RE-ENTERING THE WORKFORCE.
