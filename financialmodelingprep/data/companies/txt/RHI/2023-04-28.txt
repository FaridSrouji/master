ROBERT HALF NAMED TO FORBES' LIST OF AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS FOR DIVERSITY 2023. MENLO PARK, CALIF. , APRIL 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF (NYSE: RHI), INCLUDING ITS SUBSIDIARY PROTIVITI, HAS BEEN NAMED TO FORBES' LIST OF AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS FOR DIVERSITY FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
