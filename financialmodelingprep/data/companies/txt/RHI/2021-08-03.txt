MENLO PARK, CALIF., AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI HAS LAUNCHED AN ENHANCED RANSOMWARE OFFERING WITHIN THE FIRM'S BROADER CYBERSECURITY AND PRIVACY PRACTICE TO HELP COMPANIES MANAGE THE RISING THREAT LEVELS TO THEIR BUSINESS FROM MALICIOUS ACTORS ATTACKING AND DISRUPTING MISSION-CRITICAL OPERATIONS.
