PROTIVITI ACQUIRES R2I TO EXPAND DIGITAL SERVICES. COMBINATION OF PROTIVITI'S DIGITAL SOLUTION AND R2I BRINGS ENHANCED MARTECH SERVICES TO THE MARKET, FURTHER ENABLING CAPABILITIES TO DELIVER DIGITAL STRATEGY THROUGH TO EXECUTION FOR CLIENTS MENLO PARK, CALIF. , DEC. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI HAS ACQUIRED DIGITAL EXPERIENCE AGENCY R2INTEGRATED (R2I) TO GROW ITS DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION AND CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE OFFERING TO CLIENTS.
