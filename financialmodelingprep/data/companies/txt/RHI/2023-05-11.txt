ROBERT HALF NAMED TO FORTUNE'S MODERN BOARD 25. MENLO PARK, CALIF. , MAY 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF (NYSE: RHI), INCLUDING ITS SUBSIDIARY PROTIVITI, HAS BEEN SELECTED FOR FORTUNE'S MODERN BOARD 25 WHICH HIGHLIGHTS THE MOST INNOVATIVE BOARDS OF DIRECTORS AMONG S&P 500 COMPANIES.
