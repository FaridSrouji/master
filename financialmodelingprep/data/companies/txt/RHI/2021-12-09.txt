GLOBAL LEADERS EXPECT CHALLENGES OF RETAINING, ATTRACTING AND DEVELOPING TOP TALENT WILL PERSIST OVER NEXT DECADE, PROTIVITI-NC STATE UNIVERSITY SURVEY FINDS. MENLO PARK, CALIF., DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ACCORDING TO A NEW SURVEY FROM PROTIVITI AND NC STATE UNIVERSITY, PANDEMIC-RELATED REGULATION AND MARKET CONDITIONS DUE TO COVID-19 REMAIN TOP CONCERNS FOR 2022 FOR BOARD MEMBERS AND C-SUITE EXECUTIVES.
