PROTIVITI CEO JOSEPH TARANTINO AND SENIOR MANAGING DIRECTOR ROBERT HIRTH NAMED TO THE 2021 NACD DIRECTORSHIP 100 LIST. MENLO PARK, CALIF., SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOSEPH TARANTINO, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI, AND ROBERT HIRTH, SENIOR MANAGING DIRECTOR, PROTIVITI, HAVE BEEN NAMED TO THE NACD (NATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF CORPORATE DIRECTORS) 2021 DIRECTORSHIP 100™ LIST.
