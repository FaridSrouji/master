ROBERT HALF PROMOTES JAMES JOHNSON TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER. MENLO PARK, CALIF., JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PROMOTION OF JAMES JOHNSON TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER.

MENLO PARK, CALIF. , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KATHERINE SPENCER LEE, A 27-YEAR VETERAN OF GLOBAL TALENT SOLUTIONS AND BUSINESS CONSULTING FIRM ROBERT HALF, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER.
