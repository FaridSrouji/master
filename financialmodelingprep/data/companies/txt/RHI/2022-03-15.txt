NEARLY THREE-QUARTERS OF INTERNAL AUDIT LEADERS REPORT THEY ARE WORKING ON TRANSFORMATION INITIATIVES, ACCORDING TO NEW PROTIVITI SURVEY. OVER HALF OF INTERNAL AUDIT LEADERS REPORT STRONG ROI FROM EFFORTS TO IMPLEMENT NEXT-GENERATION CONCEPTS IN TALENT, PROCESSES AND TECHNOLOGIES MENLO PARK, CALIF. , MARCH 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ACCORDING TO PROTIVITI'S 2022 NEXT-GENERATION INTERNAL AUDIT SURVEY, NEARLY THREE OUT OF FOUR (74%) CHIEF AUDIT EXECUTIVES (CAES) AND SENIOR INTERNAL AUDIT LEADERS REPORT THAT THEIR ORGANIZATIONS HAVE COMPLETED OR ARE CURRENTLY UNDERTAKING INITIATIVES TO TRANSFORM AND INNOVATE THEIR INTERNAL AUDIT FUNCTION.
