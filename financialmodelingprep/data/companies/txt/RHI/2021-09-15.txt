CFOS AND FINANCE LEADERS FACE GROWING RESPONSIBILITIES, INCLUDING ESG REPORTING, ACCORDING TO NEW PROTIVITI SURVEY. MENLO PARK, CALIF., SEPT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE 2021 FINANCE TRENDS SURVEY, CONDUCTED BY GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI, REVEALS THAT CFOS AND VPS OF FINANCE ARE BEING LOOKED TO FOR ADDITIONAL ACTIONS AND DATA THAT, UNTIL RECENTLY, WERE CONSIDERED OUTSIDE THEIR PURVIEW.
