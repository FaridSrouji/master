PROTIVITI CEO JOSEPH TARANTINO AND MANAGING DIRECTOR FRANK KURRE NAMED TO THE 2022 NACD 'DIRECTORSHIP 100' LIST. EVELYN DILSAVER, MEMBER OF MULTIPLE BOARDS AND PROTIVITI ADVISORY BOARD MEMBER, HONORED ON DIRECTORS LIST MENLO PARK, CALIF. , JUNE 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOSEPH TARANTINO, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI, AND FRANK KURRE, MANAGING DIRECTOR AND GLOBAL ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT LEADER WITH PROTIVITI, HAVE BEEN NAMED TO THE NACD (NATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF CORPORATE DIRECTORS) 2022 DIRECTORSHIP 100™ LIST, IN THE GOVERNANCE PROFESSIONALS AND INSTITUTIONS CATEGORY.
