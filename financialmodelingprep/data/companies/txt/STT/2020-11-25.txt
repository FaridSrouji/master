STATE STREET ANNOUNCES CHAIRMAN & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER TO PARTICIPATE IN THE GOLDMAN SACHS 2020 US FINANCIAL SERVICES CONFERENCE. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS CHAIRMAN & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, RON O'HANLEY, AND ITS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, ERIC ABOAF, WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE GOLDMAN SACHS 2020 US FINANCIAL SERVICES CONFERENCE, HELD VIRTUALLY, ON WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 9, 2020, AT 11:20 AM EST.
