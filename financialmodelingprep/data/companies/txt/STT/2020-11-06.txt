STATE STREET ANNOUNCES CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER TO PARTICIPATE IN CITI'S 2020 FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, ERIC ABOAF, AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, LOU MAIURI, WILL PARTICIPATE IN CITI'S 2020 FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 18, 2020, AT 12:45 PM EST. THE WEBCAST OF THE EVENT AND PRESENTATION MATERIALS WILL BE ACCESSIBLE ON STATE STREET'S INVESTOR RELATIONS HOME PAGE AT INVESTORS.STATESTREET.COM.
