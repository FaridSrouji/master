STATE STREET CORPORATION DECLARES DIVIDENDS ON ITS NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK SERIES “D”, “F”, “G” AND “H”. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED A CASH DIVIDEND ON EACH OF THE BELOW OUTSTANDING SERIES OF NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK: SERIES D (REPRESENTED BY DEPOSITARY SHARES, EACH REPRESENTING A 1/4000TH INTEREST IN A SHARE OF SERIES D PREFERRED STOCK).
