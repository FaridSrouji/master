STATE STREET AND FUNDGUARD ANNOUNCE STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO DELIVER ACCOUNTING SOLUTIONS FOR STATE STREET ALPHA. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET (NYSE:STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FUNDGUARD HAS BEEN SELECTED TO SERVE AS A STRATEGIC ACCOUNTING SOLUTION FOR STATE STREET ALPHA. FUNDGUARD IS A HIGHLY SCALABLE CLOUD-NATIVE INVESTMENT ACCOUNTING, FUND ACCOUNTING, AND CONTINGENCY NAV SOLUTION. THE FUNDGUARD ACCOUNTING SOLUTION WILL BE INTEGRATED FOR STRAIGHT THROUGH PROCESSING FROM PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT AND TRADING IN THE CHARLES RIVER INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT SOLUTION THROUGH TO CUSTODY AND ADMINISTRATION.
