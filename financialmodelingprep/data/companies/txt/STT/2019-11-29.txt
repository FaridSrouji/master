STATE STREET APPOINTS CARA BROSNAN AS HEAD FOR SECTOR SOLUTIONS. STATE STREET HAS ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS NAMED A HEAD FOR SECTOR SOLUTIONS. THE FIRM HAS APPOINTED CARA BROSNAN TO THE NEWLY CREATED ROLE FOR AUSTRALIA AND SOUTHEAST ASIA. BROSNAN IS TO BE BASED IN SYDNEY AND IS TO LEAD THE FIRM'S SALES ACROSS ALL OF ITS PRODUCTS AND CLIENT SECTORS. SHE WAS PREVIOUSLY BASED IN HONG KONG.
