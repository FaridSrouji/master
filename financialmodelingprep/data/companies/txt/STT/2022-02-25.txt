EFFECTIVE ON OR ABOUT FRIDAY, APRIL 22, 2022, THE SPDR MSCI ACWI LOW CARBON TARGET ETF WILL UNDERGO CHANGES TO ITS FUND NAME, TICKER, BENCHMARK, LISTING EXCHANGE AND WILL UNDERGO A 4:1 STOCK SPLIT.
