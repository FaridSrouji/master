STATE STREET, PRICESTATS TO PROVIDE ONLINE FOOD INFLATION DATA TO POLICYMAKERS, THE PUBLIC AND EXTEND PROVISION OF COMPREHENSIVE INFLATION DATA TO CENTRAL BANKS. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL BEGIN PROVIDING THE PRICESTATS GLOBAL FOOD INFLATION SERIES TO THE PUBLIC, ON A COMPLIMENTARY BASIS, TO HELP POLICYMAKERS GRAPPLE WITH RAPIDLY RISING FOOD PRICES AND SUPPLY SHORTAGES DRIVEN BY THE WAR IN UKRAINE. STATE STREET ALSO ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL CONTINUE TO OFFER THE FULL SUITE OF STATE STREET PRICESTATS SERIES, A COMPREHENSIVE SUITE OF INDICATORS TO MONITOR TRENDS IN RETAILER PRICING AND CONSUMER
