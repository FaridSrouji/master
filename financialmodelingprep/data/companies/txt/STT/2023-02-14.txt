STATE STREET CORPORATION DECLARES FIRST-QUARTER DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE: STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.63 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON APRIL 13, 2023 TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 3, 2023. ABOUT STATE STREET CORPORATION STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE: STT) IS ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDERS OF FINANCIAL SERVICES TO INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS INCLUDING INVESTMENT SERVICING, INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT AND INVESTMENT RESEARCH AND TRADING.
