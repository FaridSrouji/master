STATE STREET REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER 2020 RESULTS. STATE STREET REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER 2020 EPS OF $1.62, UP 37% YEAR-ON-YEAR.QTRLY NET INTEREST INCOME $664 MILLION VERSUS $673 MILLION.QTRLY TOTAL REVENUE $3.07 BILLION VERSUS $2.93 BILLION.QTRLY TOTAL FEE REVENUE $2,399 MILLION VERSUS $2,260 MILLION.QTRLY TOTAL EXPENSES $2.26 BILLION VERSUS $2.29 BILLION.QUARTER- END AUM $2.69 TRILLION VERSUS $3.12 TRILLION IN Q4.SAYS Q1 RESULTS WERE 'SOMEWHAT' IMPACTED BY COVID-19 PANDEMIC.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.35, REVENUE VIEW $2.90 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.STATE STREET SAYS MUST BE CAUTIOUS ABOUT PACE AND TIMING OF ECONOMIC RECOVERY.QUARTER-END ASSETS UNDER CUSTODY $31.9 TRILLION VERSUS $34.4 TRILLION IN Q4.
