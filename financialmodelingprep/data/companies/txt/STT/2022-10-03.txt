STATE STREET TO PARTICIPATE IN BANCANALYSTS ASSOCIATION OF BOSTON CONFERENCE. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE: STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS VICE CHAIRMAN & CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, ERIC ABOAF, WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE BANCANALYSTS ASSOCIATION OF BOSTON CONFERENCE IN BOSTON ON THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 3, 2022, AT 12:45 PM ET. A WEBCAST OF THE EVENT WILL BE ACCESSIBLE ON STATE STREET'S INVESTOR RELATIONS HOME PAGE AT INVESTORS.STATESTREET.COM.
