STATE STREET ANNOUNCES CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER TO PARTICIPATE IN CREDIT SUISSE'S 23RD ANNUAL FINANCIAL SERVICES FORUM. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE: STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, ERIC ABOAF, WILL PARTICIPATE IN CREDIT SUISSE'S 23RD ANNUAL FINANCIAL SERVICES FORUM IN MIAMI, FLORIDA ON FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 18, 2022, AT 11:20 AM ET. AN AUDIO WEBCAST WILL BE ACCESSIBLE ON THE HOME PAGE OF STATE STREET'S INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE, INVESTORS.STATESTREET.COM.
