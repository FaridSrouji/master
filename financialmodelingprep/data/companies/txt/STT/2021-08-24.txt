STATE STREET ANNOUNCES THAT COLLATERAL+ IS NOW CONNECTED TO DTCC'S MARGIN TRANSIT UTILITY (MTU). BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS COLLATERAL + BUSINESS IS OPERATIONAL WITH DTCC'S MARGIN TRANSIT UTILITY (MTU), WHICH HAS BEEN DESIGNED FOR SIMPLIFYING THE TRANSFER OF COLLATERAL AMONG MARKET PARTICIPANTS. BY LEVERAGING MTU, STATE STREET'S COLLATERAL+ BUSINESS WILL NOW BE ABLE TO MORE EFFICIENTLY VALIDATE, ENRICH, SETTLE, REPORT AND MONITOR THIRD PARTY COLLATERAL WHILE EASILY CONNECTING TO AND SHARING INFORMATION WITH MULTIPLE COUNTERPARTIES
