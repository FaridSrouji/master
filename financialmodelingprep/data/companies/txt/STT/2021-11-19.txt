STATE STREET HIRES VETERAN-OWNED FIRMS TO UNDERWRITE 2027 SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE: STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WORKED WITH VETERAN-OWNED FIRMS ACTING AS BOOKRUNNERS AND CO-MANAGERS TO SYNDICATE A $500 MILLION SENIOR NOTE ISSUANCE. THE OFFERING, WHICH CLOSED YESTERDAY, REINFORCES THE FIRM'S COMMITMENT TO VETERANS AND THEIR FAMILIES AND OVERALL REPRESENTATION IN THE CAPITAL MARKETS AND BROADER FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY.
