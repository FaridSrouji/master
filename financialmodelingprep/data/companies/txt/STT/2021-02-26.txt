STATE STREET ANNOUNCES CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER TO PARTICIPATE IN THE RBC CAPITAL MARKETS GLOBAL FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS CONFERENCE. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, ERIC ABOAF, WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE RBC CAPITAL MARKETS GLOBAL FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, MARCH 9, 2021 AT 2:00 PM EST. THE WEBCAST OF THE EVENT WILL BE ACCESSIBLE ON STATE STREET'S INVESTOR RELATIONS HOME PAGE AT INVESTORS.STATESTREET.COM.
