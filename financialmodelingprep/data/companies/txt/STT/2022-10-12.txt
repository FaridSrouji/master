STATE STREET DIGITAL'S TRADENEXUS PLATFORM PARTNERS WITH CAPITOLIS TO LAUNCH INTEGRATED PORTFOLIO OPTIMIZATION OFFERING. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE: STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TRADENEXUS, A GLOBALLINK DIGITAL PLATFORM FOR FOREIGN EXCHANGE TRANSACTIONS AND AN INTEGRAL PART OF STATE STREET'S DIGITAL DIVISION, HAS PARTNERED WITH CAPITOLIS, THE TECHNOLOGY COMPANY REIMAGINING CAPITAL MARKETS, TO LAUNCH AN AUTOMATED POST-TRADE SOLUTION TO OPTIMIZE PORTFOLIOS.
