STATE STREET CORPORATION DECLARES DIVIDENDS ON ITS NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK SERIES “D”, “F”, “G” AND “H”. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED A CASH DIVIDEND ON EACH OF THE BELOW OUTSTANDING SERIES OF NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK: SERIES D (REPRESENTED BY DEPOSITARY SHARES, EACH REPRESENTING A 1/4000TH INTEREST IN A SHARE OF SERIES D PREFERRED STOCK). THE CASH DIVIDEND IS IN THE AMOUNT OF $1,475.00 PER SHARE OF SERIES D PREFERRED STOCK (RESULTING IN A DISTRIBUTION OF APPROXIMATELY $0.3687 PER DEPOSITARY SHARE) AND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2021
STATE STREET ANNOUNCES FIRST TRANSACTION UNDER PEER-TO-PEER REPO PROGRAM. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FIRST TRANSACTION WITHIN ITS PEER-TO-PEER REPO PROGRAM FOLLOWING ITS LAUNCH EARLIER THIS YEAR. THE TRADE WAS EXECUTED BETWEEN A LARGE ASSET OWNER AND A NON-TRADITIONAL INVESTMENT MANAGER. THE PEER TO PEER REPO PROGRAM SEEKS TO FACILITATE OVERNIGHT AND TERM REPO TRADING BETWEEN BUY-SIDE COUNTERPARTIES, INCLUDING TRADITIONAL AND NON-TRADITIONAL ASSET MANAGERS, ASSET OWNERS, INSURERS, CORPORATES, REITS, AND SOVEREIGN
