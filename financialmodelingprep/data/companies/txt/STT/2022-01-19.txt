STATE STREET ANNOUNCES PLANNED RETIREMENT OF CYRUS TARAPOREVALA. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CYRUS TARAPOREVALA, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF STATE STREET GLOBAL ADVISORS, WILL RETIRE IN 2022. TARAPOREVALA WILL REMAIN IN HIS ROLE THROUGH THE COMPLETION OF THE SEARCH FOR HIS SUCCESSOR AND TRANSITION PROCESS TO ENSURE A SEAMLESS SUCCESSION WHICH WE ANTICIPATE HAPPENING IN THE SECOND HALF OF THIS YEAR.
