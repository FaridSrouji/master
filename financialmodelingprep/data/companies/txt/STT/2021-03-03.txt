STATE STREET INTRODUCES NEW PEER-TO-PEER REPO FINANCING MARKETPLACE FOR THE BUY-SIDE. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF A NEW PEER-TO-PEER REPO PROGRAM FOR THE BUY-SIDE. BUILDING FROM ITS MARKET-LEADING POSITION IN SPONSORED REPO AND SECURITIES LENDING, INCLUDING PEER-TO-PEER OPPORTUNITIES WITHIN ITS AGENCY LENDING PROGRAM, PEER-TO-PEER REPO ENABLES COMPETITIVE FINANCING COSTS ACROSS A BROADER RANGE OF COLLATERAL TYPES AND YIELD ENHANCEMENT OPPORTUNITIES COMPARED TO TRADITIONAL REPO MARKETS.
