STATE STREET ELECTS DONNALEE DEMAIO TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF DONNALEE (DONNA) DEMAIO TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MS. DEMAIO, AGE 63, WAS PREVIOUSLY EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND GLOBAL CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER FOR THE GENERAL INSURANCE DIVISION OF AMERICAN INTERNATIONAL GROUP (AIG).
