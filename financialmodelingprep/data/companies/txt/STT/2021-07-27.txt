STATE STREET ANNOUNCES RESOLUTION PLAN FOR 2021. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS FILED ITS 2021 RESOLUTION PLAN. STATE STREET, ALONG WITH OTHER BANKING INSTITUTIONS, PERIODICALLY PREPARES AND FILES ITS RESOLUTION PLAN, COMMONLY REFERRED TO AS A “LIVING WILL,” WITH THE BOARD OF GOVERNORS OF THE FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM AND THE FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION.
