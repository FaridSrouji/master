YIE-HSIN HUNG NAMED PRESIDENT & CEO OF STATE STREET GLOBAL ADVISORS. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE: STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS APPOINTED YIE-HSIN HUNG AS PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER (CEO) OF STATE STREET GLOBAL ADVISORS (GLOBAL ADVISORS), ITS ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS. HUNG SUCCEEDS CYRUS TARAPOREVALA, WHOSE PLANNED RETIREMENT WAS ANNOUNCED EARLIER THIS YEAR.
