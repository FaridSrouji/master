OUTDEFINE RAISES $2.5 MILLION TO BUILD A DECENTRALIZED TALENT COMMUNITY. SAN FRANCISCO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--OUTDEFINE, A SAN FRANCISCO-BASED STARTUP, ANNOUNCED ITS $2.5 MILLION FUNDRAISE TO BUILD A DECENTRALIZED HIRING COMMUNITY FOR WEB3 TALENT. THE ROUND WAS LED BY WEB3 INVESTORS TCG CRYPTO AND JUMP CRYPTO, WITH PARTICIPATION FROM BIG BRAIN HOLDINGS, FORMLESS CAPITAL, BLOCORE, MASK ECO FUND, CRYPTO ANGELS, AND SYNDICATES.
