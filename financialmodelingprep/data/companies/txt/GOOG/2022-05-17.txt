THE BANK IS MOVING FROM AN ON-PREMISE IMPLEMENTATION TO USING TEMENOS CORE BANKING SOLUTIONS ON GOOGLE CLOUD TO TAP NEW GROWTH OPPORTUNITIES AND DRIVE EFFICIENCIES AT SCALE.
