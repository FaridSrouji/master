BUNGE ANNOUNCES REPORTING SEGMENT CHANGES. ST. LOUIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BUNGE LIMITED (NYSE:BG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS CHANGED ITS SEGMENT REPORTING TO ALIGN WITH THE COMPANY'S NEW VALUE CHAIN OPERATING STRUCTURE. BEGINNING WITH THE FIRST QUARTER 2021, BUNGE WILL REPORT CORE SEGMENT RESULTS AS FOLLOWS: AGRIBUSINESS, WHICH INCLUDES PROCESSING AND MERCHANDISING; REFINED AND SPECIALTY OILS; AND MILLING. BUNGE WILL ALSO CONTINUE TO REPORT CORPORATE AND OTHER RESULTS AND NON-CORE SEGMENT RESULTS, WHICH IS MADE UP OF SUGAR & BIOENERGY
