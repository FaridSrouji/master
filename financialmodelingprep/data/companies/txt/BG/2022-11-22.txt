BZ GROUP AND BUNGE ENTER INTO STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP. ST. LOUIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--BZ GROUP AND BUNGE (NYSE: BG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP. AS PART OF THIS AGREEMENT, BUNGE ACQUIRES 49% OF BZ GROUP WHILE THE BEUZELIN FAMILY REMAINS THE MAJORITY SHAREHOLDER WITH 51% OWNERSHIP. THE TRANSACTION IS SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS AND REGULATORY APPROVAL.
