UPL'S ADVANTA SEEDS AND BUNGE ANNOUNCE INTENTION TO ACQUIRE A COMBINED 40% STAKE IN SEEDCORP|HO. LONDON , NOV. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UPL LTD. (NSE: UPL & BSE: 512070 LSE: UPLL) ('UPL'), A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURAL SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS COMPANY, ADVANTA SEEDS UK, AND BUNGE (NYSE: BG) HAVE SIGNED AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE A 20% STAKE EACH IN SEEDCORP|HO.
