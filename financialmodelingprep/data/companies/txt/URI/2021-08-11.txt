UNITED RENTALS NAMES LARRY DE SHON TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT LARRY DE SHON HAS JOINED THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. HIS APPOINTMENT EXPANDS THE COMPOSITION OF THE BOARD TO 12 MEMBERS, OF WHICH 10 ARE INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS. MICHAEL KNEELAND, CHAIRMAN OF UNITED RENTALS, SAID, “LARRY DE SHON IS A STRONG ADDITION TO OUR BOARD. HE'S AN INNOVATIVE THINKER WHOSE CREDENTIALS ARE DIRECTLY RELEVANT TO UNITED RENTALS' STRATEGY AND BUSINESS MODEL — CRIT
