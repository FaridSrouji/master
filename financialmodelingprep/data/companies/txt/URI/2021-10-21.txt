UNITED RENTALS RELEASES NINTH CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY REPORT. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI), THE WORLD'S LARGEST EQUIPMENT RENTAL COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS NINTH CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY REPORT DOCUMENTING ITS PROGRESS IN 2020 AND MOMENTUM INTO 2021. THE REPORT COVERS ALL MAJOR ASPECTS OF THE COMPANY'S CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY FRAMEWORK, INCLUDING ITS COMMITMENT TO REDUCE THE INTENSITY OF ITS GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS BY 35% BY 2030.
