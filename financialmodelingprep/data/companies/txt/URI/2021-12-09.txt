UNITED RENTALS NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES BY NEWSWEEK. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI) ANNOUNCED IT WAS NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES 2022 BY NEWSWEEK. UNITED RENTALS HAS BEEN SELECTED THREE CONSECUTIVE YEARS FOR THIS DISTINGUISHED LIST WHICH WAS FOUNDED IN 2020 AND ACKNOWLEDGES THE TOP 500 MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES IN THE UNITED STATES.
