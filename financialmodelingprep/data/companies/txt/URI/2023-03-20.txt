UNITED RENTALS DETAILS ADVANTAGES OF BUYING USED EQUIPMENT AHEAD OF BUSY CONSTRUCTION AND PLANT MAINTENANCE SEASON. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI), THE WORLD'S LARGEST EQUIPMENT RENTAL COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS 2023 SPRING USED EQUIPMENT SALE EVENT WILL TAKE PLACE AT 176 UNITED RENTALS LOCATIONS IN THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA ON THURSDAY, MARCH 23. DURING THE EVENT, COMPANIES AND GOVERNMENT ORGANIZATIONS CAN REVIEW A ROBUST FLEET OF WELL-MAINTAINED USED EQUIPMENT ORIGINALLY SOURCED FROM TOP-TIER MANUFACTURERS.
