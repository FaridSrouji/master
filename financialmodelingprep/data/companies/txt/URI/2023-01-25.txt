UNITED RENTALS ANNOUNCES RECORD FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR1 2022 RESULTS, INTRODUCES 2023 OUTLOOK, DIVIDEND PROGRAM AND RESTART OF SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM. STAMFORD, CONN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI) TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022 AND REPORTED ITS FULL-YEAR RESULTS ON FORM 10-K. THE COMPANY ALSO ANNOUNCED ITS FULL-YEAR 2023 GUIDANCE, THE INITIATION OF A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.48 PER SHARE, EQUATING TO AN INITIAL ANNUALIZED YIELD OF APPROXIMATELY 1.5%, AND ITS PLAN TO RESTART ITS SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM, WITH THE INTENT TO BUYBACK $1.0 BILLION OF COMMON STOCK IN 2023.
