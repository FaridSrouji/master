UNITED RENTALS NAMED TO JUST 100 LIST OF TOP PERFORMING COMPANIES. STAMFORD, CONN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI), THE WORLD'S LARGEST EQUIPMENT RENTAL COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WAS NAMED TO THE 2023 JUST 100, A RANKING OF AMERICA'S LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANIES ON ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE (ESG) ISSUES. JUST 100 IS A PREEMINENT LIST OF COMPANIES THAT ARE DOING RIGHT BY ALL THEIR STAKEHOLDERS – EMPLOYEES, CUSTOMERS, COMMUNITIES AND THE ENVIRONMENT – WHILE ALSO GENERATING STRONG PERFORMANCE FOR SHAREHOLDERS.
