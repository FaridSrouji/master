UNITED RENTALS RECOGNIZED FOR ITS COMMITMENT TO HIRING AND RETAINING VETERANS. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI) ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED AWARDS AS A LEADING EMPLOYER OF MILITARY VETERANS FROM FIVE ORGANIZATIONS, IN RECOGNITION OF THE COMPANY'S ACHIEVEMENTS IN HIRING VETERANS AND SUPPORTING THEIR CAREERS. UNITED RENTALS HAS BEEN AWARDED A HIRE VETS GOLD MEDALLION BY THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF LABOR FOR THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO VETERAN HIRING, RETENTION, AND PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT.
