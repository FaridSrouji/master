UNITED RENTALS INVESTS IN FORD ELECTRIC TRUCKS FOR NORTH AMERICAN FLEET. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI), THE WORLD'S LARGEST EQUIPMENT RENTAL COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW AGREEMENT WITH FORD PRO TO PURCHASE ALL-ELECTRIC VEHICLES FOR ITS NORTH AMERICAN RENTAL AND COMPANY FLEETS. THE AGREEMENT INITIALLY INCLUDES ORDERS FOR 500 F-150 LIGHTNING TRUCKS AND 30 E-TRANSIT VANS, WITH 120 TRUCKS AND ALL 30 VANS TO BE DELIVERED IN 2022.
