UNITED RENTALS SURPASSES 335,000 TELEMATICS-ENABLED ASSETS. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI), THE WORLD'S LARGEST EQUIPMENT RENTAL COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SURPASSED 335,000 PIECES OF EQUIPMENT OUTFITTED WITH TELEMATICS TECHNOLOGY IN ITS RENTAL FLEET. THE EQUIPMENT INCLUDES AERIAL WORK PLATFORMS, EARTHMOVING EQUIPMENT, GENERATORS, ALL-ELECTRIC TRUCKS, COMPRESSORS, E-DUMPSTERS, LIGHT TOWERS AND MORE. “BUSINESSES FACE EVER-INCREASING PRESSURE TO IMPROVE PRODUCTIVITY AND SAFETY ON JOBSITES.
