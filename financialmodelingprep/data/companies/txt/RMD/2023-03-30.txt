RESMED CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER AND GLOBAL GENERAL COUNSEL TO RETIRE. SAN DIEGO, MARCH 30, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RESMED (NYSE: RMD, ASX: RMD) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DAVID PENDARVIS, CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER, GLOBAL GENERAL COUNSEL, AND SECRETARY, PLANS TO RETIRE, EFFECTIVE JUNE 30, 2023. HE WILL REMAIN IN A CONSULTING ROLE WITH RESMED THROUGH DECEMBER 31, 2023.
