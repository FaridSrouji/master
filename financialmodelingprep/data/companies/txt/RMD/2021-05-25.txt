RESMED ANNOUNCES PARTICIPATION IN THE WILLIAM BLAIR 41ST ANNUAL GROWTH STOCK CONFERENCE. SAN DIEGO, MAY 25, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RESMED (NYSE: RMD, ASX: RMD) TODAY ANNOUNCED ROB DOUGLAS, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A VIRTUAL FIRESIDE CHAT AT THE WILLIAM BLAIR 41ST ANNUAL GROWTH STOCK CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, JUNE 1, 2021, BEGINNING AT APPROXIMATELY 1:20 P.M. (CENTRAL DAYLIGHT TIME) VIA VIDEO WEBCAST.
