RESMED ELECTS HEALTH TECH LEADERS DESNEY TAN AND JOHN HERNANDEZ TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. SAN DIEGO, NOV. 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RESMED (NYSE: RMD, ASX: RMD) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ELECTED DESNEY TAN AND JOHN HERNANDEZ, BOTH DIGITAL HEALTH LEADERS AT WORLD-LEADING TECH COMPANIES, TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, DURING ITS ANNUAL STOCKHOLDERS' MEETING.
