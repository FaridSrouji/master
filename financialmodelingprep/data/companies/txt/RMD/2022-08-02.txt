RESMED ACQUIRES LEIPZIG-BASED MEMENTOR WITH INSOMNIA DIGITAL HEALTH SOLUTION SOMNIO. MARTINSRIED, GERMANY AND LEIPZIG, GERMANY, AUG. 02, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  RESMED (NYSE: RMD, ASX: RMD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF LEIPZIG-BASED COMPANY MEMENTOR; THE DEAL WAS COMPLETED MONDAY, AUGUST 1. WITH THIS ACQUISITION, RESMED STRENGTHENS ITS OVERALL SLEEP PORTFOLIO IN GERMANY WITH A DIGITAL SOLUTION FOR INSOMNIA.
