RESMED'S ANNUAL GLOBAL SLEEP SURVEY FINDS 8 IN 10 ADULTS EXPERIENCE SIGNS OF DISRUPTION RELATED TO THEIR QUALITY OF SLEEP. SAN DIEGO, MARCH 13, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RESMED (NYSE: RMD, ASX: RMD) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE RESULTS FROM ITS 2023 GLOBAL SLEEP SURVEY IN CONJUNCTION WITH NATIONAL SLEEP AWARENESS WEEK (MARCH 12-18) AND WORLD SLEEP DAY (MARCH 17) TO RAISE AWARENESS FOR THE IMPORTANCE OF SLEEP AS A KEY COMPONENT OF PHYSICAL AND MENTAL HEALTH.
