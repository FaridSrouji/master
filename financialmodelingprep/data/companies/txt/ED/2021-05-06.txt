CON EDISON REPORTS 2021 FIRST QUARTER EARNINGS. NEW YORK, MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) TODAY REPORTED 2021 FIRST QUARTER NET INCOME FOR COMMON STOCK OF $419 MILLION OR $1.23 A SHARE COMPARED WITH $375 MILLION OR $1.13 A SHARE IN THE 2020 FIRST QUARTER. ADJUSTED EARNINGS WERE $491 MILLION OR $1.44 A SHARE IN THE 2021 PERIOD COMPARED WITH $451 MILLION OR $1.35 A SHARE IN THE 2020 PERIOD.
