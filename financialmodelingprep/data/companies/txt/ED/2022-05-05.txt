CON EDISON REPORTS 2022 FIRST QUARTER EARNINGS. NEW YORK , MAY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) TODAY REPORTED 2022 FIRST QUARTER NET INCOME FOR COMMON STOCK OF $602 MILLION OR $1.70 A SHARE COMPARED WITH $419 MILLION OR $1.23 A SHARE IN THE 2021 FIRST QUARTER. ADJUSTED EARNINGS WERE $522 MILLION OR $1.47 A SHARE IN THE 2022 PERIOD COMPARED WITH $491 MILLION OR $1.44 A SHARE IN THE 2021 PERIOD.
