CON EDISON DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, APRIL 20, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 81 CENTS A SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 15, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 17, 2023. CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. IS ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST INVESTOR-OWNED ENERGY-DELIVERY COMPANIES, WITH APPROXIMATELY $16 BILLION IN ANNUAL REVENUES AND $69 BILLION IN ASSETS AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2022.
