CON EDISON REPORTS 2022 EARNINGS. NEW YORK , FEB. 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) TODAY REPORTED 2022 NET INCOME FOR COMMON STOCK OF $1,660 MILLION OR $4.68 A SHARE COMPARED WITH $1,346 MILLION OR $3.86 A SHARE IN 2021. ADJUSTED EARNINGS WERE $1,620 MILLION OR $4.57 A SHARE IN 2022 COMPARED WITH $1,528 MILLION OR $4.39 A SHARE IN 2021.
