NEW YORK , FEB. 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON'S SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF CUSTOMER ENERGY SOLUTIONS, VICKI KUO, AND VICE PRESIDENT OF MANHATTAN ELECTRIC OPERATIONS, LISA PRIMEGGIA, WILL SPEAK ON A PANEL HOSTED BY RBC CAPITAL MARKETS AND FOCUSING ON ELECTRIC VEHICLES AND THE GRID.
