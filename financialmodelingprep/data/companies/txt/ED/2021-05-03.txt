MIDTOWN BUILDING IS FIRST WITH VEHICLE CHARGERS UNDER CON EDISON'S POWER-READY PROGRAM. NEW YORK, MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A LARGE APARTMENT BUILDING IN MIDTOWN MANHATTAN HAS THE DISTINCTION OF BEING THE FIRST PROPERTY IN THE REGION TO INSTALL ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGERS UNDER CON EDISON'S POWERREADY INCENTIVE PROGRAM. BUILDING OWNER SOLOW BUILDING CO. PLACED THE CHARGERS IN A GARAGE AT 685 FIRST AVENUE WITH HELP FROM POWERREADY, WHICH PROVIDES INCENTIVES TO BRING ELECTRICAL SERVICE TO EV CHARGING STATIONS.
