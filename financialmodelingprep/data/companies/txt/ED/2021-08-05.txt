CON EDISON REPORTS 2021 SECOND QUARTER EARNINGS. NEW YORK, AUG. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) TODAY REPORTED 2021 SECOND QUARTER NET INCOME FOR COMMON STOCK OF $165 MILLION OR $0.48 A SHARE COMPARED WITH $190 MILLION OR $0.57 A SHARE IN THE 2020 SECOND QUARTER. ADJUSTED EARNINGS WERE $182 MILLION OR $0.53 A SHARE IN THE 2021 PERIOD COMPARED WITH $201 MILLION OR $0.60 A SHARE IN THE 2020 PERIOD.
