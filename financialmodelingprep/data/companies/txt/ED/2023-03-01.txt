CON EDISON FINALIZES SALE OF ITS CLEAN ENERGY BUSINESSES. NEW YORK , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. ("CON EDISON") (NYSE:ED) HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED THE SALE OF ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY, CON EDISON CLEAN ENERGY BUSINESSES, INC. (THE "CLEAN ENERGY BUSINESSES"), INCLUDING THE TRANSFER OF SUBSTANTIALLY ALL OF ITS ASSETS, TO RWE RENEWABLES AMERICAS, LLC.  THE TRANSACTION IS VALUED AT $6.8 BILLION.
