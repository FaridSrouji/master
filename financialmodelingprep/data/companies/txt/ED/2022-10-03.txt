CON EDISON ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO SELL RENEWABLE ENERGY SUBSIDIARIES. NEW YORK , OCT. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. ("CON EDISON") (NYSE: ED) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT ENTERED INTO A PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT PURSUANT TO WHICH CON EDISON AGREED TO SELL ITS INTEREST IN ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY, CON EDISON CLEAN ENERGY BUSINESSES, INC. AND ITS SUBSIDIARIES (THE "CLEAN ENERGY BUSINESSES") TO RWE RENEWABLES AMERICAS, LLC, A SUBSIDIARY OF RWE AKTIENGESELLSCHAFT ("RWE"). THE TRANSACTION IS VALUED AT $6.8 BILLION, SUBJECT TO CERTAIN CUSTOMARY ADJUSTMENTS.
