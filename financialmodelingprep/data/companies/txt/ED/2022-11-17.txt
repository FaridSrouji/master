CON EDISON WINS RELIABILITYONE® AWARDS FOR RELIABLE SERVICE, TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT. VETERAN EMPLOYEE IS NAMED COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING CONTRIBUTOR NEW YORK , NOV. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CON EDISON HAS EARNED AWARDS FROM PA CONSULTING FOR PROVIDING RELIABLE ELECTRIC SERVICE AND FOR ITS DEVELOPMENT OF NEW TECHNOLOGY. THE COMPANY WON THE 2022 RELIABILITYONE® AWARD FOR OUTSTANDING RELIABILITY PERFORMANCE IN THE NORTHEAST METROPOLITAN SERVICE AREA AND THE OUTSTANDING TECHNOLOGY & INNOVATION AWARD.
