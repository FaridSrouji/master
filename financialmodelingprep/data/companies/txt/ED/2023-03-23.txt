PRINCETON IDENTITY'S IRIS-ON-THE-MOVE SUPPORT'S CON EDISON'S COMPLIANCE WITH CIP STANDARDS FOR MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION. HAMILTON TOWNSHIP, N.J. , MARCH 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PRINCETON IDENTITY INC., PROVIDER OF THE FASTEST, SIMPLEST AND MOST SECURE BIOMETRIC SECURITY SYSTEMS ON THE MARKET, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT CONSOLIDATED EDISON INC (CON EDISON) HAS DEPLOYED PRINCETON IDENTITY'S (PI) IRIS-ON-THE-MOVE BIOMETRIC IDENTITY SOLUTION IN COMPLIANCE WITH THE FEDERAL ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION'S CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE PROTECTION (CIP) STANDARDS.
