CON EDISON DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. NEW YORK , APRIL 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 79 CENTS A SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 15, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 18, 2022. CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. IS ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST INVESTOR-OWNED ENERGY-DELIVERY COMPANIES, WITH APPROXIMATELY $14 BILLION IN ANNUAL REVENUES AND $63 BILLION IN ASSETS.
