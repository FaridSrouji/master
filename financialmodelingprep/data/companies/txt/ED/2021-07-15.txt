CON EDISON DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 77.5 CENTS A SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE SEPTEMBER 15, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUGUST 18, 2021. CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. IS ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST INVESTOR-OWNED ENERGY-DELIVERY COMPANIES, WITH APPROXIMATELY $12 BILLION IN ANNUAL REVENUES AND $62 BILLION IN ASSETS.
