CON EDISON REPORTS 2022 THIRD QUARTER EARNINGS. NEW YORK , NOV. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) TODAY REPORTED 2022 THIRD QUARTER NET INCOME FOR COMMON STOCK OF $613 MILLION OR $1.73 A SHARE COMPARED WITH $538 MILLION OR $1.52 A SHARE IN THE 2021 THIRD QUARTER. ADJUSTED EARNINGS WERE $579 MILLION OR $1.63 A SHARE IN THE 2022 PERIOD COMPARED WITH $499 MILLION OR $1.41 A SHARE IN THE 2021 PERIOD.
