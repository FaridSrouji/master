CRESTWOOD AND CON EDISON ANNOUNCE CLOSING OF STAGECOACH GAS SERVICES DIVESTITURE. HOUSTON & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CRESTWOOD EQUITY PARTNERS LP (NYSE: CEQP) (“CRESTWOOD”) AND CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (NYSE: ED) (“CON EDISON”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SUCCESSFUL DIVESTITURE OF THE SUBSIDIARIES OF STAGECOACH GAS SERVICES LLC (“STAGECOACH”), WITH THE EXCEPTION OF TWIN TIER PIPELINE LLC, TO A SUBSIDIARY OF KINDER MORGAN, INC. (NYSE: KMI) FOR $1.195 BILLION. THE CASH PROCEEDS FROM THE DIVESTITURE WERE SHARED BETWEEN CRESTWOOD AND CON EDISON IN LINE WITH EACH MEMBER'S 50% OWNERSHI
