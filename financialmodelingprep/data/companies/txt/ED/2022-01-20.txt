CON EDISON DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, JAN. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 79 CENTS A SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE MARCH 15, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEBRUARY 16, 2022, AN ANNUALIZED INCREASE OF 6 CENTS OVER THE PREVIOUS ANNUALIZED DIVIDEND OF $3.10 A SHARE. "THE 48TH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL INCREASE FOR STOCKHOLDERS, THE LONGEST PERIOD OF CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASES OF ANY UTILITY IN THE S&P 500 INDEX, REFLECTS OUR CONTINUED EMPHASIS ON PROVIDING A RETURN TO OUR INVESTORS WHILE MEETING THE NEEDS OF OUR CUSTOMERS DURING THE CLEAN ENERGY TRANSITION," SAID ROBERT HOGLUND, CON EDISON'S SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.
