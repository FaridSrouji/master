CON EDISON REPORTS 2021 THIRD QUARTER EARNINGS. NEW YORK, NOV. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) TODAY REPORTED 2021 THIRD QUARTER NET INCOME FOR COMMON STOCK OF $538 MILLION OR $1.52 A SHARE COMPARED WITH $493 MILLION OR $1.47 A SHARE IN THE 2020 THIRD QUARTER. ADJUSTED EARNINGS WERE $499 MILLION OR $1.41 A SHARE IN THE 2021 PERIOD COMPARED WITH $495 MILLION OR $1.48 A SHARE IN THE 2020 PERIOD.
