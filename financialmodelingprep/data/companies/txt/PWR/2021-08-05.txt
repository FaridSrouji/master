QUANTA SERVICES REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. HOUSTON, AUG. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUANTA SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: PWR) TODAY ANNOUNCED RESULTS FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED JUNE 30, 2021. REVENUES IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021 WERE $3.00 BILLION COMPARED TO REVENUES OF $2.51 BILLION IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020, AND NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON STOCK WAS $117.0 MILLION, OR $0.81 PER DILUTED SHARE, IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021 COMPARED TO NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON STOCK OF $73.9 MILLION, OR $0.52 PER DILUTED SHARE, IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020.
