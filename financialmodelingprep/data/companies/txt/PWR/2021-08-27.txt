QUANTA SERVICES ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. HOUSTON , AUG. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUANTA SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: PWR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO STOCKHOLDERS OF $0.06 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 15, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF OCTOBER 1, 2021.
