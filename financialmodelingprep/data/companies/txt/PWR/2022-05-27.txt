QUANTA SERVICES ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. HOUSTON , MAY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUANTA SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: PWR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO STOCKHOLDERS OF $0.07 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JULY 15, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 1, 2022.
