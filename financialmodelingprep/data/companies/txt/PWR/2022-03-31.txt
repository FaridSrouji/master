QUANTA SERVICES ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. HOUSTON , MARCH 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUANTA SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: PWR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO STOCKHOLDERS OF $0.07 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON APRIL 18, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 11, 2022.
