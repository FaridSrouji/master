SHAREHOLDER ALERT: MONTEVERDE & ASSOCIATES PC ANNOUNCES AN INVESTIGATION OF DISCOVERY, INC. - DISCA. NEW YORK, MAY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  JUAN MONTEVERDE , FOUNDER AND MANAGING PARTNER AT MONTEVERDE & ASSOCIATES PC, A NATIONAL SECURITIES FIRM RATED TOP 50 IN THE 2018-2020 ISS SECURITIES CLASS ACTION SERVICES REPORT AND HEADQUARTERED AT THE EMPIRE STATE BUILDING IN NEW YORK CITY, IS INVESTIGATING DISCOVERY, INC. ("DISCA" OR THE "COMPANY") (DISCA) RELATING TO ITS PROPOSED MERGER WITH AT&T INC. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT, DISCOVERY;S ENTERTAINMENT AND SPORTS BUSINESSES ARE TO COMBINE WITH WARNERMEDIA TO CREATE A STANDALONE COMPANY. DISCOVERY SHAREHOLDERS ARE EXPECTED TO OWN ONLY 29% OF THE NEW COMPANY.
