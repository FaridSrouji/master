("DENTSPLY" OR "THE COMPANY") (NASDAQ: XRAY) FOR VIOLATIONS OF THE SECURITIES LAWS.THE INVESTIGATION FOCUSES ON WHETHER THE COMPANY ISSUED FALSE AND/OR MISLEADING STATEMENTS AND/OR FAILED TO DISCLOSE INFORMATION PERTINENT TO INVESTORS.
