DENTSPLY SIRONA ANNOUNCES APPOINTMENT OF MR. JOHN P. GROETELAARS AS NEW BOARD MEMBER. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 06, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  DENTSPLY SIRONA INC. (“DENTSPLY SIRONA”) (NASDAQ: XRAY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MR. JOHN P. GROETELAARS HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE “BOARD”), INCREASING THE SIZE OF ITS BOARD FROM ELEVEN TO TWELVE MEMBERS.
