DENTSPLY SIRONA DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CHARLOTTE, N.C., DEC. 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DENTSPLY SIRONA INC. (“DENTSPLY SIRONA”) (NASDAQ: XRAY), ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.11 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, AN INDICATED ANNUAL RATE OF $0.44 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 14, 2022 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2021.
