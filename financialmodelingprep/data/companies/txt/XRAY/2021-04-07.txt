DENTSPLY SIRONA TO HOST FIRST QUARTER 2021 CONFERENCE CALL ON MAY 6TH. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DENTSPLY SIRONA INC. (“DENTSPLY SIRONA”) (NASDAQ: XRAY), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY WILL HOST AN INVESTOR CONFERENCE CALL AND LIVE WEBCAST ON THURSDAY, MAY 6, 2021 AT 8:30 AM ET AND SEND OUT AN EARNINGS PRESS RELEASE EARLIER THAT MORNING. A PRESENTATION RELATED TO THE CALL WILL BE AVAILABLE ON WWW.DENTSPLYSIRONA.COM IN THE INVESTORS SECTION.
