DENTSPLY SIRONA DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MAY 24, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DENTSPLY SIRONA INC. (“DENTSPLY SIRONA”) (NASDAQ: XRAY), ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.14 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, AN INDICATED ANNUAL RATE OF $0.56 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JULY 14, 2023, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 30, 2023.
