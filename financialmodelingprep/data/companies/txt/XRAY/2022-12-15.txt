DENTSPLY SIRONA DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CHARLOTTE, N.C., DEC. 15, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DENTSPLY SIRONA INC. (“DENTSPLY SIRONA”) (NASDAQ: XRAY), ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.125 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, AN INDICATED ANNUAL RATE OF $0.50 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 13, 2023 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 30, 2022.
