DENTSPLY SIRONA APPOINTS SIMON CAMPION AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. CHARLOTTE, N.C., AUG. 25, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DENTSPLY SIRONA INC. (“DENTSPLY SIRONA” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: XRAY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SIMON CAMPION HAS BEEN APPOINTED PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND WILL JOIN THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS (“BOARD”), EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 12, 2022. HE SUCCEEDS JOHN GROETELAARS, WHO HAS SERVED AS INTERIM CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER SINCE APRIL 2022. MR. GROETELAARS WILL CONTINUE TO SERVE ON THE BOARD.
