DENTSPLY SIRONA REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.Q2 LOSS PER SHARE $0.44.Q2 SALES $491 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $492.6 MILLION.Q2 NON-GAAP LOSS PER SHARE $0.18.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $-0.02 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.DENTSPLY SIRONA - AS RESULT OF UNCERTAINTY AROUND DURATION OF COVID-19 PANDEMIC AND IMPACT ON BUSINESS, CO NOT PROVIDING 2020 GUIDANCE.DENTSPLY SIRONA - TAKING NUMBER OF ADDITIONAL STEPS, INCLUDING TWO PORTFOLIO SHAPING INITIATIVES, ADDITIONAL ACTIONS DESIGNED TO SIMPLIFY ORGANIZATION.SAYS NOW ANTICIPATES ANNUAL COST SAVINGS OF ABOUT $250 MILLION, UP FROM PREVIOUS TARGET RANGE OF $200 TO $225 MILLION.DENTSPLY SIRONA - PORTFOLIO SHAPING INITIATIVES AND ADDITIONAL ACTIONS EXPECTED TO RESULT IN CLOSURE OF SEVERAL FACILITIES.DENTSPLY SIRONA SAYS PORTFOLIO SHAPING INITIATIVES ALSO RESULT INCREMENTAL REDUCTION OF ABOUT 6% TO 7% OF CO'S WORKFORCE BY END OF 2021.
