LOEWS CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. NEW YORK, NOV. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOEWS CORPORATION (NYSE: L) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE DECLARATION OF THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.0625 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE DECEMBER 7, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 24, 2021. LOEWS CORPORATION IS A DIVERSIFIED COMPANY WITH BUSINESSES IN THE INSURANCE, ENERGY, HOSPITALITY, AND PACKAGING INDUSTRIES.
