LOEWS CORPORATION PROMOTES BENJAMIN TISCH TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT AND STRATEGY. NEW YORK , MAY 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOEWS CORPORATION (NYSE: L) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT BEN TISCH HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT AND STRATEGY, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.   TISCH WILL OVERSEE THE PERFORMANCE OF LOEWS'S SUBSIDIARIES AND WILL WORK CLOSELY WITH LOEWS'S CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER RICHARD SCOTT TO MANAGE THE PORTFOLIOS FOR LOEWS AND ITS PUBLICLY TRADED P&C INSURANCE SUBSIDIARY, CNA FINANCIAL.
