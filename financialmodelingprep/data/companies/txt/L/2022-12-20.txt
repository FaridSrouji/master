LOEWS CORPORATION ANNOUNCES REVERSAL OF TRIAL COURT DECISION IN BOARDWALK PIPELINES ACQUISITION LITIGATION. NEW YORK , DEC. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOEWS CORPORATION (NYSE: L) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE DELAWARE SUPREME COURT REVERSED THE NOVEMBER 2021 DECISION OF THE DELAWARE COURT OF CHANCERY THAT HAD AWARDED FORMER MINORITY UNITHOLDERS IN ITS BOARDWALK PIPELINES SUBSIDIARY APPROXIMATELY $690 MILLION, PLUS INTEREST. THIS AWARD WAS IN CONNECTION WITH LOEWS'S 2018 ACQUISITION OF BOARDWALK UNITS.
