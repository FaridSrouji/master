LOEWS CARRYING VALUE OF DIAMOND OFFSHORE DRILLING, OTHER UNITS $1.5 BLN ON DEC 31. LOEWS - LOEWS’S CARRYING VALUE OF DIAMOND OFFSHORE DRILLING & SOME OF ITS UNITS, AT DEC 31, 2019 WAS $1.5 BILLION.LOEWS - IS NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR, & DOES NOT PROVIDE GUARANTEES OF, LIABILITIES & OBLIGATIONS OF ITS UNITS, INCLUDING DIAMOND.LOEWS - EXPECTS TO RECORD IN Q2 A NON-CASH LOSS FOR DIFFERENCE BETWEEN CARRYING VALUE, ESTIMATED FAIR VALUE OF INTEREST IN DIAMOND AS OF APRIL 26.LOEWS - TO NO LONGER CONTROL DIAMOND FOR ACCOUNTING PURPOSES, DIAMOND TO BE DECONSOLIDATED FROM CO'S CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS EFFECTIVE APRIL 26.
