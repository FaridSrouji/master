LOEWS CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. NEW YORK , AUG. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOEWS CORPORATION (NYSE: L) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE DECLARATION OF THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.0625 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE AUGUST 30, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 17, 2022. LOEWS CORPORATION IS A DIVERSIFIED COMPANY WITH BUSINESSES IN THE INSURANCE, ENERGY, HOSPITALITY, AND PACKAGING INDUSTRIES.
