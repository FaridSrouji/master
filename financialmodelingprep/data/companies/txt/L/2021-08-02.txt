LOEWS CORPORATION REPORTS NET INCOME OF $754 MILLION FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021. NEW YORK, AUG. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOEWS CORPORATION (NYSE:L) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $754 MILLION, OR $2.86 PER SHARE, FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021 COMPARED TO A NET LOSS OF $835 MILLION, OR $2.96 PER SHARE, FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020. NET INCOME FOR THE SIX MONTHS ENDED JUNE 30, 2021 WAS $1.0 BILLION, OR $3.82 PER SHARE, COMPARED TO A NET LOSS OF $1.5 BILLION, OR $5.16 PER SHARE, FOR THE SIX MONTHS ENDED JUNE 30, 2020.
