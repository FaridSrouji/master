E& AND SES JOIN FORCES TO OFFER ONE-HOP CONNECTIVITY TO MICROSOFT AZURE. LUXEMBOURG & DUBAI, UNITED ARAB EMIRATES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE CARRIER AND WHOLESALE SERVICES (C&WS) DIVISION OF E& (FORMERLY KNOWN AS ETISALAT GROUP) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS PARTNERSHIP WITH MICROSOFT AND SES TO HOST THE CO-LOCATED SES O3B MPOWER AND MICROSOFT GROUND STATION AT RAS AL KHAIMAH.
