MICROSOFT AND THE KNIGHT CENTER FOR JOURNALISM IN THE AMERICAS LAUNCH DIGITAL STORYTELLING ONLINE COURSE FOR THE NEXT GENERATION OF LATINX JOURNALISTS. FREE, FOUR-WEEK ONLINE COURSE AIMS TO EMPOWER AND INSPIRE LATINX STUDENTS TO PURSUE CAREERS IN MEDIA FORT LAUDERDALE, FLA. , MAY 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MICROSOFT AND THE KNIGHT CENTER FOR JOURNALISM IN THE AMERICAS ON WEDNESDAY ANNOUNCED THEIR COLLABORATION TO CREATE AN ONLINE COURSE THAT AIMS TO REACH LATINX GEN Z AND MILLENNIAL STORYTELLERS.
