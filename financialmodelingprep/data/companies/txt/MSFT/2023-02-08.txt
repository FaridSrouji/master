ADOBE AND MICROSOFT BRING INDUSTRY-LEADING ACROBAT PDF EXPERIENCE TO 1.4 BILLION WINDOWS USERS THROUGH MICROSOFT EDGE. SAN JOSE, CALIF. & REDMOND, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, ADOBE (NASDAQ:ADBE) AND MICROSOFT CORP. (NASDAQ: MSFT) HAVE TAKEN THE NEXT STEP IN THEIR COMMITMENT TO TRANSFORM THE FUTURE OF DIGITAL WORK AND LIFE BY BRINGING ADOBE ACROBAT'S PDF CAPABILITIES TO MORE THAN 1.4 BILLION MICROSOFT WINDOWS USERS IN MICROSOFT EDGE.
