LALIGA TEAMS UP WITH MICROSOFT TO DIGITALLY TRANSFORM FOOTBALL GLOBALLY AND REIMAGINE A NEW ERA IN SPORTS. MADRID, MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LALIGA, SPAIN'S PREMIER FOOTBALL ASSOCIATION, AND MICROSOFT CORP. ON WEDNESDAY ANNOUNCED AN EXPANSION OF THEIR PARTNERSHIP FOCUSED ON DIGITALLY TRANSFORMING THE SPORTS EXPERIENCE GLOBALLY. THE COMPANIES WILL ALSO COLLABORATE ON DEVELOPING TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS TO THE MEDIA AND ENTERTAINMENT INDUSTRY THROUGH LALIGA'S TECHNOLOGY OFFERING, LALIGA TECH.
