KPMG AND MICROSOFT SIGN FIVE-YEAR AGREEMENT TO ENABLE KPMG MEMBER FIRMS AND MUTUAL CLIENTS TO BENEFIT FROM MICROSOFT TECHNOLOGY, INNOVATION AND ADVANCEMENTS IN MICROSOFT'S CLOUD PLATFORMS. KPMG ANNOUNCED INVESTMENTS IN TECHNOLOGY, PEOPLE AND INNOVATION, AND EXPECTS TO SPEND USD 5 BILLION OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS TO ENHANCE ITS LEADERSHIP POSITION IN THE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION OF PROFESSIONAL SERVICES. KPMG ENTERS FIVE-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH MICROSOFT TO INVEST IN CLOUD-BASED TECHNOLOGIES. AS PART OF THIS INVESTMENT, KPMG AND MICROSOFT HAVE SIGNED A FIVE-YEAR AGREEMENT THAT ENABLES KPMG MEMBER FIRMS AND MUTUAL CLIENTS TO BENEFIT FROM MICROSOFT TECHNOLOGY, INNOVATION AND ADVANCEMENTS IN MICROSOFT'S CLOUD PLATFORMS. KPMG IS UTILIZING MICROSOFT TECHNOLOGY 'INCLUDING MICROSOFT AZURE AND MICROSOFT 365' TO CREATE A CONSISTENT, GLOBAL CLOUD-BASED PLATFORM AND EXTEND ITS RANGE OF DIGITAL OFFERINGS. KPMG FULLY DEPLOYING CLOUD-BASED AUDIT ACROSS THE GLOBE.
MICROSOFT SETS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.51 PER SHARE. SETS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.51PER SHARE.
