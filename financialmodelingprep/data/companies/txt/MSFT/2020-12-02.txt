MICROSOFT ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. REDMOND, WASH., DEC. 2, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MICROSOFT CORP. ON WEDNESDAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.56 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 11, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEB. 18, 2021. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE WILL BE FEB. 17, 2021....
