SONATA SOFTWARE IS PROUD TO PARTNER WITH MICROSOFT FOR THE LAUNCH OF MICROSOFT FABRIC. BANGALORE, INDIA , MAY 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SONATA SOFTWARE, A LEADING MODERNIZATION ENGINEERING COMPANY, ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS PROUD TO BE PART OF THE GLOBAL LAUNCH OF MICROSOFT FABRIC AT MICROSOFT BUILD EVENT IN SEATTLE, UNITED STATES. MICROSOFT FABRIC IS AN END-TO-END UNIFIED ANALYTICS PLATFORM BRINGING TOGETHER ALL THE DATA AND ANALYTICS TOOLS THAT ORGANIZATIONS NEED.
