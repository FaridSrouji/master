SES BECOMES MICROSOFT AZURE ORBITAL FOUNDING CONNECTIVITY PARTNER. SES ANNOUNCED ON TUESDAY IT BECAME MICROSOFT AZURE ORBITAL FOUNDING CONNECTIVITY PARTNER .INTEGRATION IS PART OF A MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE TWO COMPANIES.TWO COMPANIES WILL MAKE JOINT INVESTMENTS IN AZURE ORBITAL GROUND STATIONS THAT SES WILL DEPLOY AND MANAGE FOR THE MEO AND EARTH OBSERVATION SEGMENT.
