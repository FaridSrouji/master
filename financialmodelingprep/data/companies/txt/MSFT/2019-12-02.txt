NORTHSTAR ASSET SUBMITS A SHAREHOLDER PROPOSAL TO MICROSOFT CORPORATION. ON DECEMBER 2, 2019, NORTHSTAR ASSET MANAGEMENT, INC. FILED AN EXEMPT SOLICITATION STATEMENT SOLICITING PROXIES AND URGED THE SHAREHOLDERS OF MICROSOFT CORPORATION TO VOTE FOR SHAREHOLDER PROPOSAL, URGING THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS TO PREPARE A REPORT TO SHAREHOLDERS DESCRIBING OPPORTUNITIES FOR THE COMPANY TO ENCOURAGE THE INCLUSION OF NON-MANAGEMENT EMPLOYEE REPRESENTATION ON THE BOARD, AT THE COMPANY’S ANNUAL SHAREHOLDERS MEETING.
