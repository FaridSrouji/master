ONESOFT REPORT RESULTS OF AGSM AND GRANT OF STOCK OPTIONS. EDMONTON, AB / ACCESSWIRE / MAY 26, 2023 / ONESOFT SOLUTIONS INC. (TSXV:OSS)(OTCQB:OSSIF) ("ONESOFT" OR THE "COMPANY") ANNOUNCES THAT THE COMPANY'S ANNUAL GENERAL AND SPECIAL MEETING ("AGSM") OF THE SHAREHOLDERS WAS HELD MAY 23, 2023. THE SCRUTINEER'S REPORT STATED THAT THE MEETING QUORUM WAS MET, WITH VOTING BY 119 SHAREHOLDERS PRESENT IN PERSON OR REPRESENTED BY PROXY AT THE MEETING, REPRESENTING 69,228,906 OR 57.2% OF THE ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING COMMON SHARES OF ONESOFT.
