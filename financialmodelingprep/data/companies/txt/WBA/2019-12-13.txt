MOTHERCARE ENTERS INTO PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT WITH BOOTS UK. REG-MOTHERCARE'S NEW UK FRANCHISE PARTNER.MOTHERCARE PLC - HAS ENTERED INTO BINDING HEADS OF TERMS WITH BOOTS UK.MOTHERCARE PLC - NEW PARTNERSHIP WILL PROVIDE "STRONG FOUNDATIONS" FOR MOTHERCARE'S CONTINUING PRESENCE IN UK.MOTHERCARE PLC - BOOTS WILL STOCK MOTHERCARE-BRANDED CLOTHING, AND HOME AND TRAVEL PRODUCTS.MOTHERCARE PLC - EXPECTED THAT A WIDER MOTHERCARE PRODUCT OFFER WILL BE AVAILABLE IN BOOTS STORES FROM LATE SUMMER 2020.MOTHERCARE PLC - PROPOSED FRANCHISEE PARTNERSHIP WILL BE FOR AN INITIAL PERIOD OF 5 YEARS.MOTHERCARE PLC - WILL NOW PUSH ON WITH FINALISATION OF DETAILED CONTRACTUAL ARRANGEMENTS WITH BOOTS WHICH ARE EXPECTED TO BE SIGNED IN NEXT QUARTER.MOTHERCARE - WILL NOW PUSH ON WITH FINALISATION OF DETAILED CONTRACTUAL ARRANGEMENTS WITH BOOTS WHICH ARE EXPECTED TO BE SIGNED IN NEXT QUARTER.
