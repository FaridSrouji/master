WALGREENS NOW OFFERING FLU AND COVID-19 TESTING IN A SINGLE VISIT AT OVER 5,000 LOCATIONS. DEERFIELD, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WALGREENS TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT RAPID FLU TESTING WILL NOW BE AVAILABLE AT MORE THAN 5,000 PHARMACIES NATIONWIDE TO HELP COMMUNITIES IDENTIFY AND MANAGE THE HIGHLY TRANSMISSIBLE VIRUSES CIRCULATING THIS HOLIDAY SEASON.* WALGREENS WILL USE ABBOTT'S ID NOW™ DEVICES TO ADMINISTER THE TESTS, AND RESULTS WILL BE AVAILABLE WITHIN TWO HOURS.
