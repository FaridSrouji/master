FROM THE WORKPLACE TO THE CLASSROOM, WALGREENS EXPANDS COMMITMENT TO IMPROVING MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS PART OF WALGREENS' COMMITMENT TO THE HEALTH AND WELLBEING OF THE COMMUNITIES IT SERVES, AS WELL AS TO FOSTERING A HEALTHY AND INCLUSIVE WORKPLACE, THE COMPANY IS EXPANDING ITS EFFORTS TO MEET THE GROWING NEED FOR ACCESS TO MENTAL HEALTH RESOURCES. THE ANNOUNCEMENT COMES IN CONJUNCTION WITH MENTAL HEALTH AWARENESS MONTH, WHICH RUNS THROUGHOUT MAY IN THE U.S. AS THE PREVALENCE OF MENTAL HEALTH CONDITIONS HAS CONTINUED TO GROW EXPONENTIALLY OVER THE PAST SEVERA.
