BLUE SHIELD OF CALIFORNIA, WALGREENS COLLABORATE TO BRING INNOVATIVE HEALTHCARE AND WELLNESS SERVICES TO COMMUNITIES IN GOLDEN STATE. OAKLAND, CALIF., SEPT. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BLUE SHIELD OF CALIFORNIA AND WALGREENS TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW COLLABORATION TO EXPAND ACCESS TO HEALTH CARE, LOWER COSTS, AND BRING INNOVATIVE SERVICES TO ENHANCE THE CONSUMER EXPERIENCE FOR INDIVIDUALS, FAMILIES, AND COMMUNITIES THROUGHOUT CALIFORNIA.
