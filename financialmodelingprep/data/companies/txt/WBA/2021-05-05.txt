VIM CLOSES INVESTMENTS FROM WALGREENS, ANTHEM, AND FRIST CRESSEY VENTURES TO BUILD DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE FOR HIGHER PERFORMING HEALTH CARE. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #DIGITALHEALTH--VIM, A LEADING TECHNOLOGY COMPANY BUILDING DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE FOR US HEALTH CARE, TODAY ANNOUNCED INVESTMENTS FROM WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE (WBA), ANTHEM (ANTM), AND FRIST CRESSEY VENTURES. THE ADDITION OF THESE STRATEGIC INVESTORS REFLECTS VIM'S ACCELERATING PRODUCT AND MARKET PROGRESS DEPLOYING INTEGRATED DATA AND WORKFLOW SOLUTIONS FOR LEADING HEALTH PLANS AND PROVIDERS ACROSS THE COUNTRY. VIM'S SOLUTIONS ARE WIDELY USED BY LEADING HEALTH PLANS, PROVIDERS,
