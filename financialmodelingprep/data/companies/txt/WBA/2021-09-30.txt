SAFEGUARD HAND SOAP AND WALGREENS COLLABORATE WITH AMERICARES TO BRING 10 MILLION HANDWASHES TO COMMUNITIES IN NEED. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, WALGREENS AND SAFEGUARD ANNOUNCED AN EXCITING NEW COLLABORATION WITH AMERICARES TO LAUNCH THE HOPE IS IN YOUR HANDS PROGRAM, WHICH WILL PROVIDE 10 MILLION HANDWASHES TO COMMUNITIES AND FAMILIES IN NEED.
