WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE SELLS 15.5 MILLION SHARES OF OPTION CARE HEALTH. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE, INC. (NASDAQ: WBA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SOLD 13.0 MILLION SHARES OF OPTION CARE HEALTH, INC. (“OPTION CARE HEALTH”) (NASDAQ: OPCH) COMMON STOCK IN AN UNDERWRITTEN SECONDARY OFFERING AND, SUBJECT TO COMPLETION OF THE OFFERING, A CONCURRENT SHARE REPURCHASE BY OPCH IN THE AMOUNT OF APPROXIMATELY 2.5 MILLION SHARES.
