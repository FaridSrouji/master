WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DEERFIELD, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE, INC. (NASDAQ: WBA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 48 CENTS PER SHARE, UNCHANGED FROM THE PREVIOUS QUARTER AND AN INCREASE OF 0.5 PERCENT FROM THE YEAR-AGO QUARTER. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 10, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEB. 16, 2023.
