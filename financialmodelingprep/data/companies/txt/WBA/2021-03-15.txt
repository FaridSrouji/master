WALGREENS CALLS FOR DIVERSE LOCAL AND REGIONAL VENDOR PARTICIPATION NATIONWIDE AT THE LOCALIZATION SUMMIT. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALGREENS ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE FIRST-EVER LOCALIZATION SUMMIT, A VIRTUAL SHOWCASE FOR DIVERSE, LOCAL AND REGIONAL BUSINESSES AND ENTREPRENEURS TO SHARE THEIR RETAIL OFFERINGS WITH THE MERCHANTS OF THE NATIONAL DRUGSTORE CHAIN.
