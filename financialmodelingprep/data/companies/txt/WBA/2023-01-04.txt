WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE TO PARTICIPATE IN 41ST ANNUAL J.P. MORGAN HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE. DEERFIELD, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE, INC. (NASDAQ: WBA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, ROZ BREWER, AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND GLOBAL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, JAMES KEHOE, WILL PRESENT AT THE 41ST ANNUAL J.P. MORGAN HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE IN SAN FRANCISCO, CALIFORNIA ON TUESDAY, JAN. 10 AT 11:15 A.M. PT (2:15 P.M. ET).
