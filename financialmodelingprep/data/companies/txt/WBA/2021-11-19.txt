WALGREENS NOW PROVIDING ALL COVID-19 VACCINE BOOSTERS FOR INDIVIDUALS AGES 18 AND OLDER. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALGREENS ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ALL INDIVIDUALS OVER THE AGE OF 18 CAN NOW RECEIVE A PFIZER, MODERNA OR JOHNSON & JOHNSON COVID-19 VACCINE BOOSTER IN STORES NATIONWIDE. THIS FOLLOWS THE FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION'S (FDA) RECENT DECISION TO EXPAND EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR PFIZER AND MODERNA BOOSTER VACCINES TO ALL INDIVIDUALS 18 AND OLDER, IN ADDITION TO NEW GUIDANCE FROM THE CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION (CDC).
