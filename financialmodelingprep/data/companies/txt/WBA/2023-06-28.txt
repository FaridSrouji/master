WALGREENS DEEPENS COMMITMENT TO THOSE LIVING WITH DIABETES AT CHILDREN WITH DIABETES' FRIENDS FOR LIFE CONFERENCE. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, WALGREENS ANNOUNCED ITS PARTICIPATION AS THE PREMIER SPONSOR OF CHILDREN WITH DIABETES (CWD), A NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO PROVIDING EDUCATION AND SUPPORT TO FAMILIES LIVING WITH TYPE 1 DIABETES, AT THEIR UPCOMING FRIENDS FOR LIFE (FFL) CONFERENCE. WALGREENS WILL SERVE AS THE EXCLUSIVE PHARMACY SPONSOR FOR THE ANNUAL EVENT, HOSTED JULY 4TH – 9TH IN ORLANDO, FLA.
