THIRTEENTH ANNUAL EXPRESSIONS BY WALGREENS HIGH SCHOOL ART CHALLENGE OPENS FOR ENTRIES. DEERFIELD, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TEENAGERS NATIONWIDE CAN BEGIN SUBMITTING ENTRIES FOR THE 13TH ANNUAL EXPRESSIONS BY WALGREENS, AN ARTISTIC CHALLENGE FOR HIGH SCHOOL STUDENTS THAT AWARDS MONETARY PRIZES IN FOUR CATEGORIES: VISUAL ARTS, MEDIA ARTS, SPOKEN WORD, AND CREATIVE WRITING. ENTRIES ARE BEING ACCEPTED NOW THROUGH MARCH 31, 2023.
