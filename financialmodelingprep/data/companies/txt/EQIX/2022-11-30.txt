EQUINIX ENTERS MALAYSIA WITH $40M DATA CENTER INVESTMENT. KUALA LUMPUR, MALAYSIA AND REDWOOD CITY, CALIF. , NOV. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS MARKET ENTRY INTO MALAYSIA WITH PLANS TO BUILD A NEW INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS EXCHANGETM (IBX®) DATA CENTER LOCATED IN JOHOR, CALLED JH1.
