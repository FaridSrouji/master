EQUINIX DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 11, 2019. EQUINIX, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $2.46 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK. THE QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON DECEMBER 11, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 20, 2019.
