EQUINIX PROMOTES JON LIN TO EVP & GM, DATA CENTER SERVICES. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF., JAN. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF JON LIN TO THE POSITION OF EVP & GENERAL MANAGER, DATA CENTER SERVICES, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.
