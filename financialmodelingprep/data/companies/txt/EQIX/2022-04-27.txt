EQUINIX DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF. , APRIL 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $3.10 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK.
