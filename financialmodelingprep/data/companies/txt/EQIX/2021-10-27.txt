EQUINIX LEVELS UP UK DIGITAL ECONOMY WITH £61 MILLION MANCHESTER DATA CENTER. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF., OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO BUILD A NEW INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS EXCHANGE™ (IBX®) DATA CENTER IN THE AGECROFT COMMERCE PARK IN SALFORD, MANCHESTER, UK.
