EQUINIX HIGHLIGHTS LATEST PROGRESS ON ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) COMMITMENTS IN ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. THE COMPANY IS COMMITTED TO REACHING GLOBAL CLIMATE NEUTRALITY BY 2030 AND GREENING THE SUPPLY CHAINS OF CUSTOMERS WHO LEVERAGE ITS SUSTAINABLE INFRASTRUCTURE REDWOOD CITY, CALIF. , MAY 19, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, HAS RELEASED ITS 2021 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT WHICH OUTLINES PROGRESS, INNOVATION AND ACCOMPLISHMENTS ON KEY ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) COMMITMENTS FROM THE PAST YEAR AND SINCE FORMALIZING ITS CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY PROGRAM IN 2015.
