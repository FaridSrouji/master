DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE NEEDS AND EFFICIENCY GOALS SUPPORTED BY NEW EQUINIX DATA CENTER IN FRANKFURT. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF., SEPT. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, TODAY OPENED A NEW INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS EXCHANGE™ (IBX®) DATA CENTER IN FRANKFURT, GERMANY.
