EQUINIX TO BUILD NEW DATA CENTER IN BARCELONA--A GROWING CONNECTIVITY HUB OF THE MEDITERRANEAN. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF. , FEB. 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS BARCELONA BECOMES AN INCREASINGLY STRATEGIC HUB FOR THE TERRESTRIAL AND SUBSEA CABLE NETWORKS CONNECTING OUR DIGITAL LIVES, EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, IS BUILDING ITS SECOND DATA CENTER IN THE METRO.
