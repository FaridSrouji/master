EQUINIX TO EXPAND INTO AFRICA WITH ACQUISITION OF MAINONE. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF., DEC. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC.  (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS EXPANSION INTO AFRICA THROUGH ITS INTENDED ACQUISITION OF MAINONE, A LEADING WEST AFRICAN DATA CENTER AND CONNECTIVITY SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, WITH PRESENCE IN NIGERIA, GHANA AND CÔTE D'IVOIRE.
