OVER 40% OF IT LEADERS DOUBT THEIR DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE IS PREPARED TO ACCOMMODATE COVETED AI TECHNOLOGY. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF. , JUNE 14, 2023  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MORE THAN FOUR IN 10 IT LEADERS SURVEYED GLOBALLY (42%) BELIEVE THEIR EXISTING IT INFRASTRUCTURE IS NOT FULLY PREPARED FOR THE DEMANDS OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) TECHNOLOGY, DESPITE ITS WIDESPREAD ADOPTION ACROSS INDUSTRIES, ACCORDING TO THE EQUINIX 2023 GLOBAL TECH TRENDS SURVEY.
