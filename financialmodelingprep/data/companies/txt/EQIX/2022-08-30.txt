ORANGE AND EQUINIX BRING CLOUD AGILITY TO TELCO INFRASTRUCTURE THROUGH GROUNDBREAKING "AS A SERVICE" CAPABILITY. PARIS AND REDWOOD CITY, CALIF. , AUG. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORANGE AND EQUINIX ANNOUNCED TODAY A COLLABORATION TO EXPAND THE ORANGE TELCO CLOUD FOOTPRINT, USING EQUINIX'S BARE METAL AS A SERVICE CAPABILITY—EQUINIX METAL®—TO SPEED THE DEPLOYMENT OF ORANGE'S NEW GENERATION INTERNATIONAL NETWORK.
