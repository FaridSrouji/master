EQUINIX INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF. , FEB. 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANYTM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $3.41 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, A 10% INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTER.
