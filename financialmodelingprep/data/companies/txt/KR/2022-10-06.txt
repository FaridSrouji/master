KROGER HEALTH URGES CUSTOMERS TO GET VACCINATED AGAINST THE FLU BY THE END OF OCTOBER TO ENSURE PROTECTION DURING PEAK FLU SEASON. GROCER OFFERS $5 COUPON TO CUSTOMERS WHO RECEIVE FLU VACCINE AT ITS PHARMACIES OR CLINICS CINCINNATI , OCT. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KROGER HEALTH, THE HEALTHCARE DIVISION OF THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), IS URGING CUSTOMERS TO GET VACCINATED AGAINST THE FLU THIS MONTH TO ENSURE PROTECTION DURING PEAK FLU SEASON. "THE FIRST STEP IN PROTECTING YOURSELF AND OTHERS FROM THE FLU IS TO GET VACCINATED," SAID DOUG CORNELIUS, RPH, SENIOR DIRECTOR, PHARMACY OPERATIONS, KROGER HEALTH.
