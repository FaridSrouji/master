KROGER HEALTH WINS 2022 AMERICAN PHARMACISTS ASSOCIATION IMMUNIZATION CHAMPION AWARD. CINCINNATI, JAN. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KROGER HEALTH, THE HEALTHCARE DIVISION OF THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), RECEIVES THE 2022 AMERICAN PHARMACISTS ASSOCIATION (APHA) IMMUNIZATION CHAMPION AWARD. THE AWARD RECOGNIZES INDIVIDUALS AND ORGANIZATIONS MAKING EXTRAORDINARY CONTRIBUTIONS TOWARD IMPROVED VACCINATION RATES WITHIN THEIR COMMUNITIES.
