RICHMOND/HAMPTON ROADS ASSOCIATES RATIFY NEW AGREEMENT WITH THE KROGER MID-ATLANTIC DIVISION. RICHMOND, VA., AUG. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KROGER (NYSE: KR) MID-ATLANTIC DIVISION ASSOCIATES IN THE RICHMOND/HAMPTON ROADS BARGAINING UNIT HAVE RATIFIED A NEW COLLECTIVE BARGAINING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE COMPANY AND THE UNITED FOOD AND COMMERCIAL WORKERS UNION, LOCAL 400.
