THE KROGER FAMILY OF COMPANIES HOSTING SPRING HIRING EVENT ON APRIL 30 TO FILL 23,000 ROLES. AMERICA'S LARGEST GROCERY RETAILER RECRUITING NATIONALLY FOR SALARIED AND HOURLY ROLES CINCINNATI , APRIL 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) TODAY ANNOUNCED A SPRING HIRING EVENT SCHEDULED FOR SATURDAY, APRIL 30, THE GROCER'S THIRD NATIONWIDE HYBRID HIRING EVENT . THE KROGER FAMILY OF COMPANIES IS HIRING FOR 23,000 OPEN ROLES ACROSS ALL DEPARTMENTS, INCLUDING RETAIL, E-COMMERCE, MANUFACTURING, SUPPLY CHAIN, MERCHANDISING, LOGISTICS, CORPORATE, PHARMACY AND HEALTHCARE ROLES.
