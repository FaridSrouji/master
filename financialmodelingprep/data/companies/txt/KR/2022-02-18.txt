KROGER TO HOST 2022 BUSINESS UPDATE. CINCINNATI, FEB. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WILL HOST A BUSINESS UPDATE IN FLORIDA, INCLUDING A TOUR OF THE GROVELAND CUSTOMER FULFILLMENT CENTER IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING FORMAL PRESENTATIONS ON FRIDAY, MARCH 4, 2022. KROGER EXECUTIVES WILL PROVIDE AN UPDATE ON THE COMPANY'S STRATEGIC INITIATIVES, INCLUDING LEADING WITH FRESH AND ACCELERATING WITH DIGITAL AND THE VALUE CREATION MODEL.
