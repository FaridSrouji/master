KROGER'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CINCINNATI, OHIO , SEPT. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO.'S (NYSE: KR) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 26 CENTS PER SHARE TO BE PAID ON DECEMBER 1, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 15, 2022.
