WITH FLU SEASON IN FULL SWING, RALPHS REMINDS FAMILIES IT'S TIME TO VACCINATE. LOS ANGELES , OCT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RALPHS PHARMACY, A HEALTHCARE DIVISION OF KROGER HEALTH, IS URGING CUSTOMERS TO GET VACCINATED AGAINST THE FLU THIS MONTH TO ENSURE PROTECTION DURING PEAK FLU SEASON. THE CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION (CDC) RECOMMENDS INDIVIDUALS GET VACCINATED BY THE END OF OCTOBER FOR PEAK FLU PROTECTION.
