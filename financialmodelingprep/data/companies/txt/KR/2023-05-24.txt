KROGER RECOGNIZED FOR COMMITMENT TO WORKPLACE MENTAL HEALTH. FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR, RETAILER AWARDED THE GOLD BELL SEAL BY MENTAL HEALTH AMERICA CINCINNATI , MAY 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN AWARDED THE 2023 GOLD BELL SEAL FOR WORKPLACE MENTAL HEALTH BY MENTAL HEALTH AMERICA (MHA). THE BELL SEAL IS A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND WORKPLACE MENTAL HEALTH CERTIFICATION THAT RECOGNIZES EMPLOYERS WHO STRIVE TO CREATE MENTALLY HEALTHY WORKPLACES FOR THEIR ASSOCIATES.
