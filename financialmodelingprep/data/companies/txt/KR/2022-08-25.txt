KROGER ANNOUNCES SECOND ANNUAL GO FRESH & LOCAL SUPPLIER ACCELERATOR COHORT. FIVE SMALL LOCAL AND REGIONAL BRANDS SELECTED TO DIVERSIFY KROGER'S PRODUCT OFFERINGS AND RECEIVE STRATEGIC MENTORSHIP CINCINNATI , AUG. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE WINNERS OF ITS SECOND ANNUAL GO FRESH & LOCAL SUPPLIER ACCELERATOR. THE ACCELERATOR COHORT CONSISTS OF FIVE NEW AND EMERGING BRANDS, EACH SELECTED TO JOIN KROGER'S FRESH SUPPLIERS AND ENHANCE THE SELECTION OF LOCAL ITEMS AVAILABLE ACROSS THE KROGER FAMILY OF COMPANIES.
