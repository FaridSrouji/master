HOME CHEF ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP TEAM PROMOTIONS AND EXPANSION. CHICAGO, JUNE 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HOME CHEF, THE FAST-GROWING MEAL KIT SUBSCRIPTION AND RETAIL BUSINESS, TODAY ANNOUNCED LEADERSHIP PROMOTIONS AND NEW ROLES INTENDED TO STRENGTHEN PLANS FOR GROWTH AND ACCELERATE INNOVATION. ERIK JENSEN, THE COMPANY'S CHIEF PRODUCT OFFICER, WILL BE PROMOTED TO PRESIDENT WHERE HE WILL BE REPORTING DIRECTLY TO PAT VIHTELIC, THE COMPANY'S FOUNDER AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
