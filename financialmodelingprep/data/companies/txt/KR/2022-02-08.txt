KROGER ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGES. CINCINNATI, FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RETIREMENT OF MARK TUFFIN, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF RETAIL DIVISIONS, AFTER MORE THAN 26 YEARS OF DISTINGUISHED SERVICE, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1. TUFFIN WILL BE SUCCEEDED BY KENNY KIMBALL, CURRENTLY PRESIDENT OF THE SMITH'S DIVISION.
