RALPHS AND KITCHEN UNITED MIX OPEN FIRST LOCATION. LOS ANGELES, JAN. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RALPHS AND KITCHEN UNITED MIX ANNOUNCED TODAY THE OPENING OF THE FIRST KITCHEN CENTER IN THE WESTWOOD NEIGHBORHOOD OF LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA. CUSTOMERS CAN BUILD THEIR FRESHLY PREPARED, ON-DEMAND RESTAURANT MEALS FROM ANY OR ALL OF THE PARTICIPATING RESTAURANTS TO SATISFY EVEN THE PICKIEST EATERS.
