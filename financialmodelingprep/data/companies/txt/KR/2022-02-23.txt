KROGER'S FRED MEYER STORES LAUNCH LOOP IN PORTLAND, OREGON METRO. CINCINNATI AND TRENTON, N.J., FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), AMERICA'S LARGEST GROCERY RETAILER, AND LOOP, THE CIRCULAR REUSE PLATFORM DEVELOPED BY TERRACYCLE, HAVE LAUNCHED A NEW WAY FOR CUSTOMERS TO SHOP, OFFERING A SELECTION OF PRODUCTS IN REUSABLE PACKAGING RATHER THAN IN SINGLE-USE PLASTIC.
