KROGER REPORTS Q1 EPS OF $1.52. KROGER REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2020 RESULTS AND PROVIDES UPDATE ON COVID-19 RESPONSE.Q1 SALES $42 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $40.45 BILLION.QTRLY EPS OF $1.52; ADJUSTED EPS OF $1.22.QTRLY IDENTICAL SALES WITHOUT FUEL GREW 19.0%."COVID-19 PANDEMIC HAS DRAMATICALLY CHANGED OUTLOOK FOR FOOD RETAIL IN 2020".LIFO CHARGE FOR QUARTER WAS $31 MILLION.NOT REAFFIRMING OR PROVIDING NEW 2020 GUIDANCE.QTRLY DIGITAL SALES GREW 92%.EXPECT TO EXCEED FY OUTLOOK SHARED IN APRIL 1 BUSINESS UPDATE FOR IDENTICAL SALES WITHOUT FUEL, ADJUSTED FIFO OPERATING PROFIT.EXPECT TO EXCEED FY OUTLOOK SHARED IN APRIL 1 BUSINESS UPDATE FOR ADJUSTED EPS AND ADJUSTED FCF.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.09 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.KROGER - CONTRIBUTED ADDITIONAL $236 MILLION IN QUARTER TO MULTI-EMPLOYER PENSION PLANS TO HELP STABILIZE FUTURE ASSOCIATE BENEFITS.
