SEAL AWARDS RECOGNIZE LEADERSHIP, TRANSPARENCY AND COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES CINCINNATI , MAY 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO.
