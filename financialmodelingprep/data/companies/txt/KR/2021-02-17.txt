KROGER HEALTH INTRODUCES COVID-19 VACCINE SCHEDULING SOLUTIONS TO ENHANCE VACCINATION EFFORTS. CINCINNATI, FEB. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KROGER HEALTH, THE HEALTH CARE DIVISION OF THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS NEW VACCINE SCHEDULING TOOL, MAKING IT EASY AND EFFICIENT TO BOOK COVID-19 VACCINE APPOINTMENTS ONLINE. THE NEW TOOL CAN MANAGE MORE THAN 250,000 REQUESTS...
