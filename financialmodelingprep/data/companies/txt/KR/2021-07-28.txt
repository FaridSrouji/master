KROGER CENTRAL DIVISION AND CENTRAL ILLINOIS ASSOCIATES RATIFY NEW AGREEMENTS. INDIANAPOLIS, JULY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KROGER'S CENTRAL DIVISION AND ITS ASSOCIATES WORKING IN CENTRAL ILLINOIS AREA STORES HAVE RATIFIED NEW LABOR AGREEMENTS WITH UFCW LOCAL 536. KROGER CENTRAL DIVISION WILL INVEST $11.3 MILLION IN WAGE INCREASES DURING THE THREE-YEAR TERM OF THE NEW AGREEMENTS.
