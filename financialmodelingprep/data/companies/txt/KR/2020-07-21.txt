KROGER CO - KROGER REACHES TENTATIVE AGREEMENT WITH UFCW LOCAL UNIONS. KROGER CO - KROGER REACHES TENTATIVE AGREEMENT WITH UFCW LOCAL UNIONS.KROGER REACHES TENTATIVE AGREEMENT WITH UFCW LOCAL UNIONS TO IMPROVE SECURITY AND STABILITY OF FUTURE PENSION BENEFITS FOR ASSOCIATES.KROGER CO - NO IMPACT TO ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE FOR 2020.KROGER CO - STILL EXPECTS TO EXCEED ADJUSTED FREE CASH FLOW OUTLOOK FOR 2020 SHARED IN APRIL 1 BUSINESS UPDATE.KROGER CO - ANNOUNCED TENTATIVE AGREEMENT WITH 20 LOCAL UNIONS OF UFCW TO WITHDRAW FROM UFCW INTERNATIONAL UNION-INDUSTRY PENSION FUND.KROGER CO - TENTATIVE AGREEMENT HAS BEEN APPROVED BY KROGER AND NATIONAL FUND BOARD OF TRUSTEES.KROGER CO - STOP AND SHOP SUPERMARKET COMPANY, ALBERTSONS COMPANIES HAVE ENTERED INTO TENTATIVE AGREEMENT WITH UFCW LOCAL UNIONS TO WITHDRAW FROM NATIONAL FUND.KROGER CO - CO, STOP & SHOP TO TRANSITION TO NEW VARIABLE ANNUITY PENSION PLAN FOR 33,000 KROGER FAMILY OF COMPANIES' ASSOCIATES, 18,000 STOP & SHOP EMPLOYEES.KROGER CO - WOULD INCUR A CHARGE TO NET EARNINGS DURING QUARTER THAT RESPECTIVE UFCW LOCALS RATIFY AGREEMENT.KROGER CO - CHARGE TO NET EARNINGS DUE TO TENTATIVE UFCW LOCALS AGREEMENT IS EXPECTED TO BE IN Q3.KROGER - EXPECTS TO PAY NATIONAL FUND WITHDRAWAL LIABILITY OF $962 MILLION TO FULFILL OBLIGATIONS FOR PAST SERVICE FOR ASSOCIATES, RETIREES IN NATIONAL FUND.KROGER CO - WOULD ALSO MAKE A $27 MILLION CONTRIBUTION TO A TRANSITION RESERVE IN NEW VARIABLE ANNUITY PENSION PLAN.
