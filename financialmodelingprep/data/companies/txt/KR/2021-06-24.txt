KROGER BOARD OF DIRECTORS RAISES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND 17 PERCENT. CINCINNATI, JUNE 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO.'S (NYSE: KR) BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A DIVIDEND INCREASE FROM 72 TO 84 CENTS PER YEAR. THE NEXT QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 21 CENTS PER SHARE WILL BE PAID ON SEPTEMBER 1, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 13, 2021.
