THE KROGER CO. ZERO HUNGER | ZERO WASTE FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES SECOND INNOVATION FUND COHORT. CINCINNATI, MAY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) ZERO HUNGER | ZERO WASTE FOUNDATION ("FOUNDATION") TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SECOND COHORT OF ITS INNOVATION FUND ("FUND"). FROM A POOL OF MORE THAN 145 APPLICANTS, THE FUND SELECTED 10 STARTUPS TO RECEIVE A TOTAL OF $2.5 MILLION IN FUNDING TO LAUNCH INNOVATIVE NEW CONSUMER PRODUCTS MADE WITH SURPLUS FOOD OR FOOD BYPRODUCTS AND TECHNOLOGIES TO ADVANCE THE UPCYCLED FOOD INDUSTRY.
