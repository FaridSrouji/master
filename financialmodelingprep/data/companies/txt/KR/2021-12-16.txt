KROGER FULFILLMENT NETWORK EXPANDS IN INDIANAPOLIS AREA. INDIANAPOLIS, DEC. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), AMERICA'S LARGEST GROCERY RETAILER, TODAY ANNOUNCED AN EXPANSION OF ITS KROGER FULFILLMENT NETWORK. THE ADDITION OF A DELIVERY "SPOKE" BRINGS INNOVATION AND MODERN E-COMMERCE TO THE INDIANAPOLIS AREA, EXTENDING THE GROCER'S REACH AND ABILITY TO PROVIDE ITS CUSTOMERS ANYTHING, ANYTIME, ANYWHERE.
