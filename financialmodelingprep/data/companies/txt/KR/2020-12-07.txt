KROGER SUED FOR $85 MILLION BY K7 DESIGN GROUP. NEW YORK, DEC. 7, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- K7 DESIGN GROUP, A NEW YORK-BASED FAMILY-OWNED BUSINESS, FILED AN $85 MILLION LAWSUIT ON DECEMBER 2, 2020 AGAINST THE KROGER COMPANY, THE NATION'S LARGEST GROCERY RETAILER, OVER KROGER'S FAILURE TO TAKE DELIVERY OF HAND SANITIZER THAT KROGER AGREED...
