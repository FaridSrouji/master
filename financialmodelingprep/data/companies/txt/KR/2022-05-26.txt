KROGER #25 ON AXIOS HARRIS POLL 100 RANKING OF MOST VISIBLE U.S. COMPANIES. CINCINNATI , MAY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT RANKED #25 ON THE 2022 AXIOS HARRIS POLL 100, AN ANNUAL RANKING OF THE REPUTATIONS OF THE MOST VISIBLE U.S. COMPANIES. "AS THE NATION'S LARGEST GROCER, KROGER IS CHANGING THE WAY AMERICA EATS," SAID RODNEY MCMULLEN, KROGER'S CHAIRMAN AND CEO.
