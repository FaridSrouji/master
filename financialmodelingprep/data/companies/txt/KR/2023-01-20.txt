KROGER'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CINCINNATI , JAN. 20, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO.'S (NYSE: KR) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 26 CENTS PER SHARE TO BE PAID ON MARCH 1, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 15, 2023. THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY DIVIDEND HAS GROWN AT A 14% COMPOUNDED ANNUAL GROWTH RATE SINCE IT WAS REINSTATED IN 2006.
