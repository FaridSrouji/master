KROGER DELIVERY EXPANDS TO SOUTH FLORIDA. GROCER OPENS NEW SPOKE ALSO SERVING MIAMI, FLORIDA AND SURROUNDING AREAS -- CELEBRATES 90 NEW ASSOCIATES MIAMI , FEB. 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), AMERICA'S GROCER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF A NEW SPOKE FACILITY IN SOUTH FLORIDA. JOINED BY THE LOCAL COMMUNITY, KROGER CELEBRATED THE EXPANSION OF THE DELIVERY SERVICE AND WELCOMED 90 NEW ASSOCIATES.
