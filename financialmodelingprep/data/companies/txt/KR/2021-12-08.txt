KROGER FULFILLMENT NETWORK EXPANDS WITH CUSTOMER FULFILLMENT CENTER IN NORTH CAROLINA. RALEIGH, N.C., DEC. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), AMERICA'S LARGEST GROCERY RETAILER, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL OFFER MORE AMERICANS DELIVERY THROUGH THE ADDITION OF A NEW CUSTOMER FULFILLMENT CENTER (CFC) IN NORTH CAROLINA, POWERED BY THE OCADO GROUP (LSE: OCDO) AND COMBINING VERTICAL INTEGRATION, MACHINE LEARNING, AND ROBOTICS TO PROVIDE AN AFFORDABLE, FRIENDLY, AND FAST FRESH FOOD DELIVERY SERVICE AS PART OF THE COMPANY'S SEAMLESS ECOSYSTEM.
