KROGER ANNOUNCES 2021 GO FRESH & LOCAL SUPPLIER ACCELERATOR COHORT. CINCINNATI, AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INAUGURAL GO FRESH & LOCAL SUPPLIER ACCELERATOR COHORT – MAAZAH, NONA LIM, QUEEN CHARLOTTE'S PIMENTO CHEESE ROYALE, SIMPLY SOUTHERN SIDES, AND SOUPERGIRL. "WE INVENTED THE GO FRESH & LOCAL SUPPLIER ACCELERATOR TO DISCOVER THE BEST, MOST INNOVATIVE, AND FRESHEST LOCAL AND REGIONAL PRODUCTS – AND OUR GROUP OF WINNERS IS JUST THAT," SAID DAN DE LA ROSA, KROGER'S GROUP VICE PRESIDENT OF FRESH MERCHANDISING.
