KROGER ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGES. CINCINNATI, JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RETIREMENT OF STEVE MCKINNEY, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF RETAIL DIVISIONS, AFTER MORE THAN 40 YEARS OF DISTINGUISHED SERVICE, EFFECTIVE AUGUST 7. MCKINNEY WILL BE SUCCEEDED BY VALERIE JABBAR, CURRENTLY GROUP VICE PRESIDENT OF CENTER STORE MERCHANDISING.
