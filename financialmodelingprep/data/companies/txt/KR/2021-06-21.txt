INSPIRING HEALTHY LIVES: THE WELLNESS EXPERIENCE BY KROGER AND CO-FOUNDER JEWEL RETURNS AUGUST 20-21, 2021 IN CINCINNATI, OH. CINCINNATI, JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INCLUSION COMPANIES LLC, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), ANNOUNCED TODAY PLANS TO HOST THE WELLNESS EXPERIENCE BY KROGER AND CO-FOUNDER AND GRAMMY-NOMINATED SINGER-SONGWRITER JEWEL. THE THIRD ANNUAL WELLNESS FESTIVAL WILL TAKE PLACE AUGUST 20-21, 2021 AT THE BANKS IN DOWNTOWN CINCINNATI, OH.
