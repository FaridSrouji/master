AXIOS HARRIS POLL NAMES KROGER ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST VISIBLE & TRUSTED COMPANIES. CINCINNATI , MAY 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INCLUSION AMONG THE 2023 AXIOS HARRIS POLL 100, AN ANNUAL RANKING OF THE REPUTATIONS OF THE MOST VISIBLE COMPANIES IN THE U.S. "WE DO NOT TAKE FOR GRANTED THE TRUST AMERICA PLACES IN US TO PROVIDE FAMILIES WITH FRESH, AFFORDABLE FOOD AND TO SUPPORT HEALTHY, THRIVING COMMUNITIES," SAID RODNEY MCMULLEN, KROGER'S CHAIRMAN AND CEO. "THIS IS RECOGNITION OF OUR INCREDIBLE ASSOCIATES WHO DELIVER OUR CUSTOMERS A FRESH AND FRIENDLY SHOPPING EXPERIENCE AND AFFORDABLE PRICES, EACH AND EVERY DAY.
