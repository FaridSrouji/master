KROGER NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST TRUSTWORTHY COMPANIES. GROCER RECOGNIZED FOR ITS CUSTOMER, INVESTOR AND ASSOCIATE TRUST SCORES FOR SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR CINCINNATI , MARCH 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), AMERICA'S GROCER, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY NEWSWEEK AS ONE OF "AMERICA'S MOST TRUSTWORTHY COMPANIES" FOR 2023, EARNING THE DISTINCTION FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR. THE AWARD RECOGNIZES COMPANIES ACROSS THREE MAIN PUBLIC PILLARS OF TRUST – CUSTOMER TRUST, INVESTOR TRUST AND ASSOCIATE TRUST.
