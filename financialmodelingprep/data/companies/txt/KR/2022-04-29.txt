KROGER HEALTH REDUCES BARRIERS TO COVID-19 TREATMENT OPTIONS NATIONWIDE. RETAILER USES STORE PHARMACIES, CLINICS, AND TELEHEALTH TO INCREASE ACCESS TO CARE CINCINNATI , APRIL 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KROGER HEALTH, THE HEALTHCARE DIVISION OF THE KROGER CO. FAMILY OF COMPANIES (NYSE: KR), URGES AMERICANS TO REMAIN DILIGENT IN SEEKING PROPER TESTING AND CARE FOR COVID-19. IN ADDITION TO ADMINISTERING 11 MILLION DOSES OF COVID-19 VACCINES TO DATE, THE COMPANY IS SUPPORTING THE BIDEN-HARRIS ADMINISTRATION'S "TEST TO TREAT" INITIATIVE FOR PATIENTS EXHIBITING SYMPTOMS OF COVID-19 AT ITS 225 THE LITTLE CLINIC LOCATIONS IN ARIZONA, COLORADO, GEORGIA, INDIANA, KANSAS, KENTUCKY, TENNESSEE, OHIO, AND VIRGINIA.
