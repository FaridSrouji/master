KROGER PROMOTES LAURA GUMP TO HOUSTON DIVISION PRESIDENT. HOUSTON, DEC. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. ANNOUNCED TODAY LAURA GUMP, FORMERLY VICE PRESIDENT OF MERCHANDISING FOR THE HOUSTON DIVISION, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO PRESIDENT OF THE DIVISION. SHE SUCCEEDS JOE KELLEY WHO WAS RECENTLY NAMED PRESIDENT OF KING SOOPERS.
