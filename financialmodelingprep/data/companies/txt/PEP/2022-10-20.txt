PEPSICO ANNOUNCES SECOND ROUND OF PROJECTS FUNDED BY ITS GLOBAL AGRICULTURE ACCELERATOR, THE POSITIVE AGRICULTURE OUTCOMES FUND. LAUNCHES NEW ONLINE SERIES – "GROWING OUR FUTURE" – SHOWCASING GAME-CHANGING REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE INITIATIVES PURCHASE, N.Y. , OCT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PEPSICO (NASDAQ: PEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CONTINUATION OF ITS GLOBAL AGRICULTURE ACCELERATOR, THE POSITIVE AGRICULTURE OUTCOMES (PAO) FUND, BY GRANTING 14 BUSINESS PROJECTS ACROSS 11 COUNTRIES FUNDING TO ADDRESS SOME OF THE MOST INTRACTABLE CHALLENGES FACING AGRICULTURE TODAY.
