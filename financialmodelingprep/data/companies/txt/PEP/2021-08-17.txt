PEPSICO ANNOUNCES "NET WATER POSITIVE" COMMITMENT. PURCHASE, N.Y., AUG. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, PEPSICO, INC. (NASDAQ:PEP) ANNOUNCED ITS AMBITION TO BECOME "NET WATER POSITIVE" BY 2030,  AIMING TO REPLENISH MORE WATER THAN THE COMPANY USES, WHICH WOULD PLACE IT AMONG THE MOST WATER-EFFICIENT FOOD OR BEVERAGE MANUFACTURERS OPERATING IN HIGH-RISK WATERSHEDS.
