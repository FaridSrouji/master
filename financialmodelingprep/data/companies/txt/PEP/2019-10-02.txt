PEPSICO, INC. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 7, 2019. PEPSICO, INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 7, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 17,188 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 16,485 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 2,855 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,844 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 2,100 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,498 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.5 COMPARED TO USD 1.77 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.49 COMPARED TO USD 1.75 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 46,521 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 45,137 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 7,592 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 7,679 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 5,548 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 5,661 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 3.96 COMPARED TO USD 3.99 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 3.94 COMPARED TO USD 3.97 A YEAR AGO.
