SAVORING THE SUMMER: U.S. SNACK INDEX REVEALS KEY INSIGHTS FOR A WORRY-FREE AND MEMORABLE SEASON. DATA FROM FRITO-LAY AND QUAKER SHOW SNACKING IS CRITICAL IN REDUCING THE STRESS OF LONG SUMMER TRIPS – AND MORE IMPORTANT THAN WINNING OR CLEAN BATHROOMS PLANO, TEXAS , MAY 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS TEMPERATURES RISE AND AMERICANS EAGERLY ANTICIPATE THE PLEASURES OF SUMMERTIME, FRITO-LAY AND QUAKER TODAY RELEASED THE FIRST-EVER JOINT U.S. SUMMER SNACK INDEX, SHEDDING LIGHT ON CONSUMER PREFERENCES AND HABITS TO ENSURE UNFORGETTABLE MEMORIES THIS SEASON. "FOOD IS AN IMPORTANT CENTERPIECE FOR THE JOY THAT SUMMERTIME BRINGS," SAID DENISE LEFEBVRE, SVP, PEPSICO FOODS R&D.
