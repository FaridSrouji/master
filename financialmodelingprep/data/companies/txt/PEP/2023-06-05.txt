GATORADE UNVEILS NEW SPORTS SCIENCE LAB FOR EXTENSIVE ATHLETE RESEARCH AND NEW PRODUCT INNOVATION. THE STATE-OF-THE-ART GATORADE SPORTS SCIENCE INSTITUTE JOINS PEPSICO'S R&D CENTER OF EXCELLENCE IN VALHALLA, NY TO STRENGTHEN A WORLD-CLASS CAMPUS FOR END-TO-END INNOVATION VALHALLA, N.Y. , JUNE 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GATORADE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF ITS NEWEST GATORADE SPORTS SCIENCE INSTITUTE (GSSI) RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT LAB IN VALHALLA, NEW YORK, PROVIDING UNPARALLELED OPPORTUNITY FOR INNOVATION AND DISCOVERY THAT BUILDS UPON THE STRENGTH AND HISTORY OF GATORADE'S BACKED-BY-SCIENCE APPROACH.
