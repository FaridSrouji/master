PEPSICO LAUNCHES $50 MILLION JUNTOS CRECEMOS PLATFORM TO SUPPORT HISPANIC-OWNED BUSINESSES ACROSS THE U.S.. PURCHASE, N.Y., AUG. 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PEPSICO, INC. (NASDAQ:PEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED JUNTOS CRECEMOS, (TOGETHER WE GROW), A $50 MILLION PLATFORM AIMED AT STRENGTHENING HISPANIC-OWNED BUSINESSES, SPECIFICALLY RESTAURANTS, BODEGAS AND CARNICERÍAS (MEAT MARKETS), ADDRESSING FOUNDATIONAL BUSINESS CHALLENGES, AND SUPPORTING BUSINESS GROWTH OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS.
