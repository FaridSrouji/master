RUFFLES® ENCOURAGES FANS TO OWN THEIR RIDGES WITH THE RELEASE OF THE ULTRA-CUSTOM ARMORED RIDGE TOPS SNEAKER. PLANO, TEXAS, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RUFFLES®, OFFICIAL CHIP OF THE NBA AND ONE OF THE MARQUEE BRANDS FROM PEPSICO'S FRITO-LAY DIVISION, THIS WEEK RELEASED ITS SECOND SIGNATURE SHOE, THE RUFFLES® ARMORED RIDGE TOPS, JUST IN TIME FOR THE NBA PLAYOFFS. BRAND PARTNER AND RAPPER T-PAIN WAS SEEN COURTSIDE AT SUNDAY'S UTAH JAZZ PLAYOFF GAME ROCKING HIS PAIR OF THE SUPER-EXCLUSIVE AND COMPLETELY CUSTOM SNEAKER.
