SYDNEY THORVALDSON NAMED 2020-21 GATORADE® NATIONAL GIRLS CROSS COUNTRY PLAYER OF THE YEAR. RAWLINS, WYO., APRIL 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN ITS 36TH YEAR OF HONORING THE NATION'S MOST ELITE HIGH SCHOOL ATHLETES, THE GATORADE COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED SYDNEY THORVALDSON OF RAWLINS HIGH SCHOOL IN RAWLINS, WYO.
