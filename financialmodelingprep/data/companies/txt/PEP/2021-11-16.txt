PEPSICO ANNOUNCES A NEW GOAL TO ADVANCE FOOD SECURITY FOR 50 MILLION PEOPLE BY 2030. PEPSICO EXPANDS FOOD FOR GOOD PLATFORM GLOBALLY, PLEDGES TO GROW AFFORDABLE NUTRITIOUS OPTIONS FROM BRANDS INCLUDING QUAKER PURCHASE, N.Y., NOV. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, PEPSICO (NASDAQ: PEP) ANNOUNCED A NEW GOAL TO HELP 50 MILLION PEOPLE GAIN READY ACCESS TO NUTRITIOUS FOODS BY 2030 (2020 BASELINE) THROUGH ITS FOOD FOR GOOD FOOD SECURITY PROGRAM AND THE EXPANSION OF PEPSICO'S AFFORDABLE NUTRITION OFFERINGS AS PART OF ITS PEPSICO POSITIVE (PEP+) JOURNEY.
