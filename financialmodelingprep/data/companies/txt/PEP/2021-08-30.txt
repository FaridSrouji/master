FRITO-LAY AND FEED THE CHILDREN PROVIDE CRITICAL FOOD ITEMS TO STUDENTS THROUGHOUT 2021-2022 SCHOOL YEAR. PLANO, TEXAS, AUG. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AT THE HEIGHT OF THE PANDEMIC, ONE IN FOUR CHILDREN WERE FOOD INSECURE. ALTHOUGH THIS NUMBER IS IMPROVING, THE ONGOING COVID-19 PANDEMIC CONTINUES TO AFFECT CHILDREN ACROSS THE U.S. 1 TO SUPPORT STUDENTS IN UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES, FRITO-LAY AND FEED THE CHILDREN ARE KICKING OFF THE SECOND YEAR OF THE "BUILDING THE FUTURE TOGETHER" INITIATIVE IN EIGHT CITIES, REACHING NEARLY 1,200 SCHOOLS WITH FOOD AND ESSENTIAL ITEMS.
