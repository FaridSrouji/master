FRITO-LAY AND QUAKER OPEN NEW INDUSTRY-LEADING GREENHOUSE LEARNING CENTER TO FAST-TRACK SUSTAINABLE PACKAGING INNOVATION. PLANO, TEXAS , APRIL 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FRITO-LAY AND QUAKER TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF AN INDUSTRY-LEADING GREENHOUSE LEARNING CENTER AT ITS R&D HEADQUARTERS. THE LEARNING CENTER WILL BE USED TO FIELD TEST, MEASURE, AND ANALYZE COMPOSTABLE PACKAGING WITH AN AIM TO SPEED UP THE RATE OF INNOVATION.
