AMERICAN AIRLINES GROUP INC SAYS EXPECTS TO PAY $4.3 BILLION IN CAPEX IN 2019. AMERICAN AIRLINES GROUP INC - EXPECTS TO PAY $4.3 BILLION IN CAPEX IN 2019, INCLUDING $2.6 BILLION IN AIRCRAFT AND $1.7 BILLION IN NON-AIRCRAFT CAPEX.AMERICAN AIRLINES - IN 2020, SEES TOTAL CAPEX TO DECLINE BY ABOUT $600 MILLION WITH AIRCRAFT CAPEX SPEND OF $2.0 BILLION, NON-AIRCRAFT CAPEX SPEND OF $1.7 BILLION.AMERICAN AIRLINES - FOR 2021, SEES CAPEX FALLING BY $1.5 BILLION WITH AIRCRAFT CAPEX SPEND EXPECTED TO BE $0.9 BILLION & NON-AIRCRAFT CAPEX EXPECTED TO BE $1.2 BILLION.AMERICAN AIRLINES GROUP INC - UPDATED DELIVERY SCHEDULE FOR THE REMAINDER OF 2019 BY MOVING 11 BOEING 737 MAX AIRCRAFT FROM 2019 INTO 2020.
