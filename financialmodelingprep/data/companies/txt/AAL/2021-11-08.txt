AMERICAN AIRLINES FAILS IN ATTEMPT TO STOP FLIGHT ATTENDANT'S SEXUAL ASSAULT CLAIM. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, NOV. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AN AMERICAN AIRLINES (NASDAQ:AAL) FLIGHT ATTENDANT WHO SAYS SHE WAS SEXUALLY ASSAULTED BY A CELEBRITY CHEF HIRED BY THE AIRLINE WILL GET THE CHANCE TO TELL HER STORY TO A JURY, FOLLOWING A KEY RULING BY A TARRANT COUNTY DISTRICT COURT JUDGE. THE RULING, BY JUDGE KIMBERLY FITZPATRICK, REJECTS ALL PORTIONS OF A MOTION FOR SUMMARY JUDGMENT FILED BY AMERICAN THAT SOUGHT TO AVOID ALLOWING A JURY TO HEAR THE CASE.
