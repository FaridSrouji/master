ETSY ANNOUNCES THE APPOINTMENT OF ANDY BALLARD TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. BROOKLYN, N.Y., SEPT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ETSY, INC. (NASDAQ: ETSY), WHICH OPERATES TWO-SIDED ONLINE MARKETPLACES THAT CONNECT MILLIONS OF PASSIONATE AND CREATIVE BUYERS AND SELLERS AROUND THE WORLD, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED ANDY BALLARD TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD, EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 30, 2021.
