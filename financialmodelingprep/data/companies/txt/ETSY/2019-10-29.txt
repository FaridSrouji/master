ETSY, INC. REVISES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2019. ETSY, INC. HAS UPDATED ITS 2019 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE TO INCORPORATE THE IMPACT OF REVERB'S BUSINESS FOR THE PERIOD FROM AUGUST 15-DECEMBER 31, 2019. THE COMPANY EXPECTS REVENUE YEAR OVER YEAR GROWTH OF 34-35%, THAT IS $809 MILLION TO $815 MILLION AGAINST PREVIOUS GUIDANCE OF 32-34%, THAT IS $797 MILLION TO $809 MILLION.
