ETSY COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF DEPOP, THE GLOBAL FASHION RESALE MARKETPLACE FOR GEN Z. BROOKLYN, N.Y., JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ETSY, INC. (NASDAQ: ETSY) (THE "COMPANY"), WHICH OPERATES TWO-SIDED ONLINE MARKETPLACES THAT CONNECT MILLIONS OF PASSIONATE AND CREATIVE BUYERS AND SELLERS AROUND THE WORLD, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF ITS PREVIOUSLY-ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF DEPOP FOR APPROXIMATELY $1.625 BILLION CONSISTING OF PRIMARILY CASH AND SUBJECT TO CERTAIN ADJUSTMENTS.
