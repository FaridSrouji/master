ETSY ANNOUNCES PROPOSED PRIVATE OFFERING OF $1.0 BILLION OF CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES. BROOKLYN, N.Y., JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ETSY, INC. (NASDAQ: ETSY), WHICH OPERATES TWO-SIDED ONLINE MARKETPLACES THAT CONNECT MILLIONS OF PASSIONATE AND CREATIVE BUYERS AND SELLERS AROUND THE WORLD, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT INTENDS TO OFFER, SUBJECT TO MARKET CONDITIONS AND OTHER FACTORS, $1.0 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (THE "NOTES") IN A PRIVATE PLACEMENT TO QUALIFIED INSTITUTIONAL BUYERS PURSUANT TO RULE 144A UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED (THE "SECURITIES ACT").
