NORWEGIAN CRUISE LINE HOLDINGS ANNOUNCES EXTENSION OF EXISTING UNDRAWN $1 BILLION COMMITMENT. MIAMI, JULY 29, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORWEGIAN CRUISE LINE HOLDINGS LTD. (NYSE: NCLH) ( “NORWEGIAN”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS SUBSIDIARY, NCL CORPORATION LTD. (THE “COMPANY”), HAS AMENDED ITS EXISTING $1 BILLION COMMITMENT WITH FUNDS MANAGED BY AFFILIATES OF APOLLO GLOBAL MANAGEMENT, INC. (“APOLLO”) AND EXTENDED IT THROUGH MARCH 31, 2023, PROVIDING ADDITIONAL LIQUIDITY TO THE COMPANY. THE COMPANY HAS NOT DRAWN AND DOES NOT CURRENTLY INTEND TO DRAW UNDER THIS COMMITMENT.
