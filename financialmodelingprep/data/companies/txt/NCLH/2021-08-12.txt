OCEANIA CRUISES REVEALS NEW RESTAURANTS AND CULINARY EXPERIENCES. MIAMI, AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OCEANIA CRUISES, THE WORLD'S LEADING CULINARY- AND DESTINATION-FOCUSED CRUISE LINE, REVEALED THE RESTAURANTS AND DINING EXPERIENCES ABOARD ITS NEWEST SHIP, VISTA, DUE TO DEBUT IN EARLY 2023. "VISTA WILL PRESENT A FRESH PERSPECTIVE ON THE FINEST CUISINE AT SEA WITH AN ASTOUNDING ARRAY OF DINING OPTIONS THAT RANGE FROM INFORMAL TO THE EXTRAVAGANT," STATED BOB BINDER, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF OCEANIA CRUISES.
