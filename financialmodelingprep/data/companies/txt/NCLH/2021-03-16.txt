OCEANIA CRUISES WELCOMES "VISTA" TO ITS ACCLAIMED FLEET. MIAMI, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  OCEANIA CRUISES , THE WORLD'S LEADING CULINARY- AND DESTINATION-FOCUSED CRUISE LINE, INTRODUCED VISTA  TO THE WORLD TODAY, NAMING THE FIRST OF TWO NEW 1,200-GUEST ALLURA CLASS SHIPS THAT ARE BEING CONSTRUCTED BY RENOWNED ITALIAN SHIPBUILDER FINCANTIERI S.P.A. THE LUXURY VESSEL, THE SEVENTH FOR THE LINE, WILL SET SAIL IN 2023, FOLLOWED BY A SISTER SHIP IN 2025.
