OCEANIA CRUISES ANNOUNCES TWO MORE BETTER-THAN-NEW SHIPS. RIVIERA AND MARINA TO UNDERGO STEM-TO-STERN RE-INSPIRATION IN 2022 AND 2023 REBUILD WILL YIELD 1,210 NEW SUITES AND STATEROOMS AND TRANSFORMED PUBLIC SPACES MIAMI , APRIL 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OCEANIA CRUISES, THE WORLD'S LEADING CULINARY- AND DESTINATION-FOCUSED CRUISE LINE, ANNOUNCED DETAILS OF ITS MOST AMBITIOUS OCEANIANEXT EXPERIENCE ELEVATION INITIATIVE TO DATE WITH THE STEM-TO-STERN RE-INSPIRATION OF THE 1,210-GUEST SHIPS RIVIERA AND MARINA, TWO OF THE MOST ACCLAIMED SHIPS IN MODERN HISTORY. WITH THE ADVENT OF RIVIERA'S AND MARINA'S RE-INSPIRATION, OCEANIA CRUISES WILL, FOR THE FIRST TIME, FIELD A FLEET ENTIRELY COMPOSED OF BRAND-NEW OR BETTER-THAN-NEW SHIPS IN EVERY DESTINATION ACROSS THE GLOBE.
