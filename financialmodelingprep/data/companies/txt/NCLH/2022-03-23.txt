OCEANIA CRUISES APPOINTS FRANK DEL RIO JR. AS CHIEF SALES & MARKETING OFFICER. INDUSTRY VETERAN AND BRAND CO-FOUNDER TO PROVIDE GLOBAL VISION AND STRATEGIC DIRECTION MIAMI , MARCH 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OCEANIA CRUISES, THE WORLD'S LEADING CULINARY- AND DESTINATION-FOCUSED CRUISE LINE, ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF FRANK DEL RIO JR. AS CHIEF SALES & MARKETING OFFICER FOR THE BRAND. AS CHIEF SALES & MARKETING OFFICER, DEL RIO WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR ALL SALES AND MARKETING ACTIVITIES ACROSS THE GLOBE AND LEAD THE TEAMS BASED IN THE BRAND'S OFFICES IN MIAMI, SOUTHAMPTON, SYDNEY, AND SAO PAULO.
