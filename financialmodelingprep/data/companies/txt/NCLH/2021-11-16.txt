NORWEGIAN CRUISE LINE HOLDINGS LTD. ANNOUNCES PRICING OF 46,858,854 ORDINARY SHARES. MIAMI, NOV. 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORWEGIAN CRUISE LINE HOLDINGS LTD. (NYSE: NCLH) (THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED ITS REGISTERED DIRECT OFFERING OF 46,858,854 ORDINARY SHARES OF THE COMPANY (THE “OFFERING”) TO CERTAIN HOLDERS OF NCL CORPORATION LTD.'S (“NCLC”), A SUBSIDIARY OF THE COMPANY, 6.00% EXCHANGEABLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2024 AT A PRICE OF $23.64 PER SHARE.
