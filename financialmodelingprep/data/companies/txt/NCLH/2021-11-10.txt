OCEANIA CRUISES REVEALS NEXT PHASE OF OCEANIANEXT ENHANCEMENTS. MIAMI, NOV. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OCEANIA CRUISES, THE WORLD'S LEADING CULINARY- AND DESTINATION-FOCUSED CRUISE LINE, HAS ANNOUNCED THE SECOND PHASE OF CULINARY ENHANCEMENTS ROLLING OUT ACROSS THE LUXURIOUS SMALL-SHIP FLEET AS PART OF ITS OCEANIANEXT PROGRAM. FOLLOWING THE MAY INTRODUCTION OF NEW THOUGHTFULLY CRAFTED DINING EXPERIENCES, MENUS AND ELEVATED SERVICE LEVELS, THIS NEXT PHASE BRINGS NEW WINE TASTING PROGRAMS, EXPANDED IN-ROOM DINING OFFERINGS AND A SOUFFLÉ OF THE DAY IN THE GRAND DINING ROOM.
