OCEANIA CRUISES INTRODUCES 2024-2025 TROPICS & EXOTICS VOYAGES. MIAMI , OCT. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OCEANIA CRUISES, THE WORLD'S LEADING CULINARY- AND DESTINATION-FOCUSED CRUISE LINE, UNVEILED ITS 2024-2025 TROPICS AND EXOTICS COLLECTION OF ITINERARIES, WHICH OPEN FOR SALE ON NOVEMBER 2, 2022. THE NEW COLLECTION OF 157 VOYAGES SPANS SEVEN CONTINENTS AND RANGES FROM 7 TO 200 DAYS IN LENGTH.
