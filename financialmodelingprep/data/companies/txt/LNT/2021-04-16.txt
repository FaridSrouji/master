ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION ANNOUNCES FIRST QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS RELEASE AND CONFERENCE CALL. MADISON, WIS., APRIL 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LNT) HAS SCHEDULED ITS FIRST QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS RELEASE FOR THURSDAY, MAY 6TH, AFTER MARKET CLOSE. A CONFERENCE CALL TO REVIEW THE FIRST QUARTER RESULTS IS SCHEDULED FOR FRIDAY, MAY 7TH AT 9 A.M. CT.
