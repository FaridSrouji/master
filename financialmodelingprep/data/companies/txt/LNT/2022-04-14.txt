ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. MADISON, WIS., APRIL 14, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LNT) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.4275 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON MAY 16, 2022, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 29, 2022.
