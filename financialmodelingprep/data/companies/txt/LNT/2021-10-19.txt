ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. MADISON, WIS., OCT. 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LNT) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.4025 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 15, 2021, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 1, 2021.
