ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. MADISON, WIS., JULY 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LNT) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.4025 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON AUGUST 16, 2021, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 30, 2021.
IN NEW CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY REPORT, ALLIANT ENERGY ANNOUNCES PROGRESS TOWARD CLEAN ENERGY VISION. REPORT ALSO HIGHLIGHTS NEW 1 MILLION TREES PLANTING COMMITMENT REPORT ALSO HIGHLIGHTS NEW 1 MILLION TREES PLANTING COMMITMENT
