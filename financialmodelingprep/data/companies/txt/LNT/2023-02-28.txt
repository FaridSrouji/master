ALLIANT ENERGY PRICES OFFERING OF $500 MILLION OF 3.875% CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2026. MADISON, WIS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LNT) ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 3.875% CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2026 IN A PRIVATE PLACEMENT UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED (THE “SECURITIES ACT”).
