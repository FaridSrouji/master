VERTEX ANNOUNCES PRIMARY ENDPOINT ACHIEVED IN PHASE 2 STUDY OF VX-864 IN ALPHA-1 ANTITRYPSIN DEFICIENCY. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VERTEX PHARMACEUTICALS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: VRTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IN A PHASE 2 PROOF-OF-CONCEPT STUDY, VX-864 ACHIEVED RAPID, CONSISTENT AND STATISTICALLY SIGNIFICANT INCREASES IN MEAN FUNCTIONAL ALPHA-1 ANTITRYPSIN (FAAT) LEVELS OF 2.2 TO 2.3 MICROMOLAR FROM BASELINE IN PEOPLE WITH ALPHA-1 ANTITRYPSIN DEFICIENCY (AATD) WITH THE PIZZ GENOTYPE, ACROSS THREE DOSE GROUPS OF VX-864 COMPARED TO PLACEBO.
