VERTEX AND CRISPR THERAPEUTICS ANNOUNCE GLOBAL EXA-CEL REGULATORY SUBMISSIONS FOR SICKLE CELL DISEASE AND BETA THALASSEMIA IN 2022. BOSTON & ZUG, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VERTEX PHARMACEUTICALS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: VRTX) AND CRISPR THERAPEUTICS (NASDAQ: CRSP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT VERTEX HAS CONCLUDED DISCUSSIONS WITH THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA), AND THE FDA GRANTED EXAGAMGLOGENE AUTOTEMCEL (EXA-CEL) A ROLLING REVIEW FOR THE POTENTIAL TREATMENT OF SICKLE CELL DISEASE (SCD) AND TRANSFUSION-DEPENDENT BETA THALASSEMIA (TDT).
