VERTEX ANNOUNCES NEW PORTFOLIO REIMBURSEMENT AGREEMENT IN ITALY INCLUDING KAFTRIO®, SYMKEVI® AND ADDITIONAL INDICATIONS OF ORKAMBI® AND KALYDECO® FOR ELIGIBLE PATIENTS WITH CYSTIC FIBROSIS. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VERTEX PHARMACEUTICALS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: VRTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS REACHED A NEW PORTFOLIO AGREEMENT WITH THE ITALIAN MEDICINES AGENCY, AIFA, FOR THE REIMBURSEMENT OF ALL OF VERTEX'S APPROVED MEDICINES FOR THE TREATMENT OF CYSTIC FIBROSIS (CF), INCLUDING KAFTRIO® (IVACAFTOR/TEZACAFTOR/ELEXACAFTOR) IN A COMBINATION REGIMEN WITH IVACAFTOR. ITALIAN PATIENTS AGES 12 YEARS AND OLDER WITH ONE F508DEL MUTATION AND ONE MINIMAL FUNCTION MUTATION (F/MF) OR TWO F508DEL MUTA
