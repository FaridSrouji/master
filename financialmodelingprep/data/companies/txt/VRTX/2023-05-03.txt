VERTEX ANNOUNCES U.S. FDA APPROVAL FOR KALYDECO® (IVACAFTOR) TO TREAT ELIGIBLE INFANTS WITH CF AGES 1 MONTH AND OLDER. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VERTEX PHARMACEUTICALS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: VRTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) APPROVED KALYDECO® (IVACAFTOR) FOR USE IN CHILDREN WITH CYSTIC FIBROSIS (CF) AGES 1 MONTH TO LESS THAN FOUR MONTHS OLD WHO HAVE AT LEAST ONE MUTATION IN THEIR CYSTIC FIBROSIS TRANSMEMBRANE CONDUCTANCE REGULATOR (CFTR) GENE THAT IS RESPONSIVE TO KALYDECO® BASED ON CLINICAL AND/OR IN VITRO ASSAY DATA. KALYDECO® IS ALREADY APPROVED IN THE U.S. AND EU FOR THE TREAT.
