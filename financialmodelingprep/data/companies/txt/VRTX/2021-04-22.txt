VERTEX AND OBSIDIAN THERAPEUTICS ESTABLISH COLLABORATION TO DISCOVER NOVEL THERAPIES THAT REGULATE GENE EDITING. BOSTON & CAMBRIDGE, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VERTEX AND OBSIDIAN THERAPEUTICS ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC RESEARCH COLLABORATION AND LICENSING AGREEMENT FOR NOVEL THERAPIES TO REGULATE GENE EDITING.
