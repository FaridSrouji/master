VERTEX AND CRISPR THERAPEUTICS ANNOUNCE ACCEPTANCE OF LATE-BREAKING ABSTRACT FOR CTX001™ AT THE 2022 ANNUAL EUROPEAN HEMATOLOGY ASSOCIATION (EHA) CONGRESS. VERTEX ALSO ANNOUNCED THREE ABSTRACTS ACCEPTED FOR POSTER PRESENTATION AT EHA.
