VERTEX PHARMACEUTICALS INC REPORTS QUARTERLY REVENUE OF $949.8 MILLION. VERTEX REPORTS THIRD-QUARTER 2019 FINANCIAL RESULTS.QTRLY PRODUCT REVENUES OF $950 MILLION, A 21% INCREASE COMPARED TO Q3 2018.QTRLY GAAP NET INCOME PER SHARE $0.22.QTRLY NON-GAAP NET INCOME PER SHARE $1.23.REITERATED ITS FULL-YEAR 2019 TOTAL PRODUCT REVENUE GUIDANCE.QTRLY REVENUE $949.8 MILLION VERSUS $784.5 MILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.15, REVENUE VIEW $950.9 MILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES FY 2019 COMBINED GAAP RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT AND SG&A EXPENSES $2.35 BILLION TO 2.45 BILLION.
