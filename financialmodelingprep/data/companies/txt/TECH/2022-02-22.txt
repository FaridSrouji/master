BIO-TECHNE ANNOUNCES EXCLUSIVE DEVELOPMENT AND LICENSE AGREEMENT WITH THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC FOR EXOTRU® KIDNEY TRANSPLANT REJECTION ASSAY. MINNEAPOLIS, FEB. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT WITH THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC TO EXCLUSIVELY COMPLETE DEVELOPMENT OF AND COMMERCIALIZE THE EXOTRU KIDNEY TRANSPLANT REJECTION TEST DEVELOPED BY EXOSOME DIAGNOSTICS, A BIO-TECHNE BRAND. EXOTRU IS A NON-INVASIVE MULTIGENE URINE BASED LIQUID BIOPSY ASSAY THAT PROVIDES CRITICAL ALLOGRAFT HEALTH INFORMATION TO ASSIST CLINICIAN DECISION MAKING IN MANAGING KIDNEY TRANSPLANT PATIENTS AND OPTIMIZING PATIENT CARE.
