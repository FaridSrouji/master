ASURAGEN LAUNCHES NEW KIT TO ENABLE BROADER COVERAGE OF GENE VARIANTS LINKED TO CYSTIC FIBROSIS. MINNEAPOLIS, NOV. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ:TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ASURAGEN, INC., A BIO-TECHNE BRAND, HAS LAUNCHED A NEW KIT TO DETECT PATHOGENIC VARIANTS IN THE CFTR GENE. THE AMPLIDEX® PCR/CE CFTR KIT, INTENDED FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY, PROVIDES BROADER COVERAGE OF THE DIVERSE U.S. POPULATION1,2 THAN ANY OTHER COMMERCIALLY AVAILABLE, TARGETED CFTR TESTING ASSAY.
