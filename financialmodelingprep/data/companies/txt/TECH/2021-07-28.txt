BIO-TECHNE AND PROGEN INTRODUCE NEW SIMPLE PLEX AAV2 IMMUNOASSAY. MINNEAPOLIS, JULY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) AND PROGEN TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE SIMPLE PLEX™ ADENO-ASSOCIATED VIRUS (AAV2) VIRAL TITER ASSAY FOR AAV2 TOTAL CAPSID QUANTIFICATION.  BIO-TECHNE, OWNER OF THE LEADING PROTEINSIMPLE BRANDED PROTEIN ANALYSIS PORTFOLIO OF PRODUCTS, AND PROGEN WILL OFFER THE SIMPLE PLEX AAV2 VIRAL TITER ASSAY ON ELLA™ INSTRUMENTS, ITS MULTIPLEXING IMMUNOASSAY SYSTEM.
