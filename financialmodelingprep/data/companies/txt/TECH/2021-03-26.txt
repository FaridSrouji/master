BIO-TECHNE ANNOUNCES RECEIPT OF COVID-19 INNOVATION AWARD FROM CITEAB. MINNEAPOLIS, MARCH 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS R&D SYSTEMS AND NOVUS BRANDS HAVE BEEN NAMED WINNERS OF THE CITEAB COVID-19 INNOVATION AWARD.  THIS AWARD CELEBRATES INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS OR PRODUCTS THAT HELPED SHAPE THE LIFE SCIENCES SECTORS UNDERSTANDING OF, OR RESILIENCE TO COVID-19.
