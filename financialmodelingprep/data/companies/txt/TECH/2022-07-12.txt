BIO-TECHNE LAUNCHES NEW TURBO CE-SDS™ CARTRIDGE, ACCELERATING PROTEIN PURITY ANALYSIS THROUGHPUT BY 400%. MINNEAPOLIS , JULY 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PROTEINSIMPLE, A BIO-TECHNE BRAND, HAS LAUNCHED THE INNOVATIVE MAURICE™ TURBO CE-SDS CARTRIDGE. AS THE NAME SUGGESTS, TURBO ENABLES HIGH SPEED AND HIGH RESOLUTION ANALYSIS OF PROTEIN SIZE AND PURITY ON MAURICE, THE FLAGSHIP CAPILLARY ELECTROPHORESIS (CE) INSTRUMENT OF THE ICE FAMILY.
