BIO-TECHNE ANNOUNCES OPENING OF NEW IMMUNOASSAY MANUFACTURING FACILITY. MINNEAPOLIS , NOV. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF ITS NEW 52,000 SQUARE FOOT STATE-OF-THE-ART PRODUCT INNOVATION AND MANUFACTURING FACILITY. THE EXPANSION MORE THAN DOUBLES THE OPERATIONAL FOOTPRINT AT BIO-TECHNE'S WALLINGFORD, CONNECTICUT SITE AND PROVIDES A FIVE-FOLD INCREASE IN ELLA™ IMMUNOASSAY CARTRIDGE PRODUCTION CAPACITY.
