BIO-TECHNE LAUNCHES THE MAURICEFLEX™ SYSTEM, ADDING CIEF FRACTIONATION CAPABILITY TO THE GOLD STANDARD MAURICE ASSAYS. MINNEAPOLIS , JAN. 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS PROTEINSIMPLE-BRANDED MAURICEFLEX™ DURING THE WCBP CONFERENCE IN WASHINGTON D.C. MAURICEFLEX™ ENABLES PROTEIN CHARGE VARIANT FRACTIONATION PLUS ROUTINE CIEF & CE-SDS ASSAYS, MAKING IT A UNIQUE MULTI-FUNCTIONAL SYSTEM THAT FURTHER STREAMLINES PROTEIN CHARACTERIZATION WORKFLOWS.
