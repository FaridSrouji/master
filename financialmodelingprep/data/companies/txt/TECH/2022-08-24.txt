BIO-TECHNE INCLUDED IN THE FORBES BEST-IN-STATE EMPLOYERS 2022 LIST. MINNEAPOLIS , AUG. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE FORBES LIST OF BEST-IN-STATE EMPLOYERS 2022. THIS PRESTIGIOUS AWARD IS PRESENTED BY FORBES AND STATISTA INC., THE WORLD-LEADING STATISTICS PORTAL AND INDUSTRY RANKING PROVIDER.
