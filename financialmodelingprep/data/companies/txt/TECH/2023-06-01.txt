BIO-TECHNE SUCCESSFUL ON CLAIM OF REVERSE ENGINEERING BY MILTENYI BIOTEC. MINNEAPOLIS , JUNE 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT PREVAILED ON A CLAIM THAT MILTENYI BIOTEC B.V. & CO. KG COMMERCIALIZED ANTIBODIES IT DEVELOPED BASED ON INFORMATION OBTAINED BY REVERSE ENGINEERING BIO-TECHNE'S PROPRIETARY R&D SYSTEMS BRANDED TIGIT AND CCR-7 ANTIBODIES.
