BIO-TECHNE LAUNCHES EXCELLERATE™ IPSC EXPANSION MEDIUM. MINNEAPOLIS, SEPT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) BIO-TECHNE ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A NEW MEDIUM FOR THE EXPANSION AND MAINTENANCE OF INDUCED PLURIPOTENT STEM CELLS (IPSCS) FOR USE IN BOTH RESEARCH AND TRANSLATIONAL WORKFLOWS.
