BIO-TECHNE ANNOUNCES RELEASE OF QUANTIST LUMINEX® DATA ANALYSIS SOFTWARE. MINNEAPOLIS , AUG. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT R&D SYSTEMS®, A BIO-TECHNE BRAND, HAS RELEASED ITS NEW QUANTIST LUMINEX® DATA ANALYSIS SOFTWARE. COMPATIBLE WITH ALL AVAILABLE LUMINEX XMAP® INSTRUMENTS, QUANTIST SOFTWARE PROVIDES FAST, RELIABLE ANALYSIS OF LUMINEX MULTIPLEX ASSAY DATA, HELPING RESEARCHERS EXTRACT VALUABLE INSIGHTS FROM COMPLEX DATA.
