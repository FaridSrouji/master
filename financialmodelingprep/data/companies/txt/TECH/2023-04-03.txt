BIO-TECHNE COMPLETES INVESTMENT IN WILSON WOLF MANUFACTURING. MINNEAPOLIS , APRIL 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED ITS INVESTMENT IN WILSON WOLF MANUFACTURING. WILSON WOLF ACHIEVED THE TRAILING 12-MONTH EARNINGS BEFORE INTEREST, TAXES, DEPRECIATION, AND AMORTIZATION (EBITDA) TARGET OF $55 MILLION, TRIGGERING BIO-TECHNE'S INITIAL INVESTMENT IN THE COMPANY.
