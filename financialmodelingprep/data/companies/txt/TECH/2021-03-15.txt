BIO-TECHNE ANNOUNCES ISO 9001:2015 CERTIFICATION FOR ITS BRISTOL MANUFACTURING FACILITY.. MINNEAPOLIS, MARCH 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH), A GLOBAL LIFE SCIENCES COMPANY PROVIDING INNOVATIVE TOOLS AND BIOACTIVE REAGENTS FOR THE RESEARCH AND CLINICAL DIAGNOSTIC COMMUNITIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BRISTOL SITE IN THE UNITED KINGDOM RECEIVED ISO 9001:2015 QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS CERTIFICATION. BIO-TECHNE'S BRISTOL FACILITY PRODUCES LIFE SCIENCE REAGENTS FOR RESEARCH USE.
