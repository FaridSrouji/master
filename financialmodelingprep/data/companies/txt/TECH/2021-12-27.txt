BIO-TECHNE ANNOUNCES APPOINTMENT OF BRENDA EVERSON AS SVP HUMAN RESOURCES. MINNEAPOLIS, DEC. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF BRENDA EVERSON AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, HUMAN RESOURCES EFFECTIVE JANUARY 10, 2022. MS. EVERSON SUCCEEDS STRUAN ROBERTSON, WHO WILL BE RETIRING FROM THE COMPANY.
