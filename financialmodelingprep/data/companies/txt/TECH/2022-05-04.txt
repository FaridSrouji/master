BIO-TECHNE RELEASES THIRD QUARTER FISCAL 2022 RESULTS. MINNEAPOLIS, MAY 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY REPORTED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2022. THIRD QUARTER FY2022 HIGHLIGHTS THIRD QUARTER ORGANIC REVENUE INCREASED BY 17% (19% REPORTED) TO $290.4 MILLION AND 18% (22% REPORTED) IN THE FIRST NINE MONTHS OF FISCAL 2022 TO $817.4 MILLION.
