BIO-TECHNE ANNOUNCES LAUNCH OF PROFESSIONAL ASSAY SERVICES - DEDICATED SOURCE FOR RNA IN SITU HYBRIDIZATION ASSAYS IN SPATIAL GENE EXPRESSION ANALYSIS. MINNEAPOLIS, APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ADVANCED CELL DIAGNOSTICS (ACD), A BIO-TECHNE BRAND, COMPLETED AN EXTENSIVE REBRANDING EFFORT IN RESPONSE TO INCREASED DEMAND FOR ITS ASSAY SERVICES OFFERED AT ITS NEWARK, CA LABORATORY. AT THE HEART OF THIS RE-LAUNCH IS A NAME CHANGE FROM PHARMA ASSAY SERVICES TO PROFESSIONAL ASSAY SERVICES, WITH AN EXPANSION OF OFFERINGS TO GLOBAL ACADEMIC AND CLINICAL INSTITUTIONS, BUILDING ON 10 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE PROVIDING SERVICES TO OUR GLOBAL PHARMA AND BIOTECH PARTNERS.
