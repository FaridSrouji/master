BIO-TECHNE RELEASES SECOND QUARTER FISCAL 2022 RESULTS. MINNEAPOLIS, FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY REPORTED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2021. SECOND QUARTER FY2022 SNAPSHOT SECOND QUARTER ORGANIC REVENUE INCREASED BY 17% (20% REPORTED) TO $269.3 MILLION AND 19% (23% REPORTED) IN THE FIRST HALF OF FISCAL 2022 TO $527.0 MILLION.
