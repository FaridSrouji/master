BIO-TECHNE RELEASES FIRST QUARTER FISCAL 2023 RESULTS. MINNEAPOLIS , NOV. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY REPORTED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2022. FIRST QUARTER FY2023 HIGHLIGHTS FIRST QUARTER ORGANIC REVENUE INCREASED BY 7% (5% REPORTED) TO $269.7 MILLION.
