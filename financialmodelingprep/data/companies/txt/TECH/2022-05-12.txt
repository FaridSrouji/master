BIO-TECHNE AND NONAGEN BIOSCIENCE ANNOUNCE EXCLUSIVE SUPPLY AGREEMENT FOR NONAGEN'S ONCURIA® BLADDER CANCER TEST. MINNEAPOLIS AND LOS ANGELES, MAY 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) AND NONAGEN BIOSCIENCE  TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT FOR R&D SYSTEMS, A BIO-TECHNE BRAND, TO EXCLUSIVELY MANUFACTURE NONAGEN BIOSCIENCE'S ONCURIA® BLADDER CANCER DIAGNOSTIC PANEL USING XMAP LUMINEX® TECHNOLOGY. ONCURIA IS THE FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND MULTIPLEX PROTEIN-BASED URINE TEST TO PROVIDE EARLY AND ACCURATE DETECTION, THERAPY CHOICE, AND DISEASE MONITORING OF BLADDER CANCER.
