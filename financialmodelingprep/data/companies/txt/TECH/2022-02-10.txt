BIO-TECHNE AWARDED ON THE FORBES AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS 2022 LIST. MINNEAPOLIS, FEB. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BIO-TECHNE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: TECH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN AWARDED ON THE FORBES LIST OF AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS 2022. THIS PRESTIGIOUS AWARD IS PRESENTED BY FORBES AND STATISTA INC., THE WORLD-LEADING STATISTICS PORTAL AND INDUSTRY RANKING PROVIDER.
