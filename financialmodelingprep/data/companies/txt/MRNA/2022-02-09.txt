MODERNA CHIEF TECHNICAL OPERATIONS AND QUALITY OFFICER JUAN ANDRES ELECTED TO NATIONAL ACADEMY OF ENGINEERING. CAMBRIDGE, MA / ACCESSWIRE / FEBRUARY 9, 2022 / MODERNA, INC. "WE ARE DELIGHTED THAT THE NATIONAL ACADEMY OF ENGINEERING HAS RECOGNIZED JUAN FOR HIS OUTSTANDING CONTRIBUTIONS.
