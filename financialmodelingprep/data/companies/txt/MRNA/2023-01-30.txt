MODERNA GRANTED FDA BREAKTHROUGH THERAPY DESIGNATION FOR MRNA-1345, AN INVESTIGATIONAL RESPIRATORY SYNCYTIAL VIRUS (RSV) VACCINE CANDIDATE. DESIGNATION BASED ON POSITIVE TOPLINE DATA FROM THE CONQUERRSV PHASE 3 PIVOTAL EFFICACY TRIAL, WHICH DEMONSTRATED VACCINE EFFICACY OF 83.7% AGAINST RSV LOWER RESPIRATORY TRACT DISEASE, DEFINED BY 2 OR MORE SYMPTOMS IN OLDER ADULTS CAMBRIDGE, MA / ACCESSWIRE / JANUARY 30, 2023 / MODERNA, INC. (NASDAQ:MRNA), A BIOTECHNOLOGY COMPANY PIONEERING MESSENGER RNA (MRNA) THERAPEUTICS AND VACCINES, TODAY ANNOUNCED MRNA-1345, AN INVESTIGATIONAL MRNA VACCINE CANDIDATE FOR RESPIRATORY SYNCYTIAL VIRUS (RSV), HAS BEEN GRANTED BREAKTHROUGH THERAPY DESIGNATION BY THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR THE PREVENTION OF RSV-ASSOCIATED LOWER RESPIRATORY TRACT DISEASE (RSV-LRTD) IN ADULTS AGED 60 YEARS OR OLDER. THE DESIGNATION WAS BASED ON POSITIVE TOPLINE DATA FROM THE CONQUERRSV PHASE 3 PIVOTAL EFFICACY TRIAL.
