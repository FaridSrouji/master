DNA SCRIPT PARTNERS WITH MODERNA TO DEVELOP ON-DEMAND VACCINES AND THERAPEUTICS FOR DARPA. SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, CALIF. AND PARIS, APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DNA SCRIPT TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP WITH MODERNA, INC. (NASDAQ: MRNA) TO DEVELOP A PROTOTYPE FOR RAPID MOBILE MANUFACTURING OF VACCINES AND THERAPEUTICS AS PART OF THE DEFENSE ADVANCED RESEARCH PROJECTS AGENCY'S (DARPA) NUCLEIC ACIDS ON-DEMAND WORLD-WIDE (NOW) PROGRAM.
