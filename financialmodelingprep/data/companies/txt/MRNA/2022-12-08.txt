MODERNA RECEIVES FDA EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR OMICRON-TARGETING BIVALENT COVID-19 BOOSTER VACCINE IN CHILDREN 6 MONTHS THROUGH 5 YEARS OF AGE. MRNA-1273.222 TARGETS THE BA.4/BA.5 STRAINS OF OMICRON VARIANT AUTHORIZATION IS BASED UPON CLINICAL AND PRE-CLINICAL DATA FOR MODERNA'S BIVALENT VACCINES CAMBRIDGE, MA / ACCESSWIRE / DECEMBER 8, 2022 / MODERNA, INC. (NASDAQ:MRNA), A BIOTECHNOLOGY COMPANY PIONEERING MESSENGER RNA (MRNA) THERAPEUTICS AND VACCINES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) FROM THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR ITS BA.4/BA.5 OMICRON-TARGETING BIVALENT COVID-19 BOOSTER VACCINE, MRNA-1273.222, IN CHILDREN 6 MONTHS THROUGH 5 YEARS OF AGE. THE AUTHORIZATION IS BASED ON A 10 ΜG BOOSTER DOSE FOLLOWING A COMPLETED PRIMARY SERIES OF MODERNA'S ORIGINAL VACCINE.
