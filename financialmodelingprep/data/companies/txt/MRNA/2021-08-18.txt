AZZUR GROUP AND MODERNA EXPAND THEIR ONGOING RELATIONSHIP. BURLINGTON, MASS., AUG. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AZZUR CLEANROOMS ON DEMAND™ ("COD"), AN AZZUR GROUP COMPANY, MARKET-LEADING PROVIDERS FOR EARLY-PHASE LIFE SCIENCES GXP MANUFACTURING, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY ARE EXPANDING THEIR ONGOING BUSINESS RELATIONSHIP WITH THE LIFE SCIENCES COMPANY MODERNA TX, INC. ("MODERNA").
