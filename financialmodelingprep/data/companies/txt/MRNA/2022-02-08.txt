MODERNA ANNOUNCES NEW SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH COLOMBIA FOR ADDITIONAL 10.8 MILLION DOSES OF MODERNA'S COVID-19 VACCINE. CAMBRIDGE, MA / ACCESSWIRE / FEBRUARY 8, 2022 / MODERNA, INC. (NASDAQ:MRNA), A BIOTECHNOLOGY COMPANY PIONEERING MESSENGER RNA (MRNA) THERAPEUTICS AND VACCINES, TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH THE GOVERNMENT OF COLOMBIA FOR 10.8 MILLION DOSES OF MODERNA'S COVID-19 VACCINE. THESE DOSES ARE IN ADDITION TO 10 MILLION DOSES SUPPLIED TO COLOMBIA IN 2021.
