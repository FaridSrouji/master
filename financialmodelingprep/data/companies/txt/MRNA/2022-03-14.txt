MODERNA ANNOUNCES FIRST PARTICIPANT DOSED IN PHASE 1 STUDY OF ITS HIV TRIMER MRNA VACCINE. CAMBRIDGE, MA / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 14, 2022 / MODERNA, INC. (NASDAQ:MRNA), A BIOTECHNOLOGY COMPANY PIONEERING MESSENGER RNA (MRNA) THERAPEUTICS AND VACCINES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE FIRST PARTICIPANT HAS BEEN DOSED IN A CLINICAL TRIAL OF AN EXPERIMENTAL HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS (HIV) TRIMER MRNA VACCINE (MRNA-1574). "DEVELOPING A VACCINE REGIMEN THAT INDUCES SUSTAINED PROTECTIVE LEVELS OF HIV NEUTRALIZING ANTIBODIES IN HUMANS HAS BEEN DIFFICULT TO ACHIEVE.
