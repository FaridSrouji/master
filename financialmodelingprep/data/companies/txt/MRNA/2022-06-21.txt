MODERNA ANNOUNCES PLAN TO BRING MRNA INNOVATION TO THE UNITED KINGDOM. MANUFACTURING FACILITY WOULD PROVIDE ACCESS TO DOMESTICALLY MANUFACTURED VACCINES AGAINST RESPIRATORY VIRUSES MODERNA WOULD EXPAND ITS PRESENCE IN THE UK THROUGH INVESTMENTS IN R&D ACTIVITIES AND CAPABILITIES COLLABORATION WOULD SUPPORT THE UK WITH DIRECT ACCESS TO RAPID PANDEMIC RESPONSE CAPABILITIES CAMBRIDGE, MA / ACCESSWIRE / JUNE 21, 2022 / MODERNA, INC . (NASDAQ:MRNA), A BIOTECHNOLOGY COMPANY PIONEERING MESSENGER RNA (MRNA) THERAPEUTICS AND VACCINES, TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT IN PRINCIPLE WITH THE UNITED KINGDOM GOVERNMENT TO ESTABLISH AN MRNA INNOVATION AND TECHNOLOGY CENTER IN THE UK.
