MODERNA ANNOUNCES FIRST PARTICIPANTS DOSED IN PHASE 2/3 STUDY OF COVID-19 VACCINE CANDIDATE IN PEDIATRIC POPULATION. CAMBRIDGE, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MODERNA ANNOUNCES THE FIRST PARTICIPANTS HAVE BEEN DOSED IN THE PHASE 2/3 STUDY OF ITS COVID-19 VACCINE CANDIDATE IN THE PEDIATRIC POPULATION.
