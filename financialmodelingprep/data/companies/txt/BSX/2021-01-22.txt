GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP REMINDS INVESTORS OF LOOMING DEADLINE IN THE CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (BSX). LOS ANGELES, JAN. 22, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP (“GPM”) REMINDS INVESTORS OF THE UPCOMING FEBRUARY 2, 2021 DEADLINE TO FILE A LEAD PLAINTIFF MOTION IN THE CLASS ACTION FILED ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (“BOSTON SCIENTIFIC” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: BSX ) SECURITIES BETWEEN APRIL 24, 2019 AND NOVEMBER 16, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”).
