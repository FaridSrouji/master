BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCES RESULTS FOR SECOND QUARTER 2021. MARLBOROUGH, MASS., JULY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) GENERATED NET SALES OF $3.077 BILLION DURING THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021, GROWING 53.6 PERCENT ON A REPORTED BASIS, 49.6 PERCENT ON AN OPERATIONAL1 BASIS AND 52.4 PERCENT ON AN ORGANIC2 BASIS, ALL COMPARED TO THE PRIOR YEAR PERIOD.
