THE LAW OFFICES OF FRANK R. CRUZ ANNOUNCES THE FILING OF A SECURITIES CLASS ACTION ON BEHALF OF BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (BSX) INVESTORS. LOS ANGELES, DEC. 17, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LAW OFFICES OF FRANK R. CRUZ ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF PERSONS AND ENTITIES THAT PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION ("BOSTON SCIENTIFIC" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: BSX)...
