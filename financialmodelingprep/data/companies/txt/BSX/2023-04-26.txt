BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCES RESULTS FOR FIRST QUARTER 2023. MARLBOROUGH, MASS. , APRIL 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) GENERATED NET SALES OF $3.389 BILLION DURING THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023, GROWING 12.0 PERCENT ON A REPORTED BASIS, 14.9 PERCENT ON AN OPERATIONAL1 BASIS AND 14.0 PERCENT ON AN ORGANIC2 BASIS, ALL COMPARED TO THE PRIOR YEAR PERIOD.
