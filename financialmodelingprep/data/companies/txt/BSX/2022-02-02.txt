BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCES RESULTS FOR FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2021. MARLBOROUGH, MASS., FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) GENERATED NET SALES OF $3.127 BILLION DURING THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021, GROWING 15.4 PERCENT ON A REPORTED BASIS, 16.9 PERCENT ON AN OPERATIONAL1 BASIS AND 15.1 PERCENT ON AN ORGANIC2 BASIS, ALL COMPARED TO THE PRIOR YEAR PERIOD.
