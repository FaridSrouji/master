BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCES STRATEGIC INVESTMENT TO ACQUIRE MAJORITY STAKE OF ACOTEC SCIENTIFIC HOLDINGS LIMITED. MARLBOROUGH, MASS. , DEC. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) AND ACOTEC SCIENTIFIC HOLDINGS LIMITED ("ACOTEC" AND 6669.HK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT BOSTON SCIENTIFIC WILL MAKE A PARTIAL OFFER TO ACQUIRE A MAJORITY STAKE, UP TO A MAXIMUM OF 65%, OF SHARES OF ACOTEC, A CHINESE MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY THAT OFFERS SOLUTIONS DESIGNED FOR A VARIETY OF INTERVENTIONAL PROCEDURES.
