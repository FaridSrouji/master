BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCES CONVERSION DATE OF SERIES A MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED STOCK. MARLBOROUGH, MASS. , MAY 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS SERIES A 5.50% MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED STOCK (THE PREFERRED STOCK) WILL AUTOMATICALLY CONVERT INTO SHARES OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK ON JUNE 1, 2023.
