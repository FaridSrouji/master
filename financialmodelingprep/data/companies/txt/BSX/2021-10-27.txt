BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCES RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER 2021. MARLBOROUGH, MASS., OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) GENERATED NET SALES OF $2.932 BILLION DURING THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021, GROWING 10.3 PERCENT ON A REPORTED BASIS, 9.7 PERCENT ON AN OPERATIONAL1 BASIS AND 10.6 PERCENT ON AN ORGANIC2 BASIS, ALL COMPARED TO THE PRIOR YEAR PERIOD.
