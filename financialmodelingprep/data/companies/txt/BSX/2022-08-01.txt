NEVRO AND BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCE THE SETTLEMENT OF THEIR ONGOING INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY LITIGATIONS. PARTIES REACH AN AGREEMENT THAT CONCLUDES OUTSTANDING INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY DISPUTES REDWOOD CITY, CALIF. , AUG. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEVRO CORP. (NYSE: NVRO) AND BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORP. (NYSE: BSX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THEY HAVE REACHED A SETTLEMENT IN THEIR ONGOING INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY LITIGATIONS THAT GIVES BOSTON SCIENTIFIC THE FREEDOM TO OPERATE USING THE FEATURES AND CAPABILITIES EMBODIED IN ITS CURRENT LINE OF PRODUCTS FOR FREQUENCIES BELOW 1,500 HZ, AND GIVES NEVRO THE FREEDOM TO OPERATE USING THE FEATURES AND CAPABILITIES EMBODIED IN ITS CURRENT LINE OF PRODUCTS.
