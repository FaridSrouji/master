LATE-BREAKING TRIAL DATA AT TVT DEMONSTRATE SUSTAINED SAFETY AND PERFORMANCE OF WATCHMAN FLX™ LEFT ATRIAL APPENDAGE CLOSURE DEVICE. MARLBOROUGH, MASS., JULY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, BOSTON SCIENTIFIC (NYSE: BSX) ANNOUNCED POSITIVE 24-MONTH RESULTS FROM THE PINNACLE FLX CLINICAL TRIAL ASSESSING THE SAFETY AND EFFICACY OF THE NEXT-GENERATION WATCHMAN FLX™ LEFT ATRIAL APPENDAGE CLOSURE (LAAC) DEVICE FOR PATIENTS WITH NON-VALVULAR ATRIAL FIBRILLATION (NVAF).
