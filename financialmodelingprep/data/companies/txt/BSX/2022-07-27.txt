BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCES RESULTS FOR SECOND QUARTER 2022. MARLBOROUGH, MASS. , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) GENERATED NET SALES OF $3.244 BILLION DURING THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022, GROWING 5.4 PERCENT ON A REPORTED BASIS, 9.6 PERCENT ON AN OPERATIONAL1 BASIS AND 6.6 PERCENT ON AN ORGANIC2 BASIS, ALL COMPARED TO THE PRIOR YEAR PERIOD.
