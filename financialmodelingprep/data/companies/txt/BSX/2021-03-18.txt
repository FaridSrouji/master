BOSTON SCIENTIFIC RECEIVES FDA APPROVAL FOR THERASPHERE™ Y-90 GLASS MICROSPHERES. MARLBOROUGH, MASS., MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) APPROVAL OF THE THERASPHERE™ Y-90 GLASS MICROSPHERES, DEVELOPED FOR THE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH HEPATOCELLULAR CARCINOMA (HCC).
