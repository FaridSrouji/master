BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CLOSES ACQUISITION OF BAYLIS MEDICAL COMPANY INC.. MARLBOROUGH, MASS., FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CLOSE OF ITS ACQUISITION OF BAYLIS MEDICAL COMPANY INC., A COMPANY THAT OFFERS ADVANCED TRANSSEPTAL ACCESS SOLUTIONS AS WELL AS GUIDEWIRES, SHEATHS AND DILATORS USED TO SUPPORT CATHETER-BASED LEFT-HEART PROCEDURES.
