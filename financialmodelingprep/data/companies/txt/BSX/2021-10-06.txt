BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ELUVIA™ DRUG-ELUTING VASCULAR STENT SYSTEM EXHIBITS SUPERIORITY COMPARED TO BARE METAL STENTS. LAS VEGAS AND MARLBOROUGH, MASS., OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) ANNOUNCED POSITIVE DATA FOR THE ELUVIA™ DRUG-ELUTING VASCULAR STENT SYSTEM (ELUVIA STENT) DURING A LATE-BREAKING CLINICAL TRIAL PRESENTATION AT THE VASCULAR INTERVENTIONAL ADVANCES (VIVA) MEETING IN LAS VEGAS.
BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE BAYLIS MEDICAL COMPANY INC.. MARLBOROUGH, MASS., OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE BAYLIS MEDICAL COMPANY INC. FOR AN UPFRONT PAYMENT OF $1.75 BILLION, SUBJECT TO CLOSING ADJUSTMENTS.
