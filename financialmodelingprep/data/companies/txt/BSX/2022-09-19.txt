LATE-BREAKING DATA FROM PROTECTED TAVR TRIAL DEMONSTRATE REDUCED RISK OF DISABLING STROKE, LOW COMPLICATION RATE WITH SENTINEL™ CEREBRAL PROTECTION SYSTEM. RESULTS FROM LARGEST RANDOMIZED TAVR TRIAL TO DATE PRESENTED AT TCT 2022 MARLBOROUGH, MASS. , SEPT. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE:BSX) HAS ANNOUNCED RESULTS FROM THE PROTECTED TAVR CLINICAL TRIAL EVALUATING THE SENTINEL™ CEREBRAL PROTECTION SYSTEM, WHICH IS DESIGNED TO CAPTURE AND REMOVE EMBOLIC DEBRIS STEMMING FROM TRANSCATHETER AORTIC VALVE REPLACEMENT (TAVR) BEFORE IT CAN REACH THE BRAIN AND POTENTIALLY CAUSE A STROKE.
