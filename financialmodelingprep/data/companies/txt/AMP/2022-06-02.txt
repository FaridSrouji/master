AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL HOSTS NATIONWIDE VOLUNTEER EVENTS IN PARTNERSHIP WITH FEEDING AMERICA® TO HELP PEOPLE FACING HUNGER. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH FEEDING AMERICA®, IS BRINGING TOGETHER ITS EMPLOYEES, ADVISORS AND CLIENTS TO MAKE A COLLECTIVE IMPACT IN THE FIGHT AGAINST HUNGER. AS PART OF THE COMPANY'S TWICE-ANNUAL NATIONAL DAYS OF SERVICE, AMERIPRISE EMPLOYEES, ADVISORS AND CLIENTS WILL STOCK SHELVES, SORT FOOD AND HOST VIRTUAL FOOD DRIVES TO SUPPORT THE 38 MILLION PEOPLE IN AMERICA, INCLUDING 12 MILLION CHILDREN, STRUGGLING WITH HUNGER.
