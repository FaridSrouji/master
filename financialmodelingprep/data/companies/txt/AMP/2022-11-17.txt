ADVISOR WITH MORE THAN $210 MILLION IN ASSETS JOINS AMERIPRISE FOR THE FIRM'S STELLER REPUTATION AND CLIENT-CENTRIC CULTURE. MINNEAPOLIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: AMP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JOHN (CARTER) PIPER, CFP® JOINED A WELL-ESTABLISHED AMERIPRISE INDEPENDENT PRACTICE, FOUNDATION WEALTH MANAGEMENT. THE TEAM IS BASED IN BAKERSFIELD, CALIFORNIA, AND LED BY FINANCIAL ADVISORS LANCE MACKALL AND RICK YANEZ. PIPER BRINGS 25 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE AS A FINANCIAL ADVISOR, MOST RECENTLY WITH WELLS FARGO, AND MORE THAN $210 MILLION IN CLIENT ASSETS. PIPER SAID AMERIPRISE WAS HIS TOP CHOICE BASED ON THE FIRM'S STELLAR CLIENT-CENTRIC REPUTATION AND ITS SUITE OF ROBUST TECHNOLOGY CAPABILITIES.
