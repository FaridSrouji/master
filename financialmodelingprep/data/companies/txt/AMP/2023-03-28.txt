ADVISORS JOIN AMERIPRISE FOR INTEGRATED TECHNOLOGY AND FINANCIAL PLANNING CAPABILITIES. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SAXON ADVISORS, A WEALTH MANAGEMENT PRACTICE MANAGING NEARLY $180 MILLION IN CLIENT ASSETS, RECENTLY JOINED THE INDEPENDENT CHANNEL OF AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: AMP). GARRY RUTLEDGE, AIF®, BRIAN BUSHMAN, CRC®, AND DONALD MCCLURG, CRPC™, WHO OPERATE SAXON ADVISORS IN CINCINNATI, OHIO, MOVED THEIR PRACTICE TO AMERIPRISE FROM CAMBRIDGE INVESTMENT RESEARCH INC.
