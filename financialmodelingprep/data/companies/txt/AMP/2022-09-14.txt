AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL HOSTS INAUGURAL BLACK ADVISOR SUMMIT. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: AMP) RECENTLY HOSTED ITS FIRST ANNUAL BLACK ADVISOR SUMMIT, BRINGING TOGETHER ADVISORS, FIELD LEADERS AND STAFF TO CHAMPION THE SUCCESS OF BLACK FINANCIAL ADVISORS AND DIVERSITY AT THE FIRM. THE TWO-DAY EVENT WAS HELD IN AUGUST IN WASHINGTON D.C. IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE FIRM'S BLACK EMPOWERMENT ADVISOR COMMUNITY NETWORK (BEACN). THE SUMMIT FEATURED COMPELLING REMARKS FROM AMERIPRISE EXECUTIVES, BEACN LEADERS, AND KEYNOTE SPEAKERS.
