AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL EARNS J.D. POWER CUSTOMER SERVICE CERTIFICATION FOR THE SECOND YEAR IN A ROW. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR, J.D. POWER HAS AWARDED AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL (NYSE: AMP) WITH CERTIFICATION FOR PROVIDING ITS FINANCIAL ADVISORS WITH AN “OUTSTANDING CUSTOMER SERVICE EXPERIENCE” WHEN THEY CONTACT THE COMPANY BY PHONE FOR ASSISTANCE. THE AMERIPRISE ADVICE & WEALTH MANAGEMENT AND RIVERSOURCE CALL CENTERS EARNED THE CERTIFICATION FOLLOWING A RIGOROUS EVALUATION AND SURVEY OF ADVISORS' RECENT SERVICING INTERACTIONS.
