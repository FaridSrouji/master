COMERICA BANK SELECTS AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL AS ITS NEW INVESTMENT PROGRAM PROVIDER. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: AMP) AND COMERICA BANK TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL SERVICES, LLC AND COMERICA BANK HAVE SIGNED AN AGREEMENT FOR AMERIPRISE TO BECOME COMERICA'S NEW INVESTMENT PROGRAM PROVIDER.
