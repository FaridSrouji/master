AMERIPRISE EMPLOYEES AND ADVISORS UNITE TO MAKE A DIFFERENCE IN COMMUNITIES AHEAD OF THE HOLIDAY SEASON. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL (NYSE: AMP) IS ONCE AGAIN PARTNERING WITH FEEDING AMERICA®, THE NATION'S LARGEST DOMESTIC HUNGER ASSOCIATION, TO MAKE THE HOLIDAYS BRIGHTER FOR FAMILIES FACING HUNGER. THE FIRM'S EMPLOYEES, FINANCIAL ADVISORS AND CLIENTS ARE VOLUNTEERING AT NONPROFITS TO SERVE MEALS, STOCK SHELVES, SORT FOOD OR HOST VIRTUAL FOOD DRIVES TO MAKE A COLLECTIVE IMPACT. THE EFFORT IS PART OF THE FIRM'S NATIONAL DAYS OF SERVICE VOLUNTEER PROGRAM THAT HAPPENS TWICE A Y
