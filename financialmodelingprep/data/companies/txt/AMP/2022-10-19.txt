MAZUMA CREDIT UNION MOVES WEALTH MANAGEMENT PROGRAM TO AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL FOR ENHANCED TECHNOLOGY AND SOLUTIONS. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MAZUMA CREDIT UNION, WHICH OPERATES 12 LOCATIONS IN MISSOURI AND KANSAS AND OVERSEES NEARLY $900 MILLION IN ASSETS, HAS TRANSITIONED ITS WEALTH MANAGEMENT PROGRAM TO AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: AMP) FOLLOWING AN EXTENSIVE SEARCH FOR A NEW BROKER-DEALER.
