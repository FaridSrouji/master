FOUR ADVISORS MOVE TO AMERIPRISE FOR DIGITAL FINANCIAL PLANNING CAPABILITIES TO ENHANCE THEIR CLIENT EXPERIENCE. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, LLC (NYSE: AMP) RECENTLY ADDED THREE ADVISOR PRACTICES TO ITS AMERIPRISE ADVISOR GROUP WITH $379 MILLION IN COMBINED CLIENT ASSETS. DAVID MOSS AND JAY CAMPBELL JOINED FROM MERRILL LYNCH WITH $190 MILLION IN CLIENT ASSETS IN HOUSTON, TEXAS. SEPARATELY, FINANCIAL ADVISOR AND BRANCH MANAGER LARRY PADRON JOINED FROM STIFEL WITH $121 MILLION IN CLIENT ASSETS IN SOUTHLAKE, TEXAS. LASTLY, TROY MALBON JOINED FROM UBS WITH $68 MILLION IN CLIENT ASSETS
