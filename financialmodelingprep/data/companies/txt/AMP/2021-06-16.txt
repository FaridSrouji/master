AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL EARNS 2021 DIGITAL WEALTH MANAGEMENT IMPACT INNOVATION AWARD FOR DIGITAL CLIENT ENGAGEMENT FROM THE AITE GROUP. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL (NYSE: AMP), A PIONEER AND LONGSTANDING LEADER IN FINANCIAL PLANNING, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS WON A DIGITAL WEALTH MANAGEMENT IMPACT INNOVATION AWARD FOR DIGITAL CLIENT ENGAGEMENT FROM THE AITE GROUP, A GLOBAL RESEARCH AND ADVISORY FIRM. AITE GROUP'S ANNUAL AWARD PROGRAM HONORS WEALTH MANAGEMENT FIRMS THAT ARE ADVANCING AND LEADING THE INDUSTRY WITH INNOVATIVE PRODUCTS, CAPABILITIES, OR LEVELS OF AUTOMATION.
