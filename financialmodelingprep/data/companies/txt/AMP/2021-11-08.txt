BMO COMPLETES SALE OF EMEA ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS. TORONTO, NOV. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ - BMO FINANCIAL GROUP (TSX: BMO) (NYSE: BMO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SALE OF THE ENTITIES THAT REPRESENT ITS EMEA ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS TO AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: AMP). THE TRANSACTION SUPPORTS BMO'S GOALS TO OPTIMIZE EFFICIENCY AND TO FOCUS CAPITAL AND INVESTMENT IN AREAS WHERE IT HAS AN ADVANTAGED MARKET POSITION, INCLUDING ITS NORTH AMERICAN WEALTH MANAGEMENT BUSINESSES.
