HOW UDB FINANCIAL GREW BY MORE THAN 30% SINCE JOINING AMERIPRISE IN 2019 TO MANAGE $833 MILLION IN CLIENT ASSETS. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UDB FINANCIAL, A WEALTH MANAGEMENT PRACTICE LOCATED IN ALEXANDRIA, LOUISIANA, RECENTLY CELEBRATED TWO YEARS WITH AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: AMP) AFTER LEAVING LPL FINANCIAL IN 2019. FINANCIAL ADVISORS MICHAEL UPTON, AAMS®, JEFF DRAUGHON, AAMS®, AND JOEY BOLLINGER, MBA, AAMS® LEAD THE TEAM AND BELIEVE THE SWITCH TO AMERIPRISE'S BRANCH CHANNEL HELPED THEM CAPITALIZE ON MARKET PERFORMANCE AND ACCELERATED GROWTH FOR THEIR PRACTICE, WHILE DELIVERING AN OUTSTANDIN
