AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL ONCE AGAIN RECOGNIZED BY J.D. POWER FOR ITS “OUTSTANDING CUSTOMER SERVICE EXPERIENCE”. MINNEAPOLIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--FOR THE FOURTH YEAR IN A ROW, AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: AMP) EARNED A J.D. POWER CERTIFICATIONI FOR PROVIDING AN “OUTSTANDING CUSTOMER SERVICE EXPERIENCE” TO ITS FINANCIAL ADVISORS WHO CONTACT THE COMPANY BY PHONE FOR ASSISTANCE. THE AMERIPRISE ADVICE & WEALTH MANAGEMENT AND RIVERSOURCE CALL CENTERS EARNED THE CERTIFICATION FOLLOWING A RIGOROUS EVALUATION AND SURVEY OF ADVISORS' RECENT SERVICING INTERACTIONS.
