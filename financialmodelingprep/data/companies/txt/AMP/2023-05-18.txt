HAPPY STATE BANK JOINS THE AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS GROUP THROUGH ITS MERGER WITH CENTENNIAL BANK. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL (NYSE: AMP) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT ADDED THE WEALTH MANAGEMENT ARM OF HAPPY STATE BANK, A DIVISION OF CENTENNIAL BANK, TO ITS FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS CHANNEL FOLLOWING THE BANK'S MERGER WITH EXISTING CLIENT CENTENNIAL BANK. THROUGH THE MERGER, HAPPY STATE BANK CUSTOMERS HAVE ACCESS TO THE PERSONALIZED FINANCIAL PLANNING, COMPREHENSIVE SOLUTIONS AND LEADING DIGITAL CAPABILITIES THAT CENTENNIAL RECEIVES FROM AMERIPRISE FINANCIAL.
