IHS MARKIT DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND IN SECOND QUARTER 2021. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IHS MARKIT (NYSE: INFO), A WORLD LEADER IN CRITICAL INFORMATION, ANALYTICS AND SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021, IN THE AMOUNT OF $0.20 PER SHARE. THE CASH DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MAY 17, 2021 TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 3, 2021.
