DESPITE BEING WORLD'S 4TH LARGEST OIL PRODUCER, MAJORITY OF CRUDE OIL DEMAND IN CANADA IS MET VIA THE UNITED STATES. CALGARY, ALBERTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MAJORITY OF CRUDE OIL DEMAND IN CANADA--WORLD'S 4TH LARGEST PRODUCER--IS MET VIA THE UNITED STATES, UNDERSCORING TWO NATIONS' ENERGY INTERDEPENDENCE.
