TERADYNE REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL YEAR 2022 RESULTS. NORTH READING, MASS., JAN. 25, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TERADYNE, INC. (NASDAQ: TER) REPORTED REVENUE OF $732 MILLION FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022 OF WHICH $481 MILLION WAS IN SEMICONDUCTOR TEST, $100 MILLION IN SYSTEM TEST, $40 MILLION IN WIRELESS TEST AND $110 MILLION IN INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION (IA). GAAP NET INCOME FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER WAS $172.3 MILLION OR $1.04 PER DILUTED SHARE. ON A NON-GAAP BASIS, TERADYNE'S NET INCOME IN THE FOURTH QUARTER WAS $150.8 MILLION, OR $0.92 PER DILUTED SHARE, WHICH EXCLUDED PENSION ACTUARIAL GAINS, ACQUIRED INTANGIBLE ASSET AMORTIZATION, RESTRUCTURING AND OTHER CHARGES, AND INCLUDED THE RELATED TAX IMPACT ON NON-GAAP ADJUSTMENTS.
