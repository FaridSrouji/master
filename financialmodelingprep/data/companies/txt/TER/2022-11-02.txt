ERNEST E. MADDOCK ELECTED TO TERADYNE'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NORTH READING, MASS., NOV. 02, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TERADYNE, INC. (NASDAQ:TER) ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF ERNEST E. MADDOCK TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 14, 2022. MR. MADDOCK WAS ALSO APPOINTED TO THE BOARD'S AUDIT COMMITTEE.
