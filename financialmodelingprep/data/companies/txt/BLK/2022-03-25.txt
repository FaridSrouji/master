BLACKROCK® CANADA ANNOUNCES FINAL MARCH CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES® PREMIUM MONEY MARKET ETF. TORONTO, MARCH 25, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FINAL MARCH 2022 CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES PREMIUM MONEY MARKET ETF. UNITHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MARCH 28, 2022 WILL RECEIVE CASH DISTRIBUTIONS PAYABLE ON MARCH 31, 2022.
