BLACKROCK® CANADA ANNOUNCES FINAL MARCH CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES® PREMIUM MONEY MARKET ETF. TORONTO, MARCH 22, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FINAL MARCH 2023 CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES PREMIUM MONEY MARKET ETF. UNITHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MARCH 23, 2023 WILL RECEIVE CASH DISTRIBUTIONS PAYABLE ON MARCH 31, 2023.
