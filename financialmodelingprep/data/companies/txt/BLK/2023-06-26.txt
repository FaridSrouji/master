BLACKROCK® CANADA ANNOUNCES FINAL JUNE CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES® PREMIUM MONEY MARKET ETF. TORONTO, JUNE 26, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FINAL JUNE 2023 CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES PREMIUM MONEY MARKET ETF. UNITHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 27, 2023 WILL RECEIVE CASH DISTRIBUTIONS PAYABLE ON JUNE 30, 2023.
