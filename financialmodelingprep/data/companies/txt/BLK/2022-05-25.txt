BLACKROCK DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $4.88 ON COMMON STOCK. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE:BLK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $4.88 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 23, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 6, 2022.
