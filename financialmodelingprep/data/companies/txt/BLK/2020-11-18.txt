BLACKROCK® CANADA ANNOUNCES NOVEMBER CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES® ETFS. TORONTO, NOV. 18, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE NOVEMBER 2020 CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES ETFS LISTED ON THE TSX OR NEO WHICH PAY ON A MONTHLY BASIS AS WELL AS XIU. UNITHOLDERS OF RECORD OF A FUND ON NOVEMBER 25, 2020 WILL RECEIVE CASH DISTRIBUTIONS PAYABLE IN RESPECT OF THAT FUND ON NOVEMBER 30, 2020.
