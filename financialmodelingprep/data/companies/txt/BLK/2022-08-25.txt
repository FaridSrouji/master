BLACKROCK® CANADA ANNOUNCES FINAL AUGUST CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES® PREMIUM MONEY MARKET ETF. TORONTO, AUG. 25, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FINAL AUGUST 2022 CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES PREMIUM MONEY MARKET ETF. UNITHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 26, 2022 WILL RECEIVE CASH DISTRIBUTIONS PAYABLE ON AUGUST 31, 2022.
