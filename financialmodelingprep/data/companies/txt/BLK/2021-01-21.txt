BLACKROCK DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $4.13 ON COMMON STOCK. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE:BLK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A 14% INCREASE IN THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $4.13 PER SHARE, PAYABLE MARCH 23, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 5, 2021.
