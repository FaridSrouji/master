RBC ISHARES EXPANDS ITS SUSTAINABLE ETF PLATFORM AND FIXED INCOME LINEUP WITH THE ADDITION OF FOUR ETFS. TORONTO, AUG. 12, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TODAY, RBC ISHARES EXPANDED ITS EXCHANGE TRADED FUND (“ETF”) LINEUP WITH THE LAUNCH OF FOUR NEW FIXED INCOME ETFS (COLLECTIVELY THE “ISHARES ETFS”), INCLUDING TWO SUSTAINABLE FIXED INCOME ETFS. ALL FOUR ISHARES ETFS ARE EXPECTED TO BEGIN TRADING ON THE TORONTO STOCK EXCHANGE (THE “TSX”) TODAY.
