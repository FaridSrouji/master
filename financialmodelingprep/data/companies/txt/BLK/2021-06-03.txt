BLACKROCK SURVEY: OVERWHELMING DEMAND FROM WORKERS, EMPLOYERS, AND RETIREES FOR RETIREMENT INCOME. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--A LARGE MAJORITY OF WORKERS SAVING FOR RETIREMENT THROUGH THEIR EMPLOYERS' 401(K) PLANS WANT OPTIONS THAT WILL HELP THEM GENERATE INCOME IN RETIREMENT, ACCORDING TO THE FIFTH ANNUAL DC PULSE SURVEY FROM BLACKROCK (NYSE: BLK).
