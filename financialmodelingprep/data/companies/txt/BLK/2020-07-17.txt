BLACKROCK REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2020 DILUTED EPS OF $7.85.QTRLY $100 BILLION OF QUARTERLY TOTAL NET INFLOWS.QTRLY GAAP REVENUE $3.65 BILLION VERSUS $3.52 BILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $6.99, REVENUE VIEW $3.54 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.$1.1 BILLION OF SHARES REPURCHASES IN QUARTER AS PART OF PNC’S SUCCESSFUL SALE OF ITS ENTIRE 22% OWNERSHIP POSITION.QTRLY ADJUSTED OPERATING INCOME OF $1,406 MILLION VERSUS $1,278  MILLION.4% INCREASE IN Q2 REVENUE YEAR-OVER-YEAR REFLECTS HIGHER PERFORMANCE FEES AND 17% GROWTH IN TECHNOLOGY SERVICES REVENUE.QTRLY ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $7.85.QTRLY TOTAL NET INFLOWS WAS DRIVEN BY FIXED INCOME AND CONTINUED MOMENTUM IN CASH MANAGEMENT.ISHARES FIXED INCOME ETFS AND BLACKROCK’S ACTIVE EQUITY STRATEGIES BOTH SAW RECORD INFLOWS IN QUARTER.
