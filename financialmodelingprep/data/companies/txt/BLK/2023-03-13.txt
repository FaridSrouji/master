BLACKROCK® CANADA ANNOUNCES CERTAIN CHANGES TO XEC AND XSEM. TORONTO, MARCH 13, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (“BLACKROCK”) (NYSE: BLK), HAS DETERMINED TO CHANGE THE MANNER IN WHICH THE INVESTMENT STRATEGIES OF ISHARES CORE MSCI EMERGING MARKETS IMI INDEX ETF (“XEC”) AND ISHARES ESG AWARE MSCI EMERGING MARKETS INDEX ETF (“XSEM”, AND TOGETHER WITH XEC, THE “ISHARES ETFS”) ARE IMPLEMENTED. THE CHANGES ARE EXPECTED TO REDUCE THE NEGATIVE EFFECT OF FOREIGN WITHHOLDING TAXES ON XEC AND XSEM'S PERFORMANCE.
