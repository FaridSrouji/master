BLACKROCK® CANADA ANNOUNCES ESTIMATED ANNUAL REINVESTED CAPITAL GAINS DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES® ETFS. TORONTO, DEC. 22, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ESTIMATED ANNUAL REINVESTED CAPITAL GAINS DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES ETFS LISTED ON THE TSX OR NEO FOR THE 2020 TAX YEAR. PLEASE NOTE THAT THESE ARE ESTIMATED AMOUNTS ONLY AS OF DECEMBER 15, 2020 AND COULD CHANGE IF THE ISHARES FUNDS EXPERIENCE SUBSCRIPTIONS OR REDEMPTIONS PRIOR TO THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE OR DUE TO OTHER FACTORS.
BLACKROCK® CANADA ANNOUNCES ESTIMATED DECEMBER CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES® ETFS. TORONTO, DEC. 22, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ESTIMATED DECEMBER CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES ETFS LISTED ON THE TSX OR NEO. UNITHOLDERS OF RECORD OF A FUND ON DECEMBER 31, 2020 WILL RECEIVE CASH DISTRIBUTIONS PAYABLE IN RESPECT OF THAT FUND ON JANUARY 6, 2021.
