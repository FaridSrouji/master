BLACKROCK® CANADA ANNOUNCES FINAL ANNUAL REINVESTED CAPITAL GAINS DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES® ETFS. TORONTO, DEC. 30, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FINAL ANNUAL REINVESTED CAPITAL GAINS DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES ETFS LISTED ON THE TSX OR NEO FOR THE 2021 TAX YEAR.
