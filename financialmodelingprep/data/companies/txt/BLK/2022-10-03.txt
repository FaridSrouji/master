MARTIN SMALL TO SUCCEED GARY SHEDLIN AS BLACKROCK CFO IN 2023. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MARTIN SMALL WILL SUCCEED GARY S. SHEDLIN AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER IN 2023. MR. SHEDLIN WILL TRANSITION HIS CFO RESPONSIBILITIES AFTER THE FIRM COMPLETES ITS 2022 FINANCIAL REPORTING PROCESSES, WHICH IS ANTICIPATED TO BE ON OR ABOUT MARCH 1, 2023, AND WILL TAKE ON A NEW ROLE AS VICE CHAIRMAN WORKING DIRECTLY WITH KEY STRATEGIC CLIENTS OF THE FIRM.
