BLACKROCK DEBUTS FIRST BUFFER ETFS WITH LAUNCH OF TWO FUNDS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BLACKROCK DEBUTED TODAY ITS FIRST BUFFER ETFS WITH THE LAUNCH OF THE ISHARES LARGE CAP MODERATE BUFFER ETF (CBOE: IVVM) AND THE ISHARES LARGE CAP DEEP BUFFER ETF (CBOE: IVVB). EACH ETF OFFERS INVESTORS AN EFFICIENT TOOL FOR SEEKING CLEARER FINANCIAL OUTCOMES AND IS DESIGNED TO BALANCE UPSIDE PARTICIPATION WITH LESS DRAWDOWN RISK. “WHETHER YOU ARE NEARING RETIREMENT OR A FIRST-TIME INVESTOR, MARKET VOLATILITY REMAINS A TOP CONCERN FOR INVESTORS,” SAID DOMINIK ROHE, HEA.
