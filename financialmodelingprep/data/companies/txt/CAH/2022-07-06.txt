CARDINAL HEALTH ACQUIRES THE BENDCARE CPO-GPO ENTITY AND MAKES A MINORITY INVESTMENT IN THE BENDCARE MSO. STRENGTHENING CORNERSTONE RHEUMATOLOGY™ GPO AS A LEADING RHEUMATOLOGY-FOCUSED GPO BY PROVIDING INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS AND ROBUST SPECIALTY DRUG ACCESS TO OVER 1,300 RHEUMATOLOGY PROVIDERS NATIONWIDE.   DUBLIN, OHIO , JULY 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL HEALTH (NYSE: CAH) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED THE BENDCARE GROUP PURCHASING ORGANIZATION (CPO-GPO) ENTITY AND MADE A MINORITY INVESTMENT IN THE BENDCARE MANAGEMENT SERVICES ORGANIZATION (MSO).
