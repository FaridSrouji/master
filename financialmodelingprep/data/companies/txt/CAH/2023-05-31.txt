CARDINAL HEALTH LAUNCHES MODERN COMMERCE SOLUTIONS FOR RETAIL INDEPENDENT PHARMACIES POWERED BY SQUARE. DUBLIN, OHIO AND SAN FRANCISCO , MAY 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL HEALTH (NYSE: CAH) TODAY LAUNCHED MODERN PAYMENT SOLUTIONS, POWERED BY SQUARE, TO HELP INDEPENDENT PHARMACIES INCREASE OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY, STAY CURRENT WITH CUSTOMER PAYMENT PREFERENCES, AND ACCESS INTEGRATED SOFTWARE TOOLS FOR THEIR BUSINESS. THE COLLABORATION WITH SQUARE ALLOWS CARDINAL HEALTH TO OFFER ITS INDEPENDENT PHARMACY CUSTOMERS ACCESS TO INNOVATIVE PRODUCTS THAT CAN SEAMLESSLY MANAGE BUSINESS OPERATIONS, INTEGRATE FLEXIBLE PAYMENT OPTIONS FOR IN-STORE, ONLINE AND DELIVERY PURCHASES, AND REDUCE PAYMENT PROCESSING COSTS.
