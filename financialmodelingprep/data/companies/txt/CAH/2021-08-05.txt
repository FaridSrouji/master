CARDINAL HEALTH REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR RESULTS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2021. DUBLIN, OHIO, AUG. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL HEALTH (NYSE: CAH) TODAY REPORTED THAT FOURTH-QUARTER FISCAL 2021 REVENUE INCREASED 16% TO $42.6 BILLION. THIS INCREASE INCLUDES THE FAVORABLE PRIOR YEAR COMPARISON FROM REDUCED PHARMACEUTICAL DEMAND RELATED TO COVID-19.
