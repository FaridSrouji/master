CARDINAL HEALTH ANNOUNCES THE MERGER OF ITS OUTCOMES™ BUSINESS INTO TRANSACTION DATA SYSTEMS AND RELATED PARTNERSHIP. CARDINAL HEALTH'S OUTCOMES™ BUSINESS WILL COMBINE WITH TRANSACTION DATA SYSTEMS TO OFFER IMPROVED PATIENT ENGAGEMENT AND CLINICAL PHARMACY SOLUTIONS, EXPANDING CONNECTIONS BETWEEN PAYERS AND PHARMA TO ITS NATIONWIDE PHARMACY NETWORK DUBLIN, OHIO , JUNE 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL HEALTH (NYSE: CAH) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO CONTRIBUTE ITS OUTCOMES™ BUSINESS TO TRANSACTION DATA SYSTEMS (TDS), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF BLACKROCK LONG TERM PRIVATE CAPITAL AND GTCR, IN EXCHANGE FOR A MINORITY STAKE IN THE COMBINED ENTITY. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT, CARDINAL HEALTH'S PHARMACEUTICAL SEGMENT CEO, DEBBIE WEITZMAN, WILL BE APPOINTED TO THE TDS BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
