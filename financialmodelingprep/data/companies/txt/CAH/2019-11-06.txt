CARDINAL HEALTH, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 15, 2020. CARDINAL HEALTH BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.4811 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON JANUARY 15, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 2, 2020.
