CARDINAL HEALTH ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF OUTSTANDING NOTES. DUBLIN, OHIO, AUG. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL HEALTH (NYSE: CAH) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT IS SENDING AN IRREVOCABLE NOTICE OF EARLY REDEMPTION TO THE HOLDERS OF ALL OF THE COMPANY'S 2.616% NOTES DUE JUNE 2022 THAT ARE OUTSTANDING AS OF THE EARLY REDEMPTION DATE (AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $572,171,000). THE EARLY REDEMPTION DATE IS SET TO SEPTEMBER 15, 2021, PURSUANT TO THE JUNE 2, 2008 INDENTURE AND THE 2.616% NOTES.
