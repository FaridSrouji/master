CARDINAL HEALTH BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DUBLIN, OHIO , FEB. 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL HEALTH (NYSE: CAH) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.4957 PER SHARE OUT OF THE COMPANY'S CAPITAL SURPLUS. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON APRIL 15, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 3, 2023.
