NEW CARDINAL HEALTH RESEARCH FINDS WIDESPREAD ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF HEALTH INEQUITIES AMONG ONCOLOGISTS. DUBLIN, OHIO , DEC. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL HEALTH (NYSE: CAH) RELEASED TODAY ITS LATEST EDITION OF ONCOLOGY INSIGHTS, A BIANNUAL, RESEARCH-BASED REPORT FROM CARDINAL HEALTH. FEATURING PERSPECTIVES FROM NEARLY 200 ONCOLOGISTS NATIONWIDE, THE 12TH EDITION OF ONCOLOGY INSIGHTS FOCUSES ON PATIENT DIVERSITY, HEALTH EQUITY IN ONCOLOGY, CARE MANAGEMENT AND SYMPTOM MONITORING.
