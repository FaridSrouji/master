CARDINAL HEALTH'S OUTCOMES™ COLLABORATES WITH SIGNIFY HEALTH TO OFFER IN-HOME MEDICATION THERAPY MANAGEMENT. DUBLIN, OHIO , MARCH 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL HEALTH  (NYSE:CAH) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS COLLABORATION WITH SIGNIFY HEALTH (NYSE:SGFY) TO OFFER IN-HOME CLINICAL AND MEDICATION MANAGEMENT SERVICES THROUGH ITS OUTCOMES™ BUSINESS. THIS COLLABORATION WILL HELP REDUCE COSTS AND ELIMINATE GAPS IN CARE FOR MORE THAN 2.3 MILLION MEMBERS NATIONWIDE TO SUPPORT THEIR TREATMENT JOURNEY FROM PRESCRIPTION TO PHARMACY TO HOME.
