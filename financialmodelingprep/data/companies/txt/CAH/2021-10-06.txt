NEW CARDINAL HEALTH SURVEY EXPLORES HOW RHEUMATOLOGISTS ARE MANAGING CURRENT INDUSTRY DYNAMICS INCLUDING CHANGING REIMBURSEMENT AND IMPACTS OF COVID-19. DUBLIN, OHIO, OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHILE DEMAND FOR RHEUMATOLOGY CARE CONTINUES TO GROW, RHEUMATOLOGISTS ARE FEELING PRESSURED BY REIMBURSEMENT CHANGES, ADMINISTRATIVE DUTIES AND WORKFORCE SHORTAGES. THESE FINDINGS WERE RELEASED TODAY IN THE LATEST EDITION OF  RHEUMATOLOGY INSIGHTS, A RESEARCH-BASED REPORT PUBLISHED BY CARDINAL HEALTH SPECIALTY SOLUTIONS.
