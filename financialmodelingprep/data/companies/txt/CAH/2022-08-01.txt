CARDINAL HEALTH NAMES MICHELLE GREENE AS CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER. GREENE PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS SVP OF EIT FOR THE COMPANY'S PHARMACEUTICAL SEGMENT DUBLIN, OHIO , AUG. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL HEALTH (NYSE: CAH) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MICHELLE GREENE HAS BEEN NAMED CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER. GREENE WILL REPORT TO CEO, MIKE KAUFMANN AND JOIN THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE.
