ENTERGY CORPORATION ADDS BRIAN ELLIS TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW ORLEANS, NOV. 2, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ETR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF BRIAN ELLIS TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE OCT. 30, 2020. ELLIS BRINGS A STRONG BUSINESS ACUMEN COUPLED WITH NEARLY 30 YEARS OF LEGAL, CORPORATE GOVERNANCE AND BUSINESS...
