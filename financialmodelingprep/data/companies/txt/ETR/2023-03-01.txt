ENTERGY NAMES TWO MEMBERS TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ACCOUNTING AND EXTERNAL AFFAIRS EXPERTISE ADDED TO BOARD'S LEADERSHIP NEW ORLEANS , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF TWO NEW MEMBERS TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, GINA ADAMS AND JOHN BLACK. ADAMS HAS A LEGAL AND GOVERNMENT AFFAIRS CAREER THAT SPANS NEARLY 40 YEARS, CURRENTLY SERVING AS CORPORATE VICE PRESIDENT OF GOVERNMENT AND REGULATORY AFFAIRS FOR FEDEX CORPORATION.
