ENTERGY ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT TO SHAREHOLDERS. NEW ORLEANS, APRIL 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $1.07 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 1, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 4, 2023.
