ENTERGY TEXAS PRESIDENT & CEO, SALLIE RAINER, ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT AFTER 37 YEARS WITH ENTERGY; VIAMONTES NAMED NEW LEADER. NEW ORLEANS, AUG. 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALLIE RAINER, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF ENTERGY TEXAS INC., WILL BE RETIRING EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 14, 2021, AFTER MORE THAN 37 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY, ENTERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY. RAINER, WHO ASSUMED HER CURRENT POSITION IN 2012, IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE COMPANY'S CUSTOMER SERVICE, RESOURCE PLANNING, ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, CHARITABLE CONTRIBUTIONS, REGULATORY AND GOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS, AND FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE OF ENTERGY'S TEXAS SERVICE TERRITORY.
