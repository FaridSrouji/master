ENTERGY REPORTS FIRST QUARTER EARNINGS. COMPANY AFFIRMS GUIDANCE AND OUTLOOKS NEW ORLEANS , APRIL 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ETR) REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2023 EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $1.47 ON AN AS-REPORTED BASIS AND $1.14 ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS (NON-GAAP). "WE HAD A PRODUCTIVE START TO THE YEAR WITH STRONG EXECUTION ON IMPORTANT OPERATIONAL AND REGULATORY FRONTS," SAID DREW MARSH, ENTERGY CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
