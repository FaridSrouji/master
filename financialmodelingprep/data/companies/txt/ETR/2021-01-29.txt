ENTERGY CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT TO SHAREHOLDERS. NEW ORLEANS, JAN. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ENTERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ETR) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.95 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 1, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEB. 12, 2021. ENTERGY...
