D. E. SHAW RENEWABLE INVESTMENTS (DESRI) ANNOUNCES SIGNING OF 350MWAC OF CLEAN POWER PROJECTS WITH ENTERGY LOUISIANA. NEW YORK, NOV. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- D. E. SHAW RENEWABLE INVESTMENTS (DESRI), A LEADING RENEWABLE ENERGY PRODUCER IN THE U.S., TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF THREE RENEWABLE ENERGY OFF-TAKE AGREEMENTS WITH ENTERGY LOUISIANA (ELL).
