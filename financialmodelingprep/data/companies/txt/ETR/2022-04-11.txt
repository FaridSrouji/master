ENTERGY ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT TO SHAREHOLDERS. NEW ORLEANS , APRIL 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $1.01 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 1, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 5, 2022.
