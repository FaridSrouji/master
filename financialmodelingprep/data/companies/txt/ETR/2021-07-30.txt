ENTERGY CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT TO SHAREHOLDERS. NEW ORLEANS, JULY 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ENTERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ETR) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.95 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE SEPTEMBER 1, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUGUST 12, 2021.
