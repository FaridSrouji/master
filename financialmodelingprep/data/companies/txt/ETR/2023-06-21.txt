ENTERGY TEXAS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON PREFERRED STOCK. THE WOODLANDS, TEXAS , JUNE 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE ENTERGY TEXAS, INC. BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.3359375 PER SHARE ON ITS SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 15, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 3, 2023.
