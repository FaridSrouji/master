ENTERGY REPORTS 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS, INITIATES 2023 EARNINGS GUIDANCE. RESULTS IN TOP HALF OF GUIDANCE RANGE FOR 7TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR NEW ORLEANS , FEB. 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ETR) REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER 2022 EARNINGS PER SHARE OF 51 CENTS ON AN AS-REPORTED AND ADJUSTED BASIS (NON-GAAP). FOR THE FULL YEAR, THE COMPANY REPORTED 2022 EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $5.37 ON AN AS-REPORTED BASIS AND $6.42 ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS.
