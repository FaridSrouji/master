ENTERGY RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF THE TOP 50 MOST COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN AMERICA AND THIS YEAR'S UTILITIES SECTOR LEADER. NEW ORLEANS , JUNE 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY HAS BEEN NAMED A 2023 HONOREE OF THE CIVIC 50, POINTS OF LIGHT'S PRESTIGIOUS ANNUAL LIST RECOGNIZING THE TOP COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN THE UNITED STATES. THIS IS THE EIGHTH TIME ENTERGY HAS BEEN PART OF THIS LIST.
