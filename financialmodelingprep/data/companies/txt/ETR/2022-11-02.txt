ENTERGY REPORTS THIRD QUARTER EARNINGS. COMPANY NARROWS GUIDANCE AND EXTENDS FINANCIAL OUTLOOKS NEW ORLEANS , NOV. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ETR) REPORTED THIRD QUARTER 2022 EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $2.74 ON AN AS-REPORTED BASIS AND $2.84 ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS (NON-GAAP). "WE CONTINUED TO MAKE MEANINGFUL PROGRESS IN THE THIRD QUARTER, EXECUTING OUR STRATEGY ACROSS REGULATORY, FINANCIAL, AND OPERATIONAL PATHS TO CREATE SUSTAINABLE VALUE FOR ALL OUR STAKEHOLDERS," SAID DREW MARSH, ENTERGY CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
