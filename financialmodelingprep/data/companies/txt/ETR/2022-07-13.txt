ENTERGY'S CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER PAUL HINNENKAMP TO RETIRE, PETE NORGEOT NAMED SUCCESSOR. WITH A FOCUS ON SAFETY AND OPERATIONS, EFFECTIVE SUCCESSION PLANNING ENSURES CONTINUITY IN ENTERGY'S LEADERSHIP NEW ORLEANS , JULY 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PAUL HINNENKAMP, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, WILL RETIRE FROM THE COMPANY FOLLOWING A NEARLY 40-YEAR CAREER IN THE ENERGY INDUSTRY. HIS LAST DAY WILL BE AUG. 12.
