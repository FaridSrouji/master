ENTERGY MISSISSIPPI COMPLETES EASTER STORM RESTORATION. ENTERGY MISSISSIPPI COMPLETES EASTER STORM RESTORATION.CREWS HAVE RESTORED SERVICE TO NEARLY 41,000 CUSTOMERS WHO LOST POWER DURING STORMS THAT STRUCK OUR SERVICE AREA ON SUNDAY, APRIL 12.ALL CUSTOMERS AFFECTED BY THIS STORM WHO CAN SAFELY ACCEPT ELECTRICAL SERVICE HAVE BEEN RESTORED.EXPECT TO RESTORE POWER TO ALL CUSTOMERS AFFECTED BY STORM FROM I-20 TO SOUTHERN PART OF CO'S SERVICE TERRITORY BY END OF DAY TODAY.
