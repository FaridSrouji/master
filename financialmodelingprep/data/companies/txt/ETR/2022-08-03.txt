ENTERGY REPORTS SECOND QUARTER EARNINGS. COMPANY AFFIRMS GUIDANCE AND FINANCIAL OUTLOOKS; EXPECT 2022 RESULTS IN TOP HALF OF THE RANGE NEW ORLEANS , AUG. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ETR) REPORTED SECOND QUARTER 2022 EARNINGS PER SHARE OF 78 CENTS ON AN AS-REPORTED BASIS AND $1.78 ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS (NON-GAAP). "WE HAD A PRODUCTIVE SECOND QUARTER WITH ACCOMPLISHMENTS THAT MADE MEANINGFUL PROGRESS TOWARD OUR STAKEHOLDER OBJECTIVES," SAID LEO DENAULT, ENTERGY CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
