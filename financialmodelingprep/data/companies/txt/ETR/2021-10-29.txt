ENTERGY CORPORATION ANNOUNCES INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEW ORLEANS, OCT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ENTERGY CORPORATION HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $1.01, AN INCREASE OF $0.06 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DEC. 1, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF NOV. 15, 2021.
