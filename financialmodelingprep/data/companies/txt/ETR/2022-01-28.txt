ENTERGY CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT TO SHAREHOLDERS. NEW ORLEANS, JAN. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE ENTERGY CORPORATION BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $1.01 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 1, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEB. 11, 2022.
