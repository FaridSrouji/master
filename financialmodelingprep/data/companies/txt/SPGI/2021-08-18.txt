DEMAND PROLONGS U.S. BROADBAND BOOM THROUGH MID-2021. NEW YORK, AUG. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- U.S. BROADBAND GAINS ILLUSTRATED DURABLE DEMAND FOR WIRELINE CONNECTIONS IN THE SECOND QUARTER, ALLAYING FEARS OF A 2021 HANGOVER, ACCORDING TO KAGAN, A MEDIA RESEARCH GROUP WITHIN S&P GLOBAL MARKET INTELLIGENCE. WHILE THE 945,000 NEW BROADBAND SUBS IN SECOND QUARTER 2021 FALL SHORT OF THE YEAR-AGO BOOM, THE JUMP FAR EXCEEDS THE SECOND-QUARTER 2019 FIGURE THAT CABLE OPERATORS HAVE BEEN POINTING TO AS A MORE LIKELY TEMPLATE FOR CURRENT-YEAR SUCCESS.
