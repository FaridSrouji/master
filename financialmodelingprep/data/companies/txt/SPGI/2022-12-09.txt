S&P CORELOGIC CASE-SHILLER INDEX CONTINUED TO DECLINE IN SEPTEMBER. NEW YORK , DEC. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES (S&P DJI) TODAY RELEASED THE LATEST RESULTS FOR THE S&P CORELOGIC CASE-SHILLER INDICES, THE LEADING MEASURE OF U.S. HOME PRICES. DATA RELEASED TODAY FOR SEPTEMBER 2022 SHOW THAT HOME PRICE GAINS DECLINED ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
