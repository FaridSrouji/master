PERFORMANCE FOOD GROUP & DIGITAL TURBINE SET TO JOIN S&P MIDCAP 400; TREEHOUSE FOODS & LIGAND PHARMACEUTICALS TO JOIN S&P SMALLCAP 600. NEW YORK, AUG. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES WILL MAKE THE FOLLOWING CHANGES TO THE S&P MIDCAP 400 AND S&P SMALLCAP 600: PERFORMANCE FOOD GROUP CO. (NYSE: PFGC) WILL REPLACE TREEHOUSE FOODS INC. (NYSE: THS) IN THE S&P MIDCAP 400, AND TREEHOUSE FOODS WILL REPLACE CORE-MARK HOLDING COMPANY INC. (NASD: CORE) IN THE S&P SMALLCAP 600 EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPENING OF TRADING ON FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 3. PERFORMANCE FOOD GROUP IS ACQUIRING CORE-MARK HOLDING COMPANY IN A DEAL PENDING FINAL CONDITIONS.
