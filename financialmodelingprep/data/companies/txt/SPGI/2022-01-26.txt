S&P GLOBAL DECLARES FIRST QUARTER DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) HAS APPROVED A CASH DIVIDEND ON THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. THE DIVIDEND OF $0.77 IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 10, 2022.
