S&P GLOBAL DECLARES THIRD QUARTER DIVIDEND. NEW YORK , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) HAS APPROVED A CASH DIVIDEND ON THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2023. THE DIVIDEND OF $0.90 IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 12, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 28, 2023.
MASTERMIND AND REYNOLDS AND REYNOLDS INTEGRATION NOW AVAILABLE FOR DEALERSHIPS. NEW YORK , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTOMOTIVEMASTERMIND (AM), PART OF S&P GLOBAL MOBILITY AND THE LEADING PROVIDER OF PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS AND MARKETING AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS FOR DEALERSHIPS, HAS ANNOUNCED DEALERS CAN OFFICIALLY SIGN UP FOR AN INTEGRATION WITH MASTERMIND, AM'S FLAGSHIP AUTOMATED SALES AND MARKETING PLATFORM, AND THE REYNOLDS AND REYNOLDS ERA® AND POWER DEALER MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (DMS). AM PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED AT THIS YEAR'S NADA SHOW THAT THE COMPANY HAD SIGNED A CONTRACT TO JOIN THE REYNOLDS CERTIFIED INTERFACE (RCI) PROGRAM.
S&P CORELOGIC CASE-SHILLER INDEX CONTINUED GAINS IN APRIL. NEW YORK , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES (S&P DJI) TODAY RELEASED THE LATEST RESULTS FOR THE S&P CORELOGIC CASE-SHILLER INDICES, THE LEADING MEASURE OF U.S. HOME PRICES. DATA RELEASED TODAY FOR APRIL 2023 SHOW ALL 20 MAJOR METRO MARKETS AGAIN REPORTED MONTH-OVER-MONTH PRICE INCREASES WITH GAINS ACCELERATING IN 12 MARKETS.
