NATIONAL GRID PLC ASSIGNED ESG EVALUATION SCORE OF 82; PREPAREDNESS STRONG. NEW YORK, MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL RATINGS SAID TODAY THAT IT HAS ASSIGNED NATIONAL GRID PLC (NG) AN ESG EVALUATION SCORE OF 82. THE COMPANY'S ESG EVALUATION SCORE IS THE RESULT OF AN ESG PROFILE OF 75 COMBINED WITH STRONG PREPAREDNESS.
