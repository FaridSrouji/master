S&P GLOBAL CLARIFIES $0.90 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEW YORK , FEB. 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ON JANUARY 25, 2023, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.90 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE PRIOR RELEASE ERRONEOUSLY STATED THE DIVIDEND INCREASED 6.4%.
