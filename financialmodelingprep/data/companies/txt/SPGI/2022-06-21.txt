S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES SHOW SIXTH CONSECUTIVE RISE IN COMPOSITE RATE IN MAY 2022. COMPOSITE RATE AT HIGHEST LEVEL SINCE MARCH 2021 NEW YORK , JUNE 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES AND EXPERIAN RELEASED TODAY DATA THROUGH MAY 2022 FOR THE S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES. THE INDICES REPRESENT A COMPREHENSIVE MEASURE OF CHANGES IN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULTS AND SHOW THAT THE COMPOSITE RATE ROSE ONE BASIS POINT TO 0.51%.
