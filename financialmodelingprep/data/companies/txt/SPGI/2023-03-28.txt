S&P CORELOGIC CASE-SHILLER INDEX DECLINING TREND CONTINUED IN JANUARY. NEW YORK , MARCH 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES (S&P DJI) TODAY RELEASED THE LATEST RESULTS FOR THE S&P CORELOGIC CASE-SHILLER INDICES, THE LEADING MEASURE OF U.S. HOME PRICES. DATA RELEASED TODAY FOR JANUARY 2023 SHOW THAT THE TREND OF DECLINING HOME PRICE GAINS CONTINUED ACROSS THE UNITED STATES WITH DECLINING PRICES REPORTED IN THE SAN FRANCISCO, SAN DIEGO, PORTLAND, AND SEATTLE MARKETS.
