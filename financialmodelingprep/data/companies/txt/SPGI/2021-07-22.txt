S&P GLOBAL SUSTAINABLE1 APPOINTS DR. RICHARD MATTISON AS PRESIDENT. NEW YORK AND LONDON, JULY 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE APPOINTMENT OF DR. RICHARD MATTISON AS PRESIDENT OF ITS ESG AND SUSTAINABILITY ORGANIZATION, S&P GLOBAL SUSTAINABLE1. PRIOR TO THIS APPOINTMENT, DR. MATTISON HELD THE ROLE OF CEO OF TRUCOST, ACQUIRED BY S&P GLOBAL IN 2016 AND CHIEF PRODUCT OFFICER, S&P GLOBAL SUSTAINABLE1.
