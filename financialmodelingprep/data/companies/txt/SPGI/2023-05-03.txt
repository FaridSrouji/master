S&P GLOBAL DECLARES SECOND QUARTER DIVIDEND. NEW YORK , MAY 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) HAS APPROVED A CASH DIVIDEND ON THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2023. THE DIVIDEND OF $0.90 IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 12, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 26, 2023.
