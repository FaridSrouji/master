S&P DOW JONES INDICES REPORTS U.S. INDICATED DIVIDEND PAYMENTS INCREASED $20.9 BILLION IN Q3 2021, BEST GAIN SINCE Q1 2012. NEW YORK, OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT INDICATED DIVIDEND NET CHANGES (INCREASES LESS DECREASES) FOR U.S. DOMESTIC COMMON STOCKS INCREASED $20.9 BILLION DURING Q3 2021, THE LARGEST INCREASE SINCE Q1 2012 ($24.2 BILLION), COMPARED TO $12.9 BILLION IN Q2 2021, AND A DECLINE OF $2.3 BILLION IN Q3 2020 (THE COVID-INSPIRED Q2 2020 WAS -$42.5 BILLION). FOR THE 12-MONTH SEPTEMBER 2021 PERIOD, THE NET DIVIDEND RATE INCREASED $61.4 BILLION, COMPARED TO A NET DECLINE OF $39.7 BILLION FOR 12-MONTH SEPTEMBER 2020 PERIOD, AS INCREASES WERE $71.8 BILLION VERSUS $39.5 BILLION, AND DECREASES WERE $10.5 BILLION COMPARED TO $79.2 BILLION FOR 2020 PERIOD.
