S&P DOW JONES INDICES REACHES SETTLEMENT WITH SEC. NEW YORK, MAY 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES ("S&P DJI"), A DIVISION OF S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS REACHED A SETTLEMENT WITH THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION ("SEC") TO RESOLVE A MATTER THAT WAS THE SUBJECT OF A PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED WELLS NOTICE. THE SETTLEMENT ORDER FINDS THAT S&P DJI, WHICH NEITHER ADMITS NOR DENIES THE SEC'S ALLEGATIONS, ACTED NEGLIGENTLY IN VIOLATION OF SECTION 17(A)(3) OF THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933 WITH RESPECT TO THE OPERATION OF A THEN UNDISCLOSED QUALITY ASSURANCE MECHANISM AND ITS IMPACT ON CERTAIN REAL-TIME VALUES OF THE S&P 500 VIX SHORT-TERM FUTURES INDEX ER ON A SINGLE BUSINESS DAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2018.
