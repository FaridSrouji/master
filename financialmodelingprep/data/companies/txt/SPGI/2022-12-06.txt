S&P GLOBAL ACQUIRES SHADES OF GREEN BUSINESS FROM CICERO. BRINGS COMPLEMENTARY CAPABILITIES WITHIN S&P GLOBAL RATINGS RELATED TO THE PROVISION OF SECOND PARTY OPINIONS NEW YORK , DEC. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI), PROVIDER OF CREDIT RATINGS, BENCHMARKS AND ANALYTICS, HAS ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF THE SHADES OF GREEN BUSINESS FROM THE CENTER FOR INTERNATIONAL CLIMATE RESEARCH (CICERO), NORWAY'S FOREMOST INSTITUTE FOR INTERDISCIPLINARY CLIMATE RESEARCH. THE ACQUISITION WILL BE INTEGRATED INTO S&P GLOBAL RATINGS AND FURTHER EXPAND THE BREADTH AND DEPTH OF ITS SECOND PARTY OPINIONS (SPOS) OFFERING.
