CARA THERAPEUTICS SET TO JOIN S&P SMALLCAP 600. NEW YORK, APRIL 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARA THERAPEUTICS INC. (NASD:CARA) WILL REPLACE MTS SYSTEMS CORP. (NASD:MTSC) IN THE S&P SMALLCAP 600 EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPENING OF TRADING ON THURSDAY, APRIL 8. S&P 500 CONSTITUENT AMPHENOL CORP. (NYSE: APH) IS ACQUIRING MTS SYSTEMS IN A DEAL EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED SOON PENDING FINAL CLOSING CONDITIONS.
