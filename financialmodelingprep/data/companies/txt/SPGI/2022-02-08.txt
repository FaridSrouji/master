S&P GLOBAL REPORTS 4TH QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2021 RESULTS. NEW YORK, FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) TODAY REPORTED FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2021 RESULTS.  THE COMPANY REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER 2021 REVENUE OF $2.09 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF 12% COMPARED TO THE SAME PERIOD IN 2020 WITH GROWTH IN ALL FOUR BUSINESSES.
