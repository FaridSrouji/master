INSULET SET TO JOIN S&P 500. NEW YORK , MARCH 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INSULET CORP. (NASD:PODD) WILL REPLACE SVB FINANCIAL GROUP (NASD:SIVB) IN THE S&P 500 EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPENING OF TRADING ON WEDNESDAY, MARCH 15. THE FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION (FDIC) ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS TAKEN SVB FINANCIAL GROUP INTO FDIC RECEIVERSHIP AND THEREFORE SVB FINANCIAL GROUP IS NO LONGER ELIGIBLE FOR INCLUSION.
