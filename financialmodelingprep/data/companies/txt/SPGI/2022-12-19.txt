STEEL DYNAMICS SET TO JOIN S&P 500; SUPER MICRO COMPUTER TO JOIN S&P MIDCAP 400. NEW YORK , DEC. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P MIDCAP 400 CONSTITUENT STEEL DYNAMICS INC. (NASD:STLD) WILL REPLACE ABIOMED INC. (NASD:ABMD) IN THE S&P 500 AND SUPER MICRO COMPUTER INC. (NASD:SMCI) WILL REPLACE STEEL DYNAMICS IN THE S&P MIDCAP 400 EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPENING OF TRADING ON THURSDAY, DECEMBER 22. S&P 500 CONSTITUENT JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) IS ACQUIRING ABIOMED IN A DEAL EXPECTED TO CLOSE SOON PENDING FINAL CONDITIONS.
