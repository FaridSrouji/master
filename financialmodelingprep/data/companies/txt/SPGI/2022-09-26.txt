S&P GLOBAL MOBILITY: SUPPLY CONSTRAINTS, LACK OF INVENTORY CAP US LIGHT VEHICLE SALES IN SEPTEMBER. 13.4 MILLION-UNIT SAAR EXPECTED FOR 2022 SOUTHFIELD, MICH. , SEPT. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL MOBILITY ANALYSTS EXPECT US LIGHT VEHICLE SALES TO BE LIMITED TO 1.105 MILLION UNITS IN SEPTEMBER, MARKING AN ANNUAL RATE OF 13.4 MILLION UNITS.
