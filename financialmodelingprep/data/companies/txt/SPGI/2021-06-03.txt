S&P DOW JONES INDICES ANNOUNCES UPDATE TO S&P COMPOSITE 1500 MARKET CAP GUIDELINES. NEW YORK, JUNE 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES ("S&P DJI") IS UPDATING THE MARKET CAPITALIZATION ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR NEW ADDITIONS TO THE S&P COMPOSITE 1500 INDICES. EFFECTIVE JUNE 3, 2021, THE SIZE RANGES WILL BE UPDATED AS FOLLOWS.
