S&P GLOBAL MARKET INTELLIGENCE 2023 OUTLOOK SEES ENERGY TRANSITION PICKING UP PACE AND HELPING KEY METALS MARKETS OVERCOME ECONOMIC HEADWINDS AS BURGEONING DEMAND OUTSTRIPS SUPPLY.
