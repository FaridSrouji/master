FUJCON MAKES ITS PHYSICAL RETURN AT FUJAIRAH, UAE, MARCH 13-15 2023. DUBAI AND LONDON , DEC. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE INTERNATIONAL FUJAIRAH BUNKERING & FUEL OIL FORUM (FUJCON) RETURNS FOR AN IN-PERSON, 13TH  CONVENING IN FUJAIRAH ON MARCH 13-15, 2023, FOLLOWING THE 2021 VIRTUAL SESSION AND THE LAST IN-PERSON SESSION IN 2019, PRIOR TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC. THE INTERNATIONAL FUJAIRAH BUNKERING & FUEL OIL FORUM IS NOW ORGANIZED BY S&P GLOBAL COMMODITY INSIGHTS AND HOSTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRY & ECONOMY, GOVERNMENT OF FUJAIRAH AND THE PORT OF FUJAIRAH.
