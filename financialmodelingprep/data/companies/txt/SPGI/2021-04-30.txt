S&P GLOBAL RATINGS PROVIDES FULL ALIGNMENT OPINION ON DANA INC.'S GREEN FINANCING FRAMEWORK. NEW YORK, APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- (S&P GLOBAL RATINGS) -- S&P GLOBAL RATINGS SAID TODAY THAT DANA INC.'S GREEN FINANCING FRAMEWORK IS FULLY ALIGNED WITH THE FOUR COMPONENTS OF THE GREEN BOND PRINCIPLES (GBP) AND GREEN LOAN PRINCIPLES (GLP).
