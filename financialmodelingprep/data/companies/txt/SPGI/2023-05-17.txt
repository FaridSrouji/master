CARFAX: ALARMINGLY HIGH NUMBER OF CARS WITH URGENT RECALLS REMAIN UNREPAIRED IN U.S.. MORE THAN 2.5 MILLION VEHICLES WITH "DO NOT DRIVE" OR "PARK OUTSIDE" ORDERS NEED FIXING CENTREVILLE, VA. , MAY 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MORE THAN 2.5 MILLION VEHICLES THAT HAVE BEEN TAGGED WITH "DO NOT DRIVE" OR "PARK OUTSIDE" SAFETY RECALLS REMAIN UNREPAIRED AS OF MAY 1, CARFAX DATA SHOWS.
