S&P GLOBAL DECLARES THIRD QUARTER DIVIDEND. NEW YORK , JUNE 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) HAS APPROVED A CASH DIVIDEND ON THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022. THE DIVIDEND OF $0.85 IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 12, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 26, 2022.
