S&P GLOBAL SUSTAINABLE1 APPOINTS CHRIS HEUSLER AS PRESIDENT AND DR. RICHARD MATTISON AS VICE CHAIR. NEW YORK AND LONDON , JUNE 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE APPOINTMENT OF DR. RICHARD MATTISON TO THE NEWLY CREATED ROLE OF VICE CHAIR OF S&P GLOBAL SUSTAINABLE1 AND THE APPOINTMENT OF CHRIS HEUSLER  AS PRESIDENT OF S&P GLOBAL SUSTAINABLE1. PRIOR TO THIS APPOINTMENT, CHRIS HEUSLER HELD THE ROLE OF CHIEF COMMERCIAL OFFICER OF S&P GLOBAL RATINGS AND DR. MATTISON HELD THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT OF S&P GLOBAL SUSTAINABLE1.
