S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES SHOW LOWER BANK CARD AND COMPOSITE RATES IN NOVEMBER 2021. NEW YORK, DEC. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES AND EXPERIAN RELEASED TODAY DATA THROUGH NOVEMBER 2021 FOR THE S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES. THE INDICES REPRESENT A COMPREHENSIVE MEASURE OF CHANGES IN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULTS AND SHOW THAT THE COMPOSITE RATE FELL ONE BASIS POINT TO 0.37%.
