(NASD: REGI) IN THE S&P SMALLCAP 600 EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPENING OF TRADING ON THURSDAY, JUNE 16. S&P 500 CONSTITUENT CHEVRON CORP. (NYSE: CVX) ACQUIRED RENEWABLE ENERGY GROUP IN A DEAL THAT CLOSED TODAY, JUNE 13.
