S&P DOW JONES INDICES REPORTS U.S. INDICATED DIVIDEND PAYMENTS INCREASED $18.0 BILLION IN Q4 2021 AND A RECORD $69.8 BILLION IN 2021. NEW YORK, JAN. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT INDICATED DIVIDEND NET CHANGES (INCREASES LESS DECREASES) FOR U.S. DOMESTIC COMMON STOCKS INCREASED $18.0 BILLION DURING Q4 2021, COMPARED TO $20.9 BILLION IN Q3 2021, AND $9.5 BILLION IN Q4 2020. FOR 2021, THE NET DIVIDEND RATE INCREASED $69.8 BILLION, COMPARED TO A NET DECLINE OF $40.8 BILLION FOR 2020, AS INCREASES WERE $78.6 BILLION VERSUS $41.4 BILLION, AND DECREASES WERE $8.8 BILLION COMPARED TO $82.2 BILLION FOR 2020.
S&P GLOBAL ACQUIRES THE CLIMATE SERVICE, INC.. NEW YORK, JAN. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) AND THE CLIMATE SERVICE, INC. ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT S&P GLOBAL HAS ACQUIRED THE CLIMATE SERVICE. BASED IN DURHAM, NORTH CAROLINA THE CLIMATE SERVICE, INC. WAS FOUNDED IN 2017.
