S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES SHOW SECOND STRAIGHT INCREASE IN COMPOSITE RATE IN JANUARY 2022. NEW YORK, FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES AND EXPERIAN RELEASED TODAY DATA THROUGH JANUARY 2022 FOR THE S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES. THE INDICES REPRESENT A COMPREHENSIVE MEASURE OF CHANGES IN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULTS AND SHOW THAT THE COMPOSITE RATE ROSE THREE BASIS POINTS TO 0.43%.
