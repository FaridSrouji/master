S&P CORELOGIC CASE-SHILLER INDEX REPORTS 18.8% ANNUAL HOME PRICE GAIN FOR CALENDAR 2021. NEW YORK, FEB. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES (S&P DJI) TODAY RELEASED THE LATEST RESULTS FOR THE S&P CORELOGIC CASE-SHILLER INDICES, THE LEADING MEASURE OF U.S. HOME PRICES. DATA RELEASED TODAY FOR DECEMBER 2021 SHOW THAT HOME PRICES CONTINUE TO INCREASE ACROSS THE U.S. MORE THAN 27 YEARS OF HISTORY ARE AVAILABLE FOR THE DATA SERIES AND CAN BE ACCESSED IN FULL BY GOING TO HTTPS://WWW.SPGLOBAL.COM/SPDJI/.
