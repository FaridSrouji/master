S&P GLOBAL REVENUE INCREASED 8% IN THE SECOND QUARTER, COMPARED TO EXCEPTIONAL 2Q 2020. NEW YORK, JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) TODAY REPORTED SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS WITH REVENUE OF $2,106 MILLION, AN INCREASE OF 8% COMPARED TO THE SAME PERIOD LAST YEAR WITH EVERY SEGMENT DELIVERING REVENUE GROWTH.  NET INCOME INCREASED 1% TO $798 MILLION.
