THE JOINT CORP. SET TO JOIN S&P SMALLCAP 600. NEW YORK, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE JOINT CORP. (NASD:JYNT) WILL REPLACE CUBIC CORP. (NYSE:CUB) IN THE S&P SMALLCAP 600 EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPENING OF TRADING ON THURSDAY, MAY 27. VERITAS CAPITAL IS ACQUIRING CUBIC IN A DEAL EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED SOON PENDING FINAL CLOSING CONDITIONS.
