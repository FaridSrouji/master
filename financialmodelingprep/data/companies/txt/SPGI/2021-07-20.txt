S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES SHOW THIRD STRAIGHT DROP IN COMPOSITE RATE IN JUNE 2021. NEW YORK, JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES AND EXPERIAN RELEASED TODAY DATA THROUGH JUNE 2021 FOR THE S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES. THE INDICES REPRESENT A COMPREHENSIVE MEASURE OF CHANGES IN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULTS AND SHOW THAT THE COMPOSITE RATE WAS THREE BASIS POINTS LOWER AT 0.41%.
