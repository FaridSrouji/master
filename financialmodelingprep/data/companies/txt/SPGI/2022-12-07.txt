AMERESCO SELECTED AS 2022 S&P GLOBAL PLATTS GLOBAL ENERGY AWARDS FINALIST IN TWO CATEGORIES. FRAMINGHAM, MASS.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMERESCO, INC., (NYSE: AMRC), A LEADING CLEANTECH INTEGRATOR SPECIALIZING IN ENERGY EFFICIENCY AND RENEWABLE ENERGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED A FINALIST IN THE S&P GLOBAL PLATTS 2022 GLOBAL ENERGY AWARDS FOR THE INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECT OF THE YEAR AND CORPORATE IMPACT, SUSTAINED COMMITMENT CATEGORIES.
