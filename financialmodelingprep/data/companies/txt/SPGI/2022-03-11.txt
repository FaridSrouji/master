AUTOMOTIVEMASTERMIND ENHANCES OFFERINGS TO HELP DEALERS GENERATE MORE SERVICE-TO-SALES OPPORTUNITIES. PLATFORM ENHANCEMENTS WILL SUPPORT CUSTOMER LOYALTY AND NEW CUSTOMER ACQUISITION NEW YORK , MARCH 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTOMOTIVEMASTERMIND (AM), PART OF S&P GLOBAL MOBILITY AND A PROVIDER OF PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS AND MARKETING AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS FOR DEALERSHIPS, HAS ANNOUNCED THREE NEW ENHANCEMENTS TO ITS SERVICE-TO-SALES OFFERINGS THROUGH ITS FLAGSHIP AUTOMATED SALES AND MARKETING PLATFORM, MASTERMIND. THE MASTERMIND ENHANCEMENTS SUPPORT THE ENTIRE DEALERSHIP SERVICE-TO-SALES ECOSYSTEM – INCLUDING BRINGING NEW CUSTOMERS INTO THE DOOR, ACTIVATING SERVICE-TO-SALES OPPORTUNITIES AND MAINTAINING CUSTOMER LOYALTY.
