OIL SANDS TO CONTINUE TO GROW BUT HEIGHTENED ENERGY SECURITY CONCERNS HAVE NOT YET BOOSTED OUTLOOK. S&P GLOBAL COMMODITY INSIGHTS 10-YEAR FORECAST EXPECTS OIL SANDS PRODUCTION IN 2030 TO BE 3.5 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY—17 PERCENT HIGHER THAN CURRENT LEVELS CALGARY, AB , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHILE THE RUSSIAN INVASION OF UKRAINE HAS INCREASED THE INCENTIVE TO RAISE CANADIAN OIL SANDS PRODUCTION IN THE SHORT-TERM, IT HAS NOT FUNDAMENTALLY ALTERED THE PRODUCTION OUTLOOK OVER THE LONGER-TERM, ACCORDING TO THE LATEST FORECAST BY THE S&P GLOBAL OIL SANDS DIALOGUE. THE UPDATED 10-YEAR FORECAST EXPECTS CANADIAN OIL SANDS PRODUCTION TO REACH 3.5 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY (MMBD) IN 2030, AN INCREASE OF ABOUT HALF A MILLION BARRELS PER DAY COMPARED TO 2021 PRODUCTION LEVELS, ACCORDING TO THE S&P GLOBAL COMMODITY INSIGHTS FORECAST.
