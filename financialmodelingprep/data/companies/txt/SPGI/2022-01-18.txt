S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES SHOW HIGHER RATES FOR ALL LOAN TYPES IN DECEMBER 2021. NEW YORK, JAN. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES AND EXPERIAN RELEASED TODAY DATA THROUGH DECEMBER 2021 FOR THE S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES. THE INDICES REPRESENT A COMPREHENSIVE MEASURE OF CHANGES IN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULTS AND SHOW THAT THE COMPOSITE RATE ROSE THREE BASIS POINTS TO 0.40%.
