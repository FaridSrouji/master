S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES SHOW LOWER BANK CARD AND COMPOSITE RATES IN OCTOBER 2021. NEW YORK, NOV. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES AND EXPERIAN RELEASED TODAY DATA THROUGH OCTOBER 2021 FOR THE S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES. THE INDICES REPRESENT A COMPREHENSIVE MEASURE OF CHANGES IN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULTS AND SHOW THAT THE COMPOSITE RATE FELL ONE BASIS POINT TO 0.38%.
