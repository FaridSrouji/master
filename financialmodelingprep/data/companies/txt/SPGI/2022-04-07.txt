S&P DOW JONES INDICES ANNOUNCES TREATMENT OF AT&T TRANSACTION WITH DISCOVERY. NEW YORK , APRIL 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P 500 & 100 CONSTITUENT AT&T INC. (NYSE:T) WILL SPIN OFF ITS INTEREST IN WARNERMEDIA, AND IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING THE SPIN-OFF, WARNERMEDIA WILL MERGE WITH S&P 500 CONSTITUENT DISCOVERY INC. (NASD:DISCK,DISCA,DISCB). AT THE TIME OF THE TRANSACTION, ALL CLASSES OF SHARES OF DISCOVERY COMMON STOCK WILL BE CONVERTED AND RECLASSIFIED INTO A SINGLE SHARE CLASS LINE OF NEWLY NAMED WARNER BROS.
