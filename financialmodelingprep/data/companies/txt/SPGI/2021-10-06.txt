JOB LISTINGS AT S&P 500 COMPANIES UP 52% IN 2021 ACCORDING TO S&P 500 LINKUP JOBS INDEX. NEW YORK, OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES (S&P DJI) TODAY RELEASED THE LATEST DATA FOR THE S&P 500 LINKUP JOBS INDEX. THE INDEX, WHICH MEASURES THE CHANGE IN OPEN JOB LISTINGS AT S&P 500 COMPANIES, SHOWS THAT AVAILABLE POSITIONS HAVE INCREASED BY 52% IN 2021 AS COMPANIES LOOK TO BOOST HIRING IN THE ECONOMIC RECOVERY FROM THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
