S&P DOW JONES INDICES LAUNCHES NET ZERO 2050 CLIMATE TRANSITION AND PARIS-ALIGNED SELECT INDICES. LONDON, NOV. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES ("S&P DJI"), THE WORLD'S LEADING INDEX PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE S&P NET ZERO 2050 CLIMATE TRANSITION SELECT INDEX SERIES AND S&P NET ZERO 2050 PARIS-ALIGNED SELECT INDEX SERIES. THESE NEW INDICES ARE SPECIFICALLY DESIGNED AS UNDERLYING INDICES FOR THE STRUCTURED PRODUCT MARKET WHICH HAS BEEN INCREASINGLY INCORPORATING ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PRINCIPLES.
