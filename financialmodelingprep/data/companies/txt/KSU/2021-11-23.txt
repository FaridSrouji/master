SURFACE TRANSPORTATION BOARD ACCEPTS CP-KCS MERGER APPLICATION AS COMPLETE, SETS PROCEDURAL SCHEDULE. CALGARY, AB AND KANSAS CITY, MO., NOV. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ - CANADIAN PACIFIC RAILWAY LIMITED (TSX: CP) (NYSE: CP) ("CP") AND KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN (NYSE: KSU) ("KCS") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE SURFACE TRANSPORTATION BOARD ("STB") HAS ACCEPTED THE JOINT CP-KCS MERGER APPLICATION AS COMPLETE.
