KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN DECLARES DIVIDENDS ON 4% NON-CUMULATIVE PREFERRED STOCK AND COMMON STOCK. KANSAS CITY, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN'S (KCS) (NYSE:KSU) BOARD OF DIRECTORS ON NOVEMBER 5, 2021 DECLARED A REGULAR DIVIDEND OF $0.25 PER SHARE ON THE OUTSTANDING KCS 4% NON-CUMULATIVE PREFERRED STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 18, 2022 TO PREFERRED STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 31, 2021.
