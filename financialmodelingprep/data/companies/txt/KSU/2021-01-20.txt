KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN AND NORTHPOINT DEVELOPMENT TO DEVELOP 220-ACRE WYLIE LOGISTICS PARK IN TEXAS. KANSAS CITY, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN (KCS) (NYSE: KSU) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A JOINT AGREEMENT WITH NORTHPOINT DEVELOPMENT TO DEVELOP THE MASTER PLANNED WYLIE LOGISTICS PARK IN WYLIE, TEXAS, LOCATED ADJACENT TO KCS' DAVID L. STARLING WYLIE INTERMODAL TERMINAL. THE WYLIE LOGISTICS PARK OFFERS 2.4 MILLION SQUARE FEET OF POTENTIAL BUILDING CAPACITY FOR TRADITIONAL WAREHOUSING AND DISTRIBUTION; INDUSTRIAL GRADE AMENITIES; DUAL FEED ELECTRICAL SYSTEM WITH REDUND
