SAVAGE PARTNERS WITH KCS TO CONSTRUCT AND OPERATE LOUISIANA RAILPORT TO PROVIDE NEEDED TRANSLOAD AND RAILCAR STORAGE SOLUTIONS FOR AREA INDUSTRIES. SALT LAKE CITY & KANSAS CITY, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SAVAGE IS PARTNERING WITH KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN (KCS) (NYSE: KSU) TO CONSTRUCT A MULTI-COMMODITY RAILPORT WITH TRANSLOAD AND RAILCAR STORAGE CAPABILITIES IN MOSSVILLE, LA. SAVAGE WILL OWN AND OPERATE THE RAILPORT, BEING BUILT ON PROPERTY LEASED FROM KCS AT ITS MOSSVILLE RAIL YARD.
