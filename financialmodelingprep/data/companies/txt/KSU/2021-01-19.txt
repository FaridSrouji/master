KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN COMMITS TO SETTING A SCIENCE-BASED EMISSIONS REDUCTION TARGET. KANSAS CITY, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN (KCS) (NYSE: KSU) ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SUBMITTED A COMMITMENT LETTER TO THE SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE (SBTI) PLEDGING TO SET A SCIENCE-BASED GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS REDUCTION TARGET. KCS' SCIENCE-BASED TARGET WILL ALIGN WITH WHAT CLIMATE SCIENTISTS SAY IS NEEDED TO MEET THE PARIS AGREEMENT GOAL OF LIMITING GLOBAL WARMING TO WELL BELOW 2°C ABOVE PRE-INDUSTRIAL LEVELS.
