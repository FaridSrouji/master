KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN REPORTS THIRD QUARTER RESULTS. KANSAS CITY, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN (KCS) (NYSE:KSU) REPORTED REVENUES OF $744.0 MILLION, AN INCREASE OF 13% FROM THIRD QUARTER 2020.
