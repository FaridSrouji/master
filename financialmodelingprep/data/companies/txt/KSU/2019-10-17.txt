KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 747.7 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 699 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 282 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 265.4 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 180.2 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 173.6 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.81 COMPARED TO USD 1.71 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.81 COMPARED TO USD 1.7 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 2,136.5 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,020 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 650.3 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 729.9 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 411.7 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 466.3 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 4.12 COMPARED TO USD 4.56 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 4.1 COMPARED TO USD 4.55 A YEAR AGO.
