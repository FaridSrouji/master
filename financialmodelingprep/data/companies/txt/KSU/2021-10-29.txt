CANADIAN PACIFIC AND KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN FILE MERGER APPLICATION WITH STB TO CREATE ONLY SINGLE-LINE RAIL NETWORK LINKING U.S.-MEXICO-CANADA. CALGARY, AB AND KANSAS CITY, MO., OCT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ - CANADIAN PACIFIC RAILWAY LIMITED (TSX: CP) (NYSE: CP) ("CP") AND KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN (NYSE: KSU) ("KCS") TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE JOINTLY FILED A RAILROAD CONTROL APPLICATION WITH THE SURFACE TRANSPORTATION BOARD ("STB") REGARDING THE PROPOSED TRANSACTION TO CREATE CANADIAN PACIFIC KANSAS CITY ("CPKC"), THE ONLY SINGLE-LINE RAILROAD LINKING THE UNITED STATES, MEXICO AND CANADA.
