CENTERPOINT ENERGY ANNOUNCES 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS. HOUSTON, MARCH 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTERPOINT ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: CNP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS WILL BE HELD ON FRIDAY, APRIL 23, 2021 AT 9 A.M. CDT IN THE CENTERPOINT ENERGY TOWER AUDITORIUM, 1111 LOUISIANA STREET, HOUSTON, TEXAS.
