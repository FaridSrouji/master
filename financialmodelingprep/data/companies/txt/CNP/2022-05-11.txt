CENTERPOINT ENERGY ISSUES ALL-SOURCE RFP TARGETING RENEWABLES, THERMAL AND DEMAND SIDE RESOURCES AND SHORT-TERM CAPACITY. EVANSVILLE, IND. , MAY 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTERPOINT ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: CNP) HAS ANNOUNCED THAT ITS  INDIANA-BASED ELECTRIC UTILITY BUSINESS, CENTERPOINT ENERGY INDIANA SOUTH, IS OPENING A NEW ALL-SOURCE REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS (RFP) TO SEEK A COMBINATION OF RESOURCES INCLUDING RENEWABLES (WIND, SOLAR AND BATTERY STORAGE), THERMAL AND DEMAND-SIDE RESOURCES, AND SHORT-TERM CAPACITY TO MEET THE FUTURE NEEDS OF ITS 150,000 ELECTRIC CUSTOMERS IN SOUTHWESTERN INDIANA.
