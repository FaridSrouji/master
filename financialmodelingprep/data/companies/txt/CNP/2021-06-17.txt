CENTERPOINT ENERGY PROPOSES NEXT STEP IN SMART ENERGY FUTURE PLAN. EVANSVILLE, IND., JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTERPOINT ENERGY (NYSE: CNP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FILING OF AN APPLICATION WITH THE INDIANA UTILITY REGULATORY COMMISSION (IURC) REQUESTING APPROVAL TO CONSTRUCT TWO NATURAL GAS COMBUSTION TURBINES TO REPLACE PORTIONS OF ITS EXISTING COAL-FIRED GENERATION FLEET.
