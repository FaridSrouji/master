CENTERPOINT ENERGY DECLARES REGULAR COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.1700. HOUSTON , APRIL 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTERPOINT ENERGY, INC.'S (NYSE: CNP) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.1700 PER SHARE ON ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING SHARES OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON JUNE 9, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 19, 2022. ABOUT CENTERPOINT ENERGY  AS THE ONLY INVESTOR-OWNED ELECTRIC AND NATURAL GAS UTILITY BASED IN TEXAS, CENTERPOINT ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: CNP) IS AN ENERGY DELIVERY COMPANY WITH ELECTRIC TRANSMISSION AND DISTRIBUTION, POWER GENERATION AND NATURAL GAS DISTRIBUTION OPERATIONS THAT SERVE MORE THAN 7 MILLION METERED CUSTOMERS IN INDIANA, LOUISIANA, MINNESOTA, MISSISSIPPI, OHIO AND TEXAS.
