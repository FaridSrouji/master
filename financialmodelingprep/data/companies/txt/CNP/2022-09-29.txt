CENTERPOINT ENERGY DECLARES REGULAR COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.1800. HOUSTON , SEPT. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTERPOINT ENERGY, INC.'S (NYSE: CNP) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.1800 PER SHARE ON THE ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING SHARES OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 8, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 17, 2022.
