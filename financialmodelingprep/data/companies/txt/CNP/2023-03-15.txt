CENTERPOINT ENERGY ANNOUNCES APPOINTMENT OF CHRISTOPHER FOSTER AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. HOUSTON , MARCH 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTERPOINT ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: CNP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CHRISTOPHER FOSTER AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE MAY 5. FOSTER WILL OVERSEE THE COMPANY'S FINANCE ORGANIZATION, INCLUDING ACCOUNTING, FINANCIAL PLANNING AND ANALYSIS, TREASURY, TAX, INTERNAL AUDIT, AND INVESTOR RELATIONS.
