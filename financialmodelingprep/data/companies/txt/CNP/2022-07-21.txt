CENTERPOINT ENERGY DECLARES AN INCREASE TO THE REGULAR COMMON STOCK QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO $0.1800 AND SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND OF $30.6250. HOUSTON , JULY 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTERPOINT ENERGY, INC.'S (NYSE: CNP) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED DIVIDENDS ON SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK AND SERIES A PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK. COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.1800 PER SHARE ON THE ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING SHARES OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 8, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 18, 2022.
