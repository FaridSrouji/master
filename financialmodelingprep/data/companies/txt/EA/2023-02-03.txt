MAX VERSTAPPEN SIGNS WITH EA SPORTS™. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ELECTRONIC ARTS INC. (NASDAQ: EA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT EA SPORTS™, A LEADER IN INTERACTIVE SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT, HAS SIGNED A PARTNERSHIP WITH BACK-TO-BACK FORMULA 1® WORLD CHAMPION MAX VERSTAPPEN. THE AGREEMENT WILL SEE THE ORACLE RED BULL RACING DRIVER COLLABORATE WITH THE BRAND TO CREATE CONTENT ACROSS THE EA SPORTS PORTFOLIO. THE DEAL ALSO INCLUDES SPONSORSHIP WITH VERSTAPPEN FOR THE 2023 SEASON, WITH EA SPORTS BRANDING FEATURING ON THE CHIN OF HIS RACE HELMET.
