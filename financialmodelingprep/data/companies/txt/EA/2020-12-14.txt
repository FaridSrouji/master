ELECTRONIC ARTS REACHES AGREEMENT FOR RECOMMENDED ACQUISITION OF CODEMASTERS GROUP HOLDINGS PLC. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, ELECTRONIC ARTS INC. (NASDAQ: EA) ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS REACHED AN AGREEMENT WITH THE BOARD OF CODEMASTERS FOR THE RECOMMENDED ACQUISITION OF CODEMASTERS, THE UK-BASED GAME DEVELOPER AND PUBLISHER. IN THE TRANSACTION, CODEMASTERS' SHAREHOLDERS WILL BE ENTITLED TO RECEIVE 604 PENCE (APPROXIMATELY US$7.98*) IN CASH FOR EACH ORDINARY SHARE OF CODEMASTERS WITH AN IMPLIED ENTERPRISE VALUE OF US$1.2 BILLION*. THE ACQUISITION IS ANTICIPATED TO BE COMPLETED
