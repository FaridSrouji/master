FOX CORPORATION AND COX COMMUNICATIONS ANNOUNCE LONG-TERM RENEWAL AGREEMENT. FOX CORPORATION AND COX COMMUNICATIONS ANNOUNCED A LONG-TERM RENEWAL OF THEIR DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT FOR FOX'S PORTFOLIO OF CHANNELS, INCLUDING RETRANSMISSION CONSENT FOR THE FOX TELEVISION STATIONS. THE RENEWAL COVERS DISTRIBUTION OF THE FOX TELEVISION STATIONS, FOX NEWS CHANNEL, FOX BUSINESS NETWORK, FS1, FS2, BTN, AND FOX DEPORTES.
