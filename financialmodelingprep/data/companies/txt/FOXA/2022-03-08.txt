FOX CORPORATION CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER JOHN NALLEN TO PARTICIPATE AT UPCOMING DEUTSCHE BANK ANNUAL MEDIA, INTERNET & TELECOM CONFERENCE. NEW YORK AND LOS ANGELES, MARCH 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: FOXA, FOX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER JOHN NALLEN WILL PARTICIPATE IN A KEYNOTE SESSION AT THE DEUTSCHE BANK ANNUAL MEDIA, INTERNET & TELECOM CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, MARCH 15 AT APPROXIMATELY 8:05AM (EASTERN), 5:05AM (PACIFIC). A LIVE AND ARCHIVED WEBCAST OF THE PRESENTATION WILL BE AVAILABLE AT INVESTOR.FOXCORPORATION.COM.
