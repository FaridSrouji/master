COMSCORE ANNOUNCES EXPANDED RELATIONSHIP WITH FOX. RESTON, VA., AUG. 6 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMSCORE (NASDAQ: SCOR), A TRUSTED PARTNER FOR PLANNING, TRANSACTING AND EVALUATING MEDIA ACROSS PLATFORMS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS EXPANDED AND EXTENDED ITS RELATIONSHIP WITH FOX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: FOXA, FOX), WHICH INCLUDES COMSCORE'S NATIONAL TV MEASUREMENT.
