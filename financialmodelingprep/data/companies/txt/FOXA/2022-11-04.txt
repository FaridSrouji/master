FOX CORPORATION STATEMENT ON ARBITRATION RULING. NEW YORK AND LOS ANGELES , NOV. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: FOXA, FOX; "FOX" OR THE "COMPANY") TODAY MADE THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT IN RESPONSE TO THE ARBITRATION RULING ISSUED BY THE JUDICIAL ARBITRATION AND MEDIATION SERVICES (JAMS) DECIDING THE CLAIMS A FOX SUBSIDIARY FILED AGAINST FLUTTER ENTERTAINMENT PLC ("FLUTTER") LAST YEAR.   "FOX IS PLEASED WITH THE FAIR AND FAVORABLE OUTCOME OF THE FLUTTER ARBITRATION.
