FOX CORPORATION ANNOUNCES FORMATION OF TUBI MEDIA GROUP. PAUL CHEESBROUGH SET TO LEAD THE NEW BUSINESS UNIT AS CEO FARHAD MASSOUDI TO STEP DOWN, NEW CEO OF TUBI STREAMING TO BE ANNOUNCED NEW YORK AND LOS ANGELES , APRIL 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOX CORPORATION ("FOX" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: FOXA, FOX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FORMATION OF THE TUBI MEDIA GROUP UNDER THE LEADERSHIP OF NEWLY APPOINTED CEO, PAUL CHEESBROUGH. THE NEW DIVISION WILL HOUSE FOX'S STANDALONE DIGITAL BUSINESSES - INCLUDING TUBI, CREDIBLE AND BLOCKCHAIN CREATIVE LABS - AS WELL AS THE DIGITAL PLATFORMS AND TEAMS THAT UNDERPIN FOX'S WIDER DIGITAL BUSINESS IN NEWS, SPORTS AND ENTERTAINMENT.
