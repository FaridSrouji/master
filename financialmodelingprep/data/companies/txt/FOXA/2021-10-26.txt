FOX CORPORATION NAMES STEPHEN POTENZA DEPUTY GENERAL COUNSEL OF FOX NEWS MEDIA. LOS ANGELES, OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: FOXA, FOX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT STEPHEN POTENZA HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS DEPUTY GENERAL COUNSEL OF FOX NEWS MEDIA. POTENZA WILL REPORT TO BERNARD GUGAR, GENERAL COUNSEL AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT OF FOX NEWS MEDIA.
