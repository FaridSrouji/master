FOX CORPORATION CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER STEVE TOMSIC TO PARTICIPATE IN UPCOMING CREDIT SUISSE 24TH ANNUAL COMMUNICATIONS CONFERENCE. NEW YORK AND LOS ANGELES , JUNE 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: FOXA, FOX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER STEVE TOMSIC WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE CREDIT SUISSE 24TH ANNUAL COMMUNICATIONS CONFERENCE ON JUNE 14, AT APPROXIMATELY 9:30AM (EASTERN), 6:30AM (PACIFIC). A LIVE AND ARCHIVED WEBCAST OF THE PRESENTATION WILL BE AVAILABLE AT INVESTOR.FOXCORPORATION.COM.
