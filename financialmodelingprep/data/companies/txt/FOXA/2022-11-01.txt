FOX BUSINESS NETWORK MARKS SEVEN MONTHS AS LEADER IN BUSINESS NEWS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FOX BUSINESS NETWORK (FBN) CELEBRATED ITS 15TH ANNIVERSARY NOTCHING ITS SEVENTH CONSECUTIVE MONTH AS THE LEADER IN BUSINESS NEWS, BESTING CNBC WITH A 29% ADVANTAGE IN BUSINESS DAY VIEWERS, ACCORDING TO NIELSEN MEDIA RESEARCH.
