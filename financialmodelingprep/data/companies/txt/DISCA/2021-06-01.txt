DISCOVERY, INC. ANNOUNCES "WARNER BROS. DISCOVERY" AS NEW NAME FOR PROPOSED LEADING GLOBAL ENTERTAINMENT COMPANY. BURBANK, CALIF., JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  DISCOVERY, INC. (NASDAQ: DISCA, DISCB, DISCK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE NEW NAME OF THE PROPOSED STANDALONE GLOBAL ENTERTAINMENT COMPANY THAT WILL EMERGE FROM THE COMBINATION OF WARNERMEDIA AND DISCOVERY ASSETS: "WARNER BROS.
