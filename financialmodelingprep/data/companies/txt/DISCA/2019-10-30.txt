SKYLIFETV CO., LTD TO FORM JOINT VENTURE WITH DISCOVERY, INC. SKYLIFETV CO., LTD HAS SIGNED AN AGREEMENT TO FORM A JOINT VENTURE WITH DISCOVERY, INC. THE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT INCLUDES THE LAUNCH OF A PRODUCTION COMPANY BY DISCOVERY NETWORKS ASIA PACIFIC AND SKYLIFETV TO CREATE NON-SCRIPTED KOREAN ENTERTAINMENT CONTENT BOTH FOR KOREAN AND INTERNATIONAL AUDIENCES. THE JOINT VENTURE IS EXPECTED TO BE LAUNCHED BY THE END OF THE YEAR.
