VALERO ANNOUNCES LONG-TERM AGREEMENTS FOR THREE ADDITIONAL REFINED PRODUCT TERMINALS IN MEXICO.VALERO MARKETING AND SUPPLY DE MÉXICO - GUADALAJARA TERMINAL IS EXPECTED TO HAVE ABOUT 900,000 BARRELS OF STORAGE CAPACITY.VALERO MARKETING AND SUPPLY DE MÉXICO - MONTERREY TERMINAL IS EXPECTED TO HAVE ABOUT 425,000 BARRELS OF STORAGE CAPACITY.VALERO MARKETING AND SUPPLY DE MÉXICO - THREE NEW REFINED PRODUCT TERMINALS ARE EXPECTED TO START OPERATIONS IN 2021 IN MEXICO.VALERO MARKETING AND SUPPLY DE MÉXICO - GUADALAJARA, MONTERREY TERMINALS TO BE CONSTRUCTED IN SEPARATE JV WITH NO CASH CONTRIBUTIONS FROM VALERO.
