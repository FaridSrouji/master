VALERO ANNOUNCES CEO TRANSITION PLAN. SAN ANTONIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VALERO ENERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: VLO, “VALERO”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JOSEPH W. GORDER, VALERO'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, HAS CHOSEN TO RETIRE AS CEO AND HAS BEEN ELECTED AS EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, BOTH EFFECTIVE JUNE 30, 2023. IN HIS NEW ROLE, MR. GORDER WILL LEAD THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, AND CONTINUE TO PROVIDE ADVICE AND COUNSEL TO THE CEO AND VALERO'S LEADERSHIP ON COMPANY DECISIONS. R. LANE RIGGS, VALERO'S PRESIDENT AND CHIEF O.
