VALERO ENERGY CORPORATION ANNOUNCES THE REDEMPTION OF ITS 2.700% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2023. THE NOTES WERE PREVIOUSLY SUBJECT TO AN “ANY AND ALL” TENDER OFFER BY VALERO, WHICH IS EXPECTED TO SETTLE TOMORROW.
