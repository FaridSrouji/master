VALERO ENERGY REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. SAN ANTONIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VALERO ENERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: VLO, “VALERO”) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO VALERO STOCKHOLDERS OF $2.8 BILLION, OR $7.19 PER SHARE, FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022, COMPARED TO $463 MILLION, OR $1.13 PER SHARE, FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021.
