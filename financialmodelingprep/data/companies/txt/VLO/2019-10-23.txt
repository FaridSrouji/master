VALERO ENERGY CORPORATION REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. VALERO ENERGY CORPORATION ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 27,249 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 30,849 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 881 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,219 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 609 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 856 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.48 COMPARED TO USD 2.01 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 80,445 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 88,303 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 2,097 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 3,273 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 1,362 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,170 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 3.28 COMPARED TO USD 5.05 A YEAR AGO.
