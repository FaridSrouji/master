T. ROWE PRICE GROUP DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. 37TH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASE BALTIMORE , FEB. 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- T. ROWE PRICE GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ-GS: TROW), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.22 PER SHARE PAYABLE MARCH 30, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 15, 2023.
