ROBERT HIGGINBOTHAM TO RETIRE FROM T. ROWE PRICE. DEE SAWYER NAMED AS SUCCESSOR AND NEW HEAD OF GLOBAL DISTRIBUTION BALTIMORE , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- T. ROWE PRICE GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ: TROW), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ROBERT HIGGINBOTHAM, HEAD OF GLOBAL DISTRIBUTION AND GLOBAL PRODUCT, PLANS TO RETIRE AT YEAR-END AFTER 11 YEARS WITH THE FIRM AND 33 YEARS IN THE INDUSTRY.
