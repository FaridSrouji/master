T. ROWE PRICE NAMES THERESA MCLAUGHLIN AS HEAD OF GLOBAL MARKETING. BALTIMORE, OCT. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- T. ROWE PRICE GROUP (NASDAQ-GS: TROW) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THERESA MCLAUGHLIN, A RECOGNIZED MARKETING LEADER WITH MORE THAN 25 YEARS OF FINANCIAL INDUSTRY EXPERIENCE, WILL JOIN THE FIRM ON OCTOBER 25, 2021, AS HEAD OF GLOBAL MARKETING.
