T. ROWE PRICE ISSUES STATEMENT ON CARDIN-PORTMAN RETIREMENT SAVINGS LEGISLATION INTRODUCED IN THE SENATE. BALTIMORE, MAY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- "WE COMMEND SENATORS CARDIN AND PORTMAN FOR REINTRODUCING THEIR BIPARTISAN RETIREMENT SAVINGS BILL, WHICH INCLUDES MANY COMMON-SENSE CHANGES TO CURRENT LAW THAT IMPROVE INDIVIDUALS' ABILITY TO SAVE FOR RETIREMENT," SAID GEORGE RIEDEL, HEAD OF U.S. INTERMEDIARIES AT T. ROWE PRICE.
