T. ROWE PRICE GROUP REPORTS PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT FOR MARCH 2023. BALTIMORE, APRIL 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- T. ROWE PRICE GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ-GS: TROW), TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT OF $1.34 TRILLION AS OF MARCH 31, 2023. PRELIMINARY NET OUTFLOWS FOR MARCH 2023 WERE $4.5 BILLION.
