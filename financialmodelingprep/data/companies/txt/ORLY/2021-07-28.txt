O'REILLY AUTOMOTIVE, INC. REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. SPRINGFIELD, MO., JULY 28, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- O'REILLY AUTOMOTIVE, INC. (THE “COMPANY” OR “O'REILLY”) ( NASDAQ: ORLY), A LEADING RETAILER IN THE AUTOMOTIVE AFTERMARKET INDUSTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED RECORD REVENUE AND EARNINGS FOR ITS SECOND QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021, AND ANNOUNCED THE FUTURE RETIREMENT AND LEADERSHIP SUCCESSION PLAN FOR JEFF SHAW, COO AND CO-PRESIDENT.
