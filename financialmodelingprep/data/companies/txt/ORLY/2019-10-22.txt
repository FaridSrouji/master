O'REILLY AUTOMOTIVE INC. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. O'REILLY AUTOMOTIVE INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 2,666.528 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,482.717 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 391.293 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 366.151 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 5.14 COMPARED TO USD 4.54 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 5.08 COMPARED TO USD 4.5 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 7,667.010 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 7,221.471 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 1,066.126 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,024.130 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 13.77 COMPARED TO USD 12.5 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 13.63 COMPARED TO USD 12.36 A YEAR AGO.
