O'REILLY AUTOMOTIVE, INC. REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2022 RESULTS. SPRINGFIELD, MO., FEB. 08, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- O'REILLY AUTOMOTIVE, INC. (THE “COMPANY” OR “O'REILLY”) ( NASDAQ: ORLY), A LEADING RETAILER IN THE AUTOMOTIVE AFTERMARKET INDUSTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED RECORD REVENUE AND EARNINGS FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2022. THE RESULTS REPRESENT 30 CONSECUTIVE YEARS OF COMPARABLE STORES SALES GROWTH AND RECORD REVENUE AND OPERATING INCOME FOR O'REILLY SINCE BECOMING A PUBLIC COMPANY IN APRIL OF 1993.
