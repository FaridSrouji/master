HERSHEY COMPANY DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 16, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE HERSHEY COMPANY ANNOUNCED QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS OF $0.773 ON THE COMMON STOCK AND $0.702 ON THE CLASS B COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDENDS WERE DECLARED ON OCTOBER 22, 2019, AND ARE PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 16, 2019, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD NOVEMBER 22, 2019.
