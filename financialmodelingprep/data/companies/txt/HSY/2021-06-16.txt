HERSHEY NAMED TO THE CIVIC 50 AND RECOGNIZED AS CONSUMER STAPLES LEADER FOR COMMITMENT TO EMPOWERING COMMUNITIES. HERSHEY, PA., JUNE 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) HAS BEEN NAMED A 2021 HONOREE OF THE CIVIC 50 BY POINTS OF LIGHT, THE WORLD'S LARGEST ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO VOLUNTEER SERVICE.
