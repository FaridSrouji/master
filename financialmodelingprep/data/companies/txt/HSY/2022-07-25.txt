ORGANIQ KANGAROO ISLAND TEAMS WITH THE HERSHEY'S BRAND TO INTRODUCE LUXURIOUS CHOCOLATE BODY SCRUB. ADELAIDE, AUSTRALIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ORGANIQ KANGAROO ISLAND, AN AUSTRALIAN CLEAN BEAUTY BRAND, TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP WITH THE HERSHEY'S BRAND TO INTRODUCE A BODY TREATMENT INSPIRED BY THE CLASSIC HERSHEY'S MILK CHOCOLATE CANDY BAR. LAUNCHING THIS FALL, ORGANIQ HERSHEY'S CACAO BODY SCRUB PROVIDES A DECADENT SELFCARE EXPERIENCE USING 100% NATURAL, VEGAN, AND CRUELTY FREE INGREDIENTS.
