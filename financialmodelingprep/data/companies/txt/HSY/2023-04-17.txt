HERSHEY TO ACQUIRE TWO MANUFACTURING FACILITIES FROM WEAVER POPCORN MANUFACTURING. HERSHEY, PA., APRIL 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE TWO MANUFACTURING PLANTS FROM WEAVER POPCORN MANUFACTURING, A RECOGNIZED LEADER IN POPCORN PRODUCTION AND CO-PACKING, AND A CO-MANUFACTURER OF HERSHEY'S SKINNYPOP BRAND. THROUGH THE DEAL, HERSHEY WILL ACQUIRE WEAVER'S OPERATIONS IN BETHLEHEM, PENNSYLVANIA AND WHITESTOWN, INDIANA.
