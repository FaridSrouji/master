THE HERSHEY COMPANY ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $350,000,000 4.250% NOTES DUE 2028 AND $400,000,000 4.500% NOTES DUE 2033. HERSHEY, PA. , MAY 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF ITS OFFERING OF $350,000,000 OF 4.250% NOTES DUE 2028 AND $400,000,000 OF 4.500% NOTES DUE 2033 (THE "NOTES OFFERING") IN A PUBLIC OFFERING.
