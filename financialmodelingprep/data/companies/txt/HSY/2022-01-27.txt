THE HERSHEY COMPANY EARNS TOP SCORE ON HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN'S 2022 CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX. HERSHEY, PA. , JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT RECEIVED A SCORE OF 100 ON THE HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION'S 2022 CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX (CEI), THE NATION'S FOREMOST BENCHMARKING REPORT MEASURING CORPORATE POLICIES AND PRACTICES RELATED TO LGBTQ+ WORKPLACE EQUALITY.
