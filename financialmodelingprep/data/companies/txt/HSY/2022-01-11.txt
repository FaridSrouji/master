THE HERSHEY COMPANY RECOGNIZED FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR AS ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES BY JUST CAPITAL AND CNBC. HERSHEY, PA., JAN. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY HAS BEEN NAMED AS ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES BY JUST CAPITAL AND MEDIA PARTNER CNBC FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
