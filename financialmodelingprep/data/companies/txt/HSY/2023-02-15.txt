THE HERSHEY COMPANY PURCHASES STOCK FROM HERSHEY TRUST COMPANY. HERSHEY, PA. , FEB. 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED A PURCHASE OF ONE MILLION SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK FROM HERSHEY TRUST COMPANY, AS TRUSTEE FOR THE MILTON HERSHEY SCHOOL TRUST, FOR APPROXIMATELY $240 MILLION, OR $239.91 PER SHARE.
