HERSHEY ANNOUNCES INTENT TO ACQUIRE DOT'S HOMESTYLE PRETZELS AND PRETZELS INC.. HERSHEY, PA., NOV. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE DOT'S PRETZELS LLC, THE OWNER OF DOT'S HOMESTYLE PRETZELS, THE FASTEST-GROWING SCALE 1  BRAND IN THE PRETZEL CATEGORY.
