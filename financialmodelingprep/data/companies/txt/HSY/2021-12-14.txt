HERSHEY COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF DOT'S PRETZELS AND PRETZELS INC.. HERSHEY, PA., DEC. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF ITS ACQUISITION OF DOT'S PRETZELS, THE OWNER OF DOT'S HOMESTYLE PRETZELS, AND PRETZELS INC., A LEADING MANUFACTURER OF PRETZELS IN THE UNITED STATES.
