KISCO SENIOR LIVING, WELLTOWER BREAK GROUND ON THE CARNEGIE AT WASHINGTONIAN CENTER IN GAITHERSBURG, MD.. GAITHERSBURG, MD., OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KISCO SENIOR LIVING AND JOINT VENTURE PARTNER WELLTOWER (NYSE: WELL) ARE PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THE GROUNDBREAKING OF THE CARNEGIE AT WASHINGTONIAN CENTER, A UNIQUE CONCEPT FOR SENIOR LIVING IN THE HEART OF GAITHERSBURG.
