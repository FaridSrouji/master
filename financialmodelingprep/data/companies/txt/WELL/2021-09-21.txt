S&P GLOBAL RATINGS REVISES WELLTOWER'S OUTLOOK TO STABLE AND AFFIRMS 'BBB+' ISSUER CREDIT RATING. TOLEDO, OHIO, SEPT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WELLTOWER INC. (NYSE: WELL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT S&P GLOBAL RATINGS ("S&P") HAS REVISED ITS RATING OUTLOOK FOR WELLTOWER TO STABLE FROM NEGATIVE AND AFFIRMED WELLTOWER'S ISSUER CREDIT RATING AS 'BBB+'.
