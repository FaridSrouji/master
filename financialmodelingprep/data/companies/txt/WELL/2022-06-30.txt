WELLTOWER RELEASES 2021 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE REPORT. TOLEDO, OHIO , JUNE 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WELLTOWER INC.® (NYSE: WELL) TODAY RELEASED ITS 2021 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE ("ESG") REPORT. THE REPORT SUMMARIZES WELLTOWER'S PROGRESS AND ACHIEVEMENTS ACROSS A RANGE OF ESG INITIATIVES INCLUDING DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION, ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE.
