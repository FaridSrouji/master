WELLTOWER RELEASES 2020 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE REPORT. TOLEDO, OHIO, JULY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WELLTOWER INC.® (NYSE: WELL) TODAY RELEASED ITS ANNUAL ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE ("ESG") REPORT. THE REPORT SUMMARIZES WELLTOWER'S CONTINUED STRONG PERFORMANCE IN 2020 ACROSS A RANGE OF ESG INITIATIVES INCLUDING DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION, ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY, AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE.
