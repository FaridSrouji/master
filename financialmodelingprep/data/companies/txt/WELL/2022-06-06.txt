WELLTOWER RAISES SECOND QUARTER 2022 GUIDANCE AND ISSUES BUSINESS UPDATE. TOLEDO, OHIO , JUNE 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WELLTOWER® INC. (NYSE: WELL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS REVISED ITS SECOND QUARTER 2022 OUTLOOK. THE COMPANY NOW EXPECTS TO ACHIEVE NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS IN A RANGE OF $0.22 - $0.25 PER DILUTED SHARE AS COMPARED TO PREVIOUS GUIDANCE OF $0.20 - $0.25 PER DILUTED SHARE.
