WELLTOWER RAISES 2023 GUIDANCE AND ISSUES BUSINESS UPDATE. TOLEDO, OHIO , JUNE 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WELLTOWER® INC. (NYSE: WELL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS REVISED ITS 2023 OUTLOOK. THE COMPANY NOW EXPECTS TO ACHIEVE NORMALIZED FUNDS FROM OPERATIONS IN A RANGE OF $3.43 - $3.56 PER DILUTED SHARE AS COMPARED TO PREVIOUS GUIDANCE OF $3.39 - $3.54 PER DILUTED SHARE.
