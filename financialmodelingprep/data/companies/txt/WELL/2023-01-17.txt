NIKHIL CHAUDHRI PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT - CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER AYESHA MENON PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT - WELLNESS HOUSING & DEVELOPMENT ADDITIONAL SENIOR LEADERSHIP TEAM PROMOTIONS ANNOUNCED TOLEDO, OHIO , JAN. 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WELLTOWER INC.
