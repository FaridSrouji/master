SBA COMMUNICATIONS CORPORATION TO SPEAK AT THE 30TH ANNUAL DEUTSCHE BANK MEDIA, INTERNET AND TELECOM CONFERENCE. BOCA RATON, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SBA COMMUNICATIONS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: SBAC) (“SBA”) ANNOUNCES THAT JEFFREY A. STOOPS, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER IS SCHEDULED TO SPEAK AT THE 30TH ANNUAL DEUTSCHE BANK MEDIA, INTERNET AND TELECOM CONFERENCE, TUESDAY, MARCH 15, 2022 AT 11:50AM ET. THE CONFERENCE WILL BE AT THE BREAKERS HOTEL IN PALM BEACH, FLORIDA. THE AUDIO PRESENTATION FOR SBA CAN BE ACCESSED BY VISITING WWW.SBASITE.COM.
