CHURCH & DWIGHT CO., INC. DECLARES 486TH REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. EWING, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHURCH & DWIGHT CO., INC. (NYSE:CHD) REPORTED YESTERDAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF TWENTY SIX AND ONE QUARTER ($0.2625) CENTS PER SHARE. THIS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE SEPTEMBER 1, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 15, 2022. IT IS THE COMPANY'S 486TH REGULAR CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
