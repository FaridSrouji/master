THE COMPANY CONTINUES TO EXPERIENCE STRONG CONSUMER DEMAND FOR MANY OF ITS PRODUCTS. ORGANIC SALES GREW 3.7%, EXCEEDING THE COMPANY'S OUTLOOK OF 1.5% GROWTH.2 THIRD QUARTER 2021 EPS INCREASED 8.2% TO $0.92 PER SHARE.
