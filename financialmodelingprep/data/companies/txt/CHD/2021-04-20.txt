CHURCH & DWIGHT INITIATES VOLUNTARY RECALL OF SELECT VITAMINS DUE TO ISOLATED MANUFACTURING ISSUE. EWING, N.J., APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHURCH & DWIGHT CO., INC. (NYSE:CHD) TODAY INITIATED A VOLUNTARY RECALL OF SELECT VITAFUSION GUMMY PRODUCTS AFTER THE COMPANY'S INVESTIGATION OF TWO CONSUMER REPORTS IDENTIFIED THE POSSIBLE PRESENCE OF A METALLIC MESH MATERIAL IN PRODUCT LOTS MANUFACTURED IN A FOUR-DAY PERIOD BETWEEN OCTOBER 29 AND NOVEMBER 3, 2020.
