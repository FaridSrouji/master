CONSTELLATION BRANDS PRICES OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES. VICTOR, N.Y., APRIL 27, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CONSTELLATION BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: STZ), A LEADING BEVERAGE ALCOHOL COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT PRICED THE PUBLIC OFFERING OF $750.0 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 4.900% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2033 (THE “NOTES”) FOR A PUBLIC OFFERING PRICE OF 99.306% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF THE NOTES. THE NOTES WILL BE SENIOR OBLIGATIONS THAT RANK EQUALLY WITH ALL OF CONSTELLATION'S OTHER SENIOR UNSECURED INDEBTEDNESS.
