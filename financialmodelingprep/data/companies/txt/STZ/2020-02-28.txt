CONSTELLATION BRANDS SAYS SAW NO IMPACT TO PEOPLE, FACILITIES OR OPERATIONS FROM COVID-19. CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - SEEN NO IMPACT TO PEOPLE, FACILITIES OR OPERATIONS FROM COVID-19.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - "BUSINESS CONTINUES TO PERFORM VERY WELL".CONSTELLATION BRANDS - CO DOES NOT HAVE MUCH EXPOSURE TO INTERNATIONAL MARKETS SUCH AS CHINA THAT HAVE BEEN MOST IMPACTED BY COVID-19.CONSTELLATION BRANDS - ADDRESSES CONCERNS ABOUT "IMPACT OF COVID-19 VIRUS ON ITS BUSINESS & CONSUMER SENTIMENT RELATED TO CO'S CORONA BEER BRAND".
