CONSTELLATION BRANDS ANNOUNCES COMPLETION OF PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ELIMINATION OF CLASS B COMMON STOCK. VICTOR, N.Y., NOV. 10, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CONSTELLATION BRANDS (NYSE: STZ), A LEADING BEVERAGE ALCOHOL COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED RECLASSIFICATION TRANSACTION AND ELIMINATED ITS CLASS B COMMON STOCK. TRADING IN THE CLASS B COMMON STOCK WILL CEASE AFTER THE MARKETS CLOSE TODAY, AT WHICH TIME CONSTELLATION'S PUBLICLY LISTED STOCK WILL CONSIST SOLELY OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK.
