GENERAL MILLS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND DECLARED. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE GENERAL MILLS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AT THE PREVAILING RATE OF $0.51 PER SHARE, PAYABLE FEBRUARY 1, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JANUARY 8, 2021. THE CURRENT QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE REPRESENTS A 4 PERCENT INCREASE FROM THE $0.49 PER SHARE RATE PAID A YEAR AGO.
