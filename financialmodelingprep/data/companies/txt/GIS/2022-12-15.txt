GENERAL MILLS NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD AND NORTH AMERICA INDICES FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. MINNEAPOLIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--GENERAL MILLS (NYSE: GIS) HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX (DJSI WORLD) AND THE DOW JONES NORTH AMERICA INDEX (DJSI NORTH AMERICA) FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
