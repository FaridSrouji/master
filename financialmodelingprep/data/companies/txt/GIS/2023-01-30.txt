GENERAL MILLS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND DECLARED. MINNEAPOLIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE GENERAL MILLS BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AT THE PREVAILING RATE OF $0.54 PER SHARE, PAYABLE MAY 1, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 10, 2023. GENERAL MILLS (NYSE: GIS) AND ITS PREDECESSOR COMPANY HAVE PAID DIVIDENDS WITHOUT INTERRUPTION FOR 124 YEARS.
