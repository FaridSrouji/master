GENERAL MILLS' MONSTER CEREALS RETURN IN KAWS-DESIGNED BOXES. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GENERAL MILLS' BELOVED FRANKEN BERRY, COUNT CHOCULA, BOO-BERRY AND – FOR THE FIRST TIME IN NEARLY A DECADE – FRUTE BRUTE MONSTER CEREALS ARE BACK WITH THE LIMITED-EDITION RELEASE OF FOUR KAWS-DESIGNED BOXES. INSPIRED BY CLASSIC HORROR FILM CHARACTERS, THE MONSTER CEREALS HAVE BEEN FAN FAVORITES FOR MORE THAN HALF A CENTURY AND THIS YEAR RENOWNED AMERICAN ARTIST AND MONSTER ENTHUSIAST KAWS WAS INSPIRED BY THE VINTAGE STYLING OF THE ORIGINAL BOXES.
