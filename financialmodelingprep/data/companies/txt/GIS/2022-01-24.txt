GENERAL MILLS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND DECLARED. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE GENERAL MILLS BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AT THE PREVAILING RATE OF $0.51 PER SHARE, PAYABLE MAY 2, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 8, 2022. GENERAL MILLS (NYSE: GIS) AND ITS PREDECESSOR COMPANY HAVE PAID DIVIDENDS WITHOUT INTERRUPTION FOR 123 YEARS. ABOUT GENERAL MILLS GENERAL MILLS MAKES FOOD THE WORLD LOVES.
