GENERAL MILLS PARTNERS WITH REGROW AGRICULTURE TO MONITOR AGRICULTURE AT SCALE. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, GENERAL MILLS AND REGROW AGRICULTURE ANNOUNCED A MULTI-YEAR PARTNERSHIP TO MONITOR AGRICULTURAL PRACTICES AND THEIR ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACTS ACROSS 175 MILLION ACRES OF FARMLAND IN NORTH AMERICA, EUROPE, AND SOUTH AMERICA. ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIVE MILLION ACRES REPRESENTS GENERAL MILLS' ESTIMATED SUPPLY SHEDS – THE REGIONS WHERE THE COMPANY SOURCES ITS KEY INGREDIENTS, LIKE WHEAT, OAT, AND DAIRY.
