GENERAL MILLS ANNOUNCES PROPOSED SALE OF EUROPEAN DOUGH BUSINESSES TO CÉRÉLIA. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GENERAL MILLS (NYSE: GIS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS AGREED TO SELL ITS EUROPEAN DOUGH BUSINESSES TO CÉRÉLIA, A GLOBAL LEADER IN READY-TO-BAKE DOUGH SOLUTIONS. THE PROPOSED TRANSACTIONS WOULD INCLUDE GENERAL MILLS BRANDED AND PRIVATE LABEL DOUGH BUSINESSES IN GERMANY, THE UK, AND IRELAND, INCLUDING THE KNACK & BACK AND JUS-ROL BRANDS.
