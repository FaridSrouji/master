GENERAL MILLS NAMED NO. 2 ON NEWSWEEK'S AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES 2023 LIST. MINNEAPOLIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--GENERAL MILLS HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY NEWSWEEK AS ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES, RANKING NO. 2 OVERALL AND NO.1 IN THE CONSUMER GOODS INDUSTRY. THIS PRESTIGIOUS AWARD IS PRESENTED BY NEWSWEEK AND STATISTA INC., THE WORLD-LEADING STATISTICS PORTAL AND INDUSTRY RANKING PROVIDER.
