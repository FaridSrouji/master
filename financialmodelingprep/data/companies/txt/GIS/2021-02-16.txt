GENERAL MILLS OUTLINES “ACCELERATE” GROWTH STRATEGY AT 2021 CAGNY CONFERENCE. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AT THE CONSUMER ANALYST GROUP OF NEW YORK (CAGNY) 2021 CONFERENCE, GENERAL MILLS (NYSE: GIS) OUTLINED ITS ACCELERATE STRATEGY TO DELIVER TOP-TIER SHAREHOLDER RETURNS. THE COMPANY ALSO REAFFIRMED ITS FISCAL 2021 THIRD-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR FINANCIAL GUIDANCE, WHICH WAS ORIGINALLY PROVIDED ON DECEMBER 17, 2020.
