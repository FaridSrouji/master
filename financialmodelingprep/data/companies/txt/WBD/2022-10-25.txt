POMERANTZ LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THE FILING OF A CLASS ACTION AGAINST DISCOVERY, INC., WARNER BROS. DISCOVERY, INC., AND CERTAIN OFFICERS - WBD. ("WBD") (NASDAQ: WBD), AND CERTAIN OFFICERS.
