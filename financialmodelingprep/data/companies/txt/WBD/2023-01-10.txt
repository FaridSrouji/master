SKYSHOWTIME AND WARNER BROS. DISCOVERY ANNOUNCE MAJOR PROGRAMMING ACQUISITION DEAL. LONDON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--SKYSHOWTIME – THE NEXT GREAT STREAMING SERVICE FOR EUROPE – ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED EXCLUSIVE EUROPEAN RIGHTS FOR 21 LOCAL SERIES FROM WARNER BROS. DISCOVERY, INCLUDING SOME OF HBO MAX'S POPULAR AND HIGHLY-ACCLAIMED SHOWS FROM THE REGION FOLLOWING A COMPETITIVE PROCESS. IN TOTAL, THE DEAL PROVIDES SKYSHOWTIME 168 EPISODES—OVER 150 HOURS—OF NEW AND PREVIOUSLY AIRED ORIGINAL PROGRAMMING. FINANCIAL TERMS WERE NOT DISCLOSED.
