INTRODUCING BEAUTY&YOU 2023: SUPPORTING THE NEXT GENERATION OF INDIAN BEAUTY ENTREPRENEURS AND CREATORS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES (NYSE: EL) (“ELC”) IS EXCITED TO ANNOUNCE THE SECOND EDITION OF BEAUTY&YOU. CREATED BY ELC'S NEW INCUBATION VENTURES (“NIV”) AND LAUNCHED IN PARTNERSHIP WITH INDIA'S PREFERRED BEAUTY AND LIFESTYLE RETAILER, NYKAA, BEAUTY&YOU AIMS TO CONTINUE ITS MISSION TO DISCOVER, SPOTLIGHT AND PROPEL THE NEXT GENERATION OF INDIAN BEAUTY BRANDS. THE PROGRAM SUPPORTS INDIA-FOCUSED COMPANIES, ENTREPRENEURS, INNOVATORS, AND CREATORS THROUGH A COMPETIT.
