THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES ANNOUNCES APPOINTMENT OF MARK LOOMIS TO LEAD NORTH AMERICA. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES (NYSE:EL) (“ELC”) ANNOUNCED THAT MARK LOOMIS, CURRENTLY PRESIDENT, ASIA/PACIFIC, WILL BE APPOINTED PRESIDENT, NORTH AMERICA, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 1, 2022. MARK WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO FABRIZIO FREDA, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AND HE WILL REMAIN A MEMBER OF ELC'S EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM.
