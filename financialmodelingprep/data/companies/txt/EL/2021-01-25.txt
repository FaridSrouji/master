THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES ANNOUNCES SUPPLY CHAIN LEADERSHIP UPDATES. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES (NYSE:EL) ANNOUNCED THAT AFTER NEARLY 13 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY, GREGORY F. POLCER, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL SUPPLY CHAIN, HAS MADE THE DECISION TO RETIRE, EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2021. HE WILL BE SUCCEEDED BY ROBERTO CANEVARI, WHO WILL ASSUME THE ROLE OF EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL SUPPLY CHAIN, EFFECTIVE MAY 1, 2021.
