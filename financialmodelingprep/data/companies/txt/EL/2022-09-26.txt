GUILLAUME JESEL PROMOTED TO PRESIDENT, GLOBAL BRANDS, TOM FORD BEAUTY, BALMAIN BEAUTY AND LUXURY BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT. THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES AND BALMAIN ARE PARTNERING TO LAUNCH BALMAIN BEAUTY. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES INC. (NYSE: EL) (“ELC”) AND BALMAIN HAVE ENTERED INTO A LICENSE AGREEMENT TO COLLABORATIVELY DEVELOP, PRODUCE, AND DISTRIBUTE AN INNOVATIVE LINE OF BEAUTY PRODUCTS THAT WILL SPEAK TO LUXURY CONSUMERS AROUND THE WORLD: BALMAIN BEAUTY. THE COLLABORATION IS EXPECTED TO LAUNCH IN FALL 2024, WITH THE GOAL TO TRANSFORM THE LUXURY AND COUTURE BEAUTY WORLD THROUGH EXCEPTIONAL DESIGN, SINGULAR CRAFTSMANSHIP, AND AN UNYIELDING COMMITMENT TO INNOVATION
