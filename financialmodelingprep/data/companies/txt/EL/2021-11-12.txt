THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES HOLDS ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES INC. (NYSE: EL) HELD ITS ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS TODAY. WILLIAM P. LAUDER, EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN, CHAIRED THE MEETING. FABRIZIO FREDA, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, REVIEWED RECENT FINANCIAL RESULTS AND THE COMPANY'S LONG-TERM STRATEGY.
