THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES ANNOUNCES NEW EQUITY AND ENGAGEMENT CENTER OF EXCELLENCE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES (NYSE:EL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ESTABLISHED A NEW EQUITY AND ENGAGEMENT CENTER OF EXCELLENCE (COE). THE CREATION OF THIS NEW CAPABILITY UNDERSCORES THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES' (ELC) CORE VALUES AND COMMITMENTS TO BUILD GREATER EQUITY AND REPRESENTATION WITHIN EVERY ASPECT OF ITS BUSINESS, FROM ITS EMPLOYEES TO ITS CONSUMERS. THE EQUITY AND ENGAGEMENT COE WILL BE LED BY NICOLE MONSON, WHO HAS BEEN APPOINTED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, EQUITY
