HANS DORSINVILLE APPOINTED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL CREATIVE, BALMAIN BEAUTY. PARIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BALMAIN ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF HANS DORSINVILLE TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT (SVP), GLOBAL CREATIVE, BALMAIN BEAUTY, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 2023. HANS WILL BE BASED IN NEW YORK AND REPORT DIRECTLY TO OLIVIER ROUSTEING, CREATIVE DIRECTOR OF BALMAIN.
