COMERICA NAMES FREDY SALGUERO VICE PRESIDENT, SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA EXTERNAL AFFAIRS MANAGER. DALLAS , FEB. 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK HAS NAMED FREDY SALGUERO SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA EXTERNAL AFFAIRS MARKET MANAGER. SALGUERO'S ROLE INCLUDES STRENGTHENING COMERICA'S INITIATIVES OF ASSISTING LOCAL COMMUNITIES AND DEVELOPING RELATIONSHIPS IN SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA WITH COMMUNITY PARTNERS, NONPROFITS AND BUSINESSES.
