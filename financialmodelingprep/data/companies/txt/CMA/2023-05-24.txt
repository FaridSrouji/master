COMERICA BANK AND KALAMAZOO GROWLERS PARTNER TO HOST 2ND ANNUAL FOOD DRIVE BENEFITTING SOUTH MICHIGAN FOOD BANK. COMMUNITY ENCOURAGED TO DONATE NONPERISHABLE FOOD IN EXCHANGE FOR BASEBALL TICKETS KALAMAZOO, MICH. , MAY 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR THE SECOND STRAIGHT YEAR, COMERICA BANK AND THE KALAMAZOO GROWLERS ARE PARTNERING TO HOST A SUMMER-LONG FOOD DRIVE BENEFITTING SOUTH MICHIGAN FOOD BANK FROM JUNE 2 THROUGH JULY 27.
