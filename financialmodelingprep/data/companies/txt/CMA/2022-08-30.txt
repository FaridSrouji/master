COMERICA INCORPORATED NAMES VON E. HAYS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER - GENERAL COUNSEL. DALLAS , AUG. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT VON E. HAYS , SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT – GENERAL COUNSEL HUMAN RESOURCES, LITIGATION AND CORPORATE OPERATIONS, HAS BEEN NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER – GENERAL COUNSEL, EFFECTIVE AUG. 26, 2022.
