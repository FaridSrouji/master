COMERICA APPOINTS SINERIA ORDÓÑEZ NATIONAL HISPANIC BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT MANAGER. DALLAS , MAY 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK ANNOUNCED IT HAS APPOINTED SINERIA ORDÓÑEZ AS NATIONAL HISPANIC BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT MANAGER TO SUPPORT ITS ONGOING COMMITMENT TO STRENGTHENING RELATIONSHIPS WITH HISPANIC BUSINESS LEADERS, ENTREPRENEURS AND COMMUNITIES ACROSS ITS FIVE PRIMARY MARKETS OF CALIFORNIA, TEXAS, MICHIGAN, ARIZONA AND FLORIDA. THESE RESPONSIBILITIES ARE AN EXTENSION OF ORDÓÑEZ'S CURRENT ROLE AS VICE PRESIDENT, EXTERNAL AFFAIRS MARKET MANAGER – SOUTH TEXAS.
