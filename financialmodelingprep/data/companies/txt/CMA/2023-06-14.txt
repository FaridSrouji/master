COMERICA BANK'S NEW LENDING PLATFORM ACCELERATES ACCESS TO CAPITAL FOR SMALL BUSINESSES. COMERICA SMALL BUSINESS CONVENIENT CAPITAL™, POWERED BY AMOUNT, IS AIMED AT PROVIDING FASTER APPROVALS AND FUNDING FOR QUALIFIED SMALL BUSINESS CUSTOMERS DALLAS , JUNE 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK ANNOUNCED TODAY THE LAUNCH OF COMERICA SMALL BUSINESS CONVENIENT CAPITAL™, ITS FIRST FULLY DIGITAL, END-TO-END NEW LENDING PLATFORM TO PROVIDE CONVENIENT ACCESS TO CAPITAL AND INNOVATIVE VALUE-ADDED BENEFITS FOR COMERICA SMALL BUSINESS CUSTOMERS. BUILT IN COLLABORATION WITH AMOUNT, A LEADING DIGITALLY ENABLED LENDER AND CREDIT SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, THE COMERICA SMALL BUSINESS CONVENIENT CAPITAL PLATFORM WAS DEVELOPED TO SERVE SMALL BUSINESS CUSTOMERS THROUGHOUT THE COUNTRY WITH LOAN NEEDS UP TO $100,000.
