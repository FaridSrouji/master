COMERICA BANK AND KALAMAZOO GROWLERS FOOD DRIVE BENEFITTING SOUTH MICHIGAN FOOD BANK GENERATES OVER 61,000 MEALS. COMBINATION OF COMMUNITY FOOD DONATIONS AND $10,000 IN FINANCIAL SUPPORT FROM COMERICA BANK HELPS COMBAT FOOD INSECURITY IN KALAMAZOO KALAMAZOO, MICH. , AUG. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA BANK AND KALAMAZOO GROWLERS FOOD DRIVE, WHICH CULMINATED DURING A PRE-GAME CEREMONY PRIOR TO THE GROWLERS HOME GAME ON FRIDAY, AUGUST 12, GENERATED OVER 61,000 MEALS FOR THE SOUTH MICHIGAN FOOD BANK AND WILL AID IN THE EFFORTS TO REDUCE FOOD INSECURITY IN THE COMMUNITY.
