COMERICA NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES BY JUST CAPITAL AND CNBC. DALLAS , JAN. 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK WAS NAMED TO THE LIST OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES AS RECOGNITION FOR ITS COMMITMENT TO SERVING ITS WORKERS, CUSTOMERS, COMMUNITIES, ENVIRONMENT AND SHAREHOLDERS. JUST CAPITAL, ALONG WITH MEDIA PARTNER CNBC, RELEASED THE 2023 RANKINGS OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES, INCLUDING THE MARQUEE JUST 100.
