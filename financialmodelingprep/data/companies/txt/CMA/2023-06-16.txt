INVESTIGATION ALERT: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES IT IS INVESTIGATING CLAIMS AGAINST COMERICA INCORPORATED AND ENCOURAGES INVESTORS WITH LOSSES TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / JUNE 16, 2023 / THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, ANNOUNCES THAT IT IS INVESTIGATING CLAIMS ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS OF COMERICA INCORPORATED ("COMERICA" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE:CMA) FOR VIOLATIONS OF THE SECURITIES LAWS. THE INVESTIGATION FOCUSES ON WHETHER THE COMPANY ISSUED FALSE AND/OR MISLEADING STATEMENTS AND/OR FAILED TO DISCLOSE INFORMATION PERTINENT TO INVESTORS.
