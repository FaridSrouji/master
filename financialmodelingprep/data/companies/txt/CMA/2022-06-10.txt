COMERICA BANK SELECTED AS 2022 LIFE AWARD WINNER BY TEXAS BANKERS FOUNDATION FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. AUSTIN, TEXAS , JUNE 10, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK HAS BEEN SELECTED AS A RECIPIENT OF THE LEADERS IN FINANCIAL EDUCATION (LIFE) AWARD FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR BY THE TEXAS BANKERS FOUNDATION (TBF). THE HONOR WAS ANNOUNCED AT THE TEXAS BANKERS ASSOCIATION'S 137TH ANNUAL CONVENTION DURING A SPECIAL AWARDS LUNCHEON.
