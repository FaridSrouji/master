COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA INDEX'S DECLINE MODERATED IN JANUARY. DALLAS , APRIL 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA CALIFORNIA ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX FELL 0.3% ANNUALIZED IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH JANUARY, THE SMALLEST SUCH DECLINE SINCE JULY 2022. THE INDEX ROSE 3.2% FROM A YEAR EARLIER.
COMERICA BANK'S TEXAS INDEX GREW SLOWER IN JANUARY. DALLAS , APRIL 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA TEXAS ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX ROSE AT A 0.9% ANNUALIZED RATE IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH JANUARY AND WAS UP 4.2% FROM A YEAR AGO. JUST TWO OF THE INDEX'S NINE COMPONENTS ROSE IN THE MONTH.
COMERICA BANK'S MICHIGAN INDEX FELL SHARPLY THROUGH JANUARY. DALLAS , APRIL 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA MICHIGAN ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX FELL 4.6% ANNUALIZED IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH JANUARY AND WAS DOWN 0.4% FROM A YEAR AGO. ONLY THREE OF THE INDEX'S NINE COMPONENTS INCREASED IN JANUARY, WHILE FIVE FELL AND ONE WAS UNCHANGED.
