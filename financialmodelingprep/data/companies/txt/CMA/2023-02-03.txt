COMERICA NAMES KEVIN WATKINS VICE PRESIDENT, SOUTHEAST MICHIGAN EXTERNAL AFFAIRS MANAGER. DETROIT, FEB. 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK HAS NAMED KEVIN WATKINS VICE PRESIDENT, SOUTHEAST MICHIGAN EXTERNAL AFFAIRS MARKET MANAGER. IN HIS ROLE, WATKINS WILL SUPPORT COMERICA BANK'S COMMUNITY COMMITMENT AND ENGAGEMENTS AS HE MANAGES AND COORDINATES COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AND EXTERNAL AFFAIRS OUTREACH THROUGHOUT SOUTHEAST MICHIGAN.
