COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA INDEX IMPROVES. DALLAS, MARCH 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX INCREASED IN JANUARY TO A LEVEL OF 108.9. JANUARY'S READING IS 21 PERCENT ABOVE THE HISTORICAL LOW OF 90.2 SET IN JUNE 2020.
