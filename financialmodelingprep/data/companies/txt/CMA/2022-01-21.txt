COMERICA INCORPORATED NAMES BILL ADAMS, CHIEF ECONOMIST; ROBERT DYE TO RETIRE ON JAN. 28. DALLAS, JAN. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT WILLIAM (BILL) ADAMS WILL BE NAMED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF ECONOMIST, EFFECTIVE JAN. 24, 2022. ADAMS WILL SUCCEED ROBERT DYE, PH.D.
