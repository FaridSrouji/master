COMERICA BANK COMMITS $5 BILLION TO SMALL BUSINESS LENDING OVER NEXT THREE YEARS; EXPANDS RISE! INITIATIVE TO SUPPORT SMALL BUSINESS GROWTH. DALLAS, MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WILL COMMIT $5 BILLION TO SMALL BUSINESS LENDING OVER THE NEXT THREE YEARS (2021-2023). ADDITIONALLY, COMERICA WILL EXPAND RISE!
