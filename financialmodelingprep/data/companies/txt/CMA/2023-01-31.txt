COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA INDEX DECLINED SHARPLY IN OCTOBER. DALLAS , JAN. 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA CALIFORNIA ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX DECLINED 4.9% ANNUALIZED IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH OCTOBER. THE INDEX HAS TURNED LOWER AFTER ROBUST INCREASES IN THE FIRST HALF OF THE YEAR, BUT WAS STILL UP 5.7% FROM A YEAR-AGO IN OCTOBER.
COMERICA BANK'S TEXAS INDEX ROSE IN OCTOBER. DALLAS , JAN. 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA TEXAS ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX ROSE AT A 2.7% ANNUALIZED RATE IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH OCTOBER AND WAS UP 6.4% FROM A YEAR-AGO. FIVE OF THE INDEX'S NINE COMPONENTS ROSE IN OCTOBER.
