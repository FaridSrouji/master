COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA INDEX IMPROVES. DALLAS, JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX INCREASED IN MAY TO A LEVEL OF 114.0. MAY'S READING IS 23 PERCENT ABOVE THE HISTORICAL LOW OF 92.7 SET IN JUNE 2020.
