LINDA NOSEGBE NAMED NATIONAL COMMUNITY IMPACT MANAGER. DALLAS , AUG. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) HAS NAMED LINDA NOSEGBE, VICE PRESIDENT, SOUTHEAST EXTERNAL AFFAIRS MARKET MANAGER, AS VICE PRESIDENT, NATIONAL COMMUNITY IMPACT MANAGER. SHE WILL REPORT TO IRVIN ASHFORD JR., CHIEF COMMUNITY OFFICER.
