COMERICA BANK NAMES BOB BUCHANAN SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, NATIONAL PRACTICE LEADER - BUSINESS TRANSITION PLANNING. DALLAS, JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK ANNOUNCED THAT BOB BUCHANAN HAS BEEN NAMED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND NATIONAL PRACTICE LEADER – BUSINESS TRANSITION PLANNING. BUCHANAN JOINED COMERICA ON JULY 12, 2021, AND REPORTS TO LISA FEATHERNGILL, NATIONAL DIRECTOR OF WEALTH PLANNING.
