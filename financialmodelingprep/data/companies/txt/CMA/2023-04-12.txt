COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA INDEX DOWN SHARPLY AGAIN IN DECEMBER. DALLAS , APRIL 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA CALIFORNIA ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX FELL 4.3% ANNUALIZED IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH DECEMBER, BUT WAS STILL UP 3.1% FROM A YEAR EARLIER.
COMERICA BANK'S TEXAS INDEX EDGED DOWN IN DECEMBER. DALLAS , APRIL 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA TEXAS ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX ROSE AT A 1.5% ANNUALIZED RATE IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH DECEMBER AND WAS UP 4.6% FROM A YEAR AGO. TWO OF THE INDEX'S NINE COMPONENTS ROSE IN DECEMBER.
COMERICA BANK'S MICHIGAN INDEX FALLS SHARPLY THROUGH DECEMBER. DALLAS , APRIL 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA MICHIGAN ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX FELL 3.9% ANNUALIZED IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH DECEMBER, BUT WAS STILL UP A MODEST 0.5% FROM A YEAR AGO. ONLY THREE OF THE INDEX'S NINE COMPONENTS INCREASED IN DECEMBER, WHILE FIVE FELL AND ONE WAS UNCHANGED.
