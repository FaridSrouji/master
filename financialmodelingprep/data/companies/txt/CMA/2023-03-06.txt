COMERICA BANK NAMES JEFF BANKS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF DATA AND ANALYTICS OFFICER. DALLAS , MARCH 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) ANNOUNCED THAT JEFF BANKS HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE NEW ROLE OF SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF DATA AND ANALYTICS OFFICER. HE WILL REPORT TO MEGAN CRESPI, SENIOR EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER.
