COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA INDEX LOWER IN AUGUST. DALLAS , NOV. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA CALIFORNIA ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX DECLINED AT A 2.8% ANNUALIZED RATE IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH AUGUST. THE INDEX HAS SLOWED FROM THE ROBUST INCREASES IN THE FIRST HALF OF THE YEAR.
COMERICA BANK'S TEXAS INDEX GREW SLOWER IN AUGUST. DALLAS , NOV. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA TEXAS ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX ROSE AT A 1.5% ANNUALIZED RATE IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH AUGUST. THE INDEX HAS SLOWED SHARPLY AFTER ROBUST INCREASES EARLIER IN THE YEAR.
COMERICA BANK'S MICHIGAN INDEX ROSE THROUGH AUGUST. DALLAS , NOV. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COMERICA MICHIGAN ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX ROSE AT A 2.2% ANNUALIZED PACE IN THE THREE MONTHS THROUGH AUGUST. THE INDEX HAS SLOWED IN THE PAST TWO MONTHS, AFTER POSTING STRONGER GAINS IN THE FIRST HALF OF THE YEAR.
