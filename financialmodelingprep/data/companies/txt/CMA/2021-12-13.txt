COMERICA INCORPORATED ESTABLISHES NEW OFFICE OF CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY; WENDY BRIDGES TO SERVE IN NEW ROLE AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY. DALLAS, DEC. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ESTABLISHED A NEW OFFICE OF CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY WITH WENDY BRIDGES , SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE COMMUNICATIONS AND EXECUTIVE ADMINISTRATION, APPOINTED TO LEAD THE DEPARTMENT AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY. BRIDGES REPORTS DIRECTLY TO CURT FARMER, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AND SERVES ON COMERICA'S MANAGEMENT EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE.
