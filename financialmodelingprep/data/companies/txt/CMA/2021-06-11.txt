COMERICA BANK EMPOWERS MOMS AND DAUGHTERS THROUGH FINANCIAL EDUCATION PROGRAM. DALLAS, JUNE 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK AND SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA NONPROFIT PF BRESEE FOUNDATION ARE DEMONSTRATING HOW FINANCIAL EDUCATION CAN SERVE AS A CATALYST FOR CHANGE WITH THE CREATION OF THE WOMEN IN CHARGE FINANCIAL EMPOWERMENT CAMP. MOTHERS AND THEIR DAUGHTERS WILL PARTICIPATE IN A SIX-WEEK CAMP DESIGNED TO EDUCATE AND EMPOWER WITH THE GOAL OF IMPROVING CREDIT AND INCREASING SAVINGS.
