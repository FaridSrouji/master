COMERICA BANK NAMED A 2021 BEST EMPLOYER FOR WOMEN BY FORBES. DALLAS, OCT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FORBES NAMED COMERICA BANK TO ITS 2021 LIST OF BEST EMPLOYERS FOR WOMEN. THIS MARKS THE FIRST TIME COMERICA HAS RECEIVED THE HONOR AND JOINS THE EXCLUSIVE CATEGORY AS ONE OF 300 COMPANIES TO RECEIVE THE PRESTIGIOUS RECOGNITION.
