COMERICA BANK NAMES JOE URSUY EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, NATIONAL BUSINESSES FOR ENERGY, RENEWABLES, AND WASTE. DALLAS , AUG. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) ANNOUNCED THAT JOE URSUY HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE NEW ROLE OF EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, NATIONAL BUSINESSES FOR ENERGY, RENEWABLES, AND WASTE. HE WILL REPORT TO MIKE RITCHIE, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, HEAD OF NATIONAL AND SPECIALTY BUSINESSES.
