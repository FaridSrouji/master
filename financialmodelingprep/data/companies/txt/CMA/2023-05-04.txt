COMERICA BANK NAMED U.S. SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION MICHIGAN DISTRICT LENDER OF THE YEAR. U.S. SBA ALSO HONORS COMERICA AS TOP 10 LENDER DETROIT , MAY 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE U.S. SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION (SBA) NAMED COMERICA BANK AS THE MICHIGAN DISTRICT OFFICE LENDER OF THE YEAR FOR THE 2022 FISCAL YEAR (OCT. 1, 2021 THROUGH SEPT. 30, 2022).
