COMERICA ISSUES 2021 CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY REPORT. DALLAS , JULY 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) RELEASED ITS 2021 CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY REPORT DETAILING ADVANCEMENTS MADE ON KEY ENVIRONMENTAL AND SOCIAL MATTERS. "AT COMERICA, WE HAVE IDENTIFIED KEY ENVIRONMENTAL AND SOCIAL ISSUES WHERE WE HAVE THE ABILITY TO DRIVE POSITIVE OUTCOMES AND HELP CUSTOMERS MEET THEIR OWN EVER-EVOLVING NEEDS," SAID CURT FARMER, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, COMERICA BANK AND COMERICA INCORPORATED.
