VICKIE COHN NAMED COMERICA BANK CENTRAL & SOUTH TEXAS RETAIL REGIONAL DIRECTOR. HOUSTON , JULY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK HAS ANNOUNCED VICKIE COHN WILL LEAD ITS CENTRAL AND SOUTH TEXAS RETAIL TEAM, REPLACING THERESA BAZAN, WHO RECENTLY WAS PROMOTED TO NATIONAL SALES ENABLEMENT DIRECTOR FOR CONSUMER AND SMALL BUSINESS BANKING.  IN THIS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT ROLE, COHN WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE DEVELOPMENT AND EFFECTIVE LEADERSHIP OF THE AUSTIN, KERRVILLE, HOUSTON AND SAN ANTONIO MARKETS' RETAIL BANKING CENTER NETWORK.
