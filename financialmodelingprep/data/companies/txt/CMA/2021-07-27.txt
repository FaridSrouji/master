COMERICA DECLARES COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. DALLAS, JULY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND FOR COMMON STOCK OF 68 CENTS ($0.68) PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE OCT. 1, 2021, TO COMMON STOCK SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPT.
