COMERICA BUILDS ON ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES BUSINESS WITH LAUNCH OF NEW DEDICATED RENEWABLE ENERGY SOLUTIONS GROUP. DALLAS , MAY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK ANNOUNCED TODAY THE EXPANSION OF ITS ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES DEPARTMENT (ESD) WITH THE INTRODUCTION OF A NEW GROUP DEDICATED TO GROWING AND SUPPORTING COMERICA'S RENEWABLE ENERGY BUSINESS. MATT BREIGHT , SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND ESD GROUP MANAGER, HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO LEAD RENEWABLE ENERGY SOLUTIONS.
