COMERICA EXPANDS COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT LENDING PROGRAM. DALLAS, MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL EXPAND ITS COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT LENDING PROGRAM, SPECIFICALLY AS IT RELATES TO AFFORDABLE HOUSING. COMERICA PLANS TO STRENGTHEN ACCESS TO CAPITAL ASSISTANCE FOR NONPROFIT AND FOR-PROFIT DEVELOPERS OF LOW-INCOME HOUSING TAX CREDIT (LIHTC) PROJECTS.
