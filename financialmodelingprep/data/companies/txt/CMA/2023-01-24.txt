$100,000 COMERICA HATCH DETROIT CONTEST BY TECHTOWN OPENS APPLICATIONS, SEEKS DETROIT'S NEXT WINNING BRICK-AND-MORTAR SMALL BUSINESS. CONTEST BOLSTERS CITY'S SMALL BUSINESS INCUBATION DETROIT , JAN. 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK AND TECHTOWN DETROIT ANNOUNCE THE RETURN OF THE COMERICA HATCH DETROIT CONTEST BY TECHTOWN, WHICH AWARDS $100,000 IN STARTUP FUNDS FROM COMERICA BANK TO AN ENTREPRENEUR SEEKING TO OPEN A BRICK-AND-MORTAR SPACE IN DETROIT, HAMTRAMCK OR HIGHLAND PARK. THE COMPETITION PROMOTES SMALL BUSINESS ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AND WILL TAKE PLACE OVER THE NEXT FOUR MONTHS, WITH THE CROWNING OF THIS YEAR'S WINNER IN THE SPRING.
