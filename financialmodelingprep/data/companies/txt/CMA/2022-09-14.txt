COMERICA BANK UNVEILS PLANS FOR BUSINESS & INNOVATION HUB IN FRISCO, TEXAS. FRISCO BUSINESS & INNOVATION HUB SET TO MAKE DEBUT AT THE STAR IN LATE 2023 DALLAS , SEPT. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) TODAY ANNOUNCED CORPORATE EXPANSION PLANS IN NORTH TEXAS WITH DEVELOPMENT UNDERWAY FOR ITS NEW BUSINESS & INNOVATION HUB IN FRISCO.
