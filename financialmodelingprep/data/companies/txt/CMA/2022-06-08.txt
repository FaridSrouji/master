AMERICAN AIRLINES CENTER, COMERICA BANK ANNOUNCES NEW PARTNERSHIP. DALLAS, JUNE 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN AIRLINES CENTER ANNOUNCED TODAY A NEW FOUR-YEAR PARTNERSHIP WITH COMERICA BANK, WHICH INCLUDES EXCLUSIVE NAMING RIGHTS OF THE COMERICA GARAGE, FORMERLY THE SILVER GARAGE. THE 1,100-SPOT PARKING GARAGE IS LOCATED ON VICTORY AVE.
