COMERICA BANK ADDS TO RETAIL BANK LEADERSHIP TEAM. DALLAS , JUNE 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF JAMES JOHNSON AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, DIRECTOR, RETAIL RISK MANAGEMENT & OPERATIONS AND THERESA BAZAN AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, DIRECTOR, SALES ENABLEMENT. BOTH WILL REPORT TO CASSANDRA MCKINNEY, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR, RETAIL BANK.
