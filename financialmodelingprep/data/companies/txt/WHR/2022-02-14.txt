WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 25% AND SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM AUTHORIZATION BY $2 BILLION. BENTON HARBOR, MICH., FEB. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION (THE "COMPANY") AUTHORIZED AN ADDITIONAL $2 BILLION IN SHARE REPURCHASES UNDER THE COMPANY'S ONGOING SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM.
