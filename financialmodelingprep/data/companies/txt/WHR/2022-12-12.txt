WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION NAMED TO 2022 DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX. BENTON HARBOR, MICH. , DEC. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION (NYSE: WHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE 2022 DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX (DJSI) IN RECOGNITION OF THE COMPANY'S ONGOING FOCUS ON RESPONSIBLE AND ETHICAL BUSINESS PRACTICES.
