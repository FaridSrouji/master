WHIRLPOOL CORP. RECOGNIZED FOR SIXTH STRAIGHT YEAR AS A "BEST PLACE TO WORK FOR DISABILITY INCLUSION" FOR 2022. BENTON HARBOR, MICH. , JULY 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ONCE AGAIN, WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED AS A "BEST PLACE TO WORK FOR DISABILITY INCLUSION" AFTER BEING AWARDED A 100 PERCENT SCORE ON THE 2022 DISABILITY EQUALITY INDEX (DEI), MAKING THIS THE SIXTH STRAIGHT YEAR OF RECEIVING A HIGH SCORE.
