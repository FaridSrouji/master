WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION HELPS BUILDER CUSTOMERS WITH NEW BUILDER SUPPORT REPRESENTATIVE PROGRAM. BENTON HARBOR, MICH., FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION IS ANNOUNCING THE EXPANSION OF A PROGRAM DESIGNED TO PROVIDE LOCAL MARKET ASSISTANCE TO BUILDERS WHO ARE FACING A VARIETY OF CHALLENGES IN THE GROWING ECONOMY.
