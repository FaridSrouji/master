WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION ANNOUNCES ADDITIONAL $2 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM AUTHORIZATION AND INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 12%. BENTON HARBOR, MICH., APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION HAS AUTHORIZED AN ADDITIONAL $2 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM.
