WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION AND HABITAT FOR HUMANITY INTERNATIONAL LAUNCH HOUSE + HOME WORLD TOUR. BLOOMINGTON, IND., OCT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN PARTNERSHIP WITH HABITAT FOR HUMANITY INTERNATIONAL, WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT IS KICKING OFF ITS HOUSE + HOME WORLD TOUR, DURING WHICH THE COMPANY WILL PROVIDE MORE THAN $6 MILLION IN FUNDING AND ENERGY-EFFICIENT HOME APPLIANCES TO HABITAT AFFILIATES ACROSS THE U.S., CANADA, MEXICO, HONG KONG, POLAND, THE UK AND THE LATIN AMERICA REGION IN SUPPORT OF NEW HOME CONSTRUCTION, REPAIR AND REHAB PROJECTS.
