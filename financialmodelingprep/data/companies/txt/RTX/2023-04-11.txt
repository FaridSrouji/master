RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES AND SPIDEROAK COLLABORATE TO SECURE SATELLITE COMMUNICATIONS IN PROLIFERATED LOW-EARTH ORBIT. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., APRIL 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES' (NYSE: RTX) BBN DIVISION AND SPIDEROAK ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO DEVELOP AND FIELD A NEW GENERATION OF ZERO-TRUST SECURITY SYSTEMS FOR SATELLITE COMMUNICATIONS IN PROLIFERATED LOW-EARTH ORBIT, OR PLEO.
