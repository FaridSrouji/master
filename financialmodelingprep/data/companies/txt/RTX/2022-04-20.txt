US NAVY AWARDS RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE UP TO $1.68 BILLION FOR ZUMWALT DESTROYER ENGINEERING SERVICES. TUCSON, ARIZ. , APRIL 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE U.S. NAVY AWARDED RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS, AN ACTIVATION, SUSTAINMENT, AND MODERNIZATION CONTRACT FOR $483 MILLION WITH OPTIONS, IF EXERCISED, TOTALING $1.68 BILLION ACROSS FIVE YEARS.
