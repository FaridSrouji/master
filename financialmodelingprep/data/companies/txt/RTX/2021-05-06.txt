RAYTHEON INTELLIGENCE & SPACE TO SUPPORT AIR FORCE INTELLIGENCE PROGRAM UNDER $175M CONTRACT. EL SEGUNDO, CALIF., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON INTELLIGENCE & SPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS, WILL PROVIDE SIGNALS INTELLIGENCE FIELD SERVICES FOR THE U.S. AIR FORCE'S DISTRIBUTED COMMON GROUND SYSTEM, WHICH COLLECTS, PROCESSES AND ANALYZES INTELLIGENCE.
