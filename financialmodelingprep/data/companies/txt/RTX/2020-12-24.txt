FINAL DEADLINE TUESDAY: ROSEN, RECOGNIZED INVESTOR COUNSEL, REMINDS RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION F/K/A RAYTHEON COMPANY INVESTORS OF IMPORTANT DECEMBER 29 DEADLINE IN SECURITIES CLASS ACTION; ENCOURAGES INVESTORS WITH LOSSES IN EXCESS OF $100K TO CON. NEW YORK, DEC. 24, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ROSEN LAW FIRM, A GLOBAL INVESTOR RIGHTS LAW FIRM, REMINDS PURCHASERS OF THE SECURITIES OF RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION F/K/A RAYTHEON COMPANY (NYSE: RTX, RTN) BETWEEN FEBRUARY 10, 2016 AND OCTOBER 27, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”), OF THE IMPORTANT DECEMBER 29, 2020 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE IN THE SECURITIES CLASS ACTION COMMENCED BY THE FIRM. THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER DAMAGES FOR RAYTHEON INVESTORS UNDER THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
