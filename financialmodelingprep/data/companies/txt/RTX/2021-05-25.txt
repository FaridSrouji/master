RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES RETROFITTING COLLINS AEROSPACE OXYGEN TRUCKS TO AID COVID-19 RELIEF EFFORTS IN INDIA. WALTHAM, MASS., MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES CORP. (NYSE: RTX) IS RETROFITTING OXYTRUCK MOBILE OXYGEN FILLING STATIONS – PRODUCED BY ITS COLLINS AEROSPACE UNIT IN LÜBECK, GERMANY – TO HELP WITH THE COVID-19 CRISIS IN INDIA.
