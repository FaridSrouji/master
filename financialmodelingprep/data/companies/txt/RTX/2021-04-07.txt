U.S. AIR FORCE SELECTS COLLINS AEROSPACE TO MODERNIZE B-52 WHEELS AND BRAKES. TROY, OHIO, APRIL 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS THE U.S. AIR FORCE EXTENDS THE OPERATIONAL SERVICE LIFE OF THE B-52 STRATOFORTRESS INTO THE 2050S, IT HAS SELECTED COLLINS AEROSPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS, TO DESIGN AND DEVELOP A NEW WHEEL AND CARBON BRAKE FOR THE PLATFORM, LEADING TO FLEET RETROFIT OF 77 AIRCRAFT. COLLINS WILL DESIGN THE NEW WHEEL AND CARBON BRAKE FOR THE B-52H USING THE COMPANY'S PROPRIETARY DURACARB® CARBON HEAT SINK MATERIAL.
