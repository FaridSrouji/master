IBM AND RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES TO COLLABORATE ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, CRYPTOGRAPHY AND QUANTUM TECHNOLOGIES. ARMONK, N.Y., OCT. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) AND RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) WILL JOINTLY DEVELOP ADVANCED ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, CRYPTOGRAPHIC AND QUANTUM SOLUTIONS FOR THE AEROSPACE, DEFENSE AND INTELLIGENCE INDUSTRIES, INCLUDING THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT, AS PART OF A STRATEGIC COLLABORATION AGREEMENT THE COMPANIES ANNOUNCED TODAY.
