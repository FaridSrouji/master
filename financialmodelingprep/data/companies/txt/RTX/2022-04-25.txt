RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES BOARD OF DIRECTORS INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. WALTHAM, MASS. , APRIL 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION (NYSE: RTX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 55 CENTS PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF RTX COMMON STOCK, WHICH REPRESENTS AN INCREASE OF 7.8 PERCENT OVER THE PRIOR QUARTER'S DIVIDEND AMOUNT.
