BOEING SELECTS COLLINS AS NEXT-GENERATION LAVATORY SUPPLIER FOR THE 737. WINSTON-SALEM, N.C., JAN. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOEING COMPANY HAS SELECTED COLLINS AEROSPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS TO BE THE LONG-TERM PROVIDER OF NEXT-GENERATION LAVATORIES FOR THE 737 FAMILY OF AIRCRAFT.
