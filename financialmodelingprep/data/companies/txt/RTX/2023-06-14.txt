KIM KINSLEY SUCCEEDS EARL EXUM AS PRESIDENT OF INTERNATIONAL AERO ENGINES AG. EAST HARTFORD, CONN., JUNE 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERNATIONAL AERO ENGINES AG (IAE), A MULTINATIONAL CONSORTIUM COMPRISED OF SHAREHOLDERS INCLUDING RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES' BUSINESS PRATT & WHITNEY, PRATT & WHITNEY AERO ENGINES INTERNATIONAL GMBH, JAPANESE AERO ENGINES CORPORATION AND MTU AERO ENGINES, TODAY NAMED KIM KINSLEY AS ITS PRESIDENT.
