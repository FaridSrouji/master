RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES APPOINTS JEFF SHOCKEY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF GLOBAL GOVERNMENT RELATIONS. WALTHAM, MASS., JULY 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JEFF SHOCKEY WILL JOIN RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF GLOBAL GOVERNMENT RELATIONS, SUCCEEDING TIMOTHY MCBRIDE, WHO PLANS TO LEAVE THE COMPANY LATER THIS YEAR.
