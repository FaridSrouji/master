RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES UPDATES ITS FREE CASH FLOW OUTLOOK FOR THE CAPITALIZATION OF RESEARCH AND EXPERIMENTATION FOR TAX PURPOSES. ARLINGTON, VA. , SEPT. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) UPDATES ITS FREE CASH FLOW OUTLOOK FOR THE IMPACT OF THE LEGISLATION REQUIRING CAPITALIZATION OF RESEARCH AND EXPERIMENTATION FOR TAX PURPOSES AND, AS PREVIOUSLY DISCUSSED, IT NOW EXPECTS ITS FULL YEAR 2022 FREE CASH FLOW TO BE APPROXIMATELY $4.0 BILLION INSTEAD OF APPROXIMATELY $6.0 BILLION.
