RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES AWARDED $1.2 BILLION CONTRACT TO PROVIDE PATRIOT AIR DEFENSE SYSTEM TO SWITZERLAND. ARLINGTON, VA. , APRIL 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) WAS AWARDED A $1.2 BILLION FOREIGN MILITARY SALES CONTRACT FROM THE U.S. ARMY TO PROVIDE SWITZERLAND WITH THE PATRIOT™ AIR DEFENSE SYSTEM.
