US ARMY AWARDS RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE $182 MILLION NASAMS CONTRACT FOR UKRAINE. TUCSON, ARIZ. , AUG. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS, WAS AWARDED A $182 MILLION CONTRACT FOR THE NATIONAL ADVANCED SURFACE-TO-AIR MISSILE SYSTEM, ALSO KNOWN AS NASAMS™, A HIGHLY ADAPTABLE MID-RANGE AIR DEFENSE SOLUTION.
