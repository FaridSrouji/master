RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES AWARDED $237 MILLION COUNTER-UAS CONTRACT. DELIVERING CRITICAL CAPABILITIES TO SUPPORT THE ARMY'S DEFENSE AGAINST ENEMY DRONES TUCSON, ARIZ. , APRIL 19, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) WAS AWARDED A $237 MILLION U.S. ARMY CONTRACT FOR KU-BAND RADIO FREQUENCY SENSORS (KURFS) AND COYOTE® EFFECTORS TO DETECT AND DEFEAT UNMANNED AIRCRAFT.
