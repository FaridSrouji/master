RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES SECURES $135M IDIQ AWARD FOR C-130 NP2000 PROPELLER SYSTEM. ARLINGTON, VA. , FEB. 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE U.S. AIR FORCE LIFE CYCLE MANAGEMENT CENTER RECENTLY CONTRACTED RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES' (NYSE: RTX) COLLINS AEROSPACE BUSINESS TO CONTINUE PRODUCING NEW, ADVANCED PROPELLERS FOR C-130 AIRCRAFT.
