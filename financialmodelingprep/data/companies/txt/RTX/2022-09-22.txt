US AIR FORCE SELECTS RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE, NORTHROP GRUMMAN TO DELIVER FIRST HYPERSONIC AIR-BREATHING MISSILE. TUCSON, ARIZ. , SEPT. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH NORTHROP GRUMMAN (NYSE: NOC), HAS BEEN SELECTED TO DEVELOP THE HYPERSONIC ATTACK CRUISE MISSILE FOR THE U.S. AIR FORCE (USAF).
