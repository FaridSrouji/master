RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES TO ADVANCE U.S. AIR FORCE COMMON TACTICAL EDGE NETWORK. CTEN PROGRAM SUPPORTS AIR FORCE JADC2 TRANSFORMATION CEDAR RAPIDS, IOWA , MAY 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) SUCCESSFULLY DEMONSTRATED THE COMMON TACTICAL EDGE NETWORK DEVELOPED BY THE COMPANY'S COLLINS AEROSPACE BUSINESS AND WILL NOW BE DEVELOPING AN OPERATIONAL ARCHITECTURE FOR THE SERVICE. THE COMMON TACTICAL EDGE NETWORK, OR CTEN, WILL ENABLE TACTICAL DATA TO BE SHARED ACROSS DISPARATE NETWORKS TO ENABLE FASTER DECISION MAKING ON THE BATTLEFIELD.
