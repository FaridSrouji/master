COLLINS AEROSPACE SELECTED TO OUTFIT THE NEXT GENERATION OF SPACE EXPLORERS. COLLINS-LED TEAM TO PROVIDE NEW SPACESUIT FOR ISS CREW AND ARTEMIS MOON MISSIONS CEDAR RAPIDS, IOWA , JUNE 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COLLINS AEROSPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS - ALONG WITH TEAMMATES ILC DOVER AND OCEANEERING - HAVE BEEN SELECTED TO PRODUCE NASA'S NEXT-GENERATION SPACESUIT, WHICH ASTRONAUTS COULD WEAR WHEN WORKING OUTSIDE THE INTERNATIONAL SPACE STATION AND – WITHIN THE NEXT DECADE – ON THE MOON. THE NEW SUITS WERE DESIGNED BY ASTRONAUTS FOR ASTRONAUTS AND OFFER ENHANCED MOBILITY AND WEIGH LESS THAN THE CURRENT GENERATION SPACESUITS, ALLOWING FOR INCREASED MISSION TIMES.
