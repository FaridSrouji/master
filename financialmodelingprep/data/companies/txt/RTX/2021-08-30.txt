COLLINS AEROSPACE TO ACQUIRE FLIGHTAWARE. CEDAR RAPIDS, IOWA, AUG. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COLLINS AEROSPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES BUSINESS (NYSE: RTX), HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE PRIVATELY HELD FLIGHTAWARE, A LEADING DIGITAL AVIATION COMPANY PROVIDING GLOBAL FLIGHT TRACKING SOLUTIONS, PREDICTIVE TECHNOLOGY, ANALYTICS AND DECISION-MAKING TOOLS. CLOSURE OF THE ACQUISITION IS SUBJECT TO THE COMPLETION OF CUSTOMARY CONDITIONS AND REGULATORY APPROVALS.
