RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES COMPLETES DELIVERY OF 23 JOINT PRECISION APPROACH AND LANDING SYSTEMS TO THE U.S. NAVY. ARLINGTON, VA. , MARCH 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) DELIVERED ALL 23 CONTRACTED JOINT PRECISION APPROACH AND LANDING SYSTEMS LOW-RATE PRODUCTION UNITS TO THE U.S. NAVY ON TIME OR AHEAD OF SCHEDULE.
