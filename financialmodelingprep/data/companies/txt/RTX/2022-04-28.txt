RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE TO WORK WITH FIREHAWK AEROSPACE ON HYBRID ROCKET PROPULSION TECHNOLOGIES. TUCSON, ARIZ. , APRIL 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS, HAS COMPLETED A SERIES A INVESTMENT IN FIREHAWK AEROSPACE, A COMPANY THAT SPECIALIZES IN HIGH-PERFORMANCE PROPULSION TECHNOLOGY.
