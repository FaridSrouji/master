RAYTHEON INTELLIGENCE & SPACE TO BEGIN NEXT GENERATION JAMMER MID-BAND PRODUCTION. EL SEGUNDO, CALIF., JULY 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON INTELLIGENCE & SPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES BUSINESS (NYSE: RTX), HAS BEEN AWARDED A $171.6 MILLION CONTRACT FOR LOW-RATE INITIAL PRODUCTION LOT I, OR LRIP I, OF THE U.S. NAVY'S NEXT GENERATION JAMMER MID-BAND.
