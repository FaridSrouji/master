COLLINS AEROSPACE TO SUPPORT IATA TRAVEL PASS. ANNAPOLIS, MD., JULY, 28 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO HELP PASSENGERS MANAGE THEIR TESTING REQUIREMENTS, GOVERNMENTS SAFELY RE-OPEN THEIR BORDERS, AND RESTART INTERNATIONAL AIR TRAVEL, COLLINS AEROSPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS, IS TEAMING WITH THE INTERNATIONAL AIR TRANSPORT ASSOCIATION (IATA) TO SUPPORT ITS TRAVEL PASS DIGITAL HEALTH PLATFORM.
