SUGAR LAND NATIVE CHANNING YANG NAMED 2023 RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES MATHCOUNTS® NATIONAL CHAMPION. TEXAS CROWNED TOP STATE MATHCOUNTS® TEAM ORLANDO, FLA. , MAY 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHANNING YANG, A 13-YEAR-OLD SEVENTH GRADER FROM SUGAR LAND, TEXAS, SOLVED HIS WAY TO VICTORY OVER 222 COMPETITORS, BECOMING THE 2023 RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES MATHCOUNTS NATIONAL CHAMPION FOLLOWING AN INTENSE, ELITE COMPETITION AT THE HILTON ORLANDO LAKE BUENA VISTA THIS WEEKEND.
