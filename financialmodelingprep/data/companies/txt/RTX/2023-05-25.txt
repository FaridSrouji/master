RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES TO DELIVER FULL RATE PRODUCTION FOR TCTS INCREMENT II AIR COMBAT TRAINING SYSTEM FOR U.S. NAVY. CEDAR RAPIDS, IOWA , MAY 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COLLINS AEROSPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES BUSINESS (NYSE: RTX) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WILL DELIVER ON THE FULL RATE PRODUCTION CONTRACT AWARDED BY THE U.S. NAVY FOR TACTICAL COMBAT TRAINING SYSTEM – INCREMENT II (TCTS II), AIR COMBAT TRAINING SYSTEM (ACMI) FOR THE U.S. NAVY. THE CONTRACT INCLUDES BOTH AIRBORNE AND GROUND SUBSYSTEMS AND WILL SUPPORT FIELDING REQUIREMENTS AT VARIOUS U.S. NAVY TRAINING RANGES.
