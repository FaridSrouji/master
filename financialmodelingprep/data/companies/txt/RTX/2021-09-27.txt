RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE, NORTHROP GRUMMAN SUCCESSFULLY TEST FIRE HYPERSONIC WEAPON. TUCSON, ARIZ., SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON MISSILES & DEFENSE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH NORTHROP GRUMMAN (NYSE: NOC), SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED THE FIRST FLIGHT TEST OF A SCRAMJET-POWERED HYPERSONIC AIR-BREATHING WEAPON CONCEPT, OR HAWC, FOR THE DEFENSE ADVANCED RESEARCH PROJECTS AGENCY AND THE U.S. AIR FORCE.
