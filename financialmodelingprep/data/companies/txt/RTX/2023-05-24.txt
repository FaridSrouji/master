RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES AND VISTA BRING LUXSTREAM UNLIMITED DATA PLAN TO CUSTOMERS. GENEVA , MAY 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES' (NYSE: RTX) COLLINS AEROSPACE BUSINESS TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS PLAN TO BRING UNLIMITED DATA TO THE CUSTOMERS OF VISTA, A LEADING PRIVATE AVIATION GROUP. USING COLLINS' LUXSTREAM, WHICH RUNS EXCLUSIVELY ON THE SES'S HIGH-THROUGHPUT SATELLITE (HTS) NETWORK, VISTA CUSTOMERS CAN NOW ENJOY FAST, RELIABLE, UNLIMITED INTERNET ACCESS FOR THE DURATION OF THEIR FLIGHTS.
