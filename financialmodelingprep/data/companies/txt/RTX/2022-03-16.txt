RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES APPOINTS BARBARA BORGONOVI AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE STRATEGY & DEVELOPMENT. WALTHAM, MASS., MARCH 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BARBARA BORGONOVI HAS BEEN NAMED RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES' (NYSE: RTX) SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE STRATEGY & DEVELOPMENT, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1, 2022.
