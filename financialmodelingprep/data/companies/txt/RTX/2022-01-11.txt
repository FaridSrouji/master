BOEING SELECTS COLLINS AEROSPACE ELECTRIC POWER GENERATION SYSTEM FOR B-52. ROCKFORD, ILL., JAN. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF THE U.S. AIR FORCE'S ONGOING MODERNIZATION EFFORTS TO KEEP THE B-52 BOMBER FLYING INTO THE 2050S, BOEING HAS SELECTED COLLINS AEROSPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) COMPANY, TO UPGRADE THE AIRCRAFT WITH A NEW ELECTRIC POWER GENERATION SYSTEM (EPGS).
