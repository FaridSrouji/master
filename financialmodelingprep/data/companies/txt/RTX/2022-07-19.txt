RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES SELECTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY TO TEST HYDROGEN AND AMMONIA AS CLEAN POWER FUEL SOURCES. EAST HARTFORD, CONN. , JULY 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) HAS BEEN SELECTED BY THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY FOR TWO RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS TO TEST THE USE OF HYDROGEN AND AMMONIA AS EFFECTIVE, ZERO-CARBON OPTIONS FOR ELECTRICITY GENERATION.
