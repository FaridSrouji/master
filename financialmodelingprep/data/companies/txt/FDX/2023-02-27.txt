FEDEX LAUNCHES 50 DAYS OF CARING TO CELEBRATE ITS 50TH ANNIVERSARY. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) TODAY KICKED OFF A 50-DAY COUNTDOWN TO APRIL 17, 2023, THE DAY FEDEX CELEBRATES 50 YEARS OF OPERATIONS, WITH ITS 50 DAYS OF CARING INITIATIVE. DURING THIS TIME, FEDEX TEAM MEMBERS WILL BE GIVING BACK TO THEIR COMMUNITIES THROUGH SERVICE PROJECTS AROUND THE WORLD. IN ADDITION, THE COMPANY WILL HOLD EVENTS AND SHARE STORIES THAT CELEBRATE ITS TEAM MEMBERS, SHINE A SPOTLIGHT ON ITS CUSTOMERS, AND GIVE THE WORLD A GLIMPSE INTO WHAT'S NEXT.
