FEDEX ESTABLISHES NEW INITIATIVE TO SUPPORT LOCAL HBCUS. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) ANNOUNCED TODAY A PLEDGE TO COMMIT $5 MILLION TO FOUR HISTORICALLY BLACK COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES (HBCUS) IN MISSISSIPPI AND TENNESSEE. THE INITIATIVE WILL CREATE VALUABLE EDUCATION AND JOB READINESS FOR STUDENTS AT TENNESSEE STATE UNIVERSITY, JACKSON STATE UNIVERSITY, MISSISSIPPI VALLEY STATE UNIVERSITY, AND LEMOYNE-OWEN COLLEGE.
