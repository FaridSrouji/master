SHIPSTATION ANNOUNCES FEDEX CARRIER INTEGRATION IN CANADA. AUSTIN, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SHIPSTATION, THE WORLD'S LEADING CLOUD-BASED ECOMMERCE SHIPPING SOLUTION, HAS ANNOUNCED THAT CANADIAN SMALL BUSINESS MERCHANTS CAN NOW BENEFIT FROM A NEW ALLIANCE AGREEMENT WITH FEDEX, ONE OF THE WORLD'S LARGEST EXPRESS TRANSPORTATION COMPANIES, WITH THE COMPANY'S SHIPPING SERVICES NOW AVAILABLE THROUGH SHIPSTATION CARRIER. THIS NEW OFFERING ALLOWS SMALL AND MEDIUM-SIZED BUSINESSES (SMBS) IN CANADA TO ACCESS FAST, EASY, AND RELIABLE FEDEX SHIPPING SERVICES SEAMLE.
