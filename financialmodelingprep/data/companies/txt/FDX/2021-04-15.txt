FEDEX OFFICE, VERICAST UNVEIL BRANDED PRODUCT MARKETPLACE. PLANO, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX OFFICE, A WORLD-CLASS PROVIDER OF CONVENIENT, STATE-OF-THE-ART PRINTING, PACKING AND SHIPPING SERVICES AND SUBSIDIARY OF FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX), AND VERICAST, A LEADING MARKETING SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TOGETHER WITH ITS LINE OF HARLAND CLARKE PROMOTIONAL SOLUTIONS, HAVE JOINED FORCES TO PROVIDE A WIDE SELECTION OF CUSTOM BRANDED MERCHANDISE TO HELP BUSINESSES ACROSS INDUSTRIES BUILD BRAND RECOGNITION AND DRIVE COMPANY GROWTH AS COVID-19 CHALLENGES CONTINUE. ACC
