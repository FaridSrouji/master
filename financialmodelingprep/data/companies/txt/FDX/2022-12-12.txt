FEDEX TO LAUNCH EXPANDED CONSOLIDATED RETURNS SOLUTION IN 2023. MEMPHIS, TENN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF FEDEX CONSOLIDATED RETURNS IN THE U.S., A SOLUTION THAT OFFERS A LOW-PRICED, EASY E-COMMERCE RETURNS OPTION THAT MERCHANTS CAN PASS ON TO SHOPPERS. THE EXPANDED SOLUTION WILL LAUNCH IN EARLY 2023.
