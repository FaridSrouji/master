FEDEX ANNOUNCES PLANNED CONSOLIDATION OF OPERATING COMPANIES. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) IS ANNOUNCING TODAY AT ITS DRIVE INVESTOR EVENT THAT IT WILL CONSOLIDATE ITS OPERATING COMPANIES INTO ONE ORGANIZATION, CREATING EFFICIENCIES THAT WILL ENHANCE THE COMPANY'S ABILITY TO MEET THE EVOLVING NEEDS OF CUSTOMERS AND ULTIMATELY BUILD A STRONGER, MORE PROFITABLE ENTERPRISE.
