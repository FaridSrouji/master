FEDEX CORP. BOARD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.15 PER SHARE ON FEDEX CORP. COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE APRIL 3, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 13, 2023. FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) PROVIDES CUSTOMERS AND BUSINESSES WORLDWIDE WITH A BROAD PORTFOLIO OF TRANSPORTATION, E-COMMERCE AND BUSINESS SERVICES.
