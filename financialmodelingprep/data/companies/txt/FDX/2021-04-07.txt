FEDEX OFFICE DONATES $100,000 IN PRINTING TO FUTURE READY SCHOOLS IN SUPPORT OF STUDENTS IMPACTED BY PANDEMIC. PLANO, TEXAS, APRIL 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  FEDEX OFFICE, A WORLD-CLASS PROVIDER OF CONVENIENT, STATE-OF-THE-ART PRINTING, PACKING AND SHIPPING SERVICES AND SUBSIDIARY OF FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE DONATION OF A RETAIL VALUE OF $100,000 FOR IN-KIND PRINTING SERVICES AND SOLUTIONS TO FUTURE READY SCHOOLS ®, AN EQUITY-FOCUSED INITIATIVE TO TRANSFORM TEACHING AND LEARNING. THE DONATION AIMS TO REDUCE DISPARITIES IN STUDENT LEARNING, WORSENED BY COVID-19.
