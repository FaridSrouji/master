NATIXIS PROVIDES $51.5 MILLION FINANCING FOR THE CLASS-A, FEDEX LOGISTICS HEADQUARTERS OFFICE BUILDING IN MEMPHIS. NEW YORK, AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NATIXIS ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PROVIDED FLOATING-RATE FINANCING OF $51.5 MILLION TO SUBSIDIARIES OF SOMERAROAD INC. FOR THE REFINANCING OF A 193,836 SQUARE FOOT, CLASS-A OFFICE BUILDING LOCATED AT 145 LT. GEORGE W LEE AVENUE IN DOWNTOWN MEMPHIS, AND THE PARKING LOT ACROSS THE STREET NEAR THE FEDEX FORUM.
