FEDEX LAUNCHES NINTH ANNUAL SMALL BUSINESS GRANT CONTEST. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS NINTH ANNUAL SMALL BUSINESS GRANT CONTEST. THE CONTEST OFFERS GRANTS AND SERVICES TO 12 U.S.- BASED SMALL BUSINESSES, DISTRIBUTING A COLLECTIVE PRIZE POOL OF MORE THAN $250,000. WINNERS ALSO RECEIVE FEDEX OFFICE® PRINT AND BUSINESS SERVICES CREDIT TO HELP SUPPORT THEIR SMALL BUSINESS. IN ADDITION, THEY JOIN A COMMUNITY OF SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS WHO SHARE PEER INSIGHTS.
