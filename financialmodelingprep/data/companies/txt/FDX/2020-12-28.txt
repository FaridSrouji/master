FEDEX COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF SHOPRUNNER, EXPANDING ITS E-COMMERCE CAPABILITIES. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF SHOPRUNNER, THE E-COMMERCE PLATFORM THAT DIRECTLY CONNECTS BRANDS AND MERCHANTS WITH ONLINE SHOPPERS. SHOPRUNNER'S CAPABILITIES COMPLEMENT AND EXPAND THE FEDEX E-COMMERCE PORTFOLIO AND ARE EXPECTED TO CREATE INCREASED VALUE FOR BRANDS, MERCHANTS AND CONSUMERS.
