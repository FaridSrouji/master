FEDEX CORP. BOARD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 2, 2020. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF FEDEX CORPORATION DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.65 PER SHARE ON FEDEX CORPORATION COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JANUARY 2, 2020 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 9, 2019.
