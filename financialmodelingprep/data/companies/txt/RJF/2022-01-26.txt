RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL REPORTS FIRST QUARTER OF FISCAL 2022 RESULTS. RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: RJF) TODAY REPORTED NET REVENUES OF $2.78 BILLION AND NET INCOME OF $446 MILLION, OR $2.10 PER DILUTED SHARE, FOR THE FISCAL FIRST QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2021. EXCLUDING $6 MILLION OF ACQUISITION-RELATED EXPENSES, QUARTERLY ADJUSTED NET INCOME WAS $451 MILLION(2), OR $2.12 PER DILUTED SHARE(2).
