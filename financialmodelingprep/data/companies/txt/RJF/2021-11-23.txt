RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL REPORTS OCTOBER 2021 OPERATING DATA. ST. PETERSBURG, FLA., NOV. 23, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: RJF) TODAY REPORTED SELECTED OPERATING DATA FOR OCTOBER 2021, IN AN EFFORT TO PROVIDE TIMELY INFORMATION TO INVESTORS ABOUT GENERAL TRENDS IN OUR MAJOR BUSINESS SEGMENTS. DUE TO THE LIMITED NATURE OF THIS DATA, A CONSISTENT CORRELATION TO EARNINGS SHOULD NOT BE ASSUMED.
