STEWARD PARTNERS GLOBAL ADVISORY WELCOMES O'HARE WEALTH MANAGEMENT, A $420 MILLION TEAM IN WISCONSIN. WASHINGTON, OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STEWARD PARTNERS GLOBAL ADVISORY, LLC, AN EMPLOYEE-OWNED, FULL-SERVICE INDEPENDENT PARTNERSHIP ASSOCIATED WITH RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL SERVICES, INC. (MEMBER FINRA/SIPC), HAS EXPANDED ITS NETWORK WITH THE ADDITION OF O'HARE WEALTH MANAGEMENT AFFILIATED WITH STEWARD PARTNERS IN MEQUON, WISCONSIN. THE TEAM, WHICH IS STEWARD'S FIRST TEAM IN THE STATE, HAS $420 MILLION IN ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT.
