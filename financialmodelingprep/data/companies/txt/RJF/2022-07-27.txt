RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL REPORTS THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL 2022 RESULTS. RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: RJF) TODAY REPORTED NET REVENUES OF $2.72 BILLION AND NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $299 MILLION, OR $1.38 PER DILUTED SHARE, FOR THE FISCAL THIRD QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2022. EXCLUDING $65 MILLION OF EXPENSES RELATED TO ACQUISITIONS, QUARTERLY ADJUSTED NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS WAS $348 MILLION(2), OR $1.61 PER DILUTED SHARE(2).
