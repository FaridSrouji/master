RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ST. PETERSBURG, FLA., MAY 23, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ON MAY 23, 2022, THE RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: RJF) BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK OF $0.34 PER SHARE, PAYABLE JULY 15, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JULY 1, 2022.
