STEWARD PARTNERS MOVES UP TO #20 ON BARRON'S TOP 100 RIA FIRMS LIST. WASHINGTON, SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STEWARD PARTNERS, AN EMPLOYEE-OWNED, FULL-SERVICE WEALTH MANAGEMENT FIRM ASSOCIATED WITH RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL SERVICES, INC. (MEMBER FINRA/SIPC), HAS BEEN RANKED #20 IN BARRON'S TOP 100 RIA FIRMS LIST FOR 2021, MOVING UP FROM #24 IN 2020.
