MAINFIRST BANK AG CANCELLED THE ACQUISITION OF EUROPEAN EQUITIES INSTITUTIONAL BROKERAGE BUSINESS FROM RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL, INC.. MAINFIRST BANK AG AGREED TO ACQUIRE THE EUROPEAN EQUITIES INSTITUTIONAL BROKERAGE BUSINESS FROM RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL, INC., ON OCTOBER 9, 2018. THESE EUROPEAN BUSINESSES ARE BASED IN PARIS AND LONDON. AS PART OF THIS ACQUISITION, MAINFIRST WILL ALSO ADD 29 EMPLOYEES FROM RAYMOND JAMES IN PARIS INCLUDING RESEARCH, SALES AND SALES TRADING AND A FURTHER SIX RESEARCH EMPLOYEES IN LONDON. THESE INDIVIDUALS WILL BE INTEGRATED INTO THE MAINFIRST ORGANIZATION. THE TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO COMPLETE ON DECEMBER 1, 2018.
