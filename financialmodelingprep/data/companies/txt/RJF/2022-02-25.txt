RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ST. PETERSBURG, FLA., FEB. 25, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ON FEBRUARY 25, 2022, THE RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: RJF) BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK OF $0.34 PER SHARE, PAYABLE APRIL 18, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON APRIL 4, 2022.
