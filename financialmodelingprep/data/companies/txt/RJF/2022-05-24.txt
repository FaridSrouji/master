RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL REPORTS APRIL 2022 OPERATING DATA. ST. PETERSBURG, FLA., MAY 24, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: RJF) TODAY REPORTED SELECTED OPERATING DATA FOR APRIL 2022, IN AN EFFORT TO PROVIDE TIMELY INFORMATION TO INVESTORS ABOUT MONTHLY DEVELOPMENTS IN CERTAIN KEY PERFORMANCE METRICS. DUE TO THE LIMITED NATURE OF THIS DATA, A CONSISTENT CORRELATION TO EARNINGS SHOULD NOT BE ASSUMED.
