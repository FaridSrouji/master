NEW YORK , MAY 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN ALPHABET INC. ("ALPHABET" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: GOOGL) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF ALPHABET INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN FEBRUARY 4, 2020 AND JANUARY 23, 2023.
