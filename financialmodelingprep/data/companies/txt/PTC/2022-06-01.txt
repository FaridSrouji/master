ITC INFOTECH AND PTC CREATE ONE OF THE INDUSTRY'S LARGEST ORGANISATIONS OF WINDCHILL SERVICES EXPERTISE WITH EXPANDED ALLIANCE. COMPANIES COMBINE PLM EXPERTS UNDER DXP SERVICES, A NEW ITC INFOTECH BUSINESS UNIT BANGALORE, INDIA AND BOSTON , JUNE 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ITC INFOTECH AND PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CLOSING OF THE TRANSACTION CONTEMPLATED BETWEEN THE PARTIES UNDER THE STRATEGIC PARTNER AGREEMENT ANNOUNCED IN APRIL. THE TRANSACTION IS DESIGNED TO ACCELERATE CUSTOMER DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION INITIATIVES AND THE ADOPTION OF PTC'S INDUSTRY-LEADING WINDCHILL® PRODUCT LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT (PLM) SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SAAS).
