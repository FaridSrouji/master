KALYPSO NAMED PTC SYSTEM INTEGRATOR PARTNER OF THE YEAR. MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KALYPSO, A PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM HELPING CLIENTS DISCOVER, CREATE, MAKE, AND SELL BETTER PRODUCTS WITH DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES, ANNOUNCES IT HAS BEEN NAMED PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) SYSTEM INTEGRATOR PARTNER OF THE YEAR. THIS AWARD RECOGNIZES PARTNERS WHO HAVE DEMONSTRATED EXCELLENCE AND EXPERTISE IN HELPING PTC CUSTOMERS ACCELERATE THEIR DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION AGENDAS. KALYPSO ALSO WON THE AWARD FOR GREATEST GROWTH OF ACV, REPRESENTING THE LARGEST GROWTH PERCENTAGE OF ACV
