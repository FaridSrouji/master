PTC EXPANDS ROLES FOR EXECUTIVE TEAM MEMBERS. COMPANY NAMES MIKE DITULLIO TO PRESIDENT, DIGITAL THREAD GROUP; CATHERINE KNIKER TO CHIEF STRATEGY AND SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER BOSTON , MAY 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) TODAY ANNOUNCED EXPANDED ROLES FOR TWO MEMBERS OF ITS EXECUTIVE TEAM, BOTH EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. LONG-TIME PTC EXECUTIVE AND CURRENT PRESIDENT OF PTC'S VELOCITY GROUP MIKE DITULLIO WILL TAKE OVER AS PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY'S LARGER DIGITAL THREAD GROUP.
