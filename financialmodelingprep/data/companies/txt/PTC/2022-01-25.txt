PTC AND SCHAEFFLER ANNOUNCE STRATEGIC ALLIANCE TO ACCELERATE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION ROADMAP. BOSTON, JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) AND GLOBAL AUTOMOTIVE AND INDUSTRIAL SUPPLIER SCHAEFFLER TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW ALLIANCE THAT WILL SEE SCHAEFFLER STANDARDIZE ON PTC SOLUTIONS TO DEPLOY AN INTEGRATED, END-TO-END IT LANDSCAPE. SCHAEFFLER WILL ALSO INFORM PTC'S EFFORTS TO FURTHER DEVELOP AND EVOLVE MARKET-SPECIFIC STANDARD SOLUTIONS TO BETTER MEET THE NEEDS OF AUTOMOTIVE AND INDUSTRIAL COMPANIES.
