PTC ANNOUNCES THE RETURN OF LIVEWORX TO BOSTON'S SEAPORT INNOVATION DISTRICT. PTC TO HOST ITS PREMIER ANNUAL IN-PERSON EVENT MAY 15-18, 2023 BOSTON , DEC. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RETURN OF ITS ANNUAL LIVEWORX®, PREMIER EVENT FOCUSED ON DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION FOR MANUFACTURERS. THE LIVEWORX 2023 EXPERIENCE WILL RETURN IN PERSON, HOSTED IN THE HEART OF BOSTON'S SEAPORT INNOVATION DISTRICT AT THE BOSTON CONVENTION AND EXHIBITION CENTER, MAY 15-18, 2023.
