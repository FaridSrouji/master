PTC AND ROCKWELL AUTOMATION EXTEND RELATIONSHIP WITH FOCUS ON IOT AND AUGMENTED REALITY FOR MANUFACTURING. STRATEGIC RELATIONSHIP TO FOCUS ON SALES OF PTC'S IOT AND AR SOFTWARE BOSTON AND MILWAUKEE , JUNE 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) AND ROCKWELL AUTOMATION (NYSE: ROK) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN EXTENSION OF THEIR RELATIONSHIP, FOCUSED ON THE ADOPTION OF INTERNET OF THINGS (IOT) AND AUGMENTED REALITY (AR) SOFTWARE BY MANUFACTURING COMPANIES. ROCKWELL AUTOMATION WILL CONTINUE TO RESELL PTC'S THINGWORX® IOT SOFTWARE, INCLUDING THE DIGITAL PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT (DPM)™ MANUFACTURING SOLUTION, AND VUFORIA® AR SOFTWARE TO NEW AND EXISTING CUSTOMERS ACROSS DISCRETE AND PROCESS MANUFACTURING VERTICALS.
