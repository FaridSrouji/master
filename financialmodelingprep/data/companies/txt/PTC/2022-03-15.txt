PTC APPOINTS AMAR HANSPAL TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. BOSTON, MARCH 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) HAS APPOINTED AMAR HANSPAL TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1, 2022. HANSPAL BRINGS MORE THAN 30 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE DRIVING STRATEGIC AND OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE THROUGH A COMBINATION OF PRODUCT EXPERTISE, STRATEGIC TEAM LEADERSHIP, AND HIGH-PERFORMANCE EXECUTION.
