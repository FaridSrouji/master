PTC SHIPS TWO MORE SAAS PRODUCTS ON PTC ATLAS PLATFORM. BOSTON, APRIL 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS EXPANDED ITS ROBUST SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SAAS) CAPABILITIES WITH TWO NEW PRODUCTS SHIPPING ON THE PTC ATLAS™ SAAS PLATFORM. ATLAS, ANNOUNCED AT THE LIVEWORX® 2020 DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION CONFERENCE, NOW SUPPORTS AN EXPANDING PORTFOLIO OF SAAS APPLICATIONS AND PRODUCTION-READY SOLUTIONS.
