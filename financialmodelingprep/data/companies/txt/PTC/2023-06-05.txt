PTC NAMES JANESH MOORJANI TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. BOSTON , JUNE 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) HAS APPOINTED JANESH MOORJANI TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JUNE 7, 2023. AN EXPERIENCED PUBLIC COMPANY FINANCE LEADER WITH INTERNATIONAL OPERATING EXPERIENCE IN SALES, MOORJANI HAS SPENT MORE THAN TWO DECADES HELPING FUEL GROWTH AND DRIVE TRANSFORMATION IN LARGE AND MID-SIZE TECHNOLOGY COMPANIES.
