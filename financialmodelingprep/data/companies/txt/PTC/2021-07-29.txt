LTI WINS PTC PARTNER NETWORK AWARD FOR TRANSFORMATION IN MANUFACTURING OPERATIONS. MUMBAI, INDIA, JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LARSEN & TOUBRO INFOTECH (BSE: 540005) (NSE: LTI), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY CONSULTING AND DIGITAL SOLUTIONS COMPANY, HAS BEEN NAMED WINNER OF THE PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) PARTNER NETWORK AWARDS IN THE CATEGORY OF TRANSFORMATION IN MANUFACTURING OPERATIONS. LTI ALSO EMERGED AS A FINALIST IN TWO ADDITIONAL CATEGORIES OF MOST IMPACT IN DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION AT SCALE AND TRAILBLAZER/INNOVATION PROJECT.
