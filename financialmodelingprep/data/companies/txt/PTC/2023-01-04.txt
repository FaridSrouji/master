PTC COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF SERVICEMAX. ACQUISITION EXPANDS SERVICE CAPABILITIES OF PTC'S CLOSED-LOOP PLM PORTFOLIO BOSTON , JAN. 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PTC  (NASDAQ: PTC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF SERVICEMAX FROM AN ENTITY MAJORITY OWNED BY SILVER LAKE. THE ACQUISITION ADDS IMPORTANT FIELD SERVICE MANAGEMENT (FSM) CAPABILITIES TO PTC'S CLOSED-LOOP PRODUCT LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT (PLM) AND DIGITAL THREAD OFFERINGS.
