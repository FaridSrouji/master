NEW YORK ISLANDERS INTEGRATE BLUEJEANS BY VERIZON TO ELEVATE HOMEICE FAN EXPERIENCE. NEW YORK, APRIL 27, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON BUSINESS TODAY ANNOUNCED THE NEW YORK ISLANDERS ARE USING BLUEJEANS BY VERIZON TO PROVIDE VIDEO ENABLED WATCH PARTIES IN THEIR HOMEICE EXPERIENCE PLATFORM WITHIN THE UBS ARENA MOBILE APP. LEVERAGING THE BLUEJEANS SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT KIT (SDK), THE NEW BLUEJEANS MEETINGS CAPABILITY FOR HOMEICE, POWERED BY HOMETURF, WILL BE AVAILABLE AS AN IN-APP “SECOND SCREEN” EXPERIENCE FOR FANS LOOKING TO CONNECT LIVE OVER VIDEO WITH THEIR FRIENDS AND FAVORITE TEAM DURING GAMES MOVING FORWARD.
