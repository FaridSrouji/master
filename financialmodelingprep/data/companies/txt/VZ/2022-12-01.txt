12/1/22: VERIZON DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, DEC. 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VERIZON COMMUNICATIONS INC. (NYSE, NASDAQ: VZ) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 65.25 CENTS PER OUTSTANDING SHARE, UNCHANGED FROM THE PREVIOUS QUARTER. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 1, 2023, TO VERIZON SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 10, 2023.
