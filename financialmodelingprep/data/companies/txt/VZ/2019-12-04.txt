VERIZON COMMUNICATIONS INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 3, 2020. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VERIZON COMMUNICATIONS INC. DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 61.50 CENTS PER OUTSTANDING SHARE, UNCHANGED FROM THE PREVIOUS QUARTER. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 3, 2020, TO VERIZON SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 10, 2020.
