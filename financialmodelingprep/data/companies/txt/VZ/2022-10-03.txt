VERIZON DEPLOYS ADDITIONAL 5G ULTRA WIDEBAND TO HELP WITH HURRICANE IAN RECOVERY. LEE COUNTY, FLA., OCT. 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- IN THE WAKE OF THE MOST DESTRUCTIVE HURRICANE TO HIT THE SOUTHWESTERN COAST OF FLORIDA IN DECADES, VERIZON'S ENGINEERS ARE RESPONDING QUICKLY TO COMMUNITY NEEDS BY DEPLOYING ADDITIONAL 5G ULTRA WIDEBAND TECHNOLOGY TO CRITICAL CELL SITES IN LEE AND CHARLOTTE COUNTIES THIS MORNING TO PROVIDE GREATER CAPACITY FOR FIRST RESPONDERS, PUBLIC SAFETY OFFICIALS AND RESIDENTS CONNECTING TO LOVED ONES AND CRITICAL RESOURCES.
