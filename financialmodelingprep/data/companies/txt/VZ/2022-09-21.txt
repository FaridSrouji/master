VERIZON BUSINESS AND VISIONABLE EXPAND CONNECTED HEALTHCARE PARTNERSHIP. LONDON, SEPT. 21, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON BUSINESS AND VISIONABLE, A LEADING HEALTH TECHNOLOGY COMPANY BASED IN THE U.K., TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY ARE EXPANDING THEIR PARTNERSHIP WHICH WILL ENABLE BOTH COMPANIES TO WORK ON A RANGE OF CONNECTED HEALTHCARE SOLUTIONS, VIA VISIONABLE'S PATENTED TECHNOLOGY POWERED BY VERIZON'S 5G ULTRA WIDEBAND AND 5G EDGE NETWORK, IN THE U.S.
