VERIZON FRONTLINE SUPPORTS ARIZONA FIRST RESPONDERS BATTLING WILDFIRES. PHOENIX, JULY 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON FRONTLINE RECENTLY DEPLOYED ITS ADVANCED PUBLIC SAFETY PLATFORMS AND TECHNOLOGY, VIA THE VERIZON RESPONSE TEAM (VRT) , TO SUPPORT FIRST RESPONDERS BATTLING WILDFIRES THROUGHOUT ARIZONA.
