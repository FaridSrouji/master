VERIZON COMMISSIONS MURAL IN GREENVILLE AS PART OF #ACALLFORKINDNESS. GREENVILLE, S.C., AUG. 18, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON IS SPREADING KINDNESS IN GREENVILLE, THANKS TO A PARTNERSHIP WITH LOCAL ARTISTS, GIRLS WHO PAINT MURALS - CHRISTINE CRAWFORD AND ALLI DUNAVANT. LOCATED AT 54 PIEDMONT HIGHWAY, IT'S ALL PART OF VERIZON'S NATIONAL #ACALLFORKINDNESS CAMPAIGN WHICH AIMS TO MAKE THE WORLD A KINDER PLACE, ONE ACT AT A TIME.
