VERIZON BOOSTS INVESTMENT IN FLORIDA TO ACCOMMODATE RECENT MASSIVE, PANDEMIC-RELATED INFLUX OF PEOPLE. TAMPA, FLA., MAY 16, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SINCE THE BEGINNING OF THE PANDEMIC, FLORIDA HAS SEEN PEOPLE FLOCK TO THE STATE, DRAWN TO THE UNBEATABLE WEATHER AND FRIENDLY HOSPITALITY, THE FAMILY FUN IN THE MANY THEME PARKS THROUGHOUT THE STATE, AND THE MILES AND MILES OF BEAUTIFUL COASTLINE. VERIZON IS RESPONDING TO FLORIDA'S EXPONENTIAL AND UNEXPECTED POPULATION INCREASE BY ALLOCATING AN ADDITIONAL $149 MILLION TO ITS LOCAL CAPITAL SPENDING TO MEET SKYROCKETING DEMANDS ON THE NETWORK.
