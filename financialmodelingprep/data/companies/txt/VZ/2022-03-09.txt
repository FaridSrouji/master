VERIZON EXPANDS HUMANITARIAN CRISIS RELIEF. NEW YORK, MARCH 09, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TODAY, VERIZON EXTENDED ITS RELIEF EFFORTS TO HELP KEEP ITS CUSTOMERS CONNECTED DURING THE HUMANITARIAN CRISIS IN UKRAINE. THROUGH MARCH 17, VERIZON IS WAIVING INTERNATIONAL CALLING, TEXTING AND DATA ROAMING CHARGES FOR CONSUMER AND SMALL BUSINESS WIRELESS CUSTOMERS TO AND FROM HUNGARY, MOLDOVA, POLAND, ROMANIA, SLOVAKIA AND UKRAINE. RESIDENTIAL LANDLINE CUSTOMERS WILL SEE WAIVED INTERNATIONAL CHARGES TO THOSE COUNTRIES AS WELL. CUSTOMERS DO NOT HAVE TO TAKE ANY ACTION TO TAKE ADVANTAGE OF THESE OFFERS.
