VERIZON FRONTLINE SUPPORTS NM, AZ FIRST RESPONDERS BATTLING WILDFIRES. LAS VEGAS, N.M., MAY 09, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE VERIZON FRONTLINE RESPONSE TEAM HAS DEPLOYED TO MULTIPLE LOCATIONS ACROSS THE SOUTHWEST TO PROVIDE THE THOUSANDS OF FIRST RESPONDERS CURRENTLY ENGAGED IN FIREFIGHTING EFFORTS IN ARIZONA AND NEW MEXICO WITH MISSION-CRITICAL COMMUNICATIONS CAPABILITIES.
