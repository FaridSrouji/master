THE DEPLOYMENT TOOK PLACE AT PENN STATE'S INNOVATION PARK , WHICH OFFERS 118-ACRES OF OFFICE, MANUFACTURING AND RESEARCH SPACE, AND IS PART OF ONE OF THE WORLD'S PREMIER RESEARCH INSTITUTIONS, WITH ACCESS TO PENN STATE'S SCIENTIFIC, ENGINEERING, TECHNOLOGY AND BUSINESS RESOURCES. THE EFFORT AIMS TO FOSTER NEW RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT PARTNERSHIPS TO IMPROVE COMMERCIAL APPLICATIONS AND WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT THROUGH ADVANCED WIRELESS TECHNOLOGIES AND EXPAND 3D PRINTING ACCESS AND EDUCATION TO PENN STATE STUDENTS.
