VERIZON PUTS MORE TOOLS IN PLACE TO HELP PROTECT CUSTOMERS FROM UNWANTED SPAM TEXTS. SPAM TEXTS ARE ALL THOSE UNWANTED MESSAGES THAT CUSTOMERS DID NOT AGREE TO RECEIVE. JUST AS VERIZON CONTINUES TO COMBAT ROBOCALLS ON BEHALF OF OUR CUSTOMERS — 20 BILLION BLOCKED TO DATE — WE ARE WORKING DILIGENTLY TO CRACK DOWN ON PESKY SPAM TEXTS AND HAVE PUT A NUMBER OF TOOLS IN PLACE TO PREVENT THEM FROM EVER REACHING YOU.
