NEXSTAR AND VERIZON FIOS REACH MULTI-YEAR DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT. IRVING, TEXAS & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEXSTAR MEDIA GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ: NXST) (“NEXSTAR”), AND VERIZON FIOS (NYSE: VZ) (“VERIZON”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE REACHED A COMPREHENSIVE MULTI-YEAR DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT REGARDING LOCAL TELEVISION STATIONS IN 10 MARKETS AND NEXSTAR'S FAST-GROWING NATIONAL CABLE NEWS NETWORK, NEWSNATION.
