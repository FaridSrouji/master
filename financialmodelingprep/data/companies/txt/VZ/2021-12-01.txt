VERIZON AWARDED $34.6M IN DIGITAL MODERNIZATION CONTRACTS WITH U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY. WASHINGTON, DEC. 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON PUBLIC SECTOR HAS BEEN AWARDED $34.6 MILLION THROUGH TWO CONTRACT AWARDS WITH THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (DOE). THE TASK ORDERS WERE AWARDED THROUGH THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT'S ENTERPRISE INFRASTRUCTURE SOLUTIONS (EIS) CONTRACT VEHICLE. THE WIN FOR VERIZON INCLUDES VOICE AND DATA SERVICES AND WILL INVOLVE A NUMBER OF DOE FACILITIES, INCLUDING THEIR WASHINGTON, D.C. HEADQUARTERS.
