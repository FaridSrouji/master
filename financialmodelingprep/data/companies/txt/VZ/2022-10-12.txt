VERIZON FRONTLINE INTRODUCES NEWEST FIRST RESPONDER ADVISORY COUNCIL MEMBER. BASKING RIDGE, N.J., OCT. 12, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON FRONTLINE TODAY INTRODUCED CHIEF KARA KALKBRENNER (RET.), FORMER CHIEF OF THE PHOENIX FIRE DEPARTMENT, AS THE NEWEST MEMBER OF THE VERIZON FIRST RESPONDER ADVISORY COUNCIL (VFRAC) , BRINGING 37 YEARS OF PUBLIC SAFETY EXPERIENCE TO THIS NEW ROLE.
