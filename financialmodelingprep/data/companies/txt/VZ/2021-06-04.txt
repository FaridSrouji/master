VERIZON EVP & CTO KYLE MALADY TO SPEAK AT WELLS FARGO CONFERENCE JUNE 7. BASKING RIDGE, N.J., JUNE 04, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- KYLE MALADY, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER FOR VERIZON (NYSE, NASDAQ: VZ), IS SCHEDULED TO SPEAK AT THE VIRTUAL WELLS FARGO MEDIA TELCO DAY ON MONDAY, JUNE 7, AT 9:30 A.M. ET. HIS REMARKS WILL BE WEBCAST, WITH ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS AVAILABLE ON VERIZON'S INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE, WWW.VERIZON.COM/ABOUT/INVESTORS/.
