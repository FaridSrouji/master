VERIZON AND DREAMSCAPE IMMERSIVE INK 5G INNOVATION PARTNERSHIP. BASKING RIDGE, N.J., APRIL 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON AND DREAMSCAPE IMMERSIVE TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP AIMED AT INNOVATING VIRTUAL REALITY (VR) APPLICATIONS USING 5G AND MOBILE EDGE COMPUTING (MEC). THE PARTNERSHIP'S FIRST PRIORITIES WILL FOCUS ON 5G IMMERSIVE-LEARNING AND TRAINING INNOVATIONS FOR USE IN ENTERPRISE, PUBLIC SECTOR, AND EDUCATION ENVIRONMENTS.
VERIZON SIGNS ITS FIRST EUROPEAN PRIVATE 5G DEAL WITH ASSOCIATED BRITISH PORTS.
