NRG STADIUM GETS MAJOR UPGRADE FROM VERIZON. HOUSTON, MARCH 06, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NRG STADIUM, HOME TO FOOTBALL SHOWDOWNS, BASKETBALL BUZZER BEATERS AND RODEO LEGENDS, RECENTLY RECEIVED SOME MAJOR NETWORK ENHANCEMENTS FROM VERIZON. VERIZON ENGINEERS RECENTLY ADDED 45 NODES (THE EQUIVALENT OF 15 FULL SIZE CELL SITES) INSIDE THE STADIUM. THESE UPGRADES DELIVER 5G COVERAGE USING BOTH VERIZON'S NEWLY ACQUIRED C-BAND SPECTRUM AND ITS PREMIUM HIGH-BAND MMWAVE SPECTRUM. ADDITIONALLY, VERIZON DEPLOYED ADVANCED PERFORMANCE CONTROL FEATURES, AND UPGRADED COVERAGE AND PERFORMANCE IN THE OUTDOOR PARKING LOT IN ANTICIPATION OF MAJOR EVENTS THIS YEAR.
