VERIZON FRONTLINE DEPLOYED IN STATEWIDE S.C. EMERGENCY RESPONSE EXERCISE. COLUMBIA, S.C., APRIL 14, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON FRONTLINE'S RESPONSE TEAM RECENTLY COMPLETED A MULTI-DAY DEPLOYMENT IN SUPPORT OF A STATEWIDE FLOODING RESPONSE EXERCISE HOSTED BY THE SOUTH CAROLINA EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT DEPARTMENT (SCEMD).
