3/2/23: VERIZON DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, MARCH 02, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT VERIZON COMMUNICATIONS INC. (NYSE, NASDAQ: VZ) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 65.25 CENTS PER OUTSTANDING SHARE, UNCHANGED FROM THE PREVIOUS TWO QUARTERS. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MAY 1, 2023, TO VERIZON SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 10, 2023.
