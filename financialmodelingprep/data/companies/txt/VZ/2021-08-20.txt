VERIZON FRONTLINE DEPLOYED TO SUPPORT EMERGENCY RESPONSE EFFORTS IN N.C.. HAYWOOD COUNTY, N.C., AUG. 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --   VERIZON FRONTLINE DEPLOYED ITS ADVANCED PUBLIC SAFETY PLATFORMS AND TECHNOLOGY WEDNESDAY, VIA THE VERIZON RESPONSE TEAM , TO SUPPORT FIRST RESPONDERS IN WESTERN NORTH CAROLINA CONDUCTING RESCUE OPERATIONS AND DEALING WITH EXTENSIVE FLOOD DAMAGE CAUSED BY TROPICAL STORM FRED.
