VERIZON ANNOUNCES $250,000 DONATION TO WOUNDED WARRIOR PROJECT. NEW YORK, NOV. 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  TO MARK THE CELEBRATION OF VETERAN'S DAY ACROSS THE U.S, VERIZON HAS ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL DONATE $250,000 TO THE WOUNDED WARRIOR PROJECT , A U.S. BASED CHARITY SUPPORTING A VARIETY OF VETERAN PROGRAMS AND SERVICES DESIGNED TO HELP AMERICAN VETERANS BUILD THE FUTURE THEY DESERVE.
