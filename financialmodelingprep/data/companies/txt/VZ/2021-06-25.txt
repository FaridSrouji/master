VERIZON FRONTLINE SUPPORTING FIRST RESPONDERS BATTLING COLORADO WILDFIRES. PUEBLO, COLO., JUNE 25, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON FRONTLINE RECENTLY DEPLOYED ITS ADVANCED PUBLIC SAFETY PLATFORMS AND TECHNOLOGY, VIA THE VERIZON RESPONSE TEAM (VRT) , TO SUPPORT FIRST RESPONDERS BATTLING WILDFIRES THROUGHOUT COLORADO.
LAXMAN NARASIMHAN ELECTED TO VERIZON BOARD OF DIRECTORS. BASKING RIDGE, N.J., JUNE 25, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON COMMUNICATIONS INC. (NYSE, NASDAQ: VZ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF LAXMAN NARASIMHAN, TO THE VERIZON BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2021. LAXMAN IS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF RECKITT BENCKISER GROUP PLC (‘RECKITT'), A FTSE 12 LISTED BRITISH MULTINATIONAL CONSUMER HEALTH, HYGIENE AND NUTRITION COMPANY. HE ALSO SERVES AS A DIRECTOR OF RECKITT.
