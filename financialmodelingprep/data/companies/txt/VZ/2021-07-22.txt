VERIZON FRONTLINE SUPPORTING WILDFIRE RESPONSE EFFORTS NATIONWIDE. VERIZON MEDIA BOLSTERS IDENTITY CONSENT MANAGEMENT, PARTNERS WITH NAI, OSANO. NEW YORK, JULY 22, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON MEDIA TODAY ANNOUNCED THE INTEGRATION OF VERIZON MEDIA CONNECTID WITH THE NETWORK ADVERTISING INITIATIVE (NAI) 'S “AUDIENCE MATCHED ADVERTISING OPT-OUT” PLATFORM. THE NAI IS A NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO RESPONSIBLE DATA COLLECTION AND USE FOR DIGITAL ADVERTISING. THE INTEGRATION, ENABLING CONSUMERS TO OPT-OUT FROM VERIZON MEDIA CONNECTID WITH JUST THEIR EMAIL ADDRESS, ADDS TO VERIZON MEDIA'S OWN PRIVACY AND PEOPLE-FIRST CONTROLS FOR TARGETED ADVERTISING.
