VERIZON TO EXPAND HIGH-SPEED BROADBAND TO RURAL HOUSEHOLDS IN BEDFORD COUNTY. NEW YORK, MARCH 23, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TO BRING HIGH SPEED INTERNET TO RURAL COMMUNITIES IN BEDFORD COUNTY, VIRGINIA, VERIZON WILL EXPAND ITS ALL-FIBER, HIGH-SPEED BROADBAND SERVICES TO APPROXIMATELY 349 ADDRESSES IN THE BIG ISLAND AND WHEATS VALLEY AREAS.
