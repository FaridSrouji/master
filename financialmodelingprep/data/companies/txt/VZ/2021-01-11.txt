VERIZON, UPS, AND SKYWARD ANNOUNCE CONNECTED DRONE DELIVERY AT CES 2021. PORTLAND, ORE., JAN. 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SKYWARD, A VERIZON COMPANY, AND UPS FLIGHT FORWARD TODAY ANNOUNCED COLLABORATIVE EFFORTS TO DELIVER RETAIL PRODUCTS WITH DRONES CONNECTED TO VERIZON 4G LTE, AS WELL AS 5G TESTING AND INTEGRATION FOR DELIVERY. THE COMPANIES AIM TO DELIVER RETAIL PRODUCTS VIA CONNECTED DRONES AT THE VILLAGES IN FLORIDA.
