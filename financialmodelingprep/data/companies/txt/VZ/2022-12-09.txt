VERIZON CONNECT FLEET EARNS CANADA'S ELD CERTIFICATION. NEW YORK, DEC. 09, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON BUSINESS TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE VERIZON CONNECT FLEET PLATFORM HAS OBTAINED CANADIAN ELECTRONIC LOGGING DEVICE (ELD) CERTIFICATION FROM A THIRD-PARTY ORGANIZATION. INTEGRATED WITH THE VERIZON CONNECT FLEET MANAGEMENT SYSTEM, THE ALL-IN-ONE ELD SOLUTION NOW PROVIDES CUSTOMERS WITH THE RELIABILITY AND FLEXIBILITY NEEDED TO SIMPLIFY COMPLIANCE WITH THE NEW HOURS OF SERVICE (HOS) REGULATIONS IN CANADA.
