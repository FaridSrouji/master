VERIZON FRONTLINE SUPPORTS FIRST RESPONDERS BATTLING NORTHERN MICHIGAN WILDFIRE. CHEBOYGAN COUNTY, MICH., MAY 17, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ON MAY 14, THE VERIZON FRONTLINE RESPONSE TEAM DEPLOYED IN SUPPORT OF FIRST RESPONDERS BATTLING THE BLUE LAKES FIRE, A BLAZE WHICH HAS BURNED MORE THAN 2,000 ACRES IN NORTHERN MICHIGAN.
