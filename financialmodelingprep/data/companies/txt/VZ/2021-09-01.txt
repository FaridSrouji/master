VERIZON FRONTLINE SUPPORTS HURRICANE IDA RESPONSE AND RECOVERY. HAMMOND, LA., SEPT. 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE VERIZON RESPONSE TEAM ARRIVED IN SOUTHEASTERN LOUISIANA MONDAY TO DELIVER VERIZON FRONTLINE TECHNOLOGY TO FIRST RESPONDERS CONDUCTING SEARCH AND RESCUE AND DISASTER RESPONSE OPERATIONS IN SOME OF THE AREAS HARDEST HIT BY HURRICANE IDA.
