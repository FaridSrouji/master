VERIZON REPORTS Q3 RESULTS; Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.25. VERIZON REPORTS STRONG 3Q RESULTS PROPELLED BY WIRELESS CUSTOMER GROWTH.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.25.Q3 ADJUSTED NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.25 EXCLUDING ITEMS.Q3 REVENUE $32.9 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $32.75 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.24 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.VERIZON - CAPITAL SPENDING FOR 2019 TO BE IN RANGE OF $17 BILLION TO $18 BILLION, INCLUDING EXPANDED COMMERCIAL LAUNCH OF 5G.VERIZON - 601,000 RETAIL WIRELESS POSTPAID NET ADDITIONS IN QUARTER.VERIZON SEES LOW SINGLE-DIGIT PERCENTAGE GROWTH IN FULL-YEAR CONSOLIDATED REVENUES ON A GAAP REPORTED BASIS.QTRLY WIRELESS TOTAL RETAIL POSTPAID CHURN OF 1.09 PERCENT.VERIZON - ARE FOCUSED ON 5G ROLLOUT STRATEGY, LOOKING TO DEPLOY NEXT-GENERATION NETWORKS WHILE ENHANCING 4G LTE NETWORK.VERIZON SEES LOW SINGLE-DIGIT PERCENTAGE GROWTH IN ADJUSTED 2019 EPS, EXCLUDING IMPACT OF LEASE ACCOUNTING STANDARD.VERIZON - IN THIRD-QUARTER 2019, VERIZON REALIZED OVER $400 MILLION OF EXPENSE SAVINGS FROM VOLUNTARY SEPARATION PROGRAM.FY2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $4.81, REVENUE VIEW $131.35 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.VERIZON - COMPANY'S REPORTED QTRLY EARNINGS INCLUDE A NET PRE-TAX GAIN OF $261 MILLION FROM DISPOSITIONS OF ASSETS AND BUSINESSES.VERIZON - COMPANY'S REPORTED QTRLY EARNINGS ALSO INCLUDE A PENSION RE-MEASUREMENT PRE-TAX CHARGE OF $291 MILLION.
