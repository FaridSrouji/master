CITIGROUP DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND; CITIGROUP DECLARES PREFERRED DIVIDENDS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CITIGROUP INC. TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON CITIGROUP'S COMMON STOCK OF $0.51 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 24, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 6, 2023.
