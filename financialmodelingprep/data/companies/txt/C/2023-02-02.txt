CITI APPOINTED AS DEPOSITARY BANK FOR QUANTASING GROUP LIMITED ADR PROGRAM. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CITI'S ISSUER SERVICES, ACTING THROUGH CITIBANK, N.A., HAS BEEN APPOINTED AS THE DEPOSITARY BANK BY QUANTASING GROUP LIMITED (“QUANTASING”) – A LEADING ONLINE LEARNING SERVICE PROVIDER IN CHINA'S ADULT LEARNING MARKET, TO ACT AS DEPOSITARY BANK FOR ITS AMERICAN DEPOSITARY RECEIPT (“ADR”) PROGRAM.
