DATADOG APPOINTS TITI COLE TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW YORK , JUNE 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DATADOG, INC. (NASDAQ: DDOG), THE MONITORING AND SECURITY PLATFORM FOR CLOUD APPLICATIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CITIGROUP'S (NYSE: C) TITI COLE TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. COLE IS CITIGROUP'S CEO OF LEGACY FRANCHISES, OVERSEEING THE BANK'S CONSUMER BUSINESS IN ASIA, EUROPE, MIDDLE EAST, AFRICA AND MEXICO.
