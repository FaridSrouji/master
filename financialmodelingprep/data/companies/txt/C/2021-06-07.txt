CITI APPOINTED AS DEPOSITARY BANK FOR CENTESSA PHARMACEUTICALS PLC'S ADR PROGRAMME. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITI, ACTING THROUGH CITIBANK N.A., HAS BEEN APPOINTED BY CENTESSA PHARMACEUTICALS PLC (“CENTESSA”), A NEXT-GENERATION BIOPHARMACEUTICAL COMPANY, TO ACT AS DEPOSITARY BANK FOR ITS AMERICAN DEPOSITARY RECEIPT (“ADR”) PROGRAMME. CENTESSA'S AMERICAN DEPOSITARY SHARES (“ADSS”) TRADE ON THE NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKET UNDER THE SYMBOL “CNTA”. EACH ADS REPRESENTS ONE ORDINARY SHARE OF THE COMPANY.
