CITI LAUNCHES NEW U.S. DEPOSIT SWEEP SOLUTION FOR INSTITUTIONAL CLIENTS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BUILDING ON ITS HISTORY OF INNOVATION IN CASH MANAGEMENT, CITI'S TREASURY AND TRADE SOLUTIONS TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A NEW SOLUTION TO ALLOW INSTITUTIONAL CLIENTS (CORPORATE, PUBLIC SECTOR, AND FINANCIAL INSTITUTION) WITH U.S. ACCOUNTS TO SWEEP CASH INTO DEMAND DEPOSIT ACCOUNTS AT PARTICIPATING U.S. BRANCHES OF NON-U.S. BANKS.
