CITIGROUP INVESTIGATION UPDATE BY FORMER LOUISIANA ATTORNEY GENERAL: KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC CONTINUES TO INVESTIGATE THE OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS OF CITIGROUP INC. - C. NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - DECEMBER 10, 2020) - FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA, CHARLES C. FOTI, JR., ESQ., A PARTNER AT THE LAW FIRM OF KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC ("KSF"), ANNOUNCES THAT KSF CONTINUES ITS INVESTIGATION INTO CITIGROUP INC. (NYSE: C).
