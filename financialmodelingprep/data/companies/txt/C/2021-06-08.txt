CITIBANK ANNOUNCES $1.75 BILLION REDEMPTION OF 3.400% NOTES DUE JULY 2021 AND $750 MILLION REDEMPTION OF FLOATING RATE NOTES DUE JULY 2021. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITIBANK, N.A. IS ANNOUNCING THE REDEMPTION, IN WHOLE, CONSTITUTING $1,750,000,000 IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT, OF ITS 3.400% NOTES DUE JULY 2021 (THE “FXD NOTES”) (ISIN: US17325FAQ19), AND THE REDEMPTION, IN WHOLE, CONSTITUTING $750,000,000 IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT, OF ITS FLOATING RATE NOTES DUE JULY 2021 (THE “FRN NOTES”, AND TOGETHER WITH THE FXD NOTES, THE “NOTES”) (ISIN: US17325FAR91). THE REDEMPTION DATE FOR THE NOTES IS JUNE 23, 2021. THE CASH REDEMPTIO
