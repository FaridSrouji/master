CITIGROUP DECLARES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND; CITIGROUP DECLARES PREFERRED DIVIDENDS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CITIGROUP INC. TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON CITIGROUP'S COMMON STOCK OF $0.51 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 23, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 7, 2022.
