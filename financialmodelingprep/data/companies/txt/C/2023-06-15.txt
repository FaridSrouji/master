CITI TTS SELECTS PISMO TO ENRICH GLOBAL DEMAND DEPOSIT ACCOUNT SOLUTIONS. NEW YORK & BRISTOL, ENGLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITI TREASURY AND TRADE SOLUTIONS (TTS) AND PISMO, THE NEXT-GEN BANKING AND PAYMENTS SOFTWARE COMPANY, JOINTLY ANNOUNCED THEIR RELATIONSHIP IN WHICH CITI WILL USE PISMO'S TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM TO HELP STRENGTHEN CITI'S CORPORATE DEMAND DEPOSIT ACCOUNTS (DDA), TO CLIENTS WORLDWIDE.
