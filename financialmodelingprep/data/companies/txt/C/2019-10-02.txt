CITI WINS MANDATE FROM VICTORY CAPITAL TO PROVIDE ADDITIONAL FUND ADMINISTRATION AND CUSTODY SERVICES. CITI HAS BEEN APPOINTED BY VICTORY CAPITAL TO PROVIDE FUND ADMINISTRATION, CUSTODY, ETF SERVICES AND SECURITIES LENDING FOR VICTORY'S MOST RECENT ACQUISITION. THE NEW MANDATE RESULTED IN THE TRANSFER OF SERVICING FOR 53 MUTUAL FUNDS AND ETFS TO CITI, REPRESENTING APPROXIMATELY $80 BILLION IN ASSETS UNDER CUSTODY.
