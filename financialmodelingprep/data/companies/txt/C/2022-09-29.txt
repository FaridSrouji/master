MACQUARIE RAISES OVER $C3 MILLION FOR CALGARY CHARITIES IN ANNUAL BREW-OFF EVENT. CALGARY, ALBERTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MACQUARIE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS ANNUAL CHARITY FUNDRAISER, THE BREW-OFF, RAISED MORE THAN $C1.7 MILLION FOR LOCAL CALGARY CHARITIES. THE EVENT, NOW IN ITS SIXTH YEAR AFTER TAKING A TWO-YEAR PAUSE DUE TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC, WAS HELD ON SEPT. 22 IN COLLABORATION WITH ENERGY INDUSTRY PARTNERS.
