CITI APPOINTED AS DEPOSITARY BANK FOR THE GDR PROGRAMS OF GEM CO. LTD., GOTION HIGH-TECH CO. LTD.. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITI'S ISSUER SERVICES, ACTING THROUGH CITIBANK, N.A., HAS BEEN APPOINTED BY GEM CO., LTD., GOTION HIGH-TECH CO., LTD., KEDA INDUSTRIAL GROUP CO., LTD. AND NINGBO SHANSHAN CO., LTD. - TO ACT AS THE DEPOSITARY BANK FOR THEIR GLOBAL DEPOSITARY RECEIPT (“GDR”) PROGRAMS. THESE ARE THE FIRST FOUR CHINA-SWISS STOCK CONNECT PROGRAMS LISTED ON SIX SWISS EXCHANGE.
