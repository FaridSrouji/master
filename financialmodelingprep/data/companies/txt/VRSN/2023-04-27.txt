VERISIGN REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS. RESTON, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VERISIGN, INC. (NASDAQ: VRSN), A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DOMAIN NAME REGISTRY SERVICES AND INTERNET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023. VERISIGN, INC. AND ITS SUBSIDIARIES (“VERISIGN”) REPORTED REVENUE OF $364 MILLION FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023, UP 5.1 PERCENT FROM THE SAME QUARTER IN 2022. OPERATING INCOME WAS $241 MILLION FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023, COMPARED TO $225 MILLION FOR THE SAME QUARTER OF 2022. VERISIGN REPORT.
