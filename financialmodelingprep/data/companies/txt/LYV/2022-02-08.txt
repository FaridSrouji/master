CHEF TIM LOVE ANNOUNCES TANNAHILL'S TAVERN & MUSIC HALL. FORT WORTH, TEXAS , FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHEF TIM LOVE ANNOUNCED TODAY TANNAHILL'S TAVERN & MUSIC HALL —A NEW LIVE MUSIC VENUE, RESTAURANT, AND PRIVATE EVENT SPACE—WILL OPEN IN 2022 IN THE HEART OF FORT WORTH'S STOCKYARDS IN THE HISTORIC HORSE AND MULE BARNS OF THE MULE ALLEY REDEVELOPMENT.  CHEF TIM LOVE ANNOUNCES TANNAHILL'S TAVERN & MUSIC HALL NAMED FOR LOVE'S SON, THE NEW VENTURE WILL FEATURE STATE-OF-THE-ART AUDIO AND LIGHTING CAPABILITIES AND ACCOMMODATE UP TO 1,000 CONCERTGOERS IN GENERAL ASSEMBLY AND THREE 20-PERSON VIP SUITE OPTIONS.
