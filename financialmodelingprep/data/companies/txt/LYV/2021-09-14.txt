LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT ANNOUNCES LAUNCH OF $450 MILLION COMMON STOCK OFFERING. LOS ANGELES, SEPT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT, INC. (NYSE: LYV) (THE "COMPANY") ANNOUNCED THAT IT INTENDS TO OFFER, SUBJECT TO MARKET AND OTHER CONDITIONS, $450 MILLION OF COMMON STOCK REGISTERED UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED.
