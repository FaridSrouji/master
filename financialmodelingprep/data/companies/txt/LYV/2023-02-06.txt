VAN MORRISON ANNOUNCES 2023 LAS VEGAS RUN AT ZAPPOS THEATER AT PLANET HOLLYWOOD RESORT & CASINO SEPTEMBER 6, 8 & 9, 2023. LAS VEGAS , FEB. 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEGENDARY GRAMMY AWARD-WINNER VAN MORRISON  HAS ANNOUNCED A THREE-NIGHT RUN OF SHOWS AT ZAPPOS THEATER AT PLANET HOLLYWOOD RESORT & CASINO SEPTEMBER 6, 8 AND 9, 2023. THE SHOWS ARE SCHEDULED TO BEGIN AT 8 P.M.
