LIVE NATION CLOSES ACQUISITION OF OCESA, THE THIRD LARGEST PROMOTER GLOBALLY AND LEADING LIVE ENTERTAINMENT COMPANY IN MEXICO, FURTHER GROWING BUSINESS IN LATIN AMERICA. LOS ANGELES AND MEXICO CITY, DEC. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT (NYSE: LYV), THE WORLD'S LEADING LIVE ENTERTAINMENT COMPANY, HAS CLOSED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF A 51% CONTROLLING INTEREST IN OCESA ENTRETENIMIENTO, A LEADING PROMOTER IN LATIN AMERICA. THE MOVE GROWS LIVE NATION'S BUSINESS IN LATIN AMERICA, EXPANDING ITS GLOBAL LIVE ENTERTAINMENT PLATFORM AS WELL AS ALLOWING THE COMPANY TO BETTER SERVICE ARTISTS WHO TRAVEL THE WORLD TO PLAY TO THEIR INCREASINGLY GLOBAL FAN BASE.
