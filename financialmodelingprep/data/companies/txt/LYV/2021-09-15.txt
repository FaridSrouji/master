LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $450 MILLION COMMON STOCK OFFERING. LOS ANGELES, SEPT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT, INC. (NYSE: LYV) ("WE" OR THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED OFFERING OF 5,239,259 SHARES OF COMMON STOCK, AT A PUBLIC OFFERING PRICE OF $86.90 PER SHARE.
