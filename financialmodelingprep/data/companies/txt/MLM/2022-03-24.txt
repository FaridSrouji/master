MARTIN MARIETTA TO SELL COLORADO AND CENTRAL TEXAS READY MIXED CONCRETE OPERATIONS TO SMYRNA. RALEIGH, N.C., MARCH 24, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARTIN MARIETTA MATERIALS, INC. (NYSE: MLM) (“MARTIN MARIETTA” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS COLORADO AND CENTRAL TEXAS READY MIXED CONCRETE OPERATIONS TO SMYRNA READY MIX CONCRETE LLC.
