MARTIN MARIETTA DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. RALEIGH, N.C., MAY 11, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARTIN MARIETTA MATERIALS, INC. (NYSE: MLM) (“MARTIN MARIETTA” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.66 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND, WHICH REPRESENTS A CASH DIVIDEND OF $2.64 PER SHARE ON AN ANNUALIZED BASIS, IS PAYABLE JUNE 30, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 1, 2023.
