MARTIN MARIETTA ANNOUNCES SECOND-QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL. RALEIGH, N.C., JULY 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARTIN MARIETTA MATERIALS, INC. (NYSE:MLM) (“MARTIN MARIETTA” OR THE “COMPANY”) WILL HOST ITS SECOND-QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL ON THURSDAY, JULY 29, 2021 AT 11:00 A.M. EASTERN TIME. THE COMPANY WILL RELEASE RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021, THAT MORNING BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS.
