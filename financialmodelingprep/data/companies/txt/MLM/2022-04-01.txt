MARTIN MARIETTA COMPLETES DIVESTITURE OF COLORADO AND CENTRAL TEXAS READY MIXED CONCRETE OPERATIONS. RALEIGH, N.C., APRIL 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARTIN MARIETTA MATERIALS, INC. (NYSE: MLM) (“MARTIN MARIETTA” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY-ANNOUNCED DIVESTITURE OF ITS COLORADO AND CENTRAL TEXAS READY MIXED CONCRETE OPERATIONS TO SMYRNA READY MIX CONCRETE LLC.
