MARTIN MARIETTA DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. RALEIGH, N.C., FEB. 18, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARTIN MARIETTA MATERIALS, INC. (NYSE: MLM) (“MARTIN MARIETTA” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.57 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND, WHICH REPRESENTS A CASH DIVIDEND OF $2.28 PER SHARE ON AN ANNUALIZED BASIS, IS PAYABLE MARCH 31, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 1, 2021.
