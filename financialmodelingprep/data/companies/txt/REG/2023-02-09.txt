REGENCY CENTERS REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2022 RESULTS. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., FEB. 09, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- REGENCY CENTERS CORPORATION (“REGENCY” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: REG) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2022. FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2022 AND 2021, NET INCOME WAS $0.56 PER DILUTED SHARE AND $0.39 PER DILUTED SHARE, RESPECTIVELY. FOR THE TWELVE MONTHS ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2022 AND 2021, NET INCOME WAS $2.81 PER DILUTED SHARE AND $2.12 PER DILUTED SHARE, RESPECTIVELY.
