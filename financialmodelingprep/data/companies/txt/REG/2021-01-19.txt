REGENCY CENTERS PROVIDES AN UPDATE ON FINANCING ACTIVITY, PROPERTY TRANSACTIONS, AND ITS INVESTMENT PIPELINE REVIEW. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., JAN. 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- REGENCY CENTERS CORPORATION (”REGENCY” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: REG) TODAY PROVIDED AN UPDATE ON THE REPAYMENT OF ITS $265 MILLION TERM LOAN DUE 2022, FOURTH QUARTER 2020 PROPERTY TRANSACTION ACTIVITY, AND CHARGES ASSOCIATED WITH CERTAIN PRE-DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS FOLLOWING ITS INVESTMENT PIPELINE REVIEW.
