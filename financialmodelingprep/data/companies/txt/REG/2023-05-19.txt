REGENCY CENTERS ANNOUNCES TRANSFORMATIVE SUNVET REDEVELOPMENT. LONG ISLAND, N.Y., MAY 19, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- IN A JOINT VENTURE WITH BLUMENFELD DEVELOPMENT GROUP, REGENCY CENTERS CORPORATION (“REGENCY” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY PLANS TO REDEVELOP THE SUNVET MALL IN LONG ISLAND, NY. THIS VACANT MALL WILL BE CONVERTED INTO A 168,000-SQUARE-FOOT, GROCERY-ANCHORED, OPEN-AIR SHOPPING CENTER. ONCE COMPLETE, IT WILL BE HOME TO THE FOURTH WHOLE FOODS MARKET IN SUFFOLK COUNTY AND EXPAND REGENCY CENTERS' FOOTPRINT IN THE MARKET TO 11 PROPERTIES, WHICH INCLUDES LAST YEAR'S FOUR-PROPERTY ACQUISITION FROM SEROTA PROPERTIES.
