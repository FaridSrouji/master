REGENCY CENTERS INVITES YOU TO JOIN ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., JUNE 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- REGENCY CENTERS CORPORATION (THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: REG) WILL ANNOUNCE ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS RESULTS ON THURSDAY, AUGUST 5, 2021, AFTER THE MARKET CLOSES. THE COMPANY'S EARNINGS RELEASE AND SUPPLEMENTAL INFORMATION PACKAGE WILL BE POSTED ON THE INVESTOR RELATIONS SECTION OF THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE – INVESTORS.REGENCYCENTERS.COM. THE COMPANY WILL HOST AN EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL ON FRIDAY, AUGUST 6, 2021, AT 11:00 A.M. ET.
