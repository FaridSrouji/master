REGENCY CENTERS REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., AUG. 04, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- REGENCY CENTERS CORPORATION (“REGENCY” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: REG) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED JUNE 30, 2022 AND PROVIDED UPDATED 2022 EARNINGS GUIDANCE. FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED JUNE 30, 2022 AND 2021, NET INCOME WAS $0.61 PER DILUTED SHARE AND $0.56 PER DILUTED SHARE, RESPECTIVELY.
