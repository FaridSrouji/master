REGENCY CENTERS RECEIVES GREEN LEASE LEADERS GOLD RECOGNITION FOR SUSTAINABILITY. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., MAY 18, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- REGENCY CENTERS CORPORATION (“REGENCY” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: REG) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT IT HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE 2022 GREEN LEASE LEADERS LIST WITH GOLD RECOGNITION, GRANTED BY THE INSTITUTE FOR MARKET TRANSFORMATION AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY'S BETTER BUILDINGS ALLIANCE. THIS AWARD SETS NATIONAL STANDARDS FOR A GREEN LEASE WHILE RECOGNIZING LANDLORDS AND TENANTS WHO MODERNIZE THEIR LEASES TO SPUR COLLABORATIVE ACTION ON ENERGY EFFICIENCY, COST SAVINGS, AIR QUALITY, AND SUSTAINABILITY IN BUILDINGS. THROUGHOUT THE YEARS, REGENCY CENTERS HAS MADE GREAT EFFORTS TO REDUCE ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACTS THROUGH HIGH-PERFORMING BUILDINGS AND STRONG PARTNERSHIPS WITH PARTICIPATING TENANTS.
