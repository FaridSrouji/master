COSTAR GROUP FOUNDER AND CEO ANDY FLORANCE NAMED AN INMAN 2023 POWER PLAYER. WASHINGTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ANDY FLORANCE, FOUNDER AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF COSTAR GROUP (NASDAQ: CSGP), A LEADING PROVIDER OF ONLINE REAL ESTATE MARKETPLACES, INFORMATION AND ANALYTICS IN THE COMMERCIAL AND RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY MARKETS, TODAY WAS HONORED AS ONE OF INMAN'S INAUGURAL 2023 POWER PLAYERS.
