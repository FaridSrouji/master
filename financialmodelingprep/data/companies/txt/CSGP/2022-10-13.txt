COSTAR GROUP ACQUIRES FAIRMAS BENCHMARKING, A LEADING HOSPITALITY BENCHMARKING PRODUCT IN GERMANY. WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COSTAR GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ: CSGP), A LEADING PROVIDER OF ONLINE REAL ESTATE MARKETPLACES, INFORMATION AND ANALYTICS IN THE COMMERCIAL AND RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY MARKETS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED FAIRMAS BENCHMARKING, A LEADING GERMAN HOSPITALITY BENCHMARKING PRODUCT, FROM FAIRMAS GMBH.
