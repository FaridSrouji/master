COSTAR GROUP ACQUIRES BUREAUXLOCAUX, A LEADING COMMERCIAL PROPERTY DIGITAL MARKETPLACE IN FRANCE. WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COSTAR GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ: CSGP), A LEADING PROVIDER OF COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE INFORMATION, ANALYTICS AND ONLINE MARKETPLACES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED COMREAL INFO, THE OWNER AND OPERATOR OF BUREAUXLOCAUX.COM. BASED IN PARIS, BUREAUXLOCAUX IS ONE OF THE LARGEST SPECIALIZED PROPERTY PORTALS FOR BUYING AND LEASING COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE IN FRANCE, WITH OVER 60,000 FOR SALE AND LEASE LISTINGS AND OVER 425,000 VISITS TO ITS WEBSITE EACH MONTH.
