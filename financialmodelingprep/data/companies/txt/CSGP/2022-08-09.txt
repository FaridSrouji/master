COSTAR GROUP PARTNERS WITH RICHMOND PUBLIC SCHOOLS TO PROVIDE SCHOOL SUPPLIES FOR 50,000+ STUDENTS. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COSTAR GROUP (NASDAQ: CSGP) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS PARTICIPATION AS THE LEAD SPONSOR IN THE FIFTH ANNUAL ULTIMATE BACKPACK DRIVE, AN INITIATIVE TO PROVIDE STUDENTS AT RICHMOND PUBLIC SCHOOLS WITH 50,000+ BACKPACKS FILLED WITH SCHOOL SUPPLIES, FREE OF CHARGE, TO ENSURE STUDENTS START THEIR SCHOOL YEAR ON THE RIGHT FOOT.
