COSTAR GROUP THIRD QUARTER 2022 REVENUE INCREASED 12% AND NET NEW BOOKINGS GREW 62% YEAR-OVER-YEAR. APARTMENTS.COM CLIMBS TO DOUBLE-DIGIT REVENUE GROWTH. WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COSTAR GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ: CSGP), A LEADING PROVIDER OF ONLINE REAL ESTATE MARKETPLACES, INFORMATION AND ANALYTICS IN THE PROPERTY MARKETS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT REVENUE FOR THE QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2022 WAS $557 MILLION, AN INCREASE OF 12% OVER REVENUE OF $499 MILLION FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021.
