COSTAR GROUP LAUNCHES THE NEXT GENERATION OF STR. STR BENCHMARKING REPRESENTS THE NEXT GENERATION OF STR, WITH THE COMPANY'S HOTEL DATA BENCHMARKING, ANALYTICS AND MARKETPLACE INSIGHTS INTEGRATED INTO COSTAR'S INDUSTRY-LEADING INFORMATION,.
