COSTAR GROUP NAMED ‘COMPANY OF THE YEAR' AT 2022 INMAN INNOVATOR AWARDS. WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COSTAR GROUP (NASDAQ: CSGP), A LEADING PROVIDER OF ONLINE REAL ESTATE MARKETPLACES, INFORMATION AND ANALYTICS IN THE COMMERCIAL AND RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY MARKETS, WAS AWARDED THE PRESTIGIOUS TITLE OF COMPANY OF THE YEAR AT THE 2022 INMAN INNOVATOR AWARDS. THE AWARD, WHICH WAS ANNOUNCED DURING INMAN'S SEMIANNUAL CONNECT EVENT, RECOGNIZES COSTAR GROUP'S COMMITMENT TO INNOVATION AND TRANSPARENCY ACROSS THE REAL ESTATE INDUSTRY.
