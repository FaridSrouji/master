COSTAR GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCES PUBLIC OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK IN CONJUNCTION WITH INCLUSION INTO THE S&P 500 INDEX. WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COSTAR GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ: CSGP) (“COSTAR”), A LEADING PROVIDER OF ONLINE REAL ESTATE MARKETPLACES, INFORMATION AND ANALYTICS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IN CONJUNCTION WITH THE SEPTEMBER 2, 2022 ANNOUNCEMENT BY STANDARD & POOR'S (“S&P”) TO INCLUDE COSTAR IN THE S&P 500 INDEX, COSTAR HAS COMMENCED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $750 MILLION OF ITS COMMON STOCK, REPRESENTING APPROXIMATELY 9.79 MILLION SHARES (BASED ON THE CLOSING PRICE ON SEPTEMBER 13, 2022 OF $76.64).
