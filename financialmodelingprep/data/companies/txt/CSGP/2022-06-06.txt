COSTAR GROUP'S MICHAEL DESMARAIS NAMED 2022 HUMAN RESOURCES PROFESSIONAL OF THE YEAR IN THE GLOBEE® AWARDS' COVETED ANNUAL GOLDEN BRIDGE AWARDS PROGRAM. WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COSTAR GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ: CSGP), A LEADING PROVIDER OF ONLINE REAL ESTATE MARKETPLACES, INFORMATION AND ANALYTICS IN THE COMMERCIAL AND RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY MARKETS, PROUDLY ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE GLOBEE® AWARDS, ORGANIZERS OF WORLD'S PREMIER BUSINESS AWARDS PROGRAMS AND BUSINESS RANKING LISTS, HAS NAMED COSTAR GROUP'S CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER MICHAEL DESMARAIS AS THE GOLD GLOBEE ® HUMAN RESOURCES PROFESSIONAL OF THE YEAR IN THE WORKPLACE SAFETY CATEGORY.
