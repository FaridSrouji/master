CERIDIAN PULSE OF TALENT REPORT: BURNOUT IMPACTING MAJORITY OF U.S. WORKFORCE. TORONTO AND MINNEAPOLIS, NOV. 30, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CERIDIAN (NYSE: CDAY; TSX: CDAY) TODAY RELEASED RESULTS FROM ITS ANNUAL PULSE OF TALENT REPORT, WHICH SHOWED THE MAJORITY OF AMERICAN WORKERS HAVE STRUGGLED WITH BURNOUT OVER THE COURSE OF THE PANDEMIC. MEANWHILE, ONE IN FOUR WORKERS ARE ACTIVELY SEEKING NEW JOBS.
CERIDIAN PULSE OF TALENT REPORT: BURNOUT IMPACTING MAJORITY OF WORKFORCE ACROSS UNITED KINGDOM AS JOB VACANCIES GROW. LONDON, NOV. 30, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CERIDIAN (NYSE: CDAY; TSX: CDAY), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM) TECHNOLOGY, TODAY RELEASED RESULTS FROM ITS ANNUAL PULSE OF TALENT REPORT, WHICH FOUND THE VAST MAJORITY OF WORKERS SURVEYED IN THE UK HAVE STRUGGLED WITH BURNOUT, WHILE 58% OF SURVEYED WORKERS ARE EITHER ACTIVELY SEEKING OR OPEN TO NEW EMPLOYMENT AS JOB VACANCIES RISE TO RECORD HIGHS.
