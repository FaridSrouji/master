CERIDIAN APPOINTS BILL MCDONALD TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL COUNSEL. TORONTO AND MINNEAPOLIS, JUNE 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CERIDIAN (NYSE: CDAY; TSX: CDAY), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS APPOINTED BILL MCDONALD AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL COUNSEL EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2021. IN THIS ROLE, BILL WILL CONTINUE TO SERVE AS CORPORATE SECRETARY AND ASSUME EXPANDED RESPONSIBILITY FOR LEADING THE LEGAL FUNCTION ACROSS THE BUSINESS GLOBALLY.
