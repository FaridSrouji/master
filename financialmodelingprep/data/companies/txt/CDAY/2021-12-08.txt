CERIDIAN PULSE OF TALENT REPORT: BURNOUT IMPACTING MAJORITY OF WORKFORCE IN AUSTRALIA AND NEW ZEALAND. MELBOURNE, AUSTRALIA, DEC. 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CERIDIAN (NYSE: CDAY; TSX: CDAY), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM) TECHNOLOGY, TODAY RELEASED RESULTS FROM ITS ANNUAL PULSE OF TALENT REPORT, WHICH FOUND THE VAST MAJORITY OF SURVEYED WORKERS IN AUSTRALIA AND NEW ZEALAND (ANZ) HAVE STRUGGLED WITH BURNOUT.
