MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL CLOSES PURCHASE OF INFINITY WORLD'S 50% INTEREST IN CITYCENTER AND COMPLETES SALE-LEASEBACK TRANSACTION OF CITYCENTER REAL ESTATE ASSETS WITH BLACKSTONE. LAS VEGAS, SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL ("MGM RESORTS" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: MGM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CLOSING OF THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED PURCHASE OF INFINITY WORLD DEVELOPMENT CORP'S ("INFINITY WORLD") 50 PERCENT INTEREST IN CITYCENTER HOLDINGS, LLC FOR $2.125 BILLION.
MGM RESORTS SELECTED BY OSAKA AS INTEGRATED RESORT PARTNER. LAS VEGAS, SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL (NYSE: MGM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MGM JAPAN, TOGETHER WITH ITS JOINT-VENTURE PARTNER ORIX, HAS BEEN SELECTED BY OSAKA AS THE REGION'S INTEGRATED RESORT PARTNER.
