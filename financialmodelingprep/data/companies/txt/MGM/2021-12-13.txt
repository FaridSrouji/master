MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO SELL OPERATIONS OF THE MIRAGE FOR $1.075 BILLION. LAS VEGAS, DEC. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL ("MGM RESORTS" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: MGM) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS REACHED AN AGREEMENT TO SELL THE OPERATIONS OF THE MIRAGE HOTEL & CASINO ("THE MIRAGE") TO HARD ROCK INTERNATIONAL ("HARD ROCK") FOR $1.075 BILLION IN CASH, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY WORKING CAPITAL ADJUSTMENTS. "THIS TRANSACTION IS A SIGNIFICANT MILESTONE FOR MGM RESORTS, AND FOR LAS VEGAS," SAID BILL HORNBUCKLE, CEO & PRESIDENT, MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL.
