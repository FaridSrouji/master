WAYNE GRETZKY AND CONNOR MCDAVID STAR IN NEWEST BETMGM TELEVISION SPOT. JERSEY CITY, N.J. , OCT. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BETMGM, A LEADING SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING OPERATOR, PREMIERED ITS NEWEST COMMERCIAL CAMPAIGN SPOT TODAY BRINGING TOGETHER TWO OF HOCKEY'S ICONIC CAPTAINS – WAYNE GRETZKY AND CONNOR MCDAVID.
