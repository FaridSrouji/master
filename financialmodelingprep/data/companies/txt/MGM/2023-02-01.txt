MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL HONORED AS A NATIONAL LEADER IN SUPPORTING WOMEN-OWNED BUSINESSES. COMPANY RECOGNIZED BY WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISE NATIONAL COUNCIL AS ONE OF AMERICA'S TOP CORPORATIONS FOR WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISES LAS VEGAS , FEB. 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL (NYSE: MGM) ("MGM RESORTS" OR THE "COMPANY") HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE 2023 AMERICA'S TOP CORPORATIONS FOR WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISES (WBES) BY THE WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISE NATIONAL COUNCIL (WBENC). MGM RESORTS IS ONE OF ONLY FOUR HONOREES IN THE TRAVEL & HOSPITALITY AND ARTS, ENTERTAINMENT & RECREATION CATEGORY AND THE ONLY GAMING COMPANY TO BE RECOGNIZED.
