BETMGM LAUNCHES MOBILE SPORTS BETTING IN PUERTO RICO. SPORTS BETTING LEADER EXPANDS MOBILE PLATFORM OUTSIDE OF U.S. MAINLAND COMPLEMENTING RETAIL SPORTSBOOK AT CASINO DEL MAR AT LA CONCHA RESORT SAN JUAN, PUERTO RICO , JUNE 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BETMGM, A LEADING SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING OPERATOR, ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE BETMGM MOBILE SPORTS BETTING APP IN PUERTO RICO IN PARTNERSHIP WITH CASINO DEL MAR AT LA CONCHA RESORT. PUERTO RICO IS THE SECOND MARKET OUTSIDE OF THE U.S. MAINLAND FOR BETMGM'S MOBILE SPORTS BETTING APP.
