AMERICAN HUMANE REUNITES HEROIC MILITARY K-9 WITH HANDLER IN LAS VEGAS. LAS VEGAS, MAY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RETIRED MILITARY WORKING DOG (MWD) POPEYE COMPLETED HIS GLOBE-SPANNING TOUR OF DUTY FRIDAY ON THE LAS VEGAS STRIP, WHERE HE WAS REUNITED WITH HIS FORMER HANDLER, U.S. ARMY VETERAN AND MGM RESORTS EMPLOYEE MICHAEL STEPONOVICH. AMERICAN HUMANE, THE COUNTRY'S FIRST NATIONAL HUMANE ORGANIZATION, HELPED BRING THESE BEST FRIENDS TOGETHER AFTER MONTHS SEPARATED BY THOUSANDS OF MILES.
