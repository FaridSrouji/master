BETMGM SIGNS FORMER NFL RUNNING BACK CHRIS JOHNSON AS BRAND AMBASSADOR. JERSEY CITY, N.J., NOV. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BETMGM, A LEADING SPORTS BETTING AND DIGITAL GAMING COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SIGNING OF FORMER TENNESSEE TITANS AND ARIZONA CARDINALS RUNNING BACK CHRIS JOHNSON AS ITS NEWEST BRAND AMBASSADOR.
