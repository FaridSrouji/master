BETMGM PARTNERING WITH NYRA BETS ON HORSE RACE WAGERING. JERSEY CITY, N.J., MAY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BETMGM ANNOUNCED TODAY A PARTNERSHIP WITH NYRA BETS, LLC (NYRA BETS), THE OFFICIAL ONLINE WAGERING PLATFORM OF THE NEW YORK RACING ASSOCIATION, INC. (NYRA) AND A LEADING PROVIDER OF HORSE RACING CONTENT IN THE UNITED STATES.
