VIRTUAL REGISTRATION OPEN TO HBCU STUDENTS NATIONWIDE IN FALL 2022 DALLAS , JUNE 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE TOM JOYNER FOUNDATION, A DRIVING FORCE BEHIND SCHOLARSHIP DISTRIBUTION AND FUNDRAISING FOR HISTORICALLY BLACK COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES (HBCUS), AND BETMGM, A LEADING SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING OPERATOR, ARE PARTNERING TO OFFER AN EXECUTIVE SERIES TO HBCUS NATIONWIDE BEGINNING THIS FALL. THE PARTNERSHIP WILL OFFER HBCU STUDENTS THE OPPORTUNITY TO NETWORK WITH BETMGM EXECUTIVES AND NAVIGATE THE RECRUITMENT PROCESS IN PREPARATION FOR CAREER OPPORTUNITIES AT THE COMPANY AFTER GRADUATION.
