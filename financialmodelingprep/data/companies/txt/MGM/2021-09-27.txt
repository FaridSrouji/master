MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL ANNOUNCES TRANSACTION TO ACQUIRE THE OPERATIONS OF THE COSMOPOLITAN OF LAS VEGAS. LAS VEGAS, SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL (THE "COMPANY" OR "MGM RESORTS") (NYSE: MGM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT WITH BLACKSTONE TO ACQUIRE THE OPERATIONS OF THE COSMOPOLITAN OF LAS VEGAS (THE "PROPERTY" OR "THE COSMOPOLITAN") FOR CASH CONSIDERATION OF $1.625 BILLION, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY WORKING CAPITAL ADJUSTMENTS.
