MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL AND BETMGM INTRODUCE "MGM RICHES," THEIR FIRST CUSTOM OMNICHANNEL SLOT EXPERIENCE IN U.S.. LAS VEGAS , FEB. 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL AND  BETMGM , A LEADING SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING OPERATOR, HAVE LAUNCHED MGM RICHES, THE COMPANIES' FIRST CUSTOM SLOT EXPERIENCE THAT CAN BE PLAYED BOTH ONLINE VIA BETMGM AND AS A TRADITIONAL SLOT GAME AT MGM RESORTS PROPERTIES THROUGHOUT THE U.S. DEVELOPED IN COLLABORATION WITH EVERI HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: EVRI), MGM RICHES IS A MULTI-DENOMINATION, MULTI-LEVEL PROGRESSIVE SLOT GAME OFFERING PLAYERS TWO THEMES – 'MGM RICHES TRIPLE RUBY' AND 'MGM RICHES 5X SAPPHIRE.' GAMEPLAY IS CONSISTENT WHETHER IN-PERSON AT 15 MGM RESORTS DESTINATIONS ACROSS THE COUNTRY OR ONLINE VIA BETMGM'S ICASINO PLATFORM IN MICHIGAN, NEW JERSEY AND PENNSYLVANIA.
