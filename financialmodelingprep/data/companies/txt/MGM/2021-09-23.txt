MGM RESORTS & BETMGM NAMED OFFICIAL PARTNERS OF AMERICAN GAMING ASSOCIATION'S HAVE A GAME PLAN® MISSION. LAS VEGAS, SEPT. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL AND BETMGM, THE JOINT VENTURE BETWEEN MGM RESORTS AND ENTAIN, ARE PROUD TO JOIN THE AMERICAN GAMING ASSOCIATION (AGA) AS OFFICIAL PARTNERS OF ITS HAVE A GAME PLAN ® MISSION.
