MGM GROWTH PROPERTIES, MGM RESORTS REDEEMED 30.3 MLN OF MGM RESORTS' OPERATING PARTNERSHIP UNITS FOR $700 MLN. MGM GROWTH PROPERTIES LLC -  CO, MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL HAS REDEEMED 30.3 MILLION OF MGM RESORTS' OPERATING PARTNERSHIP UNITS FOR $700 MILLION.MGM GROWTH PROPERTIES LLC - FOLLOWING REDEMPTION, MGM RESORTS HAS $700 MILLION REMAINING UNDER ITS AGREEMENT WITH MGP.MGM GROWTH PROPERTIES LLC - MGM RESORTS INTENDS TO USE $700 MILLION IN PROCEEDS TO REPAY AMOUNTS DRAWN UNDER ITS REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.MGM GROWTH PROPERTIES LLC - EXPECTS NEXT QTRLY DIVIDEND WILL BE INCREASED TO $1.95 PER SHARE ON ANNUALIZED BASIS, $0.05 INCREASE FROM ITS PRIOR RATE.
