BETMGM LAUNCHES MOBILE SPORTS BETTING IN LOUISIANA. JERSEY CITY, N.J.  , JAN. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BETMGM, A LEADING SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING OPERATOR, TODAY LAUNCHED ITS ONLINE SPORTS BETTING PLATFORM IN LOUISIANA, GIVING CUSTOMERS ACCESS TO A WIDE VARIETY OF BETTING OPTIONS AS WELL AS EXPERIENCES AT MGM RESORTS' BEAU RIVAGE RESORT & CASINO IN MISSISSIPPI.
