MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL RECOGNIZED AS A LEADER IN CREATING AN INCLUSIVE WORKPLACE. SERAMOUNT IS THE PREEMINENT ORGANIZATION FOR DIVERSITY THOUGHT LEADERS TO SHARE BEST PRACTICES AND DEVELOP INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS FOR CULTURAL CHANGE.
