MGM RESORTS ROLLS OUT COMPREHENSIVE HEALTH & SAFETY PROGRAM TO BRING MEETINGS BACK TO LAS VEGAS. LAS VEGAS, APRIL 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOLLOWING MONTHS OF TRIALS, MGM RESORTS IS ADDING CUTTING-EDGE ENHANCEMENTS TO ITS CONVENE WITH CONFIDENCE PROGRAM, ONE OF THE MEETING INDUSTRY'S MOST COMPREHENSIVE HEALTH AND SAFETY PLANS, DESIGNED TO GET BUSINESSES BACK TO DOING BUSINESS IN PERSON.  NOW WELCOMING LARGER GROUPS IN ACCORDANCE WITH INCREASED CAPACITY LIMITS IN NEVADA, MGM RESORTS IS OFFERING MEETING PLANNERS THE OPTION FOR ON-SITE RAPID, MOLECULAR COVID-19 TESTING DELIVERING HIGHLY ACCURATE RESULTS FOR EVENTS LARGE AND SMALL.
