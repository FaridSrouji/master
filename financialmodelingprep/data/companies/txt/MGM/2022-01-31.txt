BETMGM RETAIL SPORTSBOOK OFFICIALLY OPENS AT NATIONALS PARK IN WASHINGTON, D.C.. WASHINGTON, JAN. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BETMGM AND THE WASHINGTON NATIONALS ANNOUNCED TODAY THE OPENING OF THE FIRST RETAIL SPORTSBOOK CONNECTED TO A MAJOR LEAGUE BASEBALL STADIUM. THE BETMGM SPORTSBOOK AT NATIONALS PARK IS OPEN YEAR-ROUND, PROVIDING SPORTS FANS AN INNOVATIVE ENTERTAINMENT DESTINATION TO WATCH AND WAGER ON GAMES.
