GARY FRITZ APPOINTED PRESIDENT OF MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL INTERACTIVE. FRITZ WILL OVERSEE COMPANY'S GROWTH IN GLOBAL DIGITAL GAMING LAS VEGAS , OCT. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL (NYSE: MGM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE APPOINTMENT OF GARY FRITZ TO PRESIDENT OF MGM RESORTS INTERACTIVE. FRITZ WILL OVERSEE AND ADVANCE MGM RESORTS' DIVERSIFICATION STRATEGY BY EXPANDING THE COMPANY'S DIGITAL PLATFORMS ORGANICALLY AND THROUGH ACQUISITIONS.
