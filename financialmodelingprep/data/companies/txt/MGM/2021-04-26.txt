CITYCENTER ENTERS INTO AGREEMENT TO SELL TWO-ACRE PARCEL LAND IN LAS VEGAS. LAS VEGAS, APRIL 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CITYCENTER HOLDINGS, LLC ("CITYCENTER"), A VENTURE BETWEEN MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL (NYSE: MGM) AND INFINITY WORLD DEVELOPMENT CORP, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS TWO-ACRE SITE TO 63SLVB, LLC, OWNED BY LOCALLY-BASED LONG-TIME NATIONAL RETAIL DEVELOPERS BRETT TORINO OF TORINO DEVELOPMENT AND PAUL AND DAYSSI KANAVOS OF FLAG LUXURY, FOR APPROXIMATELY $80 MILLION. LOCATED IN THE HEART OF THE ICONIC LAS VEGAS STRIP, THIS UNDEVELOPED PARCEL OF LAND SITS WITHIN THE CITYCENTER CAMPUS, ADJACENT TO THE SHOPS AT CRYSTALS ON THE CORNER OF LAS VEGAS BOULEVARD AND HARMON AVENUE.
