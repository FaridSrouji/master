MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL SAYS CEO JAMES MURREN'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $13.1 MLN VS $12.8 MLN IN FY 2018 - SEC FILING. MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL SAYS CEO JAMES MURREN'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $13.1 MILLION VERSUS $12.8 MILLION IN FY 2018 - SEC FILING.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL SAYS FOR 2019, CEO PAY RATIO WAS 337:1.
MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL ADDRESSES FINANCIAL IMPACT OF COVID-19 AND STEPS TO MITIGATE. MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL ADDRESSES FINANCIAL IMPACT OF COVID-19 AND STEPS TO MITIGATE COVID-19 IMPACT.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - CONSOLIDATED NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO MGM RESORTS WAS APPROXIMATELY $1.3 BILLION FOR FIRST TWO MONTHS OF 2020.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - CONSOLIDATED NET REVENUES WERE DOWN 10% FOR FIRST 2 MONTHS OF YEAR COMPARED TO PRIOR YEAR.MGM RESORTS - UNDERTAKING THOROUGH REVIEW TO SIGNIFICANTLY MINIMIZE COSTS, SUCH AS IMPLEMENTATION OF HIRING FREEZES, FURLOUGHS, OTHER HEADCOUNT CUTS.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - ESTIMATES THAT 60-70% OF ITS DOMESTIC PROPERTY LEVEL OPERATING EXPENSES ARE VARIABLE.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - ACTIVELY REVIEWING ITS FIXED PROPERTY LEVEL OPERATING EXPENSES.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - EVALUATING ALL CAPITAL SPEND PROJECTS AND EXPECTS TO DEFER AT LEAST 33% OF PLANNED 2020 DOMESTIC CAPITAL EXPENDITURES.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - REVIEWING CORPORATE EXPENSES.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - SINCE MARCH 16, 2020, ALL OF COMPANY'S DOMESTIC PROPERTIES HAVE BEEN TEMPORARILY CLOSED TO PUBLIC.MGM RESORT - AS OF MAR 26 ,CO, EXCLUDING MGM CHINA AND MGM GROWTH PROPERTIES HAD OPERATING CASH AND CASH INVESTMENT BALANCES OF APPROXIMATELY $3.9 BILLION.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - WHILE MACAU PROPERTIES ARE NOW OPEN, VISITATION REMAINS AT LOW LEVELS AND TRAVEL CONSTRAINTS CONTINUE TO IMPACT MARKET.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - INCURRED SUBSTANTIAL OPERATING LOSSES IN MARCH.
