BETMGM PARTNERS WITH TAPPP TO RELEASE RETAIL GIFT CARDS ACROSS THE U.S.. NEW YORK, SEPT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BETMGM, A LEADING SPORTS BETTING AND DIGITAL GAMING OPERATOR, ANNOUNCED TODAY A PARTNERSHIP WITH TAPPP, A LEADER IN FLEXIBLE PAYMENT OPTIONS, TO MAKE BETMGM GIFT CARDS AVAILABLE AT MAJOR CONVENIENCE AND GROCERY RETAIL CHAINS IN SELECT U.S. STATES OFFERING LEGAL SPORTS BETTING.
