BETMGM ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT WITH SONY PICTURES TELEVISION, IGT TO LAUNCH ONLINE WHEEL OF FORTUNE CASINO. WHEEL OF FORTUNE CASINO TO DEBUT IN NEW JERSEY THIS YEAR JERSEY CITY, N.J. , JUNE 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BETMGM, A LEADING SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING OPERATOR, ANNOUNCED TODAY AN AGREEMENT WITH SONY PICTURES TELEVISION AND INTERNATIONAL GAME TECHNOLOGY PLC ("IGT") (NYSE: IGT) TO LAUNCH WHEEL OF FORTUNE CASINO, SET TO BECOME THE FIRST FULL BRAND-LED ONLINE CASINO IN NORTH AMERICA.
