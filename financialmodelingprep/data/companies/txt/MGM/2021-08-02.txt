BETMGM PARTNERS WITH CHEDDAR NEWS TO LAUNCH SPORTS BETTING BUSINESS SHOW. JERSEY CITY, N.J., AUG. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING SPORTS BETTING OPERATOR BETMGM IS PARTNERING WITH DIGITAL-FIRST NEWS NETWORK CHEDDAR NEWS TO LAUNCH CHEDDAR BETS, A WEEKLY PROGRAM OFFERING A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND EXPLORATION OF THE INTERSECTION OF SPORTS BETTING AND FINANCIAL MARKETS.
