MGM RESORTS FOUNDATION AWARDS NEARLY $2 MILLION IN GRANTS TO AREA NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS. LAS VEGAS , AUG. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE MGM RESORTS FOUNDATION (THE "FOUNDATION") HAS AWARDED NEARLY $2 MILLION IN THE FORM OF 82 GRANTS TO NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS LOCATED IN COMMUNITIES AROUND THE COUNTRY IN WHICH MGM RESORTS OPERATES, INCLUDING SOUTHERN NEVADA, MICHIGAN, MISSISSIPPI, NEW JERSEY, NEW YORK, NORTHEAST OHIO, THE DC METROPOLITAN AREA AND WESTERN MASSACHUSETTS. FUNDING WAS AWARDED TO ORGANIZATIONS THAT PROVIDE BASIC NEEDS SERVICES TO COMMUNITY RESIDENTS.
