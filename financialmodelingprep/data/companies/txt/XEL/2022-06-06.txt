XCEL ENERGY IS FIRST IN THE NATION TO ADD ALL-ELECTRIC BUCKET TRUCKS TO FLEET. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--XCEL ENERGY IS TAKING THE NEXT STEPS IN ITS JOURNEY TOWARD ELECTRIC TRANSPORTATION BY BECOMING THE FIRST ENERGY COMPANY IN THE NATION TO ADD ALL-ELECTRIC BUCKET TRUCKS TO ITS FLEET.
