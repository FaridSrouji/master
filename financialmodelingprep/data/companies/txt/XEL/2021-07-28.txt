XCEL ENERGY CO-CHAIRS, INVESTS IN FUND DESIGNED TO SUPPORT DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--XCEL ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT IS CO-CHAIRING AND INVESTING IN ENERGY IMPACT PARTNERS' ELEVATE FUTURE FUND (“ELEVATE”) AS A FOUNDING, LIMITED PARTNER. ENERGY IMPACT PARTNERS IS A GLOBAL INVESTMENT PLATFORM THAT SUPPORTS THE DEVELOPMENT OF NEW, CLEAN ENERGY TECHNOLOGIES, AND XCEL ENERGY IS A LONG-TIME INVESTOR IN THE COMPANY.
