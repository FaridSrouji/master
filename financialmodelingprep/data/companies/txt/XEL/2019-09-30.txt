XCEL ENERGY INC SAYS MPUC VOTED TO DENY NSP-MINNESOTA'S REQUEST TO PURCHASE MEC. XCEL ENERGY INC -  ON SEPT. 27, MPUC VOTED TO DENY NSP-MINNESOTA'S REQUEST TO PURCHASE MEC.XCEL ENERGY -MPUC DETERMINED THERE WAS UNCERTAINTY ON ESTIMATED CUSTOMER BENEFITS RELATED TO MEC DEAL WITHOUT REVIEWING NSP-MINNESOTA'S RESOURCE PLAN.XCEL ENERGY INC - ANNOUNCED INTENTION TO FORM A NEW SUBSIDIARY AND ACQUIRE EXISTING MEC ENTITIES AS A NON-RATE REGULATED INVESTMENT.XCEL ENERGY INC - TO ACQUIRE EXISTING MEC ENTITIES UNDER SAME PURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH SOUTHERN POWER COMPANY.XCEL ENERGY INC - UNIT WOULD ASSUME EXISTING PURCHASE POWER AGREEMENTS AND CONTINUE TO SELL POWER TO NSP-MINNESOTA THROUGH 2026 AND 2039.XCEL ENERGY INC - MPUC'S DECISION IS NOT EXPECTED TO HAVE A MATERIAL IMPACT ON 2019 RESULTS.XCEL ENERGY INC - REAFFIRMS 2019 GUIDANCE OF $2.55 TO $2.65 PER SHARE, EXPECTS TO BE AT OR ABOVE MIDPOINT OF GUIDANCE RANGE.
