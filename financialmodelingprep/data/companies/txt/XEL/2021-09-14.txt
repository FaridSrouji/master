STATEMENT FROM XCEL ENERGY EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN BEN FOWKE ON PRESIDENT BIDEN'S VISIT TO COLORADO. DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY XCEL ENERGY EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN BEN FOWKE MET WITH PRESIDENT JOE BIDEN DURING HIS VISIT TO THE NATIONAL RENEWABLE ENERGY LAB IN COLORADO. CONGRESSIONAL LEADERS AND COLORADO GOVERNOR JARED POLIS WERE ALSO ON HAND. FOWKE RELEASED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT AFTER THE EVENT: “WE THANK PRESIDENT BIDEN FOR VISITING THE NATIONAL RENEWABLE ENERGY LAB IN COLORADO TO SEE FIRST-HAND THE INNOVATIVE, COLLABORATIVE WORK BEING DONE TO DELIVER A CLEAN ENERGY FUTURE IN OUR STATE.
