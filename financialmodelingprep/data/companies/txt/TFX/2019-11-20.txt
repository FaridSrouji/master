TELEFLEX INCORPORATED ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF 5.25% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2024. ON NOVEMBER 15, 2019, TELEFLEX INCORPORATED REDEEMED $250,000,000 OUTSTANDING PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.25% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2024 USING BORROWINGS UNDER THE COMPANY’S REVOLVING CREDIT AGREEMENT AND CASH ON HAND. THE REDEEMED NOTES WERE REDEEMED AT A REDEMPTION PRICE EQUAL TO 102.625% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT THEREOF, PLUS ACCRUED AND UNPAID INTEREST TO, BUT NOT INCLUDING, THE REDEMPTION DATE.
