TELEFLEX ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. WAYNE, PA., OCT. 27, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TELEFLEX INCORPORATED (NYSE: TFX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF THIRTY-FOUR CENTS ($0.34) PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DECEMBER 15, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 15, 2022. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION ABOUT TELEFLEX CAN BE OBTAINED FROM THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE AT TELEFLEX.COM .
