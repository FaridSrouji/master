ACTIVE MOTIF INCORPORATED LICENSES TARGETED TRANSPOSITION TO DIAGENODE S.A. TECHNOLOGY. CARLSBAD, CALIF. , JULY 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ACTIVE MOTIF INCORPORATED, A COMPANY WITH THE VISION OF BRINGING EPIGENETICS MORE DEEPLY INTO PRECISION MEDICINE, ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS NON-EXCLUSIVELY LICENSED ITS FOUNDATIONAL PATENTS IN TRANSPOSITION FOR USE IN CUT&TAG EPIGENETIC ASSAYS TO DIAGENODE S.A.
