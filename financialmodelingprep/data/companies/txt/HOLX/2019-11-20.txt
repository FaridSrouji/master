HOLOGIC TO SELL CYNOSURE MEDICAL AESTHETICS BUSINESS TO CLAYTON, DUBILIER & RICE. PRESS RELEASE - HOLOGIC TO SELL CYNOSURE MEDICAL AESTHETICS BUSINESS TO CLAYTON, DUBILIER & RICE.HOLOGIC INC - DEAL FOR PURCHASE PRICE OF $205 MILLION IN CASH.HOLOGIC INC - UNDER TERMS OF AGREEMENT, AOUT 825 EMPLOYEES WILL TRANSFER WITH CYNOSURE BUSINESS.HOLOGIC INC - NET OF ADJUSTMENTS, EXPECTS NET CASH PROCEEDS OF ABOUT $138 MILLION.HOLOGIC INC - UNDER TERMS, ABOUT  825 EMPLOYEES WILL TRANSFER WITH CYNOSURE BUSINESS.HOLOGIC INC - INTENDS TO ENTER INTO AN ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM TO BUY BACK $205 MILLION OF CO'S COMMON STOCK.HOLOGIC INC - DEAL EXPECTED TO RESULT IN A SIGNIFICANT ADDITIONAL NON-CASH IMPAIRMENT CHARGE THAT WILL BE RECORDED IN Q4 GAAP RESULTS.HOLOGIC INC - IN FISCAL 2020, DIVESTITURE AND ASR TOGETHER ARE FORECAST TO INCREASE CO'S DILUTED EPS BY ABOUT $0.02 ON BOTH A GAAP AND NON-GAAP BASIS.HOLOGIC - EXPECTS TO RECORD ADDITIONAL PRE-TAX IMPAIRMENT CHARGE OF $155 MILLION TO $185 MILLION IN Q4 TO REDUCE CARRYING VALUE OF ASSET GROUP TO FAIR VALUE.HOLOGIC INC - LOSS FROM DIVESTITURE IS EXPECTED TO CONTRIBUTE TO A U.S. CASH TAX REFUND OF AT LEAST $300 MILLION TO BE RECEIVED DURING CALENDAR 2022.HOLOGIC INC CEO SAYS "SINCE WE ACQUIRED CYNOSURE IN 2017, IT HAS SIGNIFICANTLY UNDERPERFORMED OUR EXPECTATIONS".HOLOGIC INC - GOING FORWARD, DIVESTITURE IS EXPECTED TO HAVE IMMATERIAL IMPACT ON CO'S REVENUE GROWTH RATE.
