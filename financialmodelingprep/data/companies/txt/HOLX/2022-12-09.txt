STACEY D. STEWART ELECTED TO HOLOGIC BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MARLBOROUGH, MASS.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--HOLOGIC, INC. (NASDAQ: HOLX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT STACEY D. STEWART HAS BEEN ELECTED TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 2, 2023. MS. STEWART WAS ALSO APPOINTED TO THE AUDIT AND FINANCE COMMITTEE EFFECTIVE JANUARY 2, 2023.
