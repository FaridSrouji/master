LETITIA A. LONG APPOINTED TO T‑MOBILE US BOARD OF DIRECTORS. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--T-MOBILE US, INC. (NASDAQ: TMUS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT LETITIA A. “TISH” LONG HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JUNE 22, 2021. LONG HAS ALSO BEEN APPOINTED TO THE BOARD'S NOMINATING AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE COMMITTEE AND WILL SERVE AS THE COMPANY'S NATIONAL SECURITY DIRECTOR AS PART OF NATIONAL SECURITY COMMITMENTS CONNECTED TO ITS MERGER WITH SPRINT.
