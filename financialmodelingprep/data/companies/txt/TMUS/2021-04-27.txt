T-MOBILE AND ZYTER PARTNER TO DELIVER VIRTUAL HEALTHCARE ACCESS TO MORE AMERICANS. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, T-MOBILE (NASDAQ: TMUS) AND ZYTER, INC. ANNOUNCED A PREFERRED PARTNERSHIP TO DELIVER VIRTUAL HEALTHCARE SOLUTIONS — INCLUDING TELEHEALTH AND REMOTE PATIENT MONITORING AND CARE TEAM COLLABORATION — TO MORE HEALTHCARE ORGANIZATIONS AND THEIR PATIENTS ACROSS THE U.S. TOGETHER, THE COMPANIES ARE MAKING VIRTUAL CARE MORE ACCESSIBLE TO MORE PEOPLE, WHICH SERVES TO IMPROVE PATIENT OUTCOMES, REDUCE READMISSION RATES AND INCREASE OVERALL PATIENT WELLBEING.
