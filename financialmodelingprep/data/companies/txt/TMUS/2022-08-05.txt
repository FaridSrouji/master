T-MOBILE SECURES FIRST-EVER FULL INVESTMENT GRADE RATING. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--T-MOBILE US, INC. (NASDAQ: TMUS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FOLLOWING AN INVESTMENT GRADE ISSUER RATING FROM S&P GLOBAL RATINGS (S&P) – THE THIRD IT HAS RECEIVED FROM CREDIT RATING AGENCIES – THE COMPANY NOW HAS ITS FIRST-EVER FULL INVESTMENT GRADE RATING. S&P HAS ASSIGNED THE COMPANY A BBB- WITH POSITIVE OUTLOOK. THIS FOLLOWS THE COMPANY SECURING A BAA3 RATING WITH A STABLE OUTLOOK FROM MOODY'S AND A BBB- RATING WITH A POSITIVE OUTLOOK FROM FITCH.
