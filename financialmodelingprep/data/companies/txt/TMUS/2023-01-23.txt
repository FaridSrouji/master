LEADING CONSUMER RIGHTS LAW FIRM HAGENS BERMAN LAUNCHES INVESTIGATION INTO T-MOBILE DATA BREACH AFFECTING AT LEAST 37 MILLION USERS NATIONWIDE. BELLEVUE, WASH.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ATTORNEYS AT SEATTLE-BASED PLAINTIFFS LAW FIRM HAGENS BERMAN ARE INVESTIGATING T-MOBILE, FOLLOWING REPORTS OF A MASSIVE DATA BREACH IN NOVEMBER 2022. IF YOU BELIEVE YOU ARE AMONG THE 37 MILLION PEOPLE WHOSE SENSITIVE PERSONAL INFORMATION WAS COMPROMISED IN THIS INCIDENT, YOU CAN JOIN THE INVESTIGATION AND HELP HOLD T-MOBILE ACCOUNTABLE.
