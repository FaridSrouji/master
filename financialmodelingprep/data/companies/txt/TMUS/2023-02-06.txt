T-MOBILE AGREES TO SELL $3.0 BILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--T-MOBILE US, INC. (NASDAQ: TMUS) (“T-MOBILE”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT T-MOBILE USA, INC., ITS DIRECT WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY (“T-MOBILE USA” OR THE “ISSUER”), HAS AGREED TO SELL $1,000,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 4.950% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (THE “2028 NOTES”), $1,250,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.050% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2033 (THE “2033 NOTES”) AND $750,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.650% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2053 (THE “NEW 2053 NOTES,” AND COLLECTIVELY WITH THE 2028 NOTES AND THE 2033 NOTES, THE “NOTES”) IN A REGISTERED PUBLIC OFFERING. THE NEW 2053 NOTES WILL CONSTITUTE AN ADDITIONAL ISSUANCE OF T-MOBILE USA'S 5.650% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2053, OF WHICH $1,000,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT WAS ISSUED ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2022.
