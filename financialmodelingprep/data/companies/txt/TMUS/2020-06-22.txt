T-MOBILE ANNOUNCES PROPOSED PUBLIC SECURITIES OFFERINGS IN CONNECTION WITH SOFTBANK'S MONETIZATION OF A PORTION OF ITS SHAREHOLDING IN CO. T-MOBILE ANNOUNCES PROPOSED PUBLIC SECURITIES OFFERINGS IN CONNECTION WITH SOFTBANK’S MONETIZATION OF A PORTION OF ITS SHAREHOLDING IN T-MOBILE.T-MOBILE US INC - COMMENCEMENT OF A REGISTERED PUBLIC OFFERING OF 133.5 MILLION SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK.T-MOBILE US INC - ANNOUNCED ITS INTENTION TO SELL UP TO 30 MILLION SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK TO A DELAWARE STATUTORY TRUST.T-MOBILE US INC - ANNOUNCED INTENTION OF DELAWARE STATUTORY TRUST TO OFFER ITS CASH MANDATORY EXCHANGEABLE TRUST SECURITIES IN A PRIVATE OFFERING.T-MOBILE US - FOR EVERY SHARE SOLD BY T-MOBILE IN PUBLIC SECURITIES TRANSACTIONS, CO TO REPURCHASE ONE SHARE OF COMMON STOCK FROM UNIT OF SOFTBANK.T-MOBILE US INC - PUBLIC SECURITIES TRANSACTIONS WILL NOT INVOLVE GAIN OR LOSS TO T-MOBILE.T-MOBILE US INC - PUBLIC SECURITIES TRANSACTIONS WILL NOT AFFECT NUMBER OF OUTSTANDING SHARES OF CO'S COMMON STOCK.T-MOBILE US - ANNOUNCED EXPECTED DISTRIBUTION ON PRO RATA BASIS TO RECORD HOLDERS OF COMMON STOCK SUBSCRIPTION RIGHTS TO BUY 0.05 SHARES OF COMMON STOCK.T-MOBILE US - RIGHTS ARE EXPECTED TO BE LISTED, AND TRADING IS EXPECTED TO COMMENCE, ON NASDAQ GLOBAL SELECT MARKET EFFECTIVE JUNE 24.
