5G OPEN INNOVATION LAB, COMOTION AT THE UNIVERSITY OF WASHINGTON, AND T-MOBILE COLLABORATE TO ACCELERATE 5G HARDWARE INNOVATION. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--5G OPEN INNOVATION LAB (5G OI LAB), COMOTION AT THE UNIVERSITY OF WASHINGTON (UW), AND T-MOBILE TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION THAT WILL ENABLE HARDWARE STARTUPS TO DEVELOP, TEST AND ROLL OUT NEW PRODUCTS, SERVICES, AND PROTOTYPES POWERED BY T-MOBILE 5G. WITH A NEW ULTRA CAPACITY 5G NETWORK DEPLOYED AT COMOTION LABS ON THE UW CAMPUS, THE FACILITY IS NOW THE FIRST 5G-EQUIPPED INCUBATOR IN THE REGION FOCUSED ON SUPPORTING THE GROWTH OF THE HARDWARE STARTUP ECOSYSTEM.
