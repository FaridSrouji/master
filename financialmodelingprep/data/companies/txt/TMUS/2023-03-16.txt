25 NEW TOWNS SELECTED TO RECEIVE T-MOBILE HOMETOWN GRANTS FOR LOCAL INITIATIVES. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--T-MOBILE (NASDAQ: TMUS): WHAT'S THE NEWS: T-MOBILE ANNOUNCED ITS LATEST 25 HOMETOWN GRANT WINNERS, BRINGING THE TOTAL TO 175 SMALL TOWNS SINCE THE UN-CARRIER LAUNCHED THE INITIATIVE IN APRIL 2021 TO SUPPORT RURAL COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
