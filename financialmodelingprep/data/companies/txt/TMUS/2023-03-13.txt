T-MOBILE ANNOUNCES CONSENT SOLICITATIONS WITH RESPECT TO CERTAIN SERIES OF NOTES ISSUED BY ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARIES SPRINT LLC AND SPRINT CAPITAL CORPORATION. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--T-MOBILE US, INC. (NASDAQ: TMUS) (“T-MOBILE”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARIES SPRINT LLC (“SPRINT”) AND SPRINT CAPITAL CORPORATION (“SCC”) HAVE EACH COMMENCED A CONSENT SOLICITATION (THE “SPRINT CONSENT SOLICITATION” AND THE “SCC CONSENT SOLICITATION”, RESPECTIVELY, AND TOGETHER, THE “CONSENT SOLICITATIONS”).
