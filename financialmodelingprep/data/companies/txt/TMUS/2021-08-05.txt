T-MOBILE ENHANCES PARTNERSHIPS WITH LULAC AND UNIDOSUS TO BRING DIGITAL LITERACY RESOURCES TO HISPANIC COMMUNITIES. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY T-MOBILE ANNOUNCED DONATIONS TO TWO OF THE COUNTRY'S LEADING HISPANIC ADVOCACY ORGANIZATIONS, THE LEAGUE OF UNITED LATIN AMERICAN CITIZENS (LULAC) AND UNIDOSUS (FORMERLY NATIONAL COUNCIL OF LA RAZA) TO FUND TWO PROGRAMS FOCUSED ON PROVIDING DIGITAL LITERACY RESOURCES TO HISPANIC COMMUNITIES ACROSS AMERICA. LULAC'S CONEXIONES AND UNIDOSUS'S LATINX IN BUSINESS WILL OFFER WORKSHOPS AND EDUCATIONAL RESOURCES ON HOW TO ENGAGE AND THRIVE IN THE DIGITAL ECONOMY
