INTEL AND PENN MEDICINE ANNOUNCE RESULTS OF LARGEST MEDICAL FEDERATED LEARNING STUDY. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WHAT'S NEW: INTEL LABS AND THE PERELMAN SCHOOL OF MEDICINE AT THE UNIVERSITY OF PENNSYLVANIA (PENN MEDICINE) HAVE COMPLETED A JOINT RESEARCH STUDY USING FEDERATED LEARNING – A DISTRIBUTED MACHINE LEARNING (ML) ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) APPROACH – TO HELP INTERNATIONAL HEALTHCARE AND RESEARCH INSTITUTIONS IDENTIFY MALIGNANT BRAIN TUMORS. THE LARGEST MEDICAL FEDERATED LEARNING STUDY TO DATE WITH AN UNPRECEDENTED GLOBAL DATASET EXAMINED FROM 71 INSTITUTIONS ACROSS SIX CONTINENTS, THE PROJECT DEMONSTRATED THE ABILITY TO IMPROVE BRAIN TUMOR DETECTION BY 33%.
