CRYSTAL GROUP ADDS SPEED, DENSITY AND MEMORY TO COMPUTE SOLUTIONS WITH LATEST GENERATION INTEL XEON® SCALABLE PROCESSOR. HIAWATHA, IOWA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CRYSTAL GROUP, INC., THE MOST TRUSTED DESIGNER AND MANUFACTURER OF RUGGED HIGH-PERFORMANCE EDGE COMPUTE SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE INTEGRATION OF THE 4TH GENERATION INTEL® XEON® SCALABLE PROCESSOR (CODE NAMED SAPPHIRE RAPIDS) INTO THEIR PORTFOLIO OF RUGGED HARDWARE.
