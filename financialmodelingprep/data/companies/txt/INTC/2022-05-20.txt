INTEL RECOGNIZES IRON BOW TECHNOLOGIES AS PUBLIC SECTOR SOLUTION OF THE YEAR DURING 2022 NORTH AMERICA CHANNEL PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARDS. HERNDON, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IRON BOW TECHNOLOGIES WAS RECOGNIZED BY INTEL AT THE 2022 NORTH AMERICAN CHANNEL PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARDS FOR PUBLIC SECTOR SOLUTION OF THE YEAR.
