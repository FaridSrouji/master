INTEL AND BCG ANNOUNCE COLLABORATION TO DELIVER ENTERPRISE-GRADE, SECURE GENERATIVE AI. PIONEERING SOLUTION POWERED BY INTEL AI SUPERCOMPUTER UNLOCKS BUSINESS VALUE WITH CUSTOM DATASETS WHILE MAINTAINING HIGH LEVELS OF SECURITY AND DATA PRIVACY BOSTON , MAY 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON CONSULTING GROUP (BCG) AND INTEL TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC COLLABORATION TO ENABLE GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (GENAI) USING END-TO-END INTEL AI HARDWARE AND SOFTWARE, BRINGING FULLY CUSTOM AND PROPRIETARY SOLUTIONS TO ENTERPRISE CLIENTS WHILE KEEPING PRIVATE DATA IN THE ISOLATION OF THEIR TRUSTED ENVIRONMENTS. "GENERATIVE AI IS AN EMERGING AND DYNAMIC SPACE, WHICH MEANS ORGANIZATIONS MUST PICK THE RIGHT TECHNOLOGY TO POWER THEIR GENAI JOURNEY," SAYS SUCHI SRINIVASAN, A MANAGING DIRECTOR AND PARTNER AT BCG.
