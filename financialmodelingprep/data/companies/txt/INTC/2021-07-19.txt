INTEL ELECTS ANDREA GOLDSMITH TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ANDREA GOLDSMITH, DEAN OF ENGINEERING AND APPLIED SCIENCE AT PRINCETON UNIVERSITY, WAS ELECTED TO INTEL CORPORATION'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
