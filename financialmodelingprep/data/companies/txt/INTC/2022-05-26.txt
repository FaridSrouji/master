INTEL SELECTS DIVERSE OHIO-BASED TEAM TO LEAD EARLY EXCAVATION WORK FOR OHIO FABS. MCDANIEL'S CONSTRUCTION CORP., NORTHSTAR CONTRACTING INC. AND GTSA CONSTRUCTION CONSULTING, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH GILBANE BUILDING COMPANY, REPRESENT FIRST OF PROJECTED 7,000 CONSTRUCTION JOBS. COLUMBUS, OHIO, MAY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  WHAT'S NEW: INTEL CORPORATION HAS SELECTED A DIVERSE OHIO-BASED TEAM LED BY GILBANE BUILDING COMPANY, WITH PARTNERS MCDANIEL'S CONSTRUCTION CORP., NORTHSTAR CONTRACTING INC., AND GTSA CONSTRUCTION CONSULTING, TO MANAGE THE EARLY EXCAVATION WORK FOR ITS TWO NEW LEADING-EDGE CHIP FACTORIES IN OHIO.
