INTEL CORP SUSPENDS STOCK REPURCHASES. INTEL CORP - SUSPENDING STOCK REPURCHASES.INTEL CORP - TO DATE, INTEL HAS KEPT ITS FACTORIES OPERATIONAL.INTEL CORP - SUSPENSION OF STOCK REPURCHASES WILL NOT IMPACT DIVIDEND PAYMENTS TO STOCKHOLDERS.INTEL, UNDER RISK FACTORS, SAYS RECENT NOVEL CORONAVIRUS OUTBREAK COULD MATERIALLY ADVERSELY AFFECT FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS.
