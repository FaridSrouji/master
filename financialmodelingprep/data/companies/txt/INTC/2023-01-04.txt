MOBILEYE AND WNC COLLABORATE ON IMAGING RADAR PRODUCTION. LAS VEGAS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--OVER THE PAST FEW YEARS, MOBILEYE HAS BEEN DEVELOPING A NEW TECHNOLOGY TO HELP AUTONOMOUS VEHICLES SENSE AND UNDERSTAND THEIR ENVIRONMENT – REGARDLESS OF WEATHER, LIGHTING OR ROAD TYPES – IN ADDITION TO THE COMPANY'S RENOWNED CAMERA-BASED PERCEPTION SYSTEMS. KNOWN AS SOFTWARE-DEFINED IMAGING RADAR, OR 4D RADAR, THE TECHNOLOGY WILL PLAY A KEY ROLE IN BRINGING AUTONOMOUS VEHICLES AND THE MOST ADVANCED FORMS OF DRIVER-ASSISTANCE TECHNOLOGY TO LIFE.
