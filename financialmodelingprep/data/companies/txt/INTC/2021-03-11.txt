INTEL DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTEL CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.3475 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK.
