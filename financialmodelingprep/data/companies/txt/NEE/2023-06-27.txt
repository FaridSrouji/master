NEE ALERT: THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF JULY 25, 2023 IN THE CLASS ACTION FILED ON BEHALF OF NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. SHAREHOLDERS. NEW YORK , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION COMPLAINT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NEE) ALLEGING THAT THE COMPANY VIOLATED FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS. CLASS PERIOD: DECEMBER 2, 2021 TO FEBRUARY 1, 2023 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE: JULY 25, 2023 NO OBLIGATION OR COST TO YOU.
