NEXTERA ENERGY AND NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS TO MEET WITH INVESTORS THROUGHOUT FEBRUARY. JUNO BEACH, FLA., FEB. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NEE) AND NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS, LP (NYSE: NEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MEMBERS OF THE SENIOR MANAGEMENT TEAM WILL PARTICIPATE IN VARIOUS INVESTOR MEETINGS THROUGHOUT FEBRUARY.
