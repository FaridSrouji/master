NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS, LP ANNOUNCES OFFERING OF $500 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2026. JUNO BEACH, FLA. , DEC. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS, LP (NYSE: NEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN OFFERING OF $500 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2026 (THE "NOTES") IN A PRIVATE PLACEMENT TO QUALIFIED INSTITUTIONAL BUYERS PURSUANT TO RULE 144A UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED (THE "SECURITIES ACT").
