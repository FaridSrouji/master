NEXTERA ENERGY AND NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS TO MEET WITH INVESTORS THROUGHOUT MARCH AND EARLY APRIL. JUNO BEACH, FLA., MARCH 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NEE) AND NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS, LP (NYSE: NEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MEMBERS OF THE SENIOR MANAGEMENT TEAM WILL PARTICIPATE IN VARIOUS INVESTOR MEETINGS THROUGHOUT MARCH AND EARLY APRIL.
