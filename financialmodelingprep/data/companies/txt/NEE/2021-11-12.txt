NEXTERA ENERGY TRANSMISSION SUBMITS OFFSHORE WIND TRANSMISSION PROPOSALS TO SUPPORT NEW YORK'S CLEAN ENERGY GOALS. JUNO BEACH, FLA., NOV. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEXTERA ENERGY TRANSMISSION NEW YORK, INC, A SUBSIDIARY OF NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NEE), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SUBMITTED MULTIPLE PROPOSALS, COLLECTIVELY THE NEW YORK RENEWABLE CONNECT, IN RESPONSE TO THE NEW YORK INDEPENDENT SYSTEM OPERATOR'S (NYISO) LONG ISLAND OFFSHORE WIND PUBLIC POLICY TRANSMISSION NEED.
