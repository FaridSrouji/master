FPL PROPOSES PLAN TO REFUND CUSTOMERS NEARLY $400 MILLION IN FEDERAL CORPORATE TAX SAVINGS. THE HUNDREDS OF MILLIONS OF DOLLARS IN SAVINGS ARE THE RESULT OF FEDERAL PRODUCTION TAX CREDITS ON THE LARGE-SCALE SOLAR ENERGY CENTERS FPL IS BUILDING THROUGHOUT FLORIDA. A ONE-TIME, $25 MILLION REFUND WILL TAKE EFFECT IN THE MONTH OF JANUARY 2023, WHILE ADDITIONAL SAVINGS WILL BE PHASED IN FROM 2023 THROUGH 2025.
