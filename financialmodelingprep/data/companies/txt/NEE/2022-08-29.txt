NEXTERA ENERGY AND NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS TO MEET WITH INVESTORS THROUGHOUT THE END OF AUGUST AND THE MONTH OF SEPTEMBER. JUNO BEACH, FLA. , AUG. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NEE) AND NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS, LP (NYSE: NEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MEMBERS OF THE SENIOR MANAGEMENT TEAM WILL PARTICIPATE IN VARIOUS INVESTOR MEETINGS THROUGHOUT THE END OF AUGUST AND THE MONTH OF SEPTEMBER.
