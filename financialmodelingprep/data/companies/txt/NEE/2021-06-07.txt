FPL'S '30-BY-30' PLAN REACHES KEY MILESTONE WITH MORE THAN 12 MILLION SOLAR PANELS GENERATING ELECTRICITY IN THE STATE OF FLORIDA. JUNO BEACH, FLA., JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FLORIDA POWER & LIGHT COMPANY (FPL) HAS ACHIEVED A MAJOR MILESTONE BY SURPASSING 40% COMPLETION OF ITS GROUNDBREAKING "30-BY-30" PLAN TO INSTALL 30 MILLION SOLAR PANELS BY 2030, WHICH IS ONE OF THE LARGEST SOLAR EXPANSIONS IN THE UNITED STATES.
