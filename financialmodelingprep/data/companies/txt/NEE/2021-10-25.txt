AMERICAN INDIAN GRADUATE CENTER UNVEILS NEXTERA ENERGY SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM. ALBUQUERQUE, N.M., OCT. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NEE) TODAY ANNOUNCED AMERICAN INDIAN GRADUATE CENTER HAS LAUNCHED THE NEXTERA ENERGY SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM, A CULTURALLY RELEVANT AND INDIGENOUS-CENTERED SCHOLARSHIP SPECIFICALLY BUILT FOR NATIVE STUDENTS PURSUING CAREERS IN THE FIELD OF ENERGY AND ACROSS SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, ENGINEERING AND MATHEMATICS (STEM) AS A WHOLE.
