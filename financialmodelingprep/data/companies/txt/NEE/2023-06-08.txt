INVESTOR ACTION NOTICE: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. ("NEXTERA" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE:NEE) FOR VIOLATIONS OF 10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION.
