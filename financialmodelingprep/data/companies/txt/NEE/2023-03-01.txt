FPL FILES PLAN TO REDUCE CUSTOMER FUEL CHARGES BY $379 MILLION. JUNO BEACH, FLA. , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FLORIDA POWER & LIGHT COMPANY TODAY FILED A PLAN TO REDUCE CUSTOMER FUEL CHARGES BY $379 MILLION BEGINNING IN MAY TO PARTIALLY OFFSET PROPOSED BILL INCREASES TO PAY FOR LAST YEAR'S TWO HURRICANE RESTORATIONS AND HIGH FUEL COSTS.
