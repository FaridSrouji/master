JOHN KETCHUM APPOINTED CHAIRMAN OF NEXTERA ENERGY. JUNO BEACH, FLA., MAY 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NEE) APPOINTED PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER JOHN KETCHUM AS CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, EFFECTIVE JULY 29, 2022, AT WHICH TIME HE WILL SERVE AS NEXTERA ENERGY'S CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
