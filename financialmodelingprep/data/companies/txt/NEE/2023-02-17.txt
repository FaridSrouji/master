NEXTERA ENERGY BOARD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. JUNO BEACH, FLA. , FEB. 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NEXTERA ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NEE) DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.4675 PER SHARE, AN APPROXIMATE 10% INCREASE VERSUS THE PRIOR-YEAR COMPARABLE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
