NEXTERA ENERGY RESOURCES AND COFFEE COUNTY, ALABAMA TO BUILD FIRST RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS PROJECT IN THE STATE. JUNO BEACH, FLA. , NOV. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEXTERA ENERGY RESOURCES, LLC, AND COFFEE COUNTY, ALABAMA ANNOUNCED PLANS TO BUILD THE FIRST LANDFILL RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS (RNG) PRODUCTION FACILITY IN THE STATE OF ALABAMA.
