AVERY DENNISON NAMES HASSAN RMAILE AND FRANCISCO MELO AS PRESIDENTS FOR ITS TWO BUSINESS SEGMENTS. MENTOR, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #AVY--AVERY DENNISON CORPORATION (NYSE: AVY) HAS APPOINTED LEADERS FOR ITS TWO BUSINESS SEGMENTS. HASSAN RMAILE HAS BEEN NAMED PRESIDENT OF THE MATERIALS GROUP. FRANCISCO MELO HAS BEEN NAMED PRESIDENT OF THE SOLUTIONS GROUP, A ROLE THAT HAS BEEN OPEN SINCE DEON STANDER'S APPOINTMENT TO PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER IN MARCH OF 2022. AS EARLIER ANNOUNCED, THE COMPANY NOW CONSISTS OF TWO REPORTABLE SEGMENTS.
