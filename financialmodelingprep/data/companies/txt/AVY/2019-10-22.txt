AVERY DENNISON CORPORATION REVISES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019. AVERY DENNISON CORPORATION REVISED EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019. FOR THE PERIOD, THE COMPANY HAS REVISED ITS GUIDANCE RANGE FOR 2019 REPORTED EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM A RANGE OF $3.15 TO $3.30 TO A RANGE OF $3.15 TO $3.25.
