FRANCESCA REVERBERI APPOINTED TO AVERY DENNISON BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MENTOR, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AVERY DENNISON CORPORATION (NYSE: AVY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED FRANCESCA REVERBERI AS A DIRECTOR, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 23, 2023. REVERBERI, 51, IS CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER AND SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, SUSTAINABLE PLASTICS, AT TRINSEO PLC, A SPECIALTY MATERIALS SOLUTIONS PROVIDER AND MANUFACTURER IN THE UNITED STATES, EUROPE AND ASIA PACIFIC WITH APPROXIMATELY $5 BILLION IN 2022 REVENUES.
