AVERY DENNISON ANNOUNCES UPCOMING INVESTOR EVENT. MENTOR, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AVERY DENNISON CORPORATION (NYSE: AVY) ANNOUNCED THAT MITCH BUTIER, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A FIRESIDE CHAT DURING THE BOFA SECURITIES GLOBAL AGRICULTURE AND MATERIALS CONFERENCE ON THURSDAY, MARCH 2, AT 10:50 AM ET. THIS EVENT WILL BE WEBCAST LIVE, AND A REPLAY WILL BE AVAILABLE ON AVERY DENNISON'S INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE (WWW.INVESTORS.AVERYDENNISON.COM).
