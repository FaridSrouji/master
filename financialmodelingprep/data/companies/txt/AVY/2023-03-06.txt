AVERY DENNISON PRICES $400 MILLION SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. MENTOR, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AVERY DENNISON CORPORATION (NYSE: AVY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF $400 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 5.750% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2033. THE SENIOR NOTES WERE PRICED AT 99.557% OF THEIR PRINCIPAL AMOUNT. THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON MARCH 15, 2023, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
