FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST ACQUIRES TWINBROOKE SHOPPING CENTRE IN FAIRFAX, VIRGINIA. N. BETHESDA, MD., SEPT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST (NYSE:FRT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF A 100% INTEREST IN TWINBROOKE SHOPPING CENTRE, A 110,000 SQUARE FOOT GROCERY ANCHORED NEIGHBORHOOD SHOPPING CENTER ON 10 ACRES LOCATED IN FAIRFAX, VIRGINIA.
