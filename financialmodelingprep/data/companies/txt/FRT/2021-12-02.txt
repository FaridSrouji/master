FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST ANNOUNCES HOLDING COMPANY REORGANIZATION. NORTH BETHESDA, MD., DEC. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST (NYSE: FRT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT INTENDS TO COMPLETE A HOLDING COMPANY REORGANIZATION ("REORGANIZATION") THAT WOULD STRUCTURE THE COMPANY AS AN UMBRELLA PARTNERSHIP REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST, OR UPREIT.
