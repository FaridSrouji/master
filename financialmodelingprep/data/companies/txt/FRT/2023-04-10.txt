FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST ANNOUNCES PRICING OF GREEN BONDS. NORTH BETHESDA, MD. , APRIL 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST (NYSE: FRT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS OPERATING PARTNERSHIP, FEDERAL REALTY OP LP (THE "OPERATING PARTNERSHIP"), HAS PRICED ITS PUBLIC OFFERING OF $350 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 5.375% NOTES DUE 2028 (THE "GREEN BONDS") AT AN EFFECTIVE YIELD OF 5.468%, MATURING MAY 1, 2028.
