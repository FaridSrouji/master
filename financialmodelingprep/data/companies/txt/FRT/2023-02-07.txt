FEDERAL REALTY ANNOUNCES EXECUTIVE PROMOTIONS. NORTH BETHESDA, MD. , FEB. 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST (NYSE: FRT) IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE A SERIES OF EXECUTIVE PROMOTIONS THAT RECOGNIZE THE CONTRIBUTIONS AND EXPANDED RESPONSIBILITIES OF A NUMBER OF LONG-SERVING TEAM MEMBERS.
