FEDERAL REALTY RANKS FIRST AMONG PEER GROUPS IN 2022 GRESB REAL ESTATE ASSESSMENT. RESULTS REFLECT FEDERAL'S ENVIRONMENTAL LEADERSHIP IN ITS OPERATIONS AND DEVELOPMENT NORTH BETHESDA, MD. , OCT. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST (NYSE: FRT) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED AS A PEER GROUP LEADER IN MULTIPLE CATEGORIES IN THE 2022 GLOBAL REAL ESTATE SUSTAINABILITY BENCHMARK (GRESB) REAL ESTATE ASSESSMENT.
