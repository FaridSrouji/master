FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST SELECTED AS 2021 GREEN LEASE LEADER BY THE DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY'S BETTER BUILDINGS ALLIANCE AND THE INSTITUTE FOR MARKET TRANSFORMATION. NORTH BETHESDA, MD., MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EARLIER THIS WEEK, THE INSTITUTE FOR MARKET TRANSFORMATION ( IMT ) AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY'S (DOE) BETTER BUILDINGS ALLIANCE  ANNOUNCED FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST (NYSE:FRT) AS A 2021 GREEN LEASE LEADER.
