FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST RELEASES TAX STATUS OF 2022 DISTRIBUTIONS. NORTH BETHESDA, MD. , JAN. 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST (NYSE:FRT) RELEASED TODAY THE FEDERAL INCOME TAX TREATMENT FOR 2022 DISTRIBUTIONS TO HOLDERS OF ITS COMMON SHARES OF BENEFICIAL INTEREST (NYSE: FRT) AND ITS 5.000% SERIES C CUMULATIVE REDEEMABLE PREFERRED SHARES (NYSE: FRT.PRC).
