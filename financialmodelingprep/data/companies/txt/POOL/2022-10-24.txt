POOLCORP TO RING THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET OPENING BELL. COVINGTON, LA., OCT. 24, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POOL CORPORATION (NASDAQ: POOL) WILL RING THE OPENING BELL AT THE NASDAQ MARKETSITE ON TUESDAY, OCTOBER 25, 2022, TO CELEBRATE MORE THAN 25 YEARS OF BEING LISTED ON THE EXCHANGE. PETER ARVAN, CEO, WILL BE JOINED BY SEVERAL OF POOLCORP'S OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS TO COMMEMORATE THE OCCASION.
