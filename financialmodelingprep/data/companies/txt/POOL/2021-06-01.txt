POOL CORPORATION TO PRESENT AT THE UBS GLOBAL INDUSTRIALS AND TRANSPORTATION VIRTUAL CONFERENCE. COVINGTON, LA., JUNE 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POOL CORPORATION (NASDAQ:POOL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MARK W. JOSLIN, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, AND MELANIE HOUSEY HART, VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER, WILL BE PARTICIPATING IN THE UBS GLOBAL INDUSTRIALS AND TRANSPORTATION VIRTUAL CONFERENCE. THEY WILL BE GIVING A VIRTUAL PRESENTATION ON TUESDAY, JUNE 8, 2021, AT 1:00 PM EASTERN TIME. INFORMATIONAL MATERIALS USED DURING THE CONFERENCE WILL BE POSTED ON POOLCORP'S WEBSITE ON THE MORNING OF THE CONFERENCE.
