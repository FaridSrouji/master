POOL CORPORATION ANNOUNCES AN INCREASE IN ITS SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM TO A TOTAL AUTHORIZATION OF $600 MILLION, A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND INCREASE AND 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS VOTING RESULTS. COVINGTON, LA., MAY 04, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POOL CORPORATION (NASDAQ/GSM:POOL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE BOARD) HAS AUTHORIZED AN ADDITIONAL $196.2 MILLION UNDER ITS EXISTING SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM FOR THE PURCHASE OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK IN THE OPEN MARKET AT PREVAILING MARKET PRICES. THIS AMOUNT ADDS TO THE $403.8 MILLION REMAINING UNDER ITS EXISTING AUTHORIZATION AS OF MAY 3, 2022, BRINGING ITS TOTAL AUTHORIZATION AVAILABLE TO $600.0 MILLION.
