POOL CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. COVINGTON, LA., OCT. 28, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POOL CORPORATION (NASDAQ/GSM:POOL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.80 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 24, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 11, 2021.
