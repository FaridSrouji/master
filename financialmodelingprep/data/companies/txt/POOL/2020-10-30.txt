POOL CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. COVINGTON, LA., OCT. 30, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POOL CORPORATION (NASDAQ/GSM:POOL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.58 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 25, 2020, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 12, 2020.
