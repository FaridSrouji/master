POOL CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. COVINGTON, LA., FEB. 25, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POOL CORPORATION (NASDAQ/GSM:POOL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.80 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON MARCH 24, 2022, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON MARCH 10, 2022.
