POOL CORPORATION TO PRESENT AT THE WILLIAM BLAIR 41ST ANNUAL GROWTH STOCK VIRTUAL CONFERENCE. COVINGTON, LA., MAY 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POOL CORPORATION (NASDAQ:POOL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PETER D. ARVAN, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AND MARK W. JOSLIN, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL BE PARTICIPATING IN THE WILLIAM BLAIR 41ST ANNUAL GROWTH STOCK VIRTUAL CONFERENCE. THE GENERAL SESSION WILL OCCUR ON THURSDAY, JUNE 3, 2021, AT 12:40 PM CENTRAL STANDARD TIME AND WILL BE IN A ‘FIRESIDE CHAT' FORMAT CONSISTING OF A SHORT PRESENTATION FOLLOWED BY Q&A. INFORMATIONAL MATERIALS USED DURING THE CONFERENCE WILL BE POSTED ON POOLCORP'S WEBSITE ON THE MORNING OF THE CONFERENCE.
