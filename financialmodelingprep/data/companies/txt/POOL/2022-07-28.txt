POOL CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. COVINGTON, LA., JULY 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POOL CORPORATION (NASDAQ/GSM:POOL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.00 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON AUGUST 25, 2022, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 11, 2022.
