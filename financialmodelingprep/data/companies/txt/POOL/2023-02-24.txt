POOL CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. COVINGTON, LA., FEB. 24, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- POOL CORPORATION (NASDAQ/GSM:POOL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.00 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON MARCH 29, 2023, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON MARCH 15, 2023.
