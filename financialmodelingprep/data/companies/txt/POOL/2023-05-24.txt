PRITZKER PRIVATE CAPITAL ADDS MANNY PEREZ DE LA MESA TO THE PRITZKER ADVISORY BOARD. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PRITZKER PRIVATE CAPITAL ("PPC"), A LEADER IN FAMILY DIRECT INVESTING, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MANNY PEREZ DE LA MESA HAS JOINED THE PRITZKER ADVISORY BOARD. MR. PEREZ DE LA MESA, WHO FORMERLY SERVED AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF POOLCORP (NASDAQGS:POOL), BRINGS MORE THAN 40 YEARS OF BUSINESS MANAGEMENT EXPERIENCE TO THE PRITZKER ADVISORY BOARD. AN ACCOMPLISHED EXECUTIVE, MR. PEREZ DE LA MESA HAS HELD SEVERAL DIRECTOR SEATS ON THE BOARDS OF COMPANIES ACROSS MULTIPLE INDUS.
