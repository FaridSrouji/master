POOL CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 27, 2019. POOL CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.55 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 27, 2019 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 14, 2019.
