CRYPTOCURRENCY EXCHANGE CEX.IO SELECTS FIS TO SUPPORT LAUNCH OF CONSUMER DEBIT CARDS. CEX.IO WAS LOOKING FOR A PROVEN TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM TO SUPPORT ITS PLANNED LAUNCH OF DEBIT CARDS FOR ITS CRYPTOCURRENCY CUSTOMERS.
