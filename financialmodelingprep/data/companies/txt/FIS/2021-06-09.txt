MARK J. HAWKINS JOINS FIS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. JACKSONVILLE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIS® (NYSE: FIS), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FINANCIAL SERVICES TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MARK J. HAWKINS HAS JOINED THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MR. HAWKINS IS CURRENTLY THE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER EMERITUS AT SALESFORCE.COM, A LEADING PROVIDER OF ENTERPRISE CLOUD COMPUTING SOLUTIONS, A POSITION HE HAS HELD SINCE FEBRUARY 2021. HE PREVIOUSLY HELD THE POSITIONS OF PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AT SALESFORCE.COM. PRIOR TO SALESFORCE.
