ROSEN, A LEADING AND RANKED FIRM, ENCOURAGES FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. INVESTORS WITH LOSSES TO SECURE COUNSEL BEFORE IMPORTANT DEADLINE IN SECURITIES CLASS ACTION - FIS. NEW YORK , MARCH 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHY: ROSEN LAW FIRM, A GLOBAL INVESTOR RIGHTS LAW FIRM, ANNOUNCES THE FILING OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT ON BEHALF OF PURCHASERS OF THE COMMON STOCK OF FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: FIS) BETWEEN FEBRUARY 9, 2021 AND FEBRUARY 10, 2023, BOTH DATES INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD"). A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS ALREADY BEEN FILED.
INVESTOR ACTION ALERT: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 8, 2023 / THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. ("FIDELITY NATIONAL" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE: FIS ) FOR VIOLATIONS OF §§10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION. INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED THE COMPANY'S SECURITIES BETWEEN FEBRUARY 9, 2021 AND FEBRUARY 10, 2023, INCLUSIVE (THE ''CLASS PERIOD''), ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THE FIRM BEFORE MAY 5, 2023.

(“FIDELITY NATIONAL” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: FIS) AND CERTAIN OF ITS OFFICERS, ON BEHALF OF ALL PERSONS AND ENTITIES THAT PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED FIDELITY COMMON STOCK BETWEEN FEBRUARY 9, 2021 AND FEBRUARY 10, 2023, INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”).
