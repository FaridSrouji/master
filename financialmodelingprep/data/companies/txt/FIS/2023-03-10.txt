SHAREHOLDER ACTION REMINDER: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 10, 2023 / THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. ("FIDELITY NATIONAL" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE: FIS ) FOR VIOLATIONS OF §§10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION.
