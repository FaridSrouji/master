FIS ALERT: THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF MAY 5, 2023 IN THE CLASS ACTION FILED ON BEHALF OF FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. SHAREHOLDERS. NEW YORK , APRIL 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION COMPLAINT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: FIS) ALLEGING THAT THE COMPANY VIOLATED FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
