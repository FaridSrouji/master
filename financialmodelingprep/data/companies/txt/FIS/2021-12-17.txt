FIS CLEARED DERIVATIVES SUITE NAMED POST-TRADE SYSTEM OF THE YEAR BY FOW. FIS RECEIVED THE AWARD AT FOW'S (FUTURES & OPTIONS WORLD) CEREMONY EARLIER THIS MONTH.
