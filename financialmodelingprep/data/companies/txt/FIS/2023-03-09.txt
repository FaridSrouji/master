INVESTOR ACTION NOTICE: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 9, 2023 / THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES, INC. ("FIDELITY NATIONAL" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE:FIS) FOR VIOLATIONS OF 10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION. INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED THE COMPANY'S SECURITIES BETWEEN FEBRUARY 9, 2021 AND FEBRUARY 10, 2023, INCLUSIVE (THE ''CLASS PERIOD''), ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THE FIRM BEFORE MAY 5, 2023.
