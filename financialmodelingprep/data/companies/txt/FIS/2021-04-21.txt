FIS OPENS NEW GROWTH OPPORTUNITIES FOR GLOBAL BRANDS WITH WORLDPAY DOMESTIC ACQUIRING EXPANSION INTO SOUTH AFRICA, NIGERIA AND MALAYSIA. JACKSONVILLE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CONTINUING TO BUILD ITS FOOTPRINT AS A LEADING GLOBAL ACQUIRER, FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER FIS® (NYSE: FIS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT IS EXPANDING ITS PAYMENT PROCESSING CAPABILITIES INTO THE RAPIDLY GROWING MARKETS OF MALAYSIA, SOUTH AFRICA AND NIGERIA. FIS HAS SECURED A DOMESTIC ACQUIRING LICENSE IN MALAYSIA TO PROVIDE ITS WORLDPAY PAYMENT PROCESSING PLATFORM.
