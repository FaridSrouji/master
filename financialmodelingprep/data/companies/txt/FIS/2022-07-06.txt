FIS ANNOUNCES PROPOSED OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES. JACKSONVILLE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIS® (NYSE: FIS), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FINANCIAL SERVICES TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT INTENDS, SUBJECT TO MARKET AND OTHER CONSIDERATIONS, TO MAKE A MULTI-TRANCHE OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES DENOMINATED IN U.S. DOLLARS (THE “SENIOR NOTES”). FIS INTENDS TO USE THE NET PROCEEDS FROM THE SENIOR NOTES OFFERING FOR THE REPAYMENT OF DEBT UNDER ITS COMMERCIAL PAPER PROGRAMS, WITH ANY REMAINING PROCEEDS TO BE USED FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES.
