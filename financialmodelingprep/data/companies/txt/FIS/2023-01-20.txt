FIS APPOINTS LEE ADREAN AND MARK BENJAMIN AS INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS. JACKSONVILLE, FLA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--FIS (NYSE: FIS) (“FIS” OR THE “COMPANY”), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FINANCIAL SERVICES TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENTS OF LEE ADREAN AND MARK BENJAMIN TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE “BOARD”), EFFECTIVE JANUARY 20, 2023.
