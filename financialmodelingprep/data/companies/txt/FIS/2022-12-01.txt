FIS CHALLENGES NEARLY 200 STARTUPS TO PITCH THEIR BOLDEST IDEAS IN FIRST APAC FINTECH COMPETITION. JACKSONVILLE, FLA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--GLOBAL FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER FIS ® (NYSE: FIS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF ITS ANNUAL INNOVATEIN48 FINTECH COMPETITION TO INCLUDE EARLY-STAGE FINTECH STARTUPS IN THE APAC REGION. ORIGINALLY AN EMPLOYEE-BASED INNOVATION COMPETITION IN 2013, FIS EXPANDED THE INNOVATEIN48 PROGRAM TO STUDENTS IN 2021. INNOVATEIN48-PARTNER EDITION IS THE FIRST TIME THE COMPANY HAS HOSTED AN IDEATION COMPETITION FOR STARTUPS IN THE APAC REGION.
