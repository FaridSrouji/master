PAYMENTS VETERAN STEPHANIE FERRIS REJOINS FIS EXECUTIVE TEAM. JACKSONVILLE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER FIS® (NYSE: FIS) ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF FORMER FIS AND WORLDPAY EXECUTIVE STEPHANIE FERRIS TO ITS EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM, REPORTING TO GARY NORCROSS, FIS CHAIRMAN AND CEO. STEPHANIE JOINED FIS ON SEPTEMBER 2, 2021 AS CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER. IN THIS NEWLY CREATED POSITION, FERRIS WILL WORK IN PARTNERSHIP WITH FIS BUSINESS AND CORPORATE LEADERS INCLUDING STRATEGY, MERGERS AND ACQUISITIONS AND KEY STRATEGIC INITIATIVES
