VIJAY D'SILVA AND KENNETH T. LAMNECK JOIN THE FIS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. JACKSONVILLE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIS® (NYSE: FIS), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FINANCIAL SERVICES TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT VIJAY D'SILVA AND KENNETH T. LAMNECK HAVE JOINED THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MR. D'SILVA WAS A SENIOR PARTNER AT MCKINSEY & CO., A MANAGEMENT CONSULTING FIRM, FROM 1994 UNTIL HIS RETIREMENT FROM MCKINSEY ON MARCH 6, 2022.
