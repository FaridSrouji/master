NORDSON CORPORATION APPOINTS MILTON MORRIS TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. WESTLAKE, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORDSON CORPORATION (NASDAQ: NDSN) ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DR. MILTON MORRIS, RECENTLY RETIRED PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF NEUSPERA MEDICAL, INC., TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. DR. MORRIS WILL BRING ROBUST BUSINESS LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCE, AS WELL AS A LEGACY OF SUCCESSFUL PRODUCT INNOVATION, TO THE BOARD. “WE ARE PLEASED TO WELCOME MILTON AS A NEW INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR. HIS PASSION FOR INNOVATION AND NEW PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT ALIGNS WELL WITH NORDSON'
