NORDSON RECEIVES HIGHEST HONOR FROM SMT CHINA VISION AWARDS FOR SQ3000™+ INSPECTION AND METROLOGY SYSTEM. MINNEAPOLIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORDSON CORPORATION (NASDAQ: NDSN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN SELECTED FOR A 2022 SMT CHINA VISION AWARD IN THE CATEGORY OF TEST & INSPECTION – AOI FOR ITS CYBEROPTICS SQ3000™+ MULTI-FUNCTION SYSTEM. THE HIGHEST-LEVEL AWARD WAS GARNERED BASED ON THE LEVEL OF PRODUCT INNOVATION.
