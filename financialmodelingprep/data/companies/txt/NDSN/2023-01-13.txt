NORDSON TEST & INSPECTION TO DEMONSTRATE BEST-IN-CLASS INSPECTION AND METROLOGY SYSTEMS AT APEX 2023. MINNEAPOLIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORDSON TEST & INSPECTION TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL EXHIBIT AT THE 2023 IPC APEX EXPO, SCHEDULED TO TAKE PLACE JAN. 24-26, 2023 AT THE SAN DIEGO CONVENTION CENTER IN CALIFORNIA. THE COMPANY WILL HIGHLIGHT THE MULTI-AWARD-WINNING CYBEROPTICS SQ3000™+ MULTI-FUNCTION SYSTEM FOR AOI, SPI AND CMM, THE M2 AOI SYSTEM, THE CYBEROPTICS SE3000™ SPI SYSTEM AND QUADRA 7 X-RAY SYSTEM IN BOOTH #915.
