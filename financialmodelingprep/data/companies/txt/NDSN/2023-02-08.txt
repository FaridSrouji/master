NORDSON CORPORATION DECLARES SECOND QUARTER DIVIDEND FOR FISCAL YEAR 2023. WESTLAKE, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORDSON CORPORATION (NASDAQ: NDSN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A FISCAL YEAR 2023 SECOND QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND IN THE AMOUNT OF $0.65 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON MARCH 7, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 21, 2023. THIS AMOUNT EQUALS THE $0.65 PER COMMON SHARE DIVIDEND PAID IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2023.
