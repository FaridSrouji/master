NORDSON CORPORATION APPOINTS ANAND PATEL AS VICE PRESIDENT, TREASURY AND FINANCE. WESTLAKE, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORDSON CORPORATION (NASDAQ: NDSN) ANNOUNCED THAT ANAND PATEL WILL SUCCEED RAY CUSHING AS VICE PRESIDENT, TREASURY AND FINANCE FOLLOWING RAY'S UPCOMING RETIREMENT. ANAND JOINS NORDSON ON SEPTEMBER 26 FROM O-I GLASS, INC. (NASDAQ: OI), A GLOBAL MANUFACTURING COMPANY, WHERE HE SERVED AS VICE PRESIDENT AND TREASURER.
