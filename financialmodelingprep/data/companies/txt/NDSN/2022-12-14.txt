NORDSON CORPORATION REPORTS A STRONG FOURTH QUARTER AND RECORD FISCAL YEAR 2022 RESULTS. WESTLAKE, OHIO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORDSON CORPORATION (NASDAQ: NDSN) TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE FISCAL FOURTH QUARTER ENDED OCTOBER 31, 2022. SALES WERE $684 MILLION, A 14% INCREASE COMPARED TO THE PRIOR YEAR'S FOURTH QUARTER SALES OF $599 MILLION. THE INCREASE IN FOURTH QUARTER 2022 SALES INCLUDED 18% ORGANIC VOLUME GROWTH, A FAVORABLE 4% ACQUISITION IMPACT AND UNFAVORABLE CURRENCY TRANSLATION OF 8%. ORGANIC SALES GROWTH WAS STRONG IN VIRTUALLY ALL END MARKETS, AND PARTICULARLY ROBUST FOR ELECTRONICS AND MEDICAL.
