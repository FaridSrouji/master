NORDSON CORPORATION AND NORDSON CORPORATION FOUNDATION GIVE NEARLY $13 MILLION TO CHARITY IN FISCAL YEAR 2022. WESTLAKE, OHIO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORDSON CORPORATION (NASDAQ: NDSN) AND THE NORDSON CORPORATION FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE CONTRIBUTED APPROXIMATELY $13 MILLION TO CHARITY DURING THE 2022 FISCAL YEAR, WHICH ENDED OCTOBER 31, 2022.
