EQUIFAX COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF EFFICIENT HIRE. ATLANTA, FEB. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EQUIFAX ® (NYSE: EFX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF EFFICIENT HIRE, A PROVIDER OF CLOUD RECRUITING, ONBOARDING AND HR MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS. THE DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE EFFICIENT HIRE WAS ANNOUNCED ON JANUARY 31, 2022.
