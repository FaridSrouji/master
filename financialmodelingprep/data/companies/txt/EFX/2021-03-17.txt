EQUIFAX NAMES JULIA HOUSTON CHIEF STRATEGY AND MARKETING OFFICER. ATLANTA, MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EQUIFAX ® (NYSE: EFX) ANNOUNCED JULIA HOUSTON AS CHIEF STRATEGY AND MARKETING OFFICER, EXPANDING HER CURRENT SCOPE TO INCLUDE LEADERSHIP OF  GLOBAL MARKETING WHERE SHE WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR ENSURING STRONG ALIGNMENT WITH THE COMPANY'S STRATEGY, WORKING CLOSELY WITH THE BUSINESS UNITS AND GLOBAL REGIONS. THIS EXPANSION OF HOUSTON'S ROLE UNLOCKS SYNERGIES AND EFFICIENCIES THAT SUPPORT THE COMPANY'S STRATEGY TO BRING NEW PRODUCTS, INNOVATION AND CAPABILITIES TO CUSTOMERS, PARTNERS AND CONSUMERS AROUND THE WORLD.
