SMALL BUSINESS REVIVAL IS UNDERWAY, WHILE DEMAND FOR CREDIT REMAINS LOW. TORONTO, DEC. 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HEADING INTO THE NEW YEAR, INCREASINGLY MORE CANADIANS ARE STARTING THEIR OWN BUSINESS ACCORDING TO EQUIFAX CANADA'S Q3 SMALL BUSINESS CREDIT TRENDS REPORT. WHILE THE NUMBER OF NEW FINANCIALLY ACTIVE BUSINESSES ISN'T QUITE AS HIGH AS IT WAS PRIOR TO THE PANDEMIC, A 40 PER CENT INCREASE NATIONALLY IS BEING STOKED BY ALBERTA AND ATLANTIC CANADA.
