EQUIFAX WINS GOOGLE CLOUD FINANCIAL SERVICES CUSTOMER AWARD. ATLANTA, OCT. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX ® (NYSE: EFX), A DATA, ANALYTICS AND TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT GOOGLE CLOUD RECOGNIZED EQUIFAX AS THE TOP FINANCIAL SERVICES CUSTOMER IN 2021. THE EQUIFAX CLOUD™ TRANSFORMATION WAS RECOGNIZED FOR DEMONSTRATING INNOVATIVE THINKING, TECHNICAL EXCELLENCE AND TRANSFORMATION EXECUTION.
