EQUIFAX, EXPERIAN, AND TRANSUNION SUPPORT U.S. CONSUMERS WITH CHANGES TO MEDICAL COLLECTION DEBT REPORTING. ATLANTA & COSTA MESA, CALIF. & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE THREE NATIONWIDE CREDIT REPORTING AGENCIES (NCRAS) – EQUIFAX (NYSE: EFX), EXPERIAN (LON: EXPN), AND TRANSUNION (NYSE: TRU) – TODAY ANNOUNCED SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO MEDICAL COLLECTION DEBT REPORTING TO SUPPORT CONSUMERS FACED WITH UNEXPECTED MEDICAL BILLS. THESE JOINT MEASURES WILL REMOVE NEARLY 70% OF MEDICAL COLLECTION DEBT TRADELINES FROM CONSUMER CREDIT REPORTS, A STEP TAKEN AFTER MONTHS OF INDUSTRY RESEARCH. ACCORDING TO THE KAISE
