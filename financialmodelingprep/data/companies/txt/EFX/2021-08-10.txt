EQUIFAX ANNOUNCES DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE APPRISS INSIGHTS. ATLANTA, AUG. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX® (NYSE: EFX) A GLOBAL DATA, ANALYTICS AND TECHNOLOGY COMPANY HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE APPRISS INSIGHTS, A TRUSTED AND COMPREHENSIVE SOURCE OF RISK AND CRIMINAL JUSTICE INTELLIGENCE, FOR $1.825 BILLION. IN CONJUNCTION WITH THE ACQUISITION, EQUIFAX WILL RECEIVE A TAX BENEFIT WITH A NET PRESENT VALUE AT TIME OF PURCHASE OF APPROXIMATELY $360 MILLION.
