EQUIFAX NAMES KAREN FICHUK TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ATLANTA , FEB. 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX ® (NYSE: EFX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KAREN FICHUK, EXECUTIVE BOARD MEMBER FOR RANDSTAD N.V., AND FORMER CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER FOR RANDSTAD NORTH AMERICA, HAS BEEN ELECTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
EQUIFAX BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA , FEB. 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX ® (NYSE: EFX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EQUIFAX BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.39 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON MARCH 15, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 22, 2023. EQUIFAX HAS PAID CASH DIVIDENDS FOR MORE THAN 100 CONSECUTIVE YEARS.
