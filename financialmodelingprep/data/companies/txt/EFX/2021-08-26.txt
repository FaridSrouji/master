EQUIFAX BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA, AUG. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX INC. (NYSE: EFX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EQUIFAX BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.39 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON SEPT. 17, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPT.
