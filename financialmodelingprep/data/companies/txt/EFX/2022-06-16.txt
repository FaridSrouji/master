EQUIFAX WINS GOOGLE CLOUD CUSTOMER AWARDS IN DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION AND FINANCIAL SERVICES. ATLANTA , JUNE 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX® (NYSE: EFX) HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED WITH THE GOOGLE CLOUD CUSTOMER AWARD FOR DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION. THE COMPANY ALSO RECEIVED THE GOOGLE CLOUD CUSTOMER AWARD FOR FINANCIAL SERVICES FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
