EQUIFAX ANNOUNCES ACQUISITION OF I2VERIFY. ATLANTA, MARCH 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EQUIFAX ® (NYSE: EFX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS CLOSED THE ACQUISITION OF I2VERIFY, AN INCOME AND EMPLOYMENT VERIFICATION PROVIDER.  WITH THIS ACQUISITION, EQUIFAX EXPANDS ITS SUITE OF AUTOMATED VERIFICATION CAPABILITIES AND BRINGS INCREMENTAL EFFICIENCIES TO BOTH CONSUMERS AND CREDENTIALED VERIFIERS.
