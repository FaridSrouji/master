EQUIFAX ANNOUNCES DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE MIDIGATOR. ACQUISITION TO FURTHER EQUIFAX GLOBAL FOOTPRINT IN DIGITAL IDENTITY AND FRAUD PREVENTION SOLUTIONS ATLANTA , JULY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX® (NYSE: EFX) IS EXPANDING ITS GLOBAL FOOTPRINT IN DIGITAL IDENTITY AND FRAUD PREVENTION WITH A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE MIDIGATOR LLC , A PROVIDER OF POST-TRANSACTION FRAUD MITIGATION SOLUTIONS. WITH THE RISE OF GLOBAL ECOMMERCE SALES, EXPECTED TO HIT $5.5 TRILLION IN 2022, TRANSACTION DISPUTES AND CHARGEBACKS ARE ALSO SURGING.
