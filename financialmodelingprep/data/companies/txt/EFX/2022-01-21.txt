EQUIFAX AND GEORGIA TECH ANNOUNCE FINANCIAL INCLUSION RESEARCH PARTNERSHIP. ATLANTA, JAN. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX® (NYSE: EFX) AND THE GEORGIA INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY ("GEORGIA TECH") HAVE ANNOUNCED A NEW RESEARCH PARTNERSHIP DESIGNED TO DEVELOP NEW WAYS TO REACH UNDERSERVED, UNDERBANKED AND CREDIT-REBUILDING CONSUMERS. AS PART OF THIS RELATIONSHIP, THE FINANCIAL SERVICES INNOVATION LAB AT GEORGIA TECH WILL LEVERAGE ANONYMIZED EQUIFAX DATA ASSETS TO FURTHER ITS INNOVATIVE RESEARCH DESIGNED TO INCREASE CONSUMER ACCESS TO CREDIT AND EQUIFAX DATA SCIENTISTS WILL PROVIDE HANDS-ON DATA EDUCATION TO SPEED RESEARCH TIME.
