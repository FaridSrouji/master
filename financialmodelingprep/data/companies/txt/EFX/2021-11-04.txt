EQUIFAX BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA, NOV. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EQUIFAX ®(NYSE: EFX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EQUIFAX BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.39 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON DEC. 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOV. 24, 2021. EQUIFAX HAS PAID CASH DIVIDENDS FOR MORE THAN 100 CONSECUTIVE YEARS.
