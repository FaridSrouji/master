EQUIFAX TO ACQUIRE EFFICIENT HIRE. ATLANTA, JAN. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EQUIFAX ® (NYSE: EFX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE EFFICIENT HIRE, A PROVIDER OF CLOUD RECRUITING, ONBOARDING AND HR MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS. THIS ACQUISITION WILL ENABLE EQUIFAX WORKFORCE SOLUTIONS TO BRING EMPLOYERS MORE OF THE SERVICES AND INSIGHTS THEY NEED TO HELP THEM MANAGE AMIDST A RAPIDLY CHANGING HIRING AND EMPLOYMENT LANDSCAPE.
