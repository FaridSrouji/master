EQUIFAX CHIEF INFORMATION SECURITY OFFICER JAMIL FARSHCHI APPOINTED AS A STRATEGIC ENGAGEMENT ADVISOR FOR THE FBI. APPOINTMENT UNDERSCORES EQUIFAX COMMITMENT TO BEING A FORCE FOR GOOD IN CYBERSECURITY ATLANTA , SEPT. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EQUIFAX  (NYSE: EFX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF INFORMATION SECURITY OFFICER JAMIL FARSHCHI HAS BEEN APPOINTED AS A STRATEGIC ENGAGEMENT ADVISOR TO THE FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION (FBI).
