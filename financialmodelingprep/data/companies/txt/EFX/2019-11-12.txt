PRISMHR ANNOUNCES INTEGRATION OF WORK NUMBER SERVICE FROM EQUIFAX. PRISMHR ANNOUNCED THE INTEGRATION OF THE WORK NUMBER(R) SERVICE, FROM EQUIFAX, WITHIN THE PRISMHR PLATFORM. THE INTEGRATION PERMITS ACCESS TO THE INDUSTRY-LEADING, CENTRALIZED REPOSITORY OF EMPLOYMENT AND INCOME INFORMATION IN THE U.S. TO PRISMHR HR OUTSOURCING PARTNERS. PRISMHR HRO PARTNERS AND THEIR SMALL BUSINESS CUSTOMERS CAN NOW USE THE WORK NUMBER SERVICE TO SECURELY AND INSTANTLY DELIVER EMPLOYMENT AND INCOME VERIFICATIONS TO LENDERS AND GOVERNMENT AGENCIES, REDUCING WORKLOAD AND RISK OF FRAUD AND INACCURACY FOR SERVICE PROVIDERS AND INCREASING RESPONSE TIME FOR INQUIRIES ON BEHALF OF EMPLOYEES. VERIFICATIONS FOR EMPLOYMENT AND INCOME STATUS CAN BE TRIGGERED BY A VARIETY OF EMPLOYEE TRANSACTIONS, INCLUDING HOME PURCHASING AND REFINANCING, RENTING A PROPERTY, BUYING A CAR, APPLYING FOR A LOAN OR CREDIT OR APPLYING FOR GOVERNMENT BENEFITS. THE WORK NUMBER FROM EQUIFAX IS AVAILABLE TO ALL PRISMHR HRO PARTNERS THROUGH PRISMHR MARKETPLACE. THE SERVICE IS ABLE TO AUTOMATICALLY RESPOND TO EMPLOYMENT AND INCOME VERIFICATION REQUESTS DIRECTLY BY USING PAYROLL INFORMATION ALREADY MANAGED BY THE PRISMHR PLATFORM.
