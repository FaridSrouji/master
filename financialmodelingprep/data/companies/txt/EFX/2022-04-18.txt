EQUIFAX NAMES TREVOR BURNS AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE INVESTOR RELATIONS. ATLANTA , APRIL 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EQUIFAX ® (NYSE: EFX) HAS APPOINTED TREVOR BURNS AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE INVESTOR RELATIONS. WITH THIS ANNOUNCEMENT, BURNS IS RETURNING TO THE ROLE THAT HE HELD PRIOR TO HIS POSITION OF SENIOR FINANCIAL OFFICER FOR EQUIFAX GLOBAL CONSUMER SOLUTIONS.
