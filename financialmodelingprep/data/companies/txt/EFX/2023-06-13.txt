VESTA ANNOUNCES INTEGRATION WITH THE WORK NUMBER® FROM EQUIFAX TO HELP INCREASE LENDER EFFICIENCY. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VESTA, THE NEXT-GENERATION LOAN ORIGINATION SYSTEM (LOS), HAS ANNOUNCED ITS INTEGRATION WITH THE WORK NUMBER® FROM EQUIFAX. THIS INTEGRATION ENABLES LENDERS USING VESTA'S LOS TO EFFORTLESSLY ACCESS ROBUST VERIFICATION OF EMPLOYMENT AND INCOME CAPABILITIES FROM THE WORK NUMBER WITHIN THE LOS INTERFACE. BY COMBINING VESTA'S ADVANCED LOS FEATURES WITH AN INDUSTRY-LEADING VERIFICATION PLATFORM, LENDERS CAN STREAMLINE THEIR PROCESSES, MAKE MORE INFORMED LENDING DECISI.
