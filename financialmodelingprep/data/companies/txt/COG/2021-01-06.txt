DEADLINE ALERT: BRAGAR EAGEL & SQUIRE, P.C. REMINDS INVESTORS THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED AGAINST CABOT OIL & GAS CORPORATION AND ENCOURAGES INVESTORS TO CONTACT THE FIRM. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #CABOTOILANDGAS--BRAGAR EAGEL & SQUIRE, P.C., A NATIONALLY RECOGNIZED SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LAW FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE MIDDLE DISTRICT OF PENNSYLVANIA ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS THAT PURCHASED CABOT OIL & GAS CORPORATION (NYSE: COG) SECURITIES BETWEEN OCTOBER 23, 2015 AND JUNE 12, 2020 (THE “CLASS PERIOD”). INVESTORS HAVE UNTIL JANUARY 12, 2021 TO APPLY TO THE COURT TO BE APPOINTED AS LEAD PLAINTI
