CABOT OIL & GAS AND CIMAREX ENERGY SHAREHOLDERS APPROVE MERGER. HOUSTON AND DENVER, SEPT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CABOT OIL & GAS CORPORATION ("CABOT") (NYSE: COG) AND CIMAREX ENERGY CO. ("CIMAREX") (NYSE: XEC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BOTH COMPANIES' SHAREHOLDERS VOTED TO APPROVE THE PENDING TRANSACTION.
