CABOT OIL & GAS AND CIMAREX ENERGY COMPLETE COMBINATION, FORMING COTERRA ENERGY. HOUSTON, OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COTERRA ENERGY INC. ("COTERRA" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: COG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF THE COMBINATION OF CABOT OIL & GAS CORPORATION ("CABOT") AND CIMAREX ENERGY CO. ("CIMAREX"), CREATING A PREMIER, DIVERSIFIED ENERGY COMPANY WITH A STRONG FREE CASH FLOW PROFILE, WELL POSITIONED TO DELIVER SUPERIOR AND SUSTAINABLE RETURNS TO SHAREHOLDERS THROUGH COMMODITY CYCLES. COTERRA'S COMMON STOCK WILL TRADE ON THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE UNDER THE TICKER SYMBOL "CTRA" AT THE OPEN OF TRADING ON OCTOBER 4, 2021 AND UNDER THE SYMBOL "COG" UNTIL THEN.
