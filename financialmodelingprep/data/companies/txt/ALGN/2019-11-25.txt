ALIGN TECHNOLOGY APPOINTS FRANK QUINN TO VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER, UNITED STATES. ALIGN TECHNOLOGY, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT FRANK QUINN, VICE PRESIDENT, MARKETING, AMERICAS, WILL MOVE TO THE ROLE OF VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER OF THE UNITED STATES. THE NEW ROLE TAKES EFFECT ON DECEMBER 9, 2019. IN THIS NEW ROLE, MR. QUINN WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR DRIVING CONTINUED GROWTH AND PROFITABILITY WITHIN THE UNITED STATES, OVERSEEING THE US SALES, MARKETING, DENTAL SERVICE ORGANIZATIONS (DSOS), COMMERCIAL EXCELLENCE AND FUNCTIONAL LEADERS SUPPORTING THE US COMMERCIAL ORGANIZATION. SINCE JOINING ALIGN IN 2013, MR. QUINN HAS HELD SEVERAL LEADERSHIP ROLES THROUGHOUT THE ORGANIZATION. MOST RECENTLY, HE SERVED AS THE VICE PRESIDENT OF MARKETING, AMERICAS.
