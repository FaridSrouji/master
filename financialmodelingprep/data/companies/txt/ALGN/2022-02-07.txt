(NASDAQ: ALGN), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL DEVICE COMPANY THAT DESIGNS, MANUFACTURES, AND SELLS THE INVISALIGN SYSTEM OF CLEAR ALIGNERS, ITERO INTRAORAL SCANNERS, AND EXOCAD CAD/CAM SOFTWARE FOR DIGITAL ORTHODONTICS AND RESTORATIVE DENTISTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SETTLED ITS LONGSTANDING PATENT INFRINGEMENT AND ANTITRUST LITIGATION WITH 3SHAPE A/S WHICH BEGAN IN NOVEMBER OF 2017.
