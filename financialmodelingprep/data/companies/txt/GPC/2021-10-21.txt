GENUINE PARTS COMPANY REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. ATLANTA, OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENUINE PARTS COMPANY (NYSE: GPC) ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER AND NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2021. "WE ARE PLEASED TO REPORT STRONG FINANCIAL RESULTS IN THE THIRD QUARTER, AS THE COMBINATION OF OUR GROWTH INITIATIVES AND THE GLOBAL MARKET RECOVERY DROVE POSITIVE SALES TRENDS, GROSS MARGIN GAINS AND IMPROVED OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCIES.
