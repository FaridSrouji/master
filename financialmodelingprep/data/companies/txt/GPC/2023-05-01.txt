GENUINE PARTS COMPANY DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA , MAY 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENUINE PARTS COMPANY (NYSE: GPC) ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF NINETY-FIVE CENTS ($0.95) PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 3, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD JUNE 2, 2023.
