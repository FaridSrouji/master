GENUINE PARTS COMPANY ENTERS DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE KAMAN DISTRIBUTION GROUP. ATLANTA, DEC. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENUINE PARTS COMPANY (NYSE: GPC) ("GPC"), A LEADING DISTRIBUTOR OF AUTOMOTIVE AND INDUSTRIAL REPLACEMENT PARTS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY, MOTION INDUSTRIES, INC. ("MOTION") WILL ACQUIRE KAMAN DISTRIBUTION GROUP ("KDG") FROM PRIVATE INVESTMENT FIRM LITTLEJOHN & CO., LLC. THE ACQUISITION IS VALUED AT A TOTAL PURCHASE PRICE OF APPROXIMATELY $1.3 BILLION IN CASH.
