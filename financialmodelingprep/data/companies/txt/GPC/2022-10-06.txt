GENUINE PARTS COMPANY RELEASES 2022 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. THE NEW REPORT ADVANCES GPC'S LONG-TERM SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY AND EXPANDS ITS REPORTING AND DISCLOSURE TO REFLECT THE COMPANY'S GLOBAL OPERATIONS.
