GENUINE PARTS COMPANY DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA , APRIL 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENUINE PARTS COMPANY (NYSE: GPC) ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF EIGHTY-NINE AND ONE-HALF CENTS ($0.895) PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 1, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD JUNE 3, 2022.
