GENUINE PARTS COMPANY REVISES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019. GENUINE PARTS COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT IN CONSIDERATION OF RESULTS THUS FAR IN 2019, AS WELL AS THE IMPACT OF THE SALE OF EIS ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2019, THE COMPANY NOW EXPECTS SALES TO INCREASE APPROXIMATELY 3.5%. THIS UPDATED SALES OUTLOOK REPRESENTS A CHANGE FROM THE COMPANY'S PREVIOUS GUIDANCE FOR A 4.5% TO 5.5% SALES INCREASE. THE COMPANY EXPECTS DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE TO RANGE FROM $5.44 TO $5.52 AND IS UPDATING ITS OUTLOOK FOR ADJUSTED DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE, WHICH EXCLUDES ANY NINE MONTH AND FUTURE TRANSACTION AND OTHER COSTS AND INCOME, TO $5.60 TO $5.68. THIS IS A CHANGE FROM THE COMPANY'S PRIOR GUIDANCE OF $5.65 TO $5.75 AND IS PRIMARILY DUE TO THE SALE OF EIS.
