QORVO® APPOINTS GRANT BROWN CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. GREENSBORO, N.C., AUG. 30, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- QORVO® (NASDAQ:QRVO), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF CONNECTIVITY AND POWER SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF GRANT BROWN AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE AUGUST 29, 2022. MR. BROWN WAS NAMED INTERIM CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER ON APRIL 18, 2022, AND PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS QORVO'S VICE PRESIDENT OF TREASURY.
