QORVO® RECEIVES 2022 MOST RESPECTED PUBLIC SEMICONDUCTOR COMPANY AWARD FROM THE GSA. GREENSBORO, N.C., DEC. 12, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- QORVO® (NASDAQ: QRVO), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF CONNECTIVITY AND POWER SOLUTIONS, HAS RECEIVED THE 2022 AWARD FOR MOST RESPECTED PUBLIC SEMICONDUCTOR COMPANY IN ITS CATEGORY BY THE GLOBAL SEMICONDUCTOR ALLIANCE (GSA).
