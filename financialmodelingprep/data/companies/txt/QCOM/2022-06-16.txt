STRATEGY ANALYTICS: HEADLINE: QUALCOMM, APPLE AND MEDIATEK DOMINATE THE ARM-BASED MOBILE COMPUTING CHIP MARKET. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #AP--THE ARM-BASED MOBILE COMPUTING CHIP MARKET (SMARTPHONES, TABLETS AND NOTEBOOK PCS) GREW 27 PERCENT TO $35.1 BILLION IN 2021, ACCORDING TO STRATEGY ANALYTICS' HANDSET COMPONENT TECHNOLOGIES (HCT) SERVICE REPORT. STRATEGY ANALYTICS' RESEARCH REPORT "APPLE POWERS ARM-BASED COMPUTING" ESTIMATES THAT QUALCOMM, APPLE AND MEDIATEK CAPTURED THE TOP-THREE REVENUE SHARE RANKING SPOTS IN THE ARM-BASED MOBILE COMPUTING CHIP (AP) MARKET IN 2021.
