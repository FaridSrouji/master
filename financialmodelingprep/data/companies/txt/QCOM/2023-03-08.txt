QUALCOMM INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY 7 PERCENT. SAN DIEGO , MARCH 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: QCOM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A 7 PERCENT INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND WILL INCREASE FROM $0.75 TO $0.80 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK AND WILL BE EFFECTIVE FOR QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS PAYABLE AFTER MARCH 23, 2023.
