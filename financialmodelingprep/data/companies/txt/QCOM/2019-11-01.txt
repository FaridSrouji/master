MONTEREY RESEARCH FILES PATENT INFRINGEMENT SUIT AGAINST QUALCOMM INCORPORATED. IPVALUE MANAGEMENT INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY MONTEREY RESEARCH LLC HAS FILED A PATENT INFRINGEMENT ACTION AGAINST QUALCOMM INCORPORATED AND RELATED ENTITIES IN DELAWARE DISTRICT COURT. THE ACTION INVOLVES EIGHT MONTEREY PATENTS COVERING A VARIETY OF INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGIES, INCLUDING CIRCUIT DESIGN AND SEMICONDUCTOR MANUFACTURING.
