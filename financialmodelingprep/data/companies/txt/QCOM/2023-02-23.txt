QUALCOMM SELECTS ADOBE TO DEEPEN CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIPS WITH REAL-TIME PERSONALIZATION. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, ADOBE (NASDAQ:ADBE) ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION WITH QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ:QCOM) TO HELP FUEL ITS DIGITAL STRATEGY AND THAT OF ITS AFFILIATED COMPANIES. QUALCOMM WILL ADOPT ADOBE EXPERIENCE CLOUD INCLUDING ADOBE EXPERIENCE PLATFORM (AEP) TO DEEPEN CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIPS AND ACCELERATE GROWTH THROUGH HIGHLY PERSONALIZED BUSINESS-TO-BUSINESS (B2B) MARKETING.
