QUALCOMM INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY 10 PERCENT. SAN DIEGO, MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: QCOM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A 10 PERCENT INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND WILL INCREASE FROM $0.68 TO $0.75 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK AND WILL BE EFFECTIVE FOR QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS PAYABLE AFTER MARCH 24, 2022.
