QUALCOMM APPOINTS JIM CATHEY AS CHIEF COMMERCIAL OFFICER. SAN DIEGO , APRIL 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF JIM CATHEY AS CHIEF COMMERCIAL OFFICER, QUALCOMM TECHNOLOGIES, INC., JOINING QUALCOMM'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE. CATHEY'S APPOINTMENT WILL BE EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY AND HE WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO CRISTIANO AMON, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF QUALCOMM INCORPORATED.
