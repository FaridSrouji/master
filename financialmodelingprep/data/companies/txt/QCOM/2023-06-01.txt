VEONEER COMPLETES SALE OF ACTIVE SAFETY BUSINESS TO MAGNA. STOCKHOLM , JUNE 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE AUTOMOTIVE TECHNOLOGY COMPANY VEONEER ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF THE SALE OF ITS ACTIVE SAFETY BUSINESS TO MAGNA, A MOBILITY TECHNOLOGY COMPANY AND ONE OF THE LARGEST SUPPLIERS IN THE AUTO INDUSTRY. THE TRANSACTION WAS FIRST ANNOUNCED ON DECEMBER 20, 2022.
