QUALCOMM SETS NEW GROWTH TARGETS AND FINANCIAL GUIDANCE THROUGH FISCAL 2024. SAN DIEGO, NOV. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: QCOM) TODAY SAID IT IS CONTINUING TO EXPAND ITS CHIPSET BUSINESS TO MEET THE GROWING OPPORTUNITY FOR ITS TECHNOLOGY AT ITS 2021 INVESTOR DAY. THE COMPANY EXPECTS ITS ADDRESSABLE OPPORTUNITY TO GROW FROM APPROXIMATELY $100 BILLION TODAY TO $700 BILLION IN THE NEXT DECADE, AS MORE DEVICES BECOME INTELLIGENTLY CONNECTED.
