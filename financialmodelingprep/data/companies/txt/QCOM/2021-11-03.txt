QUALCOMM EARNINGS RELEASE AVAILABLE ON COMPANY'S INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE. SAN DIEGO, NOV. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: QCOM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL 2021 THROUGH AN EARNINGS RELEASE THAT IS AVAILABLE ON THE QUALCOMM INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE AT HTTP://INVESTOR.QUALCOMM.COM/RESULTS.CFM. THE EARNINGS RELEASE WILL BE FURNISHED TO THE SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION (SEC) ON A FORM 8-K AND WILL BE AVAILABLE ON THE SEC WEBSITE AT HTTP://WWW.SEC.GOV.
