QUALCOMM COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF NUVIA. SAN DIEGO, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: QCOM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS SUBSIDIARY, QUALCOMM TECHNOLOGIES, INC., HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF THE WORLD-CLASS CPU AND TECHNOLOGY DESIGN COMPANY, NUVIA FOR $1.4 BILLION BEFORE WORKING CAPITAL AND OTHER ADJUSTMENTS. "THE WORLD-CLASS NUVIA TEAM ENHANCES OUR CPU ROADMAP, EXTENDING QUALCOMM'S LEADING TECHNOLOGY POSITION WITH THE WINDOWS, ANDROID AND CHROME ECOSYSTEMS," SAID CRISTIANO AMON, PRESIDENT AND CEO-ELECT, QUALCOMM INCORPORATED.
