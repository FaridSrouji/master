QUALCOMM ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SAN DIEGO , JAN. 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: QCOM) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.75 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON MARCH 23, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 2, 2023.
