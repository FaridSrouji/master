QUALCOMM ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SAN DIEGO , JULY 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: QCOM) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.75 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 22, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 1, 2022.
