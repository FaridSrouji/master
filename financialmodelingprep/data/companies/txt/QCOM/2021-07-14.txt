QUALCOMM ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SAN DIEGO, JULY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: QCOM) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 23, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 2, 2021. ABOUT QUALCOMM QUALCOMM IS THE WORLD'S LEADING WIRELESS TECHNOLOGY INNOVATOR AND THE DRIVING FORCE BEHIND THE DEVELOPMENT, LAUNCH, AND EXPANSION OF 5G.
