MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.315 PER SHARE. CHICAGO, FEB. 04, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.315 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON APRIL 14, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 31, 2021.
