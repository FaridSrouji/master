FEDERMAN & SHERWOOD INVESTIGATES MONDELEZ GROUP, LLC FOR DATA BREACH. OKLAHOMA CITY--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #DATABREACH--THE LAW FIRM OF FEDERMAN & SHERWOOD HAS INITIATED AN INVESTIGATION INTO MONDELEZ GROUP, LLC [NASDAQ: MDLZ] WITH RESPECT TO A DATA BREACH. ON JUNE 15, 2023, MONDELEZ GROUP, LLC, A MONDELEZ INTERNATIONAL LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY INFORMED 51,110 CURRENT AND FORMER EMPLOYEES THAT A THIRD PARTY WAS TRUSTED WITH THEIR PERSONAL IDENTIFYING INFORMATION (“PII”) AND WAS EXTRACTED DURING A DATA BREACH AFTER AN UNAUTHORIZED PARTY ACCESSED SENSITIVE EMPLOYEE DATA ENTRUST.
