MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL ACQUIRES HIGH-GROWTH EUROPEAN SNACKING COMPANY, CHIPITA S.A.. CHICAGO, MAY 26, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE CHIPITA S.A., A HIGH-GROWTH KEY PLAYER IN THE CENTRAL AND EASTERN EUROPEAN CROISSANTS AND BAKED SNACKS CATEGORY.
