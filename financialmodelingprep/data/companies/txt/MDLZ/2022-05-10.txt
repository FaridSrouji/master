MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL PROVIDES UPDATE ON LONG-TERM GROWTH STRATEGY AT 2022 INVESTOR DAY. CHICAGO, MAY 10, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY UNVEILED THE EVOLUTION OF ITS STRATEGY TO ACCELERATE GROWTH AND FOCUS ITS PORTFOLIO TO GENERATE STRONG, SUSTAINABLE SHAREHOLDER VALUE. AS A RESULT, THE COMPANY IS UPDATING ITS LONG-TERM ALGORITHM TO 3% - 5% ORGANIC NET REVENUE GROWTH, UP FROM PREVIOUS GUIDANCE OF 3% PLUS.
