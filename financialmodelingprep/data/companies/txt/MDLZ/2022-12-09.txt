MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.385 PER SHARE. CHICAGO, DEC. 09, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.385 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 13, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 30, 2022.
