MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL AGREES TO ACQUIRE RICOLINO, MEXICO'S LEADING CONFECTIONERY COMPANY, FROM GRUPO BIMBO. CHICAGO, APRIL 25, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH GRUPO BIMBO TO ACQUIRE ITS CONFECTIONERY BUSINESS, RICOLINO, FOR APPROXIMATELY $1.3 BILLION USD. RICOLINO IS A GROWING BUSINESS WITH A LEADING CONFECTIONERY POSITION IN MEXICO. THE ACQUISITION WILL TRANSFORM MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL'S BUSINESS IN THE HIGH-PRIORITY MEXICO MARKET TO ACCELERATE GROWTH AND SCALE IN CORE SNACKING CATEGORIES.
