HOLIDAY HELP: 1,000 D.C. FAMILIES TO RECEIVE FRESH FOOD FROM TRISCUIT AND THE PARTNERSHIP FOR HEALTHIER AMERICA. EAST HANOVER, N.J., DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, THE TRISCUIT BRAND AND PARTNERSHIP FOR A HEALTHIER AMERICA (PHA) ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF PHA'S GOOD FOOD FOR ALL PROGRAM TO BRING 600,000 SERVINGS OF FRESH VEGETABLES AND FRUITS TO FOOD-INSECURE FAMILIES IN WASHINGTON, D.C.
