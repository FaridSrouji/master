MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL APPOINTS ANN MUKHERJEE TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. CHICAGO, DEC. 13, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ANN MUKHERJEE, CHAIRWOMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF PERNOD RICARD NORTH AMERICA, TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2023.
