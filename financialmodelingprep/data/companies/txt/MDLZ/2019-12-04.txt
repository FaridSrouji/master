MONDELEZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 14, 2020. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MONDELEZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.285 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 14, 2020, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2019.
