MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF CHIPITA GLOBAL S.A., HIGH-GROWTH EUROPEAN LEADER IN CROISSANTS AND BAKED SNACKS. CHICAGO, JAN. 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF CHIPITA GLOBAL S.A., A HIGH-GROWTH LEADER IN THE CENTRAL AND EASTERN EUROPEAN CROISSANTS AND BAKED SNACKS CATEGORY.
