MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL AND OLAM FOOD INGREDIENTS ANNOUNCE PARTNERSHIP IN ASIA TO CREATE WORLD'S LARGEST SUSTAINABLE COMMERCIAL COCOA FARM. CHICAGO, APRIL 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ), A GLOBAL LEADER IN SNACKING, AND OLAM FOOD INGREDIENTS (OFI), A LEADING SUPPLIER OF COCOA BEANS AND COCOA INGREDIENTS, HAVE TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW COLLABORATION IN INDONESIA TO CREATE THE WORLD'S SINGLE LARGEST SUSTAINABLE COMMERCIAL COCOA FARM. THE MODEL BUILDS ON MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL'S EXPERIENCE WITH THE COMPANY'S SIGNATURE SUSTAINABLE SOURCING PROGRAM, COCOA LIFE, AND OFI'S AMBITION FOR SUSTAINABLE COCOA, COCOA COMPASS, TO TEST A SCALABLE APPROACH FOR THE FUTURE OF COMMERCIAL COCOA FARMING.
