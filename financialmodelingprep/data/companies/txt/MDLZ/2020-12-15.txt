MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL APPOINTS LAURA STEIN AS EVP, GENERAL COUNSEL, CORPORATE & LEGAL AFFAIRS. CHICAGO, DEC. 15, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT LAURA STEIN WILL BECOME EVP, GENERAL COUNSEL, CORPORATE & LEGAL AFFAIRS, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 11.
