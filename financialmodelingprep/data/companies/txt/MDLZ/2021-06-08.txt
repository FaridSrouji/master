TRISCUIT NAMES THE MISSING INGREDIENTS PROJECT'S FIRST GRANT RECIPIENTS IMPROVING ACCESS TO FRESH, AFFORDABLE, NUTRITIOUS FOOD IN FOOD DESERT COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE U.S. EAST HANOVER, N.J., JUNE 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, TRISCUIT, A BRAND KNOWN FOR ITS ICONIC WOVEN WHEAT CRACKERS, ANNOUNCES THE FIRST TWO CHANGEMAKERS, MAX KANIGER AND ASHA WALKER, TO JOIN THE MISSING INGREDIENTS PROJECT.
