MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION PRICES FOR 1.000% NOTES DUE 2022, 1.625% NOTES DUE 2023, 2.125% NOTES DUE 2023 AND 4.000% NOTES DUE 2024. CHICAGO, MARCH 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) (“MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE REDEMPTION PRICES FOR ITS OUTSTANDING 1.000% NOTES DUE 2022 (THE “1.000% NOTES”), 1.625% NOTES DUE 2023 (THE “1.625% NOTES”), 2.125% NOTES DUE 2023 (THE “2.125% NOTES”) AND 4.000% NOTES DUE 2024 (THE “4.000% NOTES” AND, TOGETHER WITH THE 1.000% NOTES, THE 1.625% NOTES AND THE 2.125% NOTES, THE “NOTES”).
