ENJOY LIFE NATURAL BRANDS, LLC EXPANDS VOLUNTARY RECALL OF SELECT BAKERY PRODUCTS DUE TO POTENTIAL PRESENCE OF FOREIGN MATERIAL. CHICAGO, JULY 20, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ENJOY LIFE NATURAL BRANDS LLC ANNOUNCED TODAY, OUT OF AN ABUNDANCE OF CAUTION, AN EXPANSION OF ITS JUNE 30TH VOLUNTARY RECALL, TO ADD ONE PRODUCT AND ADD ADDITIONAL BEST BY DATES FOR FOUR PRODUCTS TO THE LIST OF RECALLED PRODUCTS.  THIS RECALL IS DUE TO THE POTENTIAL PRESENCE OF HARD PLASTIC PIECES IN THE PRODUCTS.  THIS VOLUNTARY RECALL IS NOT RELATED TO AN ALLERGEN AND NO OTHER ENJOY LIFE PRODUCTS ARE AFFECTED BY THIS VOLUNTARY RECALL.  THE COMPANY BECAME AWARE OF THE ADDITIONAL ITEMS AS A RESULT OF ITS INTERNAL INVESTIGATION.
