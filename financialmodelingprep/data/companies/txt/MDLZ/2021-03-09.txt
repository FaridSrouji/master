MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL ACQUIRES AUSTRALIAN PREMIUM CRACKER COMPANY GOURMET FOOD HOLDINGS. CHICAGO, MARCH 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE GOURMET FOOD HOLDINGS PTY LTD, A LEADING AUSTRALIAN FOOD COMPANY WITH A STRONG POSITION IN THE ATTRACTIVE PREMIUM BISCUIT AND CRACKER CATEGORY. TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION WERE NOT DISCLOSED.
