MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL APPOINTS ERTHARIN COUSIN TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. CHICAGO, NOV. 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ERTHARIN COUSIN, FOUNDER, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF THE FOOD SYSTEMS OF THE FUTURE INSTITUTE, TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2022.
