MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL'S SNACKFUTURES OPENS APPLICATIONS FOR COLAB 2022 START-UP ENGAGEMENT PROGRAM. CHICAGO, NOV. 04, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SNACKFUTURES, THE MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC.
