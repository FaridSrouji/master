MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.385 PER SHARE. CHICAGO, MAY 17, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.385 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JULY 14, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 30, 2023.
