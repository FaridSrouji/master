TRISCUIT NAMES THE MISSING INGREDIENTS PROJECT'S FINAL 2021 GRANT RECIPIENTS. EAST HANOVER, N.J., NOV. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, TRISCUIT, A BRAND KNOWN FOR ITS ICONIC WOVEN WHEAT CRACKERS, ANNOUNCED JAMILA NORMAN OF PATCHWORK CITY FARMS AND JAMES KANOFF AND AIDAN REILLY OF THE FARMLINK PROJECT, HAVE JOINED THE MISSING INGREDIENTS PROJECT AS THE BRAND'S FINAL 2021 CHANGEMAKERS.
