GALLAGHER EARNS TOP SCORE IN HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION'S 2022 CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX. ROLLING MEADOWS, ILL., JAN. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GALLAGHER, A GLOBAL INSURANCE BROKERAGE, RISK MANAGEMENT AND CONSULTING SERVICES FIRM, PROUDLY ANNOUNCED THAT IT RECEIVED A SCORE OF 100 ON THE HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION'S 2022 CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX, THE NATION'S FOREMOST BENCHMARKING SURVEY AND REPORT MEASURING CORPORATE POLICIES AND PRACTICES RELATED TO LGBTQ+ WORKPLACE EQUALITY.
