GALLAGHER NAMES JAGGAER A BEST-IN-CLASS EMPLOYER. RESEARCH TRIANGLE, N.C.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--GALLAGHER, A GLOBAL INSURANCE BROKERAGE, RISK MANAGEMENT AND CONSULTING SERVICES FIRM SPECIALIZING IN HR CONSULTANCY, HAS NAMED JAGGAER A BEST-IN-CLASS EMPLOYER IN RECOGNITION OF ITS STRATEGIC INVESTMENT IN EMPLOYEE BENEFITS, COMPENSATION, RETIREMENT AND EMPLOYEE COMMUNICATION.
