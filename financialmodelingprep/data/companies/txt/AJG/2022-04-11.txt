ADVISOR GROUP WELCOMES GALLAGHER'S RETIREMENT PLAN CONSULTING PRACTICE TO ITS NETWORK. NEW MEMBER ADDS 90 ADVISORS, 50 SUPPORT STAFF TO INDEPENDENT WEALTH MANAGEMENT NETWORK PHOENIX , APRIL 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ADVISOR GROUP INC., THE NATION'S LARGEST NETWORK OF INDEPENDENT WEALTH MANAGEMENT FIRMS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ADDITION OF GALLAGHER'S RETIREMENT PLAN CONSULTING PRACTICE, A 90+ADVISOR TEAM, TO THE ADVISOR GROUP NETWORK. GALLAGHER JOINS ADVISOR GROUP THROUGH ITS AFFILIATION WITH NETWORK PARTNER TRIAD ADVISORS, A NETWORK OF FIRMS THAT ALSO INCLUDES FSC SECURITIES, ROYAL ALLIANCE ASSOCIATES INC., SAGEPOINT FINANCIAL, SECURITIES AMERICA AND WOODBURY FINANCIAL SERVICES.
