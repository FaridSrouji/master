FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,387.5 BILLION AT DECEMBER 31, 2022, COMPARED TO $1,410.6 BILLION AT NOVEMBER 30, 2022. THIS MONTH'S DECREASE IN AUM REFLECTED THE IMPACT OF NEGATIVE MARKETS, PARTIALLY OFFSET BY LONG-TERM NET INFLOWS.
