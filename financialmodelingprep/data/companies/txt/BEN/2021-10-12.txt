FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,530.1 BILLION AT SEPTEMBER 30, 2021, COMPARED TO $1,572.1 BILLION AT AUGUST 31, 2021. THIS MONTH'S DECREASE IN AUM PRIMARILY REFLECTED MARKET DEPRECIATION AND SLIGHT LONG-TERM NET OUTFLOWS, EXCLUDING A $2 BILLION FIXED INCOME INSTITUTIONAL REDEMPTION THAT HAD MINIMAL IMPACT ON REVENUE.
