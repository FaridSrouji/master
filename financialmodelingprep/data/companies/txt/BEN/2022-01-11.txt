FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,578.1 BILLION AT DECEMBER 31, 2021, COMPARED TO $1,546.8 BILLION AT NOVEMBER 30, 2021. THIS MONTH'S INCREASE IN AUM PRIMARILY REFLECTED LONG-TERM NET INFLOWS, THE ACQUISITION OF O'SHAUGHNESSY ASSET MANAGEMENT (OSAM), AND THE POSITIVE IMPACT OF MARKETS.
