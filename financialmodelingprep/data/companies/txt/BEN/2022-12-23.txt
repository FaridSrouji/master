UPDATE ON FRANKLIN FTSE RUSSIA ETF (FLRU). NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 23, 2022, THE FRANKLIN FTSE RUSSIA ETF (FLRU) HAS SUSPENDED THE RIGHT OF REDEMPTION OF ITS SHARES PURSUANT TO AN EXEMPTIVE ORDER ISSUED BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION IN ORDER TO PERMIT FLRU TO LIQUIDATE ITS PORTFOLIO.
