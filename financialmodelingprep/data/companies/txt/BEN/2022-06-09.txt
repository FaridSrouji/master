FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,445.9 BILLION AT MAY 31, 2022, COMPARED TO $1,455.8 BILLION AT APRIL 30, 2022. THIS MONTH'S DECREASE IN AUM PRIMARILY REFLECTED LONG-TERM NET OUTFLOWS, PARTIALLY OFFSET BY THE POSITIVE IMPACT OF MARKETS.
