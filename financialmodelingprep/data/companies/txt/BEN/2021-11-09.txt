FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,561.7 BILLION AT OCTOBER 31, 2021, COMPARED TO $1,530.1 BILLION AT SEPTEMBER 30, 2021. THIS MONTH'S INCREASE IN AUM PRIMARILY REFLECTED THE POSITIVE IMPACT OF MARKETS PARTIALLY OFFSET BY SLIGHT LONG-TERM NET OUTFLOWS.
