FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,557.3 BILLION AT JULY 31, 2021, COMPARED TO $1,552.1 BILLION AT JUNE 30, 2021. THIS MONTH'S INCREASE IN AUM PRIMARILY REFLECTED THE POSITIVE IMPACT OF MARKETS AND THE ACQUISITION OF DIAMOND HILL'S HIGH YIELD-FOCUSED, U.S. CORPORATE CREDIT MUTUAL FUNDS OF APPROXIMATELY $3.4 BILLION, PARTIALLY OFFSET BY LONG-TERM NET OUTFLOWS.
