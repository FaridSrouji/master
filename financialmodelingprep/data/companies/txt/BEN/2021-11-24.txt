NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ON NOVEMBER 19, 2021, THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF LEGG MASON ETF INVESTMENT TRUST, ON BEHALF OF THE LEGG MASON GLOBAL INFRASTRUCTURE ETF (THE “FUND”), APPROVED A PROPOSAL TO LIQUIDATE AND DISSOLVE THE FUND. THE LIQUIDATION IS ANTICIPATED TO OCCUR ON OR ABOUT FEBRUARY 3, 2022. AFTER THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 5, 2022, THE FUND WILL NO LONGER ACCEPT CREATION ORDERS.
