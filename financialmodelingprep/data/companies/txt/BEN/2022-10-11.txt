FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,297.4 BILLION AT SEPTEMBER 30, 2022, COMPARED TO $1,387.6 BILLION AT AUGUST 31, 2022. THIS MONTH'S DECREASE IN AUM REFLECTED THE IMPACT OF NEGATIVE MARKETS AND LONG-TERM NET OUTFLOWS.
