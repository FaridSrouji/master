RUBICON MINERALS CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED CAD 12 MILLION IN FUNDING FROM FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. AND OTHER INVESTORS. ON OCTOBER 10, 2019, RUBICON MINERALS CORPORATION (TSX:RMX) CLOSED THE TRANSACTION. THE COMPANY ISSUED 12,000,000 SHARES FOR GROSS PROCEEDS OF CAD 12,000,000. THE TRANSACTION INCLUDED PARTICIPATION FROM RETURNING INVESTOR FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (NYSE:BEN) FOR CAD 1,800,000 AND OTHER INVESTORS. FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (NYSE:BEN) INVESTED THROUGH ITS TWO FUNDS. THE COMPANY RECEIVED CAD 2,525,656.04 FROM 3 INVESTORS PURSUANT TO REGULATION D.
