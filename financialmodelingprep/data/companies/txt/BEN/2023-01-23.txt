FRANKLIN TEMPLETON ANNOUNCES PLANS TO CONVERT FRANKLIN FOCUSED GROWTH MUTUAL FUND TO AN ETF. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--FRANKLIN TEMPLETON ANNOUNCES PLANS TO CONVERT ITS FRANKLIN FOCUSED GROWTH MUTUAL FUND TO AN EXCHANGE-TRADED FUND (ETF) FOR U.S. INVESTORS. THE CONVERSION, WHICH IS SUBJECT TO SHAREHOLDER APPROVAL, IS EXPECTED TO BE EFFECTIVE IN THE FOURTH CALENDAR QUARTER OF 2023. IF APPROVED, THIS WILL BE THE FIRM'S THIRD MUTUAL FUND-TO-ETF CONVERSION.
