PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT ROLES CHANGING IN ROYCE PREMIER FUND. NEW YORK, FEB. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EFFECTIVE 4/1/22, STEVEN MCBOYLE AND LAUREN ROMEO, CFA® WILL BECOME CO-LEAD PORTFOLIO MANAGERS ON ROYCE PREMIER FUND, WHERE THEY HAVE BEEN PORTFOLIO MANAGERS SINCE 2016. EACH HAS BEEN INVOLVED IN THE FUND'S MANAGEMENT SINCE 2014 AND 2006, RESPECTIVELY.
