FRANKLIN TEMPLETON APPOINTS DEAN R. SACKETT III AS MANAGING DIRECTOR OF GOVERNMENT AFFAIRS. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. [NYSE:BEN], A GLOBAL INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT ORGANIZATION OPERATING AS FRANKLIN TEMPLETON, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DEAN R. SACKETT III AS MANAGING DIRECTOR OF GOVERNMENT AFFAIRS. IN THIS NEWLY CREATED ROLE, SACKETT WILL REPRESENT FRANKLIN TEMPLETON AND ITS SPECIALIST INVESTMENT MANAGERS ON A VARIETY OF U.S. POLICY MATTERS.
