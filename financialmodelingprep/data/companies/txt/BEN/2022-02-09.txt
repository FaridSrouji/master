FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,521.8 BILLION AT JANUARY 31, 2022, COMPARED TO $1,578.1 BILLION AT DECEMBER 31, 2021. THIS MONTH'S DECREASE IN AUM PRIMARILY REFLECTED THE NEGATIVE IMPACT OF MARKETS. LONG-TERM NET FLOWS WERE APPROXIMATELY FLAT.
