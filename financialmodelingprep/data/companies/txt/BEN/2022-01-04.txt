BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION OF AMERICA (“BDCA”) CHANGES NAME TO FRANKLIN BSP LENDING CORPORATION (“FBLC”). NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION OF AMERICA (“BDCA”) HAS ELECTED TO CHANGE THE NAME OF THE COMPANY TO FRANKLIN BSP LENDING CORPORATION (“FBLC”) EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2022. FBLC IS A BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT COMPANY THAT PRIMARILY UNDERWRITES LOANS TO MIDDLE MARKET CORPORATE BORROWERS. FBLC PROVIDES FLEXIBLE FINANCING SOLUTIONS TO A VARIETY OF BUSINESSES ACROSS A VARIETY OF INDUSTRIES. FBLC IS EXTERNALLY MANAGED BY BENEFIT STREET PARTNERS (“BSP”).
