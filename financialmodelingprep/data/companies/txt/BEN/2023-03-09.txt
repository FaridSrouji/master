FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,416.5 BILLION AT FEBRUARY 28, 2023, COMPARED TO $1,451.9 BILLION AT JANUARY 31, 2023. THIS MONTH'S DECREASE IN AUM REFLECTED THE IMPACT OF NEGATIVE MARKETS, PARTIALLY OFFSET BY LONG-TERM NET INFLOWS INCLUSIVE OF THE PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED $7.5 BILLION INSTITUTIONAL MANDATE INVESTED ACROSS FIXED INCOME STRATEGIES.
