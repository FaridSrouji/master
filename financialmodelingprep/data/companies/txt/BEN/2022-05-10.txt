FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,455.8 BILLION AT APRIL 30, 2022, COMPARED TO $1,477.5 BILLION AT MARCH 31, 2022. THIS MONTH'S DECREASE IN AUM PRIMARILY REFLECTED THE NEGATIVE IMPACT OF MARKETS AND LONG-TERM NET OUTFLOWS, PARTIALLY OFFSET BY THE APRIL 1ST ACQUISITION OF LEXINGTON PARTNERS, A GLOBAL LEADER IN SECONDARY PRIVATE EQUITY AND CO-INVESTMENTS.
