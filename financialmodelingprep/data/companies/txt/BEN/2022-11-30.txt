FRANKLIN TEMPLETON ACADEMY INTRODUCES ALTERNATIVES EDUCATION LEARNING PLATFORM. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE FRANKLIN TEMPLETON ACADEMY TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS ALTERNATIVES EDUCATION PROGRAM, A ROBUST LEARNING SUITE AIMED TO HELP FINANCIAL PROFESSIONALS EXPAND THEIR KNOWLEDGE OF ALTERNATIVE INVESTMENTS AND NAVIGATE THE GROWING SPACE WITH CONFIDENCE. THE PROGRAM OFFERS A COMPREHENSIVE CURRICULUM ON VARIOUS TYPES OF ALTERNATIVES, INCLUDING COURSES ON PRIVATE EQUITY, REAL ESTATE, PRIVATE CREDIT, INFRASTRUCTURE AND HEDGE STRATEGIES.
