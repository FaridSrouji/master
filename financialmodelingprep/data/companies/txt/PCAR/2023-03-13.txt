PACCAR RECOGNIZES 10 PPM QUALITY AWARD WINNERS. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACCAR ANNUALLY RECOGNIZES SUPPLIERS WHO EXCEED PACCAR'S 10 PPM QUALITY STANDARD, OR THE EQUIVALENT OF 10 DEFECTIVE PARTS FOR EVERY MILLION COMPONENTS SHIPPED TO PACCAR. TO QUALIFY, SUPPLIERS MUST ALSO MEET DEMANDING CRITERIA FOR CUSTOMER SUPPORT AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT. FOR 2022, PACCAR RECOGNIZES 453 SUPPLIERS IN 34 COUNTRIES ON FIVE CONTINENTS FOR ACHIEVING THIS HIGH LEVEL OF QUALITY PERFORMANCE.
