PACCAR BUSINESS UPDATE. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THERE IS CURRENTLY A SEMICONDUCTOR SHORTAGE THAT IS AFFECTING SOME INDUSTRIES INCLUDING THE AUTOMOTIVE SECTOR. THE SEMICONDUCTOR SHORTAGE HAS REDUCED PACCAR'S TRUCK DELIVERIES IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021 BY APPROXIMATELY 3,000 VEHICLES. PACCAR'S FIRST QUARTER 2021 TRUCK DELIVERIES ARE ESTIMATED TO BE APPROXIMATELY 42,000 VEHICLES, COMPARED TO 40,800 VEHICLES DELIVERED IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020.
