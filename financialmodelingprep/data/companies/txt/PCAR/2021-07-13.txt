PACCAR DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACCAR INC'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF THIRTY-FOUR CENTS ($.34) PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 8, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 18, 2021. PACCAR IS A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER IN THE DESIGN, MANUFACTURE AND CUSTOMER SUPPORT OF HIGH-QUALITY LIGHT-, MEDIUM- AND HEAVY-DUTY TRUCKS UNDER THE KENWORTH, PETERBILT AND DAF NAMEPLATES.
