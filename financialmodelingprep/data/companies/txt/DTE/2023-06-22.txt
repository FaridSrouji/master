DTE ENERGY NAMES DEBORAH BYERS TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. DETROIT, JUNE 22, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DETROIT, JUNE 22, 2023 — DTE ENERGY (NYSE: DTE) HAS NAMED DEBORAH BYERS, A RETIRED PARTNER FROM ERNST & YOUNG LLP TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. BYERS SERVED AS AMERICAS INDUSTRY LEADER OVERSEEING THE MARKETS AND GROWTH STRATEGY ACROSS ERNST & YOUNG'S PRIMARY INDUSTRY MARKETS. SHE HAS HELD SEVERAL SENIOR LEADERSHIP POSITIONS AT ERNST & YOUNG, INCLUDING SERVING AS ITS HOUSTON OFFICE MANAGING PARTNER AND U.S. ENERGY LEADER. HER NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL WORK IN THE ENERGY SECTOR GIVES HER IMPORTANT STRATEGIC INSIGHT INTO KEY ISSUES FACING DTE AND THE NATION AS WE TRANSITION TO CLEAN AND SUSTAINABLE ENERGY PRODUCTION.
