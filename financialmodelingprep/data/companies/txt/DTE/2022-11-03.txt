DTE ENERGY PROPOSES TRANSFORMATIONAL INVESTMENT IN MICHIGAN-MADE RENEWABLE ENERGY AND ACCELERATES EMISSION REDUCTIONS. DETROIT, NOV. 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DTE ENERGY (NYSE:DTE) TODAY RELEASED ITS CLEANVISION PLAN, A 20-YEAR PROPOSAL TO DRAMATICALLY TRANSFORM HOW THE COMPANY GENERATES ELECTRICITY AS PART OF ITS CLEAN ENERGY JOURNEY, ALL WHILE INVESTING IN A MODERN GRID THAT IS RELIABLE AND AFFORDABLE. THE PROPOSED PLAN INCREASES INVESTMENT IN SOLAR AND WIND ENERGY, ACCELERATES THE RETIREMENT OF COAL PLANTS, AND INCLUDES THE DEVELOPMENT OF NEW ENERGY STORAGE – ALL REINFORCING DTE'S COMMITMENT TO CLEANER ENERGY.
