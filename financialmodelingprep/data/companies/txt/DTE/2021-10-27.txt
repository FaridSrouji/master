DTE ENERGY REPORTS STRONG THIRD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS, RAISES 2021 GUIDANCE MIDPOINT, PROVIDES 2022 EARLY OUTLOOK, AND INCREASES DIVIDEND 7%. DETROIT, OCT. 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DTE ENERGY (NYSE:DTE) TODAY REPORTED THIRD QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS OF $25 MILLION, OR $0.13 PER DILUTED SHARE, COMPARED WITH $476 MILLION, OR $2.46 PER DILUTED SHARE IN 2020.
