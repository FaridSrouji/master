DTE ENERGY CLOSES YEAR WITH STRONG FINANCIAL RESULTS; WELL-POSITIONED FOR 2023. DETROIT, FEB. 23, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ENDING A YEAR FOCUSED ON DELIVERING CLEANER ENERGY, EXCELLENT CUSTOMER SERVICE AND A REWARDING WORKPLACE EXPERIENCE, DTE ENERGY (NYSE:DTE) TODAY REPORTED 2022 EARNINGS FOR THE YEAR OF $1.1 BILLION, OR $5.52 PER DILUTED SHARE, COMPARED WITH $907 MILLION, OR $4.67 PER DILUTED SHARE IN 2021.
