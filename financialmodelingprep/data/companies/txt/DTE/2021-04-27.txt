DTE ENERGY REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2021 RESULTS, CONTINUES PROGRESS IN SUSTAINING COMMUNITIES. DETROIT, APRIL 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DTE ENERGY (NYSE:DTE) TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS OF $397 MILLION, OR $2.05 PER DILUTED SHARE, COMPARED WITH $340 MILLION, OR $1.76 PER DILUTED SHARE IN 2020.
