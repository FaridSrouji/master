DTE ENERGY CHAIRMAN GERRY ANDERSON TO RETIRE; PRESIDENT AND CEO JERRY NORCIA IS ELECTED DTE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD. DETROIT, MARCH 10, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DETROIT, MARCH 10, 2022 — DTE ENERGY EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN GERRY ANDERSON WILL RETIRE ON JUNE 30 AFTER A 29-YEAR CAREER WITH THE COMPANY. CURRENT DTE PRESIDENT AND CEO JERRY NORCIA WILL ASSUME THE ADDITIONAL ROLE OF CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD EFFECTIVE UPON THE EXPIRATION OF ANDERSON'S CURRENT TERM ON MAY 5, 2022.
