DTE ENERGY ANNOUNCES INTENT TO SPIN-OFF MIDSTREAM BUSINESS. U NLOCK ING SIGNIFICANT SHAREHOLDER VALUE THROUGH SPIN-OFF OF MIDSTREAM
DTE ENERGY REPORTS STRONG THIRD-QUARTER 2020 RESULTS; COMPANY INCREASES 2020 GUIDANCE, PROVIDES 2021 EARLY OUTLOOK, AND INCREASES DIVIDEND 7%. DETROIT, OCT. 27, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DTE ENERGY (NYSE: DTE) TODAY REPORTED THIRD QUARTER 2020 EARNINGS OF $476 MILLION, OR $2.46 PER DILUTED SHARE, COMPARED WITH $319 MILLION, OR $1.73 PER DILUTED SHARE IN 2019.
