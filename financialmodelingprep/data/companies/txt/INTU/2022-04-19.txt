CERITY TEAMS UP WITH INTUIT QUICKBOOKS TO HELP PROTECT SMALL BUSINESSES WITH DIGITAL WORKERS' COMPENSATION INSURANCE COVERAGE. AUSTIN, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CERITY®, A DIGITAL PROVIDER OF WORKERS' COMPENSATION INSURANCE POLICIES, HAS TEAMED UP WITH INTUIT (NASDAQ: INTU) TO PROVIDE QUICKBOOKS SMALL BUSINESS CUSTOMERS WITH MORE WAYS TO PROTECT THEIR EMPLOYEES AND BUSINESSES. CERITY IS PART OF EMPLOYERS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: EIG). THE U.S. HAS EXPERIENCED A SURGE IN SMALL BUSINESS FORMATION IN RECENT YEARS, HITTING A RECORD HIGH IN 2021.
