BLOOMERANG ACCELERATES NONPROFITS' ABILITY TO KEEP FUNDRAISING AND FINANCIAL OPERATIONS IN HARMONY. BLOOMERANG AND   INTUIT QUICKBOOKS COLLABORATION HELPS CREATE FINANCIAL TRANSPARENCY FOR NONPROFITS INDIANAPOLIS , DEC. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BLOOMERANG , THE COMPLETE DONOR RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT SOLUTION FOR THOUSANDS OF SMALL AND MEDIUM-SIZED NONPROFITS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SIGNED A QUICKBOOKS SOLUTION PROVIDER SALES AND MARKETING AGREEMENT WITH INTUIT  (NASDAQ: INTU), THE GLOBAL FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM THAT MAKES  TURBOTAX ,  CREDIT KARMA ,  QUICKBOOKS , AND  MAILCHIMP . THIS RELATIONSHIP BRINGS A NEW LEVEL OF VISIBILITY AND RECONCILIATION BETWEEN BLOOMERANG'S DONOR RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE AND INTUIT QUICKBOOKS TO HELP STREAMLINE NONPROFITS' ABILITY TO EFFECTIVELY MANAGE THE ENTIRE CONSTITUENT ECOSYSTEM.
