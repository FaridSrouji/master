NEW INTUIT QUICKBOOKS SURVEY REVEALS INCREASED CONSUMER APPETITE TO SHOP SMALL THIS HOLIDAY SEASON. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEW SURVEY DATA RELEASED TODAY FROM INTUIT (NASDAQ: INTU) QUICKBOOKS REVEALS THAT 93% OF U.S. SHOPPERS SAY SUPPORTING SMALL BUSINESSES THIS UPCOMING HOLIDAY SEASON IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN EVER BECAUSE OF THE PANDEMIC. FURTHERMORE, NEARLY HALF OF CONSUMERS (48%) ARE MORE LIKELY TO TRY NEW BUSINESSES THAN THEY WERE PRE-PANDEMIC, PRESENTING A LARGE OPPORTUNITY FOR RETAIL-BASED SMALL BUSINESSES LOOKING TO MAXIMIZE THEIR HOLIDAY SALES.
