INTUIT BRINGS RENEWABLE ENERGY AND JOB OPPORTUNITIES TO US COMMUNITIES WITH INNOVATIVE SOLAR PROJECTS. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTUIT INC. (NASDAQ: INTU), THE GLOBAL FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM THAT MAKES TURBOTAX, CREDIT KARMA, QUICKBOOKS, AND MAILCHIMP, IS TACKLING ITS CLIMATE-POSITIVE COMMITMENT BY BRINGING RENEWABLE ENERGY AND MEANINGFUL JOBS TO UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES IN THE US. INTUIT HAS CREATED A MODEL FOR COMMUNITIES TO OFFER NEW CAREER PATHWAYS AND EDUCATION OPPORTUNITIES, WHILE ALSO HELPING REDUCE CARBON EMISSIONS IN THE TRANSITION FROM FOSSIL FUELS TO CLEAN ENERGY.
