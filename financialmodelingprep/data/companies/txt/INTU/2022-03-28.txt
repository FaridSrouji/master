INTUIT RESPONDS TO COMPLAINT FROM U.S. FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTUIT INC. (NASDAQ: INTU), THE GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM THAT MAKES TURBOTAX, QUICKBOOKS, MINT, CREDIT KARMA AND MAILCHIMP, ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL VIGOROUSLY CHALLENGE A COMPLAINT FILED TODAY IN THE U.S. DISTRICT COURT IN THE NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA BY THE U.S. FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION (FTC).
