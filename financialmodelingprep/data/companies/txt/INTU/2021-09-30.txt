INTUIT HOSTS VIRTUAL INVESTOR DAY, REAFFIRMS FIRST-QUARTER AND FISCAL 2022 GUIDANCE. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTUIT INC. (NASDAQ: INTU) REAFFIRMED ITS FINANCIAL GUIDANCE FOR THE FIRST QUARTER AND FULL FISCAL YEAR 2022 IN CONJUNCTION WITH ITS VIRTUAL INVESTOR DAY, BEING HELD TODAY AT 8:00 A.M. PACIFIC TIME. THE COMPANY'S FISCAL YEAR RUNS FROM AUG. 1 TO JULY 31. INTUIT LEADERS WILL DISCUSS THE COMPANY'S PLAN TO ACCELERATE INNOVATION AND DRIVE DURABLE GROWTH.
