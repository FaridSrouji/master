PERKINELMER RECEIVES FDA EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR RESPIRATORY SARS-COV-2 PANEL. WALTHAM, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PERKINELMER, INC. (NYSE: PKI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ISSUED EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) FOR THE PKAMP™ RESPIRATORY SARS-COV-2 RT-PCR PANEL 1 ASSAY. QUALIFIED LABORATORIES CAN IMMEDIATELY BEGIN USING THIS SINGLE TEST FOR THE SIMULTANEOUS QUALITATIVE DETECTION AND DIFFERENTIATION OF SARS-COV-2, INFLUENZA A, INFLUENZA B AND RESPIRATORY SYNCYTIAL VIRUS (RSV) ISOLATED FROM NASOPHARYNGEAL SWABS, ANTERIOR NASAL SWABS
