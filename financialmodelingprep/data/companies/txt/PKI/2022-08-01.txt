PERKINELMER ANNOUNCES FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022. WALTHAM, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PERKINELMER, INC. (NYSE: PKI), A GLOBAL LEADER COMMITTED TO INNOVATING FOR A HEALTHIER WORLD, TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER ENDED JULY 3, 2022. THE COMPANY REPORTED GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $1.42, AS COMPARED TO GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $2.19 IN THE SAME PERIOD A YEAR AGO. GAAP REVENUE FOR THE QUARTER WAS $1.23 BILLION, AS COMPARED TO $1.23 BILLION IN THE SAME PERIOD A YEAR AGO.
