EUROIMMUN ANTI-SARS-COV-2 S1 CURVE ELISA RECEIVES FDA EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION. WALTHAM, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EUROIMMUN, A PERKINELMER, INC. COMPANY (NYSE: PKI), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION HAS PROVIDED EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) FOR ITS ANTI-SARS-COV-2 S1 CURVETM ELISA (IGG). THIS ASSAY ALLOWS FOR THE QUALITATIVE AND SEMI-QUANTITATIVE DETECTION OF IGG ANTIBODIES FORMED AGAINST THE SARS-COV-2 S1 ANTIGEN, IN HUMAN SERUM AND PLASMA.
