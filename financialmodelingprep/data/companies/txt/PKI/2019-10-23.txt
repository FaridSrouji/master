PERKINELMER, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 7, 2020. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PERKINELMER, INC. DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.07 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK ON OCTOBER 24, 2019. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 7, 2020 TO ALL SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 17, 2020.
