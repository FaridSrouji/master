PERKINELMER BOARD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. WALTHAM, MASS.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PERKINELMER, INC. (NYSE: PKI) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.07 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MAY 12, 2023 TO ALL SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 21, 2023.
