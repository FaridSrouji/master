PERKINELMER ANNOUNCES FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR OF 2022. WALTHAM, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PERKINELMER, INC. (NYSE: PKI), A GLOBAL LEADER COMMITTED TO INNOVATING FOR A HEALTHIER WORLD, TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR ENDED JANUARY 1, 2023. FOURTH QUARTER 2022 THE COMPANY REPORTED GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $0.85, AS COMPARED TO GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $1.45 IN THE SAME PERIOD A YEAR AGO.
