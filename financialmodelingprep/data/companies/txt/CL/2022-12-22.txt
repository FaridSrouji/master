COLGATE-PALMOLIVE RECOGNIZED BY S&P DOW JONES INDICES & CDP FOR ITS SUSTAINABILITY EFFORTS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--COLGATE-PALMOLIVE HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDICES FOR THE SIXTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, AS WELL AS TO CDP'S CLIMATE CHANGE AND WATER SECURITY A LISTS FOR THE THIRD YEAR RUNNING. THESE ACHIEVEMENTS RECOGNIZE COLGATE'S ONGOING COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABILITY LEADERSHIP, SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES AND TRANSPARENCY ABOUT ITS SUSTAINABILITY EFFORTS.
