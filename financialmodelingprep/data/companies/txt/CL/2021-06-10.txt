COLGATE DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF COLGATE-PALMOLIVE COMPANY (NYSE:CL) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.45 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON AUGUST 16, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 21, 2021. THE COMPANY HAS PAID UNINTERRUPTED DIVIDENDS ON ITS COMMON STOCK SINCE 1895. *** COLGATE-PALMOLIVE COMPANY IS A CARING, INNOVATIVE GROWTH COMPANY REIMAGINING A HEALTHIER FUTURE FOR ALL PEOPLE, THEIR PETS AND OUR PLANET.
