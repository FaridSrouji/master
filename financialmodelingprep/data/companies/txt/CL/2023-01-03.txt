COLGATE-PALMOLIVE COMPANY APPOINTS LORRIE NORRINGTON  AS LEAD INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR OF THE BOARD. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--COLGATE-PALMOLIVE COMPANY (NYSE: CL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED LORRIE NORRINGTON AS LEAD INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR, EFFECTIVE MARCH 1, 2023. MS. NORRINGTON WILL SUCCEED STEPHEN I. SADOVE, THE COMPANY'S CURRENT LEAD INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR. MR. SADOVE WILL REMAIN ON THE AUDIT AND PERSONNEL AND ORGANIZATION COMMITTEES OF THE COLGATE BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
