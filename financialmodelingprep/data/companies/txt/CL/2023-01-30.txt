COLGATE ELECTS STEVE CAHILLANE TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--COLGATE-PALMOLIVE COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT STEVE CAHILLANE, CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF KELLOGG COMPANY, HAS BEEN ELECTED TO COLGATE'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 1, 2023.
