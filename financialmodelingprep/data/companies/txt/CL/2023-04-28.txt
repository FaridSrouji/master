COLGATE ANNOUNCES 1ST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COLGATE-PALMOLIVE COMPANY (NYSE:CL): NET SALES INCREASED 8.5%; ORGANIC SALES* INCREASED 10.0% WITH GROWTH IN EVERY DIVISION AND IN ALL FOUR CATEGORIES GAAP EPS DECLINED 32% TO $0.45; BASE BUSINESS EPS* DECLINED 1% TO $0.73 GAAP GROSS PROFIT MARGIN AND BASE BUSINESS GROSS PROFIT MARGIN* BOTH DECREASED 160 BASIS POINTS TO 56.9%, INCLUDING A NEGATIVE 90 BASIS POINT IMPACT FROM PRIVATE LABEL SALES RESULTING FROM THE PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED ACQUISITIONS OF PET FOOD BUSINESSES.
