COLGATE ANNOUNCES 4TH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2022 RESULTS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--COLGATE-PALMOLIVE COMPANY (NYSE:CL) TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2022. NOEL WALLACE, CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, COMMENTED, “WE ARE VERY PLEASED TO HAVE FINISHED 2022 WITH A CONTINUATION OF OUR STRONG GROWTH MOMENTUM, AS 2022 WAS OUR FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR DELIVERING ORGANIC SALES GROWTH AT OR ABOVE OUR 3% TO 5% LONG-TERM TARGETED RANGE. NET SALES INCREASED 5.0% IN THE FOURTH QUARTER, AND ORGANIC SALES GREW 8.5% WITH GROWTH IN EVERY DIVISION AND IN ALL FOUR OF OUR CATEGORIES, DESPITE CHALLENGING MACROECONOMIC CONDITIONS WORLDWIDE.
