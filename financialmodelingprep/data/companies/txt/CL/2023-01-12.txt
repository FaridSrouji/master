COLGATE DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF COLGATE-PALMOLIVE COMPANY (NYSE:CL) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.47 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 14, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JANUARY 23, 2023. THE COMPANY HAS PAID UNINTERRUPTED DIVIDENDS ON ITS COMMON STOCK SINCE 1895.
