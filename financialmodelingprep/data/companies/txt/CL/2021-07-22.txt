COLGATE RECOMMENDS SHAREHOLDERS REJECT “MINI-TENDER” OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT CORPORATION. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COLGATE-PALMOLIVE COMPANY (“COLGATE”) HAS RECEIVED NOTIFICATION OF AN UNSOLICITED “MINI-TENDER” OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT CORPORATION (“TRC”) TO PURCHASE UP TO 1.5 MILLION SHARES, OR APPROXIMATELY 0.18% OF THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF COLGATE AS OF MARCH 31, 2021, AT A PRICE OF $80.63 PER SHARE IN CASH. TRC'S OFFER PRICE IS 4.46% LESS THAN THE $84.39 CLOSING PRICE PER SHARE OF COLGATE'S COMMON STOCK ON JULY 16, 2021, THE LAST TRADING DAY BEFORE THE MINI-TENDE
