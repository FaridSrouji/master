VORNADO COMPLETES SALE OF LONG ISLAND CITY OFFICE BUILDING FOR $172.8 MILLION. NEW YORK, JULY 05, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VORNADO REALTY TRUST (NYSE:VNO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SALE OF THE CENTER BUILDING, AN EIGHT-STORY 498,000 SQUARE FOOT OFFICE BUILDING LOCATED AT 33-00 NORTHERN BOULEVARD IN LONG ISLAND CITY, NEW YORK, FOR $172.8 MILLION. THE FINANCIAL STATEMENT GAIN, WHICH WILL BE RECOGNIZED IN THE SECOND QUARTER, IS ESTIMATED TO BE $15 MILLION. THE TAX GAIN IS ESTIMATED TO BE $74 MILLION.
