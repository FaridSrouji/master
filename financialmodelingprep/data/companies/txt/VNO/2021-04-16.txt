VORNADO EXTENDS ONE OF ITS TWO REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITIES. NEW YORK, APRIL 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VORNADO REALTY TRUST (NYSE:VNO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT VORNADO REALTY L.P., THE OPERATING PARTNERSHIP THROUGH WHICH VORNADO REALTY TRUST CONDUCTS ITS BUSINESS, HAS EXTENDED ONE OF ITS TWO UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITIES TO APRIL 2026 (AS FULLY EXTENDED). THE INTEREST RATE ON THE EXTENDED FACILITY WAS LOWERED TO LIBOR PLUS 90 BASIS POINTS FROM LIBOR PLUS 100 BASIS POINTS.   THE FACILITY FEE REMAINS AT 20 BASIS POINTS.
