ANTHEM TO HOST VIRTUAL INVESTOR DAY CONFERENCE ON MARCH 3, 2021. INDIANAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ANTHEM, INC. (NYSE: ANTM) WILL HOST ITS VIRTUAL INVESTOR DAY CONFERENCE ON MARCH 3, 2021. THE EVENT WILL FEATURE PRESENTATIONS AND A Q&A SESSION WITH THE COMPANY'S LEADERSHIP TEAM. THE CONFERENCE WILL BE WEBCAST LIVE AND ALL INTERESTED PARTIES ARE INVITED TO ACCESS THE WEBCAST AT WWW.ANTHEMINC.COM UNDER THE “INVESTORS” LINK. THE CONFERENCE WILL BEGIN AT 8:30 A.M.
