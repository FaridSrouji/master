WELSH, CARSON, ANDERSON & STOWE ACQUIRES MAJORITY STAKE IN LIBERTY DENTAL PLAN CORPORATION. IRVINE, CALIF. & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LIBERTY DENTAL PLAN CORPORATION (LIBERTY) AND WELSH, CARSON, ANDERSON & STOWE (WCAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PARTIES HAVE ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT THROUGH WHICH WCAS WILL MAKE AN EQUITY INVESTMENT AND BECOME THE MAJORITY INVESTOR IN LIBERTY. ANTHEM, INC. (NYSE: ANTM), A LEADING HEALTH COMPANY AND A LIBERTY CUSTOMER SINCE 2010, WILL ALSO BECOME A MINORITY INVESTOR. TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT WERE NOT DISCLOSED.
