FASTENAL NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S SAFEST COMPANIES BY EHS TODAY. WINONA, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SINCE 2002, EHS TODAY HAS RECOGNIZED JUST OVER 250 U.S. FIRMS AS AMERICA'S SAFEST COMPANIES. TEN ORGANIZATIONS RECENTLY JOINED THIS SELECT GROUP. ONE OF THEM WAS FASTENAL COMPANY (NASDAQ: FAST). "I WANT TO SAY THANK YOU TO EHS TODAY FOR THIS HONOR," SAID FASTENAL CEO AND PRESIDENT DAN FLORNESS.
