FASTENAL COMPANY ANNOUNCES CASH DIVIDEND. WINONA, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FASTENAL COMPANY (NASDAQ:FAST) REPORTED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.31 PER SHARE TO BE PAID IN CASH ON MARCH 2, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 2, 2022. EXCEPT FOR SHARE AND PER SHARE INFORMATION, DOLLAR AMOUNTS ARE STATED IN MILLIONS.
