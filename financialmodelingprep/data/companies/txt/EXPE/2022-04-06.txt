EXPEDIA RELEASES A SUMMER TRAVEL CHEAT SHEET, REVEALING CHEAPEST TIMES TO FLY. SEATTLE , APRIL 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHILE SPRING BREAK HAS YET TO WRAP FOR SOME STATES, MANY U.S. TRAVELERS ARE ALREADY PLANNING THEIR SUMMER VACATIONS. ACCORDING TO EXPEDIA® DATA, SEARCHES FOR SUMMER GETAWAYS HAVE STEADILY INCREASED OVER THE LAST MONTH, WITH MANY TOP DESTINATIONS SEEING DOUBLE-DIGIT INCREASES WEEK-OVER-WEEK IN MARCH.
