SUMMER VACATIONS JUST GOT MORE AFFORDABLE WITH SOFI TRAVEL POWERED BY EXPEDIA GROUP. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SOFI TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (“SOFI”), THE DIGITAL PERSONAL FINANCE COMPANY, AND EXPEDIA GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING TRAVELER TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM, TODAY ANNOUNCED “SOFI TRAVEL POWERED BY EXPEDIA”1, A NEW WAY FOR SOFI MEMBERS TO SAVE MONEY AND EARN REWARDS WHILE TRAVELING. SOFI MEMBERS WILL BE ABLE TO BOOK FLIGHTS, HOTELS, CAR RENTALS, TRAVEL PACKAGES, AND EXPERIENCES VIA THE SOFI TRAVEL PORTAL, AND WILL BENEFIT FROM DISCOUNTS ON BOOKINGS WITH SOFI MEMBER PRICES, INCLUD.
