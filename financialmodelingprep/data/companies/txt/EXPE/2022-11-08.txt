BREX CO-FOUNDER HENRIQUE DUBUGRAS JOINS EXPEDIA GROUP BOARD OF DIRECTORS. SAN FRANCISCO , NOV. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BREX TODAY ANNOUNCED HENRIQUE DUBUGRAS, CO-FOUNDER AND CO-CEO OF BREX, HAS BEEN ELECTED TO EXPEDIA GROUP'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. AS A BOARD MEMBER, DUBUGRAS WILL HELP THE COMPANY EXPAND ITS MISSION OF POWERING GLOBAL TRAVEL FOR EVERYONE, EVERYWHERE.
