AMERICAN AIRLINES AND EXPEDIA GROUP OFFER TRAVELERS MORE CHOICE VIA SCALABLE DIRECT NDC CONNECTION. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRAVELERS BOOKING AMERICAN AIRLINES FLIGHTS ON EXPEDIA GROUP SITES1 NOW HAVE MORE OPTIONS TO CUSTOMIZE THEIR TRAVEL AND CHOOSE ELEVATED OFFERS LIKE MAIN PLUS, FOLLOWING A RENEWED PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN THE TWO COMPANIES. THE FARE PRODUCTS ARE NOW AVAILABLE THANKS TO A DIRECT CONNECTION THROUGH NEW DISTRIBUTION CAPABILITY (NDC) TECHNOLOGY, MAKING ALL OF AMERICAN'S OFFERS AVAILABLE TO TRAVELERS ON EXPEDIA GROUP SITES.
