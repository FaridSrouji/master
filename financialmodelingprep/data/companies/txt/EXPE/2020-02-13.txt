EXPEDIA GROUP EXEC SAYS CORONAVIRUS OUTBREAK IS ADDING FURTHER PRESSURE ON TOP AND BOTTOM LINE IN Q1 - CONF CALL. EXPEDIA GROUP EXEC - "OF COURSE, THERE IS THE CORONAVIRUS AND A TERRIBLE THING FOR HUMANITY AND ALSO A NOT VERY GOOD THING FOR OUR BUSINESS" - CONF CALL.EXPEDIA GROUP EXEC SAYS CO HAS A GOOD LINE OF SIGHT ON CORONAVIRUS IMPACT IN Q1, BUT IN TERMS OF DURATION AND DEPTH OF IMPACT IT IS HARD TO PREDICT - CONF CALL.EXPEDIA GROUP EXEC SAYS AFTER RUNNING A NUMBER OF SCENARIOS ON THE VIRUS BASED ON HISTORIC PATTERNS, CO IS CONFIDENT IT WILL GET INTO DOUBLE DIGITS ON 2020 ADJUSTED EBITDA GROWTH.EXPEDIA GROUP EXEC SAYS LOOKING AT Q1 SPECIFICALLY CO EXPECTS ADJUSTED EBITDA TO BE DOWN SUBSTANTIALLY.EXPEDIA GROUP EXEC SAYS THE CORONAVIRUS OUTBREAK IS ADDING FURTHER PRESSURE ON THE TOP AND BOTTOM LINE IN Q1.EXPEDIA GROUP EXEC SAYS BASED ON CURRENT TRENDS, CO EXPECTS ABOUT $30 MILLION TO $40 MILLION IMPACT TO ADJUSTED EBITDA IN Q1, AND SOME IMPACT BEYOND Q1 AND 2020.EXPEDIA GROUP EXEC ON CORONAVIRUS - "I THINK IT'S GOING MUCH BEYOND IT. I THINK PEOPLE ARE WORRIED... I MEAN THEY ARE ALL OVER THE PLACE".EXPEDIA GROUP EXEC SAYS CORONAVIRUS IMPACT WILL GO BEYOND ASIA.EXPEDIA EXEC ON CORONAVIRUS - "IF YOU THINK ABOUT HOW IT HAS AFFECTED THE BUSINESS OVER TIME, IT STARTED IN MAINLAND CHINA DOMESTIC, THEN INBOUND/OUTBOUND, THEN IT STARTED GOING THROUGH ASIA PACIFIC".
