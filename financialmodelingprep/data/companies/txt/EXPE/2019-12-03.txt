EXPEDIA GROUP ANNOUNCES EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP CHANGES. EXPEDIA GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCED THE RESIGNATION OF MARK OKERSTROM AS CEO AND A MEMBER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND CFO ALAN PICKERILL, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. BARRY DILLER, CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, AND VICE CHAIRMAN PETER KERN, DIRECTOR OF EXPEDIA, WILL OVERSEE THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM, MANAGING DAY-TO-DAY OPERATIONS, WHILE THE BOARD DETERMINES THE LONG-TERM LEADERSHIP OF THE COMPANY. ERIC HART, THE COMPANY'S CHIEF STRATEGY OFFICER, WILL SERVE AS ACTING CFO. ARIANE GORIN, WHO MOST RECENTLY SERVED AS PRESIDENT OF EXPEDIA PARTNER SOLUTIONS, IS BEING PROMOTED AND WILL HAVE AN EXPANDED ROLE AS PRESIDENT, EXPEDIA BUSINESS SERVICES. KERN WAS MOST RECENTLY CEO AND BOARD MEMBER OF TRIBUNE MEDIA COMPANY AND IS MANAGING PARTNER OF INTERMEDIA PARTNERS VII, LP, A PRIVATE EQUITY FIRM. MR. KERN ALSO SERVES AS CHAIRMAN OF HEMISPHERE MEDIA GROUP, INC., AND IS ON THE BOARD OF TRIVAGO N.V.
