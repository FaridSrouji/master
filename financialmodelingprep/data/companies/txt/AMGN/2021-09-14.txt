AMGEN AND THE LUNDQUIST INSTITUTE ANNOUNCE THAT DIADEM THERAPEUCTICS WILL RECEIVE THE FIRST AMGEN GOLDEN TICKET TO BIOLABS LA. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., SEPT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) AND THE LUNDQUIST INSTITUTE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DIADEM BIOTHERAPEUTICS, INC., HAS BEEN AWARDED THE FIRST AMGEN GOLDEN TICKET IN SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA.
