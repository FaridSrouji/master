AMGN INVESTORS HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD AMGEN INC. SECURITIES FRAUD LAWSUIT. LOS ANGELES , APRIL 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP ("GPM") ANNOUNCES THAT INVESTORS WITH SUBSTANTIAL LOSSES HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD THE SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST AMGEN INC. ("AMGEN" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: AMGN).
