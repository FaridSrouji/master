ONGOING INVESTIGATION REMINDER: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN AMGEN INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - MAY 4, 2022) - THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, ANNOUNCES THAT IT IS INVESTIGATING CLAIMS ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS OF AMGEN INC. ("AMGEN" OR "THE COMPANY") (NASDAQ: AMGN) FOR VIOLATIONS OF THE SECURITIES LAWS.THE INVESTIGATION FOCUSES ON WHETHER THE COMPANY ISSUED FALSE AND/OR MISLEADING STATEMENTS AND/OR FAILED TO DISCLOSE INFORMATION PERTINENT TO INVESTORS.
