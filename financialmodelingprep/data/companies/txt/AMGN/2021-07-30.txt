AMGEN ANNOUNCES 2021 THIRD QUARTER DIVIDEND. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., JULY 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A $1.76 PER SHARE DIVIDEND FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021.
