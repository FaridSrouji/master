AMGEN TO ACQUIRE RODEO THERAPEUTICS CORPORATION. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF. AND SEATTLE, MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN INC. (NASDAQ:AMGN) AND RODEO THERAPEUTICS CORPORATION (RODEO) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH AMGEN WILL ACQUIRE RODEO, A PRIVATELY HELD BIOPHARMACEUTICAL COMPANY BASED IN SEATTLE THAT DEVELOPS SMALL-MOLECULE THERAPIES DESIGNED TO PROMOTE REGENERATION AND REPAIR OF MULTIPLE TISSUES.
