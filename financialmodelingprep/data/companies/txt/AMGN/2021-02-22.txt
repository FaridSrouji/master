AMGEN SUBMITS SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION FOR OTEZLA® (APREMILAST) FOR ADULTS WITH MILD-TO-MODERATE PLAQUE PSORIASIS. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., FEB. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ:AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SUBMISSION OF A SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION (SNDA) TO THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR OTEZLA® (APREMILAST) FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULTS WITH MILD-TO-MODERATE PLAQUE PSORIASIS...
