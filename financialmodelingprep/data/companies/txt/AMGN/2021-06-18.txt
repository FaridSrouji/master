DECODE GENETICS: PREDICTING THE PROBABILITY OF DEATH. REYKJAVIK, ICELAND, JUNE 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SCIENTISTS FROM DECODE GENETICS HAVE DEVELOPED A PREDICTOR BASED ON PROTEIN MEASUREMENTS IN BLOOD SAMPLES THAT PREDICTS  THE TIME TO ALL-CAUSE  DEATH BETTER THAN TRADITIONAL RISK FACTORS. IN A PAPER PUBLISHED TODAY IN COMMUNICATIONS BIOLOGY, SCIENTISTS FROM DECODE GENETICS, A SUBSIDIARY OF AMGEN, DESCRIBE HOW THEY DEVELOPED  PREDICTOR OF HOW MUCH IS LEFT OF THE LIFE OF A PERSON.
