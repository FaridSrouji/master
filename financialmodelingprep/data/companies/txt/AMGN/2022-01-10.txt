EUROPEAN COMMISSION APPROVES LUMYKRAS® (SOTORASIB) FOR PATIENTS WITH KRAS G12C-MUTATED ADVANCED NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., JAN. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION (EC) HAS GRANTED CONDITIONAL MARKETING AUTHORIZATION FOR LUMYKRAS® (SOTORASIB), A FIRST-IN-CLASS KRASG12C INHIBITOR, FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULTS WITH ADVANCED NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (NSCLC) WITH KRAS G12C MUTATION AND WHO HAVE PROGRESSED AFTER AT LEAST ONE PRIOR LINE OF SYSTEMIC THERAPY.
