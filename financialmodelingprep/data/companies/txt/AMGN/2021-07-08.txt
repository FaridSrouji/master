TEZEPELUMAB GRANTED PRIORITY REVIEW BY U.S. FDA. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., JULY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ:AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ACCEPTED A BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) AND GRANTED PRIORITY REVIEW FOR TEZEPELUMAB IN THE TREATMENT OF ASTHMA.
