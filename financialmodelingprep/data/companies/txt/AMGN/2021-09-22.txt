AMGEN RELEASES 8TH EDITION OF BIOSIMILAR TRENDS REPORT. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY RELEASED THE 8TH EDITION OF THE BIOSIMILAR TRENDS REPORT, WHICH EXAMINES THE CURRENT STATE OF THE U.S. BIOSIMILARS MARKETPLACE ACROSS INFLAMMATION, ONCOLOGY AND NEPHROLOGY CATEGORIES, WHILE A NEW FEATURE CONSIDERS HOW ADVANCEMENTS IN BIOSIMILARS CAN SUPPORT THE LONG-TERM SUCCESS AND SUSTAINABILITY OF THE U.S. HEALTHCARE SYSTEM.
