LUMAKRAS® (SOTORASIB) RECEIVES APPROVAL IN JAPAN FOR PATIENTS WITH KRAS G12C-MUTATED ADVANCED NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., JAN. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT LUMAKRAS® (SOTORASIB) HAS BEEN APPROVED IN JAPAN FOR THE TREATMENT OF KRAS G12C-MUTATED POSITIVE, UNRESECTABLE, ADVANCED AND/OR RECURRENT NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (NSCLC) THAT HAS PROGRESSED AFTER SYSTEMIC ANTICANCER THERAPY.
