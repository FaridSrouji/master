FDA APPROVES OTEZLA® (APREMILAST) FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULT PATIENTS WITH PLAQUE PSORIASIS, REGARDLESS OF SEVERITY LEVEL. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF. , DEC. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED OTEZLA® (APREMILAST) FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULT PATIENTS WITH PLAQUE PSORIASIS WHO ARE CANDIDATES FOR PHOTOTHERAPY OR SYSTEMIC THERAPY.
