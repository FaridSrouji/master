AMGEN TO SHOWCASE NEW DATA FROM INFLAMMATION PORTFOLIO AT EADV VIRTUAL CONGRESS 2021. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., SEPT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT NEW DATA FROM ITS EXPANDING INFLAMMATION PIPELINE AND MARKETED PORTFOLIO WILL BE PRESENTED AT THE EUROPEAN ACADEMY OF DERMATOLOGY AND VENEREOLOGY (EADV) 30TH CONGRESS, WHICH WILL TAKE PLACE VIRTUALLY SEPT.
