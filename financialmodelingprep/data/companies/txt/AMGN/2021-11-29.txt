AMGEN NAMED TO DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX FOR EIGHTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., NOV. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ:AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX FOR THE EIGHTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, REFLECTING THE COMPANY'S ENDURING COMMITMENT TO ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) ISSUES.
