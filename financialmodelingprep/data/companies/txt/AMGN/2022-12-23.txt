LEGOCHEM BIOSCIENCES AND AMGEN ENTER INTO A MULTI-TARGET RESEARCH COLLABORATION AND LICENSE AGREEMENT FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF ANTIBODY-DRUG CONJUGATES. DAEJEON, SOUTH KOREA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--LEGOCHEM BIOSCIENCES, INC. (‘LCB') (KOSDAQ:141080) ANNOUNCED ON DECEMBER 23 THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A RESEARCH COLLABORATION AND LICENSE AGREEMENT WITH AMGEN, WHEREBY IT GRANTED AMGEN RIGHTS TO RESEARCH, DEVELOP, AND COMMERCIALIZE ADCS DIRECTED AGAINST UP TO 5 TARGETS SELECTED BY AMGEN BASED ON LCB'S PROPRIETARY CONJUALL ADC TECHNOLOGY.
