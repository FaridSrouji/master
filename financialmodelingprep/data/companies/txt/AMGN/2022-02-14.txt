LUMAKRAS® (SOTORASIB) SHOWS ENCOURAGING AND CLINICALLY MEANINGFUL ANTICANCER ACTIVITY IN PATIENTS WITH KRAS G12C-MUTATED ADVANCED PANCREATIC CANCER IN CODEBREAK 100 TRIAL. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., FEB. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRESENTATION OF EFFICACY AND SAFETY DATA FROM THE CODEBREAK 100 PHASE 1/2 TRIAL IN PATIENTS WITH KRAS G12C-MUTATED ADVANCED PANCREATIC CANCER WHO RECEIVED LUMAKRAS® (SOTORASIB)*.
