(NASDAQ: AMGN).  UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $116.50 IN CASH FOR EACH SHARE OF HORIZON THERAPEUTICS COMMON STOCK OWNED.
