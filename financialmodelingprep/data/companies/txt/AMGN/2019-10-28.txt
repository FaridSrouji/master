AMGEN INC. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. AMGEN INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 2,476 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,323 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 1,968 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,859 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 3.29 COMPARED TO USD 2.88 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 3.27 COMPARED TO USD 2.86 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 17,165 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 17,517 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 7,626 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 7,881 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 6,139 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 6,466 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 10.08 COMPARED TO USD 9.67 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 10.01 COMPARED TO USD 9.61 A YEAR AGO.
