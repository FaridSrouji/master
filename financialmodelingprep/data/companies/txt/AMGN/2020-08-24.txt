LIGAND ANNOUNCES AMGEN’S KYPROLIS APPROVED BY FDA TO TREAT MULTIPLE MYELOMA. LIGAND ANNOUNCES AMGEN’S KYPROLIS® APPROVED BY FDA AS COMBINATION REGIMEN WITH DARZALEX® AND DEXAMETHASONE IN ONCE- AND TWICE-WEEKLY DOSING REGIMENS FOR PATIENTS WITH RELAPSED/REFRACTORY MULTIPLE MYELOMA.LIGAND PHARMACEUTICALS - AMGEN HAS SUBMITTED ADDITIONAL MARKETING APPLICATIONS GLOBALLY FOR KYPROLIS.
