NEW DATA FURTHER REINFORCES CLINICAL BENEFIT OF OTEZLA IN MILD TO MODERATE PLAQUE PSORIASIS AND IN PATIENTS WITH PALMOPLANTAR PUSTULOSIS REAL-WORLD EVIDENCE DATA BEING PRESENTED DEMONSTRATES CONTINUED BURDEN OF DISEASE FOR PATIENTS WITH MILD TO MODERATE PLAQUE PSORIASIS OTEZLA IS THE FIRST AND ONLY ORAL THERAPY FDA APPROVED IN ADULT PATIENTS WITH PLAQUE PSORIASIS ACROSS ALL SEVERITIES, INCLUDING MILD, MODERATE AND SEVERE THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF. , MARCH 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT NEW OTEZLA (APREMILAST) DATA, ALONG WITH FINDINGS ON THE UNMET NEED FOR PATIENTS WITH PLAQUE PSORIASIS, WILL BE PRESENTED AT THE AMERICAN ACADEMY OF DERMATOLOGY (AAD) CONGRESS IN BOSTON, MASSACHUSETTS, MARCH 25-29, 2022.
