AMGEN AND BIOLABS LA AT THE LUNDQUIST INSTITUTE ANNOUNCE NAMMI THERAPEUTICS TO RECEIVE THE THIRD AMGEN GOLDEN TICKET. BIOTHERAPEUTICS COMPANY TO RECEIVE LAB SPACE, MENTORING AND OTHER BENEFITS THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF. , JAN. 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ:AMGN) AND BIOLABS LA AT THE LUNDQUIST INSTITUTE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT NAMMI THERAPEUTICS HAS BEEN AWARDED THE THIRD AMGEN GOLDEN TICKET IN SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA.
