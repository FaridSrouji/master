AMGEN WINS PATENT CASE ON OTEZLA® (APREMILAST). THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., SEPT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. DISTRICT COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF NEW JERSEY HAS UPHELD PATENTS THAT PROTECT AMGEN'S PSORIASIS THERAPY OTEZLA® (APREMILAST) IN A PATENT INFRINGEMENT LAWSUIT AGAINST SANDOZ INC. ("SANDOZ") AND ZYDUS PHARMACEUTICALS (USA), INC. ("ZYDUS").
