SAVION EXPANDS AS INDEPENDENT POWER PRODUCER, ORDERS 2.6 GW OF RESPONSIBLY PRODUCED ULTRA-LOW CARBON FIRST SOLAR MODULES. KANSAS CITY, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #ENERGYSTORAGE--SAVION, LLC (SAVION) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE AGREEMENT TO PURCHASE 2.6 GIGAWATTS (GWDC) OF HIGH-PERFORMANCE, RESPONSIBLY PRODUCED SERIES 7 THIN FILM SOLAR MODULES FROM FIRST SOLAR, INC. (NASDAQ: FSLR). THE DEAL WITH SAVION WAS BOOKED PRIOR TO THE RELEASE OF FIRST SOLAR'S Q3 2022 EARNINGS IN OCTOBER 2022, AND THE MODULES ARE SCHEDULED TO BE DELIVERED BETWEEN 2025 AND 2027.
