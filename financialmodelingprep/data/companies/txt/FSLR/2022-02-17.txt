FSLR LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES FIRST SOLAR, INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK, FEB. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN FIRST SOLAR, INC. ("FIRST SOLAR" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: FSLR) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF FIRST SOLAR INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD.
