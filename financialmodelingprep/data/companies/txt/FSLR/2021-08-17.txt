FIRST SOLAR BREAKS GROUND ON NEW $680M, 3.3 GW OHIO MANUFACTURING FACILITY. TEMPE, ARIZ., AUG. 17, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FIRST SOLAR, INC. (NASDAQ: FSLR) TODAY BROKE GROUND ON ITS THIRD MANUFACTURING FACILITY IN OHIO AT A CEREMONY THAT WAS ATTENDED BY UNITED STATES SECRETARY OF LABOR MARTY WALSH, THE LIEUTENANT GOVERNOR OF OHIO, JON HUSTED, AND US REPRESENTATIVES BOB LATTA (OH-05) AND MARCY KAPTUR (OH-09).
