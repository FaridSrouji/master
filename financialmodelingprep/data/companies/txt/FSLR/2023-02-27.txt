SILICON RANCH COMMITS TO ADDITIONAL 1.5 GIGAWATTS OF FIRST SOLAR'S AMERICAN SOLAR MODULES. NASHVILLE, TENN. & TEMPE, ARIZ.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SILICON RANCH, ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST INDEPENDENT POWER PRODUCERS, HAS PROCURED AN ADDITIONAL 1.5 GIGAWATTS (GW) OF ADVANCED AMERICAN THIN FILM SOLAR MODULES FROM FIRST SOLAR, INC. (NASDAQ: FSLR). FIRST SOLAR'S SERIES 6 PLUS MODULES WERE DESIGNED AND DEVELOPED AT THE COMPANY'S RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT FACILITIES IN CALIFORNIA AND OHIO, AND IN THE UNITED STATES ARE MANUFACTURED IN OHIO.
