FSLR INVESTORS HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD FIRST SOLAR, INC. SECURITIES FRAUD LAWSUIT. LOS ANGELES, JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP ("GPM") ANNOUNCES THAT INVESTORS WITH SUBSTANTIAL LOSSES HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD THE SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST FIRST SOLAR, INC. ("FIRST SOLAR" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: FSLR). CLASS PERIOD: FEBRUARY 22, 2019 – FEBRUARY 20, 2020 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE: MARCH 8, 2022 IF YOU WISH TO SERVE AS LEAD PLAINTIFF OF THE FIRST SOLAR LAWSUIT, YOU CAN SUBMIT YOUR CONTACT INFORMATION AT WWW.GLANCYLAW.COM/CASES/FIRST-SOLAR-INC/.
