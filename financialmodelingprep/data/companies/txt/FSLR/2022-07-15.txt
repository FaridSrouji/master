NATIONAL GRID RENEWABLES HOSTS CHARITABLE STUFF-THE-BUS EVENT AT 200 MW PRAIRIE WOLF SOLAR PROJECT. MISO-IL PROJECT'S EDUCATION FUND TO DONATE ~$720,000 OVER NEXT 20 YEARS MINNEAPOLIS , JULY 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALONGSIDE COMMUNITY MEMBERS, PROJECT CUSTOMERS AND PARTNERS, NATIONAL GRID RENEWABLES HOSTED A COMMUNITY CELEBRATION AND STUFF-THE-BUS EVENT ON JULY 13, 2022 AT THE PRAIRIE WOLF SOLAR PROJECT (PRAIRIE WOLF) IN COLES COUNTY, ILLINOIS. THE EVENT CELEBRATED THE 200 MEGAWATT (MW) PROJECT AND THE SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC BENEFITS IT PROVIDES THE LOCAL COMMUNITY AND SCHOOL DISTRICT.
