FIRST SOLAR, INC. TO ANNOUNCE FIRST QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS ON APRIL 29, 2021. TEMPE, ARIZ., APRIL 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FIRST SOLAR, INC. (NASDAQ: FSLR) WILL REPORT FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021, AFTER THE MARKET CLOSES ON THURSDAY, APRIL 29, 2021.
