FIRST SOLAR APPOINTS KATHRYN A. HOLLISTER TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. TEMPE, ARIZ., MARCH 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FIRST SOLAR, INC. (NASDAQ: FSLR) HAS APPOINTED KATHRYN A. “KATY” HOLLISTER, A SEASONED GLOBAL LEADER AND FINANCIAL EXPERT WITH NEARLY FOUR DECADES OF LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCE AT DELOITTE TAX LLP AND ITS AFFILIATES, TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MS. HOLLISTER WILL SERVE ON FIRST SOLAR'S AUDIT AND COMPENSATION COMMITTEES.
