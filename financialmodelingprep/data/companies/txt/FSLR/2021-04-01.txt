FIRST SOLAR COMPLETES SALE OF US DEVELOPMENT PLATFORM TO LEEWARD. DALLAS AND TEMPE, ARIZ., APRIL 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FIRST SOLAR, INC. (NASDAQ: FSLR), ANNOUNCED THAT IT COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SALE OF A UTILITY-SCALE SOLAR PROJECT PLATFORM OF APPROXIMATELY 10 GIGAWATTS (GW)AC TO LEEWARD RENEWABLE ENERGY, LLC (“LEEWARD”), YESTERDAY.
