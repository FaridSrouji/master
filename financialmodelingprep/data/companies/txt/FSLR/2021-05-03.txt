NEL ASA: ENTERS COLLABORATION WITH FIRST SOLAR. OSLO, NORWAY, MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEL HYDROGEN ELECTROLYSER, A DIVISION OF NEL ASA (NEL) (OSE:NEL), HAS ENTERED A COLLABORATION WITH LEADING SOLAR COMPANY FIRST SOLAR, INC. (NASDAQ: FSLR), TO DEVELOP INTEGRATED PHOTO VOLTAIC (PV) HYDROGEN POWER PLANTS. "WE ARE VERY EXCITED TO ANNOUNCE THE COLLABORATION WITH FIRST SOLAR, A LEADING GLOBAL MANUFACTURER OF SOLAR PANELS AND DEVELOPER OF UTILITY-SCALE SOLAR POWER PLANTS.
