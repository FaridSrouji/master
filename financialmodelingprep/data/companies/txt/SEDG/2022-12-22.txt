SEDG LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES SOLAREDGE TECHNOLOGIES, INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , DEC. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN SOLAREDGE TECHNOLOGIES, INC. ("SOLAREDGE" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: SEDG) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF SOLAREDGE INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD.
