CME GROUP U.S. TREASURY FUTURES OPEN INTEREST SURPASSES RECORD 17 MILLION CONTRACTS. RECORD OPEN INTEREST OF 17,222,551 CONTRACTS REACHED ON MAY 12, REPRESENTING $2 TRILLION IN NOTIONAL VALUE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE DAY OF RECORD OPEN INTEREST FOR U.S. TREASURY FUTURES CHICAGO , MAY 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP , THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT OPEN INTEREST (OI) IN ITS U.S. TREASURY FUTURES REACHED A RECORD 17,222,551 CONTRACTS ON MAY 12, THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE DAY OF RECORD OI. PRIOR TO THE RECORD WEEK, THE PREVIOUS OI RECORD OF 16,698,659 CONTRACTS WAS SET ON FEBRUARY 19, 2020.
