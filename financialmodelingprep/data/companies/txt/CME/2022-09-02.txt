CME GROUP REPORTS 22% ADV GROWTH IN AUGUST 2022. HIGHEST-EVER AUGUST FOR EQUITY INDEX ADV RECORD SOFR FUTURES AND OPTIONS OI CHICAGO , SEPT. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY REPORTED ITS AUGUST 2022 MARKET STATISTICS, SHOWING AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME (ADV) INCREASED 22% TO 21.2 MILLION CONTRACTS DURING THE MONTH.
