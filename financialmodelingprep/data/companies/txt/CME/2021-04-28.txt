CME GROUP INC. REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. CHICAGO, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP INC. (NASDAQ: CME) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. THE COMPANY REPORTED REVENUE OF $1.3 BILLION AND OPERATING INCOME OF $725 MILLION FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021.
CME GROUP TO LAUNCH E-MINI S&P EUROPE 350 ESG INDEX FUTURES. LONDON, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING AND MOST DIVERSE DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL LAUNCH E-MINI S&P EUROPE 350 ESG INDEX FUTURES, A TARGETED RISK MANAGEMENT TOOL TO ALIGN WITH ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) VALUES. BUILDING ON THE COMPANY'S SUCCESSFUL E-MINI S&P 500 ESG INDEX FUTURES CONTRACT, THIS NEW PRODUCT WILL BE AVAILABLE FOR TRADING ON MAY 24, PENDING REGULATORY REVIEW.
