CME GROUP SOFR FUTURES AND OPTIONS TRADE A RECORD 7.56 MILLION CONTRACTS ON JANUARY 12. RECORD 7,558,467 SOFR-BASED CONTRACTS TRADED, WITH RECORD OPEN INTEREST OF 35,698,298 CONTRACTS CHICAGO , JAN. 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP , THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED NEW MILESTONES IN THE GROWTH OF ITS SOFR DERIVATIVES CONTRACTS, WITH A SINGLE-DAY RECORD OF 7,558,467 SOFR FUTURES AND OPTIONS TRADED AND RECORD OPEN INTEREST (OI) OF 35,698,298 CONTRACTS ON JANUARY 12. INDIVIDUALLY, SOFR FUTURES REACHED A RECORD OF 4,513,725 CONTRACTS TRADED ON THE SAME DAY.
