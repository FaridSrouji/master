CME GROUP INTERNATIONAL AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME REACHED 6.1 MILLION CONTRACTS IN Q3 2022, UP 21% FROM Q3 2021. LONDON AND SINGAPORE , OCT. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS QUARTERLY INTERNATIONAL AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME (ADV) REACHED 6.1 MILLION CONTRACTS IN Q3 2022, UP 21% YEAR ON YEAR.  REFLECTING ALL TRADING REPORTED FROM OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES, VOLUME WAS DRIVEN LARGELY BY 36% GROWTH IN FOREIGN EXCHANGE PRODUCTS, A 32% INCREASE IN INTEREST RATE PRODUCTS AND A 25% GROWTH IN EQUITY INDEX PRODUCTS.
