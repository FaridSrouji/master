CME GROUP TO LAUNCH MONDAY AND WEDNESDAY GOLD, SILVER AND COPPER WEEKLY OPTIONS ON MAY 2. CHICAGO , APRIL 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL LAUNCH MONDAY AND WEDNESDAY GOLD, SILVER AND COPPER WEEKLY OPTIONS ON MAY 2, PENDING REGULATORY REVIEW. THESE NEW OPTIONS CONTRACTS WILL COMPLEMENT THE COMPANY'S EXISTING FRIDAY WEEKLY, END-OF-MONTH AND QUARTERLY OPTIONS ON GOLD, SILVER AND COPPER FUTURES.
