CME GROUP ANNOUNCES FIRST TRADES OF MOLYBDENUM OXIDE (PLATTS) FUTURES. CHICAGO , MARCH 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FIRST TRADES OF MOLYBDENUM OXIDE (PLATTS) FUTURES. A TOTAL OF 90 CONTRACTS WERE TRADED ON MARCH 13, 2023, WITH OPEN INTEREST EXTENDING OUT TO FEBRUARY 2024.
