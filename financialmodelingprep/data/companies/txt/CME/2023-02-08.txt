CME GROUP INC. REPORTS FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS. CHICAGO , FEB. 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP INC. (NASDAQ: CME) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR OF 2022. THE COMPANY REPORTED REVENUE OF $1.2 BILLION AND OPERATING INCOME OF $668 MILLION FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022.
