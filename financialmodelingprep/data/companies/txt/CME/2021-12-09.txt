CME GROUP TO LAUNCH TWO CHINA PORTSIDE IRON ORE FUTURES CONTRACTS ON JANUARY 10. SINGAPORE, DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING AND MOST DIVERSE DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL LAUNCH TWO NEW CHINA PORTSIDE IRON ORE FUTURES CONTRACTS ON JANUARY 10, 2022, PENDING ALL RELEVANT REGULATORY REVIEWS. THESE CONTRACTS WILL BE FINANCIALLY-SETTLED BASED ON ASSESSMENTS FOR PORTSIDE PRICES PUBLISHED BY ARGUS MEDIA.
