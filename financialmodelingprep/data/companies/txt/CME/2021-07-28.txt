CME GROUP INC. REPORTS SECOND-QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. CHICAGO, JULY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP INC. (NASDAQ: CME) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021. THE COMPANY REPORTED REVENUE OF $1.2 BILLION AND OPERATING INCOME OF $675 MILLION FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021.
