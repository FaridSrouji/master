CME GROUP ANNOUNCES FIRST TRADES FOR 20-YEAR U.S. TREASURY BOND FUTURES. CHICAGO , MARCH 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP , THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED FIRST DAY TRADING VOLUME FOR ITS NEWLY LAUNCHED 20-YEAR U.S. TREASURY BOND FUTURES CONTRACT, WHICH BECAME AVAILABLE FOR TRADING ON MONDAY, MARCH 7.  IN TOTAL, MORE THAN 1,300 CONTRACTS WERE TRADED BY 30 PARTICIPANTS, WITH OPEN INTEREST OF 1,041 CONTRACTS, SIGNIFYING STRONG CLIENT INTEREST IN AN ADDITIONAL FIXED INCOME HEDGING TOOL AT A TIME WHEN MANAGING U.S. TREASURY MARKET RISK IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN EVER.
