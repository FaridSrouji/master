CME GROUP ANNOUNCES Q4 2022 LAUNCH OF EUROPEAN OVERNIGHT INDEX FUTURES. CHICAGO , AUG. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL LAUNCH EUROPEAN OVERNIGHT INDEX FUTURES BASED ON REPOFUNDS RATE (RFR) BENCHMARKS AND THE EURO SHORT-TERM RATE (€STR) IN Q4 2022, PENDING REGULATORY REVIEW. RFR FUTURES WILL BE CASH-SETTLED CONTRACTS BASED ON RFR BENCHMARKS, A ROBUST MEASURE OF OVERNIGHT FUNDING COSTS.
