CME GROUP INC. REPORTS THIRD-QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. CHICAGO, OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP INC. (NASDAQ: CME) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021. THE COMPANY REPORTED REVENUE OF $1.1 BILLION AND OPERATING INCOME OF $614 MILLION FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021.
