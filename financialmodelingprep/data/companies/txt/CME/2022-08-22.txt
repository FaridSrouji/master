CME GROUP OPENS REGISTRATION FOR ITS 19TH ANNUAL GLOBAL UNIVERSITY TRADING CHALLENGE 2022. CHICAGO , AUG. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, IS CALLING ON GLOBAL COLLEGE STUDENTS INTERESTED IN PURSUING CAREERS IN FINANCE AND MARKETS TO REGISTER FOR ITS ANNUAL UNIVERSITY TRADING CHALLENGE WHICH WILL BE HELD DURING OCTOBER 2022. REGISTRATION FOR THE GLOBAL EDUCATIONAL COMPETITION OPENS TODAY.
