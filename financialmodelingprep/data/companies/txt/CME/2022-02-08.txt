CME GROUP TO LAUNCH CBL CORE GLOBAL EMISSIONS OFFSET FUTURES. CHICAGO, FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL LAUNCH CBL CORE GLOBAL EMISSIONS OFFSET™ (C-GEO™) FUTURES ON MARCH 7, 2022, PENDING ALL RELEVANT REGULATORY REVIEWS. CBL C-GEO FUTURES ARE INTENDED TO ALIGN WITH THE CORE CARBON PRINCIPLES, AN EMERGING SET OF TRANSPARENT AND CONSISTENT STANDARDS AROUND THE SUPPLY OF CARBON CREDITS TO BE OVERSEEN BY THE INTEGRITY COUNCIL FOR THE VOLUNTARY CARBON MARKETS.
