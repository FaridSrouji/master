CME GROUP TO LAUNCH ETHER/BITCOIN RATIO FUTURES ON JULY 31. CHICAGO , JUNE 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP , THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO LAUNCH ETHER/BITCOIN RATIO FUTURES ON JULY 31, PENDING REGULATORY REVIEW. "HISTORICALLY, ETHER AND BITCOIN HAVE BEEN HIGHLY CORRELATED; HOWEVER, AS THE TWO ASSETS HAVE GROWN OVER TIME, MARKET DYNAMICS MAY AFFECT THE PERFORMANCE OF ONE MORE THAN THE OTHER, CREATING RELATIVE VALUE TRADING OPPORTUNITIES," SAID GIOVANNI VICIOSO, CME GROUP GLOBAL HEAD OF CRYPTOCURRENCY PRODUCTS.
