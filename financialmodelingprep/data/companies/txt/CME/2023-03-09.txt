CME GROUP SOFR FUTURES AND OPTIONS REACH SINGLE-DAY TRADING, OPEN INTEREST RECORDS. CHICAGO, MARCH 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED NEW MILESTONES IN THE GROWTH OF ITS SOFR DERIVATIVES CONTRACTS, WITH A SINGLE-DAY RECORD OF 9,784,528 SOFR FUTURES AND OPTIONS TRADED ON MARCH 7 AND RECORD OPEN INTEREST (OI) OF 54,666,191 CONTRACTS ON MARCH 8.
CME GROUP ANNOUNCES INITIAL TRADING OF NEW CROP WEEKLY OPTIONS. CHICAGO , MARCH 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT OPEN INTEREST IN NEW CROP WEEKLY OPTIONS HAS SURPASSED 500 CONTRACTS, WHILE A TOTAL OF 984 CONTRACTS ACROSS 28 UNIQUE ACCOUNT NUMBERS HAVE TRADED SINCE LAUNCHING ON JANUARY 23. NEW CROP WEEKLY OPTIONS BUILD ON THE SUCCESS OF SHORT-DATED NEW CROP OPTIONS, WHICH LAUNCHED A DECADE AGO AND ACHIEVED RECORD AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME (ADV) OF 11,000 CONTRACTS IN 2022.
