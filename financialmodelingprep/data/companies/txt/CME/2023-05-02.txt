CME GROUP REPORTS APRIL 2023 MONTHLY MARKET STATISTICS. SECOND-HIGHEST APRIL ADV ON RECORD SOFR FUTURES AND OPTIONS CONTINUE ADV GROWTH STRONG ADV INCREASES IN OPTIONS, COMMODITIES AND CRYPTOCURRENCIES CHICAGO , MAY 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY REPORTED ITS APRIL 2023 MARKET STATISTICS, REACHING AN AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME (ADV) OF 19.8 MILLION CONTRACTS DURING THE MONTH, THE SECOND-HIGHEST APRIL ADV IN COMPANY HISTORY.
