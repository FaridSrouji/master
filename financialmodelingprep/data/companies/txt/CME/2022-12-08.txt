CME GROUP DECLARES ANNUAL VARIABLE DIVIDEND. CHICAGO , DEC. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP INC., THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY DECLARED ITS ANNUAL VARIABLE DIVIDEND, AMOUNTING TO $4.50 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JANUARY 18, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON DECEMBER 28, 2022.
