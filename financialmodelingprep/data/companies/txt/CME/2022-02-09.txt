CME GROUP INC. REPORTS FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. CHICAGO, FEB. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP INC. (NASDAQ: CME) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR OF 2021. THE COMPANY REPORTED REVENUE OF $1.1 BILLION AND OPERATING INCOME OF $632 MILLION FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021.
