CME GROUP ANNOUNCES RESIGNATION OF ALEX J. POLLOCK FROM ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 15, 2019. CME GROUP ANNOUNCED THAT ALEX J. POLLOCK WILL STEP DOWN FROM THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 15, 2019 AS HE ACCEPTS A NEW ROLE WITHIN THE U.S. TREASURY DEPARTMENT AS PRINCIPAL DEPUTY DIRECTOR OF THE OFFICE OF FINANCIAL RESEARCH. POLLOCK HAS SERVED AS A CME GROUP DIRECTOR SINCE 2004.
