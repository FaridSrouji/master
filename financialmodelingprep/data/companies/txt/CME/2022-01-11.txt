CME GROUP ACHIEVES RECORD INTERNATIONAL AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME OF 5.5 MILLION CONTRACTS IN 2021, UP 4% FROM 2020. LONDON AND SINGAPORE , JAN. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING AND MOST DIVERSE DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT ACHIEVED RECORD INTERNATIONAL AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME (ADV) OF 5.5 MILLION CONTRACTS IN 2021, UP 4% FROM 2020. THIS RECORD, REFLECTING ALL TRADING DONE OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES, WAS DRIVEN LARGELY BY GROWTH IN INTEREST RATE PRODUCTS, UP 19%.
