CME GROUP REPORTS JANUARY 2022 MONTHLY MARKET STATISTICS. CHICAGO, FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY REPORTED ITS JANUARY 2022 MARKET STATISTICS, SHOWING AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME (ADV) INCREASED 28% TO 24.6 MILLION CONTRACTS DURING THE MONTH. MARKET STATISTICS ARE AVAILABLE IN GREATER DETAIL AT HTTPS://CMEGROUPINC.GCS-WEB.COM/MONTHLY-VOLUME.
