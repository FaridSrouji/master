CME GROUP ACHIEVES RECORD INTERNATIONAL AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME OF 6.3 MILLION CONTRACTS IN 2022, UP 15% FROM 2021. LONDON AND SINGAPORE , JAN. 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP , THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT ACHIEVED RECORD INTERNATIONAL AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME (ADV) OF 6.3 MILLION CONTRACTS IN 2022, UP 15% FROM 2021. THIS RECORD, REFLECTING ALL TRADING DONE OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES, WAS DRIVEN LARGELY BY GROWTH IN EQUITY INDEX AND FOREIGN EXCHANGE PRODUCTS, UP 26% AND 22% RESPECTIVELY.
