CME GROUP ANNOUNCES RECORD COPPER OPTIONS OPEN INTEREST AS PARTICIPATION GROWS ACROSS BASE METALS. CHICAGO , JAN. 19, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT OPEN INTEREST IN COPPER OPTIONS (HX) HAS REACHED MULTIPLE, BACK-TO-BACK RECORDS, REACHING 137,574 CONTRACTS ON JANUARY 18, 2023. AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME (ADV) ACROSS THE COMPANY'S COPPER FUTURES AND OPTIONS COMPLEX IS AT 131,466 CONTRACTS MONTH-TO-DATE, UP 42% FROM FULL-YEAR 2022 ADV.
