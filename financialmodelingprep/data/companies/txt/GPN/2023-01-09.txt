FINTECH STARTUP CLOWD9 PARTNERS WITH NETCETERA TO HELP COMBAT GLOBAL PAYMENTS FRAUD.. LONDON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CLOWD9, THE WORLD'S FIRST CLOUD NATIVE AND DECENTRALIZED PAYMENTS PROCESSING PLATFORM HAS PARTNERED WITH NETCETERA, A GLOBAL SOFTWARE COMPANY FOR SECURE AND CONVENIENT DIGITAL PAYMENT SOLUTIONS, AS ITS PAYMENT SECURITY PROVIDER. CLOWD9 WILL USE ITS 3-D SECURE ISSUER SERVICE FOR TRANSACTION AUTHENTICATION AND ACCESS CONTROL SERVER (ACS).
