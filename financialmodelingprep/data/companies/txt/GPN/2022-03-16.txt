HST PATHWAYS WELCOMES NEW CHIEF REVENUE OFFICER NICK LATZ TO EXECUTIVE TEAM. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HST PATHWAYS, A LEADING PROVIDER OF END-TO-END SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS FOR THE AMBULATORY SURGERY INDUSTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ADDITION OF NICK LATZ TO ITS EXECUTIVE TEAM AS THE NEW CHIEF REVENUE OFFICER (CRO) FOR THE COMPANY. LATZ JOINS HST PATHWAYS AFTER FOUR YEARS AT ZEGO WHERE HE LED MARKETING, SALES, AND CUSTOMER SUCCESS.
