CLASS ACTION ALERT: WOLF HALDENSTEIN ADLER FREEMAN & HERZ LLP REMINDS INVESTORS THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE NORTHERN DISTRICT OF GEORGIA ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS. LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE IS APRIL 10, 2023 NEW YORK , FEB. 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WOLF HALDENSTEIN ADLER FREEMAN & HERZ LLP ANNOUNCES THAT A FEDERAL SECURITIES CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE NORTHERN DISTRICT OF GEORGIA ON BEHALF OF A CLASS CONSISTING OF ALL PERSONS AND ENTITIES OTHER THAN DEFENDANTS THAT PURCHASED GLOBAL PAYMENTS, INC. ("GLOBAL PAYMENTS" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: GPN). BETWEEN OCTOBER 31, 2019 AND OCTOBER 18, 2022, BOTH DATES INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
