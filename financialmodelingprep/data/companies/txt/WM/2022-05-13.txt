WM ANNOUNCES THE REDEMPTION OF SENIOR NOTES DUE IN 2022. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WM (NYSE: WM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL REDEEM THE ENTIRE OUTSTANDING PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 2.90% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2022 (THE “NOTES”). THE REDEMPTION DATE FOR THE NOTES IS JUNE 15, 2022 (THE “REDEMPTION DATE”). THE AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF THE NOTES OUTSTANDING IS $500 MILLION.
