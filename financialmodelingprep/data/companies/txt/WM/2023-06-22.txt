ROUTEWARE ANNOUNCES ACQUISITION OF COMPLIANCE PUBLISHING, EXPANDING ITS SOLUTIONS FOR MEDICAL WASTE MANAGEMENT. PORTLAND, ORE., JUNE 22, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ROUTEWARE, A LEADING PROVIDER OF INTEGRATED TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS FOR THE WASTE AND RECYCLING INDUSTRY, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THE ACQUISITION OF ST. LOUIS PARK, MINNESOTA BASED COMPLIANCE PUBLISHING, A RENOWNED PROVIDER OF SOLUTIONS FOR MEDICAL WASTE COMPANIES. THIS STRATEGIC ACQUISITION STRENGTHENS ROUTEWARE'S PRODUCT OFFERINGS FOR MEDICAL WASTE HAULERS, INCLUDING THE ABILITY TO MANAGE BACK-OFFICE OPERATIONS AND PROVIDE OSHA TRAINING AND COMPLIANCE.
