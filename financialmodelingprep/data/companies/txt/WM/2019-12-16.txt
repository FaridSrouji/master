WASTE MANAGEMENT TO INCREASE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE, CONFIRMS AUTHORIZATION FOR SHARE REPURCHASES. WASTE MANAGEMENT ANNOUNCES PLAN TO INCREASE THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE AND CONFIRMS AUTHORIZATION FOR SHARE REPURCHASES.WASTE MANAGEMENT INC - APPROVED A 6.3% INCREASE IN PLANNED QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE FOR 2020, FROM $0.5125 TO $0.545 PER SHARE.WASTE MANAGEMENT INC - CONFIRMS THAT IT HAS $1.32 BILLION REMAINING ON ITS EXISTING BOARD OF DIRECTORS' AUTHORIZATION TO REPURCHASE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK.
