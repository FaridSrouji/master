WM ANNOUNCES PLAN FOR DOUBLE-DIGIT INCREASE IN ITS DIVIDEND RATE AND AUTHORIZATION FOR UP TO $1.5 BILLION IN SHARE REPURCHASES. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WASTE MANAGEMENT, INC. (NYSE: WM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A 13% INCREASE IN THE PLANNED QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE FOR 2022, FROM $0.575 TO $0.65 PER SHARE. ON AN ANNUAL BASIS, THE DIVIDEND RATE INCREASES FROM $2.30 TO $2.60. THIS WILL MARK THE NINETEENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR THAT THE COMPANY HAS INCREASED ITS PER SHARE DIVIDEND.
