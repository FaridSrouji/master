MONDAY DEADLINE REMINDER: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN WASTE MANAGEMENT, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - AUGUST 3, 2022) - THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST WASTE MANAGEMENT, INC. ("WASTE MANAGEMENT" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE: WM) FOR VIOLATIONS OF THE SECURITIES LAWS.
SHAREHOLDER ALERT: THE GROSS LAW FIRM NOTIFIES SHAREHOLDERS OF WASTE MANAGEMENT, INC. OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF AUGUST 8, 2022 - (NYSE: WM). NEW YORK , AUG. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE GROSS LAW FIRM ISSUES THE FOLLOWING NOTICE TO SHAREHOLDERS OF WASTE MANAGEMENT, INC.. SHAREHOLDERS WHO PURCHASED SHARES OF WM DURING THE CLASS PERIOD LISTED ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THE FIRM REGARDING POSSIBLE LEAD PLAINTIFF APPOINTMENT.
