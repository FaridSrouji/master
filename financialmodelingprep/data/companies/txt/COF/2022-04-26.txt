CAPITAL ONE REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2022 NET INCOME OF $2.4 BILLION, OR $5.62 PER SHARE. MCLEAN, VA., APRIL 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED NET INCOME FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022 OF $2.4 BILLION, OR $5.62 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, COMPARED WITH NET INCOME OF $2.4 BILLION, OR $5.41 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021, AND WITH NET INCOME OF $3.3 BILLION, OR $7.03 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021.
