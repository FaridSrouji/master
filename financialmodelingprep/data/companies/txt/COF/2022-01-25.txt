CAPITAL ONE REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER 2021 NET INCOME OF $2.4 BILLION, OR $5.41 PER SHARE. MCLEAN, VA., JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED NET INCOME FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021 OF $2.4 BILLION, OR $5.41 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, COMPARED WITH NET INCOME OF $3.1 BILLION, OR $6.78 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021, AND WITH NET INCOME OF $2.6 BILLION, OR $5.35 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020.
