CAPITAL ONE ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. COMPANY ALSO DECLARES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND   MCLEAN, VA. , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.60 PER SHARE PAYABLE AUGUST 19, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUGUST 8, 2022.
