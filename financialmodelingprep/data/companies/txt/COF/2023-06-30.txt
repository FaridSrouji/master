CAPITAL ONE ANNOUNCES STRESS CAPITAL BUFFER REQUIREMENT. MCLEAN, VA. , JUNE 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS PRELIMINARY STRESS CAPITAL BUFFER REQUIREMENT ("SCB"), AS CALCULATED BASED ON THE FEDERAL RESERVE'S 2023 COMPREHENSIVE CAPITAL ANALYSIS AND REVIEW PROCESS ("CCAR"), IS 4.8 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 1, 2023.
