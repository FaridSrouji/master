CAPITAL ONE ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. MCLEAN, VA., JULY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED A ONE-TIME SPECIAL DIVIDEND OF $0.60 AND A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.60 PER SHARE PAYABLE AUGUST 20, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUGUST 9, 2021.
