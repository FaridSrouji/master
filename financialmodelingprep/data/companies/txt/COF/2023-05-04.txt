CAPITAL ONE ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. COMPANY ALSO DECLARES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND   MCLEAN, VA. , MAY 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.60 PER COMMON SHARE PAYABLE MAY 26, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 15, 2023.
