CAPITAL ONE ANNOUNCES STRESS TEST RESULTS. MCLEAN, VA., JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY'S STRESS CAPITAL BUFFER REQUIREMENT ("SCB"), AS CALCULATED BY THE FEDERAL RESERVE 2021 COMPREHENSIVE CAPITAL ANALYSIS AND REVIEW PROCESS ("CCAR"), IS 2.5 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 1, 2021.
