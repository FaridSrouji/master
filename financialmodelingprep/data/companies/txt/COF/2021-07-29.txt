CAPITAL ONE ANNOUNCES FULL REDEMPTION OF ITS SERIES E PREFERRED STOCK. MCLEAN, VA., JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL REDEEM ALL OUTSTANDING SHARES OF ITS FIXED-TO-FLOATING RATE NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK, SERIES E, $0.01 PAR VALUE PER SHARE ("SERIES E PREFERRED STOCK").
