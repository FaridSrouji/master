CAPITAL ONE ANNOUNCES STRESS TEST RESULTS. MCLEAN, VA. , JUNE 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY'S STRESS CAPITAL BUFFER REQUIREMENT ("SCB"), AS CALCULATED BY THE FEDERAL RESERVE 2022 COMPREHENSIVE CAPITAL ANALYSIS AND REVIEW PROCESS ("CCAR"), IS 3.1 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 1, 2022.
