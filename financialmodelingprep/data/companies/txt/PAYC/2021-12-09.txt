CINCINNATI'S COBY BRYANT NAMED 2021 PAYCOM JIM THORPE AWARD WINNER. OKLAHOMA CITY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COBY BRYANT OF THE UNIVERSITY OF CINCINNATI HAS BEEN NAMED THE 2021 PAYCOM JIM THORPE AWARD WINNER, AWARDED TO THE TOP DEFENSIVE BACK IN COLLEGE FOOTBALL. THE ANNOUNCEMENT WAS MADE LIVE VIRTUALLY DURING THE HOME DEPOT COLLEGE FOOTBALL AWARDS ON ESPN. BRYANT WAS PRESENTED THE 2021 PAYCOM JIM THORPE AWARD ON BEHALF OF PAYCOM AND OKLAHOMA SPORTS HALL OF FAME.
