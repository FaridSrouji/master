(NYSE: PAYC), A LEADING PROVIDER OF COMPREHENSIVE, CLOUD-BASED HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE, ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS LATEST DEVELOPMENT, CLIENT ACTION CENTER. DESIGNED FOR PAYROLL ADMINISTRATORS, CLIENT ACTION CENTER CREATES INSTANT VISIBILITY.
