THE JIM THORPE ASSOCIATION AND OKLAHOMA SPORTS HALL OF FAME ARE PROUD TO REVEAL THE PAYCOM JIM THORPE AWARD 2022 FINALISTS. OKLAHOMA CITY--( BUSINESS WIRE )--OKLAHOMA SPORTS HALL OF FAME HAS ANNOUNCED THE THREE FINALISTS FOR THE 2022 PAYCOM JIM THORPE AWARD, WHICH IS AWARDED TO THE BEST DEFENSIVE BACK IN COLLEGE FOOTBALL. THE AWARD IS BASED ON PERFORMANCE ON THE FIELD, ATHLETIC ABILITY AND CHARACTER.
