CROSS RIVER AND PAY.COM PARTNER TO CREATE ALL-INCLUSIVE PAYMENTS PLATFORM FOR BUSINESSES. FORT LEE, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CROSS RIVER BANK (“CROSS RIVER”), A LEADING TECHNOLOGY INFRASTRUCTURE PROVIDER THAT OFFERS EMBEDDED FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW PARTNERSHIP WITH U.K. BASED PAY.COM. THIS PARTNERSHIP WILL ENHANCE AND EXPAND PAY.COM'S AUTOMATED PAYMENT PLATFORM INTO THE U.S. AND DRIVE ADDITIONAL GROWTH OPPORTUNITIES.
