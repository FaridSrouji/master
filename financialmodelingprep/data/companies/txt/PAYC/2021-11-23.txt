THE JIM THORPE ASSOCIATION AND OKLAHOMA SPORTS HALL OF FAME ARE PROUD TO REVEAL THE PAYCOM JIM THORPE AWARD 2021 FINALISTS. OKLAHOMA CITY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE OKLAHOMA SPORTS HALL OF FAME HAS ANNOUNCED THE THREE FINALISTS FOR THE 2021 PAYCOM JIM THORPE AWARD, WHICH IS AWARDED TO THE BEST DEFENSIVE BACK IN COLLEGE FOOTBALL. THE AWARD IS BASED ON PERFORMANCE ON THE FIELD, ATHLETIC ABILITY AND CHARACTER. COBY BRYANT (UNIVERSITY OF CINCINNATI, AAC), VERONE MCKINLEY III (UNIVERSITY OF OREGON, PAC-12) AND JALEN PITRE (BAYLOR UNIVERSITY, BIG 12) ARE THE THREE DEFENSIVE BACKS CHOSEN AS THE 2021 FINALISTS.
