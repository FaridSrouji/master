NRG ENERGY, INC. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NRG ENERGY, INC. (NYSE:NRG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK OF $0.35 PER SHARE, OR $1.40 PER SHARE ON AN ANNUALIZED BASIS. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MAY 16, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 2, 2022.
