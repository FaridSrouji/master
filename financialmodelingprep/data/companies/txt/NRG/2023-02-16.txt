NRG ENERGY, INC. REPORTS FULL YEAR RESULTS AND REAFFIRMS 2023 GUIDANCE. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NRG ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NRG) TODAY REPORTED FULL YEAR 2022 NET INCOME OF $1.2 BILLION, OR $5.17 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE. ADJUSTED EBITDA FOR THE FULL YEAR 2022 WAS $1.8 BILLION, NET CASH PROVIDED BY OPERATING ACTIVITIES WAS $0.4 BILLION, AND FREE CASH FLOW BEFORE GROWTH (FCFBG) WAS $0.6 BILLION.
