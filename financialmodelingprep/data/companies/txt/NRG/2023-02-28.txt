SB ENERGY WELCOMES GAETAN FROTTE AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. FORMER NRG ENERGY EXECUTIVE ENTERS SB ENERGY AT STAGE OF HYPERGROWTH REDWOOD CITY, CALIF. , FEB. 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SB ENERGY GLOBAL, LLC ("SB ENERGY") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT GAETAN FROTTE HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER TO LEAD SB ENERGY'S CORPORATE FINANCE FUNCTIONS.
