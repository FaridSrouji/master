NRG ENERGY BECOMES FIRST NORTH AMERICAN COMPANY TO ISSUE SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED BOND. PRINCETON, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $NRG #ESG--NRG ENERGY INC. (NYSE:NRG) HAS COMPLETED THE ISSUANCE OF $900 MILLION IN SENIOR SECURED FIRST LIEN NOTES IN A LANDMARK ISSUANCE, WITH NRG PIONEERING THE FIRST SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED BOND (SLB) IN NORTH AMERICA, AND THE FIRST ISSUED BY ANY ENERGY COMPANY OUTSIDE OF EUROPE. IN CONCERT WITH THE DIRECT ENERGY ACQUISITION, THE SLB WILL SUPPORT THE COMPANY'S EFFORTS TO PURSUE GROWTH, ACHIEVE ITS CLIMATE TRANSITION STRATEGY, AND BRING INCREASING VALUE TO ITS STAKEHOLDE
