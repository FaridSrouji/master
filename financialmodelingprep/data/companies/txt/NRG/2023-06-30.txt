HELMERICH & PAYNE, INC. ANNOUNCES THE APPOINTMENT OF NEW DIRECTOR. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HELMERICH & PAYNE, INC. (NYSE: HP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ELIZABETH KILLINGER WAS APPOINTED TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. KILLINGER IS CURRENTLY EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, NRG HOME, A DIVISION OF NRG ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NRG), WHICH PROVIDES RESIDENTIAL POWER AND GAS SERVICES TO MILLIONS OF CUSTOMERS THROUGH MULTIPLE BRANDS AND CHANNELS IN THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA. SHE HAS OVER 30 YEARS OF DOMESTIC AND INTERNATIONAL EXPERIENCE IN THE ENERGY AND SERVICES.
