NRG ENERGY JOINS THE EV100 INITIATIVE. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NRG ENERGY, INC. ANNOUNCED ITS PARTICIPATION IN THE CLIMATE GROUP'S EV100 INITIATIVE, COMMITTING TO ELECTRIFY 100 PERCENT OF ITS LIGHT-DUTY FLEET BY 2030. THIS COMMITMENT EXPANDS THE NUMBER OF VEHICLES TO BE ELECTRIFIED BEYOND THE GOAL FIRST ANNOUNCED IN JUNE IN THE COMPANY'S 11TH ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT1.
