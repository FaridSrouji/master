NRG ENERGY, INC. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NRG ENERGY, INC. (NYSE:NRG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK OF $0.35 PER SHARE, OR $1.40 PER SHARE ON AN ANNUALIZED BASIS. THIS DIVIDEND REPRESENTS AN 8% INCREASE FROM THE PRIOR YEAR IN-LINE WITH THE COMPANY'S PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ANNUAL DIVIDEND GROWTH RATE TARGET OF 7-9% PER SHARE.
