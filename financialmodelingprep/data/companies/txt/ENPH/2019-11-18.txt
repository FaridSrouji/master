ENPHASE ENERGY, INC. ANNOUNCES STRATEGIC SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH SUNRUN. ENPHASE ENERGY, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS SIGNED A STRATEGIC SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH SUNRUN INC. AS PART OF THE AGREEMENT, ENPHASE WILL PROVIDE ITS SEVENTH-GENERATION ENPHASE IQ™ MICROINVERTERS TO SUNRUN FOR USE IN ITS RESIDENTIAL SOLAR BUSINESS. SUNRUN’S CUSTOMERS WILL BENEFIT FROM ENPHASE’S IQ 7™ AND IQ 7+™ MICROINVERTERS, WHICH LEVERAGE ENPHASE’S UNIQUE SOFTWARE-DEFINED ARCHITECTURE AND SEMICONDUCTOR INTEGRATION FOR EXCELLENT RELIABILITY AND ECONOMIES OF SCALE. ENPHASE MICROINVERTERS ARE SUBJECTED TO A RIGOROUS RELIABILITY AND QUALITY TESTING REGIMEN WITH MORE THAN ONE MILLION HOURS OF POWER-ON TESTING TO ENSURE EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE UNDER HEAT, HIGH HUMIDITY, SALTY AIR, EXTREME COLD, AND HARSH CLIMATE CONDITIONS. THE COMPANY’S MICROINVERTERS ARE DESIGNED TO BE LONG-LIVED ENERGY ASSETS AND ARE BACKED BY A 25-YEAR WARRANTY.
