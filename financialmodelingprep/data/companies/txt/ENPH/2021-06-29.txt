ENPHASE LAUNCHES ENCHARGE® BATTERY STORAGE SYSTEM IN GERMANY. FREMONT, CALIF., JUNE 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ENPHASE ENERGY, INC.  (NASDAQ: ENPH), A GLOBAL ENERGY MANAGEMENT TECHNOLOGY COMPANY AND THE WORLD'S LEADING SUPPLIER OF MICROINVERTER-BASED SOLAR-PLUS-STORAGE SYSTEMS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS ENCHARGE BATTERY STORAGE SYSTEM IN GERMANY, THE PRODUCT'S FIRST EXPANSION INTO A MARKET OUTSIDE OF THE UNITED STATES.
