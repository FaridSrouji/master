DISNEY RECOMMENDS SHAREHOLDERS REJECT MINI-TENDER OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT. BURBANK, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY (NYSE: DIS) RECENTLY BECAME AWARE THAT TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT CORP. (TRC) HAS MADE AN UNSOLICITED MINI-TENDER OFFER TO PURCHASE UP TO 1,500,000 SHARES OF DISNEY COMMON STOCK, AND, PER A PRESS RELEASE ISSUED BY TRC ON DECEMBER 28, 2022 (THE TRC PRESS RELEASE), HAS AMENDED THE TERMS OF SUCH OFFER TO DECREASE THE OFFER PRICE PAYABLE TO $85.00 A SHARE, DOWN FROM A PRIOR OFFER PRICE OF $89.15 A SHARE.
