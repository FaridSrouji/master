DIS LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , JUNE 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY ("DISNEY" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: DIS) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF DISNEY INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN DECEMBER 10, 2020 AND NOVEMBER 8, 2022.
