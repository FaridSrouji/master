THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY ANNOUNCES SEMI-ANNUAL CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 16, 2020. THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT A SEMI-ANNUAL CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.88 PER SHARE, PAYABLE JANUARY 16, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 16, 2019.  THE COMPANY LAST PAID A SEMI-ANNUAL DIVIDEND OF $0.88 PER SHARE IN JULY, BRINGING ITS TOTAL DIVIDENDS FOR THE FISCAL YEAR TO $1.76 PER SHARE.
