PAUL RICHARDSON NAMED CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER OF THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY, EFFECTIVE JULY 1. BURBANK, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PAUL RICHARDSON HAS BEEN NAMED SENIOR EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER OF THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY (NYSE: DIS), EFFECTIVE JULY 1, IT WAS ANNOUNCED TODAY BY BOB CHAPEK, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. MR. RICHARDSON, WHO IS CURRENTLY SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, HUMAN RESOURCES FOR ESPN AND WAS DISNEY'S FIRST CHIEF DIVERSITY OFFICER, WILL SUCCEED LONGTIME VETERAN JAYNE PARKER, WHO PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SHE IS LEAVING THE COMPANY AT THE END OF JUNE AFT
