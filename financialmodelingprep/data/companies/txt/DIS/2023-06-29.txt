DIS INVESTORS HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY SECURITIES FRAUD LAWSUIT. LOS ANGELES , JUNE 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LAW OFFICES OF FRANK R. CRUZ ANNOUNCES THAT INVESTORS WITH SUBSTANTIAL LOSSES HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD THE SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY ("DISNEY" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: DIS).
DIS LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , JUNE 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY ("DISNEY" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: DIS) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF DISNEY INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN DECEMBER 10, 2020 AND NOVEMBER 8, 2022.
