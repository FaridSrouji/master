THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY INVITES APPLICATIONS FROM INNOVATIVE GROWTH-STAGE COMPANIES FOR 2022 DISNEY ACCELERATOR PROGRAM. BURBANK, CALIF. , APRIL 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT IS ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS FOR THE 2022 DISNEY ACCELERATOR, A BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM DESIGNED TO ACCELERATE THE GROWTH OF INNOVATIVE COMPANIES FROM AROUND THE WORLD.
