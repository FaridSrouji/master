THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY NAMES JAN KOEPPEN AS NEW PRESIDENT IN EUROPE, THE MIDDLE EAST AND AFRICA, EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 11, 2019. THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY HAS NAMED JAN KOEPPEN AS ITS NEW PRESIDENT IN EUROPE, THE MIDDLE EAST AND AFRICA. KOEPPEN, WHO HAS BEEN SERVING AS PRESIDENT OF TELEVISION AND DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER FOR EUROPE AND AFRICA, WILL TAKE OVER FROM REBECCA CAMPBELL, WHO IS MOVING TO THE DISNEYLAND RESORT BUSINESS. KOEPPEN'S NEW ROLE WILL BEGIN ON NOVEMBER 11TH, REPORTING TO KEVIN MAYER, DISNEY'S CHAIRMAN OF DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER AND INTERNATIONAL. HE'LL TAKE RESPONSIBILITY FOR DISNEY'S DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER BUSINESSES IN EUROPE, INCLUDING THE NEW DISNEY+, ALONGSIDE ITS MOVIE BUSINESSES, BUT EXCLUDING DISNEYLAND PARIS.
