ASAD AYAZ NAMED THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY'S FIRST-EVER CHIEF BRAND OFFICER. BURBANK, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ASAD AYAZ HAS BEEN NAMED THE FIRST-EVER CHIEF BRAND OFFICER OF THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY (NYSE: DIS), EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, IT WAS ANNOUNCED TODAY BY ROBERT A. IGER, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. IN THIS NEWLY CREATED ROLE REPORTING TO IGER, AYAZ WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR STEWARDING AND ELEVATING THE DISNEY BRAND GLOBALLY ACROSS THE ENTIRE ECOSYSTEM OF COMPANY TOUCHPOINTS AND CONSUMER EXPERIENCES.
