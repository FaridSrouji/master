ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC. REPORTS UNAUDITED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER AND NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC. ANNOUNCED UNAUDITED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER AND NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 3,479 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 3,613 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 868 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 889 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 660 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 638 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.05 COMPARED TO USD 1.91 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.04 COMPARED TO USD 1.9 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 10,640 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 11,188 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 2,578 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,724 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 1,880 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,956 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 5.79 COMPARED TO USD 5.81 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 5.76 COMPARED TO USD 5.77 A YEAR AGO.
