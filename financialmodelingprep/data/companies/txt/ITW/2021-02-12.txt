ITW BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. GLENVIEW, ILL., FEB. 12, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC. (NYSE: ITW) DECLARED A DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK OF $1.14 PER SHARE FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. THE DIVIDEND EQUATES TO $4.56 PER SHARE ON A FULL-YEAR BASIS. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 14, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MARCH 31, 2021.
