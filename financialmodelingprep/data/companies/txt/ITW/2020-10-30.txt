ITW BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. GLENVIEW, ILL., OCT. 30, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC. (NYSE: ITW) DECLARED A DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK OF $1.14 PER SHARE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020. THE DIVIDEND EQUATES TO $4.56 PER SHARE ON A FULL-YEAR BASIS. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON THURSDAY, JANUARY 14, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2020.
