ITW BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. GLENVIEW, ILL., OCT. 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC. (NYSE: ITW) DECLARED A DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK OF $1.31 PER SHARE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022. THE DIVIDEND EQUATES TO $5.24 PER SHARE ON A FULL-YEAR BASIS. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JANUARY 12, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 30, 2022.
