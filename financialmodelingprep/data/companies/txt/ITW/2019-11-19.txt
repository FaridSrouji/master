ALPHA ASSEMBLY SOLUTIONS, INC. ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE KESTER INC. FROM ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC. (NYSE:ITW).. ALPHA ASSEMBLY SOLUTIONS, INC. ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE KESTER INC. FROM ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC. (NYSE:ITW) ON NOVEMBER 20, 2019. ELEMENT SOLUTIONS INC (NYSE:ESI), PARENT OF ALPHA ASSEMBLY SOLUTIONS, INC., WILL MAKE KESTER A PART OF THE MACDERMID ALPHA BUSINESS. THE TRANSACTION WILL CLOSE EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 2, 2019.
