ITW BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. GLENVIEW, ILL., MAY 05, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC. (NYSE: ITW) DECLARED A DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK OF $1.31 PER SHARE FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2023. THE DIVIDEND EQUATES TO $5.24 PER SHARE ON A FULL-YEAR BASIS. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JULY 13, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 30, 2023.
