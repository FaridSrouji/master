PVH Q2 REVENUE DROPS 33% TO $1.581 BLN. . REPORTS 2020 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS AND PROVIDES BUSINESS UPDATE RELATING TO COVID-19 PANDEMIC.Q2 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.13.Q2 GAAP LOSS PER SHARE $0.72.Q2 REVENUE FELL 33 PERCENT TO $1.581 BILLION.Q2 REVENUE ESTIMATE $1.25 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $-2.43 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.ANTICIPATES ITS SECOND HALF REVENUE AND EARNINGS WILL CONTINUE TO BE NEGATIVELY IMPACTED BY COVID-19 PANDEMIC.EXPECTS REVENUE IN SECOND HALF TO DECLINE ABOUT 25% COMPARED TO PRIOR YEAR PERIOD.CANNOT PROVIDE MORE DETAILED GUIDANCE AT THIS TIME.AS WE HEAD INTO Q3, TRENDS IN CHINA AND EUROPE CONTINUE TO BE VERY ENCOURAGING.PVH - NORTH AMERICA BUSINESS CONTINUES TO EXPERIENCE PRESSURE DUE TO RESURGENCE OF COVID-19 CASES, LACK OF INTERNATIONAL TOURIST TRAFFIC COMING TO U.S..ENDED QUARTER WITH CASH ON HAND OF ABOUT $1.4 BILLION, ABOUT $1.3 BILLION OF AVAILABLE BORROWINGS UNDER ITS REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITIES.AS OF END OF FISCAL 2020, CO IS PROJECTING TO CARRY ABOUT $125 MILLION OF BASIC INVENTORY INTO SPRING 2021.
