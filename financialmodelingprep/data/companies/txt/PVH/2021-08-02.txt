PVH CORP. COMPLETES SALE OF HERITAGE BRANDS TO AUTHENTIC BRANDS GROUP. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $PVH #FASHION--PVH CORP. [NYSE: PVH] , PARENT COMPANY OF CALVIN KLEIN AND TOMMY HILFIGER, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SALE OF CERTAIN INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND OTHER ASSETS OF ITS HERITAGE BRANDS BUSINESS TO AUTHENTIC BRANDS GROUP (ABG). THE CASH PURCHASE PRICE FOR THE TRANSACTION, WHICH INCLUDES THE IZOD, VAN HEUSEN, ARROW AND GEOFFREY BEENE TRADEMARKS AND CERTAIN RELATED INVENTORIES AND OTHER ASSETS, WAS $223 MILLION, SUBJECT TO ADJUSTMENT. STEFAN LAR
