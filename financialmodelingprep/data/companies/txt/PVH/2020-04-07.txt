PVH CORP - FURLOUGHS, DECREASED WORKING HOURS AND SALARY REDUCTIONS IN NORTH AMERICA. PVH CORP. ANNOUNCES BUSINESS UPDATE RELATING TO COVID-19 OUTBREAK.PVH CORP - BOARD OF DIRECTORS COMPENSATION SUSPENDED.PVH CORP - FURLOUGHS, DECREASED WORKING HOURS AND SALARY REDUCTIONS IN NORTH AMERICA.PVH CORP - ADDITIONALLY, APPROXIMATELY 250 SENIOR LEADERS AND EXECUTIVES GLOBALLY WILL EXPERIENCE SALARY REDUCTIONS OF UP TO 50%.PVH CORP - FURLOUGHS WILL BE UNPAID AND ASSOCIATES WHO HAVE THEIR HOURS REDUCED WILL HAVE THEIR PAY REDUCED PROPORTIONALLY TO THEIR HOUR REDUCTIONS.PVH CORP - ALMOST ALL ASSOCIATES IN AUSTRALIA ARE FURLOUGHED.PVH CORP - ALL REMAINING FULL-TIME ASSOCIATES WILL HAVE A TEMPORARY SALARY REDUCTION OF 5%-20% DEPENDING ON SALARY LEVEL..PVH CORP - COMPANY WILL COVER ALL ASSOCIATES' SHARE OF MEDICAL BENEFIT COSTS DURING PERIOD REGARDLESS OF SITUATION..PVH CORP - INVESTIGATING ITS OPTIONS IN REGARD TO GOVERNMENTAL PAY SUBSIDIES, WORKING HOUR REDUCTIONS AND SALARY REDUCTIONS IN BRAZIL.PVH CORP - IN NORTH AMERICA, APPROXIMATELY 75% OF STORE, OFFICE AND WAREHOUSE ASSOCIATES WILL BE FURLOUGHED OR HAVE THEIR WORKING HOURS DECREASED.PVH CORP - IMPLEMENTING TEMPORARY SALARY REDUCTIONS FOR ALL OFFICE ASSOCIATES IN ASIA.PVH CORP - MANNY CHIRICO, CHAIRMAN AND CEO, HAS ELECTED TO FORGO HIS SALARY WHILE CRISIS CONTINUES.PVH CORP - PVH HAS PUT ALL HIRING ON HOLD AND WILL NOT MAKE ANY MERIT INCREASES TO SALARIES IN 2020..PVH CORP - PAYOUT LEVELS FOR 2020 PERFORMANCE BONUSES WILL BE REDUCED BY 50%.
