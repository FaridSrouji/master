(NYSE: TYL) ANNOUNCED THAT GOOCHLAND COUNTY, VIRGINIA, HAS SUCCESSFULLY GONE LIVE WITH TYLER'S ENTERPRISE PERMITTING & LICENSING (EPL) SOLUTION TO MANAGE ITS PERMITTING, PLANNING, AND CODE ENFORCEMENT.
