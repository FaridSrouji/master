TYLER TECHNOLOGIES NAMED TO DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX NORTH AMERICA. PLANO, TEXAS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TYLER TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: TYL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX (DJSI) NORTH AMERICA IN RECOGNITION OF ITS COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
