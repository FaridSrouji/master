TYLER TECHNOLOGIES NAMED TO WASHINGTON POST'S TOP WORKPLACES LIST FOR 2021. HERNDON, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $TYL #TYLERTECHNOLOGIES--TYLER TECHNOLOGIES ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS HERNDON, VIRGINIA, OFFICE HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY THE WASHINGTON POST AS A TOP WORKPLACE FOR 2021.
SAFEGRAPH ANNOUNCES OFFICIAL PARTNERSHIP WITH TYLER TECHNOLOGIES. DENVER, JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SAFEGRAPH, A DATA COMPANY THAT SPECIALIZES IN PROVIDING HIGH QUALITY DATA ON PLACES, HAS ANNOUNCED TODAY AN OFFICIAL PARTNERSHIP WITH TYLER TECHNOLOGIES, THE LARGEST PROVIDER OF INTEGRATED SOFTWARE AND TECHNOLOGY SERVICES TO THE PUBLIC SECTOR. THROUGH THIS PARTNERSHIP, SAFEGRAPH WILL MAKE THEIR POINTS OF INTEREST (POIS) AND FOOT TRAFFIC DATA AVAILABLE THROUGH THE TYLER RECOVERY INSIGHTS SOLUTION FOR MUNICIPAL AND STATE GOVERNMENTS.
