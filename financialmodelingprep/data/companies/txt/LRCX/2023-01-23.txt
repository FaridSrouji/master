REYNOLDS ADVISORY PARTNERS ACTS AS EXCLUSIVE FINANCIAL ADVISOR TO ESGEE TECHNOLOGIES ON ITS SALE TO LAM RESEARCH. LOS ANGELES--( BUSINESS WIRE )--REYNOLDS ADVISORY PARTNERS, LLC (“RAP” OR “REYNOLDS”) ACTED AS THE EXCLUSIVE FINANCIAL ADVISOR TO ESGEE TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (“ESGEE”) OF AUSTIN, TEXAS, ON ITS SALE TO LAM RESEARCH CORPORATION (“LAM”) OF FREMONT, CALIFORNIA. THE TRANSACTION CLOSED ON NOVEMBER 16, 2022. FINANCIAL TERMS HAVE NOT BEEN DISCLOSED.
