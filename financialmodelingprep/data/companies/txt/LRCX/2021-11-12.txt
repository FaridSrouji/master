LAM RESEARCH CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. FREMONT, CALIF., NOV. 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LAM RESEARCH CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LRCX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.50 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND PAYMENT WILL BE MADE JANUARY 5, 2022 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON DECEMBER 15, 2021. FUTURE DIVIDEND PAYMENTS ARE SUBJECT TO REVIEW AND APPROVAL BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
