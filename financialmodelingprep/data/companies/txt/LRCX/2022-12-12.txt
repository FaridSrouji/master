LAM RESEARCH CONTINUES ESG MOMENTUM WITH INCLUSION ON THE 2022 DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX FOR NORTH AMERICA. LAM ALSO EXTENDS FOCUS ON GLOBAL IMPACT BY JOINING THE UNITED NATIONS GLOBAL COMPACT FREMONT, CALIF. , DEC. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LAM RESEARCH CORP. (NASDAQ: LRCX) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED FOR ITS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PERFORMANCE WITH ITS INCLUSION ON THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX FOR NORTH AMERICA (DJSI NORTH AMERICA) FOR A SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
