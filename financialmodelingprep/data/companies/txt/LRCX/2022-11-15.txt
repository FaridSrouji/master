LAM RESEARCH ACQUIRES SEMSYSCO TO ADVANCE CHIP PACKAGING. FREMONT, CALIF. , NOV. 15, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LAM RESEARCH CORP. (NASDAQ: LRCX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF SEMSYSCO GMBH, A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF WET PROCESSING SEMICONDUCTOR EQUIPMENT FROM GRUENWALD EQUITY AND OTHER INVESTORS.
