LAM RESEARCH CORPORATION ANNOUNCES $5 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION AND DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. FREMONT, CALIF., MAY 11, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LAM RESEARCH CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LRCX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A $5 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION AND A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.50 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.
