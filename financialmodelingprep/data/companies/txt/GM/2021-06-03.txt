GM TO INCREASE VEHICLE DELIVERIES TO CUSTOMERS IN U.S., CANADA. DETROIT, JUNE 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, GENERAL MOTORS OUTLINED STEPS IT IS TAKING TO INCREASE DELIVERIES TO DEALERS AND CUSTOMERS IN THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA TO MEET STRONG CONSUMER DEMAND FOR CHEVROLET, BUICK, GMC AND CADILLAC VEHICLES. PRODUCTION OF THE CHEVROLET SILVERADO HD AND GMC SIERRA HD FULL-SIZE PICKUPS WILL INCREASE BY ABOUT 1,000 TRUCKS PER MONTH BEGINNING IN MID-JULY AS A RESULT OF PRODUCTION LINE EFFICIENCIES DELIVERED BY THE TEAM AT FLINT ASSEMBLY IN MICHIGAN.
