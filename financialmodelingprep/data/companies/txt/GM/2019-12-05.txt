GM TO LAY OFF 814 HOURLY AND SALARY WORKERS AT DETROIT-HAMTRAMCK STARTING FEBRUARY 28, 2020. GM ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS LAYING OFF 814 HOURLY AND SALARY WORKERS AT DETROIT-HAMTRAMCK STARTING FEB. 28. THE 753 HOURLY WORKERS IMPACTED WILL BE OFFERED BUYOUTS OR JOBS AT OTHER GM FACILITIES IN MICHIGAN AND OHIO.
