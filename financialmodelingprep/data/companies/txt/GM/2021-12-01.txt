GM AND POSCO CHEMICAL WILL BUILD A NEW NORTH AMERICA FACTORY TO PROCESS CATHODE ACTIVE MATERIAL FOR ULTIUM BATTERIES. DETROIT, DEC. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL MOTORS (NYSE: GM) AND POSCO CHEMICAL TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO FORM A JOINT VENTURE THROUGH WHICH THE PARTIES WILL CONSTRUCT A FACTORY IN NORTH AMERICA TO PROCESS CRITICAL BATTERY MATERIALS FOR GM'S ULTIUM ELECTRIC VEHICLE PLATFORM. THE JOINT VENTURE WILL PROCESS CATHODE ACTIVE MATERIAL (CAM), A KEY BATTERY MATERIAL THAT REPRESENTS ABOUT 40 PERCENT OF THE COST OF A BATTERY CELL.
