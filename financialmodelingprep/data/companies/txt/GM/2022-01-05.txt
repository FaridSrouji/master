PURE WATERCRAFT UNVEILS NEW ELECTRIC PONTOON BOAT. LAS VEGAS, JAN. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PURE WATERCRAFT, A LEADING MANUFACTURER OF ELECTRIC BOATS AND PROPULSION SYSTEMS, TODAY UNVEILED ITS GROUNDBREAKING ALL-ELECTRIC PONTOON BOAT AT CES AND OPENED PRE-ORDERS ON ITS WEB SITE.  THIS IS THE FIRST PRODUCT TO RESULT FROM PURE WATERCRAFT'S COLLABORATION WITH GENERAL MOTORS (NYSE: GM).
