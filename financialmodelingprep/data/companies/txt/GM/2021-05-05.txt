GM REPORTS STRONG FIRST-QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. DETROIT, MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL MOTORS CO. (NYSE: GM) TODAY REPORTED FIRST-QUARTER EARNINGS DRIVEN BY STRONG PRICE AND MIX PERFORMANCE IN NORTH AMERICA, STRONG CREDIT AND RESIDUAL VALUE PERFORMANCE AT GM FINANCIAL, AND THE INDUSTRY RECOVERY IN CHINA. THE COMPANY IS HIGHLY CONFIDENT IN ITS FULL-YEAR 2021 GUIDANCE OUTLINED EARLIER THIS YEAR AS IT WORKS TO MANAGE THROUGH THE SEMICONDUCTOR SHORTAGE, WHICH IS IMPACTING AUTOMAKERS GLOBALLY.
