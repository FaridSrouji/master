GM AND INRIX JOIN FORCES ON SAAS-BASED ROAD SAFETY SOLUTION TO HELP TRANSPORTATION PLANNERS ACHIEVE VISION ZERO GOALS FOR THEIR COMMUNITIES. SAFETY VIEW BY GM FUTURE ROADS & INRIX IS A CLOUD-BASED ANALYTICS APPLICATION LEVERAGING CONNECTED VEHICLE, PUBLIC SAFETY AND DEMOGRAPHIC DATA DESIGNED FOR VISION ZERO PLANS AND U.S. DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION SAFE STREETS AND ROADS FOR ALL PROGRAM DETROIT AND KIRKLAND, WASH. , MAY 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, GENERAL MOTORS (NYSE: GM) AND INRIX INC., A WORLD LEADER IN MOBILITY ANALYTICS AND CONNECTED CAR SERVICES, ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION TO DEVELOP SAFETY SOLUTIONS TO HELP TRANSPORTATION AND PLANNING AGENCIES ACHIEVE ROAD SAFETY GOALS.
