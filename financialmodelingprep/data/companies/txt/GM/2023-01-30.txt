AMERICAN RHEINMETALL VEHICLES AND GM DEFENSE WIN CONTRACT FOR THE FIRST PHASE OF THE U.S. ARMY'S COMMON TACTICAL TRUCK PROGRAM. STERLING HEIGHTS, MICH. AND WASHINGTON , JAN. 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN RHEINMETALL VEHICLES (STERLING HEIGHTS, MI) AND GM DEFENSE LLC (WASHINGTON, DC) HAVE WON A CONTRACT FOR THE FIRST PHASE OF THE U.S. ARMY'S COMMON TACTICAL TRUCK (CTT) PROGRAM.
