AUTOMOTIVE GIANT "GENERAL MOTORS" STRIKES A DEAL WITH SPARTA'S E-WASTE RECYCLING DIVISION TO DISCARD THE AUTOMAKER'S ELECTRONIC WASTE. TORONTO, ON / ACCESSWIRE / SEPTEMBER 7, 2022 / SPARTA GROUP (TSXV:SAY) (THE "CORPORATION", THE "COMPANY", "SPARTA GROUP", "SPARTA CAPITAL", "SAY.V" OR "SPARTA") HAS JUST ANNOUNCED THAT ITS E-WASTE RECYCLING DIVISION ("ERS INTERNATIONAL") HAS SIGNED A DEAL TO RECYCLE ELECTRONICS FROM GENERAL MOTORS CANADA'S MASSIVE 5.1 MILLION SQUARE FOOT MANUFACTURING FACILITY IN OSHAWA, ONTARIO. ELECTRONICS ARE NOW AT THE HEART OF THE AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRY WITH HIGH-POWER ELECTRONICS PROVIDING INFOTAINMENT, LIGHTING, AND SENSOR APPLICATIONS IN VEHICLES.
