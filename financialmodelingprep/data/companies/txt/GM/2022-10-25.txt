GM REPORTS THIRD-QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. DETROIT , OCT. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL MOTORS CO. (NYSE: GM) TODAY REPORTED THIRD-QUARTER NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO STOCKHOLDERS OF $3.3 BILLION AND EBIT-ADJUSTED OF $4.3 BILLION DRIVEN BY RECORD THIRD-QUARTER REVENUE OF $41.9 BILLION. GM REAFFIRMS ITS FULL-YEAR EARNINGS GUIDANCE.
