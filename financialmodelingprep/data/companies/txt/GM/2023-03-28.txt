GENERAL MOTORS NAMES HANON SYSTEMS A 2022 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR. SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA , MARCH 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL MOTORS RECOGNIZED GLOBAL AUTOMOTIVE THERMAL AND ENERGY MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS SUPPLIER HANON SYSTEMS AS A 2022 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR. GM CELEBRATED HONOREES AT ITS 31ST ANNUAL SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR AWARDS CEREMONY HELD ON MARCH 22 IN SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS.
