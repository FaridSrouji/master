GM ACCELERATES ITS DRIVE TO LEAD THE EV INDUSTRY WITH $7 BILLION INVESTMENT IN MICHIGAN, CREATING 4,000 NEW JOBS AND RETAINING 1,000. LANSING, MICH., JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL MOTORS CO. (NYSE: GM) ANNOUNCED TODAY AN INVESTMENT OF MORE THAN $7 BILLION IN FOUR MICHIGAN MANUFACTURING SITES, CREATING 4,000 NEW JOBS AND RETAINING 1,000, AND SIGNIFICANTLY INCREASING BATTERY CELL AND ELECTRIC TRUCK MANUFACTURING CAPACITY.
