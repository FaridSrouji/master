GENERAL MOTORS HIT WITH $102.6 MILLION JURY VERDICT IN CLASS ACTION OVER DEFECTIVE ENGINES. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN A RARE CLASS ACTION TRIAL VERDICT, A CALIFORNIA FEDERAL JURY TODAY FOUND THAT THE NATION'S LARGEST AUTOMOBILE MANUFACTURER HID AN ENGINE DEFECT THAT RESULTED IN EXCESSIVE OIL CONSUMPTION, LEADING TO ENGINE DAMAGE, STALLING, AND PREMATURE BREAKDOWN IN TENS OF THOUSANDS OF GENERAL MOTORS'S (NYSE: GM) 5.3-LITER SUVS AND LIGHT TRUCKS.
