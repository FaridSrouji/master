GM DEFENSE ANNOUNCES NEW ENTITY TO DRIVE INTERNATIONAL EXPANSION. WASHINGTON , JUNE 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GM DEFENSE IS TAKING AN IMPORTANT STEP TO FURTHER EXPAND ITS OFFERINGS ACROSS THE GLOBE BY ANNOUNCING A NEWLY FORMED ENTITY, GM DEFENSE INTERNATIONAL. THE NEW ENTITY WILL EXTEND GM DEFENSE'S REACH INTO GLOBAL MARKETS IN SUPPORT OF U.S. ALLIES AND PARTNERS' TRANSITION TO A MORE ELECTRIC, AUTONOMOUS AND CONNECTED FUTURE.
