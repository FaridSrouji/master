GM DEFENSE SELECTED AS A WINNER OF THE 2022 MANUFACTURING LEADERSHIP AWARDS. WASHINGTON , APRIL 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE NATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF MANUFACTURERS' (NAM) MANUFACTURING LEADERSHIP COUNCIL HAS SELECTED GM DEFENSE, A SUBSIDIARY OF GENERAL MOTORS, AS A WINNER OF THE 2022 MANUFACTURING LEADERSHIP AWARD IN THE OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE CATEGORY. NAM PROVIDES ANNUAL RECOGNITION TO TRANSFORMATIVE PROJECTS AND OUTSTANDING OPERATIONAL LEADERS SHAPING THE FUTURE OF GLOBAL MANUFACTURING.
