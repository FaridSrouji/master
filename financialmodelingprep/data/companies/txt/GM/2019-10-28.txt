GENERAL MOTORS COMPANY REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. GENERAL MOTORS COMPANY ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 35,473 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 35,791 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 2,304 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,610 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 2,351 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,534 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.62 COMPARED TO USD 1.77 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.6 COMPARED TO USD 1.75 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 106,411 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 108,650 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 6,035 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 3,616 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 6,926 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 5,970 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 4.79 COMPARED TO USD 4.19 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 4.74 COMPARED TO USD 4.13 A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 4.79 COMPARED TO USD 4.24 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 4.74 COMPARED TO USD 4.18 A YEAR AGO.
GENERAL MOTORS CO. DECLARES FOURTH QUARTER 2019 DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 19, 2019. GENERAL MOTORS COMPANY DECLARED A FOURTH QUARTER 2019 DIVIDEND OF 38 CENTS PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 19, 2019, TO ALL COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 6, 2019.
