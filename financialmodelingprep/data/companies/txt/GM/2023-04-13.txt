GM DEFENSE NAMES HENDRICK MOTORSPORTS 2022 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR. WASHINGTON , APRIL 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GM DEFENSE LLC, A SUBSIDIARY OF GENERAL MOTORS (GM), HAS AWARDED HENDRICK MOTORSPORTS WITH ITS 2022 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR DISTINCTION. THE SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR AWARD RECOGNIZES GLOBAL SUPPLIERS THAT DISTINGUISH THEMSELVES BY EXCEEDING REQUIREMENTS, IN TURN PROVIDING DEFENSE AND GOVERNMENT CUSTOMERS WITH INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGIES AND HIGH-QUALITY SOLUTIONS.
