GENERAL MOTORS NAMES HANON SYSTEMS A 2021 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR. SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA, MARCH 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL MOTORS RECOGNIZED GLOBAL AUTOMOTIVE THERMAL AND ENERGY MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS SUPPLIER HANON SYSTEMS AS A 2021 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR. GM CELEBRATED HONOREES AT ITS 30TH ANNUAL SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR AWARDS CEREMONY IN PHOENIX, ARIZONA, EARLIER THIS WEEK.
