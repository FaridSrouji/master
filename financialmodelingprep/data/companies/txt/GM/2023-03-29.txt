GM FINANCIAL ANNOUNCES 2022 AUCTION OF THE YEAR WINNERS. FORT WORTH, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GM FINANCIAL TODAY NAMED FIVE REGIONAL AUCTION OF THE YEAR WINNERS FOR 2022 AND ONE MOST VALUABLE AUCTION OF THE YEAR WINNER. THESE PRESTIGIOUS AWARDS ARE PART OF GM FINANCIAL'S ANNUAL AUCTION OF EXCELLENCE PROGRAM RECOGNIZING THE NATION'S HIGHEST-PERFORMING AUCTION LOCATIONS.
DANA RECOGNIZED AS A 2022 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR BY GENERAL MOTORS FOR THE SIXTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. MAUMEE, OHIO , MARCH 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DANA INCORPORATED (NYSE: DAN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ONCE AGAIN IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY GENERAL MOTORS AS A 2022 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR. GM CELEBRATED HONOREES AT ITS 31ST ANNUAL SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR EVENT IN SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS, LAST WEEK.
