GM DEFENSE INCORPORATES GM DEFENSE CANADA. WASHINGTON , JUNE 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GM DEFENSE ANNOUNCED THAT IT INCORPORATED GM DEFENSE CANADA, IN ALIGNMENT WITH GM DEFENSE INTERNATIONAL, WHICH WAS ESTABLISHED IN 2022. AS GM DEFENSE EXPANDS AND PURSUES ADDITIONAL INTERNATIONAL PROGRAMS, THE FORMATION OF GM DEFENSE CANADA WILL STRENGTHEN THE BUSINESS' ABILITY TO SUPPORT EFFORTS TO MODERNIZE THE CANADIAN ARMED FORCES.
