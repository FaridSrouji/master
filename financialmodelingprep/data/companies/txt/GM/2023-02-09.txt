GLOBALFOUNDRIES AND GM ANNOUNCE LONG-TERM DIRECT SUPPLY AGREEMENT FOR U.S. PRODUCTION OF SEMICONDUCTOR CHIPS. MALTA, N.Y. AND DETROIT , FEB. 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL MOTORS CO. (NYSE: GM) AND GLOBALFOUNDRIES (NASDAQ: GFS) (GF) TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC, LONG-TERM AGREEMENT ESTABLISHING A DEDICATED CAPACITY CORRIDOR EXCLUSIVELY FOR GM'S CHIP SUPPLY.
