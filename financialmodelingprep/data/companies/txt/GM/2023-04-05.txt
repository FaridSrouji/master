GM DEFENSE RECEIVES FULL-RATE PRODUCTION DECISION FROM U.S. ARMY FOR INFANTRY SQUAD VEHICLE PROGRAM. WASHINGTON , APRIL 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GM DEFENSE LLC, A SUBSIDIARY OF GENERAL MOTORS, SAID TODAY THAT THE U.S. ARMY PROVIDED A FULL-RATE PRODUCTION DECISION FOR THE INFANTRY SQUAD VEHICLE (ISV). GM DEFENSE WON THE INITIAL $214.3 MILLION CONTRACT TO DEVELOP THE LIGHT AND AGILE ALL-TERRAIN TROOP CARRIER TO TRANSPORT A NINE-SOLDIER INFANTRY SQUAD AND THEIR EQUIPMENT IN JUNE 2020.
