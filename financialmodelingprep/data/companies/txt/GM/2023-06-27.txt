GM DEFENSE TO PROTOTYPE AN ADVANCED ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM FOR THE DEFENSE INNOVATION UNIT. WASHINGTON , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GM DEFENSE, A SUBSIDIARY OF GENERAL MOTORS, WAS SELECTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE'S (DOD) DEFENSE INNOVATION UNIT (DIU) TO PROTOTYPE AN ENERGY STORAGE UNIT. GM DEFENSE'S SOLUTION WILL MEET THE REQUIREMENTS OF DIU'S STABLE TACTICAL EXPEDITIONARY ELECTRIC POWER (STEEP) PROGRAM.
