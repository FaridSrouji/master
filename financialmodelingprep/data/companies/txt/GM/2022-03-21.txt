GENERAL MOTORS NAMES HARMAN A 2021 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR, GENERAL MOTORS HAS RECOGNIZED HARMAN, A WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS CO., LTD. FOCUSED ON CONNECTED TECHNOLOGIES FOR AUTOMOTIVE, CONSUMER AND ENTERPRISE MARKETS, AS A 2021 SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR. GM CELEBRATED HONOREES AT ITS 30TH ANNUAL SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR AWARDS CEREMONY IN PHOENIX, ARIZONA, EARLIER THIS WEEK.
