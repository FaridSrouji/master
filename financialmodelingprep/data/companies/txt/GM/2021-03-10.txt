GM FINANCIAL NAMED PRESIDENT'S VOLUNTEER SERVICE AWARD WINNER. FORT WORTH, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GM FINANCIAL HAS BEEN AWARDED THE U.S. PRESIDENT'S VOLUNTEER SERVICE AWARD FOR ITS COMMITMENT TO JUNIOR ACHIEVEMENT USA (JA). THE COMPANY RECEIVED THE AWARD IN RECOGNITION OF ITS HUNDREDS OF EMPLOYEES WHO VOLUNTEERED TIME TO JA IN SUPPORT OF ITS EDUCATIONAL INITIATIVES.
