GENERAL MOTORS NAMES RAL MIER AS NEW DIRECTOR OF SALES AND OPERATIONS FOR COLOMBIA. GENERAL MOTORS HAS NAMED RAL MIER AS ITS NEW DIRECTOR OF SALES AND OPERATIONS FOR COLOMBIA. THE MOVE IS PART OF THE MANUFACTURERS CONSOLIDATION OF ITS SOUTH AMERICAN BUSINESS AND FOLLOWS THE MERGE OF ITS THREE BRANCHES IN THE REGION EARLIER THIS YEAR. MIER HAS WORKED IN THE AUTOMOTIVE SECTOR FOR MORE THAN 20 YEARS IN ROLES SUCH AS MARKETING, PLANNING, EXPORTS AND FINANCING. HIS PREVIOUS POSTS HAVE INCLUDED CHILE AND JAPAN AND HE HOLDS AN UNDERGRADUATE DEGREE FROM THE UNIVERSIDAD DE LOS ANDES WITH POSTGRADUATE STUDIES IN INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS AND NEGOTIATION STRATEGIES.
