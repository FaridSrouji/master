P&G SHAREHOLDERS ELECT ALL 11 P&G DIRECTORS; BOARD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AT 2022 ANNUAL MEETING. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY (NYSE:PG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT AT ITS 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS, RAJESH SUBRAMANIAM, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF FEDEX CORPORATION, WAS NEWLY ELECTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. P&G SHAREHOLDERS ELECTED ALL 11 P&G DIRECTOR NOMINEES, INCLUDING MR. SUBRAMANIAM AND 10 INCUMBENT DIRECTORS, WITH EACH RECEIVING MORE THAN 90 PERCENT SUPPORT ACCORDING TO PRELIMINARY VOTING RESULTS.
