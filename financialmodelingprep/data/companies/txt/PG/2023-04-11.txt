P&G APPOINTS SHEILA BONINI AND ROB PORTMAN TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY (NYSE:PG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED SHEILA BONINI, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF THE WORLD WILDLIFE FUND, AND ROB PORTMAN, FORMER U.S. SENATOR, CONGRESSMAN AND UNITED STATES TRADE REPRESENTATIVE, TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. THE APPOINTMENTS ARE EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MS.
