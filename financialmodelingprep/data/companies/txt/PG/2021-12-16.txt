P&G VENTURES SELECTS FINALISTS FOR CES INNOVATION CHALLENGE PITCH COMPETITION. CINCINNATI, DEC. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- P&G VENTURES, THE EARLY-STAGE STARTUP STUDIO WITHIN PROCTER & GAMBLE (NYSE:PG), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE TOP FOUR FINALISTS OF THIS YEAR'S INNOVATION CHALLENGE.
