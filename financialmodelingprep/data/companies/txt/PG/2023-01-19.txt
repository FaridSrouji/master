P&G ANNOUNCES FISCAL YEAR 2023 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS. CINCINNATI--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY (NYSE:PG) REPORTED SECOND QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2023 NET SALES OF $20.8 BILLION, A DECREASE OF ONE PERCENT VERSUS THE PRIOR YEAR. EXCLUDING THE IMPACTS OF FOREIGN EXCHANGE AND ACQUISITIONS AND DIVESTITURES, ORGANIC SALES INCREASED FIVE PERCENT. DILUTED NET EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $1.59, A DECREASE OF FOUR PERCENT VERSUS PRIOR YEAR EPS.
