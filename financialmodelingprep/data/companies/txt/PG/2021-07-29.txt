JON R. MOELLER ELECTED P&G PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY (NYSE: PG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JON R. MOELLER, VICE CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, WILL SUCCEED DAVID TAYLOR AS PROCTER & GAMBLE'S PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 1, 2021. MR.
