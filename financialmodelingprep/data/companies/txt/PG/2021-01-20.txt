P&G ANNOUNCES FISCAL YEAR 2021 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY (NYSE:PG) REPORTED SECOND QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2021 NET SALES OF $19.7 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF EIGHT PERCENT VERSUS THE PRIOR YEAR. EXCLUDING THE NET IMPACTS OF FOREIGN EXCHANGE, ACQUISITIONS AND DIVESTITURES, ORGANIC SALES ALSO INCREASED EIGHT PERCENT.
