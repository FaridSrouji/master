COMPANIES UNITE TO ADDRESS EDUCATION'S DIGITAL DIVIDE WITH $25 MILLION INVESTMENT. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, A NATIONWIDE COALITION LED BY PROCTER & GAMBLE (NYSE:PG) ANNOUNCED $25 MILLION IN INVESTMENTS TOWARDS A GOAL TO PUT ONE MILLION CONNECTED DEVICES IN THE HANDS OF STUDENTS WITHOUT ACCESS TO THE BASIC TOOLS NEEDED TO ENGAGE IN DIGITAL LEARNING, A NEED THAT'S BEEN AMPLIFIED BY THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
