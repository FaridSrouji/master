P&G ANNOUNCES FISCAL YEAR 2021 THIRD QUARTER RESULTS. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY (NYSE:PG) REPORTED THIRD QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2021 NET SALES OF $18.1 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF FIVE PERCENT VERSUS THE PRIOR YEAR. EXCLUDING THE IMPACTS OF FOREIGN EXCHANGE, ACQUISITIONS AND DIVESTITURES, ORGANIC SALES INCREASED FOUR PERCENT.
