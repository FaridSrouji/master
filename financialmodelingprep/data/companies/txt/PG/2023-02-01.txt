ALLSTATE ELECTS MONICA TURNER TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) ANNOUNCED THAT MONICA TURNER, 57, PRESIDENT, NORTH AMERICA, AT PROCTER & GAMBLE (NYSE: PG), HAS BEEN ELECTED TO ALLSTATE'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE FEB. 1. AS OF THAT DATE, ALLSTATE'S BOARD WILL HAVE 12 DIRECTORS.
