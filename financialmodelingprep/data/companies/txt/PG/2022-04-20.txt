P&G ANNOUNCES FISCAL YEAR 2022 THIRD QUARTER RESULTS. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY (NYSE:PG) REPORTED THIRD QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2022 NET SALES OF $19.4 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF SEVEN PERCENT VERSUS THE PRIOR YEAR. EXCLUDING THE IMPACTS OF FOREIGN EXCHANGE, ACQUISITIONS AND DIVESTITURES, ORGANIC SALES INCREASED 10%. DILUTED NET EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $1.33, AN INCREASE OF SIX PERCENT VERSUS PRIOR YEAR EPS.
