PROCTER & GAMBLE ANNOUNCES FIRST LENOR PAPER BOTTLE PILOT AND JOINS PABOCO COMMUNITY OF PIONEER BRANDS. GENEVA, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--P&G UNVEILS ITS FIRST PAPER BOTTLE FOR LENOR IN PARTNERSHIP WITH PAPER BOTTLE COMPANY PABOCO, ANNOUNCING A PILOT FOR WESTERN EUROPE IN 2022. THE TRIAL ROLLOUT WILL FORM THE BASIS OF A TEST AND LEARN STRATEGY TO SCALE UP PAPER PACKAGING AND INCORPORATE IT MORE WIDELY ACROSS P&G'S PORTFOLIO.
