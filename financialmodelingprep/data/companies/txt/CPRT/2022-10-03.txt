BETTERINVESTING™ MAGAZINE UPDATE ON COPART INC. AND CHARTER COMMUNICATIONS INC.. TROY, MICH. , OCT. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE EDITORIAL ADVISORY AND SECURITIES REVIEW COMMITTEE OF BETTERINVESTING MAGAZINE TODAY ANNOUNCED COPART INC. (NASDAQ: CPRT) AS ITS "STOCK TO STUDY" AND CHARTER COMMUNICATIONS INC. (NASDAQ: CHTR) AS ITS "UNDERVALUED STOCK" IN THE DECEMBER 2022 ISSUE FOR INVESTORS' INFORMATIONAL AND EDUCATIONAL USE.
