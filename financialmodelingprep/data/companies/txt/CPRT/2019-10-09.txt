COPART, INC. EXPANDS MONTREAL, QUEBEC LOCATION IN CANADA. COPART, INC. ANNOUNCED A 24-ACRE EXPANSION TO ITS MONTREAL LOCATION IN QUEBEC, CANADA. THIS LOCATION IS ONE OF SEVEN COPART LOCATIONS IN CANADA INCLUDING: CALGARY, EDMONTON, MONCTON, HALIFAX, LONDON AND TORONTO.
