MATCH GROUP CLOSES ACQUISITION OF HYPERCONNECT. DALLAS AND SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA, JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MATCH GROUP (NASDAQ: MTCH) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF HYPERCONNECT, A LEADING SOCIAL DISCOVERY AND VIDEO TECHNOLOGY COMPANY. THE $1.725 BILLION TRANSACTION WAS COMPLETED 50% IN CASH AND 50% IN MATCH GROUP STOCK.
