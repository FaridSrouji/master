MATCH GROUP ANNOUNCES PRICING OF SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. DALLAS, SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MATCH GROUP (NASDAQ: MTCH) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS INDIRECT WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY, MATCH GROUP HOLDINGS II, LLC (THE "ISSUER") HAS AGREED TO SELL $500.0 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 3.625% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE "NOTES") IN A PRIVATE OFFERING (THE "NOTES OFFERING").
