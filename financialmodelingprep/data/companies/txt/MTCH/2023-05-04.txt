FINAL DEADLINE TOMORROW: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN MATCH GROUP, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / MAY 4, 2023 / THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST MATCH GROUP, INC. ("MATCH" OR "THE COMPANY") (NASDAQ:MTCH) FOR VIOLATIONS OF 10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION. INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED THE COMPANY'S SECURITIES BETWEEN NOVEMBER 3, 2021 AND JANUARY 31, 2023, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD"), ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THE FIRM BEFORE MAY 5, 2023.
