HINGE PROMOTES MICHELLE PARSONS TO CHIEF PRODUCT OFFICER. NEW YORK, JULY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HINGE, THE DATING APP "DESIGNED TO BE DELETED," HAS ANNOUNCED THE PROMOTION OF MICHELLE PARSONS TO CHIEF PRODUCT OFFICER. AS CHIEF PRODUCT OFFICER, MICHELLE WILL FOCUS ON BOTH ENHANCING THE USER EXPERIENCE THROUGH PRODUCT INNOVATIONS AND DEVELOPING INTERNAL SYSTEMS THAT WILL HELP SCALE PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT.
