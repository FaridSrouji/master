SINGLES IDENTIFY 2023'S TOP DATING TRENDS. ACCORDING TO SINGLES, "MCE" (MAIN CHARACTER ENERGY) IS THE NEW BDE VANCOUVER, BC , OCT. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MATCH GROUP COMPANY AND DATING APP PLENTY OF FISH TODAY REVEALED ITS SIXTH ANNUAL LIST OF DATING TRENDS FOR THE UPCOMING YEAR. TO IDENTIFY THE NEW TRENDS, PLENTY OF FISH POLLED OVER 8,000 U.S. MEMBERS, FINDING THAT AFTER THE CHALLENGES OF DATING DURING THE PANDEMIC, 2023 WILL BE THE YEAR OF MAIN CHARACTER ENERGY, WITH SINGLES PRIORITIZING THEIR WANTS AND NEEDS AND FOCUSING ON THEMSELVES MORE THAN EVER.
