MTCH ALERT: THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF MAY 5, 2023 IN THE CLASS ACTION FILED ON BEHALF OF MATCH GROUP, INC. SHAREHOLDERS. NEW YORK , MARCH 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION COMPLAINT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF MATCH GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ: MTCH) ALLEGING THAT THE COMPANY VIOLATED FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS. THIS LAWSUIT IS ON BEHALF OF A CLASS OF ALL PERSONS AND ENTITIES WHO PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED MATCH COMMON STOCK BETWEEN NOVEMBER 3, 2021, THROUGH JANUARY 31, 2023.LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE: MAY 5, 2023 NO OBLIGATION OR COST TO YOU.
