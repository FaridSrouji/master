VETERAN TECH INDUSTRY CMO CHRISTINA KOZLOFF JOINS PLENTY OF FISH. VANCOUVER, BC , JUNE 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PLENTY OF FISH TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS NAMED TECHNOLOGY INDUSTRY VETERAN CHRISTINA KOZLOFF AS ITS CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER (CMO). AN EXCEPTIONALLY EXPERIENCED CONSUMER MARKETER AND LEADER, CHRISTINA HAS LED TEAMS AND PROGRAMS AT EXPEDIA, MICROSOFT, AND ZILLOW.
