LOVE AT FIRST CUE: PLENTY OF FISH INTRODUCES CATEGORY'S FIRST IN-APP DATING CARD GAME. CUE'D UP OFFERS SINGLES A FUN AND FRIENDLY WAY TO BREAK THE ICE VANCOUVER, BC , OCT. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, PLENTY OF FISH , THE MOST WELCOMING DATING APP FOR SINGLES, ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ' CUE'D UP ', THE CATEGORY'S FIRST IN-APP CARD GAME DESIGNED TO MAKE DATING MORE FUN. WITH NEARLY TWO-THIRDS (63%) OF SINGLES SAYING THAT BREAKING THE ICE IS THE HARDEST PART ABOUT MEETING SOMEONE NEW1, CUE'D UP HELPS SINGLES MAKE MORE MEANINGFUL FIRST IMPRESSIONS WITH REFRESHING CONVERSATION STARTERS VIA A SERIES OF "SPICY" FILL-IN-THE-BLANK "CUES.
