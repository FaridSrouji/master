THE DATING TRENDS THAT WILL DEFINE 2022. VANCOUVER, BC, NOV. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DATING APP PLENTY OF FISH TODAY REVEALED ITS FIFTH ANNUAL LIST OF DATING TRENDS FOR THE UPCOMING YEAR. TO IDENTIFY THE NEW TRENDS, PLENTY OF FISH POLLED NEARLY 7,000 U.S. MEMBERS AND FOUND THAT SINGLES ARE SIMULTANEOUSLY EXPERIENCING CLARITY AND UNCERTAINTY AS IT RELATES TO RELATIONSHIPS, REINFORCING JUST HOW ACUTELY THE PANDEMIC HAS, AND WILL CONTINUE TO, INFLUENCE DATING.
