MTCH INVESTORS HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD MATCH GROUP, INC. SECURITIES FRAUD LAWSUIT. LOS ANGELES , APRIL 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP ("GPM") ANNOUNCES THAT INVESTORS WITH SUBSTANTIAL LOSSES HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD THE SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST MATCH GROUP, INC. ("MATCH" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: MTCH ). CLASS PERIOD: NOVEMBER 3, 2021 – JANUARY 31, 2023 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE: MAY 5, 2023 IF YOU WISH TO SERVE AS LEAD PLAINTIFF OF THE MATCH LAWSUIT, YOU CAN SUBMIT YOUR CONTACT INFORMATION AT WWW.GLANCYLAW.COM/CASES/MATCH-GROUP-INC/.
MTCH LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES MATCH GROUP, INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , APRIL 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN MATCH GROUP, INC. ("MATCH" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: MTCH) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF MATCH INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD.
