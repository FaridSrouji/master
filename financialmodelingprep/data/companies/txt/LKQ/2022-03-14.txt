ONE EQUITY PARTNERS TO ACQUIRE LKQ CORPORATION'S PGW AUTO GLASS BUSINESS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ONE EQUITY PARTNERS TO ACQUIRE LKQ CORPORATION'S PGW AUTO GLASS BUSINESS
LKQ CORPORATION AGREES TO SELL PGW AUTO GLASS BUSINESS TO ONE EQUITY PARTNERS. CHICAGO, MARCH 14, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LKQ CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LKQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL PGW AUTO GLASS (“PGW”), A DISTRIBUTOR OF AFTERMARKET GLASS TO THE NORTH AMERICAN AUTOMOTIVE SECTOR, TO ONE EQUITY PARTNERS. THE TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED DURING THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022 AND IS SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS AND NECESSARY REGULATORY APPROVALS.
