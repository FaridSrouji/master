LKQ CORPORATION ANNOUNCES AN INCREASE IN EQUITY BUYBACK.. ON OCTOBER 25, 2019, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED AN INCREASE IN ITS EQUITY BUYBACK PLAN. THE COMPANY INCREASED ITS AUTHORIZATION BY $500 MILLION, THEREBY BRINGING THE TOTAL AUTHORIZATION TO $1,000 MILLION. THE COMPANY EXTENDED ITS PLAN DURATION TILL OCTOBER 25, 2022.
