LKQ CORP ANNOUNCES AMENDMENT TO SENIOR SECURED CREDIT FACILITY. LKQ CORPORATION ANNOUNCES SECOND QUARTER 2020 BUSINESS UPDATE AND AMENDMENT TO SENIOR SECURED CREDIT FACILITY.LKQ CORP - REVENUE IS TRACKING AHEAD OF OUR INTERNAL EXPECTATIONS, WITH Q2 TO DATE PARTS AND SERVICES ORGANIC REVENUE DOWN APPROXIMATELY 25%.LKQ - WITH ECONOMIES REOPENING IN RECENT WEEKS, CAUTIOUSLY OPTIMISTIC THAT CAN CAPITALIZE ON MOMENTUM WE'VE BUILT THROUGH MAY TO FINISH QUARTER STRONG.LKQ - ALSO ANNOUNCED THAT IT ENTERED INTO AMENDMENT NO. 4 TO FOURTH AMENDED AND RESTATED CREDIT AGREEMENT DATED JANUARY 29, 2016.
