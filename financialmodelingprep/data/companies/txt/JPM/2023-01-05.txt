JPMORGAN CHASE SURVEY: MOST BUSINESSES EXPECT RECESSION IN 2023. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE MAJORITY OF SMALL AND MIDSIZE U.S. BUSINESS LEADERS ANTICIPATE A RECESSION IN 2023, ACCORDING TO JPMORGAN CHASE'S 2023 ANNUAL BUSINESS LEADERS OUTLOOK SURVEY RELEASED TODAY. IN TOTAL, 65% OF MIDSIZE BUSINESSES AND 61% OF SMALL BUSINESSES EXPECT A RECESSION IN THE YEAR AHEAD.
