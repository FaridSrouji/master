JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. EXPECTS TO HAVE 17 BRANCHES OPEN IN GREATER BOSTON. BY THE END OF DECEMBER, JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. EXPECTS TO HAVE 17 BRANCHES OPEN IN GREATER BOSTON. NEARLY HALF OF THEM WILL BE IN BOSTON ITSELF, WITH MOST OF THE REST LOCATED JUST OUTSIDE THE CITY'S BORDERS, IN PLACES LIKE DEDHAM AND BROOKLINE. THERE ARE MORE CHASE BRANCHES TO COME IN 2020, AS PART OF THE MEGABANK'S PLANS TO OPEN 50 RETAIL LOCATIONS LOCALLY WITHIN THE NEXT FEW YEARS. BUT IT'S STARTING TO LOOK BEYOND THE HUB AND ITS IMMEDIATE ENVIRONS. NEXT YEAR, CHASE PLANS TO OPEN LOCATIONS IN HINGHAM TO THE SOUTH AND WOBURN AND BURLINGTON TO THE NORTH. THAT'S NOT A DEFINITIVE LIST, BUT RATHER THOSE OPENINGS THAT HAVE BEEN REVEALED PUBLICLY BY THE BANK AND THROUGH FILINGS WITH FEDERAL BANKING REGULATORS. THE HINGHAM BRANCH, CHASE'S FIRST ON THE SOUTH SHORE, IS SCHEDULED TO OPEN IN MID-FEBRUARY IN DERBY STREET SHOPS AT ONE POINT CHASE HAD PLANS TO OPEN A NORTH QUINCY BRANCH IN 2019. IT STILL PLANS A LOCATION THERE, THOUGH THE TIMING IS UNCERTAIN, ACCORDING TO EVERT. ALSO, CHASE HAD APPLIED WITH FEDERAL REGULATORS TO OPEN A BRANCH IN ATTLEBORO ON ROUTE 1A, BUT WITHDREW THE APPLICATION EARLIER THIS MONTH.
