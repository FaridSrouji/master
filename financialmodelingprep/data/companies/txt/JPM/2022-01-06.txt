AVANTA RESIDENTIAL REACHES FINANCIAL CLOSE FOR HOMES AT PAINTED TREE IN MCKINNEY, TEXAS. MORGAN.  THIS MARKS AVANTA'S SECOND PROJECT UNDER THE AVANTA BRAND IN TEXAS AND J.P.
