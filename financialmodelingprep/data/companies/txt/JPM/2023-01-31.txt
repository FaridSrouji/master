CONRATH TO LEAD RETIREMENT INSIGHTS TEAM AND DRIVE RETIREMENT THOUGHT LEADERSHIP AGENDA NEW YORK , JAN. 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- J.P. MORGAN ASSET MANAGEMENT TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF MICHAEL CONRATH AS CHIEF RETIREMENT STRATEGIST, COMMENCING FEBRUARY 1.
