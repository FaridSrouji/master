THE WOMEN PRESIDENTS ORGANIZATION AND JPMORGAN CHASE RECOGNIZE THE 2022 RANKING OF 50 FASTEST GROWING WOMEN-OWNED/LED COMPANIES DURING AWARD CEREMONY MONTREAL , MAY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE WOMEN PRESIDENTS ORGANIZATION (WPO), IN COLLABORATION WITH JPMORGAN CHASE, TODAY RELEASED THE 15TH ANNUAL RANKING OF THE 50 FASTEST GROWING WOMEN-OWNED/LED COMPANIES. THIS YEAR'S 50 FASTEST LIST IS LED BY COMPANIES FOCUSED ON HEALTHCARE, GOVERNMENT AND TECHNOLOGY, AND FOR THE SECOND YEAR IN A ROW, THE TOP THREE COMPANIES ARE RUN BY WOMEN OF COLOR.
