ROSEN, A LEADING AND LONGSTANDING LAW FIRM, REMINDS JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. INVESTORS OF IMPORTANT DECEMBER 23 DEADLINE IN FIRST FILED SECURITIES CLASS ACTION COMMENCED BY THE FIRM; ENCOURAGES INVESTORS WITH LOSSES IN EXCESS OF $100K TO CONTACT THE FIRM - JPM. NEW YORK, NEW YORK--(NEWSFILE CORP. - DECEMBER 4, 2020) -  ROSEN LAW FIRM, A GLOBAL INVESTOR RIGHTS LAW FIRM, REMINDS PURCHASERS OF THE SECURITIES OF JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. (NYSE: JPM) BETWEEN FEBRUARY 23, 2016 AND SEPTEMBER 23, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD"), OF THE IMPORTANT DECEMBER 23, 2020 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE IN THE SECURITIES CLASS ACTION FIRST FILED BY THE FIRM. THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER DAMAGES FOR JPMORGAN INVESTORS UNDER THE FEDERAL SECURITIES...
