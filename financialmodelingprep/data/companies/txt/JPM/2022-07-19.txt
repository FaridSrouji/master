JPMORGAN CHASE ELECTS ALEX GORSKY TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JPMORGAN CHASE (NYSE: JPM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ALEX GORSKY, 62, HAS BEEN ELECTED DIRECTOR OF THE COMPANY, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MR. GORSKY SERVES AS EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN OF JOHNSON & JOHNSON. HE PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF THE COMPANY FROM 2012-2021. UNDER HIS LEADERSHIP, JOHNSON & JOHNSON HAS BECOME ONE OF THE WORLD'S LARGEST HEALTHCARE COMPANIES AND ONE OF THE FOREMOST INNOVATORS IN RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT FOR EMERGING HEALT
