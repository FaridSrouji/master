JPMORGAN CHASE COMMERCIAL BANKING LAUNCHES SPECIALIZED GREEN ECONOMY TEAM. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, JPMORGAN CHASE COMMERCIAL BANKING ANNOUNCED THE NEW GREEN ECONOMY SPECIALIZED INDUSTRY TEAM, WHICH WILL PROVIDE DEDICATED BANKING SERVICES AND EXPERTISE TO COMPANIES THAT PRODUCE ENVIRONMENTALLY-FRIENDLY GOODS AND SERVICES OR FOCUS ON ENVIRONMENTAL CONSERVATION. JPMORGAN CHASE COMMERCIAL BANKING HAS NAMED BRIAN LEHMAN AS THE HEAD OF GREEN ECONOMY.
