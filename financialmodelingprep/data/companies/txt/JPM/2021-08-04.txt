CHASE EXPANDS RETAIL BRANCHES TO ALL LOWER 48 STATES. BILLINGS, MONT.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHASE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS OFFICIALLY OPENED BANK BRANCHES IN ALL OF THE LOWER 48 STATES, MARKING AN IMPORTANT MILESTONE IN ITS EXPANSION PLANS AND AS THE FIRST NATIONAL BANK TO HAVE A RETAIL PRESENCE IN THE CONTIGUOUS UNITED STATES. THE BANK OPENED ITS FIRST BRANCH IN BILLINGS, MONTANA EARLIER THIS WEEK FOLLOWING OPENINGS IN ARKANSAS, IOWA, MAINE, MISSISSIPPI, NEW MEXICO, NORTH AND SOUTH DAKOTA, WYOMING AND VERMONT IN JULY. THE BANK IS MORE THAN HALFWAY
