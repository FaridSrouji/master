JPMORGAN CHASE ACQUIRES SUBSTANTIAL MAJORITY OF ASSETS AND ASSUMES CERTAIN LIABILITIES OF FIRST REPUBLIC BANK. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JPMORGAN CHASE (NYSE: JPM) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ACQUIRED THE SUBSTANTIAL MAJORITY OF ASSETS AND ASSUMED THE DEPOSITS AND CERTAIN OTHER LIABILITIES OF FIRST REPUBLIC BANK FROM THE FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION (FDIC). IN CARRYING OUT THIS TRANSACTION, JPMORGAN CHASE IS SUPPORTING THE U.S. FINANCIAL SYSTEM THROUGH ITS SIGNIFICANT STRENGTH AND EXECUTION CAPABILITIES.
