GERMAN MITTELSTAND LEADERS BRACE FOR RECESSION WHILE FINDING WAYS TO ADAPT TO ONGOING BUSINESS CHALLENGES, THIRD ANNUAL J.P. MORGAN SURVEY FINDS. FRANKFURT, GERMANY--( BUSINESS WIRE )--OPTIMISM IS FALLING AMONG GERMAN MITTELSTAND LEADERS, WITH NEARLY SIX IN TEN (59%) EXPECTING A RECESSION IN 2023, AND MORE ARE FEELING PESSIMISTIC ABOUT THE GLOBAL (24%) AND NATIONAL (28%) ECONOMY COMPARED TO LAST YEAR, ACCORDING TO J.P. MORGAN'S THIRD ANNUAL GERMANY BUSINESS LEADERS OUTLOOK SURVEY RELEASED TODAY.
