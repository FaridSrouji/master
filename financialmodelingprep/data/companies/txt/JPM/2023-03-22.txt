J.P. MORGAN TO ACQUIRE AUMNI, A LEADING PROVIDER OF INVESTMENT ANALYTICS SOFTWARE FOR VENTURE AND PRIVATE MARKET INVESTORS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--J.P. MORGAN (NYSE: JPM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE AUMNI, A LEADING PROVIDER OF INVESTMENT ANALYTICS SOFTWARE TO THE VENTURE CAPITAL INDUSTRY. FINANCIAL TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION WERE NOT DISCLOSED AND CLOSING IS EXPECTED IN THE FIRST HALF OF 2023.
