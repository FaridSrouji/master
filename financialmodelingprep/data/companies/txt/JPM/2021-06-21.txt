BRIGHTSPHERE INVESTMENT GROUP INC. ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO SELL CAMPBELL GLOBAL, LLC. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BRIGHTSPHERE INVESTMENT GROUP INC. (NYSE: BSIG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS 75% OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN CAMPBELL GLOBAL, LLC, (“CAMPBELL”) TO J.P. MORGAN ASSET MANAGEMENT (“J.P. MORGAN”), A DIVISION OF JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. (NYSE: JPM). AS PART OF THE TRANSACTION, J.P. MORGAN WILL ALSO ACQUIRE THE 25% OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN CAMPBELL HELD BY CAMPBELL MANAGEMENT.
