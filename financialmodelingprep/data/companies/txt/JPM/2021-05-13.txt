JPMORGAN CHASE RELEASES CARBON REDUCTION TARGETS FOR PARIS-ALIGNED FINANCING COMMITMENT. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JPMORGAN CHASE TODAY RELEASED COMPREHENSIVE STEPS IT IS TAKING IN ITS EFFORTS TO ALIGN ITS FINANCING ACTIVITIES WITH THE CLIMATE GOALS OF THE PARIS AGREEMENT. AS PART OF ITS PARIS-ALIGNED FINANCING COMMITMENT ANNOUNCED LAST FALL, THE FIRM HAS PUBLISHED 2030 CARBON INTENSITY TARGETS FOR THE OIL & GAS, ELECTRIC POWER AND AUTO MANUFACTURING SECTORS.
