MORGAN ASSET MANAGEMENT (JPMAM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF JPMORGAN ACTIVE GROWTH ETF (JGRO), AN ACTIVELY MANAGED STYLE PURE GROWTH ETF THAT SEEKS TO OUTPERFORM THE RUSSELL 1000 GROWTH INDEX.
