J.P. MORGAN ASSET MANAGEMENT ANNOUNCES LIQUIDATION OF TWO EXCHANGE-TRADED FUNDS. FUNDS ARE EXPECTED TO LIQUIDATE IN SEPTEMBER 2022 NEW YORK , AUG. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- J.P. MORGAN ASSET MANAGEMENT TODAY ANNOUNCED THE UPCOMING LIQUIDATION AND DISSOLUTION OF TWO EXCHANGE-TRADED FUNDS: JPMORGAN U.S. MINIMUM VOLATILITY ETF (JMIN), AND JPMORGAN U.S. DIVIDEND ETF (JDIV) (COLLECTIVELY, THE "FUNDS").
