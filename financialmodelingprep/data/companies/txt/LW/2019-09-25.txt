LAMB WESTON HOLDINGS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 29, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF LAMB WESTON HOLDINGS, INC. DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.20 PER SHARE OF LAMB WESTON COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 29, 2019, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 1, 2019.
