SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--SERVICENOW (NYSE: NOW), THE LEADING DIGITAL WORKFLOW COMPANY MAKING THE WORLD WORK BETTER FOR EVERYONE, HAS BEEN NAMED A LEADER IN THE 2023 GARTNER MAGIC QUADRANT FOR ENTERPRISE LOW-CODE APPLICATION PLATFORMS.
