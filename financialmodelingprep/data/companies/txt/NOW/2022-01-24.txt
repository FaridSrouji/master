TRANSLATIONS.COM ANNOUNCES CERTIFICATION OF THEIR NEXT-GENERATION SERVICENOW INTEGRATION. NEW YORK AND SAN JUAN, JAN. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRANSLATIONS.COM, THE TECHNOLOGY DIVISION OF TRANSPERFECT, THE WORLD'S LARGEST PROVIDER OF LANGUAGE AND TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS FOR GLOBAL BUSINESS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CERTIFICATION OF THE NEW GLOBALLINK CONNECT FOR SERVICENOW INTEGRATION. GLOBALLINK CONNECT FOR SERVICENOW, A NOW CERTIFIED APP, IS TRANSLATIONS.COM'S ALL-IN-ONE SOLUTION TO INITIATE, AUTOMATE, CONTROL, TRACK, AND COMPLETE ALL FACETS OF THE TRANSLATION PROCESS, ALL WITHIN THE SERVICENOW USER INTERFACE.
