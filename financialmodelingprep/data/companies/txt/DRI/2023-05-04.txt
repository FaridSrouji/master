SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISS LAW INVESTIGATES RUTH'S HOSPITALITY GROUP, INC.. NEW YORK , MAY 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISS LAW IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF RUTH'S HOSPITALITY GROUP, INC. (NASDAQ: RUTH), IN CONNECTION WITH ITS ACQUISITION VIA TENDER OFFER BY DARDEN RESTAURANTS, INC. (NASDAQ: DRI). UNDER THE MERGER AGREEMENT, RUTH SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $21.50 CASH PER SHARE.
