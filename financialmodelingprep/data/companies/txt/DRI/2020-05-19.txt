DARDEN REPORTS SAME-RESTAURANT SALES FELL 47.9% FOR Q4 TO DATE THROUGH MAY 17. DARDEN RESTAURANTS - FOR Q4 TO DATE THROUGH MAY 17, DARDEN SAME-RESTAURANT SALES DECLINED 47.9%.DARDEN RESTAURANTS - AS OF MAY 17, HAD 49% OF DINING ROOMS OPEN IN LIMITED CAPACITY.DARDEN RESTAURANTS INC - BEGINNING APRIL 27, BEGAN REOPENING DINING ROOMS LIMITED TO BETWEEN 25% - 50% CAPACITY.DARDEN RESTAURANTS - BASED ON INFORMATION AVAILABLE ON MAY 19, EXPECT TO HAVE MORE THAN 65% OF  DINING ROOMS OPEN WITH LIMITED CAPACITY BY END OF MAY.DARDEN RESTAURANTS INC - APPROXIMATELY $700 MILLION OF CASH ON HAND AS OF MAY 17.DARDEN RESTAURANTS - WELL POSITIONED FOR NEAR TERM WHILE RETAINING FINANCIAL FLEXIBILITY TO FULLY ACCESS $750 MILLION CREDIT FACILITY.DARDEN RESTAURANTS - BASED ON WEEK ENDING MAY 17 RESULTS, CO'S ONGOING WEEKLY CASH BURN RATE HAS IMPROVED TO LESS THAN $10 MILLION INCLUDING CAPEX.
