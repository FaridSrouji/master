MARSH MCLENNAN AGENCY ACQUIRES SOLV RISK SOLUTIONS. WHITE PLAINS, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MARSH MCLENNAN AGENCY, A SUBSIDIARY OF MARSH, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF SOLV RISK SOLUTIONS, LLC, A LEADING INDEPENDENT AGENCY BASED IN AUSTIN, TEXAS. TERMS OF THE ACQUISITION WERE NOT DISCLOSED. FOUNDED IN 2016, SOLV PROVIDES RISK MANAGEMENT ADVISORY SERVICES TO BUSINESSES, FAMILY OFFICES, AND PRIVATE EQUITY FIRMS ACROSS THE COUNTRY. FRANK BARBELLA, ADVISOR, AND A TEAM OF FOUR SOLV EMPLOYEES WILL JOIN MARSH MCLENNAN AGENCY AND WORK OUT OF THE HOUST.
