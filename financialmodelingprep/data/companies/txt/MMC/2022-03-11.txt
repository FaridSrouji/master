MARSH MCLENNAN APPOINTS KATHERINE J. BRENNAN SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL COUNSEL. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, MARSH MCLENNAN (NYSE: MMC), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM IN THE AREAS OF RISK, STRATEGY, AND PEOPLE, ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF KATHERINE J. BRENNAN AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL COUNSEL. SHE WILL REPORT TO DAN GLASER, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF MARSH MCLENNAN, AND WILL JOIN THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE. THE APPOINTMENT IS EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MS. BRENNAN WILL LEAD MARSH MCLENNAN'S GLOBAL LEGAL, COMPLIANCE AND PUBLIC AFFAIRS FUNCTION
