MARSH MCLENNAN DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MARSH MCLENNAN (NYSE: MMC) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.590 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MAY 15, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON APRIL 5, 2023. ABOUT MARSH MCLENNAN MARSH MCLENNAN (NYSE: MMC) IS THE WORLD'S LEADING PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM IN THE AREAS OF RISK, STRATEGY AND PEOPLE. THE COMPANY'S MORE THAN 85,000 COLLEAGUES ADVISE CLIENTS IN 130 COUNTRIES.
