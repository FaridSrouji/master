MARSH MCLENNAN AGENCY ACQUIRES HMS INSURANCE ASSOCIATES. WHITE PLAINS, N.Y.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MARSH MCLENNAN AGENCY (MMA), A SUBSIDIARY OF MARSH, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF HUNT VALLEY, MARYLAND-BASED HMS INSURANCE ASSOCIATES, INC., ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST INDEPENDENT AGENCIES. TERMS OF THE ACQUISITION WERE NOT DISCLOSED.
