MERCER REPORT: 3 OUT OF 4 US EMPLOYEES ARE SIGNIFICANTLY FINANCIALLY STRESSED. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AFTER TWO YEARS OF EXISTENTIAL CRISES – THE PANDEMIC, A WAR IN EUROPE, AND HISTORIC INFLATION – EMPLOYEES ARE MORE FOCUSED ON THEIR FINANCIAL SECURITY AND WELL-BEING THAN CLIMBING THE LADDER OR CAREER ADVANCEMENT, ACCORDING TO MERCER'S 2022 INSIDE EMPLOYEES' MINDS STUDY.
