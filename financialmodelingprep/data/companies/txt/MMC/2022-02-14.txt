MARSH MCLENNAN APPOINTS HAFIZE GAYE ERKAN TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ON FEBRUARY 11, 2022, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MARSH MCLENNAN (NYSE: MMC), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM IN THE AREAS OF RISK, STRATEGY, AND PEOPLE, APPOINTED HAFIZE GAYE ERKAN, AGE 42, AS DIRECTOR EFFECTIVE ON MARCH 1, 2022. WITH MS. ERKAN'S APPOINTMENT, THE COMPANY'S BOARD WILL CONSIST OF 14 DIRECTORS. PRIOR TO 2022, MS.
