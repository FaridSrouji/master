MERCER APPOINTS MUSTAFA FAIZANI AS US WEST MARKET CEO. FAIZANI JOINS MERCER'S US & CANADA LEADERSHIP TEAM. HE WILL FOCUS ON DRIVING PROFITABLE GROWTH BY EXPANDING MERCER'S CAPABILITIES IN THE WEST MARKET TO DELIVER HIGH-QUALITY CLIENT SERVICE, AS WELL AS INNOVATIVE INVESTMENTS IDEAS
