LEADING COMPANIES USE SKILLS AS THE NEW CURRENCY FOR WORKFORCE TRANSFORMATION. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--A SKILLS-BASED WORKFORCE STRATEGY WILL ENABLE ORGANIZATIONS TO IDENTIFY FUTURE SKILLS NEEDS AND DEVELOP A PLAN TO RETAIN AND DEPLOY TALENT, AS NEEDED.
