MARSH MCLENNAN ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $1 BILLION SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MARSH MCLENNAN (NYSE: MMC) (THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED $500,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.750% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2032 (THE “2032 NOTES”) AND $500,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 6.250% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2052 (THE “2052 NOTES” AND, TOGETHER WITH THE 2032 NOTES, THE “NOTES”).
