ABIOMED CONTINUES TO STRENGTHEN ITS INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, NOW HOLDS MORE THAN 1,000 PATENTS. DANVERS, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ABIOMED (NASDAQ: ABMD) ANNOUNCES IT HAS SURPASSED MORE THAN 1,000 PATENTS WORLDWIDE, WITH MORE THAN 850 ADDITIONAL PATENTS PENDING.
