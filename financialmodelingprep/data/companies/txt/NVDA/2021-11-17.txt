NVIDIA ANNOUNCES FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER FISCAL 2022. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., NOV. 17, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) TODAY REPORTED RECORD REVENUE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED OCTOBER 31, 2021, OF $7.10 BILLION, UP 50 PERCENT FROM A YEAR EARLIER AND UP 9 PERCENT FROM THE PREVIOUS QUARTER, WITH RECORD REVENUE FROM THE COMPANY'S GAMING, DATA CENTER AND PROFESSIONAL VISUALIZATION MARKET PLATFORMS.
