NVIDIA ANNOUNCES FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER FISCAL 2021. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., NOV. 18, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) TODAY REPORTED RECORD REVENUE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED OCTOBER 25, 2020, OF $4.73 BILLION, UP 57 PERCENT FROM $3.01 BILLION A YEAR EARLIER, AND UP 22 PERCENT FROM $3.87 BILLION IN THE PREVIOUS QUARTER.
