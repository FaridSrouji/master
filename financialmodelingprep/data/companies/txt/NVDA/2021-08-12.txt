NVIDIA FOUNDER AND CEO JENSEN HUANG TO RECEIVE SEMICONDUCTOR INDUSTRY'S TOP HONOR. WASHINGTON, AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SEMICONDUCTOR INDUSTRY ASSOCIATION (SIA) TODAY ANNOUNCED JENSEN HUANG, FOUNDER AND CEO OF NVIDIA (NASDAQ:NVDA) AND A TRAILBLAZER IN BUILDING ACCELERATED COMPUTING PLATFORMS, IS THE 2021 RECIPIENT OF THE INDUSTRY'S HIGHEST HONOR, THE  ROBERT N. NOYCE AWARD.
