NVIDIA STOCKHOLDER MEETING SET FOR JUNE 22; INDIVIDUALS CAN PARTICIPATE ONLINE. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., JUNE 08, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NVIDIA TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL HOLD ITS 2023 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS ONLINE ON THURSDAY, JUNE 22, AT 11 A.M. PT. THE MEETING WILL TAKE PLACE VIRTUALLY AT HTTP://WWW.VIRTUALSHAREHOLDERMEETING.COM/NVDA2023 .
