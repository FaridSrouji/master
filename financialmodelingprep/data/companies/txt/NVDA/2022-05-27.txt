NVIDIA RECOMMENDS STOCKHOLDERS REJECT ‘MINI-TENDER' OFFER BY TUTANOTA LLC. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MAY 27, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NVIDIA TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT RECENTLY BECAME AWARE OF AN UNSOLICITED “MINI-TENDER” OFFER BY TUTANOTA LLC TO PURCHASE UP TO 215,000 SHARES OF NVIDIA COMMON STOCK, REPRESENTING SIGNIFICANTLY LESS THAN 1% OF NVIDIA'S OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK.
