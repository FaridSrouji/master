NVIDIA ANNOUNCES FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR FIRST QUARTER FISCAL 2023. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MAY 25, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) TODAY REPORTED RECORD REVENUE FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED MAY 1, 2022, OF $8.29 BILLION, UP 46% FROM A YEAR AGO AND UP 8% FROM THE PREVIOUS QUARTER, WITH RECORD REVENUE IN DATA CENTER AND GAMING.
