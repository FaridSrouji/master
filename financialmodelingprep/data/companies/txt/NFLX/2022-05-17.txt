NEW YORK, MAY 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION COMPLAINT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF NETFLIX, INC. (NASDAQ: NFLX) ALLEGING THAT THE COMPANY VIOLATED FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS. THIS LAWSUIT IS ON BEHALF OF PERSONS AND ENTITIES THAT PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED NETFLIX COMMON STOCK OR CALL OPTIONS, OR SOLD PUT OPTIONS, BETWEEN OCTOBER 19, 2021 AND APRIL 19, 2022, INCLUSIVE.LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE: JULY 5, 2022 NO OBLIGATION OR COST TO YOU.
