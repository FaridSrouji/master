NFLX LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES NETFLIX, INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , JUNE 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN NETFLIX, INC. ("NETFLIX, INC." OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: NFLX) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF NETFLIX, INC. INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD.
