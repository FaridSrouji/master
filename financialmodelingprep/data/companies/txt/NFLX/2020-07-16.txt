NETFLIX INC - QTRLY UCAN STREAMING PAID NET ADDITIONS  2.94 MILLION.NETFLIX INC - QTRLY APAC STREAMING PAID NET ADDITIONS 2.66 MILLION VERSUS 0.80 MILLION.NETFLIX INC - ADDED Q2 10.1 MILLION PAID MEMBERSHIPS VERSUS 2.7 MILLION IN LAST YEAR'S Q2.NETFLIX INC -  EXPECT FREE CASH FLOW FOR FY 2020 TO BE BREAKEVEN TO POSITIVE, COMPARED WITH OUR PRIOR EXPECTATION FOR NEGATIVE $1 BILLION OR BETTER.NETFLIX INC - FOR FULL YEAR 2020, STILL TARGETING A 16% OPERATING MARGIN.NETFLIX INC - SLOWLY RESUMING PRODUCTIONS IN MANY PARTS OF THE WORLD.NETFLIX - FURTHEST ALONG IN ASIA PACIFIC IN RESUMING PRODUCTIONS, NOW SHOOTING SEASON 2 OF JAPANESE ORIGINAL THE NAKED DIRECTOR.NETFLIX - IN EMEA, NOW BACK IN PRODUCTION IN MANY COUNTRIES, INCLUDING GERMANY, FRANCE, SPAIN, POLAND, ITALY, AND THE UK.NETFLIX - RECENTLY RESUMED PRODUCTION ON TWO FILMS IN CALIFORNIA.NETFLIX - EXPECT SOME MORE OF U.S. PRODUCTIONS TO GET GOING THIS QUARTER, CURRENT INFECTION TRENDS CREATE MORE UNCERTAINTY FOR OUR PRODUCTIONS IN U.S..NETFLIX - PARTS OF WORLD LIKE INDIA & SOME OF LATIN AMERICA ALSO MORE CHALLENGING & WE ARE HOPING TO RESTART PRODUCTIONS LATER IN YEAR IN THESE REGIONS.NETFLIX INC - SAW PAUSE IN PRODUCTION FROM THE PANDEMIC COMBINED WITH HIGHER-THAN-FORECAST PAID NET ADDS YEAR TO DATE.
