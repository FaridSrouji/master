METTLER-TOLEDO INTERNATIONAL INC. REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. COLUMBUS, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METTLER-TOLEDO INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE: MTD) TODAY ANNOUNCED FOURTH QUARTER RESULTS FOR 2022. PROVIDED BELOW ARE THE HIGHLIGHTS: REPORTED SALES INCREASED 2% COMPARED WITH THE PRIOR YEAR. IN LOCAL CURRENCY, SALES INCREASED 9% IN THE QUARTER AS CURRENCY REDUCED SALES GROWTH BY 7%. NET EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE AS REPORTED (EPS) WERE $11.86, COMPARED WITH $9.94 IN THE PRIOR-YEAR PERIOD. ADJUSTED EPS WAS $12.
