BXP DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BOSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )-- BOSTON PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: BXP), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED DEVELOPER, OWNER, AND MANAGER OF PREMIER WORKPLACES IN THE UNITED STATES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.98 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK FOR THE PERIOD OCTOBER 1, 2022 TO DECEMBER 31, 2022, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 30, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 30, 2022.
