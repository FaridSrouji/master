BOSTON PROPERTIES SECURES A 140,000 SQUARE FOOT LEASE AT SANTA MONICA BUSINESS PARK IN SANTA MONICA, CA. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BOSTON PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: BXP), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED DEVELOPER, OWNER, AND MANAGER OF CLASS A OFFICE PROPERTIES IN THE UNITED STATES, ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A 140,000 SQUARE-FOOT EXPANSION LEASE WITH A TECHNOLOGY COMPANY AT 3100 OCEAN PARK BLVD. AT SANTA MONICA BUSINESS PARK, THE COMPANY'S 1.2 MILLION SQUARE-FOOT CREATIVE CAMPUS IN SANTA MONICA, CALIFORNIA. “WE ARE EXCITED TO CONTINUE TO PROVIDE GREAT SPACE AND PLACE FOR THIS HIGH GROWTH COMPANY,” SAID JONA
