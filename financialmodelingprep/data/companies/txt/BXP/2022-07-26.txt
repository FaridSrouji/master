BXP ANNOUNCES 2ND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS; REPORTS Q2 EPS OF $1.42 AND FFO PER SHARE OF $1.94. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BOSTON PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: BXP), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED DEVELOPER, OWNER, AND MANAGER OF CLASS A OFFICE PROPERTIES IN THE UNITED STATES, REPORTED RESULTS TODAY FOR THE SECOND QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2022. FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER INCLUDE: REVENUE GREW MORE THAN 8% TO $773.9 MILLION FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2022, AS COMPARED TO $713.8 MILLION FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021. NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $223.0 MIL
