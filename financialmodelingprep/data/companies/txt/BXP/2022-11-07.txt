BXP PRICES $750 MILLION OFFERING OF GREEN BONDS. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BOSTON PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: BXP) (“BXP”), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED DEVELOPER, OWNER AND MANAGER OF PREMIER WORKPLACES IN THE UNITED STATES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS OPERATING PARTNERSHIP, BOSTON PROPERTIES LIMITED PARTNERSHIP (“BPLP”), HAS AGREED TO SELL $750 MILLION OF 6.750% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2027 IN AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING THROUGH BOFA SECURITIES, INC., J.P.
