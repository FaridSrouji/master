BAKER HUGHES DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON AND LONDON, JAN. 25, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BAKER HUGHES (NASDAQ: BKR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BAKER HUGHES BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $.19 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON FEB. 17, 2023 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEB. 6, 2023.
