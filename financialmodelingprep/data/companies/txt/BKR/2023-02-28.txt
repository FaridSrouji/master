BAKER HUGHES AWARDED MAJOR SUBSEA CONTRACT WITH AZULE ENERGY FOR AGOGO OILFIELD OFFSHORE OF ANGOLA. HOUSTON, FEB. 28, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BAKER HUGHES (NASDAQ: BKR), AN ENERGY TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, HAS BEEN AWARDED A MAJOR CONTRACT TO PROVIDE SUBSEA EQUIPMENT AND SERVICES BY AZULE ENERGY FOR WORK IN THE AGOGO OILFIELD, OFFSHORE ANGOLA. THIS AWARD REPRESENTS THE FIRST MAJOR NEW GREENFIELD PROJECT AWARDED IN OFFSHORE ANGOLA IN MORE THAN FIVE YEARS.
