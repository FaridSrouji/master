AKASTOR ASA AND BAKER HUGHES COMPLETE MERGER BETWEEN MHWIRTH AND SDS. OSLO, NORWAY, OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REFERENCE IS MADE TO THE JOINT PRESS ANNOUNCEMENT DATED 2 MARCH 2021 FROM BAKER HUGHES (NYSE: BKR) AND AKASTOR ASA (OSLO: AKAST). THE PARTIES HAVE TODAY COMPLETED THE TRANSACTION THAT COMBINES THE OPERATIONS OF BAKER HUGHES' SUBSEA DRILLING SYSTEMS (SDS) BUSINESS WITH AKASTOR'S WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY, MHWIRTH AS (MHWIRTH) IN LINE WITH THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS DESCRIBED IN THE EARLIER ANNOUNCEMENT.

OSLO, NORWAY, OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REFERENCE IS MADE TO THE JOINT PRESS ANNOUNCEMENT DATED 2 MARCH 2021 FROM BAKER HUGHES (NYSE: BKR) AND AKASTOR ASA (OSLO: AKAST). BAKER HUGHES' SUBSEA DRILLING SYSTEMS AND AKASTOR'S MHWIRTH COMPLETE MERGER TO FORM HMH. HOUSTON & FORNEBU, NORWAY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE MERGER OF BAKER HUGHES' (NYSE: BKR) SUBSEA DRILLING SYSTEMS BUSINESS (SDS) AND AKASTOR ASA'S (OSLO: AKAST) WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY, MHWIRTH AS (MHWIRTH), HAS BEEN COMPLETED TO FORM A GLOBAL OFFSHORE DRILLING EQUIPMENT COMPANY. THE NEW COMPANY WILL BE KNOWN AS HMH (“THE COMPANY”), AND AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED, BAKER HUGHES AND AKASTOR OWN EQUAL EQUITY IN THE COMPANY.
