BAKER HUGHES ADDED TO THE NASDAQ-100 INDEX. HOUSTON AND LONDON, DEC. 12, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BAKER HUGHES (NASDAQ: BKR), AN ENERGY TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED MONDAY IT HAS BEEN ADDED TO THE NASDAQ-100® INDEX, THE TOP 100 LARGEST DOMESTIC AND INTERNATIONAL NON-FINANCIAL COMPANIES ON THE NASDAQ BASED ON MARKET CAPITALIZATION. NASDAQ ANNOUNCED THE INCLUSION ON FRIDAY, DEC. 9, WHICH WILL BECOME EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO MARKET OPEN ON MONDAY, DEC. 19.
