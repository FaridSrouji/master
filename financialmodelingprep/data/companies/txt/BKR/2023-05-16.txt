BAKER HUGHES DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON AND LONDON, MAY 16, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BAKER HUGHES (NASDAQ: BKR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BAKER HUGHES BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $.19 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON JUNE 9, 2023, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 30, 2023.
