BAKER HUGHES DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON AND LONDON, JULY 29, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BAKER HUGHES (NASDAQ: BKR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BAKER HUGHES BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $.18 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON AUGUST 19, 2022 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 9, 2022.
