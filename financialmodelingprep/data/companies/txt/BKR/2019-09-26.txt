BAKER HUGHES, A GE COMPANY WIN CONTRACT FOR BALDER X PROJECT IN NORWAY. BAKER HUGHES, A GE COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAD WON A SUBSEA CONTRACT FROM VAR ENERGI. THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY WILL ENGINEER, PROCURE, CONSTRUCT, AND INSTALL 16 NEW SUBSEA PRODUCTION SYSTEMS, UMBILICALS, RISERS AND FLOWLINES FOR THE BALDER X PROJECT ON THE NORWEGIAN CONTINENTAL SHELF.
