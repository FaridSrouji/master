BAKER HUGHES SIGNS AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE COMPACT CARBON CAPTURE TECHNOLOGY TO ADVANCE INDUSTRIAL DECARBONIZATION. HOUSTON & LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BAKER HUGHES (NYSE: BKR) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT IS ACQUIRING COMPACT CARBON CAPTURE (3C), A PIONEERING TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT COMPANY SPECIALIZING IN CARBON CAPTURE SOLUTIONS. THE ACQUISITION UNDERPINS BAKER HUGHES' STRATEGIC COMMITMENT TO LEAD IN THE ENERGY TRANSITION BY PROVIDING DECARBONIZATION SOLUTIONS FOR CARBON-INTENSIVE INDUSTRIES, INCLUDING OIL AND GAS AND BROADER INDUSTRIAL OPERATIONS.
