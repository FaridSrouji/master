BAKER HUGHES NAMES NANCY BUESE AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. HOUSTON AND LONDON, OCT. 19, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BAKER HUGHES (NASDAQ: BKR) HAS ANNOUNCED NANCY BUESE AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO) EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 2, 2022. BUESE BRINGS MORE THAN 30 YEARS OF FINANCIAL AND LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCE TO THE ROLE, PREVIOUSLY SERVING AS EVP & CFO OF GLOBAL MINING COMPANY NEWMONT CORPORATION FROM 2016 UNTIL EARLIER THIS YEAR.
