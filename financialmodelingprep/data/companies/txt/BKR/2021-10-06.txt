BAKER HUGHES' SUBSEA DRILLING SYSTEMS AND AKASTOR'S MHWIRTH COMPLETE MERGER TO FORM HMH. HOUSTON & FORNEBU, NORWAY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE MERGER OF BAKER HUGHES' (NYSE: BKR) SUBSEA DRILLING SYSTEMS BUSINESS (SDS) AND AKASTOR ASA'S (OSLO: AKAST) WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY, MHWIRTH AS (MHWIRTH), HAS BEEN COMPLETED TO FORM A GLOBAL OFFSHORE DRILLING EQUIPMENT COMPANY. THE NEW COMPANY WILL BE KNOWN AS HMH (“THE COMPANY”), AND AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED, BAKER HUGHES AND AKASTOR OWN EQUAL EQUITY IN THE COMPANY.
