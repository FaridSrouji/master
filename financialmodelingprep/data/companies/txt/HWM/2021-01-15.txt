HOWMET AEROSPACE COMPLETES EARLY REDEMPTION OF 5.40% NOTES DUE 2021. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HOWMET AEROSPACE INC. (NYSE:HWM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY INDICATED EARLY REDEMPTION OF ALL OF ITS OUTSTANDING 5.40% NOTES DUE 2021 (THE “NOTES”) IN THE AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $360,808,000. THE NOTES WERE REDEEMED AT 100% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF THE NOTES, PLUS ACCRUED AND UNPAID INTEREST UP TO, BUT NOT INCLUDING, THE REDEMPTION DATE.
