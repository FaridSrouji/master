HOWMET AEROSPACE BOARD APPROVES COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. PITTSBURGH--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF HOWMET AEROSPACE (NYSE:HWM) DECLARED (A) A DIVIDEND OF 4 CENTS PER SHARE ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY, TO BE PAID ON FEBRUARY 27, 2023, TO THE HOLDERS OF RECORD OF THE COMMON STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 10, 2023; AND (B) A DIVIDEND OF 93.75 CENTS PER SHARE ON THE OUTSTANDING $3.75 CUMULATIVE PREFERRED STOCK (“CLASS A STOCK”) OF THE COMPANY, TO BE PAID ON APRIL 1, 2023 TO THE HOLDERS OF RECORD OF THE CLASS A STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 10, 2023.
