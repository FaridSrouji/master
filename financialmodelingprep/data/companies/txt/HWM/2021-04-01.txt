HOWMET AEROSPACE ANNOUNCES PLANNED EARLY REDEMPTION OF 5.87% NOTES DUE 2022. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HOWMET AEROSPACE INC. (NYSE:HWM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL REDEEM ALL $476,230,000 OUTSTANDING AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.87% NOTES DUE 2022 (THE “2022 NOTES”) ON MAY 1, 2021 (THE “REDEMPTION DATE”). THE COMPANY EXPECTS THE AGGREGATE REDEMPTION PRICE TO BE APPROXIMATELY $500 MILLION, WHICH THE COMPANY INTENDS TO PAY WITH CASH ON HAND. BECAUSE THE REDEMPTION DATE IS NOT A BUSINESS DAY, PAYMENT OF THE REDEMPTION PRICE WILL BE MADE ON MAY 3, 2021. FOLLOWING
