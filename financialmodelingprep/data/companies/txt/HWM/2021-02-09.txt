HOWMET AEROSPACE BOARD APPROVES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF HOWMET AEROSPACE (NYSE:HWM) TODAY DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 93.75 CENTS PER SHARE ON THE OUTSTANDING $3.75 CUMULATIVE PREFERRED STOCK (“CLASS A STOCK”) OF THE COMPANY, TO BE PAID ON APRIL 1, 2021 TO THE HOLDERS OF RECORD OF THE CLASS A STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 12, 2021.
