SCHNEIDER FULLY TRANSITIONED TO UNION PACIFIC FOR WESTERN RAIL SERVICE. GREEN BAY, WIS.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--SCHNEIDER (NYSE: SNDR), A PREMIER MULTIMODAL PROVIDER OF TRANSPORTATION, INTERMODAL AND LOGISTICS SERVICES, COMPLETED THE TRANSITION OF ITS WESTERN RAIL OPERATIONS TO UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD (NYSE: UNP), MAKING IT THE LARGEST COMPANY DRIVER DRAY FLEET ON THE RAIL NETWORK.
