FORTUNE NAMES UNION PACIFIC ONE OF THE WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES. OMAHA, NEB. , FEB. 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD HAS AGAIN BEEN NAMED TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S LIST OF THE WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES AND IS THE HIGHEST RANKED RAILROAD IN THE TRUCKING, TRANSPORTATION AND LOGISTICS CATEGORY.
