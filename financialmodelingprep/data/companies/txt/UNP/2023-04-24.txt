UNION PACIFIC RECOGNIZES COMPANIES FOR SAFE CHEMICAL TRANSPORTATION. NINETY-FIVE COMPANIES HONORED WITH 2022 PINNACLE AWARD OMAHA, NEB. , APRIL 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT 95 COMPANIES WON THE 2022 PINNACLE AWARD FOR THEIR DEDICATION AND COMMITMENT TO SAFELY TRANSPORTING CHEMICALS BY RAIL.
