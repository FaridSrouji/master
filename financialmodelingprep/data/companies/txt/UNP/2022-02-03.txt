UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION DECLARES FIRST QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND AND ANNOUNCES NEW SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION. OMAHA, NEB., FEB. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: UNP) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.18 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE MARCH 31, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD FEBRUARY 28, 2022.
UNION PACIFIC BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPOINTS BETH WHITED TO NEW SUSTAINABILITY AND STRATEGY POSITION. OMAHA, NEB., FEB. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNION PACIFIC TODAY ANNOUNCED BETH WHITED WAS APPOINTED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT – SUSTAINABILITY AND STRATEGY BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.
