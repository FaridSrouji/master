BLET AND UNION PACIFIC REACH HISTORIC AGREEMENT TO IMPROVE WORK SCHEDULES AND TIME OFF FOR LOCOMOTIVE ENGINEERS. OMAHA, NEB. , MAY 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BROTHERHOOD OF LOCOMOTIVE ENGINEERS AND TRAINMEN (BLET) AND UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD TODAY ANNOUNCED A HISTORIC TENTATIVE AGREEMENT THAT ENHANCES THE QUALITY OF LIFE FOR UNION PACIFIC'S LOCOMOTIVE ENGINEERS AND THEIR FAMILIES BY MAKING IT POSSIBLE FOR THEM TO HAVE MORE PREDICTABLE SCHEDULES.
