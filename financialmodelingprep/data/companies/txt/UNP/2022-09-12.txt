UNION PACIFIC ISSUES $600 MILLION IN GREEN BONDS TO FUND INVESTMENTS AIMED AT REDUCING CARBON FOOTPRINT. OMAHA, NEB. , SEPT. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: UNP) ISSUED A $600 MILLION GREEN BOND OFFERING TO FUND INVESTMENTS AIMED AT DECARBONIZING THE COMPANY'S FOOTPRINT AND ACHIEVING SET EMISSIONS REDUCTION TARGETS.
