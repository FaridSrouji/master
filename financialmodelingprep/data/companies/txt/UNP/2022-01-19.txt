SCHNEIDER INTERMODAL MOVING WESTERN RAIL SERVICE TO UNION PACIFIC. GREEN BAY, WISC., JAN. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SCHNEIDER (NYSE: SNDR), A PREMIER PROVIDER OF TRANSPORTATION, INTERMODAL AND LOGISTICS SERVICES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE COMPANY IS EXPANDING INTERMODAL SERVICE BY MOVING ITS PRIMARY WESTERN UNITED STATES RAIL PARTNERSHIP TO UNION PACIFIC (NYSE: UNP) BEGINNING IN 2023.
