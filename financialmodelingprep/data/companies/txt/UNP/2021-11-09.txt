UNION PACIFIC AWARDS $5.5 MILLION IN GRANTS SUPPORTING LOCAL COMMUNITIES AND UNDERSERVED POPULATIONS. OMAHA, NEB., NOV. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD'S COMMUNITY TIES GIVING PROGRAM AWARDED $5.5 MILLION IN LOCAL GRANTS TO 576 NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS, WITH 81% GOING TO LOCAL ORGANIZATIONS THAT ALIGN WITH UNION PACIFIC'S DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION (DE&I) VALUES.
