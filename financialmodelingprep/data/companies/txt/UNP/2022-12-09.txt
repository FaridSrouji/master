UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION DECLARES FOURTH QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. OMAHA, NEB. , DEC. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: UNP) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.30 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE DEC. 29, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD DEC. 19, 2022.
