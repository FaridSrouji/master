UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION EXECUTIVES TO ADDRESS THE DEUTSCHE BANK 2021 TRANSPORTATION CONFERENCE. OMAHA, NEB., AUG. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JENNIFER HAMANN, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, AND ERIC GEHRINGER, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OPERATIONS, OF UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: UNP), WILL ADDRESS THE DEUTSCHE BANK 2021 TRANSPORTATION CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, AUGUST 17, 2021, AT 9:00 A.M.
