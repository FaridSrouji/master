UNION PACIFIC TAKES ADDITIONAL ACTION TO ALLEVIATE SUPPLY CHAIN CONGESTION. OMAHA, NEB., OCT. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD TODAY ANNOUNCED ADDITIONAL PROACTIVE STEPS TO REDUCE GLOBAL SUPPLY CHAIN CONGESTION, HELPING RESTORE FLUIDITY FOR GOODS AND PRODUCTS MOVING ACROSS THE UNITED STATES FROM WEST COAST PORTS.
