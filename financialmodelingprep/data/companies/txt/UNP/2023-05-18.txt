UNION PACIFIC ELEVATES CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER RAHUL JALALI TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT. OMAHA, NEB. , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNDERSTANDING THE PIVOTAL ROLE TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION PLAY IN THE RAILROAD INDUSTRY AND ITS FUTURE GROWTH, UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD TODAY ANNOUNCED CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER RAHUL JALALI HAS BEEN NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, EFFECTIVE JUNE 1.
UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION DECLARES SECOND QUARTER 2023 DIVIDEND. OMAHA, NEB. , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: UNP) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.30 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 30, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD MAY 31, 2023.
