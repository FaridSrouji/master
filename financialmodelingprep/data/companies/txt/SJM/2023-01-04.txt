THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. NAMES REBECCA SCHEIDLER SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER OF CONSUMER FOODS. ORRVILLE, OHIO , JAN. 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. (NYSE: SJM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF REBECCA SCHEIDLER AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT & GENERAL MANAGER, CONSUMER FOODS, EFFECTIVE JAN. 16, 2023.
