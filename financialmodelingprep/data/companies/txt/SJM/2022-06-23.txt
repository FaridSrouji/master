THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. ANNOUNCES UPDATES TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS LEADERSHIP. ORRVILLE, OHIO , JUNE 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. (NYSE: SJM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MARK SMUCKER, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, WILL ASSUME THE EXPANDED ROLE OF CHAIR OF THE BOARD EFFECTIVE AUGUST 17, 2022.
