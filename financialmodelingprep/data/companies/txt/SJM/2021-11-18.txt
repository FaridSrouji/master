THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. TO ACCELERATE SMUCKER'S® UNCRUSTABLES® GROWTH THROUGH INVESTMENT IN NEW MANUFACTURING FACILITY IN MCCALLA, ALABAMA. ORRVILLE, OHIO, NOV. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. (NYSE: SJM) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL INVEST $1.1 BILLION TO BUILD A NEW MANUFACTURING FACILITY AND DISTRIBUTION CENTER IN MCCALLA, ALABAMA, DEDICATED TO PRODUCTION OF SMUCKER'S® UNCRUSTABLES® SANDWICHES.
