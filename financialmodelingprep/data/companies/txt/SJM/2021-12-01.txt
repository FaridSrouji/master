THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. DIVESTS PRIVATE LABEL DRY PET FOOD BUSINESS AS PART OF CONTINUED PORTFOLIO OPTIMIZATION. ORRVILLE, OHIO, DEC. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. (NYSE: SJM) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS SOLD ITS PRIVATE LABEL DRY PET FOOD BUSINESS, INCLUDING ITS MANUFACTURING FACILITY IN FRONTENAC, KANSAS, TO DIAMOND PET FOODS, INC., IN A CASH TRANSACTION VALUED AT APPROXIMATELY $33 MILLION.
