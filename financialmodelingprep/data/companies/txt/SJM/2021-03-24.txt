THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. AND JDE PEET'S ANNOUNCE STRATEGIC LIQUID COFFEE PARTNERSHIP. ORRVILLE, OHIO AND AMSTERDAM, MARCH 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. (NYSE: SJM) AND JDE PEET'S ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TODAY WHICH WILL ALLOW JDE PEET'S TO SUPPORT THE SMUCKER AWAY FROM HOME LIQUID COFFEE BUSINESS WITH PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT, PRODUCTION AND FOODSERVICE EQUIPMENT INNOVATION.
