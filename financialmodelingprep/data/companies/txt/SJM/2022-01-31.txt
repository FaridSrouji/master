THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. COMPLETES THE DIVESTITURE OF ITS NATURAL BEVERAGE AND GRAINS BUSINESSES TO NEXUS CAPITAL. ORRVILLE, OHIO, JAN. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. (NYSE: SJM) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE CLOSING OF THE TRANSACTION TO DIVEST ITS NATURAL AND ORGANIC BEVERAGE AND GRAINS BUSINESSES TO NEXUS CAPITAL MANAGEMENT LP IN A CASH TRANSACTION VALUED AT $110 MILLION, SUBJECT TO A WORKING CAPITAL ADJUSTMENT.
