PALO ALTO NETWORKS AND SIEMENS PARTNER TO PROTECT CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., NOV. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PALO ALTO NETWORKS (NASDAQ: PANW), THE GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXTENSION OF ITS TECHNOLOGY PARTNERSHIP WITH SIEMENS TO IMPROVE THE SECURITY OF MISSION-CRITICAL NETWORKS AND PREVENT THE THREAT OF CYBERATTACKS ON CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE.
