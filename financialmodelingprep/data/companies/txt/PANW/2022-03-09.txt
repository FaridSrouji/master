PALO ALTO NETWORKS HELPS ORGANIZATIONS COMBAT SOFTWARE SUPPLY CHAIN THREATS WITH NEW PRISMA CLOUD SUPPLY CHAIN SECURITY. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH SOFTWARE SUPPLY CHAIN ATTACKS RISING RAPIDLY, PALO ALTO NETWORKS (NASDAQ: PANW) TODAY ANNOUNCED PRISMA® CLOUD SUPPLY CHAIN SECURITY TO PROVIDE A COMPLETE VIEW OF WHERE POTENTIAL VULNERABILITIES OR MISCONFIGURATIONS EXIST IN THE SOFTWARE SUPPLY CHAIN — ALLOWING ORGANIZATIONS TO QUICKLY TRACE TO THE SOURCE AND FIX THEM.
