PALO ALTO NETWORKS ANNOUNCES CFO TRANSITION; DIPAK GOLECHHA APPOINTED CFO AS LUIS VISOSO DEPARTS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PALO ALTO NETWORKS (NYSE: PANW), THE GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DIPAK GOLECHHA TO THE ROLE OF CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.
