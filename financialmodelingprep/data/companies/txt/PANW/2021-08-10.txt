PALO ALTO NETWORKS ANNOUNCES EXPANSION OF MANAGEMENT TEAM. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., AUG. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PALO ALTO NETWORKS (NYSE: PANW), THE GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF ITS MANAGEMENT TEAM, WITH AMIT SINGH MOVING INTO THE NEW ROLE OF CHIEF BUSINESS OFFICER AND BJ JENKINS JOINING THE COMPANY IN THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT.
