PALO ALTO NETWORKS POSITIONED AS A LEADER IN 2021 GARTNER MAGIC QUADRANT FOR WAN EDGE INFRASTRUCTURE. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PALO ALTO NETWORKS (NYSE: PANW ), THE GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PALO ALTO NETWORKS PRISMA® SD-WAN HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED AS A LEADER IN THE 2021 GARTNER® MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR WAN EDGE INFRASTRUCTURE.
