PALO ALTO NETWORKS PARTNERS WITH INGRAM MICRO TO BRING OKYO GARDE TO SMALL BUSINESSES. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., OCT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PALO ALTO NETWORKS (NYSE: PANW), THE GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED AN AGREEMENT WITH INGRAM MICRO INC., A GLOBAL LEADER IN TECHNOLOGY AND SUPPLY CHAIN SERVICES, FOR INGRAM MICRO TO DISTRIBUTE THE PALO ALTO NETWORKS OKYO™ GARDE CYBERSECURITY SOLUTION TO ITS VALUE-ADDED RESELLERS AND TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS PROVIDERS, WHO WILL BRING OKYO GARDE TO SMALL BUSINESSES ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
