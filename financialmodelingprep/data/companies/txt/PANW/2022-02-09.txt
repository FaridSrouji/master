PALO ALTO NETWORKS CONTINUES ROLLOUT OF FOURTH-GENERATION HARDWARE WITH THE PA-3400 AND PA-5400 SERIES ML-POWERED NEXT-GENERATION FIREWALLS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., FEB. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PALO ALTO NETWORKS (NASDAQ: PANW), RECOGNIZED AS A 10- TIME LEADER IN NETWORK FIREWALLS , TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PA-3400 AND PA-5400 SERIES ML-POWERED NEXT-GENERATION FIREWALLS (NGFWS) — OFFERING THREE TIMES BETTER SECURITY PERFORMANCE OVER THE PREVIOUS GENERATIONS.
