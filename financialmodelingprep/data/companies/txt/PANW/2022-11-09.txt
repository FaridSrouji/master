PALO ALTO NETWORKS NAMED A LEADER IN INAUGURAL FROST & SULLIVAN CLOUD-NATIVE APPLICATION PROTECTION PLATFORMS REPORT. PRISMA CLOUD RECOGNIZED FOR REMARKABLE GROWTH, CONTINUOUS INNOVATION AND COMPLETE CODE-TO-CLOUD SECURITY SANTA CLARA, CALIF. , NOV. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PALO ALTO NETWORKS (NASDAQ: PANW), THE GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS FLAGSHIP CLOUD SECURITY PLATFORM, PRISMA® CLOUD, HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED AS A LEADER IN THE INAUGURAL FROST RADAR™: CLOUD-NATIVE APPLICATION PROTECTION PLATFORMS (CNAPP), 2022.
