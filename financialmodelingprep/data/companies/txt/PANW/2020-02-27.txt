PALO ALTO SAYS ON FEB 26, ENTERED INTO ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH MSCO TO REPURCHASE ABOUT $1.0 BILLION OF CO'S COMMON STOCK. PALO ALTO -  ON FEB 26, ENTERED INTO ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH MSCO TO REPURCHASE ABOUT $1.0 BILLION OF CO'S COMMON STOCK.PALO ALTO-  UNDER ASR, CO WILL MAKE PAYMENTS OF ABOUT $1 BILLION TO MORGAN STANLEY & RECEIVE INITIAL DELIVERY OF ABOUT 4.2 MILLION SHARES.PALO ALTO NETWORKS INC -  FINAL SETTLEMENT OF TRANSACTIONS UNDER ASR CONTRACT IS EXPECTED TO OCCUR DURING FOURTH FISCAL QUARTER OF 2020.
