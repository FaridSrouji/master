PALO ALTO NETWORKS APPOINTS HELMUT REISINGER TO LEADERSHIP TEAM. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., JAN. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PALO ALTO NETWORKS (NASDAQ: PANW), THE GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF HELMUT REISINGER TO THE POSITION OF CEO FOR EUROPE, THE MIDDLE EAST AND AFRICA (EMEA) AND LATIN AMERICA (LATAM).
