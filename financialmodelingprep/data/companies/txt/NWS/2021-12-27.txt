NEWS CORP TO ACQUIRE BASE CHEMICALS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWS CORP ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE THE BASE CHEMICALS BUSINESS FROM S&P GLOBAL AND IHS MARKIT. BASE CHEMICALS WILL BECOME PART OF DOW JONES' PROFESSIONAL INFORMATION BUSINESS, ALONGSIDE OPIS, THE ENERGY AND RENEWABLES DATA AND INFORMATION BUSINESS.
