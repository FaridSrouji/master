NEWS CORP COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF BASE CHEMICALS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWS CORP ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF THE BASE CHEMICALS BUSINESS FROM S&P GLOBAL MARKET INTELLIGENCE. THE BUSINESS WILL OPERATE UNDER THE NAME CHEMICAL MARKET ANALYTICS BY OPIS, A DOW JONES COMPANY. NEWS CORP ACQUIRED OPIS (THE OIL PRICE INFORMATION SERVICE) AND RELATED ASSETS FROM S&P GLOBAL AND IHS MARKIT IN FEBRUARY.
