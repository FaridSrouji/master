STATEMENT OF ROBERT THOMSON, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OF NEWS CORP, REGARDING THE SALE OF SIMON & SCHUSTER TO BERTELSMANN. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWS CORP CHIEF EXECUTIVE ROBERT THOMSON HAS COMMENTED ON VIACOMCBS'S ANNOUNCEMENT REGARDING THE SALE OF SIMON & SCHUSTER TO BERTELSMANN: “THERE IS CLEARLY NO MARKET LOGIC TO A BID OF THAT SIZE - ONLY ANTI-MARKET LOGIC.
