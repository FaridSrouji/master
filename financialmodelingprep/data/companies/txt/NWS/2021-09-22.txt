NEWS CORP ANNOUNCES $1 BILLION STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM; STOCKHOLDER RIGHTS AGREEMENT TERMINATED. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWS CORP (THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE “BOARD”) HAS AUTHORIZED A $1 BILLION STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM. SUBJECT TO MARKET CONDITIONS AND THE MARKET PRICE OF THE COMPANY'S STOCK, AS WELL AS OTHER FACTORS, THE COMPANY INTENDS TO REPURCHASE, FROM TIME TO TIME, IN THE OPEN MARKET OR OTHERWISE, A COMBINATION OF THE COMPANY'S CLASS A COMMON STOCK AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK.
