NEWS CORP ANNOUNCES PRICING OF PRIVATE OFFERING OF $500 MILLION SENIOR NOTES DUE 2032. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWS CORPORATION (“NEWS CORP”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF ITS PRIVATE OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR NOTES DUE 2032 (THE “NOTES”). THE NOTES WILL BEAR INTEREST AT A RATE OF 5.125% PER ANNUM, PAYABLE SEMI-ANNUALLY ON FEBRUARY 15 AND AUGUST 15 OF EACH YEAR, COMMENCING AUGUST 15, 2022. THE NOTES WILL MATURE ON FEBRUARY 15, 2032.
