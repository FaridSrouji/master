FOXTEL GROUP TO HOST STRATEGY DAY. THE FOXTEL GROUP STRATEGY DAY WILL BE INTRODUCED BY NEWS CORP CHIEF EXECUTIVE ROBERT THOMSON AND TELSTRA CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER ANDY PENN.
