NUCOR INCREASES ITS REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY 23% AND ANNOUNCES NEW SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM. CHARLOTTE, N.C., DEC. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE INCREASE OF ITS REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON NUCOR'S COMMON STOCK TO $0.50 PER SHARE.
