NUCOR ANNOUNCES EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT MARYEMILY SLATE TO RETIRE, JOHN HOLLATZ TO BE PROMOTED AND OTHER EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT CHANGES. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , MAY 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MARYEMILY SLATE, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF COMMERCIAL, PLANS TO RETIRE DUE TO HEALTH ISSUES ON JUNE 11, 2022 AFTER MORE THAN 21 YEARS OF SERVICE WITH NUCOR.
