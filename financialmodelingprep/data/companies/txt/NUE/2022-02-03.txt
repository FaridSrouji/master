NUCOR COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF CALIFORNIA STEEL INDUSTRIES. CHARLOTTE, N.C., FEB. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF A MAJORITY OWNERSHIP POSITION IN CALIFORNIA STEEL INDUSTRIES, INC. (CSI) BY PURCHASING A 50% EQUITY INTEREST FROM A SUBSIDIARY OF VALE S.A.
