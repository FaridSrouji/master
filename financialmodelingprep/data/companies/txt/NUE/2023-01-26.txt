NUCOR REPORTS RECORD ANNUAL EARNINGS FOR 2022. NUCOR REPORTS SAFEST AND MOST PROFITABLE YEAR IN COMPANY HISTORY, ECLIPSING PRIOR RECORDS SET IN 2021 FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2022 EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE OF $4.89 AND $28.79, RESPECTIVELY RETURNED $3.3 BILLION OF CAPITAL TO STOCKHOLDERS THROUGH DIVIDENDS AND SHARE REPURCHASES ANNOUNCED INCREASED DIVIDEND FOR 50TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR SINCE INITIAL NYSE LISTING IN 1972 CHARLOTTE, N.C. , JAN. 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) TODAY ANNOUNCED CONSOLIDATED NET EARNINGS OF $1.26 BILLION, OR $4.89 PER DILUTED SHARE, FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022.
