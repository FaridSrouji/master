NUCOR ANNOUNCES 193RD CONSECUTIVE CASH DIVIDEND. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JUNE 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) DECLARED THE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.405 PER SHARE ON NUCOR'S COMMON STOCK.
