NUCOR TO BUILD NEW TUBE MILL IN KENTUCKY NEAR ITS GALLATIN SHEET MILL. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL BUILD ITS NEW TUBE MILL ON THE SITE OF ITS NUCOR STEEL GALLATIN SHEET MILL IN KENTUCKY.
