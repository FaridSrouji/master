NUCOR TO ACQUIRE STEEL RACKING MANUFACTURER HANNIBAL INDUSTRIES, INC.. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO PURCHASE HANNIBAL INDUSTRIES, INC. FOR $370 MILLION, WHICH REPRESENTS 6.9X EBITDA FOR THE TRAILING-TWELVE MONTHS ENDED MARCH 2021.
