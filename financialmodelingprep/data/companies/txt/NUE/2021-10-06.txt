NUCOR FASTENER TO EXPAND WITH ACQUISITION OF COIL PROCESSING FACILITY. CHARLOTTE, N.C., OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT IS EXPANDING ITS NUCOR FASTENER DIVISION WITH THE ACQUISITION OF AN EXISTING STATE-OF-THE-ART COIL PROCESSING FACILITY IN SHELBYVILLE, INDIANA.
