NUCOR LEADS EFFORT TO DEVELOP CARBON EMISSIONS STANDARD FOR THE STEEL INDUSTRY. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , APRIL 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A COALITION OF STEEL COMPANIES AND INDUSTRY PARTNERS FOUNDED BY NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) AND FIVE OTHER ORGANIZATIONS RELEASED FOR COMMENT TODAY A DRAFT GLOBAL STANDARD TO MEASURE AND REPORT CARBON EMISSIONS FROM STEEL COMPANIES.
