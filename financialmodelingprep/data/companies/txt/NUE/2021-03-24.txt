NUCOR SIGNS SECOND VIRTUAL POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR RENEWABLE ENERGY IN TEXAS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MARCH 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A 10-YEAR VIRTUAL POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT (VPPA) WITH ØRSTED ONSHORE NORTH AMERICA, LLC FOR 100 MEGAWATTS FROM ØRSTED'S WESTERN TRAIL WIND FARM (WTW) IN NORTH TEXAS.
