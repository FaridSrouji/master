NUCOR COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF INSULATED METAL PANELS BUSINESS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF CORNERSTONE BUILDING BRANDS' INSULATED METAL PANELS (IMP) BUSINESS FOR A CASH PURCHASE PRICE OF APPROXIMATELY $1 BILLION, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY ADJUSTMENTS.
