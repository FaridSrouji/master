NUCOR CEO, LEON TOPALIAN, ELECTED CHAIRMAN OF AISI. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER LEON TOPALIAN WAS ELECTED TODAY TO SERVE A TWO-YEAR TERM AS CHAIRMAN OF THE AMERICAN IRON AND STEEL INSTITUTE (AISI) BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
