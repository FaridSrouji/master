NUCOR TO BUILD NEW MELT SHOP AT AN EXISTING BAR MILL IN THE WESTERN UNITED STATES. CHARLOTTE, N.C., SEPT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED THE CONSTRUCTION OF A NEW MELT SHOP AT ONE OF THE COMPANY'S EXISTING BAR MILLS IN THE WESTERN UNITED STATES.
