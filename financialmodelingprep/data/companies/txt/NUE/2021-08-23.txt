NUCOR COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF HANNIBAL INDUSTRIES, INC.. CHARLOTTE, N.C., AUG. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF HANNIBAL INDUSTRIES, INC., A LEADING NATIONAL PROVIDER OF STEEL RACKING SOLUTIONS, FOR A CASH PURCHASE PRICE OF $370 MILLION.
