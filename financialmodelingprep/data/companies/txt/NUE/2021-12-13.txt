NUCOR ACQUIRES MAJORITY OWNERSHIP OF CALIFORNIA STEEL INDUSTRIES. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , DEC. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS REACHED AGREEMENTS TO ACQUIRE A MAJORITY OWNERSHIP POSITION IN CALIFORNIA STEEL INDUSTRIES, INC. (CSI) BY PURCHASING A 50% EQUITY INTEREST FROM A SUBSIDIARY OF VALE S.A.
