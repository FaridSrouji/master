NUCOR ENTERS INTO CARBON CAPTURE & STORAGE AGREEMENT WITH EXXONMOBIL. EXXONMOBIL WILL CAPTURE, TRANSPORT AND STORE CARBON FROM LOUISIANA DRI PLANT CHARLOTTE, N.C. , JUNE 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED AN AGREEMENT WITH EXXONMOBIL TO CAPTURE, TRANSPORT, AND STORE CARBON FROM THE COMPANY'S DIRECT REDUCED IRON (DRI) PLANT IN CONVENT, LOUISIANA.
