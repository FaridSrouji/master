NUCOR CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER JIM FRIAS TO RETIRE; STEVE LAXTON TO BE PROMOTED. CHARLOTTE, N.C., FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JIM FRIAS, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, TREASURER AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PLANS TO RETIRE EFFECTIVE JUNE 11, 2022, AND WILL TRANSITION OUT OF THE ROLE AS OF MARCH 6, 2022.
