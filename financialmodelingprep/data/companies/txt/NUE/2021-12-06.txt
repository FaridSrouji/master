NUCOR TO BUILD REBAR MICRO MILL IN THE SOUTH ATLANTIC REGION. CHARLOTTE, N.C., DEC. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED THE CONSTRUCTION OF A REBAR MICRO MILL, WITH SPOOLING CAPABILITIES, TO BE LOCATED IN THE SOUTH ATLANTIC REGION.
