NUCOR TO FULLY REDEEM ITS OUTSTANDING 4.000% NOTES DUE 2023. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , MARCH 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ISSUED A NOTICE TO REDEEM ALL $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OUTSTANDING OF ITS 4.000% NOTES DUE AUGUST 1, 2023 (THE "NOTES").
