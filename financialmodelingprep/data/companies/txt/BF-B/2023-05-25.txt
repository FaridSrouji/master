BROWN-FORMAN DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND. LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BROWN-FORMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: BFA) (NYSE: BFB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.2055 PER SHARE ON ITS CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JULY 3, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 8, 2023. BROWN-FORMAN, A MEMBER OF THE PRESTIGIOUS S&P 500 DIVIDEND ARISTOCRATS INDEX, HAS PAID REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDENDS FOR 79 YEARS AND HAS INCREASED THE CASH DIVIDEND FOR 39 CONSECUTIVE YEARS.
