BROWN-FORMAN INCREASES CASH DIVIDEND FOR 39TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR; ELECTS MARK CLOUSE AND ELIZABETH SMITH TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. LOUISVILLE, KY.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--BROWN-FORMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: BFA, BFB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED AN INCREASE OF 9% TO THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND FROM $0.1885 PER SHARE TO $0.2055 PER SHARE ON ITS CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK. AS A RESULT, THE INDICATED ANNUAL CASH DIVIDEND WILL RISE FROM $0.7540 PER SHARE TO $0.8220 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 3, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON DECEMBER 2, 2022. BROWN-FORMAN, A MEMBER OF THE PRESTIGIOUS S&P 500 DIVIDEND ARISTOCRATS INDEX, HAS PAID REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDENDS FOR 79 YEARS AND HAS INCREASED THE CASH DIVIDEND FOR 39 CONSECUTIVE YEARS.
