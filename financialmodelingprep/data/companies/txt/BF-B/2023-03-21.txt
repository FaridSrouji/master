BROWN-FORMAN ANNOUNCES $650 MILLION DEBT OFFERING. LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BROWN-FORMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: BFA, BFB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF A $650 MILLION 10-YEAR SENIOR UNSECURED NOTE. THE 4.750% 10-YEAR SENIOR NOTES ARE DUE APRIL 15, 2033. BROWN-FORMAN INTENDS TO USE THE NET PROCEEDS FROM THE OFFERING TO REFINANCE ITS EXISTING $600 MILLION OF OUTSTANDING DEBT UNDER ITS SENIOR UNSECURED 364-DAY TERM LOAN CREDIT AGREEMENT, WITH THE REMAINDER TO BE USED FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES. BOFA SECURITIES, INC.
