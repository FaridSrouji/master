BROWN‑FORMAN TO RING NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE CLOSING BELL ON DECEMBER 14. LOUISVILLE, KY.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--BROWN‑FORMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: BFA, BFB) WILL RING THE CLOSING BELL AT THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE (NYSE) IN CELEBRATION OF MORE THAN 150 YEARS OF ENRICHING THE EXPERIENCE OF LIFE. THE COMPANY HAS BEEN A PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANY SINCE 1933, MAKING ITS FIRST PUBLIC STOCK ISSUE AFTER THE END OF PROHIBITION.
