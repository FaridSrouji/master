CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CINCINNATI, NOV. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CINF) ANNOUNCED THAT AT TODAY'S REGULAR MEETING, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A 63-CENTS-PER-SHARE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JANUARY 14, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 16, 2021.
