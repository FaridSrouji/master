CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CINCINNATI , MAY 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CINF) ANNOUNCED THAT AT ITS REGULAR MEETING ON MAY 7, 2022, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A 69-CENTS-PER-SHARE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 15, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 17, 2022.
