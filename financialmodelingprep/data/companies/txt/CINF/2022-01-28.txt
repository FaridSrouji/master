CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION INCREASES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CINCINNATI, JAN. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CINF) ANNOUNCED THAT AT TODAY'S REGULAR MEETING, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A 69-CENTS-PER-SHARE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, INCREASING BY 10% FROM THE PREVIOUS 63-CENTS-PER-SHARE DIVIDEND PAID ON JANUARY 14, 2022. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE APRIL 15, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MARCH 18, 2022.
