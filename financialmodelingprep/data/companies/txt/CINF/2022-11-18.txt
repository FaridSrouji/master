CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CINCINNATI , NOV. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CINF) ANNOUNCED THAT AT TODAY'S REGULAR MEETING, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A 69-CENTS-PER-SHARE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JANUARY 13, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 16, 2022.
