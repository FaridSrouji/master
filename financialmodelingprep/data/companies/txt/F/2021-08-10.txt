FORD CREDIT CFO BRIAN SCHAAF TO SPEAK AUGUST 12 AT J.P. MORGAN AUTOMOTIVE CONFERENCE. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BRIAN SCHAAF, FORD MOTOR CREDIT COMPANY CFO, WILL HOLD A FIRESIDE CHAT AT THE J.P. MORGAN AUTOMOTIVE CONFERENCE AT 11:25 A.M. THURSDAY, AUG. 12. THE VIRTUAL DISCUSSION WILL INCLUDE FORD CREDIT'S FINANCIAL RESULTS, FUNDING, LIQUIDITY AND PORTFOLIO PERFORMANCE, AS WELL AS Q&A. PARTICIPANTS ARE ENCOURAGED TO LISTEN TO THE WEBCAST ONLINE.
