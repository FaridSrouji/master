FORD PREVIEWS EFFECT OF PARTS SHORTAGES ON Q3 PERFORMANCE, REAFFIRMS FULL-YEAR ADJUSTED EBIT GUIDANCE OF $11.5-$12.5B. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORD AGAIN AFFIRMED ITS EXPECTATION FOR FULL-YEAR 2022 ADJUSTED EARNINGS BEFORE INTEREST AND TAXES OF BETWEEN $11.5 BILLION TO $12.5 BILLION, DESPITE LIMITS ON AVAILABILITY OF CERTAIN PARTS AS WELL AS HIGHER PAYMENTS MADE TO SUPPLIERS TO ACCOUNT FOR THE EFFECTS OF INFLATION.
