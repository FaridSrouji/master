KID TRAX TOYS PARTNERS WITH FORD MOTOR COMPANY TO RECREATE A TOT-SIZED VERSION OF THE ICONIC FORD BRONCO. MADISON, WIS., NOV. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KID TRAX, A BRAND OF DOREL INDUSTRIES, INC. (TSX: DII.B, TSX: DII.A), TODAY ANNOUNCED A UNIQUE PARTNERSHIP WITH FORD MOTOR COMPANY TO CELEBRATE THE RETURN OF THE ICONIC FORD BRONCO.
