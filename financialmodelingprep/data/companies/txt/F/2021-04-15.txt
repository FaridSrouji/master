FORD: VIRTUAL ANNUAL SHAREHOLDER MEETING DETAILS. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORD MOTOR COMPANY WILL HOST ITS 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS AT 8:30 A.M. EDT ON THURSDAY, MAY 13. SHAREHOLDERS CAN LISTEN, VOTE AND SUBMIT QUESTIONS BY LOGGING IN AT WWW.VIRTUALSHAREHOLDERMEETING.COM/FORD2021. THE PROXY STATEMENT AND DETAILS OF THE VIRTUAL ANNUAL SHAREHOLDER MEETING ARE AVAILABLE IN THE FINANCIALS & FILINGS SECTION AT WWW.SHAREHOLDER.FORD.COM.
