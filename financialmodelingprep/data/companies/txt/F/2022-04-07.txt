FORD MOTOR COMPANY BOARD DECLARES DIVIDEND FOR SECOND-QUARTER 2022. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF FORD MOTOR COMPANY TODAY DECLARED A SECOND-QUARTER REGULAR DIVIDEND OF 10 CENTS PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING COMMON AND CLASS B STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 1 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 26.
