FORD AND ADT TO FORM JOINT VENTURE TO FORTIFY VEHICLE SECURITY WITH BREAKTHROUGH TECHNOLOGY. DETROIT, LONDON AND BOCA RATON, FLA., JAN. 18, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FORD (NYSE: F) AND ADT INC. (NYSE: ADT) WILL INVEST IN A NEW JOINT VENTURE CALLED CANOPY THAT COMBINES ADT'S PROFESSIONAL SECURITY MONITORING AND FORD'S AI-DRIVEN VIDEO CAMERA TECHNOLOGY TO HELP CUSTOMERS STRENGTHEN SECURITY OF NEW AND EXISTING VEHICLES ACROSS AUTOMOTIVE BRANDS.
