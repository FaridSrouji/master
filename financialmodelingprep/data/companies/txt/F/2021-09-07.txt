FORD NAMES DOUG FIELD CHIEF ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY AND EMBEDDED SYSTEMS OFFICER. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORD MOTOR COMPANY TODAY NAMED DOUG FIELD AS THE COMPANY'S CHIEF ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY AND EMBEDDED SYSTEMS OFFICER AS IT CONTINUES TO ADVANCE ITS FORD+ GROWTH PLAN. FIELD JOINS FORD FROM APPLE, WHERE HE SERVED AS VP, SPECIAL PROJECTS. BEFORE THAT, HE WAS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, ENGINEERING, AT TESLA.
