FORD MOTOR CO AND UNITED AUTO WORKERS REACH TENTATIVE AGREEMENT. FORD MOTOR CO. AND THE UNITED AUTO WORKERS HAVE REACHED A PROPOSED TENTATIVE AGREEMENT LESS THAN A WEEK AFTER UAW MEMBERS RATIFIED AN AGREEMENT WITH GENERAL MOTORS CO. THE PACE OF NEGOTIATIONS AT FORD STARKLY CONTRASTS THAT SET BY GENERAL MOTORS CO. AND THE UAW. THE UNION RATIFIED A CONTRACT WITH GM AFTER A SIX-WEEK STRIKE. FORD AND THE UAW, WHICH CONTINUED NEGOTIATING DURING THE STRIKE. THEY WRAPPED THREE DAYS OF NEGOTIATIONS. EXPERTS HAD EXPECTED A TENTATIVE CONTRACT WITH FORD WOULD COME RELATIVELY QUICKLY. GM AND THE UAW HAD TO START FROM SCRATCH TO ESTABLISH A NEW FOUR-YEAR CONTRACT. BARGAINING AT FORD - AND AT FIAT CHRYSLER AUTOMOBILES NV, WHICH WILL BE THE FINAL DETROIT AUTOMAKER TO NEGOTIATE A 2019 CONTRACT WITH THE UNION - HAVE THE BENEFIT OF FOLLOWING THE PATTERN SET BY GM.
