FORD COMMITS TO MANUFACTURING BATTERIES, TO FORM NEW JOINT VENTURE WITH SK INNOVATION TO SCALE NA BATTERY DELIVERIES. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORD AND SK INNOVATION ANNOUNCED TODAY THEY HAVE SIGNED AN MOU TO CREATE A JOINT VENTURE – TO BE CALLED BLUEOVALSK – TO PRODUCE APPROXIMATELY 60 GWH ANNUALLY IN TRACTION BATTERY CELLS AND ARRAY MODULES, STARTING MID-DECADE, WITH POTENTIAL TO EXPAND.
