LISA DRAKE TO DISCUSS FORD'S PLANS TO LEAD IN THE ELECTRIC REVOLUTION AT MORGAN STANLEY VIRTUAL LAGUNA CONFERENCE. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LISA DRAKE, COO, FORD NORTH AMERICA, WILL DISCUSS FORD'S COMMITMENT TO ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND ITS PLANS TO LEAD IN THE ELECTRIC REVOLUTION AT THE MORGAN STANLEY LAGUNA CONFERENCE AT 1:30 P.M. ET ON MONDAY, SEPT. 13. DRAKE WILL HIGHLIGHT FORD'S DIFFERENTIATED APPROACH AS IT ELECTRIFIES ITS ICONIC NAMEPLATES LIKE THE F-150 LIGHTNING, MUSTANG MACH-E, AND E-TRANSIT.
