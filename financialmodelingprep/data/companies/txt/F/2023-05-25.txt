FORD EV CUSTOMERS TO GAIN ACCESS TO 12,000 TESLA SUPERCHARGERS; COMPANY TO ADD NORTH AMERICAN CHARGING STANDARD PORT IN FUTURE EVS. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORD MOTOR COMPANY SAID TODAY IT REACHED AN AGREEMENT WITH TESLA MOTORS THAT WILL PROVIDE FORD ELECTRIC VEHICLE CUSTOMERS ACCESS TO MORE THAN 12,000 TESLA SUPERCHARGERS ACROSS THE U.S. AND CANADA, DOUBLING THE NUMBER OF FAST-CHARGERS AVAILABLE TO FORD EV CUSTOMERS STARTING SPRING 2024. “THIS IS GREAT NEWS FOR OUR CUSTOMERS WHO WILL HAVE UNPRECEDENTED ACCESS TO THE LARGEST NETWORK OF FAST-CHARGERS IN THE U.S. AND CANADA WITH 12,000+ TESLA SUPERCHARGERS PLUS 10,0.
