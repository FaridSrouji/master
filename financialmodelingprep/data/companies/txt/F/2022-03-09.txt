JIM FARLEY TO DISCUSS THE AUTO INDUSTRY'S SHIFT TO ELECTRIFICATION AT CERAWEEK ANNUAL CONFERENCE. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORD PRESIDENT AND CEO, JIM FARLEY, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A FIRESIDE CHAT ABOUT THE AUTO INDUSTRY'S TRANSITION TO ELECTRIC VEHICLES AT THE 2022 CERAWEEK CONFERENCE ON THURSDAY, MARCH 10 AT 8:30 P.M. ET. FARLEY WILL BE JOINED BY PATTI POPPE, CEO PG&E CORPORATION, IN A SPECIAL DIALOGUE MODERATED BY DANIEL YERGIN, VICE CHAIRMAN S&P GLOBAL. THE DISCUSSION WILL FOCUS ON THE SHIFT TO ELECTRIC VEHICLES, THE ROLE OF AUTOMAKERS, THE ENERGY SECTOR AND POLICYMAKERS
