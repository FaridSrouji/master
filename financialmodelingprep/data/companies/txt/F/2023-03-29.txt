JIM BAUMBICK TO DISCUSS ACCELERATION OF DELIVERING FORD+ PLAN AT BANK OF AMERICA'S 2023 AUTOMOTIVE SUMMIT. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORD VICE PRESIDENT OF PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT OPERATIONS & QUALITY, JIM BAUMBICK, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A FIRESIDE CHAT AND DISCUSS DELIVERING THE FORD+ PLAN AT BANK OF AMERICA'S 2023 AUTOMOTIVE SUMMIT WITH AUTO ANALYST JOHN MURPHY. THE CONFERENCE WILL TAKE PLACE ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 5, AT 12:10 P.M. ET.
