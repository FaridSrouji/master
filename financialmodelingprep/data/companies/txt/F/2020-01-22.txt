FORD MOTOR CO EXPECT TO RECORD PRE-TAX REMEASUREMENT LOSS OF APPROXIMATELY $2.2 BILLION RELATED TO PENSION & OPEB PLANS. FORD MOTOR CO - FOR QUARTER ENDED DEC 31, 2019, EXPECT TO RECORD PRE-TAX REMEASUREMENT LOSS OF APPROXIMATELY $2.2 BILLION RELATED TO PENSION & OPEB PLANS.FORD MOTOR CO - EXPECTED PRE-TAX REMEASUREMENT LOSS FOR QUARTER ENDED DEC. 31 INCLUDESA $2.0 BILLION LOSS ASSOCIATED WITH PENSION PLANS OUTSIDE U.S..FORD MOTOR CO - REMEASUREMENT LOSS DID NOT HAVE AN IMPACT ON OUR CASH IN 2019, AND DOES NOT CHANGE OUR EXPECTATIONS FOR PENSION CONTRIBUTIONS IN 2020.FORD MOTOR CO - EXPECTED PRE-TAX REMEASUREMENT LOSS FOR QUARTER ENDED DEC. 31 INCLUDES $600 MILLION LOSS ASSOCIATED WITH OPEB PLANS GLOBALLY.FORD MOTOR CO - ON AFTER-TAX BASIS, REMEASUREMENT LOSS IS EXPECTED TO REDUCE NET INCOME BY ABOUT $1.7 BILLION FOR QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2019.FORD - INCLUDING IMPACT OF REMEASUREMENT GAINS, LOSSES, EXPECT UNDERFUNDED STATUS FOR PENSION & OPEB PLANS AT 2019 END  OF ABOUT $6.8 BILLION & $6.1 BILLION.
