HUNT VALVE AWARDED $2 MILLION CONTRACT BY NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING FOR FORD CLASS CARRIER ESSENTIAL PARTS. BELOIT, WIS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #FMD--FAIRBANKS MORSE DEFENSE (FMD), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF ARCLINE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT, HAS BEEN AWARDED A CONTRACT BY NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING (NNS) TO PROVIDE ESSENTIAL PARTS THROUGH HUNT VALVE FOR THE FORD CLASS AIRCRAFT CARRIERS CVN 78 – CVN 81. THE CONTRACT, VALUED AT APPROXIMATELY $2 MILLION, COVERS PARTS THAT WILL BE DELIVERED DURING THE SECOND AND THIRD QUARTERS OF 2022.
