FORD TO HOST MARCH 23 ‘TEACH-IN' ABOUT FINANCIAL REPORTING, STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS OF NEW CUSTOMER-FOCUSED SEGMENTS. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORD NEXT WEEK WILL TAKE THE INVESTMENT COMMUNITY AND OTHER STAKEHOLDERS THROUGH HOW NEW, CUSTOMER-FOCUSED BUSINESS SEGMENTS – FORD BLUE FOR ICONIC GAS-POWERED AND HYBRID VEHICLES, FORD MODEL E FOR BREAKTHROUGH ELECTRIC VEHICLES AS WELL AS THE DIGITAL PLATFORM AND SOFTWARE FOR ALL FORD AND LINCOLN VEHICLES, AND FORD PRO FOR COMMERCIAL VEHICLES AND SERVICES – WILL AFFECT ITS FINANCIAL REPORTING AND IS EXPECTED TO UNLOCK SIGNIFICANT STRATEGIC POTENTIAL OF THE FORD+ GROWTH PLAN.
