KLFB TRIAL LAWYERS WIN IMPORTANT U.S. SUPREME COURT RULING IN FORD PRODUCT DEFECT LAWSUIT. WASHINGTON, MARCH 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KASTER, LYNCH, FARRAR & BALL TRIAL LAWYERS HAVE WON AN IMPORTANT U.S. SUPREME COURT VICTORY, SECURING A UNANIMOUS OPINION THAT FORD MOTOR CO. (NYSE: F) CANNOT LIMIT THE ABILITY OF INJURED INDIVIDUALS TO HOLD MANUFACTURERS ACCOUNTABLE FOR DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS BY RESTRICTING WHERE THOSE LAWSUITS CAN BE FILED.
