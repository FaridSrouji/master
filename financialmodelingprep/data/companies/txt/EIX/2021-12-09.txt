EDISON INTERNATIONAL RAISES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND 5.66%; 18TH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL INCREASE. ROSEMEAD, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EDISON INTERNATIONAL DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.70 PER SHARE.
EDISON INTERNATIONAL ANNOUNCES THE APPOINTMENT OF PETER TAYLOR AS INDEPENDENT NON-EXECUTIVE CHAIR OF THE BOARD. ROSEMEAD, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EDISON INTERNATIONAL ANNOUNCED THAT PETER TAYLOR WILL SERVE AS THE COMPANY'S NEXT INDEPENDENT, NON-EXECUTIVE CHAIR OF ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
