SCE TO ADD 535 MEGAWATTS OF ENERGY STORAGE TO IMPROVE GRID RELIABILITY — RESOURCES ARE EXPECTED FOR SUMMER 2022. ROSEMEAD, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TO INCREASE GRID RELIABILITY FOR NEXT SUMMER, SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA EDISON WILL ADD 535 MEGAWATTS OF BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE AT THREE STRATEGICALLY LOCATED SCE SUBSTATIONS. THIS ACTION RESPONDS TO GOV. GAVIN NEWSOM'S EMERGENCY PROCLAMATION TO ACCELERATE THE RAPID DEPLOYMENT OF NEW CLEAN ENERGY AND STORAGE PROJECTS FOR SUMMER 2021 AND 2022.
