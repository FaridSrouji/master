CALIFORNIA FIREFIGHTING AGENCIES, SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA EDISON TEAM UP TO PROVIDE STATE-OF-THE-ART FIREFIGHTING AIRCRAFT. ROSEMEAD, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WITH FUNDING FROM SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA EDISON, LOCAL FIREFIGHTING AGENCIES NOW HAVE ADDITIONAL STATE-OF-THE-ART AIRCRAFT TO COMBAT WILDFIRES.
