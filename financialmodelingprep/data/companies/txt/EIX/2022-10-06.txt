APPLICATION PERIOD OPENS FOR $1.5 MILLION EDISON SCHOLARS PROGRAM. ROSEMEAD, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HIGH SCHOOL SENIORS WITH BIG DREAMS WHO WANT TO MAKE A DIFFERENCE IN THE WORLD THROUGH THE STUDY OF SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, ENGINEERING OR MATH (STEM) ARE INVITED TO BECOME EDISON SCHOLARS. EDISON INTERNATIONAL IS ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS FOR THE $1.5 MILLION SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM THROUGH DEC. 9. STUDENTS CAN SEE IF THEY ARE ELIGIBLE AND APPLY AT EDISONSCHOLARS.COM.
