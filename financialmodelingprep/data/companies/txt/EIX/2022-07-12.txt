SCE ACHIEVES WILDFIRE MITIGATION WORK MILESTONE TO REDUCE IGNITION RISK WHILE KEEPING COMMUNITIES SAFE. ROSEMEAD, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA EDISON ESTIMATES THAT IT HAS REDUCED THE PROBABILITY OF LOSSES FROM CATASTROPHIC WILDFIRES ASSOCIATED WITH SCE'S INFRASTRUCTURE BY 65% TO 70% COMPARED TO PRE-2018 LEVELS. THE ONGOING DEPLOYMENT OF COVERED CONDUCTOR AND OTHER GRID HARDENING MEASURES, ENHANCED INSPECTIONS, EXPANDED VEGETATION MANAGEMENT AND MORE TARGETED USE OF PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFFS (PSPS) ALLOWED THE COMPANY TO ACHIEVE THIS SIGNIFICANT REDUCTION IN WILDFIRE RISK.
