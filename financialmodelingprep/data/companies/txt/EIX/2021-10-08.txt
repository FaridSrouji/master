SCE'S 2020 SPENDING WITH DIVERSE SUPPLIERS CREATES MORE THAN 23,000 JOBS. ROSEMEAD, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA EDISON RECORDED 37.66% OF ITS CORPORATE PURCHASES, OR $2.4 BILLION IN SPENDING, WITH MORE THAN 600 SMALL AND DIVERSE BUSINESSES IN 2020. THE COMPANY'S CONTRACTING WITH THESE COMPANIES HAD AN ECONOMIC IMPACT OF MORE THAN $3.5 BILLION AND SUSTAINED MORE THAN 23,000 JOBS.
