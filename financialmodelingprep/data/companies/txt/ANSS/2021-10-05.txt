ANSYS AND AUTODESK TO DELIVER FUSION 360 PCB EXTENSION. PITTSBURGH, OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- / KEY HIGHLIGHTS AUTODESK FUSION 360 EXTENSION WILL PROVIDE FAST, PREDICTIVELY ACCURATE INSIGHT, HELPING DESIGNERS ACHIEVE FIRST-PASS SUCCESS IN PRINTED CIRCUIT BOARD (PCB) DESIGN THE EXTENSION WILL INCREASE CONSUMER PRODUCT DESIGNERS' AND ENGINEERS' ELECTROMAGNETIC ANALYSIS ACCESSIBILITY INTRODUCING SIMULATION EARLIER IN THE DESIGN PROCESS WILL EMPOWER DESIGN TEAMS TO EXPLORE AND VALIDATE NEW PCB DESIGNS MORE QUICKLY AND ACCELERATE THE DEVELOPMENT OF NEXT-GENERATION SMART PRODUCTS ANSYS (NASDAQ: ANSS) AND AUTODESK ARE COLLABORATING ON A PRINTED CIRCUIT BOARD (PCB) EXTENSION, MARKING AUTODESK FUSION 360'S FIRST THIRD-PARTY EXTENSION. THE EXTENSION ALIGNS WITH THE COMPANIES' SHARED VISION TO INCREASE CONSUMER PRODUCT DESIGNERS' AND ENGINEERS' ACCESS TO ELECTROMAGNETIC ANALYSIS.
