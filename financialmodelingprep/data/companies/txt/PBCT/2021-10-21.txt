M&T BANK CORPORATION RECEIVES APPROVAL FROM NEW YORK STATE DEPARTMENT OF FINANCIAL SERVICES AND CONNECTICUT DEPARTMENT OF BANKING FOR THE MERGER OF M&T BANK AND PEOPLE'S UNITED BANK. BUFFALO, N.Y., OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK CORPORATION (NYSE: MTB) ("M&T") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS RECEIVED APPROVAL FROM THE NEW YORK STATE DEPARTMENT OF FINANCIAL SERVICES AND THE CONNECTICUT DEPARTMENT OF BANKING TO COMPLETE THE MERGER OF M&T BANK WITH PEOPLE'S UNITED BANK.
PEOPLE'S UNITED FINANCIAL DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND ON PREFERRED STOCK. BRIDGEPORT, CONN. , OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PEOPLE'S UNITED FINANCIAL, INC. (NASDAQ: PBCT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.3515625 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S FIXED-TO-FLOATING RATE NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK, SUBJECT TO AND CONDITIONED UPON THE EFFECTIVE TIME OF THE MERGER BETWEEN THE COMPANY AND M&T BANK CORPORATION NOT OCCURRING PRIOR TO THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 1, 2021.
