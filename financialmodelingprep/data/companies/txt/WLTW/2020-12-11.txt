WILLIS TOWERS WATSON ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ARLINGTON, VA. AND LONDON, DEC. 11, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WILLIS TOWERS WATSON (NASDAQ: WLTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, ANNOUNCED THAT IT APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.71 PER ORDINARY SHARE FOR THE QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2020. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON OR AROUND JANUARY 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 31, 2020.
