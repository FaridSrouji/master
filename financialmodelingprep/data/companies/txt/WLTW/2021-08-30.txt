WILLIS TOWERS WATSON SELECTS ANDREW KRASNER AS NEW CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. ARLINGTON, VA., AUG. 30, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WILLIS TOWERS WATSON (NASDAQ: WLTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SELECTED ANDREW KRASNER AS THE COMPANY'S NEW CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO). KRASNER RETURNS TO WILLIS TOWERS WATSON AFTER DRIVING FINANCIAL STRATEGY AND PERFORMANCE AS THE CFO OF ASSUREDPARTNERS, INC. HE SUCCEEDS MICHAEL (MIKE) BURWELL IN THIS ROLE. MIKE WILL BE TAKING ON A SENIOR ROLE IN THE MEDICAL AND DATA ANALYTICS INDUSTRY GOING FORWARD.
