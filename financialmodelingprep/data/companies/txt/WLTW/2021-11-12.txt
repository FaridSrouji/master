ELENI LYKOUDI APPOINTED LEADER OF CEEMEA REGION AT WILLIS TOWERS WATSON. LONDON, NOV. 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WILLIS TOWERS WATSON (NASDAQ:WLTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING, AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TODAY WELCOMED ELENI LYKOUDI AS THE NEW HEAD OF THE CENTRAL AND EASTERN EUROPE, MIDDLE EAST, AND AFRICA (CEEMEA) REGION OF WILLIS TOWERS WATSON (WTW).
