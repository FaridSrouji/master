INVESTMENT INDUSTRY LEADERS ACKNOWLEDGE THE POWER OF CULTURE. ARLINGTON, VA., NOV. 09, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ACCORDING TO A POLL OF C-SUITE INVESTMENT LEADERS, AN OVERWHELMING MAJORITY (89%) BELIEVE THAT ORGANIZATIONAL CULTURE IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN BUSINESS STRATEGY IN INFLUENCING GOOD ORGANIZATIONAL OUTCOMES. THE SURVEY,* WHICH INVOLVED REPRESENTATIVES FROM 15 INVESTMENT ORGANIZATIONS AROUND THE WORLD WITH TOTAL ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT OF OVER US$8.5 TRILLION, WAS CONDUCTED AS PART OF THE THINKING AHEAD INSTITUTE'S POWER OF CULTURE LEADERSHIP STUDY AND WHITE PAPER, WHICH IS BEING RELEASED PUBLICLY TODAY.
