ADM NAMED TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANY LIST FOR 13TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADM (NYSE: ADM) WAS NAMED ONE OF THE WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES IN THE FOOD PRODUCTION INDUSTRY BY FORTUNE MAGAZINE FOR THE 13TH YEAR IN A ROW. FORTUNE'S “WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES” LIST IS THE DEFINITIVE REPORT CARD ON CORPORATE REPUTATION, AND IS DEVELOPED BY FORTUNE AND KORN FERRY.
