ADM ANNOUNCES CHANGE TO VIRTUAL-ONLY 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADM (NYSE: ADM), A GLOBAL LEADER IN SUSTAINABLE HUMAN AND ANIMAL NUTRITION, AND ONE OF THE WORLD'S PREMIER AGRICULTURAL ORIGINATION AND PROCESSING COMPANIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL BE HOLDING ITS 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS (THE “ANNUAL MEETING”) AS A VIRTUAL-ONLY MEETING DUE TO CONCERNS OVER RECENT GLOBAL OUTBREAKS AND THE POTENTIAL FOR RESURGENCE OF THE COVID-19 VIRUS AS THE ANNUAL MEETING APPROACHES.
