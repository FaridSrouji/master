ADM CARBON CAPTURE AND STORAGE PROJECT EARNS TOP HONORS FROM ENVIRONMENT + ENERGY LEADER. THE AWARD RECOGNIZES EXEMPLARY WORK BEING DONE TODAY IN THE FIELDS OF ENERGY AND ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT.
