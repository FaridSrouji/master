ADM RECOGNIZED AS A “DIVERSITY LEADER 2023” BY THE FINANCIAL TIMES. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ADM (NYSE:ADM) WAS NAMED A DIVERSITY LEADER IN THIS YEAR'S FINANCIAL TIMES RANKING OF 850 EUROPEAN COMPANIES ON DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION. ADM PLACED 15TH OVERALL AND 2ND IN THE FOOD AND BEVERAGES SECTOR FOR THE 2023 STUDY.
