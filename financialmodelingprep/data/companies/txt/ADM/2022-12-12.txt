ADM RECEIVES BUSINESS SUSTAINABILITY AWARD FOR WORK TO EXPAND REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ADM (NYSE:ADM) HAS WON THE ENVIRONMENTAL INITIATIVES CATEGORY OF THE 2022 SEAL BUSINESS SUSTAINABILITY AWARDS FOR EXPANDING ITS REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE PROGRAMS SUPPORTING FARMERS ACROSS NORTH AMERICA. GRANTED BY A JUDGING PANEL OF RENOWNED ENVIRONMENTAL AND ENERGY INDUSTRY EXPERTS, THE AWARD RECOGNIZES EXCEPTIONAL PROGRAMS OR INITIATIVES THAT PUSH THE FRONTIER ON ENVIRONMENTAL LEADERSHIP AND PROGRESS.
