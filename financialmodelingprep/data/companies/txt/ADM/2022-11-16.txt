ADM AND PARTNERS AWARDED FIELD TO MARKET'S COLLABORATION OF THE YEAR AWARD FOR REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE PROJECT. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ADM (NYSE: ADM) AND ITS PARTNERS, KEURIG DR PEPPER, PEPSICO, AND PRACTICAL FARMERS OF IOWA, HAVE WON THE FIELD TO MARKET: ALLIANCE FOR SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE™ 2022 COLLABORATION OF THE YEAR AWARD FOR THEIR REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE PROJECT SUPPORTING NEBRASKA FARMERS. THE AWARD, ONE OF FIELD TO MARKET'S SUSTAINABILITY LEADERSHIP AWARDS, RECOGNIZES AN OUTSTANDING COLLABORATION AND CROSS-SECTOR PARTNERSHIP IN ADVANCING CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT IN SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE AT THE FIELD AND LANDSCAPE LEVEL.
