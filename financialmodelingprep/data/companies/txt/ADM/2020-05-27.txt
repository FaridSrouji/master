ARCHER DANIELS MIDLAND AND MARFRIG ENTER AGREEMENT TO CREATE PLANTPLUS FOODS. ARCHER DANIELS MIDLAND CO - CO, MARFRIG ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO CREATE PLANTPLUS FOODS.ARCHER DANIELS MIDLAND CO - PLANTPLUS FOODS IS A JV FOR SALE OF PLANT-BASED FOOD PRODUCTS ACROSS SOUTH AMERICAN AND NORTH AMERICAN MARKETS.ARCHER DANIELS MIDLAND CO - MARFRIG WILL INITIALLY OWN 70 PERCENT OF PLANTPLUS FOODS, WITH ADM OWNING 30 PERCENT.ARCHER DANIELS MIDLAND - MARFRIG WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR PRODUCTION AND DISTRIBUTION, UTILIZING ITS FACILITIES IN SOUTH AMERICA.ARCHER DANIELS MIDLAND CO - MARFRIG, ADM PLAN TO LAUNCH NEW COMPANY AND START OPERATIONS AS SOON AS REQUIRED REGULATORY APPROVALS HAVE BEEN RECEIVED.
