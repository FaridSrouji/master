SOLAREA BIO ANNOUNCES LICENSING AGREEMENT WITH ADM. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., OCT. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOLAREA BIO, A BIOTECH COMPANY BASED IN CAMBRIDGE, MA THAT IS DEVELOPING MICROBIAL-BASED SOLUTIONS TO AID HUMAN HEALTH, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A TECHNOLOGY LICENSING AGREEMENT WITH GLOBAL NUTRITION LEADER ADM (NYSE: ADM).
