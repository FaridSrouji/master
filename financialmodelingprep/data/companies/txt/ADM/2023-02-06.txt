ADM OPENS MORE THAN $30 MILLION STATE-OF-THE-ART PRODUCTION FACILITY IN SPAIN TO MEET GROWING DEMAND FOR PROBIOTICS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADM (NYSE: ADM), ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADERS IN SCIENCE-BACKED NUTRITION SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS OPENED A NEW PRODUCTION FACILITY IN VALENCIA, SPAIN TO HELP MEET RISING GLOBAL DEMAND FOR PROBIOTICS, POSTBIOTICS AND OTHER PRODUCTS THAT SUPPORT HEALTH AND WELL-BEING.
