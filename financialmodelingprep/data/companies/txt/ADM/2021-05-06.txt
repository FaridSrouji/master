ADM ELECTS DIRECTORS AT ANNUAL MEETING. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADM (NYSE: ADM) TODAY ANNOUNCED AT ITS 98TH ANNUAL STOCKHOLDERS' MEETING THE ELECTION OF ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, INCLUDING NEW DIRECTOR TED COLBERT. COLBERT HAS BEEN EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF THE BOEING COMPANY AND PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF BOEING GLOBAL SERVICES SINCE OCTOBER 2019.
