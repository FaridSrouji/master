LG CHEM AND ADM LAUNCH JOINT VENTURES, ANNOUNCE INTENDED LOCATION FOR U.S. PRODUCTION OF LACTIC ACID AND POLYLACTIC ACID PRODUCTION. SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LG CHEM (KRX: 051910), A LEADING GLOBAL DIVERSIFIED CHEMICAL COMPANY, AND ADM (NYSE: ADM), A GLOBAL LEADER IN NUTRITION AND BIOSOLUTIONS, TODAY HELD A SIGNING CEREMONY LAUNCHING TWO JOINT VENTURES FOR U.S. PRODUCTION OF LACTIC ACID AND POLYLACTIC ACID TO MEET GROWING DEMAND FOR A WIDE VARIETY OF PLANT-BASED PRODUCTS, INCLUDING BIOPLASTICS.
