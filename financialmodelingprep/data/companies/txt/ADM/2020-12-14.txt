CALYXT ANNOUNCES SALE OF ENTIRE 2020 GRAIN PRODUCTION TO ADM. ROSEVILLE, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CALYXT, INC. (NASDAQ: CLXT), A PLANT-BASED TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS CONTRACTED TO SELL ALL 2020 GRAIN PRODUCTION OF ITS HIGH OLEIC SOYBEAN TO ADM (NYSE: ADM), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HUMAN AND ANIMAL NUTRITION AND THE WORLD'S PREMIER AGRICULTURAL ORIGINATION AND PROCESSING COMPANY HEADQUARTERED IN CHICAGO. THE TRANSACTION IS A KEY STEP IN CALYXT'S 2020 TRANSITION TO AN ADVANCED GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY FOCUSED ON PROVIDING PLANT-BASED INNOVATION
