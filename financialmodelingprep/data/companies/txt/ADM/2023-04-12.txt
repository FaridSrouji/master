ADM, BELIEVER MEATS TO BRING EXPERTISE TOGETHER TO ADVANCE THE HORIZONS OF CULTIVATED MEAT. CHICAGO & REHOVOT, ISRAEL--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADM (NYSE:ADM), A GLOBAL LEADER IN SUSTAINABLE NUTRITION, AND BELIEVER MEATS, A LEADING PIONEER OF THE CULTIVATED MEAT INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THEY HAVE SIGNED A NON-EXCLUSIVE MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) TO COLLABORATE ON NEW WAYS TO PROPEL THE DEVELOPMENT AND COMMERCIALIZATION OF CULTIVATED MEAT PRODUCTS.
