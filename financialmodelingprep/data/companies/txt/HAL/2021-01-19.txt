HALLIBURTON ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER 2020 RESULTS. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HALLIBURTON COMPANY (NYSE: HAL) ANNOUNCED TODAY A NET LOSS OF $235 MILLION, OR $0.27 PER DILUTED SHARE, FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020. THIS COMPARES TO A NET LOSS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2020 OF $17 MILLION, OR $0.02 PER DILUTED SHARE. ADJUSTED NET INCOME FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020, EXCLUDING IMPAIRMENTS AND OTHER CHARGES, WAS $160 MILLION, OR $0.18 PER DILUTED SHARE.
