HALLIBURTON ANNOUNCES SECOND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HALLIBURTON COMPANY (NYSE: HAL) ANNOUNCED TODAY NET INCOME OF $109 MILLION, OR $0.12 PER DILUTED SHARE, FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022. THIS COMPARES TO NET INCOME FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022 OF $263 MILLION, OR $0.29 PER DILUTED SHARE. ADJUSTED NET INCOME FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022, EXCLUDING IMPAIRMENTS AND OTHER CHARGES, WAS $442 MILLION, OR $0.49 PER DILUTED SHARE.
