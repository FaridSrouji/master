FREESTONE ANNOUNCES CLOSING OF GROWTH FINANCING FOR MOMENTUM TECHNOLOGIES. DALLAS, DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FREESTONE HOLDCO, LLC ("FREESTONE"), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF TAILWATER CAPITAL LLC ("TAILWATER"), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SIGNED DEFINITIVE AGREEMENTS TO LEAD A $20 MILLION INVESTMENT IN MOMENTUM TECHNOLOGIES ("MOMENTUM" OR THE "COMPANY"), A DALLAS-BASED LITHIUM-ION BATTERY AND RARE EARTH PERMANENT MAGNET RECYCLING COMPANY. FOUNDED IN 2016, MOMENTUM INSTALLS AND OPERATES RECYCLING PLANTS FOR ITS CUSTOMERS AT A WIDE RANGE OF CAPACITIES USING ITS PATENTED MODULAR, SCALABLE RECYCLING TECHNOLOGY, MEMBRANE SOLVENT EXTRACTION ("MSX").
