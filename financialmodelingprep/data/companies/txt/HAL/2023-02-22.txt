HALLIBURTON NAMES JANET WEISS, MAURICE SMITH TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HALLIBURTON COMPANY (NYSE: HAL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ADDITION OF MS. JANET WEISS AND MR. MAURICE SMITH TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. THE APPOINTMENTS WERE EFFECTIVE FEB. 21, AND BOTH WILL STAND FOR ELECTION BY SHAREHOLDERS AT THE ANNUAL MEETING ON MAY 17. “ON BEHALF OF THE BOARD, I AM DELIGHTED TO WELCOME JANET AND MAURICE TO HALLIBURTON,” SAID JEFF MILLER, HALLIBURTON CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT, AND CEO.
