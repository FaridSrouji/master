HALLIBURTON APPOINTS NEW EASTERN HEMISPHERE PRESIDENT. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HALLIBURTON COMPANY (NYSE: HAL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SHANNON SLOCUM, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT AND MARKETING, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT, EASTERN HEMISPHERE, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. JOE RAINEY, PRESIDENT, EASTERN HEMISPHERE, IS RETIRING AND WILL SERVE AS SENIOR ADVISOR TO CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT, AND CEO JEFF MILLER DURING THE TRANSITION.
