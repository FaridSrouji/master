PETROBRAS ACCELERATES DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION WITH HALLIBURTON'S IENERGY® CLOUD. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HALLIBURTON COMPANY (NYSE: HAL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PETROBRAS WILL USE THE LANDMARK IENERGY® DIGITAL PLATFORM TO ADDRESS ITS SUBSURFACE CHALLENGES. THE COMPANIES EXECUTED A CONTRACT THAT GIVES PETROBRAS ACCESS TO THE ENTIRE HALLIBURTON LANDMARK DECISIONSPACE® 365 GEOSCIENCE SUITE INCLUDING CLOUD-BASED, NEXT-GENERATION TECHNOLOGIES LIKE SEISMIC ENGINE, SCALABLE EARTH MODELING, ASSISTED LITHOLOGY INTERPRETATION, DS365.
