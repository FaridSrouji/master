HALLIBURTON DECLARES DIVIDEND. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #DIVIDEND--HALLIBURTON COMPANY (NYSE: HAL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A 2022 THIRD QUARTER DIVIDEND OF TWELVE CENTS ($0.12) A SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 28, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 7, 2022.
