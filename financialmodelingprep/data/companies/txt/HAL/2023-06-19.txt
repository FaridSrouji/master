HALLIBURTON AND NABORS INDUSTRIES COLLABORATE ON LEADING WELL CONSTRUCTION AUTOMATION. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HALLIBURTON COMPANY (NYSE: HAL) AND NABORS INDUSTRIES (NYSE: NBR, “NABORS”) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT ON LEADING WELL CONSTRUCTION AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS. UNDER THE AGREEMENT, HALLIBURTON AND NABORS WILL COLLABORATE TOGETHER ON THEIR TECHNOLOGIES INCLUDING THE HALLIBURTON WELL CONSTRUCTION 4.0 DIGITAL SURFACE AND SUBSURFACE DRILLING TECHNOLOGIES, THE LOGIX® AUTONOMOUS DRILLING PLATFORM, AND THE NABORS SMARTROS® UNIVERSAL RIG CONTROLS AND AUTOMATION PLATFORM AND RIGCLO.
