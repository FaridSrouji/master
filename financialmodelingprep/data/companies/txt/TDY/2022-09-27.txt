TELEDYNE INTRODUCES CONTACT IMAGE SENSORS FOR HIGH-SPEED, HIGH-RESOLUTION LINE SCAN IMAGING. WATERLOO, ONTARIO, SEPT. 27, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TELEDYNE DALSA IS PLEASED TO INTRODUCE AXCIS™, A NEW FAMILY OF HIGH-SPEED AND HIGH-RESOLUTION FULLY INTEGRATED LINE SCAN IMAGING MODULES. THESE EASY-TO-USE CONTACT IMAGE SENSORS (CIS) COMBINE SENSORS, LENSES, AND LIGHTS ALL-IN-ONE, OFFERING A LOWER COST INSPECTION SYSTEM FOR MANY DEMANDING MACHINE VISION APPLICATIONS.
