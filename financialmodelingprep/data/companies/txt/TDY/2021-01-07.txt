TELEDYNE PARADISE RECEIVES WGS CERTIFICATION FOR SATCOM MODEMS. RANCHO CORDOVA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE U.S. ARMY FORCES STRATEGIC COMMAND (ARSTRAT) HAS CERTIFIED TWO SATCOM MODEMS FROM TELEDYNE PARADISE FOR USE ON WGS SATELLITE SYSTEMS.
