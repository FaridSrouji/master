TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INC SAYS DETERMINE TO TERMINATE DISCUSSIONS REGARDING ITS PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF PHOTONIS. TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INC -  DETERMINED TO TERMINATE DISCUSSIONS REGARDING ITS PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF PHOTONIS.TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INC -  HAS DETERMINED THAT POTENTIAL ACQUISITION UNDER TERMS PROPOSED BY FRENCH MOE WAS NOT FEASIBLE.TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INC -  HAS VOLUNTARILY AGREED TO WITHDRAW ITS APPLICATION FOR FOREIGN INVESTMENT AUTHORIZATION.
