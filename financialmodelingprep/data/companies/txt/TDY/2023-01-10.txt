TELEDYNE E2V RELEASES HYDRA3D+, THE FIRST HIGH RESOLUTION TOF SENSOR TO WORK IN ALL LIGHT CONDITIONS WITHOUT MOTION ARTEFACTS. GRENOBLE, FRANCE, JAN. 10, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TELEDYNE E2V, A PART OF TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES [NYSE: TDY] ANNOUNCES THE RELEASE OF ITS HYDRA3D+ , A NEW TIME-OF-FLIGHT (TOF) CMOS IMAGE SENSOR WHICH INCORPORATES 832 X 600 PIXEL RESOLUTION AND IS TAILORED FOR VERSATILE 3D DETECTION AND MEASUREMENT.
