TELEDYNE AWARDED $596 MILLION INTERNATIONAL SPACE STATION SUPPORT CONTRACT. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED (NYSE:TDY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS SUBSIDIARY, TELEDYNE BROWN ENGINEERING, INC., HAS BEEN AWARDED THE MARSHALL OPERATIONS, SYSTEMS, SERVICES AND INTEGRATION II (MOSSI II) CONTRACT. AS THE PRIME CONTRACTOR, TELEDYNE IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE MANAGEMENT, PERSONNEL, SUPPLIES, AND EQUIPMENT TO SUPPORT MISSION OPERATIONS AND GROUND SYSTEMS DEVELOPMENT SERVICES TO SUPPORT THE INTERNATIONAL SPACE STATION (ISS) AND ITS MISSIONS.
