TELEDYNE FLIR DEFENSE DELIVERING 127 UNMANNED GROUND VEHICLES TO GERMAN ARMY. PARIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TELEDYNE FLIR DEFENSE, PART OF TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED (NYSE:TDY), ANNOUNCED TODAY AT EUROSATORY THAT IT IS COMPLETING DELIVERIES OF 127 PACKBOT® 525 UNMANNED GROUND VEHICLES (UGVS) TO THE GERMAN ARMY (DEUTSCHES HEER). FINAL SHIPMENTS ARE EXPECTED TO OCCUR IN JULY. CONTRACT AWARD AND DELIVERIES WERE FACILITATED THROUGH TELEDYNE FLIR'S PARTNER, EUROPEAN LOGISTIC PARTNERS (ELP) OF WUPPERTAL, GERMANY.
