TELEDYNE CONTROLS' AIRCRAFT CABIN ENVIRONMENT SENSOR (ACES) NOW CERTIFIED FOR AIRBUS A320 AIRCRAFT SERIES. EL SEGUNDO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TELEDYNE CONTROLS, THE AIRCRAFT DATA MANAGEMENT BUSINESS AND SUBSIDIARY OF TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:TDY), HAS OBTAINED FAA SUPPLEMENTAL TYPE CERTIFICATION (STC) APPROVAL FOR INSTALLATION OF ITS AIRCRAFT CABIN ENVIRONMENT SENSOR (ACES™) ON THE AIRBUS A320 AIRCRAFT SERIES.
