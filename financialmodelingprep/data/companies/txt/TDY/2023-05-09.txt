TELEDYNE TO HOLD INVESTOR MEETINGS. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED (NYSE:TDY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JASON VANWEES, VICE CHAIRMAN, AND EDWIN ROKS, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT, TELEDYNE DIGITAL IMAGING SEGMENT, WILL BE HOLDING INVESTOR MEETINGS AT THE GOLDMAN SACHS INDUSTRIALS & MATERIALS CONFERENCE 2023 ON WEDNESDAY, MAY 10. TELEDYNE'S LATEST INVESTOR PRESENTATION IS PUBLICLY AVAILABLE ON THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE AT WWW.TELEDYNE.COM/INVESTORS/EVENTS-AND-PRESENTATIONS. TELEDYNE.
