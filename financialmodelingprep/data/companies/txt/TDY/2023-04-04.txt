TELEDYNE FLIR DEBUTS G-SERIES OPTICAL GAS IMAGING CAMERAS WITH SUPERIOR GAS QUANTIFICATION AND WIRELESS DATA TRANSFER. GOLETA, CALIF. & DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TELEDYNE FLIR, PART OF TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED, TODAY DEBUTED THE NEW G-SERIES, A FAMILY OF HIGH-TECH, COOLED-CORE OPTICAL GAS IMAGING (OGI) CAMERAS THAT CAN HELP LEAK DETECTION AND REPAIR (LDAR) PROFESSIONALS SEAMLESSLY LOCATE AND DOCUMENT HARMFUL GAS EMISSIONS.
