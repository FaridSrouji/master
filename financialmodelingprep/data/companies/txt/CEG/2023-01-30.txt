QUAKER HOUGHTON SUPPORTS CLEAN ENERGY WITH CONSTELLATION ADVANCING ITS GOAL OF ACHIEVING CARBON NEUTRALITY. CONSHOHOCKEN, PA. , JAN. 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUAKER HOUGHTON (NYSE: KWR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A CONTRACT WITH CONSTELLATION TO PURCHASE RENEWABLE ENERGY CERTIFICATES ("RECS") TO MATCH APPROXIMATELY HALF OF ITS TOTAL U.S. BASED ELECTRICITY NEEDS.
