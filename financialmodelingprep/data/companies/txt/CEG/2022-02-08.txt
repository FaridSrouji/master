CONSTELLATION ENERGY CORPORATION DECLARES DIVIDEND. BALTIMORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CONSTELLATION ENERGY CORPORATION DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.141 PER SHARE ON CONSTELLATION'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON THURSDAY, MARCH 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF 5 P.M. EASTERN TIME ON FRIDAY, FEB. 25, 2022.
