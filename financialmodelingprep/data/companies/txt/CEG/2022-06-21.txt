CONSTELLATION CEO JOE DOMINGUEZ LAYS OUT VISION FOR CLEAN ENERGY TRANSITION. WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN AN ADDRESS TO ENERGY AND CLIMATE LEADERS TODAY, CONSTELLATION CEO JOE DOMINGUEZ SAID NUCLEAR ENERGY WILL PLAY A CENTRAL ROLE IN ACCELERATING THE TRANSITION TO A CLEAN-ENERGY ECONOMY BY PROVIDING A FOUNDATION OF SECURE, CARBON-FREE ENERGY TO THE GRID AND ENABLING NEW TECHNOLOGIES, SUCH AS CLEAN HYDROGEN TO POWER DIFFICULT-TO-DECARBONIZE INDUSTRIES AND DIRECT AIR CAPTURE (DAC) SYSTEMS, WHICH REMOVE CARBON DIOXIDE FROM THE ATMOSPHERE.
