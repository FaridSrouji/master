CONSTELLATION ENERGY CORPORATION DECLARES DIVIDEND. BALTIMORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CONSTELLATION ENERGY CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CEG) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.141 PER SHARE ON CONSTELLATION'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON FRIDAY, JUNE 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF 5 P.M. EASTERN TIME ON FRIDAY, MAY 13, 2022.
