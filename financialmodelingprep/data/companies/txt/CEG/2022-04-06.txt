CONSTELLATION URGES SHAREHOLDERS TO REJECT “MINI-TENDER” OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL. BALTIMORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CONSTELLATION (NASDAQ: CEG), AMERICA'S LARGEST PRODUCER OF CARBON-FREE ENERGY, IS RECOMMENDING THAT INVESTORS REJECT A “MINI-TENDER” OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT CORP. (TRC), A CANADIAN INVESTMENT FIRM. CONSTELLATION RECEIVED NOTIFICATION OF AN UNSOLICITED MINI-TENDER OFFER BY TRC DATED APRIL 1, 2022, TO PURCHASE UP TO 2 MILLION SHARES OF CONSTELLATION COMMON STOCK AT A PRICE THAT WAS 4.26 PERCENT BELOW CONSTELLATION'S COMMON STOCK SHARE PRICE ON THE NASDAQ STOCK
