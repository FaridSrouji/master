BGE HOME CHANGING NAME TO CONSTELLATION HOME. BALTIMORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CONSTELLATION (NASDAQ: CEG), AMERICA'S LEADING CLEAN ENERGY COMPANY, HAS ANNOUNCED THAT ITS MARYLAND-BASED HOME SERVICES BUSINESS, FORMERLY BGE HOME, IS CHANGING ITS NAME TO CONSTELLATION HOME. THE NAME CHANGE IS THE RESULT OF CONSTELLATION'S SEPARATION FROM EXELON ON FEB. 1, 2022. CONSTELLATION HOME WILL REMAIN HEADQUARTERED IN THE BALTIMORE AREA.
