CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE REVEALS ADDITIONAL ITINERARIES INTO 2024 RELATED TO SHIP REASSIGNMENTS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED. MIAMI, MARCH 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE CONTINUES TO IMPLEMENT ITS SHIP DEPLOYMENT CHANGES ANNOUNCED EARLIER IN FEBRUARY, WITH SHIP AND HOMEPORT ASSIGNMENTS EXTENDED INTO 2023 AND 2024, INCLUDING: CARNIVAL ELATION – JACKSONVILLE CARNIVAL ELATION, PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED AS MOVING TO JACKSONVILLE, FLA., WILL CONTINUE SERVICE OF 4- AND 5-DAY SAILINGS FORMERLY ASSIGNED TO CARNIVAL ECSTASY.
