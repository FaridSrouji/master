CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE FIRST TO RESUME SAILINGS FROM PORT OF NEW ORLEANS. NEW ORLEANS, SEPT. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARNIVAL GLORY IS SET TO DEPART ON A SEVEN-DAY CRUISE FROM THE PORT OF NEW ORLEANS THIS AFTERNOON MARKING THE FIRST SAILING FROM THE CITY SINCE THE INDUSTRY-WIDE PAUSE IN OPERATIONS 18 MONTHS AGO.
