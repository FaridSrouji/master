CARNIVAL CORPORATION ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $775 MLN, €425 MLN SECOND-PRIORITY SENIOR SECURED NOTES. CARNIVAL CORPORATION & PLC ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $775 MILLION AND €425 MILLION SECOND-PRIORITY SENIOR SECURED NOTES DUE 2026.CARNIVAL CORP - PRICED PRIVATE OFFERINGS OF $775 MILLION 10.500% SECOND-PRIORITY SENIOR SECURED NOTES DUE 2026 OF CORPORATION.CARNIVAL CORP -  PRICED EUR 425 MILLION OF 10.125% SECOND-PRIORITY SENIOR SECURED NOTES DUE 2026 OF CORPORATION.
BONHEUR SAYS SUBSIDIARIES OF CO HAVE ENTERED DEALS WITH HAL NEDERLAND FOR ACQUISITION OF TWO CRUISE VESSELS TO BE OPERATED BY FRED. ACQUISITION OF CRUISE VESSELS AND FLEET OPTIMIZATION.SUBSIDIARIES OF BONHEUR ENTERED DEALS WITH HAL NEDERLAND (PART OF CARNIVAL) FOR ACQUISITION OF TWO CRUISE VESSELS TO BE OPERATED BY FRED.DELIVERY IS EXPECTED TO TAKE PLACE IN UK DURING SEPTEMBER 2020.OLSEN CRUISE LINES' FLEET COMPOSITION WILL IMPLY BONHEUR ITSELF CONTRIBUTING APPROX. GBP 7 MILLION IN FINANCING TO FRED.BONHEUR- SELLER CREDIT TO HAVE 5 YRS TENOR WITH 3 YRS OF ZERO AMORTIZATION, SUBSEQUENT ANNUAL INSTALMENTS OF GBP 7.43 MILLION AT 2.5% FIXED INTEREST COST.
