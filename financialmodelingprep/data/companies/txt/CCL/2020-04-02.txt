CARNIVAL CORPORATION ANNOUNCES PRICING OF 62.5 MLN SHARES OF COMMON STOCK. CARNIVAL CORPORATION & PLC ANNOUNCES PRICING OF 62,500,000 SHARES OF COMMON STOCK.CARNIVAL CORP SAYS  PRICED ITS UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 62.5 MILLION SHARES OF COMMON STOCK OF CORPORATION AT A PRICE OF $8.00 PER SHARE.CARNIVAL CORP - AGGREGATE AMOUNT OF SHARES OF COMMON STOCK TO BE ISSUED WAS DECREASED TO ABOUT $500 MILLION FROM PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED $1.25 BILLION.
