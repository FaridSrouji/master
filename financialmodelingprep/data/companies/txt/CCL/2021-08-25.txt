PRINCESS CRUISES ANNOUNCES UPDATED RETURN TO CRUISE OPERATIONS TIMING FOR ISLAND PRINCESS AND DIAMOND PRINCESS. SANTA CLARITA, CALIF., AUG. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PRINCESS CRUISES, THE WORLD'S LEADING INTERNATIONAL PREMIUM CRUISE LINE IS ANNOUNCING UPDATED RESTART TIMING FOR ISLAND PRINCESS AND DIAMOND PRINCESS.
