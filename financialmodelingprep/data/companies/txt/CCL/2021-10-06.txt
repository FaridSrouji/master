CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE ANNOUNCES SHIP RESTARTS FOR JANUARY & FEBRUARY 2022. MIAMI, OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE ANNOUNCED PLANS FOR MORE SHIP RESTARTS FOR JANUARY AND FEBRUARY, AS IT WORKS TOWARDS THE RETURN OF ITS FULL FLEET SAILING FROM U.S. HOMEPORTS IN THE SPRING OF 2022.  WITH 17 SHIPS SCHEDULED TO BE OPERATING BY YEAR-END, CARNIVAL SUNSHINE WILL RESTART FROM CHARLESTON ON JAN. 13 AND CARNIVAL LIBERTY FROM PORT CANAVERAL ON FEB. 11.
