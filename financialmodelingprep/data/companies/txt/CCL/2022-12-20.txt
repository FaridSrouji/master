HOLLAND AMERICA LINE'S 2024 EUROPE SEASON INTRODUCES NEW ITINERARIES AND HOMEPORTS, LONGER CRUISES AND MORE NORTHERN CAPITALS. ROTTERDAM SAILS SEVERAL NEW NORTHERN EUROPE ITINERARIES FROM NEW HOMEPORT CITY OF ROTTERDAM, ZUIDERDAM EXPANDS ICELAND AND GREENLAND ROUTES, BERMUDA RETURNS SEATTLE , DEC. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR HOLLAND AMERICA LINE'S 2024 EUROPE SEASON, THE CRUISE LINE IS ADDING SEVERAL NEW ITINERARIES THAT COMBINE REGIONS, INCREASING THE NUMBER OF VOYAGES OF 14 DAYS OR LONGER AND ENHANCING NORTHERN EUROPE  WITH NEW DEPARTURE PORTS.
