EARLY FEEDBACK IS POSITIVE AS HOLLAND AMERICA LINE INSTALLS SPACEX'S STARLINK ON FIRST SHIP IN ALASKA. NEXT GENERATION OF INTERNET LAUNCHES ON KONINGSDAM WITH PRELIMINARY SUCCESS SEATTLE , MAY 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HOLLAND AMERICA LINE GUESTS AND CREW HAVE BEGUN USING NEXT GENERATION INTERNET WITH THE INTRODUCTION OF SPACEX'S STARLINK ON ITS PINNACLE CLASS SHIP KONINGSDAM, NOW SAILING IN ALASKA. THE TECHNOLOGY WAS INSTALLED DURING AN APRIL DRYDOCK AND HAS BEEN OPERATING FOR FOUR WEEKS.
