HOLLAND AMERICA LINE'S OOSTERDAM BACK TO CRUISING AS NINTH SHIP IN THE FLEET TO RETURN TO SERVICE. OOSTERDAM TO SAIL A FULL SEASON OF MEDITERRANEAN CRUISES BEFORE HEADING TO SOUTH AMERICA SEATTLE, MAY 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HOLLAND AMERICA LINE WELCOMED ITS NINTH SHIP BACK INTO SERVICE SUNDAY, MAY 8, AS OOSTERDAM EMBARKED GUESTS IN TRIESTE (VENICE), ITALY, FOR THE FIRST TIME SINCE THE INDUSTRYWIDE PAUSE BEGAN IN 2020 DUE TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC. THE SHIP DEPARTED ON A 12-DAY "HOLY LANDS AND ANCIENT KINGDOMS" CRUISE THAT INCLUDES AN OVERNIGHT AT HAIFA, ISRAEL, AND ADDITIONAL PORTS IN ISRAEL AND GREECE.
