PRINCESS CRUISES EXTENDS PAUSE OF CRUISE VACATIONS FROM SEATTLE THROUGH JUNE 27, 2021. SANTA CLARITA, CALIF., MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHILE PRINCESS CRUISES CONTINUES TO WORK WITH VARIOUS UNITED STATES AND CANADIAN GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS TO TRY TO PRESERVE A PORTION OF THE ALASKA 2021 CRUISE SEASON, THE COMPANY IS EXTENDING ITS PAUSE OF CRUISE VACATIONS FOR SAILINGS FROM SEATTLE THROUGH JUNE 27, 2021.
