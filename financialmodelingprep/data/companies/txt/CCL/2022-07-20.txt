CARNIVAL CORPORATION & PLC ANNOUNCES PRICING OF OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK. MIAMI , JULY 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARNIVAL CORPORATION & PLC (NYSE/LSE: CCL; NYSE: CUK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CARNIVAL CORPORATION (THE "COMPANY") HAS PRICED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 102,139,621 SHARES OF COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY AT A PUBLIC OFFERING PRICE OF $9.95 PER SHARE. THE COMPANY HAS GRANTED THE UNDERWRITER A 30-DAY OPTION TO PURCHASE UP TO 15,320,943 ADDITIONAL SHARES OF COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY.
