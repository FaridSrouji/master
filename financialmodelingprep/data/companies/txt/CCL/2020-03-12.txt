PRINCESS CRUISES ANNOUNCES A VOLUNTARY AND TEMPORARY PAUSE OF ITS GLOBAL SHIP OPERATIONS FOR 60 DAYS. PRINCESS CRUISES ANNOUNCES A VOLUNTARY AND TEMPORARY PAUSE OF ITS GLOBAL SHIP OPERATIONS FOR 60 DAYS.PRINCESS CRUISES - WILL VOLUNTARILY PAUSE GLOBAL OPERATIONS OF ITS 18 CRUISE SHIPS FOR TWO MONTHS, IMPACTING VOYAGES DEPARTING MARCH 12 TO MAY 10.PRINCESS CRUISES - CURRENT VOYAGES THAT ARE UNDERWAY AND EXTEND BEYOND MARCH 17 WILL BE ENDED AT MOST "CONVENIENT LOCATION" FOR GUESTS.PRINCESS CRUISES - PLANS TO BE BACK IN OPERATION BEGINNING WITH CRUISES DEPARTING MAY 11, 2020..PRINCESS CRUISES - WILL OFFER IMPACTED GUESTS OPPORTUNITY TO TRANSFER 100% OF MONEY PAID FOR THEIR CANCELLED CRUISE TO A FUTURE CRUISE OF THEIR CHOICE.PRINCESS CRUISES - WILL PROTECT TRAVEL ADVISOR COMMISSIONS ON BOOKINGS FOR CANCELLED CRUISES PAID IN FULL & FOR TOTAL AMOUNT OF FUTURE CRUISE CREDITS.
