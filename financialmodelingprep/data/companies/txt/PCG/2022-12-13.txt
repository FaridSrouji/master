GIVE THE GIFT OF ENERGY TO THOSE IN NEED THIS HOLIDAY SEASON. OAKLAND, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THIS HOLIDAY SEASON, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) CUSTOMERS CAN GET INTO THE GIVING SPIRIT BY MAKING A TAX-DEDUCTIBLE DONATION TO THE RELIEF FOR ENERGY ASSISTANCE THROUGH COMMUNITY HELP (REACH) PROGRAM. THE REACH PROGRAM PROVIDES FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE OF UP TO $300 FOR CUSTOMERS STRUGGLING TO PAY PAST DUE ENERGY BILLS.
