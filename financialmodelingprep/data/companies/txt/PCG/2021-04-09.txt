PG&E CONTRIBUTES $500,000 TO RESTAURANT RESILIENCE FUND TO STRENGTHEN BUSINESSES FOR THE LONG-TERM. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BEGINNING NEXT WEEK, RESTAURANTS IN SIX NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA COUNTIES CAN APPLY FOR A $3,500 GRANT TO HELP THEM REBOUND FROM THE ECONOMIC IMPACTS OF THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC. THE CALIFORNIA RESTAURANT FOUNDATION'S (CRF) RESTAURANTS CARE RESILIENCE FUND IS ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS APRIL 11-18, 2021.
