U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY CONFIRMS DIABLO CANYON POWER PLANT'S ELIGIBILITY FOR CIVIL NUCLEAR CREDIT PROGRAM FUNDING. AVILA BEACH, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TODAY, THE U. S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (DOE) CONFIRMED PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY'S (PG&E) DIABLO CANYON POWER PLANT (DCPP) IS ELIGIBLE FOR FEDERAL FUNDING THROUGH THE CIVIL NUCLEAR CREDIT PROGRAM.
