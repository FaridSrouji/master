PG&E COWORKERS ROLL UP SLEEVES WITH CALIFORNIA STATE PARKS FOUNDATION FOR EARTH DAY CLIMATE ACTION EVENTS IN APRIL. PG&E VOLUNTEERS CELEBRATE 25 YEARS OF HELPING TO PRESERVE STATE PARKS OAKLAND, CALIF. , MARCH 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN WHAT HAS BECOME A TRADITION FOR A QUARTER-CENTURY, PG&E AND THE PG&E CORPORATION FOUNDATION ARE AGAIN PARTNERING WITH THE CALIFORNIA STATE PARKS FOUNDATION TO SUPPORT EARTH DAY CLIMATE ACTION EVENTS THROUGHOUT THE MONTH OF APRIL.
