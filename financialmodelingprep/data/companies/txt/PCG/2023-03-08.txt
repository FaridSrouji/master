PG&E MOBILIZING FOR POWERFUL WINTER STORM EXPECTED TO ARRIVE IN NORTHERN CALIFORNIA THURSDAY, SPREAD TO REST OF THE STATE FRIDAY. WARM WEATHER EVENT MAY LEAD TO FLOODING OAKLAND, CALIF. , MARCH 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS MOBILIZING PERSONNEL AHEAD OF A SIGNIFICANT WINTER STORM THAT WILL ARRIVE THURSDAY AFTERNOON AND MOVE ACROSS MUCH OF NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA FRIDAY, BRINGING WITH IT SIGNIFICANT RAIN, SNOW LEVELS RISING TO 6,000 TO 8,000 FEET NORTH TO SOUTH IN THE SIERRA, AS WELL AS STRONG WIND GUSTS.
