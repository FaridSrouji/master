ADDRESSING CALIFORNIA'S DIGITAL DIVIDE: PG&E SUPPORTS OAKLAND AND STATEWIDE INITIATIVES TO BOOST TECHNOLOGY ACCESS FOR YOUNG STUDENTS. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AT A TIME WHEN ACCESS TO TECHNOLOGY AND TECH SUPPORT HAS NEVER BEEN MORE CRITICAL, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS CONTINUING ITS ONGOING COMMITMENT TO DIGITAL ACCESS IN EDUCATION. TODAY, PG&E ANNOUNCED TWO CHARITABLE CONTRIBUTIONS TOTALING $1 MILLION: FUNDING FOR #OAKLANDUNDIVIDED TO PURCHASE COMPUTERS AND INTERNET HOT SPOTS AND PROVIDE TECH SUPPORT FOR STUDENTS; AND FUNDING FOR CLOSING THE DIGITAL DIVIDE, A CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
