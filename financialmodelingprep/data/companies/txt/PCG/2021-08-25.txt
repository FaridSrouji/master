CLEANER, QUIETER RIDES TO SCHOOL COMING TO 30 SCHOOL DISTRICTS IN NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE WHEELS ON THE BUS STILL GO 'ROUND AND 'ROUND, BUT IN SEVERAL COMMUNITIES IN NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA, THEY'LL SOON BE POWERED BY CLEAN ELECTRICITY. PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS HELPING SCHOOL DISTRICTS IN ITS SERVICE AREA AS THEY TRANSITION TO ELECTRIC BUSES, SUPPORTING CLEANER AIR, LOWER MAINTENANCE COSTS AND QUIETER RIDES.
