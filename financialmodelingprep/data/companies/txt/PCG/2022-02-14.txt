PG&E TO CUSTOMERS: STAY SAFE, SECURE VALENTINE'S DAY BALLOONS WITH A WEIGHT. OAKLAND, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WITH VALENTINE'S DAY FESTIVITIES TAKING PLACE NEXT WEEK, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) REMINDS CUSTOMERS THAT NOTHING PUTS A DAMPER ON A ROMANTIC EVENING FASTER THAN A WIDESPREAD POWER OUTAGE. IF YOUR HOLIDAY CELEBRATION INCLUDES METALLIC BALLOONS, MAKE SURE THEY ARE SECURELY TIED WITH A WEIGHT TO KEEP THEM FROM FLOATING AWAY.
