PG&E OFFERING TO REMOVE WOOD DEBRIS FROM TREES CUT DOWN FOR SAFETY DURING 2021 WILDFIRES. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN RESPONSE TO CUSTOMER AND COMMUNITY FEEDBACK, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS OFFERING CUSTOMERS FREE REMOVAL OF LARGE-DIAMETER WOOD FROM TREES THAT WERE CUT DOWN FOR SAFETY DURING THE COMPANY'S 2021 WILDFIRE RESPONSE. AS PG&E WORKED TO QUICKLY AND SAFELY RESTORE POWER DURING THE 2021 WILDFIRE SEASON, CREWS INSPECTED AND CUT DOWN HAZARDOUS TREES THAT POSED A POTENTIAL SAFETY RISK TO CREWS AND ELECTRIC EQUIPMENT.
