PG&E PAYS OVER $339 MILLION IN PROPERTY TAXES TO 50 CALIFORNIA COUNTIES. SEMI-ANNUAL PAYMENTS ARE 9% HIGHER THAN PRIOR YEAR OAKLAND, CALIF. , DEC. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DURING THE SECOND HALF OF 2022, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) PAID PROPERTY TAXES OF OVER $339 MILLION TO THE 50 COUNTIES WHERE IT OWNS PROPERTIES THAT SUPPORT GAS AND ELECTRIC SERVICE TO 16 MILLION CALIFORNIANS.
