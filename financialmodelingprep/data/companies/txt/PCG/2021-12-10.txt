STRONG STORM EXPECTED TO IMPACT NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA STARTING SATURDAY AND EXTENDING INTO NEXT WEEK. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WITH METEOROLOGISTS FORECASTING AN IMPACTFUL STORM SYSTEM THIS SATURDAY AND INTO NEXT WEEK IN NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) HAS THOUSANDS OF WORKERS STANDING BY TO RESPOND TO POTENTIAL WIDESPREAD POWER OUTAGES. IN ADVANCE OF THE STORM, PG&E VEGETATION-MANAGEMENT CREWS ARE OUT TODAY, WORKING TO KEEP TREES AWAY FROM POWERLINES DURING THE FORECASTED WET AND WINDY WEATHER.
