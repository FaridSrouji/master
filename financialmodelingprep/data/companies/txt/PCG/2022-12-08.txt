PG&E PREPARED TO RESPOND TO SIGNIFICANT STORM SYSTEM EXPECTED TO IMPACT NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA THIS WEEKEND. OAKLAND, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--METEOROLOGISTS ARE FORECASTING A SIGNIFICANT WEATHER SYSTEM THIS WEEKEND IN NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA THAT COULD BRING RAIN, GUSTY WINDS, HEAVY SNOW AND THE POSSIBILITY OF THUNDERSTORMS. PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS PREPARING TO RESPOND TO POTENTIAL POWER OUTAGES AND URGES CUSTOMERS TO BE PREPARED AS WELL.
