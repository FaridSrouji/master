CREATING OUR CLEAN ENERGY FUTURE: PG&E COMMISSIONS ITS MOSS LANDING ELKHORN BATTERY. OAKLAND, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE COMMISSIONING OF ITS 182.5-MEGAWATT (MW) TESLA MEGAPACK BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM (BESS) – KNOWN AS THE ELKHORN BATTERY – LOCATED AT ITS MOSS LANDING ELECTRIC SUBSTATION IN MONTEREY COUNTY. AFTER FINAL TESTING, THE BESS WAS FULLY ENERGIZED AND CERTIFIED FOR MARKET PARTICIPATION BY THE CALIFORNIA INDEPENDENT SYSTEM OPERATOR (CAISO) ON APRIL 7, 2022.
