PG&E APPOINTS ENERGY INDUSTRY EXPERT CARLA J. PETERMAN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE AFFAIRS. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PG&E CORPORATION (NYSE: PCG; THE “CORPORATION”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CARLA J. PETERMAN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE AFFAIRS, EFFECTIVE JUNE 1, 2021. IT ALSO ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENTS OF MARGARET K. BECKER AS VICE PRESIDENT AND TREASURER OF BOTH THE CORPORATION AND PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (“UTILITY”), AND CHRISTOPHER M. PATTERSON AS VICE PRESIDENT, STATE LEGISLATIVE AFFAIRS OF THE UTILITY, BOTH EFFECTIVE MAY 1, 2021. CARLA J.
