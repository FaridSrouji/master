PG&E MOBILIZES FOR POWERFUL WINTER STORM ARRIVING IN NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA WEDNESDAY. MORE THAN 3,000 PG&E, CONTRACT AND MUTUAL-AID PERSONNEL ARE RESPONDING AFTER SIGNIFICANT NEW YEAR'S EVE STORM AND READYING FOR NEW, APPROACHING STORM SYSTEM OAKLAND, CALIF. , JAN. 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS MOBILIZING PERSONNEL AHEAD OF A SIGNIFICANT WET AND WINDY WINTER STORM DUE TO ARRIVE ACROSS NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA WEDNESDAY AND INTO THURSDAY.
