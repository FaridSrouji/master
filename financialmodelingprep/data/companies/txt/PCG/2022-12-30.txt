PG&E READY TO RESPOND TO NEW YEAR'S WEATHER SYSTEM IMPACTING NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA. AS PG&E PREPARES FOR ADVERSE WEATHER, CUSTOMERS URGED TO PLAN FOR POTENTIAL OUTAGES OAKLAND, CALIF. , DEC. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) METEOROLOGISTS ARE FORECASTING A POTENT STORM SYSTEM IMPACTING NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA OVER THE WEEKEND, WHICH WILL INCLUDE HEAVY RAINS AND GUSTY WINDS.
