PG&E AND SHASTA COUNTY DISTRICT ATTORNEY REACH SETTLEMENT TO FURTHER ENHANCE WILDFIRE SAFETY. AGREEMENT RESOLVES LEGAL PROCEEDING IN 2020 ZOGG FIRE OAKLAND, CALIF. , MAY 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT REACHED AN AGREEMENT WITH THE SHASTA COUNTY DISTRICT ATTORNEY UNDER WHICH CRIMINAL CHARGES ARISING FROM THE 2020 ZOGG FIRE WILL BE DISMISSED.
