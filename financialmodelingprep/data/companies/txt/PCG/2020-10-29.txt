PG&E CORPORATION REPORTS THIRD-QUARTER 2020 FINANCIAL RESULTS, AND 2020 AND 2021 GAAP AND NON-GAAP CORE EARNINGS AND EPS GUIDANCE. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PG&E CORPORATION (NYSE: PCG) RECORDED THIRD-QUARTER 2020 INCOME AVAILABLE FOR COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $83 MILLION, OR $0.04 PER SHARE, AS REPORTED IN ACCORDANCE WITH GENERALLY ACCEPTED ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES (GAAP). THIS COMPARES WITH LOSSES ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $1.6 BILLION, OR $3.06 PER SHARE, FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2019.
