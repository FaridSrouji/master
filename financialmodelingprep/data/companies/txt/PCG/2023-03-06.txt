STAY SAFE FROM SCAMS: PG&E PARTNERS WITH THE FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION TO HELP CUSTOMERS RECOGNIZE AND AVOID UTILITY SCAMS. NATIONAL CONSUMER PROTECTION WEEK IS MARCH 5-11 OAKLAND, CALIF. , MARCH 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS JOINING THE FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION (FTC) TO HELP CUSTOMERS RECOGNIZE AND AVOID POTENTIAL SCAMS DURING NATIONAL CONSUMER PROTECTION WEEK FROM MARCH 5-11, 2023.
