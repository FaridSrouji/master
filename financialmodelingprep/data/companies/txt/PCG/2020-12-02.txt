TO HELP FLEET CUSTOMERS MAKE THE SWITCH TO ELECTRIC VEHICLES, PG&E INTRODUCES EV FLEET SAVINGS CALCULATOR. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) ANNOUNCED A NEW ONLINE TOOL FOR COMPANIES AND ORGANIZATIONS CONSIDERING TRANSITIONING THEIR FLEETS TO ELECTRIC VEHICLES (EVS). PG&E'S NEW EV FLEET SAVINGS CALCULATOR ALLOWS BUSINESS CUSTOMERS AND PUBLIC AGENCIES WITH MEDIUM- TO HEAVY-DUTY FLEET VEHICLES TO EXPLORE EV INVESTMENT ESTIMATES BASED ON THEIR OWN FLEET SIZES, INFRASTRUCTURE, BUDGET AND OTHER FACTORS.
