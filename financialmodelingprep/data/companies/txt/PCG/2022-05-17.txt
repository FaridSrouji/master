HELP GRADUATES CELEBRATE SAFETY BY SECURING BALLOONS WITH A WEIGHT. OAKLAND, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CALIFORNIA'S GRADUATION SEASON HAS BEGUN, AND IT'S IMPORTANT THAT ALL CELEBRANTS UNDERSTAND THE PUBLIC SAFETY RISKS ASSOCIATED WITH HELIUM-FILLED METALLIC BALLOONS. IF YOUR GRADUATION CELEBRATION INVOLVES BALLOONS, MAKE SURE THEY ARE SECURED WITH A WEIGHT. UNWEIGHTED BALLOONS CAN FLOAT AWAY AND COME INTO CONTACT WITH OVERHEAD POWER LINES, CAUSING POWER OUTAGES AND A PUBLIC SAFETY RISK.
