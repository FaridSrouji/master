PG&E CORPORATION APPOINTS CAROLYN BURKE AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. MS. BURKE BRINGS DECADES OF FINANCIAL ACUMEN AND TURNAROUND EXPERIENCE TO PG&E OAKLAND, CALIF. , MARCH 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PG&E CORPORATION (NYSE: PCG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CAROLYN BURKE, A FINANCE AND OPERATIONS EXECUTIVE WITH EXTENSIVE LEADERSHIP AND TURNAROUND EXPERIENCE IN FORTUNE 500 ENERGY AND FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANIES, WILL ASSUME THE ROLE OF EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE MAY 4, 2023.
