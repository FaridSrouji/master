PG&E CONTINUES TO MAKE PROGRESS AS HUNDREDS OF THOUSANDS OF CUSTOMERS HAVE BEEN RESTORED AFTER SEVERE WINDSTORM AND PSPS EVENT. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) CREWS CONTINUE TO PATROL AND INSPECT POWERLINES, AND MAKE ANY NECESSARY REPAIRS, AFTER A STRONG WINDSTORM INTERRUPTED ELECTRIC SERVICE FOR MORE THAN 371,000 CUSTOMERS ACROSS CENTRAL AND NORTHERN CALIFORNIA THIS WEEK. AS OF EARLY THURSDAY MORNING, MORE THAN 93 PERCENT OF CUSTOMERS WHO LOST POWER DUE TO THE EXTREMELY HIGH WINDS HAVE BEEN RESTORED.
