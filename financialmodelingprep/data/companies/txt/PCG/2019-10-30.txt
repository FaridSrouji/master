PG&E SAYS RESTORED POWER TO 328,255 CUSTOMERS AFFECTED BY OCT. 29 PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFF. OCT 31  - PG&E CORP <PCG.N>::11 A.M. UPDATE: PG&E HAS RESTORED POWER TO 328,255 CUSTOMERS AFFECTED BY THE OCT. 29 PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFF.PG&E CORP - CURRENTLY, 36,745 TOTAL PSPS-IMPACTED CUSTOMERS REMAIN WITHOUT POWER.PG&E CORP - CREWS WORKING TO RESTORE POWER, GAS SERVICE TO CUSTOMERS WHO CAN RECEIVE IT AFFECTED BY KINCADE FIRE.PG&E CORP - 143 DAMAGE/HAZARD INCIDENTS IDENTIFIED.PG&E CORP - 143 INCIDENTS OF DAMAGE OR OTHER HAZARDS TO ELECTRIC SYSTEM HAVE BEEN IDENTIFIED AND NUMBER IS EXPECTED TO INCREASE.PG&E CORP - 1,857 CUSTOMERS ARE WITHOUT ELECTRIC SERVICE IN KINCADE FIRE AREA.PG&E CORP - PG&E PROACTIVELY TURNED OFF NATURAL GAS SERVICE FOR SAFETY TO APPROXIMATELY 25,000 CUSTOMERS LOCATED WITHIN KINCADE FIRE AREAS.PG&E CORP - DAMAGES OR OTHER HAZARDS TO ELECTRIC SYSTEM COULD LEAD TO WILDFIRE IGNITIONS IF POWER LINES ARE NOT DE-ENERGIZED..
