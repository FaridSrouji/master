PG&E MOBILIZING FOR POWERFUL WINTER STORM ARRIVING IN NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA TODAY. HIGH WINDS, RAIN AND LOW-ELEVATION SNOW FORECASTED OAKLAND, CALIF. , FEB. 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS MOBILIZING PERSONNEL AHEAD OF A SIGNIFICANT COLD AND WINDY WINTER STORM THAT WILL ARRIVE IN COASTAL AREAS THIS AFTERNOON AND MOVE ACROSS MUCH OF NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA WEDNESDAY AND INTO THURSDAY, BRINGING WITH IT SNOW FALL AS LOW AS 500 TO 1,000 FEET IN SOME AREAS.
