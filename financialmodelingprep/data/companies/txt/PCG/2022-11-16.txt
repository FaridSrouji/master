GOBBLE UP ENERGY SAVINGS TO HELP REDUCE HIGHER HOLIDAY ENERGY BILLS. OAKLAND, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE HOLIDAY SEASON IS UPON US. WITH A DROP IN TEMPERATURES, SHORTER DAYS AND LONGER NIGHTS, GUESTS STAYING OVER, AND APPLIANCES OPERATING ON OVERDRIVE THE THANKSGIVING HOLIDAY CAN LEAD TO AN INCREASE IN ENERGY USE.
