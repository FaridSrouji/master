SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) CREWS ARE RESPONDING TO LOCAL OUTAGES FROM A POWERFUL STORM NOW MOVING THROUGH NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA. PG&E HAS NEARLY 400 POWER RESTORATION CREWS PRE-STAGED, INCLUDING 278 TROUBLEMEN, WHO ARE THE ENERGY COMPANY'S FIRST RESPONDERS TO AN OUTAGE.
