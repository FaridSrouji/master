PG&E EXPECTS TO PROVIDE LONG-TERM SAVINGS TO CUSTOMERS FROM REDUCED REAL ESTATE COSTS AND GAIN ON SALE OF SAN FRANCISCO COMPLEX. PRESS RELEASE - AS PART OF COMMITMENT TO REIMAGINING PG&E FOR THE FUTURE, COMPANY PLANS TO RELOCATE HEADQUARTERS TO OAKLAND AND WILL SEEK TO SELL SAN FRANCISCO HEADQUARTERS COMPLEX.PG&E EXPECTS TO PROVIDE LONG-TERM SAVINGS TO CUSTOMERS FROM REDUCED REAL ESTATE COSTS AND GAIN ON SALE OF SAN FRANCISCO COMPLEX.HEADQUARTERS MOVE WILL BE PHASED OVER PERIOD OF YEARS.EXPECTS MOVE TO ACHIEVE NEAR-TERM COST SAVINGS AND RESULT IN SUBSTANTIALLY LOWER HEADQUARTERS COSTS FOR PG&E OVER A LONG-TERM PERIOD.PG&E EXPECTS TO LAUNCH A COMPETITIVE MARKET PROCESS TO SELL COMPANY'S SAN FRANCISCO HEADQUARTERS OFFICE COMPLEX.PG&E - WILL REQUEST APPROVAL FROM CPUC TO RETURN NET GAIN REALIZED UPON FUTURE SALE OF ITS SAN FRANCISCO HEADQUARTERS TO ITS CUSTOMERS.PG&E - SAN FRANCISCO HEADQUARTERS OFFICE COMPLEX SALE IS EXPECTED TO OCCUR AFTER COMPANY EMERGES FROM CHAPTER 11.
PG&E ANNOUNCES $3.25 BLN COMMON STOCK INVESTMENT FROM MULTIPLE INVESTORS. PG&E CORPORATION ANNOUNCES $3.25 BILLION COMMON STOCK INVESTMENT FROM MULTIPLE INVESTORS.PG&E - ENTERED INVESTMENT AGREEMENT WITH NUMBER OF INVESTORS, INCLUDING AFFILIATES OF APPALOOSA, THIRD POINT LLC, ZIMMER PARTNERS.PG&E CORP - ENTERED INVESTMENT AGREEMENT WITH A SELECT NUMBER OF INVESTORS, INCLUDING AFFILIATES OF FIDELITY MANAGEMENT & RESEARCH COMPANY, LLC.PG&E CORP - INVESTORS WILL PAY UP TO $10.50 PER SHARE, DEPENDING ON PRICE PER SHARE TO BE PAID IN AN EXPECTED PUBLIC OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK.PG&E CORP - SHARES PURCHASED IN PRIVATE PLACEMENT WILL BE SUBJECT TO A 90-DAY LOCK-UP, SUBJECT TO CERTAIN EXCEPTIONS.PG&E CORP - PROCEEDS FROM PRIVATE PLACEMENT WILL BE USED TO FUND A PORTION OF PG&E'S EXIT FINANCING.PG&E - INVESTORS MAY TERMINATE INVESTMENT AGREEMENT IF EQUITY BACKSTOP COMMITMENTS, ARE DRAWN/IF PG&E DOES NOT EMERGE FROM CHAPTER 11 WITHIN 45 DAYS.PG&E - HAS GOT CONSENTS NEEDED TO AMEND EQUITY BACKSTOP COMMITMENT LETTERS TO ELIMINATE PREVIOUSLY EXISTING TIER STRUCTURE.PG&E - BY ENTERING INTO PRIVATE PLACEMENT AND RFSPA, PG&E HAS PREARRANGED FOR ALMOST $3.8 BILLION OF $9.0 BILLION OF EQUITY REQUIRED FOR EMERGENCE.PG&E - PARTIES TO BACKSTOP COMMITMENT LETTERS HAVE COMMITTED TO ENTER INTO A REDEEMABLE FORWARD STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR $523 MILLION.PG&E CORP - TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON EFFECTIVE DATE OF PG&E'S EMERGENCE FROM CHAPTER 11.
