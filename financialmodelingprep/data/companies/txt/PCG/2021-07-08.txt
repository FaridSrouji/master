STATE'S GRID OPERATOR ASKS ELECTRIC CUSTOMERS ACROSS CALIFORNIA TO CONSERVE POWER FRIDAY AS HEAT WAVE SET TO INCREASE ENERGY DEMAND ACROSS THE WEST. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WITH EXTREME TEMPERATURES FORECAST ACROSS MUCH OF CALIFORNIA ON FRIDAY (JULY 9), THE STATE'S GRID OPERATOR IS ASKING RESIDENTS TO VOLUNTARILY CONSERVE ELECTRICITY TOMORROW AFTERNOON AND EVENING TO HELP EASE THE STRAIN ON THE GRID DURING CRUCIAL EVENING HOURS WHEN SOLAR ENERGY IS DIMINISHED OR NO LONGER AVAILABLE. THE FLEX ALERT, CALLED BY THE CALIFORNIA INDEPENDENT SYSTEM OPERATOR (CAISO), WAS ISSUED THURSDAY AND WILL BE IN EFFECT ON FRIDAY FROM 4 P.M. TO 9 P.M.
