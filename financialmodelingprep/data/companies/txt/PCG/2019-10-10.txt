PG&E DECLINES SAN FRANCISCO'S INTEREST IN BUYING SOME FACILITIES. OCT 11  - PG&E CORP <PCG.N>::PG&E ISSUES LETTER TO SAN FRANCISCO'S MAYOR BREED.PG&E TO SAN FRANCISCO'S MAYOR BREED SAYS DECLINES TO ACCEPT SAN FRANCISCO'S INTEREST IN PURCHASING SOME PG&E ELECTRIC FACILITIES.PG&E TO SAN FRANCISCO'S MAYOR BREED SAYS SAN FRANCISCO-BASED FACILITIES ARE NOT FOR SALE.PG&E CORP - FINANCING STRATEGY TO EMERGE FROM BANKRUPTCY DOES NOT ENVISION SELLING OFF COMPANY ASSETS.PG&E SAYS BELIEVE CO CAN FAIRLY RESOLVE, FUND ALL CLAIMS, OTHER ITEMS THROUGH CONVENTIONAL FINANCIAL MARKETS.PG&E CORP -  SAN FRANCISCO'S OFFER REFLECTS AN AMOUNT SIGNIFICANTLY BELOW FAIR-MARKET VALUE OF UNDERLYING PG&E ASSETS.PG&E SAYS SAN FRANCISCO'S OFFER APPEARS TO UNDERESTIMATE SEPARATION, IMPAIRMENT COSTS THAT THE CITY WOULD HAVE TO BEAR AS PART OF PROPOSED DEAL.
