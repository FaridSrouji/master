PG&E HAS TURNED OFF POWER FOR SAFETY IN PORTIONS OF 15 COUNTIES IN SIERRA FOOTHILLS AND NORTH BAY. OCT 23  - PG&E CORP <PCG.N>::PG&E HAS TURNED OFF POWER FOR SAFETY IN PORTIONS OF 15 COUNTIES IN SIERRA FOOTHILLS AND NORTH BAY.PG&E CORP - POWER SHUTOFFS ALSO EXPECTED TO IMPACT ABOUT 1,000 CUSTOMERS IN SAN MATEO AND KERN COUNTIES AROUND 1:00 A.M. THURSDAY.PG&E CORP - PG&E'S EMERGENCY OPERATIONS CENTER IS MONITORING AN ADDITIONAL DRY, OFFSHORE WIND EVENT THIS WEEKEND.PG&E CORP - IN TOTAL, CURRENT PSPS IS EXPECTED TO IMPACT APPROXIMATELY 179,000 CUSTOMERS IN 17 COUNTIES.PG&E - IT IS POSSIBLE THAT ADDITIONAL CUSTOMERS NOT IMPACTED BY PSPS MAY EXPERIENCE POWER OUTAGES DUE TO PG&E EQUIPMENT DAMAGED DURING WIND EVENT.
