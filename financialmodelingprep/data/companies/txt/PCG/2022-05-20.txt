COMMITTED TO CUSTOMER SAFETY: PG&E PROVIDES NEW SAFETY INFORMATION ABOUT DOWNED POWERLINES IN EMERGENCY SITUATIONS. OAKLAND, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT SAFETY TIPS SHARED BY PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS TO TREAT ALL DOWNED POWERLINES AS IF THEY ARE ENERGIZED AND KEEP AWAY. CIRCUMSTANCES, HOWEVER, CAN BECOME MORE COMPLICATED IF A POWERLINE IS IN THE PATH OF AN EVACUATION ROUTE. “IT'S VERY UNUSUAL FOR A POWERLINE TO MAKE CONTACT WITH A VEHICLE, BUT SHOULD THAT OCCUR, WE WANT OUR CUSTOMERS TO BE PREPARED WITH THE KNOWLEDGE THAT CAN SAVE THEIR LIVES,” SAID FRANK FRAONE,
