PG&E LAUNCHES EV FAST CHARGE PROGRAM TO HELP ACCELERATE ELECTRIC VEHICLE ADOPTION IN CALIFORNIA. DRIVERS CAN CHARGE THEIR VEHICLES AT THE FOUR NEW ADVANCED 125KW-CAPABLE EV FAST CHARGERS.
