PG&E BEGINS ISSUING WEATHER 'ALL CLEAR' IN SOME LOCATIONS FOLLOWING PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFF. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) CREWS ARE PATROLLING LINES DE-ENERGIZED DURING TUESDAY'S PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFF (PSPS), AFTER COMPANY METEOROLOGISTS BEGAN ISSUING THE WEATHER “ALL-CLEAR” THIS MORNING FOR PORTIONS OF AFFECTED AREAS IMPACTED BY SAFETY SHUTOFFS.
