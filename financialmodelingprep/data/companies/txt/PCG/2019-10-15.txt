PG&E CORP - DAMAGE AND HAZARDS FOUND AFTER SAFETY SHUTOFF CONFIRM TURNING OFF POWER WAS RIGHT DECISION. OCT 16  - PG&E CORP <PCG.N>::INTERIM UPDATE (1): DAMAGE AND HAZARDS FOUND AFTER SAFETY SHUTOFF CONFIRM THAT TURNING OFF POWER WAS THE RIGHT DECISION.PRELIMINARY REVIEW CONFIRMS MORE THAN 100 INCIDENTS OF DAMAGE AND HAZARD LOCATIONS.POSSIBLE THAT DAMAGES AND HAZARDS REPRESENTED POTENTIAL SOURCES OF WILDFIRE IGNITION.CONTINUE TO REVIEW REPORTS FROM SAFETY INSPECTIONS OF 25,000 MILES OF DISTRIBUTION LINES AND 2,500 MILES OF TRANSMISSION LINES.
CALIFORNIA GOVERNOR - DEMANDS PG&E ACCOUNTABILITY FOR MISMANAGED PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFFS. OCT 14  - PG&E CORP <PCG.N>::CALIFORNIA GOVERNOR GAVIN NEWSOM - DEMANDS PG&E ACCOUNTABILITY FOR MISMANAGED PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFFS.CALIFORNIA GOVERNOR NEWSOM - URGED PG&E TO PROVIDE POWER SHUTOFF AFFECTED CUSTOMERS AN AUTOMATIC CREDIT OR REBATE OF $100/RESIDENTIAL CUSTOMER.CALIFORNIA GOVERNOR NEWSOM - URGED PG&E TO GIVE POWER SHUTOFF AFFECTED CUSTOMERS AUTOMATIC CREDIT OR REBATE OF $250/SMALL BUSINESS.CALIFORNIA GOVERNOR NEWSOM - ASKS CPUC TO CONDUCT COMPREHENSIVE INQUIRY, REVIEW INTO PG&E’S ACTIONS RELATED TO POWER SHUTOFFS.
PG&E SAYS THAT ALL CUSTOMERS IMPACTED BY SAFETY SHUTOFFS HAVE NOW BEEN RESTORED. OCT 12  - PG&E CORP <PCG.N>::PG&E CORP SAYS IN PSPS UPDATE THAT ALL CUSTOMERS IMPACTED BY SAFETY SHUTOFFS HAVE NOW BEEN RESTORED.FINAL 2,500 CUSTOMERS WITHOUT POWER ENERGIZED SATURDAY AFTERNOON.PG&E WILL SUBMIT A REPORT DETAILING DAMAGES TO CPUC WITHIN 10 DAYS OF COMPLETION OF PSPS.
