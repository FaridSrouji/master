TO HELP COMBAT FOOD INSECURITY IN NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA, THE PG&E CORPORATION FOUNDATION CONTRIBUTES $750,000 TO LOCAL FOOD BANKS. OAKLAND, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PG&E CORPORATION FOUNDATION (THE FOUNDATION) IS PROVIDING $750,000 TO LOCAL FOOD BANKS TO HELP FEED INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES STRUGGLING WITH FOOD INSECURITY. THE CONTRIBUTION IS EQUAL TO APPROXIMATELY 4.5 MILLION POUNDS OF FOOD, OR 3.75 MILLION MEALS, FOR INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES IN NEED.
