MOST CUSTOMERS WHO LOST POWER DUE TO WINTER STORM HAVE BEEN RESTORED. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) CREWS TODAY CONTINUE TO ASSESS DAMAGE, MAKE REPAIRS AND RESTORE SERVICE TO CUSTOMERS AFTER THE FIRST OF TWO WINTER STORMS BATTERED MUCH OF NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA SUNDAY AND MONDAY. AS OF 2 P.M. TODAY, 90 PERCENT OF THE 247,000 CUSTOMERS WHO LOST POWER HAVE BEEN RESTORED.
