PSPS UPDATE: PG&E BEGINS SHUTTING OFF POWER FOR SAFETY DURING OFFSHORE WIND EVENT, AFFECTING ABOUT 51,000 CUSTOMERS IN SMALL PARTS OF 18 COUNTIES. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) CONFIRMS THAT IT IS IN THE PROCESS OF IMPLEMENTING A PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFF (PSPS) AFFECTING ABOUT 51,000 CUSTOMERS IN SMALL PORTIONS OF 18 COUNTIES FOCUSED IN THE SIERRA NEVADA FOOTHILLS, THE NORTH COAST, THE NORTH VALLEY AND THE NORTH BAY MOUNTAINS.
