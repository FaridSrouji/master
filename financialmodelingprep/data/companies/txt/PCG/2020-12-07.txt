PSPS UPDATE: BASED ON MORE FAVORABLE WEATHER CONDITIONS, PG&E CANCELS PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFF ANTICIPATED FOR TODAY IN PORTIONS OF FIVE COUNTIES. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BASED ON MORE FAVORABLE WEATHER CONDITIONS, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) WILL BE NOTIFYING CUSTOMERS THIS MORNING THAT IT WILL NOT BE INITIATING A PUBLIC SAFETY POWER SHUTOFF (PSPS) TODAY. PG&E ANTICIPATED THE NEED TO TURN OFF POWER OVERNIGHT TO APPROXIMATELY 8,500 CUSTOMERS IN PORTIONS OF FRESNO, MADERA, MARIPOSA, TULARE AND TUOLUMNE COUNTIES.
