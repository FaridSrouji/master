PG&E CONTINUES 2022 WILDFIRE MITIGATION WORK: VEGETATION MANAGEMENT EFFORTS UNDERWAY IN AREAS AT HIGHEST RISK OF WILDFIRES. OAKLAND, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) VEGETATION MANAGEMENT CREWS AND CONTRACTORS ACROSS NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA COMMUNITIES ARE CONTINUING WORK TO MAINTAIN SAFE AND RELIABLE POWERLINES BY PRUNING AND CUTTING DOWN TREES IN HIGH FIRE-THREAT AREAS. PG&E TEAMS ARE WORKING CLOSELY WITH CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES IN 2022 TO COMPLETE THIS IMPORTANT SAFETY WORK ACROSS 1,800 MILES OF OVERHEAD POWERLINES. PG&E'S ENHANCED VEGETATION MANAGEMENT (EVM)
