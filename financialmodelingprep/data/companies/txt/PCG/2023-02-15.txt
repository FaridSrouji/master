PG&E CUSTOMERS TO RECEIVE OVER $55 MILLION IN BILL CREDITS THROUGH NEW INCENTIVE PROGRAM. PARTICIPATING CUSTOMERS REWARDED FOR REDUCING ENERGY USE AND STRAIN ON THE GRID LAST SUMMER OAKLAND, CALIF. , FEB. 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AT A TIME WHEN HIGHER NATURAL GAS PRICES ARE DRIVING UP ENERGY COSTS, THE NEW POWER SAVER REWARDS PROGRAM IS PROVIDING OVER $55 MILLION IN BILL CREDITS TO PARTICIPATING PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) CUSTOMERS.
