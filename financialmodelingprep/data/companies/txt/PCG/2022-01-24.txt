THE NEXT GIANT LEAP FOR ELECTRIC SYSTEM RELIABILITY: PG&E PROPOSES NEARLY 1,600 MW OF NEW BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE CAPACITY. OAKLAND, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS PART OF ITS MISSION TO BUILD A STRONGER, MORE RESILIENT ENERGY GRID FOR THE HOMETOWNS IT SERVES, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS PROPOSING NINE NEW BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE PROJECTS TOTALING APPROXIMATELY 1,600 MEGAWATTS (MW), TO FURTHER INTEGRATE RENEWABLE ENERGY RESOURCES AND IMPROVE RELIABILITY OF THE CALIFORNIA ELECTRIC SYSTEM.
