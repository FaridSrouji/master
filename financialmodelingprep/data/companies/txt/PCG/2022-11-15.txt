ON UTILITY SCAM AWARENESS DAY, PG&E WANTS TO HELP CUSTOMERS RECOGNIZE AND AVOID UTILITY SCAMS. OAKLAND, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AS THE NUMBER OF SCAMS TARGETING UTILITY CUSTOMERS CONTINUE AT AN ALARMING RATE, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) IS JOINING UTILITIES UNITED AGAINST SCAMS TO HELP CUSTOMERS RECOGNIZE POTENTIAL SCAMS AS PART OF UTILITY SCAM AWARENESS DAY ON NOVEMBER 16.
