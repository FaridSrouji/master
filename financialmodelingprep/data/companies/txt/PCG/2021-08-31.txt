PG&E'S PROPOSAL TO RETURN $400 MILLION TO CUSTOMERS FROM NET GAIN ON SALE OF SAN FRANCISCO HEADQUARTERS APPROVED BY CALIFORNIA PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION. SAN FRANCISCO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE CALIFORNIA PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION (CPUC) HAS APPROVED THE SALE OF THE SAN FRANCISCO HEADQUARTERS COMPLEX OF PG&E CORPORATION AND PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (TOGETHER, “PG&E” OR THE “COMPANY”) TO A HINES COMPANY. THE CPUC'S FINAL DECISION APPROVES PG&E'S REQUEST TO RETURN AN ESTIMATED $400 MILLION NET GAIN TO CUSTOMERS OVER A FIVE-YEAR PERIOD.
