WITH TRIPLE-DIGIT HEAT EXPECTED THIS WEEK IN MANY LOCATIONS ACROSS CALIFORNIA, PG&E OFFERS ENERGY SAVING TIPS TO REDUCE IMPACT OF HOT WEATHER ON POWER BILLS. OAKLAND, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS THE STATE PREPARES FOR A HEAT WAVE THAT COULD SEND TEMPERATURES SOARING IN PARTS OF NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA THIS WEEK, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS TO TAKE STEPS TO CONSERVE ENERGY TO AVOID BILL SURPRISES AS THE MERCURY RISES. ACCORDING TO PG&E'S IN-HOUSE METEOROLOGISTS, TEMPERATURES ARE FORECAST TO BEGIN CLIMBING TODAY AND RESULT IN WIDESPREAD TRIPLE-DIGIT HEAT ACROSS THE INTERIOR VALLEYS FRIDAY AND SATURDAY.
