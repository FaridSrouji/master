PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC SAYS SED OF CPUC, AND CUE JOINTLY SUBMITTED TO CPUC A PROPOSED SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT. OCT 4  - PG&E CORP <PCG.N>::PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC - SED OF CPUC, AND CUE JOINTLY SUBMITTED TO CPUC A PROPOSED SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT.PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC CO - PURSUANT TO SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT, UTILITY AGREED TO A TOTAL FINANCIAL REMEDY OF $65 MILLION.PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC CO - FINANCIAL REMEDY CONSISTS OF A FINE OF $5 MILLION TO BE PAID TO GENERAL FUND OF STATE OF CALIFORNIA.PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC CO - FINANCIAL REMEDY ALSO CONSISTS OF $60 MILLION IN SHAREHOLDER-FUNDED SYSTEM ENHANCEMENT INITIATIVES.PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC - SUBMITTED PROPOSED SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT RELATING TO ORDER INSTITUTING INVESTIGATION, SHOW CAUSE INTO CO'S LOCATE, MARK PRACTICES.PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC - UTILITY EXPECTS THAT SYSTEM ENHANCEMENT SPENDING PURSUANT TO SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT WILL OCCUR THROUGH 2022.PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC - PARTIES WHO HAVE NOT JOINED SETTLEMENT ARE PUBLIC ADVOCATES OFFICE OF CPUC, UTILITY REFORM NETWORK.PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC - PARTIES WHO HAVE NOT JOINED SETTLEMENT ARE CPUC'S OFFICE OF SAFETY ADVOCATE, AND CITY AND COUNTY OF SAN FRANCISCO.PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC - SYSTEM ENHANCEMENTS WILL INCLUDE LOCATE AND MARK TICKET COMPLIANCE AUDITS TO VERIFY ACCURATE CATEGORIZATION OF TIMELINESS.PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC - SYSTEM ENHANCEMENTS INCLUDES COMPLIANCE AUDITS USING FIELD REVIEWS OF GAS & ELECTRIC LOCATE, MARK TICKETS TO ASSESS COMPLIANCE.
