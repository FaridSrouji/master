A CLEANER CALIFORNIA: PG&E TEAMS UP WITH CALIFORNIA UNIVERSITIES TO PROPOSE CLEAN ENERGY PILOT PROGRAM. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) ANNOUNCED IT PROPOSED A NEW PILOT TO HELP CALIFORNIA UNIVERSITIES REDUCE GREENHOUSE-GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS IN SUPPORT OF THE STATE'S BOLD CLIMATE GOALS. IF APPROVED BY THE CALIFORNIA PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION (CPUC), PG&E WOULD TEAM UP WITH THE UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA (UC) AND CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY (CSU) SYSTEMS TO INTRODUCE A CLEAN ENERGY OPTIMIZATION PILOT (CEOP) TO CAMPUSES ACROSS NORTHERN AND CENTRA
