MOST CUSTOMERS RESTORED FOLLOWING THIS WEEK'S TWO MAJOR WINTER STORMS. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) HAS RESTORED MOST OF THE CUSTOMERS WHO LOST ELECTRICITY FOLLOWING TWO POWERFUL WINTER STORMS THAT HIT NORTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA JUST DAYS APART THIS WEEK, AND CAUSED POWER OUTAGES TO NEARLY 490,000 CUSTOMERS. THESE STORMS BATTERED THE COMPANY'S SERVICE AREA WITH SEVERAL DAYS OF STRONG WIND GUSTS, HEAVY RAIN AND DEEP SNOW ACCUMULATION.
