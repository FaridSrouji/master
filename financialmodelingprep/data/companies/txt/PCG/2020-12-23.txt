PG&E PROPOSES EXPANDING ITS BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE PORTFOLIO TO IMPROVE ELECTRIC RELIABILITY, FURTHER INTEGRATE RENEWABLE ENERGY. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) HAS REQUESTED CALIFORNIA PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION (CPUC) APPROVAL OF SIX ADDITIONAL BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE PROJECTS TOTALING 387 MEGAWATTS (MW) OF CAPACITY, INTENDED TO FURTHER INTEGRATE CLEAN ENERGY FROM RENEWABLE GENERATION SOURCES WHILE HELPING TO ENSURE FUTURE RELIABILITY OF THE ELECTRIC SYSTEM.
