PG&E JOINS WITH AEMETIS, INC. TO OFFICIALLY OPEN NEW RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS INTERCONNECTION. OAKLAND, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) TODAY JOINED WITH AEMETIS, INC. TO OFFICIALLY COMMISSION THE INTERCONNECTION OF A NEW RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS (RNG) FACILITY IN KEYES, CALIF.
