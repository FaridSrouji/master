KEEPING CUSTOMERS SAFE: PG&E WANTS TO HELP CUSTOMERS RECOGNIZE UTILITY SCAMS AND AVOID FALLING VICTIM. SCAM REPORTS AT AN ALL-TIME HIGH DURING THE FIRST FOUR MONTHS OF 2023 OAKLAND, CALIF. , MAY 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH REPORTS OF SCAMS TARGETING UTILITY CUSTOMERS AT AN ALL-TIME HIGH, PACIFIC GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (PG&E) SHARES TIPS WITH CUSTOMERS TO HELP RECOGNIZE THE SIGNS OF A POTENTIAL SCAM.
