THE INTERNATIONAL GAUCHER ALLIANCE AND CERNER ENVIZA ENROLL FIRST PATIENT IN GARDIAN REGISTRY. KANSAS CITY, MO. , APRIL 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE INTERNATIONAL GAUCHER ALLIANCE (IGA) AND CERNER CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CERN) ANNOUNCE THAT THE GARDIAN REGISTRY (GAUCHER REGISTRY FOR DEVELOPMENT INNOVATION AND ANALYSIS OF NEURONOPATHIC DISEASE) IS NOW OPEN FOR REGISTRATIONS, AND THE FIRST PATIENT HAS ALREADY ENROLLED APRIL 25, 2022.
