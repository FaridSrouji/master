CERNER TO RELEASE SECOND QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS RESULTS JULY 30. KANSAS CITY, MO., JULY 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CERNER CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CERN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL RELEASE ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS RESULTS BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS FRIDAY, JULY 30. FOLLOWING THE RELEASE, THE COMPANY WILL HOLD A CONFERENCE CALL AT 8 A.M. CT THAT CAN BE ACCESSED VIA DIAL-IN OR WEBCAST. ON THE CALL, CERNER WILL DISCUSS ITS RESULTS AND OUTLOOK AND ANSWER QUESTIONS FROM THE INVESTMENT COMMUNITY. THE CALL MAY ALSO INCLUDE DISCUSSION OF CERNER DEVELOPMENTS AND OTHER MATERIAL AND FORWARD-LOOKING INFORMATION ABOUT BUSINESS AND FINANCIAL MATTERS.
