CERNER ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. KANSAS CITY, MO., SEPT. 10, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CERNER CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CERN), A GLOBAL HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND TO STOCKHOLDERS OF $0.22 PER ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING SHARE. THE CASH DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 12, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 27, 2021.
