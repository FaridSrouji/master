CERNER COLLABORATION TO INCREASE ACCESS TO CANCER CLINICAL TRIALS. THE COMPANY'S LEARNING HEALTH NETWORK T EAMS WITH ELLIGO HEALTH RESEARCH AND FREENOME TO ADVANCE EARLY CANCER DETECTION KANSAS CITY, MO. , MAY 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CERNER CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CERN), ELLIGO HEALTH RESEARCH®  AND FREENOME ARE COLLABORATING TO ENABLE A CLINICAL TRIAL THROUGH THE LEARNING HEALTH NETWORK TO HELP ADVANCE EARLY CANCER DETECTION.
