CERNER ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. KANSAS CITY, MO., MARCH 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CERNER CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CERN), A GLOBAL HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE "BOARD") DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND TO STOCKHOLDERS OF $0.27 PER ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING SHARE.
