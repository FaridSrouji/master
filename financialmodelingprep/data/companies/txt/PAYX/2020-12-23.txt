PAYCHEX, INC. REPORTS SECOND QUARTER RESULTS. ROCHESTER, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PAYCHEX, INC. (THE “COMPANY,” “PAYCHEX,” “WE,” “OUR,” OR “US”) (NASDAQ:PAYX) TODAY ANNOUNCED SERVICE REVENUE WAS $968.9 MILLION FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED NOVEMBER 30, 2020 (THE “SECOND QUARTER”), CONSISTENT WITH THE PRIOR YEAR PERIOD. TOTAL REVENUE DECREASED 1% FROM THE PRIOR YEAR PERIOD TO $983.7 MILLION FOR THE SECOND QUARTER.
