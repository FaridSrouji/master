PAYCHEX NAMES ELIZABETH ROALDSEN AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF OPERATIONS AND CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE. VETERAN FINANCIAL INDUSTRY EXECUTIVE TO LEAD THE COMPANY'S CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE ROCHESTER, N.Y. , MAY 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PAYCHEX, INC., A LEADING PROVIDER OF INTEGRATED HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS FOR HUMAN RESOURCES, PAYROLL, BENEFITS, AND INSURANCE SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ELIZABETH ROALDSEN HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF OPERATIONS AND CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE.
