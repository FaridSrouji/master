PAYCHEX ADDS SUPPORT FOR VACCINATION STATUS TRACKING TO ITS COVID-19 TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS. ROCHESTER, N.Y., OCT. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PAYCHEX, INC., A LEADING PROVIDER OF INTEGRATED HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM) SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS FOR HUMAN RESOURCES, PAYROLL, BENEFITS, AND INSURANCE SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BUILT FUNCTIONALITY TO ASSIST BUSINESSES WITH VACCINATION STATUS TRACKING OF THEIR EMPLOYEES.
