GEN Z SURPASSES BABY BOOMERS IN SMALL BUSINESS WORKFORCE PARTICIPATION. PAYCHEX SPECIAL REPORT INVESTIGATES GEN Z'S GROWING IMPACT ON WORKFORCE IN THE U.S. ROCHESTER, N.Y. , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ACCORDING TO A NEW REPORT FROM PAYCHEX, INC., GENERATION Z (BORN 1997-2012) PARTICIPATION IN THE SMALL BUSINESS WORKFORCE HAS SURPASSED BABY BOOMERS (BORN 1946-1964) AND IS THE ONLY SEGMENT IN THE LABOR MARKET SHOWING GROWTH.
