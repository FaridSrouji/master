SMALL BUSINESS HIRING AND WAGE GROWTH STRENGTH CONTINUES; GROWTH RATES MODERATE SLIGHTLY FROM PREVIOUS MONTH. ROCHESTER, N.Y. , JULY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SMALL BUSINESSES CONTINUED TO ADD JOBS IN JUNE AND WORKER WAGES CONTINUED TO GROW AT A STRONG RATE, BUT THE PACE OF GROWTH MODERATED SLIGHTLY FROM PREVIOUS MONTH.
