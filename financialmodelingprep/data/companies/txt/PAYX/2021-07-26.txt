TALENT RECRUITMENT, WORKFORCE DIVERSITY, AND EMPLOYEE BENEFITS LEAD THE CHALLENGES FOR BUSINESSES EMERGING FROM COVID-19. ROCHESTER, N.Y., JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE 2021 PAYCHEX PULSE OF HR REPORT, RELEASED TODAY, SHOWS THAT ATTRACTING TALENT TOPS THE LIST OF CHALLENGES IMPACTING HR PROFESSIONALS AS THEY ADAPT TO THE EVOLVING WORKPLACE SHAPED BY THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
