PAYCHEX RECOGNIZED FOR STRENGTH OF TECHNOLOGY AND SERVICE DURING COVID-19 WITH HR TECH AWARD. ROCHESTER, N.Y., JUNE 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PAYCHEX, INC., THE HR SOFTWARE AND SERVICES COMPANY THAT PROVIDES THE POWER OF SIMPLICITY FOR INCREASINGLY COMPLEX WORKPLACES, EARNED A SECOND CONSECUTIVE HR TECH AWARD ON THE STRENGTH OF THE COMPANY'S CLIENT SUPPORT DURING THE PANDEMIC.
