SIGNATURE BANK PROVIDES DIGITAL ASSET BANKING UPDATE. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--SIGNATURE BANK (NASDAQ:SBNY), A NEW YORK-BASED, FULL-SERVICE COMMERCIAL BANK, ANNOUNCED TODAY A DIGITAL ASSET BANKING UPDATE, CITING THAT ITS DEPOSIT RELATIONSHIP WITH FTX AND THEIR RELATED COMPANIES IS LESS THAN 0.1 PERCENT OF THE BANK'S OVERALL DEPOSITS AS OF NOVEMBER 14, 2022.
