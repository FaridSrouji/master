SIGNATURE BANK EXPANDS INTO NEVADA WITH APPOINTMENT OF BANKERS AND OPENING OF RENO PRIVATE CLIENT BANKING OFFICE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SIGNATURE BANK (NASDAQ: SBNY), A NEW YORK-BASED FULL-SERVICE COMMERCIAL BANK, ANNOUNCED TODAY FURTHER EXPANSION OF ITS WEST COAST PRESENCE WITH THE APPOINTMENT OF VETERAN BANKING PROFESSIONALS AND THE OPENING OF A NEW PRIVATE CLIENT BANKING OFFICE IN RENO, NEVADA. SIGNATURE BANK IS NOW HOME TO 35 TEAMS ON THE WEST COAST, LED BY 56 GROUP DIRECTORS FROM ITS EIGHT OFFICES.
