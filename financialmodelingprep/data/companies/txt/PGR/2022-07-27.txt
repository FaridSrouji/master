PROGRESSIVE ANNOUNCES INVESTOR RELATIONS EVENT. MAYFIELD VILLAGE, OHIO, JULY 27, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED, THE PROGRESSIVE CORPORATION (NYSE: PGR) WILL HOST AN INVESTOR RELATIONS EVENT ON WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 3, 2022, BEGINNING AT 9:30 A.M. EASTERN TIME. THE EVENT, WHICH WILL CONSIST OF BOTH A CONFERENCE CALL AND A WEBCAST, IS SCHEDULED TO LAST 90 MINUTES AND WILL BEGIN WITH AN APPROXIMATE 45-MINUTE PRESENTATION ON THE PROPERTY BUSINESS, FOLLOWED BY A QUESTION-AND-ANSWER SESSION WITH TRICIA GRIFFITH, OUR CEO, AND JOHN SAUERLAND, OUR CFO. CALL-IN PARTICIPANTS WILL BE ABLE TO ASK QUESTIONS VIA THE PHONE, HOWEVER, WEBCAST PARTICIPANTS WILL NOT BE ABLE TO SUBMIT QUESTIONS ONLINE.
