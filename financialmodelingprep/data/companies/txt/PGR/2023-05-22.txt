PROGRESSIVE PRICES $500 MILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. MAYFIELD VILLAGE, OHIO, MAY 22, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE PROGRESSIVE CORPORATION (NYSE: PGR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 4.95% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2033 (THE “NOTES”) IN AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING.  THE NOTES WERE PRICED AT 99.941% OF PAR. GOLDMAN SACHS & CO. LLC AND J.P. MORGAN SECURITIES LLC ARE ACTING AS JOINT BOOKRUNNERS FOR THE OFFERING.
