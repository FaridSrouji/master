PROGRESSIVE ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY COMMON SHARE DIVIDEND AND APPOINTMENT OF CALCULATION AGENT FOR PREFERRED SHARES. MAYFIELD VILLAGE, OHIO, MARCH 06, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ON MARCH 3, 2023, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE PROGRESSIVE CORPORATION (NYSE:PGR) DECLARED A $0.10 PER COMMON SHARE DIVIDEND, PAYABLE APRIL 14, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 6, 2023 (EX-DIVIDEND DATE OF APRIL 5, 2023).
