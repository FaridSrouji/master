YUM! BRANDS, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.605 PER SHARE. LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--YUM! BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: YUM) BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.605 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND WILL BE DISTRIBUTED JUNE 9, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 30, 2023. YUM! BRANDS, INC., BASED IN LOUISVILLE, KENTUCKY, AND ITS SUBSIDIARIES FRANCHISE OR OPERATE A SYSTEM OF OVER 55,000 RESTAURANTS IN MORE THAN 155 COUNTRIES AND TERRITORIES UNDER THE COMPANY'S CONCEPTS – KFC, TACO BELL, PIZZA HUT AND THE.
