YUM! BRANDS, INC. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. YUM! BRANDS, INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 1,339 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,391 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 480 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 553 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 255 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 454 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.83 COMPARED TO USD 1.43 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.81 COMPARED TO USD 1.4 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 3,903 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 4,130 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 1,384 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,555 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 806 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,208 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.63 COMPARED TO USD 3.72 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.57 COMPARED TO USD 3.64 A YEAR AGO.
