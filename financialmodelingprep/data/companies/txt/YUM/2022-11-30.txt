KFC® LAUNCHES SHAREMOBILE TOUR TO SHARE FRIED CHICKEN WITH FAMILIES THIS HOLIDAY SEASON. KFC AND BLESSINGS IN A BACKPACK TEAM UP TO PROVIDE NEARLY 70,000 MEALS TO FAMILIES IN NEED LOUISVILLE, KY. , NOV. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN THE SPIRIT OF GIVING THIS HOLIDAY SEASON, KENTUCKY FRIED CHICKEN® IS HITTING THE ROAD WITH LONGTIME PARTNER BLESSINGS IN A BACKPACK TO FEED FAMILIES IN NEED WITH THE KFC SHAREMOBILE , A HOLIDAY-EDITION CHARITABLE FOOD TRUCK.
