NPC INTERNATIONAL REACHES AGREEMENT WITH PIZZA HUT ON OPTIMIZATION OF RESTAURANT PORTFOLIO AND LAUNCHES SALE PROCESS FOR PIZZA HUT BUSINESS. NPC INTERNATIONAL REACHES AGREEMENT WITH PIZZA HUT ON OPTIMIZATION OF RESTAURANT PORTFOLIO AND LAUNCHES SALE PROCESS FOR PIZZA HUT BUSINESS.NPC INTERNATIONAL INC - AGREEMENT ALLOWS NPC TO CLOSE UP TO 300 OF ITS PIZZA HUT RESTAURANTS.NPC INTERNATIONAL - CO, PIZZA HUT WILL ENDEAVOR TO REALLOCATE EMPLOYEE RESOURCES TO LOCATIONS AT NPC, OTHER YUM! BRANDS LOCATIONS, WHEREVER POSSIBLE.NPC INTERNATIONAL - IN CONJUNCTION WITH SALE PROCESS, DEAL PROVIDES FLEXIBILITY TO EXPLORE OPTIONS AS CO SEEKS TO FINALIZE TERMS & EMERGE FROM CHAPTER 11.
