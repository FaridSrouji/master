KFC® TEAMS UP WITH DEION SANDERS AND HIS FAMILY TO CHAMPION THE JOY OF FAMILY DINNER TIME. THE SANDERS FAMILY – LONGTIME KFC FANS – WILL PROMOTE NEW KENTUCKY FRIED CHICKEN NUGGETS AND OTHER MENU INNOVATIONS IN THE BRAND'S LINEUP LOUISVILLE, KY. , JUNE 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KENTUCKY FRIED CHICKEN® IS TEAMING UP WITH LEGENDARY PRO FOOTBALL HALL OF FAMER AND HEAD FOOTBALL COACH AT THE UNIVERSITY OF COLORADO BOULDER, DEION "PRIME TIME" SANDERS, AND HIS FAMILY TO PROMOTE THE BRAND'S LATEST MENU INNOVATIONS, INCLUDING NEW KFC CHICKEN NUGGETS AND OTHER SOON-TO-BE-ANNOUNCED MENU INNOVATIONS.
