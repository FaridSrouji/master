LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--YUM! BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: YUM) TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE SECOND-QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021. WORLDWIDE SYSTEM SALES EXCLUDING FOREIGN CURRENCY TRANSLATION GREW 26%, WITH 23% SAME-STORE SALES AND 2% UNIT GROWTH. SECOND-QUARTER GAAP EPS WAS $1.29, AN INCREASE OF 91% OVER THE PRIOR YEAR QUARTER. SECOND-QUARTER EPS EXCLUDING SPECIAL ITEMS WAS $1.16, AN INCREASE OF 41% OVER THE PRIOR YEAR QUARTER.
