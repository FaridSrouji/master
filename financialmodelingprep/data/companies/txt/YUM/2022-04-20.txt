YUM CHINA ANNOUNCES DISCLOSURE UNDER HONG KONG STOCK EXCHANGE RULES IN RELATION TO A POSSIBLE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. SHANGHAI , APRIL 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  YUM CHINA HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: YUMC AND HKEX: 9987, "YUM CHINA" OR THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED, IN COMPLIANCE WITH THE RULES GOVERNING THE LISTING OF SECURITIES ON THE STOCK EXCHANGE OF HONG KONG LIMITED (THE "HKEX") WHICH REQUIRE ADVANCE NOTICE OF BOARD MEETINGS AT WHICH A DIVIDEND IS EXPECTED TO BE DECLARED, THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE "BOARD") WILL CONSIDER THE DECLARATION AND PAYMENT OF A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND (THE "DIVIDEND"). IF THE BOARD DECIDES TO PROCEED, THE DECLARATION WILL BE ADOPTED BY BOARD RESOLUTION ON OR AROUND MAY 4, 2022 (BEIJING/HONG KONG TIME) AND WILL BE PROMPTLY DISCLOSED BY THE COMPANY.
