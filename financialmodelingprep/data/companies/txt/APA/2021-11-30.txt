APA CORPORATION ANNOUNCES EGYPTIAN PARLIAMENT'S APPROVAL OF MODERNIZED PRODUCTION SHARING CONTRACT. HOUSTON, NOV. 30, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EGYPTIAN PARLIAMENT HAS APPROVED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED AGREEMENT TO MODERNIZE AND CONSOLIDATE ITS CURRENT PRODUCTION SHARING CONTRACTS (PSCS) WITH EGYPT'S MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND MINERAL RESOURCES (MOP) AND THE EGYPTIAN GENERAL PETROLEUM CORPORATION (EGPC).
