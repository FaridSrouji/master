APA CORPORATION PUBLISHES 2022 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. HOUSTON, JULY 26, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) TODAY PUBLISHED ITS 2022 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT, HIGHLIGHTING THE ACHIEVEMENT OF NUMEROUS ENVIRONMENT, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) GOALS THROUGHOUT 2021 WHILE TRACKING PROGRESS MADE ON ESG COMMITMENTS FOR 2022.
