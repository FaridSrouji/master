APA CORPORATION NAMED TO FORBES LIST OF AMERICA'S BEST MIDSIZE EMPLOYERS FOR 2022. HOUSTON, FEB. 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) HAS BEEN NAMED TO FORBES' 2022 LIST OF AMERICA'S BEST MIDSIZE EMPLOYERS FOR THE SECOND YEAR IN A ROW. THE FINAL LIST SPANS 25 INDUSTRIES, RECOGNIZING THE TOP 500 COMPANIES THAT RECEIVED THE MOST RECOMMENDATIONS FROM THEIR EMPLOYEES.
