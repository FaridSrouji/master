APA CORPORATION ACHIEVES ESG GOAL OF REDUCING UPSTREAM ROUTINE FLARING IN EGYPT BY 40%. HOUSTON, NOV. 14, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACHIEVEMENT OF A COMPENSATION-LINKED ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) GOAL TO REDUCE UPSTREAM ROUTINE FLARING ACROSS EGYPT OPERATIONS BY 40%. THE GOAL WAS REACHED AHEAD OF SCHEDULE AND IS THE RESULT OF NUMEROUS EMISSIONS REDUCTION PROJECTS EXECUTED IN EGYPT THROUGHOUT 2022.
