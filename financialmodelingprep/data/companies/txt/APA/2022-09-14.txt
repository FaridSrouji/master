APA CORPORATION ANNOUNCES DIVIDEND INCREASE, DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND ON COMMON SHARES AND INCREASES SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION. HOUSTON, SEPT. 14, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) HAS ANNOUNCED AN INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S DIVIDEND ON COMMON SHARES FROM AN ANNUALIZED RATE OF 50 CENTS PER SHARE TO $1.00 PER SHARE.
