APACHE CORPORATION AND LONGPATH TECHNOLOGIES EXPAND METHANE MONITORING PARTNERSHIP IN THE PERMIAN BASIN. HOUSTON, MARCH 07, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APACHE CORPORATION, A SUBSIDIARY OF APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA), AND LONGPATH TECHNOLOGIES, INC. ARE ANNOUNCING AN EXPANSION OF THEIR PARTNERSHIP FOR CONTINUOUS METHANE EMISSIONS MONITORING IN THE PERMIAN BASIN OF SOUTHEAST NEW MEXICO AND WEST TEXAS.
