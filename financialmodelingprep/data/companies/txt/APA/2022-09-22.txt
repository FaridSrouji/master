APA CORPORATION ANNOUNCES ENGAGEMENT OF GHD FOR THIRD-PARTY EMISSIONS VERIFICATION. HOUSTON, SEPT. 22, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS ENGAGEMENT OF GHD, A PRIVATELY-OWNED GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES COMPANY SPECIALIZING IN THE IMPLEMENTATION AND VERIFICATION OF SUSTAINABILITY PROJECTS. THIS ENGAGEMENT UNDERPINS APA'S COMMITMENT TO PROTECTING THE ENVIRONMENT, WHICH INCLUDES AN ALL EMPLOYEE COMPENSATION-LINKED GOAL ANNOUNCED EARLIER THIS YEAR TO ELIMINATE AT LEAST 1 MILLION TONNES OF CO2E BY THE END OF 2024.
