APACHE CORPORATION COMMENCES CASH TENDER OFFER FOR ANY AND ALL OF CERTAIN OF ITS OUTSTANDING NOTES FOR UP TO $1.5 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT. HOUSTON, AUG. 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APACHE CORPORATION ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMMENCED CASH TENDER OFFERS (EACH, AN “OFFER” AND COLLECTIVELY, THE “OFFERS”) TO PURCHASE UP TO $1.5 BILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT (THE “MAXIMUM PURCHASE AMOUNT”) OF ITS OUTSTANDING NOTES LISTED IN THE TABLE BELOW (THE “NOTES,” AND EACH, A “SERIES” OF NOTES). SUBJECT TO THE MAXIMUM PURCHASE AMOUNT, THE AMOUNT OF A SERIES OF NOTES THAT IS PURCHASED IN THE OFFERS WILL BE BASED ON THE ACCEPTANCE PRIORITY LEVELS SET FORTH BELOW.
