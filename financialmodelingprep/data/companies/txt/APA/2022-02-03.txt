LIEUTENANT GENERAL CHARLES HOOPER (RET.) APPOINTED TO APA CORPORATION BOARD OF DIRECTORS. HOUSTON, FEB. 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF RETIRED LIEUTENANT GENERAL CHARLES HOOPER TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. GENERAL HOOPER WILL SERVE ON THE CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY, GOVERNANCE, AND NOMINATING COMMITTEE AND THE MANAGEMENT DEVELOPMENT AND COMPENSATION COMMITTEE.
