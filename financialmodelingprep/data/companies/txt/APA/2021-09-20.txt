APA CORPORATION INCREASES CASH DIVIDEND ON COMMON SHARES. HOUSTON, SEPT. 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) HAS DECLARED A REGULAR CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON SHARES, INCREASING THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE TO 6.25 CENTS PER SHARE, UP FROM 2.5 CENTS PER SHARE.
