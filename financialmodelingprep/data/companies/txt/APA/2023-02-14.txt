APA CORPORATION NAMED ONE OF WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES BY FORTUNE MAGAZINE. HOUSTON, FEB. 14, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) HAS BEEN NAMED BY FORTUNE MAGAZINE AS ONE OF THE WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES FOR A FIFTH TIME. APA RANKED FIFTH THIS YEAR AMONG INTERNATIONAL ENERGY EXPLORATION COMPANIES.
