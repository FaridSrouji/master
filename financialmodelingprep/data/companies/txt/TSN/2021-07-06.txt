TYSON FOODS VOLUNTARILY RECALLS FROZEN, FULLY COOKED CHICKEN. SPRINGDALE, ARK., JULY 03, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TYSON FOODS, INC. (NYSE: TSN), IS VOLUNTARILY RECALLING APPROXIMATELY 8.5 MILLION POUNDS OF FROZEN, FULLY COOKED CHICKEN. TYSON HAS BEEN WORKING CLOSELY WITH THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE ON THIS RECALL, AND WHILE THERE IS NO CONCLUSIVE EVIDENCE THAT THE PRODUCTS WERE CONTAMINATED AT THE TIME OF SHIPMENT, THE VOLUNTARY RECALL IS BEING INITIATED OUT OF AN ABUNDANCE OF CAUTION.
