FOOD ROBOTICS LEADER SOFT ROBOTICS RAISES $10M TO MEET PANDEMIC-INDUCED DEMAND AND BRING ITS 3D VISION AND AI-POWERED SOLUTIONS TO MARKET. BEDFORD, MASS., JUNE 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AWARD-WINNING FOOD ROBOTICS COMPANY SOFT ROBOTICS INC. ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS RAISED A $10M SERIES B EXTENSION, TO FURTHER EXPAND COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS TO MEET COVID-19 PANDEMIC-FUELED DEMAND.
