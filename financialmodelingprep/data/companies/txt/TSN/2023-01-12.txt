TYSON FOODS APPOINTS ADAM DECKINGER GENERAL COUNSEL AND SECRETARY. SPRINGDALE, ARK., JAN. 12, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TYSON FOODS, INC. (NYSE: TSN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ADAM DECKINGER HAS BEEN NAMED GENERAL COUNSEL AND SECRETARY, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. DECKINGER HAS BEEN WITH THE COMPANY SINCE 2018, MOST RECENTLY SERVING AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, HEAD OF LAW AND COMPLIANCE. DECKINGER WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO DONNIE KING, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF TYSON FOODS.
