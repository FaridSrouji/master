TYSON FOODS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. SPRINGDALE, ARK., FEB. 09, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TYSON FOODS (NYSE: TSN), AT A MEETING ON FEB. 8, 2023, DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.48 PER SHARE ON CLASS A COMMON STOCK AND $0.432 PER SHARE ON CLASS B COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 15, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 1, 2023.
TYSON FOODS HOLDS 60TH ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS. SPRINGDALE, ARK., FEB. 09, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TYSON FOODS (NYSE: TSN) HELD ITS 60TH ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS EARLIER TODAY AT ITS EMMA ST. LOCATION, THE SITE OF THE COMPANY'S ORIGINAL HEADQUARTERS, TO ADDRESS COMPANY BUSINESS FOLLOWING ITS FIRST QUARTER RESULTS.
