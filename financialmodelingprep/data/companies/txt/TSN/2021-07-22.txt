TYSON FOODS JOINS MCDONALD'S IN MUTUAL COMMITMENT TO DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION. SPRINGDALE, ARK., JULY 22, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TYSON FOODS, INC. (NYSE: TSN) ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING PROTEIN PRODUCERS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS JOINED THE MCDONALD'S MUTUAL COMMITMENT TO DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION (MCDEI). TYSON'S EMPLOYEE BASE IS ENORMOUSLY DIVERSE, WITH MORE THAN 50 LANGUAGES SPOKEN BY TEAM MEMBERS ACROSS THE COMPANY'S U.S. BUSINESS. TODAY'S ANNOUNCEMENT FOLLOWS THE COMPANY'S LONGSTANDING AND PUBLICLY STATED COMMITMENT TO DIVERSITY, ALSO A PART OF ITS CORE VALUES.
