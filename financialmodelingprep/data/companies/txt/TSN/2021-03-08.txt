ROSEN, A LEADING LAW FIRM, ENCOURAGES TYSON FOODS, INC. INVESTORS WITH LARGE LOSSES TO SECURE COUNSEL BEFORE IMPORTANT DEADLINE – TSN. NEW YORK, MARCH 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WHY: ROSEN LAW FIRM, A GLOBAL INVESTOR RIGHTS LAW FIRM, REMINDS PURCHASERS OF THE SECURITIES OF TYSON FOODS, INC. (NYSE: TSN) BETWEEN MARCH 13, 2020 AND DECEMBER 15, 2020, BOTH DATES INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”), OF THE IMPORTANT APRIL 5, 2021 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE IN THE SECURITIES CLASS ACTION FIRST FILED BY THE FIRM.
