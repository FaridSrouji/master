TYSON FOODS TO GIVE OVER 90,000 U.S. HOURLY TEAM MEMBERS APPROXIMATELY $50 MILLION IN YEAR-END BONUSES. SPRINGDALE, ARK., DEC. 06, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TYSON FOODS IS SAYING THANK YOU ONCE AGAIN TO MORE THAN 90,000 HOURLY TEAM MEMBERS ACROSS THE U.S. BY GIVING THEM APPROXIMATELY $50 MILLION IN YEAR-END BONUSES FOR THEIR EFFORTS OVER THE PAST YEAR. FOR TEAM MEMBERS IN THE U.S., THESE BONUSES RANGE FROM $300 TO $700, AND WILL BE DISTRIBUTED STARTING MID-DECEMBER.
