ROSEN, TOP RANKED INVESTOR COUNSEL, ENCOURAGES TYSON FOODS, INC. INVESTORS TO SECURE COUNSEL BEFORE IMPORTANT APRIL 5 DEADLINE IN SECURITIES CLASS ACTION - TSN. (NYSE: TSN) BETWEEN MARCH 13, 2020 AND DECEMBER 15, 2020, BOTH DATES INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD"), OF THE IMPORTANT APRIL 5, 2021 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE IN THE SECURITIES CLASS ACTION FIRST FILED BY THE FIRM. SO WHAT: IF YOU PURCHASED TYSON SECURITIES DURING THE CLASS PERIOD YOU MAY BE ENTITLED TO COMPENSATION WITHOUT PAYMENT OF ANY OUT OF POCKET FEES OR COSTS THROUGH A CONTINGENCY FEE ARRANGEMENT.
