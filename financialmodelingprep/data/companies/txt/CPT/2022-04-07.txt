CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST ANNOUNCES PRICING OF COMMON SHARES. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST (NYSE:CPT) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF 2,900,000 COMMON SHARES FOR GROSS PROCEEDS OF APPROXIMATELY $493 MILLION. THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON APRIL 12, 2022, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS. BOFA SECURITIES AND WELLS FARGO SECURITIES, THE JOINT BOOK-RUNNING MANAGERS FOR THE OFFERING, HAVE BEEN GRANTED A 30-DAY OPTION TO PURCHASE UP TO AN ADDITIONAL 435,000 COMMON SHARES.
