CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST ANNOUNCES ACTIONS TO SUPPORT RESIDENTS AND EMPLOYEES DURING COVID-19 PANDEMIC.CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST -  OFFERING ZERO RENT INCREASES ON LEASE RENEWALS, WAIVING LATE FEES, CREATING PAYMENT PLANS, FOR THOSE IMPACTED BY COVID-19.CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST - HALTING EVICTIONS FOR RESIDENTS WHO HAVE BEEN FINANCIALLY IMPACTED BY COVID-19 PANDEMIC.CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST - LEASING OFFICES TEMPORARILY CLOSED BUT CONTINUE TO PROVIDE ONGOING ESSENTIAL SERVICES TO RESIDENTS, PROSPECTIVE RESIDENTS.
