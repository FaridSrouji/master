CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER 2019 DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 17, 2020. THE BOARD OF TRUST MANAGERS OF CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST DECLARED A FOURTH QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.80 PER SHARE TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 16, 2019 OF ITS COMMON SHARES OF BENEFICIAL INTEREST. THE DIVIDEND IS TO BE PAID ON JANUARY 17, 2020.
