CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. HOUSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE BOARD OF TRUST MANAGERS OF CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST (NYSE:CPT) (THE “COMPANY”) DECLARED A FOURTH QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.94 PER SHARE TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 16, 2022 OF ITS COMMON SHARES OF BENEFICIAL INTEREST. THE DIVIDEND IS TO BE PAID ON JANUARY 17, 2023.
