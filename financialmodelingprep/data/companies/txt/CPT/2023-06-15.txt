CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST ANNOUNCES SECOND QUARTER 2023 DIVIDEND. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF TRUST MANAGERS OF CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST (NYSE:CPT) (THE “COMPANY”) DECLARED A SECOND QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.00 PER SHARE TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 30, 2023 OF ITS COMMON SHARES OF BENEFICIAL INTEREST. THE DIVIDEND IS TO BE PAID ON JULY 17, 2023. CAMDEN PROPERTY TRUST, AN S&P 500 COMPANY, IS A REAL ESTATE COMPANY PRIMARILY ENGAGED IN THE OWNERSHIP, MANAGEMENT, DEVELOPMENT, REDEVELOPMENT, ACQUISITION, AND CONSTRUCTION OF MULTIFAMILY APARTMENT.
