80 PERCENT OF PARENTS WITH CHILDREN AGES 3 TO 13 PLAN TO SEND CHILD TO SCHOOL WITH A REUSABLE WATER BOTTLE THIS FALL, ACCORDING TO NEW RESEARCH FROM CONTIGO. CHICAGO, JULY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SUMMER MAY HAVE JUST BEGUN, BUT BELIEVE IT OR NOT, THE 2021-2022 BACK-TO-SCHOOL SHOPPING SEASON IS HERE. SEVENTY-ONE PERCENT OF PARENTS HAVE ALREADY TAKEN STEPS TO PREPARE TO SEND THEIR CHILDREN BACK TO SCHOOL, ACCORDING TO A NEW SURVEY CONDUCTED BY ONEPOLL FOR CONTIGO®, A LEADING INNOVATOR OF KIDS WATER BOTTLES.
