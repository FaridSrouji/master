NEWELL BRANDS ANNOUNCES RESTRUCTURING PROGRAM. ATLANTA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NEWELL BRANDS (NASDAQ: NWL) TODAY ANNOUNCED A RESTRUCTURING AND SAVINGS INITIATIVE, PROJECT PHOENIX, THAT AIMS TO STRENGTHEN THE COMPANY BY LEVERAGING ITS SCALE TO FURTHER REDUCE COMPLEXITY, STREAMLINING ITS OPERATING MODEL AND DRIVING OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCIES.
