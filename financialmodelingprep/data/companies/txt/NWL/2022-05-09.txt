YANKEE CANDLE CELEBRATES ALL EXPRESSIONS OF LOVE WITH NEW LOVE IS LOVE COLLECTION. FRAGRANCE BRAND HELPS NONPROFIT RAINBOW RAILROAD LIGHT THE WAY TO SAFETY AHEAD OF PRIDE MONTH SOUTH DEERFIELD, MASS. , MAY 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE YANKEE CANDLE COMPANY, INC., PART OF THE NEWELL BRANDS PORTFOLIO AND THE LEADER IN HOME FRAGRANCE, ANNOUNCES THE CREATION OF A NEW, LIMITED-EDITION FRAGRANCE, LOVE IS LOVE, TO RAISE AWARENESS OF GLOBAL NONPROFIT RAINBOW RAILROAD'S MISSION TO PROVIDE PATHWAYS TO SAFETY FOR LGBTQIA+ PEOPLE AT RISK IN THEIR HOME COUNTRIES.
