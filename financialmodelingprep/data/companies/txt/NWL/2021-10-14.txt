THE CROCKPOT BRAND AUCTIONING FIRST-EVER NFT TO CELEBRATE ITS 50TH ANNIVERSARY. BOCA RATON, FLA., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SUNBEAM PRODUCTS, INC., THE MANUFACTURER OF CROCKPOT ("CROCKPOT"),—THE ORIGINAL SLOW COOKER—TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS CREATED ITS FIRST-EVER NON-FUNGIBLE TOKEN (NFT) IN HONOR OF THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE CROCKPOT BRAND.
