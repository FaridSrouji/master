NEWELL BRANDS ELECTS STEPHANIE STAHL TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ATLANTA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NEWELL BRANDS (NASDAQ: NWL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT STEPHANIE STAHL HAS BEEN ELECTED TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2023. WITH THE APPOINTMENT OF MS. STAHL, THE SIZE OF THE COMPANY'S BOARD WILL INCREASE FROM TEN MEMBERS TO ELEVEN.
