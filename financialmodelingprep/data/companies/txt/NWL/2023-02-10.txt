NEWELL BRANDS ANNOUNCES BOARD AND CHAIR REFRESHMENT. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWELL BRANDS (NASDAQ: NWL) ANNOUNCED TODAY A SERIES OF STEPS TO REFRESH THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND ITS LEADERSHIP. “TODAY'S ANNOUNCEMENTS, IN CONJUNCTION WITH THE EARLIER APPOINTMENT OF STEPHANIE STAHL TO THE BOARD, DEMONSTRATE OUR STRONG COMMITMENT TO SUCCESSION PLANNING, PROACTIVE REFRESHMENT OF THE BOARD, AND IMPROVING THE BOARD'S DIVERSITY,” SAID BRIDGET RYAN BERMAN, CHAIR OF THE NOMINATING/GOVERNANCE COMMITTEE.
NEWELL BRANDS ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2022 RESULTS. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWELL BRANDS (NASDAQ: NWL) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS. "FOURTH QUARTER RESULTS WERE IN LINE WITH OUR EXPECTATIONS AND BROUGHT TO A CLOSE A DIFFICULT SECOND HALF. THE BUSINESS CONTINUED TO BE IMPACTED BY A TOUGH OPERATING ENVIRONMENT, INCLUDING SLOWING CONSUMER DEMAND FOR GENERAL MERCHANDISE CATEGORIES, AS WELL AS INVENTORY REDUCTIONS AT RETAIL,” SAID RAVI SALIGRAM, NEWELL BRANDS CEO.
NEWELL BRANDS APPOINTS CHRIS PETERSON AS PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER; RAVI SALIGRAM TO RETIRE. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWELL BRANDS (NASDAQ: NWL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CHRIS PETERSON, CURRENTLY PRESIDENT, TO SERVE AS PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, EFFECTIVE UPON THE CONCLUSION OF THE COMPANY'S 2023 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS ON MAY 16TH, 2023. THE BOARD WILL ALSO NOMINATE MR. PETERSON FOR ELECTION TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT THE COMPANY'S 2023 ANNUAL MEETING.
