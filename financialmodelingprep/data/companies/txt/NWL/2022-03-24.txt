THE WOODWICK® CANDLE BRAND ANNOUNCES PARTNERSHIP WITH REFORESTATION NONPROFIT, ONE TREE PLANTED. THE BRAND CELEBRATES EARTH MONTH WITH COMMITMENT TO PLANT 75,000 TREES IN 2022 SOUTH DEERFIELD, MASS. , MARCH 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE YANKEE CANDLE COMPANY, INC., PART OF THE NEWELL BRANDS PORTFOLIO AND MAKER OF PREMIUM WOODWICK® BRAND CANDLES, ANNOUNCES TODAY ITS PARTNERSHIP WITH ONE TREE PLANTED, AN ENVIRONMENTAL CHARITY THAT SUPPORTS REFORESTATION AROUND THE WORLD.
