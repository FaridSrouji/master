PODA COMPLETES MULTIPARTY SALE OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSETS FOR US$100.5 MILLION. VANCOUVER, BC , JUNE 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ - PODA HOLDINGS, INC. ("PODA" OR THE "COMPANY") (CSE: PODA) (‎FSE: 99L) (OTC: PODAF) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT, FURTHER TO ITS NEWS RELEASE DATED MAY 13, 2022, THE COMPANY, TOGETHER WITH RYAN SELBY AND RYAN KARKAIRAN (THE ‎‎"OWNERS"), HAS COMPLETED THE SALE OF SUBSTANTIALLY ALL OF THE ASSETS AND PROPERTIES ‎USED IN THE COMPANY'S BUSINESS (THE "PURCHASED ASSETS") TO ALTRIA CLIENT SERVICES LLC ("ALCS"), A SUBSIDIARY OF ALTRIA GROUP, INC. (NYSE:MO), FOR A TOTAL PURCHASE PRICE OF US$100.5 MILLION ("PURCHASE PRICE"), SUBJECT TO CERTAIN ‎ADJUSTMENTS AND HOLDBACKS (THE ‎‎"TRANSACTION"), PURSUANT TO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT DATED MAY 13, 2022 (THE "ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT").
