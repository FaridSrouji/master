ALTRIA GROUP INC. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. ALTRIA GROUP INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 6,856 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 6,837 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 2,944 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,557 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET LOSS WAS USD 2,600 MILLION COMPARED TO NET INCOME OF USD 1,943 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC LOSS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.39 COMPARED TO BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE OF USD 1.03 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 19,103 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 19,250 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 7,899 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 7,371 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 516 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 5,713 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.27 COMPARED TO USD 3.02 A YEAR AGO.
