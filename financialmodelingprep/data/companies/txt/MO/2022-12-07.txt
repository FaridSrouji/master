ALTRIA DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.94 PER SHARE. RICHMOND, VA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ALTRIA GROUP, INC. (NYSE: MO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT OUR BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.94 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 10, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 22, 2022. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS DECEMBER 21, 2022.
