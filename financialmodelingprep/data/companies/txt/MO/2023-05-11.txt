KELLER ROHRBACK L.L.P.: ALTRIA AGREES IN PRINCIPLE TO $235 MILLION SETTLEMENT IN NATIONWIDE JUUL E-CIGARETTE YOUTH VAPING LITIGATION. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KELLER ROHRBACK IS THRILLED TO ANNOUNCE A $235 MILLION SETTLEMENT WITH ALTRIA AND RELATED COMPANIES, IN THE MASSIVE NATION-WIDE JUUL E-CIGARETTE YOUTH VAPING PREDATORY ADVERTISING, FRAUD, ADDICTION AND INJURY LITIGATION: IN RE JUUL LABS, INC. MKTG., SALES PRACTICES, & PRODS. LIAB. LITIG., MDL NO. 2913 (N.D. CAL.). ALTRIA IS THE PARENT COMPANY OF PHILIP MORRIS USA AND THE COMPANY BEHIND MARLBORO CIGARETTES. THEY WERE ALSO ONE THE MAJOR INVESTORS IN THE JUUL E-CIGARE.
