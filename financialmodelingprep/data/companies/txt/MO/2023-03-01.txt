ALTRIA DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.94 PER SHARE. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALTRIA GROUP, INC. (NYSE: MO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT OUR BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.94 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON APRIL 28, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MARCH 24, 2023. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS MARCH 23, 2023.
