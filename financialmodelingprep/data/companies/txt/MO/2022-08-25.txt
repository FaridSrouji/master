ALTRIA INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 4.4% TO $0.94 PER SHARE. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $MO #ALTRIA--ALTRIA GROUP, INC. (NYSE: MO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT OUR BOARD OF DIRECTORS VOTED TO INCREASE OUR REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 4.4% TO $0.94 PER SHARE VERSUS THE PREVIOUS RATE OF $0.90 PER SHARE. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 11, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF SEPTEMBER 15, 2022. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS SEPTEMBER 14, 2022.
