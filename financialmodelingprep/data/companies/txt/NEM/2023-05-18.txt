NEWMONT APPOINTS EXPERIENCED CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWMONT CORPORATION (NYSE: NEM, TSX: NGT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF KARYN OVELMAN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. KARYN OVELMAN IS A HIGHLY EXPERIENCED FINANCIAL PROFESSIONAL WHO WILL FOCUS ON FINANCIAL DISCIPLINE AND MAINTAINING A ROBUST AND FLEXIBLE BALANCE SHEET TO SUPPORT NEWMONT'S CAPITAL ALLOCATION STRATEGY. KARYN WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR NEWMONT'S GLOBAL FINANCE FUNCTIONS AND WILL JOIN NEWMONT DURING THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2023. KA.
