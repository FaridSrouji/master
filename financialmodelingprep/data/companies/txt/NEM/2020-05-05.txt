NEWMONT CEO SAYS ASSUMING GREATEST IMPACT TO FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE COULD BE IN Q2. NEWMONT CEO SAYS FOR 2020, CO ASSUMES GREATEST IMPACT TO OPERATIONS, FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE COULD BE IN Q2 - CONF CALL.NEWMONT CEO SAYS PLANNING FOR SCENARIOS WHERE CO COULD SEE A RESURGENCE OF THE VIRUS LATER IN 2020 AND EARLY 2021 - CONF CALL.NEWMONT CEO SAYS RAMPING UP OPERATIONS AT CERRO NEGRO, ÉLÉONORE AND YANACOCHA - CONF CALL.NEWMONT EXECUTIVE SAYS AT CERRO NEGRO, OPERATIONS WERE PLACED ON CARE MAINTENANCE ON MARCH 23 DUE TO TRAVEL RESTRICTIONS - CONF CALL.NEWMONT CEO SAYS EVALUATING A LOWER LIKELIHOOD SCENARIO WHERE THERE IS A RECURRING SEASONAL IMPACT FROM THE VIRUS- CONF CALL.NEWMONT CEO SAYS SUSTAINING CAPITAL THIS YEAR IS TRACKING TO AROUND THE $900 MILLION MARK, DEVELOPMENT CAPITAL AROUND THE $500 MILLION MARK-CONF CALL.NEWMONT CEO SAYS ASSUMING PEÑASQUITO IS ABLE TO RAMP-UP IN THE COMING WEEKS,  2020 GOLD PRODUCTION WILL BE TOWARDS THE LOWER END OF PREVIOUS GUIDANCE OR ABOUT 6 MILLION ATTRIBUTABLE GOLD OUNCES-CONF CALL.
NEWMONT CORP Q1 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.40. NEWMONT ANNOUNCES SOLID FIRST QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.Q1 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.40.Q1 REVENUE $2.581 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $2.7 BILLION.Q1 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.04 FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.42 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.NEWMONT - PRODUCED 1.5 MILLION ATTRIBUTABLE OUNCES OF GOLD IN QUARTER.NEWMONT - DECLARED Q1 DIVIDEND OF $0.25 PER SHARE.NEWMONT - QTRLY AISC OF $1,030 PER OUNCE.NEWMONT - TWO OPERATIONS TEMPORARILY IN CARE AND MAINTENANCE.NEWMONT - SITES REPRESENTING APPROXIMATELY 90 PERCENT OF COMPANY'S PLANNED 2020 PRODUCTION ARE OPERATING.NEWMONT - PRODUCED 1.5 MILLION ATTRIBUTABLE OUNCES OF GOLD IN QUARTER.NEWMONT - NEWMONT HAS NO CONFIRMED CASES OF COVID-19 AT ANY OF ITS SITES.NEWMONT - 4 NEWMONT OPERATIONS WERE TEMPORARILY PUT INTO CARE AND MAINTENANCE IN MARCH 2020 INCLUDING MUSSELWHITE, ÉLÉONORE, CERRO NEGRO AND YANACOCHA.NEWMONT - IN APRIL 2020, COMPANY ALSO BEGAN TAKING STEPS TOWARDS A SAFE AND ORDERLY RAMP DOWN OF OPERATIONS AT PEÑASQUITO.NEWMONT - IN PROCESS OF RAMPING UP OPERATIONS AT CERRO NEGRO, YANACOCHA AND ÉLÉONORE.
