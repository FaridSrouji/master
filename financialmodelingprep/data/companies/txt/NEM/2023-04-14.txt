NEWCREST AND NEWMONT ENTER INTO EXCLUSIVITY DEED. MELBOURNE, AUSTRALIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - APRIL 14, 2023) - FURTHER TO THE ASX ANNOUNCEMENT DATED 11 APRIL 2023, NEWCREST MINING LIMITED (ASX: NCM) (TSX: NCM) (PNGX: NCM) PROVIDES THE FOLLOWING UPDATE ON THE REVISED CONDITIONAL AND NON-BINDING PROPOSAL FROM NEWMONT CORPORATION (NEWMONT) TO ACQUIRE 100% OF THE ISSUED SHARES IN NEWCREST, BY WAY OF A SCHEME OF ARRANGEMENT (REVISED PROPOSAL). NEWCREST AND NEWMONT HAVE NOW ENTERED INTO AN EXCLUSIVITY DEED, SATISFYING A CONDITION OF NEWMONT'S PROPOSAL.
