CBOE CLEAR EUROPE PLANS TO INTRODUCE CLEARING SERVICE FOR SECURITIES FINANCING TRANSACTIONS. AMSTERDAM AND LONDON , JUNE 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS INC. (CBOE: CBOE), THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES AND SECURITIES EXCHANGE NETWORK, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CBOE CLEAR EUROPE, ITS AMSTERDAM-BASED CLEARING HOUSE, IS PLANNING TO INTRODUCE A CENTRAL COUNTERPARTY (CCP) CLEARING SERVICE FOR SECURITIES FINANCING TRANSACTIONS (SFT) IN Q3 2024, SUBJECT TO REGULATORY APPROVALS. THIS NEW SERVICE IS EXPECTED TO INTRODUCE MATCHING, CCP CLEARING, SETTLEMENT AND POST-TRADE LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT FOR SFT TRANSACTIONS IN EUROPEAN CASH EQUITIES AND ETFS, FOR (AGENT) LENDERS AND BORROWERS, WITH SETTLEMENT TAKING PLACE IN 19 EUROPEAN CENTRAL SECURITIES DEPOSITORIES (CSDS).
