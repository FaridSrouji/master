CBOE CLEAR EUROPE RECEIVES PERMANENT RECOGNITION AS A NON-UK CENTRAL COUNTERPARTY. AMSTERDAM AND LONDON , JAN. 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS INC. (CBOE: CBOE), A LEADING PROVIDER OF GLOBAL MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE AND TRADABLE PRODUCTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CBOE CLEAR EUROPE, ITS AMSTERDAM-BASED CLEARING HOUSE, HAS RECEIVED PERMANENT RECOGNITION BY THE BANK OF ENGLAND TO OPERATE AS A CENTRAL COUNTERPARTY (CCP) IN THE UK. THIS RECOGNITION WAS EFFECTIVE FROM JANUARY 10 2023, AND APPLIES TO CASH EQUITIES AND EQUITY DERIVATIVES.
