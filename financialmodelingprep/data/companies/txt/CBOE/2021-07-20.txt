CBOE EUROPE ENHANCES CBOE LIS BLOCK TRADING PLATFORM WITH DIRECTED IOI SERVICE. LONDON, JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CBOE EUROPE, A PAN-EUROPEAN EXCHANGE OPERATOR AND SUBSIDIARY OF CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS, INC. (CBOE: CBOE), TODAY ANNOUNCED AN ENHANCEMENT TO ITS BLOCK TRADING PLATFORM, CBOE LIS, WHICH IS POWERED BY BIDS TECHNOLOGY, WITH THE ADDITION OF DIRECTED INDICATION OF INTEREST (DIOI) FUNCTIONALITY. THE SERVICE WAS SUCCESSFULLY LAUNCHED ON 12 JULY 2021 ON THE CBOE UK LIS PLATFORM.
