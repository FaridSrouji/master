CBOE EXPANDS EUROPEAN LEADERSHIP TEAM WITH SENIOR HIRE. LONDON, MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CBOE EUROPE, A PAN-EUROPEAN EXCHANGE OPERATOR AND A SUBSIDIARY OF CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS (CBOE: CBOE), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT NATAN TIEFENBRUN WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, HEAD OF EUROPEAN EQUITIES. IN THIS NEW ROLE, NATAN WILL OVERSEE CBOE'S EUROPEAN EQUITIES TRADING BUSINESS, REPORTING TO DAVID HOWSON, EVP, PRESIDENT EUROPE AND ASIA-PACIFIC, CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS.
