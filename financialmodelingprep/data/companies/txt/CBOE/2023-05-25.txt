CBOE JOINS GOLDMAN SACHS AND BNP PARIBAS IN THE FORMATION OF A NEW GLOBAL BLOCKCHAIN NETWORK. CHICAGO, IL / ACCESSWIRE / MAY 25, 2023 / PARTNERING WITH GOLDMAN SACHS AND DIGITAL ASSETS, CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS (BATS:CBOE) IS LAUNCHING CANTON NETWORK, THE FIRST PRIVACY-ENABLED INTEROPERABLE BLOCKCHAIN NETWORK FOR INSTITUTIONAL ASSETS. CANTON NETWORK AIMS TO OVERCOME THE LIMITATIONS OF PUBLIC BLOCKCHAINS BY PROVIDING A DECENTRALIZED INFRASTRUCTURE THAT ALLOWS PREVIOUSLY ISOLATED SYSTEMS IN FINANCIAL MARKETS TO INTEROPERATE WHILE MAINTAINING THE NECESSARY GOVERNANCE, PRIVACY, PERMISSIONING AND CONTROLS REQUIRED FOR HIGHLY REGULATED INDUSTRIES.
