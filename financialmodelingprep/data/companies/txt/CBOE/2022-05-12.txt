CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS DECLARES SECOND-QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. CHICAGO , MAY 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS, INC. (CBOE: CBOE), A LEADING PROVIDER OF GLOBAL MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE AND TRADABLE PRODUCTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.48 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022. THE SECOND-QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 15, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 31, 2022.
