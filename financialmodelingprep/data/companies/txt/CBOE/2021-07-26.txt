CBOE EUROPE AND EUROCCP RECEIVE DUTCH REGULATORY APPROVALS FOR NEW EUROPEAN DERIVATIVES MARKET. LONDON, JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CBOE EUROPE, THE PAN-EUROPEAN EXCHANGE OPERATOR AND SUBSIDIARY OF CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS, INC. (CBOE: CBOE), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED THE NECESSARY REGULATORY APPROVALS TO LAUNCH CBOE EUROPE DERIVATIVES, A NEW AMSTERDAM-BASED FUTURES AND OPTIONS MARKET, ON 6 SEPTEMBER, 2021.
