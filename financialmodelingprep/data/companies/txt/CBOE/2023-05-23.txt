CBOE REPORTS RECORD Q1 2023 EARNINGS WITH A 60% INCREASE IN DILUTED EPS. CHICAGO, IL / ACCESSWIRE / MAY 23, 2023 / CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS (BATS:CBOE) RECENTLY ANNOUNCED ITS EARNINGS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023, SHOWCASING RECORD-BREAKING RESULTS. THE COMPANY REPORTED A 60% INCREASE IN DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS), REACHING $1.63, WHILE ACHIEVING RECORD ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS OF $1.90, REPRESENTING A 10% INCREASE.
