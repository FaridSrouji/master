CBOE DATA REVEALS INTRADAY OPTION TRADES ARE ON THE RISE. CHICAGO, IL / ACCESSWIRE / MAY 31, 2023 / CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS (BATS:CBOE) HAS REPORTED AN INCREASE IN INTRADAY OPTIONS TRADING, INDICATING A FUNDAMENTAL SHIFT IN HOW MARKET PARTICIPANTS ENGAGE WITH OPTIONS. EDWARD TILLY, THE CHAIRMAN AND CEO OF CBOE, STATED THAT THERE HAS BEEN A FUNDAMENTAL EVOLUTION IN OPTIONS TRADING, WITH MANY MARKET PARTICIPANTS NOW OPENING AND TRADING POSITIONS ON THE SAME DAY THAT OPTIONS EXPIRE.
