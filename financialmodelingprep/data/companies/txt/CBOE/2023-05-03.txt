CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS REPORTS TRADING VOLUME FOR APRIL 2023. CHICAGO , MAY 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS, INC. (CBOE: CBOE), THE WORLD'S LEADING DERIVATIVES AND SECURITIES EXCHANGE NETWORK, TODAY REPORTED APRIL MONTHLY TRADING VOLUME STATISTICS ACROSS ITS GLOBAL BUSINESS LINES. THE DATA SHEET "CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS MONTHLY VOLUME & RPC/NET REVENUE CAPTURE REPORT" CONTAINS AN OVERVIEW OF CERTAIN APRIL TRADING STATISTICS AND MARKET SHARE BY BUSINESS SEGMENT, VOLUME IN SELECT INDEX PRODUCTS, AND RPC/NET CAPTURE, WHICH IS REPORTED ON A ONE-MONTH LAG, ACROSS BUSINESS LINES.
