CBOE ANNOUNCES LAUNCH OF NEW CBOE 3-MONTH IMPLIED CORRELATION INDEX. CHICAGO, JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS, INC. (CBOE: CBOE), A LEADING PROVIDER OF GLOBAL MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE AND TRADABLE PRODUCTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS PUBLISHING INTRADAY VALUES FOR THE CBOE 3-MONTH IMPLIED CORRELATION INDEX (TICKER SYMBOL: COR3M), A NEW BENCHMARK INDEX THAT IS DESIGNED TO PROVIDE MARKET PARTICIPANTS WITH FURTHER GRANULARITY INTO SOME OF THE FACTORS DRIVING VOLATILITY IN THE EQUITY MARKETS. CORRELATION IS A RISK MANAGEMENT TOOL GENERALLY USED BY MARKET PARTICIPANTS TO SET SYSTEMATIC RISK EXPOSURE LEVELS AND MAXIMIZE RISK DIVERSIFICATION BENEFITS.
