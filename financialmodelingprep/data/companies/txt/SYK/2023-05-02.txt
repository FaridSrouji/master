STRYKER COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF CERUS ENDOVASCULAR. KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN, USA, MAY 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK), ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANIES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF CERUS ENDOVASCULAR LTD., A PRIVATELY HELD, COMMERCIAL-STAGE, MEDICAL DEVICE COMPANY ENGAGED IN THE DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT OF NEUROINTERVENTIONAL DEVICES FOR THE TREATMENT OF INTRACRANIAL ANEURYSMS.
