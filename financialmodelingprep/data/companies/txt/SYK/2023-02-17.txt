STRYKER'S Q GUIDANCE SYSTEM FOR CRANIAL APPLICATIONS RECEIVES FDA CLEARANCE. KALAMAZOO, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STRYKER (NYSE:SYK), ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS Q GUIDANCE SYSTEM WITH CRANIAL GUIDANCE SOFTWARE RECEIVED 510(K) CLEARANCE FROM THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION. THE Q GUIDANCE SYSTEM IS AN IMAGE-BASED PLANNING AND INTRAOPERATIVE GUIDANCE SYSTEM DESIGNED TO SUPPORT CRANIAL SURGERIES. RECENTLY LAUNCHED IN SEPTEMBER 2022, THE Q GUIDANCE SYSTEM FOR SPINAL APPLICATIONS IS CURRENTLY AVAILABLE ON THE MARKET.
