STRYKER ANNOUNCES NEW INVESTOR RELATIONS LEADER. KALAMAZOO, MAY 05, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JASON BEACH WILL BE APPOINTED VICE PRESIDENT, INVESTOR RELATIONS AND REPORT TO GLENN BOEHNLEIN, THE COMPANY'S CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2022. PRESTON WELLS, VICE PRESIDENT, INVESTOR RELATIONS WILL BE APPOINTED VICE PRESIDENT, GROUP CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER FOR STRYKER'S ORTHOPAEDICS AND SPINE BUSINESSES. MR. BEACH WILL WORK CLOSELY WITH MR. BOEHNLEIN AND MR. WELLS TO ENSURE A SMOOTH TRANSITION.
STRYKER ANNOUNCES BOARD CHANGES FOLLOWING ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS. KALAMAZOO, MAY 05, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE: SYK) ANNOUNCED THAT ON MAY 4, 2022, THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED BOARD LEADERSHIP AND COMMITTEE ASSIGNMENT CHANGES, NAMING SHERILYN S. MCCOY TO REPLACE ALLAN C. GOLSTON AS LEAD INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR, AND MARY K. BRAINERD TO REPLACE MS. MCCOY AS CHAIR OF THE GOVERNANCE AND NOMINATING COMMITTEE.
