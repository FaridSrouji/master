STRYKER'S DEBUT AT WORLD INTELLIGENCE CONGRESS: BRINGING STRYKER'S ADVANCED DIGITAL HEALTHCARE TO CHINA. SHANGHAI , JUNE 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK), A LEADING MEDICAL TECHNOLOGIES COMPANY, ATTENDED THE 6TH WORLD INTELLIGENCE CONGRESS (WIC) IN CHINA, SHOWCASING HOW ITS ADVANCED DIGITAL HEALTHCARE SOLUTIONS BRINGS CLINICAL, OPERATIONAL AND FINANCIAL VALUES FOR THE HEALTHCARE MARKET. THIS IS THE FIRST TIME FOR STRYKER TO PARTICIPATE IN WIC AS A STRATEGIC PARTNER IN HEALTHCARE INDUSTRY, MARKING A SIGNIFICANT MILESTONE FOR STRYKER AS IT BRINGS MORE ADVANCED DIGITAL HEALTHCARE SOLUTIONS TO THE CHINA MARKET.
