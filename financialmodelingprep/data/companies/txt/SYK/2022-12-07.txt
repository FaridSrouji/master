STRYKER DECLARES A $0.75 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN, DEC. 07, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.75 PER SHARE PAYABLE JANUARY 31, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 30, 2022, REPRESENTING AN INCREASE OF  7.9% VERSUS THE PRIOR YEAR AND PREVIOUS QUARTER.
