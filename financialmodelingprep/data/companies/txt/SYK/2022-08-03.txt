STRYKER DECLARES A $0.695 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. KALAMAZOO MICHIGAN, AUG. 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.695 PER SHARE PAYABLE OCTOBER 31, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2022, REPRESENTING AN INCREASE OF 10.3% VERSUS THE PRIOR YEAR AND UNCHANGED FROM THE PREVIOUS QUARTER.
