STRYKER DECLARES A $0.75 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN, MAY 10, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  STRYKER (NYSE:SYK) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.75 PER SHARE PAYABLE JULY 31, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 30, 2023, REPRESENTING AN INCREASE OF 7.9% VERSUS THE PRIOR YEAR AND UNCHANGED FROM THE PREVIOUS QUARTER.
