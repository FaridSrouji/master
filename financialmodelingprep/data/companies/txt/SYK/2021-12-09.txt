STRYKER DECLARES A $0.695 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN, DEC. 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.695 PER SHARE PAYABLE JANUARY 31, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 31, 2021, REPRESENTING AN INCREASE OF 10.3% VERSUS THE PRIOR YEAR AND PREVIOUS QUARTER.
