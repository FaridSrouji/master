STRYKER TO HOST CONFERENCE CALL ON APRIL 27, 2021. KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN, MARCH 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK) ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL ON TUESDAY, APRIL 27, 2021 AT 4:30 P.M., EASTERN TIME, TO DISCUSS THE COMPANY'S OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021 AND PROVIDE AN OPERATIONAL UPDATE. FINAL OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021 WILL BE RELEASED AT 4:05 P.M. THAT DAY.
