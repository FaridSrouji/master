STRYKER CORPORATION ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF LOUISE L. FRANCESCONI FROM THE BOARD. ON NOVEMBER 29, 2019, MS. LOUISE L. FRANCESCONI, A MEMBER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF STRYKER CORPORATION INFORMED THE COMPANY THAT SHE PLANS TO RETIRE AS A DIRECTOR AND WILL NOT STAND FOR RE-ELECTION TO THE BOARD AT THE COMPANY’S ANNUAL SHAREHOLDERS MEETING EXPECTED TO BE HELD ON MAY 5, 2020. MS. FRANCESCONI WILL CONTINUE TO SERVE AS A DIRECTOR UNTIL THE ANNUAL MEETING. MS. FRANCESCONI’S DECISION TO NOT STAND FOR RE-ELECTION DID NOT INVOLVE ANY DISAGREEMENT WITH THE COMPANY.
STRYKER DECLARES $0.575 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. STRYKER INCREASES DIVIDEND 11%, DECLARING A $0.575 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
