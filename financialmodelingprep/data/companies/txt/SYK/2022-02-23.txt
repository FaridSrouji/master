STRYKER COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF VOCERA COMMUNICATIONS. KALAMAZOO, MI, FEB. 23, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE: SYK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF VOCERA COMMUNICATIONS, INC. (NYSE: VCRA), A LEADER IN DIGITAL CARE COORDINATION AND COMMUNICATION.
