WEST ANNOUNCES SECOND-QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. EXTON, PA., FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEST PHARMACEUTICAL SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: WST), A GLOBAL LEADER IN INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS FOR INJECTABLE DRUG ADMINISTRATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A SECOND-QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND OF $0.18 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON MAY 4, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 20, 2022.
