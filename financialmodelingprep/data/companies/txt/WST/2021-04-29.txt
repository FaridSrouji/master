WEST ANNOUNCES FIRST-QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. EXTON, PA., APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEST PHARMACEUTICAL SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: WST) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST-QUARTER 2021 AND UPDATED FULL-YEAR 2021 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE. FIRST-QUARTER 2021 SUMMARY (COMPARISONS TO PRIOR-YEAR PERIOD) NET SALES OF $670.7 MILLION GREW 36.5%; ORGANIC SALES GROWTH WAS 31.1%.
