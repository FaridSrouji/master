WEST INTRODUCES DELTACUBE™ MODELING PLATFORM AND NOVAGUARD® SA PRO SAFETY SYSTEM FOR 0.5 ML STANDARD GLASS SYRINGES AT INTERPHEX. EXTON, PA., OCT. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEST PHARMACEUTICAL SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: WST), A GLOBAL LEADER IN INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS FOR INJECTABLE DRUG ADMINISTRATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL UNVEIL ITS DELTACUBE™ MODELING PLATFORM AS WELL AS THE NEW NOVAGUARD® SA PRO SAFETY SYSTEM FOR ISO 0.5ML STANDARD GLASS SYRINGES AT INTERPHEX, TAKING PLACE OCTOBER 19 TO 21 IN NEW YORK, NEW YORK. THE DELTACUBE™ MODELING PLATFORM IS AN ONLINE VIAL INTEGRITY MODELING PLATFORM DESIGNED TO HELP DEVELOPERS MAKE MORE CONFIDENT DECISIONS ABOUT THEIR CONTAINER CLOSURE SYSTEMS.
