WEST ANNOUNCES THIRD-QUARTER 2021 RESULTS AND DECLARES FOURTH-QUARTER 2021 DIVIDEND. EXTON, PA., OCT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEST PHARMACEUTICAL SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: WST) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE THIRD-QUARTER 2021 AND UPDATED FULL-YEAR 2021 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE. THIRD-QUARTER 2021 SUMMARY (COMPARISONS TO PRIOR-YEAR PERIOD) NET SALES OF $706.5 MILLION GREW 28.9%; ORGANIC SALES GROWTH WAS 27.9%.
