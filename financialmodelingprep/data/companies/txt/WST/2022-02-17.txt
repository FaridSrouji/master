(NYSE: WST) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2021 AND INTRODUCED FULL-YEAR 2022 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE. FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2021 SUMMARY (COMPARISONS TO PRIOR-YEAR PERIOD) FOURTH-QUARTER 2021 NET SALES OF $730.8 MILLION GREW 26.0%; ORGANIC SALES GROWTH WAS 28.3%.
