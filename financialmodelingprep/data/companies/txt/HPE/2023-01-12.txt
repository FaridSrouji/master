HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE ACQUIRES PACHYDERM TO EXPAND AI-AT-SCALE CAPABILITIES WITH REPRODUCIBLE AI. HOUSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE (NYSE: HPE) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN EXPANSION TO ITS AI-AT-SCALE OFFERINGS WITH THE ACQUISITION OF PACHYDERM, A STARTUP THAT DELIVERS SOFTWARE, BASED ON OPEN-SOURCE TECHNOLOGY, TO AUTOMATE REPRODUCIBLE MACHINE LEARNING PIPELINES THAT TARGET LARGE-SCALE AI APPLICATIONS.
