HPE GREENLAKE SELECTED TO EXPAND CORE CLOUD OFFERING AND ENHANCE DESKTOP-AS-A-SERVICE FOR CDW SERVICEWORKS. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE (NYSE: HPE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CDW, A LEADING GLOBAL MANAGED SERVICE PROVIDER, HAS SELECTED THE HPE GREENLAKE EDGE-TO-CLOUD PLATFORM TO ENHANCE ITS CORE UK CLOUD SUITE OF PRODUCTS CALLED SERVICEWORKS. HPE GREENLAKE WILL ENABLE SERVICEWORKS TO MEET INCREASING DEMAND, ACCELERATE DEPLOYMENT OF NEW SERVICES AND IMPROVE OVERALL CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE FOR ITS DESKTOP-AS-A-SERVICE PLATFORM.
