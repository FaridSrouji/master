KAUST SELECTS HPE TO BUILD THE MIDDLE EAST'S MOST POWERFUL SUPERCOMPUTER. RIYADH, SAUDI ARABIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE (NYSE: HPE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KING ABDULLAH UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (KAUST) SELECTED HPE TO BUILD ITS NEXT-GENERATION SUPERCOMPUTER, SHAHEEN III, TO DELIVER STATE-OF-THE-ART SUPERCOMPUTING AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) CAPABILITIES FOR ADVANCING RESEARCH IN FIELDS SUCH AS FOOD, WATER, ENERGY AND THE ENVIRONMENT.
