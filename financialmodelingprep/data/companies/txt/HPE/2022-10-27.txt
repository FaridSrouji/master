METEOROLOGICAL SERVICE SINGAPORE ADVANCES WEATHER FORECASTING WITH NEW SUPERCOMPUTER FROM HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE. SINGAPORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE (NYSE: HPE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BUILT A NEW SUPERCOMPUTER FOR THE METEOROLOGICAL SERVICE SINGAPORE (MSS) TO ADVANCE WEATHER FORECASTING AND TROPICAL CLIMATE RESEARCH FOR SINGAPORE AND THE BROADER SOUTHEAST ASIA REGION.
