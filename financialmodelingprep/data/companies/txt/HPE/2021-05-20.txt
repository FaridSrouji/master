ARUBA AND ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES DELIVER UNMATCHED VISIBILITY INTO MOBILE USER EXPERIENCES, APPLICATION PERFORMANCE, AND NETWORK OPERATIONS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #ARUBAESP--ARUBA ANNOUNCED THE AVAILABILITY OF A SOLUTION THAT COMBINES ARUBA UXI ON ZEBRA MOBILE COMPUTERS TO DELIVER UNMATCHED VISIBILITY AT THE EDGE.
