KDDI ADVANCES O-RAN COMPLIANT 5G STANDALONE VIRTUALIZED BASE STATIONS IN JAPAN WITH HPE TELCO INFRASTRUCTURE. TOKYO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE (NYSE: HPE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KDDI, A LEADING JAPANESE TELECOMMUNICATIONS PROVIDER, IS USING THE HPE PROLIANT DL110 GEN10 PLUS - TELCO SERVER FOR THE COMMERCIAL NETWORK OPERATION OF O-RAN COMPLIANT 5G STANDALONE BASE STATIONS. KDDI IS ACCELERATING CONSTRUCTION OF 5G BASE STATIONS ACROSS JAPAN IN EFFORTS TO EXPEDITE 5G AVAILABILITY.
