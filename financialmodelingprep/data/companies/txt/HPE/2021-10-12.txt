HPE ADVANCES ENI'S HPC4 SUPERCOMPUTER TO BOLSTER RESEARCH FOR NEW ENERGY SOURCES WITH HPE GREENLAKE. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE (NYSE: HPE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE UPGRADE OF ENI'S EXISTING SUPERCOMPUTER SYSTEM, HPC4, TO ACCELERATE DISCOVERY OF NEW ENERGY SOURCES. THE NEW SUPERCOMPUTER, DELIVERED AS A SERVICE THROUGH THE HPE GREENLAKE EDGE-TO-CLOUD PLATFORM, WILL INCREASE HPC4 PERFORMANCE, IMPROVE ITS COMPUTING CAPACITY WHEN RUNNING SIMULATIONS, AND DOUBLE STORAGE CAPACITY TO IMPROVE ACCURACY OF IMAGE-INTENSIVE MODELING AND SIMULATIONS OF COMPLEX ENERGY RESEARCH.
