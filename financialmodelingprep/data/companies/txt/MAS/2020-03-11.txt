MASCO CORP ANNOUNCES $350 MLN ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE. MASCO CORPORATION ANNOUNCES $350 MILLION ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE.MASCO - ENTERED INTO ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH HSBC BANK USA, NATIONAL ASSOCIATION TO REPURCHASE $350 MILLION OF CO'S STOCK.MASCO - UNDER ASR AGREEMENT, CO WILL RECEIVE INITIAL DELIVERY OF 7.3 MILLION SHARES ON MARCH 11.MASCO -  FINAL NUMBER OF SHARES TO BE REPURCHASED EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED NO LATER THAN MAY 8.
