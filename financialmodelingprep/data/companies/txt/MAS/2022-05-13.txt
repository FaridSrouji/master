MASCO CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. LIVONIA, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MASCO CORPORATION (NYSE: MAS) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $.28 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON JUNE 13, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 27, 2022. HEADQUARTERED IN LIVONIA, MICHIGAN, MASCO CORPORATION IS A GLOBAL LEADER IN THE DESIGN, MANUFACTURE AND DISTRIBUTION OF BRANDED HOME IMPROVEMENT AND BUILDING PRODUCTS.
