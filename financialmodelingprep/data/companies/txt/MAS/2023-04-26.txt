MASCO CORPORATION REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS. LIVONIA, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MASCO CORPORATION (NYSE: MAS), ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING MANUFACTURERS OF BRANDED HOME IMPROVEMENT AND BUILDING PRODUCTS, REPORTED ITS FIRST QUARTER RESULTS. 2023 FIRST QUARTER RESULTS ON A REPORTED BASIS, COMPARED TO FIRST QUARTER 2022: NET SALES DECREASED 10 PERCENT TO $1,979 MILLION; IN LOCAL CURRENCY, NET SALES DECREASED 9 PERCENT IN LOCAL CURRENCY, NORTH AMERICAN SALES DECREASED 10 PERCENT AND INTERNATIONAL SALES DECREASED 3 PERCENT GROSS MARGIN INCREASED.
