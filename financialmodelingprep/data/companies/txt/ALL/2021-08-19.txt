ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES JULY 2021 CATASTROPHE LOSSES. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY ANNOUNCED ESTIMATED CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR THE MONTH OF JULY OF $227 MILLION OR $179 MILLION, AFTER-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES IN JULY COMPRISED 18 EVENTS AT AN ESTIMATED COST OF $211 MILLION PLUS UNFAVORABLE PRIOR PERIOD RESERVE ESTIMATES.
