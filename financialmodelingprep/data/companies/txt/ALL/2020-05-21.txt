ALLSTATE CORP REPORTS ESTIMATED APRIL CATASTROPHE LOSSES OF $632 MILLION. ALLSTATE CORP - ESTIMATED APRIL CATASTROPHE LOSSES OF $632 MILLION, PRE-TAX ($499 MILLION, AFTER-TAX).ALLSTATE - ABOUT 55% OF ESTIMATED APRIL CATASTROPHE LOSSES WERE DUE TO 2 SEVERE WIND, HAIL EVENTS IMPACTING ROUGHLY HALF OF STATES.
