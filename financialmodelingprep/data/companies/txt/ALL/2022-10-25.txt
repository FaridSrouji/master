ALLSTATE SELECTS VOYA INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT FOR PORTFOLIO MANDATE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VOYA INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT, THE ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF VOYA FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: VOYA), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WAS SELECTED BY ALLSTATE (NYSE: ALL) TO MANAGE THE FIRM'S $850 MILLION COMMERCIAL MORTGAGE PORTFOLIO EFFECTIVE NOV. 14, 2022.
