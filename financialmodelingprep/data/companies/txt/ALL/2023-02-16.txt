ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES JANUARY 2023 CATASTROPHE LOSSES AND IMPLEMENTED AUTO RATES. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY ANNOUNCED ESTIMATED CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR THE MONTH OF JANUARY OF $307 MILLION OR $243 MILLION, AFTER-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR JANUARY EVENTS WERE ESTIMATED AT $309 MILLION, RELATED TO NINE EVENTS PRIMARILY IN TEXAS AND CALIFORNIA, PARTIALLY OFFSET BY FAVORABLE RESERVE REESTIMATES FOR PRIOR EVENTS.
