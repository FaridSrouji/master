ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES AUGUST 2021 CATASTROPHE LOSSES. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY ANNOUNCED ESTIMATED CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR THE MONTH OF AUGUST OF $876 MILLION OR $692 MILLION, AFTER-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR JULY AND AUGUST TOTALED $1.1 BILLION, PRE-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES IN AUGUST COMPRISED 11 EVENTS AT AN ESTIMATED COST OF $843 MILLION PLUS UNFAVORABLE PRIOR PERIOD RESERVE REESTIMATES.
