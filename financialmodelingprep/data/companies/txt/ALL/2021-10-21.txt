ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES SEPTEMBER 2021 AND THIRD QUARTER 2021 CATASTROPHE LOSSES AND RUN-OFF RESERVE REVIEW. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY ANNOUNCED ESTIMATED CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR THE MONTH OF SEPTEMBER OF $165 MILLION OR $130 MILLION, AFTER-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR THE THIRD QUARTER TOTALED $1.3 BILLION, PRE-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES IN SEPTEMBER WERE ESTIMATED AT $103 MILLION, PLUS REINSURANCE REINSTATEMENT PREMIUM AND MODEST UNFAVORABLE RESERVE REESTIMATES.
