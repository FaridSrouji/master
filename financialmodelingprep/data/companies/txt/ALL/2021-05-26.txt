ALLSTATE PUBLISHES 19TH SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. NORTHBROOK, ILL., MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) HAS PUBLISHED ITS 19TH SUSTAINABILITY REPORT, HIGHLIGHTING PROGRESS IN ADDRESSING CLIMATE CHANGE, IMPROVING INCLUSIVE DIVERSITY & EQUITY, ENHANCING CULTURE AND RESPONDING TO THE PANDEMIC.
