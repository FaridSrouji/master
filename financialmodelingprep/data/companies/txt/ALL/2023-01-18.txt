ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES ESTIMATED FOURTH QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY ANNOUNCED PRELIMINARY RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022, WITH AN ESTIMATED NET LOSS BETWEEN $285 MILLION AND $335 MILLION AND ESTIMATED ADJUSTED NET LOSS* BETWEEN $335 MILLION AND $385 MILLION.
