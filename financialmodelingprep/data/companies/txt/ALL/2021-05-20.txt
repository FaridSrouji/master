ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES APRIL 2021 CATASTROPHE LOSSES. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY ANNOUNCED ESTIMATED CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR THE MONTH OF APRIL OF $544 MILLION, $430 MILLION AFTER-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES IN APRIL COMPRISED FIVE EVENTS AT AN ESTIMATED COST OF $490 MILLION PLUS UNFAVORABLE PRIOR PERIOD RESERVE ESTIMATES. ONE LARGE HAIL EVENT, PRIMARILY IMPACTING TEXAS AND OKLAHOMA, ACCOUNTED FOR APPROXIMATELY 60% OF APRIL ESTIMATED CATASTROPHE LOSSES. FINANCIAL INFORMATION, INCLUDING MATERIAL ANNOUNC
