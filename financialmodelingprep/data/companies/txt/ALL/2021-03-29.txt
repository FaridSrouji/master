ALLSTATE COMPLETES EXIT OF LIFE AND ANNUITY BUSINESSES. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) HAS AGREED TO A TRANSACTION IN WHICH WILTON RE WILL PAY $220 MILLION TO ACQUIRE ALLSTATE LIFE INSURANCE COMPANY OF NEW YORK (ALNY). THE TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN THE SECOND HALF OF 2021, SUBJECT TO REGULATORY APPROVAL AND OTHER CLOSING CONDITIONS.
