ALLSTATE: REPLACEMENTS OF CATALYTIC CONVERTERS UP NEARLY 1,200% SINCE 2019. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE LATEST NUMBERS FROM ALLSTATE SHOW THAT CATALYTIC CONVERTER REPLACEMENTS AMONG ITS CUSTOMERS GREW 1,155% NATIONALLY BETWEEN 2019 AND 2022. YOU READ THAT RIGHT: OVER A THOUSAND PERCENT! AND ALLSTATE IS NOT ALONE IN THIS EPIDEMIC. ACCORDING TO THE NATIONAL INSURANCE CRIME BUREAU, AN INSURANCE INDUSTRY TRADE ORGANIZATION, CATALYTIC CONVERTER THEFTS NATIONWIDE INCREASED 1,215% BETWEEN 2019 AND 2022. IN SEVERAL STATES, ALLSTATE SAW REPLACEMENTS SOAR WELL BEYOND.
