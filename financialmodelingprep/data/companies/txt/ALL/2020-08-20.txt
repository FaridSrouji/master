ALLSTATE REPORTS JULY CATASTROPHE PRE-TAX LOSSES OF $145 MILLION. ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES IMPACT OF PG&E RECOVERIES AND JULY 2020 CATASTROPHE LOSSES.ALLSTATE - CATASTROPHE RESULTS FOR JULY GENERATED INCOME OF $334 MILLION, PRE-TAX AS PG&E SUBROGATION RECOVERIES MORE THAN OFFSET IMPACT OF EVENTS IN MONTH.ALLSTATE - LOSSES FROM JULY CATASTROPHE EVENTS $145 MILLION, PRE-TAX, INCLUDING HURRICANE HANNA AND TWO SEVERE WIND AND HAIL WEATHER EVENTS.
