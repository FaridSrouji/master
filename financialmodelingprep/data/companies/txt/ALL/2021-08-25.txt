ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY DECLARED APPROXIMATELY $26.3 MILLION IN AGGREGATE DIVIDENDS ON THREE SERIES OF PREFERRED STOCK FOR THE DIVIDEND PERIOD FROM JULY 15, 2021, THROUGH OCT. 14, 2021.
