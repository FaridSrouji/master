ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES DECEMBER 2021 AND FOURTH QUARTER 2021 CATASTROPHE LOSSES. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY ANNOUNCED ESTIMATED CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR THE MONTH OF DECEMBER OF $411 MILLION OR $325 MILLION, AFTER-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER TOTALED $528 MILLION, PRE-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR DECEMBER EVENTS WERE ESTIMATED AT $381 MILLION, PLUS UNFAVORABLE RESERVE REESTIMATES.
