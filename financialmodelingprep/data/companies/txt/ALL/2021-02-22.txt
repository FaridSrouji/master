ALLSTATE INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 50%. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.81 CENTS ON EACH OUTSTANDING SHARE OF THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE IN CASH ON APRIL 1, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 4, 2021. THIS REPRESENTS A 50% INCREASE FROM THE DIVIDEND DECLARED IN THE PREVIOUS QUARTER. ALLSTATE'S HISTORY OF EXCELLENT OPERATING RESULTS HAS SUPPORTED INVESTMENTS IN GROWTH AND CASH RETURNS TO SHAREH
