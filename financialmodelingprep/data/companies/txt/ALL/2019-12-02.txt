THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 15, 2020. THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION DECLARED APPROXIMATELY $26.8 MILLION IN AGGREGATE DIVIDENDS ON THREE SERIES OF PREFERRED STOCK FOR THE DIVIDEND PERIOD FROM OCT. 15, 2019, THROUGH JAN. 14, 2020. ALL THE PREFERRED DIVIDENDS ARE PAYABLE IN CASH ON JAN. 15, 2020, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DEC. 31, 2019, AS: SERIES A AND SERIES G AT $0.3515625 PER SHARE, SERIES H AT $0.3187500. THE COMPANY ALSO DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF APPROXIMATELY $2.7 MILLION AT AN ANNUAL DIVIDEND RATE EQUAL TO 4.75% ON ITS SERIES I PREFERRED STOCK FOR THE DIVIDEND PERIOD FROM NOV. 8, 2019, THROUGH JAN. 14, 2020. ACCORDINGLY, HOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $0.2210069 PER DEPOSITARY SHARE. THIS SERIES I PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE IN CASH ON JAN. 15, 2020, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DEC. 31, 2019.
