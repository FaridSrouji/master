ALLSTATE ANNOUNCES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY DECLARED APPROXIMATELY $26.3 MILLION IN AGGREGATE DIVIDENDS ON THREE SERIES OF PREFERRED STOCK FOR THE DIVIDEND PERIOD FROM JAN. 15, 2021, THROUGH APRIL 14, 2021. ALL THE PREFERRED DIVIDENDS ARE PAYABLE IN CASH ON APRIL 15, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 31, 2021, AS FOLLOWS:   SERIES ANNUAL DIVIDEND RATE QUARTERLY AMOUNT PER DEPOSITARY SHARE SERIES G 5.625% $0.3515625 SERIES H 5.100
