EQUITY RESIDENTIAL DECLARES SECOND QUARTER DIVIDENDS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EQUITY RESIDENTIAL (NYSE: EQR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF TRUSTEES DECLARED QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON AND PREFERRED SHARES. A REGULAR COMMON SHARE DIVIDEND FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF $0.625 PER SHARE WILL BE PAID ON JULY 8, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 27, 2022. A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.03625 PER SHARE WILL BE PAID ON JUNE 30, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 17, 2022 OF THE COMPANY'S SERIES K PREFERRED SHARES. ABOUT EQUITY RES
