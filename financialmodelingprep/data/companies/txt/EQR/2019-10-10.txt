THE DINERSTEIN CO., INC. AND HARRISON STREET REAL ESTATE CAPITAL, LLC ACQUIRED BERKELEY APARTMENT PORTFOLIO FROM EQUITY RESIDENTIAL (NYSE:EQR).. THE DINERSTEIN CO., INC. AND HARRISON STREET REAL ESTATE CAPITAL, LLC ACQUIRED BERKELEY APARTMENT PORTFOLIO FROM EQUITY RESIDENTIAL (NYSE:EQR) ON OCTOBER 11, 2019. THE PORTFOLIO CONSISTS OF STERLING ADDISON, STERLING ALLSTON, STERLING HASTE , STERLING JEFFERSON, STERLING OXFORD, STERLING UNIVERSITY AVE. THE PORTFOLIO IS REBRANDED AS STERLING BERKELEY COLLECTION AND WILL BECOME A COMPONENT OF STERLING HOUSING, THE STUDENT HOUSING DIVISION OF THE DINERSTEIN CO., INC.  JOSEPH SMOLEN OF EASTDIL SECURED BROKERED THE DEAL.
