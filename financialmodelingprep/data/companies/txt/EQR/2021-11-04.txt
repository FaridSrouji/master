EQUITY RESIDENTIAL EXPANDS PARTNERSHIP WITH ANYONE HOME. LAKE FOREST, CALIF., NOV. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  ANYONE HOME , THE INDUSTRY-LEADING PROVIDER OF TECHNOLOGY THAT HELPS RENTAL-HOUSING OPERATORS ENGAGE AND CONNECT WITH RESIDENTS AND PROSPECTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A NATIONAL PARTNERSHIP WITH CHICAGO-BASED  EQUITY RESIDENTIAL (NYSE: EQR) AS ITS PORTFOLIO-WIDE CRM SOLUTION.
