COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS SEPTEMBER SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., OCT. 05, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $21.46 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF SEPTEMBER, THE FIVE WEEKS ENDED OCTOBER 2, 2022, AN INCREASE OF 10.1 PERCENT FROM $19.50 BILLION LAST YEAR.
