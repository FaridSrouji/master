COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. ISSAQUAH, WASH., OCT. 12, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COSTCO COMMON STOCK OF 90 CENTS PER SHARE. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE NOVEMBER 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON OCTOBER 28, 2022.
