COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL YEAR 2021 OPERATING RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., SEPT. 23, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE 16-WEEK FOURTH QUARTER AND THE 52-WEEK FISCAL YEAR, ENDED AUGUST 29, 2021.
