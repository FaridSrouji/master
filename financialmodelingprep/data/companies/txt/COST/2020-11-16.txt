COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION DECLARES SPECIAL CASH DIVIDEND OF $10 PER SHARE. ISSAQUAH, WASH., NOV. 16, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A SPECIAL CASH DIVIDEND ON COSTCO COMMON STOCK OF $10 PER SHARE, PAYABLE DECEMBER 11, 2020, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 2, 2020. THE AGGREGATE PAYMENT WILL BE APPROXIMATELY $4.4 BILLION. THE SPECIAL DIVIDEND WILL BE FUNDED THROUGH EXISTING CASH.
