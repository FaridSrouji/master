COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION ACQUIRES MINORITY INTEREST IN COSTCO-TAIWAN. ISSAQUAH, WASH., JUNE 30, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) HAS COMPLETED A PURCHASE FOR $1.05 BILLION BY A WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF THE 45% MINORITY INTEREST IN COSTCO-TAIWAN, A JOINT VENTURE, FROM OUR LONG-TIME JOINT VENTURE PARTNERS. THE COMPANY NOW INDIRECTLY OWNS ALL OF COSTCO TAIWAN. WE ESTIMATE THAT THE PURCHASE WILL BE APPROXIMATELY ONE TO ONE AND ONE-HALF PERCENT ACCRETIVE TO EARNINGS PER SHARE.
