COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS AUGUST SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., SEPT. 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $15.75 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF AUGUST, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED AUGUST 29, 2021, AN INCREASE OF 16.2 PERCENT FROM $13.56 BILLION LAST YEAR.
