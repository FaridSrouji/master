COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS JANUARY SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., FEB. 02, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $15.76 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF JANUARY, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED JANUARY 30, 2022, AN INCREASE OF 15.5 PERCENT FROM $13.64 BILLION LAST YEAR.
