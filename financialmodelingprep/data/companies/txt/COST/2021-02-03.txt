COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS JANUARY SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., FEB. 03, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $13.64 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF JANUARY, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED JANUARY 31, 2021, AN INCREASE OF 17.9 PERCENT FROM $11.57 BILLION LAST YEAR.
