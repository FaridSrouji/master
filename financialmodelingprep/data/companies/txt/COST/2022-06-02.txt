COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS MAY SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., JUNE 02, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $18.23 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF MAY, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED MAY 29, 2022, AN INCREASE OF 16.9 PERCENT FROM $15.59 BILLION LAST YEAR.
