COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. ISSAQUAH, WASH., JULY 13, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COSTCO COMMON STOCK OF 79 CENTS PER SHARE. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE AUGUST 13, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 30, 2021.
