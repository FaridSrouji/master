COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS APRIL SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., MAY 05, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $15.21 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF APRIL, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED MAY 2, 2021, AN INCREASE OF 33.5 PERCENT FROM $11.39 BILLION LAST YEAR.
