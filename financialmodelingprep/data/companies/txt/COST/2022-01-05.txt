COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS DECEMBER SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., JAN. 05, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $22.24 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF DECEMBER, THE FIVE WEEKS ENDED JANUARY 2, 2022, AN INCREASE OF 16.2 PERCENT FROM $19.14 BILLION LAST YEAR.
