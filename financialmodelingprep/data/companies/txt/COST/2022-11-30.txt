COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS NOVEMBER SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., NOV. 30, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $19.17 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF NOVEMBER, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED NOVEMBER 27, 2022, AN INCREASE OF 5.7 PERCENT FROM $18.13 BILLION LAST YEAR.
