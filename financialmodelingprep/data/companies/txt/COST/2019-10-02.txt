COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 1, 2019. COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 1, 2019. FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 47,498 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 44,411 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 1,463 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,446 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 1,097 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,043 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.49 COMPARED TO USD 2.38 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.47 COMPARED TO USD 2.36 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE FULL YEAR, TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 152,703 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 141,576 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 4,737 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 4,480 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 3,659 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 3,134 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 8.32 COMPARED TO USD 7.15 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 8.26 COMPARED TO USD 7.09 A YEAR AGO.
