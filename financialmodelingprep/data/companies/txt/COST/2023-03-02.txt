COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS SECOND QUARTER AND YEAR-TO-DATE OPERATING RESULTS FOR FISCAL 2023 AND FEBRUARY SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., MARCH 02, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER (TWELVE WEEKS) AND THE FIRST 24 WEEKS OF FISCAL 2023, ENDED FEBRUARY 12, 2023.
