COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS DECEMBER SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., JAN. 05, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $23.80 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF DECEMBER, THE FIVE WEEKS ENDED JANUARY 1, 2023, AN INCREASE OF 7.0 PERCENT FROM $22.24 BILLION LAST YEAR.
