UBER AND COSTCO KICK OFF GROCERY DELIVERY PILOT IN TEXAS. DALLAS, JULY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UBER TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: UBER) AND COSTCO (NASDAQ: COST) HAVE ENTERED A DELIVERY PILOT WITH 25 LOCATIONS ACROSS THE STATE OF TEXAS WHERE COSTCO MEMBERS COULD HAVE THEIR GROCERIES DELIVERED WITHIN HOURS, IF NOT MINUTES, WITH UBER AND UBER EATS. OVER THE COMING WEEKS, 7 ADDITIONAL COSTCO WAREHOUSES WILL BE AVAILABLE TO UBER USERS VIA UBER AND UBER EATS MOBILE APPS.
