COSTCO WHOLESALE REPORTS NET SALES OF $17.04 BLN FOR RETAIL MONTH OF DEC. COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS DECEMBER SALES RESULTS.COSTCO - NET SALES OF $17.04 BILLION FOR RETAIL MONTH OF DEC, FIVE WEEKS ENDED JANUARY 5, 2020, AN INCREASE OF 10.5 PERCENT FROM $15.42 BILLION LAST YEAR.COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP - TOTAL AND COMPARABLE SALES FOR DEC WERE POSITIVELY IMPACTED BY ABOUT ONE PERCENT.COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP - DEC U.S. COMPARABLE SALES UP 9.4%.COSTCO WHOLESALE - E-COMMERCE SALES IN DEC WERE POSITIVELY IMPACTED BY ESTIMATED 20 PERCENTAGE POINTS.COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP - TOTAL COMPANY DEC COMPARABLE SALES UP 9.0%.COSTCO - COMPARABLE SALES EXCLUDING IMPACTS FROM CHANGES IN GASOLINE PRICES AND FOREIGN EXCHANGE WERE UP 7.8% IN DEC.
