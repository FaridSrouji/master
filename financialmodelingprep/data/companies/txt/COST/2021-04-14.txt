COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES AN INCREASE IN ITS QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. ISSAQUAH, WASH., APRIL 14, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COSTCO COMMON STOCK AND APPROVED A QUARTERLY INCREASE FROM 70 TO 79 CENTS PER SHARE, OR $3.16 ON AN ANNUALIZED BASIS. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MAY 14, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 30, 2021.
