COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS MARCH SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., APRIL 05, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $21.71 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF MARCH, THE FIVE WEEKS ENDED APRIL 2, 2023, AN INCREASE OF 0.5 PERCENT FROM $21.61 BILLION LAST YEAR.
