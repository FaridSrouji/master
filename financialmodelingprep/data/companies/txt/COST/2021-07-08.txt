COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS JUNE SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., JULY 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $18.92 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF JUNE, THE FIVE WEEKS ENDED JULY 4, 2021, AN INCREASE OF 16.9 PERCENT FROM $16.18 BILLION LAST YEAR.
