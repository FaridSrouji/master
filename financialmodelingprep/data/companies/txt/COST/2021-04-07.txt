COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS MARCH SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., APRIL 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $18.21 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF MARCH, THE FIVE WEEKS ENDED APRIL 4, 2021, AN INCREASE OF 17.6 PERCENT FROM $15.49 BILLION LAST YEAR.
