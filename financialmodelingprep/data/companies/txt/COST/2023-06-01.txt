COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS MAY SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., JUNE 01, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $18.45 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF MAY, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED MAY 28, 2023, AN INCREASE OF 1.2 PERCENT FROM $18.23 BILLION LAST YEAR.
