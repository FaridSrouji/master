COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES REAUTHORIZATION OF STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM AND QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. ISSAQUAH, WASH., JAN. 19, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS REAUTHORIZED A COMMON STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM OF UP TO $4 BILLION. THIS PROGRAM WILL EXPIRE IN JANUARY 2027 AND REPLACES THE CURRENT $4 BILLION PROGRAM (ADOPTED IN 2019 AND SCHEDULED TO EXPIRE APRIL 2023), UNDER WHICH APPROXIMATELY $1.4 BILLION HAD BEEN PURCHASED. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS ALSO DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COSTCO COMMON STOCK OF 90 CENTS PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE FEBRUARY 17, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 3, 2023.
