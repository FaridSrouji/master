COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. ISSAQUAH, WASH., JAN. 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COSTCO COMMON STOCK OF 70 CENTS PER SHARE. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE FEBRUARY 19, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 5, 2021.
