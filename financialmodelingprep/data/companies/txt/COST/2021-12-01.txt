COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS NOVEMBER SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., DEC. 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $18.13 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF NOVEMBER, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED NOVEMBER 28, 2021, AN INCREASE OF 15.7 PERCENT FROM $15.67 BILLION LAST YEAR.
