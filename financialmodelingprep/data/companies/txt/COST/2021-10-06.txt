COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS SEPTEMBER SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., OCT. 06, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $19.50 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF SEPTEMBER, THE FIVE WEEKS ENDED OCTOBER 3, 2021, AN INCREASE OF 15.8 PERCENT FROM $16.84 BILLION LAST YEAR.
