COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS NOVEMBER AND FIRST QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2021 SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., DEC. 02, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $15.67 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF NOVEMBER, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED NOVEMBER 29, 2020, AN INCREASE OF 15.1 PERCENT FROM $13.62 BILLION LAST YEAR.
