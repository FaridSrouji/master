COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS AUGUST SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., AUG. 31, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $17.55 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF AUGUST, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED AUGUST 28, 2022, AN INCREASE OF 11.4 PERCENT FROM $15.75 BILLION LAST YEAR.
