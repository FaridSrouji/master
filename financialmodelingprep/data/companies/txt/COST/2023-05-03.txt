COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS APRIL SALES RESULTS. ISSAQUAH, WASH., MAY 03, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $17.85 BILLION FOR THE RETAIL MONTH OF APRIL, THE FOUR WEEKS ENDED APRIL 30, 2023, AN INCREASE OF 3.0 PERCENT FROM $17.33 BILLION LAST YEAR.
