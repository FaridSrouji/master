MONSTER BEVERAGE ANNOUNCES WEBCAST DETAILS FOR ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS. CORONA, CALIF., JUNE 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONSTER BEVERAGE CORPORATION (NASDAQ:MNST) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY WILL HOST A LIVE WEBCAST OF ITS ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS ON TUESDAY, JUNE 15, 2021. THE LIVE WEBCAST WILL START AT 2:30 P.M. PACIFIC TIME.
