EVERGY PARTNERS WITH ERICSSON AND BURNS & MCDONNELL TO TURN ON PRIVATE NETWORK IN RECORD TIME. PLANO, TEXAS , OCT. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EVERGY (NYSE: EVRG), ERICSSON (NASDAQ: ERIC) AND BURNS & MCDONNELL HAVE LAUNCHED A NEW PRIVATE LTE (PLTE) CORE AND RADIO ACCESS NETWORK (RAN) TO HELP ENABLE GRID MODERNIZATION EFFORTS AT EVERGY, A U.S. INVESTOR-OWNED UTILITY, IN RECORD TIME. THE PROJECT CREATED ONE UNIFIED NETWORK CAPABLE OF SUPPORTING AN ARRAY OF BOTH KNOWN AND FUTURE-FOCUSED USE CASES TO MODERNIZE THE GRID, INCREASING RELIABILITY AND SAFETY WHILE DECREASING LATENCY FOR MISSION-CRITICAL APPLICATIONS.
