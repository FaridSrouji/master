SCOUT CLEAN ENERGY CLOSES ON PERSIMMON CREEK WIND FARM SALE TO EVERGY. 199MW WIND FARM CLOSING MARKS SIX-MONTHS UNDER BROOKFIELD RENEWABLE OWNERSHIP BOULDER, COLO. , MAY 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SCOUT CLEAN ENERGY (SCOUT), A COLORADO BASED RENEWABLE ENERGY DEVELOPER-OWNER-OPERATOR, AND ITS JOINT VENTURE PARTNER ELAWAN ENERGY, A GLOBAL OPERATOR IN THE RENEWABLE ENERGY INDUSTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE COMPLETED THE SALE OF THE 199-MEGAWATT (MW) PERSIMMON CREEK WIND FARM (PERSIMMON) IN WESTERN OKLAHOMA TO EVERGY, INC. (NASDAQ: EVRG).
