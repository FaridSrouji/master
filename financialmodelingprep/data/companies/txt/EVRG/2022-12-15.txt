EVERGY ANNOUNCES DEC. 28, 2022, TRANSFER TO NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. KANSAS CITY, MO.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--EVERGY, INC. (NYSE: EVRG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL TRANSFER ITS STOCK EXCHANGE LISTING FROM THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE (NYSE) TO THE NASDAQ GLOBAL SELECT MARKET, EFFECTIVE AS OF THE OPENING OF TRADING ON WEDNESDAY, DEC. 28, 2022. THE LAST DAY OF TRADING ON THE NYSE WILL BE TUESDAY, DEC. 27, 2022. EVERGY'S COMMON STOCK WILL CONTINUE TO TRADE UNDER THE TICKER SYMBOL “EVRG.”
