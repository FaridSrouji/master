EVERGY ANNOUNCES FIRST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS, DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AND REAFFIRMS 2023 GUIDANCE. KANSAS CITY, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EVERGY, INC. (NASDAQ: EVRG) TODAY ANNOUNCED FIRST QUARTER 2023 GAAP EARNINGS OF $143 MILLION, OR $0.62 PER SHARE, COMPARED TO GAAP EARNINGS OF $123 MILLION, OR $0.53 PER SHARE, FOR FIRST QUARTER 2022. EVERGY'S FIRST QUARTER 2023 ADJUSTED EARNINGS (NON-GAAP) AND ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (NON-GAAP) WERE $136 MILLION AND $0.59, RESPECTIVELY, COMPARED TO $130 MILLION AND $0.56 IN 2022.
