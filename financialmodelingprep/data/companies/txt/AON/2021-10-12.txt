AON DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DUBLIN, OCT. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AON PLC (NYSE: AON), A LEADING GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.51 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING CLASS A ORDINARY SHARES. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE NOVEMBER 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 1, 2021.
