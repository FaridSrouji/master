AON ANNOUNCES LAST MILE DELIVERY INSURANCE PROGRAM IN PARTNERSHIP WITH CARRIERHQ. INDIANAPOLIS, NOV. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AON PLC , THE LEADING GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM PROVIDING A BROAD RANGE OF RISK, RETIREMENT, AND HEALTH SOLUTIONS, AND CARRIERHQ, AN INDIANAPOLIS-BASED INSURTECH FIRM FOCUSING ON USAGE BASED COMMERCIAL INSURANCE PROGRAMS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THEIR LAST MILE DELIVERY INSURANCE PROGRAM. THE PROGRAM, A FIRST OF ITS KIND, PROVIDES OPERATORS IN THE LAST MILE DELIVERY SPACE WITH CUSTOM, AFFORDABLE, AND CONTINUOUS COVERAGE SOLUTIONS ELIMINATING THE UNCERTAINTY OF FREQUENT POLICY RENEWALS WHILE ALSO PROVIDING MUCH-NEEDED FLEXIBILITY BY ONLY CHARGING A PREMIUM WHEN VEHICLES ARE IN USE.
