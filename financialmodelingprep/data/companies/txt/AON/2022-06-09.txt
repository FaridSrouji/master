TCDI SIGNS AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE AON'S EDISCOVERY PRACTICE. GREENSBORO, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #ACQUISITION--TCDI, A LEADING PROVIDER OF LEGAL SERVICES, SOFTWARE AND CYBERSECURITY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM AON'S EDISCOVERY PRACTICE. THE ACQUISITION WILL STRENGTHEN TCDI'S EXISTING EDISCOVERY OFFERINGS, ADDING EXPANSIVE ADVISORY SERVICES SUCH AS INFORMATION GOVERNANCE TO ITS COMPREHENSIVE LEGAL SOLUTIONS.
