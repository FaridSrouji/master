AON: U.S. EMPLOYER HEALTH CARE COSTS PROJECTED TO INCREASE 6.5 PERCENT NEXT YEAR. CHICAGO , AUG. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A VERAGE COSTS FOR U.S. EMPLOYERS THAT PAY FOR THEIR EMPLOYEES' HEALTH CARE WILL INCREASE 6.5 PERCENT TO MORE THAN $13,800 PER EMPLOYEE IN 2023, ACCORDING TO AON (NYSE: AON), A LEADING GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM. THIS PROJECTION IS MORE THAN DOUBLE THE 3 PERCENT INCREASE TO HEALTH CARE BUDGETS WHICH EMPLOYERS EXPERIENCED FROM 2021 TO 2022; BUT IS SIGNIFICANTLY BELOW THE 9.1 INFLATION FIGURE REPORTED THROUGH THE CONSUMER PRICE INDEX.
