TCDI COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF AON'S EDISCOVERY PRACTICE. GREENSBORO, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TCDI, A LEADING PROVIDER OF LEGAL SERVICES, SOFTWARE AND CYBERSECURITY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM AON'S EDISCOVERY PRACTICE. WITH THE CLOSING OF THE DEAL, TCDI ADDS EXPANSIVE ADVISORY SERVICES SUCH AS INFORMATION GOVERNANCE AS WELL AS THE NOMAD MOBILE PROCESSING PLATFORM, PHI/PII DETECTION AND POST-DATA-BREACH SUPPORT TOOLS.
