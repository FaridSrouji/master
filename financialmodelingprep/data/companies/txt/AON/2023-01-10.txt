AON DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. DUBLIN , JAN. 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AON PLC (NYSE: AON), A LEADING GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.56 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING CLASS A ORDINARY SHARES. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE FEBRUARY 15, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 1, 2023.
