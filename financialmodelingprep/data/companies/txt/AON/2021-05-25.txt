AON'S NEW VACCINE SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTION TO BENEFIT RESPONSE FUND FOR THE WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION. CHICAGO, MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AON PLC  (NYSE: AON), A LEADING GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM PROVIDING A BROAD RANGE OF RISK, RETIREMENT AND HEALTH SOLUTIONS, HAS ANNOUNCED IT WILL DONATE 100 PERCENT OF ALL 2021 REVENUES EARNED FROM ITS NEW VACCINE SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTION TO THE COVID-19 SOLIDARITY RESPONSE FUND , POWERED BY THE WHO FOUNDATION AND THE UNITED NATIONS FOUNDATION IN SUPPORT OF THE WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION (WHO). AON COMMITS TO DONATING A MINIMUM OF $100,000.
