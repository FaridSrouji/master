ANONOS SECURES $50 MILLION IN IP-BACKED FINANCING TO DELIVER DATA PRIVACY TECHNOLOGY WITH 100% ACCURACY AND UTILITY TO DATA-DRIVEN ENTERPRISES. NEW YORK & BRUSSELS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ANONOS, PROVIDER OF THE ONLY TECHNOLOGY THAT RESOLVES THE CONFLICT BETWEEN DATA USE AND PROTECTION WITH 100% ACCURACY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RAISED $50 MILLION IN GROWTH FINANCING BACKED BY ITS INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (IP) PORTFOLIO, FACILITATED BY AON (NYSE: AON) AND LED BY GT INVESTMENT PARTNERS (“GHOST TREE PARTNERS”).
