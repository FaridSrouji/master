AON APPOINTS ADRIANA KARABOUTIS TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. DUBLIN , SEPT. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AON PLC (NYSE: AON), A LEADING GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ADRIANA KARABOUTIS, CHIEF INFORMATION AND DIGITAL OFFICER OF NATIONAL GRID PLC, HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
