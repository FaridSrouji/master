AON/MERGERMARKET M&A STUDY FINDS REASONS FOR OPTIMISM BUT HEADWINDS PERSIST IN WAKE OF COVID-19 PANDEMIC. GLOBAL M&A RISK IN REVIEW REPORT TRACKS TRENDS; SEES DEALMAKERS SEEK TRANSACTION INSURANCE TO HELP NAVIGATE UNCERTAINTY LONDON , JUNE 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AON PLC (NYSE: AON), A LEADING GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM, AND MERGERMARKET, A PROVIDER OF INTELLIGENCE, DATA AND ANALYSIS OF GLOBAL M&A, TODAY RELEASED THE LATEST EDITION OF THEIR GLOBAL M&A RISK IN REVIEW REPORT. THE REPORT SUGGESTS THAT LAST YEAR'S RESURGENCE OF M&A ACTIVITY CAN BE SOMEWHAT SUSTAINED, ALBEIT PERHAPS NOT AT THE RECORD LEVELS SEEN IN 2021, WITH DEALMAKERS LOOKING FORWARD TO FUTURE TRANSACTIONS AS THEY SEEK OPPORTUNITIES AMID RECOVERING GLOBAL GROWTH AND PURSUE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION.
