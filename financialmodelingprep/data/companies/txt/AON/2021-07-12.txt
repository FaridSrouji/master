AON DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DUBLIN, JULY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AON PLC (NYSE:AON), A LEADING GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM PROVIDING A BROAD RANGE OF RISK, RETIREMENT AND HEALTH SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.51 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING CLASS A ORDINARY SHARES. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE AUGUST 13, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 2, 2021.
