AON DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. DUBLIN , JULY 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AON PLC (NYSE: AON), A LEADING GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.56 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING CLASS A ORDINARY SHARES. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE AUGUST 12, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 1, 2022.
