ADOBE NAMES BRETT BIGGS AND SPENCER NEUMANN TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADOBE (NASDAQ:ADBE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF BRETT BIGGS AND SPENCER NEUMANN TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. THE ADDITION OF BIGGS AND NEUMANN BRINGS ADOBE'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS TO 12 MEMBERS FOLLOWING THE ANNOUNCEMENT IN NOVEMBER 2021 THAT JIM DALEY WAS RETIRING AFTER NEARLY TWO DECADES AS AN ADOBE DIRECTOR.
