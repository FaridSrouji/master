ADOBE APPOINTS DAN DURN CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADOBE (NASDAQ:ADBE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DAN DURN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO), EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 18, 2021. DURN WILL ASSUME THE CFO ROLE FROM ADOBE EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CFO JOHN MURPHY, WHO ANNOUNCED HIS RETIREMENT EARLIER THIS YEAR.
