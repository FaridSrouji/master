ROCKWELL AUTOMATION NAMED LEADER IN GARTNER® 2023 MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR MANUFACTURING EXECUTION SYSTEMS. MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROCKWELL AUTOMATION, THE WORLD'S LARGEST COMPANY DEDICATED TO INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION AND DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN POSITIONED BY GARTNER AS A LEADER IN THE MAGIC QUADRANT FOR MANUFACTURING EXECUTION SYSTEMS (MES) WITH ITS PORTFOLIO OF OFFERINGS BEING EVALUATED, INCLUDING FACTORYTALK MES AND THE PLEX SMART MANUFACTURING PLATFORM.1 THE EVALUATION WAS BASED ON SPECIFIC CRITERIA THAT ANALYZED THE COMPANY'S OVERALL COMPLETENESS OF VISION AND ABILI.
