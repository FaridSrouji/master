PLEX SYSTEMS RECEIVES IDC 2021 SAAS CUSTOMER SATISFACTION AWARD FOR ERP. TROY, MICH., JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PLEX SYSTEMS, A ROCKWELL AUTOMATION COMPANY AND THE LEADER IN CLOUD-DELIVERED SMART MANUFACTURING SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED THE IDC 2021 SAAS CSAT CUSTOMER SATISFACTION AWARD FOR ERP.
