PLEX SYSTEMS ACHIEVES INTERNATIONAL INFORMATION SECURITY STANDARDS ISO 27001 CERTIFICATION. TROY, MICH., DEC. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  PLEX SYSTEMS, A ROCKWELL AUTOMATION COMPANY AND THE LEADER IN CLOUD-DELIVERED SMART MANUFACTURING SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS EARNED THE ISO/IEC 27001:2013 CERTIFICATION FOR ITS SMART MANUFACTURING PLATFORM.
