NEW STATE OF SMART MANUFACTURING STUDY FINDS OVER 40% OF MANUFACTURERS STRUGGLE TO OUTPACE COMPETITORS DUE TO LACK OF TECHNOLOGY AND SKILLED WORKFORCE. MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROCKWELL AUTOMATION, INC. (NYSE: ROK), THE WORLD'S LARGEST COMPANY DEDICATED TO INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION AND DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RESULTS OF THE 8TH ANNUAL “STATE OF SMART MANUFACTURING REPORT.” THE GLOBAL STUDY SURVEYED MORE THAN 1,350 MANUFACTURERS ACROSS 13 OF THE LEADING MANUFACTURING COUNTRIES.
