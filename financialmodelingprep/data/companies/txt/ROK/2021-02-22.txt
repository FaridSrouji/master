ROCKWELL AUTOMATION NAMES BOBBY GRIFFIN AS ITS FIRST CHIEF DIVERSITY, EQUITY & INCLUSION OFFICER. MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROCKWELL AUTOMATION, INC. (NYSE:ROK), THE WORLD'S LARGEST COMPANY DEDICATED TO INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION AND DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION, TODAY NAMED BOBBY GRIFFIN TO THE NEWLY CREATED ROLE OF VICE PRESIDENT, HUMAN RESOURCES, AND CHIEF DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION OFFICER. GRIFFIN WILL REPORT TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF PEOPLE AND LEGAL OFFICER BECKY HOUSE. GRIFFIN JOINS ROCKWELL WITH MORE THAN 25 YEARS OF DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION STRATEGY AND LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCE AT F
