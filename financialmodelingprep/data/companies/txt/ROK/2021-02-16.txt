ROCKWELL AUTOMATION ANNOUNCES NEW CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROCKWELL AUTOMATION, INC. HE WILL REPORT TO ROCKWELL CHAIRMAN AND CEO BLAKE MORET.
