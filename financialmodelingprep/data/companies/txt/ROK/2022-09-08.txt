ROCKWELL AUTOMATION JOINS CESMII TO ADVANCE ACCELERATION OF SMART MANUFACTURING. SHARED PRIORITIES INCLUDE SUSTAINABILITY, WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT LOS ANGELES , SEPT. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROCKWELL AUTOMATION, INC. (NYSE: ROK), THE WORLD'S LARGEST COMPANY DEDICATED TO INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION AND DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION, HAS JOINED CESMII –THE SMART MANUFACTURING INSTITUTE AS ITS NEWEST MEMBER.
