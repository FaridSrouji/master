ROCKWELL AUTOMATION NAMES NICOLE DARDEN FORD CHIEF INFORMATION SECURITY OFFICER. MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROCKWELL AUTOMATION, INC. (NYSE: ROK), THE WORLD'S LARGEST COMPANY DEDICATED TO INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION AND DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT NICOLE DARDEN FORD IS JOINING THE COMPANY AS VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION SECURITY OFFICER ON MARCH 21. SHE WILL REPORT TO CHRIS NARDECCHIA, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER AT ROCKWELL AUTOMATION. DARDEN FORD SUCCEEDS DAWN CAPPELLI, WHO RETIRED IN FEBRUARY.
