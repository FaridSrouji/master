ROCKWELL AUTOMATION INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND 5% TO $1.12 PER SHARE ON COMMON STOCK. MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ROCKWELL AUTOMATION, INC. (NYSE: ROK), FOLLOWING ITS REGULAR REVIEW, TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.12 PER SHARE ON ITS OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE DECEMBER 10, 2021 TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 15, 2021. THIS INCREASE OF 5% FROM LAST QUARTER'S DIVIDEND OF $1.07 REFLECTS CONTINUED STRONG CASH GENERATION AND REINFORCES THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO RETURNING CASH TO SHAREOWNERS. ABOUT ROCKWELL A
