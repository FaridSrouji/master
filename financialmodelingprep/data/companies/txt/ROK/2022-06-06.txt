ROCKWELL AUTOMATION NAMED A VISIONARY; PLEX SYSTEMS NAMED A LEADER IN 2022 GARTNER® MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR MANUFACTURING EXECUTION SYSTEMS. MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROCKWELL AUTOMATION, INC.
