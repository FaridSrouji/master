BROADCOM INC. ANNOUNCES CONVERSION DATE FOR MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED STOCK, SERIES A. SAN JOSE, CALIF. , SEPT. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADCOM INC. (NASDAQ: AVGO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS 8.00% MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED STOCK, SERIES A (THE "PREFERRED STOCK"), IS EXPECTED TO AUTOMATICALLY CONVERT INTO SHARES OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2022 (THE "CONVERSION DATE") IN ACCORDANCE WITH BROADCOM INC.'S CERTIFICATE OF DESIGNATIONS OF 8.00% MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED STOCK, SERIES A, DATED AS OF SEPTEMBER 30, 2019 (THE "CERTIFICATE OF DESIGNATIONS").
