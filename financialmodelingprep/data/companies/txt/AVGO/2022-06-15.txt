VMWARE INVESTOR ALERT BY THE FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA: KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC INVESTIGATES ADEQUACY OF PRICE AND PROCESS IN PROPOSED SALE OF VMWARE, INC. - VMW. NEW ORLEANS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA CHARLES C. FOTI, JR., ESQ. AND THE LAW FIRM OF KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC (“KSF”) ARE INVESTIGATING THE PROPOSED SALE OF VMWARE, INC. (NYSE: VMW) TO BROADCOM INC. (NASDAQ: AVGO). UNDER THE TERMS OF THE PROPOSED TRANSACTION, SHAREHOLDERS OF VMWARE WILL ELECT TO RECEIVE EITHER $142.50 IN CASH OR 0.2520 SHARES OF BROADCOM FOR EACH SHARE OF VMWARE THAT THEY OWN, SUBJECT TO PRORATION.
