BROADCOM INC. ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER FISCAL YEAR 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS AND QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS. SAN JOSE, CALIF., SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADCOM INC. (NASDAQ: AVGO), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER THAT DESIGNS, DEVELOPS AND SUPPLIES SEMICONDUCTOR AND INFRASTRUCTURE SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS, TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2021, ENDED AUGUST 1, 2021, PROVIDED GUIDANCE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF ITS FISCAL YEAR 2021 AND ANNOUNCED ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS.
