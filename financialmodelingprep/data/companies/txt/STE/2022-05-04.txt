STERIS DECLARES $0.43 PER SHARE DIVIDEND. DUBLIN, IRELAND , MAY 04, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  STERIS PLC (NYSE: STE) (“STERIS” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY WILL DISTRIBUTE A QUARTERLY INTERIM DIVIDEND OF $0.43 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 28, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 14, 2022.
