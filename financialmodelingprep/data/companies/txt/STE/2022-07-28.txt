STERIS RAISES DIVIDEND TO $0.47 PER SHARE. DUBLIN, IRELAND, JULY 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  STERIS PLC (NYSE: STE) (“STERIS” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY WILL DISTRIBUTE A QUARTERLY INTERIM DIVIDEND OF $0.47 PER SHARE. THIS REPRESENTS A $0.04 INCREASE IN THE DIVIDEND AND THE COMPANY'S 17TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR OF DIVIDEND GROWTH.
