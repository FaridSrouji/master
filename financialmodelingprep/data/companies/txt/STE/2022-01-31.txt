STERIS DECLARES $0.43 PER SHARE DIVIDEND. DUBLIN, IRELAND, JAN. 31, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STERIS PLC (NYSE: STE) (“STERIS” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY WILL DISTRIBUTE A QUARTERLY INTERIM DIVIDEND OF $0.43 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 24, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 25, 2022.
