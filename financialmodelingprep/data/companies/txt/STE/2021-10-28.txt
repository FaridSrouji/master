STERIS DECLARES $0.43 PER SHARE DIVIDEND. DUBLIN, IRELAND, OCT. 28, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STERIS PLC (NYSE: STE) (“STERIS” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY WILL DISTRIBUTE A QUARTERLY INTERIM DIVIDEND OF $0.43 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DECEMBER 17, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 23, 2021.
