SYSCO DECLARES INCREASE TO QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON, APRIL 27, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SYSCO CORPORATION (NYSE:SYY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A 1 CENT INCREASE IN THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.50 PER SHARE FROM THE CURRENT $0.49 PER SHARE. THE NEW DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE FOR THE FIRST TIME ON JULY 28, 2023, TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 7, 2023.
