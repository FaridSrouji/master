TOM PECK JOINS SYSCO AS CHIEF INFORMATION AND DIGITAL OFFICER. HOUSTON, DEC. 03, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SYSCO CORPORATION (NYSE: SYY), THE LEADING GLOBAL FOODSERVICE DISTRIBUTION COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TOM PECK WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION AND DIGITAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JAN. 4, 2021. IN THIS ROLE, PECK WILL LEAD SYSCO'S STRATEGIC EFFORTS TO ACCELERATE ITS BUSINESS TRANSFORMATION THROUGH CUSTOMER-FACING TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS AND WILL LEAD THE COMPANY EFFORTS TO MODERNIZE ITS TECHNOLOGY INFRASTRUCTURE.
