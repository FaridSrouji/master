FEDERMAN & SHERWOOD INVESTIGATES SYSCO CORPORATION FOR DATA BREACH. OKLAHOMA CITY--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #DATABREACH--THE LAW FIRM OF FEDERMAN & SHERWOOD HAS INITIATED AN INVESTIGATION INTO SYSCO CORPORATION WITH RESPECT TO THEIR RECENT DATA BREACH. ON MAY 5, 2023, SYSCO CORPORATION INFORMED CUSTOMERS THAT THE COMPANY EXPERIENCED A DATA BREACH AFTER AN UNAUTHORIZED PARTY ACCESSED SENSITIVE CONSUMER DATA ENTRUSTED TO THE COMPANY.
