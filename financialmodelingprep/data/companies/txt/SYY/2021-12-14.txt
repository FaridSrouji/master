SYSCO RELAUNCHES FOODIE MAGAZINE. HOUSTON, DEC. 14, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SYSCO CORPORATION (NYSE: SYY), THE LEADING GLOBAL FOODSERVICE DISTRIBUTION COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS FOODIE MAGAZINE HAS BEEN RELAUNCHED WITH A NEW LOOK AND FEEL TO CREATE A MORE ENGAGING EXPERIENCE FOR FOODSERVICE OPERATORS. THE NEWLY DESIGNED MAGAZINE CAN BE FOUND ONLINE AS PART OF SYSCO'S FOODIE SOLUTIONS PLATFORM, PROVIDING HELPFUL RESOURCES, IDEAS AND INFORMATION TO SUPPORT CUSTOMERS' SUCCESS.
