PAULO PEEREBOOM JOINS SYSCO AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & PRESIDENT, FOODSERVICE OPERATIONS - INTERNATIONAL. HOUSTON, JULY 13, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SYSCO CORPORATION (NYSE:SYY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PAULO PEEREBOOM WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS THE EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & PRESIDENT, FOODSERVICE OPERATIONS – INTERNATIONAL, EFFECTIVE AUGUST 8, 2022.
