HI-CRUSH INC. ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO DEPLOY ITS SIXTH ONCORE IN-FIELD MINE UNIT WITH PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES IN THE PERMIAN BASIN. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HI-CRUSH INC., OR "HI-CRUSH", TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES (NYSE:PXD), OR “PIONEER”, TO DEPLOY HI-CRUSH'S SIXTH ONCORE MOBILE MINING UNIT TO SUPPLY FRAC SAND FOR USE IN PIONEER'S WELL COMPLETIONS ACTIVITY IN THE MIDLAND BASIN.
