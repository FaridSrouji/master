PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2020 FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS; PROVIDES 2021 OUTLOOK. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES COMPANY (NYSE:PXD) ("PIONEER" OR "THE COMPANY") TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER AND YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2020. PIONEER REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF $43 MILLION, OR $0.26 PER DILUTED SHARE.
