PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES DECLARES SECOND QUARTER DIVIDEND ON COMMON SHARES. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES COMPANY (NYSE:PXD) ("PIONEER" OR "THE COMPANY") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY BASE-PLUS-VARIABLE CASH DIVIDEND OF $3.34 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 21, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 1, 2023.
