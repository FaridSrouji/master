PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES ANNOUNCES PRICING OF PUBLIC OFFERING OF $1.1 BILLION OF 5.100% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2026. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES COMPANY (NYSE:PXD) (“PIONEER” OR “THE COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $1.1 BILLION OF 5.100% SENIOR NOTES THAT WILL MATURE MARCH 29, 2026 (THE “NOTES”), PURSUANT TO AN EFFECTIVE SHELF REGISTRATION STATEMENT THAT WAS PREVIOUSLY FILED WITH THE SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION.
