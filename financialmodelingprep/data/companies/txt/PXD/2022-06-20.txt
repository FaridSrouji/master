ENCAP INVESTMENTS, L.P. AND DOUBLE EAGLE ENERGY ANNOUNCE FORMATION OF DOUBLE EAGLE ENERGY HOLDINGS IV WITH OVER $1.7 BILLION OF EQUITY COMMITTED.
