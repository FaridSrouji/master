PPG ANNOUNCES PPG INNOVEL PRO TECHNOLOGY, AN UPGRADE TO ITS NON-BISPHENOL BEVERAGE CAN COATING. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED PPG INNOVEL® PRO, AN ENHANCED INTERNAL SPRAY COATING THAT USES NO BISPHENOL-A (BPA) OR BISPHENOL STARTING SUBSTANCES AND PROVIDES MORE ROBUST APPLICATION PROPERTIES FOR THE INFINITELY RECYCLABLE ALUMINUM BEVERAGE CAN. PPG INNOVEL PRO IS A NEXT-GENERATION, HIGH-PERFORMANCE ACRYLIC COATING THAT DRAWS ON PPG INNOVEL'S NEARLY 10 YEARS AS THE MARKET'S MOST WIDELY USED NON-BPA INTERNAL BEVERAGE SPRAY COATINGS.
