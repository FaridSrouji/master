PPG DONATES COATINGS, SEALANTS TO HELP RESTORE DOUGLAS C-54 SKYMASTER AIRCRAFT. ESSEX, UNITED KINGDOM--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT RECENTLY SUPPLIED COATINGS AND SEALANTS TO HELP RESTORE A 74-YEAR-OLD, FOUR-ENGINE TRANSPORT AIRCRAFT – THE DOUGLAS C-54 SKYMASTER, SERIAL NUMBER 56498. MANUFACTURED IN 1945, THE AIRCRAFT WAS USED BY THE U.S. ARMY AIR FORCE AS A WORKHORSE TO TRANSPORT MEDICAL SUPPLIES, TROOPS AND MILITARY EQUIPMENT DURING WORLD WAR II, THE KOREAN WAR AND THE VIETNAM WAR.
