PPG RECOGNIZED IN NEWSWEEK'S AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES LIST FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. PITTSBURGH--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO NEWSWEEK MAGAZINE'S AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES LIST FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. THOUSANDS OF COMPANIES WERE CONSIDERED FOR THE 2023 LIST, WHICH RECOGNIZES THE 500 MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES IN THE UNITED STATES ACROSS 14 DIFFERENT INDUSTRY SUBCATEGORIES.
