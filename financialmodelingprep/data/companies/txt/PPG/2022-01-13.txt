PPG, CELLFORCE GROUP TO DEVELOP SUSTAINABLE BATTERY SOLUTIONS. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS PARTNERED WITH CELLFORCE GROUP, WHICH IS A JOINT VENTURE BETWEEN PORSCHE AND CUSTOMCELLS, TO DEVELOP EXCLUSIVE SUSTAINABLE BATTERY CELL SOLUTIONS TO BETTER SERVE THE ELECTRIC VEHICLE AND MOBILITY SEGMENT. PPG WILL SUPPLY CATHODE BINDER SYSTEMS, WHICH ARE FREE OF N-METHYLPYRROLIDONE (NMP) SOLVENT, TO THE CELLFORCE GROUP.
