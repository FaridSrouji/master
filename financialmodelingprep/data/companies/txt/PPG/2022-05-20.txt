PPG SHOWCASES INNOVATIONS IN PAINTS, COATINGS, SPECIALTY MATERIALS THAT ENHANCE SUSTAINABILITY, EFFICIENCY, MOBILITY. AMSTERDAM--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY SHOWCASED ITS LATEST INNOVATIONS TO MEDIA AT ITS PRODUCTION AND RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT (R&D) FACILITY IN AMSTERDAM. THE EVENT FOCUSED ON ADVANCEMENTS IN THREE KEY AREAS – SUSTAINABILITY, EFFICIENCY AND MOBILITY – REFLECTING THE COMPANY'S GOALS OF HELPING CUSTOMERS LOWER COSTS, REDUCE THEIR ENVIRONMENTAL FOOTPRINT AND SUPPORT THE GLOBAL SHIFT TO ELECTRIC VEHICLES (EVS).
