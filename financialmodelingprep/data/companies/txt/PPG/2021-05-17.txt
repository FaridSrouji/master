PPG COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF GLOBAL COATINGS MANUFACTURER WÖRWAG. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF WÖRWAG, A GLOBAL MANUFACTURER OF COATINGS FOR INDUSTRIAL AND AUTOMOTIVE APPLICATIONS. THE COMPANY SPECIALIZES IN DEVELOPING SUSTAINABLE LIQUID, POWDER AND FILM COATINGS AND OPERATES LOCATIONS IN GERMANY, THE U.S., CHINA, SOUTH AFRICA, MEXICO, SPAIN, SWITZERLAND AND POLAND, WITH HEADQUARTERS IN STUTTGART, GERMANY.
