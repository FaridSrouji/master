PPG APPOINTS LG TACKETT AS VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL OPERATIONS, INDUSTRIAL SEGMENT. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF LG TACKETT AS VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL OPERATIONS, INDUSTRIAL SEGMENT, EFFECTIVE AUGUST 9. REPORTING TO REBECCA LIEBERT, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, TACKETT WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR LEADING THE COMPANY'S INDUSTRIAL SEGMENT GLOBAL MANUFACTURING, SUPPLY CHAIN, ENGINEERING, ASSET MANAGEMENT AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT ACTIVITIES.
