PPG CHAIRMAN AND CEO MICHAEL MCGARRY NAMED INDUSTRIALIST OF THE YEAR. PITTSBURGH--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MICHAEL MCGARRY, PPG CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, HAS BEEN NAMED INDUSTRIALIST OF THE YEAR BY THE WESTERN PENNSYLVANIA CHAPTER OF THE SOCIETY OF INDUSTRIAL AND OFFICE REALTORS (SIOR). THE AWARD RECOGNIZES SOUTHWESTERN PENNSYLVANIA BUSINESS LEADERS FOR THEIR POSITIVE CONTRIBUTIONS ACROSS THEIR INDUSTRY, THEIR COMMUNITY AND THE REGION.
