PPG'S VINING IVY, 2023 COLOR OF THE YEAR, EXPECTED TO INFLUENCE INDUSTRIAL DESIGN. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS 2023 COLOR OF THE YEAR, VINING IVY, IS EXPECTED TO SHAPE THE STYLING DIRECTION OF INDUSTRIAL AND CONSUMER PRODUCTS, INCLUDING COOKWARE, CONSUMER ELECTRONICS AND OFFICE FURNITURE. PPG ALSO ANNOUNCED A COLLECTION OF THREE COORDINATING COLOR PALETTES CALLED REFLECTION. VINING IVY REFLECTS THE CONTINUED GLOBAL DOMINANCE OF GREEN AROUND THE WORLD.
