PPG RECOGNIZED AS ‘MOST ADMIRED COMPANY' BY FORTUNE MAGAZINE. PITTSBURGH--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WAS AGAIN RECOGNIZED BY FORTUNE MAGAZINE ON ITS 2023 MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES® LIST. THE STUDY, CONSIDERED TO BE THE ULTIMATE CORPORATE REPUTATION RANKING, MEASURES THE LARGEST COMPANIES AND THEIR REPUTATIONS WITHIN THEIR RESPECTIVE INDUSTRIES. THIS YEAR MARKS PPG'S 15TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR ON THE LIST.
