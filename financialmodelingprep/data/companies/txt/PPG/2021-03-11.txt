PPG RECEIVES EUROPEAN COMMISSION APPROVAL FOR PENDING ACQUISITION OF TIKKURILA'S SHARES. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION HAS GRANTED UNCONDITIONAL APPROVAL OF PPG'S ACQUISITION OF TIKKURILA'S SHARES (NASDAQ OMX:TIK1V). PPG STATED THAT THE APPROVAL IS AN IMPORTANT MILESTONE IN THE PROCESS AND FURTHER ENHANCES THE CERTAINTY OF COMPLETING THE TRANSACTION. THE TRANSACTION REMAINS SUBJECT TO ADDITIONAL CUSTOMARY CONDITIONS TO COMPLETION AND OTHER REGULATORY APPROVALS. THE TENDER OFFER IS CURRENTLY SCHEDULED TO EXPIRE ON MARCH 15,
