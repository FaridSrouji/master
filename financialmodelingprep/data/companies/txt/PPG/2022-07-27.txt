AURA COMPLETES THE SALE OF GOLD ROAD MINE TO PPG. ROAD TOWN, BRITISH VIRGIN ISLANDS, JULY 27, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AURA MINERALS INC. (TSX: ORA) (B3: AURA33) (“AURA” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCES TODAY THAT THE COMPANY, THROUGH ITS WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY, COMPLETED THE SALE OF ALL THE ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING SHARES OF ITS INDIRECT WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY Z79 RESOURCES, INC. (“Z79”) WHICH OWNS THROUGH GOLD ROAD MINING CORP. (“GRMC”) THE GOLD ROAD MINE LOCATED IN ARIZONA TO PPG ARIZONA HOLDINGS ACQUISITION, LP (“PPG”), AN AFFILIATE OF PANDION MINE FINANCE, LP. THE DEAL WAS COMPLETED FOR NOMINAL CASH CONSIDERATION OF US$1.
