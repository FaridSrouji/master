PPG COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF GLOBAL COATINGS MANUFACTURER ENNIS-FLINT. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF ENNIS-FLINT. ENNIS-FLINT IS A GLOBAL LEADER IN PAVEMENT MARKINGS AND TRAFFIC SAFETY SOLUTIONS WITH A WIDE RANGE OF PRODUCTS, INCLUDING TRAFFIC PAINT, HOT-APPLIED AND PREFORMED THERMOPLASTICS, RAISED PAVEMENT MARKERS AND INTELLIGENT TRANSPORTATION SYSTEMS.
