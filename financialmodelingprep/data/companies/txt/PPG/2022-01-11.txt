PPG INVESTS $10 MILLION TO EXPAND AUTOMOTIVE OEM COATINGS PRODUCTION IN GERMANY. WEINGARTEN, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL INVEST MORE THAN $10 MILLION (9 MILLION EUROS) TO EXPAND PRODUCTION OF AUTOMOTIVE ORIGINAL EQUIPMENT MANUFACTURER (OEM) COATINGS AT ITS SITE IN WEINGARTEN, GERMANY. EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETE IN THE SECOND QUARTER 2022, PPG IS CONSTRUCTING A 10,000 SQUARE FOOT ADDITION TO ITS EXISTING FACILITY WITH A CAPACITY TO PRODUCE MORE THAN 5,000 METRIC TONS OF WATERBORNE BASECOATS PER YEAR.
