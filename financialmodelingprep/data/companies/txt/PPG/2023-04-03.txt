PPG EXPECTS FIRST QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS TO EXCEED PREVIOUS GUIDANCE; DETAILED FINANCIAL UPDATE TO BE PROVIDED APRIL 20. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FIRST QUARTER 2023 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE (EPS) IS EXPECTED TO BE BETWEEN $1.52 AND $1.58, WHICH EXCEEDS THE COMPANY'S PREVIOUSLY COMMUNICATED GUIDANCE OF $1.10 TO $1.20. “THE PACE OF OUR OPERATING MARGIN RECOVERY ACCELERATED DURING THE QUARTER, DRIVEN BY HIGHER SALES VOLUMES AND ADDITIONAL SELLING PRICE CAPTURE,” SAID TIM KNAVISH, PPG PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
