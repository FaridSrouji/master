PPG NAMED TO CORPORATE KNIGHTS' CLEAN200 LIST AS TOP PRODUCER OF SUSTAINABLE PAINTS AND COATINGS. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WAS RECOGNIZED ON THE CLEAN200™ GLOBAL LIST AS THE TOP PAINTS, COATINGS AND SPECIALTY MATERIALS MANUFACTURER WITH THE GREATEST PERCENT OF 2020 REVENUE FROM SUSTAINABLY ADVANTAGED PRODUCTS. PPG RANKED 74TH OVERALL ON THE LIST IN GREEN REVENUE, REPORTING 35% OF SALES FROM SUSTAINABLY ADVANTAGED PRODUCTS IN 2020.
