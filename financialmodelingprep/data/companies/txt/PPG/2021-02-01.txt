PPG TO INVEST $20 MILLION BY 2025 TO ADVANCE RACIAL EQUITY. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) AND THE PPG FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED A COMMITMENT TO INVEST $20 MILLION BY 2025 TO ADDRESS SYSTEMIC RACISM AND ADVANCE RACIAL EQUITY IN THE U.S. BY FUNDING EDUCATIONAL PATHWAYS FOR BLACK COMMUNITIES AND PEOPLE OF COLOR.
