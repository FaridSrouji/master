PPG ACHIEVES ISO 14001 CERTIFICATION OF ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM. SHANNON, IRELAND--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ACHIEVED INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATION FOR STANDARDIZATION (ISO) 14001 CERTIFICATION FOR AN ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (EMS) AT ITS SHANNON, IRELAND, OPERATIONS. THE COMPANY MANUFACTURES HIGHLY EFFICIENT, HIGH-PERFORMING ORGANIC LIGHT-EMITTING DIODE (OLED) MATERIALS AT THE UNIVERSAL DISPLAY CORPORATION (UDC) (NASDAQ: OLED) SITE.
