PPG LAUNCHES DIGITAL STYLING PROGRAM FOR ADVANCED AUTOMOTIVE COLOR MODELING. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE INTRODUCTION OF A DIGITAL STYLING PROGRAM THAT ENABLES AUTOMOTIVE DESIGNERS TO EXECUTE EXCEPTIONALLY REALISTIC THREE-DIMENSIONAL MODELING OF AUTOMOTIVE COLORS AND EFFECTS ON VIRTUAL CAR DESIGNS AND SURFACES. EXCLUSIVE TO THE PROGRAM IS THE PPG PROPRIETARY “SPEED SHAPE” THAT INCORPORATES ALL THE COMPLEX SHAPES, EDGES, CURVES AND FLAT SURFACES THAT WOULD COMPRISE ANY TYPE OF AUTOMOBILE.
