PPG HIGHLIGHTS 2022 PERFORMANCE DURING ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY HELD ITS ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS WHERE TIM KNAVISH, PPG PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, HIGHLIGHTED THE COMPANY'S PERFORMANCE IN 2022. “WE ACHIEVED RECORD FULL-YEAR NET SALES OF APPROXIMATELY $17.7 BILLION, UP 5% COMPARED WITH 2021, LED BY AN 8% INCREASE IN ORGANIC SALES DRIVEN BY HIGHER SELLING PRICES,” KNAVISH SAID.
