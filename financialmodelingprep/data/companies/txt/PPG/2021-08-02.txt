PPG COMPLETES $15 MILLION EXPANSION AT SYLMAR MANUFACTURING FACILITY. SYLMAR, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF A $15 MILLION INVESTMENT AT ITS SYLMAR, CALIFORNIA MANUFACTURING FACILITY TO EXPAND THE DEVELOPMENT AND PRODUCTION OF AIRCRAFT TRANSPARENCY TECHNOLOGIES FOR MILITARY AVIATION PROGRAMS, INCLUDING CANOPY TRANSPARENCIES FOR LOCKHEED MARTIN'S F-35 FIGHTER JETS. AS PART OF THE FACILITY'S 30,000-SQUARE-FOOT (2,787-SQUARE-METER) EXPANSION, PPG WILL DEPLOY STATE-OF-THE-ART PROCESSING EQUIPMENT AND NEW INFORMATION TECHNOLO
