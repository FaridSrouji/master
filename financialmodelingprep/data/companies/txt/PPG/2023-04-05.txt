GLIDDEN PAINT BY PPG BECOMES PRIMARY PAINT BRAND AT WALMART IN THE U.S.. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT GLIDDEN® PAINT BY PPG WILL BECOME THE PRIMARY PAINT BRAND AT WALMART. THE MULTIYEAR AGREEMENT SIGNIFICANTLY EXPANDS PPG'S CURRENT RELATIONSHIP WITH WALMART, PROVIDING SHOPPERS WITH A WELL RECOGNIZED, HIGH QUALITY AND CONVENIENT DO-IT-YOURSELF (DIY) PAINT AND STAIN ASSORTMENT.
