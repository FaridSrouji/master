PPG APPOINTS MERI VAINIKKA AS VICE PRESIDENT, ARCHITECTURAL COATINGS, EMEA, NORTH AND EAST. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF MERI VAINIKKA AS VICE PRESIDENT, ARCHITECTURAL COATINGS (AC), EUROPE, MIDDLE EAST AND AFRICA (EMEA), NORTH AND EAST, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. SHE WILL REPORT TO TIM KNAVISH, PPG EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT. IN THIS KEY LEADERSHIP ROLE, VAINIKKA WILL HAVE FULL ACCOUNTABILITY FOR THE AC EMEA NORTH AND EAST REGION, WHICH INCLUDES THE TIKKURILA LEGACY BUSINESS.
