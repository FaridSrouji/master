FISCHER HOMES SELECTS PPG AS EXCLUSIVE PAINT PROVIDER FOR NEW HOME BUILDS. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED A FOUR-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH THE 30TH LARGEST HOMEBUILDER, FISCHER HOMES, UNDER WHICH FISCHER HOMES WILL EXCLUSIVELY OFFER PPG PAINTS PRODUCTS TO ITS CUSTOMERS IN OHIO, KENTUCKY, INDIANA, MISSOURI AND GEORGIA.
