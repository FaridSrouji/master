ONE ROCK CAPITAL PARTNERS COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF EASTMAN CHEMICAL'S TIRE ADDITIVES BUSINESS, RENAMES COMPANY FLEXSYS. NEW YORK, NOV. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ONE ROCK CAPITAL PARTNERS, LLC ("ONE ROCK"), A VALUE-ORIENTED, OPERATIONALLY FOCUSED PRIVATE EQUITY FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ONE OF ITS AFFILIATES HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF THE TIRE ADDITIVES BUSINESS OF EASTMAN CHEMICAL COMPANY ("EASTMAN"; NYSE: EMN), A LEADER IN CRITICAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS FOR THE TIRE INDUSTRY WITH A GLOBAL ASSET FOOTPRINT AND SUPERIOR TECHNICAL SERVICES. THE COMPANY WILL OPERATE UNDER THE NAME FLEXSYS, A TRIBUTE TO THE LEGACY NAME OF THE BUSINESS.
EASTMAN COMPLETES SALE OF TIRE ADDITIVES PRODUCT LINES. KINGSPORT, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EASTMAN HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY REPORTED SALE OF ITS GLOBAL TIRE ADDITIVES BUSINESS TO AN AFFILIATE OF ONE ROCK CAPITAL PARTNERS, LLC.
