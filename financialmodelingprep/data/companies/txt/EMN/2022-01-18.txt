EASTMAN TO INVEST UP TO $1 BILLION TO ACCELERATE CIRCULAR ECONOMY THROUGH BUILDING WORLD'S LARGEST MOLECULAR PLASTICS RECYCLING FACILITY IN FRANCE. PARIS, JAN. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THIS MORNING, FRENCH PRESIDENT EMMANUEL MACRON AND EASTMAN (NYSE:EMN) BOARD CHAIR AND CEO MARK COSTA WILL JOINTLY ANNOUNCE EASTMAN'S PLAN TO INVEST UP TO $1 BILLION IN A MATERIAL-TO-MATERIAL MOLECULAR RECYCLING FACILITY IN FRANCE. THIS FACILITY WOULD USE EASTMAN'S POLYESTER RENEWAL TECHNOLOGY TO RECYCLE UP TO 160,000 METRIC TONNES ANNUALLY OF HARD-TO-RECYCLE PLASTIC WASTE THAT IS CURRENTLY BEING INCINERATED.
