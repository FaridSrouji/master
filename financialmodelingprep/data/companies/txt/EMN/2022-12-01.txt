EASTMAN INCREASES DIVIDEND FOR 13TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. KINGSPORT, TENN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF EASTMAN CHEMICAL COMPANY (NYSE: EMN) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.79 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, A 4 PERCENT INCREASE FROM $0.76 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JAN. 6, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DEC. 15, 2022.
