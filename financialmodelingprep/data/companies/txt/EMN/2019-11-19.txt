MICHAEL P. CONNORS NOT TO STAND FOR RE-ELECTION OF EASTMAN CHEMICAL COMPANY AS A DIRECTOR. ON NOVEMBER 16, 2019, MICHAEL P. CONNORS, A DIRECTOR OF EASTMAN CHEMICAL COMPANY SINCE 2005, NOTIFIED THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS THAT HE WILL NOT STAND FOR RE-ELECTION AS A DIRECTOR WHEN HIS CURRENT TERM OF OFFICE EXPIRES AT THE 2020 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS.
