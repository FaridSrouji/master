EASTMAN CHEMICAL COMPANY DECLARES A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 3, 2020. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF EASTMAN CHEMICAL COMPANY HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.66 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, A 6.5% INCREASE FROM $0.62 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JAN. 3, 2020, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DEC. 16, 2019.
