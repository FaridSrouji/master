INVESTOR ACTION NOTICE: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN CATALENT, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 2, 2023 / THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST CATALENT, INC. ("CATALENT" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE:CTLT) FOR VIOLATIONS OF §§10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION. INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED THE COMPANY'S SECURITIES BETWEEN AUGUST 30, 2021 AND OCTOBER 31, 2022, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD"), ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THE FIRM BEFORE APRIL 25, 2023.

GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP, A LEADING SECURITIES FRAUD LAW FIRM, ANNOUNCES THE FILING OF A SECURITIES CLASS ACTION ON BEHALF OF CATALENT, INC. (CTLT) INVESTORS. CTLT LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES CATALENT, INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , MARCH 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN CATALENT, INC. ("CATALENT" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: CTLT) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT.
