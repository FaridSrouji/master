CTLT INVESTORS HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD CATALENT, INC. SECURITIES FRAUD LAWSUIT. LOS ANGELES, APRIL 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LAW OFFICES OF FRANK R. CRUZ ANNOUNCES THAT INVESTORS WITH SUBSTANTIAL LOSSES HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD THE SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST CATALENT, INC. ("CATALENT" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: CTLT).
