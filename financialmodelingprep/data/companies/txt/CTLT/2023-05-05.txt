CATALENT RECOMMENDS STOCKHOLDERS REJECT MINI-TENDER OFFER FROM TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT CORPORATION. SOMERSET, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CATALENT, INC. (“CATALENT”) (NYSE: CTLT) HAS BEEN NOTIFIED OF AN UNSOLICITED “MINI-TENDER OFFER” BY TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT CORPORATION (“TRC”) TO PURCHASE UP TO 2,000,000 SHARES OF CATALENT'S COMMON STOCK AT A PRICE OF $42.95 PER SHARE IN CASH. TRC'S OFFER PRICE IS APPROXIMATELY 4.43% LOWER THAN THE $44.94 CLOSING PRICE OF CATALENT'S COMMON STOCK ON APRIL 24, 2023, THE LAST TRADING DAY PRIOR TO THE DATE OF THE MINI-TENDER OFFER (APRIL 25, 2023).
