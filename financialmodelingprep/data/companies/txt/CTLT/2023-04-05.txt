FILING DEADLINE--KUZNICKI LAW PLLC ANNOUNCES CLASS ACTION ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF CATALENT, INC. - CTLT. CEDARHURST, N.Y. , APRIL 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SECURITIES LITIGATION LAW FIRM OF KUZNICKI LAW PLLC ISSUES THIS ALERT TO SHAREHOLDERS OF CATALENT, INC. (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: CTLT), IF THEY PURCHASED THE COMPANY'S SECURITIES BETWEEN AUGUST 30, 2021 AND OCTOBER 31, 2022, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
