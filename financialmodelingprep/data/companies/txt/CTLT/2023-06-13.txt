CATALENT REGAINS COMPLIANCE WITH NYSE CONTINUED LISTING STANDARDS. SOMERSET, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CATALENT, INC. (“CATALENT” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: CTLT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY HAS RECEIVED WRITTEN NOTIFICATION (“NOTICE”) FROM THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE (“NYSE”) THAT IT HAS OFFICIALLY REGAINED COMPLIANCE WITH THE NYSE CONTINUED LISTING STANDARDS. FOLLOWING THE FILING BY THE COMPANY ON JUNE 12, 2023 OF ITS QUARTERLY REPORT ON FORM 10-Q FOR THE THREE AND NINE MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2023, THE NYSE'S NOTICE CURED CATALENT OF THE PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED.
