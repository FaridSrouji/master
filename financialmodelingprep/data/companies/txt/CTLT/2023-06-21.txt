CATALENT ALERT: BRAGAR EAGEL & SQUIRE, P.C. IS INVESTIGATING CATALENT, INC. ON BEHALF OF LONG-TERM STOCKHOLDERS AND ENCOURAGES INVESTORS TO CONTACT THE FIRM. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #ACTION--BRAGAR EAGEL & SQUIRE, P.C., A NATIONALLY RECOGNIZED SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LAW FIRM, IS INVESTIGATING POTENTIAL CLAIMS AGAINST CATALENT, INC. (NYSE: CTLT) ON BEHALF OF LONG-TERM STOCKHOLDERS FOLLOWING A CLASS ACTION COMPLAINT THAT WAS FILED AGAINST CATALENT ON FEBRUARY 28, 2023 WITH A CLASS PERIOD FROM AUGUST 30, 2021 TO OCTOBER 31, 2022. OUR INVESTIGATION CONCERNS WHETHER THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CATALENT HAVE BREACHED THEIR FIDUCIARY DUTIES TO THE COMPANY. ACCORDI.
