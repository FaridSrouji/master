ANNOUNCES PRICING OF PUBLIC OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK.ANNOUNCED PRICING OF AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK, EXPECTED TO RESULT IN GROSS PROCEEDS OF ABOUT $500 MILLION.INTENDS TO USE ABOUT $330 MILLION OF NET PROCEEDS FROM OFFERING TO FUND PURCHASE PRICE FOR ACQUISITION OF MASTHERCELL GLOBAL INC.
CATALENT INC ANNOUNCES PUBLIC OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK. CATALENT, INC. ANNOUNCES PUBLIC OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK.CATALENT INC - LAUNCHED PUBLIC OFFERING OF $500 MILLION OF STOCK.CATALENT INC - INTENDS TO USE ABOUT $330 MILLION OF PROCEEDS FROM OFFERING TO FUND PURCHASE PRICE FOR MASTHERCELL GLOBAL DEAL.
CATALENT TO ACQUIRE CELL THERAPY CO MASTHERCELL GLOBAL FOR $315 MLN. CATALENT TO ACQUIRE LEADING CELL THERAPY COMPANY MASTHERCELL GLOBAL FOR $315 MILLION, CREATING AN INDUSTRY-LEADING CELL & GENE THERAPY PLATFORM.CATALENT INC - ACQUISITION IS AN ALL-CASH TRANSACTION.CATALENT INC - EXPECTS TO FINANCE WITH EITHER A PARTIAL DRAWN DOWN OF ITS REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY OR PROCEEDS FROM FUTURE CAPITAL-RAISING ACTIVITY.CATALENT INC - AGREEMENT GOVERNING ACQUISITION IS NOT CONTINGENT ON ANY FINANCING ACTIVITY.CATALENT INC - UPON COMPLETION OF DEAL, MASTHERCELL'S 240 EMPLOYEES, INCLUDING ITS LEADERSHIP TEAM, WILL BECOME PART OF CATALENT BIOLOGICS BUSINESS.
