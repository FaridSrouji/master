FILING DEADLINE--KUZNICKI LAW PLLC ANNOUNCES CLASS ACTION ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF CATALENT, INC. - CTLT. CEDARHURST, N.Y. , MARCH 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SECURITIES LITIGATION LAW FIRM OF KUZNICKI LAW PLLC ISSUES THIS ALERT TO SHAREHOLDERS OF CATALENT, INC. (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: CTLT), IF THEY PURCHASED THE COMPANY'S SECURITIES BETWEEN AUGUST 30, 2021 AND OCTOBER 31, 2022, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
INVESTOR ACTION ALERT: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN CATALENT, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 8, 2023 / THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST CATALENT, INC. ("CATALENT" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE: CTLT ) FOR VIOLATIONS OF §§10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION. INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED THE COMPANY'S SECURITIES BETWEEN AUGUST 30, 2021 AND OCTOBER 31, 2022, INCLUSIVE (THE ''CLASS PERIOD''), ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THE FIRM BEFORE APRIL 25, 2023.

SHAREHOLDERS WHO PURCHASED SHARES OF CTLT DURING THE CLASS PERIOD LISTED ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THE FIRM REGARDING POSSIBLE LEAD PLAINTIFF APPOINTMENT.
