KEURIG AND BLK & BOLD COFFEE ANNOUNCE NEW K-CUP POD PARTNERSHIP. AGREEMENT MAKES AVAILABLE THE LARGEST BLACK-OWNED COFFEE BRAND TO THE 36 MILLION+ HOUSEHOLDS WITH A KEURIG BREWER BURLINGTON, MASS., FRISCO, TEXAS AND DES MOINES, IOWA , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEURIG DR PEPPER INC. (NASDAQ: KDP) AND BLK & BOLD SPECIALTY BEVERAGES TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF BLK & BOLD COFFEES IN K-CUP® PODS FOR THE KEURIG® BREWING SYSTEM.
