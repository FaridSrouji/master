KEURIG DR PEPPER ANNOUNCES NEW SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION. BURLINGTON, MASS. AND FRISCO, TEXAS, OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEURIG DR PEPPER INC. (NASDAQ: KDP) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED A SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM OF UP TO $4 BILLION OF THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK ("SHARES"), ENABLING THE COMPANY TO OPPORTUNISTICALLY RETURN VALUE TO SHAREHOLDERS.
