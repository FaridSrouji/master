KEURIG DR PEPPER ANNOUNCES ACCEPTED AMOUNTS AND PRICING OF TENDER OFFERS FOR OUTSTANDING SERIES OF ITS SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES. BURLINGTON, MASS.
