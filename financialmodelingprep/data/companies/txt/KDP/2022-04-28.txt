KEURIG DR PEPPER REPORTS Q1 2022 RESULTS AND RAISES FULL-YEAR NET SALES GUIDANCE. TOP-LINE RESULTS REMAIN STRONG, WITH DOUBLE-DIGIT GROWTH REPORTED IN THREE BUSINESS SEGMENTS IN Q1 COMPANY ALSO REAFFIRMS EPS GUIDANCE FOR 2022 BURLINGTON, MASS. AND FRISCO, TEXAS ,  APRIL 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEURIG DR PEPPER INC. (NASDAQ: KDP) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2022 AND RAISED ITS GUIDANCE FOR NET SALES GROWTH IN 2022 TO THE HIGH-SINGLE-DIGIT RANGE, FROM THE PREVIOUS MID-SINGLE-DIGIT RANGE.
