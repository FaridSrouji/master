KEURIG DR PEPPER REPORTS STRONG Q3 2022 RESULTS AND REAFFIRMS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR. COMPANY DELIVERS STRONG NET SALES MOMENTUM, WITH ALL BUSINESS SEGMENTS POSTING GROWTH IN THE QUARTER BURLINGTON, MASS. AND FRISCO, TEXAS , OCT. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEURIG DR PEPPER INC. (NASDAQ: KDP) TODAY REPORTED STRONG RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2022 AND REAFFIRMED ITS FULL-YEAR GUIDANCE FOR CONSTANT CURRENCY NET SALES GROWTH IN THE LOW-DOUBLE-DIGIT RANGE AND ADJUSTED EPS GROWTH IN THE MID-SINGLE-DIGIT RANGE.
