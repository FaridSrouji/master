KEURIG DR PEPPER ANNOUNCES SECONDARY OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK ON BEHALF OF MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL. BURLINGTON, MASS. AND FRISCO, TEXAS, JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEURIG DR PEPPER (NASDAQ: KDP) (THE "COMPANY" OR "KDP") ANNOUNCED TODAY THE COMMENCEMENT OF A REGISTERED PUBLIC SECONDARY OFFERING OF 28 MILLION SHARES OF THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK, ON BEHALF OF MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL HOLDINGS LLC ("MONDELĒZ").
