KEURIG DR PEPPER STRENGTHENS ITS COMMITMENT TO WATER STEWARDSHIP. BURLINGTON, MASS. AND PLANO, TEXAS, MARCH 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN CELEBRATION OF WORLD WATER DAY, KEURIG DR PEPPER (NASDAQ: KDP) TODAY STRENGTHENED ITS COMMITMENT TO WATER STEWARDSHIP ACROSS ITS VALUE CHAIN BY JOINING THE NEW TEXAS WATER ACTION COLLABORATIVE (TXWAC), A COALITION OF INDUSTRY, NONPROFIT AND GOVERNMENTAL ORGANIZATIONS TO BENEFIT THE QUALITY AND VOLUME OF WATER ON THE TRINITY RIVER IN TEXAS, AND ANNOUNCING A NEW REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE GOAL.
