SEE DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SEE (NYSE: SEE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.20 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 24, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 10, 2023.
