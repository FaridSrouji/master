SEALED AIR CORPORATION REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. SEALED AIR CORPORATION ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 1,218.5 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,186.2 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 154 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 163.2 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 68 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 79 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 0.52 COMPARED TO USD 0.48 A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.44 COMPARED TO USD 0.5 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 0.51 COMPARED TO USD 0.48 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 3,492.2 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 3,472.4 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 373.5 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 490.9 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 158.7 MILLION COMPARED TO NET LOSS OF USD 7.2 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 1.1 COMPARED TO BASIC LOSS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF USD 0.31 A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.03 COMPARED TO BASIC LOSS PER SHARE OF USD 0.05 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 1.09 COMPARED TO DILUTED LOSS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF USD 0.31 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.02 COMPARED TO DILUTED LOSS PER SHARE OF USD 0.05 A YEAR AGO.
