SEALED AIR DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SEALED AIR CORPORATION (NYSE: SEE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.16 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 19, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 5, 2021.
