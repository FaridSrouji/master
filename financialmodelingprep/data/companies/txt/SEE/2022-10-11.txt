SEE ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF CFO CHRISTOPHER J. STEPHENS. CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SEE (NYSE: SEE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHRISTOPHER J. STEPHENS, SEE'S SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, HAS INFORMED THE COMPANY OF HIS INTENT TO RETIRE. STEPHENS WILL CONTINUE IN HIS ROLE AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER UNTIL A SUCCESSOR IS IN PLACE TO ENSURE A SEAMLESS AND SUCCESSFUL TRANSITION OF RESPONSIBILITIES.
