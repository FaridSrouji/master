SEALED AIR CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 20, 2019. SEALED AIR CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.16 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 20, 2019 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 6, 2019.
