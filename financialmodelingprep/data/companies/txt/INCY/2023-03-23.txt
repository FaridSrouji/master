INCYTE PROVIDES REGULATORY UPDATE ON RUXOLITINIB EXTENDED-RELEASE TABLETS. WILMINGTON, DEL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INCYTE (NASDAQ:INCY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ISSUED A COMPLETE RESPONSE LETTER FOR RUXOLITINIB EXTENDED-RELEASE (XR) TABLETS, A JAK1/JAK2 INHIBITOR, FOR ONCE-DAILY (QD) USE IN THE TREATMENT OF CERTAIN TYPES OF MYELOFIBROSIS (MF), POLYCYTHEMIA VERA (PV) AND GRAFT-VERSUS-HOST DISEASE (GVHD).
