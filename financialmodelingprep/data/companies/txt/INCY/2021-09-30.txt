OLUMIANT® SIGNIFICANTLY IMPROVED HAIR REGROWTH TO AT LEAST 80% SCALP COVERAGE AS EARLY AS 24 WEEKS ACROSS FIRST COMPLETED PHASE 3 STUDIES FOR ALOPECIA AREATA. INDIANAPOLIS, SEPT. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) AND INCYTE (NASDAQ: INCY) ANNOUNCED TODAY DETAILED RESULTS FROM TWO PIVOTAL PHASE 3 TRIALS (BRAVE-AA1 AND BRAVE-AA2), WHICH FOUND ONCE-DAILY OLUMIANT® (BARICITINIB) 4-MG WAS SUPERIOR TO PLACEBO IN ACHIEVING SIGNIFICANT SCALP HAIR REGROWTH AS EARLY AS 24 WEEKS IN ADULTS WITH SEVERE ALOPECIA AREATA (AA) AS DEFINED BY ≥50% SCALP HAIR LOSS AT BASELINE.
