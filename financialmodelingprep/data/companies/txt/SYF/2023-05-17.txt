SYNCHRONY PARTNERS WITH THRIVE GLOBAL TO SUPPORT EMPLOYEE WELL-BEING. DURING MENTAL HEALTH AWARENESS MONTH, COMPANY FURTHERS COMMITMENT TO PRIORITIZE MENTAL HEALTH IN THE WORKPLACE STAMFORD, CONN. AND NEW YORK , MAY 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY, A PREMIER FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, AND THRIVE GLOBAL, THE LEADING ENTERPRISE BEHAVIOR CHANGE TECHNOLOGY COMPANY FOUNDED BY ARIANNA HUFFINGTON, ANNOUNCED A NEW PARTNERSHIP THAT BRINGS WELL-BEING TOOLS AND TECHNOLOGY TO SYNCHRONY EMPLOYEES AND LEADERS DIRECTLY IN THEIR FLOW OF WORK, FURTHERING THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO PRIORITIZE THE MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLNESS OF ITS PEOPLE.
