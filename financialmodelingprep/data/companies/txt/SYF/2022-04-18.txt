SYNCHRONY ANNOUNCES APPROVAL OF AN INCREMENTAL $2.8 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM AND PLANS TO INCREASE QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND BY 5% TO $0.23 PER SHARE. STAMFORD, CONN. , APRIL 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL (NYSE: SYF) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED AN INCREMENTAL SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM OF UP TO $2.8 BILLION, COMMENCING THIS QUARTER THROUGH JUNE 30, 2023, AND INTENDS TO INCREASE THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND FROM $0.22 TO $0.23 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, COMMENCING IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022.
SYNCHRONY REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. STAMFORD, CONN. , APRIL 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL (NYSE: SYF) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FIRST QUARTER 2022 RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDING MARCH 31, 2022.
