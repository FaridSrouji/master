SYNCHRONY ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.23 PER SHARE. COMPANY ALSO DECLARES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND STAMFORD, CONN. , JAN. 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL (NYSE: SYF) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.23 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 17, 2023 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 6, 2023.
