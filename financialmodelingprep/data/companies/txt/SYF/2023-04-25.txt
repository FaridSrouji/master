SYNCHRONY ANNOUNCES APPROVAL OF AN INCREMENTAL $1.0 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM AND PLANS TO INCREASE QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND BY 9% TO $0.25 PER SHARE. STAMFORD, CONN. , APRIL 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL (NYSE: SYF) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED AN INCREMENTAL SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM OF UP TO $1.0 BILLION, COMMENCING THIS QUARTER THROUGH JUNE 30, 2024, AND INTENDS TO INCREASE THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND FROM $0.23 TO $0.25 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, COMMENCING IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2023.
