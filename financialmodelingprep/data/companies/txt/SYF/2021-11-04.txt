SYNCHRONY LAUNCHES FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND PROGRAM IN STAMFORD TO PREPARE UNDERSERVED YOUTH FOR THE DIGITAL ECONOMY. STAMFORD, CONN., NOV. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY (NYSE: SYF), TOGETHER WITH CONNECTICUT NON-PROFITS DISTRICT ARTS & EDUCATION (DAE), THE UNIVERSITY OF CONNECTICUT'S ENGINEERING AMBASSADORS AND FUTURE5, TODAY ANNOUNCED A FIRST-OF-ITS KIND PROGRAM IN STAMFORD TO EQUIP HIGH SCHOOL STUDENTS FROM UNDERREPRESENTED BACKGROUNDS WITH DIGITAL AND SOFTWARE SKILLS.
