SYNCHRONY ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.23 PER SHARE. COMPANY ALSO DECLARES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND STAMFORD, CONN. , OCT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL (NYSE: SYF) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.23 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 10, 2022 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 1, 2022.
