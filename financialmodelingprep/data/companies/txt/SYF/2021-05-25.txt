SYNCHRONY ANNOUNCES APPROVAL OF A $2.9 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM AND PLANS TO MAINTAIN QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.22 PER SHARE. STAMFORD, CONN., MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL (NYSE: SYF) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM OF UP TO $2.9 BILLION, FOR THE PERIOD WHICH COMMENCED APRIL 1, 2021 THROUGH JUNE 30, 2022, AND INTENDS TO MAINTAIN THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.22 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.
