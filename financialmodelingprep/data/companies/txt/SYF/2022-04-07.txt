SYNCHRONY JOINS THE WORKER FINANCIAL WELLNESS INITIATIVE. COMPANIES IN THE INITIATIVE HAVE A SHARED MISSION OF IMPROVING THE FINANCIAL HEALTH AND RESILIENCE OF AMERICAN WORKERS STAMFORD, CONN. , APRIL 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY (NYSE: SYF) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS JOINED THE WORKER FINANCIAL WELLNESS INITIATIVE, A COALITION AIMED AT MAKING WORKERS' FINANCIAL SECURITY AND HEALTH A C-SUITE AND INVESTOR PRIORITY.
