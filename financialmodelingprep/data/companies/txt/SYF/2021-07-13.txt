SYNCHRONY RAISES U.S. MINIMUM HOURLY WAGE TO $20. STAMFORD, CONN., JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FURTHERING ITS COMMITMENT TO A CULTURE OF CARING AND PROVIDING ITS EMPLOYEES WITH COMPETITIVE WAGES AND PROGRESSIVE BENEFITS, SYNCHRONY (NYSE: SYF) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL RAISE ITS MINIMUM WAGE TO $20 PER HOUR FOR ALL HOURLY EMPLOYEES IN THE U.S. AND PUERTO RICO, EFFECTIVE IN AUGUST.
