SYNCHRONY CAR CARE™ LETS CUSTOMERS "CHARGE" ELECTRONIC VEHICLE (EV) REFILLS. STAMFORD, CONN., MARCH 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMMITTED TO ADDRESS RISING ENVIRONMENTAL CONCERNS AND GOVERNMENT LEGISLATION, SYNCHRONY (NYSE: SYF), A PREMIER CONSUMER FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CUSTOMERS CAN NOW USE THE SYNCHRONY CAR CARE CREDIT CARD TO PAY FOR REFILLS AT CHARGING STATIONS.
