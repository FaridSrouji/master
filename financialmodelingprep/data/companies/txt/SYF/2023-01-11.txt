BASSETT FURNITURE AND SYNCHRONY PARTNER ON MULTI-YEAR FINANCING PROGRAM. PROGRAM WILL LAUNCH AS PART OF SYNCHRONY'S HOME NETWORK™ STAMFORD, CONN. , JAN. 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY (NYSE: SYF), A LEADING CONSUMER FINANCING COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY ARE REESTABLISHING A PARTNERSHIP WITH BASSETT FURNITURE INDUSTRIES, INC. (NASDAQ: BSET) TO LAUNCH A MULTI-YEAR FINANCING PROGRAM.
