SYNCHRONY NAMES KAMILA CHYTIL TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. LEADING TECHNOLOGY AND OPERATIONS EXECUTIVE BRINGS ADDITIONAL EXPERTISE IN PAYMENTS AND FINANCIAL SERVICES STAMFORD, CONN. , APRIL 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY (NYSE: SYF), A PREMIER CONSUMER FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED KAMILA CHYTIL HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO SYNCHRONY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1.
