2021 DISABILITY EQUALITY INDEX AWARDS SYNCHRONY TOP SCORE FOR FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. STAMFORD, CONN., JULY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, SYNCHRONY (NYSE: SYF) ANNOUNCED THAT FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION HELPED ADVANCE IT TO THE TOP OF THE  2021 DISABILITY EQUALITY INDEX (DEI).
