CARECREDIT, CWH ADVISORS PATIENT PAY SURVEY FINDS MAJORITY OF PATIENTS WOULD CONSIDER FINANCING OPTIONS TO PAY HEALTHCARE COSTS. COSTA MESA, CALIF., MARCH 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIXTY-THREE PERCENT OF ALL PATIENTS AND 85% OF YOUNGER PATIENTS (AGED 18-34) WOULD CONSIDER POINT-OF-SERVICE (POS) FINANCING FOR THEIR HEALTHCARE BILLS, ACCORDING TO A NEW SURVEY SPONSORED BY CARECREDIT, A SYNCHRONY (NYSE:SYF) SOLUTION AND LEADING PROVIDER OF PROMOTIONAL FINANCING FOR HEALTHCARE CONSUMERS.
