TREEZ SECURES $51 MILLION IN SERIES C FUNDING, NOW VALUED AT $260 MILLION, DRIVEN BY RAPID GROWTH. SERIES C COMPRISED OF LEADING FINTECH INVESTORS INCLUDING: LONG RIDGE, KAYNE PARTNERS, AND SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL SAN FRANCISCO , APRIL 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TREEZ (WWW.TREEZ.IO ),  THE LEADING ENTERPRISE CLOUD COMMERCE PLATFORM THAT POWERS RETAIL AND SUPPLY CHAIN OPERATIONS FOR THE CANNABIS MARKET, TODAY ANNOUNCED COMPLETION OF A $51 MILLION SERIES C FUNDING AT A VALUATION IN EXCESS OF $260 MILLION. THE ROUND BRINGS NEW INVESTMENT FROM LONG RIDGE EQUITY PARTNERS, A NEW YORK-BASED GROWTH EQUITY FUND FOCUSED ON HIGH GROWTH SOFTWARE AND FINTECH COMPANIES, KAYNE PARTNERS, A LOS ANGELES-BASED GROWTH EQUITY GROUP, AND SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL (NYSE: SYF), A LEADING CONSUMER (B2C) FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANY.
