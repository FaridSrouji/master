FTD LAUNCHES PERSONALIZED DIGITAL GIFTING EXPERIENCES FOR CUSTOMERS. SAN FRANCISCO, JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FTD, LLC, A LEADER IN THE FLORAL INDUSTRY FOR MORE THAN A CENTURY, IS ENABLING SHOPPERS TO DIGITALLY GIFT MERCHANDISE THAT MATTERS MOST TO THEIR LOVED ONES AND FRIENDS IF THAT MERCHANDISE CANNOT PHYSICALLY ARRIVE BY A DESIRED DAY. FTD IS THE MODERN FLORIST COLLECTIVE, DEDICATED TO HELPING PEOPLE FIND AND GIVE THE MOST BEAUTIFUL FLOWERS.
