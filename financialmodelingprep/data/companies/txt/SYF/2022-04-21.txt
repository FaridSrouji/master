SYNCHRONY ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.22 PER SHARE. COMPANY ALSO DECLARES PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND STAMFORD, CONN. , APRIL 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL (NYSE: SYF) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.22 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MAY 12, 2022 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 2, 2022.
