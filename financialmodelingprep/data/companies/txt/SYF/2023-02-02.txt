SYNCHRONY COMMITS $100 MILLION TO GROW MINORITY-OWNED BUSINESSES. INVESTS IN ARIEL ALTERNATIVES' PROJECT BLACK FUND TO SCALE MINORITY BUSINESSES INVESTMENT BUILDS ON SYNCHRONY'S EFFORTS TO ADVANCE EQUITY AMONG DIVERSE BUSINESSES STAMFORD, CONN. , FEB. 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY (NYSE: SYF), A PREMIER CONSUMER FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED A $100 MILLION COMMITMENT IN ARIEL ALTERNATIVES' PROJECT BLACK, WHICH AIMS TO SCALE SUSTAINABLE MINORITY-OWNED BUSINESSES AND POSITION THEM AS LEADING SUPPLIERS TO FORTUNE 500 COMPANIES.
