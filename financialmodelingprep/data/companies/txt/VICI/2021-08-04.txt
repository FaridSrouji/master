NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HALPER SADEH LLP, A GLOBAL INVESTOR RIGHTS LAW FIRM, IS INVESTIGATING WHETHER THE MERGER OF VICI PROPERTIES INC. (NYSE: VICI) AND MGM GROWTH PROPERTIES LLC (“MGP”) IS FAIR TO VICI PROPERTIES SHAREHOLDERS. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT, MGP CLASS A SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE 1.366 SHARES OF NEWLY ISSUED VICI PROPERTIES STOCK IN EXCHANGE FOR EACH CLASS A SHARE OF MGP.
