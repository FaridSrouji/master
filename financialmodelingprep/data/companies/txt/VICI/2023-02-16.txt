CHEROKEE NATION BUSINESSES COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF GOLD STRIKE CASINO RESORT. TUNICA, MISS. , FEB. 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CNE GAMING HOLDINGS LLC, AN INDIRECT SUBSIDIARY OF CHEROKEE NATION BUSINESSES, OFFICIALLY ASSUMED OPERATIONS OF GOLD STRIKE CASINO RESORT AFTER RECEIVING APPROVAL FROM THE MISSISSIPPI GAMING COMMISSION.
