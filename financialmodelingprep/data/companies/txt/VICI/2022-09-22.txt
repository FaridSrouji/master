VICI PROPERTIES INC. INVESTS IN GREAT WOLF LODGE GULF COAST TEXAS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VICI PROPERTIES INC. (NYSE: VICI) (“VICI PROPERTIES” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY HAS AGREED TO PROVIDE A MEZZANINE LOAN FOR UP TO $127.0 MILLION TO GREAT WOLF RESORTS, INC. (“GREAT WOLF”) RELATED TO THE DEVELOPMENT OF GREAT WOLF LODGE GULF COAST TEXAS, A MORE THAN $200 MILLION FAMILY RESORT PROJECT IN WEBSTER, TX.
