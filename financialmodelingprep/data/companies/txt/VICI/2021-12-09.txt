VICI PROPERTIES INC. DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VICI PROPERTIES INC. (NYSE: VICI) (“VICI PROPERTIES” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.36 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK FOR THE PERIOD FROM OCTOBER 1, 2021 TO DECEMBER 31, 2021. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON JANUARY 6, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 23, 2021.
