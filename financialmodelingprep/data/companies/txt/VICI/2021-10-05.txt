SHAREHOLDER ALERT: MONTEVERDE & ASSOCIATES PC ANNOUNCES AN INVESTIGATION OF VICI PROPERTIES, INC. - VICI. NEW YORK, OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  JUAN MONTEVERDE , FOUNDER AND MANAGING PARTNER AT MONTEVERDE & ASSOCIATES PC, A NATIONAL SECURITIES FIRM RATED TOP 50 IN THE 2018-2020 ISS SECURITIES CLASS ACTION SERVICES REPORT AND HEADQUARTERED AT THE EMPIRE STATE BUILDING IN NEW YORK CITY, IS INVESTIGATING VICI PROPERTIES, INC. ("VICI" OR THE "COMPANY") (VICI) RELATING TO ITS PROPOSED MERGER WITH MGM GROWTH PROPERTIES LLC (MGP). UNDER THE TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT, MGP SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE 1.366 SHARES OF VICI PER SHARE THEY OWN.
