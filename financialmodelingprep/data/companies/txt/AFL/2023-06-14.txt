AFLAC RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF 50 MOST COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN U.S. FOR SIXTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR BY POINTS OF LIGHT. COLUMBUS, GA. , JUNE 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AFLAC INCORPORATED, A LEADING PROVIDER OF SUPPLEMENTAL HEALTH INSURANCE IN THE U.S.,1 ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED BY POINTS OF LIGHT TO THE CIVIC 50 LIST FOR THE SIXTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, RECOGNIZING THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY.
