AFLAC U.S. PRESIDENT TERESA WHITE NAMED A 2022 AACSB INFLUENTIAL LEADER. COLUMBUS, GA., FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AFLAC, A LEADING PROVIDER OF SUPPLEMENTAL INSURANCE IN THE U.S., ANNOUNCED THAT AFLAC U.S. PRESIDENT TERESA WHITE WAS RECOGNIZED AS A 2022 INFLUENTIAL LEADER BY THE PRESTIGIOUS ASSOCIATION TO ADVANCE COLLEGIATE SCHOOLS OF BUSINESS INTERNATIONAL (AACSB), THE WORLD'S LARGEST BUSINESS EDUCATION ALLIANCE.
