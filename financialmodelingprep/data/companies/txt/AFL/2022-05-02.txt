AFLAC ENHANCES GROUP CRITICAL ILLNESS INSURANCE TO INCLUDE SERIOUS MENTAL ILLNESSES AND INFECTIOUS DISEASES LIKE COVID-19. FLEXIBLE BENEFITS COVER CHANGING NEEDS OF WORKERS IN POST-PANDEMIC ENVIRONMENT COLUMBUS, GA. , MAY 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AFLAC INCORPORATED, A LEADING PROVIDER OF SUPPLEMENTAL INSURANCE PRODUCTS IN THE U.S., TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS NEWLY REDESIGNED AFLAC GROUP CRITICAL ILLNESS INSURANCE TO HELP EMPLOYERS BETTER SUPPORT THE CHANGING NEEDS OF THEIR VALUED WORKERS IN A POST-PANDEMIC ENVIRONMENT.
