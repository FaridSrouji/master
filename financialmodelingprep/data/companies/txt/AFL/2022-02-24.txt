AFLAC PARTNERS WITH NEUROFLOW TO ENHANCE GROUP LONG-TERM DISABILITY OFFERING WITH INNOVATIVE MENTAL HEALTH SOLUTION. COLUMBUS, GA., FEB. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MENTAL HEALTH AND EMOTIONAL WELL-BEING CHALLENGES ARE ON THE RISE, AND THAT'S WHY AFLAC, A LEADING PROVIDER OF SUPPLEMENTAL INSURANCE IN THE U.S., ENTERED INTO A STRATEGIC RELATIONSHIP  WITH NEUROFLOW THAT ADDS AN INNOVATIVE MENTAL HEALTH SOLUTION DESIGNED TO HELP INDIVIDUALS SCREEN AND MONITOR EXISTING AND UNDETECTED BEHAVIORAL HEALTH CONDITIONS.
