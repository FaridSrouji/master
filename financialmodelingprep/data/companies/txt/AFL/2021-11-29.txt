AFLAC CHIEF DIGITAL INFORMATION OFFICER RICH GILBERT NAMED A 2021 CIO OF THE YEAR® ORBIE AWARD WINNER. COLUMBUS, GA., NOV. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AFLAC, A LEADING PROVIDER OF SUPPLEMENTAL INSURANCE IN THE U.S., ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF DIGITAL INFORMATION OFFICER RICH GILBERT WAS RECOGNIZED AS A 2021 CIO OF THE YEAR® HONOREE BY GEORGIACIO.
