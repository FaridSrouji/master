AFLAC INCORPORATED NAMES TAKEKO UEMOTO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT; ENTERPRISE CORPORATE ACTUARY. COLUMBUS, GA., MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AFLAC INCORPORATED (NYSE: AFL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TAKEKO UEMOTO HAS BEEN NAMED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT; ENTERPRISE CORPORATE ACTUARY, REPORTING TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER OF AFLAC INCORPORATED MAX K.
