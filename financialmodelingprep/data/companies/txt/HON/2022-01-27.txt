AMERICA'S LARGEST PLASTICS RECYCLER WILL USE HONEYWELL'S ADVANCED PLASTICS RECYCLING TECHNOLOGY. HOUSTON, JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INTENT TO FORM A JOINT VENTURE WITH AVANGARD INNOVATIVE TO BUILD AN ADVANCED RECYCLING PLANT IN TEXAS. THE FACILITY WILL USE HONEYWELL'S RECENTLY ANNOUNCED UPCYCLE PROCESS TECHNOLOGY TO TRANSFORM END-OF-LIFE PLASTIC WASTE INTO RECYCLED POLYMER FEEDSTOCK THAT CAN BE USED TO CREATE NEW PLASTICS.
