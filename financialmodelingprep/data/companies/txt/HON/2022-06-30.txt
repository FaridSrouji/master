HONEYWELL FORGE RECOGNIZED BY SIIA AS BEST OVERALL BUSINESS SOLUTION AT 2022 CODIE™ AWARDS. HONEYWELL'S ENTERPRISE PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT SOLUTION ALSO NAMED BEST EMERGING TECHNOLOGY ATLANTA , JUNE 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT HONEYWELL FORGE HAS BEEN NAMED BEST OVERALL BUSINESS TECHNOLOGY SOLUTION AND BEST EMERGING TECHNOLOGY OF 2022 AS PART OF THE ANNUAL SIIA CODIE™ AWARDS. THE CODIE AWARDS RECOGNIZE COMPANIES PRODUCING THE MOST INNOVATIVE BUSINESS TECHNOLOGY PRODUCTS ACROSS THE COUNTRY AND AROUND THE WORLD.
