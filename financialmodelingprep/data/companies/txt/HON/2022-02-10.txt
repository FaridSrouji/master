HONEYWELL SELECTED BY LOCKHEED MARTIN SIKORSKY-BOEING TO POWER DEFIANT X HELICOPTER IN KEY U.S. ARMY PURSUIT. PHOENIX, FEB. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) HAS BEEN SELECTED TO PROVIDE ITS NEW HTS7500 TURBOSHAFT ENGINE AS THE POWER BEHIND THE LOCKHEED MARTIN SIKORSKY-BOEING DEFIANT X® HELICOPTER, CURRENTLY A CONTENDER TO WIN THE U.S. ARMY'S FUTURE LONG-RANGE ASSAULT AIRCRAFT (FLRAA) COMPETITION. THE HTS7500 TURBOSHAFT ENGINE IS THE NEWEST AND MOST TECHNOLOGICALLY ADVANCED MODEL IN HONEYWELL'S FAMILY OF MILITARY ENGINES, WHICH INCLUDES THE ICONIC T55 ENGINE THAT HAS POWERED THE H-47 FLEET FOR OVER 60 YEARS.
