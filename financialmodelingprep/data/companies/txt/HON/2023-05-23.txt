HONEYWELL RELEASES CYBER INSIGHTS TO BETTER IDENTIFY CYBERSECURITY THREATS AND VULNERABILITIES. THE NEW SOFTWARE-LED SOLUTION ENABLES ORGANIZATIONS TO DEFEND AGAINST CYBERSECURITY THREATS IN THEIR OPERATIONAL TECHNOLOGY (OT) ENVIRONMENTS ATLANTA , MAY 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS OPERATIONAL TECHNOLOGY (OT) CYBERSECURITY SOLUTION, HONEYWELL FORGE CYBERSECURITY+ | CYBER INSIGHTS, TO ASSIST CUSTOMERS IN IMPROVING THE AVAILABILITY, RELIABILITY AND SAFETY OF THEIR INDUSTRIAL CONTROL SYSTEMS AND OPERATIONS. CYBER INSIGHTS IS DESIGNED TO INTEGRATE INFORMATION FROM MULTIPLE OT DATA SOURCES IN ORDER TO PROVIDE A CUSTOMER WITH ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS INTO THEIR FACILITY'S CYBERSECURITY VULNERABILITIES AND THREATS, ALLOWING THE CUSTOMER TO MANAGE THEIR COMPLIANCE STRATEGY, THEREBY HELPING REDUCE THEIR OVERALL CYBERSECURITY RISKS.
