HONEYWELL COLLABORATES WITH THE UNIVERSITY OF TEXAS AT AUSTIN FOR INNOVATIVE CARBON CAPTURE AND STORAGE TECHNOLOGY. DES PLAINES, ILL., DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) ANNOUNCED TODAY AN AGREEMENT WITH THE UNIVERSITY OF TEXAS AT AUSTIN THAT WILL ENABLE THE LOWER-COST CAPTURE OF CARBON DIOXIDE EMISSIONS FROM POWER PLANTS AND HEAVY INDUSTRY1.
