VSE AVIATION ANNOUNCES DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT WITH HONEYWELL AEROSPACE. VSE AVIATION ANNOUNCES DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT WITH HONEYWELL AEROSPACE.VSE CORP - VSE AVIATION SUBSIDIARY HAS ENTERED INTO AN EXCLUSIVE, TWO-YEAR DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT WITH HONEYWELL AEROSPACE.VSE CORP - VSE EXPECTS TO SELL SYSTEM UNITS UNDER BOTH PROGRAMS BEGINNING IN Q3 2020.VSE CORP - UNDER AGREEMENT, WILL BE EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTOR OF HONEYWELL'S LASEREF IV INERTIAL REFERENCE SYSTEM IN AMERICAS & ASIA PACIFIC REGIONS.
