HONEYWELL TO OPEN ADVANCED WAREHOUSE AUTOMATION R&D CENTER IN CZECH REPUBLIC. CHARLOTTE, N.C., NOV. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO BUILD A NEW ADVANCED RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT (R&D) TESTING FACILITY FOR ITS HONEYWELL INTELLIGRATED WAREHOUSE AUTOMATION BUSINESS TO MEET GROWING DEMAND FOR TECHNOLOGIES THAT ENABLE FASTER, MORE ACCURATE SUPPLY CHAINS.
