HONEYWELL APPOINTS ROBIN WATSON TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , SEPT. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED ROBIN WATSON, 55, FORMER CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER (CEO) OF JOHN WOOD GROUP PLC, TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS AS AN INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR.
