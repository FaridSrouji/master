DENSO, HONEYWELL ASCEND INTO URBAN AIR MOBILITY WITH EXPANDED ALLIANCE. SOUTHFIELD, MICH., AND PHOENIX, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DENSO, A LEADING MOBILITY SUPPLIER, HAS SIGNED A LONG-TERM AGREEMENT WITH AEROSPACE LEADER HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON), ESTABLISHING AN ALLIANCE FOCUSED ON ELECTRIC PROPULSION UNITS TO MEET NEW AEROSPACE NEEDS.
