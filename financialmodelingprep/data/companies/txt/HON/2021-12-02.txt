HONEYWELL'S NEW AIR MONITOR ALERTS WHEN INDOOR CONDITIONS MAY PRESENT INCREASED RISK FACTORS FOR EXPOSURE TO AIRBORNE VIRUSES. CHARLOTTE, N.C., DEC. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW, USER-FRIENDLY MONITOR THAT ALERTS USERS WHEN INDOOR AIR CONDITIONS MAY PRESENT AN INCREASED RISK OF POTENTIALLY TRANSMITTING AIRBORNE VIRUSES IN SCHOOLS, RESTAURANTS AND OTHER SPACES.
