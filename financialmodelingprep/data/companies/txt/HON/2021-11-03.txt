HONEYWELL SINE SOFTWARE NOW HELPS EMPLOYERS MANAGE AND TRACK COVID-19 VACCINATION STATUS. ATLANTA, NOV. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT HONEYWELL SINE, A WORKPLACE SAAS SOLUTION, CAN NOW BE USED TO COLLECT PROOF OF COVID-19 VACCINATION AND TESTING FROM VISITORS, CONTRACTORS AND STAFF WHILE ALSO MANAGING SITE ACCESS. THE VACCINATION TRACKER FEATURE WITHIN SINE WORKFLOWS HELPS COMPANIES CUSTOMIZE AND MANAGE THE COLLECTION OF INFORMATION TO SATISFY BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS, INCLUDING COVID-19 VACCINE MANDATES.
