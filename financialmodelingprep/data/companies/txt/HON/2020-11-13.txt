TOTAL TO USE HONEYWELL UOP TECHNOLOGY TO PRODUCE RENEWABLE JET FUEL AND DIESEL AT ITS ZERO-CRUDE PLATFORM IN FRANCE. DES PLAINES, III., NOV. 13, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NYSE: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED TOTAL WILL USE HONEYWELL UOP ECOFINING™ PROCESS TECHNOLOGY TO PRODUCE RENEWABLE FUELS, PRIMARILY FOR THE AVIATION INDUSTRY, AT ITS GRANDPUITS PLATFORM AT SEINE-ET-MARNE IN NORTH CENTRAL FRANCE....
