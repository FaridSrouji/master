HONEYWELL AND LOCKHEED MARTIN TO PROVIDE COMPONENTS FOR NASA'S ORION SPACECRAFT. HONEYWELL AND LOCKHEED MARTIN TO PROVIDE CRITICAL COMPONENTS FOR NASA'S ORION SPACECRAFT.HONEYWELL - AWARDED A CONTRACT BY LOCKHEED MARTIN TO SUPPORT PRODUCTION OF NASA'S ORION SPACECRAFT FLEET FOR UPCOMING ARTEMIS MISSIONS.HONEYWELL - CONTRACT TO SUPPLY COMPONENTS OF ORION CREW MODULE & SERVICE MODULE WILL BE MANAGED & PERFORMED OUT OF CO'S CLEARWATER, FLORIDA FACILITY.HONEYWELL - WILL PROVIDE 14 PRODUCT TYPES FOR ARTEMIS MISSIONS III THROUGH V, TO SUPPORT NASA'S LUNAR MISSIONS.HONEYWELL - NASA AWARDED LOCKHEED MARTIN A LONG-TERM, MULTIBILLION-DOLLAR PRODUCTION CONTRACT FOR ORION SPACECRAFT.HONEYWELL - WORK UNDER CONTRACT FOR COMPONENTS FOR NASA'S ORION SPACECRAFT WILL ALSO BE CONDUCTED AT CO'S GLENDALE, ARIZONA & PUERTO RICO FACILITIES.
