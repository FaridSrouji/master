ULTIUM CELLS SELECTS HONEYWELL QUALITY CONTROL SYSTEM FOR STATE-OF-THE-ART LITHIUM-ION BATTERY PRODUCTION PLANT. HOUSTON, SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ULTIUM CELLS LLC, A JOINT VENTURE BETWEEN LG ENERGY SOLUTION AND GENERAL MOTORS (GM), WILL USE HONEYWELL'S QUALITY CONTROL SYSTEM (QCS) TO ENSURE THE QUALITY AND INTEGRITY OF THE LITHIUM-ION BATTERIES PRODUCED AT ITS NEW MANUFACTURING PLANT IN LORDSTOWN, OHIO.
HONEYWELL EXPANDS PRODUCT OFFERINGS FOR RAPIDLY GROWING SMALL-SATELLITE MARKET. PHOENIX, SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON), HAS RAMPED UP ITS COMMITMENT TO THE RAPIDLY GROWING SMALL SATELLITE MARKET SEGMENT BY ADDING THREE NEW PRODUCTS TO ITS LINEUP OF SPACE OFFERINGS.
