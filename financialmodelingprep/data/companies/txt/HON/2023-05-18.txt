HONEYWELL SUPPORTS SYDNEY OPERA HOUSE TO REACH SIX STAR GREEN STAR RATING. HONEYWELL – A GLOBAL GOALS PARTNER OF THE SYDNEY OPERA HOUSE – SUPPORTS AUSTRALIA'S MOST FAMOUS BUILDING TO ATTAIN GLOBALLY RECOGNISED SUSTAINABILITY RATING THROUGH INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGY SYDNEY , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON), A SYDNEY OPERA HOUSE GLOBAL GOALS PARTNER SINCE 2021, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS CONTRIBUTIONS TO HELP THE UNESCO WORLD HERITAGE-LISTED BUILDING ACHIEVE A 6  STAR GREEN STAR RATING FROM THE GREEN BUILDING COUNCIL OF AUSTRALIA (GBCA). KEY TO THIS ACHIEVEMENT WAS RETROFITTING THE SYDNEY OPERA HOUSE WITH HONEYWELL'S INNOVATIVE, READY NOW TECHNOLOGY TO HELP SUPPORT MORE SUSTAINABLE OPERATIONS AND TO REDUCE THE BUILDING'S ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT.
