HONEYWELL BEGINS USING SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL TO TEST ITS WORLD-CLASS AIRCRAFT AUXILIARY POWER UNITS AND PROPULSION ENGINES. SAF BLEND TO BE USED ON APUS AND ENGINES AT THE PHOENIX FACILITY PHOENIX , JAN. 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) RECENTLY RECEIVED ITS FIRST DELIVERY OF SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL AT ITS PHOENIX ENGINES CAMPUS. THE FUEL WILL SUPPORT DEVELOPMENT AND PRODUCTION TESTING OF AUXILIARY POWER UNITS (APUS) AND PROPULSION ENGINES AT THE SITE, ALONG WITH TESTING OF FIELDED UNITS FROM HONEYWELL'S REPAIR AND OVERHAUL FACILITY.
