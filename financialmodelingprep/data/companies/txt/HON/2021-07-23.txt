HONEYWELL BEATS GUIDANCE AND DELIVERS OUTSTANDING SECOND-QUARTER RESULTS DRIVEN BY SALES AND PROFIT GROWTH; RAISES MIDPOINT OF ADJUSTED EPS GUIDANCE BY 15 CENTS.
