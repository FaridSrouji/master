HONEYWELL ANNOUNCES AVAILABILITY OF HONEYWELL SINE SOLUTION TO EMPOWER OCCUPANTS AND ENABLE SAFER RETURN TO WORKPLACE. ATLANTA, JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT HONEYWELL SINE, AN INNOVATIVE MOBILE SOLUTION TO SUPPORT ORGANIZATIONS IN THEIR PLANS TO RETURN WORKERS TO BUILDINGS, IS NOW AVAILABLE. FROM MANAGING VARYING OCCUPANCY LEVELS TO PRE-SCREENING VISITORS BEFORE ARRIVAL, SINE CAN HELP FACILITY MANAGERS AND OPERATORS CREATE A SIMPLIFIED SITE EXPERIENCE THAT OFFERS FRICTIONLESS ACCESS BY CHECKING CREDENTIALS WITH A MOBILE DEVICE THROUGH INTEGRATIONS WITH ACCESS CONTROL SYSTEMS.
