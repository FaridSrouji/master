HONEYWELL SURVEY REVEALS 72% OF OFFICE WORKERS WORLDWIDE WORRY ABOUT AIR QUALITY IN THEIR BUILDINGS. ATLANTA, FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEARLY THREE OUT OF FOUR SURVEYED OFFICE WORKERS EXPRESS CONCERN ABOUT THEIR BUILDING'S INDOOR AIR QUALITY (IAQ), ACCORDING TO A REPORT RELEASED TODAY BY HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON). "WORKPLACE AIR QUALITY: A GLOBAL CONCERN EMERGES" PRESENTS THE FINDINGS OF HONEYWELL'S SECOND ANNUAL HEALTHY BUILDINGS SURVEY, WHICH RECENTLY QUERIED 3,000 OFFICE WORKERS IN BUILDINGS WITH 500-PLUS WORKERS IN ASEAN, GERMANY, INDIA, THE MIDDLE EAST, THE UNITED KINGDOM AND THE UNITED STATES.
