HONEYWELL TEAMS WITH ASTRAZENECA TO DEVELOP NEXT-GENERATION RESPIRATORY INHALERS THAT USE NEAR-ZERO GLOBAL WARMING POTENTIAL PROPELLANT. CHARLOTTE, N.C., FEB. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED A COMMERCIAL PARTNERSHIP WITH ASTRAZENECA (LON: AZN) TO DEVELOP AND BRING TO MARKET NEXT-GENERATION RESPIRATORY INHALERS THAT USE NEAR-ZERO GLOBAL WARMING POTENTIAL (GWP) PROPELLANTS TO TREAT ASTHMA AND CHRONIC OBSTRUCTIVE PULMONARY DISEASE (COPD).
