JACKSONVILLE JAGUARS AND HONEYWELL COLLABORATE TO CREATE A SAFER EXPERIENCE FOR PLAYERS AND STAFF AT TIAA BANK FIELD. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NYSE: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS SUPPORTING THE NFL'S JACKSONVILLE JAGUARS TO HELP PROTECT PLAYERS AND STAFF THROUGHOUT FOOTBALL AND FRONT OFFICE SPACES AT TIAA BANK FIELD.
