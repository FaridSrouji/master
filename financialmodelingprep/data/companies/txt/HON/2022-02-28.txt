HONEYWELL ENERGY STORAGE SOLUTIONS TO POWER HECATE ENERGY'S NEW SOLAR PARK LOCATED IN NEW MEXICO. HOUSTON, FEB. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL SUPPLY HECATE ENERGY WITH AN ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM (ESS) FOR A SOLAR PARK LOCATED IN NORTHERN NEW MEXICO. WHEN COMPLETED IN MID-2022, THE 50-MEGAWATT (MW) SOLAR FARM WILL BE CAPABLE OF SUPPLYING ENOUGH ELECTRICITY TO POWER UP TO 16,000 AVERAGE NEW MEXICAN HOMES FOR A YEAR.
