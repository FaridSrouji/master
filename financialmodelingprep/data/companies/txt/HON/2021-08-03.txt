FORT BENNING EXPANDS WORK WITH HONEYWELL TO IMPROVE ENERGY RESILIENCE. ATLANTA, AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE NEXT PHASE OF A MODERNIZATION PROJECT WITH FORT BENNING, A U.S. ARMY POST LOCATED ON THE BORDER OF ALABAMA AND GEORGIA, TO IMPROVE ENERGY RESILIENCE AND CREATE A MORE COMFORTABLE LIVING AND WORKING ENVIRONMENT FOR FORT BENNING SOLDIERS, EMPLOYEES AND TENANTS. THE $23 MILLION PROJECT WILL HELP SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCE ENERGY CONSUMPTION ON THE BASE AND GENERATE $1.4 MILLION IN ANNUAL ENERGY SAVINGS.
