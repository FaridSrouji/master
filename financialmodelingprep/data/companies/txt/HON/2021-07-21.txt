HONEYWELL AND CAMBRIDGE QUANTUM REACH NEW MILESTONES. BROOMFIELD, COLO., JULY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) AND CAMBRIDGE QUANTUM TODAY ANNOUNCED THREE SCIENTIFIC AND TECHNICAL MILESTONES THAT DEMONSTRATE THE VIABILITY OF LARGE-SCALE QUANTUM COMPUTING AND SHOW THAT QUANTUM-ENABLED SOLUTIONS FOR OPTIMIZATION ARE CLOSER THAN EXPECTED.
