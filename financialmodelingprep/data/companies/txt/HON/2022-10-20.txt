HONEYWELL LAUNCHES ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY INDEX SHOWING SUSTAINABILITY LEADERS' SENTIMENT ON PAST PROGRESS AND FUTURE EXPECTATIONS TOWARDS CORPORATE ENVIRONMENTAL GOALS. FIRST MULTI-INDUSTRY AND MULTI-REGIONAL INDEX POINTS TO CORPORATE CONFIDENCE ABOUT PAST PROGRESS, BUT LESS OPTIMISM ABOUT SHORT- AND LONG-TERM FUTURE GOALS NEARLY NINE OUT OF TEN ORGANIZATIONS ARE PLANNING TO INCREASE SUSTAINABILITY BUDGETS FOR THE COMING TWELVE MONTHS RESPONDENTS REVEAL NEAR-TERM CLIMATE EFFORTS REVOLVE AROUND PROCESS AND BEHAVIORAL CHANGES ACROSS THE ORGANIZATION, WITH LESS EMPHASIS ON TECHNOLOGICAL INTEGRATIONS CHARLOTTE, N.C. , OCT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON), A GLOBAL LEADER IN ENERGY AND CLIMATE TRANSITION SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY INDEX.
