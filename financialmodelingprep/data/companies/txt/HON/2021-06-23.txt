HONEYWELL SURVEY REVEALS HEALTHIER BUILDINGS TOP THE LIST OF BACK-TO-WORK PRIORITIES. ATLANTA, JUNE 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ACCORDING TO A REPORT RELEASED TODAY BY HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON), 75% OF SURVEYED U.S. FACILITY MANAGERS SAY COVID-19 HAS CAUSED THEM TO PERMANENTLY RETHINK HOW THEIR FACILITY OPERATES. NEARLY 6 IN 10 RESPONDENTS ARE MORE LIKELY TO INVEST IN INDOOR AIR QUALITY OPTIMIZATION AND OTHER HEALTHY BUILDING SOLUTIONS, WHILE 7 IN 10 ARE NOW MORE WILLING TO INVEST IN SMART BUILDING SOLUTIONS THAT HELP DRIVE EFFICIENCY AND SUPPORT SUSTAINABILITY EFFORTS.
