HONEYWELL FUEL TANK SAFETY TECHNOLOGY SELECTED BY COPA AIRLINES FOR BOEING 737 AIRCRAFT. PHOENIX, AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) HAS BEEN SELECTED BY COPA AIRLINES TO PROVIDE ITS AIR SEPARATION MODULE (ASM) KIT FOR COPA'S FLEET OF BOEING 737 AIRCRAFT. THIS NEW TECHNOLOGY OFFERING IS A DROP-IN REPLACEMENT FOR COPA'S EXISTING ASMS, WHICH ARE INSTALLED ON LARGER AIRCRAFT TO HELP REDUCE THE RISK OF FUEL TANK FIRES.
