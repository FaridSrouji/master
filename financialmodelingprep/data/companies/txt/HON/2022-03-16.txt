LINCOLN HARRIS SELECTS HONEYWELL FORGE SAAS SOLUTION TO HELP LOWER COSTS AND IMPROVE OCCUPANT EXPERIENCE. THE FULL-SERVICE REAL ESTATE FIRM IMPLEMENTS HONEYWELL'S ENTERPRISE PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE TO IMPROVE BUILDING MAINTENANCE AND HELP ACHIEVE SUSTAINABILITY TARGETS.
