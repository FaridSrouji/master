HONEYWELL EXPANDS N95 FACE MASK PRODUCTION. HONEYWELL FURTHER EXPANDS N95 FACE MASK PRODUCTION BY ADDING MANUFACTURING CAPABILITIES IN PHOENIX.HONEYWELL - ADDING MANUFACTURING CAPABILITIES IN PHOENIX TO PRODUCE N95 FACE MASKS IN SUPPORT OF U.S. GOVERNMENT'S RESPONSE TO NOVEL CORONAVIRUS.HONEYWELL - ANTICIPATES THAT NEW MASK PRODUCTION LINE IN PHOENIX WILL CREATE MORE THAN 500 NEW JOBS IN ARIZONA.
