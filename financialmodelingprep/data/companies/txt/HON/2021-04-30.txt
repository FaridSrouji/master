HONEYWELL ANNOUNCES TRANSFER OF STOCK EXCHANGE LISTING TO NASDAQ. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NYSE: HON) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL TRANSFER THE LISTING OF ITS COMMON STOCK AND LISTED BONDS FROM THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE TO THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET ("NASDAQ").
