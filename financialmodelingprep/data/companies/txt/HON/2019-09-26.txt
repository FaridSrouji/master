HONEYWELL TO INCREASE DIVIDEND BY 10% EFFECTIVE IN FOURTH QUARTER 2019, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 6, 2019. HONEYWELL ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A 10% INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S REGULAR ANNUAL CASH DIVIDEND FROM $3.28 TO $3.60 PER SHARE. THE INCREASE WILL BE EFFECTIVE STARTING WITH THE FOURTH-QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.90 PER SHARE, WHICH WAS DECLARED, AND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 6, 2019, OUT OF SURPLUS TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 15, 2019.
