HONEYWELL TO PROVIDE COCKPIT TECHNOLOGIES FOR 7-SEATER LILIUM JET. PHOENIX, JUNE 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) HAS BEEN SELECTED BY LILIUM TO DEVELOP THE 7-SEATER LILIUM JET'S AVIONICS AND FLIGHT CONTROL SYSTEMS. THE COMPACT FLY-BY-WIRE SYSTEM WILL ACT AS THE FLIGHT CONTROL SYSTEM ON THE ALL-ELECTRIC 7-SEATER LILIUM JET, RESPONSIBLE FOR CONTROLLING ITS MOVEABLE PARTS, INCLUDING THE 36 CONTROL SURFACES AND DUCTED FANS THAT PROVIDE ITS HIGH LEVELS OF MANEUVERABILITY IN EVERY STAGE OF FLIGHT.
