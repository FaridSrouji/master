HONEYWELL INVESTS IN ENERGY-AS-A-SERVICE MARKET LEADER REDAPTIVE TO DRIVE PRIVATE SECTOR ENERGY CONTRACTING. LEVERAGES COMPLEMENTARY ABILITIES AND EXPERTISE TO ENABLE ENERGY EFFICIENCY AND SUSTAINABILITY UPGRADES WITH LITTLE-TO-NO UPFRONT CAPITAL INVESTMENT FOR PRIVATE-SECTOR-OWNED COMMERCIAL BUILDINGS ATLANTA , MARCH 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC INVESTMENT IN REDAPTIVE, WHICH WILL ACCELERATE A COLLABORATION TO BRING ENERGY-AS-A-SERVICE (EAAS) CAPABILITIES TO PRIVATE SECTOR-OWNED COMMERCIAL AND INDUSTRIAL BUILDINGS. THIS INVESTMENT HELPS ENABLE THE RAPID DEPLOYMENT OF TECHNOLOGIES DESIGNED TO REDUCE CARBON EMISSIONS ACROSS A LARGE PORTFOLIO OF BUILDINGS.
