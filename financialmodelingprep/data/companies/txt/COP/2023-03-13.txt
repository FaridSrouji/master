CONOCOPHILLIPS WELCOMES RECORD OF DECISION ON THE WILLOW PROJECT. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CONOCOPHILLIPS (NYSE: COP) WELCOMES THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR'S RECORD OF DECISION (ROD) ON THE WILLOW PROJECT, ADOPTING THE THREE CORE PADS DETAILED IN THE BUREAU OF LAND MANAGEMENT (BLM) PREFERRED ALTERNATIVE E. CONOCOPHILLIPS ALASKA COMPLETED EXTENSIVE PREPARATIONS WITH KEY CONTRACTORS AND EXPECTS TO IMMEDIATELY INITIATE GRAVEL ROAD CONSTRUCTION ACTIVITIES. THE COMPANY CONTINUES TO REVIEW THE ROD AND WILL ADVANCE INTERNAL APPROVAL PROCESSES TOWARDS A FINAL INVESTMENT DECISION.
