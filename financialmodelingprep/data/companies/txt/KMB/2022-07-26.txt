KIMBERLY-CLARK ANNOUNCES SECOND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. DALLAS , JULY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) TODAY REPORTED SECOND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY SECOND QUARTER 2022 NET SALES OF $5.1 BILLION INCREASED 7 PERCENT COMPARED TO THE YEAR-AGO PERIOD, INCLUDING ORGANIC SALES GROWTH OF 9 PERCENT.
