NATIONAL DIAPER NEED AWARENESS WEEK AIMS TO SPREAD AWARENESS AND EDUCATION AROUND DIAPER NEED IN THE U.S., WHICH AFFECTS 1 IN 3 FAMILIES. NEENAH, WIS., SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THIS WEEK KICKS OFF NATIONAL DIAPER NEED AWARENESS WEEK, WHICH WAS CREATED IN 2012 BY THE NATIONAL DIAPER BANK NETWORK (NDBN) AND HUGGIES®  AS A WAY TO DRAW ATTENTION TO THE ISSUE OF DIAPER NEED IN THE UNITED STATES.
