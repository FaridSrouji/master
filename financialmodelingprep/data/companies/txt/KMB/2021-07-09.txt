KIMBERLY-CLARK ANNOUNCES SCHEDULE FOR SECOND QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. DALLAS, JULY 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) WILL ISSUE ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS ON FRIDAY, JULY 23 WITH A NEWS RELEASE ISSUED VIA PR NEWSWIRE AND FIRST CALL AT APPROXIMATELY 6:30 A.M. CDT. IN ADDITION TO THE NEWS RELEASE, THE COMPANY WILL MAKE AVAILABLE PREPARED MANAGEMENT REMARKS FROM KIMBERLY-CLARK'S CHAIRMAN AND CEO MIKE HSU AND SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CFO MARIA HENRY AT APPROXIMATELY 7 A.M.
