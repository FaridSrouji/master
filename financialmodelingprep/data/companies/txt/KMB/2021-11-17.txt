KIMBERLY-CLARK NAMED BY SERAMOUNT AS ONE OF THE TOP 75 COMPANIES FOR EXECUTIVE WOMEN FOR FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. DALLAS, NOV. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WAS NAMED ONE OF THE 2021 TOP 75 COMPANIES FOR EXECUTIVE WOMEN BY SERAMOUNT (FORMERLY WORKING MOTHER MEDIA) FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. THIS HONOR RECOGNIZES U.S. CORPORATIONS THAT CHAMPION WOMEN'S ADVANCEMENT, WITH A FOCUS ON SUCCESSION PLANNING, PROFIT AND LOSS RESPONSIBILITY, GENDER PAY PARITY, SUPPORT PROGRAMS, AND WORK-LIFE BALANCE PROGRAMS.
