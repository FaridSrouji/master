POISE® BRAND DONATING UP TO $1 MILLION IN PRODUCT AS PART OF FIRST-EVER TELL-A-THONS SHATTERING POSTPARTUM STIGMAS. CHICAGO, DEC. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  POISE® BRAND,  THE NUMBER ONE BRAND AND CATEGORY LEADER DEDICATED TO BLADDER LEAKAGE SOLUTIONS, IS DONATING TWO BOXES OF ONE BY POISE® TO CHARITIES SERVING WOMEN IN NEED FOR EVERY COMMENT SHARED DURING ITS FIRST-EVER TELL-A-THON SERIES THIS DECEMBER.† IN ADDITION TO SUPPORTING CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS, INCLUDING NEW MOMS AND WOMEN IN NEED (WIN) WITH UP TO $1 MILLION IN PRODUCT, THE ONE BY POISE® TELL-A-THONS CREATE A FORUM FOR WOMEN TO SHARE WHAT THEY WISH THEY HAD BEEN TOLD ABOUT THE JOURNEY OF MOTHERHOOD AND POSTPARTUM BODY CHANGES.
