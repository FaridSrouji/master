KIMBERLY-CLARK DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS, AUG. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.14 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 4, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON SEPTEMBER 10, 2021.
