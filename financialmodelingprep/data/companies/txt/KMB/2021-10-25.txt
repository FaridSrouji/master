KIMBERLY-CLARK ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. DALLAS, OCT. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) TODAY REPORTED THIRD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY THIRD QUARTER 2021 NET SALES OF $5.0 BILLION INCREASED 7 PERCENT COMPARED TO THE YEAR-AGO PERIOD, WITH AN ORGANIC SALES INCREASE OF 4 PERCENT.
