KIMBERLY-CLARK ANNOUNCES NOMINATION OF SYLVIA MATHEWS BURWELL TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. DALLAS, MARCH 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) ANNOUNCED THAT SYLVIA MATHEWS BURWELL HAS BEEN NOMINATED FOR ELECTION TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT THE COMPANY'S 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS ON APRIL 27, 2022. CURRENT BOARD MEMBER IAN READ WILL BE RETIRING IN 2022.
