NEW MISSISSIPPI INITIATIVE WILL COMBAT LEADING CAUSE OF INVOLUNTARY LAND LOSS AMONG BLACK FAMILIES. JACKSON, MISS., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CENTER FOR HEIRS' PROPERTY PRESERVATION™ AND THE MISSISSIPPI CENTER FOR JUSTICE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE MOBILE BASIN HEIRS' PROPERTY SUPPORT INITIATIVE, A TWO-YEAR PROGRAM DESIGNED TO HELP HISTORICALLY UNDERSERVED FAMILIES IN MISSISSIPPI PROTECT AND KEEP THEIR FORESTLAND; BUILD GENERATIONAL WEALTH; AND PROMOTE PRODUCTIVE, SUSTAINABLY MANAGED FORESTS.
