KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION REVISES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE FULL-YEAR 2019. KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION REVISED EARNING GUIDANCE FOR THE FULL-YEAR 2019. FOR THE YEAR, THE COMPANY RAISED ITS OUTLOOK FOR FISCAL 2019 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE AND ORGANIC SALES GROWTH. THE COMPANY NOW FORECASTS FULL-YEAR 2019 ORGANIC SALES GROWTH OF 3% TO 4% AND ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $6.75 TO $6.90. EARLIER, THE COMPANY'S OUTLOOK WAS FOR ORGANIC SALES GROWTH OF 3%, AND ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $6.65 TO $6.80. ON AVERAGE, ANALYSTS POLLED BY THOMSON REUTERS EXPECT THE COMPANY TO REPORT EARNINGS OF $6.81 PER SHARE ON REVENUES OF $18.43 BILLION FOR THE YEAR. ANALYSTS' ESTIMATES TYPICALLY EXCLUDE SPECIAL ITEMS.
