NEW STUDY BY U BY KOTEX® SHOWS 35% INCREASE IN PERIOD POVERTY SINCE 2018. NEENAH, WIS., MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN A NEW STUDY CONDUCTED BY U BY KOTEX ®, RESEARCH SHOWS THAT TWO IN FIVE PEOPLE HAVE STRUGGLED TO PURCHASE PERIOD PRODUCTS1, A 35% INCREASE FROM THE BRAND'S INITIAL RESEARCH IN 2018.
