KIMBERLY-CLARK EARNS PERFECT SCORE ON HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION'S 2022 CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX. DALLAS, JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT ONCE AGAIN RECEIVED A PERFECT SCORE OF 100 PERCENT ON THE HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION'S 2022 CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX (CEI), THE NATION'S FOREMOST BENCHMARKING SURVEY AND REPORT MEASURING CORPORATE POLICIES AND PRACTICES RELATED TO LESBIAN, GAY, BISEXUAL, TRANSGENDER AND QUEER (LGBTQ+) WORKPLACE EQUALITY. KIMBERLY-CLARK MET ALL OF THE CEI'S CRITERIA, EARNING THE DESIGNATION AS ONE OF THE 'BEST PLACES TO WORK FOR LGBTQ+ EQUALITY' IN THE UNITED STATES.
