KIMBERLY-CLARK ANNOUNCES SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. DALLAS, JULY 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) TODAY REPORTED SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY SECOND QUARTER 2021 NET SALES OF $4.7 BILLION INCREASED 2 PERCENT COMPARED TO THE YEAR-AGO PERIOD, WITH AN ORGANIC SALES DECLINE OF 3 PERCENT.
