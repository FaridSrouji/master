KIMBERLY-CLARK NAMES DOUG CUNNINGHAM TO LEAD ITS EMEA CONSUMER BUSINESS. DALLAS, SEPT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE:KMB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DOUG CUNNINGHAM, CURRENTLY MANAGING DIRECTOR OF KIMBERLY-CLARK AUSTRALIA AND NEW ZEALAND, HAS BEEN NAMED PRESIDENT OF ITS EMEA CONSUMER BUSINESS, AND REPORT TO CHAIRMAN AND CEO MIKE HSU.
