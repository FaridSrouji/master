KIMBERLY-CLARK NAMES ZACK HICKS AS CHIEF DIGITAL AND TECHNOLOGY OFFICER. DALLAS , JULY 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ZACK HICKS IN THE NEWLY CREATED ROLE OF CHIEF DIGITAL AND TECHNOLOGY OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JULY 26, 2022. HE IS A MEMBER OF THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM.
