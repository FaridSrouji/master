KIMBERLY-CLARK DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS , AUG. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.16 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 4, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON SEPTEMBER 9, 2022.
