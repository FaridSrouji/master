KIMBERLY-CLARK NAMES ANDREA ZAHUMENSKY TO LEAD ITS NORTH AMERICAN PERSONAL CARE PORTFOLIO, INCLUDING HUGGIES®, DEPEND® AND U BY KOTEX®. CHICAGO , SEPT. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ANDREA ZAHUMENSKY TO THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT, PERSONAL CARE FOR ITS NORTH AMERICA CONSUMER BUSINESS EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 13, 2022.
