FAIRFAX COUNTY PD DEPLOYS AXON'S MY90 FEEDBACK TOOL TO BETTER ENGAGE WITH COMMUNITY. FIRST MAJOR CITY CHIEF'S ASSOCIATION MEMBER TO DEPLOY AXON'S MY90 PLATFORM TO COLLECT CONFIDENTIAL FEEDBACK FROM COMMUNITY MEMBERS SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ., AUG. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED FAIRFAX COUNTY POLICE DEPARTMENT (FCPD), A MAJOR CITY CHIEF'S ASSOCIATION  MEMBER, IS UTILIZING AXON'S LATEST COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT TOOL, MY90 , AS A PART OF A COLLABORATIVE PARTNERSHIP.
