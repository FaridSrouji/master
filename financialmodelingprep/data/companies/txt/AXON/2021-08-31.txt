SIX FLAGS ENTERTAINMENT PARTNERS WITH AXON TO IMPLEMENT BODY-WORN CAMERAS AT ALL U.S. PARK LOCATIONS. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ., AUG. 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SIX FLAGS ENTERTAINMENT CORP., HAS EQUIPPED SECURITY STAFF ACROSS U.S. AMUSEMENT PARK LOCATIONS WITH BODY-WORN CAMERAS BACKED BY AXON'S DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT SOLUTION, AXON EVIDENCE.
