PUEBLA MUNICIPAL POLICE PARTNERS WITH AXON FOR LARGEST TASER DEVICE DEPLOYMENT IN MEXICO. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ., SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PUEBLA MUNICIPAL POLICE DEPARTMENT WILL EQUIP OFFICERS WITH TASER CONDUCTED ELECTRICAL DEVICES (CEDS) AS A CRITICAL DE-ESCALATION TOOL TO SAFELY HANDLE SITUATIONS AND REDUCE INJURIES TO OFFICERS AND THE PUBLIC.
