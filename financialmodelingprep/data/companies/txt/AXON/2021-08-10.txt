TASER SELF-DEFENSE PARTNERS WITH KRYPTEK TO LAUNCH LIMITED EDITION PERSONAL SELF-DEFENSE PRODUCT. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ., AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TASER SELF-DEFENSE , THE CONSUMER DIVISION OF AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS NEW SELF-DEFENSE PRODUCT, THE TASER PULSE: KRYPTEK EDITION, FEATURING A UNIQUE CAMOUFLAGE PATTERN FROM KRYPTEK'S ALTITUDE LINE.
