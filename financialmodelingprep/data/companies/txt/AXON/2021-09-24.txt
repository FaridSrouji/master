AXON ANNOUNCES MICROSOFT, SKYDIO AND VERIZON AS TOP SPONSORS FOR 2021 ACCELERATE CONFERENCE. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ., SEPT. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED MICROSOFT AS THE TITLE SPONSOR OF AXON ACCELERATE, THE PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE.
