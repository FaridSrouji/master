AXON LAUNCHES MOONSHOT GOAL AND RALLIES SUPPORT TO CUT GUN-RELATED DEATHS BETWEEN POLICE AND PUBLIC BY 50% IN 10 YEARS. LAW ENFORCEMENT AND COMMUNITY LEADERS JOIN AXON IN SHARED COMMITMENT AXON TO FUND THIRD-PARTY RESEARCH ON GUN-RELATED DEATHS BETWEEN POLICE AND THE PUBLIC TO BETTER TRACK DATA AND INFORM SOLUTIONS SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ. , OCT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AXON  (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGY LEADER, ANNOUNCED THIS WEEK AT THE ANNUAL INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF CHIEFS OF POLICE (IACP) CONFERENCE IN DALLAS, THAT IT IS JOINING FORCES WITH LAW ENFORCEMENT AND COMMUNITY LEADERS IN A MOONSHOT GOAL TO CUT GUN-RELATED DEATHS BETWEEN POLICE AND THE PUBLIC IN HALF BY 2033.
