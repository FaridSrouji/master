PRIMECORP SELECTS AXON EVIDENCE TO CONNECT ALL POLICE SERVICES ACROSS BRITISH COLUMBIA. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ., DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, AND ITS SUBSIDIARY, AXON PUBLIC SAFETY CANADA, INC., TODAY ANNOUNCED PRIMECORP , WILL BE DEPLOYING AXON'S DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT SOLUTION, AXON EVIDENCE , TO ALL SUPPORTED POLICE SERVICES IN BRITISH COLUMBIA.
