SAN BERNARDINO COUNTY DISTRICT ATTORNEY'S OFFICE DEPLOYS AXON JUSTICE PREMIER. SAN BERNARDINO COUNTY IN CALIFORNIA ADOPTS AXON'S DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM DESIGNED SPECIFICALLY FOR PROSECUTORS AND CRIMINAL DEFENSE LAWYERS SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ. , MAY 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AXON (NASDAQ: AXON ), THE GLOBAL PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE SAN BERNARDINO COUNTY DISTRICT ATTORNEY'S OFFICE SIGNED A 10-YEAR CONTRACT FOR AXON JUSTICE PREMIER  TO MANAGE THEIR DIGITAL EVIDENCE.
