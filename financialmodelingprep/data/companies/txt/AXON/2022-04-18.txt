COLUMBUS PD PARTNERS WITH AXON TO OUTFIT OFFICERS WITH NEXT GENERATION IN-CAR AND BODY CAMERA TECHNOLOGY. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ. , APRIL 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --   AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COLUMBUS DIVISION OF POLICE (OH), A MAJOR CITIES CHIEFS ASSOCIATION MEMBER, HAS SIGNED A 5-YEAR CONTRACT WITH AXON TO DEPLOY 2,000 AXON BODY 3 CAMERAS, 75 AXON FLEX 2 CAMERAS, 15 AXON INTERVIEW SYSTEMS, 460 AXON FLEET 3 IN-CAR CAMERA SYSTEMS FEATURING AXON'S AI-POWERED AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER (ALPR) AND 2,017 LICENSES FOR AXON EVIDENCE, AXON'S DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM.
