AXON APPOINTS JOSH ISNER AS CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER. SEASONED EXECUTIVE TO HELP DRIVE AXON'S NEXT PHASE OF GROWTH SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ. , JUNE 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, HAS ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF JOSH ISNER TO THE ROLE OF CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER (COO).
