GUJARAT STATE POLICE JOINS THE AXON NETWORK WITH LARGEST BODY-WORN CAMERA ROLLOUT IN INDIA. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ., AUG. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE GUJARAT STATE POLICE WILL EQUIP OFFICERS THROUGHOUT GUJARAT, INDIA WITH 10,350 BODY-WORN CAMERAS AND AXON'S DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT SOLUTION.
