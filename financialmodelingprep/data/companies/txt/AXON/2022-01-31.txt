AXON DIRECTOR DR. RICHARD CARMONA SPEARHEADS SHAREHOLDER-FRIENDLY GOVERNANCE PROPOSALS; CONCLUDES 15 YEARS OF BOARD SERVICE. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ., JAN. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DR. RICHARD CARMONA, THE 17TH US SURGEON GENERAL, WILL BE RETIRING FROM AXON'S BOARD AFTER 15 YEARS OF SERVING AS A DIRECTOR.
