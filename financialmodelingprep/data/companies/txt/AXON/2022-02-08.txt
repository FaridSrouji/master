AXON ANNOUNCES NEW EUROPEAN R&D OFFICE IN LONDON. LONDON AND SEATTLE, FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL CONTINUE ITS GLOBAL EXPANSION BY OPENING ITS FIRST EUROPEAN RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT (R&D) OFFICE IN LONDON. AXON HAS APPOINTED RAN MOKADY AS SITE LEAD AND WILL LOOK TO HIRE WORLD-CLASS ENGINEERS, PRODUCT MANAGERS AND RELATED ROLES OVER THE COMING MONTHS TO FURTHER AXON'S MISSION TO PROTECT LIFE AND GLOBALLY DELIVER DISRUPTIVE TECHNOLOGIES TO PUBLIC SAFETY.
