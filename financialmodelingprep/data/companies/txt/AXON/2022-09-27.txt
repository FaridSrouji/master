AXON APPOINTS ANDREA JAMES AS CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER. AWARDS EQUITY INDUCEMENT GRANT TO BRITTANY BAGLEY, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ. , SEPT. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AXON (NASDAQ: AXON), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTED PUBLIC SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ANDREA JAMES TO THE ROLE OF CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER (CCO).
