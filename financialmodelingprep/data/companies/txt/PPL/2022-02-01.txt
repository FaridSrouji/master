PPL CORPORATION JOINS ENERGY IMPACT PARTNERS' DEEP DECARBONIZATION FRONTIER FUND. ALLENTOWN, PA., FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL CORPORATION (NYSE: PPL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS JOINED ENERGY IMPACT PARTNERS, A LEADING GLOBAL INVESTOR IN THE TRANSITION TO A SUSTAINABLE FUTURE, IN THE LAUNCH OF ITS DEEP DECARBONIZATION FRONTIER FUND.
