GLOBAL RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT ORGANIZATION RECOGNIZES LG&E AND KU ENERGY LOUISVILLE, KY. , APRIL 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LG&E AND KU ENERGY WAS RECENTLY HONORED AS THE RECIPIENT OF A TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER AWARD FROM THE ELECTRIC POWER RESEARCH INSTITUTE (EPRI) FOR ITS ACHIEVEMENTS IN RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT.
