PPL CAPITAL FUNDING, INC. ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $900 MILLION OF 2.875% EXCHANGEABLE SENIOR NOTES. ALLENTOWN, PA. , FEB. 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL CAPITAL FUNDING, INC. ("PPL CAPITAL FUNDING"), A WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF PPL CORPORATION (NYSE: PPL), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF $900 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 2.875% EXCHANGEABLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (THE "NOTES") IN A PRIVATE PLACEMENT (THE "OFFERING") TO PERSONS REASONABLY BELIEVED TO BE QUALIFIED INSTITUTIONAL BUYERS PURSUANT TO RULE 144A UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED (THE "SECURITIES ACT").
