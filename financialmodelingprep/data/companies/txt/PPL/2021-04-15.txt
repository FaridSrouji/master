PPL CORPORATION SHOWCASES ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE INITIATIVES IN ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. ALLENTOWN, PA., APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL CORPORATION (NYSE: PPL) PUBLISHED ITS ANNUAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT TODAY, HIGHLIGHTING THE COMPANY'S PROGRESS AND APPROACH ON A WIDE RANGE OF ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE INITIATIVES, INCLUDING CLIMATE STRATEGY, ELECTRIFICIATION GOALS, COVID-19 RESPONSE AND DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION COMMITMENTS.
