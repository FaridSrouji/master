PPL RELEASES 2021 CLIMATE ASSESSMENT REPORT. ALLENTOWN, PA., NOV. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL CORPORATION RELEASED ITS UPDATED CLIMATE ASSESSMENT REPORT, WHICH HIGHLIGHTS RISKS AND OPPORTUNITIES ASSOCIATED WITH CLIMATE CHANGE, EVALUATES POTENTIAL FUTURE EMISSIONS AND GENERATION MIX USING SCENARIO ANALYSIS, AND OUTLINES THE COMPANY'S STRATEGY AND GOALS TO ENABLE A RESPONSIBLE TRANSITION TO A CLEANER ENERGY FUTURE.
