PPL ELECTRIC UTILITIES EARNS 2021 ENERGY STAR® PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARD. ALLENTOWN, PA., APRIL 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL ELECTRIC UTILITIES ANNOUNCED TUESDAY THAT IT HAS RECEIVED THE 2021 ENERGY STAR PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARD FROM THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (EPA) AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY.
