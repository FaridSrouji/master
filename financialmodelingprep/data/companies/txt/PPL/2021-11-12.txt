PPL CORPORATION JOINS ENERGY IMPACT PARTNERS' $1 BILLION FUND TO ACCELERATE GLOBAL DECARBONIZATION. ALLENTOWN, PA., NOV. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL CORPORATION (NYSE: PPL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS JOINED ENERGY IMPACT PARTNERS, A GLOBAL VENTURE CAPITAL PLATFORM LEADING THE TRANSITION TO A SUSTAINABLE FUTURE, IN THEIR FLAGSHIP FUND, FUND II.
