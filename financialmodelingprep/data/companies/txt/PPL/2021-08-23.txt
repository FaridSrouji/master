LANDIS+GYR SIGNS CONTRACT WITH LOUISVILLE GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY AND KENTUCKY UTILITIES COMPANY FOR ADVANCED METERING INFRASTRUCTURE AND IOT NETWORK. CHAM, SWITZERLAND, AUG. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LANDIS+GYR TECHNOLOGY INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF LANDIS+GYR GROUP AG (SIX:LAND) SIGNED A FIVE-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH LOUISVILLE GAS AND ELECTRIC COMPANY (LG&E) AND KENTUCKY UTILITIES COMPANY (KU) TO PROVIDE A COMPREHENSIVE ADVANCED METERING AND SMART GRID INFRASTRUCTURE. THE CONTRACT, WHICH COVERS BOTH OPERATING UTILITIES, INCLUDES 930,000 ADVANCED ELECTRICITY METERS AND 300,000 GAS MODULES, IN ADDITION TO NETWORK EQUIPMENT AND SOFTWARE.
