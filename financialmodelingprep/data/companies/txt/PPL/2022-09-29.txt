PPL CORPORATION ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO SELL SAFARI ENERGY LLC. ALLENTOWN, PA. , SEPT. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL CORPORATION HAS REACHED AN AGREEMENT TO SELL PPL SAFARI HOLDINGS LLC, PARENT COMPANY OF SAFARI ENERGY LLC – A SUBSIDIARY THAT ACQUIRES SOLAR PROJECTS AND DEVELOPS AND MANAGES SOLAR FACILITIES FOR COMMERCIAL AND INDUSTRIAL CUSTOMERS AND PUBLIC SECTOR ORGANIZATIONS.
