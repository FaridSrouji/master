PPL CORPORATION RECEIVES APPROVAL FROM RHODE ISLAND DIVISION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES AND CARRIERS TO ACQUIRE THE NARRAGANSETT ELECTRIC COMPANY. ALLENTOWN, PA., FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL CORPORATION (NYSE: PPL) TODAY RECEIVED APPROVAL FROM THE RHODE ISLAND DIVISION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES AND CARRIERS TO ACQUIRE THE NARRAGANSETT ELECTRIC COMPANY FROM NATIONAL GRID USA.
