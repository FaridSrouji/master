PPL CORPORATION COMPLETES SALE OF U.K. UTILITY BUSINESS, RESULTING IN NET CASH PROCEEDS OF $10.4 BILLION. ALLENTOWN, PA., JUNE 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL CORPORATION (NYSE: PPL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED THE SALE OF ITS U.K. UTILITY BUSINESS, WESTERN POWER DISTRIBUTION (WPD), TO NATIONAL GRID FOR £7.8 BILLION, CONCLUDING THE FIRST OF TWO STRATEGIC TRANSACTIONS ANNOUNCED IN MARCH TO REPOSITION PPL AS A LEADING, HIGH-GROWTH, U.S.-FOCUSED ENERGY COMPANY.
