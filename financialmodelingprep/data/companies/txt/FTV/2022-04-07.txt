FORTIVE DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK. EVERETT, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORTIVE CORPORATION (“FORTIVE”) (NYSE: FTV) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.07 PER SHARE OF ITS COMMON STOCK, PAR VALUE $0.01 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JUNE 24, 2022 TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 27, 2022.
