FORTIVE APPOINTS ELENA ROSMAN AS VICE PRESIDENT OF INVESTOR RELATIONS. EVERETT, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORTIVE CORPORATION (“FORTIVE”) (NYSE: FTV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ELENA ROSMAN AS VICE PRESIDENT OF INVESTOR RELATIONS EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 29, 2021. MS. ROSMAN BRINGS EXTENSIVE FINANCE, SUSTAINABILITY, MARKETING AND STRATEGIC COMMUNICATIONS EXPERIENCE, INCLUDING MORE THAN TEN YEARS LEADING THE INVESTOR RELATIONS FUNCTIONS AT APTIV PLC AND HONEYWELL INTERNATIONAL, INC. IN THIS ROLE, MS. ROSMAN WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO CHUCK MCLAUGHLIN, FORTIVE'S SENIOR
