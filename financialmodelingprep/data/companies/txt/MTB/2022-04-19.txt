M&T BANK CORPORATION ANNOUNCES SERIES H PREFERRED STOCK QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BUFFALO, N.Y. , APRIL 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK CORPORATION ("M&T") (NYSE:MTB) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.3515625 PER SHARE ON ITS PERPETUAL FIXED-TO-FLOATING RATE NON-CUMULATIVE PREFERRED STOCK, SERIES H ("SERIES H PREFERRED STOCK").
