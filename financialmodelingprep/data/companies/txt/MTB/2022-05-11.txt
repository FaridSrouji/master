M&T BANK NAMES GRACE LEE EASTERN MASSACHUSETTS REGIONAL PRESIDENT AND GROUP LEAD OF NEW ENGLAND GOVERNMENT BANKING. BOSTON , MAY 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GRACE LEE HAS BEEN NAMED EASTERN MASSACHUSETTS REGIONAL PRESIDENT AND GROUP LEAD OF NEW ENGLAND GOVERNMENT BANKING AT M&T BANK. IN HER NEW ROLE, SHE WILL ENSURE COLLABORATION ACROSS ALL LINES OF BUSINESS, AND DIRECT COMMUNITY BANKING ACTIVITIES THAT ADVANCE THE BANK'S LOCALIZED FOCUS TO PROVIDE MORE VALUE FOR CUSTOMERS, BUSINESSES AND COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE REGION.
