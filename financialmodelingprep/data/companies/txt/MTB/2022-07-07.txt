EPSG SIGNIFICANTLY EXPANDS CREDIT FACILITY WITH PEOPLE'S UNITED, A DIVISION OF M&T BANK CORPORATION. MELVILLE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EPSG, A LEADING INTEGRATOR OF MERCHANT PAYMENT TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNIFICANTLY EXPANDED ITS EXISTING REVOLVING LINE OF CREDIT (“CREDIT FACILITY”) WITH PEOPLE'S UNITED, A DIVISION OF M&T BANK CORPORATION (“M&T”) (NASDAQ: MTB). EXPANSION OF THE CREDIT FACILITY INCLUDES AN INCREASE TO THE EXISTING REVOLVING LINE OF CREDIT ALONG WITH A SUBLIMIT TO TERM OUT ANY NEW CAPITAL EXPENDITURES OVER THE NEXT 12 MONTHS.
