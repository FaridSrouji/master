M&T BANK CORPORATION PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER RICH GOLD ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT. BUFFALO, N.Y. , JUNE 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RICHARD ("RICH") GOLD, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER FOR M&T BANK CORPORATION (NYSE: MTB), HAS ANNOUNCED HIS INTENTION TO RETIRE, EFFECTIVE AFTER THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023.
