M&T BANK OUTLINES $43 BILLION COMMUNITY GROWTH PLAN TO SUPPORT UNDERSERVED, COMMUNITIES OF COLOR AND SMALL BUSINESSES. BUFFALO, N.Y., OCT. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK CORPORATION (NYSE: MTB) ("M&T") ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS PLAN TO PROVIDE $43 BILLION IN LOANS, INVESTMENTS, AND OTHER FINANCIAL SUPPORT TO CREATE GREATER ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY FOR LOW-TO-MODERATE INCOME (LMI) FAMILIES AND NEIGHBORHOODS, AS WELL AS PEOPLE AND COMMUNITIES OF COLOR.
