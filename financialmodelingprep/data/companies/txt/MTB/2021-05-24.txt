M&T BANK CUSTOMERS SECURE NEARLY $10 BILLION IN PAYCHECK PROTECTION PROGRAM FUNDING. BUFFALO, N.Y., MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK CORPORATION ("M&T") (NYSE: MTB), THE NATION'S FIFTH LARGEST SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION (SBA) LENDER IN FISCAL YEAR 2020, TODAY ANNOUNCED MORE THAN 58,000 OF ITS BUSINESS CUSTOMERS RECEIVED APPROVAL FOR A TOTAL OF ABOUT $9.8 BILLION IN FUNDING THROUGH THE PAYCHECK PROTECTION PROGRAM (PPP), WHICH HAS NOW CLOSED TO MOST NEW APPLICANTS.
