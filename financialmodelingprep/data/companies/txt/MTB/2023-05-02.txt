M&T CHARITABLE FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES $11.8M IN PHASE - ONE AMPLIFY FUND GRANTS COMMITTED TO 105 NEW ENGLAND AND METRO NEW YORK NON-PROFITS. NONPROFITS RECEIVING FUNDING AS PART OF $25 MILLION INVESTMENT, WITH MORE PLANNED IN FUTURE FUNDING ROUNDS BUFFALO, N.Y. , MAY 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE M&T CHARITABLE FOUNDATION, THE PHILANTHROPIC ARM OF M&T BANK (NYSE: MTB), TODAY ANNOUNCED AMPLIFY FUND GRANT RECIPIENTS IN NEW ENGLAND AND METRO NEW YORK, TOTALING OVER $11.8M TO 105 NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATIONS ACROSS THE FORMER PEOPLE'S UNITED FOOTPRINT, WITH SOME PAYMENTS COMMITTED OVER A TWO- OR THREE-YEAR PERIOD.
