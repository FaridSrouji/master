M&T BANK AGAIN EARNS 'OUTSTANDING' RATING ON COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT ACT EXAM. BUFFALO, N.Y., JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK (NYSE: MTB) HAS AGAIN RECEIVED AN "OUTSTANDING" COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT ACT (CRA) RATING FROM THE FEDERAL RESERVE BANK OF NEW YORK – A STREAK OF EARNING THE REGULATORY AGENCY'S HIGHEST RATING FOR MEETING THE CREDIT NEEDS OF THE BANK'S COMMUNITIES THAT DATES TO 1982.
