M&T BANK NAMES JOHN D'ANGELO FIRST DIRECTOR OF ITS NEWLY CREATED ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE OFFICE. BUFFALO, N.Y., APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF JOHN D'ANGELO AS DIRECTOR OF ITS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) OFFICE, A NEWLY CREATED ROLE AND DIVISION.
