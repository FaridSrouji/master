M&T BANK EXPANDS COMMUNITY INVESTMENT WITH NEW $25 MILLION 'AMPLIFY FUND'. FIRST SIGNIFICANT PHILANTHROPIC EFFORT BY M&T BANK ACROSS THE FORMER PEOPLE'S UNITED FOOTPRINT BUFFALO, N.Y., MAY 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOLLOWING THE COMPLETION OF ITS MERGER WITH PEOPLE'S UNITED FINANCIAL INC. (PEOPLE'S UNITED) IN APRIL, M&T BANK CORPORATION (NYSE: MTB) ("M&T") TODAY LAUNCHED THE AMPLIFY FUND – A NEW $25 MILLION PHILANTHROPIC INVESTMENT TO PROVIDE FURTHER SUPPORT IN THE LEGACY PEOPLE'S UNITED COMMUNITIES THROUGHOUT CONNECTICUT, THE LARGER NEW ENGLAND MARKET AND NEW YORK.
