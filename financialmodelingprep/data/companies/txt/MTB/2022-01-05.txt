ERIC FELDSTEIN NAMED M&T BANK WESTERN NEW YORK REGIONAL PRESIDENT. BUFFALO, N.Y., JAN. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ERIC FELDSTEIN HAS BEEN NAMED M&T BANK (NYSE: MTB) REGIONAL PRESIDENT OF WESTERN NEW YORK – THE COMPANY'S HEADQUARTER MARKET AND WHERE IT WAS FOUNDED 165 YEARS AGO – IN ADDITION TO HIS ROLE AS HEAD OF BUSINESS BANKING, BEGINNING IN MARCH OF 2022.
