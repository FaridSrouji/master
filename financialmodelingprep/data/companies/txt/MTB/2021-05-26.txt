M&T BANK APPOINTS LONG-TIME LEADER MIKE KEEGAN TO HEAD NEW COMMUNITY MARKETS TEAM. BUFFALO, N.Y., MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK HAS APPOINTED MIKE KEEGAN, ITS LONG-TIME AREA EXECUTIVE FOR UPSTATE NEW YORK AND NEW ENGLAND, TO A NEW ROLE AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND HEAD OF COMMUNITY MARKETS.
M&T BANK CORPORATION, PEOPLE'S UNITED RECEIVE SHAREHOLDER APPROVAL FOR MERGER. BUFFALO, N.Y. AND BRIDGEPORT, CONN.
