M&T BANK SPOTLIGHTS CONNECTICUT SMALL BUSINESSES IN INNOVATIVE POP-UP SHOP CONCEPT AT CAPTAIN'S COVE SEAPORT. LOCAL SMALL BUSINESSES WILL HAVE ACCESS TO RETAIL SPACE AT BRIDGEPORT'S  PREMIER TOURIST DESTINATION  BRIDGEPORT, CONN. , AUG. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK IS BRINGING ITS INNOVATIVE SPOTLIGHT SHOP CONCEPT TO CONNECTICUT, PROVIDING LOCAL SMALL BUSINESSES WITH AN OPPORTUNITY TO MARKET THEIR UNIQUE PRODUCTS FROM A POP-UP SPACE IN BRIDGEPORT'S POPULAR CAPTAIN'S COVE SEAPORT.
