SIMPLY BUSINESS PARTNERS WITH ARCH INSURANCE TO EXPAND PROFESSIONAL LIABILITY PRODUCT OFFERING. BOSTON , DEC. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SIMPLY BUSINESS, LLC. , A BOSTON-BASED DIGITAL INSURANCE COMPANY FOCUSED ON SMALL BUSINESSES, TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP WITH SPECIALTY INSURER ARCH INSURANCE TO OFFER MONOLINE PROFESSIONAL LIABILITY SOLUTIONS TO SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS IN THE UNITED STATES.
