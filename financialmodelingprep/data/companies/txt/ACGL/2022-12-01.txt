ARCH INSURANCE NORTH AMERICA ANNOUNCES PROMOTION OF BRIAN FIRST TO PRESIDENT. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ARCH INSURANCE NORTH AMERICA (ARCH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BRIAN FIRST HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO PRESIDENT OF ARCH INSURANCE NORTH AMERICA, EFFECTIVE DEC. 1. FIRST WAS PROMOTED FROM CHIEF UNDERWRITING OFFICER (CUO) OF PROGRAMS, PROPERTY AND SPECIALTY, AND HE WILL CONTINUE TO REPORT TO MATT SHULMAN, CEO OF ARCH INSURANCE NORTH AMERICA.
