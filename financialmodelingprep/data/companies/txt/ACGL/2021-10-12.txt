ARCH CAPITAL GROUP LTD. ANNOUNCES CATASTROPHE LOSS ESTIMATES AND PROVIDES UPDATE ON SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION. PEMBROKE, BERMUDA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ARCH CAPITAL GROUP LTD. [NASDAQ: ACGL; “THE COMPANY”] REPORTS THAT ITS 2021 THIRD QUARTER RESULTS WILL BE NEGATIVELY IMPACTED BY THE EFFECTS OF HURRICANE IDA AND THE JULY FLOODING EVENTS IN CENTRAL EUROPE, AS WELL AS FROM OTHER MINOR GLOBAL EVENTS.
