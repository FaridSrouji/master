JACOBS TO PLAN DISMANTLING OF SOVIET-ERA NUCLEAR REACTORS. PROJECT IN LITHUANIA COULD OFFER IMPORTANT LESSONS FOR U.K. POWER STATION CLEANUP DALLAS , JAN. 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A CONSORTIUM COMPRISING WESTINGHOUSE ELECTRIC SPAIN, JACOBS (NYSE:J) AND THE LITHUANIAN ENERGY INSTITUTE WAS SELECTED TO PLAN DISMANTLING AND WASTE MANAGEMENT AT THE IGNALINA NUCLEAR POWER PLANT (INPP) IN LITHUANIA. SOVIET-DESIGNED IGNALINA COULD BE THE FIRST GRAPHITE-MODERATED REACTOR PLANT TO BE DISMANTLED, MAKING IT AN IMPORTANT TEST BED FOR METHODOLOGIES THAT COULD BE USED TO DECOMMISSION THE U.K.'S MAGNOX AND ADVANCED GAS-COOLED REACTORS, WHICH ALSO HAVE GRAPHITE CORES.
