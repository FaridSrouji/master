JACOBS WINS WEST DESERT TEST CENTER MISSION SUPPORT SERVICES CONTRACT FOR US ARMY'S MISSION AND INSTALLATION CONTRACTING COMMAND. DALLAS, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS AWARDED A COST-PLUS-FIXED-FEE CONTRACT TO SUPPORT THE WEST DESERT TEST CENTER (WDTC) AT DUGWAY PROVING GROUND (DPG), UTAH. THE DPG CONTRACT HAS AN ANTICIPATED VALUE OF MORE THAN $60 MILLION OVER A FIVE-YEAR PERIOD OF PERFORMANCE.
