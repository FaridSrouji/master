JACOBS NAMED PRIME DESIGNER ON MTA'S PENN STATION ACCESS. DALLAS, MARCH 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS SELECTED BY THE METROPOLITAN TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY (MTA) AS THE PRIME DESIGNER FOR PENN STATION ACCESS, THE LARGEST EXPANSION OF METRO-NORTH'S REGIONAL RAILROAD SYSTEM IN ITS HISTORY. JACOBS WILL DESIGN THE EXPANSION AND SUPPORT CONSTRUCTION, WHICH WILL BE LED BY THE HALMAR INTERNATIONAL/RAILWORKS JOINT VENTURE (JV).
