JACOBS WINS NATIONAL GEOSPATIAL-INTELLIGENCE AGENCY CONTRACT. DALLAS, SEPT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS AWARDED A CONTRACT BY THE NATIONAL GEOSPATIAL-INTELLIGENCE AGENCY (NGA) TO PROVIDE MISSION-DRIVEN AND DATA-CENTRIC SOLUTIONS IN SUPPORT OF GEOSPATIAL-INTELLIGENCE ENTERPRISE OPENDATASTORE (GEODS) SERVICES.
