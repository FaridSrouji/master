JACOBS AWARDED CONTRACT EXTENSION TO SUPPORT UK'S TRANSITION TO NET-ZERO. PROJECT MAXIMIZES EMISSION-FREE ELECTRICITY GENERATION DALLAS , JUNE 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE: J) WAS AWARDED A TWO-YEAR EXTENSION TO ITS STATIONS SERVICES AGREEMENT (SSA) WITH EDF NUCLEAR GENERATION (EDF), OPERATOR OF THE U.K.'S NUCLEAR POWER PLANTS. JACOBS' SCOPE INCLUDES SUPPORTING THE SAFE OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE OF THE ADVANCED GAS-COOLED REACTOR (AGR) STATIONS WHICH ACCOUNT FOR ABOUT 17% OF THE COUNTRY'S ELECTRICITY OUTPUT.
