JACOBS AWARDED PROJECT MANAGEMENT CONTRACT WITH JOHNS HOPKINS MEDICINE. DALLAS, JAN. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS SELECTED BY JOHNS HOPKINS MEDICINE TO PROVIDE PROJECT MANAGEMENT SERVICES FOR THE REDEVELOPMENT OF LABORATORY SPACE ON ITS MEDICAL CAMPUS IN BALTIMORE, MARYLAND.  PLANS ARE TO RENOVATE THE JOHNS HOPKINS HOSPITAL'S EXISTING CHILDREN'S MEDICAL AND SURGICAL CENTER (CMSC) AND CONSTRUCT A NEW 12-STORY NORTH TOWER ADDITION.
