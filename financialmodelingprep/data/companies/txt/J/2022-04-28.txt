JACOBS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS , APRIL 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JACOBS (NYSE:J) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYABLE TO SHAREHOLDERS IN THE AMOUNT OF $0.23 PER SHARE OF JACOBS COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JUNE 24, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 27, 2022.
