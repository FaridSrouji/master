JACOBS TO DESIGN KEY SAFETY FEATURE FOR ITER FUSION PROJECT. DALLAS, AUG. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS SELECTED TO DESIGN AND DELIVER THE FIRST PLASMA RADIOLOGICAL ENVIRONMENTAL MONITORING SYSTEM (REMS), A KEY SAFETY SYSTEM FOR ITER, THE WORLD'S LARGEST FUSION ENERGY EXPERIMENT. THE FIRST PLASMA REMS, WHICH PROTECTS WORKERS AND THE ENVIRONMENT AGAINST IONIZING RADIATION BY MONITORING RADIOLOGICAL ACTIVITY, IS NEEDED FOR THE MILESTONE MOMENT WHEN ITER STARTS OPERATIONS.
