JACOBS SELECTED AS PROGRAM MANAGER FOR ORLANDO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT TERMINAL C. PROGRAM WILL PROVIDE CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE FOR GROWING NUMBERS OF PASSENGERS AND ENGAGE LOCAL WORKFORCE DALLAS , MAY 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS SELECTED BY THE GREATER ORLANDO AVIATION AUTHORITY (GOAA) TO PROVIDE EXECUTIVE PROGRAM MANAGEMENT AND OVERSIGHT SERVICES AT ORLANDO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT. THROUGH INFRASTRUCTURE MANAGEMENT AT THE AIRPORT'S NEW TERMINAL C, JACOBS WILL HELP GOAA ACCOMMODATE AN ADDITIONAL 10-12 MILLION PASSENGERS ANNUALLY.
