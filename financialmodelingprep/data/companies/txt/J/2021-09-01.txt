JACOBS DELIVERS BROADLAND FLOOD ALLEVIATION PROJECT. DALLAS, SEPT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  JACOBS (NYSE:J), TOGETHER WITH THE ENVIRONMENT AGENCY AND CONTRACTOR BAM NUTTALL , CELEBRATES THE COMPLETION OF THE 20-YEAR BROADLAND FLOOD ALLEVIATION PROJECT TO IMPROVE 149 MILES OF FLOOD DEFENSES IN THE BROADS NATIONAL PARK, A WETLAND AREA OF INTERNATIONAL IMPORTANCE IN EASTERN NORFOLK, ENGLAND.
