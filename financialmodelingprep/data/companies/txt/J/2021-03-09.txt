JACOBS TO DELIVER PROGRAM MANAGEMENT SERVICES FOR DELHI NOIDA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT. DALLAS, MARCH 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) HAS BEEN SELECTED BY YAMUNA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT PRIVATE LIMITED (YIAPL), A SUBSIDIARY OF ZURICH AIRPORT INTERNATIONAL AG, AS PROGRAM MANAGEMENT CONSULTANT FOR THE UPCOMING DELHI NOIDA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (DNIA) PROJECT IN INDIA....
