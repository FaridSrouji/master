JACOBS SELECTED FOR URBAN DEVELOPMENT PROJECT IN SAUDI ARABIA. CONTRACT TO SUPPORT COUNTRY'S VISION 2030 TO DIVERSIFY ITS ECONOMY DALLAS , FEB. 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) AND JASARA HAVE BEEN APPOINTED BY NEOM COMPANY (NEOM) FOR THE LINE, ITS LINEAR AND COGNITIVE CITY UNDER DEVELOPMENT IN THE NORTHWEST OF SAUDI ARABIA. A CENTERPIECE FOR NEOM, THE LINE WILL PLAY A LEADING ROLE IN NEOM'S AIM OF ESTABLISHING ITSELF AS A NEW DESTINATION FOR GLOBAL TOURISM, INDUSTRY AND INNOVATION.
