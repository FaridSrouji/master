JACOBS AWARDED COMMERCIAL SPACE HEADQUARTERS CAMPUS CONTRACT. DALLAS, JAN. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) HAS BEEN AWARDED THE ARCHITECTURE AND ENGINEERING PHASE ONE DESIGN CONTRACT BY AXIOM SPACE FOR THE ASSEMBLY, INTEGRATION AND TESTING FACILITY PLANNED FOR THE 400-ACRE HOUSTON SPACEPORT IN THE HEART OF SPACE CITY AT ELLINGTON AIRPORT. THIS NEW 100,000 SQ FT FACILITY WILL SUPPORT AXIOM SPACE'S MISSION TO PROVIDE ACCESS TO LOW EARTH ORBIT AND ASSEMBLE THE FIRST COMMERCIAL INTERNATIONAL SPACE STATION.
