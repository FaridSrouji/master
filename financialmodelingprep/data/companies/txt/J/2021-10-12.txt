JACOBS AWARDED CONTRACT EXTENSION TO SUPPORT UK'S NUCLEAR POWER PLANTS. DALLAS, OCT. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS AWARDED AN EXTENSION TO ITS PROJECT MANAGEMENT RESOURCES (PMR) CONTRACT WITH EDF NUCLEAR GENERATION, OPERATOR OF THE U.K.'S NUCLEAR POWER PLANTS. THE JACOBS TEAM WILL CONTINUE TO SUPPORT THE STATIONS, WHICH ACCOUNT FOR ABOUT 17% OF THE COUNTRY'S ELECTRICITY OUTPUT.
