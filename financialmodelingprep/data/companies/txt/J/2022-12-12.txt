JACOBS SUPPORTS SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF NASA'S ARTEMIS I MOON MISSION. JACOBS' CRITICAL SOLUTIONS AND TECHNOLOGIES HELP BRING ORION HOME SAFELY DALLAS , DEC. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) CONGRATULATES NASA ON THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF THE 25.5-DAY ARTEMIS I MISSION TO ORBIT THE MOON, FOLLOWING THE SAFE SPLASHDOWN OF THE ORION SPACECRAFT IN THE PACIFIC OCEAN. POWERED BY THE SPACE LAUNCH SYSTEM (SLS) ROCKET, THE UNCREWED TEST FLIGHT SENT ORION ON A 1.4-MILLION-MILE JOURNEY AROUND THE MOON, TRAVELING FURTHER THAN ANY SPACE VEHICLE CAPABLE OF SAFELY TRANSPORTING HUMANS.
