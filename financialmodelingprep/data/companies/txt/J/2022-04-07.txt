JACOBS SUPPORTS UK'S DRIVE FOR CLEAN ENERGY WITH POWER PLANT LIFE EXTENSION PROGRAM. WORK SAFEGUARDS FUTURE OF EMISSION-FREE ELECTRICITY GENERATION DALLAS , APRIL 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS SELECTED ALONGSIDE GENERAL ELECTRIC AND WESTINGHOUSE ELECTRIC COMPANY LLC TO SUPPORT PRELIMINARY PHASE LIFE EXTENSION WORK AT THE SIZEWELL B NUCLEAR POWER STATION IN SUFFOLK, UNITED KINGDOM. EDF, OPERATOR OF SIZEWELL B, IS LEADING A LONG-TERM OPERATION (LTO) PROGRAM AIMED AT EXTENDING THE STATION'S OPERATING LIFESPAN BY 20 YEARS TO 2055.
