JACOBS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS , APRIL 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JACOBS (NYSE:J) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYABLE TO SHAREHOLDERS IN THE AMOUNT OF $0.26 PER SHARE OF JACOBS COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JUNE 23, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 26, 2023.
