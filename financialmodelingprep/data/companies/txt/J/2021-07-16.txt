JACOBS APPOINTED EAST WEST RAIL PROGRAM PARTNER. DALLAS, JULY 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) HAS BEEN APPOINTED AS THE EAST WEST RAIL PROGRAM PARTNER, PROVIDING A SIGNIFICANT INTEGRATION ROLE ACROSS A DIVERSE SUPPLY CHAIN DURING THE THREE-YEAR DEVELOPMENT PHASE OF THIS PROGRAM TO CONSTRUCT A NEW RAIL LINK BETWEEN OXFORD AND CAMBRIDGE.  AS PROGRAM PARTNER, JACOBS UNITES WORLD-CLASS TECHNICAL SKILLS AND BEST PRACTICE TO DRIVE OPPORTUNITIES FOR PROGRAM INNOVATION AND OUTCOME-FOCUSED BENEFITS FOR THE COMMUNITIES IN THE REGION.
