JACOBS WINS NEW CONTRACT TO SUPPORT UK FUSION PROGRAM. ENGINEERS AND SCIENTISTS WILL WORK ON THE WORLD'S FUTURE LOW CARBON ENERGY SOURCE DALLAS , DEC. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS AWARDED A CONTRACT TO SUPPORT THE U.K.'S FUSION ENERGY RESEARCH PROGRAM. THE UNITED KINGDOM ATOMIC ENERGY AUTHORITY (UKAEA) HAS APPOINTED THE COMPANY ON ALL LOTS OF ITS NEW PLANT MAINTENANCE AND OPERATIONAL SUPPORT SERVICES (PMOSS) FRAMEWORK.
