JACOBS' BOARD APPROVES $1 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION AND INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE BY 13%. DALLAS , JAN. 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE JACOBS' (NYSE:J) BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A NEW INCREMENTAL SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION (THE "2023 SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM"). THE 2023 SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM AUTHORIZES THE PURCHASE OF UP TO $1 BILLION OF JACOBS' COMMON STOCK.
