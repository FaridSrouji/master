WOOD GROUP RECEIVES CMA'S APPROVAL ON PROPOSED SALE OF ITS NUCLEAR BUSINESS TO JACOBS. WOOD GROUP (JOHN)PLC  - RE: SALE OF NUCLEAR BUSINESS.WOOD PLC - WOOD AND JACOBS CONTINUE TO WORK TOWARDS COMPLETION OF TRANSACTION WHICH IS EXPECTED LATER THIS MONTH..WOOD - RECEIVED FORMAL CONFIRMATION FROM CMA THAT IT HAS NO OBJECTIONS TO PROPOSED SALE OF WOOD'S NUCLEAR BUSINESS TO JACOBS.
