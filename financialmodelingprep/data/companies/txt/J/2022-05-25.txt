JACOBS BUILDS ROBOT TO SOLVE DEBRIS RIDDLE IN DAMAGED FUKUSHIMA REACTOR. HARNESSING INNOVATION TO SUPPORT COMPLEX NUCLEAR CLEANUP DALLAS , MAY 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) HAS DESIGNED AND BUILT A ROBOTIC TOOL TO OBTAIN CRUCIAL INFORMATION ABOUT THE STATE OF A DAMAGED NUCLEAR POWER REACTOR AT FUKUSHIMA DAIICHI IN JAPAN. THE REMOTELY OPERATED DEVICE WILL COLLECT PEBBLE-LIKE DEBRIS THAT WAS DEPOSITED AT THE BOTTOM OF THE REACTOR CONTAINMENT VESSEL AFTER THE MELTDOWN CAUSED BY THE EARTHQUAKE AND TSUNAMI IN MARCH 2011.
