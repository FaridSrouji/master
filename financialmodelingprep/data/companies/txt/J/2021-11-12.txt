JACOBS WINS TWO CZECH REPUBLIC NUCLEAR CONTRACTS. DALLAS, NOV. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) HAS WON TWO NEW CONTRACTS TO SUPPORT THE CZECH NUCLEAR SECTOR WITH RADIOACTIVE WASTE MANAGEMENT SERVICES. NATIONAL UTILITY ČEZ HAS SELECTED JACOBS' SIAL® GEOPOLYMER ENCAPSULATION TECHNOLOGY TO SAFELY SOLIDIFY 250 METRIC TONS OF LOW AND INTERMEDIATE-LEVEL RADIOACTIVE SLUDGE AT THE DUKOVANY NUCLEAR POWER PLANT.
