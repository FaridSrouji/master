JACOBS INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS, JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JACOBS (NYSE:J) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYABLE TO SHAREHOLDERS IN THE AMOUNT OF $0.23 PER SHARE OF JACOBS COMMON STOCK, AN INCREASE OF 10% FROM ITS PREVIOUS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.21. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON MARCH 25, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEB. 25, 2022.
