JACOBS APPOINTS PRESIDENT FOR PEOPLE AND PLACES SOLUTIONS LINE OF BUSINESS. DALLAS, AUG. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) TODAY NAMED PATRICK HILL AS EVP AND PRESIDENT OF PEOPLE & PLACES SOLUTIONS (P&PS) EFFECTIVE AUG. 1, 2021. HILL'S CAREER AT JACOBS SPANS MORE THAN 23 YEARS IN SEVERAL SENIOR LEADERSHIP POSITIONS, CROSSING MULTIPLE SECTORS AND OPERATIONS AROUND THE GLOBE.
