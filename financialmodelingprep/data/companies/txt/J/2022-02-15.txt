JACOBS TO DESIGN FIRST INDUCTIVE CHARGING PROJECT ON A US PUBLIC ROAD. DALLAS, FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  JACOBS (NYSE:J) IS PART OF A CONSORTIUM SELECTED BY THE MICHIGAN DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION (MDOT) TO DEVELOP AND IMPLEMENT AN INDUCTIVE VEHICLE CHARGING PILOT – THE FIRST OF ITS KIND IN THE UNITED STATES. THE PROJECT TEAM WILL BE LED BY ELECT R EON , A LEADING PROVIDER OF WIRELESS CHARGING SOLUTIONS FOR ELECTRIC VEHICLES (EVS).
