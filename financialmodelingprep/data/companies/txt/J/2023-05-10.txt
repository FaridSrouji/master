JACOBS AND CIRBA SOLUTIONS EXPAND SUSTAINABLE BATTERY PROCESSING IN US. EXPANSION WILL PROMOTE A CIRCULAR, MORE SUSTAINABLE ELECTRIC VEHICLE SUPPLY CHAIN DALLAS , MAY 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS SELECTED BY CIRBA SOLUTIONS, A COMPREHENSIVE BATTERY MATERIALS AND MANAGEMENT COMPANY FOR END-OF-LIFE BATTERIES AND GIGAFACTORY SCRAP, TO EXPAND MANUFACTURING CAPACITY FOR SUSTAINABLE ELECTRIC VEHICLE (EV) BATTERY MATERIALS ACROSS NORTH AMERICA. JACOBS IS MANAGING THE FACILITY DESIGN AND SUPPORTING CONSTRUCTION OF MULTIPLE SITE LOCATIONS, INCLUDING AN EXPANSION AT CIRBA SOLUTIONS' LITHIUM-ION PROCESSING SITE IN LANCASTER, OHIO.
