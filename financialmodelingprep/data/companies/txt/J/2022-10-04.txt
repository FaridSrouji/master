JACOBS WINS NATIONAL GRID DESIGN & PROJECT SERVICES FRAMEWORK. FRAMEWORK TO INCREASE THE RESILIENCY AND CAPACITY NEEDED IN THE ELECTRICITY TRANSMISSION NETWORK SUPPORTS UK EFFORTS TO MEET NET-ZERO AND DECARBONIZATION COMMITMENTS DALLAS , OCT. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS AWARDED THE DESIGN & PROJECT SERVICES FRAMEWORK WITH ENERGY UTILITY NATIONAL GRID IN THE U.K. TO SUPPORT THEIR FULL CONSTRUCTION OF THE ELECTRICITY AND GAS TRANSMISSION CYCLE. THROUGH THE NEW FOUR-YEAR FRAMEWORK, JACOBS WILL PROVIDE FEASIBILITY AND SURVEY WORKS, FRONT-END CONCEPTUAL AND DETAILED DESIGN, THROUGH TO ASSURANCE, VERIFICATION AND COMMISSIONING SERVICES.
