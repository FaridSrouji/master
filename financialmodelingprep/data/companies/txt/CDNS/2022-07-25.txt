CADENCE EXPANDS INTO MOLECULAR SIMULATION WITH ACQUISITION OF OPENEYE SCIENTIFIC, A PIONEERING LEADER IN COMPUTATIONAL MOLECULAR DESIGN. SAN JOSE, CALIF. & SANTA FE, N.M.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CADENCE ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE OPENEYE SCIENTIFIC, A PROVIDER OF COMPUTATIONAL MOLECULAR MODELING AND SIMULATION SOFTWARE.
