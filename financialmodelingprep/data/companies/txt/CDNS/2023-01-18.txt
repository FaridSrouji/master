GUC DELIVERS ITS FIRST TSMC N3 CHIP AND FIRST AI-OPTIMIZED N5 DESIGN USING CADENCE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CADENCE DESIGN SYSTEMS, INC. (NASDAQ: CDNS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT GLOBAL UNICHIP CORP. (GUC) SUCCESSFULLY DELIVERED AN ADVANCED HPC DESIGN AND A CPU DESIGN USING CADENCE® DIGITAL SOLUTIONS. THE HPC DESIGN WAS CREATED USING THE CADENCE INNOVUS™ IMPLEMENTATION SYSTEM ON TSMC'S ADVANCED N3 PROCESS AND FEATURED A 3.5 MILLION INSTANCE COUNT THAT REACHED CLOCK SPEEDS OF UP TO 3.16GHZ. THE CPU DESIGN WAS CREATED USING THE AI-ENABLED CADENCE CEREBRUS™ INTELLIGENT CHIP EXPLORER AND THE DIGITAL FULL FLOW ON THE TSMC N5 PROCESS TECHNOLOGY, DELIVERING 8% REDUCED POWER AND A 9% AREA IMPROVEMENT WHILE SIGNIFICANTLY IMPROVING ENGINEERING PRODUCTIVITY.
