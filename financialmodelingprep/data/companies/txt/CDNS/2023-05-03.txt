CADENCE LEVERAGES IBM CLOUD HPC TO ACCELERATE ELECTRONIC CHIP AND SYSTEM DESIGN SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT. AS PART OF CADENCE'S HYBRID, MULTI-CLOUD STRATEGY, IBM CLOUD HPC IS HELPING PERFORM LARGE-SCALE TASKS WITH SPEED ARMONK, N.Y. , MAY 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, IBM (IBM: NYSE) ANNOUNCED CADENCE DESIGN SYSTEMS, INC. (NASDAQ: CDNS), IS LEVERAGING HIGH-PERFORMANCE COMPUTING (HPC) WITH IBM CLOUD HPC TO HELP DEVELOP ITS CHIP AND SYSTEM DESIGN SOFTWARE FASTER.
