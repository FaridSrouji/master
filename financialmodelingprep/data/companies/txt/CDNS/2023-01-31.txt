UMC AND CADENCE COLLABORATE ON 3D-IC HYBRID BONDING REFERENCE FLOW. HSINCHU, TAIWAN & SAN JOSE, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--UNITED MICROELECTRONICS CORPORATION (NYSE: UMC; TWSE: 2303) (“UMC”), A LEADING GLOBAL SEMICONDUCTOR FOUNDRY, AND CADENCE DESIGN SYSTEMS, INC. (NASDAQ: CDNS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE CADENCE® 3D-IC REFERENCE FLOW, FEATURING THE INTEGRITY ™ 3D-IC PLATFORM, HAS BEEN CERTIFIED FOR UMC'S CHIP STACKING TECHNOLOGIES, ENABLING FASTER TIME TO MARKET.
