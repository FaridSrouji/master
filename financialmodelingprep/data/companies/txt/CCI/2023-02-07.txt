CROWN CASTLE DECLARES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. HOUSTON, FEB. 07, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CROWN CASTLE INC. (NYSE: CCI) ("CROWN CASTLE") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.565 PER COMMON SHARE. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON MARCH 31, 2023 TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 15, 2023. FUTURE DIVIDENDS ARE SUBJECT TO THE APPROVAL OF CROWN CASTLE'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
