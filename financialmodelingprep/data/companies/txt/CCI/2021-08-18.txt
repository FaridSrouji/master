CROWN CASTLE ANNOUNCES CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER TRANSITION FOR TOWERS BUSINESS. HOUSTON, AUG. 18, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL CORP. (NYSE: CCI) ("CROWN CASTLE") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT CATHERINE PICHE HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO SUCCEED ROBERT C. ACKERMAN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER–TOWERS, EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 1, 2021. MR. ACKERMAN PLANS TO RETIRE FOLLOWING A 50-YEAR CAREER THAT INCLUDED 23 YEARS IN VARIOUS SENIOR LEADERSHIP ROLES AT CROWN CASTLE, AND HE HAS AGREED TO REMAIN WITH CROWN CASTLE IN AN ADVISORY CAPACITY UNTIL FEBRUARY 2022 TO ASSIST WITH VARIOUS MATTERS, INCLUDING THE TRANSITION OF HIS SUCCESSOR.
