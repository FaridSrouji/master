HORMEL FOODS ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF FRED HALVIN, VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT. AUSTIN, MINN., FEB. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A FORTUNE 500 GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FRED HALVIN, VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT, WILL BE RETIRING AFTER A 37-YEAR CAREER WITH THE COMPANY.
