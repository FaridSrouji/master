HORMEL FOODS AND JENNIE-O WIN BEST IN DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION AWARD AT PRWEEK'S BRAND FILM AWARDS. AUSTIN, MINN., MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS COMPANY-PRODUCED FILM COOPERATION BEYOND WORDS, WAS AWARDED THE TOP HONOR IN THE DIVERSITY, EQUITY & INCLUSION CATEGORY FOR THE MOST EFFECTIVE FILM IN HIGHLIGHTING DE&I ISSUES AND/OR IMPACTING SOCIETY AT LARGE.
