HAGENS BERMAN: WAGE-FIXING LAWSUIT AFFECTING HUNDREDS OF THOUSANDS OF WORKERS AIMED AT MEAT PROCESSING INDUSTRY. DENVER--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AN ANTITRUST LAWSUIT SEEKING TO REPRESENT WORKERS IN THE RED MEAT PROCESSING INDUSTRY IS ACCUSING 11 OF THE NATION'S LARGEST MEAT PROCESSORS OF COLLUDING TO SUPPRESS THE WAGES AND BENEFITS OF HUNDREDS OF THOUSANDS OF THEIR EMPLOYEES SINCE 2014, ACCORDING TO LAW FIRM HAGENS BERMAN.
