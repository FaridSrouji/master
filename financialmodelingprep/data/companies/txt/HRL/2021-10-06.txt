HORMEL FOODS TO PARTNER WITH THE BETTER MEAT CO. FOR FOOD FORWARD ALTERNATIVE PROTEIN. AUSTIN, MINN., OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS VENTURING COMPANY, 199 VENTURES, HAS ENTERED INTO AN EXCLUSIVE PARTNERSHIP WITH THE BETTER MEAT CO. TO BRING NEW MYCOPROTEIN AND PLANT-BASED PROTEIN PRODUCTS TO THE MARKETPLACE.
