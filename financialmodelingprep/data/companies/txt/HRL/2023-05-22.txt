HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. AUSTIN, MINN. , MAY 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A FORTUNE 500 GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK, AUTHORIZED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT 27.5 CENTS ($0.275) A SHARE ON MAY 22, 2023, WILL BE PAID AUGUST 15, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 17, 2023.
