HORMEL FOODS ANNOUNCES EXECUTIVE APPOINTMENTS. AUSTIN, MINN., FEB. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENTS OF JEFF FRANK TO VICE PRESIDENT OF GROCERY PRODUCTS, LEADING THE STRATEGIC INTEGRATION OF THE PLANTERS AND CORN NUTS BRANDS INTO HORMEL FOODS....
