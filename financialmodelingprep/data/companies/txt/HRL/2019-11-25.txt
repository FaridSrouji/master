HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION PROVIDES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE FISCAL 2020. HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION PROVIDED EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE FISCAL 2020. FOR THE PERIOD, THE COMPANY EXPECTS NET SALES GUIDANCE IN THE RANGE OF $9.50 BILLION TO $10.30 BILLION, EARNINGS PER SHARE GUIDANCE IN THE RANGE OF $1.69 TO $1.83.
HORMEL FOODS ANNOUNCES 11% INCREASE TO ANNUAL DIVIDEND. HORMEL FOODS ANNOUNCES 54TH CONSECUTIVE INCREASE TO ANNUAL DIVIDEND.ANNOUNCES 11 PERCENT INCREASE TO ANNUAL DIVIDEND.SETS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.2325PER SHARE.
HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION ELECTS PRAMA BHATT TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ON NOVEMBER 25, 2019, HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF PRAMA BHATT TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 25, 2019. MS. BHATT IS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, DIGITAL AND E-COMMERCE FOR ULTA BEAUTY. MS. BHATT WILL JOIN THE AUDIT COMMITTEE OF THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
