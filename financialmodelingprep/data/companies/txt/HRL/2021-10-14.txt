JENNIE-O THANKSGIVING HOTLINE OPENS NOV. 1. WILLMAR, MINN., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JENNIE-O TURKEY STORE, ONE OF THE MOST BELOVED TURKEY BRANDS IN THE WORLD, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RETURN OF ITS 1-800-TURKEYS HOTLINE, AS WELL AS ADDITIONAL HELPFUL TOOLS AND RESOURCES FOR CONSUMERS TO HELP WITH PLANNING THEIR TURKEY DAY MEALS.
