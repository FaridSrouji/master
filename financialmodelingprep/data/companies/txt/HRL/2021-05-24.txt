HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. AUSTIN, MINN., MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK, AUTHORIZED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT 24.50 CENTS ($0.2450) A SHARE ON MAY 24, 2021, WILL BE PAID AUGUST 16, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 12, 2021.
