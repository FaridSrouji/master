HORMEL FOODS PROVIDES FOOD FOR HURRICANE IAN RELIEF EFFORTS. AUSTIN, MINN. , SEPT. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION, (NYSE: HRL), A FORTUNE 500 GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS PROVIDED SUPPORT TO CONVOY OF HOPE'S DISASTER RECOVERY RESPONSE IN THE WAKE OF HURRICANE IAN.
