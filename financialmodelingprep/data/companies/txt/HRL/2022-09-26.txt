HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. AUSTIN, MINN. , SEPT. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A FORTUNE 500 GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK, AUTHORIZED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT 26 CENTS ($0.26) A SHARE ON SEPTEMBER 26, 2022, WILL BE PAID NOVEMBER 15, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON OCTOBER 17, 2022.
