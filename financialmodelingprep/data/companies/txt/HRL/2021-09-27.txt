HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. AUSTIN, MINN., SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK, AUTHORIZED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT 24.50 CENTS ($0.2450) A SHARE ON SEPTEMBER 27, 2021, WILL BE PAID NOVEMBER 15, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON OCTOBER 18, 2021.
