HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. AUSTIN, MINN. , MARCH 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK, AUTHORIZED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT 26 CENTS ($0.26) A SHARE ON MARCH 28, 2022, WILL BE PAID MAY 16, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 11, 2022.
