HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION ANNOUNCES 56TH CONSECUTIVE INCREASE TO ANNUAL DIVIDEND. AUSTIN, MINN., NOV. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION (NYSE: HRL), A GLOBAL BRANDED FOOD COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED A 6 PERCENT INCREASE TO THE ANNUAL DIVIDEND TO SHAREHOLDERS, MARKING THE 56TH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASE.
