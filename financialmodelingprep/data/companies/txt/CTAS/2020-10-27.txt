CINTAS CORPORATION ANNOUNCES ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASED FOR THE 37TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CINTAS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CTAS) ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A $2.81 PER SHARE ANNUAL DIVIDEND AT ITS MEETING TODAY, REPRESENTING AN INCREASE OF 10.2% OVER LAST YEAR'S DIVIDEND. THIS IS THE 37TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR THAT THE ANNUAL DIVIDEND HAS INCREASED, WHICH IS EVERY YEAR SINCE CINTAS' INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING IN 1983.
