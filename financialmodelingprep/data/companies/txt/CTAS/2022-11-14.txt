CINTAS UNVEILS NEW ESG BRANDING TO ALIGN ITS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL & GOVERNANCE AMBITIONS. CINCINNATI--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TODAY CINTAS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CTAS) UNVEILED ITS NEW ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) BRANDING IDENTITY, WHICH WAS CREATED TO HELP RAISE AWARENESS AND BRING CONSISTENCY AND COHESION TO CINTAS' ONGOING ESG EFFORTS AND INITIATIVES.
