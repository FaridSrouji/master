CINTAS RANKS NO. 5 ON SELLING POWER'S “50 BEST COMPANIES TO SELL FOR” LIST IN 2022. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $CTAS--CINTAS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CTAS) IS RANKED FIFTH ON THE 2022 SELLING POWER 50 BEST COMPANIES TO SELL FOR LIST, WHICH WAS RELEASED TODAY. IN 2022, CINTAS MOVED UP ONE PLACE FROM ITS 2021 RANKING OF NO. 6. CINTAS HAS CONTINUED ITS STEADY CLIMB ON SELLING POWER'S LIST, RISING FROM NO. 27 IN 2013.
