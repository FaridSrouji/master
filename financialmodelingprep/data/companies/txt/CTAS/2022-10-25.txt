CINTAS CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CINTAS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CTAS) ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.15 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 15, 2022.
