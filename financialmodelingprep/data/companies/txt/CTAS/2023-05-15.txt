DESIGN COLLECTIVE BY CINTAS REVEALS NEW APPAREL FOR THE ONBOARD LADIES AND GENTLEMEN OF THE RITZ-CARLTON YACHT COLLECTION. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DESIGN COLLECTIVE BY CINTAS, AN AWARD-WINNING APPAREL DESIGN AGENCY RECOGNIZED FOR CREATING INSPIRING AND INNOVATIVE EMPLOYEE APPAREL COLLECTIONS, REVEALED THE NEWLY DESIGNED UNIFORM PROGRAM FOR THE STAFF ON BOARD EVRIMA, THE FIRST YACHT FROM THE RITZ-CARLTON YACHT COLLECTION. ENVISIONED TO REFLECT THE LIFESTYLE OF THE RITZ-CARLTON RESORTS AND THE CASUAL FREEDOM OF A YACHTING VACATION, THE APPAREL COLLECTION MADE ITS DEBUT ON THE MAIDEN VOYAGE OF EVRIMA THIS PAST FALL.
