NATIONAL MENTOR MONTH: MENTORING IS CRITICAL TO PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT AT CINTAS. CINCINNATI--( BUSINESS WIRE )--JANUARY IS NATIONAL MENTORING MONTH. ORIGINALLY CREATED BY THE YOUTH ORGANIZATION MENTOR, NATIONAL MENTORING MONTH WAS ESTABLISHED TO HELP BRING AWARENESS TO THE POWER AND IMPACT OF MENTORING, AND INSPIRE PEOPLE TO GET INVOLVED BY PROVIDING ENRICHMENT AND SUPPORT TO OTHERS.
