SCOTT D. FARMER TO RETIRE AS CINTAS CORPORATION CEO ON MAY 31; TODD M. SCHNEIDER ELECTED NEW CEO & PRESIDENT, EFFECTIVE JUNE 1. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CINTAS CORPORATION (NASDAQ:CTAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED SCOTT D. FARMER, CINTAS CHAIRMAN & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, WILL RETIRE AS CEO ON MAY 31, 2021. MR. FARMER WILL REMAIN EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN, A ROLE HE HAS HELD SINCE 2016. THE COMPANY ALSO ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS INCREASED THE NUMBER OF DIRECTORS FROM 8 TO 9 AND ELECTED TODD M.
