CINTAS CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CINTAS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CTAS) ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.95 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 15, 2021. CINTAS HAS A STRONG RECORD OF RETURNING CAPITAL TO ITS SHAREHOLDERS AND HAS CONSISTENTLY RAISED ITS DIVIDEND EACH YEAR SINCE CINTAS' INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING 38 YEARS AGO IN 1983.
