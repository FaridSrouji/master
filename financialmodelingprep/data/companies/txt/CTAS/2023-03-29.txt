CINTAS CORPORATION ANNOUNCES FISCAL 2023 THIRD QUARTER RESULTS. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CINTAS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CTAS) TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR ITS FISCAL 2023 THIRD QUARTER ENDED FEBRUARY 28, 2023. REVENUE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL 2023 WAS $2.19 BILLION COMPARED TO $1.96 BILLION IN LAST YEAR'S THIRD QUARTER, AN INCREASE OF 11.7%. THE ORGANIC REVENUE GROWTH RATE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL 2023, WHICH ADJUSTS FOR THE IMPACTS OF ACQUISITIONS AND FOREIGN CURRENCY EXCHANGE RATE FLUCTUATIONS, WAS 11.8%.
