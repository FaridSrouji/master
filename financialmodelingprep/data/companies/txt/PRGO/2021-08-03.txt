PERRIGO ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DUBLIN, AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PERRIGO COMPANY PLC (NYSE; TASE: PRGO), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF QUALITY, AFFORDABLE SELF-CARE PRODUCTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 21, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON SEPTEMBER 3, 2021. ABOUT PERRIGO   PERRIGO COMPANY PLC (NYSE; TASE: PRGO) IS A LEADING PROVIDER OF QUALITY, AFFORDABLE SELF-CARE PRODUCTS AND OVER-THE-COUNTER (OTC) HEALTH AND WELLNESS SOLUTIONS THAT ENHANCE INDIVIDUAL WELL-BEING BY EMPOWERING CONSUMERS TO PROACTIVELY PREVENT OR TREAT CONDITIONS THAT CAN BE SELF-MANAGED.
