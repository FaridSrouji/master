IRISH HIGH COURT RULES PERRIGO'S LEGITIMATE EXPECTATIONS NOT VIOLATED; COMPANY REMAINS COMMITTED TO VIGOROUSLY CHALLENGING THE NOTICE OF AMENDED ASSESMENT. DUBLIN, NOV. 4, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PERRIGO COMPANY PLC (NYSE; TASE: PRGO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE IRISH HIGH COURT, WHICH IS A COURT OF FIRST INSTANCE, RULED THAT THE ISSUANCE OF THE NOTICE OF AMENDED ASSESSMENT BY IRISH REVENUE IN NOVEMBER 2018 DID NOT VIOLATE THE COMPANY'S LEGITIMATE...
