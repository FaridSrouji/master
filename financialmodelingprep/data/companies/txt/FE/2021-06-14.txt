OHIO EDISON TO HELP KEEP LAKESHORE ROADS SAFE DURING MAYFLY SEASON. AKRON, OHIO, JUNE 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS COMMUNITIES ALONG LAKE ERIE PREPARE FOR THE MASS ARRIVAL OF MAYFLIES THIS MONTH, OHIO EDISON, A FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) ELECTRIC COMPANY, IS PROACTIVELY WORKING WITH LOCAL MUNICIPALITIES TO KEEP ROADS ALONG THE LAKESHORE SAFE BY TEMPORARILY TURNING OFF STREETLIGHTS TO DETER SWARMS OF FLIES. MAYFLIES ARE ATTRACTED TO STREETLIGHTS AND CAN CREATE HAZARDOUS DRIVING CONDITIONS WHEN LARGE NUMBERS ACCUMULATE ALONG ROADS AND BRIDGES NEAR THE LIGHTS.
