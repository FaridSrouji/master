FIRSTENERGY PENNSYLVANIA UTILITIES LAUNCH REQUEST FOR LONG-TERM SOLAR PPA. READING, PA. , OCT. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) IS ISSUING A REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP) TO ENTER INTO LONG-TERM SOLAR POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENTS (PPAS) ON BEHALF OF FOUR FIRSTENERGY PENNSYLVANIA UTILITIES – PENNSYLVANIA POWER COMPANY (PENN POWER), PENNSYLVANIA ELECTRIC COMPANY (PENELEC), METROPOLITAN EDISON COMPANY (MET-ED) AND WEST PENN POWER COMPANY (WEST PENN).
