PENELEC'S UNDERGROUND CABLE REPLACEMENT PROJECT TO HELP ENHANCE ELECTRIC SERVICE TO TREASURE LAKE COMMUNITY. ERIE, PA. , FEB. 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PENELEC, A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS BEGUN A SIGNIFICANT PROJECT TO INSTALL NEW UNDERGROUND ELECTRIC CABLE TO ENHANCE SERVICE RELIABILITY FOR MORE THAN 5,000 RESIDENTS AND SEVERAL BUSINESSES IN TREASURE LAKE, A RESIDENTIAL COMMUNITY JUST NORTH OF DUBOIS, PA., IN CLEARFIELD COUNTY.
