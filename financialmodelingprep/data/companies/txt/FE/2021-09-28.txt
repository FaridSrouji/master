JCP&L PROPOSAL SUPPORTS NEW JERSEY CLEAN ENERGY PLAN BY DELIVERING WIND POWER TO CUSTOMERS WITH MINIMAL COMMUNITY IMPACTS. HOLMDEL, N.J., SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JERSEY CENTRAL POWER AND LIGHT (JCP&L), A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS SUBMITTED A PROPOSAL TO CONNECT CLEAN ENERGY GENERATED BY NEW JERSEY'S OFFSHORE WIND FARMS TO THE POWER GRID.
