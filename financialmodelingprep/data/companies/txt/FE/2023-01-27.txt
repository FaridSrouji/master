FIRSTENERGY FOUNDATION PRESENTS "GIFTS OF THE SEASON" TOTALING $40,000 TO CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS IN MET-ED'S SERVICE AREA. GIFTS ARE PART OF THE FIRSTENERGY FOUNDATION'S ANNUAL HOLIDAY PHILANTHROPIC CAMPAIGN READING, PA. , JAN. 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE FIRSTENERGY FOUNDATION HAS GRANTED SURPRISE "GIFTS OF THE SEASON" TOTALING $40,000 TO FOUR LOCAL NONPROFITS THAT ARE WORKING TO MAKE LIVES BETTER IN COMMUNITIES WITHIN MET-ED'S SERVICE AREA.
