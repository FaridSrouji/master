PENELEC UPGRADING UNDERGROUND ELECTRICAL NETWORK IN CAMBRIA COUNTY. PROJECT TO BENEFIT NEARLY 1,000 CUSTOMERS IN DOWNTOWN JOHNSTOWN AREA ERIE, PA. , JUNE 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PENELEC, A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS BEGUN A SIGNIFICANT PROJECT TO HELP PREVENT OR MINIMIZE SERVICE DISRUPTIONS IN DOWNTOWN JOHNSTOWN, CAMBRIA COUNTY, BY REINFORCING ITS UNDERGROUND ELECTRICAL NETWORK.
