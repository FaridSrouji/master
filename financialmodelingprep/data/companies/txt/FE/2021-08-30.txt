FIRSTENERGY UTILITY CREWS MOBILIZE TO ASSIST HURRICANE IDA POWER RESTORATION EFFORTS IN LOUISIANA. AKRON, OHIO , AUG. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF ITS LONG-STANDING TRADITION OF ASSISTING OTHER ELECTRIC COMPANIES DURING LARGE-SCALE POWER OUTAGES, FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) UTILITIES HAVE SENT APPROXIMATELY 500 LINE WORKERS, FORESTRY CREWS, SUPPORT PERSONNEL AND INTERNAL CONTRACTORS TO LOUISIANA TO HELP ENTERGY WITH POWER RESTORATION EFFORTS FOLLOWING HURRICANE IDA. CREWS BEGAN LEAVING SUNDAY MORNING AND EXPECT TO ARRIVE IN LOUISIANA THROUGHOUT THE DAY TOMORROW.
