FIRSTENERGY JOINS ELECTRIC HIGHWAY COALITION. AKRON, OHIO, JULY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) HAS JOINED THE ELECTRIC HIGHWAY COALITION, A GROUP OF ELECTRIC COMPANIES COMMITTED TO ENABLING LONG-DISTANCE ELECTRIC VEHICLE (EV) TRAVEL THROUGH A NETWORK OF EV FAST-CHARGING STATIONS CONNECTING MAJOR HIGHWAY SYSTEMS.   FORMED IN MARCH 2021, THE ELECTRIC HIGHWAY COALITION (EHC) INCLUDES MANY OF THE NATION'S LEADING UTILITIES IN 29 STATES AND THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA SERVING MORE THAN 60 MILLION CUSTOMERS.
