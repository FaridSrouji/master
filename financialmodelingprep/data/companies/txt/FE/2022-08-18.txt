FIRSTENERGY FOUNDATION COMPLETES FINAL ROUND OF "INVESTING WITH PURPOSE" GRANTS TO COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS. COMBINED WITH DONATIONS FROM FIRSTENERGY CORP., INITIATIVE HAS COLLECTIVELY GIFTED $7 MILLION TO ORGANIZATIONS SINCE 2020 AKRON, OHIO , AUG. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE FIRSTENERGY FOUNDATION AWARDED MORE THAN $3.43 MILLION TO NEARLY 100 ORGANIZATIONS ACROSS ITS SIX-STATE SERVICE TERRITORY FOR THE FINAL ROUND OF "INVESTING WITH PURPOSE," AN INITIATIVE FOCUSED ON SUPPORTING NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS THAT ADVANCE HEALTH AND SAFETY, WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT, EDUCATIONAL AND SOCIAL JUSTICE INITIATIVES. IN ADDITION, FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) PROVIDED $220,000 TO FIVE ORGANIZATIONS AS PART OF THE INITIATIVE.
