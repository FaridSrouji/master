MARYLAND PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION APPROVES POTOMAC EDISON PROPOSAL AIMED AT SAVING CUSTOMERS UP TO $80 MILLION. WARRIOR RUN POWER GENERATION CONTRACT TO END SEVEN YEARS EARLY WILLIAMSPORT, MD. , JUNE 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE MARYLAND PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION (PSC) HAS APPROVED A PROPOSAL FROM POTOMAC EDISON, A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY (NYSE: FE), TO BUY OUT ITS CONTRACT WITH AES'S WARRIOR RUN GENERATING STATION APPROXIMATELY SEVEN YEARS EARLY, POTENTIALLY SAVING CUSTOMERS NEARLY $80 MILLION.
