FIRSTENERGY ANNOUNCES FIRST QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. AKRON, OHIO, APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2021 GAAP EARNINGS OF $335 MILLION, OR $0.62 PER BASIC AND DILUTED SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, ON REVENUE OF $2.7 BILLION . IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020, THE COMPANY REPORTED GAAP EARNINGS OF $74 MILLION, OR $0.14 CENTS PER BASIC AND DILUTED SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, ON REVENUE OF $2.7 BILLION.
