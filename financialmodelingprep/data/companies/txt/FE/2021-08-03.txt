JCP&L LINE REPLACEMENT ENHANCES SERVICE RELIABILITY FOR BARRIER ISLAND CUSTOMERS. HOLMDEL, N.J., AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JERSEY CENTRAL POWER AND LIGHT (JCP&L), A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS COMPLETED THE REPLACEMENT OF AN UNDERWATER HIGH-VOLTAGE TRANSMISSION LINE, STRETCHING MORE THAN A MILE ACROSS BARNEGAT BAY ALONG THE TUNNEY-MATHIS BRIDGES, TO ENHANCE ELECTRIC SERVICE RELIABILITY TO NEW JERSEY'S BARRIER ISLANDS.
