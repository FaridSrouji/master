FIRSTENERGY CORP. DONATION BRINGS NEW POP-UP LIBRARY TO GREATER AKRON. ELECTRIC VEHICLE WILL SERVE AS A MOBILE AKRON-SUMMIT COUNTY PUBLIC LIBRARY STARTING IN SPRING AKRON, OHIO , MARCH 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) HAS DONATED NEARLY $46,000 TO THE AKRON-SUMMIT COUNTY PUBLIC LIBRARY (ASCPL) TO FUND A CUSTOMIZED ELECTRIC VEHICLE THAT WILL FUNCTION AS A POP-UP LIBRARY IN THE GREATER AKRON AREA BEGINNING THIS SPRING. THE THREE-WHEELED VEHICLE, CALLED AN ETUK, WILL SERVE AS A MOBILE LIBRARY FOR RESIDENTS, ESPECIALLY CHILDREN, WHO MAY NOT HAVE EASY ACCESS TO THE LIBRARY'S MAIN BRANCHES.
