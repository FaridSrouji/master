MON POWER USING AERIAL SAWS TO TRIM TREES IN REMOTE AREAS. FAIRMONT, W.VA., JULY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MON POWER, A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), IS USING TWO HELICOPTERS EQUIPPED WITH AERIAL SAWS TO TRIM TREES AND MAINTAIN ELECTRICAL CLEARANCES ALONG EIGHT DIFFICULT-TO-ACCESS TRANSMISSION LINE CORRIDORS IN WEST VIRGINIA.
