JCP&L WELCOMES NEW LINE AND SUBSTATION EMPLOYEES FROM POWER SYSTEMS INSTITUTE TRAINING PROGRAMS. MORRISTOWN, N.J. , JULY 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JERSEY CENTRAL POWER & LIGHT (JCP&L), FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE:FE) UTILITIES, HAVE HIRED 20 GRADUATES OF POWER SYSTEMS INSTITUTE (PSI), AWARD-WINNING, TWO-YEAR EDUCATIONAL PROGRAM THAT HELPS PREPARE THE NEXT GENERATION OF LINE AND SUBSTATION WORKERS FOR FIRSTENERGY'S 10 ELECTRIC UTILITY COMPANIES.
