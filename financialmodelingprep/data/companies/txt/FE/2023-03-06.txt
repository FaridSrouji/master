FIRSTENERGY DEMOLISHES THREE EMISSIONS STACKS AT HATFIELD'S FERRY POWER STATION. AKRON, OHIO , MARCH 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) HAS DEMOLISHED THREE EMISSIONS STACKS AT THE FORMER HATFIELD'S FERRY POWER STATION NEAR MASONTOWN, PENNSYLVANIA, A KEY MILESTONE IN PREPARING THE 236-ACRE SITE OF THE RETIRED COAL-FIRED PLANT FOR FUTURE DEVELOPMENT.   ON MARCH 4, A SPECIALIZED DEMOLITION CONTRACTOR USED STRATEGICALLY PLACED CHARGES AROUND THE BASES OF THE THREE STACKS, SAFELY BRINGING THEM DOWN IN A CONTROLLED IMPLOSION.
