FIRSTENERGY UTILITIES INVITE CUSTOMERS TO ENTER HOLIDAY LIGHTS SWEEPSTAKES. AKRON, OHIO, NOV. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP.'S (NYSE:FE) ANNUAL "MERRY & BRIGHT" HOLIDAY LIGHTS SWEEPSTAKES IS BACK, AND UTILITY CUSTOMERS ARE ONCE AGAIN INVITED TO SHOW OFF THEIR BEST AND BRIGHTEST OUTDOOR HOLIDAY LIGHTING DISPLAYS FOR THE CHANCE TO WIN A WEEKLY PRIZE. CUSTOMERS CAN SUBMIT A PHOTO OR VIDEO OF THEIR OUTDOOR LIGHTING DISPLAYS ON THEIR RESPECTIVE ELECTRIC COMPANY'S FACEBOOK® PAGE UNTIL FRIDAY, DEC. 17.
