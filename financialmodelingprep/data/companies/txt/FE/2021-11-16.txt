FIRSTENERGY PREPARES CUSTOMERS FOR WINTER COLD WITH ENERGY EFFICIENCY TIPS. AKRON, OHIO, NOV. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS TEMPERATURES FALL, FIRSTENERGY CORP.
