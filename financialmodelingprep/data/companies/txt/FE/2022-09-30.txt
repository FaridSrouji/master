FIRSTENERGY TO GIVE AWAY 1,500 TREES AT MOUNTAIN STATE FOREST FESTIVAL. EVENT IS PART OF COMPANY GOAL TO PLANT 20,000 TREES IN 2022 FAIRMONT, W.VA. , SEPT. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF ITS COMMITMENT TO BUILDING A BRIGHTER AND MORE SUSTAINABLE FUTURE FOR THE COMMUNITIES IT SERVES, FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) WILL BE GIVING AWAY 1,500 TREES AT THE 2022 MOUNTAIN STATE FOREST FESTIVAL IN ELKINS, WEST VIRGINIA.
