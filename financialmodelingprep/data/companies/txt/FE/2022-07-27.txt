MET-ED COMPLETES UPGRADE TO ELECTRIC SYSTEM IN MONROE COUNTY. PROJECT DESIGNED TO ENHANCE SERVICE RELIABILITY FOR NEARLY 2,300 CUSTOMERS READING, PA. , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MET-ED, A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), RECENTLY COMPLETED A PROJECT TO UPGRADE ITS DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM IN SOUTHERN MONROE COUNTY TO HELP PREVENT OR MINIMIZE THE LENGTH OF SERVICE DISRUPTIONS, PARTICULARLY DURING SEVERE SUMMER STORMS.
