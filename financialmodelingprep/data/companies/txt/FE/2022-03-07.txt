POTOMAC EDISON INSTALLS FOUR NEW EV FAST-CHARGING STATIONS IN MARYLAND. WILLIAMSPORT, MD., MARCH 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- POTOMAC EDISON, A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS COMPLETED THE INSTALLATION OF FOUR NEW ELECTRIC VEHICLE (EV) FAST-CHARGING STATIONS IN FROSTBURG AND FRIENDSVILLE, EXPANDING ITS NETWORK OF PUBLICLY AVAILABLE STATIONS IN MARYLAND.
