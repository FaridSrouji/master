POTOMAC EDISON ENCOURAGES MARYLAND CUSTOMERS TO REVIEW OPTIONS TO HELP MANAGE SUMMER ELECTRIC BILLS. " STANDARD OFFER SERVICE" RATE RISING JUNE 1 FOLLOWING HIGHER COMPETITIVE AUCTION PRICES WILLIAMSPORT, MD. , MAY 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) IS ENCOURAGING MARYLAND CUSTOMERS OF ITS SUBSIDIARY POTOMAC EDISON TO EVALUATE ELECTRIC SUPPLY OFFERS FROM COMPETITIVE SUPPLIERS THAT FIT THEIR BUDGET AND PROTECT AGAINST RISING ENERGY PRICES THIS SUMMER.
