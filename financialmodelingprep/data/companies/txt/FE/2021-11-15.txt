FIRSTENERGY ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS TO SHOP FOR BEST ENERGY DEALS AS FUEL COSTS RISE. AKRON, OHIO, NOV. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH HIGHER FUEL COSTS EXPECTED TO INFLUENCE ENERGY RATES THIS WINTER, FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) IS ENCOURAGING CUSTOMERS OF ITS UTILITY COMPANIES IN OHIO, PENNSYLVANIA, NEW JERSEY AND MARYLAND TO EVALUATE ELECTRIC SUPPLY DEALS THAT FIT THEIR BUDGET AND PROTECT AGAINST RISING ENERGY PRICES. THE PRICE OF NATURAL GAS – WHICH MAKES UP THE LARGEST SHARE OF U.S. ELECTRICITY GENERATION – HAS BEEN RISING IN RECENT MONTHS DUE TO LOW INVENTORY LEVELS AND CONTINUING DEMAND.
