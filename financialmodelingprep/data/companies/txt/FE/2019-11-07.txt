FIRSTENERGY CORP. INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON MARCH 1, 2020. FIRSTENERGY CORP.'S BOARD DECLARED AN INCREASED QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.39 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 1, 2020, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEBRUARY 7, 2020. THIS REPRESENTS A 3% INCREASE COMPARED TO QUARTERLY PAYMENTS OF $0.38 PER COMMON SHARE PAID BY THE COMPANY SINCE MARCH 2019.
