FIRSTENERGY TAKING PROACTIVE STEPS TO PREPARE TRANSMISSION SYSTEM FOR SUMMER WEATHER. AKRON, OHIO, JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) HAS COMPLETED ANNUAL INSPECTIONS AND MAINTENANCE THROUGHOUT ITS TRANSMISSION SYSTEM TO HELP ENSURE RELIABILITY FOR CUSTOMERS AHEAD OF THE SUMMER SEASON. THE TRANSMISSION SYSTEM, WHICH CHANNELS HIGH-VOLTAGE POWER FROM GENERATION SOURCES TO LOCAL LINES SERVING TOWNS AND COMMUNITIES, SERVES AS THE INTERSTATE HIGHWAY SYSTEM OF THE ELECTRIC GRID.
