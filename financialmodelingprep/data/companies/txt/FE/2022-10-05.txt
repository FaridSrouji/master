POTOMAC EDISON HOSTS TREE PLANTING EVENTS IN OCTOBER. FIRSTENERGY DONATING 650 TREES AS PART OF LARGER ENVIRONMENTAL INITIATIVE WILLIAMSPORT, MD. , OCT. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EMPLOYEE VOLUNTEERS FROM POTOMAC EDISON, A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), WILL BE PLANTING 650 TREES AT PLANTING EVENTS THROUGHOUT THE COMPANY'S MARYLAND SERVICE AREA DURING THE MONTH OF OCTOBER.
