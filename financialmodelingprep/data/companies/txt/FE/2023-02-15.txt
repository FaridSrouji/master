JCP&L CONNECTS SOLAR PROJECT IN MOUNT OLIVE TO ELECTRIC GRID. LARGEST LANDFILL SOLAR PROJECT IN NORTH AMERICA NOW DELIVERING CLEAN ENERGY MORRISTOWN, N.J. , FEB. 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JERSEY CENTRAL POWER & LIGHT (JCP&L), A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS COMPLETED A GRID CONNECTION FOR A 19.8-MEGAWATT SOLAR PROJECT LOCATED AT A FORMER LANDFILL PROPERTY IN MOUNT OLIVE, NEW JERSEY, THAT IS NOW DELIVERING CLEAN ENERGY THROUGH FIRSTENERGY'S TRANSMISSION LINES.
