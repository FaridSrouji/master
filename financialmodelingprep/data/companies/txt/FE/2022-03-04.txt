JCP&L HONORED FOR WORK ON ENERGY CONSTRUCTION PROJECT BY NEW JERSEY ALLIANCE FOR ACTION. MORRISTOWN, N.J., MARCH 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JERSEY CENTRAL POWER AND LIGHT (JCP&L), A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS BEEN HONORED FOR THEIR WORK SUPPORTING CONSTRUCTION OF ATLANTIC HEALTH SYSTEM'S COMBINED HEAT AND POWER PLANT AT MORRISTOWN MEDICAL CENTER, WHICH RECEIVED THE 2022 NEW JERSEY ALLIANCE FOR ACTION LEADING CAPITAL CONSTRUCTION PROJECTS AWARD.
