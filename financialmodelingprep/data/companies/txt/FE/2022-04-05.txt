FIRSTENERGY COMPLETES PROACTIVE AERIAL INSPECTIONS OF HIGH-VOLTAGE POWER LINES IN EASTERN PENNSYLVANIA. READING, PA. , APRIL 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MID-ATLANTIC INTERSTATE TRANSMISSION (MAIT), A TRANSMISSION SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS COMPLETED PROACTIVE VISUAL INSPECTIONS OF NEARLY 1,400 MILES OF ITS HIGH-VOLTAGE TRANSMISSION LINES IN AREAS SERVED BY FIRSTENERGY'S MET-ED ELECTRIC UTILITY.
