MON POWER AND POTOMAC EDISON MOVE CLOSER TO CONSTRUCTION OF SOLAR POWER PLANTS WITH CLEAN ENERGY COMMITMENT FROM HARPERS FERRY. FAIRMONT, W.VA. , JAN. 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MON POWER AND POTOMAC EDISON, SUBSIDIARIES OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAVE SIGNED AN AGREEMENT WITH THE TOWN OF HARPERS FERRY TO ADVANCE THE FUTURE OF CLEAN ENERGY.
