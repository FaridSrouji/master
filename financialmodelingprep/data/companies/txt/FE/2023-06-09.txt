FIRSTENERGY NAMES ABIGAIL PHILLIPS VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF RISK OFFICER. EXTENSIVE RISK MANAGEMENT EXPERTISE IN ELECTRIC INDUSTRY WILL HELP COMPANY ELEVATE ITS RISK PROGRAM TO BEST-IN-CLASS AKRON, OHIO , JUNE 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ABIGAIL PHILLIPS HAS BEEN NAMED VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF RISK OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JULY 10. IN THIS ROLE, SHE WILL EXECUTE A RISK PROGRAM THAT REFLECTS FIRSTENERGY'S CORE VALUES AND BEHAVIORS; IMPLEMENT ROBUST PROCESSES, PROCEDURES AND STANDARDS TO IDENTIFY, MITIGATE AND PREVENT RISKS ACROSS THE COMPANY; AND SUPPORT EFFORTS BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND SENIOR LEADERSHIP TO IMPLEMENT, REVIEW AND APPROVE THE COMPANY'S ENTERPRISE-WIDE RISK GOVERNANCE AND COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK.
