FIRSTENERGY EARNS 2022 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX DESIGNATION. AKRON, OHIO, JAN. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI), RECOGNIZING ITS COMMITMENT TO GENDER EQUALITY IN THE WORKPLACE AND THE COMMUNITIES IN WHICH IT OPERATES. THE GEI USES A STANDARDIZED REPORTING FRAMEWORK TO MEASURE GENDER EQUALITY ACROSS FIVE PILLARS: FEMALE LEADERSHIP AND TALENT PIPELINE, EQUAL PAY AND GENDER PAY PARITY, INCLUSIVE CULTURE, SEXUAL HARASSMENT POLICIES AND FACTORS LIKE SUPPLIER DIVERSITY AND COMMUNITY SUPPORT.
