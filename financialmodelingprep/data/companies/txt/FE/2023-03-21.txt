FIRSTENERGY NAMED TOP 50 DIVERSITY EMPLOYER BY MINORITY ENGINEER™ MAGAZINE. RANKING REFLECTS COMPANY'S REPUTATION AMONG MAGAZINE'S READERS AKRON, OHIO , MARCH 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR THE FIRST TIME IN COMPANY HISTORY, FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) HAS BEEN DESIGNATED AS A TOP 50 DIVERSITY EMPLOYER BY MINORITY ENGINEER™ MAGAZINE. THE RANKINGS ARE DETERMINED BASED ON A SURVEY OF RANDOMLY SELECTED MINORITY ENGINEER READERS WHO WERE ASKED TO NAME THE EMPLOYERS WHERE THEY WOULD MOST LIKE TO WORK OR THAT THEY BELIEVE PROVIDE A POSITIVE WORKING ENVIRONMENT FOR MEMBERS OF MINORITY GROUPS.
