FIRSTENERGY ELECTRIC COMPANIES PREPARING FOR SEVERE WEEKEND WEATHER. COMPANY PERSONNEL READY TO RESPOND TO OUTAGES CAUSED BY HIGH WINDS AND HEAVY RAIN AKRON, OHIO , MARCH 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) ELECTRIC COMPANY PERSONNEL ARE PREPARED TO RESPOND TO SERVICE INTERRUPTIONS CAUSED BY A STORM SYSTEM THAT IS EXPECTED TO BRING HIGH WINDS AND HEAVY RAIN THIS WEEKEND TO OHIO, PENNSYLVANIA AND WEST VIRGINIA. COMPANY METEOROLOGISTS HAVE BEEN TRACKING THE STORM FOR SEVERAL DAYS.
