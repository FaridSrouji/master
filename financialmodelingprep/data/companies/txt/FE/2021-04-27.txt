JCP&L HONORED WITH ENVIRONMENTAL LEADERSHIP AWARD BY CIANJ AND COMMERCE MAGAZINE. MORRISTOWN, N.J., APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JERSEY CENTRAL POWER AND LIGHT (JCP&L), A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED WITH THE ENVIRONMENTAL LEADERSHIP AWARD FOR RECYCLING/POLLUTION PREVENTION PROGRAMS BY THE COMMERCE & INDUSTRY ASSOCIATION OF NEW JERSEY (CIANJ) AND COMMERCE MAGAZINE.
