FIRSTENERGY TO DONATE, PLANT 700 TREES ACROSS MAHONING VALLEY TO CELEBRATE EARTH AND ARBOR DAYS. AKRON, OHIO , APRIL 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR, FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) WILL DONATE 300 TREES TO MILL CREEK METROPARKS IN MAHONING COUNTY, OHIO, TO CELEBRATE EARTH AND ARBOR DAYS. A MIXED VARIETY OF HARDWOOD TREES WILL BE PLANTED ON MONDAY, APRIL 25, BY EMPLOYEES OF FIRSTENERGY AND ITS OHIO EDISON ELECTRIC COMPANY THROUGHOUT THE METROPARKS' 402-ACRE FARM IN CANFIELD.
