WEST PENN POWER ELECTRICAL SYSTEM UPGRADES TO HELP PREVENT SERVICE DISRUPTIONS FOR CUSTOMERS IN ALLEGHENY AND WASHINGTON COUNTIES. GREENSBURG, PA. , JAN. 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEST PENN POWER, A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), IS UPGRADING ITS DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM IN ALLEGHENY AND WASHINGTON COUNTIES TO HELP PREVENT LENGTHY SERVICE INTERRUPTIONS, PARTICULARLY DURING SEVERE WEATHER.
