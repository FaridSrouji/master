MON POWER PLANTS 1,000 TREES AT WATTERS SMITH MEMORIAL STATE PARK. FAIRMONT, W.VA., NOV. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) RECENTLY DONATED 1,000 TREES TO THE WATTERS SMITH MEMORIAL STATE PARK IN HARRISON COUNTY, AND EMPLOYEES FROM ITS MON POWER ELECTRIC COMPANY PLANTED THE TREES AT THE 532-ACRE HISTORICAL PARK OVER TWO DAYS IN EARLY NOVEMBER.
