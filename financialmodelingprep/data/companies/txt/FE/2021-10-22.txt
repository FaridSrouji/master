FIRSTENERGY PENNSYLVANIA UTILITIES ENCOURAGE CUSTOMERS TO ARRANGE PAYMENT PLANS AND APPLY FOR BILL ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS. GREENSBURG, PA., OCT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RESIDENTIAL CUSTOMERS OF PENELEC, PENN POWER, MET-ED AND WEST PENN POWER, SUBSIDIARIES OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), WHO ARE HAVING DIFFICULTY MAKING ENDS MEET ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THEIR UTILITY NOW TO ENROLL IN PAYMENT PLANS OR TO RECEIVE REFERRALS FOR BILL ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS.
