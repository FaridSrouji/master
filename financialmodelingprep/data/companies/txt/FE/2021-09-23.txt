FIRSTENERGY, PENN POWER DONATE AND PLANT NEARLY 130 TREES AT BUHL PARK. HERMITAGE, PA., SEPT. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO CELEBRATE THE START OF THE FALL SEASON, FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) AND ITS ELECTRIC COMPANY PENN POWER RECENTLY DONATED NEARLY 130 TREES TO BUHL PARK IN HERMITAGE, PENNSYLVANIA.
FIRSTENERGY HONORED BY WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISE CENTER FOR SUPPLIER DIVERSITY PROGRAMS. AKRON, OHIO, SEPT. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN RECOGNITION OF ITS COMMITMENT TO SUPPLIER DIVERSITY AND WOMEN-OWNED BUSINESSES, FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) HAS BEEN PRESENTED WITH THE 2021 "REGIONAL COUNCIL MEMBER DONE DEALS" AWARD BY THE WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISE CENTER – EAST (WBEC-EAST).
