IDEX REPORTS FIRST QUARTER RESULTS; RAISES FULL YEAR GUIDANCE; Q1 ORDERS AND SALES UP 10 PERCENT OVERALL AND 6 PERCENT ORGANICALLY; Q1 REPORTED EPS WAS $1.48 WITH ADJUSTED EPS OF $1.51. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEX CORPORATION (NYSE: IEX) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE THREE MONTH PERIOD ENDED MARCH 31, 2021.
