IDEX CORPORATION INCREASES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND SEVEN PERCENT. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEX CORPORATION (NYSE:IEX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A SEVEN PERCENT INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.64 PER COMMON SHARE. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID JUNE 23, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 9, 2023. THIS DIVIDEND REPRESENTS THE COMPANY'S 114TH CONSECUTIVE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYMENT.
