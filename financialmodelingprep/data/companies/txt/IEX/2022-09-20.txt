IDEX CORPORATION DECLARE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEX CORPORATION (NYSE:IEX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.60 PER COMMON SHARE. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID OCTOBER 28, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF OCTOBER 14, 2022. THIS DIVIDEND REPRESENTS THE COMPANY'S 112TH CONSECUTIVE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYMENT.
