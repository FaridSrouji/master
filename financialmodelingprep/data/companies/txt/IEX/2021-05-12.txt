IDEX CORPORATION INCREASES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND EIGHT PERCENT. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEX CORPORATION (NYSE:IEX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED AN EIGHT PERCENT INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.54 PER COMMON SHARE. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID MAY 28, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 24, 2021. THIS DIVIDEND REPRESENTS THE COMPANY'S 106TH CONSECUTIVE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYMENT.
