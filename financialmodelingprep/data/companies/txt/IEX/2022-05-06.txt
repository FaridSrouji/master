IDEX CORPORATION INCREASES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ELEVEN PERCENT. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEX CORPORATION (NYSE:IEX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED AN ELEVEN PERCENT INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.60 PER COMMON SHARE. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID MAY 27, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 17, 2022. THIS DIVIDEND REPRESENTS THE COMPANY'S 110TH CONSECUTIVE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYMENT.
