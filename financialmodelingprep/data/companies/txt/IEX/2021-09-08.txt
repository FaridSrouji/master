IDEX CORPORATION DECLARE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEX CORPORATION (NYSE:IEX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.54 PER COMMON SHARE. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID OCTOBER 29, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF OCTOBER 15, 2021. THIS DIVIDEND REPRESENTS THE COMPANY'S 108TH CONSECUTIVE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYMENT.
