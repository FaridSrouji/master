IDEX CORPORATION DECLARE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEX CORPORATION (NYSE:IEX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.60 PER COMMON SHARE. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID JULY 29, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 15, 2022. THIS DIVIDEND REPRESENTS THE COMPANY'S 111TH CONSECUTIVE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYMENT.
