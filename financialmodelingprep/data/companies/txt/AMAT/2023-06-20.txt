APPLIED MATERIALS CHARTS PROGRESS TOWARDS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE GOALS IN LATEST SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., JUNE 20, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APPLIED MATERIALS, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PUBLICATION OF ITS LATEST SUSTAINABILITY REPORT, DETAILING ITS ESG (ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE) INITIATIVES AND RESULTS OVER THE PAST YEAR. THE REPORT HIGHLIGHTS THE IMPACT OF THE COMPANY'S ESG EFFORTS WITHIN ITS OWN ORGANIZATION, WITH ITS SUPPLIERS AND CUSTOMERS AND ON THE GLOBAL ELECTRONICS ECOSYSTEM.
