APPLIED MATERIALS ANNOUNCES CASH DIVIDEND. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., JUNE 08, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APPLIED MATERIALS, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.32 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON SEPT. 14, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUG. 24, 2023.
