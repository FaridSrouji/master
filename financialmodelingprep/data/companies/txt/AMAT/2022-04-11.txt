APPLIED MATERIALS ANNOUNCES 2022 SUPPLIER EXCELLENCE AWARDS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., APRIL 11, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APPLIED MATERIALS, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RECIPIENTS OF ITS 2022 SUPPLIER EXCELLENCE AWARDS FOR CONTRIBUTIONS MADE TO APPLIED'S BUSINESS OVER THE PAST YEAR. THE AWARDS REFLECT OUTSTANDING TECHNICAL AND OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE IN SEVERAL AREAS INCLUDING QUALITY, SERVICE, LEAD TIME, DELIVERY, COST AND RESPONSIVENESS.
