APPLIED MATERIALS APPOINTS SEMICONDUCTOR INDUSTRY VETERAN BRICE HILL AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MARCH 07, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APPLIED MATERIALS, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BRICE HILL HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. HILL HAS 25 YEARS OF FINANCE LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCE WITHIN THE SEMICONDUCTOR INDUSTRY, MOST RECENTLY AS CFO OF XILINX, INC., AND PREVIOUSLY AT INTEL CORPORATION WHERE HE WAS THE FINANCE LEADER OF MANY OF THE COMPANY'S LARGEST BUSINESSES AND FUNCTIONS.
