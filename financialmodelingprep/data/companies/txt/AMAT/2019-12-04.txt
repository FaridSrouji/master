APPLIED MATERIALS, INC. APPROVES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON MARCH 11, 2020. APPLIED MATERIALS, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.21 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON THE COMPANY’S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 11, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEB. 19, 2020.
