APPLIED MATERIALS ANNOUNCES CASH DIVIDEND. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., DEC. 12, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APPLIED MATERIALS, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.26 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 16, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEB. 23, 2023.
