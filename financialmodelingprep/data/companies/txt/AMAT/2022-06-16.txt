APPLIED MATERIALS BROADENS ITS TECHNOLOGY PORTFOLIO FOR SPECIALTY CHIPS WITH ACQUISITION OF PICOSUN. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., JUNE 16, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APPLIED MATERIALS, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ACQUIRED PICOSUN OY, A PRIVATELY HELD SEMICONDUCTOR EQUIPMENT COMPANY BASED IN ESPOO, FINLAND. PICOSUN IS AN INNOVATOR IN ATOMIC LAYER DEPOSITION (ALD) TECHNOLOGY, PRIMARILY FOR SPECIALTY SEMICONDUCTORS.
