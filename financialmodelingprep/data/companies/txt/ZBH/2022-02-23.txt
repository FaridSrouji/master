ZIMVIE TO JOIN S&P SMALLCAP 600. NEW YORK, FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ZIMVIE INC. (NASD:ZIMV) WILL REPLACE TABULA RASA HEALTHCARE INC. (NASD:TRHC) IN THE S&P SMALLCAP 600 EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPENING OF TRADING ON WEDNESDAY, MARCH 2. S&P 500 CONSTITUENT ZIMMER BIOMET HOLDINGS INC. (NYSE: ZBH) IS SPINNING OFF ZIMVIE IN A TRANSACTION EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED ON MARCH 1.
