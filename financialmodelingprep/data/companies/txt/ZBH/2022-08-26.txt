ZIMMER BIOMET ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FOR THIRD QUARTER OF 2022. WARSAW, IND. , AUG. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ZIMMER BIOMET HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE AND SIX: ZBH), A GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED THE PAYMENT OF A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO STOCKHOLDERS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022.
