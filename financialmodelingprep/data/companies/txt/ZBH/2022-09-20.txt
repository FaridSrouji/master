ZIMMER BIOMET RECEIVES FDA CLEARANCE FOR IDENTITY™ SHOULDER SYSTEM FOR SHOULDER REPLACEMENT. WARSAW, IND. , SEPT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ZIMMER BIOMET HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE AND SIX: ZBH), A GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) 510(K) CLEARANCE OF THE IDENTITY™ SHOULDER SYSTEM FOR ANATOMIC, REVERSE AND REVISION SHOULDER REPLACEMENT.
