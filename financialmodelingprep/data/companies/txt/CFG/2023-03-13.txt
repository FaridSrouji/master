CITIZENS EXTENDS BRANCH HOURS TO FURTHER SERVE CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GIVEN RECENT MARKET DEVELOPMENTS, CITIZENS HAS SEEN HIGHER THAN NORMAL INTEREST FROM PROSPECTIVE NEW CUSTOMERS OVER THE PAST FEW DAYS. IN AN EFFORT TO FURTHER SUPPORT THESE POTENTIAL NEW CUSTOMERS AS THEY NAVIGATE ONGOING MARKET UNCERTAINTY, CITIZENS WILL TEMPORARILY EXTEND HOURS AT BRANCHES ACROSS ITS RETAIL FOOTPRINT, EFFECTIVE TOMORROW, MARCH 14.
