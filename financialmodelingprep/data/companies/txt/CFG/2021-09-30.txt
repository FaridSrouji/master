CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP NAMES SARAH LINDSTROM TO LEAD BUSINESS BANKING. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. (NYSE: CFG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SARAH LINDSTROM WILL BECOME HEAD OF BUSINESS BANKING, EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 4. LINDSTROM WILL REPORT TO SIMON GRIFFITHS, HEAD OF CORE BANKING FOR CITIZENS' CONSUMER BANK. LINDSTROM JOINED CITIZENS IN 2017 AND WAS MOST RECENTLY HEAD OF RETAIL NETWORK TRANSFORMATION & BRANCH OPERATIONS.
