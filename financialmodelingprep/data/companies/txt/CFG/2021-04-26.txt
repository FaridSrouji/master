CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP NAMES CHRIS WEYRAUCH TO LEAD WEALTH BUSINESS. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. (NYSE: CFG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHRIS WEYRAUCH WILL JOIN THE BANK AS HEAD OF WEALTH MANAGEMENT, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. WEYRAUCH WILL REPORT TO BRENDAN COUGHLIN, HEAD OF CONSUMER BANKING, AND WILL SUCCEED RYAN PARKER, WHO WILL BE DEPARTING THE COMPANY FOR FAMILY REASONS. WEYRAUCH COMES TO CITIZENS AFTER A 25-YEAR CAREER AT TIAA WHERE HE BUILT AND LED ITS DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER BUSINESS. HE MOST RECENTLY SERVED CONCURRENTLY AS HEAD OF
