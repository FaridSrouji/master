CITIZENS' 2023 PAYMENT TRENDS SURVEY FINDS MIDDLE-MARKET COMPANIES ARE EMBRACING MODERN PAYMENTS, YET TRADITIONAL METHODS PERSIST. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #CBCI--CORPORATE TREASURY DEPARTMENTS ARE EMBRACING SOCIAL TOKENS, REAL-TIME PAYMENTS, VIRTUAL CARDS AND OTHER DIGITAL INNOVATIONS FOR THEIR SPEED, SECURITY AND CONVENIENCE, ACCORDING TO NEW RESEARCH FROM CITIZENS. YET, TRADITIONAL PAYMENT MODES – CHECKS, AUTOMATED CLEARING HOUSE (ACH) AND PHYSICAL CREDIT CARDS – STILL HAVE A STRONGHOLD. “THIS IS A TIME OF DISRUPTION IN THE PAYMENTS WORLD,” SAID MATT RICHARDSON, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND HEAD OF TREASURY PRODUCT S.
