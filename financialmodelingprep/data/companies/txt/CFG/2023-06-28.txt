CITIZENS AND THE TILE SHOP PARTNER TO PROVIDE FLEXIBLE FINANCING OPTIONS FOR HOME DESIGN PROJECTS. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITIZENS TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW PARTNERSHIP WITH THE TILE SHOP, ENABLING THE SPECIALTY RETAILER OF TILES, MATERIALS, AND RELATED ACCESSORIES TO OFFER SHOPPERS A MORE PREDICTABLE, AFFORDABLE WAY TO PAY FOR IN-STORE PURCHASES AND BRING THEIR HOME DECOR PROJECTS TO LIFE WITHOUT COMPROMISE.
