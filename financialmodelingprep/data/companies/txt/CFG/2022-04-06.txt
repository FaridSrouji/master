CITIZENS NAMES BEN VINZANT AS CHIEF DATA AND ANALYTICS OFFICER. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. (NYSE: CFG) HAS NAMED BEN VINZANT AS CHIEF DATA AND ANALYTICS OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JUNE 27, 2022. VINZANT WILL JOIN CITIZENS FROM JPMORGAN CHASE, WHERE HE MOST RECENTLY SERVED AS MANAGING DIRECTOR, HEAD OF SALES SCIENCE. DURING THAT TIME, HE WAS RESPONSIBLE FOR INTEGRATING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND MACHINE LEARNING (AI/ML) INTO SALES CHANNELS, LEADING THE CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT TOOLS AND TECHNOLOGY AS THE PRODUCT OWNER
