CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCES TRANSITION OF U.S. LIBOR-LINKED PREFERRED STOCK TO TERM SOFR REPLACEMENT RATE. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. (NYSE: CFG OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT, AFTER JUNE 30, 2023, THREE-MONTH CME TERM SOFR, AS ADMINISTERED BY CME GROUP BENCHMARK ADMINISTRATION, LTD. (OR ANY SUCCESSOR ADMINISTRATOR), PLUS A TENOR SPREAD ADJUSTMENT OF 0.26161% (“SOFR RATE”) WILL BE THE BENCHMARK REPLACEMENT TO BE USED, IF AND WHEN NECESSARY, FOR THE FOLLOWING OUTSTANDING FIXED-TO-FLOATING RATE PREFERRED STOCK AND DEPOSITARY SHARES REPRESENTING OWNERSHIP.
