CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP ANNOUNCES PRIME RATE CHANGE. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. (NYSE: CFG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT CITIZENS BANK, N.A. HAS RAISED ITS PRIME LENDING RATE TO 8.00 PERCENT FROM 7.75 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE THURSDAY, MARCH 23, 2023. ABOUT CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. IS ONE OF THE NATION'S OLDEST AND LARGEST FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS, WITH $226.7 BILLION IN ASSETS AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2022.
