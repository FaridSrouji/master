CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $800 MILLION OF CITIZENS BANK, N.A. SENIOR NOTES. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITIZENS FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. (NYSE: CFG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF $800 MILLION OF CITIZENS BANK, N.A. (“CBNA”) 4.575% FIXED/FLOATING RATE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (THE “NOTES”). CBNA INTENDS TO USE THE NET PROCEEDS OF THE NOTES OFFERING FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES. THE NOTES OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON AUGUST 9, 2022, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
