BALL CORPORATION AND NOVELIS INCREASE RECYCLED CONTENT OF THE BALL ALUMINUM CUP TO 90%. THE INFINITELY RECYCLABLE BALL ALUMINUM CUP NOW BOASTS THE HIGHEST RECYCLED CONTENT RATE OF ANY BEVERAGE PACKAGING IN ITS CATEGORY WESTMINSTER, COLO., APRIL 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BLL), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF INFINITELY RECYCLABLE ALUMINUM BEVERAGE PACKAGING, AND ALUMINUM RECYCLER AND SUPPLIER NOVELIS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BALL ALUMINUM CUP™ IS NOW COMPOSED OF 90% RECYCLED CONTENT.
