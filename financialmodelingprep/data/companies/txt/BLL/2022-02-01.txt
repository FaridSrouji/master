BALL CORPORATION RECOGNIZED IN TOP 1 PERCENT OF INDUSTRY BY S&P GLOBAL IN THE SUSTAINABILITY YEARBOOK 2022. WESTMINSTER, COLO., FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BLL) RECEIVED THE GOLD CLASS AND INDUSTRY MOVER AWARDS IN THE S&P GLOBAL 2022 SUSTAINABILITY YEARBOOK FOR ITS SUSTAINABILITY PRACTICES ACROSS THE COMPANY'S PACKAGING AND AEROSPACE BUSINESSES.
