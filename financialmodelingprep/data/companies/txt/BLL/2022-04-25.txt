BALL ALUMINUM CUP™ RECOGNIZED WITH A 2022 EDISON AWARD FOR INNOVATION IN PROVIDING CONSUMERS SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS. WESTMINSTER, COLO. , APRIL 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BLL) ANNOUNCED THAT THE BALL ALUMINUM CUP™ WAS NAMED A GOLD WINNER BY THE 2022 EDISON AWARDS IN THE FIELD OF CONSUMER SOLUTIONS: SUSTAINABILITY.
