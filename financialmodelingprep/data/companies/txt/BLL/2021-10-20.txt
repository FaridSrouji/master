BALL CORPORATION TO ADVANCE GLOBAL SUSTAINABILITY EFFORTS WITH DEVELOPMENT OF BRAZIL'S FIRST CIRCULAR ECONOMY LAB. WESTMINSTER, COLO., OCT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BLL) TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO CREATE BRAZIL'S FIRST CIRCULAR ECONOMY LAB IN FERNANDO DE NORONHA , ONE OF THE COUNTRY'S MOST POPULAR TOURIST DESTINATIONS AND A LEADER ON SUSTAINABLE TOURISM.
