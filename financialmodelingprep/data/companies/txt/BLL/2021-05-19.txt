BALL AEROSPACE-MICROSOFT TEAM COMPLETES DEMONSTRATIONS OF CLOUD-BASED DATA PROCESSING, EXPLOITATION AND DISSEMINATION. BOULDER, COLO., MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL AEROSPACE, IN COLLABORATION WITH MICROSOFT, SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED A SERIES OF DEMONSTRATIONS SHOWING THE VIABILITY OF USING COMMERCIAL CLOUD COMPUTING TO PROCESS AND SECURELY DELIVER ACTIONABLE INFORMATION QUICKLY TO THOSE WHO NEED IT, WHETHER THEY ARE IN A GROUND STATION, COMMAND CENTER OR ON THE BATTLEFIELD.
