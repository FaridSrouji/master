BALL AEROSPACE SUPPORTS CRITICAL DESIGN REVIEW OF NASA'S SPHEREX MISSION. BROOMFIELD, COLO., FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL AEROSPACE SUPPORTED COMPLETION OF CRITICAL DESIGN REVIEW (CDR) FOR NASA'S SPECTRO-PHOTOMETER FOR THE HISTORY OF THE UNIVERSE, EPOCH OF REIONIZATION AND ICES EXPLORER (SPHEREX) MISSION.
