BALL CORPORATION BOARD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, ELECTS DUNE IVES AS DIRECTOR. WESTMINSTER, COLO., OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION'S (NYSE: BLL) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF 20 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE DEC. 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DEC. 1, 2021.
