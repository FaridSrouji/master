BALL CORPORATION PRICES $850 MILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. WESTMINSTER, COLO., SEPT. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BLL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT PRICED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF $850 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 3.125% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE "NOTES").
BALL CORPORATION ANNOUNCES PUBLIC OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES. WESTMINSTER, COLO., SEPT. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BLL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMMENCED AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF APPROXIMATELY $750 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE "NOTES").
