BALL AEROSPACE-BUILT SPACECRAFT FOR NASA X-RAY ASTROPHYSICS MISSION SUCCESSFULLY LAUNCHES FROM KENNEDY SPACE CENTER. CAPE CANAVERAL, FLA., DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NASA'S IMAGING X-RAY POLARIMETRY EXPLORER SPACECRAFT BUILT BY BALL AEROSPACE SUCCESSFULLY LAUNCHED TODAY ABOARD A SPACEX FALCON 9 ROCKET FROM NASA'S KENNEDY SPACE CENTER IN FLORIDA.
