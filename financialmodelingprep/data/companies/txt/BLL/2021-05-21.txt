BALL AEROSPACE SHIPS POLLUTION MONITORING INSTRUMENT TO SPACECRAFT MANUFACTURER FOR INTEGRATION. BROOMFIELD, COLO.,   MAY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL AEROSPACE SHIPPED NASA'S TROPOSPHERIC EMISSION: MONITORING OF POLLUTION (TEMPO) SPECTROMETER, AN AIR QUALITY MONITORING INSTRUMENT, TO THE SPACECRAFT MANUFACTURER FOR INTEGRATION.
