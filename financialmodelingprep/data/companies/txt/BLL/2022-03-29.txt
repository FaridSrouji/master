BALL CORPORATION RECEIVES ALUMINUM STEWARDSHIP INITIATIVE (ASI) CERTIFICATION FOR NORTH AND CENTRAL AMERICA BEVERAGE PACKAGING OPERATIONS. WESTMINSTER, COLO. , MARCH 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BLL) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT EARNED THE ALUMINUM STEWARDSHIP INITIATIVE (ASI) CERTIFICATION FOR ITS 25 BEVERAGE PACKAGING NORTH AND CENTRAL AMERICA (BPNCA) SITES WHICH INCLUDES 23 MANUFACTURING FACILITIES, ONE OFFICE, AND ONE TECHNICAL SUPPORT CENTER.
