BALL TO DONATE $1 MILLION TO COLORADO WILDFIRE RECOVERY EFFORTS. WESTMINSTER, COLO., JAN. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BLL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BALL FOUNDATION WILL DONATE $1 MILLION AND DEPLOY OTHER DISASTER RELIEF EFFORTS TO SUPPORT COMMUNITIES IN COLORADO DEVASTATED BY THE RECENT WILDFIRES.
