BALL BOARD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND; STOCK TICKER SYMBOL CHANGING TO BALL. WESTMINSTER, COLO. , APRIL 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION'S (NYSE: BLL) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF 20 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE JUNE 15, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 1, 2022.
