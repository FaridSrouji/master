BALL AEROSPACE COMPLETES PRELIMINARY DESIGN REVIEW OF NOAA'S SPACE WEATHER SATELLITE. BOULDER, COLO., JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL AEROSPACE SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED THE PRELIMINARY DESIGN REVIEW (PDR) FOR THE NATIONAL OCEANIC AND ATMOSPHERIC ADMINISTRATION'S (NOAA'S) SPACE WEATHER FOLLOW ON-LAGRANGE 1 (SWFO-L1) SPACECRAFT.
