CLOROX HEALTHCARE INVESTS IN NEXT GENERATION OF INFECTION PREVENTIONISTS WITH APIC 2021 ANNUAL CONFERENCE SCHOLARSHIPS. PLEASANTON, CALIF., JUNE 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CLOROX HEALTHCARE IS PROUD TO ONCE AGAIN PARTNER WITH THE ASSOCIATION FOR PROFESSIONALS IN INFECTION CONTROL AND EPIDEMIOLOGY (APIC) FOR ITS 2021 ANNUAL CONFERENCE, WHICH WILL BE HELD VIRTUALLY FROM JUNE 28-30, 2021.
