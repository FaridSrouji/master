CLOROX REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL YEAR 2021 RESULTS, PROVIDES FISCAL YEAR 2022 OUTLOOK. OAKLAND, CALIF., AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CLOROX COMPANY (NYSE: CLX) REPORTED A 9% SALES DECREASE AND A 68% DECREASE IN DILUTED NET EARNINGS PER SHARE (DILUTED EPS) FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER, WHICH ENDED JUNE 30, 2021.
