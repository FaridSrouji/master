THE CLOROX COMPANY DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 14, 2020. THE CLOROX COMPANY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.06 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE FEB. 14, 2020, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JAN. 29, 2020.
