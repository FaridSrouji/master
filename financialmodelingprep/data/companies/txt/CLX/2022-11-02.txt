EPA AGAIN NAMES CLOROX AS SAFER CHOICE PARTNER OF THE YEAR FOR ADVANCING INGREDIENT AND PRODUCT SAFETY. CLOROX RECEIVES ADDITIONAL EPA RECOGNITION FOR LEADERSHIP ON THE DESIGN FOR THE ENVIRONMENT INDUSTRY COALITION , DEMONSTRATING CONTINUED COMMITMENT TO PRODUCT STEWARDSHIP OAKLAND, CALIF. , NOV. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CLOROX COMPANY (NYSE: CLX) WAS NAMED A 2022 SAFER CHOICE PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARD WINNER FOR OUTSTANDING ACHIEVEMENT IN THE MANUFACTURING OF PRODUCTS WITH INGREDIENTS THAT THE ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (EPA) DESIGNATES AS SAFER FOR FAMILIES, PETS, WORKPLACES, COMMUNITIES, AND THE ENVIRONMENT.
