BILLY PORTER AND CLOROX® SCENTIVA® REINVENT SPRING CLEANING AS SELF-CARE. OAKLAND, CALIF., MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH SPRING UPON US, CLOROX® SCENTIVA® AND ACTOR, ACTIVIST AND FASHION ICON BILLY PORTER ARE CHALLENGING CONSUMERS TO REIMAGINE THEIR CLEAN ROUTINE WITH A MUSICALLY-CHARGED TIKTOK TRANSFORMATION CHALLENGE TO TURN HOMES ACROSS THE COUNTRY FROM DRAB TO FAB.
