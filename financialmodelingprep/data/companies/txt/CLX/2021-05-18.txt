GLAD® TEAMS UP WITH RECYCLOPS TO BRING RECYCLING TO 100,000 NEW HOUSEHOLDS. OAKLAND, CALIF., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLAD, THE NATION'S LEADING HOUSEHOLD WASTE SOLUTIONS BRAND, AND RECYCLOPS, A TECHNOLOGY-ENABLED SUSTAINABILITY AND RECYCLING STARTUP, ARE JOINING FORCES TO EXPAND ACCESS TO RECYCLING FOR CONSUMERS THROUGHOUT THE COUNTRY.
RECYCLOPS RAISES $3M SEED ROUND TO BRING RECYCLING WHERE IT DOESN'T EXIST. SANDY, UTAH--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #GLAD--SUSTAINABILITY STARTUP RECYCLOPS HAS JUST CLOSED A $3-MILLION SEED ROUND INVESTMENT LED BY LERER HIPPEAU AND GLAD, A MEMBER OF THE CLOROX COMPANY.
