INVITATION HOMES NAMES SENIOR VICE PRESIDENTS. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVITATION HOMES INC. (NYSE: INVH) (“INVITATION HOMES” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED SEVERAL LEADERSHIP CHANGES AND PROMOTIONS. DAVID AYERS IS NOW SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT (SVP), ENGINEERING. AYERS JOINED INVITATION HOMES IN MARCH 2020 AS VICE PRESIDENT, ENGINEERING. PRIOR TO JOINING INVITATION HOMES, HE WAS GROUP VICE PRESIDENT OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY FOR LESLIE'S POOLMART, INC.
