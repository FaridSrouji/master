INVITATION HOMES ANNOUNCES NEW $725 MILLION SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED SEVEN-YEAR TERM LOAN. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVITATION HOMES INC. (NYSE: INVH) (“INVITATION HOMES” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A NEW $725 MILLION SEVEN-YEAR UNSECURED TERM LOAN MATURING IN JUNE 2029. THE SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED TERM LOAN BEARS INTEREST AT RATES BASED ON THE COMPANY'S SENIOR UNSECURED CREDIT RATING, WHICH EQUATED TO AN INTEREST RATE OF ADJUSTED TERM SOFR PLUS 125 BASIS POINTS AT THE TIME OF CLOSING BASED ON THE COMPANY'S CURRENT CREDIT RATINGS.
