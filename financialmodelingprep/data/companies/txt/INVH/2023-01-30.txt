INVITATION HOMES ANNOUNCES CFO SUCCESSION. DALLAS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--INVITATION HOMES INC. (NYSE: INVH) (“INVITATION HOMES” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED ERNIE FREEDMAN WILL STEP DOWN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND TREASURER, AS OF JUNE 1, 2023, AND CONCURRENTLY ANNOUNCED THE PLANNED SUCCESSION APPOINTMENT OF JONATHAN OLSEN, CURRENT EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE STRATEGY & FINANCE, AS THE COMPANY'S NEW EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND TREASURER. TO PROVIDE FOR A SMOOTH TRANSITION, FREEDMAN WILL ENTER INTO A CONSULTING ARRANGEMENT WITH THE COMPANY TO PROVIDE ADVISORY SERVICES THROUGH MARCH 31, 2024.
