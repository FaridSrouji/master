INVITATION HOMES PROMOTES CHARLES YOUNG TO PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVITATION HOMES INC. (NYSE: INVH) (“INVITATION HOMES” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PROMOTION OF CHARLES YOUNG TO PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER (COO). DALLAS TANNER WILL CONTINUE IN THE ROLE OF CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER (CEO). “WE ARE EXTREMELY PLEASED TO EXPAND THE ROLE CHARLES PLAYS IN THE ORGANIZATION.
