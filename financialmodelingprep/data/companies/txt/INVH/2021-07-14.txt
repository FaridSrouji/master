INVITATION HOMES GIVES NOTICE OF INTENT FOR PHYSICAL SETTLEMENT OF 2022 CONVERTIBLE NOTES. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVITATION HOMES INC. (NYSE: INVH) ("INVITATION HOMES" OR THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS NOTIFIED HOLDERS OF ITS 3.50% CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2022 (THE “NOTES”) OF THE COMPANY'S INTENT TO SETTLE CONVERSIONS OF THE NOTES WITH COMMON STOCK. THE NOTES MATURE ON JANUARY 15, 2022, AND HAVE A PAR VALUE OF $345 MILLION.
