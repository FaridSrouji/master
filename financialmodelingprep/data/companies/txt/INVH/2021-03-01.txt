INVITATION HOMES NAMES CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVITATION HOMES INC. (NYSE: INVH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PROMOTION OF JOHN GIBSON TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MR. GIBSON JOINED INVITATION HOMES IN DECEMBER 2016 AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, ASSET MANAGEMENT. HE WAS PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT, IN MARCH 2019.
