INVITATION HOMES INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON OR BEFORE NOVEMBER 27, 2019. INVITATION HOMES INC. ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.13 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON OR BEFORE NOVEMBER 27, 2019 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 13, 2019.
