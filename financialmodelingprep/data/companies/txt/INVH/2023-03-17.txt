INVITATION HOMES RECEIVES CREDIT RATING UPGRADE TO ‘BBB' FROM STANDARD AND POOR'S. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVITATION HOMES INC. (NYSE: INVH) (“INVITATION HOMES” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT S&P GLOBAL RATINGS (“S&P”) UPGRADED THE COMPANY'S ISSUER AND ISSUE-LEVEL CREDIT RATINGS TO ‘BBB' FROM ‘BBB-' WITH A STABLE OUTLOOK.
