INVITATION HOMES ANNOUNCES NEARLY 30% INCREASE TO CASH DIVIDEND. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVITATION HOMES INC. (NYSE: INVH) (“INVITATION HOMES” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.22 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK, REPRESENTING A 29.4% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.17 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON OR BEFORE FEBRUARY 28, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 14, 2022. ABOUT INVITATION HOMES INVITATIO
