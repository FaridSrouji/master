INVITATION HOMES INC. NAMES VIRGINIA SULIMAN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER. INVITATION HOMES INC. ANNOUNCED THAT VIRGINIA SULIMAN HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER. SULIMAN IS A SEASONED EXECUTIVE WHO BRINGS NEARLY THREE DECADES OF LEADERSHIP, TECHNOLOGY AND DIGITAL COMMERCE EXPERIENCE. SHE WILL LEAD THE COMPANY'S INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY TEAM AND ENSURE A WELL-DEVELOPED TECHNOLOGY STRATEGY, STRONG EXECUTION AND CUSTOMER SERVICE, AS WELL AS A SEAMLESS TECHNOLOGY EXPERIENCE FOR ASSOCIATES AND RESIDENTS. PRIOR TO JOINING INVITATION HOMES, SULIMAN WAS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF SOFTWARE ENGINEERING AT HILTON WORLDWIDE, WHERE SHE HELD ESCALATING ROLES SINCE 1997.
