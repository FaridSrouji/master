SANDIE O'CONNOR ELECTED TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BNY MELLON. NEW YORK, DEC. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON CORPORATION ("BNY MELLON") (NYSE: BK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED SANDIE O'CONNOR AS AN INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR, EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 13, 2021.  WITH THE ADDITION OF MS.
