BNY MELLON NAMES ROMAN REGELMAN CEO OF SECURITIES SERVICES AND DIGITAL. NEW YORK , APRIL 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON CORPORATION ("BNY MELLON") (NYSE: BK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ROMAN REGELMAN, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF ASSET SERVICING, WILL ALSO ASSUME LEADERSHIP OF THE ISSUER SERVICES BUSINESS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MR. REGELMAN, A MEMBER OF THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE, WILL CONTINUE TO LEAD DIGITAL FOR BNY MELLON.
