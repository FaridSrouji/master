BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT EXPANDS DENVER OFFICE WITH THREE NEW HIRES. DENVER, MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT EXPANDS ITS DENVER OFFICE WITH THREE NEW ADDITIONS INCLUDING MARIE DAWSON AS SENIOR FIDUCIARY SPECIALIST, STEVE STARZEC AS CLIENT STRATEGIST AND MATT MCCONATY AS ASSOCIATE CLIENT STRATEGIST.
