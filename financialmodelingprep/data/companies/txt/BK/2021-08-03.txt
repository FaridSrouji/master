BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT NAMES DANIEL GEBHART REGIONAL DIRECTOR, HEAD OF PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT IN NEW ENGLAND. BOSTON, AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT HAS APPOINTED DANIEL GEBHART AS REGIONAL DIRECTOR, HEAD OF PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT IN NEW ENGLAND. HE OVERSEES THE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT, WEALTH PLANNING AND ADVICE SERVICES FOR INDIVIDUALS, ENTREPRENEURS, AND ENDOWMENTS AND FOUNDATIONS FOR THE REGION, AS WELL AS PROVIDING DIRECT LEADERSHIP FOR THE WEALTH MANAGERS.
