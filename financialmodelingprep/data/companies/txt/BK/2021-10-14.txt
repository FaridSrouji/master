BNY MELLON'S PERSHING LAUNCHES NEW BUSINESS UNIT, PERSHING X(SM), TO BE LED BY AINSLIE SIMMONDS. JERSEY CITY, N.J., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON'S PERSHING ("PERSHING") HAS APPOINTED AINSLIE SIMMONDS AS PRESIDENT OF PERSHING X SM, A NEW BUSINESS UNIT WITHIN PERSHING THAT WILL DESIGN AND BUILD INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS FOR THE ADVISORY INDUSTRY.
