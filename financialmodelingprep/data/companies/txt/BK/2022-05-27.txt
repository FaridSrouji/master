BNY MELLON ALCENTRA GLOBAL MULTI-STRATEGY CREDIT FUND, INC. DECLARES DISTRIBUTION. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ON MAY 27, 2022, BNY MELLON ALCENTRA GLOBAL MULTI-STRATEGY CREDIT FUND, INC. (TICKER: XALCX) TODAY DECLARED A DISTRIBUTION OF $1.75 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 28, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 13, 2022. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS JUNE 10, 2022. THE PREVIOUS DIVIDEND DECLARED IN FEBRUARY WAS $1.75 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.
