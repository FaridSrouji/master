BNY MELLON ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF 3.55% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2021. NEW YORK, JULY 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON CORPORATION ("BNY MELLON") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL REDEEM ALL OF THE ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING 3.55% SENIOR NOTES DUE SEPTEMBER 23, 2021 (CUSIP 06406HBY4) (THE "NOTES"). THE NOTES HAVE AN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $1,500,000,000.
