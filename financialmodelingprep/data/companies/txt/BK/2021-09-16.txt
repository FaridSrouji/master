BNY MELLON EXPANDS OPTUS BANK RELATIONSHIP WITH $3 MILLION INVESTMENT AS PART OF THE U. S. DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY FINANCIAL AGENT MENTOR-PROTÉGÉ PROGRAM. NEW YORK, SEPT. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT IS EXPANDING ITS RELATIONSHIP WITH SOUTH CAROLINA-BASED OPTUS BANK AS PART OF THE TREASURY DEPARTMENT'S FINANCIAL AGENT MENTOR-PROTÉGÉ PROGRAM.
