BNY MELLON NAMES DERMOT MCDONOGH AS NEXT CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER; EMILY PORTNEY TO LEAD TREASURY SERVICES, CREDIT SERVICES, AND CLEARANCE & COLLATERAL MANAGEMENT BUSINESSES. NEW YORK , JULY 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON CORPORATION ("BNY MELLON") (NYSE: BK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DERMOT MCDONOGH HAS BEEN APPOINTED AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER ("CFO") FOR THE COMPANY, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 1, 2023. MR. MCDONOGH WILL JOIN THE COMPANY ON NOVEMBER 1, 2022, REPORTING TO BNY MELLON'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER ("CEO"), AND HE WILL JOIN THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE.
