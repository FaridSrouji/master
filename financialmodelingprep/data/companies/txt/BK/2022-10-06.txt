BUSINESS ADOPTION OF EMERGING ELECTRONIC PAYMENT TOOLS IS ON THE RISE, AITE-NOVARICA/BNY MELLON REPORT SHOWS. NEW YORK , OCT. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON, IN COLLABORATION WITH AITE-NOVARICA GROUP, TODAY RELEASED A STUDY EXAMINING COMMERCIAL PAYMENT TRENDS, FINDING THAT BUSINESSES ARE EMBRACING A RANGE OF EMERGING DIGITAL TOOLS SUCH AS REAL-TIME PAYMENTS (RTP) AND DIGITAL WALLETS. THE REPORT ALSO FOUND SIGNIFICANT REGIONAL DIFFERENCES IN CHANNEL USAGE.
