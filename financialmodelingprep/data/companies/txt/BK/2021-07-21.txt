BNY MELLON APPOINTS AKASH SHAH AS ITS FIRST CHIEF GROWTH OFFICER. NEW YORK, JULY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON CORPORATION ("BNY MELLON") (NYSE: BK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT AKASH SHAH HAS BEEN APPOINTED CHIEF GROWTH OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. THIS NEWLY CREATED ROLE WILL OVERSEE STRATEGY, MARKETING & COMMUNICATIONS, AND GLOBAL CLIENT MANAGEMENT.
