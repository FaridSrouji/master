BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT NAMES JEREMY GONSALVES AS NATIONAL DIRECTOR OF PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT. BOSTON, JULY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT HAS APPOINTED JEREMY GONSALVES AS NATIONAL DIRECTOR OF PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT. JEREMY MANAGES THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE FIRM'S ASSET ALLOCATION AND INVESTMENT STRATEGY.
