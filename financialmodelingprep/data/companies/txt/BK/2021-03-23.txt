BNY MELLON ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF 2.050% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2021. NEW YORK, MARCH 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON CORPORATION ("BNY MELLON") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL REDEEM ALL OF THE ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING 2.050% SENIOR NOTES DUE MAY 3, 2021 (CUSIP 06406FAB9) (THE "NOTES"). THE NOTES HAVE AN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $1,250,000,000.
