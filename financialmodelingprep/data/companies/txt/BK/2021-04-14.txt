BNY MELLON SELECTED BY CHARLES SCHWAB INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT TO PROVIDE TRANSFER AGENCY SERVICES. NEW YORK, APRIL 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON HAS SECURED A MANDATE FROM GLOBAL INVESTMENT MANAGER, CHARLES SCHWAB INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT, INC., TO DELIVER TRANSFER AGENCY SERVICES. BNY MELLON WAS SELECTED BASED ON ITS LONG-TERM COMMITMENT TO THE TRANSFER AGENCY BUSINESS, AS WELL AS ITS EXPERIENCE DELIVERING A LEADING CLIENT SERVICE MODEL, TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM AND OPERATIONAL SERVICES.
