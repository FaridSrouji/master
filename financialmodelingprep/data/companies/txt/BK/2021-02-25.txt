BNY MELLON ALCENTRA GLOBAL MULTI-STRATEGY CREDIT FUND, INC. DECLARES DISTRIBUTION. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ON FEBRUARY 25, 2021, BNY MELLON ALCENTRA GLOBAL MULTI-STRATEGY CREDIT FUND, INC. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS MARCH 10, 2021. THE PREVIOUS DIVIDEND DECLARED IN NOVEMBER WAS $1.75 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.
