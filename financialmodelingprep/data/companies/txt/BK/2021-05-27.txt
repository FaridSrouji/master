BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT APPOINTS KAREN WAWRZASZEK AS REGIONAL PRESIDENT IN WASHINGTON DC. NEW YORK, MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT NAMED KAREN WAWRZASZEK AS THE NEW REGIONAL PRESIDENT FOR THE DC AREA. SHE SUCCEEDS CAMILLE ALEXANDER, WHO WAS APPOINTED HEAD OF SALES-INVESTOR SOLUTIONS, BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT.
