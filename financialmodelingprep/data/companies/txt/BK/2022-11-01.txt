COMPLETION OF SALE OF ALCENTRA NY, LLC BY THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON CORPORATION TO FRANKLIN TEMPLETON. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ON NOVEMBER 1, 2022, THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON CORPORATION'S SALE OF ALCENTRA NY, LLC ("ALCENTRA NY") TO FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC., A GLOBAL INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT ORGANIZATION OPERATING AS FRANKLIN TEMPLETON ("FRANKLIN TEMPLETON"), WAS COMPLETED TODAY, NOVEMBER 1, 2022 (THE "CLOSING DATE").
FRANKLIN TEMPLETON COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF ALCENTRA, ONE OF THE LARGEST EUROPEAN ALTERNATIVE CREDIT MANAGERS. SAN MATEO, CALIF.
