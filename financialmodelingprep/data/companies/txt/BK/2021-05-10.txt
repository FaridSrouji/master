BNY MELLON MARKETS APPOINTS LAIDE MAJIYAGBE HEAD OF FINANCING AND LIQUIDITY. NEW YORK, MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LAIDE MAJIYAGBE HAS BEEN APPOINTED HEAD OF FINANCING AND LIQUIDITY ASSUMING OVERSIGHT OF BNY MELLON'S SECURITIES FINANCE, LIQUIDITY SERVICES AND COLLATERAL SEGREGATION BUSINESSES. IN THIS NEWLY CREATED POSITION, MS.
