BNY MELLON ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP SUCCESSION PLAN NAMING ROBIN VINCE CEO-ELECT. NEW YORK, MARCH 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON CORPORATION ("BNY MELLON") (NYSE: BK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THOMAS P. (TODD) GIBBONS HAS DECIDED TO RETIRE AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND MEMBER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS ON AUGUST 31, 2022, AFTER A DISTINGUISHED 36-YEAR CAREER WITH THE COMPANY.
