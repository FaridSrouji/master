INVESCO EXPANDS RELATIONSHIP WITH BNY MELLON FOR TRANSFER AGENCY SERVICES. LONDON, JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON HAS BEEN APPOINTED BY INVESCO FUND MANAGEMENT LIMITED AND INVESCO MANAGEMENT S.A. TO PROVIDE TRANSFER AGENCY SERVICES TO US$100BN* OF THEIR RESPECTIVE ICVC AND SICAV FUND RANGES.
