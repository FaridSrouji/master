BNY MELLON TRANSFORMS CROSS-BORDER PAYMENT TRANSACTIONS BETWEEN EGYPT AND CHINA. NEW YORK , OCT. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON ANNOUNCES IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY FACILITATED THE FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND, FULLY-TRANSPARENT PAYMENT TRANSACTION BETWEEN EGYPT AND CHINA. PREVIOUSLY, LOW-VALUE INTERNATIONAL PAYMENTS EXPERIENCED LIMITED COST TRANSPARENCY AND UNCERTAINTY OVER SETTLEMENT TIMELINES.
