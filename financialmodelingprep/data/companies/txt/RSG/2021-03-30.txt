REPUBLIC SERVICES ANNOUNCES COMPLETION OF COMPREHENSIVE CEO SUCCESSION PLAN. PHOENIX, MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG) TODAY ANNOUNCED INDUSTRY ICON DONALD W. SLAGER HAS PROVIDED HIS NOTICE OF INTENT TO RETIRE FROM THE COMPANY AFTER SERVING AS ITS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER FOR MORE THAN A DECADE AND SERVING AS CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER FOR NEARLY A DECADE PRIOR.
