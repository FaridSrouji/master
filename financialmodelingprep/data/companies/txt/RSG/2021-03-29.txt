REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. RELEASES INAUGURAL REPORT ALIGNED WITH RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE TASK FORCE ON CLIMATE-RELATED FINANCIAL DISCLOSURES. PHOENIX, MARCH 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG) TODAY RELEASED THE COMPANY'S INAUGURAL CLIMATE DISCLOSURE REPORT ALIGNED WITH RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE TASK FORCE ON CLIMATE-RELATED FINANCIAL DISCLOSURES (TCFD). THE REPORT, WHICH IDENTIFIES THE COMPANY'S CLIMATE-RELATED RISKS AND OPPORTUNITIES, IS THE INDUSTRY'S FIRST COMPREHENSIVE TCFD REPORTING AND REPRESENTS REPUBLIC'S COMMITMENT TO ENVIRONMENTAL LEADERSHIP AND SUSTAINABILITY.
