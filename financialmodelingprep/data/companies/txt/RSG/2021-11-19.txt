REPUBLIC SERVICES NAMED TO DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX FOR SIXTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. PHOENIX, NOV. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG), A LEADER IN ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES, WAS NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX (DJSI) FOR NORTH AMERICA FOR A SIXTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. THE COMPANY ALSO ADVANCED IN MSCI'S ESG RATINGS TO AN "A," AHEAD OF ITS LARGEST INDUSTRY PEERS.
