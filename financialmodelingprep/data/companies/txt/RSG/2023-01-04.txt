REPUBLIC SERVICES NAMED TO DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX FOR SEVENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. COMPANY ALSO RANKS HIGHEST AMONG INDUSTRY PEERS FOR CDP CLIMATE CHANGE PHOENIX , JAN. 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR THE SEVENTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG), A LEADER IN THE ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES INDUSTRY, HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY NORTH AMERICA INDEX (DJSI). THE 2022 DJSI NORTH AMERICA INDEX REPRESENTS THE TOP 20% OF THE 600 LARGEST NORTH AMERICAN COMPANIES IN THE S&P GLOBAL BROAD MARKET INDEX BASED ON LONG-TERM ECONOMIC, ENVIRONMENTAL AND SOCIAL CRITERIA.
