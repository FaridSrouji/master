REPUBLIC SERVICES OPENS CALIFORNIA'S FIRST FULLY SOLAR-POWERED COMPOST FACILITY. PHOENIX, OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF CALIFORNIA'S FIRST FULLY SOLAR-POWERED COMPOST FACILITY, WHICH WILL RECYCLE FOOD AND YARD WASTE FROM THE SAN DIEGO REGION.
