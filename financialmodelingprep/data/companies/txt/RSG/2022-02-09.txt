REPUBLIC SERVICES TO ACQUIRE US ECOLOGY, A LEADING ENVIRONMENTAL SOLUTIONS COMPANY. PHOENIX AND BOISE, IDAHO, FEB. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG) AND US ECOLOGY, INC. (NASDAQ-GS: ECOL) HAVE ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH REPUBLIC SERVICES WILL ACQUIRE ALL OUTSTANDING SHARES OF US ECOLOGY FOR $48 PER SHARE IN CASH, REPRESENTING A TOTAL VALUE OF APPROXIMATELY $2.2 BILLION INCLUDING NET DEBT OF APPROXIMATELY $0.7 BILLION. US ECOLOGY IS A LEADING PROVIDER OF ENVIRONMENTAL SOLUTIONS OFFERING TREATMENT, RECYCLING AND DISPOSAL OF HAZARDOUS, NON-HAZARDOUS AND SPECIALTY WASTE.
