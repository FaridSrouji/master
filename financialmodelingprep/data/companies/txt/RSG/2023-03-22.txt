REPUBLIC SERVICES CHARITABLE FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES 2023 NATIONAL NEIGHBORHOOD PROMISE® GRANTS. TWELVE COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS AWARDED $2.5 MILLION FOR NEIGHBORHOOD REVITALIZATION PHOENIX , MARCH 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG) AND THE REPUBLIC SERVICES CHARITABLE FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THE 12 ORGANIZATIONS RECEIVING 2023 NATIONAL NEIGHBORHOOD PROMISE® GRANTS. THE FOUNDATION'S NATIONAL NEIGHBORHOOD PROMISE PROGRAM AIMS TO REVITALIZE COMMUNITIES AND THE QUALITY OF LIFE OF RESIDENTS THROUGH VOLUNTEER PROJECTS, MONETARY DONATIONS AND IN-KIND SERVICES.
