REPUBLIC SERVICES' FELIX MARTINEZ NAMED NWRA'S 2023 NATIONAL RESIDENTIAL DRIVER OF THE YEAR. PRESTIGIOUS INDUSTRY AWARD RECOGNIZES DRIVER FOR OUTSTANDING SAFETY PERFORMANCE PHOENIX , MAY 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FELIX MARTINEZ, A REPUBLIC SERVICES RESIDENTIAL COLLECTION DRIVER FROM MOUNT PROSPECT, ILL., WAS RECENTLY NAMED THE NATIONAL WASTE & RECYCLING ASSOCIATION'S (NWRA) 2023 NATIONAL RESIDENTIAL DRIVER OF THE YEAR, THE HIGHEST HONOR IN THE INDUSTRY.
