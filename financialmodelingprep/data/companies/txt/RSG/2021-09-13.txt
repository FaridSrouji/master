REPUBLIC SERVICES CERTIFIED AS GREAT PLACE TO WORK® FOR FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. PHOENIX, SEPT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG) HAS BEEN CERTIFIED AS A GREAT PLACE TO WORK FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, AND FOR THE FIRST TIME, THE COMPANY WAS NAMED ONE OF THE 2021 PEOPLE COMPANIES THAT CARE BY GREAT PLACE TO WORK AND PEOPLE MAGAZINE.
