REPUBLIC SERVICES CROWNS WINNERS OF THE 2023 ROAD-EO NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP. EIGHT CHAMPIONS REPRESENT THE TOP OF THE INDUSTRY IN SAFETY AND SKILL PHOENIX , FEB. 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG), A LEADER IN THE ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES INDUSTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE WINNERS OF THE 2023 ROAD-EO NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP, A COMPETITION TO DETERMINE THE BEST-OF-THE-BEST COLLECTION DRIVERS, HEAVY EQUIPMENT OPERATORS AND TECHNICIANS FROM ACROSS NORTH AMERICA.   TO QUALIFY FOR THE ROAD-EO NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP, REPUBLIC EMPLOYEES MUST HAVE A PERFECT SAFETY RECORD AND WIN LOCAL AND REGIONAL ROAD-EO COMPETITIONS.
