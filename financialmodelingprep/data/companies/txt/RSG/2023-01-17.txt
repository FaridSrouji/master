REPUBLIC SERVICES HOSTS MULTI-CITY DAY OF SERVICE AND CHARITABLE CAMPAIGN IN HONOR OF DR. MARTIN LUTHER KING JR. DAY. VOLUNTEER EVENTS AND DONATIONS IN CHICAGO, DETROIT AND MEMPHIS PHOENIX , JAN. 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO ENCOURAGE SERVICE AND SUPPORT ITS COMMUNITIES,  REPUBLIC SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: RSG) HOSTED A MULTI-CITY VOLUNTEERISM AND GIVING CAMPAIGN TO COINCIDE WITH MARTIN LUTHER KING JR. DAY. THE COMPANY ORGANIZED EMPLOYEE VOLUNTEER EVENTS IN CHICAGO, DETROIT AND MEMPHIS, THREE CITIES WHERE DR. KING SPOKE DURING THE CIVIL RIGHTS MOVEMENT, AND THE REPUBLIC SERVICES CHARITABLE FOUNDATION SUPPORTED THE EVENTS BY CONTRIBUTING $100,000 TO PARTNER ORGANIZATIONS.
