SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISSLAW LLP INVESTIGATES NEW SENIOR INVESTMENT GROUP INC.. NEW YORK, JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISSLAW LLP IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NEW SENIOR INVESTMENT GROUP INC. ("SNR" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: SNR) IN CONNECTION WITH THE COMPANY'S PROPOSED MERGER WITH VENTAS, INC. ("VENTAS") (NYSE: VTR). UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, SNR SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE 0.1561 SHARES OF VENTAS STOCK FOR EACH SNR SHARE THEY OWN, REPRESENTING IMPLIED PER-SHARE MERGER CONSIDERATION OF APPROXIMATELY $9.10 BASED UPON VENTAS'S JUNE 25, 2021 CLOSING PRICE OF $58.31.
