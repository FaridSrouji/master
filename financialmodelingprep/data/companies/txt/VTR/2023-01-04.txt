CADENCE AND ARDENT HEALTH SERVICES LAUNCH REMOTE CARE MANAGEMENT PROGRAM TO IMPROVE CARE AND OUTCOMES FOR PATIENTS OUTSIDE OF CLINICS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CADENCE TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP WITH ARDENT HEALTH SERVICES TO IMPLEMENT A REMOTE PATIENT MONITORING AND RESPONSIVE VIRTUAL CARE PROGRAM ACROSS ARDENT'S OPERATIONS, WHICH INCLUDE 30 HOSPITALS AND 200 SITES OF CARE IN SIX STATES. CADENCE DELIVERS PERSONALIZED, CONSISTENT CARE AND INTERVENTION AS NEEDED FROM HOME BY COLLECTING, ANALYZING, AND RESPONDING TO PATIENTS' VITALS DAILY AS AN EXTENSION OF THEIR CARE TEAM.
