ARDENT HEALTH SERVICES AND BIOINTELLISENSE LAUNCH STATE-OF-THE-ART CONTINUOUS INPATIENT MONITORING INITIATIVE TO ADVANCE CARE AND OPTIMIZE WORKFLOW. NASHVILLE, TENN. & DENVER--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ARDENT HEALTH SERVICES AND BIOINTELLISENSE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A NEW CONTINUOUS PATIENT MONITORING INITIATIVE TO EMPOWER CLINICIANS WITH GREATER DATA-DRIVEN INSIGHTS FOR PATIENT CARE AND TO OPTIMIZE CLINICAL AND OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW.
