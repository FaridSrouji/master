VENTAS ANNOUNCES PLANS TO TAKE OWNERSHIP OF FULL COLLATERAL POOL SUPPORTING ITS $486 MILLION SANTERRE MEZZANINE LOAN. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VENTAS, INC. (NYSE: VTR) (“VENTAS” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT INTENDS TO TAKE OWNERSHIP OF THE COLLATERAL THAT SUPPORTS ITS APPROXIMATELY $486 MILLION CASH-PAY MEZZANINE LOAN TO SANTERRE HEALTH INVESTORS (THE “SANTERRE MEZZANINE LOAN”).
