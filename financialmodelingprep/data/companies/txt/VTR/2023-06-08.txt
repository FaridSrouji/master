VENTAS REALTY, LIMITED PARTNERSHIP ANNOUNCES UPSIZE AND PRICING OF $750 MILLION OF EXCHANGEABLE SENIOR NOTES. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VENTAS REALTY, LIMITED PARTNERSHIP (“VENTAS REALTY”), A WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF VENTAS, INC. (“VENTAS”) (NYSE: VTR), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF $750 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 3.75% EXCHANGEABLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2026 (THE “NOTES”) IN A PRIVATE PLACEMENT (THE “OFFERING”) TO PERSONS REASONABLY BELIEVED TO BE QUALIFIED INSTITUTIONAL BUYERS PURSUANT TO RULE 144A UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED (THE “SECURITIES ACT”). THE OFFERING WAS UPSI.
