VENTAS COMMITS TO ACHIEVE CARBON NEUTRAL OPERATIONS BY 2040. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VENTAS, INC. (NYSE: VTR) (“VENTAS” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS COMMITMENT TO ACHIEVE NET-ZERO OPERATIONAL CARBON EMISSIONS BY 2040, BUILDING UPON ITS LONGSTANDING ACTIONS AND COMMITMENT TO ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP. THIS NEW PLEDGE EXCEEDS THE COMPANY'S EXISTING SCIENCE-BASED TARGET INITIATIVE (SBTI) VERIFIED CARBON REDUCTION GOAL, ADOPTED IN 2021, TO DECREASE ABSOLUTE CARBON EMISSIONS BY 30% BY 2030.
