VENTAS PRICES CDN$600 MILLION OF 5.398% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VENTAS, INC. (NYSE: VTR) (“VENTAS” OR THE “COMPANY”) SAID TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED A PRIVATE OFFERING IN CANADA OF CDN$600 MILLION OF 5.398% SENIOR NOTES, SERIES I DUE 2028 (THE “NOTES”). THE SALE OF THE NOTES IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON APRIL 21, 2023, SUBJECT TO SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
