VENTAS EARNS ENERGY STAR® PARTNER OF THE YEAR SUSTAINED EXCELLENCE IN ENERGY MANAGEMENT AWARD. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VENTAS, INC. (NYSE: VTR) (“VENTAS” OR THE “COMPANY”), IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE IT HAS RECEIVED THE 2023 ENERGY STAR PARTNER OF THE YEAR SUSTAINED EXCELLENCE IN ENERGY MANAGEMENT AWARD FROM THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (EPA) AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY. THIS DISTINGUISHED RECOGNITION IS ENERGY STAR'S HIGHEST HONOR AND REFLECTS VENTAS'S THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR EARNING THE PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARD.
