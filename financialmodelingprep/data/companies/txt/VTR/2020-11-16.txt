VENTAS TO PARTICIPATE IN NAREIT REITWORLD 2020 CONFERENCE. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VENTAS, INC. (NYSE: VTR) (“VENTAS” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MANAGEMENT WILL MAKE A PRESENTATION REGARDING THE COMPANY AT THE VIRTUAL NAREIT REITWORLD 2020 CONFERENCE (THE “NAREIT CONFERENCE”) ON NOVEMBER 17, 2020 AT 9:30 A.M. EASTERN TIME. ANY COMPANY WRITTEN MATERIALS ACCOMPANYING THE PRESENTATION AT THE NAREIT CONFERENCE WILL BE AVAILABLE ON THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE STARTING AT 8 A.M. EASTERN TIME ON NOVEMBER 17. THESE MATERIALS WILL BE ARCHIVED AT WWW.VEN
