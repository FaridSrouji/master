VENTAS ANNOUNCES PRICING OF SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VENTAS, INC. (NYSE: VTR) (“VENTAS” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.500% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE “NOTES”) AT 99.744% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT. THE NOTES ARE BEING ISSUED BY VENTAS REALTY, LIMITED PARTNERSHIP (“VENTAS REALTY”), A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF THE COMPANY, AND WILL BE GUARANTEED, ON A SENIOR UNSECURED BASIS, BY THE COMPANY.
