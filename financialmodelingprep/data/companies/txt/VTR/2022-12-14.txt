HEALTHTRUST AND ARDENT HEALTH SERVICES RENEW LONG-STANDING GPO AGREEMENT. NASHVILLE, TENN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--HEALTHTRUST PERFORMANCE GROUP, A COMPREHENSIVE HEALTHCARE IMPROVEMENT ORGANIZATION, AND ARDENT HEALTH SERVICES, ONE OF THE LARGEST PRIVATE INTEGRATED HEALTH SYSTEMS IN THE U.S., TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RENEWAL OF THEIR EXCLUSIVE, DECADES-LONG RELATIONSHIP FOR SUPPLY CHAIN RESILIENCY AND GROUP PURCHASING. UNDER A NEW TEN-YEAR AGREEMENT, ARDENT PROVIDERS ARE ASSURED OF ADVANTAGES IN PRICING AND CONTRACTING ACROSS A BROAD PORTFOLIO OF HEALTHCARE SUPPLIES AND SERVICES.
