BIOGEN SUBMITS FINAL PROTOCOL FOR ADUHELM® (ADUCANUMAB-AVWA) PHASE 4 ENVISION TRIAL TO FDA. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., MARCH 30, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC.  (NASDAQ: BIIB) HAS SUBMITTED THE FINAL STUDY PROTOCOL FOR THE CONFIRMATORY PHASE 4 ENVISION TRIAL TO THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR REVIEW AND APPROVAL. THIS IS IN LINE WITH THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO ACCELERATE THE TIMELINES FOR THE TRIAL, INCLUDING SUBMITTING THE FINAL STUDY PROTOCOL TO THE FDA IN MARCH 2022, AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED. BIOGEN EXPECTS THE FIRST PATIENT TO ENTER SCREENING IN MAY 2022 AND THE TRIAL'S PRIMARY COMPLETION APPROXIMATELY FOUR YEARS AFTER THE STUDY BEGINS.
