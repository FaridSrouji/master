BIOGEN APPOINTS CHUCK TRIANO AS HEAD OF INVESTOR RELATIONS. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., MARCH 14, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CHUCK TRIANO AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, HEAD OF INVESTOR RELATIONS EFFECTIVE 10 APRIL 2023. MR. TRIANO WILL REPORT TO MICHAEL MCDONNELL, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.
