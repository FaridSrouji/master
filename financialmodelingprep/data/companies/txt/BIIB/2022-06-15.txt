NEW DATA PRESENTED AT CURE SMA REVEAL RESIDUAL UNMET NEEDS IN YOUNG SMA PATIENTS TREATED WITH GENE THERAPY AND SUGGEST FURTHER POTENTIAL OF USING SPINRAZA® (NUSINERSEN). CAMBRIDGE, MASS., JUNE 15, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) WILL PRESENT NEW DATA FROM CLINICAL STUDIES AIMED AT ASSESSING REMAINING UNMET NEEDS FOR PEOPLE LIVING WITH SPINAL MUSCULAR ATROPHY (SMA) AND EVALUATING THE POTENTIAL IMPACT OF SPINRAZA® (NUSINERSEN) IN DIFFERENT PATIENT POPULATIONS AT THE SMA RESEARCH & CLINICAL CARE MEETING HOSTED BY CURE SMA THIS WEEK IN ANAHEIM, CALIF. BIOGEN IS A PRESENTING SPONSOR OF CURE SMA'S 2022 ANNUAL SMA CONFERENCE, THE WORLD'S LARGEST MEETING DEDICATED TO SMA RESEARCH AND CARE.
