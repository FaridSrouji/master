BIIB LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES BIOGEN INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK, FEB. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN BIOGEN INC. ("BIOGEN" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: BIIB) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF BIOGEN INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN JUNE 7, 2021 AND JANUARY 11, 2022.
