BIOGEN ADVANCES LEADING RESEARCH IN SPINAL MUSCULAR ATROPHY (SMA) WITH NEW DATA AT AAN 2021 EXPLORING OPPORTUNITIES TO IMPROVE OUTCOMES FOR PATIENTS. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., APRIL 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED NEW DATA FROM THE SPINRAZA® (NUSINERSEN) CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM AIMED AT OPTIMIZING OUTCOMES FOR PEOPLE WITH SPINAL MUSCULAR ATROPHY (SMA) AND ADVANCING UNDERSTANDING OF THE DISEASE. THESE DATA ARE BEING PRESENTED AT THE AMERICAN ACADEMY OF NEUROLOGY (AAN) 2021 VIRTUAL ANNUAL MEETING, APRIL 17-22.
