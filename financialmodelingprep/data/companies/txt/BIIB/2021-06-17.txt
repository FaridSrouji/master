BIOGEN ANNOUNCES FIRST PATIENT DOSED IN PHASE 3 SYSTEMIC LUPUS ERYTHEMATOSUS STUDY. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., JUNE 17, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE FIRST PATIENT HAS BEEN DOSED IN THE GLOBAL CLINICAL STUDY, TOPAZ-1. THE PHASE 3 STUDY WILL EVALUATE THE CLINICAL EFFICACY AND ASSESS THE SAFETY OF BIIB059, A FIRST IN-CLASS, HUMANIZED IGG1 MONOCLONAL ANTIBODY (MAB) TARGETING BLOOD DENDRITIC CELL ANTIGEN 2 (BDCA2), AS COMPARED TO PLACEBO, IN PARTICIPANTS WITH ACTIVE SYSTEMIC LUPUS ERYTHEMATOSUS (SLE). TOPAZ-1 IS EXPECTED TO BE CONDUCTED AT APPROXIMATELY 135 SITES WORLDWIDE AND AIMS TO ENROLL 540 ADULTS WITH ACTIVE SLE.
