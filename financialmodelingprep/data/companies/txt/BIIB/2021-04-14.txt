BIOGEN TO PRESENT TRIAL DESIGN OF ADUCANUMAB EMBARK RE-DOSING STUDY IN POSTER SESSION AT 2021 VIRTUAL AAN ANNUAL MEETING ON APRIL 17-22, 2021. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., APRIL 14, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL HOST A POSTER PRESENTATION ON THE DESIGN OF THE EMBARK TRIAL OF ITS ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE INVESTIGATIONAL THERAPY, ADUCANUMAB, AT THE UPCOMING 2021 VIRTUAL AMERICAN ACADEMY OF NEUROLOGY (AAN) ANNUAL MEETING.
