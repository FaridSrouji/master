BIOGEN R&D DAY TO PROVIDE OVERVIEW OF DIVERSIFIED PIPELINE AND CAPABILITIES WITH POTENTIAL FOR MULTIPLE NOVEL THERAPIES IN NEUROSCIENCE. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., SEPT. 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) WILL HOLD A VIRTUAL INVESTOR R&D DAY TODAY, PROVIDING A COMPREHENSIVE OVERVIEW OF THE COMPANY'S DIVERSIFIED PIPELINE IN NEUROSCIENCE. BIOGEN PLANS TO ADVANCE INNOVATIVE THERAPIES FOR PATIENTS THROUGH THE WORK OF ITS WORLD-CLASS RESEARCHERS AND THE STRENGTH OF ITS GLOBAL NETWORK OF COLLABORATORS. AS PART OF R&D DAY, BIOGEN'S SCIENTIFIC LEADERS WILL DISCUSS ONGOING RESEARCH AND HIGHLIGHT KEY ADVANCEMENTS AMONG BIOGEN'S 33 CLINICAL PROGRAMS, INCLUDING 12 PROGRAMS IN PHASE 3 OR FILED.
