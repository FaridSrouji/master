BIIB ALERT: THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF APRIL 8, 2022 IN THE CLASS ACTION FILED ON BEHALF OF BIOGEN INC. SHAREHOLDERS. NEW YORK, FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION COMPLAINT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) ALLEGING THAT THE COMPANY VIOLATED FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS. CLASS PERIOD: JUNE 7, 2021 TO JANUARY 11, 2022 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE: APRIL 8, 2022 NO OBLIGATION OR COST TO YOU.
