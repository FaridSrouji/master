BIOGEN EXERCISES OPTION TO PARTICIPATE IN THE DEVELOPMENT AND COMMERCIALIZATION OF A LATE-STAGE BISPECIFIC ANTIBODY. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., FEB. 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT EXERCISED ITS OPTION TO PARTICIPATE IN THE DEVELOPMENT AND COMMERCIALIZATION OF MOSUNETUZUMAB. BIOGEN WILL PAY A $30 MILLION ONE-TIME OPTION FEE TO GENENTECH, A MEMBER OF THE ROCHE GROUP, AS PART OF THE COMPANIES' LONG-STANDING COLLABORATION ON ANTIBODIES TARGETING CD20.
