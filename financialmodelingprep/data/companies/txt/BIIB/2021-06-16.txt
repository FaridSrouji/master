BIOGEN ANNOUNCES TOPLINE RESULTS FROM PHASE 2 STUDY OF GOSURANEMAB, AN ANTI-TAU ANTIBODY, FOR ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., JUNE 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED TOPLINE RESULTS FROM ITS PHASE 2 TANGO STUDY OF GOSURANEMAB (BIIB092), AN INVESTIGATIONAL ANTI-TAU ANTIBODY THAT WAS BEING EVALUATED AS A POTENTIAL TREATMENT FOR ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE.
