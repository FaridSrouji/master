BIOGEN APPOINTS ADAM KEENEY AS HEAD OF CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., APRIL 04, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ADAM KEENEY, PH.D., AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, HEAD OF CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT EFFECTIVE 17 APRIL 2023. DR. KEENEY WILL BE A MEMBER OF BIOGEN'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE REPORTING TO CHRISTOPHER A. VIEHBACHER, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
