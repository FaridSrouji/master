BIOGEN AND CAPSIGEN ANNOUNCE COLLABORATION TO DISCOVER AND DEVELOP NOVEL AAV CAPSIDS FOR TARGETED CNS AND NEUROMUSCULAR DISORDERS. CAMBRIDGE, MASS. AND VANCOUVER, WASH., MAY 10, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) AND CAPSIGEN INC. ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THEY HAVE ENTERED INTO A STRATEGIC RESEARCH COLLABORATION TO ENGINEER NOVEL ADENO-ASSOCIATED VIRUS (AAV) CAPSIDS THAT HAVE THE POTENTIAL TO DELIVER TRANSFORMATIVE GENE THERAPIES THAT ADDRESS THE UNDERLYING GENETIC CAUSES OF VARIOUS CNS AND NEUROMUSCULAR DISORDERS.
