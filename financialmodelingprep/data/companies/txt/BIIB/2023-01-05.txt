BIOGEN NAMES PRIYA SINGHAL AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, HEAD OF DEVELOPMENT. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., JAN. 05, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) ANNOUNCED THAT PRIYA SINGHAL, M.D., M.P.H., CURRENTLY HEAD OF GLOBAL SAFETY AND REGULATORY SCIENCES AND INTERIM HEAD OF RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT (R&D), HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, HEAD OF DEVELOPMENT, FOLLOWING A DECISION TO SEPARATE RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT INTO TWO DISTINCT FUNCTIONS THAT WILL BOTH REPORT DIRECTLY TO THE CEO.
