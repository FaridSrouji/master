LIFE SCIENCE CARES AND BIOGEN FOUNDATION HELP LAUNCH FOOD FOR FREE'S JUST EATS PROGRAM. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., FEB. 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LIFE SCIENCE CARES BOSTON AND THE BIOGEN FOUNDATION HAVE TEAMED UP AS INAUGURAL SPONSORS OF FOOD FOR FREE'S NEW JUST EATS GROCERY BOX PROGRAM, A $170,000 EFFORT TO REDUCE FOOD INSECURITY FOR 3,000 AREA FAMILIES, STARTING THIS MONTH. TO LAUNCH JUST EATS, LIFE SCIENCE CARES, THE LIFE SCIENCE INDUSTRY PLATFORM FOR GIVING BACK, HAS COMMITTED $50,000, AND THE BIOGEN FOUNDATION, THE PHILANTHROPIC ARM OF CAMBRIDGE-BASED NEUROSCIENCE PIONEER BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB), HAS COMMITTED $100,000, MATCHING THE $50,000 FROM LIFE SCIENCE CARES AND DONATING AN ADDITIONAL $50,000 TO COMBATTING FOOD INSECURITY FOR CHILDREN IN GREATER BOSTON. COMMITTING AN ADDITIONAL $20,000 TOWARD THE PROGRAM IS ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC., THE LARGEST AND PREEMINENT OWNER, OPERATOR, AND DEVELOPER OF COLLABORATIVE LIFE SCIENCE CAMPUSES IN GREATER BOSTON.
