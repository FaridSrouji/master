SHAREHOLDER ALERT: BIOGEN INC. (BIIB) OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS UNDER INVESTIGATION FOR POSSIBLE FALSE STATEMENTS ABOUT ADUHELM. SAN FRANCISCO , OCT. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SCHUBERT JONCKHEER & KOLBE LLP IS INVESTIGATING POTENTIAL DERIVATIVE CLAIMS ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) RELATING TO POSSIBLE FALSE AND MISLEADING STATEMENTS TO INVESTORS ABOUT ITS ALZHEIMER'S DRUG ADUHELM AND AN ALLEGED ILLEGAL KICKBACK SCHEME.  BIOGEN AND THREE OF ITS EXECUTIVE OFFICERS ARE CURRENTLY SUBJECT TO A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT IN THE U.S. DISTRICT COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF MASSACHUSETTS ALLEGING CERTAIN FALSE AND MISLEADING STATEMENTS CONCERNING THE COMMERCIAL PROSPECTS FOR THE COMPANY'S ALZHEIMER'S DRUG, ADUHELM.
