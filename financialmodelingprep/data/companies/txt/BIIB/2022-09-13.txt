SHAREHOLDER ALERT: BIOGEN INC. (BIIB) OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS UNDER INVESTIGATION FOR POSSIBLE FALSE STATEMENTS ABOUT ADUHELM. SAN FRANCISCO , SEPT. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SCHUBERT JONCKHEER & KOLBE LLP IS INVESTIGATING POTENTIAL DERIVATIVE CLAIMS ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) RELATING TO POSSIBLE FALSE AND MISLEADING STATEMENTS TO INVESTORS ABOUT ITS ALZHEIMER'S DRUG ADUHELM AND AN ALLEGED ILLEGAL KICKBACK SCHEME.
