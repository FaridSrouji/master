THE LAWSUIT WAS FILED IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF MASSACHUSETTS AND ALLEGES VIOLATIONS OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934.
