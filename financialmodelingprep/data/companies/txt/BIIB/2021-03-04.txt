BIOGEN ANNOUNCES PLANS TO BUILD A NEW, STATE-OF-THE-ART GENE THERAPY MANUFACTURING FACILITY IN RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK, NORTH CAROLINA. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., MARCH 04, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS PLANS TO BUILD A NEW GENE THERAPY MANUFACTURING FACILITY AT ITS RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK (RTP) MANUFACTURING CAMPUSES IN NORTH CAROLINA TO SUPPORT ITS GROWING GENE THERAPY PIPELINE ACROSS MULTIPLE THERAPEUTIC AREAS.
