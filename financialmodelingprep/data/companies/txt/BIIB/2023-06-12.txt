BIOGEN ANNOUNCES CHANGES TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., JUNE 12, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) ANNOUNCED SEVERAL CHANGES TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY. THE CHANGES WILL REFRESH THE COMPOSITION OF THE BOARD AND FOLLOW THE BOARD'S CONTINUAL INTERACTION WITH COMPANY STOCKHOLDERS TO SOLICIT THEIR PERSPECTIVE ON A RANGE OF ISSUES AROUND THE COMPANY'S PERFORMANCE AND STRATEGY.
