CATERPILLAR ANNOUNCES INVESTMENT IN LITHOS ENERGY INC. TO FURTHER BATTERY PACK DEVELOPMENT AND MANUFACTURING. DEAL WILL HELP TO ACCELERATE THE DEVELOPMENT OF CATERPILLAR'S ELECTRIFIED PRODUCT PORTFOLIO IRVING, TEXAS , JAN. 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE:CAT) ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY IS INVESTING IN LITHOS ENERGY, INC., A U.S.-BASED BATTERY TECHNOLOGY COMPANY THAT PRODUCES LITHIUM-ION BATTERY PACKS. "CATERPILLAR'S COLLABORATION WITH LITHOS SUPPORTS OUR COMMITMENT TO DELIVERING ROBUST ELECTRIFIED PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS FOR OUR CUSTOMERS," SAID JOE CREED, GROUP PRESIDENT OF CATERPILLAR'S ENERGY & TRANSPORTATION SEGMENT.
