CATERPILLAR TO RELOCATE GLOBAL HEADQUARTERS TO DALLAS-FORT WORTH AREA. DEERFIELD, ILL. , JUNE 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL MOVE ITS GLOBAL HEADQUARTERS TO THE COMPANY'S EXISTING OFFICE IN IRVING, TEXAS, FROM ITS CURRENT LOCATION IN DEERFIELD, ILLINOIS.
