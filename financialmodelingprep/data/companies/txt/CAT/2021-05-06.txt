CATERPILLAR AND THE CATERPILLAR FOUNDATION DONATE ADDITIONAL $3.4 MILLION IN COVID-19 RELIEF. DEERFIELD, ILL., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) AND THE CATERPILLAR FOUNDATION, THE COMPANY'S PHILANTHROPIC ARM, ANNOUNCED TODAY A SERIES OF TARGETED RELIEF EFFORTS TO SUPPORT COUNTRIES STRUGGLING WITH A SIGNIFICANT RISE OF COVID-19 CASES.
