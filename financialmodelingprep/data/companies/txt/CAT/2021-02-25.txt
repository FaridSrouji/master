GENERAL MOTORS EXECUTIVE TO JOIN CATERPILLAR'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. DEERFIELD, ILL., FEB. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT GERALD JOHNSON HAS BEEN ELECTED TO THE CATERPILLAR BOARD OF DIRECTORS EFFECTIVE MARCH 1, 2021. JOHNSON, 58, IS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF GLOBAL MANUFACTURING AT GENERAL MOTORS (GM) COMPANY,...
