CATERPILLAR INC. MAINTAINS DIVIDEND. DEERFIELD, ILL., APRIL 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) VOTED TODAY TO MAINTAIN THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND THREE CENTS ($1.03) PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MAY 20, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 26, 2021.
