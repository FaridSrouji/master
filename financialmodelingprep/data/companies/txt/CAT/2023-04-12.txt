CATERPILLAR INC. MAINTAINS DIVIDEND. IRVING, TEXAS , APRIL 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) VOTED TODAY TO MAINTAIN THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND TWENTY CENTS ($1.20) PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE MAY 19, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 24, 2023.
