CATERPILLAR INC. MAINTAINS DIVIDEND. DEERFIELD, ILL., OCT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) VOTED TODAY TO MAINTAIN THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND ELEVEN CENTS ($1.11) PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE NOVEMBER 19, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON OCTOBER 25, 2021.
