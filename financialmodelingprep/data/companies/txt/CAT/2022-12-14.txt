CATERPILLAR INC. MAINTAINS DIVIDEND. IRVING, TEXAS , DEC. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) VOTED TODAY TO MAINTAIN THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND TWENTY CENTS ($1.20) PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE FEBRUARY 17, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 20, 2023. CATERPILLAR HAS PAID A CASH DIVIDEND EVERY YEAR SINCE THE COMPANY WAS FORMED AND HAS PAID A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND SINCE 1933.
