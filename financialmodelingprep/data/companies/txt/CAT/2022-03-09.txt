CATERPILLAR FOUNDATION SUPPORTS URGENT HUMANITARIAN NEEDS FOR THE UKRAINIAN PEOPLE. DEERFIELD, ILL., MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CATERPILLAR FOUNDATION, THE PHILANTHROPIC ORGANIZATION OF CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT), IS DONATING MORE THAN $1 MILLION TO SUPPORT BOTH URGENT AND LONG-TERM NEEDS OF THE UKRAINE HUMANITARIAN CRISIS.
