CATERPILLAR NAMES FIRST CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY & STRATEGY OFFICER AND WILL ENHANCE CLIMATE-RELATED DISCLOSURES. DEERFIELD, ILL., OCT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) IS CREATING THE POSITION OF CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY & STRATEGY OFFICER, ENHANCING ITS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG)-RELATED DISCLOSURES AND TYING A PORTION OF EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION TO ESG.
