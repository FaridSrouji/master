CATERPILLAR TO LAUNCH DEMONSTRATION PROJECT USING HYDROGEN-FUELED COMBINED HEAT AND POWER SYSTEM. DEERFIELD, ILL. , MAY 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) TODAY ANNOUNCED A THREE-YEAR PROJECT WITH MINNESOTA-BASED DISTRICT ENERGY ST. PAUL TO DEMONSTRATE A HYDROGEN-FUELED COMBINED HEAT AND POWER (CHP) SYSTEM.
