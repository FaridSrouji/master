CATERPILLAR INC. MAINTAINS DIVIDEND. IRVING, TEXAS , OCT. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) VOTED TODAY TO MAINTAIN THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND TWENTY CENTS ($1.20) PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE NOVEMBER 18, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON OCTOBER 24, 2022. CATERPILLAR HAS PAID A CASH DIVIDEND EVERY YEAR SINCE THE COMPANY WAS FORMED AND HAS PAID A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND SINCE 1933.
