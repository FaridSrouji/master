CATERPILLAR SUCCESSFULLY DEMONSTRATES FIRST BATTERY ELECTRIC LARGE MINING TRUCK AND INVESTS IN SUSTAINABLE PROVING GROUND. IRVING, TEXAS , NOV. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) ANNOUNCED TODAY A SUCCESSFUL DEMONSTRATION OF ITS FIRST BATTERY ELECTRIC 793 LARGE MINING TRUCK AND A SIGNIFICANT INVESTMENT TO TRANSFORM ITS ARIZONA-BASED PROVING GROUND INTO A SUSTAINABLE TESTING AND VALIDATION HUB OF THE FUTURE.   CATERPILLAR COMPLETED DEVELOPMENT OF ITS FIRST BATTERY ELECTRIC 793 PROTOTYPE WITH SUPPORT FROM KEY MINING CUSTOMERS PARTICIPATING IN CATERPILLAR'S EARLY LEARNER PROGRAM.
