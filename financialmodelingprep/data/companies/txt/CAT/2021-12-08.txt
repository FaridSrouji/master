CATERPILLAR INC. MAINTAINS DIVIDEND. DEERFIELD, ILL., DEC. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) VOTED TODAY TO MAINTAIN THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND ELEVEN CENTS ($1.11) PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE FEBRUARY 18, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 20, 2022.
