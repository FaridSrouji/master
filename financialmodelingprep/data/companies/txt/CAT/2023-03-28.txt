FREYR BATTERY TARGETS STRATEGIC COALITION WITH FOUR MAJOR GLOBAL PARTNERS. NEW YORK & OSLO, NORWAY & LUXEMBOURG--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FREYR BATTERY (NYSE: FREY) (“FREYR”), A DEVELOPER OF CLEAN, NEXT-GENERATION BATTERY CELL PRODUCTION CAPACITY, HAS ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS ENTERED INTO DISCUSSIONS ON A POTENTIAL STRATEGIC COALITION WITH GLENCORE PLC (LN: GLEN), CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT), SIEMENS AG (GY: SIE), AND NIDEC CORPORATION (TSE: 6594) TO PURSUE THE SCALE UP OF SUSTAINABLE BATTERY SOLUTIONS ACROSS EUROPE, NORTH AMERICA, AND BEYOND.
