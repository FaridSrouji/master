PARKER TO ANNOUNCE FISCAL 2021 FIRST QUARTER EARNINGS ON NOVEMBER 5TH; CONFERENCE CALL AND WEBCAST SCHEDULED FOR 11 A.M. EASTERN. CLEVELAND, OCT. 26, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL RELEASE ITS FISCAL 2021 FIRST QUARTER EARNINGS BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS ON THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 5, 2020, FOLLOWED BY A CONFERENCE CALL AT 11:00 A.M., EASTERN TIME.  DURING THE CALL, THE COMPANY WILL DISCUSS FISCAL 2021 FIRST QUARTER RESULTS AND RESPOND TO QUESTIONS FROM INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS AND SECURITY ANALYSTS.  THE CONFERENCE CALL WILL BE WEBCAST SIMULTANEOUSLY ON PARKER'S INVESTOR WEBSITE AT WWW.PHSTOCK.COM WITH AN ACCOMPANYING SLIDE PRESENTATION. THE WEBCAST WILL BE ARCHIVED ON THE SITE FOR ONE YEAR AND AVAILABLE FOR REPLAY LATER THAT DAY.
