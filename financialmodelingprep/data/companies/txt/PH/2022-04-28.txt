PARKER INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CLEVELAND, APRIL 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.33 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 13, 2022. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 3, 2022. THE DIVIDEND REPRESENTS A 29% INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.03 PER COMMON SHARE AND IS THE COMPANY'S 288TH CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
