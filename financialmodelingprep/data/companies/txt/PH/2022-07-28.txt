PARKER ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF ANDREW M. WEEKS, VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT - ENGINEERED MATERIALS GROUP. CLEVELAND, JULY 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE:PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ANDREW M. WEEKS, VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT – ENGINEERED MATERIALS GROUP, WILL RETIRE EFFECTIVE AUGUST 31, 2022.
