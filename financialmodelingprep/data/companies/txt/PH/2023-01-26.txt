PARKER DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CLEVELAND, JAN. 26, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.33 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEBRUARY 10, 2023. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 3, 2023.
