PARKER INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CLEVELAND, APRIL 27, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.48 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 12, 2023. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 2, 2023. THE DIVIDEND REPRESENTS AN 11% INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.33 PER COMMON SHARE AND IS THE COMPANY'S 292NDCONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
