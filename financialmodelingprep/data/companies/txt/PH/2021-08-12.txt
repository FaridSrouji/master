PARKER DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CLEVELAND, AUG. 12, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.03 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUGUST 27, 2021. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE SEPTEMBER 10, 2021.
