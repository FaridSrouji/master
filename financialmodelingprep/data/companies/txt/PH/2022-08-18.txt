PARKER DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CLEVELAND, AUG. 18, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.33 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUGUST 29, 2022. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE SEPTEMBER 9, 2022.
