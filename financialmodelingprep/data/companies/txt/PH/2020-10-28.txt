PARKER DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CLEVELAND, OCT. 28, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 88 CENTS PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF NOVEMBER 6, 2020.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DECEMBER 4, 2020.
