PARKER-HANNIFIN CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 6, 2019. PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 88 CENTS PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF NOVEMBER 8, 2019. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DECEMBER 6, 2019.
