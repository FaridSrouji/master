PARKER ANNOUNCES RECOMMENDED ALL CASH ACQUISITION OF MEGGITT PLC. CLEVELAND, AUG. 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS REACHED AN AGREEMENT ON THE TERMS OF A RECOMMENDED CASH ACQUISITION OF THE ENTIRE ISSUED AND TO BE ISSUED ORDINARY SHARE CAPITAL OF MEGGITT (THE “ACQUISITION”), A GLOBAL LEADER IN AEROSPACE AND DEFENSE MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES.
