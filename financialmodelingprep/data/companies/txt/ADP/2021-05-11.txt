ADP ANNOUNCES PRICING OF ITS SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028. ROSELAND, N.J., MAY 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ADP (NASDAQ: ADP), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM) SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $1.0 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 1.70% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028.
