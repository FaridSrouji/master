ADP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER FISCAL 2022 RESULTS. ROSELAND, N.J. , APRIL 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ADP (NASDAQ: ADP), A LEADING GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY PROVIDING HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM) SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS THIRD QUARTER FISCAL 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS ALONG WITH ITS REVISED FISCAL 2022 OUTLOOK THROUGH AN EARNINGS RELEASE AVAILABLE ON THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE AT INVESTORS.ADP.COM/EVENTS-AND-PRESENTATIONS.
