ADP RESEARCH INSTITUTE® GLOBAL STUDY UNCOVERS A RENEWED DESIRE FOR CAREER PROGRESSION WITHIN AN EVOLVING WORKPLACE ENVIRONMENT IN "PEOPLE AT WORK 2023: A GLOBAL WORKFORCE VIEW". WORKERS' EXPECTATIONS FOR CAREER DEVELOPMENT AND TRAINING OPPORTUNITIES ARE ON THE RISE, ALONG WITH EXPECTATIONS FOR PAY RAISES, FLEXIBILITY AND A SUPPORTIVE WORKPLACE CULTURE. OVERALL, WORKER OPTIMISM ABOUT THE NEXT FIVE YEARS IN THE WORKPLACE IS STRONG.
