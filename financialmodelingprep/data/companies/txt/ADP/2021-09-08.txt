ADP NAMED A LEADER IN IDC MARKETSCAPES FOR WORLDWIDE INTEGRATED TALENT MANAGEMENT, LEARNING, PERFORMANCE AND COMPENSATION. ROSELAND, N.J., SEPT. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ADP HAS BEEN NAMED AS A LEADER IN THE IDC MARKETSCAPES FOR WORLDWIDE INTEGRATED TALENT MANAGEMENT, WORLDWIDE LEARNING MANAGEMENT, WORLDWIDE PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT AND WORLDWIDE COMPENSATION MANAGEMENT 2021 VENDOR ASSESSMENTS*.
