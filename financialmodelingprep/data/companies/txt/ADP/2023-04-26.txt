ADP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER FISCAL 2023 RESULTS. ROSELAND, N.J. , APRIL 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ADP (NASDAQ: ADP), A LEADING GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY PROVIDING HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM) SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS THIRD QUARTER FISCAL 2023 FINANCIAL RESULTS ALONG WITH ITS REVISED FISCAL 2023 OUTLOOK THROUGH AN EARNINGS RELEASE AVAILABLE ON THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE AT INVESTORS.ADP.COM/EVENTS-AND-PRESENTATIONS.
