ADP REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER FISCAL 2022 RESULTS. ROSELAND, N.J. , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ADP (NASDAQ: ADP), A LEADING GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY PROVIDING HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM) SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FOURTH QUARTER FISCAL 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS ALONG WITH ITS FISCAL 2023 OUTLOOK THROUGH AN EARNINGS RELEASE AVAILABLE ON THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE AT INVESTORS.ADP.COM/EVENTS-AND-PRESENTATIONS.
