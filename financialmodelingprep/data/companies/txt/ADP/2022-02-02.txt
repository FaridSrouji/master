ADP NAMED ONE OF THE "WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES" FOR 16TH STRAIGHT YEAR. ROSELAND, N.J., FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ADP, A LEADING GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY PROVIDING HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM) SOLUTIONS, HAS BEEN NAMED BY FORTUNE® MAGAZINE AS ONE OF THE "WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES" IN 2022.
