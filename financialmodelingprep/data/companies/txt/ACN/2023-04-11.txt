THROUGH NEW STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP, ACCENTURE AND CONGA COLLABORATE TO HELP COMPANIES TRANSFORM REVENUE LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ACCENTURE (NYSE: ACN) AND CONGA, A GLOBAL LEADER IN REVENUE LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS, HAVE FORMED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO HELP CLIENTS TRANSFORM THEIR REVENUE LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT PROCESSES—FROM PRICE QUOTES AND CONTRACTS TO FULFILLMENT AND RENEWAL SERVICES. TOGETHER, THE COMPANIES ARE HELPING ORGANIZATIONS AROUND THE WORLD STREAMLINE AND AUTOMATE THEIR REVENUE PROCESSES TO DRIVE EFFICIENCIES AND SUPPORT BUSINESS GROWTH.
