ACCENTURE EXPANDS STRATEGY CAPABILITIES IN LIFE SCIENCES WITH THE ACQUISITION OF BIONEST. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ACCENTURE (NYSE: ACN) HAS ACQUIRED BIONEST, A STRATEGY AND CONSULTING FIRM DEDICATED TO ADDRESSING COMPLEX STRATEGIC DECISIONS FOR LEADING BIOPHARMA ORGANIZATIONS ACROSS INNOVATIVE AREAS OF SCIENCE INCLUDING PRECISION MEDICINE AND DIAGNOSTICS, ONCOLOGY, CELL & GENE THERAPY (CGT), AND RARE DISEASES.
