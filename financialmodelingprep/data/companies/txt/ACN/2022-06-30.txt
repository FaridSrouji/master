UMLAUT LAUNCHES "5G CAMPUS LAB," AN OPEN RAN PRIVATE NETWORK TO ACCELERATE THE DEVELOPMENT OF CONNECTIVITY SOLUTIONS FOR CLIENTS. AACHEN, GERMANY , JUNE 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UMLAUT, PART OF ACCENTURE (NYSE: ACN), HAS OPENED A PRIVATE 5G STANDALONE, OPEN RAN NETWORK IN AACHEN, GERMANY. THE "5G CAMPUS LAB" ENABLES COMPANIES ACROSS INDUSTRIES TO DESIGN, TEST AND IMPLEMENT ULTRA-HIGH-SPEED AND LOW-LATENCY CONNECTIVITY SOLUTIONS FASTER WITHOUT HAVING TO INVEST IN BUILDING THEIR OWN NETWORK.
