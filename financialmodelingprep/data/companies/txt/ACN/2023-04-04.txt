VIRTUAL TECHNOLOGIES GROUP NAMES IT VETERAN DEAN MARSH AS CEO. MAUMEE, OHIO , APRIL 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VIRTUAL TECHNOLOGIES GROUP (VTG), A LEADING IT, SECURITY, AND CLOUD SERVICES FIRM PROVIDING CUSTOMIZED SOLUTIONS WITH VERTICAL FOCUS IN EDUCATION, NON-PROFIT, MANUFACTURING, AND HEALTHCARE HAS ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS NAMED DEAN J. MARSH AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
