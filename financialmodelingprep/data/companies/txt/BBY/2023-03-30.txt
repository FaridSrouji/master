BEST BUY APPOINTS SIMA SISTANI AND MELINDA WHITTINGTON TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BEST BUY CO., INC. (NYSE:BBY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SIMA SISTANI, A SEASONED MEDIA AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, AND MELINDA WHITTINGTON, A LEADING EXECUTIVE IN THE FURNITURE INDUSTRY, HAVE BEEN APPOINTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. SISTANI SERVES AS THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF WW INTERNATIONAL, INC., A COMPANY FOCUSED ON HELPING PEOPLE ADOPT HEALTHY HABITS THROUGH HUMAN-CENTRIC TECHNOLOGY AND COMMUNITY, WHICH SHE JOINED IN MARCH 2022.
