BEST BUY SAYS EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN HUBERT JOLY TO STEP DOWN. BEST BUY CO - ON MARCH 10, HUBERT JOLY, CURRENT EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN OF BOARD, INFORMED BOARD THAT HE WOULD NOT STAND FOR RE-ELECTION.BEST BUY CO - BOARD APPROVED APPOINTMENT OF J. PATRICK DOYLE, LEAD INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR, AS BOARD'S CHAIRMAN, EFFECTIVE UPON JOLY'S RETIREMENT.
