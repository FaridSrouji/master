ROKU AND BEST BUY HELP CREATE BETTER TV EXPERIENCE THROUGH NEW ADVERTISING AND RETAIL RELATIONSHIP. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, ROKU, (NASDAQ: ROKU) THE #1 TV STREAMING PLATFORM IN THE U.S., CANADA, AND MEXICO*, AND BEST BUY (NYSE: BBY), THE LEADING CONSUMER TECH RETAILER IN THE U.S., ANNOUNCED A FIRST-TO-MARKET RELATIONSHIP ACROSS THEIR ADVERTISING AND RETAIL BUSINESSES. FOR THE FIRST TIME, BEST BUY IS PARTNERING WITH A TV STREAMING PLATFORM TO PROVIDE ITS FIRST-PARTY DATA FOR TARGETING AND CLOSED-LOOP MEASUREMENT.
