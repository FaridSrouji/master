BEST BUY SAYS BEGINNING APRIL 19, CO IS TEMPORARILY FURLOUGHING ABOUT 51,000 DOMESTIC HOURLY STORE EMPLOYEES. BEST BUY PROVIDES BUSINESS UPDATE RELATED TO COVID-19.BEST BUY CO INC -  SHIFT STORES TO A TEMPORARY ENHANCED CURBSIDE SERVICE-ONLY MODEL STARTING MARCH 22.BEST BUY CO INC - BEGINNING APRIL 19, COMPANY IS TEMPORARILY FURLOUGHING ABOUT 51,000 DOMESTIC HOURLY STORE EMPLOYEES.BEST BUY CO INC - ALSO SUSPENDED ALL IN-HOME DELIVERY, INSTALLATION AND REPAIRS.BEST BUY CO INC - COMPANY IS RETAINING ABOUT 82% OF ITS FULL-TIME STORE AND FIELD EMPLOYEES ON ITS PAYROLL.BEST BUY CO INC - CEO CORIE BARRY WILL FOREGO 50% OF HER BASE SALARY.BEST BUY CO INC - MEMBERS OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS WILL FOREGO 50% OF THEIR CASH RETAINER FEES THROUGH AT LEAST SEPTEMBER 1, 2020.BEST BUY CO INC - COMPANY EXECUTIVES REPORTING DIRECTLY TO CEO WILL TAKE A 20% REDUCTION IN BASE SALARY THROUGH AT LEAST SEPTEMBER 1, 2020.BEST BUY CO INC - LOWERING MERCHANDISE RECEIPTS TO MATCH DEMAND WITH A FOCUS ON ESSENTIAL ITEMS.BEST BUY CO INC - EXTENDING PAYMENT TERMS IN PARTNERSHIP WITH KEY MERCHANDISING VENDORS.BEST BUY CO INC - SUSPENDING 401(K) COMPANY MATCHING PROGRAM.BEST BUY CO INC - SALES FOR 9-WEEK PERIOD ENDED APRIL 4, 2020, DECLINED APPROXIMATELY 5% ON A YEAR-OVER-YEAR BASIS.BEST BUY CO INC - IN EIGHT-DAY PERIOD ENDED MARCH 20, 2020, SALES GREW ABOUT 25%.BEST BUY CO INC - COMPANY'S QUARTER-TO-DATE SALES THROUGH MARCH 20, 2020, GREW APPROXIMATELY 4% AND WERE AHEAD OF ORIGINAL EXPECTATIONS.BEST BUY CO INC - AT THAT TIME, SUSPENDED ALL IN-HOME DELIVERY, INSTALLATION AND REPAIRS.
