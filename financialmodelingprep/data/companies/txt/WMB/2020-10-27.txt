WILLIAMS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS' (NYSE: WMB) BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR DIVIDEND OF $0.40 PER SHARE, OR $1.60 ANNUALIZED, ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON DEC. 28, 2020, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DEC. 11, 2020. THE DIVIDEND IS CONSISTENT WITH THE THIRD-QUARTER 2020 DIVIDEND AND IS A 5.3% INCREASE FROM WILLIAMS FOURTH-QUARTER 2019 DIVIDEND OF $0.38 PER SHARE, PAID IN DECEMBER 2019. SOME PORTION OF THIS DISTRIBUTION MAY BE CONSIDERED A RETURN
