THE WILLIAMS COMPANIES, INC. APPROVES REGULAR DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 30, 2019. WILLIAMS’ BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR DIVIDEND OF $0.38 PER SHARE, OR $1.52 ANNUALIZED, ON THE COMPANY’S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON DEC. 30, 2019, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DEC. 13, 2019.
