SATLANTIS, LLC, ANNOUNCES STRATEGIC INVESTMENT. GAINESVILLE, FLA. , MARCH 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SATLANTIS, LLC ("SATLANTIS"), A US TECHNOLOGY LEADER IN HIGH PRECISION MINIATURIZED SATELLITES AND CAMERAS IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE A STRATEGIC INVESTMENT FROM ENCINO ENVIRONMENTAL HOLDINGS, LLC ("ENCINO").
