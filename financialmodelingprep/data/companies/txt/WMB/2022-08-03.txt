WILLIAMS PRICES $1.75 BILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS (NYSE: WMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $1.00 BILLION OF ITS 4.650% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2032 AT A PRICE OF 99.635 PERCENT OF PAR AND $750 MILLION OF ITS 5.300% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2052 AT A PRICE OF 99.954 PERCENT OF PAR. THE EXPECTED SETTLEMENT DATE FOR THE OFFERING IS AUGUST 8, 2022, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
