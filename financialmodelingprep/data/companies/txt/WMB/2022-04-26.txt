WILLIAMS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS' (NYSE: WMB) BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR DIVIDEND OF $0.425 PER SHARE, OR $1.70 ANNUALIZED, ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 27, 2022, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 10, 2022. THIS IS A 3.7% INCREASE FROM WILLIAMS' SECOND-QUARTER 2021 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.41 PER SHARE, PAID IN JUNE 2021.
