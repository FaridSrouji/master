TAILWATER CAPITAL ANNOUNCES SALE OF NORTEX MIDSTREAM PARTNERS TO WILLIAMS. DALLAS , SEPT. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TAILWATER CAPITAL, LLC ("TAILWATER"), A PRIVATE EQUITY FIRM THAT TAKES A FULL IMMERSION APPROACH TO INVESTING IN ENERGY AND GROWTH INFRASTRUCTURE SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL NORTEX MIDSTREAM, LLC ("NORTEX" OR THE "COMPANY") TO WILLIAMS FIELD SERVICES GROUP, LLC ("WILLIAMS"), A SUBSIDIARY OF THE WILLIAMS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: WMB) FOR A $423 MILLION PURCHASE PRICE.
