WILLIAMS COLLABORATES WITH MICROSOFT ON CLEAN ENERGY TRANSITION. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS (NYSE: WMB) HAS SIGNED A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) WITH MICROSOFT TO EXPLORE WAYS TO TRANSFORM ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST ENERGY INFRASTRUCTURE NETWORKS THROUGH DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION WHILE ADVANCING OUR NET ZERO EMISSIONS GOALS. “WILLIAMS SHARES MICROSOFT'S VISION FOR A LOW CARBON FUTURE. LIKEWISE, WILLIAMS IS COMMITTED TO HELPING OUR CUSTOMERS ACHIEVE THEIR SUSTAINABILITY GOALS WHILE MEETING ENERGY DEMAND WITH THE RELIABILITY OF CLEA
