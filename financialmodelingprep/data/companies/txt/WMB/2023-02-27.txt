WILLIAMS PRICES $1.50 BILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS (NYSE: WMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $750 MILLION OF ITS 5.400% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2026 AT A PRICE OF 99.907 PERCENT OF PAR AND $750 MILLION OF ITS 5.650% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2033 AT A PRICE OF 99.891 PERCENT OF PAR. THE EXPECTED SETTLEMENT DATE FOR THE OFFERING IS MARCH 2, 2023, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
