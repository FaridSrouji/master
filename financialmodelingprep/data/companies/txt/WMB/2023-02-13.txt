WILLIAMS APPOINTS CARRI LOCKHART TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS (NYSE: WMB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED CARRI LOCKHART AS AN INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR ON THE BOARD, EFFECTIVE FEB. 10, 2023. MS. LOCKHART BRINGS NEARLY 30 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE IN THE OIL AND GAS INDUSTRY BOTH IN THE UNITED STATES AND OVERSEAS, MOST RECENTLY SERVING AS CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF TECHNOLOGY, DIGITAL AND INNOVATION AT EQUINOR IN OSLO, NORWAY BEFORE RETIRING IN 2022.
