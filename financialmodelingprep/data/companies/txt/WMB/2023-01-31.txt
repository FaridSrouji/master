WILLIAMS INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY 5.3%. TULSA, OKLA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WILLIAMS' (NYSE: WMB) BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR DIVIDEND OF $0.4475 PER SHARE, OR $1.79 ANNUALIZED, ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MARCH 27, 2023, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 13, 2023.
