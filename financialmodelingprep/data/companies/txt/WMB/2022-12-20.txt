WILLIAMS OUTPACES INDUSTRY ACROSS KEY SUSTAINABILITY RANKINGS IN 2022. TULSA, OKLA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WILLIAMS (NYSE: WMB) WAS ONCE AGAIN RECOGNIZED ACROSS SEVERAL KEY RANKINGS IN 2022 – INCLUDING CDP, DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX (DJSI), SUSTAINALYTICS AND MSCI – FOR THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO TRANSPARENCY AND GOVERNANCE AROUND CLIMATE CHANGE AND OTHER ESG TOPICS. WILLIAMS WAS NAMED FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR TO THE DJSI NORTH AMERICA INDEX AND FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR TO THE DJSI WORLD INDEX.
