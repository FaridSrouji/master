THE WILLIAMS COMPANIES, INC. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. THE WILLIAMS COMPANIES, INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 1,999 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,303 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 628 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 501 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 221 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 129 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 0.18 COMPARED TO USD 0.13 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 6,094 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 6,482 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 1,687 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,402 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 726 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 416 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 0.6 COMPARED TO USD 0.47 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WAS USD 0.6 COMPARED TO USD 0.46 A YEAR AGO.
