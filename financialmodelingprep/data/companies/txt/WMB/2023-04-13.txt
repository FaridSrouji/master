WILLIAMS EMPLOYEES TO DONATE TIME AND ENERGY TO 116 VOLUNTEER PROJECTS ACROSS 19 STATES. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS (NYSE: WMB) EMPLOYEES WILL BE VOLUNTEERING IN COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE UNITED STATES AS PART OF THE COMPANY'S SECOND ANNUAL VOLUNTEER WEEK, APRIL 16 – 22. WITH 116 PROJECTS SCHEDULED IN 19 STATES, ABOUT 1,100 EMPLOYEES WILL ASSIST TEACHERS IN ELEMENTARY CLASSROOMS, COLLECT ELECTRONICS FOR RECYCLING AND HELP STOCK FOOD PANTRIES AMONG DOZENS OF OTHER VOLUNTEER JOBS. PROJECTS ARE EMPLOYEE IDENTIFIED AND EMPLOYEE LED.
