WILLIAMS TO PARTICIPATE IN 2023 J.P. MORGAN ENERGY, POWER AND RENEWABLES CONFERENCE. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS (NYSE: WMB) PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER ALAN ARMSTRONG ALONG WITH CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER JOHN PORTER ARE SCHEDULED TO PARTICIPATE IN MEETINGS WITH INVESTORS AT THE 2023 J.P. MORGAN ENERGY, POWER & RENEWABLES CONFERENCE IN NEW YORK CITY ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 21. ARMSTRONG IS SCHEDULED TO PRESENT AT 9:35 A.M. EASTERN TIME (8:35 A.M. CENTRAL TIME) ON JUNE 21.
