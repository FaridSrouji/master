NVR, INC. ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR RESULTS. RESTON, VA., FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NVR, INC. (NYSE: NVR), ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST HOMEBUILDING AND MORTGAGE BANKING COMPANIES, ANNOUNCED NET INCOME FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2021 OF $334.6 MILLION, OR $89.09 PER DILUTED SHARE.
