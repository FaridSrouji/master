NVR, INC. ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER RESULTS. RESTON, VA. , JAN. 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NVR, INC. (NYSE: NVR), ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST HOMEBUILDING AND MORTGAGE BANKING COMPANIES, ANNOUNCED NET INCOME FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2022 OF $454.8 MILLION, OR $133.44 PER DILUTED SHARE.
