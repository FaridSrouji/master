HANESBRANDS NAMES TANYA DEANS PRESIDENT OF HANES AUSTRALASIA. WINSTON-SALEM, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HANESBRANDS (NYSE: HBI), A LEADING GLOBAL MARKETER OF BRANDED EVERYDAY BASIC APPAREL, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT TANYA DEANS HAS BEEN NAMED PRESIDENT OF HANES AUSTRALASIA (HAA), EFFECTIVE FEB. 8, 2021. DEANS, CURRENTLY GROUP GENERAL MANAGER, BRAS N THINGS, SUCCEEDS DAVID BORTOLUSSI, WHO IN AUGUST ANNOUNCED HIS DEPARTURE FOR ANOTHER OPPORTUNITY.
