HANESBRANDS REPORTS SOLID FOURTH-QUARTER RESULTS. WINSTON-SALEM, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HANESBRANDS INC. (NYSE: HBI), A LEADING GLOBAL MARKETER OF BRANDED EVERYDAY BASIC APPAREL, TODAY ANNOUNCED FOURTH-QUARTER RESULTS, REFLECTING CONTINUED MOMENTUM ACROSS THE BUSINESS WITH SOLID SALES, ADJUSTED OPERATING PROFIT AND CASH FLOW. NET SALES FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER ENDED JANUARY 2, 2021, WERE $1.8 BILLION, COMPARED WITH $1.75 BILLION FOR THE COMPARABLE PERIOD ENDED DECEMBER 28, 2019.
