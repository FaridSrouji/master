LEXUS, SALEHE BEMBURY ANNOUNCE NEW COLLABORATIVE APPAREL COLLECTION. NEW YORK, AUG. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DRIVEN BY A SHARED DEDICATION TO PUSHING THE BOUNDARIES OF DESIGN AND CREATIVITY, LUXURY AUTOMAKER LEXUS AND DESIGNER SALEHE BEMBURY TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLECTION SHOWCASING  AN ASSORTMENT OF COLLABORATIVE APPAREL PRINTED ON CHAMPION SILHOUETTES. LEXUS' FUTURE-LOOKING MOBILITY INNOVATION AND BEMBURY'S SIGNATURE NATURE-INSPIRED DESIGN ETHOS COME TOGETHER FOR THIS CAPSULE THAT CHALLENGES THE STATUS QUO IN THE AUTOMOTIVE SPACE.
