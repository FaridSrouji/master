MCCORMICK RECOGNIZES 2023 UNSUNG HEROES DURING IN-PERSON EVENT AT M&T BANK STADIUM; AWARDS $105,000 IN TOTAL SCHOLARSHIPS. TWO $40,000 CHARLES PERRY MCCORMICK SCHOLARSHIPS AWARDED TO GEORGIANA BENCKINI OF NOTRE DAME PREPARATORY SCHOOL AND CARTER HERBERT OF PERRY HALL HIGH SCHOOL HUNT VALLEY, MD. , JUNE 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE: MKC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FLAVOR, ANNOUNCED GEORGIANA BENCKINI AND CARTER HERBERT AS THE 89TH AND 90TH RECIPIENTS OF THE CHARLES PERRY MCCORMICK SCHOLARSHIP, WORTH $40,000 OVER FOUR YEARS.
