BRENDAN FOLEY PROMOTED TO MCCORMICK CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER; LAWRENCE KURZIUS TO CONTINUE AS EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , JUNE 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE: MKC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FLAVOR, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PROMOTION OF BRENDAN FOLEY TO PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER (CEO) FOR THE COMPANY EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 1, 2023.
