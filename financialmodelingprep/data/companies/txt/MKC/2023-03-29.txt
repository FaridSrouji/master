MCCORMICK DECLARES $0.39 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , MARCH 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE:MKC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.39 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCKS, PAYABLE APRIL 24, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD APRIL 10, 2023.
