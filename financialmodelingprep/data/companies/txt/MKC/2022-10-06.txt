MCCORMICK REPORTS THIRD QUARTER PERFORMANCE AND REAFFIRMS 2022 OUTLOOK. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , OCT. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE:MKC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FLAVOR, TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED AUGUST 31, 2022 AND REAFFIRMED ITS FINANCIAL OUTLOOK FOR FISCAL YEAR 2022.
