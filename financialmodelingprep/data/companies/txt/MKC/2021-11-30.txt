MCCORMICK ANNOUNCES INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HUNT VALLEY, MD., NOV. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE:MKC) TODAY DECLARED AN INCREASE IN THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FROM $0.34 TO $0.37 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCKS, PAYABLE JANUARY 10, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD DECEMBER 31, 2021.
