GRANT THORNTON'S KIM MCCORMICK NAMED ONE OF SILICON VALLEY BUSINESS JOURNAL'S 2023 “WOMEN OF INFLUENCE”. CHICAGO & SAN JOSE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KIM MCCORMICK, AN AUDIT PARTNER AND THE BAY AREA NOT-FOR-PROFIT PRACTICE LEADER AT GRANT THORNTON LLP, HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE SILICON VALLEY BUSINESS JOURNAL'S 2023 “WOMEN OF INFLUENCE.” THE ANNUAL AWARDS RECOGNIZE OUTSTANDING BUSINESS, NONPROFIT AND COMMUNITY LEADERS WHO HAVE MADE A DEMONSTRABLE IMPACT IN THE BAY AREA. MCCORMICK AND HER FELLOW HONOREES WILL BE CELEBRATED AT AN EVENT AT THE SIGNIA BY HILTON HOTEL IN DOWNTOWN SAN JOSE ON THURSDAY,.
