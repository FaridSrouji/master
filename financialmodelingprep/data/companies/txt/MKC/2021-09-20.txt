MCCORMICK & COMPANY ANNOUNCED AS GLOBAL COMPACT LEAD. HUNT VALLEY, MD., SEPT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE:MKC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FLAVOR, ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS OFFICIALLY BEEN NAMED A GLOBAL COMPACT LEAD COMPANY BY THE UNITED NATIONS FOR ITS ONGOING COMMITMENT TO THE UN GLOBAL COMPACT AND ITS TEN PRINCIPLES FOR RESPONSIBLE BUSINESS.
