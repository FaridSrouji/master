MCCORMICK & COMPANY NAMED TO BARRON'S 2023 100 MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES LIST. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , MARCH 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE: MKC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FLAVOR, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED ON THE 2023 BARRON'S 100 MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES LIST RELEASED BY BARRON'S MAGAZINE.
