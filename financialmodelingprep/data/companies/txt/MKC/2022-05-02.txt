BRENDAN FOLEY NAMED CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER AND PRESIDENT FOR MCCORMICK. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , MAY 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE: MKC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FLAVOR, ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF BRENDAN FOLEY TO THE ROLE OF CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER AND PRESIDENT, EFFECTIVE JUNE 1, 2022.
