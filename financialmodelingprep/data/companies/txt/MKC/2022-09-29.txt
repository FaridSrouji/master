MCCORMICK DECLARES $0.37 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , SEPT. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE: MKC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.37 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCKS, PAYABLE OCTOBER 25, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD OCTOBER 11, 2022.
