MCCORMICK ANNOUNCES MALCOLM SWIFT'S PLAN TO RETIRE. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , MAY 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE: MKC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FLAVOR, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MALCOLM SWIFT, PRESIDENT GLOBAL FLAVOR SOLUTIONS AND CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER (CAO), WILL RETIRE AT THE END OF FISCAL YEAR 2023.
