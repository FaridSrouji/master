MCCORMICK REPORTS FIRST QUARTER PERFORMANCE AND REAFFIRMS 2023 OUTLOOK. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , MARCH 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE:MKC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FLAVOR, TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED FEBRUARY 28, 2023 AND REAFFIRMED ITS FINANCIAL OUTLOOK FOR FISCAL YEAR 2023.
