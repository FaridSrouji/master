MCCORMICK & COMPANY JOINS THE FORTUNE 500 WITH STRONG TRACK RECORD OF INDUSTRY LEADING PERFORMANCE. HUNT VALLEY, MD., JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE: MKC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FLAVOR, WAS OFFICIALLY NAMED TO THE FORTUNE 500 LIST OF COMPANIES BY FORTUNE MAGAZINE IN EARLY JUNE.
