MCCORMICK ANNOUNCES INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , NOV. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE:MKC) TODAY DECLARED AN INCREASE IN THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FROM $0.37 TO $0.39 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCKS, PAYABLE JANUARY 9, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD DECEMBER 30, 2022.
