MCCORMICK DECLARES $0.34 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HUNT VALLEY, MD., SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE: MKC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.34 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCKS, PAYABLE OCTOBER 26, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD OCTOBER 12, 2021.
