MCCORMICK DECLARES $0.34 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HUNT VALLEY, MD., MARCH 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE:MKC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.34 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCKS, PAYABLE APRIL 26, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD APRIL 12, 2021.
