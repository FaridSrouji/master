MCCORMICK DECLARES $0.37 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HUNT VALLEY, MD. , MARCH 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MCCORMICK & COMPANY, INCORPORATED (NYSE:MKC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.37 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCKS, PAYABLE APRIL 25, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD APRIL 11, 2022.
