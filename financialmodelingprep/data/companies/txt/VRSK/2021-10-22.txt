VERISK NAMED DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION INDUSTRY CHAMPION OF THE YEAR BY GAMMA IOTA SIGMA. JERSEY CITY, N.J., OCT. 22, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK) HAS BEEN NAMED DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION INDUSTRY CHAMPION OF THE YEAR BY GAMMA IOTA SIGMA , AN INTERNATIONAL PROFESSIONAL STUDENT ORGANIZATION, SERVING A MISSION OF PROMOTING AND SUSTAINING STUDENT INTEREST IN CAREERS IN THE INSURANCE INDUSTRY. THE AWARD RECOGNIZES A DEMONSTRATED COMMITMENT TO ADVANCING THE FUTURE OF INSURANCE THROUGH THE SUPPORT OF A DIVERSE TALENT PIPELINE.
