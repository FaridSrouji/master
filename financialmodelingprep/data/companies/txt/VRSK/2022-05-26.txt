PROPERTY AND CASUALTY INSURERS EXPERIENCE UNDERWRITING LOSS IN 2021, BUT REMAIN STRONG. JERSEY CITY, N.J., MAY 26, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DESPITE EXPERIENCING AN UNDERWRITING LOSS, THE PROPERTY/CASUALTY INSURANCE INDUSTRY ENDED 2021 STRONG AND ABLE TO SUPPORT POLICYHOLDERS, ACCORDING TO A REPORT FROM VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER, AND THE AMERICAN PROPERTY CASUALTY INSURANCE ASSOCIATION (APCIA).
