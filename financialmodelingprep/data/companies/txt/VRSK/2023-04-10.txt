VERISK PUBLISHES 2022 CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY REPORT. JERSEY CITY, N.J., APRIL 10, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS AND TECHNOLOGY PROVIDER, HAS PUBLISHED ITS ANNUAL  CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY REPORT . FOCUSED ON WORKING TOGETHER TO BUILD GLOBAL RESILIENCE FOR INDIVIDUALS, COMMUNITIES AND BUSINESSES, THE COMPANY'S REPORT OFFERS A VIEW INTO VERISK'S ESG JOURNEY AND PROGRESS TOWARD MAXIMIZING VALUE FOR STAKEHOLDERS.
