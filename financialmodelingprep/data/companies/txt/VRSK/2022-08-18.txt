VERISK WELCOMES OLUMIDE SOROYE TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. JERSEY CITY, N.J., AUG. 18, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT OLUMIDE SOROYE HAS BEEN APPOINTED INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR OF THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. SOROYE BRINGS THREE DECADES OF LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCE ACROSS MULTIPLE INDUSTRIES.
