VERISK CEO LEE SHAVEL TO ASSUME ROLE AS PRESIDENT, AS MARK ANQUILLARE STEPS DOWN. JERSEY CITY, N.J., DEC. 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER, ANNOUNCED THAT MARK V. ANQUILLARE IS STEPPING DOWN FROM VERISK AS OF JANUARY 15, 2023. ANQUILLARE HAS BEEN SERVING AS PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER SINCE MAY 2022. CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER LEE M. SHAVEL WILL ASSUME THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT, SERVING CONCURRENT ROLES AS PRESIDENT AND CEO.
