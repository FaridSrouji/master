CLIMATE TECH FIRM SCEPTER, INC. ENGAGES AER TO MONITOR GREENHOUSE EMISSIONS FROM SPACE. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SCEPTER, INC., AN EMERGING COMPANY BACKED BY L37 VENTURES AND TABLEROCK THAT USES GLOBAL EARTH AND SPACE-BASED DATA TO MEASURE AIR POLLUTION IN REAL TIME, HAS TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION WITH ATMOSPHERIC AND ENVIRONMENTAL RESEARCH (AER), A VERISK BUSINESS. THE AGREEMENT COMES AS THE COP26 ENVIRONMENTAL SUMMIT IN GLASGOW MEETS, AND IT WILL UNITE AN UP-AND-COMING PLAYER WITH A TRUSTED ENVIRONMENTAL TRAILBLAZER.
