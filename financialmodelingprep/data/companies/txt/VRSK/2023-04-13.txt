VERISK PROMOTES CARRIE BARR AND AARON BRUNKO TO LEAD GROWING CLAIMS BUSINESSES. JERSEY CITY, N.J., APRIL 13, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS AND TECHNOLOGY PROVIDER, HAS PROMOTED CARRIE BARR TO PRESIDENT OF CASUALTY SOLUTIONS AND AARON BRUNKO TO PRESIDENT OF PROPERTY ESTIMATING SOLUTIONS. BOTH BUSINESSES ARE PART OF VERISK'S CLAIMS SOLUTIONS GROUP.
