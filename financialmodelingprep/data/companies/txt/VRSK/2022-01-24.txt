VERISK ANNOUNCES SALE OF 3E BUSINESS TO NEW MOUNTAIN CAPITAL. JERSEY CITY, N.J., JAN. 24, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS 3E BUSINESS TO NEW MOUNTAIN CAPITAL, A GROWTH-ORIENTED INVESTMENT FIRM WITH OVER $35 BILLION IN ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT, FOR A POTENTIAL AGGREGATE CASH CONSIDERATION OF UP TO $950 MILLION.
