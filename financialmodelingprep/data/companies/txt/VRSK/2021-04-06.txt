VERISK TO ANNOUNCE FISCAL FIRST-QUARTER 2021 RESULTS ON MAY 4, 2021. JERSEY CITY, N.J., APRIL 06, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NASDAQ:VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER, WILL REPORT ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FISCAL FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021, ON TUESDAY, MAY 4, 2021, AFTER THE MARKET CLOSE. THE PRESS RELEASE, WITH ACCOMPANYING FINANCIAL INFORMATION, WILL BE POSTED ON THE VERISK INVESTOR WEBSITE AT  HTTP://INVESTOR.VERISK.COM .
