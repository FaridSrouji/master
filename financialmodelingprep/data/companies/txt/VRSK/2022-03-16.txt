VERISK ANNOUNCES EUROPEAN COLLABORATION WITH GT MOTIVE TO EXPEDITE MOTOR DAMAGE CLAIMS. LONDON, MARCH 16, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  VERISK (NASDAQ:VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER, HAS TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW COLLABORATION TO INTEGRATE ITS IVI MOTOR REPAIR PRICING AND TRIAGING SOLUTION WITH THE GT ESTIMATE CLAIMS CALCULATOR FROM GT MOTIVE, A PROVIDER OF REPAIR DATA IN THE AUTOMOTIVE, INSURANCE AND FLEET SECTORS. THE NEW INTEGRATION WILL BE ROLLED OUT ACROSS THE UK AND THE WIDER EUROPEAN MARKET TO HELP ENHANCE DIGITAL COLLABORATION AND EXPEDITE SPEED AND ACCURACY DURING THE MOTOR DAMAGE CLAIMS PROCESS.
