LIFE INSURERS CAN NOW DISTILL INSIGHTS FROM UNSTRUCTURED DATA IN ELECTRONIC HEALTH RECORDS, AS AUTHORIZED BY CONSUMERS, WITH NEW VERISK SOLUTION.
