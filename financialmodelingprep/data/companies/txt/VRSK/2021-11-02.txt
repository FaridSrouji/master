VERISK ACQUIRES DATA DRIVEN SAFETY TO FURTHER EXPAND AUTO INSURANCE ANALYTICS. JERSEY CITY, N.J., NOV. 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NASDAQ:VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER, HAS ACQUIRED DATA DRIVEN SAFETY, A LEADING PUBLIC RECORD DATA AGGREGATION FIRM THAT SPECIALIZES IN DRIVER RISK ASSESSMENT IN THE UNITED STATES. THE ACQUISITION WILL EXPAND VERISK'S ROBUST AUTO INSURANCE ANALYTICS, PROVIDING INSURERS WITH INFORMATION TO FURTHER REFINE UNDERWRITING, IMPROVE THE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE AND PROMOTE PUBLIC SAFETY.
