VERISK NAMES BRUCE HANSEN CHAIRPERSON OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS. JERSEY CITY, N.J., MAY 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BRUCE HANSEN HAS BEEN NAMED CHAIRPERSON OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE AS OF MAY 5, 2022. HANSEN WILL SUCCEED SCOTT G. STEPHENSON WHO, AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED, WILL RETIRE FROM THE COMPANY FOLLOWING VERISK'S 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS.
