VERISK EARNS SIXTH CONSECUTIVE GREAT PLACE TO WORK CERTIFICATION™. JERSEY CITY, N.J., JAN. 05, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FOR THE SIXTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK) IS CERTIFIED™ IN THE UNITED STATES BY GREAT PLACE TO WORK. BASED ON EMPLOYEE FEEDBACK, THE CERTIFICATION HONORS VERISK FOR ITS OUTSTANDING WORKPLACE CULTURE.
VERISK EARNS SECOND CONSECUTIVE GREAT PLACE TO WORK CERTIFICATION™ IN THE UK AND SPAIN. LONDON/MADRID, JAN. 05, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK) HAS OBTAINED THE CERTIFICATION AS A GREAT PLACE TO WORK IN THE UNITED KINGDOM AND SPAIN FOR THE SECOND YEAR IN A ROW, GRANTED BY THE CONSULTANCY GREAT PLACE TO WORK®, LEADER IN THE IDENTIFICATION AND CERTIFICATION OF BEST WORKPLACES. BASED ON EMPLOYEE FEEDBACK, THE CERTIFICATION HONORS VERISK FOR ITS OUTSTANDING WORKPLACE CULTURE.
