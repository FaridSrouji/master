VERISK NAMED ONE OF UK'S BEST WORKPLACES™ AND BEST WORKPLACES FOR WELLBEING™ BY GREAT PLACE TO WORK®. LONDON, APRIL 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER VERISK (NASDAQ: VRSK) HAS BEEN RECOGNISED AS ONE OF THE UK'S BEST WORKPLACES™ AND UK'S BEST WORKPLACES™ FOR WELLBEING BY GREAT PLACE TO WORK®, THE GLOBAL AUTHORITY ON WORKPLACE CULTURE. BASED ON EMPLOYEE FEEDBACK, EACH RECOGNITION HONOURS VERISK FOR CREATING AN ENVIRONMENT OF TRUST, PRIDE AND CAMARADERIE.
