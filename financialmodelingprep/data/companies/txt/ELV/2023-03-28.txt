THE STANDARD TO ACQUIRE LIFE & DISABILITY BUSINESS FROM ELEVANCE HEALTH; COMPANIES ANNOUNCE FUTURE DISTRIBUTION PARTNERSHIP. PORTLAND, ORE.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STANCORP FINANCIAL GROUP, INC., (THE STANDARD) AND ELEVANCE HEALTH (NYSE: ELV) TODAY ANNOUNCED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH THE STANDARD WILL ACQUIRE THE LIFE & DISABILITY BUSINESS FROM ELEVANCE HEALTH AND ENTER INTO A PRODUCT DISTRIBUTION PARTNERSHIP. THE STANDARD AND ELEVANCE HEALTH ARE LEADING BENEFITS PROVIDERS WITH DEEP EXPERTISE IN GROUP BENEFITS ADMINISTRATION AND A SHARED COMMITMENT TO CUSTOMER SERVICE.
