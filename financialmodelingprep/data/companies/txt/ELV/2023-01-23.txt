ELEVANCE HEALTH TO ACQUIRE BLUE CROSS AND BLUE SHIELD OF LOUISIANA. INDIANAPOLIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ELEVANCE HEALTH, INC. (NYSE: ELV) AND BLUE CROSS AND BLUE SHIELD OF LOUISIANA ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANIES HAVE ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT THROUGH WHICH ELEVANCE HEALTH AGREES TO ACQUIRE BLUE CROSS AND BLUE SHIELD OF LOUISIANA (BCBSLA). BCBSLA WILL JOIN ELEVANCE HEALTH'S AFFILIATED ANTHEM BLUE CROSS BLUE SHIELD FAMILY OF BRANDS.
