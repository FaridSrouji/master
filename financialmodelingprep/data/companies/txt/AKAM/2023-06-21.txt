ANALYST FIRM CITES PROVEN TRACK RECORD OF DELIVERING SECURITY WITHOUT PERFORMANCE TRADE-OFFS CAMBRIDGE, MASS. , JUNE 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AKAMAI TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NASDAQ: AKAM), THE CLOUD COMPANY THAT POWERS AND PROTECTS LIFE ONLINE, HAS BEEN NAMED A LEADER IN THE IDC MARKETSCAPE: WORLDWIDE NETWORK EDGE SECURITY AS A SERVICE (NESAAS) 2023 VENDOR ASSESSMENT (DOC #US5072823, JUNE 2023).
