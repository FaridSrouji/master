VIDEO GAME INDUSTRY FACED HIGHEST GROWTH IN CYBERATTACKS DURING PANDEMIC. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., JUNE 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  AKAMAI TECHNOLOGIES, INC.  (NASDAQ: AKAM), THE WORLD'S MOST TRUSTED SOLUTION FOR PROTECTING AND DELIVERING DIGITAL EXPERIENCES, TODAY RELEASED RESEARCH SHOWING THAT CYBERATTACK TRAFFIC TARGETING THE VIDEO GAME INDUSTRY GREW MORE THAN ANY OTHER INDUSTRY DURING THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
