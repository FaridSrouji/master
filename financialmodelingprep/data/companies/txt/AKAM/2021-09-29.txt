AKAMAI TO ACQUIRE GUARDICORE TO EXTEND ITS ZERO TRUST SOLUTIONS TO HELP STOP RANSOMWARE. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., SEPT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AKAMAI TECHNOLOGIES, INC.  (NASDAQ: AKAM), THE WORLD'S MOST TRUSTED SOLUTION FOR PROTECTING AND DELIVERING DIGITAL EXPERIENCES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE TEL AVIV, ISRAEL-BASED GUARDICORE.
