AKAMAI TECHNOLOGIES TO ACQUIRE CLOUD STORAGE TECHNOLOGY COMPANY ONDAT. CAMBRIDGE, MASS. , MARCH 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AKAMAI TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NASDAQ: AKAM), THE CLOUD COMPANY THAT POWERS AND PROTECTS LIFE ONLINE, TODAY ANNOUNCES THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE ONDAT , A CLOUD-BASED STORAGE TECHNOLOGY PROVIDER WITH A KUBERNETES-NATIVE PLATFORM FOR RUNNING STATEFUL APPLICATIONS ANYWHERE AT SCALE.
