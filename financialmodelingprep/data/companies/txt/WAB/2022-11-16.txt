WABTEC DONATES $25,000 TO SUPPORT FLORIDA HURRICANE RELIEF EFFORTS. PITTSBURGH--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE WABTEC FOUNDATION, THE PHILANTHROPIC ARM OF WABTEC CORPORATION (NYSE: WAB), TODAY ANNOUNCED A DONATION OF $25,000 TO SUPPORT LOCAL RELIEF EFFORTS IN FLORIDA FOLLOWING HURRICANES IAN AND NICOLE. THE DONATION IS BEING MADE TO THE RED CROSS.
