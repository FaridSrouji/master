WABTEC ANNOUNCES SENIOR LEADERSHIP MOVES IN SUPPORT OF CONTINUED GROWTH. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WABTEC CORPORATION (NYSE: WAB) ANNOUNCED TODAY LEADERSHIP MOVES AIMED AT ACCELERATING THE LONG-TERM GROWTH STRATEGY IN THE SERVICES AND TRANSIT BUSINESSES, AS WELL AS THE COMPANY'S OVERALL SUSTAINABILITY, AND BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT INITIATIVES. “WABTEC IS WELL POSITIONED TO DRIVE PROFITABLE GROWTH BY DELIVERING CONTINUED INNOVATION TO OUR CUSTOMERS AND THE WORLD,” SAID RAFAEL SANTANA, WABTEC'S PRESIDENT AND CEO.
