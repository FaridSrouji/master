WABTEC SIGNS MEMORANDA OF UNDERSTANDING WITH CARNEGIE MELLON UNIVERSITY AND GENESEE & WYOMING TO DECARBONIZE FREIGHT RAIL AND IMPROVE FREIGHT RAIL SAFETY. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WABTEC (NYSE: WAB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) WITH CARNEGIE MELLON UNIVERSITY, A TOP UNIVERSITY IN ENGINEERING, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, BATTERY TECHNOLOGY, AUTONOMY AND ROBOTICS, WHICH FORMALIZES THEIR JOINT AIM TO CREATE TECHNOLOGIES THAT WILL DECARBONIZE FREIGHT RAIL TRANSPORT, IMPROVE FREIGHT SAFETY, AND GENERATE GREATER RAIL NETWORK UTILIZATION.
