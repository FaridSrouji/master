WABTEC BOARD MEMBERS, LEE FOSTER AND MICHAEL HOWELL, ANNOUNCE RETIREMENTS. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WABTEC CORPORATION'S (NYSE: WAB) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RETIREMENT OF DIRECTORS LEE FOSTER AND MICHAEL HOWELL UPON REACHING THE BOARD'S RETIREMENT AGE. THE RETIREMENTS WERE EFFECTIVE MAY 18, 2022. “THE BOARD AND MANAGEMENT TEAM ARE EXTREMELY GRATEFUL TO LEE AND MICHAEL FOR THEIR SERVICE AND LEADERSHIP DURING A TIME OF TREMENDOUS GROWTH FOR THE COMPANY,” SAID AL NEUPAVER, CHAIRMAN OF WABTEC. “IT HAS BEEN A PRIVILEGE WORKING WITH THEM.
