WABTEC DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WABTEC CORPORATION (NYSE: WAB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND OF 12 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON AUGUST 27, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 13, 2021.
