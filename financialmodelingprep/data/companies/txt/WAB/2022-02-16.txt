WABTEC REPORTS STRONG 4TH QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WABTEC CORPORATION (NYSE: WAB) TODAY REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER 2021 GAAP EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE OF $1.02, UP 121.7% VERSUS THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020. ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE WERE $1.18, UP 20.4% VERSUS THE SAME QUARTER A YEAR AGO. FOURTH QUARTER SALES WERE $2.07 BILLION AND CASH FROM OPERATIONS WAS $314 MILLION. FULL YEAR 2021 EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE WAS $2.96, UP 36.4% VERSUS FULL YEAR 2020. FULL YEAR ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE WERE $4.26
