WABTEC REPORTS STRONG FOURTH QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WABTEC CORPORATION (NYSE: WAB) TODAY REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER 2022 GAAP EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE OF $0.86, DOWN 15.7% VERSUS THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021. ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE WERE $1.30, UP 10.2% VERSUS THE SAME QUARTER A YEAR AGO. FOURTH QUARTER SALES WERE $2.31 BILLION AND CASH FROM OPERATIONS WAS $410 MILLION.
