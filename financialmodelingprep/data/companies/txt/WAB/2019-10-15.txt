WABTEC CORPORATION OPENS NEW HEADQUARTERS IN PITTSBURGH. WABTEC CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THE GRAND OPENING OF ITS NEW HEADQUARTERS IN PITTSBURGH. THE COMPANY’S ORIGINS BEGAN IN WESTERN PENNSYLVANIA WITH FOUNDER GEORGE WESTINGHOUSE JR.’S INVENTION OF THE AUTOMATIC AIR BRAKE – A BREAKTHROUGH THAT REDEFINED THE TRANSPORTATION INDUSTRY AND SAVED LIVES. THE OFFICE WILL HOUSE ROUGHLY 250 EMPLOYEES AND PLAY A CRITICAL ROLE IN ACCELERATING THE FUTURE OF TRANSPORTATION.
