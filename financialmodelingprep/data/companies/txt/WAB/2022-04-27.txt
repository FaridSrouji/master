WABTEC REPORTS STRONG 1ST QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WABTEC CORPORATION (NYSE: WAB) TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2022 GAAP EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE OF $0.80, UP 35.6% VERSUS THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE WERE $1.13, UP 27.0% VERSUS THE SAME QUARTER A YEAR AGO.
