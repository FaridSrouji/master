WABTEC'S INNOVATIVE ADAPTIVE WHEEL SLIDE PROTECTION SOLUTION GAINS CERTIFICATION. PITTSBURGH--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WABTEC CORPORATION'S (NYSE: WAB) DISTANCEMASTER SOLUTION TOOK A MAJOR STEP TOWARD IMPROVING BRAKING PERFORMANCE FOR TRANSIT RAIL OPERATORS WITH THE CERTIFICATION OF THE COMPANY'S ADAPTIVE WHEEL SLIDE PROTECTION (WSP) TECHNOLOGY. THE SOLUTION ADDRESSES THE WHEEL AND RAIL ADHESION BOTTLENECK ENABLING TRANSIT SYSTEMS TO IMPROVE EFFICIENCY AND INCREASE NETWORK CAPACITY.
