WABTEC DEBUTS ON HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN'S 2021 CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WABTEC CORPORATION (NYSE: WAB) DEBUTED ON THE HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION'S 2021 CORPORATE EQUALITY INDEX (CEI), THE STANDARD FOR MEASURING POLICIES AND PRACTICES THAT PROMOTE A LGBTQ-INCLUSIVE WORKPLACE. THE ADDITION TO THE CEI BUILDS UPON THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO FOSTER A CULTURE OF DIVERSITY, INCLUSION AND BELONGING AS OUTLINED IN ITS 2020 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. “IT IS AN HONOR TO BE RANKED BY THIS RESPECTED AUTHORITY ON LGBTQ WORKPLACE EQUALITY,” SAID NI
