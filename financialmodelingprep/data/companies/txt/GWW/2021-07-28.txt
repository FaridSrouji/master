GRAINGER DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CHICAGO, JULY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF W.W. GRAINGER, INC. (NYSE: GWW) TODAY DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.62 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 1, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 9, 2021.
