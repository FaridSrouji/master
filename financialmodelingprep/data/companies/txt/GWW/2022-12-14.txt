ZORO.COM REACHES $1 BILLION IN ANNUAL SALES. THE BUSINESS ACHIEVES THIS MILESTONE AFTER GROWING ASSORTMENT AND CUSTOMER BASE CHICAGO , DEC. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ZORO.COM, AN ECOMMERCE BUSINESS THAT SELLS MILLIONS OF SUPPLIES, EQUIPMENT AND TOOLS TO SMALL BUSINESSES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS REACHED $1 BILLION IN ANNUAL SALES THROUGH ITS U.S. WEBSITE FOR THE FIRST TIME IN A CALENDAR YEAR. "ZORO EXISTS TO MAKE OUR CUSTOMERS' LIVES EASIER, AND THIS ACHIEVEMENT IS A TESTAMENT TO THE WAYS WE CONTINUE TO HELP SMALL BUSINESSES FIND AND BUY MORE OF WHAT THEY NEED IN ONE PLACE," SAID KEVIN WEADICK, PRESIDENT OF ZORO.COM.
