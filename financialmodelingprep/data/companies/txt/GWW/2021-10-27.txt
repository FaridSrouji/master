THREE GRAINGER BOARD MEMBERS AND CORPORATE OFFICER RECOGNIZED IN SAVOY MAGAZINE'S 2021 INFLUENTIAL BLACK CORPORATE DIRECTORS LIST. CHICAGO, OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GRAINGER (NYSE: GWW), THE LEADING BROAD LINE SUPPLIER OF MAINTENANCE, REPAIR AND OPERATING (MRO) PRODUCTS SERVING BUSINESSES AND INSTITUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THREE OF ITS BOARD MEMBERS AND A CORPORATE OFFICER ARE RECOGNIZED IN  SAVOY MAGAZINE'S "2021 MOST INFLUENTIAL BLACK CORPORATE DIRECTORS." GRAINGER PROUDLY RECOGNIZES ITS BOARD MEMBERS RODNEY ADKINS, BRIAN ANDERSON AND STEVEN WHITE, AS WELL AS GRAINGER SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER DEIDRA (DEE) MERRIWETHER WHO SERVES ON THE BOARD OF WEYERHAEUSER.
GRAINGER DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CHICAGO, OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GRAINGER (NYSE: GWW) TODAY DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.62 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 1, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 8, 2021. ABOUT GRAINGER W.W.
