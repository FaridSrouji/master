GRAINGER TO ADD 100 JOBS IN LAKE COUNTY, ILLINOIS. CHICAGO, NOV. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GRAINGER (NYSE: GWW), THE LEADING BROAD LINE SUPPLIER OF MAINTENANCE, REPAIR AND OPERATING (MRO) PRODUCTS SERVING BUSINESSES AND INSTITUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL EXPAND ITS CUSTOMER SERVICE CAPABILITIES BY ADDING APPROXIMATELY 100 NEW JOBS TO ITS LAKE COUNTY, ILLINOIS, HEADQUARTERS. THE COMPANY'S NEW CUSTOMER SERVICE CENTER WILL OPEN IN JANUARY, ALLOWING GRAINGER TO BETTER SUPPORT ITS GROWING CUSTOMER BASE BY CONTINUING TO DELIVER EXCEPTIONAL SERVICE.
