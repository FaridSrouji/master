GRAINGER AND THE AMERICAN RED CROSS CELEBRATE 20 YEARS OF COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIP. CHICAGO, SEPT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GRAINGER (NYSE: GWW), THE LEADING BROAD LINE SUPPLIER OF MAINTENANCE, REPAIR AND OPERATING (MRO) PRODUCTS SERVING BUSINESSES AND INSTITUTIONS, ANNOUNCED THAT THIS YEAR MARKS A SIGNIFICANT MILESTONE WITH THE AMERICAN RED CROSS, THE COUNTRY'S PREMIER DISASTER RELIEF ORGANIZATION.
