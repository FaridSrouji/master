KEYSIGHT BRINGS ADVANCED SOLUTION EXPERTISE TO FOUR KEY EUROPEAN 6G PROJECTS. SANTA ROSA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--KEYSIGHT TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: KEYS), A LEADING TECHNOLOGY COMPANY THAT DELIVERS ADVANCED DESIGN AND VALIDATION SOLUTIONS TO HELP ACCELERATE INNOVATION TO CONNECT AND SECURE THE WORLD, ANNOUNCED IT BRINGS ADVANCED SOLUTION EXPERTISE TO FOUR PROJECTS THAT ARE A PART OF THE 6G SMART NETWORKS AND SERVICES JOINT UNDERTAKING (SNS-JU), A RESEARCH AND INNOVATION PROGRAM CO-FUNDED BY THE EUROPEAN UNION (EU).
