KEYSIGHT COORDINATES PAN-EUROPEAN 6G TESTBED. SANTA ROSA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--KEYSIGHT TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: KEYS), A LEADING TECHNOLOGY COMPANY THAT DELIVERS ADVANCED DESIGN AND VALIDATION SOLUTIONS TO HELP ACCELERATE INNOVATION TO CONNECT AND SECURE THE WORLD, ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY JOINS FORCES WITH SIXTEEN ORGANIZATIONS TO CREATE 6G-SANDBOX, A PAN-EUROPEAN TESTBED FOR 6G EXPERIMENTATION AND VALIDATION OF 5G-ADVANCED AND 6G CAPABILITIES. AS A PART OF 6G-SANDBOX, KEYSIGHT WILL ACT AS THE PROJECT COORDINATOR.
