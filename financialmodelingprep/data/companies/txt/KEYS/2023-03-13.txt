KEYSIGHT O-RAN SOLUTIONS ADDED TO CABLELABS' 5G LAB FOR SUPPORT OF 2023 5G CHALLENGE. SANTA ROSA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $KEYS #5G--KEYSIGHT TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: KEYS) ANNOUNCES THAT ITS KEYSIGHT OPEN RAN ARCHITECT (KORA) SOLUTIONS HAVE BEEN ADDED TO CABLELABS' 5G LAB TO VALIDATE THE INTEROPERABILITY OF OPEN RADIO ACCESS NETWORK (O-RAN) COMPONENTS DURING THE 2023 5G CHALLENGE, SPONSORED BY THE NATIONAL TELECOMMUNICATIONS AND INFORMATION ADMINISTRATION (NTIA) AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE.
