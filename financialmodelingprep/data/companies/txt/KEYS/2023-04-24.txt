KEYSIGHT MAKES UK'S FIRST 100GBPS 6G SUB-THZ CONNECTION WITH NATIONAL PHYSICAL LABORATORY AND UNIVERSITY OF SURREY. SANTA ROSA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $KEYS #100GBPS--KEYSIGHT TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: KEYS), IN COLLABORATION WITH NATIONAL PHYSICAL LABORATORY (NPL) AND THE UNIVERSITY OF SURREY, HAS MADE THE FIRST 6G CONNECTION AT SPEEDS GREATER THAN 100 GIGABITS PER SECOND (GBPS) OVER SUB-TERAHERTZ (THZ) FREQUENCIES IN THE U.K. FUTURE 6G USE CASES, SUCH AS AUGMENTED REALITY AND AUTONOMOUS VEHICLES, WILL REQUIRE DATA THROUGHPUT SPEEDS FROM 100 GBPS TO 1 TERABIT PER SECOND (TBPS).
