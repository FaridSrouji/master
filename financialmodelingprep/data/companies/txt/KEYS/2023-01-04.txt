KEYSIGHT, QUALCOMM ACCELERATE 5G NON-TERRESTRIAL NETWORK COMMUNICATION TO SUPPORT BROADBAND IN REMOTE AREAS. SANTA ROSA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--KEYSIGHT TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: KEYS), A LEADING TECHNOLOGY COMPANY THAT DELIVERS ADVANCED DESIGN AND VALIDATION SOLUTIONS TO HELP ACCELERATE INNOVATION TO CONNECT AND SECURE THE WORLD, ANNOUNCED IT HAS COLLABORATED WITH QUALCOMM TECHNOLOGIES, INC. TO ESTABLISH AN END-TO-END 5G NON-TERRESTRIAL NETWORK (NTN) CONNECTION. BASED ON THIS SUCCESSFUL DEMONSTRATION OF CALL SIGNALING AND DATA TRANSFER USING ORBIT TRAJECTORY EMULATION, KEYSIGHT AND QUALCOMM TECHNOLOGIES AIM TO ACCELERATE 5G NTN TECHNOLOGY TO PROVIDE AFFORDABLE BROADBAND CONNECTIVITY IN REMOTE AREAS.
