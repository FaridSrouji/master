KEYSIGHT JOINS INTEL FOUNDRY SERVICES ACCELERATOR EDA ALLIANCE PROGRAM. SANTA ROSA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--KEYSIGHT TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: KEYS), A LEADING TECHNOLOGY COMPANY THAT DELIVERS ADVANCED DESIGN AND VALIDATION SOLUTIONS TO HELP ACCELERATE INNOVATION TO CONNECT AND SECURE THE WORLD, ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS JOINED THE INTEL FOUNDRY SERVICES (IFS) ACCELERATOR ELECTRONIC DESIGN AUTOMATION (EDA) ALLIANCE PROGRAM.
