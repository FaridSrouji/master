KEYSIGHT ENABLES RITT7LAYERS AS NEWEST O-RAN ALLIANCE OPEN TESTING & INTEGRATION CENTER. SANTA ROSA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $KEYS #5G--KEYSIGHT TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: KEYS) HAS ENABLED RITT7LAYERS TO JOIN THE O-RAN ALLIANCE COMMUNITY OF OPEN TESTING & INTEGRATION CENTERS (OTICS) IN THE ASIA-PACIFIC REGION TO PROVIDE O-RAN CERTIFICATION AND BADGING FOR OPEN RADIO ACCESS NETWORK (OPEN RAN) COMPONENTS. RADIO ACCESS NETWORK (RAN) DEPLOYMENTS HAVE TRADITIONALLY RELIED ON PROPRIETARY NETWORK EQUIPMENT FROM SINGLE VENDORS. HOWEVER, SERVICE PROVIDERS ARE NOW ADOPTING AN OPEN RAN MODEL FOR 5G.
