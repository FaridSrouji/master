DOLLAR GENERAL CORPORATION SCHEDULED TO PARTICIPATE IN THE GOLDMAN SACHS 29TH ANNUAL GLOBAL RETAILING CONFERENCE. GOODLETTSVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DOLLAR GENERAL CORPORATION (NYSE: DG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DOLLAR GENERAL MANAGEMENT PLANS TO PARTICIPATE IN A FIRESIDE CHAT AT THE GOLDMAN SACHS 29TH ANNUAL GLOBAL RETAILING CONFERENCE ON SEPTEMBER 7, 2022 AT 12:15 P.M. ET. A LIVE WEBCAST WILL BE AVAILABLE AT HTTPS://INVESTOR.DOLLARGENERAL.COM UNDER “NEWS & EVENTS, EVENTS & PRESENTATIONS,” AND A REPLAY OF THE SESSION WILL BE ACCESSIBLE AT THE SAME LOCATION THROUGH OCTOBER 7, 2022.
