MACE SECURITY INTERNATIONAL ADDS DOLLAR GENERAL TO ITS CUSTOMER PORTFOLIO. CLEVELAND, OH / ACCESSWIRE / OCTOBER 13, 2022 / MACE SECURITY INTERNATIONAL, INC., (OTCQX:MACE) A GLOBALLY RECOGNIZED LEADER IN PERSONAL SAFETY PRODUCTS, IS EXCITED TO ANNOUNCE A NEW PARTNERSHIP WITH DOLLAR GENERAL (NYSE: DG), THE FOURTH LARGE RETAILER TO BE ADDED TO THE COMPANY'S PORTFOLIO IN 2022. DOLLAR GENERAL OPERATES NEARLY 19,000 LOCATIONS ACROSS THE UNITED STATES WITH A GOAL TO SERVE THEIR CUSTOMER BASE THROUGH HIGH-QUALITY, CONVENIENT ITEMS, AT GREAT PRICES.
