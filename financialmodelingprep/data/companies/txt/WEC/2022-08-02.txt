WEC ENERGY GROUP REPORTS SECOND-QUARTER RESULTS. MILWAUKEE , AUG. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $287.5 MILLION, OR 91 CENTS PER SHARE, FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022 – UP FROM $276.0 MILLION, OR 87 CENTS PER SHARE, IN LAST YEAR'S SECOND QUARTER. FOR THE FIRST SIX MONTHS OF 2022, THE COMPANY RECORDED NET INCOME OF $853.4 MILLION, OR $2.70 PER SHARE – UP FROM $786.1 MILLION, OR $2.49 PER SHARE, IN THE CORRESPONDING PERIOD A YEAR AGO.
