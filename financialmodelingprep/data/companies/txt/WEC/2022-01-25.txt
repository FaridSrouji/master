WEC ENERGY GROUP ANNOUNCES HYDROGEN POWER PILOT PROGRAM. MILWAUKEE, JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS LEADING A PILOT PROJECT TO TEST HYDROGEN AS A FUEL SOURCE FOR POWER GENERATION. THE COMPANY WILL TEST CO-FIRING HYDROGEN WITH NATURAL GAS AT ONE OF ITS POWER GENERATION PLANTS IN MICHIGAN'S UPPER PENINSULA.
