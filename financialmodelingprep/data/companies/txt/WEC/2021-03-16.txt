WE ENERGIES AND WISCONSIN PUBLIC SERVICE ANNOUNCE ANOTHER LARGE-SCALE RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECT. MILWAUKEE, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WE ENERGIES AND WISCONSIN PUBLIC SERVICE (WPS), SUBSIDIARIES OF WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC), ARE ADVANCING PLANS FOR A NEW 325-MEGAWATT (MW) SOLAR AND BATTERY STORAGE PROJECT. THE DARIEN SOLAR ENERGY CENTER IS THE COMPANY'S SECOND PROPOSED LARGE-SCALE SOLAR AND BATTERY PROJECT ANNOUNCED THIS YEAR.
