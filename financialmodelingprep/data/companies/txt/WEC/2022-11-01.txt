WEC ENERGY GROUP, EPRI COMPLETE WORLD'S FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND HYDROGEN POWER TEST. MILWAUKEE , NOV. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) AND EPRI ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SUCCESSFUL DEMONSTRATION OF BLENDING HYDROGEN IN A NATURAL GAS GENERATOR. THE PROJECT IS THE FIRST HYDROGEN POWER TEST OF A UTILITY-SCALE, GRID-CONNECTED RECIPROCATING ENGINE GENERATOR IN THE WORLD.
