WEC ENERGY GROUP CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY REPORT HIGHLIGHTS COMMITMENT TO AFFORDABLE, RELIABLE AND CLEAN ENERGY. MILWAUKEE, JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY RELEASED ITS 2020 CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY REPORT, A COMPREHENSIVE OVERVIEW OF THE COMPANY'S ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PERFORMANCE. THE REPORT DETAILS PROGRESS MADE BY WEC ENERGY GROUP AND ITS FAMILY OF COMPANIES ON MAJOR PROJECTS AND SUSTAINABILITY GOALS.
