WEC ENERGY GROUP TO ACQUIRE 80% OWNERSHIP OF MAPLE FLATS SOLAR ENERGY CENTER. MILWAUKEE , OCT. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS AGREED TO ACQUIRE AN 80% OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN THE MAPLE FLATS SOLAR ENERGY CENTER. LOCATED IN CLAY COUNTY, ILLINOIS, THE PROJECT IS BEING DEVELOPED BY INVENERGY — A LEADING GLOBAL DEVELOPER AND OPERATOR OF SUSTAINABLE ENERGY SOLUTIONS.
