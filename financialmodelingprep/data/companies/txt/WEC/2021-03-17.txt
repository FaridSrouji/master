PEOPLES GAS SAFETY PROGRAM BRINGING IMPROVEMENTS TO ENTIRE CITY NOW APPROXIMATELY ONE-THIRD COMPLETE. CHICAGO, MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH THE ARRIVAL OF SPRING CONSTRUCTION, PEOPLES GAS CONTINUES TO MAKE LARGE-SCALE IMPROVEMENTS TO THE SAFETY AND RELIABILITY OF CHICAGO'S NATURAL GAS DELIVERY SYSTEM. THE UPGRADES ARE THE FOCUS OF THE COMPANY'S SYSTEM MODERNIZATION PROGRAM (SMP), WHICH IS BRINGING SAFETY IMPROVEMENTS TO THOUSANDS OF MILES OF NATURAL GAS PIPES IN EVERY CHICAGO NEIGHBORHOOD.
