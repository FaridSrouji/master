WEC ENERGY GROUP ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENTS. MILWAUKEE, OCT. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY TOOK THE NEXT STEPS IN THE COMPANY'S SUCCESSION PLANNING PROCESS, PROVIDING CONTINUITY AND SENIOR LEADERSHIP FOR THE NEXT CHAPTER OF THE COMPANY'S GROWTH, DEVELOPMENT AND SERVICE TO CUSTOMERS.  GALE KLAPPA WILL CONTINUE TO SERVE AS EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN UNTIL MAY 2024.
