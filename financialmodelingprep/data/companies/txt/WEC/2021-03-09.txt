BLOOMING GROVE WIND ENERGY CENTER BEGINS COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVENERGY, A LEADING PRIVATELY-HELD GLOBAL DEVELOPER AND OPERATOR OF SUSTAINABLE ENERGY SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE 250-MEGAWATT BLOOMING GROVE WIND ENERGY CENTER COMMENCED COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS IN DECEMBER 2020. LOCATED IN MCLEAN COUNTY, ILLINOIS, BLOOMING GROVE GENERATES ENOUGH ELECTRICITY TO POWER ABOUT 69,000 AMERICAN HOMES EACH YEAR.
