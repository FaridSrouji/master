WEC ENERGY GROUP REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER RESULTS. MILWAUKEE , MAY 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $565.9 MILLION, OR $1.79 PER SHARE, FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022 — UP FROM $510.1 MILLION, OR $1.61 PER SHARE, FOR LAST YEAR'S FIRST QUARTER. CONSOLIDATED REVENUES TOTALED $2.9 BILLION, UP $216.7 MILLION FROM THE FIRST QUARTER A YEAR AGO.
