WEC ENERGY GROUP REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER RESULTS. MILWAUKEE , MAY 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $507.5 MILLION, OR $1.61 PER SHARE, FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023 — DOWN FROM $565.9 MILLION, OR $1.79 PER SHARE, IN LAST YEAR'S FIRST QUARTER. CONSOLIDATED REVENUES TOTALED $2.9 BILLION, DOWN $20.0 MILLION FROM THE FIRST QUARTER A YEAR AGO.
