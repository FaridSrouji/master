ILLINOIS RESIDENTS SUE PEOPLES GAS, SUBSIDIARY OF WEC, FOR CONTAMINATING EAST CENTRAL ILLINOIS' MAJOR SOURCE OF WATER, ACCORDING TO FEGANSCOTT LAW FIRM. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, A GROUP OF RESIDENTS FILED A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST NATURAL GAS PROVIDER PEOPLES GAS FOR NEGLECTING TO MAINTAIN WELLS THAT INJECTED NATURAL GAS INTO UNDERGROUND STORAGE IN CENTRAL ILLINOIS AND CAUSING NATURAL GAS TO LEAK INTO THE LOCAL AQUIFER. THE AQUIFER IS ONE OF ILLINOIS' MOST IMPORTANT GROUNDWATER RESOURCES, SERVING AS THE PRIMARY SOURCE OF DRINKING WATER FOR MORE THAN 500,000 PEOPLE IN 15 ILLINOIS COUNTIES AND PROVIDING HUNDREDS OF MILLIONS OF GALLON
