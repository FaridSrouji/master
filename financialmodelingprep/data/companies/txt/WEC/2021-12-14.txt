WEC ENERGY GROUP'S JAYHAWK WIND FARM GOES INTO SERVICE. MILWAUKEE, DEC. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE 190-MEGAWATT JAYHAWK WIND FARM IN BOURBON AND CRAWFORD COUNTIES, KANSAS, HAS ACHIEVED COMMERCIAL OPERATION. JAYHAWK IS GENERATING RENEWABLE ENERGY THAT IS BEING SOLD UNDER LONG-TERM CONTRACT TO META, FORMERLY THE FACEBOOK COMPANY.
