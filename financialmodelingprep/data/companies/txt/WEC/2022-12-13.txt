WEC ENERGY GROUP ANNOUNCES PLAN TO INCREASE DIVIDEND BY 7.2 PERCENT. MILWAUKEE , DEC. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS PLANNING TO RAISE THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK TO 78 CENTS PER SHARE IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023. THIS WOULD REPRESENT AN INCREASE OF 5.25 CENTS PER SHARE, OR 7.2 PERCENT.
