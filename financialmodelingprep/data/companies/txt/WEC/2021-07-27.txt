WEC ENERGY GROUP TO ACQUIRE 90% OWNERSHIP OF SAPPHIRE SKY WIND ENERGY CENTER. MILWAUKEE, JULY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS AGREED TO ACQUIRE A 90% OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN THE SAPPHIRE SKY WIND ENERGY CENTER. LOCATED IN MCLEAN COUNTY, ILLINOIS, THE PROJECT IS BEING DEVELOPED BY INVENERGY — A LEADING GLOBAL DEVELOPER AND OPERATOR OF SUSTAINABLE ENERGY SOLUTIONS.
