TRANSDIGM GROUP REPORTS FISCAL 2022 FOURTH QUARTER AND YEAR-END RESULTS. CLEVELAND , NOV. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRANSDIGM GROUP INCORPORATED (NYSE: TDG), A LEADING GLOBAL DESIGNER, PRODUCER AND SUPPLIER OF HIGHLY ENGINEERED AIRCRAFT COMPONENTS, TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2022. FOURTH QUARTER HIGHLIGHTS INCLUDE: NET SALES OF $1,510 MILLION, UP 18% FROM $1,279 MILLION IN THE PRIOR YEAR'S QUARTER; INCOME FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $266 MILLION, UP 27% FROM $209 MILLION IN THE PRIOR YEAR'S QUARTER; EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $3.98, UP 11% FROM $3.58 IN THE PRIOR YEAR'S QUARTER; EBITDA AS DEFINED OF $752 MILLION, UP 18% FROM $636 MILLION IN THE PRIOR YEAR'S QUARTER; AND ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $5.50, UP 29% FROM $4.25 IN THE PRIOR YEAR'S QUARTER.
