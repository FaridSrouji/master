TRANSDIGM GROUP REPORTS FISCAL 2020 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS.Q2 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $5.10.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE $5.63 FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS.Q2 SALES $1.443 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.45 BILLION.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $4.36 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FISCAL 2020 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE WITHDRAWN DUE TO COVID-19 PANDEMIC.TRANSDIGM - AS OF MARCH 28, SAW MODEST ADVERSE IMPACT TO COMMERCIAL AFTERMARKET & OEM SALES FROM COVID-19 PANDEMIC.TRANSDIGM - CURRENTLY EXPECTS COVID-19 PANDEMIC TO HAVE SIGNIFICANT ADVERSE IMPACT ON SALES, EBITDA AS DEFINED & NET INCOME FOR REMAINDER FISCAL 2020.IN NEAR TERM, OUTBREAK AND WORSENING OF COVID-19 PANDEMIC WILL ADVERSELY IMPACT OUR COMMERCIAL AFTERMARKET SALES.BELIEVE THAT COVID-19 PANDEMIC WILL ALSO ADVERSELY IMPACT OUR COMMERCIAL OEM SALES OVER LONG TERM.REDUCTION IN WORKFORCE BY UP TO 15% TO ALIGN OPERATIONS WITH CUSTOMER DEMAND.TRANSDIGM GROUP - IMPLEMENTING ONE TO EIGHT-WEEK FURLOUGHS AT MANY BUSINESSES OVER ABOUT NEXT SIX MONTHS.TRANSDIGM GROUP - ENDED Q2 OF FISCAL 2020 WITH A CASH BALANCE OF ABOUT $2.7 BILLION & OVER $500 MILLION OF CAPACITY AVAILABLE ON REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.
