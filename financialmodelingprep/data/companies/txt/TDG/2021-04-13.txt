SEARCHLIGHT CAPITAL PARTNERS TO ACQUIRE TECHNICAL AIRBORNE COMPONENTS FROM TRANSDIGM GROUP. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TECHNICAL AIRBORNE COMPONENTS (“TAC”, “THE COMPANY”), A LEADING DESIGNER AND MANUFACTURER OF RODS AND STRUTS FOR THE AEROSPACE INDUSTRY, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT SEARCHLIGHT CAPITAL PARTNERS (“SEARCHLIGHT”), A LEADING GLOBAL PRIVATE INVESTMENT FIRM, HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE THE COMPANY FROM TRANSDIGM GROUP INCORPORATED ("TRANSDIGM GROUP") (NYSE: TDG).
