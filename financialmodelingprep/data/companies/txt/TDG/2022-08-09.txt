TRANSDIGM GROUP REPORTS FISCAL 2022 THIRD QUARTER RESULTS AND DECLARES A SPECIAL CASH DIVIDEND OF $18.50 PER SHARE. CLEVELAND , AUG. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRANSDIGM GROUP INCORPORATED (NYSE: TDG), A LEADING GLOBAL DESIGNER, PRODUCER AND SUPPLIER OF HIGHLY ENGINEERED AIRCRAFT COMPONENTS, TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED JULY 2, 2022. THE COMPANY ALSO ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED AND DECLARED A SPECIAL CASH DIVIDEND OF $18.50 ON EACH OUTSTANDING SHARE OF COMMON STOCK AND CASH DIVIDEND EQUIVALENT PAYMENTS UNDER OPTIONS GRANTED UNDER ITS STOCK OPTIONS PLANS.
