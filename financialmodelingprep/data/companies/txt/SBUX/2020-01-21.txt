STARBUCKS SAYS WILL EXPAND PLANT-BASED OPTIONS, MIGRATING TOWARD MORE ENVIRONMENTALLY-FRIENDLY MENU.STARBUCKS SAYS WILL SHIFT FROM SINGLE-USE TO REUSABLE PACKAGING; SAYS WILL INVEST IN BETTER WAYS TO MANAGE ITS WASTE.STARBUCKS SAYS PRELIMINARY 2030 TARGETS INCLUDE 50% REDUCTION IN CARBON EMISSIONS IN DIRECT OPERATIONS, SUPPLY CHAIN.STARBUCKS SAYS PRELIMINARY 2030 TARGETS ALSO INCLUDE 50% REDUCTION IN WASTE SENT TO LANDFILL FROM STORES, MANUFACTURING.
