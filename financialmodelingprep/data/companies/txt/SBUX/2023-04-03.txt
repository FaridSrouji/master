STARBUCKS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STARBUCKS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: SBUX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.53 PER SHARE OF OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE IN CASH ON MAY 26, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 12, 2023. ABOUT STARBUCKS SINCE 1971, STARBUCKS COFFEE COMPANY HAS BEEN COMMITTED TO ETHICALLY SOURCING AND ROASTING HIGH-QUALITY ARABICA COFFEE.
