STARBUCKS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STARBUCKS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: SBUX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A DIVIDEND OF $0.49 PER SHARE OF OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE IN CASH ON FEBRUARY 25, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 11, 2022.
