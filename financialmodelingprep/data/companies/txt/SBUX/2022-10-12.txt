DELTA AIR LINES AND STARBUCKS LAUNCH LOYALTY PARTNERSHIP. ATLANTA & SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, DELTA AIR LINES (NYSE: DAL) AND STARBUCKS COFFEE COMPANY (NASDAQ: SBUX) ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP THAT WILL OFFER MEMBERS OF DELTA SKYMILES AND STARBUCKS® REWARDS, TWO OF AMERICA'S MOST HIGHLY REGARDED LOYALTY PROGRAMS, THE ABILITY TO UNLOCK EVEN MORE WAYS TO EARN REWARDS AT DELTA AND STARBUCKS.
