STARBUCKS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STARBUCKS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: SBUX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A DIVIDEND OF $0.45 PER SHARE OF OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE IN CASH ON MARCH 5, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 18, 2021.
