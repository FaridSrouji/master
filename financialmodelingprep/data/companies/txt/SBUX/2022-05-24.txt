BOLTHOUSE FARMS ACQUIRES EVOLUTION FRESH FROM STARBUCKS. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STARBUCKS (NASDAQ: SBUX) AND BOLTHOUSE FARMS TODAY ANNOUNCED ENTRY INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT FOR BOLTHOUSE FARMS TO ACQUIRE THE BRAND AND BUSINESS OF EVOLUTION FRESH. THE PARTNERS (EMPLOYEES) THAT SUPPORT THE BUSINESS WILL ALSO TRANSITION TO BOLTHOUSE FARMS UPON CLOSE OF THE TRANSACTION.
