STARBUCKS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. SEATTLE--( BUSINESS WIRE )--STARBUCKS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: SBUX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.53 PER SHARE OF OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE IN CASH ON FEBRUARY 24, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 10, 2023.
