AMCOR ANNOUNCES GLOBAL PRICE INCREASES. ZURICH, DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMCOR (NYSE: AMCR) (ASX: AMC) TODAY CONFIRMED PRICE INCREASES OF UP TO 15% FOR PRODUCTS ACROSS ITS GLOBAL BUSINESSES EFFECTIVE JAN. 1, 2022. THE INCREASES WILL VARY DEPENDING ON CIRCUMSTANCES PER CUSTOMER, REGION AND PRODUCT TYPE.
