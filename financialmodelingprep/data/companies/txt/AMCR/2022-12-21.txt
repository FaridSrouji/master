AMCOR PARTNERS WITH LICELLA TO DELIVER RECYCLED CONTENT FROM ONE OF AUSTRALIA'S FIRST PLASTIC ADVANCED RECYCLING FACILITIES. MELBOURNE, AUSTRALIA , DEC. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMCOR (NYSE: AMCR) (ASX:AMC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN RESPONSIBLE PACKAGING SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS AMCOR AUSTRALIA & NEW ZEALAND (ANZ) BUSINESS HAS SIGNED A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) WITH AUSTRALIAN-BASED TECHNOLOGY PIONEERS, LICELLA HOLDINGS, TO INVEST IN ONE OF AUSTRALIA'S FIRST PLASTIC ADVANCED RECYCLING FACILITIES. AS ONE OF THE FIRST ADVANCED RECYCLING FACILITIES TO BE BUILT IN AUSTRALIA, THE AGREEMENT SUPPORTS AMCOR AND LICELLA'S COMMITMENT TO CREATING A LOCAL CIRCULAR ECONOMY FOR SOFT PLASTICS IN AUSTRALIA AND HELPS ADVANCE AMCOR'S TARGET TO ACHIEVE 30% RECYCLED CONTENT ACROSS ITS PORTFOLIO BY 2030.
