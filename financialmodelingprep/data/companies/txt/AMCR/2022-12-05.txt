AMCOR DRIVES PROGRESS ON ADVANCED RECYCLING MATERIALS FOR FOOD AND HEALTHCARE MARKETS. FIVE-YEAR DEAL TO SECURE THOUSANDS OF TONS OF HIGH-PERFORMANCE, CERTIFIED-CIRCULAR MATERIAL FROM EXXONMOBIL'S ADVANCED RECYCLING PLANT ZURICH , DEC. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE / -- AMCOR (NYSE: AMCR, ASX: AMC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN DEVELOPING AND PRODUCING RESPONSIBLE PACKAGING SOLUTIONS, HAS TODAY ANNOUNCED A FIVE-YEAR DEAL WITH EXXONMOBIL TO PURCHASE CERTIFIED-CIRCULAR POLYETHYLENE MATERIAL IN SUPPORT OF ITS TARGET TO ACHIEVE 30% RECYCLED MATERIAL ACROSS ITS PORTFOLIO BY 2030. THE VOLUME OF MATERIAL WILL INCREASE INCREMENTALLY EACH YEAR, AND IS EXPECTED TO REACH 100,000 METRIC TONS ANNUALLY AT THE END OF THE FIVE-YEAR PERIOD.
