AMCOR ANNOUNCES BREAKTHROUGH HEALTHCARE LIDDING TECHNOLOGY FOR COMBINATION PRODUCTS. ZURICH, AUG. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMCOR, A GLOBAL LEADER IN DEVELOPING AND PRODUCING A DIVERSE OFFERING OF RESPONSIBLE PACKAGING SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A PROPRIETARY HEALTHCARE LIDDING TECHNOLOGY THAT WILL BE UTILIZED FOR COMBINATION PRODUCTS – THOSE CONSISTING OF TWO OR MORE REGULATED COMPONENTS (DEVICE, DRUG OR BIOLOGIC). THIS LATEST INNOVATION FROM AMCOR IS BASED ON A PATENTED INERT FILM DEVELOPMENT AND LAMINATE DESIGN.
