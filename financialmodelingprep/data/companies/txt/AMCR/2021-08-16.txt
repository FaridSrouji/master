AMCOR EXPANDS GLOBAL NETWORK OF INNOVATION CENTERS. ZURICH, AUG. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMCOR (NYSE: AMCR) (ASX: AMC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN DEVELOPING AND PRODUCING RESPONSIBLE PACKAGING, TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO BUILD TWO NEW STATE-OF-THE-ART INNOVATION CENTERS. THE NEW FACILITIES IN GHENT, BELGIUM, AND JIANGYIN, CHINA, WILL WELCOME CUSTOMERS AS OF MID-2022, WITH FULL BUILD-OUT OVER THE NEXT TWO YEARS.
