AMCOR BUYS WORLD-CLASS SCALABLE FLEXIBLE PACKAGING PLANT IN CENTRAL EUROPE. INVESTMENT SUPPORTS CUSTOMER DEMAND ACROSS EUROPE IN ATTRACTIVE SEGMENTS, INCLUDING COFFEE AND PET FOOD ZURICH , AUG. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMCOR (NYSE: AMCR) (ASX:AMC), A GLOBAL LEADER IN DEVELOPING AND PRODUCING RESPONSIBLE PACKAGING SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS CLOSED THE ACQUISITION OF A WORLD-CLASS FLEXIBLE PACKAGING PLANT LOCATED IN THE CZECH REPUBLIC. THE STRATEGIC LOCATION OF THE SITE IMMEDIATELY INCREASES AMCOR'S ABILITY TO SATISFY STRONG DEMAND AND CUSTOMER GROWTH ACROSS ITS FLEXIBLE PACKAGING NETWORK IN EUROPE.
