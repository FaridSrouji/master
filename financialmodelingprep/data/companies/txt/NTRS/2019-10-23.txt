NORTHERN TRUST CORPORATION APPOINTS JENNIFER GRAVENOR AS ITS AUSTIN REGION PRESIDENT AND SENIOR MANAGING DIRECTOR. NORTHERN TRUST CORPORATION HAS ANNOUNCED HAS APPOINTED JENNIFER GRAVENOR AS ITS AUSTIN REGION PRESIDENT AND SENIOR MANAGING DIRECTOR. GRAVENOR WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR LEADING THE FIRM’S TEAM IN AUSTIN IN ITS DELIVERY OF HOLISTIC ADVICE, OUTSTANDING CLIENT SERVICE AND GROWTH OF THE WEALTH MANAGEMENT BUSINESS ACROSS THE REGION. GRAVENOR PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS A SENIOR WEALTH STRATEGIST FOR NORTHERN TRUST, ADVISING AFFLUENT INDIVIDUALS, FAMILIES, BUSINESS OWNERS, EXECUTIVES AND CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS IN COMPREHENSIVE WEALTH MANAGEMENT, COMPLEX PRIVATE BANKING, AND TRUST SERVICES.
