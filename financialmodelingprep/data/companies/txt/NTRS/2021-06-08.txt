NORTHERN TRUST NAMES JULIE ZHU AS BEIJING BRANCH MANAGER. BEIJING--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) HAS APPOINTED JULIE ZHU TO LEAD ITS ASSET SERVICING BUSINESS IN CHINA. ZHU, WHO IS BASED IN BEIJING, ASSUMES THE ROLE OF BRANCH MANAGER FROM MICHAEL WU WHO WAS MOST RECENTLY COUNTRY EXECUTIVE FOR GREATER CHINA. SHE WILL REPORT TO ANGELO CALVITTO WHO WAS RECENTLY APPOINTED HEAD OF THE ASIA-PACIFIC REGION. “WE'RE DELIGHTED TO APPOINT JULIE AS BRANCH MANAGER FOR BEIJING.
