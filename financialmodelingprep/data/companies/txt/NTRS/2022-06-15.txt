NORTHERN TRUST ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGES. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST ANNOUNCED LEADERSHIP CHANGES TODAY TO ACCELERATE THE EXECUTION OF STRATEGIC PLANS. LONGTIME NORTHERN TRUST LEADERS DAVE BLOWERS, PRESIDENT OF NATIONAL SERVICES IN WEALTH MANAGEMENT, AND LYLE LOGAN, MANAGING DIRECTOR, FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS GROUP, HAVE BEEN NAMED AS VICE CHAIRMEN. THEIR RESPONSIBILITIES INCLUDE KEY STRATEGIC INITIATIVES AND THE DEVELOPMENT OF SELECT CLIENTS AND PROSPECTS AROUND THE GLOBE, WITH BLOWERS CONTINUING TO REPORT TO WEALTH MANAGE
