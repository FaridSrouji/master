NORTHERN TRUST TO OFFER INTEGRATION WITH SIMCORP AS PART OF ITS WHOLE OFFICE™ STRATEGY. LONDON & COPENHAGEN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS PART OF ITS CONTINUING WHOLE OFFICE™ STRATEGY TO SERVE CLIENT NEEDS ACROSS THE ENTIRE INVESTMENT LIFECYCLE, NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) HAS REACHED AN AGREEMENT WITH SIMCORP®, A LEADING PROVIDER OF SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SAAS) INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS, TO ENABLE INTEGRATION FOR EFFICIENT DATA EXCHANGE FOR CLIENTS USING THE SIMCORP DIMENSION® PLATFORM.
