NORTHERN TRUST ASSET MANAGEMENT NAMES CLIENT PORTFOLIO MANAGER FOR MUNI TEAM. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORTHERN TRUST ASSET MANAGEMENT (NTAM), A LEADING GLOBAL INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT FIRM WITH $1.0 TRILLION IN ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2022, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 9, JOHN CEFFALIO HAS BEEN HIRED AS HEAD OF MUNICIPAL CLIENT PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT, A NEWLY CREATED POSITION WITHIN ITS GLOBAL FIXED INCOME GROUP.
