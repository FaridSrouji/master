NORTHERN TRUST RELEASES 2022 PHILANTHROPIC IMPACT REPORT. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) DONATED MORE THAN $165 MILLION OVER THE PAST DECADE TO NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATIONS, INCLUDING MORE THAN $19 MILLION IN 2022 TO ORGANIZATIONS WORKING TO PROVIDE FOOD SECURITY, AFFORDABLE HOUSING, ACCESSIBLE HEALTHCARE AND QUALITY EDUCATION. ADDITIONALLY, NORTHERN TRUST EMPLOYEES HAVE PROVIDED MORE THAN 1 MILLION HOURS OF VOLUNTEER SERVICE OVER THE PAST DECADE.
