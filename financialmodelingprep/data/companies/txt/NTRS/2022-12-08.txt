NORTHERN TRUST APPOINTS MONICA SAFAPOUR MARKET PRESIDENT IN SANTA BARBARA. SANTA BARBARA, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORTHERN TRUST HAS APPOINTED MONICA SAFAPOUR MARKET PRESIDENT IN THE SANTA BARBARA OFFICE, WHERE SHE WILL LEAD THE TEAM IN THE DELIVERY OF OUTSTANDING CLIENT SERVICE AND ACCELERATE THE GROWTH OF THE NORTHERN TRUST WEALTH MANAGEMENT PLATFORM.
