NORTHERN TRUST OBTAINS HARD-TO-FIND TECHNICAL TALENT FOR BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE ANALYSIS & DECISION MAKING WITH CAI'S AUTISM2WORK PROGRAM. ALLENTOWN, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #A2W--NORTHERN TRUST HAS SELECTED CAI'S AUTISM2WORK PROGRAM TO PROVIDE INDIVIDUALS WITH AUTISM EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITIES IN CRITICAL TECHNICAL AREAS.
