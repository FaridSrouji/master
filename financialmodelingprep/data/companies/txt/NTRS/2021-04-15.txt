NORTHERN TRUST TO DEPLOY ELECTRONIC FOREIGN EXCHANGE PRICING ENGINE IN SINGAPORE. SINGAPORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL SET UP A NEW ELECTRONIC FOREIGN EXCHANGE (EFX) PRICING ENGINE IN SINGAPORE, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE MONETARY AUTHORITY OF SINGAPORE (MAS).
