NORTHERN TRUST APPOINTS KHOLOUD AL DOSARI AS COUNTRY HEAD, SAUDI ARABIA. RIYADH, SAUDI ARABIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) HAS APPOINTED KHOLOUD AL DOSARI AS COUNTRY HEAD FOR NORTHERN TRUST IN THE KINGDOM OF SAUDI ARABIA. BASED IN RIYADH, AL DOSARI OVERSEES THE STRATEGIC DEVELOPMENT, BUSINESS GROWTH AND OPERATIONS OF NORTHERN TRUST'S ASSET SERVICING BUSINESS IN THE COUNTRY.
