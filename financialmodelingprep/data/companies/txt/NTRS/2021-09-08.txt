NORTHERN TRUST'S 5-YEAR MARKET FORECAST CALLS FOR GLOBAL ECONOMY TO GROW BY 2.9%. CHICAGO & LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE GLOBAL ECONOMY WILL EXPERIENCE ANNUALIZED REAL GROWTH OF 2.9% OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS, ALONG WITH ANNUALIZED INFLATION OF 1.7% IN DEVELOPED MARKETS, ACCORDING TO NORTHERN TRUST'S CAPITAL MARKET ASSUMPTIONS REPORT, WHICH IS A FIVE-YEAR OUTLOOK. THE FIRM PREDICTS THE PERSISTENTLY LOW INTEREST RATES WILL FOSTER POSITIVE BUT LOW RETURNS, WITH TECHNOLOGY REMAINING A POTENT FORCE THAT WILL MUTE INFLATION.
