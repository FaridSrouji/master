NORTHERN TRUST MAKES KEY FUND ACCOUNTING AND FUND ADMINISTRATION HIRES IN NORTH AMERICA GLOBAL FUND SERVICES. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORTHERN TRUST TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENTS OF STEVEN REYDEL AS HEAD OF NORTH AMERICA FUND ACCOUNTING AND MICHAEL MCMASTER AS HEAD OF NORTH AMERICA FUND ADMINISTRATION. LEVERAGING THEIR EXPERTISE IN FUND ACCOUNTING AND FUND ADMINISTRATION, REYDEL AND MCMASTER WILL PLAY A PIVOTAL ROLE IN SUPPORTING ONGOING GROWTH IN THE GLOBAL FUND SERVICES (GFS) BUSINESS.
