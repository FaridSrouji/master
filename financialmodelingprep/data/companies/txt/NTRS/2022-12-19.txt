NORTHERN TRUST 2023 OUTLOOK: MARKETS AND ECONOMY PIVOT FROM INFLATION AND MONETARY POLICY FEARS TO A WEAK GLOBAL ECONOMY. CHICAGO & LONDON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORTHERN TRUST, A LEADING GLOBAL ASSET MANAGER WITH US$1.2 TRILLION IN ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT, EXPECTS 2023 TO BE A TURBULENT YEAR AS CONDITIONS PIVOT FROM INFLATION AND MONETARY POLICY FEARS TO A WEAK GLOBAL ECONOMY, BUT THE FIRM ALSO EXPECTS MARKET VOLATILITY TO SOMEWHAT TEMPER DUE TO LOWER INFLATION AND A PAUSE IN CENTRAL BANK INTEREST RATE INCREASES. A REDUCTION IN RATES IS NOT SEEN AS LIKELY.
NORTHERN TRUST RELEASES SECOND ANNUAL BUSINESS OWNER BENCHMARK. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE RESULTS OF ITS SECOND ANNUAL BUSINESS OWNER BENCHMARK, A SURVEY OF MORE THAN 150 BUSINESS OWNERS CONDUCTED BY THE NORTHERN TRUST INSTITUTE. THE REPORT, BASED ON THE SURVEY CONDUCTED IN THIRD QUARTER OF 2022, PROVIDES AN INSIDE VIEW ACROSS FOUR KEY THEMES ON HOW BUSINESS OWNERS AND ENTREPRENEURS ARE DEPLOYING THEIR WEALTH AND WHAT DRIVES THEM AS THEY LOOK TO THE FUTURE.
