NORTHERN TRUST OFFERS CLIENTS COMPREHENSIVE VIEW OF DISTRIBUTION ACTIVITY. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH FINSCAPE TO PROVIDE CLIENTS WITH A COMPREHENSIVE VIEW OF THEIR FUND DISTRIBUTION ACTIVITY. BY STREAMLINING MARKET INTELLIGENCE GATHERING, THE SERVICE – ALSO CALLED FINSCAPE – AIMS TO HELP ADDRESS INDUSTRY-WIDE CHALLENGES AROUND DATA TRANSPARENCY.
