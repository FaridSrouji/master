NORTHERN TRUST RAISES PRIME RATE. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE NORTHERN TRUST COMPANY HAS INCREASED ITS PRIME RATE FROM 3.50% TO 4.00%, EFFECTIVE THURSDAY, MAY 5, 2022.
