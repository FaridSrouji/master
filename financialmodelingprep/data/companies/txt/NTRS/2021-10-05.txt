NORTHERN TRUST APPOINTS GARY PAULIN AS HEAD OF GLOBAL STRATEGIC SOLUTIONS. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) HAS APPOINTED GARY PAULIN AS HEAD OF GLOBAL STRATEGIC SOLUTIONS FOR ITS ASSET SERVICING BUSINESS. IN THIS NEWLY-CREATED POSITION, PAULIN, WHO IS BASED IN LONDON, WILL LEAD THE DEVELOPMENT AND DESIGN OF INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS THAT WILL ENABLE CLIENTS TO ACCESS AND BENEFIT FROM THE FULL RANGE OF NORTHERN TRUST'S GLOBAL CAPABILITIES.
