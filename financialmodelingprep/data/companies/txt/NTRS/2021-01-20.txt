NORTHERN TRUST DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS ON COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST CORPORATION (NASDAQ: NTRS), HOLDING COMPANY OF THE NORTHERN TRUST COMPANY, HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.70 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK ($1.66-2/3 PAR VALUE), PAYABLE ON APRIL 1, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT 5:00 P.M., CHICAGO TIME, ON MARCH 12, 2021.
