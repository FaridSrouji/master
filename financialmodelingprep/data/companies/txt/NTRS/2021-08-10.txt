NORTHERN TRUST ASSET MANAGEMENT RECOGNIZED BY INVESTMENTNEWS AS A 2021 EXCELLENCE IN DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION AWARD WINNER. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INVESTMENTNEWS HAS RECOGNIZED NORTHERN TRUST ASSET MANAGEMENT (NTAM) AS A 2021 EXCELLENCE IN DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION AWARD WINNER. NTAM PRESIDENT SHUNDRAWN THOMAS WAS HONORED AS THE RECIPIENT OF THE LIFETIME ACHIEVEMENT AWARD AND, FOR THE THIRD TIME IN THE FOUR YEARS SINCE THE AWARDS WERE INTRODUCED, NTAM WAS NAMED A DIVERSITY CHAMPION. THE AWARDS AIM TO TELL THE STORIES OF INDIVIDUALS AND FIRMS THAT HAVE USED THEIR SUCCESSES, LEADERSHIP SKILLS, AND WILLINGNESS
