NORTHERN TRUST ANNOUNCES NEW PRESIDENT OF ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DANIEL GAMBA WILL SERVE AS NORTHERN TRUST'S NEW PRESIDENT OF ASSET MANAGEMENT (NTAM), EFFECTIVE APRIL 3. HE WILL JOIN NORTHERN TRUST'S MANAGEMENT GROUP AND REPORT TO CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER MICHAEL O'GRADY. GAMBA JOINS NORTHERN TRUST FROM BLACKROCK, INC.
