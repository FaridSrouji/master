NORTHERN TRUST PROVIDES MIDDLE OFFICE SERVICES FOR MARTIN CURRIE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT. MARTIN CURRIE IS A LONG-TERM GLOBAL EQUITY GROWTH INVESTOR WITH ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF £15.4 BILLION (APPROXIMATELY US$21.2 BILLION).
