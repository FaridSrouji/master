NORTHERN TRUST ASSET MANAGEMENT INTRODUCES ESG VECTOR SCORE. LONDON & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST ASSET MANAGEMENT (NTAM) INTRODUCED TODAY THE NORTHERN TRUST ESG VECTOR SCORETM, A MEASUREMENT THAT ASSESSES PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANIES IN THE CONTEXT OF FINANCIALLY RELEVANT ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) RELATED CRITERIA THAT COULD IMPACT THEIR OPERATING PERFORMANCE. IT CAN BE USED IN CONSTRUCTING AND MANAGING INVESTMENT PORTFOLIOS AND STEWARDSHIP ACTIVITIES. THE ESG VECTOR SCORE FOCUSES ON THE MAGNITUDE AND DIRECTION OF KEY ESG RELA
