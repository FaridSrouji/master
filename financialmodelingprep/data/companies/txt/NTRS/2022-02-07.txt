NORTHERN TRUST ASSET MANAGEMENT'S FLEXSHARES® EXPANDS LISTING OF PRIVATE EQUITY ETF. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST ASSET MANAGEMENT'S FLEXSHARES EXCHANGE TRADED FUNDS TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LISTING OF THE FLEXSHARES® LISTED PRIVATE EQUITY UCITS ETF (TICKER: FLPE), THE FIRM'S FIRST EXCHANGE TRADED FUND (ETF) THAT OFFERS INVESTORS DIRECT EXPOSURE TO THE LISTED PRIVATE EQUITY MARKET.
