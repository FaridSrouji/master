PNC APPOINTS RENU KHATOR TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. PITTSBURGH , MAY 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) APPOINTED RENU KHATOR, PH.D., CHANCELLOR OF THE UNIVERSITY OF HOUSTON SYSTEM AND PRESIDENT OF UNIVERSITY OF HOUSTON, TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE TODAY.
