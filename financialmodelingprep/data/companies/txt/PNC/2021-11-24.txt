PNC ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF SENIOR NOTES DUE DECEMBER 9, 2022. PITTSBURGH, NOV. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE REDEMPTION ON DEC. 9, 2021, OF THE FOLLOWING SENIOR NOTES ISSUED BY PNC BANK, NATIONAL ASSOCIATION: ALL OUTSTANDING SENIOR FIXED RATE/FLOATING RATE NOTES DUE DEC. 9, 2022, IN THE AMOUNT OF $650,000,000 (CUSIP 69353RFS2). THE SECURITIES HAVE A DISTRIBUTION RATE OF 2.028% AND AN ORIGINAL SCHEDULED MATURITY DATE OF DEC. 9, 2022.
