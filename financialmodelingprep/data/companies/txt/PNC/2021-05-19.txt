HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES TRANSACTION DATA SYSTEMS ON ITS SALE TO BLACKROCK LONG TERM PRIVATE CAPITAL. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED TRANSACTION DATA SYSTEMS (TDS), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF GTCR, ON ITS SALE TO BLACKROCK LONG TERM PRIVATE CAPITAL (BLACKROCK LTPC). TDS IS A LEADER IN PHARMACY SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS AND SERVICES. THE TRANSACTION WAS LED BY SAM HENDLER, DAN LINSALATA, RYAN COSTA AND REBECCA EVANS OF THE HARRIS WILLIAMS TECHNOLOGY GROUP AND PAUL HEPPER OF THE FIRM'S HEALTHCARE &
