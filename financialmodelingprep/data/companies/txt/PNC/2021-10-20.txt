HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES BLUSKY RESTORATION CONTRACTORS, LLC ON ITS SALE TO PARTNERS GROUP AND KOHLBERG & COMPANY, LLC. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED BLUSKY RESTORATION CONTRACTORS, LLC (BLUSKY), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF DOMINUS CAPITAL, ON ITS SALE TO PARTNERS GROUP, A LEADING GLOBAL PRIVATE MARKETS FIRM ACTING ON BEHALF OF ITS CLIENTS, AND KOHLBERG & COMPANY, LLC (KOHLBERG), A LEADING PRIVATE EQUITY FIRM.
