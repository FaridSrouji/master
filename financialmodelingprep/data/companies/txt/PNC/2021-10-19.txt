HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES AGAPE CARE GROUP ON ITS SALE TO RIDGEMONT EQUITY PARTNERS. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED AGAPE CARE GROUP (AGAPE CARE), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF THE VISTRIA GROUP (VISTRIA), ON ITS SALE TO RIDGEMONT EQUITY PARTNERS (RIDGEMONT). AGAPE CARE IS A LEADER IN HOSPICE AND PALLIATIVE CARE IN THE SOUTHEAST.
