DAILYPAY, PNC BANK AND THE CLEARING HOUSE TEAM UP TO PROVIDE REAL-TIME ACCESS TO EARNED PAY FOR MILLIONS OF AMERICAN WORKERS. NEW YORK, JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DAILYPAY, THE LEADER IN ON-DEMAND PAY, HAS TEAMED UP WITH PNC BANK AND THE CLEARING HOUSE TO OFFER REAL-TIME PAYMENTS OVER THE RTP ®  NETWORK, CREATING THE ABILITY TO FULFILL EMPLOYEE PAYMENT NEEDS 24/7/365 WITH UNMATCHED SPEED AND ACCURACY.  "WE ARE CONSTANTLY EXPLORING WAYS TO INNOVATE OUR INDUSTRY-LEADING TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM AND BUILD UPON THE GOLD-STANDARD SERVICE WE PROVIDE OUR CLIENT PARTNERS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES," SAID RON MUNKITTRICK, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, EXTERNAL OPERATIONS, DAILYPAY.
