HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES OWEN EQUIPMENT COMPANY ON ITS SALE TO WYNNCHURCH CAPITAL, LTD.. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED OWEN EQUIPMENT COMPANY (OWEN), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF CENTERGATE CAPITAL (CENTERGATE), ON ITS SALE TO WYNNCHURCH CAPITAL, LTD. (WYNNCHURCH). OWEN IS A LEADING SPECIALTY EQUIPMENT PLATFORM PROVIDING CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE SOLUTIONS TO MUNICIPAL, UTILITY AND COMMERCIAL CUSTOMERS THROUGHOUT THE WESTERN UNITED STATES. THE TRANSACTION WAS LED BY LUKE SEMPLE, NEHA
