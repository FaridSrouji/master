HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES MAGNATE WORLDWIDE ON ITS SALE TO LITTLEJOHN & CO. LLC. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED MAGNATE WORLDWIDE (MWW), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF CIVC PARTNERS, L.P. (CIVC), ON ITS SALE TO LITTLEJOHN & CO. LLC (LITTLEJOHN). MWW IS A DIVERSIFIED SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT COMPANY OFFERING HIGHLY SPECIALIZED INTERNATIONAL AND DOMESTIC THIRD PARTY LOGISTICS (3PL) SOLUTIONS TO SHIPPERS AROUND THE WORLD.
