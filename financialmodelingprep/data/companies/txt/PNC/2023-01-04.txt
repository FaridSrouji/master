PNC DECLARES DIVIDEND OF $1.50 ON COMMON STOCK. PITTSBURGH , JAN. 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK OF $1.50 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND, WITH A PAYMENT DATE OF FEB. 5, 2023, WILL BE PAYABLE THE NEXT BUSINESS DAY TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS JAN. 17, 2023.
