HARRIS WILLIAMS ENHANCES SENIOR CAPABILITIES ACROSS THE FIRM WITH 26 PROMOTIONS. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES THE PROMOTION OF 26 SENIOR PROFESSIONALS FROM THE FIRM'S FRANKFURT, GERMANY; LONDON; MINNEAPOLIS; RICHMOND, VIRGINIA; SAN FRANCISCO; AND WASHINGTON, D.C. OFFICES.
