HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES WEST STAR AVIATION ON ITS SALE TO THE STERLING GROUP. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED WEST STAR AVIATION (WEST STAR), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF NORWEST EQUITY PARTNERS (NEP), ON ITS SALE TO THE STERLING GROUP (STERLING). WEST STAR IS A LEADING INDEPENDENT PROVIDER OF MAINTENANCE, REPAIRS AND OPERATIONS (MRO) SERVICES TO THE BUSINESS AVIATION INDUSTRY.
