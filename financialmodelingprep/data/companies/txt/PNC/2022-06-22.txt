HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES BAMBOO ROSE ON ITS STRATEGIC INVESTMENT FROM RUBICON TECHNOLOGY PARTNERS. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED BAMBOO ROSE, A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF MCCARTHY CAPITAL (MCCARTHY), ON ITS STRATEGIC INVESTMENT FROM RUBICON TECHNOLOGY PARTNERS (RUBICON). BAMBOO ROSE IS A LEADING UNIFIED PRODUCT LIFECYCLE AND SUPPLY CHAIN SOFTWARE PLATFORM FOR RETAILERS, BRANDS, AND SUPPLIERS.
