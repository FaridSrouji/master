PNC RAISES COMMON DIVIDEND TO $1.25 PER SHARE. PITTSBURGH, JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK OF $1.25 PER SHARE, AN INCREASE OF $0.10 PER SHARE, OR 9%, FROM THE SECOND QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $1.15 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE AUG. 5, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS JULY 16, 2021.
