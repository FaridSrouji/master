PNC DECLARES DIVIDEND OF $1.25 ON COMMON STOCK. PITTSBURGH, OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK OF $1.25 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE NOV. 5, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS OCT. 15, 2021.
