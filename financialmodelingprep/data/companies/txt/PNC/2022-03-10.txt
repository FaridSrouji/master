SQUEEZED BY INFLATION, SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS EXPECT TO RAISE PRICES; SUPPLY CHAIN DISRUPTIONS TO EASE, PNC SURVEY SHOWS. PITTSBURGH, MARCH 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SUPPLY CHAIN ISSUES TOP THE LIST OF CONCERNS FOR SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS OVER THE LAST SIX MONTHS BUT THEY SEE THOSE PRESSURES EASING BY MID-YEAR. IN THE MEANTIME, INFLATION PRESSURES ARE EXPECTED TO CONTINUE TO IMPACT THESE BUSINESS OWNERS, WITH A MAJORITY PLANNING TO FURTHER RAISE THEIR OWN PRICES IN THE NEAR TERM, ACCORDING TO THE LATEST PNC SEMI-ANNUAL ECONOMIC OUTLOOK SURVEY OF SMALL AND MID-SIZE BUSINESS OWNERS AND EXECUTIVES, WHICH CONCLUDED FEB. 3.
