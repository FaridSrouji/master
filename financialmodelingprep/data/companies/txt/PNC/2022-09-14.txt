HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES PHARMALEX GROUP ON ITS PENDING SALE TO AMERISOURCEBERGEN. PHARMALEX, A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF AUCTUS CAPITAL PARTNERS AG (AUCTUS), IS A LEADING PROVIDER OF SPECIALIZED SERVICES FOR THE PHARMA, BIOTECH, AND MEDTECH INDUSTRIES WORLDWIDE.
