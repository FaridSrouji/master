HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES ADB COMPANIES, LLC ON ITS SALE TO WARREN EQUITY PARTNERS. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED ADB COMPANIES, LLC AND ITS AFFILIATES (ADB), A LEADING TURNKEY PROVIDER OF TELECOMMUNICATION AND UTILITY INFRASTRUCTURE SOLUTIONS IN THE UNITED STATES, ON ITS SALE TO WARREN EQUITY PARTNERS (WARREN EQUITY).
