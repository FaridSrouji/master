PNC DECLARES DIVIDEND OF $1.25 ON COMMON STOCK. PITTSBURGH, JAN. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK OF $1.25 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND, WITH A PAYMENT DATE OF FEB. 5, 2022, WILL BE PAYABLE THE NEXT BUSINESS DAY TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS JAN. 18, 2022.
PNC APPOINTS ROBERT A. NIBLOCK TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. PITTSBURGH, JAN. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) APPOINTED ROBERT A. NIBLOCK TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE TODAY.
