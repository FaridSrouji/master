PNC ISSUES INAUGURAL SOCIAL BOND. PITTSBURGH, AUG. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE CLOSING OF ITS INAUGURAL SOCIAL BOND. THE PROCEEDS WILL BE USED TO FINANCE OR RE-FINANCE ELIGIBLE SOCIAL PROJECTS THAT PROMOTE POSITIVE SOCIAL OUTCOMES AND THAT BENEFIT LOW- TO MODERATE-INCOME (LMI) INDIVIDUALS AND COMMUNITIES, MAJORITY-MINORITY CENSUS TRACTS, AND/OR VULNERABLE OR UNDERSERVED POPULATIONS.
