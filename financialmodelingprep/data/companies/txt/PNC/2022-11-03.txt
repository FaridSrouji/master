HARRIS WILLIAMS ADVISES ST PIERRE GROUPE ON ITS SALE TO GRUPO BIMBO. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED ST PIERRE GROUPE (ST PIERRE), AN INTERNATIONAL MARKET LEADER IN THE BAKERY SECTOR, ON ITS SALE TO GRUPO BIMBO (BMV: BIMBO).
