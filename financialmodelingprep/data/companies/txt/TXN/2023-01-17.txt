INDUSTRY'S FIRST ULTRASONIC LENS CLEANING CHIPSET ENABLES SELF-CLEANING CAMERAS AND SENSORS. DALLAS , JAN. 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TEXAS INSTRUMENTS (TI) (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY INTRODUCED THE FIRST PURPOSE-BUILT SEMICONDUCTORS WITH ULTRASONIC LENS CLEANING (ULC) TECHNOLOGY, ENABLING CAMERA SYSTEMS TO QUICKLY DETECT AND REMOVE DIRT, ICE AND WATER USING MICROSCOPIC VIBRATIONS. REMOVING CONTAMINANTS FROM CAMERA LENSES TRADITIONALLY REQUIRES MANUAL CLEANING, CAUSING SYSTEM DOWNTIME, OR THE USE OF VARIOUS MECHANICAL PARTS THAT COULD MALFUNCTION.
