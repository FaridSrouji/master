TI REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS AND SHAREHOLDER RETURNS. DALLAS, OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INCORPORATED (TI) (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY REPORTED THIRD QUARTER REVENUE OF $4.64 BILLION, NET INCOME OF $1.95 BILLION AND EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $2.07. REGARDING THE COMPANY'S PERFORMANCE AND RETURNS TO SHAREHOLDERS, RICH TEMPLETON, TI'S CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CEO, MADE THE FOLLOWING COMMENTS: "REVENUE INCREASED 22% FROM THE SAME QUARTER A YEAR AGO DUE TO STRONG DEMAND IN INDUSTRIAL, AUTOMOTIVE AND PERSONAL ELECTRONICS.
