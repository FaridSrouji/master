TI BUFFER AMPLIFIER INCREASES SIGNAL BANDWIDTH TENFOLD IN DATA-ACQUISITION SYSTEMS. DALLAS, JAN. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TEXAS INSTRUMENTS (TI) (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY INTRODUCED THE INDUSTRY'S WIDEST-BANDWIDTH HIGH-INPUT-IMPEDANCE (HI-Z) BUFFER AMPLIFIER, CAPABLE OF SUPPORTING FREQUENCY BANDWIDTHS AS HIGH AS 3 GHZ. THE WIDER BANDWIDTH AND HIGH SLEW RATES OF THE BUF802 ENABLE HIGHER SIGNAL THROUGHPUT AND MINIMAL INPUT SETTLING TIME.
