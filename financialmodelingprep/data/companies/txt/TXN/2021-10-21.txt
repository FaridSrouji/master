TEXAS INSTRUMENTS BOARD DECLARES FOURTH QUARTER 2021 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS, OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.15 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE NOV. 15, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOV. 1, 2021.     ABOUT TEXAS INSTRUMENTS TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: TXN) IS A GLOBAL SEMICONDUCTOR COMPANY THAT DESIGNS, MANUFACTURES, TESTS AND SELLS ANALOG AND EMBEDDED PROCESSING CHIPS FOR MARKETS SUCH AS INDUSTRIAL, AUTOMOTIVE, PERSONAL ELECTRONICS, COMMUNICATIONS EQUIPMENT AND ENTERPRISE SYSTEMS.
