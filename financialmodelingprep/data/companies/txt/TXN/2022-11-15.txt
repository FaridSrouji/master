TEXAS INSTRUMENTS PRICES $800 MILLION OF INVESTMENT GRADE NOTES. DALLAS , NOV. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INCORPORATED (TI) (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF TWO SERIES OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES FOR AN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $800 MILLION. THE NOTES CONSIST OF THE FOLLOWING: $300 MILLION OF 4.700% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE NOVEMBER 18, 2024; AND $500 MILLION OF 4.600% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE FEBRUARY 15, 2028.
