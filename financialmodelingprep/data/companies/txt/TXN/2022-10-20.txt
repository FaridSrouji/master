TEXAS INSTRUMENTS BOARD DECLARES FOURTH QUARTER 2022 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS , OCT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.24 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE NOV. 15, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON OCT. 31, 2022.     ABOUT TEXAS INSTRUMENTS TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: TXN) IS A GLOBAL SEMICONDUCTOR COMPANY THAT DESIGNS, MANUFACTURES, TESTS AND SELLS ANALOG AND EMBEDDED PROCESSING CHIPS FOR MARKETS SUCH AS INDUSTRIAL, AUTOMOTIVE, PERSONAL ELECTRONICS, COMMUNICATIONS EQUIPMENT AND ENTERPRISE SYSTEMS.
