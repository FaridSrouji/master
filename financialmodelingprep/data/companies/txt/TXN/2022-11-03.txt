TI UNIFIES FRAGMENTED IOT ECOSYSTEMS WITH MATTER-ENABLED WIRELESS MCU SOFTWARE. DALLAS , NOV. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENABLING SMARTER CONNECTIONS TO THE WORLD AROUND US, TEXAS INSTRUMENTS (TI) (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY INTRODUCED NEW MATTER-ENABLED SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT KITS FOR WI-FI AND THREAD SIMPLELINK™ WIRELESS MICROCONTROLLERS (MCUS) THAT WILL STREAMLINE ADOPTION OF THE MATTER PROTOCOL IN INTERNET OF THINGS (IOT) APPLICATIONS. THE SOFTWARE BUILDS ON TI'S CLOSE INVOLVEMENT WITH THE CONNECTIVITY STANDARDS ALLIANCE AND INNOVATION IN THE 2.4-GHZ CONNECTIVITY SPACE.
