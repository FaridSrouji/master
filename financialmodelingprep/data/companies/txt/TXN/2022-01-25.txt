TI REPORTS Q4 2021 AND 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS AND SHAREHOLDER RETURNS. DALLAS, JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INCORPORATED (TI) (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER REVENUE OF $4.83 BILLION, NET INCOME OF $2.14 BILLION AND EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $2.27. EARNINGS PER SHARE INCLUDED A 4-CENT NET COST THAT WAS NOT IN THE COMPANY'S ORIGINAL GUIDANCE.
