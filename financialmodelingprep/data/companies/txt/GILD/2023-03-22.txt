GILEAD SCIENCES ANNOUNCES $3 MILLION IN GRANT FUNDING TO EIGHT ORGANIZATIONS ADDRESSING HIV DISPARITIES IN RURAL U.S. COMMUNITIES. FOSTER CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: GILD) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS PROVIDING A TOTAL OF $3 MILLION IN GRANT FUNDING TO EIGHT ORGANIZATIONS TO PROVIDE SERVICES TO COMMUNITIES DISPROPORTIONATELY IMPACTED BY HIV IN RURAL AREAS IN THE UNITED STATES. MY BROTHER'S KEEPER, BASED IN RIDGELAND, MISSISSIPPI, WILL PROVIDE TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE AND CAPACITY BUILDING TO THE ORGANIZATIONS FUNDED THROUGH THIS INITIATIVE.
