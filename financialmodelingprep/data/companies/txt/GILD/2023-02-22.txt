GILEAD PRESENTS POSITIVE PROOF-OF-CONCEPT DATA FOR INVESTIGATIONAL COMBINATION REGIMEN OF LENACAPAVIR WITH BROADLY NEUTRALIZING ANTIBODIES AS A POTENTIAL TWICE-YEARLY APPROACH FOR THE TREATMENT OF HIV. FOSTER CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: GILD) TODAY ANNOUNCED DATA EVALUATING LENACAPAVIR IN COMBINATION WITH BROADLY NEUTRALIZING ANTIBODIES (BNABS) TEROPAVIMAB AND ZINLIRVIMAB AS A POTENTIAL LONG-ACTING TREATMENT REGIMEN WITH TWICE-YEARLY DOSING. RESULTS FROM THE PHASE 1B CLINICAL TRIAL DEMONSTRATED THE INVESTIGATIONAL COMBINATION WAS GENERALLY WELL TOLERATED WITH HIGH EFFICACY IN SELECT VIROLOGICALLY SUPPRESSED PARTICIPANTS LIVING WITH HIV.
