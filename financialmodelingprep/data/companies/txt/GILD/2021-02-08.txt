KITE APPOINTS FRANK NEUMANN AS WORLDWIDE HEAD OF CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT. SANTA MONICA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KITE, A GILEAD COMPANY (NASDAQ: GILD), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FRANK NEUMANN, MD, PHD, HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND GLOBAL HEAD OF CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT.
