GILEAD AND VIR BIOTECHNOLOGY ESTABLISH CLINICAL COLLABORATION TO EXPLORE COMBINATION STRATEGIES FOR FUNCTIONAL CURE FOR CHRONIC HEPATITIS B VIRUS. FOSTER CITY, CALIF. & SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: GILD) AND VIR BIOTECHNOLOGY, INC. (NASDAQ: VIR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANIES HAVE ENTERED INTO A CLINICAL COLLABORATION TO EVALUATE NOVEL THERAPEUTIC COMBINATION STRATEGIES AIMED AT DEVELOPING A FUNCTIONAL CURE FOR CHRONIC HEPATITIS B VIRUS (HBV).
