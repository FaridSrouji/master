KITE ANNOUNCES NEW ZUMA-1 COHORT ANALYSIS EVALUATING PROPHYLACTIC CORTICOSTEROID USE WITH YESCARTA® IN PATIENTS WITH RELAPSED OR REFRACTORY LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA. SANTA MONICA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KITE, A GILEAD COMPANY (NASDAQ: GILD), TODAY ANNOUNCED FINDINGS FROM A NEW ANALYSIS OF THE ZUMA-1 TRIAL OF YESCARTA® (AXICABTAGENE CILOLEUCEL) IN ADULT PATIENTS WITH RELAPSED OR REFRACTORY LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA (LBCL). RESULTS WERE PRESENTED TODAY IN AN ORAL SESSION AT THE TRANSPLANTATION & CELLULAR THERAPY MEETINGS OF THE AMERICAN SOCIETY OF TRANSPLANTATION AND CELLULAR THERAPY (ASTCT) AND CENTER FOR INTERNATIONAL BLOOD & MARROW TRANSPLANT RESEARC
