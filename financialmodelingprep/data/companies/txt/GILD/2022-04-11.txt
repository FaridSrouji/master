FDA LIFTS PARTIAL CLINICAL HOLD ON MDS AND AML MAGROLIMAB STUDIES. FOSTER CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: GILD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) LIFTED THE PARTIAL CLINICAL HOLD PLACED ON STUDIES EVALUATING ITS INVESTIGATIONAL AGENT MAGROLIMAB IN COMBINATION WITH AZACITIDINE. THE FDA REMOVED THE PARTIAL CLINICAL HOLD AFTER A REVIEW OF THE COMPREHENSIVE SAFETY DATA FROM EACH TRIAL.
