GILEAD REACHES NUMBER ONE SPOT AS TOP OVERALL PHILANTHROPIC FUNDER OF HIV-RELATED PROGRAMS, ACCORDING TO FUNDERS CONCERNED ABOUT AIDS REPORT. FOSTER CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: GILD) TODAY WAS OFFICIALLY RECOGNIZED AS THE NUMBER ONE PHILANTHROPIC FUNDER OF HIV-RELATED PROGRAMS IN A NEW TRACKING REPORT RELEASED BY FUNDERS CONCERNED ABOUT AIDS (FCAA). THE REPORT, FOUND AT: HTTPS://WWW.FCAAIDS.ORG/INFORM/PHILANTHROPIC-SUPPORT-TO-ADDRESS-HIV-AIDS/, IS RELEASED ANNUALLY AND IS WIDELY REGARDED AS THE MOST COMPREHENSIVE STUDY OF ITS KIND.
