KITE AND ARCELLX ANNOUNCE STRATEGIC COLLABORATION TO CO-DEVELOP AND CO-COMMERCIALIZE LATE-STAGE CLINICAL CART-DDBCMA IN MULTIPLE MYELOMA. SANTA MONICA, CALIF. & REDWOOD CITY, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--KITE, A GILEAD COMPANY (NASDAQ: GILD), AND ARCELLX, INC. (NASDAQ: ACLX), TODAY ANNOUNCED A GLOBAL STRATEGIC COLLABORATION TO CO-DEVELOP AND CO-COMMERCIALIZE ARCELLX'S LEAD LATE-STAGE PRODUCT CANDIDATE, CART-DDBCMA, FOR THE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH RELAPSED OR REFRACTORY MULTIPLE MYELOMA. MULTIPLE MYELOMA IS AN INCURABLE DISEASE FOR MOST PATIENTS AND THE NEED REMAINS FOR EFFECTIVE, SAFE, AND BROADLY ACCESSIBLE THERAPIES.
