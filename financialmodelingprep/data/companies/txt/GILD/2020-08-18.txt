GALAPAGOS ANNOUNCES THAT GILEAD RECEIVED COMPLETE RESPONSE LETTER FROM U.S. FDA FOR FILGOTINIB. GALAPAGOS ANNOUNCES THAT GILEAD RECEIVED A COMPLETE RESPONSE LETTER FOR FILGOTINIB FOR TREATMENT OF MODERATELY TO SEVERELY ACTIVE RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS.FDA HAS REQUESTED DATA FROM MANTA AND MANTA-RAY STUDIES BEFORE COMPLETING ITS REVIEW OF NDA.FDA ALSO HAS EXPRESSED CONCERNS REGARDING OVERALL BENEFIT/RISK PROFILE OF FILGOTINIB 200 MG DOSE.FOLLOWING THIS CRL, GALAPAGOS REVISES ITS FULL YEAR 2020 OPERATIONAL CASH BURN GUIDANCE TO BETWEEN EUR 490 AND EUR 520 MILLION.
