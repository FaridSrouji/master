GILEAD'S INVESTIGATIONAL ANTIVIRAL VEKLURY RECEIVES U.S. FDA EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR THE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH MODERATE COVID-19. GILEAD’S INVESTIGATIONAL ANTIVIRAL VEKLURY® (REMDESIVIR) RECEIVES U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR THE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH MODERATE COVID-19.GILEAD SCIENCES INC - EUA EXPANDS PREVIOUS AUTHORIZATION OF VEKLURY TO TREAT HOSPITALIZED PATIENTS WITH COVID-19 REGARDLESS OF OXYGEN STATUS.GILEAD SCIENCES - EXPANDED EUA BASED ON RESULTS FROM PHASE 3 SIMPLE TRIAL EVALUATING VEKLURY IN HOSPITALIZED PATIENTS WITH MODERATE COVID-19 PNEUMONIA.
