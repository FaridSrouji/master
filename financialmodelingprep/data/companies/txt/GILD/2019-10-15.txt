GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. APPOINTS ANDREW DICKINSON AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 1, 2019. GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ANDREW DICKINSON HAS BEEN APPOINTED CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO), EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 1, 2019. MR. DICKINSON CURRENTLY SERVES AS THE COMPANY’S EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT AND STRATEGY, AND IN HIS NEW ROLE HE WILL CONTINUE TO BE A MEMBER OF THE SENIOR LEADERSHIP TEAM, REPORTING TO DANIEL O’DAY, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. MR. DICKINSON JOINED GILEAD IN 2016 AND PRIOR TO HIS TENURE AT GILEAD, MR. DICKINSON WORKED FOR NINE YEARS AT LAZARD FRÈRES & CO., WHERE HE WAS GLOBAL CO-HEAD OF HEALTHCARE INVESTMENT BANKING.
