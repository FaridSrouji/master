GILEAD SCIENCES ANNOUNCES $24 MILLION IN GRANTS TO HELP END THE HIV EPIDEMIC FOR EVERYONE, EVERYWHERE. FOSTER CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC., (NASDAQ: GILD) TODAY ANNOUNCED $24 MILLION IN GRANTS TO HELP REDUCE HEALTH DISPARITIES, IMPROVE ACCESS TO QUALITY HEALTHCARE, ADVANCE MEDICAL EDUCATION AND SUPPORT LOCAL COMMUNITIES MOST IMPACTED BY THE HIV EPIDEMIC AND COVID-19 PANDEMIC. THE ZEROING IN: ENDING THE HIV EPIDEMIC PROGRAM, WILL SUPPORT 116 ORGANIZATIONS IN 41 COUNTRIES.
