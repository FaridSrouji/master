YESCARTA® RECEIVES U.S. FDA APPROVAL AS FIRST CAR T-CELL THERAPY FOR INITIAL TREATMENT OF RELAPSED OR REFRACTORY LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA (LBCL). SANTA MONICA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KITE, A GILEAD COMPANY (NASDAQ: GILD), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED YESCARTA® (AXICABTAGENE CILOLEUCEL) CAR T-CELL THERAPY FOR ADULT PATIENTS WITH LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA THAT IS REFRACTORY TO FIRST-LINE CHEMOIMMUNOTHERAPY OR THAT RELAPSES WITHIN 12 MONTHS OF FIRST-LINE CHEMOIMMUNOTHERAPY.
