FDA APPROVES NEW TREATMENT FOR PEDIATRIC PATIENTS WITH ANY STRAIN OF HEPATITIS C. FDA APPROVES NEW TREATMENT FOR PEDIATRIC PATIENTS WITH ANY STRAIN OF HEPATITIS C.FDA - GRANTED PRIORITY REVIEW OF EPCLUSA TO GILEAD SCIENCES INC.FDA - EPCLUSA IN COMBINATION WITH RIBAVIRIN INDICATED TO TREAT PEDIATRIC PATIENTS 6 YRS & OLDER OR WEIGHING AT LEAST 37 POUNDS WITH SEVERE CIRRHOSIS.
