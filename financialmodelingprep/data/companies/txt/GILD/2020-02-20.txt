GALAPAGOS REPORTS FY GROUP REVENUES & OTHER INCOME OF EUR 896 MILLION, COMPARED TO EUR 318 MILLION IN 2018. GALAPAGOS NV - FY GROUP REVENUES & OTHER INCOME OF EUR 896 MILLION, COMPARED TO EUR 318 MILLION IN 2018.GALAPAGOS NV- FY NET PROFIT OF EUR 150 MILLION, COMPARED TO A NET LOSS OF EUR 29 MILLION IN 2018.GALAPAGOS NV - FY DILUTED INCOME PER SHARE 2.49 EUROS.GALAPAGOS - IN 2020, EXPECT AN OPERATIONAL CASH BURN BETWEEN €420 AND €450 MILLION.GALAPAGOS - CO, GILEAD EXPECT APPROVAL OF OUR FIRST PRODUCT CANDIDATE, FILGOTINIB, IN RA IN THE U.S., EUROPE, AND JAPAN.
