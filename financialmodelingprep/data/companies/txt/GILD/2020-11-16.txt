GILEAD AND NOVO NORDISK PRESENT NEW DATA FROM PROOF-OF-CONCEPT TRIAL IN NASH. FOSTER CITY, CALIF. & BAGSVÆRD, DENMARK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: GILD) AND NOVO NORDISK A/S (NASDAQ COPENHAGEN: NOVO B) TODAY ANNOUNCED RESULTS FROM A PHASE 2 PROOF-OF-CONCEPT TRIAL. THE FIVE-ARM TRIAL EVALUATED COMBINATIONS OF NOVO NORDISK'S SEMAGLUTIDE, A GLP-1 RECEPTOR AGONIST, WITH GILEAD'S INVESTIGATIONAL FXR AGONIST CILOFEXOR AND/OR GILEAD'S INVESTIGATIONAL ACC INHIBITOR FIRSOCOSTAT OVER 24 WEEKS IN 108 PEOPLE WITH NON-ALCOHOLIC STEATOHEPATITIS (NASH). THE RESULTS
