TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY CELEBRATES SERVICE MEMBERS WITH NATIONWIDE DISCOUNT ON THE FOURTH OF JULY AND ANNOUNCES GRANT DONATION FOR FARMER VETERANS. ON MONDAY, JULY 4, TRACTOR SUPPLY STORES WILL OFFER A 15% DISCOUNT TO ALL ACTIVE MILITARY, VETERANS AND THEIR DEPENDENTS.
