TRACTOR SUPPLY RAISES $1,004,920 FOR GRANTS FOR GROWING. BRENTWOOD, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #4H--TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY (NASDAQ: TSCO), THE LARGEST RURAL LIFESTYLE RETAILER IN THE UNITED STATES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE RESULTS OF ITS EIGHTH ANNUAL GRANTS FOR GROWING FUNDRAISER. CUSTOMERS AND TEAM MEMBERS GENERATED A TOTAL OF $1,004,920 THROUGH THE PURCHASE OF FFA PAPER EMBLEMS AT CHECKOUT. THE FUNDS RAISED WILL BE USED TO SUPPORT STUDENT-LED AGRICULTURAL PROJECTS RUN BY FFA CHAPTERS ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
