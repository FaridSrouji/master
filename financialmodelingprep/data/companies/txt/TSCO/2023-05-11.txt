TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BRENTWOOD, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #4H--TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY (NASDAQ: TSCO), THE LARGEST RURAL LIFESTYLE RETAILER IN THE UNITED STATES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.03 PER SHARE OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JUNE 13, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 30, 2023.
