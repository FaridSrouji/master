TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY RAISES $790,269 FOR GRANTS FOR GROWING PROGRAM. BRENTWOOD, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY, THE LARGEST RURAL LIFESTYLE RETAILER IN THE UNITED STATES, HAS SUCCESSFULLY RAISED $790,269 DURING ITS SIXTH ANNUAL GRANTS FOR GROWING FUNDRAISER. THE FUNDS WILL BE USED TO SUPPORT SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE PROJECTS LED BY FFA STUDENTS ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
