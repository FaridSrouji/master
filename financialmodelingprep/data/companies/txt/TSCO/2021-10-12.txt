TRACTOR SUPPLY ANNOUNCES ANNUAL FALL PAPER CLOVER CAMPAIGN SUPPORTING 4-H YOUTH. BRENTWOOD, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #4H--TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY (NASDAQ: TSCO), THE LARGEST RURAL LIFESTYLE RETAILER IN THE UNITED STATES, HAS ANNOUNCED ITS 2021 FALL PAPER CLOVER FUNDRAISING CAMPAIGN IN PARTNERSHIP WITH NATIONAL 4-H COUNCIL. THE SEMI-ANNUAL EVENT RAISES MONEY FOR 4-H PROGRAMMING NATIONWIDE, WITH 90% OF FUNDS USED FOR STATE-LEVEL SCHOLARSHIPS TO 4-H CAMPS AND LEADERSHIP EXPERIENCES.
