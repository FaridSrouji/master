TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY REVISES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019. TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY REVISED EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019. BASED ON YEAR-TO-DATE PERFORMANCE, THE COMPANY IS PROVIDING THE FOLLOWING UPDATED GUIDANCE FOR THE EXPECTED RESULTS OF OPERATIONS IN FISCAL 2019: NET SALES OF $8.40 BILLION TO $8.42 BILLION, COMPARED TO ITS PREVIOUS EXPECTATION OF $8.40 BILLION TO $8.46 BILLION; NET INCOME OF $564 MILLION TO $569 MILLION, COMPARED TO ITS PREVIOUS GUIDANCE OF $562 MILLION TO $575 MILLION; AND EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE OF $4.66 TO $4.70, COMPARED TO ITS PREVIOUS GUIDANCE OF $4.65 TO $4.75.
