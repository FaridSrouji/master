DONALD TEMPLIN JOINS VOYA FINANCIAL AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--VOYA FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: VOYA), A LEADING HEALTH, WEALTH AND INVESTMENT COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DONALD C. TEMPLIN HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO).
