MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. FINDLAY, OHIO , APRIL 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. (NYSE: MPC) HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.75 PER SHARE ON COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 12, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS MAY 17, 2023.
