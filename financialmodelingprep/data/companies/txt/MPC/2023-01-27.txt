MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. TO HOST 2023 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS. FINDLAY, OHIO , JAN. 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. (NYSE: MPC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS WILL TAKE PLACE APRIL 26, 2023, AT 10 A.M. ET IN A VIRTUAL-ONLY FORMAT VIA LIVE WEBCAST.
MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. FINDLAY, OHIO , JAN. 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. (NYSE: MPC) HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.75 PER SHARE ON COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 10, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS FEB. 16, 2023.
