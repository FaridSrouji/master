MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. ANNOUNCES ACQUISITION OF A 49.9% INTEREST IN EMERGING RNG PRODUCER. FINDLAY, OHIO , MARCH 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. (NYSE: MPC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF A 49.9% INTEREST IN LF BIOENERGY, AN EMERGING PRODUCER OF RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS (RNG) IN THE UNITED STATES, FROM CRESTA FUND MANAGEMENT FOR $50 MILLION. THE AGREEMENT INCLUDES THE POTENTIAL FOR UP TO AN ADDITIONAL $50 MILLION BASED ON THE ACHIEVEMENT OF PREDETERMINED EARN-OUT TARGETS.
