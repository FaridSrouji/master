MPLX LP REPORTS THIRD-QUARTER 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS. FINDLAY, OHIO , NOV. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REPORTED THIRD-QUARTER NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO MPLX OF $1,428 MILLION; INCLUDES $509 MILLION NON-CASH LEASE RECLASSIFICATION GAIN REPORTED ADJUSTED EBITDA ATTRIBUTABLE TO MPLX OF $1,471 MILLION, UP 6% YEAR OVER YEAR GENERATED $1,039 MILLION IN NET CASH PROVIDED BY OPERATING ACTIVITIES RETURNED $935 MILLION OF CAPITAL TO UNITHOLDERS THROUGH $755 MILLION OF DISTRIBUTIONS AND $180 MILLION OF UNIT REPURCHASES ANNOUNCED DISTRIBUTION INCREASE OF 10% TO $0.775 PER UNIT, RESULTING IN A COVERAGE RATIO OF 1.6X MPLX LP (NYSE: MPLX) TODAY REPORTED THIRD-QUARTER 2022 NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO MPLX OF $1,428 MILLION, COMPARED WITH $802 MILLION FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021. THIRD-QUARTER 2022 NET INCOME INCLUDES A $509 MILLION NON-CASH GAIN RESULTING FROM THE ACCOUNTING FOR THE RECLASSIFICATION OF A THIRD-PARTY CONTRACT FROM AN OPERATING LEASE TO A SALES-TYPE LEASE.
MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. REPORTS THIRD-QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. FINDLAY, OHIO , NOV. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO MPC OF $4.5 BILLION , OR $9.06 PER DILUTED SHARE; REPORTED ADJUSTED NET INCOME OF $3.9 BILLION, OR $7.81 PER DILUTED SHARE ADJUSTED EBITDA OF $6.8 BILLION; IMPROVING OPERATIONAL AND COMMERCIAL EXECUTION AS THE REFINING SYSTEM RAN AT NEAR FULL UTILIZATION TO MEET DEMAND MPLX INCREASES DISTRIBUTION 10%; MPC EXPECTS TO RECEIVE A TOTAL OF $2 BILLION ON AN ANNUAL BASIS COMPLETED $15 BILLION RETURN OF CAPITAL COMMITMENT UTILIZING THE PROCEEDS FROM THE SPEEDWAY DIVESTITURE; REPURCHASED APPROXIMATELY 30% OF OUTSTANDING SHARES ANNOUNCED DIVIDEND INCREASE OF APPROXIMATELY 30% TO $0.75 PER SHARE MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. (NYSE: MPC) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO MPC OF $4.5 BILLION, OR $9.06 PER DILUTED SHARE, FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022, COMPARED WITH NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO MPC OF $694 MILLION, OR $1.09 PER DILUTED SHARE, FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021. ADJUSTED NET INCOME WAS $3.9 BILLION, OR $7.81 PER DILUTED SHARE, FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022.
MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. FINDLAY, OHIO , NOV. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. (NYSE: MPC) HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.75 PER SHARE ON COMMON STOCK, AN INCREASE OF APPROXIMATELY 30% OVER ITS PREVIOUS DIVIDEND OF $0.58 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DEC. 12, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS NOV. 16, 2022.
