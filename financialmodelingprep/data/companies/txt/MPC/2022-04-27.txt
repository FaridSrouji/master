MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. FINDLAY, OHIO , APRIL 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. (NYSE: MPC) HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.58 PER SHARE ON COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS MAY 18, 2022.
