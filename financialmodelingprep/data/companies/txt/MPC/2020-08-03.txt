REPORTS SECOND-QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.Q2 ADJUSTED LOSS PER SHARE $1.33.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.01.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $-1.75 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.INDEFINITELY IDLING GALLUP AND MARTINEZ REFINERIES.EVALUATING STRATEGIC REPOSITIONING OF MARTINEZ TO RENEWABLE DIESEL FACILITY.QTRLY REFINING & MARKETING LOSS FROM OPERATIONS OF $1.62 BILLION VERSUS INCOME OF $906 MILLION.QTRLY TOTAL REVENUES AND OTHER INCOME $15,198 MILLION VERSUS $33,670 MILLION.QTRLY TOTAL NET REFINERY THROUGHPUT OF 2,276 MBPD VERSUS 3,135 MBPD.SEES Q3 2020 TOTAL REFINERY THROUGHPUTS 2,345 MBPD.MARATHON PETROLEUM - DEMAND FOR PRODUCT, SERVICES CONTINUES TO BE SIGNIFICANTLY DEPRESSED, PARTICULARLY ACROSS WEST COAST AND MIDWEST."CONFIDENT WE WILL MEET $950 MILLION EXPENSE REDUCTION TARGET WE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED FOR 2020".ALSO IMPLEMENTING PLANS TO STRUCTURALLY LOWER COSTS IN 2021 AND BEYOND.
MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT FOR $21 BILLION SALE OF SPEEDWAY. ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT FOR $21 BILLION SALE OF SPEEDWAY.7-ELEVEN TO ACQUIRE SPEEDWAY IN $21 BILLION ALL-CASH TRANSACTION.$16.5 BILLION ESTIMATED AFTER-TAX CASH PROCEEDS EXPECTED TO BE USED TO STRENGTHEN BALANCE SHEET AND RETURN CAPITAL TO SHAREHOLDERS.MARATHON PETROLEUM - ARRANGEMENT INCLUDES A 15-YEAR FUEL SUPPLY AGREEMENT FOR APPROXIMATELY 7.7 BILLION GALLONS PER YEAR ASSOCIATED WITH SPEEDWAY.TRANSACTION HAS BEEN UNANIMOUSLY APPROVED BY BOARDS OF DIRECTORS OF BOTH COMPANIES.
