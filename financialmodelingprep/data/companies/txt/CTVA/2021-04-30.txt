CORTEVA DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. WILMINGTON, DEL., APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CORTEVA, INC. (NYSE: CTVA) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED A COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF 13 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE JUNE 15, 2021, TO THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 14, 2021.
