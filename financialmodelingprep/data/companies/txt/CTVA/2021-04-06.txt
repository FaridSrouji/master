CORTEVA NAMES DAVE ANDERSON EVP & CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. WILMINGTON, DEL., APRIL 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CORTEVA, INC. (NYSE: CTVA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS APPOINTED DAVE ANDERSON AS ITS NEW EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO), EFFECTIVE APRIL 12, 2021.
