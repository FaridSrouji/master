CORTEVA ANNOUNCES EVP RAJAN GAJARIA'S RETIREMENT, DESIGNATES INDIANAPOLIS GLOBAL HEADQUARTERS. INDIANAPOLIS, FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CORTEVA, INC. (NYSE: CTVA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT RAJAN GAJARIA, WHO HAS SERVED AS CORTEVA'S EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF BUSINESS PLATFORMS SINCE 2019, WILL RETIRE FROM THE COMPANY EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 18, 2022. MR. GAJARIA'S DIRECT REPORTS WILL REPORT TO TIM GLENN, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, OR SAM EATHINGTON, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, ON AN INTERIM BASIS.
