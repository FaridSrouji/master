CORTEVA ANNOUNCES PRICING OF SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. INDIANAPOLIS , MAY 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CORTEVA, INC. ("CORTEVA") (NYSE: CTVA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF A PUBLIC OFFERING BY ITS DIRECT SUBSIDIARY, EIDP, INC. ("EIDP") TOTALING $1.2 BILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. THE NET PROCEEDS FROM THE SALE OF THE NOTES WILL BE USED FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES.
