CORTEVA AGRISCIENCE ANNOUNCES HAVIZA™ ACTIVE AS GLOBAL TRADEMARK FOR NEWEST ACTIVE INGREDIENT IN ITS INNOVATION PIPELINE. WILMINGTON, DEL., FEB. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CORTEVA AGRISCIENCE TODAY ANNOUNCES HAVIZA™ ACTIVE AS THE BRAND NAME FOR THE NEWEST FUNGICIDE TO COME FROM ITS ROBUST INNOVATION PIPELINE.
