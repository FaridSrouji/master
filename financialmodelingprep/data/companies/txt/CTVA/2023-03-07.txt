CORTEVA AGRISCIENCE ANNOUNCES COMMERCIAL LAUNCH OF VORCEED™ ENLIST® CORN PRODUCTS. THE NEXT GENERATION OF CORN ROOTWORM TRAIT TECHNOLOGY WILL LAUNCH THROUGH EXCLUSIVE CORTEVA HORIZON NETWORK PROGRAM INDIANAPOLIS , MARCH 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CORTEVA AGRISCIENCE TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS FOR THE COMMERCIAL LAUNCH OF VORCEED™ ENLIST® CORN PRODUCTS. U.S. FARMERS WILL ACCESS VORCEED ENLIST CORN TECHNOLOGY ACROSS PIONEER® SEEDS, DAIRYLAND SEED® AND BREVANT® SEEDS THROUGH THE EXCLUSIVE CORTEVA HORIZON NETWORK IN AREAS OF HIGH CORN ROOTWORM (CRW) PRESSURE IN THE CORN BELT.
