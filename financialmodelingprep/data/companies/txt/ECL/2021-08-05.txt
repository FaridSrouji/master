ECOLAB DECLARES REGULAR DIVIDEND. ST. PAUL, MINN., AUG. 05, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ECOLAB INC. TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.48 PER COMMON SHARE, TO BE PAID OCTOBER 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 14, 2021.
