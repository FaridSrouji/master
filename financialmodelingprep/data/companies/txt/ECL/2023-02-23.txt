ECOLAB DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND. ST. PAUL, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ECOLAB INC. TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.53 PER COMMON SHARE, TO BE PAID APRIL 17, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 21, 2023. ECOLAB HAS PAID CASH DIVIDENDS ON ITS COMMON STOCK FOR 86 CONSECUTIVE YEARS.
