ECOLAB ANNOUNCES OFFERS TO EXCHANGE CERTAIN OF ITS OUTSTANDING NOTES FOR UP TO $500 MILLION OF ITS 2.750% NOTES DUE 2055 AND CASH. ST. PAUL, MINN., AUG. 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ECOLAB INC. (“ECOLAB”) (NYSE: ECL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMMENCED OFFERS (THE “EXCHANGE OFFERS”) TO CERTAIN ELIGIBLE HOLDERS TO EXCHANGE ITS OUTSTANDING NOTES OF THE FOUR SERIES DESCRIBED IN THE TABLE BELOW (COLLECTIVELY, THE “OLD NOTES”) FOR UP TO $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT (THE “EXCHANGE CAP”) OF ITS NEWLY ISSUED 2.750% NOTES DUE 2055 (THE “NEW NOTES”) AND CASH. THE NEW NOTES WILL BE A FURTHER ISSUANCE OF, AND WILL BE IN ADDITION TO, THE 2.750% NOTES DUE 2055 (THE “ORIGINAL 2055 NOTES”) THAT ECOLAB EXPECTS TO ISSUE FOR CASH ON AUGUST 18, 2021, IN THE AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $300 MILLION.
ECOLAB OPENS HEALTHCARE ADVANCED DESIGN CENTER TO DRIVE INNOVATION FOR THE MEDICAL DEVICE INDUSTRY.
