PSSI ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP TRANSITION. KIELER, WIS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PSSI, A LEADING FOOD SAFETY SOLUTION AND SANITATION PROVIDER, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TIM MULHERE, A FORMER ECOLAB (NYSE: ECL) EXECUTIVE, WILL BECOME THE COMPANY'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. MR. MULHERE JOINS PSSI AFTER SPENDING MORE THAN 20 YEARS IN VARIOUS EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP POSITIONS AT ECOLAB, A GLOBAL LEADER IN FOOD SAFETY, HYGIENE AND INFECTION PREVENTION SOLUTIONS, AND WATER AND ENERGY USE OPTIMIZATION.
