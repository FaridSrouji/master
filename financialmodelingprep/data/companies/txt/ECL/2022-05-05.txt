ECOLAB DECLARES REGULAR DIVIDEND. ST. PAUL, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ECOLAB INC. TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.51 PER COMMON SHARE, TO BE PAID JULY 15, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 21, 2022. ECOLAB HAS PAID CASH DIVIDENDS ON ITS COMMON STOCK FOR 85 CONSECUTIVE YEARS.
