ECOLAB RECOGNIZED AS A BEST PLACE TO WORK FOR DISABILITY INCLUSION. ST. PAUL, MINN., JULY 13, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ECOLAB INC., THE GLOBAL LEADER IN WATER, HYGIENE AND INFECTION PREVENTION SOLUTIONS AND SERVICES, HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED ON THE 2021 DISABILITY EQUALITY INDEX (DEI) AS A BEST PLACE TO WORK FOR DISABILITY INCLUSION BY THE AMERICAN ASSOCIATION OF PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES (AAPD), THE NATION'S LARGEST DISABILITY RIGHTS ORGANIZATION, AND DISABILITY:IN, THE GLOBAL BUSINESS DISABILITY INCLUSION NETWORK.
