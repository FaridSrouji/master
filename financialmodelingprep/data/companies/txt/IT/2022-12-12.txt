GARTNER PREDICTS HALF OF SALES LEADERSHIP ROLES WILL BE FILLED BY HISTORICALLY UNDERREPRESENTED GROUPS WITHIN FIVE YEARS. STAMFORD, CONN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--BY 2028, 50% OF SALES LEADERSHIP ROLES WILL BE FILLED BY INDIVIDUALS FROM HISTORICALLY UNDERREPRESENTED GROUPS, ACCORDING TO GARTNER, INC. TO ADDRESS CHRONIC TALENT SHORTAGES, CHIEF SALES OFFICERS (CSOS) MUST CHALLENGE EXISTING RECRUITMENT PRACTICES AND TAKE STEPS TO RETAIN AND MOTIVATE DIVERSE TALENT.
