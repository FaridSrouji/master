GARTNER ANNOUNCES UPSIZING AND PRICING OF ITS $600 MILLION 3.625% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2029. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GARTNER, INC. (NYSE:IT) (“GARTNER,” “WE” OR “OUR”) ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS UPSIZED OFFERING OF $600 MILLION 3.625% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2029.
GARTNER ANNOUNCES INTENTION TO OFFER $500 MILLION IN SENIOR NOTES. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GARTNER, INC. (NYSE:IT) (“GARTNER,” “WE” OR “OUR”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMMENCED AN UNREGISTERED OFFERING OF $500 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR NOTES DUE 2029 (THE “NOTES”). GARTNER INTENDS TO USE THE NET PROCEEDS FROM THE OFFERING OF THE NOTES (I) TO REPAY A PORTION OF THE OUTSTANDING BORROWINGS UNDER OUR EXISTING TERM LOAN FACILITY, (II) TO PAY RELATED FEES AND EXPENSES AND (III) THE REMAINDER, FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES.
