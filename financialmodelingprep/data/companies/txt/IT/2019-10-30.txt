GARTNER INC. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. GARTNER INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 1,000.502 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 921.674 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 69.147 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 52.724 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 41.388 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 11.753 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.46 COMPARED TO USD 0.13 A YEAR AGO.
