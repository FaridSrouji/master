FISCALNOTE APPOINTS RICHARD HENDERSON AS CHIEF REVENUE OFFICER TO DRIVE NEXT PHASE OF COMPANY'S GLOBAL SALES GROWTH AND CUSTOMER EXPANSION. WASHINGTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )-- FISCALNOTE HOLDINGS, INC . (NYSE: NOTE) ("FISCALNOTE"), A LEADING AI-DRIVEN ENTERPRISE SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE (SAAS) TECHNOLOGY PROVIDER OF GLOBAL POLICY AND MARKET INTELLIGENCE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF RICHARD HENDERSON AS CHIEF REVENUE OFFICER (“CRO”), EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. HENDERSON WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO JOSH RESNIK, FISCALNOTE'S PRESIDENT & CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER.
