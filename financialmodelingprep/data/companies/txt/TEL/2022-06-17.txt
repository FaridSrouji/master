TE CONNECTIVITY BOARD APPROVES ADDITIONAL $1.5 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE. SCHAFFHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND , JUNE 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TE CONNECTIVITY LTD. (NYSE: TEL), A GLOBAL LEADER IN CONNECTORS AND SENSORS, ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED AN INCREASE IN ITS SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM BY AN ADDITIONAL $1.5 BILLION.
