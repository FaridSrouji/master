TE CONNECTIVITY ANNOUNCES APPROVAL OF QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS BY SHAREHOLDERS AT 2023 ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING. SCHAFFHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND , MARCH 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SHAREHOLDERS OF TE CONNECTIVITY LTD. (NYSE: TEL), A WORLD LEADER IN CONNECTORS AND SENSORS, APPROVED A DIVIDEND OF $2.36 PER SHARE AT THE COMPANY'S ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS HELD ON MARCH 15, 2023.
