TE CONNECTIVITY EARNS RECOGNITION FOR CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY AND SUSTAINABILITY EFFORTS. SCHAFFHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND, NOV. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TE CONNECTIVITY, A WORLD LEADER IN CONNECTIVITY AND SENSORS, HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED FOR ITS EFFORTS TO BE A GOOD CORPORATE CITIZEN THROUGH POSITIVE ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE (ESG) POLICIES. THE COMPANY HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX FOR THE 10TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
