WESTROCK ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK COMPANY (NYSE: WRK) TODAY REPORTED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE WILL BE PAID TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 13, 2021 AND WILL BE PAID ON AUGUST 24, 2021.
