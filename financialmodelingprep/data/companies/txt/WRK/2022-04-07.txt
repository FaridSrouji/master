WESTROCK TO CLOSE PANAMA CITY, FLORIDA, PAPER MILL. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK COMPANY (NYSE: WRK) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL PERMANENTLY CEASE OPERATIONS AT ITS MILL IN PANAMA CITY, FLORIDA, BY JUNE 6, 2022.
