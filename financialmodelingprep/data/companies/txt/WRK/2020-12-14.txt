BRANDI COLANDER JOINS WESTROCK AS CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK (NYSE: WRK), A LEADING PROVIDER OF DIFFERENTIATED PAPER AND PACKAGING SOLUTIONS, WELCOMES BRANDI COLANDER TO ITS EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM AS CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER. COLANDER FURTHER STRENGTHENS WESTROCK'S COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABILITY, BOTH WITHIN THE COMPANY AND IN THE PACKAGING SOLUTIONS IT PROVIDES ITS CUSTOMERS. "WESTROCK IS COMMITTED TO A WORLD IN WHICH FIBER-BASED PACKAGING PLAYS A CENTRAL ROLE IN ADVANCING THE CIRCULAR ECONOMY,” SAID STEVE VOORHEE
