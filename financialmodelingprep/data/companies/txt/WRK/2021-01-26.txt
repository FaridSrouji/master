WESTROCK PROVIDES UPDATE ON RANSOMWARE INCIDENT. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK COMPANY (NYSE: WRK) TODAY PROVIDED THE FOLLOWING UPDATE ON ITS RANSOMWARE INCIDENT WHICH WAS ANNOUNCED ON JAN. 25, 2021. WESTROCK'S SECURITY TEAMS, SUPPORTED BY LEADING CYBER DEFENSE FIRMS, CONTINUE TO WORK ON REMEDIATION OF AND RECOVERY FROM THIS INCIDENT. WITH SOME EXCEPTIONS, THE COMPANY'S OPERATIONS HAVE CONTINUED TO RUN AND DELIVER FOR CUSTOMERS.
