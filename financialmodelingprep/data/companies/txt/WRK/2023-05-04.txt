WESTROCK REPORTS FISCAL 2023 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS; TRANSFORMATION PROGRESSING WELL. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK COMPANY (NYSE:WRK), A LEADING PROVIDER OF SUSTAINABLE PAPER AND PACKAGING SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED RESULTS FOR ITS FISCAL SECOND QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2023. SECOND QUARTER HIGHLIGHTS AND OTHER NOTABLE ITEMS: NET SALES OF $5.3 BILLION NET LOSS OF $2.0 BILLION, INCLUDED A $1.9 BILLION PRE-TAX, NON-CASH GOODWILL IMPAIRMENT AND $445 MILLION OF PRE-TAX RESTRUCTURING AND OTHER COSTS; ADJUSTED NET INCOME OF $198 MILLION SIMPLIFYING OUR PORTFOLIO TO STREAMLINE OUR.
