WESTROCK NAMED TO DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX FOR NORTH AMERICA FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. ATLANTA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WESTROCK COMPANY (NYSE: WRK), A LEADING PROVIDER OF SUSTAINABLE PAPER AND PACKAGING SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY NORTH AMERICA INDEX (DSJI NORTH AMERICA) FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR, IN RECOGNITION OF ITS COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES.
