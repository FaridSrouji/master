WESTROCK REPORTS FISCAL 2021 THIRD QUARTER RESULTS: RECORD NET SALES AND 40% INCREASE IN NET INCOME. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK COMPANY (NYSE:WRK), A LEADING PROVIDER OF DIFFERENTIATED, SUSTAINABLE PAPER AND PACKAGING SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED RESULTS FOR ITS FISCAL THIRD QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021. NOTABLE ITEMS IN THE THIRD QUARTER INCLUDE: RECORD NET SALES OF $4.8 BILLION INCREASED 14% COMPARED TO THE PRIOR YEAR QUARTER NET INCOME OF $250 MILLION INCREASED 40% COMPARED TO $179 MILLION IN THE PRIOR YEAR QUARTER ADJUSTED SEGMENT EBITDA OF $811 MILLION INCREASED 15% COMPARED TO $708
