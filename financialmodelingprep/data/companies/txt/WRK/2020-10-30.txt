WESTROCK ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.20 PER SHARE. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK COMPANY (NYSE: WRK) TODAY REPORTED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.20 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.20 PER SHARE WILL BE PAID TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 13, 2020 AND WILL BE PAID ON NOVEMBER 24, 2020. ABOUT WESTROCK WESTROCK (NYSE: WRK) PARTNERS WITH OUR CUSTOMERS TO PROVIDE DIFFERENTIATED PAPER AND PACKAGING SOLUTIONS THAT HELP THEM WIN IN THE MARKETPLACE. WES
