WESTROCK ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.275 PER SHARE. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK COMPANY (NYSE: WRK) TODAY REPORTED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.275 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.275 PER SHARE WILL BE PAID TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 11, 2023, AND WILL BE PAID ON MAY 25, 2023.
