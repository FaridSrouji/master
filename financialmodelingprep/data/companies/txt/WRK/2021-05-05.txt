ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK COMPANY (NYSE: WRK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, AN INCREASE OF $0.04 PER SHARE FROM ITS PREVIOUS DIVIDEND RATE AND REPRESENTING AN ANNUALIZED INCREASE OF $0.16 PER SHARE, OR 20%.
