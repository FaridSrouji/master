AMEREN HIGHLIGHTS CLIMATE COMMITMENTS: NEW REPORT PROVIDES UPDATE ON COMPANY PROGRESS TOWARD ACHIEVING NET-ZERO CARBON EMISSIONS BY 2045. ST. LOUIS , NOV. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE) RELEASED ITS LATEST CLIMATE REPORT ALIGNED WITH THE TASK FORCE ON CLIMATE-RELATED FINANCIAL DISCLOSURES (TCFD) RECOMMENDATIONS. THE REPORT HIGHLIGHTS ACTIONS THE COMPANY IS TAKING TO MANAGE CLIMATE-RELATED RISKS AND TRANSITION TO A CLEANER ENERGY FUTURE, ACHIEVING NET-ZERO CARBON EMISSIONS BY 2045.
