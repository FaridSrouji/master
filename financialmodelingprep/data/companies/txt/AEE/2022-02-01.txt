AMEREN ILLINOIS RECEIVES APPROVAL TO CONSTRUCT LOGAN COUNTY CONNECTOR PROJECT. LINCOLN, ILL., FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMEREN ILLINOIS, A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE), RECEIVED APPROVAL FROM THE ILLINOIS COMMERCE COMMISSION (ICC) TO PROCEED WITH THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE LOGAN COUNTY CONNECTOR PROJECT.
