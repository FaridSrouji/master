AMEREN CORPORATION ANNOUNCES ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGES. AMEREN CORPORATION ANNOUNCES ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGES.AMEREN - MARTY LYONS, CURRENTLY EVP AND CFO, AND PRESIDENT OF AMEREN SERVICES, WILL BECOME PRESIDENT OF AMEREN MISSOURI.AMEREN CORP - ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGES WILL BE EFFECTIVE DEC. 1..AMEREN CORP -  MICHAEL MOEHN WILL BECOME EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CFO, AND PRESIDENT OF AMEREN SERVICES.
AMEREN CORPORATION APPOINTS MICHAEL L. MOEHN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE AS OF DECEMBER 1, 2019. ON OCTOBER 11, 2019, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMEREN CORPORATION ELECTED MICHAEL L. MOEHN TO THE POSITION OF EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER OF AMEREN, EFFECTIVE AS OF DECEMBER 1, 2019. ON DECEMBER 1, 2019, MR. MOEHN WILL RELINQUISH HIS POSITION AS CHAIRMAN AND PRESIDENT OF UNION ELECTRIC COMPANY, DOING BUSINESS AS AMEREN MISSOURI (AMEREN MISSOURI"). MR. MOEHN WAS ELECTED CHAIRMAN AND PRESIDENT OF AMEREN MISSOURI ON APRIL 1, 2014.
