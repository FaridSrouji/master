AMEREN ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. ST. LOUIS, NOV. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THIRD QUARTER 2021 NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $425 MILLION, OR $1.65 PER DILUTED SHARE, COMPARED TO THIRD QUARTER 2020 NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $367 MILLION, OR $1.47 PER DILUTED SHARE. THIRD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS REFLECTED EARNINGS ON INCREASED INFRASTRUCTURE INVESTMENTS MADE ACROSS ALL BUSINESS SEGMENTS DRIVEN BY STRONG EXECUTION OF THE COMPANY'S STRATEGY.
