AMEREN ANNOUNCES PRICING OF SENIOR NOTES OFFERING DUE 2027. ST. LOUIS, NOV. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 1.95% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2027 AT 99.981% OF THEIR PRINCIPAL AMOUNT.  THE TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON NOVEMBER 18, 2021, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
