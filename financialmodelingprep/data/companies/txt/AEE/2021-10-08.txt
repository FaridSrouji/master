AMEREN CORPORATION DIRECTORS DECLARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ST. LOUIS, OCT. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF 55 CENTS PER SHARE. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DEC. 31, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DEC. 8, 2021.
