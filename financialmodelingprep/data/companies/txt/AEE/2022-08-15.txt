AMEREN ILLINOIS ANNOUNCES PRICING OF FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2032. ST. LOUIS , AUG. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMEREN ILLINOIS COMPANY, A SUBSIDIARY OF AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 3.85% FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2032 AT 99.827% OF THEIR PRINCIPAL AMOUNT.  THE TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON AUG. 29, 2022, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
