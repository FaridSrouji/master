AMEREN CORPORATION DIRECTORS DECLARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ST. LOUIS , MAY 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF 59 CENTS PER SHARE. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 30, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 8, 2022.
