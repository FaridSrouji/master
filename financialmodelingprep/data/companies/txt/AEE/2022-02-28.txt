AMEREN NAMES CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY AND DIVERSITY OFFICER TO FURTHER OPTIMIZE ESG IMPACT. ST. LOUIS, FEB. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNDERSCORING ITS COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABILITY, WHICH INCORPORATES ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, GOVERNANCE (ESG) AND GROWTH GOALS, AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE) HAS EXPANDED THE ROLE OF A SENIOR LEADER AT THE COMPANY TO ALSO INCLUDE DIVERSITY, EQUITY, INCLUSION AND CORPORATE PHILANTHROPY. EFFECTIVE MARCH 1, GWEN MIZELL HAS BEEN NAMED CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY AND DIVERSITY OFFICER FOR AMEREN CORPORATION, FURTHER ALIGNING HER LEADERSHIP, EXPERTISE AND ENGAGEMENT TO OVERSEE AND ADVANCE THE COMPANY'S IMPACTS ACROSS THESE INTERCONNECTED AREAS.
