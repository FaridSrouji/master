AMEREN ENTERS AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE LARGE SOLAR FACILITY. ST. LOUIS, FEB. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY AMEREN MISSOURI, A SUBSIDIARY OF AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE), ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT WITH INVENERGY, A LEADING DEVELOPER, OWNER AND OPERATOR OF SUSTAINABLE ENERGY SOLUTIONS, TO ACQUIRE A 150-MEGAWATT SOLAR FACILITY PROJECT BEING DEVELOPED IN SOUTHEASTERN ILLINOIS. THE DEAL IS SUBJECT TO CLOSING CONDITIONS, INCLUDING REGULATORY APPROVALS.
