AMEREN MISSOURI ANNOUNCES PRICING OF FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2053. ST. LOUIS , FEB. 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNION ELECTRIC COMPANY, DOING BUSINESS AS AMEREN MISSOURI, A SUBSIDIARY OF AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 5.45% FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2053 AT 99.794% OF THEIR PRINCIPAL AMOUNT. THE TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON MARCH 13, 2023, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
