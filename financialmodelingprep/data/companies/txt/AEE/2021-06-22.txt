AMEREN ILLINOIS ANNOUNCES PRICING OF GREEN FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2051 AND FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2023. ST. LOUIS, JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMEREN ILLINOIS COMPANY, A SUBSIDIARY OF AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $350 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.90% FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2051 AT 99.583% OF THEIR PRINCIPAL AMOUNT AND $100 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 0.375% FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2023 AT 99.975% OF THEIR PRINCIPAL AMOUNT.  THE TRANSACTIONS ARE EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON JUNE 29, 2021, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS, AND ARE NOT CONTINGENT UPON EACH OTHER.
