AMEREN ILLINOIS ANNOUNCES PRICING OF FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2033. ST. LOUIS , MAY 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMEREN ILLINOIS COMPANY, A SUBSIDIARY OF AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 4.95% FIRST MORTGAGE BONDS DUE 2033 AT 99.696% OF THEIR PRINCIPAL AMOUNT.  THE TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON MAY 31, 2023, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
