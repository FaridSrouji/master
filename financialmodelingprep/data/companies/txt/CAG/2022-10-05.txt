CONAGRA BRANDS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT. CHICAGO , OCT. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.33 PER SHARE OF CAG COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON DECEMBER 1, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 3, 2022.  ABOUT CONAGRA BRANDS CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG), HEADQUARTERED IN CHICAGO, IS ONE OF NORTH AMERICA'S LEADING BRANDED FOOD COMPANIES.
