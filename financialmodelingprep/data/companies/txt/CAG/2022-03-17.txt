CONAGRA BRANDS ISSUES FISCAL 2021 CITIZENSHIP REPORT. REPORT HIGHLIGHTS PROGRESS ON KEY ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) DATA CHICAGO, MARCH 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PUBLICATION OF ITS FISCAL 2021 CITIZENSHIP REPORT, WHICH SHARES THE COMPANY'S PROGRESS AGAINST KEY ESG INITIATIVES AND ACTIONS THAT SUPPORT EACH OF THE 17 UNITED NATIONS SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS. THE REPORT DETAILS CONAGRA'S EFFORTS TO NOURISH PEOPLE, THE PLANET AND COMMUNITIES, AND FOCUSES ON FOUR DIFFERENT TOPIC AREAS: GOOD FOOD, RESPONSIBLE SOURCING, BETTER PLANET AND STRONGER COMMUNITIES.
