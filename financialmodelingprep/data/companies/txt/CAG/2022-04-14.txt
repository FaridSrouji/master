CONAGRA BRANDS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT. CHICAGO , APRIL 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.3125 PER SHARE OF CAG COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON JUNE 1, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 29, 2022. ABOUT CONAGRA BRANDS CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG), HEADQUARTERED IN CHICAGO, IS ONE OF NORTH AMERICA'S LEADING BRANDED FOOD COMPANIES.
