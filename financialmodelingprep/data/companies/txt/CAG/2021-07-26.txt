CONAGRA BRANDS ANNOUNCES APPOINTMENT OF FRAN HOROWITZ TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. CHICAGO, JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF FRAN HOROWITZ TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE AUG. 2, 2021. WITH THIS ADDITION, THE CONAGRA BRANDS BOARD WILL INCREASE TO 12 MEMBERS.
