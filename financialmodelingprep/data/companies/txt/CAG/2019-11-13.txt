8TH AVENUE FOOD & PROVISIONS, INC. AGREED TO ACQUIRE PEANUT BUTTER FACILITY OF CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE:CAG).. 8TH AVENUE FOOD & PROVISIONS, INC. AGREED TO ACQUIRE PEANUT BUTTER FACILITY OF CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE:CAG) ON NOVEMBER 14, 2019. THE FACILITY IS LOCATED IN STREATOR, ILLINOIS. 8TH AVENUE EXPECTS TO FUND THE TRANSACTION WITH CASH ON HAND. THE TRANSACTION IS SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS AND IS EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED IN THE FOURTH CALENDAR QUARTER OF 2019.
