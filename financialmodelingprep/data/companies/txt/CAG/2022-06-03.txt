CONAGRA BRANDS CELEBRATES EMPLOYEE-LED SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT AWARDS PROGRAM. EMPLOYEE SUSTAINABILITY INITIATIVES GENERATE NEARLY $9 MILLION IN COMPANY SAVINGS CHICAGO , JUNE 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN CELEBRATION OF WORLD ENVIRONMENT DAY ON JUNE 5, CONAGRA BRANDS (NYSE: CAG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE WINNERS OF ITS ANNUAL SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT AWARDS, AN EMPLOYEE-LED PROGRAM THAT ENCOURAGES THE DEVELOPMENT AND IMPLEMENTATION OF INNOVATIVE IDEAS RELATED TO SUSTAINABLE PRODUCTION AND BUSINESS PRACTICES. THIS YEAR, EMPLOYEES FROM ACROSS THE ORGANIZATION SUBMITTED A RECORD 211 SUSTAINABILITY-FOCUSED PROJECTS THAT CONSERVE WATER, REDUCE WASTE AND SAVE ENERGY, UNDERSCORING CONAGRA'S COMMITMENT TO NOURISHING AND RESTORING THE PLANET.
