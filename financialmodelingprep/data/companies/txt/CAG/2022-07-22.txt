CONAGRA BRANDS ANNOUNCES INCREASED QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT. CHICAGO , JULY 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.33 PER SHARE OF CAG COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON SEPTEMBER 1, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 3, 2022.  THIS REPRESENTS A 5.6% INCREASE OVER CONAGRA BRANDS' PREVIOUS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
