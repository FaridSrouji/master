LUCY BRADY NAMED BRAND PRESIDENT OF GROCERY & SNACKS FOR CONAGRA BRANDS. CHICAGO, MAY 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF LUCY BRADY AS PRESIDENT OF GROCERY & SNACKS, EFFECTIVE JUNE 15, 2022. IN THIS ROLE, BRADY WILL LEAD THE ONGOING MODERNIZATION AND GROWTH OF CONAGRA'S GROCERY AND SNACK PORTFOLIO, WHICH INCLUDES BRANDS SUCH AS SLIM JIM®, DUNCAN HINES®, AND ANGIE'S® BOOMCHICKAPOP®, WITH A FOCUS ON DELIVERING PRODUCTS THAT REFLECT EVOLVING CONSUMER DEMAND AND TRENDS.
