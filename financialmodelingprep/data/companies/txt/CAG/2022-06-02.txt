CONAGRA BRANDS NAMES CAREY BARTELL EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GENERAL COUNSEL AND CORPORATE SECRETARY. CHICAGO , JUNE 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CAREY BARTELL TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GENERAL COUNSEL AND CORPORATE SECRETARY, EFFECTIVE JUNE 6, 2022. "FOR MORE THAN SIX YEARS, CAREY HAS BEEN A VALUABLE CONAGRA LEADER WHO HAS SERVED AS VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF COUNSEL LEADING THE COMPANY'S LITIGATION EFFORTS AND COMPLIANCE PROGRAMS," SAID SEAN CONNOLLY, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, CONAGRA BRANDS.
