CONAGRA BRANDS FOUNDATION AWARDS GRANTS TO 23 LOCAL NONPROFITS THROUGH "NOURISH OUR COMMUNITY" PROGRAM. EMPLOYEE-NOMINATED GRANTS DRIVE COMMUNITY IMPACT DURING HUNGER ACTION MONTH CHICAGO , SEPT. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS FOUNDATION HAS ANNOUNCED THE 2022 RECIPIENTS OF THE "NOURISH OUR COMMUNITY" GRANTS, AWARDING ONE-YEAR GRANTS TO 23 NONPROFITS ACROSS 12 STATES, TOTALING $350,000.
