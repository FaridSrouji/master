CONAGRA BRANDS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT. CHICAGO , APRIL 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.33 PER SHARE OF CAG COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON JUNE 1, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 28, 2023.
