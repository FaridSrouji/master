EATON NAMES KURT MCMAKEN SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, OPERATIONS FINANCE AND FINANCE TRANSFORMATION. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--POWER MANAGEMENT COMPANY EATON (NYSE:ETN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KURT MCMAKEN HAS BEEN NAMED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, OPERATIONS FINANCE AND FINANCE TRANSFORMATION. IN THIS ROLE, MCMAKEN WILL LEAD THE GLOBAL OPERATIONS AND TRANSFORMATION ORGANIZATION. THIS ORGANIZATION INCLUDES THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL PLANNING AND ANALYSIS AND OPERATIONS FINANCE TEAMS AND HAS RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF FINANCE TECHNOLOGY ENHANCEMENTS.
