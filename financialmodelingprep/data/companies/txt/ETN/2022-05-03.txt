EATON REPORTS RECORD FIRST QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--POWER MANAGEMENT COMPANY EATON CORPORATION PLC (NYSE:ETN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $1.33 FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. EXCLUDING CHARGES OF $0.25 PER SHARE RELATED TO INTANGIBLE AMORTIZATION, $0.03 PER SHARE RELATED TO A MULTI-YEAR RESTRUCTURING PROGRAM, AND $0.01 PER SHARE RELATED TO ACQUISITIONS AND DIVESTITURES, ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $1.62 WERE A FIRST QUARTER RECORD AND UP 13% OVER THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021.
