EATON REPORTS RECORD FOURTH QUARTER 2022 RESULTS, GUIDANCE ON 2023 OUTLOOK. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTELLIGENT POWER MANAGEMENT COMPANY EATON CORPORATION PLC (NYSE:ETN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $1.80 FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022. EXCLUDING CHARGES OF $0.24 PER SHARE RELATED TO INTANGIBLE AMORTIZATION, $0.04 PER SHARE RELATED TO ACQUISITIONS AND DIVESTITURES, AND INCOME OF $0.02 PER SHARE RELATED TO A MULTI-YEAR RESTRUCTURING PROGRAM, ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $2.06 WERE A QUARTERLY RECORD AND UP 20% OVER THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021.
