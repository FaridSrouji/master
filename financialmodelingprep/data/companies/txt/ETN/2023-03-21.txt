EATON STRENGTHENS COMMITMENT TO GLOBAL SUPPLY CHAIN SUSTAINABILITY BY JOINING THE SUSTAINABLE PROCUREMENT PLEDGE'S LEAGUE OF CHAMPIONS PROGRAM. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTELLIGENT POWER MANAGEMENT COMPANY EATON (NYSE:ETN) HAS JOINED THE SUSTAINABLE PROCUREMENT PLEDGE'S (SPP) LEAGUE OF CHAMPIONS PROGRAM, FURTHER DEMONSTRATING THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES. SPP IS AN INTERNATIONAL, NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION FOR PROCUREMENT PROFESSIONALS THAT DRIVES AWARENESS AND KNOWLEDGE OF RESPONSIBLE SOURCING PRACTICES.
