EATON PROMOTES TARAS “TERRY” SZMAGALA JR. AND NANCY BERARDINELLI-KRANTZ TO NEW LEADERSHIP POSITIONS WITHIN LEGAL TEAM. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--POWER MANAGEMENT COMPANY EATON (NYSE:ETN) TODAY ANNOUNCED TARAS “TERRY” SZMAGALA JR. HAS BEEN NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER. IN THIS ROLE, HE WILL SERVE AS COUNSEL TO EATON'S EXECUTIVE MANAGEMENT TEAM AND ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, AND WILL LEAD THE COMPANY'S GLOBAL LEGAL ORGANIZATION, INCLUDING THE CORPORATE GOVERNANCE AND COMPLIANCE FUNCTIONS.
