EATON REPORTS RECORD SECOND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--POWER MANAGEMENT COMPANY EATON CORPORATION PLC (NYSE:ETN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $1.50 FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022. EXCLUDING CHARGES OF $0.24 PER SHARE RELATED TO INTANGIBLE AMORTIZATION, $0.11 PER SHARE RELATED TO ACQUISITIONS AND DIVESTITURES, AND $0.02 PER SHARE RELATED TO A MULTI-YEAR RESTRUCTURING PROGRAM, ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $1.87 WERE A QUARTERLY RECORD AND UP 9% OVER THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021.
