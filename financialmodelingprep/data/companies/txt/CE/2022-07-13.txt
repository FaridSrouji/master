CELANESE CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER SHARE. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CELANESE CORPORATION (NYSE: CE), A GLOBAL CHEMICAL AND SPECIALTY MATERIALS COMPANY, TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE AUGUST 8, 2022. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 25, 2022. ABOUT CELANESE CELANESE CORPORATION IS A GLOBAL CHEMICAL LEADER IN THE PRODUCTION OF DIFFERENTIATED CHEMISTRY SOLUTIONS AND SPECIALTY MATERIALS USED IN MOST MAJOR INDUSTRIES AND CONSUMER APPLICATIONS.
