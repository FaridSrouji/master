CELANESE CORPORATION REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS; RAISES FULL YEAR FINANCIAL OUTLOOK. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $CE--CELANESE CORPORATION (NYSE: CE), A GLOBAL CHEMICAL AND SPECIALTY MATERIALS COMPANY, TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER GAAP DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $2.83 AND ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $3.46. NET SALES FOR THE QUARTER WERE $1.8 BILLION AS PRICING INCREASED BY 15 PERCENT FROM THE PRIOR QUARTER, MORE THAN OFFSETTING A VOLUME DECLINE OF 3 PERCENT.
