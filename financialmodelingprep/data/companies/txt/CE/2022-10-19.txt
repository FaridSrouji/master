CELANESE CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.70 PER SHARE. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CELANESE CORPORATION (NYSE: CE), A GLOBAL CHEMICAL AND SPECIALTY MATERIALS COMPANY, TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.70 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 14, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF OCTOBER 31, 2022.
