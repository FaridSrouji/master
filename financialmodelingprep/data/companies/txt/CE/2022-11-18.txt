CELANESE ANNOUNCES ENGINEERED MATERIALS PRICE INCREASES. DALLAS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CELANESE CORPORATION (NYSE: CE), A GLOBAL SPECIALTY MATERIALS AND CHEMICAL COMPANY, WILL INCREASE PRICES FOR A RANGE OF PRODUCTS. THESE PRICE INCREASES WILL BE EFFECTIVE ON DECEMBER 1, 2022, OR AS CONTRACTS OTHERWISE ALLOW. ADDITIONALLY, INDIVIDUAL GRADES MAY BE SUBJECT TO HIGHER INCREASES THAN SPECIFIED BELOW.
