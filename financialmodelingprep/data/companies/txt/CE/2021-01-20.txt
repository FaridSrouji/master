CELANESE TO HOST VIRTUAL 2021 INVESTOR DAY. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CELANESE CORPORATION (NYSE: CE), A GLOBAL CHEMICAL AND SPECIALTY MATERIALS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL HOST A VIRTUAL INVESTOR DAY ON THURSDAY, MARCH 25, 2021. CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, LORI RYERKERK, AND OTHER MEMBERS OF CELANESE'S EXECUTIVE MANAGEMENT TEAM WILL PROVIDE DETAILS ON THE COMPANY'S BUSINESS STRATEGIES AND OUTLINE THE PATH FOR GROWTH THROUGH 2023. THE EVENT WILL BEGIN AT 9:00 A.M. EASTERN TIME AND WILL CONCLUDE AT APPROXIMATELY
