ORGANON MAKES STRATEGIC INVESTMENT IN CLARIA MEDICAL TO SUPPORT DEVELOPMENT OF INVESTIGATIONAL MEDICAL DEVICE FOR MINIMALLY INVASIVE HYSTERECTOMY. JERSEY CITY, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ORGANON (NYSE: OGN), A GLOBAL WOMEN'S HEALTHCARE COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC INVESTMENT IN CLARIA MEDICAL, INC. A PRIVATELY-HELD COMPANY DEVELOPING AN INVESTIGATIONAL MEDICAL DEVICE BEING STUDIED FOR USE DURING MINIMALLY INVASIVE LAPAROSCOPIC HYSTERECTOMY. THE AGREEMENT ALSO GRANTS ORGANON THE OPTION TO ACQUIRE CLARIA MEDICAL.
