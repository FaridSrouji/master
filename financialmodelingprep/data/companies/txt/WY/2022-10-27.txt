WEYERHAEUSER REPORTS THIRD QUARTER RESULTS. ACHIEVED NET EARNINGS OF $310 MILLION, OR $0.42 PER DILUTED SHARE GENERATED $583 MILLION OF ADJUSTED EBITDA REPURCHASED $404 MILLION OF COMMON SHARES YEAR TO DATE, THROUGH THE THIRD QUARTER SEATTLE , OCT. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY (NYSE: WY) TODAY REPORTED THIRD QUARTER NET EARNINGS OF $310 MILLION, OR 42 CENTS PER DILUTED SHARE, ON NET SALES OF $2.3 BILLION. THIS COMPARES WITH NET EARNINGS OF $482 MILLION, OR 64 CENTS PER DILUTED SHARE, ON NET SALES OF $2.3 BILLION FOR THE SAME PERIOD LAST YEAR AND NET EARNINGS OF $788 MILLION FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022.
