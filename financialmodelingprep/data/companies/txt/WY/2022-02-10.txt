WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY ANNOUNCES 5.9% DIVIDEND INCREASE. SEATTLE, FEB. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY (NYSE: WY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY BASE CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.18 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY, REFLECTING A 5.9 PERCENT INCREASE FROM THE PRIOR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.17 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE IN CASH ON MARCH 18, 2022, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD OF SUCH COMMON STOCK AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 4, 2022.
