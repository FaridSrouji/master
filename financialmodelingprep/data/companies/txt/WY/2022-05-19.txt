WEYERHAEUSER COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF TIMBERLANDS IN NORTH AND SOUTH CAROLINA. SEATTLE , MAY 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY  (NYSE: WY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF ITS ACQUISITION OF 80,800 ACRES OF HIGH-QUALITY TIMBERLANDS IN NORTH AND SOUTH CAROLINA FROM A FUND MANAGED BY CAMPBELL GLOBAL FOR APPROXIMATELY $265 MILLION. THE TRANSACTION WAS ANNOUNCED IN APRIL 2022.
