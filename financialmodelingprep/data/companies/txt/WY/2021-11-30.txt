WEYERHAEUSER APPOINTS DAVID GRAHAM AS PRESIDENT OF ITS CANADIAN SUBSIDIARY; FRED DZIDA TO RETIRE. SEATTLE, NOV. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY (NYSE: WY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DAVID GRAHAM AS PRESIDENT OF WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY LIMITED, ITS WHOLLY OWNED CANADIAN SUBSIDIARY, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2022. GRAHAM WILL REPORT TO TRAVIS KEATLEY, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF TIMBERLANDS, AND HIS APPOINTMENT FOLLOWS THE PLANNED RETIREMENT OF FRED DZIDA, WHO HAS SERVED AS PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY'S CANADIAN SUBSIDIARY SINCE 2014 AND WILL STAY ON THROUGH MARCH 2022 TO AID IN THE TRANSITION.
