WEYERHAEUSER ANNOUNCES TAX TREATMENT OF 2021 DISTRIBUTIONS. SEATTLE, JAN. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR SHAREHOLDERS OF WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY (NYSE: WY), THE 2021 DIVIDEND DISTRIBUTIONS TOTALING $1.18 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK CUSIP 96216610 TRADED UNDER THE SYMBOL WY ARE CLASSIFIED FOR INCOME TAX PURPOSES AS CAPITAL GAIN DISTRIBUTIONS, WHICH ARE TREATED AS LONG-TERM CAPITAL GAIN. THE COMMON SHARE DIVIDENDS WERE PAID AS FOLLOWS: $0.17 ON MARCH 19, 2021; $0.17 ON JUNE 18, 2021; $0.17 ON SEPTEMBER 17, 2021; $0.50 ON OCTOBER 19, 2021; AND $0.17 ON DECEMBER 17, 2021.
