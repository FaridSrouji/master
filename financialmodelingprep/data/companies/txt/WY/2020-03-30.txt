WEYERHAEUSER SAYS REDUCING 2020 CAPITAL EXPENDITURES BY ABOUT $70-90 MLN. WEYERHAEUSER PROVIDES COVID-19 BUSINESS UPDATE.WEYERHAEUSER CO - FOR MONTH OF APRIL, COMPANY EXPECTS TO REDUCE OPERATING CAPACITY BY APPROXIMATELY 20 PERCENT FOR LUMBER.WEYERHAEUSER - FOR APRIL, EXPECTS TO REDUCE OPERATING CAPACITY BY 15% FOR ORIENTED STRAND BOARD, AND 15 TO 25 PERCENT FOR ENGINEERED WOOD PRODUCTS.WEYERHAEUSER CO - REDUCING 2020 CAPITAL EXPENDITURES BY APPROXIMATELY $70-90 MILLION.WEYERHAEUSER - INCREASING CASH ON HAND BY $550 MILLION THROUGH DRAW ON REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY,EXPIRES IN JAN 2025 & HAS $950 MILLION OF CAPACITY REMAINING.
