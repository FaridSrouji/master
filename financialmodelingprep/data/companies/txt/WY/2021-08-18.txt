WEYERHAEUSER APPOINTS TRAVIS KEATLEY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF TIMBERLANDS. SEATTLE, AUG. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY (NYSE: WY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF TRAVIS KEATLEY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF TIMBERLANDS, EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 13, 2021. KEATLEY HAS SERVED AS VICE PRESIDENT OF WESTERN TIMBERLANDS FOR THE COMPANY SINCE JANUARY 2020, AND HIS APPOINTMENT FOLLOWS THE PLANNED RETIREMENT OF ADRIAN BLOCKER, WHO WILL STAY ON THROUGH MID-OCTOBER TO SERVE AS A SENIOR ADVISOR AND AID IN THE TRANSITION.
