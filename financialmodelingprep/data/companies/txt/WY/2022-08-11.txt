WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY DECLARES DIVIDEND ON COMMON SHARES. SEATTLE , AUG. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY (NYSE: WY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY BASE CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.18 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY, PAYABLE IN CASH ON SEPTEMBER 16, 2022, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD OF SUCH COMMON STOCK AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 2, 2022. IN FOURTH QUARTER 2020, WEYERHAEUSER IMPLEMENTED A "BASE PLUS VARIABLE SUPPLEMENTAL" DIVIDEND FRAMEWORK.
