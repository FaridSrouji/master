SCOTT MARSHALL NAMED GLOBAL HEAD OF PROLOGIS' CUSTOMER LED SOLUTIONS DIVISION. SAN FRANCISCO, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PROLOGIS, INC. (NYSE: PLD), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LOGISTICS REAL ESTATE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SCOTT MARSHALL HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS GLOBAL HEAD OF ITS CUSTOMER LED SOLUTIONS DIVISION, WHICH DEVELOPS, MANAGES AND EXECUTES RELATIONSHIPS WITH MULTIMARKET CUSTOMERS ACROSS THE GLOBE. REPORTING DIRECTLY TO PROLOGIS CHIEF CUSTOMER OFFICER MICHAEL CURLESS, MARSHALL WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR TRANSFORMING THE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE AND EXPANDING CUSTOMER AND BROKER RELATIONSHIPS.
