EMPLOY PRINCE GEORGE'S LAUNCHES THE TRANSPORTATION AND LOGISTICS CONSORTIUM TRAINING PROGRAM TO PREPARE THOUSANDS FOR DISTRIBUTION AND LOGISTICS JOBS IN THE DMV REGION. LARGO, MD., NOV. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EMPLOY PRINCE GEORGE'S (EPG), THE PREMIER WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT NON-PROFIT ENTITY OF PRINCE GEORGE'S COUNTY, ANNOUNCED ITS NEW TRANSPORTATION AND LOGISTICS CONSORTIUM (TALC) INDUSTRY BRIDGE PROGRAM. IN PARTNERSHIP WITH JOBS FOR THE FUTURE (JFF) AND PROLOGIS, INC. (NYSE: PLD), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LOGISTICS REAL ESTATE, THE TALC PROGRAM WILL HELP RESIDENTS IN PRINCE GEORGE'S COUNTY AND THE SURROUNDING DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, MARYLAND, AND VIRGINIA REGION (DMV), TO GAIN THE NECESSARY SKILLS FOR JOBS AND PLACEMENT IN THE TRANSPORTATION, DISTRIBUTION, WAREHOUSING, AND LOGISTICS INDUSTRIES.
