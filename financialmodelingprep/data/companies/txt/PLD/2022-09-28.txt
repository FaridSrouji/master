PROLOGIS STOCKHOLDERS AND DUKE REALTY SHAREHOLDERS APPROVE MERGER. SAN FRANCISCO AND INDIANAPOLIS , SEPT. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PROLOGIS, INC. (NYSE: PLD) ("PROLOGIS") AND DUKE REALTY CORPORATION (NYSE: DRE) ("DUKE REALTY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PROLOGIS STOCKHOLDERS AND DUKE REALTY SHAREHOLDERS HAVE VOTED, SEPARATELY, TO APPROVE THE PROPOSED MERGER AT THEIR RESPECTIVE SPECIAL MEETINGS HELD VIRTUALLY TODAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2022.
