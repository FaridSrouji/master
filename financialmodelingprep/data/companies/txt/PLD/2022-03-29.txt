FIBRA PROLOGIS ACQUIRES 285,450 SQUARE FEET. MEXICO CITY , MARCH 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIBRA PROLOGIS (BMV: FIBRAPL14), A LEADING OWNER AND OPERATOR OF CLASS-A INDUSTRIAL REAL ESTATE IN MEXICO, HAS ACQUIRED ONE INDUSTRIAL BUILDING IN CIUDAD JUAREZ FROM ITS SPONSOR AND ONE LAST TOUCH® FACILITY IN MEXICO CITY FROM A THIRD PARTY.  THESE ASSETS REPRESENT A TOTAL INVESTMENT OF US$28 MILLION, INCLUDING CLOSING COSTS.
