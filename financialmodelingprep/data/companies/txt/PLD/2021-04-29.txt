PROLOGIS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS. SAN FRANCISCO, APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PROLOGIS, INC. (NYSE: PLD) DECLARED A REGULAR CASH DIVIDEND FOR THE QUARTER ENDING JUNE 30, 2021, ON THE FOLLOWING SECURITIES: A DIVIDEND OF $0.63 PER SHARE OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 30, 2021, TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 16, 2021; AND A DIVIDEND OF $1.0675 PER SHARE OF THE COMPANY'S 8.54% SERIES Q CUMULATIVE REDEEMABLE PREFERRED STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 30, 2021, TO SERIES Q STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 18, 2021. ABOUT PROLOGIS PROLOGIS, INC. IS THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LOGISTICS REAL ESTATE WITH A FOCUS ON HIGH-BARRIER, HIGH-GROWTH MARKETS.
