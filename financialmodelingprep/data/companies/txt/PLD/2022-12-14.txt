OXFORD ECONOMICS: PROLOGIS PROPERTIES FACILITATE GOODS EQUAL TO NEARLY 3% OF THE WORLD'S GDP AND HOUSE 1.1 MILLION JOBS. SAN FRANCISCO , DEC. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PROLOGIS , INC. (NYSE: PLD), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LOGISTICS REAL ESTATE, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH INDEPENDENT ADVISORY FIRM OXFORD ECONOMICS, TODAY RELEASED A NEW STUDY ON THE ECONOMIC IMPACT OF ITS GLOBAL OPERATIONS ENCOMPASSING APPROXIMATELY 1.2 BILLION SQUARE FEET1. ACCORDING TO THE "THE FUTURE FLOW OF GOODS" REPORT, 2.8% OF THE WORLD'S GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT (GDP) FLOWS THROUGH A PROLOGIS PROPERTY (OR NEARLY 3% OF ALL GOODS PRODUCED AND SOLD GLOBALLY CAME THROUGH A PROLOGIS LOGISTICS PROPERTY IN 2022).
