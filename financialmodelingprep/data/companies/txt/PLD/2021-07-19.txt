PROLOGIS REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS RESULTS. SAN FRANCISCO, JULY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PROLOGIS, INC. (NYSE: PLD), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LOGISTICS REAL ESTATE, TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021. NET EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE WAS $0.81 FOR THE QUARTER COMPARED WITH $0.54 FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020.
