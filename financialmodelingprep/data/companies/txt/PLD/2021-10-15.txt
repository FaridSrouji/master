PROLOGIS REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS RESULTS. SAN FRANCISCO, OCT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PROLOGIS, INC. (NYSE: PLD), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LOGISTICS REAL ESTATE, TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021. NET EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE WAS $0.97 FOR THE QUARTER COMPARED WITH $0.40 FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2020.
