WHEELS UP ANNOUNCES COLLABORATION WITH WALDORF ASTORIA HOTELS & RESORTS MARKING ITS LARGEST HOTEL PARTNERSHIP TO DATE. NEW YORK, MARCH 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHEELS UP, THE LEADING BRAND IN PRIVATE AVIATION, ANNOUNCED TODAY A PARTNERSHIP WITH WALDORF ASTORIA HOTELS & RESORTS, HILTON'S ICONIC LUXURY HOTEL BRAND, OFFERING WHEELS UP MEMBERS EXCLUSIVE BENEFITS AT TEN WALDORF ASTORIA PROPERTIES ACROSS NORTH AMERICA. FROM STAR-STUDDED BEVERLY HILLS TO THE SHORES OF MAUI AND BEYOND, WHEELS UP MEMBERS CAN NOW FURTHER INDULGE IN THE EFFORTLESS LUXURY AND SENSE OF PLACE THAT IS FOUND AT ALL WALDORF ASTORIA HOTELS.
