CHARTWELL HOSPITALITY RECEIVES THE 2022 NEW BUILD AWARD FROM HILTON. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHARTWELL HOSPITALITY TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY ARE THE RECIPIENT OF THE 2022 NEW BUILD AWARD FOR THE CONRAD BRAND FOR THE CONRAD NASHVILLE FROM HILTON. THE ANNUAL AMERICAS DEVELOPMENT AWARDS RECOGNIZE THE ACHIEVEMENTS OF DEVELOPMENT PARTNERS, OWNERS AND HOTEL TEAMS IN THE AMERICAS ACROSS NUMEROUS CATEGORIES WHILE CELEBRATING THE RESILIENT AND COMMITTED OWNERS AND TEAM MEMBERS WHO CONTINUE TO SPREAD CHEER AND SHOW PASSION IN THE HOSPITALITY INDUSTRY.
