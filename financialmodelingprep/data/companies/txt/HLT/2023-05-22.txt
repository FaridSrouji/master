ATRIUM HOSPITALITY NAMES JOE BROWNING GENERAL MANAGER OF EMBASSY SUITES BY HILTON EAST PEORIA RIVERFRONT HOTEL & CONFERENCE CENTER IN ILLINOIS. PEORIA, ILL. & ALPHARETTA, GA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JOE BROWNING IS THE NEW GENERAL MANAGER OF THE ATRIUM HOSPITALITY-MANAGED EMBASSY SUITES BY HILTON EAST PEORIA RIVERFRONT HOTEL & CONFERENCE CENTER.
