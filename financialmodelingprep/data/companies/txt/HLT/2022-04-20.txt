THE FIRST ANTICIPATED NET-ZERO HOTEL AND FIRST PASSIVE HOUSE-CERTIFIED HOTEL IN THE US SLATED TO OPEN THIS SPRING. NEW HAVEN, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HOTEL MARCEL NEW HAVEN, TAPESTRY COLLECTION BY HILTON WILL SOON MAKE HISTORY AS WHAT IS BELIEVED WILL BE THE FIRST NET-ZERO HOTEL IN THE UNITED STATES. THE REVOLUTIONARY HOTEL WILL UTILIZE RENEWABLE SOLAR POWER SOURCES ON SITE TO GENERATE THE ELECTRICITY NEEDED FOR ITS COMMON AREAS, RESTAURANT, LAUNDRY, MEETING ROOMS, AND 165 GUEST ROOMS AND SUITES. SET TO DEBUT THIS SPRING, THE ALL-ELECTRIC HOTEL IS CURRENTLY ACCEPTING RESERVATIONS FOR MAY 19, 2022 AND BEYOND
