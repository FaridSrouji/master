HILTON ANNOUNCES THE OPENING OF HILTON HOTEL TAHITI, INVITING THE SOPHISTICATED TRAVELER TO EXPERIENCE FRENCH POLYNESIA. PAPEETE, TAHITI, NOV. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRAVELERS NOW HAVE A NEW ESCAPE WHEN VISITING THE CAPITAL OF FRENCH POLYNESIA, WITH THE OPENING OF THE NEW 200-ROOM HILTON HOTEL TAHITI. CONVENIENTLY OFFERING EASY ACCESS TO THE DOWNTOWN ATTRACTIONS OF PAPEETE AND MARITIME PORT, THE RESORT IS SITUATED JUST TWO MILES FROM THE INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT, ALLOWING GUESTS TO QUICKLY START THEIR VISIT.
