RESORTS WORLD LAS VEGAS TO OPEN JUNE 24, 2021. LAS VEGAS, APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RESORTS WORLD LAS VEGAS, THE FIRST GROUND-UP RESORT DEVELOPMENT ON THE LAS VEGAS STRIP IN MORE THAN A DECADE, WILL OFFICIALLY OPEN ITS DOORS ON JUNE 24, 2021. GUESTS CAN NOW BOOK THEIR STAY AT LAS VEGAS'S HIGHLY ANTICIPATED RESORT BY VISITING WWW.RWLASVEGAS.COM, OR CALLING THE CONTACT CENTER AT 702-676-7000 OR TOLL FREE AT 844-436-8464.
