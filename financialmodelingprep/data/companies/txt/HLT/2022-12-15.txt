LORI HYLAND BECOMES 100% SHAREHOLDER AND OWNER OF HILTON & HYLAND. BEVERLY HILLS, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--HILTON & HYLAND, ONE OF THE WORLD'S MOST VENERATED LUXURY RESIDENTIAL REAL ESTATE BROKERAGES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT LORI HYLAND AND CHAIRMAN AND CO-FOUNDER RICK HILTON HAVE AGREED TO TERMS WHEREIN HYLAND HAS BECOME THE 100% SHAREHOLDER AND OWNER OF HILTON & HYLAND.
