HII RECEIVES ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING APPROVAL FROM NAVAL SEA SYSTEMS COMMAND. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., MARCH 07, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- GLOBAL ALL-DOMAIN DEFENSE PARTNER HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION RECENTLY RECEIVED APPROVAL AS A VENDOR TO PROVIDE SOME ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING COMPONENTS TO NAVAL SEA SYSTEMS (NAVSEA) PLATFORMS.
