UK ROYAL NAVY ACQUIRES LATEST GENERATION REMUS 100S. MCLEAN, VA., SEPT. 21, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- GLOBAL DEFENSE AND TECHNOLOGIES PARTNER HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE DELIVERY OF THREE REMUS 100S UNMANNED UNDERWATER VEHICLES (UUVS) TO THE UNITED KINGDOM'S ROYAL NAVY. THE NEW SYSTEMS BRING ENHANCED ENDURANCE AND THE LATEST GENERATION OF SENSORS AND PAYLOADS, ALLOWING FOR INCREASED DATA QUALITY AND MISSION EFFICIENCY.
