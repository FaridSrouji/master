HII DEMONSTRATES OPEN ARCHITECTURE AUTONOMY INTEGRATION CAPABILITY WITH SEA MACHINES' SM300. THE INTEGRATION OF THESE AUTONOMOUS CAPABILITIES SUPPORTS COMPLEX MISSION PLANNING AND COLLABORATION BETWEEN UNMANNED SYSTEMS.
