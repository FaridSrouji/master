PHOTO RELEASE — HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES EXPANDS UNMANNED CAPABILITIES BY ACQUIRING AUTONOMY BUSINESS FROM SPATIAL INTEGRATED SYSTEMS. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., JAN. 04, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED THE AUTONOMY BUSINESS OF SPATIAL INTEGRATED SYSTEMS INC. (SIS). THE ACQUISITION FURTHER EXPANDS HII'S UNMANNED SYSTEMS CAPABILITIES WITH THIS HIGHLY SKILLED TEAM AND PROVEN UNMANNED SURFACE VESSEL (USV) SOLUTIONS.
