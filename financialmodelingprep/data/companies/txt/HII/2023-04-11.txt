HII IS AWARDED CONTRACT MODIFICATION FOR COLUMBIA-CLASS BALLISTIC MISSILE SUBMARINES. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., APRIL 11, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION HAS BEEN AWARDED A $567.6 MILLION SUBCONTRACT MODIFICATION FROM GENERAL DYNAMICS ELECTRIC BOAT TO PROVIDE LONG-LEAD-TIME MATERIAL AND ADVANCE CONSTRUCTION ACTIVITIES FOR COLUMBIA-CLASS BALLISTIC MISSILE SUBMARINES.
