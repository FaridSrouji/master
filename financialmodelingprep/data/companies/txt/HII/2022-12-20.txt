HII'S PHAROS DEMONSTRATES LAUNCH AND RECOVERY WITH NAVY UNMANNED VEHICLE. NEWPORT, R.I., DEC. 20, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- GLOBAL ALL-DOMAIN DEFENSE PARTNER HII (NYSE: HII) COLLABORATED WITH THE NAVY ON A RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT EFFORT THAT ADVANCED THE LAUNCH AND RECOVERY OF A LARGE SIZE UNMANNED UNDERSEA VEHICLE (UUV), USING AN AMPHIBIOUS SHIP AND HII'S PHAROS SYSTEM.
