HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES TO HOST SECOND QUARTER EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL AND WEBCAST ON AUG. 5. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., JULY 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) WILL RELEASE ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS ON THURSDAY, AUG. 5 AND HOST AN EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL AT 9 A.M. EASTERN TIME THE SAME DAY. THE CALL WILL BE WEBCAST LIVE ON HII'S WEBSITE: HTTP://WWW.HUNTINGTONINGALLS.COM.
