HII HOSTS U.S. REP. MIKE EZELL AT INGALLS SHIPBUILDING. PASCAGOULA, MISS., FEB. 16, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION HOSTED U.S. REP. MIKE EZELL, R-MISS., YESTERDAY FOR HIS FIRST OFFICIAL VISIT TO THE SHIPYARD AS REPRESENTATIVE OF MISSISSIPPI'S 4TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT. DURING HIS VISIT, EZELL MET WITH INGALLS LEADERSHIP AND TOURED THE SHIPYARD INCLUDING AMPHIBIOUS TRANSPORT DOCK SHIP RICHARD M. MCCOOL JR. (LPD 29).
