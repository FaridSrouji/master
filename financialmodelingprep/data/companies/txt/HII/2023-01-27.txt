HII HOSTS CHIEF OF NAVAL OPERATIONS ADM. MICHAEL GILDAY AT INGALLS SHIPBUILDING. PASCAGOULA, MISS., JAN. 27, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF OF NAVAL OPERATIONS ADM. MICHAEL GILDAY VISITED THE COMPANY'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION ON THURSDAY, JAN. 26 DURING A TRIP TO THE GULF COAST. WHILE IN PASCAGOULA, GILDAY MET WITH INGALLS LEADERSHIP AND TOURED THE SHIPYARD INCLUDING AMPHIBIOUS ASSAULT SHIP RICHARD M. MCCOOL JR. (LPD 29) AND GUIDED MISSILE DESTROYER JACK H. LUCAS (DDG 125).
