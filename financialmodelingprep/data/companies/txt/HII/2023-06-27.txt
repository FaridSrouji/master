HII DELIVERS FIRST FLIGHT III DESTROYER JACK H. LUCAS (DDG 125) TO US NAVY. PASCAGOULA, MISS., JUNE 27, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION DELIVERED THE FIRST FLIGHT III ARLEIGH BURKE-CLASS GUIDED MISSILE DESTROYER, JACK H. LUCAS (DDG 125), TO THE U.S. NAVY TODAY. DELIVERY OF DDG 125 REPRESENTS THE OFFICIAL TRANSFER OF THE SHIP FROM THE SHIPBUILDER TO THE NAVY.
