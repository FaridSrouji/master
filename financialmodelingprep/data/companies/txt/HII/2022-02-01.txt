HII COMPLETES ACCEPTANCE SEA TRIALS ON FORT LAUDERDALE (LPD 28). NEWPORT NEWS, VA., FEB. 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF ACCEPTANCE SEA TRIALS ON FRIDAY FOR THE AMPHIBIOUS TRANSPORT DOCK FORT LAUDERDALE (LPD 28). THE SAN ANTONIO-CLASS SHIP, BUILT AT HII'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION, SPENT SEVERAL DAYS WITH THE U.S. NAVY'S BOARD OF INSPECTION AND SURVEY. INGALLS' SHIPBUILDERS WILL NOW COMPLETE FINAL FINISH WORK ON THE SHIP IN PREPARATION FOR DELIVERY THIS QUARTER.
HII ELECTS FRANK R. JIMENEZ TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., FEB. 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FORMER GENERAL COUNSEL OF THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF THE NAVY FRANK R. JIMENEZ HAS BEEN ELECTED TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JAN. 27, 2022.
