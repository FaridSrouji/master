HII AND OCEAN AERO TO PARTNER ON ADVANCED UNMANNED MARITIME CAPABILITIES. MCLEAN, VA. AND GULFPORT, MISS., MARCH 02, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) AND OCEAN AERO INITIATED A STRATEGIC AGREEMENT TO ADVANCE THE COMBINED CAPABILITIES OF THEIR RESPECTIVE UNMANNED MARITIME PLATFORMS AND AUTONOMY SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS. THE UNMANNED SOLUTION PROVIDERS RECENTLY COMMENCED MULTIPLE, SIMULTANEOUS EFFORTS TO ENHANCE THE OPERATIONAL REACH AND DURATION OF THE PLATFORMS, COLLABORATIVE AUTONOMY BEHAVIORS, SHARED SENSOR FUSION AND PERCEPTION CAPABILITIES, AND ACCELERATED SEABED-TO-SHORE DATA TRANSMISSION METHODS.
