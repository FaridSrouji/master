PHOTO RELEASE - HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES NAMES STEWART HOLMES AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GOVERNMENT AND CUSTOMER RELATIONS. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., SEPT. 03, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT STEWART HOLMES HAS BEEN NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GOVERNMENT AND CUSTOMER RELATIONS, SUCCEEDING MITCH WALDMAN, WHO WILL RETIRE ON SEPT. 30.
