PHOTO RELEASE — HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES ANNOUNCES NEW VICE PRESIDENT, BUSINESS MANAGEMENT, AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AT TECHNICAL SOLUTIONS. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., JUNE 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ASHUTOSH GOKHALE HAS BEEN APPOINTED VICE PRESIDENT, BUSINESS MANAGEMENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER FOR ITS TECHNICAL SOLUTIONS DIVISION.
