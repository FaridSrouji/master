HII COMPLETES ACCEPTANCE TRIALS FOR THE FUTURE USS JACK H. LUCAS (DDG 125). PASCAGOULA, MISS., MAY 19, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF ACCEPTANCE TRIALS FOR ARLEIGH BURKE-CLASS GUIDED MISSILE DESTROYER THE FUTURE USS JACK H. LUCAS (DDG 125).
