DESTROYER FRANK E. PETERSEN JR. (DDG 121) SAILED AWAY FROM INGALLS SHIPBUILDING. PASCAGOULA, MISS.,, APRIL 13, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ARLEIGH BURKE-CLASS GUIDED-MISSILE DESTROYER FRANK E. PETERSEN JR. (DDG 121) DEPARTED FROM HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION ON FRIDAY. FRANK E. PETERSEN JR. WILL BE COMMISSIONED NEXT MONTH IN CHARLESTON, SOUTH CAROLINA, BEFORE SAILING TO ITS HOMEPORT AT HAWAII'S JOINT BASE PEARL HARBOR-HICKAM.
