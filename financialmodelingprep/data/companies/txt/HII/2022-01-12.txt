HII ACHIEVES SIGNIFICANT LIGHT-OFF MILESTONE ABOARD GUIDED MISSILE DESTROYER JACK H. LUCAS (DDG 125). PASCAGOULA, MISS., JAN. 12, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES' (NYSE:HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION RECENTLY ACHIEVED THE AEGIS LIGHT-OFF MILESTONE ON THE NAVY'S FIRST FLIGHT III ARLEIGH BURKE-CLASS GUIDED MISSILE DESTROYER JACK H. LUCAS (DDG 125). THIS MILESTONE MARKS THE BEGINNING OF COMBAT SYSTEM TESTING AS SHIPBUILDERS READY THE SHIP FOR PROPULSION TESTS AND EVENTUALLY SEA TRIALS.
