HII ANNOUNCES NEW VICE PRESIDENT OF CONTRACTS AND PRICING AT INGALLS SHIPBUILDING. PASCAGOULA, MISS., APRIL 25, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ALL-DOMAIN DEFENSE AND TECHNOLOGIES PARTNER HII (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT STEPHEN FITTS HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO VICE PRESIDENT OF CONTRACTS AND PRICING AT ITS INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION. FITTS SUCCEEDS ERIC CROOKER WHO ASSUMED A NEW ROLE IN MARCH AS VICE PRESIDENT OF INFRASTRUCTURE AND SUSTAINABILITY.
