HII'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING AWARDED ADVANCED PLANNING CONTRACT FOR ZUMWALT-CLASS SHIPS. PASCAGOULA, MISS., JAN. 06, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION WAS AWARDED A $10.5 MILLION CONTRACT TODAY FOR THE MODERNIZATION PERIOD PLANNING OF ZUMWALT-CLASS GUIDED MISSILE DESTROYERS, USS ZUMWALT (DDG 1000) AND USS MICHAEL MONSOOR (DDG 1001).
