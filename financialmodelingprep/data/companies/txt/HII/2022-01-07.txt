HII LAUNCHES AMPHIBIOUS TRANSPORT DOCK RICHARD M. MCCOOL JR. (LPD 29). PASCAGOULA, MISS., JAN. 07, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES' (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SUCCESSFUL LAUNCH OF AMPHIBIOUS TRANSPORT DOCK RICHARD M. MCCOOL JR. (LPD 29). RICHARD M. MCCOOL JR., THE 13TH LPD IN THE SAN ANTONIO CLASS OF AMPHIBIOUS ASSAULT FORCE SHIPS, WILL SUPPORT U.S. AMPHIBIOUS ASSAULT, SPECIAL OPERATIONS AND EXPEDITIONARY WARFARE MISSIONS THROUGH THE FIRST HALF OF THE 21ST CENTURY.
