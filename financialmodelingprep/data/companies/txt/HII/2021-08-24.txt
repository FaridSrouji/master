PHOTO RELEASE — HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES NAMES BROOKE HART AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, COMMUNICATIONS. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., AUG. 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT BROOKE HART HAS BEEN NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, COMMUNICATIONS. HART WILL SUCCEED JERRI DICKSESKI, WHO WILL RETIRE ON SEPT. 1 AFTER NEARLY 31 YEARS OF SERVICE.
