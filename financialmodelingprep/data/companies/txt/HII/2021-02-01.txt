HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES AWARDED $175 MILLION U.S. NAVY SUPPORT CONTRACT. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., FEB. 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES' (NYSE:HII) TECHNICAL SOLUTIONS DIVISION WAS AWARDED A CONTRACT LAST WEEK TO PROVIDE MAINTENANCE, TRAINING AND PLANNING SUPPORT FOR U.S. NAVY AIRCRAFT CARRIERS. THE INDEFINITE-DELIVERY/INDEFINITE-QUANTITY CONTRACT INCLUDES A FIVE-YEAR ORDERING TERM, WITH A TOTAL POTENTIAL VALUE OF $175 MILLION.
