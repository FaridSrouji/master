HII'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING HONORS EMPLOYEES FOR CONTINUOUS SERVICE. PASCAGOULA, MISS., SEPT. 07, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION IS HONORING 27 EMPLOYEES WHO HAVE ACHIEVED 40 YEARS OF CONTINUOUS SERVICE IN 2022. HII REFERS TO THIS ELITE GROUP AS “MASTER SHIPBUILDERS,” A DESIGNATION RESERVED FOR INDIVIDUALS WHOSE CAREERS IN SHIPBUILDING SPAN FOUR DECADES, NEARLY HALF OF INGALLS' 84-YEAR SHIPBUILDING LEGACY.
