PHOTO RELEASE — HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES EMPLOYEES HONORED AT ANNUAL ENGINEERING CONFERENCES. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., NOV. 12, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FOUR EMPLOYEES FROM ACROSS THE ENTERPRISE RECEIVED ACHIEVEMENT AWARDS DURING THE 2021 SOCIETY OF ASIAN SCIENTISTS AND ENGINEERS (SASE) NATIONAL CONFERENCE AND STEM CAREER FAIR, THE LARGEST CONFERENCE AND CAREER FAIR FOCUSED ON SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, ENGINEERING AND MATH FOR ASIAN AMERICANS IN THE UNITED STATES.
