PHOTO RELEASE — NATIONAL SECURITY CUTTER STONE (WMSL 758) SAILS AWAY FROM INGALLS SHIPBUILDING. PASCAGOULA, MISS., DEC. 22, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE U.S. COAST GUARD'S NEWEST LEGEND-CLASS NATIONAL SECURITY CUTTER, STONE (WMSL 758), DEPARTED FROM HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES' (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION TODAY, SAILING TO ITS HOMEPORT IN CHARLESTON, SOUTH CAROLINA.
