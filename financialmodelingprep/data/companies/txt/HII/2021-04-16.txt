PHOTO RELEASE — HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES ACHIEVES MILESTONE IN REFUELING AND COMPLEX OVERHAUL OF USS GEORGE WASHINGTON (CVN 73). NEWPORT NEWS, VA., APRIL 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES' (NYSE:HII) NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION REACHED A MAJOR MILESTONE ON THE REFUELING AND COMPLEX OVERHAUL (RCOH) OF USS GEORGE WASHINGTON (CVN 73).
