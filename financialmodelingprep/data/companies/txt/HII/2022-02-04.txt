HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., FEB. 04, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.18 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON MARCH 11, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEB. 25, 2022.
