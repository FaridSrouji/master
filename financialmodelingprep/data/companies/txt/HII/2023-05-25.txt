HII REDELIVERS USS GEORGE WASHINGTON (CVN 73) TO U.S. NAVY. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., MAY 25, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION HAS REDELIVERED THE NUCLEAR-POWERED AIRCRAFT CARRIER USS GEORGE WASHINGTON (CVN 73) TO THE U.S. NAVY. THE REDELIVERY TOOK PLACE AFTER SUCCESSFUL SEA TRIALS THAT TESTED THE SHIP'S SYSTEMS FOLLOWING ITS REFUELING AND COMPLEX OVERHAUL (RCOH) AT NNS.
