HII IS AWARDED CONTRACT MODIFICATION FOR AIRCRAFT CARRIER JOHN F. KENNEDY (CVN 79). NEWPORT NEWS, VA., JUNE 23, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING (NNS) DIVISION HAS RECEIVED CONTRACT MODIFICATIONS TOTALING $393.3 MILLION FROM THE U.S. NAVY TO SHIFT THE DELIVERY STRATEGY FOR THE AIRCRAFT CARRIER JOHN F. KENNEDY (CVN 79).
