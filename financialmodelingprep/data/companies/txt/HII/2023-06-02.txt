KEEL OF PITTSBURGH (LPD 31) AUTHENTICATED AT HII'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING. PASCAGOULA, MISS., JUNE 02, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION HAS CEREMONIALLY AUTHENTICATED THE KEEL TODAY FOR THE SAN ANTONIO-CLASS AMPHIBIOUS TRANSPORT DOCK PITTSBURGH (LPD 31). THE KEEL IS THE FOUNDATION OF THE SHIP AND THE AUTHENTICATION CEREMONY IS THE FIRST IMPORTANT MILESTONE IN A SHIP'S LIFE.
