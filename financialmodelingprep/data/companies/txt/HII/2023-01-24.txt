HII'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING GRANTS $100,000 IN STEM AWARDS. PASCAGOULA, MISS., JAN. 24, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION AWARDED $100,000 IN GRANTS TO 26 STEM-RELATED INITIATIVES FROM SCHOOLS AND EDUCATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS LOCATED IN MISSISSIPPI AND ALABAMA. THE STEM AWARDS ARE CURRENTLY DISTRIBUTED ON AN ANNUAL BASIS AND ARE USED TO FURTHER THE EDUCATION OF SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, ENGINEERING AND MATH.
HII NAMES NEW EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., JAN. 24, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT CHRIS SOONG HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER. SOONG, PREVIOUSLY THE CIO AT HII'S MISSION TECHNOLOGIES DIVISION, WILL REPLACE BHARAT AMIN, WHO WILL RETIRE ON MARCH 31 AFTER NEARLY A DECADE OF SERVICE WITH THE COMPANY.
