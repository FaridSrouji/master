HII AWARDED $1.3 BILLION CONTRACT FOR THE DETAIL DESIGN & CONSTRUCTION OF LPD 32. PASCAGOULA, MISS., MARCH 31, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION HAS RECEIVED A $1.3 BILLION MODIFICATION TO A PREVIOUSLY AWARDED CONTRACT FROM THE U.S. NAVY FOR THE PROCUREMENT OF THE DETAIL DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF AMPHIBIOUS TRANSPORT DOCK LPD 32. THE RESULTING FIXED-PRICE-INCENTIVE CONTRACT TOTALS $1.54 BILLION. THE SHIP WILL BE THE 16TH IN THE SAN ANTONIO CLASS AND THE THIRD FLIGHT II LPD.
