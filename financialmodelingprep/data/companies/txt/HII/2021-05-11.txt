HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES BEGINS FABRICATION OF NATIONAL SECURITY CUTTER FRIEDMAN (NSC 11). PASCAGOULA, MISS., MAY 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES' (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION ANNOUNCED TODAY THE START OF FABRICATION OF LEGEND-CLASS NATIONAL SECURITY CUTTER FRIEDMAN (NSC 11). THE START OF FABRICATION SIGNIFIES THE FIRST 100 TONS OF STEEL HAVE BEEN CUT.
