PHOTO RELEASE — HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES LAUNCHES VIRGINIA-CLASS SUBMARINE MONTANA (SSN 794). NEWPORT NEWS, VA., MARCH 03, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE VIRGINIA-CLASS SUBMARINE MONTANA (SSN 794) WAS LAUNCHED INTO THE JAMES RIVER AT THE COMPANY'S NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION.
