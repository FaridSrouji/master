HII AWARDED $995 MILLION CONTRACT TO SUPPORT U.S. AIR FORCE IN EUROPE-AIR FORCES AFRICA. MCLEAN, VA., APRIL 26, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) MISSION TECHNOLOGIES DIVISION WAS AWARDED A $995 MILLION CONTRACT TO PROVIDE ADVISORY AND ASSISTANCE SERVICES FOR THE U.S AIR FORCES IN EUROPE-AIR FORCES AFRICA (USAFE-AFAFRICA). THE INDEFINITE DELIVERY, INDEFINITE QUANTITY, MULTIPLE-AWARD CONTRACT HAS A ONE-YEAR BASE PERIOD OF PERFORMANCE AND FOUR ONE-YEAR OPTION PERIODS.
