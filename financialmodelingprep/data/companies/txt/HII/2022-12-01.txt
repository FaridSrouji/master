HII DELIVERS DESTROYER LENAH SUTCLIFFE HIGBEE (DDG 123) TO U S. NAVY. PASCAGOULA, MISS., DEC. 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION DELIVERED THE ARLEIGH BURKE-CLASS GUIDED MISSILE DESTROYER LENAH SUTCLIFFE HIGBEE (DDG 123) TO THE U.S. NAVY TODAY. DELIVERY OF DDG 123 REPRESENTS THE OFFICIAL TRANSFER OF THE SHIP FROM THE SHIPBUILDER TO THE NAVY.
