HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO $1.18 PER SHARE. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., NOV. 03, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.18 PER SHARE, A $0.04 INCREASE OVER THE $1.14 PER SHARE DIVIDEND PAID IN EACH OF THE PRIOR FOUR QUARTERS. THE $1.18 PER SHARE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON DEC. 10, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOV. 26, 2021.
