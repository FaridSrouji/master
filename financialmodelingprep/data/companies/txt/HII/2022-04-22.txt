HII ANNOUNCES TODD CORILLO AS NEW MEDIA RELATIONS MANAGER AT NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., APRIL 22, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ALL-DOMAIN DEFENSE AND TECHNOLOGIES PARTNER HII (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TODD CORILLO WILL JOIN ITS CORPORATE COMMUNICATIONS TEAM AS A MANAGER OF MEDIA RELATIONS MAY 9. HE WILL SERVE AS HII'S SPOKESPERSON FOR THE COMPANY'S NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION AND REPORT TO HII'S CORPORATE DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC AFFAIRS.
