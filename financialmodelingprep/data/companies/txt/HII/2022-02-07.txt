HII COMPLETES INITIAL SEA TRIALS OF VIRGINIA-CLASS SUBMARINE MONTANA (SSN 794). NEWPORT NEWS, VA., FEB. 07, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF THE INITIAL SEA TRIALS LAST WEEK OF FAST ATTACK SUBMARINE MONTANA (SSN 794). THE VIRGINIA-CLASS SUBMARINE, BUILT AT HII'S NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION, SPENT SEVERAL DAYS AT SEA LAST WEEK TO TEST THE SHIP'S SYSTEMS AND COMPONENTS.
