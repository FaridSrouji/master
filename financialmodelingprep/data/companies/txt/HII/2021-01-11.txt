HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES COMPLETES FIRST PHASE OF UNMANNED SYSTEMS CENTER OF EXCELLENCE CAMPUS. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., JAN. 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE: HII) HAS COMPLETED THE FIRST PHASE OF ITS UNMANNED SYSTEMS CENTER OF EXCELLENCE WITH THE CONSTRUCTION OF A 22,000-SQUARE-FOOT FACILITY.
