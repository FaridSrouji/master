HII ANNOUNCES NEW VICE PRESIDENT OF CONTRACTS AT NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., JAN. 04, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MATT MULHERIN JR. HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO VICE PRESIDENT OF CONTRACTS AT ITS NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION. MULHERIN ASSUMED THE NEW ROLE ON MONDAY, JAN. 3, SUCCEEDING CHRISTIE THOMAS, WHO HAS BEEN APPOINTED CORPORATE VICE PRESIDENT, INVESTOR RELATIONS.
