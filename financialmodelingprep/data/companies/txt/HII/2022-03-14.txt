HII DELIVERS VIRGINIA-CLASS SUBMARINE MONTANA (SSN 794) TO U.S. NAVY. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., MARCH 14, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII, AMERICA'S LARGEST SHIPBUILDER AND LEADING PROVIDER OF MISSION-DRIVEN DEFENSE TECHNOLOGIES, ON FRIDAY DELIVERED THE NEWEST VIRGINIA-CLASS FAST-ATTACK SUBMARINE TO THE U.S. NAVY.
