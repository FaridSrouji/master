HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2020 RESULTS. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., NOV. 05, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) REPORTED THIRD QUARTER 2020 REVENUES OF $2.3 BILLION, UP 4.3% FROM THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2019. THE INCREASE WAS DRIVEN BY GROWTH AT BOTH HII'S NEWPORT NEWS AND INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISIONS.
