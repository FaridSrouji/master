HII'S NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING PROMOTES THREE TO SENIOR LEADERSHIP TEAM. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., MAY 31, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PROMOTION OF THREE SEASONED LEADERS TO THE SENIOR LEADERSHIP TEAM TO FILL THE ROLES OF UPCOMING RETIREMENTS.
