HII REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., AUG. 04, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE:HII) REPORTED SECOND QUARTER 2022 REVENUES OF $2.7 BILLION, UP 19.3% FROM THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021, PRIMARILY DRIVEN BY REVENUE ATTRIBUTABLE TO THE ACQUISITION OF ALION SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (ALION) IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021, AS WELL AS GROWTH AT NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING.
