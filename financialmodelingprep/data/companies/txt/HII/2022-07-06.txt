HII'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING TO HIRE 2,000-PLUS FULL-TIME SHIPBUILDERS. PASCAGOULA, MISS., JULY 06, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- GLOBAL DEFENSE AND TECHNOLOGIES PARTNER HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION PLANS TO HIRE OVER 2,000 FULL-TIME SHIPBUILDERS AS PART OF ITS FUTURE WORKFORCE PLANS.
