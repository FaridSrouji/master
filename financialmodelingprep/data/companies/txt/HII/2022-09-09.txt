HII BEGINS FABRICATION OF AMPHIBIOUS TRANSPORT DOCK PITTSBURGH (LPD 31). PASCAGOULA, MISS., SEPT. 09, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII'S (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION STARTED FABRICATION OF THE U.S. NAVY'S NEWEST SAN ANTONIO-CLASS AMPHIBIOUS TRANSPORT DOCK PITTSBURGH (LPD 31) ON WEDNESDAY. THE START OF FABRICATION SIGNIFIES THAT THE FIRST 100 TONS OF STEEL HAVE BEEN CUT FOR THE SHIP.
