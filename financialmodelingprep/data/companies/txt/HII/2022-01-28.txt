HII NAMES CHRIS KASTNER PRESIDENT & CEO; MIKE PETTERS TO BECOME EXECUTIVE VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., JAN. 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED THURSDAY ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS ELECTED CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER CHRIS KASTNER TO BECOME HII PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, CONSISTENT WITH THE COMPANY'S SUCCESSION PLAN. THE BOARD ALSO ELECTED PRESIDENT AND CEO MIKE PETTERS TO BECOME EXECUTIVE VICE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD FOR A TRANSITION PERIOD. BOTH CHANGES ARE EFFECTIVE MARCH 1.
