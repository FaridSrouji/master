INGALLS SHIPBUILDING APPOINTS KIMBERLY NASTASI TO VICE PRESIDENT, INTEGRATED COMMUNICATIONS AND STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT. PASCAGOULA, MISS., JAN. 11, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION PROMOTED KIMBERLY NASTASI TO VICE PRESIDENT OF THE NEWLY FORMED INTEGRATED COMMUNICATIONS AND STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT DEPARTMENT. THIS NEW DEPARTMENT WILL ENHANCE COMMUNICATION AND ENGAGEMENT WITH KEY STAKEHOLDERS INSIDE AND OUTSIDE OF THE SHIPBUILDING COMMUNITY.
