HII IS AWARDED $127 MILLION DEFENSE CONTRACT FOR RESEARCH, DEVELOPMENT, TEST AND EVALUATION OF EMERGING TECHNOLOGIES. MCLEAN, VA., AUG. 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- GLOBAL DEFENSE AND TECHNOLOGIES PARTNER HII (NYSE: HII) HAS BEEN AWARDED A $127 MILLION TASK ORDER TO SUPPORT THE DEFENSE SECURITY COOPERATION AGENCY (DSCA) TO PERFORM RESEARCH, DEVELOPMENT, TEST AND EVALUATION OF EMERGING TECHNOLOGIES.
