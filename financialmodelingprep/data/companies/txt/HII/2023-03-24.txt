HII ANNOUNCES NEW VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF COUNSEL OF INGALLS SHIPBUILDING. PASCAGOULA, MISS., MARCH 24, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JULIE JARRELL GRESHAM HAS BEEN NAMED VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF COUNSEL OF THE COMPANY'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION. SHE WILL SUCCEED GEORGE SIMMERMAN JR. WHO WILL RETIRE MARCH 31, 2023, AFTER 32 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY.
