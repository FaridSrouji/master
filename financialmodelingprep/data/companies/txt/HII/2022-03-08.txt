HII ANNOUNCES LABOR CONTRACT AGREEMENT WITH UNITED STEELWORKERS AT NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., MARCH 08, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- GLOBAL ENGINEERING AND DEFENSE TECHNOLOGIES PROVIDER HII (NYSE:HII) WAS NOTIFIED TODAY THAT UNITED STEELWORKER MEMBERS AT THE COMPANY'S NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION VOTED IN FAVOR OF A NEW LABOR CONTRACT. THE NEW CONTRACT TOOK EFFECT MONDAY AND WILL RUN THROUGH FEB. 7, 2027.
