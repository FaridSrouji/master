HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES AWARDED $187 MILLION ADVANCE PROCUREMENT CONTRACT FOR AMPHIBIOUS ASSAULT SHIP LHA 9.HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES SAYS ITS INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION HAS RECEIVED A $187.46 MILLION ADVANCE PROCUREMENT CONTRACT FROM U.S. NAVY.
