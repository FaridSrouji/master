HII'S MISSION TECHNOLOGIES DIVISION APPOINTS SENIOR DIRECTOR OF TALENT DEVELOPMENT JEFF JACKSON. MCLEAN, VA., JUNE 10, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JEFF JACKSON HAS BEEN NAMED SENIOR DIRECTOR OF TALENT DEVELOPMENT FOR ITS MISSION TECHNOLOGIES DIVISION. HE WILL REPORT TO MELANIE ANDERSON, MISSION TECHNOLOGIES' SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF HUMAN RESOURCES.
