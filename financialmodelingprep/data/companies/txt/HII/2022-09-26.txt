HII NAMES TODD BORKEY AS CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., SEPT. 26, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- GLOBAL DEFENSE AND TECHNOLOGIES PARTNER HII (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TODD BORKEY HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY AND REPORTING DIRECTLY TO HII PRESIDENT AND CEO CHRIS KASTNER.
