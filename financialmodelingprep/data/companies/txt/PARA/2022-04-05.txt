PARAMOUNT GLOBAL DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDENDS. NEW YORK , APRIL 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PARAMOUNT GLOBAL (NASDAQ: PARA; PARAA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE ON BOTH ITS CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON JULY 1, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 15, 2022.
