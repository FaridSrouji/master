PARAMOUNT APPOINTS PAMELA KAUFMAN TO LEAD INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS AS PRESIDENT AND CEO, INTERNATIONAL MARKETS, GLOBAL CONSUMER PRODUCTS & EXPERIENCES. *CLICK HERE FOR IMAGE ASSETS NEW YORK , JULY 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PARAMOUNT GLOBAL (NASDAQ: PARA, PARAA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PAMELA KAUFMAN, PRESIDENT OF GLOBAL CONSUMER PRODUCTS AND EXPERIENCES FOR PARAMOUNT, HAS BEEN APPOINTED PRESIDENT AND CEO OF INTERNATIONAL MARKETS, GLOBAL CONSUMER PRODUCTS & EXPERIENCES, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. THIS NEWLY CREATED POSITION REPORTS TO BOB BAKISH, PRESIDENT AND CEO OF PARAMOUNT, AND REFLECTS PARAMOUNT'S ONGOING STRATEGY OF GLOBALIZING ITS OPERATIONS.
