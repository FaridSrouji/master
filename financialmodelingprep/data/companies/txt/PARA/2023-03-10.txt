PARAMOUNT GLOBAL DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDENDS. NEW YORK , MARCH 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PARAMOUNT GLOBAL (NASDAQ: PARA, PARAA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE ON BOTH ITS CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON APRIL 3, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 20, 2023.
