PARAMOUNT GLOBAL FILES PROXY STATEMENT, ANNOUNCES CHANGES TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW YORK , MARCH 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PARAMOUNT GLOBAL (NASDAQ: PARA, PARAA) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY FILED ITS PROXY STATEMENT AND ANNOUNCED CHANGES TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE "BOARD"), INCLUDING THE ADDITION OF DAWN OSTROFF AS AN INDEPENDENT, NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR, PENDING A STOCKHOLDER VOTE AT PARAMOUNT'S 2023 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS, WHICH WILL BE HELD ON MONDAY, MAY 8, 2023. MS. OSTROFF IS AN ACCOMPLISHED MEDIA & ENTERTAINMENT INDUSTRY EXECUTIVE, WITH EXTENSIVE EXPERIENCE ACROSS CONTENT DEVELOPMENT, MULTIPLATFORM DISTRIBUTION AND ADVERTISING.
