TRIMBLE ACQUIRES AGILEASSETS TO EXPAND ITS INFRASTRUCTURE SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS PORTFOLIO. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS ACQUIRED AGILEASSETS, A PROVIDER OF ENTERPRISE INFRASTRUCTURE ASSET MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE TO PRIVATE ORGANIZATIONS AND GOVERNMENT, INCLUDING NATIONAL ROAD AUTHORITIES AND STATE-LEVEL TRANSPORTATION AGENCIES IN THE UNITED STATES.
