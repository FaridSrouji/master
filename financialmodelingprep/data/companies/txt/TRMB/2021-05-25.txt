TRIMBLE INTRODUCES VEGETATION MANAGEMENT SOLUTION FOR IMPROVING ELECTRIC UTILITY INFRASTRUCTURE RESILIENCE. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ:TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY A NEW SOLUTION THAT HELPS UTILITIES BETTER MANAGE THE RISK POSED BY VEGETATION-RELATED OUTAGES—TRIMBLE® VEGETATION MANAGER.
