TRIMBLE LAUNCHES FIRST INDUSTRY DWELL TIME METRICS FOR FLEET MANAGEMENT. METRICS HELP IMPROVE DELIVERY, SCHEDULING AND DRIVER UTILIZATION WESTMINSTER, COLO. , MARCH 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TRIMBLE FLEET MANAGER, PROVIDED AS PART OF ITS MOBILITY TELEMATICS PORTFOLIO, IS NOW THE FIRST BACK-OFFICE FLEET MANAGEMENT SOLUTION TO OFFER INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC AVERAGE DWELL TIME METRICS.
