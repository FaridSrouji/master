TRIMBLE CHANGES HEADQUARTERS TO WESTMINSTER, COLORADO. SUNNYVALE CAMPUS NAMED SILICON VALLEY CENTER FOR INNOVATION AND OPERATIONS WESTMINSTER, COLO. , OCT. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY HAS CHANGED ITS HEADQUARTERS FROM SUNNYVALE, CALIFORNIA TO WESTMINSTER, COLORADO.
