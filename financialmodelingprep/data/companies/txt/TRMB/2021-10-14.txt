TRIMBLE INTRODUCES CONNECTED MODEL-BASED ESTIMATING WORKFLOW FOR MECHANICAL PIPING AND ELECTRICAL CONTRACTORS. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) INTRODUCED TODAY ITS MODEL-BASED ESTIMATING WORKFLOW, WHICH SEAMLESSLY CONNECTS THE BUILDING INFORMATION MODEL (BIM) TO THE ESTIMATE FOR MECHANICAL PIPING AND ELECTRICAL CONTRACTORS.
