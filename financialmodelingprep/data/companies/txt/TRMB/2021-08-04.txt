TRIMBLE LAUNCHES $200 MILLION VENTURE FUND. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., AUG. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE CREATION OF TRIMBLE VENTURES, A VENTURE FUND FOCUSED ON INVESTING IN EARLY AND GROWTH-STAGE INNOVATIVE COMPANIES THAT ALIGN WITH TRIMBLE'S MISSION OF TRANSFORMING WORK IN THE AGRICULTURE, CONSTRUCTION, GEOSPATIAL AND TRANSPORTATION INDUSTRIES.
