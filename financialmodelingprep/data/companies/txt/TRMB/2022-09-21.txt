TRIMBLE VENTURES INVESTS IN CIV ROBOTICS--A CONSTRUCTION TECH STARTUP FOCUSED ON AUTONOMOUS SURVEYING SOLUTIONS. SUNNYVALE, CALIF. , SEPT. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE VENTURES, TRIMBLE'S (NASDAQ: TRMB) CORPORATE VENTURE CAPITAL FUND, ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS INVESTMENT IN CIV ROBOTICS, A SAN FRANCISCO-BASED CONSTRUCTION TECH STARTUP FOCUSING ON TRANSFORMING SURVEYING LAYOUT FOR CIVIL ENGINEERING AND INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS.
