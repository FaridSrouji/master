TRIMBLE AND MICROSOFT PARTNER TO DRIVE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION ACROSS INDUSTRIES. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) AND MICROSOFT ANNOUNCED TODAY A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO ADVANCE TECHNOLOGY ADOPTION AND ACCELERATE THE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION OF THE CONSTRUCTION, AGRICULTURE AND TRANSPORTATION INDUSTRIES.
