TRIMBLE TO DIVEST FOUR BUSINESSES TO THE JORDAN COMPANY. SUNNYVALE, CALIF. , APRIL 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS TIME AND FREQUENCY, LOADRITE, SPECTRA PRECISION TOOLS AND SECO ACCESSORIES BUSINESSES TO PRECISIONAL LLC, AN AFFILIATE OF THE JORDAN COMPANY ("TJC").
