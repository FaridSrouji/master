OSLO METROPOLITAN UNIVERSITY IN NORWAY TO ESTABLISH TRIMBLE TECHNOLOGY LAB FOR CIVIL ENGINEERING AND ENERGY TECHNOLOGY. SUNNYVALE, CALIF. AND OSLO, NORWAY, JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORWAY'S OSLO METROPOLITAN UNIVERSITY (OSLOMET) HAS RECEIVED A GIFT FROM TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) TO ESTABLISH A STATE-OF-THE-ART TECHNOLOGY LAB FOR CIVIL ENGINEERING AND ENERGY TECHNOLOGY.
