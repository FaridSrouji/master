TRIMBLE TO ACQUIRE BILBERRY TO EXTEND ITS SELECTIVE SPRAYING CAPABILITIES FOR SUSTAINABLE FARMING. SUNNYVALE, CALIF. , AUG. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE PRIVATELY-HELD BILBERRY, A FRENCH TECHNOLOGY COMPANY SPECIALIZING IN SELECTIVE SPRAYING SYSTEMS FOR SUSTAINABLE FARMING.
