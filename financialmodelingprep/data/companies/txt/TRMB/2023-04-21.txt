TRIMBLE BREAKS GROUND ON 1.7-MEGAWATT SOLAR ARRAY AT WESTMINSTER, COLORADO HEADQUARTERS. PROJECT TO OFFSET MORE THAN 100 PERCENT OF TRIMBLE'S ON-SITE ENERGY CONSUMPTION,  INCLUDES 660-KILOWATT SOLAR CARPORT WITH 49 ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGING STATIONS WESTMINSTER, COLO. , APRIL 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY HAS BROKEN GROUND ON THE CONSTRUCTION OF A 1.7-MEGAWATT SOLAR ARRAY AS A RENEWABLE ENERGY SOURCE AT ITS WESTMINSTER, COLORADO HEADQUARTERS.
