TRIMBLE VENTURES INVESTS IN SABANTO--AN AUTONOMOUS FARMING-AS-A-SERVICE COMPANY. SUNNYVALE, CALIF. , AUG. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE VENTURES, TRIMBLE'S (NASDAQ: TRMB) CORPORATE VENTURE CAPITAL FUND, ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS INVESTMENT IN SABANTO, INC. SABANTO IS A U.S.-BASED AGRICULTURAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY FOCUSING ON AUTONOMOUS WORKFLOWS THROUGHOUT THE FARMING CYCLE BEING OFFERED AS FARMING AS A SERVICE (FAAS).
