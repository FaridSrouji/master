MIX TELEMATICS TO ACQUIRE TRIMBLE'S FIELD SERVICE MANAGEMENT BUSINESS. BOCA RATON, FLA. , AUG. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MIX TELEMATICS (JSE: MIX) (NYSE: MIXT), A LEADING GLOBAL SAAS PROVIDER OF CONNECTED FLEET MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE TRIMBLE'S FIELD SERVICE MANAGEMENT'S (FSM) BUSINESS.
