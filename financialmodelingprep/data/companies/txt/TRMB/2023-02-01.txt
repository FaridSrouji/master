ONERAIL LEVERAGES TRIMBLE TECHNOLOGY TO ENHANCE LAST MILE LOGISTICS. LAS VEGAS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) AND ONERAIL, AN ORLANDO-BASED TRANSPORTATION TECHNOLOGY STARTUP FOCUSING ON LAST MILE LOGISTICS AND VISIBILITY SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE INTEGRATION OF TRIMBLE MAPS TECHNOLOGY AS PART OF ONERAIL'S DELIVERY PLATFORM TO ENHANCE LAST MILE LOGISTICS.
