TRIMBLE AND CLAAS STRATEGIC ALLIANCE DEVELOPS NEXT-GENERATION PRECISION FARMING SYSTEM FOR CLAAS AGRICULTURE EQUIPMENT. SUNNYVALE, CALIF. , AUG. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF A STRATEGIC ALLIANCE, TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) AND CLAAS HAVE DEVELOPED A NEXT-GENERATION PRECISION FARMING SYSTEM FOR CLAAS TRACTORS, COMBINES AND FORAGE HARVESTERS.
