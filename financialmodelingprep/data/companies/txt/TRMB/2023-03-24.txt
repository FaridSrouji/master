TRIMBLE JOINS RESPONSIBLESTEEL TO FURTHER SUSTAINABILITY EFFORTS IN THE STEEL INDUSTRY. FIRST SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGY PROVIDER TO JOIN INDUSTRY ORGANIZATION  WESTMINSTER, COLO. , MARCH 24, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS JOINED RESPONSIBLESTEEL, AN INDEPENDENT, NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION THAT IS THE STEEL INDUSTRY'S FIRST GLOBAL MULTI-STAKEHOLDER STANDARD AND CERTIFICATION INITIATIVE.
