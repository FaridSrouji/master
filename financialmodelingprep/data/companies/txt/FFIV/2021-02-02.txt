F5 NETWORKS REPORTS INDUCEMENT GRANTS UNDER NASDAQ LISTING RULE 5635(C)(4). SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--F5 NETWORKS (NASDAQ: FFIV) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ON FEBRUARY 1, 2021, THE COMPANY ISSUED A TOTAL OF 122,056 RESTRICTED STOCK UNITS (“RSUS”) TO 71 EMPLOYEES WHO JOINED F5 AS A RESULT OF THE ACQUISITION OF VOLTERRA INC., COMPLETED ON JANUARY 22, 2021. THE RSUS WERE GRANTED AS INDUCEMENTS TO EMPLOYMENT IN ACCORDANCE WITH NASDAQ LISTING RULE 5635(C)(4).
