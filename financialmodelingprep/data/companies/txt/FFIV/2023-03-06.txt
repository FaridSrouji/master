F5 ANNOUNCES PARTNERSHIP WITH VISA TO EMPOWER MERCHANTS TO CREATE A SECURE, PERSONALIZED, AND SEAMLESS ONLINE EXPERIENCE. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--F5 (NASDAQ: FFIV) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS PARTNERING WITH VISA, THE WORLD LEADER IN DIGITAL PAYMENTS, TO HELP MERCHANTS SECURELY REDUCE LOGIN FRICTION FOR CUSTOMERS. CUSTOMERS EXPECT SEAMLESS COMMERCE EXPERIENCES AND TRANSACTIONS TO BE SECURE. YET, IN TODAY'S DIGITAL-FIRST WORLD, CUSTOMERS ARE UNDER THREAT FROM BAD ACTORS LOOKING TO STEAL DATA AND COMMIT FRAUD.
