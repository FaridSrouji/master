‘ENTERPRISE ARCHITECTURE FOR DIGITAL BUSINESS' OUTLINES WHY AND HOW TO MODERNIZE IT FOR A DIGITAL-FIRST WORLD. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS ORGANIZATIONS BUILD FOR A DIGITAL-FIRST WORLD, DISAPPOINTMENT AND DISRUPTION LIE AHEAD. THE CHALLENGE IS ROOTED IN THE FACT THAT ORGANIZATIONS ARE BANKING THEIR FUTURE ON AN ARCHITECTURAL APPROACH THAT WAS DEVELOPED BEFORE MOBILE AND THE INTERNET BECAME PERVASIVE, AND WELL BEFORE THE ERA OF DIGITIZATION.
