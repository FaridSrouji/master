F5 TO ACQUIRE VOLTERRA TO CREATE THE FIRST EDGE 2.0 PLATFORM FOR ENTERPRISES AND SERVICE PROVIDERS. SEATTLE & SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--F5 NETWORKS (NASDAQ: FFIV), THE LEADER IN APPLICATION SECURITY AND DELIVERY, AND VOLTERRA, THE FIRST UNIVERSAL EDGE-AS-A-SERVICE PLATFORM, TODAY ANNOUNCED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH F5 WILL ACQUIRE ALL ISSUED AND OUTSTANDING SHARES OF PRIVATELY HELD VOLTERRA FOR APPROXIMATELY $440 MILLION IN CASH AND APPROXIMATELY $60 MILLION IN DEFERRED CONSIDERATION AND ASSUMED UNVESTED INCENTIVE COMPENSATION TO FOUNDERS AND EMPLOYEES.
