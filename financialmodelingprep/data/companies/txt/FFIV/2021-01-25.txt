SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--F5 NETWORKS (NASDAQ: FFIV), THE LEADER IN APPLICATION SECURITY AND DELIVERY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF VOLTERRA, THE FIRST UNIVERSAL EDGE-AS-A-SERVICE PLATFORM.
