UNIVERSAL HEALTH SERVICES, INC. ANNOUNCES DIVIDEND. KING OF PRUSSIA, PA., AUG. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNIVERSAL HEALTH SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: UHS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS VOTED TO PAY A CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.20 PER SHARE ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF SEPTEMBER 1, 2021.
