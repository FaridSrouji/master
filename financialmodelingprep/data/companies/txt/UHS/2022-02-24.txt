UNIVERSAL HEALTH SERVICES, INC. ANNOUNCES 2021 FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR FINANCIAL RESULTS, 2022 FULL YEAR EARNINGS GUIDANCE AND $1.4 BILLION INCREASE TO STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM. KING OF PRUSSIA, PA., FEB. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNIVERSAL HEALTH SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: UHS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS REPORTED NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO UHS WAS $239.1 MILLION, OR $3.00 PER DILUTED SHARE, DURING THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021, AS COMPARED TO $308.7 MILLION, OR $3.60 PER DILUTED SHARE, DURING THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020.
