PSEG ANNOUNCES 2020 FIRST QUARTER RESULTS. PSEG ANNOUNCES 2020 FIRST QUARTER RESULTS.SEES FY 2020 NON-GAAP OPERATING EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.30 TO $3.50.PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP - QTRLY EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.88.PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP - QTRLY NON-GAAP OPERATING EARNINGS $1.03 PER SHARE.PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP - QTRLY  OPERATING REVENUES $2,781 MILLION VERSUS $2,980 MILLION.RE-AFFIRMS NON-GAAP 2020 OPERATING EARNINGS GUIDANCE OF $3.30 - $3.50 PER SHARE.PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP - PSE&G SERVICE TERRITORY EXPERIENCED DECLINE OF ABOUT 5% TO 7% IN ELECTRIC LOAD FROM END OF MARCH THROUGH APRIL.PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP - ANTICIPATE REDUCTION IN ELECTRIC LOAD FOR PSE&G SERVICE TERRITORY MAY EXTEND THROUGH Q2 AND POSSIBLY LONGER.PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP - REMAIN COMMITTED TO EXECUTING OUR FIVE-YEAR, $12 TO $16 BILLION CAPITAL PLAN WITHOUT NEED TO ISSUE EQUITY.PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP - IMPROVED OUR NET LIQUIDITY POSITION, ENDING MARCH WITH APPROXIMATELY $4 BILLION OF AVAILABLE LIQUIDITY.PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP - COVID-19 RELATED REDUCTION IN DEMAND APPEARED LATE IN QUARTER.
