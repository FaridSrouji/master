PSEG INCREASES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. NEWARK, N.J., FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP (NYSE: PEG) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022 OF $0.54 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON MARCH 31, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MARCH 10, 2022.
