PSEG DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FOR THIRD QUARTER OF 2022. NEWARK, N.J. , JULY 19, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP (NYSE:PEG) TODAY DECLARED A $0.54 PER SHARE DIVIDEND ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022.
