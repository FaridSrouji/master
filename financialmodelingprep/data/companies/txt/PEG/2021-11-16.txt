PSEG DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FOR FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021. NEWARK, N.J., NOV. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP (NYSE:PEG) TODAY DECLARED A $0.51 PER SHARE DIVIDEND ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021.
