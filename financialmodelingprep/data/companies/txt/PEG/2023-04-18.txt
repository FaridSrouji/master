PSEG DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FOR SECOND QUARTER OF 2023. NEWARK, N.J. , APRIL 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP (NYSE:PEG) TODAY DECLARED A $0.57 PER SHARE DIVIDEND ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2023.
