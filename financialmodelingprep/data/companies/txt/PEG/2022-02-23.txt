PSEG CLOSES ON SALE OF NEW YORK AND CONNECTICUT GENERATING ASSETS TO COMPLETE SALE OF PSEG FOSSIL PORTFOLIO TO AFFILIATES OF ARCLIGHT CAPITAL PARTNERS. NEWARK, N.J., FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP (PSEG) TODAY COMPLETED THE SALE OF ITS FOSSIL GENERATING ASSETS IN NEW YORK AND CONNECTICUT TO SUBSIDIARIES OF ARCLIGHT ENERGY PARTNERS FUND VII, L.P.
