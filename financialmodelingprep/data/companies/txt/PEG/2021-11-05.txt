PSEG CEO RALPH IZZO ATTENDS COP26, CALLS FOR URGENT ACTION ON CLIMATE. NEWARK, N.J., NOV. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PSEG CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CEO RALPH IZZO IS TRAVELING TO GLASGOW, SCOTLAND, TO ATTEND THE 26TH CONFERENCE OF THE PARTIES TO THE UNITED NATIONS FRAMEWORK CONVENTION ON CLIMATE CHANGE (COP26), WHERE HE WILL CALL FOR URGENT ACTION TO ADDRESS CLIMATE CHANGE.
