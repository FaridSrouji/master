PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE PLANS TO ACQUIRE STAKE IN OCEAN WIND FROM ØRSTED. PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP INCORPORATED (NYSE:PEG) (PSEG) ANNOUNCED THEY WILL ENTER INTO EXCLUSIVE NEGOTIATIONS FOR PSEG TO POTENTIALLY BECOME AN EQUITY INVESTOR IN ONE OF ØRSTED A/S (CPSE:ORSTED) OFFSHORE WIND PROJECTS. SUBJECT TO NEGOTIATIONS TOWARD A JOINT VENTURE AGREEMENT, ADVANCED DUE DILIGENCE AND ANY REQUIRED REGULATORY APPROVALS, PSEG WOULD ACQUIRE 25% OF OCEAN WIND. PSEG POWER PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER RALPH LAROSSA SAID, "WE ARE PLEASED ABOUT THE OPPORTUNITY TO EXPLORE A PARTNERSHIP WITH ØRSTED".
