PSEG ANNOUNCES 2021 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS. NEWARK, N.J., AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP (NYSE: PEG) REPORTED A NET LOSS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021 OF $177 MILLION, OR $0.35 PER SHARE, COMPARED TO NET INCOME OF $451 MILLION, OR $0.89 PER SHARE, IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020.
