PSEG LONG ISLAND TEMPORARILY CLOSES 11 CUSTOMER SERVICE CENTERS. PSEG LONG ISLAND - IS TEMPORARILY CLOSING ITS 11 CUSTOMER SERVICE CENTERS.PSEG LONG ISLAND - HAS SUSPENDED SHUT-OFFS OF ELECTRIC SERVICE TO RESIDENTIAL CUSTOMERS FOR NON-PAYMENT.PSEG LONG ISLAND - ALSO WAIVING NEW LATE PAYMENT FEES UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE.PSEG LONG ISLAND - DIRECTED ALL EMPLOYEES WHO CAN WORK REMOTELY TO DO SO.
