PSEG LONG ISLAND ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS IN ARREARS TO APPLY FOR UP TO $10,000 IN STATE ASSISTANCE. UNIONDALE, N.Y., OCT. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PSEG LONG ISLAND ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS STRUGGLING WITH THEIR UTILITY BILLS TO TAKE ADVANTAGE OF A LIMITED-TIME NEW YORK STATE PROGRAM THAT PROVIDES ELIGIBLE HOUSEHOLDS WITH UP TO $10,000 TO COVER OUTSTANDING BALANCES.
