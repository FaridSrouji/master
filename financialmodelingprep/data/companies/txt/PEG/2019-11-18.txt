PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FOR FOURTH QUARTER OF 2019, PAYABLE ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 31, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP DECLARED A $0.47 PER SHARE DIVIDEND ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2019. ALL DIVIDENDS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ARE PAYABLE ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 31, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON DECEMBER 10, 2019.
