PSE&G REACHES AGREEMENT TO LOWER TRANSMISSION RATES; WILL FILE AGREEMENT WITH FERC; ELECTRIC CUSTOMERS TO SAVE APPROXIMATELY $140 MILLION A YEAR. NEWARK, N.J., JULY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PSE&G EXECUTED AN AGREEMENT TODAY WITH THE NEW JERSEY BOARD OF PUBLIC UTILITIES AND NEW JERSEY DIVISION OF RATE COUNSEL RELATED TO ITS TRANSMISSION RATES THAT WOULD SAVE THE TYPICAL ELECTRIC RESIDENTIAL CUSTOMER ABOUT 3% ON THEIR BILL, OR APPROXIMATELY $3 PER MONTH.
