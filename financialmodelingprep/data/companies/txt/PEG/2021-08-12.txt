PSEG AGREES TO SELL PSEG FOSSIL GENERATING PORTFOLIO TO ARCLIGHT CAPITAL. NEWARK, N.J., AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP (PSEG) HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS 6,750-MEGAWATT FOSSIL GENERATING PORTFOLIO TO NEWLY FORMED SUBSIDIARIES OF ARCLIGHT ENERGY PARTNERS FUND VII, L.P.
