DIGITAL REALTY DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND FOR COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK. AUSTIN, TEXAS , MAY 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DIGITAL REALTY (NYSE: DLR), THE LARGEST GLOBAL PROVIDER OF CLOUD- AND CARRIER-NEUTRAL DATA CENTER, COLOCATION AND INTERCONNECTION SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDENDS FOR COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022.  COMMON STOCK DIGITAL REALTY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS AUTHORIZED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.22 PER SHARE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 15, 2022.
