FIBERSENSE BOARD OF DIRECTORS ADDS INTERNATIONAL IT INFRASTRUCTURE EXPERIENCE WITH CHRIS SHARP JOINING AS CO-CHAIR. BOULDER, COLO. , JUNE 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAPIDLY EXPANDING INFRASTRUCTURE SENSING AND MONITORING COMPANY, FIBER SENSE LIMITED (FIBERSENSE), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CHRIS SHARP, DIGITAL REALTY (NYSE: DLR) CHIEF TECHNOLOGY OFFICER (CTO), AS A NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR.
