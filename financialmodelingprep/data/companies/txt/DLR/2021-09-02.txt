INTERXION: A DIGITAL REALTY COMPANY EXPANDS GLOBAL DATA CENTRE PLATFORM WITH NEW FACILITY IN THE EAST OF PARIS. PARIS, SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INTERXION: A DIGITAL REALTY COMPANY (NYSE: DLR) AND LEADING EUROPEAN PROVIDER OF CARRIER- AND CLOUD-NEUTRAL COLOCATION DATA CENTRE SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE OPENING OF ITS LATEST EUROPEAN DATA CENTRE IN PARIS.
