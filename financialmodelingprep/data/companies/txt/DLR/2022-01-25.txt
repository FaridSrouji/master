DIGITAL REALTY OPENS FIRST CARRIER-NEUTRAL DATA CENTER IN SOUTH KOREA. SINGAPORE, JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DIGITAL REALTY (NYSE: DLR), THE LARGEST GLOBAL PROVIDER OF CLOUD- AND CARRIER-NEUTRAL DATA CENTER, COLOCATION AND INTERCONNECTION SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OFFICIAL OPENING OF ITS FIRST DATA CENTER IN SOUTH KOREA, AND THE FIRST CARRIER-NEUTRAL FACILITY IN THE COUNTRY. DIGITAL SEOUL 1 (ICN10) WILL SERVE AS A GATEWAY TO GLOBAL EXPANSION FOR ENTERPRISES IN KOREA TO SCALE THEIR DIGITAL BUSINESS INTO NEW MARKETS GLOBALLY, AND VICE VERSA.
