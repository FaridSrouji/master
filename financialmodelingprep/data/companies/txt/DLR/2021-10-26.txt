DIGITAL REALTY REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. AUSTIN, TEXAS, OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DIGITAL REALTY (NYSE: DLR), THE LARGEST GLOBAL PROVIDER OF CLOUD- AND CARRIER-NEUTRAL DATA CENTER, COLOCATION AND INTERCONNECTION SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021.  ALL PER-SHARE RESULTS ARE PRESENTED ON A FULLY-DILUTED SHARE AND UNIT BASIS.
