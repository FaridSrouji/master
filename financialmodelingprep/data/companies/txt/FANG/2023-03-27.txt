DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. JOINS THE OIL AND GAS METHANE PARTNERSHIP 2.0. MIDLAND, TEXAS, MARCH 27, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. (NASDAQ: FANG) (“DIAMONDBACK” OR “THE COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS JOINED THE OIL AND GAS METHANE PARTNERSHIP 2.0 (OGMP 2.0), THE UNITED NATIONS ENVIRONMENT PROGRAMME'S FLAGSHIP OIL AND GAS REPORTING AND MITIGATION PROGRAM.
