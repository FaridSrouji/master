DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. ACCELERATES CAPITAL RETURN TO STOCKHOLDERS AND INITIATES SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM. MIDLAND, TEXAS, SEPT. 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. (NASDAQ: FANG) (“DIAMONDBACK” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ACCELERATED ITS PLANS TO RETURN 50% OF FREE CASH FLOW TO STOCKHOLDERS TO THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021, AND THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED AN UP TO $2.0 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM TO COMPLEMENT THIS RETURN COMMITMENT.
