DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. ANNOUNCES AMENDED AND RESTATED BYLAWS. ON NOVEMBER 18, 2019, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. AMENDED THE COMPANY’S AMENDED AND RESTATED BYLAWS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, TO IMPLEMENT PROXY ACCESS AND MAKE CERTAIN OTHER TECHNICAL AND CONFORMING REVISIONS, AS WELL AS TO INCORPORATE THE FIRST AMENDMENT TO THE AMENDED AND RESTATED BYLAWS, ADOPTED APRIL 23, 2018 AND FILED WITH THE SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION ON APRIL 27, 2018.
