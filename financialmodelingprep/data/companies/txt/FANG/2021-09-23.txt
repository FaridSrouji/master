DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. RELEASES 2021 CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. MIDLAND, TEXAS, SEPT. 23, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. (NASDAQ: FANG) (“DIAMONDBACK” OR “THE COMPANY”) TODAY RELEASED ITS 2021 CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. THE ONLINE REPORT OUTLINES DIAMONDBACK'S COMMITMENT TO ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY, MANAGING AND REDUCING RISKS, GOVERNANCE AND BUSINESS ETHICS, AND OUR COMMITMENT TO OUR PEOPLE AND COMMUNITIES. THE REPORT HIGHLIGHTS DIAMONDBACK'S PERFORMANCE IN THESE CATEGORIES THROUGH DECEMBER 31, 2020, DISCUSSES DIAMONDBACK'S SUSTAINABILITY TARGETS AND 2021 INITIATIVES AND IS AVAILABLE AT WWW.DIAMONDBACKENERGY.COM/ABOUT/SUSTAINABILITY.
