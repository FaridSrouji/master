BANGL ADDS RATTLER MIDSTREAM LP AS STRATEGIC PARTNER FOLLOWING FOURTH QUARTER 2021 IN-SERVICE DATE. AUSTIN, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WHITEWATER MIDSTREAM (WHITEWATER), MPLX LP (MPLX NYSE: MPLX), AND WEST TEXAS GAS (WTG) ARE PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THE ADDITION OF A FOURTH PARTNER, RATTLER MIDSTREAM LP (RATTLER NASDAQ: RTLR), TO THE COMPANIES' BANGL, LLC JOINT VENTURE (BANGL). CONCURRENT WITH RATTLER'S INVESTMENT IN THE PROJECT, DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. (NASDAQ: FANG), RATTLER'S UPSTREAM OPERATING PARENT, ENTERED INTO A SIGNIFICANT, LONG-TERM NATURAL GAS LIQUIDS (NGL) PRODUCT DEDICATION WITH BANGL.
