DIAMONDBACK ENERGY PRICES OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES. MIDLAND, TEXAS, NOV. 29, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. (NASDAQ: FANG) (“DIAMONDBACK”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED AN OFFERING (THE “NOTES OFFERING”) OF $650,000,000 IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 6.250% SENIOR NOTES THAT WILL MATURE ON MARCH 15, 2053 (THE “NOTES”). THE PRICE TO THE PUBLIC FOR THE NOTES IS 99.985% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT.
