MEDTRONIC PLC REITERATES REVENUE GROWTH GUIDANCE FOR FISCAL 2020. MEDTRONIC PLC REITERATED ITS REVENUE GROWTH GUIDANCE FOR FISCAL 2020. THE COMPANY CONTINUES TO EXPECT REVENUE GROWTH IN ITS FISCAL YEAR 2020 TO APPROXIMATE 4.0% ON AN ORGANIC BASIS AND FOR REVENUE GROWTH TO ACCELERATE IN THE SECOND HALF RELATIVE TO THE FIRST. IF CURRENT EXCHANGE RATES HOLD, REVENUE GROWTH IN FISCAL YEAR 2020 WOULD BE NEGATIVELY AFFECTED BY 0.8 TO 1.2%.
