CONTINUOUS RHYTHM MONITORING REVEALS STRONG SHORT-TERM RISK ASSOCIATION BETWEEN ATRIAL FIBRILATION EPISODES AND ISCHEMIC STROKE. DUBLIN, OCT. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE: MDT), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PUBLICATION OF A STUDY DEMONSTRATING, THROUGH THE USE OF A MEDTRONIC CONTINUOUS RHYTHM MONITORING DEVICE, THAT ATRIAL FIBRILLATION (AF) IS DIRECTLY AND TRANSIENTLY ASSOCIATED WITH ISCHEMIC STROKE. THE FINDINGS WERE PUBLISHED ONLINE ON SEPTEMBER 29 IN JAMA CARDIOLOGY.
