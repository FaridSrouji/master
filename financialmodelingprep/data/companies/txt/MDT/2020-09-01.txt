MEDTRONIC TO IMPLEMENT NEW OPERATING MODEL. MEDTRONIC PLC - IMPLEMENTING A NEW OPERATING MODEL.MEDTRONIC PLC - INITIATING A SIMPLIFICATION RESTRUCTURING PROGRAM.MEDTRONIC - REORGANIZING CURRENT GROUPS TO CREATE OPERATING UNITS (OUS), EACH FORMED FROM EXISTING BUSINESSES, ORGANIZED AROUND SPECIFIC THERAPY AREAS.MEDTRONIC PLC - EXPECTED TO INCUR TOTAL PRE-TAX COSTS OF APPROXIMATELY $400 - $450 MILLION RELATED TO PROGRAM.MEDTRONIC PLC - PROGRAM EXPECTED TO RESULT IN ANNUALIZED ONGOING COST SAVINGS OF ABOUT $450 - $475 MILLION REALIZED BY FISCAL YEAR 2023.MEDTRONIC PLC -  WILL ALSO ESTABLISH TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENT CENTERS IN AREAS WHERE CO HAS CORE TECHNOLOGY COMPETENCIES LEVERAGED BY MULTIPLE OUS.MEDTRONIC PLC - WILL TRANSITION TO NEW MODEL BEGINNING IN Q3 AND WILL BE FULLY EFFECTIVE AT START OF Q4 2021.MEDTRONIC PLC - MEDTRONIC IS CONSOLIDATING OPERATIONS AT ENTERPRISE LEVEL, INCLUDING CO'S GLOBAL MANUFACTURING, SUPPLY CHAIN AND FACILITIES.
