FIRST PROCEDURE IN ASIA-PACIFIC PERFORMED WITH MEDTRONIC HUGO™ ROBOTIC-ASSISTED SURGERY SYSTEM. THE ROBOTIC PROSTATECTOMY TOOK PLACE AT APOLLO HOSPITALS, GREAMS ROAD, CHENNAI, INDIA DUBLIN AND CHENNAI, INDIA, SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT) AND APOLLO HOSPITALS GROUP TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FIRST CLINICAL PROCEDURE IN ASIA-PACIFIC WITH THE HUGO™ ROBOTIC-ASSISTED SURGERY (RAS) SYSTEM.
