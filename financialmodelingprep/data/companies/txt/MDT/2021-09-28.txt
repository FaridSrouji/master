MEDTRONIC RECEIVES CE MARK APPROVAL FOR RADIAL ARTERY ACCESS PORTFOLIO. DUBLIN, SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED CE MARK APPROVAL FOR ITS RADIAL ARTERY ACCESS PORTFOLIO, WHICH INCLUDES THE RIST™ 079 RADIAL ACCESS GUIDE CATHETER AND RIST™ RADIAL ACCESS SELECTIVE CATHETER.
