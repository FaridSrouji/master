MEDTRONIC CLASS ACTION: LEVI & KORSINSKY REMINDS MEDTRONIC PLC INVESTORS OF THE PENDING CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT WITH A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF NOVEMBER 7, 2022 - MDT.
