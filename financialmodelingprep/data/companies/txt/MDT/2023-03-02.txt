MEDTRONIC ANNOUNCES CASH DIVIDEND FOR FOURTH QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2023. DUBLIN , MARCH 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT) ON THURSDAY, MARCH 2, 2023, APPROVED THE FISCAL YEAR 2023 FOURTH QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER ORDINARY SHARE, REPRESENTING AN 8% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR YEAR. THIS QUARTERLY DECLARATION IS CONSISTENT WITH THE DIVIDEND ANNOUNCEMENT MADE BY THE COMPANY IN MAY 2022.
