MEDTRONIC ANNOUNCES CASH DIVIDEND FOR SECOND QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2022. DUBLIN, AUG. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE: MDT) ON FRIDAY, AUGUST 20, 2021, APPROVED THE FISCAL YEAR 2022 SECOND QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.63 PER ORDINARY SHARE, REPRESENTING A 9 PERCENT INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR YEAR. THIS QUARTERLY DECLARATION IS CONSISTENT WITH THE DIVIDEND ANNOUNCEMENT MADE BY THE COMPANY IN MAY 2021.
