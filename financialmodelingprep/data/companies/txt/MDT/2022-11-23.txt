MEDTRONIC EXECUTIVES TO SPEAK AT THE EVERCORE ISI 5TH ANNUAL HEALTHCONX CONFERENCE. DUBLIN , NOV. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE EVERCORE ISI 5TH ANNUAL HEALTHCONX CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 29, 2022.  GEOFF MARTHA, MEDTRONIC CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AND KAREN PARKHILL, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL ANSWER QUESTIONS ON THE COMPANY BEGINNING AT 5:10 P.M.
