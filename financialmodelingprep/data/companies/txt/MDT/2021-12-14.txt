MEDTRONIC INVOS™ 7100 CEREBRAL/SOMATIC OXIMETRY SYSTEM RECEIVES 510(K) CLEARANCE FOR PEDIATRIC INDICATIONS. FDA CLEARANCE EXPANDS USE OF THE INVOS™ 7100 SYSTEM, HELPING CLINICIANS BETTER MONITOR ORGAN-SPECIFIC OXYGEN LEVELS AND IDENTIFY WARNINGS SIGNS FOR NEONATES AND CHILDREN EARLIER DUBLIN, DEC. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS GRANTED 510(K) CLEARANCE FOR ITS INVOS™ 7100 CEREBRAL/SOMATIC OXIMETRY SYSTEM FOR CHILDREN FROM BIRTH THROUGH AGE 18. THE INVOS SYSTEM PICKS UP KEY SIGNALS TO INFORM TIME-CRITICAL DECISIONS BY PEDIATRIC CLINICIANS RELATED TO HEMODYNAMIC MANAGEMENT, VENTILATION, AND RESUSCITATION FOR PREMATURE INFANTS, NEONATES, CHILDREN, AND OTHER PATIENTS TREATED BY PEDIATRIC CLINICIANS.
