MEDTRONIC NAMED AS A LEADING SUSTAINABILITY COMPANY. STRONG ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) MANAGEMENT AND PERFORMANCE RESULT IN CONTINUED INCLUSION ON THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX DUBLIN , DEC. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE: MDT), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS CONTINUED INCLUSION IN THE DOW JONES® SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX (DJSI) AS ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING COMPANIES FOR SUSTAINABILITY. THE DJSI WORLD INDEX INCLUDES GLOBAL SUSTAINABILITY LEADERS THAT ARE IDENTIFIED BY S&P DOW JONES INDICES BASED ON THEIR PERFORMANCE IN THE S&P GLOBAL CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY ASSESSMENT ACROSS A VARIETY OF SUSTAINABILITY CRITERIA, INCLUDING LONG-TERM ECONOMIC PERFORMANCE, ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP, SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY, AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE.
