MEDTRONIC NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES BY JUST CAPITAL AND CNBC. NOTABLE CONTRIBUTIONS THAT RESULTED IN MEDTRONIC'S OVERALL RANKING AND OUTPERFORMANCE AGAINST PEERS ON THE JUST 100 LIST INCLUDE THE COMPANY'S FOCUS ON: COMMUNITY AND CUSTOMERS.
