MEDTRONIC ANNOUNCES 2045 NET ZERO EMISSIONS AMBITION TO COMBAT CLIMATE CHANGE. DUBLIN, NOV. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS AMBITION TO ACHIEVE NET ZERO CARBON EMISSIONS BY FISCAL YEAR (FY) 2045 ACROSS ITS OPERATIONS AND VALUE CHAIN (SCOPES 1, 2, AND 3) TO ACCELERATE EFFORTS TO COMBAT CLIMATE CHANGE. THE ANNOUNCEMENT COMES DURING THE 2021 UNITED NATIONS CLIMATE CHANGE CONFERENCE (COP 26), WHERE WORLD LEADERS ARE FOCUSING ON COLLECTIVELY CUTTING GREENHOUSE GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS AND LIMITING GLOBAL WARMING TO 1.5 DEGREES CELSIUS COMPARED TO PRE-INDUSTRIAL LEVELS.
