MEDTRONIC EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CFO TO SPEAK AT COWEN HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE. DUBLIN, FEB. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE: MDT), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL PARTICIPATE VIRTUALLY IN THE 42ND ANNUAL COWEN HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE ON MONDAY, MARCH 7, 2022. KAREN PARKHILL, MEDTRONIC EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL ANSWER QUESTIONS ON THE COMPANY BEGINNING AT 1:30 P.M.
