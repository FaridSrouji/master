SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISSLAW LLP INVESTIGATES INTERSECT ENT, INC.. NEW YORK, AUG. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISSLAW LLP IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF INTERSECT ENT, INC. ("INTERSECT" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: XENT) IN CONNECTION WITH THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF THE COMPANY BY MEDTRONIC PLC ("MEDTRONIC") (NYSE:MDT).  UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $28.25 PER SHARE IN CASH FOR EACH SHARE OF INTERSECT COMMON STOCK THAT THEY HOLD.
