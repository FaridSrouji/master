MEDTRONIC ANNOUNCES CASH DIVIDEND FOR THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2023. DUBLIN , DEC. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT) ON THURSDAY, DECEMBER 8, 2022, APPROVED THE FISCAL YEAR 2023 THIRD QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER ORDINARY SHARE, REPRESENTING AN 8% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR YEAR. THIS QUARTERLY DECLARATION IS CONSISTENT WITH THE DIVIDEND ANNOUNCEMENT MADE BY THE COMPANY IN MAY 2022.
