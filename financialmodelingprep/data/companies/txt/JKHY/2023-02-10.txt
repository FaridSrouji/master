CHESAPEAKE BANK DEBUTS NEW DIGITAL DEBIT CARD PRODUCT BY LEVERAGING PARTNERSHIPS WITH TRABIAN, Q2, AND JACK HENRY. KILMARNOCK, VA / ACCESSWIRE / FEBRUARY 10, 2022 / CHESAPEAKE BANK IS HAPPY TO ANNOUNCE THE RELEASE OF A NEW PRODUCT - THE DIGITAL DEBIT CARD. THE DIGITAL DEBIT CARD ALLOWS CONSUMERS TO CREATE VIRTUAL CARDS WITHIN ONLINE AND MOBILE BANKING FOR USE IN ONLINE PURCHASES, AND IT CAN BE LOADED INTO THEIR MOBILE WALLET.
