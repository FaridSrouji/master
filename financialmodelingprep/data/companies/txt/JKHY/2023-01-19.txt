JACK HENRY SIGNS SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE (SBTI) COMMITMENT LETTER. SCIENCE-BASED EMISSIONS REDUCTION TARGETS SUPPORT THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO A LOW-CARBON TRANSITION MONETT, MO. , JAN. 19, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACK HENRY™ (NASDAQ: JKHY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BOARD CHAIR AND CEO DAVID FOSS SIGNED A COMMITMENT LETTER INDICATING THE COMPANY'S INTENTION TO SET SCIENCE-BASED CLIMATE TARGETS WITH THE SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE (SBTI).
