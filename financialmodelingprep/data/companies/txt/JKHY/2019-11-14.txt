JACK HENRY & ASSOCIATES, INC. DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 20, 2019. JACK HENRY & ASSOCIATES, INC. ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.40 PER SHARE. THE CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAR VALUE $0.01 PER SHARE, IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 20, 2019, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 2, 2019.
