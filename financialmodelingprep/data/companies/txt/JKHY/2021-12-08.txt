JACK HENRY LENDING AUTOMATES TAX RETURN SPREADING, STREAMLINING THE LOAN PROCESS. MONETT, MO., DEC. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACK HENRY & ASSOCIATES, INC. (NASDAQ: JKHY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT  JACK HENRY LENDING HAS EMBEDDED TAX RETURN SPREADING INTO ITS DIGITAL LENDING PLATFORM TO STREAMLINE AND AUTOMATE THE PROCESS, INCREASING EFFICIENCIES AND REDUCING LOAN TURNAROUND TIME FOR BANKS AND CREDIT UNIONS.
