JACK HENRY & ASSOCIATES INCREASES THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK BY 7 PERCENT TO $.52 PER SHARE. MONETT, MO. , FEB. 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACK HENRY & ASSOCIATES, INC. (NASDAQ: JKHY) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS INCREASED THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY SEVEN PERCENT (7%) TO $.52 PER SHARE.
