LAND GORILLA JOINS THE JACK HENRY BANKING VENDOR INTEGRATION PROGRAM. SAN LUIS OBISPO, CALIF., JUNE 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  LAND GORILLA , THE LEADING TECHNOLOGY PROVIDER OF CONSTRUCTION LOAN MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS JOINED THE JACK HENRY BANKING® VENDOR INTEGRATION PROGRAM (VIP).
