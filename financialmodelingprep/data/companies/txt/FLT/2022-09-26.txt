FLEETCOR ANNOUNCES DEPARTURE OF CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FLEETCOR TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: FLT), A LEADING GLOBAL BUSINESS PAYMENTS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE DEPARTURE OF ITS GLOBAL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (“CFO”), CHARLES FREUND. AFTER ALMOST 22 YEARS, MR. FREUND IS LEAVING THE COMPANY TO JOIN A PRIVATE EQUITY BACKED SOFTWARE COMPANY, AS CFO.
