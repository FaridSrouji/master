FLEETCOR APPOINTS TOM PANTHER AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FLEETCOR TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: FLT), A LEADING GLOBAL BUSINESS PAYMENTS COMPANY (“FLEETCOR”), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF TOM PANTHER AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (“CFO”), EFFECTIVE MAY 12, 2023. “WE ARE THRILLED TO WELCOME TOM TO FLEETCOR. TOM IS AN EXPERIENCED PUBLIC COMPANY CFO WHO KNOWS PAYMENTS. HE IS A CLASSICALLY TRAINED FINANCIAL EXECUTIVE WHO CAN ALSO HELP US RUN THE BUSINESS.
