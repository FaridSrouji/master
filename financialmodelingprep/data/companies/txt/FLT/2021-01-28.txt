FLEETCOR ACQUIRES CLOUD SOFTWARE PLATFORM PROVIDER OF B2B ONLINE BILL PAYMENT. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FLEETCOR TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: FLT), A LEADING GLOBAL BUSINESS PAYMENTS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ACQUIRED ROGER, A GLOBAL ACCOUNTS PAYABLE (AP) CLOUD SOFTWARE PLATFORM FOR SMALL BUSINESSES. THE ACQUISITION WILL EXTEND FLEETCOR'S PORTFOLIO OF ACCOUNTS PAYABLE AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS TO SMALL BUSINESSES, HELPING THEM AUTOMATE THEIR MANUAL PAYMENT PROCESSES. THE ACQUISITION PROVIDES FLEETCOR WITH A PROVEN, MODERN AUTOMATION PLATFORM FOR B2B ONLINE BILL PAYMENT. TH
