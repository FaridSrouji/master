CAMBRIDGE GLOBAL PAYMENTS EXTENDS SPONSORSHIP OF EXCLUSIVE AUTOSPORT RACING TEAM. TORONTO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMBRIDGE GLOBAL PAYMENTS (“CAMBRIDGE”), A FLEETCOR COMPANY (NYSE: FLT) AND A PROVIDER OF INTEGRATED CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS AND CURRENCY RISK MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THEY HAVE EXTENDED THEIR SPONSORSHIP OF EXCLUSIVE AUTOSPORT (EA), A CANADIAN-OWNED PROFESSIONAL RACING TEAM FOR THE UPCOMING 2021 RACING SEASON. AS PART OF THIS SPONSORSHIP EXTENSION FOR THE 2021 RACING SEASON, CAMBRIDGE HAS BEEN NAMED THE TITLE SPONSOR FOR #91 CAMBRIDGE EXCLUSIVE AUTOS
