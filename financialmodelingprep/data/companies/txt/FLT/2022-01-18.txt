CORPAY ANNOUNCES NEW SPONSORSHIP OF THE SMALL BUSINESS ASSOCIATION FOR INTERNATIONAL COMPANIES. TORONTO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CORPAYI, A FLEETCOR (NYSE: FLT) BRAND THAT PROVIDES INTEGRATED CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS AND CURRENCY RISK MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT THEY HAVE BEEN NAMED A DIAMOND-LEVEL SPONSOR AND ENDORSED PROVIDER OF GLOBAL PAYMENTS AND FOREIGN CURRENCY EXCHANGE SOLUTIONS OF THE SMALL BUSINESS ASSOCIATION FOR INTERNATIONAL COMPANIES (“SBAIC”).
