FLEETCOR REPORTS Q3 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.10. FLEETCOR REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2019 FINANCIAL RESULTS.Q3 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.10.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.49.Q3 REVENUE $681 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $679.7 MILLION.SEES FY 2019 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $11.68 TO $11.78.SEES FY 2019 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $9.80 TO $9.90.SEES FY 2019 REVENUE $2.64 BILLION TO $2.66 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $3.05 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FY2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $11.74, REVENUE VIEW $2.65 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FLEETCOR TECHNOLOGIES - MACRO-ECONOMIC ENVIRONMENT WAS MIXED IN QUARTER AND OVERALL CAME IN WORSE THAN ASSUMPTIONS USED IN DEVELOPING PRIOR 2019 GUIDANCE.MACRO-ECONOMIC ENVIRONMENT NEGATIVELY IMPACTED REVENUES IN QUARTER BY APPROXIMATELY $2 MILLION TO $3 MILLION.FLEETCOR TECHNOLOGIES - FOREIGN EXCHANGE RATES CAME IN WORSE THAN EXPECTED, PARTIALLY OFFSET BY MORE FAVORABLE SPREADS AND FUEL PRICE IN QUARTER.RAISING FISCAL YEAR ADJUSTED NET INCOME PER DILUTED SHARE GUIDANCE BY $0.05.FOR Q4, EXPECT MACRO ENVIRONMENT WILL CONTINUE TO BE UNFAVORABLE VERSUS ASSUMPTIONS USED IN PRIOR GUIDANCE.SEES EXPECT THAT MACRO ENVIRONMENT NEGATIVELY IMPACTING Q4 REVENUE BY APPROXIMATELY $10 MILLION.
