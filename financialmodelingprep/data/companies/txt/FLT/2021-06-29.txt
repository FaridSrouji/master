CAMBRIDGE GLOBAL PAYMENTS ANNOUNCES NEW PARTNERSHIP WITH STEAMCHAIN CORP.. TORONTO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMBRIDGE GLOBAL PAYMENTS (“CAMBRIDGE”), A FLEETCOR COMPANY (NYSE: FLT) AND A PROVIDER OF INTEGRATED CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS AND CURRENCY RISK MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS, AND STEAMCHAIN CORP. (“STEAMCHAIN”), A TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM FOCUSED ON HELPING TO REDUCE THE COST OF CURRENCY CONVERSION, CURRENCY FLUCTUATION RISK AND CUMBERSOME DOCUMENT FLOW, ARE PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THEY HAVE PARTNERED TO BRING A TAILORED SOLUTION TO HELP COMPANIES IN THE GLOBAL SHIPPING AND LOGISTICS INDUS
