BETTERINVESTING™ MAGAZINE UPDATE ON ULTA BEAUTY AND GENTEX. TROY, MICH. , NOV. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE EDITORIAL ADVISORY AND SECURITIES REVIEW COMMITTEE OF BETTERINVESTING MAGAZINE TODAY ANNOUNCED ULTA BEAUTY (NASDAQ: ULTA) AS ITS "STOCK TO STUDY" AND GENTEX (NASDAQ: GNTX) AS ITS "UNDERVALUED STOCK" IN THE JANUARY/FEBRUARY 2023 ISSUE FOR INVESTORS' INFORMATIONAL AND EDUCATIONAL USE.
