ULTA BEAUTY WITHDRAWS PREVIOUSLY ISSUED GUIDANCE FOR FISCAL 2020. ULTA BEAUTY PROVIDES COVID-19-RELATED BUSINESS UPDATE.ULTA BEAUTY INC - IS WITHDRAWING GUIDANCE PREVIOUSLY ISSUED FOR FISCAL 2020.ULTA BEAUTY INC - IS NOT PROVIDING AN UPDATED OUTLOOK AT THIS TIME.ULTA BEAUTY - COMPANY IS NOT PROVIDING AN UPDATED OUTLOOK AT THIS TIME.ULTA BEAUTY INC - BEAUTY WILL TEMPORARILY CLOSE ALL OF ITS STORES EFFECTIVE 6:00 P.M. ON MARCH 19, 2020 UNTIL AT LEAST MARCH 31, 2020.ULTA BEAUTY INC - MOST STORES WILL CONTINUE TO BE OUTLETS FOR BUY ONLINE AND PICK UP IN STORE AS ALLOWED BY LOCAL AND STATE REGULATIONS.ULTA BEAUTY INC - ALL GUESTS CAN CONTINUE TO SHOP THROUGH ULTA BEAUTY APP OR VISIT ULTA.COM.ULTA BEAUTY INC - WILL CONTINUE TO PAY STORE AND SALON ASSOCIATES AND PROVIDE BENEFITS FOR THOSE WHO ARE CURRENTLY ENROLLED DURING THIS PERIOD.
