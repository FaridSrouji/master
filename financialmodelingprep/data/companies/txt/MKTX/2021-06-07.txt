MARKETAXESS TO PARTICIPATE IN THE PIPER SANDLER GLOBAL EXCHANGE & FINTECH CONFERENCE. NEW YORK, JUNE 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE PIPER SANDLER GLOBAL EXCHANGE & FINTECH CONFERENCE ON JUNE 9, 2021.
