MARKETAXESS ANNOUNCES MONTHLY VOLUME STATISTICS FOR SEPTEMBER 2021. NEW YORK, OCT. 05, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED MONTHLY TRADING VOLUME FOR SEPTEMBER 2021 OF $559.5 BILLION CONSISTING OF $215.0 BILLION IN CREDIT VOLUME AND $344.5 BILLION IN RATES VOLUME.
