MARKETAXESS ANNOUNCES MONTHLY VOLUME STATISTICS FOR MAY 2021. NEW YORK, JUNE 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED MONTHLY TRADING VOLUME FOR MAY 2021 OF $478.7 BILLION CONSISTING OF $204.4 BILLION IN CREDIT VOLUME AND $274.3 BILLION IN RATES VOLUME.
