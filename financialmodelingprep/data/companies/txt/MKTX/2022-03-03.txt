MARKETAXESS ANNOUNCES MONTHLY VOLUME STATISTICS FOR FEBRUARY 2022. NEW YORK, MARCH 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED MONTHLY TRADING VOLUME FOR FEBRUARY 2022 OF $716.8 BILLION, CONSISTING OF $221.4 BILLION IN CREDIT VOLUME AND $495.4 BILLION IN RATES VOLUME.
