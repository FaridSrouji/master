MARKETAXESS APPOINTS RAJ PARANANDI AS CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER FOR EMEA & APAC. LONDON, JAN. 13, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF RAJ PARANANDI AS CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, EMEA & APAC. THE APPOINTMENT IS EFFECTIVE MARCH 1ST, 2021.
