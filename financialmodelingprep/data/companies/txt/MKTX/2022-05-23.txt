MARKETAXESS TO PARTICIPATE IN THE DEUTSCHE BANK 12TH ANNUAL GLOBAL FINANCIAL SERVICES CONFERENCE. NEW YORK, MAY 23, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHRIS CONCANNON, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, AND CHRIS GEROSA, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE DEUTSCHE BANK 12TH ANNUAL GLOBAL FINANCIAL SERVICES CONFERENCE ON MAY 31, 2022 AT 4:15 P.M. ET.
