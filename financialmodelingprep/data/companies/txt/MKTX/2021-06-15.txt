MARKETAXESS TO PARTICIPATE IN THE MORGAN STANLEY U.S. FINANCIALS, PAYMENTS AND CRE CONFERENCE. NEW YORK, JUNE 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT TONY DELISE, CFO, IS SCHEDULED TO SPEAK AT THE MORGAN STANLEY U.S. FINANCIALS, PAYMENTS AND CRE CONFERENCE AT 11:00 A.M. EST ON JUNE 16, 2021.
