MARKETAXESS ANNOUNCES MONTHLY VOLUME STATISTICS FOR NOVEMBER 2021. NEW YORK, DEC. 06, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED MONTHLY TRADING VOLUME FOR NOVEMBER 2021 OF $653.4 BILLION CONSISTING OF $212.4 BILLION IN CREDIT VOLUME AND $440.9 BILLION IN RATES VOLUME.
