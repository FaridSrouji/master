MARKETAXESS ANNOUNCES MONTHLY VOLUME STATISTICS FOR NOVEMBER 2020. NEW YORK, DEC. 02, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED MONTHLY TRADING VOLUME FOR NOVEMBER 2020 OF $503.8 BILLION CONSISTING OF $219.2 BILLION IN CREDIT VOLUME AND $284.7 BILLION IN RATES VOLUME.
