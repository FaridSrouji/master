MARKETAXESS ANNOUNCES MONTHLY VOLUME STATISTICS FOR DECEMBER 2021. NEW YORK, JAN. 05, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED MONTHLY TRADING VOLUME FOR DECEMBER 2021 OF $538.1 BILLION CONSISTING OF $183.6 BILLION IN CREDIT VOLUME AND $354.5 BILLION IN RATES VOLUME.
