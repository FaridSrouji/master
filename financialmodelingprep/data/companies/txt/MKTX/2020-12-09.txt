MARKETAXESS LAUNCHES CENTRALIZED FIXED INCOME TRADING MARKETPLACE WITH INTEGRATED U.S. TREASURY MARKET LIQUIDITY. NEW YORK, DEC. 09, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A CENTRALIZED FIXED INCOME TRADING MARKETPLACE THAT FULLY INTEGRATES RATES TRADING CAPABILITIES WITHIN THE MARKETAXESS TRADING SYSTEM.
