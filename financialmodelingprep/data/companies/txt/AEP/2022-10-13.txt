AEP RECOGNIZED AS A TOP UTILITY IN ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT. COLUMBUS, OHIO , OCT. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE NATION'S TOP UTILITIES IN ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BY SITE SELECTION MAGAZINE. SITE SELECTION ANNUALLY EVALUATES UTILITIES' CORPORATE PROJECT INVESTMENT AND JOB CREATION ON A CUMULATIVE AND PER-CAPITA BASIS.
