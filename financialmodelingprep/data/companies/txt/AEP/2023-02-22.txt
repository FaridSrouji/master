AEP SIGNS AGREEMENT TO SELL UNREGULATED RENEWABLES ASSETS. COLUMBUS, OHIO , FEB. 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS 1,365-MEGAWATT (MW) UNREGULATED, CONTRACTED RENEWABLES PORTFOLIO TO IRG ACQUISITION HOLDINGS, A PARTNERSHIP OWNED BY INVENERGY, CDPQ AND FUNDS MANAGED BY BLACKSTONE INFRASTRUCTURE, AT AN ENTERPRISE VALUE OF $1.5 BILLION INCLUDING PROJECT DEBT. THE SALE IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2023.
