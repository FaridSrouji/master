AEP DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. COLUMBUS, OHIO, JAN. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER CO. (NASDAQ: AEP) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 78 CENTS A SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEB. 10, 2022, AND IS THE COMPANY'S 447TH CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK CASH DIVIDEND.
