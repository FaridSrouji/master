AEP TO SELL KENTUCKY OPERATIONS TO LIBERTY, SUBSIDIARY OF ALGONQUIN POWER & UTILITIES. COLUMBUS, OHIO, OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS KENTUCKY OPERATIONS, WHICH INCLUDE KENTUCKY POWER AND AEP KENTUCKY TRANSCO, TO LIBERTY, THE REGULATED UTILITY BUSINESS OF PARENT COMPANY ALGONQUIN POWER & UTILITIES CORP. (NYSE: AQN), FOR $2.846 BILLION ENTERPRISE VALUE. THE SALE IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022, PENDING REGULATORY APPROVALS.
