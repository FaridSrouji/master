AEP NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES FOR SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR. COLUMBUS, OHIO, JAN. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE JUST 100 2022 LIST, WHICH RECOGNIZES COMPANIES THAT SET THE STANDARD IN COMMITMENT TO THEIR STAKEHOLDERS. THE JUST 100 IS A LIST OF AMERICA'S BEST CORPORATE CITIZENS.
