DORAL SECURES PROJECT FINANCING FOR THE MAMMOTH NORTH SOLAR PROJECT. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DORAL RENEWABLES LLC (“DORAL”) HAS SUCCESSFULLY CLOSED CONSTRUCTION PROJECT FINANCING FOR MAMMOTH NORTH, THE FIRST PHASE OF DORAL'S BROADER MAMMOTH SOLAR PROJECT. MAMMOTH NORTH IS LOCATED ON 4,500 ACRES IN STARKE COUNTY, INDIANA, IN THE NORTHWESTERN REGION OF PJM. THE PROJECT WILL BE A GROUND-MOUNTED SINGLE AXIS PV SYSTEM WITH 400 MWAC OF SOLAR POWER CAPACITY.
