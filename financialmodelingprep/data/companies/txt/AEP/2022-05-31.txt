AEP NAMES MARTIN VICE PRESIDENT OF INFRASTRUCTURE ENGINEERING, IT OPERATIONS AND SUPPORT. COLUMBUS, OHIO , MAY 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS NAMED STEVEN MARTIN VICE PRESIDENT, INFRASTRUCTURE ENGINEERING, IT OPERATIONS & SUPPORT, EFFECTIVE MAY 30. HE REPORTS TO THERACE RISCH, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION & TECHNOLOGY OFFICER.
SWEPCO SEEKS APPROVAL FOR THREE NEW WIND AND SOLAR PROJECTS. $2.2 BILLION INVESTMENT PART OF SWEPCO'S ONGOING EFFORTS TO SECURE RENEWABLE ENERGY RESOURCES COLUMBUS, OHIO , MAY 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWESTERN ELECTRIC POWER COMPANY (SWEPCO), A SUBSIDIARY OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP), HAS ANNOUNCED PLANS TO ADD THREE RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECTS TOTALING 999 MEGAWATTS (MW). THE PROJECTS REPRESENT A $2.2 BILLION INVESTMENT IN CLEAN, COST-EFFECTIVE RESOURCES.
