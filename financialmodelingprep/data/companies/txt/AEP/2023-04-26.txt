AEP RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS FOR DIVERSITY. COLUMBUS, OHIO , APRIL 26, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) WAS INCLUDED IN FORBES BEST EMPLOYERS FOR DIVERSITY LIST. THE LIST WAS DEVELOPED BASED ON AN INDEPENDENT SURVEY FROM A SAMPLE OF APPROXIMATELY 45,000 U.S.-BASED EMPLOYEES.
