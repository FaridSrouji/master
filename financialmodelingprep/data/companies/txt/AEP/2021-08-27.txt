AEP NAMES MURPHY VICE PRESIDENT OF APPLICATIONS AND BUSINESS SOLUTIONS. COLUMBUS, OHIO, AUG. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS NAMED ANNE MURPHY VICE PRESIDENT OF APPLICATIONS AND BUSINESS SOLUTIONS, EFFECTIVE AUG. 30. SHE WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS FOR NUMEROUS AEP BUSINESS UNITS AND ORGANIZATIONS INCLUDING: ENERGY DELIVERY; GENERATION; GRID SOLUTIONS; COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS; SUPPLY CHAIN, PROCUREMENT AND FLEET; AND SECURITY AND RISK.
