AEP NAMES MARSH VICE PRESIDENT OF SAFETY AND HEALTH. COLUMBUS, OHIO , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS NAMED MICHELLE MARSH VICE PRESIDENT, SAFETY AND HEALTH, EFFECTIVE MARCH 4. IN THIS ROLE, SHE WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR LEADING THE DEVELOPMENT AND IMPLEMENTATION OF STRATEGIC INITIATIVES DESIGNED TO STRENGTHEN AEP'S SAFETY CULTURE AND PERFORMANCE.
