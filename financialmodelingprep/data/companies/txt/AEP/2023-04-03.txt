EDF RENEWABLES NORTH AMERICA SIGNS AGREEMENT WITH INDIANA MICHIGAN POWER FOR SOLAR ENERGY PROJECTS. SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EDF RENEWABLES NORTH AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED TWO AGREEMENTS WITH INDIANA MICHIGAN POWER (I&M), A SUBSIDIARY OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP), BY WHICH I&M WILL ADD 554 MWDC (425 MWAC) OF SOLAR ENERGY TO ITS CLEAN ENERGY MIX.
