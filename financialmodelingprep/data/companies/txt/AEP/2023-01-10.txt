AEP NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR, RANKED FIRST IN UTILITIES CATEGORY. COLUMBUS, OHIO , JAN. 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE JUST 100 2023 LIST, WHICH RECOGNIZES AMERICA'S BEST CORPORATE CITIZENS, AND RECEIVED THE TOP RANKING IN THE UTILITIES CATEGORY. THE JUST 100 IDENTIFIES COMPANIES THAT SET THE STANDARD IN COMMITMENT TO THEIR STAKEHOLDERS.
