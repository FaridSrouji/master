AEP NAMES GRANATA VICE PRESIDENT OF SAFETY AND HEALTH. COLUMBUS, OHIO, JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS NAMED MICHAEL GRANATA, VICE PRESIDENT, SAFETY & HEALTH, EFFECTIVE FEB. 5. HE WILL REPORT TO LISA BARTON, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER.
