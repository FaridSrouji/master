AEP RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES. COLUMBUS, OHIO, DEC. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES BY NEWSWEEK FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR. AEP WAS ONE OF 500 COMPANIES IN 14 INDUSTRIES INCLUDED ON THE LIST, WHICH HIGHLIGHTS COMPANIES THAT ARE LEADERS IN ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE (ESG) POLICIES.
