AEP ANNOUNCES EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP CHANGES. COLUMBUS, OHIO, JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED SEVERAL EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP CHANGES TO SUPPORT THE COMPANY'S STRATEGIC PRIORITIES, ACCORDING TO NICHOLAS K. AKINS, AEP CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
