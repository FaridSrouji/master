AEP RECEIVES GALLUP'S EXCEPTIONAL WORKPLACE AWARD FOR SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR. COLUMBUS, OHIO, MARCH 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS RECEIVED THE 2021 GALLUP EXCEPTIONAL WORKPLACE AWARD, WHICH RECOGNIZES ORGANIZATIONS WITH ENGAGED WORKPLACE CULTURES. AEP WAS ONE OF 39 COMPANIES SELECTED TO RECEIVE THE AWARD.
