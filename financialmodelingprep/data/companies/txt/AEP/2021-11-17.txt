PSO ISSUES REQUESTS FOR PROPOSALS FOR PURCHASE OF WIND AND SOLAR GENERATION RESOURCES. TULSA, OKLA., NOV. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PUBLIC SERVICE COMPANY OF OKLAHOMA (PSO), A SUBSIDIARY OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP), TODAY SUBMITTED REQUESTS FOR PROPOSALS (RFP) FOR RENEWABLE ENERGY RESOURCES TO SUPPLY THE NEEDS OF ITS CUSTOMERS.
