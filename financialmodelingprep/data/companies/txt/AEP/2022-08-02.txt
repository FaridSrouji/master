AEP COMPLETES SALE OF CARDINAL PLANT UNIT 1. COLUMBUS, OHIO , AUG. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS COMPLETED THE SALE OF THE 595-MEGAWATT (MW) CARDINAL PLANT UNIT 1 IN BRILLIANT, OHIO, FROM AEP GENERATION RESOURCES, THE COMPANY'S COMPETITIVE GENERATION AFFILIATE, TO BUCKEYE POWER. IN MARCH, AEP REACHED AN AGREEMENT TO SELL CARDINAL PLANT UNIT 1 AND THE COMPANY'S OWNERSHIP STAKE IN CARDINAL OPERATING COMPANY TO BUCKEYE POWER.
