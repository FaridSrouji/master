AEP DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. COLUMBUS, OHIO , APRIL 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER CO. (NASDAQ: AEP) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 83 CENTS A SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 9, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 10, 2023, AND IS THE COMPANY'S 452ND CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK CASH DIVIDEND.
