AEP INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO 83 CENTS A SHARE. COLUMBUS, OHIO , OCT. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER CO. (NASDAQ: AEP) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 83 CENTS A SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, AN INCREASE OF 5 CENTS PER SHARE. THE INCREASE IS IN LINE WITH THE COMPANY'S 6% TO 7% OPERATING EARNINGS GROWTH RANGE.
