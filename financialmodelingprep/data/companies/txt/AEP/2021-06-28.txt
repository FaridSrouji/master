SWEPCO ISSUES REQUESTS FOR PROPOSALS FOR PURCHASE OF WIND, SOLAR AND SHORT-TERM GENERATION. SHREVEPORT, LA., JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWESTERN ELECTRIC POWER CO.
