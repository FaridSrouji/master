AEP HONORED AS ONE OF FORTUNE'S WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES FOR 10TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. COLUMBUS, OHIO , FEB. 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) WAS INCLUDED IN FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S 2023 WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES LIST, WHICH EVALUATES COMPANIES' FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE AND CORPORATE REPUTATION. THIS IS THE 10TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR THE COMPANY IS BEING RECOGNIZED IN THE LIST'S ELECTRIC AND GAS UTILITIES SECTOR.
AEP NAMES BURBURE VICE PRESIDENT OF FERC AND RTO STRATEGY & POLICY. COLUMBUS, OHIO , FEB. 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS NAMED STACEY BURBURE VICE PRESIDENT, FERC AND RTO STRATEGY AND POLICY, EFFECTIVE FEB. 4. BURBURE REPLACES AMANDA CONNER, WHO NOW SERVES AS CHIEF OF STAFF TO JULIE SLOAT, AEP'S PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
