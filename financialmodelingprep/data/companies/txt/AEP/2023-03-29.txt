AEP RECOGNIZED FOR EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT EFFORTS, RECEIVES GALLUP'S EXCEPTIONAL WORKPLACE AWARD. COLUMBUS, OHIO , MARCH 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS RECEIVED THE 2023 GALLUP EXCEPTIONAL WORKPLACE AWARD, WHICH RECOGNIZES ORGANIZATIONS THAT INCORPORATE EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT INTO CORE BUSINESS VALUES. THIS IS THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR AEP HAS BEEN HONORED WITH THE AWARD.
