AEP SEEKS BIDS FOR FUEL OIL. COLUMBUS, OHIO, JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP), ON BEHALF OF ITS SUBSIDIARY PUBLIC SERVICE COMPANY OF OKLAHOMA, IS SEEKING TO SELL DYED NO. 2 FUEL OIL FROM ITS RIVERSIDE PLANT IN JENKS, OKLAHOMA, BEGINNING JULY 21, 2021.
AEP ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGES. COLUMBUS, OHIO, JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED SEVERAL CHANGES TO EXPAND THE SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE OF THE COMPANY'S LEADERSHIP TEAM, ACCORDING TO NICHOLAS K. AKINS, AEP CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
