AEP COMPLETES SALE OF RACINE HYDRO PLANT. COLUMBUS, OHIO, DEC. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) TODAY COMPLETED THE SALE OF RACINE PLANT, A 48-MEGAWATT (MW) HYDROELECTRIC FACILITY IN RACINE, OHIO, FROM AEP GENERATION RESOURCES, THE COMPANY'S COMPETITIVE GENERATION AFFILIATE, TO EAGLE CREEK RENEWABLE ENERGY. AEP ANNOUNCED FEB. 9, 2021, THAT IT HAD REACHED AN AGREEMENT TO SELL THE PLANT.
