AEP RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS FOR WOMEN. COLUMBUS, OHIO, AUG. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) WAS NAMED TO FORBES' BEST EMPLOYERS FOR WOMEN 2021 LIST. TO COMPILE THE LIST, FORBES PARTNERED WITH STATISTA TO SURVEY 50,000 AMERICANS, INCLUDING 30,000 WOMEN, WORKING FOR BUSINESSES WITH AT LEAST 1,000 EMPLOYEES.
