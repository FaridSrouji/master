AEP DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. COLUMBUS, OHIO , APRIL 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER CO. (NASDAQ: AEP) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 78 CENTS A SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 10, 2022, AND IS THE COMPANY'S 448TH CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK CASH DIVIDEND.
