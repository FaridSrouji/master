AEP NAMES STURGESS SVP, CONTROLLER AND CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER. COLUMBUS, OHIO , MARCH 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) HAS NAMED KATE STURGESS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CONTROLLER AND CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER EFFECTIVE MAY 9. IN THIS ROLE, SHE WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR ALL ASPECTS OF ACCOUNTING, FINANCIAL REPORTING AND REGULATORY ACCOUNTING SERVICES.
