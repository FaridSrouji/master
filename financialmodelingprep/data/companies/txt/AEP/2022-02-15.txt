AEP RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS. COLUMBUS, OHIO, FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP) WAS INCLUDED IN FORBES MAGAZINE'S 2022 AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS LIST. THE LIST WAS CREATED BASED ON AN INDEPENDENT, ANONYMOUS SURVEY ADMINISTERED TO APPROXIMATELY 60,000 EMPLOYEES WORKING FOR COMPANIES WITH AT LEAST 1,000 PEOPLE IN THEIR U.S. OPERATIONS.
