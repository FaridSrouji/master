AEP SEEKS BIDS FOR NATURAL GAS STORAGE SERVICE. COLUMBUS, OHIO , OCT. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP), ON BEHALF OF ITS SUBSIDIARY PUBLIC SERVICE COMPANY OF OKLAHOMA, TODAY ISSUED A REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP) FOR FIRM PHYSICAL NATURAL GAS STORAGE SERVICE. PROPOSALS FOR NATURAL GAS STORAGE SERVICE ARE SOUGHT FOR A TWO-YEAR TERM, BEGINNING APRIL 1, 2023 AND ENDING MARCH 31, 2025.
