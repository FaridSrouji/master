STANLEY BLACK & DECKER COMPLETES SALE OF SECURITY BUSINESS TO SECURITAS AB FOR $3.2 BILLION. TRANSACTION FURTHER FOCUSES STANLEY BLACK & DECKER'S PORTFOLIO ON ITS CORE BUSINESSES NEW BRITAIN, CONN. , JULY 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SALE OF MOST OF ITS SECURITY ASSETS TO SECURITAS AB (STO: SECU B) FOR $3.2 BILLION IN CASH.
