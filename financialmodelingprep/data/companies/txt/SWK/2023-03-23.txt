CONTINUED INVESTIGATION ALERT: SCOTT+SCOTT ATTORNEYS AT LAW LLP INVESTIGATES STANLEY BLACK & DECKER INC.'S DIRECTORS AND OFFICERS FOR BREACH OF FIDUCIARY DUTIES – SWK. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SCOTT+SCOTT ATTORNEYS AT LAW LLP (“SCOTT+SCOTT”), AN INTERNATIONAL SECURITIES AND CONSUMER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, CONTINUES ITS INVESTIGATION WHETHER CERTAIN DIRECTORS AND OFFICERS OF STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, INC. (“STANLEY BLACK & DECKER”) (NYSE: SWK) BREACHED THEIR FIDUCIARY DUTIES TO STANLEY BLACK & DECKER AND ITS SHAREHOLDERS.
