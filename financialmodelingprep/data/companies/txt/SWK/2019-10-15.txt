STANLEY BLACK & DECKER APPROVES REGULAR FOURTH QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 17, 2019. STANLEY BLACK & DECKER ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A REGULAR FOURTH QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.69 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 17, 2019 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 29, 2019.
