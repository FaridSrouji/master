STANLEY BLACK & DECKER REACHES AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS OIL & GAS BUSINESS. TRANSACTION FURTHER ADVANCES STANLEY BLACK & DECKER'S PORTFOLIO TRANSFORMATION AND STRATEGIC FOCUS ON CORE BUSINESSES NEW BRITAIN, CONN. , JUNE 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT FOR THE SALE OF ITS STANLEY OIL & GAS BUSINESS TO PIPELINE TECHNIQUE LIMITED, A LEADING PROVIDER OF SOLUTIONS TO THE ENERGY INDUSTRY.
