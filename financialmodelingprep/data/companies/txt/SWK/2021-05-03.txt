STANLEY BLACK & DECKER TO HOST 2021 VIRTUAL GROWTH SUMMIT ON MAY 13TH. NEW BRITAIN, CONN., MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL HOST ITS VIRTUAL GROWTH SUMMIT ON THURSDAY, MAY 13, 2021.
