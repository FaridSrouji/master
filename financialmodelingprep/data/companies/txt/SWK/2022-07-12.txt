STANLEY BLACK & DECKER ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP TRANSITION IN TOOLS BUSINESS. JAIME RAMIREZ STEPPING DOWN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT, TOOLS & STORAGE ROBERT RAFF, 20-YEAR COMPANY VETERAN TO LEAD TOOLS AS ACTING EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT, TOOLS NEW BRITAIN, CONN. , JULY 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JAIME RAMIREZ, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT, TOOLS & STORAGE, WILL BE STEPPING DOWN FROM HIS ROLE TO PURSUE OPPORTUNITIES OUTSIDE OF THE COMPANY, EFFECTIVE JULY 22, 2022.
