MCLAREN RACING LAUNCHES MULTI-YEAR PARTNERSHIP WITH STANLEY BLACK & DECKER. NEW BRITAIN, CONN. AND LONDON, JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCLAREN RACING AND STANLEY BLACK & DECKER TODAY ANNOUNCED A MULTI-YEAR PARTNERSHIP THAT WILL MAKE THE WORLD'S LARGEST TOOLS AND STORAGE COMPANY THE OFFICIAL TOOLS AND STORAGE PARTNER OF THE MCLAREN FORMULA 1 TEAM.
