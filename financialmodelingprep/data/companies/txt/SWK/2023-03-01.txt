STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, INC. ANNOUNCES PRICING OF NOTES OFFERING. NEW BRITAIN, CONN. , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, INC. (NYSE: SWK) (THE "COMPANY") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT PRICED ITS OFFERING OF $350 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 6.272% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2026 (THE "2026 NOTES") AT 100.000% OF PAR AND $400 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 6.000% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (THE "2028 NOTES," AND TOGETHER WITH THE 2026 NOTES, THE "NOTES") AT 99.893% OF PAR.
