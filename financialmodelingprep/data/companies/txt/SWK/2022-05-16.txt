CRAFTSMAN® CELEBRATES 95 YEARS OF BUILDING PRIDE. AMERICAN TOOL ICON, CRAFTSMAN, CELEBRATES ANNIVERSARY WITH GIVEAWAYS INCLUDING AN ALL-EXPENSES-PAID TRIP TO THE JULY 31 INDIANAPOLIS RACE KNOWN FOR SOME OF THE MOST TRUSTED AND RELIABLE WARRANTIES IN THE INDUSTRY, CRAFTSMAN'S SIGNATURE RED TOOLS CONTINUE TO EXPAND AND EVOLVE TOWSON, MD. , MAY 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --AMERICAN TOOL ICON CRAFTSMAN®, A STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) BRAND, IS CELEBRATING ITS 95TH ANNIVERSARY THIS MONTH BY THANKING ITS CUSTOMERS – THE HOMEOWNERS, HOME BUILDERS, AUTO ENTHUSIASTS AND MASTER MECHANICS – WITH WEEKLONG GIVEAWAYS INCLUDING A GRAND PRIZE TRIP TO THE JULY 31 INDIANAPOLIS RACE.
