STANLEY X APPOINTS MICHELLE BOCKMAN AS PRESIDENT. BOCKMAN BRINGS DEEP EXPERIENCE LEADING MAJOR CORPORATE TRANSFORMATIONS TO BUILD NEW SOURCES OF GROWTH AND INNOVATION FOR THE COMPANY SUNNYVALE, CALIF. , MAY 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY X, THE INNOVATION DIVISION OF STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK), OFFICIALLY ANNOUNCES MICHELLE BOCKMAN HAS BEEN APPOINTED PRESIDENT.
