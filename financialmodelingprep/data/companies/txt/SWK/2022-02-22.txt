STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, INC. ANNOUNCES PRICING OF NOTES OFFERING. NEW BRITAIN, CONN., FEB. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, INC. (NYSE: SWK) (THE "COMPANY") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT PRICED ITS OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.300% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025 (THE "2025 NOTES") AT 99.865% OF PAR AND $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 3.000% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2032 (THE "2032 NOTES," AND TOGETHER WITH THE 2025 NOTES, THE "NOTES") AT 99.810% OF PAR.
