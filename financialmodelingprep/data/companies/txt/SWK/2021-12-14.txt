STANLEY BLACK & DECKER RECOGNIZED WITH CDP'S DOUBLE 'A' SCORE FOR GLOBAL CLIMATE AND WATER STEWARDSHIP. NEW BRITAIN, CONN., DEC. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FOR THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED FOR ITS LEADERSHIP IN CLIMATE ACTION AND WATER SECURITY BY GLOBAL ENVIRONMENTAL NON-PROFIT CDP.
