SWK INVESTORS HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, INC. SECURITIES FRAUD LAWSUIT. LOS ANGELES , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LAW OFFICES OF FRANK R. CRUZ ANNOUNCES THAT INVESTORS WITH SUBSTANTIAL LOSSES HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD THE SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, INC. ("STANLEY" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: SWK).
SWK LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, INC. ("STANLEY" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: SWK) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF STANLEY INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN OCTOBER 28, 2021 AND JULY 28, 2022.
