SEC CHARGES STANLEY BLACK & DECKER AND FORMER EXECUTIVE FOR FAILURES IN EXECUTIVE PERKS DISCLOSURE. WASHINGTON, D.C.--(NEWSFILE CORP. - JUNE 20, 2023) - THE SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION TODAY ANNOUNCED SETTLED CHARGES AGAINST STANLEY BLACK & DECKER INC., A PUBLICLY TRADED TOOLS COMPANY, FOR FAILING TO DISCLOSE PERQUISITES IT PROVIDED TO CERTAIN EXECUTIVES.
