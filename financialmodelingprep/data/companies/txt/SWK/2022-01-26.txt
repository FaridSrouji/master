STANLEY BLACK & DECKER TO RESTATE PREVIOUSLY ISSUED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS. NEW BRITAIN, CONN., JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT PLANS TO RESTATE ITS PREVIOUSLY ISSUED CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS TO CORRECT ITS ACCOUNTING WITH RESPECT TO ITS EQUITY UNITS IN RESPONSE TO COMMENTS RECEIVED FROM THE SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION (THE "SEC").
