STANLEY BLACK & DECKER TO SELL SECURITY BUSINESS FOR $3.2 BILLION TO SECURITAS. NEW BRITAIN, CONN., DEC. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT FOR THE SALE OF MOST OF ITS SECURITY ASSETS TO SECURITAS AB (STO: SECU B) FOR $3.2 BILLION IN CASH.
