LINCOLN FINANCIAL APPOINTS MATTHEW GROVE AS HEAD OF INDIVIDUAL LIFE & ANNUITIES AND LINCOLN FINANCIAL NETWORK. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE:LNC) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT MATTHEW GROVE WILL JOIN THE COMPANY IN JULY AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, HEAD OF INDIVIDUAL LIFE & ANNUITIES AND LINCOLN FINANCIAL NETWORK. GROVE WILL BE A MEMBER OF LINCOLN'S SENIOR MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE AND WILL REPORT TO ELLEN COOPER, LINCOLN'S CEO-ELECT, WHO WILL STEP INTO THE CEO ROLE AFTER THE COMPANY'S ANNUAL SHAREHOLDER MEETING THIS MAY.
