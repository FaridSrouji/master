THE INDEX'S GLOBAL ALLOCATION PROVIDES INVESTORS WITH GREATER DIVERSIFICATION IN THEIR PORTFOLIOS, AS WELL AS DAILY VOLATILITY CONTROLS TO HELP MITIGATE RISK.
