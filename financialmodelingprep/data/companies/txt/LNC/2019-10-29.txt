LINCOLN NATIONAL CORPORATION APPROVES RAISING THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 1, 2020. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF LINCOLN NATIONAL CORPORATION APPROVED RAISING THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON SHARES TO $0.40 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND REPRESENTS AN 8% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR-YEAR LEVEL. THE INCREASEDDIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK WILL BE PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 1, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 10, 2020.
