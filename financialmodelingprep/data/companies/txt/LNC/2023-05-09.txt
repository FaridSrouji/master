LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2023 RESULTS. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE: LNC) TODAY REPORTED A NET LOSS AVAILABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023 OF $(909) MILLION, OR $(5.37) PER DILUTED SHARE, COMPARED TO NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022 OF $1,481 MILLION, OR $8.39 PER DILUTED SHARE. THE NET LOSS AVAILABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS THIS QUARTER WAS PRIMARILY DRIVEN BY UNFAVORABLE IMPACTS FROM A PORTION OF THE MRB AND HEDGE INSTRUMENT FAIR VALUE CH.
