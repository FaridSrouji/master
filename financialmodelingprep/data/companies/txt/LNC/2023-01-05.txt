LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP NAMES RODNEY KUNTZ VICE PRESIDENT, GROUP BENEFITS SMALL BUSINESS SALES AND CHANNEL DEVELOPMENT. RADNOR, PA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE: LNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT RODNEY KUNTZ HAS BEEN NAMED VICE PRESIDENT, GROUP BENEFITS SMALL BUSINESS SALES AND CHANNEL DEVELOPMENT. IN THIS ROLE, KUNTZ WILL SERVE AS STRATEGY LEAD, PARTNERING WITH BENEFITS CONSULTANTS AND BROKERS TO DISTRIBUTE LINCOLN'S INDUSTRY-LEADING GROUP INSURANCE PRODUCTS AND SERVICES TO SMALL-BUSINESS OWNERS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES. HE WILL REPORT TO CARL JONES, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, HEAD OF GROUP BENEFITS SALES.
