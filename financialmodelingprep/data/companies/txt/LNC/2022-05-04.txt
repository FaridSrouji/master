LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE: LNC) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022 OF $104 MILLION, OR $0.58 PER DILUTED SHARE AVAILABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS, COMPARED TO NET INCOME IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021 OF $225 MILLION, OR $1.16 PER DILUTED SHARE AVAILABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS.
