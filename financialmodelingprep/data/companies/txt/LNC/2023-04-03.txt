GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP, A LEADING SECURITIES FRAUD LAW FIRM, ANNOUNCES INVESTIGATION OF LINCOLN NATIONAL CORPORATION (LNC) ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS. LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP (“GPM”), A LEADING NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LAW FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMMENCED AN INVESTIGATION ON BEHALF OF LINCOLN NATIONAL CORPORATION (“LINCOLN” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: LNC) INVESTORS CONCERNING THE COMPANY'S POSSIBLE VIOLATIONS OF THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
