LINCOLN INVESTS TO PRESERVE THE PAST AND ADVANCE THE FUTURE WITH THE HISTORYMAKERS AND UNCF. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS PART OF ITS ACTION PLAN TO ADVANCE RACIAL EQUITY, LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE: LNC) TODAY ANNOUNCED PARTNERSHIPS WITH THE HISTORYMAKERS AND UNCF TO FURTHER SUPPORT BLACK COMMUNITIES THROUGH THE PRESERVATION OF AFRICAN AMERICAN HISTORICAL RECORDS AND CREATION OF A FUTURE TALENT PIPELINE OF HIGH-ACHIEVING AFRICAN AMERICAN STUDENTS.
