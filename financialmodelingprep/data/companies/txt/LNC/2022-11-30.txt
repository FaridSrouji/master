LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP ENHANCES SUPPLEMENTAL HEALTH SUITE WITH EXPANDED HOSPITAL INDEMNITY INSURANCE COVERAGE. THE NEW ENHANCEMENTS INCLUDE TWO FAMILY-FOCUSED NEWBORN CARE OPTIONS AND AN ADDITIONAL BENEFIT FOR HEALTHCARE CLIENTS THAT INCLUDES COVERAGE WHEN BEING TREATED AT AN EMPLOYER-AFFILIATED HOSPITAL.
