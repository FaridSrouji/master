LINCOLN FINANCIAL NETWORK WELCOMES PAIR OF HERITAGE FINANCIAL CONSULTANTS FINANCIAL PROFESSIONALS. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINCOLN FINANCIAL NETWORK (LFN), THE RETAIL WEALTH MANAGEMENT AFFILIATE OF LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE: LNC), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FINANCIAL PLANNERS ANDY ANDREWS AND JOE DANKOWSKI OF HERITAGE FINANCIAL CONSULTANTS (HFC), LLC HAVE JOINED THE FIRM.
