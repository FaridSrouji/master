FOUR OUT OF FIVE SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS VIEW EMPLOYEE BENEFITS AS A TOP PRIORITY DUE TO PANDEMIC. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE LAST TWO YEARS HAVE HAD AN IMPACT ON BUSINESSES OF ALL SIZES, AND SMALL BUSINESSES ARE NO EXCEPTION — IN FACT, NEARLY ALL SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS SURVEYED (93%) HAVE RE-EVALUATED THEIR STRATEGY AND PLAN TO MAKE CHANGES TO THEIR BUSINESS DUE TO COVID-19, ACCORDING TO A NEW SMALL BUSINESS OWNER SURVEY FROM LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE: LNC).
