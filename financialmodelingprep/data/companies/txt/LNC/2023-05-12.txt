LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP NAMES ANDREW D. RALLIS CHIEF RISK OFFICER. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE: LNC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ANDREW (ANDY) RALLIS WILL JOIN THE COMPANY ON MAY 15, 2023 AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF RISK OFFICER. RALLIS WILL HAVE INDEPENDENT OVERSIGHT OF ALL FORMS OF RISK ACROSS THE COMPANY – INCLUDING MARKET RISK, ASSET-LIABILITY MANAGEMENT RISK, INVESTMENT AND CREDIT RISK, PRODUCT AND INSURANCE RISK, OPERATIONAL RISK AND STRATEGIC RISK. HE WILL BE A MEMBER OF THE COMPANY'S SENIOR MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE AND WILL.
