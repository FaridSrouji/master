ELLEN COOPER ASSUMES ROLE OF LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ELLEN COOPER ASSUMES THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT AND CEO OF LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP.
LINCOLN NATIONAL CORPORATION'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE:LNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF LINCOLN NATIONAL CORPORATION DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.45 PER SHARE ON THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK WILL BE PAYABLE AUGUST 1, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 11, 2022.
