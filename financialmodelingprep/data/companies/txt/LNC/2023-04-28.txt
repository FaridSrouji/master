SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS HAVE BIG PERSONAL FINANCE CONCERNS, ACCORDING TO NEW LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP STUDY. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #SMALLBUSINESSWEEK--LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE: LNC) RELEASED NEW RESEARCH TODAY THAT SHOWS SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS HAVE BIG PERSONAL FINANCE CONCERNS. ACCORDING TO THE COMPANY'S CONSUMER SENTIMENT TRACKER, THERE ARE SEVERAL INSURANCE AND RETIREMENT PLANNING ISSUES THAT TOP THEIR LISTS. SPECIFICALLY, RESPONDENTS WHO OWN SMALL BUSINESSES (VERSUS THOSE WHO DO NOT) ARE MOST CONCERNED ABOUT: HAVING ENOUGH INCOME IN RETIREMENT (65% VS. 48%) THE IMPACT OF TAXES ON RETIREMENT SAVINGS/INVEST.
