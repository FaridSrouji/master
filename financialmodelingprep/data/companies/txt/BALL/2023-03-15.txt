BALL TO BEGIN DISCUSSIONS REGARDING POSSIBLE CLOSURE OF WALLKILL, NEW YORK BEVERAGE PACKAGING PLANT. WESTMINSTER, COLO. , MARCH 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BALL) HAS NOTIFIED ITS EMPLOYEES AND THE UNION, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE COLLECTIVE BARGAINING AGREEMENT (CBA), ABOUT THE POTENTIAL CLOSURE OF THE WALLKILL, NEW YORK, PLANT.
