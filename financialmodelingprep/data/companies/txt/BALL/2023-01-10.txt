BALL AEROSPACE ENTERS FINAL STAGES OF BUILDING SPACE FORCE'S NEXT-GEN OPERATIONAL WEATHER SATELLITE. BALL AEROSPACE COMPLETES SATELLITE BUS INTEGRATION AND MICROWAVE IMAGER TESTING BROOMFIELD, COLO. , JAN. 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL AEROSPACE COMPLETED THE SPACECRAFT BUS FOR THE WEATHER SYSTEM FOLLOW-ON-MICROWAVE (WSF-M) SATELLITE, THE U.S. SPACE FORCE'S NEXT-GENERATION OPERATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL SATELLITE SYSTEM.
