BALL CORPORATION EXPANDS THE PORTFOLIO OF ITS INFINITELY RECYCLABLE BALL ALUMINUM CUP® WITH THE ANNOUNCEMENT OF TWO NEW, SMALLER SIZES. IN RESPONSE TO GROWING DEMAND FOR SUSTAINABLE BEVERAGE CUP OPTIONS, BALL ADDS 9-OUNCE AND 12-OUNCE ALUMINUM CUP SIZES TO ITS EXISTING 16-OUNCE, 20-OUNCE AND 24-OUNCE CUP OFFERINGS WESTMINSTER, COLO. , OCT. 20, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BALL), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF INFINITELY RECYCLABLE ALUMINUM BEVERAGE PACKAGING AS WELL AS AEROSPACE AND OTHER TECHNOLOGIES, ANNOUNCED THE AVAILABILITY OF TWO NEW SIZES OF ITS INFINITELY RECYCLABLE BALL ALUMINUM CUP®, THEREFORE BROADENING THE OPPORTUNITY FOR VENUES, CONCESSIONAIRES AND MORE TO OFFER CONSUMERS A FULL PORTFOLIO OF SUSTAINABLE CUP OPTIONS.
