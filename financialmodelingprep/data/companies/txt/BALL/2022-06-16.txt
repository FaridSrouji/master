BALL CORPORATION RECOMMENDS REJECTION OF AMENDED BELOW-MARKET MINI-TENDER OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL CORPORATION. WESTMINSTER, COLO. , JUNE 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BALL) RECEIVED NOTICE TODAY OF AN AMENDED UNSOLICITED MINI-TENDER OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL CORPORATION (TRC) TO PURCHASE UP TO 2,000,000 BALL COMMON SHARES, REPRESENTING APPROXIMATELY .63% OF THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING SHARES.
