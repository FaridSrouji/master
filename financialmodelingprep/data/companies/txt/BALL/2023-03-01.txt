BALL AEROSPACE SELECTED TO SUPPORT NATIONAL SCIENTIFIC AND TECHNICAL INTELLIGENCE MISSION FOR NEXT DECADE. BROOMFIELD, COLO. , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL AEROSPACE RECEIVED A SUBCONTRACT AWARD TO SUPPORT THE NOVASTAR PROGRAM OPERATED BY THE NATIONAL AIR AND SPACE INTELLIGENCE CENTER (NASIC), LOCATED ON WRIGHT-PATTERSON AIR FORCE BASE NEAR DAYTON, OHIO.
