BALL ANNOUNCES COMPLETION OF CREDIT FACILITIES. WESTMINSTER, COLO. , JUNE 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BALL) ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED THE CLOSING OF ITS NEW REVOLVER AND TERM LOAN SENIOR SECURED CREDIT FACILITIES THAT REFINANCE ITS EXISTING SENIOR SECURED CREDIT FACILITIES ENTERED INTO ON MARCH 25, 2019.
