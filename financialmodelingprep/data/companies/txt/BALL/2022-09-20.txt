BALL CORPORATION ENTERS INTO AN ALLIANCE WITH BOOMERANG WATER TO PROVIDE CUSTOMERS WITH ON-SITE, REFILLABLE BOTTLED WATER SOLUTIONS. BOOMERANG WATER'S INNOVATIVE NEW TECHNOLOGY, PAIRED WITH BALL'S REFILLABLE ALUMINUM BOTTLES, MEET GROWING DEMAND FOR SUSTAINABLE BEVERAGE PACKAGING IN VENUES SUCH AS RESORTS, CRUISE LINES AND CAMPUSES WESTMINSTER, COLO. , SEPT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BALL), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF INFINITELY RECYCLABLE ALUMINUM BEVERAGE PACKAGING, AND BOOMERANG WATER LLC, A LEADER IN SUSTAINABLE WATER BOTTLING TECHNOLOGY, ANNOUNCED AN ALLIANCE TO BRING CONSUMERS A SUSTAINABLE WAY TO STAY HYDRATED.
