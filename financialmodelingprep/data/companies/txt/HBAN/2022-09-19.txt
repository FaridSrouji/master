HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS SERIES I PREFERRED STOCK. COLUMBUS, OHIO , SEPT. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED AND SET ASIDE A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S 5.70% SERIES I NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK (NASDAQ: HBANM) OF $356.25 PER SHARE (EQUIVALENT TO $0.35625 PER DEPOSITARY SHARE) PAYABLE DECEMBER 1, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 15, 2022.
