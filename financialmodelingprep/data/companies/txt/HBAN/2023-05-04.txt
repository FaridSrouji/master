HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED INCREASES ITS PRIME RATE TO 8.25 PERCENT. COLUMBUS, OHIO , MAY 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: HBAN) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS PRIME RATE IS INCREASING FROM 8 PERCENT TO 8.25 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE MAY 4, 2023. THE RATE WAS LAST CHANGED ON MARCH 23, 2023, WHEN HUNTINGTON INCREASED IT FROM 7.75 PERCENT TO 8 PERCENT.
