HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED INCREASES ITS PRIME RATE TO 8 PERCENT. COLUMBUS, OHIO , MARCH 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: HBAN) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS PRIME RATE IS INCREASING FROM 7.75 PERCENT TO 8 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE MARCH 23, 2023. THE RATE WAS LAST CHANGED ON FEBRUARY 2, 2023, WHEN HUNTINGTON INCREASED IT FROM 7.5 PERCENT TO 7.75 PERCENT.
