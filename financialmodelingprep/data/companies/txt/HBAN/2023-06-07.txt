CAPSTONE PARTNERS BROADENS SERVICE OFFERINGS WITH FORMATION OF ESOP GROUP. NEW SERVICE LINE BOLSTERS THE FIRM'S FULLY INTEGRATED SUITE OF SOLUTIONS WITH A UNIQUE ALTERNATIVE FOR CLIENTS SEEKING LIQUIDITY. BOSTON , JUNE 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPSTONE PARTNERS, A LEADING MIDDLE MARKET INVESTMENT BANKING FIRM, HAS LAUNCHED THE FORMATION OF AN EMPLOYEE STOCK OWNERSHIP (ESOP) ADVISORY GROUP ("THE GROUP"), AMPLIFYING ITS FULL-SCOPE SUITE OF SOLUTIONS OFFERED TO CLIENTS.
