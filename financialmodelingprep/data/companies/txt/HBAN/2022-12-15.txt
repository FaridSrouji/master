HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED INCREASES ITS PRIME RATE TO 7.5 PERCENT. COLUMBUS, OHIO , DEC. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: HBAN) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS PRIME RATE IS INCREASING FROM 7 PERCENT TO 7.5 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 15, 2022. THE RATE WAS LAST CHANGED ON NOVEMBER 3, 2022, WHEN HUNTINGTON INCREASED ITS PRIME RATE FROM 6.25 PERCENT TO 7 PERCENT.
