HUNTINGTON 2020 ESG REPORT HIGHLIGHTS COMMITMENT TO SHAREHOLDERS, CUSTOMERS, COLLEAGUES AND COMMUNITIES. COLUMBUS, OHIO, AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: HBAN) HAS ISSUED ITS 2020 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) REPORT, WHICH HIGHLIGHTS HOW HUNTINGTON HELPS PEOPLE REACH THEIR GOALS, PROVIDES BUSINESSES WITH THE RESOURCES TO GROW, AND WORKS TO CREATE PROSPEROUS AND RESILIENT COMMUNITIES. THE REPORT SHINES A LIGHT ON HUNTINGTON'S 2020 COMMITMENTS TO SHAREHOLDERS, CUSTOMERS, COLLEAGUES, AND COMMUNITIES, ESPECIALLY DURING A YEAR OF SOCIAL AND RACIAL UNREST, ECONOMIC UNCERTAINTY AND CLIMATE-RELATED DISRUPTION.
