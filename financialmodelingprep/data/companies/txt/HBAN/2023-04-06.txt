CHRISTIAN CORTS NAMED REGIONAL BANKING DIRECTOR FOR HUNTINGTON NATIONAL BANK. EXPERIENCED LEADER BRINGS DEEP EXPERTISE AND STRONG RECORD OF PERFORMANCE IN ALL ASPECTS OF BANKING AND OPERATIONS TO THIS NEWLY CREATED ROLE COLUMBUS, OHIO , APRIL 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON NATIONAL BANK (NASDAQ: HBAN) HAS SELECTED CHRISTIAN CORTS AS ITS NEW DIRECTOR, OVERSEEING ITS REGIONAL BANKING TEAMS ACROSS HUNTINGTON'S 11-STATE FOOTPRINT. MOST RECENTLY, CORTS SERVED AS THE NORTH TEXAS REGIONAL PRESIDENT AT TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION.
