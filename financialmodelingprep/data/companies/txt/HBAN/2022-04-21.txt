HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDENDS ON ITS COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCKS. COLUMBUS, OHIO , APRIL 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK (NASDAQ: HBAN) OF $0.155 PER COMMON SHARE, UNCHANGED FROM THE PRIOR QUARTER.  THE COMMON STOCK CASH DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 1, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 17, 2022.
