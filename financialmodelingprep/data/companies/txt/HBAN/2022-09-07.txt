HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES TO SPEAK AT THE BARCLAYS GLOBAL FINANCIAL SERVICES CONFERENCE. COLUMBUS, OHIO , SEPT. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: HBAN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER ZACH WASSERMAN WILL PRESENT AT THE BARCLAYS GLOBAL FINANCIAL SERVICES CONFERENCE ON MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 12, 2022, AT 9:00 AM (EASTERN TIME).
