HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED INCREASES ITS PRIME RATE TO 4 PERCENT. COLUMBUS, OHIO , MAY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: HBAN) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS PRIME RATE IS INCREASING FROM 3.5 PERCENT TO 4 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE MAY 5, 2022. THE RATE WAS LAST CHANGED ON MARCH 17, 2022, WHEN HUNTINGTON INCREASED ITS PRIME RATE FROM 3.25 PERCENT TO 3.5 PERCENT.
