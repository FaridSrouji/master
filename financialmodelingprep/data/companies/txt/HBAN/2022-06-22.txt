CAPSTONE PARTNERS NAMES PETER NAM HEAD OF INDUSTRIAL TECHNOLOGY GROUP. BOSTON , JUNE 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPSTONE PARTNERS, A LEADING MIDDLE MARKET INVESTMENT BANKING FIRM, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PETER NAM , MANAGING DIRECTOR, HAS BEEN NAMED HEAD OF THE FIRM'S INDUSTRIAL TECHNOLOGY INVESTMENT BANKING GROUP (ITG). BASED IN ORANGE COUNTY, CA, PETER HAS BEEN WITH CAPSTONE FOR 15 YEARS, FOCUSING ON ADVISING MIDDLE MARKET BUSINESSES THAT PROVIDE SENSING, METROLOGY, CONTROL, ROBOTICS, AUTOMATION, PHOTONICS, OPTICS, AI, AND INDUSTRIAL IOT TECHNOLOGIES.
