HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES INCORPORATED REPORTS 2021 THIRD-QUARTER EARNINGS. COLUMBUS, OHIO, OCT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- 2021 THIRD-QUARTER HIGHLIGHTS: EARNINGS PER COMMON SHARE (EPS) FOR THE QUARTER WERE $0.22, A DECREASE OF $0.05 YEAR-OVER-YEAR. EXCLUDING APPROXIMATELY $0.13 PER COMMON SHARE AFTER TAX OF TCF FINANCIAL CORPORATION (TCF) ACQUISITION-RELATED NOTABLE ITEMS, ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER COMMON SHARE WERE $0.35.
