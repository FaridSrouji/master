HUNTINGTON NATIONAL BANK AND MAGNUSMODE COLLABORATE TO ENHANCE THE BANKING EXPERIENCE FOR AUTISTIC AND NEURODIVERSE PEOPLE. BANKING-SPECIFIC DIGITAL GUIDES WILL HELP MAKE ACTIVITIES MORE ACCESSIBLE; EQUITABLE COLUMBUS, OHIO , DEC. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HUNTINGTON NATIONAL BANK, A SUBSIDIARY OF HUNTINGTON BANCSHARES, INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: HBAN), ANNOUNCED A NEW COLLABORATION WITH MAGNUSCARDS BY MAGNUSMODE, A FREE APP THAT PROVIDES DIGITAL, STEP-BY-STEP VISUAL GUIDES IN THE FORM OF CARD DECKS TO MAKE EVERYDAY EXPERIENCES EASIER TO NAVIGATE FOR AUTISTIC AND NEURODIVERSE PEOPLE. EACH DIGITAL CARD DECK COMBINES EDUCATIONAL METHODS, VISUAL INSTRUCTION, PERSONAL ASSISTANCE FROM MAGNUSMODE, AND REALISTIC SCENARIOS TO SUPPORT INCLUSIVE AND INDEPENDENT LIVING.
