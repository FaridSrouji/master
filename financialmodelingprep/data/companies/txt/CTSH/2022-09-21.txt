COGNIZANT SELECTED AS FRESHFIELDS BRUCKHAUS DERINGER'S NEW GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY SERVICES PARTNER. COGNIZANT WILL PROVIDE MANAGED IT SERVICES TO SUPPORT THE GLOBAL LAW FIRM'S BUSINESS GROWTH AND ENHANCE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE LONDON , SEPT. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN SELECTED BY GLOBAL LAW FIRM FRESHFIELDS BRUCKHAUS DERINGER TO MANAGE GLOBAL IT OPERATIONS AND SUPPORT ITS AMBITIOUS GLOBAL EXPANSION PLANS.
