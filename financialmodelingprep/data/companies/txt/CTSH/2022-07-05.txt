COGNIZANT WINS MULTI-YEAR CONTRACT FROM NATIONAL INSURANCE COMPANY TO ACCELERATE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION. PUBLIC SECTOR INSURER SELECTS COGNIZANT TO FURTHER STRENGTHEN ITS OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE AND FUTURE-READY TECHNOLOGY ROADMAP NEW DELHI , JULY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN SELECTED AS A STRATEGIC TECHNOLOGY PROVIDER BY NATIONAL INSURANCE COMPANY LIMITED (NICL) TO ACCELERATE AND HELP MANAGE ITS DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION. NICL IS ONE OF INDIA'S LEADING PUBLIC SECTOR GENERAL INSURANCE COMPANIES.
