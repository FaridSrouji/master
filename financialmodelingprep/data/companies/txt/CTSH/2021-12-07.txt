COGNIZANT EXPANDS ITS GLOBAL DIGITAL ENGINEERING PORTFOLIO WITH THE ACQUISITION OF DEVBRIDGE. TEANECK, N.J., DEC. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS AGREED TO ACQUIRE DEVBRIDGE, A PRIVATELY-HELD SOFTWARE CONSULTANCY AND PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT COMPANY HEADQUARTERED IN CHICAGO.
