COGNIZANT RECOGNIZED FOR INTELLIGENT PROCESS AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS AND LEADERSHIP BY EVEREST GROUP. TEANECK, N.J., APRIL 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT  (NASDAQ: CTSH) ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN HIGHLIGHTED AS A LEADER IN THE NEW EVEREST GROUP PEAK MATRIX® FOR INTELLIGENT PROCESS AUTOMATION (IPA) SOLUTION PROVIDERS 2021.
