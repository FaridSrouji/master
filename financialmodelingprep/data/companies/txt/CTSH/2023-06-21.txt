COGNIZANT IS PARTNERING WITH ACCURAY TO ACCELERATE CORPORATE TRANSFORMATION AND DRIVE OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY IN THEIR RADIATION THERAPY BUSINESS. PARTNERSHIP WILL SUPPORT ACCURAY WITH SAP S/4HANA IMPLEMENTATION TEANECK, N.J. , JUNE 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS PARTNERING WITH ACCURAY INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: ARAY) TO SUPPORT THE COMPANY'S DEPLOYMENT OF SAP S/4HANA TO OBTAIN BETTER DATA AND ANALYTICS, AND ACHIEVE GREATER BUSINESS EFFICIENCIES.
