COGNIZANT NAMES BRAM SCHOT TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. SEASONED PUBLIC COMPANY EXECUTIVE BRINGS STRONG OPERATIONAL, MANAGERIAL AND TECHNOLOGICAL EXPERTISE TEANECK, N.J. , MARCH 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ABRAHAM ("BRAM") SCHOT TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE "BOARD") AS AN INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR, EFFECTIVE APRIL 3, 2023.
