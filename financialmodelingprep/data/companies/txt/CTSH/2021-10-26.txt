CABOT CORPORATION SELECTS COGNIZANT TO TRANSFORM ITS DIGITAL OPERATING MODEL. TEANECK, N.J., OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) ANNOUNCED TODAY A NEW BUSINESS ENGAGEMENT WITH LEADING GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS AND PERFORMANCE MATERIALS COMPANY CABOT CORPORATION (NYSE: CBT) TO HELP THE COMPANY TRANSFORM ITS DIGITAL OPERATING MODEL.
