COGNIZANT COLLABORATES WITH QUALCOMM TO LAUNCH 5G EXPERIENCE CENTER FOR DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION ACROSS INDUSTRY VERTICALS. A NEW 5G EXPERIENCE CENTER IN ATLANTA INVITES CLIENTS ACROSS INDUSTRIES TO EXPERIENCE THE TRUE BUSINESS VALUE OF 5G TECHNOLOGY TEANECK, N.J. , OCT. 25, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT IS EXPANDING ITS COLLABORATION WITH QUALCOMM TECHNOLOGIES, INC., TO ACCELERATE ENTERPRISE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION THROUGH A NEW 5G EXPERIENCE CENTER IN ATLANTA, GA.
