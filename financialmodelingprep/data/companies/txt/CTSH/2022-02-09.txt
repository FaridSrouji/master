COGNIZANT SELECTED BY VOLVO CARS FOR FINANCE & ACCOUNTING AND PROCUREMENT BUSINESS PROCESS SERVICES. TEANECK, N.J., FEB. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS RENEWED ITS CONTRACT AGREEMENT WITH VOLVO CARS TO PROVIDE FINANCE AND ACCOUNTING (F&A), AND PROCUREMENT SERVICES.
