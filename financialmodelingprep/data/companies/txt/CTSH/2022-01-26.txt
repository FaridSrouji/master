DJE HOLDINGS TO ACQUIRE MUSTACHE. NEW YORK, JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DJE HOLDINGS, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL ACQUIRE  MUSTACHE LLC,  AN INTEGRATED CONTENT AGENCY BASED IN BROOKLYN, NEW YORK. UPON CLOSING OF THE TRANSACTION, MUSTACHE WILL JOIN THE DJE HOLDINGS PORTFOLIO AND WILL SERVE AS THE CONTENT, CREATIVE AND DIGITAL HUB FOR THE FIRM'S STRATEGIC SECTOR AGENCIES (SSAS), WHICH INCLUDE REVERE, SALUTEM, AND EDIBLE.
