COGNIZANT TO ACQUIRE MOBICA TO ENHANCE ITS IOT SOFTWARE ENGINEERING SERVICE OFFERINGS. ACQUISITION EXPANDS COGNIZANT'S IOT EMBEDDED SOFTWARE ENGINEERING CAPABILITIES ACROSS TECHNOLOGY AND AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRIES TEANECK, N.J. , JAN. 23, 2023  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE MOBICA, AN IOT SOFTWARE ENGINEERING SERVICES PROVIDER HEADQUARTERED IN MANCHESTER, UNITED KINGDOM.
