COGNIZANT TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS TO BUY EI-TECHNOLOGIES. ENTERED INTO EXCLUSIVE NEGOTIATIONS TO ACQUIRE THE FRENCH OPERATIONS OF EI-TECHNOLOGIES, A PARIS-BASED, PRIVATELY-HELD DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY CONSULTING FIRM AND LEADING INDEPENDENT SALESFORCE SPECIALIST IN FRANCE..PROPOSED ACQUISITION WOULD COMPLEMENT COGNIZANT'S GLOBAL SALESFORCE PRACTICE, EXPANDING CLIENT RESOURCES IN EUROPE..THROUGH THIS ACQUISITION, COGNIZANT WOULD ADD APPROXIMATELY 345 EMPLOYEES IN FRANCE.
