DSB SELECTS COGNIZANT AS SOLE OFFSHORE SUPPLIER OF IT CONSULTING SERVICES. DENMARK'S LARGEST RAILWAY OPERATOR EXTENDS ITS RELATIONSHIP WITH COGNIZANT FOR ITS ONGOING STRATEGY TO USE DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES TO IMPROVE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE ON ITS PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION SYSTEMS TEANECK, N.J. , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN SELECTED BY DSB, DENMARK'S STATE RAILWAY OPERATOR, AS THE SOLE PROVIDER OF OFFSHORE IT CONSULTING SERVICES TO ACCELERATE, SCALE AND FURTHER IMPROVE ITS ABILITY TO DELIVER AGAINST DSB'S DIGITAL STRATEGY.
