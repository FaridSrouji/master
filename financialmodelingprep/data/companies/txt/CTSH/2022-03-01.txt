COGNIZANT COLLABORATES WITH MICROSOFT TO OFFER NEW VIRTUAL HEALTHCARE SOLUTION FOR REMOTE PATIENT MONITORING AND IMPROVED CARE. TEANECK, N.J., MARCH 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS COLLABORATING WITH MICROSOFT TO DELIVER A NEW DIGITAL HEALTH SOLUTION TO ENHANCE REMOTE PATIENT MONITORING FOR IMPROVED MEDICAL CARE.
