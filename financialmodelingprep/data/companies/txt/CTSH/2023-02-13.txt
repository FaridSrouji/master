COGNIZANT NAMES NELLA DOMENICI TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. DOMENICI BRINGS STRONG EXPERTISE IN STRATEGIC FINANCE, CORPORATE STRATEGY AND M&A TEANECK, N.J. , FEB. 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF NELLA DOMENICI TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE "BOARD") AS AN INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 21, 2023.
