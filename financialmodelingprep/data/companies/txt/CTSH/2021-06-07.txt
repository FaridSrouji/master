AKER SOLUTIONS EXTENDS RELATIONSHIP WITH COGNIZANT TO MODERNIZE ITS IT SYSTEMS AND HELP SPEED TRANSITION TO RENEWABLE ENERGY. TEANECK, N.J., JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS EXTENDED ITS RELATIONSHIP WITH GLOBAL ENGINEERING COMPANY AKER SOLUTIONS TO MODERNIZE AND SIMPLIFY ITS ENTIRE IT INFRASTRUCTURE, INCLUDING ITS BUSINESS TECHNOLOGY NETWORK AND APPLICATION MAINTENANCE AND DEVELOPMENT.
