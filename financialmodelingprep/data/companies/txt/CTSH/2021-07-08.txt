PHILIPS AND COGNIZANT COLLABORATE TO INTRODUCE DIGITAL HEALTH SOLUTIONS TO PROVIDERS, RESEARCHERS AND PATIENTS. AMSTERDAM AND TEANECK, N.J., JULY 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  ROYAL PHILIPS  (NYSE: PHG, AEX: PHIA), A GLOBAL LEADER IN HEALTH TECHNOLOGY, AND COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH), A WORLD-LEADING PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW COLLABORATION TO DEVELOP END-TO-END DIGITAL HEALTH SOLUTIONS THAT WILL ENABLE HEALTHCARE ORGANIZATIONS AND LIFE SCIENCES COMPANIES TO IMPROVE PATIENT CARE AND ACCELERATE CLINICAL TRIALS.
