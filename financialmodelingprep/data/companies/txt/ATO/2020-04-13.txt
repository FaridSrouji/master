ATMOS ENERGY CORP ENTERS TERM LOAN AGREEMENT WITH CRÉDIT AGRICOLE CORPORATE AND INVESTMENT BANK. ATMOS ENERGY CORP - ENTERED INTO A TERM LOAN AGREEMENT WITH CRÉDIT AGRICOLE CORPORATE AND INVESTMENT BANK.ATMOS ENERGY CORP - TERM LOAN AGREEMENT PROVIDING CO WITH A $200 MILLION SENIOR UNSECURED TERM LOAN FACILITY.ATMOS ENERGY - PROCEEDS OF TERM LOAN FACILITY WILL BE USED BY CO TO REPAY $200 MILLION OF OUTSTANDING COMMERCIAL PAPER OUTSTANDING AS OF MARCH 31, 2020.ATMOS ENERGY CORP - TERM LOAN FACILITY WILL MATURE ON APRIL 9, 2022.
