ATMOS ENERGY DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ATO) SAID TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK OF 62.5 CENTS PER SHARE. THE INDICATED ANNUAL DIVIDEND IS $2.50. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JUNE 7, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 24, 2021. THIS IS THE COMPANY'S 150TH CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
