ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION REPORTS EARNINGS FOR FISCAL 2023 SECOND QUARTER; TIGHTENS FISCAL 2023 GUIDANCE. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ATO) TODAY REPORTED CONSOLIDATED RESULTS FOR ITS SECOND FISCAL QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2023. HIGHLIGHTS EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE WAS $4.40 FOR THE SIX MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2023; $2.48 PER DILUTED SHARE FOR THE SECOND FISCAL QUARTER. CONSOLIDATED NET INCOME WAS $629.5 MILLION FOR THE SIX MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2023; $357.7 MILLION FOR THE SECOND FISCAL QUARTER. CAPITAL EXPENDITURES TOTALED $1,415.3 MILLION FOR THE SIX MONTHS ENDED MARC.
ATMOS ENERGY DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ATO) SAID TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK OF 74.0 CENTS PER SHARE. THE INDICATED ANNUAL DIVIDEND IS $2.96. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JUNE 5, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 22, 2023. THIS IS THE COMPANY'S 158TH CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION, AN S&P 500 COMPANY HEADQUARTERED IN DALLAS, IS THE COUNTRY'S LARGEST NATURAL GAS-ONLY DISTRIBUTOR.
