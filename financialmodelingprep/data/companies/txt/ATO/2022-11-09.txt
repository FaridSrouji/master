ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION REPORTS EARNINGS FOR FISCAL 2022; INITIATES FISCAL 2023 GUIDANCE; RAISES DIVIDEND. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ATO) TODAY REPORTED CONSOLIDATED RESULTS FOR ITS FOURTH FISCAL QUARTER AND YEAR ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2022.
ATMOS ENERGY INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ATO) SAID TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND INCREASE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK TO 74.0 CENTS PER SHARE.
