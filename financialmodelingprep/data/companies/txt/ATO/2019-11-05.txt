ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 9, 2019. ATMOS ENERGY CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND INCREASE ON THE COMPANY’S COMMON STOCK TO $0.575 CENTS PER SHARE. THE FISCAL 2020 INDICATED ANNUAL DIVIDEND IS $2.30. THE FISCAL 2019 ANNUAL DIVIDEND WAS $2.10. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON DECEMBER 9, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 25, 2019.
