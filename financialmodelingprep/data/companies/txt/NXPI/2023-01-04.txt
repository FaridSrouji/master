NXP'S NEW I.MX 95 FAMILY OF APPLICATIONS PROCESSORS DELIVERS SAFE, SECURE AND SCALABLE AI-ENABLED EDGE PLATFORMS. LAS VEGAS, JAN. 04, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CES -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS (NASDAQ: NXPI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE I.MX 95 FAMILY, THE NEWEST ADDITION TO ITS I.MX 9 SERIES OF APPLICATIONS PROCESSORS. THE NEW I.MX 95 FAMILY COMBINES HIGH-PERFORMANCE COMPUTE, IMMERSIVE ARM® MALI™-POWERED 3D GRAPHICS, AN INNOVATIVE NEW NXP ACCELERATOR FOR MACHINE LEARNING, AND HIGH-SPEED DATA PROCESSING. TOGETHER, THIS TECHNOLOGY ENABLES ADVANCED APPLICATIONS IN AUTOMOTIVE, INDUSTRIAL, NETWORKING, CONNECTIVITY, ADVANCED HUMAN MACHINE INTERFACE (HMI), AND MORE. ADDITIONALLY, THE I.MX 95 FAMILY DELIVERS HIGH PERFORMANCE SAFETY AND SECURITY FEATURES, DEVELOPED IN COMPLIANCE WITH AUTOMOTIVE ASIL-B AND INDUSTRIAL SIL-2 FUNCTIONAL SAFETY STANDARDS AND INCLUDING AN INTEGRATED EDGELOCK® SECURE ENCLAVE.
