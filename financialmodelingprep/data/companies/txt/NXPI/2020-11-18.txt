NXP SEMICONDUCTORS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. EINDHOVEN, THE NETHERLANDS, NOV. 18, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. (NASDAQ: NXPI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT, AS PART OF ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PROGRAM, ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED THE PAYMENT OF AN INTERIM DIVIDEND FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020 OF $0.375 PER ORDINARY SHARE. THE INTERIM DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID IN CASH ON JANUARY 5, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 15, 2020.
