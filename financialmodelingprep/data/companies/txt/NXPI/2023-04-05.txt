NXP REINFORCES COMMITMENT TO ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE GOALS IN ANNUAL CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. EINDHOVEN, THE NETHERLANDS, APRIL 05, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. (NASDAQ: NXPI) HAS PUBLISHED ITS ANNUAL CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY REPORT (CSR), HIGHLIGHTING ITS PROGRESS, ACCOUNTABILITY AND ONGOING COMMITMENT TO FURTHER SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES. THE REPORT OUTLINES THE COMPANY'S YEAR-OVER-YEAR PROGRESS IN REACHING ITS SHORT AND LONG-TERM ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) GOALS.
