VINFAST AND NXP COLLABORATE ON DEVELOPING THE NEXT GENERATION OF SMART ELECTRIC VEHICLES. LAS VEGAS , JAN. 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AT THIS YEAR'S CES EXPOSITION, VINFAST AND NXP® SEMICONDUCTORS HAVE ANNOUNCED THEIR COLLABORATION ON VINFAST'S NEXT-GENERATION OF AUTOMOTIVE APPLICATIONS. THE COLLABORATION SUPPORTS VINFAST'S GOAL IN DEVELOPING SMARTER, CLEANER AND CONNECTED ELECTRIC VEHICLES.
