NXP SEMICONDUCTORS SHAREHOLDERS APPOINT NEW DIRECTORS. EINDHOVEN, THE NETHERLANDS, MAY 26, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. (NASDAQ: NXPI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS (“AGM”), OVERWHELMINGLY APPROVED THE APPOINTMENT OF ANNETTE CLAYTON AND ANTHONY FOXX, AS NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MS. CLAYTON AND MR. FOXX JOIN THE OTHER TEN MEMBERS OF NXP'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE “BOARD”), ALL OF WHOM WERE ALSO RE-APPOINTED AT THE AGM. TODAY'S AGM ALSO APPROVED ALL OTHER AGENDA ITEMS.
