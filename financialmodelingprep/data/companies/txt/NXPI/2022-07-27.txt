SILICON LABS GROWS REVENUE 55% IN SECOND QUARTER 2022. IOT LEADER ACHIEVES SIGNIFICANT REVENUE GROWTH IN INDUSTRIAL & COMMERCIAL AND HOME & LIFE BUSINESS UNITS AUSTIN, TEXAS , JULY 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SILICON LABS (NASDAQ: SLAB), A LEADER IN SECURE, INTELLIGENT WIRELESS TECHNOLOGY FOR A MORE CONNECTED WORLD, REPORTED ROBUST FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER, WHICH ENDED JULY 2, 2022. REVENUE EXCEEDED THE TOP END OF THE GUIDANCE RANGE AT $263 MILLION, UP 13% SEQUENTIALLY AND 55% YEAR-ON-YEAR.
