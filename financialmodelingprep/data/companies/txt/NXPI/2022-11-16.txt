NXP SEMICONDUCTORS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. EINDHOVEN, THE NETHERLANDS, NOV. 16, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AS PART OF ITS ONGOING CAPITAL RETURN PROGRAM, NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. (NASDAQ: NXPI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED THE PAYMENT OF AN INTERIM DIVIDEND. THE ACTIONS ARE BASED ON THE CONTINUED AND SIGNIFICANT STRENGTH OF THE NXP CAPITAL STRUCTURE, AND THE BOARD'S CONFIDENCE IN THE COMPANY'S ABILITY TO DRIVE LONG-TERM GROWTH AND STRONG CASH FLOW.
