NXP'S I.MX 93 APPLICATIONS PROCESSOR FAMILY POWERS A NEW ERA OF SECURE EDGE INTELLIGENCE. EINDHOVEN, THE NETHERLANDS, NOV. 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS (NASDAQ: NXPI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE I.MX 93 FAMILY OF APPLICATIONS PROCESSORS DESIGNED FOR AUTOMOTIVE, SMART HOME, SMART BUILDING AND SMART FACTORY APPLICATIONS, WHICH LEVERAGE EDGE MACHINE LEARNING TO ANTICIPATE AND AUTOMATE BASED ON USER NEEDS. AS THE FIRST APPLICATIONS PROCESSORS IN NXP'S I.MX 9 SERIES, THE NEW I.MX 93 FAMILY COMBINES THE INDUSTRY'S FIRST IMPLEMENTATION OF THE ARM ETHOS-U65 MICRONPU WITH STATE-OF-THE-ART SECURITY AND A HIGH DEGREE OF INTEGRATION TO DELIVER EFFICIENT, FAST, SECURE MACHINE LEARNING AT THE EDGE. THESE FAMILY ATTRIBUTES ENABLE DEVELOPERS TO ADDRESS DIVERSE AREAS, FROM VOICE-ASSISTED SMART HOME AND BUILDING SYSTEMS, TO LOW-POWER INDUSTRIAL GATEWAYS AND AUTOMOTIVE DRIVER MONITORING SYSTEMS.
