NXP SEMICONDUCTORS NAMES BILL BETZ AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. EINDHOVEN, THE NETHERLANDS, OCT. 12, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. (NASDAQ: NXPI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT BILL BETZ HAS BEEN NAMED NXP'S EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. BETZ, FORMERLY SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, BUSINESS FINANCE FOR NXP, REPLACES PETER KELLY, WHO – AS ANNOUNCED THIS FEBRUARY – HAS DECIDED TO RETIRE FROM NXP. BETZ WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO NXP PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER KURT SIEVERS.
