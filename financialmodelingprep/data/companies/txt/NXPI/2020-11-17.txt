NXP COLLABORATES WITH AMAZON WEB SERVICES (AWS) TO EXTEND CONNECTED VEHICLE OPPORTUNITIES. EINDHOVEN, THE NETHERLANDS, NOV. 17, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS HAS ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC RELATIONSHIP WITH AMAZON WEB SERVICES (AWS) FOCUSED ON EXTENDING THE OPPORTUNITIES OF CONNECTED VEHICLES. THE COLLABORATION AIMS TO DELIVER A SECURE, EDGE-TO-CLOUD COMPUTE SOLUTION FOR NEXT-GENERATION VEHICLES THAT CAN ENABLE NEW CLOUD-POWERED SERVICES TO BENEFIT CARMAKERS, THEIR BUSINESS PARTNERS AND CONSUMERS ALIKE.
