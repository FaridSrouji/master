NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 2019, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 6, 2020. NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. ANNOUNCED THAT, AS PART OF THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PROGRAM, ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED THE PAYMENT OF AN INTERIM DIVIDEND FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2019 OF $0.375 PER ORDINARY SHARE. THE INTERIM DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JANUARY 6, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 16, 2019.
