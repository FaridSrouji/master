ILLUMINA APPOINTS JOYDEEP GOSWAMI CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. SAN DIEGO , FEB. 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ILLUMINA, INC. (NASDAQ: ILMN), A GLOBAL LEADER IN DNA SEQUENCING AND ARRAY-BASED TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED JOYDEEP GOSWAMI HAS BEEN APPOINTED CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. GOSWAMI PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS ILLUMINA'S CHIEF STRATEGY AND CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT OFFICER AND INTERIM CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.
