ILLUMINA SUPPORTS ISRAEL'S NATIONAL PROGRAM TO ACCELERATE DIAGNOSES OF CRITICALLY-ILL NEWBORNS WITH SUSPECTED GENETIC DISEASE. SAN DIEGO, OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ILLUMINA, INC. (NASDAQ: ILMN) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT WITH ISRAEL'S MINISTRY OF HEALTH (MOH) FOR A PILOT PROGRAM TO IMPLEMENT THE USE OF WHOLE-GENOME SEQUENCING (WGS) IN CRITICALLY-ILL INFANTS SUSPECTED OF HAVING A GENETIC DISORDER IN NEONATAL INTENSIVE CARE UNITS (NICU). THE PROGRAM, LED BY THE GENETICS INSTITUTE (TEL-AVIV SOURASKY MEDICAL CENTER), WILL EVALUATE THE USE OF WGS IN ROUTINE CARE AS AN EFFECTIVE FIRST-TIER DIAGNOSTIC TOOL TO ENABLE FASTER IDENTIFICATION OF DISEASE-CAUSING GENETIC ABNORMALITIES IN INFANTS, AIDING THEIR CLINICAL CARE AND MANAGEMENT.
