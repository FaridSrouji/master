GENOMIC LEADERS COLLABORATE TO CREATE AUSTRALIA'S FIRST NATIONAL COVID-19 TRACKING SYSTEM. GENOMIC LEADERS COLLABORATE TO CREATE AUSTRALIA’S FIRST NATIONAL COVID-19 TRACKING SYSTEM.AUSTRALIAN GOVERNMENT HAS PROVIDED A AUD $3.3M MEDICAL RESEARCH FUTURE FUND (MRFF) GRANT THROUGH UNIVERSITY OF NEW SOUTH WALES.ILLUMINA- COMMUNICABLE DISEASE GENOMICS NETWORK AND ILLUMINA ARE COLLABORATING TO TRACK COVID-19 USING NEXT-GENERATION GENOMIC SEQUENCING TECHNOLOGY.
