ILLUMINA ENTERS CO-DEVELOPMENT PARTNERSHIP WITH SOMALOGIC. SAN DIEGO, JAN. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ILLUMINA, INC. (NASDAQ: ILMN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE CO-DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT WITH SOMALOGIC TO BRING THE SOMASCAN® PROTEOMICS ASSAY ONTO ILLUMINA'S CURRENT AND FUTURE HIGH THROUGHPUT NEXT-GENERATION SEQUENCING (NGS) PLATFORMS. THIS PARTNERSHIP ACCELERATES THE RAPIDLY GROWING HIGH THROUGHPUT SECTOR OF THE PROTEOMICS MARKET.
