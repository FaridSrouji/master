ILLUMINA SUPPORTS NORWAY'S PIONEERING PRECISION MEDICINE STUDY. SAN DIEGO, DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ILLUMINA, INC. (NASDAQ:ILMN), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS SUPPORTING A NATIONAL PRECISION MEDICINE STUDY IN NORWAY TO ASSESS THE EFFICACY OF BLOOD-BASED COMPREHENSIVE GENOMIC PROFILING (CGP) IN CANCER THERAPY SELECTION TESTING. THE STUDY WILL ALSO EVALUATE THE COST-EFFECTIVENESS OF IMPLEMENTING BLOOD-BASED CGP IN THE PUBLIC HEALTHCARE SYSTEM.
