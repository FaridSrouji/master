ILLUMINA DONATES $1 MILLION IN SEQUENCING CAPABILITIES TO MUMBAI TO SUPPORT SEQUENCING-BASED COVID-19 SURVEILLANCE IN INDIA. SAN DIEGO, AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ILLUMINA, INC. (NASDAQ: ILMN) HAS DONATED $1 MILLION IN SEQUENCING CAPABILITIES TO THE MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTIC REFERENCE LABORATORY AT KASTURBA HOSPITAL, IN THE MUNICIPAL CORPORATE OF GREATER MUMBAI, TO HELP EXPAND SARS-COV-2 SEQUENCING CAPABILITIES IN THE REGION AND SUPPORT THE BROADER GENOMIC SURVEILLANCE EFFORT ACROSS INDIA. KASTURBA HOSPITAL WAS THE FIRST HOSPITAL IN MUMBAI TO BEGIN SEQUENCING SARS-COV-2 THIS YEAR AND WILL EXAMINE HOW VARIANTS OF THE VIRUS ARE BEING SPREAD ACROSS INDIA.
