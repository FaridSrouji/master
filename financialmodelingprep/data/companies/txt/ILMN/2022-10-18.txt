ILLUMINA AND GENOSCREEN PARTNER TO EXPAND ACCESS TO GENOMIC TESTING FOR MULTIDRUG-RESISTANT TUBERCULOSIS. TRANSFORMATIVE ALLIANCE WILL HELP ADVANCE THE WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION'S STRATEGY TO END THE GLOBAL TB EPIDEMIC BY 2035 SAN DIEGO , OCT. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ILLUMINA INC. (NASDAQ: ILMN), A GLOBAL LEADER IN DNA SEQUENCING AND ARRAY-BASED TECHNOLOGIES, AND GENOSCREEN, AN INNOVATIVE GENOMICS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP TO ACCELERATE PROGRESS TO END TUBERCULOSIS (TB) WORLDWIDE. THE PARTNERSHIP WILL EXPAND CAPABILITIES FOR COUNTRIES MOST IMPACTED BY TUBERCULOSIS TO MORE EFFECTIVELY DETECT AND COMBAT MULTIDRUG-RESISTANT TB (MDR-TB).
