ILLUMINA ANNOUNCES PRELIMINARY RESULTS OF ANNUAL MEETING. ILLUMINA SHAREHOLDERS RE-ELECT EIGHT OF NINE ILLUMINA DIRECTORS SAN DIEGO , MAY 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ILLUMINA (NASDAQ: ILMN), A GLOBAL LEADER IN DNA SEQUENCING AND ARRAY-BASED TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BASED ON THE PRELIMINARY RESULTS OF ITS 2023 ANNUAL MEETING, ILLUMINA SHAREHOLDERS HAVE VOTED TO ELECT EIGHT OF NINE ILLUMINA DIRECTOR NOMINEES – FRANCIS DESOUZA, ROBERT EPSTEIN, MD, FRANCES ARNOLD, PHD, CAROLINE DORSA, SCOTT GOTTLIEB, MD, GARY GUTHART, PHD, PHILIP SCHILLER, AND SUE SIEGEL, AS WELL AS ANDREW TENO, TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS. THE BOARD THANKS JOHN THOMPSON FOR HIS BOARD TENURE AND EXTRAORDINARY LEADERSHIP.
