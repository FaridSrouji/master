ADMINISTRATIVE LAW JUDGE RULES IN FAVOR OF ILLUMINA IN FTC CHALLENGE OF GRAIL DEAL. WASHINGTON , SEPT. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ILLUMINA, INC. (NASDAQ:ILMN), TODAY RECEIVED A FAVORABLE DECISION FROM THE ADMINISTRATIVE LAW JUDGE (ALJ) PRESIDING OVER THE FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION'S (FTC) CHALLENGE OF THE GRAIL ACQUISITION.
