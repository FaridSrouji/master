ILLUMINA APPOINTS BOB RAGUSA AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER (CEO) OF GRAIL. SAN DIEGO, OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ILLUMINA, INC. (NASDAQ:ILMN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BOB RAGUSA HAS BEEN APPOINTED AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF GRAIL, A HEALTHCARE COMPANY WHOSE MISSION IS FOCUSED ON MULTI-CANCER EARLY DETECTION, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. GRAIL CURRENTLY OPERATES AS A SEPARATE AND INDEPENDENT UNIT OF ILLUMINA.
