WESTERN DIGITAL SAYS REORGANIZED ITS MANAGEMENT STRUCTURE. WESTERN DIGITAL CORP - ON APRIL 1, REORGANIZED ITS MANAGEMENT STRUCTURE - SEC FILING.WESTERN DIGITAL CORP - REORGANIZED TO HAVE ALL BUSINESS, OPERATIONS, ENGINEERING, SALES, ADMINISTRATIVE FUNCTIONS REPORT DIRECTLY TO CEO DAVID GOECKELER.WESTERN DIGITAL - AS PART OF ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGE, MICHAEL CORDANO, PRESIDENT & COO OF CO, TRANSITIONING TO NEW ROLE AS PRESIDENT, WESTERN DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES.
