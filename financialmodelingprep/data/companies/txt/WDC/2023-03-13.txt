ETHISPHERE NAMES WESTERN DIGITAL AS ONE OF THE 2023 WORLD'S MOST ETHICAL COMPANIES® FOR THE FIFTH TIME. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTERN DIGITAL CORPORATION (NASDAQ: WDC) HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY ETHISPHERE, A GLOBAL LEADER IN DEFINING AND ADVANCING THE STANDARDS OF ETHICAL BUSINESS PRACTICES, AS ONE OF THE 2023 WORLD'S MOST ETHICAL COMPANIES. WESTERN DIGITAL HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED FIVE TIMES AND IS ONE OF ONLY SIX HONOREES IN THE TECHNOLOGY INDUSTRY. IN 2023, 135 HONOREES WERE RECOGNIZED SPANNING 19 COUNTRIES AND 46 INDUSTRIES.
