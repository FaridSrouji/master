(NASDAQ: WDC) TODAY ANNOUNCED DETAILS OF THEIR NEWEST 3D FLASH MEMORY TECHNOLOGY. APPLYING ADVANCED SCALING AND WAFER BONDING TECHNOLOGIES, THE 3D FLASH MEMORY DELIVERS EXCEPTIONAL CAPACITY, PERFORMANCE AND RELIABILITY AT A COMPELLING COST, WHICH MAKES IT IDEAL FOR MEETING THE NEEDS OF EXPONENTIAL DATA GROWTH ACROSS A BROAD RANGE OF MARKET SEGMENTS.
