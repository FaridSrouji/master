DATADIRECT NETWORKS, INC. COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF INTELLIFLASH BUSINESS OF WESTERN DIGITAL CORPORATION (NASDAQGS:WDC).. DATADIRECT NETWORKS, INC. ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE INTELLIFLASH BUSINESS OF WESTERN DIGITAL CORPORATION (NASDAQGS:WDC) ON SEPTEMBER 19, 2019. FOLLOW THE TRANSACTION, WDC INTELLIFLASH TEAM WILL BE A PART OF DATADIRECT. THE TRANSACTION IS SUBJECT TO SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS AND EXPECTED TO CLOSE LATER THIS CALENDAR YEAR. MERRILL LYNCH, PIERCE, FENNER & SMITH INCORPORATED ACTED AS FINANCIAL ADVISOR TO WESTERN DIGITAL CORPORATION. TAYLOR STEVENS AND  DAVID SIKES OF JONES DAY ACTED AS LEGAL ADVISOR TO WESTERN DIGITAL CORPORATION.
DATADIRECT NETWORKS, INC. COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF INTELLIFLASH BUSINESS OF WESTERN DIGITAL CORPORATION (NASDAQGS:WDC) ON NOVEMBER 20, 2019.
