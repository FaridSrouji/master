BANKERS HELPING BANKERS NAMES PULSE® EXCLUSIVE DEBIT PARTNER. HOUSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--BANKERS HELPING BANKERS, AN ONLINE PLATFORM THAT PROMOTES INNOVATION AMONG COMMUNITY BANKS, HAS NAMED DISCOVER® FINANCIAL SERVICES' PULSE® DEBIT NETWORK ITS EXCLUSIVE DEBIT PARTNER. THE AGREEMENT BUILDS UPON PULSE'S LONGSTANDING SUPPORT OF COMMUNITY BANKS AND THE INDEPENDENT BANKERS ASSOCIATION OF TEXAS (IBAT), WHICH CO-FOUNDED BANKERS HELPING BANKERS IN PARTNERSHIP WITH FEDFIS.
