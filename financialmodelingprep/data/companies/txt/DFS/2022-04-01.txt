DISCOVER, CHICAGO STATE UNIVERSITY PARTNER TO OFFER FULLY FUNDED UNDERGRADUATE DEGREE PROGRAM TO CHICAGO EMPLOYEES. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DISCOVER WILL EXPAND ITS FULLY PAID EDUCATION BENEFIT PROGRAM TO THE CITY OF CHICAGO THROUGH A PARTNERSHIP WITH CHICAGO STATE UNIVERSITY (CSU). BOTH DISCOVER EMPLOYEES LIVING IN CHICAGO WHO ENROLL AT CSU AND CURRENT CSU STUDENTS WHO WORK FULL-TIME AT DISCOVER WILL BE ELIGIBLE TO PARTICIPATE.
