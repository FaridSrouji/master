DISCOVER® IDENTITY THEFT PROTECTION NOW AVAILABLE TO ALL CONSUMERS WHO WANT MORE PROTECTION FOR THEIR PERSONAL INFORMATION. RIVERWOODS, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DISCOVER TODAY ANNOUNCED DISCOVER® IDENTITY THEFT PROTECTION, A FEE-BASED IDENTITY THEFT PROTECTION PRODUCT THAT PROVIDES INDIVIDUALS WITH CREDIT AND IDENTITY MONITORING, ALERTS AND FRAUD RESOLUTION SERVICES, IS AVAILABLE TO ALL U.S. CONSUMERS. THIS EXPANSION COMES AS IDENTITY THEFT REPORTS MORE THAN DOUBLED NATIONWIDE FROM 2019 TO 2020, ACCORDING TO THE CONSUMER WATCHDOG FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION1.
