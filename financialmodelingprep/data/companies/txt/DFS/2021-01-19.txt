DISCOVER FINANCIAL SERVICES BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVES REPURCHASE OF UP TO $1.1 BILLION OF COMMON STOCK, DECLARES COMMON AND PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDENDS. RIVERWOODS, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DISCOVER FINANCIAL SERVICES (NYSE: DFS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A NEW $1.1 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM, HAVING SUSPENDED SHARE REPURCHASES SINCE MARCH 2020 IN RESPONSE TO THE ECONOMIC RECESSION. THE NEW PROGRAM EXPIRES ON DECEMBER 31, 2021, AND CAN BE TERMINATED AT ANY TIME.
