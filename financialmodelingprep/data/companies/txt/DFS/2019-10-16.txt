DISCOVER FINANCIAL SERVICES DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 5, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DISCOVER FINANCIAL SERVICES DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.44 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 5, 2019, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 21, 2019.
