HOPE MEHLMAN JOINS DISCOVER AS CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER AND GENERAL COUNSEL. RIVERWOODS, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--HOPE MEHLMAN, AN ACCOMPLISHED LEADER, BUSINESS EXECUTIVE AND LEGAL EXPERT WITH OVER 20 YEARS OF LEGAL, COMPLIANCE, REGULATORY, AND RISK MANAGEMENT EXPERIENCE IN THE FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY AND IN PRIVATE PRACTICE, WILL BE JOINING DISCOVER AS CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER AND GENERAL COUNSEL AND A MEMBER OF THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE.
