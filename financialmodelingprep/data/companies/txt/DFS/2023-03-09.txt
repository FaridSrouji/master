JASON HANSON TO SUCCEED DIANE OFFEREINS AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PRESIDENT OF PAYMENT SERVICES FOR DISCOVER FINANCIAL SERVICES. RIVERWOODS, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DISCOVER FINANCIAL SERVICES (NYSE: DFS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JASON HANSON WILL SUCCEED DIANE OFFEREINS AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PRESIDENT OF PAYMENT SERVICES. HANSON WILL OVERSEE DISCOVER'S PAYMENT SERVICES, WHICH ENCOMPASSES THE DISCOVER GLOBAL NETWORK, PULSE, AND DINER'S CLUB INTERNATIONAL. OFFEREINS PLANS TO RETIRE AFTER MORE THAN 25 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY EFFECTIVE JUNE 30.
