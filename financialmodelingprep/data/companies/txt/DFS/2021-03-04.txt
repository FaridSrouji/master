RIVERWOODS, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DISCOVER TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO OPEN A NEW CHICAGO-BASED CUSTOMER CARE CENTER IN CHICAGO'S CHATHAM NEIGHBORHOOD. LOCATED AT 86TH STREET AND COTTAGE GROVE, THE CENTER, ONCE FULLY OPERATIONAL, WILL PROVIDE NEARLY 1,000 FULL-TIME JOBS TO NEIGHBORHOOD AND AREA RESIDENTS.
