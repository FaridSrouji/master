LABCORP NOW OFFERS OBSERVED COLLECTION FOR COVID-19 PCR TESTING IN OVER 500 LOCATIONS NATIONWIDE, WITH PLANS TO EXPAND TO MORE THAN 1,000 LOCATIONS. BURLINGTON, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LABCORP (NYSE: LH), A GLOBAL LIFE SCIENCES COMPANY, IS NOW OFFERING OBSERVED SELF-COLLECTION FOR COVID-19 PCR TESTING IN MORE THAN 500 OF ITS PATIENT SERVICE CENTERS (PSCS) NATIONWIDE FOR PEOPLE WITHOUT SYMPTOMS OR KNOWN EXPOSURE. BY MID-DECEMBER, THE COMPANY PLANS TO FURTHER EXPAND THE COVID-19 PCR TEST COLLECTION SERVICE TO OVER 1,000 LOCATIONS.
