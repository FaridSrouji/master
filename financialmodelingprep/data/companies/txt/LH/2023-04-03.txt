LABCORP EXPANDS ACCESS TO COLORECTAL CANCER SCREENING THROUGH PARTNERSHIP WITH THE CONFERENCE OF NATIONAL BLACK CHURCHES. PARTNERSHIP WILL DISTRIBUTE SCREENING KITS TO COMMUNITIES AT HIGHER RISK OF COLON CANCER BURLINGTON, N.C. , APRIL 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LABCORP (NYSE: LH), A LEADING GLOBAL LIFE SCIENCES COMPANY, IS PARTNERING WITH THE CONFERENCE OF NATIONAL BLACK CHURCHES (CNBC) TO ADDRESS HEALTH EQUITY GAPS IN COLORECTAL CANCER SCREENING.
