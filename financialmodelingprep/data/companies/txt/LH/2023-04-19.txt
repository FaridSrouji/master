DYNACARE ACQUIRES VALLEY MEDICAL LABORATORIES. BRAMPTON, ONT., APRIL 19, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DYNACARE, A LABCORP SUBSIDIARY AND ONE OF CANADA'S LEADING HEALTH AND WELLNESS SOLUTIONS PROVIDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE VALLEY MEDICAL LABORATORIES IN BRITISH COLUMBIA EFFECTIVE MAY 1, 2023.
