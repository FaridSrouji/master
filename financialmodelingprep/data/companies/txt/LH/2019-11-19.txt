QIAGEN AND LABCORP DEEPEN RELATIONSHIP WITH WIDESPREAD USE OF BIOINFORMATICS SOLUTIONS. QIAGEN N.V. ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT WITH LABCORP TO EXTEND THEIR CURRENT QIAGEN CLINICAL INSIGHTS (QCI) LICENSE WITH QIAGEN’S HUMAN GENE MUTATION DATABASE (HGMD) OF HUMAN GENETIC VARIANTS. THIS AGREEMENT BUILDS ON THE EXISTING SEVEN-YEAR RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE TWO COMPANIES TO DEVELOP, INTRODUCE AND SUPPORT NEW DIAGNOSTIC TESTS. LABCORP WILL USE HGMD ACROSS ITS NETWORK OF LABORATORIES TO IMPROVE IDENTIFICATION AND INTERPRETATION OF GENETIC VARIANTS WITHIN INHERITED DISEASES.
