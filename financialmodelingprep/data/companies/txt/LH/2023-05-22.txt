PROTEOMEDIX AGREES TO ISSUE LABCORP EXCLUSIVE LICENSE FOR PROCLARIX® BLOOD TEST TO DETECT PROSTATE CANCER. ZURICH-SCHLIEREN, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PROTEOMEDIX, A SWISS DIAGNOSTICS COMPANY COMMITTED TO ADVANCING PROSTATE CANCER CARE, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT FOR LABCORP, A LEADING GLOBAL LIFE SCIENCES COMPANY, TO BE THE ONLY LABORATORY TO DEVELOP AND COMMERCIALIZE THE PROCLARIX® PROSTATE SPECIFIC ANTIGEN (PSA) TEST IN THE U.S.. PROCLARIX IS PERFORMED USING THE SAME BLOOD SAMPLE AS A PSA TEST, AND IS DESIGNED TO HELP DETERMINE THE RISK OF CLINICALLY SIGNIFICANT PROSTAT.
