LABCORP TO EXPAND BIOANALYTICAL SERVICES WITH INTEGRATED LABORATORY IN SINGAPORE. BURLINGTON, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LABCORP (NYSE: LH), A LEADING GLOBAL LIFE SCIENCES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF ITS DRUG DEVELOPMENT OFFERING IN THE ASIA-PACIFIC REGION WITH THE ADDITION OF BIOANALYTICAL SERVICES IN SINGAPORE. “SINGAPORE IS A CRUCIAL REGION FOR STRENGTHENING LABCORP'S EXTENSIVE GLOBAL PRESENCE, OFFERING OUR CLIENTS—BOTH IN-REGION AND INTERNATIONAL—A COMPLEMENTARY SITE TO SUPPORT THEIR BIOANALYTICAL STUDIES,” SAID STEPHANE SIDOBRE, VICE PRESIDENT AND HEAD OF LABC
