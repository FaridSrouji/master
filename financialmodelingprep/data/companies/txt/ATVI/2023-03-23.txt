ACTIVISION BLIZZARD RELEASES INAUGURAL DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION LOOK-BACK. SANTA MONICA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ACTIVISION BLIZZARD TODAY RELEASED ITS FIRST ANNUAL DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION (DE&I) LOOK-BACK, WHICH SHARES NEW AND EXPANDED DATA ON DIVERSE REPRESENTATION WITHIN THE COMPANY'S EMPLOYEE POPULATION. THE LOOK-BACK ALSO HIGHLIGHTS HOW INVESTING IN DE&I ACROSS ITS PEOPLE, BUSINESS, AND COMMUNITY HAS BECOME AN INTEGRAL PART OF ACTIVISION BLIZZARD'S BUSINESS.
