BLIZZARD ENTERTAINMENT AND NETEASE SUSPENDING GAME SERVICES IN CHINA. IRVINE, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--BLIZZARD ENTERTAINMENT, INC. ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL BE SUSPENDING MOST BLIZZARD GAME SERVICES IN MAINLAND CHINA DUE TO THE EXPIRATION OF THE CURRENT LICENSING AGREEMENTS WITH NETEASE, INC. ON JANUARY 23, 2023. THIS INCLUDES WORLD OF WARCRAFT ®, HEARTHSTONE ®, WARCRAFT ® III: REFORGED, OVERWATCH ®, THE STARCRAFT ® SERIES, DIABLO III ®, AND HEROES OF THE STORM ®. DIABLO IMMORTAL ® CO-DEVELOPMENT AND PUBLISHING IS COVERED UNDER A SEPARATE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE TWO COMPANIES.
