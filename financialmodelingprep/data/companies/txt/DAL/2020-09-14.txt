DELTA AIR LINES ANNOUNCES $6.5 BLN SENIOR SECURED NOTES OFFERING & TERM LOAN FACILITY. DELTA AIR LINES ANNOUNCES PROPOSED SENIOR SECURED NOTES OFFERING AND TERM LOAN FACILITY BY DELTA AND SKYMILES SUBSIDIARY.DELTA AIR LINES - CO, SKYMILES IP LTD WILL BE CO-ISSUERS OF NOTES AND CO-BORROWERS UNDER NEW CREDIT FACILITY.DELTA AIR LINES - AMOUNT OF NOTES AND NEW CREDIT FACILITY IS EXPECTED TO BE $6.5 BILLION.DELTA AIR LINES - SKYMILES IP LTD TO LEND NET PROCEEDS FROM NOTES OFFERING, CREDIT FACILITY TO DELTA, AFTER DEPOSITING PORTION IN RESERVE ACCOUNT.DELTA AIR LINES - NOTES AND NEW CREDIT FACILITY WILL BE GUARANTEED BY CERTAIN OF DELTA'S SUBSIDIARIES.
