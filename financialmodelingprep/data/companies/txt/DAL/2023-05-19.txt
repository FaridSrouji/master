AEG AND DELTA AIR LINES GROW MORE THAN DECADE-LONG PARTNERSHIP ACROSS MARQUEE SPORTS AND LIVE ENTERTAINMENT ASSETS WITH EXPANDED NEW RENEWAL AGREEMENT. LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DELTA AIR LINES AND AEG ANNOUNCE EXPANDED PARTNERSHIP RENEWAL TO ENHANCE AIRLINE'S PRESENCE WITH SOME OF AEG'S SPORTS AND LIVE ENTERTAINMENT ASSETS.
