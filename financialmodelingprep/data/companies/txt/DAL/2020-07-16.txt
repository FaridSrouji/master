INDIA CIVIL AVIATION MINISTER SAYS MAY NEED SHORT EXTENSION OF AIRFARE CAP ON DOMESTIC ROUTES. INDIA CIVIL AVIATION MINISTER SAYS IN ADVANCED STAGE OF NEGOTIATION WITH AT LEAST 3 COUNTRIES FOR BILATERAL AIR BUBBLES.INDIA CIVIL AVIATION MINISTER SAYS AIR FRANCE WILL BE FLYING 28 FLIGHTS BETWEEN DELHI-MUMBAI-BENGALURU-PARIS BETWEEN JULY 18 TO AUG. 1.INDIA CIVIL AVIATION MINISTER SAYS U.S. AIRLINE DELTA WILL BE FLYING 18 FLIGHTS TO INDIA BETWEEN JULY 17 AND JULY 31 .AIR INDIA EXEC SAYS WORKING TO CONTAIN COSTS BY REDUCING DEBT, AIRCRAFT LEASE RENTALS, STAFFING COST AND OPERATING COST.AIR INDIA EXEC SAYS IN TALKS WITH LESSORS TO REDUCE AIRCRAFT RENTAL COSTS.AIR INDIA EXEC SAYS HAVE LAID OFF EMPLOYEES WORKING WITH THE AIRLINE POST RETIREMENT.INDIA CIVIL AVIATION MINISTER SAYS DONT HAVE A CHOICE BUT TO PERSIST WITH PRIVATISATION OF AIR INDIA.INDIA CIVIL AVIATION MINISTER SAYS MAY NEED A SHORT EXTENSION OF THE AIRFARE CAP ON DOMESTIC ROUTES.INDIA CIVIL AVIATION MINISTER SAYS EXPECT DOMESTIC OPERATIONS TO INCREASE TO 55% BY DIWALI THIS YEAR.
