HYDROGENPRO'S PARTNER, DG FUELS, SIGNS OFFTAKE AGREEMENT WITH DELTA AIR LINES. PORSGRUNN, NORWAY , SEPT. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REFERENCE IS MADE TO RECENT PRESS RELEASE FROM DELTA AIR LINES ("DELTA") REGARDING AN OFFTAKE AGREEMENT BETWEEN DELTA AND DG FUELS WHERE DG FUELS WILL SUPPLY DELTA WITH 385 MILLION GALLONS OF SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL, STARTING IN 2026.
