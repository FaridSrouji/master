CAPRI SUN® CUTS SUGAR BY AN AVERAGE OF 40 PERCENT ACROSS ITS ENTIRE ORIGINAL JUICE DRINK PORTFOLIO, USING MONK FRUIT CONCENTRATE TO MAINTAIN ICONIC TASTE KIDS LOVE. NEW RECIPE REDUCES TOTAL SUGARS TO AN AVERAGE OF 8G AND ADDED SUGARS TO 5G PER SERVING CHICAGO , JULY 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPRI SUN, THE ICONIC BRAND KIDS AND FAMILIES HAVE LOVED FOR GENERATIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE ROLL-OUT OF ITS ORIGINAL JUICE DRINK POUCHES WITH AN AVERAGE OF 40 PERCENT LESS SUGAR THAN THE CURRENT PRODUCT. EACH SINGLE SERVING WILL NOW HAVE ON AVERAGE, 8G OF TOTAL SUGARS AND 5G OF ADDED SUGARS.
