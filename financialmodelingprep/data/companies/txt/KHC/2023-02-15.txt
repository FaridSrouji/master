PITTSBURGH & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE KRAFT HEINZ COMPANY (NASDAQ: KHC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.40 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON MARCH 31, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MARCH 10, 2023.
