SIMPLOT COMPANY (“SIMPLOT”), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE TWO COMPANIES HAVE SIGNED A LONG-TERM AGREEMENT NAMING SIMPLOT AS THE EXCLUSIVE MANUFACTURER AND SUPPLIER OF ORE-IDA.
