KRAFT HEINZ ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP TEAM TRANSITIONS. PITTSBURGH & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE KRAFT HEINZ COMPANY (NASDAQ: KHC) (“KRAFT HEINZ” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE APPOINTMENT OF ANDRE MACIEL AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND GLOBAL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE MARCH 2, 2022. MACIEL WILL SUCCEED PAULO BASILIO, WHO WILL STEP DOWN ON MARCH 1, 2022, AS PART OF A PLANNED TRANSITION AND WILL REMAIN WITH THE COMPANY AS A STRATEGIC ADVISOR THROUGH AUGUST 2022.
