KATHY KRENGER TO JOIN KRAFT HEINZ AS CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER; MICHAEL MULLEN TO RETIRE. PITTSBURGH & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE KRAFT HEINZ COMPANY (NASDAQ: KHC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KATHY KRENGER IS JOINING THE COMPANY AS ITS NEW CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER. CURRENTLY SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL COMMUNICATIONS, FOR HYATT HOTELS CORPORATION, SHE WILL BEGIN HER NEW ROLE ON JULY 21, REPORTING DIRECTLY TO CEO MIGUEL PATRICIO.
