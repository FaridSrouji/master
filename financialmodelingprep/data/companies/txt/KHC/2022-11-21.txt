JANELLE OROZCO JOINS KRAFT HEINZ AS NORTH AMERICA ZONE CHIEF PROCUREMENT OFFICER. PITTSBURGH & CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE KRAFT HEINZ COMPANY (NASDAQ: KHC) (“KRAFT HEINZ”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE APPOINTMENT OF JANELLE OROZCO TO THE ROLE OF CHIEF PROCUREMENT OFFICER, NORTH AMERICA. IN THIS ROLE, OROZCO WILL SHAPE THE OVERALL VISION OF NORTH AMERICA PROCUREMENT AND DIRECT ITS CONTINUED TRANSFORMATION TO BECOME A BEST-IN-CLASS PROCUREMENT ORGANIZATION.
