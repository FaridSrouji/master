DAVITA'S NORTH AMERICAN OPERATIONS NOW POWERED BY 100% RENEWABLE ENERGY. DENVER, DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DAVITA INC. BECAME THE FIRST KIDNEY CARE PROVIDER TO COMPLETELY POWER ITS NORTH AMERICAN OPERATIONS WITH RENEWABLE ENERGY. DAVITA ACHIEVED THIS GOAL, WHICH IT SET IN 2019, BY CONTRACTING WITH LONGROAD ENERGY ON TWO VIRTUAL POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENTS (PPAS) TO CONSTRUCT A WIND FARM AND A SOLAR FARM IN TEXAS.
