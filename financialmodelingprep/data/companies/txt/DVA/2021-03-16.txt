NEPHROSANT ANNOUNCES $16 MILLION SERIES A, LED BY DAVITA VENTURE GROUP. SAN FRANCISCO, CA / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 16, 2021 / NEPHROSANT, A KIDNEY HEALTH SPIN OUT OF UC SAN FRANCISCO, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS CLOSED ITS OVERSUBSCRIBED SERIES A FUNDING ROUND OF $16 MILLION. DAVITA VENTURE GROUP LED THE INVESTMENT ROUND, WITH PARTICIPATION FROM DIGITALDX VENTURES AND OTHER PREVIOUS INVESTORS.
