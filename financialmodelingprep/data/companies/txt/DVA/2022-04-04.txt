NEW RESEARCH AFFIRMS INCREASE IN ACCESS, EQUITY OF COVID-19 VACCINES ADMINISTERED IN DIALYSIS CENTERS. RESULTS DEMONSTRATE THE IMPORTANCE OF VACCINATING PATIENTS AT TRUSTED SITES OF CARE DENVER AND WALTHAM, MASS. , APRIL 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A FEDERAL PARTNERSHIP ESTABLISHED TO HELP INCREASE ACCESS TO COVID-19 VACCINES FOR END STAGE KIDNEY DISEASE (ESKD) PATIENTS RECEIVING DIALYSIS WAS SUCCESSFUL IN REDUCING RACIAL DISPARITIES IN VACCINATION COVERAGE, NEW RESEARCH PUBLISHED TODAY IN JAMA INTERNAL MEDICINE AFFIRMS.
