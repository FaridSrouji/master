AMERICAN DIABETES ASSOCIATION FOCUSES ON PREVENTION OF KIDNEY DISEASE THROUGH COLLABORATION WITH DAVITA. AMERICAN DIABETES ASSOCIATION AND DAVITA LAUNCH A DIGITAL EXPERIENCE WITH RESOURCES FOR PREVENTING AND MANAGING DIABETES-RELATED KIDNEY COMPLICATIONS ARLINGTON, VA. AND DENVER , JULY 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE AMERICAN DIABETES ASSOCIATION® (ADA), ALONG WITH DAVITA, ARE EXCITED TO ANNOUNCE THE LAUNCH OF AN INTERACTIVE DIGITAL EXPERIENCE AIMED AT HELPING THOSE LIVING WITH DIABETES PREVENT AND MANAGE KIDNEY DISEASE.
