EXTRAORDINARY NURSES AT DAVITA KIDNEY CARE HONORED WITH DAISY AWARDS. DENVER, MAY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  DAVITA KIDNEY CARE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT 60 OF ITS NEPHROLOGY NURSES WERE HONORED WITH THE DAISY AWARD FOR EXTRAORDINARY NURSES® DURING NATIONAL NURSES WEEK. THE AWARD IS PART OF THE DAISY FOUNDATION'S MISSION TO RECOGNIZE THE EXTRAORDINARY, COMPASSIONATE CARE THAT NURSES PROVIDE TO PATIENTS AND FAMILIES EVERY DAY.
