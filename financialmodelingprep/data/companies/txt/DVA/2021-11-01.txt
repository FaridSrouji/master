THE AMERICAN DIABETES ASSOCIATION® AND DAVITA RAISE AWARENESS ABOUT THE LINK BETWEEN DIABETES AND KIDNEY DISEASE DURING AMERICAN DIABETES MONTH®. DENVER, NOV. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN HONOR OF AMERICAN DIABETES MONTH® (ADM), THE AMERICAN DIABETES ASSOCIATION® (ADA) AND DAVITA ARE COMING TOGETHER TO EDUCATE AMERICANS ABOUT THE CONNECTION BETWEEN DIABETES AND KIDNEY DISEASE. THROUGHOUT THE MONTH OF NOVEMBER, BOTH ORGANIZATIONS WILL BE SHARING RESOURCES TO INFORM PEOPLE ABOUT TAKING STEPS TO HELP PREVENT OR SLOW THESE CHRONIC CONDITIONS.
