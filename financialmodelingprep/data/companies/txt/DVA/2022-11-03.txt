DAVITA CLINICAL RESEARCH SHOWCASES CHRONIC KIDNEY DISEASE AND RARE DISEASE NETWORK, PRESENTS NEW RESEARCH FINDINGS AT KIDNEY WEEK 2022. THIS YEAR'S RESEARCH CENTERED ON ADVANCEMENTS IN KIDNEY TRANSPLANTATION AND EQUITY IN PATIENT CARE DAVITA ADDRESSES EQUITABLE ACCESS TO BREAKTHROUGH TRIALS AND DECENTRALIZED RESEARCH BY INTRODUCING NEW CHRONIC KIDNEY DISEASE & RARE DISEASE NETWORK DENVER , NOV. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DAVITA CLINICAL RESEARCH (DCR) WILL HIGHLIGHT ITS LATEST RESEARCH DURING THE AMERICAN SOCIETY OF NEPHROLOGY (ASN) KIDNEY WEEK, BEING HELD IN ORLANDO FROM NOVEMBER 2-6, 2022. "WE LOOK FORWARD TO KIDNEY WEEK AS A CRUCIAL INFLECTION POINT TO SOCIALIZE NEW DISCOVERIES AND ADVANCEMENTS THAT ARE CHANGING THE LIVES OF PATIENTS," SAID DR. STEVEN BRUNELLI, VICE PRESIDENT OF HEALTH ECONOMICS AND OUTCOMES RESEARCH FOR DAVITA.
