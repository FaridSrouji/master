DAVITA INTEGRATED KIDNEY CARE EXPANDS CLINICAL LEADERSHIP TEAM. DENVER, DEC. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DAVITA INTEGRATED KIDNEY CARE (IKC) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS NEW CLINICAL LEADERSHIP TEAM, CHARGED WITH REACHING MORE PATIENTS THROUGH IKC PROGRAMS AND FURTHER INNOVATING CARE DELIVERY ACROSS THE KIDNEY CARE CONTINUUM. THIS TEAM OF EXPERIENCED NEPHROLOGISTS IS LED BY DR. DAVID ROER, DAVITA IKC'S NEWLY APPOINTED CHIEF MEDICAL OFFICER (CMO).
