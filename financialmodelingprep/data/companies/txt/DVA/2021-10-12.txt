DAVITA CLIMATE ACTION GOALS APPROVED BY THE SCIENCE BASED TARGET INITIATIVE. DENVER, OCT. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DAVITA FURTHERS COMMITMENT TO CLIMATE ACTION GOALS WITH VALIDATION FROM THE SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE (SBTI). THE ORGANIZATION HAS APPROVED TWO GOALS: SUPPLY CHAIN PARTNERS REPRESENTING 70% OF EMISSIONS WILL SET CLIMATE CHANGE GOALS BY 2025 REDUCE ITS CARBON EMISSIONS BY 50% BY 2025 "OUR ENVIRONMENTAL GOALS PROACTIVELY ADDRESS OUR CONTRIBUTION TO CLIMATE CHANGE," SAID MISHA PALECEK, CHIEF TRANSFORMATION OFFICER FOR DAVITA.
