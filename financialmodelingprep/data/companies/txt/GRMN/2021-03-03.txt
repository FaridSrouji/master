GARMIN AUTOLAND NAMED 2020 FINALIST FOR ESTEEMED ROBERT J. COLLIER TROPHY. OLATHE, KAN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GARMIN® INTERNATIONAL, INC., A UNIT OF GARMIN LTD. (NASDAQ: GRMN), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS GROUND-BREAKING GARMIN AUTOLAND SYSTEM WAS SELECTED AS A 2020 ROBERT J. COLLIER TROPHY FINALIST BY THE NATIONAL AERONAUTIC ASSOCIATION (NAA). FOR OVER A CENTURY, THE COLLIER TROPHY HAS BEEN THE BENCHMARK OF AEROSPACE ACHIEVEMENT AND IS AWARDED ANNUALLY TO RECOGNIZE “THE GREATEST ACHIEVEMENT IN AERONAUTICS OR ASTRONAUTICS IN AMERICA.” PART OF THE GARMIN AUTONOMI™ FAMILY OF A
