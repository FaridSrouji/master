GARMIN RECEIVES 2022 BMW GROUP SUPPLIER INNOVATION AWARD. AUTOMOTIVE OEM TEAM RECOGNIZED FOR CONTRIBUTION TO NEW BMW THEATRE SCREEN FEATURED ON THE BMW 7 SERIES LAUNCHED THIS YEAR OLATHE, KAN. , NOV. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GARMIN (NYSE: GRMN) IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE THAT ITS AUTOMOTIVE OEM GROUP HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY BMW GROUP WITH A COVETED 2022 SUPPLIER INNOVATION AWARD IN THE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE CATEGORY.
