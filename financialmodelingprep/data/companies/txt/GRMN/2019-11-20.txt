GARMIN LTD. APPROVES AMENDMENT TO ARTICLE 14.3 OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL REGULATIONS. ON OCTOBER 25, 2019, GARMIN LTD.’S BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED AN AMENDMENT TO ARTICLE 14.3 OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL REGULATIONS OF THE COMPANY TO PERMIT WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS TO BE ADOPTED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS BY ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE. THE FOREGOING DESCRIPTION OF THE AMENDMENT TO THE ORGANIZATIONAL REGULATIONS IS QUALIFIED IN ITS ENTIRETY BY REFERENCE TO THE AMENDED AND RESTATED ORGANIZATIONAL REGULATIONS.
