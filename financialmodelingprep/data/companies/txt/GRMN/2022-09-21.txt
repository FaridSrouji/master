GARMIN, THE WORLD'S MOST RECOGNIZED MARINE ELECTRONICS MANUFACTURER, JOINS FORZA X1 TO CREATE ENHANCED USER EXPERIENCE FOR FX1 ALL-ELECTRIC BOAT FORT PIERCE, FL / ACCESSWIRE / SEPTEMBER 21, 2022 / FORZA X1, INC. (NASDAQ:FRZA) ("FORZA", THE "COMPANY"), A NEW DEVELOPER OF ELECTRIC SPORT BOATS WITH A MISSION TO INSPIRE THE ADOPTION OF SUSTAINABLE RECREATIONAL BOATING, TODAY ANNOUNCED DETAILS FROM ITS COLLABORATION WITH GARMIN® INTERNATIONAL ("GARMIN") TO BRING INTUITIVE AND INTERACTIVE TECHNOLOGY TO THE COMPANY'S FIRST FULLY ELECTRIC BOAT MODEL, THE FX1. OVER THE PAST SEVERAL MONTHS, FORZA AND GARMIN HAVE WORKED TOGETHER TO DEVELOP A CUSTOMIZED SYSTEM DRIVEN BY SELECT GARMIN PRODUCTS TO PRIORITIZE EASE OF USE AND CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE.
