GARMIN NAMED AN OFFICIAL SPONSOR OF THE PROFESSIONAL TRIATHLETES ORGANISATION FOR 2023. GLOBAL SPONSORSHIP IS COMPRISED OF INTERNATIONAL TRIATHLON EVENTS AS PART OF THE PTO TOUR OLATHE, KAN. , MAY 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GARMIN (NYSE: GRMN) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO BECOME AN OFFICIAL SPONSOR OF PROFESSIONAL TRIATHLETES ORGANISATION AND PTO'S 2023 GLOBAL TOUR OF EVENTS, WHICH ARE SET TO TAKE PLACE ACROSS THE GLOBE STARRING THE WORLD'S BEST TRIATHLETES.
