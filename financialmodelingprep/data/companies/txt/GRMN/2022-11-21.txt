PALOMINO RV SELECTS GARMIN ONE SOLUTION TO OUTFIT PAUSE TRAILERS. STATE-OF-THE-ART CAMPING TRAILER WILL BE FULLY INTEGRATED WITH GARMIN TECHNOLOGY INCLUDING NAVIGATION, ENTERTAINMENT AND DIGITAL SWITCHING OLATHE, KAN. , NOV. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GARMIN (NYSE: GRMN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PALOMINO RV HAS SELECTED THE GARMIN ONE (OPERATION, NAVIGATION, ENTERTAINMENT) SOLUTION TO POWER ITS OFF-ROAD READY PAUSE LINE OF TRAVEL TRAILERS.
