GARMIN ACQUIRES VESPER MARINE, A LEADING PROVIDER OF MARINE COMMUNICATION EQUIPMENT AND SERVICES. SCHAFFHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GARMIN® LTD. (NYSE:GRMN), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ACQUIRED VESPER MARINE, A PRIVATELY-HELD PROVIDER OF AIS, VHF AND VESSEL MONITORING SOLUTIONS FOR THE MARINE INDUSTRY. THIS ACQUISITION IS EXPECTED TO BOLSTER GARMIN'S INDUSTRY-LEADING LINEUP OF MARINE PRODUCTS AND TECHNOLOGY DESIGNED TO PROVIDE PEACE OF MIND AND ENHANCED SITUATIONAL AWARENESS FOR BOATERS.
