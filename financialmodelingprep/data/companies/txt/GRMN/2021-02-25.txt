GARMIN AUTOLAND HONORED WITH PRESTIGIOUS FLYING MAGAZINE EDITORS' CHOICE AWARD. OLATHE, KAN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GARMIN® INTERNATIONAL, INC., A UNIT OF GARMIN LTD. (NASDAQ: GRMN), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS REVOLUTIONARY GARMIN AUTOLAND SYSTEM WAS HONORED BY FLYING MAGAZINE WITH A 2021 EDITORS' CHOICE AWARD. PART OF THE GARMIN AUTONOMI™ FAMILY OF AUTONOMOUS SAFETY-ENHANCING TECHNOLOGIES, AUTOLAND IS THE WORLD'S FIRST CERTIFIED SYSTEM OF ITS KIND WITH THE ABILITY TO ACTIVATE DURING AN EMERGENCY SITUATION TO AUTONOMOUSLY CONTROL AND LAND AN AIRCRAFT WITHOUT HUMAN INTERVENTION1.
