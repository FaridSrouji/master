AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION PRICES SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION (NYSE: AMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS REGISTERED PUBLIC OFFERING OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2028 AND 2033 IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNTS OF $650.0 MILLION AND $850.0 MILLION, RESPECTIVELY. THE 2028 NOTES WILL HAVE AN INTEREST RATE OF 5.250% PER ANNUM AND ARE BEING ISSUED AT A PRICE EQUAL TO 99.712% OF THEIR FACE VALUE.
