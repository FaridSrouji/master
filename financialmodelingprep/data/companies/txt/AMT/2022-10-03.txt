AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION ANNOUNCES MANAGEMENT CHANGES. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION (NYSE: AMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED CHANGES TO ITS SENIOR MANAGEMENT TEAM. ED DISANTO INTENDS TO STEP DOWN FROM HIS POSITION AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER, GENERAL COUNSEL AND SECRETARY, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2023. RUTH DOWLING, WHO HAS BEEN WITH AMERICAN TOWER FOR OVER TEN YEARS, WILL SUCCEED MR. DISANTO IN THE ROLE OF EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER, GENERAL COUNSEL AND SECRETARY, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1
