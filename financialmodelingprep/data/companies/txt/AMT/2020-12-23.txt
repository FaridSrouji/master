AMERICAN TOWER CLOSES INSITE WIRELESS GROUP ACQUISITION. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION (NYSE: AMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS CLOSED ITS ACQUISITION OF INSITE WIRELESS GROUP, LLC (“INSITE”). THE TOTAL CONSIDERATION FOR THE TRANSACTION, SUBJECT TO CERTAIN POST-CLOSING ADJUSTMENTS, WAS APPROXIMATELY $3.5 BILLION, INCLUDING THE ASSUMPTION OR REPAYMENT OF EXISTING INSITE DEBT, AND WAS FUNDED BY A COMBINATION OF CASH ON HAND AND REVOLVER BORROWINGS.
