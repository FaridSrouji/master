AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION ANNOUNCES COMPLETION OF REDEMPTION OF ALL OUTSTANDING 4.70% SENIOR NOTES. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION (NYSE: AMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED REDEMPTION OF ALL OF ITS OUTSTANDING 4.70% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2022. THE COMPANY REDEEMED THE NOTES PURSUANT TO THEIR TERMS AT 101.7270% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT, PLUS ACCRUED AND UNPAID INTEREST UP TO, BUT EXCLUDING, OCTOBER 18, 2021.
