SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISSLAW LLP INVESTIGATES CORESITE REALTY CORPORATION. NEW YORK, NOV. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISSLAW LLP IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CORESITE REALTY CORPORATION ("CORESITE" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: COR) IN CONNECTION WITH THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF THE COMPANY BY AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION ("AMERICAN TOWER") (NYSE: AMT) VIA A TENDER OFFER.  UNDER THE TERMS OF THE ACQUISITION AGREEMENT, THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $170.00 PER SHARE IN CASH FOR EACH SHARE OF CORESITE COMMON STOCK THAT THEY HOLD.
