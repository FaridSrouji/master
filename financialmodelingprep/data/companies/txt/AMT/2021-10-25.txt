AMERICAN TOWER ADOPTS SCIENCE-BASED TARGETS. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION (NYSE: AMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS ADOPTION OF SCIENCE-BASED TARGETS (SBTS) ACROSS ITS GLOBAL OPERATIONS AND SUPPLY CHAIN, WHICH HAVE BEEN APPROVED BY THE SCIENCE BASED TARGETS INITIATIVE AND ARE IN LINE WITH THE GOALS SET FORTH IN THE 2015 PARIS AGREEMENT.
