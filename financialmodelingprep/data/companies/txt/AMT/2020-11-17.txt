AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION PRICES SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION (NYSE: AMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS REGISTERED PUBLIC OFFERING OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2024, 2028 AND 2051 IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNTS OF $500.0 MILLION, $650.0 MILLION AND $550.0 MILLION, RESPECTIVELY. THE 2024 NOTES WILL HAVE AN INTEREST RATE OF 0.600% PER ANNUM AND ARE BEING ISSUED AT A PRICE EQUAL TO 99.825% OF THEIR FACE VALUE.
