AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DISTRIBUTION AND CALLS FOR REDEMPTION ALL OF ITS OUTSTANDING 4.70% SENIOR NOTES. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION (NYSE: AMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED ITS QUARTERLY CASH DISTRIBUTION OF $1.31 PER SHARE ON SHARES OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DISTRIBUTION IS PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 15, 2021 TO THE STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 28, 2021.
