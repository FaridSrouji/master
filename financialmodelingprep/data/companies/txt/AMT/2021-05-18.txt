AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION PRICES SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION (NYSE: AMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS REGISTERED PUBLIC OFFERING OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2027, 2029 AND 2033 IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNTS OF €750.0 MILLION (APPROXIMATELY $911.4 MILLION), €750.0 MILLION (APPROXIMATELY $911.4 MILLION) AND €500.0 MILLION (APPROXIMATELY $607.6 MILLION), RESPECTIVELY. THE 2027 NOTES WILL HAVE AN INTEREST RATE OF 0.450% PER ANNUM AND ARE BEING ISSUED AT A PRICE EQUAL TO 99.783% OF THEIR FACE VALUE.
