ALBEMARLE RECOGNIZED BY AMERICAN CHEMISTRY COUNCIL WITH EIGHT RESPONSIBLE CARE AWARDS. INCLUDES THREE AWARDS FOR THE COMPANY'S LITHIUM PRODUCTION SITE IN SILVER PEAK, NEVADA CHARLOTTE, N.C. , APRIL 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT EARNED EIGHT AMERICAN CHEMISTRY COUNCIL (ACC) RESPONSIBLE CARE AWARDS FOR ITS CONTINUED EXCELLENCE IN SUSTAINABILITY INITIATIVES FOR HEALTH, SAFETY AND ENVIRONMENTAL AREAS OF FOCUS.
