ALBEMARLE CORPORATION TO PARTICIPATE IN VIRTUAL PANEL DISCUSSION AT LOOP CAPITAL MARKETS 2022 INDUSTRY CONFERENCE. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , MAY 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ERIC NORRIS, PRESIDENT, LITHIUM, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A VIRTUAL PANEL DISCUSSION AT THE LOOP CAPITAL MARKETS 2022 INVESTOR CONFERENCE ON JUNE 1, 2022, AT 12:00 PM ET.
