ALBEMARLE UNVEILS MERCLOK™, INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGY FOR MERCURY REMEDIATION. SOIL TREATMENT THAT CAPTURES DANGEROUS NEUROTOXINS CHARLOTTE, N.C. , DEC. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, TODAY UNVEILED A BREAKTHROUGH PRODUCT THAT CAPTURES MERCURY FROM SOIL AND MINING WASTE, HELPING TO REMOVE THIS HARMFUL ELEMENT FROM THE FOOD CHAIN.
