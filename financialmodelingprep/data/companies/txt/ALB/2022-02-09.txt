ALBEMARLE IN DISCUSSIONS WITH MINERAL RESOURCES TO EXPAND LITHIUM JOINT VENTURE. CHARLOTTE, N.C., FEB. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB) ("ALBEMARLE"), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A NON-BINDING LETTER AGREEMENT WITH MINERAL RESOURCES LIMITED (ASX: MIN) ("MRL") TO EXPLORE A POTENTIAL EXPANSION OF THE MARBL LITHIUM JOINT VENTURE ("MARBL").
