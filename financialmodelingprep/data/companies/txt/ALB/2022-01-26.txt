ALBEMARLE CORPORATION RECOGNIZED IN 2022 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT IS ONE OF 418 COMPANIES ACROSS 11 SECTORS TO BE INCLUDED IN THE 2022 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI), A MODIFIED MARKET CAPITALIZATION-WEIGHTED INDEX THAT AIMS TO TRACK THE PERFORMANCE OF PUBLIC COMPANIES COMMITTED TO TRANSPARENCY IN GENDER-DATA REPORTING.
