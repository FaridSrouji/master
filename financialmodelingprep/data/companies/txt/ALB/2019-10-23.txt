ALBEMARLE CORPORATION PROVIDES EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2019; REVISES FINANCIAL GUIDANCE FOR THE FULL YEAR 2019. ALBEMARLE CORPORATION PROVIDED EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2019. THE COMPANY EXPECTS THIRD QUARTER 2019 DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE TO BE APPROXIMATELY $1.46 ON NET INCOME OF APPROXIMATELY $155 MILLION. THE COMPANY REVISED FINANCIAL GUIDANCE FOR THE FULL YEAR 2019. THE COMPANY NOW EXPECTS NET SALES TO BE IN THE RANGE OF $3.6 BILLION TO $3.7 BILLION AGAINST $3.65 BILLION TO $3.85 BILLION AS PREVIOUSLY FORECASTED.
