ALBEMARLE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES NEW U.S. LITHIUM MEGA-FLEX PROCESSING FACILITY IN SOUTH CAROLINA. PLANNED INVESTMENT OF AT LEAST $1.3 BILLION TO HELP MEET SURGING DEMAND FOR ELECTRIC VEHICLES AND LITHIUM-ION BATTERIES  CHARLOTTE, N.C. , MARCH 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A GLOBAL LEADER IN PROVIDING ESSENTIAL ELEMENTS FOR MOBILITY, ENERGY, CONNECTIVITY AND HEALTH, TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO LOCATE ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED LITHIUM HYDROXIDE MEGA-FLEX FACILITY IN CHESTER COUNTY, SOUTH CAROLINA.
