ALBEMARLE EARNS MULTIPLE AMERICAN CHEMISTRY COUNCIL RESPONSIBLE CARE AWARDS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JUNE 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT RECEIVED 10 AMERICAN CHEMISTRY COUNCIL (ACC) RESPONSIBLE CARE AWARDS FOR ITS EXEMPLARY HEALTH, SAFETY AND ENVIRONMENTAL INITIATIVES.
