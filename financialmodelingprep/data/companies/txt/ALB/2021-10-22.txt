ALBEMARLE SIGNS TWO AGREEMENTS FOR STRATEGIC INVESTMENTS IN CHINA TO EXPAND LITHIUM CONVERSION CAPACITY. CHARLOTTE, N.C., OCT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INTENT FOR STRATEGIC INVESTMENTS IN CHINA IN SUPPORT OF THE EXPANSION OF ITS LITHIUM CONVERSION CAPACITY.
