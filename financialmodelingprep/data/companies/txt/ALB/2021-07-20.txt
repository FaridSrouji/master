ALBEMARLE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES DIVIDEND. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB) ANNOUNCES THAT IT HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.39 PER SHARE.
