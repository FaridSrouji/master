ALBEMARLE ANNOUNCES NEW GLOBAL BUSINESS UNIT ALIGNMENT. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , AUG. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL REALIGN ITS BROMINE AND LITHIUM GLOBAL BUSINESS UNITS (GBU) INTO A NEW CORPORATE STRUCTURE DESIGNED TO BETTER MEET CUSTOMER NEEDS AND FOSTER TALENT REQUIRED TO DELIVER IN A COMPETITIVE GLOBAL ENVIRONMENT.
