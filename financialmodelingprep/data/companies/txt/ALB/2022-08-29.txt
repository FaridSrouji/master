ALBEMARLE CONCLUDES STRATEGIC REVIEW OF CATALYSTS BUSINESS. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , AUG. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT AS A RESULT OF ITS STRATEGIC REVIEW OF THE BUSINESS, ALBEMARLE HAS CHOSEN TO RETAIN ITS CATALYSTS BUSINESS UNDER A SEPARATE, TO-BE-NAMED ENTITY AND WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF ALBEMARLE.
