ALBEMARLE ANNOUNCES NEW SUPPLY CHAIN LEADERSHIP. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , APRIL 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MARIA BRENNAN HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS CHIEF SUPPLY CHAIN OFFICER, EFFECTIVE APRIL 29, 2022.
