ALBEMARLE JOINS UNITED NATIONS GLOBAL COMPACT CEO WATER MANDATE. COMPANY COMMITS TO SIX CORE WATER MANAGEMENT AREAS AND ANNUAL REPORTING CHARLOTTE, N.C. , APRIL 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENDORSED THE UNITED NATIONS (UN) GLOBAL COMPACT CEO WATER MANDATE, A GLOBAL INITIATIVE IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE PACIFIC INSTITUTE, THAT MOBILIZES BUSINESS LEADERS TO ADVANCE WATER STEWARDSHIP PRACTICES.
