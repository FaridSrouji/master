ALBEMARLE COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF GUANGXI TIANYUAN NEW ENERGY MATERIALS CO., LTD.. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , OCT. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALBEMARLE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALB), A LEADER IN THE GLOBAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS SUBSIDIARY, ALBEMARLE LITHIUM UK LIMITED, HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF GUANGXI TIANYUAN NEW ENERGY MATERIALS CO., LTD.
