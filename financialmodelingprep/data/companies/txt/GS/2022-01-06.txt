BARINGS PROVIDES FINANCING FOR GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT'S ACQUISITION OF THE COVE. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JAN. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BARINGS, ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING INVESTMENT MANAGERS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PROVIDED A $76.9 MILLION FINANCING PACKAGE FOR THE REAL ESTATE BUSINESS WITHIN GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT (GOLDMAN SACHS) ACQUISITION OF THE COVE, A 220-UNIT, BEST-IN-CLASS SUBURBAN APARTMENT COMMUNITY IN HINGHAM, MA.
