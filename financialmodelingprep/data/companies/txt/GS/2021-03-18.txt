GOLDMAN SACHS SELECTS MOODY'S ESG SOLUTIONS DATASET ON SOVEREIGN CLIMATE HAZARDS. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOODY'S ESG SOLUTIONS GROUP ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT (GOLDMAN SACHS) HAS SELECTED SOVEREIGN CLIMATE RISK SCORES POWERED BY MOODY'S AFFILIATE FOUR TWENTY SEVEN FOR USE IN ITS ESG EVALUATION OF SOVEREIGN RISK. THE DATASET PROVIDES A DETAILED VIEW OF THE FUTURE EXPOSURE OF THE GLOBAL POPULATION, THE ECONOMY, AND AGRICULTURE TO A RANGE OF PHYSICAL CLIMATE HAZARDS.
