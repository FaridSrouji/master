DEALERPOLICY ANNOUNCES $110 MILLION SERIES C FUNDING LED BY GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT. BURLINGTON, VT., AUG. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DEALERPOLICY, THE LEADING INSURANCE MARKETPLACE FOR AUTOMOTIVE RETAIL, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS $110 MILLION SERIES C INVESTMENT LED BY THE GROWTH EQUITY BUSINESS WITHIN GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT (GOLDMAN SACHS) WITH PARTICIPATION FROM EXISTING INVESTORS INCLUDING 3L CAPITAL AND HUDSON STRUCTURED CAPITAL MANAGEMENT LTD.
