ZINGEROO HONORED BY GOLDMAN SACHS FOR ENTREPRENEURSHIP. ZOË BARRY AMONG THE MOST EXCEPTIONAL ENTREPRENEURS AT 2022 BUILDERS AND INNOVATORS SUMMIT BOSTON , OCT. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GOLDMAN SACHS (NYSE:GS) IS RECOGNIZING ZINGEROO FOUNDER AND CEO ZOË BARRY AS ONE OF THE MOST EXCEPTIONAL ENTREPRENEURS OF 2022 AT ITS BUILDERS AND INNOVATORS SUMMIT IN HEALDSBURG, CALIFORNIA. GOLDMAN SACHS SELECTED ZOË BARRY FROM MULTIPLE INDUSTRIES TO BE HONORED AT THE TWO-DAY EVENT.
