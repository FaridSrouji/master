GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT AND CHARLESBANK CAPITAL PARTNERS TO ACQUIRE MAJORITY OWNERSHIP OF MDVIP FROM LEONARD GREEN & PARTNERS AND SUMMIT PARTNERS. BOCA RATON, FLA., AUG 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MDVIP, THE NATIONAL LEADER IN PERSONALIZED MEMBERSHIP-BASED HEALTHCARE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE PRIVATE EQUITY BUSINESS WITHIN GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT ("GOLDMAN SACHS") AND CHARLESBANK CAPITAL PARTNERS ("CHARLESBANK") HAVE ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE MAJORITY OWNERSHIP OF MDVIP FROM LEONARD GREEN & PARTNERS ("LGP") AND SUMMIT PARTNERS.
