GOLDMAN SACHS APPOINTS GRAZIANO GEMMA AS EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR. GOLDMAN SACHS ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS HIRED AN EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR FROM DEUTSCHE BANK IN MILAN. THE MOVE COMES AS OPPORTUNITIES FOR FINANCE PROFESSIONALS IN THE ITALIAN CITY ARE EXPANDING. GRAZIANO GEMMA, A FORMER DIRECTOR OF INVESTMENT BANKING AT DEUTSCHE BANK IN MILAN HAS JOINED IT. GOLDMAN SACHS HAS BEEN EXPANDING ITS MILAN OFFICE AS PART OF MOVES AIMED AT EXPANDING ITS BUSINESS IN EUROPE.
