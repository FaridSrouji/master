GOLDMAN SACHS ANNOUNCES NEW BUSINESS SEGMENTS. GOLDMAN SACHS ANNOUNCES NEW BUSINESS SEGMENTS.GOLDMAN SACHS - BUSINESS SEGMENTS ARE INVESTMENT BANKING, GLOBAL MARKETS, ASSET MANAGEMENT AND CONSUMER & WEALTH MANAGEMENT.GOLDMAN SACHS - ANNOUNCED NEW BUSINESS SEGMENTS FOR REPORTING OF FINANCIAL RESULTS, BEGINNING WITH 2019 Q4 & ANNUAL RESULTS TO BE ANNOUNCED JAN. 15.GOLDMAN SACHS - INSTITUTIONAL CLIENT SERVICES HAS BEEN RENAMED GLOBAL MARKETS.GOLDMAN SACHS - INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT HAS BEEN RENAMED ASSET MANAGEMENT.GOLDMAN SACHS - CONSUMER & WEALTH MANAGEMENT IS A NEW SEGMENT.GOLDMAN SACHS - INVESTMENT BANKING ADDITIONALLY INCLUDES RESULTS FROM LENDING TO CORPORATE CLIENTS.GOLDMAN SACHS - CONSUMER & WEALTH MANAGEMENT IS A NEW SEGMENT THAT INCLUDES MANAGEMENT AND OTHER FEES, INCENTIVE FEES.GOLDMAN SACHS - CONSUMER & WEALTH MANAGEMENT ALSO INCLUDES RESULTS FROM DEPOSIT-TAKING ACTIVITIES RELATED TO THE FIRM’S WEALTH MANAGEMENT BUSINESS.
