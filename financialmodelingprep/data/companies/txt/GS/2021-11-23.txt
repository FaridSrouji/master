LIME ROCK ANNOUNCES CLOSING OF LIME ROCK PARTNERS VI AF ACQUISITION FUND. HOUSTON, NOV. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LIME ROCK PARTNERS, PRIVATE EQUITY INVESTORS OF GROWTH CAPITAL IN E&P AND OILFIELD SERVICE COMPANIES, ANNOUNCES THE SUCCESSFUL CLOSING OF A $203 MILLION ACQUISITION FUND, LIME ROCK PARTNERS VI AF. THE FUND HAS ACQUIRED ASSETS FORMERLY OWNED BY LIME ROCK PARTNERS VI, L.P.
