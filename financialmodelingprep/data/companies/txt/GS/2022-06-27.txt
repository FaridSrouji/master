THE LARGEST ONLINE MARKETPLACE IN AUSTRALIA TO ADD TRADER INTERACTIVE'S US MARKETPLACES TO THEIR BRAND PORTFOLIO     VIRGINIA BEACH, VA. , JUNE 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARSALES.COM LTD (CARSALES), THE LARGEST ONLINE MARKETPLACE IN AUSTRALIA FOCUSING ON THE AUTOMOTIVE, MOTORCYCLE, AND MARINE REALMS, HAS ANNOUNCED THE INTENTION TO PURCHASE THE REMAINING 51% OF TRADER INTERACTIVE FROM THE CURRENT INVESTORS, EURAZEO AND GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT TO BRING THEIR HOLDING UP TO 100%.
