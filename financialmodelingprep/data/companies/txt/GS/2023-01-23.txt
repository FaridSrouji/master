GOLDMAN SACHS ALERT: BRAGAR EAGEL & SQUIRE, P.C. IS INVESTIGATING THE GOLDMAN SACHS GROUP, INC. ON BEHALF OF GOLDMAN SACHS STOCKHOLDERS AND ENCOURAGES INVESTORS TO CONTACT THE FIRM. (“GOLDMAN SACHS” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: GS) ON BEHALF OF GOLDMAN SACHS STOCKHOLDERS.
