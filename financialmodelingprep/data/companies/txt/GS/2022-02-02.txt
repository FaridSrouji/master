GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT COMPLETES INVESTMENT IN ELEA DIGITAL, PIEMONTE HOLDING'S BRAZILIAN DATA CENTER PLATFORM. RIO DE JANEIRO, FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PIEMONTE HOLDING AND THE PRIVATE CREDIT BUSINESS WITHIN GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT ANNOUNCE THEY HAVE COMPLETED AN INVESTMENT IN ELEA DIGITAL, AN ECOSYSTEM OF SIX DATA CENTERS ACROSS DIFFERENT METRO-AREAS IN BRAZIL. THE DEAL IS AIMED AT BOOSTING GROWTH OF ELEA DIGITAL, A BRAZILIAN INTERCONNECTED PLATFORM OF EDGE DATA CENTERS.
