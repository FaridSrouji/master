UNITED AIRLINES TEAMS UP WITH GOHEALTH URGENT CARE AND COLOR TO MAKE COVID-19 TESTS AVAILABLE FOR CUSTOMERS. UNITED AIRLINES - TEAMS UP WITH GOHEALTH URGENT CARE, COLOR TO MAKE COVID-19 TESTS AVAILABLE FOR THOSE FLYING FROM SAN FRANCISCO INTERNATIONAL TO HAWAII.UNITED AIRLINES SAYS PLANS TO INCREASE SERVICE TO HAWAII ON OCT. 15.UNITED - FROM OCT. 15, CUSTOMERS FLYING FROM SFO TO HAWAII CAN TAKE RAPID COVID-19 TEST AT AIRPORT OR SELF-COLLECTED, MAIL-IN TEST AHEAD OF TRIP.UNITED AIRLINES - WORKED TO ALIGN COVID-19 TESTS TO MEET HAWAII'S REQUIREMENTS, SO CO'S CUSTOMERS WITH NEGATIVE RESULT ARE NOT REQUIRED TO QUARANTINE.
