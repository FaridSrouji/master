UNITED AIRLINES TO LEAD INDUSTRY SWITCH TO SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL WITH GLOBAL CORPORATIONS, CUSTOMERS. CHICAGO, APRIL 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNITED AIRLINES CONTINUES TO LEAD THE INDUSTRY TOWARDS A MORE SUSTAINABLE FUTURE WITH THE LAUNCH OF THE FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND ECO-SKIES ALLIANCE  SM  PROGRAM. WORKING WITH THE AIRLINE, MORE THAN A DOZEN LEADING GLOBAL CORPORATIONS WILL COLLECTIVELY CONTRIBUTE TOWARDS THE PURCHASE OF APPROXIMATELY 3.4 MILLION GALLONS OF SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL (SAF) THIS YEAR.
