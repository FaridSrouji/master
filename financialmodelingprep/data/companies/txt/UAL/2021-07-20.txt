UNITED RELEASES SECOND-QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS; EXPECTS PROFITABILITY* IN THE THIRD QUARTER AND BEYOND. CHICAGO, JULY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNITED AIRLINES (UAL) TODAY ANNOUNCED SECOND-QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. THE COMPANY NOW EXPECTS POSITIVE ADJUSTED PRE-TAX INCOME¹ IN THE THIRD AND FOURTH QUARTERS OF 2021 AS TRAVEL DEMAND REBOUNDS.
