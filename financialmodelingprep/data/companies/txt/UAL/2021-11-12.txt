UNITED ADDS 44 FLIGHTS TO LAS VEGAS FOR CES 2022. CHICAGO, NOV. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN RESPONSE TO FEEDBACK FROM ITS BUSINESS CUSTOMERS AND AN UPTICK IN DEMAND, UNITED AIRLINES IS EXPANDING ITS SCHEDULE TO MAKE IT EASIER FOR CES 2022 ATTENDEES TO JOIN THE IN-PERSON SHOW IN LAS VEGAS. THE AIRLINE IS ADDING 14 NEW DIRECT FLIGHTS IN EARLY JANUARY BETWEEN LAS VEGAS AND SAN JOSE, CALIF.
