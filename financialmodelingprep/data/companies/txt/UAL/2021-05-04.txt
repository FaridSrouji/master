UNITED AIRLINES EXPANDS INDIA RELIEF EFFORTS WITH ONLINE FUNDRAISING CAMPAIGN. CHICAGO, MAY 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, UNITED AIRLINES EXPANDS ITS EFFORTS TO SUPPORT THOSE IMPACTED BY THE COVID-19 CRISIS IN INDIA WITH THE LAUNCH OF A NEW ONLINE FUNDRAISING CAMPAIGN. CUSTOMERS CAN DONATE TO THE AIRLINE'S RELIEF PARTNERS: AIRLINK, AMERICARES, GLOBALGIVING FOUNDATION AND WORLD CENTRAL KITCHEN.
