UNITED RELEASES FIRST-QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS - REBOUNDING DEMAND IS DRIVING CLEAR PATH TO PROFITABILITY. CHICAGO, APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNITED AIRLINES (UAL) TODAY ANNOUNCED FIRST-QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. THE COMPANY HAS ITS EYES ON THE FUTURE, MAKING CONTINUED PROGRESS ON ITS COMMITMENT TO REMOVE $2 BILLION IN STRUCTURAL COSTS AND INVESTING IN KEY CUSTOMER PROGRAMS THAT WILL POSITION THE AIRLINE TO CAPITALIZE ON THE RECOVERY OF BUSINESS TRAVEL AND LONG-HAUL INTERNATIONAL DEMAND.
