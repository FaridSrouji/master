BOEING, UNITED AIRLINES FINALIZE 737 MAX AND 787 ORDER, INCLUDING RECORD PURCHASE FOR 100 DREAMLINERS. NORTH CHARLESTON, S.C. , DEC. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE:BA] AND UNITED AIRLINES [NASDAQ:UAL] TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CARRIER IS INVESTING IN ITS FUTURE FLEET WITH AN ORDER FOR 100 787 AIRPLANES, WITH THE OPTION TO PURCHASE 100 MORE.
