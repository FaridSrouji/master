UNITED AND ABBOTT PARTNER TO MAKE RETURN TO U.S. "WORRY FREE" FOR INTERNATIONAL TRAVELERS WITH HOME-TESTING KITS. CHICAGO AND ABBOTT PARK, ILL., MAY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNITED AND ABBOTT TODAY ANNOUNCED A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND COLLABORATION TO USE ABBOTT'S BINAXNOW™ COVID-19 HOME TEST AND ABBOTT'S NAVICA APP TO HELP MAKE THE INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL EXPERIENCE MORE SEAMLESS.
