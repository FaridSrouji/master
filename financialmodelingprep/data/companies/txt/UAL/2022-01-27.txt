UNITED OFFICIALLY OPENS FLIGHT ACADEMY AND WELCOMES HISTORIC INAUGURAL PILOT CLASS. GOODYEAR, ARIZ., JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNITED, THE ONLY MAJOR U.S. AIRLINE TO OWN A FLIGHT TRAINING SCHOOL, OFFICIALLY OPENED UNITED AVIATE ACADEMY TODAY AND WELCOMED A HISTORIC INAUGURAL CLASS OF FUTURE PILOTS, 80% OF WHOM ARE WOMEN OR PEOPLE OF COLOR.
