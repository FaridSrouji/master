UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS ENTERES INTO A TERM LOAN CREDIT AND GUARANTY AGREEMENT. UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS INC - ON MARCH 20, ENTERED INTO A TERM LOAN CREDIT AND GUARANTY AGREEMENT.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS INC - CREDIT AGREEMENT PROVIDES FOR A TERM LOAN FACILITY OF $500 MILLION.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS INC - UNITED BORROWED FULL AMOUNT OF TERM LOAN FACILITY  IN TWO SEPARATE DISBURSEMENTS ON MARCH 23, 2020 AND MARCH 24, 2020.
