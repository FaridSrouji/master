UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS EXPECTS CONSOLIDATED CAPACITY FOR AUGUST 2020 TO BE DOWN APPROX 65% Y-O-Y. UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS INC - DURING TOWN HALL, MEMBERS OF MANAGEMENT INDICATED CONSOLIDATED CAPACITY FOR JUNE 2020 WAS DOWN ABOUT 88% YEAR-OVER-YEAR.UNITED AIRLINES - CONSOLIDATED CAPACITY FOR AUGUST 2020 IS EXPECTED TO BE DOWN APPROXIMATELY 65% YEAR-OVER-YEAR.UNITED AIRLINES - PLANS TO CONTINUE TO PROACTIVELY EVALUATE AND CANCEL FLIGHTS ON A ROLLING 60-DAY BASIS UNTIL IT SEES SIGNS OF A RECOVERY IN DEMAND.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS INC - MEMBERS OF MANAGEMENT INDICATED JULY 2020 CONSOLIDATED CAPACITY IS EXPECTED TO BE DOWN ABOUT 75% YEAR-OVER-YEAR.UNITED AIRLINES - EXPECTS DEMAND TO REMAIN SUPPRESSED UNTIL A WIDELY ACCEPTED TREATMENT AND/OR VACCINE FOR COVID-19 IS AVAILABLE.UNITED AIRLINES - CONSOLIDATED CAPACITY THROUGH END OF 2020 IS EXPECTED TO BE GENERALLY CONSISTENT WITH AUGUST.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS INC - MEMBERS OF MANAGEMENT NOTED THAT COMPANY DOES NOT EXPECT RECOVERY FROM COVID-19 TO FOLLOW A LINEAR PATH.UNITED AIRLINES - POTENTIALLY IMPACTED FRONTLINE EMPLOYEES BASED IN CA, IL, NJ AND NY WILL GET WRITEN WARN NOTIFICATION IN EARLY TO MID-JULY.UNITED AIRLINES - ALL EMPLOYEES WILL BE NOTIFIED ABOUT THEIR POSITION BY EARLY AUGUST WITH CHANGES EFFECTIVE OCT 1.
UNITED AIRLINES EXECUTIVE SAYS DURING EMPLOYEE TOWN HALL THAT NOT ENOUGH WORKERS HAVE SIGNED UP FOR VOLUNTARY DEPARTURES SO FAR - SOURCES. UNITED AIRLINES EXECUTIVE SAYS DURING EMPLOYEE TOWN HALL THAT NOT ENOUGH WORKERS HAVE SIGNED UP FOR VOLUNTARY DEPARTURES SO FAR - SOURCES.UNITED WORKING WITH PILOTS UNION ON WAYS TO MITIGATE FURLOUGHS - SOURCES.UNITED EXECUTIVE SAYS NO STRAIGHT LINE TO RECOVERY; UNITED NETWORK SLIGHTLY MORE IMPACTED THAN OTHER DOMESTIC U.S. AIRLINES - SOURCES.UNITED SEES DOMESTIC LOAD FACTORS AROUND 50%, INTERNATIONAL 30% IN JULY VERSUS AROUND 80% NORMALLY - SOURCES.
