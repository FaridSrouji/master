UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS - NET BOOKINGS FOR REMAINDER OF Q2 AND Q3 HAVE REMAINED POSITIVE SINCE END OF MAY.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS -  FOR JULY 2020, EXPECTS CONSOLIDATED CAPACITY TO BE DOWN ABOUT75%.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS - CONTINUES TO SEE A STEADY IMPROVEMENT IN DEMAND IN DOMESTIC U.S. AND SOME INTERNATIONAL DESTINATIONS.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS -  FOR JULY 2020, EXPECTS DOMESTIC CAPACITY TO BE DOWN ABOUT 70%.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS INC - JUNE TICKETED PASSENGER REVENUE IS EXPECTED TO BE UP CLOSE TO 400% VERSUS APRIL.UNITED AIRLINES -  JUNE TICKETED PASSENGER REVENUE IS EXPECTED TO BE UP CLOSE TO 400% VERSUS APRIL.UNITED AIRLINES -  INCLUDING CARGO AND OTHER REVENUE, NOW EXPECTS TOTAL REVENUE TO BE DOWN 88% IN Q2 VERSUS Q2 2019.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS INC - CARGO REVENUE CONTINUE TO BE STRONG AND ARE EXPECTED TO BE UP OVER 30% IN Q2 OF 2020 VERSUS Q2 OF 2019.UNITED AIRLINES HOLDINGS -  SEES JULY PASSENGER REVENUE UP 50%-100% VERSUS CO'S JUNE 2020 PASSENGER REVENUE ESTIMATE.UNITED AIRLINES - ON TRACK TO ACHIEVE OVER $2.5 BILLION OF FY REDUCTIONS IN ADJUSTED CAPEX, BRINGING EXPECTED 2020 FY ADJUSTED CAPEX TO BELOW $4.5 BILLION.UNITED AIRLINES - OPERATING EXPENSES IN Q2 EXPECTED TO DECLINE BY 53% AS COMPARED Q2 2019.UNITED AIRLINES - CONTINUES TO EXPECT THAT DEMAND WILL BE REDUCED YEAR-OVER-YEAR AS OF OCTOBER 1, 2020.UNITED AIRLINES - EXPECT JULY TICKETED PASSENGER REVENUE TO BE DOWN 82% - 88% YOY.
