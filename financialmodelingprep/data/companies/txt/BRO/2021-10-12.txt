BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES BRAND UNIFICATION FOR ITS RETAIL SEGMENT. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., OCT. 12, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BROWN & BROWN, INC. PROUDLY ANNOUNCES ALL BRANDS UNDER ITS RETAIL SEGMENT WILL BE UNIFIED UNDER THE BRAND NAME BROWN & BROWN AS OF JAN. 1, 2022.
