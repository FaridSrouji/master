BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES THE ACQUISITION OF DEALER SPECIALTIES GROUP CORP. BY BROWN & BROWN DEALER SERVICES. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., JUNE 01, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- J. SCOTT PENNY, CHIEF ACQUISITIONS OFFICER OF BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE:BRO), AND ANDREW (ANDY) COURCHAINE, OWNER OF DEALER SPECIALTIES GROUP CORP. (“DEALER SPECIALTIES”), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BROWN & BROWN DEALER SERVICES (“BBDS”) HAS ACQUIRED SUBSTANTIALLY ALL OF THE ASSETS OF DEALER SPECIALTIES.
