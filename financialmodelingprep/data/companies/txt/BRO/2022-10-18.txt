BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES 12.2% INCREASE IN QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND RATE. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., OCT. 18, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE: BRO) ANNOUNCES THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.1150 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 16, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 2, 2022. THE DIVIDEND REPRESENTS A 12.2% INCREASE FROM THE PREVIOUS REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.1025 PER SHARE AND IS THE TWENTY-NINTH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASE FOR THE COMPANY.
