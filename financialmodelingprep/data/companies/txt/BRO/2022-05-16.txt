SCOTT PENNY, CHIEF ACQUISITIONS OFFICER OF BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE:BRO), AND DONALD (DON) R. BRANDT AND RANDALL (RANDY) J. BRANDT, THE PRINCIPALS OF CLAIM TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED (CTI), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BROWN & BROWN OF MASSACHUSETTS, LLC, A SUBSIDIARY OF BROWN & BROWN, INC., HAS ACQUIRED SUBSTANTIALLY ALL OF THE ASSETS OF CTI.
