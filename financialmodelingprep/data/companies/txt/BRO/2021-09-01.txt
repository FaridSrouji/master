BROWN & BROWN, INC. COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF BERKSHIRE INSURANCE GROUP, INC.. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., SEPT. 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE:BRO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF THE ASSETS AND OPERATIONS OF BERKSHIRE INSURANCE GROUP, INC. BY BROWN & BROWN OF MASSACHUSETTS, LLC, A SUBSIDIARY OF BROWN & BROWN.
