BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES THE ASSET ACQUISITION OF SMITHWICK & MARINERS INSURANCE, MIDCOAST INSURANCE AND CHAPMAN & CHAPMAN INSURANCE. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., SEPT. 13, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- J. SCOTT PENNY, CHIEF ACQUISITIONS OFFICER OF BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE:BRO), AND SCOTT SMITHWICK AND CHRIS SMITHWICK, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BROWN & BROWN OF MASSACHUSETTS, INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF BROWN & BROWN, INC., HAS ACQUIRED SUBSTANTIALLY ALL OF THE ASSETS OF SMITHWICK & MARINERS INSURANCE, MIDCOAST INSURANCE AND CHAPMAN & CHAPMAN INSURANCE (COLLECTIVELY, SMITHWICK & MARINERS).
