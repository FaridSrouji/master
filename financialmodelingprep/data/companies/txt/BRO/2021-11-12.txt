BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES THE ACQUISITION OF HEACOCK INSURANCE GROUP. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., NOV. 12, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- J. SCOTT PENNY, CHIEF ACQUISITIONS OFFICER OF BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE:BRO), AND STACEY HEACOCK WEEKS, JASON HEACOCK AND FORD HEACOCK, III THE PRINCIPALS OF HEACOCK INSURANCE GROUP, INC. (“HEACOCK INSURANCE”), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BROWN & BROWN HAS ACQUIRED THE ASSETS OF HEACOCK INSURANCE.
