BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES 10.8% INCREASE IN QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND RATE. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., OCT. 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE: BRO) ANNOUNCES THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.1025 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 17, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 3, 2021. THE DIVIDEND REPRESENTS A 10.8% INCREASE FROM THE PREVIOUS REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.0925 PER SHARE AND IS THE TWENTY-EIGHTH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASE FOR THE COMPANY.
BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES THE STOCK ACQUISITION OF REMEDY ANALYTICS, INC.. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., OCT. 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- J. SCOTT PENNY, CHIEF ACQUISITIONS OFFICER OF BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE:BRO), AND THE SHAREHOLDERS OF REMEDY ANALYTICS INC., TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BROWN & BROWN, INC. HAS ACQUIRED ALL OF THE STOCK OF REMEDY ANALYTICS.
