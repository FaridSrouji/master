BERKSHIRE HILLS AND BROWN & BROWN ANNOUNCE DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL BERKSHIRE INSURANCE GROUP TO BROWN & BROWN OF MASSACHUSETTS. BOSTON, AUG. 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BERKSHIRE HILLS BANCORP, INC. (NYSE: BHLB) AND BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE: BRO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXECUTION OF A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT FOR THE SALE OF THE ASSETS AND OPERATIONS OF BERKSHIRE INSURANCE GROUP, INC. (BIG), A SUBSIDIARY OF BERKSHIRE HILLS, TO BROWN & BROWN OF MASSACHUSETTS, LLC, A SUBSIDIARY OF BROWN & BROWN. THE TRANSACTION IS SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CONDITIONS AND IS EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED IN THE THIRD QUARTER.
