BROWN & BROWN, INC. NAMES STEPHEN BOYD AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF TECHNOLOGY, INNOVATION, AND DIGITAL STRATEGY. BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT STEPHEN BOYD HAS BEEN NAMED AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF TECHNOLOGY, INNOVATION, AND DIGITAL STRATEGY. AS AN INDUSTRY LEADER, BROWN & BROWN RECOGNIZES THE CRITICAL NEED TO CONTINUOUSLY MODERNIZE OFFERINGS TO MEET THE EVER-CHANGING NEEDS OF BOTH CUSTOMERS AND TRADING PARTNERS. WITH THAT COMES THE NEED FOR CONSTANT INNOVATION AND A STRATEGIC FOCUS ON BUSINESS-DRIVEN TECHNOLOGY. OYD’S NEW ROLE WILL INCLUDE WORKING CLOSELY WITH BUSINESS LEADERS WITHIN THE COMPANY’S FOUR BUSINESS SEGMENTS TO FINETUNE LONG-TERM TECHNOLOGY ROADMAPS THAT DRIVE THE DEVELOPMENT OF INNOVATIVE, INSURANCE PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS. IN HIS NEW CAPACITY, BOYD WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO PRESIDENT & CEO POWELL BROWN. BOYD PREVIOUSLY HELD THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER OF ARROWHEAD GENERAL INSURANCE AGENCY, INC. AND WAS A SENIOR LEADER WITHIN BROWN & BROWN’S NATIONAL PROGRAMS SEGMENT.
