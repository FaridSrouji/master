BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., JULY 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE:BRO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.0925 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON AUGUST 18, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON AUGUST 11, 2021.
