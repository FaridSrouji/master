BROWN & BROWN, INC. JOINS S&P 500. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., SEPT. 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE:BRO) ANNOUNCES THAT S&P DOW JONES INDICES REPORTED ON SEPTEMBER 3, 2021, THAT THE COMPANY WOULD BE INCLUDED IN THE PRESTIGIOUS S&P 500 INDEX EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPEN OF TRADING ON MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 20, 2021.
