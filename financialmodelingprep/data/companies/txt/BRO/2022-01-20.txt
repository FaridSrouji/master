BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., JAN. 20, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE: BRO) ANNOUNCES THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.1025 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 16, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 4, 2022.
