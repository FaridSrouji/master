BROWN & BROWN, INC. ANNOUNCES THE ASSET ACQUISITION OF PIPER JORDAN. DAYTONA BEACH, FLA., MAY 17, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- J. SCOTT PENNY, CHIEF ACQUISITIONS OFFICER OF BROWN & BROWN, INC. (NYSE:BRO), AND ROBYN PIPER AND JAY JORDAN, THE PRINCIPALS OF PIPER JORDAN, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BROWN & BROWN HAS ACQUIRED SUBSTANTIALLY ALL OF THE ASSETS OF PIPER JORDAN.
