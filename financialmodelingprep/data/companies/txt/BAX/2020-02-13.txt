BAXTER INTERNATIONAL - FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AS OF DEC 31, 2018 & 2017, FOR YRS ENDED DEC 31, 2018, 2017 & 2016, SHOULD NO LONGER BE RELIED UPON.BAXTER - BELIEVES USE OF PREVIOUS EXCHANGE RATE CONVENTION TO GENERATE NON-OPERATING FOREX GAINS & AVOID LOSSES HAD OCCURRED FOR AT LEAST 10 YRS.BAXTER - IMPACT OF CORRECTING MISSTATEMENTS OF NON-OPERATING FOREX GAINS, LOSSES RELATING TO EARLIER PERIODS WILL REDUCE OPENING RETAINED EARNINGS.BAXTER - HAS REASSESSED ITS CONCLUSIONS REGARDING EFFECTIVENESS OF COMPANY'S INTERNAL CONTROL OVER FINANCIAL REPORTING AS OF DEC 31, 2018.BAXTER - DETERMINED THAT ONE OR MORE MATERIAL WEAKNESSES IN INTERNAL CONTROL OVER FINANCIAL REPORTING EXISTED FOR REPORTING PERIOD AS OF DEC 31, 2018.BAXTER - EXPECTS TO REPORT ONE OR MORE MATERIAL WEAKNESSES AS OF DEC 31, 2019 IN ITS ANNUAL REPORT ON FORM 10-K FOR YEAR ENDED DEC 31, 2019.
