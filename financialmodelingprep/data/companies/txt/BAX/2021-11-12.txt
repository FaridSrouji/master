BAXTER ANNOUNCES $100 MILLION INVESTMENT IN BIOPHARMA SOLUTIONS HALLE/WESTFALEN, GERMANY STERILE FILL/FINISH MANUFACTURING FACILITY. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BAXTER ANNOUNCED A $100M INVESTMENT IN ITS MANUFACTURING FACILITY IN HALLE/WESTFALEN, GERMANY THAT WILL ADD NEW CAPACITY AND CAPABILITIES.
