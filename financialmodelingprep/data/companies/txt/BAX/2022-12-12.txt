BAXTER NAMED TO DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDICES FOR 23RD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. DEERFIELD, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--BAXTER INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE: BAX), A GLOBAL MEDTECH LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WAS AGAIN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDICES (DJSI), INCLUDING BOTH DJSI WORLD AND DJSI NORTH AMERICA. BAXTER HAS PROUDLY BEEN INCLUDED IN THE DJSI WORLD AND DJSI NORTH AMERICA EACH YEAR SINCE THEY LAUNCHED, IN 1999 AND 2005, RESPECTIVELY.
