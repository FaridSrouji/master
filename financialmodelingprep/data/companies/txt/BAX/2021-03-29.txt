BAXTER LAUNCHES INITIATIVE TO INCREASE AWARENESS AND EDUCATION IN BLACK COMMUNITIES ABOUT KIDNEY HEALTH AND MANAGING CHRONIC KIDNEY DISEASE. DEERFIELD, ILL. & WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BAXTER INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE:BAX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL PRODUCTS COMPANY, AND THE CENTRAL AREA OF THE LINKS, INCORPORATED, AN INTERNATIONAL NOT-FOR-PROFIT COMPRISED OF 16,000 WOMEN OF COLOR, ANNOUNCE A NEW PARTNERSHIP TO BRING AWARENESS AND RESOURCES TO THE DISPROPORTIONATE CHALLENGES AFFECTING BLACK AMERICANS RELATED TO KIDNEY HEALTH. THROUGH THE PARTNERSHIP, BAXTER IS COMMITTING $600,000 TO FUND GRANTS TO LOCAL LINKS CHAPTERS IN THE ORGANI
