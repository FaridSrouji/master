BAXTER INCREASES DIVIDEND AND DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BAXTER INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE:BAX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDTECH COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED AN INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO A RATE OF $0.29 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THIS REPRESENTS AN APPROXIMATELY 3.5% INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE OF $0.28 PER SHARE. BAXTER'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED THE DIVIDEND PAYABLE ON JULY 1, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 3, 2022. THE INDICATED ANNUAL DIVIDEND RATE IS NOW $1.16
