BAXTER DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BAXTER INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE:BAX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL PRODUCTS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.245 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 4, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 4, 2020. THE INDICATED ANNUAL DIVIDEND RATE IS $0.98 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.
