JOHNSON CONTROLS NAMED TO PRESTIGIOUS FT EUROPEAN CLIMATE LEADERS LIST. CORK, IRELAND, MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE INAUGURAL FT CLIMATE LEADERS IN EUROPE LIST.
