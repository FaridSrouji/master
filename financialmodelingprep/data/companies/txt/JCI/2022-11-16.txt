JOHNSON CONTROLS LAUNCHES OPENBLUE ON ALIBABA CLOUD. SHANGHAI , NOV. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE:JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, HAS OFFICIALLY LAUNCHED OPENBLUE ENTERPRISE MANAGER (OBEM) ON THE ALIBABA CLOUD, DEEPENING THE COLLABORATION BETWEEN THE TWO COMPANIES. ALIBABA CLOUD IS A LEADING CLOUD COMPUTING AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SERVICE PROVIDER.
