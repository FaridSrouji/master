JOHNSON CONTROLS DEEPENS ITS COLLABORATION WITH THE INTERNATIONAL WELL BUILDING INSTITUTE AS AN ENTERPRISE PROVIDER TO DELIVER HEALTHY BUILDING SOLUTIONS. WITH A GOAL OF SCALING HEALTH AND WELL-BEING EFFORTS EFFICIENTLY AND EFFECTIVELY, JOHNSON CONTROLS' DESIGNATION AS A WELL ENTERPRISE PROVIDER (EP) WILL HELP DRIVE A GLOBAL CULTURE OF HEALTH FOR EMPLOYEES, CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES JOHNSON CONTROLS U.S. AND CHINA HEADQUARTERS RENEWED ITS WELL HEALTH-SAFETY RATING FOR FACILITY OPERATIONS AND MANAGEMENT BY THE INTERNATIONAL WELL BUILDING INSTITUTE MILWAUKEE , APRIL 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS  (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS INVESTED IN ITS COLLABORATION WITH THE INTERNATIONAL WELL BUILDING INSTITUTE (IWBI) TO STRENGTHEN ITS COMMITMENT TO HEALTHY BUILDING SOLUTIONS FOR BOTH ITS EMPLOYEES AND CUSTOMERS. JOHNSON CONTROLS INVESTS IN ITS COMMITMENT TO CREATE A GLOBAL CULTURE OF HEALTH FOR EMPLOYEES, CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES.
