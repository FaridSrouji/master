JOHNSON CONTROLS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CORK, IRELAND , JUNE 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC (NYSE: JCI) APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.35 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON JULY 15, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 21, 2022. JOHNSON CONTROLS HAS PAID A CONSECUTIVE DIVIDEND SINCE 1887.
