JOHNSON CONTROLS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CORK, IRELAND , DEC. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC (NYSE: JCI) APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.35 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON JAN. 13, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DEC. 19, 2022. JOHNSON CONTROLS HAS PAID A CONSECUTIVE DIVIDEND SINCE 1887.
