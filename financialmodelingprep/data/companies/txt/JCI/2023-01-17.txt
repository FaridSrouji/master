SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS BY JOHNSON CONTROLS COLLABORATES WITH ZLIIDE TO REIMAGINE SELF-CHECKOUT. NEUHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND--( BUSINESS WIRE )--SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS, THE LEADING GLOBAL RETAIL SOLUTIONS PORTFOLIO OF JOHNSON CONTROLS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS COLLABORATION WITH ZLIIDE, A TECHNOLOGY COMPANY THAT SEEKS TO BRING PHYSICAL STORES INTO THE NEXT ERA THROUGH CONVENIENCE AND DIGITALIZATION. THE TWO BRANDS ARE WORKING IN TANDEM TO DELIVER A NEW APPROACH TO SELF-CHECKOUT THAT ELIMINATES REGISTER QUEUES AND STREAMLINES SHOPPER EXPERIENCES.
COMPUTER VISION TECHNOLOGY DELIVERED BY JOHNSON CONTROLS SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS POWERS A PORTFOLIO OF OUTCOME-FOCUSED ANALYTICS TO SOLVE RETAILERS' EVOLVING CHALLENGES. NEUHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND--( BUSINESS WIRE )--SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS, THE LEADING GLOBAL RETAIL SOLUTIONS PORTFOLIO OF JOHNSON CONTROLS, IS DEMONSTRATING THE FULL BREADTH OF ITS COMPUTER VISION ANALYTICS TECHNOLOGY AT THE 2023 NRF BIG SHOW. EACH OF SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS COMPUTER VISION CAPABILITIES LEVERAGE PROPRIETARY DEEP LEARNING ALGORITHMS AND HELP RETAILERS OPTIMIZE LABOR ALLOCATION, STRENGTHEN LOSS PREVENTION EFFORTS, MAINTAIN SAFE ENVIRONMENTS FOR SHOPPERS AND ASSOCIATES, AND GATHER INSIGHTS ON SHOPPER EXPERIENCES.
