JOHNSON CONTROLS TO HOST VIRTUAL INVESTOR DAY. CORK, IRELAND, AUG. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC (NYSE: JCI) WILL HOST A VIRTUAL INVESTOR MEETING ON WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 2021 BEGINNING AT 8:00AM EDT AND EXPECTED TO CONCLUDE AT APPROXIMATELY 11:30AM EDT. THE AGENDA WILL INCLUDE PRESENTATIONS FROM THE SENIOR LEADERSHIP TEAM FOLLOWED BY A QUESTION-AND-ANSWER SESSION.
