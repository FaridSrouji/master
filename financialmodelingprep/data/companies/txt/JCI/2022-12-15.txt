JOHNSON CONTROLS AWARDED PLATINUM SUSTAINABILITY RATING BY ECOVADIS, PLACING IT IN TOP 1% OF COMPANIES EVALUATED. CORK, IRELAND , DEC. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE:JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, HAS BEEN AWARDED THE PLATINUM ECOVADIS SUSTAINABILITY RATING, THE HIGHEST DISTINCTION GRANTED. ECOVADIS IS A LEADING RATINGS ORGANIZATION FOR THE SUSTAINABILITY OF SUPPLY CHAINS.
