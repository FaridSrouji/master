JOHNSON CONTROLS AND PHYLAGEN SIGN HEALTHY BUILDINGS JOINT DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT. CORK, IRELAND, SEPT. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A JOINT DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT WITH PHYLAGEN , A LEADING BIOTECHNOLOGY COMPANY IN THE FIELD OF MICROBIOME OPTIMIZATION OF INDOOR ENVIRONMENTS.
