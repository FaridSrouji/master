JOHNSON CONTROLS ANNOUNCES INCREASED QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CORK, IRELAND , MARCH 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $.36 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON APRIL 14, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 20, 2023. THIS REPRESENTS A $0.01 CENT INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
