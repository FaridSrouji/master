SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS BY JOHNSON CONTROLS SURVEY SHOWS U.S. CONSUMERS WILL SHOP EARLIER THIS HOLIDAY SEASON. NEUHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS, THE LEADING GLOBAL RETAIL SOLUTIONS PORTFOLIO OF JOHNSON CONTROLS, TODAY RELEASED ITS THIRD ANNUAL U.S. HOLIDAY CONSUMER SENTIMENT SURVEY, WHICH FOUND MORE SHOPPERS ARE PLANNING TO SHOP IN-STORE THIS YEAR THAN DURING THE 2020 AND 2021 HOLIDAY SEASONS.
