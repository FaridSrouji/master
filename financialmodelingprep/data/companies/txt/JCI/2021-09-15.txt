JOHNSON CONTROLS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CORK, IRELAND, SEPT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC (NYSE: JCI) APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.27 PER COMMON SHARE PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 27, 2021.
