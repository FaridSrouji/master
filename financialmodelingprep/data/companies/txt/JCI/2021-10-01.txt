JOHNSON CONTROLS APPOINTS MARLON SULLIVAN AS CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER. CORK, IRELAND, OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS APPOINTED MARLON SULLIVAN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MARLON BRINGS SKILLS AND DYNAMISM TO DRIVE OUR TALENT STRATEGIES AND HIGH-PERFORMANCE CULTURE  -- CEO GEORGE OLIVER SULLIVAN REPORTS TO JOHNSON CONTROLS CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER GEORGE OLIVER AND JOINS THE COMPANY FROM DELTA AIRLINES, WHERE HE HELD THE ROLE OF SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT FOR HUMAN RESOURCES.
