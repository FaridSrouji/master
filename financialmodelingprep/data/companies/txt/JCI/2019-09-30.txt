JOHNSON CONTROLS APPOINTS TOMAS BRANNEMO AS VP & PRESIDENT, BUILDING SOLUTIONS EMEALA. JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC HAS NAMED TOMAS BRANNEMO VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT, BUILDING SOLUTIONS EUROPE, MIDDLE EAST, AFRICA, AND LATIN AMERICA (EMEALA) EFFECTIVE SEPT. 30, 2019. IN THIS ROLE BRANNEMO WILL SERVE AS AN OFFICER OF THE COMPANY AND OVERSEE JOHNSON CONTROLS BUILDING TECHNOLOGIES & SOLUTIONS OPERATIONS ACROSS EMEALA. BRANNEMO SUCCEEDS JEFF WILLIAMS, WHO WAS RECENTLY APPOINTED VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT, GLOBAL PRODUCTS, BUILDING TECHNOLOGIES & SOLUTIONS. BRANNEMO MOST RECENTLY SERVED A SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, WATER INFRASTRUCTURE AND EUROPE COMMERCIAL TEAM, AND PRESIDENT, XYLEM EUROPE GMBH WHERE HE WAS RESPONSIBLE FOR ALL ASPECTS OF GLOBAL OPERATIONS AS WELL AS OVERSEEING XYLEM'S KEY FUNCTIONS FOR THE EUROPEAN REGION.
