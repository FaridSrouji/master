JOHNSON CONTROLS APPOINTS LEI SCHLITZ AS GLOBAL PRODUCTS PRESIDENT. -   SCHLITZ WILL LEAD GLOBAL PRODUCTS RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT, PRODUCT MANAGEMENT, AND THE FUTURE ROADMAP FOR NEW PRODUCTION INTRODUCTIONS CORK, IRELAND , NOV. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE:JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, HAS NAMED LEI SCHLITZ AS VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT, GLOBAL PRODUCTS EFFECTIVE TODAY. IN THIS ROLE, SCHLITZ WILL LEAD THE GLOBAL PRODUCTS BUSINESS, SUCCEEDING JEFF WILLIAMS WHO RETIRED EARLIER THIS YEAR.
