JOHNSON CONTROLS COLLABORATES WITH NEARLY 900 U.S. HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS TO PREPARE CAMPUSES FOR FALL 2021 REOPENING. MILWAUKEE, OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, COLLABORATED WITH 894 U.S. HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS IN ITS FISCAL THIRD QUARTER TO IMPLEMENT HEALTHY BUILDING STRATEGIES IN PREPARATION FOR CAMPUS REOPENING IN FALL 2021. HEALTHY CAMPUS ENVIRONMENTS HAVE A DIRECT POSITIVE INFLUENCE ON STUDENT ACHIEVEMENT AND WELLNESS AS PART OF ITS OPENBLUE HEALTHY BUILDINGS OFFERINGS, JOHNSON CONTROLS HAS HELPED COLLEGE ADMINISTRATORS CREATE AND IMPLEMENT STRATEGIES TO SAFELY AND EFFICIENTLY WELCOME BACK STUDENTS, STAFF AND FACULTY.
