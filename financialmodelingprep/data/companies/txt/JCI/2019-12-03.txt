JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JAN. 10, 2020. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.26 PER COMMON SHARE PAYABLE ON JAN. 10, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DEC.16, 2019.
