SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS BY JOHNSON CONTROLS EXPANDS ITS CONNECTED SOLUTION SUITE WITH FOOD SAFETY COMPLIANCE PLATFORM. NEUHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JOHNSON CONTROLS TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS IS ADDING PENN CONNECTED TO ITS SENSORMATIC IQ PLATFORM CAPABILITIES, TO INCREASE OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY THROUGH REMOTE REFRIGERATION MONITORING AND DIGITAL COMPLIANCE. THE DIGITAL SOLUTION HELPS RETAILERS COMBAT FOOD WASTE, AND ASSISTS WITH COMPLIANCE BY MONITORING PRODUCT STORING TEMPERATURES, BOTH HOT AND COLD.
