JOHNSON CONTROLS NAMES MANDEVILLE TO LEAD CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT, DRIVE M&A AND PARTNERSHIP GROWTH STRATEGY. CORK, IRELAND, AUG. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT FRANÇOIS MANDEVILLE HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS ITS CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT OFFICER. MANDEVILLE WILL PLAY A LEAD ROLE IN THE SELECTION, EXECUTION AND INTEGRATION OF MERGERS AND ACQUISITIONS THAT FURTHER THE COMPANY'S BUSINESS GROWTH STRATEGY.
