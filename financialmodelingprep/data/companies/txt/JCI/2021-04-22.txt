JOHNSON CONTROLS PARTNERS WITH INTERNATIONAL WELL BUILDING INSTITUTE; FORMS COMPLEMENTARY HEALTHY BUILDINGS CENTER OF EXCELLENCE. MILWAUKEE, APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  JOHNSON CONTROLS  (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP WITH THE INTERNATIONAL WELL BUILDING INSTITUTE™ (IWBI™), A TRAILBLAZER IN THE PEOPLE-FIRST APPROACH TO BUILDINGS AND COMMUNITIES, TO PROMOTE GLOBAL APPLICATION OF THE WELL BUILDING STANDARD™ (WELL™). LEADING THE BUILDINGS SPACE WITH MORE THAN 135 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE, JOHNSON CONTROLS IS IDEALLY POSITIONED WITH TECHNOLOGIES AND SOLUTIONS THAT ACCELERATE THE REINVENTION OF HEALTHY BUILDINGS.
