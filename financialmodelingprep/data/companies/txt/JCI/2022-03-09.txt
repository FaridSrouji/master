JOHNSON CONTROLS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CORK, IRELAND, MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC (NYSE: JCI) APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.35 PER COMMON SHARE, AN INCREASE OF $0.01 FROM LAST QUARTER, PAYABLE ON APRIL 14, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 21, 2022. JOHNSON CONTROLS HAS PAID A CONSECUTIVE DIVIDEND SINCE 1887.
