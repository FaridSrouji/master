JOHNSON CONTROLS AI-ENHANCED OPENBLUE PLATFORM CUTS MICROSOFT'S BEIJING CAMPUS ENERGY FOOTPRINT. JOHNSON CONTROLS HELPS MICROSOFT BEIJING CAMPUS  ACHIEVE ENERGY SAVINGS OF 27.9% THROUGH CONTINUOUS RETROFITTING AND OPTIMIZATION, DELIVERING ON SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS POWERED BY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI), OPENBLUE SUITE OF CONNECTED SOLUTIONS PLAYS A KEY ROLE IN ACHIEVING LEAN CAMPUS MANAGEMENT WITH HIGHER ENERGY EFFICIENCY AND BETTER USER EXPERIENCE PROFESSIONAL SERVICE BRINGS MORE THAN 98% UPTIME OF KEY EQUIPMENT AND INCREASE THE LEVEL OF OPERATIONAL AUTOMATION SHANGHAI , AUG. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, HAS PARTNERED WITH MICROSOFT BEIJING CAMPUS FOR ITS ONGOING RETROFIT AND OPTIMIZATION OF BUILDING OPERATIONS, ACHIEVING 27.9% ENERGY SAVINGS AND ENSURING KEY EQUIPMENT UPTIME TO 98%. AS A RESULT, THE CAMPUS HAS BEEN GRANTED AN ENERGY SAVING ENDORSEMENT AND FINANCIAL SUBSIDY BY THE BEIJING MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENT AND THE HAIDIAN DISTRICT GOVERNMENT.
