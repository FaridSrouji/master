SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS BY JOHNSON CONTROLS RELEASES SPRING 2021 U.S. CONSUMER SENTIMENT SURVEY. NEUHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JOHNSON CONTROLS, THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS, ITS LEADING GLOBAL RETAIL SOLUTIONS PORTFOLIO, RELEASED THE RESULTS OF ITS SPRING 2021 U.S. CONSUMER SENTIMENT SURVEY. THE SURVEY REVEALED A SHIFT TOWARDS RESTORED CONFIDENCE AMONG CONSUMERS TO SHOP IN-STORE ONE YEAR INTO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
