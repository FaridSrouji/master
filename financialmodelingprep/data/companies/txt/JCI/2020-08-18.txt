JOHNSON CONTROLS SAYS OLIVIER LEONETTI TO SUCCEED BRIAN STIEF AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & CFO. JOHNSON CONTROLS ANNOUNCES THAT OLIVIER LEONETTI WILL SUCCEED BRIAN STIEF AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, AS PART OF PLANNED TRANSITION.JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC - STIEF WILL REMAIN IN HIS ROLE AS VICE CHAIRMAN.JOHNSON CONTROLS - MOST RECENTLY, LEONETTI SERVED AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CFO FOR ZEBRA TECHNOLOGIES.
