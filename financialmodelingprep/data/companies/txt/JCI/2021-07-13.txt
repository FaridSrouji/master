JOHNSON CONTROLS LAUNCHES COMMUNITY COLLEGE PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM, INVESTING IN THE TECHNICIANS OF TOMORROW. MILWAUKEE, JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, TODAY LAUNCHED THE JOHNSON CONTROLS COMMUNITY COLLEGE PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM. AS PART OF THE PROGRAM, JOHNSON CONTROLS WILL GIVE $15 MILLION OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS TO SUPPORT ACADEMIC PROGRAMS AT NONPROFIT COMMUNITY COLLEGES.
