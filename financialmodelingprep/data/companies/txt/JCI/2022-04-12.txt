JOHNSON CONTROLS ANNUAL ENERGY EFFICIENCY INDICATOR SURVEY REVEALS INVESTMENTS IN SUSTAINABILITY HAVE REBOUNDED TO PRE-PANDEMIC LEVELS, YET CHALLENGES REMAIN. DUE TO ELECTRIFICATION, INVESTMENTS IN ENERGY GENERATION OR STORAGE HAVE FLOURISHED, WITH A GROWING FOCUS ON HEAT PUMP TECHNOLOGY. U.K., FRANCE, JAPAN AND US LEAD REST OF THE WORLD IN PLANS TO INCREASE INVESTMENT IN ENERGY EFFICIENCY, RENEWABLE ENERGY OR SMART BUILDING TECHNOLOGY.
