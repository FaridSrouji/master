SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS BY JOHNSON CONTROLS RELEASES 2021 NORTH AMERICA HOLIDAY SHOPPING RECAP BASED ON SHOPPER TRAFFIC ANALYTICS. NEUHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SENSORMATIC SOLUTIONS, THE LEADING GLOBAL RETAIL SOLUTIONS PORTFOLIO OF JOHNSON CONTROLS, TODAY RELEASED ITS ANNUAL TRAFFIC AND BEHAVIOR TRENDS RECAP FOR THE 2021 HOLIDAY SEASON FOR THE U.S. AND CANADA. THIS INCLUDES THE PERIOD OF SIX WEEKS SPANNING FROM THE SUNDAY BEFORE THANKSGIVING DAY, NOV. 21, 2021, THROUGH SATURDAY, JAN. 1, 2022.
