MRM HEALTH AND IFF REACH SECOND MILESTONE IN THE PARTNERED TYPE 2 DIABETES PROGRAM. GHENT, BELGIUM--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MRM HEALTH—A CLINICAL-STAGE BIOPHARMACEUTICAL COMPANY FOCUSED ON DEVELOPING NEXT-GENERATION LIVE MICROBIOME CONSORTIA THERAPEUTICS—TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS TYPE-2 DIABETES PROGRAM, DEVELOPED IN COLLABORATION WITH IFF (NYSE: IFF) HAS ACHIEVED PRECLINICAL DEVELOPMENT TARGETS. WITH THE ACHIEVEMENT OF THIS NEW MILESTONE, BOTH COMPANIES WILL NOW ADVANCE THIS RESEARCH WHICH FOCUSES ON DEVELOPING INNOVATIVE THERAPIES FOR METABOLIC DISEASES TO THE CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT STAGE.
