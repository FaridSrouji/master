IFF ANNOUNCES THE SALE OF ITS SAVORY SOLUTIONS GROUP TO PAI PARTNERS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--IFF (NYSE: IFF) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS SAVORY SOLUTIONS GROUP TO PAI PARTNERS, A LEADING GLOBAL PRIVATE EQUITY FIRM WITH A STRONG FOCUS ON THE FOOD AND CONSUMER INDUSTRY. THE TRANSACTION VALUES THE BUSINESS AT APPROXIMATELY $900 MILLION AND CASH PROCEEDS NET OF TAXES AND EXPENSES WILL BE PRIMARILY USED TO REDUCE OUTSTANDING DEBT.
