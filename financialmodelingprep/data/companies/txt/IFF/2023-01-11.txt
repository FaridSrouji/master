IFF RECOGNIZED FOR ACHIEVING SUSTAINABILITY LEADERSHIP THROUGHOUT TRANSFORMATIONAL YEAR. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--IFF (NYSE:IFF) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS QUALIFIED FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR AS A CONSTITUENT OF THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDICES (DJSI), A FAMILY OF BENCHMARKS WIDELY REGARDED AS ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING TOOLS FOR INVESTORS TO INTEGRATE SUSTAINABILITY CONSIDERATIONS INTO THEIR PORTFOLIOS TO INCREASE LONG-TERM SHAREHOLDER VALUE. NAMED TO BOTH THE 2022 WORLD INDEX AND THE NORTH AMERICA INDEX, IFF IS THE ONLY COMPANY IN ITS INDUSTRY TO BE INCLUDED.
