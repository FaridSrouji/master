IFF INVESTS $87 MILLION IN INDONESIA TO SUPPORT GROWTH IN GREATER ASIA. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IFF (NYSE: IFF) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY INVESTED A TOTAL OF $87 MILLION IN THEIR NEWLY EXTENDED FLAVORS MANUFACTURING FACILITY IN KARAWANG, INDONESIA DURING THE SITE'S VIRTUAL OPENING. THE FACILITY, LOCATED IN KARAWANG INTERNATIONAL INDUSTRIAL CITY, FIRST STARTED OPERATIONS IN 2015 TO ADDRESS THE FAST-GROWING DEMAND FOR FLAVOR TECHNOLOGY IN THE REGION.
