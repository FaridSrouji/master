IFF SCENT DIVISION LAUNCHES SCIENCE OF WELLNESS PROGRAM. NEUILLY-SUR-SEINE, FRANCE, MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ON WORLD MEDITATION DAY, IFF'S (NYSE:IFF) SCENT DIVISION HAS LAUNCHED ITS SCIENCE OF WELLNESS PROGRAM TO ADDRESS CONSUMER DESIRES FOR A MORE HOLISTIC APPROACH TO THEIR WELLBEING. THE PROGRAM, WHICH BUILDS ON THE COMPANY'S MORE THAN FORTY YEARS OF RESEARCH IN FRAGRANCE AND ITS IMPACT ON EMOTION AND WELLNESS, IS DESIGNED TO GUIDE THE CREATION OF SCENTS THAT SUPPORT SCIENCE-BASED CONSUMER WELLBEING, WITH EMOTIONAL, COGNITIVE AND PHYSICAL BENEFITS.
