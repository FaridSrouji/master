IFF TO SHOWCASE RENEWABLE INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS AND POLYMER PORTFOLIO AT AMERICAN COATINGS SHOW. WILMINGTON, DEL., MARCH 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IFF'S INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS WILL BRING ITS DEEP TECHNICAL EXPERTISE, COMMITMENT TO QUALITY AND BROAD PORTFOLIO OF RENEWABLE POLYMERS TO THE AMERICAN COATINGS SHOW (ACS) FOR THE FIRST TIME ON APRIL 5-7.
