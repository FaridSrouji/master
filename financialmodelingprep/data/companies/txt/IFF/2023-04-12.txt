IFF MOURNS THE PASSING OF BOARD CHAIR DALE F. MORRISON. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WITH HEAVY HEARTS AND DEEP SADNESS, IFF (NYSE: IFF) SHARES THE RECENT PASSING OF DALE F. MORRISON, CHAIR OF THE IFF BOARD. MORRISON SERVED MORE THAN A DECADE AS A MEMBER OF THE IFF BOARD, INCLUDING AS CHAIR SINCE FEBRUARY 2022.
