IFF'S PHILIPPE HORVATH INDUCTED INTO THE NATIONAL INVENTORS HALL OF FAME. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PHILIPPE HORVATH—RENOWNED MOLECULAR BIOLOGIST AND IFF TECHNICAL FELLOW—IS BEING INDUCTED INTO THE NATIONAL INVENTORS HALL OF FAME® CLASS OF 2023. HORVATH WILL BE FORMALLY HONORED FOR TWO PATENTS RELATED TO CRISPR (CLUSTERED REGULARLY INTERSPACED SHORT PALINDROMIC REPEATS) SEQUENCES AND ASSOCIATED CAS PROTEINS, THAT CONSTITUTE AN ACQUIRED IMMUNE SYSTEM IN BACTERIA. APPLYING THIS RESEARCH TO ENHANCE STARTER CULTURES IN THE DAIRY INDUSTRY, HORVATH AND RESEARCH PARTNER RODOLPHE BARRANGOU IMPROVED THE WORLD'S FOOD SUPPLY AND LAID THE FOUNDATION FOR THE FIELD OF GENE EDITING. THE FORMAL INDUCTION WILL TAKE PLACE DURING A CEREMONY IN OCTOBER 2023 IN WASHINGTON, D.C.
