THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC OPENS CELL THERAPY FACILITY AT UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA, SAN FRANCISCO, TO ACCELERATE DEVELOPMENT OF BREAKTHROUGH THERAPIES. WALTHAM, MASS. & SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC. (NYSE: TMO), THE WORLD LEADER IN SERVING SCIENCE, AND THE UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA, SAN FRANCISCO (UCSF), WILL ACCELERATE ADVANCED CELL THERAPIES FOR DIFFICULT TO TREAT CONDITIONS, INCLUDING CANCER, RARE DISEASES, AND OTHER ILLNESSES, FROM A NEWLY OPENED CGMP MANUFACTURING FACILITY ADJACENT TO UCSF MEDICAL CENTER'S MISSION BAY CAMPUS.
