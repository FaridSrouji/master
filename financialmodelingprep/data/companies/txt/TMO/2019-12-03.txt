THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC. OPENS ITS NEW $90 MILLION VIRAL VECTOR CDMO SITE IN LEXINGTON, MASS. THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC. OPENED ITS NEW $90 MILLION VIRAL VECTOR CDMO (CONTRACT DEVELOPMENT AND MANUFACTURING ORGANIZATION) SITE IN LEXINGTON, MASS. THE 50,000-SQUARE-FOOT FACILITY WILL ADD MORE THAN 200 JOBS AND SUPPORT THE DEVELOPMENT, TESTING AND MANUFACTURE OF VIRAL VECTORS, WHICH ARE CRITICAL TO ADVANCING NEW LIFE-SAVING GENE AND CELL THERAPIES GLOBALLY.
