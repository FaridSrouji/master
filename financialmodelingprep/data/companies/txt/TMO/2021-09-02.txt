THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC AWARDED $192.5 MILLION U.S. GOVERNMENT CONTRACT TO EXPAND DOMESTIC PIPETTE TIP MANUFACTURING. WALTHAM, MASS., SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC. (NYSE: TMO), THE WORLD LEADER IN SERVING SCIENCE, TODAY ANNOUNCED A $192.5 MILLION CONTRACT AWARD FROM THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE (DOD) TO ENSURE RELIABLE DOMESTIC PRODUCTION OF PIPETTE TIPS, WHICH ARE USED WITHIN RESEARCH AND DIAGNOSTIC LABS TO DISPENSE PRECISE AMOUNTS OF LIQUID.
