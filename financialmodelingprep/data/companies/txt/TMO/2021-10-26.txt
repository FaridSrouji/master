THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC PUBLISHES 2020 CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY REPORT. WALTHAM, MASS., OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC.
