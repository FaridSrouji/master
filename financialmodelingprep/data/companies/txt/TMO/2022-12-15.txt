THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC'S ADVANCED ANALYTICAL INSTRUMENTS RECEIVE R&D 100 AWARDS. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC., THE WORLD LEADER IN SERVING SCIENCE, RECEIVED R&D 100 AWARDS FROM R&D WORLD MAGAZINE FOR TWO OF ITS MOST RECENTLY LAUNCHED ANALYTICAL INSTRUMENTS. THE R&D 100 AWARDS COMPETITION HONORS THE LATEST INNOVATIONS IN SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY, IDENTIFYING THE TOP 100 REVOLUTIONARY TECHNOLOGIES OF THE PAST YEAR.
