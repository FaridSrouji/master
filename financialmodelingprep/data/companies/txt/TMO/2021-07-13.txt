THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC AND MY GREEN LAB COLLABORATE TO EXPAND USE OF ACT LABEL IN DRIVING ENVIRONMENTALLY SUSTAINABLE LAB PRACTICES. WALTHAM, MASS., JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC. (NYSE:TMO), THE WORLD LEADER IN SERVING SCIENCE, TODAY ANNOUNCED AN EXPANSION OF ITS COLLABORATION WITH MY GREEN LAB, A NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION COMMITTED TO IMPROVING THE ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY OF SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH.
