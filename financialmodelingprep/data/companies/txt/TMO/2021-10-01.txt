THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC OPENS BIOLOGICS MANUFACTURING SITE IN LENGNAU, SWITZERLAND. WALTHAM, MASS., OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC. (NYSE:TMO), THE WORLD LEADER IN SERVING SCIENCE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ASSUMED OPERATIONAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR A NEW BIOLOGICS MANUFACTURING SITE IN LENGNAU, SWITZERLAND AS PART OF ITS STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH CSL LIMITED ANNOUNCED LAST YEAR.
