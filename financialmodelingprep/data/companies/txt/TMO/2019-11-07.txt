THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYABLE ON JANUARY 15, 2020. THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.19 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JANUARY 15, 2020, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 17, 2019.
