SHELDON THORPE ELECTED VICE PRESIDENT OF HUMAN RESOURCES. BERWYN, PA., AUG. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMETEK, INC. (NYSE: AME) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED SHELDON THORPE AS VICE PRESIDENT OF HUMAN RESOURCES.
AMETEK DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. BERWYN, PA., AUG. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMETEK, INC. (NYSE: AME) DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.20 PER SHARE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2021.
