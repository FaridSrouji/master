AMETEK APPOINTS CHRISTOPH MAETZIG VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER, ULTRA PRECISION TECHNOLOGIES DIVISION; BRUCE WILSON TO RETIRE FOLLOWING 22 YEARS OF SERVICE. BERWYN, PA., DEC. 6, 2021  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMETEK, INC. (NYSE: AME) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DR. CHRISTOPH MAETZIG AS VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER, AMETEK ULTRA PRECISION TECHNOLOGIES DIVISION.
