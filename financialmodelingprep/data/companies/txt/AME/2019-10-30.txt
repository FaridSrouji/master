AMETEK INC. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. AMETEK INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED SALES WAS USD 1,276.633 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,192.962 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 301.056 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 265.266 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 220.749 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 191.213 MILLION A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.96 COMPARED TO USD 0.82 A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 0.97 COMPARED TO USD 0.83 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, SALES WAS USD 3,853.736 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 3,574.544 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 879.725 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 793.520 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 640.520 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 566.413 MILLION A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.79 COMPARED TO USD 2.43 A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.82 COMPARED TO USD 2.45 A YEAR AGO.
