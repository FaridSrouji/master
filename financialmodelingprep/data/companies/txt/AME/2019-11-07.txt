AMETEK, INC. DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER ENDING DECEMBER 31, 2019, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 20, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AMETEK, INC. DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.14 PER SHARE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER ENDING DECEMBER 31, 2019. THIS FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DECEMBER 20, 2019 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 6, 2019.
