DUKE ENERGY SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS ENTERS MISSISSIPPI WITH ACQUISITION OF 100-MW WILDFLOWER SOLAR FROM CLEARWAY ENERGY GROUP. TOYOTA NORTH AMERICA HAS SIGNED A 15-YEAR PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR THE SOLAR ENERGY PRODUCED BY THE PROJECT CHARLOTTE, N.C. , NOV. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS IS EXPANDING ITS SOLAR ENERGY PORTFOLIO BY ACQUIRING THE 100-MEGAWATT (MW) WILDFLOWER SOLAR PROJECT FROM CLEARWAY ENERGY GROUP.
