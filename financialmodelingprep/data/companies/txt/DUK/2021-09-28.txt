DUKE ENERGY PROVIDES $750,000 IN GRANTS ACROSS NORTH CAROLINA FOR SMALL-BUSINESS REVITALIZATION. CHARLOTTE, N.C., SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE DUKE ENERGY FOUNDATION TODAY DISTRIBUTED $750,000 IN GRANTS TO HELP LOCAL BUSINESSES ACROSS NORTH CAROLINA – FROM RESTAURANTS TO RETAILERS – ADAPT TO THE UNPRECEDENTED CHALLENGES CAUSED BY THE PANDEMIC.
