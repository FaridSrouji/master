DUKE ENERGY CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AND SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK, PAYABLE ON DEC. 16, 2019. DUKE ENERGY CORPORATION DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $0.945 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON DEC. 16, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS NOV. 15, 2019. THE COMPANY ALSO DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK OF $359.375 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON DEC. 16, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS NOV. 15, 2019. THIS IS EQUIVALENT TO $0.359375 PER DEPOSITARY SHARE.
