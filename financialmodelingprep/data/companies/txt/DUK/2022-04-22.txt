INDIANA FIRST LADY JANET HOLCOMB JOINS DUKE ENERGY VOLUNTEERS TO PLANT 5,000 TREES AT RAVINIA STATE FOREST. PLAINFIELD, IND. , APRIL 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INDIANA FIRST LADY JANET HOLCOMB, ALONG WITH INDIANA DEPARTMENT OF NATURAL RESOURCES (DNR) DIRECTOR DAN BORTNER AND INDIANA NATURAL RESOURCES FOUNDATION (INRF) EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR JODY KRESS, TODAY JOINED DUKE ENERGY INDIANA STATE PRESIDENT STAN PINEGAR AND COMPANY EMPLOYEES TO PLANT 5,000 TREES AT RAVINIA STATE FOREST IN MORGAN COUNTY.
