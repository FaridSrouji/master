DUKE ENERGY NAMED ONE OF FORTUNE'S 'WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES' FOR FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. CHARLOTTE, N.C., FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN RECOGNITION OF THE GREAT PROGRESS THE COMPANY HAS MADE AGAINST ITS STRATEGIC AND FINANCIAL GOALS, DUKE ENERGY (NYSE: DUK) WAS ONCE AGAIN NAMED TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S 2022 LIST OF THE WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES FOR THE FIFTH YEAR IN A ROW.
