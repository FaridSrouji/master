DUKE ENERGY BEGINS OPERATING 207-MW LEDYARD WINDPOWER, ITS FIRST WIND PROJECT IN IOWA. VERIZON COMMUNICATIONS HAS ENTERED INTO A 180-MEGAWATT VIRTUAL POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT COMPANY AWARDS $60,000 GRANT TO SUPPORT THE LEDYARD FIRE DEPARTMENT CHARLOTTE, N.C. , JAN. 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS (DESS), A NONREGULATED COMMERCIAL BRAND OF DUKE ENERGY (NYSE: DUK), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS PLACED INTO COMMERCIAL OPERATION THE 207-MEGAWATT (MW) LEDYARD WINDPOWER PROJECT IN KOSSUTH COUNTY, IOWA.
