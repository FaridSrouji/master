DUKE ENERGY RENEWABLES ANNOUNCES COMMERCIAL OPERATION OF ITS LARGEST WINDPOWER PROJECT - 350-MW FRONTIER II IN OKLAHOMA. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY RENEWABLES, A COMMERCIAL BUSINESS UNIT OF DUKE ENERGY (NYSE: DUK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMMERCIAL OPERATION OF THE LARGEST WINDPOWER PROJECT IN ITS FLEET – THE 350-MEGAWATT (MW) FRONTIER WINDPOWER II PROJECT IN KAY COUNTY, OKLA.
