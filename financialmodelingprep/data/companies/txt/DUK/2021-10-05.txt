PIEDMONT NATURAL GAS PREPARES CUSTOMERS FOR HIGHER WINTER BILLS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH SEASONAL COLD WEATHER ON THE HORIZON, PAIRED WITH AN INCREASE IN THE COST OF NATURAL GAS CUSTOMERS ARE FACING ACROSS THE GLOBE, PIEDMONT NATURAL GAS IS REMINDING CUSTOMERS OF TIPS, TOOLS AND ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS AVAILABLE TO HELP CUSTOMERS PREPARE FOR HIGHER WINTER BILLS.
