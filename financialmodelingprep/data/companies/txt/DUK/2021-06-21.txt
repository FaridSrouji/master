DUKE ENERGY SEEKS SUBSEQUENT LICENSE RENEWAL FOR OCONEE NUCLEAR STATION. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY HAS FILED AN APPLICATION WITH THE U.S. NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION (NRC) TO RENEW OCONEE NUCLEAR STATION'S OPERATING LICENSES FOR AN ADDITIONAL 20 YEARS.
