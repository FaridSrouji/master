DUKE ENERGY NAMES KENDAL BOWMAN NEW NORTH CAROLINA STATE PRESIDENT, SUCCEEDING STEPHEN DE MAY. BOWMAN WILL LEAD STATE AND LOCAL REGULATORY AND GOVERNMENT RELATIONS, AND COMMUNITY AFFAIRS DE MAY TO RETIRE AFTER 33 YEARS OF SERVICE CHARLOTTE, N.C. , NOV. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KENDAL BOWMAN WILL BECOME NORTH CAROLINA STATE PRESIDENT, EFFECTIVE JAN. 1.
