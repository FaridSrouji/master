DUKE ENERGY PREPARES FLORIDA CUSTOMERS FOR HIGHER WINTER ENERGY BILLS. ST. PETERSBURG, FLA., DEC. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH SEASONAL WINTER WEATHER ON THE HORIZON AND AN INCREASE IN THE COST OF ENERGY ACROSS THE GLOBE, DUKE ENERGY IS REMINDING FLORIDA CUSTOMERS OF AVAILABLE TIPS, TOOLS AND ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS TO HELP THEM PREPARE FOR HIGHER WINTER BILLS.
