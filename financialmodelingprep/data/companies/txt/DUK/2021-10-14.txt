DUKE ENERGY FOUNDATION AWARDS $190,000 IN GRANTS TO SUPPORT ENVIRONMENTAL INITIATIVES IN INDIANA COMMUNITIES. PLAINFIELD, IND., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE DUKE ENERGY FOUNDATION HAS AWARDED 17 GRANTS TOTALING $190,000 TO PROGRAMS THAT SUPPORT A WIDE RANGE OF ENVIRONMENTAL INITIATIVES ACROSS INDIANA, INCLUDING PROJECTS TO SUPPORT WATER QUALITY, CONSERVATION, AND HABITAT AND FOREST RESTORATION.
