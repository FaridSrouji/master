DUKE ENERGY INDIANA FILES PLAN TO IMPROVE RELIABILITY AND RESILIENCE OF ITS STATEWIDE ELECTRIC GRID WITH INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGY. PLAINFIELD, IND., NOV. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY INDIANA FILED A PLAN TUESDAY WITH INDIANA STATE UTILITY REGULATORS TO IMPROVE THE RELIABILITY AND RESILIENCE OF ITS STATEWIDE NETWORK OF POWER LINES AND INFRASTRUCTURE THAT DELIVER ELECTRICITY TO MORE THAN 860,000 HOOSIER HOMES, BUSINESSES AND INDUSTRIES.
