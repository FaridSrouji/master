DUKE ENERGY ESTABLISHES RELATIONSHIP WITH OPTUS BANK TO SUPPORT MINORITY-OWNED BUSINESSES. GREENVILLE, S.C., JAN. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY (NYSE: DUK) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS DEPOSITED $5 MILLION INTO OPTUS BANK, A BLACK-OWNED BANK BASED IN SOUTH CAROLINA, FURTHERING THE COMPANY'S SUPPORT FOR DIVERSE AND MINORITY-OWNED BUSINESSES, INDIVIDUALS AND LOW INCOME...
