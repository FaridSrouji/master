DUKE ENERGY ANNOUNCES KEY LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENTS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY (NYSE: DUK) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS EXPANDING THE COMPANY'S SENIOR MANAGEMENT TEAM AND REALIGNING ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES TO FURTHER ACCELERATE ITS CLEAN ENERGY TRANSITION AND BEST POSITION THE COMPANY TO GROW AND SERVE ITS CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES.
