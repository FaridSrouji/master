DUKE ENERGY BUILDS PIPELINE OF WORKERS FOR INDIANA BUSINESSES WITH $280,000 IN WORKFORCE TRAINING GRANTS. PLAINFIELD, IND., AUG. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --THE DUKE ENERGY FOUNDATION HAS AWARDED APPROXIMATELY $280,000 IN GRANTS TO 11 WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT AND EDUCATION PROGRAMS IN INDIANA TO HELP CREATE A PIPELINE OF WORKERS PREPARED FOR EMPLOYMENT.
