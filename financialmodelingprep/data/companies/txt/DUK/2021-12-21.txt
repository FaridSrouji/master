PIEDMONT NATURAL GAS SEEKS APPROVAL TO REDUCE CUSTOMER BILLING RATES IN THE CAROLINAS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., DEC. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PIEDMONT NATURAL GAS LATE LAST WEEK FILED REQUESTS WITH THE UTILITIES COMMISSIONS IN NORTH CAROLINA AND SOUTH CAROLINA TO REDUCE CUSTOMER BILLING RATES DUE TO A DECREASE IN THE MARKET COST OF NATURAL GAS.
