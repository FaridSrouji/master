DUKE ENERGY CAROLINAS SUBMITS ANNUAL UPDATE FOR CHARGES RELATED TO FUEL IN SOUTH CAROLINA. INCREASED DEMAND FOLLOWING COVID-19 SHUTDOWN PAIRED WITH SHARPLY RISING FUEL COSTS, WHICH DUKE ENERGY DOES NOT PROFIT ON, WILL INCREASE CUSTOMER PRICES IF APPROVED, CUSTOMERS WILL SEE THEIR MONTHLY BILLS INCREASE STARTING IN OCTOBER GREENVILLE, S.C. , AUG. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY CAROLINAS MADE ITS ANNUAL FILING JULY 29 WITH THE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION OF SOUTH CAROLINA (PSCSC) FOR COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH THE FUEL USED TO GENERATE ELECTRICITY AT ITS POWER PLANTS.
