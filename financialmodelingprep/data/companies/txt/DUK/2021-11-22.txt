DUKE ENERGY, SOUTH CAROLINA STATE UNIVERSITY OFFER $150,000 IN SCHOLARSHIPS TO SUPPORT NUCLEAR ENGINEERING STUDENTS. GREENVILLE, S.C., NOV. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY TODAY ANNOUNCED $150,000 IN STUDENT SCHOLARSHIPS TO SOUTH CAROLINA STATE UNIVERSITY THAT WILL HELP TO CREATE A DIVERSE PIPELINE OF TALENT FOR THE NUCLEAR WORKFORCE OF THE 21ST CENTURY.
