DUKE ENERGY COMPLETES RESTORATION TO ALL CUSTOMERS IN MOORE COUNTY AND SURROUNDING COUNTIES. WEEKEND ATTACK DISABLED TWO LOCAL SUBSTATIONS RESTORATION COMPLETED A DAY EARLIER THAN PROJECTED CHARLOTTE, N.C. , DEC. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY HAS RESTORED POWER TO ALL CUSTOMERS IN MOORE COUNTY AND THE SURROUNDING COUNTIES AFFECTED BY AN ATTACK ON SATURDAY ON TWO MAJOR SUBSTATIONS.
