CLEAN GENERATION REMAINS DUKE ENERGY'S FOCUS AS IT PLANS TO TRIPLE RENEWABLE ENERGY THIS DECADE. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BY THE END OF THIS DECADE, RENEWABLES WILL MAKE UP A LARGE PORTION OF THE COMPANY'S GENERATION MIX AS DUKE ENERGY (NYSE: DUK) LOOKS TO TRIPLE THE AMOUNT OF RENEWABLE ENERGY IT PRODUCES FROM COMPANY POWER PLANTS AND DRAMATICALLY REDUCE CARBON EMISSIONS.
