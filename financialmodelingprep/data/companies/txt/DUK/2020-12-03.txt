DUKE ENERGY IMPLEMENTS TRELLIS PLATFORM TO IMPROVE NATURAL GAS FORECASTING AND PURCHASING FOR LARGE INDUSTRIAL CUSTOMERS. CHARLOTTE, N.C. & SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #TRELLIS--TRELLIS, THE LEADING CLOUD SOLUTION DIGITIZING THE NATURAL GAS INDUSTRY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DUKE ENERGY IS LIVE ON ITS ETMS PLATFORM.
