DUKE ENERGY FOUNDATION SUPPORTS RESILIENCY AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE EFFORTS WITH $523,000 IN FLORIDA GRANTS. ST. PETERSBURG, FLA., DEC. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE DUKE ENERGY FOUNDATION HAS AWARDED $523,000 IN GRANTS TO SUPPORT A WIDE RANGE OF EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS EFFORTS, AS WELL AS DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION INITIATIVES THROUGHOUT FLORIDA.
