DUKE ENERGY SUPPORTS NORTH CAROLINA COMMUNITIES WITH MORE THAN $750,000 IN GRANTS TO SUPPORT EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS. FUNDING WILL SUPPORT 24 NONPROFITS AND GOVERNMENT AGENCIES FROM ACROSS THE STATE GRANTS WILL PAY FOR TRAINING, LIFE-SAVING EQUIPMENT AND ESSENTIAL TOOLS TO AID IN DISASTER PLANNING AND RECOVERY OPERATIONS RALEIGH, N.C. , DEC. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN THE WAKE OF ANOTHER ACTIVE STORM SEASON IN THE CAROLINAS, FIRST RESPONDERS AND EMERGENCY MANAGERS ARE LOOKING AHEAD TO PREPARE THEIR COMMUNITIES FOR THE CONTINUED IMPACTS OF SEVERE WEATHER.
