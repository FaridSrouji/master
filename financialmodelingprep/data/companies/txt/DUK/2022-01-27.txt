DUKE ENERGY'S SOLAR PORTFOLIO GROWS IN NORTH CAROLINA WITH THE ADDITION OF TWO NEW PLANTS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONTINUING ITS EFFORTS TO GROW RENEWABLE ENERGY IN NORTH CAROLINA, DUKE ENERGY SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS, A NONREGULATED COMMERCIAL BRAND OF DUKE ENERGY (NYSE: DUK), RECENTLY BEGAN COMMERCIAL OPERATION OF TWO MAJOR SOLAR FACILITIES IN THE STATE.
