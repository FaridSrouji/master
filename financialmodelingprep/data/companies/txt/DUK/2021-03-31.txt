DUKE ENERGY AWARDS $375,000 IN GRANTS TO SOUTH CAROLINA TUTORING PROGRAMS, EDUCATION INITIATIVES IMPACTED BY PANDEMIC. GREENVILLE, S.C., MARCH 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY THE RECIPIENTS OF $375,000 IN GRANTS THROUGH THE DUKE ENERGY FOUNDATION TO SOUTH CAROLINA ORGANIZATIONS THAT WILL FUND TUTORING AND READING PROGRAMS IN UNDERSERVED AND MINORITY COMMUNITIES TO COMBAT LEARNING GAPS CREATED BY THE PANDEMIC.
