DUKE ENERGY COMMITS MORE THAN $400,000 IN MONTHLONG FOCUS TO SUPPORT SUSTAINABILITY AND ENVIRONMENTAL EFFORTS IN SOUTH CAROLINA. THROUGH APRIL, COMPANY WILL INVEST IN ORGANIZATIONS, PROGRAMS THAT FOSTER VIBRANT COMMUNITIES AND ENHANCE NATURAL RESOURCES OF STATE. THIS WEEK'S FOCUS IS ON PALMETTOPRIDE – STATE'S ANTI-LITTER AND BEAUTIFICATION ORGANIZATION.
