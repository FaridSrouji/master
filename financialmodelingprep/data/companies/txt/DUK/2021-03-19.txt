PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION OF SOUTH CAROLINA TO HOLD PUBLIC HEARING ON DUKE ENERGY'S SOLAR CHOICE METERING PROPOSAL. GREENVILLE, S.C., MARCH 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION OF SOUTH CAROLINA (PSCSC) HAS SCHEDULED A VIRTUAL PUBLIC HEARING IN RESPONSE TO REQUESTS BY DUKE ENERGY CAROLINAS (DEC) AND DUKE ENERGY PROGRESS (DEP) FOR APPROVAL OF THE PROPOSED SOLAR CHOICE NET METERING RIDERS AND RATE SCHEDULES.
