DUKE ENERGY SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS ANNOUNCES ITS FIRST WIND ENERGY PROJECT IN IOWA - 207-MW LEDYARD WINDPOWER. CHARLOTTE, N.C., SEPT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS,* A NONREGULATED COMMERCIAL BRAND OF DUKE ENERGY (NYSE: DUK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE 207-MEGAWATT (MW) LEDYARD WINDPOWER PROJECT IN KOSSUTH COUNTY, IOWA.
