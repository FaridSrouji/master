DUKE ENERGY FLORIDA AWARDS $250,000 TO ORGANIZATIONS THAT EMPOWER STUDENTS, EDUCATORS, FAMILIES DURING SEASON OF GIVING. ST. PETERSBURG, FLA., DEC. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY HAS AWARDED $250,000 IN GRANTS TO SUPPORT A WIDE RANGE OF EDUCATION AND COMMUNITY ASSISTANCE INITIATIVES ACROSS FLORIDA.
