DUKE ENERGY OHIO PROPOSES NEW CUSTOMER BENEFITS; REQUESTS ELECTRIC DISTRIBUTION RATE REVIEW AS IT CONTINUES GRID, SERVICE IMPROVEMENTS. CINCINNATI, OCT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS A RESULT OF THE COMPANY'S ONGOING INVESTMENTS TO IMPROVE SOUTHWEST OHIO'S ELECTRICITY INFRASTRUCTURE AND ENHANCE VALUE TO CUSTOMERS, DUKE ENERGY OHIO TODAY ASKED ITS STATE REGULATOR, THE PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION OF OHIO, TO PERFORM A PUBLIC REVIEW OF THE COMPANY'S ELECTRIC DISTRIBUTION RATES AND APPROVE A RATE ADJUSTMENT. OVERALL, THE PROPOSED ADJUSTMENT AMOUNTS TO AN APPROXIMATELY $55 MILLION INCREASE.
