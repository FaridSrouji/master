DUKE ENERGY NAMED ONE OF NORTH AMERICA'S TOP SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES FOR 17TH STRAIGHT YEAR. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , DEC. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS DUKE ENERGY CONTINUES TO MAKE SIGNIFICANT PROGRESS IN DELIVERING THE AFFORDABLE, RELIABLE AND CLEAN ENERGY ITS CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES COUNT ON, THE COMPANY WAS RECENTLY NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX (DJSI) FOR NORTH AMERICA FOR THE 17TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
