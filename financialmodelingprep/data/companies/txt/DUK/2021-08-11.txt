PIEDMONT NATURAL GAS, DUKE ENERGY REMIND COMMUNITIES TO CALL 811 BEFORE DIGGING. CHARLOTTE, N.C., AUG. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN RECOGNITION OF 811 DAY, PIEDMONT NATURAL GAS AND DUKE ENERGY ARE REMINDING THEIR CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES TO CALL 811 BEFORE DIGGING TO PROTECT THE NATION'S UNDERGROUND UTILITY INFRASTRUCTURE, PREVENT POTENTIAL PERSONAL INJURY AND AVOID ELECTRIC AND NATURAL GAS OUTAGES.
