DUKE ENERGY ANNOUNCES FINAL PAYMENTS TO CONTINGENT VALUE OBLIGATION HOLDERS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MARCH 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A SUBSIDIARY OF DUKE ENERGY (NYSE: DUK), PROGRESS ENERGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL DIRECT THE TRUSTEE FOR ITS CONTINGENT VALUE OBLIGATIONS TO PAY HOLDERS OF OUTSTANDING CONTINGENT VALUE OBLIGATIONS (CVO) APPROXIMATELY $1.03 PER CVO.
