NEW PLAZA IN UPTOWN CHARLOTTE TO BECOME DUKE ENERGY'S CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MAY 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DUKE ENERGY PLAZA, THE 40-FLOOR OFFICE TOWER CURRENTLY UNDER CONSTRUCTION IN UPTOWN CHARLOTTE, WILL BECOME THE COMPANY'S NEW CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS.
