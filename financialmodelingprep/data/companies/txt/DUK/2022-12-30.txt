DUKE ENERGY RESPONDS TO CONSTRUCTIVE CARBON PLAN DECISION BY NORTH CAROLINA UTILITIES COMMISSION. CHARLOTTE, N.C. , DEC. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY ISSUED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT IN RESPONSE TO TODAY'S ORDER BY THE NORTH CAROLINA UTILITIES COMMISSION (NCUC) ON ITS CARBON PLAN, AN OUTCOME PRESCRIBED BY NORTH CAROLINA STATE LAW (ENERGY SOLUTIONS FOR NORTH CAROLINA, HB951).
