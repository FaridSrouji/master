EY ANNOUNCES JOHN SHEN OF AMERICAN LENDING CENTER AS AN ENTREPRENEUR OF THE YEAR® 2023 PACIFIC SOUTHWEST AWARD FINALIST. IRVINE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ERNST & YOUNG ANNOUNCED THAT JOHN SHEN, CEO OF AMERICAN LENDING CENTER, WAS NAMED AN ENTREPRENEUR OF THE YEAR® 2023 PACIFIC SOUTHWEST AWARD FINALIST.
