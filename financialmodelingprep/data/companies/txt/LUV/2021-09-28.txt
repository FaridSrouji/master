SOUTHWEST AIRLINES AND WALT DISNEY WORLD RESORT CELEBRATE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF BOTH ICONIC BRANDS WITH COMMEMORATIVE AIRCRAFT. DALLAS, SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) JOINS WALT DISNEY WORLD ® RESORT WITH A SPECIAL CO-BRANDED COMMEMORATIVE AIRCRAFT HONORING  50 YEARS OF CONNECTING PEOPLE WITH MEMORIES AND MAGIC, AS EACH COMPANY CELEBRATES A 50TH ANNIVERSARY IN 2021.
