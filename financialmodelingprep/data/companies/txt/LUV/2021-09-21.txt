SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CONTINUES COMPANYWIDE COMMITMENT TOWARD DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION. DALLAS, SEPT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) SHARED AN UPDATE TODAY WITH ITS EMPLOYEES, CUSTOMERS, AND PARTNERS ON THE PROGRESS THE AIRLINE HAS MADE DURING THE LAST YEAR AND ITS NEXT STEPS WITHIN THE DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION (DEI) ROADMAP.
