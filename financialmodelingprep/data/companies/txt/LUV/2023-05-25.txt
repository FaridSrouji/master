EY ANNOUNCES SEAN MINTER, CEO AND FOUNDER OF AMPLIFAI, AS AN ENTREPRENEUR OF THE YEAR® 2023 SOUTHWEST AWARD FINALIST. PLANO, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #CX--ERNST & YOUNG LLP (EY US) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CEO AND FOUNDER, SEAN MINTER OF AMPLIFAI WAS NAMED AN ENTREPRENEUR OF THE YEAR® 2023 SOUTHWEST AWARD FINALIST. THIS IS THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR THAT MINTER HAS BEEN SELECTED AS A FINALIST FOR THIS PRESTIGIOUS AWARD, WHICH RECOGNIZES TRANSFORMATIVE ENTREPRENEURS AND LEADERS OF HIGH-GROWTH COMPANIES WHO ARE BUILDING A MORE EQUITABLE, SUSTAINABLE, AND PROSPEROUS WORLD FOR ALL. MINTER WAS SELECTED BY AN INDEPENDENT.
