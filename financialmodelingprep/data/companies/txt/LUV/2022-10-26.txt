SOUTHWEST AIRLINES' CUSTOMER SERVICE EMPLOYEES TO VOTE ON NEW CONTRACT. DALLAS , OCT. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) (THE "COMPANY") AND THE INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF MACHINISTS AND AEROSPACE WORKERS (IAM) HAVE REACHED A TENTATIVE AGREEMENT FOR A NEW CONTRACT TO COVER THE COMPANY'S MORE THAN 8,000 CUSTOMER SERVICE EMPLOYEES. "OUR GOAL FROM THE BEGINNING HAS BEEN TO REACH AN AGREEMENT THAT REWARDS OUR CUSTOMER SERVICE EMPLOYEES FOR THEIR MANY CONTRIBUTIONS TO SOUTHWEST®, WHILE BEING MINDFUL OF NEEDED EFFICIENCIES TO KEEP OUR COMPANY AGILE AND COMPETITIVE.
