SOUTHWEST AIRLINES NOW OFFERING PRE-CLEARED ARRIVAL INTO HAWAII. DALLAS, MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) HAS INTRODUCED AN ADDED CONVENIENCE FOR CUSTOMERS FLYING TO HAWAII ON SOUTHWEST AIRLINES®—AN ABILITY TO PRESENT EVIDENCE OF THEIR COMPLIANCE WITH THE HAWAII SAFE TRAVELS PROGRAM. CUSTOMERS WHO HAVE UPLOADED AN APPROVED NEGATIVE COVID-19 TEST RESULT, REQUIRED TRAVEL INFORMATION, AND COMPLETED A HEALTH QUESTIONNAIRE BEFORE DEPARTING THE MAINLAND MAY BE ELIGIBLE TO BYPASS AIRPORT SCREENING WHEN ARRIVING IN HONOLULU (OAHU) AND KAHULUI (MAUI).
