SOUTHWEST CLASS ACTION: LEVI & KORSINSKY REMINDS SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. INVESTORS OF THE PENDING CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT WITH A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF MARCH 13, 2023 - LUV. NEW YORK, NEW YORK--(NEWSFILE CORP. - FEBRUARY 7, 2023) - LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. ("SOUTHWEST" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: LUV) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT.THE LAWSUIT ON BEHALF OF SOUTHWEST INVESTORS HAS BEEN COMMENCED IN THE THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE SOUTHERN DISTRICT OF TEXAS. AFFECTED INVESTORS PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED CERTAIN SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. SECURITIES BETWEEN JUNE 13, 2020 AND DECEMBER 31, 2022.
