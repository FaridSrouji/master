SOUTHWEST AIRLINES ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF 4.750% NOTES DUE 2023. DALLAS , AUG. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. (NYSE: LUV) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL REDEEM ALL OF ITS OUTSTANDING 4.750% NOTES DUE 2023 (THE "NOTES"). THE AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OUTSTANDING OF THE NOTES IS $1,234,195,000.
