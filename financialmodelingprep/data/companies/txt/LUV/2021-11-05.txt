DENVER MAYOR DECLARES "SOUTHWEST AIRLINES DAY" TO CELEBRATE 15 YEARS OF SOUTHWEST SERVICE IN THE MILE HIGH CITY. DALLAS, NOV. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) TODAY JOINS IN CELEBRATING A MILESTONE OF SERVING DENVER AS MAYOR MICHAEL B. HANCOCK OFFICIALLY DECLARES NOV. 5, 2021, AS "SOUTHWEST AIRLINES DAY" IN THE CITY.
