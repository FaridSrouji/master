BOOK TODAY: SOUTHWEST AIRLINES EXTENDS FLIGHT SCHEDULE THROUGH APRIL 24, 2022. DALLAS, SEPT. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT EXTENDED ITS BOOKABLE FLIGHT SCHEDULE THROUGH APRIL 24, 2022, GIVING CUSTOMERS THE ABILITY TO PLAN AND BOOK THEIR EARLY 2022 TRAVEL AT SOUTHWEST.COM.
