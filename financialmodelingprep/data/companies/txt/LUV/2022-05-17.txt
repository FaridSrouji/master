SOUTHWEST AIRLINES LAUNCHES WANNA GET AWAY PLUS, A NEW FARE INTRODUCING TRANSFERABLE FLIGHT CREDITS AND MORE FLEXIBILITY. CUSTOMERS CAN NOW BOOK A WANNA GET AWAY PLUS FARE FOR ALL TRAVEL ON SOUTHWEST.COM  AND THE SOUTHWEST AIRLINES® APP.
