SOUTHWEST AIRLINES ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGES. DALLAS, DEC. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED LEADERSHIP CHANGES AND APPOINTMENTS WITHIN VARIOUS DEPARTMENTS OF THE COMPANY.    OPERATIONAL LEADERSHIP JUSTIN JONES IS PROMOTED TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OPERATIONAL STRATEGY & DESIGN, WHERE HE WILL LEAD THE AIRLINE'S MODERNIZATION AND OPERATIONAL IMPROVEMENTS IN THIS NEWLY CREATED ROLE.
