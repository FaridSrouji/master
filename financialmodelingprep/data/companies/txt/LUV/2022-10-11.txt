SOUTHWEST AIRLINES LAUNCHES 'GO WITH HEART' BRAND CAMPAIGN. CARRIER HIGHLIGHTS POINTS OF DIFFERENCE, FLEXIBLE POLICIES, AND ONGOING EFFORTS TOWARD CUSTOMER MODERNIZATION FOR AN ENHANCED ONBOARD EXPERIENCE DALLAS , OCT. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO . (NYSE: LUV) TODAY LAUNCHED A NEW BRAND CAMPAIGN, "GO WITH HEART," SHOWCASING THE CARRIER'S POINTS OF DIFFERENCE, INCLUDING BAGS FLY FREE®, 1 NO CHANGE OR CANCELLATION FEES, 1 POINTS DON'T EXPIRE, 2 AND FLIGHT CREDITS DON'T EXPIRE.
