SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CELEBRATES 15 YEARS OF PROVIDING LIFE-CHANGING MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION. DALLAS, MARCH 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. (NYSE: LUV) IS EMBARKING ON THE NEXT ERA OF ITS LIFE-CHANGING MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION GRANT PROGRAM AS IT CELEBRATES 15 YEARS OF PROVIDING FREE TRAVEL TO FAMILIES ACROSS NORTH AMERICA. THROUGH THE PROGRAM, HOSPITALS AND MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION ORGANIZATIONS ARE ABLE TO PROVIDE ROUND TRIP TICKETS TO PATIENTS AND THEIR CAREGIVERS, EASING THE FINANCIAL BURDEN MANY FACE WHEN IT COMES TO RECEIVING THE CRITICAL MEDICAL CARE THEY NEED.
