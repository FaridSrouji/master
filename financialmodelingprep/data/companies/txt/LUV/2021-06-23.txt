SOUTHWEST ANNOUNCES 2022 EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP PLANS. DALLAS, JUNE 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) ANNOUNCED THAT CHAIRMAN AND CEO GARY KELLY  WILL TRANSITION ROLES IN EARLY 2022, BECOMING THE CARRIER'S EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN WITH THE DESIRE TO SERVE IN THAT ROLE THROUGH AT LEAST 2026 AT THE DISCRETION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS. EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT CORPORATE SERVICES ROBERT JORDAN  WILL BECOME KELLY'S SUCCESSOR AS CEO EFFECTIVE FEB. 1, 2022.
