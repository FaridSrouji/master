SOUTHWEST AIRLINES AND AIRCRAFT MECHANICS FRATERNAL ASSOCIATION REACH TENTATIVE AGREEMENT FOR AIRCRAFT APPEARANCE TECHNICIANS. DALLAS , SEPT. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) AND THE AIRCRAFT MECHANICS FRATERNAL ASSOCIATION (AMFA), THE UNION THAT REPRESENTS SOUTHWEST AIRLINES® AIRCRAFT APPEARANCE TECHNICIANS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE REACHED A TENTATIVE AGREEMENT.
