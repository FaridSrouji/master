SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 5,639 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 5,575 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 819 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 798 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 659 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 615 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.24 COMPARED TO USD 1.08 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 1.23 COMPARED TO USD 1.08 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 16,698 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 16,261 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 2,292 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,386 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 1,787 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,811 MILLION A YEAR AGO. BASIC EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 3.3 COMPARED TO USD 3.13 A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 3.29 COMPARED TO USD 3.13 A YEAR AGO.
