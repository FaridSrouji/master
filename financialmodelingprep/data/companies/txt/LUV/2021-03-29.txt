SOUTHWEST AIRLINES ADDS 100 FIRM ORDERS FOR THE BOEING 737 MAX 7. DALLAS, MARCH 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO .  (NYSE: LUV) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE COMPLETION OF ITS PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED DISCUSSIONS WITH THE BOEING COMPANY (BOEING) REGARDING THE RESTRUCTURING OF ITS DELIVERY SCHEDULE FOR MAX AIRCRAFT.
