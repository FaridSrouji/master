SOUTHWEST AIRLINES ANNOUNCES TWO LEADERSHIP PROMOTIONS. DALLAS, DEC. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED TWO LEADERSHIP PROMOTIONS WITHIN THE TECHNOLOGY DEPARTMENT TO REPLACE RECENTLY VACATED ROLES, WITH BOTH EFFECTIVE JAN. 1, 2022.      KAYCE FORD IS PROMOTED TO VICE PRESIDENT OF ENTERPRISE MANAGEMENT.
