SOUTHWEST AIRLINES JOINS VISION 2045 CAMPAIGN TO HIGHLIGHT ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY EFFORTS. DALLAS, FEB. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO . (NYSE: LUV) (THE "COMPANY") IS EXCITED TO ANNOUNCE IT HAS JOINED THE VISION 2045 CAMPAIGN, A COLLABORATION AMONG MULTIPLE ORGANIZATIONS AND COMPANIES TO SHARE FILMS AND RESOURCES THAT AIM TO INSPIRE BUSINESSES AND PEOPLE TO TAKE ACTION TOWARD A MORE SUSTAINABLE FUTURE.
