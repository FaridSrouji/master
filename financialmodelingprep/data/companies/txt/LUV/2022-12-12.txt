SOUTHWEST AIRLINES ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP PROMOTIONS. DALLAS , DEC. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) TODAY ANNOUNCED TWO LEADERSHIP PROMOTIONS WITHIN ITS INTERNAL AUDIT AND LEGAL DEPARTMENTS, BOTH EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. INTERNAL AUDIT TED GORDON  WAS PROMOTED TO VICE PRESIDENT INTERNAL AUDIT WHERE HE WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR LEADING INTERNAL AUDIT FUNCTIONS.
