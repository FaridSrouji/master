SOUTHWEST AIRLINES EXTENDS FLIGHT SCHEDULE THROUGH MARCH 8, 2023 DALLAS , JULY 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. (NYSE: LUV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ADDITION OF TWO NEW NONSTOP ROUTES FOR THIS UPCOMING WINTER SEASON AS IT EXTENDS ITS FLIGHT SCHEDULE THROUGH MARCH 8, 2023. STARTING IN JANUARY, NEW SEASONAL SERVICE WILL GIVE CUSTOMERS THE ABILITY TO TRAVEL NONSTOP BETWEEN LONG BEACH AND NEW ORLEANS, AND BETWEEN AUSTIN AND MONTROSE, COLO.
