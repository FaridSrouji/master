SOUTHWEST AIRLINES JOINS SABRE PROVIDING INDUSTRY-STANDARD LEVEL OF ACCESS TO CARRIER'S LOW FARES AND NETWORK AS IT WELCOMES BUSINESS TRAVELERS BACK TO THE SKY. DALLAS, JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) ("COMPANY") TODAY BEGAN OFFERING FLIGHTS FOR SALE WITHIN SABRE'S GLOBAL DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM (GDS), THE LEADING CORPORATE BOOKING CHANNEL IN THE US. WITH A ROLLOUT OF THE PARTNERSHIP NOW COMPLETE, SOUTHWEST AIRLINES® FOR THE FIRST TIME IN COMPANY HISTORY OFFERS AN ABILITY FOR BUSINESS TRAVELERS AND TRAVEL DECISION MAKERS TO BOOK SOUTHWEST AND ITS EVERYDAY LOW FARES AT AN INDUSTRY-STANDARD LEVEL OF PARTICIPATION WITHIN ALL THREE MAJOR GDSS.
