INVESTORS OF SOUTHWEST AIRLINES STOCK ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT KEHOE LAW FIRM, P.C. - BREACH OF FIDUCIARY DUTIES INVESTIGATION - LUV. NEW YORK, NEW YORK--(NEWSFILE CORP. - JANUARY 11, 2023) - KEHOE LAW FIRM, P.C. IS INVESTIGATING WHETHER CERTAIN DIRECTORS AND OFFICERS OF SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. ("SOUTHWEST AIRLINES") (NYSE: LUV) BREACHED THEIR FIDUCIARY DUTIES TO SOUTHWEST AIRLINES AND ITS SHAREHOLDERS.
