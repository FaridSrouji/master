SOUTHWEST AIRLINES BRINGS PRISM TO CORPORATE TRAVEL MANAGERS AND TRAVEL MANAGEMENT COMPANIES. CARRIER CONTINUES STREAMLINING BUSINESS TRAVEL PROGRAM CONTRACTS  WITH INDUSTRY-STANDARD DATA SOLUTION DALLAS , MARCH 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. (NYSE: LUV) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN EXPANDED PARTNERSHIP WITH SABRE CORPORATION TO BEGIN UTILIZING PRISM, AN INDUSTRY-STANDARD DATA SOLUTION DESIGNED TO STREAMLINE THE SALES PROCESS WITH CORPORATE TRAVEL MANAGERS AND TRAVEL MANAGEMENT COMPANIES. SABRE'S PRISM BRINGS DECISION SCIENCE TO THE SOUTHWEST® BUSINESS SALES TEAM, ALLOWING THE ABILITY TO DEVELOP A MORE CUSTOMIZED TRAVEL SOLUTION FOR BUSINESS CUSTOMERS THROUGH AUTOMATED, REAL-TIME DATA INSIGHTS AND TRENDS.
