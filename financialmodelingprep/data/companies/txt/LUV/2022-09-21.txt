SOUTHWEST AIRLINES LAUNCHES LIMITED-TIME TIER STATUS ACCELERATION PROMOTION FOR RAPID REWARDS MEMBERS. CARRIER SURPRISES RAPID REWARDS MEMBERS WITH MORE WAYS TO EARN AND MAINTAIN  A-LIST AND A-LIST PREFERRED TIER STATUS THROUGH 2023 DALLAS , SEPT. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) IS REWARDING ITS RAPID REWARDS MEMBERS FOR THEIR LONG-TERM SUPPORT BY GIVING THEM A LIMITED-TIME INCENTIVE, MAKING IT EASIER TO EARN TIER STATUS THROUGH 2023.
