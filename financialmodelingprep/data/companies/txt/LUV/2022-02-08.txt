SOUTHWEST AIRLINES AND TRANSPORT WORKERS UNION LOCAL 550 REACH TENTATIVE AGREEMENT ON TERMS OF NEW CONTRACT FOR DISPATCHERS. DALLAS, FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) AND THE TRANSPORT WORKERS UNION LOCAL 550 (TWU 550) HAVE REACHED A TENTATIVE AGREEMENT FOR THE MORE THAN 400 SOUTHWEST AIRLINES DISPATCH EMPLOYEES. "THIS AGREEMENT REWARDS OUR SKILLED DISPATCHERS, WHILE PROVIDING SOUTHWEST ADDITIONAL EFFICIENCIES," SAID VICE PRESIDENT LABOR RELATIONS ADAM CARLISLE.
