SOUTHWEST AIRLINES ANNOUNCES 15-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH VELOCYS FOR 219 MILLION GALLONS OF SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL. DALLAS, NOV. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) TODAY ANNOUNCED A 15-YEAR OFFTAKE AGREEMENT WITH VELOCYS RENEWABLES LLC ("VELOCYS") FOR 219 MILLION GALLONS OF SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL (SAF). AS ANNOUNCED BY VELOCYS TODAY, ONCE BLENDED WITH CONVENTIONAL JET FUEL, THE SAF COULD PRODUCE THE EQUIVALENT OF 575 MILLION GALLONS OF NET-ZERO1 FUEL AND AVOID 6.5 MILLION METRIC TONS OF CO2 OVER THE TERM OF THE AGREEMENT.
