4AIR TO PARTNER WITH SOUTHWEST AIRLINES TO DOCUMENT SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL USE. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--4AIR, THE FIRST AND ONLY RATING SYSTEM FOCUSED ON COMPREHENSIVE SUSTAINABILITY IN AVIATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL PROVIDE SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL (SAF) INVENTORY ACCOUNTING AND DOCUMENTATION SERVICES FOR SOUTHWEST AIRLINES® (NYSE: LUV).
