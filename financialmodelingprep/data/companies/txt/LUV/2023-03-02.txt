LUV LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , MARCH 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. ("SOUTHWEST" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: LUV) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF SOUTHWEST INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN JUNE 13, 2020 AND DECEMBER 31, 2022.
