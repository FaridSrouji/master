(NASDAQ: TTWO) AND ZYNGA INC. (NASDAQ: ZNGA), TWO LEADERS IN INTERACTIVE ENTERTAINMENT, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE STOCKHOLDERS OF EACH ORGANIZATION HAVE APPROVED ALL PROPOSALS RELATED TO TAKE-TWO'S COMBINATION WITH ZYNGA AT EACH COMPANY'S RESPECTIVE SPECIAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS.
