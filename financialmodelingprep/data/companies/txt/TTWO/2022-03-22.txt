KASKELA LAW LLC ANNOUNCES STOCKHOLDER INVESTIGATION OF TAKE-TWO INTERACTIVE SOFTWARE, INC. AND ENCOURAGES INVESTORS TO CONTACT THE FIRM - TTWO. PHILADELPHIA , MARCH 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KASKELA LAW LLC ANNOUNCES THAT IT IS INVESTIGATING TAKE-TWO INTERACTIVE SOFTWARE, INC. ("TAKE-TWO") (NASDAQ: TTWO) ON BEHALF OF THE COMPANY'S STOCKHOLDERS. ON JANUARY 10, 2022, TAKE-TWO ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAD ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE ZYNGA IN A CASH AND STOCK TRANSACTION VALUED AT $9.86 PER ZYNGA SHARE.
