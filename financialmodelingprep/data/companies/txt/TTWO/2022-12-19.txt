NATE V. RACKIEWICZ JOINS RESET DIGITAL AS CHIEF DATA OFFICER. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--RESET DIGITAL, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF NATE V. RACKIEWICZ AS CHIEF DATA OFFICER, A NEWLY-CREATED ROLE OVERSEEING THE COMPANY'S VISION, STRATEGY, AND EXECUTION OF DATA, ANALYTICS, AND SCIENCE ACROSS THE NEUROPROGRAMMATIC LEADER'S BUSINESS.
