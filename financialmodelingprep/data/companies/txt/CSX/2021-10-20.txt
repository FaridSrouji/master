CSX ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., OCT. 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THIRD QUARTER 2021 NET EARNINGS OF $968 MILLION, OR $0.43 PER SHARE, COMPARED TO $736 MILLION, OR $0.32 PER SHARE IN THE SAME PERIOD LAST YEAR. THIRD QUARTER 2021 OPERATING RATIO WAS 56.4% COMPARED TO 56.9% IN THE PRIOR YEAR PERIOD.
