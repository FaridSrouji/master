CSX AND SMART-TD CSRA UNION REACH TENTATIVE AGREEMENT ON PAID SICK LEAVE. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., MAY 22, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS REACHED A TENTATIVE AGREEMENT WITH THE SHEET METAL, AIR, RAIL AND TRANSPORTATION WORKERS-TRANSPORTATION DIVISION CSRA (SMART-TD CSRA) TO PROVIDE PAID SICK LEAVE BENEFITS. SMART-TD CSRA REPRESENTS TRAINMEN, CONDUCTORS AND YARDMEN ON TERRITORIES OF THE FORMER A&WP, WSSB, L&N, NC&STL AND SCL RAILROADS.
