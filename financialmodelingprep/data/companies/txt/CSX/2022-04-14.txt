CSX RECEIVES APPROVAL FROM SURFACE TRANSPORTATION BOARD TO ACQUIRE PAN AM RAILWAYS. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., APRIL 14, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE SURFACE TRANSPORTATION BOARD (STB) APPROVED CSX'S APPLICATION TO ACQUIRE PAN AM RAILWAYS, INC. (PAN AM). CSX WILL MOVE FORWARD WITH THE ACQUISITION WITH A PLANNED CLOSING DATE OF JUNE 1, 2022, AT WHICH TIME CSX WILL ACQUIRE CONTROL OF PAN AM.
