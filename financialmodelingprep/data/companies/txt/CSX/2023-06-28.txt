RAILROADS REACH AGREEMENT TO CREATE NEW DIRECT CONNECTION AND CORRIDOR LINKING MEXICO, TEXAS AND THE U.S. SOUTHEAST. CALGARY, ALBERTA AND JACKSONVILLE, FLA., JUNE 28, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CANADIAN PACIFIC KANSAS CITY (TSX: CP) (NYSE: CP) (CPKC), CSX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CSX) (CSX) AND GENESEE & WYOMING INC. (G&W) TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE REACHED AGREEMENTS THAT WHEN COMPLETED WILL CREATE A NEW DIRECT CPKC-CSX INTERCHANGE CONNECTION IN ALABAMA.
