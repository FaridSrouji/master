CSX CONTRIBUTES $200,000 TO HURRICANE IAN RELIEF EFFORTS. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., SEPT. 30, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL CONTRIBUTE $200,000 TO SUPPORT RELIEF AND RECOVERY EFFORTS UNDERWAY IN THE FLORIDA AND SOUTH CAROLINA COMMUNITIES AFFECTED BY HURRICANE IAN.
