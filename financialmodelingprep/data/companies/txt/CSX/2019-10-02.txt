CSX CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 13, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CSX CORPORATION APPROVED A $0.24 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 13, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 29, 2019.
CSX CORP SAYS APPOINTED KEVIN BOONE AS CFO. CSX ANNOUNCES EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENTS.CSX CORP - APPOINTED KEVIN BOONE AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.CSX CORP - JAMIE BOYCHUK NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF OPERATIONS.
