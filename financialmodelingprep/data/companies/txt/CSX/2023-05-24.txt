CSX RANKED AMONG AMERICA'S CLIMATE LEADERS 2023 BY USA TODAY. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., MAY 24, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY USA TODAY AS ONE OF AMERICA'S CLIMATE LEADERS, RANKING AMONG U.S. COMPANIES THAT HAVE ACHIEVED THE GREATEST REDUCTION IN EMISSIONS INTENSITY BETWEEN 2019 AND 2021.
