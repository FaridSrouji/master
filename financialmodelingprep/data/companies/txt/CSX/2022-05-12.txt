CSX HONORS 65 CUSTOMERS WITH 28TH ANNUAL CHEMICAL SAFETY EXCELLENCE AWARD. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., MAY 12, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP.
