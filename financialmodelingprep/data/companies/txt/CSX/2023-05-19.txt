CSX NAMED BEST RAILROAD BY INTERMODAL SERVICE PROVIDERS. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., MAY 19, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY INTERMODAL PROVIDERS AS THE TOP-PERFORMING RAILROAD IN NORTH AMERICA. THE “INTERMODAL SERVICE SCORECARD” RANKING WAS COMPILED BY THE JOURNAL OF COMMERCE, AND ASSESSED PERFORMANCE THROUGH THE FIRST FOUR MONTHS OF 2023.
