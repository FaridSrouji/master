CSX EARNS NINTH-CONSECUTIVE ‘A LIST' RECOGNITION FOR CLIMATE LEADERSHIP. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., DEC. 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX (NASDAQ: CSX) LEADERSHIP IN ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED FOR THE NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR WITH PLACEMENT ON THE CDP “A LIST” OF COMPANIES TAKING ACTION TO ADDRESS CLIMATE CHANGE.
