CSX EARNS SUPPORT FROM KEY NEW ENGLAND PASSENGER RAIL AUTHORITY. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., AUG. 06, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT REACHED AGREEMENT WITH THE NORTHERN NEW ENGLAND PASSENGER RAIL AUTHORITY (“NNEPRA”), ONE OF NEW ENGLAND'S LARGEST PASSENGER RAIL TRADE AUTHORITIES TO SUPPORT ITS PLANNED MERGER WITH PAN AM RAILWAYS INC. (PAN AM).
