CSX JOINS FRA CLOSE CALL REPORTING SYSTEM. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., MARCH 02, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX (NASDAQ: CSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS JOINING THE FEDERAL RAILROAD ADMINISTRATION'S  CONFIDENTIAL CLOSE CALL REPORTING SYSTEM (C3RS) , AS PART OF ITS COMMITMENT TO WORKING TOGETHER TO KEEP ITS EMPLOYEES AND COMMUNITIES SAFE.
