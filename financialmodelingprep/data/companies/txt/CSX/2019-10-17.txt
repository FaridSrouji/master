CSX CORP SAYS CO AGREED TO PURCHASE ABOUT 4.7 MLN SHARES OF CO'S COMMON STOCK FROM MANTLE RIDGE. CSX CORP - EFFECTIVE OCT 17, 2019,  STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH MR ARGENT ADVISOR LLC, ON BEHALF OF CERTAIN LIMITED PARTNERS OF ITS AFFILIATED FUNDS.CSX CORP - CO AGREED TO PURCHASE ABOUT 4.7 MILLION SHARES OF CO'S COMMON STOCK FROM MANTLE RIDGE.CSX CORP - THESE SHARES WILL BE REPURCHASED UNDER CSX'S EXISTING $5 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION AND FUNDED WITH CASH ON HAND.
