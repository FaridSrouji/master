CSX CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., OCT. 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- YESTERDAY, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CSX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CSX) APPROVED A $0.093* PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 30, 2021.
