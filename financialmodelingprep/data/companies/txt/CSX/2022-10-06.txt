CSX CORP. DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., OCT. 06, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A $0.10 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 30, 2022.
