CPKC AND CSX ANNOUNCE PLANNED COLLABORATION TO DEVELOP ADDITIONAL HYDROGEN LOCOMOTIVES. CALGARY, AB AND JACKSONVILLE, FLA. , JUNE 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ - CANADIAN PACIFIC KANSAS CITY (TSX: CP) (NYSE: CP) (CPKC) AND CSX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CSX) (CSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THEIR INTENT TO ENTER INTO A JOINT VENTURE FOR THE BUILDING AND DEPLOYMENT OF HYDROGEN LOCOMOTIVE CONVERSION KITS FOR DIESEL ELECTRIC LOCOMOTIVES.
