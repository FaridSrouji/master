CSX REACHES TENTATIVE PAID SICK LEAVE AGREEMENT WITH SMART-TD B&O UNION. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., APRIL 03, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX (NASDAQ: CSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS REACHED A TENTATIVE AGREEMENT WITH THE SHEET METAL, AIR, RAIL AND TRANSPORTATION WORKERS-TRANSPORTATION DIVISION B&O (SMART-TD B&O) UNION TO PROVIDE CSX TRAINMEN AND CONDUCTORS WITH PAID SICK LEAVE BENEFITS.
