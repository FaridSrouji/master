REGIONS FINANCIAL NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. BIRMINGHAM, ALA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--REGIONS FINANCIAL CORP. (NYSE:RF), THE PARENT COMPANY OF REGIONS BANK, ON FRIDAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY HAS BEEN NAMED AS ONE OF AMERICA'S MOST JUST COMPANIES BY JUST CAPITAL, ALONG WITH ITS MEDIA PARTNER, CNBC. THIS MAKES THE THIRD YEAR IN ROW REGIONS HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED BY JUST FOR ITS COMMITMENT TO SERVING ASSOCIATES, CUSTOMERS, COMMUNITIES, THE ENVIRONMENT AND SHAREHOLDERS.
