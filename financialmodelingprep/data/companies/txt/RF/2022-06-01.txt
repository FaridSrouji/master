REGIONS NEXT STEP SURVEY FINDS AMERICANS ARE INCREASINGLY PRIORITIZING RENOVATIONS TO BOOST HOME VALUE. BIRMINGHAM, ALA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--REGIONS BANK ON WEDNESDAY ANNOUNCED THE RESULTS OF A NEW HOMEOWNERSHIP SURVEY THAT FINDS AN INCREASING NUMBER OF PEOPLE ARE REMODELING THEIR HOMES IN AN EFFORT TO RAISE ITS VALUE. THIS COMES AS THE HOMEBUYING MARKET REMAINS VERY COMPETITIVE, WITH SOME PEOPLE CHOOSING TO UPGRADE THEIR CURRENT HOMES RATHER THAN LOOK FOR A NEW HOUSE.
