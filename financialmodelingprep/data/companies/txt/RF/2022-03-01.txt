NAVIGATING MEDICAL DEBT: REGIONS BANK ANNOUNCES GRANT AND FINANCIAL WELLNESS TOOLS TO HELP PEOPLE AND FAMILIES. BIRMINGHAM, ALA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--REGIONS BANK ON TUESDAY ANNOUNCED A NEW GRANT AS WELL AS ADDITIONAL ONLINE FINANCIAL WELLNESS TOOLS DESIGNED TO HELP PEOPLE AND FAMILIES ADDRESS URGENT ISSUES SURROUNDING MEDICAL FINANCIAL HARDSHIPS. U.S. CENSUS BUREAU DATA HAS SHOWN NEARLY ONE IN FIVE HOUSEHOLDS IN THE U.S. CARRIED MEDICAL DEBT – AND THOSE FIGURES WERE COMPILED BEFORE THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC PROMPTED ADDITIONAL MEDICAL EXPENSES FOR MANY PEOPLE NATIONWIDE.
