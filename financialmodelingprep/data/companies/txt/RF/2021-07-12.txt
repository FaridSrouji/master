REGIONS BANK DIGITIZES HOMEBUILDER FINANCING WITH CONVENIENT, SEAMLESS SOLUTION. BIRMINGHAM, ALA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--REGIONS BANK ON MONDAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A DIGITAL SOLUTION DESIGNED TO MAKE IT EASIER AND MORE CONVENIENT FOR HOMEBUILDERS AND DEVELOPERS TO UTILIZE THE CONSTRUCTION LENDING PROCESS. THE CENTRALIZED, DIGITAL SOLUTION IS OFFERED BY REGIONS IN COLLABORATION WITH BUILT, A CONSTRUCTION FINANCE SOFTWARE AND PAYMENT TECHNOLOGY LEADER.
