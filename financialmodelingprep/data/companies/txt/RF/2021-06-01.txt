NEW SURVEY: REGIONS FINDS PEOPLE PREFER SPACE OVER LOCATION WHEN LOOKING FOR A NEW HOME. BIRMINGHAM, ALA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--A NEW REGIONS BANK SURVEY FINDS 1 IN 5 AMERICANS PLANS TO BUY A NEW HOME WITHIN THE NEXT YEAR. THESE INSIGHTS CAN HELP THEM MAKE IT A REALITY.
