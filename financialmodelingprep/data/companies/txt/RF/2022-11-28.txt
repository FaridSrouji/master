REGIONS BANK LAUNCHES MULTI-ASSETS SOLUTIONS GROUP, COMBINING INVESTMENT RESEARCH STRENGTHS OF REGIONS AND HIGHLAND ASSOCIATES. BIRMINGHAM, ALA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--REGIONS BANK ON MONDAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS MULTI-ASSETS SOLUTIONS GROUP, THE RESULT OF COMBINING THE BANK'S INVESTMENT RESEARCH GROUP WITH THE RESEARCH TEAM FROM REGIONS SUBSIDIARY HIGHLAND ASSOCIATES.
