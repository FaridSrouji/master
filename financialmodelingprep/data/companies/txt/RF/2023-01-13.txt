REGIONS FINANCIAL CORPORATION DECLARES SERIES C PREFERRED STOCK DIVIDEND. BIRMINGHAM, ALA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE REGIONS FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE:RF) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $14.25 PER SHARE OF SERIES C PREFERRED STOCK OUTSTANDING (EQUIVALENT TO APPROXIMATELY $0.35625 PER DEPOSITARY SHARE), PAYABLE ON FEB. 15, 2023, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEB. 1, 2023.
