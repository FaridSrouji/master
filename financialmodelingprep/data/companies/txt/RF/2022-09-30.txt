GRABAR LAW OFFICE INVESTIGATES SHAREHOLDER CLAIMS AGAINST OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS OF REGIONS FINANCIAL CORPORATION (RF) AFTER CFPB ORDERS IT TO PAY $191 MILLION IN REFUNDS AND FINES. PHILADELPHIA, PENNSYLVANIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - SEPTEMBER 30, 2022) - REGIONS BANK HAS BEEN ORDERED BY THE CONSUMER FINANCIAL PROTECTION BUREAU (CFPB) TO PAY $191 MILLION IN REFUNDS AND FINES FOR CHARGING UNLAWFUL OVERDRAFT FEES ON CERTAIN DEBIT CARD TRANSACTIONS AND ATM WITHDRAWALS.
