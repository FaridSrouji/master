MOHAWK INDUSTRIES' TWELFTH ANNUAL ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL & GOVERNANCE REPORT FOCUSES ON INNOVATING FOR A BETTER TOMORROW. CALHOUN, GA., DEC. 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- IN ITS TWELFTH ANNUAL ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE REPORT RELEASED TODAY ONLINE (MOHAWKSUSTAINABILITY.COM), MOHAWK INDUSTRIES, THE WORLD'S LARGEST FLOORING MANUFACTURER, STRESSES ITS COMMITMENT TO INNOVATING FOR A BETTER TOMORROW THROUGH STRATEGIC INITIATIVES THAT ARE BETTER FOR PEOPLE, THE PLANET AND THE BUSINESS' PERFORMANCE.
