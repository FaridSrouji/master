MOHAWK INDUSTRIES REPORTS Q1 RESULTS. CALHOUN, GEORGIA, APRIL 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MOHAWK INDUSTRIES, INC. (NYSE: MHK) TODAY ANNOUNCED 2021 FIRST QUARTER NET EARNINGS OF $237 MILLION AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) OF $3.36. ADJUSTED NET EARNINGS WERE $246 MILLION, AND EPS WAS $3.49, EXCLUDING RESTRUCTURING, ACQUISITION AND OTHER CHARGES. NET SALES FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021 WERE $2.7 BILLION, UP 16.8% AS REPORTED AND 9.1% ON A CONSTANT CURRENCY AND DAYS BASIS. FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020, NET SALES WERE $2.3 BILLION, NET EARNINGS WERE $111 MILLION AND EPS WAS $1.54, ADJUSTED NET EARNINGS WERE $119 MILLION, AND EPS WAS $1.66, EXCLUDING RESTRUCTURING, ACQUISITION AND OTHER CHARGES.
