MOHAWK INDUSTRIES REPORTS Q1 RESULTS. CALHOUN, GA., APRIL 28, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MOHAWK INDUSTRIES, INC. (NYSE: MHK) TODAY ANNOUNCED 2022 FIRST QUARTER NET EARNINGS OF $245 MILLION AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) OF $3.78. ADJUSTED NET EARNINGS WERE $246 MILLION, AND ADJUSTED EPS WAS $3.78, EXCLUDING RESTRUCTURING, ACQUISITION, AND OTHER CHARGES. NET SALES FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022 WERE $3.0 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF 13.0% AS REPORTED AND 17.3% ON A CONSTANT CURRENCY AND DAYS BASIS. FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021, NET SALES WERE $2.7 BILLION, NET EARNINGS WERE $237 MILLION AND EPS WAS $3.36. ADJUSTED NET EARNINGS WERE $246 MILLION, AND ADJUSTED EPS WAS $3.49, EXCLUDING RESTRUCTURING, ACQUISITION, AND OTHER CHARGES.
