TAP TO RIDE! MONTEREY-SALINAS TRANSIT BECOMES 1ST IN CALIFORNIA TO OFFER RIDERS THE ABILITY TO TAP TO PAY WITH VISA. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, IN COLLABORATION WITH VISA, CALTRANS, CYBERSOURCE, LITTLEPAY, AND SC SOFT, MONTEREY-SALINAS TRANSIT (MST) BECAME THE FIRST TRANSIT AGENCY IN CALIFORNIA TO OFFER BUS RIDERS A CONTACTLESS PAYMENT OPTION. AS CITIES BEGIN TO REOPEN, PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION WILL PLAY A CRITICAL ROLE IN RECOVERY, AND CONTACTLESS PAYMENTS CAN HELP PROVIDE A SAFE AND EASY EXPERIENCE FOR RIDERS AND TRANSIT OPERATORS SEEKING LIMITED PHYSICAL TOUCHPOINTS AND INTERACTIONS. MST RIDERS CA
