VISA INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 3, 2019. ON OCTOBER 22, 2019, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VISA INC. DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND IN THE AGGREGATE AMOUNT OF $0.30 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK (DETERMINED IN THE CASE OF CLASS B AND C COMMON STOCK AND SERIES B AND C CONVERTIBLE PARTICIPATING PREFERRED STOCK ON AN AS-CONVERTED BASIS), PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 3, 2019, TO ALL HOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF NOVEMBER 15, 2019.
