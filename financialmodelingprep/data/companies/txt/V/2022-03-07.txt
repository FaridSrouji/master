VISA SUSPENDS ALL RUSSIA OPERATIONS. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VISA INC. (NYSE:V) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS SUSPENDING ITS RUSSIA OPERATIONS. EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, VISA WILL WORK WITH ITS CLIENTS AND PARTNERS WITHIN RUSSIA TO CEASE ALL VISA TRANSACTIONS OVER THE COMING DAYS.
