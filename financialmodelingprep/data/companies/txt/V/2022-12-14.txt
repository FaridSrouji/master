AMC THEATRES® ANNOUNCES A CO-BRANDED CREDIT CARD; PERFECT FOR AMC MOVIEGOERS AND AMC SHAREHOLDERS, THE AMC ENTERTAINMENT VISA CARD ENABLES AMC STUBS MEMBERS TO EARN REWARDS FASTER THROUGH EVERYDAY PURCHASES. LEAWOOD, KAN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMC THEATRES® (NYSE: AMC AND APE), THE LARGEST THEATRICAL EXHIBITOR IN THE UNITED STATES AND THE WORLD, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL LAUNCH THE AMC ENTERTAINMENT VISA CARD – THE ONLY CO-BRANDED MOVIE THEATRE CREDIT CARD IN THE UNITED STATES. IN PARTNERSHIP WITH VISA, A WORLD LEADER IN DIGITAL PAYMENTS, AND DESERVE, A MODERN CREDIT CARD TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM, THE AMC ENTERTAINMENT VISA CARD IS EXPECTED TO BE AVAILABLE IN EARLY 2023.
