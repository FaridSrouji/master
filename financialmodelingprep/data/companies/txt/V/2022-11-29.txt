VISA AND GOHENRY ANNOUNCE GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP TO ACCELERATE FINANCIAL EDUCATION FOR CHILDREN AND TEENS. SAN FRANCISCO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--VISA (NYSE: V) TODAY ANNOUNCED A SEVEN-YEAR GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP WITH GOHENRY, THE PREPAID CARD AND FINANCIAL EDUCATION APP FOR 6-TO 18-YEAR-OLDS. VISA WILL PROVIDE SUPPORT AS THE EXCLUSIVE GLOBAL NETWORK PARTNER FOR GOHENRY'S PREPAID CARDS FOR KIDS AND TEENS IN THE US, UK, AND EUROPE.
