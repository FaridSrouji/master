VISA AND ASCENDA PARTNER ON NEXT-GEN LOYALTY AND REWARDS IN ASIA PACIFIC. SINGAPORE, AUG. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VISA, THE WORLD LEADER IN DIGITAL PAYMENTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP IN ASIA PACIFIC WITH ASCENDA, THE LOYALTY TECHNOLOGY COMPANY. THE PAYMENTS NETWORK WILL BE FIRST GLOBALLY TO LEVERAGE ASCENDA'S NEW NEXUS PLATFORM, WHICH WILL ENABLE VISA'S PARTNERS TO ADOPT A COMPREHENSIVE NEW REWARDS PROGRAM FOR THEIR CUSTOMERS.
