VISA U.S. SPENDING MOMENTUM INDEX FALLS AGAIN IN SEPTEMBER. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VISA (NYSE: V) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. SPENDING MOMENTUM INDEX (SMI) WAS 108.5 IN SEPTEMBER (SEASONALLY ADJUSTED), DOWN 1.1 POINTS FROM AUGUST. WITH THE SMI READING STILL ABOVE 100, THIS SIGNALS THAT MORE CONSUMERS ARE STILL SPENDING MORE THAN THEY DID A YEAR AGO.
