VISA TO ACQUIRE CURRENCYCLOUD. LONDON & SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VISA (NYSE: V) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE CURRENCYCLOUD, A GLOBAL PLATFORM THAT ENABLES BANKS AND FINTECHS TO PROVIDE INNOVATIVE FOREIGN EXCHANGE SOLUTIONS FOR CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS. THE ACQUISITION BUILDS ON AN EXISTING STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN THE TWO COMPANIES AND VALUES CURRENCYCLOUD AT £700 MILLION, INCLUSIVE OF CASH AND RETENTION INCENTIVES.
