I2C PARTNERS WITH VISA FOR FINTECH PROCESSING IN MENA REGION. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--I2C INC., A LEADING PROVIDER OF DIGITAL PAYMENT AND BANKING TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS PARTNERSHIP WITH VISA, THE WORLD'S LEADER IN DIGITAL PAYMENTS, AS FINTECH PROCESSOR IN THE MENA (MIDDLE EAST AND NORTH AFRICA) REGION. THROUGH THIS ARRANGEMENT, FINTECHS IN THE REGION WILL BE ABLE TO ACCESS VISA'S GLOBAL NETWORK AS WELL AS I2C'S AGILE ISSUING AND PROCESSING PLATFORM BY TAPPING INTO THE COMBINED COMPANIES' SUITE OF DIGITAL-FIRST SOLUTIONS AND ADVANCE
