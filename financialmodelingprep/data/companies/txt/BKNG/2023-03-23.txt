BOOKING.COM HITS A HOME RUN AS THE OFFICIAL ONLINE TRAVEL PARTNER OF MAJOR LEAGUE BASEBALL. DIGITAL TRAVEL LEADER INKS DEAL WITH MLB, MAKING IT EASIER FOR EVERYONE TO BOOK  BASEBALL-INSPIRED TRIPS ACROSS THE U.S. AMERICAN AND NATIONAL LEAGUE DIVISION SERIES NOW PRESENTED BY BOOKING.COM NEW YORK , MARCH 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, BOOKING.COM IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE IT HAS SIGNED ON AS THE OFFICIAL ONLINE TRAVEL PARTNER OF MAJOR LEAGUE BASEBALL (MLB) IN THE UNITED STATES. WITH BOOKING.COM'S MISSION TO MAKE IT EASIER FOR EVERYONE TO EXPERIENCE THE WORLD, THE GOAL OF THIS STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH MLB IS TO SIMPLIFY THE WAY AMERICANS PLAN THEIR UNIQUE BASEBALL-INSPIRED TRAVELS ACROSS THE U.S. AS PART OF THIS LONG-TERM PARTNERSHIP, BOOKING.COM IS NOW THE PRESENTING SPONSOR OF THE AMERICAN AND NATIONAL LEAGUE DIVISION SERIES, TOO.
