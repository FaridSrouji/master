DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE AND PRICELINE EXPAND AMERICAN FORCES TRAVEL. NORWALK, CONN., JULY 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, THE UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE (DOD) OFFICIALLY ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF AMERICAN FORCES TRAVEL , A LEISURE TRAVEL SITE CREATED WITH PRICELINE, FOR CURRENT AND RETIRED MEMBERS OF ALL SIX BRANCHES OF THE U.S. MILITARY, THE NATIONAL GUARD, AND THEIR FAMILIES, ALONG WITH DOD CIVILIANS.
