BOOKING HOLDINGS ENTERS INTO AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE GETAROOM. NORWALK, CONN., NOV. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOOKING HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: BKNG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE B2B DISTRIBUTOR OF HOTEL ROOMS, GETAROOM, FROM COURT SQUARE CAPITAL PARTNERS FOR APPROXIMATELY $1.2 BILLION.
