BOOKING HOLDINGS COMPLETES THE ACQUISITION OF GETAROOM. NORWALK, CONN., DEC. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOOKING HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: BKNG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED PLAN TO ACQUIRE GETAROOM FROM COURT SQUARE CAPITAL PARTNERS FOR APPROXIMATELY $1.2 BILLION.
