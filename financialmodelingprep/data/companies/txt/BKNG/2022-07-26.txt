MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL NAMES OPENTABLE AS A PREFERRED RESTAURANT TECHNOLOGY PROVIDER. AGREEMENT BRINGS TOGETHER GLOBAL LEADERS IN RESTAURANT TECHNOLOGY AND HOSPITALITY SAN FRANCISCO , JULY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OPENTABLE, PART OF BOOKING HOLDINGS, SIGNED A GLOBAL AGREEMENT TO BECOME A PREFERRED RESTAURANT TECHNOLOGY PROVIDER TO MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL. CURRENTLY, OPENTABLE SERVES MORE THAN 1,400 RESTAURANTS OPERATED BY MARRIOTT HOTELS IN PROPERTIES ACROSS 56 COUNTRIES THROUGHOUT THE WORLD.
