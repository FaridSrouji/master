PRICELINE LAUNCHES STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH BRITISH AIRWAYS AND IBERIA. THE NEW DISTRIBUTION CAPABILITY EXPANDS INVENTORY ACROSS BOOKING HOLDINGS NORWALK, CONN. , DEC. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PRICELINE, ONE OF NORTH AMERICA'S LEADING ONLINE TRAVEL AGENCIES, ANNOUNCED TODAY A NEW DISTRIBUTION CAPABILITY (NDC) AGREEMENT WITH BRITISH AIRWAYS AND IBERIA, PART OF INTERNATIONAL AIRLINES GROUP (IAG), ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING AIRLINE GROUPS.
