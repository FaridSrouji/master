BOOKING HOLDINGS TO ATTEND THE BANK OF AMERICA MERRILL LYNCH 2022 SECURITIES CONSUMER & RETAIL TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE. NORWALK, CONN., MARCH 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOOKING HOLDINGS (NASDAQ: BKNG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER GLENN FOGEL AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER DAVID GOULDEN WILL BE SPEAKING AT THE BANK OF AMERICA MERRILL LYNCH 2022 CONSUMER & RETAIL TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE ON MARCH 8TH AT 1:50 PM ET.
