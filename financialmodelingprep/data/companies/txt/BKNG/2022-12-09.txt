TURO AND KAYAK LAUNCH WINTER TRAVEL HUB TO HELP TRAVELERS BOOK THEIR DREAM CAR THIS HOLIDAY SEASON. SAN FRANCISCO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--TODAY, KAYAK, THE WORLD'S LEADING TRAVEL SEARCH ENGINE, AND TURO, THE WORLD'S LARGEST PEER-TO-PEER CAR SHARING MARKETPLACE, LAUNCHED A WINTER TRAVEL HUB TO BRING TRAVELERS A PERSONALIZED CAR BOOKING SOLUTION AHEAD OF THE UPCOMING HOLIDAY TRAVEL SEASON. TRAVELERS CAN NOW BROWSE TURO'S EXTENSIVE SELECTION OF OVER 1,400 MAKES AND MODELS THROUGH AN INTERACTIVE TRAVEL QUIZ AND A CO-BRANDED SOCIAL SERIES THAT HELPS THEM DETERMINE THE PERFECT VEHICLE FOR THEIR HOLIDAY GETAWAY.
