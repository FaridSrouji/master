AGODA DATA INDICATES EARLY SIGNS OF RECOVERY FOR BALI FOLLOWING INDONESIA'S EARLY REOPENING. DENPASAR, INDONESIA , APRIL 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOLLOWING INDONESIA'S ANNOUNCEMENT TO RE-OPEN ITS BORDERS TO INTERNATIONAL TRAVELERS, DIGITAL TRAVEL PLATFORM AGODA HAS SEEN A SURGE IN SEARCHES FOR BALI FROM NEIGHBOURING MARKETS KEEN TO RETURN TO ONE OF THE REGION'S MOST POPULAR BEACH DESTINATIONS. BALI RANKED THE TOP SEARCH DESTINATION IN INDONESIA, WHILE THE ENTIRE COUNTRY RANKED THIRD AS A DESTINATION IN ASIA, FOLLOWING CLOSELY BEHIND THAILAND AND PHILIPPINES.
