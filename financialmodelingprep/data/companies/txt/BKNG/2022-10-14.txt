LARRY QUINLAN JOINS THE BOOKING HOLDINGS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NORWALK, CONN. , OCT. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BOOKING HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: BKNG) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT LARRY QUINLAN, FORMER GLOBAL CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER FOR DELOITTE, JOINED THE BOARD EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 13, 2022.
