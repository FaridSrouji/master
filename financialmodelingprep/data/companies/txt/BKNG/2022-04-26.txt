SUMIT SINGH, CEO OF CHEWY, INC., NOMINATED FOR ELECTION TO THE BOOKING HOLDINGS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NORWALK, CONN. , APRIL 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BOOKING HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: BKNG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS NOMINATED SUMIT SINGH, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF CHEWY, INC. - A LEADING ONLINE DESTINATION FOR PET PARENTS AND PARTNERS - FOR ELECTION TO THE BOARD AT THE COMPANY'S ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS IN JUNE 2022.
