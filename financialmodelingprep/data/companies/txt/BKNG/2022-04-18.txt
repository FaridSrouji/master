OMRI MORGENSHTERN NAMED CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF AGODA. CURRENT CHIEF EXECUTIVE, JOHN BROWN, ASSUMES ROLE OF CHAIRPERSON AT AGODA NORWALK, CONN. , APRIL 18, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOOKING HOLDINGS, THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF ONLINE TRAVEL AND RELATED SERVICES, AND PARENT COMPANY OF CONSUMER-FACING BRANDS INCLUDING BOOKING.COM , PRICELINE, AGODA, RENTALCARS.COM , KAYAK AND OPENTABLE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT OMRI MORGENSHTERN HAS BEEN NAMED CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF AGODA EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2022.
