DOMINO'S® AND NURO LAUNCH AUTONOMOUS PIZZA DELIVERY WITH ON-ROAD ROBOT. ANN ARBOR, MICH., APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINO'S (NYSE: DPZ), THE LARGEST PIZZA COMPANY IN THE WORLD BASED ON GLOBAL RETAIL SALES, AND NURO, THE LEADING SELF-DRIVING DELIVERY COMPANY, ARE LAUNCHING AUTONOMOUS PIZZA DELIVERY IN HOUSTON.
