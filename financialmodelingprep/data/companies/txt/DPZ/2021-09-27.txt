DOMINO'S® AND THE NATIONAL FIRE PROTECTION ASSOCIATION TEAM UP TO DELIVER FIRE SAFETY MESSAGES. ANN ARBOR, MICH., SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINO'S PIZZA INC. (NYSE: DPZ), THE LARGEST PIZZA COMPANY IN THE WORLD, AND THE NATIONAL FIRE PROTECTION ASSOCIATION (NFPA) ARE JOINING FORCES TO DELIVER FIRE SAFETY MESSAGES TO HOMES ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
