DOMINO'S® ANNOUNCES EXECUTIVE PROMOTIONS. ANN ARBOR, MICH., MARCH 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINO'S PIZZA, INC. (NYSE: DPZ), THE LARGEST PIZZA COMPANY IN THE WORLD, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PROMOTIONS OF JOE JORDAN TO THE ROLE OF PRESIDENT, U.S. & GLOBAL SERVICES AND ART D'ELIA TO THE ROLE OF EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT – INTERNATIONAL, EFFECTIVE MAY 1, 2022.
