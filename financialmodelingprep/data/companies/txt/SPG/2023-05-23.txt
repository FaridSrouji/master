THE WEBSTER SET TO OPEN THREE NEW LOCATIONS BY END OF 2024 WITH SIMON®. INDIANAPOLIS , MAY 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUXURY MULTI-BRANDED RETAILER, THE WEBSTER , ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THEY WILL BE EXPANDING THEIR PHYSICAL STORE FOOTPRINT WITH THE OPENING OF THREE NEW BOUTIQUES BEGINNING IN THE FALL OF 2023. IN COLLABORATION WITH SIMON ®, THE BRAND WILL OPEN THE NEW BOUTIQUES AT THE SHOPS AT RIVERSIDE  (NEW JERSEY) IN EARLY OCTOBER OF 2023, LENOX SQUARE  (ATLANTA) IN LATE SUMMER 2024, AND THE DOMAIN  (AUSTIN) IN EARLY FALL 2024.
