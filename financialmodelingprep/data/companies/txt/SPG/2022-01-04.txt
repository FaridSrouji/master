SIMON PROPERTY GROUP SELLS $1.2 BILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. INDIANAPOLIS, JAN. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIMON, A GLOBAL LEADER IN THE OWNERSHIP OF PREMIER SHOPPING, DINING, ENTERTAINMENT AND MIXED-USE DESTINATIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS MAJORITY-OWNED OPERATING PARTNERSHIP SUBSIDIARY, SIMON PROPERTY GROUP, L.P. (THE "OPERATING PARTNERSHIP"), HAS AGREED TO SELL: $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS FLOATING RATE NOTES DUE 2024, AND   $700 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 2.650% NOTES DUE 2032.
