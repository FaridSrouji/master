KITCHEN UNITED AND SIMON LAUNCH "GRAB GO EAT" PLATFORM. INDIANAPOLIS, FEB. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIMON HAS ANNOUNCED "GRAB GO EAT", A NEW COLLABORATION WITH GHOST KITCHEN INDUSTRY LEADER, KITCHEN UNITED. THROUGH THE "GRAB GO EAT" PLATFORM, CUSTOMERS CAN ORDER FROM MULTIPLE RESTAURANTS IN A SINGLE TRANSACTION FOR ON-MALL PICKUP OR HOME DELIVERY, ALLOWING GUESTS TO CHOOSE WHETHER FOOD IS DELIVERED TO THEIR TABLE OR ANY STORE WITHIN THE SHOPPING CENTER AS WELL AS SKIP THE LINES AND PICK UP FOOD DIRECTLY FROM THE RESTAURANT OR ON-PREMISE FOOD LOCKERS.
