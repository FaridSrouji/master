SIMON PROPERTY GROUP ANNOUNCES APPOINTMENT OF PEGGY FANG ROE TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. INDIANAPOLIS, DEC. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIMON, A GLOBAL LEADER IN THE OWNERSHIP OF PREMIER SHOPPING, DINING, ENTERTAINMENT AND MIXED-USE DESTINATIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PEGGY FANG ROE HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MS. ROE IS THE GLOBAL OFFICER, CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE, AND LOYALTY & NEW VENTURES AT MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL, A POSITION WHICH SHE HAS HELD SINCE 2020.
