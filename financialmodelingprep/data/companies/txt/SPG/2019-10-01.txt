SIMON PROPERTY GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCES AN AGREEMENT WITH RUE GILT GROUPE TO CREATE NEW MULTI-PLATFORM VENTURE DEDICATED TO DIGITAL VALUE SHOPPING. SIMON ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT WITH RUE GILT GROUPE TO CREATE A NEW MULTI-PLATFORM VENTURE DEDICATED TO DIGITAL VALUE SHOPPING. THE NEW VENTURE WILL COMBINE SIMON'S IN-STORE RETAIL LEADERSHIP WITH RGG'S E-COMMERCE EXPERTISE AND INCLUDE RGG'S RUE LA LA AND GILT PLATFORMS AS WELL AS SHOP PREMIUM OUTLETS (SPO), SIMON'S NEW ONLINE OUTLET MARKETPLACE. SIMON WILL CONTRIBUTE APPROXIMATELY $280 MILLION TO THE NEW VENTURE INCLUDING THE VALUE OF SPO, WHICH HAS BEEN SUCCESSFULLY BETA TESTED SINCE MARCH 2019. DEBUTING, SPO WILL DELIVER WORLD-CLASS BRANDS AT OUTLET PRICING. FOR BRANDS AND RETAILERS ON THE SITE, SPO WILL HELP DRIVE ADDITIONAL ONLINE SALES AND SUPPORT INVENTORY OPTIMIZATION AND CUSTOMER ACQUISITION. SPO CURRENTLY ENCOMPASSES OVER 2,000 DESIGNERS AND 300,000 PRODUCTS WITH MANY MORE MERCHANTS TO JOIN OVER TIME AS NEW ENHANCEMENTS ARE INTRODUCED TO THE SITE. OVER THE COMING YEAR, SIMON WILL PROMOTE THE NEW VENTURE UTILIZING A PORTION OF ITS WELL OVER $100 MILLION PLUS ANNUAL MARKETING BUDGET AND ACCESS TO TWO BILLION ANNUAL SHOPPER VISITS. THE PARTNERSHIP WILL ALSO LEVERAGE THE COMBINED DATABASES OF SIMON AND RGG REACHING OVER 35 MILLION SHOPPERS.
