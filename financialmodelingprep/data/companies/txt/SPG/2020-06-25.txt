TAUBMAN CENTERS SHAREHOLDERS APPROVE MERGER WITH SIMON PROPERTY GROUP. TAUBMAN CENTERS SHAREHOLDERS APPROVE MERGER WITH SIMON PROPERTY GROUP.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - APPROXIMATELY 99.7% OF SHARES VOTED WERE IN FAVOR OF MERGER AGREEMENT.TAUBMAN CENTERS - SHARES VOTING IN FAVOR INCLUDED ABOUT 78.3% OF OUTSTANDING SHARES HELD BY SHAREHOLDERS OTHER THAN TAUBMAN FAMILY MEMBERS.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - "CONTINUES TO BELIEVE THAT SIMON'S PURPORTED TERMINATION OF MERGER AGREEMENT IS INVALID AND WITHOUT MERIT".TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - "TAUBMAN FILED AN ANSWER AND COUNTERCLAIM IN LAWSUIT, REJECTING SIMON'S ALLEGATIONS".
