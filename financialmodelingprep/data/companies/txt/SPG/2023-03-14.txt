SIMON PROPERTY GROUP ANNOUNCES NEW $5.0 BILLION REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY. INDIANAPOLIS , MARCH 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIMON®, A REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST ENGAGED IN THE OWNERSHIP OF PREMIER SHOPPING, DINING, ENTERTAINMENT AND MIXED-USE DESTINATIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS MAJORITY-OWNED OPERATING PARTNERSHIP SUBSIDIARY, SIMON PROPERTY GROUP, L.P. (THE "OPERATING PARTNERSHIP"), HAS CLOSED ON A NEW $5.0 BILLION MULTI-CURRENCY UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY, WHICH REPLACES THE OPERATING PARTNERSHIP'S EXISTING $4.0 BILLION SENIOR UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.
