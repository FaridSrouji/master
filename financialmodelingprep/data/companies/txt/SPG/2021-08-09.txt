SIMON PROPERTY GROUP SELLS $1.25 BILLION OF SENIOR NOTES. INDIANAPOLIS, AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIMON, A GLOBAL LEADER IN THE OWNERSHIP OF PREMIER SHOPPING, DINING, ENTERTAINMENT AND MIXED-USE DESTINATIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS MAJORITY-OWNED OPERATING PARTNERSHIP SUBSIDIARY, SIMON PROPERTY GROUP, L.P. (THE "OPERATING PARTNERSHIP"), HAS AGREED TO SELL: $550 MILLION PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 1.375% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2027, AND $700 MILLION PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 2.250% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2032.
