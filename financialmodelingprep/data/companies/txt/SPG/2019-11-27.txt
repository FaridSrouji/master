EML PAYMENTS US LLC ENTERS INTO A NON-EXCLUSIVE MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH SIMON PROPERTY GROUP, INC. FOR THE DISTRIBUTION OF MULTIPLE PAYMENT CARD PRODUCTS. EML PAYMENTS USA, LLC, HAS ENTERED INTO A NON-EXCLUSIVE MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH SIMON PROPERTY GROUP, INC. (SIMON) FOR THE DISTRIBUTION OF MULTIPLE PAYMENT CARD PRODUCTS THROUGH SELECT SIMON SHOPPING MALLS AND B2B DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS IN THE USA. EML EXPECTS THE GDV TO REVENUE CONVERSION RATIO TO BE MATERIALLY IN LINE WITH OTHER NON-RELOADABLE CARD PROGRAMS THE GROUP OPERATES. EML IS ANNOUNCING THE CONTRACT SIGNATURE.
