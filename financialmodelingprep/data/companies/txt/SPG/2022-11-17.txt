SIMON AWARDED 2022 NAREIT RETAIL LEADER IN THE LIGHT ACHIEVEMENT. INDIANAPOLIS , NOV. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIMON ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN PRESENTED WITH NAREIT'S 2022 RETAIL LEADER IN THE LIGHT AWARD. THIS AWARD IS GIVEN TO REITS THAT HAVE DEMONSTRATED OUTSTANDING SUSTAINABILITY PRACTICES THROUGHOUT THE YEAR.
