SIMON® EXPANDS RELATIONSHIP WITH FIVE BELOW IN 2023. INDIANAPOLIS , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIMON ® AND FIVE BELOW ARE EXPANDING THEIR RELATIONSHIP BY ADDING MORE LOCATIONS AT SIMON'S INDUSTRY LEADING PORTFOLIO, INCLUDING PREMIUM OUTLETS®. FIVE BELOW WILL BRING AN ARRAY OF ITEMS FOR TWEENS, TEENS, AND BEYOND, INTO AT LEAST SEVEN DIFFERENT PREMIUM OUTLETS.
