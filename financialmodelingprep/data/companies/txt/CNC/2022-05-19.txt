CENTENE CORPORATION TO HOST VIRTUAL INVESTOR MEETING ON JUNE 17, 2022. ST. LOUIS , MAY 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) WILL HOST ITS NEXT INVESTOR MEETING IN A VIRTUAL FORMAT ON FRIDAY, JUNE 17 TH, 2022. CENTENE CORPORATION'S SENIOR MANAGEMENT WILL HOST THE VIRTUAL MEETING, WHICH WILL BE STREAMED LIVE ON THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE AT WWW.CENTENE.COM, UNDER THE INVESTORS SECTION.
