QUARTET HEALTH, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED $67.5 MILLION IN FUNDING FROM A GROUP OF INVESTORS. ON NOVEMBER 27, 2019, QUARTET HEALTH, INC. CLOSED THE TRANSACTION. THE COMPANY AMENDED THE TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION. THE COMPANY RECEIVED $67,500,000 IN THE TRANSACTION. THE COMPANY HAS RAISED $7,500,000 IN ITS SECOND TRANCHE. THE TRANSACTION INCLUDED PARTICIPATION FROM RETURNING INVESTOR DEERFIELD MANAGEMENT COMPANY, L.P. ALONG WITH ECHO HEALTH VENTURES. THE COMPANY HAS RAISED $160,500,000 IN FUNDING TILL DATE.
