CENTENE AFFILIATE WELLCARE OF NORTH CAROLINA TO PARTNER WITH SELECTED BEHAVIORAL HEALTH I/DD TAILORED PLANS. ST. LOUIS, AUG. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS NORTH CAROLINA SUBSIDIARY, WELLCARE OF NORTH CAROLINA, WILL HELP COORDINATE PHYSICAL HEALTH SERVICES FOR MULTIPLE LOCAL MANAGEMENT ENTITIES/MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATIONS (LME/MCOS) FOR THE STATE'S BEHAVIORAL HEALTH AND INTELLECTUAL/DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITY TAILORED PLANS (BEHAVIORAL HEALTH I/DD TAILORED PLANS). THE NORTH CAROLINA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES ANNOUNCED THAT AWARDEES ARE ANTICIPATED TO BEGIN OPERATING AS TAILORED PLANS ON JULY 1, 2022.
