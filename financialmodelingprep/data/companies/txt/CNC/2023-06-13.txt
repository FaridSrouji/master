CENTENE CORPORATION COMPLETES DIVESTITURE OF APIXIO. ST. LOUIS , JUNE 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED DIVESTITURE OF APIXIO, A LEADING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM THAT ENABLES VALUE-BASED CARE, TO NEW MOUNTAIN CAPITAL. "WE ARE CONFIDENT THIS TRANSACTION WILL BEST POSITION APIXIO TO EXPAND ITS IMPACT AS A LEADING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM THAT ENABLES VALUE-BASED CARE PROGRAMS ACROSS THE COUNTRY," SAID CENTENE'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, SARAH M.
