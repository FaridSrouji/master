CENTENE APPOINTS DREW ASHER AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. ST. LOUIS, MAY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DREW ASHER IS APPOINTED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE TODAY. HE WILL REPORT TO MICHAEL NEIDORFF, CENTENE CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CEO.
