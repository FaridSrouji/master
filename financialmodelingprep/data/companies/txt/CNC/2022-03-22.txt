CENTENE APPOINTS SARAH LONDON AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. ST. LOUIS, MARCH 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT SARAH LONDON, VICE CHAIRMAN OF CENTENE, HAS BEEN APPOINTED CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, SUCCEEDING MICHAEL NEIDORFF. MR. NEIDORFF REMAINS ON A PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED MEDICAL LEAVE OF ABSENCE FROM HIS POSITION ON THE BOARD OF THE COMPANY.
