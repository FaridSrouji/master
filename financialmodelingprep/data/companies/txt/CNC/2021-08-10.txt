CALIFORNIA HEALTH & WELLNESS ASSISTING MEMBERS ACROSS NEVADA AND PLACER COUNTIES DURING STATE OF EMERGENCY. SACRAMENTO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN RESPONSE TO GOV. GAVIN NEWSOM'S DECLARED STATES OF EMERGENCY IN NEVADA AND PLACER COUNTIES, CALIFORNIA HEALTH & WELLNESS IS PROVIDING SPECIAL ASSISTANCE TO MEMBERS IN THESE COUNTIES. THE COMPANY IS TAKING STEPS TO ENSURE MEMBERS AFFECTED BY THE FIRE CAN ACCESS ESSENTIAL PRESCRIPTION MEDICATIONS AND HAVE IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT THEIR BENEFITS IF THEIR SERVICES ARE INTERRUPTED. PRESCRIPTION INFORMATION DURING THE DURATION OF THE STATE OF EMERGENCY,
