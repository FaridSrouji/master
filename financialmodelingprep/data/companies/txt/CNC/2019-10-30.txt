WELLCARE REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.Q3 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $5.50.Q3 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $4.74.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $3.82 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q3 REVENUE $7.1 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $6.79 BILLION.QTRLY TOTAL REVENUE (GAAP)  $7,140.2 MILLION VERSUS $5,058.1 MILLION.WELLCARE HEALTH PLANS- MEDICAID HEALTH PLANS MEMBERSHIP WAS 4.1 MILLION AS OF SEPT 30, 2019 INCREASED BY ABOUT 181,000 MEMBERS COMPARED WITH SEPT 30, 2018.WELLCARE HEALTH PLANS - MEDICARE HEALTH PLANS MEMBERSHIP WAS ABOUT 563,000 AS OF SEPTEMBER 30, 2019, UP 3.5 PERCENT, COMPARED WITH SEPTEMBER 30, 2018.MEDICARE HEALTH PLANS REVENUE WAS $1.8 BILLION FOR Q3 OF 2019 AND INCREASED BY 16.4 PERCENT COMPARED WITH Q3 OF 2018.DUE TO PENDING MERGER WITH CENTENE, WELLCARE IS NOT CURRENTLY PROVIDING UPDATED FINANCIAL GUIDANCE.GAAP MEDICAID HEALTH PLANS REVENUE WAS $4.9 BILLION, UP 51.3%, IN Q3.ADJUSTED MEDICAID HEALTH PLANS REVENUE WAS $4.8 BILLION, UP 55.2%, IN Q3.WELLCARE HEALTH PLANS- MEDICARE PDP MEMBERSHIP WAS APPROXIMATELY 1.7 MILLION AS OF SEPTEMBER 30, 2019 AND INCREASED BY 60.7%, COMPARED WITH Q3 2018..
