CENTENE AND MAGELLAN SATISFY ALL REGULATORY APPROVALS FOR COMPLETION OF ACQUISITION AGREEMENT. ST. LOUIS, DEC. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) AND MAGELLAN HEALTH, INC. (NASDAQ: MGLN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE OBTAINED ALL REQUIRED REGULATORY APPROVALS NECESSARY TO CLOSE CENTENE'S PENDING ACQUISITION OF MAGELLAN HEALTH. SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING DELIVERIES, THE PARTIES ANTICIPATE CLOSING THE ACQUISITION ON OR ABOUT JANUARY 4, 2022.
