CENTENE ANNOUNCES NATIONWIDE VACCINATION EDUCATION AND OUTREACH INITIATIVE. ST. LOUIS, JUNE 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE LAUNCH OF A NEW INITIATIVE TO ENCOURAGE CENTENE HEALTH PLAN MEMBERS, AS WELL AS ALL AMERICANS, TO RECEIVE THE COVID-19 VACCINE. THIS WEEK, CENTENE WILL BEGIN WORKING WITH NETWORK PHYSICIANS TO DEPLOY A CALL CAMPAIGN TO ITS HEALTH PLAN MEMBERS NATIONWIDE.
