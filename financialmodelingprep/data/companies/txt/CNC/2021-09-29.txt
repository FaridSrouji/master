U.S. MEDICAL MANAGEMENT ACCOUNTABLE CARE PARTNERS (ACO) IMPROVES QUALITY OF CARE AND GENERATES SHARED SAVINGS THROUGH CMS SHARED SAVINGS PROGRAM IN 2020.
