CENTENE APPOINTS SUZY DEPRIZIO AS CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER. ST. LOUIS, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SUZY DEPRIZIO HAS BEEN APPOINTED AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER, EFFECTIVE TODAY. MS. DEPRIZIO WILL PLAY A CRITICAL ROLE LEADING THE MARKETING STRATEGY, BRAND POSITION AND ADVERTISING FOR THE COMPANY.
