CENTENE CORPORATION REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2022 RESULTS AND INCREASES 2022 GUIDANCE. -- DILUTED EPS OF $1.44; ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS OF $1.83 -- -- RAISES TOTAL REVENUE AND ADJUSTED EPS GUIDANCE -- ST. LOUIS , APRIL 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2022, REPORTING DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) OF $1.44 AND ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS OF $1.83.
