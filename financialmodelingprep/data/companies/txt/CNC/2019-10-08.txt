CENTENE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES EDMUND KROLL'S INTENT TO RETIRE, EFFECTIVE APRIL 30, 2020. CENTENE CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT EDMUND KROLL, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, FINANCE & INVESTOR RELATIONS, HAS ADVISED THE COMPANY OF HIS INTENTION TO RETIRE, EFFECTIVE APRIL 30, 2020. FROM NOW UNTIL HIS RETIREMENT, MR. KROLL WILL CONTINUE TO LEAD INVESTOR RELATIONS FOR THE COMPANY.
