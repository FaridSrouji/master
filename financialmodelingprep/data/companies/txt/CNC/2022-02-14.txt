CENTENE SUBSIDIARY LOUISIANA HEALTHCARE CONNECTIONS SELECTED TO CONTINUE SERVING LOUISIANA'S MEDICAID MANAGED CARE PROGRAM STATEWIDE. ST. LOUIS, FEB. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS SUBSIDIARY LOUISIANA HEALTHCARE CONNECTIONS WAS SELECTED BY THE LOUISIANA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH TO CONTINUE ADMINISTERING QUALITY, INTEGRATED HEALTHCARE SERVICES TO MEDICAID ENROLLEES ACROSS THE STATE. THE NEW STATEWIDE CONTRACT IS ANTICIPATED TO BEGIN JULY 1, 2022.
