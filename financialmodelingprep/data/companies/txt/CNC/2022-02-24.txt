CENTENE ANNOUNCES APPROVAL OF REQUEST BY MICHAEL NEIDORFF, CHAIRMAN & CEO, TO TAKE A MEDICAL LEAVE OF ABSENCE. ST. LOUIS, FEB. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ("CENTENE") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A REQUEST FROM MICHAEL NEIDORFF, CHAIRMAN & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, FOR A MEDICAL LEAVE OF ABSENCE. JAMES DALLAS HAS BEEN NAMED ACTING CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD.
