WELLCARE BY HEALTH NET MEDICARE ADVANTAGE PLANS IN CALIFORNIA EARN 4-STAR RATING IN ANNUAL CMS STAR QUALITY RATINGS. SACRAMENTO, CALIF., OCT. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WELLCARE BY HEALTH NET ANNOUNCED TODAY TWO OF ITS MEDICARE CONTRACTS IN CALIFORNIA RECEIVED A 4-STAR RATING (OUT OF A POSSIBLE FIVE STARS) FROM THE CENTERS FOR MEDICARE & MEDICAID SERVICES (CMS) ANNUAL STAR QUALITY RATINGS.
