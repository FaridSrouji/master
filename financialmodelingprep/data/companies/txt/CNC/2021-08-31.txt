HEALTH NET'S MARTHA SANTANA-CHIN NAMED “INSURANCE EXECUTIVE OF THE YEAR” BY THE LOS ANGELES BUSINESS JOURNAL. SACRAMENTO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MARTHA SANTANA-CHIN, MEDI-CAL PRESIDENT OF HEALTH NET, HAS BEEN HAS BEEN NAMED “INSURANCE EXECUTIVE OF THE YEAR” BY THE LOS ANGELES BUSINESS JOURNAL. THE BUSINESS JOURNAL RECENTLY ANNOUNCED ITS 2021 HEALTH CARE LEADERSHIP AWARDS, WHICH RECOGNIZED SANTANA-CHIN AND 15 OTHER ORGANIZATIONS AND INDIVIDUALS IN LOS ANGELES FOR THEIR CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE HEALTH CARE SECTOR DURING THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
