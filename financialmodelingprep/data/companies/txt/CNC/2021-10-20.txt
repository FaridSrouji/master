CHARLOTTE FC AND CENTENE CORPORATION ANNOUNCE MULTI-YEAR PARTNERSHIP TO SUPPORT SOCCER FANS ACROSS THE CAROLINAS. CHARLOTTE, N.C., OCT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) AND CHARLOTTE FC ANNOUNCED TODAY A MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT, DESIGNATING THE COMPANY AS THE OFFICIAL HEALTH INSURANCE PARTNER FOR THE CLUB, WHICH WILL KICK OFF ITS INAUGURAL CAMPAIGN IN 2022.
