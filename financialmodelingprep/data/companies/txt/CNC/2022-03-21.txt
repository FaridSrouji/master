CENTENE RECOGNIZED WITH INNOVATION AWARD FOR HEALTH EQUITY. ST. LOUIS , MARCH 21, 2022  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ("CENTENE") ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS BEEN AWARDED THE INNOVATION AWARD FOR HEALTH EQUITY BY THE NATIONAL COMMITTEE FOR QUALITY ASSURANCE (NCQA). THE AWARD RECOGNIZES CENTENE'S DATA-DRIVEN HEALTH EQUITY IMPROVEMENT MODEL FOR BEING A LEADING-EDGE STRATEGY FOR IMPROVING HEALTH EQUITY AND HEALTHCARE QUALITY THROUGH INNOVATIVE AND CREATIVE SOLUTIONS.
