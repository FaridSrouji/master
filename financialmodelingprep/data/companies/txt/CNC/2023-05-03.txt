CENTENE CORPORATION SIGNS DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO DIVEST APIXIO TO NEW MOUNTAIN CAPITAL. ST. LOUIS , MAY 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL APIXIO, A LEADING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM THAT ENABLES VALUE-BASED CARE, TO NEW MOUNTAIN CAPITAL, A GROWTH-ORIENTED INVESTMENT FIRM WITH MORE THAN $37 BILLION IN ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. CENTENE ACQUIRED APIXIO IN DECEMBER 2020.
