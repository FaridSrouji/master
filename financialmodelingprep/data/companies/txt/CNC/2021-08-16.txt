CENTENE NAMES RICH FISHER SVP AND MEDICARE CEO. ST. LOUIS, AUG. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY RICH FISHER HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND MEDICARE CEO. MR. FISHER WILL PLAY A CRITICAL ROLE OVERSEEING THE COMPANY'S MEDICARE STRATEGY, PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT, SALES, AND OPERATIONS.
