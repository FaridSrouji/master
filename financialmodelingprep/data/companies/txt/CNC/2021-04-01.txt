MORE SAN BERNARDINO COUNTY RESIDENTS CAN NOW GET VACCINATED THROUGH A PARTNERSHIP WITH HEALTH NET. SAN BERNARDINO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, HEALTH NET AND SAN BERNARDINO COUNTY ANNOUNCED A NEW PARTNERSHIP TO VACCINATE MORE ELIGIBLE RESIDENTS AGAINST COVID-19. THE JOINT EFFORT WILL TAKE THE VACCINES DIRECTLY TO THOSE WHO NEED THEM MOST THROUGH A SERIES OF POP-UP CLINICS ACROSS THE COUNTY. STARTING APRIL 1, HEALTH NET WILL STAFF AND DEPLOY ITS MOBILE VACCINATION RV TO SITES HOSTED BY SAN BERNARDINO COUNTY.
