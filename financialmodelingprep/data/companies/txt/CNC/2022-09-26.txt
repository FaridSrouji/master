CENTENE SUBSIDIARY SELECTED TO CONTINUE SERVING NEBRASKA'S MEDICAID MANAGED CARE MEMBERS. ST. LOUIS , SEPT. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS NEBRASKA SUBSIDIARY, NEBRASKA TOTAL CARE, HAS BEEN AWARDED THE NEBRASKA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES (DHHS) STATEWIDE MEDICAID MANAGED CARE CONTRACT.
