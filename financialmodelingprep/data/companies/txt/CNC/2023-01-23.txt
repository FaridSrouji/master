CENTENE COMPLETES DIVESTITURE OF MAGELLAN SPECIALTY HEALTH. ST. LOUIS , JAN. 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED DIVESTITURE OF MAGELLAN SPECIALTY HEALTH TO EVOLENT HEALTH, INC. (EVOLENT). "WE ARE PLEASED TO REACH ANOTHER SIGNIFICANT MILESTONE IN OUR ONGOING PORTFOLIO REVIEW AND VALUE CREATION PLAN," SAID SARAH LONDON, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF CENTENE.
