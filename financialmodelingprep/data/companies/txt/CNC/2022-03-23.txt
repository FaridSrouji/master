JESSE HUNTER JOINS WELSH, CARSON, ANDERSON & STOWE AS HEALTHCARE OPERATING PARTNER. NEW YORK, MARCH 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WELSH, CARSON, ANDERSON & STOWE (WCAS), A LEADING PRIVATE EQUITY FIRM FOCUSED EXCLUSIVELY ON THE HEALTHCARE AND TECHNOLOGY INDUSTRIES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JESSE HUNTER WILL JOIN THE FIRM AS AN OPERATING PARTNER IN ITS HEALTHCARE GROUP. MR. HUNTER IS AN ACCOMPLISHED EXECUTIVE WITH EXTENSIVE EXPERIENCE DRIVING GROWTH AND CREATING VALUE ACROSS THE HEALTHCARE ECOSYSTEM.
