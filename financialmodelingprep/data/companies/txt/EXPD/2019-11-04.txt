EXPEDITORS INTERNATIONAL OF WASHINGTON, INC. ANNOUNCES SEMI-ANNUAL CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 16, 2019. EXPEDITORS INTERNATIONAL OF WASHINGTON, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ON NOVEMBER 4, 2019, ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A SEMI-ANNUAL CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.50 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 16, 2019 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 2, 2019.
