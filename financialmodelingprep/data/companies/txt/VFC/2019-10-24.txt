VF CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 20, 2019. V.F. CORPORATION'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.48 PER SHARE, REFLECTING A 12% INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTER'S DIVIDEND. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 20, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON DECEMBER 10, 2019.
