VANS CHECKERBOARD DAY BRINGS PEOPLE TOGETHER TO REBUILD AND REVITALIZE LOCAL COMMUNITIES THROUGH CREATIVITY. COSTA MESA, CALIF., OCT. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VANS, THE ORIGINAL ACTION SPORTS BRAND AND ADVOCATE FOR CREATIVE EXPRESSION, IS BRINGING THE VANS FAMILY TOGETHER TO REBUILD AND REVITALIZE COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE WORLD IN HONOR OF VANS CHECKERBOARD DAY ON NOVEMBER 18, 2021.
