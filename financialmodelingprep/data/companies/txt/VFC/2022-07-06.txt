2022 VANS US OPEN OF SURFING RELEASES FULL SCHEDULE OF EVENTS. WORLD'S LARGEST ACTION SPORTS FESTIVAL RETURNS TO HUNTINGTON BEACH SHOWCASING SURF, SKATEBOARDING AND BMX COMPETITION, AND SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA BEACH CULTURE FROM JULY 30-AUGUST 7; NINE-DAY FESTIVAL WILL FEATURE ENGAGING CREATIVE ACTIVITIES FOR THE ENTIRE FAMILY AND COMMUNITY EVENTS COSTA MESA, CALIF., JULY 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE 2022 VANS US OPEN OF SURFING (VUSOS) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS SCHEDULE OF EVENTS FOR THE NINE-DAY ACTION SPORTS FESTIVAL, INCLUDING SURF, SKATEBOARDING, AND BMX COMPETITION, AS WELL AS A FULL CALENDAR OF ENGAGING CREATIVE ACTIVITIES FOR THE ENTIRE FAMILY TO ENJOY.
