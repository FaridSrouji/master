TORTOISEECOFIN ANNOUNCES CONSTITUENT CHANGE DUE TO MERGER AND ACQUISITION ACTIVITY. LEAWOOD, KS / ACCESSWIRE / MAY 25, 2023 / TORTOISEECOFIN TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT EVOQUA WATER TECHNOLOGIES CORP (NYSE:AQUA) WILL BE REMOVED FROM THE ECOFIN GLOBAL WATER ESG INDEX SM (EGWESG) AS A RESULT OF THE APPROVED MERGER WITH XYLEM INC (NYSE:XYL). DUE TO THE MERGER, AQUA WILL BE REMOVED FROM THE INDEX AT MARKET OPEN ON FRIDAY, MAY 26, 2023.
