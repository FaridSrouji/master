YOUTH INNOVATORS STEP UP TO HELP COMMUNITIES AROUND THE WORLD SOLVE URGENT WATER CHALLENGES. WASHINGTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--A GROWING GLOBAL MOVEMENT OF YOUNG INNOVATORS IS HELPING THE WATER SECTOR MAKE PROGRESS AGAINST URGENT CHALLENGES LIKE WATER ACCESSIBILITY AND SECURITY. BRILLIANT YOUNG MINDS ARE HELPING SHAPE A MORE SUSTAINABLE WORLD BY DEVELOPING INNOVATIVE WAYS TO MANAGE WATER AND BY EDUCATING OTHERS IN THEIR COMMUNITIES ABOUT WATER CHALLENGES.
