CRL LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES CHARLES RIVER LABORATORIES INTERNATIONAL, INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , JUNE 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN CHARLES RIVER LABORATORIES INTERNATIONAL, INC. ("CHARLES RIVER" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: CRL) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF CHARLES RIVER INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN MAY 5, 2020 AND FEBRUARY 21, 2023.
