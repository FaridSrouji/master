SHAREHOLDER ACTION ALERT: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN CHARLES RIVER LABORATORIES INTERNATIONAL, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / JUNE 4, 2023 / THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST CHARLES RIVER LABORATORIES INTERNATIONAL, INC. ("CHARLES RIVER" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE: CRL) FOR VIOLATIONS OF §§10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION. INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED THE COMPANY'S SECURITIES BETWEEN MAY 5, 2020 AND FEBRUARY 21, 2023, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD"), ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THE FIRM BEFORE JULY 18, 2023.
