DEADLINE ACTION NOTICE: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ENCOURAGES INVESTORS IN CHARLES RIVER LABORATORIES INTERNATIONAL, INC. WITH LOSSES OF $100,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / JUNE 15, 2023 / THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST CHARLES RIVER LABORATORIES INTERNATIONAL, INC. ("CHARLES RIVER" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE:CRL) FOR VIOLATIONS OF §§10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION.
