CF INDUSTRIES HOLDINGS, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 29, 2019. CF INDUSTRIES HOLDINGS, INC. REPORTED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A $0.30 PER SHARE DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 29, 2019 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF NOVEMBER 15, 2019.
