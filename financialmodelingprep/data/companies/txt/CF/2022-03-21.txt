CF INDUSTRIES HOLDINGS, INC. ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF SENIOR NOTES DUE 2023. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CF INDUSTRIES HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: CF) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY CF INDUSTRIES, INC. HAS ELECTED TO REDEEM IN FULL ON APRIL 21, 2022 ALL OF THE $500,000,000 OUTSTANDING PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 3.450% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2023 (THE “NOTES”), IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE OPTIONAL REDEMPTION PROVISIONS OF THE INDENTURE GOVERNING THE NOTES.
