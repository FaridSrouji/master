CF INDUSTRIES AND LOTTE CHEMICAL CORPORATION TO EXPLORE JOINT U.S.-BASED CLEAN AMMONIA PROJECT AND LONG-TERM CLEAN AMMONIA OFFTAKE AGREEMENT INTO SOUTH KOREA. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CF INDUSTRIES HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: CF), THE WORLD'S LARGEST PRODUCER OF AMMONIA, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) WITH LOTTE CHEMICAL CORPORATION (011170.KS) (“LOTTE”), SOUTH KOREA'S LEADING CHEMICAL COMPANY, THAT WILL GUIDE THE COMPANIES IN A JOINT EXPLORATION OF CLEAN AMMONIA PRODUCTION AND LONG-TERM CLEAN AMMONIA OFFTAKE INTO SOUTH KOREA.
