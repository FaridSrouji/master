CF INDUSTRIES HOLDINGS, INC. WELCOMES AFFIRMATIVE ITC PRELIMINARY DETERMINATIONS CONCERNING IMPORTS OF UAN FROM RUSSIA AND TRINIDAD AND TOBAGO. THE INVESTIGATION IS BEING CONDUCTED IN RESPONSE TO PETITIONS FILED BY CF INDUSTRIES HOLDINGS, INC.
