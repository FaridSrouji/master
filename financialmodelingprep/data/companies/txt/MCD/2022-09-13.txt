EDF RENEWABLES NORTH AMERICA SIGNS AGREEMENT WITH MCDONALD'S CORPORATION FOR APOLLO SOLAR PROJECT. SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EDF RENEWABLES NORTH AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED A 15-YEAR VIRTUAL POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT (VPPA) WITH MCDONALD'S CORP (NYSE: MCD). THE 255 MWAC / 332 MWDC APOLLO SOLAR PROJECT WILL HELP MCDONALD'S TO MEET ITS SUSTAINABILITY GOALS AND ADVANCE ITS COMMITMENT TO CLIMATE ACTION.
