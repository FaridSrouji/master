MCDONALD'S RAISES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY 10%. CHICAGO , OCT. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, MCDONALD'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.52 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 1, 2022. THE DIVIDEND INCREASE OF 10% OVER THE COMPANY'S PREVIOUS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND REFLECTS CONFIDENCE IN THE ACCELERATING THE ARCHES GROWTH STRATEGY AND A CONTINUED FOCUS ON DRIVING LONG-TERM PROFITABLE GROWTH FOR ALL STAKEHOLDERS.
