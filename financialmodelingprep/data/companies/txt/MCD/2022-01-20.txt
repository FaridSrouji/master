MCDONALD'S ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CHICAGO, JAN. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, MCDONALD'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.38 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON MARCH 15, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 1, 2022.
