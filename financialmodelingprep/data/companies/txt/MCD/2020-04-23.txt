CTW INVESTMENT GROUP ASKS MCDONALD'S SHAREHOLDERS TO VOTE AGAINST RE-ELECTION OF CHAIRMAN OF BOARD & COMPENSATION COMMITTEE CHAIR.CTW INVESTMENT GROUP SAYS ENRIQUE HERNANDEZ JR, RICHARD LENNY, WERE "RESPONSIBLE FOR DELIVERING" $44 MILLION PAYOUT TO TERMINATED CEO STEVE EASTERBROOK.CTW INVESTMENT GROUP SAYS ALSO CALLING ON SHAREHOLDERS TO OPPOSE 'SAY-ON-PAY’ PROPOSAL.
