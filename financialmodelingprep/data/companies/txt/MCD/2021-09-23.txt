MCDONALD'S RAISES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY 7% AND RESUMES SHARE REPURCHASES. CHICAGO, SEPT. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, MCDONALD'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.38 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 1, 2021.
