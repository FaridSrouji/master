MCDONALD'S COMMS LEAD ROBERT GIBBS STEPPING DOWN AMID RESTRUCTURING. MCDONALD'S CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER ROBERT GIBBS WILL BE LEAVING THE FAST-FOOD GIANT NEXT MONTH, MARKING THE LATEST STEP IN THE COMPANY'S RESTRUCTURING OF ITS COMMUNICATIONS DEPARTMENT. GIBBS, A FORMER OBAMA AIDE AND WHITE HOUSE PRESS SECRETARY, JOINED MCDONALD'S IN 2015 TO LEAD ITS CORPORATE RELATIONS FUNCTION, WHICH HAS HISTORICALLY HOUSED CORPORATE AND EXTERNAL COMMUNICATONS AND GOVERNMENT RELATIONS.
