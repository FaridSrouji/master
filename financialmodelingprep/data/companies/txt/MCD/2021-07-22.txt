MCDONALD'S ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CHICAGO, JULY 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, MCDONALD'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.29 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 1, 2021.
