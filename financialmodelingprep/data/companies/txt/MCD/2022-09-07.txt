MCDONALD'S INVESTS IN 40 LOCAL CHICAGO NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS DEDICATED TO SUPPORTING OPPORTUNITY YOUTH. THE CHICAGO COMMUNITY IMPACT GRANTS PROGRAM, DEVELOPED IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE CHICAGO COMMUNITY TRUST, DEEPENS MCDONALD'S LONGSTANDING COMMITMENT TO THE CITY IT CALLS HOME  CHICAGO , SEPT. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCDONALD'S CORPORATION ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WILL INVEST IN 40 CHICAGO-BASED NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS THAT ARE ADDRESSING SOME OF THE CITY'S MOST PRESSING NEEDS FOR OPPORTUNITY YOUTH THROUGH ITS CHICAGO COMMUNITY IMPACT GRANTS PROGRAM, DEVELOPED IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE CHICAGO COMMUNITY TRUST (THE TRUST).
