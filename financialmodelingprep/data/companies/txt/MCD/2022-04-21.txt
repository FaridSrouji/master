STATEMENT FROM MCDONALD'S CORPORATION IN RESPONSE TO MR. ICAHN'S MEDIA OUTREACH. CHICAGO , APRIL 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCDONALD'S CORPORATION (NYSE: MCD) TODAY ISSUED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT IN RESPONSE TO CARL ICAHN:  MCDONALD'S CARES ABOUT THE HEALTH AND WELFARE OF THE ANIMALS IN OUR SUPPLY CHAIN AND HAS LONG LED THE INDUSTRY WITH OUR ANIMAL WELFARE COMMITMENTS. OUR PIONEERING 2012 COMMITMENT REGARDING GROUP HOUSING FOR PREGNANT SOWS WAS SHAPED WITH INPUT FROM INDUSTRY EXPERTS AND THE AMERICAN ASSOCIATION OF SWINE VETERINARIANS.
