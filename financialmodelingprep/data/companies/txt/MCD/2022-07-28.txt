MCDONALD'S ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CHICAGO , JULY 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, MCDONALD'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.38 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 16, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 1, 2022.
