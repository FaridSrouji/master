MCDONALD'S ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. CHICAGO , MAY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, MCDONALD'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.38 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON JUNE 20, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 6, 2022.
