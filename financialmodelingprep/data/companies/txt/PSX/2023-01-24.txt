PHILLIPS 66 ACHIEVES ISCC PLUS CERTIFICATION TO TURN OIL FROM WASTE PLASTICS INTO FEEDSTOCKS AT ITS SWEENY REFINERY. HOUSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PHILLIPS 66 (NYSE: PSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED INTERNATIONAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CARBON CERTIFICATION (ISCC) PLUS CERTIFICATION FOR ITS SWEENY REFINERY IN TEXAS TO PROCESS OIL MADE FROM WASTE PLASTICS INTO FEEDSTOCKS FOR NEW PLASTICS.
