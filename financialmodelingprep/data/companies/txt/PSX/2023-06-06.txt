LINDEN COGENERATION AND PHILLIPS 66 BAYWAY REFINERY COMPLETE HYDROGEN BLENDING COMMISSIONING. LINDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINDEN COGENERATION (LINDEN COGEN) HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED THE COMMISSIONING PROCESS OF ITS HYDROGEN BLENDING INITIATIVE WHICH STANDS TO CURB CO2 EMISSIONS BY REDUCING THE AMOUNT OF NATURAL GAS USED FOR POWER AND STEAM GENERATION. AFTER SUCH MODIFICATIONS, INCLUDING PSM'S FLAMESHEETTM SYSTEM, LINDEN COGEN IS NOW TAKING REFINERY OFF GAS CONTAINING HYDROGEN PRODUCED BY THE PHILLIPS 66 (NYSE: PSX) BAYWAY REFINERY AND BLENDING IT WITH NATURAL GAS TO FUEL THE UNIT 6.
