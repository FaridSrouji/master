PHILLIPS 66 APPOINTS TWO NEW DIRECTORS. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PHILLIPS 66 HAS APPOINTED MARK E. LASHIER AND GREGORY J. HAYES TO SERVE ON THE BOARD, EFFECTIVE JULY 12, 2022.
PHILLIPS 66 ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PHILLIPS 66 HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 97 CENTS PER SHARE ON PHILLIPS 66 COMMON STOCK.
