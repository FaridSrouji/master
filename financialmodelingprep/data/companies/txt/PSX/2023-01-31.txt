PHILLIPS 66 REPORTS FOURTH-QUARTER 2022 FINANCIAL RESULTS. HOUSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PHILLIPS 66 (NYSE: PSX), A DIVERSIFIED ENERGY COMPANY, ANNOUNCES FOURTH-QUARTER 2022 EARNINGS OF $1.9 BILLION, COMPARED WITH EARNINGS OF $5.4 BILLION IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022. EXCLUDING SPECIAL ITEMS OF $15 MILLION, THE COMPANY HAD ADJUSTED EARNINGS OF $1.9 BILLION IN THE FOURTH QUARTER, COMPARED WITH THIRD-QUARTER ADJUSTED EARNINGS OF $3.1 BILLION.
