PHILLIPS 66 RECEIVES $3 MILLION GRANT TO ADVANCE REVERSIBLE SOLID OXIDE FUEL CELL TECHNOLOGY. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PHILLIPS 66 (NYSE: PSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED A $3 MILLION GRANT FROM THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY TO ADVANCE THE DEVELOPMENT OF HIGH-PERFORMANCE REVERSIBLE SOLID OXIDE FUEL CELLS. PHILLIPS 66 WILL COLLABORATE WITH THE GEORGIA INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GEORGIA TECH) TO DEMONSTRATE THE COMMERCIAL FEASIBILITY OF A LOW-COST AND HIGHLY EFFICIENT REVERSIBLE SOLID OXIDE FUEL CELL (RSOFC) SYSTEM FOR HYDROGEN AND ELECTRICITY GENERATION.
