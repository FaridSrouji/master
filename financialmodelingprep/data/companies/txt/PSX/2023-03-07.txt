PHILLIPS 66® HONORS TWO OKLAHOMA CITY EDUCATORS AT BIG 12 BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIPS. LIVE TO THE FULL HEROES™  PROGRAM SHINES SPOTLIGHT ON EDUCATORS GOING ABOVE AND BEYOND IN THEIR COMMUNITIES HOUSTON , MARCH 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TWO OKLAHOMA CITY EDUCATORS HAVE BEEN NAMED THE 2023 PHILLIPS 66® LIVE TO THE FULL HEROES™ HONOREES AND WILL BE RECOGNIZED DURING THE PHILLIPS 66 BIG 12 MEN'S AND WOMEN'S BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIPS THIS WEEK. SINCE 2018, THE PHILLIPS 66 LIVE TO THE FULL HEROES PROGRAM HAS HONORED PEOPLE WHO HAVE GONE ABOVE AND BEYOND TO SERVE THEIR COMMUNITIES BY HELPING TO FURTHER THEIR PROGRAMS AND EFFORTS.
