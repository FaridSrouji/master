SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISSLAW LLP INVESTIGATES PHILLIPS 66 PARTNERS LP. NEW YORK, OCT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISSLAW LLP IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PHILLIPS 66 PARTNERS LP ("PSXP" OR THE "PARTNERSHIP") (NYSE: PSXP) IN CONNECTION WITH THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF PSXP BY PHILLIPS 66 ("PHILLIPS 66") (NYSE: PSX).  UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, EACH OUTSTANDING PSXP COMMON UNITHOLDER WOULD RECEIVE 0.50 SHARES OF PHILLIPS 66 COMMON STOCK FOR EACH PSXP COMMON UNIT THEY HOLD.
