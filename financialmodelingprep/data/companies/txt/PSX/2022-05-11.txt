PHILLIPS 66 MAKES FINAL INVESTMENT DECISION TO CONVERT SAN FRANCISCO REFINERY TO A RENEWABLE FUELS FACILITY. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $PSX #RODEORENEWED--PHILLIPS 66 MADE A FINAL INVESTMENT DECISION TO PROGRESS RODEO RENEWED, A PROJECT TO CONVERT ITS CALIFORNIA REFINERY INTO A RENEWABLE FUELS FACILITY.
PHILLIPS 66 ANNOUNCES INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $PSX--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PHILLIPS 66 HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 97 CENTS PER SHARE, REPRESENTING A 5% INCREASE.
