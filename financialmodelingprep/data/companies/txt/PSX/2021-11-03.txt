TRIAL LAWYER MAJED NACHAWATI APPOINTED CO-LEAD COUNSEL IN LITIGATION OVER WEED KILLER'S LINKS TO PARKINSON'S DISEASE. CONTRA COSTA, CALIF., NOV. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIAL LAWYER MAJED NACHAWATI HAS BEEN NAMED CO-LEAD COUNSEL FOR CONSOLIDATED LITIGATION IN CALIFORNIA STATE COURT ON BEHALF OF PEOPLE WHO HAVE DEVELOPED PARKINSON'S DISEASE AFTER EXPOSURE TO PARAQUAT-BASED HERBICIDES INCLUDING GRAMOXONE.
