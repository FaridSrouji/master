SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISS LAW INVESTIGATES DCP MIDSTREAM, LP. NEW YORK , JAN. 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISS LAW IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE VIOLATIONS OF LAW CONNECTED WITH THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF DCP MIDSTREAM, LP ("DCP MIDSTREAM") (NYSE: DCP) BY PHILLIPS 66 (NYSE: PSX). UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $41.75 IN CASH FOR EACH DCP MIDSTREAM COMMON UNIT OWNED.
