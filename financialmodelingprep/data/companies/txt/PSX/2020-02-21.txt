PHILLIPS 66 PARTNERS ACQUIRES INTEREST IN LIBERTY PIPELINE. PHILLIPS 66 PARTNERS ACQUIRES INTEREST IN LIBERTY PIPELINE.PHILLIPS 66 PARTNERS LP - REACHED AGREEMENT WITH PHILLIPS 66 TO ACQUIRE ITS 50% INTEREST IN LIBERTY PIPELINE PROJECT FOR APPROXIMATELY $75 MILLION.PHILLIPS 66 PARTNERS LP - PLANS TO FUND TRANSACTION THROUGH A COMBINATION OF CASH ON HAND AND REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.PHILLIPS 66 PARTNERS LP - TERMS OF TRANSACTION WERE APPROVED BY BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GENERAL PARTNER OF PHILLIPS 66 PARTNERS.PHILLIPS 66 PARTNERS LP - COST OF PIPELINE IS EXPECTED TO BE APPROXIMATELY $1.6 BILLION, ON A GROSS BASIS, OR $800 MILLION NET TO PARTNERSHIP.
