"GLOBAL VOLUNTEER MONTH" CELEBRATES VOLUNTEERS THROUGHOUT APRIL AND SHINES A LIGHT ON THE IMPORTANCE OF CIVIC ENGAGEMENT. ATLANTA , MARCH 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL VOLUNTEER MONTH, FROM APRIL 1 – 30, 2023, IS A TIME TO RECOGNIZE VOLUNTEERS AND PEOPLE WHO ACTIVELY SUPPORT THEIR COMMUNITIES, WHETHER THROUGH VOLUNTEERISM OR OTHER PATHWAYS TO ACTIVE CIVIC ENGAGEMENT.
