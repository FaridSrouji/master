CLEARSENSE AND LEIDOS WORK TOGETHER TO TRANSFORM HEALTHCARE DATA. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., MARCH 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CLEARSENSE®, A TECHNOLOGY COMPANY THAT HELPS HEALTHCARE ORGANIZATIONS REALIZE MEASURABLE VALUE FROM THEIR DATA, ANNOUNCED THAT THEIR FIRM HAS ENTERED INTO A LONG-TERM STRATEGIC AGREEMENT WITH LEIDOS (NYSE: LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER.
