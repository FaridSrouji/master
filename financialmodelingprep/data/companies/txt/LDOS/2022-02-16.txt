LEIDOS APPOINTS FORMER ACTING SECRETARY OF DEFENSE PATRICK SHANAHAN TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. RESTON, VA., FEB. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE: LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PATRICK SHANAHAN HAS BEEN ELECTED TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 16, 2022.
