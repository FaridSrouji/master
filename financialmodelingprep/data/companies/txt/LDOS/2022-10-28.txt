LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. RESTON, VA. , OCT. 28, 2022 LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE:LDOS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.36 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF COMMON STOCK OF LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. THE CASH DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 30, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 15, 2022.
