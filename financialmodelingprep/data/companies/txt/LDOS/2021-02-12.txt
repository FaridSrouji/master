LEIDOS CEO ROGER KRONE ELECTED TO NATIONAL ACADEMY OF ENGINEERING. RESTON, VA., FEB. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE:LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER ROGER A.
