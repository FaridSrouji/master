QTC MEDICAL SERVICES AWARDED MILITARY RESERVE HEALTH READINESS CONTRACT. RESTON, VA., JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QTC MEDICAL SERVICES, INC. (QTC), A LEIDOS COMPANY (NYSE: LDOS), WAS AWARDED A NEW PRIME CONTRACT BY THE U.S. ARMY CONTRACTING COMMAND-NEW JERSEY TO PROVIDE COMMERCIAL HEALTH SERVICES TO ALL U.S. MILITARY RESERVE COMPONENT FORCES.
