U.S. CUSTOMS AND BORDER PROTECTION AWARDS LEIDOS OVER $200 MILLION TO PROVIDE SCREENING PORTALS TO PROTECT U.S. BORDERS. RESTON, VA., SEPT. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE:LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, WAS AWARDED A NEW PRIME CONTRACT BY U.S. CUSTOMS AND BORDER PROTECTION (CBP) TO PROVIDE LOW-ENERGY PORTAL (LEP) SYSTEMS FOR NON-INTRUSIVE INSPECTION (NII) OF PASSENGER VEHICLES AT U.S.-MEXICO LAND BORDER CROSSINGS.
