LEIDOS AWARDED SPOT ON $12.6 BILLION DIA SITE III IT SUPPORT CONTRACT. RESTON, VA., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE:LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, WAS RECENTLY NAMED AS AN AWARDEE FOR THE DEFENSE INTELLIGENCE AGENCY'S (DIA) SOLUTIONS FOR THE INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ENTERPRISE III (SITE III) CONTRACT VEHICLE.
