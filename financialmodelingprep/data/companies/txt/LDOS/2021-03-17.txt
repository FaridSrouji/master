KESSLER TOPAZ MELTZER & CHECK, LLP REMINDS INVESTORS OF SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT FILED AGAINST LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC.. RADNOR, PA., MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LAW FIRM OF KESSLER TOPAZ MELTZER & CHECK, LLP ALERTS INVESTORS THAT A SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED AGAINST LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: LDOS) ("LEIDOS") ON BEHALF OF THOSE WHO PURCHASED OR ACQUIRED LEIDOS SECURITIES BETWEEN MAY 4, 2020 AND FEBRUARY 23, 2021 , INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
HAGENS BERMAN, NATIONAL TRIAL ATTORNEYS, ALERTS INVESTORS IN LEIDOS HOLDINGS (LDOS) TO SECURITIES FRAUD LAWSUIT, INVESTORS WITH LOSSES SHOULD CONTACT FIRM. SAN FRANCISCO, CALIFORNIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - MARCH 17, 2021) - HAGENS BERMAN URGES LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: LDOS) INVESTORS TO SUBMIT THEIR LOSSES NOW.
