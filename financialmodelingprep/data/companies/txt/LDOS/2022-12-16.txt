LEIDOS TAPS FORMER TSA ACTING ADMINISTRATOR AS STRATEGIC ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE. RESTON, VA. , DEC. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE:LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED DARBY LAJOYE AS THE COMPANY'S VICE PRESIDENT AND STRATEGIC ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE FOR THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY (DHS).
