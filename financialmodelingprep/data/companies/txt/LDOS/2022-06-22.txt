LEIDOS' DES CONTRACT WIN, LARGEST IN COMPANY HISTORY, IS UPHELD BY THE GAO. RESTON, VA. , JUNE 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE: LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE DEFENSE ENCLAVE SERVICES (DES) CONTRACT AWARDED BY THE DEFENSE INFORMATION SYSTEMS AGENCY (DISA) WAS UPHELD BY THE GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY OFFICE (GAO) AFTER A THOROUGH AND DISCIPLINED REVIEW.
