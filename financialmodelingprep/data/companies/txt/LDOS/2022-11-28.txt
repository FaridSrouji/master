THE LAW OFFICES OF FRANK R. CRUZ ANNOUNCES INVESTIGATION OF LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (LDOS) ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS. LOS ANGELES--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE LAW OFFICES OF FRANK R. CRUZ ANNOUNCES AN INVESTIGATION OF LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (“LEIDOS” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: LDOS) ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS CONCERNING THE COMPANY'S POSSIBLE VIOLATIONS OF FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
