LEIDOS DONATES $1M TO ALABAMA SCHOOL OF CYBER TECHNOLOGY AND ENGINEERING. RESTON, VA. , AUG. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS  (NYSE:LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED A $1 MILLION DONATION TOWARD THE CONSTRUCTION OF A NEW STATE-OF-THE-ART ACADEMIC AND RESIDENTIAL FACILITY IN HUNTSVILLE, ALABAMA.
