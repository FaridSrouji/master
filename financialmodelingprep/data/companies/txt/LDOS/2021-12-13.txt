U.S. AIR FORCE SELECTS LEIDOS FOR COUNTER-SMALL UNMANNED AERIAL SYSTEMS CONTRACT. RESTON, VA., DEC. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE: LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN AWARDED A PRIME CONTRACT BY THE U.S. AIR FORCE TO SUPPORT THE SERVICE'S COUNTER-SMALL UNMANNED AERIAL SYSTEM (C-SUAS) EFFORTS.
