LEIDOS PARTNERSHIP REACHES 50% DEPLOYMENT OF MHS GENESIS SYSTEM. TEAM SUCCESSFULLY DEPLOYS ELECTRONIC HEALTH RECORD TO 14 ADDITIONAL COMMANDS RESTON, VA. , MARCH 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LEIDOS PARTNERSHIP FOR DEFENSE HEALTH (LPDH) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT SUCCESSFULLY DELIVERED THE MHS GENESIS ELECTRONIC HEALTH RECORD TO AN ADDITIONAL 15,000 CLINICIANS AND PROVIDERS AS PART OF ITS LARGEST DOUBLE WAVE DEPLOYMENT TO DATE, SPANNING 14 MILITARY TREATMENT FACILITY COMMANDS AND FIVE STATES.
