LEIDOS AWARDED $61.5 MILLION DHS CISA CONTRACT TO SUPPORT EMERGENCY RESPONDERS. RESTON, VA., DEC. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE:LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, WAS RECENTLY AWARDED THE PRIORITY SERVICES SCIENTIFIC, ENGINEERING, AND TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE (PS-SETA) PRIME CONTRACT IN SUPPORT OF THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY (DHS) CYBERSECURITY AND INFRASTRUCTURE SECURITY AGENCY (CISA).
