LEIDOS AWARDED CONTRACT TO SUPPORT FEDERAL HIGHWAY ADMINISTRATION POLICY INITIATIVES. RESTON, VA. , SEPT. 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE: LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, HAS BEEN AWARDED A NEW BLANKET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (BPA) BY THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION (USDOT) TO PROVIDE PROGRAM AND TECHNICAL SUPPORT TO THE FEDERAL HIGHWAY ADMINISTRATION'S (FHWA) OFFICE OF POLICY AND GOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS.
