KESSLER TOPAZ MELTZER & CHECK, LLP: IMPORTANT DEADLINE REMINDER FOR LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. INVESTORS IN SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT. RADNOR, PA, MARCH 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LAW FIRM OF KESSLER TOPAZ MELTZER & CHECK, LLP REMINDS LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE:  LDOS) ("LEIDOS") INVESTORS THAT A SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF THOSE WHO PURCHASED OR ACQUIRED LEIDOS SECURITIES BETWEEN MAY 4, 2020 AND FEBRUARY 23, 2021 , INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
