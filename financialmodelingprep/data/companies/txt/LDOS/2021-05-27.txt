QTC AWARDED FBI MEDICAL EXAMS CONTRACT. RESTON, VA., MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QTC MEDICAL SERVICES, INC. (QTC), A LEIDOS (NYSE: LDOS) COMPANY, WAS RECENTLY AWARDED A NEW PRIME CONTRACT TO PROVIDE FITNESS FOR DUTY MEDICAL EXAM SERVICES FOR FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION (FBI) EMPLOYEES AND CAREER APPLICANTS.
