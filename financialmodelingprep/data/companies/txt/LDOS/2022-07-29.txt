LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. RESTON, VA. , JULY 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE:LDOS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.36 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF COMMON STOCK OF LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. THE CASH DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2022, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2022.
