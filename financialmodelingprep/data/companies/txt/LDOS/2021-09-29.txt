LEIDOS AWARDED DARPA CONTRACT TO DEVELOP ADVANCED PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT FOR U.S. MILITARY. RESTON, VA., SEPT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE:LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, WAS RECENTLY AWARDED A PRIME CONTRACT BY DEFENSE ADVANCED RESEARCH PROJECTS AGENCY (DARPA) FOR THEIR PERSONALIZED PROTECTIVE BIOSYSTEM (PPB) PROGRAM.
