U.S. AIR FORCE SELECTS LEIDOS FOR ABMS DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE CONSORTIUM. RESTON, VA. , SEPT. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE: LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, WAS RECENTLY SELECTED BY THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF THE AIR FORCE TO JOIN THE SERVICE'S NEWLY LAUNCHED ADVANCED BATTLE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (ABMS) DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE (DI) CONSORTIUM.
