LEIDOS COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF 1901 GROUP. RESTON, VA., JAN. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: LDOS) ("LEIDOS"), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETED ACQUISITION OF 1901 GROUP FOR APPROXIMATELY $215 MILLION IN CASH. THE TRANSACTION WAS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ON DEC. 10, 2020....
