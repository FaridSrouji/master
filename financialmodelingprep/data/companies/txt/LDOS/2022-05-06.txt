NATO AWARDS LEIDOS BALLISTIC MISSILE DEFENSE CONTRACTS. RESTON, VA. , MAY 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE: LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, HAS BEEN AWARDED TWO INTERNATIONALLY COMPETED CONTRACTS BY THE NATO COMMUNICATIONS AND INFORMATION AGENCY (NCI AGENCY) TO ENHANCE THE ALLIANCE'S BALLISTIC MISSILE DEFENSE (BMD) CAPABILITIES.
