U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION ISSUES COMPLETE RESPONSE LETTER FOR MIRIKIZUMAB. INDIANAPOLIS , APRIL 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) ANNOUNCED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ISSUED A COMPLETE RESPONSE LETTER FOR THE MIRIKIZUMAB BIOLOGIC LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) FOR THE TREATMENT OF ULCERATIVE COLITIS (UC). IN THE LETTER, THE FDA CITED ISSUES RELATED TO THE PROPOSED MANUFACTURING OF MIRIKIZUMAB, WITH NO CONCERNS ABOUT THE CLINICAL DATA PACKAGE, SAFETY, OR LABEL FOR THE MEDICINE.
