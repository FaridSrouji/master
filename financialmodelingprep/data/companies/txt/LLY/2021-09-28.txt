LILLY AGAIN REDUCES LIST PRICE OF INSULIN LISPRO INJECTION AS LATEST CHANGE TO AFFORDABILITY OPTIONS. INDIANAPOLIS, SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) WILL LOWER THE LIST PRICE OF INSULIN LISPRO INJECTION IN THE U.S. BY AN ADDITIONAL 40 PERCENT EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2022, EFFECTIVELY BRINGING THE LIST PRICE DOWN TO 2008 LEVELS.
