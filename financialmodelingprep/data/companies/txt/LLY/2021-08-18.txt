US FDA APPROVES JARDIANCE® (EMPAGLIFLOZIN) TO TREAT ADULTS LIVING WITH HEART FAILURE WITH REDUCED EJECTION FRACTION. RIDGEFIELD, CONN. AND INDIANAPOLIS, AUG. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JARDIANCE® (EMPAGLIFLOZIN) 10 MG HAS BEEN APPROVED BY THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION TO REDUCE THE RISK OF CARDIOVASCULAR DEATH PLUS HOSPITALIZATION FOR HEART FAILURE IN ADULTS WITH HEART FAILURE WITH REDUCED EJECTION FRACTION (HFREF), BOEHRINGER INGELHEIM AND ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) ANNOUNCED TODAY.
