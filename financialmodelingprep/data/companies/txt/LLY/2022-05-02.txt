LILLY DECLARES SECOND-QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. INDIANAPOLIS , MAY 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022 OF $0.98 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 10, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 16, 2022.
