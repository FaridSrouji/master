LILLY TO SUPPLY 388,000 DOSES OF ETESEVIMAB TO U.S. GOVERNMENT FOR TREATMENT OF COVID-19. INDIANAPOLIS, SEPT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN ADDITIONAL PURCHASE BY THE U.S. GOVERNMENT FOR ITS NEUTRALIZING ANTIBODY THERAPIES AUTHORIZED FOR EMERGENCY USE AS A TREATMENT FOR COVID-19.
