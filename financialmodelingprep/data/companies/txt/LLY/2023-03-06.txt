INTENSIVE EDUCATION AT CLINICS ABOUT MULTIDISCIPLINARY CARE IMPROVED LIKELIHOOD THAT ADULTS WITH TYPE 2 DIABETES AND CARDIOVASCULAR DISEASE WERE RECEIVING GUIDELINE-DIRECTED MEDICINES, ACCORDING TO REAL-WORLD STUDY. THE FINDINGS, FROM THE COORDINATE-DIABETES TRIAL, DEMONSTRATE THE BENEFIT OF CLINIC-LEVEL EDUCATIONAL INTERVENTIONS, INCLUDING A FOCUS ON COORDINATING CARE BETWEEN CARDIOLOGY AND DIABETES CARE PROVIDERS   RESULTS WERE PRESENTED AT THE JOINT AMERICAN COLLEGE OF CARDIOLOGY/JOURNAL OF THE AMERICAN MEDICAL ASSOCIATION LATE-BREAKING CLINICAL TRIALS SESSION 2023 AND SIMULTANEOUSLY PUBLISHED IN JOURNAL OF THE AMERICAN MEDICAL ASSOCIATION RIDGEFIELD, CONN. AND INDIANAPOLIS , MARCH 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PROVIDING CLINICS WITH A COORDINATED, MULTIFACETED INTERVENTION, WITH ASSESSMENT OF BARRIERS, CARE PATHWAYS AND FEEDBACK, IMPROVED THE LIKELIHOOD THAT ADULTS WITH TYPE 2 DIABETES AND CARDIOVASCULAR DISEASE WERE RECEIVING GUIDELINE-DIRECTED MEDICAL THERAPY AFTER ONE YEAR VERSUS USUAL CARE.
