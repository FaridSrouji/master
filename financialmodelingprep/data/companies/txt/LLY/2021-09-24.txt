ELI LILLY AND COMPANY ISSUES VOLUNTARY NATIONWIDE RECALL OF ONE LOT OF GLUCAGON® EMERGENCY KIT DUE TO LOSS OF POTENCY. INDIANAPOLIS, SEPT. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) IS VOLUNTARILY RECALLING LOT D239382D, EXPIRATION APRIL 2022, OF GLUCAGON EMERGENCY KIT FOR LOW BLOOD SUGAR (GLUCAGON FOR INJECTION, 1 MG PER VIAL; DILUENT FOR GLUCAGON, 1 ML SYRINGE), TO THE CONSUMER/USER LEVEL.
