LILLY ANNOUNCES UPDATED VERZENIO® (ABEMACICLIB) PHASE 3 MONARCHE TRIAL DATA PRESENTED AT ESMO VIRTUAL PLENARY AND SIMULTANEOUSLY PUBLISHED IN THE ANNALS OF ONCOLOGY. INDIANAPOLIS, OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) TODAY ANNOUNCED UPDATED DATA FROM THE POSITIVE PHASE 3 MONARCHE TRIAL EVALUATING THE INVESTIGATIONAL USE OF VERZENIO® (ABEMACICLIB) IN COMBINATION WITH STANDARD ADJUVANT ENDOCRINE THERAPY (ET) FOR THE TREATMENT OF HORMONE RECEPTOR-POSITIVE (HR+), HUMAN EPIDERMAL GROWTH FACTOR RECEPTOR 2-NEGATIVE (HER2-), NODE-POSITIVE, HIGH RISK EARLY BREAST CANCER (EBC). THESE DATA WERE PRESENTED AT TODAY'S ESMO VIRTUAL PLENARY AND SIMULTANEOUSLY PUBLISHED IN THE ANNALS OF ONCOLOGY.
