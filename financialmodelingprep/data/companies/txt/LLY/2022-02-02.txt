UNICEF AND LILLY COLLABORATE TO HELP IMPROVE HEALTH OUTCOMES FOR 10 MILLION CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS. NEW YORK AND INDIANAPOLIS, FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) AND UNICEF HAVE TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW COLLABORATION TO HELP IMPROVE HEALTH OUTCOMES FOR 10 MILLION CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS LIVING WITH CHRONIC, NON-COMMUNICABLE DISEASES (NCDS) THROUGH 2025. LILLY HAS COMMITTED $14.4 MILLION IN SUPPORT OF UNICEF'S LIFESAVING WORK TO ADDRESS NCD RISK FACTORS, STRENGTHEN HEALTH SYSTEMS, AND ENHANCE THE ABILITY OF HEALTH CARE WORKERS TO CARE FOR PATIENTS IN BANGLADESH, MALAWI, NEPAL, THE PHILIPPINES AND ZIMBABWE 1.
