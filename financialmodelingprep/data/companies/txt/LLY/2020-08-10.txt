PIERIS PHARMACEUTICALS INC - ENTERED INTO A CLINICAL TRIAL COLLABORATION AND SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH ELI LILLY AND CO. PIERIS PHARMACEUTICALS INC -  ENTERED INTO A CLINICAL TRIAL COLLABORATION AND SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH ELI LILLY AND COMPANY.PIERIS PHARMACEUTICALS - CO, LILLY WILL COLLABORATE IN A PHASE 2 CLINICAL STUDY OF PRS-343 WITH RAMUCIRUMAB AND PACLITAXEL, FOR GASTRIC CANCER.PIERIS PHARMACEUTICALS INC - CO SHALL SPONSOR STUDY AND LILLY SHALL SUPPLY PIERIS WITH RAMUCIRUMAB.PIERIS -  LILLY TO PROVIDE INPUT ON SOME ASPECTS OF GASTRIC CANCER STUDY IN EXHANGE FOR JOINTLY OWNING POSSIBLE INVENTIONS FROM COMBINATION REGIMEN.
INNOVENT AND ELI LILLY RELEASE CLINICAL TRIAL RESULTS OF SINTILIMAB IN COMBINATION WITH ALIMTA (PEMETREXED) AND PLATINUM CHEMOTHERAPY. INNOVENT AND LILLY RELEASE CLINICAL TRIAL RESULTS OF SINTILIMAB IN COMBINATION WITH ALIMTA® (PEMETREXED) AND PLATINUM CHEMOTHERAPY AS FIRST-LINE TREATMENT FOR NONSQUAMOUS NSCLC IN AN ORAL PRESENTATION AT IASLC WCLC 2020 VIRTUAL PRESIDENTIAL SYMPOSIUM.SAFETY PROFILE WAS CONSISTENT WITH PREVIOUSLY REPORTED SINTILIMAB STUDIES, AND NO NEW SAFETY SIGNALS WERE IDENTIFIED.INNOVENT BIOLOGICS-NATIONAL MEDICAL PRODUCTS ADMINISTRATION (NMPA) OF CHINA HAS ACCEPTED SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION (SNDA) FOR THIS INDICATION.
