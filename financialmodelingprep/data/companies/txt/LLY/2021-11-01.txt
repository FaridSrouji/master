LILLY TO PARTICIPATE IN BERNSTEIN'S SECOND ANNUAL OPERATIONAL DECISIONS CONFERENCE. INDIANAPOLIS, NOV. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) WILL PARTICIPATE IN BERNSTEIN'S SECOND ANNUAL OPERATIONAL DECISIONS CONFERENCE ON THURSDAY, NOV. 4, 2021. JAKE VAN NAARDEN, CEO OF LOXO ONCOLOGY AT LILLY AND PRESIDENT OF LILLY ONCOLOGY; MARK MINTUN, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT – NEUROSCIENCE, AND PRESIDENT, AVID RADIOPHARMACEUTICALS; AND JAMIE CROANING, GLOBAL DEVELOPMENT LEADER – TIRZEPATIDE, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A VIRTUAL FIRESIDE CHAT AT 12:30 P.M.
