CHMP RECOMMENDS APPROVAL OF LILLY AND INCYTE'S OLUMIANT® (BARICITINIB) AS THE FIRST AND ONLY CENTRALLY-AUTHORIZED TREATMENT FOR ADULTS WITH SEVERE ALOPECIA AREATA (AA). INDIANAPOLIS , MAY 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) AND INCYTE (NASDAQ:INCY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE EUROPEAN MEDICINES AGENCY'S (EMA) COMMITTEE FOR MEDICINAL PRODUCTS FOR HUMAN USE (CHMP) HAS ISSUED A POSITIVE OPINION FOR OLUMIANT® (BARICITINIB) FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULTS WITH SEVERE ALOPECIA AREATA (AA). THIS OPINION MARKS THE FIRST STEP TOWARD EUROPEAN REGULATORY APPROVAL OF OLUMIANT FOR PATIENTS WITH SEVERE AA, AND IT IS NOW REFERRED TO THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION FOR FINAL ACTION.
