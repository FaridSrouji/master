LILLY'S LEBRIKIZUMAB DEMONSTRATED SIGNIFICANT SKIN IMPROVEMENT AND ITCH RELIEF WHEN COMBINED WITH TOPICAL CORTICOSTEROIDS IN PEOPLE WITH ATOPIC DERMATITIS IN THIRD PHASE 3 STUDY. BY WEEK 16, THE STUDY MET ALL PRIMARY AND KEY SECONDARY ENDPOINTS FOR PATIENTS ON THE LEBRIKIZUMAB COMBINATION ARM.
