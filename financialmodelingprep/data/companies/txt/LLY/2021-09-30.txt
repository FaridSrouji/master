INDIANAPOLIS, SEPT. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TIRZEPATIDE LED TO GREATER IMPROVEMENTS IN LIVER FAT CONTENT AND ABDOMINAL ADIPOSE TISSUE COMPARED TO TITRATED INSULIN DEGLUDEC IN ADULTS WITH TYPE 2 DIABETES IN AN MRI SUB-STUDY OF ELI LILLY AND COMPANY'S (NYSE: LLY) PHASE 3 SURPASS-3 CLINICAL TRIAL.1 THE RESULTS WERE PRESENTED TODAY AT THE 57TH EUROPEAN ASSOCIATION FOR THE STUDY OF DIABETES (EASD) ANNUAL MEETING IN AN EASD-SPONSORED SYMPOSIUM.
