LILLY DECLARES FOURTH-QUARTER 2021 DIVIDEND. INDIANAPOLIS, OCT. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021 OF $0.85 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 10, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 15, 2021.
