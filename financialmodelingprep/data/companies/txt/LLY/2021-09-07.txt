LILLY ANNOUNCES CASH TENDER OFFER FOR UP TO $1.5 BILLION COMBINED AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS OUTSTANDING DEBT SECURITIES. INDIANAPOLIS, SEPT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMMENCED A CASH TENDER OFFER FOR UP TO $1.5 BILLION COMBINED AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT (THE "TENDER CAP") OF SPECIFIED SERIES OF ITS OUTSTANDING DEBT SECURITIES.
