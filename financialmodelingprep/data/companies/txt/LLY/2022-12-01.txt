LILLY COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF AKOUOS EXPANDING EFFORTS TO HELP PEOPLE WITH GENETIC DISEASES. INDIANAPOLIS , DEC. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF ITS ACQUISITION OF AKOUOS, INC. (NASDAQ: AKUS). THE ACQUISITION EXPANDS LILLY'S EFFORTS IN GENETIC MEDICINES TO INCLUDE AKOUOS'S PORTFOLIO OF POTENTIAL FIRST-IN-CLASS ADENO-ASSOCIATED VIRAL GENE THERAPIES FOR THE TREATMENT OF INNER EAR CONDITIONS, INCLUDING SENSORINEURAL HEARING LOSS.
