READY FOR THE CHALLENGE? BOEHRINGER INGELHEIM AND ELI LILLY AND COMPANY LAUNCH FIRST EVER ONLINE GAME ON CARDIO-RENAL-METABOLIC CONDITIONS FOR HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONALS. RIDGEFIELD, CONN.
