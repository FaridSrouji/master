LILLY ANNOUNCES DETAILS OF PRESENTATIONS AT ESMO CONGRESS 2022. INDIANAPOLIS , SEPT. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DATA FROM STUDIES OF VERZENIO® (ABEMACICLIB) AND RETEVMO® (SELPERCATINIB) WILL BE PRESENTED AT THE 2022 EUROPEAN SOCIETY FOR MEDICAL ONCOLOGY (ESMO) CONGRESS, TO BE HELD SEPTEMBER 9-13, 2022.
