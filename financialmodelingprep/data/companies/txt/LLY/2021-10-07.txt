LOXO ONCOLOGY AT LILLY ANNOUNCES DETAILS OF PRESENTATIONS AT 2021 AACR-NCI-EORTC VIRTUAL INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON MOLECULAR TARGETS AND CANCER THERAPEUTICS. INDIANAPOLIS, OCT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOXO ONCOLOGY AT LILLY, A RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT GROUP OF ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PRECLINICAL CHARACTERIZATION DATA FOR TWO NEW AGENTS, LOXO-783 AND LOXO-435, WILL BE PRESENTED AT THE 2021 AACR-NCI-EORTC VIRTUAL INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON MOLECULAR TARGETS AND CANCER THERAPEUTICS, HELD OCTOBER 7-10, 2021. THE DATA WILL BE PRESENTED AS PART OF THE PLENARY SESSION TITLED "NEW DRUGS ON THE HORIZON II" AS WELL AS IN INDIVIDUAL POSTER PRESENTATIONS.
