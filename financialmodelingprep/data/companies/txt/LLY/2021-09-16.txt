EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR LILLY'S BAMLANIVIMAB AND ETESEVIMAB ADMINISTERED TOGETHER EXPANDED TO INCLUDE POST-EXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS FOR COVID-19. INDIANAPOLIS, SEPT. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS EXPANDED THE EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) FOR BAMLANIVIMAB 700 MG AND ETESEVIMAB 1400 MG ADMINISTERED TOGETHER TO INCLUDE POST-EXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS (PEP) IN CERTAIN INDIVIDUALS FOR THE PREVENTION OF SARS-COV-2 INFECTION, ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) ANNOUNCED TODAY.
