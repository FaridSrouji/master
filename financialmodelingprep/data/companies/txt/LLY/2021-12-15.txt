LILLY HIGHLIGHTS INNOVATION-BASED GROWTH STRATEGY AND PIPELINE DEVELOPMENTS; ANNOUNCES 2022 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE AT INVESTMENT COMMUNITY MEETING. INDIANAPOLIS, DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) IS PROVIDING EXTENSIVE UPDATES ACROSS ITS RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT (R&D) PROGRAMS TO HIGHLIGHT THE COMPANY'S STRONG PIPELINE AND POTENTIAL FOR FUTURE GROWTH. AT AN INVESTMENT COMMUNITY MEETING TODAY, THE COMPANY IS SHARING KEY INFORMATION ACROSS ITS FOUR THERAPEUTIC AREAS – INCLUDING PIPELINE UPDATES AND FUTURE R&D INVESTMENTS – ALONG WITH 2022 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE AND UPDATED 2021 GUIDANCE.
