LILLY PLANS TO INVEST $2.1 BILLION IN NEW MANUFACTURING SITES IN INDIANA. INDIANAPOLIS , MAY 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) PLANS TO EXPAND ITS MANUFACTURING FOOTPRINT IN INDIANA BY INVESTING $2.1 BILLION IN TWO NEW MANUFACTURING SITES AT INDIANA'S LEAP LEBANON INNOVATION AND RESEARCH DISTRICT IN BOONE COUNTY. THESE NEW FACILITIES WILL EXPAND LILLY'S MANUFACTURING NETWORK FOR ACTIVE INGREDIENTS AND NEW THERAPEUTIC MODALITIES, SUCH AS GENETIC MEDICINES, AND REPRESENT THE COMPANY'S DEDICATION TO STRENGTHENING ITS PORTFOLIO OF POTENTIALLY LIFE-CHANGING TREATMENTS.
