DERMIRA TO PAY ELI LILLY $40 MILLION IF DEAL TERMINATED. UPON TERMINATION OF MERGER AGREEMENT, UNDER SPECIFIED CIRCUMSTANCES CO MAY BE REQUIRED TO PAY ELI LILLY A TERMINATION FEE OF $40.0 MILLION.
LILLY ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE DERMIRA FOR $1.1 BLN. LILLY ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE DERMIRA.DERMIRA INC - LILLY TO ACQUIRE DERMIRA FOR $1.1 BILLION.DERMIRA INC - DEAL FOR FOR $18.75 PER SHARE, OR APPROXIMATELY $1.1 BILLION, IN AN ALL-CASH TRANSACTION.DERMIRA INC - LILLY WILL COMMENCE A TENDER OFFER TO ACQUIRE ALL OUTSTANDING SHARES OF DERMIRA, INC. FOR A PURCHASE PRICE OF $18.75 PER SHARE IN CASH.DERMIRA INC - TRANSACTION IS NOT SUBJECT TO ANY FINANCING CONDITION.DERMIRA - TRANSACTION WILL BE REFLECTED IN LILLY'S FINANCIAL RESULTS AND FINANCIAL GUIDANCE ACCORDING TO GAAP.DERMIRA -  LILLY WILL PROVIDE AN UPDATE TO 2020 GUIDANCE, INCLUDING EXPECTED IMPACT FROM DERMIRA DEAL, AS PART OF Q4 RESULTS ANNOUNCEMENT.DERMIRA - DERMIRA ACQUISITION WILL EXPAND LILLY'S PORTFOLIO WITH ADDITION OF QBREXZA CLOTH APPROVED BY FDA FOR TOPICAL TREATMENT OF UNDERARM SWEATING.
