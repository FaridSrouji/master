LILLY TO MAKE RECORD-BREAKING INVESTMENT IN INDIANA MANUFACTURING FACILITIES. INDIANAPOLIS, APRIL 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL INVEST AN ADDITIONAL $1.6 BILLION AND ADD ANOTHER 200 NEW JOBS AT ITS TWO NEW MANUFACTURING SITES WITHIN LEAP INNOVATION PARK IN BOONE COUNTY, BRINGING THE COMPANY'S TOTAL COMMITMENT TO $3.7 BILLION AND UP TO 700 NEW JOBS. THE COMPANY ALSO TODAY COMMITTED $15 MILLION OVER FIVE YEARS TO THE IVY TECH FOUNDATION TO FUND UP TO 1,000 SCHOLARSHIPS FOR INDIVIDUALS INTERESTED IN PURSUING CAREERS IN PHARMACEUTICAL MANUFACTURING.
