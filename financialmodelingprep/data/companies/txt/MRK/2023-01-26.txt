MERCK ANIMAL HEALTH RECEIVES U.S. FDA APPROVAL OF EXPANDED INDICATION FOR BRAVECTO (FLURALANER) CHEWS FOR DOGS. MADISON, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MERCK ANIMAL HEALTH, KNOWN AS MSD ANIMAL HEALTH OUTSIDE OF THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA, A DIVISION OF MERCK & CO., INC., RAHWAY, N.J., USA (NYSE:MRK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION'S APPROVAL OF AN EXPANDED INDICATION FOR BRAVECTO® CHEWS FOR DOGS. THE NEW INDICATION TREATS AND CONTROLS ASIAN LONGHORNED TICKS, WHICH ARE AN INVASIVE IXODID SPECIES LOCATED ACROSS MORE THAN ONE-THIRD OF THE U.S1,2.
