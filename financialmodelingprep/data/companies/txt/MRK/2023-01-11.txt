MERCK COMPLETES TENDER OFFER TO ACQUIRE IMAGO BIOSCIENCES, INC.. RAHWAY, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MERCK (NYSE: MRK), KNOWN AS MSD OUTSIDE OF THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF THE CASH TENDER OFFER, THROUGH A SUBSIDIARY, FOR ALL OF THE OUTSTANDING SHARES OF COMMON STOCK OF IMAGO BIOSCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: IMGO), AT A PURCHASE PRICE OF $36.00 PER SHARE IN CASH, WITHOUT INTEREST AND SUBJECT TO DEDUCTION FOR ANY REQUIRED TAX WITHHOLDING. AS OF THE TENDER OFFER EXPIRATION AT ONE MINUTE AFTER 11:59 P.M., EASTERN TIME, ON JANUARY 10, 2023, 31,307,028 SHARES OF COMMON STOCK OF IMAGO WERE VALIDLY TENDERED AND NOT WITHDRAWN FROM THE TENDER OFFER, REPRESENTING APPROXIMATELY 89.2% OF THE TOTAL NUMBER OF IMAGO'S OUTSTANDING SHARES ON A FULLY DILUTED BASIS. ALL SUCH SHARES HAVE BEEN ACCEPTED FOR PAYMENT IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE TERMS OF THE TENDER OFFER, AND MERCK EXPECTS TO PROMPTLY PAY FOR SUCH SHARES.
