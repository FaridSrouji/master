FDA APPROVES KOSELUGO FOR PEDIATRIC PATIENTS 2 YEARS AND OLDER WITH NEUROFIBROMATOSIS TYPE 1. KOSELUGO® (SELUMETINIB) APPROVED BY FDA FOR PEDIATRIC PATIENTS TWO YEARS AND OLDER WITH NEUROFIBROMATOSIS TYPE 1 AND SYMPTOMATIC, INOPERABLE PLEXIFORM NEUROFIBROMAS.MERCK & CO -  FDA APPROVAL IS BASED ON POSITIVE RESULTS FROM PHASE 2 SPRINT STRATUM 1 TRIAL.
