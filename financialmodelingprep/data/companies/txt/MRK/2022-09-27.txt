MERCK ANIMAL HEALTH COMPLETES MINORITY INVESTMENT IN LEEO PRECISION FARMING. MADISON, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MERCK ANIMAL HEALTH, KNOWN AS MSD ANIMAL HEALTH OUTSIDE OF THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA, A DIVISION OF MERCK & CO., INC., RAHWAY, N.J., USA (NYSE:MRK), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED A MINORITY INVESTMENT IN LEEO PRECISION FARMING B.V. (LEEO). MERCK ANIMAL HEALTH ALSO WILL ASSUME DISTRIBUTION OF LEEO'S DIGITAL SWINE TRACEABILITY SOLUTION IN SELECTED MARKETS OVER THE COMING MONTHS.
