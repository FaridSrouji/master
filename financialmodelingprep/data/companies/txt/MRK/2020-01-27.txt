FDA APPROVES MERCK’S DIFICID TO TREAT CLOSTRIDIOIDES DIFFICILE IN CHILDREN AGED SIX MONTHS AND OLDER. FDA APPROVES MERCK’S DIFICID® (FIDAXOMICIN) TO TREAT CLOSTRIDIOIDES DIFFICILE IN CHILDREN AGED SIX MONTHS AND OLDER.MERCK & CO - APPLICATIONS FOR BOTH DIFICID FOR ORAL SUSPENSION & DIFICID TABLETS FOR TREATMENT OF CLOSTRIDIOIDES RECEIVED PRIORITY REVIEW CLASSIFICATION BY FDA.MERCK & CO - DIFICID IS NOT EXPECTED TO BE EFFECTIVE FOR TREATMENT OF OTHER TYPES OF INFECTIONS DUE TO MINIMAL SYSTEMIC ABSORPTION OF FIDAXOMICIN.
