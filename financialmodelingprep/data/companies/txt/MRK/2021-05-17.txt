GABRIELLE UNION-WADE JOINS 'DON'T SKIP' CAMPAIGN TO ENCOURAGE DOCTOR WELL-VISITS AND RECOMMENDED VACCINATIONS. WASHINGTON AND KENILWORTH, N.J., MAY 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VACCINATE YOUR FAMILY, IN COLLABORATION WITH MERCK (NYSE: MRK), KNOWN AS MSD OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE LAUNCH OF A NEW PUBLIC HEALTH CAMPAIGN CALLED DON'T SKIP WITH ACTRESS, ACTIVIST AND AUTHOR GABRIELLE UNION-WADE TO ENCOURAGE FAMILIES TO SCHEDULE THEIR WELL-VISITS AND STAY UP TO DATE ON ALL RECOMMENDED VACCINES.
