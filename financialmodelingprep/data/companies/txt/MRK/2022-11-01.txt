VEEVA AND MERCK FORM LONG-TERM STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP. TEN-YEAR PARTNERSHIP WILL HELP MERCK REDUCE OPERATIONAL COSTS, DELIVER VALUE TO PATIENTS, AND OPTIMIZE THE HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONAL AND PATIENT EXPERIENCE PLEASANTON, CALIF. , NOV. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VEEVA SYSTEMS (NYSE: VEEV) TODAY ANNOUNCED A TEN-YEAR STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT WITH MERCK (NYSE: MRK), KNOWN AS MSD OUTSIDE OF THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA, THAT BUILDS ON THE EXISTING 12-YEAR PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN THE COMPANIES.
