MERCK ANNOUNCES TWO US REGULATORY MILESTONES FOR KEYTRUDA® (PEMBROLIZUMAB) IN TRIPLE-NEGATIVE BREAST CANCER. MERCK ANNOUNCES TWO US REGULATORY MILESTONES FOR KEYTRUDA® (PEMBROLIZUMAB) IN TRIPLE-NEGATIVE BREAST CANCER (TNBC).MERCK & CO INC - FDA ACCEPTS SBLA FOR KEYTRUDA FOR TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH HIGH-RISK EARLY-STAGE TNBC BASED ON KEYNOTE-522 TRIAL.MERCK - FDA GRANTS PRIORITY REVIEW TO SBLA FOR KEYTRUDA+CHEMOTHERAPY FOR TREATMENT OF SOME PATIENTS WITH METASTATIC TNBC BASED ON KEYNOTE-355 TRIAL.MERCK & CO INC - FDA GRANTS PRIORITY REVIEW TO SBLA FOR KEYTRUDA PLUS CHEMOTHERAPY FOR TREATMENT OF CERTAIN PATIENTS WITH METASTATIC TNBC.MERCK & CO INC - FDA HAS SET A PRESCRIPTION DRUG USER FEE ACT OR TARGET ACTION, DATE OF NOV. 28, 2020.MERCK - PDUFA FOR NEW SBLA FOR KEYTRUDA FOR TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH HIGH-RISK EARLY-STAGE TNBC, IN COMBINATION WITH CHEMOTHERAPY IS MARCH 29, 2021.
