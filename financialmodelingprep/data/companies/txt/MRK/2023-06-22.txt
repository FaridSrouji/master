IRBM EXTENDS PEPTIDE THERAPEUTICS COLLABORATION WITH MERCK & CO. INC., RAHWAY NJ USA. ROME, JUNE 22, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- IRBM, AN INNOVATIVE CONTRACT RESEARCH ORGANIZATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A NEW AGREEMENT WITH MERCK & CO. INC., RAHWAY NJ USA, KNOWN AS MSD OUTSIDE THE USA AND CANADA, TO CONTINUE THEIR COLLABORATION IN THE PEPTIDE THERAPEUTICS AREA.
