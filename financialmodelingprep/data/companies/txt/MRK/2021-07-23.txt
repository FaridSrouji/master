MERCK ANNOUNCES APPOINTMENT OF HEAD OF CHINA & INTERNATIONAL FOR HEALTHCARE. DARMSTADT, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MERCK, A LEADING SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT HONG CHOW WILL JOIN ITS HEALTHCARE BUSINESS SECTOR AS HEAD OF CHINA & INTERNATIONAL ON OCTOBER 1. IN THIS ROLE AT MERCK, CHOW WILL LEAD CHINA, JAPAN AS WELL THE REGIONS EUROPE, LATIN AMERICA, ASIA-PACIFIC AND MIDDLE EAST, AFRICA AND RUSSIA, THE FRANCHISE OF CARDIOVASCULAR METABOLISM & ENDOCRINOLOGY (CM&E), AND THE GLOBAL BUSINESS INNOVATION TEAM. CHOW WILL REPORT TO PETER GUENT
