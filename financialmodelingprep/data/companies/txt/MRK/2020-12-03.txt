BETTERINVESTING™ MAGAZINE CHOOSES UNITED RENTALS AS "GROWTH" STOCK AND MERCK AS "UNDERVALUED" FOR MARCH 2021 ISSUE. MADISON HEIGHTS, MICH., DEC. 3, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE EDITORIAL ADVISORY AND SECURITIES REVIEW COMMITTEE OF BETTERINVESTING MAGAZINE TODAY ANNOUNCED UNITED RENTALS INC. (NYSE: URI) AS ITS "STOCK TO STUDY" AND MERCK & CO., INC. (NYSE: MRK) ITS "UNDERVALUED STOCK" IN THE MARCH 2021 ISSUE...
