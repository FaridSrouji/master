FDA APPROVES KEYTRUDA® (PEMBROLIZUMAB) PLUS LENVIMA® (LENVATINIB) COMBINATION FOR FIRST-LINE TREATMENT OF ADULT PATIENTS WITH ADVANCED RENAL CELL CARCINOMA (RCC). KENILWORTH, N.J. & WOODCLIFF LAKE, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $MRK #MRK--MERCK (NYSE: MRK), KNOWN AS MSD OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA, AND EISAI TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED THE COMBINATION OF KEYTRUDA, MERCK'S ANTI-PD-1 THERAPY, PLUS LENVIMA, THE ORALLY AVAILABLE MULTIPLE RECEPTOR TYROSINE KINASE INHIBITOR DISCOVERED BY EISAI, FOR THE FIRST-LINE TREATMENT OF ADULT PATIENTS WITH ADVANCED RENAL CELL CARCINOMA (RCC).
