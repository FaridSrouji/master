MERCK AND HANMI PHARMACEUTICAL ENTER INTO LICENSING AGREEMENT TO DEVELOP EFINOPEGDUTIDE. MERCK AND HANMI PHARMACEUTICAL ENTER INTO LICENSING AGREEMENT TO DEVELOP EFINOPEGDUTIDE, AN INVESTIGATIONAL ONCE-WEEKLY THERAPY FOR NONALCOHOLIC STEATOHEPATITIS (NASH).UNDER AGREEMENT, MERCK WILL BE GRANTED EXCLUSIVE LICENSE TO DEVELOP, MANUFACTURE, COMMERCIALIZE EFINOPEGDUTIDE IN US AND GLOBALLY.MERCK - DATA FROM PHASE 2 STUDIES HAS PROVIDED COMPELLING CLINICAL EVIDENCE THAT WARRANTS FURTHER EVALUATION OF EFINOPEGDUTIDE FOR TREATMENT OF NASH.HANMI WILL RECEIVE UPFRONT PAYMENT OF $10 MILLION & IS ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE MILESTONE PAYMENTS UP TO $860 MILLION ASSOCIATED WITH DEVELOPMENT.HANMI RETAINS AN OPTION TO COMMERCIALIZE EFINOPEGDUTIDE IN KOREA.
