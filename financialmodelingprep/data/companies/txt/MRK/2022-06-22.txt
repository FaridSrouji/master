CDC'S ACIP UNANIMOUSLY VOTES TO PROVISIONALLY RECOMMEND USE OF MERCK'S VAXNEUVANCE™ (PNEUMOCOCCAL 15-VALENT CONJUGATE VACCINE) AS AN OPTION FOR PNEUMOCOCCAL VACCINATION IN INFANTS AND CHILDREN. RAHWAY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $MRK #MRK--CDC'S ACIP UNANIMOUSLY VOTES TO PROVISIONALLY RECOMMEND USE OF MERCK'S VAXNEUVANCE™ AS AN OPTION FOR PNEUMOCOCCAL VACCINATION IN INFANTS AND CHILDREN
U.S. FDA APPROVES MERCK'S VAXNEUVANCE™ (PNEUMOCOCCAL 15-VALENT CONJUGATE VACCINE) FOR THE PREVENTION OF INVASIVE PNEUMOCOCCAL DISEASE IN INFANTS AND CHILDREN.
