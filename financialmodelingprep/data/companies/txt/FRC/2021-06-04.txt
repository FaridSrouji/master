THE PROMISE HOMES COMPANY SECURES MULTI-MILLION DOLLAR EQUITY INVESTMENT FROM LEADING U.S. BANK; CRA-ELIGIBLE CAPITAL. ATLANTA & SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PROMISE HOMES COMPANY (TPHC), ONE OF THE LARGEST MINORITY-LED OWNERS OF INSTITUTIONAL-QUALITY, SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL RENTAL PROPERTY IN THE UNITED STATES THAT SERVES WORKING-CLASS AND MIDDLE-CLASS COMMUNITIES ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FIRST REPUBLIC BANK (NYSE: FRC), A LEADING PRIVATE BANK AND WEALTH MANAGEMENT COMPANY, HAS MADE A MULTI-MILLION DOLLAR PURCHASE OF PREFERRED UNITS.
