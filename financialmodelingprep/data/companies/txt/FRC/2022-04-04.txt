FIRST REPUBLIC BANK DECLARES DIVIDENDS ON FIVE SERIES OF PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIRST REPUBLIC BANK (NYSE:FRC), A LEADING PRIVATE BANK AND WEALTH MANAGEMENT COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED CASH DIVIDENDS ON THE FOLLOWING OUTSTANDING SERIES OF ITS PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK. A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $11.750 PER SHARE WAS DECLARED ON ITS NONCUMULATIVE PERPETUAL SERIES J PREFERRED STOCK.
