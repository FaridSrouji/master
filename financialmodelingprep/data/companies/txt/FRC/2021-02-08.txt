WEALTH MANAGEMENT TEAM LED BY MICHAEL KRAMER JOINS FIRST REPUBLIC. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIRST REPUBLIC BANK (NYSE:FRC), A LEADING PRIVATE BANK AND WEALTH MANAGEMENT COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT A WEALTH MANAGEMENT TEAM LED BY MICHAEL KRAMER HAS JOINED FIRST REPUBLIC INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT IN NEW YORK CITY. KRAMER, WHO WAS NAMED MANAGING DIRECTOR AND WEALTH MANAGER, IS JOINED BY TWO ASSOCIATES.
