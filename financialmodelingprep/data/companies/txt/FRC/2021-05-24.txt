WEALTH MANAGERS SCOTT GRENERT AND KEVIN MACINTYRE JOIN FIRST REPUBLIC IN BOSTON. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIRST REPUBLIC BANK (NYSE:FRC), A LEADING PRIVATE BANK AND WEALTH MANAGEMENT COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SCOTT GRENERT AND KEVIN MACINTYRE HAVE JOINED FIRST REPUBLIC INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT IN BOSTON. GRENERT AND MACINYTYRE WERE EACH NAMED MANAGING DIRECTOR AND WEALTH MANAGER. THEY WILL PROVIDE PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT, RETIREMENT PLANNING, INVESTMENT CONSULTING AND OTHER WEALTH MANAGEMENT SERVICES TO INDIVIDUALS, FAMILIES, NONPROFITS, AND PRIVATE FAMILY FOUNDATIONS.
