FIRST REPUBLIC BANK PRICES COMMON STOCK OFFERING. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIRST REPUBLIC BANK (“FIRST REPUBLIC”) (NYSE:FRC), A LEADING PRIVATE BANK AND WEALTH MANAGEMENT COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 2,500,000 SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK FOR EXPECTED GROSS PROCEEDS OF APPROXIMATELY $350 MILLION BEFORE UNDERWRITING DISCOUNTS AND COMMISSIONS AND ESTIMATED OFFERING EXPENSES.
