NORTHERN LIGHT HEALTH AND OPTUM LAUNCH STRATEGIC RELATIONSHIP TO ENHANCE THE HEALTH CARE EXPERIENCE. BREWER, MAINE & EDEN PRAIRIE, MINN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--NORTHERN LIGHT HEALTH AND OPTUM TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC RELATIONSHIP TO ENHANCE THE HEALTH CARE EXPERIENCE FOR PATIENTS AND PROVIDERS THROUGHOUT MAINE. AS PART OF THIS RELATIONSHIP, SELECT NORTHERN LIGHT HEALTH EMPLOYEES WILL BECOME PART OF OPTUM, PROVIDING THEM WITH NEW OPPORTUNITIES FOR GROWTH AND CAREER ADVANCEMENT WHILE THEY CONTINUE TO SUPPORT NORTHERN LIGHT HEALTH.
