SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISS LAW INVESTIGATES LHC GROUP, INC.. NEW YORK , MARCH 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISS LAW IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF LHC GROUP, INC. ("LHC GROUP" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: LHCG), IN CONNECTION WITH THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF THE COMPANY BY OPTUM, PART OF UNITEDHEALTH GROUP (NYSE: UNH).  UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $170.00 IN CASH FOR EACH SHARE OF LHC GROUP COMMON STOCK OWNED.
