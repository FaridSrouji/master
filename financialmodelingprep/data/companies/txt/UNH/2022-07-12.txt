UNITEDHEALTH GROUP COMMITS $25 MILLION TO NEW ENGLAND HEALTHY NEIGHBORHOODS FUND. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITEDHEALTH GROUP (NYSE: UNH) TODAY ANNOUNCED A $25 MILLION COMMITMENT TO THE HEALTHY NEIGHBORHOODS EQUITY FUND II (HNEF II). THE FUND, LAUNCHED BY CONSERVATION LAW FOUNDATION (CLF) AND MASSACHUSETTS HOUSING INVESTMENT CORPORATION (MHIC), WAS CREATED TO FINANCE INCLUSIVE MIXED-USE DEVELOPMENTS NEAR PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION THAT OFFER RETAIL, HOUSING AND SOCIAL SERVICES TO SUPPORT HEALTHIER COMMUNITIES.
