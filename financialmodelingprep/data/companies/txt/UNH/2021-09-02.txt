UNITED HEALTH FOUNDATION DONATES $1 MILLION TO SUPPORT RESIDENTS OF LOUISIANA AFFECTED BY HURRICANE IDA. MINNETONKA, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE UNITED HEALTH FOUNDATION, THE PHILANTHROPIC FOUNDATION OF UNITEDHEALTH GROUP (NYSE:UNH) – THE PARENT COMPANY OF UNITEDHEALTHCARE AND OPTUM – ANNOUNCED TODAY A $1 MILLION DONATION TO HELP LOUISIANA RESIDENTS RECOVER AND REBUILD FOLLOWING THE DEVASTATING IMPACT OF HURRICANE IDA.
