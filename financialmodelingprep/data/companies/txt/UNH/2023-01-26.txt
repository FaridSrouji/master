UNITEDHEALTH GROUP INVESTS $10 MILLION IN NEW FUND, INVEST APPALACHIA, TO ADDRESS SOCIAL NEEDS AND ADVANCE ECONOMIC EQUITY. MINNETONKA, MINN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--UNITEDHEALTH GROUP (NYSE: UNH) HAS COMMITTED $10 MILLION AS A LEAD INVESTOR IN THE FIRST ROUND OF THE INVEST APPALACHIA (IA) FUND, A REGIONAL INITIATIVE LAUNCHED TO ACCELERATE FUNDING IN COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ACROSS APPALACHIAN COUNTIES IN KENTUCKY, NORTH CAROLINA, OHIO, TENNESSEE, VIRGINIA AND WEST VIRGINIA.
