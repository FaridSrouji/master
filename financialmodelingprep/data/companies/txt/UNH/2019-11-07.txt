UNITEDHEALTH GROUP INCORPORATED ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 17, 2019. THE UNITEDHEALTH GROUP BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED PAYMENT OF A CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.08 PER SHARE, TO BE PAID ON DECEMBER 17, 2019, TO ALL SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD OF UNITEDHEALTH GROUP COMMON STOCK AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 9, 2019.
