UNITEDHEALTHCARE TO HELP MILLIONS OF AARP MEMBERS SAVE ON HEARING AIDS. MINNETONKA, MINN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--UNITEDHEALTHCARE IS NOW ADMINISTERING AARP HEARING SOLUTIONS, HELPING MAKE IT EASIER AND MORE AFFORDABLE FOR MILLIONS OF AMERICANS TO PURCHASE PRESCRIPTION AND OVER-THE-COUNTER (OTC) HEARING AIDS.
