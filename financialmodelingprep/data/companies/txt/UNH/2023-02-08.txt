UNITEDHEALTHCARE INTRODUCES NEW REWARDS PROGRAM WITH A MODERN APPROACH TO WELL-BEING. MINNETONKA, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITEDHEALTHCARE HAS INTRODUCED UNITEDHEALTHCARE REWARDS, A NEW REWARDS PROGRAM IN WHICH ELIGIBLE MEMBERS, INCLUDING SPOUSES, CAN USE WEARABLE DEVICES TO EARN UP TO $1,000 EACH PER YEAR BY COMPLETING VARIOUS DAILY HEALTH GOALS AND ONE-TIME ACTIVITIES.
