UNITED HEALTH FOUNDATION LAUNCHES $2.6 MILLION PARTNERSHIP WITH TOURO UNIVERSITY NEVADA TO IMPROVE MATERNAL HEALTH AND REDUCE DISPARITIES IN UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES. HENDERSON, NEV.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE UNITED HEALTH FOUNDATION, THE PHILANTHROPIC FOUNDATION OF UNITEDHEALTH GROUP (NYSE: UNH), IS PARTNERING WITH TOURO UNIVERSITY NEVADA (TOURO) TO IMPROVE ACCESS TO HIGH-QUALITY PRENATAL CARE FOR UNDERSERVED AND HOMELESS WOMEN IN SOUTHERN NEVADA. THE THREE-YEAR, $2.6 MILLION PARTNERSHIP WILL HELP REDUCE MATERNAL HEALTH DISPARITIES AND INCREASE THE STATE'S OB-GYN AND FAMILY MEDICINE PHYSICIAN WORKFORCE.
