OPTUM IDAHO DONATES $200,000 TO INCREASE MENTAL HEALTH CARE ACCESS STATEWIDE. BOISE, IDAHO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--OPTUM IDAHO, THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION FOR THE STATE OF IDAHO'S MEDICAID BEHAVIORAL HEALTH PLAN AND PART OF UNITEDHEALTH GROUP (NYSE: UNH), HAS AWARDED $200,000 TO THE UNIVERSITY OF IDAHO'S PROJECT ECHO (EXTENSION FOR COMMUNITY HEALTHCARE OUTCOMES) AIMED AT INCREASING MENTAL HEALTH CARE ACCESS STATEWIDE FOR ADULTS, CHILDREN AND FAMILIES. THE ENTIRE STATE OF IDAHO IS DESIGNATED AS A MENTAL HEALTH PROVIDER SHORTAGE AREA BY THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN
