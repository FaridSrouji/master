CHANGE HEALTHCARE INVESTOR ALERT BY THE FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA: KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC INVESTIGATES ADEQUACY OF PRICE AND PROCESS IN PROPOSED SALE OF CHANGE HEALTHCARE - CHNG. NEW ORLEANS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA CHARLES C. FOTI, JR., ESQ. AND THE LAW FIRM OF KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC (“KSF”) ARE INVESTIGATING THE PROPOSED SALE OF CHANGE HEALTHCARE (NASDAQGS: CHNG) TO UNITEDHEALTH GROUP (NYSE: UNH). UNDER THE TERMS OF THE PROPOSED TRANSACTION, SHAREHOLDERS OF CHANGE WILL RECEIVE ONLY $25.75 IN CASH FOR EACH SHARE OF CHANGE THAT THEY OWN. KSF IS SEEKING TO DETERMINE WHETHER THIS CONSIDERATION AND THE PROCESS THAT LED TO IT ARE ADEQUATE,
