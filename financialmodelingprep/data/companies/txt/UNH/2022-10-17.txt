UNITEDHEALTHCARE INVESTS $5 MILLION IN MORGAN'S WONDERLAND TO PROMOTE HEALTH AND EMPLOYMENT EQUITY FOR INDIVIDUALS WITH DISABILITIES. SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITEDHEALTHCARE COMMUNITY PLAN OF TEXAS TODAY ANNOUNCED A $5 MILLION INVESTMENT IN THE MULTI-ASSISTANCE CENTER AT MORGAN'S WONDERLAND (THE MAC), A NEW ONE-STOP-FACILITY OFFERING MEDICAL AND NON-MEDICAL SERVICES FOR INDIVIDUALS WITH DISABILITIES.
