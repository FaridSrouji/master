CONSUMERS WANT BETTER DIGITAL TOOLS FOR FINDING HEALTH CARE: SURVEY. EDEN PRAIRIE, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--A GROWING NUMBER OF HEALTH CARE CONSUMERS PREFER MORE SEAMLESS DIGITAL EXPERIENCES IN SCHEDULING AND INTERACTING WITH THEIR HEALTH CARE PROVIDER AND HEALTH PLAN, ACCORDING TO THE RESULTS OF A SURVEY OPTUM ANNOUNCED TODAY. MANY OF THESE CONSUMERS, ESPECIALLY YOUNGER ADULTS, ARE NOT SATISFIED WITH THEIR CURRENT EXPERIENCE, THE SURVEY SHOWED.
