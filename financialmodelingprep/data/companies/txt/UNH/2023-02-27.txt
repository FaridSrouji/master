UNITEDHEALTH GROUP BOARD AUTHORIZES PAYMENT OF QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. MINNETONKA, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE UNITEDHEALTH GROUP (NYSE: UNH) BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED PAYMENT OF A CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.65 PER SHARE, TO BE PAID MARCH 21, 2023, TO COMMON STOCK SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS MARCH 13, 2023.
