UNITEDHEALTH GROUP CONTINUES EFFORTS TO COMBAT COVID-19, REPORTS Q2 RESULTS. UNITEDHEALTH GROUP CONTINUES EFFORTS TO COMBAT COVID-19, REPORTS SECOND QUARTER PERFORMANCE.SEES FY 2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE $15.45 TO $15.75.Q2 2020 NET EARNINGS AND ADJUSTED NET EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $6.91 AND $7.12.Q2 2020 REVENUES INCREASED $1.5 BILLION TO $62.1 BILLION.Q2 MEDICAL CARE RATIO WAS IMPACTED BY TEMPORARY DEFERRAL OF CARE DUE TO PANDEMIC, DECLINING TO 70.2% FROM 83.1% LAST YEAR.SEES FY 2020 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $16.25 TO $16.55.EXPECTS MEDICAL CARE RATIO TO RISE IN COMING QUARTERS AS NEEDED CARE IS ACCESSED.UNITEDHEALTH -EXPECTS RESULTS WILL BE OFFSET IN QUARTERS AHEAD BY ASSISTANCE MEASURES ALREADY TAKEN, RESUMPTION OF DEFERRED CARE, FUTURE COVID-19 COST.UNITEDHEALTH GROUP - OPTUM SECOND QUARTER REVENUE & OPERATING EARNINGS OF $32.7 BILLION & $2.2 BILLION RESPECTIVELY ADVANCED FROM $28.0 BILLION & $2.1 BILLION A YEAR AGO.UNITEDHEALTH - AS PANDEMIC ADVANCED, ACCESS TO AND DEMAND FOR CARE WAS MOST CONSTRAINED FROM MID-MARCH THROUGH APRIL.UNITEDHEALTH - DEMAND FOR CARE BEGAN TO RECOVER IN MAY AND APPROACHED MORE TYPICAL LEVELS BY END OF Q2.MAINTAINING ITS FULL YEAR EARNINGS PER SHARE OUTLOOK FOR 2020.EXPECT ANOTHER $1 BILLION IN REBATES TO BE PAID IN FUTURE PERIODS.UNITEDHEALTH - PROVIDED $1.5 BILLION IN DIRECT CUSTOMER AND CONSUMER SUPPORT THROUGH PREMIUM CREDITS, COST-SHARING WAIVERS AND OTHER EFFORTS.ANNOUNCED THAT WE WILL NOT REQUEST, NOR DO WE INTEND TO RETAIN, ANY GOVERNMENT ASSISTANCE.
