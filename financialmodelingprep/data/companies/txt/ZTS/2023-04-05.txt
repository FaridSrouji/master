ZOETIS FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES FIRST ROUND OF 2023 GRANTS TO ADVANCE FARMER AND VETERINARIAN EDUCATION, LIVELIHOODS AND WELL-BEING. PARSIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $ZTS #DRIVENTOCARE--TODAY, THE ZOETIS FOUNDATION ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL DISTRIBUTE $5.3 MILLION DURING ITS FIRST ROUND OF 2023 GRANTS, SUPPORTING 21 INITIATIVES IMPACTING 15 COUNTRIES TO HELP ENABLE THRIVING PROFESSIONS AND LIVELIHOODS FOR LIVESTOCK FARMERS AND VETERINARY PROFESSIONALS, AS WELL AS RELIEF EFFORTS FOR THOSE IMPACTED BY THE EARTHQUAKE IN TÜRKIYE.
