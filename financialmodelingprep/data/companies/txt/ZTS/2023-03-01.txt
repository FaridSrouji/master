ZOETIS SECURES $15.3 MILLION GRANT TO ADVANCE SUSTAINABLE LIVESTOCK PRODUCTION AND IMPROVE LIVELIHOODS IN SUB-SAHARAN AFRICA. PARSIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $ZTS #DRIVENTOCARE--ZOETIS IS PLEASED TO RECEIVE A $15.3 MILLION GRANT FROM THE BILL & MELINDA GATES FOUNDATION TO FURTHER DEVELOP AND INTEGRATE INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS TO ADVANCE VETERINARY CARE AND DIAGNOSTIC SERVICES THAT WILL ULTIMATELY IMPROVE LIVESTOCK HEALTH AND PRODUCTIVITY IN SUB-SAHARAN AFRICA.
