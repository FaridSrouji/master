ZOETIS ANNOUNCES FDA APPROVAL OF SOLENSIA™ (FRUNEVETMAB INJECTION) TO CONTROL OSTEOARTHRITIS PAIN IN CATS. PARSIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $ZTS #ANIMALHEALTH--ZOETIS TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED SOLENSIA™ TO CONTROL THE PAIN OF OSTEOARTHRITIS (OA) IN CATS.
