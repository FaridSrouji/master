ZOETIS DECLARES THIRD QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. PARSIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $ZTS #ANIMALHEALTH--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ZOETIS INC. (NYSE:ZTS) HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.325 PER SHARE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 1, 2022, TO ALL HOLDERS OF RECORD OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON THURSDAY, JULY 21, 2022.
