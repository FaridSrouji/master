IQVIA PUBLISHES ITS 2022 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE REPORT. RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IQVIA (NYSE:IQV) HAS PUBLISHED ITS 2022 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) REPORT. THE ANNUAL REPORT PROVIDES A DETAILED ACCOUNT OF THE ORGANIZATION'S FOCUS ON REDUCING ITS ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT AND COMMITMENT TO ACCELERATING POSITIVE OUTCOMES FOR PUBLIC HEALTH AND PATIENTS.
