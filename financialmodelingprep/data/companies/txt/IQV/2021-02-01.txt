IQVIA NAMED TO FORTUNE'S 2021 LIST OF “WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES”. DANBURY, CONN. & RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IQVIA HOLDINGS INC. (“IQVIA”) (NYSE: IQV) HAS BEEN NAMED TO FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S “WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES” LIST. IQVIA HAS RECEIVED THIS DISTINCTION EVERY YEAR, FOLLOWING THE MERGER BETWEEN IMS HEALTH AND QUINTILES IN 2016.
