IQVIA LAUNCHES RIM SMART LABELING TO DELIVER INTELLIGENCE-DRIVEN APPROACH FOR GLOBAL LABEL MANAGEMENT. INNOVATION PARK, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IQVIA™ (NYSE:IQV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF IQVIA RIM SMART LABELING, A COMPREHENSIVE LABELING SOLUTION FOR THE LIFE SCIENCES INDUSTRY THAT PROVIDES AN INTELLIGENCE-DRIVEN APPROACH FOR MORE EFFICIENT AND COMPLIANT GLOBAL LABEL MANAGEMENT. THROUGH THIS UNIQUE SOLUTION, LIFE SCIENCES COMPANIES CAN CONSOLIDATE REGULATORY LABEL PLANNING, TRACKING AND EXECUTION SYSTEMS AND WORKFLOWS INTO ONE STREAMLINED PROCESS. RIM SMART LABELING IS PART OF IQVIA'S SUITE.
