IQVIA SELECTED TO SUPPORT EMA'S DARWIN EU® COORDINATION CENTRE INITIATIVE. AMSTERDAM--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IQVIA™ (NYSE:IQV), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF ADVANCED ANALYTICS, TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS AND CLINICAL RESEARCH SERVICES TO THE LIFE SCIENCES INDUSTRY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EUROPEAN MEDICINES AGENCY (EMA) HAS AWARDED A CONTRACT TO ERASMUS UNIVERSITY MEDICAL CENTER ROTTERDAM TO PROVIDE ANALYTICS SERVICES TO THE DARWIN EU® COORDINATION CENTRE WHICH IQVIA WAS SELECTED TO SUPPORT. IQVIA WILL ALSO WORK ALONGSIDE THE UNIVERSITY OF OXFORD, SYNAPSE, THE HYVE, AND ODYSSEUS
