LASSO ANNOUNCES A SIGNIFICANT STEP FORWARD IN HEALTHCARE MARKETING MEASUREMENT. THE HEALTHCARE MARKETING AND ANALYTICS LEADER EXPANDS THEIR MEASUREMENT SUITE TO INCLUDE UNPRECEDENTED AUDIENCE AND CAMPAIGN INSIGHTS. AUSTIN, TEXAS , JAN. 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LASSO, AN IQVIA (IQV) BUSINESS AND PROVIDER OF A LEADING HEALTHCARE MARKETING AND ANALYTICS OPERATING SYSTEM, ANNOUNCED THE INTEGRATION OF IQVIA'S AIM XR (AUDIENCE IDENTITY MANAGER® XR) INTO THEIR OPERATING SYSTEM, GIVING OVER 4,000 MEDICAL PUBLISHERS ACCESS TO TIMELY INSIGHTS ON CAMPAIGN REACH AND EFFECTIVENESS.
