HASBRO, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 18, 2020. HASBRO, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 18, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 1, 2020.
