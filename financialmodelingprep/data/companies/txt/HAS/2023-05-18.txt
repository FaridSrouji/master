HASBRO ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COMMON SHARES. PAWTUCKET, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $HAS #HAS--HASBRO, INC. (NASDAQ: HAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.70 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON AUGUST 15, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 1, 2023.
