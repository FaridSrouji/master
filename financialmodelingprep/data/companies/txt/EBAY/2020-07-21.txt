SCHIBSTED ACQUIRES EBAY CLASSIFIEDS IN DENMARK. REG-SCHIBSTED ASA (SCHA/SCHB) – SCHIBSTED SUPPORTS ADEVINTA’S ACQUISITION OF EBAY CLASSIFIEDS GROUP, AND ACQUIRES EBAY CLASSIFIEDS IN DENMARK.SCHIBSTED'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND MANAGEMENT STRONGLY SUPPORTS AGREEMENT BETWEEN ADEVINTA AND EBAY.WE ARE CONFIDENT THAT IT WILL FURTHER STRENGTHEN VALUE CREATION POTENTIAL FOR SCHIBSTED AND REST OF ADEVINTA'S SHAREHOLDERS.SCHIBSTED WILL, WITH AN OWNERSHIP STAKE AROUND 33 PERCENT OF CAPITAL AND AROUND 39.5 PERCENT OF VOTES,.SCHIBSTED ASA AND BLOMMENHOLM INDUSTRIER AS HAVE IRREVOCABLY COMMITTED TO VOTE IN FAVOR OF TRANSACTION IN ADEVINTA'S GENERAL MEETING.AGREED NOT TO SELL ANY SHARES DURING FIRST THREE MONTHS.SCHIBSTED'S ACQUISITION OF EBAY CLASSIFIEDS' DANISH OPERATIONS WILL BE FINANCED THROUGH A BRIDGE LOAN WITH UP TO THREE YEARS MATURITY,.RECEIVED A PRE-CONSENT FOR A TEMPORARY WAIVER OF OUR FINANCIAL COVENANT FOR OUR EXISTING FINANCING..WE HAVE CLEAR GOAL TO REDUCE NIBD/EBITDA BACK AGAIN TO WITHIN TARGET RANGE OF 1-3..BRIDGE LOAN WILL BE PAID DOWN PRIMARILY THROUGH A FUTURE DISPOSAL OF ADEVINTA SHARES..CITIGROUP ACTED AS FINANCIAL ADVISORS TO ADEVINTA..
