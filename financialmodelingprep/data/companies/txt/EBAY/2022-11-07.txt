EBAY INC. ANNOUNCES PRICING $1.15 BILLION SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES OFFERING. SAN JOSE, CALIF. , NOV. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EBAY INC. (NASDAQ: EBAY) (THE "COMPANY" OR "EBAY") ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF A $1,150,000,000 UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF ITS SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES, CONSISTING OF $425,000,000 OF 5.900% NOTES DUE 2025 (THE "2025 NOTES"), $300,000,000 OF 5.950% NOTES DUE 2027 (THE "2027 NOTES") AND $425,000,000 OF 6.300% NOTES DUE 2032 (THE "2032 NOTES").
