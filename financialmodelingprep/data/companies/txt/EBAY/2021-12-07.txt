EBAY ANNOUNCES 2021 CLASS OF UP & RUNNING GRANT RECIPIENTS. SAN JOSE, CALIF., DEC. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EBAY INC.  (NASDAQ: EBAY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RECIPIENTS OF ITS SECOND ANNUAL UP & RUNNING GRANT PROGRAM, WHICH WAS CREATED TO SUPPORT SMALL BUSINESSES WITH INCREMENTAL FUNDING AND BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT RESOURCES.
