EBAY ANNOUNCES CHANGE TO GROSS MERCHANDISE VOLUME DEFINITION AND RELEASES UPDATED HISTORICAL METRICS. SAN JOSE, CALIF., DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EBAY INC. (NASDAQ: EBAY), A GLOBAL COMMERCE LEADER THAT CONNECTS MILLIONS OF BUYERS AND SELLERS AROUND THE WORLD, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS CHANGED ITS DEFINITION OF GROSS MERCHANDISE VOLUME (GMV) TO ALIGN WITH CUSTOMER MONEY FLOWS ON ITS PLATFORMS.
