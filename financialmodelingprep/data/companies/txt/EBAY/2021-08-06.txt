EBAY LAUNCHES 2021 "UP & RUNNING GRANTS" TO SUPPORT SMALL BUSINESS SUCCESS. SAN JOSE, CALIF., AUG. 6, 2021  /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, EBAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS EXTENDING ITS HIGHLY SUCCESSFUL UP & RUNNING GRANTS PROGRAM INTO ANOTHER YEAR, TO HELP SMALL BUSINESSES THRIVE ONLINE AND SECURE THEIR FUTURE.
