EBAY INC. REPORTS KOREA BUSINESSES AS DISCONTINUED OPERATIONS AND RELEASES UPDATED HISTORICAL FINANCIALS. SAN JOSE, CALIF., JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DURING THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021, EBAY INC. (NASDAQ: EBAY) AND EMART ANNOUNCED THEY REACHED AN AGREEMENT FOR THE PURCHASE BY EMART OF EBAY'S BUSINESSES IN KOREA AND DETERMINED THAT IT MET THE CRITERIA FOR THE CLASSIFICATION OF HELD-FOR-SALE ACCOUNTING AND DISCONTINUED OPERATIONS.
