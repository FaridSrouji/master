SIDELINESWAP SECURES INVESTMENT FROM EBAY VENTURES, STRENGTHENING ITS POSITION AS THE LEADING MARKETPLACE FOR SPORTING GOODS. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #CIRCULARECONOMY--SIDELINESWAP, THE PREMIER ONLINE MARKETPLACE FOR NEW AND USED SPORTING GOODS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC INVESTMENT FROM EBAY VENTURES, THE CORPORATE VENTURE ARM OF GLOBAL COMMERCE LEADER EBAY INC. (NASDAQ: EBAY). SIDELINESWAP, WHICH LAUNCHED IN 2015, ENABLES RESALE WHEREVER SPORTS ENTHUSIASTS SHOP OR PLAY, BOTH THROUGH ITS PEER-TO-PEER ONLINE MARKETPLACE AND THROUGH ITS TRADE-IN PARTNERSHIPS WITH RETAILERS AND BRANDS. THE EBAY VENTURES INVESTMENT FOLLOWS ANOTHE.
