COOPERCOMPANIES APPOINTS CYNTHIA LUCCHESE TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. SAN RAMON, CALIF., OCT. 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COOPERCOMPANIES (NYSE: COO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED CYNTHIA LUCCHESE AS AN INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR, EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 1, 2022. LUCCHESE HAS ALSO BEEN APPOINTED TO SERVE ON THE AUDIT COMMITTEE AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE AND NOMINATING COMMITTEE WHEN SHE JOINS THE BOARD.
