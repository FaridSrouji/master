COOPERCOMPANIES ACQUIRES SAFE OBSTETRIC SYSTEMS. SAN RAMON, CALIF., MARCH 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COOPERCOMPANIES (NYSE: COO), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT COOPERSURGICAL HAS ACQUIRED SAFE OBSTETRIC SYSTEMS, A PRIVATELY HELD MANUFACTURER OF THE MEDICAL DEVICE, FETAL PILLOW®. THIS FDA APPROVED PRODUCT IS USED TO ELEVATE THE FETAL HEAD DURING A FULLY DILATED CESAREAN SECTION MAKING THE DELIVERY EASIER AND LESS TRAUMATIC FOR THE MOTHER AND BABY.
