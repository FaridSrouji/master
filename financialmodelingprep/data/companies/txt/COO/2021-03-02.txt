COOPERCOMPANIES TO PARTICIPATE IN THE KEYBANC LIFE SCIENCES MEDTECH INVESTOR FORUM. SAN RAMON, CALIF., MARCH 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COOPERCOMPANIES (NYSE: COO), WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE KEYBANC LIFE SCIENCES MEDTECH INVESTOR FORUM ON WEDNESDAY, MARCH 24, 2021. BRIAN ANDREWS, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER & TREASURER, WILL REPRESENT THE COMPANY IN A VIRTUAL SESSION SCHEDULED TO BEGIN AT 1:15PM ET.
