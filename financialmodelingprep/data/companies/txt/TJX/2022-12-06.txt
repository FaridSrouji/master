THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. FRAMINGHAM, MASS.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TJX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE DECLARATION OF A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $.295 PER SHARE PAYABLE MARCH 2, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 9, 2023.
