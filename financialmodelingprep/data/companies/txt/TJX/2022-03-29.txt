THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. ANNOUNCES 13% INCREASE IN COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND. FRAMINGHAM, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TJX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS RAISED THE AMOUNT OF ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 13% FROM THE LAST DIVIDEND PAID. THE BOARD DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IN THE AMOUNT OF $.295 PER SHARE, PAYABLE JUNE 2, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON MAY 12, 2022.
