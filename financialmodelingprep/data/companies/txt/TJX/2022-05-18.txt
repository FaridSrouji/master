THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. REPORTS Q1 FY23 RESULTS; MARMAXX COMP STORE SALES INCREASED 3%. FRAMINGHAM, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. NET SALES FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF FISCAL 2023 WERE $11.4 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF 13% VERSUS THE FIRST QUARTER OF FISCAL 2022.
