FDA APPROVES EXPANDED BOTOX® (ONABOTULINUMTOXINA) LABEL TO INCLUDE EIGHT NEW MUSCLES TO TREAT ADULTS WITH UPPER LIMB SPASTICITY. NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALLERGAN, AN ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED A LABEL EXPANSION OF BOTOX® TO INCLUDE EIGHT NEW MUSCLES FOR THE TREATMENT OF UPPER LIMB SPASTICITY IN ADULTS.
