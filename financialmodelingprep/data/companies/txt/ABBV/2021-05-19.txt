ABBVIE TO SHOWCASE BREADTH OF ONCOLOGY PORTFOLIO AND PIPELINE AT THE 2021 ASCO AND EHA ANNUAL CONGRESSES. NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) WILL PRESENT RESULTS FROM 43 ABSTRACTS ACROSS 12 TYPES OF CANCER DURING THE UPCOMING VIRTUAL AMERICAN SOCIETY OF CLINICAL ONCOLOGY (ASCO) ANNUAL MEETING (JUNE 4-8) AND THE VIRTUAL EUROPEAN HEMATOLOGY ASSOCIATION (EHA) CONGRESS (JUNE 9-17).
