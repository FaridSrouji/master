U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION APPROVES VUITY™ (PILOCARPINE HCI OPHTHALMIC SOLUTION) 1.25%, THE FIRST AND ONLY EYE DROP TO TREAT PRESBYOPIA (AGE-RELATED BLURRY NEAR VISION). NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., OCT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALLERGAN, AN ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) APPROVAL OF VUITY™ (PILOCARPINE HCL OPHTHALMIC SOLUTION) 1.25% FOR THE TREATMENT OF PRESBYOPIA, COMMONLY KNOWN AS AGE-RELATED BLURRY NEAR VISION, IN ADULTS.
