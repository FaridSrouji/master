NEW ENGLAND JOURNAL OF MEDICINE PUBLISHES 12-WEEK RESULTS FROM STUDY EVALUATING ATOGEPANT FOR THE PREVENTIVE TREATMENT OF MIGRAINE. NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., AUG. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE NEW ENGLAND JOURNAL OF MEDICINE (NEJM) HAS PUBLISHED 12-WEEK RESULTS FROM THE PHASE 3 ADVANCE TRIAL EVALUATING ATOGEPANT FOR THE PREVENTIVE TREATMENT OF MIGRAINE IN ADULTS WHO MEET CRITERIA FOR EPISODIC MIGRAINE.
