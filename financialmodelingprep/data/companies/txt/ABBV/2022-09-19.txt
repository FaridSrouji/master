ABBVIE SECURES POSITIVE CHMP OPINION FOR RISANKIZUMAB (SKYRIZI®) FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE CROHN'S DISEASE. -  CHMP POSITIVE OPINION IS BASED ON RESULTS FROM THREE PHASE 3 STUDIES: TWO FOR INDUCTION AND ONE FOR MAINTENANCE1,2 -  CROHN'S DISEASE IS A CHRONIC, SYSTEMIC DISEASE THAT MANIFESTS AS INFLAMMATION WITHIN THE GASTROINTESTINAL TRACT, CAUSING PERSISTENT DIARRHEA, ABDOMINAL PAIN AND CAN REQUIRE URGENT MEDICAL CARE3,4 -  IF APPROVED, RISANKIZUMAB WILL BE THE FIRST SPECIFIC IL-23 INHIBITOR FOR THE TREATMENT OF CROHN'S DISEASE IN THE EUROPEAN UNION (EU) -  THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION DECISION IS EXPECTED IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022. THIS ANTICIPATED APPROVAL WOULD MARK THE THIRD INDICATION FOR RISANKIZUMAB IN THE EU NORTH CHICAGO, ILL.
