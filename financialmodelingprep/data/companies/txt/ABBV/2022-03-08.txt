ALVOTECH RESOLVES U.S. PATENT AND TRADE SECRET DISPUTES WITH ABBVIE, SECURING U.S. RIGHTS FOR ALVOTECH'S PROPOSED HIGH-CONCENTRATION BIOSIMILAR (AVT02) FOR HUMIRA®. REYKJAVIK, ICELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALVOTECH HOLDINGS S.A. (“ALVOTECH”), A GLOBAL BIOPHARMACEUTICAL COMPANY FOCUSED SOLELY ON THE DEVELOPMENT AND MANUFACTURE OF BIOSIMILAR MEDICINES FOR PATIENTS WORLDWIDE, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IS HAS EXECUTED A U.S. SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT WITH ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) THAT GRANTS ALVOTECH NON-EXCLUSIVE RIGHTS TO MARKET AVT02 (100MG/ML), ITS HIGH-CONCENTRATION, CITRATE-FREE BIOSIMILAR CANDIDATE FOR HUMIRA® (ADALIMUMAB) IN THE UNITED STATES.
