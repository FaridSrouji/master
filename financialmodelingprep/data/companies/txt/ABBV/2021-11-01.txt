ABBVIE HIGHLIGHTS INNOVATIVE RESEARCH FROM ITS RHEUMATOLOGY PORTFOLIO AT ACR CONVERGENCE 2021. NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., NOV. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRESENTATION OF NEW DATA ON RINVOQ® (UPADACITINIB), SKYRIZI® (RISANKIZUMAB) AND HUMIRA® (ADALIMUMAB) ACROSS MULTIPLE RHEUMATIC DISEASES AT THE AMERICAN COLLEGE OF RHEUMATOLOGY'S ANNUAL MEETING (ACR CONVERGENCE 2021), TO BE HELD VIRTUALLY NOV. 3-9.
