DALVANCE® (DALBAVANCIN) RECEIVES FDA APPROVAL TO TREAT ACUTE BACTERIAL SKIN AND SKIN STRUCTURE INFECTIONS IN PEDIATRIC PATIENTS. NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., JULY 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) APPROVED DALVANCE® (DALBAVANCIN) FOR THE TREATMENT OF ACUTE BACTERIAL SKIN AND SKIN STRUCTURE INFECTIONS (ABSSSI) IN PEDIATRIC PATIENTS FROM BIRTH.
