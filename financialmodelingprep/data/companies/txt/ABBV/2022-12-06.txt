ABBVIE TO SHOWCASE MIGRAINE PORTFOLIO AND PIPELINE DURING THE 16TH EUROPEAN HEADACHE FEDERATION CONGRESS. -- FIFTEEN ABSTRACTS UNDERSCORE ABBVIE'S COMMITMENT TO PEOPLE LIVING WITH MIGRAINE -- ABBVIE TO PRESENT LATE-BREAKING DATA FROM THE CHRONIC MIGRAINE EPIDEMIOLOGY AND OUTCOMES – INTERNATIONAL (CAMEO-I) STUDY ON NECK PAIN WITH HEADACHE IN PEOPLE WITH AND WITHOUT MIGRAINE -- RESULTS FROM THE PROGRESS PHASE 3 TRIAL ON ATOGEPANT FOR THE PREVENTIVE TREATMENT OF CHRONIC MIGRAINE IN EUROPE WILL ALSO BE PRESENTED NORTH CHICAGO, ILL. , DEC. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DATA FROM ITS ROBUST MIGRAINE PORTFOLIO WILL BE PRESENTED AT THE 16TH EUROPEAN HEADACHE FEDERATION CONGRESS 2022 (EHC 2022) TAKING PLACE IN VIENNA, AUSTRIA, DECEMBER 7-10.
