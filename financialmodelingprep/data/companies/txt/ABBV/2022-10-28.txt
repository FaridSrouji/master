GENMAB ANNOUNCES SUBMISSIONS OF REGULATORY APPLICATIONS FOR EPCORITAMAB (DUOBODY®-CD3XCD20) FOR THE TREATMENT OF RELAPSED/REFRACTORY LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA (LBCL) AND DIFFUSE LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA (DLBCL). COPENHAGEN, DENMARK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GENMAB A/S (NASDAQ: GMAB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS SUBMITTED A BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) TO THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR SUBCUTANEOUS EPCORITAMAB (DUOBODY®-CD3XCD20), AN INVESTIGATIONAL BISPECIFIC ANTIBODY, FOR THE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH RELAPSED/REFRACTORY LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA (LBCL) AFTER TWO OR MORE LINES OF SYSTEMIC THERAPY.
