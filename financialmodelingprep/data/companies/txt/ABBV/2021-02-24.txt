HUMIRA® (ADALIMUMAB) RECEIVES FDA APPROVAL TO TREAT PEDIATRIC PATIENTS LIVING WITH MODERATELY TO SEVERELY ACTIVE ULCERATIVE COLITIS. NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., FEB. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) APPROVED HUMIRA® (ADALIMUMAB) FOR THE TREATMENT OF MODERATELY TO SEVERELY ACTIVE ULCERATIVE COLITIS IN PEDIATRIC PATIENTS 5 YEARS OF AGE AND OLDER.
