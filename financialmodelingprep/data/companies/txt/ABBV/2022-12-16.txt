U.S. FDA APPROVES VRAYLAR® (CARIPRAZINE) AS AN ADJUNCTIVE TREATMENT FOR MAJOR DEPRESSIVE DISORDER. -  APPROVAL MARKS FOURTH INDICATION FOR VRAYLAR, BACKED BY PROVEN EFFICACY AND WELL-ESTABLISHED TOLERABILITY AS AN ADJUNCTIVE TREATMENT FOR MAJOR DEPRESSIVE DISORDER (MDD) WITH AN ANTIDEPRESSANT THERAPY (ADT), SHOWING IMPROVEMENT IN SYMPTOMS WHEN COMPARED TO PLACEBO + ADT -  DESIGNED FOR SPECIFIC MOOD DISORDERS, VRAYLAR IS NOW THE FIRST AND ONLY DOPAMINE AND SEROTONIN PARTIAL AGONIST FDA-APPROVED FOR THE MOST COMMON FORMS OF DEPRESSION – AS AN ADJUNCTIVE TREATMENT FOR MDD AND THE TREATMENT OF DEPRESSIVE EPISODES ASSOCIATED WITH BIPOLAR I DISORDER -  ABOUT ONE IN FIVE U.S. ADULTS WILL EXPERIENCE MDD DURING THEIR LIFETIME, AND MANY OF THEM MAY HAVE PARTIAL RESPONSE TO THE TREATMENT WITH AN ADT NORTH CHICAGO, ILL. , DEC. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED VRAYLAR® (CARIPRAZINE) AS AN ADJUNCTIVE THERAPY TO ANTIDEPRESSANTS FOR THE TREATMENT OF MAJOR DEPRESSIVE DISORDER (MDD) IN ADULTS.
