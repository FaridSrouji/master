ABBVIE CONFIRMS GUIDANCE OF GREATER THAN $15 BILLION IN COMBINED RISK-ADJUSTED SALES FOR RINVOQ AND SKYRIZI IN 2025. NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., JAN. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) IS CONFIRMING PRIOR REVENUE GUIDANCE OF GREATER THAN $15 BILLION IN COMBINED RINVOQ (UPADACITINIB) AND SKYRIZI (RISANKIZUMAB) RISK-ADJUSTED SALES IN 2025.
