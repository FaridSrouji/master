EUROPEAN COMMISSION APPROVES ABBVIE'S RINVOQ™ (UPADACITINIB) FOR THE TREATMENT OF PSORIATIC ARTHRITIS AND ANKYLOSING SPONDYLITIS. NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., JAN. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION (EC) HAS APPROVED RINVOQTM (UPADACITINIB, 15 MG), AN ORAL, ONCE DAILY SELECTIVE AND REVERSIBLE JAK INHIBITOR FOR THE TREATMENT OF ACTIVE PSORIATIC ARTHRITIS (PSA) IN ADULT...
