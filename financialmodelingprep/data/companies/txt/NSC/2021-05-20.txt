NORFOLK SOUTHERN ANNOUNCES $5 MILLION CONTRIBUTION TO THE HAMPTON ROADS COMMUNITY. NORFOLK, VA., MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN (NYSE:NSC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY IS CONTRIBUTING $5 MILLION TO THE HAMPTON ROADS COMMUNITY ON BEHALF OF THE MANY NS EMPLOYEES AND RETIREES WHO CALL HAMPTON ROADS HOME, AND THE MANY MORE WHO HAVE LIVED AND RAISED THEIR FAMILIES IN THE AREA OVER THE YEARS.
