NORFOLK SOUTHERN TO ESTABLISH REGIONAL SAFETY TRAINING CENTER IN OHIO. WILL EXPAND SAFETY PROGRAM THAT TRAINS 5,000 FIRST RESPONDERS ANNUALLY ATLANTA , MARCH 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) ANNOUNCED WEDNESDAY THAT IT WILL CREATE A NEW REGIONAL TRAINING CENTER IN OHIO FOR FIRST RESPONDERS. ADDITIONALLY, NORFOLK SOUTHERN WILL EXPAND ITS OPERATION AWARENESS & RESPONSE (OAR) PROGRAM, WHICH TRAVELS THE COMPANY'S 22-STATE NETWORK AND TRAINS FIRST RESPONDERS ON HOW TO RESPOND SAFELY TO RAIL INCIDENTS.
