NORFOLK SOUTHERN ANNOUNCES INAUGURAL THOROUGHBRED SUSTAINABILITY PARTNER AWARDS WINNERS. FIFTEEN CUSTOMERS AND SUPPLIERS HONORED FOR THEIR EFFORTS TO FURTHER  ENERGY EFFICIENCY, INNOVATION, AND ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP  ATLANTA , DEC. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RECIPIENTS OF ITS INAUGURAL THOROUGHBRED SUSTAINABILITY PARTNER AWARDS. THE AWARDS RECOGNIZE NORFOLK SOUTHERN PARTNER COMPANIES WHO ARE LEADERS IN THE CATEGORIES OF ENERGY EFFICIENCY, INNOVATION, AND ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP.
