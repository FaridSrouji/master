NORFOLK SOUTHERN AIMS TO REDUCE GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS INTENSITY 42% BY 2034. NORFOLK, VA., JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  NORFOLK  SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE:NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS SCIENCE-BASED TARGET TO ACHIEVE A 42% REDUCTION IN SCOPE 1 AND 2 GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS INTENSITY BY 2034 FROM A 2019 BASE YEAR.
