NORFOLK SOUTHERN APPLAUDS 43 CHEMICAL CUSTOMERS FOR SHIPPING SAFELY BY RAIL. NORFOLK, VA., JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN (NYSE: NSC) APPLAUDS 43 CHEMICAL CUSTOMERS WHO EARNED ITS 2020 THOROUGHBRED CHEMICAL SAFETY AWARD FOR SAFELY HANDLING PRODUCTS REGULATED AS HAZARDOUS MATERIALS.
