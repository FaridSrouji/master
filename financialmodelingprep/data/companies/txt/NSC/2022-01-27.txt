NORFOLK SOUTHERN INSTALLS EFFICIENT, HYBRID-POWERED CRANES AT CHICAGO AND ATLANTA INTERMODAL TERMINALS. ATLANTA, JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) HAS COMPLETED THE INSTALLATION OF FIVE ADDITIONAL NEW DIESEL-ELECTRIC HYBRID OVERHEAD GANTRY CRANES AT CHICAGO'S LANDER'S AND ATLANTA'S INMAN INTERMODAL TERMINALS. THE FUEL-EFFICIENT CRANES FURTHER THE COMPANY'S SUSTAINABILITY EFFORTS BY INCREASING FUEL EFFICIENCY IN TERMINAL OPERATIONS AND REDUCING AIR EMISSIONS.
