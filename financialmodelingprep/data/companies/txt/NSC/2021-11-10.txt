NORFOLK SOUTHERN OPENS NEW HEADQUARTERS BUILDING IN ATLANTA. ATLANTA, NOV. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY FORMALLY OPENED ITS STATE-OF-THE-ART HEADQUARTERS IN MIDTOWN ATLANTA FOLLOWING A RIBBON-CUTTING CEREMONY THAT FEATURED GOVERNOR BRIAN P. KEMP, SENATOR RAPHAEL WARNOCK, MAYOR KEISHA LANCE BOTTOMS, NORFOLK SOUTHERN EMPLOYEES, AND CIVIC AND BUSINESS LEADERS FROM ACROSS ATLANTA.
