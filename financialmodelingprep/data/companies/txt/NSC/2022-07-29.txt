NORFOLK SOUTHERN DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA , JULY 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.24 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK. THE COMPANY HAS PAID A DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK FOR 160 CONSECUTIVE QUARTERS SINCE ITS FORMATION IN 1982.
