NORFOLK SOUTHERN TO REOPEN PENNSYLVANIA INTERMODAL FACILITY. NORFOLK, VA., AUG. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO REOPEN ITS INTERMODAL FACILITY IN GREENCASTLE, PENNSYLVANIA, CREATING CAPACITY TO SUPPORT ECONOMIC GROWTH AND HELP EASE SUPPLY-CHAIN CONGESTION THAT HAS SLOWED THE FLOW OF COMMERCE ACROSS ALL MODES OF THE U.S. TRANSPORTATION SECTOR.
