NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION REPORTS EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION ANNOUNCED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 2,841 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,947 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 996 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,020 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 657 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 702 MILLION A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 2.49 COMPARED TO USD 2.52 A YEAR AGO. FOR THE NINE MONTHS, TOTAL REVENUE WAS USD 8,606 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 8,562 MILLION A YEAR AGO. OPERATING INCOME WAS USD 3,027 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 2,881 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS USD 2,056 MILLION COMPARED TO USD 1,964 MILLION A YEAR AGO. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS USD 7.7 COMPARED TO USD 6.95 A YEAR AGO.
NORFOLK SOUTHERN SETS REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.94 PER SHARE. SETS REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.94 PER SHARE.
