NORFOLK SOUTHERN ESTABLISHES $1 MILLION FUND TO SUPPORT EAST PALESTINE COMMUNITY. ATLANTA , FEB. 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS CREATING A $1 MILLION CHARITABLE FUND TO BE AVAILABLE IMMEDIATELY AS ONE COMPONENT OF ITS PLANNED SUPPORT FOR THE COMMUNITY OF EAST PALESTINE, OHIO. THE COMPANY WILL WORK WITH STATE AND LOCAL LEADERS TO IDENTIFY WHERE THE DONATIONS CAN DO THE MOST GOOD.
