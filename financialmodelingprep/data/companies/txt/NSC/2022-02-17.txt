NORFOLK SOUTHERN OFFERING STARTING BONUSES OF UP TO $5,000 FOR CONDUCTOR TRAINEES. ATLANTA, FEB. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS OFFERING STARTING BONUSES OF UP TO $5,000 FOR CONDUCTOR TRAINEES IN PRIORITY LOCATIONS, AND $2,500 FOR OTHER LOCATIONS. PRIORITY LOCATIONS INCLUDE: BINGHAMTON, NY; BUFFALO, NY; CINCINNATI, OH; LOUISVILLE, KY; MANASSAS, VA; HARRISBURG, PA; CONWAY, PA; BIRMINGHAM, AL; SHEFFIELD, AL; CHATTANOOGA, TN; ROANOKE, VA; AND LINWOOD, NC.
NORFOLK SOUTHERN APPOINTS PAUL B. DUNCAN VICE PRESIDENT OF NETWORK PLANNING AND OPERATIONS, JOHN FRIEDMANN TO RETIRE. ATLANTA, FEB. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PAUL B. DUNCAN HAS BEEN APPOINTED VICE PRESIDENT NETWORK PLANNING AND OPERATIONS.
