NORFOLK SOUTHERN DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AND ACCELERATES PAYMENT DATE. NORFOLK, VA., APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 99 CENTS PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK.
