NORFOLK SOUTHERN INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 10 CENTS. NORFOLK, VA., JULY 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A 10% INCREASE IN ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, FROM $0.99 TO $1.09 PER SHARE.
