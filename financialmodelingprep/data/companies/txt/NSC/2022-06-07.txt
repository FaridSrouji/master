NORFOLK SOUTHERN CELEBRATES 40TH ANNIVERSARY BY RINGING THE OPENING BELL® AT THE NYSE. NEW YORK , JUNE 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY RANG THE OPENING BELL® AT THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE (NYSE), CELEBRATING THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE HISTORIC MERGER OF NORFOLK AND WESTERN RAILWAY AND SOUTHERN RAILWAY THAT CREATED NORFOLK SOUTHERN ON JUNE 1, 1982. JOINING PRESIDENT AND CEO ALAN H.
