NORFOLK SOUTHERN COMMITS $400,000 TO COMBAT FOOD INSECURITY. GRANTS WILL SUPPORT 31 FOOD BANKS ACROSS THE COMPANY'S 22-STATE NETWORK ATLANTA , SEPT. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED A COMMITMENT OF $400,000 IN GRANTS TO 31 FOOD BANKS LOCATED ACROSS THE STATES SERVED BY THE COMPANY.
