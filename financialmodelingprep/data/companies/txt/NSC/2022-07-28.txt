CHILDREN OF RAILROADERS BENEFIT FROM NEW $1.2 MILLION SCHOLARSHIP FUND. NORFOLK SOUTHERN LAUNCHES ANNUAL PROGRAM SUPPORTING COLLEGE STUDENTS ATLANTA , JULY 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ONE HUNDRED CHILDREN OF NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) EMPLOYEES WILL RECEIVE COLLEGE SCHOLARSHIPS OF UP TO $10,000 OVER FOUR YEARS THROUGH THE COMPANY'S NEW THOROUGHBRED SCHOLARS PROGRAM. IN ADDITION, THREE STUDENTS WILL RECEIVE SPECIAL AWARDS OF UP TO $40,000 OVER FOUR YEARS.
