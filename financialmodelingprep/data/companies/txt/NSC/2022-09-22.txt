NORFOLK SOUTHERN HONORS 48 CUSTOMERS WITH THOROUGHBRED CHEMICAL SAFETY AWARD. AWARD RECOGNIZES THE SAFE HANDLING OF HAZARDOUS MATERIALS ATLANTA , SEPT. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT 48 OF ITS CUSTOMERS HAVE EARNED THE 2021 THOROUGHBRED CHEMICAL SAFETY AWARD FOR SAFELY HANDLING PRODUCTS REGULATED AS HAZARDOUS MATERIALS.
