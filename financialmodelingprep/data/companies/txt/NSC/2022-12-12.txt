NORFOLK SOUTHERN AWARDS $1 MILLION TO 57 HAMPTON ROADS NONPROFITS. ORGANIZATIONS SUPPORT EDUCATIONAL, ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL EQUITY, AND COMMUNITY ADVANCEMENT INITIATIVES ATLANTA , DEC. 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) WILL AWARD $1 MILLION IN GRANTS TO A DIVERSE GROUP OF FIFTY-SEVEN HAMPTON ROADS NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS. THE GRANTS FURTHER NORFOLK SOUTHERN'S LEGACY OF SUPPORTING PEOPLE AND FAMILIES IN THE COMMUNITIES IT SERVES.
