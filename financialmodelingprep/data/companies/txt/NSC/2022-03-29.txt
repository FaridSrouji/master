NORFOLK SOUTHERN ANNOUNCES NEW $10 BILLION STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM. ATLANTA , MARCH 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED A NEW PROGRAM FOR THE REPURCHASE OF UP TO $10 BILLION OF ITS COMMON STOCK BEGINNING APRIL 1, 2022. THE COMPANY'S CURRENT PROGRAM WILL BE TERMINATED ON MARCH 31, 2022.
