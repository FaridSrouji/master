UNDER ARMOUR SAYS BOARD APPROVED $75 MLN INCREASE TO 2020 RESTRUCTURING PLAN. UNDER ARMOUR - ON SEPT 2, BOARD APPROVED A $75 MILLION INCREASE TO 2020 RESTRUCTURING PLAN.UNDER ARMOUR INC - CURRENTLY ANTICIPATES THAT MAJORITY OF REMAINING RESTRUCTURING AND RELATED CHARGES WILL OCCUR BY END OF 2020.UNDER ARMOUR INC - CONTINUES TO EXPECT APPROXIMATELY $40 MILLION TO $60 MILLION IN PRE-TAX BENEFITS DURING 2020.UNDER ARMOUR INC - REDUCTION OF APPROXIMATELY 600 EMPLOYEES PRIMARILY IN ITS GLOBAL CORPORATE WORKFORCE.UNDER ARMOUR INC - UPDATED 2020 RESTRUCTURING PLAN OF APPROXIMATELY $550 MILLION TO $600 MILLION OF TOTAL ESTIMATED PRE-TAX RESTRUCTURING AND RELATED CHARGES.
