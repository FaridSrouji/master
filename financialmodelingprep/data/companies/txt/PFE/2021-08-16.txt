PFIZER PRICES $1.0 BILLION SUSTAINABILITY BOND. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF A SUSTAINABILITY BOND OF $1,000,000,000 IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 1.750% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031. THE CLOSING OF THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO OCCUR ON AUGUST 18, 2021, SUBJECT TO SATISFACTION OF CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
