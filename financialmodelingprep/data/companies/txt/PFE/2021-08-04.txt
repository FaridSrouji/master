PFIZER ANNOUNCES POSITIVE TOP-LINE RESULTS FROM PHASE 2B/3 TRIAL OF RITLECITINIB IN ALOPECIA AREATA. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED POSITIVE TOP-LINE RESULTS FROM THE PHASE 2B/3 ALLEGRO TRIAL EVALUATING ORAL ONCE-DAILY RITLECITINIB IN PATIENTS WITH ALOPECIA AREATA, AN AUTOIMMUNE DISEASE DRIVEN BY AN IMMUNE ATTACK ON THE HAIR FOLLICLES THAT CAUSES HAIR LOSS ON THE SCALP AND CAN ALSO AFFECT THE FACE AND BODY.1,2 RITLECITINIB 50 MG AND 30 MG ACHIEVED THE PRIMARY EFFICACY ENDPOINT OF THE STUDY, NAMELY THE PROPORTION OF PATIENTS WITH LESS THAN OR EQUAL TO 20 PERC
