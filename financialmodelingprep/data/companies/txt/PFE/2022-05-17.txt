PFIZER AND BIONTECH GRANTED U.S. EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR BOOSTER DOSE OF THEIR COVID-19 VACCINE IN CHILDREN 5 THROUGH 11 YEARS OF AGE. NEW YORK & MAINZ, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) AND BIONTECH SE (NASDAQ: BNTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) EXPANDED EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) TO INCLUDE A BOOSTER DOSE AFTER COMPLETION OF THE PRIMARY SERIES OF THE PFIZER-BIONTECH COVID-19 VACCINE IN CHILDREN 5 THROUGH 11 YEARS OF AGE.
