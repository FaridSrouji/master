PFIZER AND IONIS ANNOUNCE DISCONTINUATION OF VUPANORSEN CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM. NEW YORK AND CARLSBAD, CALIF., JAN. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) AND IONIS PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: IONS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE DISCONTINUATION OF THE PFIZER-LED CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM FOR VUPANORSEN (PF-07285557), AN INVESTIGATIONAL ANTISENSE THERAPY THAT WAS BEING EVALUATED FOR POTENTIAL INDICATIONS IN CARDIOVASCULAR (CV) RISK REDUCTION AND SEVERE HYPERTRIGLYCERIDEMIA (SHTG).
