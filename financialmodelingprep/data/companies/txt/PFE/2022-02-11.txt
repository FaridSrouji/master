PFIZER AND BIONTECH PROVIDE UPDATE ON ROLLING SUBMISSION FOR EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION OF THEIR COVID-19 VACCINE IN CHILDREN 6 MONTHS THROUGH 4 YEARS OF AGE. NEW YORK & MAINZ, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) AND BIONTECH SE (NASDAQ: BNTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO EXTEND THEIR ROLLING SUBMISSION TO THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) SEEKING TO AMEND THE EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION OF THE PFIZER-BIONTECH COVID-19 VACCINE TO INCLUDE CHILDREN 6 MONTHS THROUGH 4 YEARS OF AGE, WHICH HAD BEEN REQUESTED BY FDA.
