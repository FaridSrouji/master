VIIV HEALTHCARE SUBMITS SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION TO US FDA FOR EXPANDED USE OF CABENUVA (CABOTEGRAVIR, RILPIVIRINE) AS AN HIV TREATMENT FOR USE EVERY 2-MONTHS. RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VIIV HEALTHCARE, THE GLOBAL SPECIALIST HIV COMPANY MAJORITY OWNED BY GLAXOSMITHKLINE PLC (“GSK”), WITH PFIZER INC. AND SHIONOGI LIMITED AS SHAREHOLDERS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SUBMISSION OF A SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION (SNDA) TO THE US FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR THE EXPANDED USE OF CABENUVA (CABOTEGRAVIR, RILPIVIRINE).
