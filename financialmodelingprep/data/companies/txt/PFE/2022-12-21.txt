EVEREST MEDICINES' LICENSING PARTNER PFIZER ANNOUNCES FDA AND EMA ACCEPTANCE OF ETRASIMOD REGULATORY SUBMISSIONS FOR ULCERATIVE COLITIS. SHANGHAI , DEC. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EVEREST MEDICINES (HKEX 1952.HK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS LICENSING PARTNER, PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) HAS RECEIVED ACCEPTANCE FROM THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR REVIEW A NEW DRUG APPLICATION (NDA) FOR ETRASIMOD FOR INDIVIDUALS LIVING WITH MODERATELY-TO-SEVERELY ACTIVE ULCERATIVE COLITIS (UC). THE FDA'S DECISION IS EXPECTED IN THE SECOND HALF OF 2023.
PFIZER ANNOUNCES FDA AND EMA ACCEPTANCE OF ETRASIMOD REGULATORY SUBMISSIONS FOR ULCERATIVE COLITIS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ACCEPTED FOR REVIEW A NEW DRUG APPLICATION (NDA) FOR ETRASIMOD FOR INDIVIDUALS LIVING WITH MODERATELY-TO-SEVERELY ACTIVE ULCERATIVE COLITIS (UC). THE FDA'S DECISION IS EXPECTED IN THE SECOND HALF OF 2023. THE EUROPEAN MEDICINES AGENCY (EMA) HAS ALSO ACCEPTED THE MARKETING AUTHORIZATION APPLICATION (MAA) FOR ETRASIMOD IN THE SAME PATIENT POPULATION WITH THE DECISION ANTICIPATED IN THE FIRST HALF OF 2024.
