PFIZER INVITES SHAREHOLDERS TO ATTEND VIRTUAL-ONLY 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS ON APRIL 22. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT SHAREHOLDERS AND THE GENERAL PUBLIC ARE INVITED TO ACCESS ITS VIRTUAL-ONLY 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS AT 9:00 A.M. EDT ON THURSDAY, APRIL 22, 2021. AS PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED, THIS YEAR'S MEETING WILL BE HELD VIRTUALLY DUE TO CONTINUED PUBLIC HEALTH CONCERNS RELATED TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC AND TO SUPPORT THE HEALTH AND WELL-BEING OF OUR SHAREHOLDERS AND OTHER MEETING PARTICIPANTS.
