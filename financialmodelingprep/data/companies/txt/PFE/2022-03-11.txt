PFIZER COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF ARENA PHARMACEUTICALS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE COMPLETION OF ITS ACQUISITION OF ARENA PHARMACEUTICALS, A CLINICAL STAGE COMPANY DEVELOPING INNOVATIVE POTENTIAL THERAPIES FOR THE TREATMENT OF SEVERAL IMMUNO-INFLAMMATORY DISEASES. ARENA PHARMACEUTICALS BRINGS TO PFIZER A PORTFOLIO OF DIVERSE AND PROMISING DEVELOPMENT-STAGE THERAPEUTIC CANDIDATES IN GASTROENTEROLOGY, DERMATOLOGY, AND CARDIOLOGY, INCLUDING ETRASIMOD, AN ORAL, SELECTIVE SPHINGOSINE 1-PHOSPHATE (S1P) RECEPTOR
