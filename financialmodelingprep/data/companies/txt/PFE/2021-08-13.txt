U.S. FDA APPROVES TICOVAC™, PFIZER'S TICK-BORNE ENCEPHALITIS (TBE) VACCINE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE:PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED TICOVAC™ (TICK-BORNE ENCEPHALITIS (TBE) VACCINE) FOR ACTIVE IMMUNIZATION TO PREVENT TBE IN INDIVIDUALS 1 YEAR OF AGE AND OLDER.1 TICOVAC™ IS THE ONLY FDA-APPROVED VACCINE TO HELP PROTECT U.S. ADULTS AND CHILDREN AGAINST THE TBE VIRUS WHEN VISITING OR LIVING IN TBE ENDEMIC AREAS.
