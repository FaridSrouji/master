U.S. FDA APPROVES PFIZER'S CIBINQO® (ABROCITINIB) FOR ADULTS WITH MODERATE-TO-SEVERE ATOPIC DERMATITIS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE UNITED STATES (U.S.) FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) APPROVED CIBINQO® (ABROCITINIB), AN ORAL, ONCE-DAILY, JANUS KINASE 1 (JAK1) INHIBITOR, FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULTS LIVING WITH REFRACTORY, MODERATE-TO-SEVERE ATOPIC DERMATITIS (AD) WHOSE DISEASE IS NOT ADEQUATELY CONTROLLED WITH OTHER SYSTEMIC DRUG PRODUCTS, INCLUDING BIOLOGICS, OR WHEN USE OF THOSE THERAPIES IS INADVISABLE.
