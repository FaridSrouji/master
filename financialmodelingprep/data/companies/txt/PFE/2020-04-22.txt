BIONTECH, PFIZER ANNOUNCE REGULATORY APPROVAL FROM GERMAN AUTHORITY TO COMMENCE FIRST CLINICAL TRIAL OF COVID-19 VACCINE CANDIDATES. BIONTECH AND PFIZER ANNOUNCE REGULATORY APPROVAL FROM GERMAN AUTHORITY PAUL-EHRLICH-INSTITUT TO COMMENCE FIRST CLINICAL TRIAL OF COVID-19 VACCINE CANDIDATES.PFIZER AND BIONTECH WILL ALSO CONDUCT TRIALS FOR BNT162 IN UNITED STATES UPON REGULATORY APPROVAL, WHICH IS EXPECTED SHORTLY.
