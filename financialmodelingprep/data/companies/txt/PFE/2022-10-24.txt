VIIV HEALTHCARE PRESENTS POSITIVE DATA SHOWING VOCABRIA (CABOTEGRAVIR) AND REKAMBYS (RILPIVIRINE) CAN BE SUCCESSFULLY IMPLEMENTED ACROSS A RANGE OF EUROPEAN HEALTHCARE SETTINGS.
