PFIZER CONFIRMS U.S. PATENT TERM EXTENSION FOR IBRANCE® (PALBOCICLIB) UNTIL MARCH 2027. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. PATENT AND TRADEMARK OFFICE (USPTO) RECENTLY ISSUED A U.S. PATENT TERM EXTENSION (PTE) CERTIFICATE FOR IBRANCE® (PALBOCICLIB). THE CERTIFICATE EXTENDS THE TERM OF U.S. PATENT NO. RE47,739 (‘739) BY MORE THAN FOUR YEARS UNTIL MARCH 5, 2027.
