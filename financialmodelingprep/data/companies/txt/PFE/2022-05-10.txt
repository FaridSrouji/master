SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISS LAW INVESTIGATES BIOHAVEN PHARMACEUTICAL HOLDING COMPANY LTD.. NEW YORK , MAY 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISS LAW IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BIOHAVEN PHARMACEUTICAL HOLDING COMPANY LTD. ("BIOHAVEN" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: BHVN) IN CONNECTION WITH THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF THE COMPANY BY PFIZER INC. ("PFIZER") (NYSE: PFE).
