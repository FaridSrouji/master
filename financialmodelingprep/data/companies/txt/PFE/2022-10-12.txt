PFIZER AND BIONTECH RECEIVE U.S. FDA EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR OMICRON BA.4/BA.5-ADAPTED BIVALENT COVID-19 VACCINE BOOSTER IN CHILDREN 5 THROUGH 11 YEARS OF AGE. NEW YORK & MAINZ, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) AND BIONTECH SE (NASDAQ: BNTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) GRANTED EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) FOR A 10-ΜG BOOSTER DOSE OF THEIR OMICRON BA.4/BA.5-ADAPTED BIVALENT COVID-19 VACCINE IN CHILDREN 5 THROUGH 11 YEARS OF AGE.
