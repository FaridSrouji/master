U.S. FDA ACCEPTS FOR PRIORITY REVIEW THE BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION FOR PFIZER'S RESPIRATORY SYNCYTIAL VIRUS VACCINE CANDIDATE FOR THE PREVENTION OF RSV DISEASE IN OLDER ADULTS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) ACCEPTED FOR PRIORITY REVIEW A BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) FOR ITS RESPIRATORY SYNCYTIAL VIRUS (RSV) VACCINE CANDIDATE, PF-06928316 OR RSVPREF, AS SUBMITTED FOR THE PREVENTION OF LOWER RESPIRATORY TRACT DISEASE CAUSED BY RSV IN INDIVIDUALS 60 YEARS OF AGE AND OLDER.
