GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP, A LEADING SECURITIES FRAUD LAW FIRM, ANNOUNCES INVESTIGATION OF PFIZER INC. (PFE) ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS. LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP (“GPM”), A LEADING NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LAW FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMMENCED AN INVESTIGATION ON BEHALF OF PFIZER INC. (PFE) (“PFIZER” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: PFE) INVESTORS CONCERNING THE COMPANY'S POSSIBLE VIOLATIONS OF THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
INVESTIGATION REMINDER: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES IT IS INVESTIGATING CLAIMS AGAINST PFIZER INC. AND ENCOURAGES INVESTORS WITH LOSSES TO CONTACT THE FIRM.
