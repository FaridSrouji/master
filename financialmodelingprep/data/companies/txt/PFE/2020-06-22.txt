MERCK KGAA: EMA VALIDATES APPLICATION FOR CANCER DRUG BAVENCIO. MERCK KGAA - EUROPEAN MEDICINES AGENCY VALIDATES APPLICATION FOR BAVENCIO® (AVELUMAB) FOR FIRST-LINE MAINTENANCE TREATMENT OF LOCALLY ADVANCED OR METASTATIC UROTHELIAL CARCINOMA.A SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION HAS ALSO BEEN ACCEPTED BY JAPAN'S MINISTRY OF HEALTH, LABOUR AND WELFARE FOR BAVENCIO AS A FIRST-LINE MAINTENANCE THERAPY FOR LOCALLY ADVANCED OR METASTATIC UC.
