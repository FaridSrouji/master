PFIZER DECLARES SECOND-QUARTER 2023 DIVIDEND. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A $0.41 SECOND-QUARTER 2023 DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 9, 2023, TO HOLDERS OF THE COMMON STOCK OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 12, 2023. THE SECOND-QUARTER 2023 CASH DIVIDEND WILL BE THE 338TH CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAID BY PFIZER.
