PFIZER AND BIONTECH ANNOUNCE NEW AGREEMENT WITH U.S. GOVERNMENT TO PROVIDE ADDITIONAL DOSES OF COVID-19 VACCINE. NEW YORK & MAINZ, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) AND BIONTECH SE (NASDAQ: BNTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW VACCINE SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH THE U.S. GOVERNMENT TO SUPPORT THE CONTINUED FIGHT AGAINST COVID-19. UNDER THE AGREEMENT, THE U.S. GOVERNMENT WILL RECEIVE 105 MILLION DOSES (30 ΜG, 10 ΜG AND 3 ΜG).
