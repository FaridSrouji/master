EVEREST MEDICINES ANNOUNCES THAT SPERO THERAPEUTICS ENTERED INTO LICENSING AGREEMENT WITH PFIZER INC. FOR SPR206 IN EX-U.S. AND EX-ASIA TERRITORIES. SHANGHAI, JULY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EVEREST MEDICINES (HKEX 1952.HK), A BIOPHARMACEUTICAL COMPANY FOCUSED ON DEVELOPING AND COMMERCIALIZING TRANSFORMATIVE PHARMACEUTICAL PRODUCTS FOR PATIENTS IN GREATER CHINA AND OTHER PARTS OF ASIA, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SPERO THERAPEUTICS, INC. (NASDAQ: SPRO), A LICENSING PARTNER OF EVEREST MEDICINES, ENTERED INTO A REGIONAL LICENSING AGREEMENT WITH PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) FOR SPR206, SPERO'S INTRAVENOUSLY (IV)-ADMINISTERED NEXT-GENERATION POLYMYXIN PRODUCT CANDIDATE BEING DEVELOPED TO TREAT SERIOUS MULTI-DRUG RESISTANT (MDR) GRAM-NEGATIVE INFECTIONS IN THE HOSPITAL SETTING.  UNDER THE TERMS OF THE LICENSING AGREEMENT, SPERO HAS GRANTED PFIZER THE RIGHTS TO DEVELOP, MANUFACTURE, AND COMMERCIALIZE SPR206 IN EX-U.S. AND EX-ASIA TERRITORIES.
