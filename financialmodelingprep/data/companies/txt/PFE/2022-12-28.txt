U.S. FDA ACCEPTS FOR REVIEW THE BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION FOR PFIZER'S INVESTIGATIONAL PENTAVALENT MENINGOCOCCAL VACCINE CANDIDATE (MENABCWY) IN ADOLESCENTS. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PFIZER INC. (NYSE:PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) ACCEPTED FOR REVIEW A BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) FOR ITS INVESTIGATIONAL PENTAVALENT MENINGOCOCCAL VACCINE CANDIDATE (MENABCWY). PFIZER SUBMITTED MENABCWY FOR THE PREVENTION OF MENINGOCOCCAL DISEASE CAUSED BY THE MOST COMMON SEROGROUPS IN INDIVIDUALS 10 THROUGH 25 YEARS OF AGE.
