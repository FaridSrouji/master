FDA WARNS OF SERIOUS BREATHING PROBLEMS WITH GABAPENTIN & PREGABALIN WHEN USED WITH CNS DEPRESSANTS OR IN PATIENTS WITH LUNG PROBLEMS. FDA - WARNS OF SERIOUS BREATHING PROBLEMS WITH SEIZURE & NERVE PAIN MEDICINES GABAPENTIN (NEURONTIN, GRALISE, HORIZANT) & PREGABALIN (LYRICA, LYRICA CR).FDA - WARNS OF SERIOUS BREATHING PROBLEMS WITH GABAPENTIN & PREGABALIN WHEN USED WITH CNS DEPRESSANTS OR IN PATIENTS WITH LUNG PROBLEMS.FDA - IS REQUIRING NEW WARNINGS ABOUT RISK OF RESPIRATORY DEPRESSION TO BE ADDED TO PRESCRIBING INFORMATION OF GABAPENTINOIDS.FDA- AGENCY REQUIRED DRUG MAKERS TO HOLD CLINICAL TRIALS TO FURTHER EVALUATE GABAPENTINOIDS' ABUSE POTENTIAL, PARTICULARLY IN COMBINATION WITH OPIOIDS.
