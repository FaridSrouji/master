U.S. FDA SAYS APPROVES FIRST GENERICS OF ELIQUIS. U.S. FDA - APPROVES FIRST GENERICS OF ELIQUIS.FDA - APPROVED 2 APPLICATIONS FOR GENERICS OF ELIQUIS TABLETS TO LOWER RISK OF STROKE, SYSTEMIC EMBOLISM IN PATIENTS WITH NONVALVULAR ATRIAL FIBRILLATION.FDA - GRANTED APPROVAL OF GENERIC APIXABAN APPLICATIONS TO MICRO LABS LIMITED & MYLAN PHARMACEUTICALS.
THERAVANCE BIOPHARMA AND PFIZER ENTER GLOBAL LICENSE AGREEMENT FOR SKIN-TARGETED, LOCALLY-ACTING PAN-JANUS KINASE INHIBITOR PROGRAM. THERAVANCE BIOPHARMA AND PFIZER INC. ENTER GLOBAL LICENSE AGREEMENT FOR SKIN-TARGETED, LOCALLY-ACTING PAN-JANUS KINASE (JAK) INHIBITOR PROGRAM.THERAVANCE BIOPHARMA - WILL RECEIVE AN UPFRONT CASH PAYMENT OF $10 MILLION.THERAVANCE BIOPHARMA INC - WILL BE ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE UP TO AN ADDITIONAL $240 MILLION IN DEVELOPMENT AND SALES MILESTONE PAYMENTS FROM PFIZER.THERAVANCE BIOPHARMA - WILL BE ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE ROYALTIES ON WORLDWIDE NET SALES OF ANY POTENTIAL PRODUCTS EMERGING FROM PROGRAM.
