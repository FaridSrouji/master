EMD SERONO AND PFIZER SUBMIT APPLICATION FOR BAVENCIO FOR FIRST-LINE MAINTENANCE TREATMENT OF LOCALLY ADVANCED OR METASTATIC UROTHELIAL CARCINOMA. EMD SERONO AND PFIZER RECEIVE US FDA BREAKTHROUGH THERAPY DESIGNATION AND SUBMIT APPLICATION FOR BAVENCIO® FOR FIRST-LINE MAINTENANCE TREATMENT OF LOCALLY ADVANCED OR METASTATIC UROTHELIAL CARCINOMA.EMD SERONO - APPLICATION IS BASED ON POSITIVE RESULTS FROM AN INTERIM ANALYSIS OF PHASE III JAVELIN BLADDER 100 TRIAL.EMD SERONO - SUPPLEMENTAL BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION IS BEING REVIEWED BY FDA UNDER ITS REAL-TIME ONCOLOGY REVIEW PILOT PROGRAM.
