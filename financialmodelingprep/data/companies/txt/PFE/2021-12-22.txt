PFIZER TO PROVIDE THE UNITED KINGDOM AN ADDITIONAL 2.5 MILLION TREATMENT COURSES OF INVESTIGATIONAL ORAL ANTIVIRAL CANDIDATE TO HELP COMBAT COVID-19. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) ANNOUNCED AN ADDITIONAL AGREEMENT WITH THE UNITED KINGDOM (UK) GOVERNMENT TO SUPPLY AN ADDITIONAL 2.5 MILLION TREATMENT COURSES OF ITS INVESTIGATIONAL CANDIDATE PAXLOVID™ (NIRMATRELVIR [PF-07321332] TABLETS AND RITONAVIR TABLETS), SUBJECT TO LOCAL AUTHORIZATION. THIS IS IN ADDITION TO THE 250,000 TREATMENT COURSES PREVIOUSLY CONTRACTED BY THE UK GOVERNMENT, PENDING AUTHORIZATION AND RECOMMENDATION FOR USE, BRINGING THE FULL AMOUNT OF TREATMENT
