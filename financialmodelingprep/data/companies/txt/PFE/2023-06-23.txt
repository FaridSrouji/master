FDA APPROVES PFIZER'S LITFULO™ (RITLECITINIB) FOR ADULTS AND ADOLESCENTS WITH SEVERE ALOPECIA AREATA. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED LITFULO™ (RITLECITINIB), A ONCE-DAILY ORAL TREATMENT, FOR INDIVIDUALS 12 YEARS OF AGE AND OLDER WITH SEVERE ALOPECIA AREATA. THE APPROVED RECOMMENDED DOSE FOR LITFULO IS 50 MG.
