PFIZER PROVIDES UPDATE ON U.S. FDA REVIEW OF ABROCITINIB AND XELJANZ® FILINGS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS NOTIFIED THE COMPANY THAT IT WILL NOT MEET THE PRESCRIPTION DRUG USER FEE ACT (PDUFA) GOAL DATES FOR THE NEW DRUG APPLICATION FOR ABROCITINIB FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULTS AND ADOLESCENTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE ATOPIC DERMATITIS AND THE SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION FOR XELJANZ/XELJANZ XR (TOFACITINIB) FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULTS WITH ACTIVE ANKYLOSING SPONDYLITIS. PFIZER AND BIONTECH ANNOUNCE COLLABORATION WITH BIOVAC TO MANUFACTURE AND DISTRIBUTE COVID-19 VACCINE DOSES WITHIN AFRICA. NEW YORK & MAINZ, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) AND BIONTECH SE (NASDAQ: BNTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF A LETTER OF INTENT WITH THE BIOVAC INSTITUTE (PTY) LTD, KNOWN AS “BIOVAC,” A CAPE TOWN-BASED, SOUTH AFRICAN BIOPHARMACEUTICAL COMPANY, TO MANUFACTURE THE PFIZER-BIONTECH COVID-19 VACCINE FOR DISTRIBUTION WITHIN THE AFRICAN UNION.
