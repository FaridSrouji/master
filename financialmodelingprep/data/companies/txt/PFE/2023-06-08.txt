CONTINUED STRONG CRESEMBA® (ISAVUCONAZOLE) SALES BY PFIZER TRIGGER USD 26 MILLION SALES MILESTONE PAYMENTS TO BASILEA. AD HOC ANNOUNCEMENT PURSUANT TO ART. 53 LR ALLSCHWIL, SWITZERLAND, JUNE 08, 2023 BASILEA PHARMACEUTICA LTD, ALLSCHWIL (SIX: BSLN), A COMMERCIAL-STAGE BIOPHARMACEUTICAL COMPANY COMMITTED TO MEETING THE NEEDS OF PATIENTS WITH SEVERE BACTERIAL OR FUNGAL INFECTIONS, REPORTED TODAY THAT SALES OF THE ANTIFUNGAL CRESEMBA® (ISAVUCONAZOLE) BY ITS LICENSE PARTNER PFIZER IN EUROPE AND THE ASIA PACIFIC AND CHINA REGIONS, RESPECTIVELY, EXCEEDED THRESHOLDS THAT TRIGGERED TWO SEPARATE MILESTONE PAYMENTS TOTALING USD 26.25 MILLION TO BASILEA.
