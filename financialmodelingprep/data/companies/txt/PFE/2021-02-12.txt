U.S. FDA APPROVES PANZYGA® FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULTS WITH CHRONIC INFLAMMATORY DEMYELINATING POLYNEUROPATHY (CIDP). NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED THE SUPPLEMENTAL BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (SBLA) FOR PANZYGA® (IMMUNE GLOBULIN INTRAVENOUS [HUMAN] – IFAS 10% LIQUID PREPARATION) TO TREAT ADULT PATIENTS WITH A RARE NEUROLOGICAL DISEASE OF THE PERIPHERAL NERVES CALLED CHRONIC INFLAMMATORY DEMYELINATING POLYNEUROPATHY (CIDP). PANZYGA IS THE ONLY INTRAVENOUS IMMUNOGLOBULIN (IVIG) WITH TWO FDA-APPROVED MAINTENANCE
