ROIVANT AND PFIZER UNVEIL PRIOVANT THERAPEUTICS AND ONGOING REGISTRATIONAL STUDIES FOR ORAL BREPOCITINIB IN DERMATOMYOSITIS AND LUPUS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROIVANT SCIENCES AND PFIZER TODAY ANNOUNCED THE UNVEILING OF PRIOVANT THERAPEUTICS, DEDICATED TO DEVELOPING AND COMMERCIALIZING NOVEL THERAPIES FOR AUTOIMMUNE DISEASES WITH THE GREATEST MORBIDITY AND MORTALITY. PRIOVANT WAS ESTABLISHED IN SEPTEMBER 2021 THROUGH A TRANSACTION BETWEEN ROIVANT (NASDAQ: ROIV) AND PFIZER (NYSE: PFE), IN WHICH PFIZER LICENSED ORAL AND TOPICAL BREPOCITINIB'S GLOBAL DEVELOPMENT RIGHTS AND US AND JAPAN COMMERCIAL RIGHTS TO PRIOVANT.
