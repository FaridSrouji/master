EVEREST MEDICINES' LICENSING PARTNER PFIZER PRESENTS ELEVATE PIVOTAL FINDINGS DEMONSTRATING ETRASIMOD'S POTENTIALLY BEST-IN-CLASS PROFILE IN ULCERATIVE COLITIS. SHANGHAI , MAY 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EVEREST MEDICINES (HKEX 1952.HK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS LICENSING PARTNER, PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) PRESENTED AT DIGESTIVE DISEASE WEEK (DDW) 2022 DETAILED RESULTS FROM TWO PIVOTAL STUDIES THAT MAKE UP THE ELEVATE UC PHASE 3 REGISTRATIONAL PROGRAM EVALUATING ETRASIMOD, A ONCE-DAILY, ORAL, SELECTIVE SPHINGOSINE 1-PHOSPHATE (S1P) RECEPTOR MODULATOR FOR THE TREATMENT OF MODERATELY-TO-SEVERELY ACTIVE ULCERATIVE COLITIS (UC). BOTH PHASE 3, MULTI-CENTER, RANDOMIZED, PLACEBO-CONTROLLED TRIALS ACHIEVED ALL PRIMARY AND KEY SECONDARY ENDPOINTS, WITH ETRASIMOD DEMONSTRATING A SAFETY PROFILE CONSISTENT WITH PREVIOUS STUDIES.
