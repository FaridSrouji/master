(NYSE: PFE) AND BIONTECH SE (NASDAQ: BNTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS EXPANDED THE EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) OF A BOOSTER DOSE OF THE PFIZER-BIONTECH COVID-19 VACCINE TO INCLUDE INDIVIDUALS 12 YEARS OF AGE AND OLDER.
