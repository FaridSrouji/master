LONGEVITY BIOTECH GERO ENTERED A RESEARCH COLLABORATION WITH PFIZER TO DISCOVER POTENTIAL TARGETS FOR FIBROTIC DISEASES. SAN FRANCISCO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--GERO TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A RESEARCH COLLABORATION WITH PFIZER TO APPLY GERO'S MACHINE LEARNING TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM TO DISCOVER POTENTIAL THERAPEUTIC TARGETS FOR FIBROTIC DISEASES USING LARGE-SCALE HUMAN-BASED DATA.
