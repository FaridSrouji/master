DERMAPHARM HOLDING SE SIGNS COOPERATION AND DELIVERY AGREEMENT WITH BIONTECH SE.DERMAPHARM HOLDING AND BIONTECH SE, MAINZ, GERMANY, HAVE AGREED THAT THEIR PRODUCTION SUBSIDIARIES WILL COOPERATE ON PRODUCING A COVID-19 VACCINE..PRODUCTION CAPACITIES AT DERMAPHARM WILL BE AVAILABLE TO COOPERATION PARTNERS BIONTECH SE AND PFIZER INC. ALREADY WHEN PRODUCTION OF VACCINE BEGINS.
