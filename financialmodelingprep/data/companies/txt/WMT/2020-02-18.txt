WALMART SEES POTENTIAL IMPACT TO CHINA BUSINESS IN Q1 FROM CORONAVIRUS. WALMART INC- THE CORONAVIRUS TEMPERS OUR EXPECTATIONS FOR FISCAL 2021- INVESTOR DAY.WALMART INC CFO SAYS EXPECTS QRTLY E-COMMERCE SALES GROWTH TO RANGE BETWEEN MID 20% TO MID 30%- INVESTOR DAY.WALMART EXEC - CONTINUE TO MONITOR ONGOING TARIFF DISCUSSION.WALMART EXEC - SEE POTENTIAL IMPACT TO CHINA BUSINESS IN Q1 FROM CORONAVIRUS.WALMART EXEC - ALSO SEE POTENTIAL FINANCIAL IMPACT TO CHINA BUSINESS POTENTIALLY INTO Q2 DUE TO CORONAVIRUS.WALMART EXEC SAYS EXPECT OPERATING MARGINS TO BE FAIRLY FLAT FOR 2021.WALMART EXEC ON CORONAVIRUS SAYS HAVE A MAJORITY OF STORES IN CHINA WITH REDUCED HOURS.WALMART EXEC SAYS WE COULD SEE A COUPLE OF CENTS NEGATIVE IMPACT IN Q1 DUE TO CORONAVIRUS.WALMART- EXPECTS SAME-DAY PICK UP TO EXPAND INTO 500 LOCATIONS THIS YEAR.WALMART- EXPECTS SAME-DAY DELIVERY TO BE IN ABOUT HALF OF THE FLEET BY THE END OF THE YEAR.WALMART SAYS EXPANDING GENERAL MERCHANDISE ASSORTMENT FOR SAME-DAY PICKUP AND DELIVERY.WALMART EXEC SAYS COMBINING WALMART.COM AND THE GROCERY APP INTO ONE, LATER THIS YEAR.WALMART EXEC SAYS FLIPKART IS NOW TRACKING TO OVER 1 BILLION VISITS PER MONTH.WALMART EXPECTS TO SAVE MORE THAN $60 MILLION ANNUALLY BY CHANGING ITS  BUYING PROCESS, BETTER UTILIZING SCALE FOR SHOPPING BAGS.
WALMART REPORTS Q4 ADJUSTED EPS OF $1.38. WALMART Q4 FY20 GAAP EPS OF $1.45.QUARTERLY ADJUSTED EPS OF $1.38.QUARTERLY TOTAL REVENUE WAS $141.7 BILLION, INCREASE OF $2.9 BILLION, OR 2.1%.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.43, REVENUE VIEW $142.49 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QUARTERLY WALMART U.S. COMP SALES INCREASED ON A TWO-YEAR STACKED BASIS BY 6.0%.QUARTERLY NET SALES $140.61 BILLION VERSUS $137.74 BILLION.QUARTERLY WALMART U.S. NET SALES $92.27 BILLION VERSUS $90.52 BILLION.QUARTERLY WALMART INTERNATIONAL NET SALES $33.05 BILLION VERSUS $32.32 BILLION.QUARTERLY SAM'S CLUB NET SALES $15.29 BILLION VERSUS $14.91 BILLION.QUARTERLY SAM'S CLUB COMPARABLE SALES, WITHOUT FUEL, UP 0.8%.QUARTERLY TOTAL U.S. COMPARABLE SALES, WITHOUT FUEL, UP 1.8%.SAYS DISRUPTION IN CHILE AND A LEGAL MATTER LOWERED QUARTERLY GAAP AND ADJUSTED EPS BY ABOUT $0.05.SAYS REDUCED TOBACCO SALES NEGATIVELY AFFECTED SAM'S CLUB QUARTERLY COMP SALES BY ABOUT 300 BASIS POINTS.
