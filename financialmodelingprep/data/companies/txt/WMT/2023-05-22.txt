WALMART OPENS FIRST MARKET FULFILLMENT CENTER IN ARKANSAS AS THE RETAILER DOUBLES DOWN ON STORE FULFILLMENT. BENTONVILLE, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALMART OPENED ITS FIRST HIGH-TECH MARKET FULFILLMENT CENTER IN ARKANSAS AT STORE 100 LOCATED AT 406 S WALTON BLVD IN BENTONVILLE ON MONDAY, UNDERLINING THE RETAILER'S STRATEGIC MOVE TOWARD EXPANDING THE USE OF ITS STORES AS FULFILLMENT CENTERS TO ENHANCE THE SHOPPING EXPERIENCE FOR CUSTOMERS EVERYWHERE. THE MARKET FULFILLMENT CENTER (MFC) IS BUILT WITHIN THE STORE AND IS POWERED BY A PROPRIETARY STORAGE AND RETRIEVAL SYSTEM – NAMED ALPHABOT. WALMART BELIEVES.
