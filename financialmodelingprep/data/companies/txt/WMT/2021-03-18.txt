NEW YORK, MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- POMERANTZ LLP ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED AGAINST WALMART INC. ("WALMART" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: WMT) AND CERTAIN OF ITS OFFICERS.  THE CLASS ACTION, FILED IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF DELAWARE, AND DOCKETED UNDER 21-CV-01811, IS ON BEHALF OF A CLASS CONSISTING OF ALL INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED PUBLICLY TRADED WALMART SECURITIES BETWEEN MARCH 30, 2016 AND DECEMBER 22, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
