BLENDER BITES HITS SHELVES OF WALMART CANADA AFTER SUCCESSFUL COMMERCIALIZATION OF ITS 1-STEP FRAPPÉ. VANCOUVER, BRITISH COLUMBIA, JUNE 01, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLENDER BITES LTD. (THE “COMPANY”, “BLENDER BITES” OR “BLENDER”), (CSE: BITE, FWB: JL4, WKN: A3DMEJ), FURTHER TO ITS PRESS RELEASE DATED MARCH 22, 2023, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS FIRST EVER PRODUCTION RUN OF ITS NEWEST INNOVATION – THE 1-STEP FRAPPÉ – AND HAS SHIPPED ITS ENTIRE PRODUCT LINEUP IN CLOSE TO 300 WALMART CANADA STORES. PRODUCTS HAVE ALREADY HIT SHELVES AT SOME WALMART LOCATIONS IN THE COUNTRY, AND REPLENISHMENT ORDERS HAVE ALREADY COME IN AS THE RETAILER PREPARES FOR A SUCCESSFUL LAUNCH.
