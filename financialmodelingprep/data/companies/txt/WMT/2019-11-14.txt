WALMART INC. APPOINTS KATHRYN MCLAY AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF SAM'S CLUB DIVISION. WALMART INC. ANNOUNCED THAT KATHRYN MCLAY, WAS APPOINTED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF WALMART INC.'S SAM'S CLUB DIVISION, EFFECTIVE AS OF NOVEMBER 15, 2019. IN THIS POSITION, MS. MCLAY WILL REPORT TO THE COMPANY'S PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. SINCE FEBRUARY 2019, MS. MCLAY HAS SERVED AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, WALMART U.S. NEIGHBORHOOD MARKETS. MS. MCLAY ORIGINALLY JOINED THE COMPANY IN APRIL 2015 AS VICE PRESIDENT OF U.S. FINANCE AND STRATEGY. SHE WAS PROMOTED TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, U.S. SUPPLY CHAIN IN DECEMBER 2015, IN WHICH ROLE SHE SERVED UNTIL FEBRUARY 2019. PRIOR TO JOINING THE COMPANY, MS. MCLAY SERVED FOR 14 YEARS IN A VARIETY OF STRATEGIC, FINANCE, AND SUPPLY CHAIN ROLES AT WOOLWORTHS LIMITED.
