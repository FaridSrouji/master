WALMART'S 9TH ANNUAL OPEN CALL APPLICATION PERIOD CLOSING SOON.. BENTONVILLE, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ENTREPRENEURS AND SMALL BUSINESSES LOOKING TO LAND ON WALMART SHELVES, HAVE A FEW MORE DAYS TO APPLY FOR WALMART'S NINTH ANNUAL OPEN CALL FOR PRODUCTS MADE, GROWN, OR ASSEMBLED IN THE UNITED STATES. THE DEADLINE TO APPLY IS FRIDAY, APRIL 8. SPACE IS LIMITED AND PRODUCTS WILL BE CONSIDERED IN THE ORDER THEY ARE RECEIVED UNTIL THE VENUE IS FULL.
