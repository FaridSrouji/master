WALMART - WAIVING RENT FOR ALL WALMART PROPERTY PARTNERS FOR MONTH OF APRIL, AS WELL AS FOR HUNDREDS OF EYE DOCTORS WHO OPERATE AT SAM'S CLUBS.WALMART - CONTINUE TO MONITOR NEED FOR ADDITIONAL SUPPORT PAST APRIL FOR WALMART PROPERTY PARTNERS, EYE DOCTORS WHO OPERATE AT SAM'S CLUBS.WALMART - MAKING CHANGES TO OUR SUPPLY CHAIN FINANCING PROGRAM.WALMART -  WORKING WITH PARTNERS TO ENCOURAGE THEIR IMPACTED EMPLOYEES TO APPLY FOR 150,000 TEMPORARY JOBS CO PLANS TO FILL IN COMING MONTHS.
WALMART NAMES SAMEER AGGARWAL AS CEO OF BEST PRICE, WALMART INDIA. WALMART SAYS NAMED SAMEER AGGARWAL AS CEO OF BEST PRICE, WALMART INDIA.
WALMART SAYS ASSOCIATES TO HAVE QUICKER ACCESS TO EARNINGS THROUGH NEW WEEKLY ACCESS OPTION. ASSOCIATES WILL HAVE QUICKER ACCESS TO THEIR EARNINGS THROUGH A NEW WEEKLY ACCESS OPTION.THIS NEW OPTION WILL HELP PROVIDE MORE CASH IN HAND FOR ASSOCIATES SOONER THAN NORMAL TWO-WEEK PAY PERIOD.THROUGH JUNE, ASSOCIATES NATIONWIDE, WILL RECEIVE INSTANT ACCESS TO 50% OF THEIR EARNED, NET WAGES ON A WEEKLY BASIS.
