WALMART SELECTS TRANSCARENT TO PROVIDE GO-TO-MARKET SOLUTION FOR SELF-INSURED EMPLOYERS. BENTONVILLE, ARK. & PALO ALTO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALMART (NYSE: WMT) AND TRANSCARENT TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY WOULD BE WORKING TOGETHER AS GO-TO-MARKET PARTNERS FOR SELF-INSURED EMPLOYERS ACROSS THE COUNTRY. THE AGREEMENT ALLOWS TRANSCARENT, WHICH OFFERS EMPLOYEES AND THEIR DEPENDENTS A NEW, DIFFERENT AND BETTER HEALTH AND CARE EXPERIENCE, TO SHARE WALMART'S EVERYDAY LOW COST ON PHARMACEUTICALS AND OTHER SERVICES WITH SELF-INSURED EMPLOYERS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES FOR THE FIRST TIME.
