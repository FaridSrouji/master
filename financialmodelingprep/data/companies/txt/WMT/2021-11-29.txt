WALMART ANNOUNCES CFO TRANSITION PLAN. BENTONVILLE, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALMART INC. ANNOUNCED THAT EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER BRETT BIGGS WILL BEGIN TRANSITIONING FROM WALMART DURING THIS UPCOMING YEAR, AFTER 22 YEARS WITH THE COMPANY, TO BEGIN A NEW CHAPTER OF OPPORTUNITIES IN BOTH THE FOR-PROFIT AND NON-PROFIT SECTORS.
