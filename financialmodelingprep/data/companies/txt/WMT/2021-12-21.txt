VERIFIQUE™ LAUNCHES ON WALMART MARKETPLACE. SAN DIEGO, DEC. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VERITEQUE USA, INC. IS ANNOUNCING THE LAUNCH OF THE VERIFIQUE BRAND ON WALMART MARKETPLACE IN THE UNITED STATES, AS A NEWLY REGISTERED SELLER ON THE PLATFORM. VERIFIQUE HAS LISTED BOTH OF THEIR FLAGSHIP MADE IN THE USA PRODUCTS ON THE PLATFORM, THE VERIFIQUE CANNABIS DETECTION TEST, AND THE VERIFIQUE FENTANYL+ DETECTION TEST.
