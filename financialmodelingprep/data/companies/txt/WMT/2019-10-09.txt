GREGORY S. FORAN TO CONTINUE AS AN EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF WALMART INC. WALMART INC. ANNOUNCED THAT BEGINNING ON NOVEMBER 1, 2019, MR. FORAN WILL CONTINUE TO BE EMPLOYED BY THE COMPANY AND SERVE AS AN EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY IN A TRANSITIONAL ROLE REPORTING TO THE COMPANY’S PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER UNTIL JANUARY 31, 2020, AT WHICH TIME HIS EMPLOYMENT WITH THE COMPANY WILL END. MR. FORAN HAS SERVED AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF THE COMPANY’S WALMART U.S. SEGMENT SINCE AUGUST 2014.
