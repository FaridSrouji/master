POPABLE AND WALMART JOIN FORCES TO HELP SMALL BUSINESS RETAILERS GET BACK ON THEIR FEET AFTER THE PANDEMIC. HOUSTON , OCT. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- POPABLE, A POP-UP SHOP MARKETPLACE PLATFORM CONNECTING BRANDS AND SPACES FOR SHORT-TERM RETAIL LEASING, AND WALMART, THE LARGEST NATIONAL RETAIL DEPARTMENT STORE, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THEY HAVE ENTERED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP THAT WILL ALLOW SMALL BUSINESSES TO RENT RETAIL SPACE IN WALMART STORES ACROSS THE COUNTRY FOR SHORT-TERM LEASING. SMALL BUSINESSES WILL HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY TO RENT OUT POP-UP SPACE IN DESIGNATED WALMART STORES WITHOUT BEING COMMITTED TO A LONG-TERM LEASE.
