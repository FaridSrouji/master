WALMART OUTLINES GROWTH STRATEGY, UNVEILS NEXT GENERATION SUPPLY CHAIN AT 2023 INVESTMENT COMMUNITY MEETING. TAMPA, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALMART INC. (NYSE: WMT) IS KICKING OFF ITS TWO-DAY 2023 INVESTMENT COMMUNITY MEETING, WHERE LEADERSHIP WILL HIGHLIGHT HOW THE COMPANY IS INVESTING TO STRENGTHEN ITS BUSINESS THROUGH ITS PEOPLE AND AN UNPARALLELED, NEXT GENERATION SUPPLY CHAIN NETWORK OF STORES, CLUBS AND FULFILLMENT CENTERS, AND DRIVING FUTURE GLOBAL GROWTH OPPORTUNITIES ACROSS ITS OMNICHANNEL ECOSYSTEM AND HIGH VALUE INITIATIVES.
