WALMART TO HOST 2023 INVESTMENT COMMUNITY MEETING. BENTONVILLE, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALMART INC. (NYSE: WMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL HOST ITS 2023 INVESTMENT COMMUNITY MEETING ON APRIL 4-5 IN FLORIDA. ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 5, BEGINNING AT 8 A.M. EDT, THE COMPANY WILL WEBCAST PRESENTATIONS FROM DOUG MCMILLON, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, JOHN DAVID RAINEY, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, AND THE COMPANY'S BUSINESS UNIT CEOS FOR WALMART U.S.
