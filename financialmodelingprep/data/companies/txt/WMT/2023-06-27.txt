GENPACT AND WALMART EXTEND PARTNERSHIP IN NORTH AMERICA. CONTRACT EXTENSION BUILDS ON COLLABORATIVE RELATIONSHIP TO LAYER DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY INTO FINANCE OPERATIONS NEW YORK , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENPACT (NYSE: G), A GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM FOCUSED ON DELIVERING OUTCOMES THAT TRANSFORM BUSINESSES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS EXTENDED ITS PARTNERSHIP WITH WALMART (NYSE: WMT) TO CONTINUE TO SUPPORT ITS NORTH AMERICAN FINANCE AND ACCOUNTING OPERATIONS. THE CURRENT EXTENSION BUILDS ON THE RELATIONSHIP THAT THE TWO COMPANIES BEGAN IN 2018 IN NORTH AMERICA WHICH HAS SINCE EXPANDED TO OTHER MARKETS IN LATIN AMERICA AND SOUTH AFRICA.
