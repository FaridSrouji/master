SAM'S CLUB BRINGS SAMPLING BACK TO CLUBS NATIONWIDE. BENTONVILLE, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SAM'S CLUB TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RETURN OF ITS SAMPLING AND DEMO PROGRAM, TASTE & TIPS. OFFICIALLY RE-LAUNCHING THIS WEEK, TASTE & TIPS ALLOWS MEMBERS TO SAMPLE FOOD AND REVIEW PRODUCTS WHEN THEY VISIT THE CLUB. INITIALLY, TASTE & TIPS WILL TAKE PLACE ON THE WEEKENDS AND IN LIMITED QUANTITIES, USING NEW SAFETY MEASURES, SUCH AS SEALED SAMPLES. SAM'S CLUB'S SAMPLING PROGRAM HAS BEEN AN ATTRACTION IN CLUBS FOR MORE THAN 35 YEARS, BUT IT WAS PAUSED LAS
