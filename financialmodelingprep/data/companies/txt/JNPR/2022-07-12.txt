OIL INDIA SELECTS JUNIPER NETWORKS TO ACCELERATE INDIA'S JOURNEY TOWARD ACHIEVING ENERGY INDEPENDENCE. BANGALORE, INDIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JUNIPER NETWORKS (NYSE: JNPR), A LEADER IN SECURE, AI-DRIVEN NETWORKS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE BEEN SELECTED BY OIL INDIA LIMITED, THE COUNTRY'S SECOND LARGEST STATE-OWNED OIL AND GAS COMPANY, FOR UPGRADES TO ITS DATA CENTER AND CAMPUS NETWORKS TO SUPPORT THE COMPANY'S SUSTAINABILITY GOALS AND DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION JOURNEY THAT WILL CONTRIBUTE TO INDIA'S VISION OF ENERGY INDEPENDENCE.
