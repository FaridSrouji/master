UNIVERSITI MALAYSIA PERLIS GRADUATES TO AN AI-DRIVEN NETWORK FROM JUNIPER NETWORKS TO ELEVATE THE LEARNING EXPERIENCES OF THE NATION'S FUTURE ENGINEERS. KUALA LUMPUR, MALAYSIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JUNIPER NETWORKS (NYSE: JNPR), A LEADER IN SECURE, AI-DRIVEN NETWORKS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT UNIVERSITI MALAYSIA PERLIS (UNIMAP), A LEADING TECHNICAL UNIVERSITY IN MALAYSIA, HAS SELECTED JUNIPER'S SOLUTIONS FOR UPGRADES TO ITS CAMPUS AND DATA CENTERS NETWORKS, ENHANCING DIGITAL LEARNING EXPERIENCES FOR ITS STUDENT AND STAFF.
