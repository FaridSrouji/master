JUNIPER NETWORKS, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 23, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JUNIPER NETWORKS, INC. HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.19 PER SHARE TO BE PAID ON DECEMBER 23, 2019 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 2, 2019.
