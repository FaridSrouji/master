SYNOPSYS AND JUNIPER NETWORKS INVEST IN NEW COMPANY TO PURSUE FAST-GROWING SILICON PHOTONICS MARKET. NEW COMPANY WILL PROVIDE INDUSTRY'S FIRST "LASER-ON-A-CHIP" OPEN SILICON PHOTONICS PLATFORM FOR TELECOM, DATACOM, LIDAR, HEALTHCARE, HPC, AI, AND OPTICAL COMPUTING APPLICATIONS MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF. , APRIL 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) AND JUNIPER NETWORKS (NYSE: JNPR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE CLOSED A TRANSACTION TO FORM A NEW, SEPARATE COMPANY THAT WILL PROVIDE THE INDUSTRY WITH AN OPEN SILICON PHOTONICS PLATFORM TO ADDRESS THE GROWING PHOTONIC REQUIREMENTS IN APPLICATIONS SUCH AS TELECOM, DATACOM, LIDAR, HEALTHCARE, HPC, AI, AND OPTICAL COMPUTING.
