GENERAL DYNAMICS APPOINTS AIKEN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CFO; MARZILLI TO RETIRE. RESTON, VA. , DEC. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS APPOINTED CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER JASON AIKEN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF THE TECHNOLOGIES SEGMENT EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2023.
