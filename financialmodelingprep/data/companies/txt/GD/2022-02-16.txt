GDIT AWARDED $162M DISA IDENTITY, CREDENTIAL AND ACCESS MANAGEMENT PHASE III PRODUCTION OTA AGREEMENT. FALLS CHURCH, VA., FEB. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL DYNAMICS INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY (GDIT), A BUSINESS UNIT OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE:GD), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN AWARDED THE IDENTITY, CREDENTIAL AND ACCESS MANAGEMENT (ICAM) OTHER TRANSACTION AUTHORITY (OTA) PHASE III PRODUCTION AGREEMENT BY THE DEFENSE INFORMATION SYSTEMS AGENCY (DISA).
