GENERAL DYNAMICS ELECTRIC BOAT AWARDED $698 MILLION CONTRACT MODIFICATION FOR OVERHAUL OF USS HARTFORD. GROTON, CONN. , JULY 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL DYNAMICS ELECTRIC BOAT, A BUSINESS UNIT OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD), ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WAS AWARDED A MODIFICATION OF THE PREVIOUSLY AWARDED U.S. NAVY CONTRACT FOR THE REPAIR, MAINTENANCE AND MODERNIZATION OF THE SUBMARINE USS HARTFORD (SSN 768).
