GENERAL DYNAMICS BOARD ANNOUNCES DIVIDEND. RESTON, VA. , JUNE 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.32 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE AUGUST 11, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JULY 7, 2023.
