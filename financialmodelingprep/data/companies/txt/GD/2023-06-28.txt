GDIT'S INTELLIGENCE BUSINESS AWARDED $580 MILLION IN CONTRACT WINS. COMPANY TO PROVIDE CYBERSECURITY AND SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT SERVICES FALLS CHURCH, VA. , JUNE 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL DYNAMICS INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY (GDIT), A BUSINESS UNIT OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS INTELLIGENCE & HOMELAND SECURITY DIVISION WAS AWARDED TWO CONTRACTS VALUED AT $580 MILLION.
