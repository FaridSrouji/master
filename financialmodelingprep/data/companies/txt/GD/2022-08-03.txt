GENERAL DYNAMICS BOARD DECLARES DIVIDEND. RESTON, VA. , AUG. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.26 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE NOVEMBER 10, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON OCTOBER 7, 2022.
