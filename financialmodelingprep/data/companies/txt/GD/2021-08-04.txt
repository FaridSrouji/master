GENERAL DYNAMICS BOARD DECLARES DIVIDEND. RESTON, VA., AUG. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.19 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE NOVEMBER 12, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON OCTOBER 8, 2021.
