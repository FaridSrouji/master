GENERAL DYNAMICS BOARD INCREASES DIVIDEND. RESTON, VA. , MARCH 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.32 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE MAY 12, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON APRIL 14, 2023.
