GDIT AWARDED $383 MILLION U.S. NAVY TRAINING SUPPORT SERVICES CONTRACT. COMPANY WILL TRAIN OVER 100,000 U.S. AND ALLIED SAILORS GLOBALLY FALLS CHURCH, VA. , JUNE 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL DYNAMICS INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY (GDIT), A BUSINESS UNIT OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE:GD), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WAS AWARDED A $383 MILLION TRAINING SUPPORT SERVICES CONTRACT BY THE NAVAL SURFACE WARFARE CENTER DAHLGREN DIVISION IN VIRGINIA.
