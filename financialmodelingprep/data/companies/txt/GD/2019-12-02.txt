GENERAL DYNAMICS CORPORATION WINS $22.2 BILLION CONTRACT FOR NINE VIRGINIA-CLASS SUBMARINES. GENERAL DYNAMICS CORP. HAS BEEN AWARDED A $22.2 BILLION CONTRACT MODIFICATION TO BUILD NINE VIRGINIA-CLASS SUBMARINES FOR THE U.S. NAVY. THE DEAL COVERS THE NINE VESSELS OF BLOCK 5, EIGHT OF WHICH CONTAIN THE VIRGINIA PAYLOAD MODULE, TO BE DESIGNED AND BUILT OVER THE NEXT DECADE. THE CONTRACT ALSO INCLUDES AN OPTION FOR SPARE MATERIALS AND AN ADDITIONAL SUBMARINE TO INCLUDE THE VPM, WHICH, IF EXERCISED, WOULD PUSH THE TOTAL VALUE OF THE DEAL OVER $24 BILLION.
GENERAL DYNAMICS - U.S. NAVY CONTRACT INCLUDES OPTION OF A TENTH SHIP TO BE CONSTRUCTED, BRINGING TOTAL POTENTIAL CONTRACT VALUE TO ABOUT $24.1 BILLION.
