ALL-NEW GULFSTREAM G800 MAKES FIRST FLIGHT. INDUSTRY'S LONGEST-RANGE AIRCRAFT DEMONSTRATES PROGRAM MATURITY, LAUNCHES FLIGHT-TEST PROGRAM SAVANNAH, GA. , JUNE 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GULFSTREAM AEROSPACE CORP., A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE ALL-NEW ULTRALONG-RANGE GULFSTREAM G800 SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED ITS FIRST FLIGHT, OFFICIALLY LAUNCHING THE FLIGHT-TEST PROGRAM OF THE INDUSTRY'S LONGEST-RANGE AIRCRAFT.
