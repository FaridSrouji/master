GENERAL DYNAMICS MISSION SYSTEMS AWARDED $229 MILLION U.S. ARMY CONTRACT TO BUILD NEXT GENERATION CRYPTOGRAPHIC KEY LOADER. FAIRFAX, VA., FEB. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL DYNAMICS MISSION SYSTEMS ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WAS AWARDED A CONTRACT BY THE U.S. ARMY TO DEVELOP AND PRODUCE A CERTIFIED HAND-HELD DEVICE TO MANAGE AND TRANSFER CRYPTOGRAPHIC KEY MATERIAL AND MISSION PLANNING DATA.
