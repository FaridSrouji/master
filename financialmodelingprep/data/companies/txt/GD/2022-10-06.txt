GENERAL DYNAMICS ELECTRIC BOAT AWARDED $533 MILLION FOR VIRGINIA-CLASS SUBMARINE SUPPORT. GROTON, CONN. , OCT. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL DYNAMICS ELECTRIC BOAT, A BUSINESS UNIT OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD), ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WAS AWARDED A U.S. NAVY CONTRACT MODIFICATION FOR LEAD-YARD SUPPORT, DEVELOPMENT STUDIES AND DESIGN EFFORTS RELATED TO VIRGINIA-CLASS ATTACK SUBMARINES.
