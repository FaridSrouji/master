GENERAL DYNAMICS SEES FULL-YEAR EPS OF BETWEEN $11 AND $11.10 - CONF CALL. GENERAL DYNAMICS EXEC CEO SAYS MUCH OF THE PROBLEM IN Q2 RESTS WITH JET AVIATION WHERE CO INCURRED A $19 MILLION OPERATING LOSS DRIVEN, IN PART DUE TO COVID-19 - CONF CALL.GENERAL DYNAMICS CEO SAYS ALL OF CO'S MAJOR PROGRAMS ARE WELL SUPPORTED IN THE CONGRESSIONAL MARKUPS OF THE DEFENSE FUNDING BILLS.GENERAL DYNAMICS CEO SAYS SHUTDOWN OF TWO OF CO'S LARGE EUROPEAN MANUFACTURING SITES IN SEVILLE & TRUVIA BY SPANISH GOVERNMENT HURT OPERATIONS; SITES NOW REOPENED.GENERAL DYNAMICS CEO SAYS CO'S INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY UNIT IS MOST DIRECTLY IMPACTED BY COVID-19.GENERAL DYNAMICS CEO SAYS STRIKE AT BATH WAS IMMATERIAL TO RESULTS; IT IS CO'S SMALLEST SHIPYARD GENERATING LESS THAN 2% OF CO'S PROFIT.GENERAL DYNAMICS SEES FULL-YEAR EPS OF BETWEEN $11 AND $11.10.GENERAL DYNAMICS SEES FULL-YEAR AEROSPACE REVENUE OF ABOUT $8.4 BILLION.GENERAL DYNAMICS CEO - "THIS WILL BE THE LOW POINT OF THE YEAR".GENERAL DYNAMICS CEO SAYS RIGHT NOW AT GULFSTREAM, CO HAS SEEN AN INCREASE IN DEMAND; SAYS IT IS TOO SOON TO BE ABLE TO TELL THE SLOPE OF THAT RECOVERY.GENERAL DYNAMICS SEES FULL-YEAR REVENUE OF ABOUT $38.4 BILLION.
GENERAL DYNAMICS REPORTS Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.18. GENERAL DYNAMICS REPORTS SECOND-QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.18.Q2 REVENUE $9.3 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $8.95 BILLION.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $2.15 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.32 AIRCRAFT DELIVERED DESPITE PANDEMIC-CAUSED DELIVERY CHALLENGES.TOTAL BACKLOG AT END OF SECOND-QUARTER 2020 WAS $82.7 BILLION, 22% HIGHER THAN YEAR-AGO QUARTER.ESTIMATED POTENTIAL CONTRACT VALUE WAS $49.6 BILLION AT QUARTER-END.COMPANY-WIDE OPERATING MARGIN FOR Q2 WAS 9.1% VERSUS 11.4%.
