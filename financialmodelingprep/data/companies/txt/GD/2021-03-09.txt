GULFSTREAM AWARDED $696 MILLION IN CONTRACTS FOR U.S. AIR FORCE SPECIAL MISSIONS SUPPORT. SAVANNAH, GA., MARCH 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GULFSTREAM AEROSPACE CORP., A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE:GD), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN AWARDED $696 MILLION IN CONTRACTS FROM THE U.S. AIR FORCE LIFE CYCLE MANAGEMENT CENTER FOR ENGINEERING SERVICES SUPPORT AND CONTRACTOR LOGISTICS FOR C-20 AND C-37 AIRCRAFT.
