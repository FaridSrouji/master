GENERAL DYNAMICS LAND SYSTEMS AWARDED UP TO $280 MILLION FOR TROPHY ACTIVE PROTECTION SYSTEM KITS FOR ABRAMS TANKS. U.S. ARMY CONTRACT WILL INCREASE TANKS' SURVIVABILITY ON THE BATTLEFIELD  STERLING HEIGHTS, MICH. , JULY 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL DYNAMICS LAND SYSTEMS, A BUSINESS UNIT OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN AWARDED A COMPETITIVE AWARD WORTH UP TO $280,112,700 TO PROCURE TROPHY READY KITS AS AN ACTIVE PROTECTION SYSTEM (APS) FOR ABRAMS MAIN BATTLE TANKS.
