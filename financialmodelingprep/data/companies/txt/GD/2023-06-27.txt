GENERAL DYNAMICS LAND SYSTEMS AWARDED $769 MILLION TO ADVANCE TO NEXT PHASE OF OMFV COMPETITION. STERLING HEIGHTS, MICH. , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAL DYNAMICS LAND SYSTEMS, A BUSINESS UNIT OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN SELECTED BY THE U.S. ARMY TO ADVANCE TO THE DETAILED DESIGN AND PROTOTYPE BUILD AND TEST PHASES OF THE XM30 MECHANIZED INFANTRY COMBAT VEHICLE COMPETITION.
