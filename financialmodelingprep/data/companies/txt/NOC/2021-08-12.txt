GRAHAM ROBINSON JOINS NORTHROP GRUMMAN BOARD OF DIRECTORS. FALLS CHURCH, VA., AUG. 12, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) ANNOUNCED IT HAS ELECTED GRAHAM ROBINSON TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ROBINSON IS THE SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT OF STANLEY INDUSTRIAL, A BUSINESS SEGMENT OF STANLEY BLACK & DECKER, A POSITION HE HAS HELD SINCE APRIL 2020.
