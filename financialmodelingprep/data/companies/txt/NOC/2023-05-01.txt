NORTHROP GRUMMAN'S INTEGRATED BATTLE COMMAND SYSTEM ACHIEVES INITIAL OPERATIONAL CAPABILITY. HUNTSVILLE, ALA., MAY 01, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION'S (NYSE: NOC) INTEGRATED BATTLE COMMAND SYSTEM (IBCS) HAS ACHIEVED INITIAL OPERATIONAL CAPABILITY FROM THE U.S. ARMY. WITH THIS DECLARATION, THE SYSTEM IS NOW READY TO BE FIELDED TO U.S. ARMY UNITS TO FURTHER SUPPORT THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE SYSTEM'S CAPABILITIES.
