NORTHROP GRUMMAN DEVELOPING THE NEXT GENERATION RADAR FOR THE F-35 LIGHTNING II. BALTIMORE, JAN. 11, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) IS DEVELOPING AN/APG-85, AN ADVANCED ACTIVE ELECTRONICALLY SCANNED ARRAY (AESA) RADAR FOR THE F-35 LIGHTNING II. NORTHROP GRUMMAN CURRENTLY MANUFACTURES THE AN/APG-81 ACTIVE ELECTRONICALLY SCANNED ARRAY (AESA) FIRE CONTROL RADAR, THE CORNERSTONE TO THE F-35 LIGHTNING II'S SENSOR SUITE.
