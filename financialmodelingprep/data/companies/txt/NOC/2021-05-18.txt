NORTHROP GRUMMAN INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND 8 PERCENT TO $1.57. FALLS CHURCH, VA., MAY 18, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.57 PER SHARE ON NORTHROP GRUMMAN COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 16, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS JUNE 1, 2021.
