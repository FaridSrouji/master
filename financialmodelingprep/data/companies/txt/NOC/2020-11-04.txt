NORTHROP GRUMMAN TO PARTICIPATE IN BAIRD'S 2020 GLOBAL INDUSTRIAL CONFERENCE. FALLS CHURCH, VA., NOV. 04, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) WILL PARTICIPATE IN BAIRD'S 2020 GLOBAL INDUSTRIAL CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, NOV. 11. KATHY WARDEN, CHAIRMAN, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND PRESIDENT, AND DAVE KEFFER, CORPORATE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL PRESENT BEGINNING AT 9:05 A.M. EASTERN TIME. THE PRESENTATION WILL BE WEBCAST LIVE AT HTTPS://INVESTOR.NORTHROPGRUMMAN.COM.
