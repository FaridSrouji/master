NORTHROP GRUMMAN BOARD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. FALLS CHURCH, VA., AUG. 17, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.73 PER SHARE ON NORTHROP GRUMMAN COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE SEPT. 14, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS AUG. 29, 2022.
