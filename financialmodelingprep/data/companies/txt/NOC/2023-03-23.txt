NORTHROP GRUMMAN DEMONSTRATES PLATFORM AGNOSTIC IN-FLIGHT CONNECTIVITY FOR US AIR FORCE. SAN DIEGO, MARCH 23, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) SUCCESSFULLY DEMONSTRATED THE TECHNICAL CAPABILITY TO CONNECT A FULL AIRCRAFT ECOSYSTEM. THE COMPANY SHOWCASED ITS SOFTWARE PROGRAMMABLE OPEN MISSION SYSTEMS COMPLIANT (SPOC) MULTIFUNCTION PROCESSOR IN A FLIGHT TEST FOR THE U.S. AIR FORCE, HIGHLIGHTING THE ABILITY TO INTEGRATE EXISTING AND FUTURE PLATFORMS USING ADVANCED DATA LINKS.
