MYNARIC ACTS AS SOLE LASER COMMUNICATION SUPPLIER FOR NORTHROP GRUMMAN AS PART OF MILESTONE U.S. GOVERNMENT PROGRAM. IN MARCH 2022, MYNARIC RECEIVED LARGEST ORDER OF OPTICAL COMMUNICATIONS TERMINALS TO DATE IN THE FRAMEWORK OF THE SPACE DEVELOPMENT AGENCY'S TRANCHE 1 TRANSPORT LAYER PROGRAM LOS ANGELES, CA / ACCESSWIRE / JUNE 14, 2022 / AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED, MYNARIC (NASDAQ:MYNA)(FRA:M0Y) HAS BEEN SELECTED BY NORTHROP GRUMMAN (NYSE:NOC) FOR DELIVERY OF CONDOR MK3 OPTICAL COMMUNICATION TERMINALS IN THE FRAMEWORK OF A MILESTONE U.S. GOVERNMENT PROGRAM. NORTHROP GRUMMAN WAS SELECTED BY THE SPACE DEVELOPMENT AGENCY (SDA) TO PROVIDE 42 SATELLITES FOR THE TRANCHE 1 TRANSPORT LAYER PROGRAM THAT WILL SERVE CRITICAL NEEDS OF THE U.S. NATIONAL DEFENSE SPACE ARCHITECTURE (NDSA).
