NORTHROP GRUMMAN TAPS SOLSTAR SPACE TO PROVIDE WIRELESS ACCESS POINTS FOR HALO MODULE OF NASA'S LUNAR GATEWAY. SUPPORTING NORTHROP GRUMMAN INNOVATION SYSTEMS (NGIS) AND NASA'S ARTEMIS PROGRAM, SOLSTAR SPACE WILL DEVELOP THE WIFI ACCESS POINTS THAT WILL BE UTILIZED FOR COMMUNICATIONS ABOARD HALO. SANTA FE, N.M.
