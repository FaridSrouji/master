NORTHROP GRUMMAN INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND 10 PERCENT TO $1.73. FALLS CHURCH, VA., MAY 17, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.73 PER SHARE ON NORTHROP GRUMMAN COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 15, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS MAY 31, 2022.
