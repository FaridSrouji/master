INVESTIGATION NOTICE: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES IT IS INVESTIGATING CLAIMS AGAINST NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION AND ENCOURAGES INVESTORS WITH LOSSES TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - FEBRUARY 13, 2023) - THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, ANNOUNCES THAT IT IS INVESTIGATING CLAIMS ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS OF NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION ("NORTHROP GRUMMAN" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE: NOC) FOR VIOLATIONS OF THE SECURITIES LAWS.THE INVESTIGATION FOCUSES ON WHETHER THE COMPANY ISSUED FALSE AND/OR MISLEADING STATEMENTS AND/OR FAILED TO DISCLOSE INFORMATION PERTINENT TO INVESTORS.
