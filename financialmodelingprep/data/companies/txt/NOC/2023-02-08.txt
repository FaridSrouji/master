NORTHROP GRUMMAN TO PARTICIPATE IN CITI'S 2023 GLOBAL INDUSTRIAL TECH AND MOBILITY CONFERENCE. FALLS CHURCH, VA., FEB. 08, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) WILL PARTICIPATE IN CITI'S 2023 GLOBAL INDUSTRIAL TECH AND MOBILITY CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, FEB. 22. KATHY WARDEN, CHAIR, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND PRESIDENT, AND DAVE KEFFER, CORPORATE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL PRESENT BEGINNING AT 8:50 A.M. EASTERN TIME. THE PRESENTATION WILL BE WEBCAST LIVE AT HTTP://INVESTOR.NORTHROPGRUMMAN.COM .
