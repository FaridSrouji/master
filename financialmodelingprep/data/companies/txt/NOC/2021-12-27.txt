JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE BEGINS MILLION-MILE JOURNEY. KOUROU, FRENCH GUIANA, DEC. 25, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE, BUILT IN PARTNERSHIP WITH NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC), SUCCESSFULLY LAUNCHED TODAY FROM THE EUROPEAN SPACE AGENCY'S (ESA) SPACEPORT IN KOUROU, FRENCH GUIANA ABOARD AN ARIANE 5 ROCKET.
