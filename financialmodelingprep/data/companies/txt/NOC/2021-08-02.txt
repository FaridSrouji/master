NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION COMMENCES EXCHANGE OFFERS AND CONSENT SOLICITATIONS. FALLS CHURCH, VA., AUG. 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) (“NORTHROP GRUMMAN”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMMENCED OFFERS TO ELIGIBLE HOLDERS TO EXCHANGE (EACH, AN “EXCHANGE OFFER” AND, COLLECTIVELY, THE “EXCHANGE OFFERS”) ANY AND ALL OUTSTANDING NOTES OF ITS DIRECT WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY, NORTHROP GRUMMAN SYSTEMS CORPORATION (“NGSC”), AS SET FORTH IN THE TABLE BELOW (THE “EXISTING NOTES”) FOR (1) UP TO $1,171,189,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF NEW NOTES ISSUED BY NORTHROP GRUMMAN (THE “NEW NOTES”) AND (2) CASH.
