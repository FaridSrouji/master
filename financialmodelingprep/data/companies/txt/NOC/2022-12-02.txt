NORTHROP GRUMMAN AND THE US AIR FORCE INTRODUCE THE B-21 RAIDER, THE WORLD'S FIRST SIXTH-GENERATION AIRCRAFT. PALMDALE, CALIF., DEC. 02, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) AND THE U.S. AIR FORCE UNVEILED THE B-21 RAIDER TO THE WORLD TODAY. THE B-21 JOINS THE NUCLEAR TRIAD AS A VISIBLE AND FLEXIBLE DETERRENT DESIGNED FOR THE U.S. AIR FORCE TO MEET ITS MOST COMPLEX MISSIONS.
