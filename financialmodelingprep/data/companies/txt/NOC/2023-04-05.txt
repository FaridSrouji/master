NORTHROP GRUMMAN WINS COLLIER TROPHY FOR NASA'S JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE. WASHINGTON, APRIL 05, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE INDUSTRY TEAM, LED BY NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC), HAS WON THE PRESTIGIOUS NATIONAL AERONAUTIC ASSOCIATION (NAA) ROBERT J. COLLIER TROPHY FOR REVOLUTIONIZING THE FIELD OF ASTROPHYSICS WITH THE TEAM'S PIONEERING DESIGN AND EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE OF NASA'S JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE.
