NORTHROP GRUMMAN'S IBCS READY FOR FIELDING, CONNECTING THE BATTLESPACE. HUNTSVILLE, ALA., NOV. 08, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN'S INTEGRATED BATTLE COMMAND SYSTEM (IBCS) COMPLETED THE FINAL, DEMANDING ASSESSMENT, DURING ITS RECENT OPERATIONAL TEST AND EVALUATION (IOT&E). COMPLETION OF IOT&E IS THE LAST MAJOR TEST EVENT BEFORE THE U.S. ARMY MAKES THE DECISION TO MOVE THE PROGRAM FROM LOW RATE INITIAL PRODUCTION TO FULL RATE PRODUCTION, ALLOWING IBCS TO BE FIELDED AROUND THE WORLD.
