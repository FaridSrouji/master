NORTHROP GRUMMAN BOARD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. FALLS CHURCH, VA., FEB. 15, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.57 PER SHARE ON NORTHROP GRUMMAN COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE MARCH 16, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS FEB. 28, 2022.
