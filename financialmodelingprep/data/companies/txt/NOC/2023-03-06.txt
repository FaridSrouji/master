NORTHROP GRUMMAN TEST FIRES STAGE-ONE SOLID ROCKET MOTOR FOR SENTINEL MISSILE. PROMONTORY, UTAH, MARCH 06, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) CONDUCTED ITS FIRST FULL-SCALE STATIC TEST FIRE OF THE SENTINEL STAGE-ONE SOLID ROCKET MOTOR AT THE COMPANY'S TEST FACILITY IN PROMONTORY.
