EMERSON MARKS 100 YEARS OF AIR CONDITIONING AND REFRIGERATION INNOVATION THROUGH ITS COPELAND™ TECHNOLOGY. HONG KONG--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EMERSON (NYSE: EMR) IS CELEBRATING THIS YEAR AS THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF ITS COPELAND™ BRAND, A NAME THAT HAS BECOME SYNONYMOUS WITH LEADERSHIP IN THE DESIGN AND MANUFACTURE OF ENERGY EFFICIENT, RELIABLE COMPRESSORS TO POWER AIR CONDITIONING AND REFRIGERATION SYSTEMS THAT ARE ENHANCING AND PROTECTING ENVIRONMENTS WHERE PEOPLE LIVE AND WORK. IT'S A MILESTONE THAT EMERSON WILL RECOGNIZE OVER THE NEXT 12 MONTHS AS THE COMPANY CONTINUES TO INNOVATE ADVANCED COPELAND PROD
