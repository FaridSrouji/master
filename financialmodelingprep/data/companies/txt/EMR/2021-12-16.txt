EMERSON STRENGTHENS PRESENCE IN RENEWABLE ENERGY SECTOR WITH ACQUISITION OF MITA-TEKNIK. ST. LOUIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EMERSON (NYSE: EMR) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ACQUIRED MITA-TEKNIK IN AN ALL-CASH TRANSACTION.
