EMERSON'S MAJORITY OWNED SUBSIDIARY, ASPENTECH, ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE METALS AND MINING SOFTWARE LEADER MICROMINE. ST. LOUIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EMERSON'S (NYSE: EMR) MAJORITY OWNED SUBSIDIARY, ASPENTECH (NASDAQ: AZPN), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE MICROMINE, AN END-TO-END MINING SOFTWARE PROVIDER. THIS ACQUISITION WILL BUILD ON ASPENTECH'S LEADING SOFTWARE PORTFOLIO, FURTHERING ITS DIVERSIFICATION STRATEGY INTO THE METALS AND MINING MARKET AND FACILITATING THE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION AND SUSTAINABILITY GOALS OF THE INDUSTRY.
