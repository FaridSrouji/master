EMERSON ANNOUNCES STRATEGIC APPOINTMENTS, ENHANCED FOCUS ON ESG WITH CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER. ST. LOUIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TO ENHANCE ITS FOCUSED EFFORTS AND PURPOSE AROUND ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY, GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY AND INDUSTRIAL SOFTWARE LEADER EMERSON (NYSE: EMR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ITS FIRST CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER. MIKE TRAIN, WHO HAS SERVED AS PRESIDENT SINCE 2018 AND A MEMBER OF EMERSON'S OFFICE OF THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE (OCE) SINCE 2016, WILL STEP DOWN AS PRESIDENT TO TAKE ON THIS NEW AND IMPORTANT ROLE FOR EMERSON.
