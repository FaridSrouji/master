SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISSLAW LLP INVESTIGATES ASPEN TECHNOLOGY, INC.. NEW YORK, OCT. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISSLAW LLP IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ASPEN TECHNOLOGY, INC. ("ASPEN TECHNOLOGY" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: AZPN) IN CONNECTION WITH THE COMPANY'S PROPOSED MERGER WITH EMERSON ELECTRIC CO.'S (NYSE: EMR) INDUSTRIAL SOFTWARE BUSINESSES. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, ASPEN TECHNOLOGY SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE APPROXIMATELY $87 PER SHARE IN CASH AND 0.42 SHARES OF COMMON STOCK OF THE NEW ASPENTECH, A NEWLY FORMED COMPANY, FOR EACH SHARE OF ASPEN TECHNOLOGY COMMON STOCK THEY OWN, FOR TOTAL IMPLIED PER-SHARE MERGER CONSIDERATION OF APPROXIMATELY $160 PER SHARE.
