EMERSON NAMED 2023 ENERGY STAR® PARTNER OF THE YEAR FOR ENERGY MANAGEMENT. AWARD RECOGNIZES COMPANY'S PROGRESS TOWARD ITS NET ZERO EMISSIONS TARGETS ST. LOUIS , MARCH 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY AND SOFTWARE COMPANY EMERSON (NYSE: EMR) HAS RECEIVED THE 2023 ENERGY STAR PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARD FROM THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (EPA) AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY.
