EMERSON ELECTRIC CO. SOLICITS PROXIES FROM THE SHAREHOLDERS. ON NOVEMBER 27, 2019, EMERSON ELECTRIC CO. FILED A PRELIMINARY PROXY STATEMENT AND URGED THE SHAREHOLDERS OF THE COMPANY TO VOTE FOR THE COMPANY’S NOMINEES IN THE UPCOMING ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS TO BE HELD ON FEBRUARY 4, 2020.
