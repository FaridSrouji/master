SMART DEVICES WITH PRIVACY FOCUS HAVE MORE CONSUMER APPEAL, ACCORDING TO RESEARCH BY SENSI SMART THERMOSTATS. ST. LOUIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--SENSI SMART THERMOSTATS HAS ANNOUNCED THE FINDINGS FROM ITS “SMART HOME DATA PRIVACY” INAUGURAL SURVEY. THE SURVEY, COMMISSIONED BY EMERSON'S (NYSE: EMR) CLIMATE TECHNOLOGIES BUSINESS, ASKED 2,000 U.S. HOMEOWNERS AGES 18-76 TO ANSWER QUESTIONS FOCUSING ON SMART HOME PRODUCT USE AND ADOPTION. KEY AMONG THE FEEDBACK WAS THAT 4 IN 5 (80%) OF SMART THERMOSTAT OWNERS AND NON-OWNERS ARE CONCERNED ABOUT SMART THERMOSTAT MANUFACTURERS HAVING ACCESS TO PERSONAL DATA.
