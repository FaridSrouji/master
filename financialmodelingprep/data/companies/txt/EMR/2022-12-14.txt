ZEDEDA AND EMERSON EXPAND RELATIONSHIP TO MODERNIZE THE INDUSTRIAL EDGE. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ZEDEDA, THE LEADER IN EDGE ORCHESTRATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC INVESTMENT FROM EMERSON VENTURES, THE CORPORATE VENTURE CAPITAL ARM OF EMERSON (NYSE: EMR), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY, SOFTWARE AND AUTOMATION LEADER. THE MOVE EXTENDS THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE TWO COMPANIES, AS EMERSON IS ALSO A ZEDEDA CUSTOMER.
