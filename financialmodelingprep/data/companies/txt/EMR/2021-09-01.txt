TURNSPIRE CAPITAL PARTNERS COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF THE DANIEL MEASUREMENT AND CONTROL BUSINESS OF EMERSON ELECTRIC. NEW YORK , SEPT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TURNSPIRE CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC ("TURNSPIRE") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF THE DANIEL MEASUREMENT AND CONTROL BUSINESS ("DANIEL" OR THE "COMPANY") FROM EMERSON ELECTRIC CO. (NYSE: EMR).
