BIO-RAD REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER 2023 FINANCIAL RESULTS. HERCULES, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BIO-RAD LABORATORIES, INC. (NYSE: BIO AND BIOB), A GLOBAL LEADER IN LIFE SCIENCE RESEARCH AND CLINICAL DIAGNOSTIC PRODUCTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2023. FIRST-QUARTER 2023 NET SALES WERE $676.8 MILLION, A DECREASE OF 3.3 PERCENT COMPARED TO $700.1 MILLION REPORTED FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022. ON A CURRENCY-NEUTRAL BASIS, QUARTERLY SALES DECREASED 0.3 PERCENT COMPARED TO THE SAME PERIOD IN 2022. COVID-RELATED S.
