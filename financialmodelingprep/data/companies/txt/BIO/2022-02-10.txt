BIO-RAD REPORTS FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. HERCULES, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BIO-RAD LABORATORIES, INC. (NYSE: BIO AND BIOB), A GLOBAL LEADER OF LIFE SCIENCE RESEARCH AND CLINICAL DIAGNOSTIC PRODUCTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER AND YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2021. FOURTH-QUARTER 2021 NET SALES WERE $732.8 MILLION, A DECREASE OF 7.2 PERCENT COMPARED TO $789.8 MILLION REPORTED FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020. ON A CURRENCY-NEUTRAL BASIS, QUARTERLY SALES DECREASED 6.9 PERCENT COMPARED TO THE SAME PERIOD IN 2020.
