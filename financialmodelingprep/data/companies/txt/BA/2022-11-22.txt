THINKYOUNG AND BOEING LAUNCH FIRST CODING SCHOOL FOR DISPLACED UKRAINIAN TEENAGERS IN GDAŃSK. GDAŃSK, POLAND , NOV. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY THINKYOUNG AND BOEING [NYSE: BA] KICKED OFF THE FIRST CODING SCHOOL FOR UKRAINIAN TEENAGERS. BOEING INTERNATIONAL PRESIDENT SIR MICHAEL ARTHUR, BOEING POLAND MANAGING DIRECTOR RAFAŁ STEPNOWSKI, BOEING ENGINEERING TECHNOLOGY CENTER LEADER IRYNA BIELIENKO FROM THE COMPANY'S UKRAINE TEAM AND THINKYOUNG FOUNDER AND CEO ANDREA GEROSA HOSTED AN OPENING CEREMONY AT THE BOEING OFFICE IN GDAŃSK TODAY.
