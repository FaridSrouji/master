BOEING DOUBLES SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL PURCHASE FOR COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS, BUYING 5.6 MILLION GALLONS FOR 2023. SEATTLE , FEB. 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE:BA] HAS AGREEMENTS TO PURCHASE 5.6 MILLION GALLONS (21.2 MILLION LITERS) OF BLENDED SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL (SAF) PRODUCED BY NESTE, THE WORLD'S LEADING SAF PRODUCER, TO SUPPORT ITS U.S. COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS THROUGH 2023. THESE AGREEMENTS MORE THAN DOUBLE THE COMPANY'S SAF PROCUREMENT FROM LAST YEAR.
