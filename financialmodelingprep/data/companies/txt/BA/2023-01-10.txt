BOEING REPORTS COMMERCIAL ORDERS AND DELIVERIES FOR 2022. SEATTLE , JAN. 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] REPORTED 774 COMMERCIAL ORDERS LAST YEAR AFTER CANCELLATIONS AND CONVERSIONS, INCLUDING 561 ORDERS FOR THE 737 FAMILY AND 213 ORDERS FOR THE COMPANY'S MARKET-LEADING TWIN-AISLE AIRPLANES. BOEING DELIVERED 69 COMMERCIAL JETS IN DECEMBER, INCLUDING 53 737 MAX, BRINGING TOTAL DELIVERIES FOR 2022 TO 480 AIRPLANES.
BOEING ANNOUNCES FOURTH-QUARTER DELIVERIES. ARLINGTON, VA. , JAN. 10, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOEING COMPANY [NYSE: BA] ANNOUNCED TODAY MAJOR PROGRAM DELIVERIES ACROSS ITS COMMERCIAL AND DEFENSE OPERATIONS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022.
