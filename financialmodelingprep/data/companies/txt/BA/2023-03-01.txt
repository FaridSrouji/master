BOEING CHIEF ENGINEER GREG HYSLOP TO RETIRE; NEW ENGINEERING LEADERS NAMED. ARLINGTON, VA. , MARCH 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT GREG HYSLOP, THE COMPANY'S CHIEF ENGINEER AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF ENGINEERING, TEST & TECHNOLOGY, WILL RETIRE THIS JUNE AFTER A 41-YEAR CAREER THAT SPANNED BOEING'S AEROSPACE AND DEFENSE PORTFOLIO.
