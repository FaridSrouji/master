BOEING TO HIGHLIGHT SUSTAINABILITY, TECHNOLOGY AND PARTNERSHIPS AT SINGAPORE AIRSHOW 2022. SINGAPORE, FEB. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AT THE SINGAPORE AIRSHOW THIS MONTH, THE LARGEST IN SOUTHEAST ASIA, BOEING (NYSE: BA) WILL SHOWCASE ITS INDUSTRY-LEADING PORTFOLIO OF COMMERCIAL, DEFENSE AND SUPPORT SERVICES BY HIGHLIGHTING ADVANCED SYSTEMS AND CAPABILITIES FOCUSED ON SAFE AND SUSTAINABLE AEROSPACE. BOEING'S PRESENCE AT THE SHOW WILL INCLUDE ITS NEWEST FUEL-EFFICIENT WIDEBODY JET, THE 777X, ALONG WITH THE COMPANY'S OFFERINGS IN MARITIME PATROL AIRCRAFT, ADVANCED FIGHTERS AND TRAINERS, AND AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS.
