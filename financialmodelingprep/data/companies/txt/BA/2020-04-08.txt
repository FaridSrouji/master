SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HAS HALTED PRODUCTION FOR BOEING PROGRAMS. SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS' ACTIONS IN RESPONSE TO COVID-19.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - HAS HALTED PRODUCTION FOR BOEING PROGRAMS.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - IMPLEMENTED WORKFORCE REDUCTIONS OF 2,800 EMPLOYEES IN WICHITA, KANSAS AND 400 EMPLOYEES IN OKLAHOMA.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - SPIRIT DEFENSE WORK AS WELL AS AIRBUS AND OTHER NON-BOEING WORK WILL CONTINUE AT FACILITIES.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - INITIATED A VOLUNTARY RETIREMENT PROGRAM FOR 850 HOURLY AND SALARIED WORKERS.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - DEFERRED OVER $120 MILLION OF CAPITAL EXPENDITURES.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - REDUCED ITS CASH DIVIDEND TO A PENNY PER SHARE.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - NEGOTIATED A NEW PRODUCTION AGREEMENT WITH BOEING; EXTENDED MAX CONTRACT THREE YEARS TO 2033.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS - HALTED PRODUCTION FOR BOEING PROGRAMS AT FACILITIES IN WICHITA AND IN TULSA AND MCALESTER FOR INDEFINITE PERIOD OF TIME.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - RECEIVED $225 MILLION ADVANCE FROM BOEING.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - DEFERRED REPAYMENT OF $123 MILLION ADVANCE FROM BOEING TO 2022.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC - CONTINUED SUSPENSION OF ITS SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS - AMENDED CREDIT FACILITY PROVIDING FOR COVENANT RELIEF INTO 2021, SECURED $375 MILLION SHORT-TERM DELAYED DRAW TERM LOAN FACILITY.
