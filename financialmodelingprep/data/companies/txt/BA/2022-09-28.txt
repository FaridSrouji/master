CHINA AIRLINES FINALIZES LANDMARK ORDER FOR UP TO 24 BOEING 787 DREAMLINERS. SEATTLE , SEPT. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING (NYSE:BA) AND CHINA AIRLINES ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE FINALIZED AN ORDER FOR UP TO 24 787 DREAMLINERS, AS THE CARRIER INVESTS IN THE FUEL-EFFICIENT WIDEBODY TO EXPAND PASSENGER AND CARGO OPERATIONS.
