JAPAN AIRLINES SELECTS 737-8 TO GROW SUSTAINABLE WORLD-CLASS FLEET. SEATTLE , MARCH 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE:BA] AND JAPAN AIRLINES (JAL) HAVE FINALIZED AN ORDER FOR 21 SUPER-EFFICIENT 737-8 JETS. THE VERSATILE 737-8 WILL POSITION JAL TO STRENGTHEN ITS DOMESTIC AND REGIONAL NETWORK AS PASSENGER TRAFFIC CONTINUES TO RETURN TO PRE-PANDEMIC LEVELS.
