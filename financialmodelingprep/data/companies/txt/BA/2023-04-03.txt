IS&S RECEIVES PRODUCTION ORDER FOR ITS GPS UNIT FROM BOEING FOR THE T-7A RED HAWK TRAINER. EXTON, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS & SUPPORT (IS&S) (NASDAQ: ISSC) RECEIVED A PRODUCTION ORDER FROM THE BOEING COMPANY (NYSE: BA) TO PROVIDE ITS GLOBAL POSITIONING SYSTEM (GPS) SENSOR UNITS FOR THE BOEING T-7A RED HAWK TRAINER. DELIVERIES WILL BEGIN IN JULY 2023 WITH ADDITIONAL OPTIONS FOR CONTINUOUS DELIVERIES THROUGHOUT THE LIFE OF THE CURRENT U.S. AIR FORCE PROGRAM.
