AS AIR TRAVEL REBOUNDS, BOEING FORECASTS DEMAND FOR MORE THAN 41,000 NEW AIRPLANES BY 2041. FARNBOROUGH, UNITED KINGDOM , JULY 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH DEMAND REBOUNDING FOR INTERNATIONAL AIR TRAVEL FOLLOWING ONGOING RECOVERY IN MANY DOMESTIC MARKETS, BOEING [NYSE: BA] TODAY PROJECTED DEMAND FOR MORE THAN 41,000 NEW AIRPLANES THROUGH 2041, UNDERSCORING AVIATION INDUSTRY RESILIENCE TWO YEARS AFTER THE PANDEMIC BEGAN. BOEING RELEASED ITS 2022 COMMERCIAL MARKET OUTLOOK (CMO), THE COMPANY'S ANNUAL LONG-TERM FORECAST, IN ADVANCE OF THE FARNBOROUGH INTERNATIONAL AIRSHOW.
