BOEING TO DELIVER AH-64E APACHE HELICOPTERS TO THREE COUNTRIES. DEC 26  - BOEING CO <BA.N>::BOEING - CO, U.S. ARMY HAVE FINALIZED ORDERS FROM 3 NATIONS TO PROVIDE THEIR ARMED FORCES WITH AH-64E APACHE MODEL.BOEING - CONTRACTS ARE FOR REMANUFACTURE OF 47 EXISTING AH-64D APACHES.BOEING - TOTAL COMBINED VALUE OF ORDERS FROM THREE NATIONS FOR AH-64E APACHE MODEL IS OVER $560 MILLION.BOEING - REMANUFACTURED AIRCRAFT WILL BE DELIVERED IN THE EARLY 2020S.
