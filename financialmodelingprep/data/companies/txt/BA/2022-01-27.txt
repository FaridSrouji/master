CHINA AIRLINES AND BOEING ANNOUNCE ORDER FOR FOUR 777 FREIGHTERS. SEATTLE, JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] AND CHINA AIRLINES TODAY ANNOUNCED THE TAIWAN FLAG CARRIER HAS ORDERED FOUR 777 FREIGHTERS, ADDING TO ITS EXTENSIVE FLEET OF BOEING AIRPLANES. VALUED AT $1.4 BILLION AT LIST PRICES, THE ORDER WILL ENABLE THE AIRLINE TO CAPTURE NEW MARKET OPPORTUNITIES AS GLOBAL AIR CARGO DEMAND CONTINUES TO GROW.
