BOEING REAFFIRMS COMMITMENT TO HBCUS WITH $8 MILLION INVESTMENT THROUGH THURGOOD MARSHALL COLLEGE FUND. ARLINGTON, VA., JUNE 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, BOEING (NYSE: BA) AND THE THURGOOD MARSHALL COLLEGE FUND (TMCF) ANNOUNCED A NEW $8 MILLION, MULTI-YEAR PARTNERSHIP TO SUPPORT THE DEVELOPMENT OF FUTURE TECHNICAL TALENT.
