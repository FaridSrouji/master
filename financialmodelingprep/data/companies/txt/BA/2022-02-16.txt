WESTERN GLOBAL AIRLINES PURCHASES TWO BOEING 777 FREIGHTERS. SEATTLE, FEB. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] AND WESTERN GLOBAL AIRLINES TODAY ANNOUNCED A FIRM ORDER FOR TWO 777 FREIGHTERS, THE FIRST NEW-PRODUCTION FREIGHTERS FOR THE ALL-BOEING CARGO OPERATOR BASED IN ESTERO, FLA. THE ORDER WAS FINALIZED IN JANUARY 2022 AND IS CURRENTLY LISTED AS UNIDENTIFIED IN BOEING'S ORDER BACKLOG.
