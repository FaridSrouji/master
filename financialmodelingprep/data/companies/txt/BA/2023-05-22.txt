VIP CUSTOMERS ORDER UP TO FOUR BOEING BUSINESS JETS. GENEVA , MAY 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] ANNOUNCED TODAY ORDERS FOR UP TO FOUR PREMIUM, ULTRA-LONG-RANGE BOEING BUSINESS JETS (BBJ). THE ORDERS TO UNDISCLOSED VIP CUSTOMERS INCLUDE TWO BBJ 787-8S, ONE BBJ 737-7 JET AND AN OPTION TO PURCHASE A BBJ 777-9.
