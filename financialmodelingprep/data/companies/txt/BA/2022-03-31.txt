ASL ORDERS UP TO 20 ADDITIONAL 737-800 BOEING CONVERTED FREIGHTERS TO DRIVE ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY. -  DEAL INCLUDES 10 FIRM ORDERS AND 10 PURCHASE RIGHTS -  ASL CEO: 737-800BCF OFFERS LOWER FUEL BURN AND REDUCES CARRIER'S ENVIRONMENTAL FOOTPRINT SEATTLE , MARCH 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUBLIN-HEADQUARTERED ASL AVIATION HOLDINGS (ASL) AND BOEING [NYSE: BA] TODAY ANNOUNCED AN ORDER FOR UP TO 20 ADDITIONAL 737-800 BOEING CONVERTED FREIGHTERS (BCF). THE AGREEMENT IS FOR 10 FIRM ORDERS AND 10 PURCHASE RIGHTS.
