BOEING REPORTS INCREASED STABILITY AND GROWTH FOR AIRCRAFT FINANCE SECTOR. CHICAGO , MAY 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING CAPITAL CORPORATION, A WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BOEING [NYSE: BA], TODAY RELEASED THE 2022 COMMERCIAL AIRCRAFT FINANCING MARKET OUTLOOK (CAFMO) SHOWING IMPROVING FINANCING STABILITY AS THE INDUSTRY RECOVERS FROM THE IMPACTS OF THE GLOBAL PANDEMIC. "FINANCIERS AND INVESTORS REMAIN COMMITTED TO THE LONG-TERM FUNDAMENTALS THAT CONTINUE TO MAKE AIRCRAFT A VALUABLE ASSET CLASS," SAID TIM MYERS, PRESIDENT OF BOEING CAPITAL CORPORATION.
