BOEING TO OPEN JAPAN RESEARCH CENTER AND EXPAND SUSTAINABILITY PARTNERSHIPS. TOKYO , AUG. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE:BA] WILL STRENGTHEN ITS PARTNERSHIP WITH JAPAN BY OPENING A NEW BOEING RESEARCH AND TECHNOLOGY (BR&T) CENTER. THE FACILITY WILL FOCUS ON SUSTAINABILITY AND SUPPORT A NEWLY EXPANDED COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH JAPAN'S MINISTRY OF ECONOMY, TRADE AND INDUSTRY (METI).
