BOEING FORECASTS SUFFICIENT CAPITAL FOR AVIATION FINANCE. CHICAGO, APRIL 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] PROJECTS GLOBAL AND DIVERSIFIED FUNDING WILL CONTINUE TO FLOW INTO THE AIRCRAFT FINANCING SECTOR AS THE AVIATION SECTOR NAVIGATES THE GLOBAL PANDEMIC AND VACCINE DEPLOYMENT CONTINUES TO ACCELERATE. "FINANCIERS AND INVESTORS UNDERSTAND THE INDUSTRY'S RESILIENCE AND THE LONG-TERM FUNDAMENTALS THAT MAKE AIRCRAFT A VALUABLE ASSET CLASS," SAID TIM MYERS, PRESIDENT OF BOEING CAPITAL CORPORATION.
