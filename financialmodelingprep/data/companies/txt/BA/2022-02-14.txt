BOEING REACHES RECORD $2 BILLION IN E-COMMERCE SALES. DALLAS, FEB. 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] ACHIEVED AN ANNUAL RECORD FOR E-COMMERCE PARTS SALES LAST YEAR WITH MORE THAN $2 BILLION IN ONLINE ORDERS. FUELED BY INVESTMENT IN DIGITAL TOOLS, BOEING DISTRIBUTION INC. (FORMERLY AVIALL) SOLD NEARLY 70,000 PARTS PRODUCTS THROUGH ITS E-COMMERCE SITE TO COMMERCIAL AND GOVERNMENT CUSTOMERS, ECLIPSING PRE-PANDEMIC LEVELS.
