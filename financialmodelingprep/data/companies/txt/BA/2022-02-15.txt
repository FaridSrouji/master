KOREA SELECTS BOEING TO SUPPORT READINESS FOR THREE CRITICAL DEFENSE PLATFORMS. SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA, FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] HAS RECEIVED THREE PERFORMANCE BASED LOGISTICS (PBL) CONTRACTS FROM THE REPUBLIC OF KOREA'S DEFENSE ACQUISITION PROGRAM ADMINISTRATION (DAPA) TO MANAGE SUSTAINMENT OF REPUBLIC OF KOREA AIR FORCE (ROKAF) F-15K FIGHTER JETS AND AIRBORNE EARLY WARNING & CONTROL (AEW&C) AIRCRAFT, AND REPUBLIC OF KOREA ARMY (ROKA) CH-47 CHINOOK HELICOPTERS. THE FIVE-YEAR CONTRACTS WILL IMPROVE AIRCRAFT AVAILABILITY BY USING PERFORMANCE DATA TO INFORM LOGISTICS AND SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT ACTIVITIES.
