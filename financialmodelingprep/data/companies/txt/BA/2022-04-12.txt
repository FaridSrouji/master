BOEING SELECTS SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS TO SUPPORT B-52 COMMERCIAL ENGINE REPLACEMENT PROGRAM. WICHITA, KAN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS, INC. [NYSE: SPR] TODAY ANNOUNCED A CONTRACT AWARD FROM THE BOEING COMPANY [NYSE: BA] TO PROVIDE ENGINE PYLONS AND NACELLES FOR THE INITIAL PHASE OF THE B-52 COMMERCIAL ENGINE REPLACEMENT PROGRAM (CERP). THE TOTAL CERP EFFORT IS EXPECTED TO REPLACE 608 ENGINES ON THE U.S. AIR FORCE FLEET OF B-52H MODELS.
