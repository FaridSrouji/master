WESTJET EXPANDS FLEET WITH LARGEST BOEING 737 MAX JET, ORDERING UP TO 64 FUEL-EFFICIENT AIRPLANES. SEATTLE , SEPT. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE:BA] AND WESTJET TODAY ANNOUNCED THE AIRLINE IS STRENGTHENING ITS COMMITMENT TO THE 737 MAX FAMILY WITH AN ORDER FOR 42 737-10S AND OPTIONS FOR AN ADDITIONAL 22 JETS.
