BOEING DONATES $1 MILLION TO ASSIST WITH HURRICANE IDA RECOVERY AND RELIEF EFFORTS. CHICAGO, SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING (NYSE: BA) IS COMMITTING $1 MILLION FROM THE BOEING CHARITABLE TRUST TO ASSIST WITH DISASTER RECOVERY AND RELIEF EFFORTS IN THE AFTERMATH OF HURRICANE IDA, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TODAY.
