BOEING PARTNERS WITH TRANSLATION APP TARJIMLY TO HELP BREAK LANGUAGE BARRIERS. ARLINGTON, VA. , NOV. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, BOEING (NYSE: BA) AND TARJIMLY, AN APP-BASED LANGUAGE AND TRANSLATION SERVICE, ANNOUNCED A NEW PARTNERSHIP TO INCREASE TRANSLATION ACCESS FOR REFUGEES AND IMMIGRANTS AROUND THE WORLD.
