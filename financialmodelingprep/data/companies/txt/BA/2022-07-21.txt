BOEING AND QATAR AIRWAYS FINALIZE ORDER FOR 25 737 MAX AIRPLANES. FARNBOROUGH, UNITED KINGDOM , JULY 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE:BA] AND QATAR AIRWAYS TODAY FINALIZED AN ORDER FOR 25 737 MAX AIRPLANES, PROVIDING THE FLAG CARRIER'S SHORT- AND MEDIUM-HAUL FLEET WITH IMPROVED ECONOMICS, FUEL EFFICIENCY AND SUSTAINABLE OPERATIONS. COMPANY LEADERS ANNOUNCED THE ORDER FOR THE 737-10, BOEING'S LARGEST AND MOST EFFICIENT SINGLE-AISLE JET, IN A SIGNING CEREMONY AT THE FARNBOROUGH INTERNATIONAL AIRSHOW.
