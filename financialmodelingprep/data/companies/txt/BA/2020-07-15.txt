BOEING SAYS TO SUPPORT INTERNATIONAL SPACE STATION OPERATIONS THROUGH 2024. TO SUPPORT INTERNATIONAL SPACE STATION OPERATIONS THROUGH 2024.CO WILL CONTINUE SUPPORTING ORBITING LABORATORY THROUGH SEPTEMBER OF 2024 UNDER A $916 MILLION CONTRACT EXTENSION .WILL PROVIDE ENGINEERING SUPPORT SERVICES, RESOURCES, AND PERSONNEL FOR ACTIVITIES ABOARD THE ISS AND MANAGE MANY OF THE STATION’S SYSTEMS.ISS CONTRACT IS VALUED AT ABOUT $225 MILLION ANNUALLY.
