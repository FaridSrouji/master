BOEING CONTRIBUTES $1 MILLION FOR HISPANIC AND LATINX SUPPORT ORGANIZATIONS ACROSS THE U.S.. CHICAGO, SEPT. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, IN OBSERVANCE OF HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH IN THE U.S., BOEING [NYSE: BA] ANNOUNCED A $1 MILLION DONATION TO ORGANIZATIONS SUPPORTING HISPANIC AND LATINX COMMUNITIES.
