BOEING 2023 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT HIGHLIGHTS PROGRESS AND PARTNERS TO DECARBONIZE AEROSPACE. ARLINGTON, VA., JUNE 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] HAS INCREASED RENEWABLE-ENERGY USE IN ITS FACTORIES, PURCHASED MORE SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL FOR COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS AND CONTINUES SIGNIFICANT INVESTMENTS IN AEROSPACE TECHNOLOGIES TO REDUCE FUEL USE, EMISSIONS AND NOISE.
