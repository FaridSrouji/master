BOC AVIATION ANNOUNCES ORDER FOR 40 ADDITIONAL BOEING 737-8 JETS. SEATTLE, DEC. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOC AVIATION LIMITED AND BOEING [NYSE:BA] TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE AIRPLANE LESSOR IS GROWING ITS 737 MAX PORTFOLIO WITH AN ORDER FOR 40 ADDITIONAL 737-8 JETS. WITH THE ORDER, BOC AVIATION IS ADVANCING ITS STRATEGY OF PURCHASING NEW, FUEL-EFFICIENT, ENVIRONMENTALLY SUSTAINABLE AIRPLANES THAT ARE IN DEMAND WITH AIRLINE CUSTOMERS.
