AMERICAN AIRLINES PROVIDES UPDATE ON BOEING 737 MAX. AMERICAN AIRLINES GROUP INC. - AN UPDATE ON THE BOEING 737 MAX.AMERICAN AIRLINES - ANTICIPATES RESUMPTION OF SCHEDULED COMMERCIAL SERVICE ON AMERICAN'S FLEET OF BOEING 737 MAX AIRCRAFT WILL OCCUR AUG. 18, 2020.AMERICAN AIRLINES - REMAINS IN CONTINUOUS CONTACT WITH FEDERAL AVIATION ADMINISTRATION, DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION AND BOEING.AMERICAN AIRLINES - EXPECTS TO GRADUALLY PHASE IN MAX FOR COMMERCIAL SERVICE & WILL INCREASE FLYING ON AIRCRAFT THROUGHOUT MONTH OF AUG AND INTO SEPT.

BOEING, SPEEA ANNOUNCE TENTATIVE AGREEMENT ON FOUR-YEAR CONTRACT EXTENSION. AND SPEEA ANNOUNCE A TENTATIVE AGREEMENT ON FOUR-YEAR CONTRACT EXTENSION.FOUR-YEAR AGREEMENT WOULD EXTEND CONTRACT WITH ENGINEERING AND TECHNICAL PROFESSIONALS THROUGH OCTOBER 2026.FOUR-YEAR AGREEMENT INCLUDES SPECIFIC SALARY ADJUSTMENT FUNDS EACH YEAR FROM 2020 THROUGH 2026.AGREEMENT MAKES SPEEA-REPRESENTED EMPLOYEES ELIGIBLE FOR COMPANY'S PAID PARENTAL LEAVE POLICY.SPEEA'S EXECUTIVE BOARD HAS ENDORSED OFFER, WHICH WILL BE PUT UP FOR A VOTE BY MEMBERSHIP.
