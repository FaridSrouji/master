LUFTHANSA GROUP SELECTS NEW 777-8 FREIGHTER, ORDERS ADDITIONAL 787S. SEATTLE , MAY 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] AND THE LUFTHANSA GROUP TODAY ANNOUNCED THE AIRLINE GROUP WILL CONTINUE ITS STRATEGIC DECISION TO STRENGTHEN LUFTHANSA CARGO WITH AN ORDER FOR SEVEN 777-8 FREIGHTERS, THE INDUSTRY'S NEWEST AND MOST FUEL-EFFICIENT TWIN-ENGINE FREIGHTER. THE GROUP HAS ALSO PLACED A NEW ORDER FOR TWO 777 FREIGHTERS TO ADD TO ITS CARGO FLEET, PROVIDING EXTRA CARGO CAPACITY IN THE NEAR-TERM UNTIL THE DELIVERY OF ITS FIRST 777-8 FREIGHTER.
