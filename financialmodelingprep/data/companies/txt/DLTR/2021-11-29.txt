DOLLAR TREE ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $1.2 BILLION IN SENIOR NOTES. CHESAPEAKE, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DOLLAR TREE, INC. (NASDAQ: DLTR) (THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING (THE “OFFERING”) OF $1.2 BILLION OF ITS SENIOR NOTES, CONSISTING OF $800 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 2.650% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE “2031 NOTES”) AND $400 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 3.375% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2051 (THE “2051 NOTES” AND, TOGETHER WITH THE 2031 NOTES, THE “NOTES”).
