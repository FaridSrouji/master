VECTRA BANK RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF THE 50 MOST COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN COLORADO. DENVER, DEC. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VECTRA BANK HAS BEEN NAMED AN HONOREE OF THE CIVIC 50 COLORADO BY CSR SOLUTIONS OF COLORADO AND POINTS OF LIGHT, THE WORLD'S LARGEST ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO VOLUNTEER SERVICE. THE AWARD RECOGNIZES VECTRA BANK AS ONE OF THE MOST COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN COLORADO, DETERMINED BY AN INDEPENDENTLY ADMINISTERED AND SCORED SURVEY.
