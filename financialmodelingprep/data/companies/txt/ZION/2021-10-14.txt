VECTRA BANK WELCOMES NEW MUNICIPAL FINANCE RELATIONSHIP MANAGER. DENVER, OCT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VECTRA BANK COLORADO IS PLEASED TO WELCOME RYAN MORRILL TO THE MUNICIPAL FINANCE TEAM. MORRILL WILL BE FILLING THE ROLE OF MUNICIPAL FINANCE RELATIONSHIP MANAGER, SERVING VECTRA BANK'S FOOTPRINT ACROSS COLORADO AND NEW MEXICO.
