NEVADA STATE BANK SECURES “BEST IN STATE” DISTINCTION FOR FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. LAS VEGAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEVADA STATE BANK HAS EARNED TOP ACCOLADES FROM LEADING READER POLLS THROUGHOUT THE STATE OF NEVADA, CLAIMING THE “BEST IN STATE” FOR THE FIFTH YEAR IN A ROW. THE HONORS WERE RECENTLY CAPPED OFF WITH THE GOLD FOR BEST BANK IN THE LAS VEGAS REVIEW-JOURNAL'S ANNUAL “BEST OF LAS VEGAS” SURVEY, RELEASED ON DECEMBER 12. NSB ALSO TOOK GOLD FOR BEST WEALTH MANAGEMENT AND SILVER FOR BEST MORTGAGE LENDER FOR THE SOUTHERN NEVADA MARKET. THE WINNING STREAK FOR NEVADA STATE BANK
