NIELSEN REPORTS 2ND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. NEW YORK, JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, NIELSEN HOLDINGS PLC (NYSE: NLSN) ANNOUNCED ITS RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021.  THE COMPANY ALSO INCREASED ITS 2021 GUIDANCE, RAISING THE LOW-END OF ITS REVENUE AND ADJUSTED EBITDA RANGES AND RAISING ITS ADJUSTED EBITDA MARGIN, ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE ("EPS"), AND FREE CASH FLOW GUIDANCE BASED ON STRONG Q2 RESULTS AND INCREASED CONFIDENCE IN ITS FULL-YEAR OUTLOOK.
