NIELSEN REPORTS 3RD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. NEW YORK, OCT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NIELSEN HOLDINGS PLC (NYSE: NLSN) ANNOUNCED ITS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2021. THE COMPANY ALSO INCREASED ITS 2021 GUIDANCE, RAISING ITS REVENUE AND ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE ("EPS") RANGES, AND RAISING THE LOW-END OF ITS ADJUSTED EBITDA AND FREE CASH FLOW GUIDANCE RANGES.
