NIELSEN DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEW YORK, OCT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NIELSEN HOLDINGS PLC (NYSE: NLSN) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.06 PER SHARE OF NIELSEN'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 2, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 18, 2021.
