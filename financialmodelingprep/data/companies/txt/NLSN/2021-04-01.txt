NIELSEN ANNUAL MARKETING REPORT UNDERSCORES THE NEED FOR BRANDS TO EVOLVE STRATEGIES IN WAKE OF COVID-19. NEW YORK, APRIL 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NIELSEN (NYSE: NLSN) TODAY RELEASED ITS ANNUAL MARKETING REPORT: THE ERA OF ADAPTATION . THE REPORT DELVES INTO HOW MARKETERS ARE REVAMPING THEIR STRATEGIES AFTER FACING AND MITIGATING THE DISRUPTION CAUSED BY THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
