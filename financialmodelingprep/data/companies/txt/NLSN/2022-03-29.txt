SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISS LAW INVESTIGATES NIELSEN HOLDINGS PLC. NEW YORK , MARCH 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISS LAW IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NIELSEN HOLDINGS PLC ("NIELSEN" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: NLSN) IN CONNECTION WITH THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF THE COMPANY BY A CONSORTIUM OF PRIVATE EQUITY, LED BY EVERGREEN COAST CAPITAL CORPORATION AND BROOKFIELD BUSINESS PARTNERS L.P. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $28.00 IN CASH FOR EACH SHARE OF NIELSEN COMMON STOCK.
