NIELSEN ANNOUNCES EXPIRATION OF GO-SHOP PERIOD. AFTER THOROUGH OUTREACH, NO ALTERNATIVE PROPOSALS RECEIVED NEW YORK , MAY 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NIELSEN HOLDINGS PLC (NYSE: NLSN) ("NIELSEN") TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPIRATION OF THE 45-DAY "GO-SHOP" PERIOD UNDER ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT (THE "TRANSACTION AGREEMENT") TO BE ACQUIRED BY A CONSORTIUM (THE "CONSORTIUM") OF PRIVATE INVESTMENT FUNDS LED BY EVERGREEN COAST CAPITAL CORPORATION, AN AFFILIATE OF ELLIOTT INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT L.P., AND BROOKFIELD BUSINESS PARTNERS L.P.
