NIELSEN LAUNCHES DIVERSE MEDIA EQUITY PROGRAM. NEW YORK, FEB. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NIELSEN ANNOUNCES THE LAUNCH OF ITS DIVERSE MEDIA EQUITY PROGRAM, WHICH IS DESIGNED TO ELEVATE THE VISIBILITY OF DIVERSE-OWNED MEDIA COMPANIES WITH ADVERTISERS AND AGENCIES. THE COMPREHENSIVE INITIATIVE INCLUDES MEASUREMENT OF DIVERSE-OWNED MEDIA HISTORICALLY EXCLUDED FROM INVESTMENT, FUNDING CERTIFICATION FEES OF DIVERSE-OWNED MEDIA, AS WELL AS THE LAUNCH OF THE FIRST EVER REPORT ON DIVERSE-OWNED MEDIA REACH AND AUDIENCE PROFILES.
