THE CONFERENCE BOARD CONSUMER CONFIDENCE INDEX® SURGED IN MARCH. NEW YORK, MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CONFERENCE BOARD CONSUMER CONFIDENCE INDEX® SURGED IN MARCH TO ITS HIGHEST READING IN A YEAR, AFTER A MODEST INCREASE IN FEBRUARY. THE INDEX NOW STANDS AT 109.7 (1985=100), UP FROM 90.4 IN FEBRUARY.
