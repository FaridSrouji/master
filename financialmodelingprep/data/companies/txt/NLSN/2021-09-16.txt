NIELSEN'S THE GAUGE: MONTHLY TOTAL TV AND STREAMING VIEWING SNAPSHOT FOR AUGUST SHOWS BACK-TO-SCHOOL INFLUENCING STREAMING BEHAVIOR. NEW YORK, SEPT. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NIELSEN (NYSE: NLSN) ANNOUNCED THAT THE GAUGE , ITS MONTHLY TOTAL TV AND STREAMING SNAPSHOT, UNVEILED THAT IN AUGUST 2021, STREAMING MAINTAINED A 28% SHARE OF TOTAL TV VIEWING, COMPARED WITH BROADCAST TV (24%) AND CABLE (38%).
