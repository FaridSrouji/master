FIFTH THIRD FOUNDATION AND NATIONAL URBAN LEAGUE MARK WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT MONTH, CELEBRATE SUCCESS OF WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE FIFTH THIRD FOUNDATION AND THE NATIONAL URBAN LEAGUE ARE COMMEMORATING SEPTEMBER'S WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT MONTH BY ANNOUNCING RESULTS FROM THE FIRST YEAR OF A COLLABORATIVE WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM THAT PROVIDES CAREER ADVANCEMENT OPPORTUNITIES THROUGH UPSKILLING AND RE-SKILLING SERVICES TO UNDER-EMPLOYED PARTICIPANTS.
