FIFTH THIRD ANNOUNCES ACQUISITION OF RIZE MONEY, INC.. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIFTH THIRD BANCORP (NASDAQ: FITB) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THE ACQUISITION OF RIZE MONEY, INC., AN EMBEDDED PAYMENTS PLATFORM THAT PROVIDES PAYMENT INFRASTRUCTURE AND RISK MANAGEMENT CAPABILITIES TO FINTECHS AND OTHER TECHNOLOGY COMPANIES THAT WANT TO OFFER INNOVATIVE FINANCIAL PRODUCTS THROUGH A SINGLE APPLICATION PROGRAMMING INTERFACE. EMBEDDED PAYMENTS IS A CORE COMPONENT OF FIFTH THIRD'S LEADING TREASURY MANAGEMENT BUSINESS, SUPPORTING CLIENTS WHO VALUE SCALE, RI.
