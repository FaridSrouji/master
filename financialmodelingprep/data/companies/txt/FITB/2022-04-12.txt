TIM SPENCE NAMED FIFTH THIRD CEO EFFECTIVE JULY 5. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIFTH THIRD BANCORP (NASDAQ: FITB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT GREG CARMICHAEL HAS ELECTED TO RETIRE AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER EFFECTIVE JULY 5. AT THAT TIME, CARMICHAEL WILL BECOME EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN, CONTINUING TO LEAD THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND PROVIDE ADVICE AND COUNSEL TO THE CEO. TIM SPENCE WILL SUCCEED CARMICHAEL AS FIFTH THIRD'S CEO.
