CORRECTING AND REPLACING FIFTH THIRD FOUNDATION INVESTS $1 MILLION INTO NATIONAL URBAN LEAGUE WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIFTH THIRD BANK AND THE NATIONAL URBAN LEAGUE TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM THAT WILL PROVIDE CAREER ADVANCEMENT OPPORTUNITIES.
