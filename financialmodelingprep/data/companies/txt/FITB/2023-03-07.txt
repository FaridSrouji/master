FIFTH THIRD ANNOUNCES ACQUISITION OF BIG DATA HEALTHCARE, A PAYMENTS COMPANY SERVING NATIONAL HEALTH SYSTEMS, PHYSICIANS. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIFTH THIRD BANCORP (NASDAQ: FITB) TODAY ANNOUNCED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE BIG DATA HEALTHCARE, A TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS PROVIDER FOR HEALTHCARE PAYMENTS AND REMITTANCE. THIS TRANSACTION ADDS NATIONAL HEALTHCARE REVENUE CYCLE CAPABILITIES, ADDRESSING THE COMPLEX AND COMPLICATED NEEDS OF CLIENTS, WHILE BUILDING ON FIFTH THIRD'S LEGACY OF DIGITAL INNOVATION AND FOCUS ON THE HEALTHCARE SECTOR.
