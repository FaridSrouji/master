GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP, A LEADING SECURITIES FRAUD LAW FIRM, ANNOUNCES INVESTIGATION OF GENERAC HOLDINGS INC. (GNRC) ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS. LOS ANGELES--( BUSINESS WIRE )--GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP (“GPM”), A LEADING NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LAW FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMMENCED AN INVESTIGATION ON BEHALF OF GENERAC HOLDINGS INC. (“GENERAC” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: GNRC) INVESTORS CONCERNING THE COMPANY'S POSSIBLE VIOLATIONS OF THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
