GNRC LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES GENERAC HOLDINGS INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , DEC. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN GENERAC HOLDINGS INC. ("GENERAC" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: GNRC) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF GENERAC INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD.
