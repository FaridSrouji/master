MEAN GREEN LAUNCHES FURY, NEW COMPACT ELECTRIC MOWER. ONE OF THE NARROWEST BATTERY-POWERED, STAND-ON MOWERS AVAILABLE IN MARKET WAUKESHA, WIS. , FEB. 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEAN GREEN, A LEADING BATTERY-POWERED, COMMERCIAL-GRADE TURF CARE MANUFACTURER AND A GENERAC POWER SYSTEMS (NYSE: GNRC) COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FURY, A COMPACT STAND-ON MOWER DESIGNED TO ALLOW ACCESS TO AREAS THAT MOST OTHER COMMERCIAL MOWERS ARE TOO WIDE TO ENTER.
