POMERANTZ LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THE FILING OF A CLASS ACTION AGAINST GENERAC HOLDINGS INC. AND CERTAIN OFFICERS - GNRC. NEW YORK, AUG. 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- POMERANTZ LLP ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED AGAINST GENERAC HOLDINGS INC. ("GENERAC" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: GNRC) AND CERTAIN OF ITS OFFICERS. THE CLASS ACTION, FILED IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE CENTRAL DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA, AND DOCKETED UNDER 21-CV-07009, IS ON BEHALF OF A CLASS CONSISTING OF ALL PERSONS AND ENTITIES OTHER THAN DEFENDANTS THAT PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED PUBLICLY TRADED GENERAC SECURITIES BETWEEN FEBRUARY 23, 2021 AND JULY 29, 2021, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
