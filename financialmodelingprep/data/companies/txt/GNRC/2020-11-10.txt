MILWAUKEE WORLD FESTIVAL, INC. AND GENERAC POWER SYSTEMS ANNOUNCE PARTNERSHIP. MILWAUKEE, NOV. 10, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MILWAUKEE WORLD FESTIVAL, INC. AND GENERAC POWER SYSTEMS TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW LONG-TERM PARTNERSHIP, WHICH INCLUDES TITLE SPONSORSHIP OF A MAJOR STAGE AT HENRY MAIER FESTIVAL PARK.
