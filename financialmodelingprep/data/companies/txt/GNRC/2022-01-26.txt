GENERAC GRID SERVICES PARTNERS WITH CALIFORNIA UTILITY ON VIRTUAL POWER PLANT PROGRAM. DENVER, JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENERAC GRID SERVICES TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN SELECTED BY SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA EDISON (SCE) AS ONE OF THE VIRTUAL POWER PLANT (VPP) PROVIDERS FOR THE COMPANY'S POWER FLEX PROGRAM. THE INITIATIVE EMPOWERS SCE RESIDENTIAL CUSTOMERS TO EXTEND THE VALUE AND RETURN ON INVESTMENT OF THEIR RESIDENTIAL SOLAR PV AND BATTERY STORAGE SYSTEMS BY ENROLLING THEM IN A VPP DESIGNED TO ALLEVIATE PRESSURE ON THE POWER GRID DURING TIMES OF PEAK DEMAND.
