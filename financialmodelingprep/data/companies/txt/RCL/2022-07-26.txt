SES TO POWER INNOVATIVE, IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCES ONBOARD LEADING FAMILY CRUISE LINES FLEET WITH O3B MPOWER. LUXEMBOURG--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SES, THE WORLD'S LEADING CONTENT CONNECTIVITY SATELLITE SERVICE PROVIDER, WILL BE PROVIDING GROUND-BREAKING HIGH-SPEED SATELLITE-BASED CONNECTIVITY SERVICES TO THE NEWEST LANDMARK SHIP OF A LEADING FAMILY CRUISE LINE, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED TODAY. THE CRUISE LINE EXISTING FLEET WILL ALSO TRANSITION ITS CONNECTIVITY TO SES'S SECOND-GENERATION MEDIUM EARTH ORBIT (MEO) SYSTEM O3B MPOWER, ALONGSIDE INSTALLING THE SERVICE ONTO ITS NEWBUILD PROGRAMME.
