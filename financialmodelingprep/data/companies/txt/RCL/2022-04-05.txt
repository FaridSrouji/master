ROYAL CARIBBEAN CELEBRATES KEEL LAYING FOR ICON OF THE SEAS. MIAMI , APRIL 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BUILDING OF ROYAL CARIBBEAN INTERNATIONAL'S HIGHLY ANTICIPATED CRUISE SHIP, ICON OF THE SEAS , HAS REACHED A PIVOTAL MILESTONE AHEAD OF ITS FALL 2023 DEBUT. A KEEL-LAYING CEREMONY TOOK PLACE AT FINNISH SHIPYARD MEYER TURKU TO OFFICIALLY MARK THE START OF THE REVOLUTIONARY SHIP'S PHYSICAL CONSTRUCTION, WITH ROYAL CARIBBEAN AND MEYER TURKU SENIOR EXECUTIVES AND TEAMS IN ATTENDANCE.
