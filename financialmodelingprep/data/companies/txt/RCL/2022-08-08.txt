ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP ANNOUNCES CLOSING OF $1.15 BILLION SENIOR CONVERTIBLE NOTES OFFERING AND REPURCHASE OF $1.15 BILLION EXISTING SENIOR CONVERTIBLE NOTES. MIAMI , AUG. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT CLOSED ITS OFFERING OF $1.15 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 6.00% CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025 (THE "CONVERTIBLE NOTES"), INCLUDING $150 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF THE CONVERTIBLE NOTES ISSUED PURSUANT TO THE EXERCISE IN FULL OF THE INITIAL PURCHASERS' OPTION TO BUY ADDITIONAL CONVERTIBLE NOTES. THE CONVERTIBLE NOTES WILL MATURE ON AUGUST 15, 2025.
