ROYAL CARIBBEAN NAMES WONDER MOM AS GODMOTHER OF WONDER OF THE SEAS. A PENNSYLVANIA LOCAL RECEIVES THE TIME-HONORED ROLE AFTER THE CRUISE LINE'S POPULAR TIKTOK SEARCH MIAMI , NOV. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN INTERNATIONAL'S SEARCH FOR A WONDER MOM AND THE GODMOTHER OF WONDER OF THE SEAS IS COMPLETE. PENNSYLVANIA RESIDENT MARIE MCCREA , NOMINATED BY HER DAUGHTER – ALLIE – IN THE #SEARCHFORWONDERMOM TIKTOK CONTEST, WAS CHOSEN TO TAKE ON THE TIME-HONORED ROLE FOR THE NEWEST OASIS CLASS SHIP.
