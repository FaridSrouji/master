ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP ANNOUNCES CLOSING OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES OFFERING. MIAMI, AUG. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS CLOSED ITS PRIVATE OFFERING OF $1,000,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 5.500% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2026 (THE "NOTES"). THE NOTES WILL MATURE ON AUGUST 31, 2026 UNLESS EARLIER REDEEMED OR REPURCHASED.
