ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP PROVIDES BUSINESS UPDATE AND REPORTS ON FIRST QUARTER 2021. MIAMI, APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) TODAY COMMENTED ON THE BUSINESS CONSIDERING THE GLOBAL COVID-19 PANDEMIC AND REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. BUSINESS UPDATE SINCE THE LAST BUSINESS UPDATE, THE COMPANY HAS ANNOUNCED NEW ITINERARIES FOR THIS SUMMER FOR ELEVEN ADDITIONAL SHIPS FROM THE CARIBBEAN AND EUROPE IN ADDITION TO THE FOUR SHIPS ALREADY SAILING.
