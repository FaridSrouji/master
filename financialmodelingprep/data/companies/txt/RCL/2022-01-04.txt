ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP ANNOUNCES UPSIZING AND PRICING OF $1,000,000,000 SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES. MIAMI, JAN. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS PRICED ITS PRIVATE OFFERING OF $1,000,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 5.375% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2027 (THE "NOTES"). THE AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF NOTES TO BE ISSUED WAS INCREASED TO $1.0 BILLION.
