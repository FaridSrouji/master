ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP FORMS A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH ICON INFRASTRUCTURE TO LAUNCH NEW CHAPTER OF DESTINATION DEVELOPMENT. MIAMI , DEC. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A NEW PARTNERSHIP WITH ICON INFRASTRUCTURE PARTNERS VI, L.P. ("ICON VI")1, A FUND ADVISED BY ICON INFRASTRUCTURE LLP ("ICON INFRASTRUCTURE" OR "ICON") TO DEVELOP STRATEGIC CRUISE PORT INFRASTRUCTURE IN SUPPORT OF ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP'S ROBUST GROWTH PLANS.
