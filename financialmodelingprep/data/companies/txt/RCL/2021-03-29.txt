ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP ANNOUNCES CLOSING OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES OFFERING. MIAMI, MARCH 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS CLOSED ITS PRIVATE OFFERING OF $1,500,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 5.50% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2028 (THE "NOTES"). THE NOTES WILL MATURE ON APRIL 1, 2028 UNLESS EARLIER REDEEMED OR REPURCHASED.
