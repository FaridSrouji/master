ADVENTURE IS BACK: ROYAL CARIBBEAN MAKES LONG-AWAITED RETURN TO THE CARIBBEAN. MIAMI, JUNE 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A MILESTONE MORE THAN 15 MONTHS IN THE MAKING: ROYAL CARIBBEAN INTERNATIONAL AND THE COMMUNITIES OF THE CARIBBEAN ARE CELEBRATING THE CRUISE LINE'S HIGHLY ANTICIPATED RETURN TO THE REGION AS ADVENTURE OF THE SEAS  PREPARES TO SET SAIL FROM NASSAU, THE BAHAMAS YESTERDAY. THE FIRST OF ROYAL CARIBBEAN'S SHIPS TO RESUME CRUISING IN THE WESTERN HEMISPHERE, ADVENTURE WELCOMED MORE THAN 1,000 VACATIONERS ON ITS OPENING CRUISE.
