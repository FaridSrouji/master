ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP SETS JUNE RETURN FOR SAILING FROM THE U.S.. MIAMI, MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) HAS RECEIVED APPROVAL TO RESUME SAILINGS FROM THE UNITED STATES AFTER MORE THAN A YEAR OF SUSPENDED OPERATIONS DURING THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC. ON JUNE 26, THE CRUISE COMPANY WILL MARK A LONG-AWAITED RETURN WITH CELEBRITY CRUISES' CELEBRITY EDGE DEPARTING FROM PORT EVERGLADES IN FORT LAUDERDALE.
