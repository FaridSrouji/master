BEYOND EXPECTATIONS: CELEBRITY CRUISES' NEWEST SHIP, CELEBRITY BEYOND, DEFIES IMAGINATION, WONDER AND LUXURY. MIAMI, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, CELEBRITY CRUISES PRESIDENT AND CEO LISA LUTOFF-PERLO UNVEILED THE COMPANY'S THIRD SHIP IN THE COMPANY'S INDUSTRY-TRANSFORMING "NEW LUXURY" EDGE SERIES® -- CELEBRITY BEYOND SM. DESIGNED TO GIVE GUESTS ENDLESS OPPORTUNITIES TO DISCONNECT FROM THE WORLD -- WHILE SIMULTANEOUSLY RECONNECTING WITH THE WORLD THAT SURROUNDS THEM -- CELEBRITY BEYOND GOES FURTHER THAN IMAGINED POSSIBLE FOR A CRUISE SHIP, CREATING A NEW WORLD OF WONDER AND LUXURY.
