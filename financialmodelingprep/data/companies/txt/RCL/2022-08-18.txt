ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP ANNOUNCES CLOSING OF $1.25 BILLION SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES OFFERING TO REFINANCE NEAR TERM DEBT MATURITIES. MIAMI , AUG. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS CLOSED ITS PRIVATE OFFERING OF $1,250,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 11.625% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2027 (THE "NOTES"). THE NOTES WILL MATURE ON AUGUST 15, 2027 UNLESS EARLIER REDEEMED OR REPURCHASED.
