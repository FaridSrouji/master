SILVERSEA'S FLAGSHIP SILVER NOVA(SM) TAKES DISCERNING TRAVELLERS TO NEW HORIZONS ON 71-DAY GRAND VOYAGE TO SOUTH AMERICA. MONACO, FEB. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, SILVERSEA CRUISES® LAUNCHES ITS ALL-NEW  71-DAY GRAND VOYAGE  TO CENTRAL AND SOUTH AMERICA ABOARD SILVERSEA'S LATEST SHIP SILVER NOVASM . SETTING SAIL ON JANUARY 4, 2024, SILVERSEA'S GUESTS WILL JOURNEY DEEP INTO 38 DESTINATIONS IN 18 COUNTRIES, TRAVELLING IN THE LUXURY CRUISE LINE'S TRADEMARK LEVEL OF SERVICE ABOARD THE SOON-TO-BE-LAUNCHED SILVER NOVA.
