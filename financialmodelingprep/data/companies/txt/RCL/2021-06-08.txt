CELEBRITY EQUINOX HEATS UP SUMMER VACATION OPTIONS AS THE LATEST SHIP IN THE CELEBRITY CRUISES' FLEET TO BE APPROVED TO SAIL FROM A U.S. PORT BEGINNING JULY 25. MIAMI, JUNE 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING THE WAY WITH THE FIRST SHIP TO SAIL IN NORTH AMERICA JUST DAYS AGO ON JUNE 5,  CELEBRITY CRUISES' MUCH ANTICIPATED RETURN TO SAILING CONTINUES, AS THE NEW-LUXURY SHIP CELEBRITY EQUINOX HAS NOW BEEN APPROVED BY THE CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION (CDC) TO SAIL FROM FORT LAUDERDALE ON JULY 25, 2021. IT WILL CLOSELY FOLLOW CELEBRITY EDGE 'S INDUSTRY-FIRST CRUISE DEPARTURE FROM U.S. WATERS FOR THE CARIBBEAN ON JUNE 26, 2021.
