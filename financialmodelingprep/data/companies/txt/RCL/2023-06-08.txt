ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP EXTENDS COLLABORATION TO CAPTURE CRITICAL OCEAN CONSERVATION DATA ONBOARD SHIPS. OCEANSCOPE PROGRAM RENEWAL – ANNOUNCED ON WORLD OCEANS DAY – BUILDS ON DECADES-LONG PUBLIC-PRIVATE COLLABORATION WITH THE UNIVERSITY OF MIAMI, NASA, AND NOAA TO ADDRESS OCEAN HEALTH ISSUES. MIAMI , JUNE 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) STRENGTHENING A DECADES-LONG COMMITMENT TO OCEAN CONSERVATION AND ENVIRONMENTAL RESEARCH EFFORTS, ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP TODAY ANNOUNCED A FOUR-YEAR EXTENSION OF ITS INVESTMENT IN OCEANSCOPE, AN OPEN-SOURCE DATA PROGRAM THAT PROVIDES SCIENTISTS WITH CRITICAL INFORMATION TO STUDY CLIMATE AND OCEAN CONSERVATION.
