ROYAL CARIBBEAN OFFICIALLY NAMES THE WORLD'S NEWEST WONDER, WONDER OF THE SEAS. THE CELEBRATION IN PORT CANAVERAL, FLORIDA, HONORS A MOTHER  FROM PENNSYLVANIA AS THE GODMOTHER OF THE NEW SHIP   MIAMI , DEC. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A CELEBRATION FIT FOR THE WORLD'S NEWEST WONDER LIT UP PORT CANAVERAL, FLORIDA, LAST NIGHT WHEN ROYAL CARIBBEAN INTERNATIONAL OFFICIALLY WELCOMED WONDER OF THE SEAS INTO THE FAMILY. WITH THE ULTIMATE COMBINATION OF FIRSTS AND SIGNATURE FAVORITES FOR PARENTS, KIDS AND GRANDPARENTS ALIKE, THE CRUISE LINE'S LATEST SHIP WAS NAMED BY ITS GODMOTHER AND WONDER MOM  MARIE MCCREA  FROM PENNSYLVANI A.
