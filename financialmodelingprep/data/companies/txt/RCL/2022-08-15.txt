ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP ANNOUNCES UPSIZING AND PRICING OF $1,250,000,000 SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES TO REFINANCE NEAR TERM DEBT MATURITIES. MIAMI , AUG. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP (NYSE: RCL) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS PRICED ITS PRIVATE OFFERING OF $1,250,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 11.625% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2027 (THE "NOTES"). THE NOTES WILL MATURE ON AUGUST 15, 2027.
