CELEBRITY CRUISES AND GOOP SAIL INTO FALL WITH WELL-BEING CRUISES. MIAMI, AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CELEBRITY CRUISES AND GOOP TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS FURTHERING ITS PARTNERSHIP BY BRINGING SOME OF GOOP'S FAVORITE PRACTITIONERS ON BOARD FOR A SERIES OF FOUR CARIBBEAN SAILINGS THIS FALL. KICKING OFF IN OCTOBER, GOOP PRACTITIONERS AND EXPERTS WILL LEAD EXCLUSIVE PROGRAMMING, INCLUDING WORKOUTS AND MINDFULNESS SESSIONS WITH A GOAL OF ENRICHING GUESTS' COLLECTIVE WELL-BEING WHILE ABOARD THE NEW LUXURY BRAND'S MOST EXQUISITE SHIPS.
