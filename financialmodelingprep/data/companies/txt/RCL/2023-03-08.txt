ROYAL CARIBBEAN'S BEACH CLUB IN THE BAHAMAS MOVES FORWARD FOR 2025 OPENING. THE ROYAL BEACH CLUB AT PARADISE ISLAND WILL CREATE A UNIQUE INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY FOR BAHAMIANS MIAMI , MARCH 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROYAL CARIBBEAN INTERNATIONAL, THE WORLD'S LARGEST AND MOST POPULAR CRUISE LINE, IS MOVING AHEAD WITH THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE FIRST ROYAL BEACH CLUB AFTER RECEIVING APPROVAL FROM THE GOVERNMENT OF THE BAHAMAS. THE 17-ACRE DESTINATION EXPERIENCE COMING TO THE WESTERN END OF PARADISE ISLAND IN NASSAU, THE BAHAMAS , WILL COMBINE THE ISLAND'S STRIKING BEACHES WITH THE CRUISE LINE'S SIGNATURE EXPERIENCES TO CREATE THE ULTIMATE BEACH DAY – THE ROYAL BEACH CLUB AT PARADISE ISLAND.
