CHIPOTLE LAUNCHES THE ALUMINARIES PROJECT WITH MENTOR AND AVOCADO SUPPLIER JASON MRAZ. NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ALUMINARIES PROJECT, AN ACCELERATOR PROGRAM DESIGNED TO SUPPORT VENTURES FROM ACROSS THE COUNTRY THAT ARE BUILDING SUSTAINABLE, EQUITABLE, AND EFFICIENT FOOD SYSTEMS.
