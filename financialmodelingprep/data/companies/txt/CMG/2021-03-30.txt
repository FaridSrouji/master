CHIPOTLE NAMES MATT CAREY AND MAURICIO GUTIERREZ TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE:CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED TWO NEW ADDITIONS TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, MATT CAREY AND MAURICIO GUTIERREZ, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY.
BURRITOS OR BITCOIN: CHIPOTLE TO GIVE AWAY $200K IN FREE BURRITOS AND BITCOIN TO CELEBRATE NATIONAL BURRITO DAY. NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL GIVE AWAY $100,000 IN FREE BURRITOS AND $100,000 IN BITCOIN TO CELEBRATE NATIONAL BURRITO DAY ON APRIL 1.
