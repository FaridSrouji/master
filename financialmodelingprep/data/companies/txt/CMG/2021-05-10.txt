CHIPOTLE INCREASES WAGES RESULTING IN $15 PER HOUR AVERAGE WAGE AND PROVIDES PATH TO SIX FIGURE COMPENSATION IN ~3 YEARS. NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS INCREASING RESTAURANT WAGES RESULTING IN A $15 AVERAGE HOURLY WAGE BY THE END OF JUNE.
