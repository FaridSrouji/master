CHIPOTLE INTRODUCES ITS FIRST-EVER CREATOR CLASS, GIVES ONE FAN THE CHANCE TO JOIN THE EXCLUSIVE GROUP. NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., SEPT. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE CHIPOTLE CREATOR CLASS, A PROGRAM THAT SUPERCHARGES SOME OF THE BRAND'S MOST INFLUENTIAL CREATORS THROUGH EXCLUSIVE PERKS AND PARTNERSHIPS TO HELP SHAPE THE FUTURE OF CHIPOTLE.
