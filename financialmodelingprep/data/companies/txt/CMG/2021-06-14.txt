CHIPOTLE CELEBRATES AMERICAN ATHLETES WITH "TEAM CHIPOTLE" MENU AND NEW EPISODES OF "UNWRAPPED". NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., JUNE 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS CELEBRATING AMERICAN ATHLETES WITH NEW DIGITAL MENU ITEMS EXCLUSIVELY ON THE CHIPOTLE APP AND CHIPOTLE.COM AND NEW EPISODES OF ITS POPULAR "UNWRAPPED" VIDEO SERIES.
