CHIPOTLE ROLLS OUT GOLD FOIL TO CELEBRATE AMERICAN ATHLETES IN TOKYO. NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., JULY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL BE SERVING GOLD FOIL-WRAPPED BURRITOS FOR A LIMITED TIME AT PARTICIPATING LOCATIONS IN-RESTAURANT AND THROUGH DIGITAL ORDERS STARTING JULY 23.
