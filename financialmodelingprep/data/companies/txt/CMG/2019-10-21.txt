CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL, INC. PROVIDES SALES AND NEW RESTAURANT OPENING GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019; PLANS TO OPEN NEW RESTAURANTS IN 2020. CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL, INC. ANNOUNCED EARNINGS GUIDANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019. FOR THE YEAR, THE COMPANY EXPECTS TO BE AT THE TOP END OF PRIOR HIGH SINGLE DIGIT COMPARABLE RESTAURANT SALES GROWTH GUIDANCE. THE COMPANY ANTICIPATES OF BEING AT OR SLIGHTLY BELOW PRIOR GUIDANCE OF 140 TO 155 NEW RESTAURANT OPENINGSFOR 2020, MANAGEMENT IS ANTICIPATING 150 TO 165 NEW RESTAURANT OPENINGS.
