CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL - NOTEWORTHY NEW ITEM WAS CARNE ASADA, WHICH WAS LAUNCHED IN SEPT AND WILL TRANSITION OUT BY THE END OF Q1- CEO ON CONF CALL.CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL - CARNE ASADA STEAK CONTINUES TO EXCEED OUR EXPECTATIONS- CEO ON CONF CALL.CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL - EXPLORING OPTIONS TO SEE IF WE CAN ADD CARNE ASADA AS PERMANENT MENU ITEM AT SOME POINT IN FUTURE - CEO ON CONF CALL.
CHIPOTLE POSTS Q4 EPS OF $2.55. CHIPOTLE ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2019 RESULTS.Q4 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.86.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.55.Q4 REVENUE $1.4 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.4 BILLION.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $2.75 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY COMPARABLE RESTAURANT SALES INCREASED 13.4%.QTRLY FOOD, BEVERAGE AND PACKAGING COSTS WERE 33.1% OF REVENUE, A DECREASE OF 10 BPS COMPARED TO Q4 OF 2018.RESTAURANT LEVEL OPERATING MARGIN WAS 19.2% IN QUARTER, AN IMPROVEMENT FROM 17.0% IN Q4 OF 2018.FOR 2020 ANTICIPATING MID-SINGLE DIGIT COMPARABLE RESTAURANT SALES GROWTH.CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL- INCLUDED IN LEGAL MATTERS THIS QUARTER WAS $10 MILLION RESERVE RELATED TO U.S. ATTORNEY'S INVESTIGATION THAT BEGAN IN JANUARY 2016.FOR 2020 ANTICIPATING 150 TO 165 NEW RESTAURANT OPENINGS.QTRLY  DIGITAL SALES GREW 78.3%.HAVE BEEN COOPERATING WITH US ATTORNEY'S INVESTIGATION THAT BEGAN IN JAN. 2016. AND ARE IN DISCUSSIONS TO RESOLVE IT.
