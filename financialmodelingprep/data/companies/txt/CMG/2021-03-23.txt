CHIPOTLE TO ACCELERATE EXPANSION INTO CANADA. BRAND TO OPEN EIGHT NEW LOCATIONS INCLUDING THE COUNTRY'S FIRST CHIPOTLANE OVER THE NEXT YEAR NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., MARCH 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL CONTINUE ITS EXPANSION INTO CANADA WITH A NEW LOCATION IN SURREY, BC THAT IS SCHEDULED TO OPEN ON MARCH 30.
