CHIPOTLE TO HIDE UP TO ONE MILLION DOLLARS (OR EVEN MORE) OF FREE BURRITOS IN BASKETBALL TV ADS. NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., JULY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS RAISING THE STAKES ON THE 2021 MEN'S PROFESSIONAL BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP SERIES AND HIDING UP TO ONE MILLION DOLLARS (OR EVEN MORE) OF FREE BURRITOS IN ITS TV ADS.
