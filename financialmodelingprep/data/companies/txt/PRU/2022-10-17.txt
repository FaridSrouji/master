WOOD PARTNERS AND PGIM REAL ESTATE BREAK GROUND ON NEW RESIDENTIAL COMMUNITY IN ONE OF RALEIGH'S FASTEST GROWING AREAS. RALEIGH, N.C. , OCT. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WOOD PARTNERS, A NATIONAL LEADER IN MULTIFAMILY REAL ESTATE DEVELOPMENT, AND PGIM REAL ESTATE, THE REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT AND FINANCING ARM OF PGIM, TODAY ANNOUNCED GROUNDBREAKING OF THE PARTNERSHIP'S RESIDENTIAL COMMUNITY—ALTA TOWN CENTER—LOCATED IN RALEIGH, NORTH CAROLINA.
