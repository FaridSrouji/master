GROWTH INVESTING PIONEER AND JENNISON ASSOCIATES FOUNDER, SPIROS ‘SIG' SEGALAS, HAS PASSED AWAY. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--JENNISON ASSOCIATES ANNOUNCED, WITH GREAT SADNESS, THE DEATH OF SPIROS “SIG” SEGALAS, CO-FOUNDER AND LONG-TIME LARGE CAP GROWTH PORTFOLIO MANAGER. SEGALAS WAS A PIONEER IN GROWTH EQUITY INVESTING AND BUILT ONE OF THE INDUSTRY'S LONGEST GROWTH EQUITY TRACK RECORDS.
