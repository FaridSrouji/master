PRUDENTIAL FINANCIAL ANNOUNCES NEW LEADER OF US INSURANCE AND RETIREMENT BUSINESSES. NEWARK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $PRU--PRUDENTIAL FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: PRU) HAS APPOINTED CAROLINE FEENEY AS CEO OF ITS U.S. INSURANCE & RETIREMENT BUSINESSES. FEENEY ASSUMES THIS NEWLY CREATED ROLE EFFECTIVE TODAY AND WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR DRIVING GROWTH ACROSS THE CONSOLIDATED PORTFOLIO OF U.S. BUSINESSES, WHILE ENSURING ALIGNMENT WITH PGIM, THE COMPANY'S ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS. FEENEY'S EXPANDED REMIT WILL INCLUDE OVERSIGHT OF GROUP INSURANCE, INDIVIDUAL LIFE INSURANCE, PRUDENTIAL ANNUITI
