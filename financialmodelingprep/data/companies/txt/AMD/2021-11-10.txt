IBM CLOUD SELECTS 3RD GEN AMD EPYC™ PROCESSORS FOR NEW BARE METAL OFFERING FOR COMPUTE-INTENSIVE WORKLOADS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., NOV. 10, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AMD (NASDAQ: AMD) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IBM CLOUD HAS CHOSEN 3RD GEN AMD EPYC™ PROCESSORS TO EXPAND ITS BARE METAL SERVICE OFFERINGS DESIGNED TO POWER CUSTOMERS' DEMANDING WORKLOADS AND SOLUTIONS. THE NEW SERVERS, FEATURING 128 CORES, UP TO 4TB OF MEMORY AND 10 NVME DRIVES PER SERVER, GIVE USERS FULL ACCESS TO HIGH-END, DUAL-SOCKET PERFORMANCE WITH AMD EPYC 7763 PROCESSORS; A FIRST FOR IBM CLOUD IN A DUAL-SOCKET PLATFORM.
