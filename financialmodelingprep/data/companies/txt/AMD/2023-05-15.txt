AMD TO PARTICIPATE IN J.P. MORGAN'S 51ST ANNUAL GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY, MEDIA AND COMMUNICATIONS CONFERENCE. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MAY 15, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TODAY, AMD (NASDAQ: AMD) ANNOUNCED THAT JEAN HU, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND TREASURER, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A FIRESIDE CHAT AT THE J.P. MORGAN 51ST ANNUAL GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY, MEDIA AND COMMUNICATIONS CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, MAY 23, 2023 AT 3:15 PM EDT/12:15 PM PDT.
