DISPLAY CONTROLLER MARKET TO TOUCH VALUATION OF $56.13 BILLION BY 2028 | NVIDIA AND AMD ARE ENJOYING KING'S POSITION IN MARKET. GLOBAL DISPLAY CONTROLLER MARKET WAS VALUED AT USD 29.12 BILLION IN 2021, AND THE MARKET SIZE IS ESTIMATED TO REACH USD 56.13 BILLION BY 2028, AT A CAGR OF 9.68% OVER THE FORECAST PERIOD (2022-2028).
