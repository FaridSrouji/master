AMD TO PARTICIPATE IN BANK OF AMERICA GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MAY 24, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TODAY, AMD (NASDAQ: AMD) ANNOUNCED THAT DAN MCNAMARA, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER, SERVER BUSINESS, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A FIRESIDE CHAT AT THE BANK OF AMERICA GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, JUNE 6, 2023 AT 2:20 PM EDT/11:20 AM PDT.
