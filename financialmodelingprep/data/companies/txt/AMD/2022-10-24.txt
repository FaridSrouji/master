AMD TO UNVEIL NEXT GENERATION EPYC PROCESSORS DURING “TOGETHER WE ADVANCE_DATA CENTERS” EVENT. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., OCT. 24, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TODAY, AMD (NASDAQ: AMD) ANNOUNCED “TOGETHER WE ADVANCE_DATA CENTERS,” AN IN-PERSON AND LIVESTREAMED EVENT TO UNVEIL THE NEXT GENERATION OF AMD EPYC™ DATA CENTER PROCESSORS. AMD EXECUTIVES, ALONG WITH OTHER KEY ECOSYSTEM PARTNERS, WILL PRESENT DETAILS ON THE NEXT GENERATION DATA CENTER PROCESSOR AND SOLUTIONS.
