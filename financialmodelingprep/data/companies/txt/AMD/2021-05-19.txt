AMD ANNOUNCES $4 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MAY 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AMD (NASDAQ: AMD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A NEW SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM. UNDER THIS PROGRAM, THE COMPANY INTENDS TO REPURCHASE UP TO $4 BILLION OF OUTSTANDING AMD COMMON STOCK. AMD EXPECTS TO FUND REPURCHASES THROUGH CASH GENERATED FROM OPERATIONS WHICH HAVE BEEN STRENGTHENED BY THE COMPANY'S STRONG OPERATIONAL RESULTS.
