NICK LAZARIDIS APPOINTED AMD SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, SALES, EMEA, INDIA AND APJ. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., JULY 20, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AMD (NASDAQ: AMD) ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF NICK LAZARIDIS TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, AMD SALES, RESPONSIBLE FOR MANAGING SALES ACROSS EMEA, INDIA, AND APJ. LAZARIDIS WILL REPORT TO AMD EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF SALES OFFICER (CSO) DARREN GRASBY.
