AMD REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2023 FINANCIAL RESULTS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MAY 02, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AMD (NASDAQ:AMD) TODAY ANNOUNCED REVENUE FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023 OF $5.4 BILLION, GROSS MARGIN OF 44%, OPERATING LOSS OF $145 MILLION, NET LOSS OF $139 MILLION AND DILUTED LOSS PER SHARE OF $0.09. ON A NON-GAAP( *) BASIS, GROSS MARGIN WAS 50%, OPERATING INCOME WAS $1.1 BILLION, NET INCOME WAS $970 MILLION AND DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE WAS $0.60.
