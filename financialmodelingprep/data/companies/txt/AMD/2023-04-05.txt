SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS AND AMD EXTEND STRATEGIC IP LICENSING AGREEMENT TO BRING AMD RADEON™ GRAPHICS TO FUTURE MOBILE PLATFORMS. SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS CO., LTD., A WORLD LEADER IN ADVANCED SEMICONDUCTOR TECHNOLOGY, AND AMD (NASDAQ: AMD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE SIGNED A MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT EXTENSION TO BRING MULTIPLE GENERATIONS OF HIGH-PERFORMANCE, ULTRA-LOW-POWER AMD RADEON GRAPHICS SOLUTIONS TO AN EXPANDED PORTFOLIO OF SAMSUNG EXYNOS SOCS.
