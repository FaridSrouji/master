IRON MOUNTAIN INC - ON OCTOBER 29 CO COMMENCED A GLOBAL PROGRAM CALLED PROJECT SUMMIT.IRON MOUNTAIN - AS A RESULT OF PROGRAM, EXPECT TO REDUCE NUMBER OF VICE PRESIDENT LEVEL AND ABOVE POSITIONS BY ABOUT 45%.IRON MOUNTAIN - TOTAL PROGRAM IS EXPECTED TO REDUCE TOTAL MANAGERIAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE WORKFORCE BY ABOUT 700 POSITIONS OVER NEXT TWO YEARS.IRON MOUNTAIN INC - EXPECT PROJECT SUMMIT TO IMPROVE ADJUSTED EBITDA BY APPROXIMATELY $80.0 MILLION IN 2020.IRON MOUNTAIN INC - ESTIMATE THAT IMPLEMENTATION OF PROJECT SUMMIT WILL RESULT IN TOTAL RESTRUCTURING CHARGES OF APPROXIMATELY $240.0 MILLION.IRON MOUNTAIN INC - ACTIVITIES ASSOCIATED WITH PROJECT SUMMIT WILL BEGIN IN Q4 OF 2019 AND ARE EXPECTED TO BE SUBSTANTIALLY COMPLETE BY END OF 2021.
