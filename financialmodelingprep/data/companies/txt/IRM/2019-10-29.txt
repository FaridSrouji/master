IRON MOUNTAIN INCORPORATED AND CYARK PRESERVE PHILIPPINES' SAN SEBASTIAN BASILICA. IRON MOUNTAIN INCORPORATED AND CYARK ANNOUNCED A NEW PROJECT TO 3D MAP AND MODEL THE SAN SEBASTIAN BASILICA IN MANILA, PHILIPPINES. THIS PROJECT, THE LATEST THROUGH IRON MOUNTAIN AND CYARK'S ONGOING PARTNERSHIP, CONTINUES THEIR SHARED MISSION TO DIGITALLY CAPTURE, ARCHIVE AND SHARE THE WORLD'S CULTURAL AND HISTORICAL HERITAGE.
