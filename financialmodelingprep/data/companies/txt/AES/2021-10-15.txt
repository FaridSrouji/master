AES ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ARLINGTON, VA., OCT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE AES CORPORATION (NYSE: AES) DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.1505 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 2, 2021.
