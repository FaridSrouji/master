IPL ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE SOLAR PROJECT IN CLINTON COUNTY. INDIANAPOLIS, FEB. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INDIANAPOLIS POWER & LIGHT COMPANY (IPL), A SUBSIDIARY OF THE AES CORPORATION (NYSE: AES), TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE A 195 MEGAWATT SOLAR PROJECT. EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED IN 2023, THE SOLAR PROJECT WILL BE LOCATED IN CLINTON...
