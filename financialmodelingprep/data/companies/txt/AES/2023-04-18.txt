AES ANNOUNCES NEXT DECARBONIZATION MILESTONE. AGREES TO PPA BUYOUT AT 205 MW WARRIOR RUN PLANT IN MARYLAND ARLINGTON, VA. , APRIL 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE AES CORPORATION (NYSE: AES) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO TERMINATE THE POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT (PPA) FOR ITS 205 MW WARRIOR RUN COAL-FIRED POWER PLANT IN MARYLAND.
