AES COMMENCES OPERATION OF FIRST PHASE OF CHEVELON BUTTE WIND FARM. LARGEST WIND PROJECT IN ARIZONA TO GENERATE 238 MW OF CLEAN ENERGY IN PHASE ONE ARLINGTON, VA. , JUNE 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE AES CORPORATION (NYSE: AES) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE START OF COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS FOR PHASE ONE OF THE CHEVELON BUTTE WIND FARM.
