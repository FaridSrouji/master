AES INDIANA ANNOUNCES ITS ACQUISITION OF THE PETERSBURG SOLAR PROJECT ADDING NEW TECHNOLOGIES AND ECONOMIC BENEFITS TO PIKE CO.. INDIANAPOLIS, AUG. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AES INDIANA, A SUBSIDIARY OF THE AES CORPORATION (NYSE: AES), TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO ACQUIRE AND CONSTRUCT A 250 MW SOLAR + 180MWH ENERGY STORAGE FACILITY IN PIKE COUNTY, IND., 125 MILES SOUTHWEST OF DOWNTOWN INDIANAPOLIS.
