FLUENCE FILES REGISTRATION STATEMENT WITH THE SEC FOR PROPOSED INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING. ARLINGTON, VA., SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FLUENCE, THE AES CORPORATION'S JOINT VENTURE WITH SIEMENS, A LEADING PROVIDER OF ENERGY STORAGE PRODUCTS AND SERVICES AND DIGITAL APPLICATIONS FOR RENEWABLES AND STORAGE, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FLUENCE ENERGY, INC. HAS FILED A REGISTRATION STATEMENT ON FORM S-1 WITH THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION ("SEC") RELATING TO A PROPOSED INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING OF ITS CLASS A COMMON STOCK.
