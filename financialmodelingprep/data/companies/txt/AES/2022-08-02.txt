AES NAMED IN THE TOP TEN ON FAST COMPANY'S 2022 RANKING OF BEST WORKPLACES FOR INNOVATORS. AES NAMED THE WINNER IN THE BEST WORKPLACES FOR EARLY CAREER INNOVATORS CATEGORY ARLINGTON, VA. , AUG. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE AES CORPORATION (NYSE: AES) TODAY WAS NAMED TO FAST COMPANY'S 2022 LIST OF BEST WORKPLACES FOR INNOVATORS, RANKED #7 AND RECOGNIZED BOTH FOR ITS COMMITMENT TO CLEAN ENERGY INNOVATION AND FOR DEVELOPING TALENT AND ENCOURAGING NEW IDEAS IN THE PURSUIT OF LEADING-EDGE CLEAN ENERGY TECHNOLOGIES.
