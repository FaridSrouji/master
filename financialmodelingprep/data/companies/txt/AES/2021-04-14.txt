AES ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ARLINGTON, VA., APRIL 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE AES CORPORATION (NYSE: AES) DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.1505 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON MAY 14, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 30, 2021.
