AES ACQUIRES LARGEST PERMITTED SOLAR-PLUS-STORAGE PROJECT IN THE UNITED STATES. STRENGTHENS POSITION AS A LEADER IN PROVIDING RENEWABLE ENERGY TO CORPORATE CUSTOMERS ARLINGTON, VA. , JUNE 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE AES CORPORATION (NYSE: AES) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF THE 2 GW BELLEFIELD PROJECT, WHICH IS CURRENTLY IN LATE-STAGE DEVELOPMENT, AND IS THE LARGEST PERMITTED SOLAR-PLUS-STORAGE PROJECT IN THE UNITED STATES.
