AES ANNOUNCES 5% INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ARLINGTON, VA., DEC. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE AES CORPORATION (NYSE: AES) APPROVED AN INCREASE OF 5% IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND, FROM $0.1505 PER SHARE TO $0.1580 PER SHARE, BEGINNING IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022.
