(NASDAQ: PENN) (“PENN NATIONAL” OR THE “COMPANY”) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THE EXPANSION OF ITS EXCLUSIVE MYHEROES PROGRAM TO RECOGNIZE AND ENGAGE THE COMPANY'S TEAM MEMBERS WHO ARE VETERANS, ACTIVE-DUTY MILITARY OR FIRST RESPONDERS. THE TEAM MEMBER-FOCUSED EXTENSION OF THE PROGRAM STRENGTHENS PENN NATIONAL'S COMMITMENT TO SUPPORTING OUR NATION'S HEROES.
