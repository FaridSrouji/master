PENN NATIONAL GAMING NAMES VIMLA BLACK-GUPTA TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS. WYOMISSING, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENN NATIONAL GAMING, INC. (NASDAQ: PENN) (“PENN NATIONAL” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED VIMLA BLACK-GUPTA TO THE BOARD, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY REGULATORY APPROVALS. MS. BLACK-GUPTA'S APPOINTMENT EXPANDS THE SIZE OF THE BOARD TO NINE MEMBERS, EIGHT OF WHOM SATISFY THE STANDARDS FOR DIRECTOR INDEPENDENCE UNDER THE CURRENT LISTING RULES OF THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. MS.
