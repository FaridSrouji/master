POINTSBET SECURES SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING MARKET ACCESS IN PENNSYLVANIA AND MISSISSIPPI. MELBOURNE, AUSTRALIA, MARCH 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- POINTSBET HOLDINGS LIMITED (ASX: PBH) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY, POINTSBET USA INC. ("POINTSBET"), AND PENN NATIONAL GAMING INC. (NASDAQ: PENN) ("PNG") HAVE AGREED TO EXTEND THE ONLINE GAMING SERVICES FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT DATED 31 JULY 2019 (THE "AGREEMENT") TO PROVIDE POINTSBET WITH ONLINE SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING MARKET ACCESS IN PENNSYLVANIA AND MISSISSIPPI (THE "ADDITIONAL STATES"), SUBJECT TO ENABLING LEGISLATION (IN MISSISSIPPI) AND LICENSURE IN EACH OF THOSE STATES. PENNSYLVANIA CURRENTLY PERMITS ONLINE SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING (THE "TRANSACTION").
