PENN NATIONAL GAMING SET TO OPEN HOLLYWOOD CASINO MORGANTOWN ON DECEMBER 22ND. WYOMISSING & CAERNARVON TOWNSHIP, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENN NATIONAL GAMING, INC. (NASDAQ: PENN)(“PENN NATIONAL” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT EXPECTS TO OPEN HOLLYWOOD CASINO MORGANTOWN TO THE PUBLIC ON WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 22ND, AT NOON, PENDING FINAL REGULATORY APPROVALS. HOLLYWOOD CASINO MORGANTOWN IS CONVENIENTLY LOCATED AT THE INTERSECTIONS OF THREE MAJOR ROADWAYS: THE PENNSYLVANIA TURNPIKE (I-76), I-176, AND THE MORGANTOWN EXPRESSWAY (RT. 10).
