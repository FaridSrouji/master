PENN NATIONAL GAMING CELEBRATES GRAND OPENING OF HOLLYWOOD CASINO MORGANTOWN. WYOMISSING, PA. & CAERNARVON TOWNSHIP, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY MARKS THE GRAND OPENING OF HOLLYWOOD CASINO MORGANTOWN, THE FOURTH GAMING AND ENTERTAINMENT FACILITY IN PENNSYLVANIA BY PENN NATIONAL GAMING, INC. (NASDAQ: PENN)(“PENN NATIONAL” OR THE “COMPANY”). THE $111 MILLION STATE-OF-THE-ART CASINO IS THE COMPANY'S 44TH PROPERTY IN NORTH AMERICA.
