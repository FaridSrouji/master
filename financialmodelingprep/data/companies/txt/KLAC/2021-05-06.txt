KLA FOUNDATION PLEDGES $550,000 TO COVID-19 RELIEF IN INDIA. MILPITAS, CALIF., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KLA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: KLAC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE KLA FOUNDATION IS EXPANDING ITS COVID-19 RELIEF EFFORTS IN INDIA WITH A DONATION OF $550,000 TO HELP COMBAT THE SECOND WAVE OF CORONAVIRUS INFECTIONS CURRENTLY TAXING THE COUNTRY'S HEALTHCARE SYSTEM AMIDST A NATIONAL VACCINE SHORTAGE.
