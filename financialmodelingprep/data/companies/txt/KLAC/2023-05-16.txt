KLA AND IMEC ANNOUNCE MOU TO ADVANCE THE ELECTRIFICATION OF THE AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRY. ANTWERP, BELGIUM , MAY 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY KLA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: KLAC) AND IMEC ANNOUNCED THE INTENTION TO ESTABLISH THE SEMICONDUCTOR TALENT AND AUTOMOTIVE RESEARCH (STAR) INITIATIVE, FOCUSING ON DEVELOPING THE TALENT BASE AND INFRASTRUCTURE NECESSARY TO ACCELERATE ADVANCED SEMICONDUCTOR APPLICATIONS FOR ELECTRIFICATION AND AUTONOMOUS MOBILITY AND MOVE THE AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRY FORWARD. THE INITIATIVE BUILDS ON OVER 25 YEARS OF COLLABORATION BETWEEN IMEC AND KLA.
