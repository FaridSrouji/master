KLA CORPORATION REPORTS FISCAL 2022 FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR RESULTS. FOR THE QUARTER, TOTAL REVENUES WERE $2.49 BILLION, GAAP DILUTED EPS ATTRIBUTABLE TO KLA WAS $5.40 AND NON-GAAP DILUTED EPS ATTRIBUTABLE TO KLA WAS $5.81, EACH FINISHING ABOVE THE MID-POINT OF GUIDANCE RANGES; CASH FLOW FROM OPERATIONS FOR THE QUARTER AND FISCAL YEAR WAS $819.2 MILLION AND $3.31 BILLION AND FREE CASH FLOW WAS $746.1 MILLION AND $3.01 BILLION, RESPECTIVELY; AND CAPITAL RETURNS FOR THE QUARTER AND FISCAL YEAR WERE $3.63 BILLION AND $5.51 BILLION, RESPECTIVELY. MILPITAS, CALIF.
