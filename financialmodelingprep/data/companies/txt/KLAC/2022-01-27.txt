KLA CORPORATION REPORTS FISCAL 2022 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS. MILPITAS, CALIF., JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KLA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: KLAC) TODAY ANNOUNCED OPERATING RESULTS FOR ITS SECOND QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2022, WHICH ENDED ON DEC. 31, 2021, AND REPORTED GAAP NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO KLA OF $717.4 MILLION AND GAAP EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE ATTRIBUTABLE TO KLA OF $4.71 ON REVENUE OF $2.35 BILLION.
