KLA DECLARES REGULAR CASH DIVIDEND. MILPITAS, CALIF. , MAY 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: KLAC) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.30 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 1, 2023, TO KLA SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 15, 2023.
