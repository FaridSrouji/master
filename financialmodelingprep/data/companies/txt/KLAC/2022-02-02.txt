KLA DECLARES REGULAR CASH DIVIDEND. MILPITAS, CALIF. , FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: KLAC) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.05 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MARCH 1, 2022, TO KLA SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEB. 14, 2022.
