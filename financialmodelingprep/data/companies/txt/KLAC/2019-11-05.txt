KLA CORPORATION DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 3, 2019. KLA CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.85 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 3, 2019 TO KLA SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 18, 2019.
