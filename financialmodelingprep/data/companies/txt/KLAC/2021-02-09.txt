KLA DECLARES REGULAR CASH DIVIDEND. MILPITAS, CALIF., FEB. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: KLAC) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.90 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MARCH 2, 2021 TO KLA SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEB. 19, 2021.
