KLA CORPORATION REPORTS FISCAL 2022 THIRD QUARTER RESULTS. TOTAL REVENUES WERE $2.29 BILLION, TOWARDS THE HIGH-END OF THE RANGE OF GUIDANCE; GAAP DILUTED EPS ATTRIBUTABLE TO KLA WAS $4.83 AND NON-GAAP DILUTED EPS ATTRIBUTABLE TO KLA WAS $5.13, EACH ABOVE THE MIDPOINT OF THE RANGES OF GUIDANCE; CASH FLOW FROM OPERATING ACTIVITIES AND FREE CASH FLOW WERE $818.9 MILLION AND $718.6 MILLION, RESPECTIVELY; AND CAPITAL RETURNS WERE $723.7 MILLION WITH $159.0 MILLION IN DIVIDENDS PAID AND $564.7 MILLION IN SHARE REPURCHASES. MILPITAS, CALIF.
