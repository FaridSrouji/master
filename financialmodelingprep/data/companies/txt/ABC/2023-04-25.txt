AMERISOURCEBERGEN LAUNCHES CELL AND GENE THERAPY INTEGRATION HUB. CONSHOHOCKEN, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERISOURCEBERGEN, A GLOBAL HEALTHCARE COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS CELL AND GENE THERAPY (CGT) INTEGRATION HUB, A SYSTEM THAT CAN BE INTEGRATED WITH BIOPHARMA OR PROVIDER-FACING PLATFORMS TO FACILITATE REAL-TIME DATA EXCHANGE AND HELP ORCHESTRATE SERVICES ACROSS THE TREATMENT DEVELOPMENT AND PATIENT JOURNEY. THE CGT INTEGRATION HUB – WHICH IS PLATFORM-AGNOSTIC AND POWERED BY AMERISOURCEBERGEN'S INNOVATIVE CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT (CRM).
