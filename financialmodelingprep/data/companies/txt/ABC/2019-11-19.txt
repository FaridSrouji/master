AMERISOURCEBERGEN SAYS MAY BE UNABLE TO RECOVER AMOUNTS OWED FROM DIPLOMAT PHARMACY. AMERISOURCEBERGEN - AS RESULT OF FOREGOING OF GOING CONCERN ASSESSMENT OF DIPLOMAT PHARMACY'S MANAGEMENT, MAY BE UNABLE TO RECOVER AMOUNTS OWED.AMERISOURCEBERGEN - AS OF NOV 15, TRADE RECEIVABLE BALANCE DUE FROM DIPLOMAT PHARMACY WAS ABOUT $109.6 MILLION, NONE OF WHICH WAS PAST DUE - SEC FILING.
