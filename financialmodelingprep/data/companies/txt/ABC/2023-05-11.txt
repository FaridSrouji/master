AMERISOURCEBERGEN ANNOUNCES COMMON SHARE REPURCHASE FROM WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE. CONSHOHOCKEN, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERISOURCEBERGEN CORPORATION (NYSE: ABC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS AGREED TO REPURCHASE SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK FROM WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE HOLDINGS LLC IN THE AMOUNT OF APPROXIMATELY $50 MILLION IN CONCURRENCE WITH WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE'S ENTRY INTO CERTAIN VARIABLE PRE-PAID FORWARD TRANSACTIONS EXECUTED IN RELIANCE ON RULE 144 UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED.
