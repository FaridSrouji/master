AMERISOURCEBERGEN REPORTS FISCAL 2021 THIRD QUARTER RESULTS. CONSHOHOCKEN, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERISOURCEBERGEN CORPORATION (NYSE: ABC) TODAY REPORTED THAT IN ITS FISCAL YEAR 2021 THIRD QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021, REVENUE INCREASED 17.7 PERCENT YEAR-OVER-YEAR TO $53.4 BILLION. ON THE BASIS OF U.S. GENERALLY ACCEPTED ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES (GAAP), DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) WAS $1.40 FOR THE JUNE QUARTER OF FISCAL 2021, COMPARED TO $1.41 IN THE PRIOR YEAR QUARTER.
