AMERISOURCEBERGEN ANNOUNCES PHARMACY TELEHEALTH PARTNERSHIP WITH STEADYMD. CONSHOHOCKEN, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERISOURCEBERGEN (AB), A GLOBAL HEALTHCARE COMPANY, HAS ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH STEADYMD, A B2B TELEHEALTH INFRASTRUCTURE PROVIDER THAT POWERS HIGH-QUALITY TELEHEALTH PATIENT EXPERIENCES FOR LEADERS AND INNOVATORS IN HEALTHCARE. THE TWO ORGANIZATIONS HAVE KICKED OFF THEIR COLLABORATION WITH THE COCREATION OF A TELEHEALTH SOLUTION FOCUSED ON TEST TO TREAT OPPORTUNITIES. THE SOLUTION IS BEING OFFERED AS A PILOT TO INDEPENDENT COMMUNITY PHARMACIE.
