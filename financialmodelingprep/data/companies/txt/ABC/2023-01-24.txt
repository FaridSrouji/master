AMERISOURCEBERGEN ANNOUNCES INTENT TO CHANGE NAME TO CENCORA. CONSHOHOCKEN, PA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMERISOURCEBERGEN (NYSE: ABC) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INTENT TO CHANGE ITS NAME TO BETTER REFLECT ITS BOLD VISION AND PURPOSE-DRIVEN APPROACH TO CREATING HEALTHIER FUTURES. AMERISOURCEBERGEN INTENDS TO BECOME CENCORA. THE NEW NAME REPRESENTS A UNIFIED PRESENCE THAT WILL CONTINUE TO FUEL THE COMPANY'S ONGOING GROWTH STRATEGY AND ADVANCE ITS IMPACT ACROSS HEALTHCARE.
