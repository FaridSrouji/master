BORGWARNER RECEIVES U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY AWARD FOR HIGH POWER-DENSE INVERTER. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., OCT. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER, A GLOBAL PRODUCT LEADER IN DELIVERING INNOVATIVE AND SUSTAINABLE MOBILITY SOLUTIONS FOR THE VEHICLE MARKET, IS THE RECIPIENT OF A $4.97 MILLION U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (DOE) AWARD FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A SCALABLE ULTRA POWER-DENSE EXTENDED RANGE (SUPER) INVERTER.
