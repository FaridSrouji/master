BORGWARNER ENTERS LICENSING AGREEMENT WITH POLYCHARGE AMERICA, INC. FOR DISRUPTIVE CAPACITOR TECHNOLOGY. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER IS STRENGTHENING ITS INVERTER CAPABILITIES AND OFFERINGS THROUGH AN EXCLUSIVE LICENSING AGREEMENT WITH POLYCHARGE AMERICA, INC., A STARTUP COMPANY FORMED TO DELIVER DISRUPTIVE CAPACITOR PRODUCTS.
