BORGWARNER WINS GOLD AND SILVER BRANDON HALL GROUP EXCELLENCE AWARDS. AUBURN HILLS, MICH. , AUG. 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER, A GLOBAL PRODUCT LEADER IN DELIVERING INNOVATIVE AND SUSTAINABLE MOBILITY SOLUTIONS FOR THE VEHICLE MARKET, WON A COVETED BRANDON HALL GROUP GOLD AWARD IN THE CATEGORY OF EXCELLENCE IN DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION AND A SILVER AWARD IN THE EXCELLENCE IN LEARNING CATEGORY.
