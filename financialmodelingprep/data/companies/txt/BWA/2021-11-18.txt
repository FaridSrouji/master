BORGWARNER AWARDED GRANT TO LAUNCH E-PRODUCT IN CADILLAC, MICHIGAN PLANT. CADILLAC, MICH., NOV. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER THERMAL SYSTEMS HAS BEEN AWARDED A GRANT TO GROW ITS ELECTRIC VEHICLE BUSINESS AND IS SET TO LAUNCH A NEW HIGH-VOLTAGE COOLANT HEATER (HVCH) AT ITS EXISTING FACILITY IN CADILLAC, MICHIGAN.
