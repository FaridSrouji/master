BORGWARNER INVESTS IN CELLINK, EXPANDING ELECTRIFICATION EFFORTS. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., AUG. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER, A GLOBAL PRODUCT LEADER IN DELIVERING INNOVATIVE AND SUSTAINABLE MOBILITY SOLUTIONS FOR THE VEHICLE MARKET, INVESTED IN CELLINK CORPORATION, A COMPANY THAT DEVELOPS LARGE, HIGH-CONDUCTANCE FLEXIBLE CIRCUITS FOR THE AUTOMOTIVE AND ENERGY STORAGE INDUSTRIES.
