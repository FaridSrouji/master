BORGWARNER PRICES €1.0 BILLION SENIOR NOTES OFFERING. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., MAY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER INC. (NYSE: BWA) ("BORGWARNER" OR THE "COMPANY") ANNOUNCED TODAY IT PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF €1.0 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 1.00% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE "SENIOR NOTES").
