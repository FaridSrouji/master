BORGWARNER INVESTS IN ENEXOR BIOENERGY, WIDENS BREADTH OF SUSTAINABILITY BUSINESS. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., AUG. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER IS EXPANDING THE BREADTH OF ITS SUSTAINABILITY BUSINESS ACTIVITIES BY INVESTING IN ENEXOR BIOENERGY, LLC, A COMPANY THAT PROVIDES ON-SITE, RENEWABLE ENERGY AND CARBON CONVERSION SOLUTIONS TO HELP SOLVE THE WORLD'S ORGANIC WASTE AND PLASTIC WASTE PROBLEMS.
