BORGWARNER VICE PRESIDENT INDUCTED INTO WOMEN IN MANUFACTURING HALL OF FAME. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER'S ARVIS WILLIAMS, VICE PRESIDENT OF GLOBAL QUALITY, MANUFACTURING AND PROGRAM MANAGEMENT, HAS BEEN INDUCTED INTO THE 2021 CLASS OF WOMEN IN MANUFACTURING (WIM) HALL OF FAME.
