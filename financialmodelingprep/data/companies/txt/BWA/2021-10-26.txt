BORGWARNER TO SUPPLY ELECTRIC MOTORS FOR NEW ELECTRIC TRUCK. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER IS BOOSTING ITS LEADERSHIP POSITION IN COMMERCIAL VEHICLE ELECTRIFICATION WITH A GERMAN OEM'S SELECTION OF ITS HVH250 (HIGH VOLTAGE HAIRPIN) ELECTRIC MOTORS TO POWER A HEAVY-DUTY TRUCK MODEL.
