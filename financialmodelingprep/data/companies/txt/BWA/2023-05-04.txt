BORGWARNER SECURES EFAN BUSINESS FOR BATTERY-ELECTRIC TRUCK FROM GLOBAL OEM. BORGWARNER TO SUPPLY ENTIRE SYSTEM, INCLUDING FAN, E-MOTOR AND INTEGRATED INVERTER START OF PRODUCTION SLATED FOR Q4 2025 ACQUISITION OF DRIVETEK AG IN DECEMBER 2022 SUPPORTED NEW BUSINESS WIN AUBURN HILLS, MICH. , MAY 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER HAS BEEN SELECTED TO SUPPLY ITS HIGH-VOLTAGE EFAN SYSTEM TO A GLOBAL COMMERCIAL VEHICLE OEM FOR USE IN BOTH THE EUROPEAN AND NORTH AMERICAN MARKETS.
