BORGWARNER JOINS CLEAN ENERGY BUYERS ALLIANCE (CEBA). AUBURN HILLS, MICH., NOV. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER, A GLOBAL PRODUCT LEADER IN DELIVERING INNOVATIVE AND SUSTAINABLE MOBILITY SOLUTIONS FOR THE VEHICLE MARKET, HAS JOINED THE CLEAN ENERGY BUYERS ALLIANCE (CEBA) AS A "LARGE ENERGY BUYER" MEMBER TO SUPPORT ITS COMMITMENT TO ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND VISION OF A CLEAN, ENERGY-EFFICIENT WORLD.
