BORGWARNER DIXON AWARDED CEO'S SAFETY EXCELLENCE AWARD, RECORDED OVER ONE MILLION ACCIDENT-FREE WORKING HOURS. ACCIDENT-FREE WORKING HOURS EXCEEDS ONE MILLION AT BORGWARNER DIXON LOCATED IN ILLINOIS, US BORGWARNER CEO FRÉDÉRIC LISSALDE AND PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER EMISSIONS, THERMAL AND TURBO SYSTEMS JOSEPH FADOOL PRESENTED AWARD AT SPECIAL CEREMONY LOCAL ORGANIZATIONS AND SUMMER PROGRAM CHOSEN FOR AWARD FUND AUBURN HILLS, MICH. , MAY 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER'S MANUFACTURING SITE IN DIXON, ILLINOIS, US HAS BEEN AWARDED THE 2022 CEO'S SAFETY EXCELLENCE AWARD AFTER RECORDING OVER ONE MILLION ACCIDENT-FREE WORKING HOURS.
