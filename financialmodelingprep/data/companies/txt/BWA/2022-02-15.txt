(NYSE: BWA), SANTROLL ELECTRIC AUTO AND SANTROLL AUTOMOTIVE COMPONENTS TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE SIGNED AN EQUITY TRANSFER AGREEMENT (ETA) UNDER WHICH BORGWARNER WILL ACQUIRE SANTROLL AUTOMOTIVE COMPONENTS, A CARVE-OUT OF SANTROLL'S EMOTOR BUSINESS, FOR UP TO ¥1.4 BILLION, COMPRISED OF A CLOSING PAYMENT OF ¥1.1 BILLION AND AN EARNOUT OF UP TO ¥0.3 BILLION.
