BORGWARNER JOINS DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY'S BETTER CLIMATE CHALLENGE AIMED AT SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCING GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., FEB. 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER INC., (BORGWARNER) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS JOINED THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY'S (DOE) BETTER CLIMATE CHALLENGE, A NATIONAL LEADERSHIP INITIATIVE THAT CALLS ON CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICERS, UNIVERSITY PRESIDENTS AND STATE AND LOCAL LEADERS TO REDUCE GREENHOUSE GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS, CREATE JOBS, AND PROMOTE HEALTHY, SAFE AND THRIVING COMMUNITIES.
