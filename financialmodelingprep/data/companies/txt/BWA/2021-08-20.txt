BORGWARNER SHARES CONGRATULATORY MESSAGE ON AKASOL'S GRAND OPENING OF THEIR GIGAFACTORY. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., AUG. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER PRESIDENT AND CEO, FRÉDÉRIC LISSALDE, AND OTHER MEMBERS OF THE BORGWARNER LEADERSHIP TEAM ATTENDED THE GRAND OPENING OF AKASOL'S GIGAFACTORY 1, EUROPE'S LARGEST FACTORY FOR COMMERCIAL-VEHICLE BATTERY SYSTEMS, LOCATED IN DARMSTADT, GERMANY.
