BORGWARNER CELEBRATES SENECA PLANT GRAND REOPENING, 25TH YEAR OF OPERATION. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., SEPT. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER CELEBRATED THE GRAND REOPENING OF ITS SENECA, SOUTH CAROLINA, PRODUCTION FACILITY TODAY, FOLLOWING A 16-MONTH REBUILD AFTER THE PLANT WAS RAVAGED BY A TORNADO ON APRIL 13, 2020.
