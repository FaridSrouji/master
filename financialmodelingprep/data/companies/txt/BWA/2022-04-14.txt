BORGWARNER RECEIVES U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY AWARD FOR DC FAST CHARGER. $4.09 MILLION AWARDED FROM THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY GOAL TO DEVELOP A MORE COST-EFFECTIVE DIRECT CURRENT FAST CHARGER BORGWARNER WILL TEAM UP WITH AMERICAN-MADE SUPPLIER PARTNERS FOR 36-MONTH PROJECT AUBURN HILLS, MICH. , APRIL 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER, A GLOBAL PRODUCT LEADER IN DELIVERING INNOVATIVE AND SUSTAINABLE MOBILITY SOLUTIONS FOR THE VEHICLE MARKET, HAS RECEIVED A $4.09 MILLION U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY (DOE) AWARD TO DESIGN AND DEVELOP A COST-EFFECTIVE, ADVANCED DIRECT CURRENT FAST CHARGER (DCFC).
