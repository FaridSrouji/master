BORGWARNER DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ON JULY 28, 2021, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BORGWARNER INC. (NYSE: BWA) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.17 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.
