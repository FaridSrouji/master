U.S. BANK INCREASES PRIME LENDING RATE TO 4.00 PERCENT. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--U.S. BANCORP (NYSE: USB) ANNOUNCED IT HAS INCREASED ITS PRIME LENDING RATE TO 4.00 PERCENT FROM 3.50 PERCENT, EFFECTIVE TOMORROW, MAY 5, 2022, AT ALL U.S. BANK LOCATIONS. ABOUT U.S. BANCORP: U.S. BANCORP, WITH APPROXIMATELY 70,000 EMPLOYEES AND $587 BILLION IN ASSETS AS OF MARCH 31, 2022, IS THE PARENT COMPANY OF U.S. BANK NATIONAL ASSOCIATION.
