U.S. BANCORP TO SPEAK AT THE RBC CAPITAL MARKETS GLOBAL FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS CONFERENCE. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--U.S. BANCORP (NYSE: USB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT VICE CHAIR AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER TERRY DOLAN AND VICE CHAIR OF PAYMENT SERVICES SHAILESH KOTWAL WILL PRESENT AT THE RBC CAPITAL MARKETS GLOBAL FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS CONFERENCE.
