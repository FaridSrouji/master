W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION LAUNCHES LIFSON RE. GREENWICH, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION (NYSE: WRB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FORMATION OF LIFSON RE LTD., A SPECIAL PURPOSE INSURER REGISTERED IN BERMUDA AND LICENSED BY THE BERMUDA MONETARY AUTHORITY. LIFSON RE HAS BEEN CAPITALIZED WITH MORE THAN $250 MILLION OF EQUITY FROM A SMALL GROUP OF SOPHISTICATED GLOBAL INVESTORS WITH LONG-TERM INVESTMENT HORIZONS, INCLUDING A MINORITY PARTICIPATION BY W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION. THE NEW COMPANY WILL PARTICIPATE ALONGSIDE TRADITIONAL REI
