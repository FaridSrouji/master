W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. GREENWICH, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION (NYSE: WRB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF 10 CENTS PER SHARE TO BE PAID ON OCTOBER 3, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 26, 2022.
