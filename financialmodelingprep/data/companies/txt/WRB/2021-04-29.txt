W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION NAMES CLARE HIMMER PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY RE UK LIMITED. GREENWICH, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION (NYSE: WRB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF CLARE HIMMER AS PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY RE UK LIMITED EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 1, 2021. RICHARD FOTHERGILL WILL CONTINUE AS BERKLEY RE UK'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER WITH THE EXPECTATION THAT, BY THE END OF 2021, MS. HIMMER WILL BE APPOINTED CEO AND MR. FOTHERGILL WILL BECOME CHAIRMAN.
