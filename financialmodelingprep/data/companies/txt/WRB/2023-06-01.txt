BERKLEY CORPORATION WILL RECOGNIZE AN ESTIMATED PRE-TAX NET REALIZED GAIN ON INVESTMENT OF $86 MILLION ON THE SALE IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2023. FOUNDED IN 1967, W. R.
