W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION NAMES DANIEL SMYRL PRESIDENT OF ADMIRAL INSURANCE GROUP. GREENWICH, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION (NYSE: WRB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DANIEL SMYRL AS PRESIDENT OF ADMIRAL INSURANCE GROUP (ADMIRAL). HE SUCCEEDS CURTIS E. FLETCHER, WHO HAS BEEN NAMED CHAIRMAN OF ADMIRAL. THE APPOINTMENTS ARE EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MR. SMYRL JOINED ADMIRAL IN 2002 AND MOST RECENTLY SERVED AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF UNDERWRITING. HE HAS NEARLY 40 YEARS OF COMMERCIAL PROPERTY AND CASUALTY INSURANCE EXPERIENCE IN UNDERWRITING AND MANAGEME
