W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION NAMES MICHAEL MARCUS PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY INDUSTRIAL COMP. GREENWICH, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION (NYSE: WRB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF MICHAEL MARCUS AS PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY INDUSTRIAL COMP. HE SUCCEEDS CHANDLER F. COX, JR., WHO HAS BEEN NAMED CHAIRMAN OF THE BUSINESS. THE APPOINTMENTS ARE EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MR. MARCUS JOINED BERKLEY IN 2008 AS A REGIONAL DIRECTOR AT KEY RISK AND MOST RECENTLY SERVED AS KEY RISK'S SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT – SALES, DISTRIBUTION AND BUSINESS ANALYTICS.
