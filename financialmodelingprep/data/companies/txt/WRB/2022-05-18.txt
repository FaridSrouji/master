W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION APPOINTS LUIS RIVERA PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY GLOBAL PRODUCT RECALL. GREENWICH, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION (NYSE: WRB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF LUIS RIVERA AS PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY GLOBAL PRODUCT RECALL. MR. RIVERA SUCCEEDS LOUIS LUBRANO, WHO HAS BEEN NAMED CHAIRMAN OF THE BUSINESS. THE APPOINTMENTS ARE EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MR. RIVERA IS A VETERAN EXECUTIVE WITH MORE THAN 25 YEARS OF COMMERCIAL PROPERTY AND CASUALTY INSURANCE EXPERIENCE IN UNDERWRITING, CLAIMS AND MANAGEMENT.
