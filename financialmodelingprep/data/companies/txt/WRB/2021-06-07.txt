W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION NAMES MICHELLE D. MIDDLETON PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY MID-ATLANTIC GROUP. GREENWICH, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION (NYSE: WRB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF MICHELLE D. MIDDLETON AS PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY MID-ATLANTIC GROUP (BMAG). SHE SUCCEEDS JOHN F. KEARNS, WHO HAS BEEN NAMED CHAIRMAN OF THE OPERATING UNIT AND WILL REMAIN IN AN ADVISORY ROLE FOR THE GROUP OVERALL. THE APPOINTMENTS ARE EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MS.
