W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION NAMES BRADLEY T. LONDON PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY AGRIBUSINESS. GREENWICH, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION (NYSE: WRB) TODAY APPOINTED BRADLEY T. LONDON AS PRESIDENT OF BERKLEY AGRIBUSINESS. HE SUCCEEDS MICHAEL EKISS WHO WILL CONTINUE TO ENGAGE ON SPECIAL PROJECTS AND INITIATIVES THROUGHOUT W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION. MR. LONDON HAS NEARLY 30 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE IN THE INSURANCE INDUSTRY WITH A PARTICULAR FOCUS ON UNDERWRITING FOR AGRICULTURAL BUSINESSES.
