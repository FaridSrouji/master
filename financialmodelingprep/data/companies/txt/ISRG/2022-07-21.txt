INTUITIVE ANNOUNCES SECOND QUARTER EARNINGS. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., JULY 21, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- INTUITIVE (THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: ISRG), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER IN MINIMALLY INVASIVE CARE AND THE PIONEER OF ROBOTIC-ASSISTED SURGERY, TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2022. ALL PRIOR YEAR PER-SHARE INFORMATION HAS BEEN RETROACTIVELY ADJUSTED TO REFLECT A THREE-FOR-ONE STOCK SPLIT THAT WAS EFFECTIVE AS OF OCTOBER 4, 2021.
