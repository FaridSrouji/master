INTUITIVE APPOINTS NEW GENERAL MANAGER IN SOUTH KOREA. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., AUG. 31, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- INTUITIVE (NASDAQ: ISRG), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER IN MINIMALLY INVASIVE CARE AND THE PIONEER OF ROBOTIC-ASSISTED SURGERY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF YONG-BUM CHOI AS GENERAL MANAGER FOR SOUTH KOREA, WHERE HE WILL DIRECT BUSINESS STRATEGY AND OPERATIONS FOR THE COMPANY.
