INTUITIVE BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVES A THREE-FOR-ONE STOCK SPLIT. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., AUG. 05, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- INTUITIVE (NASDAQ: ISRG), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER IN MINIMALLY INVASIVE CARE AND THE PIONEER OF ROBOTIC-ASSISTED SURGERY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A THREE-FOR-ONE SPLIT OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK TO BE EFFECTED THROUGH AN AMENDMENT TO THE COMPANY'S CERTIFICATE OF INCORPORATION.
