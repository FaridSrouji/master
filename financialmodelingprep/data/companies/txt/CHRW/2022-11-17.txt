C.H. ROBINSON INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. EDEN PRAIRIE, MINN.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--C.H. ROBINSON WORLDWIDE, INC. (“C.H. ROBINSON”) (NASDAQ: CHRW) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED AN INCREASE TO ITS REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO 61 CENTS ($0.61) PER SHARE FROM 55 CENTS ($0.55) PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 3, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON DECEMBER 2, 2022.
