NEW TECHNOLOGY AND DATA FROM C.H. ROBINSON ARE HELPING COMPANIES AROUND THE WORLD CUT CARBON EMISSIONS. EDEN PRAIRIE, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--C.H. ROBINSON IS PAVING THE WAY FOR MORE SUSTAINABLE SUPPLY CHAINS WITH TECHNOLOGY AND DATA ANY COMPANY CAN USE TO REDUCE ITS CARBON FOOTPRINT.
