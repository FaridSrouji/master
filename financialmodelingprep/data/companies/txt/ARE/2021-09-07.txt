ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.12 PER COMMON SHARE FOR 3Q21, AN AGGREGATE OF $4.42 FOR THE 12 MONTHS ENDING 3Q21, UP 24 CENTS, OR 6 PERCENT, OVER THE 12 MONTHS ENDED 3Q20. PASADENA, CALIF., SEPT. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. (NYSE: ARE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.12 PER COMMON SHARE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021.
