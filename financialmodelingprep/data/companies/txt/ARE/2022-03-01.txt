ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.15 PER COMMON SHARE FOR 1Q22, FOR AN AGGREGATE OF $4.54 PER COMMON SHARE FOR THE 12 MONTHS ENDING MARCH 31, 2022, UP 24 CENTS, OR 6 PERCENT, OVER THE 12 MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2021. PASADENA, CALIF., MARCH 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. (NYSE: ARE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.15 PER COMMON SHARE FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2022.
