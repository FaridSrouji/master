ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.18 PER COMMON SHARE FOR 2Q22, AN INCREASE OF 3 CENTS OVER 1Q22, AND AN AGGREGATE OF $4.60 PER COMMON SHARE FOR THE 12 MONTHS ENDING JUNE 30, 2022, UP 24 CENTS, OR 6 PERCENT, OVER THE 12 MONTHS ENDED JUNE 30, 2021. PASADENA, CALIF. , MAY 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. (NYSE: ARE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.18 PER COMMON SHARE FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022.
