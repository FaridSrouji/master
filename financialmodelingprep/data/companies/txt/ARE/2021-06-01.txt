ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.12 PER COMMON SHARE FOR 2Q21, AN INCREASE OF 3 CENTS OVER 1Q21, AND AN AGGREGATE OF $4.36 FOR THE 12 MONTHS ENDING 2Q21, UP 24 CENTS, OR 6 PERCENT, OVER THE 12 MONTHS ENDED 2Q20. PASADENA, CALIF., JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. (NYSE: ARE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.12 PER COMMON SHARE FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021.
