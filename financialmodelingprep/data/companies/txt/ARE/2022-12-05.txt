ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.21 PER COMMON SHARE FOR 4Q22, AN INCREASE OF 3 CENTS OVER 3Q22, AND AN AGGREGATE OF $4.72 PER COMMON SHARE FOR 2022, UP 24 CENTS, OR 5 PERCENT, OVER 2021. PASADENA, CALIF. , DEC. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. (NYSE: ARE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.21 PER COMMON SHARE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2022.
