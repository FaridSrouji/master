ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. ANNOUNCES CLOSING OF PUBLIC OFFERING OF 7,000,000 SHARES OF COMMON STOCK. PASADENA, CALIF., JAN. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. ("ALEXANDRIA" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: ARE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CLOSING OF ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 7,000,000 SHARES OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK AT A PUBLIC OFFERING PRICE OF $210.00 PER SHARE.
