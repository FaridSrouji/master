MOODY'S COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF CORTERA. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOODY'S CORPORATION (NYSE:MCO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF CORTERA, WHICH WAS ANNOUNCED ON FEBRUARY 10, 2021. CORTERA IS A LEADING PROVIDER OF NORTH AMERICAN CREDIT DATA AND WORKFLOW SOLUTIONS. THE ACQUISITION ENHANCES MOODY'S INTEGRATED RISK ASSESSMENT CAPABILITIES AND SIGNIFICANTLY EXTENDS ITS COVERAGE IN THE SMALL AND MEDIUM ENTERPRISE SEGMENT.
