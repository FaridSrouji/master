DATES SET FOR MOODY'S EARNINGS RELEASE AND INVESTOR TELECONFERENCE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOODY'S CORPORATION (NYSE: MCO) WILL RELEASE ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS BEFORE THE START OF NYSE TRADING ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 28, 2021. A COPY OF THE RELEASE WILL BE POSTED ON MOODY'S INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE, IR.MOODYS.COM. MOODY'S CORPORATION INVITES YOU TO PARTICIPATE IN A TELECONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 28, 2021 AT 11:30 A.M. EASTERN TIME TO DISCUSS ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. ROBERT FAUBER, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AND MARK KAYE, CHIEF F
