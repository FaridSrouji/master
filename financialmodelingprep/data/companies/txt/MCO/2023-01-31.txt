MOODY'S NAMED TO BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX FOR FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MOODY'S CORPORATION (NYSE:MCO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI) FOR THE FOURTH YEAR IN A ROW. MOODY'S WAS RECOGNIZED FOR CHAMPIONING GENDER EQUALITY IN ITS WORKPLACE AND IN THE GLOBAL BUSINESS COMMUNITY.
