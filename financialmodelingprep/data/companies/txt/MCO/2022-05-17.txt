RMS LAUNCHES NEW CLOUD-BASED APPLICATION UNDERWRITEIQ. NEWARK, CA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--RMS®, A MOODY'S ANALYTICS COMPANY AND WORLD-LEADING RISK MODELING AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, ANNOUNCES IT WILL BE LAUNCHING A NEW APPLICATION, UNDERWRITEIQ™, HOSTED ON THE CLOUD-BASED RMS INTELLIGENT RISK PLATFORM™. UNDERWRITEIQ ENABLES UNDERWRITERS TO HAVE GREATER CONTROL OF RISKS BY BRINGING HAZARD, EXPOSURE, AND LOSS DATA TOGETHER IN THE UNDERWRITING PROCESS AT THE POINT OF DECISION – ALL BASED ON RMS MODEL SCIENCE AND DATA.
