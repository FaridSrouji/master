NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOODY'S CORPORATION (NYSE:MCO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MOODY'S INVESTORS SERVICE (MIS) INTENDS TO WITHDRAW ITS CREDIT RATINGS ON RUSSIAN ENTITIES.
