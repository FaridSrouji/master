MOODY'S ANALYTICS WINS ANALYTICS INNOVATION AWARD FOR CRE SOLUTION. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOODY'S ANALYTICS HAS WON THE ANALYTICS INNOVATION CATEGORY IN THE 2021 FINTECH BREAKTHROUGH AWARDS. WE EARNED THIS AWARD FOR INNOVATION IN OUR COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE (CRE) OFFERING. SPECIFICALLY RECOGNIZED WAS OUR CUSTOM MARKETS MODEL, WHICH GIVES CUSTOMERS THE ABILITY TO CREATE UNIQUE, CUSTOMIZED SUBMARKETS OF COMMERCIAL AND MULTIFAMILY PROPERTIES. “WINNING A FINTECH BREAKTHROUGH AWARD FOR OUR INNOVATION IN THE CRE SPACE IS REALLY EXCITING,” SAID KEITH BERRY, GENERA
