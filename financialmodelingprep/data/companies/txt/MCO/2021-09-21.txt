MOODY'S ANNOUNCES PARTICIPATION IN NEW GFANZ ALLIANCE: COMMITS TO ALIGN PRODUCTS AND SERVICES TO ACHIEVE NET-ZERO GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS BY 2050. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOODY'S CORPORATION (NYSE: MCO) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS PARTICIPATION IN LAUNCHING THE NET ZERO FINANCIAL SERVICES PROVIDER ALLIANCE AS PART OF THE GLASGOW FINANCIAL ALLIANCE FOR NET ZERO (GFANZ). AS A FOUNDING MEMBER, MOODY'S COMMITS TO ALIGN ALL OF ITS RELEVANT PRODUCTS AND SERVICES TO ACHIEVE NET-ZERO GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS BY 2050, IN ADDITION TO REDUCING ITS OWN OPERATIONAL EMISSIONS. “CLIMATE CHANGE IS THE WORLD'S GREATEST RISK MULTIPLIER AND A PROFOUND CHALLENGE
