MOODY'S NAMED TO BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX FOR THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOODY'S CORPORATION (NYSE:MCO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI) FOR THE THIRD YEAR IN A ROW.
