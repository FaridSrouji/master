MOODY'S SEEKING NONPROFIT PARTNERS ALIGNED WITH COMMUNITY IMPACT STRATEGY. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOODY'S CORPORATION (NYSE:MCO) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS CORPORATE SOCIAL INVESTING ARM, MOODY'S FOUNDATION, IS ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS FOR GLOBAL NONPROFIT PARTNERS ALIGNED WITH ITS TWO STRATEGIC FOCUS AREAS: EMPOWERING SMALL BUSINESSES AND SUPPORTING ECOSYSTEM RESTORATION IN EMERGING MARKETS. “AT MOODY'S, WE BELIEVE THAT PROMOTING INCLUSION AND RESILIENCE FUELS A MORE PROSPEROUS FUTURE,” SAID JENNIFER STULA RIVERA, GLOBAL HEAD OF COMMUNITY IMPACT AT MOODY'S.
