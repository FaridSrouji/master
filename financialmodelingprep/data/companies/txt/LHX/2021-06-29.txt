CHRISTOPHER E. KUBASIK SUCCEEDS WILLIAM M.. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHRISTOPHER E. KUBASIK TODAY SUCCEEDED WILLIAM M. BROWN AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LHX) – BECOMING THE SECOND CEO IN ITS HISTORY AS A COMBINED COMPANY. THE PLANNED TRANSITION WAS FIRST ANNOUNCED IN OCTOBER 2018 AS PART OF L3HARRIS' MERGER OF EQUALS AND HAS BEEN UNANIMOUSLY RE-AFFIRMED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
