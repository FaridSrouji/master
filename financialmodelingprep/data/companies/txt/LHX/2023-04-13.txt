L3HARRIS TO BUILD GEOSTATIONARY WEATHER SATELLITE SENSORS FOR JAPAN. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LHX) RECEIVED A CONTRACT TO PROVIDE NEXT-GENERATION GEOSTATIONARY IMAGING AND SOUNDING INSTRUMENTS FOR THE JAPAN METEOROLOGICAL AGENCY (JMA) THAT WILL MONITOR AND PREDICT SEVERE WEATHER EVENTS MORE ACCURATELY. THE AWARD CONTINUES L3HARRIS' LEGACY OF PRODUCING CRITICAL SPACE-BASED WEATHER INSTRUMENTS FOR JAPAN.
