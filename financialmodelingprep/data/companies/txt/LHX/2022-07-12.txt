L3HARRIS INTEGRATED AND TESTED CRITICAL INSTRUMENTS ON NASA'S JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE; FIRST IMAGES UNVEILED. ROCHESTER, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NASA, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH ESA (EUROPEAN SPACE AGENCY) AND CSA (CANADIAN SPACE AGENCY), RELEASED THE JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE'S FIRST FULL-COLOR IMAGES AND SPECTROSCOPIC DATA TUESDAY, JULY 12. L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) ENGINEERS AND TECHNICIANS INTEGRATED THE COMPLEX SYSTEM OF MIRRORS AND RIGOROUSLY TESTED THE TELESCOPE'S HARDWARE TO SIMULATE THE HARSH CONDITIONS OF SPACE.
