L3HARRIS AND SHIELD CAPITAL FORM STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO ACCELERATE DEFENSE, COMMERCIAL TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) AND VENTURE CAPITAL FIRM SHIELD CAPITAL FORMED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO FOSTER EMERGING DEFENSE AND COMMERCIAL TECHNOLOGIES THAT ADDRESS CUSTOMERS' GROWING REQUIREMENTS FOR INNOVATIVE, AGILE SOLUTIONS THAT CAN BE RAPIDLY FIELDED. THE AGREEMENT PROVIDES L3HARRIS ACCESS TO DISRUPTIVE INNOVATORS FOR TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER, TEAMING ARRANGEMENTS, DIRECT INVESTMENTS OR POTENTIAL ACQUISITIONS AND PARTNERED CONTRACTS.
