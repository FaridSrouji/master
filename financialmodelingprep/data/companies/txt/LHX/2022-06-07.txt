US NAVY AWARDS L3HARRIS $205 MILLION CONTRACT FOR NEW PASSIVE EO/IR CAPABILITY TO PROTECT FLEET. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--A TEAM LED BY L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) HAS BEEN SELECTED TO PROVIDE THE SHIPBOARD PANORAMIC ELECTRO-OPTIC/INFRARED (SPEIR) SYSTEM TO THE U.S. NAVY THAT WILL PROVIDE IMPROVED FLEET PROTECTION. THE INITIAL $205 MILLION CONTRACT HAS A POTENTIAL VALUE OF $593 MILLION IF ALL OPTIONS ARE EXERCISED THROUGH MARCH 2031.
