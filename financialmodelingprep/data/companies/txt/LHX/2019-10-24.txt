L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE DECEMBER 6, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES, INC. HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 75 CENTS PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE DECEMBER 6, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 22, 2019.
