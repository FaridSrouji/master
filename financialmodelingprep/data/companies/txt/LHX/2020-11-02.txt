L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES AWARDED CONTRACT FOR THREE CANADIAN SPECIAL MISSION AIRCRAFT. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) HAS BEEN AWARDED A FIRM-FIXED PRICE CONTRACT TO MISSIONIZE THREE NEW KING AIR 350ER AIRCRAFT FOR THE CANADIAN MANNED AIRBORNE INTELLIGENCE, SURVEILLANCE AND RECONNAISSANCE (CMAISR) PROJECT.
