L3HARRIS VIPER SHIELD ELECTRONIC WARFARE SYSTEM AWARDED LONG-LEAD MATERIAL CONTRACT TO ACCELERATE GLOBAL PRODUCTION. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LHX) TODAY ANNOUNCED RECEIVING AN UNDEFINITIZED CONTRACT FROM LOCKHEED MARTIN TO PROCURE LONG-LEAD MATERIAL FOR THE VIPER SHIELD ELECTRONIC WARFARE SYSTEM TO ACCELERATE GLOBAL PRODUCT DELIVERY.
