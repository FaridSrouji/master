L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.02 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 1, 2021.
