L3HARRIS COMPLETES SALE OF VISUAL INFORMATION SOLUTIONS BUSINESS. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) HAS COMPLETED THE SALE OF ITS VISUAL INFORMATION SOLUTIONS (VIS) BUSINESS TO NV5 GLOBAL INC. (NASDAQ: NVEE). THE VIS BUSINESS IS A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF COMMERCIAL GEOSPATIAL SOFTWARE, TECHNOLOGY AND SERVICES USED TO EXTRACT AND ANALYZE RELIABLE, ACCURATE AND ACTIONABLE INFORMATION FROM GEOSPATIAL TO TERRESTRIAL IMAGERY.
