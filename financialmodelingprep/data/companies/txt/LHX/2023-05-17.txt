L3HARRIS RECEIVES US MARINE CORPS ORDERS FOR MULTI-CHANNEL RADIOS. ROCHESTER, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) ANNOUNCED ORDERS TOTALING $160 MILLION FROM THE MARINE CORPS FOR MULTI-CHANNEL HANDHELD AND VEHICULAR RADIO SYSTEMS, BRINGING TOTAL PROGRAM ORDERS TO $336 MILLION. THE TWO NEW ORDERS FROM THE MARINE CORPS ARE UNDER A 10-YEAR, COMPETITIVELY AWARDED $750 MILLION INDEFINITE DELIVERY, INDEFINITE QUANTITY CONTRACT FOR L3HARRIS FALCON IV® MANPACK AND HANDHELD RADIOS. THE TECHNOLOGY IN THESE RADIOS ENABLES GREATER INTEROPERABILITY AMON.
