L3HARRIS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.12 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE SEPTEMBER 16, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 2, 2022.
