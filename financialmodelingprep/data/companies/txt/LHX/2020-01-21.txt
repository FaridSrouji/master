EXACTEARTH REACHES AGREEMENT ON UPDATED TERMS TO SATELLITE AIS BUSINESS AGREEMENT WITH L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES. EXACTEARTH ANNOUNCES UPDATED TERMS TO SATELLITE AIS BUSINESS AGREEMENT WITH L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES.EXACTEARTH - REACHED AGREEMENT ON UPDATED TERMS TO ORIGINAL SATELLITE-AIS BUSINESS AGREEMENT WITH L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES ENTERED IN 2015.EXACTEARTH - UNDER AMENDED & RESTATED AGREEMENT, CO WILL PAY 30% SHARE OF SAIS DATA REVENUE FOR PORTION OF CO'S ANNUAL SAIS DATA REVENUE IN EXCESS OF $16 MILLION.EXACTEARTH - WILL RECEIVE NO SHARE OF L3HARRIS SAIS DATA REVENUES.
