L3HARRIS AWARDED $117 MILLION SPACE OBJECT-TRACKING MODERNIZATION CONTRACT. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) HAS BEEN AWARDED $117 MILLION FOR OPTION-YEAR THREE OF A U.S. SPACE FORCE AND U.S. SPACE COMMAND CONTRACT TO CONTINUE MAINTAINING AND MODERNIZING INFRASTRUCTURE TO TRACK OBJECTS IN SPACE. THE MAINTENANCE OF SPACE SITUATIONAL AWARENESS INTEGRATED CAPABILITIES (MOSSAIC) PROGRAM HAS AN ESTIMATED CONTRACT VALUE OF $1.2 BILLION OVER 10 YEARS.
