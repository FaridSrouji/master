SIDUS SPACE AND L3HARRIS TEAM FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE MENTOR-PROTÉGÉ PROGRAM. CAPE CANAVERAL, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SIDUS SPACE, INC. (NASDAQ:SIDU), A SPACE-AS-A-SERVICE COMPANY FOCUSED ON COMMERCIAL SATELLITE DESIGN, MANUFACTURE, LAUNCH, AND DATA COLLECTION, IS TEAMING WITH L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE MENTOR-PROTÉGÉ PROGRAM (MPP). THE DOD PROGRAM HELPS BUSINESSES EXPAND THEIR FOOTPRINT IN THE DEFENSE INDUSTRIAL BASE BY SUPPORTING THEIR PARTNERING EFFORTS WITH LARGER COMPANIES, SUCH AS L3HARRIS.
