AIG TO SELL VALIDUS RE TO RENAISSANCERE. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN INTERNATIONAL GROUP (NYSE: AIG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL VALIDUS RE, INCLUDING ALPHACAT AND THE TALBOT TREATY REINSURANCE BUSINESS, TO RENAISSANCERE HOLDINGS LTD. (NYSE: RNR) (“RENAISSANCERE”) FOR $2.985 BILLION, CONSISTING OF $2.735 BILLION IN CASH AND $250 MILLION IN RENAISSANCERE COMMON SHARES. IN ADDITION, AIG WILL RECEIVE ALL CAPITAL IN EXCESS OF $2.1 BILLION OF SHAREHOLDERS' EQUITY OF VALIDUS RE AND ACHIEVE.
