(NYSE: AIG), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE KICK-OFF OF A COMMUNITY-BASED PROGRAM CREATED TO SUPPORT AND PROVIDE FINANCIAL EDUCATION AND CAREER OPPORTUNITIES FOR STUDENTS AT HISTORICALLY BLACK COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES (HBCUS) AND HIGH SCHOOLS IN THE GREATER HOUSTON AREA.
