GRAHAM FULCHER TO JOIN AIG AS INTERNATIONAL GENERAL INSURANCE CHIEF ACTUARY. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN INTERNATIONAL GROUP (NYSE: AIG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT GRAHAM FULCHER WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS INTERNATIONAL GENERAL INSURANCE CHIEF ACTUARY, A NEWLY CREATED ROLE. BASED IN LONDON, MR. FULCHER WILL REPORT TO JON HANCOCK, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, INTERNATIONAL GENERAL INSURANCE AND RICHARD OLSEN, CHIEF ACTUARY, GENERAL INSURANCE. MR.
