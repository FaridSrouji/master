AIG SAYS JONATHAN WISMER, DEPUTY CFO WILL BE LEAVING CO. AIG - BOARD APPOINTED ELIAS HABAYEB, CFO OF GENERAL INSURANCE, TO ADDITIONAL ROLE OF PRINCIPAL ACCOUNTING OFFICER, EFFECTIVE MARCH 9.AIG - JONATHAN WISMER, DEPUTY CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER AND CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER, WILL BE LEAVING CO EFFECTIVE MARCH 31.AIG - HABAYEB WILL NOW SERVE AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, DEPUTY CFO, CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER, AIG AND CFO, GENERAL INSURANCE.
