AIG LIFE & RETIREMENT STUDY SHOWS AMERICANS SETTING RENEWED FOCUS ON FINANCIAL SECURITY AND PLANNING. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AIG LIFE & RETIREMENT TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RESULTS FROM A NEW STUDY, AMERICANS MOVING FORWARD, WHICH SHOWS INDIVIDUALS ARE THINKING MORE CRITICALLY ABOUT HOW FUTURE EVENTS COULD IMPACT THEIR FINANCES. OVER HALF OF SURVEY RESPONDENTS SAID LONG-TERM FINANCIAL PLANNING HAS BECOME MORE IMPORTANT (58%) AND THAT THEY INTEND TO SAVE MORE (56%) AND PAY MORE ATTENTION TO HOW THEY SPEND AND MANAGE THEIR MONEY (55%).
