AIG ANNOUNCES FIVE-YEAR EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT WITH PETER ZAFFINO. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMERICAN INTERNATIONAL GROUP, INC. (NYSE: AIG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT AIG AND PETER ZAFFINO, PRESIDENT & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, HAVE ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT SECURING MR. ZAFFINO'S EMPLOYMENT THROUGH NOVEMBER 10, 2027.
