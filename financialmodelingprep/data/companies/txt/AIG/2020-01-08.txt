VARAGON CAPITAL AGREES TO FORM NEW STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH AFLAC. VARAGON CAPITAL PARTNERS, L.P. - HAS ENTERED INTO DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO FORM NEW STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH AFLAC GLOBAL INVESTMENTS.VARAGON CAPITAL PARTNERS, L.P. - AFLAC WILL MAKE NEW MULTI-YEAR INVESTMENT COMMITMENT OF UP TO $3.0 BILLION TO VARAGON TO INVEST IN MIDDLE MARKET LOANS.VARAGON CAPITAL PARTNERS - AFLAC WILL ALSO ACQUIRE A "SIGNIFICANT" NONCONTROLLING MINORITY INTEREST IN VARAGON.VARAGON CAPITAL PARTNERS, L.P. - TRANSACTION WILL NOT REDUCE OWNERSHIP OF VARAGON BY MANAGEMENT OR AIG.VARAGON CAPITAL PARTNERS - AFLAC WILL ACQUIRE INTERESTS IN VARAGON CURRENTLY HELD BY CERTAIN FORMER, CURRENT PARTNERS OF OAK HILL CAPITAL MANAGEMENT.VARAGON CAPITAL PARTNERS, L.P. - WILL CONTINUE TO OPERATE AS INDEPENDENT COMPANY.VARAGON CAPITAL PARTNERS - UPON CLOSING OF TRANSACTION, AFLAC AND AIG WILL HOLD EQUAL OWNERSHIP STAKES.
