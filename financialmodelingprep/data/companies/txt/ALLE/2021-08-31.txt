LIVLY AND ALLEGION COLLABORATE TO LAUNCH LIVLY KEY SOLUTION FOR MULTIFAMILY APARTMENTS IN THE U.S.. CHICAGO  , AUG. 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LIVLY, INC., THE CHICAGO-BASED TECHNOLOGY COMPANY OFFERING THE FIRST UNIFIED OPERATING SYSTEM FOR MULTIFAMILY RESIDENTIAL PROPERTIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A NEW COLLABORATION WITH ALLEGION U.S., A LEADING SECURITY PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS PROVIDER. THIS COLLABORATION RESULTED IN THE INTEGRATION OF THE ALLEGION ENGAGE™ PLATFORM WITH LIVLY KEY.
