ALLEGION TO HOST 2023 ‘INVESTOR & ANALYST DAY'. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE), A LEADING GLOBAL SECURITY PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, WILL HOLD A “2023 INVESTOR & ANALYST DAY” IN CARMEL, INDIANA, ON TUESDAY, MAY 2. PRESIDENT AND CEO JOHN H. STONE WILL LEAD THIS MEETING AND WILL BE JOINED BY ALLEGION'S EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM TO DISCUSS THE COMPANY'S LONG-TERM STRATEGY AND VISION FOR THE FUTURE. FORMAL PRESENTATIONS WILL BE HELD FROM 12:30 P.M. TO 2:30 P.M.
