ALLEGION TO ATTEND 2023 GOLDMAN SACHS INDUSTRIALS & MATERIALS CONFERENCE. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE) SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER MIKE WAGNES WILL DISCUSS THE COMPANY'S LONG-TERM STRATEGY AT THE 2023 GOLDMAN SACHS INDUSTRIALS & MATERIALS CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, MAY 9. THE CONFERENCE WILL BE HELD AT THE CONRAD HOTEL IN DOWNTOWN NEW YORK CITY.
