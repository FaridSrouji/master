ALLEGION'S BOARD INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 10%. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE), A LEADING GLOBAL SECURITY PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.45 PER ORDINARY SHARE OF THE COMPANY – REPRESENTING A 10-PERCENT INCREASE FROM 2022 AND THE COMPANY'S NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR OF ANNUAL INCREASE IN DIVIDENDS.
