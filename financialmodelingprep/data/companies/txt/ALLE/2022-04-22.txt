STANLEY BLACK & DECKER TO SELL ACCESS TECHNOLOGIES BUSINESS FOR $900 MILLION IN CASH TO ALLEGION. TRANSACTION ADVANCES STANLEY BLACK & DECKER'S PORTFOLIO TRANSFORMATION AND SHARPENS STRATEGIC FOCUS ON ITS CORE BUSINESSES NEW BRITAIN, CONN. , APRIL 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT FOR THE SALE OF ITS AUTOMATIC DOORS BUSINESS, ACCESS TECHNOLOGIES, TO ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE) FOR $900 MILLION IN CASH.
