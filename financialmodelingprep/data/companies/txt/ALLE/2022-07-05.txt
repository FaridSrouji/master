STANLEY BLACK & DECKER COMPLETES SALE OF ACCESS TECHNOLOGIES BUSINESS TO ALLEGION. NEW BRITAIN, CONN., JULY 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE: SWK) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SALE OF ITS AUTOMATIC DOORS BUSINESS, ACCESS TECHNOLOGIES, TO ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE) FOR $900 MILLION IN CASH.
