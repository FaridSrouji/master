DACK INTEGRATES WITH YONOMI. LOS ANGELES , APRIL 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DACK, A MOBILE-FIRST SAAS PLATFORM THAT ENABLES SHORT-TERM RENTAL OPERATORS TO PROVIDE THEIR GUESTS WITH A SEAMLESS DIGITAL STAY EXPERIENCE HAS RECENTLY PARTNERED WITH CONNECTED HOME INTEGRATION PLATFORM YONOMI, AN ALLEGION BRAND. THE PARTNERSHIP IS A WELCOME ADDITION TO DACK'S POWERFUL GUEST EXPERIENCE PLATFORM WHICH INCLUDES KEYCODE / DIGITAL KEY MANAGEMENT, CUSTOMIZABLE PROPERTY GUIDES AND UPSELLS, CONTACTLESS CHECK-IN AND CHECKOUT, DYNAMIC LOCAL RECOMMENDATIONS, ONE-TOUCH WIFI, AND MORE.
