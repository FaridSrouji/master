ALLEGION BOARD OF DIRECTORS ELECTS LONG-STANDING MEMBER KIRK S. HACHIGIAN AS CHAIRMAN. DUBLIN--( BUSINESS WIRE )--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS (“BOARD”) OF ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE), A LEADING GLOBAL SECURITY PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, HAS ELECTED KIRK S. HACHIGIAN AS ITS NEXT CHAIRMAN, EFFECTIVE JAN. 1, 2023. HACHIGIAN HAS SERVED AS A MEMBER OF THE BOARD SINCE ALLEGION BECAME A STANDALONE COMPANY IN 2013, AND WILL SUCCEED DAVID D. PETRATIS, WHO IS RETIRING FROM THE ROLE AS OF DEC. 31, 2022.
