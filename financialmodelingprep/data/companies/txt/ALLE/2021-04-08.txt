ALLEGION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE), A LEADING GLOBAL SECURITY PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.36 PER ORDINARY SHARE OF THE COMPANY. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 30, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 16, 2021.
