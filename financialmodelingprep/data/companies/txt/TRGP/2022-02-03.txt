TARGA RESOURCES CORP. EXECUTES AGREEMENTS TO SELL ITS EQUITY INTEREST IN GULF COAST EXPRESS PIPELINE. HOUSTON, FEB. 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TARGA RESOURCES CORP. (NYSE: TRGP) (“TARGA” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS EXECUTED AGREEMENTS SELLING ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY THAT HOLDS A 25 PERCENT EQUITY INTEREST IN THE GULF COAST EXPRESS PIPELINE (“GCX”) FOR $857 MILLION. TARGA EXPECTS TO RECEIVE THE FULL PROCEEDS FROM THE SALE IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022 FOLLOWING A CUSTOMARY CALL RIGHT PERIOD IN FAVOR OF THE OTHER MEMBERS OF GCX.
