TARGA RESOURCES PARTNERS LP PRICES UPSIZED $1.0 BILLION OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES. HOUSTON, JAN. 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TARGA RESOURCES PARTNERS LP (THE “PARTNERSHIP”), A SUBSIDIARY OF TARGA RESOURCES CORP. (NYSE: TRGP), AND THE PARTNERSHIP'S SUBSIDIARY TARGA RESOURCES PARTNERS FINANCE CORPORATION ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF AN UPSIZED OFFERING OF $1.0 BILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2032 (THE “2032 NOTES”). THE 2032 NOTES WILL ACCRUE INTEREST AT A RATE OF 4% PER ANNUM, WILL MATURE ON JANUARY 15, 2032, AND WERE PRICED AT PAR.
