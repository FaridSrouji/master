TARGA RESOURCES CORP. RELEASES SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. HOUSTON, OCT. 03, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TARGA RESOURCES CORP. (NYSE: TRGP) ("TARGA" OR THE "COMPANY") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS SUSTAINABILITY REPORT FOR 2021 IS NOW AVAILABLE ON THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE AT HTTPS://WWW.TARGARESOURCES.COM/SUSTAINABILITY. THE REPORT ADVANCES TARGA'S SUSTAINABILITY DISCLOSURES AND PROVIDES A REVIEW OF TARGA'S PERFORMANCE FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2021 AGAINST VARIOUS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE TOPICS THAT ARE IMPORTANT TO OUR INDUSTRY.
