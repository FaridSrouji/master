TARGA RESOURCES CORP. COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF LUCID ENERGY. HOUSTON, JULY 29, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TARGA RESOURCES CORP. (NYSE: TRGP) (“TARGA” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ONE OF ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARIES HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF LUCID ENERGY DELAWARE, LLC (“LUCID”) FOR $3.55 BILLION. THIS ACQUISITION EXTENDS AND FURTHER STRENGTHENS TARGA'S POSITION IN THE CORE OF THE DELAWARE BASIN.
