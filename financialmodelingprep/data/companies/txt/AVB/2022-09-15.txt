AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC. DECLARES THIRD QUARTER 2022 DIVIDENDS. ARLINGTON, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC. (NYSE: AVB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK (PAR VALUE $0.01 PER SHARE) FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022. THE COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND IS $1.59 PER SHARE AND IS PAYABLE OCTOBER 17, 2022, TO ALL COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF SEPTEMBER 30, 2022. ABOUT AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC.
