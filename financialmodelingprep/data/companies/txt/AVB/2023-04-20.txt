AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC.'S CUSTOMER CARE CENTER PROVIDES BACK-OFFICE SERVICES TO GABLES RESIDENTIAL. ARLINGTON, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC. (NYSE: AVB) AND GABLES RESIDENTIAL ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THEY HAVE ENTERED INTO A THIRD-PARTY SERVICING AGREEMENT FOR AVALONBAY'S CENTRALIZED SERVICE CENTER TO PROVIDE COMPREHENSIVE BACK-OFFICE, FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATIVE SUPPORT SERVICES TO GABLES RESIDENTIAL.
