AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC. ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT OF H. JAY SARLES FROM BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND NOMINATION OF CHRISTOPHER HOWARD AND NNENNA LYNCH. ARLINGTON, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC. (NYSE: AVB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT H. JAY SARLES, WHO HAS BEEN A DIRECTOR OF THE COMPANY SINCE 2005, WILL RETIRE FROM THE BOARD AT THE END OF HIS CURRENT TERM IN MAY 2021. DURING HIS TENURE ON THE BOARD, MR. SARLES HAS, AT VARIOUS TIMES, SERVED AS THE LEAD INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR AND AS THE CHAIR OF THE NOMINATING AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE AND AUDIT COMMITTEES OF THE BOARD. THE BOARD HAS NOMINATED ALL CURRENT DIRECTORS OTHER THAN MR. SARLES
