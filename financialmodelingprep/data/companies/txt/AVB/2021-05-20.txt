AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC. DECLARES SECOND QUARTER 2021 DIVIDENDS. ARLINGTON, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC. (NYSE: AVB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK (PAR VALUE $0.01 PER SHARE) FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021. THE COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND IS $1.59 PER SHARE AND IS PAYABLE JULY 15, 2021 TO ALL COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 30, 2021. ABOUT AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC.
