OCCIDENTAL RELEASES 2020 CLIMATE REPORT. HOUSTON, DEC. 02, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- OCCIDENTAL (NYSE:OXY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS 2020 CLIMATE REPORT, WHICH PROVIDES A PATHWAY DETAILING MILESTONES TO ACHIEVE ITS NET-ZERO ASPIRATIONS. THE REPORT, WHICH IS AVAILABLE ON OXY.COM, ALSO PROVIDES AN OVERVIEW OF PROGRESS ON ITS COMMITMENTS, CLIMATE-RELATED GOVERNANCE AND RISK MANAGEMENT PROCESSES AND SYSTEMS, PLANNING AND EXECUTION OF CLIMATE STRATEGIES, AND METRICS AND TARGETS FOR REDUCING GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS.
