OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM OPENS 16MW SOLAR FACILITY TO POWER PERMIAN BASIN OPERATIONS. OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM HAS OPENED ITS 16MW GOLDSMITH FIELD SOLAR FACILITY IN TEXAS. THE COMPANY SAID THAT THE ELECTRICITY GENERATED FROM THE SOLAR FACILITY WILL BE DIRECTLY USED TO POWER AN ENHANCED OIL RECOVERY FIELD OPERATION IN THE PERMIAN BASIN. THROUGH ITS OXY LOW CARBON VENTURES (OLCV) SUBSIDIARY, OCCIDENTAL ALSO ANNOUNCED IT SIGNED A LONG-TERM POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT (PPA) FOR 109MW OF SOLAR ENERGY BEGINNING IN 2021 FOR PERMIAN OPERATIONS. LOCATED IN ECTOR COUNTY NEAR ODESSA, THE GOLDSMITH FIELD SOLAR FACILITY IS CONSTRUCTED BY OCCIDENTAL AND WILL BE OPERATED BY FIRST SOLAR. THE 120-ACRE SOLAR FARM COMPRISES OF 174,000 PHOTOVOLTAIC (PV) PANELS, WHICH ARE MANUFACTURED BY FIRST SOLAR. USING SOLAR ENERGY IN OPERATIONS IS ANOTHER WAY OXY LOW CARBON VENTURES IS ENHANCING THE PROFITABILITY AND SUSTAINABILITY OF BUSINESS WHILE MEETING THE CHALLENGE OF REDUCING ATMOSPHERIC GREENHOUSE GASES. THE SOLAR FACILITY CONTRIBUTES TO THE OCCIDENTALS COMMITMENT TO ECONOMICALLY LOWER ITS CARBON FOOTPRINT BY USING EMISSIONS-FREE POWER SOURCES IN OPERATIONS. RECENTLY, OLCV SIGNED A 12-YEAR SOLAR POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH A JOINT VENTURE BETWEEN MACQUARIES GREEN INVESTMENT GROUP AND CORE SOLAR, WHOSE SOLAR PROJECT IN WEST TEXAS WILL BEGIN OPERATIONS IN 2021.
