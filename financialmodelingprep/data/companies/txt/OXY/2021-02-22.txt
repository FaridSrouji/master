OCCIDENTAL ANNOUNCES 4TH QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2020 RESULTS. HOUSTON, FEB. 22, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- OCCIDENTAL (NYSE: OXY) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2020 FINANCIAL RESULTS. THE ANNOUNCEMENT AND FINANCIAL SCHEDULES CAN BE ACCESSED VIA THE INVESTOR RELATIONS SECTION OF THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE, OXY.COM. THE EARNINGS RELEASE IS ALSO AVAILABLE ON THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION'S WEBSITE AT SEC.GOV.
