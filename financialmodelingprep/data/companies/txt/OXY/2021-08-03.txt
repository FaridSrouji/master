OCCIDENTAL ANNOUNCES 2ND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. HOUSTON, AUG. 03, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- OCCIDENTAL (NYSE: OXY) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. THE ANNOUNCEMENT AND FINANCIAL SCHEDULES CAN BE ACCESSED VIA THE INVESTOR RELATIONS SECTION OF THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE, OXY.COM. THE EARNINGS RELEASE IS ALSO AVAILABLE ON THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION'S WEBSITE AT SEC.GOV.
