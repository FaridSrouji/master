OCCIDENTAL ANNOUNCES DISTRIBUTION OF WARRANTS TO PURCHASE COMMON STOCK. OCCIDENTAL ANNOUNCES DISTRIBUTION OF WARRANTS TO PURCHASE COMMON STOCK.OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM - HOLDERS OF COMMON STOCK WILL RECEIVE 1/8(TH) OF WARRANT FOR EACH SHARE OF COMMON STOCK HELD AS OF RECORD DATE.OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORP - DISTRIBUTION OF WARRANTS WILL BE MADE ON AUGUST 3, 2020 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 6, 2020.OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORP - WARRANTS WILL HAVE A TERM OF SEVEN YEARS.OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORP - EACH WARRANT WILL ENTITLE HOLDER TO PURCHASE ONE SHARE OF CO'S COMMON STOCK FOR A PURCHASE PRICE OF $22 PER SHARE.OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORP - EXPECTS TO LIST WARRANTS ON NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE..
