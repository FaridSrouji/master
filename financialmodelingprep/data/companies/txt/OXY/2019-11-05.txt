OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORPORATION DECLARES A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 15, 2020. OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORPORATION SAID THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.79 PER SHARE ON COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON JANUARY 15, 2020, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 10, 2019.
