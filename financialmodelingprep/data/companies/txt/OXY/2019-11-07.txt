ICHAN CAPITAL CUTS ITS HOLDINGS IN OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM. ON NOVEMBER 8, 2019, ICHAN CAPITAL LP ANNOUNCED IT HAS RELEASED A LETTER TO SHAREHOLDERS OF OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORPORATION THAT IT HAS CUT ITS HOLDINGS IN THE COMPANY BY NEARLY A 3RD.
