OCCIDENTAL ANNOUNCES 1ST QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.QTRLY NET LOSS ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF $2.2 BILLION, OR $2.49 PER DILUTED SHARE.QTRLY ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE  $0.52.Q1 PRE-TAX ITEMS AFFECTING COMPARABILITY INCLUDED APPROXIMATELY $1.4 BILLION OF GOODWILL IMPAIRMENT CHARGES.FULL-YEAR 2020 GUIDANCE HAS BEEN WITHDRAWN.QTRLY CHEMICAL PRE-TAX INCOME WAS $186 MILLION.FIRST QUARTER COMBINED PRODUCTION OF 1,416 MBOED FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS.OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM - OIL AND GAS PRE-TAX INCOME FOR FIRST QUARTER WAS $179 MILLION, COMPARED TO $921 MILLION FOR FOURTH QUARTER OF 2019.IDENTIFIED $1.2 BILLION IN OPERATING AND OVERHEAD COST REDUCTIONS TO BE REALIZED IN 2020.OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM - CONTINUED TO REDUCE 2020 CAPITAL PROGRAM; GREATER THAN 50 PERCENT FROM INITIAL PROGRAM TO BETWEEN $2.4 BILLION - $2.6 BILLION.OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM - TOTAL AVERAGE DAILY PRODUCTION VOLUME WAS 1,416 THOUSANDS OF BARRELS OF OIL EQUIVALENT PER DAY FOR FIRST QUARTER.OIL AND GAS PRE-TAX INCOME FOR THE FIRST QUARTER WAS $179 MILLION, COMPARED TO $921 MILLION FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2019.OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM - TOTAL AVERAGE DAILY PRODUCTION VOLUME OF 1,416 THOUSANDS OF BARRELS OF OIL EQUIVALENT PER DAY (MBOED) FOR THE FIRST QUARTER.QTRLY AVERAGE WORLDWIDE REALIZED CRUDE OIL PRICES DECREASED BY 16 PERCENT FROM PRIOR QUARTER TO $47.08 PER BARREL.QTRLY TOTAL REVENUE AND OTHER INCOME $6,451 MILLION VERSUS $4,089 MILLION.
