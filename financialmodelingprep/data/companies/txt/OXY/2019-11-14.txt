BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY CUTS SHARE STAKE IN WELLS FARGO. BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC REPORTS STAKE OF 7.5 MILLION SHARES IN OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORP - SEC FILING.BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY CUTS SHARE STAKE IN WELLS FARGO & CO BY 7.7% TO 378.4 MILLION SHARES.BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC - CHANGE IN HOLDINGS ARE AS OF SEPTEMBER 30, 2019 AND COMPARED WITH THE PREVIOUS QUARTER ENDED AS OF JUNE 30, 2019.
