OCCIDENTAL ANNOUNCES DIVIDEND. HOUSTON, MAY 06, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- OCCIDENTAL (NYSE:OXY) SAID TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.01 PER SHARE ON COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON JULY 15, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 10, 2021.
