AIR PRODUCTS' INVESTOR RELATIONS, CORPORATE RELATIONS AND SUSTAINABILITY VP TO SPEAK AT BANK OF AMERICA 2022 HYDROGEN CONFERENCE ON DECEMBER 14. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA. , DEC. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SIMON MOORE , VICE PRESIDENT, INVESTOR RELATIONS, CORPORATE RELATIONS AND SUSTAINABILITY, WILL SPEAK AT THE BANK OF AMERICA 2022 HYDROGEN CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 14, 2022 AT 8:15 A.M.
