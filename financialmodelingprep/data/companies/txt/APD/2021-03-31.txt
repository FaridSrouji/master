AIR PRODUCTS AND NEW FLYER FIVE-CITY TOUR IN THE STATE OF WASHINGTON DEMONSTRATES BENEFITS OF HYDROGEN FUEL CELL BUS TECHNOLOGY. HOSTED A FIVE-CITY TOUR IN WASHINGTON STATE DURING THE WEEK OF MARCH 22 ND TO DEMONSTRATE THE BENEFITS OF FUEL CELL ELECTRIC BUSES POWERED WITH AIR PRODUCTS' HYDROGEN.
