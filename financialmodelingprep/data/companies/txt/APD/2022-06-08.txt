AIR PRODUCTS AWARDED LONG-TERM HYDROGEN AND NITROGEN SUPPLY AGREEMENT FOR INDIAN OIL CORPORATION. NEW INDUSTRIAL GAS COMPLEX WILL BE AIR PRODUCTS' SECOND BUILD, OWN AND OPERATE ONSITE FACILITY IN INDIA LEHIGH VALLEY, PA. AND PUNE, INDIA AND BARAUNI, INDIA , JUNE 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD), A WORLD LEADER IN INDUSTRIAL GASES AND LARGE-SCALE PROJECT DEVELOPMENT, EXECUTION AND OPERATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF A LONG-TERM SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH INDIAN OIL CORPORATION LIMITED (IOCL), INDIA'S FLAGSHIP NATIONAL OIL COMPANY.
