AIR PRODUCTS TO SUPPLY FIRST ROTTERDAM GREEN HYDROGEN FILLING STATION FOR TRUCKS IN 2023. HYDROGEN FOR ROAD TRANSPORT: BIG STEP FORWARD AS A RESULT OF COOPERATION BETWEEN  AIR PRODUCTS, SCHENK TANKTRANSPORT AND TNO AND BENEFITS FROM SUBSIDY AWARDED  FOR USE OF TRUCKS AND CONSTRUCTION OF HYDROGEN REFUELLING STATION ROTTERDAM, THE NETHERLANDS , MAY 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD), SCHENK TANKTRANSPORT AND TNO ARE COLLABORATING ON THE CLEAN HYDROGEN AND ROAD TRANSPORT PROJECT (CH2ART), AN ENDEAVOR WHICH WILL DEVELOP HYDROGEN TRUCKS AND A PUBLIC HYDROGEN REFUELLING STATION. THE PROJECT FOCUSES ON HYDROGEN IN HEAVY-DUTY ROAD TRANSPORT AND IS SUBSIDIZED BY THE DKTI TRANSPORT SCHEME (DEMONSTRATION SCHEME FOR CLIMATE TECHNOLOGIES AND INNOVATIONS IN TRANSPORT) OF THE DUTCH MINISTRY OF INFRASTRUCTURE AND WATER MANAGEMENT.
