AIR PRODUCTS' CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER TO SPEAK AT DEUTSCHE BANK'S GLOBAL MATERIALS CONFERENCE ON JUNE 9. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA. , JUNE 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER SEIFI GHASEMI  WILL SPEAK AT DEUTSCHE BANK'S GLOBAL MATERIALS CONFERENCE ON JUNE 9, 2022 AT 8:15 AM ET.
