AIR PRODUCTS AND NEW FLYER TOUR CITIES IN OREGON TO DEMONSTRATE THE BENEFITS OF HYDROGEN FUEL CELL BUS TECHNOLOGY. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., APRIL 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) AND NEW FLYER OF AMERICA INC. CONTINUED ITS TOUR OF THE NORTHWEST UNITED STATES (U.S.) AT THE END OF MARCH, VISITING TWO CITIES IN OREGON TO DEMONSTRATE THE BENEFITS OF FUEL CELL ELECTRIC BUSES POWERED WITH AIR PRODUCTS' HYDROGEN.
