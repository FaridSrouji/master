AIR PRODUCTS SUCCESSFULLY ACHIEVES U.S. MINORITY REPRESENTATION IN PROFESSIONAL AND MANAGERIAL ROLES OBJECTIVE AND SETS A NEW GOAL FOR 2025. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., NOV. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD) IS NOT RESTING ON ITS SUCCESS IN ACHIEVING A PUBLICLY-STATED 2025 GOAL FOR MINORITY REPRESENTATION IN ITS PROFESSIONAL AND MANAGERIAL POPULATION IN THE UNITED STATES.
