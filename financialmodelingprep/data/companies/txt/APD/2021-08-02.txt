AIR PRODUCTS' LNG TECHNOLOGY SELECTED FOR NIGERIA LNG TRAIN 7 PROJECT. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., AUG. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD), THE WORLD'S LEADER IN LIQUEFIED NATURAL GAS (LNG) TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT, HAS SIGNED AN AGREEMENT WITH SCD JV S.C.A.R.L, A JOINT VENTURE OF SAIPEM, CHIYODA AND DAEWOO, FOR THE NIGERIA LNG (NLNG) TRAIN 7 PROJECT.
