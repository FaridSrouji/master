LARGE-SCALE GREEN HYDROGEN FOR THE UK: AIR PRODUCTS AND ASSOCIATED BRITISH PORTS PARTNER ON RENEWABLE HYDROGEN PRODUCTION. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA. AND LONDON , AUG. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD) AND ASSOCIATED BRITISH PORTS (ABP) ANNOUNCED TODAY THEIR INTENTION TO PARTNER IN BRINGING THE FIRST LARGE SCALE, GREEN HYDROGEN PRODUCTION FACILITY TO THE UK.
