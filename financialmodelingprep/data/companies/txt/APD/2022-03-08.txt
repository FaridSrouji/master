AIR PRODUCTS TO BUILD GREEN LIQUID HYDROGEN PRODUCTION FACILITY IN ARIZONA. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA. , MARCH 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD), A WORLD LEADER IN THE SUPPLY, DISTRIBUTION, AND DISPENSING OF HYDROGEN, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL BUILD, OWN AND OPERATE A 10 METRIC TON PER DAY FACILITY TO PRODUCE GREEN LIQUID HYDROGEN IN CASA GRANDE, ARIZONA.
