FOR THE 40TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, AIR PRODUCTS INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., FEB. 3, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD) HAS INCREASED THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK BY 12 CENTS PER SHARE—FROM $1.50 TO $1.62 PER SHARE—REPRESENTING AN EIGHT PERCENT INCREASE.
