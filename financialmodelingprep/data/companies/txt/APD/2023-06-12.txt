AIR PRODUCTS' AP-X® LNG TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT SELECTED FOR THE NORTH FIELD SOUTH PROJECT. AIR PRODUCTS HAS SUPPLIED KEY EQUIPMENT AND TECHNOLOGY FOR ALL OF QATAR'S EXISTING LNG TRAINS OPERATING IN RAS LAFFAN SINCE 1996 LEHIGH VALLEY, PA. , JUNE 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD), THE WORLD'S LEADER IN LIQUEFIED NATURAL GAS (LNG) TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT, HAS BEEN SELECTED TO PROVIDE ITS PROPRIETARY AP-X® LNG PROCESS TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT TO QATARGAS FOR THE NORTH FIELD SOUTH (NFS) PROJECT IN RAS LAFFAN, THE STATE OF QATAR.
