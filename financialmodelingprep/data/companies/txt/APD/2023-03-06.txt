AIR PRODUCTS TO HIGHLIGHT INDUSTRIAL GASES FOR IMPROVED PLANT-BASED FOOD PROCESSING AT NATURAL PRODUCTS EXPO WEST. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA. , MARCH 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) WILL BE EXHIBITING AT THE NATURAL PRODUCTS EXPO WEST IN ANAHEIM, CALIFORNIA FROM MARCH 9-11 WHERE THE COMPANY WILL HIGHLIGHT THE PRODUCTIVITY AND QUALITY BENEFITS OF USING INDUSTRIAL GASES IN A VARIETY OF NATURAL FOOD PRODUCTS, INCLUDING PLANT-BASED FOOD PROCESSING APPLICATIONS.
