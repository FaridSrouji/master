AIR PRODUCTS HONORS COMPANY FELLOWS, ENGINEERS AND SCIENTISTS FOR TECHNICAL AND INDUSTRY EXCELLENCE. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., SEPT. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) RECENTLY HELD A VIRTUAL AWARD CEREMONY TO FORMALLY RECOGNIZE TWO FELLOWS AND 12 DISTINGUISHED ENGINEERS AND SCIENTISTS FOR THEIR TECHNICAL EXPERTISE, BUSINESS CONTRIBUTIONS, STRONG INDUSTRY PRESENCE AND COMMITMENT TO AIR PRODUCTS' CORE VALUES.
