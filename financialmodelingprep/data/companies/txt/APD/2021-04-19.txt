AIR PRODUCTS' KOCHI INDUSTRIAL GAS COMPLEX RELIABLY SUPPLYING SYNGAS TO BHARAT PETROLEUM IN INDIA. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD), A WORLD LEADER IN INDUSTRIAL GASES MEGAPROJECT DEVELOPMENT AND EXECUTION, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE KOCHI INDUSTRIAL GAS COMPLEX IS RELIABLY SUPPLYING SYNGAS TO THE PROPYLENE DERIVATIVES PETROCHEMICAL PROJECT (PDPP) AT THE BHARAT PETROLEUM CORPORATION LIMITED (BPCL) KOCHI REFINERY.
