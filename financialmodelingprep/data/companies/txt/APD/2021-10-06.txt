AIR PRODUCTS' AMERICAS PRESIDENT FRANCESCO MAIONE TO DELIVER KEYNOTE SPEECH AT THE GASES AND WELDING DISTRIBUTORS ASSOCIATION ANNUAL CONVENTION ON OCTOBER 9. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS' (NYSE:APD) FRANCESCO MAIONE, PRESIDENT, AMERICAS, WILL DELIVER A KEYNOTE SPEECH AT THE GASES AND WELDING DISTRIBUTORS ASSOCIATION (GAWDA) ANNUAL CONVENTION ON SATURDAY, OCTOBER 9, IN COLORADO SPRINGS, COLORADO.
