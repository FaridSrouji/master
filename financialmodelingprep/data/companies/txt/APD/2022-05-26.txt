AIR PRODUCTS, OQ AND ACWA POWER SIGN JOINT DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT TOWARD WORLD-SCALE GREEN HYDROGEN-BASED AMMONIA PRODUCTION FACILITY IN OMAN. POWERED BY RENEWABLE ENERGY, FACILITY WOULD PRODUCE GREEN ENERGY PRODUCTS ALLENTOWN, PA. AND MUSCAT, OMAN AND RIYADH, SAUDI ARABIA , MAY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD), OQ, OMAN'S LEADING INTEGRATED ENERGY GROUP, AND ACWA POWER (TADAWUL: 2082) TODAY SIGNED A JOINT DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT (JDA) TOWARD A MULTIBILLION-DOLLAR INVESTMENT IN A WORLD-SCALE GREEN HYDROGEN-BASED AMMONIA PRODUCTION FACILITY POWERED BY RENEWABLE ENERGY IN OMAN.
