AIR PRODUCTS TO PROVIDE FOUR END FLASH COIL WOUND HEAT EXCHANGERS FOR QATAR NORTH FIELD EAST LNG PROJECT. END FLASH UNITS TO BE SUPPLIED FOR FOUR PREVIOUSLY AWARDED AP-X® LNG HEAT EXCHANGER PROCESS TRAINS BEING BUILT IN RAS LAFFAN INDUSTRIAL CITY LEHIGH VALLEY, PA. , AUG. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD), THE WORLD'S LEADER IN LIQUEFIED NATURAL GAS (LNG) TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN AWARDED THE CONTRACT TO SUPPLY FOUR END FLASH COIL WOUND HEAT EXCHANGERS (CWHE) FOR THE NORTH FIELD EAST LNG PROJECT IN RAS LAFFAN INDUSTRIAL CITY, QATAR.
