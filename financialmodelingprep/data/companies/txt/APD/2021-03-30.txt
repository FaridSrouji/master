AIR PRODUCTS SIGNS VIRTUAL POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR WIND ELECTRICITY IN POLAND. WARSAW, POLAND, MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE: APD), A WORLD-LEADING INDUSTRIAL GASES COMPANY, HAS SIGNED A LONG-TERM, VIRTUAL POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT (VPPA) IN POLAND WITH SCIEKI ONSHORE WIND FARM, OWNED BY MACQUARIE'S GREEN INVESTMENT GROUP (GIG).  BY 2030, POLAND IS AIMING FOR RENEWABLES TO MAKE UP 23 PERCENT OF ITS POWER GENERATION, WITH WIND ENERGY MAKING A SIGNIFICANT CONTRIBUTION.
