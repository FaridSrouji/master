MELISSA SCHAEFFER TO SUCCEED SCOTT CROCCO AS AIR PRODUCTS' CFO. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., JULY 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO) SCOTT CROCCO PLANS TO RETIRE ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2021 FOLLOWING A DISTINGUISHED, 31-YEAR CAREER WITH THE COMPANY AND THAT MELISSA SCHAEFFER WILL SUCCEED HIM AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CFO ON AUGUST 10, 2021.
