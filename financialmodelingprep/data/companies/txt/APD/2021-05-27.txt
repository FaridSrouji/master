AIR PRODUCTS' CEO TO SPEAK AT BERNSTEIN'S 37TH ANNUAL STRATEGIC DECISIONS CONFERENCE (VIRTUAL) ON JUNE 3. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CEO SEIFI GHASEMI  WILL SPEAK AT THE BERNSTEIN 37TH ANNUAL STRATEGIC DECISIONS  CONFERENCE (VIRTUAL) ON THURSDAY, JUNE 3, 2021 AT 2:30 P.M.
