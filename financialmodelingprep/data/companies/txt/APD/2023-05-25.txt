AIR PRODUCTS TO ACQUIRE, OWN AND OPERATE INDUSTRIAL GAS COMPLEX AT UZBEKNEFTEGAZ'S ADVANCED GAS-TO-LIQUIDS FACILITY IN UZBEKISTAN FOR US$1 BILLION. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA. , MAY 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED AN INVESTMENT AGREEMENT WITH THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF UZBEKISTAN AND UZBEKNEFTEGAZ JSC ("UNG") TO ACQUIRE, OWN AND OPERATE A NATURAL GAS-TO-SYNGAS PROCESSING FACILITY IN QASHQADARYO PROVINCE, UZBEKISTAN FOR US$1 BILLION.
