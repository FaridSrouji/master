AIR PRODUCTS TO SHOWCASE HYDROGEN FOR MOBILITY SOLUTIONS AT THE ADVANCED CLEAN TRANSPORTATION EXPO IN LONG BEACH, CALIFORNIA. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., AUG. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) WILL BE EXHIBITING ITS HYDROGEN FOR MOBILITY SOLUTIONS FOR HEAVY-DUTY TRANSPORTATION AT THE ADVANCED CLEAN TRANSPORTATION (ACT) EXPO FROM AUGUST 31 TO SEPTEMBER 1 AT THE LONG BEACH CONVENTION CENTER IN LONG BEACH, CALIFORNIA.
