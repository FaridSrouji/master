CVS HEALTH AFFORDABLE HOUSING INVESTMENTS IN CALIFORNIA SURPASS $200 MILLION. WOONSOCKET, R.I., APRIL 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) HAS SURPASSED $200 MILLION IN AFFORDABLE HOUSING INVESTMENTS IN CALIFORNIA AS PART OF AN ONGOING COMMITMENT TO ADDRESS HOUSING INSECURITY THROUGHOUT THE COUNTRY.
