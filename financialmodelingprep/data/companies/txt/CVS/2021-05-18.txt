CVS HEALTH APPOINTS SHAWN GUERTIN CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. WOONSOCKET, R.I., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS APPOINTED SHAWN GUERTIN AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE MAY 28, 2021.
