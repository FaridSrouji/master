MINUTECLINIC RECOGNIZED FOR NATIONAL "AGE-FRIENDLY HEALTH SYSTEMS" COMMITMENT. WOONSOCKET, R.I., FEB. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MINUTECLINIC ® , THE RETAIL HEALTH CLINIC INSIDE SELECT CVS PHARMACY ® AND TARGET STORES, HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED FOR ACHIEVING "AGE-FRIENDLY HEALTH SYSTEMS-COMMITTED TO CARE EXCELLENCE" IN ALL OF ITS 74 REGIONS ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
