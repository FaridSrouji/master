CVS HEALTH CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 4, 2019. CVS HEALTH CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.50 (50 CENTS) PER SHARE ON THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 4, 2019, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON OCTOBER 24, 2019.
