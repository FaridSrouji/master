CVS HEALTH NAMES TILAK MANDADI EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF DATA, DIGITAL AND TECHNOLOGY OFFICER. WOONSOCKET, R.I. , JULY 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) HAS APPOINTED TILAK MANDADI AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND TO THE NEWLY CREATED ROLE OF CHIEF DATA, DIGITAL AND TECHNOLOGY OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JULY 25.
