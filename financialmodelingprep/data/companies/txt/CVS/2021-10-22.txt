CVS HEALTH ANNOUNCES AVAILABILITY OF MODERNA BOOSTER TO ELIGIBLE POPULATIONS. WOONSOCKET, R.I., OCT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT, FOLLOWING AUTHORIZATION FROM THE CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION (CDC) BASED ON GUIDANCE FROM THE ORGANIZATION'S ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON IMMUNIZATION PRACTICES (ACIP), SELECT CVS PHARMACY LOCATIONS ARE NOW OFFERING THE MODERNA COVID-19 BOOSTER SHOT TO ELIGIBLE POPULATIONS BEGINNING FRIDAY, OCTOBER 22, IN ADDITION TO THE PREVIOUSLY AUTHORIZED PFIZER-BIONTECH COVID-19 BOOSTER.
