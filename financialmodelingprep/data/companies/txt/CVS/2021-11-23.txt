CVS HEALTH COMPLETES ROLLOUT OF TIME DELAY SAFES IN ALL 851 TEXAS PHARMACIES. WOONSOCKET, R.I., NOV. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF AN ONGOING COMMITMENT TO HELPING BUILD HEALTHIER AND SAFER COMMUNITIES, CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETED INSTALLATION OF TIME DELAY SAFE TECHNOLOGY IN ALL 851 CVS PHARMACY LOCATIONS, INCLUDING THOSE IN TARGET STORES, ACROSS TEXAS.
