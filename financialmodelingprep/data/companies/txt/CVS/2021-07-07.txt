CVS HEALTH CORPORATION ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. WOONSOCKET, R.I., JULY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH CORPORATION (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF FIFTY CENTS ($0.50) PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE CORPORATION.
