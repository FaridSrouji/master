$10 MILLION COMMITMENT MADE TO THE AMERICAN DIABETES ASSOCIATION BY CVS HEALTH TO REACH FIVE MILLION PATIENTS AND TRANSFORM HEALTH OUTCOMES. ARLINGTON, VA., NOV. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, THE AMERICAN DIABETES ASSOCIATION® (ADA) AND CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) ESTABLISHED A NEW PARTNERSHIP TO SUPPORT FAMILIES IN HELPING TO PREVENT AND MANAGE DIABETES, AS WELL AS FUND RESEARCH ON THE DEVASTATING HEALTH DISPARITIES THAT FUEL THE DIABETES EPIDEMIC.
