CVS HEALTH TO CLOSE ACQUISITION OF SIGNIFY HEALTH. WOONSOCKET, R.I. AND DALLAS AND NEW YORK , MARCH 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH® (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT EXPECTS TO COMPLETE ITS ACQUISITION OF SIGNIFY HEALTH (NYSE: SGFY) ON OR AROUND MARCH 29, 2023, SUBJECT TO THE SATISFACTION OR WAIVER OF THE REMAINING CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS SET FORTH IN THE MERGER AGREEMENT.
