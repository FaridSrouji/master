CVS HEALTH COMPLETES ROLLOUT OF TIME DELAY SAFES IN FLORIDA. ALL 838 CVS PHARMACY® LOCATIONS IN THE STATE USING TIME DELAY TECHNOLOGY  FOR CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES TO HELP REDUCE ORGANIZED RETAIL CRIME COMPANY ALSO ANNOUNCES MAJOR MILESTONES REACHED IN SAFE MEDICATION DISPOSAL AND MEDICATION SAFETY EDUCATION WOONSOCKET, R.I. , APRIL 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF AN ONGOING COMMITMENT TO SUPPORT LAW ENFORCEMENT AND BUILD SAFER COMMUNITIES, CVS HEALTH® (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE INSTALLATION OF TIME DELAY SAFE TECHNOLOGY IN ALL 838 CVS PHARMACY LOCATIONS THROUGHOUT FLORIDA, INCLUDING THOSE IN TARGET STORES.
