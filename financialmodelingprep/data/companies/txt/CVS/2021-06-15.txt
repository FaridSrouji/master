CVS PHARMACY ANNOUNCES MAJOR UPDATE TO EXTRACARE REWARDS PROGRAM, DELIVERING SIMPLER, FASTER AND RECURRING REWARDS. WOONSOCKET, R.I., JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS PHARMACY, THE RETAIL DIVISION OF CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS), TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS LONG-STANDING, POPULAR AND FREE EXTRACARE ® REWARDS LOYALTY PROGRAM IS PAYING 2% BACK IN EXTRABUCKS REWARDS MOMENTS AFTER NEARLY EVERY TRANSACTION, RATHER THAN BEING PAID OUT ON A QUARTERLY BASIS.
