MORE THAN 3 OUT OF 4 AETNA MEDICARE ADVANTAGE MEMBERS IN 4.5 STAR-RATED PLANS OR HIGHER FOR 2022 -- THE HIGHEST PERCENTAGE AMONG NATIONAL HEALTH INSURERS [1]. WOONSOCKET, R.I., OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AETNA®, A CVS HEALTH® COMPANY (NYSE: CVS), ANNOUNCED ITS 2022 MEDICARE ADVANTAGE PRESCRIPTION DRUG (MAPD) PLANS OUTPACE THE MEDICARE ADVANTAGE INDUSTRY IN THE PERCENTAGE OF MEMBERS IN PLANS RATED 4.5 STARS OR HIGHER (OUT OF 5 STARS) BY THE CENTERS FOR MEDICARE & MEDICAID SERVICES (CMS).
