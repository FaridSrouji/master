CVS HEALTH STUDY SPOTLIGHTS RISING MENTAL HEALTH CONCERNS. SIXTH MENTAL HEALTH SURVEY SINCE APRIL 2020 SHOWS DIFFERING PERCEPTIONS BETWEEN AMERICANS AND THEIR PHYSICIANS WOONSOCKET, R.I. , MAY 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A RECENT CVS HEALTH® (NYSE: CVS)/HARRIS POLL SURVEY FOUND THAT YOUNG ADULTS AND PHYSICIANS CONTINUE TO SEE THE COUNTRY STRUGGLING WITH MENTAL HEALTH, BUT MOST AMERICANS AREN'T SEEKING CARE.
