CENTENE AND CVS HEALTH ANNOUNCE AGREEMENT FOR CVS HEALTH TO ACQUIRE ILLINICARE HEALTH.CVS HEALTH CORP - TRANSACTION ENTAILS SALE OF CENTENE'S MEDICAID AND MEDICARE ADVANTAGE LINES OF BUSINESS IN ILLINOIS..CVS HEALTH CORP - CENTENE WILL RETAIN ILLINICARE'S MEDICARE-MEDICAID ALIGNMENT INITIATIVE BUSINESS.CVS HEALTH CORP - CENTENE WILL RETAIN ILLINICARE'S STATEWIDE YOUTHCARE FOSTER CARE CONTRACT.CVS HEALTH CORP - CENTENE'S AMBETTER BUSINESS IN ILLINOIS IS NOT AFFECTED BY DEAL.CVS HEALTH CORP - CENTENE AND WELLCARE CONTINUE TO EXPECT THAT CENTENE - WELLCARE TRANSACTION WILL BE COMPLETED BY FIRST HALF OF 2020.CVS HEALTH CORP - FINANCIAL TERMS OF THIS TRANSACTION WILL NOT BE DISCLOSED.CVS HEALTH CORP - TRANSACTION IMPACT TO CVS HEALTH EARNINGS ONCE CLOSED IS EXPECTED TO BE IMMATERIAL..
