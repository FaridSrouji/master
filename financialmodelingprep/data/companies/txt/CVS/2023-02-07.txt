AETNA BETTER HEALTH OF WEST VIRGINIA SELECTED TO CONTINUE SUPPORTING CHILDREN AND YOUTH IN THE STATE. CHARLESTON, W.VA. , FEB. 7, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AETNA BETTER HEALTH OF WEST VIRGINIA, A CVS HEALTH® COMPANY (NYSE: CVS), HAS BEEN SELECTED BY THE WEST VIRGINIA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN RESOURCES TO CONTINUE SERVING MOUNTAIN HEALTH PROMISE MEMBERS UNDER A NEW MEDICAID MANAGED CARE CONTRACT.
