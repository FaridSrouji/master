CVS HEALTH COMPLETES ROLLOUT OF TIME DELAY SAFES IN ALL OF ITS NEVADA PHARMACIES. NEW SAFES FOR CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ANTICIPATED TO HELP REDUCE ORGANIZED RETAIL CRIME ALL 97 CVS PHARMACY LOCATIONS IN THE STATE NOW USING TIME DELAY SAFE TECHNOLOGY WOONSOCKET, R.I. , APRIL 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF AN ONGOING COMMITMENT TO SUPPORT LAW ENFORCEMENT AND HELP BUILD SAFER COMMUNITIES, CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETED INSTALLATION OF TIME DELAY SAFE TECHNOLOGY IN ALL 97 NEVADA CVS PHARMACY LOCATIONS, INCLUDING THOSE IN TARGET STORES.
