CVS HEALTH REACHES OPIOID SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT WITH STATE OF FLORIDA. WOONSOCKET, R.I. , MARCH 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH THE STATE OF FLORIDA TO RESOLVE CLAIMS DATING BACK MORE THAN A DECADE RELATED TO PRESCRIPTIONS FOR OPIOID MEDICATIONS THAT WERE FILLED AT FLORIDA CVS PHARMACY LOCATIONS.
