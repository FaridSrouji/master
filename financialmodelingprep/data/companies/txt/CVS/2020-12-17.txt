AM BEST AFFIRMS CREDIT RATINGS OF CVS HEALTH CORPORATION'S AETNA SUBSIDIARIES. OLDWICK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AM BEST HAS AFFIRMED THE FINANCIAL STRENGTH RATING (FSR) OF A (EXCELLENT) AND THE LONG-TERM ISSUER CREDIT RATINGS (LONG-TERM ICR) OF “A” OF AETNA LIFE INSURANCE COMPANY (ALIC) (HARTFORD, CT) AND THE OTHER OPERATING ENTITIES OF AETNA INC. (AETNA) THAT ARE NOW WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARIES OF CVS HEALTH CORPORATION (CVS HEALTH) [NYSE: CVS]. THESE ENTITIES INCLUDE THE MEMBERS OF AETNA HEALTH & LIFE GROUP.
