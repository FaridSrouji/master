VIRGINIA COMMUNITY HEALTH CENTERS AND AETNA BETTER HEALTH VIRGINIA TEAM-UP TO IMPROVE CANCER OUTCOMES IN UNDER-RESOURCED COMMUNITIES. RICHMOND, VA. , MAY 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AETNA BETTER HEALTH OF VIRGINIA, A CVS HEALTH COMPANY (NYSE: CVS), AND VIRGINIA COMMUNITY HEALTHCARE ASSOCIATION (VCHA) ANNOUNCED THAT AETNA PROVIDED A TOTAL OF $7.3 MILLION IN VALUE-BASED CARE INCENTIVE PAYMENTS TO 22 FEDERALLY QUALIFIED HEALTH CENTERS, ALSO KNOWN AS COMMUNITY HEALTH CENTERS, IN VIRGINIA.
