CVS HEALTH SURPASSES 10 MILLION COVID-19 VACCINE DOSES ADMINISTERED. WOONSOCKET, R.I., APRIL 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) HAS SURPASSED 10 MILLION COVID-19 VACCINE DOSES ADMINISTERED THROUGH ITS PARTICIPATION IN THE PHARMACY PARTNERSHIP FOR LONG-TERM CARE PROGRAM AND FEDERAL RETAIL PHARMACY PROGRAM.
