AETNA BETTER HEALTH PREMIER PLAN MMAI APPROVED TO EXPAND STATEWIDE TO SERVE DUAL ELIGIBLE MEDICARE-MEDICAID CUSTOMERS ACROSS ILLINOIS. CHICAGO, JUNE 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AETNA®, A CVS HEALTH® COMPANY (NYSE: CVS), ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED APPROVAL TO EXPAND ITS EXISTING ILLINOIS MEDICARE-MEDICAID ALIGNMENT INITIATIVE (MMAI) DEMONSTRATION HEALTH PLAN (AETNA BETTER HEALTH® PREMIER PLAN MMAI) TO SERVE THE ENTIRE STATE.  THE MMAI IS A GROUNDBREAKING JOINT EFFORT BY THE ILLINOIS DEPARTMENT OF HEALTHCARE AND FAMILY SERVICES (HFS) AND CENTERS FOR MEDICARE AND MEDICAID SERVICES (CMS) TO REFORM THE WAY CARE IS DELIVERED TO INDIVIDUALS ELIGIBLE FOR BOTH MEDICARE AND MEDICAID SERVICES ("DUAL ELIGIBLES").
