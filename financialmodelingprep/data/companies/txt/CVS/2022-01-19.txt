CVS HEALTH AND UBER HEALTH COLLABORATE TO ADVANCE HEALTH EQUITY IN COMMUNITIES NATIONWIDE. WOONSOCKET, R.I., JAN. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION WITH UBER HEALTH, UBER'S HEALTHCARE ARM, TO PROVIDE CRITICAL TRANSPORTATION SUPPORT AT NO COST TO PEOPLE WHO NEED IT MOST WHEN SEEKING ACCESS TO MEDICAL CARE, WORK OR EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS.
