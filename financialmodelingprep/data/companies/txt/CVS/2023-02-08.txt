SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISS LAW INVESTIGATES OAK STREET HEALTH, INC.. NEW YORK , FEB. 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISS LAW IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF OAK STREET HEALTH, INC. ("OAK STREET" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: OSH), IN CONNECTION WITH THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF THE COMPANY BY CVS HEALTH CORPORATION (NYSE: CVS). UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $39.00 IN CASH FOR EACH SHARE OF OAK STREET COMMON STOCK OWNED.
