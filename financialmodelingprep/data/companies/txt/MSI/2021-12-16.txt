MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS ACQUIRES NEXT GENERATION 9-1-1 LOCATION SERVICES PROVIDER, 911 DATAMASTER. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ACQUIRED 911 DATAMASTER, INC., A NEXT GENERATION 9-1-1 (NG9-1-1) DATA SOLUTIONS PROVIDER. 911 DATAMASTER'S SUITE OF NG9-1-1 LOCATION SOLUTIONS HELPS ENSURE THAT EMERGENCY CALLS ARE ACCURATELY LOCATED AND ROUTED BASED ON THE CALLER'S LOCATION, ENABLING A FASTER RESPONSE FROM FIRST RESPONDERS.
