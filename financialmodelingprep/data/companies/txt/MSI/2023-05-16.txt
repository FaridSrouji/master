MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: MSI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 88 CENTS PER SHARE. THE NEXT QUARTERLY DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE IN CASH ON JULY 14, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 15, 2023.
