MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS RECOGNIZED ON FORTUNE'S ‘WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES' LIST. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI) THIS WEEK WAS RECOGNIZED ON FORTUNE MAGAZINE'S LIST OF “WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES” FOR THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. FORTUNE'S “WORLD'S MOST ADMIRED COMPANIES” RANKINGS ARE DETERMINED BY EXECUTIVES, DIRECTORS AND ANALYSTS ASKED TO RATE COMPANIES IN THEIR OWN INDUSTRY ON NINE CRITERIA, INCLUDING INNOVATION, PEOPLE MANAGEMENT, USE OF CORPORATE ASSETS, SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY, QUALITY OF MANAGEMENT, FINANCIAL SOUNDNESS, LONG-TERM INVESTMEN
