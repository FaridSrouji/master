ORLANDO POLICE DEPARTMENT UPGRADES PUBLIC SAFETY COMMUNICATIONS WITH APX NEXT™ RADIOS. ORLANDO, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ORLANDO POLICE DEPARTMENT ANNOUNCED IT IS DEPLOYING 950 APX NEXT™ RADIOS FROM MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI), ONE OF THE MOST ADVANCED BROADBAND-ENABLED RADIOS AVAILABLE.
