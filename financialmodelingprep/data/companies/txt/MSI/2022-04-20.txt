MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS ESTABLISHES THE CYBER THREAT INFORMATION SHARING AND ANALYSIS ORGANIZATION FOR PUBLIC SAFETY, RECOGNIZED BY THE CYBERSECURITY AND INFRASTRUCTURE SECURITY AGENCY. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ESTABLISHMENT OF MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS' CYBER THREAT INFORMATION SHARING AND ANALYSIS ORGANIZATION (ISAO) FOR PUBLIC SAFETY. THE PUBLIC SAFETY THREAT ALLIANCE, A CYBERSECURITY AND INFRASTRUCTURE SECURITY AGENCY (CISA)-RECOGNIZED ISAO, HIGHLIGHTS MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS' UNWAVERING COMMITMENT TO PUBLIC SAFETY ORGANIZATIONS AND THE COMMUNITIES THEY SERVE.
