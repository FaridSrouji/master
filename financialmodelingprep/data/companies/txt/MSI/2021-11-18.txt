MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: MSI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS INCREASED ITS REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 11 PERCENT TO 79 CENTS PER SHARE. THE NEXT QUARTERLY DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE IN CASH ON JANUARY 14, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 15, 2021.
