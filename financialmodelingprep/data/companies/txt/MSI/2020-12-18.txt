IDC NAMES MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS A “LEADER” IN PUBLIC SAFETY SOFTWARE THAT HELPS AGENCIES TO MANAGE EVIDENCE STORED AND TRANSMITTED ELECTRONICALLY. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE:MSI) WAS RANKED AS A “LEADER” IN THE INTERNATIONAL DATA CORPORATION (IDC) 2020 “MARKETSCAPE: WORLDWIDE DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS FOR LAW ENFORCEMENT VENDOR ASSESSMENT.” DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT IS THE PROCESS OF MANAGING DATA AND INFORMATION THAT IS ELECTRONICALLY STORED OR TRANSMITTED, WHICH MAY BE USED AS PART OF AN INVESTIGATION AND/OR COURT CASE.
