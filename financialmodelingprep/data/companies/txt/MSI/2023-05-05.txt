MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS GIVES PUBLIC SAFETY IN PORTUGAL A MISSION-CRITICAL BOOST. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI) HAS BEEN AWARDED A FIVE-YEAR CONTRACT BY THE PORTUGUESE MINISTRY OF THE INTERNAL ADMINISTRATION TO MAINTAIN AND ENHANCE THE COUNTRY'S MISSION-CRITICAL PUBLIC SAFETY COMMUNICATIONS NETWORK. THIS AGREEMENT ENABLES 180 PUBLIC SAFETY AGENCIES AND 40,000 FIRST RESPONDERS INCLUDING POLICE, FIRE AND MEDICAL SERVICES TO BENEFIT FROM A MODERN, HIGHLY RELIABLE AND SECURE TETRA COMMUNICATIONS NETWORK. BUILT AND DEPLOYED BY MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS AND OPE.
