MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS ACQUIRES FUTURECOM SYSTEMS GROUP, A LEADING PROVIDER OF RADIO COVERAGE EXTENSION SOLUTIONS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ACQUIRED FUTURECOM SYSTEMS GROUP, A LEADING PROVIDER OF RADIO COVERAGE EXTENSION SOLUTIONS FOR PUBLIC SAFETY AGENCIES, BASED IN ONTARIO, CANADA.
