MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS, INC. ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 15, 2020. MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS INCREASED ITS REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 12% TO 64 CENTS PER SHARE. THE NEXT QUARTERLY DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE IN CASH ON JANUARY 15, 2020, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 13, 2019.
