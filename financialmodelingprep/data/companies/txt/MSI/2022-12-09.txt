LITHUANIA MODERNIZES FRONTLINE POLICING WITH BODY-WORN CAMERAS FROM MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS. CHICAGO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MORE THAN 2,500 VB400 BODY-WORN CAMERAS ARE BEING DEPLOYED TO THE LITHUANIAN NATIONAL POLICE AND BORDER GUARD SERVICE. THE NEW BODY-WORN VIDEO SOLUTIONS WILL BE USED BY FRONTLINE TEAMS TO HELP INCREASE TRANSPARENCY IN POLICING AND BORDER PROTECTION.
