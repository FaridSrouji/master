MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS FOUNDATION SETS A NEW RECORD OF GIVING THROUGH EMPLOYEE VOLUNTEERISM AND STRATEGIC GRANTS. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS FOUNDATION, THE CHARITABLE AND PHILANTHROPIC ARM OF MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE), TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS YEAR-END RESULTS FOR 2022, SURPASSING $12 MILLION IN DONATIONS TO CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS AROUND THE WORLD AND GENERATING MORE THAN $2.3 MILLION* IN VALUE THROUGH EMPLOYEE VOLUNTEERISM.
