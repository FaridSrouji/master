MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS MODERNIZES 911 CALL ROUTING IN NORTH AMERICA. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI) WILL SHOWCASE HOW IT IS MODERNIZING NEXT-GENERATION CORE SERVICES (NGCS) TO SHORTEN RESPONSE TIMES AND IMPROVE LOCATION ACCURACY FOR 911 CALLS THROUGHOUT NORTH AMERICA AT NENA 2022, JUNE 11-16 IN LOUISVILLE, KY. NGCS IS THE TECHNOLOGY THAT DETERMINES AND ROUTES 911 CALLS TO THE APPROPRIATE EMERGENCY COMMUNICATIONS CENTER (ECC), AND ENABLES 911 CALL TAKERS TO SEND AND RECEIVE TEXT MESSAGES, IMAGES, VIDEO AND DATA, TO AND FROM CALLERS.
