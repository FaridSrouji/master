MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS TO APPEAL CMA'S UNPRECEDENTED FINAL DECISION. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI) TODAY ISSUED THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT IN RESPONSE TO THE UNITED KINGDOM'S COMPETITION AND MARKETS AUTHORITY'S (CMA) FINAL DECISION REGARDING ITS MOBILE RADIO NETWORK SERVICES MARKET INVESTIGATION. THE CMA INTENDS TO IMPOSE A PRICE CONTROL ON AIRWAVE, THE PRIVATE MOBILE RADIO COMMUNICATIONS NETWORK THAT MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS ACQUIRED IN 2016, ALTERING A LONG-TERM CONTRACT THAT WAS MUTUALLY AGREED, DULY EXECUTED AND IS STILL IN EFFECT.
