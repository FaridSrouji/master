PRINCIPAL® FOUNDATION INVESTS MORE THAN $30 MILLION TO HELP OVER 8 MILLION PEOPLE ACCESS GREATER FINANCIAL SECURITY. DES MOINES, IOWA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PRINCIPAL® FOUNDATION, THE CORPORATE FOUNDATION ENDOWED BY PRINCIPAL FINANCIAL GROUP® (NASDAQ: PFG), EXCEEDED ITS GOAL TO INVEST $30 MILLION OVER TWO YEARS TO ADVANCE FINANCIAL SECURITY. THE CONTRIBUTIONS IMPACT OVER EIGHT MILLION PEOPLE AROUND THE WORLD BY REMOVING BARRIERS AND EMPOWERING PEOPLE AND COMMUNITIES SO THEY MAY BUILD FINANCIALLY SECURE FUTURES. PRINCIPAL FOUNDATION FUNDS A GROWING NETWORK OF MORE THAN 140 CHARITABLE ORGANIZATIONS IN 25 COUNTRIES A.
