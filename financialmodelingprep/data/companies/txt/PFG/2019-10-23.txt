PRINCIPAL FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCES COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND FOR FOURTH QUARTER 2019, PAYABLE ON DEC. 27, 2019. PRINCIPAL FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND OF $0.55 PER SHARE FOR FOURTH QUARTER 2019 WAS AUTHORIZED BY THE COMPANY’S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, BRINGING THE TRAILING TWELVE-MONTH DIVIDEND TO $2.18 PER SHARE, A 4% INCREASE COMPARED TO THE PRIOR YEAR TRAILING TWELVE-MONTH PERIOD. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON DEC. 27, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DEC. 3, 2019.
