PRINCIPAL FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCES BETSY J. BERNARD DECLINE TO STAND FOR RE-ELECTION. ON OCTOBER 15, 2019, BETSY J. BERNARD, A DIRECTOR OF PRINCIPAL FINANCIAL GROUP, INC. INFORMED THE COMPANY OF HER DECISION TO DECLINE TO STAND FOR RE-ELECTION AT THE COMPANY’S NEXT ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS, SCHEDULED FOR MAY 19, 2020.
