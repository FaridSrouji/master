EXTRA SPACE STORAGE INC. ANNOUNCES 1ST QUARTER 2023 DIVIDEND. SALT LAKE CITY , FEB. 16, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EXTRA SPACE STORAGE INC. (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: EXR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A FIRST QUARTER 2023 DIVIDEND OF $1.62 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY, AN 8% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR QUARTER'S DIVIDEND.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 31, 2023 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 15, 2023.
