EXTRA SPACE STORAGE INC. ANNOUNCES 3RD QUARTER 2021 DIVIDEND. SALT LAKE CITY, AUG. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EXTRA SPACE STORAGE INC. (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: EXR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.25 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY FOR THE THIRD QUARTER 2021, A 25% INCREASE FROM THE PREVIOUS QUARTER.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2021.
