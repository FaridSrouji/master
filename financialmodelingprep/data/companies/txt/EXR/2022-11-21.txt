EXTRA SPACE STORAGE INC. ANNOUNCES 4TH QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. SALT LAKE CITY , NOV. 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EXTRA SPACE STORAGE INC. (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: EXR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A FOURTH QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND OF $1.50 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 30, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 14, 2022.
