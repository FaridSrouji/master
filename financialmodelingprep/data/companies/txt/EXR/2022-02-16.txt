EXTRA SPACE STORAGE INC. ANNOUNCES 1ST QUARTER 2022 DIVIDEND. SALT LAKE CITY, FEB. 16, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EXTRA SPACE STORAGE INC. (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: EXR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.50 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY FOR THE FIRST QUARTER 2022. THIS REPRESENTS A 20% INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTER DIVIDEND, AND A 50% INCREASE OVER THE FIRST QUARTER 2021 DIVIDEND.
