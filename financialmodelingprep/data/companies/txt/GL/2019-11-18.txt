GLOBE LIFE INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 31, 2020. GLOBE LIFE INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.1725 PER SHARE ON ALL OF THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY HELD OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS OF THE COMPANY'S TRANSFER AGENT ON JANUARY 3, 2020. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JANUARY 31, 2020.
