GLOBE LIFE INC. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. MCKINNEY, TEXAS, OCT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBE LIFE INC. (NYSE: GL) REPORTED TODAY THAT FOR THE QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2021, NET INCOME WAS $1.84 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, COMPARED WITH $1.76 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE FOR THE YEAR-AGO QUARTER. NET OPERATING INCOME FOR THE QUARTER WAS $1.78 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, COMPARED WITH $1.75 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE FOR THE YEAR-AGO QUARTER.
