GLOBE LIFE INC. DECLARES DIVIDEND. MCKINNEY, TEXAS , SEPT. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBE LIFE INC. (NYSE: GL) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $.2075 PER SHARE ON ALL OF THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY HELD OF RECORD AS OF CLOSE OF BUSINESS OF THE COMPANY'S TRANSFER AGENT ON OCTOBER 7, 2022.
