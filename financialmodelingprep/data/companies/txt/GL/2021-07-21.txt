GLOBE LIFE INC. REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. MCKINNEY, TEXAS, JULY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --GLOBE LIFE INC. (NYSE: GL) REPORTED TODAY THAT FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021, NET INCOME WAS $1.92 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, COMPARED WITH $1.62 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE FOR THE YEAR-AGO QUARTER. NET OPERATING INCOME FOR THE QUARTER WAS $1.85 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, COMPARED WITH $1.65 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE FOR THE YEAR-AGO QUARTER.
