GLOBE LIFE INC. DECLARES DIVIDEND. MCKINNEY, TEXAS, MARCH 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBE LIFE INC. (NYSE: GL) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS RAISED THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO $.2075 PER SHARE ON ALL OF THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY HELD OF RECORD AS OF CLOSE OF BUSINESS OF THE COMPANY'S TRANSFER AGENT ON APRIL 4, 2022. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON APRIL 29, 2022.
