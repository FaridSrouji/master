COMMUNITY GARDEN WILL HELP FEED PEOPLE IN NEED. MCKINNEY, TEXAS, MARCH 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE GARDEN AT COMMUNITY GARDEN KITCHEN (CGK) IS TAKING ROOT. TO FURTHER CGK'S MISSION, GLOBE LIFE, LOCATED IN MCKINNEY, TEXAS, HAS DONATED $50,000 TO SPONSOR THE GLOBE LIFE GARDEN, A RAISED-BED GARDEN LOCATED ON ROUGHLY .25 ACRES OF LAND AT 501 HOWARD STREET IN MCKINNEY.
