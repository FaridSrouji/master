GLOBE LIFE INC. DECLARES DIVIDEND. MCKINNEY, TEXAS, MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBE LIFE INC. (NYSE: GL) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $.1975 PER SHARE ON ALL OF THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY HELD OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS OF THE COMPANY'S TRANSFER AGENT ON JULY 5, 2021.  THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JULY 30, 2021.
