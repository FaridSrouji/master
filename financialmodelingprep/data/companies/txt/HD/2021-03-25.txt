THE HOME DEPOT PARTNERS WITH HABITAT FOR HUMANITY TO HELP COMMUNITIES "BRING ON SPRING". ATLANTA, MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HOME DEPOT®, THE WORLD'S LARGEST HOME IMPROVEMENT RETAILER, ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS PARTNERSHIP WITH HABITAT FOR HUMANITY INTERNATIONAL TO EMPOWER COMMUNITIES TO BRING ON SPRING. PROVIDING MORE THAN $200,0000 IN PRODUCTS, THE HOME DEPOT WILL GIVE HABITAT FOR HUMANITY ORGANIZATIONS ACROSS THE COUNTRY THE TOOLS NEEDED TO HELP LOCAL COMMUNITIES TACKLE SPRING DIY PROJECTS AND NEIGHBORHOOD REVITALIZATION EFFORTS.
