THE HOME DEPOT PREMIERES DOCUMENTARY FILM "HOPE BUILDS," OFFERING BEHIND-THE-SCENES LOOK AT NATURAL DISASTERS AND WHAT IT TAKES TO REBUILD. ATLANTA , JAN. 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HOME DEPOT, IN PARTNERSHIP WITH IMAGINE ENTERTAINMENT, TODAY PREMIERED "HOPE BUILDS," A NEW DOCUMENTARY DETAILING THE DEVASTATING EFFECTS OF NATURAL DISASTERS ON COMMUNITIES NATIONWIDE AND THE HUMAN SPIRIT THAT COMPELS PEOPLE TO HELP EACH OTHER REBUILD. "HOPE BUILDS" OFFERS A BEHIND-THE-SCENES LOOK AT THREE MAJOR NATURAL DISASTERS AND FEATURES FIRST-HAND ACCOUNTS FROM ASSOCIATES AND SURVIVORS ON THE GROUND: HURRICANE ANDREW, THE CATASTROPHIC CATEGORY 5 HURRICANE WHICH IMPACTED SOUTH FLORIDA IN 1992; THE DEVASTATING TORNADO THAT LEVELED MUCH OF JOPLIN, MO.
THE HOME DEPOT LAUNCHES NEW IN-STORE APPLICATION TO HELP ASSOCIATES PROVIDE BETTER CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE. ATLANTA , JAN. 12, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HOME DEPOT®  IS ROLLING OUT A NEW, HOMEGROWN APP DEVELOPED TO HELP STORE ASSOCIATES PRIORITIZE TASKS MORE EFFECTIVELY. THE APP, DUBBED SIDEKICK, IS A NEW ADDITION TO HDPHONES, THE HOME DEPOT'S MOBILE DEVICES DEDICATED TO IMPROVING THE ASSOCIATE AND CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE.
