THE HOME DEPOT FOUNDATION SURPASSES $400 MILLION IN SUPPORT OF VETERANS. ATLANTA, NOV. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HOME DEPOT FOUNDATION HAS SURPASSED $400 MILLION INVESTED IN SUPPORT OF U.S. MILITARY VETERANS SINCE 2011, WHEN IT FIRST ANNOUNCED A FORMAL COMMITMENT TO VETERAN CAUSES. THIS PLEDGE HAS SINCE GROWN TO $500 MILLION BY THE YEAR 2025, PRIMARILY FOCUSING ON VETERAN HOUSING INITIATIVES.
