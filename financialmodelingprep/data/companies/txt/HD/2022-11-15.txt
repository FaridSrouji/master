THE HOME DEPOT ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER RESULTS; REAFFIRMS FISCAL 2022 GUIDANCE. ATLANTA , NOV. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HOME DEPOT®, THE WORLD'S LARGEST HOME IMPROVEMENT RETAILER, TODAY REPORTED SALES OF $38.9 BILLION FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL 2022, AN INCREASE OF $2.1 BILLION, OR 5.6 PERCENT FROM THE THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL 2021. COMPARABLE SALES FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL 2022 INCREASED 4.3 PERCENT, AND COMPARABLE SALES IN THE U.S. INCREASED 4.5 PERCENT.
