THE HOME DEPOT ANNOUNCES NEW AND ENHANCED DISASTER PREPAREDNESS RESOURCES. ATLANTA, APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH THE FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY (FEMA) RECOGNIZING MAY 9–15 AS NATIONAL HURRICANE PREPAREDNESS WEEK AND THE MONTH OF MAY AS NATIONAL WILDFIRE PREPAREDNESS MONTH, THE HOME DEPOT®, THE WORLD'S LARGEST HOME IMPROVEMENT RETAILER, IS OFFERING NEW AND ENHANCED RESOURCES FOR NATURAL DISASTER PREPAREDNESS AND IS ENCOURAGING CUSTOMERS TO PREPARE EARLY FOR ALL NATURAL DISASTERS, INCLUDING HURRICANES, TORNADOES, FLOODS AND WILDFIRES. A VARIETY OF DIGITAL RESOURCES AND TOOLS TO HELP GUIDE CUSTOMERS THROUGHOUT A NATURAL DISASTER, FROM CRITICAL READINESS TOPICS TO STORM RECOVERY AND DISASTER RELIEF, ARE NOW AVAILABLE ON HOMEDEPOT.COM.
