THE HOME DEPOT DECLARES THIRD QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 19, 2019. THE HOME DEPOT ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A THIRD QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.36 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 19, 2019, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 5, 2019.
