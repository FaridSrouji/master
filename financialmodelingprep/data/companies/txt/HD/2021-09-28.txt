THE HOME DEPOT COMMITS $10 MILLION TO VENTURE CAPITAL FUNDS FOCUSED ON DIVERSITY AND INNOVATION. ATLANTA, SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HOME DEPOT, THE WORLD'S LARGEST HOME IMPROVEMENT RETAILER, WILL INVEST $10 MILLION IN VENTURE CAPITAL FUNDS THAT PROMOTE DIVERSITY, TECHNOLOGY AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP: THE BASE10 PARTNERS ADVANCEMENT INITIATIVE, L'ATTITUDE VENTURES AND PLEXO CAPITAL.
