THE HOME DEPOT ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL 2021 RESULTS; INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 15 PERCENT; PROVIDES FISCAL 2022 GUIDANCE. ATLANTA, FEB. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HOME DEPOT®, THE WORLD'S LARGEST HOME IMPROVEMENT RETAILER, TODAY REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL 2021 RESULTS. FOURTH QUARTER 2021 SALES FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF FISCAL 2021 WERE $35.7 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF $3.5 BILLION, OR 10.7 PERCENT FROM THE FOURTH QUARTER OF FISCAL 2020.
