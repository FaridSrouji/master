FORTINET TO ANNOUNCE SECOND QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. SUNNYVALE, CALIF. , JULY 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FORTINET® (NASDAQ: FTNT), A GLOBAL LEADER IN BROAD, INTEGRATED, AND AUTOMATED CYBERSECURITY SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL HOLD A CONFERENCE CALL TO DISCUSS ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS ON THURSDAY, JULY 29 AT 1:30 P.M. PACIFIC TIME (4:30 P.M. EASTERN TIME).
