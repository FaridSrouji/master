ADMIRAL STAVRIDIS BRINGS TREMENDOUS PUBLIC SECTOR EXPERIENCE AND CYBERSECURITY INNOVATION INSIGHTS TO ADD EVEN FURTHER DEPTH TO OUR LEADERSHIP TEAM. ADMIRAL STAVRIDIS WILL BE A KEY CONTRIBUTOR AS WE CONTINUE TO SOLIDIFY OUR LEADERSHIP POSITION AND DRIVE FUTURE GROWTH.”
