EMIRATES NATIONAL OIL COMPANY TRANSFORMS CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE AND INCREASES OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY WITH FORTINET SECURE SD-WAN. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., MARCH 15, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FORTINET ® (NASDAQ: FTNT), THE GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY LEADER DRIVING THE CONVERGENCE OF NETWORKING AND SECURITY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT EMIRATES NATIONAL OIL COMPANY LIMITED (ENOC), A WHOLLY OWNED COMPANY OF THE GOVERNMENT OF DUBAI, HAS CHOSEN FORTINET SECURE SD-WAN TO TRANSFORM CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE AND INCREASE OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY.
