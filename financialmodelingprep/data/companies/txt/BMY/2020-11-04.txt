BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB AND MYOKARDIA ANNOUNCE EXPIRATION OF HSR ACT WAITING PERIOD. NEW YORK & BRISBANE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB (NYSE: BMY) AND MYOKARDIA, INC. (NASDAQ: MYOK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPIRATION OF THE WAITING PERIOD UNDER THE HART-SCOTT-RODINO ANTITRUST IMPROVEMENTS ACT OF 1976, OR HSR ACT, IN CONNECTION WITH BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB'S PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED TENDER OFFER TO ACQUIRE ALL OUTSTANDING SHARES OF MYOKARDIA FOR A PURCHASE PRICE OF $225.00 PER SHARE IN CASH, OR APPROXIMATELY $13.1 BILLION. THE EXPIRATION OF THE WAITING PERIOD OCCURRED AT 1
