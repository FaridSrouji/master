EVOTEC EXPANDS NEUROSCIENCE COLLABORATION WITH BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB TO INCLUDE NOVEL CELL TYPE. INCLUDES NEW CELL TYPES FOR STUDYING MOLECULAR DISEASE SIGNATURES FOR DISCOVERY OF DISEASE-MODIFYING THERAPIES FOR NEURODEGENERATIVE DISEASES TRIGGERS US$ 9.0 M PAYMENT TO EVOTEC HAMBURG, GERMANY / ACCESSWIRE / OCTOBER 8, 2021 / EVOTEC SE (FRANKFURT STOCK EXCHANGE: EVT, MDAX/TECDAX, ISIN: DE0005664809) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS COLLABORATION WITH BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB (NYSE: BMY) HAS BEEN EXPANDED TO INCLUDE A NEW CELL TYPE, TRIGGERING A PAYMENT OF $ 9.0 M TO EVOTEC. THE COLLABORATION WAS INITIATED IN DECEMBER 2016 WITH THE GOAL OF IDENTIFYING DISEASE-MODIFYING TREATMENTS FOR A BROAD RANGE OF NEURODEGENERATIVE DISEASES.
