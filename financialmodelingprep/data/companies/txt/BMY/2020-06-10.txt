U.S. FDA APPROVES OPDIVO (NIVOLUMAB) FOR THE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH ADVANCED ESOPHAGEAL SQUAMOUS CELL CARCINOMA AFTER PRIOR FLUOROPYRIMIDINE- AND PLATINUM-BASED CHEMOTHERAPY. U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION APPROVES OPDIVO® (NIVOLUMAB) FOR THE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH ADVANCED ESOPHAGEAL SQUAMOUS CELL CARCINOMA (ESCC) AFTER PRIOR FLUOROPYRIMIDINE- AND PLATINUM-BASED CHEMOTHERAPY.APPLICATION WAS GRANTED PRIORITY REVIEW DESIGNATION BY FDA, AND APPROVAL IS BASED ON PHASE 3 ATTRACTION-3 TRIAL.
