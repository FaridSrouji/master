BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB AND BLUEBIRD BIO ANNOUNCE SUBMISSION OF BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) TO FDA FOR IDECABTAGENE VICLEUCEL. BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB AND BLUEBIRD BIO ANNOUNCE SUBMISSION OF BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) TO FDA FOR IDECABTAGENE VICLEUCEL (IDE-CEL, BB2121) FOR ADULTS WITH RELAPSED AND REFRACTORY MULTIPLE MYELOMA.BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB - COMPANIES ARE COMMITTED TO WORKING WITH FDA TO RAPIDLY ADVANCE IDE-CEL THROUGH REGULATORY REVIEW PROCESS.
