BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB TO PARTICIPATE IN THE GUGGENHEIM HEALTHCARE TALKS 2023 ONCOLOGY CONFERENCE. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB (NYSE: BMY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY WILL TAKE PART IN A FIRESIDE CHAT AT THE GUGGENHEIM HEALTHCARE TALKS 2023 ONCOLOGY CONFERENCE ON THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 9, 2023. CHRIS BOERNER, PH.D., EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF COMMERCIALIZATION OFFICER, WILL ANSWER QUESTIONS ABOUT THE COMPANY AT 1:00 P.M. ET.
