AGIOS AND ROYALTY PHARMA ANNOUNCE $255 MLN PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR IDHIFA ROYALTY. AGIOS AND ROYALTY PHARMA ANNOUNCE $255 MILLION PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR IDHIFA® ROYALTY.AGIOS PHARMACEUTICALS - RETAINS RIGHT TO RECEIVE $25 MILLION PAYMENT UPON ACHIEVEMENT OF A SPECIFIED EX-U.S. COMMERCIAL MILESTONE EVENT.AGIOS PHARMACEUTICALS -  CO HAS SOLD ITS TIERED, SALES-BASED ROYALTY RIGHTS ON WORLDWIDE NET SALES OF BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB'S IDHIFA.AGIOS - WILL CONTINUE TO CO-PROMOTE IDHIFA, RECEIVE REIMBURSEMENT FROM BRISTOL MYERS FOR CO-PROMOTION UNDER ITS 2010 AGREEMENT WITH CELGENE.
