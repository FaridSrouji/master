AMERICAN WATER SELECTED FOR NAVAL STATION MAYPORT WASTEWATER CONTRACT. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS MILITARY SERVICES GROUP HAS BEEN AWARDED A CONTRACT FOR OWNERSHIP, OPERATION, MAINTENANCE AND REPLACEMENT OF THE WASTEWATER UTILITY SYSTEM ASSETS AT NAVAL STATION MAYPORT (“NS MAYPORT”) IN JACKSONVILLE, FLA.
