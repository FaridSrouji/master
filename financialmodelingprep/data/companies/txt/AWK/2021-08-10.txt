ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER CELEBRATES TWO SIGNIFICANT SAFETY MILESTONES. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER IS CELEBRATING TWO SIGNIFICANT SAFETY MILESTONES ACHIEVED BY THE STREATOR AND SOUTH BELOIT DISTRICTS. THE TEAMS CELEBRATED OVER A DECADE OF OPERATIONS WITHOUT A LOST-TIME ACCIDENT. THE STREATOR DISTRICT CELEBRATED 13 YEARS AND THE SOUTH BELOIT DISTRICT CELEBRATED 15 YEARS ON AUG. 1, 2021.
