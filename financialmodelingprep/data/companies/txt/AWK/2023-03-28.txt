INDIANA AMERICAN WATER ACQUIRES CLAYPOOL WATER SYSTEM IN NORTHERN INDIANA. GREENWOOD, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INDIANA AMERICAN WATER PRESIDENT MATT PRINE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY'S ACQUISITION OF THE CLAYPOOL WATER SYSTEM IN NORTHERN INDIANA. THE PURCHASE OF THE SYSTEM ADDS APPROXIMATELY 150 WATER CUSTOMERS TO THE COMPANY'S CUSTOMER BASE, WHICH REPRESENTS A POPULATION OF MORE THAN 400 RESIDENTS.
