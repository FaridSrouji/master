AMERICAN WATER RECOGNIZED BY MILITARY FRIENDLY® AND MILITARY TIMES FOR INDUSTRY LEADING SUPPORT OF VETERANS. CAMDEN, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE NATION'S LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS EARNED THE 2023 MILITARY FRIENDLY® SILVER EMPLOYER AND TOP 10 SPOUSE EMPLOYER DESIGNATIONS AND ALSO RECOGNIZED AS A 2022 MILITARY TIMES BEST FOR VETS: EMPLOYERS FOR ITS INDUSTRY LEADING EFFORTS ON HIRING AND SUPPORTING OUR NATION'S MILITARY VETERANS.
