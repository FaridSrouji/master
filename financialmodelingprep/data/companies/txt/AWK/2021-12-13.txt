AMERICAN WATER RECEIVES HIGHEST ESG EVALUATION SCORE FOR U.S. BASED UTILITY FROM S&P GLOBAL RATINGS. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY-TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT RECEIVED A SCORE OF 87 (OUT OF 100) AS REPORTED IN THE S&P GLOBAL RATINGS ESG EVALUATION REPORT. IT IS THE HIGHEST EVALUATION SCORE GIVEN TO A U.S. UTILITY BY S&P AND THE 3RD HIGHEST GLOBALLY.
