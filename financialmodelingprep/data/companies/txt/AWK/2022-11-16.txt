ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES KYLE SMITH AS WESTERN DIVISION SR. MANAGER OF OPERATIONS. PEORIA, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER NAMED KYLE SMITH THE SENIOR OPERATIONS MANAGER FOR THE COMPANY'S WESTERN DIVISION, EFFECTIVE OCT. 17, 2022. IN THIS ROLE, SMITH WILL OVERSEE OPERATIONS IN THE PEORIA, PEKIN AND LINCOLN DISTRICTS.
