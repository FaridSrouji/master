CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER ASKS GARRAPATA AREA CUSTOMERS TO REDUCE WATER USE. PACIFIC GROVE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER IS REQUESTING THAT CUSTOMERS IN THE GARRAPATA SERVICE AREA REDUCE WATER USE TO SUPPORT FIREFIGHTING EFFORTS. THE PALO COLORADO FIRE IS AN ACTIVE FIRE THAT BEGAN ON JANUARY 21, 2022 IN BIG SUR, SOUTH OF THE COMPANY'S GARRAPATA SERVICE AREA.
