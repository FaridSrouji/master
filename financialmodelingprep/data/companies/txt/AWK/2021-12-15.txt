FOUR ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER SUPERVISORS HONORED BY DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE FOR SUPPORT OF EMPLOYEE IN NATIONAL GUARD. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FOUR ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER SUPERVISORS WERE HONORED LAST WEEK BY THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE EMPLOYEE SUPPORT OF THE GUARD AND RESERVES (ESGR) FOR THEIR SUPPORT OF AN EMPLOYEE AND CO-WORKER WHO IS DEPLOYED WITH THE NATIONAL GUARD. FOUR ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER EMPLOYEES RECEIVED THE ESGR'S PATRIOT AWARD, WHICH RECOGNIZES SUPERVISORS WHO ARE NOMINATED BY A GUARDSMAN OR RESERVIST EMPLOYEE FOR SUPPORT PROVIDED DIRECTLY TO THE NOMINATOR.
