CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER ASKS SONOMA COUNTY CUSTOMERS TO REDUCE WATER USE DURING WATER TREATMENT PLANT MAINTENANCE FROM JUNE 7 THROUGH JUNE 9. SANTA ROSA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER'S LARKFIELD WATER TREATMENT PLANT WILL BE UNDERGOING MAINTENANCE FROM TUESDAY, JUNE 7 THROUGH THURSDAY, JUNE 9, 2022. DURING THE PLANNED MAINTENANCE, SUPPLY WILL BE IMPACTED, HOWEVER LARKFIELD, MARK WEST, WIKIUP, AND FULTON CUSTOMERS WILL CONTINUE TO RECEIVE HIGH-QUALITY WATER, WITHOUT INTERRUPTIONS TO SERVICE, THANKS TO CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER'S PARTNERSHIP WITH SONOMA WATER.
