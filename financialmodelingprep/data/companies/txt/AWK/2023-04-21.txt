AMERICAN WATER CELEBRATES EARTH DAY IN CAMDEN, NEW JERSEY. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, IN COLLABORATION WITH NEW JERSEY AMERICAN WATER, A SUBSIDIARY OF AMERICAN WATER, CELEBRATES EARTH DAY IN CAMDEN, N.J. BY RAISING AWARENESS AND PARTICIPATING IN COMMUNITY-WIDE EVENTS TO HELP REDUCE OUR ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT AND STRENGTHEN, SUPPORT AND ENHANCE THE CITY OF CAMDEN – WHERE OUR EMPLOYEES LIVE AND WORK.
