PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES GRANTS FOR LOCAL ENVIRONMENTAL PROJECTS. MECHANICSBURG, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT 13 WATERSHED-RELATED PROJECTS ACROSS THE COMMONWEALTH WILL RECEIVE FINANCIAL SUPPORT THROUGH THE COMPANY'S ANNUAL ENVIRONMENTAL GRANT PROGRAM. THE RECIPIENTS WILL RECEIVE A SHARE OF GRANT FUNDS TOTALING NEARLY $75,000 FOR THEIR COMMUNITY-BASED PROJECTS THAT IMPROVE, RESTORE OR PROTECT WATERSHEDS.
