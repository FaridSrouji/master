ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER PRESIDENT REBECCA LOSLI NAMED ENGINEER OF THE YEAR. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER PRESIDENT REBECCA LOSLI, P.E. WAS NAMED ENGINEER OF THE YEAR BY THE MISSOURI SOCIETY OF PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERS (MSPE) ST. LOUIS CHAPTER. MSPE IS AN ASSOCIATION OF PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERS FOCUSED ON PROTECTING PUBLIC HEALTH, SAFETY AND WELFARE THROUGH THE ETHICAL AND COMPETENT PRACTICE OF ENGINEERING. LOSLI WAS PRESENTED THE ENGINEER OF THE YEAR AWARD BY THE MSPE ST. LOUIS CHAPTER ON FEB. 23, 2023, AT THE CHAPTER'S ANNUAL AWARDS BANQUET.
