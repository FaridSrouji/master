AMERICAN WATER SELECTS WATER FOR PEOPLE FOR NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE'S GLOBAL GIVING CAMPAIGN. CAMDEN, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, SELECTS WATER FOR PEOPLE TO BE RECOGNIZED AS PART OF THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE'S (“NYSE”) GLOBAL GIVING CAMPAIGN.
