AWWA 2021 AMERICAN WATER SCHOLARSHIP PRESENTED TO UNIVERSITY OF TOLEDO DOCTORAL STUDENT PARUL BARANWAL. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PARUL BARANWAL, A DOCTORAL STUDENT AT THE UNIVERSITY OF TOLEDO, HAS BEEN CHOSEN AS THE RECIPIENT OF THE AMERICAN WATER WORKS ASSOCIATION (AWWA) 2021 AMERICAN WATER SCHOLARSHIP. THE SCHOLARSHIP IS AN ANNUAL AWARD OF $5,000 PRESENTED TO A GRADUATE LEVEL STUDENT TO ASSIST WITH THE DEVELOPMENT OF PROFESSIONALS INTERESTED IN SERVICE TO THE WATER INDUS
