AMERICAN WATER AND AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION DONATE $29,000 TO CAMDEN, NJ ORGANIZATIONS. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY-TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS DONATED $29,000 TO SEVERAL CAMDEN, NJ BASED NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATIONS.
