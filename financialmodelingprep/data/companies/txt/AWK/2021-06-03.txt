ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER NAMES ANGIE BELL DIRECTOR OF RATES AND REGULATORY. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED TODAY ANGIE BELL HAS BEEN NAMED DIRECTOR OF RATES AND REGULATORY. BELL WILL SUCCEED RICH KERCKHOVE WHO RETIRES NEXT MONTH AFTER OVER 14 YEARS OF DEDICATED SERVICE. IN HER NEW ROLE, BELL IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE SUCCESSFUL ADMINISTRATION OF RATE REVIEWS, RATE MECHANISM COMPLIANCE FILINGS AND OTHER KEY RATE AND REGULATORY MATTERS FOR ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER.
