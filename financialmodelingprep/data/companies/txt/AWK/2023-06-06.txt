NJR CLEAN ENERGY VENTURES AND NEW JERSEY AMERICAN WATER HIGHLIGHT INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS WITH NORTH AMERICA'S LARGEST FLOATING SOLAR ARRAY. SHORT HILLS, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FROM AIR CONDITIONING TO VACUUM CLEANERS, NEW JERSEY HAS LONG BEEN KNOWN FOR ITS INNOVATIONS, AND NOW IT IS HOME TO THE LARGEST FLOATING SOLAR ARRAY IN NORTH AMERICA. CONSISTING OF 16,510 SOLAR PANELS, THE 8.9-MEGAWATT (MW) SOLAR ARRAY COVERS 17 ACRES OF THE CANOE BROOK RESERVOIR IN SHORT HILLS, NEW JERSEY. THE CLEAN POWER GENERATED IS ENOUGH TO POWER 1,400 HOMES ANNUALLY AND WILL PROVIDE APPROXIMATELY 95% OF THE POWER NEEDS FOR NEW JERSEY AMERICAN WATER'S CA.
