MISSOURI AMERICAN WATER INVESTS $1.9 MILLION IN MARYLAND HEIGHTS PIPE REPLACEMENT PROJECT ALONG MCKELVEY ROAD. MARYLAND HEIGHTS, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #INFRASTRUCTURE--MISSOURI AMERICAN WATER IS REPLACING SEVERAL THOUSAND FEET OF AGING WATER MAIN ALONG MCKELVEY ROAD. THIS PROJECT IS PART OF THE UTILITY'S MULTIMILLION-DOLLAR INITIATIVE TO REPLACE WATER INFRASTRUCTURE THAT HAS REACHED THE END OF ITS USEFUL LIFE IN COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE STATE. “WE ARE COMMITTED TO SUPPORTING THE GROWTH OF ST. LOUIS COUNTY THROUGH INVESTMENTS IN OUR WATER SYSTEM TO MEET THE NEEDS OF CURRENT AND FUTURE GENERATIONS,” SAID MISSOURI AMERICAN W
