PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER PROUDLY RECOGNIZES AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION STEM EDUCATION GRANTEES. MECHANICSBURG, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $AWK #CONSUMERCONFIDENCEREPORT--THE AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION, A 501(C)(3) ORGANIZATION ESTABLISHED BY AMERICAN WATER, THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, RECENTLY ANNOUNCED THAT STEM (SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, EDUCATION, MATH) EDUCATION GRANTS TOTALING $67,055 HAVE BEEN AWARDED TO FOUR ORGANIZATIONS SUPPORTING COMMUNITIES SERVED BY PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER.
