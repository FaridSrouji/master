ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES INCLUSION, DIVERSITY & EQUITY GRANT RECIPIENTS. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--OVER $22,000 WILL BE SHARED BY 12 ORGANIZATIONS THROUGH ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER'S INCLUSION, DIVERSITY AND EQUITY GRANT PROGRAM. THE PROGRAM PROVIDES FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS SEEKING TO DEVELOP AND IMPLEMENT PROGRAMS, TRAINING AND/OR COMMUNITY-RELATED PROJECTS THAT AIM TO PROMOTE AND FOSTER INCLUSION, DIVERSITY AND EQUITY IN THEIR COMMUNITY.
