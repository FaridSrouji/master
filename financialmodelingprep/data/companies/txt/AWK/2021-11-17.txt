AMERICAN WATER'S CHERYL NORTON RECOGNIZED BY PHILADELPHIA BUSINESS JOURNAL. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, CHERYL NORTON, WAS RECENTLY HONORED BY THE PHILADELPHIA BUSINESS JOURNAL AS ONE OF THEIR 2021 WOMEN OF DISTINCTION. AS CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, NORTON IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE SUCCESSFUL PERFORMANCE OF THE COMPANY'S REGULATED STATES.
