ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER INVESTING OVER $2.7 MILLION IN PONTIAC WATER SYSTEM. PONTIAC, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER IS INVESTING OVER $2.7 MILLION TO UPGRADE THE PONTIAC AREA WATER SYSTEM. WORK INCLUDES REHABILITATING SIX FILTERS AT THE WATER TREATMENT PLANT AND REPLACING APPROXIMATELY 3,300 FEET OF WATER MAIN. THESE INVESTMENTS SUPPORT RELIABLE SERVICE TO HOMES, BUSINESSES, AND FOR FIRE PROTECTION. FILTER REHABILITATION SIX FILTERS WILL BE REBUILT AT THE PONTIAC WATER TREATMENT PLANT TO SUPPORT CONTINUED HIGH-QUALITY WATER SERVICE FOR YEARS TO COME. WO
