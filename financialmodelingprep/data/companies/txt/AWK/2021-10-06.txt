WEST VIRGINIA AMERICAN WATER COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF TOWN OF CEDAR GROVE WATER SYSTEM. CHARLESTON, W. VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WEST VIRGINIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF THE TOWN OF CEDAR GROVE'S WATER DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM. THE COMPANY NOW OWNS AND OPERATES THE SYSTEM AS PART OF ITS KANAWHA VALLEY SYSTEM. THE TOWN OF CEDAR GROVE, WHICH SERVED APPROXIMATELY 395 CUSTOMERS ALONG RT. 60 IN KANAWHA COUNTY, HAS STRUGGLED TO MAINTAIN ADEQUATE SERVICE TO ITS CUSTOMERS IN RECENT YEARS.
