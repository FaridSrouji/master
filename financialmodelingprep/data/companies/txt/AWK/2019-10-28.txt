AMERICAN WATER DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DEC. 4, 2019. AMERICAN WATER WORKS COMPANY INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF 50 CENTS PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON DEC. 4, 2019, TO ALL SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF NOV. 12, 2019.
