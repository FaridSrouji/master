MISSOURI AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES 2022 FIREFIGHTER GRANT PROGRAM. ST. LOUIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MISSOURI AMERICAN WATER IS ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS THROUGH AUGUST 31, 2022 FOR ITS ANNUAL FIREFIGHTER GRANT PROGRAM, WHICH PROVIDES FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO FIRE PROTECTION AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE ORGANIZATIONS WITHIN ITS SERVICE AREAS. GRANTS OF UP TO $1,200 WILL BE CONSIDERED TO COVER THE COSTS OF FIREFIGHTING TOOLS, PERSONAL PROTECTIVE GEAR, AND TRAINING.
