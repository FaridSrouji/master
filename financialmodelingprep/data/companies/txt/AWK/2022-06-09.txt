AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION ANNOUNCES LAST CALL FOR WATER AND ENVIRONMENT GRANT APPLICATIONS. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION, A 501(C)3 ORGANIZATION ESTABLISHED BY AMERICAN WATER, THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCES LAST CALL FOR WATER AND ENVIRONMENT GRANT APPLICATIONS WITH A SUBMISSION DEADLINE OF JUNE 30, 2022.
