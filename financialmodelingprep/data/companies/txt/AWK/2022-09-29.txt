ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES RESULTS OF PARTNERSHIP WITH FARMERS. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--OVER THE LAST SIX YEARS FROM 2016 THROUGH 2021, ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER HAS PARTNERED WITH ILLINOIS FARMERS TO APPLY OVER 109,000 DRY TONS OF RESIDUALS AND BIOSOLIDS, RATHER THAN SENDING THEM TO LANDFILLS. THE WATER TREATMENT RESIDUALS AND WASTEWATER TREATMENT BIOSOLIDS FROM ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER'S CHAMPAIGN, FISHER, GRANITE CITY, PEORIA, STREATOR, JERSEYVILLE AND CHICAGO METRO SERVICE AREAS WERE APPLIED TO AGRICULTURAL FIELDS ACROSS THE STATE.
