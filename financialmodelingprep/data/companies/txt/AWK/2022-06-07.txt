ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER ACQUIRES VILLAGE OF HARDIN WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS. THE NEWLY ACQUIRED SYSTEM ADDS APPROXIMATELY 435 WATER AND 405 WASTEWATER CUSTOMERS TO THE COMPANY'S SOUTHERN ILLINOIS SERVICE AREA.
