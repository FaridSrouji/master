AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES LORI SUTTON AS CHIEF INCLUSION, DIVERSITY AND EQUITY OFFICER. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER WORKS COMPANY, INC. (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED THAT LORI SUTTON WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS CHIEF INCLUSION, DIVERSITY AND EQUITY (ID&E) OFFICER. REPORTING TO THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, LORI WILL LEAD THE COMPANY'S ONGOING ID&E EFFORTS.
AMERICAN WATER NAMED ONE OF AMERICA'S TOP CORPORATIONS FOR WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISES BY WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISE NATIONAL COUNCIL. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE 2023 AMERICA'S TOP CORPORATIONS FOR WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISES (WBES) BY THE WOMEN'S BUSINESS ENTERPRISE NATIONAL COUNCIL (WBENC).
