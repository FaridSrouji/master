PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER HELPS PLANT NEARLY 2,000 TREES IN RECOGNITION OF CUSTOMERS' SWITCH TO PAPERLESS BILLING. MECHANICSBURG, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #10MILLIONTREES--FOLLOWING A PAPERLESS BILLING CAMPAIGN, PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER DONATED $19,470 TO THE CHESAPEAKE BAY FOUNDATION'S KEYSTONE 10 MILLION TREES.
