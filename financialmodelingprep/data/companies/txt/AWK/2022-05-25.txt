CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER ASKS CUSTOMERS TO LIMIT OUTDOOR WATERING TO ONE-DAY-PER-WEEK IN ITS VENTURA COUNTY DISTRICT. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS DROUGHT CONDITIONS CONTINUE TO AFFECT COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE STATE, CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED THE IMPLEMENTATION OF ONE-DAY-A-WEEK-OUTDOOR WATERING FOR ITS VENTURA COUNTY DISTRICT CUSTOMERS IN THOUSAND OAKS, NEWBURY PARK, AND CAMARILLO. CUSTOMERS IN THE EL RIO WATER SYSTEM WILL FOLLOW SEPARATE RULES ALLOWING UP TO TWO-DAYS-A-WEEK-OUTDOOR WATERING.
