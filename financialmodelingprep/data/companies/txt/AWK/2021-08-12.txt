INDIANA AMERICAN WATER ACQUIRES RIVER'S EDGE WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS IN SOUTHEAST INDIANA. JEFFERSONVILLE, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INDIANA AMERICAN WATER PRESIDENT MATT PRINE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY'S ACQUISITION OF THE RIVER'S EDGE WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS IN INDIANA.
