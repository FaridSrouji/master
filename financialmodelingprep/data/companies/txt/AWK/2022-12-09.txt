AMERICAN WATER PRESIDENT AND CEO RECOGNIZED AS A 2022 MOST ADMIRED CEO AT PHILADELPHIA BUSINESS JOURNAL'S AWARDS CEREMONY. CAMDEN, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED THAT ITS PRESIDENT AND CEO SUSAN HARDWICK WAS RECOGNIZED AS PART OF THE 2022 CLASS OF MOST ADMIRED CEOS AT THE PHILADELPHIA BUSINESS JOURNAL'S ANNUAL AWARDS CEREMONY AT THE CRYSTAL TEA ROOM IN PHILADELPHIA ON DECEMBER 8, 2022.
