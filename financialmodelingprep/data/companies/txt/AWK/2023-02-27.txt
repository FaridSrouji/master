CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER'S NORTHERN DIVISION GIVES $10K TO GREATER SACRAMENTO URBAN LEAGUE, PART OF NATIONWIDE SUPPORT FOR NATIONAL URBAN LEAGUE AND THE AFFILIATE MOVEMENT. SACRAMENTO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION, A 501(C)(3) ORGANIZATION ESTABLISHED BY AMERICAN WATER, THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, RECENTLY ANNOUNCED A COMBINED DONATION OF $200,000 TO THE NATIONAL URBAN LEAGUE AND THE AFFILIATE MOVEMENT — $10,000 OF WHICH HAS BEEN DONATED TO THE GREATER SACRAMENTO URBAN LEAGUE.
PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER SEEKING ENTRIES FOR ANNUAL “PROTECT OUR WATERSHEDS” STUDENT ART CONTEST. MECHANICSBURG, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ENTRIES ARE BEING ACCEPTED FOR ITS 21ST ANNUAL “PROTECT OUR WATERSHEDS” ART CONTEST. THE CONTEST IS FOR FOURTH-, FIFTH- AND SIXTH-GRADE STUDENTS IN SCHOOLS SERVED BY PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER, AS WELL AS INDIVIDUAL STUDENTS WHO LIVE IN THE COMPANY'S SERVICE AREA.
