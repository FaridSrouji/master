CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER SEEKS BILL RELIEF FOR CUSTOMERS FINANCIALLY AFFECTED BY COVID PANDEMIC. SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER HAS FILED AN APPLICATION WITH THE STATE WATER RESOURCES CONTROL BOARD TO HELP PROVIDE FINANCIAL SUPPORT TO CUSTOMERS WHO WERE UNABLE TO PAY THEIR WATER BILLS DURING THE CORONAVIRUS PANDEMIC. THE FUNDING WILL ENABLE THE UTILITY TO FORGIVE PAST-DUE BALANCES INCURRED BY ITS CUSTOMERS BETWEEN MARCH 2020 AND JUNE 2021.
