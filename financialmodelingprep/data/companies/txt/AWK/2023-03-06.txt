AMERICAN WATER CELEBRATES SUCCESSFUL TRANSITION OF OWNERSHIP OF WASTEWATER UTILITY SYSTEM ASSETS AT NAVAL STATION MAYPORT. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS MILITARY SERVICES GROUP CELEBRATED A SUCCESSFUL TRANSITION OF OWNERSHIP OF WASTEWATER UTILITY SYSTEM ASSETS AT NAVAL STATION (“NS”) MAYPORT IN JACKSONVILLE, FLA.
