CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER ACTIVATES MAJOR INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENT PROJECT. PACIFIC GROVE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ON THE EVENING OF FEBRUARY 13TH, CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER ACTIVATED TWO NEW PUMP STATIONS AS PART OF OUR EFFORTS TO REDUCE RELIANCE ON THE CARMEL RIVER. THE NEW FOREST LAKE PUMP STATION LOCATED IN PEBBLE BEACH AND THE SAN CARLOS PUMP STATION IN CARMEL VALLEY WILL ALLOW WATER SOURCED IN SEASIDE TO BE PUMPED TO CARMEL VALLEY, REDUCING RELIANCE ON THE CARMEL RIVER AND PROTECTING THE ENTIRE CARMEL RIVER ECOSYSTEM.
