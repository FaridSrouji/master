CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER ACQUIRES WARRING WATER SERVICE, INC.. LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF WARRING WATER SERVICES, INC., A PRIVATELY OWNED WATER UTILITY IN PIRU, CALIFORNIA FOR $4.6 MILLION. APPROXIMATELY 634 HOMES AND BUSINESSES SERVED BY WARRING WATER SERVICES WILL NOW BE SERVED BY CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER'S VENTURA COUNTY DISTRICT.
