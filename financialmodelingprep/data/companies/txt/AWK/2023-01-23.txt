PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS FOR 2023 ENVIRONMENTAL GRANT PROGRAM. MECHANICSBURG, PA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT APPLICATIONS ARE NOW BEING ACCEPTED FOR THE COMPANY'S 2023 ENVIRONMENTAL GRANT PROGRAM. THE PROGRAM OFFERS FUNDING FOR INNOVATIVE, COMMUNITY-BASED ENVIRONMENTAL PROJECTS THAT IMPROVE, RESTORE OR PROTECT THE WATERSHEDS, SURFACE WATER AND GROUNDWATER SUPPLIES IN LOCAL COMMUNITIES.
