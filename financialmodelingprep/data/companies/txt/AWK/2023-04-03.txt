AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION NOW ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS FOR 2023 WATER AND ENVIRONMENT GRANT PROGRAM. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION, A 501(C)(3) ORGANIZATION ESTABLISHED BY AMERICAN WATER, THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT LAUNCHED THE 2023 WATER AND ENVIRONMENT GRANT PROGRAM, SUPPORTING CLEAN WATER, CONSERVATION, ENVIRONMENTAL EDUCATION, CLIMATE VARIABILITY AND WATER-BASED RECREATION PROJECTS.
