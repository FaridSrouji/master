MISSOURI AMERICAN WATER TO INVEST OVER $425 MILLION TO UPGRADE WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS THIS YEAR. ST. LOUIS--( BUSINESS WIRE )--MISSOURI AMERICAN WATER IS CONTINUING TO PROACTIVELY INVEST IN ITS WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS STATEWIDE, WITH OVER $425 MILLION IN IMPROVEMENTS PLANNED IN 2023. PROJECTS INCLUDE THE REPLACEMENT OF AGING WATER AND WASTEWATER PIPES AND TREATMENT PLANT UPGRADES.
