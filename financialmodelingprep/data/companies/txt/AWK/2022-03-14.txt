AMERICAN WATER HELPS EDUCATE HOMEOWNERS DURING THE EPA'S FIX A LEAK WEEK, MARCH 14-22. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER IS LEVERAGING THE NATIONWIDE AWARENESS CAMPAIGN TAKING PLACE DURING THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY'S (EPA) FIX A LEAK WEEK TO ADVANCE LOCAL WATER CONSERVATION GOALS AS WELL AS PROVIDE A SAVINGS TO HOMEOWNERS, PROPERTY OWNERS AND BUSINESSES IN THE COMMUNITIES IT SERVES.
