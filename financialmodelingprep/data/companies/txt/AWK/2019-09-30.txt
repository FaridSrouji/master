AMERICAN WATER AWARDS WEST POINT WATER AND WASTEWATER TREATMENT CONTRACT. AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED ITS MILITARY SERVICES GROUP HAS BEEN AWARDED A CONTRACT FOR OWNERSHIP, OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE OF THE WATER AND WASTEWATER SYSTEMS ON ONE OF THE NATION’S MOST STORIED INSTITUTIONS, THE UNITED STATES MILITARY ACADEMY (USMA) AT WEST POINT, N.Y. AMERICAN WATER WAS SELECTED FOR BOTH WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY PRIVATIZATION (UP) CONTRACTS AWARDED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE IN FISCAL YEAR 2019, FOLLOWING A PRIOR ANNOUNCEMENT ON SECURING THE CONTRACT FOR JOINT BASE SAN ANTONIO. THE TOTAL CONTRACT VALUE AS AWARDED IS APPROXIMATELY $519 MILLION OVER A 50-YEAR PERIOD. THE CONTRACT WILL BE SUBJECT TO AN ANNUAL ECONOMIC PRICE ADJUSTMENT.
