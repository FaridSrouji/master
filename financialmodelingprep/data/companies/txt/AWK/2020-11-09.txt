VIRGINIA AMERICAN WATER GRANTED NEW RATES BY STATE CORPORATION COMMISSION. ALEXANDRIA, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ON NOVEMBER 6, 2020, THE VIRGINIA STATE CORPORATION COMMISSION (SCC) ISSUED AN ORDER THAT APPROVES NEW RATES FOR VIRGINIA AMERICAN WATER. THE NEW RATES ARE FOR THE COMPANY'S RESIDENTIAL, COMMERCIAL AND INDUSTRIAL CUSTOMERS IN THE CITY OF ALEXANDRIA, THE CITY OF HOPEWELL, A PORTION OF PRINCE GEORGE COUNTY, IN THE HOPEWELL DISTRICT, A PORTION OF PRINCE WILLIAM COUNTY AND THE EASTERN DISTRICT SERVICE AREA IN AND AROUND VIRGINIA'S NORTHERN NECK. THE INCREASED RATES
