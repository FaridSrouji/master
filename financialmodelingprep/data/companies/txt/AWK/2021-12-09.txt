AMERICAN WATER DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER WORKS COMPANY, INC. (NYSE: AWK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.6025 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MARCH 1, 2022, TO ALL SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEBRUARY 8, 2022. THIS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS A CONTINUATION OF THE INCREASE IN THE ANNUALIZED DIVIDEND APPROVED BY THE BOARD AND ANNOUNCED IN APRIL 2021.
