DURING SOURCE WATER PROTECTION WEEK, ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES RESULTS OF PARTNERSHIP WITH ILLINOIS FARMERS. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--OVER THE LAST FOUR YEARS, ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER HAS PARTNERED WITH ILLINOIS FARMERS TO APPLY OVER 96,000 DRY TONS OF RESIDUALS AND BIOSOLIDS, RATHER THAN SENDING THEM TO A LANDFILL. THE WATER TREATMENT RESIDUALS AND WASTEWATER TREATMENT BIOSOLIDS FROM SEVERAL ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER SERVICE AREAS WERE APPLIED TO AGRICULTURAL FIELDS ACROSS THE STATE.
