PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER TO PLANT TREES FOR CUSTOMERS WHO SWITCH TO PAPERLESS BILLING IN APRIL. MECHANICSBURG, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #EARTHDAY--WITH EARTH DAY JUST AROUND THE CORNER, PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WOULD PLANT A TREE FOR EACH CUSTOMER WHO COMMITS TO GOING PAPERLESS DURING THE MONTH OF APRIL. THE COMPANY HAS PARTNERED WITH THE CHESAPEAKE BAY FOUNDATION ON THIS CAMPAIGN AND WILL DONATE $10 TO ITS KEYSTONE 10 MILLION TREES PARTNERSHIP FOR EVERY CUSTOMER WHO SWITCHES FROM A PAPER BILL TO AN ELECTRONIC BILL THIS MONTH.
