ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER CELEBRATES SAFETY EXCELLENCE MILESTONES IN THE COMPANY'S SOUTHERN ILLINOIS SERVICE AREA. ALTON, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER IS HIGHLIGHTING SAFETY EXCELLENCE ACHIEVED BY THE COMPANY'S SOUTHERN ILLINOIS TEAMS DURING NATIONAL SAFETY MONTH. THE TEAM MEMBERS CELEBRATED SEVERAL YEARS OF OPERATION WITHOUT LOST-TIME ACCIDENTS. THE GRAFTON WASTEWATER SERVICE TEAM CELEBRATED SEVEN YEARS IN MAY; AND THE JERSEYVILLE WATER AND WASTEWATER TEAMS CELEBRATED NINE YEARS AT THE BEGINNING OF JUNE.
