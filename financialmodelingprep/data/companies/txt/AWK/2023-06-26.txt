AWWA 2023 AMERICAN WATER SCHOLARSHIP PRESENTED TO WESTERN UNIVERSITY GRADUATE STUDENT MOHAMMAD NOOR TAMIM. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MOHAMMAD NOOR TAMIM, A GRADUATE STUDENT AT WESTERN UNIVERSITY IN LONDON, ONTARIO, HAS BEEN SELECTED AS THE RECIPIENT OF THE AMERICAN WATER WORKS ASSOCIATION (AWWA) 2023 AMERICAN WATER SCHOLARSHIP. THE SCHOLARSHIP IS AN ANNUAL AWARD OF $5,000 PRESENTED TO A GRADUATE-LEVEL STUDENT TO ASSIST WITH THE DEVELOPMENT OF PROFESSIONALS INTERESTED IN SERVIC.
