ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER INVESTING OVER $10 MILLION IN PEORIA WATER SYSTEM. PEORIA, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER IS INVESTING OVER $10 MILLION TO UPGRADE THE PEORIA AREA WATER SYSTEM. WORK INCLUDES A NEW BOOSTER STATION, ELEVATED STORAGE TANK AND OVER 23,000 FEET OF WATER MAIN. BOOSTER STATION PROJECT CONSTRUCTION TO REPLACE AN EXISTING BOOSTER STATION LOCATED IN BARTONVILLE NEAR ENTERPRISE DRIVE WILL BEGIN THIS SPRING.
