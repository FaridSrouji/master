PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES SUPPLIER SUMMIT. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY IS HOSTING A SUPPLIER SUMMIT ON THURSDAY, APRIL 13, 2023. THIS IN-PERSON EVENT WILL BE HELD AT THE DOUBLETREE BY HILTON HOTEL AT 500 MANSFIELD AVENUE IN GREENTREE. THE EVENT IS SCHEDULED FROM 9:30 A.M. – 3:30 P.M., AND A BOX LUNCH WILL BE PROVIDED.
