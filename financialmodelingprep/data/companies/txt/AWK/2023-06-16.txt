PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ASKS CUSTOMERS TO VOLUNTARILY REDUCE NONESSENTIAL WATER USE DUE TO DROUGHT WATCH DECLARATION. MECHANICSBURG, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #DROUGHTWATCHPA--PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS TO VOLUNTARILY REDUCE NONESSENTIAL WATER USE IN RESPONSE TO A STATEWIDE DROUGHT WATCH DECLARATION.
