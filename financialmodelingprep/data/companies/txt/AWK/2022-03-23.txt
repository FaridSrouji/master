AMERICAN WATER'S COMMITMENT TO COMMUNITY IN CAMDEN, NJ. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY-TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY, THAT ALONG WITH THE AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION (AWCF) AND NEW JERSEY AMERICAN WATER, THE COMPANY DONATED MORE THAN $1.3 MILLION IN 2021 TO COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS BENEFITING THE CITY OF CAMDEN.
