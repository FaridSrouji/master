ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES 2022 FIREFIGHTER GRANT PROGRAM. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER IS ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS FOR ITS 2022 FIREFIGHTER GRANT PROGRAM. THROUGH THIS PROGRAM, THE COMPANY PROVIDES FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO FIRE AND EMERGENCY ORGANIZATIONS IN ITS SERVICE AREAS. ELIGIBLE FIRE DEPARTMENTS CAN APPLY ONLINE BY VISITING WWW.ILLINOISAMWATER.COM > NEWS & COMMUNITY > FIREFIGHTER GRANT PROGRAM.
