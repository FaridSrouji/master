ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER'S CAIRO DISTRICT CELEBRATES 22 YEARS OF SAFETY EXCELLENCE. CAIRO, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER'S CAIRO DISTRICT IS CELEBRATING OVER TWO DECADES OF SAFETY EXCELLENCE. THE LOCAL WATER SYSTEM CELEBRATED 22 YEARS, OR OVER 8,000 DAYS, WITHOUT EXPERIENCING ANY LOST-TIME ACCIDENTS ON AUG. 17, 2021. ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER EMPLOYEES ATTEND SAFETY TRAINING ON A REGULAR BASIS WHICH INCLUDES BOTH ONLINE CLASSES AND HANDS-ON TRAINING.
