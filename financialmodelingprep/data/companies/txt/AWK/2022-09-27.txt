ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER CELEBRATES SECOND ANNUAL SOURCE WATER PROTECTION WEEK. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN CONJUNCTION WITH THE AMERICAN WATER WORKS ASSOCIATION (AWWA), ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE SECOND ANNUAL SOURCE WATER PROTECTION WEEK. THE COMPANY WILL HIGHLIGHT COLLABORATIVE EFFORTS NEEDED TO PROTECT SOURCES OF WATER USED AS DRINKING WATER SUPPLIES AND HOW TO PROTECT OUR WORLD'S MOST PRECIOUS NATURAL RESOURCE. SOURCE WATER PROTECTION WEEK IS OBSERVED SEPT. 25 – OCT. 1. ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER CONTINUOUSLY EDUCATES CUSTOMERS ABOUT SOURCE
