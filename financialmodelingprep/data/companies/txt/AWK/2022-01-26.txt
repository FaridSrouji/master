AMERICAN WATER INCLUDED IN 2022 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX FOR FOURTH STRAIGHT YEAR. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY-TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE 2022 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI) FOR THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. THE 2022 GEI EXPANDS GLOBALLY TO REPRESENT 45 COUNTRIES AND REGIONS, INCLUDING FIRMS HEADQUARTERED IN COLOMBIA AND URUGUAY FOR THE FIRST TIME.
