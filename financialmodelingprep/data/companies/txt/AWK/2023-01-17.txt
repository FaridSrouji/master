VIRGINIA AMERICAN WATER SIGNS AGREEMENT TO PURCHASE TOWN OF CAPE CHARLES WATER AND SEWER SYSTEMS. ALEXANDRIA, VA.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--VIRGINIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS SIGNED A COMPREHENSIVE ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR THE DRINKING WATER AND WASTEWATER ASSETS OF THE TOWN OF CAPE CHARLES, VIRGINIA FOR UP TO A MAXIMUM OF $15 MILLION, PENDING APPRAISED VALUES AND REGULATORY APPROVALS. THE COMBINED SYSTEMS SERVE 2,300 CUSTOMERS.
