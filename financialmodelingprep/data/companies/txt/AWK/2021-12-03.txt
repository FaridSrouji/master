AMERICAN WATER CEO WALTER LYNCH SET TO SPEAK AT BANK OF AMERICA 2021 WATER CONFERENCE. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE NATION'S LARGEST PUBLICLY-TRADED WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER WALTER LYNCH WILL SPEAK AT THE BANK OF AMERICA 2021 WATER CONFERENCE ON DECEMBER 6, TO BE HELD IN A VIRTUAL SETTING. ON MONDAY, DECEMBER 6TH, LYNCH WILL DELIVER A PRESENTATION COVERING THE CURRENT STATE OF THE WATER INDUSTRY.
