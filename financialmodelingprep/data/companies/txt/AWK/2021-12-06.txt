ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER SUPPORTS INCLUSION & DIVERSITY PROJECTS THROUGH NEW GRANT PROGRAM; OVER $18,000 AWARDED TO 14 ILLINOIS ORGANIZATIONS. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--OVER $18,000 WILL BE SHARED BY 14 ORGANIZATIONS THROUGH ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER'S INCLUSION AND DIVERSITY GRANT PROGRAM. THE PROGRAM, WHICH IS A NEW INITIATIVE FOR ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER, PROVIDES FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS SEEKING TO DEVELOP AND IMPLEMENT PROGRAMS, TRAINING AND/OR COMMUNITY-RELATED PROJECTS THAT AIM TO PROMOTE AND FOSTER INCLUSION AND DIVERSITY IN THEIR COMMUNITY.
