AMERICAN WATER DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. CAMDEN, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--AMERICAN WATER WORKS COMPANY, INC. (NYSE: AWK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.6550 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MARCH 1, 2023, TO ALL SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEBRUARY 7, 2023.
