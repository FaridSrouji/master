THE AMERICAN WATER CHARITABLE FOUNDATION IS A 501(C)(3) NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION ESTABLISHED BY AMERICAN WATER, THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY.
