MISSOURI AMERICAN WATER AND USEPA OFFER TIPS TO HELP CUSTOMERS FIND COSTLY WATER LEAKS. JOPLIN, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MISSOURI AMERICAN WATER AND THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (USEPA) WANT TO INFORM CUSTOMERS ON WAYS TO IDENTIFY AND FIX WATER LEAKS IN THEIR HOME OR PLACE OF BUSINESS DURING “FIX A LEAK WEEK,” WHICH BEGAN TODAY. ACCORDING TO THE USEPA, THE AVERAGE HOME'S LEAKS RESULT IN NEARLY 10,000 GALLONS OF WATER WASTED PER YEAR, WHICH IS ENOUGH TO WASH MORE THAN 300 LOADS OF LAUNDRY.
