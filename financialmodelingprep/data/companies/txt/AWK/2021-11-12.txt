AMERICAN WATER RECOGNIZED BY MILITARY FRIENDLY® AND MILITARY TIMES MAGAZINE FOR INDUSTRY LEADING SUPPORT OF VETERANS. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE NATION'S LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS EARNED THE 2022 MILITARY FRIENDLY® TOP 10 GOLD EMPLOYER DESIGNATION AND WAS ALSO RECOGNIZED BY MILITARY TIMES FOR ITS INDUSTRY LEADING EFFORTS ON HIRING AND SUPPORTING OUR NATION'S MILITARY VETERANS. “WE ARE PROUD TO BE RECOGNIZED BY MILITARY FRIENDLY® AND MILITARY TIMES FOR THESE PRESTIGIOUS AWARDS AND RECOGNITIONS,” SAID WALTER LYNCH, AME
