AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS REGULATED OPERATIONS IN NEW YORK TO LIBERTY UTILITIES. PRESS RELEASE - AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS REGULATED OPERATIONS IN NEW YORK TO LIBERTY UTILITIES.AMERICAN WATER WORKS COMPANY INC - PARTIES HAVE AGREED TO A SALE PRICE OF APPROXIMATELY $608 MILLION IN CASH, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY ADJUSTMENTS.AMERICAN WATER WORKS COMPANY INC - COMPANY ALSO ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS KEYSTONE CLEARWATER SOLUTIONS OPERATIONS.AMERICAN WATER WORKS - TO SELL REGULATED NEW YORK OPERATIONS TO LIBERTY UTILITIES, REGULATED UTILITY BUSINESS OF PARENT ALGONQUIN POWER & UTILITIES CORP.AMERICAN WATER WORKS COMPANY INC - TO SELL ITS KEYSTONE CLEARWATER SOLUTIONS OPERATIONS TO SC WATER LLC.
