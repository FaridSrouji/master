STATE FARM SELECTS INTELLIFLO TO GIVE AGENTS A NEW, EASY-TO-USE DIGITAL WEALTH PLATFORM TO EXPAND FINANCIAL ADVICE SERVICES. OFFERING A BETTER INVESTING EXPERIENCE IS CRITICAL TO DEEPENING THE RELATIONSHIP STATE FARM AGENTS HAVE WITH CUSTOMERS, AS WELL AS EXPANDING THE SCOPE OF INVESTMENT PRODUCTS AND SERVICES AVAILABLE TO STATE FARM CUSTOMERS.
