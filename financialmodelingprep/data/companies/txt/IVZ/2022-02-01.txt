INVESCO LTD. ANNOUNCES CHANGES TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ATLANTA, FEB. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT NELSON PELTZ, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND FOUNDING PARTNER FOR TRIAN FUND MANAGEMENT, AND ED GARDEN, CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER AND FOUNDING PARTNER FOR TRIAN, HAVE INFORMED THE FIRM THAT THEY ARE STEPPING DOWN FROM THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, IN LIGHT OF THEIR APPOINTMENT TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JANUS HENDERSON GROUP PLC.
