INVESCO LTD. ANNOUNCES APRIL 30, 2021 ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. ATLANTA, MAY 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,459.0 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF 3.9% VERSUS PREVIOUS MONTH-END.
