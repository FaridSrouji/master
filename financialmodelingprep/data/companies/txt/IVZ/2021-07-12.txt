INVESCO LTD. ANNOUNCES JUNE 30, 2021 ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT. ATLANTA, JULY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AUM) OF $1,525.0 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF 1.3% VERSUS PREVIOUS MONTH-END.
