INVESCO REAL ESTATE INCOME TRUST INC. ACQUIRES TWO SELF-STORAGE PORTFOLIOS. DALLAS , JULY 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO REAL ESTATE INCOME TRUST INC. ("INREIT"), AN INSTITUTIONALLY MANAGED, PUBLIC NON-LISTED REIT, ANNOUNCED TODAY TWO SELF-STORAGE PORTFOLIO ACQUISITIONS CONSISTING OF FIVE PROPERTIES LOCATED IN BEND, OREGON AND CLARKSVILLE, TENNESSEE. THE COMBINED PURCHASE PRICE WAS $42 MILLION, BRINGING INREIT'S SELF-STORAGE PORTFOLIO TO OVER $128 MILLION.
