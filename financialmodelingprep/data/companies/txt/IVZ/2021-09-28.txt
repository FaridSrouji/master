INVESCO MORTGAGE CAPITAL INC. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND. ATLANTA, SEPT. 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO MORTGAGE CAPITAL INC. (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: IVR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.09 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021.
