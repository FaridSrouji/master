INVESCO ANNOUNCES CHANGES TO ITS ETF LINE-UP. UPGRADES TO EXISTING ETFS REFLECT INVESCO'S CONSISTENT IMPROVEMENTS TO THE INVESTOR EXPERIENCE ATLANTA , JUNE 23, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ), A LEADING GLOBAL ASSET MANAGEMENT FIRM, HAS ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL CHANGE THE UNDERLYING INDEX OF EIGHT OF ITS EXISTING EXCHANGE TRADED FUNDS (ETFS), AS WELL AS REDUCING THE FEE ON FIVE OF THOSE ETFS, TO PROVIDE A BETTER INVESTOR EXPERIENCE FOR ITS CLIENTS.
