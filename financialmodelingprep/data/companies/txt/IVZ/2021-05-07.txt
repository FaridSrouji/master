INVESCO ADVISERS ANNOUNCES PLANS FOR REORGANIZATION AND TENDER OFFER FOR INVESCO DYNAMIC CREDIT OPPORTUNITIES FUND. ATLANTA, MAY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO ADVISERS, INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ), ANNOUNCED TODAY PLANS FOR A REORGANIZATION AND TENDER OFFER FOR INVESCO DYNAMIC CREDIT OPPORTUNITIES FUND (NYSE: VTA) (THE "FUND").
