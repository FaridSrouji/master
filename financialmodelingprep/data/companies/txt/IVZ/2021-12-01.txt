INVESCO ADDS THREE DEFINED MATURITY BOND ETFS TO ITS BULLETSHARES SUITE. ATLANTA, DEC. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF EXCHANGE-TRADED FUNDS (ETFS), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE THREE NEWEST ADDITIONS TO ITS BULLETSHARES® ETF SUITE ARE AVAILABLE TO REPLACE THE ETFS MATURING AT YEAR-END 2021.
