INVESCO LTD. DECLARES CASH DIVIDEND FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2019, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 2, 2019; DECLARES PREFERRED CASH DIVIDEND FOR THE PERIOD FROM SEPTEMBER 1, 2019 THROUGH NOVEMBER 30, 2019, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 2, 2019. INVESCO LTD. ANNOUNCED A THIRD QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.31 PER SHARE TO HOLDERS OF COMMON SHARES. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 2, 2019, TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 12, 2019, WITH AN EX-DIVIDEND DATE OF NOVEMBER 8, 2019.THE COMPANY IS ANNOUNCING A PREFERRED CASH DIVIDEND OF $14.75 PER SHARE REPRESENTING THE PERIOD FROM SEPTEMBER 1, 2019 THROUGH NOVEMBER 30, 2019, AND TOTALING $59.2 MILLION. THE PREFERRED DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 2, 2019 TO PREFERRED SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 15, 2019.
