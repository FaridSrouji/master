INVESCO ANNOUNCES FINAL CLOSE OF INVESCO CREDIT PARTNERS FUND II. ATLANTA , JULY 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ), A LEADING GLOBAL ASSET MANAGEMENT FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FINAL CLOSE OF INVESCO CREDIT PARTNERS FUND II (THE "FUND"), A GLOBAL DISTRESSED CREDIT AND SPECIAL SITUATIONS FUND.
