INVESCO REAL ESTATE INCOME TRUST INC. AND SKYLIGHT REAL ESTATE PARTNERS ACQUIRE MULTIFAMILY PROPERTY IN WEST ESSEX, NJ SUBMARKET. DALLAS , MAY 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO REAL ESTATE INCOME TRUST INC. ("INREIT"), AN INSTITUTIONALLY MANAGED, PUBLIC NON-LISTED REIT, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT ACQUIRED A MAJORITY INTEREST IN EVERLY ROSELAND, A 384,648 SQUARE FOOT, 360-UNIT GARDEN-STYLE MULTIFAMILY COMMUNITY ACROSS 30 TWO-STORY BUILDINGS, FOLLOWING A PARTIAL SALE TO AN AFFILIATE. THE PROPERTY IS LOCATED AT 28 NOB HILL ROAD IN ROSELAND, NEW JERSEY AND IT IS 95% LEASED.
