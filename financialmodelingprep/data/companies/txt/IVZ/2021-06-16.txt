INVESCO ADVISERS ANNOUNCES INVESTMENT POLICY CHANGE FOR INVESCO SENIOR INCOME TRUST. ATLANTA, JUNE 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO ADVISERS, INC. (THE "ADVISER"), A SUBSIDIARY OF INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES (THE "BOARD") OF INVESCO SENIOR INCOME TRUST (NYSE: VVR) (THE "FUND") RECENTLY APPROVED CHANGES TO THE FUND'S INVESTMENT POLICY EFFECTIVE ON OR ABOUT JUNE 16, 2021.
INVESCO 2020 CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY REPORT HIGHLIGHTS INVESCO'S COMMITMENT TO ESG AND DIVERSITY & INCLUSION. ATLANTA, JUNE 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ), A LEADING GLOBAL INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT FIRM, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE FINDINGS OF ITS 2020 CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY (CSR) REPORT, WHICH HIGHLIGHTS SEVERAL AREAS, INCLUDING THE FIRM'S RESPONSE TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC; EFFORTS TO FURTHER IMPROVE DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION; COMMITMENT TO THE ENVIRONMENT; AND PROGRESS WITH REGARD TO ESG.
