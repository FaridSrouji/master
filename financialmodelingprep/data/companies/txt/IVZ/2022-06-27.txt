INVESCO MORTGAGE CAPITAL INC. ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND AND PROVIDES UPDATE ON PORTFOLIO, LIQUIDITY AND BOOK VALUE. ATLANTA , JUNE 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO MORTGAGE CAPITAL INC. (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: IVR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.90 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2022.  THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JULY 27, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JULY 11, 2022, WITH AN EX-DIVIDEND DATE OF JULY 8, 2022.
