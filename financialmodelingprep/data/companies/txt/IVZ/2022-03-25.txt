INVESCO ADVISERS, INC. ANNOUNCES INCREASE IN MONTHLY DISTRIBUTION FOR INVESCO SENIOR INCOME TRUST. ATLANTA , MARCH 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO ADVISERS, INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES (THE "BOARD") OF INVESCO SENIOR INCOME TRUST (NYSE: VVR) (THE "FUND") APPROVED AN INCREASE IN THE MONTHLY DISTRIBUTION AMOUNT PAYABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS PURSUANT TO THE FUND'S MANAGED DISTRIBUTION PLAN (THE "PLAN").
