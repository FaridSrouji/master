LEAVE DILEMMA: NEW STUDY BY THE HARTFORD FINDS NEARLY ONE-THIRD OF U.S. WORKERS CONCERNED ABOUT TAKING A LEAVE OF ABSENCE. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEW RESEARCH FROM THE HARTFORD SHOWED THERE IS A PERCEIVED STIGMA ASSOCIATED WITH TAKING A LEAVE OF ABSENCE FROM WORK.
