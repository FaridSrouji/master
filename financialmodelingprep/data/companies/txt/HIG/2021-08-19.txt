THE HARTFORD NAMES SHAHZAD AHMAD HEAD OF WESTERN DIVISION FIELD SALES AND UNDERWRITING FOR MIDDLE AND LARGE COMMERCIAL. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HARTFORD HAS APPOINTED SHAHZAD AHMAD HEAD OF THE WESTERN DIVISION FOR THE COMPANY'S MIDDLE AND LARGE COMMERCIAL INSURANCE BUSINESS. AHMAD NOW LEADS THE SALES AND UNDERWRITING OPERATIONS FOR THE COMPANY'S INDEPENDENT AGENTS, BROKERS AND CUSTOMERS ACROSS 13 WESTERN STATES INCLUDING ALASKA, ARIZONA, CALIFORNIA, COLORADO, HAWAII, IDAHO, MONTANA, NEW MEXICO, NEVADA, OREGON, UTAH, WASHINGTON AND WYOMING.
