THE HARTFORD ANNOUNCES PRELIMINARY RESULTS FOR FIRST QUARTER 2023. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HARTFORD (NYSE: HIG) TODAY ANNOUNCED PRELIMINARY EARNINGS ESTIMATES FOR FIRST QUARTER 2023, INCLUDING NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF $530 MILLION, OR $1.66 PER DILUTED SHARE, AND CORE EARNINGS* OF $536 MILLION, OR $1.68 PER DILUTED SHARE*.
