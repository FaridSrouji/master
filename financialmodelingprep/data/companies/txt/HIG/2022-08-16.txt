THE HARTFORD APPOINTS EDMUND REESE TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HARTFORD TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF EDMUND REESE TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE OCT. 17, 2022. HE WILL SERVE ON THE BOARD'S FINANCE, INVESTMENT AND RISK MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE AS WELL AS THE AUDIT COMMITTEE. “REESE IS AN ACCOMPLISHED FINANCIAL LEADER WITH DEEP EXPERIENCE IN INVESTMENTS, STRATEGY, OPERATIONS AND PRODUCT LAUNCHES DURING HIS TIME WITH SEVERAL TRUSTED AND ADMIRED COMPANIES,” SAID THE HARTFORD'S CHAIRMAN AND CEO CHRISTOPHER
