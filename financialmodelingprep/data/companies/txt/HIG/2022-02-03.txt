THE HARTFORD ANNOUNCES FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HARTFORD (NYSE: HIG) TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER AND YEAR ENDED DEC. 31, 2021. “IN 2021, THE HARTFORD DELIVERED STRONG FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE WITH MEANINGFUL GROWTH AND P&C MARGIN EXPANSION DRIVEN BY STELLAR RESULTS IN COMMERCIAL LINES AND A SIGNIFICANT CONTRIBUTION FROM PARTNERSHIP INVESTMENT RETURNS.
