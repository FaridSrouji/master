THE HARTFORD ANNOUNCES EXPANDED ROLES FOR CIO DEEPA SONI AND JOHN KINNEY, HEAD OF CLAIMS & OPERATIONS. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HARTFORD EXPANDED ROLES FOR TWO EXECUTIVES, DEEPA SONI AND JOHN KINNEY, WHO WILL REPORT DIRECTLY TO THE HARTFORD'S CHAIRMAN AND CEO CHRISTOPHER SWIFT EFFECTIVE AUG. 2. SONI, THE COMPANY'S CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER, NOW OVERSEES TECHNOLOGY, DATA, ANALYTICS & INFORMATION SECURITY. KINNEY'S RESPONSIBILITIES WERE RECENTLY BROADENED TO INCLUDE OPERATIONS IN ADDITION TO HIS ROLE OVERSEEING CLAIMS. SONI AND KINNEY ARE SUCCEEDING BILL BLOOM, WHO WILL RETIRE LAT
