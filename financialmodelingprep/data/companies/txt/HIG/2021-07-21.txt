THE HARTFORD DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS OF $0.35 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK AND $375 PER SHARE OF SERIES G PREFERRED STOCK. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HARTFORD'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $0.35 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE OCT. 4, 2021, TO COMMON STOCK SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPT. 1, 2021. THE BOARD ALSO DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF $375 ON EACH OF THE SERIES G PREFERRED STOCK (EQUIVALENT TO $0.375 PER DEPOSITORY SHARE) PAYABLE ON NOV. 15, 2021, TO SERIES G PREFERRED STOCK SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOV. 1, 2021. ABOUT THE HARTFORD
