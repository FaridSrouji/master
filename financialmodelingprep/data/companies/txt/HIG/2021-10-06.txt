THE HARTFORD HOSTS SECOND ANNUAL NATIONAL JUNIOR FIRE MARSHAL DAY; DEPUTIZES 65,000 NEW JUNIOR FIRE MARSHALS. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HARTFORD, WHICH HAS PROVIDED FIRE-SAFETY EDUCATION TO MORE THAN 111 MILLION CHILDREN SINCE 1947, DEPUTIZED MORE THAN 65,000 NEW JUNIOR FIRE MARSHALS IN ONE DAY AS PART OF ITS SECOND ANNUAL JUNIOR FIRE MARSHAL DAY. THE COMPANY CREATED THE LIVE, VIRTUAL FIRE-SAFETY EVENT IN 2020 TO ADDRESS THE CRITICAL LACK OF IN-PERSON, FIRE-SAFETY EDUCATION AVAILABLE TO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL STUDENTS BECAUSE OF THE PANDEMIC.
