THE HARTFORD ANNOUNCES GOAL OF NET ZERO GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS FOR ALL OPERATIONS BY 2050. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HARTFORD TODAY ANNOUNCED A GOAL TO ACHIEVE NET ZERO GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS (GHGE) FOR ITS FULL RANGE OF BUSINESSES AND OPERATIONS BY 2050, IN ALIGNMENT WITH THE PARIS CLIMATE ACCORD. THIS GOAL IS IN ADDITION TO THE COMPANY'S EXISTING TARGETS TO OPERATE WITH 100% RENEWABLE-ENERGY-SOURCE CONSUMPTION FOR ITS FACILITIES BY 2030 AND TO REDUCE SELECT GHGE BY AT LEAST 2.1% EACH YEAR STARTING IN 2015 FOR A TOTAL REDUCTION OF 46.2% BY 2037. “THE HARTFORD IS PROUD
