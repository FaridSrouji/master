EMPLOYEE EXHAUSTION: THE HARTFORD SURVEY FINDS WIDENING GAP IN BURNOUT RATES OF WOMEN AND MEN; BURNED-OUT U.S. WORKERS MORE LIKELY TO SEEK NEW JOBS. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEW RESEARCH FROM THE HARTFORD FOUND A WIDENING GAP BETWEEN MEN AND WOMEN IN WORKPLACE BURNOUT RATES, AS THE OVERALL EXHAUSTION LEVEL REMAINS AT 61%.
