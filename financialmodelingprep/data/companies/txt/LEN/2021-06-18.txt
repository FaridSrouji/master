LENNAR CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS. MIAMI, JUNE 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LENNAR CORPORATION (NYSE: LEN AND LEN.B), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HOMEBUILDERS, ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.25 PER SHARE FOR BOTH CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON JULY 19, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 2, 2021. ABOUT LENNAR LENNAR CORPORATION, FOUNDED IN 1954, IS ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING BUILDERS OF QUALITY HOMES FOR ALL GENERATIONS.
