LEVEL HOME INC. ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED $71 MILLION IN FUNDING FROM LENNAR CORPORATION, WALMART INC., HUT 8 VENTURES MANAGEMENT LLC. LEVEL HOME INC ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED OVER $71,000,000 IN A ROUND OF FUNDING ON OCTOBER 15, 2019. THE TRANSACTION INCLUDED PARTICIPATION FROM HUT 8 VENTURES MANAGEMENT LLC, NEW INVESTORS LENNAR CORPORATION (NYSE:LEN) AND WALMART INC. (NYSE:WMT).
