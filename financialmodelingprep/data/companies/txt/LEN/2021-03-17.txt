LENNAR ANNOUNCES $4 BILLION SINGLE FAMILY RENTAL PLATFORM WITH CENTERBRIDGE PARTNERS AS LEAD INVESTOR. MIAMI, MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LENNAR CORPORATION (NYSE:LEN AND LEN.B), ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST HOMEBUILDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FORMATION OF THE UPWARD AMERICA VENTURE.  THE BUSINESS WILL INITIALLY BE CAPITALIZED WITH A TOTAL EQUITY COMMITMENT OF $1.25 BILLION LED BY CENTERBRIDGE ALONGSIDE ALLIANZ REAL ESTATE AND OTHER HIGH QUALITY INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS.
