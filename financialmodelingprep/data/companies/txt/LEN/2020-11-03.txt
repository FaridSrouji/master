LENNAR'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS ELECTS OFFICERS TO NEW EXECUTIVE POSITIONS. MIAMI, NOV. 3, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LENNAR CORPORATION (NYSE: LEN AND LEN.B), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HOMEBUILDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED JON JAFFE AS CO-CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND CO-PRESIDENT OF LENNAR, TITLES HE WILL NOW SHARE WITH RICK BECKWITT....
