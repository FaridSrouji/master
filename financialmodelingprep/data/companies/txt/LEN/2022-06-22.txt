LENNAR CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS. MIAMI , JUNE 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LENNAR CORPORATION (NYSE: LEN AND LEN.B), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HOMEBUILDERS, ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.375 PER SHARE FOR BOTH CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON JULY 21, 2022 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 7, 2022. ABOUT LENNAR LENNAR CORPORATION, FOUNDED IN 1954, IS ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING BUILDERS OF QUALITY HOMES FOR ALL GENERATIONS.
