LMC NO. 8 ON NMHC'S TOP 50 DEVELOPERS LIST. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  LMC , A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF LENNAR CORPORATION AND A LEADER IN APARTMENT DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED AS THE EIGHTH-LARGEST DEVELOPER OF MULTIFAMILY HOUSING, ACCORDING TO THE NATIONAL MULTIFAMILY HOUSING COUNCIL'S 2021 TOP 50 DEVELOPERS LIST.
