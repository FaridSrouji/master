LMC ANNOUNCES START OF LEASING FOR RESIDENCES KIERLAND. MID-RISE COMMUNITY FEATURES 290 APARTMENT HOMES AND TOWNHOMES IN SCOTTSDALE   SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ. , JULY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  LMC , A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF LENNAR CORPORATION [NYSE: LEN] AND A LEADER IN APARTMENT DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE START OF LEASING AT RESIDENCES KIERLAND, A MID-RISE SPA-CENTRIC COMMUNITY IN SCOTTSDALE'S BURGEONING KIERLAND NEIGHBORHOOD.
