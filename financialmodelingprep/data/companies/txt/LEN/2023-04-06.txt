QUARTERRA MULTIFAMILY ANNOUNCES START OF LEASING AT ROWAN APARTMENTS. QUARTERRA'S FIRST NEVADA COMMUNITY PROVIDES MODERN, ACTIVE LIVING EXPERIENCE JUST OUTSIDE RENO SPARKS, NEV. , APRIL 6, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUARTERRA MULTIFAMILY , A SUBSIDIARY OF LENNAR CORPORATION AND A VERTICALLY INTEGRATED MULTIFAMILY APARTMENT BUILDER, DEVELOPER, AND ASSET MANAGER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE START OF LEASING AT ROWAN , A SOPHISTICATED COMMUNITY IN THE BURGEONING RENO, NEV.
