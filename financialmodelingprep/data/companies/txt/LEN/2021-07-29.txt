LMC CLOSES ON LAND FOR NORTHERN VIRGINIA DEVELOPMENT. WASHINGTON, JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LMC , A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF LENNAR CORPORATION AND A LEADER IN APARTMENT DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAND CLOSING FOR PAXTON, A LUXURY MIXED-USE COMMUNITY IN TYSONS, A NORTHERN VIRGINIA SUBURB OF WASHINGTON, D.C. THE HIGH-RISE COMMUNITY WILL FEATURE 447 APARTMENT HOMES AND OFFER 14,713 SQUARE FEET OF RETAIL SPACE.
