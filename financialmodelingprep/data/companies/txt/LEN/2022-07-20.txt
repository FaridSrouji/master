LMC ANNOUNCES GROUNDBREAKING AT ARTEMAS. COMMUNITY TO OFFER 396 APARTMENT HOMES NORTH OF BOSTON BOSTON , JULY 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  LMC , A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF LENNAR CORPORATION (NYSE:LEN) AND A LEADER IN APARTMENT DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE GROUNDBREAKING AT ARTEMAS , A MID-RISE APARTMENT COMMUNITY IN EVERETT, MASS., AN ACTIVE SUBURB JUST 4 MILES NORTH OF DOWNTOWN BOSTON.
