LMC SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF MARKETING BETH TUTTLE ACCEPTED INTO FORBES COMMUNICATIONS COUNCIL. DALLAS, AUG. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  LMC , A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF LENNAR CORPORATION AND A LEADER IN APARTMENT DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF MARKETING BETH TUTTLE HAS BEEN ACCEPTED INTO FORBES COMMUNICATIONS COUNCIL. SHE JOINS A RENOWNED GROUP IN THIS INVITATION-ONLY COMMUNITY FOR EXECUTIVES IN COMMUNICATIONS, MARKETING AND PUBLIC RELATIONS.
