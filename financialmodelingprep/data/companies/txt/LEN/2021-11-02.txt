LMC ANNOUNCES OPENING OF CAMBER APARTMENTS. DENVER, NOV. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  LMC , A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF LENNAR CORPORATION AND A LEADER IN APARTMENT DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF CAMBER, A BRAND NEW APARTMENT COMMUNITY IN THE BURGEONING DENVER GATEWAY CENTER, IDEALLY SITUATED BETWEEN DOWNTOWN DENVER AND DENVER INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (DIA). THE GARDEN-STYLE COMMUNITY, WHICH CONSISTS OF 240 APARTMENT HOMES, IS LOCATED IN THE AEROTROPOLIS PLANNING DISTRICT – A 53-SQUARE MILE AREA POISED TO BECOME ONE OF THE DENVER METRO AREA'S MOST PROLIFIC EMPLOYMENT HUBS.
