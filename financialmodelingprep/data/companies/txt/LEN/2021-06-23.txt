LMC HUMAN RESOURCES DIRECTOR NICOLE BROWN ACCEPTED INTO FORBES HUMAN RESOURCES COUNCIL. DALLAS, JUNE 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  LMC , A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF LENNAR CORPORATION AND A LEADER IN APARTMENT DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT HUMAN RESOURCES DIRECTOR NICOLE BROWN HAS BEEN ACCEPTED INTO THE FORBES HUMAN RESOURCES COUNCIL, AN INVITATION-ONLY COMMUNITY FOR HR EXECUTIVES ACROSS INDUSTRIES. BROWN WAS VETTED AND SELECTED BY A REVIEW COMMITTEE BASED ON THE DEPTH AND DIVERSITY OF HER EXPERIENCE.
