2023 TRAVELERS CHAMPIONSHIP GENERATES MORE THAN $3 MILLION FOR CHARITY. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE 2023 TRAVELERS CHAMPIONSHIP GENERATED A TOURNAMENT-RECORD AMOUNT FOR CHARITY – MORE THAN $3 MILLION FOR MORE THAN 160 NONPROFITS. “HISTORY-MAKING MOMENTS – BOTH ON AND OFF THE COURSE – ARE WHAT THIS TOURNAMENT IS KNOWN FOR, AND AS A PGA TOUR DESIGNATED EVENT, THE 2023 TRAVELERS CHAMPIONSHIP CERTAINLY LIVED UP TO THAT REPUTATION,” SAID ALAN SCHNITZER, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AT TRAVE.
