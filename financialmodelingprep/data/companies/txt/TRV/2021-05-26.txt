TRAVELERS' WILDFIRE DEFENSE SERVICES ENDORSEMENT NOW AVAILABLE TO COLORADO CUSTOMERS. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ADDED A WILDFIRE DEFENSE SERVICES ENDORSEMENT TO ITS COLORADO HOME AND LANDLORD POLICIES AT NO EXTRA COST TO CUSTOMERS. AGRIBUSINESS CUSTOMERS IN THE STATE ARE ALSO ELIGIBLE. THROUGH ITS PARTNERSHIP WITH WILDFIRE DEFENSE SYSTEMS (WDS), TRAVELERS OFFERS WILDFIRE LOSS PREVENTION SERVICES – INCLUDING APPLYING NON-HAZARDOUS AND BIODEGRADABLE FIRE RETARDANT, RELOCATING COMBUSTIBLE MATERIAL, AND TAPING
