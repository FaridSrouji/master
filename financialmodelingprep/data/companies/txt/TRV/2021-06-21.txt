TRAVELERS ANNOUNCES THE START OF THE 2021 TRAVELERS CHAMPIONSHIP. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE START OF THE 2021 TRAVELERS CHAMPIONSHIP AT TPC RIVER HIGHLANDS IN CROMWELL, CONNECTICUT. THIS YEAR'S TOURNAMENT FEATURES MANY OF THE PGA TOUR'S TOP PLAYERS, INCLUDING DUSTIN JOHNSON, THE DEFENDING CHAMPION AND THE NO. 2-RANKED PLAYER IN THE WORLD, AS WELL AS BRYSON DECHAMBEAU, PHIL MICKELSON, PATRICK REED, BROOKS KOEPKA AND BUBBA WATSON, AMONG OTHERS.
