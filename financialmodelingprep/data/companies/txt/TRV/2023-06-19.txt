TRAVELERS ANNOUNCES THE START OF THE 2023 TRAVELERS CHAMPIONSHIP. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE START OF THE 2023 TRAVELERS CHAMPIONSHIP AT TPC RIVER HIGHLANDS IN CROMWELL, CONNECTICUT. THIS YEAR'S TOURNAMENT, WHICH IS A PGA TOUR DESIGNATED EVENT WITH A TOURNAMENT-RECORD PURSE, FEATURES EIGHT OF THE TOP 10 PLAYERS IN THE OFFICIAL WORLD GOLF RANKING, LED BY NO. 1 SCOTTIE SCHEFFLER. THE FIELD ALSO INCLUDES JON RAHM (NO. 2), RORY MCILROY (NO. 3), PATRICK CANTLAY (NO. 4), VIKTOR HOVLAND (NO. 5), DE.
