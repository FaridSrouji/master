TRŌV TECHNOLOGY ASSETS ACQUIRED BY TRAVELERS. SAN FRANCISCO, FEB. 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRŌV, ONE OF THE EARLIEST AND MOST WIDELY RECOGNIZED INSURTECHS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS TECHNOLOGY HAS BEEN ACQUIRED BY THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV), A LEADING PROVIDER OF PROPERTY CASUALTY INSURANCE FOR AUTO, HOME AND BUSINESS. MOST MEMBERS OF TRŌV'S TEAM HAVE ALSO JOINED THE COMPANY.
