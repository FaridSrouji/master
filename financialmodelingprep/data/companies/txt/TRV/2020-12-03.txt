TRAVELERS TO ACQUIRE INSURAMATCH. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS AGREED TO ACQUIRE INSURAMATCH, LLC, A DIGITAL INDEPENDENT INSURANCE AGENCY, FROM THE PLYMOUTH ROCK GROUP OF COMPANIES. THE TRANSACTION, WHICH IS SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS, IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. TERMS WERE NOT DISCLOSED.
