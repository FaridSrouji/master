TRAVELERS HONORS 2023 PERSONAL INSURANCE AGENT OF THE YEAR WINNERS. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RECIPIENTS OF ITS 2023 PERSONAL INSURANCE AGENT OF THE YEAR AWARD. THE HONOREES WERE RECOGNIZED FOR THEIR EXCELLENT CUSTOMER SERVICE, DEDICATION TO THEIR COMMUNITIES AND COMMITMENT TO TRAVELERS.
