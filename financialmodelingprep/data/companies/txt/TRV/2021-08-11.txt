SIMPLY BUSINESS OFFERS INSURANCE TO THIRD-PARTY SELLERS THROUGH AMAZON INSURANCE ACCELERATOR. BOSTON , AUG. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIMPLY BUSINESS, INC ., A BOSTON-BASED DIGITAL INSURANCE AGENCY FOCUSED ON SMALL BUSINESSES, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS PARTICIPATION IN THE NEWLY CREATED AMAZON INSURANCE ACCELERATOR TO DISTRIBUTE PRODUCT LIABILITY INSURANCE SOLUTIONS TO ITS THIRD-PARTY SELLERS.
