NEXT PHASE OF GRID TRANSFORMATION PLAN APPROVED FOR DOMINION ENERGY VIRGINIA CUSTOMERS. RICHMOND, VA., JAN. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE VIRGINIA STATE CORPORATION COMMISSION (SCC) TODAY APPROVED, SUBJECT TO CERTAIN REQUIREMENTS, PHASE II OF THE GRID TRANSFORMATION PLAN (GT PLAN), THE COMPANY'S TEN-YEAR PLAN TO TRANSFORM THE ELECTRIC DISTRIBUTION GRID.
