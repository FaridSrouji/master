DOMINION ENERGY ENVIRONMENTAL GRANT APPLICATIONS NOW OPEN TO NONPROFIT, EDUCATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS. RICHMOND, VA., AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY IS ENCOURAGING NONPROFIT AND EDUCATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS TO APPLY FOR $1.5 MILLION IN ENVIRONMENTAL GRANTS TO BE DISTRIBUTED THIS YEAR THROUGH THE DOMINION ENERGY CHARITABLE FOUNDATION.
