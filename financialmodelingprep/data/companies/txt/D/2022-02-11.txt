DOMINION ENERGY AGREES TO SELL WEST VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS DISTRIBUTION COMPANY TO ULLICO. RICHMOND, VA., FEB. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS EXECUTED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS WEST VIRGINIA NATURAL GAS UTILITY – HOPE GAS, INC. (DOMINION ENERGY WEST VIRGINIA OR DEWV) – TO ULLICO INC.'S INFRASTRUCTURE FUND FOR $690 MILLION.

DOMINION ENERGY ANNOUNCES FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2021 EARNINGS. RICHMOND, VA., FEB. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN UNAUDITED NET INCOME DETERMINED IN ACCORDANCE WITH GENERALLY ACCEPTED ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES (REPORTED EARNINGS) FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED DEC. 31, 2021, OF $1.3 BILLION ($1.63 PER SHARE) COMPARED WITH A NET GAIN OF $682 MILLION ($0.82 PER SHARE) FOR THE SAME PERIOD IN 2020.
