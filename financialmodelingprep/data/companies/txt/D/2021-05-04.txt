DOMINION ENERGY ANNOUNCES SURRY POWER STATION RECEIVES REGULATORY APPROVAL TO EXTEND OPERATIONS; HELPS VIRGINIA MEET ITS CLEAN ENERGY GOALS. RICHMOND, VA., MAY 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE NUCLEAR REGULATORY COMMISSION (NRC) HAS APPROVED DOMINION ENERGY'S, (NYSE: D), APPLICATION TO RENEW SURRY POWER STATION'S OPERATING LICENSES FOR AN ADDITIONAL 20-YEARS.
DOMINION ENERGY ANNOUNCES FIRST-QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS. RICHMOND, VA., MAY 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN UNAUDITED NET INCOME DETERMINED IN ACCORDANCE WITH GENERALLY ACCEPTED ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES (REPORTED EARNINGS) FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2021, OF $1.0 BILLION ($1.23 PER SHARE) COMPARED WITH A NET LOSS OF $270 MILLION ($0.34 PER SHARE) FOR THE SAME PERIOD IN 2020.
