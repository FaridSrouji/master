DOMINION ENERGY SECURES PIVOTAL BVLOS WAIVER FOR POWER FACILITY INSPECTIONS USING SKYDIO X2. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) AND SKYDIO, THE LEADING U.S. DRONE MANUFACTURER AND WORLD LEADER IN AUTONOMOUS FLIGHT, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FEDERAL AVIATION ADMINISTRATION (FAA) HAS GRANTED DOMINION ENERGY A PIVOTAL APPROVAL TO FLY SKYDIO DRONES BEYOND VISUAL LINE OF SIGHT (BVLOS) TO INSPECT POWER GENERATION FACILITIES IN SEVEN STATES.
