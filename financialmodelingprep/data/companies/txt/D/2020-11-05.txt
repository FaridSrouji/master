DOMINION ENERGY ANNOUNCES THIRD-QUARTER EARNINGS. RICHMOND, VA., NOV. 5, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) TODAY ANNOUNCED UNAUDITED REPORTED EARNINGS DETERMINED IN ACCORDANCE WITH GENERALLY ACCEPTED ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES (REPORTED EARNINGS) FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED SEPT. 30, 2020, OF $356 MILLION ($0.41 PER SHARE)...
