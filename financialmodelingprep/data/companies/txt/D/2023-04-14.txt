NEW SOLAR AND ENERGY STORAGE PROJECTS APPROVED FOR DOMINION ENERGY VIRGINIA CUSTOMERS. RICHMOND, VA. , APRIL 14, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE VIRGINIA STATE CORPORATION COMMISSION (SCC) APPROVED TODAY NEARLY TWO DOZEN NEW SOLAR AND ENERGY STORAGE PROJECTS TO HELP MEET THE GROWING NEEDS OF DOMINION ENERGY VIRGINIA CUSTOMERS.
