DOMINION ENERGY CHARITABLE FOUNDATION COMMITS OVER $1 MILLION TO 7 UNIVERSITIES TO SUPPORT PROMESA: HISPANIC HIGHER EDUCATION INITIATIVE. RICHMOND, VA. , SEPT. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE DOMINION ENERGY CHARITABLE FOUNDATION HAS COMMITTED $1,050,000 OVER THE NEXT THREE YEARS FOR THE NEW PROMESA: HISPANIC HIGHER EDUCATION INITIATIVE, WHICH WILL SUPPORT HISPANIC STUDENTS AT SEVEN COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES.
