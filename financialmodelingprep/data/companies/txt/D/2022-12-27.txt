DOMINION ENERGY ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS TO CONSERVE ENERGY. EXTREMELY COLD WEATHER CAUSES HIGH SYSTEM DEMAND RICHMOND, VA. , DEC. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS THE EVENING HOURS APPROACH AND TEMPERATURES DROP TO RECORD LOWS, DOMINION ENERGY ENCOURAGES CUSTOMERS IN VIRGINIA AND NORTH CAROLINA TO HELP REDUCE STRAIN ON THE ELECTRIC GRID BY CONSERVING ENERGY.
