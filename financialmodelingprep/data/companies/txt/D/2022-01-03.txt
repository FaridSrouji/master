DOMINION ENERGY ANNOUNCES CLOSING OF SALE OF QUESTAR PIPELINES TO SOUTHWEST GAS. RICHMOND, VA., DEC. 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS CLOSED THE SALE OF QUESTAR PIPELINES TO SOUTHWEST GAS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: SWX), IN A TRANSACTION VALUED AT $1.975 BILLION, INCLUDING THE ASSUMPTION OF $430 MILLION OF EXISTING DEBT.
