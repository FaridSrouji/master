DOMINION ENERGY VIRGINIA FILES FOR CUSTOMER RATE REDUCTION, SUBMITS LONG-TERM RELIABILITY PLAN TO STATE REGULATORS. RICHMOND, VA. , MAY 1, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY VIRGINIA SUBMITTED PLANS WITH THE VIRGINIA STATE CORPORATION COMMISSION (SCC) TODAY THAT, IF APPROVED, WOULD PROVIDE $7 - $14 IN MONTHLY SAVINGS FOR THE TYPICAL RESIDENTIAL CUSTOMER BEGINNING JULY 1.
