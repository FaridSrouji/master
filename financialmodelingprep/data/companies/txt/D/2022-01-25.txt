DOMINION ENERGY DONATES $1.5M TO ENVIRONMENTAL EDUCATION AND STEWARDSHIP INITIATIVES. RICHMOND, VA., JAN. 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE DOMINION ENERGY CHARITABLE FOUNDATION RECENTLY AWARDED $1.5 MILLION IN ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP GRANTS TO SUPPORT 115 COMMUNITY-BASED ORGANIZATIONS IN 10 STATES WORKING TO CREATE A CLEANER, GREENER WORLD AND TEACH CITIZENS YOUNG AND OLD TO BETTER CARE FOR THE OUTDOORS.
