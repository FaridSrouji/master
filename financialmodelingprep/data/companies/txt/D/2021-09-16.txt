DOMINION ENERGY, TRADE UNIONS ANNOUNCE COASTAL VIRGINIA OFFSHORE WIND PARTNERSHIP. RICHMOND, VA., SEPT. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY VIRGINIA, THE NABTU AND THEIR STATE AFFILIATE VIRGINIA BUILDING TRADES REAFFIRMED TODAY THEIR COMMITMENT TO THE PARTICIPATION OF A UNIONIZED WORKFORCE IN THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE COASTAL VIRGINIA OFFSHORE WIND (CVOW) PROJECT AND ALSO AFFIRMED THEIR COMMITMENT TO UTILIZING LOCAL WORKERS; THE HIRING, APPRENTICESHIP, AND TRAINING OF VETERANS; AND THE USE OF WORKERS FROM HISTORICALLY ECONOMICALLY DISADVANTAGED COMMUNITIES.
