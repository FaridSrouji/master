DOMINION ENERGY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS SETS 2022 DIVIDEND RATE. RICHMOND, VA., DEC. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) TODAY APPROVED A 2022 DIVIDEND OF $2.67 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, A 6% INCREASE ABOVE THE 2021 DIVIDEND RATE OF $2.52 PER SHARE.
