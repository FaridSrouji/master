DOMINION ENERGY ANNOUNCES SECOND-QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS. RICHMOND, VA., AUG. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN UNAUDITED NET INCOME DETERMINED IN ACCORDANCE WITH GENERALLY ACCEPTED ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES (REPORTED EARNINGS) FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED JUNE 30, 2021, OF $285 MILLION ($0.33 PER SHARE) COMPARED WITH A NET LOSS OF $1.2 BILLION ($1.52 PER SHARE) FOR THE SAME PERIOD IN 2020.
