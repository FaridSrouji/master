DOMINION ENERGY UTAH LAUNCHES NEW PROGRAM TO REDUCE CUSTOMERS' CARBON FOOTPRINT. SALT LAKE CITY , MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY CUSTOMERS IN UTAH AND IDAHO CAN NOW SIGN UP FOR CARBONRIGHT, A NEW AND AFFORDABLE WAY TO SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCE THEIR CARBON FOOTPRINT. THE PROGRAM WILL ALLOW CUSTOMERS TO OFFSET CARBON EMISSIONS FROM NATURAL GAS USE IN THEIR HOME OR BUSINESS BY SUPPORTING PROJECTS, INCLUDING IN UTAH, THAT REDUCE GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS.
