DOMINION ENERGY'S COASTAL VIRGINIA OFFSHORE WIND PROJECT ACHIEVES KEY REGULATORY MILESTONE; CONSISTENT WITH PROJECT TIMELINE. RICHMOND, VA., JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BUREAU OF OCEAN ENERGY MANAGEMENT (BOEM) WILL PUBLISH IN THE FEDERAL REGISTER ON FRIDAY THE NOTICE OF INTENT (NOI) FOR THE PROPOSED 2.6-GIGAWATT COASTAL VIRGINIA OFFSHORE WIND (CVOW) COMMERCIAL PROJECT, THE LARGEST PLANNED OFFSHORE WIND FARM IN THE UNITED STATES.
