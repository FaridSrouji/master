DOMINION ENERGY DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 63 CENTS. RICHMOND, VA., MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 63 CENTS PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.
