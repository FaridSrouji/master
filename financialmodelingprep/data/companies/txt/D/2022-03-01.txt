DOMINION ENERGY PARTNERS WITH THE HISPANIC ASSOCIATION OF COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES TO DEVELOP HISPANIC LEADERS FOR THE ENERGY INDUSTRY. RICHMOND, VA., MARCH 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP WITH THE HISPANIC ASSOCIATION OF COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES (HACU), SUPPORTING SEVEN HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS IN VIRGINIA, NORTH CAROLINA, CONNECTICUT, UTAH, AND PUERTO RICO.
