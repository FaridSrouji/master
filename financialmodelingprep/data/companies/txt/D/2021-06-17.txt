DOMINION ENERGY EDUCATIONAL EQUITY SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM AWARDS $500,000 TO 60 STUDENTS. RICHMOND, VA., JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIXTY STUDENTS FROM DIVERSE BACKGROUNDS MAKE UP THE FIRST CLASS OF DOMINION ENERGY EDUCATIONAL EQUITY SCHOLARS AND WILL RECEIVE OVER $500,0000 TO HELP PAY FOR EDUCATIONAL EXPENSES AS PART OF DOMINION ENERGY'S LARGER COMMITMENT TO AFRICAN AMERICAN AND OTHER UNDERREPRESENTED MINORITY STUDENTS.
