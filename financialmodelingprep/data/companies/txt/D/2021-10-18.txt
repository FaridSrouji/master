DOMINION ENERGY VIRGINIA, STATE CORPORATION COMMISSION STAFF, OFFICE OF ATTORNEY GENERAL, AND OTHER PARTIES FILE COMPREHENSIVE RATE SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT THAT PROVIDES SIGNIFICANT CUSTOMER BENEFITS. RICHMOND, VA., OCT. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY VIRGINIA, THE VIRGINIA STATE CORPORATION COMMISSION (SCC) STAFF, THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL, AND OTHER PARTIES TODAY FILED A COMPREHENSIVE SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT IN THE COMPANY'S PENDING TRIENNIAL BASE RATE CASE.
