DOMINION ENERGY PROPOSES NEW SOLAR AND ENERGY STORAGE PROJECTS FOR VIRGINIA CUSTOMERS. PROPOSED PROJECTS EXCEED 800 MEGAWATTS, ENOUGH TO POWER MORE THAN 200,000 HOMES PROJECTS WILL SUPPORT NEARLY 4,800 JOBS AND MORE THAN $920 MILLION IN ECONOMIC BENEFITS INCREASING USE OF RENEWABLES REDUCES FUEL COSTS FOR VIRGINIA CUSTOMERS RICHMOND, VA. , OCT. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN ITS THIRD ANNUAL CLEAN ENERGY FILING WITH THE VIRGINIA STATE CORPORATION COMMISSION (SCC), DOMINION ENERGY VIRGINIA ON FRIDAY PROPOSED NEARLY TWO DOZEN NEW SOLAR AND ENERGY STORAGE PROJECTS FOR VIRGINIA CUSTOMERS.
