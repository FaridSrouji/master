DIVERSE STUDENTS ENCOURAGED TO APPLY FOR NEW SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM BY DOMINION ENERGY. RICHMOND, VA., JAN. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY IS AWARDING $500,000 IN SCHOLARSHIPS IN 2021 TO ASSIST AFRICAN AMERICAN AND OTHER UNDERREPRESENTED MINORITY STUDENTS WHO RESIDE IN THE COMPANY'S SERVICE AREA. THE SCHOLARSHIPS WILL BE THE FIRST MADE UNDER THE DOMINION ENERGY...
