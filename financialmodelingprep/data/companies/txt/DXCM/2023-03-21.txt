MANITOBA BECOMES A PROVINCIAL TRAILBLAZER IN PROVIDING SIMPLIFIED ACCESS TO ADVANCED GLUCOSE MONITORS. BURNABY, BRITISH COLUMBIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DEXCOM, INC. (NASDAQ: DXCM), A GLOBAL LEADER IN REAL-TIME CONTINUOUS GLUCOSE MONITORING (RTCGM) FOR PEOPLE WITH DIABETES, ANNOUNCED THAT ITS DEXCOM G6 CGM SYSTEM WILL BE AVAILABLE TO PEOPLE OF ALL AGES WITH TYPE 1 AND TYPE 2 DIABETES IN MANITOBA WHO MEET ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA. EXPANDING COVERAGE FOR RTCGM TO INCLUDE ALL ELIGIBLE PATIENTS WITH TYPE 1 OR TYPE 2 DIABETES ESTABLISHES MANITOBA AS THE LEADER AMONG CANADIAN PROVINCES.
