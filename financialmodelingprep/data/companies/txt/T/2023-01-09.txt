AT&T TO RELEASE FOURTH-QUARTER 2022 EARNINGS JANUARY 25. DALLAS , JAN. 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AT&T* (NYSE:T) WILL RELEASE ITS FOURTH-QUARTER 2022 RESULTS BEFORE THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE OPENS ON WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 25, 2023. THE COMPANY'S EARNINGS RELEASE AND RELATED MATERIALS WILL BE AVAILABLE AT AT&T INVESTOR RELATIONS.
