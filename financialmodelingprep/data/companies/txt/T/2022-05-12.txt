BOONVILLE, INDIANA ANNOUNCES PLAN TO DELIVER FIBER-POWERED BROADBAND ACCESS TO MORE THAN 4,000 LOCATIONS VIA AT&T. PLAN CALLS FOR AT&T FIBER WITH SPEEDS OF UP TO 5 GIGS TO HELP CLOSE THE DIGITAL DIVIDE BOONVILLE, IND. , MAY 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOONVILLE, INDIANA TODAY ANNOUNCED A $4.4 MILLION PROJECT WITH AT&T* TO BUILD ITS STATE-OF-THE-ART FIBER NETWORK TO MORE THAN 4,000 CUSTOMER LOCATIONS THROUGHOUT THE CITY.
