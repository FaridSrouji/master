AT&T AND STATE OF LOUISIANA COLLABORATE ON PLAN TO DELIVER FIBER BROADBAND TO PORTIONS OF 13 LOUISIANA PARISHES. PLAN CALLS FOR AT&T FIBER TO HELP BRIDGE DIGITAL DIVIDE THROUGH AWARDS FROM LOUISIANA'S GUMBO GRANT PROGRAM BATON ROUGE, LA. , JULY 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE STATE OF LOUISIANA AND AT&T* ARE COLLABORATING ON A PLAN TO BRING A STATE-OF-THE-ART FIBER NETWORK TO NEARLY 9,800 HOMES, BUSINESSES AND FARMS IN PARTS OF THIRTEEN LOUISIANA PARISHES.
