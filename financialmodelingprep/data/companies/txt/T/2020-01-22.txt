CERES SAYS LAUNCHES CORPORATE ELECTRIC VEHICLE ALLIANCE. CERES ANNOUNCES LAUNCH OF NEW ALLIANCE, CORPORATE ELECTRIC VEHICLE ALLIANCE, TO HELP COMPANIES ACCELERATE TRANSITION TO ELECTRIC VEHICLES.NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION CERES SAYS AMAZON, AT&T, DHL, GENENTECH, IKEA NORTH AMERICA, SIEMENS ARE AMONG ALLIANCE’S FLAGSHIP MEMBERS.
