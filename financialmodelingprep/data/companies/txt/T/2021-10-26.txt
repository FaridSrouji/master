AT&T AND FORD TEAM UP TO BRING 5G TO FORD F-150 LIGHTNING PRODUCTION. DALLAS AND DEARBORN, MICH., OCT. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FORD MOTOR COMPANY AND AT&T ARE ENHANCING THE ROUGE ELECTRIC VEHICLE CENTER ON FORD'S HISTORIC DEARBORN ROUGE SITE – THE FUTURE OF EV PRODUCTION – BY OUTFITTING IT WITH HIGHLY SECURE, NEXT-GENERATION 5G CELLULAR CONNECTIVITY TO HELP BUILD THE NEW ALL-ELECTRIC FORD F-150 LIGHTNING PICKUP.
