DELAWARE COUNTY, INDIANA PLANS TO DELIVER AT&T FIBER-POWERED BROADBAND TO NEARLY 1,250 CUSTOMER LOCATIONS. PLANS CALL FOR AT&T FIBER WITH SPEEDS OF UP TO 5 GIGS TO HELP CLOSE THE DIGITAL DIVIDE MUNCIE, IND. , DEC. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DELAWARE COUNTY, INDIANA, TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS FOR A $2.3 MILLION PROJECT WITH AT&T* TO BUILD A STATE-OF-THE-ART NETWORK AND DELIVER AT&T FIBERSM  TO SOME 1,250 CUSTOMER LOCATIONS IN PARTS OF THE COUNTY.
