AT&T AND COX MEDIA GROUP REACH NEW MULTI-YEAR RETRANSMISSION CONSENT AGREEMENT. ATLANTA & DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AT&T* (NYSE: T) AND COX MEDIA GROUP (“CMG”) HAVE ENTERED INTO A NEW MULTI-YEAR RETRANSMISSION CONSENT AGREEMENT TO PROVIDE CMG-OWNED LOCAL BROADCAST STATIONS TO CUSTOMERS OF AT&T'S VIDEO PLATFORMS ACROSS THE COUNTRY. ALL CMG STATIONS HAVE RETURNED TO ANY IMPACTED AT&T HOMES. THE AGREEMENT INCLUDES RETRANSMISSION CONSENT FOR ALL CMG-OWNED STATIONS SERVING 20 NIELSEN MARKETS INCLUDING: ALEXANDRIA, LA ATLANTA, GA BINGHAMTON, NY BOSTON, MA CHARLOTTE, N
