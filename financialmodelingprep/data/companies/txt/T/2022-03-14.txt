AT&T CFO UPDATES SHAREHOLDERS AT DEUTSCHE BANK CONFERENCE. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PASCAL DESROCHES, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER OF AT&T INC.* (NYSE:T), SPOKE TODAY AT THE DEUTSCHE BANK MEDIA, INTERNET & TELECOM CONFERENCE WHERE HE PROVIDED AN UPDATE TO SHAREHOLDERS. DESROCHES REITERATED AT&T'S GROWTH STRATEGY — INTRODUCED AT THE COMPANY'S ANALYST & INVESTOR DAY ON MARCH 11 — FOR THE STANDALONE COMPANY FOLLOWING THE PENDING CLOSE OF THE WARNERMEDIA TRANSACTION.
