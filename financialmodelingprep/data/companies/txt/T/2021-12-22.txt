DISCOVERY, INC. ANNOUNCES EUROPEAN COMMISSION UNCONDITIONAL APPROVAL OF PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF AT&T'S WARNERMEDIA BUSINESS. NEW YORK, DEC. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DISCOVERY, INC. ("DISCOVERY" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: DISCA, DISCB, DISCK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION (EC) HAS GRANTED UNCONDITIONAL ANTITRUST CLEARANCE OF THE COMPANY'S PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF AT&T INC .'S (NYSE:T) WARNERMEDIA BUSINESS.
