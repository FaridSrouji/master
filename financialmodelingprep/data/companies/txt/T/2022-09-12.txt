AT&T CEO UPDATES SHAREHOLDERS AT GOLDMAN SACHS COMMUNICOPIA + TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JOHN STANKEY, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AT&T* INC. (NYSE:T), SPOKE TODAY AT THE GOLDMAN SACHS COMMUNICOPIA + TECHNOLOGY CONFERENCE WHERE HE PROVIDED AN UPDATE TO SHAREHOLDERS. STANKEY REITERATED THAT AT&T CONTINUES TO TAKE A DISCIPLINED AND RETURN-FOCUSED APPROACH TO GROWTH AND INVESTMENT AND MADE THE FOLLOWING POINTS: THE COMPANY CONTINUES TO ADD CUSTOMERS IN ITS STRATEGIC FOCUS AREAS OF 5G AND FIBER. STANKEY SHARED HE FEELS COMFORTABLE WITH AT&T'S BUSINESS
