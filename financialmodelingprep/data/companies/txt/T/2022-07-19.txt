STATE OF INDIANA AND AT&T COLLABORATE ON PLAN TO DELIVER FIBER-POWERED BROADBAND TO PORTIONS OF NINE INDIANA COUNTIES. PLAN CALLS FOR AT&T FIBER TO HELP BRIDGE DIGITAL DIVIDE THROUGH AWARDS FROM INDIANA'S NEXT LEVEL CONNECTIONS BROADBAND GRANT PROGRAM INDIANAPOLIS , JULY 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE STATE OF INDIANA AND AT&T* ARE COLLABORATING ON A PLAN TO BRING A STATE-OF-THE-ART FIBER NETWORK TO NEARLY 6,100 HOMES, BUSINESSES AND FARMS IN PARTS OF NINE INDIANA COUNTIES. THE $13 MILLION PROJECT IS CONTINGENT UPON A FINAL CONTRACT BETWEEN THE STATE OF INDIANA AND AT&T.
