AT&T ANNOUNCES DETAILS FOR COMPLETION OF SPIN-OFF AHEAD OF CLOSE OF WARNERMEDIA TRANSACTION. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY AT&T INC.* (NYSE:T) ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED A STOCK DIVIDEND TO EFFECT THE SPIN-OFF OF 100% OF AT&T'S INTEREST IN WARNERMEDIA TO AT&T'S SHAREHOLDERS. THE RECORD DATE FOR THE STOCK DIVIDEND IS THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 5, 2022. THIS STOCK DIVIDEND IS IN CONNECTION WITH THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED TRANSACTION TO COMBINE AT&T'S WARNERMEDIA BUSINESS WITH DISCOVERY, INC. (NASDAQ: DISCA, DISCB, DISCK).
