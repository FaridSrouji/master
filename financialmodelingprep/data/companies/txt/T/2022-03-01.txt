72% OF BUSINESSES LACK CLEAR HYBRID WORK STRATEGY ACCORDING TO THE 2022 FUTURE OF WORK STUDY. DALLAS AND MELBOURNE, AUSTRALIA, MARCH 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AT&T* AND DUBBER CORPORATION LIMITED (ASX: DUB) (DUBBER) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RESULTS OF PRIMARY MARKET RESEARCH GAUGING EXECUTIVE AND EMPLOYEE ATTITUDES TO COVID-DRIVEN HYBRID WORKING. THE STATE OF THE INDUSTRY: FUTURE OF WORK SURVEY WAS CREATED TO GAIN INSIGHTS FROM SENIOR EXECUTIVES REGARDING CURRENT AND FUTURE WORK MODELS, CHALLENGES POSED UNDER NEW WORKING MODELS, AND TECHNOLOGY ACCELERANTS TO AID CHANGE IN THE WAY THAT BUSINESSES CONDUCT WORK OUT TO 2024.
