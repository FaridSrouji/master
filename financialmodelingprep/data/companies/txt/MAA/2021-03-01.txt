MAA ANNOUNCES REGULAR QUARTERLY PREFERRED DIVIDEND. GERMANTOWN, TENN., MARCH 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MID-AMERICA APARTMENT COMMUNITIES, INC., OR MAA (NYSE: MAA), TODAY ANNOUNCED A FULL QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.0625 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF ITS 8.50% SERIES I CUMULATIVE REDEEMABLE PREFERRED STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 31, 2021...
