MAA ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND. GERMANTOWN, TENN. , MARCH 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MAA (NYSE: MAA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $1.0875 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON APRIL 29, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON APRIL 14, 2022.
