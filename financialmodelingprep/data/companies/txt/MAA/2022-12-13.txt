MAA ANNOUNCES 12% INCREASE TO QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND. GERMANTOWN, TENN. , DEC. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MAA (NYSE: MAA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $1.40 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON JANUARY 31, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JANUARY 13, 2023, REPRESENTING A 12% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR RATE.
