MAA ANNOUNCES 15% INCREASE TO QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND. GERMANTOWN, TENN. , MAY 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MAA (NYSE: MAA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $1.25 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON JULY 29, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JULY 15, 2022.
