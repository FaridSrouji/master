MAA ANNOUNCES INCREASE TO QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND. GERMANTOWN, TENN., DEC. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MAA (NYSE: MAA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $1.0875 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON JANUARY 31, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JANUARY 14, 2022, REPRESENTING A 6.1% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR RATE.
