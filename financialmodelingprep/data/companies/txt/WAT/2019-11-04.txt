WATERS ADVANCED POLYMER CHROMATOGRAPHY SYSTEM NOW FIRST FULLY SOLVENT-COMPATIBLE UPLC SYSTEM FOR GREATER FLEXIBILITY AND SPEED. WATERS CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS ACQUITY ADVANCED POLYMER CHROMATOGRAPHY (APC) SYSTEM IS THE FIRST FULLY SOLVENT-COMPATIBLE ULTRAPERFORMANCE LIQUID CHROMATOGRAPHY (UPLC)/ULTRA-HIGH PERFORMANCE SIZE EXCLUSION CHROMATOGRAPHY (UHPSEC) SYSTEM TO PERFORM SIZE EXCLUSION, GRADIENT POLYMER ELUTION AND SOLVENT COMPATIBLE REVERSED-PHASE LIQUID CHROMATOGRAPHIC SEPARATIONS ON A SINGLE PLATFORM. THE ALL-IN-ONE SYSTEM GIVES RESEARCH SCIENTISTS GREATER ANALYTICAL VERSATILITY AND SPEED WHEN CONDUCTING RESEARCH ON NEXT-GENERATION POLYMERS.
