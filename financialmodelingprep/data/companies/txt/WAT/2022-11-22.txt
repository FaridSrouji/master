WATERS INTRODUCES NEW PFAS QUANTITATION WORKFLOW ENABLED BY ENHANCEMENTS TO WATERS_CONNECT INFORMATICS PLATFORM. MILFORD, MASS.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--WATERS CORPORATION (NYSE:WAT) TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW PER-AND POLYFLUOROALKYL SUBSTANCES (PFAS) QUANTITATION WORKFLOW ENABLED BY ENHANCEMENTS TO ITS WATERS_CONNECT™ FOR QUANTITATION SOFTWARE. THROUGH A COMBINATION OF WATERS™ ULTRA-SENSITIVE INSTRUMENTATION, SAMPLE PREP CHEMISTRIES AND SOFTWARE, THE END-TO-END WORKFLOW SIMPLIFIES AND AUTOMATES THE MEASUREMENT OF PFAS IN FOOD, SOIL, AIR, AND WATER AT PARTS-PER-QUADRILLION LEVELS THAT MEET AND EXCEED REGULATORY DETECTION LIMITS.
