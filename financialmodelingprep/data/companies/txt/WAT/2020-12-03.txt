WATERS CORPORATION ANNOUNCES CFO TRANSITION. MILFORD, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WATERS CORPORATION (NYSE:WAT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SHERRY BUCK WILL STEP DOWN AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 31, 2020, IN ORDER TO PURSUE ANOTHER OPPORTUNITY AT A PRIVATELY HELD COMPANY.
