RALPH LAUREN REPORTS BETTER THAN EXPECTED SECOND QUARTER FISCAL 2023 RESULTS. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--RALPH LAUREN CORPORATION (NYSE:RL), A GLOBAL LEADER IN THE DESIGN, MARKETING, AND DISTRIBUTION OF LUXURY LIFESTYLE PRODUCTS, TODAY REPORTED EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE OF $2.18 ON A REPORTED BASIS AND $2.23 ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS, EXCLUDING RESTRUCTURING-RELATED AND OTHER NET CHARGES FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF FISCAL 2023.
