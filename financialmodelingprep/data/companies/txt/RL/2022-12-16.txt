RALPH LAUREN CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--RALPH LAUREN CORPORATION (NYSE: RL) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.75 PER SHARE ON RALPH LAUREN CORPORATION COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 13, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 30, 2022.
