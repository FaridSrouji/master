RALPH LAUREN CORPORATION APPLAUDS SELECTION OF U.S. REGENERATIVE COTTON FUND AS AN AIM FOR CLIMATE INNOVATION SPRINT PARTNER AT COP26. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--RALPH LAUREN CORPORATION TODAY APPLAUDED THE SELECTION OF THE RECENTLY LAUNCHED U.S. REGENERATIVE COTTON FUND AS AN AGRICULTURE INNOVATION MISSION FOR CLIMATE (AIM FOR CLIMATE) INNOVATION SPRINT PARTNER, A JOINT INITIATIVE CREATED BY THE UNITED STATES AND THE UNITED ARAB EMIRATES TO SUPPORT INVESTMENTS IN CLIMATE-SMART AGRICULTURE OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS. THE SELECTION WAS ANNOUNCED DURING THE UNITED NATIONS CLIMATE CHANGE CONFERENCE (COP26) IN GLASGOW ON NOV. 2, 202
