ARCLIGHT ACQUIRES AN INCREMENTAL INTEREST IN NATURAL GAS PIPELINE COMPANY OF AMERICA LLC FROM BROOKFIELD INFRASTRUCTURE. BOSTON , JUNE 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AN AFFILIATE OF ARCLIGHT CAPITAL PARTNERS, LLC ("ARCLIGHT") HAS ACQUIRED AN INCREMENTAL 12.5% INTEREST IN NATURAL GAS PIPELINE COMPANY OF AMERICA LLC ("NGPL") FROM BROOKFIELD INFRASTRUCTURE PARTNERS L.P. (NYSE: BIP; TSX: BIP.UN) ("BROOKFIELD INFRASTRUCTURE").
