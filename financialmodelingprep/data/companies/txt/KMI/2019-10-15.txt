KINDER MORGAN, INC. APPROVES CASH DIVIDEND FOR THIRD QUARTER, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 15, 2019 ; PROVIDES DIVIDEND GUIDANCE FOR THE FULL YEAR OF 2019. KINDER MORGAN, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.25 PER SHARE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ($1.00 ANNUALIZED) PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 15, 2019, TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON OCTOBER 31, 2019.FOR 2019, KMI’S BUDGET CONTEMPLATES DECLARED DIVIDENDS OF $1.00 PER COMMON SHARE, DCF OF APPROXIMATELY $5.0 BILLION ($2.20 PER COMMON SHARE).
