CUSHING® ASSET MANAGEMENT AND SWANK CAPITAL ANNOUNCE A CONSTITUENT CHANGE TO THE CUSHING® MLP HIGH INCOME INDEX. DALLAS, APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CUSHING® ASSET MANAGEMENT, LP, AND SWANK CAPITAL, LLC, ANNOUNCE AN UPCOMING INTERIM CHANGE TO THE CONSTITUENTS OF THE CUSHING ® MLP HIGH INCOME INDEX (THE "INDEX"). ON FEBRUARY 16, 2021, INDEX CONSTITUENTS ENABLE MIDSTREAM PARTNERS, LP (NYSE: ENBL) AND ENERGY TRANSFER LP (NYSE: ET) ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAD ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER THAT, IF APPROVED BY AT LEAST A MAJORITY OF ENBL COMMON UNITHOLDERS, WOULD RESULT IN ENBL COMMON UNITS CEASING TO BE PUBLICLY TRADED.
