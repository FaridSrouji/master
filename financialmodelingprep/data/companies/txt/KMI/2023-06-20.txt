BETTERINVESTING™ MAGAZINE UPDATE ON CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL CORP. AND KINDER MORGAN INC.. TROY, MICH., JUNE 20, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE EDITORIAL ADVISORY AND SECURITIES REVIEW COMMITTEE OF BETTERINVESTING MAGAZINE TODAY ANNOUNCED CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL CORP. (NYSE: CCI) AS ITS "STOCK TO STUDY" AND KINDER MORGAN INC. (NYSE: KMI) AS ITS "UNDERVALUED STOCK" IN THE SEPTEMBER 2023 ISSUE FOR INVESTORS' INFORMATIONAL AND EDUCATIONAL USE.
