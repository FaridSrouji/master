OTIS APPOINTS ANURAG MAHESHWARI EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. HE SUCCEEDS RAHUL GHAI TO LEAD FINANCIAL ACTIVITIES AT OTIS FARMINGTON, CONN. , JUNE 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OTIS WORLDWIDE CORPORATION (NYSE: OTIS) ANNOUNCES THE APPOINTMENT OF ANURAG MAHESHWARI AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, REPORTING DIRECTLY TO OTIS CHAIR, CEO & PRESIDENT, JUDY MARKS.
