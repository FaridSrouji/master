OTIS ANNOUNCES A 20.8 PERCENT INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO $0.29 PER SHARE. FARMINGTON, CONN. , APRIL 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE OTIS WORLDWIDE CORPORATION (NYSE: OTIS) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.29 PER SHARE OF OTIS' COMMON STOCK, WHICH REPRESENTS A 20.8% INCREASE.
