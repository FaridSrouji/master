NELDA CONNORS APPOINTED TO OTIS WORLDWIDE BOARD OF DIRECTORS. FARMINGTON, CONN. , OCT. 24, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OTIS WORLDWIDE (NYSE: OTIS), THE WORLD'S LEADING COMPANY FOR ELEVATOR AND ESCALATOR MANUFACTURING, INSTALLATION AND SERVICE, HAS APPOINTED NELDA CONNORS TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 28, 2022.
