DOVER OPERATING COMPANIES WILL HAVE A SUBSTANTIAL PRESENCE AT PREMIER CONVENIENCE STORE AND RETAIL FUELING INDUSTRY TRADE SHOW. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , SEPT. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER (NYSE: DOV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY WILL FEATURE MULTIPLE BRANDS AT THE NATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF CONVENIENCE STORES TRADE SHOW ("NACS SHOW") AND PETROLEUM EQUIPMENT INSTITUTE CONVENTION ("PEI CONVENTION") IN LAS VEGAS, NV, ON OCTOBER 1-4, 2022.
