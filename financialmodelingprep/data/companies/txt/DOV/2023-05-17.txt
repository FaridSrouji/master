MARKEM-IMAJE LAUNCHES THE INDUSTRY'S FIRST INNOVATIONS FOR EXTRUSION MARKING & CODING. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , MAY 17, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARKEM-IMAJE, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF END-TO-END SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTIONS AND INDUSTRIAL MARKING AND CODING SYSTEMS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE 9750 E CONTINUOUS INKJET (CIJ) PRINTER RANGE, BRINGING TO MARKET INDUSTRY-FIRST INNOVATIONS FOR THE EXTRUSION SECTOR.
