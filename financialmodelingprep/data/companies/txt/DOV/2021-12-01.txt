DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS INTRODUCES NEW DMP MAGNETOSTRICTIVE FLEX PROBE FOR FUEL RETAILERS IN EUROPE, AFRICA, THE MIDDLE EAST, LATIN AMERICA AND ASIA. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., DEC. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS ("DFS"), A PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF ADVANCED CUSTOMER-FOCUSED TECHNOLOGIES, SERVICES AND SOLUTIONS IN THE FUEL AND CONVENIENCE RETAIL INDUSTRIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE DMP MAGNETOSTRICTIVE FLEX PROBE, A NEW ADDITION TO ITS FAMILY OF FUEL MANAGEMENT PRODUCTS.
