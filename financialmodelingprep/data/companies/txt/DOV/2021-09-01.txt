CPC LAUNCHES NEW MICROCNX CONNECTORS, A FIRST FOR SMALL-VOLUME BIOPHARMA PROCESSING. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , SEPT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ – CPC (COLDER PRODUCTS COMPANY), PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND MAKER OF A BROAD RANGE OF SINGLE-USE CONNECTORS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A NEW ULTRA-COMPACT STERILE CONNECTOR PROVIDING BIOPHARMACEUTICAL MANUFACTURERS A MUCH-NEEDED ALTERNATIVE TO TUBE WELDING FOR THEIR SMALL-VOLUME CLOSED ASEPTIC PROCESSES.
