METCAL INTRODUCES CV-IOT GATEWAY MODULE AND DESKTOP APPLICATION. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , MAY 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- METCAL, PART OF OK INTERNATIONAL AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), AND A LEADER IN BENCHTOP SOLDERING FOR ELECTRONICS AND INDUSTRIAL MANUFACTURING, ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS NEW CV-IOT GATEWAY MODULE AND DESKTOP APPLICATION, WHICH ENABLES OPERATIONS MANAGEMENT TO COLLECT ADVANCED SOLDERING DATA FROM EVERY BENCHTOP STATION ON THEIR NETWORK.
