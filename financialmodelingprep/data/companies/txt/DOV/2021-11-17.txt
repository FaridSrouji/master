BLACKMER INCREASING MANUFACTURING FOOTPRINT & PRODUCTION CAPABILITIES WITH RENOVATION PROJECT. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., NOV. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BLACKMER, PART OF PSG, A DOVER (NYSE: DOV) COMPANY, AND A GLOBAL LEADER IN SPECIALTY PUMPS AND RECIPROCATING COMPRESSOR TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE INITIATION OF AN AMBITIOUS CONSTRUCTION AND RENOVATION PROJECT AT ITS HEADQUARTERS IN GRAND RAPIDS, MI, THAT WILL INCREASE ITS MANUFACTURING FOOTPRINT AND PRODUCTION CAPABILITIES.
