DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS PARTNERS WITH GLOBAL TECHNICAL SERVICES TO OFFER DX WETSTOCK IN EGYPT. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , APRIL 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS ("DFS"), A PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF ADVANCED CUSTOMER-FOCUSED TECHNOLOGIES, SERVICES AND SOLUTIONS IN THE FUEL AND CONVENIENCE RETAIL INDUSTRIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS EXPANDED ITS AGREEMENT WITH GLOBAL TECHNICAL SERVICES ("GTS") TO INCLUDE THE PROVISION OF ITS DX WETSTOCK MANAGEMENT SERVICES, AN END-TO-END FUEL MANAGEMENT SOLUTION, TO THE EGYPTIAN MARKETS.
