VEHICLE SERVICE GROUP LAUNCHES NEW RANGE OF 2-COLUMN LIFTS IN EMEA. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , SEPT. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ – VEHICLE SERVICE GROUP (VSG), PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING AUTOMOTIVE EQUIPMENT COMPANIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF A NEW RANGE OF 2-COLUMN LIFTS UNDER THE ROTARY AND RAVAGLIOLI BRANDS IN THE EMEA (EUROPE, THE MIDDLE EAST AND AFRICA) REGION, CALLED THE TOMORROW SERIES AND THE LEGEND SERIES RESPECTIVELY.
