CALDERA EXPANDS STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH BARBIERI ELECTRONIC. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , JUNE 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CALDERA, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), RECENTLY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT WITH BARBIERI UNDER WHICH CALDERA WILL SERVE AS THE MASTER DISTRIBUTOR FOR ALL BARBIERI ELECTRONIC SOLUTIONS WORLDWIDE, EXPANDING ON THE BRANDS' EXISTING STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP.
