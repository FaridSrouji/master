CHIEF COLLISION TECHNOLOGY EXPANDS PAINTLESS DENT REPAIR NETWORK OFFERING TOTAL MANAGEMENT OF HAIL DAMAGE REPAIR. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., MARCH 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIEF COLLISION TECHNOLOGY, PART OF VEHICLE SERVICE GROUP (VSG) AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), AND A LEADING PROVIDER OF COLLISION TECHNOLOGY REPAIR SOLUTIONS, HAS EXPANDED ITS NETWORK OF PAINTLESS DENT REPAIR ("PDR") SHOPS ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
