SWEP LAUNCHES TWO NEW HIGH-EFFICIENCY PRODUCTS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , JULY 14, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SWEP, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A WORLD-LEADING SUPPLIER OF BRAZED PLATE HEAT EXCHANGERS (BPHES), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE B8DW DOUBLE-WALL COMPACT HEAT EXCHANGER AND THE FI22AS HIGH-EFFICIENCY CONDENSER AND EVAPORATOR.
