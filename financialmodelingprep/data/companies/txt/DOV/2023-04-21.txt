CPC LAUNCHES BIOPHARMA'S LARGEST ASEPTIC CONNECTOR. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , APRIL 21, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CPC (COLDER PRODUCTS COMPANY), PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A LEADING MANUFACTURER OF CONNECTION TECHNOLOGIES USED IN BIOPHARMACEUTICAL PROCESSING, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS ASEPTIQUIK® W SERIES, THE INDUSTRY'S LARGEST GENDERLESS ASEPTIC CONNECTORS.
