MARKEM-IMAJE LAUNCHES EB588 INK, RAISING PRODUCT CODING STANDARD FOR FAST-MOVING CONSUMER GOODS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., MARCH 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARKEM-IMAJE, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), IS LAUNCHING A NEW, MULTI-PURPOSE BLACK EB588 INK, WHICH SUPPORTS SAFER, MORE SUSTAINABLE CODING WITH FEWER ERRORS FOR FAST-MOVING CONSUMER GOODS, SUCH AS FOOD, BEVERAGE AND PERSONAL CARE PRODUCTS.
