HILLPHOENIX EXPANDS NATURAL REFRIGERATION OFFERINGS WITH NEW R290 SELF-CONTAINED CASES. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , JUNE 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HILLPHOENIX, PART OF DOVER FOOD RETAIL AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A PIONEER IN DESIGNING ENVIRONMENTALLY SUSTAINABLE REFRIGERATION SYSTEMS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF R290 SELF-CONTAINED DISPLAY CASES, PART OF THE COMPANY'S SECOND NATURE® NATURAL PRODUCT LINE.
