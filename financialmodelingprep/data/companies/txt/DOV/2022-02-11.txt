DOVER DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., FEB. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.50 (FIFTY CENTS) PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON MARCH 15, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEBRUARY 28, 2022.
