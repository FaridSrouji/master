METCAL ANNOUNCES PATENT PENDING SOFTWARE UPGRADES TO GT SERIES SOLDERING SYSTEMS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., JUNE 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- METCAL, PART OF OK INTERNATIONAL AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), AND A LEADER IN BENCHTOP SOLDERING SYSTEMS, HAS ANNOUNCED PATENT PENDING SOFTWARE UPGRADES FOR ITS NEWLY LAUNCHED GT90 AND GT120 SOLDERING SYSTEMS.
