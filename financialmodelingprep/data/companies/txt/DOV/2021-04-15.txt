SWEP HEAT EXCHANGERS INTEGRATED WITH HYSOPT DESIGN SOFTWARE. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SWEP, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP WITH HYSOPT DESIGN SOFTWARE TO ENABLE DESIGNERS WORKING WITH HYSOPT TO SELECT SWEP HEAT EXCHANGERS WITHIN THEIR DIGITAL MODEL.
