ROTARY EXPANDS WHEEL SERVICE PRODUCT LINE WITH R1200 LEVERLESS PRO TIRE CHANGER AND R1065 MOBILE TABLET ALIGNER. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., JULY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROTARY, PART OF VEHICLE SERVICE GROUP (VSG) AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), IS EXPANDING ITS WHEEL SERVICE PRODUCT LINE WITH THE LAUNCH OF TWO NEW PRODUCTS: THE R1200 LEVERLESS PRO TIRE CHANGER AND THE R1065 MOBILE TABLET ALIGNER.
