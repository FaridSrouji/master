HILLPHOENIX EARNS 10TH CONSECUTIVE EPA GREENCHILL EXCELLENCE AWARD. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., SEPT. 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HILLPHOENIX, PART OF DOVER FOOD RETAIL AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A PIONEER IN DESIGNING ENVIRONMENTALLY SUSTAINABLE REFRIGERATION SYSTEMS, HAS EARNED THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY'S (EPA) GREENCHILL 2020 - 2021 STORE CERTIFICATION EXCELLENCE (NON-SUPERMARKET) AWARD.
