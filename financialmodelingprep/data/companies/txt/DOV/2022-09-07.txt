MARKEM-IMAJE UNVEILS ETOUCH-S INTELLIGENT PRINT AND APPLY LABELING SOLUTION. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , SEPT. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARKEM-IMAJE, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF END-TO-END SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTIONS AND INDUSTRIAL MARKING AND CODING SYSTEMS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE ETOUCH-S INTELLIGENT PRINT AND APPLY LABELING SYSTEM, FURTHER ENHANCING ITS GLOBAL PORTFOLIO OF END-TO-END SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTIONS.
