SWEP CONTINUES GLOBAL INVESTMENT PROGRAM WITH MANUFACTURING PLANT EXPANSIONS AND NEW R&D CENTER. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , OCT. 18, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SWEP, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A WORLD-LEADING SUPPLIER OF BRAZED PLATE HEAT EXCHANGERS (BPHES), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS EXPANDING ITS GLOBAL FOOTPRINT WITH MULTIPLE MANUFACTURING PLANT EXPANSIONS, A NEW R&D CENTER AND CORPORATE HEAD OFFICE.
