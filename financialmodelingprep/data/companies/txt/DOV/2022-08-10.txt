INPRO/SEAL INTRODUCES VBMAG BEARING ISOLATOR TO ADDRESS GROWING NEED FOR BEARING PROTECTION IN FLOODED AND MIST APPLICATIONS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , AUG. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INPRO/SEAL, PART OF DOVER PRECISION COMPONENTS AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS NEW MAGNETIC BEARING ISOLATOR, VBMAG™, WHICH UTILIZES MAGNETIC FORCE TO CREATE A LIQUID-TIGHT SEAL AND PROTECT AGAINST LUBRICATION LOSS AND CONTAMINATION INGRESS IN CHALLENGING APPLICATIONS, SUCH AS FLOODED, HIGH OIL SPLASH OR OIL MIST ENVIRONMENTS.
