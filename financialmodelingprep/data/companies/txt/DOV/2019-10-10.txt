DOVER CORP SAYS ON OCT 4, CO ENTERED INTO A $1 BILLION FIVE-YEAR UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY. DOVER CORP -  ON OCT 4, CO ENTERED INTO A $1 BILLION FIVE-YEAR UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY WITH A SYNDICATE OF THIRTEEN BANKS.DOVER - COMMITMENTS OF LENDERS UNDER CREDIT AGREEMENT MAY BE INCREASED BY ADDITIONAL AMOUNT OF UP TO $500 MILLION DURING TERM OF CREDIT AGREEMENT.DOVER - CREDIT AGREEMENT REPLACES EXISTING $1 BILLION FIVE-YEAR UNSECURED CREDIT FACILITY PURSUANT TO A CREDIT AGREEMENT DATED NOVEMBER 10, 2015.DOVER CORP - LENDERS' COMMITMENTS UNDER CREDIT AGREEMENT WILL TERMINATE ON OCT 4, 2024.
