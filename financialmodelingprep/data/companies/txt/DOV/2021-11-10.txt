ROTARY INTRODUCES NEW R1090 PRO 3D ALIGNMENT SYSTEM. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., NOV. 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROTARY, PART OF VEHICLE SERVICE GROUP (VSG) AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE R1090 PRO 3D ALIGNMENT SYSTEM FEATURING A TOWER-FREE DESIGN AND A PORTABLE, SPACE-SAVING CABINET THAT REQUIRES NO DEDICATED BAY AND GIVES SHOPS WITH LIMITED SPACE THE FLEXIBILITY NEEDED TO PERFORM WHEEL ALIGNMENTS ANYWHERE.
