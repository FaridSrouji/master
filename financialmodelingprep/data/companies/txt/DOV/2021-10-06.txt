HEIL STRENGTHENS NORTHEAST PRESENCE WITH NEW DEALER LOCATION IN UPSTATE NEW YORK. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., OCT. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEIL, PART OF ENVIRONMENTAL SOLUTIONS GROUP (ESG) AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), AND ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST REFUSE TRUCK BODY MANUFACTURERS, ANNOUNCED THE GROWTH OF ITS NATIONWIDE DEALER NETWORK WITH A NEW ENVIRONMENTAL EQUIPMENT SALES AND SERVICE (EESS) LOCATION IN ROCHESTER, NEW YORK.
