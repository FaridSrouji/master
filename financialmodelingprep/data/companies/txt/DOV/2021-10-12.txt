DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS COLLABORATES WITH SHEP DIGITAL SOLUTIONS TO EXPAND MANAGED MEDIA SERVICE OPTIONS FOR CUSTOMERS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., OCT. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS ("DFS"), A PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF ADVANCED CUSTOMER-FOCUSED TECHNOLOGIES, SERVICES AND SOLUTIONS IN THE FUEL AND CONVENIENCE RETAIL INDUSTRIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION WITH SHEP DIGITAL SOLUTIONS.
DOVER ANNOUNCES THE SALE OF UNIFIED BRANDS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., OCT. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER (NYSE: DOV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS UNIFIED BRANDS BUSINESS ("UNIFIED BRANDS"), WHICH IS PART OF DOVER'S REFRIGERATION & FOOD EQUIPMENT SEGMENT, TO ELECTROLUX PROFESSIONAL AB (STO: EPRO B) FOR APPROXIMATELY $244 MILLION, ON A CASH FREE AND DEBT FREE BASIS, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY POST-CLOSING ADJUSTMENTS.
