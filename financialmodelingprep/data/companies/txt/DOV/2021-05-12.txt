DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS INTRODUCES DX RETAIL FOR TOKHEIM FUEL POINT-OF-SALE CLOUD MANAGEMENT. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., MAY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS ("DFS"), A PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF ADVANCED CUSTOMER-FOCUSED TECHNOLOGIES, SERVICES AND SOLUTIONS IN THE FUEL AND CONVENIENCE RETAIL INDUSTRIES, ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF DX RETAIL™, A PART OF THE DFS DX™ CONNECTED SOLUTIONS PLATFORM.
