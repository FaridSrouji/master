SWEP ENHANCES ALL-STAINLESS RANGE OF BRAZED PLATE HEAT EXCHANGERS WITH B222 LARGE-SIZE MODEL. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , MARCH 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SWEP, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), AND A WORLD-LEADING SUPPLIER OF COMPACT BRAZED PLATE HEAT EXCHANGERS (BPHES), HAS ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF ITS ALL-STAINLESS RANGE BY INTRODUCING THE B222 LARGE-SIZE MODEL.
