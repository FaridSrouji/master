MOUVEX ANNOUNCES NORTH AMERICAN RELEASE OF H-FLO SERIES & G-FLO SERIES ECCENTRIC DISC PUMPS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , MARCH 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MOUVEX, A BRAND OF PSG, A DOVER (NYSE: DOV) COMPANY, AND A WORLDWIDE LEADER IN SPECIALTY PUMPS, ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS NEW H-FLO SERIES AND G-FLO SERIES ECCENTRIC DISC PUMPS FOR USE IN THE NORTH AMERICAN MARKET.
