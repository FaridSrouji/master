DOVER COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF ELECTRIC REFUSE COLLECTION VEHICLE INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY PORTFOLIO. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , APRIL 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER (NYSE: DOV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF CERTAIN INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY FROM BOIVIN EVOLUTION INC. ("BEV") RELATED TO ELECTRICALLY-POWERED REFUSE COLLECTION VEHICLE ("RCV") BODIES.
