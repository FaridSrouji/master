MARKEM-IMAJE LAUNCHES NEW REMOTE USER INTERFACE FOR SMARTDATE X30 THERMAL TRANSFER OVERPRINTER. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., OCT. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARKEM-IMAJE, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF A NEW REMOTE USER INTERFACE (RUI) FOR THE SMARTDATE® X30 THERMAL TRANSFER OVERPRINTER (TTO).
