WILDEN RELEASES NEW 76 MM METAL EQUALIZER SURGE DAMPENER - INTEGRATED SD SERIES. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , OCT. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WILDEN, PART OF PSG, A DOVER (NYSE: DOV) COMPANY, AND A WORLDWIDE LEADER IN SPECIALTY PUMPS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS EXPANDED ITS LINE OF METAL EQUALIZER® SURGE DAMPENERS – INTEGRATED SD SERIES (ISD) WITH THE ADDITION OF A NEW 76 MM (3") MODEL.
