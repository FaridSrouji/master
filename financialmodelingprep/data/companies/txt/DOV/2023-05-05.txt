DOVER DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , MAY 5, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.505 (FIFTY AND ONE-HALF CENTS) PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JUNE 15, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 31, 2023.
