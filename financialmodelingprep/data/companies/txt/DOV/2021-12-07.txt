DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS COLLABORATES WITH ACI WORLDWIDE ON DATA SECURITY OFFERING FOR INCREASED FUEL PUMP SECURITY. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., DEC. 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS ("DFS"), A PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF ADVANCED CUSTOMER-FOCUSED TECHNOLOGIES, SERVICES AND SOLUTIONS IN THE FUEL AND CONVENIENCE RETAIL INDUSTRIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION WITH ACI WORLDWIDE ON A POINT-TO-POINT ENCRYPTION (P2PE) DATA SECURITY OFFERING.
