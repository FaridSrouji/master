SYSTECH ADDRESSES UPDATED DSCSA REQUIREMENTS FOR PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , APRIL 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYSTECH, PART OF MARKEM-IMAJE AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), A LEADING PROVIDER OF DIGITAL IDENTIFICATION AND TRACEABILITY SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED A SERIES OF SERVICES DESIGNED TO HELP CUSTOMERS IN THE PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN MEET DRUG SUPPLY CHAIN SECURITY ACT (DSCSA) INTEROPERABILITY REQUIREMENTS BY THE NOVEMBER 27 DEADLINE.
