DOVER ANNOUNCES TWO ACQUISITIONS FOCUSED ON CLEAN ENERGY. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., DEC. 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER (NYSE: DOV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF ACME CRYOGENICS, INC. ("ACME") FOR $295 MILLION IN CASH, AND THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE ENGINEERED CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL, LLC ("REGO") FOR $631 MILLION IN CASH, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY PURCHASE PRICE ADJUSTMENTS.
