MARKEM-IMAJE RECEIVES THE MANUFACTURING INDUSTRY'S BRC GLOBAL STANDARD CERTIFICATION FOR PACKAGING MATERIALS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , JAN. 30, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARKEM-IMAJE, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF END-TO-END SUPPLY CHAIN SOLUTIONS AND INDUSTRIAL MARKING AND CODING SYSTEMS, ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED THE BRC GLOBAL STANDARD (BRCGS) CERTIFICATION FOR PACKAGING MATERIALS.
