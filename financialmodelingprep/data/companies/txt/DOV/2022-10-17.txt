COOK COMPRESSION INTRODUCES COOK CLEAN SEALING SYSTEM TO LOWER FUGITIVE EMISSIONS IN RECIPROCATING COMPRESSORS AND SUPPORT CUSTOMERS' DECARBONIZATION GOALS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , OCT. 17, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COOK COMPRESSION , PART OF DOVER PRECISION COMPONENTS AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), TODAY INTRODUCED THE COOK CLEANTM SEALING SYSTEM, A NEXT-GENERATION SEALING SYSTEM THAT HELPS LOWER FUGITIVE METHANE EMISSIONS FROM PACKING CASES TO NEAR ZERO LEVELS.
