VSG PARTNERS WITH TEXA TO EXPAND ADVANCED DRIVER ASSISTANCE SYSTEM TOOLS AND OFFER NEW AIR CONDITIONING CHARGING FOR AUTOMOTIVE SERVICE. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , NOV. 30, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROTARY, PART OF VEHICLE SERVICE GROUP (VSG) AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP WITH TEXA, A GLOBAL LEADER IN THE DESIGN, DEVELOPMENT AND PRODUCTION OF DIAGNOSTIC TOOLS FOR THE VEHICLE REPAIR INDUSTRY, THAT WILL ENABLE ROTARY TO EXPAND ITS AUTOMOTIVE SERVICE OFFERINGS.
