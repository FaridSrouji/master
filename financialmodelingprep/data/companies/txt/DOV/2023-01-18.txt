KPS INTRODUCES NEW HIGH FLOW 6" CONDUCTIVE HIGH DENSITY POLYETHYLENE DOUBLE WALL PIPING SYSTEM. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , JAN. 18, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KPS, PART OF OPW AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE WORLD'S FIRST 6" (160/200MM) CONDUCTIVE HIGH DENSITY POLYETHYLENE (HDPE) DOUBLE WALL PIPING SYSTEM.
