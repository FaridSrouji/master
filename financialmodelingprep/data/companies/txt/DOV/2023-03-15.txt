DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS INTRODUCES DX POWER TO INTEGRATE EV CHARGERS WITH PRIZMA, THE COMPANY'S CONNECTED MOBILITY AND CONVENIENCE HUB. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , MARCH 15, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS ("DFS"), A PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF ADVANCED CUSTOMER-FOCUSED TECHNOLOGIES, SERVICES AND SOLUTIONS IN THE FUEL AND CONVENIENCE RETAIL INDUSTRIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF DX POWER™ WITHIN THE EUROPEAN, MIDDLE EASTERN AND AFRICAN REGION.
