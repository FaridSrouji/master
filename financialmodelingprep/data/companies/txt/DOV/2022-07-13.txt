DESTACO INTRODUCES 82L-3E SERIES POWER CLAMPS. NEW DESIGN FEATURES CLAMPING-ARM OPENING ANGLES THAT ARE ADJUSTABLE UP TO 105° DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , JULY 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DESTACO, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE INTRODUCTION OF ITS NEW 82L-3E SERIES POWER CLAMPS, WHICH FEATURE LIGHTWEIGHT, ENCLOSED ALUMINUM HOUSING AND OPTIMIZED MECHANICS THAT ARE MORE PRECISE AND SIGNIFICANTLY LONGER-LASTING THAN PREVIOUS POWER-CLAMP DESIGNS.
