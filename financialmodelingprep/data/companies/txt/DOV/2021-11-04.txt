DOVER DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., NOV. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.50 (FIFTY CENTS) PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 14, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF NOVEMBER 30, 2021.
