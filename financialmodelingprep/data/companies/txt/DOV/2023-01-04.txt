METCAL RELEASES NEW MSA SERIES SMOKE ABSORBERS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , JAN. 4, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- METCAL, PART OF OK INTERNATIONAL AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), AND A LEADER IN BENCHTOP SOLDERING FOR ELECTRONICS AND INDUSTRIAL MANUFACTURING, ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS NEW MSA SERIES SMOKE ABSORBERS, WHICH ARE SPACE-SAVING COMPACT WORKBENCH FANS THAT USE ACTIVATED CARBON FILTERS TO EXTRACT HARMFUL FLUX FUMES AND SMOKE DURING HAND SOLDERING OPERATIONS.
