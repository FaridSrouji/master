SWEP SIGNS MULTIYEAR CONTRACT WITH KVITEBJØRN VARME AS TO ADVANCE RENEWABLE ENERGY AMBITIONS IN THE ARCTIC. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SWEP, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF A MULTIYEAR CONTRACT WITH KVITEBJØRN VARME AS, TO SUPPLY ENERGY TRANSFER STATIONS (ETS) TO ADVANCE THE EXPANSION OF DISTRICT HEATING FROM TROMSØ (NORWAY) BASED ENERGY SUPPLIER.
