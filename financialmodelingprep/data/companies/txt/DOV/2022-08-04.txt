DOVER INCREASES DIVIDEND. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , AUG. 4, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) TODAY INCREASED ITS QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.505 (FIFTY AND ONE-HALF CENTS) PER SHARE, FROM THE PREVIOUS $0.50 (FIFTY CENTS) PER SHARE, AN INCREASE OF $0.005 (ONE-HALF CENT).
