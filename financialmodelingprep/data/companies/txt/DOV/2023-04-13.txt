CDS VISUAL RELEASES UPDATED VERSION OF ITS 3D IMMERSIVE LEARNING TOOL. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL. , APRIL 13, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CDS VISUAL, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) AND A LEADING PROVIDER OF SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SAAS) 3D VISUALIZATION SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF VERSION 2.0 OF CDS MENTOR™, A NEW DIGITAL IMMERSIVE LEARNING PLATFORM THAT UTILIZES 3D VISUALIZATION TO DELIVER KNOWLEDGE AND EXPERTISE TO FRONTLINE WORKERS.
