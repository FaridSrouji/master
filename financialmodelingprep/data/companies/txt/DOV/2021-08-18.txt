DOVER'S SARA VENKATACHALAM NAMED STEP AHEAD AWARD WINNER RECOGNIZING FEMALE EMERGING LEADERS FOR EXCELLENCE IN MANUFACTURING. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., AUG. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER (NYSE: DOV) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT SARA VENKATACHALAM, SENIOR MANAGER, DOVER FUELING SOLUTIONS ENGINEERING, WILL BE HONORED AT THE WOMEN IN MANUFACTURING STEP AHEAD AWARDS ON NOVEMBER 4, 2021, IN WASHINGTON, D.C.
