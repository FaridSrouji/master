HYDRO SYSTEMS LAUNCHES NEW IOT-ENABLED CONTROLLER TO IMPROVE VISIBILITY OF ON-PREMISE LAUNDRY PROCESS. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., SEPT. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HYDRO SYSTEMS, PART OF PSG AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS CONNECTED TOTAL ECLIPSE® CONTROLLER, AN IOT-ENABLED VERSION OF HYDRO'S MOST POPULAR ELECTRONIC CONTROLLER DESIGNED FOR ON-PREMISE LAUNDRIES WITH UP TO FOUR WASHERS.
