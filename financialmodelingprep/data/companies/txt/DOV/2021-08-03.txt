SWEP INTRODUCES THE MEDIUM-SIZE, HIGH-CAPACITY B85S HEAT EXCHANGER TO THE ALL-STAINLESS PRODUCT RANGE. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., AUG. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SWEP, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), A LEADING SUPPLIER OF COMPACT BRAZED PLATE HEAT EXCHANGERS (BPHES), HAS ANNOUNCED THE ADDITION OF THE B85S MEDIUM-SIZE, HIGH-CAPACITY MODEL TO ITS EXISTING ALL-STAINLESS PRODUCT RANGE.
