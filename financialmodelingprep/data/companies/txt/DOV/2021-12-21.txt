RAVAGLIOLI PARTNERS WITH TEXA TO EXPAND AUTOMOTIVE WORKSHOP OFFERING. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., DEC. 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAVAGLIOLI, PART OF VEHICLE SERVICE GROUP (VSG) AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SIGNED A TECHNICAL AGREEMENT WITH TEXA, A LEADER IN THE DESIGN, INDUSTRIALIZATION AND CONSTRUCTION OF DIAGNOSTIC TOOLS AND DEVICES FOR REMOTE DIAGNOSIS, TO EXPAND ITS AUTOMOTIVE WORKSHOP OFFERING AND COLLABORATE ON NEW PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT WITH COMBINED TECHNOLOGIES.
