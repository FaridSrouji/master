OLD DOMINION FREIGHT LINE PROVIDES UPDATE FOR FIRST QUARTER 2023. THOMASVILLE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--OLD DOMINION FREIGHT LINE, INC. (NASDAQ: ODFL) TODAY REPORTED CERTAIN LESS-THAN-TRUCKLOAD (“LTL”) OPERATING METRICS FOR FEBRUARY 2023. REVENUE PER DAY DECREASED 2.9% AS COMPARED TO FEBRUARY 2022 DUE TO A 12.4% DECREASE IN LTL TONS PER DAY THAT WAS PARTIALLY OFFSET BY AN INCREASE IN LTL REVENUE PER HUNDREDWEIGHT. THE CHANGE IN LTL TONS PER DAY WAS ATTRIBUTABLE TO A 10.1% DECREASE IN LTL SHIPMENTS PER DAY AND A 2.5% DECREASE IN LTL WEIGHT PER SHIPMENT.
