OLD DOMINION FREIGHT LINE, INC. ANNOUNCES GENERAL RATE INCREASE. THOMASVILLE, N.C.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--OLD DOMINION FREIGHT LINE, INC. (NASDAQ: ODFL) TODAY ANNOUNCED A GENERAL RATE INCREASE (GRI) OF 4.9 PERCENT APPLICABLE TO RATES ESTABLISHED UNDER THE EXISTING ODFL 559, 670, AND 550 TARIFFS, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 3, 2023.
