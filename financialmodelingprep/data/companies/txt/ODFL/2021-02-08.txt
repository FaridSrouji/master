OLD DOMINION FREIGHT LINE GROWS SERVICE CENTER NETWORK WITH NINE NEW OR EXPANDED FACILITIES. THOMASVILLE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--OLD DOMINION FREIGHT LINE, INC. (NASDAQ: ODFL) EXPANDS ITS NETWORK BY ADDING NINE SERVICE CENTERS IN NEW AND EXISTING MARKETS. THE STEADY GROWTH BRINGS THE COMPANY'S SERVICE CENTER COUNT TO 245 AND SOLIDIFIES OLD DOMINION'S PRESENCE ACROSS THE CONTINENTAL UNITED STATES. THE LESS-THAN-TRUCKLOAD (LTL) CARRIER'S NEW OR IMPROVED FACILITIES INCLUDE BROOKLYN, N.Y., EDINBURGH, IND., GRAND ISLAND, NEB., LOUISVILLE, KY., MANSFIELD, OHIO, MCDONOUGH, GA., MESA, ARIZ., M
