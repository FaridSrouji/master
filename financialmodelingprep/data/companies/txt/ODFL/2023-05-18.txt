OLD DOMINION FREIGHT LINE ANNOUNCES $0.40 PER SHARE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. THOMASVILLE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--OLD DOMINION FREIGHT LINE, INC. (NASDAQ: ODFL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.40 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JUNE 21, 2023, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 7, 2023. THIS DIVIDEND REPRESENTS A 33.3% INCREASE OVER THE DIVIDEND PAID IN JUNE 2022.
