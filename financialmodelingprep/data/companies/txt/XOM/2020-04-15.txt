EXXONMOBIL BOOSTS PRODUCTION OF RAW MATERIALS FOR MEDICAL MASKS, GOWNS AND HAND SANITIZER. EXXONMOBIL BOOSTS PRODUCTION OF RAW MATERIALS FOR MEDICAL MASKS, GOWNS AND HAND SANITIZER.EXXONMOBIL - ISOPROPYL ALCOHOL MONTHLY PRODUCTION INCREASED BY UP TO 3,000 TONNES; ENOUGH TO MAKE 50 MILLION BOTTLES OF HAND SANITIZER.EXXONMOBIL - SPECIALIZED POLYPROPYLENE MONTHLY PRODUCTION INCREASED BY 1,000 TONNES; ENOUGH TO MAKE 200 MILLION MASKS OR 20 MILLION GOWNS.EXXONMOBIL - ISOPROPYL ALCOHOL AND POLYPROPYLENE ARE BEING PRIORITIZED AT CHEMICAL MANUFACTURING FACILITIES.EXXONMOBIL - ANTICIPATES IT WILL CONTINUE TO MEET ITS CONTRACTUAL COMMITMENTS TO MANUFACTURE OTHER CHEMICAL PRODUCTS.
