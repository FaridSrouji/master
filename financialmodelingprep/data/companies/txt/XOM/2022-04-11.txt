EXXONMOBIL INTRODUCES NEW EXCEED™ S PERFORMANCE POLYETHYLENE, ENABLING CONVERTERS TO RETHINK FILM DESIGN FOR SIMPLER SOLUTIONS. SPRING, TEXAS , APRIL 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EXXONMOBIL INTRODUCED TODAY ITS NEW EXCEED™ S PERFORMANCE POLYETHYLENE (PE) RESINS, WHICH DELIVER INDUSTRY-LEADING COMBINATIONS OF STIFFNESS AND TOUGHNESS WHILE BEING EASY TO PROCESS.  UTILIZING THE COMPANY'S LATEST INNOVATIONS IN POLYMER SCIENCE, THE NEW PE PLATFORM PROVIDES OPPORTUNITIES TO REDUCE THE COMPLEXITY OF FILM FORMULATIONS AND DESIGNS WHILE IMPROVING FILM PERFORMANCE, CONVERSION EFFICIENCY, AND PACKAGING DURABILITY VERSUS CURRENT MARKET REFERENCES.
