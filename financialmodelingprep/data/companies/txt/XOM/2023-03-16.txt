EXXONMOBIL BOOSTS FUEL SUPPLY WITH $2 BILLION BEAUMONT REFINERY EXPANSION. IRVING, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EXXONMOBIL TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SUCCESSFUL STARTUP OF ITS BEAUMONT REFINERY EXPANSION PROJECT, WHICH ADDS 250,000 BARRELS PER DAY OF CAPACITY TO ONE OF THE LARGEST REFINING AND PETROCHEMICAL COMPLEXES ALONG THE U.S. GULF COAST. SUPPORTED BY THE COMPANY'S GROWING CRUDE PRODUCTION IN THE PERMIAN BASIN, THE LARGEST REFINERY EXPANSION IN MORE THAN A DECADE WILL HELP MEET GROWING DEMAND FOR AFFORDABLE, RELIABLE ENERGY.
