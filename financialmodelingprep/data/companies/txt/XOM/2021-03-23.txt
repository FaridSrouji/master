MONDAY DEADLINE REMINDER: THE SCHALL LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THE FILING OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST EXXON MOBIL CORPORATION AND ENCOURAGES INVESTORS WITH LOSSES IN EXCESS OF $500,000 TO CONTACT THE FIRM. LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - MARCH 23, 2021) - THE SCHALL LAW FIRM, A NATIONAL SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LITIGATION FIRM, ANNOUNCES THE FILING OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST EXXON MOBIL CORPORATION ("EXXON" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE: XOM) FOR VIOLATIONS OF §§10(B) AND 20(A) OF THE SECURITIES EXCHANGE ACT OF 1934 AND RULE 10B-5 PROMULGATED THEREUNDER BY THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION.INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED THE COMPANY'S SECURITIES BETWEEN NOVEMBER 6, 2019 AND JANUARY 14, 2021,...
