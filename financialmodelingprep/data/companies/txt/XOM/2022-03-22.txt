EXXONMOBIL APPOINTS DAN AMMANN PRESIDENT OF LOW CARBON SOLUTIONS; JOE BLOMMAERT TO RETIRE. IRVING, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EXXONMOBIL SAID TODAY THAT DAN AMMANN, FORMER GENERAL MOTORS PRESIDENT AND CEO OF CRUISE AUTONOMOUS VEHICLE COMPANY, HAS BEEN APPOINTED PRESIDENT OF EXXONMOBIL LOW CARBON SOLUTIONS, EFFECTIVE MAY 1. AMMANN REPLACES JOE BLOMMAERT, WHO HAS ELECTED TO RETIRE AFTER 35 YEARS OF SERVICE. “WE WELCOME DAN TO EXXONMOBIL AND WILL USE HIS KNOWLEDGE AND EXPERIENCE TO CONTINUE TO BUILD OUR LOW CARBON SOLUTIONS BUSINESS,” SAID DARREN WOODS, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND CHAIRMAN
