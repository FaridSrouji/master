EXXON MOBIL CORPORATION DECLARES FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 10, 2019. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF EXXON MOBIL CORPORATION DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.87 CENTS PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 10, 2019 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD OF COMMON STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 12, 2019.
