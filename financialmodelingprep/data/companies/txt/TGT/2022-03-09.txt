TARGET ANNOUNCES TARGET ZERO: A NEW, CURATED COLLECTION OF PRODUCTS AIMING TO REPLACE SINGLE-USE PACKAGING. MINNEAPOLIS, MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE: TGT) TODAY ANNOUNCED TARGET ZERO, A NEW INITIATIVE THAT RESPONDS TO GROWING GUEST INTEREST IN PRODUCTS THAT HELP REDUCE WASTE. GUESTS WILL NOW SEE A TARGET ZERO ICON IN STORES AND ONLINE THAT WILL SIGNAL PRODUCTS AND PACKAGING ACROSS TARGET'S ASSORTMENT DESIGNED TO BE REFILLABLE, REUSABLE, OR COMPOSTABLE, MADE FROM RECYCLED CONTENT, OR MADE FROM MATERIALS THAT REDUCE THE USE OF PLASTIC.
