TGT LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES TARGET CORPORATION INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , MAY 25, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN TARGET CORPORATION ("TARGET" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: TGT) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF TARGET INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN AUGUST 18, 2021 AND MAY 17, 2022.
