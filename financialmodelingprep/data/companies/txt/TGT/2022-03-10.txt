TARGET CORPORATION DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. MINNEAPOLIS, MARCH 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE: TGT) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 90 CENTS PER COMMON SHARE.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 10, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS MAY 18, 2022.
