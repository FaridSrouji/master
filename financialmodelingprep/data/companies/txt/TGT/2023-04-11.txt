TGT ALERT: THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES A LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE OF MAY 30, 2023 IN THE CLASS ACTION FILED ON BEHALF OF TARGET CORPORATION SHAREHOLDERS. NEW YORK , APRIL 11, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION COMPLAINT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE: TGT) ALLEGING THAT THE COMPANY VIOLATED FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
