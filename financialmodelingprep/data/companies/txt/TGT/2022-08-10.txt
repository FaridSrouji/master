GRACE PUMA APPOINTED TO TARGET CORPORATION'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. MINNEAPOLIS , AUG. 10, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE: TGT) ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS ELECTED GRACE PUMA AS A NEW DIRECTOR, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. SHE WILL SERVE ON THE AUDIT & RISK AND INFRASTRUCTURE & FINANCE COMMITTEES.
