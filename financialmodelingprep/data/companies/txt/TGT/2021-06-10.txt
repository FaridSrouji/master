TARGET ANNOUNCES VOTING RESULTS FROM 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS. MINNEAPOLIS, JUNE 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE:TGT) TODAY ANNOUNCED VOTING RESULTS FROM ITS 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS. SHAREHOLDERS ELECTED 12 MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, RATIFIED THE APPOINTMENT OF TARGET'S INDEPENDENT REGISTERED PUBLIC ACCOUNTING FIRM, APPROVED THE "SAY ON PAY" MANAGEMENT PROPOSAL, AND REJECTED ONE SHAREHOLDER PROPOSAL.
