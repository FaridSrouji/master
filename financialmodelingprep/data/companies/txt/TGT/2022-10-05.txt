TARGET NAMES PRAT VEMANA CHIEF DIGITAL AND PRODUCT OFFICER. VEMANA WILL OVERSEE THE COMPANY'S GROWING DIGITAL BUSINESS, ALONG WITH USER EXPERIENCE AND PRODUCT OPERATIONS MINNEAPOLIS , OCT. 5, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE: TGT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE HIRE OF PRAT VEMANA AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CHIEF DIGITAL AND PRODUCT OFFICER. VEMANA WILL JOIN TARGET OCT. 31 AS A MEMBER OF TARGET'S LEADERSHIP TEAM, REPORTING TO CHIEF GUEST EXPERIENCE OFFICER CARA SYLVESTER.
