TARGET CORPORATION DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. MINNEAPOLIS, JAN. 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE:TGT) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 90 CENTS PER COMMON SHARE.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 10, 2022 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS FEBRUARY 16, 2022.
