TARGET CORPORATION DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. MINNEAPOLIS , MARCH 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE:TGT) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.08 PER COMMON SHARE.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 10, 2023 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS MAY 17, 2023.
