TARGET READIES TEAM FOR HOLIDAY WITH MORE HOURS, PAY & TRAINING. MINNEAPOLIS, SEPT. 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE: TGT) TODAY SHARED PLANS TO BUILD ON YEARS OF INVESTMENTS IN ITS TEAM BY PROVIDING FIVE MILLION MORE HOURS TO CURRENT STORES TEAM MEMBERS THIS HOLIDAY SHOPPING SEASON, RESULTING IN MORE THAN $75 MILLION MORE IN PAY FOR ITS EXISTING TEAM.
