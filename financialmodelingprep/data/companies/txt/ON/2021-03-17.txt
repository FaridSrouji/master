ON SEMICONDUCTOR AFFIRMS COMMITMENT TO CLIMATE CHANGE ACTION AND TRANSPARENCY. IN ADDITION, THE COMPANY CONFIRMED THAT ITS SUSTAINABILITY PROGRAMS ARE ALIGNED WITH THE RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE TASK FORCE ON CLIMATE-RELATED FINANCIAL DISCLOSURES (TCFD).
