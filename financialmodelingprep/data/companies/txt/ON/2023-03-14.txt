ONSEMI EXTENDS BLUETOOTH LOW ENERGY MCU FAMILY FOR AUTOMOTIVE WIRELESS APPLICATIONS. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ONSEMI (NASDAQ: ON), A LEADER IN INTELLIGENT POWER AND SENSING TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED AN ULTRA-LOW POWER AUTOMOTIVE-GRADE WIRELESS MICROCONTROLLER WITH BLUETOOTH® LOW ENERGY CONNECTIVITY. THE NCV-RSL15 IS IDEAL FOR VEHICLE MANUFACTURERS WHO ARE INCREASINGLY FAVORING WIRELESS CONNECTIVITY TO REDUCE THE COST AND WEIGHT OF EXCESS CABLING AS THE NUMBER OF SENSORS AND IN-VEHICLE COMMUNICATION GROWS.
