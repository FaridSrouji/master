ONSEMI SHIPS ONE BILLIONTH INDUCTIVE SENSOR IC TO HELLA. SCOTTSDALE, ARIZ.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ONSEMI (NASDAQ: ON), A LEADER IN INTELLIGENT POWER AND SENSING TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS SHIPPED ITS ONE BILLIONTH INDUCTIVE SENSOR INTERFACE INTEGRATED CIRCUIT (IC) TO HELLA, AN INTERNATIONAL AUTOMOTIVE SUPPLIER OPERATING UNDER THE UMBRELLA BRAND FORVIA.
