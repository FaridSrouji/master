ON SEMICONDUCTOR ANNOUNCES PRICING OF PRIVATE OFFERING OF $700 MILLION OF 0% CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES. PHOENIX--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ON SEMICONDUCTOR CORPORATION (NASDAQ: ON) (“ON SEMICONDUCTOR”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED PRIVATE OFFERING OF $700 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 0% CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2027 (THE “NOTES”) AT AN APPROXIMATELY 42.5% PREMIUM TO THE CLOSING PRICE OF ON SEMICONDUCTOR'S COMMON STOCK ON MAY 11, 2021 OF $37.17.
