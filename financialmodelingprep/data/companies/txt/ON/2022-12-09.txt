LA SEMICONDUCTOR PURCHASES FABRICATION PLANT FROM ONSEMI. POCATELLO, IDAHO--( BUSINESS WIRE )--LA SEMICONDUCTOR, THE NEWEST U.S. OWNED AND OPERATED PURE-PLAY SEMICONDUCTOR FOUNDRY FOR ANALOG, MIXED SIGNAL AND POWER PRODUCTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PURCHASE OF THE POCATELLO, IDAHO FABRICATION PLANT FROM ONSEMI (NASDAQ: ON), A LEADER IN INTELLIGENT POWER AND SENSING TECHNOLOGIES, ON OCTOBER 14, 2022.
