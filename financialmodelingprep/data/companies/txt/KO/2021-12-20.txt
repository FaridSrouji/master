HERBERT A. ALLEN III ELECTED TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE COCA-COLA COMPANY. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT HERBERT A. ALLEN III, PRESIDENT OF ALLEN & CO. LLC, HAS BEEN ELECTED AS A DIRECTOR. ALLEN, 54, IS THE SON OF HERBERT A. ALLEN JR., WHO RETIRED FROM THE COCA-COLA BOARD EARLIER THIS YEAR AFTER 39 YEARS OF SERVICE. ALLEN HAS SERVED AS PRESIDENT OF ALLEN & CO. FOR NEARLY TWO DECADES. ALLEN & CO.
