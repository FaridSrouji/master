COCA-COLA BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TODAY DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 44 CENTS PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE OCT. 3, 2022, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD OF THE COMPANY AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPT. 16, 2022.
