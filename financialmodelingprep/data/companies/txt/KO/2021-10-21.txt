COCA-COLA BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TODAY DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 42 CENTS PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DEC. 15, 2021, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD OF THE COMPANY AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DEC. 1, 2021.
