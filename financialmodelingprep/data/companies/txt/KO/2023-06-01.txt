COCA-COLA BEVERAGES FLORIDA EXECUTIVE JASON REED ELECTED CHAIRPERSON OF THE COCA-COLA SYSTEM'S NATIONAL PRODUCT SUPPLY GROUP BOARD OF DIRECTORS. TAMPA, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JASON REED, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, PRODUCT SUPPLY NETWORK FOR COCA-COLA BEVERAGES FLORIDA, LLC (COKE FLORIDA) HAS BEEN ELECTED CHAIRPERSON OF THE COCA-COLA SYSTEM'S NATIONAL PRODUCT SUPPLY GROUP (NPSG) BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NPSG ADMINISTERS KEY NATIONAL PRODUCT SUPPLY ACTIVITIES FOR COCA-COLA SYSTEM BOTTLERS, INCLUDING PRODUCT SOURCING.
