MOLSON COORS EXPANDS EXCLUSIVE AGREEMENT WITH THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TO LAUNCH TOPO CHICO SPIRITED LINE OF CANNED COCKTAILS. ALREADY THE FASTEST-GROWING BRAND IN THE HARD-SELTZER SEGMENT, THE TOPO CHICO TRADEMARK WILL MOVE INTO SPIRIT-BASED, READY-TO-DRINK COCKTAILS IN 2023 CHICAGO , SEPT. 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MOLSON COORS BEVERAGE COMPANY (NYSE: TAP) HAS EXPANDED ITS EXCLUSIVE AGREEMENT WITH THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TO DEVELOP AND COMMERCIALIZE TOPO CHICO SPIRITED, A LINE OF SPIRIT-BASED, READY-TO-DRINK COCKTAILS INSPIRED BY THE BRIGHT AND REFRESHING TASTE OF SOME OF AMERICA'S MOST BELOVED TEQUILA AND VODKA-BASED BEVERAGES.
