THE COCA-COLA COMPANY ANNOUNCES NOMINATION OF NEW DIRECTOR AND APPROVES 61ST CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND INCREASE. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BUSINESS LEADER AMITY MILLHISER HAS BEEN NOMINATED TO STAND FOR ELECTION TO THE BOARD AT THE COMPANY'S ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREOWNERS IN APRIL. IF ELECTED BY SHAREOWNERS, SHE WILL BEGIN HER SERVICE AS A COCA-COLA DIRECTOR EFFECTIVE JULY 1, FOLLOWING HER RETIREMENT FROM PRICEWATERHOUSECOOPERS LLP ON JUNE 30.
