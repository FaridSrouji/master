FOUNDER OF ZICO COCONUT WATER RE-ACQUIRES BRAND FROM THE COCA-COLA COMPANY. LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--POWERPLANT VENTURES ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE FUND HAS ACQUIRED ZICO COCONUT WATER FROM THE COCA-COLA COMPANY (NYSE: KO). POWERPLANT VENTURES, A GROWTH EQUITY INVESTOR, WAS CO-FOUNDED AND IS CO-LED BY MARK RAMPOLLA, WHO IS THE BRAND VISIONARY BEHIND ZICO, WHICH HE FOUNDED IN 2004 AND SOLD TO THE COCA-COLA COMPANY IN 2013.
