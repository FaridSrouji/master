THE COCA-COLA COMPANY ANNOUNCES ESG PRESENTATION FOR INVESTORS. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BEA PEREZ, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS, SUSTAINABILITY AND STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIPS OFFICER, AND HENRIQUE BRAUN, PRESIDENT OF THE LATIN AMERICA OPERATING UNIT OF THE COCA-COLA COMPANY, WILL DISCUSS THE COMPANY'S ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PRIORITIES DURING A WEBCAST WITH INVESTORS ON NOV. 3 AT 11 A.M. ET. PEREZ AND BRAUN WILL BE JOINED BY OTHER COMPANY LEADERS FOR A MODERATED QUESTION-AND-ANSWE
