COCA-COLA CO ANNOUNCES STRATEGIC STEPS TO REORGANIZE ITS BUSINESS. THE COCA-COLA COMPANY ANNOUNCES STRATEGIC STEPS TO REORGANIZE ITS BUSINESS FOR FUTURE GROWTH.COCA-COLA CO - WORKFORCE TO BE ALIGNED TO FOCUS ON GROWTH; REDUCTIONS EXPECTED THROUGH VOLUNTARY AND INVOLUNTARY SEPARATION PROGRAMS.COCA-COLA CO - COMPANY TO ESTABLISH NEW OPERATING UNITS AND GLOBAL BEVERAGE CATEGORY LEADS.COCA-COLA CO - NINE NEW OPERATING UNITS WILL HELP STREAMLINE ORGANIZATION BY REPLACING CURRENT BUSINESS UNITS AND GROUPS.COCA-COLA CO - WORKFORCE REDUCTIONS EXPECTED THROUGH VOLUNTARY AND INVOLUNTARY SEPARATION PROGRAMS.COCA-COLA CO - OVERALL GLOBAL SEVERANCE PROGRAMS ARE EXPECTED TO INCUR EXPENSES RANGING FROM APPROXIMATELY $350 MILLION TO $550 MILLION.COCA-COLA CO - VOLUNTARY SEPARATION PROGRAM WILL PROVIDE ENHANCED BENEFITS AND WILL BE OFFERED TO APPROXIMATELY 4,000 EMPLOYEES.COCA-COLA - OPERATIONAL BUSINESS TO CONSIST OF 9 OPERATING UNITS THAT WILL SIT UNDER 4 GEOGRAPHICAL SEGMENTS, ALONG WITH GLOBAL VENTURES, BOTTLING INVESTMENTS.COCA-COLA - VOLUNTARY PROGRAM TO FIRST BE OFFERED TO EMPLOYEES IN U.S., CANADA, PUERTO RICO WHO HAVE MOST-RECENT HIRE DATE ON/BEFORE SEPT. 1, 2017.COCA-COLA CO - SIMILAR VOLUNTARY SEPARATION PROGRAM WILL BE OFFERED IN MANY COUNTRIES INTERNATIONALLY.
