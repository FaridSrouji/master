BARRY SIMPSON TO STEP DOWN AS CHIEF PLATFORM SERVICES OFFICER OF THE COCA-COLA COMPANY. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BARRY SIMPSON WILL STEP DOWN FROM HIS ROLE AS CHIEF PLATFORM SERVICES OFFICER. SIMPSON WILL CONCLUDE HIS ROLE SEPT. 30. HE WILL REMAIN WITH THE COMPANY AS A SENIOR ADVISOR THROUGH FEBRUARY 2023. SIMPSON, 62, HAS LED THE COMPANY'S PLATFORM SERVICES ORGANIZATION SINCE IT WAS CREATED IN 2021.
