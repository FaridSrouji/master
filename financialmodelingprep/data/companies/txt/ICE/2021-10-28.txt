INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE APPROVES FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.33 PER SHARE. ATLANTA & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, ANNOUNCED TODAY A $0.33 PER SHARE DIVIDEND FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2021, WHICH IS UP 10% FROM THE $0.30 PER SHARE DIVIDEND PAID IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020. THE CASH DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 31, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 17, 2021. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS DECEMBER 16, 2021. ABOUT INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE INTERCONTINE
