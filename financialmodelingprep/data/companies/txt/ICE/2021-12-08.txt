ICE INTRODUCES REFERENCE DATA FOR LEVERAGED LOAN MARKET. ATLANTA & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL BEGIN OFFERING LEVERAGED BANK LOAN REFERENCE DATA THROUGH ITS ONGOING COLLABORATION WITH U.S. BANK. THE OFFERING WILL PROVIDE INVESTORS AND OTHER MARKET PARTICIPANTS WITH HIGH-QUALITY AND DETAILED REFERENCE DATA FROM THE LARGEST ADMINISTRATOR OF LOANS IN THE UNITED STATES.
