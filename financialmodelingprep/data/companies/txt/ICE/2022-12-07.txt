D AND Z MEDIA ACQUISITION CORP. ANNOUNCES ADJOURNMENT OF SPECIAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS TO DECEMBER 19, 2022. ATLANTA , DEC. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- D AND Z MEDIA ACQUISITION CORP. (NYSE: DNZ) (THE "COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE SPECIAL MEETING IN LIEU OF THE 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS OF THE COMPANY (THE "SPECIAL MEETING"), ORIGINALLY SCHEDULED FOR 12:00 P.M. EASTERN TIME ON DECEMBER 7, 2022, WAS CONVENED AND THEN ADJOURNED, WITHOUT CONDUCTING ANY BUSINESS, TO RECOMMENCE AT 1:30 P.M.
