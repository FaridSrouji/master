INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE ANNOUNCES 11% INCREASE TO ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. ATLANTA & NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, ANNOUNCED BOARD AUTHORIZATION OF ITS FIRST QUARTER 2023 DIVIDEND OF $0.42 PER SHARE, UP 11% FROM ITS PREVIOUS $0.38 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IN 2022.
