INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE ANNOUNCES RESULTS FROM 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS. ATLANTA & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE:ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RESULTS OF THE COMPANY'S 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS, WHICH WAS HELD FRIDAY, MAY 13. A REPLAY OF THE MEETING IS AVAILABLE AT WWW.IR.THEICE.COM. EACH OF THE TEN DIRECTOR NOMINEES RECEIVED A MAJORITY OF THE VOTES CAST “FOR” THEIR ELECTION. EACH DIRECTOR WAS ELECTED TO A ONE-YEAR TERM.
