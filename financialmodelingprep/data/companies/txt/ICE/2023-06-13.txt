ICE BENCHMARK ADMINISTRATION PLANS TO LAUNCH ICE COMMODITY TRACEABILITY SERVICE TO HELP COCOA AND COFFEE INDUSTRIES COMPLY WITH EU DEFORESTATION REGULATION. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ICE BENCHMARK ADMINISTRATION LIMITED (IBA) PLANS TO LAUNCH THE ICE COMMODITY TRACEABILITY (ICOT) SERVICE TO SUPPORT THE COCOA AND COFFEE INDUSTRIES IN MEETING THE REQUIREMENTS OF THE EUROPEAN UNION'S (EU) INCOMING REGULATION RELATING TO DEFORESTATION AND FOREST DEGRADATION. ICOT IS DESIGNED TO SUPPORT CUSTOMERS ACROSS THE COCOA AND C.
