U.K. FINANCIAL CONDUCT AUTHORITY TO REQUIRE ICE BENCHMARK ADMINISTRATION TO PUBLISH “SYNTHETIC” U.S. DOLLAR LIBOR. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE:ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY NOTES THE DECISION FROM THE U.K. FINANCIAL CONDUCT AUTHORITY (FCA) TO REQUIRE ICE BENCHMARK ADMINISTRATION LIMITED (IBA) TO PUBLISH 1-, 3- AND 6-MONTH “SYNTHETIC” U.S. DOLLAR LIBOR.
