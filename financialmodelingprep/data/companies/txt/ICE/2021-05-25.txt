ICE REPORTS RECORD TOTAL FUTURES AND OPTIONS OPEN INTEREST OF 84.5 MILLION CONTRACTS. LONDON & NEW YORK & SINGAPORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE:ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED RECORD OPEN INTEREST OF APPROXIMATELY 84.5 MILLION CONTRACTS ON MAY 21, 2021, SURPASSING THE PREVIOUS RECORD SET ON MARCH 12, 2020.
