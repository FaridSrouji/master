ICE EXPANDS ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLEX WITH THE LAUNCH OF A GLOBAL CARBON FUTURES INDEX CONTRACT. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE (NYSE:ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT PLANS TO LAUNCH A FUTURES CONTRACT BASED ON THE ICE GLOBAL CARBON FUTURES INDEX (ICE: CO2). THE NEW FUTURES CONTRACT IS DESIGNED TO OFFER THE MARKET A WAY TO ACCESS AND HEDGE A GLOBAL PRICE FOR CARBON AND IS EXPECTED TO LAUNCH IN EARLY 2022, SUBJECT TO REGULATORY APPROVAL.
