INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE REPORTS APRIL 2021 STATISTICS. ATLANTA & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY REPORTED APRIL 2021 TRADING VOLUME AND RELATED REVENUE STATISTICS, WHICH CAN BE VIEWED ON THE COMPANY'S INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE AT HTTPS://IR.THEICE.COM/IR-RESOURCES/SUPPLEMENTAL-INFORMATION IN THE MONTHLY STATISTICS TRACKING SPREADSHEET. APRIL HIGHLIGHTS INCLUDE: TOTAL OPEN INTEREST (OI) UP 7% Y/Y AND UP 15% COMPARED TO 4Q20 TOTAL
