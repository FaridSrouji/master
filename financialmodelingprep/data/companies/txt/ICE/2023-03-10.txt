ICE BENCHMARK ADMINISTRATION ANNOUNCES INTENTION TO LAUNCH SOFR SPREAD-ADJUSTED ICE SWAP RATE® AS A BENCHMARK. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE:ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ICE BENCHMARK ADMINISTRATION LIMITED (IBA) PLANS TO LAUNCH U.S. DOLLAR (USD) SOFR SPREAD-ADJUSTED ICE SWAP RATE® FOR USE AS A BENCHMARK IN FINANCIAL CONTRACTS AND FINANCIAL INSTRUMENTS BY LICENSEES ON JULY 3, 2023.
