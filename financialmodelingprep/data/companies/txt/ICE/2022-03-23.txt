CICC HONG KONG ASSET MANAGEMENT SELECTS ICE INDEX FOR CICC CARBON FUTURES ETF. ATLANTA, NEW YORK & HONG KONG--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHINA INTERNATIONAL CAPITAL CORPORATION HONG KONG ASSET MANAGEMENT LIMITED (CICC HKAM) HAS SELECTED THE ICE EUA CARBON FUTURES INDEX (EXCESS RETURN) FOR ITS CICC CARBON FUTURES ETF.
