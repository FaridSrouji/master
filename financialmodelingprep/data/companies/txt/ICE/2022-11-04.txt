THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE AND INDONESIA STOCK EXCHANGE SIGN MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING. NEW YORK & JAKARTA, INDONESIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE, PART OF INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE: ICE), AND THE INDONESIA STOCK EXCHANGE (IDX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE AGREED TO COLLABORATE ON SEVERAL AREAS OF MUTUAL INTEREST, INCLUDING JOINT PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT.
