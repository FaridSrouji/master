(NYSE: ICE), AND THE TEL AVIV STOCK EXCHANGE (TASE: TASE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE SIGNED A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING FOR A BROAD COLLABORATION THAT INCLUDES THE DUAL LISTING OF COMPANIES.
