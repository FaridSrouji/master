INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE ANNOUNCES THE RETIREMENT OF CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER SCOTT A. HILL FOLLOWING 14 YEARS OF DISTINGUISHED SERVICE. LONDON & NEW YORK & SINGAPORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING OPERATOR OF GLOBAL EXCHANGES AND CLEARING HOUSES AND PROVIDER OF MORTGAGE TECHNOLOGY, DATA AND LISTINGS SERVICES, ANNOUNCED THAT CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER SCOTT HILL PLANS TO RETIRE ON MAY 14, 2021, CAPPING A 14-YEAR CAREER AT ICE THAT SAW THE FIRM EXPAND CONSISTENTLY THROUGH ORGANIC GROWTH AND ACQUISITIONS, DELIVERING A COMPOUND ANNUAL GROWTH RATE IN ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF 16% DURING HI
