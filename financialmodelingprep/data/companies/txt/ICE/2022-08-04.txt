INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE APPROVES THIRD QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.38 PER SHARE. ATLANTA & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, ANNOUNCED TODAY A $0.38 PER SHARE DIVIDEND FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2022, WHICH IS UP 15% FROM THE $0.33 PER SHARE DIVIDEND PAID IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021. THE CASH DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF SEPTEMBER 16, 2022. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS SEPTEMBER 15, 2022. ABOUT INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE INTERCONTIN
