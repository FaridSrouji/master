EXPANSION OF ICE ESG REFERENCE DATA SHOWS BROAD DIFFERENCES IN ESG REPORTING BETWEEN EUROPE AND THE U.S.. LONDON & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) REFERENCE DATA PLATFORM NOW INCLUDES COVERAGE OF MAJOR PUBLIC EUROPEAN COMPANIES, ALLOWING USERS TO MAKE ACTIONABLE COMPARISONS ACROSS COMPANIES, SECTORS AND GEOGRAPHIES.
