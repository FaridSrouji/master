ICE LAUNCHES REAL TIME PRICING OF LINK NORTH AMERICAN PHYSICAL CRUDE TRADE DATA BRINGING NEW TRANSPARENCY AND FURTHER ESTABLISHING ICE IN THE GULF COAST. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY, AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF REAL-TIME PHYSICAL CRUDE PRICING FROM LINK DATA SERVICES (“LINK DATA”), IN COOPERATION WITH LINK CRUDE RESOURCES (“LINK”), ONE OF THE LARGEST BROKERS OF PHYSICAL NORTH AMERICAN CRUDE, BRINGING ADDITIONAL TRANSPARENCY TO THE NORTH AMERICAN CRUDE MARKET.
