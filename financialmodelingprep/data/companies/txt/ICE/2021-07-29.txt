INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE APPROVES THIRD QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.33 PER SHARE. ATLANTA & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, ANNOUNCED TODAY A $0.33 PER SHARE DIVIDEND FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021, WHICH IS UP 10% FROM THE $0.30 PER SHARE DIVIDEND PAID IN THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2020. THE CASH DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF SEPTEMBER 16, 2021. THE EX-DIVIDEND DATE IS SEPTEMBER 15, 2021. ABOUT INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE INTERCONTINE
