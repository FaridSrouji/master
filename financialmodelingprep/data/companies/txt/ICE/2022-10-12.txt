ICE DATA SERVICES NAMED MARKET DATA VENDOR OF THE YEAR IN ASIA PACIFIC REGION BY ASIA RISK. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF DATA, TECHNOLOGY AND MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ASIA RISK HAS SELECTED ICE DATA SERVICES AS MARKET DATA VENDOR OF THE YEAR IN THE ASIA PACIFIC REGION.
