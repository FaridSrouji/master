INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE APPROVES FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE. ATLANTA & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE (NYSE: ICE), A LEADING OPERATOR OF GLOBAL EXCHANGES AND CLEARING HOUSES AND PROVIDER OF MORTGAGE TECHNOLOGY, DATA AND LISTINGS SERVICES, ANNOUNCED TODAY A $0.30 PER SHARE DIVIDEND FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020, WHICH IS UP 9% FROM THE $0.275 PER SHARE DIVIDEND PAID IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2019.
