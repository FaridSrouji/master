DXC TECHNOLOGY STATEMENT. ASHBURN, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN RESPONSE TO MARKET RUMORS AND SPECULATION, ON OCT 4TH, 2022, DXC TECHNOLOGY (NYSE: DXC) ANNOUNCED THAT IT WAS APPROACHED BY A FINANCIAL SPONSOR REGARDING A POTENTIAL ACQUISITION OF THE COMPANY. CONSISTENT WITH ITS FIDUCIARY RESPONSIBILITY TO MAXIMIZE SHAREHOLDER VALUE, THE COMPANY ENGAGED IN PRELIMINARY DISCUSSIONS AND SHARED INFORMATION WITH A FINANCIAL SPONSOR.
