DXC IDENTIFIES RANSOMWARE ATTACK ON PART OF ITS XCHANGING ENVIRONMENT. THAT CERTAIN SYSTEMS OF ITS SUBSIDIARY, XCHANGING, HAVE EXPERIENCED A RANSOMWARE ATTACK.COMPANY IS CONFIDENT THAT THIS INCIDENT IS ISOLATED TO XCHANGING ENVIRONMENT.DXC DOES NOT HAVE ANY INDICATION AT THIS TIME THAT DATA HAS BEEN COMPROMISED OR LOST.DXC ACTIVELY WORKING WITH AFFECTED CUSTOMERS TO RESTORE ACCESS TO THEIR OPERATING ENVIRONMENT AS QUICKLY AS POSSIBLE.ENGAGING WITH LAW ENFORCEMENT AND APPROPRIATE CYBER AGENCIES.
