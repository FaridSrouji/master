OPTIONS TECHNOLOGY ANNOUNCES ACQUISITION OF FIXNETIX FROM DXC TECHNOLOGY. LONDON & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--OPTIONS TECHNOLOGY, BACKED BY ABRY PARTNERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAD CLOSED ON ITS ACQUISITION OF FIXNETIX, A DXC TECHNOLOGY COMPANY (NYSE: DXC).
