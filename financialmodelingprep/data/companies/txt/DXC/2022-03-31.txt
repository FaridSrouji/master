HER MAJESTY'S PASSPORT OFFICE TAPS DXC TECHNOLOGY FOR DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DXC TECHNOLOGY (NYSE: DXC), A LEADING FORTUNE 500 GLOBAL IT SERVICES PROVIDER, HAS BEEN SELECTED TO TRANSFORM AND RUN THE MISSION-CRITICAL IT INFRASTRUCTURE FOR HER MAJESTY'S PASSPORT OFFICE (HMPO) IN THE U.K. BUILDING ON A 12-YEAR RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN DXC AND HMPO, THE ORGANIZATIONS HAVE SIGNED 5-YEAR AGREEMENT WORTH UP TO $37 MILLION, THROUGH WHICH DXC WILL PROVIDE HMPO WITH DIGITAL PLATFORM SERVICES TO MEET THE GROWING DEMAND FOR BRITISH PASSPORTS.
