DXC TECHNOLOGY SUPPORTS ZURICH'S INFRASTRUCTURE TRANSFORMATION WITH FIVE-YEAR EXTENDED CONTRACT. TYSONS, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DXC TECHNOLOGY ANNOUNCED THAT ZURICH INSURANCE GROUP HAS EXTENDED ITS AGREEMENT WITH DXC TO PROVIDE IT AND SECURITY SERVICES OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS.
