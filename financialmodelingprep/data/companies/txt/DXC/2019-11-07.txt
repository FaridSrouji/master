DXC TECHNOLOGY COMPANY (NYSE:DXC) ACQUIRED THE REMAINING UNKNOWN STAKE IN VIRTUAL CLARITY LIMITED FROM STEVE PESKIN.. DXC TECHNOLOGY COMPANY (NYSE:DXC) ACQUIRED THE REMAINING UNKNOWN STAKE IN VIRTUAL CLARITY LIMITED FROM STEVE PESKIN ON NOVEMBER 8, 2019. LAWRENCE PHILLIPS AND MICHELLE DAVENPORT OF BLAKE MORGAN LLP ACTED AS LEGAL ADVISOR TO VIRTUAL CLARITY LIMITED.  IAN ROWE OF GREENBACK ALAN LLP ACTED AS ACCOUNTANT TO VIRTUAL CLARITY LIMITED.
DXC TECHNOLOGY COMPANY (NYSE:DXC) COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF THE REMAINING UNKNOWN STAKE IN VIRTUAL CLARITY LIMITED FROM STEVE PESKIN ON NOVEMBER 8, 2019. MATTHEW POXON OF PAUL HASTINGS LLP ACTED AS LEGAL ADVISOR TO DXC TECHNOLOGY COMPANY AND PADDY MCCGWIRE OF SILVERPEAK ACTED AS FINANCIAL ADVISOR TO VIRTUAL CLARITY
