MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY'S STEVE SANGHI SHARES HIS PERSONAL AND BUSINESS JOURNEY IN "UP AND TO THE RIGHT". PARADISE VALLEY, ARIZ., MAY 03, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RENOWNED SEMICONDUCTOR INDUSTRY LEADER, STEVE SANGHI, HAS RELEASED HIS LONG-AWAITED BOOK, UP AND TO THE RIGHT: MY PERSONAL AND BUSINESS JOURNEY BUILDING THE MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY JUGGERNAUT . SANGHI LED MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY FOR OVER THREE DECADES, TRANSFORMING THE COMPANY FROM A FAILING ENTERPRISE TO A MARKET LEADER WITH A MARKET CAPITALIZATION OF $43 BILLION.
