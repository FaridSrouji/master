MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY SAYS NET SALES FOR QUARTER ENDING MARCH 31, 2020 IS EXPECTED TO BE UP ABOUT 3% SEQUENTIALLY. MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY PROVIDES UPDATE ON MARCH 2020 QUARTER NET SALES AND THE BUSINESS ENVIRONMENT.MICROCHIP - NET SALES FOR QUARTER ENDING MARCH 31, 2020 IS EXPECTED TO BE UP ABOUT 3% SEQUENTIALLY VERSUS REVISED GUIDANCE PROVIDED ON MARCH 2, 2020.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - CEO, PRESIDENT AND OTHER EXECUTIVE STAFF MEMBERS WILL TAKE A 20% SALARY CUT EFFECTIVE ON APRIL 20, 2020.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - REST OF NON-FACTORY EMPLOYEES IN COMPANY WILL TAKE A 10% SALARY CUT.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - SALARY CUTS WILL BE IN ADDITION TO REDUCTION OF CASH BONUSES AND OTHER DISCRETIONARY EXPENSES.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - COST REDUCTION ACTIONS IN OUR U.S. FACTORIES WILL VARY WITH SOME EMPLOYEES TAKING SALARY CUTS.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - BOARD OF DIRECTORS WILL ALSO TAKE A 20% CUT IN THEIR CASH COMPENSATION.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - EXPECTS THAT ITS CAPITAL EXPENDITURES FOR FISCAL 2021 ENDING MARCH 31, 2021 WILL BE REDUCED TO ABOUT $50 MILLION TO $70 MILLION.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - SEE STRONG BOOKINGS AND BACKLOG RIGHT NOW.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - ALSO SEE A VERY HIGH DEGREE OF UNCERTAINLY IN OUR BUSINESS DUE TO IMPACT OF COVID-19 VIRUS.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - THAT IT RECEIVED RECORD BOOKINGS FOR MARCH 2020 QUARTER.MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC - ITS BACKLOG FOR JUNE 2020 QUARTER IS UP ABOUT 9% FROM MARCH 2020 QUARTER BACKLOG AT SAME POINT IN TIME.
