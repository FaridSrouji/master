MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY ANNOUNCES PROMOTION OF RICH SIMONCIC TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT. CHANDLER, ARIZ., APRIL 20, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INC. (NASDAQ: MCHP), A LEADING PROVIDER OF SMART, CONNECTED AND SECURE EMBEDDED CONTROL SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS PROMOTED RICH SIMONCIC TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF MICROCHIP.
