IBM INTRODUCES $3 MILLION IN CYBERSECURITY GRANTS FOR PUBLIC SCHOOLS IN UNITED STATES AS ATTACKS ON EDUCATION GROW. ARMONK, N.Y., FEB. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED IN-KIND GRANTS VALUED AT $3 MILLION TO HELP STRENGTHEN CYBERSECURITY IN SCHOOLS. THESE GRANTS WILL BE AWARDED TO SIX SCHOOL DISTRICTS IN THE UNITED STATES TO SPONSOR TEAMS OF IBMERS TO HELP THEM PROACTIVELY PREPARE...
