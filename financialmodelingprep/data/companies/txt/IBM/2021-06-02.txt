IBM SELECTS SIX SCHOOL DISTRICTS TO RECEIVE A TOTAL OF $3 MILLION IN EDUCATION SECURITY PREPAREDNESS GRANTS. ARMONK, N.Y., JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SELECTED SIX SCHOOL DISTRICTS TO RECEIVE ITS IBM EDUCATION SECURITY PREPAREDNESS GRANT, WHICH TOTALS $3 MILLION.
