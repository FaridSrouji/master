IBM AND VODAFONE JOIN FORCES IN EXPLORATION OF QUANTUM COMPUTING TECHNOLOGY AND QUANTUM-SAFE CRYPTOGRAPHY. VODAFONE PARTNERS WITH IBM TO PREPARE FOR THE QUANTUM ERA OF APPLICATIONS IN TELECOMMUNICATIONS NEW YORK , NOV. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) ANNOUNCED TODAY AT THE IBM QUANTUM SUMMIT 2022 THAT VODAFONE GROUP IS COLLABORATING WITH IBM ON QUANTUM-SAFE CYBERSECURITY, AND ALSO JOINING THE IBM QUANTUM NETWORK, WHICH WILL GIVE THE COMPANY CLOUD ACCESS TO IBM'S ADVANCED QUANTUM COMPUTING SYSTEMS, AS WELL AS IBM'S INDUSTRY-LEADING QUANTUM EXPERTISE. THE MULTINATIONAL TELECOMMUNICATIONS COMPANY WILL WORK WITH IBM TO HELP VALIDATE AND PROGRESS POTENTIAL QUANTUM USE CASES IN TELECOMMUNICATIONS.
