IBM MAKES RESOURCES AVAILABLE TO SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS AT NO COST TO THEM. ARMONK, N.Y., JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE AVAILABILITY OF REIGNITING SMALL BUSINESS FROM IBM, A PROGRAM AIMING TO PROVIDE NO-COST, EDUCATION AND COACHING DESIGNED TO HELP SMALL BUSINESSES RUN THEIR COMPANIES MORE EFFECTIVELY AS THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC EASES.
