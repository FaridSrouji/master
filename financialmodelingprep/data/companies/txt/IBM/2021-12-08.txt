IBM LAUNCHES NEW IBM Z AND CLOUD MODERNIZATION CENTER FOR ACCELERATION OF HYBRID CLOUD. ARMONK, N.Y., DEC. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY UNVEILED THE IBM Z AND CLOUD MODERNIZATION CENTER, A DIGITAL FRONT DOOR TO A VAST ARRAY OF TOOLS, TRAINING, RESOURCES AND ECOSYSTEM PARTNERS TO HELP IBM Z CLIENTS ACCELERATE THE MODERNIZATION OF THEIR APPLICATIONS, DATA AND PROCESSES IN AN OPEN HYBRID CLOUD ARCHITECTURE.
