IBM AND MERCEDES DEVELOP "STOLEN VEHICLE HELP" FOR MERCEDES ME SERVICE. SERVICE BUILDS ON IBM HYBRID CLOUD EXPERTISE IN DEVELOPING CONNECTED VEHICLE SOLUTIONS BASED ON OPEN-SOURCE TECHNOLOGIES EHNINGEN, GERMANY AND ARMONK, N.Y., SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) AND MERCEDES ANNOUNCED THE SUCCESSFUL DEVELOPMENT OF "URBAN GUARD - STOLEN VEHICLE HELP," A RECENTLY LAUNCHED SERVICE IN THE MERCEDES ME APP DESIGNED TO HELP FIND AND RECOVER STOLEN VEHICLES.
