IBM HELPS CUSTOMERS ADOPT A ZERO TRUST APPROACH TO SECURITY. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) SECURITY TODAY INTRODUCED A NEW SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SAAS) VERSION OF IBM CLOUD PAK FOR SECURITY, DESIGNED TO SIMPLIFY HOW ORGANIZATIONS DEPLOY A ZERO TRUST ARCHITECTURE ACROSS THE ENTERPRISE.
