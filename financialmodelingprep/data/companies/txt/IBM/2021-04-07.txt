IBM CLOUD FOR FINANCIAL SERVICES ACCELERATES INNOVATION WITH SUPPORT FOR RED HAT OPENSHIFT AND OTHER CLOUD-NATIVE SERVICES. ARMONK, N.Y., APRIL 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE:  IBM ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE GENERAL AVAILABILITY OF THE INDUSTRY'S FIRST FINANCIAL SERVICES-READY CLOUD PLATFORM, IBM CLOUD FOR FINANCIAL SERVICES, NOW INCLUDING THE SUPPORT FOR RED HAT OPENSHIFT AND OTHER CLOUD-NATIVE SERVICES.
