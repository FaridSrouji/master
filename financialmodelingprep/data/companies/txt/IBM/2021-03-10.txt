EGYPTAIR TOURISM AND DUTY FREE CO. LANDS CLOUD CONTRACT WITH IBM. ARMONK, N.Y., MARCH 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) AND EGYPTAIR TOURISM AND DUTY FREE CO. ANNOUNCED TODAY A NEW AGREEMENT IN WHICH THE AIRLINE WILL LEVERAGE IBM CLOUD TO SCALE AND MODERNIZE EGYPTAIR DUTY FREE BACK-END OPERATIONS AND HOST ITS SAP TRAVEL RETAIL SYSTEM.
