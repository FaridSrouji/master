EGYPT'S MINISTRY OF SUPPLY AND INTERNAL TRADE AUTOMATES WHEAT SILOS PROCESSES IN COLLABORATION WITH IBM AND ACME SAICO. CAIRO, SEPT. 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM), IN COLLABORATION WITH BUSINESS PARTNER ACME SAICO, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE AUTOMATION OF 22 WHEAT SILOS ACROSS EGYPT BY THE END OF THIS YEAR USING IBM AI-POWERED AUTOMATION SOFTWARE.
