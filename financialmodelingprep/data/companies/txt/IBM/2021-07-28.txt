KYNDRYL NAMES DAVID WYSHNER AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. ARMONK, N.Y., JULY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KYNDRYL, THE NEW, INDEPENDENT PUBLIC COMPANY THAT WILL BE CREATED FOLLOWING THE SEPARATION OF IBM'S (NYSE: IBM) MANAGED INFRASTRUCTURE SERVICES BUSINESS, ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DAVID WYSHNER AS THE COMPANY'S CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.
