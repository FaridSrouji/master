PERSISTENT SYSTEMS JOINS GROWING ECOSYSTEM OF PARTNERS USING IBM CLOUD PAKS WITH RED HAT OPENSHIFT TO MODERNIZE MISSION-CRITICAL WORKLOADS. SANTA CLARA, CALIF. AND PUNE, INDIA, MAY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PERSISTENT SYSTEMS (BSE: PERSISTENT) (NSE: PERSISTENT) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INTENT TO JOIN IBM'S (NYSE: IBM) ECOSYSTEM OF PARTNERS USING IBM'S NEW AUTOMATION FOUNDATION AND IBM CLOUD PAKS FOR AUTOMATION TO MANAGE AND MODERNIZE MISSION-CRITICAL WORKLOADS ACROSS HYBRID CLOUD ENVIRONMENTS.
