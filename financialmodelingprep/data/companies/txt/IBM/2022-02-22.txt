WORLEY, ABB, AND IBM TO COLLABORATE TO CREATE AN END-TO-END GREEN HYDROGEN SOLUTION. SYDNEY AND ZURICH AND ARMONK, N.Y., FEB. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WORLEY (ASX: WOR), ABB (ABBN: SIX SWISS EX) AND IBM (NYSE: IBM) HAVE SIGNED A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING TO COLLABORATE ON HELPING ENERGY COMPANIES BUILD AND OPERATE GREEN HYDROGEN FACILITIES MORE EFFICIENTLY AND AT SCALE.
