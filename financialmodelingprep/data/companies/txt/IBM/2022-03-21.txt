IBM FILES LAWSUIT TO PROTECT ITS INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS. ACCUSES LZLABS OF INFRINGING IBM PATENTS AND MISAPPROPRIATING IBM TRADE SECRETS ARMONK, N.Y., MARCH 21, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE:  IBM ) TODAY FILED SUIT AGAINST LZLABS, SAYING THE SWITZERLAND-BASED COMPANY HAS VIOLATED IBM'S INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS BY REPEATEDLY INFRINGING UPON COMPANY PATENTS PROTECTING VARIOUS ASPECTS OF IBM'S HIGH-PERFORMANCE MAINFRAME SYSTEMS, A CORE TECHNOLOGY THAT CLIENTS DEPEND ON FOR THEIR MOST IMPORTANT WORKLOADS.
