RED HAT BRINGS JBOSS ENTERPRISE APPLICATION PLATFORM TO MICROSOFT AZURE, EASING SHIFT TO THE CLOUD FOR TRADITIONAL JAVA APPLICATIONS. RALEIGH, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WITH JBOSS EAP TO AZURE, RED HAT IS EASING THE SHIFT TO CLOUD AND GIVING ORGANIZATIONS GREATER CHOICE AND FLEXIBILITY IN HOW THEY PLAN FOR THE FUTURE.
