IBM SURVEY: PANDEMIC-INDUCED DIGITAL RELIANCE CREATES LINGERING SECURITY SIDE EFFECTS. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) SECURITY TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RESULTS OF A GLOBAL SURVEY EXAMINING CONSUMERS' DIGITAL BEHAVIORS DURING THE PANDEMIC, AS WELL AS THEIR POTENTIAL LONG-TERM IMPACT ON CYBERSECURITY.
CURRIKI ACCELERATES SOCIAL INNOVATION BY DEVELOPING E-LEARNING SOLUTIONS WITH RED HAT OPEN INNOVATION LABS. SINGAPORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CURRIKI COMPLETED A SIX-WEEK RED HAT OPEN INNOVATION LABS RESIDENCY TO DEVELOP EDUCATION SOLUTIONS FOR STUDENTS USING A FLEXIBLE, RESPONSIVE APPROACH.
