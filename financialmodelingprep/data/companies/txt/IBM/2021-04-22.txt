PARLE PRODUCTS, WORLD'S LARGEST SELLING BISCUIT BRAND, TEAMS WITH IBM TO DRIVE GROWTH THROUGH HYBRID CLOUD. ARMONK, N.Y., APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS WORKING WITH PARLE PRODUCTS PVT LTD (PARLE), THE LARGEST SELLING BISCUIT BRAND  IN INDIA, TO HELP IT MORE QUICKLY AND EFFECTIVELY BRING ITS PRODUCTS TO MARKET BY LEVERAGING IBM CLOUD.
