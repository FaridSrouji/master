IBM UNVEILS NEW GENERATION OF IBM POWER SERVERS FOR FRICTIONLESS, SCALABLE HYBRID CLOUD. ARMONK, N.Y., SEPT. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE NEW IBM POWER E1080 SERVER, THE FIRST IN A NEW FAMILY OF SERVERS BASED ON THE NEW IBM POWER10 PROCESSOR, DESIGNED SPECIFICALLY FOR HYBRID CLOUD ENVIRONMENTS.
