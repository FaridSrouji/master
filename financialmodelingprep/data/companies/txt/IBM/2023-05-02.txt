SAP TO EMBED IBM WATSON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE INTO SAP® SOLUTIONS. WALLDORF, GERMANY AND ARMONK, N.Y. , MAY 2, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SAP SE (NYSE: SAP) AND IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IBM WATSON® TECHNOLOGY WILL BE EMBEDDED INTO SAP® SOLUTIONS TO PROVIDE NEW AI-DRIVEN INSIGHTS AND AUTOMATION TO HELP ACCELERATE INNOVATION AND CREATE MORE EFFICIENT AND EFFECTIVE USER EXPERIENCES ACROSS THE SAP APPLICATION PORTFOLIO.
