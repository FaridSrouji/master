IBM, AMAZON WEB SERVICES JOIN FORCES TO HELP OIL & GAS INDUSTRY ACCELERATE ENERGY TRANSITION. COLLABORATION WILL BRING TOGETHER AWS AND IBM OPEN DATA FOR INDUSTRIES FOR A COMPREHENSIVE OSDU™ DATA SOLUTION ABU DHABI, UNITED ARAB EMIRATES, NOV. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY AT ADIPEC, IBM (NYSE: IBM) AND AMAZON WEB SERVICES, INC. (AWS), AN AMAZON.COM, INC. COMPANY (NASDAQ: AMZN) ANNOUNCED THE TWO COMPANIES WILL COMBINE THE BENEFITS OF IBM OPEN DATA FOR INDUSTRIES FOR IBM CLOUD PAK FOR DATA AND THE AWS CLOUD TO SERVE ENERGY CUSTOMERS. THIS COMPREHENSIVE SOLUTION IS BUILT ON RED HAT OPENSHIFT AND WILL RUN ON THE AWS CLOUD, SIMPLIFYING THE ABILITY FOR CUSTOMERS TO RUN WORKLOADS IN THE AWS CLOUD AND ON-PREMISES.
