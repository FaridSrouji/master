HSBC WORKING WITH IBM TO ACCELERATE QUANTUM COMPUTING READINESS. BANK ENVISIONS APPLICATION OF QUANTUM CAPABILITIES FOR PRIORITIES SUCH AS PRICING AND PORTFOLIO OPTIMISATION, SUSTAINABILITY, RISK AND FRAUD EXPANDS INTERNAL TALENT WITH QUANTUM SPECIALISTS ARMONK, N.Y., MARCH 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HSBC AND IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY WILL WORK TOGETHER ON EXPLORING APPLICATIONS FOR QUANTUM COMPUTING IN FINANCIAL SERVICES.
