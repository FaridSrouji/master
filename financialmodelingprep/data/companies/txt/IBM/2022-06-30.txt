FRANCISCO PARTNERS COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF IBM'S HEALTHCARE DATA AND ANALYTICS ASSETS; LAUNCHES HEALTHCARE DATA COMPANY MERATIVE. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANCISCO PARTNERS, A LEADING GLOBAL INVESTMENT FIRM THAT SPECIALIZES IN PARTNERING WITH TECHNOLOGY BUSINESSES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF HEALTHCARE DATA AND ANALYTICS ASSETS THAT WERE PART OF IBM'S (NYSE: IBM) WATSON HEALTH BUSINESS, PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED IN JANUARY.
