ANNOUNCING IBM Z/OS V2.5, NEXT-GEN OPERATING SYSTEM DESIGNED FOR HYBRID CLOUD AND AI. ARMONK, N.Y., JULY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED IBM Z/OS V2.5, THE NEXT-GENERATION OPERATING SYSTEM (OS) FOR IBM Z, DESIGNED TO ACCELERATE CLIENT ADOPTION OF HYBRID CLOUD AND AI AND DRIVE APPLICATION MODERNIZATION PROJECTS.
IBM AND THE UNIVERSITY OF TOKYO UNVEIL JAPAN'S MOST POWERFUL QUANTUM COMPUTER. TOKYO, JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) AND THE UNIVERSITY OF TOKYO TODAY UNVEILED JAPAN'S MOST POWERFUL QUANTUM COMPUTER AS PART OF THEIR ONGOING COLLABORATION TO ADVANCE JAPAN'S EXPLORATION OF QUANTUM SCIENCE, BUSINESS AND EDUCATION. "IBM IS COMMITTED TO THE GROWTH OF THE GLOBAL QUANTUM ECOSYSTEM," SAID DR. DARIO GIL, DIRECTOR OF IBM RESEARCH.
