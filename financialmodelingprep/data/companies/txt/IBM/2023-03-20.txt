CLEVELAND CLINIC AND IBM UNVEIL FIRST QUANTUM COMPUTER DEDICATED TO HEALTHCARE RESEARCH. IBM QUANTUM SYSTEM ONE DEPLOYED AT CLEVELAND CLINIC AS PART OF LANDMARK 10-YEAR PARTNERSHIP CLEVELAND AND ARMONK, N.Y. , MARCH 20, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, CLEVELAND CLINIC AND IBM (NYSE: IBM) OFFICIALLY UNVEILED THE FIRST DEPLOYMENT OF AN ONSITE PRIVATE SECTOR IBM-MANAGED QUANTUM COMPUTER IN THE UNITED STATES.
