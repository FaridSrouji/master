IBM NOTICE: ROSEN, TRUSTED INVESTOR COUNSEL, ENCOURAGES INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION INVESTORS WITH LOSSES IN EXCESS OF $100K TO SECURE COUNSEL BEFORE IMPORTANT DEADLINE IN SECURITIES CLASS ACTION - IBM.
