ARLINGTON CAPITAL PARTNERS AGREES TO SELL OCTO TO IBM. WASHINGTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--ARLINGTON CAPITAL PARTNERS (“ARLINGTON CAPITAL”), A WASHINGTON, DC-BASED PRIVATE EQUITY FIRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS AGREED TO SELL OCTO (THE “COMPANY”) TO IBM (NYSE: IBM). OCTO, HEADQUARTERED IN RESTON, VA, IS A PURE-PLAY DIGITAL MODERNIZATION SOLUTIONS PROVIDER TO THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT.
IBM TO ACQUIRE OCTO. ACQUISITION TO BRING OCTO'S EXPERTISE AND AGILITY TOGETHER WITH IBM'S REACH AND SCALE TO HELP ACCELERATE U.S. FEDERAL GOVERNMENT DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION ARMONK, N.Y. , DEC. 7, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE OCTO, A U.S.-BASED IT MODERNIZATION AND DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION SERVICES PROVIDER EXCLUSIVELY SERVING THE U.S. FEDERAL GOVERNMENT, INCLUDING DEFENSE, HEALTH, AND CIVILIAN AGENCIES.
