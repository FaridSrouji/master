IBM ACQUIRES NEUDESIC, LEADING MICROSOFT AZURE CONSULTANCY. ARMONK, N.Y., FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE:  IBM ) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ACQUIRED NEUDESIC, A LEADING U.S. CLOUD SERVICES CONSULTANCY SPECIALIZING PRIMARILY IN THE MICROSOFT AZURE PLATFORM, ALONG WITH BRINGING SKILLS IN MULTICLOUD.
