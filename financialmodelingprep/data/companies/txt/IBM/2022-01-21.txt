FRANCISCO PARTNERS TO ACQUIRE IBM'S HEALTHCARE DATA AND ANALYTICS ASSETS. ARMONK, N.Y. & SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IBM (NYSE: IBM) AND FRANCISCO PARTNERS, A LEADING GLOBAL INVESTMENT FIRM THAT SPECIALIZES IN PARTNERING WITH TECHNOLOGY BUSINESSES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANIES HAVE SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH FRANCISCO PARTNERS WILL ACQUIRE HEALTHCARE DATA AND ANALYTICS ASSETS FROM IBM THAT ARE CURRENTLY PART OF THE WATSON HEALTH BUSINESS.
