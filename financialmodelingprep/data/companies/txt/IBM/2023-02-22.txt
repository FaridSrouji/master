ROSEN, A GLOBALLY RECOGNIZED FIRM, ENCOURAGES INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION INVESTORS TO SECURE COUNSEL BEFORE IMPORTANT DEADLINE IN SECURITIES CLASS ACTION COMMENCED BY THE FIRM - IBM. NEW YORK , FEB. 22, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  WHY: ROSEN LAW FIRM, A GLOBAL INVESTOR RIGHTS LAW FIRM, REMINDS PURCHASERS OF THE SECURITIES OF INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION (NYSE: IBM) BETWEEN JANUARY 18, 2018 AND OCTOBER 16, 2018, BOTH DATES INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD") OF THE IMPORTANT MARCH 14, 2023 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE.  SO WHAT: IF YOU INVESTED IN IBM SECURITIES DURING THE CLASS PERIOD YOU MAY BE ENTITLED TO COMPENSATION WITHOUT PAYMENT OF ANY OUT OF POCKET FEES OR COSTS THROUGH A CONTINGENCY FEE ARRANGEMENT.
