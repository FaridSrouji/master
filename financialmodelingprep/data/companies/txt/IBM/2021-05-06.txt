IBM UNVEILS WORLD'S FIRST 2 NANOMETER CHIP TECHNOLOGY, OPENING A NEW FRONTIER FOR SEMICONDUCTORS. ALBANY, N.Y., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY UNVEILED A BREAKTHROUGH IN SEMICONDUCTOR DESIGN AND PROCESS WITH THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE WORLD'S FIRST CHIP ANNOUNCED WITH 2 NANOMETER (NM) NANOSHEET TECHNOLOGY.
