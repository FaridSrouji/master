IBM LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , MAY 12, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION ("IBM" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: IBM) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF IBM INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN APRIL 4, 2017 AND OCTOBER 20, 2021.
