DOW NAMED AMONG OF THE "PEOPLE COMPANIES THAT CARE®" FOR 3RD CONSECUTIVE YEAR. MIDLAND, MICH. , AUG. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOW (NYSE: DOW) HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE 2022 PEOPLE COMPANIES THAT CARE BY GREAT PLACE TO WORK® AND PEOPLE, RANKING 92 ON THE LIST OF 100 COMPANIES, MARKING THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR THE COMPANY HAS EARNED A SPOT ON THIS PRESTIGIOUS LIST.
