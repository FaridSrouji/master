DOW SELECTS NEW HOPE ENERGY FOR MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT TO STRENGTHEN CIRCULARITY. TYLER, TEXAS, JAN. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEW HOPE ENERGY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SIGNED A MULTI-YEAR OFFTAKE AGREEMENT WITH DOW (NYSE: DOW) TO PROVIDE PYROLYSIS OIL FEEDSTOCK DERIVED FROM RECYCLED PLASTICS COLLECTED IN NORTH AMERICA. "THIS PARTNERSHIP WITH DOW SIGNALS THAT THE WORLD'S MOST INNOVATIVE AND SUCCESSFUL COMPANIES ARE CONFIDENT IN OUR CIRCULAR TECHNOLOGY," SAID RUSTY COMBS, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF NEW HOPE ENERGY.
