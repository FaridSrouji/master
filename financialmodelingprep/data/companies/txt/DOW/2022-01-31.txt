MARY DRAVES TO RETIRE FROM DOW; ANDRE ARGENTON NAMED VICE PRESIDENT, EH&S, AND CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER. MIDLAND, MICH., JAN. 31, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOW (NYSE: DOW) TODAY ANNOUNCES THAT MARY DRAVES, VICE PRESIDENT OF ENVIRONMENT, HEALTH AND SAFETY (EH&S) AND CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER, HAS ANNOUNCED HER DECISION TO RETIRE IN APRIL 2022, FOLLOWING MORE THAN 32 YEARS OF SERVICE WITH DOW.
