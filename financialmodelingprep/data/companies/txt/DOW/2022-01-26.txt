DOW NAMED TO BLOOMBERG'S GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX FOR SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR. MIDLAND, MICH., JAN. 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOW (NYSE: DOW) HAS BEEN NAMED TO BLOOMBERG'S GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI) FOR THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR, DEMONSTRATING ITS CONTINUED COMMITMENT TO GENDER INCLUSION AND DATA TRANSPARENCY.
