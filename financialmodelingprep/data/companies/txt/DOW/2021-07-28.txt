SIEMENS, DOW AND MXD PARTNER TO ENHANCE DIGITALIZATION IN THE PROCESS INDUSTRIES ​WITH PROCESS AUTOMATION TEST BED. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SIEMENS IS COLLABORATING WITH DOW TO SHOWCASE THE FUTURE OF AUTOMATION WITH A PROCESS INDUSTRY TEST BED AT MXD, A STATE-OF-THE-ART ADVANCED MANUFACTURING INSTITUTE AND INNOVATION CENTER NEAR DOWNTOWN CHICAGO. THIS NEW TEST BED OFFERS A HANDS-ON DEMONSTRATION OF HOW INNOVATIVE SOFTWARE AND IOT COME TOGETHER WITH HARDWARE TO ACCELERATE DIGITALIZATION FOR THE PROCESS INDUSTRIES.
