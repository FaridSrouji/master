DOW INC. REPORTS UNAUDITED CONSOLIDATED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THREE MONTHS AND CONSOLIDATED AND PROFORMA EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. DOW INC. REPORTED UNAUDITED CONSOLIDATED EARNINGS RESULTS FOR THE THREE AND NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2019. FOR THE THREE MONTHS, THE COMPANY REPORTED NET SALES OF $10,764 MILLION AGAINST $12,634 MILLION A YEAR AGO. INTEREST INCOME WAS $19 MILLION AGAINST $22 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS $347 MILLION AGAINST $1,049 MILLION A YEAR AGO. EARNINGS PER SHARE BASIC WERE $0.45 AGAINST $1.36 A YEAR AGO.FOR THE NINE MONTHS, THE COMPANY REPORTED NET SALES OF $32,747 MILLION AGAINST $37,660 MILLION A YEAR AGO. INTEREST INCOME WAS $58 MILLION AGAINST $60 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME WAS $1,038 MILLION AGAINST $3,852 MILLION A YEAR AGO. EARNINGS PER SHARE BASIC WERE $1.29 AGAINST $5.02 A YEAR AGO.FOR THE NINE MONTHS ON PROFORMA BASIS, THE COMPANY REPORTED NET SALES OF $32,794 MILLION AGAINST $37,844 MILLION A YEAR AGO. INTEREST INCOME WAS $59 MILLION AGAINST $60 MILLION A YEAR AGO. NET INCOME FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS AVAILABLE FOR COMPANY COMMON STOCKHOLDERS WAS $584 MILLION AGAINST $2,475 MILLION A YEAR AGO. EARNINGS PER SHARE BASIC FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS WERE $0.78 AGAINST $3.31 A YEAR AGO.
