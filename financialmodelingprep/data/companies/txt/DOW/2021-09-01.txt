DOW NAMED ONE OF THE "2021 PEOPLE COMPANIES THAT CARE®" FOR 2ND CONSECUTIVE YEAR. MIDLAND, MICH. , SEPT. 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  DOW (NYSE: DOW) HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE "2021 PEOPLE COMPANIES THAT CARE®" BY GREAT PLACE TO WORK® AND PEOPLE, RANKING 61 ON THE EXPANDED LIST OF 100 COMPANIES AND MARKING THE SECOND CONSECUTIVE YEAR THE COMPANY HAS EARNED A SPOT ON THIS PRESTIGIOUS LIST.
