DOW DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 70 CENTS PER SHARE. MIDLAND, MICH., AUG. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOW (NYSE: DOW) HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 70 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE SEPTEMBER 10, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD OF SAID STOCK AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 31, 2021.
