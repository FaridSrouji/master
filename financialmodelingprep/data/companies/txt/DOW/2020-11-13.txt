DOW LISTED TO 2020 DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX. MIDLAND, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, DOW (NYSE: DOW) WAS NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX (DJSI) BY S&P GLOBAL, THE INVESTMENT SPECIALIST FOCUSED EXCLUSIVELY ON SUSTAINABILITY INVESTING.
