DOW ADVANCES TO #7 ON DIVERSITYINC'S 2023 TOP 50 COMPANIES FOR DIVERSITY LIST. DOW MAKES THE LIST FOR A 6TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR MIDLAND, MICH. , MAY 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOW (NYSE: DOW) ADVANCED TO 7TH PLACE ON THE 2023 DIVERSITYINC TOP 50 COMPANIES FOR DIVERSITY LIST, WHICH WAS ANNOUNCED YESTERDAY AT DIVERSITYINC'S CELEBRATORY EVENT IN NEW YORK CITY.
