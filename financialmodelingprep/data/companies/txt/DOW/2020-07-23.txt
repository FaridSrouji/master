DOW INC REPORTS Q2 LOSS PER SHARE OF $0.31. DOW REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.Q2 SALES $8.4 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $8.01 BILLION.QTRLY GAAP LOSS PER SHARE WAS $0.31.QTRLY OPERATING LOSS PER SHARE WAS $0.26.TOTAL CASH AND AVAILABLE COMMITTED LIQUIDITY AT QUARTER-END WAS APPROXIMATELY $12 BILLION.HAS NO SUBSTANTIVE LONG-TERM DEBT DUE UNTIL SECOND HALF OF 2023.QTRLY PACKAGING & SPECIALTY PLASTICS NET SALES WERE $4 BILLION, DOWN 23% VERSUS YEAR-AGO PERIOD.WILL UPSIZE 2020 OPERATING EXPENSE REDUCTION TARGET FROM $350 MILLION TO $500 MILLION.QTRLY INDUSTRIAL INTERMEDIATES & INFRASTRUCTURE NET SALES WERE $2.4 BILLION, DOWN 28%.WILL ALSO INITIATE A RESTRUCTURING PROGRAM DURING QUARTER, TARGETING MORE THAN $300 MILLION IN ANNUALIZED EBITDA BENEFIT BY END OF 2021.QTRLY PERFORMANCE MATERIALS & COATINGS NET SALES WERE $1.9 BILLION, DOWN 21%.RESTRUCTURING PROGRAM PROGRAM INCLUDES A 6% REDUCTION IN DOW'S GLOBAL WORKFORCE AS WELL AS ACTIONS TO EXIT UNCOMPETITIVE ASSETS."WE CONTINUE TO EXPECT A GRADUAL AND UNEVEN RECOVERY".Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $-0.30 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
