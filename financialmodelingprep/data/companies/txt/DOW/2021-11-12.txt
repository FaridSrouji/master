MEIJER BRINGS RECYCLED PLASTIC BAGS FULL CIRCLE TO MICHIGAN STORE PARKING LOT IN PILOT PROGRAM USING DOW TECHNOLOGY. MIDLAND, MICH., NOV. 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN COLLABORATION WITH MEIJER, DOW (NYSE: DOW) ENABLED A NEW PAVING TECHNIQUE USING RECYCLED PLASTIC BAGS TO CREATE A MORE DURABLE PARKING LOT FOR THE RETAILER'S HOLLAND, MICH.
