DOW ANNOUNCES FULL REDEMPTION OF ALL OUTSTANDING 3.625% NOTES DUE MAY 2026. MIDLAND, MICH , JUNE 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE DOW CHEMICAL COMPANY ("TDCC"), A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF DOW INC. (NYSE: DOW), ANNOUNCED TODAY A FULL REDEMPTION OF ITS 3.625% NOTES DUE MAY 2026 (THE "NOTES") ISSUED PURSUANT TO AN INDENTURE DATED AS OF MAY 1, 2008 (AS SUPPLEMENTED, THE "INDENTURE") BETWEEN TDCC AND THE BANK OF NEW YORK MELLON TRUST COMPANY, N.A., AS TRUSTEE.
