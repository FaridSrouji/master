DOW NAMED TO BLOOMBERG'S 2021 GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX. MIDLAND, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IN RECOGNITION OF ITS COMMITMENT TO GENDER INCLUSION IN THE WORKPLACE, THE SUPPLY CHAIN AND THE COMMUNITIES IN WHICH IT OPERATES, DOW (NYSE:DOW) HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE 2021 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI). THIS IS THE FIRST YEAR DOW HAS BEEN INCLUDED IN THE GEI. EXPANDED THIS YEAR TO REPRESENT 44 COUNTRIES AND REGIONS, THE UNRANKED INDEX COMPRISES 380 COMPANIES ACROSS 11 SECTORS. DOW'S CUMULATIVE GEI SCORE SIGNIFICANTLY OUTPERFORMED THE MATERIALS SECTOR
