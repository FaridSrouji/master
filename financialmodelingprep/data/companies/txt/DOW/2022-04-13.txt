DOW ANNOUNCES NEW $3 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM; DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 70 CENTS PER SHARE. MIDLAND, MICH. , APRIL 13, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOW (NYSE: DOW) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A NEW SHARE BUYBACK PROGRAM FOR THE REPURCHASE OF UP TO $3 BILLION OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK.
