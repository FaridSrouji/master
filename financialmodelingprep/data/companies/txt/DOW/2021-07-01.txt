DOW CONTINUES TO ADVANCE INCREMENTAL, HIGH-RETURN CAPACITY EXPANSIONS ALIGNED TO FAST GROWING END-MARKETS. MIDLAND, MICH., JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOW (NYSE: DOW) TODAY ANNOUNCED A SERIES OF INCREMENTAL, HIGH-RETURN CAPACITY EXPANSIONS TO SUPPORT GROWING DEMAND ACROSS KEY END-MARKETS, ALL OF WHICH VALUE SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS AND CONTINUE TO GROW FASTER THAN GDP.
