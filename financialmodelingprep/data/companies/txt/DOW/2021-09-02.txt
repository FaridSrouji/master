DOW ANNOUNCES $2 MILLION SUPPORT TOWARD HURRICANE IDA RELIEF, RECOVERY AND RESILIENCY. MIDLAND, MICH., SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN RESPONSE TO THE WIDESPREAD DEVASTATION CAUSED BY HURRICANE IDA IN LOUISIANA, DOW (NYSE: DOW) AND THE DOW COMPANY FOUNDATION ANNOUNCED THE CONTRIBUTION OF $2 MILLION TO SUPPORT IMMEDIATE RELIEF AND LONG-TERM RECOVERY EFFORTS ASSOCIATED WITH THE STORM AND ITS AFTERMATH.
