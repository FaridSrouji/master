SEAGATE LYVE CLOUD EXPANDS TO NEW REGIONS AND ADDS KEY NEW OFFERINGS. FREMONT, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SEAGATE TECHNOLOGY HOLDINGS PLC (NASDAQ: STX), A WORLD LEADER IN MASS-DATA STORAGE INFRASTRUCTURE SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS EXTENSIVE PLAN TO EXPAND SEAGATE® LYVE™ CLOUD REGIONS AND OFFERINGS INCLUDING LYVE CLOUD ARCHIVE STORAGE SERVICE AND LYVE CLOUD MARKETPLACE. THE EXPANSION REINFORCES THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO SOLVING TODAY'S DATA COMPLEXITY CHALLENGES AND SERVING THE MASS DATA ECOSYSTEMS WITH THE OBJECT STORAGE SERVICE DESIGNED FOR MULTICLOUD FREEDO
