SEAGATE TECHNOLOGY REPORTS FISCAL SECOND QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS. FREMONT, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SEAGATE TECHNOLOGY PLC (NASDAQ: STX) (THE “COMPANY” OR “SEAGATE”) TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FISCAL SECOND QUARTER ENDED JANUARY 1, 2021. "SEAGATE DELIVERED STRONG, DOUBLE-DIGIT REVENUE, EARNINGS AND FREE CASH FLOW GROWTH IN THE DECEMBER QUARTER SUPPORTED BY BROAD-BASED IMPROVEMENT ACROSS NEARLY EVERY SERVED MARKET AND GEOGRAPHY, AND WE HAD SOLID CUSTOMER DEMAND FOR OUR MASS CAPACITY PRODUCTS," SAID DAVE MOSLEY, SEAGATE'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
