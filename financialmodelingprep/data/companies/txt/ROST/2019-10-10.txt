ROSS STORES, INC. FOR LESS TO OPEN A NEW STORE IN MINOT, NORTH DAKOTA. ROSS DRESS FOR LESS ("ROSS") WILL OPEN A NEW STORE IN MINOT ON OCTOBER 12, 2019. THE 18,000 SQUARE FOOT STORE IS LOCATED AT DAKOTA SQUARE MALL AT THE SOUTHEAST CORNER OF U.S. HIGHWAY 2 AND 16TH STREET. WITH THIS NEW OPENING, ROSS WILL OPERATE THREE STORES IN NORTH DAKOTA AND A TOTAL OF 1,551 LOCATIONS. RTOGETHER, ROSS DRESS FOR LESS® AND DD'S DISCOUNTS® CURRENTLY OPERATE OVER 1,800 OFF-PRICE APPAREL AND HOME FASHION STORES IN 39 STATES, THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, AND GUAM.
