ROSS STORES ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. DUBLIN, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROSS STORES, INC. (NASDAQ: ROST) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.31 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON JUNE 30, 2022 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 7, 2022. ROSS STORES, INC. IS AN S&P 500, FORTUNE 500, AND NASDAQ 100 (ROST) COMPANY HEADQUARTERED IN DUBLIN, CALIFORNIA, WITH FISCAL 2021 REVENUES OF $18.9 BILLION.
