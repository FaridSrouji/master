ROSS STORES WILL TEMPORARILY FURLOUGH MAJORITY OF ITS STORE AND DISTRIBUTION CENTER ASSOCIATES. ROSS STORES PROVIDES ADDITIONAL COVID-19 BUSINESS UPDATE.ROSS STORES INC - WILL TEMPORARILY FURLOUGH MAJORITY OF ITS STORE AND DISTRIBUTION CENTER ASSOCIATES.ROSS STORES INC - WILL PAY EMPLOYEE PORTION OF PREMIUMS FOR THOSE FURLOUGHED WHO CURRENTLY RECEIVE HEALTH BENEFITS THROUGH COMPANY.ROSS STORES INC - ALL FURLOUGHED EMPLOYEES WILL REMAIN ROSS ASSOCIATES WITH NO CHANGE TO THEIR HEALTH BENEFITS.ROSS STORES - WILL TEMPORARILY FURLOUGH SOME OTHER ASSOCIATES ACROSS BUSINESS STARTING ON APRIL 5 AND UNTIL OPERATIONS CAN RESUME IN THEIR AREAS..ROSS STORES INC - IN INTERIM, FURLOUGHED ASSOCIATES WILL BE ELIGIBLE TO APPLY FOR UNEMPLOYMENT BENEFITS.ROSS STORES INC - COMPANY'S OUTSIDE BOARD MEMBERS HAVE AGREED TO FORGO THEIR CASH RETAINER.ROSS STORES INC - MICHAEL BALMUTH, CHAIRMAN OF BOARD, AND CEO BARBARA RENTLER WILL RECEIVE NO SALARIES.ROSS STORES INC - SENIOR EXECUTIVE TEAM HAS ALSO AGREED TO TAKE SUBSTANTIAL SALARY CUTS DURING THIS PERIOD.ROSS STORES INC - HAD BEEN HOPING TO REOPEN STORES BY APRIL 4; BUT INCREASING SPREAD OF CORONAVIRUS PREVENTS CO FROM DOING SO..
