ROSS STORES, INC. OPENS A NEW STORE IN CUYAHOGA FALLS, OHIO. ROSS STORES, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ROSS DRESS FOR LESS WILL OPEN A NEW STORE IN CUYAHOGA FALLS ON OCTOBER 12, 2019. THE 22,000 SQUARE FOOT STORE IS LOCATED AT THE PLAZA AT CHAPEL HILL AT THE SOUTHEAST CORNER OF HOWE AVENUE AND MAIN STREET. WITH THIS NEW OPENING, ROSS WILL OPERATE FOUR STORES IN OHIO AND A TOTAL OF 1,544 LOCATIONS. TOGETHER, ROSS DRESS FOR LESS AND DD'S DISCOUNTS CURRENTLY OPERATE OVER 1,800 OFF-PRICE APPAREL AND HOME FASHION STORES IN 39 STATES, THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, AND GUAM.
