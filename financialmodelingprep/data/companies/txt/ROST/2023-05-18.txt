ROSS STORES REPORTS FIRST QUARTER EARNINGS. DUBLIN, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROSS STORES, INC. (NASDAQ: ROST) TODAY REPORTED EARNINGS PER SHARE FOR THE 13 WEEKS ENDED APRIL 29, 2023 OF $1.09 ON NET EARNINGS OF $371 MILLION. THESE RESULTS COMPARE TO EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $0.97 ON NET INCOME OF $338 MILLION FOR THE 13 WEEKS ENDED APRIL 30, 2022.
