ROSS STORES, INC. OPENS 42 NEW LOCATIONS. ROSS STORES, INC. ANNOUNCED THE RECENT OPENING OF 30 ROSS DRESS FOR LESS® ("ROSS") AND 12 DD'S DISCOUNTS® STORES ACROSS 19 DIFFERENT STATES IN SEPTEMBER AND OCTOBER. THESE NEW LOCATIONS COMPLETE THE COMPANY'S STORE GROWTH PLANS FOR FISCAL 2019 WITH THE ADDITION OF 98 NEW STORES.
