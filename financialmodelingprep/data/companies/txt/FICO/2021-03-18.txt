FICO HOSTS FREE VIRTUAL FINANCIAL EDUCATION EVENT FOR CHICAGO CONSUMERS. CHICAGO, MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING ANALYTICS SOFTWARE FIRM FICO (NYSE:FICO), WILL HOST A FREE FINANCIAL EDUCATION ONLINE EVENT FOR CHICAGO CONSUMERS ON MARCH 23 TO PROVIDE ATTENDEES WITH KNOWLEDGE AND TOOLS TO GAIN BETTER INSIGHT INTO THEIR FINANCIAL HEALTH. THE EVENT IS PART OF FICO'S "SCORE A BETTER FUTURE" PROGRAM, WHICH FOCUSES ON HELPING CONSUMERS IMPROVE THEIR UNDERSTANDING OF THEIR CREDIT AND OVERALL FINANCIAL HEALTH.
