FICO ANNOUNCES NEW STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM. BOZEMAN, MONT., NOV. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FICO (NYSE:FICO), A LEADING PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS AND DECISION MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM TO ACQUIRE UP TO $500 MILLION OF THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK.
