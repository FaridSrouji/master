FAIR ISAAC & CO. SET TO JOIN S&P 500; OTHERS TO JOIN S&P MIDCAP 400 AND S&P SMALLCAP 600. NEW YORK , MARCH 3, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES ("S&P DJI") WILL MAKE THE FOLLOWING CHANGES TO THE S&P 500, S&P MIDCAP 400 AND S&P SMALLCAP 600 INDICES EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPEN OF TRADING ON MONDAY, MARCH 20, TO COINCIDE WITH THE QUARTERLY REBALANCE. THE CHANGES ARE BEING MADE TO ENSURE EACH INDEX IS MORE REPRESENTATIVE OF ITS MARKET CAPITALIZATION RANGE.
