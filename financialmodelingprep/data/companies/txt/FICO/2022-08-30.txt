AVERAGE U.S. FICO SCORE REMAINS STEADY AT 716. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEW ANALYSIS FROM FICO (NYSE: FICO), A LEADING ANALYTICS SOFTWARE FIRM, SHOWS THAT THE NATIONAL AVERAGE FICO® SCORE CURRENTLY SITS AT 716, THE SAME AS IT WAS A YEAR AGO. FICO® SCORES ARE DYNAMIC AND EVOLVE AS CHANGES IN CONSUMER BEHAVIOR ARE REFLECTED IN THE UNDERLYING CREDIT BUREAU DATA HOUSED AND MANAGED BY THE THREE PRIMARY U.S. CONSUMER REPORTING AGENCIES (CRAS).
