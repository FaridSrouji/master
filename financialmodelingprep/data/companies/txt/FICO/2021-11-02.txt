JIM WEHMANN, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF SCORES AT FICO, JOINS MORTGAGE BANKER'S ASSOCIATION BOARD. SAN JOSE, CALIF., NOV. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING ANALYTICS SOFTWARE FIRM FICO (NYSE:FICO), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JIM WEHMANN, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF SCORES AT FICO, HAS JOINED THE MORTGAGE BANKER'S ASSOCIATION (MBA) BOARD.
