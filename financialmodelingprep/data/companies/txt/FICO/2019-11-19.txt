HOME CREDIT IN RUSSIA TO USE FICO OPTIMISATION. FICO ANNOUNCED THAT HOME CREDIT N.V. WILL USE FICO OPTIMISATION TOOLS AND METHODOLOGIES TO IMPROVE ITS CASH LOAN AND CREDIT CARD PORTFOLIOS IN RUSSIA. THESE NEW STRATEGIES WILL PROVIDE OPTIMIZED CREDIT OFFERS FOR BOTH EXISTING AND NEW CUSTOMERS, AIMED AT INCREASING ACCEPTANCE RATES AND BUILDING THE LENDER'S PORTFOLIO. IN ADDITION, AS FICO DECISION OPTIMIZER CAN BE USED TO DEVELOP LENDING STRATEGIES ACROSS THE CUSTOMER LIFECYCLE, HOME CREDIT RUSSIA WILL BE LOOKING TO USE IT TO DEVELOP DECISION MODELS FOR ITS OTHER PRODUCTS AND DECISIONS.
