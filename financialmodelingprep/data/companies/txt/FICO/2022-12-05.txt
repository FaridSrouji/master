FICO SURVEY: UK CONSUMERS WILL CHOOSE BANKS BASED ON FRAUD PROTECTION. LONDON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--GLOBAL ANALYTICS SOFTWARE COMPANY FICO HAS REVEALED THE FINDINGS FROM ITS LATEST FRAUD SURVEYI WITH CONSUMERS SHOWING THAT STRONG FRAUD PROTECTION CAN BE A COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATOR. WHEN ASKED TO RANK THEIR CONSIDERATIONS IN SELECTING A FINANCIAL ACCOUNT PROVIDER, THE TOP FACTOR FOR 30 PERCENT WAS GOOD FRAUD PREVENTION. THIS CAME ABOVE VALUE FOR MONEY AT 26 PERCENT.
