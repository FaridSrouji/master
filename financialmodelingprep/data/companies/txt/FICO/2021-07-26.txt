FICO UK CREDIT MARKET REPORT MAY 2021: MARKEDLY LOWER YEAR-ON-YEAR INCREASE IN CREDIT CARD SPEND SUGGESTS CONSUMERS ARE ADJUSTING TO 'NEW NORMAL'. LONDON, JULY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL ANALYTICS SOFTWARE PROVIDER FICO TODAY RELEASED ITS ANALYSIS OF UK CARD TRENDS FOR MAY 2021, WHICH SUGGESTS THAT SOME CONSUMERS ARE PRACTICING PRAGMATIC FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT, AS WELL AS CONTINUING TO MAKE USE OF SAVINGS ACCRUED DURING THE PANDEMIC. THERE ARE, HOWEVER, WARNING SIGNS OF THE FINANCIAL PRESSURE GROWING FOR THOSE ALREADY IN DEBT.
