FICO SURVEY: HALF OF THAIS BELIEVE IT IS OK TO EXAGGERATE INCOME ON LOAN APPLICATIONS AND INSURANCE CLAIMS. BANGKOK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FICO (NYSE: FICO), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF ANALYTICS SOFTWARE, TODAY ANNOUNCED FURTHER FINDINGS FROM ITS CONSUMER FRAUD SURVEY EXPLORING ATTITUDES AND PREFERENCES TOWARDS FRAUD CHECKS. THE STUDY REVEALED THAT ALMOST HALF OF THAIS ARE WILLING TO COMMIT FRAUD TO OBTAIN A LOAN OR FILE AN INSURANCE CLAIM. HOWEVER, IT ALSO HIGHLIGHTED THAT FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS CAN GENERATE INCREASED REVENUE AND DRIVE SALES THROUGH A SUCCESSFUL FRAUD PROTECTION FUNCTION. MORE INFORMAT.
