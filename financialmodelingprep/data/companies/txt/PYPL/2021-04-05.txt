THE SAN JOSE EARTHQUAKES INTRODUCE PAYPAL PARK. SAN JOSE, CALIF., APR. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PAYPAL HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: PYPL) AND THE SAN JOSE EARTHQUAKES TODAY ANNOUNCED A MULTI-YEAR SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT THAT INCLUDES NAMING RIGHTS FOR THE MAJOR LEAGUE SOCCER (MLS) TEAM'S STADIUM LOCATED IN SAN JOSE, CALIFORNIA, WHICH WILL BE CALLED PAYPAL PARK.
