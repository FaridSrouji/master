MOORE KUEHN IS INVESTIGATING PAYPAL INSIDERS AFTER INSIDERS SOLD APPROXIMATELY $100 MILLION OF PAYPAL STOCK FROM THEIR INSIDER STOCK SALES.

NEW YORK , NOV. 22, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KLEIN LAW FIRM ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION COMPLAINT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS OF PAYPAL HOLDINGS, INC. (NASDAQ: PYPL) ALLEGING THAT THE COMPANY VIOLATED FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
