PAYPAL ANNOUNCES NEW EMPLOYEE INDUCEMENT GRANTS. SAN JOSE, CALIF. , SEPT. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PAYPAL HOLDINGS, INC. (NASDAQ: PYPL) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS GRANTED EQUITY AWARDS ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2022, UNDER ITS 2022 INDUCEMENT EQUITY INCENTIVE PLAN (THE "PLAN") TO NEW EMPLOYEES WHO JOINED PAYPAL.
