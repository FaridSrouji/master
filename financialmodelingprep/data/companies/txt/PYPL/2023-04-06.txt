BOLD COMMERCE COLLABORATES WITH PAYPAL TO LAUNCH TAILORED CHECKOUT ON ADOBE COMMERCE. AUSTIN, TEXAS & WINNIPEG, MANITOBA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BOLD COMMERCE, THE TECHNOLOGY COMPANY THAT POWERS TAILORED CHECKOUT EXPERIENCES FOR LEADING OMNICHANNEL RETAILERS AND DTC BRANDS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS HEADLESS CHECKOUT INTEGRATION WITH MAGENTO OPEN SOURCE AND ADOBE COMMERCE. BOLD COLLABORATED WITH PAYPAL (NASDAQ: PYPL) TO CREATE THE NEW INTEGRATION.
