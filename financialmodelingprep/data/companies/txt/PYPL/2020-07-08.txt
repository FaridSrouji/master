SEZZLE PROMOTES CHIEF REVENUE OFFICER PAUL PARADIS TO PRESIDENT. EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR AND CHIEF REVENUE OFFICER, PAUL PARADIS, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO NEWLY CREATED ROLE OF PRESIDENT.VERONICA KATZ APPOINTED AS CHIEF REVENUE OFFICER.KATZ HAS BEEN AT PAYPAL SINCE 2011, MOST RECENTLY SERVING AS VP OF GLOBAL ACCOUNTS.
