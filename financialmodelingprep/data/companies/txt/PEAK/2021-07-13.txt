HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES PUBLISHES ITS 10TH ANNUAL ESG REPORT. DENVER, JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PUBLISHED ITS 10TH ANNUAL ESG REPORT.  AS THE FIRST U.S. HEALTHCARE REIT TO PUBLISH AN ESG REPORT, HEALTHPEAK HAS BEEN COMMITTED TO CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY AND TRANSPARENT REPORTING FOR OVER A DECADE.
