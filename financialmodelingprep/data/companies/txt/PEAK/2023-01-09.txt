HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES PRICES $400 MILLION OF 5.250% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2032. DENVER , JAN. 9, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $400 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 5.250% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2032. THE PRICE TO INVESTORS WAS 99.883% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF THE NOTES.
