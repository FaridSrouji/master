HCP, INC. DECLARES QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK CASH DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON NOVEMBER 19, 2019. HCP, INC. ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.37 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON NOVEMBER 19, 2019, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 4, 2019.
