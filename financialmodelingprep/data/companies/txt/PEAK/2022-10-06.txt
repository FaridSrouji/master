HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES ANNOUNCES CHANGES TO EXECUTIVE TEAM. SCOTT BRINKER APPOINTED PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND ELECTED AS MEMBER OF THE BOARD TOM HERZOG STEPS DOWN AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND BOARD DIRECTOR SCOTT BOHN NAMED CHIEF DEVELOPMENT OFFICER AND ADAM MABRY APPOINTED CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER DENVER , OCT. 6, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE "BOARD") HAS APPOINTED SCOTT BRINKER, THE COMPANY'S PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER, AS PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AND ELECTED HIM TO THE HEALTHPEAK BOARD, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MR. BRINKER SUCCEEDS TOM HERZOG, WHO MUTUALLY AGREED WITH THE BOARD TO STEP DOWN AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND FROM THE BOARD TO PURSUE OTHER ENDEAVORS.
