HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES PRICES $500 MILLION OF 2.125% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2028 IN A GREEN BOND OFFERING. DENVER, NOV. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED A PUBLIC GREEN BOND OFFERING OF $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.125% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2028. THE PRICE TO INVESTORS WAS 99.398% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF THE NOTES.
