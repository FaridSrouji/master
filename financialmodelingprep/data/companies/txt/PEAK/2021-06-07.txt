HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES ANNOUNCES NEW LIFE SCIENCE DEVELOPMENT - SORRENTO GATEWAY - IN SAN DIEGO. DENVER, JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL COMMENCE CONSTRUCTION OF ITS SORRENTO GATEWAY DEVELOPMENT LOCATED IN THE SORRENTO MESA SUBMARKET OF SAN DIEGO. THIS CLASS A DEVELOPMENT WILL CONSIST OF A FIVE-STORY BUILDING TOTALING APPROXIMATELY 163,000 SQUARE FEET LOCATED ADJACENT TO HEALTHPEAK'S FULLY-LEASED THREE-BUILDING, 196,000 SQUARE FOOT SORRENTO GATEWAY CAMPUS.
HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES ANNOUNCES FULL-CAMPUS LEASE FOR CALLAN RIDGE DENSIFICATION IN SAN DIEGO. DENVER, JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TURNING POINT THERAPEUTICS, INC. HAS EXECUTED A LONG-TERM LEASE FOR THE ENTIRE CALLAN RIDGE DENSIFICATION PROJECT LOCATED IN THE TORREY PINES SUBMARKET OF SAN DIEGO. THE LEASE IS EXPECTED TO COMMENCE AT THE END OF 2022, UPON COMPLETION OF CONSTRUCTION.
