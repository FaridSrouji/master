HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES EARNS 2022 ENERGY STAR PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARD; NAMED TO THE FORTUNE MODERN BOARD 25 LIST. DENVER , MAY 23, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS EARNED THE 2022 ENERGY STAR® PARTNER OF THE YEAR AWARD FROM THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY AND THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY, MARKING HEALTHPEAK'S SECOND TIME RECEIVING THE AWARD.  HEALTHPEAK WAS ALSO NAMED TO THE FORTUNE INAUGURAL MODERN BOARD 25 LIST.
