HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES DECLARES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK. DENVER, JULY 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON AUGUST 20, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 9, 2021.
