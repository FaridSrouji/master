VTRS ALERT: THE LAW OFFICES OF VINCENT WONG INVESTIGATE VIATRIS INC. FOR POTENTIAL VIOLATIONS OF SECURITIES LAWS. NEW YORK , MAY 8, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ATTENTION VIATRIS INC. ("VIATRIS" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQGS: VTRS) SHAREHOLDERS: THE LAW OFFICES OF VINCENT WONG ANNOUNCE THAT AN INVESTIGATION HAS COMMENCED INTO VIATRIS AND CERTAIN OF ITS OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS REGARDING POTENTIAL SECURITIES FRAUD. THE INVESTIGATION CONCERNS, IN PART, THE BELOW: BEFORE THE MARKET OPENED ON FEBRUARY 28, 2022, VIATRIS UNEXPECTEDLY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY HAD ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS BIOSIMILARS BUSINESS TO BIOCON BIOLOGICS LIMITED, WHICH WAS ANTICIPATED TO CLOSE IN THE SECOND HALF OF 2022.
