VTRS LAWSUIT ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES VIATRIS INC. INVESTORS OF A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AND UPCOMING DEADLINE. NEW YORK , JUNE 29, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP NOTIFIES INVESTORS IN VIATRIS INC. ("VIATRIS" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: VTRS) OF A CLASS ACTION SECURITIES LAWSUIT. CLASS DEFINITION: THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER LOSSES ON BEHALF OF VIATRIS INVESTORS WHO WERE ADVERSELY AFFECTED BY ALLEGED SECURITIES FRAUD BETWEEN MARCH 1, 2021 AND FEBRUARY 25, 2022.
