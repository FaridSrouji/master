VTRS INVESTOR ALERT: LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP REMINDS INVESTORS OF AN INVESTIGATION INVOLVING POSSIBLE SECURITIES FRAUD VIOLATIONS BY OFFICERS OF VIATRIS INC.. NEW YORK , APRIL 28, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEVI & KORSINSKY NOTIFIES INVESTORS THAT IT HAS COMMENCED AN INVESTIGATION OF VIATRIS INC. ("VIATRIS" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQGS: VTRS) CONCERNING POSSIBLE VIOLATIONS OF FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.   BEFORE THE MARKET OPENED ON FEBRUARY 28, 2022, VIATRIS UNEXPECTEDLY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY HAD ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS BIOSIMILARS BUSINESS TO BIOCON BIOLOGICS LIMITED, WHICH WAS ANTICIPATED TO CLOSE IN THE SECOND HALF OF 2022.
