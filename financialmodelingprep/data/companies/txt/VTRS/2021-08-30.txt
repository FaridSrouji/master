VIATRIS INC. TO PARTICIPATE IN CITI'S 16TH ANNUAL BIOPHARMA CONFERENCE. PITTSBURGH, AUG. 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VIATRIS INC. (NASDAQ: VTRS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY WILL TAKE PART IN A VIRTUAL FIRESIDE CHAT AT CITI'S 16TH ANNUAL BIOPHARMA CONFERENCE ON FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2021. CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER MICHAEL GOETTLER  AND PRESIDENT RAJIV MALIK  WILL ANSWER QUESTIONS ABOUT THE COMPANY AT 11:35 A.M.
