VIATRIS TO PARTICIPATE IN EVERCORE ISI 4TH ANNUAL HEALTHCONX CONFERENCE. PITTSBURGH, NOV. 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VIATRIS INC. (NASDAQ: VTRS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY WILL TAKE PART IN A VIRTUAL FIRESIDE CHAT AT THE EVERCORE ISI 4TH ANNUAL HEALTHCONX CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 1, 2021. CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER MICHAEL GOETTLER , PRESIDENT RAJIV MALIK  AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER SANJEEV NARULA  WILL ANSWER QUESTIONS ABOUT THE COMPANY AT 7:30 A.M.
