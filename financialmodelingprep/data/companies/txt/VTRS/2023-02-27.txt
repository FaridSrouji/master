VIATRIS ANNOUNCES APPOINTMENT OF SCOTT A. SMITH AS CEO EFFECTIVE APRIL 1, 2023. SMITH, A SEASONED SENIOR HEALTHCARE EXECUTIVE, WILL LEAD THE COMPANY'S PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED PHASE 2 STRATEGY AND EXECUTION BOARD OF DIRECTORS THANKS MICHAEL GOETTLER, WHO WILL SUPPORT A SMOOTH TRANSITION PRIOR TO HIS DEPARTURE PITTSBURGH , FEB. 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VIATRIS INC. (NASDAQ: VTRS), A GLOBAL HEALTHCARE COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT AS IT PREPARES TO ENTER PHASE 2 OF ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED STRATEGIC PLAN IN 2024, SCOTT A. SMITH HAS BEEN APPOINTED BY ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS TO LEAD THE EXECUTION OF THAT PLAN AS VIATRIS' NEW CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1, 2023.
