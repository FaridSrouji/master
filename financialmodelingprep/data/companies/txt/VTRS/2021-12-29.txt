VIATRIS WINS COURT DECISIONS ON SANOFI APPEALS OF LANTUS® PATENT INVALIDATIONS. PITTSBURGH, DEC. 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VIATRIS INC. (NASDAQ: VTRS) ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS PLEASED WITH DECISIONS ISSUED TODAY WHICH AFFIRM THE U.S. PATENT AND TRADEMARK APPEAL BOARD'S PRIOR RULINGS THAT FOUND THE CHALLENGED CLAIMS OF SANOFI'S LANTUS® SOLOSTAR® DEVICE PATENTS, U.S. PATENT NOS. 9,603,044, 8,992,486, 9,526,844, 9,604,008, AND 8,679,069, UNPATENTABLE.
