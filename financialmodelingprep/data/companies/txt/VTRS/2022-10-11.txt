VIATRIS NAMED TO FORBES' ANNUAL LIST OF THE WORLD'S BEST EMPLOYERS FOR THE SECOND YEAR IN A ROW. COMPANY RISES TO THE TOP 10% OF THE 800 GLOBAL EMPLOYERS INCLUDED ON THE LIST PITTSBURGH , OCT. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VIATRIS INC. (NASDAQ: VTRS), A GLOBAL PHARMACEUTICAL COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO FORBES' LIST OF WORLD'S BEST EMPLOYERS 2022. VIATRIS HAS RECEIVED THIS RECOGNITION IN EACH OF ITS FIRST TWO YEARS SINCE LAUNCHING IN 2020 AS A NEW COMPANY.
