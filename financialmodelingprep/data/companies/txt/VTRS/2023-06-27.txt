VTRS INVESTORS HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD VIATRIS INC. SECURITIES FRAUD LAWSUIT. LOS ANGELES , JUNE 27, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP ("GPM") ANNOUNCES THAT INVESTORS WITH SUBSTANTIAL LOSSES HAVE OPPORTUNITY TO LEAD THE SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT AGAINST VIATRIS INC. ("VIATRIS" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: VTRS). CLASS PERIOD: MARCH 1, 2021 – FEBRUARY 25, 2022 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE: JULY 14, 2023 IF YOU WISH TO SERVE AS LEAD PLAINTIFF OF THE VIATRIS LAWSUIT, YOU CAN SUBMIT YOUR CONTACT INFORMATION AT WWW.GLANCYLAW.COM/CASES/VIATRIS-INC/.
