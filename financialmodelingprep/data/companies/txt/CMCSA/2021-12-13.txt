COMCAST APPOINTMENTS CAROLYNE HANNON AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF WESTERN NEW ENGLAND REGION. BERLIN, CONN., DEC. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMCAST HAS NAMED CAROLYNE HANNAN AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY'S WESTERN NEW ENGLAND REGION, LEADING A TEAM SERVING MORE THAN 300 COMMUNITIES ACROSS CONNECTICUT, WESTERN MASSACHUSETTS, VERMONT, WESTERN NEW HAMPSHIRE AND NEW YORK.
