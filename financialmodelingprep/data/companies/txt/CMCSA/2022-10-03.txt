COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL DONATES $2 MILLION IN CASH AND IN-KIND TO SUPPORT HURRICANE IAN RELIEF EFFORTS. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL ALONG WITH THE UNIVERSAL ORLANDO FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL DONATE $2 MILLION TO SUPPORT RELIEF AND RECOVERY EFFORTS IN FLORIDA FOLLOWING THE CATASTROPHIC FLOODING AND DEVASTATING EFFECTS OF HURRICANE IAN. THE COMMITMENT INCLUDES $1 MILLION IN CASH AND IN-KIND CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE FLORIDA DISASTER FUND WITH THE REMAINDER DISTRIBUTED AMONG THE COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE FUND, UNITED WAY, SECOND HARVEST FOOD BANK OF CENTRAL
