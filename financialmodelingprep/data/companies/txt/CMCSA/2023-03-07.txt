COMCAST BUSINESS RECOGNIZED IN 2023 GARTNER® MAGIC QUADRANT™ FOR NETWORK SERVICES, GLOBAL. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST BUSINESS TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED IN THE 2023 GARTNER MAGIC QUADRANT FOR NETWORK SERVICES, GLOBAL. THIS RECOGNITION COMES ON THE HEELS OF COMCAST BUSINESS'S RECOGNITION IN THE 2022 GARTNER MAGIC QUADRANT FOR MANAGED NETWORK SERVICES AND FURTHER VALIDATES THE COMPANY AS A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF NETWORK SOLUTIONS.
