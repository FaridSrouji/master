COMCAST OPENS NEW XFINITY STORE IN ALEXANDRIA. INTERACTIVE RETAIL STORE IN PRIME LOCATION WILL PROVIDE IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE FOR RESIDENTS AND SERVE AS THE COMPANY'S 6TH XFINITY STORE IN NORTHERN VIRGINIA ALEXANDRIA, VA. , JULY 25, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMCAST TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF A NEW XFINITY STORE LOCATED IN THE SHOPPES OF FOXCHASE AT 4609 DUKE STREET SUITE #19B IN ALEXANDRIA, VIRGINIA.
