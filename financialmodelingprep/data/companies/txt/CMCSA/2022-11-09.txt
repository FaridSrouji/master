COMCAST APPOINTS MICHAEL SLOVIN VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER OF COMCAST SMART SOLUTIONS. INDUSTRY VETERAN WILL LEAD COMCAST'S NEWEST BUSINESS OFFERING, LOOKING TO BRIDGE THE GAP BETWEEN TECHNOLOGY AND ENABLEMENT FOR COMMUNITIES OF ALL KINDS PHILADELPHIA , NOV. 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMCAST HAS NAMED MICHAEL SLOVIN AS VICE PRESIDENT AND GENERAL MANAGER OF COMCAST SMART SOLUTIONS. IN THIS NEW ROLE, HE WILL OVERSEE INTEGRATION OF COMCAST'S ADVANCED CONNECTIVITY OPTIONS WITH INDUSTRY-LEADING SMART TECHNOLOGY PARTNERS TO ENABLE THE DELIVERY OF WORLD-CLASS SMART TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS TO CLIENTS.
