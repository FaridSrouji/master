COMCAST DONATES $25,000 TO CODEVA AND 500 LAPTOPS TO RICHMOND PUBLIC SCHOOLS STUDENTS TO MARK THE 10TH ANNIVERSARY OF INTERNET ESSENTIALS. RICHMOND, VA., OCT. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMCAST TODAY ANNOUNCED A $25,000 GRANT TO SUPPORT CODEVA AND ITS FREE COMPUTER SCIENCE PROGRAMS FOR CITY STUDENTS AND TEACHERS, ALONG WITH A DONATION OF 500 FREE LAPTOPS, WHICH WILL BE DISTRIBUTED TO RICHMOND PUBLIC SCHOOLS STUDENTS AND RESIDENT SENIORS ACROSS THE CITY.
