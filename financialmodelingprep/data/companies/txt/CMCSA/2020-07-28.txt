UNIVERSAL FILMED ENTERTAINMENT GROUP AND AMC ENTERTAINMENT ANNOUNCE AGREEMENT FOR EXHIBITION OF UNIVERSAL FILMS. UNIVERSAL FILMED ENTERTAINMENT GROUP AND AMC ENTERTAINMENT ANNOUNCE AGREEMENT FOR EXHIBITION OF UNIVERSAL FILMS.AMC ENTERTAINMENT HOLDINGS INC - FULL TERMS OF DEAL ARE CONFIDENTIAL AND ARE NOT BEING DISCLOSED.AMC ENTERTAINMENT HOLDINGS INC - ANNOUNCED A MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH UFEG FILMS WILL BE EXHIBITED IN AMC THEATRES IN U.S..AMC ENTERTAINMENT - AGREEMENT INCLUDES AT LEAST THREE WEEKENDS OF THEATRICAL EXCLUSIVITY FOR UNIVERSAL PICTURES, FOCUS FEATURES THEATRICAL RELEASES.AMC-  IN COMING WEEKS, CO, UFEG WILL BEGIN DISCUSSIONS ON INTERNATIONAL DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENTS IN COUNTRIES IN EUROPE, MIDDLE EAST SERVED BY AMC.AMC ENTERTAINMENT HOLDINGS INC - UNIVERSAL'S TRADITIONAL WINDOWS FOR ELECTRONIC SELL THROUGH (EST) AND VIDEO ON DEMAND (VOD) REMAIN UNCHANGED.AMC ENTERTAINMENT HOLDINGS INC - "HIGHLY CONFIDENT THAT MOVIEGOERS WILL COME TO OUR THEATRES IN HUGE NUMBERS IN A POST-PANDEMIC WORLD".
