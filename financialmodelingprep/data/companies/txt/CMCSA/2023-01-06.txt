COMCAST PROMOTES JASON S. ARMSTRONG TO CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. PHILADELPHIA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--COMCAST CORPORATION TODAY ANNOUNCED JASON S. ARMSTRONG HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. ARMSTRONG ELEVATES TO THE POSITION AFTER SERVING NINE YEARS IN SEVERAL FINANCIAL LEADERSHIP POSITIONS AT COMCAST, INCLUDING MOST RECENTLY AS DEPUTY CFO AND TREASURER. HE SUCCEEDS MIKE CAVANAGH WHO WAS NAMED PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY IN OCTOBER.
