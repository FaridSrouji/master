HISPANIC FEDERATION AND COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL TELEMUNDO ANNOUNCE NEW PARTNERSHIP TO ADVANCE DIGITAL EQUITY AMONG LATINO COMMUNITIES NATIONWIDE. WASHINGTON & PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, THE HISPANIC FEDERATION (HF), IN PARTNERSHIP WITH COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL TELEMUNDO, ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF THE LATINO DIGITAL EQUITY CENTERS INITIATIVE TO STRENGTHEN WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT AND DIGITAL TRAINING PROGRAMS IN THE LATINO COMMUNITY. THIS WILL HELP INCREASE DIGITAL EQUITY FOR THOUSANDS OF LATINOS, WHO MAKE UP 14% OF THE WORKFORCE YET REPRESENT 35% OF WORKERS WITH NO DIGITAL SKILLS.
