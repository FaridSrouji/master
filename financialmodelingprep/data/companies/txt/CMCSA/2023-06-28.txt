COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL SPORTSTECH WELCOMES THE PREMIER LEAGUE TO ITS PARTNER CONSORTIUM. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PREMIER LEAGUE, THE ORGANIZING BODY OF THE ENGLISH PREMIER LEAGUE FOOTBALL COMPETITION, HAS JOINED THE PARTNER CONSORTIUM FOR THE COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL SPORTSTECH ACCELERATOR, POWERED BY BOOMTOWN, AS STRATEGIC ADVISORS TO THE STARTUPS SELECTED FOR THE ANNUAL PROGRAM, WHICH IS CURRENTLY MIDWAY THROUGH ITS THIRD COHORT.
