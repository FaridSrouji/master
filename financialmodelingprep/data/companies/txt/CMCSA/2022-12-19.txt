COMCAST PROMOTES AMY LYNCH TO PRESIDENT OF COMCAST CABLE'S NORTHEAST DIVISION. PHILADELPHIA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--COMCAST TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS PROMOTED AMY LYNCH TO PRESIDENT OF COMCAST CABLE'S NORTHEAST DIVISION. LYNCH WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR ALL COMCAST CABLE OPERATIONS IN 14 NORTHEASTERN STATES FROM MAINE THROUGH VIRGINIA AND THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, SERVING APPROXIMATELY 8 MILLION CUSTOMERS.
