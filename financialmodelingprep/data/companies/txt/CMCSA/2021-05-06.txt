COMCAST LAUNCHES SEVEN WIFI-CONNECTED "LIFT ZONES" ACROSS WESTERN MASSACHUSETTS. SPRINGFIELD, MASS., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF ITS ONGOING COMMITMENT TO HELP CONNECT LOW-INCOME FAMILIES TO THE INTERNET SO THEY CAN FULLY PARTICIPATE IN EDUCATIONAL OPPORTUNITIES AND THE DIGITAL ECONOMY, COMCAST TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS EQUIPPED SEVEN NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS IN WESTERN MASSACHUSETTS WITH WIFI-CONNECTED "LIFT ZONES".
