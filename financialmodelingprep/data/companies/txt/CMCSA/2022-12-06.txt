THE UNIVERSAL SPHERE ON THE COMCAST CENTER CAMPUS IS NAMED A CERTIFIED AUTISM CENTER™. PHILADELPHIA--( BUSINESS WIRE )--COMCAST ANNOUNCED THAT THE UNIVERSAL SPHERE, LOCATED IN THE COMCAST TECHNOLOGY CENTER IN PHILADELPHIA, HAS FURTHER EXPAND ITS ACCESSIBILITY OFFERINGS WITH THE LAUNCH OF A SENSORY-FRIENDLY EXPERIENCE. THE SENSORY-FRIENDLY VERSION WILL INCORPORATE TECHNICAL UPDATES TO CREATE A MORE WELCOMING EXPERIENCE FOR AUTISTIC OR SENSORY-SENSITIVE PEOPLE, AND THOSE WITH SOCIAL, LEARNING, OR COGNITIVE NEEDS. GUESTS WHO REQUEST A SENSORY-FRIENDLY EXPERIENCE CAN EXPECT MODIFICATIONS TO SOUND LEVELS, REDUCED MOVEMENTS, AND LIGHTING ADJUSTMENTS TO REDUCE BLUE LIGHT AND ELIMINATE FLASHING.
