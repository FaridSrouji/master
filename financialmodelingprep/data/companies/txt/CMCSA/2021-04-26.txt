COMCAST ADVERTISING APPOINTS RICK MANDLER AS VP, GROWTH STRATEGY. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST ADVERTISING, A DIVISION OF COMCAST, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS APPOINTED RICK MANDLER AS VICE PRESIDENT, GROWTH STRATEGY. IN THIS NEWLY CREATED ROLE, MANDLER WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR FORMULATING GROWTH MARKETING STRATEGIES ACROSS THE COMCAST ADVERTISING PORTFOLIO WITH THE GOAL OF ADVANCING REVENUE GROWTH AND MARKET SHARE.
