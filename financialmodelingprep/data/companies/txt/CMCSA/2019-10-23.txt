COMCAST CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 29, 2020. COMCAST CORPORATION ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.21 A SHARE ON THE COMPANY’S COMMON STOCK. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 29, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 8, 2020.
