COMCAST CORPORATION NAMES BRODERICK D. JOHNSON EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PUBLIC POLICY AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, DIGITAL EQUITY. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST CORPORATION ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT BRODERICK D. JOHNSON WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PUBLIC POLICY AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, DIGITAL EQUITY. MR. JOHNSON WILL OVERSEE THE COMPANY'S PUBLIC POLICY TEAM IN WASHINGTON, DC, AND WILL TAKE OVERALL RESPONSIBILITY FOR COMCAST'S LEADING ROLE IN THE DIGITAL EQUITY SPACE. HE WILL REPORT TO TOM REID, CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER AND SECRETARY. MR. JOHNSON WILL SUCCEED REBECCA ARBOGAST LEADING THE PUBLIC P
