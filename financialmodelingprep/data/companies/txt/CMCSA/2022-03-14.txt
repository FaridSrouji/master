COMCAST ADVERTISING ROLLS OUT NATIONWIDE, MULTI-SCREEN MEDIA SOLUTION FOR PREMIUM TV ADVERTISING WITH THE RELAUNCH OF ITS AUDIENCEXPRESS BRAND. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, COMCAST ADVERTISING ANNOUNCED THAT ITS MARKET-LEADING MEDIA SOLUTIONS ALLOWING U.S. ADVERTISERS TO BUY PREMIUM TV ADVERTISING ON AN AUDIENCE BASIS NATIONWIDE ACROSS ALL SCREENS, INCLUDING BROADCAST, CABLE, DIGITAL AND CONNECTED TV (CTV), WILL BE RELAUNCHED UNDER THE AUDIENCEXPRESS BRAND.
