COMCAST OPENS FOURTH XFINITY STORE IN GREATER RICHMOND REGION. INTERACTIVE RETAIL STORE IN COLONIAL HEIGHTS OPENS ON THE HEELS OF CHESTERFIELD AND WILL FURTHER STRENGTHEN CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE FOR RESIDENTS RICHMOND, VA. , APRIL 26, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMCAST TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF ITS FOURTH XFINITY STORE IN THE GREATER RICHMOND REGION, WITH A NEW 2,000-SQUARE-FOOT STOREFRONT – LOCATED AT 1160 TEMPLE AVENUE – IN COLONIAL HEIGHTS AT THE SOUTHPARK CROSSING SHOPPING CENTER.
