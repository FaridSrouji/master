COMCAST BUSINESS EXPANDS GLOBAL SECURE NETWORKING PORTFOLIO WITH SOLUTIONS TAILORED FOR SMALL AND MEDIUM BUSINESSES. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST BUSINESS TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF ITS GLOBAL SD-WAN PORTFOLIO WITH THE ADDITION OF TWO NEW SOLUTIONS TAILORED FOR BUSINESSES WITH STANDALONE LOCATIONS. THE SD-WAN SOLUTIONS ENABLE SMALL AND MEDIUM BUSINESSES, WITH EITHER A SINGLE LOCATION OR MULTIPLE STANDALONE LOCATIONS, TO HELP SECURELY CONNECT AND MANAGE THEIR NETWORK, APPLICATIONS, AND USERS.
