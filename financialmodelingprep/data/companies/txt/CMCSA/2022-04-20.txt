CHARDAN ACTS AS M&A AND LEAD FINANCIAL ADVISOR IN NEOS SALE TO SKY. NEW YORK , APRIL 20, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHARDAN, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK, HAS ANNOUNCED ITS ROLE AS LEAD FINANCIAL ADVISOR IN THE SALE OF NEOS, A PROVIDER OF SMART HOME TECHNOLOGY, FROM AVIVA PLC (AV.LN) TO SKY. SKY IS EUROPE'S LEADING MEDIA AND ENTERTAINMENT COMPANY AND PART OF COMCAST CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CMCSA), A GLOBAL MEDIA AND TECHNOLOGY COMPANY.
COMCAST BECOMES FIRST ISP IN THE UNITED STATES TO DEPLOY ADVANCED HOLLOWCORE FIBER WITH FASTER SPEED, LOWER LATENCY. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST, THE NATION'S LARGEST GIG-SPEED INTERNET PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED WHAT IS BELIEVED TO BE THE FIRST-EVER END-TO-END DEPLOYMENT OF ADVANCED “HOLLOWCORE” FIBER OPTICS IN THE WORLD BY AN INTERNET PROVIDER. HOLLOWCORE FIBERS DELIVER SIGNIFICANTLY LOWER LATENCY THAN TRADITIONAL FIBERS AND OVER TIME WILL PROVIDE CRITICAL PERFORMANCE ATTRIBUTES.
