COMCAST DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CMCSA) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.27 A SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 26, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON OCTOBER 5, 2022.
