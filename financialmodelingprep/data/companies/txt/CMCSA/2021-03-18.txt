NBCUNIVERSAL AND NFL REACH 11-YEAR EXTENSION & EXPANSION FOR SUNDAY NIGHT FOOTBALL, PRIMETIME TV'S #1 SHOW. NEW YORK & STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NBCUNIVERSAL AND THE NFL ANNOUNCED TODAY AN 11-YEAR EXTENSION AND EXPANSION FOR NBC SPORTS TO CONTINUE AS THE HOME OF SUNDAY NIGHT FOOTBALL.
