COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL ANNOUNCES THE 10 STARTUPS SELECTED FOR THE INAUGURAL SPORTSTECH ACCELERATOR, POWERED BY BOOMTOWN. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL TODAY ANNOUNCED THE 10 STARTUPS SELECTED FOR THE FIRST ANNUAL COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL SPORTSTECH ACCELERATOR, POWERED BY BOOMTOWN ACCELERATORS. CHOSEN FROM OVER ONE THOUSAND APPLICANTS IN 70 COUNTRIES, THESE 10 COMPANIES WILL BRING THEIR INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS TO COMCAST NBCUNIVERSAL AND THE SPORTSTECH PARTNER CONSORTIUM, WHICH INCLUDES NBC SPORTS, SKY SPORTS, GOLF, NASCAR, U.S. SKI AND SNOWBOARD, USA CYCLING, USA SWIMMING, AND COMCAST VENTURES.
