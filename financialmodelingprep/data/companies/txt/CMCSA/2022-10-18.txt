COMCAST BUSINESS MOBILE EXPANDS TO 20 LINES; NEW SHARED DATA OPTIONS AVAILABLE FOR SMALL BUSINESSES. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST BUSINESS TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT COMCAST BUSINESS MOBILE CUSTOMERS ARE NOW ABLE TO MIX AND MATCH BOTH UNLIMITED AND AN EXPANDED RANGE OF SHARED DATA OPTIONS FOR UP TO TWENTY TOTAL LINES PER ACCOUNT.
