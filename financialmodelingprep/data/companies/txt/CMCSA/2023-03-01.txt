COMCAST CORPORATION NAMES ANAND KINI EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE STRATEGY. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST CORPORATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ANAND KINI HAS BEEN NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE STRATEGY IN ADDITION TO HIS POSITION AS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER OF NBCUNIVERSAL. IN THIS ROLE, ANAND WILL WORK ACROSS COMCAST, NBCUNIVERSAL, AND SKY TO HELP DRIVE THE COMPANY'S GLOBAL GROWTH STRATEGY.
