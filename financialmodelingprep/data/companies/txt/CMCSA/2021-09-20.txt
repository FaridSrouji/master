COMCAST'S INTERNET ESSENTIALS PROGRAM DONATES 3,000 LAPTOPS AND FREE INTERNET SERVICE TO BALTIMORE CITY AS PART OF COMPANY'S LARGEST LAPTOP DONATION OF ITS KIND. BALTIMORE, SEPT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMCAST ANNOUNCED TODAY THE DONATION OF 3,000 NEW LAPTOPS AND FREE INTERNET SERVICE FROM ITS INTERNET ESSENTIALS PROGRAM ACROSS BALTIMORE CITY TO BE DISTRIBUTED AMONG SEVERAL ORGANIZATIONS FOCUSED ON EDUCATION, DIGITAL EQUITY, AND WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT.
