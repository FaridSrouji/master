COMCAST RISE REACHES PROGRAM'S GOAL OF SUPPORTING 13,000 SMALL BUSINESSES HARDEST HIT BY COVID-19. NEW YORK--( BUSINESS WIRE )--COMCAST CORPORATION ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT COMCAST RISE, AN INITIATIVE CREATED IN 2020 TO HELP STRENGTHEN AND EMPOWER SMALL BUSINESSES HARD HIT BY COVID-19, HAS MET ITS GOAL OF SUPPORTING 13,000 SMALL BUSINESSES NATIONWIDE BY THE PROGRAM'S CLOSE AT THE END OF 2022.
