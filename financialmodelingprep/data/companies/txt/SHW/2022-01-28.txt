SHERWIN-WILLIAMS CELEBRATES OFFICIAL START OF NEW GLOBAL HEADQUARTERS CONSTRUCTION. CLEVELAND, JAN. 28, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPANY (NYSE: SHW) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT IT HELD A VIRTUAL CELEBRATION TODAY TO MARK THE OFFICIAL START OF CONSTRUCTION FOR THE COMPANY'S NEW GLOBAL HEADQUARTERS. THE 1,000,000 SQUARE-FOOT FACILITY WILL BE LOCATED IN DOWNTOWN CLEVELAND, OHIO AND WILL INCLUDE A TWO-STORY PAVILION ACTING AS THE FRONT PORCH TO PUBLIC SQUARE, A 36-FLOOR OFFICE TOWER AND AN ATTACHED MULTI-LEVEL PARKING GARAGE.
