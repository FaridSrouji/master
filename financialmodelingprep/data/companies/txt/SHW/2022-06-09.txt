SHERWIN-WILLIAMS TO ACQUIRE GERMAN INDUSTRIAL COATINGS COMPANY, GROSS & PERTHUN GMBH. CLEVELAND , JUNE 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPANY (NYSE: SHW) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE GROSS & PERTHUN GMBH, A MANNHEIM, GERMANY BASED DEVELOPER, MANUFACTURER, AND DISTRIBUTOR OF COATINGS PRIMARILY FOR THE HEAVY EQUIPMENT AND TRANSPORTATION INDUSTRIES. THE ACQUIRED BUSINESS HAS APPROXIMATELY 100 EMPLOYEES AND ANNUAL SALES OF APPROXIMATELY $50 MILLION, AND WILL BECOME PART OF THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS PERFORMANCE COATINGS GROUP REPORTABLE SEGMENT.
