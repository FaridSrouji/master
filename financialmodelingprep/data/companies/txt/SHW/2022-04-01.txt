SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF EUROPEAN INDUSTRIAL COATINGS BUSINESS OF SIKA. CLEVELAND , APRIL 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPANY (NYSE: SHW) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF THE EUROPEAN INDUSTRIAL COATINGS BUSINESS OF SIKA AG. THE ACQUIRED BUSINESS WILL BECOME PART OF THE COMPANY'S PERFORMANCE COATINGS GROUP OPERATING SEGMENT.
