SHERWIN-WILLIAMS DECLARES DIVIDEND OF $0.60 PER COMMON SHARE. CLEVELAND , OCT. 19, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPANY (NYSE: SHW) TODAY ANNOUNCED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.60 PER COMMON SHARE PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 2, 2022, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 18, 2022.
