SHERWIN-WILLIAMS TO ACQUIRE GERMAN WOOD COATINGS COMPANIES, OSKAR NOLTE GMBH AND KLUMPP COATINGS GMBH. CLEVELAND , NOV. 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPANY (NYSE: SHW) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE GERMAN-BASED SPECIALIZED INDUSTRIAL COATINGS HOLDING (SIC HOLDING), A PETER MÖHRLE HOLDING AND GP CAPITAL UG VENTURE COMPRISED OF OSKAR NOLTE GMBH AND KLUMPP COATINGS GMBH. THE BUSINESS' PORTFOLIO OF SOLUTIONS INCLUDES FOIL COATINGS AS WELL AS RADIATION-CURED AND WATERBASED COATINGS FOR THE BOARD, FURNITURE AND FLOORING INDUSTRY.
