SHERWIN-WILLIAMS DECLARES DIVIDEND OF $0.55 PER COMMON SHARE. CLEVELAND, OCT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPANY (NYSE: SHW) TODAY ANNOUNCED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.55 PER COMMON SHARE PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 3, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON NOVEMBER 19, 2021.
