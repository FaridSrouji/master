SHERWIN-WILLIAMS NAMES MINORITY CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT FIRMS FOR NEW GLOBAL HEADQUARTERS AND R&D CENTER PROJECT. CLEVELAND, SEPT. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPANY (NYSE: SHW) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE FIVE FIRMS THAT HAVE BEEN SELECTED FOR THE MINORITY CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT ROLE FOR THE BUILDING OUR FUTURE PROJECT, WHICH INCLUDES THE NEW GLOBAL HEADQUARTERS IN DOWNTOWN CLEVELAND AND THE NEW R&D CENTER IN BRECKSVILLE.
