GLANCY PRONGAY & MURRAY LLP, A LEADING SECURITIES FRAUD LAW FIRM, ANNOUNCES INVESTIGATION OF CITRIX SYSTEMS, INC. (CTXS) ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS. (“CITRIX” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: CTXS) CONCERNING WHETHER THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS BREACHED ITS FIDUCIARY DUTIES TO CITRIX SHAREHOLDERS.
