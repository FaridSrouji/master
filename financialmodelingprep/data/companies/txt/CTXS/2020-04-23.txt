CITRIX SYSTEMS REPORTS REVENUE OF $861 MILLION IN Q1, UP 20%. CITRIX REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2020 FINANCIAL RESULTS.REPORTED REVENUE WAS $861 MILLION IN FIRST QUARTER, UP 20% YEAR-OVER-YEAR.QTRLY GAAP DILUTED EPS WAS $1.42 AND NON-GAAP DILUTED EPS  WAS $1.73.QTRLY SUBSCRIPTION ARR WAS $837 MILLION, UP 50% YEAR-OVER-YEAR, & SAAS ARR WAS $555 MILLION, UP 48% YEAR-OVER-YEAR.CITRIX SYSTEMS - FIRST QUARTER RESULTS REFLECT A SURGE IN DEMAND DRIVEN BY THE COVID-19 OUTBREAK.IN MEDIUM-TERM, BROADER TOLL COVID-19 MAY TAKE ON GLOBAL ECONOMY & SLOPE OF ECONOMIC RECOVERY IS UNKNOWN.RAISING TOP END OF FULL YEAR OUTLOOK FOR REVENUE AND EPS.SEES FY 2020 REVENUE $3.10 BILLION TO $3.16 BILLION.SEES FY 2020 GAAP DILUTED EPS $3.29 TO $3.50.SEES FY 2020 NON-GAAP DILUTED EPS $5.40 TO $5.60.SEES Q2 REVENUE $760 MILLION TO $770 MILLION.SEES Q2 GAAP DILUTED EPS $0.62 TO $0.68.SEES Q2 NON-GAAP DILUTED EPS $1.18 TO $1.23.CITRIX SYSTEMS - FY OUTLOOK REFLECTS EXPECTATION THAT SUBSCRIPTION BOOKINGS AS PERCENTAGE OF PRODUCT BOOKINGS TO INCREASE TO ABOUT 65% TO 75% FOR FY2020.CITRIX SYSTEMS - Q2 OUTLOOK REFLECTS EXPECTATION THAT SUBSCRIPTION BOOKINGS AS PERCENTAGE OF PRODUCT BOOKINGS WILL BE IN RANGE OF 60% TO 65% IN Q2 2020.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.17, REVENUE VIEW $733.6 MILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.19, REVENUE VIEW $762.8 MILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FY2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $5.38, REVENUE VIEW $3.09 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
