IDEXX LABORATORIES TO RELEASE 2023 FIRST QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS. WESTBROOK, MAINE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEXX LABORATORIES, INC. (NASDAQ: IDXX), A GLOBAL LEADER IN PET HEALTHCARE INNOVATION, HAS SCHEDULED THE RELEASE OF ITS 2023 FIRST QUARTER RESULTS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 2, 2023 BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS. THE COMPANY WILL CONDUCT AN ANALYST CONFERENCE CALL BEGINNING AT 8:30 A.M. ET ON THAT DAY. INDIVIDUALS CAN ACCESS A LIVE WEBCAST OF THE CONFERENCE CALL, TRANSCRIPT OF PREPARED REMARKS, AND THE Q1 2023 EARNINGS SNAPSHOT THROUGH A LINK ON THE IDEXX WEBSITE, WWW.IDEXX.
