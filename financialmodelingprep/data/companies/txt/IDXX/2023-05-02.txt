IDEXX LABORATORIES ANNOUNCES FIRST QUARTER RESULTS. WESTBROOK, MAINE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEXX LABORATORIES, INC. (NASDAQ: IDXX), A GLOBAL LEADER IN PET HEALTHCARE INNOVATION, TODAY ANNOUNCED FIRST QUARTER RESULTS. FIRST QUARTER RESULTS THE COMPANY REPORTS REVENUES OF $900 MILLION FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2023, AN INCREASE OF 8% REPORTED AND 10% ORGANIC, DRIVEN BY COMPANION ANIMAL GROUP ("CAG") DIAGNOSTICS RECURRING REVENUE GROWTH OF 9% REPORTED AND 12% ORGANIC. FIRST QUARTER RESULTS WERE SUPPORTED BY CONTINUED SOLID DEMAND FOR VETERINARY SERVICES.
