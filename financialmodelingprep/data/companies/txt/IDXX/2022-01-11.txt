IDEXX LABORATORIES TO RELEASE 2021 FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR FINANCIAL RESULTS. WESTBROOK, MAINE, JAN. 11, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  IDEXX LABORATORIES, INC. (NASDAQ: IDXX), A GLOBAL LEADER IN VETERINARY DIAGNOSTICS, VETERINARY PRACTICE SOFTWARE AND WATER MICROBIOLOGY TESTING, HAS SCHEDULED THE RELEASE OF ITS 2021 FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 2022 BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS. THE COMPANY WILL CONDUCT AN ANALYST CONFERENCE CALL BEGINNING AT 8:30 A.M.
