IDEXX LABORATORIES TO RELEASE 2021 THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS. WESTBROOK, MAINE, OCT. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IDEXX LABORATORIES, INC. (NASDAQ: IDXX), A GLOBAL LEADER IN VETERINARY DIAGNOSTICS, VETERINARY PRACTICE SOFTWARE AND WATER MICROBIOLOGY TESTING, HAS SCHEDULED THE RELEASE OF ITS 2021 THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 2, 2021 BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS. THE COMPANY WILL CONDUCT AN ANALYST CONFERENCE CALL BEGINNING AT 8:30 A.M.
