IDEXX LABORATORIES TO HOST VIRTUAL INVESTOR DAY. WESTBROOK, MAINE, AUG. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IDEXX LABORATORIES, INC. (NASDAQ: IDXX), A GLOBAL LEADER IN VETERINARY DIAGNOSTICS, VETERINARY PRACTICE SOFTWARE AND WATER MICROBIOLOGY TESTING, WILL HOST ITS 2021 VIRTUAL INVESTOR DAY ON THURSDAY, AUGUST 12, 2021 FROM 10:00 AM TO APPROXIMATELY 12:30 PM EDT. THE VIRTUAL INVESTOR DAY WILL INCLUDE EXECUTIVE PRESENTATIONS FOLLOWED BY A QUESTION AND ANSWER SESSION.
