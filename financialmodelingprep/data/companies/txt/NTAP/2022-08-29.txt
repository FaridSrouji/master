NETAPP AND VMWARE STRENGTHEN GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP TO HELP CUSTOMERS MODERNIZE WITH MULTI-CLOUD. (NYSE: VMW) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF THE COMPANIES' LONGSTANDING GLOBAL ALLIANCE.
