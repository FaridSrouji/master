AUTOZONE 3RD QUARTER SAME STORE SALES INCREASE 2.6%; EPS INCREASES TO $29.03. MEMPHIS, TENN., MAY 24, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AUTOZONE, INC. (NYSE: AZO) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $3.9 BILLION FOR ITS THIRD QUARTER (12 WEEKS) ENDED MAY 7, 2022, AN INCREASE OF 5.9% FROM THE THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL 2021 (12 WEEKS). DOMESTIC SAME STORE SALES, OR SALES FOR STORES OPEN AT LEAST ONE YEAR, INCREASED 2.6% FOR THE QUARTER.
