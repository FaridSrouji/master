AUTOZONE RECOGNIZES CONTRIBUTIONS OF TOP VENDOR PARTNERS. MEMPHIS, TENN., JUNE 08, 2022 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AUTOZONE (NYSE:AZO) RECOGNIZED 16 OF ITS TOP VENDORS LAST NIGHT AT ITS 2022 VENDOR PARTNER SUMMIT. THE AWARDS HONOR COMPANIES WHO PROVIDED EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE AND DEMONSTRATED A STRONG COMMITMENT TO CUSTOMER SATISFACTION DURING THE PAST YEAR.
