LECTRON UNVEILS NEW RETAIL PARTNERSHIP WITH AUTOZONE, AMERICA'S LEADING AUTO PARTS RETAILER. STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP EXPANDS LECTRON'S RETAIL FOOTPRINT WHILE HIGHLIGHTING THE PUSH TOWARD MAINSTREAM ELECTRIC VEHICLE ADOPTION MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF., MARCH 29, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LECTRON, THE NATION'S LEADING ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGING BRAND, HAS ENTERED A RETAIL PARTNERSHIP WITH AUTO PARTS GIANT, AUTOZONE (NYSE: AZO), IN A BID TO MAKE ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGING MORE ACCESSIBLE TO CONSUMERS.
