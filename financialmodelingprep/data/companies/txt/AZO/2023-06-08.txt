AUTOZONE VENDOR SUMMIT RECOGNIZES CONTRIBUTIONS OF TOP SUPPLIERS. MEMPHIS, TENN., JUNE 08, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AUTOZONE (NYSE: AZO) RECOGNIZED 16 OF THEIR TOP SUPPLIERS AT ITS JUNE 7, 2023, VENDOR SUMMIT. THE AWARDS HONOR COMPANIES THAT PROVIDED EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE AND DEMONSTRATED A STRONG COMMITMENT TO CUSTOMER SATISFACTION DURING THE PAST YEAR.
