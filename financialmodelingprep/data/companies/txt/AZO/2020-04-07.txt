AUTOZONE ON APRIL 3, 2020, ENTERED INTO THAT CERTAIN 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT. AUTOZONE - ON APRIL 3, 2020, COMPANY ENTERED INTO THAT CERTAIN 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT.AUTOZONE - 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT PROVIDES FOR LOANS IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF UP TO $750.0 MILLION.AUTOZONE - 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT WILL TERMINATE, AND ALL AMOUNTS BORROWED UNDER 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT WILL BE DUE AND PAYABLE, ON APRIL 2, 2021.
AUTOZONE ANNOUNCES ACTIONS IN RESPONSE TO COVID-19. AUTOZONE ANNOUNCES ACTIONS IN RESPONSE TO COVID-19.AUTOZONE - HAS PROVIDED NEW ADDITIONAL EMERGENCY-TIME OFF BENEFIT ENHANCEMENT FOR ELIGIBLE PART-TIME, FULL-TIME HOURLY AUTOZONERS IN U.S. & PUERTO RICO.AUTOZONE INC - ANNOUNCES CLOSURE OF A NEW 364-DAY SENIOR UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY IN PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $750 MILLION.AUTOZONE INC - HAS ALSO TEMPORARILY SUSPENDED ITS SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM.AUTOZONE INC - HAS APPROXIMATELY $1.9 BILLION IN AVAILABLE CAPACITY UNDER ITS $2 BILLION MULTI-YEAR CREDIT FACILITY.
