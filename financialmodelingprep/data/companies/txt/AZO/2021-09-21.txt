AUTOZONE 4TH QUARTER SAME STORE SALES INCREASE 4.3%; 4TH QUARTER EPS INCREASES TO $35.72; ANNUAL SALES OF $14.6 BILLION. MEMPHIS, TENN., SEPT. 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AUTOZONE, INC. (NYSE: AZO) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $4.9 BILLION FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER (16 WEEKS) ENDED AUGUST 28, 2021, AN INCREASE OF 8.1% FROM THE FOURTH QUARTER OF FISCAL 2020 (16 WEEKS). DOMESTIC SAME STORE SALES, OR SALES FOR STORES OPEN AT LEAST ONE YEAR, INCREASED 4.3% FOR THE QUARTER.
