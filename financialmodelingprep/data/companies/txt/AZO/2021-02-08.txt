AUTOZONE ANNOUNCES CHANGE TO EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE. MEMPHIS, TENN., FEB. 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AUTOZONE, INC. (NYSE: AZO), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ERIC GOULD, CURRENTLY VICE PRESIDENT, SUPPLY CHAIN REPLENISHMENT, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, SUPPLY CHAIN REPLACING MITCHELL MAJOR, WHO PASSED AWAY ON JANUARY 29, 2021. IN HIS NEW ROLE, GOULD, WHO JOINED THE COMPANY IN 1992, WILL SERVE ON THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE.
