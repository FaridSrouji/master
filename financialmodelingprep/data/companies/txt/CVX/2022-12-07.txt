CHEVRON ANNOUNCES 2023 CAPITAL EXPENDITURE BUDGETS. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CHEVRON CORPORATION TODAY ANNOUNCED 2023 ORGANIC CAPITAL EXPENDITURE BUDGETS OF $14 BILLION FOR CONSOLIDATED SUBSIDIARIES (CAPEX) AND $3 BILLION FOR EQUITY AFFILIATES (AFFILIATE CAPEX), WHICH TOTAL NEAR THE HIGH END OF THE COMPANY'S GUIDANCE RANGE.
