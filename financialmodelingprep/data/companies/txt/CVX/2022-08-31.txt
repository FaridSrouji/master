CHEVRON ANNOUNCES Q&A SERIES FOR SHAREHOLDERS. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX), ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING ENERGY COMPANIES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE LAUNCH OF THE CHEVRON EXCHANGE Q&A SERIES, STARTING WITH A VIRTUAL FIRESIDE CHAT WITH CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER PIERRE BREBER ON THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 15, 2022. CHEVRON WILL PARTNER WITH SAY TECHNOLOGIES TO OFFER A PLATFORM TO RECEIVE AND ANSWER QUESTIONS FROM VERIFIED RETAIL SHAREHOLDERS.
