CHEVRON ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND TWENTY-NINE CENTS ($1.29) PER SHARE, PAYABLE MARCH 10, 2021 TO ALL HOLDERS OF COMMON STOCK AS SHOWN ON THE TRANSFER RECORDS OF THE CORPORATION AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS FEBRUARY 17, 2021.
