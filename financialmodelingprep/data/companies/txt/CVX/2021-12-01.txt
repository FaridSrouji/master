CHEVRON ANNOUNCES $15 BILLION CAPITAL AND EXPLORATORY BUDGET FOR 2022. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX) TODAY ANNOUNCED A 2022 ORGANIC CAPITAL AND EXPLORATORY SPENDING PROGRAM OF $15 BILLION, AT THE LOW END OF ITS $15 TO $17 BILLION GUIDANCE RANGE AND UP MORE THAN 20% FROM 2021 EXPECTED LEVELS. THIS CAPITAL PROGRAM SUPPORTS CHEVRON'S OBJECTIVE OF HIGHER RETURNS AND LOWER CARBON, INCLUDING APPROXIMATELY $800 MILLION IN LOWER CARBON SPENDING.
