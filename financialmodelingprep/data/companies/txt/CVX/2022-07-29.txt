CHEVRON ANNOUNCES SECOND QUARTER 2022 RESULTS. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX) TODAY REPORTED EARNINGS OF $11.6 BILLION ($5.95 PER SHARE - DILUTED) FOR SECOND QUARTER 2022, COMPARED WITH $3.1 BILLION ($1.60 PER SHARE - DILUTED) IN SECOND QUARTER 2021. INCLUDED IN THE CURRENT QUARTER WERE CHARGES ASSOCIATED WITH AN EARLY CONTRACT TERMINATION OF $600 MILLION, PENSION SETTLEMENT COSTS OF $11 MILLION, AND A GAIN ON ASSET SALES OF $200 MILLION.
