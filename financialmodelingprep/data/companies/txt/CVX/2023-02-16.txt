CHEVRON AND FAB FOUNDATION ANNOUNCE GLOBAL STEM EDUCATOR FELLOWSHIP. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX) AND THE FAB FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FIRST RECIPIENTS OF AN ALL-NEW FELLOWSHIP PROGRAM THAT SUPPORTS STEM EDUCATORS ACROSS THE WORLD. THE CHEVRON FAB STEM FELLOWSHIP WAS AWARDED TO NATHAN PRITCHETT OF TULSA, OKLAHOMA AND TO PIETER VERDUIJN OF CALABAS, ARUBA.
