CHEVRON ANNOUNCES SECOND QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX) TODAY REPORTED EARNINGS OF $3.1 BILLION ($1.60 PER SHARE - DILUTED) FOR SECOND QUARTER 2021, COMPARED WITH A LOSS OF $8.3 BILLION ($(4.44) PER SHARE - DILUTED) IN SECOND QUARTER 2020. INCLUDED IN THE CURRENT QUARTER WERE REMEDIATION CHARGES ASSOCIATED WITH PREVIOUSLY SOLD ASSETS OF $120 MILLION AND PENSION SETTLEMENT COSTS OF $115 MILLION.
