CHEVRON TO HOST CHEVRON EXCHANGE Q&A SERIES FOR RETAIL SHAREHOLDERS. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SECOND RETAIL EVENT IN THE CHEVRON EXCHANGE Q&A SERIES, A VIRTUAL FIRESIDE CHAT WITH PRESIDENT OF CHEVRON NEW ENERGIES (CNE), JEFF GUSTAVSON, ON THURSDAY, MARCH 23, 2023. CHEVRON WILL OFFER A PLATFORM TO RECEIVE AND ANSWER QUESTIONS FROM VERIFIED RETAIL SHAREHOLDERS.
