CHEVRON CORPORATION DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 10, 201. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CHEVRON CORPORATION DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.19 PER SHARE, PAYABLE DECEMBER 10, 2019, TO ALL HOLDERS OF COMMON STOCK AS SHOWN ON THE TRANSFER RECORDS OF THE CORPORATION AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS NOVEMBER 18, 2019.
