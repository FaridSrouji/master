HOCOL PETROLEUM LIMITED AGREED TO ACQUIRE 43% STAKE IN CHUCHUPA AND BALLENA FIELDS FROM CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE:CVX).. HOCOL PETROLEUM LIMITED AGREED TO ACQUIRE 43% STAKE IN CHUCHUPA AND BALLENA FIELDS FROM CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE:CVX) ON NOVEMBER 26, 2019. THE DEAL IS SUBJECT TO THE APPROVAL BY COLOMBIAN REGULATORS. JEFF SCHLEGEL AND NICHOLAS RODRIGUEZ OF JONES DAY ACTED AS LEGAL ADVISORS TO CHEVRON PETROLIUM COMPANY, A SUBSIDIARY OF CHEVRON CORPORATION. SCOTIA CAPITAL INC. ACTED AS FINANCIAL ADVISOR TO CHEVRON CORPORATION.
