CHEVRON NAMES MARK A. NELSON VICE CHAIRMAN. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX) TODAY ANNOUNCED MARK A. NELSON, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, STRATEGY, POLICY & DEVELOPMENT, HAS BEEN NAMED VICE CHAIRMAN AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, STRATEGY, POLICY & DEVELOPMENT, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 1, 2023. IN THIS NEW CORPORATE OFFICER ROLE, NELSON WILL CONTINUE LEADING CHEVRON'S STRATEGY & SUSTAINABILITY, CORPORATE AFFAIRS, AND BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT FUNCTIONS, AND TAKE ON ADDITIONAL CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITIES.
