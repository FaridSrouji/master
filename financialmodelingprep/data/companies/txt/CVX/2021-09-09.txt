CHEVRON, MERCURIA ANNOUNCE CNG FUELING NETWORK JOINT VENTURE. SAN RAMON, CALIF. & SARASOTA SPRINGS, N.Y. & HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON U.S.A. INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SIGNING OF DEFINITIVE AGREEMENTS TO FORM A JOINT VENTURE WITH MERCURIA ENERGY TRADING (MERCURIA), ONE OF THE WORLD'S LARGEST INTEGRATED ENERGY AND COMMODITIES COMPANIES, TO OWN AND OPERATE AMERICAN NATURAL GAS LLC (ANG) AND ITS NETWORK OF 60 COMPRESSED NATURAL GAS (CNG) STATIONS ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
