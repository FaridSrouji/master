CHEVRON ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF ONE DOLLAR AND THIRTY-FOUR CENTS ($1.34) PER SHARE, PAYABLE DECEMBER 10, 2021, TO ALL HOLDERS OF COMMON STOCK AS SHOWN ON THE TRANSFER RECORDS OF THE CORPORATION AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS NOVEMBER 18, 2021.
