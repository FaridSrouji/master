CHEVRON ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP CHANGES. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ALANA K. KNOWLES AS VICE PRESIDENT AND CONTROLLER, EFFECTIVE MARCH 1, 2023. KNOWLES WILL SUCCEED DAVID A. INCHAUSTI, WHO WILL RESIGN FROM THAT POSITION EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 28, 2023, AND IS EXPECTED TO RETIRE FROM THE COMPANY IN APRIL 2023, AFTER 35 YEARS OF SERVICE.
