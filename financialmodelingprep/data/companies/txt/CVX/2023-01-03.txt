CHEVRON FINALIZES ACQUISITION OF BEYOND6 CNG FUELING NETWORK. SAN RAMON, CALIF. & HOUSTON--( BUSINESS WIRE )--CHEVRON U.S.A. INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX), TODAY CLOSED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF FULL OWNERSHIP OF BEYOND6, LLC AND ITS NATIONWIDE NETWORK OF COMPRESSED NATURAL GAS STATIONS FROM MERCURIA ENERGY TRADING AND BEYOND6 FOUNDER ANDREW WEST.
