CHEVRON ANNOUNCES LOWER CARBON LNG FLEET MODIFICATION PROJECT WITH SEMBCORP MARINE. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE: CVX), THROUGH ITS SUBSIDIARY CHEVRON SHIPPING COMPANY LLC, ANNOUNCED ENTRANCE INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH SEMBCORP MARINE REPAIRS & UPGRADES PTE. LTD, A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF SEMBCORP MARINE LTD. (SEMBCORP MARINE), INTENDING TO REDUCE THE CARBON INTENSITY OF THEIR LNG FLEET OPERATIONS.
