CHEVRON EVOLVES LEADERSHIP STRUCTURE TO FURTHER ENHANCE EXECUTION. SAN RAMON, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CHEVRON CORPORATION (NYSE:CVX) TODAY ANNOUNCED A SIMPLIFIED ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND SENIOR LEADERSHIP CHANGES INTENDED TO STRENGTHEN EXECUTION AND PACE TO DELIVER ON THE COMPANY'S OBJECTIVES OF HIGHER RETURNS AND LOWER CARBON.
