SAN FRANCISCO AND SAN RAMON, CALIF. , MAY 31, 2023 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BRIGHTMARK RNG HOLDINGS LLC IS POSITIONED TO EXPAND RENEWABLE NATURAL GAS (RNG) PRODUCTION WITH FIVE NEW ANAEROBIC DIGESTION DAIRY FARM PROJECTS IN WESTERN MICHIGAN, DESIGNED TO CONVERT ANIMAL WASTE TO RENEWABLE FUELS.
