JOHNSON & JOHNSON COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF ABIOMED. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ), THE WORLD'S LARGEST, MOST DIVERSIFIED HEALTHCARE PRODUCTS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF ABIOMED, INC. ABIOMED IS NOW PART OF JOHNSON & JOHNSON AND WILL OPERATE AS A STANDALONE BUSINESS WITHIN JOHNSON & JOHNSON'S MEDTECH SEGMENT.
