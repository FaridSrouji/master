JANSSEN PHARMACEUTICAL RELEASES DATA FROM THE ONGOING TRIAL OF ITS INVESTIGATIONAL GENE THERAPY FOR TREATMENT OF RETINAL DISEASE. JANSSEN PHARMACEUTICAL - ANNOUNCED 6-MONTH DATA FROM ONGOING PHASE 1/2 TRIAL OF INVESTIGATIONAL GENE THERAPY FOR INHERITED RETINAL DISEASE XLRP TREATMENT.JANSSEN - INTERIM SIX-MONTH DATA OF RPGR GENE THERAPY SHOWS SIGNIFICANT VISION IMPROVEMENT IN PATIENTS LIVING WITH X-LINKED RETINITIS PIGMENTOSA.JANSSEN - INTERIM DATA SHOWED LOW AND INTERMEDIATE DOSES OF INVESTIGATIONAL AAV-RPGR WERE GENERALLY WELL-TOLERATED.JANSSEN - SAFETY DATA OBTAINED TO DATE HAS OCULAR AND SYSTEMIC SAFETY PROFILES THAT ARE ANTICIPATED AND MANAGEABLE.JANSSEN - COMPANY ANTICIPATES PROGRESSING PROGRAM TO PHASE 3.
