JOHNSON & JOHNSON EBOLA VACCINE REGIMEN DEMONSTRATED ROBUST AND DURABLE IMMUNE RESPONSE IN ADULTS AND CHILDREN IN DATA PUBLISHED IN THE LANCET INFECTIOUS DISEASES. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., SEPT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DATA FROM TWO PAPERS PUBLISHED IN THE LANCET INFECTIOUS DISEASES DEMONSTRATED THAT THE JOHNSON & JOHNSON (THE COMPANY) (NYSE: JNJ) EBOLA VACCINE REGIMEN, ZABDENO® (AD26.ZEBOV) AND MVABEA® (MVA-BN-FILO), GENERATED ROBUST HUMORAL (ANTIBODY) IMMUNE RESPONSES IN ADULTS AND CHILDREN (AGES 1-17) WITH THE IMMUNE RESPONSES PERSISTING IN ADULTS FOR AT LEAST TWO YEARS.
