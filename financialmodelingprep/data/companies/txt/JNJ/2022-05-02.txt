JOHNSON & JOHNSON TO PARTICIPATE IN THE BERNSTEIN 38TH ANNUAL STRATEGIC DECISIONS CONFERENCE. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J. , MAY 2, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE BERNSTEIN 38TH ANNUAL STRATEGIC DECISIONS CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 1 ST, AT THE NEW YORK HILTON MIDTOWN IN NEW YORK.
