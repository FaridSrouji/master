SHAREHOLDER ALERT: WEISS LAW INVESTIGATES ABIOMED, INC.. NEW YORK , NOV. 1, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEISS LAW IS INVESTIGATING POSSIBLE BREACHES OF FIDUCIARY DUTY AND OTHER VIOLATIONS OF LAW BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ABIOMED, INC. ("ABIOMED" OR THE "COMPANY") (NASDAQ: ABMD), IN CONNECTION WITH ITS PROPOSED TENDER OFFER BY JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ). UNDER THE TENDER OFFER, THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $380.00 IN CASH, REFLECTING AN ENTERPRISE VALUE OF APPROXIMATELY $16.6 BILLION, AND A NON-TRADEABLE CONTINGENT VALUE RIGHT (CVR) ENTITLING THE HOLDER TO RECEIVE UP TO $35.00 FOR EACH SHARE OF ABIOMED COMMON STOCK OWNED.
JOHNSON & JOHNSON TO ACQUIRE ABIOMED. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J.
