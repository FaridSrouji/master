ALVOTECH AND TEVA SECURE U.S. LICENSE DATE FOR AVT04, A PROPOSED BIOSIMILAR TO STELARA®. REYKJAVIK, ICELAND & TEL AVIV, ISRAEL & PARSIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALVOTECH (NASDAQ: ALVO), A GLOBAL BIOTECH COMPANY SPECIALIZING IN THE DEVELOPMENT AND MANUFACTURE OF BIOSIMILAR MEDICINES FOR PATIENTS WORLDWIDE, AND TEVA PHARMACEUTICALS, INC., A U.S. AFFILIATE OF TEVA PHARMACEUTICAL INDUSTRIES LTD. (NYSE AND TASE: TEVA), TODAY ANNOUNCED THEY HAVE REACHED A SETTLEMENT AND LICENSE AGREEMENT WITH JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE:JNJ) CONCERNING AVT04, ALVOTECH'S PROPOSED BIOSIMILAR TO STELARA®.
