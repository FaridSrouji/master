JOHNSON & JOHNSON TO PARTICIPATE IN THE COWEN 43RD ANNUAL HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE COWEN 43RD ANNUAL HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, MARCH 7TH, AT THE BOSTON MARRIOTT COPLEY PLACE, BOSTON, MA. JOSEPH J. WOLK, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER WILL REPRESENT THE COMPANY IN A SESSION SCHEDULED AT 11:10 A.M. (EASTERN TIME).
