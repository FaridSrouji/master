SPRINGWORKS THERAPEUTICS ANNOUNCES CLINICAL COLLABORATION WITH JANSSEN. SPRINGWORKS THERAPEUTICS ANNOUNCES CLINICAL COLLABORATION WITH JANSSEN TO EVALUATE NIROGACESTAT IN COMBINATION WITH TECLISTAMAB IN PATIENTS WITH RELAPSED OR REFRACTORY MULTIPLE MYELOMA.SPRINGWORKS THERAPEUTICS - JANSSEN WILL SPONSOR AND CONDUCT PHASE 1 STUDY TO EVALUATE SAFETY, TOLERABILITY AND PRELIMINARY EFFICACY OF COMBINATION.SPRINGWORKS THERAPEUTICS - JANSSEN WILL ASSUME ALL COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH STUDY, OTHER THAN EXPENSES RELATED TO MANUFACTURING OF NIROGACESTAT.SPRINGWORKS THERAPEUTICS - WILL ALSO FORM A JOINT OVERSIGHT COMMITTEE WITH JANSSEN.
