JOHNSON & JOHNSON COVID-19 VACCINE BOOSTER SHOT AUTHORIZED FOR EMERGENCY USE BY U.S. FDA. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., OCT. 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) (THE COMPANY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ISSUED EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) FOR A BOOSTER DOSE OF THE JOHNSON & JOHNSON COVID-19 VACCINE FOR ADULTS AGED 18 AND OLDER AT LEAST TWO MONTHS FOLLOWING PRIMARY VACCINATION WITH SINGLE-SHOT JOHNSON & JOHNSON COVID-19 VACCINE; AND FOR ELIGIBLE INDIVIDUALS WHO RECEIVED A DIFFERENT AUTHORIZED OR APPROVED COVID-19 VACCINE.
