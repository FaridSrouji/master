JOHNSON & JOHNSON TO PARTICIPATE IN THE CREDIT SUISSE 31ST ANNUAL HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE CREDIT SUISSE 31ST ANNUAL GLOBAL HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE ON THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 10TH, 2022 AT THE TERRANEA RESORT IN RANCHO PALOS VERDES, CA. JENNIFER TAUBERT, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, WORLDWIDE CHAIRMAN, PHARMACEUTICALS WILL REPRESENT THE COMPANY IN A SESSION SCHEDULED AT 12:50 P.M. (EASTERN TIME).
