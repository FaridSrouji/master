JOHNSON & JOHNSON TO PARTICIPATE IN THE 41ST ANNUAL J.P. MORGAN HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE 41ST ANNUAL J.P. MORGAN HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE ON MONDAY, JANUARY 9TH, AT THE WESTIN ST. FRANCIS IN SAN FRANCISCO, CA. JOAQUIN DUATO, CHAIRMAN ELECT OF THE BOARD AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER WILL REPRESENT THE COMPANY IN A SESSION SCHEDULED AT 12:45 P.M. (EASTERN TIME).
