WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION STRATEGIC ADVISORY GROUP OF EXPERTS (SAGE) INTERIM RECOMMENDATION SUPPORTS USE OF JOHNSON & JOHNSON COVID-19 VACCINE AS A BOOSTER. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., DEC. 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) (THE COMPANY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE INTERIM RECOMMENDATION BY THE STRATEGIC ADVISORY GROUP OF EXPERTS ON IMMUNIZATION (SAGE) FOR THE WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION (WHO) SUPPORTING THE USE OF THE JOHNSON & JOHNSON COVID-19 VACCINE AS A BOOSTER SHOT IN PERSONS AGED 18 YEARS AND ABOVE.
