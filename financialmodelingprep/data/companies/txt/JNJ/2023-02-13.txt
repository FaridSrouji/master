JOHNSON & JOHNSON APPOINTS DR. JOHN REED AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, PHARMACEUTICALS, R&D. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) (THE COMPANY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF JOHN REED, M.D., PH.D., TO THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF PHARMACEUTICALS, R&D. DR. REED PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL HEAD OF RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT FOR SANOFI AND BRINGS MORE THAN 35 YEARS OF BIOMEDICAL RESEARCH LEADERSHIP TO THE COMPANY. DR.
