JOHNSON & JOHNSON ANNOUNCES SUBMISSION OF EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION AMENDMENT TO THE U.S. FDA TO SUPPORT BOOSTER OF ITS SINGLE-SHOT COVID-19 VACCINE. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., OCT. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) (THE COMPANY) ANNOUNCED IT HAS SUBMITTED DATA TO THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) TO SUPPORT USE OF A BOOSTER SHOT OF THE JOHNSON & JOHNSON COVID-19 VACCINE IN INDIVIDUALS 18 YEARS OF AGE AND OLDER.
