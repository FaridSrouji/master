JOHNSON & JOHNSON ANNOUNCES DATA TO SUPPORT BOOSTING ITS SINGLE-SHOT COVID-19 VACCINE. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., AUG. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) (THE COMPANY) TODAY ANNOUNCED DATA SUPPORTING THE USE OF ITS COVID-19 VACCINE AS A BOOSTER SHOT FOR PEOPLE PREVIOUSLY VACCINATED WITH THE SINGLE-SHOT JOHNSON & JOHNSON VACCINE.
