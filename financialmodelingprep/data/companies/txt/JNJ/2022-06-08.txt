JOHNSON & JOHNSON RELEASES 2021 HEALTH FOR HUMANITY REPORT HIGHLIGHTING PERFORMANCE ON ESG PRIORITIES AND PROGRESS AGAINST PUBLIC COMMITMENTS. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J. , JUNE 8, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) TODAY PUBLISHED ITS 2021 HEALTH FOR HUMANITY REPORT, WHICH SHARES PROGRESS AGAINST ITS PURPOSE-DRIVEN HEALTH FOR HUMANITY 2025 GOALS AND SERVES AS ITS ANNUAL DISCLOSURE ON ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PERFORMANCE.
