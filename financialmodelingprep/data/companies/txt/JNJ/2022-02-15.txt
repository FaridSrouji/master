JOHNSON & JOHNSON NAMES DARIUS ADAMCZYK, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF HONEYWELL, TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., FEB. 15, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DARIUS ADAMCZYK, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF HONEYWELL, HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
