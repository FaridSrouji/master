JOHNSON & JOHNSON TO HOST BUSINESS REVIEW HIGHLIGHTING PHARMACEUTICALS. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., OCT. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) WILL HOLD A BUSINESS REVIEW FOR THE INVESTMENT COMMUNITY ON THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 18, 2021, IN WHICH COMPANY LEADERS WILL SHARE A DETAILED REVIEW OF ITS PHARMACEUTICALS BUSINESS, DIFFERENTIATED STRATEGY AND ROBUST PIPELINE, AND LONG-TERM GROWTH OUTLOOK.
