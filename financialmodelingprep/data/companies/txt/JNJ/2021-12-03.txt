JANSSEN TO SHOWCASE PROGRESS ON RESPIRATORY SYNCYTIAL VIRUS (RSV) VACCINE CANDIDATE AT ESWI 2021. RARITAN, N.J., DEC. 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE JANSSEN PHARMACEUTICAL COMPANIES OF JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL PRESENT DATA FROM SIX COMPANY-SPONSORED STUDIES IN SUPPORT OF THEIR RESPIRATORY SYNCYTIAL VIRUS (RSV) ADULT VACCINE CANDIDATE AT THE EIGHTH EUROPEAN SCIENTIFIC WORKING GROUP ON INFLUENZA (ESWI) MEETING.
