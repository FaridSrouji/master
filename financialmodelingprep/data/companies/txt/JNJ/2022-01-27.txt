JOHNSON & JOHNSON TO PARTICIPATE IN CITI'S 2022 VIRTUAL HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., JAN. 27, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) WILL PARTICIPATE IN CITI'S 2022 VIRTUAL HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 23 RD, ASHLEY MCEVOY, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, WORLDWIDE CHAIRMAN, MEDICAL DEVICES WILL REPRESENT THE COMPANY IN A SESSION SCHEDULED AT 11:00 A.M.
