POSITIVE NEW DATA FOR JOHNSON & JOHNSON SINGLE-SHOT COVID-19 VACCINE ON ACTIVITY AGAINST DELTA VARIANT AND LONG-LASTING DURABILITY OF RESPONSE. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) (THE COMPANY) TODAY ANNOUNCED DATA THAT DEMONSTRATED ITS SINGLE-SHOT COVID-19 VACCINE GENERATED STRONG, PERSISTENT ACTIVITY AGAINST THE RAPIDLY SPREADING DELTA VARIANT AND OTHER HIGHLY PREVALENT SARS-COV-2 VIRAL VARIANTS.
