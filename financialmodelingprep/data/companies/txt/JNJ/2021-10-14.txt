JOHNSON & JOHNSON OVARIAN CANCER VICTIMS VOW FIGHT TO STOP J&J'S BANKRUPTCY SCHEME. CHARLOTTE, N.C., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LAWYERS WHO REPRESENT MORE THAN 30,000 WOMEN WHO HAVE FILED LAWSUITS AGAINST JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE:JNJ) IN CONNECTION WITH ITS DEFECTIVE TALC BODY POWDERS, INCLUDING THE ICONIC JOHNSON'S BABY POWDER, SAY THEY WILL RESIST J&J'S DECISION TO USE BANKRUPTCY TO AVOID LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE CANCERS TIED TO THOSE PRODUCTS.
JOHNSON & JOHNSON TAKES STEPS TO EQUITABLY RESOLVE ALL CURRENT AND FUTURE TALC CLAIMS. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., OCT. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) (THE COMPANY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT LTL MANAGEMENT LLC (LTL), A NEWLY CREATED AND SEPARATE SUBSIDIARY OF JOHNSON & JOHNSON, THAT WAS ESTABLISHED TO HOLD AND MANAGE CLAIMS IN THE COSMETIC TALC LITIGATION, HAS FILED FOR VOLUNTARY CHAPTER 11 BANKRUPTCY PROTECTION.
