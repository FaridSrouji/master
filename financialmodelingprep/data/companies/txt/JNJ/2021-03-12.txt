JOHNSON & JOHNSON SINGLE-SHOT COVID-19 VACCINE GRANTED EMERGENCY USE LISTING BY THE WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., MARCH 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) (THE COMPANY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION (WHO) HAS ISSUED EMERGENCY USE LISTING (EUL) FOR ITS SINGLE-SHOT COVID-19 VACCINE, DEVELOPED BY THE JANSSEN PHARMACEUTICAL COMPANIES OF JOHNSON & JOHNSON (JANSSEN), TO PREVENT COVID-19 IN INDIVIDUALS 18 YEARS OF AGE AND OLDER.
