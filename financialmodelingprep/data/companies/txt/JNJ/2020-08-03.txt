JANSSEN ANNOUNCES U.S. FDA APPROVAL OF SPRAVATO CIII NASAL SPRAY. JANSSEN ANNOUNCES U.S. FDA APPROVAL OF SPRAVATO® (ESKETAMINE) CIII NASAL SPRAY TO TREAT DEPRESSIVE SYMPTOMS IN ADULTS WITH MAJOR DEPRESSIVE DISORDER WITH ACUTE SUICIDAL IDEATION OR BEHAVIOR.JANSSEN - APPROVAL IS BASED ON PHASE 3 DATA SHOWING SPRAVATO REDUCED DEPRESSIVE SYMPTOMS IN AS LITTLE AS FOUR HOURS IN SOME PATIENTS.JANSSEN - EFFECTIVENESS OF SPRAVATO IN PREVENTING SUICIDE OR IN REDUCING SUICIDAL IDEATION OR BEHAVIOR HAS NOT BEEN DEMONSTRATED.JANSSEN - SPRAVATO CARRIES A BOXED WARNING REGARDING A RISK EVALUATION AND MITIGATION STRATEGY (REMS) AND RISK OF SUICIDAL THOUGHTS AND BEHAVIORS.
