ATLANTIC CITY, N.J., SEPT. 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN A NEW STATE COURT FILING, WOMEN WHO HOLD JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE:JNJ) AND THE COMPANY'S ICONIC TALC BABY POWDER RESPONSIBLE FOR THEIR OVARIAN CANCER ARE SEEKING A TEMPORARY RESTRAINING ORDER AND PRELIMINARY INJUNCTION TO PREVENT J&J, OR ANY OF ITS CORPORATE AFFILIATES, FROM FRAUDULENTLY TRANSFERRING ASSETS TO A SUBSIDIARY AND PLUNGING IT INTO BANKRUPTCY.
