JOHNSON & JOHNSON NAMES PAULA A. JOHNSON, PRESIDENT OF WELLESLEY COLLEGE, TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PAULA A. JOHNSON, MD, MPH, PRESIDENT OF WELLESLEY COLLEGE, HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. “I AM EXCITED TO WELCOME DR. PAULA JOHNSON TO JOHNSON & JOHNSON'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS,” SAID JOAQUIN DUATO, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, JOHNSON & JOHNSON.
