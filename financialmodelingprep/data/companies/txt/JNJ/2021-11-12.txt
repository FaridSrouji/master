JOHNSON & JOHNSON ANNOUNCES PLANS TO ACCELERATE INNOVATION, SERVE PATIENTS AND CONSUMERS, AND UNLOCK VALUE THROUGH INTENT TO SEPARATE CONSUMER HEALTH BUSINESS. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., NOV. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: JNJ) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INTENT TO SEPARATE THE COMPANY'S CONSUMER HEALTH BUSINESS, CREATING A NEW PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANY.
