TALC-CANCER LAWSUITS PLAY CRITICAL ROLE IN EXPOSING JOHNSON & JOHNSON'S UNETHICAL EXPERIMENTS ON PRISONERS. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J. , MARCH 9, 2022 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REVELATIONS THAT JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) PAID SCIENTIFIC RESEARCHERS TO CONDUCT UNETHICAL AND INHUMANE EXPERIMENTS ON PRISONERS IN THE 1960S ARE SHEDDING NEW LIGHT ON WHAT THE COMPANY KNEW ABOUT CANCER RISKS FROM ITS TALC-BASED PRODUCTS LIKE THE ICONIC JOHNSON'S BABY POWDER, JUST AS THE COMPANY IS MOVING TO SHUT DOWN JURY TRIALS IN A CONTROVERSIAL BANKRUPTCY GAMBIT.
