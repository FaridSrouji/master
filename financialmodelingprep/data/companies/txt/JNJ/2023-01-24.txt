JOHNSON & JOHNSON REPORTS Q4 AND FULL-YEAR 2022 RESULTS. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J.--( BUSINESS WIRE )--JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) TODAY ANNOUNCED RESULTS FOR FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2022. “OUR FULL YEAR 2022 RESULTS REFLECT THE CONTINUED STRENGTH AND STABILITY OF OUR THREE BUSINESS SEGMENTS, DESPITE MACROECONOMIC CHALLENGES,” SAID JOAQUIN DUATO, CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. “I AM INSPIRED BY OUR EMPLOYEES WHO MAKE A DIFFERENCE IN THE HEALTH AND LIVES OF PEOPLE AROUND THE WORLD EVERY DAY. AS WE LOOK AHEAD TO 2023, JOHNSON & JOHNSON IS WELL-POSITIONED TO DRIVE NEAR-TERM GROWTH, WHILE ALSO INVESTING STRATEGICALLY TO DELIVER LONG-TERM VALUE.”
