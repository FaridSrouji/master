Halliburton Executive Says Co To Continue Cutting Cost in N. America - Conference Call. HALLIBURTON SAYS FOR 2020, SEE MORE INTERNATIONAL TOP LINE, MARGIN GROWTH OPPORTUNITIES COMING FOR MATURE FIELDS AND SHALLOW WATER MARKETS - CONF CALL.HALLIBURTON SAYS STACKED MORE EQUIPMENT IN Q3 THAN FIRST SIX MONTHS OF THE YEAR.HALLIBURTON SAYS "WILL KEEP REDUCING COST IN OUR NORTH AMERICAN OPERATIONS" AFTER RECENT JOB CUTS.HALLIBURTON SAYS FOR Q4, SEES CUSTOMER ACTIVITY TO DECLINE ACROSS ALL BASINS IN NORTH AMERICA LAND, IMPACTING BOTH DRILLING AND COMPLETION BUSINESS.HALLIBURTON SAYS FOR Q4, FEEDBACK FROM CUSTOMERS LEAD CO TO BELIEVE RIG COUNT, COMPLETIONS ACTIVITY MAYBE LOWER THAN IN Q4 LAST YEAR.HALLIBURTON, AFTER RECENT JOB CUTS, SAYS IN Q4 PLAN TO UNDERTAKE FURTHER COST REDUCTIONS BY STREAMLINING OPERATIONS AND CORPORATE FUNCTIONS.HALLIBURTON SAYS WATCHING TREND OF DECELERATION OF INCREMENTAL U.S. PRODUCTION GROWTH BROUGHT ABOUT BY CAPITAL DISCIPLINE.HALLIBURTON SAYS RECORD-BREAKING 2018 PRODUCTION GROWTH WILL NOT BE REPLICATED IN 2019.HALLIBURTON SAYS CURRENT PROJECTIONS FOR 2020 INDICATE A FURTHER DECLINE IN PRODUCTION FROM THE CURRENT YEAR ESTIMATES.HALLIBURTON SAYS EXPECT EQUIPMENT ATTRITION TO CONTINUE INTO 2020.HALLIBURTON CO - 2019 CAPEX GUIDANCE REMAINS UNCHANGED.HALLIBURTON- IN Q4, FOR DRILLING & EVALUATION, SEE SEQUENTIAL REVENUE TO BE FLAT TO UP LOW SINGLE DIGITS, WITH MARGIN IMPROVEMENT OF 425-475 BASIS POINTS.
Halliburton Reports Q3 EPS Of $0.34. HALLIBURTON ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.QTRLY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $0.34 PER DILUTED SHARE.QTRLY TOTAL REVENUE $5,550 MILLION VERSUS $6,172 MILLION.QTRLY COMPLETION AND PRODUCTION REVENUE $3,506 MILLION VERSUS $4,170 MILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $0.34, REVENUE VIEW $5.81 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.HALLIBURTON-QTRLY NORTH AMERICA REVENUE WAS DOWN 11% VERSUS Q2 PRIMARILY RELATED TO LOWER ACTIVITY & PRICING IN PRESSURE PUMPING & WELL CONSTRUCTION SERVICES.REMAIN CONFIDENT THAT CO WILL ACHIEVE HIGH SINGLE-DIGIT INTERNATIONAL GROWTH FOR ALL OF 2019.QTRLY DRILLING AND EVALUATION REVENUE $2,044 MILLION VERSUS $2,002 MILLION.
Halliburton Company Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Halliburton Company announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 5,550 million compared to USD 6,172 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 536 million compared to USD 716 million a year ago. Net income was USD 295 million compared to USD 435 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.34 compared to USD 0.5 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 17,217 million compared to USD 18,059 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,204 million compared to USD 1,859 million a year ago. Net income was USD 522 million compared to USD 992 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.6 compared to USD 1.13 a year ago.
