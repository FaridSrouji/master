Halliburton Sets Quarterly Dividend Of $0.18Per Share. SETS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.18PER SHARE.
Halliburton Company Declares 2019 Fourth Quarter Dividend, Payable on December 26, 2019. Halliburton Company announced that its board of directors has declared a 2019 fourth quarter dividend of eighteen cents $0.18 a share on the company’s common stock payable on December 26, 2019 to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 5, 2019.
