KLA Launches New Portfolio of Automotive Products to Improve Chip Yield and Reliability. MILPITAS, CALIF., JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, KLA CORPORATION (NASDAQ: KLAC) ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF FOUR NEW PRODUCTS FOR AUTOMOTIVE CHIP MANUFACTURING: THE 8935 HIGH PRODUCTIVITY PATTERNED WAFER INSPECTION SYSTEM, THE C205 BROADBAND PLASMA PATTERNED WAFER INSPECTION SYSTEM, THE SURFSCAN® SP A2/A3 UNPATTERNED WAFER INSPECTION SYSTEMS AND I-PAT® INLINE DEFECT PART AVERAGE TESTING SCREENING SOLUTION.
