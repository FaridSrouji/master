Archer-Daniels-Midland Company Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Archer-Daniels-Midland Company announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 16,726 million compared to USD 15,800 million a year ago. Net income was USD 407 million compared to USD 536 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.72 compared to USD 0.94 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 48,327 million compared to USD 48,394 million a year ago. Net income was USD 875 million compared to USD 1,495 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.55 compared to USD 2.64 a year ago.
