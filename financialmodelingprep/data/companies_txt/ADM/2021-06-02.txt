ADM Joins the Genesis Consortium to Support Startups Powering the Future of Sustainable Innovation. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADM VENTURES, THE CORPORATE VENTURE CAPITAL ARM OF ADM (NYSE: ADM), TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS COLLABORATION WITH THE GENESIS CONSORTIUM. GENESIS, FOUNDED BY SILICON VALLEY-BASED INVESTORS SOSV AND MAYFIELD FUND, IS A GLOBAL ALLIANCE OF VENTURE CAPITAL FIRMS AND CORPORATIONS DEDICATED TO SUPPORTING STARTUPS THAT LEVERAGE BIOLOGY TO PROMOTE HUMAN AND PLANETARY HEALTH.
