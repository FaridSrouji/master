Fertoz Secures Supply Agreement With Archer Daniels Midland. FERTOZ LTD- SECURES SUPPLY AGREEMENT WITH ARCHER DANIELS MIDLAND, ADM TO MAKE FERTOZ'S PHOSPHATE-BASED FERTILISER PRODUCTS AVAILABLE TO ITS CUSTOMERS.FERTOZ LTD - FERTOZ DISTRIBUTOR, BLAIRS AG, EXECUTED A 15,000T ORDER TO BE DELIVERED AS 5,000T/PA TARGETED OVER NEXT THREE YEARS.
