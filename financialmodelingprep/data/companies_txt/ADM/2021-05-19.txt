ADM Announces Successful Completion of One Million Metric Ton Carbon Capture and Storage Project. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ADM (NYSE: ADM) AND THE UNIVERSITY OF ILLINOIS ANNOUNCED TODAY THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF THE ILLINOIS BASIN - DECATUR PROJECT (IBDP), A CARBON CAPTURE AND STORAGE (CCS) PROJECT DESIGNED TO EVALUATE AND TEST THE TECHNOLOGY AT COMMERCIAL SCALE. THIS IS ONE OF TWO CCS PROJECTS LOCATED ADJACENT TO ADM'S CORN PROCESSING PLANT IN DECATUR, ILLINOIS. THE FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND PROJECT WAS PRIMARILY FUNDED THROUGH THE MIDWEST GEOLOGICAL SEQUESTRATION CONSORTIUM (MGSC) BY THE U.S.
