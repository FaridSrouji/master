8 Rivers Capital, ADM Announce Intention To Make Illinois Home To Game-Changing Zero Emissions Project. DECATUR, ILL., APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ONE OF THE WORLD'S FIRST ZERO EMISSIONS ALLAM-FETVEDT CYCLE POWER PLANTS, THE BROADWING CLEAN ENERGY COMPLEX, IS POISED TO BE BUILT IN ILLINOIS, GENERATING 280 MW OF CLEAN POWER TO HELP DECARBONIZE THE INDUSTRIAL, TRANSPORT, AND ELECTRICITY SECTORS.
