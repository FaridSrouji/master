Unum, McLean Hospital and Meru Health Partner to Help Employers Support Their Workers' Behavioral Health. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., APRIL 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNUM (NYSE: UNM), A LEADING PROVIDER OF FINANCIAL PROTECTION BENEFITS IN THE U.S. AND U.K., IS COLLABORATING WITH MCLEAN HOSPITAL AND MERU HEALTH TO DEVELOP A HOLISTIC BENEFITS SOLUTION FOCUSED ON PREVENTION TO HELP EMPLOYERS SUPPORT THE BEHAVIORAL HEALTH OF EMPLOYEES.
