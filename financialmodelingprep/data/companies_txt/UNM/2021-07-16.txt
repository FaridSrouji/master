Unum Group declares quarterly dividend of $0.30 per share of its common stock. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., JULY 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EFFECTIVE JULY 16, 2021, THE UNUM GROUP (NYSE:UNM) BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID AUGUST 20, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 30, 2021.
