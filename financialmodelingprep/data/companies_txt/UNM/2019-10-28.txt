Unum Group Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Unum Group announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 2,960 million compared to USD 2,927.8 million a year ago. Net income was USD 242 million compared to net loss of USD 284.7 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.16 compared to basic loss per share of USD 1.3 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 8,964.3 million compared to USD 8,717.7 million a year ago. Net income was USD 804.1 million compared to USD 274.3 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.81 compared to USD 1.24 a year ago.
