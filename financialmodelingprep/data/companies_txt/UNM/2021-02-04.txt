Unum Group Reports Fourth Quarter 2020 Results. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., FEB. 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNUM GROUP (NYSE: UNM) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $135.4 MILLION ($0.66 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE) FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020, COMPARED TO NET INCOME OF $296.2 MILLION ($1.44 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE) FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2019....
