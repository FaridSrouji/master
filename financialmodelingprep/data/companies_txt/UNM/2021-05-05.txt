Unum Group Reports First Quarter 2021 Results. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNUM GROUP (NYSE: UNM) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $153.0 MILLION ($0.75 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE) FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021, COMPARED TO NET INCOME OF $161.0 MILLION ($0.79 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE) FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020.
