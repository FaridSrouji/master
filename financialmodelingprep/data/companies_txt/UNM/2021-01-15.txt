Unum Group declares quarterly dividend of $0.285 per share of its common stock. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., JAN. 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EFFECTIVE JANUARY 15, 2021, THE UNUM GROUP (NYSE: UNM) BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.285 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK TO BE PAID ON FEBRUARY 19, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JANUARY 29, 2021.
