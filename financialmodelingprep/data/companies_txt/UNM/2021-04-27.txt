New Unum Total Leave ™ will help employers and employees better manage complex leave process. CHATTANOOGA, TENN., APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNUM (NYSE: UNM), A LEADING PROVIDER OF FINANCIAL PROTECTION BENEFITS IN THE U.S. AND U.K., IS LAUNCHING UNUM TOTAL LEAVE™, A NEW LEAVE MANAGEMENT SERVICE POWERED BY A UNIQUE DIGITAL LEAVE ECOSYSTEM TO ASSIST EMPLOYERS AND EMPLOYEES WITH THE COMPLEX LEAVE PROCESS.
