Charter Communications, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Charter Communications, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 11,450 million compared to USD 10,892 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,586 million compared to USD 1,380 million a year ago. Net income was USD 387 million compared to USD 493 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.77 compared to USD 2.14 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.74 compared to USD 2.11 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 34,003 million compared to USD 32,403 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 4,552 million compared to USD 3,782 million a year ago. Net income was USD 954 million compared to USD 934 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 4.3 compared to USD 3.99 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.23 compared to USD 3.93 a year ago.
