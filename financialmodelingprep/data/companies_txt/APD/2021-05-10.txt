Air Products Reports Fiscal 2021 Second Quarter GAAP EPS# of $2.13 and Adjusted EPS* of $2.08. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --   Q2 FY21 (COMPARISONS VERSUS PRIOR YEAR): GAAP EPS OF $2.13, DOWN FOUR PERCENT; GAAP NET INCOME OF $477 MILLION, DOWN THREE PERCENT; AND GAAP NET INCOME MARGIN OF 19.1 PERCENT, DOWN 300 BASIS POINTS.
