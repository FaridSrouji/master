Air Products And Haldor Topsoe Sign Global Alliance Agreement. AIR PRODUCTS AND HALDOR TOPSOE SIGN GLOBAL ALLIANCE AGREEMENT FOR COLLABORATION ON LARGE-SCALE AMMONIA, METHANOL, AND/OR DIMETHYL ETHER PROJECTS AROUND THE WORLD.AIR PRODUCTS - CO, HALDOR TOPSOE WILL COLLABORATE ON LARGE-SCALE AMMONIA, METHANOL AND/OR DIMETHYL ETHER PLANTS TO BE DEVELOPED AND BUILT GLOBALLY.
