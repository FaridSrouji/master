Air Products and Chemicals, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Fourth Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Air Products and Chemicals, Inc. announced earnings results for the fourth quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the fourth quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,283.2 million compared to USD 2,298.9 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 603.2 million compared to USD 533.7 million a year ago. Net income was USD 503.2 million compared to USD 452.9 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.28 compared to USD 2.06 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.27 compared to USD 2.05 a year ago. For the full year, sales was USD 8,918.9 million compared to USD 8,930.2 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,144.4 million compared to USD 1,965.6 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,760 million compared to USD 1,497.8 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 7.99 compared to USD 6.64 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 7.99 compared to USD 6.83 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 7.94 compared to USD 6.59 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 7.94 compared to USD 6.78 a year ago.
