Air Products and New Flyer Five-City Tour in the State of Washington Demonstrates Benefits of Hydrogen Fuel Cell Bus Technology. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., MARCH 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) AND NEW FLYER OF AMERICA INC. HOSTED A FIVE-CITY TOUR IN WASHINGTON STATE DURING THE WEEK OF MARCH 22 ND TO DEMONSTRATE THE BENEFITS OF FUEL CELL ELECTRIC BUSES POWERED WITH AIR PRODUCTS' HYDROGEN.
