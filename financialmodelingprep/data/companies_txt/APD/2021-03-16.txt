Air Products to Showcase Its World-Leading LNG Equipment and Technology at International LNG Congress Russia. LEHIGH VALLEY, PA., MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AIR PRODUCTS (NYSE:APD) WILL BE EXHIBITING AT THE 7TH ANNUAL INTERNATIONAL LNG CONGRESS RUSSIA IN MOSCOW MARCH 17-18, WHERE AIR PRODUCTS WILL HIGHLIGHT THE COMPANY'S WORLD-LEADING LIQUEFIED NATURAL GAS (LNG) PROCESS TECHNOLOGY AND EQUIPMENT AND SHARE SOME OF ITS LATEST DEVELOPMENTS AND INNOVATIONS.
