Kraft Heinz Announces Agreement to Sell Its Nuts Business to Hormel Foods. PITTSBURGH & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE KRAFT HEINZ COMPANY (“KRAFT HEINZ” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS NUTS BUSINESS TO HORMEL FOODS CORPORATION IN A CASH TRANSACTION FOR $3.35 BILLION. THE PROPOSED TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN THE FIRST HALF OF 2021, SUBJECT TO REGULATORY REVIEW AND APPROVAL.
