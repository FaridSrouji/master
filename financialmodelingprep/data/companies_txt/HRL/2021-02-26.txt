Sadler's Smokehouse Donates more than $285,000 of Pulled Pork to Help Feed Those in Need in Texas and Across the Country. HENDERSON, TEXAS, FEB. 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SADLER'S SMOKEHOUSE IS DONATING MORE THAN 65,000 POUNDS OF PULLED PORK (MORE THAN 56,000 MEALS*) TO HUNGER-RELIEF PARTNERS THROUGHOUT THE SOUTHERN AND MIDWEST U.S. TO HELP TO FEED FAMILIES IN NEED, INCLUDING THOSE IMPACTED BY THE RECENT...
