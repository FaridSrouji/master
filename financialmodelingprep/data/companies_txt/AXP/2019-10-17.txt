American Express Company Provides Revenue Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019; Reaffirms Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019. American Express Company provided revenue guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019. The company expects revenue growth of 8% to 10% for the fourth quarter of 2019. The company reaffirmed earnings guidance for the year 2019. For the year, the company expects GAAP EPS to be in the range of $7.64 to $8.14.
