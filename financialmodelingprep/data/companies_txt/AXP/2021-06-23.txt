American Express Commits More Than $100 Million to Inspire Consumers to Support Small Businesses Globally Through its Year-Round Shop Small® Campaign. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN EXPRESS (NYSE: AXP) ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS LAUNCHING ITS NEW “LET'S GO SHOP SMALL” CAMPAIGN WITH A MORE THAN $100 MILLION COMMITMENT TO INSPIRE CONSUMERS TO SUPPORT SMALL BUSINESSES GLOBALLY THROUGH ITS YEAR-ROUND SHOP SMALL CAMPAIGN1. AS PART OF ITS YEAR-ROUND SHOP SMALL MOVEMENT, THE NEW CAMPAIGN BUILDS ON ITS COMMITMENT LAST YEAR TO HELP FURTHER ENCOURAGE U.S. CONSUMERS TO SHOP SMALL TO SUPPORT LOCAL BUSINESSES AS THEY CONTINUE TO RECOVER.
