American Express Company Declares Semiannual Preferred Shares Dividend, Payable on November 15, 2019. American Express Company declared a semiannual dividend on the company’s 5.200% Fixed Rate /Floating Rate Noncumulative Preferred Shares, Series B, of $26,000 per share (which is equivalent to $26.00 per related Depositary Share). The dividend is payable on November 15, 2019 to shareholders of record on November 1, 2019.
American Express Company Presents at Money20/20 U.S.A., Oct-27-2019. American Express Company Presents at Money20/20 U.S.A., Oct-27-2019 . Venue: Las Vegas, Nevada, United States. Speakers: Dean Henry, EVP - Global Product Management, Global Commercial Payments, Gina Taylor, SVP & General Manager of Global Business Financing for Global Commercial Service, Jaime Steinberger, Director Consumer Onboarding & Digital Engagement, Rupa Krishnan, VP, Network Products, Sara Milsten, SVP, New Member Acquisition, Suraj Madnani, Vice President, Risk Management & Product Development.
