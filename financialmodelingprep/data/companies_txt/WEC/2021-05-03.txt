WEC Energy Group reports solid first-quarter results. MILWAUKEE, MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC) TODAY REPORTED NET INCOME OF $510.1 MILLION, OR $1.61 PER SHARE, FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021 – UP FROM $452.5 MILLION, OR $1.43 PER SHARE, FOR LAST YEAR'S FIRST QUARTER. CONSOLIDATED REVENUES TOTALED $2.7 BILLION, UP $582.8 MILLION FROM LAST YEAR'S FIRST QUARTER.
