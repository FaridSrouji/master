We Energies and Wisconsin Public Service announce state's largest renewable energy project. MILWAUKEE, APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WE ENERGIES AND WISCONSIN PUBLIC SERVICE, THE LARGEST UTILITIES IN WEC ENERGY GROUP (NYSE: WEC), ARE PROPOSING TO PURCHASE THE KOSHKONONG SOLAR ENERGY CENTER, A PLANNED 465-MEGAWATT (MW) SOLAR AND BATTERY STORAGE PROJECT IN SOUTH-CENTRAL WISCONSIN. IF APPROVED, THE PROJECT WOULD BE THE LARGEST RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECT IN THE STATE.
