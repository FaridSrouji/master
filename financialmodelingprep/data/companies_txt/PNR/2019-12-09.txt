Pentair Announces Dividend Increase And Quarterly Cash Dividend. PENTAIR ANNOUNCES DIVIDEND INCREASE AND QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND.PENTAIR PLC - BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A 6 PERCENT INCREASE IN COMPANY'S REGULAR CASH DIVIDEND RATE.PENTAIR PLC - WILL PAY A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.19 PER SHARE ON FEBRUARY 7, 2020.
