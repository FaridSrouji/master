ICE Benchmark Administration expands ICE Term SONIA Reference Rates methodology to include Tradeweb's Dealer to Client data. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE:ICE), A LEADING OPERATOR OF GLOBAL EXCHANGES AND CLEARING HOUSES AND PROVIDER OF MORTGAGE TECHNOLOGY, DATA AND LISTINGS SERVICES, ANNOUNCES THAT ICE BENCHMARK ADMINISTRATION LIMITED (“IBA”) HAS EXPANDED THE INPUT DATA USED IN ITS ICE TERM SONIA REFERENCE RATES “WATERFALL” METHODOLOGY TO INCLUDE TRADEWEB'S DEALER TO CLIENT DATA. IN JULY 2020, IBA LAUNCHED AN INITIAL, BETA VERSION OF ITS ICE TERM SONIA REFERENCE RATES (“ICE TSRR”). IC
