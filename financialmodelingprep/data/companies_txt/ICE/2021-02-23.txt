ICE Reports Record Futures Open Interest of 46.9 Million Contracts. LONDON & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE:ICE), A LEADING OPERATOR OF GLOBAL EXCHANGES AND CLEARING HOUSES AND PROVIDER OF MORTGAGE TECHNOLOGY, DATA AND LISTINGS SERVICES, ANNOUNCED RECORD TOTAL FUTURES OPEN INTEREST ON FEBRUARY 19, 2021. ICE'S BRENT FUTURES CONTRACT, WHICH SERVES AS THE GLOBAL BENCHMARK FOR CRUDE OIL, REACHED AN ALL-TIME OPEN INTEREST RECORD OF APPROXIMATELY 2.76 MILLION CONTRACTS ON FEBRUARY 19, EXCEEDING THE PREVIOUS RECORD SET IN APRIL 2020.
