ICE Benchmark Administration Launches new U.S. Dollar Reference Rates webpage to assist the market with U.S. Dollar LIBOR® Transition. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE, INC. (NYSE:ICE), A LEADING OPERATOR OF GLOBAL EXCHANGES AND CLEARING HOUSES AND PROVIDER OF MORTGAGE TECHNOLOGY, DATA AND LISTINGS SERVICES, ANNOUNCES THAT ICE BENCHMARK ADMINISTRATION LIMITED (IBA) HAS LAUNCHED A NEW WEBPAGE TO PROVIDE A COMPREHENSIVE OVERVIEW OF ALL OF IBA'S SOLUTIONS DESIGNED TO HELP STAKEHOLDERS PREPARE FOR THE TRANSITION TO NEW U.S. DOLLAR INTEREST RATE BENCHMARKS.
