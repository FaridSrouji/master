L3Harris Technologies, Inc. Issues a Notice for the Optional Redemption in Full of All of the Outstanding $400 Million Aggregate Principal Amount of Its 2.700% Notes Due April 2020. On November 14, 2019, L3Harris Technologies, Inc. issued a notice for the optional redemption in full of all of the outstanding $400 million aggregate principal amount of its 2.700% Notes due April 2020 (the “Notes”). The Notes will be redeemed on December 16, 2019 at a “make-whole” redemption price as set forth in the Notes. On and after the date of redemption, the Notes will no longer be deemed outstanding, interest on the Notes will cease to accrue, and all rights of the holders of the Notes will terminate, except for the right to receive the redemption payment upon surrender of the Notes. The notice of redemption specifying the terms, conditions and procedures for the redemption is available through The Depository Trust Company and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., as paying agent for the Notes. The foregoing does not constitute a notice of redemption for the Notes.
