L3Harris Technologies Declares Quarterly Dividend. MELBOURNE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:LHX) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.02 PER SHARE ON THE COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE MARCH 26, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 12, 2021.
