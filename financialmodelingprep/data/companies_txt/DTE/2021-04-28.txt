DTE Strengthens Pledge to Net Zero Goal by Collaborating with Automakers to Optimize EV Charging. DETROIT, APRIL 28, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DTE ENERGY, MICHIGAN'S LARGEST ENERGY COMPANY, IS COLLABORATING WITH ELECTRIC VEHICLE (EV) MANUFACTURERS TO OPTIMIZE EV CHARGING THROUGH THE NEW DTE SMART CHARGE PROGRAM. THIS PROGRAM AIMS TO HELP THE COMPANY BALANCE ENERGY DEMAND ON THE GRID WHILE MOVING CLOSER TO ITS ENVIRONMENTAL GOAL OF ACHIEVING NET ZERO CARBON EMISSIONS BY 2050.
