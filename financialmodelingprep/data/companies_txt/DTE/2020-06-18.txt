Stelco Announces Entering Into Of Energy Services Agreement With Dte Energy Services. STELCO ANNOUNCES ENTERING INTO OF ENERGY SERVICES AGREEMENT WITH DTE ENERGY SERVICES, INC..STELCO HOLDINGS - ITS WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY, STELCO INC, HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH DTE ENERGY SERVICES INC.STELCO- AGREEMENT IS FOR DEVELOPMENT, CONSTRUCTION AND OPERATION OF A 65MW COGENERATION FACILITY AT STELCO'S LAKE ERIE FACILITY IN NANTICOKE, ONTARIO.DTE WILL FUND CAPITAL COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH CONSTRUCTION OF COGEN PLANT.STELCO HOLDINGS - STELCO WILL PAY DTE A FIXED ANNUAL FEE PURSUANT TO AN ENERGY SERVICES AGREEMENT FOR OPERATING COGEN PLANT FOR A TERM OF 20 YEARS.CONSTRUCTION IS EXPECTED TO START IN SUMMER OF 2020 WITH COGEN PLANT EXPECTED TO BE OPERATIONAL IN FIRST HALF OF 2022.
