DTE Energy Declares Dividend on its Common Stock, Payable on January 15, 2020. The board of directors of DTE Energy declared a $1.0125 per share dividend on its common stock payable on January 15, 2020, to shareholders of record at the close of business December 16, 2019. This is a 7% increase from the previous quarterly dividend of $0.945 per share. The new annualized dividend per share is $4.05, up from $3.78.
