DTE Energy appoints Monique Wells as director of Diversity, Equity and Inclusion. DETROIT, FEB. 25, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DETROIT, FEB. 25, 2021 – DTE ENERGY (NYSE: DTE) ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF MONIQUE WELLS AS ITS DIRECTOR OF DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION (DEI), EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. WELLS WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR ACCELERATING DTE'S PROGRESS IN BUILDING A WORKPLACE WHERE EVERYONE FEELS VALUED AND ABLE TO CONTRIBUTE THEIR BEST ENERGY TOWARD SERVING OUR CUSTOMERS, COMMUNITIES AND EACH OTHER.
