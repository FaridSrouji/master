DTE Energy Announces Launch of DT Midstream's New Senior Secured Credit Facilities. DETROIT, MAY 17, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DETROIT — MAY 17, 2021 — DTE ENERGY (“DTE ENERGY”) (NYSE: DTE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DT MIDSTREAM, INC. (“DT MIDSTREAM”), THE NEW INDEPENDENT, PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANY THAT IS EXPECTED TO BE FORMED THROUGH THE PLANNED SPIN-OFF OF DTE ENERGY'S NON-UTILITY NATURAL GAS PIPELINE, STORAGE AND GATHERING BUSINESS, HAS LAUNCHED THE SYNDICATION OF A NEW SENIOR SECURED CREDIT FACILITY THAT WILL PROVIDE FOR UP TO $1 BILLION OF TERM LOANS PURSUANT TO A 7-YEAR TERM LOAN FACILITY AND UP TO $750 MILLION OF COMMITMENTS UNDER A 5-YEAR REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.
