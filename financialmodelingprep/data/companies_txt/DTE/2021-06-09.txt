DTE Energy Announces Closing of DT Midstream's Senior Notes. DETROIT, JUNE 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DTE ENERGY COMPANY (“DTE ENERGY”) (NYSE: DTE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DT MIDSTREAM, INC. (“DT MIDSTREAM”), THE NEW INDEPENDENT, PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANY THAT IS EXPECTED TO BE FORMED THROUGH THE PLANNED SPIN-OFF OF DTE ENERGY'S NON-UTILITY NATURAL GAS PIPELINE, STORAGE AND GATHERING BUSINESS, HAS CLOSED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED OFFERING (THE “OFFERING”) OF $1.1 BILLION OF 4.125% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2029 (THE “2029 NOTES”) AND $1 BILLION OF 4.375% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE “2031 NOTES” AND, TOGETHER WITH THE 2029 NOTES, THE “NOTES”).  THE NOTES WERE ISSUED BY DT MIDSTREAM AND GUARANTEED BY CERTAIN OF DT MIDSTREAM'S SUBSIDIARIES.
