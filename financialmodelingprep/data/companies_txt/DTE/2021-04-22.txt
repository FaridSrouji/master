Bedrock Commits to Reducing Carbon Emissions in Partnership with DTE Energy. DETROIT, APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BEDROCK, DETROIT'S LARGEST REAL-ESTATE DEVELOPER, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS CONTINUED COMMITMENT TO REDUCE OVERALL CARBON EMISSIONS ACROSS ITS PORTFOLIO THROUGH AN AGREEMENT WITH DTE ENERGY TO ACQUIRE APPROXIMATELY 22,000,000 KILOWATT HOURS (KWH) – OR 22,000 MEGAWATT HOURS (MWH) - OF MICHIGAN-MADE CLEAN ENERGY ANNUALLY THROUGH DTE ENERGY'S MIGREENPOWER VOLUNTARY RENEWABLE ENERGY PROGRAM.   BEDROCK COMMITS TO REDUCING CARBON EMISSIONS IN PARTNERSHIP WITH DTE ENERGY "AS THE CITY'S LARGEST REAL-ESTATE OPERATOR, WE BELIEVE THAT IT IS IMPERATIVE FOR US TO TAKE THE LEAD IN MODERNIZING OUR PROPERTIES AS WE LOOK TO REDUCE OUR OVERALL CARBON FOOTPRINT," SAID JOHN GUARDIOLA, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, ENGINEERING & SUSTAINABILITY AT BEDROCK.
