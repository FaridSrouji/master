CSX Announces Date for First-Quarter Earnings Release and Earnings Call. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., MARCH 23, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) WILL RELEASE FIRST QUARTER FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS AFTER THE MARKET CLOSE ON TUESDAY, APRIL 20, 2021. THIS WILL BE FOLLOWED BY A CONFERENCE CALL AND LIVE WEBCAST HOSTED BY THE COMPANY'S MANAGEMENT TEAM AT 4:30 P.M. EASTERN TIME.
