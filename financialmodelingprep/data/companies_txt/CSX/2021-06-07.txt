CSX Announces Senior Leadership Changes. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., JUNE 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENTS OF MARK K. WALLACE AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF CSX, KEVIN S. BOONE AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF SALES AND MARKETING, AND SEAN R. PELKEY AS VICE PRESIDENT AND ACTING CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.
