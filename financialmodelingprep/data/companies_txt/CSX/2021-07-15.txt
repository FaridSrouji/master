CSX Recognized As One of America's 'Best Places to Work for Disability Inclusion' for Third Consecutive Year. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., JULY 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX HAS EARNED RECOGNITION AS A “BEST PLACE TO WORK FOR DISABILITY INCLUSION” FROM DISABILITY:IN AND THE AMERICAN ASSOCIATION OF PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES (AAPD) FOR A THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR WHILE RECEIVING A TOP SCORE ON THE DISABILITY EQUALITY INDEX (DEI).
