CSX Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividend. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., MAY 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TODAY, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CSX CORP. (NASDAQ: CSX) APPROVED A $0.28 PER SHARE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 31, 2021.
