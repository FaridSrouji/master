Csx Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. CSX Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,978 million compared to USD 3,129 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,287 million compared to USD 1,293 million a year ago. Net income was USD 856 million compared to USD 894 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 1.08 compared to USD 1.05 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 9,052 million compared to USD 9,107 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 3,811 million compared to USD 3,620 million a year ago. Net income was USD 2,560 million compared to USD 2,466 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 3.18 compared to USD 2.83 a year ago.
