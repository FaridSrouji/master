CSX Improves to Highest Rating by CDP for Environmental Leadership. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., DEC. 08, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CSX (NASDAQ: CSX) IMPROVED TO THE HIGHEST POSSIBLE A RATING FOR LEADERSHIP IN CORPORATE SUSTAINABILITY BY CDP, AN INDEPENDENT, GLOBAL NON-GOVERNMENTAL ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO ADVANCING SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES. CSX WAS THE TOP U.S.-BASED CLASS I RAILROAD IN THIS YEAR'S RANKING.
