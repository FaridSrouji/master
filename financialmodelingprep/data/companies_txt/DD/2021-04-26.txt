DuPont Honored with Responsible Care® Award for Outstanding COVID-19 Response Efforts by the American Chemistry Council. WILMINGTON, DEL., APRIL 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED A 2021 RESPONSIBLE CARE® AWARDEE BY THE AMERICAN CHEMISTRY COUNCIL (ACC) FOR OUTSTANDING COVID-19 RESPONSE EFFORTS.
