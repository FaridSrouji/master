Dupont Divests Trichlorosilane Business And Stake In Hemlock Semiconductor Joint Venture. DUPONT - DIVESTS TRICHLOROSILANE BUSINESS AND ITS STAKE IN HEMLOCK SEMICONDUCTOR JOINT VENTURE.DUPONT - DIVESTED ITS TRICHLOROSILANE (TCS) BUSINESS AND ITS EQUITY INTEREST IN THE HEMLOCK SEMICONDUCTOR JOINT VENTURE TO HEMLOCK FOR $725 MILLION.DUPONT - DEAL HAS RECEIVED REGULATORY APPROVAL AND WAS CLOSED AT SIGNING.
