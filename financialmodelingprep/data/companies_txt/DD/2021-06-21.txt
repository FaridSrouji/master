DuPont Publishes 2021 Sustainability Report. WILMINGTON, DEL., JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUPONT (NYSE: DD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS 2021 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT – THRIVING TOGETHER, PROVIDING A COMPREHENSIVE OVERVIEW OF THE COMPANY'S PROGRESS MADE IN ITS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) JOURNEY, AS WELL AS EFFORTS TO ACHIEVE ITS 2030 SUSTAINABILITY GOALS.
