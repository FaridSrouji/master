Advanced Micro Devices Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019. Advanced Micro Devices provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019. For the quarter, the company expects revenue to be approximately $2.1 billion, plus or minus $50 million, an increase of approximately 48% year-over-year and approximately 17% sequentially. The year-over-year and sequential increases are expected to be driven by an increase in Ryzen, EPYC and Radeon product sales.
