AMD Reports First Quarter 2020 Financial Results. AMD REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2020 FINANCIAL RESULTS.Q1 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.18.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.14.Q1 REVENUE $1.79 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.78 BILLION.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.18 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY COMPUTING AND GRAPHICS SEGMENT REVENUE WAS $1.44 BILLION, UP 73 PERCENT YEAR-OVER-YEAR.QTRLY ENTERPRISE, EMBEDDED AND SEMI-CUSTOM SEGMENT REVENUE WAS $348 MILLION, DOWN 21 PERCENT YEAR-OVER-YEAR.ADVANCED MICRO DEVICES - QUARTERLY GROSS MARGIN 46 PERCENT, UP 5 PERCENTAGE POINTS YEAR-OVER-YEAR AND 1 PERCENTAGE POINT QUARTER-OVER-QUARTER.EXPECTS 2020 REVENUE TO GROW BY APPROXIMATELY 25 PERCENT, PLUS OR MINUS 5 PERCENTAGE POINTS, COMPARED TO 2019.AMD EXPECTS NON-GAAP GROSS MARGIN TO BE APPROXIMATELY 44 PERCENT IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020.ADVANCED MICRO DEVICES - FOR SECOND QUARTER OF 2020, AMD EXPECTS REVENUE TO BE ABOUT $1.85 BILLION, PLUS OR MINUS $100 MILLION.
