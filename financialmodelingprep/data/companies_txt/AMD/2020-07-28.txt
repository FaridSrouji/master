Advanced Micro Devices Reports Q2 Earnings Per Share $0.13. AMD REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2020 FINANCIAL RESULTS.Q2 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.18.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.13.Q2 REVENUE $1.93 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.86 BILLION.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.16 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FOR Q3 OF 2020, AMD EXPECTS REVENUE TO BE APPROXIMATELY $2.55 BILLION, PLUS OR MINUS $100 MILLION.AMD EXPECTS NON-GAAP GROSS MARGIN TO BE APPROXIMATELY 44 PERCENT IN Q3 OF 2020.QTRLY COMPUTING AND GRAPHICS SEGMENT REVENUE WAS $1.37 BILLION, UP 45 PERCENT YEAR-OVER-YEAR.QTRLY ENTERPRISE, EMBEDDED AND SEMI-CUSTOM SEGMENT REVENUE WAS $565 MILLION, DOWN 4 PERCENT YEAR-OVER-YEAR.NOW EXPECTS 2020 REVENUE TO GROW BY ABOUT 32% COMPARED TO 2019 DRIVEN BY STRENGTH IN PC, GAMING AND DATA CENTER PRODUCTS.2020 NON-GAAP GROSS MARGIN IS EXPECTED TO BE APPROXIMATELY 45 PERCENT..QTRLY GROSS MARGIN WAS 44%, UP 3 PERCENTAGE POINTS YEAR-OVER-YEAR AND DOWN 2 PERCENTAGE POINTS QUARTER-OVER-QUARTER.Q3 REVENUE VIEW $2.32 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FY2020 REVENUE VIEW $8.44 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.DESPITE SOME MACROECONOMIC UNCERTAINTY, CO IS RAISING FULL-YEAR REVENUE OUTLOOK.
