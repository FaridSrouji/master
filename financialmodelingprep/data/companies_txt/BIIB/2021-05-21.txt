Biogen and Ginkgo Bioworks Announce Collaboration and License Agreement to Develop Novel Gene Therapy Manufacturing Platform. CAMBRIDGE, MASS. AND BOSTON, MAY 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN (NASDAQ: BIIB) AND GINKGO BIOWORKS TODAY ANNOUNCED A GENE THERAPY COLLABORATION. TOGETHER, THE COMPANIES AIM TO REDEFINE THE INDUSTRY STANDARD FOR MANUFACTURING RECOMBINANT ADENO-ASSOCIATED VIRUS (AAV)-BASED VECTORS.
