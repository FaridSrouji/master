Biogen and Envisagenics Announce Collaboration to Advance RNA Splicing Research. CAMBRIDGE, MASS. AND NEW YORK, MAY 13, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) AND ENVISAGENICS TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW COLLABORATION TO ADVANCE RIBONUCLEIC ACID (RNA) SPLICING RESEARCH WITHIN CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM (CNS) DISEASES. AS PART OF THE COLLABORATION, BIOGEN WILL LEVERAGE ENVISAGENICS' PROPRIETARY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI)-DRIVEN RNA SPLICING PLATFORM, SPLICECORE®, TO DEFINE AND UNDERSTAND THE REGULATION OF DIFFERENT RNA ISOFORMS IN CNS CELL TYPES.
