Biogen and Bio-Thera Solutions Announce Commercialization and License Agreement for Proposed Biosimilar Currently in Phase 3 With the Potential to Treat Moderate to Severe Rheumatoid Arthritis. CAMBRIDGE, MASS. AND GUANGZHOU, CHINA, APRIL 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) AND BIO-THERA SOLUTIONS, LTD. (688177.SH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY ENTERED INTO A COMMERCIALIZATION AND LICENSE AGREEMENT TO DEVELOP, MANUFACTURE AND COMMERCIALIZE BAT1806, A PHASE 3 CLINICAL STAGE ANTI-INTERLEUKIN-6 (IL-6) RECEPTOR MONOCLONAL ANTIBODY THAT IS A PROPOSED BIOSIMILAR REFERENCING ACTEMRA® 1 (TOCILIZUMAB).
