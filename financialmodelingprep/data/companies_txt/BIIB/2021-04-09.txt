Biogen Announces Two New Nominees for Election to Board of Directors. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., APRIL 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE NOMINATION OF MARIA C. FREIRE, PH.D. AND WILLIAM D. JONES TO STAND FOR ELECTION TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT ITS 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS, SCHEDULED FOR JUNE 2, 2021. ADDITIONALLY, ROBERT W. PANGIA, WHO HAS SERVED ON THE BOARD SINCE 1997 AND IS CHAIR OF THE COMPENSATION AND MANAGEMENT DEVELOPMENT COMMITTEE, WILL RETIRE FROM THE COMPANY'S BOARD THIS YEAR, EFFECTIVE AT THE ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS.
