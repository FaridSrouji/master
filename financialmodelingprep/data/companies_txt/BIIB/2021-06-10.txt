New Data at Cure SMA 2021 Highlight the Long-Term Efficacy of SPINRAZA® (nusinersen) and Biogen's Commitment to Innovation in SMA Therapy. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., JUNE 10, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BIOGEN INC. (NASDAQ: BIIB) TODAY ANNOUNCED NEW RESEARCH SUPPORTING THE CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT OF AN INVESTIGATIONAL HIGHER DOSE OF SPINRAZA® (NUSINERSEN) AND ADDITIONAL DATA REINFORCING THE STRENGTH OF SPINRAZA'S CLINICAL PROFILE IN IMPROVING THE LIVES OF INDIVIDUALS WITH SPINAL MUSCULAR ATROPHY (SMA) OVER THE LONG TERM. THESE DATA ARE BEING PRESENTED AT THE VIRTUAL CURE SMA RESEARCH & CLINICAL CARE MEETING TAKING PLACE JUNE 9-11, 2021.
