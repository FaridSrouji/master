Biogen Inc Says New Data From Ongoing Phase 3 Study Further Define Effectiveness And Improved Gi Tolerability Of Vumerity. BIOGEN INC - NEW DATA FROM ONGOING PHASE 3 STUDY FURTHER DEFINE EFFECTIVENESS AND IMPROVED GI TOLERABILITY OF VUMERITY.BIOGEN - REAL-WORLD FINDINGS EVALUATE QUALITY OF LIFE BENEFITS ASSOCIATED WITH TYSABRI WHEN COMPARED TO OCREVUS.BIOGEN - ADDITIONAL REAL-WORLD FINDINGS REPORT POSITIVE BENEFITS OF PLEGRIDY & AVONEX IN OLDER INDIVIDUALS WITH RELAPSING MULTIPLE SCLEROSIS.BIOGEN - DATA FROM EVOLVE-MS-1 STUDY SHOW ANNUAL RATE OF BRAIN VOLUME CHANGE IN THOSE TREATED WITH VUMERITY FOR 2 YRS SIMILAR TO HEALTHY INDIVIDUALS.BIOGEN - DATA FROM EVOLVE-MS-1 STUDY SHOWS ABOUT 90% OF PEOPLE TREATED WITH VUMERITY REMAINED FREE FROM CONFIRMED DISABILITY PROGRESSION.
