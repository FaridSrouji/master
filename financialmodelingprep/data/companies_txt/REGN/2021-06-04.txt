FDA Authorizes Lower 1,200 mg Intravenous and Subcutaneous Dose of REGEN-COV™ (casirivimab and imdevimab) Antibody Cocktail to Treat Patients with COVID-19. TARRYTOWN, N.Y., JUNE 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  EUA SUPPORTED BY PIVOTAL PHASE 3 DATA SHOWING 1,200 MG DOSE REDUCED RISK OF HOSPITALIZATION OR DEATH BY 70% ONLY ANTIBODY THERAPY CURRENTLY AVAILABLE IN ALL 50 STATES, INCLUDING EIGHT STATES WITH HIGH RATES OF TWO VARIANTS OF CONCERN REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) UPDATED THE EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) FOR REGEN-COV™, LOWERING THE DOSE TO 1,200 MG (600 MG CASIRIVIMAB AND 600 MG IMDEVIMAB), WHICH IS HALF THE DOSE ORIGINALLY AUTHORIZED.
