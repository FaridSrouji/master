NIH COVID-19 Treatment Guidelines Strongly Recommend Use of REGEN-COV™ (casirivimab with imdevimab) in Outpatients at High Risk of Clinical Progression. TARRYTOWN, N.Y., APRIL 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT NEWLY UPDATED NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH (NIH) COVID-19 TREATMENT GUIDELINES STRONGLY RECOMMEND THAT REGEN-COV™ (CASIRIVIMAB WITH IMDEVIMAB) BE USED IN NON-HOSPITALIZED COVID-19 PATIENTS ("OUTPATIENTS") AT HIGH RISK OF CLINICAL PROGRESSION.
