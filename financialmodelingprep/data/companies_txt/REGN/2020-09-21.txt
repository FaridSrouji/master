Sanofi: Libtayo Monotherapy Increases Overall Survival In First-Line Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer. REG-LATE-BREAKING ESMO PRESENTATION SHOWS LIBTAYO (CEMIPLIMAB) MONOTHERAPY INCREASES OVERALL SURVIVAL IN FIRST-LINE ADVANCED NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER WITH PD-L1 EXPRESSION OF ≥50%.IN OVERALL TRIAL POPULATION, LIBTAYO REDUCED RISK OF DEATH BY 32% COMPARED TO CHEMOTHERAPY.IN A PRESPECIFIED ANALYSIS OF PATIENTS WITH CONFIRMED PD-L1 EXPRESSION OF ≥50%, LIBTAYO REDUCED RISK OF DEATH BY 43%.THESE RESULTS FORM BASIS OF REGULATORY SUBMISSIONS, INCLUDING IN U.S. AND EUROPEAN UNION.LIBTAYO IS BEING JOINTLY DEVELOPED BY REGENERON AND SANOFI UNDER A GLOBAL COLLABORATION AGREEMENT.
