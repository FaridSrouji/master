Regeneron Says Co, Sanofi Announced New Results From A Dupixent (Dupilumab) Phase 3 Open-Label Extension Trial. REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS SAYS CO, SANOFI ANNOUNCED NEW RESULTS FROM A DUPIXENT (DUPILUMAB) PHASE 3 OPEN-LABEL EXTENSION TRIAL.LONG-TERM DATA SHOW IMPROVEMENT IN LUNG FUNCTION, REDUCTION IN SEVERE EXACERBATIONS IN ADULTS, ADOLESCENTS WITH MODERATE-TO-SEVERE ASTHMA.DATA UP TO THREE YEARS TO BE PRESENTED AT 2020 ERS INTERNATIONAL CONGRESS SHOW A SAFETY PROFILE CONSISTENT WITH PIVOTAL ASTHMA TRIALS.
