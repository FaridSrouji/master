New England Journal of Medicine Publishes Positive Initial Regeneron Antibody Cocktail Results in Non-hospitalized Patients with COVID-19. TARRYTOWN, N.Y., DEC. 17, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS, INC. (NASDAQ: REGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE NEW ENGLAND JOURNAL OF MEDICINE (NEJM) HAS PUBLISHED INITIAL CLINICAL DATA FROM AN ONGOING SEAMLESS PHASE 1/2/3 TRIAL OF THE ANTIBODY COCKTAIL CASIRIVIMAB AND IMDEVIMAB...
