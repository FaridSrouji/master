Amphenol Corp reports Q1 Results. Q1 ADJUSTED NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.71 EXCLUDING ITEMS.Q1 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.79.Q1 SALES $1.862 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.83 BILLION.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.73 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.CURRENCY TRANSLATION HAD EFFECT OF DECREASING SALES BY ABOUT $19 MILLION IN Q1 2020.Q1 2020 WAS SIGNIFICANTLY IMPACTED BY COVID-19 PANDEMIC.AT END OF Q1, PROACTIVELY DREW DOWN $1.25 BILLION FROM EXISTING $2.5 BILLION REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.FINISHED QUARTER WITH $2.4 BILLION OF CASH ON HAND.BELIEVE IT IS PRUDENT TO WITHDRAW FULL-YEAR SALES & EPS GUIDANCE, WILL NOT BE PROVIDING SPECIFIC OUTLOOK FOR COMING QUARTER.EXPECT SALES AND EPS IN Q2 OF 2020 TO BE LOWER THAN IN Q1.
