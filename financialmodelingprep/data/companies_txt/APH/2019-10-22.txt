Amphenol Corporation Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019; Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year of 2019. Amphenol Corporation provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter and revised earnings guidance for the full year of 2019. For the fourth quarter, the company expects sales to be in the range of $1.960 billion to $2.000 billion. Diluted EPS expected to be in the range of $0.89 to $0.91.  For the full year 2019, The company expects sales in the range of $8.035 billion to $8.075 billion, a decrease of 2% over 2018. This compares to the prior full-year 2019 guidance for sales in the range of $7.920 billion to $8.000 billion. Diluted EPS expected to be in the range of $3.61 to $3.63.
