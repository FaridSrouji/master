Lockheed Martin Will Continue Planned Recruiting And Hiring. STATEMENT FROM LOCKHEED MARTIN CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT AND CEO MARILLYN HEWSON ON COVID-19 RESPONSE.LOCKHEED MARTIN CORP - WILL CONTINUE PLANNED RECRUITING AND HIRING.LOCKHEED MARTIN CORP - HAVE ACTIVATED A $6.5 MILLION EMPLOYEE DISASTER RELIEF FUND TO ASSIST LOCKHEED MARTIN EMPLOYEES AND RETIREES IMPACTED WITH COVID-19.LOCKHEED MARTIN CORP - WILL ADVANCE MORE THAN $50 MILLION TO SMALL- AND MEDIUM-SIZED BUSINESS PARTNERS IN SUPPLY CHAIN.
