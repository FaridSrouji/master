Indra Signs Contract With Lockheed Martin To Manufacture Radar Antenna For Over 150 Mln Euros. SIGNS WITH LOCKHEED MARTIN A CONTRACT FOR OVER 150 MILLION EUROS TO MANUFACTURE DIGITAL ANTENNA OF THE AESA RADAR OF THE F-110 FRAGATES.CONTRACT HAS A DURATION OF 7 YEARS.
