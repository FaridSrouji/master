Lockheed Martin Reports First Quarter 2021 Results. BETHESDA, MD., APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOCKHEED MARTIN CORPORATION [NYSE: LMT] TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2021 NET SALES OF $16.3 BILLION, COMPARED TO $15.7 BILLION IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020.
