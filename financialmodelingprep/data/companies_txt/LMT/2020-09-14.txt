Bae Systems Announces Contract From Lockheed Martin. BAE SYSTEMS - HAS BEEN AWARDED A CONTRACT FROM LOCKHEED MARTIN FOR PRODUCTION AND DELIVERY OF ADDITIONAL ELECTRONIC WARFARE.
Elbit Systems Says Awarded Contract By Lockheed Martin To Supply Additional Complex Composite Structural Assemblies For The F-35 Aircraft. ELBIT SYSTEMS AWARDED CONTRACT TO SUPPLY ADDITIONAL COMPLEX COMPOSITE STRUCTURAL ASSEMBLIES FOR THE F-35 AIRCRAFT.ELBIT SYSTEMS LTD - AWARDED A CONTRACT BY LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) FOR MANUFACTURE OF ASSEMBLIES FOR FORWARD EQUIPMENT BAY ASSEMBLIES FOR F-35.ELBIT SYSTEMS LTD - CONTRACT IS IN AN AMOUNT THAT IS NOT MATERIAL TO ELBIT SYSTEMS AND WILL BE PERFORMED OVER A PERIOD OF FOUR-YEARS.ELBIT SYSTEMS LTD - ELBIT SYSTEMS WILL DELIVER MORE THAN 1,400 COMPONENTS TO LOCKHEED MARTIN DURING CONTRACT PERIOD.
