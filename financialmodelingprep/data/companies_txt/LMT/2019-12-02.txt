Lockheed Martin Secures Two Contracts Worth $1.1 Billion with Work in Metro Orlando. Lockheed Martin Corp. won a $831 million U.S. Navy contract to produce 15 F-35 aircraft for the government of Australia. Four percent of the work will be performed in Orlando, with a completion date of March 2023. Lockheed Martin also won a $327 million contract to procure long lead materials and parts in support of the production of F-35 aircraft for the U.S. Air Force. 10% of the work will take place in Orlando, with a completion date of June 2023.
