Moog Announces Award Of Lockheed Martin Production Contracts For F-35 Lightning II. MOOG ANNOUNCES AWARD OF LOCKHEED MARTIN PRODUCTION CONTRACTS FOR F-35 LIGHTNING II.MOOG INC - LOCKHEED MARTIN HAS PUT CO UNDER CONTRACT FOR NEXT THREE YEARS OF F-35 PRODUCTION WITH A CONTRACT VALUE IN EXCESS OF $400 MILLION.
