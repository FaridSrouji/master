Lockheed Martin-Built Next Generation GPS III Satellite Propels Itself to Orbit. DENVER, JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE FIFTH GLOBAL POSITIONING SYSTEM III (GPS III) SATELLITE DESIGNED AND BUILT BY LOCKHEED MARTIN (NYSE: LMT) IS NOW HEADED TO ITS ORBIT 12,550 MILES ABOVE EARTH. THIS MARKS ANOTHER STEP IN SUPPORTING THE U.S. SPACE FORCE'S GPS SATELLITE CONSTELLATION MODERNIZATION EFFORTS.
