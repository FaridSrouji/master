Lockheed Martin's First Modernized SBIRS Missile Warning Satellite Now Under U.S. Space Force Control. BUCKLEY AIR FORCE BASE, COLO., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOLLOWING A SUCCESSFUL LAUNCH FROM CAPE CANAVERAL SPACE FORCE STATION IN FLORIDA EARLIER TODAY, THE U.S. SPACE FORCE'S SPACE DELTA 4 OPERATIONS TEAM IS NOW "TALKING" WITH THE FIFTH SPACE BASED INFRARED SYSTEM GEOSYNCHRONOUS EARTH ORBIT (SBIRS GEO-5) SATELLITE.
