Lockheed Martin-Built Orion Spacecraft is Ready for its Moon Mission. DENVER, JAN. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NASA'S ORION SPACECRAFT IS READY FOR ITS MISSION TO THE MOON. LOCKHEED MARTIN [NYSE: LMT] HAS COMPLETED ASSEMBLY AND TESTING OF THE ORION ARTEMIS I SPACECRAFT AND HAS TRANSFERRED POSSESSION TO NASA'S EXPLORATION GROUND SYSTEMS (EGS) TEAM TODAY....
