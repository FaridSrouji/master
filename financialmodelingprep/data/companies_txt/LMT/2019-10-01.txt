Lockheed Martin Corporation Win $495 Million Defense Contract. Lockheed Martin snagged a $495 million defense contract for its space division in Titusville, and 23% of the work will be performed in Cape Canaveral. Titusville and Orlando will see a small slice of the contract work as well, with each location getting a respective 4.8% and 1.3% of the work. The contract is for the production of Trident II missiles. Work is expected to be completed by 2024. The contract is one of Central Florida's large this year.
