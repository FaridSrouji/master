Lockheed Martin Announces Proactive Measures To Mitigate Covid-19 Impacts To F-35 Production. LOCKHEED MARTIN ANNOUNCES PROACTIVE MEASURES TO MITIGATE COVID-19 IMPACTS TO F-35 PRODUCTION.LOCKHEED MARTIN CORP - F-35 PRODUCTION EMPLOYEES WILL TEMPORARILY ADJUST SCHEDULES TO SUSTAIN AIRCRAFT DELIVERY.LOCKHEED MARTIN - NEW SCHEDULE FOR F-35 PRODUCTION LINE EMPLOYEES IN FORT WORTH WILL BEGIN MAY 23, DIVIDES EACH SHIFT INTO THREE GROUPS.LOCKHEED MARTIN CORP - CO, IAM HAVE ALSO AGREED TO ALLOW EMPLOYEES TO VOLUNTEER TO BE FURLOUGHED FOR 30 DAYS.LOCKHEED MARTIN - CO, IAM AGREED TO A TEMPORARY ALTERNATE WORK SCHEDULE FOR F-35 PRODUCTION LINE EMPLOYEES IN FORT WORTH.LOCKHEED MARTIN CORP - EMPLOYEES VOLUNTEERING TO BE FURLOUGHED FOR 30 DAYS WILL MAINTAIN BENEFITS BUT FORGO PAY DURING PERIOD.LOCKHEED MARTIN - AFTER FIRST THREE-WEEK CYCLE, CO CAN ALTER F-35 PRODUCTION EMPLOYEE SCHEDULE AS NEEDED.
