EDGE To Acquire Lockheed Martin Shares In AMMROC - Statement. EDGE TO ACQUIRE LOCKHEED MARTIN SHARES IN MIDDLE EAST’S  LEADING MRO PROVIDER AMMROC - STATEMENT.ENTERED INTO A CONDITIONAL AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE THE REMAINING 40 PER CENT STAKE IN ADVANCED MILITARY MAINTENANCE REPAIR AND OVERHAUL CENTER (AMMROC).UPON COMPLETION OF THIS TRANSACTION, AMMROC WILL BECOME WHOLLY OWNED BY EDGE.
Lockheed Martin Reports Q2 Earnings Per Share $5.79. LOCKHEED MARTIN REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE $5.79.Q2 SALES $16.2 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $15.25 BILLION.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $5.73 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.INCREASES 2020 OUTLOOK FOR ALL FINANCIAL METRICS.QUARTER-END BACKLOG OF $150.3 BILLION.SEES 2020 DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $23.75 - $24.05.LOCKHEED MARTIN - Q2 NET EARNINGS INCLUDE NON-CASH IMPAIRMENT CHARGE OF $128 MILLION FOR INVESTMENT IN JV THAT CO HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO SELL.FY2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $24.17 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES 2020 NET SALES $63,500 MILLION - $65,000 MILLION.LOCKHEED MARTIN - ULTIMATE IMPACT OF COVID-19 ON CO'S FINANCIAL OUTLOOK IN 2020 AND FUTURE PERIODS REMAINS UNCERTAIN.Q2 EFFECTIVE INCOME TAX RATE WAS 17.1% VERSUS 15.6%.FY2020 REVENUE VIEW $63.45 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.RECORDED 25 F-35 DELIVERIES FOR QUARTER-ENDED JUNE 28, 2020 VERSUS 29 A YEAR EARLIER.
