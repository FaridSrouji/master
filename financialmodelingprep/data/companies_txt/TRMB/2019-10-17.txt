Trimble Inc. (NasdaqGS:TRMB) completed the acquisition of Azteca Systems, LLC.. Trimble Inc. (NasdaqGS:TRMB) signed a definitive agreement to acquire Azteca Systems, LLC on September 30, 2019. In addition, the acquisition will enable Cityworks to leverage Trimble's global footprint in multiple industries. The Cityworks business will be reported as part of Trimble's Resources and Utilities Segment. The transaction is subjected to customary closing conditions and expiration of the waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act. As of October 11, 2019, Federal Trade Commission has granted the early termination of Antitrust Approval waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act. The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2019. Matthew Gemello, Eric Wall, Hari Raman, Diana Gillis and Juliano Banuelos of Orrick, Herrington & Sutcliffe LLP acted as legal advisor to Trimble in the transaction.
Trimble Inc. (NasdaqGS:TRMB) completed the acquisition of Azteca Systems, LLC on October 18, 2019. Trimble acquired 3LOG Systems and Cityworks for a purchase price of over or slightly over $200 million, with the majority associated with Cityworks in the fourth quarter.
