Trimble and Doosan North America Announce Factory-Installed Machine Control Solution for Doosan Crawler Excavators. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) AND DOOSAN INFRACORE NORTH AMERICA ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DOOSAN WILL OFFER THE TRIMBLE® EARTHWORKS GRADE CONTROL PLATFORM FOR EXCAVATORS AS AN OPTIONAL FACTORY-INSTALLED MACHINE CONTROL SOLUTION FOR THE NORTH AMERICAN MARKET.
