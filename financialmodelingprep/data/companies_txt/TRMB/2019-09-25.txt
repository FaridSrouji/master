Trimble And Qualcomm Establish Alliance To Produce High-Accuracy Positioning Solutions For Connected Vehicles. TRIMBLE AND QUALCOMM ESTABLISH ALLIANCE TO PRODUCE HIGH-ACCURACY POSITIONING SOLUTIONS FOR CONNECTED VEHICLES.TRIMBLE INC - CO AND QUALCOMM AIM TO PROVIDE SUB LANE-LEVEL ACCURACY TO AUTOMOTIVE OEMS AND TIER 1 SUPPLIERS.TRIMBLE INC - AN RTX-ENABLED SNAPDRAGON EVALUATION KIT (EVK) IS EXPECTED TO BE AVAILABLE BY EARLY 2020, FOR USE BY AUTOMOTIVE OEMS, TIER 1 SUPPLIERS.
