Trimble and VayaVision Establish Alliance to Make Intelligent Automation Even Smarter. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., MARCH 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) AND VAYAVISION, A LEDDARTECH COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY AN ALLIANCE THAT WILL BRING ADVANCED PERCEPTION TECHNOLOGY TO THE AGRICULTURE, CONSTRUCTION AND MINING MARKETS.
