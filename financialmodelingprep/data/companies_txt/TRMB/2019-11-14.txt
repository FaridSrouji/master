Börje Ekholm to Return to Trimble's Board of Directors on January 4, 2020. Trimble announced that Börje Ekholm will return to the Trimble Board of Directors on January 4, 2020. He previously served on Trimble's Board from May 2015 to May 2017. Ekholm is currently president and chief executive officer of Ericsson, a position he has held since January 2017.
