Piaggio Fast Forward and Trimble Announce Proof-of-Concept Collaboration to Utilize Proprietary Smart Following Technology. BOSTON AND SUNNYVALE, CALIF., MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PIAGGIO FAST FORWARD (PFF), A LEADER IN SMART FOLLOWING TECHNOLOGY AND SUBSIDIARY OF THE PIAGGIO GROUP (PIA.MI), AND TRIMBLE (NASDAQ: TRMB) ANNOUNCED TODAY A PROOF-OF-CONCEPT COLLABORATION TO ENABLE ROBOTS AND MACHINES TO FOLLOW HUMANS AND OTHER MACHINES IN INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS.
