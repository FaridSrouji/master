Trimble and Pana-Pacific Collaborates to Extend the Benefits of Video Intelligence for Transportation. Trimble announced that Pana-Pacific, will sell Trimble video equipment for use with a subscription to Trimble's Video Intelligence™ solution. Through the collaboration, Pana-Pacific will market both two and four-channel digital video recorders (DVRs) and a variety of cameras to the OEM heavy-duty truck dealer network. Trimble's Video Intelligence gives fleets a 360-degree view in and around company vehicles using forward, side and rear-facing cameras. Through this neutral eyewitness perspective, fleets can record video evidence in the event of an accident as well as enhance coaching opportunities with company drivers.
