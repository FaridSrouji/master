Zoé Announce U.S. Album Release Tour. MEXICAN ROCK ICONS ZOÉ ARE SET TO PERFORM MORE THAN 25 CONCERTS ACROSS THE U.S. TO CELEBRATE THE RELEASE OF THEIR NEW ALBUM  'SONIDOS DE KARMÁTICA RESONANCIA' SCROLL DOWN FOR THE FULL TOUR ROUTING; THE DATES INCLUDE MAJOR SHOWS AT GREEK THEATER-LOS ANGELES AND KINGS THEATRE-BROOKLYN PRE-SALE TICKETS AVAILABLE WEDNESDAY, APRIL 21ST AT 10AM LOCAL TIME TICKETS ON SALE BEGINNING FRIDAY, APRIL 23RD THROUGH WWW.TICKETMASTER.COM  AND ZOETHEBAND.COM BEVERLY HILLS, CALIF., APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE ICONIC MEXICAN ROCK BAND ZOÉ HAVE ANNOUNCED AN EXTENSIVE U.S. TOUR IN SUPPORT OF ITS NEW ALBUM ' SONIDOS DE KÁRMATICA RESONANCIA.
