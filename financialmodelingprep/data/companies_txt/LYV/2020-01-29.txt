Live Nation Entertainment Announces Pricing Of Private Convertible Notes Offering. LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT ANNOUNCES PRICING OF PRIVATE CONVERTIBLE NOTES OFFERING.LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT INC - PRICED AN OFFERING OF $350 MILLION OF ITS 2.00% CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025.LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT INC - CONVERTIBLE NOTES WILL MATURE ON FEBRUARY 15, 2025.
Live Nation Entertainment Announces Launch Of Private Convertible Notes Offering. LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT ANNOUNCES LAUNCH OF PRIVATE CONVERTIBLE NOTES OFFERING.LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT INC - INTENDS TO OFFER, $350 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025.LIVE NATION ENTERTAINMENT INC - EXPECTS TO GRANT INITIAL PURCHASERS OPTION TO PURCHASE ADDITIONAL $50 MILLION PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SUCH CONVERTIBLE NOTES.
