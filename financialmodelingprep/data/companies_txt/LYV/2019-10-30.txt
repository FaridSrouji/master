Live Nation Entertainment, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Live Nation Entertainment, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 3,773.684 million compared to USD 3,835.246 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 260.041 million compared to USD 234.219 million a year ago. Net income was USD 178.925 million compared to USD 172.683 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.74 compared to USD 0.73 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.71 compared to USD 0.7 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 8,658.521 million compared to USD 8,185.945 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 407.764 million compared to USD 362.914 million a year ago. Net income was USD 229.836 million compared to USD 208.136 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.86 compared to USD 0.74 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.83 compared to USD 0.71 a year ago.
