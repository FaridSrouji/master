Energy Bill Assistance Available for Mon Power and Potomac Edison Customers in West Virginia Through WV Department of Health and Human Resources. FAIRMONT, W.VA., JAN. 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MON POWER AND POTOMAC EDISON, SUBSIDIARIES OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), ARE URGING WEST VIRGINIA CUSTOMERS WHO NEED ASSISTANCE IN PAYING HOME HEATING BILLS TO APPLY FOR THE LOW INCOME ENERGY ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (LIEAP) BY 5 P.M. JANUARY 29....
