Toledo Edison Partners with Ottawa National Wildlife Refuge Complex to Protect Nesting Birds. TOLEDO, OHIO, APRIL 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF ITS ONGOING EFFORTS TO PROTECT NESTING BIRDS, TOLEDO EDISON, A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS PARTNERED WITH THE U.S. FISH AND WILDLIFE SERVICE TO DONATE AND INSTALL A 55-FOOT WOODEN POLE WITH A NESTING PLATFORM ATOP AT THE CEDAR POINT NATIONAL WILDLIFE REFUGE, WHICH IS PART OF THE OTTAWA NATIONAL WILDLIFE REFUGE COMPLEX. THIS PROACTIVE WORK WILL PROVIDE A SAFE NESTING LOCATION FOR THE GROWING OSPREY POPULATION AND PROMOTE OSPREY CONSERVATION EFFORTS IN THE AREA.
