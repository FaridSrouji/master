JCP&L Completes Inspections and Maintenance to Enhance Service Reliability for Customers Through the Summer. HOLMDEL, N.J., JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JERSEY CENTRAL POWER AND LIGHT (JCP&L), A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS COMPLETED INSPECTIONS AND CONDUCTED EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE PROJECTS ACROSS ITS 3,200-SQUARE-MILE SERVICE AREA TO ENHANCE CUSTOMER SERVICE RELIABILITY AS PART OF ITS ANNUAL SUMMER PREPAREDNESS PLAN.
