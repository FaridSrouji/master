Sandra Pianalto to Conclude Service to FirstEnergy Board of Directors; Melvin Williams Nominated for Election at the 2021 Annual Meeting. AKRON, OHIO, MARCH 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SANDRA PIANALTO, A MEMBER OF THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS SINCE 2018, HAS DECIDED NOT TO STAND FOR RE-ELECTION AT THE COMPANY'S 2021 ANNUAL MEETING IN ORDER TO FOCUS ON OTHER COMMITMENTS. THE BOARD HAS NOMINATED MELVIN D.
