New Utility Assistance Program for Renters Now Available in Pennsylvania. GREENSBURG, PA., MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE EMERGENCY RENTAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (ERAP), WHICH ASSISTS INCOME-ELIGIBLE RENTERS WHO ARE HAVING TROUBLE MAKING ENDS MEET, IS NOW AVAILABLE TO CUSTOMERS OF FIRSTENERGY CORP.'S (NYSE: FE) PENNSYLVANIA UTILITIES.
