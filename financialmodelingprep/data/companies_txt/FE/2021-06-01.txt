JCP&L Resumes Indoor Meter Reading Following Pause During Pandemic. HOLMDEL, N.J., JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JERSEY CENTRAL POWER AND LIGHT (JCP&L), A SUBSIDIARY OF FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE), HAS RESUMED INDOOR ELECTRIC METER READING, A PRACTICE SUSPENDED IN MARCH 2020 FOR THE SAFETY OF EMPLOYEES AND THE GENERAL PUBLIC DURING THE CORONAVIRUS PANDEMIC.
