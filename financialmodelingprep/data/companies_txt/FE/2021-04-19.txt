FirstEnergy to Plant Trees Marking Earth Day, Arbor Day Celebrations. AKRON, OHIO, APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FIRSTENERGY (NYSE:FE) IS SET TO PLANT THOUSANDS OF TREES THROUGHOUT ITS SERVICE TERRITORY FROM APRIL 22 THROUGH APRIL 30 TO CELEBRATE EARTH DAY AND ARBOR DAY. THE INITIATIVE IS AN IMPORTANT PART OF THE COMPANY'S EFFORTS TO REDUCE FIRSTENERGY'S CARBON FOOTPRINT, PROMOTE RESPONSIBLE USE OF NATURAL RESOURCES AND FURTHER THE ADVANCEMENT OF SUSTAINABLE PRACTICES.
