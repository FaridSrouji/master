Penelec Conducting Inspections and Maintenance to Help Enhance Service Reliability for Customers Through Summer Season. ERIE, PA., JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH THE HOT, HUMID SUMMER MONTHS EXPECTED TO PRODUCE HIGHER ELECTRIC USAGE AND POTENTIALLY SEVERE WEATHER, PENNSYLVANIA ELECTRIC COMPANY (PENELEC), A FIRSTENERGY CORP. (NYSE: FE) SUBSIDIARY, IS COMPLETING INSPECTIONS AND CONDUCTING EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE IN ITS 31-COUNTY WESTERN AND CENTRAL PENNSYLVANIA SERVICE AREA TO ENHANCE SERVICE RELIABILITY FOR CUSTOMERS. THE COMPANY IS ALSO READY TO RESPOND TO POWER OUTAGES CAUSED DURING THE ATLANTIC HURRICANE SEASON, WHICH RUNS THROUGH NOVEMBER 30 AND IS FORECAST TO BE MORE ACTIVE THAN NORMAL THIS YEAR.
