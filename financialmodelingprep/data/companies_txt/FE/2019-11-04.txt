Firstenergy Corp. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. FirstEnergy Corp. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 2,963 million compared to USD 3,064 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 681 million compared to USD 710 million a year ago. Net income was USD 391 million compared to net loss of USD 458 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.72 compared to USD 0.68 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.73 compared to basic loss per share of USD 1.02 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.72 compared to diluted loss per share of USD 1.02 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 8,362 million compared to USD 8,551 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,895 million compared to USD 1,990 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,023 million compared to USD 1,210 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 2.02 compared to USD 1.1 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.9 compared to USD 1.76 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 2 compared to USD 1.09 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.89 compared to USD 1.75 a year ago.
