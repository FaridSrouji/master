Oracle Corporation, Annual General Meeting, Nov 19, 2019. Oracle Corporation, Annual General Meeting, Nov 19, 2019, at 10:00 Pacific Standard Time. Location: Oracle Corporation Conference Center 350 Oracle Parkway Redwood City California United States Agenda: To consider election of board of directors; to approve the compensation of the named executive officers; to consider ratification of the Selection of Ernst & Young LLP as Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm for Fiscal Year 2020; to consider stockholder proposal regarding pay equity report; and to consider proposal regarding independent board chair.
