Oracle Says Co And NIH Collaborate On Cloud System To Support Covid-19 Vaccine Trials. ORACLE AND NIH COLLABORATE ON CLOUD SYSTEM TO SUPPORT COVID-19 VACCINE TRIALS.ORACLE CORP - PROGRAM IS EXPECTED TO SUPPORT HUNDREDS OF CLINICAL TRIAL SITES ACROSS U.S. & INTERNATIONALLY BY END OF YEAR.ORACLE - DEVELOPED A CLOUD SYSTEM CALLED COVPN VOLUNTEER SCREENING REGISTRY TO IDENTIFY, SCREEN THOSE WANTING TO PARTICIPATE IN COVID-19 TRIALS.ORACLE CORP - EXPANDING COOPERATION WITH HHS TO SUPPORT LARGE-SCALE CLINICAL TRIALS REQUIRED IN RACE FOR A VACCINE.
