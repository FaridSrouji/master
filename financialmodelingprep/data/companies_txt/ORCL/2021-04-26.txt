Small and Mid-Sized Universities Continue to Adopt Oracle Cloud to Improve Operations. AUSTIN, TEXAS, APRIL 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEMS & SERVICES (HESS) CONSORTIUM COLLECTIVE HAS SELECTED ORACLE FUSION CLOUD APPLICATIONS SUITE AS THE GROUP'S SINGLE ENTERPRISE RESOURCE PLANNING (ERP), HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (HCM), AND STUDENT INFORMATION SYSTEM PLATFORM. ORACLE'S CLOUD TECHNOLOGY WILL ENABLE COLLECTIVE MEMBERS TO STREAMLINE THEIR FINANCE AND HUMAN RESOURCES PROCESSES ON A SHARED TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM THAT CAN HELP IMPROVE EFFICIENCY AND COST SAVINGS.
