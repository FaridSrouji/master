New Study: 86% of Americans Report a Change in Personality Due to the Pandemic. AUSTIN, TEXAS, MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE GLOBAL PANDEMIC HAS CHANGED OUR PERSONALITIES ACCORDING TO A NEW STUDY BY ORACLE. THE REPRESENTATIVE SURVEY OF 2,000 U.S. CONSUMERS FOUND THAT THE CIRCUMSTANCES CREATED BY COVID-19 HAVE MADE MANY PEOPLE FEEL SMARTER AND MORE OPEN TO NEW EXPERIENCES, BUT ALSO MORE CONSCIENTIOUS AND DISCONNECTED FROM THEIR PERSONAL RELATIONSHIPS.
