Oracle Cloud Gives Prosper Digital Therapeutics Tools to Boost Patient Care at Lower Cost. AUSTIN, TEXAS, MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ORACLE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PROSPER DIGITAL THERAPEUTICS (PROSPERDTX), A COMPANY THAT PAIRS PATIENT MEDICAL DATA WITH CAUSAL MACHINE LEARNING TO DEVELOP PERSONALIZED TREATMENT PLANS, HAS SELECTED ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE (OCI) DATA SCIENCE TO HOST ITS MACHINE LEARNING PLATFORM. PROSPERDTX USES ORACLE AUTONOMOUS DATA WAREHOUSE AND OCI OBJECT STORAGE TO INGEST AND STORE DATA FROM MULTIPLE SOURCES, INCLUDING MEDICAL RECORDS SYSTEMS AND WEARABLES, IN REAL-TIME FOR FASTER PREDICTIVE MODELING.
