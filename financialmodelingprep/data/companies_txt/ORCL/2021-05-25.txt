Oracle Unlocks Power of Arm-based Processors at One Cent per Core Hour, Expanding Ecosystem, and Speeding App Development. AUSTIN, TEXAS, MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO HELP CUSTOMERS AND DEVELOPERS TAKE ADVANTAGE OF ARM® TECHNOLOGY, ORACLE IS PROVIDING TOOLS, SOLUTIONS, AND SUPPORT TO FUEL ARM-BASED APPLICATION DEVELOPMENT. ORACLE TODAY ALSO ANNOUNCED THAT ITS FIRST ARM-BASED COMPUTE OFFERING, OCI AMPERE A1 COMPUTE, IS AVAILABLE ON ORACLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE (OCI).
