Oracle Cloud Helps the UK Government Drive Efficiencies, Cost Savings and Productivity Across the Public Sector. AUSTIN, TEXAS, JUNE 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY ORACLE HAS FURTHER EXPANDED ITS COMMITMENT TO THE UK GOVERNMENT WITH THE UPDATE OF AN EXISTING MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) BETWEEN CROWN COMMERCIAL SERVICE (CCS) AND ORACLE, AS WELL AS A NEW COMMITMENT TO ENHANCE ORACLE'S GOVERNMENT CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE. THE UPDATED ARRANGEMENT IS EXPECTED TO FOSTER AN EVEN STRONGER WORKING RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN ORACLE AND THE UK PUBLIC SECTOR, ENABLING BETTER USE OF SECURE CLOUD TECHNOLOGIES.
