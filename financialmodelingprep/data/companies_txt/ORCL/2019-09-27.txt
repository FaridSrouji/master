Oracle Says Invested $40 Mln In Equity Fundraising For Ampere Computing. ORACLE SAYS IN APRIL, INVESTED $40 MILLION IN EQUITY FUNDRAISING FOR AMPERE COMPUTING, A DEVELOPER OF MICROPROCESSORS FOR CLOUD & EDGE SERVERS - SEC FILING.ORACLE SAYS HOLDS LESS THAN 20% OF OUTSTANDING EQUITY OF AMPERE.ORACLE SAYS RENÉE JAMES, AN ORACLE DIRECTOR, IS CHAIRMAN AND CEO OF AMPERE; CO HAS APPOINTED ONE DIRECTOR TO AMPERE’S BOARD.
Oracle Says CEO Safra Catz's 2019 Total Compensation Was $965,981. ORACLE CORP SAYS CEO SAFRA CATZ'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $965,981 - SEC FILING.ORACLE CORP SAYS CEO SAFRA CATZ'S FY 2018 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $108.3 MILLION, WHICH INCLUDED $103.7 MILLION IN OPTION AWARDS.ORACLE CORP SAYS CEO MARK HURD'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $2.5 MILLION.ORACLE CORP SAYS CEO MARK HURD'S FY 2018 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $108.3 MILLION, WHICH INCLUDED $103.7 MILLION IN OPTION AWARDS.ORACLE CORP SAYS CHAIRMAN AND CTO LAWRENCE ELLISON'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $1.7 MILLION.ORACLE CORP SAYS CHAIRMAN AND CTO LAWRENCE ELLISON'S FY 2018 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $108.9 MILLION, WHICH INCLUDED $103.7 MILLION IN OPTION AWARDS.
