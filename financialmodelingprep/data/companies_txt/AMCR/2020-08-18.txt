AMCOR REPORTS FISCAL 2020 FULL YEAR RESULTS AND PROVIDES OUTLOOK FOR FISCAL 2021.SEES FY 2021 ADJUSTED FREE CASH FLOW (BEFORE DIVIDENDS) OF APPROXIMATELY $1.0 TO $1.1 BILLION.SEES FY 2021 ADJUSTED CONSTANT CURRENCY EPS GROWTH OF ABOUT 5 TO 10%, COMPARED WITH ADJUSTED EPS OF 64.2 US CENTS PER SHARE IN FISCAL 2020.ULTIMATE MAGNITUDE AND DURATION OF PANDEMIC'S IMPACT ON BUSINESS REMAINS UNCERTAIN AT THIS TIME.
