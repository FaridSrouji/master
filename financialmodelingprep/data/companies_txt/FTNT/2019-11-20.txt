Ooredoo Kuwait Chooses Fortinet to Deliver the Region’S First Secure SD-Wan Managed Service to Enterprise Customers. Fortinet announced that it has been chosen by Ooredoo Kuwait to deliver the region’s first secure SD-WAN managed service. Fortinet Secure SD-WAN will allow Ooredoo’s existing and new enterprise customers to achieve accelerated connectivity, transport mode independence, and increased application performance while benefiting from tightly knit SD-WAN and advanced security features. Ooredoo Kuwait itself is deploying Fortinet’s Secure SD-WAN across its retail branches and will serve as a reference for customers wishing to follow suit.
