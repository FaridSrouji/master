Hilfswerk Niederösterreich Enables Digital Innovation with Fortinet Secure SD-WAN and SD-Branch. Fortinet announced that Hilfswerk Niederösterreich has chosen Fortinet Secure SD-WAN [1] and SD-Branch to digitally innovate their network infrastructure at all of their 68 locations serving over 25,000 customers. With its security-driven networking approach, Fortinet delivered cost-effective, application-aware WAN infrastructure with embeded NGFW security and simplified management to ensure reliable connectivity and best user experience to Hilfswerk’s over 3,000 on-site and remote employees.
