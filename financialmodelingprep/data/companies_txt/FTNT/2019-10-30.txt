Fortinet, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year of 2019. Fortinet, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter and full year of 2019. For the fourth quarter of 2019, Fortinet currently expects revenue in the range of $595 million to $610 million.For the fiscal year 2019, Fortinet currently expects revenue in the range of $2.135 billion to $2.150 billion.
