Malta teams up with Duke Energy to study possibility of converting coal units into clean energy storage facilities. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MALTA INC. IS TEAMING UP WITH DUKE ENERGY TO STUDY THE SOCIOECONOMIC, ENVIRONMENTAL AND OPERATIONAL BENEFITS OF CONVERTING RETIRING COAL UNITS INTO LONG-DURATION, ZERO-EMISSIONS ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS BY INTEGRATING MALTA'S 100-MEGAWATT, 10-HOUR PUMPED HEAT ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM INTO EXISTING INFRASTRUCTURE AT A DUKE ENERGY COAL PLANT IN NORTH CAROLINA.
