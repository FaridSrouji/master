Duke Energy Florida files for recovery of costs associated with powerful Category 5 Hurricane Dorian. DUKE ENERGY - DUKE ENERGY FLORIDA FILED WITH FLORIDA PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION TO RECOVER COSTS RELATED TO CO'S STORM RESPONSE TO HURRICANE DORIAN.DUKE ENERGY - STORM COSTS FOR DUKE ENERGY FLORIDA ARE ESTIMATED TO BE $171 MILLION FOR HURRICANE DORIAN AND $400,000 FOR TROPICAL STORM NESTOR.
