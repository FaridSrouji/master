Piedmont Natural Gas requires contractors to adopt new safe digging program; reports fewer damages to its underground facilities. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EVEN AS DAMAGES TO UNDERGROUND UTILITY LINES ACROSS THE NATION RISE, PIEDMONT NATURAL GAS IS REPORTING AN 8% DECREASE IN THE CAROLINAS AND TENNESSEE.
