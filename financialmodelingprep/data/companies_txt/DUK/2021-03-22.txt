Piedmont Natural Gas files rate adjustment in North Carolina for capital investments to better serve customers. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MARCH 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PIEDMONT NATURAL GAS TODAY FILED A REQUEST WITH THE NORTH CAROLINA UTILITIES COMMISSION (NCUC) TO RECOVER APPROXIMATELY $1.7 BILLION IN CAPITAL INVESTMENTS FOR EXPANSION TO ACCOMMODATE GROWTH IN ITS COMMUNITIES, FOR INFRASTRUCTURE TO DECREASE PRICE VOLATILITY FOR ITS CUSTOMERS, AND FOR FEDERALLY REQUIRED SAFETY IMPROVEMENTS TO ITS SYSTEM.
