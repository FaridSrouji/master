Duke Energy awards $1 million in grants to address pandemic learning loss in North Carolina. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DUKE ENERGY HAS AWARDED MORE THAN $1 MILLION TO ORGANIZATIONS ACROSS NORTH CAROLINA THAT ARE HELPING STUDENTS REBOUND FROM THE EFFECTS OF PANDEMIC LEARNING LOSS.
