Duke Energy Foundation awards more than $311,000 in grants to support K-12 education initiatives in 15 Indiana counties. PLAINFIELD, IND., MARCH 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE DUKE ENERGY FOUNDATION HAS AWARDED $311,246 IN GRANTS TO 22 DIFFERENT K-12 EDUCATION PROGRAMS IN 15 INDIANA COUNTIES FOR 2021.
