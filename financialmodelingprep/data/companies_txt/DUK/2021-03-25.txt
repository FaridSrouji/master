Duke Energy commits $300,000 to support internship programs for minority college students. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE DUKE ENERGY FOUNDATION TODAY ANNOUNCED A THREE-YEAR, $300,000 COMMITMENT TO SUPPORT INTERNSHIPS FOR COLLEGE STUDENTS OF COLOR TO JUMPSTART MORE CAREER OPPORTUNITIES IN ENERGY, GOVERNMENT, LAW AND POLITICS.
