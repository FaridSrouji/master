Duke Energy Announces Pricing Of Common Stock Offering With A Forward Component. DUKE ENERGY ANNOUNCES PRICING OF COMMON STOCK OFFERING WITH A FORWARD COMPONENT.DUKE ENERGY CORP - PRICED PUBLIC OFFERING OF 25 MILLION SHARES OF COMMON STOCK AT $86.45 PER SHARE IN CONNECTION WITH FORWARD SALE AGREEMENT.
