Siemens Energy teams up with Duke Energy, Clemson University to study hydrogen use. GREENVILLE, S.C., DEC. 10, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIEMENS ENERGY, DUKE ENERGY AND CLEMSON UNIVERSITY HAVE TEAMED UP TO STUDY THE USE OF HYDROGEN FOR ENERGY STORAGE AND AS A LOW- OR NO-CARBON FUEL SOURCE TO PRODUCE ENERGY AT DUKE ENERGY'S COMBINED HEAT AND POWER PLANT AT CLEMSON UNIVERSITY...
