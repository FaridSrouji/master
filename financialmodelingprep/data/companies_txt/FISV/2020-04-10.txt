Fiserv Chairman And CEO Have Each Agreed To Forgo 100% Of Base Salary. FISERV - JEFFERY YABUKI, CHAIRMAN AND CEO, AND FRANK BISIGNANO, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, HAVE EACH AGREED TO FORGO 100% OF BASE SALARY.FISERV INC - ROBERT HAU, DEVIN MCGRANAHAN AND BYRON VIELEHR HAVE EACH AGREED TO FORGO 20% OF BASE SALARY.FISERV INC - COMPENSATION COMMITTEE OF BOARD RESOLVED TO SUSPEND PAYMENT OF ALL CASH COMPENSATION PAYABLE TO NON-EMPLOYEE DIRECTORS.
