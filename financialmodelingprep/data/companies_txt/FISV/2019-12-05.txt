Motive Partners Acquires Majority Interest In Investment Services Business Of Fiserv. MOTIVE PARTNERS ACQUIRES MAJORITY INTEREST IN INVESTMENT SERVICES BUSINESS OF FISERV.MOTIVE PARTNERS - ENTERED INTO AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH MOTIVE PARTNERS-LED INVESTORS WILL ACQUIRE UP TO 60% OF INVESTMENT SERVICES BUSINESS OF FISERV.MOTIVE PARTNERS- RETAINING A 40% EQUITY INTEREST IN BUSINESS, FISERV WILL RECEIVE APPROXIMATELY $510 MILLION IN NET AFTER-TAX PROCEEDS.FISERV INC - NEWLY FORMED JOINT VENTURE WILL CONTINUE TO BE LED BY CHERYL NASH, PRESIDENT OF INVESTMENT SERVICES AT FISERV.MOTIVE PARTNERS- TRANSACTION EXPECTED TO BE SLIGHTLY DILUTIVE TO ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF FISERV IN 2020..
