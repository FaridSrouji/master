Paychex, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the First Quarter Ended August 31, 2019. Paychex, Inc. announced earnings results for the first quarter ended August 31, 2019. For the first quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 992 million compared to USD 862.8 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 349.1 million compared to USD 320.3 million a year ago. Net income was USD 264.2 million compared to USD 243.6 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.74 compared to USD 0.68 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.73 compared to USD 0.67 a year ago.
