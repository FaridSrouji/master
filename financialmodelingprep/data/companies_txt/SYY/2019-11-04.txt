Sysco Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the First Quarter Ended September 28, 2019. Sysco Corporation announced earnings results for the first quarter ended September 28, 2019. For the first quarter, the company announced sales was USD 15,303.005 million compared to USD 15,215.279 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 668.318 million compared to USD 628.140 million a year ago. Net income was USD 453.781 million compared to USD 431.042 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.88 compared to USD 0.83 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.87 compared to USD 0.81 a year ago.
