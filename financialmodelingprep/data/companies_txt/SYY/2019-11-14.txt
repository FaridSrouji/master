Sysco Corporation Elects Stephanie Lundquist to Board of Directors. Sysco Corporation announced that Stephanie Lundquist was elected to the company’s board of directors at its Annual Meeting of Stockholders, expanding the size of the board to 13 members. Stephanie A. Lundquist has served as president, food and beverage of Target Corporation since January 2019.
