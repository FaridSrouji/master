Globally Local Enters Into Agreement with Sysco and Provides Corporate Update. TORONTO, APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ - GLOBALLY LOCAL TECHNOLOGIES INC., (THE "COMPANY")(TSXV: GBLY) THE PARENT COMPANY OF GLOBALLY LOCAL, ONE OF THE WORLD'S FIRST VEGAN FAST-FOOD CHAINS, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THE FOLLOWING CORPORATE UPDATES. GLOBALLY LOCAL ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH SYSCO, A GLOBAL LEADER IN DISTRIBUTION ‎AND LOGISTICS, TO DISTRIBUTE GLOBALLY LOCAL'S PROPRIETARY MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS (SUCH AS MEAT AND DAIRY ‎ALTERNATIVES) THROUGHOUT NORTH AMERICA.
