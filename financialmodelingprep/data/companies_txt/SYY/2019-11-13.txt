Sysco Corporation Increases Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on Jan. 24, 2020. Sysco Corporation announced that the board of directors has approved a 15% increase in the quarterly cash dividend to $0.45 per share from the current $0.39 per share. The new dividend is payable on Jan. 24, 2020, to common stockholders of record at the close of business on Jan. 3, 2020.
