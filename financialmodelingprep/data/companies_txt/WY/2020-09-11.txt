Weyerhaeuser Co Confirms Current Wildfires In Oregon Have Impacted Co’S Timber Operations. WEYERHAEUSER CO -HAS CONFIRMED THAT CURRENT WILDFIRES IN OREGON HAVE IMPACTED CO’S TIMBER OPERATIONS AND REACHED SOME OF THE CO’S OREGON TIMBERLANDS.WEYERHAEUSER CO - TOO EARLY TO PROVIDE AN ESTIMATE REGARDING THE SCOPE OF THE FIRES’ EFFECT ON THE COMPANY'S ACREAGE OR THE POTENTIAL BUSINESS IMPACT.
