Weyerhaeuser Company Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Weyerhaeuser Company announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced operating income was USD 202 million compared to USD 337 million a year ago. Net income was USD 99 million compared to USD 255 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.13 compared to USD 0.34 a year ago. For the nine months, operating income was USD 562 million compared to USD 1,217 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 62 million compared to net income of USD 841 million a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 0.08 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 1.11 a year ago.
