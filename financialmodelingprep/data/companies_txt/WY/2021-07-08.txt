Weyerhaeuser Completes Sale of 145,000 Acres of Timberlands in Washington's North Cascades. SEATTLE, JULY 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  WEYERHAEUSER COMPANY  (NYSE: WY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF THE SALE OF 145,000 ACRES OF TIMBERLANDS IN THE NORTH CASCADES REGION OF WASHINGTON TO HAMPTON RESOURCES FOR $266 MILLION. THE TRANSACTION WAS ANNOUNCED IN APRIL 2021 , AND THE COMPANY ANTICIPATES NO TAX LIABILITY IN CONJUNCTION WITH THE SALE.
