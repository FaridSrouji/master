The Lyme Timber Company LP entered into an agreement to acquire 555,000 Acres of Michigan Timberlands from Weyerhaeuser Company (NYSE:WY) for $300 million on September 16, 2019. Under the terms of agreement, consideration is paid in cash. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions and is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2019.
