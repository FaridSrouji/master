U.S. FDA Says GE Healthcare Recalls Giraffe Incubators & Omnibeds Due To Potential For Infants To Fall. U.S. FDA: :U.S. FDA SAYS GE HEALTHCARE RECALLS GIRAFFE INCUBATORS & OMNIBEDS DUE TO POTENTIAL FOR INFANTS TO FALL.FDA SAYS HAS IDENTIFIED GE HEALTHCARE'S GIRAFFE INCUBATORS & OMNIBEDS RECALL AS A CLASS I RECALL.FDA SAYS 22,961 GIRAFFE INCUBATORS & GIRAFFE OMNIBEDS RECALLED IN THE U.S..FDA SAYS GE HEALTHCARE RECEIVED 6 REPORTS OF INFANT FALLS WITH INJURIES SUCH AS SKULL FRACTURES, HEMATOMA & EDEMA; NO DEATHS WERE REPORTED.
