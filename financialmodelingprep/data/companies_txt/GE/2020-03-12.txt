GE Chairman & CEO Larry Culp's 2019 Total Compensation Was $24.6 Mln - SEC Filing. GE SAYS CHAIRMAN & CEO LARRY CULP'S 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $24.6 MILLION - SEC FILING.GE SAYS CFO JAMIE MILLER'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $10.5 MILLION VERSUS $7.4 MILLION IN FY 2018 - SEC FILING.GE SAYS VICE CHAIR & CEO, AVIATION, DAVID JOYCE'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $23.8 MILLION.GE SAYS FOR 2019, RATIO OF CEO TO MEDIAN EMPLOYEE PAY WAS 486 TO 1.GE SAYS RECOMMENDS SHAREHOLDERS TO VOTE AGAINST INDEPENDENT BOARD CHAIRMAN PROPOSAL.
