GE Hitachi Nuclear Energy Announces Small Modular Reactor Technology Collaboration In Poland. Oct 21  - General Electric Co <GE.N>::GE - GE HITACHI NUCLEAR ENERGY ANNOUNCES SMALL MODULAR REACTOR TECHNOLOGY COLLABORATION IN POLAND.GE - GE HITACHI NUCLEAR ENERGY, SYNTHOS SA TO COLLABORATE ON POTENTIAL DEPLOYMENT APPLICATIONS FOR GEH’S BWRX-300 SMALL MODULAR REACTOR IN POLAND.
