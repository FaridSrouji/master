GE and Hitachi ABB Power Grids Sign Landmark Agreement to Reduce Environmental Impact in the Electrical Transmission Industry. PARIS & ZURICH--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #GEGRID--GE RENEWABLE ENERGY'S GRID SOLUTIONS BUSINESS (NYSE: GE) AND HITACHI ABB POWER GRIDS LTD. ANNOUNCED TODAY A NON-EXCLUSIVE, CROSS-LICENSING AGREEMENT RELATED TO THE USE OF AN ALTERNATIVE GAS TO SULFUR HEXAFLUORIDE (SF6) USED IN HIGH VOLTAGE EQUIPMENT. THIS FLUORONITRILE-BASED GAS MIXTURE HAS A SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCED IMPACT ON THE ENVIRONMENT COMPARED TO SF6.
