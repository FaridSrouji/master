TRU Colors Announces Strategic Partnership With Molson Coors, Advancing Its Mission to End Street Violence and Unite Communities Across America. WILMINGTON, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRU COLORS BREWERY AND MOLSON COORS ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP THAT WILL HELP THE BRAND'S GROWTH AND DISTRIBUTION OF ITS FIRST PRODUCT BY SUMMER 2021.
