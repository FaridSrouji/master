Molson Coors Brewing Company Announces Retirement of Celso L. White as Chief Supply Chain Officer, Effective November 30, 2019. Molson Coors Brewing Company announced that On October 28, 2019, the Compensation and Human Resources Committee and the Board of Directors of the Company approved various leadership appointments, effective as of November 1, 2019. In connection therewith, Celso L. White, the Company's Chief Supply Chain Officer, will retire from the Company, effective November 30, 2019. In connection with his retirement, Mr. White also agreed to release any legal claims against the Company, comply with certain post-employment covenants, and hold certain information about the Company confidential, subject to exceptions to ensure compliance with applicable law.
