Molson Coors Brewing Company Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 13, 2019. Molson Coors Brewing Company declared a regular quarterly dividend on its Class A and Class B common shares of USD 0.57 per share, payable December 13, 2019, to shareholders of record on December 2, 2019. The quarterly dividend is payable to holders of Class A and Class B common stock of Molson Coors Brewing Company.
