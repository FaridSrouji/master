News Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the First Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. News Corporation announced earnings results for the first quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the first quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 2,340 million compared to USD 2,524 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 227 million compared to net income of USD 101 million a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 0.39 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 0.17 a year ago.
