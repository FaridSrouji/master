News Corp Appoints Almar Latour As CEO Of Dow Jones, Publisher Of The Wall Street Journal. NEWS CORP APPOINTS ALMAR LATOUR AS CEO OF DOW JONES, PUBLISHER OF THE WALL STREET JOURNAL.NEWS CORP - LATOUR WILL ASSUME POST OF CEO OF DOW JONES, PUBLISHER OF WALL STREET JOURNAL WITH DEPARTURE OF WILLIAM LEWIS, EFFECTIVE MAY 15.NEWS CORP - ALMAR LATOUR IS CURRENTLY PUBLISHER FOR BARRON'S GROUP AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AT DOW JONES.
