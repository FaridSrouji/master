News Corp Says Entered Agreement That Provides For Unsecured $750 Million 5-Year Revolving Credit Facility. NEWS CORP -  ENTERED CREDIT AGREEMENT THAT PROVIDES FOR AN UNSECURED $750 MILLION 5-YEAR REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.NEWS CORP -  REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY HAS SUBLIMIT OF $100 MILLION AVAILABLE FOR ISSUANCES OF LETTERS OF CREDIT.NEWS CORP - MAY REQUEST INCREASES IN AMOUNT OF REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY UP TO MAXIMUM OF $1 BILLION.NEWS CORP - ALL AMOUNTS UNDER CREDIT AGREEMENT ARE DUE ON DECEMBER 12, 2024.
