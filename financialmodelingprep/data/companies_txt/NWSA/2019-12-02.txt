News Corp Names David Kline as Chief Technology Officer. News Corp. announced that David Kline will become Chief Technology Officer, beginning in January. He will succeed Marc Frons, who has held the post since 2016. Before joining Viacom, Mr. Kline was CIO at Discovery Communications, and also worked as CIO at Rainbow Media Holdings. He previously served in management positions at Integrated Systems Group and Northwell Health Systems.
