Allstate Announces May 2021 Catastrophe Losses. NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ALLSTATE CORPORATION (NYSE: ALL) TODAY ANNOUNCED ESTIMATED CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR THE MONTH OF MAY OF $213 MILLION PRE-TAX, OR $168 MILLION, AFTER-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES FOR APRIL AND MAY TOTALED $598 MILLION, AFTER-TAX. CATASTROPHE LOSSES IN MAY COMPRISED SEVEN EVENTS AT AN ESTIMATED COST OF $193 MILLION PLUS UNFAVORABLE PRIOR PERIOD RESERVE ESTIMATES.
