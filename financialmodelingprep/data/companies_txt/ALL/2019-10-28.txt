the Allstate Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. The Allstate Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 11,069 million compared to USD 10,465 million a year ago. Net income was USD 931 million compared to USD 979 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.71 compared to USD 2.72 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.67 compared to USD 2.68 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 33,203 million compared to USD 30,334 million a year ago. Net income was USD 3,074 million compared to USD 2,702 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 8.98 compared to USD 7.43 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 8.85 compared to USD 7.31 a year ago.
