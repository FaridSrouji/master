KeyBank Announces $40B Investment In Communities Including Affordable Housing, Economic Inclusion, And Climate Change Funding Commitments. CLEVELAND, MARCH 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BUILDING ON EARLY FULFILLMENT OF ITS GROUNDBREAKING $16.5 BILLION COMMUNITY BENEFITS PLAN, KEYBANK TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL INCREASE ITS COMMITMENT TO $40 BILLION. THE INVESTMENTS WILL CONTINUE TO FOCUS ON ECONOMIC ACCESS AND EQUITY FOR UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES AND POPULATIONS.
