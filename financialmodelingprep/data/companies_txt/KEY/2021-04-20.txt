KeyCorp Reports Record First Quarter 2021 Net Income Of $591 Million, Or $.61 Per Diluted Common Share. CLEVELAND, APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEYCORP (NYSE: KEY) TODAY ANNOUNCED NET INCOME FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS ATTRIBUTABLE TO KEY COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $591 MILLION, OR $.61 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. THIS COMPARED TO $549 MILLION, OR $.56 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020 AND $118 MILLION, OR $.12 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020.
