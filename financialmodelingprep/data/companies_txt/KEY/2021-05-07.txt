Key Private Bank Wins 'Best Regional Private Bank' At The 2021 Family Wealth Report Awards. CLEVELAND, MAY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KEY PRIVATE BANK, THE WEALTH MANAGEMENT DIVISION OF KEYCORP (NYSE: KEY), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS THE RECIPIENT OF THE 2021 BEST REGIONAL PRIVATE BANK FROM FAMILY WEALTH REPORT (FWR). THE FWR AWARDS SHOWCASES HIGH-PERFORMING PROVIDERS IN GLOBAL PRIVATE BANKING, WEALTH MANAGEMENT AND ADVISOR COMMUNITIES.
