Otis Announces Abbe Luersman as Executive Vice President, Chief People Officer. FARMINGTON, CONN., MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OTIS WORLDWIDE CORP. (NYSE: OTIS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ABBE LUERSMAN WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF PEOPLE OFFICER (CPO), EFFECTIVE JULY 26.
