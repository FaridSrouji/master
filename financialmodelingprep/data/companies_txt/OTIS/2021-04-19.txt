Otis Announces a 20 Percent Increase in Quarterly Dividend to $0.24 per Share. FARMINGTON, CONN., APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE OTIS WORLDWIDE CORPORATION (NYSE: OTIS) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE OF OTIS' COMMON STOCK, WHICH REPRESENTS A 20 PERCENT INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR QUARTER'S DIVIDEND AMOUNT.
