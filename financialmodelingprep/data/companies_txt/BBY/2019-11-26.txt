Best Buy Co., Inc. Announces Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on January 7, 2020. The Board of Directors of Best Buy Co., Inc. has authorized the payment of a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.50 per common share. The quarterly dividend is payable on January 7, 2020, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on December 17, 2019.
