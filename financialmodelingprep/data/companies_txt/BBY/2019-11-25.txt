Best Buy Co., Inc. Updates Full-Year Outlook for 2019. Best Buy Co., Inc. updated full-year outlook for 2019. For the year, Enterprise revenue of $43.2 billion to $43.6 billion, which compares to prior guidance of $43.1 billion to $43.6 billion. Enterprise comparable sales growth of 1.0% to 2.0%, which compares to prior guidance of 0.7% to 1.7%.
