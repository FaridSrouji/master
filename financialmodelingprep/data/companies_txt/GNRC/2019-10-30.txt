Generac Holdings Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. Generac Holdings Inc. provides earnings guidance for the full year 2019. For the period, the company expects net sales growth to approximately 8% to 9%, with core sales growth now expected to be approximately 7% compared to the prior year. Net income margin, before deducting for non-controlling interests, is now expected to be approximately 11.5%.
