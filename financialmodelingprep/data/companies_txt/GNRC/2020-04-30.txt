Generac Reports First Quarter 2020 Results. GENERAC REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.68.Q1 SALES $475.9 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $476.4 MILLION.SEES FY 2020 SALES DOWN ABOUT 5 TO 10 PERCENT.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.80 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q1 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.87.GENERAC - AS OF MARCH 31, HAD $573 MILLION OF LIQUIDITY BETWEEN $307 MILLION OF CASH & EQUIVALENTS, $266 MILLION AVAILABLE UNDER ABL REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.GENERAC - REMAINDER OF YEAR EXPECTED TO BE IMPACTED BY SIGNIFICANT DECLINE IN ECONOMIC ACTIVITY WITH MORE PRONOUNCED DECLINE EXPECTED IN Q2.DOWNTURN IS EXPECTED TO BE PARTICULARLY SEVERE WITHIN C&I PRODUCTS, BOTH DOMESTICALLY AND INTERNATIONALLY.GENERAC - 2020 NET INCOME MARGIN, BEFORE DEDUCTING FOR NON-CONTROLLING INTERESTS & EXCLUDING ANY POTENTIAL RESTRUCTURING, EXPECTED TO BE BETWEEN 9.5% - 10.5%.
