Sofdesk Inc. announced that it has received $5.7 million in funding from Generac Holdings Inc., EnerTech Capital, BDC Capital Inc., Desjardins Capital Inc. and another investor. Sofdesk Inc. announced that it has received $5.7 million in equity round of funding led by new investor Generac Holdings Inc. (NYSE:GNRC) on October 24, 2019. The transaction also involved participation from new investor Desjardins Capital Inc. and returning investor EnerTech Capital and BDC Capital Inc. The company has received $4,359,361 from 5 investors pursuant to exemption provided under Regulation D.
