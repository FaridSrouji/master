PPG Industries, Inc. Announces Michele J. Hooper, Not to Stand for Reelection to the Board of Directors At the Company’s 2020 Annual Meeting of Shareholders. PPG Industries, Inc. announced that on November 19, 2019, Michele J. Hooper informed the company that she will not stand for reelection to the Board of Directors at the company’s 2020 Annual Meeting of Shareholders, which is expected to be held on April 16, 2020. Ms. Hooper will continue to serve as a director of the company and as a member of the Nominating and Governance Committee and the Technology and Environment Committee of the Board of Directors until the 2020 Annual Meeting. Ms. Hooper’s decision was not due to any disagreement with the company.
