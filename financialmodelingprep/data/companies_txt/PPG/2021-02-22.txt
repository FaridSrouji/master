PPG Completes Acquisition of Coatings Manufacturer VersaFlex. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF VERSAFLEX FROM DALFORT CAPITAL PARTNERS. VERSAFLEX SPECIALIZES IN POLYUREA, EPOXY AND POLYURETHANE COATINGS FOR WATER AND WASTEWATER INFRASTRUCTURE, FLOORING, TRANSPORTATION INFRASTRUCTURE, AND INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS. FINANCIAL TERMS WERE NOT DISCLOSED. VERSAFLEX IS AN AGGREGATION OF FOUR U.S.-BASED PROTECTIVE COATINGS COMPANIES, CONSISTING OF LEGACY VERSAFLEX, RAVEN LINING SYSTEMS, MILAMAR COA
