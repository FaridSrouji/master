PPG Appoints Bhaskar Ramachandran, Vice President and Chief Information Officer; Chris Caruso to Retire. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF BHASKAR RAMACHANDRAN AS VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER (CIO). RAMACHANDRAN WILL SUCCEED CHRIS CARUSO, CURRENT VICE PRESIDENT, IT, WHO HAS ANNOUNCED HIS INTENT TO RETIRE, EFFECTIVE MARCH 1, 2021.
