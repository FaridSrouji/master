PPG Completes Purchases From Certain Major Tikkurila Shareholders. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ON FEB. 5, 2021 IT COMPLETED THE PURCHASE OF SHARES IN TIKKURILA (NASDAQ OMX:TIK1V) HELD BY VARMA MUTUAL PENSION INSURANCE COMPANY, MANDATUM LIFE INSURANCE COMPANY LIMITED AND KALEVA MUTUAL INSURANCE COMPANY. THESE SHARES REPRESENT, IN THE AGGREGATE, APPROXIMATELY 9.32% OF THE SHARES IN TIKKURILA. PPG COMPLETED THE PURCHASES AT THE TENDER OFFER PRICE OF €34 PER SHARE FOR A COMBINED PURCHASE PRICE OF APPROXIMATELY €140 MILLION. AS
