PPG Launches Traffic Solutions Business Following Ennis-Flint Acquisition. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ESTABLISHED A NEW BUSINESS UNIT CALLED TRAFFIC SOLUTIONS, FOLLOWING THE COMPANY'S DECEMBER 2020 ACQUISITION OF ENNIS-FLINT. THE BUSINESS UNIT WILL MANUFACTURE AND SUPPLY A BROAD, GLOBAL PORTFOLIO OF PAVEMENT-MARKING PRODUCTS, INCLUDING PAINT, THERMOPLASTICS AND OTHER ADVANCED TECHNOLOGIES, TO CUSTOMERS IN GOVERNMENT, MILITARY, CONTRACTING, COMMERCIAL INFRASTRUCTURE, ENGINEERING AND ARCHITECTURE.
