PPG appoints Kappas as vice president, global sustainability, Carroll as vice president, automotive OEM, Americas and mobility. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED TWO EXECUTIVE APPOINTMENTS, EFFECTIVE JULY 1. DIANE KAPPAS, CURRENT VICE PRESIDENT, AUTOMOTIVE OEM COATINGS, AMERICAS, WILL BECOME VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL SUSTAINABILITY. ANDREW CARROLL, CURRENT VICE PRESIDENT, INDUSTRIAL COATINGS, AMERICAS, WILL BECOME VICE PRESIDENT, AUTOMOTIVE OEM COATINGS, AMERICAS AND MOBILITY.
