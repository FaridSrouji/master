PPG Acquires Automotive Coatings Manufacturer Cetelon. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED CETELON LACKFABRIK GMBH, A MANUFACTURER OF COATINGS FOR AUTOMOTIVE AND LIGHT TRUCK WHEEL APPLICATIONS. FINANCIAL DETAILS OF THE TRANSACTION WERE NOT DISCLOSED. CETELON DEVELOPS AND MANUFACTURES A WIDE RANGE OF COATING SYSTEMS FOR THE WHEEL INDUSTRY, INCLUDING CERTAIN PROPRIETARY TECHNOLOGIES. IT OPERATES PRODUCTION AND SALES OFFICES IN DITZINGEN, GERMANY, FROM WHICH IT SERVES MANY LEADING WHEEL SUPPLIERS WORLDWIDE
