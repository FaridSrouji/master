PPG Appoints Scott Bartlett as Vice President, Operations, Global Architectural Coatings. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF SCOTT BARTLETT AS VICE PRESIDENT, OPERATIONS, GLOBAL ARCHITECTURAL COATINGS (AC), EFFECTIVE NOV. 1, 2020. REPORTING TO TIM KNAVISH, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, BARTLETT WILL DRIVE SYNERGIES ACROSS PPG'S GLOBAL AC PORTFOLIO OF OPERATIONS, INCLUDING STANDARDIZATION, OPERATING CADENCE, COMPLEXITY REDUCTION, FOOTPRINT INITIATIVES AND OVERALL OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY. BARTLETT JOINED PPG IN 2015 AS HEAD, SUPPLY CHAIN, AC, U.
