PPG Industries Reports Q2 Earnings Per Share $0.42 From Continuing Operations. PPG REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2020 FINANCIAL RESULTS.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.42 FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.69 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY NET SALES AND EPS WERE SIGNIFICANTLY IMPACTED BY EFFECTS OF COVID-19 PANDEMIC.QTRLY ADJUSTED EPS OF $0.99.QTRLY NET SALES OF $3.0 BILLION, 25% LOWER THAN PRIOR YEAR.PPG INDUSTRIES  - STRONG LIQUIDITY; CASH AND SHORT-TERM INVESTMENTS OF ABOUT $2.3 BILLION AT QUARTER-END.DELIVERED ABOUT $170 MILLION OF COST SAVINGS FROM VARIOUS INTERIM INITIATIVES IN QUARTER.EXPECT OVERALL ECONOMIC ACTIVITY TO CONTINUE TO RECOVER.ENDED Q2 WITH NET DEBT OF $4.0 BILLION, ABOUT $475 MILLION LOWER YEAR-OVER-YEAR.ANTICIPATE POSITIVE OVERALL GLOBAL ARCHITECTURAL COATINGS DEMAND TRENDS TO CONTINUE.Q3 AGGREGATE SALES VOLUMES ARE ANTICIPATED TO BE DOWN 8% TO 15%.AUTOMOTIVE REFINISH AND AEROSPACE COATINGS SALES ARE EXPECTED TO BE LOWER.TOTAL RESTRUCTURING SAVINGS ARE EXPECTED TO BE $60 TO $70 MILLION IN H2 2020.COMPANY'S $2.2 BILLION REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY IS CURRENTLY UNDRAWN.Q2 REVENUE VIEW $2.80 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
