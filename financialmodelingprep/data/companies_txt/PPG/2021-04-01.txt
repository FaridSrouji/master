PPG Opens China Application Innovation Center in Zhangjiagang. ZHANGJIAGANG, CHINA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE: PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF ITS CHINA APPLICATION INNOVATION CENTER (CAIC) IN ZHANGJIAGANG, JIANGSU PROVINCE, CHINA. THE FACILITY IS THE COMPANY'S FIRST CROSS-BUSINESS RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT (R&D) CENTER IN THE REGION, SERVING AS A BRIDGE BETWEEN INNOVATION AND CUSTOMER APPLICATIONS FOR ITS INDUSTRIAL, PACKAGING AND AUTOMOTIVE REFINISH COATINGS BUSINESSES. THE CAIC IS LOCATED IN THE JIANGSU YANGTZE RIVER INTERNATIONAL CHEMICAL INDUS
