PPG Appoints John Stephenson as Director, Acquisition Integration. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT JOHN STEPHENSON, CURRENT BUSINESS CONTROLLER, INDUSTRIAL COATINGS, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO DIRECTOR, ACQUISITION INTEGRATION. IN THIS NEW GLOBAL ROLE, STEPHENSON REPORTS TO MICHAEL MCGARRY, PPG CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. STEPHENSON IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE OVERALL INTEGRATION OF PPG ACQUISITIONS. HE WILL WORK TO PRIORITIZE AND ACHIEVE PROJECT GOALS, PLANS AND KEY ACTIONS; ASSURE INTEGRATION PLANS MEET PPG'S STANDARDS FOR OPE
