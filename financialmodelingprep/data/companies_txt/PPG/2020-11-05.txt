PPG PR-2930 structural adhesive earns 2020 Innovation Award from The Adhesive and Sealant Council. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PPG (NYSE:PPG) today announced that PPG PR-2930 structural adhesive received the 2020 Innovation Award from The Adhesive and Sealant Council (ASC) during the virtual 2020 ASC Convention and EXPO, which runs through Nov. 6. PR-2930 is a state-of-the-art structural adhesive technology that provides composite armor systems with unparalleled performance in the harshest operating environments.
