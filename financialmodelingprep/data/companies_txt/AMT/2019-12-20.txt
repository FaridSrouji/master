American Tower Says Co Entered Into Three Separate Amendment Agreements. AMERICAN TOWER CORP - ON DEC 20, ENTERED INTO THREE SEPARATE AMENDMENT AGREEMENTS.AMERICAN TOWER - AMENDMENTS TO 2013 CREDIT FACILITY, 2014 CREDIT FACILITY EXTEND MATURITY DATES BY 1 YEAR TO JUNE 28, 2023, JAN 31, 2025 RESPECTIVELY.AMERICAN TOWER CORP - AMENDMENTS TO 2013 TERM LOAN EXTEND MATURITY DATES BY ONE YEAR TO JANUARY 31, 2025.AMERICAN TOWER - AMENDMENTS INCREASE COMMITMENTS UNDER EACH OF 2013 CREDIT FACILITY & 2014 CREDIT FACILITY TO $3.0 BILLION AND $2.25 BILLION, RESPECTIVELY.AMERICAN TOWER - AMENDMENTS INCREASE MAXIMUM REVOLVING LOAN COMMITMENTS TO $5.0 BILLION,$3.75 BILLION IN 2013 CREDIT FACILITY,2014 CREDIT FACILITY, RESPECTIVELY.AMERICAN TOWER - AMENDMENTS INCREASE MAXIMUM COMMITMENTS AFTER GIVING EFFECT TO ANY INCREMENTAL COMMITMENTS TO $2.25 BILLION UNDER THE 2013 TERM LOAN.
