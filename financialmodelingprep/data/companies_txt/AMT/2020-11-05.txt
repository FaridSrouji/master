American Tower Announces Agreement to Acquire InSite Wireless Group. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--American Tower Corporation (NYSE: AMT) today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire InSite Wireless Group, LLC (“InSite”), which owns, operates and manages approximately 3,000 communications sites, primarily in the U.S. and Canada.
