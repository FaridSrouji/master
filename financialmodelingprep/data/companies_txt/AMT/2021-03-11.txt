American Tower Corporation Appoints Sanjay Goel as Executive Vice President and President, Asia-Pacific. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN TOWER CORPORATION (NYSE: AMT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT SANJAY GOEL HAS BEEN APPOINTED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT, ASIA-PACIFIC SUCCEEDING AMIT SHARMA. THE APPOINTMENT WILL BE EFFECTIVE MARCH 16, 2021. AS PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED, MR. SHARMA WILL SERVE AS CHAIRMAN OF THE COMPANY'S ASIA-PACIFIC REGION AND AS SPECIAL ADVISOR TO THE CEO UNTIL HIS RETIREMENT ON JULY 1, 2021. MR.
