Chipotle Launches New Virtual Mental Wellness Platform For Employees. NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS TEAMED UP WITH ADURO TO INTRODUCE A NEW WELLNESS PLATFORM CALLED STRIVE THAT WILL CULTIVATE A HOLISTIC, PROACTIVE, AND INCLUSIVE APPROACH TO IMPROVING MENTAL HEALTH AMONG ITS EMPLOYEES.
