Chipotle Launches Rewards Exchange With New Video Game And Tesla Model 3 Giveaway. NEWPORT BEACH, CALIF., JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL (NYSE: CMG) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS CELEBRATING THE LAUNCH OF REWARDS EXCHANGE, THE BIGGEST UPDATE TO THE CHIPOTLE REWARDS PROGRAM SINCE ITS DEBUT IN 2019, BY INTRODUCING A NEW VIDEO GAME CALLED CHIPOTLE RACE TO REWARDS EXCHANGE ON JUNE 23.
