Albemarle Corporation and Albemarle Wodgina Pty Ltd Issues Floating Rate Notes Due 2022 and 3.450% Senior Notes Due 2029 Notes Respectively. On November 25, 2019, Albemarle Corporation issued and sold $200,000,000 aggregate principal amount of Floating Rate Notes due 2022, Albemarle Wodgina Pty Ltd. issued and sold $300,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 3.450% Senior Notes due 2029 fully and unconditionally guaranteed on a senior unsecured basis by the Company. The USD Notes are governed by the Indenture, dated as of January 20, 2005, between the Company and U.S. Bank National Association, as successor to The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A. (as successor to The Bank of New York), as trustee, as supplemented and amended from time to time including by the Fifth Supplemental Indenture, dated as of November 25, 2019, among the Company, Wodgina, and the Trustee. The Floating Rate Notes mature on November 15, 2022 and pay interest quarterly on February 15, May 15, August 15, and November 15 of each year, commencing February 15, 2020 at a floating interest rate as more fully described in the Floating Rate Note filed as Exhibit 4.2 hereto. The 2029 Notes mature on November 15, 2029 and pay interest semi-annually in arrears on May 15 and November 15 of each year, commencing on May 15, 2020 at a per annum rate of 3.450% until maturity.
