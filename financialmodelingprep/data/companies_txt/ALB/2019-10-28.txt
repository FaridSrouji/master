Albemarle Corporation Declares Dividend, Payable on January 2, 2020. The board of directors of Albemarle Corporation announced that it has declared a dividend of $0.3675 per share. The dividend, which has an annualized rate of $1.47, is payable on January 2, 2020, to shareholders of record at the close of business as of December 13, 2019.
