AvalonBay Communities, Inc. Declares Fourth Quarter 2020 Dividends. ARLINGTON, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC. (NYSE: AVB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK (PAR VALUE $0.01 PER SHARE) FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020. THE COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND IS $1.59 PER SHARE AND IS PAYABLE JANUARY 15, 2021 TO ALL COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2020. ABOUT AVALONBAY COMMUNITIES, INC.
