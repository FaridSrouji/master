Lisa Detanna Named to Barron's 2021 Top 100 Women Financial Advisors. BEVERLY HILLS, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LISA DETANNA, MANAGING DIRECTOR OF GLOBAL WEALTH SOLUTIONS GROUP OF RAYMOND JAMES, HAS FOR THE SIXTH YEAR IN A ROW BEEN NAMED TO THE BARRON'S LIST OF TOP 100 WOMEN FINANCIAL ADVISORS.* SHE IS 24TH ON THE NATIONAL LIST, UP FROM 28TH LAST YEAR. BARRON'S DESCRIBES THE 2021 HONOREES AS LEADING WOMEN WHO ARE CHAMPIONS OF DIVERSITY AND COMPASSION. THE RANKING REFLECTS THE VOLUME OF ASSETS OVERSEEN BY THE ADVISORS AND THEIR TEAMS, REVENUES GENERATED FOR THE FIRM
