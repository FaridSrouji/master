Raymond James Financial, Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable January 16, 2020. On November 22, 2019, the Board of Directors of Raymond James Financial, Inc. declared a quarterly cash dividend on shares of its common stock of $0.37 per share, an increase of 9% over the previous quarter’s $0.34, payable January 16, 2020 to shareholders of record on January 2, 2020.
