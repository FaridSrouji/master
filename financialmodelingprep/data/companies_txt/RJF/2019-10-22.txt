Raymond James Financial, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Fourth Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Raymond James Financial, Inc. announced earnings results for the fourth quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the fourth quarter, the company announced net income was USD 265 million compared to USD 263 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.9 compared to USD 1.8 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.86 compared to USD 1.76 a year ago. For the full year, net income was USD 1,034 million compared to USD 857 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 7.32 compared to USD 5.89 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 7.17 compared to USD 5.75 a year ago.
