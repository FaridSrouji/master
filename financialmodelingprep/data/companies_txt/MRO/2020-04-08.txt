Marathon Oil To Suspend Further Drilling Activity In Northern Delaware. MARATHON OIL CORP - ANNOUNCED AN UPDATED 2020 CAPITAL SPENDING BUDGET AND HAS ALSO PROVIDED A HEDGING UPDATE.MARATHON OIL - REVISED CAPITAL BUDGET OF $1.3 BILLION OR LESS, A CUMULATIVE BUDGET REDUCTION OF $1.1 BILLION FROM INITIAL 2020 CAPITAL SPENDING GUIDANCE.MARATHON OIL - TO SUSPEND FURTHER DRILLING ACTIVITY IN NORTHERN DELAWARE, WITH ONLY LIMITED NUMBER OF WELLS TO SALES EXPECTED THROUGH BALANCE OF YEAR.MARATHON OIL - WILL CONTINUE TO OPTIMIZE DEVELOPMENT PLANS IN BAKKEN & EAGLE FORD.MARATHON OIL - "CURRENTLY EXPECT TO TRANSITION TO A MORE CONTINUOUS BUT LOWER LEVEL OF ACTIVITY IN BOTH BAKKEN & EAGLE FORD DURING SECOND HALF OF YEAR".
