Marathon Oil Corporation Announces Unaudited Consolidated Operating Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019: Provides Production Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2019. Marathon Oil Corporation announced unaudited consolidated operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. U.S. production averaged 339,000 net barrels of oil equivalent per day (boed) for third quarter 2019, including 201,000 net barrels of oil per day (bopd). Oil production was above the top end of the third quarter guidance range and up 17% from the year-ago quarter on a divestiture-adjusted basis. U.S. unit production costs were $4.75 per barrel of oil equivalent (boe), down 23% from the year-ago quarter, the lowest quarterly average unit production costs since becoming an independent exploration and production company in 2011. International production averaged 87,000 net boed for third quarter 2019. Unit production costs averaged $1.98 per boe.The company provided production guidance for the fourth quarter and full year 2019. For the quarter, the company expects total U.S. oil production of 190 million bbld to 200 million bbld and international oil production guidance is 12 million bbld to 16 million bbld. Total net oil production will be 202 million bbld to 216 million bbld. The company expects total U.S. Equivalent production of 320 million bbld to 330 million bbld and international Equivalent production guidance is 80 million bbld to 90 million bbld. Total net Equivalent production will be 400 million bbld to 420 million bbld.For the full year, divestiture-adjusted oil production growth guidance is expected to be 11% for total Company and 13% for U.S., above initial guidance of 10% and 12% respectively.
