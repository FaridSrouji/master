Amazon.com, Inc. Announces Redemption of Its 2.600% Notes Due December 5, 2019. Amazon.com, Inc. announced that on November 5, 2019 it will redeem all $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its outstanding 2.600% Notes due December 5, 2019.
