Amazon Launches Alexa For Residential, for properties to add Alexa to residential buildings. AMAZON LAUNCHES ALEXA FOR RESIDENTIAL, A SERVICE WHERE PROPERTY MANAGERS CAN MANAGE ALEXA-POWERED SMART HOME EXPERIENCES THROUGHOUT BUILDINGS.AMAZON SAYS WITH ALEXA FOR RESIDENTIAL NO ACCOUNT/DEVICE SETUP WILL BE REQUIRED BY RESIDENTS.
Myer Partners With Amazon For Parcel Pickup Points In 21 Myer Stores. PARTNERS WITH AMAZON AUSTRALIA FOR NETWORK OF AMAZON PARCEL PICKUP POINTS IN 21 MYER STORES.THERE ARE PLANS TO EXPAND AMAZON HUB INTO FURTHER MYER STORES PRE-CHRISTMAS, INCLUDING CO'S REGIONAL STORE LOCATIONS.
