New Ipsos Study Finds 8 in 10 Americans Think the Federal Minimum Wage is Too Low and Two-Thirds Support Increasing Minimum Wage to $15 Per Hour. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, GLOBAL MARKET RESEARCH FIRM IPSOS RELEASED A NEW, COMPREHENSIVE FEDERAL MINIMUM WAGE STUDY, WHICH POLLED MORE THAN 6,000 AMERICANS ON THEIR KNOWLEDGE OF AND OPINIONS ON THE CURRENT FEDERAL MINIMUM WAGE OF $7.25 AN HOUR. THE STUDY CONFIRMED THAT THE VAST MAJORITY OF AMERICANS AGREE THAT THE FEDERAL MINIMUM WAGE IS TOO LOW AND SHOULD BE INCREASED TO AT LEAST $15 PER HOUR. IN ADDITION, THE STUDY HIGHLIGHTED THE DIFFICULTIES FOR THOSE EARNING LESS THAN $15 PER HOUR
