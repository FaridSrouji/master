Amazon Plans Another Expansion in Westborough. Less than a month after Amazon.com announced plans to open a new delivery station in Revere, it's reportedly planning to announce another expansion on 06 November 2019, this time in Westborough. The retail giant is planning to lease 350,000 square feet of new space in the town west of Boston to grow its existing robotics operation there, according to the Boston Globe. The Globe reports that the company will announce that it plans to spend $40 million to renovate a former drug manufacturing facility, where it expects to employ about 200 people by 2021, designing, building, and testing robotics equipment.
