AWS to Open Data Centers in UAE. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, AMAZON WEB SERVICES (AWS), AN AMAZON.COM COMPANY (NASDAQ: AMZN), ANNOUNCED THAT IT PLANS TO OPEN AN INFRASTRUCTURE REGION IN THE UNITED ARAB EMIRATES (UAE) IN THE FIRST HALF OF 2022. THE NEW AWS MIDDLE EAST (UAE) REGION WILL CONSIST OF THREE AVAILABILITY ZONES AND BECOME AWS'S SECOND REGION IN THE MIDDLE EAST WITH THE EXISTING AWS REGION IN BAHRAIN, GIVING CUSTOMERS MORE CHOICE AND FLEXIBILITY TO LEVERAGE ADVANCED TECHNOLOGIES FROM THE WORLD'S LEADING CLOUD.
