IonQ, Inc announced that it has received $55 million in funding from a group of investors. IonQ, Inc announced that it has received $55 million in a round of funding co-led by new investors Mubadala Capital and Samsung Catalyst Fund, fund managed by Samsung Venture Investment Corporation on October 22, 2019. The transaction also included participation from new investors ACME VC, AV Capital Management, LLC, Hewlett Packard Pathfinder, Tao Capital Partners, Correlation Ventures, A&E Family Investment, LLC and returning investors Osage University Partners, New Enterprise Associates, GV  and Amazon.com, Inc. (NasdaqGS:AMZN).
