Amazon.com Announces Lease Of 12 Boeing 767-300 Converted Cargo Aircraft From Air Transport Services Group. AMAZON AIR EXPANDS AIRCRAFT FLEET.AMAZON.COM INC - AMAZON TO LEASE 12 ADDITIONAL 767-300 CONVERTED CARGO AIRCRAFT.AMAZON.COM INC - ANNOUNCED LEASE OF 12 BOEING 767-300 CONVERTED CARGO AIRCRAFT FROM AIR TRANSPORT SERVICES GROUP.AMAZON.COM INC - AIRCRAFT WILL JOIN AMAZON'S EXISTING FLEET OF 70 AIRCRAFT TO BRING ITS TOTAL NETWORK TO OVER 80 AIRCRAFT.AMAZON.COM INC - ONE OF NEW AIRCRAFT JOINED AMAZON'S AIR CARGO OPERATIONS IN MAY 2020.AMAZON.COM INC - REMAINING 11 AIRCRAFT TO BE DELIVERED IN 2021..AMAZON.COM INC - AMAZON CONTINUES TO EXPAND ON--GROUND OPERATIONS IN ADDITION TO AIRCRAFT..AMAZON.COM INC - AMAZON WILL OPEN NEW REGIONAL AIR HUBS AT LAKELAND LINDER INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT IN FLORIDA LATER THIS SUMMER.AMAZON.COM INC - WILL OPEN NEW REGIONAL AIR HUBS AT SAN BERNARDINO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT NEXT YEAR.AMAZON.COM INC - AMAZON WILL ALSO OPEN CENTRAL AMAZON AIR HUB AT CINCINNATI/NORTHERN KENTUCKY INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT IN 2021.
