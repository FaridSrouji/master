Amazon Donates $1.75 million to construct new STEAM high school in Onondaga County, New York. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMAZON.COM (NASDAQ: AMZN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS DONATING $1.75 MILLION TOWARD A NEW STEAM (SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, ENGINEERING, ARTS, AND MATH) HIGH SCHOOL IN ONONDAGA COUNTY, NEW YORK. WITH A NEW ROBOTICS FULFILLMENT CENTER COMING TO THE COUNTY THIS FALL, A PROJECT THAT WILL CREATE MORE THAN 1,000 LOCAL JOBS, AMAZON IS FURTHERING ITS COMMITMENT TO STEM EDUCATION BY INVESTING IN THE COMMUNITY AND GIVING STUDENTS TOOLS TO BUILD THEIR BEST FUTURE.
