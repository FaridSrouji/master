Raytheon Technologies awarded perfect score by Human Rights Campaign for corporate equality; company partners with HRC to enable program expansion. WALTHAM, MASS., JUNE 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HUMAN RIGHTS CAMPAIGN (HRC), AMERICA'S LEADING ADVOCATE FOR LGBTQ+ EQUALITY, HAS NAMED RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) ONE OF THE BEST PLACES TO WORK, NOTING THE COMPANY'S DEEP COMMITMENT TO ENSURING EQUAL RIGHTS FOR LESBIAN, GAY, BISEXUAL, TRANSGENDER AND QUEER EMPLOYEES.
