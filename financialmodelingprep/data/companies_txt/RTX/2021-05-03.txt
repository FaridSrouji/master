Raytheon Intelligence & Space to deploy airport screening equipment nationwide for TSA. ARLINGTON, VA., MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON INTELLIGENCE & SPACE, A RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX) BUSINESS, WILL EXPAND THE DEPLOYMENT OF CHECKED BAGGAGE SCREENING EQUIPMENT TO ALL FEDERALLY MANAGED AIRPORTS NATIONWIDE UNDER A FIVE-YEAR, $318 MILLION CONTRACT WITH THE TRANSPORTATION SECURITY ADMINISTRATION.
