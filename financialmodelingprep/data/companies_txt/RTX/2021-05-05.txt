Collins Aerospace upgrades U.S. Navy C-130 fleet with long-lasting wheels and carbon brakes. TROY, OHIO, MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COLLINS AEROSPACE CONTINUES TO HELP C-130 OPERATORS REDUCE MAINTENANCE TIME AND COST WITH ITS LONG-LASTING WHEELS AND CARBON BRAKES. THE BUSINESS, A UNIT OF RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: RTX), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED ITS FIRST UPGRADE OF THE U.S. NAVY'S FLEET OF C-130T AND KC-130T AIRCRAFT WITH NEW WHEELS AND BRAKES.
