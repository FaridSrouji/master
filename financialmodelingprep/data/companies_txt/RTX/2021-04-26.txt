Raytheon Technologies Board of Directors Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend and Announces 7 Percent Quarterly Rate Increase. WALTHAM, MASS., APRIL 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION (NYSE: RTX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 51 CENTS PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF RTX COMMON STOCK, WHICH REPRESENTS AN INCREASE OF MORE THAN 7 PERCENT OVER THE PRIOR QUARTER'S DIVIDEND AMOUNT.
