SHAREHOLDER DEADLINE: Pawar Law Group Announces a Securities Class Action Lawsuit Against Raytheon Technologies Corporation f/k/a Raytheon Company; IMPORTANT DEADLINE– RTX. NEW YORK, DEC. 09, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PAWAR LAW GROUP ANNOUNCES THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS WHO PURCHASED SHARES OF RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION F/K/A RAYTHEON COMPANY (NYSE: RTX) FROM FEBRUARY 10, 2016 THROUGH OCTOBER 27, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”). THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER DAMAGES FOR RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION INVESTORS UNDER THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
