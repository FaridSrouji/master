Mid-America Apartment Communities, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. Mid-America Apartment Communities, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the full year 2019. Net income per diluted common share is expected to be in the range of $2.97 to $3.05 per diluted common share, or $3.01 per diluted common share at the midpoint, for the full year of 2019.
