MAA Announces Regular Quarterly Preferred Dividend, Payable on December 31, 2019. Mid-America Apartment Communities, Inc. announced a full quarterly dividend of $1.0625 per outstanding share of its 8.50% Series I Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock. The dividend is payable on December 31, 2019 to shareholders of record on December 16, 2019.
