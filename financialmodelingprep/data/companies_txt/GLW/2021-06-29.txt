Corning Appoints Pamela J. Craig to its Board of Directors. CORNING, N.Y., JUNE 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORNING INCORPORATED (NYSE:GLW) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PAMELA J. CRAIG, FORMER CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER OF ACCENTURE PLC, HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO CORNING'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE JUNE 29, 2021.
