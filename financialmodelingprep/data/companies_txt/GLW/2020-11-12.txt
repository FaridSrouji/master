Corning® Guardiant® Antimicrobial Particles Enable Paint and Coatings Demonstrated to Kill More Than 99.9% of Bacteria and Viruses, Including SARS-CoV-2, in Two Hours or Less. CORNING, NY, NOV. 12, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORNING INCORPORATED (NYSE: GLW) ANNOUNCED ON TUESDAY A NEW BREAKTHROUGH IN GLASS-CERAMIC TECHNOLOGY, CORNING ® GUARDIANT ® . UNDER TEST METHODS APPROVED BY THE U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (EPA), PAINT AND COATINGS CONTAINING CORNING GUARDIANT WERE SHOWN TO KILL MORE THAN 99.9% OF SARS-COV-2, THE VIRUS THAT CAUSES COVID-19.
