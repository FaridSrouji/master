LKQ Corporation Announces Results for Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2020. CHICAGO, FEB. 18, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LKQ CORPORATION (NASDAQ:LKQ) TODAY REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2020 RESULTS THAT REFLECT CONTINUED IMPROVEMENT IN OPERATIONAL AND BALANCE SHEET PRODUCTIVITY AND FURTHER DEBT REDUCTION, DESPITE SOFTENING REVENUE TRENDS IN MANY MARKETS AS A RESULT OF MOBILITY RESTRICTIONS FROM COVID-19.
