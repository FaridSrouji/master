LKQ Corporation Releases Inaugural Sustainability Report. CHICAGO, APRIL 06, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LKQ CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LKQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS INAUGURAL SUSTAINABILITY REPORT OUTLINING THE COMPANY'S ONGOING FOCUS ON RESPONSIBLE ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (“ESG”) PRACTICES.
