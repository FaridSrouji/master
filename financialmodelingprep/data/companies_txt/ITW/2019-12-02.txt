Alpha Assembly Solutions, Inc. entered into an agreement to acquire Kester Inc. from Illinois Tool Works Inc. (NYSE:ITW) on November 20, 2019. Element Solutions Inc (NYSE:ESI), parent of Alpha Assembly Solutions, Inc., will make Kester a part of the MacDermid Alpha business. The transaction will close effective December 2, 2019.
Alpha Assembly Solutions, Inc. completed the acquisition of Kester Inc. from Illinois Tool Works Inc. (NYSE:ITW) for $68 million on December 3, 2019. The consideration was paid in cash. The transaction was funded by cash on hand. In addition, Element Solutions repriced $744 million Term Loan B to reduce borrowing cost by 25 basis points or approximately $2 million in annual interest expense. The cumulative impact of these two transactions expected to be approximately 3% to 4% accretive to adjusted EPS in 2020.
