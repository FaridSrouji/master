Illinois Tool Works Inc Declares Dividend On Common Stock Of $1.07 Per Share For Q4 Of 2019. ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC - DECLARED A DIVIDEND ON CO'S COMMON STOCK OF $1.07 PER SHARE FOR Q4 OF 2019.
Illinois Tool Works Inc. Declares Dividend for the Fourth Quarter of 2019, Payable on January 15, 2020. The Board of Directors of Illinois Tool Works Inc. declared a dividend on the company's common stock of $1.07 per share for the fourth quarter of 2019. The dividend equates to $4.28 per share on a full-year basis. The dividend will be paid on, January 15, 2020 to shareholders of record as of December 31, 2019.
