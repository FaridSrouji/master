Enphase Energy, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Enphase Energy, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 180.057 million compared to USD 78.002 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 33.706 million compared to operating loss of USD 374,000 a year ago. Net income was USD 31.099 million compared to net loss of USD 3.470 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.25 compared to basic loss per share of USD 0.03 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.23 compared to diluted loss per share of USD 0.03 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 414.301 million compared to USD 223.870 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 58.287 million compared to operating loss of USD 3.407 million a year ago. Net income was USD 44.482 million compared to net loss of USD 12.336 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.39 compared to basic loss per share of USD 0.13 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.35 compared to diluted loss per share of USD 0.13 a year ago.
