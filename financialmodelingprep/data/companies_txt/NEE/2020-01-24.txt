Nextera Energy Partners LP Reports Qtrly Earnings Per Common Unit Attributable To Co - Assuming Dilution of $0.50. NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS LP QTRLY EARNINGS PER COMMON UNIT ATTRIBUTABLE TO NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS, LP - ASSUMING DILUTION $0.50.NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS LP QTRLY TOTAL OPERATING REVENUES $206 MILLION VERSUS $155 MILLION.NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS LP - FOR YEAR-END 2020, CO'S RUN-RATE EXPECTATIONS FOR ADJUSTED EBITDA AND CAFD REMAIN UNCHANGED.NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS - EXPECTS ANNUALIZED RATE OF Q4 2020 DISTRIBUTION, PAYABLE IN FEBRUARY 2021, TO BE IN RANGE OF $2.40 TO $2.46 PER COMMON UNIT.NEXTERA ENERGY PARTNERS LP - PARTNERSHIP CONTINUES TO EXPECT IT CAN ACHIEVE LONG-TERM GROWTH EXPECTATIONS WITHOUT ADDITIONAL ACQUISITIONS UNTIL 2021.
