Nextera Energy, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. NextEra Energy, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 5,572 million compared to USD 4,416 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,593 million compared to USD 968 million a year ago. Net income was USD 879 million compared to USD 1,005 million a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 14,616 million compared to USD 12,337 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 4,476 million compared to USD 3,173 million a year ago. Net income was USD 2,794 million compared to USD 6,216 million a year ago.
