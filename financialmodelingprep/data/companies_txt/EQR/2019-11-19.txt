Equity Residential Appoints T. Zia Huque to Board of Trustees. Equity Residential announced the appointment of T. Zia Huque to the Company’s Board of Trustees to serve until the next annual meeting of shareholders. Mr. Huque, who will serve as a member of the Company’s Audit Committee, qualifies as an independent trustee under the New York Stock Exchange’s listing standards. Mr. Huque, 51, served as the President and Chief Executive Officer of Deutsche Bank Securities Inc., the U.S. Broker Dealer of Deutsche Bank, until his departure in mid-2019. During Mr. Huque’s 30 year finance career on Wall Street, he held multiple executive positions in the United States and abroad while leading Investment Banking and Sales & Trading functions spanning many industries, products and clients.
