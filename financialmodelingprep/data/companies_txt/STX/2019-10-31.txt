Seagate Technology plc Provides Earnings Guidance for Fiscal Second Quarter Ending December 2019; Provides Financial Guidance for the Fiscal 2020. Seagate Technology plc provided earnings guidance for its fiscal second quarter ending 2019. For the period, the company expects revenue of $2.72 billion, plus or minus 5%.The company continue to expect revenue and profitability to grow in fiscal 2020, with the second half projected to be somewhat stronger than the first, supported by its16-terabyte ramp and improving mass capacity storage demand.
