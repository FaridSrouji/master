Waters Corporation Introduces the New Ta Instruments™ Msf16 Multi-Specimen Fatigue Instrument. Waters Corporation introduced the new TA Instruments™ MSF16 Multi-Specimen Fatigue Instrument. The MSF16 extends the capability of accelerated cyclic testing by loading 16 specimens simultaneously, rapidly delivering insights into the failure limits of materials, components and products under repeated loading. The durability of materials and their construction is critical for the longevity of products that experience repetitive forces. From aircraft and automobiles to medical devices, understanding durability can be lifesaving. Fatigue testing of component materials or final assemblies involves repetitive mechanical loading (stress and strains) that measure when and how they fail. Failure quantification requires ten to one hundred specimens be tested for millions, even hundreds of millions of cycles. TA’s MSF16 instrument, equipped with industry leading motor technology and 16-sample capacity, significantly accelerates fatigue analysis and provides users a competitive edge by reducing their time-to-market.
Creoptix AG announced that it has received CHF 8 million in funding from Swisscanto (Lu) Equity Funds Management Company S.A., Waters Corporation, and other investors. Creoptix AG announced that it has received CHF 8 million led by new investor, Swisscanto (LU) Equity Fund - Sustainable Emerging Markets, a fund managed by Swisscanto (Lu) Equity Funds Management Company S.A. in a round of funding on September 30, 2019. The transaction also included participation from returning investor, Waters Corporation (NYSE:WAT) and other existing private investors. Robert Schier of Swisscanto (Lu) Equity Funds Management Company S.A. joined the board of directors of the company as a part of the transaction.
