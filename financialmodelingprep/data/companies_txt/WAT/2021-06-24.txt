Waters Corporation Appoints Wei Jiang to Board of Directors. MILFORD, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WATERS CORP. WILL APPOINT WEI JIANG, EVP AND PRESIDENT OF APAC AND CHINA AT BAYER PHARMACEUTICALS, TO THE WATERS BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
