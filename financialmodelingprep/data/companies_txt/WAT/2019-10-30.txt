TetraScience, Inc. announced that it has received $8 million in a round of funding on October 31, 2019. The transaction included participation from new investor Waters Corporation (NYSE:WAT), FLOODGATE Fund, LP., Underscore.VC, Founder Collective, returning investors First Round Capital and Y Combinator Management LLC.
