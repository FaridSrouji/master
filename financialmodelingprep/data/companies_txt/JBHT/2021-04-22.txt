J.B. Hunt Transport Services, Inc. Announces an Increase of Quarterly Dividend. LOWELL, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--J.B. HUNT TRANSPORT SERVICES, INC. (NASDAQ: JBHT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $ 0.30 (THIRTY CENTS) PER COMMON SHARE, A 7.1% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR QUARTER AND AN 11.1% INCREASE OVER THE PRIOR YEAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
