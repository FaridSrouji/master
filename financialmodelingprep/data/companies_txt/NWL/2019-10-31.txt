Newell Brands Inc. Reports Impairment of Goodwill, Intangibles and Other Assets for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019; Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019 and Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019. Newell Brands Inc. revised earnings guidance for the full year 2019 and provides earnings guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019. For the year, the company now expects to report net sales in the range of $9.6 billion to $9.7 billion, compared with previous guidance of net sales in the range of $9.1 billion to $9.3 billion. Core sales expected to be low single digit decline.For the fourth quarter of 2019, the company expects to report net sales in the range of $2.5 billion to $2.6 billion, and core sales 2% to 4% decline. For the third quarter ended September 30, 2019, the company reported impairment of goodwill, intangibles and other assets was $834.9 million compared to $8,133.7 million a year ago.
