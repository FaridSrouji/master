Rubbermaid® Commercial Products (RCP) Kicks Off Back to Clean With Donation Of $1.3 Million In Hand Sanitizer Product To Under-Resourced Schools Across The Nation. HUNTERSVILLE, N.C., MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, RUBBERMAID®COMMERCIAL PRODUCTS (RCP), PART OF NEWELL BRANDS' COMMERCIAL BUSINESS, ANNOUNCED THE DONATION OF $1.3 MILLION IN ITS HAND SANITIZER PRODUCT TO UNDER-RESOURCED SCHOOLS THROUGH A PARTNERSHIP WITH KIDS IN NEED FOUNDATION (KINF).
