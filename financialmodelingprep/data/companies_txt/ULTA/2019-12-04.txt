Ulta Beauty, Inc. Revises Earnings Guidance for the Fiscal Year 2019; to Open Approximately 80 New Stores. Ulta Beauty, Inc. revised earnings guidance for the fiscal year 2019. For the year, the company expected increase of total sales by approximately 10% (previously between 9% and 12%). The company expected to deliver diluted earnings per share in the range of $11.93 to $12.03 (previously $11.86 to $12.06).The company expected to open approximately 80 new stores, execute approximately 20 remodel or relocation projects, and complete approximately 270 store refreshes.
