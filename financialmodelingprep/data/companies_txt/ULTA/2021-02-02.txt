Ulta Beauty Announces Diversity and Inclusion Commitments. BOLINGBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ULTA BEAUTY, THE NATION'S LARGEST BEAUTY RETAILER, TODAY SHARED DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION COMMITMENTS WITH A KEEN FOCUS ON BRAND AMPLIFICATION AND SUPPORT, ASSORTMENT GROWTH, AND EQUITABLE, WELCOMING GUEST AND ASSOCIATE EXPERIENCES. THE 2021 COMMITMENTS REFLECT A PLANNED INVESTMENT OF MORE THAN $25 MILLION.
