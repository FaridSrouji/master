Ulta Beauty Announces Third-Quarter Earnings Per Share Of $2.25. ULTA BEAUTY ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER FISCAL 2019 RESULTS.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.25.Q3 SAME STORE SALES ROSE 3.2 PERCENT.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $2.13 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q3 SALES $1.683 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.7 BILLION.UPDATED ITS FISCAL 2019 OUTLOOK.SEES FY 2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE $11.93 TO $12.03.SEES FY 2019 SALES UP ABOUT 10 PERCENT.ULTA BEAUTY- SEES FY 2019 CAPITAL EXPENDITURES BETWEEN $305 MILLION AND $315 MILLION.EXPECTS TO OPEN ABOUT 80 NEW STORES IN FISCAL 2019.ULTA BEAUTY- SEES FY 2019 COMPARABLE SALES GROWTH IN THE RANGE OF 4.7% TO 5.0%.FY EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $11.92, REVENUE VIEW $7.41 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.MERCHANDISE INVENTORIES, NET AT END OF Q3 OF FISCAL 2019 TOTALED $1,616.9 MILLION.ULTA BEAUTY- DELIVERED QUARTER OF SOLID TOP-LINE PERFORMANCE, GROSS MARGIN EXPANSION,  EPS GROWTH, DESPITE CURRENT CHALLENGES FACING U.S. COSMETICS CATEGORY.
