ULTA BEAUTY ANNOUNCES FIRST QUARTER FISCAL 2020 RESULTS.Q1 LOSS PER SHARE $1.39.Q1 SALES $1.173 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.2 BILLION.Q1 SAME STORE SALES FELL 35.3 PERCENT.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.48 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY COMPARABLE SALES DECREASED 35.3%.TEMPORARY STORE CLOSURES IN RESPONSE TO COVID-19 SIGNIFICANTLY IMPACTED SALES AND EARNINGS IN QUARTER.HAVE BEGUN TO REOPEN STORES, AND TODAY MORE THAN 800 STORES OFFER CURBSIDE PICKUP AND MORE THAN 330 STORES ARE OPEN TO GUESTS.ENDED Q1 OF FISCAL 2020 WITH $1,153.5 MILLION IN CASH, CASH EQUIVALENTS AND SHORT-TERM INVESTMENTS.REDUCED ITS NEW STORE OPENING AND RELOCATION PLANS FOR FISCAL 2020.NOW EXPECTS TO OPEN BETWEEN 30 AND 40 NEW STORES AND EXECUTE APPROXIMATELY THREE RELOCATION PROJECTS IN FISCAL 2020.CURRENTLY ANTICIPATES CAPITAL EXPENDITURES WILL BE BETWEEN $200 AND $210 MILLION IN FISCAL 2020.
