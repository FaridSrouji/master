Cognizant Recognized for Its Growing Market Adoption as a Top Pharmacovigilance Operations Leader in New Everest Group Report. TEANECK, N.J., APRIL 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE LEADING COMPANIES IN THE PHARMACOVIGILANCE MARKET, OFFERING AN END-TO-END SPECTRUM OF PATIENT SAFETY SERVICES IN A NEW REPORT BY ANALYST FIRM EVEREST GROUP.
