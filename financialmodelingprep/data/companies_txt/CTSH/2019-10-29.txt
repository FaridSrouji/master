Cognizant Technology Solutions Corporation Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on November 29, 2019. Cognizant Technology Solutions Corporation declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.20 per share on Cognizant Class A common stock for shareholders of record at the close of business on November 19, 2019. This dividend will be payable on November 29, 2019.
