Cognizant Says Security Incident Involving Internal Systems Is Result Of A Maze Ransomware Attack. COGNIZANT - SECURITY INCIDENT INVOLVING INTERNAL SYSTEMS, CAUSING SERVICE DISRUPTIONS FOR SOME CLIENTS, IS RESULT OF A MAZE RANSOMWARE ATTACK.COGNIZANT - INTERNAL SECURITY TEAMS, SUPPLEMENTED BY LEADING CYBER DEFENSE FIRMS, ARE ACTIVELY TAKING STEPS TO CONTAIN INCIDENT.
