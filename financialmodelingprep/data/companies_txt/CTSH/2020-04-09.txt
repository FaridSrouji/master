Cognizant Technology Solutions Withdraws Its 2020 Guidance. SEES Q1 REVENUE $4.22 BILLION TO $4.23 BILLION.EXPECTS Q1 REVENUE WITHIN PREVIOUSLY GUIDED RANGE.FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE IN FIRST TWO MONTHS OF QUARTER WAS ON TRACK TO EXCEED PREVIOUS GUIDANCE.COGNIZANT TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS - DURING LATTER PART OF MARCH, COVID-19 INCREASINGLY AFFECTED COGNIZANT'S BUSINESS.LONG-TERM FUNDAMENTALS OF OUR BUSINESS REMAIN STRONG.WITHDRAWING ITS 2020 GUIDANCE THAT WAS PROVIDED ON FEBRUARY 5, 2020.DRAWING DOWN $1.74 BILLION ON ITS REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY ON MARCH 23.HAS NO SIGNIFICANT DEBT MATURITIES UNTIL 2023.SINCE MARCH 31(ST), COGNIZANT HAS NOT INITIATED ANY NEW SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAMS.COGNIZANT TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS - WILL PROVIDE THOSE AT ASSOCIATE LEVEL AND BELOW WITH AN ADDITIONAL PAYMENT OF 25% OF THEIR BASE PAY FOR MONTH OF APRIL.COGNIZANT - STANDARDIZED 14 DAYS SICK-LEAVE COVERAGE GLOBALLY FOR COVID-19 CASES/SELF-QUARANTINE WITHOUT IMPACTING OTHER SICK LEAVE/VACATION PROGRAMS.COGNIZANT - Q1 REVENUE IS EXPECTED TO BE $4.22-$4.23 BILLION, UP 2.7-2.9% FROM PRIOR-YEAR QUARTER.
