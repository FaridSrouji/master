Cognizant to Expand Expertise in Autonomous, Connected and Electric Vehicles with Acquisition of ESG Mobility. TEANECK, N.J., MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COGNIZANT (NASDAQ: CTSH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS ACQUIRING MUNICH, GERMANY-BASED ESG MOBILITY, A DIGITAL AUTOMOTIVE ENGINEERING R&D PROVIDER FOR CONNECTED, AUTONOMOUS AND ELECTRIC VEHICLES.
