Ross Stores, Inc. Declares Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 31, 2019. Ross Stores, Inc. announced that the Company’s Board of Directors declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.255 per common share, payable on December 31, 2019 to stockholders of record as of December 6, 2019.
