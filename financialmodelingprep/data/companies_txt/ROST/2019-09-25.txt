dd's DISCOUNTS to Open a New Store in Tucson, Arizona. dd's DISCOUNTS will open a new store in Tucson on September 28, 2019. The 20,000 square foot store is located at Midvale Plaza at the northwest corner of West Valencia Road and South Midvale Park Road. With this new opening, dd's DISCOUNTS will operate 15 stores in Arizona and a total of 260 locations in 19 states. Together, Ross Dress for Less and dd's DISCOUNTSZ currently operate over 1,750 off-price apparel and home fashion stores in 39 states, the District of Columbia, and Guam.
