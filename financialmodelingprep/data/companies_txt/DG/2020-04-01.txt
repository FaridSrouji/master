Dollar General Corporation Announces Pricing Of $1.5 Bln Of Senior Notes. DOLLAR GENERAL CORPORATION ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $1.5 BILLION OF SENIOR NOTES.DOLLAR GENERAL - ANNOUNCED PRICING OF OFFERING OF $1.0 BILLION OF 3.500% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2030 AND $500.0 MILLION OF 4.125% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2050.DOLLAR GENERAL - 2030 NOTES WILL PAY INTEREST AT A RATE OF 3.500% PER ANNUM AND MATURE ON APRIL 3, 2030.DOLLAR GENERAL CORP - 2050 NOTES WILL PAY INTEREST AT A RATE OF 4.125% PER ANNUM AND MATURE ON APRIL 3, 2050.
