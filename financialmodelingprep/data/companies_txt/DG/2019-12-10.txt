Dollar General Announces Availability Of CBD Products In Select States. DOLLAR GENERAL ANNOUNCES AVAILABILITY OF CBD PRODUCTS IN SELECT STATES.DOLLAR GENERAL - TO EXPAND CBD PRODUCTS' AVAILABILITY TO 7 MORE STATES BY SPRING 2020.DOLLAR GENERAL CORP - ANNOUNCED AVAILABILITY OF ABOUT 20 CBD PRODUCT SKUS IN APPROXIMATELY 1,100 STORES IN TENNESSEE AND KENTUCKY.DOLLAR GENERAL - CO'S CBD ASSORTMENT IS LIMITED TO TOPICAL COSMETIC PRODUCTS INCLUDING CREAMS, OINTMENTS, BATH BOMBS, BATH SALTS & FACE MASKS.DOLLAR GENERAL - CO DOES NOT HAVE CURRENT PLANS TO PROVIDE INGESTIBLE OR EDIBLE CBD MERCHANDISE.
