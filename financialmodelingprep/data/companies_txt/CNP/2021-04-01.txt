CenterPoint Energy named to Newsweek's Most Responsible Companies List for 2021. HOUSTON, APRIL 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTERPOINT ENERGY HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF THE MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES IN THE UNITED STATES BY NEWSWEEK. FOR THE SECOND TIME, NEWSWEEK RECOGNIZED AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES BASED ON PUBLICLY AVAILABLE KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS DERIVED FROM CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY, SUSTAINABILITY AND CORPORATE CITIZENSHIP REPORTS, AS WELL AS AN INDEPENDENT SURVEY.
