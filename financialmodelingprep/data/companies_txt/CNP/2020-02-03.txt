Centerpoint Energy To Sell Miller Pipeline And Minnesota Ltd To Powerteam For $850 Mln. PRESS RELEASE - CENTERPOINT ENERGY TO SELL MILLER PIPELINE AND MINNESOTA LIMITED TO POWERTEAM SERVICES.CENTERPOINT ENERGY INC -  WILL SELL MILLER PIPELINE AND MINNESOTA LIMITED (COLLECTIVELY, "MVERGE") TO POWERTEAM FOR $850 MILLION.CENTERPOINT ENERGY INC - NET PROCEEDS OF SALE WILL BE USED TO REPAY A PORTION OF OUTSTANDING CENTERPOINT ENERGY DEBT.CENTERPOINT ENERGY INC - DOUG BANNING, CURRENT CEO OF MVERGE WILL CONTINUE TO OVERSEE MILLER PIPELINE AND MINNESOTA LIMITED.CENTERPOINT ENERGY INC -  POST TRANSACTION, EXPECT UTILITY EARNINGS CONTRIBUTION TO INCREASE TO GREATER THAN 80% OVER NEXT FEW YEARS.
