Sherwin-Williams Issues 2020 Sustainability Report. CLEVELAND, JUNE 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPANY (NYSE: SHW) HAS ISSUED ITS 2020 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT: BUILDING IN THE GOOD, REFLECTING PROGRESS MADE IN ITS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) JOURNEY, AS WELL AS INTRODUCING ITS NEW SUSTAINABILITY GOALS AND ASPIRATIONS. THE REPORT IS AVAILABLE AT HTTPS://SUSTAINABILITY.SHERWIN-WILLIAMS.COM/DOC/SUSTAINABILITY_REPORT.
