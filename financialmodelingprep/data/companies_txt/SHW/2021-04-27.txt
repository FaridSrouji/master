The Sherwin-Williams Company Reports 2021 First Quarter Financial Results. CLEVELAND, APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SHERWIN-WILLIAMS COMPANY (NYSE: SHW) ANNOUNCED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021. ALL COMPARISONS ARE TO THE FIRST QUARTER OF THE PRIOR YEAR, UNLESS OTHERWISE NOTED.
