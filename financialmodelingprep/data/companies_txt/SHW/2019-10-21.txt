The Sherwin-Williams Company Provides Consolidated Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year Ending December 31, 2019. The Sherwin-Williams Company provided consolidated earnings guidance for the fourth quarter and full year ending December 31, 2019. For the fourth quarter, the company anticipates consolidated net sales will increase by a low single digit percentage compared to last year's fourth quarter.For the full year 2019, the company expects consolidated net sales will increase by a low single digit percentage compared to the full year 2018. The company is updating full year 2019 diluted net income per share guidance to be in the range of $17.07 to $17.47 per share.
