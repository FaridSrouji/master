Aptiv PLC Provides Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019; Reports Asset Impairments for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2019. Aptiv PLC provided earnings guidance for the full year 2019. For the period, the company expects net sales in the range of $14,255 million to $14,355 million. The company also reported asset impairments for the three months ended September 30, 2019. For the period, the company reported as asset impairments were $1 million against $1 million a year ago.
