The Home Depot Announces First Quarter Results. ATLANTA, MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HOME DEPOT®, THE WORLD'S LARGEST HOME IMPROVEMENT RETAILER, TODAY REPORTED SALES OF $37.5 BILLION FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF FISCAL 2021, AN INCREASE OF $9.2 BILLION, OR 32.7 PERCENT FROM THE FIRST QUARTER OF FISCAL 2020. COMPARABLE SALES FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF FISCAL 2021 INCREASED 31.0 PERCENT, AND COMPARABLE SALES IN THE U.S. INCREASED 29.9 PERCENT.
