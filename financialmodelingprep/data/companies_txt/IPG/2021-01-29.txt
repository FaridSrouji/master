Brian McMahon Joins Interpublic Corporate as a Senior Business Advisor. NEW YORK, JAN. 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --   INTERPUBLIC GROUP (NYSE: IPG) ANNOUNCED BRIAN MCMAHON, A 23-YEAR IPG VETERAN AND FOUNDING CEO OF ORION, IS JOINING IPG CORPORATE AS A SENIOR BUSINESS ADVISOR. IN THIS NEW POSITION, BRIAN WILL WORK ACROSS THE IPG PORTFOLIO TO FOSTER INNOVATIVE THINKING, WITH A FOCUS ON DELIVERING HIGHER VALUE SERVICES TO OUR CLIENTS. BRIAN WILL ALSO BE AN ADVISOR TO COMPANIES PARTICIPATING IN OUR VARIOUS ACCELERATOR AND VENTURE PROGRAMS.
