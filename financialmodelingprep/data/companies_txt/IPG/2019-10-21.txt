The Interpublic Group of Companies, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019. The Interpublic Group of Companies, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the year 2019. The company confirms the company's 2019 full year financial targets of organic growth at the high-end of the 2.0% to 3.0% range and adjusted EBITA margin expansion of 40 to 50 basis points.
