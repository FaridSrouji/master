Trish Taylor Joins Weber Shandwick to Lead Washington, D.C. Healthcare Practice. WASHINGTON, APRIL 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WEBER SHANDWICK, ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING GLOBAL COMMUNICATIONS AND MARKETING SOLUTIONS FIRMS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF TRISH TAYLOR AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND LEAD OF THE AGENCY'S HEALTHCARE PRACTICE IN WASHINGTON, D.C. MOST RECENTLY, TAYLOR, WHO HOLDS A DOCTORATE IN SOCIAL AND HEALTH PSYCHOLOGY, SERVED AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF HAGER SHARP'S HEALTH PRACTICE.
