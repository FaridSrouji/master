IPG Mediabrands' MAGNA Unveils Day One of Its First-Ever Equity Upfront™. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IPG MEDIABRANDS AND MAGNA TODAY LAUNCHED DAY ONE OF A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND EQUITY UPFRONT™, AN ANNUAL WEEKLONG EVENT DURING THE WEEK OF MARCH 15TH, TO RAISE VISIBILITY FOR BLACK-OWNED AND BLACK-TARGETED MEDIA BUSINESSES. DAY ONE OF THE EVENT FEATURES UPFRONT PRESENTATIONS BY BLACK-OWNED MEDIA PARTNERS INCLUDING ENTERTAINMENT STUDIOS' THE GRIO, ESSENCE COMMUNICATIONS, INC., REVOLT AND URBAN ONE, AS WELL AS BLACK-TARGETED NETWORKS BET AND OWN.
