McCann Worldgroup Launches Sustainability Department. NEW YORK, JULY 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCCANN WORLDGROUP HAS LAUNCHED A SUSTAINABILITY DEPARTMENT TO COMMIT RESOURCES TO, AND DRIVE FORWARD, THE NETWORK'S SUSTAINABILITY AGENDA. THE AGENCY ANNOUNCED THAT JACLYN KAMINSKI, A DIRECTOR OF BUSINESS LEADERSHIP AT MCCANN LONDON, HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE NEWLY-CREATED ROLE OF GLOBAL DIRECTOR OF SUSTAINABILITY, AND WILL LEAD THE DEPARTMENT.
