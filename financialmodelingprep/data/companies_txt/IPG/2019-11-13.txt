Michael Kapfer and Dieter Pivrnec acquired 80% stake in Lowe GGK Werbeagentur GmbH from The Interpublic Group of Companies, Inc. (NYSE:IPG).. Michael Kapfer and Dieter Pivrnec acquired 80% stake in Lowe GGK Werbeagentur GmbH from The Interpublic Group of Companies, Inc. (NYSE:IPG) on November 14, 2019. Under the terms of the transaction, Michael Kapfer and Dieter Pivrnec will acquire 40% stake each and the remaining The Interpublic Group of Companies will retain the remaining 20%. Upon completion of the transaction, the three company brands will be preserved. GGK Mullenlowe reported a revenue of €6.4 million in 2018.
