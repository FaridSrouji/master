Linda Sanford Joins Interpublic Board of Directors. Interpublic Group announced the appointment of Linda Sanford as an independent director of IPG, effective immediately. Ms. Sanford's appointment expands IPG's board to 12 directors, 11 of whom are independent. It also increases the number of women on the board to four, or one third of the total directors.
