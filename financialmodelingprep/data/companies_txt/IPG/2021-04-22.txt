R/GA Appoints Tania Secor as Global Chief Financial Officer. NEW YORK, APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- R/GA HAS NAMED TANIA SECOR AS ITS GLOBAL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER. THE FORMER CFO AT REPRISE DIGITAL WILL START ON MAY 3 AND REPORT TO GLOBAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER SEAN LYONS.
