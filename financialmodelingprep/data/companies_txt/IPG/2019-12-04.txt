The Interpublic Group of Companies Continues to Expand with Empire State Realty Trust. Empire State Realty Trust, Inc. announced that The Interpublic Group of Companies, Inc. signed a 15,086 square foot lease on the 30th floor of 1400 Broadway. This expansion will increase the footprint of The Interpublic Group of Companies within ESRT's portfolio to 127,781 sq. ft. located at 1400 Broadway and 111 W. 33rd St.
