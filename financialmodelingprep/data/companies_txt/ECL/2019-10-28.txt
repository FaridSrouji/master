Ecolab Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Ecolab Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 3,817.9 million compared to USD 3,747.2 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 587.6 million compared to USD 516.2 million a year ago. Net income was USD 464.2 million compared to USD 435.4 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.61 compared to USD 1.51 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.59 compared to USD 1.48 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 11,082.7 million compared to USD 10,907.7 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,453.4 million compared to USD 1,365.1 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,129.3 million compared to USD 1,034 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.92 compared to USD 3.58 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.86 compared to USD 3.53 a year ago.
