Ecolab Schedules Webcast and Conference Call on July 27. ST. PAUL, MINN., JUNE 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ECOLAB WILL HOST A LIVE WEBCAST OF ITS SECOND QUARTER EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL TO DISCUSS 2021 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS. A NEWS RELEASE CONTAINING SECOND QUARTER RESULTS IS EXPECTED TO BE ISSUED BEFORE MARKET OPEN ON JULY 27, 2021.
