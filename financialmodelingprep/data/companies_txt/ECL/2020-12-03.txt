Ecolab Increases Cash Dividend 2%. ST. PAUL, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ECOLAB INC. DECLARED A 2% INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.48 PER COMMON SHARE, TO BE PAID JANUARY 15, 2020, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 15, 2020. THIS INCREASE RESULTS IN A NEW INDICATED ANNUAL CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.92 PER SHARE IN 2021 AND REPRESENTS ECOLAB'S 29TH CONSECUTIVE ANNUAL DIVIDEND RATE INCREASE.
