Beckman Coulter Offers $4/High-throughput Antigen Test to Support U.S. Government Initiative to Expand COVID-19 Testing. BREA, CALIF., MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BECKMAN COULTER, A CLINICAL DIAGNOSTICS LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THEIR COMMITMENT TO SUPPORT THE U.S. GOVERNMENT'S PLAN TO INVEST MORE THAN $12 BILLION TO EXPAND COVID-19 TESTING WITH THEIR HIGH-THROUGHPUT, HIGH-QUALITY LAB ANTIGEN TEST, ACCESS SARS-COV-2 AG,* IDEAL FOR MASS SCREENING TESTING.
