Danaher Announces Pricing Of Common Stock, Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock Offering. DANAHER ANNOUNCES PRICING OF COMMON STOCK OFFERING AND MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED STOCK OFFERING.PRICED CONCURRENT OFFERINGS OF 9.51 MILLION SHARES OF COMMON STOCK AT A PRICE TO PUBLIC OF $163.00 PER SHARE.PRICED 1.6 MILLION SHARES OF 5.00% SERIES B MANDATORY CONVERTIBLE PREFERRED STOCK AT A PRICE TO PUBLIC OF $1,000 PER SHARE.
