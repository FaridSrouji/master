Danaher Corp. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 27, 2019. Danaher Corp. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 27, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 5,037.3 million compared to USD 4,853.1 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 834.8 million compared to USD 830.7 million a year ago. Net income was USD 668 million compared to USD 663.7 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.9 compared to USD 0.95 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.89 compared to USD 0.93 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 15,073.8 million compared to USD 14,529.5 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,442.4 million compared to USD 2,441.2 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,733.1 million compared to USD 1,904.1 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.36 compared to USD 2.72 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.32 compared to USD 2.68 a year ago.
