Danaher To Acquire Aldevron. WASHINGTON, JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DANAHER CORPORATION (NYSE: DHR) (THE "COMPANY") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DANAHER HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE PRIVATELY-HELD ALDEVRON, FOR A CASH PURCHASE PRICE OF APPROXIMATELY $9.6 BILLION. DANAHER EXPECTS TO FINANCE THE ACQUISITION USING CASH ON HAND AND/OR PROCEEDS FROM THE ISSUANCE OF COMMERCIAL PAPER.
