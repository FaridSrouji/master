Danaher Says Sent Notice To Executive Officers, Directors Regarding Certain Changes Will Occur To Danaher Stock Fund Held In Each Of Co's Plans. DANAHER - SENT NOTICE TO EXECUTIVE OFFICERS, DIRECTORS INFORMING THEM THAT CERTAIN CHANGES WILL OCCUR TO DANAHER STOCK FUND HELD IN EACH OF CO'S PLANS.DANAHER SAYS SENT NOTICE TO EXECUTIVE OFFICERS, DIRECTORS IN CONNECTION WITH CO'S OFFER TO EXCHANGE UP TO 127.9 MILLION SHARES OF ENVISTA HOLDINGS - SEC FILING.DANAHER SAYS AS A RESULT OF CHANGES TO DANAHER STOCK FUND, PARTICIPANTS IN EACH OF CO'S PLANS MAY BE SUBJECT TO BLACKOUT PERIOD.
