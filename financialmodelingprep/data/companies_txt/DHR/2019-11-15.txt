Danaher To Begin Exchange Offer For Divestiture Of Remaining Stake In Envista Through Split-Off. DANAHER WILL COMMENCE EXCHANGE OFFER RELATED TO DIVESTITURE OF ITS REMAINING INTEREST IN ENVISTA THROUGH A SPLIT-OFF.DANAHER - RECEIVED WAIVER OF 180-DAY LOCK-UP WITH RESPECT TO SHARES OF ENVISTA STOCK HELD BY IT FROM J.P. MORGAN SECURITIES.DANAHER CORP - EXCHANGE OFFER IS VOLUNTARY FOR DANAHER STOCKHOLDERS.DANAHER - EXCHANGE OFFER WILL EXPIRE ON DEC 13, 2019.
