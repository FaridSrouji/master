Iron Mountain to Expand EMEA Data Center Footprint with Agreement to Acquire Frankfurt Data Center. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IRON MOUNTAIN INCORPORATED (NYSE: IRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN INNOVATIVE STORAGE AND INFORMATION MANAGEMENT SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE A DATA CENTER IN FRANKFURT FROM CALCIUM DC PTE. LTD.
