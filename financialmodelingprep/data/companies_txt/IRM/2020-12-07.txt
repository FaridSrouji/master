Iron Mountain Executes Six Megawatt Expansion With Fortune 100 Customer at AZP-2 Data Center in Phoenix. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IRON MOUNTAIN INCORPORATED (NYSE: IRM), THE STORAGE AND INFORMATION MANAGEMENT SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SIGNED A SIX MEGAWATT DATA CENTER PRE-LEASE EXPANSION WITH A U.S. BASED FORTUNE 100 CUSTOMER IN PHOENIX, ARIZONA, AT ITS AZP-2 DATA CENTER. IRON MOUNTAIN ANNOUNCED THE INITIAL SIX MEGAWATT DEAL WITH THIS CUSTOMER IN SEPTEMBER 2020.
