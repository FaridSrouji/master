Iron Mountain to Divest Intellectual Property Management Business. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IRON MOUNTAIN INCORPORATED (NYSE: IRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN INNOVATIVE STORAGE AND INFORMATION MANAGEMENT SERVICES, TODAY SIGNED AN AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY MANAGEMENT (“IPM”) BUSINESS TO NCC GROUP (“NCC”), A PUBLICLY LISTED SOFTWARE ESCROW AND CYBER SECURITY COMPANY, LOCATED IN THE UK.
