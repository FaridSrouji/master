CDW Corporation Announces Upsizing And Pricing Of Registered Offering Of $600 Mln Of Senior Notes Due 2025. CDW CORPORATION ANNOUNCES UPSIZING AND PRICING OF REGISTERED OFFERING OF $600 MILLION OF SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025.CDW CORP - UNITS HAVE PRICED AN OFFERING OF $600 MILLION OF 4.125% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025.CDW CORP - NOTES WERE PRICED AT 100% OF PAR.CDW CORP - SALE OF NOTES IS EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED ON APRIL 21, 2020.
CDW Sees Q1 Earnings Per Share Of $1.16. SEES Q1 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.38.SEES Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.16.SEES Q1 SALES $4.39 BILLION.WITHDRAWING ITS 2020 TARGETS AND RELATED 2020 FINANCIAL INFORMATION.HAS TAKEN MEASURES TO ENHANCE LIQUIDITY, INCLUDING IMPLEMENTING COST SAVINGS INITIATIVES AND SUSPENDING SHARE REPURCHASES.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.29, REVENUE VIEW $4.15 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
