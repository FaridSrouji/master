Garmin® shareholders approve quarterly dividend through March 2022 and Garmin announces record date and payment date for June 2021 dividend installment. SCHAFFHAUSEN, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AT GARMIN LTD.'S (NASDAQ:GRMN) ANNUAL SHAREHOLDERS' MEETING HELD TODAY, APPROVAL WAS RECEIVED FROM THE SHAREHOLDERS IN ACCORDANCE WITH SWISS CORPORATE LAW FOR A CASH DIVIDEND IN THE AMOUNT OF $2.68 PER SHARE (SUBJECT TO ADJUSTMENT IN THE EVENT THAT THE SWISS FRANC WEAKENS MORE THAN 35% RELATIVE TO THE USD), PAYABLE IN FOUR EQUAL INSTALLMENTS. THE BOARD HAS DETERMINED THAT THE JUNE INSTALLMENT OF THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID AS INDICATED BELOW AND CURRENT
