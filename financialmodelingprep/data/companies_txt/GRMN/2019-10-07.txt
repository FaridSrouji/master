Garmin Launches its Latest Wearable in Malaysia --the Garmin Venu. Garmin has launched its latest wearable in Malaysia --the Garmin Venu --with a price tag of MYR 1,799. Garmins authorised local distributor Aeco Technologies (M) Sdn Bhd marketing executiveDickson Cheah said the device is a smartwatch equipped with the global positioning system (GPS) and afitness tracker that would not compromise the wearers fashion considering itsinterchangeable bands. This product has the best of both worlds --sports and fashion.
