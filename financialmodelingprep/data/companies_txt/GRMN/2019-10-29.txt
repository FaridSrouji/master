Garmin Ltd. Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year of 2019. Garmin Ltd. revised earnings guidance for the full year of 2019. For the period, the company expects revenue of $3.65 billion and EPS of $4.15 against prior guidance revenue of $3.6 billion and EPS of $3.90.
