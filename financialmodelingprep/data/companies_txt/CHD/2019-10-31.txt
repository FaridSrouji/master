Robert D. Leblanc Announces Retirement from the Board of Church & Dwight Co., Inc. On October 30, 2019, Robert D. LeBlanc notified the Board of Directors of Church & Dwight Co., Inc. that he intends to retire from the Board and will, therefore, not stand for reelection as a member of the Board at the Company’s Annual Meeting of Stockholders to be held on April 30, 2020. Mr. LeBlanc will continue to serve on the Board and as Lead Director and as a member of the Audit Committee and the Executive Committee until the end of his term, which will expire at the end of the 2020 Annual Meeting or when his successor has been elected and qualified. The Company gratefully acknowledges Mr. LeBlanc’s more than two decades of dedicated service on its Board. Mr. LeBlanc’s decision not to seek reelection was not the result of any disagreement with the Company.
