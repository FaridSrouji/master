Church & Dwight Co., Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 2, 2019. Church & Dwight Co., Inc. reported that its Board of Directors declared a regular quarterly dividend of twenty two and three quarters ($0.2275) cents per share. This quarterly dividend will be payable December 2, 2019 to stockholders of record at the close of business on November 15, 2019.
