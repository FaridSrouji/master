Deere & Co Renews Revolving Credit Facilities In Aggregate Amount Of $8 Bln. DEERE & CO -  ON MARCH 30, 2020 RENEWED ITS REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITIES IN AGGREGATE AMOUNT OF $8 BILLION.DEERE & CO -  CREDIT FACILITIES COMPRISED OF A 364-DAY CREDIT FACILITY OF $3,000 MILLION DUE MARCH 29, 2021; A $2,500 MILLION CREDIT FACILITY DUE MARCH 2024.DEERE & CO -  CREDIT FACILITIES ALSO COMPRISED OF A $2,500 MILLION CREDIT FACILITY EXPIRING IN MARCH 2025.DEERE & CO - CREDIT FACILITIES ARE COMPRISED OF THREE TRANCHES.
