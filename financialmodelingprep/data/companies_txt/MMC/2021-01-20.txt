Marsh & McLennan Companies Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MARSH & MCLENNAN COMPANIES (NYSE: MMC) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.465 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 12, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 1, 2021. ABOUT MARSH & MCLENNAN COMPANIES MARSH & MCLENNAN (NYSE: MMC) IS THE WORLD'S LEADING PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM IN THE AREAS OF RISK, STRATEGY AND PEOPLE.
