Marsh & Mclennan Companies, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Marsh & McLennan Companies, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 3,968 million compared to USD 3,504 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 467 million compared to USD 541 million a year ago. Net income was USD 303 million compared to USD 276 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.6 compared to USD 0.55 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.59 compared to USD 0.54 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 12,388 million compared to USD 11,238 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,085 million compared to USD 2,140 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,351 million compared to USD 1,497 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.67 compared to USD 2.96 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.64 compared to USD 2.93 a year ago.
