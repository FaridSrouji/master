Marsh McLennan Names Nick Studer President & CEO of Oliver Wyman Group and Vice Chair of Marsh McLennan. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MARSH MCLENNAN (NYSE: MMC), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROFESSIONAL SERVICES FIRM IN THE AREAS OF RISK, STRATEGY AND PEOPLE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT NICK STUDER HAS BEEN APPOINTED PRESIDENT AND CEO OF OLIVER WYMAN GROUP EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2021. MR. STUDER WILL ALSO BECOME A VICE CHAIR OF MARSH MCLENNAN AND JOIN THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE. IN THIS ROLE, MR.
