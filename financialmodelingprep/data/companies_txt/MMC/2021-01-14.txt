FIRST, THE COMPANY HAS PLEDGED TO BE CARBON NEUTRAL IN 2021 THROUGH THE REDUCTION OF GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS IN ITS OWN OPERATIONS AND THE PURCHASE OF VERIFIABLE OFFSETS.
