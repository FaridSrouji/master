Facebook, Inc. Announces Board Changes. Facebook, Inc. announced that Dr. Susan Desmond-Hellmann, the CEO of the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation, is stepping down from the company's board of directors, effective October 30, 2019. The Board has appointed Marc L. Andreessen to replace Dr. Desmond-Hellmann as a member of the Compensation & Governance Committee of the Board (the "Compensation & Governance Committee"), effective as of October 30, 2019.
