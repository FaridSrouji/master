Facebook Says To Block New Political & Issue Ads During Final Week Of Campaign For Nov U.S. Election. FACEBOOK SAYS IT WILL APPLY A NEW LABEL TO ANY POST FROM A CANDIDATE OR CAMPAIGN TRYING TO DECLARE VICTORY BEFORE U.S. ELECTION RESULTS ARE OFFICIAL.FACEBOOK SAYS IT WILL LABEL POSTS THAT SEEK TO  DELEGITIMIZE U.S. ELECTION OUTCOME.FACEBOOK INC SAYS TO BLOCK NEW POLITICAL AND ISSUE ADS DURING THE FINAL WEEK OF THE CAMPAIGN FOR NOVEMBER U.S. ELECTION.FACEBOOK SAYS PARTNERING WITH REUTERS AND NATIONAL ELECTION POOL TO PROVIDE AUTHORITATIVE INFORMATION ABOUT U.S. ELECTION RESULTS.FACEBOOK SAYS WILL REMOVE POSTS CONTAINING CLEAR MISINFORMATION ABOUT COVID-19 AND VOTING.FACEBOOK - EXPANDING VOTER SUPPRESSION POLICY; TO REMOVE IMPLICIT MISREPRESENTATIONS ABOUT HOW OR WHEN TO VOTE.FACEBOOK SAYS TO LIMIT THE NUMBER OF CHATS USERS CAN FORWARD A MESSAGE TO AT ONE TIME ON MESSENGER.FACEBOOK SAYS EXPANDING DEFINITION OF HIGH-RISK PEOPLE TO INCLUDE U.S. ELECTION OFFICIALS.FACEBOOK SAYS NOT PLANNING TO MAKE FURTHER CHANGES TO CO'S ELECTION-RELATED POLICIES BETWEEN NOW AND OFFICIAL DECLARATION OF U.S. ELECTION RESULT.
