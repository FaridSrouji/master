Novi Financial Introduces Novi For Digital Wallet To Send & Hold Libra Digital Currencies. NOVI FINANCIAL - INTRODUCES NOVI, NEW NAME AND BRAND FOR DIGITAL WALLET THAT WILL HELP PEOPLE SEND & HOLD LIBRA DIGITAL CURRENCIES.NOVI FINANCIAL - USERS WILL BE ABLE TO USE NOVI AS A STAND-ALONE APP, AS WELL AS IN MESSENGER AND WHATSAPP.NOVI FINANCIAL - ALL NOVI CUSTOMERS WILL BE VERIFIED USING GOVERNMENT-ISSUED ID, FRAUD PROTECTIONS WILL BE BUILT IN THROUGHOUT APP.
