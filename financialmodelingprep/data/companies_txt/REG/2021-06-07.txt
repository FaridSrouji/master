Regency Centers Issues Annual Corporate Responsibility Report. JACKSONVILLE, FLA., JUNE 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TODAY, REGENCY CENTERS CORPORATION (“REGENCY” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: REG) ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS ANNUAL CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY REPORT. THE REPORT ILLUSTRATES REGENCY'S CONTINUED COMMITMENT TO CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY, AS WELL AS KEY ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE INITIATIVES AND ACHIEVEMENTS. THE REPORT CAN BE FOUND ON REGENCY'S CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY WEBSITE.
