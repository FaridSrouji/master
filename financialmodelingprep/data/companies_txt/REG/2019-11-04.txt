Regency Centers Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Regency Centers Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 282.276 million compared to USD 278.310 million a year ago. Net income was USD 56.965 million compared to USD 69.722 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.34 compared to USD 0.41 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 844.406 million compared to USD 836.415 million a year ago. Net income was USD 199.139 million compared to USD 170.222 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.19 compared to USD 1 a year ago.
