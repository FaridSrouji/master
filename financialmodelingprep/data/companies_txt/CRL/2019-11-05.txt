Charles River Laboratories International, Inc. Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year of 2019. Charles River Laboratories International, Inc. revised earnings guidance for the full year of 2019. For the period, the company expects revenue growth to be in the range of 15.0% to 15.5% compared to prior guidance of 16% to 17%. Revenue growth, organic expected to be in the range of 8.25% to 8.75% compared to prior guidance of 8.5% to 9.5%. GAAP EPS expected to be in the range of $4.65 to $4.75 compared to prior guidance of $4.65 to $4.80.
