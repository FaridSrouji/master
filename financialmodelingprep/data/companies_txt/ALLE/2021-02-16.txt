Allegion Reports Fourth-Quarter, Full-Year 2020 Financial Results, Provides 2021 Outlook. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF SECURITY PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS, TODAY REPORTED FOURTH-QUARTER 2020 NET REVENUES OF $727.3 MILLION AND NET EARNINGS OF $93.3 MILLION, OR $1.01 PER SHARE. EXCLUDING CHARGES RELATED TO RESTRUCTURING, IMPAIRMENTS, LOSS ON ASSETS HELD FOR SALE AND ACQUISITION COSTS, ADJUSTED NET EARNINGS WERE $137.1 MILLION, OR $1.49 PER SHARE, UP 16.4 PERCENT WHEN COMPARED WITH FOURTH-QUARTER 2019 ADJUSTED EPS OF $1.28.
