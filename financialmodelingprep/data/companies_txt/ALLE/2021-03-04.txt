Allegion Closes on Sale of QMI. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE), THROUGH ONE OF ITS SUBSIDIARIES, HAS CLOSED ON THE SALE OF ITS QATAR METAL INDUSTRIES (QMI) BUSINESS TO THE HLD GROUP OF COMPANIES, A PRIVATE INDUSTRIAL PORTFOLIO OF COMPANIES BASED IN THE UK. TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION, WHICH CLOSED ON FEB. 28, 2021, ARE NOT DISCLOSED. THE DIVESTITURE OF QMI – A COMMERCIAL DOOR MANUFACTURER IN THE MIDDLE EAST – WAS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED AS A PART OF ALLEGION'S 2020 FOURTH-QUARTER, FULL-YEAR EARNINGS CALL AND REL
