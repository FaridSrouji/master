Allegion plc Provides Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. Allegion plc provided earnings guidance for the full year 2019. The company is affirming its full-year 2019 revenue outlook for total and organic growth to a range of 4.5% to 5.5% compared to 2018. The company is tightening its full-year 2019 outlook for reported EPS to a range of $4.55 to $4.65.
