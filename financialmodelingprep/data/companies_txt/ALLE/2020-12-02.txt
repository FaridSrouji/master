Allegion Declares Quarterly Dividend. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE), A LEADING GLOBAL SECURITY PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.32 PER ORDINARY SHARE OF THE COMPANY. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DEC. 30, 2020, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON DEC. 16, 2020.
