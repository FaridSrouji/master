Allegion Reports First-Quarter 2021 Financial Results, Raises Full-Year 2021 Outlook. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALLEGION PLC (NYSE: ALLE), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF SECURITY PRODUCTS AND SOLUTIONS, TODAY REPORTED FIRST-QUARTER 2021 NET REVENUES OF $694.3 MILLION AND NET EARNINGS OF $108 MILLION, OR $1.18 PER SHARE. EXCLUDING CHARGES RELATED TO RESTRUCTURING, ADJUSTED NET EARNINGS WERE $109.6 MILLION, OR $1.20 PER SHARE, UP 15.4 PERCENT WHEN COMPARED WITH FIRST-QUARTER 2020 ADJUSTED EPS OF $1.04.
