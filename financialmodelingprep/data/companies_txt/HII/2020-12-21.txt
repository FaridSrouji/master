Huntington Ingalls Industries Delivers REMUS 100 UUVs to the German Navy. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., DEC. 21, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT DELIVERED NEW REMUS 100 UNMANNED UNDERWATER VEHICLES (UUVS) TO THE GERMAN NAVY. THE VEHICLES WILL BE USED TO EXPAND THE GERMAN NAVY'S CURRENT FLEET OF REMUS 100 UUVS USED FOR MINE COUNTERMEASURE (MCM) OPERATIONS.
