Huntington Ingalls Industries Declares Quarterly Dividend. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., APRIL 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.14 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JUNE 11, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 28, 2021.
