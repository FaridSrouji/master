Huntington Ingalls Industries Increases Quarterly Dividend, Payable on Dec. 13, 2019. Huntington Ingalls Industries announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $1.03 per share, a $0.17 increase over the $0.86 per share dividend paid in each of the prior four quarters. The $1.03 per share dividend will be payable on Dec. 13, 2019, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on Nov. 29, 2019.
