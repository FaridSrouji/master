Photo Release — Huntington Ingalls Industries Announces James Loeblein as Corporate Vice President of Customer Affairs. WASHINGTON, JAN. 26, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JAMES “JIM” LOEBLEIN HAS BEEN NAMED CORPORATE VICE PRESIDENT OF CUSTOMER AFFAIRS. LOEBLEIN WILL RELIEVE DAN HOLLOWAY UPON HIS RETIREMENT LATER IN 2021 AND BE RESPONSIBLE FOR OUTREACH TO EXECUTIVE BRANCH PRINCIPALS AND THE FLEET FOR THE INGALLS PORTFOLIO. HE WILL BE LOCATED IN HII'S WASHINGTON, D.C., OFFICE AND WILL REPORT TO MITCH WALDMAN, HII'S EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, GOVERNMENT AND CUSTOMER RELATIONS.
