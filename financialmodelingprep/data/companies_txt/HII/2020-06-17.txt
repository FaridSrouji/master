Huntington Ingalls Awarded $145 Mln Advance Procurement Contract For Amphibious Assault Ship Lha 9. HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES AWARDED $145 MILLION ADVANCE PROCUREMENT CONTRACT FOR AMPHIBIOUS ASSAULT SHIP LHA 9.HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES INC - CO'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION HAS RECEIVED A THIRD CONTRACT MODIFICATION FROM U.S. NAVY FOR $145 MILLION.HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES INC - MODIFICATION BRINGS TOTAL ADVANCE FUNDING FOR LHA 9 TO $350 MILLION.
