PASCAGOULA, MISS., APRIL 25, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION CHRISTENED GUIDED MISSILE DESTROYER LENAH SUTCLIFFE HIGBEE (DDG 123) SATURDAY EVENING. DUE TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC, A SMALL, SOCIALLY DISTANCED EVENT WAS HELD WITH LIMITED IN-PERSON ATTENDANCE.
