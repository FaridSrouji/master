Photo Release — Huntington Ingalls Industries Announces New Vice President of Quality and Engineering at Ingalls Shipbuilding. PASCAGOULA, MISS., MAY 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES' (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT BRIAN BLANCHETTE HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO VICE PRESIDENT OF QUALITY AND ENGINEERING.
