Photo Release — Huntington Ingalls Industries Announces the election of Kari Wilkinson as Ingalls Shipbuilding President as Brian Cuccias Plans for Retirement. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., MARCH 05, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE: HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED KARI WILKINSON TO SERVE AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF HII AND PRESIDENT OF HII'S INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1. SHE WILL SUCCEED BRIAN CUCCIAS, WHO ANNOUNCED THAT HE WILL RETIRE APRIL 1.
