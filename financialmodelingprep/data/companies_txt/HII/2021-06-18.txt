Photo Release — Huntington Ingalls Industries Announces New Chief Information Officer at Newport News Shipbuilding. NEWPORT NEWS, VA., JUNE 18, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES (NYSE:HII) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JASON SUTTON HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER FOR ITS NEWPORT NEWS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION, EFFECTIVE JUNE 21.
