Photo Release — Huntington Ingalls Industries Announces New Vice President of Program Management at Ingalls Shipbuilding. PASCAGOULA, MISS., MARCH 22, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HUNTINGTON INGALLS INDUSTRIES' (NYSE: HII) INGALLS SHIPBUILDING DIVISION ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT GEORGE NUNGESSER HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO VICE PRESIDENT OF PROGRAM MANAGEMENT, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1.
