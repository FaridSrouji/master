Video Release — Ingalls Shipbuilding and Newport News Shipbuilding Recognized for Workplace Health Achievement. NEWPORT NEWS, Va., Nov. 06, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Huntington Ingalls Industries' (NYSE: HII) Ingalls Shipbuilding and Newport News Shipbuilding divisions have achieved national gold level recognition from the American Heart Association for taking significant steps to build a culture of health in the workplace.
