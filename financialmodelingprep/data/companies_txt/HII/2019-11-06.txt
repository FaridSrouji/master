Huntington Ingalls Industries, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Huntington Ingalls Industries, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 2,219 million compared to USD 2,083 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 214 million compared to USD 290 million a year ago. Net income was USD 154 million compared to USD 229 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.74 compared to USD 5.29 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 6,487 million compared to USD 5,977 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 550 million compared to USD 738 million a year ago. Net income was USD 400 million compared to USD 624 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 9.66 compared to USD 14.15 a year ago.
