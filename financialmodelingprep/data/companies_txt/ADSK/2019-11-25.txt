Autodesk, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter Ending January 31, 2020 and Fiscal Year 2020. Autodesk, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter ending January 31, 2020 and fiscal year 2020. For the quarter, the company expects revenue to be in the range of $880 million to $895 million and EPS GAAP to be in the range of $0.42 to $0.47.For the fiscal year ending January 31, 2020, the company expects revenue to be in the range of $3,255 million to $3,270 million and EPS GAAP to be in the range of $0.80 to $0.85.
