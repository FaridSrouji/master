Autodesk's Chief Legal Officer Announces Retirement After 23-Year Run. SAN FRANCISCO, MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RETIREMENT OF PASCAL W. DI FRONZO, THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS AND CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER, FOLLOWING 23 SUCCESSFUL YEARS AT THE COMPANY.
