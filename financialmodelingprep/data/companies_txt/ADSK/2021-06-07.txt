Autodesk Confirms Proposal to Acquire Electronic Design Software Leader Altium Limited. SAN FRANCISCO, JUNE 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AUTODESK, INC. (NASDAQ: ADSK) TODAY CONFIRMED THAT IT HAS SUBMITTED A NON-BINDING PROPOSAL TO ACQUIRE ALL THE OUTSTANDING SHARES OF COMMON STOCK OF ALTIUM LIMITED (ASX: ALU) FOR AUD$38.50, TO BE IMPLEMENTED BY WAY OF A SCHEME OF ARRANGEMENT. ALTIUM IS A GLOBAL SOFTWARE COMPANY HEADQUARTERED IN SAN DIEGO, CALIFORNIA AND PUBLICLY TRADED ON THE AUSTRALIAN SECURITIES EXCHANGE.
