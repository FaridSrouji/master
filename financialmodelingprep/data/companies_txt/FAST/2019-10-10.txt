Fastenal Company Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Fastenal Company announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 1,379.1 million compared to USD 1,279.8 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 281.9 million compared to USD 262.3 million a year ago. Net income was USD 213.5 million compared to USD 197.6 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.37 compared to USD 0.34 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 4,056.8 million compared to USD 3,733.5 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 818.3 million compared to USD 765.8 million a year ago. Net income was USD 612.2 million compared to USD 583.1 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.07 compared to USD 1.02 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.07 compared to USD 1.01 a year ago.
