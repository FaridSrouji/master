Fastenal Company Reports Q3 2019 Earnings. FASTENAL COMPANY REPORTS 2019 THIRD QUARTER EARNINGS.NET SALES INCREASED $99.3 MILLION, OR 7.8%, IN Q3 OF 2019.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.37.Q3 SALES ROSE 7.8 PERCENT.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.35 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.GENERAL SLOWING IN ECONOMIC ACTIVITY THAT CO EXPERIENCED IN Q2 OF 2019 CONTINUED IN Q3 OF 2019.CURRENTLY EXPECT TO SIGN ABOUT 22,000 VENDING DEVICES IN 2019, SLIGHTLY BELOW OUR PREVIOUS GOAL OF 23,000 TO 25,000 UNITS.A CONTRIBUTOR TO Q3 SALES GROWTH WAS HIGHER PRODUCT PRICING CO IMPLEMENTED TO MITIGATE IMPACTS OF GENERAL AND TARIFF-RELATED INFLATION.QTRLY NET SALES OF $1.38 BILLION VERSUS $1.28 BILLION.Q3 REVENUE VIEW $1.37 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
