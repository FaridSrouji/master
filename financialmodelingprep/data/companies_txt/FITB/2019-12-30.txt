Fifth Third Bancorp Says Entered Into An Agreement With Fidelity National Information Services & Worldpay. FIFTH THIRD BANCORP - ON DEC 27, ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES & WORLDPAY.FIFTH THIRD BANCORP - UNDER AGREEMENT CO & WORLDPAY TO TERMINATE & SETTLE CERTAIN CASH FLOWS PAYABLE UNDER CO'S TAX RECEIVABLE AGREEMENT WITH WORLDPAY.FIFTH THIRD BANCORP-WORLDPAY MAY BE OBLIGATED TO PAY UP TO ABOUT $366 MILLION TO CO TO TERMINATE,SETTLE SOME REMAINING CASH FLOWS CO TO GET UNDER TRA.FIFTH THIRD BANCORP - EXPECTS TO RECOGNIZE PRE-TAXGAIN & CORRESPONDING RECEIVABLE OF ABOUT $344 MILLION IN Q4 ASSOCIATED WITH OPTIONS.FIFTH THIRD BANCORP - AGREEMENT DOES NOT IMPACT ABOUT $195 MILLION OF OTHER TRA PAYMENTS EXPECTED TO BE RECOGNIZED IN Q4 OF 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 & 2024.FIFTH THIRD BANCORP - ABOUT $195 MILLION OF OTHER TRA PAYMENTS ARE EXPECTED TO BE RECEIVED IN Q1 OF 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 AND 2025, RESPECTIVELY.
