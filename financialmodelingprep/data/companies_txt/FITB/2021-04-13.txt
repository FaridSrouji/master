Fifth Third Bancorp Announces Preliminary Results of Annual Shareholders Meeting. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIFTH THIRD BANCORP (NASDAQ: FITB) HAS ANNOUNCED THAT PRELIMINARY RESULTS FROM THE ANNUAL SHAREHOLDERS MEETING HELD EARLIER TODAY HAVE INDICATED THE FOLLOWING DIRECTORS WERE RE-ELECTED: NICHOLAS K. AKINS, CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT, CEO, AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER. EVAN BAYH III, SENIOR ADVISOR, APOLLO GLOBAL MANAGEMENT. JORGE L. BENITEZ, RETIRED CEO OF NORTH AMERICA OF ACCENTURE PLC. KATHERINE B. BLACKBURN, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, CINCINNATI BENGALS, INC. EMERSON L. BRUMBA
