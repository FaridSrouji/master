Fifth Third Bancorp Launches Minority-Focused Wealth Planning Service. Fifth Third Bancorp has launched a new service that it believes is the first of its kind in the U.S. to help African Americans manage wealth transfer issues. It has launched a program called Transfer to Transformation as an African American wealth transfer initiative, Mark Walton, a vice president and wealth management adviser at Fifth Third Private Bank, told me. The pilot program started recently in Cincinnati. The goal is to expand it later to a multicultural program working with other minority groups.
