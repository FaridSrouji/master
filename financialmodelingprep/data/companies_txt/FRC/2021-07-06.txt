First Republic Bank Declares Dividends on Three Series of Perpetual Preferred Stock. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIRST REPUBLIC BANK (NYSE:FRC), A LEADING PRIVATE BANK AND WEALTH MANAGEMENT COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED CASH DIVIDENDS ON THE FOLLOWING OUTSTANDING SERIES OF ITS PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK. A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $11.75 PER SHARE WAS DECLARED ON ITS NONCUMULATIVE PERPETUAL SERIES J PREFERRED STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND EQUALS $0.293750 PER DEPOSITARY SHARE, EACH REPRESENTING 1/40TH INTEREST IN A SHARE OF SERIES J PREFERRED STOCK, WHICH IS TRADED
