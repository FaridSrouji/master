FIRST REPUBLIC REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.Q1 REVENUE $916.2 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $872.5 MILLION.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.20.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.02 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.IN RESPONSE TO COVID-19 ,  IMPLEMENTED CONTINGENCY PLANS, INCLUDING CO-WIDE REMOTE WORKING ARRANGEMENTS.ANNOUNCED AN INCREASE OF $0.01 IN ITS QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.20 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.NET INTEREST INCOME WAS $752.1 MILLION FOR QUARTER, UP 11.4%.LOAN ORIGINATIONS WERE $10.3 BILLION FOR THE QUARTER, UP 59.2%.PROVISION FOR CREDIT LOSSES AND UNFUNDED LOAN COMMITMENTS WAS $62.4 MILLION IN QUARTER.
