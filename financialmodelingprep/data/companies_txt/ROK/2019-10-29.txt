Rockwell Automation, Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 10, 2019. The board of directors of Rockwell Automation, Inc. declared a quarterly dividend of $1.02 per share on its outstanding common stock, payable on December 10, 2019 to shareowners of record at the close of business on November 12, 2019.
