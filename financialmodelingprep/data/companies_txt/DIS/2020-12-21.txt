Alan Bergman Named Chairman, Disney Studios Content; Alan Horn to Continue as Chief Creative Officer. BURBANK, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ALAN BERGMAN HAS BEEN NAMED CHAIRMAN, DISNEY STUDIOS CONTENT, AND ALAN HORN WILL CONTINUE TO SERVE AS THE DIVISION'S CHIEF CREATIVE OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 1, 2021, AS ANNOUNCED TODAY BY BOB CHAPEK, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY (NYSE: DIS). BOTH WILL REPORT TO CHAPEK. HORN AND BERGMAN HAVE JOINTLY LED THE STUDIOS GROUP AS CO-CHAIRMEN SINCE MAY 2019. “THE WALT DISNEY STUDIOS' ACHIEVEMENTS UNDER THE LEADERSHIP OF ALAN HORN AND ALAN BERGMAN
