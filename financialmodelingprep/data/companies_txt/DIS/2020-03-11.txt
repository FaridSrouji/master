Walt Disney Says Entered Into New $5.25 Billion 364-Day Credit Agreement. WALT DISNEY - ON MARCH 6, CO ENTERED INTO NEW $5.25 BILLION 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT.WALT DISNEY - ALSO ENTERED INTO NEW $3 BILLION FIVE-YEAR CREDIT AGREEMENT.WALT DISNEY - NEW 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT REPLACES $6 BILLION 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT DATED AS OF DEC 18, 2018.WALT DISNEY - NEW 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT WILL EXPIRE ON MARCH 5, 2021.WALT DISNEY - NEW 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT & NEW FIVE-YEAR CREDIT AGREEMENT EACH INCLUDES GUARANTEE BY TWDC ENTERPRISES OF CO'S PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS.
