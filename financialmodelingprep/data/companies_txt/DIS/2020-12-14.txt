The Walt Disney Company Schedules Annual Meeting of Shareholders for March 9, 2021. BURBANK, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS OF THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY (NYSE: DIS) WILL BE HELD ON TUESDAY, MARCH 9, 2021 AT 1:00 P.M. ET / 10:00 A.M. PT BY VIRTUAL MEETING AND WILL BE MADE AVAILABLE VIA WEBCAST AT WWW.DISNEY.COM/INVESTORS.
