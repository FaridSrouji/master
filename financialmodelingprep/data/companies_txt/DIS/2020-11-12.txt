The Walt Disney Company Board Decides to Forgo Next Semi-Annual Cash Dividend. BURBANK, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY (NYSE: DIS) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL NOT DECLARE A SEMI-ANNUAL CASH DIVIDEND FOR THE SECOND HALF OF FISCAL 2020, IN LIGHT OF THE ONGOING IMPACT OF COVID-19 AND THE COMPANY'S DECISION TO PRIORITIZE INVESTMENT IN ITS DIRECT-TO-CONSUMER INITIATIVES.
