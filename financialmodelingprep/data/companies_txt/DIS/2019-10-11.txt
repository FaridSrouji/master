Walt Disney Co Says Alan Braverman Entered Amendment To Employment Agreement. WALT DISNEY CO - ON OCT 8, ALAN BRAVERMAN ENTERED AMENDMENT TO EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT DATED SEPTEMBER 27, 2013.WALT DISNEY CO - UNDER AMENDMENT, CO & BRAVERMAN AGREED TO EXTEND TERM OF EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT TO DEC. 31, 2021.WALT DISNEY CO - AMENDMENT INCREASED BRAVERMAN'S ANNUAL BASE SALARY TO $1.75 MILLION EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 26.WALT DISNEY CO - AMENDMENT SETS BRAVERMAN'S TARGET LONG-TERM EQUITY INCENTIVE ANNUAL AWARD VALUE AT 350% OF BASE SALARY.
