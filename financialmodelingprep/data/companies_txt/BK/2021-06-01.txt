BNY Mellon Wealth Management Appoints Barry Halpin as Regional Director in London. LONDON, JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT HAS APPOINTED BARRY HALPIN AS REGIONAL DIRECTOR IN LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM. BARRY WILL START ON 1ST OF JUNE AND REPORT TO CHUCK LONG, HEAD OF INTERNATIONAL WEALTH MANAGEMENT.
