BNY Mellon and World Economic Forum Partner to Shape the Future of Capital Markets. NEW YORK, MARCH 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON ANNOUNCED TODAY A COLLABORATION WITH THE WORLD ECONOMIC FORUM TO ADDRESS THE CHALLENGES OF AN INCREASINGLY COMPLEX AND INTERDEPENDENT WORLD. THROUGH A PARTNERSHIP, BNY MELLON WILL WORK WITH THE WORLD ECONOMIC FORUM'S MULTI-STAKEHOLDER COMMUNITY ON INITIATIVES AIMED AT BUILDING A MORE EFFICIENT, RESILIENT AND INCLUSIVE FINANCIAL SYSTEM.
