BNY Mellon Wealth Management Names Sinead Colton Grant as Global Head of BNY Mellon Investor Solutions. NEW YORK, JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT NAMES SINEAD COLTON GRANT AS GLOBAL HEAD OF BNY MELLON INVESTOR SOLUTIONS, LLC ("INVESTOR SOLUTIONS"). SHE WILL BE LEADING THE FIRM'S MULTI-ASSET SOLUTIONS BUSINESS, WHICH HAS OVER $29 BILLION IN TOTAL ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT AND ADVISEMENT AS OF MARCH 31, 2021.
