BNY Mellon Wealth Management Names Carolina Montiel as Regional Director, Team Leader, in Atlanta, GA. ATLANTA, APRIL 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT HAS APPOINTED CAROLINA MONTIEL AS REGIONAL DIRECTOR, TEAM LEADER IN ATLANTA, GA. IN THIS ROLE, SHE WILL OVERSEE IMPLEMENTATION AND CLIENT SERVICING OF OUR CUSTOM-TAILORED INVESTMENT AND WEALTH MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS IN THE REGION.
