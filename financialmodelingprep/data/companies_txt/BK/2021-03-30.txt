BNY Mellon Wealth Management Appoints John Ippolito as Regional President of Tri-State Region. NEW YORK, MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT HAS APPOINTED JOHN IPPOLITO AS REGIONAL PRESIDENT OF THE TRI-STATE REGION. JOHN WILL JOIN THE WEALTH MANAGEMENT LEADERSHIP TEAM AND WILL REPORT TO CATHERINE KEATING, THE CEO OF WEALTH MANAGEMENT.
