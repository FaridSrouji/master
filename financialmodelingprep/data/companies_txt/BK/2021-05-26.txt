BNY Mellon to Speak at the Morgan Stanley US Financials Conference. NEW YORK, MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EMILY PORTNEY, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, AND PAUL CAMP, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF BNY MELLON TREASURY SERVICES, WILL SPEAK AT THE MORGAN STANLEY US FINANCIALS CONFERENCE, AT 12:30 P.M. ET ON MONDAY, JUNE 14, 2021.
BNY Mellon's New Solution Leads the Digital Bill-Pay Revolution. NEW YORK, MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS LAUNCHED A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND REAL-TIME ELECTRONIC BILL (E-BILL) AND PAYMENT SOLUTION. DISPLACING THE INEFFICIENT AND ANTIQUATED PROCESS HISTORICALLY USED TO HANDLE THE MAJORITY OF THE 15 BILLION BILLS PAID IN THE U.S. ANNUALLY, THIS PIONEERING CAPABILITY ENABLES U.S. BUSINESSES TO PRESENT DIGITAL BILLS TO THEIR CONSUMER CLIENTS IN REAL-TIME AND RECEIVE INSTANT PAYMENT VIA THE CONSUMERS' PREFERRED ONLINE AND MOBILE BANKING CHANNELS.
