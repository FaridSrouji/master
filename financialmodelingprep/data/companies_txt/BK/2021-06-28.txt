BNY Mellon Announces Intention to Increase Quarterly Common Stock Dividend by 10 Percent to $0.34 per Share and Authorization to Repurchase up to $6.0 Billion of Common Stock. NEW YORK, JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ON JUNE 24, 2021, THE FEDERAL RESERVE RELEASED THE RESULTS OF THE 2021 BANK STRESS TEST, WHICH DEMONSTRATES THE RESILIENCE AND STRENGTH OF BNY MELLON'S BUSINESS MODEL AND CAPITAL POSITION. THE FEDERAL RESERVE ALSO NOTIFIED THE COMPANY THAT ITS STRESS CAPITAL BUFFER (SCB) REQUIREMENT, EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 1, 2021, WILL REMAIN 2.5%¹, EQUAL TO THE REGULATORY MINIMUM.
BNY Mellon Wealth Management Announces Crystal Thompkins as Head of Philanthropic Solutions. NEW YORK, JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BNY MELLON WEALTH MANAGEMENT HAS APPOINTED CRYSTAL THOMPKINS AS HEAD OF PHILANTHROPIC SOLUTIONS. CRYSTAL LEADS AND COORDINATES STRATEGY FOR ALL OF WEALTH MANAGEMENT'S PHILANTHROPIC SERVICES, INCLUDING INDIVIDUAL AND FAMILY PHILANTHROPY, INSTITUTIONAL ENDOWMENTS AND FOUNDATIONS, PLANNED GIVING AND DONOR-ADVISED FUND SERVICES.
