Assurant, Inc. Declares Quarterly Common Stock and Preferred Stock Dividend, Payable on December 16, 2019. Assurant, Inc. announced that its Board of Directors approved a quarterly dividend increase of $0.03 to $0.63 per share of common stock. The dividend will be payable on December 16, 2019 to stockholders of record as of the close of business on November 25, 2019. The Board also declared a quarterly dividend of $1.6250 per share of 6.50% mandatory convertible preferred stock. The dividend will be payable on December 16, 2019 to stockholders of record as of the close of business on December 1, 2019.
