Assurant Repurposes its 100 Millionth Mobile Device. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ASSURANT (NYSE: AIZ), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF LIFESTYLE AND HOUSING SOLUTIONS THAT SUPPORT, PROTECT AND CONNECT MAJOR CONSUMER PURCHASES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS REPURPOSED ITS 100 MILLIONTH MOBILE DEVICE UNDERSCORING THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABILITY. THIS MILESTONE REFLECTS THE DEVICES TRANSACTED BY THE TRADE-IN AND UPGRADE OPERATIONS OF ASSURANT AND THE COMPANY IT ACQUIRED LAST YEAR, HYLA MOBILE.
