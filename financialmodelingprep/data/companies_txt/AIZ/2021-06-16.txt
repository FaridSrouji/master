Assurant Strengthens its Mobile Services Capabilities in South Korea with Acquisition of Olivar Co. Ltd.. SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ASSURANT, A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF LIFESTYLE AND HOUSING SOLUTIONS THAT SUPPORT, PROTECT AND CONNECT MAJOR CONSUMER PURCHASES, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS PURCHASED THE REMAINING SHAREHOLDING IN OLIVAR CO. LTD., A PROVIDER OF MOBILE DEVICE LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT AND ASSET DISPOSITION IN SOUTH KOREA. THE TRANSACTION SUPPORTS ASSURANT'S GROWTH STRATEGY FOR ITS CONNECTED LIVING BUSINESS AND STRENGTHENS ASSURANT'S COMMITMENT TO DELIVER A MARKET-LEADING CUSTOMER EXP
