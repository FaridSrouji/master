Berkshire Hathaway Inc. Information Regarding 2021 Annual Shareholders Meeting and First Quarter Earnings Release. OMAHA, NEB.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--(BRK.A; BRK.B) – BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY WILL HOLD ITS 2021 ANNUAL SHAREHOLDERS MEETING ON SATURDAY MAY 1, 2021 BEGINNING AT 1:30 P.M. EASTERN TIME. AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED, WE WILL NOT BE ABLE TO ALLOW SHAREHOLDERS TO PHYSICALLY ATTEND THE MEETING. HOWEVER, THE MEETING AND A PRE-MEETING SHOW BEGINNING AT 12:30 P.M. EASTERN TIME WILL BE STREAMED LIVE ON THE INTERNET BY YAHOO AND CAN BE ACCESSED AT HTTPS://FINANCE.YAHOO.COM/BRKLIVESTREAM. BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC.'S FIRST
