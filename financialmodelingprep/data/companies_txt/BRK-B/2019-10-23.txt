Berkshire Hathaway Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Berkshire Hathaway Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 4,338 million compared to USD 4,419 million a year ago. Net income was USD 16,524 million compared to USD 18,540 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 10,119 compared to USD 11,280 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 11,729 million compared to USD 11,818 million a year ago. Net income was USD 52,258 million compared to USD 29,413 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 31,944 compared to USD 17,885 a year ago.
