Humana Sees FY2020 GAAP Earnings Per Share $17.36 To $17.86. HUMANA REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2020 FINANCIAL RESULTS; CONTINUES PROACTIVE RELIEF EFFORTS AMID PANDEMIC.SEES FY 2020 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $17.36 TO $17.86.REAFFIRMS FY 2020 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $18.25 TO $18.75.FY 2020 GAAP EPS EXPECTED TO BE IN A RANGE OF $17.36 TO $17.86.2Q20 EARNINGS PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE OF $13.75 ON A GAAP BASIS, $12.56 ON AN ADJUSTED BASIS.REAFFIRMS FY 2020 ADJUSTED EPS GUIDANCE RANGE OF $18.25 TO $18.75.INCREASES FULL YEAR EXPECTED INDIVIDUAL MEDICARE ADVANTAGE MEMBERSHIP GROWTH TO 330,000 TO 360,000 MEMBERS.QTRLY REVENUES - GAAP $19,083 MILLION VERSUS $16,245 MILLION.HUMANA - REITERATING EXPECTATIONS FOR GROUP MEDICARE ADVANTAGE NET MEMBERSHIP GAINS FOR FY 2020, PROJECTING INCREASE OF ABOUT 90,000 MEMBERS Y-O-Y.FOR ITS STAND-ALONE PDP BUSINESS, CONTINUES TO ESTIMATE A NET MEMBERSHIP DECLINE OF ABOUT 550,000 MEMBERS FOR FY 2020.ANTICIPATES Q2 RESULTS WILL BE OFFSET IN LATTER HALF OF 2020 AS DEMAND FOR PREVIOUSLY DEFERRED CARE NORMALIZES.FY2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $18.72 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $10.27, REVENUE VIEW $18.63 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
