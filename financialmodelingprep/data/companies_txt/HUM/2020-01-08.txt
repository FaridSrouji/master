Humana Sees Net PDP Membership Decline Of About 600,000 Members For Year Ended Dec. 31, 2020. HUMANA - REAFFIRMING NET MEMBERSHIP GROWTH ESTIMATE FOR INDIVIDUAL MEDICARE ADVANTAGE PRODUCTS FOR YEAR ENDED 2020 OF 270,000 - 330,000 MEMBERS.2020 MEMBERSHIP ESTIMATE REPRESENTS EXPECTED NET MEMBERSHIP GAINS OF 7.5 TO 9.2 PERCENT.HUMANA - REITERATES EXPECTATIONS FOR GROUP MEDICARE ADVANTAGE NET MEMBERSHIP GAINS FOR 2020.FOR PDP, CO ESTIMATES NET MEMBERSHIP DECLINE OF ABOUT 600,000 MEMBERS FOR YEAR ENDED DEC. 31, 2020.2020 PDP MEMBERSHIP ESTIMATE REPRESENTS DECLINE OF 14%.TO REAFFIRM 2019 GAAP GUIDANCE OF ABOUT $18.32 IN DILUTED EPS.HUMANA - TO REAFFIRM 2019 ADJUSTED EPS GUIDANCE OF ABOUT $17.75.HUMANA - REAFFIRMING EXPECTATION FOR DECLINE IN GROUP COMMERCIAL MEDICAL MEMBERSHIP OF ABOUT 80,000 TO 100,000 MEMBERS IN 2020.HUMANA - REAFFIRMING INCREASE IN STATE-BASED CONTRACT MEMBERSHIP OF 150,000 TO 200,000 MEMBERS FOR YEAR ENDED DEC. 31, 2020.FY2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $17.77 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
