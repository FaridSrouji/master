Columbia Gas of Maryland Proposes Further Investments in Safety Through Replacing and Upgrading Aging Infrastructure. CANONSBURG, PA., MAY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COLUMBIA GAS OF MARYLAND, INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI), FILED A REQUEST TODAY WITH THE MARYLAND PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION (PSC) TO APPROVE REVISED RATES FOR FURTHER UPGRADING AND REPLACING PORTIONS OF THE COMPANY'S UNDERGROUND NATURAL GAS DISTRIBUTION PIPELINES.
