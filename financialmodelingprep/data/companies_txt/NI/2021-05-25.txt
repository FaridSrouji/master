NiSource Declares Common and Preferred Stock Dividends. MERRILLVILLE, IND., MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF 22 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE AUGUST 20, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 30, 2021.
