NIPSCO To Explore Range Of Options To Meet Future Electric Needs. MERRILLVILLE, IND., MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORTHERN INDIANA PUBLIC SERVICE COMPANY, LLC (NIPSCO), A SUBSIDIARY OF NISOURCE INC. (NYSE: NI), HAS ANNOUNCED THE OPENING OF ITS NEXT ROUND OF REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS (RFP) TO CONSIDER A COMBINATION OF POTENTIAL RESOURCES TO MEET THE FUTURE ELECTRIC NEEDS OF ITS CUSTOMERS.
