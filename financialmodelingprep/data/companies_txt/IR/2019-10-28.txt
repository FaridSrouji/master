Ingersoll-Rand plc Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Ingersoll-Rand Plc announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 4,344.3 million compared to USD 4,030.9 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 623.2 million compared to USD 587 million a year ago. Net income was USD 458.8 million compared to USD 515.1 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 1.78 compared to USD 2.11 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.88 compared to USD 2.06 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 12,448 million compared to USD 11,773.1 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,592.2 million compared to USD 1,470.7 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,114.8 million compared to USD 1,083.6 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 4.48 compared to USD 4.43 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.55 compared to USD 4.32 a year ago.
