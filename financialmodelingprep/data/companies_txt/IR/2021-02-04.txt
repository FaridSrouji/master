Ingersoll Rand Announces Fourth-Quarter and Full-Year 2020 Earnings Release. DAVIDSON, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INGERSOLL RAND INC., (NYSE: IR) A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF MISSION-CRITICAL FLOW CREATION AND INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS, WILL ISSUE ITS FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2020 EARNINGS RELEASE AFTER THE MARKET CLOSES ON MONDAY, FEBRUARY 22, 2021. INGERSOLL RAND WILL ALSO HOST A LIVE EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL TO DISCUSS THE FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR RESULTS ON TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 23, 2021 AT 8 A.M. (EASTERN TIME).
