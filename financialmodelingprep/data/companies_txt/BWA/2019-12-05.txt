BorgWarner to Launch New High Voltage Hairpin 146 Motor. BorgWarner is launching its new High Voltage Hairpin 146 motor. The motor is likely to enter production on a light-duty hybrid passenger car with a major OEM in early 2020. The electric motor joins the company's family of patented and versatile HVH Series motors, available as fully-housed motors or rotor/stator assemblies. The motors are available in a number of configurations and offer scalable output and torque. The HVH 146 is an interior permanent magnet electric motor and is best suited for belt-drive integration at P0 in hybrid electric vehicle applications. It is mounted to the front of the engine through a high-tension belt system that allows it to operate at high power levels and speeds up to 16,500 rpm.
