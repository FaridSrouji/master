BorgWarner Donates $1,025,000 to Kettering University to Establish Scholars Program and Other Initiatives to Support Opportunities for Minorities. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., FEB. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER IS TAKING FURTHER ACTION IN ITS COMMITMENT TO DIVERSITY THROUGH A DONATION OF $925,000 FOR SCHOLARSHIPS FOR KETTERING UNIVERSITY STUDENTS. THE BORGWARNER SCHOLARS PROGRAM WILL AIM TO DISTRIBUTE ANNUAL SCHOLARSHIPS TO STUDENTS...
