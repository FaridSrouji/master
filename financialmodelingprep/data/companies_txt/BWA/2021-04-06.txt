BorgWarner Achieves Great Place to Work-Certified™ Company Status for Second Consecutive Year. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., APRIL 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER, A GLOBAL LEADER IN CLEAN AND EFFICIENT TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS FOR ELECTRIC, HYBRID AND COMBUSTION VEHICLES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS EARNED THE DESIGNATION OF BEING A GREAT PLACE TO WORK-CERTIFIED™ COMPANY.
