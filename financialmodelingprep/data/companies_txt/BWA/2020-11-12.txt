BorgWarner Declares Quarterly Dividend. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., NOV. 12, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ON NOVEMBER 11, 2020, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BORGWARNER INC. (NYSE: BWA) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.17 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON DECEMBER 15, 2020 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON DECEMBER 1, 2020....
