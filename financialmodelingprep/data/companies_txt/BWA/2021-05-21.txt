2021 Louis Schwitzer Award Honors Biomedical Engineering for INDYCAR® Driver Safety. INDIANAPOLIS, MAY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE 55TH ANNUAL LOUIS SCHWITZER AWARD WAS PRESENTED TO TERRY TRAMMELL, M.D., FOR HIS WORK ON BIOMEDICAL ENGINEERING FOR INDYCAR® DRIVER SAFETY EARLIER TODAY AT THE INDIANAPOLIS MOTOR SPEEDWAY (IMS).
