BorgWarner to Supply Integrated Drive Module to Hyundai Motor Group Electric Vehicles. AUBURN HILLS, MICH., JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BORGWARNER'S INTEGRATED DRIVE MODULE (IDM) WAS SELECTED BY THE HYUNDAI MOTOR GROUP FOR ITS POWER ELECTRIC SYSTEM, WHICH WILL BE USED IN THE UPCOMING A-SEGMENT ELECTRIC VEHICLE PRODUCTION, PLANNED TO START IN MID OF 2023.
