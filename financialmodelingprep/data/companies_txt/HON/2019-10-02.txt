Honeywell International Inc. Announces New Headquarters Building in Charlotte. Honeywell International Inc. announced its new Charlotte headquarters in uptown, a move that the company's top executive hopes will be the first of many buildings for the company in the Queen City. Considered a huge coup for Charlotte, given Honeywell's stature, the decision for the electronics manufacturer to relocate to Charlotte was initially hard but now has company employees excited. The company will lease about 280,000 square feet at a 23-story office tower at the Legacy Union site in uptown, which Charlotte-based Lincoln Harris is developing with Goldman Sachs. Honeywell began moving into its temporary headquarters office space at 300 South Tryon this summer. The building at Legacy Union will deliver in the first half of 2021, with a goal of Honeywell moving in that summer.
