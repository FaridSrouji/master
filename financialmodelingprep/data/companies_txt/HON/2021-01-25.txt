Honeywell To Bring Next-Generation Inertial Sensors To Unmanned Vehicles Segment. PHOENIX, JAN. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NYSE: HON), WITH FUNDING FROM THE U.S. DEFENSE ADVANCED RESEARCH PROJECTS AGENCY (DARPA), IS CREATING THE NEXT GENERATION OF INERTIAL SENSOR TECHNOLOGY THAT WILL ONE DAY BE USED IN BOTH COMMERCIAL AND DEFENSE NAVIGATION APPLICATIONS....
