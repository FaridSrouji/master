Honeywell Names Two New Segment Leaders And Chief Operating Officer For High Growth Regions. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JULY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON) ANNOUNCED TODAY NEW LEADERS OF ITS PERFORMANCE MATERIALS AND TECHNOLOGIES (PMT) AND HONEYWELL BUILDING TECHNOLOGIES (HBT) SEGMENTS, AND A NEW CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER OF GLOBAL HIGH GROWTH REGIONS.
