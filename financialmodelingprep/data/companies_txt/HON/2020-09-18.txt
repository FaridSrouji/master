Boeing, Honeywell And Rolls-Royce Deutschland Partner To Service H-47 Chinook Engines. BOEING, HONEYWELL AND ROLLS-ROYCE DEUTSCHLAND PARTNER TO SERVICE H-47 CHINOOK ENGINES.BOEING - AGREEMENT TO PROVIDE IN-SERVICE SUPPORT OF T-55 ENGINE IF GOVERNMENT OF GERMANY SELECT H-47 CHINOOK FOR STH HEAVY-LIFT HELICOPTER REQUIREMENT.BOEING - HONEYWELL TO LICENSE ROLLS-ROYCE DEUTSCHLAND AS PARTNER IN GERMANY TO PERFORM MAINTENANCE OF CHINOOK T-55 ENGINE OPERATED BY LUFTWAFFE.
