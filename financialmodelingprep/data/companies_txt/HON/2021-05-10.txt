Kunsan Air Base Works With Honeywell To Expand Energy Infrastructure Upgrades. GUNSAN, SOUTH KOREA, MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NYSE: HON) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT IS STARTING THE NEXT PHASE OF AN INFRASTRUCTURE MODERNIZATION PROJECT TO IMPROVE ENERGY RESILIENCY AT KUNSAN AIR BASE, A U.S. AIR FORCE BASE IN GUNSAN, SOUTH KOREA. THE $23 MILLION PROJECT WILL IMPROVE FUEL SUPPLY RELIABILITY THROUGH THE INSTALLATION OF NATURAL GAS SERVICE ON THE BASE.
