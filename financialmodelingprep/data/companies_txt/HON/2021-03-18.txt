San Diego International Airport Works With Honeywell To Test Advanced Video Analytics To Help Create Healthier Airports. SAN DIEGO, MARCH 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL BUILDING TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: HON) IS WORKING WITH SAN DIEGO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (SAN)'S ACCELERATOR PROGRAM, THE AIRPORT INNOVATION LAB, TO TEST THE USE OF ADVANCED VIDEO ANALYTICS TECHNOLOGY TO ENHANCE AIRPORT HEALTH AND SAFETY. SAN DIEGO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT INNOVATION LAB IS TESTING HONEYWELL'S ADVANCED VIDEO ANALYTICS TECHNOLOGY.
