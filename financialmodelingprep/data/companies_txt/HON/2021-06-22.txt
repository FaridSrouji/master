Honeywell Cybersecurity Research Reports Significant Increase In USB Threats That Can Cause Costly Business Disruptions. ATLANTA, JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ACCORDING TO A REPORT RELEASED TODAY BY HONEYWELL (NASDAQ: HON), USB-BASED THREATS THAT CAN SEVERELY IMPACT BUSINESS OPERATIONS INCREASED SIGNIFICANTLY DURING A DISRUPTIVE YEAR WHEN THE USAGE OF REMOVABLE MEDIA AND NETWORK CONNECTIVITY ALSO GREW. DATA FROM THE 2021 HONEYWELL INDUSTRIAL USB THREAT REPORT INDICATES THAT 37% OF THREATS WERE SPECIFICALLY DESIGNED TO UTILIZE REMOVABLE MEDIA, WHICH ALMOST DOUBLED FROM 19% IN THE 2020 REPORT.
