Honeywell Technology Enables Large U.S. Carbon Capture And Storage Project. DES PLAINES, III., APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HONEYWELL (NYSE: HON) TODAY ANNOUNCED WABASH VALLEY RESOURCES LLC HAS SELECTED A RANGE OF HONEYWELL UOP TECHNOLOGIES TO CAPTURE AND SEQUESTER UP TO 1.65 MILLION TONS OF CARBON DIOXIDE (CO2) ANNUALLY AND TO PRODUCE CLEAN HYDROGEN ENERGY FROM A REPURPOSED GASIFICATION PLANT IN WEST TERRE HAUTE, IND.
