Wells Fargo Responds To OCC Actions Regarding Former Employees. WELLS FARGO & CO - WELLS FARGO RESPONDS TO OCC ACTIONS REGARDING FORMER EMPLOYEES.WELLS FARGO - REVIEWING THURSDAY'S FILINGS AND WILL DETERMINE WHAT, IF ANY, FURTHER ACTION BY CO IS APPROPRIATE WITH RESPECT TO ANY NAMED INDIVIDUALS.WELLS FARGO - TO NOT MAKE REMAINING COMPENSATION PAYMENTS THAT MAY BE OWED TO FORMER EMPLOYEES NAMED IN THURSDAY'S FILINGS WHILE CO REVIEWS THEM.
