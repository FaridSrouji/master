Wells Fargo Enters Its Largest Renewable Energy Purchase. WELLS FARGO ENTERS ITS LARGEST RENEWABLE ENERGY PURCHASE.WELLS FARGO & CO - ANNOUNCED A 10-YEAR STRUCTURED RENEWABLE ENERGY AGREEMENT WITH RELIANT, AN NRG ENERGY COMPANY.WELLS FARGO & CO - NEW AGREEMENT WILL PROVIDE APPROXIMATELY 62,000 MW-HOURS OF SOLAR ENERGY ANNUALLY TO APPROXIMATELY 400 WELLS FARGO PROPERTIES.WELLS FARGO - WILL EXPAND ITS ON-SITE RENEWABLES PORTFOLIO BY INSTALLING SOLAR TECHNOLOGIES ON OVER 100 CORPORATE, BRANCH, DATA FACILITIES ACROSS U.S..
