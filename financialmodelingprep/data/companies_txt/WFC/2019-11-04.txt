Mark Myers to Retire with Wells Fargo as Executive Vice President and Head of Commercial Real Estate Within the Company’s Wholesale Banking Group Effective from February 14, 2020. Wells Fargo & Company announced that Mark Myers, Executive Vice President and head of Commercial Real Estate (CRE) within the company’s Wholesale Banking group, has shared his intention to retire. Myers, has been with Wells Fargo for 39 years, and will stay in his role until February 14, 2020. Wells Fargo expects to announce a new head of Commercial Real Estate in the coming weeks.
