Wells Fargo & Company Announces Dividends on 11 Series of Preferred Stock, Payable on Dec. 16, 2019. Wells Fargo & Company announced dividends on 11 series of preferred stock. A quarterly cash dividend of $18.75 per share was declared on its 7.50% non-cumulative perpetual convertible class A preferred stock, Series L, liquidation preference $1,000 per share, which is traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol “WFCPrL”. The Series L dividend is payable on Dec. 16, 2019, to holders of record as of the close of business on Nov. 29, 2019. A quarterly cash dividend of $325.00 per share was declared on its 5.20% non-cumulative perpetual class A preferred stock, Series N, liquidation preference $25,000 per share. This dividend equals $0.325 per depositary share, each representing a 1/1,000th interest in a share of Series N preferred stock, which is traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol “WFCPrN”. The Series N dividend is payable on Dec. 16, 2019, to holders of record as of the close of business on Nov. 29, 2019.
