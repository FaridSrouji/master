Wells Fargo To Introduce No Overdraft Fee Bank Account, A Limited Overdraft Fee Account. WELLS FARGO TO INTRODUCE NO OVERDRAFT FEE BANK ACCOUNT AND A LIMITED OVERDRAFT FEE ACCOUNT.WELLS FARGO & CO - BOTH ACCOUNTS ARE EXPECTED TO BE AVAILABLE BY EARLY 2021.WELLS FARGO & CO - CHECKLESS BANK ACCOUNT WILL HAVE A FLAT MONTHLY FEE OF $5.WELLS FARGO & CO - CHECKLESS ACCOUNT WILL LIMIT SPENDING TO AMOUNT AVAILABLE IN ACCOUNT, CUSTOMERS WILL NOT INCUR OVERDRAFTS, INSUFFICIENT FUNDS FEES.WELLS FARGO-  ADDITIONAL ACCOUNT CO IS DEVELOPING WILL HAVE $10 MONTHLY SERVICE FEE, WILL CAP OVERDRAFT OR INSUFFICIENT FUNDS FEES AT ONE/MONTH.
