Wells Fargo Reaches Settlements To Resolve Outstanding DOJ And SEC Investigations Over Sales Practices. WELLS FARGO REACHES SETTLEMENTS TO RESOLVE OUTSTANDING DOJ AND SEC INVESTIGATIONS RELATED TO HISTORICAL COMMUNITY BANK SALES PRACTICES.WELLS FARGO & CO - AS PART OF THIS RESOLUTION, WELLS FARGO HAS AGREED TO MAKE PAYMENTS TOTALING $3 BILLION..WELLS FARGO & CO - SETTLEMENT INCLUDES AGREEMENT WITH DOJ RESOLVING CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION.WELLS FARGO & CO - RESOLUTION ALSO COVERS DOJ AND SEC CIVIL INVESTIGATIONS.WELLS FARGO & CO - AS PART OF AGREEMENT, NO CHARGES WILL BE FILED AGAINST WELLS FARGO PROVIDED WELLS FARGO ABIDES BY ALL TERMS OF AGREEMENT.WELLS FARGO & CO - HAD FULLY ACCRUED FOR AMOUNT OF SETTLEMENT AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2019.WELLS FARGO & CO - UNDER RESOLUTION CO AGREED TO ESTABLISHMENT OF $500 MILLION FAIR FUND FOR BENEFIT OF INVESTORS HARMED BY CONDUCT COVERED IN AGREEMENT.WELLS FARGO & CO - $500 MILLION FAIR FUND IS PART OF $3 BILLION SETTLEMENT.WELLS FARGO- AGREEMENT WITH DOJ RESOLVES CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION INTO SALES PRACTICE ACTIVITIES IN COMMUNITY BANK FROM 2002 - 2016.
