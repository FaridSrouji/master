CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY'S (NYSE: WFC) CEO OF CONSUMER & SMALL BUSINESS BANKING (CSBB) MARY MACK ANNOUNCED TODAY THE STRUCTURE AND LEADERS OF HER ORGANIZATION.
