Wells Fargo Announces Preliminary Voting Results of 2021 Annual Meeting of Shareholders. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WELLS FARGO & COMPANY (NYSE: WFC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS SHAREHOLDERS ELECTED AS DIRECTORS THE 12 NOMINEES NAMED IN THE COMPANY'S PROXY STATEMENT AND APPROVED AN ADVISORY PROPOSAL ON 2020 EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION. SHAREHOLDERS ALSO RATIFIED THE APPOINTMENT OF KPMG LLP AS THE COMPANY'S INDEPENDENT REGISTERED PUBLIC ACCOUNTING FIRM FOR 2021.
