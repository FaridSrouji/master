Dow teams up with the LPGA and LET as Official Sustainability Resource to advance environmental stewardship and inclusion through golf. MIDLAND, MICH., JULY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOW (NYSE: DOW), THE LADIES PROFESSIONAL GOLF ASSOCIATION (LPGA) AND THE LADIES EUROPEAN TOUR (LET) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DOW WILL SERVE AS THE OFFICIAL SUSTAINABILITY RESOURCE FOR THE ORGANIZATIONS.
