THE EMPOWER ETHNIC MINORITY ROLE MODEL LISTS SHOWCASE BUSINESS LEADERS WHO ARE BREAKING DOWN BARRIERS AT WORK FOR ETHNIC MINORITIES IN THE UK, IRELAND, EUROPE, AND PEOPLE OF COLOR IN THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA.
