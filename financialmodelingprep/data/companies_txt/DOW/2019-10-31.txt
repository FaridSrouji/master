ALTIVIA Ketones & Additives, LLC completed the acquisition of Acetone Derivatives Business from Dow Inc.. ALTIVIA Ketones & Additives, LLC entered into an agreement to acquire Acetone Derivatives Business from Dow Inc. (NYSE:DOW) on August 13, 2019. The acquisition includes production assets located in Institute, West Virginia, in addition to the site infrastructure, land and utilities. Dow will remain a tenant on the Institute site, retaining ownership of certain manufacturing assets. The transaction is expected to close by the end of 2019, subject to customary closing conditions.
ALTIVIA Ketones & Additives, LLC completed the acquisition of Acetone Derivatives Business from Dow Inc. (NYSE:DOW) on November 1, 2019.
