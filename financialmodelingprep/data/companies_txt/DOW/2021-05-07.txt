Dow advances to #19 on 2021 Top 50 Companies for Diversity list. MIDLAND, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--DOW (NYSE: DOW) ADVANCED TO THE 19TH PLACE ON THE 2021 DIVERSITYINC TOP 50 COMPANIES FOR DIVERSITY LIST, WHICH WAS ANNOUNCED YESTERDAY AT A VIRTUAL EVENT HOSTED BY DIVERSITYINC. DOW'S PLACEMENT ON THE LIST FOR THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR SHOWCASES THE COMPANY'S CONTINUED ACCELERATION OF ACTIONS AND STRENGTHENING OF INCLUSION AND DIVERSITY (I&D) ACROSS THE ORGANIZATION.
