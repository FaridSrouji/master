Gap Inc. Revises Earnings Guidance Full Year of 2019 and Sales Guidance for the Fiscal Year 2019. Gap Inc. revised earnings guidance full year of 2019 and sales guidance for the fiscal year 2019. For the year 2019 the company affirmed its reported diluted earnings per share guidance for fiscal year 2019 to be in the range of $1.38 to $1.47.For the sales guidance, the company now expects net sales growth for fiscal year 2019 to be down low-single digits.
