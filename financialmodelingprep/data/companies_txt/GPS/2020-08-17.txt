Third Point LLC Takes Share Stake In Facebook, Gap, Ups In Walt Disney Co. THIRD POINT LLC TAKES SHARE STAKE IN FACEBOOK OF 850,000 CLASS A SHARES.THIRD POINT LLC UPS SHARE STAKE IN WALT DISNEY CO TO 5.5 MILLION SHARES FROM 1.4 MILLION SHARES.THIRD POINT LLC TAKES SHARE STAKE IN GAP INC OF 3 MILLION SHARES.THIRD POINT LLC UPS SHARE STAKE IN FIDELITY NATIONAL INFORMATION SERVICES BY 36.5% TO 3.2 MILLION SHARES.THIRD POINT LLC - CHANGE IN HOLDINGS ARE AS OF JUNE 30, 2020 AND COMPARED WITH THE PREVIOUS QUARTER ENDED AS OF MARCH 31, 2020.
