Gap Inc Spokeswoman Says Our HQ In China Is Closed, THERE IS NO IMPACT TO SUPPLY CHAIN. GAP INC SPOKESWOMAN SAYS AT THIS TIME, OUR HQ IN CHINA IS CLOSED.GAP SPOKESWOMAN SAYS BECAUSE OF PLANNED CLOSURES OF FACTORIES FOR CHINESE NEW YEAR DURING THIS PERIOD, THERE IS NO IMPACT TO SUPPLY CHAIN AT THIS TIME.GAP SPOKESWOMAN SAYS ADJUSTING HOURS OF OPERATIONS FOR STORES IN CHINA AS NEEDED, WHICH INCLUDES CLOSURES/SHORTENED HOURS FOR A SMALL NUMBER OF STORES.GAP SPOKESWOMAN SAYS CO'S RESPONSE TO DEALING WITH 2019-NCOV VIRUS IS CLOSELY ALIGNED WITH RECOMMENDATIONS OUTLINED BY WHO, CDC AND ISOS.
