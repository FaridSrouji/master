Old Navy Partners with Welcoming America to Celebrate New US Citizens with Iconic Flag Tee. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TO CELEBRATE AMERICA'S UPCOMING BIRTHDAY AND THE DIVERSE TAPESTRY THAT MAKES UP THE U.S., OLD NAVY IS ROLLING OUT THE RED, WHITE AND BLUE CARPET TO THE COUNTRY'S NEWEST MEMBERS. THE QUINTESSENTIAL AMERICAN FASHION BRAND IS TEAMING UP WITH THE NONPROFIT WELCOMING AMERICA TO OFFICIALLY WELCOME NEWLY NATURALIZED U.S. CITIZENS WITH THE GIFT OF ITS ICONIC FLAG TEE. THIS FOURTH OF JULY, OLD NAVY IS CELEBRATING ALL THINGS AMERICAN, FROM APPLE PIE AND BARBECUES TO FREEDO
