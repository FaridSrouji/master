Invesco Mortgage Capital Inc. Announces Quarterly Common Dividend. ATLANTA, MARCH 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO MORTGAGE CAPITAL INC. (THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: IVR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK. COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.09 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021.
