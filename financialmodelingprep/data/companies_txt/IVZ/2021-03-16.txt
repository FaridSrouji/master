Invesco Announces Changes to Two ETFs. ATLANTA, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO LTD. (NYSE: IVZ), A LEADING GLOBAL ASSET MANAGEMENT FIRM, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL CHANGE THE UNDERLYING INDEXES OF TWO EXCHANGE-TRADED FUNDS (ETFS) IN ITS LINE-UP: THE INVESCO FINANCIAL PREFERRED ETF (PGF) AND THE INVESCO VARIABLE RATE PREFERRED ETF (VRP).
