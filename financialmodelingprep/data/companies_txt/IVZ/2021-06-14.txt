Jera Asset Management and Invesco Real Estate Form Joint Venture to Acquire Industrial Real Estate with a focus on Distribution Centers, Logistics Properties, and Cold Storage Warehouses Seeded with Initial Portfolio of Nearly $500 million in Assets.. CHARLOTTESVILLE, VA. AND DALLAS, JUNE 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- INVESCO REAL ESTATE, A GLOBAL REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT MANAGER, AND JERA ASSET MANAGEMENT (JERA), A NATIONAL REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT FIRM FOCUSED ON PROPERTIES THAT ARE NET-LEASED TO QUALITY CREDIT TENANTS, HAVE FORMED A STRATEGIC JOINT VENTURE, MERCURY TRUST, TO TAKE ADVANTAGE OF THE STRONG INDUSTRIAL MARKETS.
