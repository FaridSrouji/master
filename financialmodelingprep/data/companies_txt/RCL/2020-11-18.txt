SHAREHOLDER ALERT: Levi & Korsinsky, LLP Notifies Shareholders of Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. of a Class Action Lawsuit and a Lead Plaintiff Deadline of December 7, 2020 - RCL. NEW YORK, NEW YORK--(NEWSFILE CORP. - NOVEMBER 18, 2020) - THE FOLLOWING STATEMENT IS BEING ISSUED BY LEVI & KORSINSKY, LLP:TO: ALL PERSONS OR ENTITIES WHO PURCHASED OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED SECURITIES OF ROYAL CARIBBEAN CRUISES LTD. (NYSE: RCL) ("ROYAL CARIBBEAN") BETWEEN FEBRUARY 4, 2020 AND MARCH 17, 2020. YOU ARE HEREBY NOTIFIED THAT A SECURITIES CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN COMMENCED IN THE THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE SOUTHERN DISTRICT OF FLORIDA....
