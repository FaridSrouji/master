Royal Caribbean Cruises Enters Credit Agreement For Financing Of Sixth Oasis-Class Ship For Royal Caribbean International. ROYAL CARIBBEAN CRUISES - ON DEC 13, ENTERED CREDIT AGREEMENT FOR FINANCING OF SIXTH OASIS-CLASS SHIP FOR ROYAL CARIBBEAN INTERNATIONAL.ROYAL CARIBBEAN - AGREEMENT MAKES AVAILABLE TO CO, ON ACCEPTANCE, DELIVERY OF SHIP, UNSECURED TERM LOAN GUARANTEED 100% BY BPIFRANCE ASSURANCE EXPORT.ROYAL CARIBBEAN - MAXIMUM AMOUNT OF FACILITY EQUAL TO US DOLLAR EQUIVALENT OF 80% OF VESSEL PURCHASE PRICE PLUS 100% OF PREMIUM PAYABLE TO BPIFAE.ROYAL CARIBBEAN CRUISES - LOAN, ONCE ASSIGNED TO CO ON DELIVERY OF SHIP, WILL AMORTIZE SEMI-ANNUALLY AND WILL MATURE 12 YEARS THEREAFTER.
