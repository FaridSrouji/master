Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on January 6, 2020. The Board of Directors of Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. declared a quarterly dividend of $0.78 per common share payable on January 6, 2020, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 20, 2019.
