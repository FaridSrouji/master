Runway to Gangway, Fashion Industry Exec Named CMO for Celebrity Cruises. MIAMI, JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CELEBRITY CRUISES ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FORMER TOP FASHION HOUSE MARKETER MICHAEL SCHEINER HAS BEEN APPOINTED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER OF THE NEW-LUXURY TRAVEL COMPANY. REPORTING TO PRESIDENT AND CEO LISA LUTOFF-PERLO, SCHEINER WILL JOIN CELEBRITY ON JULY 12, AND WILL OVERSEE ALL ASPECTS OF DRIVING NEW CUSTOMER ACQUISITION, BRAND LOYALTY AND E-COMMERCE GLOBALLY.
