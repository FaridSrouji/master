Royal Caribbean Cruises Says Co Entered Into Credit Agreement For Financing Of Third Icon-Class Ship. ROYAL CARIBBEAN CRUISES - ENTERED INTO CREDIT AGREEMENT FOR FINANCING OF THIRD ICON-CLASS SHIP FOR ROYAL CARIBBEAN INTERNATIONAL.ROYAL CARIBBEAN CRUISES LTD - AGREEMENT MAKES AVAILABLE TO CO AN UNSECURED U.S. DOLLAR-DENOMINATED TERM LOAN.ROYAL CARIBBEAN CRUISES LTD - SUBSTANTIAL MAJORITY OF UNSECURED U.S. DOLLAR-DENOMINATED TERM LOAN IS TO BE GUARANTEED 95% BY FINNVERA PLC.ROYAL CARIBBEAN CRUISES LTD - EULER HERMES HAS ALSO AGREED TO PROVIDE 95% GUARANTEE OF A SMALLER PORTION OF FINANCING.
