Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 3,186.850 million compared to USD 2,796.187 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 890.792 million compared to USD 799.733 million a year ago. Net income was USD 883.240 million compared to USD 810.391 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 4.21 compared to USD 3.88 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.2 compared to USD 3.86 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 8,433.248 million compared to USD 7,161.548 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,783.276 million compared to USD 1,530.774 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,605.751 million compared to USD 1,495.339 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 7.67 compared to USD 7.08 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 7.65 compared to USD 7.05 a year ago.
