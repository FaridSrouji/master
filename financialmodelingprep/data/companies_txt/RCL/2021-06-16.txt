An Icon Is Born As Royal Caribbean Starts Construction On Revolutionary Ship. MIAMI, JUNE 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AN ICONIC DAY FOR THE CRUISE INDUSTRY'S NEXT ICONIC SHIP TOOK PLACE ON MONDAY, JUNE 14  WHEN ROYAL CARIBBEAN INTERNATIONAL MARKED THE START OF CONSTRUCTION ON ITS FIRST ICON CLASS SHIP. TO CELEBRATE THE MILESTONE, THE WORLD'S LARGEST CRUISE LINE HELD A STEEL-CUTTING CEREMONY AT FINNISH SHIPYARD MEYER TURKU, WHERE THE REVOLUTIONARY SHIP'S NAME WAS REVEALED AS ICON OF THE SEAS.
