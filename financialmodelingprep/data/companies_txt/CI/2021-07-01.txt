President George W. Bush Joined Cigna Corporation CEO David Cordani to Honor American Veterans and Support the Achilles International Freedom Team. WASHINGTON, JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CIGNA CORPORATION CEO DAVID CORDANI HOSTED AN EXCLUSIVE FIRESIDE CONVERSATION WITH PRESIDENT GEORGE W. BUSH ON WEDNESDAY TO HONOR AMERICAN VETERANS AND SUPPORT ATHLETES ON THE ACHILLES INTERNATIONAL FREEDOM TEAM, WHICH IS COMPOSED OF WOUNDED MEMBERS OF THE U.S. MILITARY.
