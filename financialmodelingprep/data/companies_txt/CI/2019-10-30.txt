Cigna Corp. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Cigna Corp. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 38,556 million compared to USD 11,457 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,123 million compared to USD 1,132 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,351 million compared to USD 772 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.6 compared to USD 3.18 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.57 compared to USD 3.14 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 115,321 million compared to USD 34,350 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 6,516 million compared to USD 3,601 million a year ago. Net income was USD 4,127 million compared to USD 2,493 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 10.94 compared to USD 10.28 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 10.83 compared to USD 10.14 a year ago.
