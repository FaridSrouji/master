West Plains and Evergy, Inc. Announce Solar Energy Agreement. The City of West Plains, Mo., will partner with Evergy, Inc. to provide solar power to the community. Evergy will build a new solar array in West Plains that will generate 9.6 megawatts of energy, enough to power 1,200 homes.
