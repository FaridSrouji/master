Evergy, Inc. Declared Dividend on Common Stock, Payable on December 20, 2019. The Board of Directors of Evergy, Inc. declared a dividend on the company’s common stock of $0.505 per share payable on December 20, 2019. The dividends are payable to shareholders of record as of November 27, 2019.
