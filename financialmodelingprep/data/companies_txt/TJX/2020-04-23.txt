TJX Companies Inc Says CEO Ernie Herrman's FY 2020 Total Compensation Was $19.1 Mln. TJX COMPANIES INC SAYS CEO ERNIE HERRMAN'S FY 2020 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $19.1 MILLION VERSUS $18.8 MILLION IN FY 2019 - SEC FILING.TJX COMPANIES INC SAYS EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN CAROL MEYROWITZ'S FY 2020 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $10.5 MILLION VERSUS $11.3 MILLION IN FY 2019.TJX COMPANIES INC SAYS CFO SCOTT GOLDENBERG'S FY 2020 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $6.7 MILLION VERSUS $6.4 MILLION IN FY 2019.TJX COMPANIES INC SAYS FOR FY 2020, CEO PAY RATIO WAS 1,590:1.
