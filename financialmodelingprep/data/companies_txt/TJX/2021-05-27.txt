The TJX Companies, Inc. Reinstates Share Repurchase Program, Announces Quarterly Common Stock Dividend, and Updates Q2 FY22 Sales Trends. FRAMINGHAM, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE:TJX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS REINSTATED ITS SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM. THE COMPANY PLANS TO REPURCHASE APPROXIMATELY $1.0 BILLION TO $1.25 BILLION OF TJX STOCK DURING THE FISCAL YEAR ENDING JANUARY 29, 2022, AND CURRENTLY HAS APPROXIMATELY $3.0 BILLION REMAINING UNDER ITS EXISTING STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAMS.
