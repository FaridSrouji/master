The TJX Companies, Inc. Announces 13% Increase in Its Quarterly Common Stock Dividend. FRAMINGHAM, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE:TJX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE DECLARATION OF A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON ITS COMMON STOCK OF $.26 PER SHARE PAYABLE MARCH 4, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 11, 2021. THIS REPRESENTS A 13% INCREASE IN THE PER SHARE DIVIDEND COMPARED TO THE COMPANY'S PREVIOUS DIVIDEND PAID IN MARCH 2020. ABOUT THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. THE TJX COMPANIES, INC. IS THE LEADING OFF-PRICE RETAILER OF APPAREL AND HOME FASHIONS IN THE U.S. AND WORLDWIDE
