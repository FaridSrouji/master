Leidos Awarded $470.7 Million TSA Contract to Provide Checkpoint Screening Equipment Deployment Services. RESTON, VA., JUNE 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS (NYSE:LDOS), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, WAS RECENTLY AWARDED A  PRIME CONTRACT BY THE TRANSPORTATION SECURITY AUTHORITY (TSA) TO PROVIDE SERVICES RELATED TO THE DEPLOYMENT OF TRANSPORTATION SCREENING EQUIPMENT (TSE).
