Leidos Holdings, Inc. Revises Earnings Guidance for Fiscal Year 2019. Leidos Holdings, Inc. revised earnings guidance for fiscal year 2019. For the year, the company expected revenues of $10.90 billion to $11.00 billion, up from previous guidance of $10.65 billion to $10.95 billion; Non-GAAP diluted EPS of $4.90 to $5.10, up from previous guidance of $4.50 to $4.75.
