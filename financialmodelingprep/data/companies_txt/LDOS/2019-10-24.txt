Leidos Holdings, Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 27, 2019. Leidos Holdings, Inc. announced that its board of directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.34 per outstanding share of common stock of Leidos Holdings, Inc. The cash dividend is payable on Dec. 27, 2019 to stockholders of record as of the close of business on Dec. 16, 2019.
