LDOS Class Action Alert: Kessler Topaz Meltzer & Check, LLP Announces A Securities Fraud Class Action Filed Against Leidos Holdings, Inc.. RADNOR, PA., MARCH 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE LAW FIRM OF KESSLER TOPAZ MELTZER & CHECK, LLP ANNOUNCES THAT A SECURITIES FRAUD CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE SOUTHERN DISTRICT OF NEW YORK AGAINST LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: LDOS) ("LEIDOS") ON BEHALF OF THOSE WHO PURCHASED OR ACQUIRED LEIDOS SECURITIES BETWEEN MAY 4, 2020 AND FEBRUARY 23, 2021 , INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
