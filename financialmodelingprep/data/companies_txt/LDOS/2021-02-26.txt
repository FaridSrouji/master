Bronstein, Gewirtz & Grossman, LLC Notifies Investors of Leidos Holdings, Inc. (LDOS) Investigation. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BRONSTEIN, GEWIRTZ & GROSSMAN, LLC IS INVESTIGATING POTENTIAL CLAIMS ON BEHALF OF PURCHASERS OF LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. ("LEIDOS" OR "THE COMPANY") (NYSE: LDOS). INVESTORS WHO PURCHASED LEIDOS SECURITIES ARE ENCOURAGED TO OBTAIN ADDITIONAL INFORMATION AND ASSIST THE INVESTIGATION BY VISITING THE FIRM'S SITE: WWW.BGANDG.COM/LDOS. THE INVESTIGATION CONCERNS WHETHER LEIDOS AND CERTAIN OF ITS OFFICERS AND/OR DIRECTORS HAVE VIOLATED FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS. ON FEBRUARY 1
