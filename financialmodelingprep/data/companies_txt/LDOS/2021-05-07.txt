Leidos completes acquisition of Gibbs & Cox. RESTON, VA., MAY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEIDOS HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: LDOS) ("LEIDOS"), A FORTUNE® 500 SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETED ACQUISITION OF GIBBS & COX, INC. ("GIBBS & COX") FOR APPROXIMATELY $380 MILLION IN CASH.
