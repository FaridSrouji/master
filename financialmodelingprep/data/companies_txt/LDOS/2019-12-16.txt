Department Of Energy Awards Leidos Hanford Mission Essential Services Contract. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY AWARDS LEIDOS HANFORD MISSION ESSENTIAL SERVICES CONTRACT.LEIDOS HOLDINGS - CONTRACT HAS A 5-YEAR BASE PERIOD OF PERFORMANCE FOLLOWED BY ONE TWO-YEAR OPTION PERIOD AND A FINAL OPTION PERIOD OF THREE YEARS.LEIDOS HOLDINGS- CONTRACT HAS APPROXIMATE VALUE OF $4 BILLION, IF ALL OPTIONS ARE EXERCISED.
