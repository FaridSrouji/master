Aes Announces Sale Of Itabo Generation Facility In The Dominican Republic. AES ANNOUNCES SALE OF ITABO GENERATION FACILITY IN THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC.AES CORP - NET EQUITY PROCEEDS TO AES FROM SALE OF ITS ENTIRE INTEREST IN ITABO ARE APPROXIMATELY $101 MILLION.AES CORP - AGREEMENT TO SELL 100% OF ITS EQUITY INTEREST IN 295 MW ITABO POWER PLANT IN SAN CRISTOBAL, DOMINICAN REPUBLIC TO GRUPO LINDA.AES CORP - REAFFIRMING ITS 2020 ADJUSTED EPS GUIDANCE OF $1.32 TO $1.42.AES CORP - REAFFIRMING 2020 PARENT FREE CASH FLOW EXPECTATION.AES CORP - REAFFIRMING EXPECTATION FOR AVERAGE ANNUAL GROWTH OF 7% TO 9% IN ADJUSTED EPS AND PARENT FREE CASH FLOW THROUGH 2022.
