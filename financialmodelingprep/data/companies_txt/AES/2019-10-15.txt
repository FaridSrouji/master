The AES Corporation Declares Quarterly Common Stock Dividend, Payable on November 15, 2019. The board of directors of The AES Corporation declared a quarterly common stock dividend of $0.1365 per share payable on November 15, 2019, to shareholders of record at the close of business on November 1, 2019.
