Ppl Corporation Reports Second-Quarter 2020 Earnings. ORATION REPORTS SECOND-QUARTER 2020 EARNINGS.REAFFIRMS FY 2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $2.40 TO $2.60 FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS.REAFFIRMS 2020 EARNINGS FROM ONGOING OPERATIONS FORECAST RANGE OF $2.40 TO $2.60 PER SHARE.WITHDRAWS GUIDANCE FOR 2021.QTRLY GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.45.QTRLY NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.55.QTRLY OPERATING REVENUES $1,739 MILLION VERSUS $1,803 MILLION.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $0.55 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.EXPECTS FULL-YEAR IMPACTS OF PANDEMIC TO BE MANAGEABLE.
PPL Corp To Sell U.K. Business. PRESS RELEASE - PPL CORPORATION LAUNCHES PROCESS TO SELL U.K. BUSINESS, REPOSITION ITSELF AS U.S.-FOCUSED UTILITY COMPANY.PPL CORP - EXPECTS TO EVALUATE A VARIETY OF OFFERS FOR PURCHASE OF WPD, INCLUDING ALL CASH OR A COMBINATION OF CASH AND U.S. UTILITY ASSETS.PPL CORP - ENGAGED JP MORGAN SECURITIES LLC TO ACT AS FINANCIAL ADVISOR TO ASSIST WITH SALE PROCESS, WITH INTENT TO ANNOUNCE TRANSACTION IN H1 2021.
