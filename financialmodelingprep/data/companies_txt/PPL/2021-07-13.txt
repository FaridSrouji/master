PPL recognized as a "Best Place to Work for Disability Inclusion". ALLENTOWN, PA., JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PPL CORPORATION IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE IT HAS RECEIVED A TOP SCORE OF 100% ON THE 2021 DISABILITY EQUALITY INDEX (DEI), EARNING IT THE DESIGNATION AS ONE OF THE BEST PLACES TO WORK FOR PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES.
