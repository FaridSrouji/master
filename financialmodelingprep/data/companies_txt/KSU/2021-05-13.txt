Kansas City Southern Receives Revised Proposal from Canadian National Railway That Board of Directors Determines is a "Company Superior Proposal". KANSAS CITY, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN (NYSE: KSU) (“KCS”) TODAY ANNOUNCED RECEIPT OF A REVISED ACQUISITION PROPOSAL FROM CANADIAN NATIONAL RAILWAY COMPANY (TSX: CNR, NYSE: CNI) (“CN”). UNDER THE TERMS OF CN'S REVISED PROPOSAL, EACH SHARE OF KCS COMMON STOCK WOULD BE EXCHANGED FOR $200 IN CASH AND 1.129 SHARES OF CN COMMON STOCK.
