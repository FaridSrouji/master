Kansas City Southern Announces New Capital Allocation Policy. KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN ANNOUNCES NEW CAPITAL ALLOCATION POLICY.KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN - APPROVED INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON KCS'S COMMON STOCK FROM $0.36 TO $0.40 PER SHARE.KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN - APPROVED NEW $2.0 BILLION SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM, EXPIRING DECEMBER 31, 2022.KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN - NEW PROGRAM REPLACES $800 MILLION STOCK REPURCHASE ANNOUNCED IN 2017 UNDER WHICH COMPANY PURCHASED ABOUT $741 MILLION OF COMPANY STOCK.KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN - INTENDS TO DEPLOY APPROXIMATELY 40-50% OF AVAILABLE CASH TO CAPITAL PROJECTS AND STRATEGIC INVESTMENTS.KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN - INTENDS TO DEPLOY APPROXIMATELY 50-60% OF AVAILABLE CASH TO SHARE REPURCHASES AND DIVIDENDS.
Kansas City Southern Declares Quarterly Common Stock Dividend, Payable on January 22, 2020; Declares 4% Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Payable on January 21, 2020. Kansas City Southern announced an increase in the quarterly dividend on KCS’s common stock from $0.36 to $0.40 per share. The board declared a common stock dividend for this increased amount payable on January 22, 2020, to stockholders of record at the close of business on December 31, 2019, and the directors also declared a regular dividend of $0.25 per share on the outstanding KCS 4% Non-Cumulative Preferred stock. This dividend is payable on January 21, 2020, to stockholders of record at the close of business on December 31, 2019.
