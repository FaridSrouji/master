Kansas City Southern Announces 20th Anniversary Holiday Express Fundraising Results to Benefit Salvation Army in 20 Communities. KANSAS CITY, MO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KANSAS CITY SOUTHERN (KCS) (NYSE: KSU) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY KCS HOLIDAY EXPRESS PROGRAM IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE SALVATION ARMY HAS RAISED OVER $277,000 TO BENEFIT THE SALVATION ARMY IN 20 COMMUNITIES ON ITS U.S. RAIL NETWORK.
