Microchip Technology Announces Changes to Its Board of Directors. CHANDLER, ARIZ., MARCH 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- (NASDAQ: MCHP) – MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INCORPORATED, A LEADING PROVIDER OF SMART, CONNECTED AND SECURE EMBEDDED CONTROL SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KARLTON JOHNSON, CURRENTLY THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF DELAINE STRATEGY GROUP LLC, WILL JOIN THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MICROCHIP EFFECTIVE APRIL 1, 2021.
Microchip Releases the First IEEE 802.3bt Power over Ethernet to USB Type-C® Power and Data Adapter.
