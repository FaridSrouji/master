Microchip Technology Incorporated Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 5, 2019. Microchip Technology Incorporated announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend on its common stock of 36.65 cents per share. The dividend is payable on December 5, 2019 to stockholders of record on November 21, 2019. Microchip initiated quarterly cash dividend payments in the third quarter of fiscal year 2003 and has increased its dividend 61 times since its inception.
