Microchip Technology Incorporated Announces Note Exchanges. MICROCHIP TECHNOLOGY INCORPORATED ANNOUNCES NOTE EXCHANGES.ENTERED PRIVATELY-NEGOTIATED AGREEMENTS WITH CERTAIN HOLDERS OUTSTANDING 1.625% CONVERTIBLE SENIOR SUBORDINATED NOTES DUE 2025.TO EXCHANGE $615.0 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF NOTES,ACCRUED,UNPAID INTEREST THEREON FOR CASH, SHARES PAR VALUE $0.001 PER SHARE.ESTIMATES IT WILL PAY AGGREGATE OF ABOUT $615 MILLION IN CASH PLUS ACCRUED INTEREST,ISSUE ABOUT 3.1 MILLION SHARES OF STOCK FOR NOTES.
