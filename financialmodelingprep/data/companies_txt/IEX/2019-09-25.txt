IDEX Corporation Declares Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on October 31, 2019. IDEX Corporation announced that its Board of Directors has approved a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.50 per common share. The next dividend will be paid October 31, 2019 to shareholders of record as of October 16, 2019.
