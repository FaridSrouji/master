NORTHBROOK, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IDEX CORPORATION (NYSE: IEX) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE THREE MONTH PERIOD ENDED MARCH 31, 2021.
