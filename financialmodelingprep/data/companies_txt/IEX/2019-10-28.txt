Idex Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. IDEX Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 624.246 million compared to USD 622.888 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 141.765 million compared to USD 145.133 million a year ago. Net income was USD 105.194 million compared to USD 106.352 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.39 compared to USD 1.39 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.37 compared to USD 1.37 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 1,888.576 million compared to USD 1,869.572 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 444.830 million compared to USD 429.647 million a year ago. Net income was USD 328.671 million compared to USD 312.436 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 4.34 compared to USD 4.07 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.3 compared to USD 4.02 a year ago.
