Cummins Inc. Launches Cummins Water Works. INDIANAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) ANNOUNCED A NEW GLOBAL COMMUNITY PROGRAM CALLED CUMMINS WATER WORKS, WHICH ADDRESSES THE GLOBAL WATER CRISIS BY PARTNERING WITH LEADING WATER EXPERTS AND INVESTING AND ENGAGING IN SUSTAINABLE, LARGE-SCALE, HIGH-IMPACT WATER PROJECTS AROUND THE WORLD. THIS NEW COMMUNITY PROGRAM IS SUPPORTED INITIALLY BY $8 MILLION IN CUMMINS GRANTS FOCUSING ON FIVE OF THE MOST WATER-STRESSED COUNTRIES: MEXICO, BRAZIL, INDIA, SOUTH AFRICA AND THE U.S.
