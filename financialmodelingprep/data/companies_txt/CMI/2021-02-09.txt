Cummins Inc. Declares Quarterly Common Stock Dividend. COLUMBUS, IND.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CUMMINS INC. (NYSE: CMI) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK CASH DIVIDEND OF 1.35 DOLLARS PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON MARCH 4, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON FEBRUARY 19, 2021. ABOUT CUMMINS INC. CUMMINS INC., A GLOBAL POWER LEADER, IS A CORPORATION OF COMPLEMENTARY BUSINESS SEGMENTS THAT DESIGN, MANUFACTURE, DISTRIBUTE AND SERVICE A BROAD PORTFOLIO OF POWER SOLUTIONS.
