Cummins Successful In Recent Patent Action Against Turbo Lab. CUMMINS SUCCESSFUL IN RECENT PATENT ACTION AGAINST TURBO LAB.CUMMINS - ANNOUNCES SUCCESSFUL LEGAL ACTIONS AGAINST TURBO LAB FOR INFRINGEMENT OF PATENTS BY UNAUTHORIZED MARKETING & SELLING OF TURBO NOZZLE RINGS.CUMMINS -  TURBO LAB AGREED TO CEASE AND DESIST PURCHASING, MAKING AND SELLING ANY NOZZLE RING PRODUCT THAT INFRINGES CUMMINS' PATENTS.CUMMINS -  TURBO LAB AGREED TO DESTROY ALL EXISTING INFRINGING STOCK.
