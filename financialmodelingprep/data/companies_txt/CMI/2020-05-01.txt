Cummins Increases Revolver Capacity By $2 Billion. CUMMINS INCREASES REVOLVER CAPACITY BY $2 BILLION.CUMMINS INC - ENTERED INTO AGREEMENT PROVIDING FOR ADDITIONAL $2 BILLION REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY THAT TERMINATES ON APRIL 30, 2021.CUMMINS INC - ENDED Q1 WITH CASH, CASH EQUIVALENTS AND MARKETABLE SECURITIES OF $2 BILLION AND COMMITTED BORROWING CAPACITY OF $1.9 BILLION.
