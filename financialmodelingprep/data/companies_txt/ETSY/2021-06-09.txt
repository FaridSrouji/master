Etsy Announces Pricing of $1.0 Billion of Convertible Senior Notes Offering. BROOKLYN, N.Y., JUNE 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ETSY, INC. (NASDAQ: ETSY), WHICH OPERATES TWO-SIDED ONLINE MARKETPLACES THAT CONNECT MILLIONS OF PASSIONATE AND CREATIVE BUYERS AND SELLERS AROUND THE WORLD, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF $1.0 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 0.25% CONVERTIBLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (THE "NOTES") IN A PRIVATE PLACEMENT TO QUALIFIED INSTITUTIONAL BUYERS PURSUANT TO RULE 144A UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED (THE "SECURITIES ACT").
