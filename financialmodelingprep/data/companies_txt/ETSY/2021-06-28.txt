Etsy to acquire Elo7, Brazil-based marketplace for unique, handmade items. BROOKLYN, N.Y., JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ETSY, INC. (NASDAQ: ETSY), WHICH OPERATES TWO-SIDED ONLINE MARKETPLACES THAT CONNECT MILLIONS OF PASSIONATE AND CREATIVE BUYERS AND SELLERS AROUND THE WORLD, HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE ELO7, A PRIVATELY HELD MARKETPLACE FOR UNIQUE, HANDMADE ITEMS, RANKED AS A TOP 10 ECOMMERCE SITE IN BRAZIL.
