Etsy Pledges Net Zero Carbon Emissions by 2030. BROOKLYN, N.Y., FEB. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ETSY, INC., WHICH OPERATES TWO-SIDED ONLINE MARKETPLACES THAT CONNECT MILLIONS OF PASSIONATE AND CREATIVE BUYERS AND SELLERS AROUND THE WORLD, ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS COMMITMENT TO ACHIEVING NET ZERO CARBON EMISSIONS BY 2030.
