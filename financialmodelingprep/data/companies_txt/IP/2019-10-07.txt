International Paper Declares Quarterly Common Stock and Preferred Stock Dividend for the Period from October 1, 2019, to December 31, 2019, Payable on December 16, 2019. International Paper declared a quarterly dividend of $0.5125 per share for the period from October 1, 2019, to December 31, 2019, inclusive, on its common stock, par value $1.00. This dividend is payable on December 16, 2019, to holders of record at the close of business on November 15, 2019. The company also declared a regular quarterly dividend of $1.00 per share for the period from October 1, 2019, to December 31, 2019, inclusive, on the cumulative $4.00 preferred stock of the company. This dividend is also payable on December 16, 2019, to holders of record at the close of business on November 15, 2019.
