International Paper Reaches Agreement To Sell Its Brazilian Corrugated Packaging Business To Klabin S.A.. INTERNATIONAL PAPER REACHES AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS BRAZILIAN CORRUGATED PACKAGING BUSINESS.ENTERED INTO AGREEMENT TO SELL ITS BRAZILIAN CORRUGATED PACKAGING BUSINESS TO KLABIN S.A. FOR R$330 MILLION.TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN SECOND HALF OF 2020.CO WILL CONTINUE TO RUN ITS PAPERS AND FORESTRY BUSINESSES IN BRAZIL.
