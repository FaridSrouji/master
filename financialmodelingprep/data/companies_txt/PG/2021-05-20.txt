Pampers & UNICEF: Pioneering Partnership Helps Save Estimated One Million Newborn Lives. LONDON, MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, PAMPERS AND UNICEF HOSTED AN INSIGHTFUL PANEL EVENT REFLECTING ON THEIR 15 YEARLONG PARTNERSHIP; A ONE-OF-A-KIND ALLIANCE THAT HARNESSED THE POWER OF WORKING TOGETHER TO ACHIEVE LASTING CHANGE. OVER THE LAST 15 YEARS, THE PARTNERSHIP HAS HELPED TO SAVE AN ESTIMATED ONE MILLION NEWBORN LIVES AROUND THE WORLD FROM MATERNAL AND NEONATAL TETANUS (MNT), A NEEDLESSLY FATAL DISEASE WHICH IS EASILY PREVENTABLE THROUGH IMMUNIZATION AND HYGIENIC BIRTH PRACTICES.
