P&G Appoints B. Marc Allen to Board of Directors. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY (NYSE:PG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED B. MARC ALLEN, CHIEF STRATEGY OFFICER AND SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF STRATEGY AND CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT AT THE BOEING COMPANY, TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS. THE APPOINTMENT IS EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 10, 2021. MR. ALLEN JOINED BOEING, A LEADING GLOBAL AEROSPACE AND DEFENSE COMPANY, IN 2007. IN HIS CURRENT ROLE, HE IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE COMPANY'S OVERARCHING STRATEGY
