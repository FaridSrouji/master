Vicks® Introduces New Options for Children. PROCTER & GAMBLE announced that Vicks is introducing its newest cold medication innovation specially formulated for kids. Vicks Children’s Cough & Congestion provides the relief kids need, without the artificial additives parents don’t want. Vicks Children’s products are free of artificial dyes and flavors, high fructose corn syrup and alcohol. The Vicks Children’s product lineup includes a variety of tasty options specially formulated for kids, which provide up to four hours of symptom relief and are all free of artificial dyes and flavors, high fructose corn syrup and alcohol, including: Multi-SymptomCold is an Orange flavor and provides children 6 years and older with effective relief from cold and flu symptoms, including fevers, aches, sore throats, coughs and stuffy noses. Non-Drowsy Cough & Congestion is a Strawberry flavor and provides children 4 years and older with relief from cold symptoms, including coughs, chest congestion and stuffy noses. Nighttime Cough & Congestion is a Grape flavor and provides children 6 years and older with relief of cold symptoms, including coughs, stuffy noses, runny noses and sneezing, so children – and one– can sleep.
