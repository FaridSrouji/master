Procter & Gamble Announces CFO Transition. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY (NYSE:PG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE FOLLOWING ORGANIZATION CHANGES WILL BECOME EFFECTIVE MARCH 1, 2021. ANDRE SCHULTEN, CURRENTLY SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT–BABY CARE, NORTH AMERICA, HAS BEEN ELECTED CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, SUCCEEDING JON MOELLER IN THAT CAPACITY. MR. SCHULTEN WILL REPORT TO DAVID TAYLOR, CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. MR. MOELLER RETAINS HIS ROLE AS VICE CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, LEA
