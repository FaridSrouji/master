P&G Ventures Announces Four Finalists in Latest Innovation Challenge. CINCINNATI, JUNE 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  P&G VENTURES , THE EARLY STAGE STARTUP STUDIO WITHIN P&G (NYSE:PG), HAS ANNOUNCED THE FOUR FINALISTS IN ITS LATEST INNOVATION CHALLENGE, WHICH WILL BE HELD ONLINE ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 14 AT 1PM ET. THESE FOUR FINALISTS WERE CHOSEN FROM A COMPETITIVE POOL OF APPLICANTS WHOSE PRODUCTS ARE SOLVING CONSUMER PAIN POINTS IN CATEGORIES SUCH AS ACTIVE AGING, SAFE AND EFFECTIVE GERM PROTECTION, NON-TOXIC HOME AND GARDEN, AS WELL AS OTHER CONSUMER PACKAGED GOODS SPACES WHERE P&G DOESN'T CURRENTLY COMPETE.
