The Procter & Gamble Company Declares Quarterly Dividend and Preferred Stocks. The Board of Directors of The Procter & Gamble Company declared a quarterly dividend of $0.7459 per share on the Common Stock and on the Series A and Series B ESOP Convertible Class A Preferred Stock of the Company, payable on or after November 15, 2019 to Common Stock shareholders of record at the close of business on October 18, 2019, and to Series A and Series B ESOP Convertible Class A Preferred Stock shareholders of record at the start of business on October 18, 2019.
