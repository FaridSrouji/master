Procter & Gamble Co. Launches A New Brand for Women Coping with Menopause. Procter & Gamble Co. launched a new brand for women coping with menopause. The direct-to-consumer brand is the first to rise from the partnership formed by the early-stage startup studio P&G Ventures and M13, a brand-development and investment company in Los Angeles. Kindra products include three dietary supplements and a vaginal lotion, all of which are available without prescription.
