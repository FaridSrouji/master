The Procter & Gamble Company Raises Earnings Guidance for the Fiscal Year 2020. The Procter & Gamble Company raised earnings guidance for the fiscal year 2020. The company raised its outlook for fiscal 2020 all-in sales growth from a range of 3% - 4% to a range of 3% - 5% growth versus the prior fiscal year. The company increased its guidance range for fiscal 2020 all-in GAAP diluted net earnings per share growth to 225% to 243%, noting that the comparison period is significantly depressed by the Gillette Shave Care impairment adjustments in fiscal 2019.
