P&G Says 2019–2020 Total Compensation For CEO Was $22.9 Mln. P&G - TOTAL COMPENSATION FOR 2019–20 FOR CEO DAVID TAYLOR WAS $22.9 MILLION VERSUS $20.5 MILLION.P&G - TOTAL COMPENSATION FOR 2019–20 FOR CFO JON MOELLER WAS $11.61 MILLION VERSUS $9.48 MILLION.P&G - FOR FY 2019-20, RATIO OF ANNUAL TOTAL COMPENSATION OF CEO TAYLOR TO MEDIAN OF ANNUAL TOTAL COMPENSATION OF EMPLOYEES WAS 333 TO 1.
