P&G Commits to Enable 2 Billion People to Adopt Healthy Oral Care Habits by 2030. CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PROCTER & GAMBLE (P&G) ORAL CARE SEEKS TO ADVANCE HEALTHY ORAL CARE HABITS TO TRANSFORM PEOPLE'S HEALTH WHILE REDUCING IMPACT ON THE PLANET THROUGH ITS NEW SUSTAINABILITY COMMITMENTS.
