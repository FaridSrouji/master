Perrigo Completes Divestiture Of Generic Rx Business For $1.55 Billion. DUBLIN, JULY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PERRIGO COMPANY PLC (NYSE; TASE: PRGO), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF CONSUMER SELF-CARE PRODUCTS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SALE OF ITS GENERIC RX PHARMACEUTICALS BUSINESS TO ALTARIS CAPITAL PARTNERS, LLC FOR $1.55 BILLION, WITH CUSTOMARY ADJUSTMENTS, COMPLETING PERRIGO'S TRANSFORMATION TO A GLOBAL CONSUMER SELF-CARE COMPANY.
