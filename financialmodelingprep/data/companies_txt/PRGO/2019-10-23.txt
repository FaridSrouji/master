Perrigo Company Issues Voluntary Worldwide Recall Of Ranitidine. PERRIGO COMPANY PLC ISSUES VOLUNTARY WORLDWIDE RECALL OF RANITIDINE DUE TO POSSIBLE PRESENCE OF IMPURITY, N-NITROSODIMETHYLAMINE (NDMA) IMPURITY IN THE PRODUCT.PERRIGO COMPANY PLC - RECALL IS BEING TAKEN DUE TO POSSIBLE PRESENCE OF A NITROSAMINE IMPURITY CALLED N-NITROSODIMETHYLAMINE.PERRIGO COMPANY - AFTER REGULATORY BODIES ANNOUNCED RANITIDINE MAY POTENTIALLY CONTAIN NDMA, CO BEGAN TESTING EXTERNALLY SOURCED RANITIDINE API, RANITIDINE-BASED PRODUCTS.PERRIGO COMPANY PLC - ON OCTOBER 8, 2019, PERRIGO HALTED SHIPMENTS OF PRODUCT BASED UPON PRELIMINARY RESULTS.PERRIGO COMPANY PLC - BASED ON TOTALITY OF DATA GATHERED TO DATE, PERRIGO HAS MADE DECISION TO CONDUCT THE VOLUNTARY RECALL.
