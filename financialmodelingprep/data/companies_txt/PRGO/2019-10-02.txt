Perrigo Company plc Announces the Relaunch of the AB Rated Generic Version of Transderm Scop 1.5 Mg. Perrigo Company plc announced the relaunch of the AB rated generic equivalent of Transderm Scop (scopolamine 1.5 mg). Annual market sales of Transderm Scop and therapeutic equivalents for the 12 months ending August 2019 were approximately $147 million.
