Booking Holdings Says Net Income In 2nd Quarter Was $2.97 Per Diluted Share. BOOKING HOLDINGS REPORTS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR 2ND QUARTER 2020.NET INCOME IN THE 2ND QUARTER OF 2020 WAS $2.97 PER DILUTED SHARE, AN 87% DECREASE AS COMPARED TO THE PRIOR YEAR.ROOM NIGHTS BOOKED IN THE 2ND QUARTER DECREASED 87% OVER A YEAR AGO.BOOKING HOLDINGS SAYS TOTAL REVENUES FOR 2ND QUARTER OF 2020 WERE $630 MILLION, AN 84% DECREASE FROM PRIOR YEAR.BOOKING HOLDINGS SAYS NON-GAAP NET LOSS IN 2ND QUARTER WAS $10.81 PER DILUTED SHARE.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $-11.50, REVENUE VIEW $569.3 MILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.BOOKING HOLDINGS SAYS SECOND QUARTER GROSS TRAVEL BOOKINGS WERE $2.3 BILLION, A DECREASE OF 91% OVER A YEAR AGO.BOOKING HOLDINGS SAYS ROOM NIGHTS BOOKED IN THE 2ND QUARTER DECREASED 87% OVER A YEAR AGO.
