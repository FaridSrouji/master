Published Data Demonstrate Sexual Function Improvement and Preservation with the Minimally Invasive UroLift® System Compared to Medications Prescribed for Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia. WAYNE, PA., JAN. 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TELEFLEX INCORPORATED (NYSE: TFX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT NEW DATA PUBLISHED IN THE PEER-REVIEWED JOURNAL, EUROPEAN UROLOGY FOCUS , FOUND THAT MEN TREATED WITH THE PROSTATIC URETHRAL LIFT (PUL) PROCEDURE (USING THE UROLIFT® SYSTEM) SHOWED PRESERVATION AND SOME IMPROVEMENT OF SEXUAL FUNCTION COMPARED TO MEN BEING TREATED WITH MEDICATION FOR BENIGN PROSTATIC HYPERPLASIA (BPH).
