T-Mobile Rolls Out Apprenticeship Program Offering Industry-Leading Career Opportunities to Nevadans–and Beyond. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, T-MOBILE ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY LAUNCHED ITS NEW SIX-YEAR APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAM IN THE STATE OF NEVADA. CREATED IN COORDINATION WITH THE NEVADA ATTORNEY GENERAL'S OFFICE AND THE NEVADA GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF WORKFORCE INNOVATION AND THE NEVADA STATE APPRENTICESHIP COUNCIL, THE PROGRAM HAS ALREADY FILLED TEN PAID APPRENTICESHIP ROLES ON ITS CARE AND TECHNOLOGY TEAMS, INCLUDING SEVEN AT ITS CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE CENTER IN LAS VEGAS.
