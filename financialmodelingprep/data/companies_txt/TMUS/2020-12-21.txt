WFB (Bus), Anyone? T-Mobile Turns Red Rose Transit Buses into Free Wi-Fi Hubs for Lancaster, PA Riders. BELLEVUE, WASH. & LANCASTER, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GET READY LANCASTER COMMUTERS, T-MOBILE (NASDAQ: TMUS) AND RED ROSE TRANSIT AUTHORITY (RRTA) HAVE JUST LEVELED-UP YOUR RIDE WITH A MUCH-REQUESTED AMENITY: FREE MOBILE WI-FI. RRTA'S ENTIRE FLEET OF BUSES, SERVING THE CITY OF LANCASTER AND LANCASTER COUNTY, ARE NOW EQUIPPED WITH WI-FI HOTSPOTS, POWERED BY T-MOBILE'S 4G LTE AND 5G NETWORK.
