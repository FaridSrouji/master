T-Mobile Brings Ultra Capacity 5G to Miami U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Hospital. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--T-MOBILE (NASDAQ: TMUS) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS DEPLOYED ULTRA CAPACITY 5G AT THE MIAMI VETERANS AFFAIRS (VA) HEALTHCARE SYSTEM, BRINGING INCREDIBLE 5G SPEED AND PERFORMANCE TO MEDICAL STAFF AND PATIENTS WITH CAPABLE DEVICES. T-MOBILE CURRENTLY PROVIDES IN-BUILDING 4G LTE WIRELESS CONNECTIVITY ACROSS MORE THAN 50 VA HEALTHCARE SYSTEMS WITH MORE TO COME THIS YEAR.
