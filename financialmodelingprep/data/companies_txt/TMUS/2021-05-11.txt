Let Us Bring It To You. T-Mobile, Healium and Honor Flight Network Virtually Transport Veterans to D.C.. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IT'S MILITARY APPRECIATION MONTH, AND WE HONOR THOSE WHO HAVE SERVED OUR COUNTRY. T-MOBILE (NASDAQ: TMUS) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS TEAMING UP WITH HEALIUM TO BRING A VIRTUAL HONOR FLIGHT EXPERIENCE TO VETERANS LIVING IN SMALL TOWNS ACROSS THE COUNTRY, TRANSPORTING THEM WITH THE POWER OF VIRTUAL REALITY (VR) AND 5G TO VISIT THE WASHINGTON D.C.
