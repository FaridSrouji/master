T-Mobile Says Standalone Sprint Corp Prelim Q1 Postpaid Net Additions Were 502,000. SAYS PROVIDING STANDALONE SPRINT CORPORATION CUSTOMER RESULTS FOR QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2020.SAYS STANDALONE SPRINT CORP TOTAL WIRELESS NET ADDITIONS OF 538,000 FOR QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2020.SAYS STANDALONE SPRINT CORP PRELIM Q1 POSTPAID NET ADDITIONS WERE 502,000.SAYS STANDALONE SPRINT CORP PRELIM Q1 POSTPAID CHURN OF 1.86 PERCENT INCREASED 5 BASIS POINTS.SAYS STANDALONE SPRINT CORP PRELIM Q1 PREPAID NET LOSSES WERE 10,000, IMPROVEMENT OF ABOUT 20,000.
