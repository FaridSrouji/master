General Mills Quarterly Dividend Declared. MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE GENERAL MILLS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND AT THE PREVAILING RATE OF $0.51 PER SHARE, PAYABLE MAY 3, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 9, 2021. THE CURRENT QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE REPRESENTS A 4 PERCENT INCREASE FROM THE $0.49 PER SHARE RATE PAID A YEAR AGO. GENERAL MILLS (NYSE: GIS) AND ITS PREDECESSOR COMPANY HAVE PAID DIVIDENDS WITHOUT INTERRUPTION FOR 122 YEARS. ABOUT GENERAL MILLS GENERAL MILLS IS A LEADING GLOBAL FOOD COM
