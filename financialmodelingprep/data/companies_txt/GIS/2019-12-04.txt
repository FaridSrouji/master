General Mills, Inc. Elects Mark Pallot as Vice President, Chief Accounting Officer and Principal Accounting Officer, Effective February 1, 2020. On December 2, 2019, the board of directors of General Mills, Inc. elected Mark Pallot as Vice President, Chief Accounting Officer effective February 1, 2020. In this role, Mr. Pallot will serve as the Company's principal accounting officer. Mr. Pallot, age 46, has served as the Company's Vice President, Assistant Controller since August 2017. Prior to that, Mr. Pallot served as the Company's Director of Financial Reporting from 2007 to July 2017.
