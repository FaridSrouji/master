General Mills and Union Strike Agreement on New Contract. General Mills and the union representing 520 workers at its plant in Cedar Rapids, Iowa, they reached a tentative deal on a new contract. The agreement came after workers on 06 November 2019 voted to reject the company's previous offer. Union leaders cited inadequate protections on scheduling the use of third-party contractors for the rejection. The workers will vote on the new deal on 07 November 2019.
