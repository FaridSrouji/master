Lamb Weston Holdings Raises Quarterly Dividend. LAMB WESTON HOLDINGS RAISES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.LAMB WESTON HOLDINGS INC - DECLARED QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.23 PER SHARE OF CO, ABOUT 15% INCREASE FROM $0.20 PER SHARE IN PRIOR QUARTER.LAMB WESTON HOLDINGS INC - STATED THAT IT WILL CONTINUE TO TARGET ANNUAL DIVIDEND PAYOUT RATIO OF 25% TO 35% OF ADJUSTED DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE.
