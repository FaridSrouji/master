Lamb Weston Introduces Packaging Made From Sustainable Biomaterial. EAGLE, IDAHO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #ALEXIA--DRIVING TOWARD SUSTAINABLE MATERIALS IN PACKAGING, TWO LAMB WESTON ALEXIA BRAND PRODUCTS WILL USE CORN AND POTATO STARCH IN THEIR PACKAGING.
