Declaration of Voting Results by Lamb Weston. On September 27, 2019, Lamb Weston Holdings, Inc. announced that at its annual meeting of shareholders held on September 26, 2019, a shareholder proposal requesting that the Board disclose at regular intervals, at reasonable expense and omitting proprietary or privileged information, available quantitative metrics on pesticide use in the Company's supply chain, and how pesticide use impacts pollinators was not approved.
Lamb Weston Holdings Sets Quarterly Dividend Of $0.20 Per Share. SETS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.20PER SHARE.
