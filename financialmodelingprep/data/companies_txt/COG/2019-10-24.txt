Cabot Oil & Gas Corporation Provides Its Fourth Quarter 2019 Production Guidance. Cabot Oil & Gas Corporation provides its fourth quarter 2019 production guidance. For the period, the company expects range of 2,375 to 2,425 Mmcfe per day. The Company has also updated its full-year 2019 production growth guidance to 17%, which is the midpoint of the prior guidance range of 16% to 18%.
