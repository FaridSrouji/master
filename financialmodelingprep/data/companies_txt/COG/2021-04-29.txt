Cabot Oil & Gas Corporation Reports First Quarter 2021 Results, Increases Quarterly Base Dividend by 10 Percent. HOUSTON, APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CABOT OIL & GAS CORPORATION (NYSE: COG) ("CABOT" OR THE "COMPANY") TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. "RELATIVE TO THE PRIOR-YEAR PERIOD, CABOT'S FREE CASH FLOW AND ADJUSTED NET INCOME BOTH GREW BY OVER 175 PERCENT, HIGHLIGHTING THE COMPANY'S ABILITY TO DELIVER ROBUST FINANCIAL RESULTS IN A MORE NORMALIZED NATURAL GAS PRICE ENVIRONMENT," SAID DAN O.
