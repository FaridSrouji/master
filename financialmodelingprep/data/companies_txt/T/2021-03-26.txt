AT&T Declares Dividends on Common and Preferred Shares. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF AT&T INC. (NYSE: T) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.52 A SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON SHARES.
