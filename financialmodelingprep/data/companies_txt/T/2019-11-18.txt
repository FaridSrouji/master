Sony Pictures Entertainment Acquires At&T Stake In Game Show Network. SONY PICTURES ENTERTAINMENT ACQUIRES AT&T STAKE IN GAME SHOW NETWORK.AT&T INC - IN CONNECTION WITH TRANSACTION, AT&T RECEIVED APPROXIMATELY $500 MILLION.AT&T INC - FOLLOWING TRANSACTION, GAME SHOW NETWORK CHANNEL WILL CONTINUE TO BE CARRIED ON DIRECTV.AT&T INC - GAME SHOW NETWORK WILL CONTINUE TO BE MANAGED BY SONY PICTURES TELEVISION.AT&T INC - RECEIVED ABOUT $500 MILLION, INCLUDING PROCEEDS FOR EQUITY STAKE VALUED AT $380 MILLION AND DIVIDENDS OF ABOUT $130 MILLION.AT&T INC - MARK FELDMAN WILL CONTINUE AS PRESIDENT AND CEO OF GAME SHOW NETWORK.
Sony Pictures Entertainment Inc. acquired remaining 42% stake in Game Show Network, LLC from AT&T Inc. (NYSE:T) for $380 million.. Sony Pictures Entertainment Inc. acquired remaining 42% stake in Game Show Network, LLC from AT&T Inc. (NYSE:T) for $380 million on November 18, 2019. In connection with the transaction, AT&T received approximately $500 million, including proceeds for its equity stake valued at $380 million (before transaction-related adjustments) and dividends of approximately $130 million. Game Show Network will continue to be managed by Sony Pictures Television (“SPT”) with Mark Feldman continuing as President and Chief Executive Officer of the multimedia outlet. Thomas Yang, Alison Chen and Rolf Zaiss of Akin Gump acted as legal advisor for AT&T.
Sony Pictures Entertainment Inc. completed the acquisition of remaining 42% stake in Game Show Network, LLC from AT&T Inc. (NYSE:T) on November 18, 2019.
