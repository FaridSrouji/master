Appaloosa LP Raises Share Stake In Wells Fargo, Cuts Share Stake In Tesla And Cuts Share Stake In Netflix. APPALOOSA LP RAISES SHARE STAKE IN WELLS FARGO & CO FROM 700,000 COMMON STOCK TO 1.9 MILLION COMMON STOCK - SEC FILING.APPALOOSA LP CUTS SHARE STAKE IN TESLA BY 83.3% TO 7,000 COMMON STOCK.APPALOOSA LP CUTS SHARE STAKE IN NETFLIX INC BY 9.8% TO 230,000 COMMON SHARES.APPALOOSA LP  TAKES SHARE STAKE OF 9.1 MILLION COMMON SHARES IN AT&T INC.APPALOOSA LP CUTS SHARE STAKE IN UNITEDHEALTH GROUP INC BY 46.0% TO 135,000 COMMON SHARES.APPALOOSA LP - CHANGE IN HOLDINGS ARE AS OF JUNE 30, 2020 AND COMPARED WITH THE PREVIOUS QUARTER ENDED AS OF MARCH 31, 2020.
Adage Capital Partners GP LLC Takes Share Stake In Tesla, Ups In AT&T. ADAGE CAPITAL PARTNERS GP LLC TAKES SHARE STAKE OF 17,076 SHARES IN TESLA INC SHARES - SEC FILING.ADAGE CAPITAL PARTNERS GP LLC CUTS SHARE STAKE IN BARRICK GOLD CORPORATION BY 30.8 PERCENT TO 4.5 MILLION SHARES.ADAGE CAPITAL PARTNERS GP LLC UPS SHARE STAKE IN AT&T INC BY 41.3 PERCENT TO 6.8 MILLION SHARES.ADAGE CAPITAL PARTNERS GP LLC - CHANGE IN HOLDINGS ARE AS OF JUNE 30, 2020 AND COMPARED WITH THE PREVIOUS QUARTER ENDED AS OF MARCH 31, 2020.
