At&T, CWA Agree To Southwest Wireline Contract Extension. AT&T, CWA AGREE TO SOUTHWEST WIRELINE CONTRACT EXTENSION.AT&T - AT&T SOUTHWEST WIRELINE OPERATIONS AND CWA DISTRICT 6 HAVE AGREED TO A 4-YEAR EXTENSION OF SOUTHWEST WIRELINE CONTRACT.AT&T INC - EXTENDED CONTRACT COVERS OVER 14,000 EMPLOYEES IN ARKANSAS, KANSAS, MISSOURI, OKLAHOMA AND TEXAS.AT&T INC - EXTENDED CONTRACT GOES INTO EFFECT ON APRIL 11, 2021 AND EXPIRES ON APRIL 12, 2025.AT&T INC - UNTIL APRIL 11, 2021, EMPLOYEES ARE CONTINUING TO WORK UNDER THEIR CURRENT CONTRACT.
