AT&T and Google Cloud Expand 5G and Edge Collaboration to Deliver Next-Generation Business Outcomes. SUNNYVALE, CALIF. AND DALLAS, JULY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, AT&T AND GOOGLE CLOUD ARE ANNOUNCING NEW SOLUTIONS ACROSS AT&T'S 5G AND GOOGLE CLOUD'S EDGE COMPUTING PORTFOLIO, INCLUDING AT&T'S ON-PREMISES MULTI-ACCESS EDGE COMPUTE (MEC) SOLUTION, AS WELL AS AT&T NETWORK EDGE CAPABILITIES THROUGH LTE, 5G, AND WIRELINE.
