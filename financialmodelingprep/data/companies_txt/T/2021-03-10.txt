AT&T Adds 5G to the First Nationwide Business-Focused Broadband Network. DALLAS, MARCH 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHAT'S NEW? AT&T* IS ADDING FIXED 5G WIRELESS SOLUTIONS TO THE FIRST NATIONWIDE BUSINESS-FOCUSED BROADBAND NETWORK, WHICH COMBINES OUR AT&T WIRELESS BROADBAND AND OUR LEADING BUSINESS FIBER NETWORK, NOW ENHANCED THIS WEEK WITH THE ADDITION OF NEW FIXED 5G WIRELESS SOLUTIONS.
