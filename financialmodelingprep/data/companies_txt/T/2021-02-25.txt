Jason Porter to Lead AT&T Public Sector and FirstNet. DALLAS, FEB. 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AT&T* NAMED JASON PORTER TO LEAD OUR PUBLIC SECTOR AND FIRSTNET ORGANIZATION.
