AT&T Provides Update On Capital Allocation Strategy, Increases Quarterly Dividend By 2 Percent. AT&T PROVIDES UPDATE ON CAPITAL ALLOCATION STRATEGY, INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND BY 2 PERCENT.AT&T INC - QUARTERLY DIVIDEND WILL INCREASE FROM $0.51 PER SHARE TO $0.52 PER SHARE.AT&T INC - ENTERED INTO A $4 BILLION ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE (ASR) AGREEMENT.AT&T INC - REMAINS ON TRACK TO HIT ITS 2019 NET DEBT-TO-ADJUSTED EBITDA RATIO TARGET IN 2.5X RANGE.AT&T INC - EXPECTS A LEVERAGE RATIO OF BETWEEN 2.0X TO 2.25X BY END OF 2022.
