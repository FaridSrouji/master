Labcorp Appoints Dr. Deborah Ann Sesok-Pizzini as Chief Medical Officer, Labcorp Diagnostics. BURLINGTON, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LABCORP, (NYSE: LH), A LEADING GLOBAL LIFE SCIENCES COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DR. DEBORAH ANN SESOK-PIZZINI HAS BEEN APPOINTED CHIEF MEDICAL OFFICER OF LABCORP DIAGNOSTICS, FOLLOWING THE RETIREMENT OF DR. DOT ADCOCK IN APRIL 2021. DR. SESOK-PIZZINI ASSUMED THE ROLE STARTING ON JANUARY 11, 2021. DR. SESOK-PIZZINI JOINS LABCORP WITH OVER TWO DECADES OF EXPERIENCE IN HEALTHCARE.
