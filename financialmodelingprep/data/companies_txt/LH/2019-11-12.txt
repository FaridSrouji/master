Sera Prognostics, Inc. announced that it has received $36 million in funding from Blue Ox Healthcare Partners, LLC, Domain Associates, L.L.C., InterWest Partners LLC, Laboratory Corporation of America Holdings, Catalyst Health Ventures, Chione Ltd. and other investors. On November 13, 2019, Sera Prognostics, Inc. closed the transaction. The company received $36,000,000 in the transaction. The transaction was led by new investor Blue Ox Healthcare Partners, LLC. The transaction included participation from existing investors Domain Associates, L.L.C., InterWest Partners LLC, Laboratory Corporation of America Holdings (NYSE:LH), Catalyst Health Ventures, Chione Ltd. along with other investors.
