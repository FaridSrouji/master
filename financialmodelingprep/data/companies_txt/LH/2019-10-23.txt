Laboratory Corp. of America Holdings Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Laboratory Corp. Of America Holdings announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,928.5 million compared to USD 2,831.3 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 339.9 million compared to USD 343.4 million a year ago. Net income was USD 220.7 million compared to USD 318.8 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.26 compared to USD 3.14 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.25 compared to USD 3.1 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 8,601.4 million compared to USD 8,545.9 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 993.8 million compared to USD 1,018 million a year ago. Net income was USD 596.7 million compared to USD 725.8 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 6.08 compared to USD 7.13 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 6.04 compared to USD 7.04 a year ago.
