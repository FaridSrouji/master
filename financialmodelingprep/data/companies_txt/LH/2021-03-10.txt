Circle Medical to Offer Labcorp's At Home COVID-19 Self-Collection Test and Announces Clinical Expansion to Austin and San Diego Markets. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CIRCLE MEDICAL, A DIGITAL-FIRST PRIMARY CARE PRACTICE, WILL BE OFFERING A CONVENIENT AT HOME COVID-19 SELF-COLLECTION TEST KIT IN PARTNERSHIP WITH LABCORP (NYSE: LH). TOGETHER WITH CIRCLE MEDICAL'S PRIMARY CARE TELEMEDICINE SERVICE, CIRCLE MEDICAL'S PATIENTS WILL NOW HAVE ACCESS TO AN END-TO-END COVID-19 TESTING SOLUTION THAT IS COVERED BY MOST INSURANCE PLANS. THIS HOME BASED COVID-19 SELF-COLLECTION KIT IS NOW AVAILABLE TO PATIENTS IN CALIFORNIA, NEW YORK, TEXA
