Labcorp Awarded CDC Contract to Study Variants of COVID-19 Virus and Improve Public Health Response. BURLINGTON, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LABCORP (NYSE: LH), A LEADING GLOBAL LIFE SCIENCES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN AWARDED A CONTRACT FROM THE CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION (CDC) TO PROVIDE GENOMIC SEQUENCING OF SAMPLES OF SARS-COV-2, THE VIRUS THAT CAUSES COVID-19. THIS SEQUENCING WILL AID THE CDC IN ITS GOAL OF CONDUCTING A LARGE-SCALE LONGITUDINAL GENOMIC SURVEY OF THE VIRUS USING A RANDOM SET OF SAMPLES COLLECTED FROM ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
