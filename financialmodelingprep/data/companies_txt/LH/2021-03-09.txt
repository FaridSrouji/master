Labcorp Named to Fast Company's List of the World's Most Innovative Companies for 2021. BURLINGTON, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LABCORP (NYSE: LH), A LEADING GLOBAL LIFE SCIENCES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO FAST COMPANY MAGAZINE'S LIST OF 2021 MOST INNOVATIVE COMPANIES. SINCE 2008, THE ANNUAL LIST HAS BEEN THE DEFINITIVE SOURCE FOR RECOGNIZING THE ORGANIZATIONS THAT ARE TRANSFORMING INDUSTRIES AND SHAPING SOCIETY. LABCORP WAS CITED FOR ITS LEADERSHIP IN SCIENCE, INNOVATION, AND HEALTHCARE IN THE FIGHT AGAINST COVID-19 BY ADVANCING TESTING, PARTNERING WITH BIOPHAR
