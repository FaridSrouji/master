Verizon Offers Enhanced Pay For Essential On-Site Employees. VERIZON OFFERS ENHANCED PAY FOR ESSENTIAL ON-SITE EMPLOYEES.70 PERCENT OF CORPORATE STORES NOW TEMPORARILY CLOSED NATIONWIDE.THOSE WHO CONTRACT VIRUS ARE ELIGIBLE FOR UP TO 26 WEEKS PAID LEAVE.UNDER PROGRAM, ELIGIBLE RETAIL EMPLOYEES TO GET INCREASE IN BASE HOURLY RATE WHEN WORKING IN CORPORATE-OWNED RETAIL LOCATION.UNDER PROGRAM, ELIGIBLE NETWORK, LOGISTICS, REAL ESTATE EMPLOYEES, INCLUDING FIELD TECHNICIANS, TO GET ENHANCEMENT ON TOP OF REGULAR BASE WAGES.FOR EMPLOYEES CARING FOR SOMEONE INFECTED, CO PROVIDES 8 WEEKS OF PAY AT FULL PAY, & IF NEEDED, ADDITIONAL WEEKS WITH 60% OF BASE WAGE.FOR THOSE ASKED TO STAY HOME DUE TO MEDICAL CONDITION CO GIVES 8 WEEKS OF PAY AT FULL PAY, IF NEEDED, ADDITIONAL WEEKS WITH 60% OF BASE WAGE.FOR EMPLOYEES WHO HAVE TROUBLE FINDING CHILDCARE, CO GIVES 8 WEEKS OF PAY AT FULL PAY, & IF NEEDED, ADDITIONAL WEEKS WITH 60% OF BASE WAGE.
