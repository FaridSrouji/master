Verizon's ‘Full Transparency' launches blockchain verification for news releases.             º  Verizon is implementing a blockchain-verified record of changes to its news releases            º  The Full Transparency initiative is a proof of concept built with open-source blockchain technology in partnership with Huge, MadNetwork and AdLedger            º  The Full Transparency initiative has the potential to change the way companies communicate to their audiences
