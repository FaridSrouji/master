Verizon announces acquisition of precise positioning company, Senion. BASKING RIDGE, N.J., JUNE 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TODAY, VERIZON ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF SENION , A GLOBAL LEADER IN LOCATION-RESPONSIVE SOLUTIONS. THE DEVELOPER OF AN INDOOR POSITIONING PLATFORM, SENION'S ADVANCED MACHINE-LEARNING SENSOR FUSION TECHNOLOGY DELIVERS SUB-METER ACCURACY FOR PRECISE POSITIONING AND NAVIGATION OF INDOOR SPACES.
