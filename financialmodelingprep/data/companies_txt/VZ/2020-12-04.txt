Verizon to speak at UBS Global TMT Conference December 7. ET. HIS REMARKS WILL BE WEBCAST, WITH ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS AVAILABLE ON VERIZON'S INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE, WWW.VERIZON.COM/ABOUT/INVESTORS/.
