Verizon Foundation makes $250,000 donation to Feeding Texas to aid in winter storm relief efforts. BASKING RIDGE, N.J., FEB. 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- FOLLOWING THIS WEEK'S HISTORIC WINTER STORMS, THE VERIZON FOUNDATION IS MAKING A $250,000 DONATION TO FEEDING TEXAS TO SUPPORT IMMEDIATE RELIEF EFFORTS IN COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE STATE. THE ORGANIZATION WILL USE THE FUNDS TO HELP RELIEVE HUNGER, AN ISSUE WHICH WAS FURTHER EXACERBATED BY THE STORMS.
