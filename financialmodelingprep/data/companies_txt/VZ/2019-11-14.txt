Verizon to Redeem Select Verizon InterNotes® Due 2028-2029. Verizon Communications Inc. announced that it will redeem the following outstanding notes (the “Notes”) on December 15, 2019 (the “Redemption Date”): 4.100%  Verizon InterNotes® due September 15, 2028 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,940,000; 4.050% Verizon InterNotes® due September 15, 2028 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,202,000; 4.050% Verizon InterNotes® due September 15, 2028 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,563,000; 4.100% Verizon InterNotes® due October 15, 2028 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,698,000; 4.200% Verizon InterNotes® due November 15, 2028 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,747,000; 4.200% Verizon InterNotes® due November 15, 2028 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,754,000; 4.200% Verizon InterNotes® due November 15, 2028 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,314,000; 4.250% Verizon InterNotes® due December 15, 2028 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,982,000; 4.150% Verizon InterNotes® due December 15, 2028 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,254,000; 3.500% Verizon InterNotes® due September 15, 2029 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $4,210,000; 3.500% Verizon InterNotes® due December 15, 2029 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $3,255,000; 3.400% Verizon InterNotes® due December 15, 2029 Principal Amount Outstanding and redeemed $1,993,000. The redemption price for each of the Notes will be equal to 100% of the principal amount of such Note, plus accrued interest on the principal amount being redeemed to the Redemption Date.
