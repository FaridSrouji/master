Verizon Fios customers may be impacted by Hearst Television's unreasonable demands. NEW YORK, DEC. 29, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON HAS BEGUN NOTIFYING SOME FIOS CUSTOMERS THAT, DUE TO HEARST TELEVISION DEMANDING UNREASONABLY LARGE RATE INCREASES THAT MIGHT FORCE CUSTOMERS TO PAY MORE TO ACCESS THE SAME CONTENT, VERIZON MAY SOON BE FORCED TO STOP OFFERING CHANNELS OWNED BY HEARST.
