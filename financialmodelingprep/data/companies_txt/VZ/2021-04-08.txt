BASKING RIDGE, N.J., APRIL 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERIZON BUSINESS TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PHOENIX SUNS ARE USING BLUEJEANS BY VERIZON TO DELIVER A “SECOND SCREEN” EXPERIENCE THAT PROVIDES A NEW, SAFE, AND INTERACTIVE WAY FOR FANS TO WATCH AND ENGAGE WITH THEIR FAVORITE TEAM AND BRANDS ACROSS THE NBA. PART OF A LARGER, LONG-TERM STRATEGIC RELATIONSHIP WITH VERIZON TO DRIVE SPORTS INNOVATION THROUGH THE USE OF 5G, TECHNOLOGY, AND ANALYTICS, TOGETHER, THE PHOENIX SUNS AND VERIZON ARE BRINGING THEIR COMBINED EXPERTISE TO SET A NEW BAR FOR PROFESSIONAL SPORTS.
