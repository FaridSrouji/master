Customers in Boise, Panama City and New York City Get Verizon 5G Ultra Wideband Service. Verizon’s 5G Ultra Wideband network can be accessed in parts of New York City, Panama City and Boise, bringing the total number of Verizon 5G Ultra Wideband mobility service cities to 13. Verizon plans to have 5G access in more than 30 cities by the end of this year. With Verizon’s 5G Ultra Wideband network, can enjoy near real-time experiences with higher capacity, higher throughput and lower latency. That means downloading movies in seconds versus minutes and supporting mobile workforce applications. It’s 5G built right for you, enabling unique experiences that aren’t possible on other networks.
