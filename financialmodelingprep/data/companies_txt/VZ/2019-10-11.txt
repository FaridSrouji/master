Verizon Lands $341 Million IRS Task Order Under EIS Telecom Contract. Verizon has secured a potential 13-year, $341 million task order to support the Internal Revenue Services information technology modernization effort. Nancy Sieger, acting chief information officer at IRS, said the task order awarded through the General Services Administrations $50 billion Enterprise Infrastructure Solutions telecommunications contract vehicle will support the IRS' client-facing services.
