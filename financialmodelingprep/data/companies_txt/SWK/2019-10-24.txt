Stanley Black & Decker Posts Q3 EPS $1.53, Announces $200 Mln Cost Reduction Program. STANLEY BLACK & DECKER REPORTS 3Q 2019 RESULTS; ANNOUNCES NEW $200 MILLION COST REDUCTION PROGRAM.Q3 REVENUE $3.6 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $3.65 BILLION.REAFFIRMS FY 2019 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $8.35 TO $8.45.SEES FY 2019 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $6.50 TO $6.60.Q3 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.53.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $2.03 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.COST REDUCTION PROGRAM IS CURRENTLY BEING IMPLEMENTED.STANLEY BLACK & DECKER - COST REDUCTION PROGRAM IS EXPECTED TO HAVE ABOUT PRE-TAX RESTRUCTURING CHARGE OF $150 MILLION EXPECTED TO BE RECOGNIZED PRIMARILY IN 2019.FY2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $8.56 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES 2019 ADJUSTED EPS RANGE $8.35 - $8.45.
