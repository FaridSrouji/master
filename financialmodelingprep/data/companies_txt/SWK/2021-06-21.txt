Stanley Black & Decker President and CFO Don Allan To Provide Testimony Before Senate Finance Committee on International Trade, Customs and Global Competitiveness on June 22. WASHINGTON, JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- STANLEY BLACK & DECKER (NYSE:SWK) PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER DON ALLAN WILL TESTIFY BEFORE SENATE FINANCE COMMITTEE, SUBCOMMITTEE ON INTERNATIONAL TRADE, CUSTOMS AND GLOBAL COMPETITIVENESS ON JUNE 22. ALLAN WILL PROVIDE TESTIMONY ON BEHALF OF STANLEY BLACK & DECKER ON THE COMPANY'S MANUFACTURING AND SUPPLY CHAIN RESILIENCY AND DISCUSS THE IMPORTANCE OF MANUFACTURING IN THE UNITED STATES.
