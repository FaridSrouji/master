GAMCO Investors Comments on Pending CBS Corp combination with Viacom. On November 19, 2019, GAMCO Investors, Inc. announced that issued a press release on November 18, 2019 commenting on the CBS Corporation's pending combination with Viacom Inc. GAMCO Investors stated that it wanted to highlight an omission of the merger agreement that is contrary to what it holds as a best practices in corporate governance. GAMCO Investor added that National Amusements, Inc. should stipulate that it will deploy a tag-along take-along right of voting shareholders for any sale of National Amusement's controlling interest in ViacomCBS.
