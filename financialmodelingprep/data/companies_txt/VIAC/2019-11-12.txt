Cbs Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. CBS Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 3,295 million compared to USD 3,263 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 501 million compared to USD 690 million a year ago. Net income was USD 319 million compared to USD 488 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.85 compared to USD 1.3 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.85 compared to USD 1.29 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 11,271 million compared to USD 10,490 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,424 million compared to USD 2,121 million a year ago. Net income was USD 2,342 million compared to USD 1,399 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 6.26 compared to USD 3.7 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 6.23 compared to USD 3.66 a year ago.
