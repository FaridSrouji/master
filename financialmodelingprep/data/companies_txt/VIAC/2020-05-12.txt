ViacomCBS Announces $2.0 Billion Debt Offering. VIACOMCBS ANNOUNCES $2.0 BILLION DEBT OFFERING.VIACOMCBS INC - AGREED TO SELL $1.0 BILLION OF 4.200% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2032 AT A PRICE EQUAL TO 97.395% OF PRINCIPAL AMOUNT THEREOF.VIACOMCBS INC - AGREED TO SELL $1.0 BILLION OF 4.950% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2050 AT A PRICE EQUAL TO 94.987% OF PRINCIPAL AMOUNT THEREOF.VIACOMCBS INC - INTENDS TO USE NET PROCEEDS FOR PURCHASE OF NOTES TENDERED PURSUANT TO PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED CONCURRENT TENDER OFFERS.
ViacomCBS Announces Upsize Of Previously Announced Cash Tender Offers. VIACOMCBS ANNOUNCES UPSIZE OF PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED CASH TENDER OFFERS TO UP TO $2.0 BILLION AGGREGATE PURCHASE PRICE FROM $1.0 BILLION AGGREGATE PURCHASE PRICE OF CERTAIN OUTSTANDING DEBT SECURITIES.VIACOMCBS INC - OFFERS ARE SCHEDULED TO EXPIRE AT 11:59 P.M., NEW YORK CITY TIME, ON JUNE 9, 2020.
