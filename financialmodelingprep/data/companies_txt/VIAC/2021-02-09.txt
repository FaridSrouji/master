ViacomCBS Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VIACOMCBS INC. (NASDAQ: VIAC; VIACA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.24 PER SHARE ON BOTH ITS CLASS A AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE ON APRIL 1, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 15, 2021.
