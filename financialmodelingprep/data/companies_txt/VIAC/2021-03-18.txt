ViacomCBS and NFL Reach New Long-Term Multiplatform Rights Agreement Through the 2033 Season. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE NATIONAL FOOTBALL LEAGUE (NFL) AND VIACOMCBS ANNOUNCED TODAY A NEW 11-YEAR MULTIPLATFORM RIGHTS AGREEMENT THAT EXTENDS CBS' LONG-STANDING RELATIONSHIP WITH THE NFL THROUGH THE 2033 SEASON. CBS SPORTS CONTINUES AS THE HOME OF THE NFL'S AMERICAN FOOTBALL CONFERENCE (AFC), FEATURING MANY OF THE GAME'S BRIGHTEST YOUNG STARS, WITH ALL GAMES BROADCAST ON THE CBS TELEVISION NETWORK AND STREAMED LIVE ON PARAMOUNT+, VIACOMCBS' FLAGSHIP STREAMING SERVICE.
