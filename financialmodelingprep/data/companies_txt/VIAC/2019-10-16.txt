CBS to Voluntarily Delist from NYSE. On October 16, 2019, the Board of Directors of CBS Corporation determined that, effective as of, and conditional on the occurrence of, the effective time of the Merger, CBS will voluntarily delist the CBS Common Stock from the NYSE and will list the ViacomCBS Common Stock shares, including the shares of CBS Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the effective time of the Merger (which will remain issued and outstanding shares of ViacomCBS at the effective time of the Merger), on NASDAQ. CBS expects that the ViacomCBS Class A Common Stock and ViacomCBS Class B Common Stock will be listed on NASDAQ under the new ticker symbols "VIACA" and "VIAC," respectively.
