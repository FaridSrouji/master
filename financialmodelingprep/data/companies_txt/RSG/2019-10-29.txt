Republic Services, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Year Ending December 31, 2019 and December 31, 2020. Republic Services, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the year ending December 31, 2019 and December 31, 2020. For the year ending December 31, 2019, the company expects diluted earnings per share to be in the range of $3.27 to $3.29.For the year ending December 31, 2020, the company expects diluted earnings per share to be in the range of $3.44 to $3.49.
