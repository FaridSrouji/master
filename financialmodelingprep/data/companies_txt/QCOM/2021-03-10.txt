Qualcomm Increases Quarterly Dividend by 5 Percent. SAN DIEGO, MARCH 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUALCOMM INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: QCOM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A 5 PERCENT INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND WILL INCREASE FROM $0.65 TO $0.68 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK AND WILL BE EFFECTIVE FOR QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS PAYABLE AFTER MARCH 25, 2021.
