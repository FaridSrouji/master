Bristol-Myers Squibb says Opdivo+Yervoy Demonstrates Durable Survival Benefit Vs Chemotherapy In Patients With Malignant Pleural Mesothelioma. BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB- OPDIVO+YERVOY DEMONSTRATES DURABLE SURVIVAL BENEFIT VERSUS CHEMOTHERAPY IN PATIENTS WITH MALIGNANT PLEURAL MESOTHELIOMA.BMS- OPDIVO PLUS YERVOY DEMONSTRATED SIGNIFICANT IMPROVEMENT IN OS IN PATIENTS WITH PREVIOUSLY UNTREATED MPM IN PHASE 3 CHECKMATE -743 CLINICAL TRIAL.BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB-SAFETY PROFILE OF OPDIVO PLUS YERVOY WAS CONSISTENT WITH PREVIOUSLY REPORTED STUDIES, AND NO NEW SAFETY SIGNALS WERE OBSERVED.
