Biomotiv And Bristol-Myers Squibb Announce The Launch Of Anteros Pharmaceuticals. BIOMOTIV AND BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB ANNOUNCE THE LAUNCH OF ANTEROS PHARMACEUTICALS.BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB - UNDER TERMS OF PARTNERSHIP, CO WILL CONTRIBUTE IP, DATA, REAGENTS FOR SERIES OF SMALL MOLECULES AGAINST UNDISCLOSED MECHANISM.BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB CO - ONCE ANTEROS NOMINATES PRE-CLINICAL CANDIDATE, CO HAS OPTION TO ACQUIRE ANTEROS FROM BIOMOTIV UNDER PRE-AGREED TERMS.
