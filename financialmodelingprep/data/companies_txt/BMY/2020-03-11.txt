FDA Approves Opdivo & Yervoy For Patients With Hepatocellular Carcinoma. U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION APPROVES OPDIVO® (NIVOLUMAB) + YERVOY® (IPILIMUMAB) FOR PATIENTS WITH HEPATOCELLULAR CARCINOMA (HCC) PREVIOUSLY TREATED WITH SORAFENIB.BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB CO - OPDIVO + YERVOY COMBINATION IS NOW APPROVED TO TREAT FOUR TYPES OF CANCER.
