Bristol Myers Announces Positive Topline Result From Pivotal Phase 3 Trial Evaluating Opdivo Plus Yervoy vs Chemotherapy In Previously Untreated Malignant Pleural Mesothelioma. BRISTOL MYERS ANNOUNCES POSITIVE TOPLINE RESULT FROM PIVOTAL PHASE 3 TRIAL EVALUATING OPDIVO PLUS YERVOY VERSUS. CHEMOTHERAPY IN PREVIOUSLY UNTREATED MALIGNANT PLEURAL MESOTHELIOMA.BRISTOL MYERS - SAFETY PROFILE OF OPDIVO PLUS YERVOY OBSERVED IN TRIAL REFLECTS KNOWN SAFETY PROFILE OF THE COMBINATION.BRISTOL MYERS - OPDIVO PLUS YERVOY PROLONGED SURVIVAL COMPARED TO CHEMOTHERAPY AT INTERIM ANALYSIS IN CHECKMATE -743.BRISTOL MYERS - TRIAL OF OPDIVO IN COMBINATION WITH YERVOY MET ITS PRIMARY ENDPOINT OF OVERALL SURVIVAL.
