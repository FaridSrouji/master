U.S. FDA Accepts Priority Review For Bristol-Myers's Application For Opdivo Plus Yervoy. U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION ACCEPTS FOR PRIORITY REVIEW BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB’S APPLICATION FOR OPDIVO (NIVOLUMAB) PLUS YERVOY (IPILIMUMAB) IN FIRST-LINE NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER.BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB CO - FDA HAS GRANTED APPLICATION PRIORITY REVIEW WITH A PRESCRIPTION DRUG USER FEE ACT (PDUFA) GOAL DATE OF MAY 15, 2020.
