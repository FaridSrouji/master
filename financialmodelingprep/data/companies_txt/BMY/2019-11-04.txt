PathAI, Inc. announced that it has received $75 million in funding from a group of investors. On November 4, 2019, PathAI, inc. closed the transaction. The company amended the terms of the transaction. The company now received $14,999,997 in its second tranche closing brining the total funding to $75,000,000 in the transaction. The transaction also included participation from new investors Merck Global Health Innovation Fund, LLC and Bristol-Myers Squibb Company (NYSE:BMY). The company has raised over $90,000,000 till date.
