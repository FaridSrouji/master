Bristol-Myers Squibb Decreases Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019; Announces Impairment Charges for the Third Quarter of 2019. Bristol-Myers Squibb decreased 2019 GAAP EPS guidance range from $3.73 - $3.83 to $3.46 - $3.56. For the third quarter of 2019, the company reported impairment charges of $9 million.
