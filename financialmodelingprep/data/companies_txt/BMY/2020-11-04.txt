Bristol Myers Squibb and MyoKardia Announce Expiration of HSR Act Waiting Period. NEW YORK & BRISBANE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Bristol Myers Squibb (NYSE: BMY) and MyoKardia, Inc. (Nasdaq: MYOK) today announced the expiration of the waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, or HSR Act, in connection with Bristol Myers Squibb's previously announced tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of MyoKardia for a purchase price of $225.00 per share in cash, or approximately $13.1 billion.
