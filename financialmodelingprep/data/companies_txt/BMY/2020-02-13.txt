Bristol-Myers - FDA Accepts For Priority Review Co's Biologics License Application (BLA) For Lymphoma Drug. U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) ACCEPTS FOR PRIORITY REVIEW BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB’S BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) FOR LISOCABTAGENE MARALEUCEL (LISO-CEL) FOR ADULT PATIENTS WITH RELAPSED OR REFRACTORY LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA.BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB CO - FDA HAS SET A PDUFA GOAL DATE OF AUGUST 17, 2020.
