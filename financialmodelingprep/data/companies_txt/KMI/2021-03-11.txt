Colorado Energy Organizations Launch First-of-its-Kind Responsibly Sourced Natural Gas Partnership. DENVER, MARCH 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A COLORADO NATURAL GAS PRODUCER, AN INFRASTRUCTURE OPERATOR AND LOCAL MUNICIPAL UTILITY HAVE ENTERED INTO A FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND RESPONSIBLY SOURCED GAS (RSG) PILOT PROJECT WITH PROJECT CANARY, A DENVER-BASED B-CORP FOCUSED ON PROVIDING CONTINUOUS EMISSIONS MONITORING DATA AND TECHNOLOGIES ACROSS THE ENERGY SECTOR. THE UNIQUE WELLHEAD TO BURNER-TIP RSG PILOT EXTENDS ACROSS THE ENERGY VALUE CHAIN – FROM PRODUCTION, TRANSPORTATION AND MARKETING OF RSG – FOR CONSUMER AND COMMUNITY USE LOCALLY IN COLORADO.
