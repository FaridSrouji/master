HanesBrands Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year of 2019. HanesBrands provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter and full year of 2019. For the fourth quarter, the company expected net sales to be in the range of $1.719 billion to $1.769 billion. Operating profit is expected to be $248 million to $273 million. EPS is expected to be $0.46 to $0.52For the fourth quarter, the company expected net sales of $6.935 billion to $6.985 billion against $6.885 billion to $6.985 billion as previously reported. Operating profit of $900 million to $925 million against $900 million to $930 as previously reported. EPS of $1.61 to $1.67 against $1.59 to $1.67 as previously reported.
