HanesBrands Declares Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 3, 2019. HanesBrands announced that its Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.15 per share to be paid Dec. 3, 2019, for stockholders of record at the close of business Nov. 12, 2019.
