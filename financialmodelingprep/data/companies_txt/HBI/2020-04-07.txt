HanesBrands Says Temporarily Closed About 1,200 Brand Stores In United States, Europe And Australia. HANESBRANDS TAKES ACTIONS TO NAVIGATE COVID-19 PANDEMIC, PRESERVE CASH AND STRENGTHEN LONG-TERM MARKET POSITION.HANESBRANDS INC - TEMPORARILY CLOSED APPROXIMATELY 1,200 BRAND STORES IN UNITED STATES, EUROPE AND AUSTRALIA.HANESBRANDS INC - HAS INSTITUTED TEMPORARY PAY CUTS FOR EXECUTIVES AND SALARIED EMPLOYEES, RANGING FROM 10% TO 30%.HANESBRANDS INC - HAS INSTITUTED TEMPORARY PAY CUTS FOR EXECUTIVES AND SALARIED EMPLOYEES, RANGING FROM 10% TO 30%, DEPENDING ON EMPLOYEE LEVEL.HANESBRANDS INC - IN ADDITION TO RETAIL STORE EMPLOYEES, COMPANY WILL TEMPORARILY FURLOUGH APPROXIMATELY 575 OTHER U.S. EMPLOYEES.HANESBRANDS INC - RAMPING UP ADDITIONAL PRODUCTION TO MAKE MORE THAN 320 MILLION FDA-APPROVED ALL-COTTON FACE MASKS OVER COMING WEEKS.HANESBRANDS INC - PLANS TO CALL FULL-TIME AND PART-TIME EMPLOYEES BACK TO WORK AS SOON AS STORES CAN BE REOPENED.
