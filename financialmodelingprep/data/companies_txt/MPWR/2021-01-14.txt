MPS & EPC Redefine DC/DC Power Conversion with 48V to 6V LLC Module Family for High-Density Computing and Datacenters. KIRKLAND, WASH., JAN. 14, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONOLITHIC POWER SYSTEMS, INC. (MPS) (NASDAQ: MPWR), A LEADING COMPANY IN HIGH-PERFORMANCE POWER SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THE LAUNCH OF A NEW FAMILY OF 48V TO 6V DIGITAL DC/DC POWER MODULES FOR 48V DATACENTER SOLUTIONS , UTILIZING EGAN® TRANSISTORS FROM EFFICIENT POWER CONVERSION ( EPC ) CORPORATION. THESE POWER MODULES TARGET APPLICATIONS FOR HIGH-DENSITY COMPUTING AND DATACENTERS, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, MACHINE LEARNING, AND MULTI-USER GAMING.
