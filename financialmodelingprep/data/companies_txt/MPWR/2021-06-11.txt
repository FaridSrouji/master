Monolithic Power Systems Announces Second Quarter Dividend. KIRKLAND, WASH., JUNE 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  MONOLITHIC POWER SYSTEMS (MPS) (NASDAQ: MPWR), A GLOBAL COMPANY THAT PROVIDES HIGH-PERFORMANCE, SEMICONDUCTOR-BASED POWER ELECTRONICS SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS SECOND QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.60 PER COMMON SHARE TO ALL STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 30, 2021. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JULY 15, 2021.
