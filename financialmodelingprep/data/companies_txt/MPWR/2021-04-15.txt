Monolithic Power Systems to Report First Quarter Results on May 4, 2021. KIRKLAND, WASH., APRIL 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONOLITHIC POWER SYSTEMS (MPS) (NASDAQ: MPWR), A LEADING COMPANY IN HIGH PERFORMANCE ANALOG SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO REPORT ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021.
