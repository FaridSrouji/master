Synchrony Financial Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on November 14, 2019. Synchrony Financial announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.22 per share of common stock, payable on November 14, 2019 to holders of record at the close of business on November 4, 2019.
