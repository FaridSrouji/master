Synchrony Announces Organizational Changes to Accelerate Strategy and Growth. STAMFORD, CONN., JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY (NYSE: SYF), A PREMIER CONSUMER FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGES AIMED TO FURTHER ALIGN THE COMPANY'S RESOURCES WITH ITS PARTNERS AND EVOLVING CONSUMER EXPECTATIONS, WHILE LEVERAGING ITS INNOVATION, DATA, EXPERTISE AND SCALE TO DELIVER PRODUCTS AND CAPABILITIES TO MARKET FASTER.
