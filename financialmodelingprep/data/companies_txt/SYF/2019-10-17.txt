Synchrony Financial Announces Unaudited Earnings Results for the Third Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2019. Synchrony Financial announced unaudited earnings results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, the company announced net interest income was USD 4,389 million compared to USD 4,206 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,056 million compared to USD 671 million a year ago. For the nine months, net interest income was USD 12,770 million compared to USD 11,785 million a year ago. Net income was USD 3,016 million compared to USD 2,007 million a year ago.
