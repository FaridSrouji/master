Synchrony Announces Quarterly Common Stock Dividend of $0.22 Per Share. STAMFORD, CONN., APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNCHRONY FINANCIAL (NYSE: SYF) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.22 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MAY 13, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 3, 2021.
