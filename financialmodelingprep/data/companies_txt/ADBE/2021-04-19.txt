Adobe Co-Founder Dr. Geschke Passes at 81. SAN JOSE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IT IS WITH PROFOUND SADNESS THAT ADOBE (NASDAQ:ADBE) SHARES THE PASSING OF THE COMPANY'S BELOVED CO-FOUNDER, DR. CHARLES GESCHKE. DR. GESCHKE PASSED AWAY ON FRIDAY, SURROUNDED BY HIS FAMILY. DR. GESCHKE CO-FOUNDED ADOBE IN 1982 WITH DR. JOHN WARNOCK, A COLLEAGUE FROM XEROX. THEIR FIRST PRODUCT WAS ADOBE POSTSCRIPT, A PIVOTAL TECHNOLOGY THAT SPARKED THE DESKTOP PUBLISHING REVOLUTION. DR. GESCHKE WAS CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER OF ADOBE FROM DECEMBER 1986 TO JULY 19
