People's United Financial Declares Cash Dividend on Preferred Stock. BRIDGEPORT, CONN., APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PEOPLE'S UNITED FINANCIAL, INC. (NASDAQ: PBCT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.3515625 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S FIXED-TO-FLOATING RATE NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK.
