People's United Financial, Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend on Preferred Stock, Payable on December 15, 2019. People's United Financial, Inc. announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.3515625 per share on the company's Fixed-to-Floating Rate Non-Cumulative Perpetual Preferred Stock. The dividend is payable on December 15, 2019 to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 1, 2019.
