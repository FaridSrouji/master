Freeport-McMoRan Announces Suspension Of Q1 Cash Dividend On Common Stock And Prudent Steps To Reduce Costs And Capital Spending Plans. FREEPORT-MCMORAN ANNOUNCES SUSPENSION OF FIRST-QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK AND PRUDENT STEPS TO REDUCE COSTS AND CAPITAL SPENDING PLANS.FREEPORT-MCMORAN INC - WILL SUSPEND QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.05 PER SHARE PREVIOUSLY PLANNED FOR MAY 1, 2020.FREEPORT-MCMORAN INC - REVISED OPERATING PLANS MAY RESULT IN TEMPORARY REDUCTIONS IN COPPER AND MOLYBDENUM PRODUCTION AT OPERATIONS IN AMERICAS.FREEPORT-MCMORAN - UNDERTAKEN REVIEW OF OPERATING PLANS AT EACH GLOBAL COPPER & MOLYBDENUM OPERATIONS TO TARGET REDUCTIONS IN COSTS, CAPITAL SPENDING.
