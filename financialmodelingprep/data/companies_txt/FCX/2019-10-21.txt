Freeport-McMoRan Inc. Announces Appointment of John J. Stephens to its Board of Directors and as a Member of the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors. Freeport-McMoRan Inc. announced the appointment of John J. Stephens to its Board of Directors and as a member of the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors. Mr. Stephens has served as Senior Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of AT&T Inc., since 2011. He has responsibility for financial planning, corporate development, accounting, tax, auditing, treasury, investor relations and corporate real estate. Prior to his current position, Stephens held a series of successive positions in the finance department.
