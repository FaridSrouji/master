Freeport-McMoRan Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend on Common Stock. PHOENIX--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FREEPORT-MCMORAN INC. (NYSE: FCX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (“BOARD”) DECLARED A CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.075 PER SHARE ON FCX'S COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON MAY 3, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 15, 2021.
