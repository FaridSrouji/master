FIS Unity Wealth Management Platform Named Best Technology Platform for Family Offices at Private Asset Management Awards. JACKSONVILLE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER FIS® (NYSE: FIS) HAS RECEIVED TWO AWARDS FOR ITS WEALTH MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS AND SERVICES AT THE 2021 PRIVATE ASSET MANAGEMENT (PAM) AWARDS BY FUND INTELLIGENCE. ANNOUNCED AT A VIRTUAL EVENT HELD BY FUND INTELLIGENCE ON FEB. 10, THE FIS UNITY SOLUTION WAS NAMED BEST TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM FOR FAMILY OFFICES.
