Fidelity National Information Services, Inc. Provides Financial Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year Ended December 31, 2019; and Reports Asset Impairment for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Fidelity National Information Services, Inc. provided financial guidance for the fourth quarter and full year ended December 31, 2019. For the quarter, the company expected revenue of $3,295 million - $3,335 million, net earnings of $125 million - $280 million and Diluted EPS of $0.20 - $0.45. For the full year, the company expected revenue of $10,286 million - $10,326 million, net earnings of $602 million - $772 million and Diluted EPS of $1.33 - $1.70.For the third quarter, the company reported asset impairment of $87 million against $95 million a year ago.
