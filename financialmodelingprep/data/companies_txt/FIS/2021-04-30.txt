FIS Announces Quarterly Dividend. JACKSONVILLE, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FIS® (NYSE: FIS), A GLOBAL LEADER IN FINANCIAL SERVICES TECHNOLOGY, ANNOUNCED TODAY A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.39 PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 25, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 11, 2021.
