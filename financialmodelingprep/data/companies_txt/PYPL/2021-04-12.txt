Uber, PayPal and Walgreens Introduce Vaccine Access Fund. SAN JOSE, CALIF., APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, PAYPAL HOLDINGS, INC. (NASDAQ: PYPL), UBER TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NYSE: UBER) AND WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE, INC. (NASDAQ: WBA) LAUNCHED THEIR NEW VACCINE ACCESS FUND THAT ALLOWS CUSTOMERS TO DONATE TO VACCINE ACCESS EFFORTS TO HELP CONNECT PEOPLE WITHOUT TRANSPORTATION TO COVID-19 VACCINATION SITES, PARTICULARLY THOSE IN UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES.
