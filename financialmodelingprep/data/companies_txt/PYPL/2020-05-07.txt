Paypal Holdings Says During Q1, Management Approved Strategic Reduction Of Existing Global Workforce. PAYPAL HOLDINGS SAYS DURING Q1, MANAGEMENT APPROVED STRATEGIC REDUCTION OF EXISTING GLOBAL WORKFORCE, RESULTED IN RESTRUCTURING CHARGES OF $29 MILLION.PAYPAL HOLDINGS - APPROVED STRATEGIC REDUCTION IN 2020 PART OF MULTIPHASE PROCESS TO REORGANIZE WORKFORCE CONCURRENTLY WITH REDESIGN OF OPERATING STRUCTURE.PAYPAL HOLDINGS - PRIMARILY INCURRED EMPLOYEE SEVERANCE, BENEFITS COSTS UNDER 2020 STRATEGIC REDUCTION, AS WELL AS OTHER ASSOCIATED CONSULTANCY COSTS.PAYPAL HOLDINGS SAYS STRATEGIC REDUCTION OF THE EXISTING GLOBAL WORKFORCE EXPECTED TO BE SUBSTANTIALLY COMPLETED BY THE END OF 2020 - SEC FILING.
