LyondellBasell Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 9, 2019. LyondellBasell announced that it has quarterly declared a dividend of $1.05 per share, to be paid December 9, 2019 to shareholders of record December 2, 2019, with an ex-dividend date of November 29, 2019.
