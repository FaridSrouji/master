LyondellBasell releases 2020 sustainability report. HOUSTON AND ROTTERDAM, NETHERLANDS, JUNE 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LYONDELLBASELL (NYSE: LYB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PUBLICATION OF ITS 2020 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT. THE REPORT HIGHLIGHTS THE COMPANY'S EFFORTS IN THE AREAS OF ENDING PLASTIC WASTE IN THE ENVIRONMENT, HELPING TO ADDRESS CLIMATE CHANGE AND SUPPORTING A THRIVING SOCIETY.
