LyondellBasell to Support Enterprise to Build PDH 2 Plant. LyondellBasell Industries N.V. and Enterprise Products Partners L.P. announced that their respective affiliates have executed long-term contracts that support construction of Enterprise’s second propane dehydrogenation plant. PDH 2 will have the capacity to consume up to 35,000 barrels per day (“BPD”) of propane and produce up to 1.65 billion pounds per year of polymer grade propylene. PDH 2 will be located at Enterprise’s complex in the Mont Belvieu, Texas area. The facility is scheduled to begin service in the first half of 2023.
