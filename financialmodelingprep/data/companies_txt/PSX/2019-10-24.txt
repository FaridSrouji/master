Phillips 66 Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Phillips 66 announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 27,771 million compared to USD 30,592 million a year ago. Net income was USD 712 million compared to USD 1,492 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.58 compared to USD 3.2 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.58 compared to USD 3.18 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 79,947 million. Net income was USD 2,340 million. Basic earnings per share was USD 5.15. Diluted earnings per share was USD 5.13.
