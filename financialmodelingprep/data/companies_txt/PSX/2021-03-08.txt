Phillips 66 Contributes $450,000 to Houston Winter Storm Relief Efforts. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PHILLIPS 66 (NYSE: PSX) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT IS CONTRIBUTING A TOTAL OF $450,000 TO WINTER STORM RELIEF AND RECOVERY EFFORTS ACROSS HOUSTON, WITH $250,000 GOING TO THE HOUSTON HARRIS COUNTY WINTER STORM RELIEF FUND AND $200,000 GOING TO THE HOUSTON FOOD BANK. “WE ARE VERY GRATEFUL FOR THE ORGANIZATIONS THAT STEPPED UP AND HELPED MILLIONS OF HOUSTONIANS WHO STRUGGLED TO KEEP THEMSELVES AND THEIR FAMILIES WARM AND FED WHEN THE WINTER STORM HIT,” SAID PHILLIPS 66 CHAIRMAN A
