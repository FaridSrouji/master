Phillips 66 to contribute $250,000 to new Greenwood Rising history center in Tulsa, Oklahoma. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PHILLIPS 66 IS CONTRIBUTING $250,000 TO GREENWOOD RISING, A NEW CENTER IN TULSA THAT HONORS VICTIMS AND SURVIVORS OF THE TULSA RACE MASSACRE OF 1921.
