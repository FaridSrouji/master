IBM - IBM Board Approved Compensatory Arrangements For CEO Arvind Krishna. IBM - ON APRIL 28, IBM BOARD APPROVED COMPENSATORY ARRANGEMENTS FOR CEO ARVIND KRISHNA AND PRESIDENT OF CO JAMES WHITEHURST.IBM - KRISHNA'S BASE SALARY IS $1.5 MILLION, EFFECTIVE APRIL 6, 2020.IBM - 2020 ANNUAL INCENTIVE TARGET FOR ARVIND KRISHNA IS $3 MILLION, PRORATED FOR APRIL TO DECEMBER 2020.IBM - BOARD APPROVED A 2020 LONG-TERM INCENTIVE AWARD OF $13.75 MILLION FOR KRISHNA.IBM - WHITEHURST'S BASE SALARY IS $1.2 MILLION, EFFECTIVE APRIL 6, 2020.IBM - 2020 ANNUAL INCENTIVE TARGET FOR WHITEHURST IS $1.8 MILLION, PRORATED FOR APRIL TO DECEMBER 2020.
