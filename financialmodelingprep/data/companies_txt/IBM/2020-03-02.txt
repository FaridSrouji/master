Infosys And IBM Collaborate To Help Businesses Accelerate Digital Transformation With IBM Public Cloud. INFOSYS AND IBM COLLABORATE TO HELP BUSINESSES ACCELERATE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION WITH IBM PUBLIC CLOUD.INFOSYS - AS PART OF RELATIONSHIP, INFOSYS WILL ALSO OFFER ITS CLIENTS ACCESS TO RED HAT'S PORTFOLIO OF OPEN SOURCE OFFERINGS ON IBM PUBLIC CLOUD.
