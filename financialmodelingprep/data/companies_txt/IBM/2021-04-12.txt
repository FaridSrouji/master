IBM's Independent Managed Infrastructure Services Business to be Named Kyndryl. ARMONK, N.Y., APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KYNDRYL WILL BE THE NAME OF THE NEW, INDEPENDENT COMPANY THAT WILL BE CREATED FOLLOWING THE SEPARATION OF ITS MANAGED INFRASTRUCTURE SERVICES BUSINESS, WHICH IS EXPECTED TO OCCUR BY THE END OF 2021.
