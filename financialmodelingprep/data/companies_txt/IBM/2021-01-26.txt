IBM Board Approves Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend. ARMONK, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $IBM #IBM--THE IBM (NYSE: IBM) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.63 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE MARCH 10 , 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD FEBRUARY 10 , 2021. IBM HAS PAID CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS EVERY YEAR SINCE 1916.
