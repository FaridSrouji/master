IBM Unveils New Capabilities Designed to Accelerate Hybrid Cloud Application Modernization. ARMONK, N.Y., MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED A SERIES OF NEW AND UPDATED CAPABILITIES FOR DEVELOPERS DESIGNED TO DELIVER INTELLIGENT APPLICATION ANALYSIS THROUGHOUT THE DEVOPS PIPELINE, GENERALLY AVAILABLE ON MARCH 19.
