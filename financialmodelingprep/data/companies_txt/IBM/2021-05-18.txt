IBM Acquires Waeg to Deepen Expertise Across the Salesforce Platform in Europe. ARMONK, N.Y., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO ACQUIRE WAEG, A LEADING SALESFORCE CONSULTING PARTNER IN EUROPE, TO EXTEND IBM'S PORTFOLIO OF SALESFORCE SERVICES AND ADVANCE IBM'S HYBRID CLOUD AND AI STRATEGY.
