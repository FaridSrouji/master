IBM Positioned as a Leader in Two Newly Released Gartner Magic Quadrant Reports. ARMONK, N.Y., MARCH 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IBM (NYSE: IBM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT GLOBAL RESEARCH FIRM GARTNER HAS POSITIONED IBM AS A LEADER IN TWO NEWLY PUBLISHED GARTNER MAGIC QUADRANT REPORTS: THE 2021 MAGIC QUADRANT FOR CLOUD AI DEVELOPER SERVICES1 AND THE 2021 MAGIC QUADRANT FOR DATA SCIENCE AND MACHINE LEARNING PLATFORMS2.
