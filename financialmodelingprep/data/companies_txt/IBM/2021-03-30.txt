Cleveland Clinic and IBM Unveil Landmark 10-Year Partnership to Accelerate Discovery in Healthcare and Life Sciences. ARMONK, N.Y. AND CLEVELAND, MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CLEVELAND CLINIC AND IBM (NYSE: IBM) HAVE ANNOUNCED A PLANNED 10-YEAR PARTNERSHIP TO ESTABLISH THE DISCOVERY ACCELERATOR, A JOINT CLEVELAND CLINIC - IBM CENTER WITH THE MISSION OF FUNDAMENTALLY ADVANCING THE PACE OF DISCOVERY IN HEALTHCARE AND LIFE SCIENCES THROUGH THE USE OF HIGH PERFORMANCE COMPUTING ON THE HYBRID CLOUD, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) AND QUANTUM COMPUTING TECHNOLOGIES.
