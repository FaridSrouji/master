Heifer International and IBM Work with Coffee and Cocoa Farmers in Honduras to Increase Access to Data and Global Markets. WASHINGTON, JULY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SMALLHOLDER COFFEE AND COCOA FARMERS AND THEIR COOPERATIVES IN HONDURAS ARE HARNESSING THE POWER OF TECHNOLOGY TO ACCESS NEW MARKETS AND MAKE BETTER BUSINESS DECISIONS THROUGH IBM FOOD TRUST AND IBM WATSON DECISION PLATFORM FOR AGRICULTURE, WORKING WITH HEIFER INTERNATIONAL AND IBM (NYSE: IBM).  SMALL-SCALE COFFEE FARMERS OPERATE AT AN AVERAGE OF 46–59% LOSS, WITH FARMERS EARNING LESS THAN 1% OF THE SALE OF A CUP OF COFFEE AT A COFFEE SHOP.
