Hill's Pet Nutrition Plans to Build New Factory in Tonganoxie, Kansas to Help Meet Growing Demand for its Pet Food. TOPEKA, KAN., JUNE 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HILL'S PET NUTRITION ANNOUNCED THEIR INTENT TO INVEST MORE THAN $250 MILLION TO BUILD A NEW FACTORY IN LEAVENWORTH COUNTY IN THEIR HOME STATE OF KANSAS TO HELP MEET THE GROWING DEMAND FOR THEIR #1 VET RECOMMENDED HILL'S PET NUTRITION.
