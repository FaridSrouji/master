Colgate-Palmolive Company Provides Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019. Colgate-Palmolive Company provided earnings guidance for the year 2019. For the period, the company expects 2019 net sales to be flat to up low-single-digits, with organic sales now expected to be up between 3% and 4%, roughly in line with year-to-date growth. On a GAAP basis, based on current spot rates, the company now expects a slight decline in gross margin and continue to expect a low-single-digit decline in earnings per share in 2019.
