Colgate Acquires Luxe Energy in All-Stock Transaction. MIDLAND, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COLGATE ENERGY PARTNERS III, LLC (THE “COMPANY” OR “COLGATE”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH COLGATE WILL ACQUIRE A MAJORITY OF THE ASSETS OWNED BY LUXE ENERGY LLC (“LUXE”) IN AN ALL-STOCK TRANSACTION. LUXE WILL CONTINUE TO OWN AND MANAGE CERTAIN ASSETS INCLUDING A PORTION OF THE NON-OPERATED LEASEHOLD INTERESTS THAT ARE OPERATED BY MDC REEVES ENERGY, LLC AND ITS AFFILIATES.
