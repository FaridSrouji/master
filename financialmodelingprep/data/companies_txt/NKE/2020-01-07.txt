NIKE SAYS SUBSEQUENT TO NOV. 30, 2019, TAX LAWS IN ONE OF CO’S FOREIGN JURISDICTIONS CHANGED - SEC FILING.NIKE - DUE TO CHANGE IN LAW, UNDISTRIBUTED EARNINGS IN THAT FOREIGN JURISDICTION COULD BE SUBJECT TO WITHHOLDING TAX UPON DISTRIBUTION.NIKE - CO REVIEWING APPLICATION OF CHANGE; MAY REEVALUATE ASSERTION WITH RESPECT TO UNDISTRIBUTED FOREIGN EARNINGS.NIKE - CANNOT ESTIMATE IMPACT OF CHANGE IN LAW TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AT THIS TIME.
