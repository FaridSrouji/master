Skechers Won Victory Over Nike In A Case Relating To Nike's Subsidiary Converse Inc. SKECHERS USA INC - HAS WON A VICTORY OVER NIKE INC IN A CASE RELATING TO NIKE'S WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY CONVERSE INC.SKECHERS USA - JUDGE RULES THAT CHUCK TAYLOR TRADEMARK IS NOT VALID AS TO SKECHERS.SKECHERS USA INC - JUDGE FINDS NO VIOLATION OF CHUCK TAYLOR TRADEMARK BY ANY OF SKECHERS STYLES IN CASE.
