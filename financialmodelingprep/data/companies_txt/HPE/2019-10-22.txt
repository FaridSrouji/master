Hewlett Packard Enterprise Company Declares Regular Dividend, Payable on or About January 2, 2020. Hewlett Packard Enterprise Company announced an increase in its quarterly dividend to $0.12 per share, and declared a dividend, the first in Hewlett Packard Enterprise's fiscal year 2020, payable on or about January 2, 2020, to stockholders of record as of the close of business on December 11, 2019.
