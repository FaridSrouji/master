Hewlett Packard Enterprise Company Announces Advancements to Hpe Simplivity. Hewlett Packard Enterprise Company announced advancements to HPE SimpliVity, the company’s hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) platform, ushering in a new generation of HCI powered with artificial intelligence to simplify virtual machine (VM) management and free IT staff to focus on innovation. Building upon its software-defined architecture, HPE SimpliVity is enhancing its customer experience with HPE InfoSight, one-click cluster upgrades, and the integration of HPE StoreOnce.
