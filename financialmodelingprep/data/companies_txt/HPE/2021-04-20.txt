Hewlett Packard Enterprise Wins Red Hat North American Partner Award for Artificial Intelligence (AI) Solutions. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE (HPE) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN HONORED AS ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) SOLUTION PARTNER OF THE YEAR BY RED HAT, INC., FOR AI SOLUTIONS DEVELOPED FOR THE PUBLIC SECTOR. THE AWARD IS PART OF THE ANNUAL RED HAT NORTH AMERICA PARTNER AWARDS, WHICH RECOGNIZE PARTNERS FOR THEIR COMMITMENT TO OPEN INNOVATION AND COLLABORATION WITH RED HAT. THE AWARD RECOGNIZES HPE FOR CO-DEVELOPING A HIGHLY DIFFERENTIATED “AI AT THE EDGE” SOLUTION BASED ON THE HP
