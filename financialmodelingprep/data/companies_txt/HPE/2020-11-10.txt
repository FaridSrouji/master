Hewlett Packard Enterprise Delivers Hybrid Workplace Upgrades to Support COVID-19 Response at Erasmus Medical Center in the Netherlands. AMSTELVEEN, NETHERLANDS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE (HPE) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS DELIVERING AN ENHANCED HYBRID WORKPLACE EXPERIENCE WITH ITS VIRTUAL DESKTOP INFRASTRUCTURE (VDI) SOLUTIONS FOR REMOTE AND ONSITE HOSPITAL STAFF AT ERASMUS MEDICAL CENTER (MC), ONE OF THE LARGEST MEDICAL CENTERS IN EUROPE BASED IN ROTTERDAM, NETHERLANDS.
