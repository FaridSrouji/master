Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, Reports Topline Data for Bintrafusp Alfa as Second-Line Monotherapy Treatment in Biliary Tract Cancer. NOT INTENDED FOR UK-BASED MEDIA   DARMSTADT, GERMANY, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MERCK KGAA, DARMSTADT, GERMANY, A LEADING SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED TOPLINE DATA FROM THE PHASE II INTR@PID BTC 047 STUDY EVALUATING BINTRAFUSP ALFA AS A MONOTHERAPY IN THE SECOND-LINE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH LOCALLY ADVANCED OR METASTATIC BILIARY TRACT CANCER (BTC) WHO HAVE FAILED OR ARE INTOLERANT OF FIRST-LINE PLATINUM-BASED CHEMOTHERAPY. IN THE STUDY OF 159 PATIENTS, BINTRAFUSP ALFA DEMONSTRATED SINGLE-AGENT EFFICACY AND DURABILITY WITH A MANAGEABLE SAFETY PROFILE AFTER MORE THAN NINE MONTHS OF FOLLOW-UP, WITH AN INDEPENDENT REVIEW COMMITTEE (IRC)-ADJUDICATED OBJECTIVE RESPONSE RATE (ORR) OF 10.1% (95% CI: 5.9% TO 15.8%) PER RECIST 1.1.
