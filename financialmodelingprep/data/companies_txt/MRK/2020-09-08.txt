Bayer Says Phase 4 Study Met Its Main Goal In PAH Patients Who Transitioned To Adempas. BAYER PHASE IV STUDY MET ITS PRIMARY ENDPOINT IN PAH PATIENTS WHO HAD TRANSITIONED TO ADEMPAS (RIOCIGUAT) AFTER INSUFFICIENT RESPONSE TO PDE5 INHIBITORS.BAYER - OUTCOMES FROM RANDOMIZED, CONTROLLED, OPEN-LABEL REPLACE STUDY INCLUDED RESULTS FROM 226 PATIENTS WITH PULMONARY ARTERIAL HYPERTENSION.BAYER - MOST COMMON ADVERSE EVENTS INSTUDY WERE GENERALLY CONSISTENT WITH THOSE SEEN IN PIVOTAL PATENT STUDY.
