Merck & Co., Inc. Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year of Fiscal 2019. Merck & Co., Inc. revised earnings guidance for the full year of fiscal 2019. The company narrowed and raised its full-year 2019 revenue range to be between $46.5 billion and $47.0 billion, including both the impact of the GARDASIL 9 stockpile borrowing and a negative impact from foreign exchange of approximately 2% at mid-October exchange rates. The company narrowed and reduced its full-year 2019 GAAP EPS range to be between $3.75 and $3.80. The change in the GAAP EPS range primarily reflects the impact of the intangible asset impairment charge.
