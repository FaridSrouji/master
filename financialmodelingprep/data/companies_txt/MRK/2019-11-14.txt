Merck Says Bravecto Plus For Cats Receives Approval From FDA. Nov 15  - Merck & Co Inc <MRK.N>::BRAVECTO® PLUS (FLURALANER AND MOXIDECTIN TOPICAL SOLUTION) FOR CATS RECEIVES APPROVAL FROM US FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION.MERCK ANIMAL HEALTH - BRAVECTO PLUS IS EXPECTED TO BE AVAILABLE IN COMING MONTHS..
