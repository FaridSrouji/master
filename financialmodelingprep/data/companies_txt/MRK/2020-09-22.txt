Alx Oncology Holdings Inc Says Entered Into A Clinical Trial Collaboration With Merck. ALX ONCOLOGY HOLDINGS INC - ENTERED INTO A CLINICAL TRIAL COLLABORATION WITH MERCK.ALX ONCOLOGY - COLLABORATION WITH MERCK TO EVALUATE COMBINATION OF ALX148 & KEYTRUDA FOR TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH HEAD & NECK SQUAMOUS CELL CARCINOMA.ALX ONCOLOGY HOLDINGS - UNDER TERMS OF AGREEMENT, CO WILL CONDUCT A PHASE 2 PROGRAM COMPRISING TWO SEPARATE PHASE 2 STUDIES.
