U.S. FDA approves Merck's Keytruda for bladder cancer. FDA APPROVES MERCK’S KEYTRUDA® (PEMBROLIZUMAB) FOR PATIENTS WITH BCG-UNRESPONSIVE, HIGH-RISK, NON-MUSCLE INVASIVE BLADDER CANCER WITH CARCINOMA IN SITU WITH OR WITHOUT PAPILLARY TUMORS WHO ARE INELIGIBLE FOR OR HAVE ELECTED NOT TO UNDERGO CYSTECTOMY.
