PDS Biotechnology Announces Principal Investigator And Independent Data Monitoring Committee For Its Phase 2 Clinical Collaboration With Merck. PDS BIOTECHNOLOGY ANNOUNCES PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR AND INDEPENDENT DATA MONITORING COMMITTEE FOR ITS PHASE 2 CLINICAL COLLABORATION WITH MERCK.PDS BIOTECHNOLOGY CORP - JARED WEISS WILL BE PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR FOR CO'S PHASE 2 VERSATILE-002 STUDY IN COLLABORATION WITH MERCK.PDS BIOTECHNOLOGY CORP - COMBINATION STUDY IS EXPECTED TO BE INITIATED IN NEXT THREE TO FOUR MONTHS.
