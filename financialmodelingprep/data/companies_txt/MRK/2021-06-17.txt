MSD Announces Supply Agreement with U.S. Government for Molnupiravir, an Investigational Oral Antiviral Candidate for Treatment of Mild to Moderate COVID-19. U.S. GOVERNMENT COMMITS TO PURCHASE APPROXIMATELY 1.7 MILLION COURSES OF MOLNUPIRAVIR UPON ISSUANCE OF EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION OR APPROVAL BY THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION KENILWORTH, N.J., JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MERCK (NYSE: MRK), KNOWN AS MSD OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ENTERED INTO A PROCUREMENT AGREEMENT WITH THE UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT FOR MOLNUPIRAVIR (MK-4482).
