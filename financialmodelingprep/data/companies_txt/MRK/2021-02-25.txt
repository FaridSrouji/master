Merck Animal Health Completes Acquisition of Poultry Sense Limited. MADISON, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MERCK ANIMAL HEALTH COMPLETES ACQUISITION OF POULTRY SENSE LIMITED
PANDION ALERT: Bragar Eagel & Squire, P.C. Investigates Sale of PAND and Encourages Investors to Contact the Firm. NEW YORK, FEB. 25, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BRAGAR EAGEL & SQUIRE, P.C., A NATIONALLY RECOGNIZED STOCKHOLDER RIGHTS LAW FIRM, HAS LAUNCHED AN INVESTIGATION INTO WHETHER THE BOARD MEMBERS OF PANDION THERAPEUTICS, INC. (NASDAQ: PAND) BREACHED THEIR FIDUCIARY DUTIES OR VIOLATED THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS IN CONNECTION WITH THE COMPANY'S ACQUISITION BY MERCK & CO., INC. (NYSE: MRK).
