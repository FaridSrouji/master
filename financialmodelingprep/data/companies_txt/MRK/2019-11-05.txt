Promega Enters Global Collaboration with Merck to Develop Microsatellite Instability (Msi) Companion Diagnostic for Use with Keytruda. Promega Corporation announced it has entered into a global collaboration with Merck, known as MSD outside the United States and Canada, to develop Promega’s microsatellite instability (MSI) technology as an on-label, solid tumor companion diagnostic (CDx) for use with Merck’s anti-PD-1 therapy, KEYTRUDA® (pembrolizumab). The global collaboration will initially seek regulatory approval for the Promega MSI CDx in the United States and China.  Plans to seek approvals in additional territories may follow.
