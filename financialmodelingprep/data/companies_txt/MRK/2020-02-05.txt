Merck Announces Spinoff Of Women'S Health, Trusted Legacy Brands And Biosimilars Products Into New Company. MERCK TO FOCUS ON KEY GROWTH PILLARS THROUGH SPINOFF OF WOMEN’S HEALTH, TRUSTED LEGACY BRANDS AND BIOSIMILARS PRODUCTS INTO NEW COMPANY (“NEWCO”).MERCK & CO INC - NEWCO TO BE A LEADING WOMEN'S HEALTH COMPANY; REPRESENTS TOTAL REVENUE OF APPROXIMATELY $6.5 BILLION FOR MERCK IN 2020.MERCK & CO INC - MERCK TO RETAIN ITS DIVIDEND OF $2.44 PER SHARE POST SEPARATION.MERCK & CO INC - TRANSACTION ENABLES MERCK TO ACHIEVE IN EXCESS OF $1.5 BILLION IN OPERATING EFFICIENCIES BY 2024.MERCK & CO INC - NEWCO WILL PURSUE GLOBAL LEADERSHIP AND SUSTAINABLE GROWTH IN WOMEN'S HEALTH.MERCK & CO INC - ANTICIPATES FUTURE INCREASES IN DIVIDEND WITH GOAL OF ACHIEVING A 47% TO 50% PAYOUT RATIO OVER TIME.MERCK & CO INC - NEWCO EXPECTED TO PAY AN INCREMENTAL DIVIDEND.MERCK & CO INC - SPINOFF OF NEWCO WILL REDUCE MERCK'S HUMAN HEALTH MANUFACTURING FOOTPRINT BY APPROXIMATELY 25%.MERCK & CO INC - MERCK TARGETING NON-GAAP OPERATING MARGIN GREATER THAN 40%.MERCK & CO INC - MERCK WILL RETAIN ITS CURRENT GROWTH PILLARS OF ONCOLOGY, VACCINES, HOSPITAL AND ANIMAL HEALTH.MERCK & CO INC - EXPECTS TO RECEIVE $8 BILLION TO $9 BILLION THROUGH A SPECIAL TAX-FREE DIVIDEND FROM NEWCO.MERCK & CO INC - NEWCO IS EXPECTED TO BE HEADQUARTERED IN NEW JERSEY.MERCK & CO INC - PRODUCTS TO BE SPUN OFF INTO NEWCO ARE EXPECTED TO GENERATE 2020 REVENUE OF APPROXIMATELY $6.5 BILLION WITHIN MERCK.
