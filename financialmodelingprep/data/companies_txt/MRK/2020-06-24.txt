FDA APPROVES MERCK’S KEYTRUDA® (PEMBROLIZUMAB) FOR THE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH RECURRENT OR METASTATIC CUTANEOUS SQUAMOUS CELL CARCINOMA (CSCC) THAT IS NOT CURABLE BY SURGERY OR RADIATION.
