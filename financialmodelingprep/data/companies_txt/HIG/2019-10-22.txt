The Hartford Financial Services Group, Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividends on Common Stock and Series G Preferred Stock Payable on Jan. 2, 2020 and Feb. 18, 2020 Respectively. The Hartford’s board of directors declared a dividend of $0.30 per share of common stock, payable Jan. 2, 2020, to common stock shareholders of record at the close of business on Dec. 2, 2019.The board also declared a dividend of $375 on each of the Series G preferred stock (equivalent to $0.375 per depository share) payable on Feb. 18, 2020, to Series G preferred stock shareholders of record at the close of business on Feb. 1, 2020.
