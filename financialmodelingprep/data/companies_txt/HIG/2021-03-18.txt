The Hartford Confirms Receipt Of Unsolicited Proposal. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HARTFORD (NYSE: HIG) TODAY CONFIRMED THAT IT HAS RECEIVED AN UNSOLICITED, NON-BINDING PROPOSAL FROM CHUBB LIMITED (NYSE: CB) TO ACQUIRE THE HARTFORD. THE HARTFORD'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS IS CAREFULLY CONSIDERING THE PROPOSAL WITH THE ASSISTANCE OF ITS FINANCIAL AND LEGAL ADVISORS. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS IS COMMITTED TO ACTING IN THE BEST INTERESTS OF SHAREHOLDERS OVER THE LONG TERM. ABOUT THE HARTFORD THE HARTFORD IS A LEADER IN PROPERTY AND CASUALTY INSURANCE
