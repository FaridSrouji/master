MGM Resorts Partners with UNLV College of Education to Provide Virtual Tutoring for the Children of MGM Employees. LAS VEGAS, Oct. 30, 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- MGM Resorts International (NYSE: MGM) is partnering with the University of Nevada Las Vegas (UNLV) College of Education to provide free virtual tutoring for children of active MGM Resorts employees for the remainder of the 2020-2021 school year....
