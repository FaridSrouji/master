MGM Resorts International Appoints Jyoti Chopra as Its Senior Vice President, Chief Diversity and Sustainability Officer. MGM Resorts International announced inclusion and sustainability leader Jyoti Chopra as its Senior Vice President, Chief Diversity and Sustainability Officer. Jyoti will be based in Las Vegas and will report directly to President and COO, Bill Hornbuckle. She will also serve as liaison to the Corporate Social Responsibility Committee of the company's Board of Directors. In her new role, Chopra will helm MGM Resorts' Social Impact & Sustainability efforts, working to expand data-driven methods to measure progress in attaining key social impact and environmental sustainability goals by 2025. Chopra will also oversee the MGM Resorts Community Engagement, Diversity and Inclusion, and Environmental Sustainability teams and departments, including oversight of the nonprofit MGM Resorts Foundation and Community Grant Fund, corporate giving program, and Employee Volunteer Program.
MGM Resorts International Announces Redemption of 6.750% Senior Notes Due 2020, 0.250% Senior Notes Due 2020 and 625% Senior Notes Due 2021. MGM Resorts International announced that it will redeem for cash all of its outstanding $360,826,000 6.750% Senior Notes due 2020 (the "6.750% Notes"), $267,476,000 5.250% Senior Notes due 2020 (the "5.250% Notes") and $1,250,000,000 6.625% Senior Notes due 2021 (the "6.625% Notes" and, together with the 6.750% Notes and the 5.250% Notes, the "Notes") on December 18, 2019. In each case, at a redemption price equal to 100.00% of the aggregate principal amount thereof plus a make-whole premium, to be calculated as set forth in each notice of redemption delivered to note holders for the applicable series of Notes (each, a "Notice of Redemption"), plus accrued and unpaid interest, if any, to the Redemption Date.
