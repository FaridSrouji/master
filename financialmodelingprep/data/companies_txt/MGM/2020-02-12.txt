MGM Resorts CEO Says Coronavirus Will Have Near-Term Impact To MGM China, But No Long-Term Impact On Business - Conf Call. MGM RESORTS CEO - "WHILE THE CORONAVIRUS WILL CLEARLY HAVE A NEAR-TERM IMPACT TO MGM CHINA, WE REMAIN CONFIDENT THAT IT WILL NOT HAVE A LONG-TERM IMPACT ON OUR BUSINESS".MGM RESORTS CEO - "WE'RE CURRENTLY NAVIGATING AN EXTREMELY FLUID ENVIRONMENT WITH THE CORONAVIRUS, WHICH WE ARE TAKING VERY SERIOUSLY.".MGM RESORTS CEO SAYS WITH CO'S MACAU CASINOS CLOSED, IT IS INCURRING ABOUT $1.5 MILLION OF OPERATING EXPENSES PER DAY ACROSS BOTH PROPERTIES, MAJORITY OF WHICH IS PAYROLL.MGM RESORTS EXEC SAYS CORONAVIRUS WAS HAVING "VERY MINIMAL IMPACT" ON CORE TRENDS.
Mgm Resorts International Announces CEO Succession And Transition Plan. MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL ANNOUNCES CEO SUCCESSION AND TRANSITION PLAN.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - JIM MURREN, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, HAS INFORMED COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS THAT HE WILL STEP DOWN.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - MURREN WILL CONTINUE TO SERVE IN HIS CURRENT LEADERSHIP ROLES UNTIL A SUCCESSOR IS APPOINTED.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - BOARD HAS FORMED A COMMITTEE OF INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS TO CONDUCT SEARCH PROCESS.MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL - COMMITTEE IS WORKING WITH EXECUTIVE SEARCH FIRM TO IDENTIFY, EVALUATE CANDIDATES TO ASSUME ROLE OF CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
