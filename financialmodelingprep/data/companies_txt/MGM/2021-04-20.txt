BetMGM to Integrate GameSense Responsible Gaming Program into Mobile Gaming. JERSEY CITY, N.J., APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BETMGM, THE MOBILE SPORTS BETTING AND IGAMING COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WILL INTEGRATE INTO ITS PLATFORM GAMESENSE, AN INNOVATIVE RESPONSIBLE GAMING PROGRAM LICENSED TO MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL (NYSE: MGM) BY THE BRITISH COLUMBIA LOTTERY CORPORATION (BCLC).
