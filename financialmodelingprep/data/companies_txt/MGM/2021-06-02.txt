MGM Resorts International Management To Speak At Bernstein's 37th Annual Strategic Decisions Conference. LAS VEGAS, JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MGM RESORTS INTERNATIONAL (NYSE: MGM) ("MGM RESORTS") MANAGEMENT WILL PARTICIPATE IN BERNSTEIN'S 37TH ANNUAL STRATEGIC DECISIONS CONFERENCE ON THURSDAY, JUNE 3, 2021.  MGM RESORTS CEO AND PRESIDENT BILL HORNBUCKLE WILL ALSO SPEAK AT A SESSION SCHEDULED TO BEGIN AT 11:00 A.M.
