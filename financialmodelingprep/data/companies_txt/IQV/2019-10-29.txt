IQVIA Holdings Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. IQVIA Holdings Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,769 million compared to USD 2,594 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 204 million compared to USD 181 million a year ago. Net income was USD 57 million compared to USD 60 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.29 compared to USD 0.3 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.29 compared to USD 0.29 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 8,193 million compared to USD 7,724 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 611 million compared to USD 534 million a year ago. Net income was USD 175 million compared to USD 190 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.89 compared to USD 0.93 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.87 compared to USD 0.91 a year ago.
