IQVIA Acquires Remaining Interest in Q2 Solutions from Quest Diagnostics; Raises Full Year 2021 Adjusted Diluted EPS Guidance. DANBURY, CONN. & RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IQVIA HOLDINGS INC.
