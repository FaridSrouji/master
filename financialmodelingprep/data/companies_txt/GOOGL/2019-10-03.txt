Dunzo Digital Private Limited announced that it has received $45 million in funding from Alphabet Inc., STIC Investments, Inc., 3L Capital, Lightbox Management Ltd.. Dunzo Digital Private Limited announced that it has received $45 million in a round of funding led by returning investor, Alphabet Inc. (NasdaqGS:GOOG.L), STIC Investments, Inc., 3L Capital, and Lightbox Management Ltd. on October 4, 2019. The company has raised $81 million in funding till date. The transaction was raised at a post money valuation of $200 million.
