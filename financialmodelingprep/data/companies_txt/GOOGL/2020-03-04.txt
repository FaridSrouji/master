Telecom Italia Says Co And Google Cloud Launch Strategic Partnership. CO AND GOOGLE CLOUD LAUNCH STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP.CO AND GOOGLE CLOUD HAVE AGREED TO WORK TOGETHER TO BUILD INNOVATIVE PUBLIC, PRIVATE AND HYBRID CLOUD SERVICES.AIMS TO BOOST GROWTH OF REVENUES FROM SERVICES, WITH OBJECTIVE OF REACHING 1 BILLION EUROS IN REVENUES & 0.4 BILLION EUROS IN EBITDA BY 2024.
