Ford Motor Company Reports U.S. Vehicle Sales Results for the Third Quarter of 2019. Ford Motor Company reported U.S. Vehicle Sales results for the third quarter of 2019. For the quarter, the company reported total vehicle sales of U.S. of 580,251 vehicles, representing a 4.9% decline compared to third quarter of 2018.
