Ford Motor Company Declares Fourth Quarter Regular Dividend on Class B and Common Stock, Payable on Dec. 2, 2019. The Board of Directors of Ford Motor Company declared a fourth quarter regular dividend of $0.15 per share on the company’s outstanding Class B and common stock. The fourth quarter dividend is payable on Dec. 2, 2019, to shareholders of record at the close of business on Oct. 22, 2019.
