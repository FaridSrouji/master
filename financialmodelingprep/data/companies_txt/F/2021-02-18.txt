Ford CEO Jim Farley to Speak at Wolfe Research Virtual Global Auto, Auto Tech, and Mobility Conference 2021. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--JIM FARLEY, FORD MOTOR COMPANY CEO, WILL SPEAK AT THE WOLFE RESEARCH VIRTUAL GLOBAL AUTO, AUTO TECH, AND MOBILITY CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, FEB. 24. FARLEY WILL DISCUSS KEY ELEMENTS OF FORD'S PLAN TO TURN AROUND AUTOMOTIVE OPERATIONS AND GROW KEY AREAS OF THE BUSINESS, INCLUDING ELECTRIC VEHICLES, AND EXPAND THE COMPANY'S LEADING GLOBAL COMMERCIAL VEHICLE BUSINESSES WITH A SUITE OF SOFTWARE SERVICES THAT DRIVE LOYALTY AND RECURRING REVENUE STREAMS. FARLEY'S PRES
