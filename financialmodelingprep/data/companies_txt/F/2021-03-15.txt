Ford Appoints Mobility Veteran Brett Wheatley As TransLoc CEO. DEARBORN, MICH., MARCH 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FORD MOTOR COMPANY ANNOUNCES THE APPOINTMENT OF BRETT WHEATLEY AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF TRANSLOC, A TRANSPORTATION SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS COMPANY THAT'S PART OF THE FORD MOBILITY PORTFOLIO.
