Ford Issues Safety Recall For Select 2017-19 Ford Super Duty Supercrew Vehicles With Carpet Flooring. FORD - ISSUING SAFETY RECALL FOR SELECT 2017-19 FORD SUPER DUTY SUPERCREW VEHICLES WITH CARPET FLOORING DUE TO RISK OF POST-CRASH INTERIOR FIRE.FORD MOTOR CO - RECALL AFFECTS 490,574 VEHICLES IN THE U.S. AND FEDERAL TERRITORIES.FORD MOTOR CO - AWARE OF ONE REPORT IN THE U.S. OF A FIRE RELATED TO RISK FROM CARPET FLOORING.
