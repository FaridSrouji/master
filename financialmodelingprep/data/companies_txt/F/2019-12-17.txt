Ford To Invest More Than $1.45 Billion, Add 3,000 Jobs In S.E. Mich. Plants To Deliver New Pickups, SUVs, EVs And AVs. FORD MOTOR CO - IS INVESTING MORE THAN $1.45 BILLION IN TWO SOUTHEAST MICHIGAN MANUFACTURING FACILITIES AND ADDING 3,000 NEW JOBS.FORD MOTOR CO - INVESTING ABOUT $750 MILLION & ADDING 2,700 NEW DIRECT JOBS AT MANUFACTURING FACILITY IN WAYNE, MICHIGAN, DURING NEXT 3 YEARS.FORD MOTOR CO - WILL ALSO INVEST ABOUT $700 MILLION, ADD 300 NEW DIRECT JOBS AT DEARBORN, MICHIGAN MANUFACTURING SITE.FORD - INVESTING AT DEARBORN MANUFACTURING SITE TO SUPPORT PRODUCTION OF FORD F-150, F-150 HYBRID & THE ALL-ELECTRIC F-150, INCLUDING BATTERY ASSEMBLY.FORD - EMPLOYEES AT CO’S AUTONOMOUS VEHICLE, BRONCO & RANGER MODIFICATION CENTER IN WAYNE WILL COMPLETE CO’S AUTONOMOUS VEHICLES STARTING IN 2021.FORD - WILL INSTALL NEW EQUIPMENT TO SUPPORT PRODUCTION OF FORD BRONCO & FORD RANGER, CREATE NEW MODIFICATION CENTER AT WAYNE LOCATION.FORD MOTOR CO - IN 2020, CO WILL DEBUT THE F-150 AND F-150 HYBRID.FORD MOTOR CO - FORD BRONCO WILL BE REVEALED IN THE SPRING OF 2020.
