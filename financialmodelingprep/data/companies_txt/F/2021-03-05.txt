Ford Senior Leaders to Describe Opportunities in Europe at Jefferies Conference on March 10. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORD SENIOR LEADERS WILL DISCUSS THE COMPANY'S EUROPEAN BUSINESS AND ROADMAP FOR SUSTAINED PROFITABILITY WITH JEFFERIES LEAD AUTO ANALYST PHILIPPE HOUCHOIS DURING THE EXANE BNP PARIBAS & JEFFERIES GENEVA MOTOR SHOW CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, MARCH 10, AT 8 A.M. ET. THE SESSION WILL COVER STRATEGIC TOPICS SUCH AS THE FORD OF EUROPE REDESIGN, PLANS TO CAPITALIZE ON THE COMPANY'S GROWING COMMERCIAL VEHICLES BUSINESS, AND FUTURE DIRECTION IN THE REGION FOR PASSEN
