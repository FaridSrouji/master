CelLink Corporation announced that it has received $22.5 million in funding from a group of investors. CelLink Corporation announced that it has received $22.5 million in a round of funding on October 29, 2019. The transaction included participation from Franklin Venture Partners, Fontinalis Partners, LLC, BMW i Ventures, Ford Motor Company (NYSE:F), Lear Corporation (NYSE:LEA), 3M Ventures, SK Telecom Co.,Ltd (KOSE:A017670), Robert Bosch Venture Capital GmbH, and Band of Angels.
Unifor Says Ford Advised Union That Oakville Restructuring Will Result In Loss Of 450 Jobs In Feb. 2020. Oct 28  - Unifor::UNIFOR - IS RENEWING ITS CALL FOR NEW PRODUCT ALLOCATION AT OAKVILLE ASSEMBLY PLANT FOLLOWING TODAY'S RESTRUCTURING ANNOUNCEMENT BY FORD MOTOR COMPANY.UNIFOR - FORD HAS ADVISED UNION THAT RESTRUCTURING AT OAKVILLE ASSEMBLY WILL RESULT IN LOSS OF 450 JOBS IN FEBRUARY 2020.UNIFOR - NEGOTIATIONS BETWEEN UNIFOR AND FORD FOR A NEW COLLECTIVE AGREEMENT ARE SCHEDULED TO TAKE PLACE IN 2020.UNIFOR - FORD HAS ADVISED THAT RESTRUCTURING AT OAKVILLE ASSEMBLY WILL RESULT IN ELIMINATION OF APPROXIMATELY 200 JOBS IN SEPTEMBER 2019.
