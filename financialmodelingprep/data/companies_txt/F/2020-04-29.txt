Ford Says It Believes Phased Restart Of Co's Manufacturing Plants, Supply Network Is Probable Commencing In Q2 2020. FORD MOTOR - BELIEVE PHASED RESTART OF CO'S MANUFACTURING PLANTS, SUPPLY NETWORK & OTHER DEPENDENT FUNCTIONS IS PROBABLE OF COMMENCING IN Q2 2020.FORD - ALTHOUGH ULTIMATE IMPACT ON CO FROM COVID-19 CANNOT BE DETERMINED AT THIS TIME, CO EXPECTS FY 2020 RESULTS OF OPERATIONS TO BE ADVERSELY AFFECTED.FORD - BELIEVE PHASED RESTART, CASH ON HAND, FUNDS RECEIVED IN APRIL 2020 WILL PROVIDE SUFFICIENT LIQUIDITY TO FUND OPERATIONS FOR AT LEAST NEXT 12 MONTHS.FORD - EXPECT GOVERNMENT RELIEF PROGRAMS, FORD CREDIT’S DEFERRAL PROGRAMS, AMONG OTHERS TO MITIGATE IMPACTS, BUT RESULT IN CREDIT LOSSES EXPECTED TO BE ADVERSE.FORD - ESTIMATE TO INCUR CHARGES IN 2020 OF $700 MILLION - $1.2 BILLION RELATED TO ACTIONS, ATTRIBUTABLE TO EMPLOYEE SEPARATIONS, DEPRECIATION, SOME  SETTLEMENTS.FORD - AGGREGATE RISK ESTIMATE FOR REASONABLY POSSIBLE LOSS IN EXCESS OF ACCRUALS FOR ALL MATERIAL MATTERS TO BE A RANGE OF UP TO ABOUT $400 MILLION.FORD - WE HAVE REASONABLY POSSIBLE RISK OF LOSS FOR AN EMISSION CERTIFICATION MATTER.FORD - AT THIS STAGE, CANNOT ESTIMATE RISK OF LOSS OR PREDICT THE OUTCOME OF EMISSION CERTIFICATE MATTER.FORD - IN TOTAL, THE IMPACT OF COVID-19 DETERIORATED FIRST QUARTER 2020 EBT BY ABOUT $700 MILLION.FORD - FOR 2020, FORD CREDIT NOW PROJECTS FULL YEAR PUBLIC TERM FUNDING IN THE RANGE OF $12 BILLION TO $22 BILLION.
