Ford Issues Safety Recall For Select 2006-10 Ford Fusion, Mercury Milan & Lincoln Mkz Vehicles. FORD MOTOR CO -  ISSUING A SAFETY RECALL FOR SELECT 2006-10 FORD FUSION, MERCURY MILAN AND LINCOLN MKZ VEHICLES.FORD MOTOR CO -  RECALL AFFECTS 600,166 VEHICLES IN THE U.S. AND FEDERAL TERRITORIES.FORD SAYS IS AWARE OF 15 REPORTS OF ACCIDENTS, 2 INJURIES THAT MAY BE RELATED TO THE CONDITION DUE TO WHICH IT IS RECALLING THE VEHICLES.
