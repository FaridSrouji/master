ALD Automotive And Ford Launch New Fleet Management Business. FORD AND ALD AUTOMOTIVE LAUNCH NEW FLEET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS IN EUROPE TO SUPPORT COMMERCIAL VEHICLE AND FLEET GROWTH.FORD AND ALD AUTOMOTIVE HAVE SIGNED SHAREHOLDER AGREEMENT TO CREATE AN INTEGRATED LEASING AND FLEET MANAGEMENT SOLUTION FOR EUROPEAN CUSTOMERS.FORD FLEET MANAGEMENT WILL BEGIN OPERATING IN UK BY AUTUMN, SUBJECT TO APPROVAL BY RELEVANT AUTHORITIES.
