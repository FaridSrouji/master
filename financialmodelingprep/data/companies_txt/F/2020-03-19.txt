Ford Motor Co, Ford Credit Amend 2015 Relationship Agreement. FORD MOTOR CO - IN 2015, FORD MOTOR COMPANY AND FORD CREDIT ENTERED INTO AN AMENDED AND RESTATED RELATIONSHIP AGREEMENT.FORD MOTOR CO -2015 AMENDED AND RESTATED RELATIONSHIP AGREEMENT WAS TO ALLOCATE TO FORD CREDIT PORTION OF COMMITMENTS UNDER CORPORATE CREDIT FACILITY.FORD MOTOR CO - ON MARCH 19, CO, FORD CREDIT FURTHER AMENDED AND RESTATED RELATIONSHIP AGREEMENT TO, AMONG OTHERS, REMOVE ALLOCATION TO FORD CREDIT.
Ford Withdraws Guidance For 2020 Financial Performance. FORD TAKES ACTION TO ADDRESS EFFECTS OF CORONAVIRUS PANDEMIC; COMPANY OFFERS NEW-CAR CUSTOMERS SIX-MONTH PAYMENT RELIEF.FORD MOTOR CO - WITHDRAWAL OF COMPANY GUIDANCE FOR 2020 FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE.FORD MOTOR CO - $15.4 BILLION OF ADDITIONAL CASH ON BALANCE SHEET, DRAWING FROM TWO CREDIT LINES.FORD MOTOR CO - DIVIDEND SUSPENSION TO PRESERVE CASH AND PROVIDE ADDITIONAL FLEXIBILITY IN CURRENT ENVIRONMENT.FORD MOTOR CO - NOTIFIED LENDERS THAT IT WILL BORROW TOTAL UNUSED AMOUNTS AGAINST TWO LINES OF CREDIT.FORD MOTOR CO - OFFERING SIX MONTHS OF PAYMENT RELIEF FOR ELIGIBLE NEW-CAR CUSTOMERS WHO FINANCE THEIR PURCHASES THROUGH FORD CREDIT.FORD MOTOR CO - TWO UNUSED LINES OF CREDIT INCLUDE $13.4 BILLION UNDER ITS CORPORATE CREDIT FACILITY AND $2 BILLION UNDER ITS SUPPLEMENTAL CREDIT FACILITY.FORD MOTOR CO - THREE MONTH PAYMENT DEFERRAL FOR ELIGIBLE U.S. NEW-CAR CUSTOMERS, PLUS THREE MORE PAID BY FORD.FORD - INCREMENTAL CASH FROM BORROWINGS FROM 2 LINES OF CREDIT WILL BE USED TO OFFSET TEMPORARY WORKING CAPITAL IMPACTS OF CORONAVIRUS-RELATED PRODUCTION SHUT DOWNS.FORD - INCREMENTAL CASH FROM BORROWINGS FROM 2 LINES OF CREDIT WILL ALSO BE USED TO PRESERVE FINANCIAL FLEXIBILITY.FORD MOTOR CO - WILL WORK WITH LABOR REPRESENTATIVES TO SAFELY AND EFFECTIVELY RESTART PRODUCTION IN WEEKS TO COME.
