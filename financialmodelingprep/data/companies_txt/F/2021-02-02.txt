Ford to Discuss Results for Fourth-Quarter and Full-Year 2020 with J.P. Morgan Securities. DEARBORN, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SENIOR LEADERS FROM FORD WILL DISCUSS THE FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2020 OPERATING ENVIRONMENT AND THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL RESULTS, WHICH WILL BE RELEASED THIS THURSDAY, DURING A FIRESIDE CHAT HOSTED BY J.P. MORGAN SECURITIES AUTO ANALYST RYAN BRINKMAN. THE DISCUSSION WILL TAKE PLACE TUESDAY, FEB. 9 AT 11 A.M. EST.
