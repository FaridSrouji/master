Ford Motor Company Reports Unaudited Consolidated Financial Results for the Third Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2019. Ford Motor Company reported unaudited consolidated financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, total revenues were $36,990 million against $37,666 million reported a year ago. Operating loss was $261 million against income of $864 million reported a year ago. Net income was $423 million against $993 million reported a year ago. Basic income per share is $0.11 against $0.25 reported a year ago.For the nine months, total revenues were $116,185 million against $118,545 million reported a year ago. Operating income was $1,031 million against $2,571 million reported a year ago. Net income was $1,756 million against $3,807 million reported a year ago. Basic income per share is $0.43 against $0.95 reported a year ago.
