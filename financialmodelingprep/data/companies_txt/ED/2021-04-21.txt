Con Edison's 'Smart' Ideas Earn Recognition From Consumer Research Group. NEW YORK, APRIL 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- A CONSUMER RESEARCH GROUP TODAY BESTOWED AN AWARD ON CON EDISON FOR USING ITS NEW SMART METERS TO IMPROVE PROGRAMS THAT PAY RESIDENTIAL CUSTOMERS FOR REDUCING THEIR USAGE AT TIMES OF HIGH DEMAND. THE SMART ENERGY CONSUMER COLLABORATIVE NAMED CON EDISON WINNER OF ITS BEST PRACTICES AWARD IN THE SMART ENERGY INNOVATION CATEGORY.
