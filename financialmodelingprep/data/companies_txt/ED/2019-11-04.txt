Consolidated Edison, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Consolidated Edison, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 3,365 million compared to USD 3,328 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 867 million compared to USD 826 million a year ago. Net income was USD 473 million compared to USD 435 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 1.42 compared to USD 1.4 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 1.42 compared to USD 1.39 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 9,623 million compared to USD 9,388 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,111 million compared to USD 2,007 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,048 million compared to USD 1,051 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 3.2 compared to USD 3.38 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 3.19 compared to USD 3.37 a year ago.
