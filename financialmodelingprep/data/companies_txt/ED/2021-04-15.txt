Con Edison Declares Common Stock Dividend. NEW YORK, APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. (CON EDISON) (NYSE: ED) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 77.5 CENTS A SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 15, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 19, 2021. CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. IS ONE OF THE NATION'S LARGEST INVESTOR-OWNED ENERGY-DELIVERY COMPANIES, WITH APPROXIMATELY $12 BILLION IN ANNUAL REVENUES AND $63 BILLION IN ASSETS.
