Con Edison Announces Public Offering Of Common Shares. NEW YORK, JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSOLIDATED EDISON, INC. ("CON EDISON") (NYSE: ED) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS AGREED TO ISSUE 10,100,000 OF ITS COMMON SHARES.  THESE COMMON SHARES ARE BEING OFFERED BY BARCLAYS UNDER CON EDISON'S EFFECTIVE SHELF REGISTRATION STATEMENT FILED WITH THE SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION (THE "COMMISSION").
