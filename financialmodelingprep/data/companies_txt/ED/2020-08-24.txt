Consolidated Edison Restores Service To Customers Hit By Storm Isaias. UTILITIES HAVE RESTORED ELECTRIC SERVICE TO ALL OF THEIR CUSTOMERS WHOSE SERVICE WAS INTERRUPTED BY TROPICAL STORM ISAIAS.CECONY & O&R ESTIMATE INCREMENTAL RESPONSE & RESTORATION COSTS FOR TROPICAL STORM ISAIAS TO BE UP TO $115 MILLION & $35 MILLION.NYDPS ISSUED TO EACH OF CECONY AND O&R A NOTICE OF APPARENT VIOLATIONS RELATED TO TROPICAL STORM ISAIAS.NOTICES ASSERT THAT UTILITIES ARE IN APPARENT VIOLATION OF NEW YORK STATE PUBLIC SERVICE LAW.
