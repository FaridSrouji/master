MetLife, Inc. Declares Fourth Quarter 2019 Common Stock Dividend, Payable on Dec. 13, 2019. MetLife, Inc. announced that its board of directors has declared a fourth quarter 2019 common stock dividend of $0.44 per share. The dividend will be payable on Dec. 13, 2019, to shareholders of record as of Nov. 5, 2019.
