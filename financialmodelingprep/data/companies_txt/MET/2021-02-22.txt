MetLife Investment Management Supports Prince George's County Public Schools With $203.5 Million in Public-Private Partnership Financing. WHIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (MIM), THE INSTITUTIONAL ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF METLIFE, INC. (NYSE: MET), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED A PUBLIC-PRIVATE PARTNERSHIP (PPP) TRANSACTION THAT WILL PROVIDE $203.5 MILLION IN FUNDING FOR MARYLAND'S PRINCE GEORGE'S COUNTY PUBLIC SCHOOLS, THE SECOND LARGEST PUBLIC SCHOOL SYSTEM IN THE STATE AND A TOP 20 PUBLIC SCHOOL DISTRICT IN THE UNITED STATES1.
