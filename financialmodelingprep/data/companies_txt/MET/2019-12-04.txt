MetLife, Inc. (NYSE:MET) entered into a definitive agreement to acquire PetFirst Healthcare LLC.. MetLife, Inc. (NYSE:MET) entered into a definitive agreement to acquire PetFirst Healthcare LLC on December 5, 2019. PetFirst has about 60 employees in its Jeffersonville offices and all are expected to continue in their current or similar positions. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions and approvals and is expected to close in the first quarter of 2020. Guggenheim Securities, LLC acted as the financial advisor and Mayer Brown LLP acted as the legal advisor to MetLife, Inc.
