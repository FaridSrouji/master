MetLife Investment Management to Provide USD 161 Million / GBP 117.5 Million in Financing for English Football League. WHIPPANY, N.J. & LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--METLIFE INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (MIM), THE INSTITUTIONAL ASSET MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF METLIFE, INC.
