Paycom Software, Inc. Provides Financial Guidance for the Quarter and Year Ending December 31, 2019. Paycom Software, Inc. provided financial guidance for the quarter and year ending December 31, 2019. For the quarter, the company expects total revenues in the range of $188.5 million to $190.5 million. For the year ending December 31, 2019, the company expects total revenues in the range of $733 million to $735 million.
