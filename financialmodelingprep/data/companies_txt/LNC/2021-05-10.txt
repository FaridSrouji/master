Greater Flexibility and Growth Potential Offered by Lincoln Financial Group's Indexed Universal Life Policy Enhancements. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP TODAY ANNOUNCED ENHANCEMENTS TO ITS INDEXED UNIVERSAL LIFE INSURANCE PRODUCTS, TO PROVIDE EVEN GREATER INVESTMENT FLEXIBILITY AND INCREASED CASH VALUE GROWTH POTENTIAL THAT CAN BE ACCESSED FOR LIFE'S CHANGING NEEDS. THE ENHANCEMENTS INCLUDE THE INTRODUCTION OF THE FIDELITY AIM® DIVIDEND INDEXED ACCOUNT WITH A FIXED BONUS, AND INTEGRATION OF CHANGES TO THE INTERNAL REVENUE CODE TO CREATE GREATER CONSUMER VALUE WHEN USING THE POLICIES AS A TAX
