Lincoln National Corporation's Board of Directors Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend. RADNOR, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--LINCOLN FINANCIAL GROUP (NYSE:LNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF LINCOLN NATIONAL CORPORATION DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.42 PER SHARE ON THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK WILL BE PAYABLE AUGUST 2, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 12, 2021.
