Aon plc (NYSE:AON) entered into definitive agreement to acquire CoverWallet, Inc. on November 20, 2019. The transaction is expected to close during the first quarter of 2020. Keith S. Crow and Melanie Harmon of Kirkland & Ellis LLP acted as legal advisor to Aon plc in the transaction.
