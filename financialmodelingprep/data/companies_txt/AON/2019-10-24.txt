Aon plc Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Aon plc announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,379 million compared to USD 2,349 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 360 million compared to USD 262 million a year ago. Net income was USD 222 million compared to USD 147 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.94 compared to USD 0.61 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.94 compared to USD 0.6 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.93 compared to USD 0.61 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.93 compared to USD 0.6 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 8,128 million compared to USD 8,000 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,645 million compared to USD 1,045 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,158 million compared to USD 789 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 4.83 compared to USD 3.18 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 4.83 compared to USD 3.2 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 4.79 compared to USD 3.17 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.79 compared to USD 3.19 a year ago.
