Regions Reports $237 Mln Net Loss Available To Common Shareholders For Q2. REGIONS REPORTS $237 MILLION NET LOSS AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2020.Q2 LOSS PER SHARE $0.25.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.08 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY RESULTS INCLUDE A CREDIT LOSS PROVISION IN EXCESS OF NET CHARGE-OFFS OF $700 MILLION.QTRLY TOTAL REVENUE $1.55 BILLION VERSUS $1.44 BILLION.QTRLY ANNUALIZED NET CHARGE-OFFS INCREASED 36 BASIS POINTS COMPARED WITH Q2 OF 2019.QTRLY NET INTEREST MARGIN (FTE) 3.19% VERSUS 3.45%.QTRLY NET INTEREST INCOME $972 MILLION VERSUS $942 MILLION.QTRLY AVERAGE LOANS AND LEASES INCREASED ABOUT 10%ON REPORTED BASIS & 11% ON ADJUSTED BASIS COMPARED TO PRIOR QUARTER.Q2 REVENUE VIEW $1.47 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY NET INTEREST INCOME BENEFITED FROM CO'S SIGNIFICANT HEDGING PROGRAM, ELEVATED LOAN BALANCES.DURING Q2, CREDIT LOSS PROVISION TOTALED $882 MILLION.
