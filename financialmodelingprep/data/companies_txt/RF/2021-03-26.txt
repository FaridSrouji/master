Regions Foundation Announces $100,000 in Tornado Recovery Grants. BIRMINGHAM, ALA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE REGIONS FOUNDATION, A NONPROFIT INITIATIVE OF REGIONS BANK, ANNOUNCED A SERIES OF GRANTS SUPPORTING TORNADO RECOVERY.
