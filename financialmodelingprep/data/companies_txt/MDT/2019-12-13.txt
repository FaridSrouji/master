United Therapeutics Says On Dec 13, Co Announced Delay In Potential Launch Of Implantable System For Remodulin From 2020 To 2021. UNITED THERAPEUTICS - ON DEC 13, CO ANNOUNCED DELAY IN POTENTIAL LAUNCH OF IMPLANTABLE SYSTEM FOR REMODULIN FROM 2020 TO 2021.UNITED THERAPEUTICS - CO'S ABILITY TO LAUNCH IMPLANTABLE SYSTEM FOR REMODULIN IS SUBJECT TO MEDTRONIC SATISFYING VARIOUS CONDITIONS TO ITS PMA APPROVAL.UNITED THERAPEUTICS - MEDTRONIC HAS INDICATED THAT CONDITIONS TO ITS PMA APPROVAL WILL NOT BE SATISFIED DURING 2020.
