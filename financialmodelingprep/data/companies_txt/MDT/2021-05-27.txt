Medtronic Reports Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2021 Financial Results; Announces 9% Dividend Increase. DUBLIN, MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT) TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL YEAR 2021, WHICH ENDED APRIL 30, 2021. THE COMPANY REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER WORLDWIDE REVENUE OF $8.188 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF 37 PERCENT AS REPORTED AND 32 PERCENT ON AN ORGANIC BASIS, WHICH ADJUSTS FOR THE $241 MILLION BENEFIT OF FOREIGN CURRENCY TRANSLATION.
