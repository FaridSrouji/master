Medtronic and Medtronic Foundation Partner to Address COVID-19 Surges with Focus on Equity. DUBLIN AND MINNEAPOLIS, MAY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT) AND THE MEDTRONIC FOUNDATION ANNOUNCED AN ADDITIONAL $3M USD FOR COVID-19 RELIEF EFFORTS – A COMBINATION OF FINANCIAL RESOURCES, PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE) DONATIONS, AND MEDTRONIC EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT – FOCUSED ON UNDERSERVED AREAS OF INDIA, BRAZIL, THE UNITED STATES, AND OTHER GLOBAL REGIONS. THIS LATEST COVID-19 RESPONSE EXPANDS MEDTRONIC AND MEDTRONIC FOUNDATION'S COVID-19 SUPPORT TO $56M USD.
