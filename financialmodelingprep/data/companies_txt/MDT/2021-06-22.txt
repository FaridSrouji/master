First Procedure in the World with Medtronic Hugo™ Robotic-Assisted Surgery System Performed at Clínica Santa Maria in Chile. THE ROBOTIC PROSTATECTOMY MARKED THE START OF THE HUGO™ RAS SYSTEM GLOBAL PATIENT REGISTRY DUBLIN, JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE FIRST PATIENT PROCEDURE WITH THE HUGO™ ROBOTIC-ASSISTED SURGERY (RAS) SYSTEM. THE ROBOTIC PROSTATECTOMY WAS PERFORMED JUNE 19 BY UROLOGICAL SURGEON RUBEN OLIVARES, M.D.
