Medtronic Announces Voluntary Recall of Unused Valiant Navion™ Thoracic Stent Graft System. DUBLIN, FEB. 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEDTRONIC PLC (NYSE:MDT), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY, HAS VOLUNTARILY ISSUED A GLOBAL RECALL OF UNUSED MEDTRONIC VALIANT NAVION™ THORACIC STENT GRAFT SYSTEM AND INFORMED PHYSICIANS TO IMMEDIATELY CEASE USE OF THE DEVICE UNTIL FURTHER...
