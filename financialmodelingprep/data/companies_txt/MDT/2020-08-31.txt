FDA Approves Medtronic's Minimed 770G System For Use By Individuals Aged 2 To 6 With Type 1 Diabetes. FDA - APPROVED MINIMED 770G SYSTEM FOR USE BY INDIVIDUALS AGED 2 TO 6 WITH TYPE 1 DIABETES.FDA - APPROVAL OF MINIMED 770G HYBRID CLOSED LOOP SYSTEM WAS GRANTED TO MEDTRONIC.FDA - MINIMED 770G SYSTEM IS NOT APPROVED FOR USE IN CHILDREN YOUNGER THAN 2 YRS OLD, IN INDIVIDUALS WHO REQUIRE LESS THAN 8 UNITS OF INSULIN PER DAY.FDA - AS PART OF APPROVAL,MINIMED 770G MANUFACTURER REQUIRED TO CONDUCT STUDY EVALUATING PERFORMANCE IN REAL-WORLD SETTINGS IN CHILDREN OF 2-6 YRS AGE.FDA - RISKS ASSOCIATED WITH USE OF MINIMED 770G MAY INCLUDE HYPOGLYCEMIA, HYPERGLYCEMIA, SKIN IRRITATION OR REDNESS AROUND DEVICE'S INFUSION PATCH.
