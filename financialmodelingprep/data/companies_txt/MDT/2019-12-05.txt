Medtronic plc Approves Third Quarter Cash Dividend, Payable on January 17, 2020. The board of directors of Medtronic plc approved the fiscal year 2020 third quarter cash dividend of $0.54 per ordinary share, representing an 8% increase over the prior year. This quarterly declaration is consistent with the dividend announcement made by the company in June 2019. Medtronic is a constituent of the S&P 500 Dividend Aristocrats index, having increased its annual dividend payment for the past 42 consecutive years. The dividend is payable on January 17, 2020, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 27, 2019.
