WMT BREAKING ALERT: ROSEN, NATIONAL INVESTOR COUNSEL, Encourages Walmart Inc. Investors to Secure Counsel Before Important Deadline in Securities Class Action - WMT. NEW YORK, MARCH 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHY: ROSEN LAW FIRM, A GLOBAL INVESTOR RIGHTS LAW FIRM, REMINDS PURCHASERS OF THE SECURITIES OF WALMART INC. (NYSE: WMT) BETWEEN MARCH 30, 2016 AND DECEMBER 22, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD"), OF THE IMPORTANT MARCH 22, 2021 LEAD PLAINTIFF DEADLINE IN THE SECURITIES CLASS ACTION FIRST FILED BY THE FIRM. SO WHAT: IF YOU PURCHASED WALMART SECURITIES DURING THE CLASS PERIOD YOU MAY BE ENTITLED TO COMPENSATION WITHOUT PAYMENT OF ANY OUT OF POCKET FEES OR COSTS THROUGH A CONTINGENCY FEE ARRANGEMENT.
