ROSEN, TOP RANKED INVESTOR COUNSEL, Files First Securities Class Action Lawsuit Against Walmart Inc. - WMT. NEW YORK, NEW YORK--(NEWSFILE CORP. - JANUARY 22, 2021) - ROSEN LAW FIRM, A GLOBAL INVESTOR RIGHTS LAW FIRM, ANNOUNCES IT HAS FILED A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT ON BEHALF OF PURCHASERS OF THE SECURITIES OF WALMART INC. (NYSE: WMT) BETWEEN MARCH 30, 2016 AND DECEMBER 22, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
