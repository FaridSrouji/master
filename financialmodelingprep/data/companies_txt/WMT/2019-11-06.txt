Maple Leaf Foods Names Finance Leader Geert Verellen Its New CFO. MAPLE LEAF FOODS NAMES FINANCE LEADER GEERT VERELLEN ITS NEW CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.VERELLEN WILL SUCCEED DEBBIE SIMPSON AS CFO.MAPLE LEAF FOODS - VERELLEN JOINS MAPLE LEAF FOODS FROM WALMART WHERE HE SERVED AS REGIONAL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER FOR WALMART CANADA, INDIA & JAPAN.NAMED GEERT VERELLEN ITS NEW CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 6, 2020.
