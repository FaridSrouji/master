WMT-Pawar Law Group Announces a Securities Class Action Lawsuit Against Walmart Inc.– WMT. NEW YORK, FEB. 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PAWAR LAW GROUP ANNOUNCES A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS WHO PURCHASED SHARES OF  WALMART INC. (NYSE: WMT) FROM MARCH 30, 2016 THROUGH DECEMBER 22, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”). THE LAWSUIT SEEKS TO RECOVER DAMAGES FOR WALMART INC. INVESTORS UNDER THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS.
