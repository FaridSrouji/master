Walmart Provides Digital COVID-19 Vaccine Record. BENTONVILLE, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALMART IS EMPOWERING PEOPLE WITH INCREASED ACCESS TO THEIR HEALTH DATA THROUGH A FREE, SECURE DIGITAL WALLET THAT THEY OWN AND DECIDE WHETHER TO USE TO SHARE THEIR HEALTH INFORMATION, STARTING WITH THE COVID-19 VACCINE RECORD. THE OFFERING PROVIDES INDIVIDUALS WITH AN OPPORTUNITY TO SECURELY STORE THEIR COVID-19 VACCINE RECORD IN THEIR PHARMACY ACCOUNT ON WALMART.COM OR SAMSCLUB.COM. THE VACCINE RECORD CAN BE PRINTED, SAVED ON A DEVICE OR SHARED, GIVING PEOP
