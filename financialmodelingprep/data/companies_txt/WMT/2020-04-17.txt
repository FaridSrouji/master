Walmart Announces New Commitment To Hire 50,000 Additional Associates. WALMART - ANNOUNCING A NEW COMMITMENT TO HIRE AN ADDITIONAL 50,000 ASSOCIATES.WALMART - "WE CONTINUE TO SEE STRONG DEMAND IN OUR STORES".WALMART - OF THE ASSOCIATES HIRED, APPROXIMATELY 85 PERCENT ARE BEING HIRED INTO TEMPORARY OR PART-TIME ROLES.WALMART - "WE’LL CONTINUE TO HIRE NEW ASSOCIATES THROUGH OUR EXPEDITED HIRING PROCESS".
