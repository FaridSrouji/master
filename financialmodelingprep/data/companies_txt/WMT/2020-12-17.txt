Walmart business and philanthropy come together to help fight hunger this holiday season. BENTONVILLE, ARK.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE HOLIDAYS ARE A TIME OF GIVING, BUT THEY ARE ALSO A TIME OF GREAT NEED. FOOD INSECURITY CONTINUES TO PLAGUE FAMILIES AND INDIVIDUALS ACROSS THE COUNTRY, WITH AN ESTIMATED 1 IN 6 PEOPLE AT RISK OF HUNGER AS A RESULT OF COVID-19. THIS IS COMPARED TO A PRE-PANDEMIC NUMBER OF 1 IN 9 STRUGGLING WITH FOOD INSECURITY, ACCORDING TO FEEDING AMERICA®, THE NATION'S LARGEST HUNGER-RELIEF ORGANIZATION. AS A RESULT, FOOD BANKS AND PANTRIES ARE HAVING DIFFICULTIES KEEPIN
