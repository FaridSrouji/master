Walmart CEO Says Co Needs "To Go Further In Hiring Black And African American Associates". WALMART CEO MCMILLON SAYS CO NEEDS "TO GO FURTHER IN HIRING BLACK AND AFRICAN AMERICAN ASSOCIATES ACROSS ALL LEVELS AND POSITIONS".WALMART CEO MCMILLON SAYS "WE WILL ALSO FOCUS ON TRANSPARENCY IN OUR PRACTICES AND ARE COMMITTED TO FAIR PAY".WALMART SAYS WILL REVIEW RECRUITING, HIRING PRACTICES; AN IMPORTANT COMPONENT OF WILL TAKE PLACE IN CRIMINAL JUSTICE WORKSTREAM GROUP CREATED BY CO.WALMART - CRIMINAL JUSTICE WORKSTREAM TO REVIEWING HIRING PROCESS TO ENSURE NON-VIOLENT, FORMERLY INCARCERATED APPLICANTS ARE APPROPRIATELY CONSIDERED.
