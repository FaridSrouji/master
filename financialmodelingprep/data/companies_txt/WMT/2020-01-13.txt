Massmart Begins Potential Store Closure Consultation, Says About 1,440 Jobs Could Be Affected. MASSMART COMMENCES CONSULTATION PROCESS IN TERMS OF SECTION 189 AND 189A OF THE LABOUR RELATIONS ACT.A TOTAL OF 34 DION-WIRED AND MASSCASH STORES AND APPROXIMATELY 1 440 EMPLOYEES ARE POTENTIALLY AFFECTED BY THIS PROCESS.RECENTLY CONDUCTED STORE OPTIMISATION PROJECT THAT HIGHLIGHTED A NUMBER OF "UNDERPERFORMING" STORES IN ITS PORTFOLIO.
