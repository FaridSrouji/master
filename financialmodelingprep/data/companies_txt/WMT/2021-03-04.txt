MARCH 22 DEADLINE: Zhang Investor Law Alerts Investors of Deadline in Securities Class Action Lawsuit Against Walmart Inc. – WMT. NEW YORK, MARCH 04, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ZHANG INVESTOR LAW ANNOUNCES A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS WHO BOUGHT SHARES OF WALMART INC. (NYSE: WMT) BETWEEN MARCH 30, 2016 AND DECEMBER 22, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”).
