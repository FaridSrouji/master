Walmart, AT&T Team Up through Emergency Broadband Benefit Program to Help More People Access the Internet. BENTONVILLE, ARK. & ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TO HELP THE MILLIONS OF AMERICANS WHO ARE EXPERIENCING FINANCIAL HARDSHIP AS A RESULT OF COVID-19, WALMART AND AT&T ARE WORKING TOGETHER TO OFFER ELIGIBLE CUSTOMERS SELECT HOME INTERNET AND WIRELESS PLANS VIA AT&T PREPAID AND CRICKET WIRELESS FOR LITTLE OR EVEN NO COST. THESE OFFERS ARE AVAILABLE THROUGH THE EMERGENCY BROADBAND BENEFIT PROGRAM (EBB),1 AND CUSTOMERS CAN FIND ELIGIBLE PLANS ON WALMART.COM AND IN MORE THAN 2,300 WALMART STORES
