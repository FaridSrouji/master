Walmart Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended October 31, 2019. Walmart Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended October 31, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 127,991 million compared to USD 124,894 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 4,718 million compared to USD 4,986 million a year ago. Net income was USD 3,288 million compared to USD 1,710 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.16 compared to USD 0.58 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.15 compared to USD 0.58 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 382,293 million compared to USD 375,612 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 15,246 million compared to USD 15,890 million a year ago. Net income was USD 10,740 million compared to USD 2,983 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.76 compared to USD 1.01 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.74 compared to USD 1.01 a year ago.
