Bank Of America Corp - Total Compensation For CEO Brian Moynihan For 2019 Was $26 Mln. BANK OF AMERICA CORP - TOTAL COMPENSATION FOR CEO BRIAN MOYNIHAN FOR 2019 WAS $26 MILLION VERSUS $22.8 MILLION IN 2018.BANK OF AMERICA CORP SAYS CEO TO MEDIAN EMPLOYEE PAY RATIO IS 276:1.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - TOTAL COMPENSATION FOR CFO PAUL DONOFRIO FOR 2019 WAS $12.6 MILLION VERSUS $11.9 MILLION IN 2018.BANK OF AMERICA CORP SAYS COO THOMAS MONTAG'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $19.5 MILLION VERSUS $18.8 MILLION IN FY 2018.BANK OF AMERICA CORP SAYS CHIEF RISK OFFICER, GEOFFREY GREENER'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $12.4 MILLION VERSUS $11.7 MILLION IN FY 2018 - SEC FILING.
