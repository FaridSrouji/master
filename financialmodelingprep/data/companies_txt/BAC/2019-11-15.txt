Gevo Appoints Lynn Smull as Chief Financial Officer. Gevo Inc. announced the appointment of Lynn Smull to the position of Chief Financial Officer. Smull is expected to start in this new role effective December 16, 2019. Prior to joining Gevo, Mr. Smull most recently served as Chief Financial Officer of One Energy Enterprises, LLC. Mr. Smull has thirty years of finance and commercial experience on corporate, investment banking, and private equity platforms including Calpine Corporation, Bank of America, Salomon Brothers, and Bechtel. Mr. Smull has managed large capital raisings, M&A transactions, and corporate finance and accounting functions in the renewable and conventional power and energy sectors, as well as in several other heavy infrastructure sectors.
