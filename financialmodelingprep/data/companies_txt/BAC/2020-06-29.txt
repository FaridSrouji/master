Bank Of America Provides Statement On Federal Reserve’S 2020 Comprehensive Capital Analysis And Review. BANK OF AMERICA STATEMENT ON FEDERAL RESERVE’S 2020 COMPREHENSIVE CAPITAL ANALYSIS AND REVIEW.BANK OF AMERICA - BASED ON 2020 CCAR RESULTS, WILL BE SUBJECT TO PRELIMINARY 2.5% STRESS CAPITAL BUFFER FOR PERIOD FROM OCT 1 2020 & ENDING ON SEPT 30, 2021.BANK OF AMERICA - 2020 CCAR RESULTS REQUIRES CO'S CET1 RATIO UNDER APPLICABLE REGULATORY STANDARDS TO REMAIN ABOVE 9.5% DURING OCT 1, 2020 TO SEPT 30, 2021.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - INTENDS TO MAINTAIN QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND AT CURRENT RATE OF $0.18 UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE.BANK OF AMERICA - ADDITIONAL CAPITAL DISTRIBUTIONS, INCLUDING COMMON STOCK REPURCHASES, WILL ALSO BE SUBJECT TO APPROVAL BY CO'S BOARD.
