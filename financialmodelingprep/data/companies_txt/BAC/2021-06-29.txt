Bank of America Recommends Shareholders Reject “Mini-Tender” Offer by Tutanota LLC. CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BANK OF AMERICA TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT RECENTLY LEARNED OF AN UNSOLICITED “MINI-TENDER” OFFER MADE BY TUTANOTA LLC (TUTANOTA) TO BANK OF AMERICA SHAREHOLDERS TO PURCHASE UP TO 1 MILLION SHARES OF BANK OF AMERICA COMMON STOCK AT $47 PER SHARE. THE OFFER PRICE OF $47 PER SHARE IS CONDITIONED ON, AMONG OTHER THINGS, THE CLOSING PRICE PER SHARE OF BANK OF AMERICA'S COMMON STOCK EXCEEDING $47 PER SHARE ON THE LAST TRADING DAY BEFORE THE OFFER EXPIRES.
