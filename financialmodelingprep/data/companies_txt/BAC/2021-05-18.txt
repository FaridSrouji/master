Bank of America and NACA Expand Mortgage Program to $15 Billion to help Low-to-Moderate-Income Homebuyers in Achieving Affordable Homeownership. CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BANK OF AMERICA AND THE NEIGHBORHOOD ASSISTANCE CORPORATION OF AMERICA (NACA) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EXPANSION OF THEIR NATIONAL AFFORDABLE HOMEOWNERSHIP MORTGAGE PROGRAM, WITH A GOAL OF PROVIDING $15 BILLION IN MORTGAGES TO LOW-TO-MODERATE INCOME (LMI) HOMEBUYERS THROUGH MAY 2027.
