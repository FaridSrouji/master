Bank Of America Reports Q2 Earnings Of $3.5 Billion, EPS Of $0.37. BANK OF AMERICA REPORTS QUARTERLY EARNINGS OF $3.5 BILLION, EPS OF $0.37.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - QTRLY PROVISION FOR CREDIT LOSSES INCREASED TO $5.1 BILLION, DRIVEN BY $4.0 BILLION RESERVE BUILD.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - QTRLY FICC REVENUE INCREASED 50% TO $3.2 BILLION.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - QTRLY REVENUE, NET OF INTEREST EXPENSE, DECREASED 3% TO $22.3 BILLION.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - QTRLY EQUITIES REVENUE INCREASED 7% TO $1.2 BILLION.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - ADDED ANOTHER $4 BILLION TO CREDIT RESERVES TO REFLECT CURRENT ECONOMIC OUTLOOK.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - QTRLY NET CHARGE-OFF RATIO DECREASED 1 BASIS POINT FROM THE PRIOR QUARTER TO 0.45%.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - QTRLY NET INTEREST INCOME $10.85 BILLION VERSUS $12.19 BILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - QTRLY NONINTEREST INCOME $11.48 BILLION VERSUS $10.90  BILLION  REPORTED LAST YEAR.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - QTRLY LOAN AND LEASE BALANCES IN THE BUSINESS SEGMENTS ROSE $96 BILLION, OR 11%, TO $1.0 TRILLION.BANK OF AMERICA CORP SAYS  ENDED THE QUARTER WITH RECORD DEPOSITS OF $1.7 TRILLION.BANK OF AMERICA - QTRLY NET INCOME INCLUDES IMPACT OF A $4.0 BILLION RESERVE BUILD PRIMARILY ASSOCIATED WITH A WEAKER ECONOMIC OUTLOOK RELATED TO COVID-19.BANK OF AMERICA CORP SAYS COMMITTED TO NO CORONAVIRUS-RELATED LAYOFFS IN 2020.BANK OF AMERICA CORP - QTRLY ALLOWANCE FOR CREDIT LOSSES, INCLUDING UNFUNDED COMMITMENTS, INCREASED $10.9 BILLION, OR 106%, FROM DEC 31, 2019 TO $21.1 BILLION.
