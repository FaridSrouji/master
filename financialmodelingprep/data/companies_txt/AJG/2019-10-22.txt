Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. Announces Regular Fourth Quarter 2019 Dividend, Payable on December 20, 2019. Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of forty three cents ($0.43) per share on the Common Stock of the Company for the fourth quarter of 2019, payable on December 20, 2019 to Stockholders of Record as of December 6, 2019.
