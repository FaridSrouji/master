Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. Announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 1,825.2 million compared to USD 1,778.5 million a year ago. Net income was USD 126.1 million compared to USD 127.6 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.66 compared to USD 0.68 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 5,473.6 million compared to USD 5,276.6 million a year ago. Net income was USD 570.3 million compared to USD 516.2 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.01 compared to USD 2.78 a year ago.
