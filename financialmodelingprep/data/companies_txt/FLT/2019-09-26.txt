Fleetcor Technologies Appoints Alexey Gavrilenya as Group President of its North America Fuel Card Business. Fleetcor Technologies reported that Alexey Gavrilenya, the Company's head of the Europe Fuel Card business, has been named group president of its North America Fuel Card business. Gavrilenya joined Fleetcor in 2009, as vice president finance & business development for its Russia fuel card businesses.
