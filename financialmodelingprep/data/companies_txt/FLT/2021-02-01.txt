Hotel Connections and Travelliance Merge to Form TA Connections, Leader in Airline Logistics and Lodging Programs. CHICAGO & MIAMI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRAVELLIANCE AND HOTEL CONNECTIONS, BOTH FLEETCOR (NYSE: FLT) LODGING DIVISION COMPANIES SPECIALIZING IN AIRLINE LOGISTICS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE TWO COMPANIES WILL COME TOGETHER TO FORM TA CONNECTIONS. “HOTEL CONNECTIONS' AND TRAVELLIANCE'S TEAMS AND TECHNOLOGY HAVE COME TOGETHER SEAMLESSLY, AND NOW WE'RE EXCITED TO FOCUS ON DELIVERING CLIENTS THE MOST COMPREHENSIVE AIRLINE LODGING, TRANSPORTATION AND CREW LOGISTICS PROGRAM IN THE INDUSTRY,” SAID MIKE APPLE
