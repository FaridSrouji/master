Cambridge Global Payments Announces New Endorsement Partnership With Australian Livestock Exporters' Council. SYDNEY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CAMBRIDGE GLOBAL PAYMENTS (“CAMBRIDGE”), A FLEETCOR COMPANY (NYSE:FLT) AND A PROVIDER OF INTEGRATED CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS AND CURRENCY RISK MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THEY HAVE BEEN SELECTED BY THE AUSTRALIAN LIVESTOCK EXPORTERS' COUNCIL (“ALEC”), THE PEAK INDUSTRY BODY REPRESENTING AUSTRALIA'S LIVESTOCK EXPORT SECTOR, AS AN ENDORSED PROVIDER OF FOREIGN CURRENCY EXCHANGE AND INTERNATIONAL PAYMENTS SOLUTIONS FOR ALEC MEMBERS ACROSS AUSTRALIA.
