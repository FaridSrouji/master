NRG Energy, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Years 2019 and 2020. NRG Energy, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the years 2019 and 2020. For the year 2019, the company expects income from continuing operations of $888 million to $988 million.For the year 2020, the company expects income from continuing operations of $980 million to $1,180 million.
