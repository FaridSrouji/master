NRG Energy, Inc. Financial Update on Winter Storm Uri Impacts. PRINCETON, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NRG ENERGY, INC. (NYSE: NRG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY IS PROVIDING AN INTERIM FINANCIAL IMPACT UPDATE REFLECTING THE UNPRECEDENTED EFFECT ON THE ERCOT SYSTEM AND SETTLEMENT DATA FROM THE WINTER STORM. AS A RESULT OF THIS NEW INFORMATION AND THE ONGOING REGULATORY UNCERTAINTIES WHERE THE IMPACTS OF PROPOSED CHANGES CANNOT BE REASONABLY ESTIMATED, THE COMPANY IS WITHDRAWING PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED 2021 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE.
