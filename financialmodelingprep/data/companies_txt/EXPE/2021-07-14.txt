Alpine Investors' ASG Acquires Hotel Operations Management Platform, ALICE, from Expedia Group. NEW YORK & WALNUT CREEK, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ASG TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED HOTEL OPERATIONS MANAGEMENT PLATFORM ALICE FROM EXPEDIA GROUP (NASDAQ: EXPE) IN A CARVE-OUT TRANSACTION.
