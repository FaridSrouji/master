Expedia Group Inc Announces Pricing Of Private Offering Of Senior Notes. EXPEDIA GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCES PRICING OF PRIVATE OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES.AGREED TO SELL $750 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF UNSECURED 4.625% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2027.AGREED TO SELL $500 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF UNSECURED 3.600% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2023.2023 NOTES WILL BE ISSUED AT A PRICE OF 99.922% OF AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT.2027 NOTES WILL BE ISSUED AT A PRICE OF 99.997% OF AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT.
