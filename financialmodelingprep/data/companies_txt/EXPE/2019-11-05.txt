Expedia Group, Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 12, 2019. Expedia Group, Inc. Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.34 per share of outstanding common stock to be paid to stockholders of record as of the close of business on November 19, 2019, with a payment date of December 12, 2019.
