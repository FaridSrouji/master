Expedia Group Announces Pricing Of Private Offering Of Senior Notes. EXPEDIA GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCES PRICING OF PRIVATE OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES.AGREED TO SELL $2 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF UNSECURED 6.250% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025.AGREED TO SELL $750 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF UNSECURED 7.000% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025.6.250% NOTES WILL BE ISSUED AT A PRICE OF 100% OF AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT.7.000% NOTES WILL BE ISSUED AT A PRICE OF 100% OF AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT.EXPEDIA -EXPECTS TO USE NET PROCEEDS FOR GENERAL CORPORATE PURPOSES, WHICH MAY INCLUDE, REPAYMENT OR REDEMPTION OF CO'S 5.95% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2020.
Statement From Expedia Group Chairman Barry Diller. STATEMENT FROM EXPEDIA GROUP CHAIRMAN BARRY DILLER.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - PETER KERN IS NAMED CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF EXPEDIA GROUP.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - ERIC HART IS NAMED EXPEDIA GROUP'S CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - CHAIRMAN, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND MEMBERS OF BOARD WILL FORGO CASH COMPENSATION FOR REMAINDER OF YEAR.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - WILL BE IMPLEMENTING FURLOUGHS AND REDUCED WORK WEEK PROGRAMS FOR SELECT VOLUME-BASED TEAMS WITH LIMITED WORK RIGHT NOW.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - 401(K) MATCHING CONTRIBUTIONS IN U.S. WILL BE SUSPENDED THROUGH END OF YEAR.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - SENIOR EXECUTIVES, TRAVEL LEADERSHIP TEAM WILL BE TAKING A REDUCTION IN SALARY OF 25% FOR BALANCE OF YEAR.EXPEDIA - WILL OFFER VOLUNTARY REDUCED WORK WEEK PROGRAM FOR PARENTS, CAREGIVERS & EMPLOYEES WITH PERSONAL NEEDS TO TAKE A TEMPORARY SHORTENED 3-DAY WORK WEEK.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - UNABLE TO MAKE ANY PREDICTIONS AS TO WHEN TRAVEL WILL REBOUND.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - STEPS ON FURLOUGH AND REDUCED WORK WEEK PROGRAMS AND VOLUNTARY REDUCED WORK WEEKS WILL BE ACTIVE THROUGH AUGUST 31.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - CEASING DIVIDENDS UNTIL BUSINESS REBOUNDS.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - WILL SUPPORT EMPLOYEES BY PARTICIPATING IN GOVERNMENT AID WHERE FEASIBLE IN DIFFERENT COUNTRIES.EXPEDIA GROUP INC - SINCE CRISIS BEGAN, HAD LIMITED ONLINE TOOLS TO SUPPORT WIDESPREAD CANCELLATIONS AND CALL VOLUME SPIKED 500%.
