Caterpillar Entered Into Agreement For Credit Facility In An Aggregate Amount Of $3.875 Bln. CATERPILLAR INC - ON APRIL 21 ENTERED INTO CREDIT AGREEMENT PROVIDING FOR UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY IN AN AGGREGATE AMOUNT OF UP TO $3.875 BILLION.CATERPILLAR INC - UNDER SHORT TERM FACILITY, CO IS REQUIRED TO MAINTAIN CONSOLIDATED NET WORTH NOT LESS THAN $9 BILLION AT ALL TIMES.
