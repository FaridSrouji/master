Caterpillar Foundation Announces Global Environmental Partnership with One Tree Planted. DEERFIELD, ILL., APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CATERPILLAR FOUNDATION, THE PHILANTHROPIC ARM OF CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT), ANNOUNCED TODAY AN INVESTMENT OF $1 MILLION WITH ONE TREE PLANTED, A NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION FOCUSED ON ADVANCING THE UNITED NATIONS' 17 SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS (SDGS) THROUGH GLOBAL REFORESTATION INITIATIVES.
