Caterpillar Inc. Increases Dividend. DEERFIELD, ILL., JUNE 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CATERPILLAR INC. (NYSE: CAT) VOTED TODAY TO INCREASE THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND BY EIGHT CENTS, AN 8% RAISE, TO ONE DOLLAR AND ELEVEN CENTS ($1.11) PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE AUGUST 20, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JULY 20, 2021.
