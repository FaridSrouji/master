Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. Reports Strong Results for Fiscal Third Quarter 2021. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TAKE-TWO INTERACTIVE SOFTWARE, INC. (NASDAQ: TTWO) TODAY REPORTED STRONG RESULTS FOR ITS FISCAL THIRD QUARTER 2021 ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2020. IN ADDITION, THE COMPANY PROVIDED ITS INITIAL OUTLOOK FOR ITS FISCAL FOURTH QUARTER ENDING MARCH 31, 2021 AND AGAIN INCREASED ITS OUTLOOK FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2021, ENDING MARCH 31, 2021.
