Proletariat, Inc. announced that it has received $20 million in funding from Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc., Spark Capital Partners, LLC, FirstMark Capital, L.L.C., and other investors. On November 19, 2019, Proletariat, Inc. closed the transaction. The company raised $20,000,000 in its series C round of funding led by new investor Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NasdaqGS:TTWO). The transaction also involved participation from returning investors, Spark Capital Partners, LLC, FirstMark Capital, L.L.C. and other investors. As part of the transaction, Michael Worosz of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NasdaqGS:TTWO) will join the board of directors of the company. The company issued $19,720,233 pursuant to exemption provided under Regulation D from 5 investors.
Proletariat Inc. announced a financing transaction. Proletariat Inc. announced that it will receive $20,892,635 in funding on November 18, 2019. The company will issue common shares in the transaction. The company will issue securities pursuant to exemption provided under Regulation D.
