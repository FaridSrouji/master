Dentsply Sirona Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend. CHARLOTTE, N.C., FEB. 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DENTSPLY SIRONA INC. (“DENTSPLY SIRONA”) (NASDAQ: XRAY), ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.10 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, AN INDICATED ANNUAL RATE OF $0.40 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON APRIL 9, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON MARCH 26, 2021.
Kaskela Law LLC Announces Investigation of Dentsply Siorona Inc. (XRAY) and Encourages Long-Term XRAY Stockholders to Contact the Firm. PHILADELPHIA, FEB. 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- KASKELA LAW LLC ANNOUNCES THAT IT IS INVESTIGATING DENTSPLY SIORONA INC. (“DENTSPLY” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: XRAY) ON BEHALF OF THE COMPANY'S STOCKHOLDERS.
