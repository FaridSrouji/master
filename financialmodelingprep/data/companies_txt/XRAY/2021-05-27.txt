DENTSPLY SIRONA Increases Quarterly Cash Dividend By 10%. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MAY 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DENTSPLY SIRONA INC. (“DENTSPLY SIRONA”) (NASDAQ: XRAY), ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED A 10% INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY DIVIDEND RATE, FROM THE PREVIOUS RATE OF $0.10 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO $0.11 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JULY 9, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JUNE 25, 2021.
