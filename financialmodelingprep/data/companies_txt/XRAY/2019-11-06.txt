DENTSPLY SIRONA Inc. Reaffirms Revenue Guidance for the Fiscal Year of 2019. DENTSPLY SIRONA Inc. reaffirmed revenue guidance for the fiscal year of 2019. For the year, the company expects $3.95 billion to $4.05 billion. The company now anticipate internal sales growth for the full year to be towards the upper end of the guidance range of 4% to 5%. The company is also increasing and narrowing its EPS guidance from the previous range of $2.35 to $2.45 to the new range of $2.42 to $2.48.
