The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend of $.53 Per Share. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES INC. (NYSE: EL) WILL PAY A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $.53 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S CLASS A COMMON STOCK AND CLASS B COMMON STOCK ON JUNE 15, 2021 TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 28, 2021. THE ESTÉE LAUDER COMPANIES INC. IS ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING MANUFACTURERS AND MARKETERS OF QUALITY SKIN CARE, MAKEUP, FRAGRANCE AND HAIR CARE PRODUCTS. THE COMPANY'S PRODUCTS ARE SOLD IN APPROXIMATELY 150 COUNTRIES AND TERRITORIES
