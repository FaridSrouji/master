The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 16, 2019. The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. announced an increase in the quarterly dividend on the Company’s Class A and Class B Common Stock to $0.48 per share, a 12% increase over the previous quarterly rate of $0.43 per share. The $0.48 per share quarterly dividend will be payable on December 16, 2019 to stockholders of record at the close of business on November 29, 2019.
