Masdar City And Ekar Bring First Tesla Car-Share Operation To Middle East. Oct 20  - MASDAR CITY ::EKAR HAS ANNOUNCED A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH MASDAR TO BRING ELECTRIC VEHICLE CAR-SHARING TO MIDDLE EAST BY INTRODUCING FIVE TESLA MODELS S AND X INTO ITS FLEET.VEHICLES ARE NOW AVAILABLE TO HIRE FROM MASDAR CITY IN ABU DHABI.ANNOUNCEMENT FOLLOWS MASDAR’S £35 MILLION INVESTMENT INTO CHARGING INFRASTRUCTURE INVESTMENT FUND.
