Tesla, Inc. Reports Production Results for the Third Quarter of Fiscal 2019. Tesla, Inc. reported production results for the third quarter of fiscal 2019. For the quarter, the company achieved record production of 96,155 vehicles and record deliveries of approximately 97,000 vehicles.
