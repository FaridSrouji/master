Tesla Motors Launches All-Electric Pickup. Tesla Motors has launched its all-electric pickup. The model features Blade Runner-inspired design, with angular edges and a 'stressed skin' stainless body instead of a body-on-frame configuration. The vehicle is available with a choice of three range capabilities, starting at 250 miles. It is scheduled to enter production by late 2021. The automaker is presently accepting USD 100 deposits for the pickup.
