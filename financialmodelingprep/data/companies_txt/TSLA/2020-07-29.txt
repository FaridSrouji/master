PG&E Says Aims To Have Battery Energy Storage System Fully Operational In Q2 Of 2021. PG&E, TESLA BREAK GROUND ON LANDMARK BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM.PG&E CORP - BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM WILL BE DESIGNED, CONSTRUCTED, AND MAINTAINED BY PG&E AND TESLA, AND WILL BE OWNED AND OPERATED BY PG&E.PG&E CORP - BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM CONSTRUCTION IS EXPECTED TO CONTINUE INTO EARLY NEXT YEAR.PG&E CORP - AIMS TO HAVE SYSTEM ENERGIZED IN EARLY 2021 AND FULLY OPERATIONAL IN Q2 OF 2021.
