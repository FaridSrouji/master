Tesla Enters Equity Distribution Agreement To Raise Up To $5 Billion In Share Offering. TESLA INC - ENTERED INTO AN EQUITY DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT TO SELL SHARES OF COMMON STOCK HAVING AGGREGATE SALES PROCEEDS OF UP TO $5.0 BILLION.TESLA - AGREEMENT PROVIDES SALES AGENTS TO BE ENTITLED TO COMPENSATION FOR SERVICES IN FORM OF COMMISSION OF UPTO 0.50% OF PROCEEDS FROM EACH SALE OF SHARES.TESLA - ON AUG. 28, A&R WAREHOUSE AGREEMENT WAS FURTHER AMENDED & RESTATED TO REFLECT TERMINATION OF 2018 WAREHOUSE AGREEMENT.TESLA - ON AUGUST 28, THE 2018 WAREHOUSE AGREEMENT WAS TERMINATED.TESLA - ON AUG. 28, A&R WAREHOUSE AGREEMENT AMENDED & RESTATED TO EXTEND BORROWING AVAILABILITY DATE TO AUG. 13, 2021 & EXTEND MATURITY DATE TO SEPT. 2022.TESLA - AS SO AMENDED, A&R WAREHOUSE AGREEMENT HAS AGGREGATE LENDER COMMITMENT OF $1.1 BILLION.
