613th Consecutive Common Stock Monthly Dividend Declared By Realty Income. SAN DIEGO, JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED THE 613TH CONSECUTIVE COMMON STOCK MONTHLY DIVIDEND. THE DIVIDEND AMOUNT OF $0.2355 PER SHARE, REPRESENTING AN ANNUALIZED AMOUNT OF $2.826 PER SHARE, IS PAYABLE ON AUGUST 13, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF AUGUST 2, 2021.
