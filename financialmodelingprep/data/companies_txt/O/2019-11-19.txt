Realty Income Corporation Declares Common Stock Monthly Dividend, Payable on December 13, 2019. Realty Income Corporation announced that its Board of Directors has declared the 593rd consecutive common stock monthly dividend. The dividend amount of $0.227 per share, representing an annualized amount of $2.724 per share, is payable on December 13, 2019 to shareholders of record as of December 2, 2019. The ex-dividend date for December's dividend is November 29, 2019.
