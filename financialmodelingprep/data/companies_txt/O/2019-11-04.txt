Realty Income Announces Operating Results For Q3 And First Nine Months Of 2019. REALTY INCOME ANNOUNCES OPERATING RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER AND FIRST NINE MONTHS OF 2019.SEES FY 2019 ADJUSTED FFO PER SHARE $3.29 TO $3.34.SEES FY 2019 FFO PER SHARE $3.26 TO $3.31.Q3 ADJUSTED FFO PER SHARE $0.83.FY2019 FFO PER SHARE VIEW $3.29 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
Realty Income Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Realty Income Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 374.247 million compared to USD 338.081 million a year ago. Net income was USD 101.049 million compared to USD 98.999 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.32 compared to USD 0.34 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 1,094.062 million compared to USD 985.262 million a year ago. Net income was USD 307.185 million compared to USD 278.542 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.99 compared to USD 0.97 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.98 compared to USD 0.97 a year ago.
