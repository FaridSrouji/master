111th Common Stock Monthly Dividend Increase Declared By Realty Income. SAN DIEGO, JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- REALTY INCOME CORPORATION (REALTY INCOME, NYSE: O), THE MONTHLY DIVIDEND COMPANY®, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED AN INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK MONTHLY CASH DIVIDEND TO $0.2355 PER SHARE FROM $0.235 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JULY 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF JULY 1, 2021.
