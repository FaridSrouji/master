Broadcom Inc. Announces Pricing Of Private Offering Of Senior Notes. BROADCOM INC. ANNOUNCES PRICING OF PRIVATE OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES.BROADCOM -  PRICED OFFERING OF $1 BILLION OF 2.250% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2023, $2.25 BILLION OF 3.150% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025.BROADCOM -  PRICED OFFERING OF $2.75 BILLION OF 4.150% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2030 & $2 BILLION OF 4.300% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2032.
Broadcom Announces Private Offering Of Senior Notes. BROADCOM INC. ANNOUNCES PRIVATE OFFERING OF SENIOR NOTES.BROADCOM INC - NEW NOTES WILL BE GUARANTEED ON A SENIOR UNSECURED BASIS BY CERTAIN OF CO'S SUBSIDIARIES.BROADCOM - TO USE NET PROCEEDS FROM SALE OF NEW NOTES FOR REPAYMENT, RETIREMENT OR REPURCHASE OF CERTAIN OF ITS OR ITS UNITS' EXISTING INDEBTEDNESS.BROADCOM - TO BEGIN EXCHANGE OFFERS OF SOME SERIES OF CO,UNITS' NOTES MATURING BETWEEN 2021,2024 FOR NEW SERIES OF SENIOR NOTES MATURING BETWEEN 2026 & 2028.BROADCOM INC - EXCHANGE OFFERS ARE EXPECTED TO EXPIRE ON OR ABOUT JUNE 2, 2020.
