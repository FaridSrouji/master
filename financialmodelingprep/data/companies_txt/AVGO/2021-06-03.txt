Broadcom Inc. Announces Second Quarter Fiscal Year 2021 Financial Results and Quarterly Dividends. SAN JOSE, CALIF., JUNE 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BROADCOM INC. (NASDAQ: AVGO), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER THAT DESIGNS, DEVELOPS AND SUPPLIES SEMICONDUCTOR AND INFRASTRUCTURE SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS, TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS SECOND QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2021, ENDED MAY 2, 2021, PROVIDED GUIDANCE FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF ITS FISCAL YEAR 2021 AND ANNOUNCED ITS QUARTERLY DIVIDENDS.
Broadcom Delivers Industry's Broadest Portfolio of Fiber Optics, PHY and Switch Solutions for 100/200/400/800G Data Center Connectivity.
