Southwest Airlines Reaches Agreement In Principle On Payroll Support Program Under Cares Act. SOUTHWEST AIRLINES REACHES AGREEMENT IN PRINCIPLE ON THE PAYROLL SUPPORT PROGRAM UNDER CARES ACT.SOUTHWEST AIRLINES - DISBURSEMENTS UNDER PROGRAM TOTAL MORE THAN $3.2 BILLION, CONSISTING OF OVER $2.3 BILLION IN PAYROLL SUPPORT.SOUTHWEST AIRLINES - DISBURSEMENTS UNDER PROGRAM CONSIST OF NEARLY $1 BILLION UNSECURED TERM LOAN.SOUTHWEST AIRLINES - LOAN EXPECTED TO HAVE 10-YEAR TERM WITH LOW INTEREST RATES AND MAY BE REPAID AT ANY TIME PRIOR TO MATURITY AT PAR.SOUTHWEST AIRLINES - LOAN IS EXPECTED TO INCLUDE ABOUT 2.6 MILLION WARRANTS ISSUED TO U.S. DEPARTMENT OF TREASURY..SOUTHWEST AIRLINES - PROGRAM HAS CONDITIONS SUCH AS PROHIBITIONS AGAINST INVOLUNTARY FURLOUGHS THROUGH SEPT. 30, 2020.SOUTHWEST AIRLINES - PROGRAM HAS CONDITIONS SUCH AS PROHIBITIONS AGAINST REDUCTIONS IN EMPLOYEE PAY RATES, BENEFITS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, 2020.SOUTHWEST AIRLINES - PROGRAM HAS CONDITIONS SUCH AS ELIMINATION OF SHARE REPURCHASES AND DIVIDENDS UNTIL SEPTEMBER 30, 2021.SOUTHWEST AIRLINES - PROGRAM HAS CONDITIONS SUCH AS LIMITS ON EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION UNTIL MARCH 24, 2022..
