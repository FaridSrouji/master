Southwest Airlines Co. Extends Flight Schedule Through June 6, 2020. Southwest Airlines Co. extended its flight schedule through early June 2020, putting two destinations in Hawaii within quicker reach for the carrier's San Diego Customers. Once daily service between San Diego and Kahului on Maui begins April 14, 2020. Once daily service between San Diego and Honoluluon Oahu begins April 20, 2020.
