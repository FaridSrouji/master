Southwest Airlines Says To Offer Cargo-Only Charter Services On Its Boeing 737s. SOUTHWEST AIRLINES SAYS TO OFFER CARGO-ONLY CHARTER SERVICES ON ITS BOEING 737S.SOUTHWEST AIRLINES SAYS NEW CARGO-ONLY CHARTER SERVICE BEING PROVIDED IN CONJUNCTION WITH CO'S PASSENGER CHARTER TEAM AND WOULD BE ON ON-DEMAND BASIS.
