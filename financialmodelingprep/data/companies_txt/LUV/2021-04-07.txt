Southwest Airlines Cargo Earns Top Honor From Airforwarders Association For The 12th Consecutive Year. DALLAS, APRIL 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO. (NYSE: LUV) CARGO IS BEING HONORED FOR ITS CONSISTENT WORK DELIVERING OUTSTANDING SERVICE FOR ITS CUSTOMERS IN 2020. THE CARRIER RECENTLY WAS NAMED THE AIRFORWARDERS ASSOCIATION'S DOMESTIC AIRLINE OF THE YEAR FOR THE 12TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
