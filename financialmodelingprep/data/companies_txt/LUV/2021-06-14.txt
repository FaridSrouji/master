Southwest Announces Can't-Miss Podcast Recounting The Company's Colorful History; Created In Partnership With L.A. Times Studios And At Will Media. DALLAS, JUNE 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO.  (NYSE: LUV) THIS WEEK CELEBRATES THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE CARRIER'S FIRST COMMERCIAL FLIGHTS, WHICH TOOK PLACE ON JUNE 18, 1971. TO OFFER CUSTOMERS AND EMPLOYEES A UNIQUE PERSPECTIVE ON STORIES FROM THE AIRLINE'S COLORFUL HISTORY, SOUTHWEST®, L.A.
