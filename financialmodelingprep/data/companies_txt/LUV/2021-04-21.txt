Southwest Airlines Continues Dedication To Environment Through Sustainable Aviation Fuel Initiatives. DALLAS, APRIL 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWEST AIRLINES CO . (NYSE: LUV) (THE "COMPANY") ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS ONGOING SUPPORT OF THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY'S NATIONAL RENEWABLE ENERGY LABORATORY (NREL) TO DEVELOP COMMERCIALLY VIABLE AND SCALABLE SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL (SAF).
