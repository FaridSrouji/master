Pfizer Completes Transaction to Combine Its Upjohn Business with Mylan. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE TRANSACTION TO SPIN OFF ITS UPJOHN BUSINESS AND COMBINE IT WITH MYLAN N.V. TO FORM VIATRIS INC. PFIZER CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER DR. ALBERT BOURLA SAID, “WE ARE PROUD TO HAVE COMPLETED THE COMBINATION OF UPJOHN AND MYLAN TO CREATE VIATRIS AND PLEASED TO HAVE DELIVERED VALUE TO OUR SHAREHOLDERS THROUGH THIS TRANSACTION.
