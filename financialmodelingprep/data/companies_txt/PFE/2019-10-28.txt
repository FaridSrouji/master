Pfizer Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Pfizer Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 12,680 million compared to USD 13,298 million a year ago. Net income was USD 7,680 million compared to USD 4,114 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.38 compared to USD 0.7 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.36 compared to USD 0.69 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 39,062 million compared to USD 39,670 million a year ago. Net income was USD 16,609 million compared to USD 11,546 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.98 compared to USD 1.96 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.92 compared to USD 1.92 a year ago.
