ViiV Healthcare and Halozyme enter global collaboration and license agreement for ENHANZE® drug delivery technology to enable development of “ultra long-acting” medicines for HIV. LONDON & SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VIIV HC AND HALOZYME ENTER GLOBAL COLLABORATION AND LICENSE AGREEMENT FOR ENHANZE® DRUG DELIVERY TECHNOLOGY TO DEVELOP ULTRA-LONG-ACTING HIV MEDICINES
ViiV Healthcare and Halozyme enter global collaboration and license agreement for ENHANZE® drug delivery technology to enable development of "ultra-long-acting" medicines for HIV. LONDON AND SAN DIEGO, JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- VIIV HEALTHCARE, THE GLOBAL SPECIALIST HIV COMPANY MAJORITY OWNED BY GLAXOSMITHKLINE PLC ("GSK"), WITH PFIZER INC. AND SHIONOGI LIMITED AS SHAREHOLDERS, AND HALOZYME THERAPEUTICS, INC. (NASDAQ: HALO) TODAY ANNOUNCED A GLOBAL COLLABORATION AND LICENSE AGREEMENT THAT GIVES EXCLUSIVE ACCESS TO HALOZYME'S ENHANZE® DRUG DELIVERY TECHNOLOGY, RECOMBINANT HUMAN HYALURONIDASE PH20 ENZYME (RHUPH20), FOR SPECIFIC TARGETS USED IN THE TREATMENT AND PREVENTION OF HIV.  UNDER THE TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT, VIIV HEALTHCARE WILL MAKE AN UPFRONT PAYMENT OF $40 MILLION TO HALOZYME FOR THE EXCLUSIVE LICENSE TO FOUR HIV SMALL AND LARGE MOLECULE TARGETS AND IS OBLIGATED TO MAKE POTENTIAL FUTURE PAYMENTS OF UP TO $175 MILLION IN DEVELOPMENT AND COMMERCIAL MILESTONES PER TARGET, SUBJECT TO ACHIEVEMENT OF SPECIFIED DEVELOPMENT AND COMMERCIAL MILESTONES, INCLUDING CERTAIN SPECIFIED SALES MILESTONES.
