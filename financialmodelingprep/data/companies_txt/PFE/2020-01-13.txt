Axsome Therapeutics Enters Into Exclusive License Agreement With Pfizer For Pfizer’s Reboxetine Clinical And Nonclinical Data And For New Phase 3 Esreboxetine Product Candidate. AXSOME THERAPEUTICS ENTERS INTO EXCLUSIVE LICENSE AGREEMENT WITH PFIZER INC. FOR PFIZER’S REBOXETINE CLINICAL AND NONCLINICAL DATA AND FOR NEW PHASE 3 ESREBOXETINE PRODUCT CANDIDATE.AXSOME THERAPEUTICS INC - PFIZER TO RECEIVE $11 MILLION IN AXSOME STOCK AND UPFRONT CASH, AND UP TO $323 MILLION IN REGULATORY AND SALES MILESTONES.AXSOME THERAPEUTICS - ESREBOXETINE MET PRIMARY ENDPOINTS IN COMPLETED PFIZER PHASE 3 AND PHASE 2 PLACEBO-CONTROLLED CLINICAL TRIALS IN FIBROMYALGIA.AXSOME THERAPEUTICS - AGREEMENT ALSO PROVIDES AXSOME EXCLUSIVE RIGHTS TO DEVELOP AND COMMERCIALIZE ESREBOXETINE IN U.S. FOR TREATMENT OF FIBROMYALGIA.AXSOME THERAPEUTICS INC - PFIZER WILL HAVE A RIGHT OF FIRST NEGOTIATION ON ANY POTENTIAL FUTURE STRATEGIC TRANSACTIONS INVOLVING AXS-12 OR AXS-14.
Zymeworks Announces Agreement With Pfizer. ZYMEWORKS ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT WITH PFIZER AND INITIATION OF A NEW PHASE 2 TRIAL EVALUATING ZW25 IN COMBINATION WITH IBRANCE® (PALBOCICLIB).ZYMEWORKS WILL SPONSOR STUDY, AND PFIZER WILL PROVIDE PALBOCICLIB..PLANS TO INITIATE A REGISTRATION-ENABLING PHASE 2 TRIAL IN PREVIOUSLY-TREATED OR RECURRENT HER2-POSITIVE BILIARY TRACT CANCER IN 2020.
