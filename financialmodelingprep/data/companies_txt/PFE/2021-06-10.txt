Pfizer and BioNTech to Provide 500 Million Doses of COVID-19 Vaccine to U.S. Government for Donation to Poorest Nations. NEW YORK & MAINZ, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) AND BIONTECH SE (NASDAQ: BNTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO PROVIDE THE U.S. GOVERNMENT AT A NOT-FOR-PROFIT PRICE 500 MILLION DOSES OF THE COMPANIES' COVID-19 VACCINE, 200 MILLION DOSES IN 2021 AND 300 MILLION DOSES IN THE FIRST HALF OF 2022, TO FURTHER SUPPORT THE MULTILATERAL EFFORTS TO ADDRESS THE SURGE OF INFECTION IN MANY PARTS OF THE WORLD AND TO HELP END THE PANDEMIC.
