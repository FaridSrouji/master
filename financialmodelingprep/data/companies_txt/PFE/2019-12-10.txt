ContraFect Prices About $10 Mln Public Offering Of Common Stock And Concurrent $3 Mln Private Placement Of Common Stock To Pfizer. CONTRAFECT CORPORATION PRICES APPROXIMATELY $10 MILLION PUBLIC OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK AND CONCURRENT $3 MILLION PRIVATE PLACEMENT OF COMMON STOCK TO PFIZER.CONTRAFECT CORP - PRICING OF ITS UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 37.2 MILLION SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK AT A PUBLIC OFFERING PRICE OF $0.27 PER SHARE.CONTRAFECT CORP - ALSO ANNOUNCED THAT PFIZER INC. HAS AGREED TO PURCHASE 11.1 MILLION SHARES OF CO'S COMMON STOCK PURSUANT TO A STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT.
