Pfizer, BioNTech Announce Publication of Peer-Reviewed Data from Ongoing Phase 1/2 study of mRNA-based Vaccine Candidate. PFIZER AND BIONTECH ANNOUNCE PUBLICATION OF PEER-REVIEWED DATA FROM ONGOING PHASE 1/2 STUDY OF MRNA-BASED VACCINE CANDIDATE, BNT162B1, AGAINST SARS-COV-2 IN NATURE.PRELIMINARY CLINICAL DATA ON BNT162B1 SHOWED BNT162B1 WAS ADMINISTERED IN DOSE WELL TOLERATED AND GENERATED DOSE-DEPENDENT IMMUNOGENICITY.COS CONTINUING TO COLLECT DATA FROM PHASE 1/2 TRIALS, EXPECT TO SUBMIT DATA ON BNT162B2 FOR PEER-REVIEW, POTENTIAL PUBLICATION IN NEAR FUTURE.
