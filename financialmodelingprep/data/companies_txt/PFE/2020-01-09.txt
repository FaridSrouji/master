Effector Enters Deal With Pfizer To Develop Novel First-In-Class Inhibitors Of eIF4E. EFFECTOR ENTERS INTO AGREEMENT WITH PFIZER INC. TO DEVELOP NOVEL FIRST-IN-CLASS INHIBITORS OF EIF4E TO TREAT MULTIPLE CANCER TYPES.EFFECTOR THERAPEUTICS - UNDER TERMS OF AGREEMENT, EFFECTOR WILL RECEIVE A $15 MILLION PAYMENT UPFRONT.EFFECTOR THERAPEUTICS - AS PER AGREEMENT, CO TO BE ELIGIBLE FOR ADDITIONAL POTENTIAL $492 MILLION IN RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT FUNDING, DEVELOPMENT AND SALES MILESTONE PAYMENTS.
