Pfizer Announces Extension of Review of New Drug Application of Abrocitinib for the Treatment of Moderate to Severe Atopic Dermatitis. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS EXTENDED THE PRIORITY REVIEW PERIOD FOR THE NEW DRUG APPLICATION (NDA) FOR ABROCITINIB FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULTS AND ADOLESCENTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE ATOPIC DERMATITIS. THE PRESCRIPTION DRUG USER FEE ACT (PDUFA) GOAL DATE HAS BEEN EXTENDED THREE MONTHS TO EARLY Q3 2021.
