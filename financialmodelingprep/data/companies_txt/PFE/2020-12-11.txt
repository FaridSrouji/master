Pfizer Declares First-Quarter 2021 Dividend. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PFIZER INC. (NYSE:PFE) DECLARED A 39-CENT FIRST-QUARTER 2021 DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE MARCH 5, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF THE COMMON STOCK OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 29, 2021. PFIZER INCREASED THE DIVIDEND OVER THE FOURTH-QUARTER 2020 DIVIDEND BY APPROXIMATELY 3 PERCENT TO 39 CENTS FROM 38 CENTS PER SHARE.
