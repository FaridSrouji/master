Onkure And Pfizer Enter Agreement To Evaluate Combination Of Oki-179 And Binimetinib. ONKURE AND PFIZER ENTER CLINICAL TRIAL COLLABORATION AND SUPPLY AGREEMENT TO EVALUATE COMBINATION OF OKI-179 AND BINIMETINIB.CO AND PFIZER WILL FORM A JOINT DEVELOPMENT COMMITTEE TO REVIEW CLINICAL TRIAL RESULTS FROM ONKURE-SPONSORED STUDY.
