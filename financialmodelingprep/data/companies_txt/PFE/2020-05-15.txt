Opko Health Says Entered Amendment To Commercialization License Agreement With Pfizer, Others. OPKO HEALTH - ON MAY 12, ENTERED AMENDMENT TO COMMERCIALIZATION LICENSE AGREEMENT WITH PFIZER, OTHERS.OPKO HEALTH - UNDER AMENDMENT, EFFECTIVE JAN 1, CO, PFIZER AGREED TO SHARE EQUALLY ALL COSTS FOR REMAINING DEVELOPMENT OF HGH-CTP FOR PEDIATRIC GHD.OPKO HEALTH - UNDER AMENDMENT, AGREED TO WORK TOGETHER TO IDENTIFY ADDITIONAL PEDIATRIC INDICATION FOR DEVELOPMENT OTHER THAN PGHD ON OR BEFORE DEC. 1.OPKO HEALTH - AGREED ON PROCESS WHEREBY PFIZER MAY SUBMIT SUPPLEMENTAL BLA FOR GHD IN ADULTS FOLLOWING RECEIPT OF REGULATORY APPROVAL OF BLA FOR PGHD.
