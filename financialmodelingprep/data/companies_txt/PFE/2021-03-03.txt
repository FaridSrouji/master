U.S. FDA Expands Approval of Pfizer's LORBRENA® as First-Line Treatment for ALK-Positive Metastatic Lung Cancer. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) APPROVED PFIZER INC.'S (NYSE: PFE) SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION (SNDA) FOR LORBRENA® (LORLATINIB), EXPANDING THE INDICATION TO INCLUDE FIRST-LINE TREATMENT OF PEOPLE WITH ANAPLASTIC LYMPHOMA KINASE (ALK)-POSITIVE NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (NSCLC).
