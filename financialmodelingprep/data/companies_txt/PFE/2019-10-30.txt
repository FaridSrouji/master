EMA Advises Pfizer's Xeljanz Be Used With Caution For Patients At High Risk Of Blood Clots. Oct 31  - European Medicines Agency::REVIEW BY SAFETY COMMITTEE CONCLUDED XELJANZ COULD INCREASE RISK OF BLOOD CLOTS IN LUNGS AND IN DEEP VEINS IN PATIENTS ALREADY AT HIGH RISK.PRAC IS RECOMMENDING THAT XELJANZ SHOULD BE USED WITH CAUTION IN PATIENTS AT HIGH RISK OF BLOOD CLOTS.MAINTENANCE DOSES OF XELJANZ 10 MG TWICE DAILY SHOULD NOT BE USED IN PATIENTS WITH ULCERATIVE COLITIS AT HIGH RISK UNLESS NO SUITABLE ALTERNATIVE.PRAC RECOMMENDING PATIENTS OLDER THAN 65 YEARS OF AGE SHOULD BE TREATED WITH XELJANZ ONLY WHEN THERE IS NO ALTERNATIVE TREATMENT.
