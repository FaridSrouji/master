LORBRENA® (lorlatinib) sNDA in Previously Untreated ALK-Positive Lung Cancer Accepted for Priority Review by U.S. FDA. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE:PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ACCEPTED FOR PRIORITY REVIEW THE SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION (SNDA) FOR LORBRENA ® (LORLATINIB) AS A FIRST-LINE TREATMENT FOR PEOPLE WITH ANAPLASTIC LYMPHOMA KINASE (ALK)-POSITIVE METASTATIC NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (NSCLC). THE SNDA IS BASED ON DATA FROM THE PIVOTAL CROWN STUDY AND IS BEING REVIEWED BY THE FDA UNDER ITS REAL-TIME ONCOLOGY REVIEW (RTOR) PILOT PROGRAM. T
