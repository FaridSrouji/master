U.S. FDA Accepts for Priority Review the Biologics License Application for Pfizer's Investigational 20-valent Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine for Adults 18 Years of Age and Older. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE:PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) ACCEPTED FOR PRIORITY REVIEW A BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (BLA) FOR ITS 20-VALENT PNEUMOCOCCAL CONJUGATE VACCINE (20VPNC) CANDIDATE, AS SUBMITTED FOR THE PREVENTION OF INVASIVE DISEASE AND PNEUMONIA CAUSED BY STREPTOCOCCUS PNEUMONIAE SEROTYPES IN THE VACCINE IN ADULTS AGES 18 YEARS AND OLDER. THE PRESCRIPTION DRUG USER FEE ACT (PDUFA) GOAL DATE FOR A DECISION BY THE FDA ON THE 2
