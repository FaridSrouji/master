Pfizer Announces Details for When-Issued and Ex-Distribution Trading of Viatris and Pfizer Common Stock. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Pfizer Inc. (NYSE: PFE) announced today further details regarding the proposed spin-off of its Upjohn Business and the subsequent combination of Mylan and the Upjohn Business to form Viatris Inc. As previously announced, Pfizer has set the close of business on November 13, 2020 as the record date for the spin-off, and the combination of Mylan and the Upjohn Business is expected to close on November 16, 2020.
