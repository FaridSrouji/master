Pfizer Declares Second-Quarter 2021 Dividend. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A 39-CENT SECOND-QUARTER 2021 DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE JUNE 4, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF THE COMMON STOCK OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 7, 2021. THE SECOND-QUARTER 2021 CASH DIVIDEND WILL BE THE 330TH CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAID BY PFIZER.
