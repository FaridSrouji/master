Karo Pharma Announces Termination Of Agreement With Pfizer. TERMINATION OF KARO AGREEMENT WITH PFIZER.CO INFORMED BY PFIZER ABOUT DECISION TO TERMINATE RESEARCH COLLABORATION AND LICENSE AGREEMENT, ENTERED IN DEC 2011.TERMINATION OF AGREEMENT HAS NO IMPACT ON THE RUNNING OPERATIONS NOR THE BUSINESS OUTLOOK.FOLLOWING WIND-DOWN OF THIS AGREEMENT, THERE WILL BE NO ACTIVE COLLABORATION AGREEMENTS BETWEEN TWO COMPANIES.
