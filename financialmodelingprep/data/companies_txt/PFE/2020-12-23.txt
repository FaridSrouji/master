Pfizer and BioNTech to Supply the U.S. with 100 Million Additional Doses of COVID-19 Vaccine. NEW YORK & MAINZ, GERMANY--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE: PFE) AND BIONTECH SE (NASDAQ: BNTX) TODAY ANNOUNCED A SECOND AGREEMENT WITH THE U.S. GOVERNMENT TO SUPPLY AN ADDITIONAL 100 MILLION DOSES OF THE COMPANIES' COVID-19 VACCINE FROM PRODUCTION FACILITIES IN THE U.S. THIS AGREEMENT BRINGS THE TOTAL NUMBER OF DOSES TO BE DELIVERED TO THE U.S. TO 200 MILLION.
