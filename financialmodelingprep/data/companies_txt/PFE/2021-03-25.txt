Real-World Evidence Supports Effectiveness of First-line IBRANCE® (palbociclib) Combination Therapy in HR+, HER2- Metastatic Breast Cancer. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PFIZER INC. (NYSE:PFE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PEER-REVIEWED PUBLICATION OF REAL-WORLD EVIDENCE (RWE) DEMONSTRATING THAT FIRST-LINE THERAPY WITH IBRANCE® (PALBOCICLIB) IN COMBINATION WITH LETROZOLE WAS ASSOCIATED WITH IMPROVED REAL-WORLD PROGRESSION-FREE SURVIVAL (RWPFS) AND OVERALL SURVIVAL (OS) IN WOMEN WITH HORMONE RECEPTOR-POSITIVE (HR+), HUMAN EPIDERMAL GROWTH FACTOR 2-NEGATIVE (HER2-) METASTATIC BREAST CANCER (MBC) COMPARED WITH LETROZOLE ALONE.
