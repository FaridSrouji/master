The J. M. Smucker Company Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full-Year Fiscal 2020. The J. M. Smucker Company revises earnings guidance for the full-year fiscal 2020. For the year, the company expects net sales to be down 3% compared to down 1% - 0% provided earlier. On a comparable basis, net sales are expected to be down 2%. The revision versus previous guidance includes the impact of second quarter sales results and greater than anticipated softness in the back half of the fiscal year, primarily for certain brands within the U.S. Retail Pet Foods segment.
