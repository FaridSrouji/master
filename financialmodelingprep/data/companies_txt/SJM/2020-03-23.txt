J M Smucker Says Providing Up To 12 Weeks Of Full Pay, Benefits Continuation For Employees. J M SMUCKER - PROVIDING UP TO 12 WEEKS OF FULL PAY, BENEFITS CONTINUATION FOR EMPLOYEES WHOSE MANUFACTURING/DISTRIBUTION FACILITY IS CLOSED.J M SMUCKER - A ONE-TIME $1,500 HARDSHIP AWARD IS BEING ISSUED TO ABOUT 5,700 EMPLOYEES.J M SMUCKER CO - PAID SICK LEAVE (14 DAYS) WILL BE PROVIDED TO INDIVIDUALS WHO MUST BE ABSENT FROM WORK.J M SMUCKER CO - MAXIMIZING PRODUCTION AT ITS MANUFACTURING FACILITIES.
