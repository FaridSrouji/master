The J. M. Smucker Co. Issues Limited, Voluntary Recall of Two Lots of Meow Mix® Original Choice Dry Cat Food for Potential Salmonella Contamination. ORRVILLE, OHIO, APRIL 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE J. M. SMUCKER CO. (NYSE: SJM) TODAY ANNOUNCED A LIMITED, VOLUNTARY RECALL OF TWO LOTS OF MEOW MIX® ORIGINAL CHOICE DRY CAT FOOD (30LB BAG) DUE TO POTENTIAL SALMONELLA CONTAMINATION.
