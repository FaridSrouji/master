The J.M. Smucker Co. Announces Dividend Increase. ORRVILLE, OHIO, JULY 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE J.M. SMUCKER CO. (NYSE: SJM) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED AN INCREASE IN THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND FROM $0.90 TO $0.99 PER COMMON SHARE, AN INCREASE OF 10 PERCENT.
