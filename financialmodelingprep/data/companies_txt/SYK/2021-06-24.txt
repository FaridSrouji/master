Stryker to host conference call on July 27, 2021. KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN, JUNE 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK) ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL ON TUESDAY, JULY 27, 2021 AT 4:30 P.M., EASTERN TIME, TO DISCUSS THE COMPANY'S OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021 AND PROVIDE AN OPERATIONAL UPDATE. FINAL OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2021 WILL BE RELEASED AT 4:05 P.M. THAT DAY.
