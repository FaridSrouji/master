Stryker Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on January 31, 2020. Stryker announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.575 per share payable on January 31, 2020 to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 31, 2019, representing an increase of approximately 11% versus the prior year and unchanged from the previous quarter.
