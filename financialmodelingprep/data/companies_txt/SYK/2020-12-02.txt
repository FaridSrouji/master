Stryker declares a $0.63 per share quarterly dividend. KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN, DEC. 02, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.63 PER SHARE PAYABLE JANUARY 29, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 31, 2020, REPRESENTING AN INCREASE OF 9.6% VERSUS THE PRIOR YEAR AND PREVIOUS QUARTER.
