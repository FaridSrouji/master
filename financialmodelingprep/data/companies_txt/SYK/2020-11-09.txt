Stryker elects Giovanni Caforio to Board of Directors. KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN, NOV. 09, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE:SYK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT GIOVANNI CAFORIO, M.D., CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF BRISTOL MYERS SQUIBB, HAS BEEN ELECTED TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 1, 2020.
