Stryker announces receipt of all required regulatory approvals for proposed acquisition of Wright Medical. Kalamazoo, Michigan, Nov. 04, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Stryker (NYSE: SYK) announced today that all required regulatory approvals have been obtained for the previously announced cash tender offer for all outstanding ordinary shares of Wright Medical Group N.V. (NASDAQ: WMGI) by Stryker B.V., an indirect, wholly owned subsidiary of Stryker. The tender offer is being made pursuant to the purchase agreement, dated November 4, 2019, among Stryker, Stryker B.V. and Wright Medical.
