Stryker completes acquisition of Wright Medical. KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN, NOV. 11, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STRYKER (NYSE: SYK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF WRIGHT MEDICAL GROUP N.V. (NASDAQ: WMGI), A GLOBAL MEDICAL DEVICE COMPANY FOCUSED ON EXTREMITIES AND BIOLOGICS.
