Grifols Collaborates With Hologic To Increase Spain's COVID-19 Testing Capacity. ANNOUNCES COLLABORATION WITH HOLOGIC INC TO INCREASE THE COUNTRY’S TESTING CAPACITY FOR COVID-19.SAYS WILL BE ABLE TO EXTEND SALES OF ITS PROCLEIX SARS COV-2 ASSAY INTO MOLECULAR DIAGNOSTIC TESTING LABORATORIES IN SPAIN AS PART OF THE COLLABORATION.
