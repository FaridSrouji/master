Hologic, Inc Provides Earnings Guidance for the First Quarter of 2020 and Fiscal Year of 2020. Hologic, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the first quarter of 2020 and fiscal year of 2020. For the quarter, the company expected revenue of $835 million to $3,500 million, EPS of $0.33 to $0.35. For the fiscal year, the company expected revenue of $3,450 million to $3,500 million, EPS of $1.59 to $1.64.
