AEP Energy And Opdenergy To Provide 100% Clean Energy. COLUMBUS, OHIO, MARCH 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AEP ENERGY, A SUBSIDIARY OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP), HAS SIGNED AN AGREEMENT WITH OPDENERGY, AN INDEPENDENT POWER PRODUCER WITH EXTENSIVE EXPERIENCE IN THE DEVELOPMENT, FINANCING, CONSTRUCTION AND OPERATION OF RENEWABLE ASSETS. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE 12-YEAR AGREEMENT, OPDENERGY WILL DEVELOP AN 80-MEGAWATT (MWAC) SOLAR PLANT LOCATED IN JEFFERSON COUNTY, WEST VIRGINIA.
