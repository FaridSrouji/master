American Electric Power Service Corporation Seeks Bids For Coal. COLUMBUS, OHIO, MAY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP), TODAY ISSUED A REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP) FOR THE SUPPLY OF COAL TO ONE OR MORE OF ITS GENERATING STATIONS IN MULTIPLE COAL REGIONS. AEP SEEKS PROPOSALS FOR THE FOLLOWING COAL TYPE, DELIVERY YEARS AND TONNAGES: COAL REGION JULY-DEC. 2021 2022 2023 2024 CENTRAL APPALACHIAN BASIN - 1,000,000 1,500,000 1,500,000 ILLINOIS BASIN - 650,000 1,000,000 750,000 POWDER RIVER BASIN 2,000,000 3,000,000 1,500,000 1,000,000 NORTHERN APPALACHIAN BASIN - 1,500,000 2,500,000 2,500,000 ALL COAL PROPOSAL PACKAGES SHOULD INCLUDE THE PROVIDED COAL PROPOSAL FORM INCLUDED IN THE RFP.
