Cardinal Operating Company Seeks Bids For Barging And Harbor Services. COLUMBUS, OHIO, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARDINAL OPERATING COMPANY (COC) ON BEHALF OF AEP GENERATION RESOURCES INC. (AEPGR), AN UNREGULATED WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF AEP ENERGY SUPPLY, LLC, IS SEEKING BIDS FOR BARGING AND HARBOR SERVICES. AEP ENERGY SUPPLY, LLC, WHICH OWNS CARDINAL UNIT 1, IS A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP), AND BUCKEYE POWER, INC. (BPI), WHICH OWNS CARDINAL UNITS 2 AND 3, IS A CORPORATION NOT FOR PROFIT ORGANIZED AND EXISTING UNDER THE LAWS OF THE STATE OF OHIO AND ENGAGED IN THE GENERATION AND TRANSMISSION OF ELECTRIC ENERGY IN OHIO FOR DISTRIBUTION AND USE BY ITS MEMBERSHIP.
