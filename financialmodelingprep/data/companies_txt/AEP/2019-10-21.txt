American Electric Power Company, Inc. Declares a Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 10, 2019. The Board of Directors of American Electric Power Co. declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of 70 cents a share on the company's common stock, an increase of 3 cents per share. The dividend is payable December 10, 2019, to shareholders of record as of November 8, 2019.
