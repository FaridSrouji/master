American Electric Power Announces Lionel L. Nowell, III Not Be Standing for Re-Election as a Director at the Company's 2020. On December 3, 2019, Lionel L. Nowell, III notified the Board of Directors of American Electric Power Company, Inc. that he will not be standing for re-election as a director at the Company's 2020 annual meeting of shareholders. Effective January 1, 2020, the Board appointed David J. Anderson as the Chairman of the Audit Committee. Mr. Anderson is currently a member of the Audit Committee. Effective January 1, 2020, the Board also appointed Mr. Art A. Garcia as Chairman of the Finance Committee. Mr. Garcia is currently a member of the Finance Committee.
