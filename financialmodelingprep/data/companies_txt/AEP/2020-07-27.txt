AEP Says It Participated In Legislative Process Surrounding H.B. 6 Legally, Ethically. STATEMENT FROM AEP ABOUT INVESTIGATION OF OHIO H.B. 6.AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER - "ENGAGED AND PARTICIPATED IN LEGISLATIVE PROCESS SURROUNDING H.B. 6 LEGALLY AND ETHICALLY'.AEP, ON INVESTIGATION SURROUNDING OHIO HOUSE BILL 6, SAYS NONE OF THE ALLEGED WRONGFUL CONDUCT IN CRIMINAL COMPLAINT INVOLVES AEP OR ITS SUBSIDIARIES.AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER - TO DATE, WE HAVE NOT BEEN CONTACTED BY AUTHORITIES CONDUCTING INVESTIGATION.AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER - NEITHER AEP NOR ANY OF ITS SUBSIDIARIES MADE ANY CONTRIBUTIONS TO GENERATION NOW.AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER - CONTRIBUTIONS MADE TO EMPOWERING OHIO'S ECONOMY WERE DONE APPROPRIATELY.
