SWEPCO issues Requests for Proposals for purchase of wind, solar and short-term generation. SHREVEPORT, LA., JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHWESTERN ELECTRIC POWER CO. (SWEPCO), A SUBSIDIARY OF AMERICAN ELECTRIC POWER (NASDAQ: AEP), TODAY ISSUED THREE REQUESTS FOR PROPOSALS (RFP) FOR RENEWABLE AND SHORT-TERM GENERATING CAPACITY TO SUPPLY THE NEEDS OF ITS CUSTOMERS.
