Everest Re Group, Ltd. Approves Regular Quarterly Dividend, Payable on or Before December 24, 2019. Everest Re Group, Ltd. announced that its Board of Directors approved an 11% increase to its regular quarterly dividend from $1.40 to $1.55 per common share. This dividend will be payable on or before December 24, 2019 to all shareholders of record as of December 4, 2019.
