Anterix, Motorola Solutions Announce Private LTE Technology Agreement. WOODLAND PARK, N.J. AND CHICAGO, MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ANTERIX (NASDAQ: ATEX) AND MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS (NYSE: MSI) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THEY HAVE ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO FACILITATE THE DEPLOYMENT OF PRIVATE LTE NETWORKS BY UTILITY COMPANIES AS PART OF THE SECTOR'S EFFORTS TO MODERNIZE THE ELECTRIC GRID.
