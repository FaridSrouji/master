Indigovision Group Announces Recommended Cash Acquisition By Motorola Solutions. MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS INDIGOVISION GROUP  - RECOMMENDED CASH ACQUISITION OF INDIGOVISION.INDIGOVISION GROUP PLC - ACQUISITION VALUES ENTIRE ISSUED AND TO BE ISSUED SHARE CAPITAL OF INDIGOVISION AT APPROXIMATELY £30.4 MILLION.INDIGOVISION - DEAL CONSIDERATION WILL BE FUNDED FROM EXISTING CASH RESOURCES OF MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS.INDIGOVISION GROUP PLC - INDIGOVISION DIRECTORS CONSIDER TERMS OF ACQUISITION TO BE FAIR AND REASONABLE.
