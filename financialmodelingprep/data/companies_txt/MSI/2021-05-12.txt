In-Car Video Technology from Motorola Solutions Assisting U.K. Police to “Help Reduce Road Risk and Increase Safety”. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY HAS SIGNED A FOUR-YEAR SINGLE SUPPLIER FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT WITH BLUELIGHT COMMERCIAL* TO DEPLOY INTEGRATED 4RE IN-CAR VIDEO SOLUTIONS TO ALL EMERGENCY SERVICES IN THE U.K. INCLUDING POLICE, HIGHWAYS AGENCIES, BORDER SECURITY AND THE U.K. HOME OFFICE. THE DEPLOYMENT OF IN-CAR TECHNOLOGY IS PART OF THE NATIONAL POLICE CHIEFS COUNCIL'S ROADS POLICING STRATEGY TO IMPROVE ROAD SAFETY AND SECURITY, AS WELL AS EFFICIENCY. PR
