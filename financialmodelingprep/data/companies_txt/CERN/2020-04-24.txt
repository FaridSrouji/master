Cerner Provides Access To De-Identified Patient Data For Covid-19 Research And Vaccine Development. CERNER PROVIDES ACCESS TO DE-IDENTIFIED PATIENT DATA FOR COVID-19 RESEARCH AND VACCINE DEVELOPMENT.CERNER - DE-IDENTIFIED PATIENT DATA SECURED & STORED ON CERNER HEALTHEDATALAB, POWERED BY AMAZON WEB SERVICES.CERNER - TO GIVE ELIGIBLE HEALTH CARE RESEARCHERS FREE ACCESS TO CO'S COVID-19 DATA SET FOR EPIDEMIOLOGICAL STUDIES, CLINICAL TRIALS, MEDICAL TREATMENTS.
