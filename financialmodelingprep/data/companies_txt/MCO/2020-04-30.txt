Moody's Corporation Reports Results For First Quarter 2020. ORATION REPORTS RESULTS FOR FIRST QUARTER 2020.SEES FY 2020 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $7.80 TO $8.40.SEES FY 2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE $7.25 TO $7.85.Q1 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.73.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.57.Q1 REVENUE $1.3 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.2 BILLION.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $2.22 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FY 2020 DILUTED EPS AND ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS GUIDANCE RANGES REDUCED DUE TO ANTICIPATED COVID-19 IMPACTS.EXPECT ECONOMIC IMPLICATIONS OF COVID-19 TO BE MORE PRONOUNCED THROUGH SECOND HALF OF YEAR.BOTH LOWERED AND WIDENED OUR FULL YEAR 2020 ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS GUIDANCE RANGE TO REFLECT HIGHER DEGREE OF UNCERTAINTY.IN RESPONSE TO COVID-19 PANDEMIC, COMPANY HAS SUSPENDED SHARE REPURCHASES.AT QUARTER-END, MOODY'S HAD $6.8 BILLION OF OUTSTANDING DEBT AND AN UNDRAWN $1.0 BILLION REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.TOTAL CASH, CASH EQUIVALENTS AND SHORT-TERM INVESTMENTS AT QUARTER-END WERE $2.2 BILLION, UP FROM $1.9 BILLION ON DECEMBER 31, 2019.2020 OUTLOOK ASSUMES CO DOES NOT EXPERIENCE ANY MATERIAL NEGATIVE IMPACT TO ITS ABILITY TO CONDUCT ITS OPERATIONS AS A RESULT OF COVID-19.SEES 2020 REVENUE DECLINE IN MID-SINGLE-DIGIT PERCENT RANGE.SEES 2020 FREE CASH FLOW OF $1.2 BILLION TO $1.4 BILLION.SEES 2020 OPERATING MARGIN OF 41% TO 43%.SEES 2020 MIS GLOBAL REVENUE TO DECLINE IN HIGH-SINGLE-DIGIT PERCENT RANGE.SEES 2020 MA GLOBAL REVENUE TO INCREASE IN MID-SINGLE-DIGIT PERCENT RANGE.
