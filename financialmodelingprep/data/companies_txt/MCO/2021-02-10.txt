Moody's to Acquire Cortera, a Leader in Credit Data and Insights. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MOODY'S CORPORATION (NYSE:MCO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE CORTERA, A LEADING PROVIDER OF NORTH AMERICAN CREDIT DATA AND WORKFLOW SOLUTIONS. THE ACQUISITION ENHANCES MOODY'S INTEGRATED RISK ASSESSMENT CAPABILITIES AND SIGNIFICANTLY EXTENDS COVERAGE IN THE SMALL AND MEDIUM ENTERPRISE (SME) SEGMENT. CORTERA MAINTAINS ONE OF THE MOST COMPREHENSIVE DATABASES OF CREDIT INFORMATION, FEATURING DATA AND ANALYTICS ON OVER 36 MILLI
