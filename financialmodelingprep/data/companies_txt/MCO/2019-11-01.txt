Moody's Says The Outlook For U.S. Retail Has Been Revised To Stable From Positive. MOODY'S SAYS THE OUTLOOK FOR U.S. RETAIL HAS BEEN REVISED TO STABLE FROM POSITIVE.MOODY'S SAYS LARGE U.S. RETAIL CONTRIBUTORS NEGATIVELY IMPACTED THIS YEAR BY REIMBURSEMENT PRESSURES, SOFTER HOUSING MARKET.MOODY'S-PERFORMANCE ACROSS U.S. RETAIL INDUSTRY FACES RISING PRESSURE, MARKET SHARE COMPETITION, E-COMMERCE INTEGRATION COSTS, BRICK & MORTAR CHANNELS.MOODY'S SAYS U.S. RETAIL WILL CONTINUE TO REAP REWARDS OF STRATEGIC INVESTMENTS IN E-COMMERCE AND IMPROVED OPERATING EFFICIENCIES.
