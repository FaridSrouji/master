TBWA\London Wins Health & Wellness Cannes Lions Grand Prix for BECO's #StealOurStaff campaign. LONDON, JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TBWA\LONDON HAS BEEN AWARDED A GRAND PRIX IN THE HEALTH AND WELLNESS CATEGORY AT THE CANNES LIONS INTERNATIONAL FESTIVAL OF CREATIVITY FOR ITS #STEALOURSTAFF  CAMPAIGN FOR BECO, A SOCIAL ENTERPRISE THAT MAKES ENVIRONMENTALLY-FRIENDLY TOILETRIES WITH AN 80% VISUALLY IMPAIRED, DISABLED OR DISADVANTAGED WORKFORCE. THE CAMPAIGN CHALLENGED OTHER BRITISH BUSINESSES TO STEAL ITS STAFF IN ORDER TO RAISE AWARENESS OF THE 'DISABILITY EMPLOYMENT GAP'.
