Omnicom names Karen van Bergen Chief Environmental Sustainability Officer. NEW YORK, JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF KAREN VAN BERGEN TO CHIEF ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER, REPORTING TO JOHN WREN, CHAIRMAN AND CEO, OMNICOM. IN THIS ROLE, VAN BERGEN WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR OVERSEEING THE COMPANY'S CLIMATE CHANGE INITIATIVES AND PROCESSES WHICH INCLUDE SETTING MEASURABLE GOALS, POLICIES AND PARTNERSHIPS THAT WILL REDUCE ITS CARBON FOOTPRINT.
