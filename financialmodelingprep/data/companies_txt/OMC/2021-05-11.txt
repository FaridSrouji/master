Ketchum Chairman, Public Relations Industry Luminary Rob Flaherty Announces Retirement After 40-Year Career. NEW YORK, MAY 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROB FLAHERTY, CHAIRMAN OF LEADING GLOBAL COMMUNICATIONS CONSULTANCY KETCHUM, TODAY ANNOUNCED A PLANNED RETIREMENT AFTER AN ESTEEMED 40-YEAR CAREER AS AN INNOVATOR AND LEADER IN THE PUBLIC RELATIONS INDUSTRY AND MORE THAN 32 YEARS AT KETCHUM. HIS LAST DAY WITH THE FIRM WILL BE JUNE 15.
