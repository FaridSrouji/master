The New Essentials: A Year into the Pandemic, We Aren't 'Returning' to Work -We're Redefining It. NEW YORK, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AFTER A YEAR OF COVID-19 AND ITS EFFECTS ON WORK, A NEW STUDY BY GLOBAL COMMUNICATIONS CONSULTANCY KETCHUM SHOWS THAT MORE THAN TWO-THIRDS (70%) OF EMPLOYED AMERICANS SAY THEY ARE MORE LIKELY TO STAY AT THEIR CURRENT JOBS THAN THEY WERE BEFORE THE PANDEMIC. BUT WITH BOTH FRONTLINE AND NON-FRONTLINE WORKERS EXPERIENCING HIGH LEVELS OF BURNOUT AS A RESULT OF THE PANDEMIC, THE STUDY REVEALS DRAMATIC SHIFTS IN WHAT PEOPLE WANT FROM THEIR EMPLOYERS.
