Allianz Establishes New Agency Ecosystem with Multi-Year Agreement with Omnicom Group. NEW YORK, APRIL 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM GROUP (NYSE: OMC) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH ALLIANZ, A LEADING FINANCIAL SERVICES PROVIDER, FOR CREATIVE DEVELOPMENT AND PRODUCTION SERVICES. THROUGH THIS MASTER FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT, OMNICOM WILL PRODUCE WORK FOR ALLIANZ ON A GLOBAL AND LOCAL LEVEL, OFFERING CREATIVE SOLUTIONS TO ACTIVATE THE GLOBAL BRAND STRATEGY FOR THE MORE THAN 70 COUNTRIES ALLIANZ OPERATES IN.
