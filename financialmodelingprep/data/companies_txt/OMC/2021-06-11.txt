Omnicom Group Announces Divestiture of ICON International. NEW YORK, JUNE 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM MEDIA GROUP, A SUBSIDIARY OF OMNICOM GROUP (NYSE: OMC), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SALE OF ICON INTERNATIONAL, A SPECIALTY MEDIA COMPANY, TO ICON'S MANAGEMENT TEAM. THE SALE OF ICON IS PART OF OMNICOM'S CONTINUING REALIGNMENT OF ITS PORTFOLIO OF BUSINESSES AND IS CONSISTENT WITH ITS STRATEGIC PLAN AND INVESTMENT PRIORITIES.
