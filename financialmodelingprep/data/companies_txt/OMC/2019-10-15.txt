Omnicom Group Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Omnicom Group Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 3,623.8 million compared to USD 3,714.3 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 473.3 million compared to USD 502.3 million a year ago. Net income was USD 290.2 million compared to USD 298.9 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.33 compared to USD 1.33 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.32 compared to USD 1.32 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 10,812.5 million compared to USD 11,203.5 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,475.9 million compared to USD 1,506.3 million a year ago. Net income was USD 924.1 million compared to USD 927.2 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 4.19 compared to USD 4.08 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.17 compared to USD 4.06 a year ago.
