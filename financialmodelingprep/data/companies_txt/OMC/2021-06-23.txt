Omnicom Media Group Agencies Named Top Performing Media Networks At Cannes Lions. NEW YORK, JUNE 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- OMNICOM MEDIA GROUP (OMG), THE MEDIA SERVICES DIVISION OF OMNICOM GROUP INC. (NYSE: OMC) DOMINATED THE MEDIA NETWORK OF THE FESTIVAL COMPETITION AT THE 2021 CANNES FESTIVAL OF MEDIA.  OMG AGENCIES PHD AND OMD EARNED FIRST AND SECOND PLACE RESPECTIVELY IN THE HIGHLY COMPETITIVE CATEGORY THAT HONORS THE BEST PERFORMING MEDIA AGENCY.
