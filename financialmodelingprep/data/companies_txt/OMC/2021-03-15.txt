Credera's DMW Group Rebrands to Credera. LONDON, MARCH 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CREDERA, A CONSULTING, DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION AND ENGINEERING COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS LONDON-BASED TECHNOLOGY CONSULTANCY, DMW GROUP, WILL REBRAND AS CREDERA. THE CHANGE WILL GO INTO EFFECT ON MARCH 15, 2021, WITH ALL DMW GROUP NAMING AND BRANDING CHANGING TO CREDERA.
