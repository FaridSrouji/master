TracyLocke Names New President Tina Manikas. DALLAS, JUNE 8, 2021   /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LEADING COMMERCE AND COMMUNICATIONS AGENCY TRACYLOCKE HAS ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF TINA MANIKAS AS ITS NEW U.S. PRESIDENT EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. IN THIS NEW ROLE, MANIKAS WILL FOCUS ON DRIVING AGENCY GROWTH THROUGH INNOVATION, LEVERAGING HER OVER 20 YEARS OF MULTI-CATEGORY EXPERIENCE TO INCREASE THE AGENCY'S CAPABILITIES AND MAXIMIZE ITS GROWING PORTFOLIO.
