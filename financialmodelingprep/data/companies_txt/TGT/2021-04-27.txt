Target Announces Spring Collaboration with Plant Enthusiast and Interior Stylist Hilton Carter. MINNEAPOLIS, APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE: TGT) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS LIMITED-TIME-ONLY COLLABORATION WITH PLANT ENTHUSIAST AND INTERIOR STYLIST HILTON CARTER. MORE THAN 20 YEARS AGO, TARGET PIONEERED THE CONCEPT OF DESIGNER COLLABORATIONS, AND HAS SINCE CONTINUED TO INTRODUCE NEW AND EXCITING COLLECTIONS WITH BOTH WELL-ESTABLISHED AND EMERGING PARTNERS.
