Target Announces Bold New Sustainability Strategy: Target Forward. MINNEAPOLIS, JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE: TGT) TODAY UNVEILED TARGET FORWARD, THE COMPANY'S NEW SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY THAT PUTS ITS BUSINESS TO USE TO POSITIVELY IMPACT BOTH PEOPLE AND THE PLANET. BUILDING ON THE RETAILER'S LEGACY OF CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY AND SUSTAINABLE PRACTICES, TARGET FORWARD MARKS A NEW ERA IN SUSTAINABILITY FOR THE COMPANY, AS THE RETAILER AIMS TO CO-CREATE AN EQUITABLE AND REGENERATIVE FUTURE WITH ITS GUESTS, PARTNERS AND COMMUNITIES.
