Target Corporation Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend. MINNEAPOLIS, MARCH 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TARGET CORPORATION (NYSE:TGT) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 68 CENTS PER COMMON SHARE.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 10, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS MAY 19, 2021.
