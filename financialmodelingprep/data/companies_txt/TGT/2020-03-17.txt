Target Announces Reduced Store Hours, Dedicated Shopping Hours For Vulnerable Guests. TARGET ANNOUNCES REDUCED STORE HOURS, DEDICATED SHOPPING HOURS FOR VULNERABLE GUESTS.TARGET CORP - RETAILER TO CLOSE ALL STORES BY 9 P.M. DAILY.TARGET CORP - RESERVE FIRST HOUR OF SHOPPING EACH WEDNESDAY FOR 'VULNERABLE' GUESTS - INCLUDING ELDERLY AND THOSE WITH UNDERLYING HEALTH CONCERNS.TARGET -  CLOSING ALL TARGET CAFÉS, PIZZA HUTS, SNACK BARS, BEVERAGE BARS, STARBUCKS SEATING AREAS AND CONDIMENT STATIONS IN STORES.TARGET CORP - MODIFIED HOURS WILL ALLOW EVEN MORE TIME FOR TEAMS TO CLEAN AND RESTOCK STORE SHELVES.TARGET CORP - MAINTAIN LIMITS ON SELECT PRODUCTS, WOULD ASK GUESTS TO PURCHASE ONLY WHAT THEY NEED SO THERE'S ENOUGH SUPPLY TO ACCOMMODATE INCREASED DEMAND.TARGET -  WAIVING ABSENTEEISM POLICY & COVERING UP TO 14 DAYS OF QUARANTINE AND ILLNESS PAY FOR TEAM MEMBERS WITH A CONFIRMED CASE OF COVID-19.TARGET CORP - ADDING PAYROLL HOURS TO SUPPORT MORE CLEANING ROUTINES, INCLUDING ENSURING GUEST-FACING SURFACES.TARGET CORP - LEVERAGING SUPPLY CHAIN TO PRIORITIZE, FAST-TRACK FLOW OF PRODUCTS THAT ARE IN HIGHEST DEMAND, INCLUDING CLEANING PRODUCTS, PAPER PRODUCTS, FOOD, OVER COUNTER MEDICINE & BABY PRODUCTS.
