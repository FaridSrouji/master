Jacobs Wins Global Architecture and Engineering Contract with U.S. Air Force. DALLAS, MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS SELECTED TO CONTINUE SUPPORTING THE U.S. AIR FORCE'S ARCHITECTURE AND ENGINEERING (AE) NEEDS GLOBALLY THROUGH THE AE NEXT PROGRAM. THIS IS A MULTIPLE AWARD TASK ORDER CONTRACT (MATOC) AND SUPPORTS THE INDEFINITE DELIVERY, INDEFINITE QUANTITY (IDIQ) TASK ORDER CONTRACTS PRIMARILY INVOLVED IN PROVIDING PROFESSIONAL DESIGN AND ENGINEERING SERVICES FOR SUSTAINMENT, RESTORATION AND MODERNIZATION PROJECTS INCLUDING DEMOLITION.
