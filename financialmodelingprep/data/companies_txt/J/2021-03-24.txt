Jacobs Appointed as West Yorkshire Mass Transit Design Development Partner. DALLAS, MARCH 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE: J) HAS BEEN APPOINTED AS THE LEAD CONSULTANT, ON BEHALF OF THE MCBAINS CONSORTIUM, FOR THE DESIGN AND PLACEMAKING DEVELOPMENT PARTNER COMMISSION FOR THE WEST YORKSHIRE MASS TRANSIT PROGRAM, BEING DEVELOPED BY THE WEST YORKSHIRE COMBINED AUTHORITY (WYCA). WITH A POPULATION OF 2.3 MILLION AND AN ECONOMY OF NEARLY £70 BILLION ($95 BILLION), WEST YORKSHIRE REMAINS THE LARGEST METROPOLITAN AREA IN EUROPE WITHOUT AN URBAN TRANSIT SYSTEM.
