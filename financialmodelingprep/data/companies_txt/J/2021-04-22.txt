Jacobs Declares Quarterly Dividend. DALLAS, APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JACOBS (NYSE:J) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND PAYABLE TO SHAREHOLDERS IN THE AMOUNT OF $0.21 PER SHARE OF JACOBS COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON JUNE 25, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 28, 2021.
