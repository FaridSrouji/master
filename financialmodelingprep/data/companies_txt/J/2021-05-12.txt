Jacobs Wins Three American Planning Association Awards for Federal Planning Projects. DALLAS, MAY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACOBS (NYSE:J) WAS PRESENTED WITH THREE AWARDS FROM THE AMERICAN PLANNING ASSOCIATION (APA) FEDERAL PLANNING DIVISION (FPD). THE AWARDS, WHICH RECOGNIZE TEAMS FOR OUTSTANDING FEDERAL PLANNING WORK, LEADERSHIP AND INNOVATIVE SOLUTIONS, WERE GIVEN DURING THE MAY 12, 2021 VIRTUAL AWARDS CEREMONY AS PART OF THE FPD PORTION OF THE APA CONFERENCE.
