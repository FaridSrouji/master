Jacobs Engineering Group Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year Ended September 27, 2019. Jacobs Engineering Group Inc. announced earnings results for the fourth quarter and full year ended September 27, 2019. For the fourth quarter, the company announced sales was USD 3,392.862 million compared to USD 2,991.856 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 99.086 million compared to USD 160.846 million a year ago. Net income was USD 142.324 million compared to net loss of USD 37.541 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.16 compared to basic loss per share from continuing operations of USD 0.54 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.06 compared to basic loss per share of USD 0.26 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.04 compared to diluted loss per share of USD 0.26 a year ago. For the full year, sales was USD 12,737.868 million compared to USD 10,579.773 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 404.851 million compared to USD 387.443 million a year ago. Net income was USD 847.979 million compared to USD 163.431 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 2.11 compared to basic loss per share from continuing operations of USD 0.03 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 6.14 compared to USD 1.18 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 2.09 compared to diluted loss per share from continuing operations of USD 0.03 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 6.08 compared to USD 1.18 a year ago.
