Mettler-Toledo International Inc. announced that it expects to receive €180.16 million in funding from a group of investors. Mettler-Toledo International, Inc. (NYSE:MTD) announced that it has entered into a note purchase agreement for a private placement of 1.30% series 2019-B senior notes due November 6, 2034 for €135,000,000 and 19% series 2019-C senior notes due January 24, 2035 for $50,000,000 (€45,160,000) with the accredited institutional investors for aggregate gross proceeds of €180,160,000 on November 6, 2019. The 2019-B senior notes carries a fixed coupon of 1.3% per annum and would mature on November 6, 2034 and 2019-C senior notes carries a fixed coupon of 3.19% per annum and would mature on January 24, 2035. The transaction will include participation from returning lenders The Northwestern Mutual Life Insurance Company for €30,000,000 in 2019-B senior notes and $10,900,000 (€9,844,880) in 2019-C senior notes and MetLife Insurance K.K. for €20,000,000 in 2019-B senior notes, and The Prudential Insurance Company of America, Inc. for €16,964,000 in 2019-B senior notes, and other lenders. The interest on senior notes is payable semi-annually on May 6 and November 6 of each year, beginning on May 6, 2020. The company may at any time prepay the senior notes, in whole or in part, at a price equal to 100% of the principal amount plus accrued and unpaid interest plus. In the event of a change in control of the company, the company may be required to offer to prepay the senior notes at a price equal to 100% of the principal amount plus accrued and unpaid interest. The senior notes are senior unsecured obligations of the company. The senior notes will be issued in denominations of at least €100,000. The company shall have paid on or before the closing date, the reasonable fees, charges and disbursements of special counsel to the lenders.

On the same date, the company has issued 1.30% senior 2019-B notes for gross proceeds of €135,000,000 in its first tranche closing. The tranche included participation from returning lenders, The Lincoln National Life Insurance Company for €15,000,000, Athene Annuity and Life Company for €23,000,000, Pensionskasse des Bundes PUBLICA for €16,336,000, The Northwestern Mutual Life Insurance Company for €30,000,000, and MetLife Insurance K.K. for €20,000,000, and other lenders including Metropolitan Tower Life Insurance Company for €6,700,000, Athene Annuity & Life Assurance Company for €7,000,000, The Prudential Insurance Company of America, Inc. for €16,964,000. The second tranche is expected to held on January 24, 2020.
