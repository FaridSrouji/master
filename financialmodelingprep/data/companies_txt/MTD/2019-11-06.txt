Mettler-Toledo International Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019 and Full Years of 2019 and 2020. Mettler-Toledo International Inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019 and full years of 2019 and 2020. The company continues to expect local currency sales growth in 2019 to be approximately 5%. Full year sales guidance, excluding food retailing, remains at 6% in local currency.With respect to the fourth quarter, the company would expect local currency sales growth to be approximately 3%, which is an 11% growth on a 2-year stack basis.For 2020, the company expects local currency sales growth to be approximately 4%.
