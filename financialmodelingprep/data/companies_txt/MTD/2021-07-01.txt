Mettler-Toledo International Inc. to Host Second Quarter 2021 Earnings Conference Call. COLUMBUS, OH, JULY 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- METTLER-TOLEDO INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE:MTD) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL TO DISCUSS THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY RESULTS ON THURSDAY, JULY 29, 2021 AT 5:00 P.M. EASTERN TIME. TO HEAR A LIVE WEBCAST OF THE CALL, VISIT WWW.MT.COM/INVESTORS ON THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE. A REPLAY OF THE WEBCAST WILL BE AVAILABLE UNTIL AUGUST 2, 2021.
