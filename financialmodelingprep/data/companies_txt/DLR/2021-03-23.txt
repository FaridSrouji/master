Digital Realty Introduces New Direct Connections to Google Cloud to Enable Hybrid Network Connections in Five Key Global Metros. AUSTIN, TEXAS, MARCH 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DIGITAL REALTY (NYSE: DLR), A LEADING GLOBAL PROVIDER OF CARRIER- AND CLOUD-NEUTRAL DATA CENTER, COLOCATION AND INTERCONNECTION SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS EXPANDED SECURE, PRIVATE ACCESS TO GOOGLE CLOUD'S GLOBAL NETWORK IN FIVE KEY METROS.  THESE NEW ON-RAMPS WILL ENABLE DIRECT, HIGH-SPEED, LOW-LATENCY CONNECTIONS ON DIGITAL REALTY'S ATLANTA AND LOS ANGELES CONNECTED CAMPUSES IN THE U.S. AS WELL AS VIENNA IN AUSTRIA AND DÜSSELDORF IN GERMANY.
