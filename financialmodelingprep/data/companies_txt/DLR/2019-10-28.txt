Digital Realty Trust Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Digital Realty Trust Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 806.466 million compared to USD 768.924 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 164.767 million compared to USD 139.065 million a year ago. Net income was USD 66.497 million compared to USD 87.597 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.24 compared to USD 0.33 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 2,421.778 million compared to USD 2,268.211 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 455.282 million compared to USD 426.940 million a year ago. Net income was USD 243.477 million compared to USD 279.687 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.85 compared to USD 1.06 a year ago.
