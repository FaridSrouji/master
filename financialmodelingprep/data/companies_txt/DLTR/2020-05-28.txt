Dollar Tree, Inc. Reports Results For First Quarter Fiscal 2020. DOLLAR TREE, INC. REPORTS RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER FISCAL 2020.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.04.Q1 SALES $6.29 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $6.14 BILLION.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.85 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY ENTERPRISE SAME-STORE SALES INCREASED 7.0%.QTRLY SAME-STORE SALES FOR FAMILY DOLLAR INCREASED 15.5%.QTRLY DOLLAR TREE SAME-STORE SALES DECREASED 0.9%.FAMILY DOLLAR H2 RENOVATION AND DOLLAR TREE SNACK ZONE INITIATIVES ARE IN PROCESS OF RESUMING IN SELECT, LOWER-RISK MARKETS.CAPITAL EXPENDITURES FOR 2020 ARE NOW EXPECTED TO BE ABOUT $1.0 BILLION.DOLLAR TREE - NOW EXPECTS TO COMPLETE ABOUT 750 FAMILY DOLLAR H2 RENOVATIONS IN FISCAL YEAR 2020 COMPARED TO ITS ORIGINAL EXPECTATION OF 1,250.NOW EXPECTS TO OPEN 500 NEW STORES IN FISCAL 2020.DOLLAR TREE - CO'S TARGET FOR ANNUAL DOLLAR TREE SNACK ZONE INSTALLATIONS IS UNCHANGED, ALTHOUGH TIMING OF COMPLETION WILL SHIFT RELATED TO SUSPENSION.
