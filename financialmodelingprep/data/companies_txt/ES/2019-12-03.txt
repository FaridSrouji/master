Nexans Announces Agreement With Eversource And Ørsted For Offshore Wind Farm in US. NEXANS, EVERSOURCE AND ØRSTED SIGN FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT FOR NORTH AMERICAN OFFSHORE WIND FARM DEVELOPMENT WITH OPPORTUNITY TO PROVIDE UP TO 1,000 KILOMETERS OF EXPORT CABLE FOR MULTIPLE ØRSTED PROJECTS TILL 2027 IN UNITED STATES.FIRST DELIVERY IS EXPECTED BY 2022 AND AGREEMENT EXTENDS UNTIL 2027..
