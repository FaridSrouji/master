Nisource Announces Sale Of Columbia Gas Of Massachusetts To Eversource. NISOURCE ANNOUNCES SALE OF COLUMBIA GAS OF MASSACHUSETTS TO EVERSOURCE.NISOURCE INC - AGREEMENT PROVIDES FOR PURCHASE PRICE OF $1.1 BILLION IN CASH.NISOURCE INC- PURCHASE PRICE REPRESENTS A LOSS COMPARED TO BOOK VALUE OF COLUMBIA GAS OF MASSACHUSETTS.NISOURCE-DUE TO EXECUTION OF THIS TRANSACTION, NISOURCE IS WITHDRAWING ITS 2020 NET OPERATING EARNINGS PER SHARE (NON-GAAP) GUIDANCE OF $1.36 TO $1.40.NISOURCE INC - TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO ENABLE NISOURCE TO ELIMINATE ITS PREVIOUSLY PLANNED 2020 BLOCK EQUITY ISSUANCE.NISOURCE INC - NISOURCE CONTINUES TO EXPECT TO MAKE CAPITAL INVESTMENTS OF $1.8 TO $1.9 BILLION IN 2020.NISOURCE INC - FOLLOWING COMPLETION OF TRANSACTION, COMPANY EXPECTS TO INITIATE 2021 NET OPERATING EARNINGS PER SHARE GUIDANCE.NISOURCE-FOLLOWING COMPLETION OF DEAL CO EXPECTS TO ESTABLISH 5-7% LONGTERM GROWTH RATE FOR NET OPERATING EARNINGS/SHARE&DIVIDEND WITH 2021 AS BASE YEAR.
