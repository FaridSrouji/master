DXC TECHNOLOGY REPORTS SECOND QUARTER FISCAL 2020 RESULTS.Q2 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.38 FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS.Q2 LOSS PER SHARE $8.19 FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS.Q2 REVENUE $4.851 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $4.92 BILLION.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.42 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.DXC TECHNOLOGY - Q2 LOSS FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS INCLUDES CUMULATIVE NEGATIVE IMPACT OF CERTAIN ITEMS OF $2,477 MILLION.DXC TECHNOLOGY - Q2 LOSS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS INCLUDES $11.10 PER SHARE OF GOODWILL IMPAIRMENT.DXC TECHNOLOGY - Q2 LOSS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS INCLUDES A $2.43 PER SHARE OF GAIN ON ARBITRATION AWARD.
Dxc Technology Company Reports Earnings Results for the Second Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. DXC Technology Company announced earnings results for the second quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the second quarter, the company announced sales was USD 4,851 million compared to USD 5,013 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 2,119 million compared to net income of USD 262 million a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 8.19 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 0.93 a year ago. Diluted loss per share was USD 8.19 compared to diluted earnings per share of USD 0.92 a year ago. For the half year, sales was USD 9,741 million compared to USD 10,295 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 1,956 million compared to net income of USD 521 million a year ago. Basic loss per share from continuing operations was USD 7.44 compared to basic earnings per share from continuing operations of USD 1.72 a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 7.44 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 1.84 a year ago. Diluted loss per share from continuing operations was USD 7.44 compared to diluted earnings per share from continuing operations of USD 1.69 a year ago. Diluted loss per share was USD 7.44 compared to diluted earnings per share of USD 1.81 a year ago.
