DXC Technology Company Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend for the Quarter Ending September 30, 2019, Payable on January 14, 2020. The Board of Directors of DXC Technology declared a regular quarterly dividend payment for the quarter ending September 30, 2019 of $0.21 per share on the company's Common Stock. The dividend will be paid on January 14, 2020 to stockholders of record at the close of business on December 4, 2019.
