DXC Technology Appoints Vinod Bagal as Executive Vice President, Global Transformation. DXC Technology announced the appointment of Vinod Bagal as executive vice president, Global Transformation. Bagal will report to company president and CEO Mike Salvino. His appointment is effective immediately. Bagal will manage DXC's delivery innovation to provide improved services to customers in the most efficient manner. He will focus on delivery excellence, including process improvements; the introduction of new delivery capabilities to provide DXC people with new career paths and skill development; and the use of new technologies such as DXC Bionix to automate delivery processes. Bagal is a well-known leader in the IT services industry. He most recently served as senior vice president for North America Delivery and Global Multi-Service Integration at Cognizant Technology Services.
