DXC Technology Announces Sale Of Healthcare Provider Software Business To Dedalus Group For $525 Million In Cash. DXC TECHNOLOGY ANNOUNCES THE SALE OF DXC’S HEALTHCARE PROVIDER SOFTWARE BUSINESS TO THE DEDALUS GROUP FOR $525 MILLION IN CASH.DXC TECHNOLOGY CO - TO SELL DXC'S HEALTHCARE SOFTWARE PROVIDER BUSINESS TO PRIVATELY HELD DEDALUS GROUP.DXC TECHNOLOGY CO - TRANSACTION WAS APPROVED BY DXC'S BOARD.DXC TECHNOLOGY CO - SALE IS NOT SUBJECT TO ANY FINANCING CONDITIONS OR SHAREHOLDER APPROVALS.
