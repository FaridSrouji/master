DXC Technology Appoints Kristin Slattery to Corporate Operations Role. DXC Technology announced the appointment of Kristin Slattery as vice president of corporate operations, a new position in the Office of the Chief Executive. Slattery will report to company president and CEO Mike Salvino. Her appointment is effective immediately. Slattery will be responsible for driving improvements in company performance and efficiency, with a goal of enabling DXC employees to focus more squarely on serving customers. She will streamline processes, optimize operational execution and lead key cross-functional and cross-regional initiatives.
