Equifax Ignite Live 2021 Explores Digital Convergence for Financial Services. ATLANTA, MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX INC. (NYSE:EFX), A DATA, ANALYTICS, AND TECHNOLOGY COMPANY ANNOUNCED ITS FREE, VIRTUAL CONFERENCE, IGNITE LIVE 2021 , WILL TAKE PLACE TUESDAY, MAY 25 FROM 10AM-2PM ET. THIS YEAR'S EVENT FOCUSES ON DIGITAL CONVERGENCE ACROSS THE FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY.
