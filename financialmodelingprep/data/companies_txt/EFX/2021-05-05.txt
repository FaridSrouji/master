Equifax Launches 2020 Security Annual Report. ATLANTA, MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX INC., A DATA, ANALYTICS AND TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY RELEASED AN INAUGURAL 2020 SECURITY ANNUAL REPORT , DETAILING THE COMPANY'S THREE-YEAR ENTERPRISE-WIDE CLOUD-TECHNOLOGY AND SECURITY TRANSFORMATION, ESTABLISHING THE COMPANY AS A LEADER IN SECURITY. THE STEPS EQUIFAX HAS TAKEN TO QUICKLY REBUILD ITS ENTIRE ORGANIZATION FROM TECHNOLOGY TO CULTURE SERVE AS A BLUEPRINT FOR HOW OTHER ORGANIZATIONS CAN ESTABLISH A SECURITY-FIRST APPROACH TO MAINTAIN SUCCESS AND PROTECT THEMSELVES AND THEIR CUSTOMERS.
