Equifax Brings New Decision Intelligence to the Cloud-Based OneView Report for Businesses With The Work Number Database. ATLANTA, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUIFAX (NYSE: EFX) IS BRINGING NEW DECISION INTELLIGENCE TO LENDERS AND OTHER BUSINESSES WITH THE CLOUD-BASED ONEVIEW™ REPORT WHICH NOW INCLUDES ACCESS TO REAL-TIME INCOME AND EMPLOYMENT INFORMATION FROM THE WORK NUMBER® DATABASE. THE VISUAL ONEVIEW REPORT SETS A NEW INDUSTRY STANDARD IN THE DELIVERY OF CONSUMER CREDIT INSIGHTS BY PROVIDING ACCESS TO TRADITIONAL CREDIT DATA AND DIFFERENTIATED DATA SOURCES THAT ONLY EQUIFAX CAN PROVIDE IN A SINGLE INQUIRY TO HELP BUSINESSES QUICKLY AND RESPONSIBLY OPEN UP NEW FINANCIAL OPPORTUNITIES.
