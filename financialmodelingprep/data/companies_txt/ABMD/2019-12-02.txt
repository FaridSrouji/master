Impulse Dynamics N.V. announced that it has received $80.25 million in funding from Amzak Health Investors, Llc, Wellington Management Group LLP, Kennedy Lewis Investment Management LLC, ZOLL Medical Corporation, Abiomed, Inc. and other investors. Impulse Dynamics N.V. announced that it has raised $80,250,000 in its series D round of funding led by new investor Amzak Health Investors, Llc on December 3, 2019. The transaction also included participation from new investors Wellington Management Group LLP, Kennedy Lewis Investment Management LLC for $25,000,000, Acorn Biosciences, Minth Holdings Limited, ZOLL Medical Corporation, Abiomed, Inc. (NasdaqGS:ABMD), Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officers of the company, and another additional corporate investor. The company has issued non-convertible debt and equity in the transaction. As part of the transaction, Joyce Erony from Amzak Health Investors, Llc has joined the company's board of directors.
