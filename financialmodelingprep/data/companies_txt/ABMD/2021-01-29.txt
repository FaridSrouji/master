Large Clinical Study Presented at STS 2021 Finds 79% Survival Rate with Impella 5.5 with SmartAssist. DANVERS, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ABIOMED (NASDAQ: ABMD) ANNOUNCES A LARGE STUDY OF 356 PATIENTS TREATED WITH IMPELLA 5.5 WITH SMARTASSIST AT 16 U.S. AND GERMAN CENTERS FOUND A 79% SURVIVAL RATE AT EXPLANT. A MAJORITY OF SURVIVING PATIENTS RECOVERED THEIR NATIVE HEART FUNCTION WITHOUT NEEDING FURTHER MECHANICAL SUPPORT OR A HEART TRANSPLANT.
