Mosaic Announces Temporary Curtailment At Its Esterhazy Potash Mine. MOSAIC ANNOUNCES TEMPORARY CURTAILMENT AT ITS ESTERHAZY POTASH MINE.MOSAIC CO - CURTAILMENT IS NOT EXPECTED TO IMPACT PACE OF DEVELOPMENT AT ESTERHAZY MINE K3 PROJECT.MOSAIC CO - CURTAILMENT BRINGS ANNOUNCED POTASH CURTAILMENTS TO UP TO 600,000 TONNES.MOSAIC - NEAR-TERM FERTILIZER MARKETS REMAIN CHALLENGING.MOSAIC - CONTINUE TO EXPECT A VERY STRONG APPLICATION SEASON IN BRAZIL AND NORTH AMERICA, AND A BETTER SUPPLY AND DEMAND BALANCE IN 2020.MOSAIC - IF FULL AMOUNT OF CURTAILMENT IS REALIZED IN LOWER Q4 2019 SALES, IT WOULD RESULT IN NEGATIVE IMPACT OF ABOUT $150 MILLION IN ADJUSTED EBITDA.MOSAIC CO - INCREASED CURTAILMENT IS BASED ON INCREASING INVENTORIES AS A RESULT OF A SHORT-TERM SLOWDOWN IN GLOBAL POTASH MARKETS.MOSAIC CO - INCREASED CURTAILMENT IS DUE TO INCREASED RISKS OF A DELAY IN CHINESE CONTRACT SETTLEMENT.
