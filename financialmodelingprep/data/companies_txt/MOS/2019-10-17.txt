The Mosaic Company Appoints David T. Seaton to Serve on the Audit Committee and the Compensation Committee. As previously reported, on September 8, 2019, David T. Seaton was elected to serve as a director of The Mosaic Company effective as of September 9, 2019, for a term expiring at the annual meeting of shareholders in 2020 or until his successor is elected and qualified. On October 16, 2019, the Board appointed Mr. Seaton to serve on the Audit Committee and the Compensation Committee.
