Mosaic Co Says On July 24, 2020 Co, Units Entered Into An Amendment To Its Unsecured Revolving Credit Facility. MOSAIC CO -  ON JULY 24, 2020 CO, UNITS ENTERED INTO AN AMENDMENT TO ITS UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.MOSAIC CO -  ENTERED INTO THAT CERTAIN 1ST AMENDMENT TO 2ND AMENDED AND RESTATED CREDIT AGREEMENT, EXTENSION AGREEMENT, AND INCREASE AGREEMENT.MOSAIC CO -  1ST AMENDMENT, AMONG OTHER THINGS, EXTENDS MATURITY DATE OF MOSAIC CREDIT FACILITY BY ONE YEAR, TO NOVEMBER 18, 2022.MOSAIC CO -  1ST AMENDMENT, AMONG OTHER THINGS, INCREASES REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY FROM $2.0 BILLION TO $2.2 BILLION.
