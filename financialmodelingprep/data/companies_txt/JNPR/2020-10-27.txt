Juniper Networks Reports Preliminary Third Quarter 2020 Financial Results. SUNNYVALE, Calif., Oct. 27, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Juniper Networks (NYSE: JNPR), a leader in secure, AI-driven networks, today reported preliminary financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2020 and provided its outlook for the three months ending December 31, 2020.
