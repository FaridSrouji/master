SBA Communications Corporation to Speak at Deutsche Bank's 29th Annual Media, Internet & Telecom Conference. BOCA RATON, FLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SBA COMMUNICATIONS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: SBAC) (“SBA”) ANNOUNCES THAT JEFFREY A. STOOPS, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, IS SCHEDULED TO SPEAK AT DEUTSCHE BANK'S 29TH ANNUAL MEDIA, INTERNET & TELECOM CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, MARCH 9, 2021 AT 2:00PM ET. THIS WILL BE A VIRTUAL CONFERENCE. THE AUDIO PRESENTATION FOR SBA CAN BE ACCESSED BY VISITING WWW.SBASITE.COM.
