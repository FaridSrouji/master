VF Corp Announces Organizational Changes To Advance Its Asia Pacific Regional Strategy. VF CORPORATION ANNOUNCES ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGES TO ADVANCE ITS ASIA PACIFIC REGIONAL STRATEGY.VF CORP - HAS CREATED NEW POSITION OF PRESIDENT, GREATER CHINA.VF CORP - EXPECTS PERCENTAGE OF VF'S TOTAL BUSINESS IN ASIA PACIFIC REGION TO GROW TO NEARLY 80 PERCENT BY FISCAL YEAR 2024.VF CORP - WILL ANNOUNCE LEADER APPOINTED TO POSITION OF PRESIDENT, GREATER CHINA LATER THIS SUMMER.
