Vans Spotlights the Power of Skateboarding in Partnership with Tony Hawk, Skateistan and Vans Athletes in New Community-focused Campaign. COSTA MESA, CALIF., JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN CELEBRATION OF GO SKATEBOARDING DAY,  VANS, THE GLOBAL LEADER IN SKATEBOARDING FOR OVER 50 YEARS, PROUDLY ANNOUNCES A NEW PARTNERSHIP WITH  SKATEISTAN , THE AWARD-WINNING INTERNATIONAL NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION WHICH EMPOWERS YOUTH THROUGH SKATEBOARDING AND EDUCATION IN AFGHANISTAN, CAMBODIA, SOUTH AFRICA AND SUPPORTS OTHER SOCIAL SKATE PROJECTS AROUND THE WORLD.
