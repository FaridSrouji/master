Vans Commits to Make Core Materials 100% Regenerative, Responsibly Sourced, Renewable or Recycled by 2030. COSTA MESA, CALIF., APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, VANS, THE ORIGINAL ACTION SPORTS BRAND AND GLOBAL ICON FOR CREATIVE EXPRESSION, ANNOUNCED NEW GLOBAL COMMITMENTS TO ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND RESPONSIBILITY THAT WILL BE ACHIEVED BY 2030.
