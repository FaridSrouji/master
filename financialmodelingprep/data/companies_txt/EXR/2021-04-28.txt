Extra Space Storage Inc. Reports 2021 First Quarter Results. SALT LAKE CITY, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EXTRA SPACE STORAGE INC. (NYSE: EXR) (THE "COMPANY"), A LEADING OWNER AND OPERATOR OF SELF-STORAGE FACILITIES IN THE UNITED STATES AND A MEMBER OF THE S&P 500, ANNOUNCED OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2021. HIGHLIGHTS FOR THE THREE MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2021 : ACHIEVED NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON STOCKHOLDERS OF $1.53 PER DILUTED SHARE, REPRESENTING A 84.3% INCREASE COMPARED TO THE SAME PERIOD IN 2020.
