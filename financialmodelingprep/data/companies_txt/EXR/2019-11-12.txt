Extra Space Storage Inc. Declares Dividend for the Fourth Quarter of 2019, Payable on December 31, 2019. Extra Space Storage Inc. announced that the company's board of directors has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.90 per share on the common stock of the company for the fourth quarter of 2019. The dividend is payable on December 31, 2019 to stockholders of record at the close of business on December 16, 2019.
