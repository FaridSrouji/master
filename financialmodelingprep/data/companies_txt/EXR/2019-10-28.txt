Extra Space Storage Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Extra Space Storage Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 337.505 million compared to USD 306.953 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 162.638 million compared to USD 184.398 million a year ago. Net income was USD 108.087 million compared to USD 130.418 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.84 compared to USD 1.03 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.83 compared to USD 1.02 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 972.653 million compared to USD 889.251 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 470.313 million compared to USD 463.026 million a year ago. Net income was USD 307.685 million compared to USD 313.827 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.4 compared to USD 2.49 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.37 compared to USD 2.48 a year ago.
