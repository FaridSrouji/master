Caesars Announces Universal Mask Policy In All Properties. CAESARS ANNOUNCES UNIVERSAL MASK POLICY IN ALL PROPERTIES.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT - EVERYONE INDOORS AT CO'S PROPERTIES ACROSS CAESARS NETWORK WILL BE REQUIRED TO WEAR MASKS AT ALL TIMES.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT -  UPDATED MASK POLICY APPLIES TO ALL EMPLOYEES, VENDORS, CONTRACTORS, GUESTS & PASSERSBY IN CO'S PROPERTIES.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT - ONLY EXCEPTION TO UPDATED MASK POLICY  IS WHEN EMPLOYEES OR GUESTS ARE EATING OR DRINKING.
