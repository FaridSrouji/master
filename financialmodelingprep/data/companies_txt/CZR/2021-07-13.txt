Chef Gordon Ramsay Set To Debut Largest HELL'S KITCHEN Concept To Date At Harrah's Resort Southern California. FUNNER, CALIF., JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, HARRAH'S RESORT SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA ANNOUNCED THEIR PARTNERSHIP WITH CELEBRATED MULTI-MICHELIN-STARRED CHEF AND TELEVISION PERSONALITY GORDON RAMSAY TO LAUNCH A BRAND-NEW HELL'S KITCHEN RESTAURANT, SET TO DEBUT IN SPRING 2022.
