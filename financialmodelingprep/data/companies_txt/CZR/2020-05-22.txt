Caesars Entertainment Announces Las Vegas Reopening Plans. CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT ANNOUNCES LAS VEGAS REOPENING PLANS.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT -TO RESUME GAMING AND HOSPITALITY OPERATIONS IN LAS VEGAS PROPERTIES ON DATE AND TIME FOR REOPENING SPECIFIED BY STATE OF NEVADA.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT-ANTICIPATES THAT SEVERAL RETAIL AND DINING OUTLETS ALONG LINQ PROMENADE, AS WELL AS HIGH ROLLER OBSERVATION WHEEL, WILL REOPEN.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT CORP - CAESARS' OTHER LAS VEGAS PROPERTIES AND DINING AMENITIES ARE EXPECTED TO REOPEN BASED ON CUSTOMER DEMAND.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT- EXPECTS HARRAH'S LAS VEGAS HOTEL & CASINO AND GAMING FLOOR AT LINQ HOTEL & CASINO TO BE NEXT PROPERTIES IN LAS VEGAS TO REOPEN.
