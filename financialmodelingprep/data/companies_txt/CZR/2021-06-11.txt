Harrah's Las Vegas Unveils a $200 Million Renovation, Including Enhanced Accommodations, Casino Floor and More. LAS VEGAS, JUNE 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOLLOWING A COMPLETED INVESTMENT OF MORE THAN $200 MILLION, HARRAH'S LAS VEGAS NOW FEATURES 2,542 REDESIGNED GUEST ROOMS, A COMPLETE CASINO FLOOR REMODEL AND TWO NEW LED MARQUEES AT THE NORTH AND SOUTH ENTRANCES. GUESTS CAN ALSO ENJOY A VARIETY OF HOTEL, DINING AND ENTERTAINMENT OFFERINGS INCLUDING THE SPA AT HARRAH'S LAS VEGAS, PIZZACAKE BY BUDDY VALASTRO AND DONNY OSMOND'S FIRST-EVER SOLO RESIDENCY.
