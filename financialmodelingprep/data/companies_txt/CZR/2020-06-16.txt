Caesars Says Revenue For Reopened Nevada Properties For Period They Were Operating In June Through June 10 Declined About 56-58%. CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT -REVENUE FOR REOPENED REGIONAL PROPERTIES FOR PERIOD THEY WERE OPERATING IN MAY AND/OR JUNE THROUGH JUNE 10, 2020 WERE FLAT TO UP 2%.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT - REVENUE FOR REOPENED NEVADA PROPERTIES FOR PERIOD THEY WERE OPERATING IN JUNE THROUGH JUNE 10 DECLINED ABOUT 56-58%.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT -ADJUSTED EBITDA FOR REOPENED NEVADA PROPERTIES FOR PERIOD THEY WERE OPERATING IN JUNE THROUGH JUNE 10, DECLINED ABOUT 70-80%.CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT - ADJUSTED EBITDA FOR REOPENED REGIONAL PROPERTIES IN MAY AND/OR JUNE THROUGH JUNE 10 INCREASED ABOUT 35% TO 40% VERSUS YEAR AGO.
