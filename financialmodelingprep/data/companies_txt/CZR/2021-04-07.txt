Caesars Entertainment, Inc. Completes Previously Announced Sale of MontBleu Resort Casino & Spa to Bally's Corporation. RENO, NEV. AND LAS VEGAS, APRIL 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. (NASDAQ: CZR) ("CAESARS") TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CLOSING OF THE SALE OF MONTBLEU RESORT CASINO & SPA TO BALLY'S CORPORATION. THE CLOSING SATISFIES CAESARS' AGREEMENT WITH THE FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION TO DIVEST THE ASSET IN CONNECTION WITH THE CAESARS-ELDORADO MERGER, WHICH CLOSED IN JULY 2020.
