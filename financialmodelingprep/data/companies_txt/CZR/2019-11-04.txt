Caesars Entertainment Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Caesars Entertainment Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 2,236 million compared to USD 2,185 million a year ago. Operating loss was USD 68 million compared to operating Income of USD 232 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 359 million compared to net income of USD 110 million a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 0.53 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 0.16 a year ago. Diluted loss per share was USD 0.53 compared to diluted earnings per share of USD 0.14 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 6,573 million compared to USD 6,276 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 441 million compared to USD 639 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 891 million compared to net income of USD 105 million a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 1.32 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 0.15 a year ago.
