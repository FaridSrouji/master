An Industry First, William Hill Sportsbook Officially Opens at Capital One Arena in Washington, D.C.. WASHINGTON, MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARKING A MAJOR MILESTONE IN AMERICAN SPORTS HISTORY, THE WILLIAM HILL SPORTSBOOK AT MONUMENTAL SPORTS & ENTERTAINMENT'S CAPITAL ONE ARENA OFFICIALLY OPENED TODAY, BECOMING THE FIRST EVER SPORTS BETTING VENUE TO OPEN WITHIN A U.S. PROFESSIONAL SPORTS FACILITY. WILLIAM HILL, A PART OF CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, AND MONUMENTAL SPORTS & ENTERTAINMENT ANNOUNCED A GROUNDBREAKING PARTNERSHIP DEAL IN OCTOBER 2019 TO OPEN THE FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND, MARQUEE DESTINATION FOR LOCAL, REGIONAL AND GLOBAL VISITORS.
