Caesars Entertainment to Invest $400 Million Into Its Atlantic City Resorts By 2023. ATLANTIC CITY, N.J., APRIL 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. ("CAESARS" OR THE "COMPANY") SHARED EXCITING DETAILS TODAY ON ITS $400 MILLION MASTER PLAN INVESTMENT IN ATLANTIC CITY WHERE IT OWNS AND OPERATES THREE OF THE REGION'S PREEMINENT CASINO DESTINATIONS – CAESARS, HARRAH'S RESORT AND TROPICANA ATLANTIC CITY.
