Caesars Entertainment Announces Completion of William Hill PLC Acquisition. LAS VEGAS AND RENO, NEV., APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAESARS ENTERTAINMENT, INC. (NASDAQ: CZR) ("CAESARS" OR "THE COMPANY") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF WILLIAM HILL PLC (LSE: WMH) ("WILLIAM HILL") FOR APPROXIMATELY $4.0 BILLION.
