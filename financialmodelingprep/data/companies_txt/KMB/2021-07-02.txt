Kimberly-Clark Named One of the Best Companies for Multicultural Women by Seramount. DALLAS, JULY 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT WAS NAMED ONE OF THE 2021 BEST COMPANIES FOR MULTICULTURAL WOMEN BY SERAMOUNT (FORMERLY WORKING MOTHER MEDIA). THE AWARD RECOGNIZES COMPANIES THAT CREATE AND USE BEST PRACTICES IN HIRING, RETAINING, AND PROMOTING MULTICULTURAL WOMEN IN THE UNITED STATES.
