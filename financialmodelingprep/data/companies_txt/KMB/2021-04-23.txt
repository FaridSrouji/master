Kimberly-Clark Announces First Quarter 2021 Results. DALLAS, APRIL 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2021 RESULTS. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY FIRST QUARTER 2021 NET SALES OF $4.7 BILLION DECREASED 5 PERCENT COMPARED TO THE YEAR-AGO PERIOD, INCLUDING AN ORGANIC SALES DECLINE OF 8 PERCENT.
