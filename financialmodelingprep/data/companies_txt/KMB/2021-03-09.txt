Kimberly-Clark Names Paula Ramos as Chief Strategy Officer. DALLAS, MARCH 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KIMBERLY-CLARK CORPORATION (NYSE: KMB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT PAULA RAMOS HAS BEEN NAMED CHIEF STRATEGY OFFICER, EFFECTIVE MARCH 15, 2021, WITH GLOBAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE COMPANY'S ENTERPRISE STRATEGY. RAMOS BECOMES THE FIRST LATINA TO SERVE ON THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE TEAM, AND WILL REPORT TO MIKE HSU, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.
