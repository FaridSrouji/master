Kimberly-Clark and Texas Women's Foundation Partner on GENDER MATTERS™ Podcast Series. DALLAS, JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TEXAS WOMEN'S FOUNDATION (TXWF) RECENTLY LAUNCHED THE GENDER MATTERS™ PODCAST SERIES THAT FEATURES SEVEN FEMALE LEADERS FROM KIMBERLY-CLARK INTERVIEWING THE  2021 TXWF LEADERSHIP FORUM & AWARDS CELEBRATION WINNERS . THE AWARDS HONOR SEVEN FEMALE TRAILBLAZERS IN TEXAS WHO HAVE LED THE WAY IN ADVANCING EQUITY, ECONOMIC SECURITY AND LEADERSHIP FOR WOMEN AND GIRLS AND CREATED A PATHWAY OF OPPORTUNITY FOR OTHER WOMEN TO FOLLOW.
