Citizens Financial Group, Inc. Declares Cash Dividend for the Fourth Quarter 2019, Payable on November 13, 2019. Citizens Financial Group, Inc. announced that its board of directors declared a fourth quarter 2019 cash dividend of $0.36 per common share. The dividend is payable on November 13, 2019 to stockholders of record at the close of business on October 30, 2019. The quarterly common dividend is up 33% year over year.
