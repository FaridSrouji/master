NVIDIA Announces Four-for-One Stock Split, Pending Stockholder Approval at Annual Meeting Set for June 3. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MAY 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NVIDIA TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A FOUR-FOR-ONE SPLIT OF NVIDIA'S COMMON STOCK IN THE FORM OF A STOCK DIVIDEND TO MAKE STOCK OWNERSHIP MORE ACCESSIBLE TO INVESTORS AND EMPLOYEES.
