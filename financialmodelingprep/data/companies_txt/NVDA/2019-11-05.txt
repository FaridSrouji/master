NVIDIA Wins New AI Inference Benchmarks. NVIDIA posted the results on new benchmarks measuring the performance of AI inference workloads in data centers and at the edge building on the company's equally strong position in recent benchmarks measuring AI training. NVIDIA won a series of new benchmarks tracking the performance of tools for running AI inference both at the edge and at the data center. The NVIDIA EGXplatform provides a wide range of offerings for running AI inference workloads with incredible speed. The results of the industry's first independent suite of AI benchmarks for inference, called MLPerf Inference 0.5, demonstrate the performance of NVIDIA Turing GPUs for data centers and NVIDIA Xavier system-on-a-chip for edge computing.
