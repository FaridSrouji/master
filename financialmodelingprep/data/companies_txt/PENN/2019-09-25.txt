Penn National Gaming, Inc. Announces That William J. Fair, Will Be Stepping Down as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, Effective from March 31, 2020. Penn National Gaming, Inc. announced that William J. Fair, will be stepping down as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer at the conclusion of his current employment contract term, which expires March 31, 2020. Mr. Fair, who has served in his current role as CFO since 2017, joined Penn National in 2014 as Senior Vice President and Chief Development Officer. Mr. Fair has agreed to stay with the Company through the end of the first quarter 2020, which will allow time for the Company to conduct a formal search for his successor and ensure a seamless transition of his responsibilities.
