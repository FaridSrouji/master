Penn National Gaming Prices $400 Million of 4.125% Senior Notes Due 2029. WYOMISSING, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENN NATIONAL GAMING, INC. (NASDAQ: PENN) (“PENN NATIONAL” OR THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF $400 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF NEW 4.125% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2029, WHICH WILL BE ISSUED IN A PRIVATE PLACEMENT. THE NEW NOTES WILL BE ISSUED AT PAR. THE NOTES WILL BE UNSUBORDINATED, UNSECURED OBLIGATIONS OF PENN NATIONAL AND WILL NOT BE GUARANTEED.
