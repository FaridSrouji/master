Penn National Gaming, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year Ending December 31, 2019. Penn National Gaming, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter and full year ending December 31, 2019. For the quarter, the company expects revenues of $1,350.7 million, net income of $43.1 million, operating income of $180.5 million and diluted earnings per common share of $0.37.For the year, the company expects revenues of $5,310.9 million, net income of $179.1 million, operating income of $739.6 million and diluted earnings per common share of $1.53.
