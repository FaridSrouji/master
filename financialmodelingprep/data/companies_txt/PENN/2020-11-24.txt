Penn National Gaming Names Marla Kaplowitz to Board of Directors. WYOMISSING, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENN NATIONAL GAMING, INC. (NASDAQ: PENN) (“THE COMPANY” OR “PENN NATIONAL”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED MARLA KAPLOWITZ TO THE BOARD, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY REGULATORY APPROVALS. MS. KAPLOWITZ'S APPOINTMENT WILL EXPAND THE SIZE OF THE BOARD TO EIGHT MEMBERS, SIX OF WHOM SATISFY THE STANDARDS FOR DIRECTOR INDEPENDENCE UNDER THE CURRENT LISTING RULES OF THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET. MS. KAPLOWITZ IS PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE
