Penn National Gaming Reports Covid-19 Impacted Q1 Revenues Of $1.12 Bln, Net Loss Of $608.6 Mln. PENN NATIONAL GAMING REPORTS COVID-19 IMPACTED FIRST QUARTER REVENUES OF $1.12 BILLION, NET LOSS OF $608.6 MILLION, ADJUSTED EBITDAR OF $252.3 MILLION, AND ADJUSTED EBITDA OF $154.8 MILLION.Q1 REVENUE $1.12 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.13 BILLION.IN MID-MARCH COVID-19 PANDEMIC REQUIRED TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ALL 41 PROPERTIES.FINISHED MARCH WITH $730.7 MILLION OF CASH ON BALANCE SHEET.QTRLY LOSS PER SHARE $5.26.
