Cardinal Health Says Recall Of Certain Surgical Gowns Will Have Negative Impacts. CARDINAL HEALTH INC -  EXPECT THAT RECALL OF CERTAIN SURGICAL GOWNS WILL HAVE OTHER NEGATIVE IMPACTS, WHICH COULD INCLUDE GOVERNMENT INVESTIGATIONS.CARDINAL HEALTH -EXPECT RECALL OF CERTAIN SURGICAL GOWNS COULD ALSO HAVE NEGATIVE IMPACTS INCLUDING ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS BY U.S. FDA, OTHER REGULATORS.
Cardinal Health Announces Voluntary Field Actions For Select Procedure Packs Containing Affected Surgical Gowns. CARDINAL HEALTH ANNOUNCES VOLUNTARY FIELD ACTIONS FOR SELECT PROCEDURE PACKS CONTAINING AFFECTED SURGICAL GOWNS.INITIATING ACTIONS INVOLVING 2.9 MILLION PROCEDURE PACKS MANUFACTURED BETWEEN SEPT 2018 AND JAN 2020 THAT CONTAIN AFFECTED GOWNS.PROCEDURE PACKS, ALSO KNOWN AS KITS, HAD BEEN PLACED ON VOLUNTARY HOLD AT TIME OF GOWN RECALL.INITIATING ACTIONS INVOLVING 2.9 MILLION PROCEDURE PACKS MANUFACTURED BETWEEN SEPT 2018 AND JAN 2020 THAT CONTAIN AFFECTED GOWNS.IN CONNECTION WITH THE SURGICAL GOWN-RELATED RECALLS, IN Q2 OF FISCAL YEAR 2020, EXPECT TO RECORD A $96 MILLION CHARGE.IS INCREASING ITS MANUFACTURING PRODUCTION OF SIMILAR AND REPLACEMENT PRODUCTS.IS OFFERING MORE PROTECTIVE AAMI LEVEL 4 GOWNS TO HELP BRIDGE SUPPLY GAP.MOBILIZING EMPLOYEES FROM ALL PARTS OF COMPANY TO WORK DIRECTLY WITH HEALTH CARE PROVIDERS TO REPLACE GOWNS AND PROCEDURE PACKS.ENGAGING 3RD-PARTY EXPERTS TO CONDUCT COMPREHENSIVE REVIEW OF QUALITY ASSURANCE PROCESSES, BUSINESS PRACTICES.
