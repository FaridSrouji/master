International Brotherhood of Teamsters Urges to Vote No on Cardinal Health Director Related to Opioid Crisis. As of November 4, 2019, International Brotherhood of Teamsters has urged the investors to vote against the election of J. Michael Losh to chair the Board’s audit committee in the Company’s upcoming annual stockholders meeting on November 6, 2019. Teamsters representatives also emphasized that serving on five additional corporate boards also makes Losh a poor choice given the magnitude of the challenges facing the Company.
