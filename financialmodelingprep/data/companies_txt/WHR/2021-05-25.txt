Whirlpool Corporation Brings Proactive Voice Alerts to the Home As Launch Collaborator for Google Assistant Voice Notifications. BENTON HARBOR, MICH., MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION (NYSE: WHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT IS ENHANCING ITS VOICE-COMMAND CAPABILITIES AS A LAUNCH COLLABORATOR FOR GOOGLE ASSISTANT VOICE NOTIFICATIONS, A NEW FUNCTIONALITY AVAILABLE THROUGH GOOGLE'S INTELLIGENT PERSONAL ASSISTANT SOFTWARE.
