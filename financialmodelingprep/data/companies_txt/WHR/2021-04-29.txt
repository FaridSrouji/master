Whirlpool Corporation Issues Inaugural Sustainability Bond to Support Environmental and Social Initiatives. BENTON HARBOR, MICH., APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION (NYSE: WHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE COMPLETION OF ITS $300 MILLION 10-YEAR INAUGURAL SUSTAINABILITY BOND WITH AN INTEREST COUPON OF 2.4% PER ANNUM.
