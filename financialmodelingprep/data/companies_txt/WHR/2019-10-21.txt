Whirlpool Corporation Provides Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. Whirlpool Corporation provided earnings guidance for the full year 2019. For the full-year 2019, the company decreased its GAAP earnings per diluted share guidance to $16.80 to $17.55, as additional product warranty and liability expense was partially offset by adjustments to the Embraco gain on sale calculation. The company has reaffirmed its ongoing earnings per diluted share guidance of $14.75 to $15.50, and is trending towards the high end of the range.
