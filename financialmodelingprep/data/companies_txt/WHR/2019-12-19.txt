UK Says 104,731 New Customers Have Come Forward Since June 12 After Whirlpool Washing Machines Recall Order. UK OPSS: :ACCORDING TO WHIRLPOOL, 104,731 NEW CUSTOMERS CAME FORWARD SINCE JUNE 12 AFTER INTENTION TO RECALL NOTICE IN THE HOUSE OF COMMONS ON 11 JUNE.ACCORDING TO WHIRLPOOL, 78,418 CASES HAVE BEEN FULLY RESOLVED AFTER INTENTION TO RECALL NOTICE FOR TUMBLE DRYERS.
