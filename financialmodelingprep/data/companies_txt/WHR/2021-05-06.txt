Whirlpool Corporation Commits to Achieving Net Zero Emissions in Plants and Operations by 2030. BENTON HARBOR, MICH., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION (NYSE: WHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY A GLOBAL COMMITMENT TO REACH A NET ZERO EMISSIONS TARGET IN ITS PLANTS AND OPERATIONS BY 2030.
