Whirlpool Says On June 26, Committed To Workforce Reduction Plan In U.S., As Part Of Co's Continued Cost Reduction Efforts. WHIRLPOOL CORP - ON JUNE 26, COMMITTED TO WORKFORCE REDUCTION PLAN IN UNITED STATES, AS PART OF CO'S CONTINUED COST REDUCTION EFFORTS.WHIRLPOOL- WORKFORCE REDUCTION PLAN INCLUDES VOLUNTARY RETIREMENT PROGRAM, INVOLUNTARY SEVERANCE ACTIONS WHICH WILL BE EFFECTIVE AS OF END OF Q2 2020.WHIRLPOOL CORP - EXPECTS ACTIONS TO BE SUBSTANTIALLY COMPLETE IN 2020.WHIRLPOOL CORP - ESTIMATES THAT IT WILL INCUR A TOTAL OF ABOUT $95 MILLION IN EMPLOYEE TERMINATION COSTS.WHIRLPOOL CORP - EXPECTS SUBSTANTIALLY ALL OF COSTS TO RESULT IN CASH EXPENDITURES IN 2020.WHIRLPOOL CORP - EXPECTS TOTAL RESTRUCTURING CHARGES OF $260 MILLION TO $280 MILLION FOR FISCAL YEAR 2020.
