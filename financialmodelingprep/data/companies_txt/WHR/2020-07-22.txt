Whirlpool Corporation Reports Resilient Second-Quarter 2020 Results, Despite Impact Of Covid-19 Crisis. ORATION REPORTS RESILIENT SECOND-QUARTER 2020 RESULTS, DESPITE IMPACT OF COVID-19 CRISIS.Q2 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.55 FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.87 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY NET SALES $4,042 MILLION VERSUS $5,186 MILLION.QTRLY ONGOING EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE $2.15.QTRLY ORGANIC NET SALES $4,191 MILLION VERSUS $4,863 MILLION.QTRLY NORTH AMERICA NET SALES $2,501 MILLION VERSUS $2,858 MILLION.EXPECTS FULL-YEAR 2020 NET SALES DECLINE OF APPROXIMATELY 10 PERCENT TO 15 PERCENT.EXPECTS FY ORGANIC NET SALES DECLINE OF 7 PERCENT TO 12 PERCENT.WHIRLPOOL - COVID-19 RESPONSE PLAN IS ON TRACK TO DELIVER OVER $500 MILLION IN COST TAKEOUT (INCLUDING RAW MATERIALS SAVINGS) IN 2020.WHIRLPOOL - QTRLY NET SALES FOR EUROPE, MIDDLE EAST AND AFRICA $836 MILLION VERSUS $1,032 MILLION.Q2 REVENUE VIEW $3.57 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
