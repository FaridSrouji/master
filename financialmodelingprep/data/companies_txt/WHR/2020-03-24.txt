Whirlpool Corp - Temporary Suspension Of Operations At Manufacturing Facilities In Italy. WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION PROVIDES UPDATE ON CURRENT BUSINESS OPERATIONS.WHIRLPOOL CORP - DECIDED TO WITHDRAW ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED GUIDANCE FOR 2020.WHIRLPOOL CORP - Q1 FINANCIAL IMPACT MAY SLIGHTLY EXCEED COMPANY'S PREVIOUS ESTIMATE OF $25 - $40 MILLION IN EARNINGS BEFORE INTEREST AND TAXES.WHIRLPOOL CORP - MAINTAINS A $3.5 BILLION REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY, UNDER WHICH IT INITIATED BORROWING OF APPROXIMATELY $2.2 BILLION ON MARCH 13.WHIRLPOOL CORP - TEMPORARY SUSPENSION OF OPERATIONS AT MANUFACTURING FACILITIES IN ITALY.WHIRLPOOL - TEMPORARY SUSPENSION OF OPERATIONS AT MANUFACTURING, WAREHOUSING FACILITIES IN INDIA WHILE CHINA PRODUCTION NOW BACK TO NEAR FULL CAPACITY.
