KinderCare Education and Whirlpool Open New Onsite Childcare Center. Whirlpool has partnered with KinderCare Education to open The Eddy, its first onsite childcare center. The center features an indoor terrarium in the lobby, an indoor multipurpose room, outdoor playgrounds with a soccer field, a whimsical playhouse, tricycle paths, a tree house, and an outdoor stage for plays and moments of make-believe. And because the center is onsite, Whirlpool employees can visit their children anytime, drop into classes, join special outings and events, and even nurse.
