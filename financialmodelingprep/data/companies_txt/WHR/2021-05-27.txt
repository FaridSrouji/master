Whirlpool Corporation Announces $15M Investment in Tulsa, Oklahoma Factory to Drive Job Creation and Increased Production Capabilities. BENTON HARBOR, MICH., MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WHIRLPOOL CORPORATION (NYSE: WHR) ANNOUNCED TODAY IT IS INVESTING $15 MILLION INTO ITS FACTORY IN TULSA, OKLAHOMA AS PART OF THE COMPANY'S ONGOING EFFORTS TO FURTHER STRENGTHEN ITS U.S. MANUFACTURING CAPABILITIES AND BRING EVEN MORE INNOVATION, TOP RANKING CONSUMER PRODUCTS AND HIGH-QUALITY JOBS TO THE REGION.
