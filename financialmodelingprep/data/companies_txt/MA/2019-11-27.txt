PayPal and Mastercard Extend Instant Transfer to International Markets. PayPal announced that it is extending its Instant Transfer solution to Singapore and a number of European countries as part of its long-standing partnership with Mastercard. Through Mastercard Send, PayPal users can transfer funds from their PayPal accounts to eligible Mastercard cards, typically within seconds. PayPal will initially launch Instant Transfer with Mastercard Send in Singapore, followed by multiple European markets.
