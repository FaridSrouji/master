Mastercard Pledges Net Zero Emissions; Innovates for Collective Climate Action. PURCHASE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MASTERCARD IS FURTHERING ITS COMMITMENT TO CREATE A MORE SUSTAINABLE AND INCLUSIVE DIGITAL ECONOMY, WITH A PLEDGE TO REACH NET ZERO EMISSIONS BY 2050.
