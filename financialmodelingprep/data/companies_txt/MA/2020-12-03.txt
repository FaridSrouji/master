Mastercard Names Craig Vosburg Chief Product Officer; Promotes Linda Kirkpatrick to President, North America. PURCHASE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MASTERCARD TODAY APPOINTED CRAIG VOSBURG AS CHIEF PRODUCT OFFICER AND NAMED LINDA KIRKPATRICK AS PRESIDENT, NORTH AMERICA, EFFECTIVE JAN. 1, 2021.
