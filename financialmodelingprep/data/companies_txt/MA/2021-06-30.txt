Mastercard Invites Consumers Across the U.S. To Join A 60 Million Person “Stand Up” in Support of Stand Up To Cancer. PURCHASE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MASTERCARD CALLS ON CONSUMERS TO PARTICIPATE IN A 60 MILLION PERSON “STAND UP” BY TAKING AN ACTION WITH PURPOSE, IN SUPPORT OF STAND UP TO CANCER.
