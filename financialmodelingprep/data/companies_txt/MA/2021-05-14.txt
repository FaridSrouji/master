Mastercard and National Women's Soccer League Announce Multi-Year Partnership Centered on Elevating Visibility for the Sport and Fan Engagement. PURCHASE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MASTERCARD IS JOINING THE NATIONAL WOMEN'S SOCCER LEAGUE AS AN OFFICIAL PARTNER, STRENGTHENING THE BRAND'S COMMITMENT TO WOMEN'S SOCCER.
