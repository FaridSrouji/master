Wabtec's All-Battery Locomotive, FLXdrive, Lowers Freight Train's Fuel Consumption by More Than 11 Percent in California Pilot. IT IS THE EQUIVALENT OF OVER 6,200 GALLONS OF DIESEL FUEL SAVED AND APPROXIMATELY 69 TONS OF CO2 EMISSIONS REDUCED.
