Wabtec Begins Operations at Neighborhood 91 Additive Hub. PITTSBURGH, JUNE 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WABTEC CORPORATION (NYSE: WAB) CELEBRATED TODAY THE START OF OPERATIONS AT NEIGHBORHOOD 91, THE ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING PRODUCTION CAMPUS AT PITTSBURGH INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT'S INNOVATION CAMPUS. THE NEW 11,000-SQ-FT FACILITY BUILDS UPON WABTEC'S EFFORTS TO DRIVE INNOVATION IN THE RAIL INDUSTRY THROUGH ITS GROWING ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING CAPABILITIES.
