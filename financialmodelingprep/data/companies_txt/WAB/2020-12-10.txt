Wabtec Appoints Lee Banks to Board of Directors. PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WABTEC CORPORATION (NYSE: WAB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE APPOINTMENT OF LEE BANKS TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. BANKS, 57, IS PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER FOR PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION, A FORTUNE 500 GLOBAL MANUFACTURER OF MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES AND SYSTEMS, PROVIDING PRECISION-ENGINEERED SOLUTIONS FOR A WIDE VARIETY OF MOBILE, INDUSTRIAL AND AEROSPACE MARKETS.
