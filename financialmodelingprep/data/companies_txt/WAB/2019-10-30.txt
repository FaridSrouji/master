Wabtec Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Wabtec Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,001.7 million compared to USD 1,077.8 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 169.1 million compared to USD 125.2 million a year ago. Net income was USD 91.1 million compared to USD 87.7 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.48 compared to USD 0.91 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 5,831.6 million compared to USD 3,245.7 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 437 million compared to USD 380 million a year ago. Net income was USD 191 million compared to USD 260.6 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.17 compared to USD 2.71 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.11 compared to USD 2.7 a year ago.
