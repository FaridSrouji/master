Snap-On Incorporated Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 28, 2019. Snap-on Incorporated announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 28, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 901.8 million compared to USD 898.1 million a year ago. Net income was USD 164.6 million compared to USD 163.2 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.99 compared to USD 2.9 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.96 compared to USD 2.85 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 2,774.8 million compared to USD 2,788.2 million a year ago. Net income was USD 522.9 million compared to USD 504.9 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 9.47 compared to USD 8.95 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 9.34 compared to USD 8.78 a year ago.
