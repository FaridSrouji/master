Snap-On Announces Third Quarter 2019 Results. SNAP-ON ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.96.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $2.94 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q3 SALES $901.8 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $909.6 MILLION.QTRLY ORGANIC NET SALES UP 1.4%.QTRLY ORGANIC SALES INCREASE 1.4%.EXPECTS THAT CAPITAL EXPENDITURES IN 2019 WILL BE IN A RANGE OF $95 MILLION TO $105 MILLION.
