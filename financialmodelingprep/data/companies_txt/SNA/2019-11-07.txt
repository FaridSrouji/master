Snap-On Incorporated Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 10, 2019. Snap-on Incorporated announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly common stock dividend of $1.08 per share payable December 10, 2019 to shareholders of record on November 20, 2019. This represents an increase of $0.13 per share, or 13.7%, over the company's previous quarterly dividend of $0.95 per share.
