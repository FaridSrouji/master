Northern Trust Marks 20 Years in Ireland in 2020. DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) THIS YEAR MARKS 20 YEARS SINCE IT ESTABLISHED A PRESENCE IN IRELAND AND WAS TODAY RECOGNIZED BY TÁNAISTE AND MINISTER FOR ENTERPRISE, TRADE AND EMPLOYMENT, LEO VARADKAR TD, AND IDA IRELAND FOR ITS CONTRIBUTION TO THE IRISH FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY. LEADING FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, NORTHERN TRUST IS THE THIRD LARGEST PROVIDER OF FUND ADMINISTRATION AND CUSTODY SERVICES IN IRELAND (SOURCE: MONTEREY INSIGHT IRELAND FUND REPORT, 24 FEBRUA
