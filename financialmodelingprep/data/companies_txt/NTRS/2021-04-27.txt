MIAMI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST, A LEADING PROVIDER OF FINANCIAL SERVICES FOR INSTITUTIONS AND AFFLUENT INDIVIDUALS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT LUIS BOULLON HAS JOINED THE COMPANY AS A BANKING RELATIONSHIP ADVISOR BASED IN MIAMI, FLORIDA.
