Northern Trust Appoints Jennifer Immel as Regional Wealth Advisor for West Florida. Northern Trust Corporation announced that it has appointed Jennifer Immel as regional wealth advisor for West Florida. In her new role, Immel will work with client service teams to provide wealth planning solutions for ultra-high net worth clients across the region. She will be based in the Naples Parkshore office, Florida. Immel, who has over 30 years of wealth planning experience, has previously served as director of Wealth Planning for the state of Florida for PNC Wealth Management and prior to that as its senior wealth planner.
