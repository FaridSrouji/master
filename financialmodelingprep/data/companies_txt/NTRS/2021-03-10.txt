Northern Trust Wins 18 New Asset Servicing Mandates with UK Pension Funds in 2020. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) HAS BEEN AWARDED MANDATES TO SUPPORT 18 NEW UK PENSION FUND CLIENTS WITH ASSETS UNDER CUSTODY OF ALMOST US$27 BILLION. THESE MANDATES INCLUDE COMPREHENSIVE SUPPORT FOR PENSIONS' INCREASING FOCUS ON DATA MANAGEMENT AND THE DELIVERY OF REPORTING AND ANALYTICS TO SUPPORT OVERSIGHT OF THEIR TRADITIONAL AND ALTERNATIVE INVESTMENTS.
