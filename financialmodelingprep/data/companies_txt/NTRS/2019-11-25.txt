Northern Trust Expands into Philadelphia; Announces Key Hires. Northern Trust announced that Michelle Hong and Melissa Novak, CFA, will co-head the firm’s local team and newly opened office in Philadelphia, providing wealth management services for clients in the Pennsylvania area. Both Hong and Novak will report to Mid-Atlantic Region President Brett Rees. Northern Trust opened its Philadelphia location at 2400 Market Street in October 2019. The expansion demonstrates the firm’s commitment to the market where it has served clients for many decades, and builds on its strong presence in the Mid-Atlantic region. Hong joins from Brown Brothers Harriman, where she managed assets for more than 200 clients as a Senior Vice President of Private Banking. Prior to Brown Brothers Harriman, Hong was an associate at Morgan, Lewis & Bockius, where she counseled clients on state, federal, and international tax issues, estate planning, philanthropic giving, and managing private foundations. Novak will be a Senior Vice President at Northern Trust and on the Mid-Atlantic Investment Leadership Team. She joins from Hawthorn, PNC Family Wealth®, where she was Senior Vice President and Senior Investment Advisor, managing in excess of a billion dollars for a select number of families and individuals.
