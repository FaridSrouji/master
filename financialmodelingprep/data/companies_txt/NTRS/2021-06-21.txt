Northern Trust Appointed by US$45bn Manager Fundsmith to Provide Asset Servicing Solutions to its Luxembourg-Domiciled Funds. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) TODAY ANNOUNCES IT HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO PROVIDE FUND ACCOUNTING, TRANSFER AGENCY AND GLOBAL CUSTODY SERVICES TO FUNDSMITH'S LUXEMBOURG-DOMICILED UMBRELLA, FUNDSMITH SICAV. HEADQUARTERED IN LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM (UK) WITH OFFICES IN MAURITIUS AND CONNECTICUT, USA, FUNDSMITH HAS MORE THAN £33 BILLION (APPROXIMATELY US$45BN) OF ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT (AS AT 31 DECEMBER 2020) ON BEHALF OF GLOBAL INSTITUTIONS AND INVESTORS.
