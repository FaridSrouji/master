Northern Trust Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Northern Trust Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced net interest income was USD 417.7 million compared to USD 408.2 million a year ago. Net income was USD 384.6 million compared to USD 374.5 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.7 compared to USD 1.59 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.69 compared to USD 1.58 a year ago. For the nine months, net interest income was USD 1,257.1 million compared to USD 1,205.5 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,121.1 million compared to USD 1,146.5 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 4.95 compared to USD 4.87 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.92 compared to USD 4.84 a year ago.
