Northern Trust Wealth Management Appoints Steph Wagner as Senior Wealth Strategist and Director of Women & Wealth, Based in Austin. Northern Trust Wealth Management has appointed Steph Wagner as a Senior Wealth Strategist and Director of Women & Wealth, based in Austin. In addition to advising high-net-worth families in central Texas on investment, banking and wealth management strategies, Wagner will be responsible for leading the firm’s advisory practice around women in transition. She most recently served as the Managing Director and Co-Founder of Sendero Women and Wealth, a division of the registered investment advisory firm based in central Texas. Prior to that, she built a national consulting practice as a financial strategist for high-net-worth women in transition and was the Founder & CEO of Women Wealthy Wise in San Diego.
