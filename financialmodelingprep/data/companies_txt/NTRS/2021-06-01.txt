Northern Trust Corporation to Speak at the Morgan Stanley U.S. Financials Conference on June 16th. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST CORPORATION ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICE, MICHAEL G. O'GRADY, AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, JASON J. TYLER, WILL SPEAK AT THE MORGAN STANLEY U.S. FINANCIALS CONFERENCE VIA WEBCAST ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 16, 2021 AT 11:00 A.M. (ET). THE LIVE AUDIO WEBCAST OF THE PRESENTATION CAN BE ACCESSED VIA NORTHERN TRUST'S WEBSITE UNDER THE EVENTS SECTION. Northern Trust Names Angelo Calvitto Head of Asia-Pacific. MELBOURNE, AUSTRALIA & SINGAPORE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NORTHERN TRUST (NASDAQ: NTRS) TODAY ANNOUNCES IT HAS NAMED ANGELO (ANGE) CALVITTO TO LEAD ITS ASSET SERVICING BUSINESS ACROSS THE ASIA-PACIFIC REGION. CALVITTO WHO WAS MOST RECENTLY COUNTRY HEAD FOR NORTHERN TRUST IN AUSTRALIA WILL SUCCEED WILLIAM MAK WHO IS RETIRING FROM THE COMPANY AFTER 11 YEARS.
