McKesson Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on January 2, 2020. The Board of Directors of McKesson Corporation declared a regular dividend of 41 cents per share of common stock. The dividend will be payable on January 2, 2020, to stockholders of record on December 2, 2019.
