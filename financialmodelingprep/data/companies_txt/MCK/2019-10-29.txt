Mckesson Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Second Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. McKesson Corporation announced earnings results for the second quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the second quarter, the company announced sales was USD 57,616 million compared to USD 53,075 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 626 million compared to USD 689 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 730 million compared to net income of USD 499 million a year ago. Diluted loss per share was USD 3.99 compared to diluted earnings per share of USD 2.51 a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 3.99 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 2.52 a year ago. For the half year, sales was USD 113,344 million compared to USD 105,682 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,260 million compared to USD 772 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 307 million compared to net income of USD 361 million a year ago. Diluted loss per share from continuing operations was USD 1.62 compared to diluted earnings per share from continuing operations of USD 1.79 a year ago. Diluted loss per share was USD 1.65 compared to diluted earnings per share of USD 1.8 a year ago. Basic loss per share from continuing operations was USD 1.62 compared to basic earnings per share from continuing operations of USD 1.8 a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 1.65 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 1.81 a year ago.
