W. R. Berkley Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. W. R. Berkley Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 1,965.716 million compared to USD 1,937.902 million a year ago. Net income was USD 165.208 million compared to USD 161.920 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.87 compared to USD 0.85 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.85 compared to USD 0.84 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 5,926.122 million compared to USD 5,740.066 million a year ago. Net income was USD 562.638 million compared to USD 508.392 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.95 compared to USD 2.68 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.91 compared to USD 2.64 a year ago.
