W. R. Berkley Corporation Names David Brosnan Chief Executive Officer of W. R. Berkley Syndicate Management Limited. GREENWICH, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--W. R. BERKLEY CORPORATION (NYSE: WRB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF DAVID BROSNAN AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF W. R. BERKLEY SYNDICATE MANAGEMENT LIMITED, THE LLOYD'S MANAGING AGENT FOR W. R. BERKLEY SYNDICATE 1967. HE SUCCEEDS ALASTAIR BLADES, WHO WILL CONTINUE IN HIS ROLE AS CHIEF UNDERWRITING OFFICER. THE APPOINTMENT IS SUBJECT TO REQUIRED REGULATORY APPROVALS, INCLUDING LLOYD'S. MR. BROSNAN HAS 35 YEARS OF INSURANCE INDUSTRY EXPERIENCE, HAVING SERVED
