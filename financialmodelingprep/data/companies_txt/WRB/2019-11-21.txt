W. R. Berkley Corporation Combines the Strengths of BerkleyMed and Berkley Healthcare; Announces Executive Appointments. W. R. Berkley Corporation announced the combination of its two healthcare–centric operating units, BerkleyMed and Berkley Healthcare, into a single operating unit under the name Berkley Healthcare. The newly combined operating unit will provide a broader and more cohesive array of products and services to the continuum of healthcare providers. Gregg A. Piltch has been appointed president and Collin J. Suttie has been named chairman of Berkley Healthcare. The appointments are effective immediately.
