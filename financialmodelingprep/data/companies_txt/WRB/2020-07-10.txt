BERKLEY CORP - Q2 LOSS ESTIMATE INCLUDES $85 MILLION FOR COVID-19 RELATED LOSSES, $20 MILLION FOR LOSSES RELATED TO CIVIL UNREST.W. R. BERKLEY CORP - ANTICIPATES NET PREMIUMS WRITTEN IN Q2 OF 2020 WILL BE COMPARABLE TO Q2 OF 2019.W. R.
