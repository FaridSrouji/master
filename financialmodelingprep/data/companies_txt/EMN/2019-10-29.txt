Eastman Chemical Company Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Eastman Chemical Company announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,325 million compared to USD 2,547 million a year ago. Net income was USD 266 million compared to USD 412 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 1.95 compared to USD 2.93 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 1.93 compared to USD 2.89 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 7,068 million compared to USD 7,775 million a year ago. Net income was USD 733 million compared to USD 1,046 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 5.31 compared to USD 7.38 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 5.27 compared to USD 7.28 a year ago.
