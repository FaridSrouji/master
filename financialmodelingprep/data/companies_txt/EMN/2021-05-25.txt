Eastman expands capacity to produce Naia™ cellulosic yarn. BARCELONA, SPAIN, MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO SERVE RAPIDLY GROWING CUSTOMER DEMAND, GLOBAL SPECIALTY MATERIALS PROVIDER EASTMAN (NYSE: EMN) IS INCREASING ITS CAPACITY TO PRODUCE NAIA™ FILAMENT YARN AT ITS BARCELONA, S.L., SITE BY 30% BY MID-2021 AND MORE THAN 50% BY THE END OF 2022.
