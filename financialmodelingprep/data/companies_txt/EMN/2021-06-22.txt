Eastman expands its portfolio of sustainability offerings with Cristal™ One recyclable resins for cosmetics. KINGSPORT, TENN., JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, EASTMAN (NYSE: EMN) ANNOUNCES THE LAUNCH OF EASTMAN CRISTAL™ ONE, A PORTFOLIO OF RESIN IDENTIFICATION CODE 1 (RIC1) RESINS ENGINEERED TO MEET THE EXACTING DEMANDS OF THICK-WALLED PACKAGING FOR LUXURY COSMETICS.
