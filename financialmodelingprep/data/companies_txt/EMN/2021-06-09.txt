One Rock Capital Partners to Acquire Eastman Chemical's Tire Additives Business. NEW YORK, JUNE 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ONE ROCK CAPITAL PARTNERS, LLC ("ONE ROCK") TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ONE OF ITS AFFILIATES HAS ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE THE TIRE ADDITIVES BUSINESS OF EASTMAN CHEMICAL COMPANY ("EASTMAN"; NYSE: EMN). EASTMAN'S TIRE ADDITIVES BUSINESS IS A GLOBAL LEADER IN CRITICAL SPECIALTY CHEMICALS FOR THE TIRE INDUSTRY WITH AN UNMATCHED ASSET FOOTPRINT AND SUPERIOR TECHNICAL SERVICES.
Eastman to Sell Tire Additives Business Product Lines. KINGSPORT, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EASTMAN WILL SELL THE RUBBER ADDITIVES AND OTHER PRODUCT LINES OF ITS GLOBAL TIRE ADDITIVES BUSINESS TO AN AFFILIATE OF ONE ROCK CAPITAL PARTNERS.
