AmerisourceBergen Reaches Agreement with U. S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention to Support Independent Retail Pharmacies' Access to COVID-19 Vaccine. VALLEY FORGE, Pa.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AmerisourceBergen has reached an agreement with the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) to include Good Neighbor Pharmacy and Elevate Provider Network members in the Federal Pharmacy Partnership Strategy for COVID-19 Vaccination. To support access, AmerisourceBergen will serve as a network administrator and place orders with the CDC on behalf of qualified and eligible Good Neighbor Pharmacy and Elevate Provider Network members.
