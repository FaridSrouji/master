AmerisourceBergen Announces Quarterly Dividend. VALLEY FORGE, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERISOURCEBERGEN CORPORATION (NYSE: ABC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.44 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE MARCH 1, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON FEBRUARY 12, 2021. ABOUT AMERISOURCEBERGEN AMERISOURCEBERGEN PROVIDES PHARMACEUTICAL PRODUCTS, VALUE-DRIVING SERVICES AND BUSINESS SOLUTIONS THAT IMPROVE ACCESS TO CARE.
