AmerisourceBergen Prices $1.525 Billion 0.737% Senior Notes Due 2023 and $1.0 Billion 2.700% Senior Notes Due 2031. VALLEY FORGE, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERISOURCEBERGEN CORPORATION (NYSE: ABC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT PRICED $1.525 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 0.737% SENIOR NOTES DUE MARCH 15, 2023 (THE “2023 NOTES”) AND $1.0 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 2.700% SENIOR NOTES DUE MARCH 15, 2031 (THE “2031 NOTES” AND, TOGETHER WITH THE 2023 NOTES, THE “NOTES”), IN AN UNDERWRITTEN REGISTERED PUBLIC OFFERING. THE OFFERING IS BEING MADE PURSUANT TO AN EFFECTIVE SHELF REGISTRATION STATEMEN
