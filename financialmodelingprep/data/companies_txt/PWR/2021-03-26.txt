Quanta Services Announces Quarterly Cash Dividend. HOUSTON, MARCH 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUANTA SERVICES, INC. (NYSE: PWR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND TO STOCKHOLDERS OF $0.06 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON APRIL 15, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 6, 2021.
