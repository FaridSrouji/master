Quanta Services, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. Quanta Services, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the full year 2019. Due to increased visibility and the sustained high level of infrastructure investment across Quanta's end markets, Quanta is increasing its 2019 full-year revenue expectations to approximately $12.0 billion and now expects net income attributable to common stock to range between $367 million and $385 million, diluted earnings per share attributable to common stock to range between $2.49 and $2.62.
