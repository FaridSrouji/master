AM Best Assigns Credit Ratings to Quanta Insurance Company, Inc.. OLDWICK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #INSURANCE--AM BEST HAS ASSIGNED A FINANCIAL STRENGTH RATING OF A- (EXCELLENT) AND A LONG-TERM ISSUER CREDIT RATING OF “A-” (EXCELLENT) TO QUANTA INSURANCE COMPANY, INC. (QIC) (HOUSTON, TX). THE OUTLOOK ASSIGNED TO THESE CREDIT RATINGS (RATINGS) IS STABLE. THE RATINGS REFLECT QIC'S BALANCE SHEET STRENGTH, WHICH AM BEST ASSESSES AS VERY STRONG, AS WELL AS ITS ADEQUATE OPERATING PERFORMANCE, NEUTRAL BUSINESS PROFILE AND APPROPRIATE ENTERPRISE RISK MANAGEMENT.
