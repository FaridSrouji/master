Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings Ltd. Announces Full Exercise of Greenshoe Option for Ordinary Shares. MIAMI, MARCH 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NORWEGIAN CRUISE LINE HOLDINGS LTD. (NYSE: NCLH) (THE “COMPANY”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT, IN CONNECTION WITH ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 47,577,947 ORDINARY SHARES OF THE COMPANY, THE UNDERWRITER HAS FULLY EXERCISED ITS OPTION TO PURCHASE AN ADDITIONAL 5,000,000 ORDINARY SHARES (THE “OPTION SHARES”) AT A PRICE TO THE PUBLIC OF $30.00 PER SHARE. THE ISSUANCE AND SALE OF THE OPTION SHARES CLOSED TODAY. THE COMPANY HAS ISSUED AN AGGREGATE AMOUNT OF 52,577,947 ORDINARY SHARES IN THE OFFERING, INCLUDING THE OPTION SHARES.
