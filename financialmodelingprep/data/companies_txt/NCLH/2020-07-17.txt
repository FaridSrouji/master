Ncl Corporation Ltd. Announces Upsizing And Pricing Of $750,000,000 Of Senior Secured Notes And $400,000,000 Of Exchangeable Notes. NCL CORPORATION LTD. ANNOUNCES UPSIZING AND PRICING OF $750,000,000 OF SENIOR SECURED NOTES AND $400,000,000 OF EXCHANGEABLE NOTES.NCL CORP- PRICED $750 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 10.250% SENIOR SECURED NOTES DUE 2026.NCL CORP- AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SECURED NOTES TO BE ISSUED WAS INCREASED TO $750 MILLION FROM PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED $675 MILLION.NCL CORP- NCLC ALSO PRICED $400 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.375% EXCHANGEABLE SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025.NCL CORP- AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF EXCHANGEABLE NOTES TO BE ISSUED WAS INCREASED TO $400 MILLION FROM PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED $250 MILLION.
