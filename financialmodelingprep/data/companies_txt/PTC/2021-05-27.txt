New Vuforia Instruct Offering to Streamline Creation of 3D CAD-Based Work Instructions. BOSTON, MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FROM THE STAGE OF THE VUFORIA® LIVE VIRTUAL EVENT, PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF THE NEW VUFORIA INSTRUCT ™ OUT-OF-THE-BOX OFFERING FROM THE VUFORIA ENTERPRISE AUGMENTED REALITY (AR) SUITE. THE SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SAAS)-BASED OFFERING, AVAILABLE ON THE PTC ATLAS ™ PLATFORM, EMPOWERS ORIGINAL EQUIPMENT MANUFACTURERS TO EXTEND THE VALUE OF THE DIGITAL THREAD ALL THE WAY TO THEIR FRONT-LINE WORKERS.
