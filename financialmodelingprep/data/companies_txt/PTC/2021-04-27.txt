PTC Announces Transition in Chief Strategy Officer Role. BOSTON, APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CATHERINE KNIKER HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO EVP AND CHIEF STRATEGY OFFICER, REPLACING KATHLEEN MITFORD WHO WILL LEAVE PTC TO JOIN MICROSOFT AS CORPORATE VICE PRESIDENT, AZURE MARKETING. "JOINING MICROSOFT IS A GREAT CAREER OPPORTUNITY FOR KATHLEEN.
