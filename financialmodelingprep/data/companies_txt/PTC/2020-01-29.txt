PTC Inc Launches Private Offering Of $750 Mln Of Senior Notes. PTC LAUNCHES PRIVATE OFFERING OF $750 MILLION OF SENIOR NOTES.PTC - INTENDS TO OFFER $750 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SENIOR NOTES DUE 2025 AND SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 IN A PRIVATE OFFERING.PTC INC - EXPECTS TO USE $530 MILLION OF NET PROCEEDS OF OFFERING OF NOTES TO REDEEM ALL $500 MILLION OF ITS OUTSTANDING 6.000% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2024.PTC - TO USE REMAINING NET PROCEEDS TO REPAY $210 MILLION OF OUTSTANDING LOANS UNDER ITS SENIOR REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY AT CLOSING OF OFFERING OF NOTES.PTC INC - TO INVEST $530 MILLION OF NET PROCEEDS TEMPORARILY IN MONEY MARKET FUNDS, BANK DEPOSIT ACCOUNTS, INVESTMENT GRADE SECURITIES, OTHERS.
