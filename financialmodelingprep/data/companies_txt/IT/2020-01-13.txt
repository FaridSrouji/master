Gartner Says Worldwide PC Shipments Grew 2.3% In 4Q19 And 0.6% For The Year. GARTNER SAYS WORLDWIDE PC SHIPMENTS GREW 2.3% IN 4Q19 AND 0.6% FOR THE YEAR.GARTNER SAYS WORLDWIDE PC SHIPMENTS TOTALED 70.6 MILLION UNITS IN Q4 OF 2019, A 2.3% INCREASE.GARTNER - ONGOING INTEL CPU SHORTAGE, WHICH BEGAN MID-LAST YEAR, BECAME MAJOR ISSUE AGAIN ON PC DELIVERY TO ENTERPRISE CUSTOMERS BY TOP 3 VENDORS.GARTNER - LOOKING AHEAD, GARTNER PREDICTS A CONTINUOUS DECLINE IN CONSUMER PC MARKET OVER NEXT FIVE YEARS.GARTNER SAYS U.S. PC MARKET GREW 4.6% IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2019.GARTNER - EXPECT PC MARKET GROWTH TO CONTINUE THROUGH THIS YEAR EVEN AFTER WINDOWS 7 SUPPORT COMES TO AN END THIS MONTH.GARTNER - RELATIVELY STABLE ECONOMIC CONDITIONS IN U.S. COMPARED WITH PREVIOUS QTRS, END OF WINDOWS 7 SUPPORT, LOOSENED UP SPENDING ON PCS IN Q4.
