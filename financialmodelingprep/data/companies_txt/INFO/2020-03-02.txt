IHS Markit Announces Cancellation Of Customer Events In Q2 2020. IHS MARKIT ANNOUNCES CANCELLATION OF CUSTOMER EVENTS IN SECOND QUARTER 2020.IHS MARKIT LTD- IHS MARKIT CURRENTLY ESTIMATES ADVERSE FINANCIAL IMPACT OF CANCELLING EVENTS TO BE ABOUT $50 MILLION OF NON-RECURRING REVENUE.IHS MARKIT LTD - CURRENTLY ESTIMATES ADVERSE FINANCIAL IMPACT OF CANCELLING EVENTS TO BE ABOUT $0.09 PER DILUTED SHARE OF ADJUSTED EPS.IHS MARKIT LTD - EXPECTS TO HOLD CANCELLED EVENTS IN 2021 AND BEYOND.
IHS Markit Says Ceraweek By IHS Markit 2020 Is Canceled. CERAWEEK BY IHS MARKIT 2020 IS CANCELED.IHS MARKIT LTD - IN LIGHT OF DEVELOPING CIRCUMSTANCES, IHS MARKIT IS CANCELLING CERAWEEK 2020 SCHEDULED TO TAKE PLACE IN HOUSTON, MARCH 9-13.IHS MARKIT LTD- WE WILL CONTINUE WITH CERAWEEK 2021 IN HOUSTON ON MARCH 1-5, 2021.
