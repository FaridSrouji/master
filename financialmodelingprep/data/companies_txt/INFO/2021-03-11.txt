S&P Global Shareholders Overwhelmingly Approve Proposed Merger with IHS Markit. NEW YORK, MARCH 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL (NYSE: SPGI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS SHAREHOLDERS OVERWHELMINGLY VOTED TO APPROVE THE COMPANY'S PROPOSED TRANSACTION WITH IHS MARKIT (NYSE: INFO) AT A SPECIAL MEETING OF THE COMPANY'S SHAREHOLDERS. APPROXIMATELY 99% OF VOTES CAST WERE IN FAVOR OF THE TRANSACTION.
