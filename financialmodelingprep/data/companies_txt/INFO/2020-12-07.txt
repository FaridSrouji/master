Thanksgiving Week Gasoline Consumption in the United States Lowest Since 1997, Exclusive OPIS by IHS Markit Survey Finds. GAITHERSBURG, MD.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THANKSGIVING WEEK GASOLINE CONSUMPTION IN THE UNITED STATES WAS THE LOWEST SINCE 1997, EXCLUSIVE OPIS BY IHS MARKIT SURVEY FINDS.
