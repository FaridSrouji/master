IHS Markit Ltd. (NYSE:INFO) announces an Equity Buyback for $2,500 million worth of its shares.. IHS Markit Ltd. (NYSE:INFO) ) announces a share repurchase program. Under the program, the company will repurchase up to $2,500 million of its shares. The shares repurchases will be held in treasury or cancelled. The shares repurchases will be funded from existing cash, cash equivalents, marketable securities and future cash flows, or through the incurrence of short- or long-term indebtedness. The program is valid till November 30, 2021.
