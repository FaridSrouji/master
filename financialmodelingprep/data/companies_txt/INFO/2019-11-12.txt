IHS Markit - CFO Todd Hyatt Plans To Retire At End Of 2020. IHS MARKIT LTD - ON NOVEMBER 12, TODD HYATT, CFO ANNOUNCED THAT HE PLANS TO RETIRE AT END OF 2020.IHS MARKIT LTD - COMPANY WILL APPOINT JONATHAN GEAR TO BE ITS CFO ONCE TODD HYATT LEAVES ROLE.IHS MARKIT LTD - HYATT PLANS TO REMAIN WITH CO UNTIL HIS RETIREMENT TO ASSIST WITH TRANSITION.
IHS Markit Ltd. Provides Revenue Guidance for the Year Ending November 30, 2019 and November 30, 2020. IHS Markit Ltd. provided revenue guidance for the year ending November 30, 2019 and November 30, 2020. For the year ending November 30, 2019, the company now expects revenue in a range of $4.40 billion to $4.42 billion, including total organic growth of 5% to 6% including Ipreo for the 4 month stub period.For the year ending November 30, 2020, the company expects revenue in a range of $4.52 billion to $4.59 billion, with total organic growth of 5% to 6%.
