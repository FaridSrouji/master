Verisign, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. VeriSign, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 308.421 million compared to USD 305.777 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 205.616 million compared to USD 194.997 million a year ago. Net income was USD 153.913 million compared to USD 137.680 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.3 compared to USD 1.13 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 921.118 million compared to USD 907.517 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 607.561 million compared to USD 573.426 million a year ago. Net income was USD 463.974 million compared to USD 400.294 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.9 compared to USD 3.6 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.89 compared to USD 3.25 a year ago.
