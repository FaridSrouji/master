Washington Supreme Court Upholds $81.5 Million Verdict in Mesothelioma Case. OLYMPIA, WASH., JULY 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE SUPREME COURT OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON HAS OVERTURNED AN APPELLATE COURT RULING AND REINSTATED A $81.5 MILLION VERDICT IN A WRONGFUL DEATH CLAIM BROUGHT BY THE FAMILY OF A DECEASED AUTO MECHANIC.
