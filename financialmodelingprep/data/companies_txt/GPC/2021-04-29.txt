Genuine Parts Company Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend. ATLANTA, APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GENUINE PARTS COMPANY (NYSE: GPC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF EIGHTY-ONE AND ONE-HALF CENTS ($0.815) PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 1, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD JUNE 4, 2021.
