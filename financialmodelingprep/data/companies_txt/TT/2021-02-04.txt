Trane Technologies Increases Dividend 11% and Authorizes New $2 Billion Share Repurchase Program. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TRANE TECHNOLOGIES PLC (NYSE:TT), A GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR, DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.59 CENTS PER ORDINARY SHARE, OR $2.36 ANNUALIZED, REFLECTING AN 11% INCREASE. TRANE TECHNOLOGIES HAS PAID CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDENDS ON ITS COMMON SHARES SINCE 1919 AND ANNUAL DIVIDENDS SINCE 1910.
