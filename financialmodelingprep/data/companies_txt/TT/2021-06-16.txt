Trane Technologies' Thermo King Manufacturing Production Line is Among the First in Europe to be ISO-Certified as Carbon Neutral. SWORDS, IRELAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THERMO KING, A BRAND OF GLOBAL CLIMATE INNOVATOR TRANE TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE:TT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS STATE-OF-THE-ART THERMO KING® MANUFACTURING PRODUCTION LINE IN GALWAY, IRELAND, WAS AWARDED THE ISO 14064-1 (CARBON NEUTRALITY) CERTIFICATION FROM THE NATIONAL STANDARDS AUTHORITY OF IRELAND (NSAI), THE FIRST IN THE COUNTRY AND ONE OF THE FIRST IN EUROPE.
