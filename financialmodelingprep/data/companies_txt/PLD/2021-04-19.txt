Prologis Reports First Quarter 2021 Earnings Results. SAN FRANCISCO, APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PROLOGIS, INC. (NYSE: PLD), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LOGISTICS REAL ESTATE, REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021. NET EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE WAS $0.49 FOR THE QUARTER COMPARED WITH $0.70 FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020; THE DECREASE WAS DRIVEN BY DEBT EXTINGUISHMENT COSTS OF $187 MILLION.
