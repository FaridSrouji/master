Prologis To Acquire Liberty Property Trust For $12.6 Bln. FUTURE SYNERGIES WITH POTENTIAL TO GENERATE APPROXIMATELY $60 MILLION IN ANNUAL SAVINGS .PROLOGIS TO ACQUIRE LIBERTY PROPERTY TRUST FOR $12.6 BILLION.PROLOGIS WILL ACQUIRE LIBERTY IN AN ALL-STOCK TRANSACTION, VALUED AT APPROXIMATELY $12.6 BILLION, INCLUDING ASSUMPTION OF DEBT.BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PROLOGIS AND BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF LIBERTY HAVE EACH UNANIMOUSLY APPROVED TRANSACTION..TRANSACTION IS ANTICIPATED TO CREATE IMMEDIATE COST SYNERGIES OF APPROXIMATELY $120 MILLION.INITIALLY, TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO INCREASE ANNUAL CORE FUNDS FROM OPERATIONS* (CORE FFO) PER SHARE BY $0.10-$0.12.AFTER DEAL ANNUAL STABILIZED CORE FFO PER SHARE IS FORECASTED TO INCREASE BY AN ADDITIONAL $0.04 PER SHARE FOR A TOTAL OF $0.14-$0.16.LIBERTY SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE 0.675X OF A PROLOGIS SHARE FOR EACH LIBERTY SHARE THEY OWN.BOFA SECURITIES AND MORGAN STANLEY ARE ACTING AS FINANCIAL ADVISORS TO PROLOGIS.GOLDMAN SACHS AND CITIGROUP ARE ACTING AS FINANCIAL ADVISORS TO LIBERTY.PROLOGIS PLANS TO DISPOSE OF APPROXIMATELY $3.5 BILLION OF ASSETS ON A PRO RATA SHARE BASIS.PLANS TO DISPOSE OF ASSETS INCLUDES $2.8 BILLION OF NON-STRATEGIC LOGISTICS PROPERTIES AND $700 MILLION OF OFFICE PROPERTIES.
Liberty Property Trust, Prologis, Inc. - M&A Call. To discuss Liberty Property Trust, Leaf Holdco Company Trust and Liberty Property Limited Partnership entered into an agreement and plan of merger with Prologis, Inc., Lambda REIT Acquisition LLC, Prologis, L.P. and Lambda OP Acquisition LLC
