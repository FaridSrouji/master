Prologis, Inc. (NYSE:PLD) and Government Pension Fund Global, managed by Norges Bank Investment Management completed the acquisition of Logistics property in the London from RM Coleview 1 Ltd.. Prologis, Inc. (NYSE:PLD) and Government Pension Fund Global, managed by Norges Bank Investment Management agreed to acquire Logistics property in the London from RM Coleview 1 Ltd on October 21, 2019. Norges Bank Investment Management and Prologis each acquired 50% sake in this logistics property. Norges Bank Investment Management paid £35.1 million for its 50% stake.
