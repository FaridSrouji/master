Prologis, Inc. Declares Regular Common and Preferred Stock Dividend for the Quarter Ending December 31, 2019, Payable on December 31, 2019. The Board of Directors of Prologis, Inc. declared a regular cash dividend for the quarter ending December 31, 2019, on the following securities: A dividend of $0.53 per share of the company's common stock, payable on December 31, 2019, to common stockholders of record at the close of business on December 19, 2019; and A dividend of $1.0675 per share of the company's 8.54% Series Q Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock, payable on December 31, 2019, to Series Q stockholders of record at the close of business on December 19, 2019.
