HP Provides Information to its Shareholders. On November 8, 2019, HP Inc. announced that it is still reviewing the takeover offer of Xerox Holdings Corporation in order to determine if it is in the best interest of the Company. The Company added that its board reportedly is not sure yet whether a sale is the best option at the moment. The Company also stated that it is not in a hurry to issue an opinion on the takeover approach, Xerox Holdings would prefer to receive an answer within a week.
