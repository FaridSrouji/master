Zhang Investor Law Alerts Investors to Deadline in Securities Class Action Lawsuit Against HP Inc. – HPQ. NEW YORK, DEC. 08, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ZHANG INVESTOR LAW ANNOUNCES A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS WHO BOUGHT SHARES OF HP INC. (NYSE: HPQ)  BETWEEN NOVEMBER 6, 2015 AND JUNE 21, 2016, INCLUSIVE (THE “CLASS PERIOD”).
