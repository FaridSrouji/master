Carl Icahn Is Urging HP To Push Ahead With Takeover Talks With Xerox- Bloomberg Reporter On Twitter. :CARL ICAHN IS URGING HP TO PUSH AHEAD WITH TAKEOVER TALKS WITH XEROX
Icahn Capital Provides Information to Shareholders. On December 4, 2019, Carl Icahn urged the shareholders of HP Inc. who agree with the merger with Xerox Holdings Corp. to reach out to the Company’s directors for immediate action. Carl Icahn stated that it calls the Company’s standalone restructuring plan as ‘rearranging the deck chairs on the Titanic’, and said in a letter that the Company’s refusal could be a delaying tactic to hold the ‘lucrative’ positions of its executives. Carl Icahn added that the Company shareholders deserve the opportunity to decide for themselves whether a combination with Xerox Holdings makes sense before the idea is summarily rejected by the Company’s board.
