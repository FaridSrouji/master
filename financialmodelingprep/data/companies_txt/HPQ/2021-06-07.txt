HP Inc. Announces Sustainable Bond Framework and Inaugural Sustainability Notes Offering. PALO ALTO, CALIF., JUNE 07, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HP INC. (NYSE: HPQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS SUSTAINABLE BOND FRAMEWORK, WHICH THE COMPANY WILL USE TO ISSUE BONDS TO HELP FINANCE HP PROJECTS THAT CONTRIBUTE TO A MORE SUSTAINABLE AND JUST FUTURE.
