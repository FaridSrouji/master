HP Appoints Richard Clemmer To Board Of Directors. HP APPOINTS RICHARD CLEMMER TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS.HP INC - APPOINTMENT OF RICHARD CLEMMER TO HP'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS.HP INC - CLEMMER IS CURRENTLY CEO AND EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V..HP INC - WITH CLEMMER'S APPOINTMENT, HP BOARD HAS TEMPORARILY EXPANDED TO 13 DIRECTORS, 11 OF WHOM ARE INDEPENDENT.HP INC - HP HAS PROVIDED NOTICE TO XEROX THAT IT HAS 10 DAYS TO NOMINATE AN ADDITIONAL CANDIDATE IN CONNECTION WITH 2020 ANNUAL MEETING.
