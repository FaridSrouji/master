Icahn Capital Provides Information to Shareholders of HP. On November 21, 2019, Carl Icahn announced that its backed Xerox Holdings Corp. has threatened to launch a hostile bid to buy HP Inc. unless the 2 sides could reach an agreement by November 25, 2019, allowing both companies, to review each other's books. Carl Icahn stated that Xerox stated in the letter that the Company’s response lacks a clear path forward, adding that the Company has requested customary due diligence, which Xerox has accepted, but the Company has refused to agree to corresponding due diligence for Xerox.
