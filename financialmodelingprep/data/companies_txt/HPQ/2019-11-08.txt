HP Rejects Acquisition by Xerox Holdings. On November 9, 2019, HP Inc. announced that its Board of Directors has rejected the unsolicited proposal from Xerox Holdings Corporation to acquire the Company. The Company stated that On November 5, 2019, Xerox had prepared to offer the Company shareholders USD22 per share comprised of USD17 in cash and 0.137 Xerox shares for each Company share, for a total transaction value of approximately USD33.5 billion, assuming 1,515 million fully diluted shares outstanding and the balance sheet as of July 31, 2019.
