HP Inc. Names Bruce Broussard to Board of Directors. PALO ALTO, CALIF., JUNE 22, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HP INC. (NYSE: HPQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF BRUCE BROUSSARD TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. BROUSSARD CURRENTLY SERVES AS PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF HUMANA, ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING HEALTHCARE COMPANIES.
