HP Inc. Announces Closing of Its Inaugural $1 Billion Sustainability Notes Offering. PALO ALTO, CALIF., JUNE 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HP INC. (NYSE: HPQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE CLOSING OF ITS INAUGURAL SUSTAINABILITY NOTES ISSUANCE, TOTALING $1 BILLION, WHICH WAS PART OF A $2 BILLION OVERALL DEBT OFFERING.
