Kraft Heinz Announces Nominations of Lori Dickerson Fouché and Miguel Patricio as New Board Members. PITTSBURGH & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE KRAFT HEINZ COMPANY (NASDAQ: KHC) (“KRAFT HEINZ”) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS NOMINATED LORI DICKERSON FOUCHÉ AND KRAFT HEINZ CEO MIGUEL PATRICIO TO BE ELECTED AS DIRECTORS AT THE UPCOMING KRAFT HEINZ 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS.
