Kraft Heinz Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend Of $0.40/Share. THE KRAFT HEINZ COMPANY DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.40 PER SHARE.SETS REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.40PER SHARE.
Kraft Heinz Reports Third Quarter 2019 Results. KRAFT HEINZ REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.Q3 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.69.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.74.Q3 SALES $6.1 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $6.12 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.54 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q3 2019 FINANCIAL RESULTS THAT REFLECTED A ONE-TIME GAIN ON SALE OF COMPANY'S CANADIAN NATURAL CHEESE BUSINESS.QTRLY ORGANIC NET SALES DECREASED 1.1 PERCENT VERSUS YEAR-AGO PERIOD.QTRLY UNITED STATES NET SALES WERE $4.4 BILLION, DOWN 1.6 PERCENT VERSUS YEAR-AGO PERIOD.KRAFT HEINZ SAYS OUR THIRD-QUARTER RESULTS REMAIN BELOW OUR POTENTIAL.KRAFT HEINZ SAYS ARE MAKING GOOD PROGRESS IN IDENTIFYING & ADDRESSING ROOT CAUSES OF PAST PERFORMANCE.QTRLY RESULTS INCLUDE GAINS ON SALE OF BUSINESS OF $0.13 PER SHARE.
