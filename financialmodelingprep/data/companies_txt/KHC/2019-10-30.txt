The Kraft Heinz Company Declares A Regular Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 13, 2019. The Board of Directors of The Kraft Heinz Company declared a regular quarterly dividend of $0.40 per share of common stock payable on December 13, 2019, to stockholders of record as of November 15, 2019.
