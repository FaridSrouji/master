Kraft Mac & Cheese Developing and Testing Its First Recyclable Fiber-Based Microwavable Cup. PITTSBURGH & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--KRAFT MAC & CHEESE, A BELOVED STAPLE ON AMERICAN TABLES FOR OVER 80 YEARS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS DEVELOPING AND TESTING THE BRAND'S FIRST-EVER RECYCLABLE FIBER-BASED MICROWAVABLE CUP. UPON TESTING COMPLETION, THE BRAND WILL LAUNCH A NEW KRAFT MAC & CHEESE SHAPES VARIETY LATER IN 2021 USING THE NEW CUP AND DESIGN.
