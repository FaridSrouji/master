P3 Portable Protein Pack Launches New Snack Co-Developed with UFC Performance Institute. The Kraft Heinz Company announced P3 Portable Protein Pack® is giving athletes and fans alike a more interesting way to fuel their workouts by launching a new UFC Performance Institute co-branded snack as part of its existing line of products in partnership with UFC®, the world’s premier mixed martial arts organization. The latest P3 is designed for active consumers looking for a protein-rich snack and features rotisserie-seasoned chicken, pretzels, mozzarella cheese and dried cranberries. The newest P3 is the first of its kind to be co-developed with nutritionists from the UFC Performance Institute® and uses a 2-to-1 ratio of carbohydrates to protein for a better way to fuel workouts. Each pack contains 11 grams of protein and 25 grams of carbohydrates. The newest P3 will be available for $2.99 (SRP) at retailers nationwide.
