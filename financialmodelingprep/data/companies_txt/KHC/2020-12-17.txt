The Kraft Heinz Company Commences Exchange Offer. PITTSBURGH & CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE KRAFT HEINZ COMPANY (NASDAQ: KHC) (“KRAFT HEINZ”) AND KRAFT HEINZ FOODS COMPANY (THE “ISSUER”) COMMENCED TODAY AN OFFER TO EXCHANGE (THE “EXCHANGE OFFER”) CERTAIN OF THE ISSUER'S OUTSTANDING UNREGISTERED NOTES FOR NEW REGISTERED NOTES.
