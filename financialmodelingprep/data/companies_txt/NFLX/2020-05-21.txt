Netflix Says Asking Inactive Members To Confirm If They Want To Keep Membership. NETFLIX INC - ASKING MEMBERS WHO HAVE NOT WATCHED ANYTHING ON NETFLIX FOR A YEAR TO CONFIRM IF THEY WANT TO KEEP MEMBERSHIP.NETFLIX INC - INACTIVE ACCOUNTS REPRESENT LESS THAN HALF OF ONE PERCENT OF OVERALL MEMBER BASE & ARE ALREADY FACTORED INTO FINANCIAL GUIDANCE.NETFLIX - IF USERS DON’T CONFIRM THEY WANT TO KEEP SUBSCRIBING, CO WILL AUTOMATICALLY CANCEL SUBSCRIPTION.NETFLIX - ALSO ASKING MEMBERS WHO STOPPED WATCHING FOR MORE THAN 2 YEARS TO CONFIRM IF THEY WANT TO KEEP MEMBERSHIP.
