Occidental Announces Dividend. HOUSTON, Nov. 05, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Occidental (NYSE:OXY) said today that its Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly dividend of $0.01 per share on common stock payable on January 15, 2021, to stockholders of record as of December 10, 2020.
