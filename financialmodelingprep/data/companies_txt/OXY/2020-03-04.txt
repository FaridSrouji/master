Crestwood Announces Commercial Operations At Bucking Horse II Processing Plant. CRESTWOOD ANNOUNCES COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS AT BUCKING HORSE II PROCESSING PLANT, NEW G&P AGREEMENT WITH OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM, AND PROVIDES POWDER RIVER BASIN UPDATE.CRESTWOOD -  ENTERED GATHERING AND PROCESSING AGREEMENT TO PROVIDE WELLHEAD SERVICES FOR OCCIDENTAL'S 2020 DELINEATION PROGRAM IN POWDER RIVER BASIN.CRESTWOOD - EXPECTS MINIMAL INCREMENTAL CAPITAL REQUIREMENTS TO CONNECT NEW WELLS.
