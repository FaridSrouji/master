Crown Castle Updates Outlook For Full-Year 2020, Announces Restatement Of Financial Results. CROWN CASTLE REPORTS FULL YEAR 2019 RESULTS, UPDATES OUTLOOK FOR FULL YEAR 2020, AND ANNOUNCES RESTATEMENT OF FINANCIAL RESULTS.CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL CORP - WILL RESTATE OUR FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR YEARS ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2018 AND 2017.CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL  - WILL RESTATE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS UNAUDITED FINANCIAL INFORMATION FOR QTRLY AND YTD PERIODS IN YEAR ENDED DEC 31, 2018.CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL CORP - WILL RESTATE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR FIRST THREE QUARTERS IN YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2019.CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL CORP SEES 2020 SITE RENTAL REVENUES $5,337 MILLION TO $5,382 MILLION.CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL CORP SEES 2020 FFO $2,449 MILLION TO $2,494 MILLION.CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL CORP SEES 2020 AFFO $2,572 MILLION TO $2,617 MILLION.
