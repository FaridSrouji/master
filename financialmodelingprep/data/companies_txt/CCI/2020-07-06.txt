Elliott Management Recommends Changes At Crown Castle. ELLIOTT MANAGEMENT RECOMMENDS CHANGES AT CROWN CASTLE.ELLIOTT MANAGEMENT - RECOMMENDS ROI-FOCUSED FIBER INVESTMENT APPROACH FROM CROWN CASTLE, INCREASE IN DIVIDENDS TO $7.00/SHARE IN 2021, $8.00+ IN 2023.ELLIOTT SAYS HAS BEEN ENGAGED IN A PRIVATE DIALOGUE WITH CROWN CASTLE FOR OVER A MONTH.ELLIOTT - PROPOSED RTC PLAN WILL INCREASE FCF BY 35% WHILE STILL ALLOWING FOR $600 MILLION PER YEAR OF DISCRETIONARY FIBER CAPEX AT CROWN CASTLE.ELLIOTT - CROWN CASTLE SHOULD ADDRESS ITS EXTRAORDINARILY LONG-TENURED BOARD.ELLIOTT MANAGEMENT SAYS COLLECTIVELY OWN AN ECONOMIC INTEREST OF $1 BILLION OF CROWN CASTLE INTERNATIONAL CORP.ELLIOTT - CROWN CASTLE SHOULD ENSURE UNDERPERFORMING FIBER BUSINESS HAS APPROPRIATE MANAGEMENT SKILLSET TO DELIVER IMPROVED RESULTS.
