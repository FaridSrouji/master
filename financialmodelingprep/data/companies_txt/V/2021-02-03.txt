Visa Expands Digital Currency Roadmap with First Boulevard. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VISA (NYSE:V) TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP WITH FIRST BOULEVARD, A DIGITALLY NATIVE NEOBANK FOCUSED ON BUILDING GENERATIONAL WEALTH FOR THE BLACK COMMUNITY. FIRST BOULEVARD WILL BE FIRST TO PILOT VISA'S NEW SUITE OF CRYPTO APIS, WHICH WILL ENABLE THEIR CUSTOMERS TO PURCHASE, CUSTODY AND TRADE DIGITAL ASSETS HELD BY ANCHORAGE, A FEDERALLY CHARTERED DIGITAL ASSET BANK.
