Visa Inc Says In May, Total U.S. Payments Volume Declined 5% Year-Over-Year. VISA INC - IN MAY, TOTAL U.S. PAYMENTS VOLUME DECLINED 5% YEAR-OVER-YEAR.VISA INC - INTERNATIONAL MARKETS ARE AT VARIOUS STAGES OF REOPENING AND RECOVERY..VISA INC - RECOVERY IN INTERNATIONAL MARKETS IN WHICH WE PROCESS MAJORITY OF TRANSACTIONS LAGGED U.S. IN MAY.VISA INC - GLOBAL PROCESSED TRANSACTIONS DECLINED 12% IN MAY, A 12 PPT IMPROVEMENT OVER APRIL.VISA INC - SINCE APRIL, GLOBAL PROCESSED TRANSACTIONS MIX HAS SHIFTED TO LARGER TICKET TRANSACTIONS..VISA INC - CROSS-BORDER VOLUMES EXCLUDING INTRA-EUROPE TRANSACTIONS DECLINED 45% IN MAY, A 6 PPT IMPROVEMENT OVER APRIL.VISA INC - TRAVEL RELATED CROSS-BORDER VOLUMES (CARD PRESENT AND CARD NOT PRESENT) DECLINED 78% IN MAY.VISA SAYS CROSS-BORDER ECOMMERCE (EXCLUDING TRAVEL) CONTINUED TO GROW STRONGLY, UP 18% IN MAY - SEC FILING.VISA INC - CROSS-BORDER VOLUMES INCLUDING INTRA-EUROPE TRANSACTIONS DECLINED 35% IN MAY, AN 8 PPT IMPROVEMENT OVER APRIL..VISA INC - ACROSS MOST OF EUROPE, AS WELL AS AUSTRALIA, CANADA AND JAPAN, TRAJECTORY SO FAR IS COMPARABLE TO U.S. IN MAY.
