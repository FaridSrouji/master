Visa Appoints Michelle Gethers-Clark as Chief Diversity Officer and Head of Corporate Responsibility. SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VISA (NYSE: V) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF MICHELLE GETHERS-CLARK AS CHIEF DIVERSITY OFFICER AND HEAD OF CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY. GETHERS-CLARK WILL JOIN VISA'S EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE AND REPORT TO CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, AL KELLY. IN THIS ROLE, GETHERS-CLARK WILL LEAD VISA'S INCLUSION AND DIVERSITY, SOCIAL IMPACT AND SUSTAINABILITY EFFORTS GLOBALLY. AMONG HER RESPONSIBILITIES WILL BE ENSURING VISA'S ORGANIZATION AND CULTURE ARE TRULY INCLUSIVE
