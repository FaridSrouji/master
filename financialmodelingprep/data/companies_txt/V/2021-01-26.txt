TransferWise and Visa Announce Global Partnership Following Successful Collaboration on Cloud Technology. SAN FRANCISCO & LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #CLOUD--VISA (NYSE: V) AND TRANSFERWISE TODAY ANNOUNCED A GLOBAL PARTNERSHIP AND THE FIRST USE OF VISA CLOUD CONNECT, A NEW WAY FOR FINTECHS AND PARTNERS TO SECURELY CONNECT VISA'S GLOBAL PROCESSING NETWORK, VISANET, THROUGH THE CLOUD. VISA CLOUD CONNECT UNDERPINS A NEW GLOBAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN VISA AND TRANSFERWISE THAT WILL ENABLE THE EXPANSION OF TRANSFERWISE'S MULTI-CURRENCY DEBIT CARDS IN ASIA PACIFIC, EUROPE, LATIN AMERICA AND THE CARIBBEAN, THE MIDDLE EAS
