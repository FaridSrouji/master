One Billion Additional Touch-free Visa Payments Made as Consumers Embrace Contactless Commerce. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--VISA TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS PROCESSED ONE BILLION ADDITIONAL TOUCH-FREE PAYMENTS1, WHERE PREVIOUSLY CONSUMERS WOULD HAVE NEEDED TO ENTER THEIR PIN, AS CONSUMER CONFIDENCE IN CONTACTLESS PAYMENTS CONTINUES TO GROW. THIS MILESTONE HAS BEEN REACHED IN LESS THAN A YEAR SINCE CONTACTLESS PAYMENT LIMITS WERE INCREASED IN 29 COUNTRIES ACROSS EUROPE IN RESPONSE TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
