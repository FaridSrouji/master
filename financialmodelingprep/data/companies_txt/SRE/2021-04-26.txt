Sempra Energy Launches Exchange Offer For Publicly Owned Shares Of IEnova. SAN DIEGO, APRIL 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA ENERGY (NYSE: SRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS LAUNCHED ITS EXCHANGE OFFER TO ACQUIRE ALL THE OUTSTANDING SHARES OF IENOVA (INFRAESTRUCTURA ENERGÉTICA NOVA, S.A.B DE C.V.) NOT OWNED BY SEMPRA ENERGY.
