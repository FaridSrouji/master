Sempra Energy Provides Earnings Guidance for the Full Years of 2019 and 2020; Announces Impairment Losses for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Sempra Energy provided earnings guidance for the full years of 2019 and 2020. On a GAAP basis, the company's earnings-per-common-share (EPS) guidance range for full-year 2019 is $6.50 to $7.00.The company also issued its full-year 2020 GAAP EPS guidance range of $12.78 to $14.26.The company announced impairment losses for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, the company reported impairment losses of $43 million compared to $4 million a year ago.
