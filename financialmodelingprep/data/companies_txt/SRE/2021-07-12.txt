SoCalGas and SDG&E Recognized on Nationwide List of 100 Best Places to Work in IT. LOS ANGELES AND SAN DIEGO, JULY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) AND SAN DIEGO GAS & ELECTRIC (SDG&E) – SISTER UTILITIES IN THE SEMPRA FAMILY OF COMPANIES – HAVE EARNED A SPOT IN THE TOP 20 OF LARGE COMPANIES ON THE IDG INSIDER PRO AND COMPUTERWORLD'S 2021 LIST OF 100 BEST PLACES TO WORK IN IT. THE LIST RECOGNIZES THE 100 TOP ORGANIZATIONS IN THE U.S. THAT CREATE MEANINGFUL WORK FOR THEIR IT STAFF WHILE PROVIDING GREAT BENEFITS AND COMPENSATION.
