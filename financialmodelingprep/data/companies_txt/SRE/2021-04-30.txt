Sempra Energy Named One Of 'America's Best Employers For Diversity' By Forbes. SAN DIEGO, APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SEMPRA ENERGY (NYSE: SRE) HAS BEEN NAMED TO FORBES' "AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS FOR DIVERSITY" LIST FOR 2021, MARKING THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR THAT THE COMPANY HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED. SEMPRA ENERGY RANKED 79TH OUT OF 500 COMPANIES THIS YEAR, UP FROM 328TH LAST YEAR, AND WAS THE SECOND HIGHEST-RANKING EMPLOYER IN THE UTILITY INDUSTRY.
