LA Family Housing Communities Receive $150,000 in Energy Efficiency Upgrades Courtesy of SoCalGas Energy Savings Assistance Program. LOS ANGELES, MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS CO. (SOCALGAS) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT RECENTLY MADE OVER $150,000 IN ENERGY EFFICIENCY UPGRADES AT FOUR AFFORDABLE HOUSING COMMUNITIES OWNED BY LA FAMILY HOUSING, LOCATED IN NORTH HOLLYWOOD AND VAN NUYS. SOCALGAS INSTALLED HIGH-EFFICIENCY BOILERS, 120-GALLON STORAGE TANKS FOR WATER STORAGE, PIPE INSULATION AND MORE, ASSISTING MORE THAN 220 RESIDENTS.
