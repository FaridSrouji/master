San Diego Gas & Electric Says CPUC Issued Final Decision In 2019 General Rate Case Of Co, Southern California Gas Company. SAN DIEGO GAS & ELECTRIC CO - ON SEPT 26, CPUC ISSUED A FINAL DECISION IN 2019 GENERAL RATE CASE OF CO, SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA GAS COMPANY - SEC FILING.SAN DIEGO GAS & ELECTRIC - GRC DECISION APPROVES ESTABLISHMENT OF 2-WAY LIABILITY INSURANCE PREMIUM BALANCING ACCOUNTS FOR CO, SOCALGAS.SAN DIEGO GAS & ELECTRIC - 2-WAY LIABILITY INSURANCE PREMIUM BALANCING ACCOUNTS FOR CO, SOCALGAS INCLUDES WILDFIRE INSURANCE PREMIUM COSTS.SAN DIEGO GAS & ELECTRIC -  2019 GRC FD ALSO PERMITS CO, SOCALGAS TO POTENTIALLY PURCHASE ADDITIONAL LIABILITY INSURANCE COVERAGE.SAN DIEGO GAS & ELECTRIC -  2019 GRC FD RETAINS 3-YEAR GRC CYCLE FOR CO, SOCALGAS SPECIFYING THE 2020 AND 2021 REVENUE REQUIREMENT INCREASES.
