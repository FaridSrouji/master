Teledyne Technologies Incorporated Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019; Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. Teledyne Technologies Incorporated provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019. For the fourth quarter of 2019, the company expects GAAP earnings per diluted share will be in the range of $2.71 to $2.76.For the full year 2019, the company now expects GAAP earnings per diluted share will be in the range of $10.37 to $10.42, an increase from the prior outlook of $9.86 to $9.96.
