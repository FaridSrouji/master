Teledyne Marine to Supply Seismic Source Controllers to PGS. Teledyne Marine Seismic has been awarded a long-term contract to provide seismic source controllers to PGS ASA. Teledyne Real Time Systems’ SmartSource system represents decades of experience in marine seismic exploration. The system is capable of synchronizing up to 192 sources and is the next generation in digital marine seismic source control. The SmartSource system provides real-time visual evaluation of array performance, accommodates modern array design, and features a Solenoid Control Valve Assembly with integrated Pressure, Depth, and Hydrophone sensor functions.
