Teledyne Imaging announces development of new generation of CMOS sensors and cameras. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., NOV. 18, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TELEDYNE IMAGING, PART OF TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES, ANNOUNCES THE DEVELOPMENT OF A NEW GENERATION OF CMOS SENSORS AND CAMERAS. THE INITIATIVE INVOLVES SEVERAL GROUPS WITHIN TELEDYNE IMAGING, FOCUSED ON THE DEVELOPMENT, FABRICATION, AND SUPPLY OF CMOS DETECTORS AND CAMERAS FOR THE SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH AND COMMERCIAL MARKETS INCLUDING ASTRONOMY, SOFT X-RAY, MICROSCOPY, SPECTROSCOPY AND BIOMEDICAL IMAGING.
