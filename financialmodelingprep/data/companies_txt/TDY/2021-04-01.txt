Teledyne Clears Canada and Germany Antitrust Reviews for the FLIR Acquisition. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED (NYSE:TDY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT RECEIVED ANTITRUST CLEARANCE FOR THE PENDING ACQUISITION OF FLIR SYSTEMS, INC. (NASDAQ:FLIR) FROM REGULATORY AUTHORITIES IN CANADA AND GERMANY. ON WEDNESDAY, MARCH 31, 2021, TELEDYNE RECEIVED A NO-ACTION LETTER REGARDING THE PROPOSED ACQUISITION FROM THE COMPETITION BUREAU OF THE GOVERNMENT OF CANADA. TODAY, TELEDYNE RECEIVED A CLEARANCE LETTER FROM THE FEDERAL CARTEL OFFICE OF GERMANY.
