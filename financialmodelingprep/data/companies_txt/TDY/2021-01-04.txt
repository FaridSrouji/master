Teledyne Merger Investigation: Halper Sadeh LLP Announces Investigation Into Whether the Merger of Teledyne Technologies Incorporated Is Fair to Shareholders; Investors Are Encouraged to Contact the Firm – TDY. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HALPER SADEH LLP, A GLOBAL INVESTOR RIGHTS LAW FIRM, IS INVESTIGATING WHETHER THE MERGER OF TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED (NYSE: TDY) AND FLIR SYSTEMS, INC. IS FAIR TO TELEDYNE SHAREHOLDERS. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE MERGER AGREEMENT, FLIR SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $28.00 PER SHARE IN CASH AND 0.0718 SHARES OF TELEDYNE COMMON STOCK FOR EACH FLIR SHARE.
