Teledyne e2v HiRel and Integra Technologies Inc. Partner on High Voltage GaN Devices. MILPITAS, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TELEDYNE E2V HIREL PARTNERS WITH INTEGRA TECHNOLOGIES TO BRING GAN TO EMERGING HI-REL APPLICATIONS IN SPACE FOR THE LEO AND GEO MARKETS.
Teledyne e2v and Yumain announce collaboration to create AI-based imaging solutions for machine vision. GRENOBLE, FRANCE, MARCH 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TELEDYNE E2V, A TELEDYNE TECHNOLOGIES [NYSE: TDY] COMPANY AND GLOBAL INNOVATOR OF IMAGING SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCES A NEW TECHNOLOGY AND INDUSTRIAL COLLABORATION WITH YUMAIN (DIJON, FRANCE), A LEADING AI VISION SOLUTION PROVIDER SERVING A BROAD RANGE OF INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS. TOGETHER, THE COMPANIES WILL DEVELOP CUTTING-EDGE BIO-INSPIRED VISION SOLUTIONS THAT CAN ENABLE INNOVATION IN INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS.
