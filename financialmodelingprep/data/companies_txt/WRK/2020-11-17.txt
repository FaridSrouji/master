WestRock Named to 2020 Dow Jones Sustainability North America and World Indices. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WESTROCK (NYSE: WRK), A LEADING PROVIDER OF DIFFERENTIATED PAPER AND PACKAGING SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED TO THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY NORTH AMERICA INDEX (DJSI NORTH AMERICA) AND DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY WORLD INDEX (DJSI WORLD) FOR 2020, IN RECOGNITION OF ITS COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS PRACTICES. THE INDEX IS A WIDELY RECOGNIZED STANDARD FOR MEASURING AND ADVANCING CORPORATE ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) PRACTICES ACROS
