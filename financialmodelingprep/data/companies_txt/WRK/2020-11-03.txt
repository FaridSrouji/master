Jim Porter to Retire from WestRock. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WestRock Company (NYSE: WRK) today announced that Jim Porter, president, Business Development & Latin America, has decided to retire, effective December 31, 2020. Following his retirement, Jim will continue to contribute to WestRock by serving as one of its representatives on the board of managers of Gondi, S. de R.L. de C.V., the Company's Grupo Gondi joint venture, and an advisor to the Company.
