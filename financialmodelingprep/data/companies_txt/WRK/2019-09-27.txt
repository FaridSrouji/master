Westrock Co Entered Into $600 Mln Credit Agreement. WESTROCK CO -  ON SEPT 27, CO ENTERED INTO A CREDIT AGREEMENT.WESTROCK CO -  CREDIT AGREEMENT PROVIDES FOR A SEVEN-YEAR SENIOR UNSECURED TERM LOAN FACILITY IN AN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $600 MILLION.WESTROCK CO -  PROCEEDS OF CREDIT FACILITY WERE USED TO PREPAY LOANS OUTSTANDING UNDER CREDIT AGREEMENT DATED AS OF JULY 1, 2015.
