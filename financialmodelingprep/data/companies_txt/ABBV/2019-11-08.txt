Abbvie Submits Supplemental NDA To U.S. FDA For Imbruvica In Combination With Rituximab. ABBVIE SUBMITS SUPPLEMENTAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION TO U.S. FDA FOR IMBRUVICA (IBRUTINIB) IN COMBINATION WITH RITUXIMAB FOR THE TREATMENT OF PREVIOUSLY UNTREATED, YOUNGER ADULTS WITH CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA.ABBVIE INC - SUBMISSION IS BASED ON POSITIVE RESULTS FROM PHASE 3 E1912 CLINICAL TRIAL.
