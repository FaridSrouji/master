FDA Approves Treatment For Adults, Children With All Genotypes Of Hepatitis C And Compensated Cirrhosis. Sept 26  - Abbvie Inc <ABBV.N>::FDA APPROVES TREATMENT FOR ADULTS AND CHILDREN WITH ALL GENOTYPES OF HEPATITIS C AND COMPENSATED CIRRHOSIS THAT SHORTENS DURATION OF TREATMENT TO EIGHT WEEKS.U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION - EXPANDED APPROVAL OF MAVYRET (GLECAPREVIR AND PIBRENTASVIR) TABLETS.FDA SAYS GRANTED APPROVAL OF MAVYRET TO ABBVIE INC.
