Abbvie Inc. Approves an Amendments to Its By-Laws. On October 17, 2019, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of AbbVie Inc. approved and adopted Amended and Restated By-laws to amend Article V, Sections 5.1 and 5.2 to add Vice chairman as a category of officers of the Company and to add Senior Vice Presidents as a category of officers who may be appointed by the Chairman of the Board. Article I was also amended to reflect an update to the Company’s registered agent and registered office.
