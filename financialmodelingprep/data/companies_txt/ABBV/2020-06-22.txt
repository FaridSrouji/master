FDA Accepts Supplemental Biologics License Application For Botox. FDA ACCEPTS SUPPLEMENTAL BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION (SBLA) FOR BOTOX® (ONABOTULINUMTOXINA) FOR THE TREATMENT OF PEDIATRIC PATIENTS WITH NEUROGENIC DETRUSOR OVERACTIVITY.ABBVIE INC - PRESCRIPTION DRUG USER FEE ACT DATE IS EXPECTED TO BE IN Q1 OF 2021 FOLLOWING A STANDARD 10-MONTH REVIEW..ABBVIE - SBLA IS BASED ON DATA FROM A RANDOMIZED, DOUBLE-BLIND PHASE 3 STUDY EVALUATING SAFETY AND EFFICACY OF BOTOXIN MORE THAN 100 PEDIATRIC PATIENTS.
