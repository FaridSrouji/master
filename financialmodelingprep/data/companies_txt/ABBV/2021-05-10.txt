Allergan Aesthetics to Acquire Soliton, Expanding Body Contouring Portfolio. IRVINE, CALIF. AND HOUSTON, MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALLERGAN AESTHETICS, AN ABBVIE COMPANY (NYSE: ABBV) AND SOLITON (NASDAQ: SOLY) TODAY ANNOUNCED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH ALLERGAN AESTHETICS WILL ACQUIRE SOLITON AND RESONICTM, ITS RAPID ACOUSTIC PULSE DEVICE WHICH RECENTLY RECEIVED U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) 510(K) CLEARANCE AND IS A NON-INVASIVE TREATMENT FOR THE SHORT-TERM IMPROVEMENT IN THE APPEARANCE OF CELLULITE.
