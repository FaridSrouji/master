Abbvie Provides Update From Phase 3 Study Evaluating Venclexta In Combination With Low-Dose Cytarabine In Newly-Diagnosed Patients With Acute Myeloid Leukemia. ABBVIE PROVIDES UPDATE FROM PHASE 3 STUDY EVALUATING VENCLEXTA® (VENETOCLAX) IN COMBINATION WITH LOW-DOSE CYTARABINE IN NEWLY-DIAGNOSED PATIENTS WITH ACUTE MYELOID LEUKEMIA (AML).ABBVIE-PHASE 3 VIALE-A TRIAL EVALUATING VENETOCLAX IN COMBINATION WITH AZACYTIDINE IS ONGOING, AT THIS TIME INDICATIONS FOR VENETOCLAX REMAIN UNCHANGED.ABBVIE - STUDY EVALUATING VENCLEXTA DID NOT DEMONSTRATE STATISTICALLY SIGNIFICANT IMPROVEMENT IN PRIMARY ENDPOINT OF OVERALL SURVIVAL.
