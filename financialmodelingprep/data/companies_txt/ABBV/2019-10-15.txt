Allergan Shareholders Approve Proposed Acquisition By Abbvie. Oct 14  - Allergan plc <AGN.N>::ALLERGAN SHAREHOLDERS APPROVE PROPOSED ACQUISITION BY ABBVIE.ALLERGAN PLC - PROPOSED ACQUISITION BY ABBVIE IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN EARLY 2020.ALLERGAN - MORE THAN 99% OF VOTES CAST AT SPECIAL COURT-ORDERED MEETING & EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS WERE IN FAVOR OF ABBVIE DEAL.
