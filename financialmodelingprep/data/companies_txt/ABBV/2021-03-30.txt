U.S. FDA Accepts AbbVie's New Drug Application for Atogepant for the Preventive Treatment of Migraine. NORTH CHICAGO, ILL., MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBVIE (NYSE: ABBV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS ACCEPTED ITS NEW DRUG APPLICATION (NDA) FOR ATOGEPANT, AN INVESTIGATIONAL ORALLY ADMINISTERED CALCITONIN GENE-RELATED PEPTIDE (CGRP) RECEPTOR ANTAGONIST (GEPANT), FOR THE PREVENTIVE TREATMENT OF MIGRAINE IN ADULTS WHO MEET CRITERIA FOR EPISODIC MIGRAINE.
