Fitch Downgrades Boeing To 'A-'/'F2'; Outlook Stable. Jan 17  - FITCH::FITCH DOWNGRADES BOEING TO 'A-'/'F2'; OUTLOOK STABLE.FITCH SAYS DOWNGRADED BOEING COMPANY'S LONG-TERM RATINGS TO 'A-' FROM 'A' AND SHORT-TERM RATINGS TO 'F2' FROM 'F1'.FITCH SAYS BOEING CAPITAL'S LONG-TERM RATINGS HAVE BEEN DOWNGRADED TO 'A-' FROM 'A'.FITCH SAYS BOEING'S DOWNGRADE IS BASED ON CONTINUED REGULATORY RISK REGARDING TIMING AND GLOBAL SEQUENCING OF 737 MAX'S RETURN-TO-COMMERCIAL SERVICE.FITCH SAYS STABLE OUTLOOK REFLECTS PERFORMANCE OF BOEING'S OTHER BUSINESSES, WHICH PROVIDE SIGNIFICANT SUPPORT AT CURRENT RATING LEVEL.FITCH SAYS MAX SITUATION HAS REDUCED MUCH OF BOEING'S FINANCIAL CUSHION IN 'A' CATEGORY.FITCH ON BOEING SAYS LOGISTICAL CHALLENGE OF RETURNING THE PLANES TO SERVICE AND CATCHING UP ON DELIVERIES ALSO DRIVES RATING CHANGE.FITCH SAYS BOEING'S DEBT TO RISE IN FIRST & SECOND QUARTERS OF 2020, POTENTIALLY PEAKING AT MORE THAN $32 BILLION-$34 BILLION, BEFORE BEGINNING TO FALL.FITCH SAYS EXPECTS THERE WILL BE A LINGERING OPERATING MARGIN IMPACT FOR SEVERAL YEARS EVEN AFTER BOEING'S MAX RETURNS TO COMMERCIAL SERVICE.FITCH ON BOEING SAYS MAX SITUATION ALSO LIKELY TO INCREASE BUSINESS RISK FROM FINES, LITIGATION, WEAKENED COMPETITIVE POSITION OR REPUTATION DAMAGE.FITCH SAYS BOEING'S LIQUIDITY NEEDS WILL LIKELY PEAK IN 1Q20 OR EARLY 2Q20 AS A RESULT OF CONTINUED 737 MAX CASH USAGE, AMONG OTHERS.
