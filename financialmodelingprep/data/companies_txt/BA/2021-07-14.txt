Boeing and SkyNRG Partner to Scale Sustainable Aviation Fuels Globally. SEATTLE, JULY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING (NYSE: BA), SKYNRG AND SKYNRG AMERICAS TODAY ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP FOCUSED ON SCALING THE AVAILABILITY AND USE OF SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUELS (SAF) GLOBALLY. BOEING WILL ALSO INVEST IN SKYNRG AMERICAS' SAF PRODUCTION PROJECT, FOR WHICH ALASKA AIRLINES IS A PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED PARTNER.
