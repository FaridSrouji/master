SMBC Aviation Capital Orders 14 Boeing 737 MAX Jets. SEATTLE, MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] AND SMBC AVIATION CAPITAL TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LESSOR IS POSITIONING ITS PORTFOLIO FOR AIR TRAFFIC RECOVERY BY ORDERING 14 ADDITIONAL 737-8 JETS, GROWING ITS 737 MAX PORTFOLIO. THE NEW ORDER COMES AS AIRLINES PREPARE FOR A ROBUST RETURN TO AIR TRAVEL AND MODERNIZE THEIR NARROWBODY FLEETS TO REDUCE FUEL USE AND CARBON EMISSIONS.
