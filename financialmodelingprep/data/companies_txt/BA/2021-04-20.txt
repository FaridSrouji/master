Dubai Aerospace Enterprise Orders 15 Boeing 737 MAX Jets. SEATTLE, APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOEING [NYSE: BA] AND DUBAI AEROSPACE ENTERPRISE (DAE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE AIRCRAFT LESSOR IS GROWING ITS 737 MAX PORTFOLIO WITH AN ORDER FOR 15 737-8 JETS. DAE HAD BEEN INVESTING IN THE 737 MAX BY BUYING JETS FROM EXISTING CUSTOMERS AND LEASING THEM BACK TO THE CARRIERS.
