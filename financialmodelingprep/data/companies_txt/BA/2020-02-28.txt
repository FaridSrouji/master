Spirit AeroSystems To Restart 737 MAX Production In March - Conf Call. SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS SAYS WILL RESTART 737 MAX PRODUCTION SLOWLY IN MARCH AND RAMP THROUGHOUT THE YEAR - CONF CALL.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS SAYS EXPECTS TO REACH 737 MAX PRODUCTION RATE OF 52/MONTH BY LATE 2022 - CONF CALL.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS CEO SAYS 737 MAX PRODUCTION HALT AND SLOW SUBSEQUENT PRODUCTION RAMP WILL BE CHALLENGING IN TERMS OF CASH FLOW FOR THE FIRST HALF OF 2020 - CONF CALL.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS CEO SAYS ASCO HAS RESUMED ITS OPERATIONS FOLLOWING THE CYBER ATTACK LAST YEAR AND CO REMAINS ON TRACK TO CLOSE THE DEAL IN 2020 - CONF CALL.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS CFO SAYS PROJECTING NEGATIVE OVERALL FREE CASH FLOW FOR 2020 - CONF CALL.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS CFO SAYS WILL RECORD EXCESS COSTS RELATED TO 737 MAX PRODUCTION SUSPENSION STARTING Q1 2020, AND THESE COULD BE SPREAD OVER MULTIPLE PERIODS UNTIL PRODUCTION RESOURCES ARE UTILIZED AT NORMAL CAPACITY.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS CEO SAYS FORMED A JOINT TASK FORCE WITH BOEING TO REVIEW THE HEALTH OF THE SUPPLY CHAIN - CONF CALL.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS CEO SAYS GOING THROUGH SUPPLIER PROBLEMS AND DEVELOPING A CUSTOMIZED SOLUTION FOR EACH OF THE SUPPLIERS - CONF CALL.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS CEO SAYS "WE WILL MAKE SURE THAT WE HELP ALL THE SUPPLIERS GET THROUGH THIS VERY CHALLENGING SITUATION" - CONF CALL.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS CEO SAYS CO IS NOT PERMITTED TO DO SHARE REPURCHASES UNTIL CO REGAINS INVESTMENT GRADE - CONF CALL.
Spirit Aerosystems Reports Q4 Adjusted Earnings Per Share $0.79. SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS REPORTS 2019 RESULTS.Q4 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.79.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.65.Q4 REVENUE $2.0 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.95 BILLION.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.65 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS - BOEING LOWERING 787 PRODUCTION RATE TO 10 AIRCRAFT PER MONTH RESULTED IN $34 MILLION FORWARD LOSS DUE TO FIXED COST ABSORPTION IN Q4.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS - GIVEN CONTINUED UNCERTAINTY SURROUNDING TIMING OF RETURN TO SERVICE OF THE 737 MAX, CO WILL NOT GIVE GUIDANCE AT THIS TIME.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS - CONCLUDED THAT IT SHOULD HAVE RECORDED INCREMENTAL CONTINGENT LIABILITY FOR Q3 OF 2019 OF LESS THAN $8 MILLION.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS - BELIEVE LIQUIDITY POSITION REMAINS SUFFICIENT.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS - ACCOUNTING MATTER LED TO CONCLUSION, MATERIAL WEAKNESS IN INTERNAL CONTROL OVER FINANCIAL REPORTING EXISTED AS OF DEC. 31, 2019.EXPECTS MATERIAL WEAKNESS IN FINANCIAL REPORTING TO BE FULLY REMEDIATED BY END OF YEAR.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS - BACKLOG AT END OF Q4 WAS ABOUT $43 BILLION, WITH WORK PACKAGES ON ALL COMMERCIAL PLATFORMS IN BOEING, AIRBUS BACKLOG.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS, ON ACCOUNTING REVIEW, SAYS CO VIEWS LIABILITY DIFFERENCE AS IMMATERIAL AND NO RESTATEMENT OF FINANCIAL STATEMENTS IS REQUIRED.
