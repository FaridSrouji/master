Boeing Says On Feb 6, 2020, Boeing Company Entered Into A $13.0 Billion, Two-Year Delayed Draw Term Loan Credit Agreement. BOEING - ON FEB 6, 2020, BOEING COMPANY ENTERED INTO A $13.0 BILLION,TWO-YEAR DELAYED DRAW TERM LOAN CREDIT AGREEMENT.BOEING - BOEING WILL PAY A FEE BETWEEN 0.05% AND 0.15% PER ANNUM ON COMMITMENTS, DEPENDING ON BOEING'S CREDIT RATING.BOEING - COMMITMENTS UNDER CREDIT AGREEMENT ARE SCHEDULED TO TERMINATE NO LATER THAN AUGUST 6, 2021, WITH FEBRUARY 6, 2022 AS FINAL MATURITY DATE.
Spirit Aerosystems Holdings Says MOA Provides That Boeing Will Pay $225 Mln To Spirit In Q1 Of 2020. SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS - ON FEB 6, BOEING COMPANY AND UNIT ENTERED A MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT SUPERSEDING APRIL 12, 2019 MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS INC -  MOA PROVIDES THAT BOEING WILL PAY $225 MILLION TO SPIRIT IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS - TERMS OF MOA INCLUDE EXTENDING REPAYMENT DATE OF $123 MILLION ADVANCE RECEIVED BY SPIRIT UNDER 2019 MOA TO 2022.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS HOLDINGS - TERMS OF MOA INCLUDE EXTENDING B737 MAX PRICING TERMS THROUGH 2033.SPIRIT AEROSYSTEMS-PARTIES WILL EXECUTE AMENDMENTS TO UNDERLYING CONTRACTS TO INCORPORATE MOA ON/BEFORE 60 DAYS FOLLOWING 737 MAX UNGROUNDING.
