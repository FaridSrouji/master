Myriota Raises $19.3 Mln In Series B Funding To Grow Its Satellite Network To Support Billions Of Devices. MYRIOTA RAISES $19.3M USD IN SERIES B FUNDING TO GROW ITS SATELLITE NETWORK TO SUPPORT BILLIONS OF DEVICES.MYRIOTA - ANNOUNCED A $19.3 MILLION USD SERIES B FUNDING ROUND LED BY HOSTPLUS AND MAIN SEQUENCE VENTURES.MYRIOTA - ADDITIONAL INVESTORS IN ROUND INCLUDE IN-Q-TEL, RIGHT CLICK CAPITAL, SINGTEL INNOV8, BOEING HORIZONX,  AND MALCOLM TURNBULL.
