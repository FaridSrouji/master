Aercap Holdings N.V. Reports Q1 Earnings Per Share Of $2.14. AERCAP HOLDINGS N.V. REPORTS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER 2020.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.14.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.75 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY TOTAL REVENUES AND OTHER INCOME $1,238.2 MILLION VERSUS $1,205.0 MILLION.QTRLY TOTAL LEASE REVENUE $1,165.1 MILLION VERSUS $1,162.1 MILLION.AS OF MARCH 31, 2020, WE HAD 95 BOEING 737 MAX AIRCRAFT ON ORDER WITH BOEING AND FIVE ALREADY DELIVERED AND ON LEASE.IT IS UNCERTAIN, HOWEVER, WHEN AND UNDER WHAT CONDITIONS OUR BOEING 737 MAX AIRCRAFT WILL RETURN TO SERVICE.IT IS UNCERTAIN WHEN BOEING WILL RESUME MAKING DELIVERIES OF CO'S BOEING 737 MAX AIRCRAFT ON ORDER.EXPECT TO INCUR FUTURE DELAYS ON OUR SCHEDULED BOEING 737 MAX DELIVERIES.ANY SUCH FUTURE DELAYS ON CO'S SCHEDULED BOEING 737 MAX DELIVERIES ARE LIKELY TO HAVE AN IMPACT ON OUR FINANCIAL RESULTS.CERTAIN OF OUR BOEING 737 MAX LEASES HAVE NOW BEEN CANCELLED.EXPECT ADDITIONAL BOEING 737 MAX LEASES TO BE CANCELLED IN FUTURE.BOEING 737 MAX LEASES CANCELLATIONS MAY HAVE ADVERSE EFFECT ON FUTURE CASH FLOWS & RESULTS OF OPERATIONS, WHICH COULD BE MATERIAL.WITHDRAWING OUR 2020 GUIDANCE.AERCAP HOLDINGS - WITHDRAWING 2020 GUIDANCE AS CO IS UNABLE AT THIS TIME TO ESTIMATE IMPACT OF COVID-19 OUTBREAK ON FUTURE FINANCIAL RESULTS.
