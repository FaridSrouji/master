Boeing's Senior Vice President Of Communications Resigns. BOEING - NIEL GOLIGHTLY RESIGNED TODAY AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF COMMUNICATIONS AT BOEING COMPANY.BOEING - BOEING HAS INITIATED A SEARCH FOR GOLIGHTLY'S SUCCESSOR.BOEING - IN INTERIM, COMMUNICATIONS FUNCTION WILL REPORT TO GREG SMITH, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF ENTERPRISE OPERATIONS AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.BOEING - NIEL'S DECISION TO RESIGN STEMS FROM AN EMPLOYEE COMPLAINT THAT BROUGHT TO COMPANY'S ATTENTION AN ARTICLE HE WROTE IN 1987.BOEING - ARTICLE WRITTEN IN 1987 BY GOLIGHTLY WHILE SERVING IN MILITARY WAS ABOUT WHETHER WOMEN SHOULD SERVE IN COMBAT.BOEING - BOEING DOES NOT AGREE WITH VIEWS EXPRESSED IN ARTICLE BY GOLIGHTLY.BOEING - VIEWS EXPRESSED IN GOLIGHTLY'S 1987 ARTICLE "DOES NOT REFLECT NIEL'S VIEWS TODAY".
