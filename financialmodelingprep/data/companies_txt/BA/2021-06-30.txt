Boeing Appoints Brian West as Chief Financial Officer. CHICAGO, JUNE 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOEING COMPANY [NYSE: BA] TODAY NAMED BRIAN WEST AS THE COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER EFFECTIVE AUGUST 27, 2021. IN THIS ROLE, WEST WILL LEAD ALL ASPECTS OF BOEING'S FINANCIAL STRATEGY, PERFORMANCE, REPORTING AND LONG-RANGE BUSINESS PLANNING, AS WELL AS INVESTOR RELATIONS, TREASURY, CONTROLLER, AND AUDIT OPERATIONS.
