Bel Fuse Reports Q4 Sales Of $115.1 Mln. BEL REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2019 RESULTS.Q4 SALES FELL 19.3 PERCENT TO $115.1 MILLION.QUARTERLY LOSS PER CLASS A SHARE $0.50.QUARTERLY LOSS PER CLASS B SHARE $0.52.SAYS ENTER 2020 WITH VERY LIMITED VISIBILITY GIVEN RECENT CORONAVIRUS OUTBREAK AND SEPARATELY, GROUNDING OF BOEING'S 737-MAX.SAYS CLOSELY MONITORING CORONAVIRUS OUTBREAK AND ITS IMPACT ON OPERATIONS AND SUPPLY CHANNEL.BEL FUSE - ANTICIPATE Q1 RESULTS WILL BE IMPACTED BY EXTENDED LUNAR NEW YEAR HOLIDAY BREAK AND BY LOWER PRODUCTIVITY LEVELS IN CHINA SITES.SAYS CONTINUED GROUNDING OF BOEING'S 737-MAX WILL ALSO HAVE AN IMPACT ON OUR COMPARISONS THROUGHOUT 2020.SAYS ANTICIPATE PRODUCTION OF BOEING'S 737-MAX TO RESUME IN COMING MONTHS, IT WILL LIKELY BE AT A LOWER RATE FOR A PERIOD OF TIME.SAYS WILL CONTINUE TO STREAMLINE ORGANIZATION TO IMPROVE PROFITABILITY.
