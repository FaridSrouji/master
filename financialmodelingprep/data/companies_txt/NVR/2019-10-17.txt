Nvr, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. NVR, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced net income was USD 223.787 million compared to USD 195.816 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 60.94 compared to USD 54.21 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 56.11 compared to USD 48.28 a year ago. For the nine months, net income was USD 622.402 million compared to USD 565.039 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 171.43 compared to USD 155.22 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 156.61 compared to USD 136.53 a year ago.
