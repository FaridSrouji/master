T Rowe Price Hires Tim Coyne to Lead its Newly Established ETF Team. T Rowe Price have launched their own ETF businesses and each hired a specialist from a rival group to head up their respective operations. T Rowe Price hired State Street veteran Tim Coyne to lead its newly established ETF team. At T. Rowe Price, Coyne joined the firm after having served over 13 years at State Street Global Advisors. His most recent role was senior managing director and global head of capital markets within its SPDR ETF unit. Prior to that, he spent four years as the firm's managing director of ETF institutional sales and global capital markets. Coyne has also held positions at the New York Stock Exchange, where he was managing director of ETF sales and marketing.
