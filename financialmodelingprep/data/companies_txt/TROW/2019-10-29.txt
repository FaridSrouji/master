T. Rowe Price Group Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable December 30, 2019. T. Rowe Price Group, Inc. announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.76 per share payable December 30, 2019 to stockholders of record as of the close of business on December 16, 2019.
