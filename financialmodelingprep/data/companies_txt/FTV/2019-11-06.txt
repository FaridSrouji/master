Fortive Corporation Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend on Its Common Stock and Its Preferred Stock, Payable on December 27, 2019 and January 2, 2020 Respectively. Fortive Corporation announced that its board of directors declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.07 per share of its common stock, par value $0.01 per share, payable on December 27, 2019 to common stockholders of record on November 29, 2019. In addition, company announced that its board of directors declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $12.50 per share of its 5.00% Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock, Series A, par value $0.01 per share, payable onJanuary 2, 2020 to preferred stockholders of record on December 15, 2019.
