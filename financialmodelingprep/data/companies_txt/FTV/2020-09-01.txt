Fortive Announces Intent To Proceed With Its Transformation Via A Spin-Off Of 80.1% Of Vontier. FORTIVE ANNOUNCES INTENT TO PROCEED WITH ITS TRANSFORMATION VIA A SPIN-OFF OF 80.1% OF VONTIER.FORTIVE CORP - SEPARATION WILL OCCUR BY MEANS OF A SPIN-OFF OF 80.1% OF OUTSTANDING SHARES OF VONTIER COMMON STOCK TO FORTIVE SHAREHOLDERS.FORTIVE CORP - UPON COMPLETION OF SPIN-OFF, FORTIVE WILL RETAIN A 19.9% STAKE IN VONTIER.FORTIVE CORP - WHEN COMPLETED, TRANSACTION WILL RESULT IN TWO INDEPENDENT, PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANIES.
