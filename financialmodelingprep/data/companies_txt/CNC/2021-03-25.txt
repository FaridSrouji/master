Centene Subsidiary 'Ohana Health Plan Selected to Continue Serving Hawaii's Medicaid Managed Care and CHIP Programs Statewide. ST. LOUIS, MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY ITS HAWAII SUBSIDIARY 'OHANA HEALTH PLAN WAS SELECTED BY THE HAWAII DEPARTMENT OF HUMAN SERVICES' MED-QUEST DIVISION TO CONTINUE ADMINISTERING COVERED SERVICES TO ELIGIBLE MEDICAID AND CHILDREN'S HEALTH INSURANCE PROGRAM (CHIP) MEMBERS FOR MEDICALLY NECESSARY MEDICAL, BEHAVIORAL HEALTH, AND LONG-TERM SERVICES AND SUPPORTS. THE NEW STATEWIDE CONTRACT IS ANTICIPATED TO BEGIN JULY 1, 2021.
