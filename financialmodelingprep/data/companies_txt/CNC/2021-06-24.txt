Centene Corporation Prices Offering of Senior Notes. ST. LOUIS, JUNE 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ("CENTENE" OR THE "COMPANY") ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS PRICED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF $1,800,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.450% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2028 (THE "NOTES"). THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE ON OR ABOUT JULY 1, 2021, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS.
