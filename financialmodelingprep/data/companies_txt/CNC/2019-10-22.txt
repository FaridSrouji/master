Centene Corporation Adopts Amendment to the Company's By-Laws. On October 22, 2019, the Centene Corporation board of directors adopted an amendment to the company's By-Laws (as amended and restated, the By-Laws") to increase the maximum number of members of the Board from eleven (11) to twelve (12).
