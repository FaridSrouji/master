Centene Names Colin Toney Executive Vice President, Mergers And Acquisitions. ST. LOUIS, JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT COLIN TONEY, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, ENTERPRISE STRATEGY GROUP, HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, MERGERS AND ACQUISITIONS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY. MR. TONEY WILL REPORT TO CHAIRMAN, PRESIDENT, AND CEO MICHAEL NEIDORFF.
