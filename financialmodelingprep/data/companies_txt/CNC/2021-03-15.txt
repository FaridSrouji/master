Centene and Pro Football Hall of Fame Team Up to Tackle Concerns Over COVID-19 Vaccinations. ST. LOUIS, MARCH 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) AND SEVERAL MEMBERS OF THE PRO FOOTBALL HALL OF FAME FAMILY ARE PARTNERING TO INCREASE EDUCATION AND AWARENESS AROUND COVID-19 VACCINES THROUGH A SERIES OF NEW 15-, 30- AND 60-SECOND PUBLIC SERVICE ANNOUNCEMENTS (PSAS), WITH A PARTICULAR FOCUS ON COMMUNITIES OF COLOR. THE PSAS WILL AIR NATIONALLY ON TELEVISION NETWORKS AND OVER DIGITAL PLATFORMS BEGINNING TODAY.
