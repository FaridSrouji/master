Centene Corporation Appoints Katie Casso as Corporate Controller. ST. LOUIS, MARCH 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT KATIE CASSO HAS BEEN NAMED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE CONTROLLER, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1, 2021.  CHRISTOPHER ISAAK, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, CORPORATE CONTROLLER AND CHIEF ACCOUNTING OFFICER ANNOUNCED HIS RETIREMENT, EFFECTIVE APRIL 30, 2021.
