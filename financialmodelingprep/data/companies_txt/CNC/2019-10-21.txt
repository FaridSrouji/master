Centene Corporation Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year of 2019 and Full Year 2020; Reports Impairment Results for the Third Quarter 2019. Centene Corporation provided guidance for the full year 2019. For the full year 2019, the company estimates total revenue to be in the range of $73.6 billion to $74.2 billion. GAAP diluted EPS is expected to be $3.04 billion to $3.21 billion.The company expects fourth quarter total revenues to be lower than the third quarter 2019 revenues as a result of the state-directed payments previously mentioned. For 2020, the company is still projecting total revenue to be around that $79 plus billion range. Goodwill and intangible impairment for the third quarter 2019 was $271 million compared to nill a year ago.
Centene Says On Oct. 18, Co's Board Of Directors Authorized Repurchase Of Up To $500 Mln Of Shares. Oct 21  - Centene Corp <CNC.N>::CENTENE CORP - ON OCTOBER 18, 2019, CENTENE'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS AUTHORIZED REPURCHASE OF UP TO $500 MILLION OF SHARES OF COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK.CENTENE - NEW REPURCHASE PROGRAM IS IN ADDITION TO PREVIOUSLY APPROVED STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM.
