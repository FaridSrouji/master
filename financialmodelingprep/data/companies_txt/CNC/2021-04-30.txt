Health Net Federal Services Earns Prestigious Center of Excellence Recognition from BenchmarkPortal for Ninth Consecutive Year. RANCHO CORDOVA, CALIF., APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTH NET FEDERAL SERVICES, LLC, (HNFS), A WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE:CNC), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS CALL CENTER WAS CERTIFIED AS A CENTER OF EXCELLENCE BY BENCHMARKPORTAL FOR THE NINTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
