Centene Reaches No-Fault Agreements with Ohio and Mississippi to Resolve Pharmacy Subsidiary Claims. ST. LOUIS, JUNE 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS REACHED NO-FAULT AGREEMENTS WITH THE ATTORNEYS GENERAL OF OHIO AND MISSISSIPPI TO RESOLVE CLAIMS MADE BY THE STATES RELATED TO SERVICES PROVIDED BY ENVOLVE PHARMACY SOLUTIONS, INC. (ENVOLVE), ITS PHARMACY BENEFITS MANAGER SUBSIDIARY.  UNDER THE TERMS OF THESE AGREEMENTS, CENTENE WILL PAY $88 MILLION TO OHIO AND $55 MILLION TO MISSISSIPPI.
