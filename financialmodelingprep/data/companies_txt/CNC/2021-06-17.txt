Centene Announces Winners Of Youth Impact Award For Vaping Prevention. ST. LOUIS, JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) HAS ANNOUNCED THE WINNERS OF THE CENTENE INSTITUTE YOUTH IMPACT AWARD FOR VAPING PREVENTION. THE NATIONAL PROGRAM IS A CURRICULUM AND CONTEST FOR ADOLESCENTS AGES 14 THROUGH 19 TO RAISE AWARENESS ABOUT VAPING, E-CIGARETTE USE AND PREVENTION.
