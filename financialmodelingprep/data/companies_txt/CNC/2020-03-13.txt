Centene Corp Says CEO's 2019 Total Compensation Was $26.4 Million. CENTENE CORP SAYS CEO MICHAEL NEIDORFF'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $26.4 MILLION VERSUS $26.1 MILLION IN FY 2018 - SEC FILING.CENTENE CORP SAYS CFO JEFFREY SCHWANEKE'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $9.4 MILLION VERSUS ABOUT $10.0 MILLION IN FY 2018.CENTENE CORP SAYS CEO PAY RATION FOR 2019 WAS 383:1.
