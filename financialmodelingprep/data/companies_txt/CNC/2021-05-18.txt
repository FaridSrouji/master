Centene Addresses Behavioral Health Challenges Amid National Mental Health Month. ST. LOUIS, MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CENTENE CORPORATION (NYSE: CNC) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS SUPPORT FOR NATIONAL MENTAL HEALTH MONTH THROUGH A NUMBER OF INITIATIVES THAT EMPOWER PROVIDERS AND INDIVIDUALS TO ADDRESS BEHAVIORAL HEALTH CHALLENGES, INCLUDING SUBSTANCE USE DISORDER (SUD). THIS YEAR, THE OBSERVANCE CARRIES ADDED SIGNIFICANCE AS COMMUNITIES ATTEMPT TO RECOVER FROM THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC AND THE ADDITIONAL STRESSORS THAT IMPACTED OVERALL HEALTH, INCLUDING FEAR, ISOLATION, AND ECONOMIC ANXIETY.
