CENTENE CORP SAYS EXPECTS TO SEE COVID-19 RELATED COSTS IN THE SECOND QUARTER WHICH IS EXPECTED TO CONTINUE THROUGHOUT 2020 - CONF CALL.CENTENE CORP SAYS IT EXPECTS PENT UP DEMAND FOR MEDICAL CARE IN THE BACK HALF OF THE YEAR - CONF CALL.CENTENE CORP SAYS THE TIMING OF SYNERGY CAPTURE FROM WELLCARE ACQUISITION WILL BE AFFECTED DUE TO SHIFTING REGULATORY TIMELINES - CONF CALL.
