EOG Resources Reports Fourth Quarter Results, Announces 2020 Capital Program. EOG RESOURCES REPORTS EXCELLENT FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2019 RESULTS; ANNOUNCES 2020 CAPITAL PROGRAM; RAISES DIVIDEND BY 30 PERCENT.Q4 ADJUSTED NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.35.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.15 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.10.EXPLORATION AND DEVELOPMENT EXPENDITURES FOR 2020 ARE EXPECTED TO RANGE FROM $6.3 BILLION TO $6.7 BILLION.DISCIPLINED CAPITAL PROGRAM SUPPORTS GROWTH IN CRUDE OIL PRODUCTION OF 10 TO 14 PERCENT IN 2020.TOTAL CRUDE OIL VOLUMES IN THE FOURTH QUARTER 2019 WERE 468,900 BOPD, UP EIGHT PERCENT.DISCIPLINED CAPITAL PROGRAM FUNDS DIVIDEND PAYMENTS WITH NET CASH FROM OPERATING ACTIVITIES AT LESS THAN $50 OIL.QTRLY OPERATING REVENUES AND OTHER $4.32 BILLION VERSUS $4.57 BILLION.QTRLY TOTAL CRUDE OIL EQUIVALENT VOLUMES 850.3 MBOED VERSUS 764.5 MBOED.Q4 REVENUE VIEW $4.39 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
