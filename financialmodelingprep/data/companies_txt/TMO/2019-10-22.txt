Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year of 2019. Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. revised earnings guidance for the full year of 2019. For the period, the company is raising its 2019 revenue and earnings guidance primarily to reflect stronger operational performance and the benefits of refinancing activities, partially offset by a more adverse foreign exchange environment. The company is raising its revenue guidance to a new range of $25.34 billion to $25.50 billion against its previous guidance of $25.30 billion to $25.50 billion. This would result in 4% to 5% revenue growth over 2018.
