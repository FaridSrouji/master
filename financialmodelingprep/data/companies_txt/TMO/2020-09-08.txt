FDA Approves First NGS-Based Companion Diagnostic For Ret Fusion-Positive Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer. FDA APPROVES FIRST NGS-BASED COMPANION DIAGNOSTIC FOR RET FUSION-POSITIVE NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER.THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC -  COMPANION DIAGNOSTIC APPROVAL EXPANDS CLINICAL UTILITY OF ONCOMINE DX TARGET TEST TO IDENTIFY CANDIDATES FOR GAVRETO.THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC - TO UPDATE ONCOMINE DX TARGET TEST TO ENABLE IT TO REPORT RET FUSIONS IN U.S. BEFORE END OF YEAR.
