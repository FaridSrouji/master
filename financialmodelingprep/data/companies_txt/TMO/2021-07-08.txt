Thermo Fisher Scientific Declares Quarterly Dividend. WALTHAM, MASS., JULY 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC INC. (NYSE: TMO), THE WORLD LEADER IN SERVING SCIENCE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.26 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF SEPTEMBER 15, 2021.
