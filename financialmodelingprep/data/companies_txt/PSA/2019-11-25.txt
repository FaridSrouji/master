Public Storage Announces Redemption of All Outstanding Depositary Shares Representing Interests in Its 5.875% Cumulative Preferred Shares, Series A. Public Storage announced that it is calling for redemption all outstanding depositary shares representing interests in its 5.875% Cumulative Preferred Shares, Series A on December 30, 2019 at $25 per depositary share. The aggregate redemption amount to be paid to all holders of the depositary shares is $190,000,000.
