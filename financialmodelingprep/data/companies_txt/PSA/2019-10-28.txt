Public Storage Declares Regular Common Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 30, 2019. Public Storage announced that its board of Trustees declared a regular common quarterly dividend of $2.00 per common share. Payable on December 30, 2019 to shareholders of record as of December 13, 2019.
