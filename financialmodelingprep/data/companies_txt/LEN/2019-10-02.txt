Lennar Corporation Announces Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on November 1, 2019. Lennar Corporation announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.04 per share for both Class A and Class B common stock payable on November 1, 2019 to holders of record at the close ofbusiness on October 18, 2019.
