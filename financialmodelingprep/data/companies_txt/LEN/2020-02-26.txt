Lennar Says Total Compensation For CEO Beckwitt Was $18.3 Mln In 2019 Vs $17.6 Mln In 2018. LENNAR CORP - TOTAL COMPENSATION FOR CEO RICK BECKWITT WAS $18.3 MILLION IN 2019 VERSUS $17.6 MILLION IN 2018.LENNAR CORP - TOTAL COMPENSATION FOR EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN STUART MILLER WAS $20.9 MILLION IN 2019 VERSUS $20.1 MILLION IN 2018.LENNAR - FOR 2019, RATIO OF CEO BECKWITT’S COMPENSATION TO COMPENSATION OF MEDIAN ASSOCIATE WAS 178 TO 1.
