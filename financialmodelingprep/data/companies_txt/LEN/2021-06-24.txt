Jeff Roos, Regional President for Lennar, Named 2021 Inductee into California Homebuilding Foundation's Hall of Fame. IRVINE, CALIF., JUNE 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LENNAR CORPORATION (NYSE: LEN AND LEN.B), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HOMEBUILDERS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT JEFF ROOS, REGIONAL PRESIDENT FOR LENNAR'S WESTERN REGION, IS ONE OF FIVE 2021 INDUCTEES INTO CALIFORNIA HOMEBUILDING FOUNDATION'S (CHF) HALL OF FAME.
