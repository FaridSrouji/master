Lennar Reports First Quarter EPS Of $1.27. LENNAR REPORTS FIRST QUARTER EPS OF $1.27.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.27.Q1 REVENUE $4.5 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $4.16 BILLION.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.84 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.EVENTS THAT HAVE OCCURRED SINCE QUARTER-END CURRENTLY COMMAND OUR FOCUS AND ATTENTION.Q1 CONFERENCE CALL WILL BE FOCUSED ON LENNAR'S OVERSIGHT AND MANAGEMENT THROUGH CURRENT CRISIS.REVENUES FROM HOME SALES INCREASED 15% IN Q1 OF 2020 TO $4.1 BILLION FROM $3.6 BILLION IN Q1 OF 2019.GROSS MARGIN ON HOME SALES WERE $849.0 MILLION, OR 20.5%, IN Q1 OF 2020, COMPARED TO $726.1 MILLION, OR 20.1% IN Q1 OF 2019.QTRLY DELIVERIES OF 10,321 HOMES - UP 17%.QTRLY NEW ORDERS OF 12,376 HOMES - UP 18%.QTRLY NEW ORDERS DOLLAR VALUE OF $5.0 BILLION - UP 20%.AVERAGE SALES PRICE OF HOMES DELIVERED WAS $402,000 IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020, COMPARED TO $410,000 IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2019.BACKLOG OF 17,632 HOMES - UP 2% AT QUARTER END.
