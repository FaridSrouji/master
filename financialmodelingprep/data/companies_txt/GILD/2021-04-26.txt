Gilead Sciences Announces Steps to Expand Availability of Remdesivir in India. FOSTER CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: GILD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IN RESPONSE TO THE RAPID INCREASE IN COVID-19 CASES IN INDIA, THE COMPANY IS PROVIDING ITS VOLUNTARY LICENSING PARTNERS WITH TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE, SUPPORT FOR THE ADDITION OF NEW LOCAL MANUFACTURING FACILITIES AND THE DONATION OF ACTIVE PHARMACEUTICAL INGREDIENT (API) TO RAPIDLY SCALE UP PRODUCTION OF REMDESIVIR.
