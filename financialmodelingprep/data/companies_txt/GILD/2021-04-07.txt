FDA Approves Trodelvy®, the First Treatment for Metastatic Triple-Negative Breast Cancer Shown to Improve Progression-Free Survival and Overall Survival. FOSTER CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC.
