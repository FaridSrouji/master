Gilead Announces Racial Equity Community Impact Fund to Support Black Communities Across United States. FOSTER CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: GILD) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS RACIAL EQUITY COMMUNITY IMPACT FUND, WHICH WILL SUPPORT HIGH-IMPACT ORGANIZATIONS WORKING TO TACKLE RACIAL INEQUITIES AFFECTING BLACK COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE UNITED STATES. THE FUND WILL INITIALLY PROVIDE $10 MILLION IN GRANTS TO 20 ORGANIZATIONS OVER A THREE-YEAR PERIOD.
