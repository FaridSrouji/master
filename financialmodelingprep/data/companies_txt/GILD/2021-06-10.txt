U.S. Food and Drug Administration Approves New Formulation of Epclusa®, Expanding Pediatric Indication to Treat Children Ages 3 and Older With Chronic Hepatitis C. FOSTER CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GILEAD SCIENCES, INC. (NASDAQ: GILD) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED AN EXPANSION OF THE PEDIATRIC INDICATION OF EPCLUSA® (SOFOSBUVIR/VELPATASVIR) FOR THE TREATMENT OF CHRONIC HEPATITIS C VIRUS (HCV) TO NOW INCLUDE CHILDREN AS YOUNG AS 3 YEARS OF AGE, REGARDLESS OF HCV GENOTYPE OR LIVER DISEASE SEVERITY.
