EMA Recommends Expanding Remdesivir Compassionate Use To Patients Not On Mechanical Ventilation. EMA RECOMMENDS EXPANDING REMDESIVIR COMPASSIONATE USE TO PATIENTS NOT ON MECHANICAL VENTILATION.IN ADDITION, TREATMENT DURATION OF 5 DAYS HAS BEEN INTRODUCED ALONGSIDE THE LONGER 10-DAY COURSE FOR REMDESIVIR.
