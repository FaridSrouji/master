Gilead Sciences Statement On Co's Ongoing Response To 2019 Novel Coronavirus. GILEAD SCIENCES STATEMENT ON COMPANY’S ONGOING RESPONSE TO 2019 NOVEL CORONAVIRUS (2019-NCOV).GILEAD - WORKING WITH HEALTH AUTHORITIES IN CHINA TO ESTABLISH TRIAL TO DETERMINE IF REMDESIVIR CAN SAFELY, EFFECTIVELY BE USED TO TREAT 2019-NCOV.GILEAD - WORKING WITH GLOBAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES TO RESPOND TO CORONAVIRUS OUTBREAK THROUGH EXPERIMENTAL USE OF REMDESIVIR.GILEAD - PROVIDED REMDESIVIR FOR USE IN A SMALL NUMBER OF PATIENTS WITH 2019-NCOV FOR EMERGENCY TREATMENT IN ABSENCE OF ANY APPROVED TREATMENT OPTIONS.GILEAD SCIENCES- EXPEDITING APPROPRIATE LABORATORY TESTING OF INVESTIGATIONAL COMPOUND REMDESIVIR AGAINST 2019-NCOV SAMPLES.
