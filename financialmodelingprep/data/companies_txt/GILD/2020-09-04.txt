Kite Says Yescarta Has Not Been Approved By Any Regulatory Agency. KITE SUBMITS SUPPLEMENTAL BIOLOGICS LICENSE APPLICATION TO U.S. FDA FOR YESCARTA IN RELAPSED OR REFRACTORY INDOLENT NON-HODGKIN LYMPHOMAS.KITE SAYS YESCARTA HAS NOT BEEN APPROVED BY ANY REGULATORY AGENCY FOR TREATMENT OF INDOLENT NON-HODGKIN LYMPHOMA.
