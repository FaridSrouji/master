NIH Says Clinical Trial Of Remdesivir To Treat Covid-19 Begins Study Enrolling. NIH - CLINICAL TRIAL OF REMDESIVIR TO TREAT COVID-19 BEGINS STUDY ENROLLING HOSPITALIZED ADULTS WITH COVID-19 IN NEBRASKA.NIH - ALTHOUGH REMDESIVIR HAS BEEN ADMINISTERED TO SOME PATIENTS WITH COVID-19, DO NOT HAVE SOLID DATA TO INDICATE IT CAN IMPROVE CLINICAL OUTCOMES.NIH - FIRST REMDESIVIR TRIAL PARTICIPANT IS AN AMERICAN WHO WAS REPATRIATED AFTER BEING QUARANTINED ON DIAMOND PRINCESS CRUISE SHIP.NIH - THOSE WITH CONFIRMED SARS-COV-2 INFECTION WHO HAVE MILD, COLD-LIKE SYMPTOMS OR NO APPARENT SYMPTOMS WILL NOT BE INCLUDED IN REMDESIVIR STUDY.NIH - PARTICIPANTS IN REMDESIVIR TRIAL MUST HAVE LABORATORY-CONFIRMED SARS-COV-2 INFECTION AND EVIDENCE OF LUNG INVOLVEMENT.
