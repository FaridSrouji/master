Jubilant Life Sciences Launches Remdesivir For Injection Under Brand Name 'JUBI-R'. JUBILANT LIFE SCIENCES LTD SAYS  UNIT HAS LAUNCHED REMDESIVIR FOR INJECTION UNDER BRAND NAME 'JUBI-R'.JUBILANT LIFE SCIENCES LTD SAYS  LAUNCHED  'JUBI-R' IN INDIAN MARKET AT  4,700 RUPEES PER VIAL OF 100 MG.CO TO MAKE DRUG AVAILABLE TO OVER 1,000 HOSPITALS PROVIDING COVID-19 TREATMENT IN INDIA THROUGH DISTRIBUTION NETWORK.
