Kronos Bio And Gilead Sciences Enter Into Asset Purchase Agreement For Gilead's Syk Inhibitor Portfolio. KRONOS BIO AND GILEAD SCIENCES ENTER INTO ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR GILEAD'S SYK INHIBITOR PORTFOLIO.KRONOS BIO - GILEAD WILL RECEIVE AN UPFRONT CASH PAYMENT AND A NOTE CONVERTIBLE INTO KRONOS EQUITY.KRONOS BIO - GILEAD WILL BE ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE REGULATORY, COMMERCIAL MILESTONES AND ROYALTIES ON FUTURE SALES OF PRODUCTS UNDER DEAL.
