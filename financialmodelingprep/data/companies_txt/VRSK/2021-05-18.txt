National Fire Protection Association and Verisk Collaborate to Promote Importance of Community Wildfire Risk Mitigation. JERSEY CITY, N.J., MAY 18, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MORE THAN 4.5 MILLION U.S. PROPERTIES ARE AT A HIGH-TO-EXTREME RISK OF WILDFIRE, WITH MORE THAN 2 MILLION OF THOSE IN CALIFORNIA ALONE, ACCORDING TO ESTIMATES FROM VERISK , (NASDAQ:VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER. TO HELP PROMOTE THE IMPORTANCE OF BUILDING RESILIENT COMMUNITIES TO PROPERTY INSURERS AND THEIR CUSTOMERS, THE NATIONAL FIRE PROTECTION ASSOCIATION IS PROVIDING VERISK WITH ROBUST DATA ON COMMUNITIES ENGAGING IN WILDFIRE MITIGATION EFFORTS.
