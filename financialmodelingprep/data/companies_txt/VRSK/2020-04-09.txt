Global Oil Demand To Fall Over 8 Mb/d Y-o-Y For Q2 - Wood Mackenzie. EXPECTS WORLD OIL DEMAND TO FALL OVER 8 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY YEAR-ON-YEAR FOR Q2 2020.SAYS APRIL WILL SEE THE SHARPEST DEMAND DROP, WITH A YEAR-ON-YEAR DECLINE OF OVER 15 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY DUE TO CORONAVIRUS CONTAINMENT MEASURES .SAYS THE SECOND HALF OF 2020 WILL SEE A GRADUAL EASING OF THE EXTREME OVERSUPPLY SEEN IN THE FIRST HALF OF THE YEAR AS THE FUNDAMENTALS BEGIN TO SHIFT TOWARDS DEMAND STABILIZATION.FORECASTS TOTAL GLOBAL SUPPLY, INCLUDING OPEC, NON-OPEC, AND NATURAL GAS LIQUIDS, TO SHOW NO GROWTH FOR 2021.SAYS DEMAND, WHILE WEAK, IS PROJECTED TO RISE IN 2021 .SAYS A 10 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY OIL OUTPUT CUT WOULD BE VERY SUPPORTIVE OF PRICE OVER THE SECOND QUARTER.SAYS IT WOULD SLOW THE BUILD-UP IN STORAGE AND AVOID THE SURPLUS OF SUPPLY OVER THE SECOND QUARTER.
