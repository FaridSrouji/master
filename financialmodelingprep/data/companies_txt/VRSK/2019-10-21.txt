EagleView to Broaden Customer Base After Court Orders Permanent Injunction Against Xactware, Verisk for Willful Infringement of Eagleview Patents. EagleView announced that on October 18, 2019, Judge Renee Marie Bumb for the U.S. District Court of New Jersey ruled that a permanent injunction against Xactware Solutions, Inc. and Verisk Analytics, Inc. will take effect immediately to protect EagleView's proprietary intellectual property (IP) and prevent Xactware and Verisk's further willful infringement of EagleView's technology.
