Property Carriers Beat Fraud with New Prior Loss Images in Verisk's ISO ClaimSearch®. JERSEY CITY, N.J., JAN. 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NASDAQ:VRSK) ANNOUNCED THAT ISO CLAIMSEARCH , THE WORLD'S LARGEST DATABASE OF PROPERTY AND CASUALTY INSURANCE (P&C) CLAIMS, HAS ENHANCED ITS ROBUST CLAIM INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM BY ADDING PRIOR LOSS IMAGE MATCHING. THIS AUTOMATES PREVIOUSLY LABORIOUS PROCESSES AND SIGNIFICANTLY BOLSTERS FRAUD PROTECTION.
