Verisk Announces Leadership Changes to Support Long-Term Growth Strategy. JERSEY CITY, N.J., FEB. 18, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VERISK (NYSE:VRSK), A LEADING GLOBAL DATA ANALYTICS PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED LEADERSHIP CHANGES AND EXPANDED RESPONSIBILITIES FOR MEMBERS OF ITS EXECUTIVE MANAGEMENT TEAM. THE CHANGES REINFORCE VERISK'S COMMITMENT TO BUILDING COHESIVE LEADERSHIP TEAMS THAT ACCELERATE LONG-TERM GROWTH.
