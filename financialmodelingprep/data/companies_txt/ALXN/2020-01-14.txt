Alexion Announces Planned Initiation Of Pivotal Phase 3 Study Of UltomirisIn ALS. ALEXION ANNOUNCES PLANNED INITIATION OF PIVOTAL PHASE 3 STUDY OF ULTOMIRIS® (RAVULIZUMAB) IN ALS.ALEXION PHARMACEUTICALS - PHASE 3 STUDY OF ULTOMIRIS (RAVULIZUMAB) IN ALS WILL BE 50-WEEK GLOBAL STUDY WHICH WILL EVALUATE APPROXIMATELY 350 ADULTS.ALEXION PHARMACEUTICALS INC - PLANS TO INITIATE PHASE 3 STUDY OF ULTOMIRIS (RAVULIZUMAB) IN ALS THIS QUARTER.
