Alexion Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (NasdaqGS:ALXN) entered into an asset purchase agreement to acquire certain assets of Immune Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (OTCPK:IMNP.Q) for $6 million.. Alexion Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (NasdaqGS:ALXN) entered into an asset purchase agreement to acquire certain assets of Immune Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (OTCPK:IMNP.Q) for $6 million on October 21, 2019. The transaction has been approved by the US Bankruptcy Court. On November 25, 2019 the transaction has received all requisite court approvals. The closing of the transaction is expected prior to the end of first quarter of 2020.
