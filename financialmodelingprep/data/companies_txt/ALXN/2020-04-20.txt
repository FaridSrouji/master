Alexion Announces Plans To Initiate Phase 3 Study Of Ultomiris® In Hospitalized Patients With Severe Covid-19. ALEXION ANNOUNCES PLANS TO INITIATE PHASE 3 STUDY OF ULTOMIRIS® (RAVULIZUMAB-CWVZ) IN HOSPITALIZED PATIENTS WITH SEVERE COVID-19.ALEXION PHARMACEUTICALS - GLOBAL STUDY TO ENROLL APPROXIMATELY 270 ADULTS WITH COVID-19 AND SEVERE PNEUMONIA OR ACUTE RESPIRATORY DISTRESS SYNDROME.ALEXION PHARMACEUTICALS INC - MAINTAINS COMMITMENT TO SUPPLYING ITS MEDICINES TO PATIENTS FOR CURRENTLY APPROVED INDICATIONS.
