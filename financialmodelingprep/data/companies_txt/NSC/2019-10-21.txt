Norfolk Southern Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on Dec. 10. Norfolk Southern Corporation announced a regular quarterly dividend of 94 cents per share on its common stock. The dividend is payable Dec. 10 to shareholders of record on Nov. 1.
