Norfolk Southern Corporation Raises USD 400 Million in Bonds Offering Due 2049. Norfolk Southern Corporation has raised USD 400 million in corporate bonds offering. The bonds will carry an interest of 3.40% per annum. The notes will mature on November 1, 2049.
