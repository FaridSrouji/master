Norfolk Southern Enters 5-Year, $800 Million, Unsecured Revolving Credit Facility. NORFOLK SOUTHERN- ON MARCH 27, ENTERED INTO AN AGREEMENT, ESTABLISHING A 5-YEAR, $800 MILLION, UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.NORFOLK SOUTHERN- AGREEMENT INCLUDES A SUBFACILITY FOR SWINGLINE LOANS IN AN AGGREGATE AMOUNT OF UP TO $100 MILLION.NORFOLK SOUTHERN- AGREEMENT REPLACES CO'S EXISTING $750 MILLION CREDIT FACILITY UNDER AN AGREEMENT, DATED AS OF MAY 26, 2016.NORFOLK SOUTHERN- NO DIRECT FINANCIAL OBLIGATIONS OF THE CO HAVE ARISEN UNDER THE AGREEMENT AS OF THE DATE HEREOF..
