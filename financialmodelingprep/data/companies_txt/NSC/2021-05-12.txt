Norfolk Southern issues $500 million of green bonds to fund sustainable business initiatives. NORFOLK, VA., MAY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NORFOLK SOUTHERN CORPORATION (NYSE: NSC) TODAY CLOSED A $500 MILLION GREEN BOND OFFERING TO FUND INVESTMENTS TO REDUCE THE COMPANY'S CARBON EMISSIONS AND HELP CUSTOMERS REDUCE THEIR SUPPLY-CHAIN EMISSIONS.
