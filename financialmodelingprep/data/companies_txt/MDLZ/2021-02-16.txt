Mondelēz International Confirms Long-Term Strategy and Details Opportunities to Drive Sustained Growth at 2021 CAGNY Conference. CHICAGO, FEB. 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) WILL TODAY DEMONSTRATE CLEAR PROGRESS AGAINST ITS LONG-TERM STRATEGIC PLAN AT THE 2021 CONSUMER ANALYST GROUP OF NEW YORK (CAGNY) CONFERENCE. CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER DIRK VAN DE PUT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER LUCA ZARAMELLA WILL DETAIL HOW THE COMPANY PLANS TO CONTINUE TO GENERATE SUSTAINABLE SHAREHOLDER VALUE.
