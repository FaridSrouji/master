Mondelēz International Acquires Hu, A Well-being Snacking Company. CHICAGO, JAN. 05, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS ACQUIRED HU (AS IN “HUMAN”) MASTER HOLDINGS, THE PARENT COMPANY OF HU PRODUCTS, A FAST-GROWING US-BASED SNACKING COMPANY OFFERING HIGH-QUALITY SNACKS MADE FROM SIMPLE INGREDIENTS.
