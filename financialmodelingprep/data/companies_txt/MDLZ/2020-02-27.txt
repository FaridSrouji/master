Mondelez Says On Feb 26, Entered Into Revolving Credit Agreement For 364-Day Senior Unsecured Revolving Credit Facility In Principal Amount Of $1.5 Bln. MONDELEZ - ON FEB 26, ENTERED INTO REVOLVING CREDIT AGREEMENT FOR 364-DAY SENIOR UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY IN PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $1.5 BILLION.MONDELEZ - 364-DAY REVOLVING CREDIT AGREEMENT WILL TERMINATE ON FEBRUARY 24, 2021.MONDELEZ - 364-DAY REVOLVING CREDIT AGREEMENT REQUIRES US TO MAINTAIN A MINIMUM SHAREHOLDERS' EQUITY OF NOT LESS THAN $24.6 BILLION.
