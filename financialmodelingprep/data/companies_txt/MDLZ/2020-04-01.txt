Mondelez Quarterly Says CEO's FY 2019 Total Compensation Was $18.1 Mln Vs About $15 Mln In FY 2018 - SEC Filing. MONDELEZ QUARTERLY SAYS CEO DIRK VAN DE PUT'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $18.1 MILLION VERSUS ABOUT $15 MILLION IN FY 2018 - SEC FILING.MONDELEZ QUARTERLY SAYS CFO LUCA ZARAMELLA'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $5.0 MILLION VERSUS $3.4 MILLION IN FY 2018.MONDELEZ SAYS FOR 2019, CEO PAY RATIO WAS 561 TO 1.
