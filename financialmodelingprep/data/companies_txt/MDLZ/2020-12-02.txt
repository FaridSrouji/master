Mondelēz International Declares Quarterly Dividend and Authorizes Additional $4 Billion of Share Repurchases. CHICAGO, DEC. 02, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.315 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JANUARY 14, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2020.
