Mondelēz International Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend of $0.315 per share. CHICAGO, MAY 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF MONDELĒZ INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MDLZ) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.315 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JULY 14, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 30, 2021.
