Mondelez International, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Mondelez International, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 6,355 million compared to USD 6,288 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 876 million compared to USD 737 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,423 million compared to USD 1,194 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.98 compared to USD 0.81 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 18,955 million compared to USD 19,165 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,937 million compared to USD 2,442 million a year ago. Net income was USD 3,144 million compared to USD 2,558 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.17 compared to USD 1.73 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.15 compared to USD 1.72 a year ago.
