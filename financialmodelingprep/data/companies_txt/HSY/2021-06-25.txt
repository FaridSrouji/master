Hershey Completes Acquisition of Lily's Confectionery Brand. HERSHEY, PA., JUNE 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF ITS ACQUISITION OF LILY'S, THE HIGH-GROWTH MAKER OF LOW-SUGAR, BETTER-FOR-YOU (BFY) CONFECTIONERY PRODUCTS, FOR A PURCHASE PRICE OF $425 MILLION.
