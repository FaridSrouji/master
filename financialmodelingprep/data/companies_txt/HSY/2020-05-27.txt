Hershey Announces Pricing Of Notes. THE HERSHEY COMPANY ANNOUNCES PRICING OF $300,000,000 0.900% NOTES DUE 2025, $350,000,000 1.700% NOTES DUE 2030 AND $350,000,000 2.650% NOTES DUE 2050.
Hershey Says Businesses Saw Minimal Covid-19 Impact During Q1 2020. HERSHEY CO - BUSINESSES SAW MINIMAL COVID-19 IMPACT DURING Q1 2020.HERSHEY - EXPECT MORE SIGNIFICANT IMPACT IN Q2 FROM COVID-19 GIVEN SHELTER-IN-PLACE RESTRICTIONS THAT WERE IMPLEMENTED IN LATE MARCH & EARLY APRIL.HERSHEY - CONTINUE TO SEE DECLINES IN CO'S FOOD SERVICE, OWNED RETAIL & WORLD TRAVEL RETAIL BUSINESSES & CERTAIN PARTS OF INTERNATIONAL BUSINESSES.HERSHEY - SALES IN GUM & MINT CATEGORY HAVE ALSO BEEN SIGNIFICANTLY IMPACTED BY SOCIAL DISTANCING PROTOCOLS.HERSHEY - INCURRING INCREMENTAL COSTS IN CERTAIN AREAS, SUCH AS FRONT-LINE EMPLOYEE COMPENSATION & INCENTIVES, ENHANCED SAFETY MEASURES AMONG OTHERS.HERSHEY -  IMPACT THAT COVID-19 WILL HAVE ON CO'S CONSOLIDATED RESULTS OF OPERATIONS THROUGHOUT 2020 REMAINS UNCERTAIN.HERSHEY - INVENTORY LEVELS AT SEVERAL KEY RETAILERS REMAIN BELOW HISTORIC AVERAGES.
The Hershey Company Announces Offering Of Notes. THE HERSHEY COMPANY ANNOUNCES OFFERING OF NOTES.HERSHEY - INTENDS TO USE PROCEEDS OF NOTES OFFERING TO REPAY PORTION OF COMMERCIAL PAPER IT HAS ISSUED, PAY FEES AND EXPENSES.
