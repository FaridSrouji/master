Hershey Reports Q1 Adjusted Earnings Per Share Of $1.63. HERSHEY REPORTS FIRST-QUARTER 2020 FINANCIAL RESULTS.Q1 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.63.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.29.Q1 SALES ROSE 1 PERCENT TO $2.037 BILLION.TO WITHDRAW FISCAL 2020 FULL-YEAR GUIDANCE.REAFFIRMS LONG-TERM FINANCIAL OBJECTIVES OF NET SALES GROWTH IN RANGE OF 2% TO 4% AND AN INCREASE IN EARNINGS PER SHARE OF 6% TO 8%.BELIEVES IT HAS SUFFICIENT LIQUIDITY TO SATISFY ITS CASH NEEDS.HERSHEY'S NORTH AMERICA NET SALES WERE $1,844.8 MILLION IN Q1 OF 2020, AN INCREASE OF 2.1%.FIRST-QUARTER 2020 NET SALES FOR HERSHEY'S INTERNATIONAL AND OTHER SEGMENT DECREASED 8.1% VERSUS SAME PERIOD LAST YEAR TO $192.5 MILLION."OVERALL, COMPANY'S Q1 PERFORMANCE WAS RELATIVELY IN LINE WITH EXPECTATIONS, WITH A MODEST IMPACT FROM COVID-19".Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.71, REVENUE VIEW $2.08 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
