Hershey Selects Horizon Media As Agency Of Record For U.S. Media. HERSHEY, PA., APRIL 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE HERSHEY COMPANY (NYSE: HSY) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THEY HAVE SELECTED HORIZON MEDIA AS AGENCY OF RECORD FOR THE COMPANY'S U.S. CONFECTIONARY BUSINESS.
