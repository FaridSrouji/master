Kimco Realty Says CEO Conor Flynn Tested Positive For Covid-19. KIMCO REALTY -  ON APRIL 3,CO ANNOUNCED THAT CONOR FLYNN, CO'S CEO, TESTED POSITIVE FOR CORONAVIRUS DISEASE 2019 (COVID-19).KIMCO REALTY CORP - FLYNN HAS CONTINUED TO PERFORM HIS RESPONSIBILITIES AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AT ALL TIMES.KIMCO REALTY - FULL SUCCESSION PLANS FOR SENIOR EXECUTIVES INCLUDING TEMPORARY DELEGATION OF RESPONSIBILITIES TO OTHER LEADERS WITHIN ORGANIZATION.
