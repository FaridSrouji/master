Catalent, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the First Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Catalent, Inc. announced earnings results for the first quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the first quarter, the company announced sales was USD 664.7 million compared to USD 551.8 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 34.4 million compared to USD 20.4 million a year ago. Net income was USD 0.1 million compared to net loss of USD 14.4 million a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 0.05 compared to USD 0.1 a year ago.
