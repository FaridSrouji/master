Catalent Signs Agreement With Johnson & Johnson To Be U.S. Manufacturing Partner For Lead Covid-19 Vaccine Candidate. CATALENT SIGNS AGREEMENT WITH JOHNSON & JOHNSON TO BE U.S. MANUFACTURING PARTNER FOR LEAD COVID-19 VACCINE CANDIDATE.CATALENT - COLLABORATION INCLUDES JOINT INVESTMENT & TECH TRANSFER TO PREPARE FOR RAPID SCALE-UP AND SEGREGATED CGMP COMMERCIAL MANUFACTURING CAPACITY.CATALENT INC - CATALENT TO HIRE 300 ADDITIONAL PERSONNEL TO MEET OPERATIONAL READINESS AND 24X7 MANUFACTURING SCHEDULES.
