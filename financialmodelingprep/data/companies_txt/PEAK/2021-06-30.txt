Healthpeak Properties Prices $450 Million of 1.350% Senior Unsecured Notes due 2027 in Its Inaugural Green Bond Offering. DENVER, JUNE 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY PRICED ITS FIRST GREEN BOND OFFERING, A PUBLIC OFFERING OF $450 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 1.350% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES DUE 2027. THE PRICE TO INVESTORS WAS 99.877% OF THE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF THE NOTES.
