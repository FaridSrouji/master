Healthpeak Properties® Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend on Common Stock. DENVER, APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HEALTHPEAK PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: PEAK) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON STOCK CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.30 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON MAY 21, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 10, 2021.
