CF Industries Signs Engineering and Procurement Contract with thyssenkrupp for Green Ammonia Project. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CF INDUSTRIES HAS SIGNED AN ENGINEERING AND PROCUREMENT CONTRACT WITH THYSSENKRUPP FOR THE COMPANY'S GREEN AMMONIA PROJECT IN LOUISIANA.
