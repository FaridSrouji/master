Cf Industries Holdings, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. CF Industries Holdings, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 1,038 million compared to USD 1,040 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 188 million compared to USD 136 million a year ago. Net income was USD 65 million compared to USD 30 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.29 compared to USD 0.13 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 3,541 million compared to USD 3,297 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 828 million compared to USD 571 million a year ago. Net income was USD 438 million compared to USD 241 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.98 compared to USD 1.03 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.97 compared to USD 1.03 a year ago.
