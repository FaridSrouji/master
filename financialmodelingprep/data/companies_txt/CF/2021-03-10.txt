Deborah L. DeHaas Nominated for Election to Board of Directors of CF Industries Holdings, Inc.. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CF INDUSTRIES HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE: CF) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT DEBORAH L. DEHAAS, FORMER VICE CHAIRMAN AND MANAGING PARTNER OF THE CENTER FOR BOARD EFFECTIVENESS AT DELOITTE, HAS BEEN NOMINATED FOR ELECTION TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS AT ITS ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS TO BE HELD ON MAY 4, 2021. “WE ARE THRILLED AT THE OPPORTUNITY TO HAVE DEB JOIN THE CF INDUSTRIES BOARD,” SAID STEPHEN A. FURBACHER, CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD, CF INDUSTRIES HOLDINGS, INC. “
