Comerica Bank's California Index Moves Up. DALLAS, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX INCREASED IN FEBRUARY TO A LEVEL OF 105.9. FEBRUARY'S READING IS 14 PERCENT ABOVE THE HISTORICAL LOW OF 92.7 SET IN JUNE 2020.
Comerica Bank's Texas Index Declines. DALLAS, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK'S TEXAS ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX DECREASED IN FEBRUARY TO 114.1. FEBRUARY'S INDEX READING WAS 15 PERCENT ABOVE THE RECENT LOW OF 99.5 SET IN JUNE 2020.
Comerica Bank's Michigan Index Climbs. DALLAS, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK'S MICHIGAN ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX INCREASED IN FEBRUARY TO A LEVEL OF 104.0. FEBRUARY'S READING WAS 21 PERCENT HIGHER THAN THE HISTORICAL LOW REACHED IN JUNE 2020.
