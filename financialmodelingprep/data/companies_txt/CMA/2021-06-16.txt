Comerica Bank Recognized as One of the 50 Most Community-Minded Companies in the United States for Sixth Consecutive Year. DALLAS, JUNE 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK AND ITS ONGOING COMMITMENT TO IMPROVING THE COMMUNITIES IT SERVES RECEIVED NATIONAL RECOGNITION AS IT HAS BEEN NAMED A 2021 HONOREE OF THE CIVIC 50 BY POINTS OF LIGHT, THE WORLD'S LARGEST ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO VOLUNTEER SERVICE. THE CIVIC 50 PROVIDES A NATIONAL STANDARD FOR CORPORATE CITIZENSHIP AND SHOWCASES HOW COMPANIES CAN USE THEIR TIME, SKILLS AND RESOURCES TO DRIVE SOCIAL IMPACT IN THEIR COMMUNITIES AND COMPANY.
