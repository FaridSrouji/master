Comerica Bank Names Lisa Featherngill National Director of Wealth Planning. DALLAS, APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT LISA FEATHERNGILL HAS BEEN NAMED SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, NATIONAL DIRECTOR OF WEALTH PLANNING. FEATHERNGILL WILL JOIN COMERICA ON APRIL 19, 2021, AND WILL REPORT TO GREG CARR, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, WEALTH MANAGEMENT.
