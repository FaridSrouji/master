Comerica Declares Dividends; Announces Increase in Share Repurchase Authorization and Results from Annual Shareholders' Meeting. DALLAS, APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA INCORPORATED (NYSE: CMA) ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND FOR COMMON STOCK OF 68 CENTS ($0.68) PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JULY 1, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 15, 2021.
