Comerica Bank's California Index Climbs. DALLAS, JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK'S CALIFORNIA ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX INCREASED IN MARCH TO A LEVEL OF 108.0. MARCH'S READING IS 17 PERCENT ABOVE THE HISTORICAL LOW OF 92.7 SET IN JUNE 2020.
Comerica Bank's Texas Index Increases. DALLAS, JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK'S TEXAS ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX INCREASED IN MARCH TO 115.3. MARCH'S INDEX READING WAS 16 PERCENT ABOVE THE RECENT LOW OF 99.3 SET IN JUNE 2020.
Comerica Bank's Michigan Index Ticks Higher. DALLAS, JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK'S MICHIGAN ECONOMIC ACTIVITY INDEX INCREASED IN MARCH TO A LEVEL OF 104.9. MARCH'S READING WAS 23 PERCENT HIGHER THAN THE HISTORICAL LOW REACHED IN JUNE 2020.
