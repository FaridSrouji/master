Comerica Adds Over $1 Million in Investments to Strengthen California Nonprofits. DALLAS, DEC. 18, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMERICA BANK ANNOUNCED MORE THAN $1 MILLION IN CONTRIBUTIONS SPLIT AMONG SIX CALIFORNIA NONPROFITS HELPING TO FACILITATE CRITICAL SUPPORT FOR MINORITY-OWNED SMALL BUSINESSES AND LOW- TO MODERATE-INCOME COMMUNITIES IMPACTED BY THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC....
