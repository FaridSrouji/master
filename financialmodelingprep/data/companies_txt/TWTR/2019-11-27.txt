Twitter says effort to delete inactive accounts impacts ones in EU only for now. TWITTER SAYS EFFORT TO DELETE INACTIVE ACCOUNTS IMPACTS THE ONES IN THE EU ONLY, FOR NOW.TWITTER SAYS ALWAYS HAD AN INACTIVE ACCOUNT POLICY BUT HAVEN’T ENFORCED IT CONSISTENTLY; STARTING WITH EU IN PART DUE TO LOCAL PRIVACY REGULATIONS.TWITTER SAYS WILL NOT BE REMOVING ANY INACTIVE ACCOUNTS UNTIL CO CREATES A NEW WAY FOR PEOPLE TO MEMORIALIZE ACCOUNTS.TWITTER SAYS MAY BROADEN ENFORCEMENT OF INACTIVITY POLICY IN FUTURE TO COMPLY WITH OTHER REGULATIONS AROUND THE WORLD.
