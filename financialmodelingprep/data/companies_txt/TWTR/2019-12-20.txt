Twitter Says Fixed Vulnerability Within Twitter For Android That Could Allow Bad Actor To See Nonpublic Account Info. TWITTER SAYS RECENTLY FIXED VULNERABILITY WITHIN TWITTER FOR ANDROID THAT COULD ALLOW BAD ACTOR TO SEE NONPUBLIC ACCOUNT INFO OR TO CONTROL ACCOUNT.TWITTER SAYS DON'T HAVE EVIDENCE THAT MALICIOUS CODE WAS INSERTED INTO THE APP OR THAT THE VULNERABILITY WAS EXPLOITED.
