Bigg Digital Assets Says Is First To Announce Routing Of Funds From Twitter Hack To Known Exchanges. BIGG DIGITAL ASSETS INC SAYS IS FIRST TO ANNOUNCE ROUTING OF FUNDS FROM TWITTER HACK TO KNOWN EXCHANGES.BIGG DIGITAL ASSETS- SOME FUNDS FRAUDULENTLY OBTAINED AS PART OF TWITTER HACK SEEMINGLY TRANSFERRED TO ADDRESS POSSIBLY LINKED TO HACK.BIGG DIGITAL ASSETS-SOME FUNDS FRAUDULENTLY OBTAINED AS PART OF TWITTER HACK SEEMINGLY SPLIT AND THEN SENT TO A FEW DIFFERENT KYC REGULATED EXCHANGES.BIGG DIGITAL ASSETS- FUNDS (ABOUT USD$13K) WERE NOTED AS HAVING GONE TO LUNO EXCHANGE IN UK, WHILE SMALLER AMOUNT WAS TRACED TO AN EXCHANGE IN INDIA.BIGG DIGITAL ASSETS- OTHER SPLITS OF PILFERED BITCOIN INCLUDED ORIGINAL AMOUNT VALUED AT $7,007, DIVIDED ALMOST EQUALLY BETWEEN TWO OTHER WALLETS.BIGG DIGITAL ASSETS - THIRD TRANSACTION COMPRISED A NOMINAL AMOUNT OF BTC VALUED AT USD$9.99 THAT MOVED THROUGH AN ADDRESS POSSIBLY HELD BY BINANCE.BIGG DIGITAL ASSETS INC- BLOCKCHAIN INTELLIGENCE GROUP HAS BEEN IN COMMUNICATION WITH FBI REGARDING THIS CASE AND OUR INVESTIGATION RESULTS.
Twitter Says Have No Evidence That Attackers of Recent Security Incident Accessed Passwords. DON’T BELIEVE RESETTING PASSWORD IS NECESSARY; HAVE NO EVIDENCE THAT ATTACKERS ACCESSED PASSWORDS.TOOK STEP TO LOCK ANY ACCOUNTS THAT HAD ATTEMPTED TO CHANGE THE ACCOUNT’S PASSWORD DURING THE PAST 30 DAYS.IF YOUR ACCOUNT WAS LOCKED, THIS DOES NOT NECESSARILY MEAN TWITTER HAS EVIDENCE THAT THE ACCOUNT WAS COMPROMISED OR ACCESSED.SO FAR, BELIEVES ONLY SMALL SUBSET OF LOCKED ACCOUNTS WERE COMPROMISED, STILL INVESTIGATING AND WILL INFORM THOSE WHO WERE AFFECTED.OTHER THAN ACCOUNTS THAT ARE STILL LOCKED, PEOPLE SHOULD BE ABLE TO RESET THEIR PASSWORD NOW.
Twitter Says Given Security Incident On Wednesday, Co Pausing Twitter API Launch Planned For Thursday - Blog. TWITTER SAYS GIVEN SECURITY INCIDENT ON WEDNESDAY, CO PAUSING TWITTER API LAUNCH PLANNED FOR THURSDAY - BLOG.TWITTER SAYS FOUND NO EVIDENCE THE INCIDENT HAD ANYTHING TO DO WITH API, BUT DECIDED TO MOVE THE LAUNCH TO MORE APPROPRIATE TIME.
Twitter Says Detected What We Believe To Be A Coordinated Social Engineering Attack. TWITTER - DETECTED WHAT WE BELIEVE TO BE A COORDINATED SOCIAL ENGINEERING ATTACK BY PEOPLE WHO SUCCESSFULLY TARGETED SOME EMPLOYEES.TWITTER - COORDINATED SOCIAL ENGINEERING ATTACK BY PEOPLE SUCCESSFULLY TARGETED SOME OF CO'S EMPLOYEES WITH ACCESS TO INTERNAL SYSTEMS AND TOOLS.TWITTER - LOOKING INTO WHAT OTHER MALICIOUS ACTIVITY THEY MAY HAVE CONDUCTED OR INFO THEY MAY HAVE ACCESSED.TWITTER - INTERNALLY, WE’VE TAKEN SIGNIFICANT STEPS TO LIMIT ACCESS TO INTERNAL SYSTEMS AND TOOLS WHILE OUR INVESTIGATION IS ONGOING.TWITTER - ONCE CO BECAME AWARE OF INCIDENT, IMMEDIATELY LOCKED DOWN THE AFFECTED ACCOUNTS AND REMOVED TWEETS POSTED BY THE ATTACKERS.TWITTER - LOCKED ACCOUNTS THAT WERE COMPROMISED AND WILL RESTORE ACCESS TO ORIGINAL ACCOUNT OWNER ONLY WHEN WE ARE CERTAIN WE CAN DO SO SECURELY.
