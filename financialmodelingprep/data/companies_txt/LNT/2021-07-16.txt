Alliant Energy Corporation Announces Second Quarter 2021 Earnings Release and Conference Call. MADISON, WIS., JULY 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LNT) HAS SCHEDULED ITS SECOND QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS RELEASE FOR THURSDAY, AUGUST 5TH, AFTER MARKET CLOSE. A CONFERENCE CALL TO REVIEW THE SECOND QUARTER RESULTS IS SCHEDULED FOR FRIDAY, AUGUST 6TH AT 9 A.M. CT.
