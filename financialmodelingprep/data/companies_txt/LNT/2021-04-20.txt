Alliant Energy Corporation declares quarterly common stock dividend. MADISON, WIS., APRIL 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LNT) BOARD OF DIRECTORS TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.4025 PER SHARE PAYABLE ON MAY 17, 2021, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 30, 2021.
