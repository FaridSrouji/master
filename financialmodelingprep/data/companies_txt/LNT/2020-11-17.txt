Alliant Energy Finance, LLC Announces Pricing of Senior Notes Offering. MADISON, WIS., NOV. 17, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ALLIANT ENERGY FINANCE, LLC (“AEF”), A WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF ALLIANT ENERGY CORPORATION (THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ:LNT) ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF A PRIVATE OFFERING OF $200 MILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 1.400% SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES. THE NOTES WILL BE DUE ON MARCH 15, 2026. THE CLOSING OF THE OFFERING IS EXPECTED TO OCCUR ON NOVEMBER 20, 2020, SUBJECT TO CUSTOMARY CLOSING CONDITIONS. THE COMPANY WILL FULLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY GUARANTEE THE NOTES ON A SENIOR UNSECURED BASIS.
