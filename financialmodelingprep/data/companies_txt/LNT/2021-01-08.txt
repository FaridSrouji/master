Alliant Energy named to Newsweek's America's Most Responsible Companies 2021 List. MADISON, WIS., JAN. 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ALLIANT ENERGY HAS BEEN NAMED TO NEWSWEEK'S 2021 LIST OF AMERICA'S MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES. THIS PRESTIGIOUS LIST IS PRESENTED BY NEWSWEEK AND STATISTA INC., THE WORLD-LEADING STATISTICS PORTAL AND INDUSTRY RANKING PROVIDER. THE LIST, WHICH SPANS 14 INDUSTRIES, RECOGNIZES THE TOP 400 MOST RESPONSIBLE COMPANIES IN THE UNITED STATES.
