Alliant Energy Corporation Declares Quarterly Common Stock Dividend, Payable on November 15, 2019. The Board of Directors of Alliant Energy Corporation declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.355 per share, payable on November 15, 2019, to shareowners of record as of the close of business on October 31, 2019.
