Alliant Energy Corporation Increases Annual Common Stock Dividend Target for 2020. Alliant Energy Corporation's Board of Directors approved a 7% increase, or $0.10 per share, to its 2020 expected annual common stock dividend target of $1.52 per share from the current annual common stock dividend target of $1.42 per share. Payment of the 2020 quarterly dividend is subject to the actual dividend declaration by the Board of Directors each quarter, which is expected in January 2020 for the first quarter dividend.
