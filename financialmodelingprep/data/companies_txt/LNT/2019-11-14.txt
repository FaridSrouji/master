Alliant Energy Expects To Enter Forward Sale Agreement With Barclays Bank. ALLIANT ENERGY - EXPECTS TO ENTER INTO FORWARD SALE AGREEMENT WITH BARCLAYS BANK PLC, IN RESPECT OF OFFERING $195.6 MILLION OF SHARES OF CO'S COMMON STOCK.ALLIANT ENERGY - WILL NOT INITIALLY RECEIVE ANY PROCEEDS FROM SALE OF $195.6 MILLION OF SHARES OF CO'S COMMON STOCK SOLD BY FORWARD COUNTERPARTY TO UNDERWRITER.
