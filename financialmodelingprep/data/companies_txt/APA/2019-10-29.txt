Apache Corp. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Apache Corp. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 1,477 million compared to USD 1,983 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 170 million compared to net income of USD 81 million a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 0.45 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 0.21 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 4,715 million compared to USD 5,659 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 577 million compared to net income of USD 421 million a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 1.53 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 1.1 a year ago. Diluted loss per share was USD 1.53 compared to diluted earnings per share of USD 1.09 a year ago.
