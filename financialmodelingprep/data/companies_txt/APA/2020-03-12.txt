Apache Corp Announces Revised Capital Guidance And Dividend Reduction. APACHE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES REVISED CAPITAL GUIDANCE AND DIVIDEND REDUCTION.APACHE CORP - REDUCED ITS 2020 CAPITAL INVESTMENT PLAN TO A RANGE OF $1.0 BILLION TO $1.2 BILLION.APACHE CORP - OVER COMING WEEKS, COMPANY WILL REDUCE ITS PERMIAN RIG COUNT TO ZERO.APACHE CORP - ACTIVITY REDUCTIONS ARE ALSO PLANNED IN EGYPT AND NORTH SEA.APACHE CORP - APPROVED A REDUCTION IN COMPANY'S QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PER SHARE FROM $0.25 TO $0.025.APACHE CORP - IN SURINAME, ON CONCLUSION OF OPERATIONS AT SAPAKARA WEST-1 EXPLORATION WELL, WILL PROCEED, AS PLANNED, TO A THIRD EXPLORATION PROSPECT.APACHE CORP - WILL USE $340 MILLION OF CASH RETAINED ANNUALLY FROM DIVIDEND REDUCTION TO FURTHER STRENGTHEN ITS FINANCIAL POSITION.APACHE CORP - HAS AMPLE LIQUIDITY THROUGH $4 BILLION UNDRAWN REVOLVER, FLEXIBILITY TO MANAGE $937 MILLION OF BONDS MATURING BETWEEN FEB 2021 AND JAN 2023.APACHE CORP - SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCING PLANNED RIG COUNT AND WELL COMPLETIONS FOR REMAINDER OF YEAR.APACHE CORP - CAPITAL SPENDING PLAN WILL REMAIN FLEXIBLE BASED ON MARKET CONDITIONS.APACHE CORP - FURTHER REDUCING OPERATING AND OVERHEAD COSTS AS CO CONTINUES TO IMPLEMENT CORPORATE REDESIGN PROGRAM.APACHE CORP - ANNOUNCED MULTIPLE ACTIONS BEING TAKEN IN RESPONSE TO CURRENT OIL PRICE ENVIRONMENT.APACHE CORP - REDUCED 2020 CAPITAL INVESTMENT PLAN TO RANGE OF $1.0 BILLION TO $1.2 BILLION FROM PREVIOUS RANGE OF $1.6 BILLION TO $1.9 BILLION.
