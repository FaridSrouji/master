Apache Corporation Announces Fourth Consecutive Oil Discovery in Block 58 Offshore Suriname at Keskesi East-1 Well. HOUSTON, JAN. 14, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- APACHE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: APA) AND TOTAL S.A.
