Apache Corporation Announces Exclusive Listing On Nasdaq. APACHE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES EXCLUSIVE LISTING ON NASDAQ.APACHE CORP - ELECTED TO LIST ITS COMMON STOCK SOLELY ON NASDAQ STOCK MARKET BEGINNING JUNE 9, 2020.APACHE CORP - WILL DISCONTINUE LISTING ITS COMMON STOCK ON NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE AND CHICAGO STOCK EXCHANGE AFTER MARKET CLOSES ON JUNE 8.APACHE CORP - WILL DISCONTINUE LISTING ITS 7.75% NOTES DUE 2029 FROM NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE.
