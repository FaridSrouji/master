Apache Announces Major Oil Discovery In Block 58 Offshore Suriname At Kwaskwasi-1. APACHE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES MAJOR OIL DISCOVERY IN BLOCK 58 OFFSHORE SURINAME AT KWASKWASI-1.APACHE CORP - KWASKWASI-1 WELL ENCOUNTERED 278 METERS (912 FEET) OF NET OIL AND VOLATILE OIL / GAS CONDENSATE PAY..APACHE CORP - FOURTH EXPLORATION PROSPECT IN BLOCK 58, KESKESI EAST-1, WILL BE DRILLED IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING CONCLUSION OF OPERATIONS AT KWASKWASI..APACHE CORP - KWASKWASI-1 WAS DRILLED TO A DEPTH OF APPROXIMATELY 6,645 METERS (21,800 FEET).APACHE - PRELIMINARY FLUID SAMPLES, TEST RESULTS INDICATE AT LEAST 278 METERS OF NET OIL AND VOLATILE OIL / GAS CONDENSATE PAY IN TWO INTERVALS.APACHE - SHALLOWER CAMPANIAN INTERVAL CONTAINS 63 METERS (207 FEET) OF NET OIL PAY AND 86 METERS (282 FEET) OF NET VOLATILE OIL / GAS CONDENSATE PAY.APACHE CORP - BASED ON SAMPLES TAKEN, API OIL GRAVITIES ARE BETWEEN 34 AND 43 DEGREES..APACHE CORP - UPON COMPLETION OF OPERATIONS AT KWASKWASI-1, SAM CROFT WILL MOVE TO FOURTH PROSPECT IN BLOCK 58, KESKESI.
