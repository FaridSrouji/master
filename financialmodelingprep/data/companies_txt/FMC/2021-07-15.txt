FMC Corporation appoints Zack Zaki to lead investor relations. PHILADELPHIA, JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC), A LEADING GLOBAL AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED ABIZAR "ZACK" ZAKI HAS BEEN NAMED INVESTOR RELATIONS DIRECTOR.  "ZACK IS AN EXCEPTIONAL LEADER WITH BROAD EXPERIENCE ACROSS SEVERAL AREAS OF FMC," SAID MARK DOUGLAS, FMC PRESIDENT AND CEO.
FMC Corporation declares quarterly dividend. PHILADELPHIA, JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 48 CENTS PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 21, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2021.   ABOUT FMC FMC CORPORATION IS A GLOBAL AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY DEDICATED TO HELPING GROWERS PRODUCE FOOD, FEED, FIBER AND FUEL FOR AN EXPANDING WORLD POPULATION WHILE ADAPTING TO A CHANGING ENVIRONMENT.
