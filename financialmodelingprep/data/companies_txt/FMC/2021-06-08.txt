FMC Corporation's fluindapyr fungicide receives U.S. EPA registration. PHILADELPHIA, JUNE 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  FMC CORPORATION (NYSE: FMC), A LEADING AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES COMPANY, RECEIVED U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (EPA) REGISTRATION FOR FLUINDAPYR, A NOVEL BROAD-SPECTRUM SUCCINATE DEHYDROGENASE INHIBITOR (SDHI) FUNGICIDE. FLUINDAPYR PROVIDES BROAD-SPECTRUM ACTIVITY AGAINST A WIDE RANGE OF DESTRUCTIVE DISEASES IN ROW AND SPECIALTY CROPS, AS WELL AS TURF.
