Lowe's Launches Home Décor Curation Series That Uncovers Bold, Attainable Style. MOORESVILLE, N.C., MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS HOME CONTINUES TO INCREASE IN IMPORTANCE DURING THE PANDEMIC AND BECOMES THE EPICENTER OF HOW CONSUMERS EXPRESS THEIR PERSONAL STYLE, LOWE'S IS INTRODUCING "LOWE'S HOUSE OF CURATORS," A SERIES OF BESPOKE DÉCOR CURATIONS THAT INVITES TRUSTED TASTEMAKERS TO UNCOVER COVETABLE STYLE AT UNCOMPROMISING VALUE—ALL AVAILABLE EVERY DAY AT LOWE'S.
