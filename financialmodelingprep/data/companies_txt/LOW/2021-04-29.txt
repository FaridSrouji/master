Lowe's To Host National Hiring Day On May 4 To Hire More Than 50,000 Store Associates. MOORESVILLE, N.C., APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS THE BUSIEST SEASON FOR HOME IMPROVEMENT CONTINUES, LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: LOW) WILL HOST A NATIONAL HIRING DAY TO MAKE IT AS EASY AS POSSIBLE FOR ANYONE TO START A PART-TIME JOB OR A FULL-TIME CAREER.
