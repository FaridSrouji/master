Entergy Corporation Gives Evening Restoration Update For Apr. 10. ENTERGY-THUNDERSTORMS, HIGH WINDS IMPACTED ENTERGY TEXAS SERVICE TERRITORY THURSDAY CAUSING LARGE OUTAGES WITH PEAK OF 31,100 CUSTOMERS WITHOUT POWER.AS OF FRIDAY, 4/10/20 AT 6:00PM, A TOTAL OF 2,647 CUSTOMERS ARE WITHOUT POWER IN CONROE, HUNTSVILLE, AND NAVASOTA AREAS.ENTERGY TEXAS ACQUIRED AN ADDITIONAL 260 CONTRACT LINE WORKERS AND 27 VEGETATION WORKERS.
