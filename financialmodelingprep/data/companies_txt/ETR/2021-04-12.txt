Entergy Corporation Announces Quarterly Dividend Payment to Shareholders. NEW ORLEANS, APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ENTERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ETR) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND PAYMENT OF $0.95 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 1, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 6, 2021.
