Entergy Corp Says Storm Restoration Of North Louisiana Power Grid Underway. ENTERGY CORP - STORM RESTORATION OF NORTH LOUISIANA POWER GRID UNDERWAY.ENTERGY CORP - CREWS EXPECT TO RESTORE POWER TONIGHT TO VAST MAJORITY OF LOUISIANA CUSTOMERS OUTSIDE OF OUACHITA PARISH.ENTERGY CORP - CREWS EXPECT SOME CUSTOMERS IN REMOTE AREAS OF WEBSTER AND BIENVILLE PARISHES RECEIVING POWER WEDNESDAY.ENTERGY CORP - ABOUT 4,000 CUSTOMERS REMAIN WITHOUT POWER IN MONROE AND WEST MONROE AREAS.ENTERGY- NEARLY 3,000 CUSTOMERS IN AREA SOUTH OF I-20 TO 12TH STREET &BETWEEN OUCHITA RIVER AND HIGHWAY 165 COULD BE WITHOUT POWER UNTIL THURSDAY.ENTERGY CORP - POWER FOR REMAINING 1,000 CUSTOMERS OUTSIDE OF MONROE, WEST MONROE AREAS SHOULD BE RESTORED BY TUESDAY EVENING.
