Entergy Completes Sale of Indian Point Energy Center to Holtec. BUCHANAN, N.Y., MAY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENTERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: ETR) TODAY COMPLETED THE SALE OF THE SUBSIDIARIES THAT OWN INDIAN POINT ENERGY CENTER TO A HOLTEC INTERNATIONAL SUBSIDIARY, WHICH PLANS TO COMPLETE MAJOR DECOMMISSIONING ACTIVITIES AT THE SITE DECADES SOONER THAN IF ENTERGY HAD CONTINUED TO OWN THE FACILITY.
