Entergy Corporation Approves Quarterly Dividend, Payable on Dec. 2, 2019. The board of directors of Entergy Corporation has approved a quarterly dividend payment of $0.93 per share on the company's common stock. The dividend is payable Dec. 2, 2019, to shareholders of record as of Nov. 7, 2019.
