Entergy Corporation Reports Unaudited Consolidated Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Entergy Corporation reported unaudited consolidated earnings results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, operating revenues were $3,140,575,000, operating income was $519,929,000 and consolidated net income was $369,459,000 or $1.84 per basic and $1.82 per diluted share against operating revenues of $3,104,319,000, operating income of $271,035,000 and consolidated net income of $539,818,000 or $2.96 per basic and $2.92 per diluted share a year ago.For the nine months, operating revenues were $8,416,367,000, operating income was $1,141,957,000 and consolidated net income was $868,639,000 or $4.42 per basic and $4.38 per diluted share against operating revenues of $8,496,970,000, operating income of $698,297,000 and consolidated net income of $924,877,000 or $5.06 per basic and $5.01 per diluted share a year ago.
