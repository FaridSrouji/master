Morgan Stanley Announces Commitment to Mobilize $1Trillion to Support Sustainability Solutions by 2030, including $750 Billion of Low-Carbon Solutions. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY (NYSE: MS) TODAY ANNOUNCED AN UPDATED COMMITMENT TO MOBILIZE $750 BILLION TO SUPPORT LOW-CARBON SOLUTIONS BY 2030, FOLLOWING ITS INITIAL COMMITMENT OF $250 BILLION ANNOUNCED IN 2018, REPRESENTING A THREE-FOLD INCREASE.
