Morgan Stanley and Spelman College Announce Inaugural Class of HBCU Scholars Program. ATLANTA & NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MORGAN STANLEY AND SPELMAN COLLEGE TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FIRST COHORT OF STUDENTS SELECTED TO PARTICIPATE IN THE INAUGURAL HBCU SCHOLARS PROGRAM, AN INTEGRATED PROGRAM TO SUPPORT STUDENTS AT THREE HISTORICALLY BLACK COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES (HBCU). FIVE OUTSTANDING FIRST-YEAR STUDENTS HAVE BEEN SELECTED TO RECEIVE THE FOUR-YEAR SCHOLARSHIPS TO SPELMAN, WHICH WILL COVER TUITION, ROOM AND BOARD IN ORDER TO FULLY REMOVE FINANCIAL BARRIERS TO HIGHER EDUCATION.
