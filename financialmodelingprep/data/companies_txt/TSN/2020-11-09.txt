Tyson Foods expands Raised & Rooted™ brand to Europe. VIENNA, AUSTRIA, NOV. 09, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TYSON FOODS, INC. (NYSE: TSN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE EUROPEAN LAUNCH OF ITS PLANT-BASED PROTEIN BRAND, RAISED & ROOTED ™. THIS IS THE FIRST EXPANSION OF THE RAISED & ROOTED BRAND OUTSIDE THE U.S., IN THE BRAND'S PURSUIT TO BE THE MOST LOVED ALTERNATIVE PROTEIN BRAND IN THE WORLD.
