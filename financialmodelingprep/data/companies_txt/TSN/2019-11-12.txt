Tyson Foods, Inc. Increases Previously Declared Quarterly Dividend on Class A Common Stock and Class B Common Stock, payable on December 13, 2019 and Declares Quarterly Dividend on Class A Common Stock and Class B Common Stock Payable on March 13, 2020. Effective November 11, 2019, the Board of Directors of Tyson Foods, Inc. increased the quarterly dividend previously declared on August 8, 2019, to $0.42 per share on Class A common stock and $0.378 per share on Class B common stock. The increased quarterly dividend is payable on December 13, 2019, to shareholders of record at the close of business on November 29, 2019. The Board also declared a quarterly dividend of $0.42 per share on Class A common stock and $0.378 per share on Class B common stock, payable on March 13, 2020, to shareholders of record at the close of business on February 28, 2020. Company anticipate the remaining quarterly dividends in fiscal 2020 will be $0.42 and $0.378 per share of Class A and Class B stock, respectively. This results in an annual dividend rate in fiscal 2020 of $1.68 for Class A shares and $1.512 for Class B shares, or a 12% increase compared to the fiscal 2019 annual dividend rate.
