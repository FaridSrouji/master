Tyson Fresh Meats Signs Agreement With Kazakhstan, Kusto Group To Construct Beef Processing Plant. TYSON FRESH MEATS INC - SIGNED AN AGREEMENT WITH REPUBLIC OF KAZAKHSTAN AND PRIVATE HOLDING COMPANY KUSTO GROUP TO COLLABORATE ON A PROJECT.TYSON FRESH MEATS - PROJECT COULD LEAD TO CONSTRUCTION OF BEEF PROCESSING PLANT IN KAZAKHSTAN, WITH ANTICIPATED HARVEST CAPACITY OF 2,000 HEAD/DAY.TYSON FRESH MEATS - INVESTMENT IS PROJECTED TO HELP PROVIDE ANNUAL AGRICULTURAL ECONOMIC BENEFIT TO KAZAKHSTAN IN EXCESS OF $1 BILLION.
