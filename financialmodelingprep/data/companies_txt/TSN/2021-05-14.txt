Tyson Foods, Inc. Announces Quarterly Dividend. SPRINGDALE, ARK., MAY 14, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TYSON FOODS, INC. (NYSE: TSN), AT A MEETING ON MAY 11, 2021, DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.445 PER SHARE ON CLASS A COMMON STOCK AND $0.4005 PER SHARE ON CLASS B COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPTEMBER 1, 2021.
