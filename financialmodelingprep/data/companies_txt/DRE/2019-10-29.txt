Duke Realty Corporation Declares Quarterly Cash Distribution, Payable on November 29, 2019. Duke Realty Corporation's board of directors declared a quarterly cash distribution on its common stock of $0.235 per share, or $0.94 per share on an annualized basis. The third quarter dividend will be payable on November 29, 2019 to shareholders of record on November 14, 2019.
