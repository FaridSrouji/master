Duke Realty Corporation Schedules First Quarter 2021 Earnings Release and Conference Call. INDIANAPOLIS, APRIL 01, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DUKE REALTY CORPORATION (NYSE: DRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE COMPANY WILL RELEASE ITS FIRST QUARTER 2021 OPERATING RESULTS ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 28TH, AFTER THE MARKET CLOSE. THE COMPANY WILL HOST ITS QUARTERLY EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL ON THURSDAY, APRIL 29TH, AT 3:00 P.M. EASTERN TIME.
