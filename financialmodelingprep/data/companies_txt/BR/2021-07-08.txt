Broadridge Names Keir Gumbs Chief Legal Officer. NEW YORK, JULY 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC.  (NYSE: BR), A GLOBAL FINTECH LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT KEIR GUMBS HAS BEEN APPOINTED CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JULY 27, 2021. GUMBS WILL SUCCEED ADAM AMSTERDAM, WHO WILL BE RETIRING AFTER NEARLY 30 YEARS OF LEADING BROADRIDGE'S LEGAL FUNCTION.
