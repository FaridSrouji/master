Itiviti's trading system named Best Front Office Platform at WatersTechnology Asia Awards 2021. HONG KONG, JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ITIVITI, A BROADRIDGE FINANCIAL SOLUTIONS, INC. (NYSE: BR) BUSINESS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RECOGNITION OF ITS TRADING SYSTEM AS THE BEST FRONT OFFICE PLATFORM AT THE WATERSTECHNOLOGY ASIA AWARDS 2021.  JUDGED BY A PANEL OF INDUSTRY EXPERTS FROM ACROSS THE INDUSTRY, THE WATERSTECHNOLOGY ASIA AWARDS ARE DESIGNED TO RECOGNIZE EXCELLENCE IN DEPLOYING AND MANAGING FINANCIAL INFORMATION AND TECHNOLOGY WITHIN ASIA'S CAPITAL MARKET COMMUNITY.
