The Marathon Petroleum Corp. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 10, 2019. The Marathon Petroleum Corp.'s board of directors has declared a dividend of $0.53 per share on common stock. The dividend is payable on December 10, 2019, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on November 20, 2019.
