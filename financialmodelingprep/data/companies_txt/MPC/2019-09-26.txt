Paul Foster and Jeff Stevens Seek Replacement of CEO at Marathon Petroleum. On September 27, 2019, Marathon Petroleum Corporation announced that it has received a letter from 2 shareholders calling for the ouster of CEO Gary Heminger. The Company stated that Paul Foster and Jeff Stevens are calling for the Company CEO, Gary Heminger, to be replaced saying that the Company had lost the confidence of shareholders and criticized Gary Heminger for ignoring their input.
