Marathon Petroleum Corp Announces $1 Billion Revolving Credit Facility. MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP. ANNOUNCES $1 BILLION REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP - ENTERED INTO A $1 BILLION, 364-DAY REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY..MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP - FACILITY PROVIDES MPC ADDITIONAL LIQUIDITY AND FINANCIAL FLEXIBILITY DURING ONGOING COMMODITY PRICE AND DEMAND DOWNTURN.MARATHON PETROLEUM CORP - FACILITY IS IN ADDITION TO, AND DOES NOT REPLACE, ANY OF MPC'S PREVIOUSLY EXISTING CREDIT FACILITIES..
