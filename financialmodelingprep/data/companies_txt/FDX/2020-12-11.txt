FedEx to Ship First Wave of COVID-19 Vaccines Across the United States. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX EXPRESS, A SUBSIDIARY OF FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) AND THE WORLD'S LARGEST EXPRESS TRANSPORTATION COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT OPERATIONS ARE IN MOTION TO TRANSPORT ITS FIRST SHIPMENTS OF COVID-19 VACCINES. FOLLOWING THE FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION'S APPROVAL OF EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR THE PFIZER-BIONTECH COVID-19 VACCINE, FEDEX EXPRESS WILL BEGIN TRANSPORT OF THE VACCINE USING ITS FEDEX PRIORITY OVERNIGHT® SERVICE SUPPORTED BY FEDEX PRIORITY ALERT
