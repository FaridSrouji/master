FedEx to Reduce Debt by 11 Percent Following Completion of Strategic Public Offerings. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETION OF THE OFFERINGS OF $1.75 BILLION OF USD-DENOMINATED NOTES AND €1.25 BILLION OF EURO-DENOMINATED NOTES. A PORTION OF THE PROCEEDS OF THE DEBT OFFERINGS, WHICH RECEIVED STRONG MARKET SUPPORT, WILL BE USED WITH EXISTING CASH TO REDEEM $5.8 BILLION OF THE COMPANY'S EXISTING DEBT.
