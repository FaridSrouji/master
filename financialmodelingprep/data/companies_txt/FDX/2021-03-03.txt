FedEx Commits to Carbon-Neutral Operations by 2040. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX), HOME OF THE WORLD'S LARGEST CARGO AIRLINE, ANNOUNCED TODAY AN AMBITIOUS GOAL TO ACHIEVE CARBON-NEUTRAL OPERATIONS GLOBALLY BY 2040. TO HELP REACH THIS GOAL, FEDEX IS DESIGNATING MORE THAN $2 BILLION OF INITIAL INVESTMENT IN THREE KEY AREAS: VEHICLE ELECTRIFICATION, SUSTAINABLE ENERGY, AND CARBON SEQUESTRATION.
