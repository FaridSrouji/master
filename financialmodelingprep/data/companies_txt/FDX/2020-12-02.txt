FedEx to Acquire ShopRunner to Expand E-Commerce Capabilities. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS AGREED TO ACQUIRE SHOPRUNNER, THE E-COMMERCE PLATFORM THAT CONNECTS ONLINE SHOPPERS WITH THEIR FAVORITE MERCHANTS AND BRANDS. SHOPRUNNER'S CAPABILITIES WILL COMPLEMENT AND EXPAND THE FEDEX E-COMMERCE PORTFOLIO AND ARE EXPECTED TO CREATE INCREASED VALUE FOR BOTH MERCHANTS AND CONSUMERS.
