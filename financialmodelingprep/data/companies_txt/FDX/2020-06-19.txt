Fedex Concluded That Noncash Charge Of Approximately $348 Mln For Impairment Of Value Of Goodwill Would Need To Be Recorded In Q4. FEDEX CORP - CONCLUDED THAT NONCASH CHARGE OF APPROXIMATELY $348 MILLION FOR IMPAIRMENT OF VALUE OF GOODWILL WOULD NEED TO BE RECORDED IN Q4.FEDEX CORP - IMPAIRMENT CHARGE RECOGNIZED BASED ON DECLINING PRINT REVENUE AND TEMPORARY STORE CLOSURES AT FEDEX OFFICE DURING Q4.FEDEX CORP - COVID-19 PANDEMIC IS EXPECTED TO CONTINUE TO NEGATIVELY IMPACT FEDEX OFFICE'S NEAR-TERM OPERATING PERFORMANCE.FEDEX CORP - TOTAL ASSET IMPAIRMENT CHARGES FOR Q4 ENDED MAY 31, 2020 ARE EXPECTED TO BE ABOUT $370 MILLION.
