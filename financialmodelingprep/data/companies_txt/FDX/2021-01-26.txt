FedEx Ground President and CEO Henry J. Maier to Retire July 31. MEMPHIS, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FEDEX CORP. (NYSE: FDX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT HENRY J. MAIER, PRESIDENT AND CEO, FEDEX GROUND, WILL RETIRE EFFECTIVE JULY 31, 2021. JOHN A. SMITH, PRESIDENT AND CEO, FEDEX FREIGHT, WILL SUCCEED MAIER AS PRESIDENT AND CEO, FEDEX GROUND. LANCE D. MOLL HAS BEEN NAMED THE NEXT PRESIDENT AND CEO, FEDEX FREIGHT. MAIER JOINED FEDEX IN 1986.
