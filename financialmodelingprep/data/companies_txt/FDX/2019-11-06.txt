Ursa Space Systems Inc. announced that it has received $15 million in funding from a group of investors. On November 7, 2019, Ursa Space Systems Inc. closed the transaction. The company raised $5,250,005 in its second tranche closing, bringing the total gross proceeds raised in the transaction to $15,000,000. The company has raised $12,164,670.54 pursuant to exemption provided under Regulation D from 19 investors.
