Maxim Integrated Products, Inc., Annual General Meeting, Nov 07, 2019. Maxim Integrated Products, Inc., Annual General Meeting, Nov 07, 2019, at 10:00 Pacific Standard Time. Location: Event Center 160 Rio Robles San Jose California United States Agenda: To elect nine members of the board of directors to hold office until the next annual meeting of stockholders or until their respective successors have been elected and qualified; to ratify the appointment of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as the company’s independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending June 27, 2020; to hold a non-binding advisory vote to approve the compensation of our Named Executive Officers; and to consider such other business as may properly come before the meeting.
