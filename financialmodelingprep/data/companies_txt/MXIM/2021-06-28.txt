Maxim Integrated's Infrared-Based Dynamic Gesture Sensor Detects a Broader Range of Hand Gestures at Greater Distances, Ensuring Drivers' Eyes Remain on the Road. SAN JOSE, CALIF., JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MAXIM INTEGRATED PRODUCTS, INC. (NASDAQ: MXIM) TODAY INTRODUCED THE NEXT GENERATION OF ITS INFRARED-BASED DYNAMIC OPTICAL SENSOR, ABLE TO SENSE A BROADER RANGE OF GESTURES AT EXTENDED DISTANCES.
