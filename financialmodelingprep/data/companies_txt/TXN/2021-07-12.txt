New MCU portfolio redefines microcontroller performance, enabling 10 times higher processing capability than existing devices. DALLAS, JULY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TEXAS INSTRUMENTS (TI) (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY INTRODUCED A NEW HIGH-PERFORMANCE MICROCONTROLLER (MCU) PORTFOLIO THAT ADVANCES REAL-TIME CONTROL, NETWORKING AND ANALYTICS APPLICATIONS AT THE EDGE. WITH NEW SITARA AM2X MCUS, ENGINEERS CAN ACHIEVE 10 TIMES THE COMPUTING CAPABILITY OF TRADITIONAL, FLASH-BASED MCUS.
