Texas Instruments board declares third quarter 2021 quarterly dividend. DALLAS, JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: TXN) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.02 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE AUGUST 9, 2021, TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JULY 26, 2021.     ABOUT TEXAS INSTRUMENTS TEXAS INSTRUMENTS INCORPORATED (NASDAQ: TXN) IS A GLOBAL SEMICONDUCTOR COMPANY THAT DESIGNS, MANUFACTURES, TESTS AND SELLS ANALOG AND EMBEDDED PROCESSING CHIPS FOR MARKETS SUCH AS INDUSTRIAL, AUTOMOTIVE, PERSONAL ELECTRONICS, COMMUNICATIONS EQUIPMENT AND ENTERPRISE SYSTEMS.
