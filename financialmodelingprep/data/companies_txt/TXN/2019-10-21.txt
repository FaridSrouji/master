Texas Instruments Incorporated Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019. Texas Instruments Incorporated provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019. The company fourth quarter outlook is for revenue in the range of $3.07 billion to $3.33 billion, and earnings per share between $0.91 and $1.09.
