Texas Instruments Incorporated Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on November 18, 2019. The board of directors of Texas Instruments Incorporated declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.90 per share of common stock, payable November 18, 2019, to stockholders of record on October 31, 2019.
