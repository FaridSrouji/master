Ralphs Pharmacies Provide First Doses Of COVID-19 Vaccine To Health Care Professionals In Southern California. LOS ANGELES, JAN. 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- RALPHS, A DIVISION OF THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), HAS ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS OBTAINED A SUPPLY OF THE MODERNA COVID-19 VACCINE AND HAS BEGUN ADMINISTERING VACCINATIONS TO ITS PHARMACISTS AND ANCILLARY PHARMACY STAFF AS WELL AS OTHER HEALTH CARE...
