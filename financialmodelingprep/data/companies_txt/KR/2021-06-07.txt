The Kroger Family of Companies to Hire 10,000 Associates. CINCINNATI, JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS FAMILY OF COMPANIES' FIRST NATIONWIDE HYBRID HIRING EVENT, INCLUDING VIRTUAL AND IN-STORE INTERVIEWS, WILL TAKE PLACE ON THURSDAY, JUNE 10. THE GOAL OF THE EVENT IS TO HIRE 10,000 ASSOCIATES SUPPORTING RETAIL, E-COMMERCE, PHARMACY, MANUFACTURING, AND LOGISTICS OPERATIONS.
