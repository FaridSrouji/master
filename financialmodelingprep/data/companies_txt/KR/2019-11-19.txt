Kroger Partners With Europe's Infarm To Offer In-Store Living Produce Farms. KROGER PARTNERS WITH EUROPE'S INFARM TO OFFER IN-STORE LIVING PRODUCE FARMS.KROGER CO - PARTNERSHIP WITH INFARM WILL BRING MODULAR LIVING PRODUCE FARMS TO NORTH AMERICA.KROGER CO - LIVING PRODUCE FARMS WILL LAUNCH IN NOV AT TWO OF 15 STORES PLANNED AT QFC.
