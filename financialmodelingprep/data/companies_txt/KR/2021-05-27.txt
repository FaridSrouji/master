Kroger Health to Launch #CommunityImmunity $5 Million Giveaway to Encourage Vaccinations. CINCINNATI, MAY 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KROGER HEALTH, THE HEALTHCARE DIVISION OF THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), IS LAUNCHING A NEW EFFORT TO INCENTIVIZE MORE AMERICANS TO GET THE COVID-19 VACCINE. THE #COMMUNITYIMMUNITY GIVEAWAY CAMPAIGN WILL START NEXT WEEK AND PROVIDE INDIVIDUALS, INCLUDING CUSTOMERS AND ASSOCIATES, THE OPPORTUNITY TO WIN LIFE-CHANGING PRIZES LIKE ONE OF FIVE $1 MILLION PAYOUTS AND 50 CHANCES TO WIN FREE GROCERIES FOR A YEAR.
