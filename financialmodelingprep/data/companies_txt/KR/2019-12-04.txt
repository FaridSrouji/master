the Kroger Co. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended November 9, 2019. The Kroger Co. Announced earnings results for the third quarter ended November 9, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 27,974 million compared to USD 27,831 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 254 million compared to USD 647 million a year ago. Net income was USD 263 million compared to USD 317 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.32 compared to USD 0.39 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 93,393 million compared to USD 93,566 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,714 million compared to USD 2,225 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,332 million compared to USD 2,851 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.65 compared to USD 3.46 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.64 compared to USD 3.43 a year ago.
