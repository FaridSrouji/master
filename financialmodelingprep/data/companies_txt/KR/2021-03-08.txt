Kroger Expands Partnership With 80 Acres Farms to 316 Stores. CINCINNATI, OH / ACCESSWIRE / MARCH 9, 2021 / THE KROGER CO. (NYSE:KR) AND 80 ACRES FARMS BRING FARM-FRESH PRODUCE TO CUSTOMERS ACROSS OHIO. TODAY, THE COMPANIES ARE GROWING THEIR PARTNERSHIP TO REACH MORE SHOPPERS IN THE MIDWEST - BOTH IN-STORE AND ONLINE.
