Kroger Health Partners with Trae Young and Turner Sports to Help Drive COVID-19 Vaccinations in Atlanta. ATLANTA, JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KROGER HEALTH, THE HEALTHCARE DIVISION OF THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS WORKING TO ENABLE ATLANTA'S UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES TO HAVE GREATER AWARENESS OF AND ACCESS TO THE COVID-19 VACCINE BY PARTNERING WITH TURNER SPORTS, NBA ON TNT, AND TRAE YOUNG, ALL-STAR GUARD ON THE ATLANTA HAWKS. ACCORDING TO THE CDC, 62% OF ADULTS IN AMERICA HAVE RECEIVED AT LEAST ONE VACCINATION SHOT; HOWEVER, UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES – AND SPECIFICALLY BLACK AND BROWN COMMUNITIES – ARE STILL LAGGING.
