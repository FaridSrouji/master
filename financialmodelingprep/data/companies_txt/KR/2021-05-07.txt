Kroger Celebrates Small Business Week and Offers Retail Partnership Guidebook to Advance Supplier Inclusion. CINCINNATI, MAY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) TODAY ANNOUNCED NOTABLE PROGRESS AND NEW INITIATIVES – INCLUDING THE LAUNCH OF ITS SMALL BUSINESS RESOURCE GUIDE – AS PART OF ITS FRAMEWORK FOR ACTION: DIVERSITY, EQUITY & INCLUSION PLAN, FEATURING STEPS DEVELOPED IN COLLABORATION WITH ASSOCIATES AND LEADERS TO ACCELERATE AND PROMOTE GREATER CHANGE IN THE WORKPLACE AND IN THE COMMUNITIES THE ORGANIZATION SERVES. "IN 2020, KROGER ACHIEVED $4.1 BILLION IN DIVERSE SUPPLIER SPEND, REPRESENTING AN INCREASE OF 21% VERSUS A YEAR AGO AND BUILD ON OUR COMMITMENT TO REACH $10 BILLION IN DIVERSE SUPPLIER SPEND BY 2030.
