Kroger Expands Partnership with 80 Acres Farms to 316 Stores. CINCINNATI, MARCH 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE KROGER CO. (NYSE: KR) AND 80 ACRES FARMS BRING FARM-FRESH PRODUCE TO CUSTOMERS ACROSS OHIO. TODAY, THE COMPANIES ARE GROWING THEIR PARTNERSHIP TO REACH MORE SHOPPERS IN THE MIDWEST – BOTH IN STORE AND ONLINE.
