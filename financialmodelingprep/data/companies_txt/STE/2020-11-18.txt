STERIS Completes Acquisition of Key Surgical. DUBLIN, IRELAND, NOV. 18, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- STERIS PLC (NYSE: STE) (“STERIS” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IS HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF KEY SURGICAL, A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF WATER STREET HEALTHCARE PARTNERS, LLC.
