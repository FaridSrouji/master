Steris Posts Q2 EPS Of $1.11. STERIS ANNOUNCES FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR FISCAL 2020 SECOND QUARTER.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.11.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.25 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES FY 2020 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $5.50 TO $5.65.Q2 REVENUE $736.8 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $717.8 MILLION.Q2 ADJUSTED NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.32.FY 2020 CONSTANT CURRENCY ORGANIC REVENUE GROWTH IS NOW EXPECTED TO BE IN THE RANGE OF 7.5%-8.5%.COMPANY EXPECTS CURRENCY MOVEMENTS WILL NEGATIVELY IMPACT AS REPORTED REVENUE BY ABOUT $25 MILLION IN FISCAL 2020.
STERIS plc Approves Quarterly Interim Dividend, Payable December 20, 2019. STERIS plc's Board of Directors has approved the quarterly interim dividend of $0.37 per share. The dividend is payable December 20, 2019 to hareholders of record at the close of business on November 26, 2019.
