STERIS Completes the Acquisition of Cantel Medical. DUBLIN, IRELAND, JUNE 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  STERIS PLC (NYSE: STE) (“STERIS” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF CANTEL MEDICAL, A GLOBAL PROVIDER OF INFECTION PREVENTION PRODUCTS AND SERVICES TO ENDOSCOPY, DENTAL, DIALYSIS AND LIFE SCIENCES CUSTOMERS.
