eBay Inc. Announces Pricing $2.5 Billion Senior Unsecured Notes Offering. SAN JOSE, CALIF., MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EBAY INC. (NASDAQ: EBAY) (THE "COMPANY" OR "EBAY") ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF A $2,500,000,000 UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF ITS SENIOR UNSECURED NOTES, CONSISTING OF $750,000,000 OF 1.400% NOTES DUE 2026 (THE "2026 NOTES"), $750,000,000 OF 2.600% NOTES DUE 2031 (THE "2031 NOTES") AND $1,000,000,000 OF 3.650% NOTES DUE 2051 (THE "2051 NOTES").
