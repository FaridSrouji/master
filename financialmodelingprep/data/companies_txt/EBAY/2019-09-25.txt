EBay Says CEO Devin Wenig Is Stepping Down, Appoints Scott Schenkel As Interim CEO. EBAY INC ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP TRANSITION.DEVIN WENIG STEPPING DOWN AS PRESIDENT AND CEO, AND A DIRECTOR OF COMPANY.SCOTT SCHENKEL APPOINTED INTERIM CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.EBAY BOARD WILL UNDERTAKE A SEARCH TO IDENTIFY COMPANY'S NEXT CEO, AND WILL CONSIDER INTERNAL AND EXTERNAL CANDIDATES..EBAY REAFFIRMED ITS FULL YEAR GUIDANCE FOR 2019 OF ORGANIC FX-NEUTRAL REVENUE GROWTH OF 2% TO 3%.ANDY CRING, EBAY'S VICE PRESIDENT, GLOBAL FINANCIAL PLANNING AND ANALYSIS, HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO SERVE AS INTERIM CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED OPERATING REVIEW IS ONGOING, AND COMPANY EXPECTS TO PROVIDE AN UPDATE THIS FALL.EBAY -  STRATEGIC REVIEW OF ASSET PORTFOLIO, INCLUDING STUBHUB & EBAY CLASSIFIEDS GROUP, CONTINUING TO MOVE FORWARD WITH HELP OF GOLDMAN SACHS & CO.DEVIN WENIG STEPPING DOWN AS PRESIDENT AND CEO, AND A DIRECTOR OF COMPANY.FY2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $2.75 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SAYS DEVIN WENIG STEPPING DOWN AS PRESIDENT AND CEO, AND A DIRECTOR OF COMPANY.
