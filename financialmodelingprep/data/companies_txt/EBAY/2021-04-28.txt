eBay Inc. Reports Better Than Expected First Quarter 2021 Results. SAN JOSE, CALIF., APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EBAY INC. (NASDAQ: EBAY), A GLOBAL COMMERCE LEADER THAT CONNECTS MILLIONS OF BUYERS AND SELLERS AROUND THE WORLD, TODAY REPORTED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FIRST QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021.
