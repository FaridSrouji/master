U.S. EPA Says Ordered Amazon, Ebay To Stop Selling A Wide Range Of Pesticide Products. U.S. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (EPA) - ORDERED AMAZON SERVICES LLC AND EBAY INC. TO STOP SELLING A WIDE RANGE OF PESTICIDE PRODUCTS.U.S. EPA - PRODUCTS SUBJECT TO EPA'S ORDERS ALSO INCLUDE SEVERAL PRODUCTS MARKETED WITH FALSE OR MISLEADING CLAIMS OF EFFICACY AGAINST COVID-19.
