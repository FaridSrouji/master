CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SEALED AIR CORPORATION (NYSE: SEE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHRISTOPHER J. STEPHENS HAS MORE THAN 30 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE IN LEADERSHIP ROLES IN AEROSPACE, INDUSTRIAL AND MANUFACTURING SECTORS.
