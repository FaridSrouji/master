Sealed Air Raises Quarterly Cash Dividend. CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SEALED AIR CORPORATION (NYSE: SEE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A 25% INCREASE IN THE COMPANY'S QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND. THE QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND WILL INCREASE FROM $0.16 PER COMMON SHARE TO $0.20 PER COMMON SHARE.
