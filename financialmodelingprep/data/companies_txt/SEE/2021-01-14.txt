Sealed Air Continues to Play Critical Role in Packaging Solutions to Support COVID-19 Needs. CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS AN INDUSTRY LEADER IN SUSTAINABLE PACKAGING SOLUTIONS AND SYSTEMS, SEALED AIR CORPORATION (NYSE: SEE) IS PROUD TO PLAY A ROLE IN PROVIDING THE ESSENTIAL PACKAGING SOLUTIONS NEEDED TO HELP CUSTOMERS IN THE FIGHT AGAINST COVID-19. SEALED AIR PROVIDES CUSTOMERS WITH PACKAGING SOLUTIONS INCLUDING CUSTOM-FABRICATED SHIPPING BOXES, AND AUTOMATED PACKAGING AND BAGGING SYSTEMS THAT ENABLE THE DISTRIBUTION OF MEDICAL EQUIPMENT, FACE COVERINGS, AND TEST KITS.
