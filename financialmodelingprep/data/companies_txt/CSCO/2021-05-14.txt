Cisco Announces Intent to Acquire Kenna Security to Deliver Industry Leading Vulnerability Management. SAN JOSE, CALIF., MAY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NEWS SUMMARY: INTENT TO ACQUIRE KENNA SECURITY A MARKET LEADER IN RISK-BASED VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT, TO ADVANCE CISCO'S COMMITMENT TO RADICALLY SIMPLIFY SECURITY AND HELP CUSTOMERS IMPROVE THEIR SECURITY POSTURE KENNA SECURITY USES MACHINE LEARNING (ML) AND DATA SCIENCE TO TRACK AND PREDICT REAL-WORLD EXPLOITATIONS, EMPOWERING SECURITY TEAMS TO MANAGE THE EVER-EVOLVING THREAT LANDSCAPE COMBINING THE CISCO SECUREX PLATFORM'S INDUSTRY LEADING THREAT INTELLIGENCE WITH KENNA SECURITY'S VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT CAPABILITIES WILL HELP CUSTOMERS FIND THREATS FASTER, IMPROVE COLLABORATION BETWEEN SECURITY AND IT TEAMS AND ULTIMATELY REDUCE THE ATTACK SURFACE   CISCO TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INTENT TO ACQUIRE KENNA SECURITY, INC., A PRIVATELY HELD CYBERSECURITY COMPANY HEADQUARTERED IN SANTA CLARA, CALIF.
