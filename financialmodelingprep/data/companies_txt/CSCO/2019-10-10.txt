Cisco Systems, Inc. (NasdaqGS:CSCO) completed the acquisition of Customer Analytics Technologies Inc.. Cisco Systems, Inc. (NasdaqGS:CSCO) agreed to acquire Customer Analytics Technologies Inc. on August 26, 2019. Upon completion of the transaction, the CloudCherry team will join Cisco’s Contact Center Solutions business, led by Vasili Triant, vice president and general manager. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions and required approvals and is expected to close in the first quarter of Cisco's fiscal year 2020. As of August 27, 2019, the transaction is expected to complete by end of October. AZB & Partners acted as legal advisor to Cisco Systems, Inc.
