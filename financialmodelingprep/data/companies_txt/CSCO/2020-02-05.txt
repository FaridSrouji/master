Armis Discovers 5 Zero-Day Vulnerabilities In Cisco's Discovery Protocol Impacting Tens Of Millions Of Enterprise-Grade Devices. ARMIS DISCOVERS 5 ZERO-DAY VULNERABILITIES IN CISCO'S DISCOVERY PROTOCOL IMPACTING TENS OF MILLIONS OF ENTERPRISE-GRADE DEVICES.ARMIS - BEEN WORKING IN COLLABORATION WITH CISCO, TO CONFIRM VULNERABILITIES, AUDIT THEIR TECHNICAL DETAILS, EVALUATE ASSOCIATED RISK.ARMIS- VULNERABILITIES IN CISCO'S DISCOVERY PROTOCOL COULD ALLOW ATTACKERS TO REMOTELY TAKE OVER DEVICES SUCH AS IP PHONES.ARMIS - CISCO NOTIFIED CUSTOMERS AND ISSUED PATCHES MADE AVAILABLE TO ADDRESS VULNERABILITIES ON FEB 5, 2020.ARMIS- CO'S AGENTLESS DEVICE SECURITY PLATFORM ABLE TO IDENTIFY CISCO DEVICES THAT ARE VULNERABLE TO CDPWN, DETECT PRESENCE OF EXPLOITATION ATTEMPT.
