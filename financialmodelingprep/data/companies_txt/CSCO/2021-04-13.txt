Cisco Becomes an Official Technology Partner of the National Football League. SAN JOSE, CALIF., APRIL 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE NATIONAL FOOTBALL LEAGUE (NFL) AND CISCO (NASDAQ: CSCO), A LEADER IN ENTERPRISE NETWORKING AND SECURITY, TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW MULTI-YEAR PARTNERSHIP NAMING CISCO AN OFFICIAL TECHNOLOGY PARTNER OF THE NFL.
