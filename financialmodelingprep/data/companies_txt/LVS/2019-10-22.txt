Las Vegas Sands Corp. Announces Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 26, 2019. Las Vegas Sands Corp. announced its quarterly dividend of $0.77 per common share will be paid on December 26, 2019, to Las Vegas Sands shareholders of record on December 17, 2019.
