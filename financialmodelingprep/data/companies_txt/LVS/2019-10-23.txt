Las Vegas Sands Reports Q3 Adjusted Earnings Per Share Of $0.75. LAS VEGAS SANDS REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.Q3 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.75.Q3 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.69.Q3 REVENUE $3.25 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $3.28 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.75 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.ON A HOLD-NORMALIZED BASIS, QTRLY CONSOLIDATED ADJUSTED PROPERTY EBITDA DECREASED 3.0% TO $1.25 BILLION.IN MACAO, ADJUSTED PROPERTY EBITDA WAS $755 MILLION IN QUARTER.QTRLY CONSOLIDATED ADJUSTED PROPERTY EBITDA WAS $1.28 BILLION, CONSISTENT WITH PRIOR YEAR QUARTER.QTRLY MACAO OPERATIONS NET REVENUE $2,112 MILLION VERSUS $2,152 MILLION.ADDITIONAL TOURISM AND ENTERTAINMENT AMENITIES OF LONDONER MACAO WILL DEBUT THROUGHOUT 2020 AND 2021.LAS VEGAS SANDS -  BOARD APPROVED AN INCREASE IN ANNUAL DIVIDEND FOR 2020 CALENDAR YEAR TO $0.79/SHARE PER QUARTER.LAS VEGAS SANDS - ON A GAAP BASIS QTRLY TOTAL NET REVENUES FOR SANDS CHINA LTD DECREASED 2.0% TO $2.11 BILLION.
