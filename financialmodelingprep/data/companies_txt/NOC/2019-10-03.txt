Northrop Grumman Corporation Appoints Phyllis Schneck as Vice President and Chief Information Security Officer, Effective Oct. 14, 2019. Northrop Grumman Corporation has appointed Phyllis Schneck vice president and chief information security officer, effective Oct. 14, 2019. Schneck will report to Shawn Purvis, corporate vice president and president, Northrop Grumman Enterprise Services. In this role, Schneck will oversee Northrop Grumman's global cybersecurity strategy and policies, and will be responsible the security of the company's products, services and infrastructures. Most recently, Schneck led the cybersecurity practice at Promontory, an IBM company. Previously, she served as the deputy undersecretary for cybersecurity and communications for the Department of Homeland Security, where she led responses to cybersecurity threats against corporations, civilians and the government. During this time, she led the defensive cybersecurity operational mission to mitigate and respond to cyber threats across the federal civilian government and private sector.
