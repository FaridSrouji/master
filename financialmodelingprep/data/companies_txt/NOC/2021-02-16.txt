Northrop Grumman Board Declares Quarterly Dividend. FALLS CHURCH, VA., FEB. 16, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION (NYSE: NOC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.45 PER SHARE ON NORTHROP GRUMMAN COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE MARCH 17, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS MARCH 1, 2021.
