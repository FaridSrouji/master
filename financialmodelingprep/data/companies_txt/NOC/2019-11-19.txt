Northrop Grumman Board Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on Dec. 18, 2019. The board of directors of Northrop Grumman Corporation declared a quarterly dividend of $1.32 per share on Northrop Grumman common stock, payable Dec. 18, 2019, to shareholders of record as of the close of business Dec. 2, 2019.
