Northrop Grumman Awarded Contract To Replace Aging ICBM System. NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORP - AWARDED CONTRACT TO REPLACE AGING ICBM SYSTEM.NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORP -  SELECTED BY U.S. AIR FORCE TO MODERNIZE ICBM SYSTEM UNDER A $13.3 BILLION CONTRACT.NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORP - AIR FORCE NUCLEAR WEAPONS CENTER ANNOUNCED EFFORTS RELATED TO INTERCONTINENTAL BALLISTIC MISSILE SYSTEM WILL SPAN 8.5 YEARS.
