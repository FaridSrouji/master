Northrop Grumman Wins $1.36 Billion Foreign Military Sales Contract Northrop Grumman (NOC) has been awarded a $1.36 billion contract from the US Navy to produce nine E-2D Advanced Hawkeye aircraft under a foreign military sales contract with Japan, the Pentagon website said Wednesday.
