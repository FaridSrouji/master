Northrop Grumman Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Northrop Grumman Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 8,475 million compared to USD 8,085 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 951 million compared to USD 1,172 million a year ago. Net income was USD 933 million compared to USD 1,244 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 5.52 compared to USD 7.15 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 5.49 compared to USD 7.11 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 25,120 million compared to USD 21,939 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,833 million compared to USD 2,837 million a year ago. Net income was USD 2,657 million compared to USD 2,873 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 15.67 compared to USD 16.48 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 15.6 compared to USD 16.4 a year ago.
