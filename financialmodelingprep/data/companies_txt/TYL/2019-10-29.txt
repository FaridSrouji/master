Tyler Technologies, Inc. (NYSE:TYL) acquired certain assets of Courthouse Technologies for $19 million.. Tyler Technologies, Inc. (NYSE:TYL) acquired certain assets of Courthouse Technologies for $19 million on October 30, 2019. The total purchase price was approximately $19 million in cash, subject to certain post-closing adjustments. Courthouse Technologies will complement and elevate Tyler’s software offerings for domestic and international court clients. Courthouse Technologies management and staff will become part of Tyler’s Courts & Justice Division and its current employees are expected to remain in the current office space in Vancouver. Scott Kerr is the co-founder of Courthouse Technologies and he will lead the business unit as general manager.
Tyler Technologies, Inc. completed the acquisition of certain assets of Courthouse Technologies on October 30, 2019.
