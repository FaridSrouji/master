Two More California Superior Courts Select Tyler Technologies’ Odyssey Case Management Solution. Tyler Technologies, Inc. announced that it has signed agreements with the Superior Court of California, County of Mendocino, and the Superior Court of California, County of Shasta, for Tyler’s Odyssey® case management solution. These agreements represent the 27th and 28th California superior courts to select Tyler’s Odyssey solution, which now serves approximately 70% of California’s population. Through a new master services agreement (MSA) with the state of California, Tyler is now a preferred case management vendor, making the process for courts to procure Odyssey quick and easy. The County of Mendocino is in northern California, equidistant from the San Francisco bay area and the California/Oregon border. The Mendocino superior court chose Tyler’s Odyssey Case Management, Judge Edition, and Clerk Edition solutions to replace its current case management system, which lacks e-filing and other modern functionality. The superior court was looking to replace its current case management system that would no longer be supported by its current provider and selected Tyler’s Odyssey solution to manage all case types, along with Odyssey Judge Edition™ and Odyssey Clerk Edition™. Like Mendocino, Shasta will also utilize Odyssey Judge Edition and Odyssey Clerk Edition.
