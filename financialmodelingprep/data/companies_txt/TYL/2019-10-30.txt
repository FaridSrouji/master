Tyler Technologies Reports Q3 Non-GAAP Earnings Per Share of $1.35. TYLER TECHNOLOGIES REPORTS EARNINGS FOR THIRD QUARTER 2019.Q3 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.35.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.00.Q3 REVENUE $277.2 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $282.2 MILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.36 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES FY 2019 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $5.22 TO $5.35.SEES FY 2019 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.50 TO $3.63.SEES FY 2019 REVENUE $1.082 BILLION TO $1.095 BILLION.SEES FY CAPITAL EXPENDITURES ARE EXPECTED TO BE IN RANGE OF $45 MILLION TO $47 MILLION.SEES FY 2019 NON-GAAP TOTAL REVENUES ARE EXPECTED TO BE IN RANGE OF $1.090 BILLION TO $1.103 BILLION.SEES FY 2019 NON-GAAP DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE ARE EXPECTED TO BE IN RANGE OF $5.22 TO $5.35.TYLER TECHNOLOGIES - TOTAL BACKLOG WAS $1.41 BILLION AT SEPTEMBER 30, 2019 .
Tyler Technologies Acquires Courthouse Technologies. TYLER TECHNOLOGIES ACQUIRES COURTHOUSE TECHNOLOGIES.TYLER TECHNOLOGIES INC - SCOTT KERR WILL LEAD BUSINESS UNIT AS GENERAL MANAGER.TYLER TECHNOLOGIES INC -  CURRENT CHT EMPLOYEES ARE EXPECTED TO REMAIN IN CURRENT OFFICE SPACE IN VANCOUVER.TYLER TECHNOLOGIES INC - CHT MANAGEMENT AND STAFF WILL BECOME PART OF TYLER'S COURTS & JUSTICE DIVISION.
