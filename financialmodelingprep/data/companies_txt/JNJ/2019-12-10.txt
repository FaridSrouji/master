Darzalex Regimen Shows Significant Increase In Progression-Free Survival In Treatment Of Patients With Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma. DARZALEX (DARATUMUMAB) REGIMEN SHOWS SIGNIFICANT INCREASE IN PROGRESSION-FREE SURVIVAL IN TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH RELAPSED/REFRACTORY MULTIPLE MYELOMA.JANSSEN - ADDITION OF DARZALEX TO CARFILZOMIB & DEXAMETHASONE VERSUS CARFILZOMIB & DEXAMETHASONE ALONE SIGNIFICANTLY IMPROVED PROGRESSION-FREE SURVIVAL.JANSSEN - ADDITION OF DARZALEX RESULTED IN 37% REDUCTION IN RISK OF PROGRESSION OR DEATH.JANSSEN - PRIMARY ENDPOINT OF PROGRESSION-FREE SURVIVAL WAS MET AFTER MEDIAN FOLLOW-UP OF 16.9 MONTHS AND 16.3 MONTHS FOR DKD AND KD ARMS.
