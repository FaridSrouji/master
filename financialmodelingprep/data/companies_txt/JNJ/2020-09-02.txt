Janssen To Discontinue Pimodivir Influenza Development Program. JANSSEN TO DISCONTINUE PIMODIVIR INFLUENZA DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM.JANSSEN PHARMACEUTICAL -DECISION BASED ON RECENT RESULTS FROM PRE-PLANNED INTERIM ANALYSES OF PIMODIVIR PHASE 3 TRIAL IN HOSPITALIZED PATIENTS WITH INFLUENZA A.JANSSEN - DATA SHOW THAT PIMODIVIR DOES NOT OFFER A BENEFIT ABOVE EXISTING STANDARD OF CARE.JANSSEN - PIMODIVIR PHASE 3 TRIAL IN INFLUENZA A FOUND PIMODIVIR IN COMBINATION WITH SOC WAS VERY UNLIKELY TO DEMONSTRATE ADDED BENEFIT.JANSSEN - STUDY IN HOSPITALIZED PATIENTS WITH INFLUENZA A AND PARALLEL PHASE 3 STUDY OF PIMODIVIR IN OUTPATIENTS WITH INFLUENZA A WILL BE HALTED.
