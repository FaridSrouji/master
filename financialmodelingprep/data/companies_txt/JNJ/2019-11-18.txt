Janssen Receives EU Marketing Authorisation For Darzalex With Lenalidomide, Dexamethasone. Nov 19  - The Janssen Pharmaceutical Companies Of Johnson & Johnson​::JANSSEN RECEIVES EU MARKETING AUTHORISATION FOR DARZALEX®▼(DARATUMUMAB) IN COMBINATION WITH LENALIDOMIDE AND DEXAMETHASONE FOR PATIENTS WITH NEWLY DIAGNOSED MULTIPLE MYELOMA WHO ARE TRANSPLANT INELIGIBLE.THE JANSSEN PHARMACEUTICAL COMPANIES OF JOHNSON & JOHNSON - APPROVAL WAS BASED ON RESULTS FROM PHASE 3 MAIA (MMY3008 ) STUDY.
