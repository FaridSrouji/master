Bavarian Nordic To Manufacture Additional Ebola Vaccines For Janssen. REG-BAVARIAN NORDIC TO MANUFACTURE ADDITIONAL EBOLA VACCINES FOR JANSSEN.BAVARIAN NORDIC TO MANUFACTURE ADDITIONAL EBOLA VACCINES FOR JANSSEN.ENTERED NEW SUPPLY CONTRACT WITH JANSSEN VACCINES & PREVENTION VALUED AT USD 13.9 MLN.MANUFACTURING OF VACCINES WILL BE INITIATED IN 2020, SUPPLY AND INVOICING WILL NOT OCCUR UNTIL 2021.WILL MANUFACTURE, DELIVER BULK DRUG SUBSTANCE OF MVA-BN FILO VACCINE, WHICH JANSSEN HAS LICENSED AS PART OF EBOLA VACCINE REGIMEN.
