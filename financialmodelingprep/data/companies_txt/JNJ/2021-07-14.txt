Johnson & Johnson Consumer Inc. Issues Voluntary Recall of Specific NEUTROGENA® and AVEENO® Aerosol Sunscreen Products Due to the Presence of Benzene. NEW BRUNSWICK, N.J., JULY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER INC. (JJCI) (NYSE: JNJ) IS VOLUNTARILY RECALLING ALL LOTS OF FIVE NEUTROGENA® AND AVEENO® AEROSOL SUNSCREEN PRODUCT LINES TO THE CONSUMER LEVEL.
