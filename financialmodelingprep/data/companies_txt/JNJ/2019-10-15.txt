Johnson & Johnson Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Johnson & Johnson announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 20,729 million compared to USD 20,348 million a year ago. Net income was USD 4,833 million compared to USD 3,934 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.81 compared to USD 1.44 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 61,312 million compared to USD 61,187 million a year ago. Net income was USD 14,189 million compared to USD 12,255 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 5.28 compared to USD 4.49 a year ago.
