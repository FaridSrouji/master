J&J Submits European Drug Application For Investigational Ebola Vaccine Regimen. Nov 7  - Johnson & Johnson <JNJ.N>::JOHNSON & JOHNSON ANNOUNCES SUBMISSION OF EUROPEAN MARKETING AUTHORISATION APPLICATIONS FOR JANSSEN’S INVESTIGATIONAL EBOLA VACCINE REGIMEN.J&J - TWO MAAS HAVE BEEN SUBMITTED IN PARALLEL SUPPORTING EACH VACCINE IN TWO-DOSE REGIMEN (AD26.ZEBOV, MVA-BN-FILO).J&J - VACCINE REGIMEN INCLUDES AD26.ZEBOV AS FIRST DOSE AND MVA-BN-FILO AS SECOND DOSE.J&J - DISCUSSIONS WITH FDA ONGOING TO DEFINE REQUIRED DATA SET FOR FILING OF JANSSEN EBOLA VACCINE REGIMEN UNDER ANIMAL RULE LICENSURE PATHWAY.J&J - JANSSEN IS WORKING IN COLLABORATION WITH WHO TO ENABLE REGISTRATION OF EBOLA VACCINE REGIMEN IN AFRICAN COUNTRIES.
