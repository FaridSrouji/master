Johnson & Johnson Announces Quarterly Dividend for Fourth Quarter 2019, Payable on December 10, 2019. Johnson & Johnson announced that its Board of Directors has declared a cash dividend for the fourth quarter of 2019 of $0.95 per share on the company's common stock. The dividend is payable on December 10, 2019 to shareholders of record at the close of business on November 26, 2019. The ex-dividend date is November 25, 2019.
