Johnson & Johnson Faces Class-Action Lawsuit Over Benzene-Tainted Neutrogena and Aveeno Sunscreen Products. SAN FRANCISCO, JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PHARMACEUTICAL PRODUCT-SAFETY LAWYERS AT THE BEASLEY ALLEN LAW FIRM HAVE FILED A FEDERAL CLASS-ACTION LAWSUIT ON BEHALF OF CONSUMERS WHO BOUGHT RECALLED BENZENE-TAINTED SUNSCREEN PRODUCTS MADE BY JOHNSON & JOHNSON (NYSE: JNJ) SUBSIDIARIES NEUTROGENA AND AVEENO. THE LAWSUIT SEEKS AN INJUNCTION AND DAMAGES ON BEHALF OF CONSUMERS WHO PURCHASED A RANGE OF NEUTROGENA AND AVEENO SUN-CARE PRODUCTS.
