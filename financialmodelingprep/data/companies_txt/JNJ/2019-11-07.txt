Janssen Announces Health Canada Approval Of Imbruvica In Combination With Obinutuzumab. Nov 8  - Johnson & Johnson <JNJ.N>::JANSSEN ANNOUNCES HEALTH CANADA APPROVAL OF IMBRUVICA® (IBRUTINIB) IN COMBINATION WITH OBINUTUZUMAB FOR TREATMENT-NAÏVE PATIENTS WITH CHRONIC LYMPHOCYTIC LEUKEMIA.JANSSEN INC. - ANNOUNCES HEALTH CANADA APPROVAL OF IMBRUVICA IN COMBINATION WITH OBINUTUZUMAB.JANSSEN - IMBRUVICA PRODUCT MONOGRAPH HAS BEEN UPDATED TO INCLUDE ADDITIONAL LONG-TERM EFFICACY DATA FOR IMBRUVICA USE AS A MONOTHERAPY IN CLL.JANSSEN - DATA DEMONSTRATES THAT MORE THAN SEVEN IN 10 TREATMENT-NAÏVE PATIENTS WERE STILL PROGRESSION FREE AFTER FOUR YEARS ON IMBRUVICA.
