Johnson & Johnson Names Jim Swanson as Executive Vice President and Global CIO. Former Monsanto CIO and Bayer CIO and Head of Digital Transformation for Crop Science Jim Swanson has been named Executive Vice President and Global CIO of Johnson & Johnson. Swanson is responsible for amplifying Johnson & Johnsons business impact and shaping its direction through the strategic use of technology. He will be based in New Jersey at the company headquarters.
