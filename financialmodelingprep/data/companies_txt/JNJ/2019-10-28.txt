J&J says 15 New Tests From Same Bottle Of Johnson's Baby Powder Previously Tested By FDA Find No Asbestos. Oct 29  - Johnson & Johnson Consumer Inc.::15 NEW TESTS FROM SAME BOTTLE OF JOHNSON'S BABY POWDER PREVIOUSLY TESTED BY FDA FIND NO ASBESTOS.JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER INC. - OVER 60 NEW TESTS OF RECALLED LOT CONDUCTED BY TWO THIRD-PARTY LABORATORIES FIND NO ASBESTOS.JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER - ADDITIONAL 48 NEW TESTS OF SAMPLES FROM SINGLE LOT OF OCT. 18 RECALLED BABY POWDER CONFIRM IT DOES NOT CONTAIN ASBESTOS.JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER - FOLLOWING RECALL, CO CONTRACTED 2 3RD-PARTY LABORATORIES TO EXPEDITE LARGE NUMBER OF TESTS OF RECALLED LOT OF BABY POWDER.JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER - TESTS ON THE JOHNSON'S BABY POWDER WERE CONDUCTED BY TWO THIRD-PARTY LABORATORIES AS PART OF ONGOING INVESTIGATION.JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER - IN ADDITION TO USING STANDARD PREPARATION ROOMS, ONE OF THE LABS DEVIATED FROM NORMAL PROTOCOL BY USING AN AUXILIARY ROOM.JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER - IN THAT LAB'S AUXILIARY ROOM, FIVE SAMPLES WERE PREPARED, AND THREE INITIALLY TESTED POSITIVE FOR ASBESTOS.JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER - LAB UNDERTOOK PROBE AND DETERMINED THAT PORTABLE AIR CONDITIONER IN USE IN AUXILIARY ROOM WAS CONTAMINATED WITH ASBESTOS.JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER INC - TESTS ON JOHNSON'S BABY POWDER DEMONSTRATE POSITIVE READOUTS ARE POSSIBLE DUE TO LAB CONTAMINATION.JOHNSON & JOHNSON CONSUMER INC. - NO ASBESTOS WAS DETECTED IN ANY OF SAMPLES OF BABY POWDER WHEN PREPARED IN STANDARD ROOM OF THE LAB.
