M&T Bank Corporation Announces Third Quarter Results. M&T BANK CORP ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER RESULTS.Q3 OPERATING EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.50.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $3.57 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q3 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.47.PROVISION FOR CREDIT LOSSES WAS $45 MILLION IN Q3 OF 2019, COMPARED WITH $16 MILLION IN YEAR-EARLIER QUARTER.NET LOAN CHARGE-OFFS WERE $36 MILLION DURING RECENT QUARTER, COMPARED WITH $16 MILLION IN Q3 OF 2018.M&T BANK - QTRLY NET INTEREST INCOME EXPRESSED ON A TAXABLE-EQUIVALENT BASIS $1.04 BILLION, LITTLE CHANGED FROM YEAR-EARLIER QUARTER.TANGIBLE EQUITY PER COMMON SHARE WAS $74.93 AT SEPTEMBER 30, 2019, COMPARED WITH $67.64 AT SEPTEMBER 30, 2018.
