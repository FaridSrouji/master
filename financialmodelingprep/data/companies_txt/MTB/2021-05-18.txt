M&T Bank Corporation Announces Second Quarter Common Stock Dividend. BUFFALO, N.Y., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK CORPORATION ("M&T") (NYSE:MTB) ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.10 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK.
