M&T Bank Once Again Named a Best Place to Work for Disability Inclusion. BUFFALO, N.Y., JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  M&T BANK  (NYSE: MTB) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FOR THE THIRD YEAR IN A ROW IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED AS ONE OF THE BEST PLACES TO WORK FOR DISABILITY INCLUSION IN THE 2021 DISABILITY EQUALITY INDEX (DEI).
