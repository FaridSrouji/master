M&T Bank Corporation to Participate in 2021 RBC Capital Markets Global Financial Institutions Conference. BUFFALO, N.Y., MARCH 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK CORPORATION (NYSE:MTB)("M&T") WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE RBC CAPITAL MARKETS GLOBAL FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS CONFERENCE BEING HELD IN A VIRTUAL FORMAT. REPRESENTATIVES OF M&T ARE SCHEDULED TO ADDRESS INVESTORS AND ANALYSTS ON MARCH 10, 2021...
