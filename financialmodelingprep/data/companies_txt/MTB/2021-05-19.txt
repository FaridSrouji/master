M&T Bank Names Brian Klock as Next Head of Markets and Investor Relations. BUFFALO, N.Y., MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK CORPORATION ("M&T") (NYSE: MTB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BRIAN KLOCK JOINED M&T BANK AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, MAY 17, 2021 AND WORK CLOSELY WITH CURRENT HEAD OF INVESTOR RELATIONS DON MACLEOD AS HE TRANSITIONS INTO RETIREMENT.
