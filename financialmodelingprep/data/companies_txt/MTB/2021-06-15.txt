M&T Bank and Life-Skills App Magnusmode Collaborate to Support Customers with Autism, Cognitive and Intellectual Disabilities. BUFFALO, N.Y., JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- M&T BANK ANNOUNCED IT WILL BE THE FIRST U.S. BANK TO TEAM UP WITH MAGNUSCARDS™ BY MAGNUSMODE, A FREE APP DEDICATED TO MAKING THE WORLD A MORE ACCESSIBLE PLACE FOR PEOPLE WITH COGNITIVE AND INTELLECTUAL DISABILITIES.
