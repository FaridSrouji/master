Salesforce Chair and CEO Marc Benioff to Participate in World Economic Forum's Global Technology Governance Summit 2021. SAN FRANCISCO, APRIL 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SALESFORCE (NYSE: CRM), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN CRM, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHAIR AND CEO MARC BENIOFF, AND EVENT CO-CHAIR, WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE WORLD ECONOMIC FORUM'S GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY GOVERNANCE SUMMIT TAKING PLACE VIRTUALLY FROM APRIL 5-7, 2021. AT THE EVENT, BENIOFF WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE FOLLOWING SESSIONS: MONDAY, APRIL 5: BENIOFF WILL PARTICIPATE IN A PANEL DISCUSSION ENTITLED "TECHNOLOGY GOVERNANCE OUTLOOK.
