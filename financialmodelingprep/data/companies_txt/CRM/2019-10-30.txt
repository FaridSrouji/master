Five9, Inc. Launches the Five9 Einstein Engagement Bot on Salesforce Appexchange. Five9, Inc.  has launched the Five9 Einstein Engagement Bot on Salesforce AppExchange, empowering enterprise contact centers to engage customers with intelligent, compelling interactions. This integration enables Einstein Bot to engage with the customer, determine the intent of their need, provide quick answers or, if necessary, intelligently escalate to a chat or a phone call back from an agent. In the case of escalation, the full context of the Einstein Bot interaction is leveraged for routing to the best agent and passed to the agent for an easy handover.
