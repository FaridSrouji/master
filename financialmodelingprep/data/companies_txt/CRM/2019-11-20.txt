CaixaBank to Develop Digital Financial Services with Salesforce. Salesforce and CaixaBank reached an agreement to collaborate in the development of digital financial services. The two companies reached the dreamforce 19 conference that the tech firm holds these days in San Francisco (California, USA) and explained to a small group of Spanish journalists the CEO of Salesforce in Spain, Enrique Polo Lara, collaboration will focus on the design function. In particular, CaixaBank will join the Salesforce Financial Services Cloud Design Partner Program, a Salesforce international research and development program made up of a small group of banks that, together with the technology company team, are experimenting with new ways of approaching customers. In addition, the agreement also calls for CaixaBank's adoption of Salesforce technology in all segments of its business, from private banking to small and medium-sized enterprises and corporate banking.
