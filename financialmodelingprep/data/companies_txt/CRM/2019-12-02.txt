Salesforce.Com, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year of Fiscal Year 2020 and Revenue Guidance for the First Quarter and Full Year of Fiscal Year 2021. salesforce.com, inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter and full year of fiscal year 2020 and revenue guidance for the first quarter and full year of fiscal year 2021. For the fourth quarter, the company expects revenue to be in the range of $4.743 billionto $4.753 billion and GAAP loss per share to be in the range of $0.04 to $0.03.For the fiscal year, the company expects revenue to be in the range of $16.99 billion to $17.00 billion and GAAP earnings per share to be in the range of $0.44 to $0.45 .For the first quarter of fiscal year 2021, the company expects revenue to be in the range of $4.800 billion to $4.835 billion. For the fiscal year 2021, the company expects revenue to be in the range of $20.80 billion to $20.90 billion.
