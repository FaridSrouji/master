Comcast Business and Palo Alto Networks Partner to Secure the Modern Workplace. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST BUSINESS WILL PARTNER WITH GLOBAL CYBERSECURITY LEADER PALO ALTO NETWORKS TO BRING ADVANCED SECURITY SOLUTIONS THAT HELP BUSINESSES PROTECT THEIR NETWORK ASSETS FROM CYBER THREATS ACROSS A DYNAMIC WORKPLACE THAT SPANS OFFICE, REMOTE, AND CLOUD ENVIRONMENTS. THE AGREEMENT WILL HELP MIDSIZE AND ENTERPRISE CUSTOMERS ENSURE PERFORMANCE AND SPEED WHILE REDUCING COMPLEXITY THROUGH COMPREHENSIVE AND INTEGRATED CLOUD-DELIVERED SECURITY CAPABILITIES. COMCAST BUSINE
