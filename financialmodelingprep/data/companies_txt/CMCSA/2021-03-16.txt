Comcast Opens Xfinity Store in Downtown Crossing. BOSTON, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COMCAST TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS OPENED A NEW XFINITY STORE IN DOWNTOWN CROSSING, AT 350 WASHINGTON STREET.  THE 3,300 SQUARE FOOT STORE WILL PROVIDE A VARIETY OF INTERACTIVE PRODUCT DEMONSTRATION AREAS FOR ITS FULL SUITE OF XFINITY AND COMCAST BUSINESS PRODUCTS AND SERVICES.
