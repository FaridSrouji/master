Comcast Corporation Announces Preliminary Results of 2021 Annual Meeting of Shareholders. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--COMCAST CORPORATION TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRELIMINARY RESULTS OF ITS 2021 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS HELD THIS MORNING. FINAL VOTING RESULTS WILL BE FILED WITH THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION ON A FORM 8-K WITHIN FOUR BUSINESS DAYS.
