Comcast Names Michael Parker as Senior Vice President of Keystone Region. Comcast Corporation announced that Michael Parker has been named Senior Vice President of the Keystone Region, where he will oversee more than 3,600 Comcast employees and lead operations, financial performance and customer service for approximately 1.7 million customers in Pennsylvania, Ohio, West Virginia, and Maryland. Parker has spent the last 18 years in progressively larger leadership positions at Comcast. Most recently he served as Senior Vice President of Comcast's Western New England Region, which included operations in Connecticut, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Vermont and New York. Previously, Parker served as Vice President, Operations for Comcast's Greater Chicago Region.
