Comcast Announces Redemption Of $1.430 Billion Of 4.05% Senior Notes Due August 2046. COMCAST ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF $1.430 BILLION OF 4.05% SENIOR NOTES DUE AUGUST 2046.COMCAST CORP - ELECTED TO REDEEM ON AUGUST 1, 2020 ALL $1.430 BILLION OF 4.05% SENIOR NOTES DUE AUGUST 1, 2046.COMCAST CORP - TO REDEEM SENIOR NOTES DUE AUGUST 2046 AT REDEMPTION PRICE EQUAL TO 100% OF PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF NOTES BEING REDEEMED.COMCAST CORP - NOTES ARE TRADED ON TAIPEI EXCHANGE.
