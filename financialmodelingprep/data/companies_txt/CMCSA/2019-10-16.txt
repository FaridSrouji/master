Comcast Corporation Names Michael Parker as Senior Vice President of the Keystone Region. Comcast Corporation announced that Michael Parker has been named Senior Vice President of the Keystone Region, where he will oversee more than 3,600 Comcast employees and lead operations, financial performance and customer service for approximately 1.7 million customers in Pennsylvania, Ohio, West Virginia, and Maryland. A veteran of the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve, Parker has spent the last 18 years in progressively larger leadership positions at Comcast. Most recently he served as Senior Vice President of Comcast's Western New England Region, which included operations in Connecticut, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Vermont and New York. Under his leadership, Western New England delivered excellent financial, customer experience and employee engagement results, including winning numerous "Best Places to Work" awards from area publications.
