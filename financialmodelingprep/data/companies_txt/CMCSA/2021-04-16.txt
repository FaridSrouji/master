Comcast Launching 25 WiFi-Connected "Lift Zones" Across Connecticut. HARTFORD, CONN., APRIL 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF ITS ONGOING COMMITMENT TO HELP CONNECT LOW-INCOME FAMILIES TO THE INTERNET SO THEY CAN FULLY PARTICIPATE IN EDUCATIONAL OPPORTUNITIES AND THE DIGITAL ECONOMY, COMCAST TODAY ANNOUNCED PLANS TO EQUIP 25 DIFFERENT CONNECTICUT LOCATIONS – 13 OF WHICH ARE NOW OPEN WITH PLANS FOR 12 ADDITIONAL SITES.
