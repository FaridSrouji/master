Comcast Declares Quarterly Dividend. PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Comcast Corporation (Nasdaq: CMCSA) announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.23 a share on the company's common stock. The quarterly dividend is payable on January 27, 2021 to shareholders of record as of the close of business on January 6, 2021.
