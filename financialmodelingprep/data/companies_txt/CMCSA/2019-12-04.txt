Comcast Corporation Announces Resignation of David L. Cohen, Senior Executive Vice President. On December 5, 2019, David L. Cohen, Senior Executive Vice President of Comcast Corporation announced that he will step down from his operational responsibilities effective January 1, 2020, and then transition from Senior Executive Vice President to Senior Counselor to Comcast’s Chief Executive Officer beginning December 31, 2020.
