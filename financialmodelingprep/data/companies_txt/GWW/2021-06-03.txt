Grainger Launches Its 2021 Corporate Responsibility Report. CHICAGO, JUNE 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GRAINGER (NYSE: GWW), THE LEADING BROAD LINE SUPPLIER OF MAINTENANCE, REPAIR AND OPERATING (MRO) PRODUCTS SERVING BUSINESSES AND INSTITUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS 2021 CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY REPORT ON WWW.GRAINGERESG.COM. THIS MARKS GRAINGER'S 10TH CONSECUTIVE YEAR OF PUBLISHING THIS REPORT, CONTINUING THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO ADDRESSING KEY ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) ISSUES AND OPPORTUNITIES.
