UDR, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019; Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. UDR, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019. For the quarter, the company expects net income per share in a range of $0.36 to $0.38.For the full year, the company now expects net income per share in a range of $0.68 to $0.70 compared to $0.74 to $0.77 expected previously for the same year. Revenue growth in a range of 3.50% to 3.90% compared to 3.40% to 4.00% expected previously for the same year. Net operating income growth in a range of 4.00% to 4.40% compared to 3.75% to 4.50% expected previously for the same year. Physical occupancy in a range of 96.8% to 97.0% compared to 96.8% to 97.0% expected previously for the same year.
