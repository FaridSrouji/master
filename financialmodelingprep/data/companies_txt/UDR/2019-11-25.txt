UDR, Inc. (NYSE:UDR) completed the acquisition of 50% stake in 10 UDR/MetLife Investment Management joint venture operating communities from Metlife Investment Advisors, LLC.. UDR, Inc. (NYSE:UDR) agreed to acquire 50% stake in 10 UDR/MetLife Investment Management joint venture operating communities from Metlife Investment Advisors, LLC on August 12, 2019. In related transactions, UDR, Inc. agreed to acquire 50% stake in one community under development and four accretive development land sites and agreed to sell 50% stake in five UDR/MetLife Investment Management Joint Venture communities. After accounting for the assumption of in-place debt totaling $540 million, UDR's net cash outflow to complete the swap is expected to be approximately $105 million. The consideration is subject to closing price adjustments.  The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions and is expected to close during the fourth quarter of 2019. The transaction will be accretive to future cash flow growth and estimated to be $0.00 to $0.01 accretive to 2020 net income per share and $0.01 to $0.02 accretive to 2020 Funds from Operations as Adjusted per share.
UDR, Inc. (NYSE:UDR) completed the acquisition of 50% stake in 10 UDR/MetLife Investment Management joint venture operating communities from Metlife Investment Advisors, LLC on November 25, 2019. In a related transaction, UDR sold 50% stake in five UDR/MetLife Investment Management Joint Venture communities.
