Capital One Reports First Quarter 2021 Net Income of $3.3 billion, or $7.03 per share. MCLEAN, VA., APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CAPITAL ONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NYSE: COF) TODAY ANNOUNCED NET INCOME FOR THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2021 OF $3.3 BILLION, OR $7.03 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE, COMPARED WITH NET INCOME OF $2.6 BILLION, OR $5.35 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE IN THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020, AND WITH A NET LOSS OF $1.3 BILLION, OR $3.10 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE IN THE FIRST QUARTER OF 2020.
