Jamie Henderson to Join Capital One as Head of Commercial Real Estate. Capital One announced hat Jamie Henderson will join as Executive Vice President and Head of Commercial Real Estate starting in January 2020. Henderson will report directly to Michael Slocum, President of Commercial Banking and Northeast Market President. Henderson has nearly 20 years of experience in commercial real estate and served most recently as a Partner and Head of Real Estate Debt at Ares Management Corporation and CEO of Ares Commercial Real Estate Corporation. Prior to joining Ares in April 2017 Henderson held a variety of leadership roles at Barings Real Estate Advisors, a global advisory firm, and its predecessor companies Cornerstone Real Estate Advisors and Babson Capital Management.
