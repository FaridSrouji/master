Constellation Brands to Report First Quarter Fiscal 2022 Financial Results; Host Conference Call June 30, 2021. VICTOR, N.Y., JUNE 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CONSTELLATION BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: STZ AND STZ.B), A LEADING BEVERAGE ALCOHOL COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WILL REPORT FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR ITS FISCAL FIRST QUARTER ENDED MAY 31, 2021, ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 30, 2021, BEFORE THE OPEN OF THE U.S. MARKETS. A CONFERENCE CALL TO DISCUSS THE FINANCIAL RESULTS AND OUTLOOK WILL BE HOSTED BY PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER BILL NEWLANDS AND EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER GARTH HANKINSON AT 11:30 A.M. EDT, JUNE 30, 2021.
