Constellation Brands, Inc. Declares Cash Dividend on Class A, B, C Common Stock, Payable on November 22, 2019. The Board of Directors of Constellation Brands, Inc. declared a quarterly cash dividend in the amount of $0.75 per issued and outstanding share of the Company’s Class A Common Stock, $0.68 per issued and outstanding share of the Company’s Class B Common Stock, and $0.68 per issued and outstanding share of the Company’s Class 1 Common Stock, in each case payable on November 22, 2019, to stockholders of record of each respective class as of the close of business on November 8, 2019.
