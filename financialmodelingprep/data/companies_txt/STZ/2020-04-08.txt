Constellation Brands Provides Update On Mexico Brewery Operations. CONSTELLATION BRANDS PROVIDES UPDATE ON MEXICO BREWERY OPERATIONS IN THE MIDST OF COVID-19 OUTBREAK.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - ANNOUNCED ADDITIONAL STEPS IT IS TAKING TO REDUCE ITS BREWERY PRODUCTION ACTIVITIES IN MEXICO.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - OVER PAST SEVERAL WEEKS, CO SHIFTED RESOURCES TO ACCELERATE PRODUCTION OF HIGH-VOLUME PRODUCTS.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - BUILT SUBSTANTIAL PRODUCT SUPPLY ACROSS WAREHOUSE AND DISTRIBUTION NETWORK IN U.S.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - REMAIN CONFIDENT IN ABILITY TO CONTINUE MEETING NEEDS OF U.S. CONSUMERS & DO NOT EXPECT ANY NEAR-TERM SERVICE DISRUPTIONS.
