Constellation Brands And E. & J. Gallo Agree To Revise Wine And Spirits Agreement In Connection With Federal Trade Commission Review. CONSTELLATION BRANDS AND E. & J. GALLO AGREE TO REVISE WINE AND SPIRITS AGREEMENT IN CONNECTION WITH FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION REVIEW.CONSTELLATION BRANDS - DEAL REVISED TO ADDRESS FTC AREAS OF COMPETITIVE CONCERN PRIMARILY RELATED TO SPARKLING WINE, BRANDY, DESSERT WINE.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - TRANSACTION PRICE WILL BE REVISED TO APPROXIMATELY $1.1 BILLION.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - AGREES TO ENTER AN AGREEMENT WITH E. & J. GALLO TO DIVEST ITS NEW ZEALAND-BASED NOBILO WINE BRAND FOR $130 MILLION.CONSTELLATION BRANDS - REVISED DEAL PRICE OF ABOUT $1.1 BILLION, OF WHICH $250 MILLION IS EARNOUT BASED ON DIVESTED BRAND PERFORMANCE OVER 2-YEAR PERIOD.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE BY END OF FISCAL 2020.CONSTELLATION BRANDS - WILL RETAIN SELECT BRANDS IN SPARKLING WINE, BRANDY, DESSERT WINE, AND CONCENTRATE CATEGORIES.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - REVISED DEAL & DIVESTMENT OF EXCLUDED BRANDS ARE EXPECTED TO CLOSE BY END OF FISCAL 2020.CONSTELLATION BRANDS - WILL PURSUE OTHER OPPORTUNITIES TO DIVEST BRANDS EXCLUDED FROM AGREEMENT, WHICH IS ALIGNED WITH CO'S PORTFOLIO STRATEGY.CONSTELLATION BRANDS INC - NOBILO WINE BRAND DEAL IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN FIRST HALF OF FISCAL 2021.
