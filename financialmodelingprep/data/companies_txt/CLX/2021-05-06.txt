Kingsford® and Everdure Announce Grilling Brand Partnership. OAKLAND, CALIF. , MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KINGSFORD, AMERICA'S FAVORITE WOOD-FIRED FUEL FOR MORE THAN 100 YEARS, HAS ANNOUNCED A PARTNERSHIP WITH EVERDURE BY HESTON BLUMENTHAL, ONE OF THE FASTEST GROWING GRILL BRANDS IN THE U.S. THE BRANDS ARE TEAMING UP TO BRING THE NEXT GENERATION OF GRILLERS THE BEST IN DESIGN, INNOVATION AND UNIQUE FLAVOR WITH EVERDURE GRILLS FUELED BY KINGSFORD CHARCOAL.
