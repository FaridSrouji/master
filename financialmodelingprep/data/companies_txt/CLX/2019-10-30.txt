The Clorox Company Confirms Earnings Guidance for the Fiscal Year 2020. The Clorox Company confirmed earnings guidance for the fiscal year 2020. For the year 2020, the company continues to anticipate that sales will be down from the low single digits to up 1%, reflecting about 2 points of foreign currency headwinds. Importantly, the company's organic sales outlook remains unchanged, with a range of 1% to 3% sales growth, driven primarily by a robust innovation program and the expectation of a return to growth in Bags and Wraps and Charcoal in the back half of the fiscal year. Net of all these factors, the company continues to anticipate fiscal year 2020 diluted EPS to be in the range of $6.05 to $6.25.
