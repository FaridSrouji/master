United Airlines Says Clorox Products Will Be Used At United's Hub Airports. UNITED AIRLINES LAUNCHES UNITED CLEANPLUS: A NEW STANDARD OF CLEANLINESS AND SAFETY IN PARTNERSHIP WITH CLOROX AND CLEVELAND CLINIC.UNITED AIRLINES SAYS CLOROX PRODUCTS WILL BE USED AT UNITED’S HUB AIRPORTS.UNITED AIRLINES - WILL ALLOW CUSTOMERS TO TAKE ALTERNATIVE FLIGHTS WHEN CO EXPECTS A FLIGHT TO OPERATE OVER 70% CAPACITY.UNITED AIRLINES - WILL LIMIT ADVANCE SEAT SELECTIONS WHERE POSSIBLE.UNITED AIRLINES SAYS WILL REQUIRE ALL EMPLOYEES AND CUSTOMERS ON BOARD TO WEAR A MASK OR FACE COVERING.
Clorox Increases Quarterly Dividend 5% To $1.11 Per Share. CLOROX INCREASES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND 5% TO $1.11 PER SHARE.
