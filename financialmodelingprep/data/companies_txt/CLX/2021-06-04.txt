Hamilton Beach Brands, Inc. and The Clorox Company Enter into an Exclusive Multiyear Trademark Licensing Agreement. RICHMOND, VA., JUNE 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- HAMILTON BEACH BRANDS, INC., A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF HAMILTON BEACH BRANDS HOLDING COMPANY (NYSE: HBB), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO AN EXCLUSIVE MULTIYEAR TRADEMARK LICENSING AGREEMENT WITH THE CLOROX COMPANY (NYSE: CLX).
