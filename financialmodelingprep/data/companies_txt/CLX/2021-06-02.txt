Clorox Increases Quarterly Dividend 5% to $1.16 Per Share. OAKLAND, CALIF., JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CLOROX COMPANY (NYSE: CLX) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A 5% INCREASE IN THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND, FROM $1.11 TO $1.16 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK.
