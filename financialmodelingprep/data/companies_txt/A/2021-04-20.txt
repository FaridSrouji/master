Agilent Named One of the Best U.S. Companies for Diversity for Third Straight Year. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: A) TODAY WAS NAMED ONE OF THE TOP U.S. COMPANIES FOR DIVERSITY, MARKING THE THIRD STRAIGHT YEAR IT HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED FOR ITS COMMITMENT TO FOSTERING A DIVERSE AND INCLUSIVE WORKPLACE. SPECIFICALLY, AGILENT RANKED NO. 83 OUT OF 500 COMPANIES ON FORBES'S 2021 LIST OF AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS FOR DIVERSITY. THE RATING WAS BASED ON DIMENSIONS OF DIVERSITY INCLUDING GENDER, ETHNICITY, LGBTQ+, AGE, AND THE DIFFERENTLY ABLED. AGILENT
