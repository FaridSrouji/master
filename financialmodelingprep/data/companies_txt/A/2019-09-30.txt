Molecular Assemblies, Inc. announced that it has received $12.2 million in funding from iSelect Fund Management, LLC, Argonautic Ventures, Alexandria Venture Investments, LLC, Keshif Ventures, LLC, Agilent Technologies, Inc.. On October 1, 2019, Molecular Assemblies, Inc. closed the transaction. The company received $12,200,000 led by new investor, iSelect Fund, LLC, a fund managed by iSelect Fund Management, LLC and Argonautic Ventures on October 1, 2019. The transaction also included participation from returning investors, Agilent Technologies, Inc. (NYSE:A), Alexandria Venture Investments, LLC, and Keshif Ventures, LLC. The company raised $3,718,809 pursuant to exemption provided under regulation D. The transaction included participation from eight investors pursuant to exemption provided under regulation D.
