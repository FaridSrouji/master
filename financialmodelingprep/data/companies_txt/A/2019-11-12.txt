MobilION Systems, Inc. announced that it has received $15.4 million in funding from Agilent Technologies, Inc., IP Group Inc., Cultivation Capital, iSelect Fund Management, LLC, and another investor. On November 12, 2019, MobilION Systems, Inc. closed the transaction. The company received $15,400,000 in its series A round led by Agilent Technologies, Inc. (NYSE:A). The transaction included participation from IP Group Inc., Hostplus, Cultivation Capital, and iSelect Fund Management, LLC. The company received $11,399,999 in its second and final tranche.
