Akoya Biosciences, Inc. announced that it has received $50 million in a round of funding led by PJC Capital Management LLC on December 5, 2019. The transaction included participation from new investors Innovatus Capital Partners, LLC and Agilent Technologies, Inc. (NYSE:A) along with returning investor Telegraph Hill Partners. As a part of transaction, Thomas Schnettler of PJC Capital Management LLC will join company board of directors.
