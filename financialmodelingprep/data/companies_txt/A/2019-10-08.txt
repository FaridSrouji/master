Agilent Technologies, Inc. Opens State-of-the-Art Spectroscopy Site in the UK. Agilent Technologies, Inc. announced the opening of a new state-of-the-art facility for spectroscopy research and development at the Harwell Science and Innovation Campus in Oxfordshire, UK. A leader in molecular spectroscopy, the new Agilent site will be a major research and development hub for laser spectroscopy. It will also incorporate Agilent’s Raman spectroscopy business, formerly known as Cobalt Light Systems (acquired by Agilent in 2017). Agilent is focusing on investment in two main areas: research and development to enable customers to perform better science, and harmonizing existing product lines to ensure an optimal customer experience. The site will also accommodate the company’s Laser Spectroscopy Center of Excellence (LSCE), focusing on research and development in the field of vibrational spectroscopy.
