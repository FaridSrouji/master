Agilent Receives Expanded FDA Approval for PD-L1 IHC 22C3 pharmDx in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer. SANTA CLARA, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC. (NYSE: A) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED FDA APPROVAL FOR THE USE OF PD-L1 IHC 22C3 PHARMDX AS AN AID IN IDENTIFYING PATIENTS WITH TRIPLE-NEGATIVE BREAST CANCER (TNBC) FOR TREATMENT WITH KEYTRUDA® (PEMBROLIZUMAB). PD-L1 EXPRESSION IS A CRITICAL BIOMARKER FOR RESPONSE TO ANTI-PD-1 THERAPIES SUCH AS KEYTRUDA.
