Agilent Technologies Announces Twist Bioscience Agrees To Pay $22.5 Million Settlement In Ip Litigation. AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES ANNOUNCES TWIST BIOSCIENCE AGREES TO PAY $22.5 MILLION SETTLEMENT IN IP LITIGATION.AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES - SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT PROVIDES A LICENSE TO TWIST FOR DISCRETE ASPECTS OF CO'S OLIGO-SYNTHESIS TECHNOLOGY.AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC - SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT RESOLVES ALL OUTSTANDING LITIGATION BETWEEN CO, TWIST BIOSCIENCE.
Agilent Technologies Says CEO's FY 2019 Total Compensation Was $12.52 Mln Vs $11.7 Mln In FY 2018. AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES INC SAYS CEO MICHAEL MCMULLEN'S FY 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $12.52 MILLION VERSUS $11.7 MILLION IN FY 2018 - SEC FILING.AGILENT TECHNOLOGIES - FOR FY 2019, ESTIMATE OF RATIO OF ANNUAL TOTAL COMPENSATION OF CEO TO ANNUAL TOTAL COMPENSATION OF MEDIAN EMPLOYEE WAS 179 TO 1.
