Agilent Technologies Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the First Quarter and Full Fiscal Year of 2020. Agilent Technologies Inc. provided earnings guidance for the first quarter and full fiscal year of 2020. For the first quarter, the company expects revenue in the range of $1.340 billion to $1.355 billion, representing core revenue growth of 2.5% to 3.5%.For the full year 2020, the company expects revenue to in the range of 5.50 billion to $5.55 billion, representing core growth of 4% to 5%.
