Cboe Global Markets Completes Acquisition of Chi-X Asia Pacific. CHICAGO, JUNE 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CBOE GLOBAL MARKETS, INC. (CBOE: CBOE), A LEADING PROVIDER OF GLOBAL MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE AND TRADABLE PRODUCTS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF CHI-X ASIA PACIFIC HOLDINGS, LTD. (CHI-X ASIA PACIFIC), AN ALTERNATIVE MARKET OPERATOR AND PROVIDER OF INNOVATIVE MARKET SOLUTIONS.
