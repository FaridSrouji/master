Citrix Systems Says Entered Into Amended & Restated Credit Agreement Which Amends & Restates Co's Credit Agreement, Dated As Of Jan 7, 2015. CITRIX SYSTEMS SAYS ON NOV 26 ENTERED INTO AMENDED & RESTATED CREDIT AGREEMENT WHICH AMENDS & RESTATES CO'S CREDIT AGREEMENT, DATED AS OF JAN 7, 2015.CITRIX SYSTEMS INC - CREDIT AGREEMENT PROVIDES FOR A $250 MILLION UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY FOR A TERM OF FIVE YEARS - SEC FILING.CITRIX SYSTEMS - CO MAY ELECT TO INCREASE REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY BY UP TO $250 MILLION IF EXISTING OR NEW LENDERS PROVIDE ADDITIONAL REVOLVING COMMITMENTS.
