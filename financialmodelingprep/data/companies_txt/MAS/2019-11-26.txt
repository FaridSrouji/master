Masco Corp Announces $400 Mln Accelerated Share Repurchase. MASCO CORPORATION ANNOUNCES $400 MILLION ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE.MASCO CORP - HAS ENTERED INTO AN ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH ROYAL BANK OF CANADA TO REPURCHASE $400 MILLION OF MASCO'S COMMON STOCK.MASCO CORP - UNDER ASR AGREEMENT, MASCO WILL RECEIVE INITIAL DELIVERY OF 7.3 MILLION SHARES ON NOV 26, 2019.MASCO CORP - THIS AGREEMENT IS PART OF MASCO'S EXISTING SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORIZATION OF $2 BILLION WHICH WAS ANNOUNCED ON SEPT 17, 2019.
