Masco Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividend. LIVONIA, MICH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MASCO CORPORATION (NYSE: MAS) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $.14 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON FEBRUARY 8, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JANUARY 8, 2021. HEADQUARTERED IN LIVONIA, MICHIGAN, MASCO CORPORATION IS A GLOBAL LEADER IN THE DESIGN, MANUFACTURE AND DISTRIBUTION OF BRANDED HOME IMPROVEMENT AND BUILDING PRODUCTS.
