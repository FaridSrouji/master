Activision Blizzard, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year of 2019. Activision Blizzard, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter and full year of 2019. For the quarter, the company expects net revenues of $1,812 million and EPS of $0.29. For the year, the company expects net revenues of $6,315 million and EPS of $1.56.
