Eni: Vår Energi Completes Acquisition Of Exxonmobil's Upstream Assets In Norway. VÅR ENERGI COMPLETES THE ACQUISITION OF EXXONMOBIL'S UPSTREAM ASSETS IN NORWAY.ACQUIRED FIELDS WILL ADD DAILY PRODUCTION OF ABOUT 150,000 BARRELS OF OIL EQUIVALENT (BOEPD).TOTAL PRODUCTION WILL BE ABOUT 300,000 BOEPD GROWING TO ABOUT 350,000 BOEPD IN 2023.
