Exxon Mobil Corp. Appoints Joseph L. Hooley to its Board of Directors, Effective January 1, 2020. Exxon Mobil Corp. reported that Joseph L. Hooley has been elected to its board of directors, effective January 1, 2020. Hooley currently serves as the non-executive board chairman of State Street Corp. and was its chief executive officer from 2010 to 2018. Hooley's previous experience leading a global financial services organization, along with operational and risk management expertise, will bring valuable skills to the ExxonMobil board.
