Exxon Mobil Corporation Announces Operating Results for the Third Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2019. Exxon Mobil Corporation announced operating results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, the company reported net production of crude oil, natural gas liquids, bitumen and synthetic oil of 2,392,000 barrels per day against 2,286,000 barrels per day a year ago. Oil equivalent production was 3,899 koebd against 3,786 koebd a year ago.For the nine months, the company reported net production of crude oil, natural gas liquids, bitumen and synthetic oil of 2,369,000 barrels per day against 2,238,000 barrels per day a year ago. Oil equivalent production was 3,909 koebd against 3,774 koebd a year ago.
