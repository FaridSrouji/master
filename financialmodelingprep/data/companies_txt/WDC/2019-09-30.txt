Western Digital Corporation, Annual General Meeting, Nov 14, 2019. Western Digital Corporation, Annual General Meeting, Nov 14, 2019, at 08:00 Pacific Standard Time. Location: The Fairmont San Jose 170 S. Market Street San Jose California United States Agenda: To consider the election of the eight director nominees named in the attached proxy statement to serve until next annual meeting of stockholders and until their respective successors are duly elected and qualified; to consider the approval on an advisory basis of the named executive officer compensation disclosed in the attached proxy statement; to consider the approval of the amendment and restatement of the 2017 Performance Incentive Plan to, among other things, increase by 6 million the number of shares of the common stock available for issuance under that plan; and to consider the ratification of the appointment of KPMG LLP as the independent registered public accounting firm for fiscal 2020.
