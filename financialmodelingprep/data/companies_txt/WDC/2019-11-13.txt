Western Digital Corp. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on January 21, 2020. Western Digital Corp. announced that the board of directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.50 per share of common stock. The cash dividend will be paid on January 21, 2020, to the company's stockholders of record as of January 3, 2020.
