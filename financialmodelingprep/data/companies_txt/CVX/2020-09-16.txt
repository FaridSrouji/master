Chevron Says The Hague Court Found That International Tribunal Acted Within Its Remit In Ecuador Dispute. THE HAGUE COURT RULES FOR CHEVRON IN ECUADOR DISPUTE.CHEVRON - INTERNATIONAL ARBITRAL TRIBUNAL FOUND $9.5 BILLION ECUADORIAN JUDGMENT AGAINST CO PROCURED THROUGH FRAUD, CORRUPTION BY PLAINTIFFS’ LEGAL TEAM.CHEVRON - INTERNATIONAL ARBITRAL TRIBUNAL ALSO REJECTED UNDERLYING ENVIRONMENTAL ALLEGATIONS AGAINST CHEVRON.CHEVRON - TRIBUNAL FOUND THAT CO'S SUBSIDIARY COMPLETED AN ENVIRONMENTAL REMEDIATION PROGRAM SUPERVISED AND APPROVED BY REPUBLIC OF ECUADOR.CHEVRON - DISTRICT COURT OF THE HAGUE UPHELD THE AWARD IN FULL AND REJECTED THE REPUBLIC OF ECUADOR’S ATTEMPT TO SET IT ASIDE.CHEVRON - COURT FOUND THAT INTERNATIONAL TRIBUNAL ACTED WITHIN ITS REMIT WHEN ISSUING THE AWARD.CHEVRON - COURT HELD THAT "BECAUSE NONE OF SETTING ASIDE GROUNDS BROUGHT FORWARD BY ECUADOR SUCCEED, CLAIMS WILL BE DENIED".CHEVRON-COURT CONCLUDED TRIBUNAL’S ORDERS PROPERLY SOUGHT TO “REMOVE THE CONSEQUENCES OF A FRAUDULENT JUDGMENT THAT WAS RENDERED BY A CORRUPT JUDGE.".CHEVRON-COURT’S RULING FOLLOWS DECISIONS FROM COURTS IN ARGENTINA, BRAZIL, CANADA, GIBRALTAR, U.S. REJECTING FRAUDULENT ECUADORIAN JUDGMENT AGAINST CO.
