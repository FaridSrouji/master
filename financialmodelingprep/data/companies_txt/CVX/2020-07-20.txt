Chevron To Acquire Noble Energy In All-Stock Deal Valued At $5 Bln. CHEVRON ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE NOBLE ENERGY.CHEVRON CORP - TO ACQUIRE ALL OF OUTSTANDING SHARES OF NOBLE ENERGY IN AN ALL-STOCK TRANSACTION VALUED AT $5 BILLION, OR $10.38 PER SHARE..CHEVRON CORP - DEAL DELIVERS $300 MILLION IN ANTICIPATED ANNUAL PRE-TAX SYNERGIES.CHEVRON CORP - NOBLE ENERGY SHAREHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE 0.1191 SHARES OF CHEVRON FOR EACH NOBLE ENERGY SHARE.CHEVRON CORP - TOTAL ENTERPRISE VALUE, INCLUDING DEBT, OF TRANSACTION IS $13 BILLION.CHEVRON CORP - DEAL ACCRETIVE TO ROCE, FREE CASH FLOW AND EARNINGS.CHEVRON CORP - UPON CLOSING OF DEAL, CHEVRON WILL ISSUE ABOUT 58 MILLION SHARES OF STOCK.CHEVRON CORP - TRANSACTION IS EXPECTED TO ACHIEVE RUN-RATE OPERATING AND OTHER COST SYNERGIES OF $300 MILLION BEFORE-TAX WITHIN A YEAR OF CLOSING.CHEVRON - FOLLOWING CLOSING OF TRANSACTION, NOBLE ENERGY SHAREHOLDERS WILL OWN ABOUT 3% OF COMBINED COMPANY.CHEVRON CORP - TRANSACTION HAS BEEN UNANIMOUSLY APPROVED BY BOARDS OF DIRECTORS OF BOTH COMPANIES.CHEVRON CORP - ACQUISITION IS SUBJECT TO NOBLE ENERGY SHAREHOLDER APPROVAL.
