Chevron Corporation (NYSE:CVX) signed an agreement to acquire 40% stake in three deepwater blocks in the Mexican Gulf from Royal Dutch Shell plc (ENXTAM:RDSA) earlier in the week ending October 4, 2019. The deal has been approved earlier by National Hydrocarbon Commission.
