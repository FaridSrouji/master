Chevron Announces Third Quarter 2020 Results. SAN RAMON, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Chevron Corporation (NYSE: CVX) today reported a loss of $207 million ($(0.12) per share - diluted) for third quarter 2020, compared with earnings of $2.6 billion ($1.36 per share - diluted) in third quarter 2019. Included in the current quarter was a charge of $130 million attributable to a tax item related to an international upstream end-of-contract settlement and a non-cash provision of $90 million for remediation of a former mining asset.
