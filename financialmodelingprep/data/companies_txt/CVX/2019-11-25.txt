Hocol Petroleum Limited agreed to acquire 43% stake in Chuchupa and Ballena fields from Chevron Corporation (NYSE:CVX) on November 26, 2019. The deal is subject to the approval by Colombian regulators. Jeff Schlegel and Nicholas Rodriguez of Jones Day acted as legal advisors to Chevron Petrolium Company, a subsidiary of Chevron Corporation. Scotia Capital Inc. acted as financial advisor to Chevron Corporation.
