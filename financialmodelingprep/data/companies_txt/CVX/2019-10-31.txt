Chevron Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Chevron Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 36,116 million compared to USD 43,987 million a year ago. Net income was USD 2,580 million compared to USD 4,047 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.38 compared to USD 2.13 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.36 compared to USD 2.11 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 110,166 million compared to USD 123,987 million a year ago. Net income was USD 9,534 million compared to USD 11,094 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 5.06 compared to USD 5.84 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 5.02 compared to USD 5.79 a year ago.
