Chevron Says Independent Directors Of Board Approved An Annual Base Salary Of $1.65 Million For CEO Michael Wirth. CHEVRON - INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS OF BOARD APPROVED AN ANNUAL BASE SALARY OF $1.65 MILLION FOR CEO MICHAEL K. WIRTH, AN INCREASE OF $50,000.CHEVRON CORP -  RATIFIED DECISION TO INCREASE ANNUAL BASE SALARIES OF CFO PIERRE BREBER BY $20,000, RESULTING IN ANNUAL BASE SALARY OF $1.02 MILLION.CHEVRON CORP - APPROVED A TARGET OF 160% FOR CEO MICHAEL K. WIRTH, AN INCREASE FROM 150% FOR 2019.
