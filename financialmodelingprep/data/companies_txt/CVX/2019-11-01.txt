Chevron Corp Says Qtrly Adj EPS Of $1.55. CHEVRON CORP - QTRLY ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.55.CHEVRON CORP SEES FY 2019 PRODUCTION GROWTH (EXCLUDING ASSET SALES) IN MIDDLE OF THE 4-7% RANGE.CHEVRON CORP SEES FY UPSTREAM ASSET SALES PRODUCTION IMPACT (ABOUT 30 MBOED).CHEVRON CORP SEES FY UPSTREAM TCO CO-LENDING OF LESS THAN $2 BILLION.CHEVRON CORP - UPDATES COST ESTIMATE FOR TENGIZ EXPANSION PROJECT TO $45.2 BILLION.CHEVRON ON UPDATE FOR TENGIZ EXPANSION PROJECT, SAYS ITS C&E GUIDANCE IS UNCHANGED FOR 2020 AND 2021 TO 2023 PERIODS.
Chevron Q3 Earnings Per Share $1.36. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER NET INCOME OF $2.6 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.36.Q3 SALES $35 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $37.69 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.45 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.WORLDWIDE NET OIL-EQUIVALENT PRODUCTION WAS 3.03 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY IN Q3 2019, AN INCREASE OF 3 PERCENT FROM 2.96 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY FROM A YEAR AGO.CORP - U.S. DOWNSTREAM OPERATIONS EARNED $389 MILLION IN Q3 2019, COMPARED WITH EARNINGS OF $748 MILLION A YEAR EARLIER.CORP - U.S. UPSTREAM OPERATIONS EARNED $727 MILLION IN Q3 2019, COMPARED WITH $828 MILLION A YEAR EARLIER.CORP - INTERNATIONAL UPSTREAM OPERATIONS EARNED $1.98 BILLION IN Q3 2019, COMPARED WITH $2.55 BILLION A YEAR AGO.CORP - U.S. REFINERY CRUDE OIL INPUT IN Q3 2019 INCREASED 8 PERCENT TO 992,000 BARRELS PER DAY FROM YEAR-AGO PERIOD.CORP - INTERNATIONAL DOWNSTREAM OPERATIONS EARNED $439 MILLION IN Q3 2019, COMPARED WITH $625 MILLION A YEAR EARLIER.CORP - U.S. NET OIL-EQUIVALENT PRODUCTION OF 934,000 BARRELS PER DAY IN Q3 2019 WAS UP 103,000 BARRELS PER DAY FROM A YEAR EARLIER.CORP - INTERNATIONAL NET OIL-EQUIVALENT PRODUCTION OF 2.10 MILLION BARRELS PER DAY IN Q3 2019 WAS DOWN 26,000 BARRELS PER DAY FROM A YEAR EARLIER.CORP - INTERNATIONAL REFINERY CRUDE OIL INPUT OF 625,000 BARRELS PER DAY IN Q3 2019 DECREASED 85,000 BARRELS PER DAY FROM YEAR-AGO PERIOD.CORP - NET CHARGES IN Q3 2019 WERE $952 MILLION, COMPARED WITH NET CHARGES OF $705 MILLION IN YEAR-AGO PERIOD.
