TortoiseEcofin Announces Constituent Changes Due to Merger and Acquisition Activity. LEAWOOD, KS / ACCESSWIRE / MAY 7, 2021 / TORTOISEECOFIN TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT NOBLE MIDSTREAM PARTNERS LP (NASDAQ:NBLX) WILL BE REMOVED FROM THE TORTOISE MLP INDEX ® (TMLP) AND THE TORTOISE NORTH AMERICAN PIPELINE INDEXSM (TNAP) PENDING THE CLOSE OF THE ANNOUNCED MERGER TRANSACTION WITH CHEVRON CORP (NYSE:CVX). IF APPROVED, NBLX WILL BE REMOVED FROM BOTH INDICES AT MARKET OPEN ON TUESDAY, MAY 11, 2021.
