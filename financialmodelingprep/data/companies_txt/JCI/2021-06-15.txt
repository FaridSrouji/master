Google Cloud Expands Strategic Relationship with Johnson Controls to Run Business Systems on Sustainable Cloud Infrastructure. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GOOGLE CLOUD TODAY ANNOUNCED AN EXPANDED RELATIONSHIP WITH JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE:JCI) THAT WILL INVOLVE THE LEADING BUILDING TECHNOLOGY AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY RUNNING SAP'S ENTERPRISE RESOURCE PLANNING CENTRAL COMPONENT (ECC) ENVIRONMENT ON GOOGLE CLOUD.
