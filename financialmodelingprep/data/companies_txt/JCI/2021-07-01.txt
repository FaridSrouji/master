Johnson Controls Named One of the Best Companies for Multicultural Women 2021 by Seramount. MILWAUKEE, JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JOHNSON CONTROLS (NYSE: JCI), THE GLOBAL LEADER FOR SMART, HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE BUILDINGS, HAS BEEN NAMED ONE OF THE 2021 BEST COMPANIES FOR MULTICULTURAL WOMEN BY SERAMOUNT (FORMERLY WORKING MOTHER MEDIA) – AN HONOR RECOGNIZING COMPANIES THAT CREATE AND USE BEST PRACTICES IN HIRING, RETAINING AND PROMOTING MULTICULTURAL WOMEN IN THE UNITED STATES. THROUGH ITS DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION MISSION TO EMPOWER EVERY EMPLOYEE TO TAKE AN ACTIVE ROLE IN CREATING A CULTURE THAT VALUES UNIQUENESS, CELEBRATES CREATIVITY AND DRIVES INNOVATION, JOHNSON CONTROLS WORKS TO ELEVATE EACH EMPLOYEES' RESPONSIBILITY TO CONTRIBUTE TO ITS CULTURE.
