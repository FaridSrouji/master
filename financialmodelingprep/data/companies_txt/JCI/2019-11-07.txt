Johnson Controls International plc (NYSE:JCI) signed a definitive agreement to acquire building and energy management system product line from EasyIO Holdings LTD.. Johnson Controls International plc (NYSE:JCI) signed a definitive agreement to acquire building and energy management system product line from EasyIO Holdings LTD on November 8, 2019. The agreement is expected to close in November 2019.

Johnson Controls Appoints Brian Stief Vice Chairman, CFO; To Step Down In 2020. JOHNSON CONTROLS APPOINTS BRIAN STIEF VICE CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER; TO STEP DOWN IN DECEMBER 2020.JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC - STIEF WILL SERVE AS ADVISER TO GEORGE OLIVER, CHAIRMAN AND CEO, AND CONTINUE HIS DUTIES AS CFO.JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL PLC - BOARD WILL BEGIN A PROCESS TO APPOINT A SUCCESSOR TO BRIAN STIEF.JOHNSON CONTROLS INTERNATIONAL - STIEF PLANS TO RETIRE EFFECTIVE DEC. 31, 2020 FOLLOWING EXPIRATION OF HIS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED RETENTION ARRANGEMENT.
