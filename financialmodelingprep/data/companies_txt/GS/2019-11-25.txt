GTT Selects Credit Suisse and Goldman Sachs as Financial Advisors for Sale of Infrastructure Division. GTT Communications, Inc. announced that it selected Credit Suisse and Goldman Sachs to act as financial advisors to the company in connection with the potential sale of the Infrastructure Division, which includes the company’s highly differentiated terrestrial pan-European fiber network, subsea transatlantic fiber and data center infrastructure that were part of the Interoute and Hibernia acquisitions.
