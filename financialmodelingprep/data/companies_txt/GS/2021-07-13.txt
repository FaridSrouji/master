ADDING MULTIMEDIA: EDF Renewables and Goldman Sachs Asset Management Announce Commercial Operation of Toms River Solar Project. SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EDF RENEWABLES NORTH AMERICA (EDFR) AND THE RENEWABLE POWER BUSINESS WITHIN GOLDMAN SACHS ASSET MANAGEMENT (GOLDMAN SACHS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMMERCIAL OPERATION OF THE TOMS RIVER SOLAR PROJECT, DELIVERING 28.9 MEGAWATTS (MW) OF CLEAN RENEWABLE ENERGY. THE PROJECT, BUILT ON BASF CORPORATION'S (BASF) PROPERTY BY EDFR IN COORDINATION WITH PVONE AND GOLDMAN SACHS, IS NEW JERSEY'S LARGEST SOLAR PROJECT AND THE LARGEST SOLAR PROJECT BUILT ON A SUPERFUND SITE IN THE U.S.
