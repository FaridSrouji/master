Tadawul Says Goldman Sachs Saudi Arabia Joins as Derivatives Exchange Member For New Market Launch. ANNOUNCES JOINING OF GOLDMAN SACHS SAUDI ARABIA AS A DERIVATIVES EXCHANGE MEMBER FOR NEW MARKET LAUNCH.GOLDMAN SACHS SAUDI ARABIA TO CONDUCT BROKERAGE SERVICES FOR DERIVATIVES TRADING.GOLDMAN SACHS SAUDI ARABIA TO DEAL AS A PRINCIPAL AND AS AN AGENT IN SAUDI STOCK EXCHANGE FOR THE NEW MARKET LAUNCH.
