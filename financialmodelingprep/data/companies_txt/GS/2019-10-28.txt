Goldman Sachs Announces Redemption Of 2.30% Notes Due Dec. 13. Oct 29  - Goldman Sachs Group Inc <GS.N>::GOLDMAN SACHS ANNOUNCES REDEMPTION OF 2.30% NOTES DUE DECEMBER 13, 2019.NOTES HAVE AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF $2.25 BILLION.NOTES WILL BE REDEEMED FOR A REDEMPTION PRICE EQUAL TO 100% OF PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF NOTES PLUS ACCRUED AND UNPAID INTEREST.
