Monster Beverage Corporation Provides Sales Guidance for the Month of October 2019. Monster Beverage Corporation provided sales guidance for the month of October 2019. The company estimates October 2019 gross sales to be approximately 7.3% higher than in October 2018. On a foreign currency-adjusted basis, October 2019 gross sales would have been approximately 8.6% higher than comparable October 2018 gross sales.
