Hasbro, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 29, 2019. Hasbro, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 29, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 1,575.173 million compared to USD 1,569.686 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 297.210 million compared to USD 313.336 million a year ago. Net income was USD 212.949 million compared to USD 263.861 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.68 compared to USD 2.08 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.67 compared to USD 2.06 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 3,292.220 million compared to USD 3,190.485 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 461.670 million compared to USD 320.505 million a year ago. Net income was USD 253.109 million compared to USD 211.668 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2 compared to USD 1.68 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.99 compared to USD 1.67 a year ago.
