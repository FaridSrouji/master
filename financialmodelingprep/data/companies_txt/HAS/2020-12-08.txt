Hasbro Teams up With Charlie “Rocket” Jabaley to Deliver Joy to Families Impacted By COVID-19. PAWTUCKET, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HASBRO, INC. (NASDAQ:HAS), ANNOUNCED PLANS TO JOIN FORCES WITH CHARLIE “ROCKET” JABALEY AND HIS “DREAM MACHINE TOUR” THIS HOLIDAY SEASON, BRINGING POSITIVE EXPERIENCES TO COMMUNITIES THAT HAVE BEEN GREATLY IMPACTED BY THIS DIFFICULT AND UNPRECEDENTED YEAR. THE COMPANY IS CONTRIBUTING $450,000 TO GRAMMY AND EMMY AWARD-WINNING CHARLIE "ROCKET" JABALEY, WHO WILL USE THE FUNDS TO DELIVER ACTS OF KINDNESS TO FAMILIES ACROSS THE COUNTRY VIA HIS “DREAM MACHINE TOUR.”
