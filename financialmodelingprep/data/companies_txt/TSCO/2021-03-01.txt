Tractor Supply Supports Texas in Winter Storm Recovery. BRENTWOOD, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY, THE LARGEST OUT HERE LIFESTYLE RETAILER IN THE UNITED STATES, IS SUPPORTING ITS COMMUNITIES AND CUSTOMERS IN TEXAS BY DONATING $100,000 TO AGRICULTURE, PET AND LIVESTOCK ORGANIZATIONS IN THE LONE STAR STATE RECOVERING FROM THE RECENT WINTER STORMS. “WHEN THESE UNPRECEDENTED STORMS RAVAGED TEXAS, WE KNEW OUR TRACTOR SUPPLY NEIGHBORS WOULD NEED HELP.
