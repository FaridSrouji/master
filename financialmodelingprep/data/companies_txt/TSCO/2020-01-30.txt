Tractor Supply Company Reports Q4 Earnings Per Share $1.21. MPANY REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL 2019 RESULTS; PROVIDES FISCAL 2020 FINANCIAL OUTLOOK.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.21.Q4 SAME STORE SALES ROSE 0.1 PERCENT.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.23 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES FY 2020 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $4.90 TO $5.10.Q4 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.21.NET SALES INCREASED 2.7% TO $2.19 BILLION IN Q4 OF 2019 FROM $2.13 BILLION IN Q4 OF 2018.Q4 PERFORMANCE WAS OFFSET BY WEAKNESS IN COLD WEATHER SEASONAL AND HOLIDAY DISCRETIONARY CATEGORIES.SHARE REPURCHASES FOR FISCAL 2020 ARE EXPECTED TO BE IN RANGE OF $450 MILLION TO $550 MILLION.SEES FISCAL 2020 CAPITAL EXPENDITURES $225 MILLION TO $275 MILLION.SEES FISCAL 2020 EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE $4.90 TO $5.10.SEES FISCAL 2020 NET SALES $8.75 BILLION TO $ 8.90 BILLION.SEES FISCAL 2020 COMPARABLE STORE SALES IN RANGE OF UP 1.5% TO UP 3.0%.ANTICIPATED 2020 CAP EX INCLUDE NEW STORE GROWTH OF ABOUT 80 NEW TRACTOR SUPPLY AND 10 TO 15 NEW PETSENSE STORE OPENINGS.Q4 REVENUE VIEW $2.24 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FY2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $5.21, REVENUE VIEW $8.96 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
