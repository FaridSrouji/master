Tractor Supply Company and Petsense Stores to Close for Easter Sunday. BRENTWOOD, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $TSCO #LIFEOUTHERE--TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY HAS ANNOUNCED IT WILL CLOSE ALL TRACTOR SUPPLY AND PETSENSE RETAIL STORES ON SUNDAY, APRIL 4, IN OBSERVANCE OF EASTER.
