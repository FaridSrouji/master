Tractor Supply Company Names Hal Lawton President And Chief Executive Officer. TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY ANNOUNCES CEO SUCCESSION PLAN; NAMES HAL LAWTON PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 13, 2020.TRACTOR SUPPLY CO - GREG SANDFORT WILL REMAIN AS ADVISOR AND ON BOARD OF DIRECTORS TO SUPPORT TRANSITION.TRACTOR SUPPLY CO - HAL LAWTON, WHO IS CURRENTLY PRESIDENT OF MACY'S, WILL SUCCEED TRACTOR SUPPLY'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER GREG SANDFORT.TRACTOR SUPPLY CO - SANDFORT WILL REMAIN WITH CO THROUGH FEB 29, 2020, & THEN SERVE AS ADVISOR UNTIL AUGUST 31, 2020, TO ENSURE A SEAMLESS TRANSITION.
