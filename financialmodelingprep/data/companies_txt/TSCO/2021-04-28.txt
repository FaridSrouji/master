Tractor Supply Begins Spring Paper Clover Campaign in Support of 4-H Youth. BRENTWOOD, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #4H--TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY (NASDAQ: TSCO), IN PARTNERSHIP WITH NATIONAL 4-H COUNCIL, IS KICKING OFF ITS 11TH ANNUAL SPRING PAPER CLOVER CAMPAIGN. APRIL 28 THROUGH MAY 9, TRACTOR SUPPLY CUSTOMERS AND TEAM MEMBERS CAN PURCHASE A PAPER CLOVER DURING CHECKOUT AT STORES NATIONWIDE, ONLINE AT TRACTORSUPPLY.COM OR THROUGH THE TRACTOR SUPPLY APP.
