Tractor Supply Raises $718,852 for 4-H Youth Nationwide During Spring Paper Clover Campaign. BRENTWOOD, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #4H--TRACTOR SUPPLY COMPANY, THE LARGEST RURAL LIFESTYLE RETAILER IN THE UNITED STATES—IN PARTNERSHIP WITH NATIONAL 4-H COUNCIL—TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS ANNUAL SPRING PAPER CLOVER FUNDRAISER HAS RAISED A TOTAL OF $718,852 BENEFITTING 4-H COMMUNITIES ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
