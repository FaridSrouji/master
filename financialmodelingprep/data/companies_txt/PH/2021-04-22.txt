Parker Increases Quarterly Cash Dividend. CLEVELAND, APRIL 22, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.03 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF MAY 7, 2021.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE JUNE 4, 2021.  THE DIVIDEND REPRESENTS A 17% INCREASE OVER THE PREVIOUS QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 88 CENTS PER COMMON SHARE AND IS THE COMPANY'S 284TH CONSECUTIVE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.
