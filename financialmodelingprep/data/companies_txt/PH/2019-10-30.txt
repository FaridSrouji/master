Parker-Hannifin Corporation Revises Earnings Guidance for Year Ending June 30, 2020. Parker-Hannifin Corporation revised earnings guidance for year ending June 30, 2020. For the fiscal year ending June 30, 2020, the company has revised guidance for earnings per share to the range of $8.53 to $9.33. Guidance assumes an organic sales decline in the range of 7.5% to 4.5%.
