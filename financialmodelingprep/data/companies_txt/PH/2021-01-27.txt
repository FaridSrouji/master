Parker Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend. CLEVELAND, JAN. 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PARKER HANNIFIN CORPORATION (NYSE: PH), THE GLOBAL LEADER IN MOTION AND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF 88 CENTS PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEBRUARY 10, 2021.  THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MARCH 5, 2021.
