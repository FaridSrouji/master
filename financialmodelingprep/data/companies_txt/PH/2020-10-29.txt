Parker Announces Retirement of Chief Technology and Innovation Officer M. Craig Maxwell, Board Elects Mark T.. CLEVELAND, Oct. 29, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Parker Hannifin Corporation (NYSE:PH), the global leader in motion and control technologies, today announced that M. Craig Maxwell, Vice President - Chief Technology and Innovation Officer, will retire after 24 years of dedicated service to the company. The Board of Directors has elected Mark T. Czaja, currently Vice President of Technology and Innovation - Motion Systems Group, to succeed Mr. Maxwell, effective January 1, 2021.
