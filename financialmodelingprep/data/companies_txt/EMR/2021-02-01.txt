Emerson Chief Executive Officer David N. Farr to Retire; Lal Karsanbhai to become CEO on Feb. 5. ST. LOUIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EMERSON (NYSE: EMR), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY AND INDUSTRIAL SOFTWARE LEADER, TODAY ANNOUNCED CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER DAVID N. FARR WILL RETIRE ON FEB. 5. LAL KARSANBHAI, WHO HAS SERVED AS EXECUTIVE PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY'S AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS BUSINESS SINCE 2018, WILL BECOME THE NEW CEO OF EMERSON AND JOIN THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
