Emerson Launches Lumity™ Brand to Help Protect Food, Pharmaceuticals and Commercial Goods. ST. LOUIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EMERSON (NYSE: EMR), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY AND ENGINEERING LEADER, TODAY INTRODUCED ITS NEW LUMITY™ BRAND OF ANALYTICS SOFTWARE AND DIGITAL SOLUTIONS TO HELP SAFEGUARD CRITICAL PRODUCTS LIKE FOOD AND TEMPERATURE-SENSITIVE MEDICATIONS. EMERSON'S LUMITY PORTFOLIO IS CONNECTING A SUITE OF PRODUCTS TO CREATE CUSTOMIZABLE SOLUTIONS TO ENABLE THE HIGHEST LEVEL OF VISIBILITY AND CONTROL OF CONSUMER GOODS THROUGHOUT THE ENTIRE SUPPLY CHAIN.
