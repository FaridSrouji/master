Emerson Completes Acquisition of 7AC Technologies, Inc.. ST. LOUIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EMERSON (NYSE: EMR) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED THE PURCHASE OF 7AC TECHNOLOGIES, INC. 7AC IS A TECHNOLOGY START-UP OFFERING AN EMERGING APPROACH TO AIR CONDITIONING TECHNOLOGY THAT ENABLES ENERGY SAVINGS AND SUSTAINABLE AIR MANAGEMENT. EMERSON, A LONGTIME LEADER IN ADVANCED HEATING AND COOLING TECHNOLOGIES, HAS BEEN COLLABORATING WITH 7AC TO COMMERCIALIZE A NEW TECHNOLOGY THAT ENABLES CUSTOMERS SUCH AS COMMERCIAL BUILDING OWNERS AND RETAILERS TO ACHIEVE DESIRED
