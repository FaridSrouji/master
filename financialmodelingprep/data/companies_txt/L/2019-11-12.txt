Loews Corporation Announces Quarterly Dividend on Common Stock, Payable on December 10, 2019. Loews Corporation announced the declaration of the company's quarterly dividend of $0.0625 per share of Common Stock, payable December 10, 2019 to shareholders of record as of the close of business on November 27, 2019.
