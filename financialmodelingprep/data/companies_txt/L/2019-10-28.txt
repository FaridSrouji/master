Loews Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Loews Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced net income was USD 72 million compared to USD 278 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.24 compared to USD 0.88 a year ago. For the nine months, net income was USD 715 million compared to USD 801 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.34 compared to USD 2.5 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.34 compared to USD 2.49 a year ago.
