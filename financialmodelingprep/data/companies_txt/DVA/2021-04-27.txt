DaVita Solidifies Commitment to ESG, Sets 2025 Goals. DENVER, APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DAVITA INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS 2025 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) GOALS AND RELEASED ITS NEW ESG REPORTS, BOLSTERING ITS COMMITMENT TO CORPORATE CITIZENSHIP AND HELPING CREATE HEALTHY COMMUNITIES. DAVITA'S ESG PROGRAM BUILDS UPON EXISTING EFFORTS TO TRANSFORM CARE, FIGHT CLIMATE CHANGE AND CREATE A DIVERSE COMMUNITY WHERE EVERYONE FEELS THEY BELONG.
