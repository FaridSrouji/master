DaVita and the National Kidney Foundation Join Forces to Improve Health Equity in Kidney Transplantation. NEW YORK, JUNE 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DAVITA KIDNEY CARE AND THE NATIONAL KIDNEY FOUNDATION (NKF) TODAY ANNOUNCED THEIR COLLABORATION IN LAUNCHING AN INNOVATIVE, YEARLONG PILOT AIMED AT IMPROVING HEALTH EQUITY IN KIDNEY TRANSPLANTATION WITH A NEWLY-DEVELOPED PROGRAM WITHIN NKF'S THE BIG ASK: THE BIG GIVE PLATFORM. "MANY PEOPLE NEVER FIND A LIVING DONOR SIMPLY BECAUSE THEY ARE AFRAID TO ASK," SAID KEVIN LONGINO, CEO, NATIONAL KIDNEY FOUNDATION AND A KIDNEY TRANSPLANT PATIENT.
