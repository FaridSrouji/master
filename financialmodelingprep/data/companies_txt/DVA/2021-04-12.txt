Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Minnesota Launches Program with DaVita's VillageHealth to Improve Care for Members with Kidney Disease. EAGAN, MINN., APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BLUE CROSS AND BLUE SHIELD OF MINNESOTA (BLUE CROSS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF AN INNOVATIVE NEW PROGRAM WITH VILLAGEHEALTH − A DAVITA INTEGRATED KIDNEY CARE PROGRAM − THAT ADDRESSES THE HOLISTIC HEALTH NEEDS OF CERTAIN ELIGIBLE MEMBERS WITH CHRONIC KIDNEY DISEASE (CKD) OR END STAGE KIDNEY DISEASE (ESKD).
