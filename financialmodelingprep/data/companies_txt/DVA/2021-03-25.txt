DaVita Statement on Government's Nationwide Program to Vaccinate Dialysis Patients. DENVER, MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, THE BIDEN ADMINISTRATION ANNOUNCED A NEW PARTNERSHIP WITH DIALYSIS PROVIDERS TO PROVIDE COVID-19 VACCINATIONS TO PEOPLE RECEIVING DIALYSIS AND HEALTH CARE PERSONNEL IN OUTPATIENT DIALYSIS CENTERS. DAVITA KIDNEY CARE WILL RECEIVE A PORTION OF THIS ALLOCATION AND WILL WORK WITH OTHER PROVIDERS TO MAKE VACCINES AVAILABLE IN DIALYSIS CENTERS ACROSS THE COUNTRY.
