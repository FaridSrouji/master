Packaging Corporation of America Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Packaging Corporation of America announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 1,750.7 million compared to USD 1,809.9 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 262.8 million compared to USD 298.5 million a year ago. Net income was USD 179.8 million compared to USD 206.7 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.9 compared to USD 2.19 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.89 compared to USD 2.18 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 5,244.3 million compared to USD 5,268.1 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 818.6 million compared to USD 781 million a year ago. Net income was USD 560.2 million compared to USD 533.4 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 5.93 compared to USD 5.65 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 5.91 compared to USD 5.64 a year ago.
