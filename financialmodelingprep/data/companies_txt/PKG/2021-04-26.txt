Packaging Corporation of America Reports First Quarter 2021 Results. LAKE FOREST, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA (NYSE: PKG) TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2021 NET INCOME OF $167 MILLION, OR $1.75 PER SHARE, AND NET INCOME OF $169 MILLION, OR $1.77 PER SHARE, EXCLUDING SPECIAL ITEMS. FIRST QUARTER NET SALES WERE $1.8 BILLION IN 2021 AND $1.7 BILLION IN 2020. DILUTED EARNINGS PER SHARE ATTRIBUTABLE TO PACKAGING CORPORATION OF AMERICA SHAREHOLDERS         THREE MONTHS ENDED     MARCH 31     2021   2020   CHANGE   REPORTED DILUTED EPS $ 1.75
