Vulcan Declares Quarterly Dividend On Common Stock. BIRMINGHAM, ALA., MAY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VULCAN MATERIALS COMPANY (NYSE: VMC) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF THIRTY-SEVEN CENTS PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK.
