Vulcan Materials Company Provides Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019. Vulcan Materials Company provided earnings guidance for the year 2019. The company consider outlook for the full year, it expect to finish the year with aggregates profitability better than originally expected. For the full year, reaffirming outlook for double-digit earnings growth. It expect earnings from continuing operations between $4.55 and $5.05 per diluted share.
