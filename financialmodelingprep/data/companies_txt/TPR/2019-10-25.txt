Tapestry Enters Agreement To Refinance And Replace Its Unsecured Revolving Facility Dated May 30, 2017 With New Revolving Credit Facility. TAPESTRY - ENTERED AGREEMENT TO REFINANCE AND REPLACE COMPANY'S UNSECURED REVOLVING FACILITY DATED MAY 30, 2017 WITH NEW REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY.TAPESTRY - LENDERS HAVE MADE AVAILABLE $900 MILLION UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY WITH MATURITY DATE OF OCT 24, 2024.TAPESTRY - NEW REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY PROVIDES THAT REVOLVING COMMITMENTS MAY BE INCREASED BY AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $300 MILLION.
