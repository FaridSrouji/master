Safe Kids Worldwide and Union Pacific Railroad Launch New Community and Education Rail Safety Initiatives. WASHINGTON, MAY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SAFE KIDS WORLDWIDE AND UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD TODAY ANNOUNCED A SERIES OF NEW INITIATIVES IN THEIR PARTNERSHIP AIMED AT KEEPING CHILDREN AND COMMUNITIES SAFER. THE WORK IS A CONTINUATION OF THEIR PARTNERSHIP TO RAISE AWARENESS, EDUCATE FAMILIES AND PREVENT RAIL-RELATED INJURIES AND DEATHS INVOLVING CHILDREN.
