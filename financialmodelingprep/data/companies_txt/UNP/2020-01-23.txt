Union Pacific Reports Q4 Earnings Per Share Of $2.02. UNION PACIFIC REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2019 RESULTS.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.02.Q4 REVENUE $5.2 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $5.22 BILLION.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $2.04 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q4 BUSINESS VOLUMES, AS MEASURED BY TOTAL REVENUE CARLOADS, DECREASED 11 PERCENT COMPARED TO 2018.$2.16 PER GALLON AVERAGE QUARTERLY DIESEL FUEL PRICE IN Q4 2019 WAS 7 PERCENT LOWER THAN Q4 2018.QUARTERLY FREIGHT REVENUE DECLINED 10 PERCENT, COMPARED TO Q4 2018.QTRLY OPERATING RATIO WAS 59.7 PERCENT IMPROVING 1.9 POINTS COMPARED TO Q4 2018.
Union Pacific Sees 2020 Volumes Improving, Slightly Positive Overall. SEES OPERATING RATIO OF ABOUT 59% IN 2020.SEES CAPITAL SPENDING < 15% OF REVENUE IN 2020.SEES PRICING GAINS IN EXCESS OF INFLATION DOLLARS IN 2020.SEES VOLUMES IMPROVING, SLIGHTLY POSITIVE OVERALL IN 2020.
