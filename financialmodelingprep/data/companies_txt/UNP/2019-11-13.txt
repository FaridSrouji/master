Union Pacific Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 30, 2019. The Board of Directors of Union Pacific Corporation declared quarterly dividend of 97 cents per share on the company's common stock, payable December 30, 2019, to shareholders of record November 29, 2019.
