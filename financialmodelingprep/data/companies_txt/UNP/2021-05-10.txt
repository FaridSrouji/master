Union Pacific Honors Safe Chemical Transporters with Pinnacle Award. OMAHA, NEB., MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT 63 COMPANIES WON THE 2020 PINNACLE AWARD FOR THEIR DEDICATION AND COMMITMENT TO SAFELY TRANSPORTING CHEMICALS BY RAIL.
