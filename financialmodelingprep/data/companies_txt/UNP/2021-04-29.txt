Union Pacific Builds State-of-the-Art Transload Terminal in Chicago. OMAHA, NEB., APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNION PACIFIC TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS BUILDING A STATE-OF-THE-ART GRAIN TRANSLOAD FACILITY WITHIN ITS GLOBAL IV INTERMODAL TERMINAL IN JOLIET, ILLINOIS THAT WILL REDUCE SUPPLY-CHAIN COSTS FOR AGRICULTURAL PRODUCERS AND PROCESSORS.
