Xilinx, Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 3, 2019. The Xilinx, Inc. Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.37 per outstanding share of common stock payable on December 3, 2019, to all stockholders of record at the close of business on November 12, 2019.
