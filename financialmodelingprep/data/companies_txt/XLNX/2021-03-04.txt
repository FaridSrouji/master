Monteverde & Associates PC Files Class Action Lawsuit On Behalf Of Shareholders Of Xilinx, Inc. In The Northern District Of California. NEW YORK, MARCH 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT MONTEVERDE & ASSOCIATES PC HAS FILED A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA, CASE NO. 3:21-CV-01108-JD, ON BEHALF OF COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF XILINX, INC....
