Southern Company scores 100 percent on Disability Equality Index for the fifth consecutive year. ATLANTA, JULY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE 2021 DISABILITY EQUALITY INDEX (DEI) HAS RATED SOUTHERN COMPANY AS ONE OF THE "BEST PLACES TO WORK FOR DISABILITY INCLUSION." FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR, SOUTHERN COMPANY JOINS AN ELITE GROUP OF COMPANIES WITH A 100 PERCENT SCORE ON THE DEI FOR A STRONG DEDICATION TO LEADING DISABILITY-INCLUSION PRACTICES.
