Georgia Power Solar Growth Remains Strong. ATLANTA, JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GEORGIA POWER'S SOLAR PROGRAMS CONTINUE TO DRIVE STRONG SOLAR GROWTH ACROSS THE STATE. THE SOLAR ENERGY INDUSTRIES ASSOCIATION (SEIA) HAS RANKED THE STATE OF GEORGIA AS THE #7 STATE IN THE U.S. FOR 2020 SOLAR PHOTOVOLTAIC INSTALLATIONS, MARKING THE STATE'S PLACE IN THE TOP 10 FOR THE FOURTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
