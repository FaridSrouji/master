Georgia Power encourages customers to review their current rate plan as temperatures heat up ahead of summer. ATLANTA, MAY 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH TEMPERATURES ACROSS GEORGIA CLIMBING, GEORGIA POWER IS REMINDING CUSTOMERS TO REVIEW THEIR CURRENT RATE PLAN AHEAD OF SUMMER HEAT TO HELP ENSURE THEY ARE ENROLLED IN THE ONE THAT BEST FITS THEIR ENERGY USAGE. THE COMPANY OFFERS A VARIETY OF FLEXIBLE AND CUSTOMIZABLE RATE PLANS TO MINIMIZE THE IMPACT OF HIGHER TEMPERATURES AND INCREASED ENERGY USE ON ELECTRIC BILLS.
