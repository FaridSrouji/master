Southern Co Gas Says Illinois Commission Approved $168 Mln Annual Base Rate Increase. SOUTHERN CO GAS - ON OCT 2, ILLINOIS COMMISSION VOTED ON GENERAL BASE RATE CASE PROCEEDING FILED BY NORTHERN ILLINOIS GAS COMPANY IN NOVEMBER 2018.SOUTHERN CO GAS - ILLINOIS COMMISSION APPROVED $168 MILLION ANNUAL BASE RATE INCREASE.SOUTHERN CO GAS - ILLINOIS COMMISSION APPROVAL INCLUDES $65 MILLION RELATED TO RECOVERY OF INVESTMENTS UNDER THE INVESTING IN ILLINOIS PROGRAM.
