Power To 112,000 Georgia Power Customers Restored Following Saturday Storm. POWER TO 112,000 GEORGIA POWER CUSTOMERS RESTORED FOLLOWING SATURDAY STORM.'S GEORGIA POWER SAYS AS OF 11:30 AM SUNDAY, ABOUT 11,000 CUSTOMERS ARE WITHOUT POWER WITH MOST OUTAGES IN ROME, CARTERSVILLE, CARROLLTON.'S GEORGIA POWER SAYS CUSTOMERS IN HARDEST HIT AREAS SHOULD PLAN AHEAD FOR OUTAGES THAT LAST INTO SUNDAY EVENING.'S GEORGIA POWER SAYS ABOUT 420 CASES OF INDIVIDUAL DAMAGE OR TROUBLE (INCLUDING BROKEN POLES AND LINES) COMPANY IS WORKING TO REPAIR.
