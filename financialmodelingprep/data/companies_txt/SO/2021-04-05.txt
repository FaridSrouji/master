Georgia Power invites customers to share their thanks during annual "Thank a Lineman" celebration. ATLANTA, APRIL 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DURING THE MONTH OF APRIL, GEORGIA POWER AND OTHER ELECTRIC UTILITIES IN GEORGIA ARE CELEBRATING AND THANKING UTILITY WORKERS ACROSS THE STATE. THE DAILY WORK DONE BY LINE WORKERS IN GEORGIA'S COMMUNITIES IS ALWAYS RECOGNIZED, BUT THEIR WORK HAS BEEN ESPECIALLY PRONOUNCED OVER THE PAST YEAR, AS MANY FAMILIES CONTINUE TO WORK AND LEARN AT HOME DUE TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
