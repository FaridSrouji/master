The Southern Company Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. The Southern Company announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 5,995 million compared to USD 6,159 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,013 million compared to USD 2,174 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,316 million compared to USD 1,164 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.26 compared to USD 1.14 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.25 compared to USD 1.13 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 16,505 million compared to USD 18,158 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 7,046 million compared to USD 3,613 million a year ago. Net income was USD 4,298 million compared to USD 1,948 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 4.12 compared to USD 1.92 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.09 compared to USD 1.91 a year ago.
