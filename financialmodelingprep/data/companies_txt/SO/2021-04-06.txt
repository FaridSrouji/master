Southern Company expects to achieve interim greenhouse gas emissions (GHG) reduction goal significantly ahead of schedule. ATLANTA, APRIL 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN 2020, THE SOUTHERN COMPANY SYSTEM REDUCED GREENHOUSE GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS 52% FROM ITS 2007 BENCHMARK LEVELS, EXCEEDING ITS 2030 GOAL TO REDUCE GHG EMISSIONS BY 50%. THE INTERMEDIATE 2030 GOAL IS PART OF THE SYSTEM'S TARGET TO ACHIEVE NET ZERO GHG EMISSIONS BY 2050.
