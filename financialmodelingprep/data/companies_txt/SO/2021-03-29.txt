Southern Power acquires Deuel Harvest Wind Farm. ATLANTA, MARCH 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SOUTHERN POWER, A LEADING U.S. WHOLESALE ENERGY PROVIDER AND SUBSIDIARY OF SOUTHERN COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF ITS 14TH WIND PROJECT — THE 300-MEGAWATT (MW) DEUEL HARVEST WIND FARM — FROM INVENERGY, A LEADING DEVELOPER, OWNER AND OPERATOR OF SUSTAINABLE ENERGY SOLUTIONS. "WE ARE PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THE ACQUISITION OF OUR 14TH WIND PROJECT," SAID SOUTHERN POWER PRESIDENT BILL GRANTHAM.
