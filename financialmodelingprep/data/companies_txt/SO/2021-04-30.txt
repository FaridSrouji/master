Georgia Power reminds customers to call 811 before they dig. ATLANTA, APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS SAFE DIGGING MONTH ENDS, GEORGIA POWER REMINDS BUSINESSES AND RESIDENTS ALIKE TO CONTINUE DOING THEIR PART TO PREVENT DANGEROUS AND DISRUPTIVE ACCIDENTS BY CONTACTING GEORGIA 811 BEFORE DIGGING THIS SPRING AND THROUGHOUT THE YEAR. GEORGIA POWER JOINS WITH THE STATE OF GEORGIA AND OTHER UTILITIES EACH YEAR IN WORKING WITH GEORGIA 811, A NONPROFIT CORPORATION DEDICATED TO PREVENTING DAMAGE TO UNDERGROUND UTILITIES AND PROMOTING PUBLIC SAFETY, TO ENSURE THAT PROJECTS ARE SAFE AND COMPLY WITH THE "GEORGIA DIG LAW.
