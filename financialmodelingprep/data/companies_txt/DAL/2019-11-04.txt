Delta Air Lines, Inc. Reports Operating Results for the Month and Year to Date Ended October 2019. Delta Air Lines, Inc. reported operating results for the month and year to date ended October 2019. For the month, the company reported RPMs was 19,764,828,000 against 18,780,662,000 a year ago. ASMs was 23,093,185,000 against 21,963,910,000 a year ago. Load factor was 85.6% against 85.5% a year ago. Passengers boarded was 17,506,753 against 16,546,662 a year ago. Cargo ton miles was 175,872,000 against 191,372,000 a year ago.For the year to date, the company reported RPMs was 201,422,560,000 against 190,782,516,000 a year ago. ASMs was 233,014,120,000 against 222,805,575,000 a year ago. Load factor was 86.4% against 85.6% a year ago. Passengers boarded was 171,699,933 against 161,735,111 a year ago. Cargo ton miles was 1,678,518,000 against 1,842,741,000 a year ago.
