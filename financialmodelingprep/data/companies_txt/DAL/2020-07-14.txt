Delta CEO Says Q3 Capacity Will Be About 20%-25% Of 2019 Levels. DELTA CEO SAYS Q3 CAPACITY WILL BE ABOUT 20%-25% OF 2019 LEVEL BUT UP FROM 10% IN Q2.DELTA AIR LINES SAYS EXPECTING OVERALL REVENUE FOR THE SEPTEMBER QUARTER TO BE ONLY 20% TO 25% OF LAST SUMMER.DELTA CEO SAYS DOESN'T THINK WE'LL EVER GET BACK TO THE VOLUMES OF BUSINESS TRAVEL SEEN IN 2019 - CONF. CALL.DELTA CFO SAYS NO-SHOW RATES IN HIGH SINGLE DIGITS VERSUS AROUND 3% HISTORICALLY.DELTA CFO SAYS NO-SHOW RATES MAKE IT HARD TO MANAGE SEATING CAPS, BUT FLEXIBILITY 'CLEARLY WHERE WE NEED TO BE'.DELTA AIR LINE INC CFO SAYS LOOKING AT ALL OPTIONS BUT PLANS NOT CHANGED WITH RESPECT TO REFINERY RIGHT NOW.
Delta Air Lines Reports Q2 Loss Of $9.01 Per Share. QUARTERLY LOSS PER SHARE $9.01.QUARTERLY TOTAL OPERATING REVENUE $1.47 BILLION, DOWN 88%.QUARTERLY ADJUSTED LOSS PER SHARE $4.43.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $-4.07, REVENUE VIEW $1.42 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.JUNE QUARTER CONSOLIDATED UNIT COST (CASM) 59.30 CENTS VERSUS 14.51 CENTS.JUNE QUARTER TRASM DOWN 21%.JUNE QUARTER CASM-EX 41.96 CENTS VERSUS 10.47 CENTS; JUNE QUARTER TRASM, ADJUSTED, DOWN 36%.ENDED JUNE QUARTER WITH $15.7 BILLION IN LIQUIDITY.QUARTERLY TOTAL ADJUSTED REVENUE OF $1.2 BILLION, WHICH EXCLUDES REFINERY SALES, DECLINED 91% ON SYSTEM CAPACITY REDUCTION OF 85%.QUARTERLY ADJUSTED. PRE-TAX LOSS OF $3.9 BILLION EXCLUDES $3.2 BILLION OF ITEMS DIRECTLY RELATED TO IMPACT OFCOVID-19 AND CO'S RESPONSE.AMENDING CREDIT FACILITIES TO REPLACE ALL FIXED CHARGE COVERAGE RATIO COVENANTS WITH LIQUIDITY-BASED COVENANTS.QUARTERLY PASSENGER REVENUES DOWN 94% ON 85% LOWER CAPACITY.QUARTERLY NON-TICKET REVENUE DOWN 65%.DEMAND FOR AIR TRAVEL DECLINED SIGNIFICANTLY IN Q2 AS A RESULT OF COVID-19, WITH ENPLANED PASSENGERS DOWN 93%.EXPECT TO ACHIEVE ADJUSTED COST BASE REDUCTION OF 50% IN SEPT QUARTER.AT END OF JUNE QUARTER, HAD TOTAL DEBT & FINANCING LEASE OBLIGATIONS OF $24.6 BILLION WITH ADJUSTED. NET DEBT OF $13.9 BILLION.IN Q2, RECORDED WRITE-DOWN OF $1.1 BILLION IN INVESTMENT IN LATAM AIRLINES AND $770 MILLION WRITE-DOWN IN INVESTMENT IN AEROMEXICO.WROTE DOWN INVESTMENT IN VIRGIN ATLANTIC IN Q2, RESULTING IN $200 MILLION CHARGE.
