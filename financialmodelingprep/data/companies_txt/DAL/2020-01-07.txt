Vietnam Airlines Says U.S. Govt Grants Expansion Of Codeshare Cooperation With Delta Air Lines. U.S. DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION GRANTS CO EXPANSION OF CODESHARE COOPERATION WITH DELTA AIR LINES.TWO-WAY CODESHARE AGREEMENT IS EXPECTED TO TAKE EFFECT IN JANUARY 2020.BY OBTAINING PERMIT, CO GRANTED FULL RIGHTS TO OPERATE ROUTES TO & FROM US IN ALL FORMS OF REGULAR, CHARTER,TWO-WAY CODESHARE FLIGHTS.
