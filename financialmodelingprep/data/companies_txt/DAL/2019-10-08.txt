Delta Air Lines Waives Change Fees For Japan Ahead Of Typhoon Hagibi. Oct 9  - Delta Air Lines Inc <DAL.N>::DELTA AIR LINES INC - WAIVES CHANGE FEES FOR JAPAN AHEAD OF TYPHOON HAGIBI.DELTA AIR LINES - ISSUED WEATHER WAIVER FOR 4 JAPANESE CITIES ALLOWING CUSTOMERS TO MAKE 1-TIME CHANGE TO THEIR ITINERARY WITHOUT CHANGE FEE.DELTA AIR LINES INC - ISSUED A WEATHER WAIVER FOR KANSAI (KIX), NAGOYA (NGO), TOKYO-HANEDA (HND) AND TOKYO-NARITA (NRT)  AHEAD OF TYPHOON HAGIBI.
