DELTA AIR LINES AND AEROMEXICO CELEBRATE FOUR YEARS OF LEADING TRANSBORDER PARTNERSHIP. MEXICO, MAY 14, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MAY 14, 2021.- DELTA AIR LINES AND ITS PARTNER AEROMEXICO ARE CELEBRATING FOUR YEARS OF THEIR LEADING JOINT COOPERATION AGREEMENT (JCA), A TRANSBORDER ALLIANCE BETWEEN THE U.S AND MEXICO THAT HAS PROVIDED INDUSTRY-LEADING SERVICE AND BENEFITS FOR MORE THAN 22 MILLION CUSTOMERS SINCE ITS LAUNCH IN MAY 2017.
