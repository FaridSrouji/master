Delta Air Lines, Inc. Reports Operating Results for the Month and Year to Date Ended November 2019. Delta Air Lines, Inc. reported operating results for the month and year to date ended November 2019. For the month, the company reported RPMs was 17,139,528,000 against 16,834,842,000 a year ago. ASMs was 20,407,368,000 against 19,620,050,000 a year ago. Load factor was 84.0% against 85.8% a year ago. Passengers boarded was 15,734,156 against 15,471,991 a year ago. Cargo ton miles was 158,823,000 against 165,574,000 a year ago.For the year to date, the company reported RPMs was 218,562,089,000 against 207,617,358,000 a year ago. ASMs was 253,421,487,000 against 242,425,625,000 a year ago. Load factor was 86.2% against 85.6% a year ago. Passengers boarded was 187,434,089 against 177,207,102 a year ago. Cargo ton miles was 1,837,340,000 against 2,008,315,000 a year ago.
