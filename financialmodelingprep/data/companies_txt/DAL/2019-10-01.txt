Delta Air Lines, Inc. Announces Traffic Results for the Month and Year to Date Ended September 2019. Delta Air Lines, Inc. announced traffic results for the month and year to date ended September 2019. For the month, the company reported RPMs was 19,564,427,000 against 18,311,866,000 a year ago. ASMs was 22,928,700,000 against 21,902,212,000 a year ago. Load factor was 85.3% against 83.6% a year ago. Passengers boarded was 16,475,647 against 15,250,053 a year ago. Cargo ton miles was 167,788,000 against 187,418,000 a year ago.For the year to date ended September 2019, the company reported RPMs was 181,657,733,000 against 172,001,854,000 a year ago. ASMs was 209,920,934,000 against 200,841,665,000 a year ago. Load factor was 86.5% against 85.6% a year ago. Passengers boarded was 154,193,180 against 145,188,449 a year ago. Cargo ton miles was 1,502,645,000 against 1,651,369,000 a year ago.
