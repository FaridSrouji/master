News Corp Completes Acquisition of Houghton Mifflin Harcourt Books & Media Segment. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEWS CORP ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED ITS ACQUISITION OF THE BOOKS & MEDIA SEGMENT OF HOUGHTON MIFFLIN HARCOURT (HMH BOOKS & MEDIA). AS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED, THE BUSINESS WILL BE OPERATED BY HARPERCOLLINS PUBLISHERS, A NEWS CORP SUBSIDIARY. “WE WELCOME THE TALENTED TEAM AT HMH BOOKS & MEDIA TO HARPERCOLLINS, ESPECIALLY AT A TIME WHEN SO MANY PEOPLE IN SO MANY PLACES ARE READING AND LISTENING TO BOOKS,” SAID ROBERT THOMSON, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OF NEWS
