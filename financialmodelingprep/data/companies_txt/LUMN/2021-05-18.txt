Retailers fashioning new in-store and online experiences through technology innovation. DENVER, MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR RETAILERS, DELIVERING SEAMLESS DIGITAL EXPERIENCES BOTH ON THE SALES FLOOR AND ONLINE HAS BECOME MORE IMPORTANT THAN EVER – THE NEED FOR WHICH HAS ONLY BEEN ACCELERATED BY THE PANDEMIC. EDGE COMPUTING HAS EMERGED AS AN INTEGRAL ELEMENT IN PROPELLING NEW DIGITAL APPLICATIONS IN BOTH BRICK-AND-MORTAR AND ONLINE COMMERCE, AS WELL AS DRIVING OTHER CRITICAL BUSINESS OUTCOMES.
