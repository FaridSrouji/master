Lumen Releases 2020 Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) Report. DENVER, APRIL 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) RELEASED ITS 2020 ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) REPORT SUMMARIZING 2020 EFFORTS TO INVEST IN PEOPLE, DELIVER VALUE TO CUSTOMERS, WORK FAIRLY WITH SUPPLIERS, SUPPORT COMMUNITY AND BUILD LONG-TERM VALUE FOR SHAREHOLDERS. IT ALSO REVIEWS THE COMPANY'S PLANS FOR 2021 AND BEYOND TO EVOLVE ITS INTEGRATED, COHESIVE SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY AND BUILD THE FUTURE OF THE COMPANY.
