As the cost of ransomware attacks nearly double, Lumen deploys program that helps businesses fight ransomware before it strikes. DENVER, APRIL 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SINCE 2019, THE COST OF RANSOMWARE ATTACKS ON BUSINESSES NEARLY DOUBLED[1] TO $20 BILLION PER YEAR. ATTACKERS HAVE BECOME MORE SOPHISTICATED AND TRADITIONAL MITIGATION EFFORTS OFTEN FALL SHORT IN THEIR ABILITY TO STOP THE THREATS BEFORE THEY HIT.
