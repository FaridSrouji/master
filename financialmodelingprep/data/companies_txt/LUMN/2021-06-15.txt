Lumen Technologies Completes Private Offering of Senior Notes. DENVER, JUNE 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN) ANNOUNCED THAT IT COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY-ANNOUNCED PRIVATE OFFERING OF $1.0 BILLION AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.375% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2029 (THE "2029 NOTES"). LUMEN USED THE NET PROCEEDS FROM THE OFFERING, TOGETHER WITH CASH ON HAND, TO REPAY AT MATURITY ITS OUTSTANDING 6.45% SENIOR NOTES, SERIES S, DUE 2021.
