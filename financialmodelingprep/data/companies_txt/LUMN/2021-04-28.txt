Manufacturers embracing Edge computing to manage growing factory data. DENVER, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MANUFACTURERS ARE UNDERGOING FAST, DISRUPTIVE CHANGES IN THEIR OPERATIONS AS THEY AUTOMATE THEIR FACILITIES WITH IOT, SMART SENSORS, AI AND ROBOTICS. ACCORDING TO SURVEY DATA FROM IDC RESEARCH, SPONSORED BY LUMEN TECHNOLOGIES (NYSE: LUMN), 74% OF OPERATIONAL DATA WILL BE ACQUIRED, ANALYZED, AND ACTED ON WITHIN THE FACTORY.
