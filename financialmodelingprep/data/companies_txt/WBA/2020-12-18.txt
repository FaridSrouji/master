ADDING MULTIMEDIA Walgreens Begins Administration of Pfizer's COVID-19 Vaccine in Long-Term Care Facilities. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY WALGREENS BEGAN ADMINISTERING PFIZER'S COVID-19 VACCINE TO RESIDENTS AND STAFF AT LONG-TERM CARE FACILITIES IN A MONUMENTAL FIRST IN THE COMPANY'S 119-YEAR HISTORY. PHARMACISTS ARE PROVIDING THE VACCINATIONS AT NURSING HOMES AND ASSISTED LIVING FACILITIES IN OHIO AND CONNECTICUT AS PART OF THE CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION (CDC) PHARMACY PARTNERSHIP FOR LONG-TERM CARE PROGRAM. WALGREENS PHARMACY TEAM MEMBERS WILL PROVIDE COVID-19 VACCINATIONS
