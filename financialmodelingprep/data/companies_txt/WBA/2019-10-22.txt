Walgreens Boots Alliance Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 12, 2019. Walgreens Boots Alliance, Inc. announced that its board of directors has declared a quarterly dividend of 45.75 cents per share, unchanged from the previous quarter and an increase of 4% from the year-ago quarter. The dividend is payable December 12, 2019, to stockholders of record as of November 18, 2019.
