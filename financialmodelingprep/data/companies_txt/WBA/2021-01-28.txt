Walgreens Boots Alliance Declares Quarterly Dividend. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF 46.75 CENTS PER SHARE.
