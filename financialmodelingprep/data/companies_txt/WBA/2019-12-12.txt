McKesson And Walgreens Boots Alliance To Create German Wholesale Joint Venture. WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE AND MCKESSON TO CREATE GERMAN WHOLESALE JOINT VENTURE.WBA WILL HAVE A 70% CONTROLLING EQUITY INTEREST IN JOINT VENTURE AND MCKESSON WILL HAVE A 30% OWNERSHIP INTEREST.TRANSACTION DOES NOT INCLUDE ANY OTHER WBA OR MCKESSON BUSINESSES OUTSIDE OF GERMAN WHOLESALE ACTIVITIES.
