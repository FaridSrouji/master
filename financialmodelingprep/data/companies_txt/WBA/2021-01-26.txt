Walgreens Boots Alliance Appoints Rosalind Brewer as Chief Executive Officer, and Stefano Pessina as Executive Chairman of the Board. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ROSALIND (ROZ) BREWER AS THE COMPANY'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, EFFECTIVE ON MARCH 15.
