Walgreens Continues to Boost COVID-19 Vaccination Access Across the Country. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS PART OF THE BIDEN ADMINISTRATION'S NATIONAL MONTH OF ACTION, WALGREENS WILL EXTEND PHARMACY HOURS OF OPERATION EACH FRIDAY IN JUNE TO PROVIDE THOSE WHO MAY HAVE LIMITED TIME THE ADDITIONAL FLEXIBILITY NEEDED TO GET A COVID-19 VACCINE. ON FRIDAY, JUNE 11, 18 AND 25, 4,000 WALGREENS PHARMACY LOCATIONS WILL EXTEND HOURS OF OPERATION FOR WALK-IN VACCINATIONS.
