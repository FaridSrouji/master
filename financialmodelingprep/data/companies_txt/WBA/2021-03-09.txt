Walgreens Administers 5 Million COVID Vaccinations and Nears Completion of Long-Term Care Facility Program. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALGREENS HAS PROVIDED 5 MILLION COVID-19 VACCINATIONS ACROSS LONG-TERM CARE FACILITIES, IN STORES AND THROUGH DEDICATED CLINICS.
