Walgreens Boots Alliance Makes a Majority Investment into iA to Advance Automation Capabilities for the Pharmacy Industry. DEERFIELD, ILL. & JOHNSON CITY, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WALGREENS BOOTS ALLIANCE AND IA ANNOUNCED THAT WBA HAS MADE A MAJORITY INVESTMENT IN IA TO ADVANCE AUTOMATION CAPABILITIES FOR THE PHARMACY INDUSTRY.
