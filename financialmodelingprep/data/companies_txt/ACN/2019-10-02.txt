Accenture (NYSE: ACN) agreed to acquire Nytec Inc.. Accenture plc (NYSE:ACN) signed an agreement to acquire Nytec Inc. on October 3, 2019. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions. GrowthPoint Technology Partners, LLC acted as financial advisor to Nytec Inc.
