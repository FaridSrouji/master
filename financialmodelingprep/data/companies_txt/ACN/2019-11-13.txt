Accenture plc (NYSE:ACN) agreed to acquire Silveo.. Accenture plc (NYSE:ACN) agreed to acquire Silveo on November 14, 2019. Post-completion, Silveo will become part of Accenture Industry X.0. The transaction requires prior consultation with the relevant works councils and would be subject to customary closing conditions.
