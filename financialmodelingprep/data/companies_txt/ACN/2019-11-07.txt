Ajinomoto And Accenture To Establish Joint Venture To Help Drive Ajinomoto'S Operational Transformation. AJINOMOTO AND ACCENTURE TO ESTABLISH JOINT VENTURE TO HELP DRIVE AJINOMOTO’S OPERATIONAL TRANSFORMATION.STARTING IN APRIL 2020, NEW COMPANY WILL BE 67% OWNED BY AJINOMOTO AND 33% BY ACCENTURE.NEW COMPANY, AJINOMOTO DIGITAL BUSINESS PARTNERS CO WILL RUN OPERATIONS OF AJINOMOTO'S CORPORATE DIVISIONS.
