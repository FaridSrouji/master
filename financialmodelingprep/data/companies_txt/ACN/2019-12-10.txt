Accenture Says Executive Chairman's 2019 Total Compensation Was $15 Million Versus $6.7 Million In 2018. ACCENTURE PLC - DAVID ROWLAND, EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN'S 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $15 MILLION VERSUS $6.7 MILLION IN 2018 - SEC FILING.ACCENTURE PLC - CEO JULIE SWEET'S 2019 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $6.3 MILLION VERSUS $5.9 MILLION IN 2018 - SEC FILING.ACCENTURE PLC - APPLYING A COST-OF-LIVING ADJUSTMENT, 2019 CEO PAY RATIO WAS 298:1.ACCENTURE PLC - WITHOUT THE COST-OF-LIVING ADJUSTMENT, 2019 CEO PAY RATIO WAS 817:1.
