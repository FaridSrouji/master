Accenture Reports Fourth-Quarter And Full-Year Fiscal 2020 Results In Line With Expectations. ACCENTURE REPORTS FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR FISCAL 2020 RESULTS IN LINE WITH EXPECTATIONS.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.70 EXCLUDING ITEMS.Q4 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.99.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.73 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES FY 2021 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $7.80 TO $8.10 INCLUDING ITEMS.Q4 REVENUE $10.84 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $10.91 BILLION.COMPANY INCREASES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND 10%, TO $0.88 PER SHARE.BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVES $5.0 BILLION OF ADDITIONAL SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORITY.NEW BOOKINGS ARE $14.0 BILLION FOR Q4.BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVES $5.0 BILLION OF ADDITIONAL SHARE REPURCHASE AUTHORITY.FOR FISCAL 2021, ACCENTURE EXPECTS REVENUE GROWTH OF 2% TO 5% IN LOCAL CURRENCY.FY2021 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $8.13 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.EXPECTS REVENUES FOR Q1 OF FISCAL 2021 TO BE IN RANGE OF $11.15 BILLION TO $11.55 BILLION, A DECREASE OF 3% TO FLAT IN LOCAL CURRENCY.FOR FISCAL 2021, COMPANY EXPECTS OPERATING CASH FLOW TO BE IN RANGE OF $6.35 BILLION TO $6.85 BILLION.QTRLY OUTSOURCING REVENUES WERE $5.15 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF 6% IN U.S. DOLLARS.FOR FISCAL 2021, COMPANY EXPECTS FREE CASH FLOW TO BE IN RANGE OF $5.7 BILLION TO $6.2 BILLION.QTRLY CONSULTING REVENUES WERE $5.68 BILLION, A DECREASE OF 8% IN BOTH U.S. DOLLARS AND LOCAL CURRENCY.ACCENTURE'S TOTAL CASH BALANCE AT AUG. 31, 2020 WAS $8.4 BILLION, COMPARED WITH $6.1 BILLION AT AUG. 31, 2019.FY2021 REVENUE VIEW $46.74 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
