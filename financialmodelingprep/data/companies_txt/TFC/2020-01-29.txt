Truist Reports Q4 Adjusted Earnings Per Share $1.12. Jan 30  - Truist Financial Corp <TFC.N>::TRUIST REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.Q4 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.12 EXCLUDING ITEMS.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.75.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.02 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q4 REVENUE $3.7 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $3.6 BILLION.QTRLY ROTCE WAS 12.91 PERCENT; ADJUSTED ROTCE WAS 18.60 PERCENT.IDENTIFIED $4.2 BILLION OF LENDING EXPOSURES TO SELL TO REDUCE CREDIT AND INTEREST-RATE RISK IN QUARTER.QTRLY NONINTEREST EXPENSE INCLUDES $223 MILLION OF MERGER-RELATED AND RESTRUCTURING CHARGES.
