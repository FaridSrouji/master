ADP, Best Buy, Self, Truist and Varo Join BlackRock's Emergency Savings Initiative. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, BLACKROCK ANNOUNCED THAT ADP, BEST BUY, SELF, TRUIST AND VARO HAVE JOINED THEIR PHILANTHROPIC EMERGENCY SAVINGS INITIATIVE (ESI) WHICH INCREASES ACCESS TO LOW-COST, LOW-FRICTION SAVINGS PRODUCTS FOR FINANCIALLY VULNERABLE AMERICANS. IN LINE WITH THE FIRM'S COMMITMENT TO CONTRIBUTE TO A MORE RESILIENT ECONOMY, BLACKROCK'S ESI WILL WORK WITH THESE DIVERSE PARTNERS TO INNOVATE AND SCALE SAVINGS SOLUTIONS THAT HELP FOSTER RESILIENCE TO FINANCIAL SHOCKS.
