The Pandemic Didn't Just Disrupt Our Homelife, It Changed How We Understand "Home". CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AFTER A YEAR IN WHICH "WORKING FROM HOME" BEGAN TO FEEL LIKE "LIVING AT WORK," AMERICA HAS UNDERGONE A PROFOUND SHIFT IN ITS UNDERSTANDING OF "HOME," DRIVING HOMEOWNERS TO WANT MORE SPACE, NEW USES AND A BETTER ENVIRONMENT TO NURTURE POSITIVE CHANGES THAT EVOLVED DURING LOCKDOWN.
