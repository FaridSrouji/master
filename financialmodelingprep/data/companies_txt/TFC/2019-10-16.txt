Bb&T Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. BB&T Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced net interest income was USD 1,700 million compared to USD 1,687 million a year ago. Net income was USD 825 million compared to USD 832 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.96 compared to USD 1.02 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.95 compared to USD 1.01 a year ago. For the nine months, net interest income was USD 5,086 million compared to USD 4,977 million a year ago. Net income was USD 2,503 million compared to USD 2,439 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 3.04 compared to USD 2.98 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 3 compared to USD 2.94 a year ago.
