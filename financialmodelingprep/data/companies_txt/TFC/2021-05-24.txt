Truist Insurance Holdings furthers growth through acquisition of Constellation Affiliated Partners from RedBird Capital Partners. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRUIST INSURANCE HOLDINGS, INC., A SUBSIDIARY OF TRUIST FINANCIAL CORPORATION, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT WITH REDBIRD CAPITAL PARTNERS TO ACQUIRE CONSTELLATION AFFILIATED PARTNERS, AN INSURANCE DISTRIBUTION PLATFORM OPERATING SEVEN MANAGING GENERAL AGENTS (MGAS) AND PROGRAM MANAGERS.
