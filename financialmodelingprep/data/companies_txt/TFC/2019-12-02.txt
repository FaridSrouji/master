BB&T Corporation Announces Redemption of Medium-Term Notes. BB&T Corporation announced the redemption on December 15, 2019, of all its outstanding (i) Fixed Rate Medium-Term Notes, Series E (Senior), due January 15, 2020, (CUSIP No. 05531FAS2) and (ii) Floating Rate Medium-Term Notes, Series E (Senior), due January 15, 2020, (CUSIP No. 05531FAT0). The redemption price for each of the Medium-Term Notes will be equal to 100% of the principal amount plus accrued and unpaid interest to, but excluding, the redemption date of December 15, 2019. Since December 15, 2019, is not a business day, the redemption price will be paid on the next business day, December 16, 2019.
