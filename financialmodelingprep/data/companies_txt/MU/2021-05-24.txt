Micron Hosts U.S. Secretary of Commerce and Congressional and Local Leaders at its State-of-the-Art Manassas, Virginia Fabrication Facility. MANASSAS, VA., MAY 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MICRON TECHNOLOGY, INC. (NASDAQ: MU), TODAY HOSTED U.S. SECRETARY OF COMMERCE GINA RAIMONDO AND CONGRESSIONAL AND LOCAL OFFICIALS FOR A TOUR OF ITS MANASSAS, VA., ADVANCED MANUFACTURING FACILITY. THE VISIT UNDERSCORED THE IMPORTANCE OF DOMESTIC SEMICONDUCTOR MANUFACTURERS, AND HIGHLIGHTED MICRON'S POSITION IN THE INDUSTRY AS THE ONLY MANUFACTURER OF MEMORY AND STORAGE PRODUCTS IN THE U.S. SECRETARY RAIMONDO WAS JOINED BY U.S. SENATORS JOHN CORNYN (R-TX) AND MARK R. WARNER (D-VA) ALONG WITH MANASSAS MAYOR MICHELLE DAVIS-YOUNGER.
