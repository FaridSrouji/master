Vertex Pharmaceuticals And Affinia Therapeutics Establish Multi-Year Collaboration. VERTEX PHARMACEUTICALS AND AFFINIA THERAPEUTICS ESTABLISH MULTI-YEAR COLLABORATION TO DISCOVER AND DEVELOP NOVEL AAV CAPSIDS FOR GENETIC THERAPIES.VERTEX PHARMACEUTICALS - AFFINIA THERAPEUTICS WILL BE ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE OVER $1.6 BILLION IN UPFRONT & DEVELOPMENT, REGULATORY AND COMMERCIAL MILESTONES.
