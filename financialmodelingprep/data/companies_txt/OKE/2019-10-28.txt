ONEOK, Inc. Announces Earnings Results for the Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2019; Updates Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. ONEOK, Inc. announced earnings results for the quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, the company's reported net income was $309.2 million compared to $313.9 million for the same period a year ago. Operating income was $482.2 million compared to $495.5 million for the same period a year ago. Net income per diluted share was $0.74 compared to $0.75 for the same period a year ago. For the nine months, the company's reported net income was $958.3 million compared to $862.1 million for the same period a year ago. Operating income was $1,427.0 million compared to $1,363.6 million for the same period a year ago. Net income per diluted share was $2.31 compared to $2.07 for the same period a year ago. For the year 2019, the company net income is expected to be in the range of $1,220 million to $1,330 million, compared with previous range of $1,140 million to $1,400 million.
