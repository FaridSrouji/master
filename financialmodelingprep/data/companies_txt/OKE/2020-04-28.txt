ONEOK Reports First Quarter 2020 Earnings. ONEOK ANNOUNCES FIRST QUARTER 2020 EARNINGS.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.83 EXCLUDING ITEMS.Q1 LOSS PER SHARE $0.34.GIVEN CURRENT INDUSTRY AND ECONOMIC ENVIRONMENT, IT IS IMPRACTICAL FOR CO TO PROVIDE TRADITIONAL FINANCIAL GUIDANCE FOR 2020.BELIEVES RANGE OF POSSIBLE 2020 NET INCOME RESULTS WILL LIKELY BE BETWEEN $500 MILLION AND $900 MILLION.GROWTH CAPITAL EXPENDITURES FOR 2020 NOW EXPECTED TO RANGE FROM $1,400 MILLION TO $1,800 MILLION, INCLUDING MORE THAN $900 MILLION SPENT IN Q1 2020.REPORTED A NET LOSS OF $141.9 MILLION IN Q1 2020, WHICH INCLUDES NONCASH IMPAIRMENT CHARGES OF $641.8 MILLION.
