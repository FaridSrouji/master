TransDigm Group Incorporated Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fiscal Year Ending September 30, 2020. TransDigm Group Incorporated provided earnings guidance for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2020. The company net sales are anticipated to be in the range of $6,175 million to $6,325 million compared with $5,223 million in fiscal 2019. The company expects net income of $1,040 million or $17.00 per share.
