TransDigm Group Reports Fiscal 2021 Second Quarter Results. CLEVELAND, MAY 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TRANSDIGM GROUP INCORPORATED (NYSE: TDG), A LEADING GLOBAL DESIGNER, PRODUCER AND SUPPLIER OF HIGHLY ENGINEERED AIRCRAFT COMPONENTS, TODAY REPORTED RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER ENDED APRIL 3, 2021, WHICH WERE SIGNIFICANTLY IMPACTED BY THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC. SECOND QUARTER HIGHLIGHTS INCLUDE: NET SALES OF $1,194 MILLION, DOWN 17.3% FROM $1,443 MILLION IN THE PRIOR YEAR'S QUARTER; INCOME FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $105 MILLION; EARNINGS PER SHARE FROM CONTINUING OPERATIONS OF $1.79; EBITDA AS DEFINED MARGIN OF 43.5%, REPRESENTING SEQUENTIAL IMPROVEMENT; EBITDA AS DEFINED OF $519 MILLION; ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $2.58; AND POSITIVE OPERATING CASH FLOW GENERATION OF $98 MILLION.
