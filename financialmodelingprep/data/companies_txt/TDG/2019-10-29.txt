TransDigm Group To Seek Acquisitions. TransDigm Group Incorporated (NYSE:TDG) will look for acquisitions. TransDigm Group announced that its wholly-owned subsidiary, TransDigm Inc., is planning, subject to market and other conditions, to offer senior subordinated notes in a private offering. TransDigm Group intends to use a portion of the net proceeds from the offering of the Notes to redeem all of its outstanding 6.00% senior subordinated notes due 2022. The remaining net proceeds will be used for general corporate purposes, which may include potential future acquisitions, dividends or repurchases under its stock repurchase program.
