Newmont Sells Royalty Portfolio for $90 Mln. PRESS RELEASE - NEWMONT SELLS ROYALTY PORTFOLIO FOR $90 MILLION.NEWMONT - WILL RECEIVE 15 MILLION IN CASH, 12 MILLION MAVERIX COMMON SHARES, AND UP TO $15 MILLION IN CONTINGENT CASH PAYMENTS.NEWMONT - ROYALTIES PROVIDE EXPOSURE TO FIVE GOLD ASSETS OWNED AND OPERATED BY GROWTH-ORIENTED MINING COMPANIES.NEWMONT - UPON COMPLETION, NEWMONT'S OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN MAVERIX WILL INCREASE FROM 26 PERCENT TO ABOUT 32 PERCENT.
