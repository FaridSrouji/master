Newmont Goldcorp Confirms Illegal Blockade Lifted At Peñasquito Mine In Mexico. Oct 9  - Newmont Goldcorp Corp <NEM.N>::NEWMONT GOLDCORP CONFIRMS ILLEGAL BLOCKADE LIFTED AT PEÑASQUITO MINE IN MEXICO.AT PEÑASQUITO MINE IN MEXICO, OPERATIONS REMAIN TEMPORARILY SUSPENDED PENDING FURTHER ASSURANCES.ILLEGAL BLOCKADE EXPECTED TO IMPACT COMPANY'S FULL-YEAR RESULTS FOR OPERATION.ILLEGAL BLOCKADE IMPACTED PEÑASQUITO'S Q3 PRODUCTION BY APPROXIMATELY 11,000 GOLD OUNCES.OPERATIONS AT PEÑASQUITO HAD BEEN SAFELY SUSPENDED SINCE ILLEGAL BLOCKADE BEGAN ON SEPT 14.ILLEGAL BLOCKADE IMPACTED PEÑASQUITO'S Q3 PRODUCTION BY APPROXIMATELY 1.7 MILLION SILVER OUNCES, 13.7 MILLION POUNDS OF LEAD.ILLEGAL BLOCKADE IMPACTED PEÑASQUITO'S Q3 PRODUCTION BY APPROXIMATELY 22.8 MILLION POUNDS OF ZINC.THROUGH SEPTEMBER 30, BLOCKADE REPRESENTS AN APPROXIMATE PRODUCTION SHORTFALL OF 51,000 GOLD EQUIVALENT OUNCES FROM CO-PRODUCTS.
