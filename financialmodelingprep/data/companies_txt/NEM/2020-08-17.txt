Newmont Can Exercise Option Only In Event Kirkland Intends To Restart Operations At Holt Mine. NEWMONT CORPORATION - CAN EXERCISE OPTION ONLY IN EVENT KIRKLAND INTENDS TO RESTART OPERATIONS AT HOLT MINE.NEWMONT CORPORATION - EFFECT OF OPTION STRUCTURE IS THAT CO WILL HAVE NO ADDITIONAL LIABILITY EXPOSURE IN RELATION TO HOLT ROYALTY.NEWMONT CORPORATION  - EXPECTS TO REMOVE ABOUT $350 MILLION LIABILITY FOR HOLT ROYALTY ON ITS BALANCE SHEET AT JUNE 30, 2020.
