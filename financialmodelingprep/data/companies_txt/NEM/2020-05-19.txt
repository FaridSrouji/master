Newmont Provides Updated 2020 Outlook And Confirms Long-Term Guidance. NEWMONT PROVIDES UPDATED 2020 OUTLOOK AND CONFIRMS LONG-TERM GUIDANCE.NEWMONT - NEWMONT'S 2020 OUTLOOK IS APPROXIMATELY 6.0 MILLION OUNCES OF ATTRIBUTABLE GOLD PRODUCTION.NEWMONT - 2020 COSTS APPLICABLE TO SALES IS EXPECTED TO BE $775 PER OUNCE.NEWMONT - CONTINUES TO RAMP UP OPERATIONS AT PEÑASQUITO, CERRO NEGRO, ÉLÉONORE AND YANACOCHA.NEWMONT - 2020 ALL-IN SUSTAINING COSTS IS EXPECTED TO BE $1,015 PER OUNCE.NEWMONT - AMONG NEWMONT'S 12 OPERATING MINES AND TWO JOINT VENTURES, 13 SITES WILL BE FULLY OPERATIONAL IN COMING WEEKS.NEWMONT - REVISED 2020 OUTLOOK INCLUDES PRODUCTION, COST IMPACTS FROM FIVE OPERATIONS TEMPORARILY PLACED INTO CARE AND MAINTENANCE.NEWMONT - REVISED 2020 OUTLOOK INCLUDES PRODUCTION,COST IMPACTS FROM FIVE OPERATIONS TEMPORARILY PLACED INTO CARE,MAINTENANCE FOR AVERAGE OF 45 DAYS.NEWMONT - Q2 IS EXPECTED TO BE LOWEST PRODUCTION AND HIGHEST COST QUARTER OF 2020 AS SITES RAMP UP FROM CARE AND MAINTENANCE.NEWMONT - FOR EXPLORATION & ADVANCED PROJECTS, ABOUT 80% OF CO'S EXPLORATION BUDGET IS ALLOCATED TO NEAR-MINE WITH MAJORITY OF WORK SUSPENDED IN MARCH.NEWMONT - TOTAL 2020 CAPITAL EXPENDITURE IS NOW EXPECTED TO BE ABOUT $1.3 BILLION.
