Newmont Goldcorp Entered Into A Contractual Arrangement To Support Zijin Mining Group's Bid To Acquire Continental Gold. NEWMONT GOLDCORP CORP - HAS ENTERED INTO A CONTRACTUAL ARRANGEMENT TO SUPPORT ZIJIN MINING GROUP'S BID TO ACQUIRE CONTINENTAL GOLD.NEWMONT GOLDCORP CORP - BY SELLING RED LAKE FOR $375 MILLION, CO EXPECTS TO REALIZE $635 MILLION IN TOTAL CASH PROCEEDS WHEN TRANSACTIONS CLOSE IN Q1 2020.NEWMONT GOLDCORP CORP - COMMITS TO SALE OF STAKE IN CONTINENTAL GOLD BY SELLING ITS 19.9 PERCENT EQUITY STAKE AND ITS CONVERTIBLE BOND FOR $260 MILLION.NEWMONT GOLDCORP CORP - PROPOSED TRANSACTION INVOLVES NEWMONT TENDERING ITS 19.9 PERCENT EQUITY STAKE AND SELLING ITS CONVERTIBLE BOND.
Newmont To Repurchase Up To $1 Bln Of Stock. NEWMONT TO REPURCHASE UP TO $1 BILLION OF STOCK.NEWMONT GOLDCORP CORP - STOCK REPURCHASE PROGRAM FOR UP TO $1 BILLION OF COMMON EQUITY TO BE COMPLETED IN NEXT 12 MONTHS.
Newmont Goldcorp Sees 2020 Attributable Gold Production Guidance Of 6.7 Mln Ounces At AISC Of $975 Per Ounce. NEWMONT GOLDCORP CORP - SEES 2020 ATTRIBUTABLE GOLD PRODUCTION GUIDANCE OF 6.7 MILLION OUNCES AT AISC OF $975 PER OUNCE.NEWMONT GOLDCORP - ATTRIBUTABLE GOLD PRODUCTION IS EXPECTED TO BE BETWEEN 6.5 & 7.0 MILLION OUNCES PER YEAR LONGER-TERM THROUGH 2024 WITH IMPROVING COSTS.NEWMONT GOLDCORP - EXPECTS TO PRODUCE APPROXIMATELY 1.1 MILLION GOLD EQUIVALENT OUNCES FROM OTHER METALS IN 2020 AND INCREASING LONGER-TERM THROUGH 2024.NEWMONT GOLDCORP CORP - SEES ATTRIBUTABLE GOLD EQUIVALENT OUNCE PRODUCTION FROM OTHER METALS BETWEEN 1.0 AND 1.2 MILLION OUNCES IN 2021.NEWMONT - ATTRIBUTABLE SUSTAINING CAPITAL GUIDANCE IS $975 MILLION FOR 2020 AND IS EXPECTED TO BE BETWEEN $0.9 TO $1.1 BILLION LONGER-TERM THROUGH 2024.NEWMONT GOLDCORP - SEES ATTRIBUTABLE GOLD EQUIVALENT OUNCE (GEO) PRODUCTION FROM OTHER METALS BETWEEN 1.1 AND 1.3 MILLION OUNCES IN 2022.NEWMONT GOLDCORP - SEES ATTRIBUTABLE GOLD EQUIVALENT OUNCE PRODUCTION FROM OTHER METALS BETWEEN 1.3 AND 1.5 MILLION OUNCES IN 2023 AND 2024.NEWMONT GOLDCORP - GOLD COSTS APPLICABLE TO SALES GUIDANCE IS $750 PER OUNCE FOR 2020 AND BETWEEN $650 AND $750 PER OUNCE FOR 2021 AND 2022.NEWMONT GOLDCORP - GOLD COSTS APPLICABLE TO SALES EXPECTED TO IMPROVE TO BETWEEN $600 AND $700 PER OUNCE FOR 2023 AND 2024.NEWMONT GOLDCORP - GOLD ALL-IN SUSTAINING COSTS GUIDANCE IS $975 PER OUNCE FOR 2020 AND BETWEEN $850 AND $950 PER OUNCE FOR 2021 AND 2022.
Newmont Goldcorp Corporation authorizes a Buyback Plan.. The Board of Directors of Newmont Goldcorp Corporation has authorized a buyback plan on December 2, 2019.
