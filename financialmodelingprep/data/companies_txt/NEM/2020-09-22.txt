Newmont- Entered Into A Definitive Agreement With Maverix Metals To Sell A Portfolio Of 11 Royalties To Maverix. NEWMONT - ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT WITH MAVERIX METALS INC. (MAVERIX) TO SELL A PORTFOLIO OF ELEVEN ROYALTIES TO MAVERIX.NEWMONT - AS PARTIAL CONSIDERATION UNDER TRANSACTION, NEWMONT WILL ACQUIRE 12 MILLION COMMON SHARES OF MAVERIX.NEWMONT - 12 MILLION COMMON SHARES ISSUED TO NEWMONT WILL HAVE VALUE OF $79.1 MILLION (US$60 MILLION) OR $6.591 (US$5) PER SHARE.
