Analog Devices Announces $2.5 Billion Sustainability-Linked Revolving Credit Facility. WILMINGTON, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ANALOG DEVICES, INC. (NASDAQ: ADI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SUCCESSFULLY EXECUTED A $2.5 BILLION SUSTAINABILITY-LINKED SENIOR REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY (THE “FACILITY”), REPLACING ITS EXISTING $1.25 BILLION SENIOR REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY. HALF OF THE $2.5 BILLION FACILITY WILL BECOME AVAILABLE ONLY UPON THE CONSUMMATION OF ADI'S ACQUISITION OF MAXIM INTEGRATED PRODUCTS, INC.
