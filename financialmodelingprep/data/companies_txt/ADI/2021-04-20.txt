Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution and Analog Devices Launch Ocean and Climate Innovation Accelerator. WOODS HOLE, MASS. & WILMINGTON, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WOODS HOLE OCEANOGRAPHIC INSTITUTION (WHOI) AND ANALOG DEVICES, INC. (NASDAQ: ADI) TODAY LAUNCHED THE OCEAN AND CLIMATE INNOVATION ACCELERATOR (OCIA) CONSORTIUM.
