General Dynamics Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 29, 2019. General Dynamics Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 29, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 9,761 million compared to USD 9,094 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,216 million compared to USD 1,135 million a year ago. Net income was USD 913 million compared to USD 851 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 3.17 compared to USD 2.92 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.17 compared to USD 2.88 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 3.14 compared to USD 2.89 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.14 compared to USD 2.85 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 28,577 million compared to USD 25,815 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 3,320 million compared to USD 3,231 million a year ago. Net income was USD 2,464 million compared to USD 2,436 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 8.55 compared to USD 8.27 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 8.55 compared to USD 8.23 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 8.47 compared to USD 8.16 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 8.47 compared to USD 8.12 a year ago.
