General Dynamics Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend, Payable February 7, 2020. The board of directors of General Dynamics declared a regular quarterly dividend of $1.02 per share on the company's common stock, payable February 7, 2020, to shareholders of record on January 17, 2020.
