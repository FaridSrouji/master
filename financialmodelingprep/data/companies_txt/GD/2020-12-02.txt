General Dynamics Board Declares Dividend. RESTON, VA., DEC. 2, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.10 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE FEBRUARY 5, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JANUARY 15, 2021.
