General Dynamics Board Declares Dividend, Authorizes Additional Share Repurchases. RESTON, VA., JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GENERAL DYNAMICS (NYSE: GD) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.19 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE AUGUST 6, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JULY 2, 2021.
