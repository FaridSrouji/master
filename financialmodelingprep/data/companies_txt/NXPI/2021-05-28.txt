NXP Semiconductors Appoints Joe Kaeser as Vice-Chair of the Board. EINDHOVEN, THE NETHERLANDS, MAY 28, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. (NASDAQ: NXPI) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPOINTED JOE KAESER, NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR, AS VICE-CHAIR OF THE BOARD. MR. KAESER HAS SERVED AS A NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR AND HIGHLY VALUED MEMBER OF NXP'S BOARD SINCE 2010 AND IS A MEMBER OF NXP'S NOMINATING & GOVERNANCE COMMITTEE. MR. KAESER FORMERLY SERVED AS PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF SIEMENS AG.
