NXP Brings GaN to 5G Multi-Chip Modules for Energy-Efficient Mobile Networks. EINDHOVEN, NETHERLANDS, JUNE 28, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. (NASDAQ: NXPI) TODAY ANNOUNCED A MAJOR INDUSTRY MILESTONE FOR 5G ENERGY EFFICIENCY WITH THE INTEGRATION OF GALLIUM NITRIDE (GAN) TECHNOLOGY TO ITS MULTI-CHIP MODULE PLATFORM. BUILDING ON THE COMPANY'S INVESTMENT IN ITS GAN FAB IN ARIZONA, THE MOST ADVANCED FAB DEDICATED TO RF POWER AMPLIFIERS IN THE UNITED STATES, NXP IS THE FIRST TO ANNOUNCE RF SOLUTIONS FOR 5G MASSIVE MIMO THAT COMBINE THE HIGH EFFICIENCY OF GAN WITH THE COMPACTNESS OF MULTI-CHIP MODULES.
