NXP Semiconductors Announces Conference Call to Review Second Quarter 2021 Financial Results. EINDHOVEN, THE NETHERLANDS, JULY 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NXP SEMICONDUCTORS N.V. (NASDAQ: NXPI) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL RELEASE FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE SECOND QUARTER 2021 AFTER THE CLOSE OF NORMAL TRADING ON THE NASDAQ GLOBAL SELECT MARKET ON MONDAY, AUGUST 2, 2021. THE COMPANY WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL WITH THE FINANCIAL COMMUNITY ON TUESDAY, AUGUST 3, 2021 AT 8:00 A.M. U.S. EASTERN DAYLIGHT TIME (EDT).
