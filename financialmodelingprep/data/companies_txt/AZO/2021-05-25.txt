AutoZone 3rd Quarter Same Store Sales Increase 28.9%; EPS Increases to $26.48. MEMPHIS, TENN., MAY 25, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AUTOZONE, INC. (NYSE: AZO) TODAY REPORTED NET SALES OF $3.7 BILLION FOR ITS THIRD QUARTER (12 WEEKS) ENDED MAY 8, 2021, AN INCREASE OF 31.4% FROM THE THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL 2020 (12 WEEKS). DOMESTIC SAME STORE SALES, OR SALES FOR STORES OPEN AT LEAST ONE YEAR, INCREASED 28.9% FOR THE QUARTER.
