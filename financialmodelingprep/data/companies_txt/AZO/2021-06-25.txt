AutoZone Announces Senior Vice President Promotions. MEMPHIS, TENN., JUNE 25, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AUTOZONE, INC. (NYSE: AZO), TODAY ANNOUNCES TWO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT PROMOTIONS. DENNIS LERICHE, VICE PRESIDENT, STORES, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, STORE OPERATIONS AND GRANT MCGEE, VICE PRESIDENT, STORES, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, COMMERCIAL.
