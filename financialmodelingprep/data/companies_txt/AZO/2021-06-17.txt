AutoZone Announces Organization Changes. MEMPHIS, TENN., JUNE 17, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AUTOZONE, INC. (NYSE: AZO), TODAY ANNOUNCED CHANGES TO THE ORGANIZATION. PHIL DANIELE, A TWENTY-SEVEN YEAR AUTOZONER, CURRENTLY SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, COMMERCIAL, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, MERCHANDISING, MARKETING, AND SUPPLY CHAIN.   PRESTON FRAZER, A FOURTEEN YEAR AUTOZONER, CURRENTLY SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, STORE OPERATIONS, HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT, STORE OPERATIONS, COMMERCIAL AND LOSS PREVENTION.
