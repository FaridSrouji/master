Autozone, Inc. Announces Changes to Its Board. AutoZone, Inc. announced that Directors W. Andrew McKenna and Luis P. Nieto have declined to stand for re-election to the Board of Directors at the Company Shareholder Meeting on December 18, 2019. Each will perform their Director's duties until that time and will retire from the board in December.
