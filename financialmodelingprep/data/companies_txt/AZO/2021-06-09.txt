AutoZone Vendor Partner Summit Recognizes Contributions of Top Suppliers. MEMPHIS, TENN., JUNE 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AUTOZONE (NYSE:AZO) RECOGNIZED 17 OF ITS TOP SUPPLIERS AT ITS VIRTUAL AUTOZONE VENDOR PARTNER SUMMIT ON JUNE 8, 2021. THE AWARDS HONOR COMPANIES WHO PROVIDED EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE AND DEMONSTRATED A STRONG COMMITMENT TO CUSTOMER SATISFACTION DURING THE PAST YEAR.
