Under Amour, Inc. Announces Resignation of William R. McDermott from Its Board of Directors, Effective January 1, 2020. On November 13, 2019, William R. McDermott, a member of the Board of Directors of Under Amour, Inc. notified the Company of his intention to resign from its Board of Directors, effective January 1, 2020, as he assumes the role of President and Chief Executive Officer of ServiceNow, Inc.
