Under Armour Increases its Minimum Pay Rate to $15 Per Hour. BALTIMORE, MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, UNDER ARMOUR ANNOUNCED UP TO A 50% INCREASE IN ITS MINIMUM PAY RATE FOR HOURLY TEAMMATES IN THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA. HOURLY RATES WILL INCREASE FROM A MINIMUM OF $10 PER HOUR TO $15 PER HOUR ($15.25 CANADIAN DOLLARS PER HOUR IN CANADA), WHICH WILL GO INTO EFFECT ON JUNE 6.
