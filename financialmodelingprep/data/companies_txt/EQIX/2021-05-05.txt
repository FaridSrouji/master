S&P and Fitch Upgrade Equinix to BBB on Strength of Interconnection and Improved Credit Quality. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF., MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BOTH STANDARD & POOR'S ("S&P") AND FITCH RATINGS ("FITCH") HAVE UPGRADED ALL OF EQUINIX'S CREDIT RATINGS TO BBB, FROM THE PREVIOUS RATING OF BBB-.
