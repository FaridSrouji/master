Equinix, Inc. to Open New Data Center in Warsaw. Equinix, Inc. announced the development of a new data center in Warsaw, opening in first quarter of 2020. Known as WA3, the new International Business Exchange™ (IBX®) data center will offer colocation, as well as a host of advanced interconnection services. By bringing this capacity to market, the company is helping Polish businesses to advance their business digitization and cloud transformation initiatives, as well as enabling global businesses to expand their digital operations in Poland.
