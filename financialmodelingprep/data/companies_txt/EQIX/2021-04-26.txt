Businesses in Six New Global Markets Can Now Access Private Cloud On-ramps to Microsoft Azure ExpressRoute on Platform Equinix. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF., APRIL 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- EQUINIX, INC. (NASDAQ: EQIX), THE WORLD'S DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE COMPANY™, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT IS EXPANDING PRIVATE NETWORK ACCESS TO MICROSOFT AZURE VIA AZURE EXPRESSROUTE ON PLATFORM EQUINIX® TO SERVICE CUSTOMERS IN SIX NEW GLOBAL MARKETS, INCLUDING BERLIN, BOGOTÁ, CANBERRA, DUBAI, RIO DE JANEIRO AND SEOUL.
