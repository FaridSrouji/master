Applied Therapeutics, Inc. announced that it expects to receive funding from Frostrow Capital LLP, OrbiMed Advisors LLC, Franklin Resources, Inc., Franklin Templeton International Services S.à r.l., and other investors. Applied Therapeutics, Inc. (NasdaqGM:APLT) announced that the company has entered into a definitive securities purchase agreement of 1,380,344 shares at an issue price of $14.50 per share for gross proceeds of $20,014,988 on November 7, 2019. The transaction will involve participation from The Biotech Growth Trust PLC (LSE:BIOG), a fund managed by Frostrow Capital LLP for 344,827 shares, OrbiMed Partners Master Fund Limited and OrbiMed Genesis Master Fund, L.P., funds managed by OrbiMed Advisors LLC for 344,827 shares and 137,932 shares respectively, Franklin Strategic Series - Franklin Biotechnology Discovery Fund (MutualFund:FBDI.X), a fund managed by Franklin Resources, Inc. (NYSE:BEN) for 172,413 shares, Franklin Templeton Investment Funds SICAV - Franklin Biotechnology Discovery Fund, a fund managed by Franklin Templeton International Services S.à r.l. for 172,413 shares, Hessler Finance LTD for 70,000 shares, Laurence Lytton for 55,173 shares, Richard Smith for 34,483 shares, The Ryan and Brittany Pearson Living Trust for 17,241 shares, Timothy Hogue for 13,793 shares, Reinfrank Living Trust, dated 6/13/95 for 10,345 shares, and Gregg Smith for 6,897 shares. The shares issued in the transaction have a par value of $0.0001 per share. The transaction is expected to close on or about November 12, 2019, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. The shares issued in the transaction have a lock-up period of 90 days, post the closing of the transaction. The securities will be issued, pursuant to exemption provided under Regulation D.
