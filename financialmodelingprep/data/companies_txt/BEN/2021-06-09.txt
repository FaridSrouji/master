Franklin Resources, Inc. Announces Month-End Assets Under Management. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT OF $1,543.5 BILLION AT MAY 31, 2021, COMPARED TO $1,529.3 BILLION AT APRIL 30, 2021. THIS MONTH'S INCREASE IN AUM PRIMARILY REFLECTED THE POSITIVE IMPACT OF MARKETS AND CASH MANAGEMENT NET INFLOWS, PARTIALLY OFFSET BY SLIGHT LONG-TERM NET OUTFLOWS INCLUSIVE OF APPROXIMATELY $300 MILLION OF OUTFLOWS FROM THE PREVIOUSLY DISCLOSED NON-MANAGEMENT
