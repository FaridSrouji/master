Franklin Resources, Inc. Announces Month-End Assets Under Management. SAN MATEO, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FRANKLIN RESOURCES, INC. (FRANKLIN TEMPLETON) (NYSE: BEN) TODAY REPORTED PRELIMINARY MONTH-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT OF $1,500.5 BILLION AT FEBRUARY 28, 2021, COMPARED TO $1,494.4 BILLION AT JANUARY 31, 2021. THIS MONTH'S INCREASE IN AUM PRIMARILY REFLECTED THE POSITIVE IMPACT OF MARKETS AND LONG-TERM NET INFLOWS,1 PARTIALLY OFFSET BY CASH MANAGEMENT NET OUTFLOWS.
