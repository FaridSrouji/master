ONE-TIME CHARGES, NON-RECURRING, ACQUISITION RELATED EXPENSES.FRANKLIN RESOURCES INC - ALL-CASH CONSIDERATION OF $4.5 BILLION WILL BE FUNDED FROM COMPANY'S EXISTING BALANCE SHEET CASH.FRANKLIN RESOURCES INC -  TRIAN FUND MANAGEMENT, FUNDS MANAGED BY IT, WHICH OWN 4.5% OF LEGG MASON STOCK,  ENTERED VOTING AGREEMENT IN SUPPORT OF DEAL.FRANKLIN RESOURCES INC - THERE WILL BE NO CHANGES TO SENIOR MANAGEMENT TEAMS OF LEGG MASON'S INVESTMENT AFFILIATES..FRANKLIN RESOURCES INC - COMBINED FIRM WILL OPERATE AS FRANKLIN TEMPLETON.FRANKLIN RESOURCES INC - FOLLOWING CLOSING OF DEAL, JENNY JOHNSON WILL CONTINUE TO SERVE AS PRESIDENT AND CEO.FRANKLIN RESOURCES INC -  LEGG MASON AFFILIATE ENTRUST GLOBAL TO REPURCHASE ITS BUSINESS, WHICH WILL BE ACQUIRED BY ITS MANAGEMENT AT CLOSING.FRANKLIN RESOURCES INC - ENTRUST WILL MAINTAIN AN ONGOING RELATIONSHIP WITH CO.FRANKLIN RESOURCES INC - DEAL EXPECTED TO RESULT IN ABOUT $200 MILLION IN ANNUAL COST SAVINGS.
