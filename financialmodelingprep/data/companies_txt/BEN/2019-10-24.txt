Franklin Resources, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Fourth Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Franklin Resources, Inc. announced earnings results for the fourth quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the fourth quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 1,452.5 million compared to USD 1,527.2 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 391.5 million compared to USD 478.7 million a year ago. Net income was USD 306.4 million compared to USD 502.5 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.61 compared to USD 0.96 a year ago. For the full year, total revenue was USD 5,774.5 million compared to USD 6,319.1 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,557.4 million compared to USD 2,118.6 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,195.7 million compared to USD 764.4 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.35 compared to USD 1.39 a year ago.
