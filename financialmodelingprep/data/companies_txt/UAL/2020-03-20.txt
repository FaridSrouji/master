United Airlines To Reduce Payroll In Line With 60% Scheduled April Reduction Without Govt Aid. A MESSAGE FROM OSCAR MUNOZ, SCOTT KIRBY AND LABOR LEADERSHIP.UNITED AIRLINES - COMPANY WILL BEGIN TO TAKE NECESSARY STEPS TO REDUCE OUR PAYROLL IN LINE WITH 60% SCHEDULE REDUCTION WE ANNOUNCED FOR APRIL.UNITED AIRLINES - IF CONGRESS DOESN'T ACT ON SUFFICIENT GOVERNMENT BY MARCH END, WILL BEGIN TO TAKE STEPS TO CUT PAYROLL IN LINE WITH 60% REDUCTION ANNOUNCED.
