United Airlines & Air New Zealand Announce Nonstop Service Between New York/Newark & Auckland, New Zealand. PRESS RELEASE - UNITED AIRLINES AND AIR NEW ZEALAND ANNOUNCE FIRST EVER NONSTOP SERVICE BETWEEN NEW YORK/NEWARK AND AUCKLAND, NEW ZEALAND.UNITED AIRLINES - AIR NEW ZEALAND AND CO ANNOUNCED NONSTOP SERVICE BETWEEN NEW ZEALAND AND UNITED STATES EAST COAST WILL BEGIN IN OCTOBER 2020.
