United Airlines Says Implementing 2nd Phase Of Temporary Service Reductions Between U.S. Hub Cities & Beijing, HK, Shanghai. UNITED AIRLINES - IMPLEMENTING A SECOND PHASE OF TEMPORARY REDUCTIONS IN SERVICE BETWEEN HUB CITIES AND BEIJING, HONG KONG AND SHANGHAI.UNITED AIRLINES - IMPLEMENTING 2ND PHASE OF TEMPORARY REDUCTIONS IN SERVICE AS THE SIGNIFICANT DECLINE IN DEMAND FOR TRAVEL TO CHINA CONTINUES.UNITED AIRLINES - REDUCTION OF SERVICE BEGINNING FEB. 9 & RUNNING THROUGH MARCH 28 INCLUDES 332 ADDITIONAL ROUNDTRIP CANCELLATIONS.UNITED AIRLINES - WILL CONTINUE TO MONITOR SITUATION IN CHINA AS IT DEVELOPS AND WILL ADJUST SCHEDULE AS NEEDED.UNITED AIRLINES - REDUCING OUR 12 DAILY DEPARTURES FROM THE UNITED STATES FOR MAINLAND CHINA/HONG KONG TO FOUR DAILY DEPARTURES.
