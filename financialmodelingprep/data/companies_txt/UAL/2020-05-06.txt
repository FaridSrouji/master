United Airlines Announces Proposed Senior Secured Notes Offering. UNITED AIRLINES ANNOUNCES PROPOSED SENIOR SECURED NOTES OFFERING.UNITED AIRLINES - INTENDS TO COMMENCE A PRIVATE OFFERING TO ELIGIBLE PURCHASERS OF $2.25 BILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF TWO SERIES OF NOTES.UNITED AIRLINES - TO USE NET PROCEEDS FROM OFFERING OF NOTES TO REPAY $2.0 BILLION AMOUNT OUTSTANDING UNDER TERM LOAN FACILITY ON MARCH 9, AMONG OTHERS.
