United Airlines Applauds Spain's Decision to Reopen to Vaccinated Travelers. CHICAGO, MAY 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- UNITED AIRLINES APPLAUDS SPAIN FOLLOWING ITS DECISION TO REOPEN TRAVEL TO VACCINATED VISITORS BEGINNING JUNE 7. THE ANNOUNCEMENT COMES FOLLOWING THE EUROPEAN UNION COUNCIL'S FORMAL RECOMMENDATION THAT EU MEMBER STATES CAN REOPEN TO FULLY VACCINATED TOURISTS AND UNITED LOOKS FORWARD TO WELCOMING BACK CUSTOMERS ON OVER 30 DAILY FLIGHTS TO 16 DESTINATIONS IN EUROPE THIS SUMMER, INCLUDING SERVICE BETWEEN NEW YORK/NEWARK AND BARCELONA AND MADRID.
