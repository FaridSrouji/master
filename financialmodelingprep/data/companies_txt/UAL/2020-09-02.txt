UNITED AIRLINES FLIGHT ATTENDANTS UNION SAYS COMPANY'S FURLOUGH ANNOUNCEMENT 'DOES NOT TELL THE FULL STORY', 14,000 FLIGHT ATTENDANTS WITHOUT WORK IF NO FEDERAL AID.
United Airlines To Notify 16,370 Employees Of Involuntary Furlough. UNITED AIRLINES SAYS 16,370 UNITED EMPLOYEES TO BE NOTIFIED OF INVOLUNTARY FURLOUGH EFFECTIVE AS EARLY AS OCTOBER 1.UNITED AIRLINES SAYS INVOLUNTARY FURLOUGHS INCLUDE 1,400 MANAGEMENT AND ADMINISTRATIVE EMPLOYEES.UNITED AIRLINES - DON'T EXPECT DEMAND TO RETURN TO ANYTHING RESEMBLING NORMAL UNTIL THERE IS A WIDELY AVAILABLE TREATMENT.UNITED AIRLINES SAYS EXTENSION TO PAYROLL SUPPORT PROGRAM WOULD BE ONE THING THAT WOULD PREVENT INVOLUNTARY FURLOUGHS ON OCT. 1.UNITED AIRLINES SAYS EXTENSION TO PAYROLL SUPPORT PROGRAM WOULD "HOPEFULLY DELAY ANY POTENTIAL IMPACT ON EMPLOYEES UNTIL EARLY 2021".
