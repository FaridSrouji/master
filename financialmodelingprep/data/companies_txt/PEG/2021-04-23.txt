PSEG Nuclear's Hope Creek Generating Station Commenced Planned Refueling and Maintenance Outage. LOWER ALLOWAYS CREEK, N.J., APRIL 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THIS WEEK, PSEG NUCLEAR'S HOPE CREEK GENERATING STATION MARKED ANOTHER UNINTERRUPTED 18-MONTH PRODUCTION CYCLE AND ENTERED A PLANNED REFUELING AND MAINTENANCE OUTAGE AFTER OPERATORS SAFELY REMOVED THE UNIT FROM THE REGIONAL POWER GRID.
