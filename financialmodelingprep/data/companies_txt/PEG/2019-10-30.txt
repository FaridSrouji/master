Public Service Enterprise Group Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Public Service Enterprise Group Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,302 million compared to USD 2,394 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 490 million compared to USD 5 million a year ago. Net income was USD 403 million. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.79. For the nine months, sales was USD 7,598 million compared to USD 7,228 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,436 million compared to USD 12 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,256 million. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.47.
