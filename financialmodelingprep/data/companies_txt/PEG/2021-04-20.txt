PSEG Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend For Second Quarter Of 2021. NEWARK, N.J., APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP (NYSE: PEG) TODAY DECLARED A $0.51 PER SHARE DIVIDEND ON THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021.
