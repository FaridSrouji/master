PSEG Foundation Announces $1 Million in Grants to Historically Black Colleges and Universities. NEWARK, N.J., MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE PSEG FOUNDATION HAS ANNOUNCED $1 MILLION IN GRANT FUNDING TO THREE HISTORICALLY BLACK COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES: HAMPTON UNIVERSITY, HOWARD UNIVERSITY AND NORTH CAROLINA AGRICULTURAL AND TECHNICAL STATE UNIVERSITY.
