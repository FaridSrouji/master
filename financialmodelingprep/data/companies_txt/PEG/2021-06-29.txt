PSEG Completes Sale of Solar Source Portfolio to Affiliate of LS Power. NEWARK, N.J., JUNE 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PUBLIC SERVICE ENTERPRISE GROUP INC. (PSEG) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE SALE OF ITS PSEG SOLAR SOURCE LLC PORTFOLIO TO QUATTRO SOLAR LLC, AN AFFILIATE OF LS POWER.
