PSE&G Names Deborah Affonsa New Chief Customer Officer. NEWARK, N.J., JULY 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PUBLIC SERVICE ELECTRIC & GAS CO. (PSE&G) HAS NAMED DEBORAH AFFONSA TO THE NEWLY ESTABLISHED ROLE OF VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF CUSTOMER OFFICER, EFFECTIVE MONDAY, JULY 12.
