State Street Strengthens Leadership Team with Appointment of Ann Fogarty. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ANN FOGARTY TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND DEPUTY HEAD OF GLOBAL DELIVERY WITH RESPONSIBILITY FOR STATE STREET'S CUSTODY, MIDDLE OFFICE, TRANSFER AGENCY AND CLIENT SERVICE OPERATIONS. FOGARTY WILL REPORT TO LIZ NOLAN, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND HEAD OF GLOBAL DELIVERY. SHE WILL ALSO JOIN STATE STREET'S MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE. FOGARTY JOINS FROM BNY MELLON WHERE SHE WAS MOST RECENTLY EXECUTIVE COMMIT
