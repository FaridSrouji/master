Avantis Investors Appoints State Street Corporation to Provide a Range of Broad ETF Services for its Five Newly Launched Low-Cost Funds. State Street Corporation announced that it has been appointed by Avantis Investors to provide a range of broad ETF services for its five newly launched low-cost funds. Services will include basket creation, custody, accounting, transfer agency and fund administration. Avantis Investors offers active investment strategies for investors in mutual fund and ETF formats. The five funds, launched in late September on NYSE Arca, are: Avantis U.S. Equity ETF (AVUS), Avantis International Equity ETF (AVDE), Avantis Emerging Markets Equity ETF (AVEM), Avantis U.S. Small Cap Value ETF (AVUV), Avantis International Small Cap Value ETF (AVDV). The five ETFs have had a very fast start, amassing more than $400 million in total assets through the end of October.
