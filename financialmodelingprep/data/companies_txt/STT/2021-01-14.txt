State Street Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividends on Its Non-Cumulative Perpetual Preferred Stock Series “D”, “F”, and “G”. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON EACH OF THE BELOW OUTSTANDING SERIES OF NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK: SERIES D (REPRESENTED BY DEPOSITARY SHARES, EACH REPRESENTING A 1/4000TH INTEREST IN A SHARE OF SERIES D PREFERRED STOCK). THE CASH DIVIDEND IS IN THE AMOUNT OF $1,475.00 PER SHARE OF SERIES D PREFERRED STOCK (RESULTING IN A DISTRIBUTION OF APPROXIMATELY $0.3687 PER DEPOSITARY SHARE) AND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 15
