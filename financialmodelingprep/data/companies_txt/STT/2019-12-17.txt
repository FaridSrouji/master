State Street Announced New Digital Asset Pilot In Collaboration With Gemini Trust Company. STATE STREET CORP - ANNOUNCED NEW DIGITAL ASSET PILOT IN COLLABORATION WITH GEMINI TRUST COMPANY, LLC , A CRYPTOCURRENCY EXCHANGE AND CUSTODIAN.STATE STREET - PILOT BUILDS ON ONGOING RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT IN DIGITAL ASSET SPACE TO COMBINE GEMINI CUSTODY WITH STATE STREET'S BACK OFFICE REPORTING.
