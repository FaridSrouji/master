State Street Announces Chairman & Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer to Participate in Bernstein's Annual Strategic Decisions Conference. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS CHAIRMAN & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, RON O'HANLEY, AND ITS CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, ERIC ABOAF, WILL PARTICIPATE IN BERNSTEIN'S ANNUAL STRATEGIC DECISIONS CONFERENCE, TO BE HELD VIRTUALLY, ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 2, 2021, AT 10:00 AM ET.
