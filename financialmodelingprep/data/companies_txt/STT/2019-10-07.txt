State Street Corporation Appoints Robert Dollard as the Head of US Asset Owner Relationship Management. State Street Corporation appointed Robert Dollard as the head of US Asset Owner Relationship Management, effective September 4, 2019. In his new role, Dollard will oversee the company's relationships with its leading asset servicing and asset owner clients in the US. He will report to Rob Baillie, head of North American Asset Owners. Dollard has previously served as US Segment head for the public fund, Taft-Hartley, and the outsourced business at BNY Mellon, where he led teams of relationship executives and service directors in servicing USD 2.1 trillion of public fund assets.
