State Street Appoints Marie Chandoha to Its Board of Directors. State Street Corp. has reported the appointment of Marie Chandoha to its Board of Directors as a director. Chandoha brings a wealth of diverse financial services experience and knowledge gained throughout her more than 35-year career. Most recently she was CEO of Charles Schwab's Investment Management division (CSIM) where, during her eight-year tenure, she transformed the business to support the company's broader corporate strategy.
