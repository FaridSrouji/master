State Street Corporation Announces Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. State Street Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, the company reported total revenue of $2,903 million against $2,989 million a year ago. Net income was $583 million against $764 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was $1.42 against $1.87 a year ago.
