State Street Corporation Declares Fourth-Quarter Dividend on Its Common Stock. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) TODAY ANNOUNCED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.52 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON JANUARY 19, 2021 TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JANUARY 4, 2021. ABOUT STATE STREET CORPORATION STATE STREET CORPORATION (NYSE:STT) IS ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDERS OF FINANCIAL SERVICES TO INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS INCLUDING INVESTMENT SERVICING, INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT AND INVESTMENT RESEARCH AND TRADING.
