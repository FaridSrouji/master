ConocoPhillips Company Announces Production Results for the Third Quarter and Nine Months of 2019; Provides Production Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019; Announces Impairments for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. ConocoPhillips Company announced production results for the third quarter and nine months of 2019. Production excluding Libya for the third quarter of 2019 was 1,322 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day (MBOED), a 98 MBOED increase over the same period a year ago. Total reported production was 1,366 MBOED compared to 1,261 MBOED a year ago.Production excluding Libya for the first nine months of 2019 was 1,310 MBOED, an 89 MBOED increase from 1,221 MBOED for the 2018 period. Total reported production was 1,353 MBOED compared to 1,261 MBOED a year ago.The company announced impairments for the third quarter of 2019. For the quarter, the company reported impairments of $110 million compared to $33 million a year ago.The company provided production guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019. Fourth-quarter 2019 production is expected to be 1,265 to 1,305 MBOED.
