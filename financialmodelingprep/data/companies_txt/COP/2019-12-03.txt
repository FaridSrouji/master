Gibson Energy And USD Announce Joint Venture To Construct Dru At Hardisty Utilizing. GIBSON ENERGY AND USD ANNOUNCE JOINT VENTURE TO CONSTRUCT DRU AT HARDISTY UTILIZING INNOVATIVE DRUBIT™ TECHNOLOGY AND AGREEMENT WITH CONOCOPHILLIPS FOR 50,000 BBL/D OF CAPACITY.GIBSON ENERGY INC -  USD, CO TO CONSTRUCT AND OPERATE A DILUENT RECOVERY UNIT NEAR HARDISTY, ALBERTA, CANADA.GIBSON ENERGY -  CONOCOPHILLIPS CANADA CONTRACTED TO PROCESS 50,000 BPD OF INLET BITUMEN BLEND THROUGH DILUENT RECOVERY UNIT AT HARDISTY, ALBERTA.GIBSON ENERGY - FOLLOWING SEPARATION AT DRU, DRUBIT OWNED BY CONOCOPHILLIPS WILL BE RAILED BY CP AND KCS FROM HARDISTY TERMINAL TO PORT ARTHUR, TEXAS.GIBSON ENERGY INC - DRU COULD BE PLACED INTO SERVICE AS EARLY AS Q2 OF 2021.
