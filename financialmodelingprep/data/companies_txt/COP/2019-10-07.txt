ConocoPhillips Sets Quarterly Dividend Of $0.42/Share. ANNOUNCES 38 PERCENT INCREASE IN QUARTERLY DIVIDEND; EXPECTS TO REPURCHASE $3 BILLION OF SHARES IN 2020; REITERATES CONTINUED COMMITMENT TO RETURN MORE THAN 30 PERCENT OF CASH FROM OPERATIONS ANNUALLY VIA DIVIDEND AND BUYBACKS.SETS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.42PER SHARE.
ConocoPhillips Announces an Increase in Its Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 2, 2019. ConocoPhillips announced an increase in its quarterly dividend of 38%, to 42 cents per share. This represents an annualized increase in the dividend of approximately $500 million. The dividend is payable December 2, 2019, to stockholders of record at the close of business on October 7, 2019.
