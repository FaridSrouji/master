Tranche Update on ConocoPhillips Company (NYSE:COP)'s Equity Buyback Plan announced on November 10, 2016.. From July 1, 2019 to September 30, 2019, the company has repurchased 13,193,823 shares, representing 1.19% for $749.8 million. With this, the company has completed the repurchase of 155,744,405 shares, representing 13.26% for $8,875.91 million under the buyback announced on November 10, 2016.
