ConocoPhillips makes North Sea discovery. CONOCOPHILLIPS SCANDINAVIA AS, OPERATOR OF PRODUCTION LICENCE 782 S, HAS CONCLUDED THE DRILLING OF WILDCAT WELL 25/7-7.DISCOVERY MADE BY CONOCOPHILLIPS WITH PARTNERS EQUINOR, AKER BP, WINTERSHALL DEA.PRELIMINARY ESTIMATES PLACE THE SIZE OF THE DISCOVERY BETWEEN 1 AND 10 MILLION STANDARD CUBIC METRES (SM3) OF RECOVERABLE OIL EQUIVALENT (6.29 MLN TO 62.9 MLN BARRELS OF OIL EQUIVALENT).IN THE PRIMARY EXPLORATION TARGET, THE WELL ENCOUNTERED TWO SEPARATE GAS/CONDENSATE AND OIL-BEARING INTERVALS, WITH SANDY LAYERS IN THE DRAUPNE FORMATION TOTALLING ABOUT 25 METRES WITH RESERVOIR PROPERTIES VARYING FROM POOR TO VERY GOOD.THIN WATER-BEARING SILTSTONE LAYERS WERE ENCOUNTERED IN THE SECONDARY EXPLORATION TARGET IN THE HEATHER FORMATION.THE LICENSEES WILL FURTHER ASSESS THE WELL RESULT WITH REGARD TO DELINEATION/PROFITABILITY, AS WELL AS INVESTIGATION OF NEARBY PROSPECTS.
