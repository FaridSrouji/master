ConocoPhillips Provides Update On Production Curtailment Activities. CONOCOPHILLIPS PROVIDES UPDATE ON PRODUCTION CURTAILMENT ACTIVITIES.CONOCOPHILLIPS - FOR Q2, CO'S CURTAILMENTS WERE PRIMARILY RELATED TO OIL PRODUCTION & AVERAGED ABOUT 225 THOUSAND MBOED ON A NET BASIS.CONOCOPHILLIPS - OF TOTAL NET CURTAILMENTS IN Q2, ABOUT 65% WERE IN LOWER 48, 15% WERE IN ALASKA AND 15% WERE IN SURMONT OPERATION IN CANADA.CONOCOPHILLIPS - REMAINDER OF SECOND-QUARTER CURTAILMENTS WERE PRIMARILY IN MALAYSIA.CONOCOPHILLIPS - INCLUDING IMPACTS FROM CURTAILMENTS,  EXPECTS TO REPORT Q2 PRODUCTION VOLUMES OF 960 TO 980 MBOED.CONOCOPHILLIPS - CONTINUES TO MONITOR NETBACK PRICING AND EVALUATE CURTAILMENTS ACROSS ITS OPERATED ASSETS ON A MONTH-BY-MONTH BASIS.CONOCOPHILLIPS - EXPECTS TO BEGIN RESTORING CURTAILED PRODUCTION IN ALASKA DURING MONTH OF JULY.CONOCOPHILLIPS - IN LOWER 48 REGION, COMPANY ALSO EXPECTS TO BEGIN BRINGING SOME CURTAILED VOLUMES BACK ON LINE DURING JULY.CONOCOPHILLIPS - AT SURMONT, COMPANY IS ALSO PLANNING TO INCREASE PRODUCTION FROM CURTAILED LEVELS IN Q3.CONOCOPHILLIPS - COMPANY WILL CONTINUE TO SUSPEND FORWARD-LOOKING GUIDANCE AND SENSITIVITIES.CONOCOPHILLIPS - EXCLUDING LIBYA, PRODUCTION IN Q2 IS EXPECTED TO BE IN LINE WITH SAME PERIOD A YEAR AGO AND ABOUT 5 PERCENT BELOW Q1 OF 2020.
