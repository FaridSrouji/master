AIG Promotes Tibor Nagy to the Position of CFO, Australia. AIG has promoted Tibor Nagy to the position of CFO, Australia. Mr. Nagy has worked at AIG for almost 23 years, including stints in Budapest, Warsaw, New York and Bangalore, where he was AIGs Global Finance Centre Controlleruntil his promotion to the Sydney-based CFO role. Mr. Nagy reports to CEO Australia Noel Condon and CFO International General Insurance David Junius. He graduated as an economist from the Budapest Business School.
