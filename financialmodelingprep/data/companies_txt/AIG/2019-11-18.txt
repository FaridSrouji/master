American International Group, Inc. Names Allison Barrett Head of Multinational, General Insurance. American International Group, Inc. announced it has appointed Allison Barrett as Global Head of Multinational, General Insurance. Ms. Barrett will join AIG on December 9, 2019 and will be based in New York reporting to Michael Price, Head of Global Service Businesses & Global Chief Operating Officer, General Insurance. In this new role, Ms. Barrett will drive the execution of General Insurance’s multinational strategic plan and lead the worldwide delivery of the company’s multinational expertise and capabilities across product lines to AIG’s global clients and distribution partners. Ms. Barrett’s appointment builds on AIG’s 80-year leadership in providing clients with global risk management expertise and risk transfer solutions. Ms. Barrett joins AIG from Willis Towers Watson, where she served as Managing Director and Global Head of the company’s financial institutions business within the financial, executive and professional risk specialty group (FINEX) since 2017, serving banks, asset managers, insurers, REITs and FinTech companies.
