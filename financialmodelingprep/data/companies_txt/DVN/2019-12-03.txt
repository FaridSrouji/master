Devon Energy Corporation Announces Dividend for the First Quarter of 2020, Payable on March 31, 2020. Devon Energy Corp. announced that its board of directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend on Devon’s common stock for the first quarter of 2020. The dividend is payable on March 31, 2020, at a rate of $0.09 per share based on a record date of March 13, 2020.
