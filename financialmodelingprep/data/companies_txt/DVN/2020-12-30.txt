Devon Energy and WPX Energy Shareholders Approve Merger of Equals. OKLAHOMA CITY & TULSA, OKLA., DEC. 30, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY CORPORATION (“DEVON”) (NYSE: DVN) AND WPX ENERGY, INC. (“WPX”) (NYSE: WPX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SHAREHOLDERS OF BOTH COMPANIES VOTED IN FAVOR OF ALL PROPOSALS NECESSARY FOR THE CLOSING OF THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ALL-STOCK MERGER OF EQUALS BETWEEN DEVON AND WPX. THE MERGER IS ANTICIPATED TO CLOSE ON JANUARY 7, 2021.
