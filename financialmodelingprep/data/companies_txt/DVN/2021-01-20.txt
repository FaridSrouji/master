Devon Energy Changes Time of Fourth-Quarter and Full-Year 2020 Earnings Call to 10AM ET. OKLAHOMA CITY, JAN. 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DEVON ENERGY CORP. (NYSE: DVN) WILL HOST ITS FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2020 EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL EARLIER THAN PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED. THE CONFERENCE CALL WILL BE HELD AT 9 A.M. CENTRAL TIME (10 A.M.
