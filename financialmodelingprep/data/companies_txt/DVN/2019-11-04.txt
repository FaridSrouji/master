Devon Energy Corp. Updates Production Guidance for the Fourth-Quarter and Full Year 2019. Devon Energy Corp. updated production guidance for the fourth-quarter and full year 2019. Fourth-quarter 2019 oil production from retained assets is forecasted to average 154,000 to 160,000 barrels per day, a 6% sequential quarter improvement at the midpoint.The company's updated guidance represents an estimated oil growth rate range of 20% to 21% for the full-year 2019 compared to 2018, a 550-basis point improvement compared to original guidance expectations.
