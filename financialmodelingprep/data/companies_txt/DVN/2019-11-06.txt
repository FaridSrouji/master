Ql Capital Partners Enters Into Partnership With Devon Energy To Fund Delaware Basin Gas Gathering Infrastructure. QL CAPITAL PARTNERS ENTERS INTO PARTNERSHIP WITH DEVON ENERGY TO FUND DELAWARE BASIN GAS GATHERING INFRASTRUCTURE.DEVON ENERGY CORP - PER TERMS OF AGREEMENTS, QLCP WILL FUND A $100 MILLION DISTRIBUTION TO CO.DEVON ENERGY- UNDER TERMS, QLCP WILL FUND MAJORITY OF INCREMENTAL PARTNERSHIP CAPITAL TO BUILD COTTON DRAW MIDSTREAM ASSETS OVER NEXT SEVERAL YEARS.
