Desjardins Group Says Global Payments Will Be Acquiring Desjardins' Entire Merchant Portfolio. DESJARDINS GROUP SAYS GLOBAL PAYMENTS WILL BE ACQUIRING DESJARDINS' ENTIRE MERCHANT PORTFOLIO, CURRENTLY HOUSED UNDER MONETICO BRAND.DESJARDINS GROUP SAYS STARTING IN MARCH 2020, DESJARDINS' EQUIPMENT WILL BE REPLACED WITH GLOBAL PAYMENTS' SOLUTIONS.DESJARDINS- CO HAS DECIDED TO GRADUALLY PHASE OUT IN-STORE ACCORD D FINANCING OVER NEXT FEW YEARS.DESJARDINS - IN-STORE ACCORD D FINANCING ACTIVITIES WILL BEGIN WINDING DOWN ON MAY 1, 2020, AND WILL CONTINUE BEYOND 2023.
Global Payments Reports Q3 Earnings Per Share $0.54, And Raises 2019 Outlook. GLOBAL PAYMENTS REPORTS RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER 2019 AND INCREASES 2019 OUTLOOK.SEES FY 2019 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $6.12 TO $6.20.Q3 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.70.Q3 REVENUE $1.106 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.16 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.54.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.67 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.EXPECT ADJUSTED NET REVENUE PLUS NETWORK FEES FOR 2019 TO RANGE FROM $5.60 BILLION TO $5.63 BILLION.FY2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $6.17 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.EXPECT TO REALIZE ANNUAL RUN-RATE REVENUE SYNERGIES OF AT LEAST $125 MILLION FROM TSYS DEAL.EXPECT TO REALIZE ANNUAL RUN-RATE EXPENSE SYNERGIES OF AT LEAST $325 MILLION WITHIN THREE YEARS FROM TSYS DEAL.
