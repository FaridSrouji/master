Global Payments and Banco Carrefour Sign New Multi-Year Payments Agreement. CAMPINAS, BRAZIL--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GLOBAL PAYMENTS INC. (NYSE: GPN), A LEADING WORLDWIDE PROVIDER OF PAYMENT TECHNOLOGY AND SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT TSYS, ITS ISSUER SOLUTIONS BUSINESS, HAS SIGNED A MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH BANCO CARREFOUR IN BRAZIL. BANCO CARREFOUR IS THE FINANCIAL SERVICES ARM OF CARREFOUR BRASIL, THE COUNTRY'S LEADING SUPERCENTER RETAIL CHAIN.
