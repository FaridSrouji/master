Global Payments to Participate in KBW Fintech Payments Conference. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--GLOBAL PAYMENTS INC. (NYSE: GPN), A LEADING WORLDWIDE PROVIDER OF PAYMENT TECHNOLOGY AND SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PAUL TODD, SENIOR EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT & CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL PRESENT AT THE VIRTUAL KBW FINTECH PAYMENTS CONFERENCE ON THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 25, 2021 AT 8:00 A.M. ET. INTERESTED PARTIES CAN LISTEN TO A LIVE WEBCAST OF THE EVENT FROM THE INVESTOR RELATIONS SECTION OF THE COMPANY'S WEBSITE AT WWW.GLOBALPAYMENTSINC.COM. A REPLAY
