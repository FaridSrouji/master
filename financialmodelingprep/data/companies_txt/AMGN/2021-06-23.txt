Amgen Announces Approval Of Aimovig® (Erenumab) In Japan For The Suppression Of Onset Of Migraine Attacks In Adults. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., JUNE 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ:AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE JAPANESE MINISTRY OF HEALTH, LABOUR AND WELFARE HAS GRANTED MARKETING APPROVAL FOR AIMOVIG® (ERENUMAB) FOR THE SUPPRESSION OF ONSET OF MIGRAINE ATTACKS IN ADULTS.
