Amgen Provides Updated Information On LUMAKRAS™ (Sotorasib)¹ Dose Comparison Study. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ:AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT AGREED LAST NIGHT WITH THE FDA'S PROPOSED POST-MARKETING REQUIREMENT TO CONDUCT, AS PART OF THE ONGOING DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM, A MULTI-CENTER RANDOMIZED CLINICAL TRIAL TO COMPARE THE SAFETY AND EFFICACY OF LUMAKRAS™ AT 960 MG ONCE DAILY VERSUS A LOWER DAILY DOSE OF THE DRUG.
