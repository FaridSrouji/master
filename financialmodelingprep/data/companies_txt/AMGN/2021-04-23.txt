OTEZLA® (apremilast) Significantly Improved Measures Of Disease Severity In Adults With Mild-To-Moderate Plaque Psoriasis. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., APRIL 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ:AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED OTEZLA® (APREMILAST) IMPROVED MEASURES OF DISEASE SEVERITY IN ADULTS WITH MILD-TO-MODERATE PLAQUE PSORIASIS REGARDLESS OF THEIR BODY SURFACE AREA (BSA) AFFECTED BY THE DISEASE, ACCORDING TO FINDINGS FROM THE PLACEBO-CONTROLLED, PHASE 3 ADVANCE TRIAL.
