FDA Approves LUMAKRAS™ (Sotorasib), The First And Only Targeted Treatment For Patients With KRAS G12C-Mutated Locally Advanced Or Metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., MAY 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED LUMAKRAS™ (SOTORASIB) FOR THE TREATMENT OF ADULT PATIENTS WITH KRAS G12C-MUTATED LOCALLY ADVANCED OR METASTATIC NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER (NSCLC), AS DETERMINED BY AN FDA-APPROVED TEST, WHO HAVE RECEIVED AT LEAST ONE PRIOR SYSTEMIC THERAPY.
