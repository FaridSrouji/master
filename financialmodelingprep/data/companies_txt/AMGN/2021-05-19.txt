Amgen To Showcase New Data From Oncology Portfolio At ASCO 2021. THOUSAND OAKS, CALIF., MAY 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMGEN (NASDAQ: AMGN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT NEW DATA FROM ITS EXPANDING ONCOLOGY PIPELINE AND MARKETED PORTFOLIO WILL BE PRESENTED DURING THE 2021 AMERICAN SOCIETY OF CLINICAL ONCOLOGY (ASCO) ANNUAL MEETING TAKING PLACE VIRTUALLY FROM JUNE 4-8, 2021.
