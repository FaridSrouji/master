Mccormick & Company Says In U.S., Co's Workers Who Are Required To Work On-Site Will Receive An Additional $2.00 Per Hour. MCCORMICK & COMPANY -  IN U.S., CO'S WORKERS WHO ARE REQUIRED TO WORK ON-SITE WILL RECEIVE AN ADDITIONAL $2.00 PER HOUR.MCCORMICK & COMPANY - WILL INCREASE HOURLY WAGES, FURTHER EXTEND PAID LEAVE TO CARE FOR SICK FAMILY MEMBERS.MCCORMICK & COMPANY  - WILL MAINTAIN SALARIES IF OPERATIONS ARE SUSPENDED.MCCORMICK & COMPANY - IF OPERATIONS ARE SUSPENDED, EMPLOYEES WILL RECEIVE 100% OF BASE PAY UP TO 12 WEEKS IN COORDINATION WITH ANY GOVERNMENT BENEFITS.
