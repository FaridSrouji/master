McCormick & Company, Incorporated Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fiscal Year of 2019. McCormick & Company, Incorporated provided earnings guidance for the fiscal year of 2019. For the period, the company expects to grow sales compared to 2018 by 1% to 2%, which in constant currency is a 3% to 4% projected growth rate. Operating income in 2019 is expected to grow 8% to 9% from $891 million of operating income in 2018. McCormick increased its projected 2019 earnings per share to be in the range of $5.20 to $5.25 compared to $7.00 of earnings per share in 2018.
