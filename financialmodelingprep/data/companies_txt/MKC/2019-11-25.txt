McCormick & Company, Incorporated Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on January 13, 2020. The Board of Directors of McCormick & Company, Incorporated declared an increase in the quarterly dividend from $0.57 to $0.62 per share on its common stocks, payable January 13, 2020 to shareholders of record December 31, 2019. This marks the 34th consecutive year that the Company has increased its quarterly dividend. At $0.62, the quarterly dividend is double the amount paid in 2012.
