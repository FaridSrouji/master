FDA Approves Labeling Update for Abbott's HeartMate 3 Heart Pump for use in Pediatric Patients. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., DEC. 17, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) HAS APPROVED UPDATED LABELING FOR THE COMPANY'S HEARTMATE 3™ HEART PUMP TO BE USED IN PEDIATRIC PATIENTS WITH ADVANCED REFRACTORY LEFT VENTRICULAR HEART FAILURE....
