ABBOTT PARK, ILL., FEB. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY COMMON DIVIDEND OF 45 CENTS PER SHARE.
