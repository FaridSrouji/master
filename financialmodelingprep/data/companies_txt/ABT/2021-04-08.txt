Abbott Receives CE Mark Approval for Next-Generation TriClip™ Device, Offering New Innovations for Tricuspid Heart Valve Repair. ABBOTT PARK, ILL., APRIL 8, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ABBOTT (NYSE: ABT) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED CE MARK FOR ITS NEXT-GENERATION TRICLIP™ TRANSCATHETER TRICUSPID VALVE REPAIR SYSTEM, THE FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND MINIMALLY INVASIVE TRICUSPID HEART VALVE REPAIR DEVICE AVAILABLE IN EUROPE TO TREAT TRICUSPID REGURGITATION (TR).
