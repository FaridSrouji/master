FDA Approves Less Invasive Surgical Approach For Abbott's Heart Pump To Help Patients Avoid Open Heart Surgery. FDA APPROVES LESS INVASIVE SURGICAL APPROACH FOR ABBOTT'S HEART PUMP TO HELP PATIENTS AVOID OPEN HEART SURGERY.ABBOTT - ANNOUNCED U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION APPROVAL OF A NEW ALTERNATIVE SURGICAL TECHNIQUE FOR ABBOTT'S HEARTMATE 3 HEART PUMP.ABBOTT - NEW SURGICAL APPROACH DESIGNED TO PROVIDE SURGEONS CHOICE IN SURGICAL METHOD FOR PATIENTS GETTING HEARTMATE 3 LEFT VENTRICULAR ASSIST DEVICE.
