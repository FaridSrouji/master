ANSYS, Inc. Announces Resignation of William R. Mcdermott from the Board of Directors. On October 28, 2019, William R. McDermott provided ANSYS, Inc. notice of his resignation from the Board of Directors effective October 31, 2019 due to his assumption of the position of President and Chief Executive Officer of ServiceNow, Inc. In connection with Mr. McDermott's resignation, the Board approved the reduction in the size of the Board from eight to seven directors and the number of Class III directors from three to two directors.
