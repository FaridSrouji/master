PNC Announces Redemption Of 2.150 Percent Senior Bank Notes Due April 29, 2021. PITTSBURGH, MARCH 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE REDEMPTION ON MARCH 30, 2021, OF ALL OF THE OUTSTANDING SENIOR NOTES DUE APRIL 29, 2021, ISSUED BY PNC BANK, NATIONAL ASSOCIATION IN THE AMOUNT OF $1,250,000,000 (CUSIP 69353REW4). THE SECURITIES HAVE A DISTRIBUTION RATE OF 2.150 PERCENT AND AN ORIGINAL SCHEDULED MATURITY DATE OF APRIL 29, 2021.
