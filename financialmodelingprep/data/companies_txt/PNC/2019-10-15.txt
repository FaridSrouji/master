The PNC Financial Services Group, Inc. Reports Net Charge-Offs for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. The PNC Financial Services Group, Inc. reported net charge-offs for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, the company reported net charge-offs were $128 million for the third quarter of 2019 compared with $120 million in the second quarter and $96 million in the third quarter of 2018.
