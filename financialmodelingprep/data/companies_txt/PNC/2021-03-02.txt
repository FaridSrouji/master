Harris Williams Advises International Sports Sciences Association (ISSA) on its Sale to Tailwind Capital. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED INTERNATIONAL SPORTS SCIENCES ASSOCIATION (ISSA), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF THE BERKS GROUP, ON ITS SALE TO TAILWIND CAPITAL (TAILWIND). ISSA IS A PIONEER IN ELEARNING AND CERTIFICATION FOR THE FITNESS AND WELLNESS COMMUNITY. THE TRANSACTION WAS LED BY SAM HENDLER, ANDY LEED AND BEN DE FIEBRE OF THE HARRIS WILLIAMS TECHNOLOGY GROUP AND ZACH ENGLAND AND CHRISTOPH
