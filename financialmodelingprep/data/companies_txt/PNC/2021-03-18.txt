Harris Williams Advises Rohrer Corporation on its Sale to Wellspring Capital Management. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED ROHRER CORPORATION (ROHRER), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF SHOREVIEW INDUSTRIES (SHOREVIEW), ON ITS SALE TO WELLSPRING CAPITAL MANAGEMENT (WELLSPRING). ROHRER IS A LEADING MANUFACTURER OF HIGH-VISIBILITY CONSUMER PACKAGING SOLUTIONS IN NORTH AMERICA.
