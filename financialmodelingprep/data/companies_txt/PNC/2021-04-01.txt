PNC Declares Dividend Of $1.15 On Common Stock. PITTSBURGH, APRIL 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP, INC. (NYSE: PNC) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON THE COMMON STOCK OF $1.15 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAYABLE MAY 5, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS APRIL 16, 2021.
