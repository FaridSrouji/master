Harris Williams Advises ArchKey Solutions on its Significant Investment from One Rock Capital Partners. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED ARCHKEY SOLUTIONS (ARCHKEY), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF FUNDS MANAGED BY OAKTREE CAPITAL MANAGEMENT, L.P. (OAKTREE), ON ITS SIGNIFICANT INVESTMENT FROM AN AFFILIATE OF ONE ROCK CAPITAL PARTNERS, LLC (ONE ROCK). ARCHKEY IS A PREMIER ELECTRICAL AND TECHNOLOGIES CONTRACTING AND SERVICES PROVIDER.
