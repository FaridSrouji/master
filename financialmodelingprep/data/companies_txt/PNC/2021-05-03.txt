Harris Williams Advises Velocity Clinical Research on its Sale to GHO Capital Partners LLP. RICHMOND, VA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HARRIS WILLIAMS, A GLOBAL INVESTMENT BANK SPECIALIZING IN M&A ADVISORY SERVICES, ANNOUNCES IT ADVISED VELOCITY CLINICAL RESEARCH (VELOCITY), A PORTFOLIO COMPANY OF NAVIMED CAPITAL (NAVIMED), ON ITS SALE TO GLOBAL HEALTHCARE OPPORTUNITIES, OR GHO CAPITAL PARTNERS LLP (GHO). VELOCITY IS A LEADING INTEGRATED SITE ORGANIZATION FOR CLINICAL TRIALS. THE TRANSACTION WAS LED BY PAUL HEPPER, NICK OWENS, LUCAS SCHOLL, KATIE BOWDEN AND JONATHAN FERRELL OF THE HARRIS WIL
