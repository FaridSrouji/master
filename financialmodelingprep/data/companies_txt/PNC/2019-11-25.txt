PNC Financial Services Corp. Chooses Denver for 5th Niche Office. PNC Financial Services Corp. has chosen Denver as its fifth office location for its debt capital markets team. The debt capital markets team supports PNC Bank's corporate banking team, Beiser said. More specifically, team members assist PNC Bank clients with loan syndication, public fixed-income origination, private placements, bridge loan financing, secondary loan trading and agency services. The Denver debt capital markets team will service the western portion of the U.S. along with Texas. PNC Bank's other debt capital markets teams are located in Pittsburgh, Chicago, Philadelphia and Charlotte. Ralph Phillips, who helped launch PNC Bank's Charlotte debt capital markets team back in 2012, has been selected as the managing director of Denver's debt capital markets office.
