CBRE Group, Inc. Announces Pricing of $500 Million of 2.500% Senior Notes Due 2031. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CBRE GROUP, INC. (NYSE:CBRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF THE OFFERING OF $500 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF 2.500% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2031 (THE “NOTES”). THE NOTES WILL HAVE AN INTEREST RATE OF 2.500% PER ANNUM AND ARE BEING ISSUED AT A PRICE EQUAL TO 98.451% OF THEIR FACE VALUE.
