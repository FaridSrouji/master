CBRE Group, Inc. Acquires 35 Percent Interest in Industrious, Leading Provider of Flexible Space Solutions. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CBRE GROUP, INC. (NYSE:CBRE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACQUISITION OF A 35 PERCENT INTEREST IN INDUSTRIOUS, A LEADING PROVIDER OF PREMIUM FLEXIBLE WORKPLACE SOLUTIONS IN THE U.S. CBRE IS NOW INDUSTRIOUS' LARGEST SHAREHOLDER. IN ADDITION, CBRE IS EXPECTED TO ACQUIRE AN ADDITIONAL 5 PERCENT OF INDUSTRIOUS IN THE COMING WEEKS, WHICH WOULD RESULT IN A 40 PERCENT TOTAL STAKE.
