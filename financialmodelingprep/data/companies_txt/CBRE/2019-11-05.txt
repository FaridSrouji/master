Cbre Group, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. CBRE Group, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced operating income was USD 316.630 million compared to USD 189.717 million a year ago. Net income was USD 256.599 million compared to USD 290.469 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.76 compared to USD 0.86 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.75 compared to USD 0.85 a year ago. For the nine months, operating income was USD 746.034 million compared to USD 628.642 million a year ago. Net income was USD 644.739 million compared to USD 669.424 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.92 compared to USD 1.97 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.89 compared to USD 1.95 a year ago.
