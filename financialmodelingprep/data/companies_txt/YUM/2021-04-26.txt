Yum! Brands Announces Approved Science-Based Targets to Address Climate Change with Goal to Reduce Greenhouse Gas Emissions Nearly 50% by 2030. LOUISVILLE, KY.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--YUM! ANNOUNCED IT WILL DECREASE GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS 46% BY 2030 WORKING WITH ITS FRANCHISEES, SUPPLIERS AND PRODUCERS TO ADDRESS CLIMATE CHANGE.
