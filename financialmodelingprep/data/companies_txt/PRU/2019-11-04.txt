ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.Q3 ADJUSTED OPERATING EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.22.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $3.44.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $3.07 - REFINITIV IBES DATA.BOOK VALUE PER COMMON SHARE OF $163.19 AT QUARTER-END VERSUS $110.78 PER SHARE FOR YEAR-AGO QUARTER.QUARTER-END ASSETS UNDER MANAGEMENT AMOUNTED TO $1.519 TRILLION VERSUS $1.410 TRILLION FOR YEAR-AGO QUARTER.
Prudential Financial, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Prudential Financial, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced net income was USD 1,425 million compared to USD 1,675 million a year ago. For the nine months, net income was USD 3,100 million compared to USD 3,239 million a year ago.
