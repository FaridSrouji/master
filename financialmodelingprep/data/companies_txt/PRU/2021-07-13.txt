PGIM strengthens alternatives offering with agreement to acquire Montana Capital Partners. NEWARK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PGIM, THE $1.5 TRILLION GLOBAL INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT BUSINESS OF PRUDENTIAL FINANCIAL, INC. (NYSE: PRU), HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE MONTANA CAPITAL PARTNERS (MCP), A EUROPEAN-BASED PRIVATE EQUITY SECONDARIES ASSET MANAGER. THE CLOSING OF THE TRANSACTION IS SUBJECT TO RECEIPT OF ALL REGULATORY APPROVALS. UPON CLOSING, EXPECTED IN Q3 2021, MCP WILL JOIN PGIM'S MULTI-MANAGER STRUCTURE UNDER THE OVERSIGHT OF ERIC ADLER, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE
