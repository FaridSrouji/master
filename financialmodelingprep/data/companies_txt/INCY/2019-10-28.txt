Incyte Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Incyte Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 551.581 million compared to USD 449.683 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 134.316 million compared to USD 31.119 million a year ago. Net income was USD 128.271 million compared to USD 29.176 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.6 compared to USD 0.14 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.59 compared to USD 0.14 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 1,579.370 million compared to USD 1,353.481 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 306.998 million compared to USD 47.248 million a year ago. Net income was USD 335.901 million compared to USD 40.430 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.57 compared to USD 0.19 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.55 compared to USD 0.19 a year ago.
