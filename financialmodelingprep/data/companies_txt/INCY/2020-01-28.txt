Incyte Says Overall Efficacy And Safety Profile Of Ruxolitinib Cream Is Consistent With Previous Data. INCYTE ANNOUNCES THAT THE TRUE-AD2 PIVOTAL TRIAL OF RUXOLITINIB CREAM MET ITS PRIMARY ENDPOINT IN PATIENTS WITH ATOPIC DERMATITIS.INCYTE CORP - OVERALL EFFICACY AND SAFETY PROFILE OF RUXOLITINIB CREAM IS CONSISTENT WITH PREVIOUS DATA, AND NO NEW SAFETY SIGNALS WERE OBSERVED.INCYTE CORP - LONG-TERM SAFETY PORTION OF STUDY WILL CONTINUE AS PLANNED..
