Incyte Says Phase 3 Data From True-Ad Program Of Ruxolitinib Cream Support Planned Submission Of NDA To US FDA In 2020. FIRST PRESENTATION OF PHASE 3 DATA FROM TRUE-AD PROGRAM OF RUXOLITINIB CREAM AT REVOLUTIONIZING ATOPIC DERMATITIS VIRTUAL SYMPOSIUM.DATA SUPPORT PLANNED SUBMISSION OF A NEW DRUG APPLICATION (NDA) TO U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION BEFORE END OF 2020.RUXOLITINIB CREAM RESULTED IN RAPID, ROBUST CLINICAL RESPONSE, WITH SIGNIFICANTLY MORE PATIENTS ACHIEVING IGA TREATMENT SUCCESS.
