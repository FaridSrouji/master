Lilly and Incyte's Atopic Dermatitis Therapy Succeds In Late-Stage Trial. LILLY AND INCYTE ANNOUNCE POSITIVE TOP-LINE RESULTS FROM THE NORTH AMERICAN (BREEZE-AD5) PHASE 3 STUDY OF ORAL SELECTIVE JAK INHIBITOR BARICITINIB IN PATIENTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE ATOPIC DERMATITIS.INCYTE CORP - STUDY MET PRIMARY ENDPOINT.INCYTE CORP - STUDY'S SAFETY PROFILE WAS CONSISTENT WITH KNOWN SAFETY FINDINGS OF BARICITINIB IN ATOPIC DERMATITIS.INCYTE CORP - NO VENOUS THROMBOEMBOLIC EVENTS OR DEATHS WERE REPORTED IN TRIAL.INCYTE CORP - LILLY PLANS TO SUBMIT BARICITINIB AS A TREATMENT FOR PATIENTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE AD FOR APPROVAL IN U.S. & JAPAN IN 2020.INCYTE CORP - MOST COMMON TREATMENT-EMERGENT ADVERSE EVENTS INCLUDED UPPER RESPIRATORY TRACT INFECTIONS, NASOPHARYNGITIS, & DIARRHEA.
