Incyte Announces First Patient Treated In Phase 3 Clinical Trial Program Evaluating Ruxolitinib Cream In Patients With Vitiligo. INCYTE ANNOUNCES FIRST PATIENT TREATED IN PHASE 3 CLINICAL TRIAL PROGRAM EVALUATING RUXOLITINIB CREAM IN PATIENTS WITH VITILIGO.INCYTE - TRUE-V CLINICAL TRIAL PROGRAM INCLUDES 2 PHASE 3 STUDIES, TRUE-V1 AND TRUE-V2, EVALUATING SAFETY AND EFFICACY OF RUXOLITINIB CREAM IN PATIENTS WITH VITILIGO.INCYTE CORP - RUXOLITINIB CREAM IS CURRENTLY IN PHASE 3 DEVELOPMENT WITH INITIAL RESULTS EXPECTED IN FIRST HALF OF 2020.
