Emerald City meets Queen City: Alaska Airlines connects Seattle and Cincinnati. SEATTLE, MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS TRAVELERS TAKE TO THE SKIES AGAIN, ALASKA AIRLINES IS READY TO PROVIDE ITS GUESTS WITH A BRAND-NEW DESTINATION: CINCINNATI. DAILY NONSTOP SERVICE BETWEEN SEATTLE AND CINCINNATI/NORTHERN KENTUCKY INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT (CVG) STARTS TODAY, MAY 20.
