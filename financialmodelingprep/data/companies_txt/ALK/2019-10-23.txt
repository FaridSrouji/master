Alaska Air Group, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Alaska Air Group, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 2,389 million compared to USD 2,212 million a year ago. Net income was USD 322 million compared to USD 217 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.61 compared to USD 1.76 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.6 compared to USD 1.75 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 6,553 million compared to USD 6,200 million a year ago. Net income was USD 588 million compared to USD 414 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 4.76 compared to USD 3.36 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.74 compared to USD 3.34 a year ago.
