Alaska Air Group, Inc. Announces Consolidated Operating Results for the Month and Year to Date Ended October 2019. Alaska Air Group, Inc. announced consolidated operating results for the month and year to date ended October 2019. For the month, the company reported revenue passengers of 3,913,000 compared to 3,744,000 a year ago. Revenue passenger miles were 4,626,000,000 compared to 4,472,000,000 a year ago. Available seat miles were 5,528,000,000 compared to 5,337,000,000 a year ago. Passenger load factor was 83.7% compared to 83.8% a year ago. For the year to date, the company reported revenue passengers of 38,931,000 compared to 38,429,000 a year ago. Revenue passenger miles were 46,739,000,000 compared to 45,745,000,000 a year ago. Available seat miles were 55,533,000,000 compared to 54,593,000,000 a year ago. Passenger load factor was 84.2% compared to 83.8% a year ago.
