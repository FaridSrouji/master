Alaska Airlines adds new nonstop from Anchorage to Minneapolis-St. Paul. SEATTLE, MARCH 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES ANNOUNCES TODAY NEW SEASONAL SERVICE AT ONE OF THE AIRLINE'S KEY HUBS IN ALASKA WITH NEW NONSTOP SERVICE BETWEEN ANCHORAGE AND MINNEAPOLIS-ST. PAUL. THIS ADDITIONAL NONSTOP FLIGHT MEANS ALASKA AIRLINES WILL PROVIDE THE MOST NONSTOPS FROM THE STATE OF ALASKA THAN ANY OTHER CARRIER.
