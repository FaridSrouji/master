Alaska Airlines commits to carbon, waste and water goals for 2025, announces path to net zero by 2040. SEATTLE, APRIL 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS COMMITMENT AND ROADMAP TO REDUCE THE COMPANY'S CARBON EMISSIONS TO NET-ZERO BY 2040, AND COMMITMENTS ACROSS CARBON, WASTE, AND WATER IMPACTS BY 2025. TODAY'S ANNOUNCEMENT INCLUDED THE RELEASE OF ALASKA'S 2020 LIFT SUSTAINABILITY REPORT DETAILING THE COMPANY'S BROADER CLIMATE CHANGE STRATEGY, INCLUDING WASTE AND WATER INITIATIVES.
