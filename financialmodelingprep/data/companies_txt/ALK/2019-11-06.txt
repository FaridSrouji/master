Alaska Air Group, Inc. Declares Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 5, 2019. Alaska Air Group, Inc. has declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of 35 cents per share. The dividend will be paid on December 5, 2019, to all shareholders of record as of Nov. 19, 2019. Dividends are financed from operating cash flow and cash on hand.
