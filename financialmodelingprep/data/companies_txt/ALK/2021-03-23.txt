Alaska Airlines Board of Directors elects new inflight vice president. SEATTLE, MARCH 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALASKA AIRLINES ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TRAVIS GELBRICH WILL SERVE AS THE AIRLINE'S NEW VICE PRESIDENT OF INFLIGHT. IN HIS NEW ROLE, GELBRICH WILL PROVIDE STRATEGIC AND OPERATIONAL LEADERSHIP TO THE INFLIGHT DIVISION AND OVERSEE ALASKA'S 5,600 FLIGHT ATTENDANTS.
