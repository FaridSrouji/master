Alaska Air Says As Of March 13, Including Borrowings From Credit Facilities, Had Unrestricted Cash, Short-Term Investments Of About $1.97 Bln. ALASKA AIR GROUP - AS OF MARCH 13, INCLUDING BORROWINGS FROM CREDIT FACILITIES, HAD UNRESTRICTED CASH, SHORT-TERM INVESTMENTS OF ABOUT $1.97 BILLION .ALASKA AIR GROUP INC - BORROWED $388 MILLION FROM EXISTING CREDIT FACILITIES.ALASKA AIR GROUP INC - $238 MILLION WAS DRAWN FROM A CREDIT FACILITY WHICH IS SECURED BY AIRCRAFT AND EXPIRES IN JUNE 2021.ALASKA AIR GROUP INC - $150 MILLION WAS DRAWN FROM A SECOND FACILITY AND EXPIRES IN MARCH 2022.ALASKA AIR GROUP - AIR GROUP ALSO HAS 133 UNENCUMBERED AIRCRAFT THAT COULD BE FINANCED, IF NECESSARY, WHICH COULD GENERATE ABOUT $2.5 BILLION.
