Celanese Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividend of $0.68 Per Share. DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $CE--CELANESE CORPORATION (NYSE: CE), A GLOBAL CHEMICAL AND SPECIALTY MATERIALS COMPANY, TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.68 PER SHARE ON ITS COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE MAY 10, 2021. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE TO STOCKHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF APRIL 26, 2021. ABOUT CELANESE CELANESE CORPORATION IS A GLOBAL CHEMICAL LEADER IN THE PRODUCTION OF DIFFERENTIATED CHEMISTRY SOLUTIONS AND SPECIALTY MATERIALS USED IN MOST MAJOR INDUSTRIES AND CONSUMER APPLICATIONS.
