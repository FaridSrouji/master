MSCI Inc. Completes Private Offering of $500 Million 4.000% Senior Notes Due 2029. MSCI Inc. announced that it has successfully completed its private offering of $500 million aggregate amount of its 4.000% senior notes due 2029. The notes will mature on November 15, 2029. MSCI intends to use the net proceeds from the Offering for general corporate purposes, including, without limitation, buybacks of its common stock and potential acquisitions.
