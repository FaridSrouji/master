Singapore Exchange To Launch 10 Singapore Single Stock Futures On 15 June. SINGAPORE EXCHANGE LTD: :WILL LAUNCH 10 SINGAPORE SINGLE STOCK FUTURES (SSFS) ON 15 JUNE 2020.SIGNS LICENCE AGREEMENT WITH MSCI FOR SINGAPORE INDEX PRODUCTS.SIGNED LICENCE AGREEMENT FOR 4 PRODUCTS ON MSCI SINGAPORE INDICES, INCLUDING SIMSCI FUTURES, OPTIONS & NET TOTAL RETURN CONTRACTS.LICENCE AGREEMENT FOR 4 PRODUCTS WILL CONTINUE TO BE LISTED ON SGX AFTER FEBRUARY 2021.
