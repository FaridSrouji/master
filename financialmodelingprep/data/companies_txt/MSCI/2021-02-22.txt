MSCI to Launch Investment Solutions as a Service in Collaboration with Microsoft. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--MSCI INC. (NYSE: MSCI), A LEADING PROVIDER OF INVESTMENT DECISION SUPPORT TOOLS AND SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE DEVELOPMENT OF INVESTMENT SOLUTIONS AS A SERVICE FOR THE GLOBAL INVESTMENT INDUSTRY IN COLLABORATION WITH MICROSOFT CORP. THE NEW SERVICE WILL ALLOW INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS TO USE NEXT GENERATION TECHNOLOGIES, ADVANCED ANALYTICS AND BIG DATA TO HELP ANTICIPATE AND ADDRESS KEY STRATEGIC AND INVESTMENT CHALLENGES. BUILT TO ALIGN LONG-TERM TECHNOLOGY AND DATA
