Valero Energy Corporation Declares Regular Cash Dividend on Common Stock. SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VALERO ENERGY CORPORATION (NYSE: VLO, “VALERO”) HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COMMON STOCK OF $0.98 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 8, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 17, 2021. ABOUT VALERO VALERO ENERGY CORPORATION, THROUGH ITS SUBSIDIARIES (COLLECTIVELY, “VALERO”), IS AN INTERNATIONAL MANUFACTURER AND MARKETER OF TRANSPORTATION FUELS AND PETROCHEMICAL PRODUCTS.
