the Cooper Companies, Inc. Enters into an Amendment No. 1 to Loan Agreement. On September 27, 2019, The Cooper Companies, Inc. entered into an Amendment No. 1 to Loan Agreement (the “Amendment to 2018 Loan Agreement”), among the Company, the lenders party thereto, and PNC Bank, National Association (“PNC Bank”), as administrative agent, to amend the Loan Agreement, dated as of November 1, 2018 (the “2018 Loan Agreement”), by and among the Company, the lenders from time to time party thereto, and each of PNC Capital Markets LLC and Merrill Lynch, Pierce, Fenner & Smith Incorporated, as a joint lead arranger and a joint bookrunner, Bank of America, N.A. as syndication agent, and PNC Bank, as administrative agent. The Amendment to 2018 Loan Agreement modifies certain provisions of the 2018 Loan Agreement to, among other things, extend the maturity date for the term loan balance to September 25, 2020 and increase the aggregate principal amount of the term loan facility from an original amount of $400 million to $500 million.
