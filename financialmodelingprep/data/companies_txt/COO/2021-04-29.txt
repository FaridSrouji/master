CooperCompanies Announces Release Date for Second Quarter 2021. SAN RAMON, CALIF., APRIL 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COOPERCOMPANIES (NYSE: COO), TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL RELEASE SECOND QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS ON THURSDAY, JUNE 3, 2021, AT 4:15 PM ET. FOLLOWING THE RELEASE, THE COMPANY WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL AT 5:00 PM ET TO DISCUSS THE RESULTS AND CURRENT CORPORATE DEVELOPMENTS.
