Host Hotels & Resorts, Inc. Acquires Four Seasons Resort Orlando at Walt Disney World® Resort for $610 million. BETHESDA, MD., MAY 04, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- HOST HOTELS & RESORTS, INC. (NASDAQ: HST) (THE “COMPANY”), THE NATION'S LARGEST LODGING REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST (“REIT”), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED THE FEE SIMPLE INTEREST IN THE 444-ROOM FOUR SEASONS RESORT ORLANDO AT WALT DISNEY WORLD® RESORT FOR APPROXIMATELY $610 MILLION IN CASH.
