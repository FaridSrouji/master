Illumina Reports Q3 Non-GAAP EPS Of $1.93. ILLUMINA REPORTS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2019.Q3 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.93.Q3 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.58.Q3 REVENUE $907 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $870.8 MILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.40 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES FY 2019 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $6.40 TO $6.45.SEES FY 2019 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $6.55 TO $6.60.SEES FY 2019 REVENUE UP ABOUT 6 PERCENT.FY2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $6.04, REVENUE VIEW $3.53 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.EXTENDED MERGER AGREEMENT WITH PACIFIC BIOSCIENCES TO DECEMBER 31, 2019 WITH AN OPTION TO EXTEND DEADLINE TO MARCH 31, 2020.
UK CMA's Provisional Conclusion Is Illumina/PacBio Deal To Result In Loss Of Competition. MERGER UPDATE: ILLUMINA/PACBIO PROVISIONAL FINDING.PROVISIONAL CONCLUSION IS ILLUMINA/PACBIO DEAL WILL RESULT IN SIGNIFICANT LOSS OF COMPETITION BETWEEN 2 COS.CONCERNED THAT LOSS OF PACBIO AS AN INDEPENDENT COMPETITOR WILL LEAD TO A REDUCTION IN OVERALL LEVELS OF INNOVATION IN MARKET.
