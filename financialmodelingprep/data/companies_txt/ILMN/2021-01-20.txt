Illumina Wins Patent Infringement Suit against BGI in the UK. SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLUMINA, INC. (NASDAQ: ILMN) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE HIGH COURT OF JUSTICE, CHANCERY DIVISION, PATENTS COURT, ISSUED A JUDGMENT IN ITS FAVOR IN THE PATENT INFRINGEMENT SUIT FILED AGAINST THE BGI COMPANIES, MGI TECH CO. LTD; LATVIA MGI SIA; MGI INTERNATIONAL SALES CO., LTD; AND BGI COMPLETE GENOMICS HONG KONG CO., LTD. THE COURT FOUND THAT FOUR OF THE FIVE ASSERTED PATENTS WERE VALID AND INFRINGED BY BGI.
