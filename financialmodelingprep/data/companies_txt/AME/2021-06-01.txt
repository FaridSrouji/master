Stewart Douglas Elected Vice President and Chief Information Officer. BERWYN, PA., JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMETEK, INC. (NYSE: AME) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS ELECTED STEWART DOUGLAS AS VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION OFFICER, FOLLOWING THE RETIREMENT OF KEN WEIRMAN WHO HELD THE SAME POSITION SINCE JOINING AMETEK IN 2010.
