38% Of Workers Say Pandemic Contributed To A Career Setback, Robert Half Survey Finds. MENLO PARK, CALIF., MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ACCORDING TO NEW RESEARCH FROM GLOBAL STAFFING FIRM ROBERT HALF, 38% OF PROFESSIONALS SAID THEIR CAREER HAS STALLED SINCE THE START OF THE PANDEMIC — AND THAT NUMBER JUMPS TO 66% FOR THOSE AGES 18 TO 24.
