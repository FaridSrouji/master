Protiviti Launches 'Birdies for Meals' Campaign with Professional Golfers Matt Fitzpatrick and Jennifer Kupcho to Fight Hunger. MENLO PARK, CALIF., APRIL 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PROTIVITI WILL DONATE UP TO 100,000 MEALS TO NON-PROFIT FEEDING CHILDREN EVERYWHERE BASED ON THE TOTAL NUMBER OF BIRDIES MADE BY GOLF PROS MATT FITZPATRICK AND JENNIFER KUPCHO DURING THE 2021 MAJOR CHAMPIONSHIPS.
