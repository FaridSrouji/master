Protiviti Selects Ohio as Location for New Americas Delivery Center. MENLO PARK, CALIF., JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBAL CONSULTING FIRM PROTIVITI IS EXCITED TO ANNOUNCE IT WILL LAUNCH ITS NEW AMERICAS DELIVERY CENTER (ADC) IN OHIO.
