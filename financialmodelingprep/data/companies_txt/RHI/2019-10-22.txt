Robert Half International Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of Fiscal 2019. Robert Half International Inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2019. The company offers the following fourth quarter guidance: $1.500 billion to $1.565 billion; income per share $0.94 to $1. The midpoint of the company's guidance implies year-over-year revenue growth of 4% on a same-day as adjusted basis, including Protiviti and EPS growth of 2%.
