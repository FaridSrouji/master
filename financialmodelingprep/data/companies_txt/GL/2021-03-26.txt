Globe Life Inc. Declares Dividend. MCKINNEY, TEXAS, MARCH 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- GLOBE LIFE INC. (NYSE: GL) ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS RAISED THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND TO $.1975 PER SHARE ON ALL OF THE OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK OF THE COMPANY HELD OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS OF THE COMPANY'S TRANSFER AGENT ON APRIL 5, 2021.  THE DIVIDEND WILL BE PAID ON APRIL 30, 2021.
