CME Group Reports Q2 and June 2021 Monthly Market Statistics. CHICAGO, JULY 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CME GROUP, THE WORLD'S LEADING AND MOST DIVERSE DERIVATIVES MARKETPLACE, TODAY REPORTED ITS Q2 AND JUNE 2021 MARKET STATISTICS, SHOWING IT REACHED AVERAGE DAILY VOLUME (ADV) OF 18.4 MILLION CONTRACTS FOR BOTH THE SECOND QUARTER AND THE MONTH OF JUNE. MARKET STATISTICS ARE AVAILABLE IN GREATER DETAIL AT HTTPS://CMEGROUPINC.GCS-WEB.COM/MONTHLY-VOLUME.
