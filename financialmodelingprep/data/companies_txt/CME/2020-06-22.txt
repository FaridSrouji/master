CME: NYMEX Delists Certain Contract Months For NY Fuel Oil 3% Futures. SAYS NYMEX DELISTED CERTAIN PREVIOUSLY-LISTED CONTRACT MONTHS FOR THREE NY FUEL OIL 3% FUTURES CONTRACTS.SAYS THERE IS NO OPEN INTEREST IN THE DELISTED CONTRACT MONTHS.SAYS EXCHANGE WILL PERMANENTLY DELIST THE CONTRACTS FOLLOWING THE TERMINATION OF TRADING OF THE DEC. 2020 CONTRACT MONTH WHICH WILL OCCUR ON JAN. 11, 2021.THE CONTRACTS ARE NY 3.0% FUEL OIL (PLATTS) FUTURES, NY 3.0% FUEL OIL (PLATTS) VS. GULF COAST HSFO (PLATTS) FUTURES AND NY 3.0% FUEL OIL (PLATTS) BALMO FUTURES.
CME and B3 to Jointly Develop New Soybean Futures Contracts. SAYS TO JOINTLY DEVELOP NEW SOYBEAN FUTURES CONTRACTS WITH B3 S.A. <<<B3SA3.SA>>> CONNECTING GLOBAL PARTICIPANTS TO BRAZILIAN AGRICULTURAL MARKETS.SAYS UNDER THE TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT, CME GROUP AND B3 WILL WORK TOGETHER TO LAUNCH FUTURES ON BRAZILIAN SOYBEANS IN Q3 2020, PENDING REGULATORY APPROVALS.SAYS THE NEW, EXPANDED AGREEMENT ALSO INCLUDES THE EXTENSION OF THE EXISTING B3 CROSS-LISTED MINI-SOYBEAN FUTURES AND OPTIONS CONTRACTS.SAYS THE AGREEMENT WILL ALLOW THE COMPANIES TO EVALUATE POTENTIAL PRODUCTS RELATED TO SOUTH AMERICAN SOYBEAN AND SOYBEAN-RELATED PRODUCTS.
