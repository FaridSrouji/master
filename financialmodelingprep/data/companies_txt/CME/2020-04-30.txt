CME Group Says Unit Of Co Entered Into An Amendment To Its 364-Day Multi-Currency Credit Facility. CME GROUP INC - ON APRIL 29, UNIT OF CO ENTERED INTO AN AMENDMENT TO ITS 364-DAY MULTI-CURRENCY CREDIT FACILITY.CME GROUP INC - AMENDED CREDIT FACILITY IS FOR A MULTI-CURRENCY REVOLVING SECURED CREDIT FACILITY OF $7 BILLION.CME GROUP - AMENDED CREDIT AGREEMENT IS INTENDED TO PROVIDE TEMPORARY LIQUIDITY TO CME IN EVENT OF CLEARING MEMBER DEFAULT, LIQUIDITY CONSTRAINT.CME GROUP INC - AMENDED CREDIT AGRREMENT IS INTENDED TO PROVIDE TEMPORARY LIQUIDITY TO CME IN EVENT OF DELAY IN PAYMENT SYSTEMS UTILIZED BY CME.
