Cme Group Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. CME Group Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 1,277.3 million compared to USD 904.2 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 685.2 million compared to USD 549.9 million a year ago. Net income was USD 636.3 million compared to USD 411.8 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.78 compared to USD 1.21 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 3,729.6 million compared to USD 3,072.8 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,014.8 million compared to USD 1,957.7 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,647 million compared to USD 1,576.7 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 4.61 compared to USD 4.64 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 4.6 compared to USD 4.62 a year ago.
