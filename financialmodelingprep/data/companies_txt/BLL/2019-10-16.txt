Ball Corp. Announces to Build Plant in Rome to Make Aluminum Cups. Ball Corp. announced to build a plant in Rome to make aluminum cups. The company said its new plant is meant to serve a growing market for more environmentally friendly beverage packaging. Roughly 75% of the aluminum ever produced is still in use on October 17, 2019. The Rome plant will make aluminum cups in a range of sizes. The new plant, which will be operating by the fourth quarter of 2020, will sit next to the company's existing plant.
