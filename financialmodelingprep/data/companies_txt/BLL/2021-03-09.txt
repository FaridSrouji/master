Ball Aerospace Completes Critical Design Review of Roman Space Telescope Instrument. BROOMFIELD, COLO., MARCH 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL AEROSPACE, PARTNERED WITH NASA GODDARD SPACE FLIGHT CENTER, SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED THE CRITICAL DESIGN REVIEW OF THE WIDE FIELD INSTRUMENT (WFI), WHICH WILL BE THE PRIMARY SCIENCE INSTRUMENT ON NASA'S NANCY GRACE ROMAN SPACE TELESCOPE, FORMERLY KNOWN AS THE WIDE FIELD INFRARED SURVEY TELESCOPE (WFIRST).
