Ball Reports Strong First Quarter 2021 Results. WESTMINSTER, COLO., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BALL CORPORATION (NYSE: BLL) TODAY REPORTED, ON A U.S. GAAP BASIS, FIRST QUARTER 2021 NET EARNINGS ATTRIBUTABLE TO THE CORPORATION OF $200 MILLION (INCLUDING NET AFTER-TAX CHARGES OF $40 MILLION, OR 12 CENTS PER DILUTED SHARE FOR BUSINESS CONSOLIDATION AND OTHER NON-COMPARABLE ITEMS), OR 60 CENTS PER DILUTED SHARE, ON SALES OF $3.1 BILLION, COMPARED TO $23 MILLION NET EARNINGS ATTRIBUTABLE TO THE CORPORATION, OR 7 CENTS PER DILUTED SHARE (INCLUDING NET AFTER-TAX CHARGES OF $179 MILLION, OR 54 CENTS PER DILUTED SHARE FOR BUSINESS CONSOLIDATION AND OTHER NON-COMPARABLE ITEMS), ON SALES OF $2.8 BILLION IN 2020.
