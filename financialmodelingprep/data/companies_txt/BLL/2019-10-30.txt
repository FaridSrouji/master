Ball Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Ball Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,953 million compared to USD 2,946 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 198 million compared to USD 268 million a year ago. Net income was USD 92 million compared to USD 59 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.28 compared to USD 0.17 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.27 compared to USD 0.17 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 8,755 million compared to USD 8,832 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 726 million compared to USD 737 million a year ago. Net income was USD 406 million compared to USD 303 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.22 compared to USD 0.87 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.19 compared to USD 0.86 a year ago.
