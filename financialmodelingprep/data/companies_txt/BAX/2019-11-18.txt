Baxter International Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on January 02, 2020. Baxter International Inc. announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.22 per share of common stock. The dividend is payable on January 2, 2020, to shareholders of record as of December 6, 2019. The indicated annual dividend rate is $0.88 per share of common stock.
