Baxter Launches Initiative to Increase Awareness and Education in Black Communities About Kidney Health and Managing Chronic Kidney Disease. DEERFIELD, ILL. & WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BAXTER INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE:BAX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL PRODUCTS COMPANY, AND THE CENTRAL AREA OF THE LINKS, INCORPORATED, AN INTERNATIONAL NOT-FOR-PROFIT COMPRISED OF 16,000 WOMEN OF COLOR, ANNOUNCE A NEW PARTNERSHIP TO BRING AWARENESS AND RESOURCES TO THE DISPROPORTIONATE CHALLENGES AFFECTING BLACK AMERICANS RELATED TO KIDNEY HEALTH.
