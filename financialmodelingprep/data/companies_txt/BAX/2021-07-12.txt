Baxter Declares Quarterly Dividend. DEERFIELD, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BAXTER INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE:BAX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL PRODUCTS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.28 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON OCTOBER 1, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF SEPTEMBER 3, 2021. THE INDICATED ANNUAL DIVIDEND RATE IS $1.12 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK.
