Pennsylvania American Water Completes Acquisition of Royersford Borough Wastewater System. MECHANICSBURG, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #ACQUISITION--PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ACQUIRED THE WASTEWATER SYSTEM ASSETS OF ROYERSFORD BOROUGH IN MONTGOMERY COUNTY.
American Water Honored as a VETS Indexes 3 Star Employer. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE NATION'S LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT HAS EARNED THE DESIGNATION AS A VETS INDEXES 3 STAR EMPLOYER. “AMERICAN WATER IS PROUD TO BE RECOGNIZED BY VETS INDEXES FOR OUR FOCUS ON VETERANS AS WELL AS INCLUSION AND DIVERSITY. AS A MILITARY VETERAN, I BELIEVE IT IS ESSENTIAL WE SUPPORT THE MEN AND WOMEN WHO HAVE SERVED AND PROTECTED OUR COUNTRY,” SAID WALTER LYNCH, AMERICAN WATER PRESIDEN
