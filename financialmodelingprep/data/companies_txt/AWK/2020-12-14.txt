CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE NATION'S LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WAS AWARDED THE 2020 NATIONAL CORPORATION OF THE YEAR BY THE EASTERN MINORITY SUPPLIER DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL (EMSDC), AT THEIR 2020 CHOICE AWARDS VIRTUAL EVENT HELD ON FRIDAY, DECEMBER 11.
