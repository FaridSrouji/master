American Water Works Company, Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on March 4, 2020. American Water Works Company, Inc. announced that its board of directors declared a quarterly cash dividend payment of 50 cents per share of common stock, payable on March 4, 2020, to all shareholders of record as of February 7, 2020.
