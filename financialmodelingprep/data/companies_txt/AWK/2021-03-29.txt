New Jersey American Water Signs Agreement to Purchase Egg Harbor City Water and Sewer System. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEW JERSEY AMERICAN WATER ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE THE WATER AND WASTEWATER ASSETS OF EGG HARBOR CITY, N.J. FOR $21.8 MILLION.
