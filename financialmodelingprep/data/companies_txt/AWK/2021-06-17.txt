Pennsylvania American Water Names Diane Holder and Jim Runzer as Vice Presidents of Operations. MECHANICSBURG, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER TODAY ANNOUNCED NEW VICE PRESIDENTS OF OPERATIONS FOR ITS WESTERN AND CENTRAL/EASTERN OPERATIONS.
