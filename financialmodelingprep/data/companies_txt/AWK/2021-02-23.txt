American Water Named One of the 100 Most Sustainable U.S. Companies. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AMERICAN WATER (NYSE: AWK), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY-TRADED U.S. WATER AND WASTEWATER UTILITY COMPANY, ANNOUNCED TODAY IT WAS NAMED AS ONE OF THE 100 MOST SUSTAINABLE COMPANIES BY BARRON'S MAGAZINE. “AT AMERICAN WATER DELIVERING A RELIABLE SUPPLY OF SAFE, CLEAN, AFFORDABLE WATER TO OUR CUSTOMERS AND TREATING THEIR WASTEWATER IS JUST PART OF OUR COMMITMENT TO CHALLENGE OURSELVES TO ALWAYS IMPROVE THE SUSTAINABILITY OF OUR BUSINESS AND MAKE A POSITIVE SOCIETAL IMPACT,”
