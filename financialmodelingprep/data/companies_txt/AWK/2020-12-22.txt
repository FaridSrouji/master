Pennsylvania American Water and Royersford Borough Invite Customers to Participate in Telephonic Public Input Hearing on Wastewater System Acquisition. ROYERSFORD, PA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PENNSYLVANIA AMERICAN WATER, ROYERSFORD BOROUGH AND THE PA PUC WILL HOLD A PUBLIC INPUT HEARING ON THE COMPANY'S PROPOSED WASTEWATER ACQUISITION.
