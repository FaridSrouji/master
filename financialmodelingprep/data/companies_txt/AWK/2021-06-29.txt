Illinois American Water Installs Ultraviolet Disinfection at Granite City Water Treatment Plant; Investment of about $6 million Supports Safe Drinking Water. GRANITE CITY, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER RECENTLY INVESTED APPROXIMATELY $6 MILLION TO CONSTRUCT ULTRAVIOLET (UV) DISINFECTION AT THE GRANITE CITY WATER TREATMENT PLANT. THE NEW TREATMENT TECHNOLOGY SUPPORTS SAFE DRINKING WATER TO RESIDENTS AND BUSINESSES. AS PART OF THE PROJECT, THREE NEW UV REACTORS WERE INSTALLED AND ARE CAPABLE OF TREATING UP TO 15 MILLION GALLONS OF WATER A DAY.
