Illinois American Water Celebrates Several Safety Excellence Milestones in the Company's Southern Division. BELLEVILLE, ILL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ILLINOIS AMERICAN WATER IS CELEBRATING NATIONAL SAFETY MONTH BY HIGHLIGHTING SAFETY EXCELLENCE ACHIEVED BY THREE TEAMS IN THE COMPANY'S SOUTHERN DIVISION. THE TEAM MEMBERS CELEBRATED SEVERAL YEARS OF OPERATION WITHOUT A LOST-TIME ACCIDENT. THE GODFREY WASTEWATER SERVICE TEAM CELEBRATED 21 YEARS IN MAY; THE GRAFTON WASTEWATER SERVICE TEAM CELEBRATED SIX YEARS IN MAY; AND THE JERSEYVILLE WATER AND WASTEWATER TEAMS CELEBRATED EIGHT YEARS ON JUNE 1.
