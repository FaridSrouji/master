L Brands, Inc. Board Approves Separation of Victoria's Secret & Co. and Corporate Name Change to Bath & Body Works, Inc.. COLUMBUS, OHIO, JULY 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- L BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: LB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS (THE “BOARD”) HAS APPROVED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SEPARATION OF THE VICTORIA'S SECRET BUSINESS INTO AN INDEPENDENT, PUBLICLY TRADED COMPANY. THE NEW COMPANY, NAMED VICTORIA'S SECRET & CO. (“VICTORIA'S SECRET”), WILL INCLUDE VICTORIA'S SECRET LINGERIE, PINK AND VICTORIA'S SECRET BEAUTY.
