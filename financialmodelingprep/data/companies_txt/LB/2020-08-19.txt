L Brands Reports Second Quarter 2020 Results. L BRANDS REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2020 RESULTS.Q2 SALES $2.319 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $2.21 BILLION.Q2 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.25 EXCLUDING ITEMS.Q2 LOSS PER SHARE $0.18.Q2 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $-0.42 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.AS OF END OF Q2, MOST BATH & BODY WORKS AND VICTORIA'S SECRET STORES IN NORTH AMERICA HAVE REOPENED.QTRLY TOTAL BATH & BODY WORKS Q2 SALES IN UNITED STATES AND CANADA WERE $1.197 BILLION.QTRLY TOTAL VICTORIA'S SECRET Q2 SALES IN UNITED STATES AND CANADA WERE $977.5 MILLION.NOT PROVIDING Q3 OR FULL-YEAR 2020 EARNINGS GUIDANCE.QTRLY L BRANDS COMPARABLE SALES (STORES AND DIRECT) UP 63% VERSUS DOWN 1%.
