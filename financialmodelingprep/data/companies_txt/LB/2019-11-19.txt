L Brands, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019; Announces Asset Impairment Charges for the Third Quarter Ended November 2, 2019. L Brands, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019. The company stated that it expects 2019 fourth quarter earnings per share to be about $2.00.The company also announced asset impairment charges for the third quarter ended November 2, 2019. For the quarter, the company's Victoria's Secret Asset Impairment was $247,532,000 compared to $80,878,000 a year ago.
