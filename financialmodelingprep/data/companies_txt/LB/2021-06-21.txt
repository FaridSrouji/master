L Brands, Inc. Announces Public Filing of Form 10 Registration Statement for Planned Separation of Victoria's Secret and Availability of Investor Presentation. COLUMBUS, OHIO, JUNE 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- L BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: LB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PUBLIC FILING OF A FORM 10 REGISTRATION STATEMENT WITH THE U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION IN CONNECTION WITH THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SEPARATION OF THE VICTORIA'S SECRET BUSINESS INTO AN INDEPENDENT, PUBLIC COMPANY. THE NEW COMPANY, NAMED VICTORIA'S SECRET & CO., WILL INCLUDE VICTORIA'S SECRET LINGERIE, PINK AND VICTORIA'S SECRET BEAUTY.
