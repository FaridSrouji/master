L Brands Announces Leadership Changes at Victoria's Secret. COLUMBUS, OHIO, NOV. 25, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- L BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: LB) ANNOUNCED SEVERAL KEY LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENTS AT VICTORIA'S SECRET. EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, MARTIN WATERS WAS NAMED CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF VICTORIA'S SECRET LINGERIE, REPLACING JOHN MEHAS, WHO HAS SERVED IN THE ROLE SINCE FEBRUARY 2019. WATERS WILL REPORT TO STUART BURGDOERFER, INTERIM CEO OF VICTORIA'S SECRET AND CFO OF L BRANDS. LAURA MILLER WAS NAMED CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES OFFICER OF VICTORIA'S SECRET; BECKY BEHRINGER WAS PROMOTED TO EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF NORTH AMERICA STORE SALES AND OPERATIONS; AND JANIE SCHAFFER WAS NAMED CHIEF DESIGN OFFICER OF VICTORIA'S SECRET LINGERIE.
