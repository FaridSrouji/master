L Brands Announces Partnership With Next PLC For Victoria's Secret UK. L BRANDS ANNOUNCES PARTNERSHIP WITH NEXT PLC FOR VICTORIA’S SECRET UK.L BRANDS INC - VICTORIA'S SECRET U.K. AND NEXT PLC HAVE FORMED A JOINT VENTURE IN DEAL.L BRANDS INC - JV WILL ACQUIRE MAJORITY OF ASSETS OF VICTORIA'S SECRET U.K. BUSINESS THAT IS CURRENTLY IN ADMINISTRATION.L BRANDS INC - NEWLY FORMED JV WILL OPERATE ALL VICTORIA'S SECRET STORES IN U.K. AND IRELAND.L BRANDS INC - NEXT PLC WILL OWN 51 PERCENT OF JV, WHILE VICTORIA'S SECRET WILL OWN 49 PERCENT IN DEAL.
