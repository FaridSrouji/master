Kellogg's® Nutri-Grain® Announces All-Expenses-Paid Dream Vacation Sweepstakes to Help Parents Reclaim Precious Family Time Spent on Food and Snack Negotiations. BATTLE CREEK, MICH., JULY 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUTRI-GRAIN LEARNED IN AN APRIL 2021 SURVEY THAT 43% OF PARENTS NEGOTIATE FOOD CHOICES WITH THEIR KIDS AT LEAST SIX TIMES PER DAY WITH EACH NEGOTIATION LASTING AT LEAST SIX MINUTESII, TOTALING NINE DAYS A YEAR.
