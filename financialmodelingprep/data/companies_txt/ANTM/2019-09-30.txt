Anthem, Inc. Elects Ryan Schneider to Serve as an Independent Director and Member Audit Committee of the Board Effective Immediately. Anthem, Inc. announced that on October 1, 2019, the Board of Directors of the company elected Ryan Schneider to serve as an independent director effective immediately, with a term expiring at the company's 2022 annual meeting of shareholders. The Board also appointed Mr. Schneider to the Audit Committee of the Board. Mr. Schneider, 49, is the Chief Executive Officer for Realogy Holdings Corporation.
