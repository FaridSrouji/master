AM Best Revises Issuer Credit Rating Outlook to Positive for Aflac Incorporated and Its Subsidiaries. OLDWICK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AM BEST HAS REVISED THE OUTLOOK TO POSITIVE FROM STABLE FOR THE LONG-TERM ISSUER CREDIT RATING (LONG-TERM ICR) AND AFFIRMED THE FINANCIAL STRENGTH RATING (FSR) OF A+ (SUPERIOR) AND THE LONG-TERM ICRS OF “AA-” (SUPERIOR) OF AFLAC LIFE INSURANCE JAPAN, LTD. (AFLAC JAPAN), AMERICAN FAMILY LIFE ASSURANCE COMPANY OF COLUMBUS (OMAHA, NE), AMERICAN FAMILY LIFE ASSURANCE COMPANY OF NEW YORK (ALBANY, NY) AND CONTINENTAL AMERICAN INSURANCE COMPANY (OMAHA, NE).
