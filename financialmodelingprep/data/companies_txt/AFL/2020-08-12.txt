Aflac Says Board Of Directors Increases Shares Authorized For Repurchase. AFLAC INCORPORATED BOARD OF DIRECTORS INCREASES SHARES AUTHORIZED FOR REPURCHASE.AFLAC INC- ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS AUTHORIZED PURCHASE OF UP TO 100 MILLION SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK.AFLAC- AUTHORIZATION IS IN ADDITION TO 21.9 MILLION SHARES AS OF JUNE 30, THAT REMAINED UNDER AUGUST 8, 2017 AUTHORIZATION.AFLAC- STEP BRINGS TOTAL NUMBER OF SHARES AVAILABLE FOR PURCHASE TO APPROXIMATELY 121.9 MILLION.
