Aflac Incorporated Announces First Quarter Results. ORPORATED ANNOUNCES FIRST QUARTER RESULTS, REPORTS FIRST QUARTER NET EARNINGS OF $566 MILLION, WITHDRAWS ANNUAL ADJUSTED EPS GUIDANCE, DECLARES SECOND QUARTER CASH DIVIDEND.Q1 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.21.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.78.Q1 REVENUE $5.2 BILLION VERSUS $5.7 BILLION.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.10 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SALES PRODUCTION IN BOTH JAPAN AND U.S. DID BEGIN TO FALL OFF IN MARCH, AND DECLINE ACCELERATED IN MONTH OF APRIL.AFLAC U.S. NET PREMIUM INCOME ROSE 1.5% TO $1.5 BILLION IN Q1.AFLAC - WILL CONTINUE TO INVEST IN PLATFORM & GROWTH INVESTMENTS.
