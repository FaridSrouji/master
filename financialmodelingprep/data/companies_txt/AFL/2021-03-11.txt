Aflac Brings Laughs to Help Ease Pain During March Madness®. COLUMBUS, GA., MARCH 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AFLAC, A LEADING PROVIDER OF SUPPLEMENTAL INSURANCE PRODUCTS IN THE U.S., IS PREPARING FOR THE BIG DANCE® WITH A BRACKET-BUSTING CAMPAIGN TO CONNECT WITH OVER 150 MILLION PASSIONATE BASKETBALL FANS WHO ARE MISSING THE TRADITIONAL MARCH MADNESS EXPERIENCE.
