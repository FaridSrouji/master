Pulte Homes Debuts Two Inglewood Communities in Prime Proximity to New SoFi Stadium. Pulte Homes announced the newest for-sale residential development in the City of Inglewood since 2005, with the grand opening of Grace Park. Located less than a mile away from SoFi Stadium, Grace Park is in the heart of Inglewood’s redevelopment area. At the site of the former Hollywood Park racetrack, SoFi Stadium is the centerpiece of a 298-acre mixed-use development with retail, commercial, residential, hotel and outdoor park spaces. Easily accessible by forthcoming new Los Angeles Metro Rail systems, the stadium will be home to the Los Angeles Rams and Los Angeles Chargers NFL teams.
