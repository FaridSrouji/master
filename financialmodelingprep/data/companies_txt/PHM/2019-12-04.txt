PulteGroup, Inc. Increases Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on January 3, 2020. PulteGroup, Inc. announced that its Board of Directors has voted to approve a 9% increase in the company’s quarterly cash dividend to $0.12 per common share. The increase is effective with the company’s next dividend which is payable January 3, 2020, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 18, 2019.
