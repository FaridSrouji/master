Rollins, Inc. Schedules Date For Release Of Second Quarter And Six Months 2021 Results. ATLANTA, JULY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL RELEASE ITS UNAUDITED SECOND QUARTER AND SIX MONTHS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED JUNE 30, 2021 ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 28, 2021.  IN CONJUNCTION WITH ITS RELEASE, THE COMPANY WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL TO REVIEW THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 28, 2021 AT 10:00 A.M.
