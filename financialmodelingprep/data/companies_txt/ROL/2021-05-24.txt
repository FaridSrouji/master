ATLANTA, MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOS ANGELES IS GETTING A NEW BRASS STAR THIS YEAR, AND NOT IN THE FORM OF ENTERTAINMENT ACHIEVEMENT. THE CITY OF ANGELS HAS LANDED THE NUMBER ONE SPOT ON NATIONAL PEST CONTROL LEADER ORKIN'S TOP 50 MOSQUITO CITIES LIST, BEATING OUT ATLANTA, WHO PREVIOUSLY HELD THE TOP RANK FOR SEVEN CONSECUTIVE YEARS.
