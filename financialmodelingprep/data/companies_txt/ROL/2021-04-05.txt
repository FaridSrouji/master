Rollins, Inc. Schedules Date For Release Of First Quarter 2021 Results. ATLANTA, APRIL 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ROLLINS, INC. (NYSE: ROL), A PREMIER GLOBAL CONSUMER AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL RELEASE ITS UNAUDITED FIRST QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED MARCH 31, 2021 ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 28, 2021.  IN CONJUNCTION WITH ITS RELEASE, THE COMPANY WILL HOST A CONFERENCE CALL TO REVIEW THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 28, 2021 AT 10:00 A.M.
