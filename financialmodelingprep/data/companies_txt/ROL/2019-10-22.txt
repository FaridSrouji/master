Rollins Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Rollins Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced net income was USD 44.061 million compared to USD 66.628 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.13 compared to USD 0.2 a year ago. For the nine months, net income was USD 152.582 million compared to USD 180.695 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.47 compared to USD 0.55 a year ago.
