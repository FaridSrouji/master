Rollins, Inc. Announces Regular Quarterly and Special Cash Dividend, Payable December 10, 2019. Rollins, Inc. announced that the Board of Directors declared a regular quarterly cash dividend on its common stock of $0.105 per share and a special year-end dividend of $0.05 per share both payable December 10, 2019 to stockholders of record at the close of business November 11, 2019.
