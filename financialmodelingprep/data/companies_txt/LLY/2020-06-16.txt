Eli Lilly Says FDA Approves its New Rapid-Acting Insulin Lyumjev (Insulin Lispro-Aabc Injection). FDA APPROVES LYUMJEV (INSULIN LISPRO-AABC INJECTION), LILLY'S NEW RAPID-ACTING INSULIN.LYUMJEV TO BE INCLUDED IN LILLY INSULIN VALUE PROGRAM ALLOWING THOSE WITHOUT INSURANCE AT ALL TO FILL MONTHLY PRESCRIPTION FOR $35.
