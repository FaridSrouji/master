Lilly's Mirikizumab Helps Patients Achieve Clinical Remission and Improves Symptoms in Adults with Ulcerative Colitis in 12-Week Phase 3 Induction Study. INDIANAPOLIS, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT MIRIKIZUMAB MET THE PRIMARY AND ALL KEY SECONDARY ENDPOINTS IN LUCENT-1, A 12-WEEK PHASE 3 INDUCTION STUDY EVALUATING THE EFFICACY AND SAFETY OF MIRIKIZUMAB FOR THE TREATMENT OF PATIENTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE ULCERATIVE COLITIS (UC). LUCENT-2, A MULTICENTER, RANDOMIZED, DOUBLE-BLIND, PLACEBO-CONTROLLED MAINTENANCE STUDY OF MIRIKIZUMAB IN PATIENTS WHO HAVE COMPLETED THE 12-WEEK LUCENT-1 INDUCTION STUDY IS ONGOING.
