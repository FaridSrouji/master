Lilly requests revocation of emergency use authorization for bamlanivimab alone to complete transition to bamlanivimab and etesevimab together for treatment of COVID-19 in the U.S.. INDIANAPOLIS, APRIL 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) HAS REQUESTED THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) REVOKE THE EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION (EUA) FOR BAMLANIVIMAB (LY-COV555) 700 MG ALONE. LILLY MADE THIS REQUEST DUE TO THE EVOLVING VARIANT LANDSCAPE IN THE U.S. AND THE FULL AVAILABILITY OF BAMLANIVIMAB AND ETESEVIMAB TOGETHER.
