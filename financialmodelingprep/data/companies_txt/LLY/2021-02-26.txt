U.S. Government to Purchase Minimum of 100,000 Doses of Etesevimab and Bamlanivimab Neutralizing Antibody Therapy. SHANGHAI, CHINA, FEB. 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- JUNSHI BIOSCIENCES (HKEX: 1877; SSE: 688180), A LEADING INNOVATION-DRIVEN BIOPHARMACEUTICAL COMPANY DEDICATED TO THE DISCOVERY, DEVELOPMENT AND COMMERCIALIZATION OF NOVEL THERAPIES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THE U.S. GOVERNMENT HAS AGREED TO PURCHASE A MINIMUM OF 100,000 DOSES OF ETESEVIMAB (JS016 OR LY-COV016) 1400 MG AND BAMLANIVIMAB (LY-COV555) 700 MG TOGETHER, ACCORDING TO THE COMPANY'S GLOBAL PARTNER ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY). ETESEVIMAB AND BAMLANIVIMAB TOGETHER RECENTLY RECEIVED EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION FOR THE TREATMENT OF RECENTLY DIAGNOSED, MILD TO MODERATE COVID-19 IN PATIENTS WHO ARE AT HIGH RISK OF PROGRESSING TO SEVERE COVID-19 AND/OR HOSPITALIZATION. ADDITIONALLY, THE NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH (NIH) RECENTLY UPDATED THE COVID-19 TREATMENT GUIDELINES TO RECOMMEND THE USE OF BAMLANIVIMAB PLUS ETESEVIMAB FOR THE TREATMENT OF OUTPATIENTS WITH MILD TO MODERATE COVID-19 WHO ARE AT HIGH RISK OF CLINICAL PROGRESSION.
