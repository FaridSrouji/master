Lilly and Incyte's Atopic Dermatitis Therapy Succeds In Late-Stage Trial. LILLY AND INCYTE ANNOUNCE POSITIVE TOP-LINE RESULTS FROM THE NORTH AMERICAN (BREEZE-AD5) PHASE 3 STUDY OF ORAL SELECTIVE JAK INHIBITOR BARICITINIB IN PATIENTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE ATOPIC DERMATITIS.INCYTE CORP - STUDY MET PRIMARY ENDPOINT.INCYTE CORP - STUDY'S SAFETY PROFILE WAS CONSISTENT WITH KNOWN SAFETY FINDINGS OF BARICITINIB IN ATOPIC DERMATITIS.INCYTE CORP - NO VENOUS THROMBOEMBOLIC EVENTS OR DEATHS WERE REPORTED IN TRIAL.INCYTE CORP - LILLY PLANS TO SUBMIT BARICITINIB AS A TREATMENT FOR PATIENTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE AD FOR APPROVAL IN U.S. & JAPAN IN 2020.INCYTE CORP - MOST COMMON TREATMENT-EMERGENT ADVERSE EVENTS INCLUDED UPPER RESPIRATORY TRACT INFECTIONS, NASOPHARYNGITIS, & DIARRHEA.
Lilly Sees FY 2020 Earnings Per Share $6.18 To $6.28. LILLY REPORTS STRONG FOURTH-QUARTER AND FULL-YEAR 2019 FINANCIAL RESULTS, UPDATES 2020 GUIDANCE FOR PENDING DERMIRA ACQUISITION.REAFFIRMS FY 2020 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $6.70 TO $6.80.SEES FY 2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE $6.18 TO $6.28.FOURTH-QUARTER 2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE (EPS) INCREASED TO $1.64 ON A REPORTED BASIS, OR $1.73 ON A NON-GAAP BASIS.FOURTH-QUARTER 2019 WORLDWIDE TRULICITY REVENUE WAS $1.208 BILLION, AN INCREASE OF 31 PERCENT COMPARED WITH Q4 OF 2018..FOR Q4 OF 2019, WORLDWIDE HUMALOG REVENUE DECREASED 1 PERCENT COMPARED WITH Q4 OF 2018, TO $763.4 MILLION.FOR Q4 OF 2019, WORLDWIDE TALTZ REVENUE INCREASED 37 PERCENT COMPARED WITH Q4 OF 2018, TO $420.1 MILLION.QTRLY REVENUE $6.11 BILLION VERSUS $5.64 BILLION.ANTICIPATES 2020 REVENUE BETWEEN $23.7 BILLION AND $24.2 BILLION.IN Q4 OF 2019, COMPANY RECOGNIZED ASSET IMPAIRMENT, RESTRUCTURING AND OTHER SPECIAL CHARGES OF $151.7 MILLION.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.52 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q4 REVENUE VIEW $5.91 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.2020 REVENUE GROWTH EXPECTED TO BE PARTIALLY OFFSET BY LOW-SINGLE DIGIT NET PRICE DECLINE IN U.S..FY2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $6.76, REVENUE VIEW $23.82 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
