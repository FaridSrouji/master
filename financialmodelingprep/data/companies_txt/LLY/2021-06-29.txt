Lilly Declares Third-Quarter 2021 Dividend. INDIANAPOLIS, JUNE 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) (THE "BOARD") HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2021 OF $0.85 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON SEPTEMBER 10, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON AUGUST 13, 2021.
