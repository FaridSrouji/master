Boehringer Ingelheim and Lilly To Collaborate With Duke Clinical Research Institute On A Pragmatic Trial. BOEHRINGER INGELHEIM AND LILLY TO COLLABORATE WITH DUKE CLINICAL RESEARCH INSTITUTE ON A PRAGMATIC TRIAL EXAMINING JARDIANCE'S® EFFECTS FOLLOWING AN ACUTE MYOCARDIAL INFARCTION.ELI LILLY - RANDOMIZED CLINICAL TRIAL TO BE CONDUCTED, ANALYZED, REPORTED IN PARTNERSHIP WITH DCRI, WITH BOEHRINGER INGELHEIM AND CO PROVIDING FUNDING.
