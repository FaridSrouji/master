Loxo Oncology at Lilly Announces Updated Data from the Phase 1/2 BRUIN Clinical Trial for LOXO-305 in Mantle Cell Lymphoma and Non-Hodgkin Lymphomas at the American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting. INDIANAPOLIS, DEC. 5, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- LOXO ONCOLOGY AT LILLY, A RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT GROUP OF ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY), TODAY ANNOUNCED CLINICAL DATA FROM THE LOXO-305 GLOBAL PHASE 1/2 BRUIN CLINICAL TRIAL IN MANTLE CELL LYMPHOMA (MCL) AND OTHER NON-HODGKIN LYMPHOMAS....
