Eli Lilly Begins Phase 3 Clinical Trial With Baricitinib For Hospitalized COVID-19 Patients. LILLY BEGINS A PHASE 3 CLINICAL TRIAL WITH BARICITINIB FOR HOSPITALIZED COVID-19 PATIENTS.ELI LILLY AND CO - FIRST PATIENT ENROLLED IN PHASE 3 RANDOMIZED, DOUBLE-BLIND, PLACEBO‑CONTROLLED STUDY TO EVALUATE EFFICACY, SAFETY OF BARICITINIB.ELI LILLY - EXPECTS TO ENROLL 400 PATIENTS IN PHASE 3 BARICITINIB TRIAL WITH DATA EXPECTED IN NEXT FEW MONTHS.
