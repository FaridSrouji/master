Lilly Presents New Data on Retevmo® (selpercatinib) in Advanced RET Fusion-Positive Gastrointestinal and Other Cancers at 2021 American Association for Cancer Research (AACR) Annual Meeting. INDIANAPOLIS, APRIL 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) TODAY ANNOUNCED FOR THE FIRST TIME DATA FROM THE PHASE 1/2 LIBRETTO-001 TRIAL SHOWING TREATMENT WITH RETEVMO® (SELPERCATINIB) DEMONSTRATED ENCOURAGING ANTITUMOR ACTIVITY AND SAFETY ACROSS RET FUSION-POSITIVE ADVANCED SOLID TUMORS BEYOND LUNG AND THYROID CANCERS, INCLUDING MULTIPLE TREATMENT-REFRACTORY GASTROINTESTINAL (GI) MALIGNANCIES. THE DATA WERE PRESENTED AT THE 2021 AMERICAN ASSOCIATION FOR CANCER RESEARCH (AACR) ANNUAL MEETING, HELD VIRTUALLY APRIL 10-15, 2021.
