Diogo Rau to join Lilly as Senior Vice-President and Chief Information and Digital Officer. INDIANAPOLIS, APRIL 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DIOGO RAU WILL JOIN THE COMPANY ON MAY 17, 2021 AS SENIOR VICE-PRESIDENT AND CHIEF INFORMATION AND DIGITAL OFFICER. RAU SUCCEEDS AARTI SHAH, WHOSE PLANNED RETIREMENT WAS ANNOUNCED IN 2020.
