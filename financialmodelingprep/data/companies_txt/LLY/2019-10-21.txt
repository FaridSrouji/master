Eli Lilly and Company Declares Fourth-Quarter 2019 Dividend, Payable on December 10, 2019. The board of directors of Eli Lilly and Company has declared a dividend for the fourth quarter of 2019 of $0.645 per share on outstanding common stock. The dividend is payable December 10, 2019, to shareholders of record at the close of business on November 15, 2019.
