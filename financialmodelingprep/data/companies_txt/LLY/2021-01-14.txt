Gabrielle Sulzberger Elected to Lilly Board of Directors. INDIANAPOLIS, JAN. 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) HAS ELECTED GABRIELLE SULZBERGER AS A NEW MEMBER, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 25, 2021.
