Lilly Receives FDA Approval Of Tauvid For Patients Being Evaluated For Alzheimer's Disease. LILLY RECEIVES U.S. FDA APPROVAL OF TAUVID™ (FLORTAUCIPIR F 18 INJECTION) FOR USE IN PATIENTS BEING EVALUATED FOR ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE.AVAILABILITY OF TAUVID WILL INITIALLY BE LIMITED AND WILL EXPAND IN RESPONSE TO COMMERCIAL DEMAND AND PAYOR REIMBURSEMENT.
FDA Approves First Drug To Image Tau Pathology In Patients Being Evaluated For Alzheimer's Disease. Food and Drug Administration: :FDA APPROVES FIRST DRUG TO IMAGE TAU PATHOLOGY IN PATIENTS BEING EVALUATED FOR ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE.U.S. FDA SAYS IT GRANTED APPROVAL OF TAUVID TO AVID RADIOPHARMACEUTICALS.U.S. FDA SAYS TAUVID IS A RADIOACTIVE DIAGNOSTIC AGENT FOR ADULT PATIENTS WITH COGNITIVE IMPAIRMENT BEING EVALUATED FOR ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE.
