Lilly Declares Second-Quarter 2021 Dividend and Announces Additional Share Repurchase Authorization. INDIANAPOLIS, MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) (THE "BOARD") HAS DECLARED A DIVIDEND FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021 OF $0.85 PER SHARE ON OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 10, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MAY 14, 2021.
Lilly to Commit $5 Million to Direct Relief's Fund for Health Equity. INDIANAPOLIS, MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ELI LILLY AND COMPANY (NYSE: LLY) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS SUPPORT OF DIRECT RELIEF'S FUND FOR HEALTH EQUITY (THE FUND) WITH A $5 MILLION COMMITMENT OVER THE NEXT FIVE YEARS. THE FUND LAUNCHED IN MARCH 2020 WITH $75 MILLION RAISED AGAINST A GOAL OF $150 MILLION.
