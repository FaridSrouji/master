Lilly's Taltz Receives U.S. FDA Approval For The Treatment Of Pediatric Patients. LILLY'S TALTZ® (IXEKIZUMAB) RECEIVES U.S. FDA APPROVAL FOR THE TREATMENT OF PEDIATRIC PATIENTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE PLAQUE PSORIASIS.ELI LILLY AND CO - TALTZ ALSO MET ALL MAJOR SECONDARY ENDPOINTS IN STUDY (P<0.001).
