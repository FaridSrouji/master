Xylem Inc. Provides Revenue Guidance for 2019. Xylem Inc. provided revenue guidance for 2019. The company updating forecasted full-year 2019 revenue to be in the range of $5.24 to $5.27 billion. On an organic basis, Xylem’s revenue growth is adjusted to a range of 3% to 4% (approximately 1% on a reported basis) from previous guidance of 5% to 6%.
