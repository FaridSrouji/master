Late-Breaking Registry Data at EuroPCR Congress Demonstrate Reduced Paravalvular Leak and Low Permanent Pacemaker Rates for the ACURATE neo2™ Aortic Valve System. MARLBOROUGH, MASS., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DATA PRESENTED AT HOTLINE AND LATE-BREAKING TRIAL SESSIONS TODAY AT THE EUROPCR 2021 CONGRESS DEMONSTRATED POSITIVE PROCEDURAL PERFORMANCE, INCLUDING LOW RATES OF PARAVALVULAR LEAKAGE (PVL) AND PERMANENT PACEMAKER IMPLEMENTATION (PPI), OF THE BOSTON SCIENTIFIC (NYSE: BSX) ACURATE NEO2™ AORTIC VALVE SYSTEM WITHIN THE INVESTIGATOR-INITIATED EARLY NEO2 REGISTRY1 AND ITAL-NEO REGISTRY2 STUDIES.
