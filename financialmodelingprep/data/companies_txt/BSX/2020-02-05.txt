BOSTON SCIENTIFIC ANNOUNCES RESULTS FOR FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2019.Q4 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $2.83.Q4 SALES $2.905 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $2.92 BILLION.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.44 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q4 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.46.BOSTON SCIENTIFIC - ESTIMATES REVENUE GROWTH FOR Q1 OF 2020, VERSUS PRIOR YEAR PERIOD, TO BE IN RANGE OF ABOUT 10 TO 12 PERCENT ON REPORTED BASIS.SEES REVENUE GROWTH FOR FY 2020, VERSUS PRIOR YEAR PERIOD, TO BE IN A RANGE OF ABOUT 10 TO 12 PERCENT ON A REPORTED BASIS.ESTIMATES INCOME ON A GAAP BASIS IN A RANGE OF $0.95 TO $1.00 PER SHARE FOR FY 2020.ESTIMATES ADJUSTED EARNINGS, EXCLUDING CERTAIN CHARGES IN A RANGE OF $1.74 TO $1.79 PER SHARE FOR FY 2020.BOSTON SCIENTIFIC-Q1 SALES GUIDANCE INCLUDES PRELIM NEGATIVE SALES IMPACT ESTIMATE OF $10-$40 MILLION FROM POTENTIAL EFFECT OF CORONAVIRUS ON CHINA PROCEDURE VOLUMES.ESTIMATES Q1 2020 EARNINGS ON A GAAP BASIS IN A RANGE OF $0.16 TO $0.19 PER SHARE.ESTIMATES Q1 2020 ADJUSTED EARNINGS, EXCLUDING CERTAIN CHARGES (CREDITS) IN A RANGE OF $0.37 TO $0.40 PER SHARE.REVENUE GROWTH FOR FULL YEAR 2020, VERSUS PRIOR YEAR PERIOD A GROWTH RANGE OF ABOUT 6.5 TO 8.5 PERCENT ON AN ORGANIC BASIS.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $0.41, REVENUE VIEW $2.85 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.FY2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $1.78, REVENUE VIEW $12.05 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
