Late-Breaking Clinical Trial Data for TheraSphere™ Y-90 Glass Microspheres Demonstrates Improved Survival in Primary Liver Cancer. MARLBOROUGH, MASS., MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BOSTON SCIENTIFIC CORPORATION (NYSE: BSX) ANNOUNCED POSITIVE DATA FROM THE TARGET STUDY OF THE THERASPHERE™ Y-90 GLASS MICROSPHERES (THERASPHERE) – A TYPE OF RADIOEMBOLIZATION COMPRISED OF MILLIONS OF MICROSCOPIC GLASS BEADS CONTAINING RADIOACTIVE YTTRIUM (Y-90) – DURING A LATE-BREAKING CLINICAL TRIAL PRESENTATION AT THE ANNUAL SCIENTIFIC MEETING FOR THE SOCIETY OF INTERVENTIONAL RADIOLOGY (SIR).
