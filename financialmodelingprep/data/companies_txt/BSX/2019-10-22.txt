Boston Scientific Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Boston Scientific Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,707 million compared to USD 2,393 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 383 million compared to USD 388 million a year ago. Net income was USD 126 million compared to USD 432 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.09 compared to USD 0.31 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 7,831 million compared to USD 7,262 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,308 million compared to USD 1,187 million a year ago. Net income was USD 704 million compared to USD 1,285 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.51 compared to USD 0.93 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.5 compared to USD 0.92 a year ago.
