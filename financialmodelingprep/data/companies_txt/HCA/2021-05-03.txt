HCA Healthcare to Sell Four of Its Hospitals in Georgia to Piedmont Healthcare. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HCA HEALTHCARE, INC. (NYSE:HCA), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE SIGNING OF A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT TO SELL FOUR OF ITS HOSPITALS IN GEORGIA FOR APPROXIMATELY $950,000,000. HCA HEALTHCARE HAS AGREED TO SELL THE FOLLOWING HOSPITALS IN NORTH GEORGIA AND MACON TO PIEDMONT HEALTHCARE, A NOT-FOR-PROFIT HEALTH SYSTEM HEADQUARTERED IN ATLANTA, GEORGIA: EASTSIDE MEDICAL CENTER, A 310 BED HOSPITAL IN SNELLVILLE, GEORGIA CARTERSVILLE
