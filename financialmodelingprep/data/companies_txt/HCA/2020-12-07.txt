Mitch Edgeworth Appointed President of HCA Healthcare's TriStar Division. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HCA HEALTHCARE (NYSE: HCA), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS APPOINTED MITCH EDGEWORTH AS PRESIDENT OF THE COMPANY'S TRISTAR DIVISION EFFECTIVE JANUARY 4, 2021. EDGEWORTH REPLACES HEATHER ROHAN WHO RECENTLY ANNOUNCED HER RETIREMENT AFTER A 34-YEAR CAREER WITH HCA HEALTHCARE. AS TRISTAR DIVISION PRESIDENT, EDGEWORTH WILL HAVE RESPONSIBILITY FOR 17 HOSPITALS AND A NUMBER OF OTHER SITES OF CARE SERVING COMMUNITIES IN TENNES
