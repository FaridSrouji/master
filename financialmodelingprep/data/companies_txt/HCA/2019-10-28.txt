HCA Healthcare, Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 27, 2019. HCA Healthcare, Inc. announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.40 per share on the company’s common stock. The dividend will be paid on December 27, 2019 to stockholders of record at the close of business on December 2, 2019.
