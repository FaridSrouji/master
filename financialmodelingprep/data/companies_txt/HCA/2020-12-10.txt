HCA Healthcare Collects Record-Breaking 13,523 Pounds of Medication During 2020 “Crush the Crisis” Opioid Take Back. NASHVILLE, TENN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HCA HEALTHCARE, ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT COLLECTED 13,523 POUNDS OF UNUSED AND EXPIRED PRESCRIPTION MEDICATIONS DURING ITS “CRUSH THE CRISIS” OPIOID TAKE BACK DAY ENTERPRISE EVENTS, MORE THAN TWICE AS MUCH AS IT COLLECTED LAST YEAR. THE EVENTS, WHICH TOOK PLACE AT 95 HCA HEALTHCARE FACILITIES ACROSS 18 STATES ON OCTOBER 24, 2020, IN ALIGNMENT WITH THE DRUG ENFORCEMENT ADMINISTRATION'S (DEA) NATIONAL PRESCRIPTION
