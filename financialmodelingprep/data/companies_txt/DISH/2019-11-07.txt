Dish Network Corp - Board Of Directors Has Approved A Proposed Rights Offering To Raise Proceeds Of Approximately $1 Billion. DISH NETWORK ANNOUNCES RIGHTS OFFERING.DISH NETWORK CORP - BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A PROPOSED RIGHTS OFFERING TO RAISE PROCEEDS OF APPROXIMATELY $1 BILLION.DISH NETWORK CORP - SUBSCRIPTION PRICE WILL BE $33.52 PER SHARE OF CLASS A COMMON STOCK.DISH NETWORK CORP - RIGHTS OFFERING WILL NOT PROVIDE FOR OVER-SUBSCRIPTION RIGHTS.DISH NETWORK CORP - SUBSCRIPTION RIGHTS WILL ENTITLE HOLDER TO ACQUIRE NEWLY-ISSUED SHARES OF DISH'S CLASS A COMMON STOCK.
