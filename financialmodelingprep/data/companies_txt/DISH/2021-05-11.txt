DISH Network Places Offering of $1,500,000,000 in Senior Notes. ENGLEWOOD, COLO., MAY 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DISH NETWORK CORPORATION ("DISH NETWORK") (NASDAQ: DISH) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ON MAY 10, 2021 ITS SUBSIDIARY, DISH DBS CORPORATION ("DISH DBS"), PRICED AN OFFERING OF $1,500,000,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 5.125% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2029 (THE "NOTES").
