Chubb Limited Declares A Quarterly Dividend Equal to $0.75 Per Share, Payable on January 10, 2020. The Board of Directors of Chubb Limited declared a quarterly dividend equal to $0.75 per share, payable on January 10, 2020 to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 20, 2019. The dividend will be payable out of legal reserves and will be made in United States dollars by the company's transfer agent. This will be the third installment as approved by the company's shareholders on May 16, 2019.
