Kimberly A. Ross Does Not Stand for Re-Election as Member of the Board of Directors of Chubb Limited. On October 4, 2019, Kimberly A. Ross, a member of the Board of Directors of Chubb Limited, informed the Company of her decision not to stand for re-election to the Board at the company's 2020 Annual General Meeting. Ms. Ross is currently a member of the Audit Committee of the Board. She will remain on the Board and a member of the Audit Committee until the Annual Meeting, which is scheduled to occur in May 2020.
