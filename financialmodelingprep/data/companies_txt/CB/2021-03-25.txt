Westchester, a Chubb Company, Names Dave Roberts Head of Westchester Small Business. WHITEHOUSE STATION, N.J., MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WESTCHESTER, CHUBB'S WHOLESALE EXCESS AND SURPLUS INSURANCE BUSINESS, HAS APPOINTED DAVE ROBERTS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT, WESTCHESTER SMALL BUSINESS.
