Chubb Limited Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Chubb Limited announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced net income was USD 1,091 million compared to USD 1,231 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.38 compared to USD 2.64 a year ago. For the nine months, net income was USD 3,281 million compared to USD 3,607 million a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 7.13 compared to USD 7.71 a year ago.
