Chubb To Host Second Virtual Large Account Client and Broker Engagement. WHITEHOUSE STATION, N.J., MARCH 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHUBB HAS ANNOUNCED ITS SECOND VIRTUAL LARGE ACCOUNT CLIENT AND BROKER ENGAGEMENT EVENT, APRIL 19 – 21, TO BRING TOGETHER ITS SENIOR EXECUTIVES, CLIENTS AND BROKER PARTNERS TO DISCUSS INDIVIDUAL RISK MANAGEMENT NEEDS, THE NEWEST CHUBB DATA AND INSIGHTS, AND OVERALL INDUSTRY DEVELOPMENTS.
