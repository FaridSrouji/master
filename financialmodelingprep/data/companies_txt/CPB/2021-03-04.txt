Campbell Declares Quarterly Dividend. CAMDEN, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CAMPBELL SOUP COMPANY (NYSE:CPB) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON CAMPBELL'S CAPITAL STOCK OF $0.37 PER SHARE. THE QUARTERLY DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE MAY 3, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS APRIL 8, 2021.
