Campbell Soup Company Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on January 27, 2020. Campbell Soup Company declared a regular quarterly dividend on Campbell’s capital stock of $0.35 per share. The quarterly dividend is payable on January 27, 2020 to shareholders of record at the close of business January 8, 2020.
