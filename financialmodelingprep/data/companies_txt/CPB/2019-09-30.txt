Campbell Soup Company Announces Anthony P. Disilvestro, Former Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, to Depart on March 31, 2020 Instead of October 15, 2019. On August 30, 2019, Campbell Soup Company filed a Form 8-K to report that Anthony P. DiSilvestro, former Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, was leaving his role effective September 30, 2019 and departing the Company on October 15, 2019. Subsequent to filing the Form 8-K, the Company and Mr. DiSilvestro agreed that Mr. DiSilvestro's employment would terminate on March 31, 2020 instead of October 15, 2019. During that time, Mr. DiSilvestro will assist in the first-quarter financial closing, the closing of the divestiture of Arnott’s and certain other international operations and the transitioning of his duties to the new chief financial officer.
