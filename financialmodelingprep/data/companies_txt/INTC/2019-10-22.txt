Astera Labs Accelerates PCI Express 5.0 System Deployment in Collaboration with Intel and Synopsys. Astera Labs Inc., in collaboration with Synopsys, Inc., and Intel, announced demonstration of a complete PCI Express® (PCIe®) 5.0 system, delivering 32 GT/s speeds for next-generation server workloads. The end-to-end solution showcases system-level multi-vendor interoperability between Intel's PCIe 5.0 test chip, Synopsys' silicon-proven DesignWare® Controller and PHY IP for PCIe 5.0, and Astera Labs' Smart Retimer SoC for PCIe 5.0. The joint PCIe 5.0 demonstration will be showcased in the Synopsys Booth at PCI-SIG DevCon Taipei, October 28-29 at the Taipei Marriott Hotel.
