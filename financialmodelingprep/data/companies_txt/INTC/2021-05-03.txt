Intel to Invest $3.5 Billion to Expand New Mexico Manufacturing Operations. RIO RANCHO, N.M.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--INTEL WILL INVEST $3.5 BILLION TO EQUIP ITS NEW MEXICO OPERATIONS FOR THE MANUFACTURING OF ADVANCED SEMICONDUCTOR PACKAGING TECHNOLOGIES.
