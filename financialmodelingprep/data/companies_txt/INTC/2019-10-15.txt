Intel Corporation (NasdaqGS:INTC) entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Smart Edge Business from Smart Edge Business of Smart-Edge.com, Inc. for $27 million.. Intel Corporation (NasdaqGS:INTC) entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Smart Edge Business from Smart-Edge.com, Inc. for $27 million on October 14, 2019. Intel will acquire all of the assets in cash and will assume certain specified liabilities. A portion of the purchase price will be held back by Intel at the closing as partial security for the indemnification obligations of Smart-Edge.com. In a related transaction Pivot and Intel have entered into a three-year preferred channel partner agreement which designates Pivot as a non-exclusive preferred system integrator and channel partner for Smart Edge based solutions. The agreement may be terminated any time prior to closing by mutual written consent of Intel and Smart-Edge.com. All Smart Edge employees, including Smart Edge's Chief Executive Officer, Bob Pike, will join Intel’s Network and Custom Logic Group (NCLG) once the transaction is complete. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, approvals and clearances of governmental entities, third party approval and employment agreements. The transaction is expected to close during the fourth quarter of 2019.
