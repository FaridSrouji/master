ComEd Offers Options to Help Customers Manage Electric Bills. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AS MANY NORTHERN ILLINOIS RESIDENTS EXPERIENCE MOUNTING EXPENSES AS A RESULT OF THE PANDEMIC, COMED OFFERS PAYMENT ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS THAT MAY HELP WITH PAST-DUE BALANCES NOW AND ENERGY-SAVING TOOLS THAT CAN LOWER BILLS IN THE FUTURE. “TO SUPPORT OUR CUSTOMERS AND COMMUNITIES WORKING TO REBUILD FROM COVID-19, COMED IS OFFERING MORE OPTIONS FOR CUSTOMERS TO HELP THEM MAINTAIN THEIR SERVICE AND SAVE MONEY ON THEIR ELECTRIC BILLS,” SAID JANE S.
