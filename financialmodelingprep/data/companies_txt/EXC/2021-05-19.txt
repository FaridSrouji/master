ComEd, Chicago Park District Team up With WGN's Tom Skilling to Bring Buckingham Fountain Back to Life. CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AFTER A ONE-YEAR HIATUS, COMED WILL AGAIN TEAM UP WITH THE CHICAGO PARK DISTRICT TO TURN ON CHICAGO'S ICONIC BUCKINGHAM FOUNTAIN AS PART OF THE ANNUAL “SWITCH ON SUMMER” EVENT, WHICH CELEBRATES THE ARRIVAL OF SUMMER. A VIRTUAL CELEBRATION WILL STREAM LIVE AT FACEBOOK.COM/COMED STARTING AT 2 P.M. SATURDAY, MAY 22.
