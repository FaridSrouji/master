International Flavors & Fragrances Says Lenders Have Committed To Provide Senior Unsecured 2-Year Term Loan Facility Of Up To $200 Million. INTERNATIONAL FLAVORS & FRAGRANCES INC -  ON MAY 15, ENTERED INTO A TERM LOAN CREDIT AGREEMENT.INTERNATIONAL FLAVORS & FRAGRANCES INC -  LENDERS HAVE COMMITTED TO PROVIDE, A SENIOR UNSECURED TWO YEAR TERM LOAN FACILITY OF UP TO $200 MILLION.INTERNATIONAL FLAVORS & FRAGRANCES - PROCEEDS UNDER TERM LOAN TO BE USED TO FINANCE, IN PART, REDEMPTION, PURCHASE OF $300 MILLION SENIOR NOTES DUE 2020.
