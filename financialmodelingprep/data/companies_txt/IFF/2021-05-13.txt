IFF Achieves Key 2020 Sustainability Goals. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--IFF (NYSE:IFF) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ACHIEVEMENT OF KEY 2020 SUSTAINABILITY GOALS THAT WERE SET AT THE LAUNCH OF THE COMPANY'S SUSTAINABILITY PROGRAM IN 2010. DURING THAT TIME, IFF MET AND SURPASSED ITS ORIGINAL INTENSITY-BASED TARGETS FOR THREE ENVIRONMENTAL GOALS, RESULTING IN A REDUCTION IN ITS GREENHOUSE GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS, WATER WITHDRAWAL AND HAZARDOUS WASTE GENERATION PER METRIC TON OF PRODUCTION BY -42.6%, -67.1% AND -41% BELOW 2010 LEVELS, RESPECTIVELY.
