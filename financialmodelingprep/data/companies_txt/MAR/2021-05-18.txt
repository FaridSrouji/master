Marriott International Files Lawsuit To Combat Fraudulent Robocalls. BETHESDA, MD., MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL, INC. (NASDAQ: MAR) TODAY FILED A LAWSUIT IN FEDERAL COURT IN VIRGINIA AGAINST THE UNKNOWN PERPETRATORS ("JOHN DOES") RESPONSIBLE FOR ILLEGAL AND FRAUDULENT "ROBOCALLS" MISUSING MARRIOTT'S NAME.
