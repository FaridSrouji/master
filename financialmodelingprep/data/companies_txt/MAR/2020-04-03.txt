Marriott Says On April 2, Provided Notice To Lenders To Borrow $2.0 Bln Under Credit Facility. MARRIOTT - ON APRIL 2, CO PROVIDED NOTICE TO LENDERS TO BORROW $2.0 BILLION UNDER CREDIT FACILITY.MARRIOTT - PROCEEDS FROM INCREMENTAL CREDIT FACILITY BORROWINGS ARE CURRENTLY BEING HELD AS CASH.MARRIOTT - AS OF APRIL 2, 2020, MARRIOTT HAS APPROXIMATELY $2.0 BILLION OF COMMERCIAL PAPER OUTSTANDING..MARRIOTT - PROCEEDS FROM INCREMENTAL CREDIT FACILITY BORROWINGS MAY BE USED IN FUTURE TO REPAY COMMERCIAL PAPER WHEN IT MATURES.
