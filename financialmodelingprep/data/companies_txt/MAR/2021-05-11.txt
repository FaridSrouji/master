EDITION Confirms Re-Opening For Both New York City Properties Times Square And Madison Square Park Hotels Welcome Guests Back To NYC. NEW YORK, MAY 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH THE CITY ON THE CUSP OF A CULTURAL AND SOCIAL RESURGENCE, EDITION, THE LUXURY LIFESTYLE HOTEL BRAND WHICH HAS REVOLUTIONIZED THE HOSPITALITY LANDSCAPE, ANNOUNCES THE RE-OPENING OF ITS TWO NYC HOTEL LOCATIONS IN TIMES SQUARE AND MADISON SQUARE PARK. EDITION, RECENTLY DUBBED AS "THE WORLD'S HOTTEST HOTEL BRAND," WHICH INTRODUCED A NEW GENERATION OF LUXURY PROPERTIES TO THE WORLD WITH ONE-OF-A-KIND HOTEL CONCEPTS, REOPENS THE NEW YORK EDITION ON MAY 11TH AND THE TIMES SQUARE EDITION ON JUNE 1ST, 2021.
