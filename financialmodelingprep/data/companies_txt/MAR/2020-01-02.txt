Service Properties Trust Announces New Deals With Marriott. SERVICE PROPERTIES TRUST ANNOUNCES NEW AGREEMENTS WITH MARRIOTT.SERVICE PROPERTIES TRUST - TERM OF AGREEMENTS WITH MARRIOTT WILL BE EXTENDED THROUGH 2035.SERVICE PROPERTIES - MARRIOTT TO GIVE $30 MILLION LIMITED GUARANTY FOR 85% OF ANNUAL OWNER'S PRIORITY RETURNS DUE THROUGH 2026, IF SECURITY DEPOSIT EXHAUSTED.SERVICE PROPERTIES - NEW AGREEMENTS PROVIDE THAT SVC WILL FUND ABOUT $350.0 MILLION TO $400.0 MILLION FOR PLANNED RENOVATIONS OF HOTELS OVER NEXT 4 YEARS.SERVICE PROPERTIES - CO, MARRIOTT IDENTIFIED 33 OF 122 HOTELS COVERED BY NEW AGREEMENTS TO BE SOLD OR REBRANDED, CO WOULD RETAIN PROCEEDS OF SUCH SALE.
