Electronic Arts Says CEO's FY 2020 Total Compensation Was $21.4 Mln Vs $18.3 Mln In FY 2019 - SEC Filing. ELECTRONIC ARTS SAYS CEO ANDREW WILSON'S FY 2020 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $21.4 MILLION VERSUS $18.3 MILLION IN FY 2019 - SEC FILING.ELECTRONIC ARTS SAYS CFO BLAKE JORGENSEN'S FY 2020 TOTAL COMPENSATION WAS $19.5 MILLION.ELECTRONIC ARTS - FOR FISCAL 2020, RATIO OF ANNUAL TOTAL COMPENSATION OF MEDIAN EMPLOYEE TO ANNUAL TOTAL COMPENSATION OF CEO WAS 218 TO 1.
