Real Luck Group Ltd. Announces New Chief Executive Officer. COMPANY TO HOST WEBINAR MONDAY, MAY 10 AT 8.30AM EST CALGARY, AB AND ISLE OF MAN, UNITED KINGDOM, MAY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -  REAL LUCK GROUP LTD . (TSX.V: LUCK) ("REAL LUCK GROUP" OR THE "COMPANY") AND ITS SUBSIDIARY COMPANIES DOING BUSINESS AS " LUCKBOX " (THE "GROUP"), ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT THOMAS ROSANDER HAS BEEN PROMOTED TO CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF THE COMPANY AND HAS ALSO BEEN APPOINTED TO THE COMPANY'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS, WITH QUENTIN MARTIN VOLUNTARILY STEPPING DOWN AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND A DIRECTOR OF THE COMPANY.
