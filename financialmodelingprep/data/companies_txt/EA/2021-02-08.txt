Electronic Arts to Acquire Glu Mobile, Creating a New Global Leader in the Largest and Fastest Growing Gaming Segment. REDWOOD CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ELECTRONIC ARTS INC. (NASDAQ: EA), A GLOBAL LEADER IN INTERACTIVE ENTERTAINMENT, AND GLU MOBILE INC. (NASDAQ: GLUU), A LEADING GLOBAL DEVELOPER AND PUBLISHER OF MOBILE GAMES INCLUDING DESIGN HOME, COVET FASHION, AND MLB TAP SPORTS BASEBALL, HAVE ENTERED INTO A DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT UNDER WHICH ELECTRONIC ARTS WILL ACQUIRE GLU MOBILE. UNDER THE TERMS OF THE AGREEMENT, EA WILL ACQUIRE GLU FOR $2.1 BILLION IN ENTERPRISE VALUE. GLU STOCKHOLDERS WILL RECEIVE $12
