Silver Rain Games Inks Major Deal With Electronic Arts. LONDON & REDWOOD CITY, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--SILVER RAIN GAMES, AN ENTERTAINMENT DEVELOPMENT STUDIO CO-FOUNDED BY ACTOR AND PRODUCER ABUBAKAR SALIM, HAS SIGNED A DEAL WITH THE EA ORIGINALS LABEL.
