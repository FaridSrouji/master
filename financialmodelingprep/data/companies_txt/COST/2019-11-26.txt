Costco Wholesale Corporation Announces Not Stand for Re-Election of John W. Meisenbach as Member of the Board. On November 25, 2019, John W. Meisenbach, who has been a member of the Board of Directors of Costco Wholesale Corporation for over thirty-six years, submitted a letter stating that he will not be standing for re-election as member of the Board when his current term expires, effective on commencement of the Company’s 2020 annual meeting.
