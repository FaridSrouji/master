Costco Wholesale Corporation Reports August Sales Results. COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION REPORTS AUGUST SALES RESULTS.COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP - NET SALES OF $13.56 BILLION FOR RETAIL MONTH OF AUGUST, AN INCREASE OF 15.0 PERCENT.COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP - TOTAL COMPANY COMPARABLE SALES FOR AUGUST UP 13.2%.COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP - AUGUST SALES WERE NEGATIVELY IMPACTED BY SHIFT OF LABOR DAY.COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP - ESTIMATED NEGATIVE IMPACT ON AUGUST NET AND COMPARABLE SALES WAS APPROXIMATELY 75 BPS IN U.S.
