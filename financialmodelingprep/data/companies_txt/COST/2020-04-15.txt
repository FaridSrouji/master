Costco Wholesale Corp Reports Increase In Its Quarterly Cash Dividend. COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION ANNOUNCES AN INCREASE IN ITS QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND.COSTCO WHOLESALE - BOARD DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND ON COSTCO COMMON STOCK AND APPROVED A QUARTERLY INCREASE FROM 65 TO 70 CENTS PER SHARE.
