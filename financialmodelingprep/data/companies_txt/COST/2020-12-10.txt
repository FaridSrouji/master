Costco Wholesale Corporation Reports First Quarter Fiscal Year 2021 Operating Results. ISSAQUAH, WASH., DEC. 10, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION (“COSTCO” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: COST) TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS OPERATING RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER (TWELVE WEEKS) OF FISCAL 2021, ENDED NOVEMBER 22, 2020.
