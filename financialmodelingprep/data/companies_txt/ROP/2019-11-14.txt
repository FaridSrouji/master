Roper Technologies, Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on January 23, 2020. Roper Technologies, Inc. announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.5125 per share, payable on January 23, 2020 to stockholders of record as of January 9, 2020. This represents an increase of 11% over the dividend paid in each quarter of 2019, or an expected $0.20 increase on an annual basis ($0.05 on a quarterly basis).
