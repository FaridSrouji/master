Textron Reports First Quarter 2021 Results. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TEXTRON INC. (NYSE: TXT) TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2021 NET INCOME OF $0.75 PER SHARE.
