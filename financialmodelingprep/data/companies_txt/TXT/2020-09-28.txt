Textron Aviation Defense Says Contract With Royal Thai Air Force For Integrated Training System. TEXTRON AVIATION DEFENSE - CONTRACT WITH ROYAL THAI AIR FORCE FOR INTEGRATED TRAINING SYSTEM.TEXTRON AVIATION DEFENSE - CONTRACT IS FOR 12 BEECHCRAFT T-6C TEXAN II ADVANCED MILITARY TRAINING AIRCRAFT.TEXTRON AVIATION DEFENSE - CONTRACT IS FOR GROUND-BASED TRAINING SYSTEMS FOR PILOTS AND MAINTENANCE PROFESSIONALS.TEXTRON AVIATION DEFENSE - WORK IN SUPPORT OF CONTRACT TO TAKE PLACE AT CO’S WICHITA, KANSAS FACILITIES.
