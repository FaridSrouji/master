Textron Declares Quarterly Dividend. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--The Board of Directors of Textron Inc. (NYSE:TXT) today declared a quarterly dividend of $0.02 per share on the company's Common Stock. All dividends will be paid on January 1, 2021, to holders of record at the close of business on December 11, 2020. About Textron Inc.
