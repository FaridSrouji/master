Textron Reports Fourth Quarter 2020 Results; Announces 2021 Financial Outlook. PROVIDENCE, R.I.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TEXTRON INC. (NYSE: TXT) TODAY REPORTED FOURTH QUARTER 2020 NET INCOME OF $1.03 PER SHARE.
