Textron Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on January 1, 2020. The Board of Directors of Textron declared a quarterly dividend of $0.02 per share on the company's Common Stock. All dividends will be paid on January 1, 2020 to shareholders of record as of December 13, 2019.
