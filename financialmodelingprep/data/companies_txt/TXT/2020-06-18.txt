Textron - Approved Restructuring Plan To Reduce Co’s Operating Expenses. TEXTRON - APPROVED RESTRUCTURING PLAN TO REDUCE CO’S OPERATING EXPENSES THROUGH HEADCOUNT REDUCTIONS, FACILITY CONSOLIDATIONS AND OTHER ACTIONS.TEXTRON - RESTRUCTURING PLAN PRIMARILY IMPACTS TRU SIMULATION + TRAINING BUSINESS WITHIN CO'S SYSTEMS SEGMENT & CO'S AVIATION & INDUSTRIAL SEGMENTS.TEXTRON - EXPECT TO INCUR PRE-TAX SPECIAL CHARGES RELATED TO RESTRUCTURING PLAN IN THE RANGE OF $110 MILLION TO $130 MILLION IN Q2.TEXTRON - RESTRUCTURING PLAN WILL RESULT IN ELIMINATION OF UP TO 1,950 POSITIONS, REPRESENTING 6% OF CO'S WORKFORCE.TEXTRON - ADDITIONALLY, EXPECTS TO RECORD A NON-CASH INVENTORY VALUATION CHARGE IN THE RANGE OF $50 MILLION TO $60 MILLION FROM RESTRUCTURING PLAN.TEXTRON - DURING Q2, EXPECT TO INCUR IDLE FACILITY COSTS IN RANGE OF $70 MILLION TO $80 MILLION, PRINCIPALLY IN TEXTRON AVIATION SEGMENT.
