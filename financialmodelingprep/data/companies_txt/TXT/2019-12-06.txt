Textron Inc Says Board Approved Restructuring Plan. TEXTRON INC - ON DEC 3 BOARD OF DIRECTORS APPROVED RESTRUCTURING PLAN DESIGNED TO REDUCE COSTS AND IMPROVE OVERALL OPERATING EFFICIENCY.TEXTRON INC - RESTRUCTURING PLAN PRINCIPALLY IMPACTS TEXTRON AVIATION AND INDUSTRIAL SEGMENTS.TEXTRON INC - EXPECT TO INCUR PRE-TAX CHARGES IN Q4 OF 2019 IN RANGE OF $65 MILLION TO $80 MILLION.TEXTRON INC - FUTURE CASH EXPENDITURES ARE EXPECTED TO BE IN RANGE OF $50 MILLION TO $60 MILLION, WHICH WILL BE PAID PRINCIPALLY IN FIRST HALF OF 2020.TEXTRON INC - ANTICIPATE THAT RESTRUCTURING PLAN WILL BE SUBSTANTIALLY COMPLETED BY END OF 2019.TEXTRON -  IN Q4, EXPECT SEVERANCE & RELATED COSTS ESTIMATED TO BE $40 MILLION TO $45 MILLION, WITH TOTAL HEADCOUNT REDUCTION OF ABOUT 875 POSITIONS.TEXTRON - IMPAIRMENT CHARGES RELATED TO FACILITY CLOSURES, TOOLING & OTHER ASSETS ACROSS BOTH SEGMENTS ESTIMATED TO BE IN RANGE OF $15 MILLION TO $20 MILLION.
