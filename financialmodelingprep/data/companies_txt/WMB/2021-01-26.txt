Williams Announces Quarterly Cash Dividend. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS' (NYSE: WMB) BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A REGULAR DIVIDEND OF $0.41 PER SHARE, OR $1.64 ANNUALIZED, ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK, PAYABLE ON MARCH 29, 2021, TO HOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 12, 2021. THIS IS A 2.5% INCREASE FROM WILLIAMS' FIRST-QUARTER 2020 QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.40 PER SHARE, PAID IN MARCH 2020. SOME PORTION OF THIS DISTRIBUTION MAY BE CONSIDERED A RETURN OF CAPITAL FOR TAX PURPOSES. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION R
