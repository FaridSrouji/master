Williams Announces Early In-Service Capacity for Critical Natural Gas Infrastructure Projects. TULSA, OKLA.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WILLIAMS (NYSE: WMB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT ACHIEVED EARLY IN-SERVICE CAPACITY, AND AS A RESULT, EARLIER-THAN-EXPECTED CASH FLOW IN THE FOURTH QUARTER, FOR KEY ENERGY INFRASTRUCTURE EXPANSIONS DESIGNED TO SERVE GROWING DEMAND FOR CLEAN ENERGY IN THE UNITED STATES. THE COMPANY CREDITS ITS PROACTIVE ENGAGEMENT AND OPEN COMMUNICATION WITH STAKEHOLDERS THROUGHOUT THE PERMITTING AND REGULATORY PROCESS AS WELL AS EXPEDITIOUS AND COVID-SAFE PROJECT EXECUTION FOR COMPLET
