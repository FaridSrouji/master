BlackRock® Canada Announces January Cash Distributions for the iShares® ETFs. TORONTO, JAN. 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE JANUARY 2021 CASH DISTRIBUTIONS FOR THE ISHARES ETFS LISTED ON THE TSX OR NEO WHICH PAY ON A MONTHLY BASIS. UNITHOLDERS OF RECORD OF A FUND ON JANUARY 26, 2021 WILL RECEIVE CASH DISTRIBUTIONS PAYABLE IN RESPECT OF THAT FUND ON JANUARY 29, 2021.
