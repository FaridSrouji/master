Scopely, Inc. announced that it has received $200 million in funding from a group of investors. Scopely, Inc. announced that it has received $200,000,000 in a round of funding led by new investor NewView Capital on October 29, 2019. The transaction also included participation from new investors Canada Pension Plan Investment Board, Baillie Gifford & Co., BlackRock, Inc. (NYSE:BLK),  returning investors Greycroft, Revolution Growth, and Sands Capital Ventures, LLC. The company raised the round at a post money valuation of more than $1,700,000,000.
