Blackrock Plans $1.1 Billion Stock Repurchase. BLACKROCK ANNOUNCES SECONDARY OFFERING OF ITS COMMON STOCK HELD BY PNC; PLANS $1.1 BILLION STOCK REPURCHASE.BLACKROCK - COMMENCEMENT OF SECONDARY OFFERING OF COMMON STOCK THROUGH WHICH PNC FINANCIAL SERVICES GROUP INTENDS TO EXIT ITS FULL INVESTMENT IN CO.BLACKROCK INC - SHARES BEING OFFERED BY PNC WILL BE SOLD IN AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC SECONDARY OFFERING.BLACKROCK INC - INTENDS TO REPURCHASE $1.1 BILLION OF COMMON STOCK DIRECTLY FROM PNC.BLACKROCK - PURCHASE PRICE TO BE PAID TO REPURCHASE COMMON STOCK FROM PNC WOULD BE EQUAL TO NET PRICE PER SHARE THAT PNC RECEIVES IN SECONDARY OFFERING.
