BlackRock® Canada Announces Management Fee Reduction for Certain iShares Exchange-Traded Funds. TORONTO, MARCH 31, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (NYSE:BLK) AND MANAGER OF THE ISHARES CANADIAN CORPORATE BOND INDEX ETF (“XCB”) AND THE ISHARES CANADIAN GOVERNMENT BOND INDEX ETF (“XGB”, AND TOGETHER WITH XCB, THE “ISHARES ETFS”), ANNOUNCED TODAY REDUCTIONS TO THE ANNUAL MANAGEMENT FEES OF EACH ISHARES ETF EFFECTIVE APRIL 1, 2021.
