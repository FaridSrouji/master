Unqork, Inc. announced that it has received $80 million in funding from CapitalG, BlackRock, Inc., Summerfield Capital Management, Llc, Goldman Sachs Principal Strategies, and other investors. Unqork, Inc announced that it has received $80 million in a round of funding led by new investor CapitalG on October 3, 2019. The transaction also included participation from existing investors including Summerfield Capital Management, Llc and Goldman Sachs Principal Strategies, and new investors, funds and accounts under management by subsidiaries of BlackRock, Inc. (NYSE:BLK). The company has raised more than $110 million in funding till date.
