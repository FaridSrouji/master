Blackrock Declares Quarterly Dividend Of $3.30 On Common Stock. BLACKROCK DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $3.30 ON COMMON STOCK.
BlackRock, Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 23, 2019. BlackRock, Inc. announced that its board of directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $3.30 per share of common stock, payable on December 23, 2019 to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 6, 2019.
