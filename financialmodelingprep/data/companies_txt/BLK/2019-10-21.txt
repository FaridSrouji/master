Arcutis, Inc. announced that it has received $94.5 million in funding from a group of investors. On October 21, 2019, Arcutis, Inc. closed the transaction. The company has received $94,500,000 in its series C round of funding from led by new investor HBM Healthcare Investments AG (SWX:HBMN), a fund managed by HBM Partners Ltd. The transaction also included participation from new investors BlackRock, Inc. (NYSE:BLK), Goldman Sachs Group, Merchant Banking Division, Pivotal BioVenture Partners LLC, Omega Fund Management, LLC, and Vivo Capital, LLC and existing investors Bain Capital Life Sciences Fund, L.P., a fund managed by Bain Capital Life Sciences Investors, LLC, Frazier Healthcare Partners, OrbiMed Advisors LLC, and RA Capital Management, LLC. New investors invested more than 50% of investment and rest was contributed by existing investors. Alexander Asam of HBM Partners Ltd. joined company’s board of directors. The company has received more than $160,000,000 in funding date. The company received $94,499,967 from 16 investors pursuant to exemption provided under Regulation D.
