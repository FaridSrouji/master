Press Release - Tailored Brands To Sell The Joseph Abboud Trademarks To Whp Global For $115 Mln. PRESS RELEASE - TAILORED BRANDS TO SELL THE JOSEPH ABBOUD TRADEMARKS TO WHP GLOBAL FOR $115 MILLION.TAILORED BRANDS INC - TO RETAIN EXCLUSIVE RIGHTS TO SELL AND RENT JOSEPH ABBOUD BRAND IN CERTAIN APPAREL AND ACCESSORIES CATEGORIES IN U.S. AND CANADA.TAILORED BRANDS - FOLLOWING CLOSE OF DEAL, WHP WILL HAVE OVER $1.4 BILLION IN RETAIL SALES ACROSS ITS PORTFOLIO OF BRANDS.TAILORED BRANDS INC - DESIGNER JOSEPH ABBOUD, WILL BE LEAVING CO AT END OF JANUARY.TAILORED BRANDS - WHP GLOBAL IS BACKED BY FUNDS MANAGED BY OAKTREE CAPITAL MANAGEMENT & WITH FINANCING FROM BLACKROCK.
