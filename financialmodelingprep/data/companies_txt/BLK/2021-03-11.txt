RBC iShares Expands its Sustainable ETF Platform. TORONTO, MARCH 11, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TODAY, RBC ISHARES EXPANDED ITS EXCHANGE TRADED FUND (“ETF”) LINEUP WITH THE LAUNCH OF THREE NEW SUSTAINABLE ETFS, THAT ARE EXPECTED TO BEGIN TRADING ON THE TORONTO STOCK EXCHANGE TODAY. THE ISHARES ESG MSCI LEADERS INDEX ETFS, WHICH JOIN THE RBC ISHARES SUITE OF SUSTAINABLE ETFS, WILL BE MANAGED BY BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT, WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC.
