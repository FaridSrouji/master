BlackRock® Canada Updates the Risk Rating for the Dynamic Active Preferred Shares ETF. TORONTO, JAN. 26, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BLACKROCK ASSET MANAGEMENT CANADA LIMITED (“BLACKROCK CANADA”), AN INDIRECT WHOLLY-OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF BLACKROCK, INC. (“BLACKROCK”) (NYSE: BLK), TODAY ANNOUNCED AN UPDATE TO THE RISK RATING OF THE DYNAMIC ACTIVE PREFERRED SHARES ETF (“DXP”). THIS CHANGE WILL BE REFLECTED IN THE PROSPECTUS FOR DXP THAT WILL BE FILED ON OR ABOUT JANUARY 26, 2021.
