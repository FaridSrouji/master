NortonLifeLock Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 18, 2019. The Board of Directors of NortonLifeLock Inc. declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.125 per common share (67% raised) to be paid on December 18, 2019, to all shareholders of record as of the close of business on November 25, 2019.
