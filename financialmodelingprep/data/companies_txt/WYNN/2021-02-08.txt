Wynn Resorts Announces Pricing of Public Offering of Common Stock. LAS VEGAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--WYNN RESORTS, LIMITED (NASDAQ: WYNN) (“WYNN RESORTS”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS FOLLOW-ON OFFERING OF 6,500,000 SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK IN AN UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING, WHICH REPRESENTS AN INCREASE OF 1,000,000 SHARES OF COMMON STOCK IN THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED SIZE OF THE OFFERING, AT A PRICE TO THE PUBLIC OF $115.00 PER SHARE.
