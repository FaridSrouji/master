WynnBET Partnering With San Carlos Apache Tribe To Pursue Online Sports Betting License In Arizona. LAS VEGAS, JULY 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WYNNBET, THE PREMIER CASINO AND SPORTS BETTING APP FROM THE GLOBAL LEADER IN LUXURY HOSPITALITY, WYNN RESORTS, ANNOUNCED ON FRIDAY IT HAS PARTNERED WITH THE SAN CARLOS APACHE TRIBE TO PURSUE AN ONLINE SPORTS BETTING LICENSE IN ARIZONA. THIS MARKS THE 16TH STATE ADDED TO THE RAPIDLY GROWING LIST OF JURISDICTIONS WHERE WYNNBET OPERATES OR PLANS TO LAUNCH.
