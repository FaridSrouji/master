NASCAR Authorized Gaming Operator WynnBET Launches Mobile App as New Virginia Online Sports Betting Permit Holder. RICHMOND, VA., MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NASCAR PARTNER WYNNBET, THE DIGITAL GAMING DIVISION OF WYNN RESORTS, HAS LAUNCHED THE WYNNBET MOBILE APP IN THE COMMONWEALTH OF VIRGINIA IN CONJUNCTION WITH BEING AWARDED AN ONLINE SPORTS BETTING PERMIT IN THE STATE.
