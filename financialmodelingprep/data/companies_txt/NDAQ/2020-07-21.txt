Singapore Exchange Says SGX Regco And Nasdaq Announce Regulatory Cooperation. SGX REGCO AND NASDAQ ANNOUNCE REGULATORY COOPERATION TO HELP COMPANIES ACCESS CAPITAL.SINGAPORE EXCHANGE REGULATION  AND NASDAQ HAVE EXTENDED THEIR PARTNERSHIP BY ENTERING AN AGREEMENT.LATEST COOPERATION AGREEMENT WILL FACILITATE REGULATORY EXCHANGE OF INFORMATION ON ISSUERS WHICH ARE DUAL LISTED ON BOTH EXCHANGES.
