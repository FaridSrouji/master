Nasdaq Named to 2021 Bloomberg Gender-Equality Index. NEW YORK, JAN. 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ, INC. TODAY ANNOUNCED ITS INCLUSION IN THE 2021 BLOOMBERG GENDER-EQUALITY INDEX (GEI) FOR THE FIRST TIME. THE GEI BRINGS TRANSPARENCY TO GENDER-RELATED PRACTICES AND POLICIES AT PUBLICLY LISTED COMPANIES INCREASING THE BREADTH OF ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, GOVERNANCE (ESG) DATA AVAILABLE TO INVESTORS.
