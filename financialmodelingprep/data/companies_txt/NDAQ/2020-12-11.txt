Annual Changes to the Nasdaq Biotechnology Index. NEW YORK, DEC. 11, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RESULTS OF THE ANNUAL RECONSTITUTION OF THE NASDAQ BIOTECHNOLOGY INDEX (NASDAQ: NBI), WHICH WILL BECOME EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO MARKET OPEN ON MONDAY, DECEMBER 21, 2020.
