Nasdaq Named to Dow Jones Sustainability Index for the Fifth Consecutive Year. NEW YORK, NOV. 20, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS BEEN SELECTED AS A NORTH AMERICAN INDEX COMPONENT OF THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX (DJSI), ONE OF THE MOST PRESTIGIOUS ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL, AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) RANKING EFFORTS, FOR THE FIFTH CONSECUTIVE YEAR. NASDAQ MAINTAINS ITS INDUSTRY LEADERSHIP AS THE ONLY STOCK EXCHANGE OPERATOR SELECTED FOR INCLUSION IN THE NORTH AMERICA INDEX, AND WAS AMONG EIGHT DIVERSIFIED FINANCIAL SERVICES COMPANIES SELECTED FROM THE REGION THIS YEAR.
