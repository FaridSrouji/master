Saudi Stock Exchange (Tadawul): :SAUDI STOCK EXCHANGE (TADAWUL) LAUNCHES NEW DERIVATIVES MARKET AND CLEARINGHOUSE LEVERAGING NASDAQ MARKET TECHNOLOGY.OFFICIALLY LAUNCHED ITS FIRST DERIVATIVES MARKET AND A CLEARINGHOUSE, SECURITIES CLEARING CENTRE COMPANY (MUQASSA).PLANS TO ROLL OUT MORE DERIVATIVES PRODUCTS SUCH AS SINGLE STOCK FUTURES, SINGLE STOCK OPTIONS AND INDEX OPTIONS.
