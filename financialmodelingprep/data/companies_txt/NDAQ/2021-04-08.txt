Nasdaq March 2021 Volumes and 1Q21 Statistics. NEW YORK, APRIL 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NASDAQ (NASDAQ: NDAQ) TODAY REPORTED MONTHLY VOLUMES FOR MARCH 2021, AS WELL AS QUARTERLY VOLUMES, ESTIMATED REVENUE CAPTURE, NUMBER OF LISTINGS AND INDEX STATISTICS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021 ON ITS INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE. A DATA SHEET SHOWING THE MONTHLY VOLUMES AND QUARTERLY CAPTURE RATES CAN BE FOUND AT: HTTP://IR.NASDAQ.COM/FINANCIALS/VOLUME-STATISTICS.
