Nasdaq Announces Mid-Month Open Short Interest Positions in Nasdaq Stocks as of Settlement Date November 13, 2020. NEW YORK, NOV. 24, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF NOVEMBER 13, 2020, SHORT INTEREST IN 2,541 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKETSM SECURITIES TOTALED 8,236,111,320 SHARES COMPARED WITH 8,257,390,516 SHARES IN 2,529 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF OCTOBER 30, 2020. THE MID-AUGUST SHORT INTEREST REPRESENTS 3.03 DAYS COMPARED WITH 3.43 DAYS FOR THE PRIOR REPORTING PERIOD.
