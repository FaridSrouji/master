Luckin Coffee Receives Delisting Notice From Nasdaq. LUCKIN COFFEE RECEIVES DELISTING NOTICE FROM NASDAQ AND PLANS TO REQUEST HEARING.LUCKIN COFFEE - WILL REMAIN LISTED ON NASDAQ, PENDING THE OUTCOME OF THE HEARING..LUCKIN COFFEE INC - PLANS TO TIMELY REQUEST HEARING BEFORE NASDAQ HEARINGS PANEL.LUCKIN COFFEE - HEARING WILL TYPICALLY BE SCHEDULED TO OCCUR ABOUT 30 TO 45 DAYS AFTER THE DATE OF THE HEARING REQUEST.LUCKIN COFFEE INC - LISTING QUALIFICATIONS STAFF CITED TWO BASES FOR DELISTING DETERMINATION.LUCKIN COFFEE - BASES CITED FOR DELISTING INCLUDE PUBLIC INTEREST CONCERNS AS RAISED BY FABRICATED TRANSACTIONS DISCLOSED BY CO ON APRIL 2.LUCKIN COFFEE - BASES CITED FOR DELISTING INCLUDE COMPANY’S PAST FAILURE TO PUBLICLY DISCLOSE MATERIAL INFORMATION.
