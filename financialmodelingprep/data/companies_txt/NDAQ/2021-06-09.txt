Nasdaq Announces End-of-Month Open Short Interest Positions in Nasdaq Stocks as of Settlement Date May 28, 2021. NEW YORK, JUNE 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF MAY 28, 2021, SHORT INTEREST IN 2,654 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKET℠ SECURITIES TOTALED 8,683,450,119 SHARES COMPARED WITH 8,678,246,449 SHARES IN 2,645 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF MAY 14, 2021. THE END-OF-MAY SHORT INTEREST REPRESENT 3.19 DAYS AVERAGE DAILY NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKET SHARE VOLUME FOR THE REPORTING PERIOD, COMPARED WITH 3.11 DAYS FOR THE PRIOR REPORTING PERIOD.
