Nasdaq Announces Mid-Month Open Short Interest Positions in Nasdaq Stocks as of Settlement Date March 15, 2021. NEW YORK, MARCH 24, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AT THE END OF THE SETTLEMENT DATE OF MARCH 15, 2021, SHORT INTEREST IN 2,610 NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKETSM SECURITIES TOTALED 8,163,912,977 SHARES COMPARED WITH 7,742,433,178 SHARES IN 2,614 GLOBAL MARKET ISSUES REPORTED FOR THE PRIOR SETTLEMENT DATE OF FEBRUARY 26, 2021. THE MID-MARCH SHORT INTEREST REPRESENTS 2.24 DAYS COMPARED WITH 2.31 DAYS FOR THE PRIOR REPORTING PERIOD.
