Nasdaq Resumes Trading in Generations Bancorp NY, Inc.. NEW YORK, JAN. 15, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE NASDAQ STOCK MARKET® (NASDAQ: NDAQ) ANNOUNCED THAT TRADING RESUMED TODAY IN GENERATIONS BANCORP NY, INC. (NASDAQ: GBNY) AT 8:15 A.M. EASTERN TIME ON JANUARY 15, 2021. TRADING IN THE COMPANY'S STOCK WAS HALTED ON JANUARY 14, 2021 AT 17:10:16 EASTERN TIME.
