Providence Partners with Edwards Lifesciences, Cities of Irvine, Santa Ana, Costa Mesa and Tustin to Open Two Free Mass COVID-19 Vaccination Clinics. ORANGE COUNTY, CALIF., APRIL 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PROVIDENCE, A NATIONAL NONPROFIT HEALTH SYSTEM, IS PARTNERING WITH EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES (NYSE: EW), THE ORANGE COUNTY-BASED MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, AND THE CITIES OF IRVINE, SANTA ANA, COSTA MESA AND TUSTIN TO OPEN TWO MASS VACCINATION CLINICS AT ONE OF EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES' CAMPUS BUILDINGS IN SANTA ANA AND THE ORANGE COUNTY GREAT PARK IN IRVINE, PROVIDING CONVENIENT ACCESS FOR RESIDENTS TO PROTECT THEMSELVES FROM COVID-19.
