Edwards Lifesciences Corporation Invests $100 Million to Expand Its Operations in Costa Rica. Edwards Lifesciences Corporation expanded its presence in Costa Rica with a $100 million investment and the addition of 500 new jobs by the end of 2020. The brand new facility is located in La Lima Industrial Park in Cartago and has an extension of 274,000 square feet. This new building joins the existing one within the same industrial park to collectively expand the capability to produce finished products, in addition to the processes that has been on operation since 2017.
