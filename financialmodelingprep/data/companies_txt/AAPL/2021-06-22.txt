Apple Tower Theatre Opens Thursday in Downtown Los Angeles. LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--APPLE® TODAY PREVIEWED ITS NEWEST RETAIL LOCATION AT THE HISTORIC TOWER THEATRE IN DOWNTOWN LOS ANGELES.
