Goldman Sachs Says Activated Global Business Continuity Plan, With About 98% Of Global Employees Working Remotely. GOLDMAN SACHS - ACTIVATED GLOBAL BUSINESS CONTINUITY PLAN, WITH ABOUT 98% OF GLOBAL EMPLOYEES WORKING REMOTELY.GOLDMAN SACHS - INTRODUCED TELEMEDICINE BENEFIT TO EMPLOYEES AND COVERED DEPENDENTS; WAIVING ALL COSTS FOR 2020.GOLDMAN SACHS - COVID-19 ASSISTANCE PROGRAM, GIVES FLEXIBILITY TO DEFER MARCUS LOAN OR APPLE CARD PAYMENT FOR UP TO 2 MONTHS AT NO COST TO CUSTOMERS.GOLDMAN SACHS - COVID-19 ASSISTANCE PROGRAM, ALSO GIVES FLEXIBILITY TO ACCESS FUNDS IN MARCUS CDS EARLY WITH NO PENALTY.GOLDMAN SACHS - BOOKRUNNER ON $200 PLUS BILLION OF TOTAL INVESTMENT GRADE ISSUANCE IN Q1.
