Epic Games Says Apple To Cut Epic Off From iOS, Mac Development Tools. EPIC GAMES - APPLE INFORMED CO THAT ON AUG. 28, IT WILL TERMINATE ALL DEVELOPER ACCOUNTS AND CUT EPIC OFF FROM IOS, MAC DEVELOPMENT TOOLS.EPIC GAMES - SEEKING PRELIMINARY INJUNCTION RESTRAINING APPLE FROM REMOVING, DE-LISTING, OR OTHERWISE MAKING UNAVAILABLE FORTNITE FROM THE APP STORE.EPIC GAMES SAYS SEEKING A TEMPORARY RESTRAINING ORDER AGAINST APPLE.
