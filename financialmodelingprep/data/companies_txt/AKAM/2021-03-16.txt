Akamai and Queue-it team up to deliver a cure for COVID vaccine registration frustration. CAMBRIDGE, MASS., MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AKAMAI TECHNOLOGIES, INC. (NASDAQ: AKAM), THE WORLD'S MOST TRUSTED SOLUTION FOR PROTECTING AND DELIVERING DIGITAL EXPERIENCES, TODAY ANNOUNCES A STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP WITH QUEUE-IT, A LEADER IN VIRTUAL WAITING ROOM SOFTWARE AND SERVICES, TO MEET THE HISTORIC CHALLENGE POSED BY COVID-19 VACCINATION.
