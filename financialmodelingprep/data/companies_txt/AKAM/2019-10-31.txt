Akamai Technologies, Inc. to Officially Open its New Global Headquarters at 145 Broadway on November 1, 2019. Akamai Technologies, Inc. will officially open its new global headquarters at 145 Broadway on November 1, 2019. The building was developed and is owned by Boston Properties which has granted Akamai a 15-year lease. The architect for the interior work was Watertown-based Sasaki. The general contractor for the interior buildout was the Boston office of Turner Construction.
