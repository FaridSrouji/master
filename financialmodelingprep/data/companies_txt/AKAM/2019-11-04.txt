Akamai Technologies, Inc. (NasdaqGS:AKAM) completed the acquisition of Exceda Ltda.. Akamai Technologies, Inc. (NasdaqGS:AKAM) entered into an agreement to acquire Exceda Ltda on September 9, 2019. The all-cash transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2019. The transaction is expected to be $0.01 dilutive to its non-GAAP earnings per share for 2019, and $0.01 - 0.02 accretive in 2020. The acquisition is not expected to impact Akamai's previously-stated goal of achieving a non-GAAP operating margin of 30% by 2020. Marcello Hallake of Jones Day acted as legal advisor to Exceda Ltda.
Akamai Technologies, Inc. (NasdaqGS:AKAM) completed the acquisition of Exceda Ltda for $33 million on November 4, 2019.
