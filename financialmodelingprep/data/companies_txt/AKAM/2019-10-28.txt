Akamai Technologies, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Akamai Technologies, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 709.912 million compared to USD 669.628 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 143.289 million compared to USD 116.537 million a year ago. Net income was USD 137.890 million compared to USD 107.583 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.85 compared to USD 0.65 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.84 compared to USD 0.64 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 2,121.494 million compared to USD 2,001.111 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 413.596 million compared to USD 242.211 million a year ago. Net income was USD 358.935 million compared to USD 204.358 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.2 compared to USD 1.21 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.18 compared to USD 1.2 a year ago.
