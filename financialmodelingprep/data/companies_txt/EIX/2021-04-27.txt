Edison International Reports First Quarter 2021 Results. ROSEMEAD, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--EDISON INTERNATIONAL (NYSE: EIX) TODAY REPORTED FIRST QUARTER 2021 NET INCOME OF $259 MILLION, OR $0.68 PER SHARE, COMPARED TO NET INCOME OF $183 MILLION, OR $0.50 PER SHARE, IN THE FIRST QUARTER 2020. AS ADJUSTED, FIRST QUARTER 2021 CORE EARNINGS WERE $301 MILLION, OR $0.79 PER SHARE, COMPARED TO CORE EARNINGS OF $228 MILLION, OR $0.63 PER SHARE, IN THE FIRST QUARTER 2020.
