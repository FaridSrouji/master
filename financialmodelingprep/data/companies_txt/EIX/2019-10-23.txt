Carey A. Smith Joins Edison International and Southern California Edison’s Boards of Directors. Edison International and Southern California Edison announced that Carey A. Smith has been elected to the board of directors of each company, effective Oct. 24, 2019. Smith is COO at Parsons Corporation. Previously, Smith served in progressive leadership roles at Honeywell, including president of its defense andspace division, vice president of Honeywell Aerospace customer and product support and president of Honeywell Technology Solutions Inc. Smith serves on the board of the Professional Services Council and will continue on the board of NN Inc. until Nov. 14.
