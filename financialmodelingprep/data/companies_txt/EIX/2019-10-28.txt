Edison International Revises Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019. Edison International revised earnings guidance for the year 2019. For the year, the company now expects basic EPS in a range of $4.41 to $4.61 compared to $4.46 to $4.66 expected previously as of July 25, 2019.
