Federal Realty Investment Trust Declares Regular Quarterly Dividends, Payable on January 15, 2020. Federal Realty's Board of Trustees declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $1.05 per common share, resulting in an indicated annual rate of $4.20 per common share. The regular common dividend will be payable on January 15, 2020 to common shareholders of record as of January 2, 2020. Federal Realty's Board of Trustees also declared a quarterly cash dividend on its Class C depositary shares, each representing 1/1000 of a 5.000% Series C Cumulative Preferred Share of Beneficial Interest, of $0.3125 per depositary share. All dividends on the depositary shares will be payable on January 15, 2020 to shareholders of record as of January 2, 2020.
