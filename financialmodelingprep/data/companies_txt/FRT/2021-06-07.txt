Federal Realty Investment Trust Announces Acquisition of Four Properties. NORTH BETHESDA, MD., JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FEDERAL REALTY INVESTMENT TRUST (NYSE:FRT) HAS ACQUIRED GROSSMONT SHOPPING CENTER IN GREATER SAN DIEGO, CALIFORNIA AND CHESTERBROOK SHOPPING CENTER IN MCLEAN, VIRGINIA AND HAS ENTERED INTO A BINDING CONTRACT TO ACQUIRE CAMELBACK COLONNADE AND HILTON VILLAGE IN THE PHOENIX METRO AREA.
