CVS Health Corporation Announces Quarterly Dividend. WOONSOCKET, R.I., MARCH 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH CORPORATION (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.50 (50 CENTS) PER SHARE ON THE CORPORATION'S COMMON STOCK.
