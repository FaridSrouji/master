COVID-19 Vaccine Appointments Now Available for Adolescents Ages 12 to 15 at Select CVS Pharmacy Locations Nationwide. WOONSOCKET, R.I., MAY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) ANNOUNCED THAT COVID-19 VACCINE APPOINTMENTS FOR ADOLESCENTS AGES 12 TO 15 ARE NOW AVAILABLE FOR SCHEDULING AT MORE THAN 5,600 CVS PHARMACY LOCATIONS NATIONWIDE, FOLLOWING FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) EMERGENCY USE AUTHORIZATION OF THE PFIZER-BIONTECH VACCINE FOR THIS AGE GROUP.
