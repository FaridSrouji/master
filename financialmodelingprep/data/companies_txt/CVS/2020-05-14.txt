CVS Health Says Expects To Establish Up To 1,000 Covid-19 Test Sites By End Of May. CVS HEALTH - EXPECTS TO TO ESTABLISH UP TO 1,000 COVID-19 TEST SITES BY END OF MAY.CVS HEALTH - WILL OPEN MORE THAN 50 COVID-19 TEST SITES AT SELECT DRIVE-THRU LOCATIONS IN ARIZONA, CONNECTICUT, FLORIDA, MASSACHUSETTS & PENNSYLVANIA.CVS HEALTH -  OPENING OF HUNDREDS OF ADDITIONAL TEST SITES ACROSS U.S. WILL BE ANNOUNCED OVER NEXT 2 WEEKS.CVS HEALTH - COVID-19 TESTS WILL BE SENT TO AN INDEPENDENT, THIRD-PARTY LAB FOR PROCESSING & RESULTS WILL BE AVAILABLE IN ABOUT 3 DAYS.CVS HEALTH - COVID-19 TESTING WILL NOT TAKE PLACE INSIDE ANY RETAIL LOCATIONS.CVS HEALTH - OPERATES RAPID TEST SITES IN COORDINATION WITH 5 STATES, WHICH CAN PROCESS NEARLY 30,000 COVID-19 TESTS PER WEEK.
