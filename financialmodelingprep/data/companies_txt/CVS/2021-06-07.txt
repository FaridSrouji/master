CVS Health Completes Rollout of Time Delay Safes in All of Its Georgia Pharmacies. WOONSOCKET, R.I., JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS PART OF AN ONGOING COMMITMENT TO HELPING BUILD HEALTHIER AND SAFER COMMUNITIES, CVS HEALTH (NYSE: CVS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPLETED INSTALLATION OF TIME DELAY SAFE TECHNOLOGY IN ALL 355 GEORGIA CVS PHARMACY LOCATIONS, INCLUDING THOSE IN TARGET STORES.
