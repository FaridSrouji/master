New sustainability reports demonstrate Ameren's commitment to a cleaner energy future. ST. LOUIS, MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE) ANNOUNCED THE PUBLICATION OF FIVE DATA-DRIVEN REPORTS SHOWING THE COMPANY'S COMMITMENT TO A CLEANER, SUSTAINABLE ENERGY FUTURE, INCLUDING ITS PLANS TO ACHIEVE NET-ZERO CARBON EMISSIONS BY 2050. THE REPORTS, UTILIZING THE FRAMEWORKS MOST SOUGHT AFTER BY INVESTORS AND OTHER STAKEHOLDERS, QUANTIFY AND PROVIDE FURTHER INSIGHT INTO AMEREN'S APPROACH TO IMPORTANT ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE (ESG) ISSUES.
