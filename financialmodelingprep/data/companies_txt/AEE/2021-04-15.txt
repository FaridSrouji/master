Ameren Earns 2021 Great Place to Work Certification™. ST. LOUIS, APRIL 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOR THE FIFTH YEAR IN A ROW, AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE) HAS BEEN CERTIFIED™ BY GREAT PLACE TO WORK®. THE PRESTIGIOUS AWARD IS BASED ENTIRELY ON WHAT CURRENT EMPLOYEES SAY ABOUT THEIR EXPERIENCE WORKING AT AMEREN.
