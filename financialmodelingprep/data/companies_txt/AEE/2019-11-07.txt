Ameren Corporation Revises Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019. Ameren Corporation revised earnings guidance for the year 2019. The company narrowed its 2019 earnings guidance range to $3.23 to $3.33 per diluted share, compared to the prior range of $3.15 to $3.35 per diluted share.
