Cynthia J. Brinkley Joins Ameren Corporation Board of Directors. Ameren Corporation announced that Cynthia J. Brinkley has been elected to the Ameren board of directors effective immediately. Prior to joining Centene, Brinkley served as vice president of global human resources at General Motors Company from 2011 to 2013. She has also held various leadership roles at AT&T Inc., including senior vice president of talent development, chief diversity officer and president of AT&T Missouri.
