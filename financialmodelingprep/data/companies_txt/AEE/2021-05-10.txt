Ameren Announces First Quarter 2021 Results. ST. LOUIS, MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMEREN CORPORATION (NYSE: AEE) TODAY ANNOUNCED FIRST QUARTER 2021 NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $233 MILLION, OR $0.91 PER DILUTED SHARE, COMPARED TO FIRST QUARTER 2020 NET INCOME ATTRIBUTABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $146 MILLION, OR $0.59 PER DILUTED SHARE. FIRST QUARTER 2021 RESULTS REFLECTED EARNINGS ON INCREASED INFRASTRUCTURE INVESTMENTS MADE ACROSS ALL BUSINESS SEGMENTS DRIVEN BY SOLID EXECUTION OF THE COMPANY'S STRATEGY.
