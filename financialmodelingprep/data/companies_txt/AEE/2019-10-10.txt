Ameren Corporation Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable December 31, 2019. The board of directors of Ameren Corporation declared a quarterly cash dividend on its common stock of 49.5 cents per share, a 4.2% increase from the prior quarterly cash dividend of 47.5 cents per share, resulting in an annualized equivalent dividend rate of $1.98 per share. The previous annualized equivalent dividend rate was $1.90 per share. The common share dividend is payable December 31, 2019, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 11, 2019.
