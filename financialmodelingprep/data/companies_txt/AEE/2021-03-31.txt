Ameren Missouri requests 2022 rate adjustments as it continues major upgrades to bolster electric and natural gas systems. ST. LOUIS, MARCH 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AMEREN MISSOURI FILED TODAY WITH THE MISSOURI PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION (PSC) REQUESTS TO ADJUST ITS ELECTRIC AND NATURAL GAS BASE RATES NEXT YEAR. THE NEW RATES WOULD TAKE EFFECT IN 2022 TO REFLECT MAJOR UPGRADES TO ELECTRIC AND NATURAL GAS SYSTEM RELIABILITY AND RESILIENCY FOR CUSTOMERS, AS WELL AS INVESTMENTS TO SUPPORT THE TRANSITION TO CLEANER ENERGY FOR THE BENEFIT OF CUSTOMERS AND LOCAL COMMUNITIES.
