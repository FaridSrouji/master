The Cincinnati Insurance Company Partners with Cambridge Mobile Telematics to Reduce Distracted Driving. CINCINNATI, MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CINF) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS LEAD PROPERTY CASUALTY INSURANCE SUBSIDIARY, THE CINCINNATI INSURANCE COMPANY, HAS LAUNCHED RIDEWELLSM, A DEDICATED PROGRAM TO IMPROVE ALL-AGE DRIVERS' SAFETY, IN THE ADDITIONAL STATES OF ILLINOIS, NEW JERSEY AND WEST VIRGINIA. IN PARTNERSHIP WITH CAMBRIDGE MOBILE TELEMATICS (CMT), THE WORLD'S LEADING MOBILE TELEMATICS AND ANALYTICS PROVIDER, CINCINNATI BUILT THE NEW APP TO FOSTER PEACE OF MIND FOR PARENTS AND TO ENCOURAGE SAFE DRIVING BEHAVIORS FOR ALL DRIVERS.
