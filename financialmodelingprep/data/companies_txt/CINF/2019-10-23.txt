Cincinnati Financial Corporation Announces Unaudited Consolidated Earnings Results for the Third Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2019. Cincinnati Financial Corporation announced unaudited consolidated earnings results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 1,700 million compared to USD 1,915 million a year ago. Net income was USD 248 million compared to USD 553 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.51 compared to USD 3.4 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.49 compared to USD 3.38 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 5,772 million compared to USD 4,697 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,371 million compared to USD 739 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 8.4 compared to USD 4.53 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 8.3 compared to USD 4.49 a year ago.
