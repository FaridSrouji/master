Cincinnati Financial Corporation Declares Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend. CINCINNATI, NOV. 13, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CINCINNATI FINANCIAL CORPORATION (NASDAQ: CINF) ANNOUNCED THAT, AT TODAY'S REGULAR MEETING, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A 60-CENTS-PER-SHARE REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND.
