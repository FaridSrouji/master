Cincinnati Financial Corporation Declares Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on January 15, 2020. Cincinnati Financial Corporation announced that, at it's regular meeting, the board of directors declared a 56 cents per share regular quarterly cash dividend. The dividend is payable January 15, 2020, to shareholders of record as of December 19, 2019.
