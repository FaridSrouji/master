The Coca-Cola Group Announces Earnings Results for the Third Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 2019. The Coca-Cola group announced earnings results for the nine months ended September 2019. For the period, the company reported net profit of $6.878 million. Coca-Cola increased its turnover by 6% between January and September, to $28.198 billion. Per share, Coca-Cola earned $0.60 versus $0.44 a year ago.Turnover between July and September amounted to $9.507 billion, up 8% from $8.775 billion in the same period last year.
Coca-Cola Sets Quarterly Dividend Of $0.40 Per Share. COCA-COLA BOARD OF DIRECTORS ELECTS COMPANY OFFICER AND DECLARES REGULAR QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.SETS QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.40 PER SHARE.ANNOUNCED ELECTION OF ONE NEW OFFICER.GILLES LECLERC, WHO BECAME PRESIDENT OF MCDONALD'S DIVISION OF COCA-COLA COMPANY ON JAN. 7, HAS BEEN ELECTED BY BOARD AS A VICE PRESIDENT.GILLES LECLERC PREVIOUSLY SERVED AS VICE PRESIDENT, MCDONALD'S DIVISION INTERNATIONAL.
