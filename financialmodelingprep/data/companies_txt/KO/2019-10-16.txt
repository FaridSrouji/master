The Coca-Cola Company Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 16, 2019. The Coca-Cola Company declared a regular quarterly dividend of 40 cents per common share. The dividend is payable Dec. 16, 2019, to shareowners of record of the company as of the close of business on Dec. 2, 2019.
