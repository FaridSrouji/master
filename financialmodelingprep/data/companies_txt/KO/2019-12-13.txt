Coca Cola HBC Enters Into Deal With Coca-Cola Co's Unit European Refreshments. COCA-COLA HBC AG  - JOINT VENTURE.COCA COLA HBC AG - THERE IS NO CONSIDERATION BEING PAID OR RECEIVED BY COCA-COLA HBC.COCA COLA HBC AG - ENTERED INTO A BINDING AGREEMENT FOR CONTRIBUTION OF ASSETS RELATING TO MULTON Z.A.O GROUP OF COMPANIES.COCA COLA HBC - DEAL FOR CONTRIBUTION OF ASSETS RELATING TO MULTON Z.A.O INTO A COMPANY INDIRECTLY OWNED BY CO & TCCC, EACH WITH A 50% EQUITY INTEREST.COCA COLA HBC - DEAL IS CONTRIBUTION OF BUSINESS ASSETS FOR ARRANGEMENT PRESERVING RESPECTIVE EXISTING BUSINESS AND COMMERCIAL INTERESTS OF PARTIES.COCA COLA HBC AG - THERE IS NO TRANSFER OF ECONOMIC VALUE BETWEEN COCA-COLA HBC AND TCCC.COCA COLA HBC AG - EXIT PROVISIONS AS PER DEAL SHALL APPLY TO ONGOING RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN COCA-COLA HBC AND TCCC..COCA COLA HBC - TRANSACTION HAS BEEN ENTERED INTO WITH EUROPEAN REFRESHMENTS WHICH IS A SUBSIDIARY OF TCCC A SUBSTANTIAL SHAREHOLDER OF COCA-COLA HBC.
