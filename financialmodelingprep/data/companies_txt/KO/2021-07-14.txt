Coca-Cola Board of Directors Elects Corporate Officer and Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TODAY DECLARED A DIVIDEND OF 42 CENTS PER COMMON SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE OCT. 1, 2021, TO SHAREOWNERS OF RECORD OF THE COMPANY AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON SEPT. 15, 2021. THE BOARD ALSO ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF SAADIA MADSBJERG AS A CORPORATE VICE PRESIDENT.
