The Jel Sert Company Announces The Launch Of POWERADE Sports Freezer Bars. WEST CHICAGO, ILL., APRIL 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE COCA-COLA COMPANY AND THE JEL SERT COMPANY, ONE OF THE WORLD'S LEADING MANUFACTURERS OF FREEZER BARS, ANNOUNCED THE NATIONAL ROLLOUT OF POWERADE SPORTS FREEZER BARS, OFFERING EVERYDAY ATHLETES A NEW WAY TO ENJOY POWERADE.
