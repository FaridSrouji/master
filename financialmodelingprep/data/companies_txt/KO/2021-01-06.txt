The Coca-Cola Company Names the Honorable J. Michael Luttig Counselor and Special Advisor. ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE COCA-COLA COMPANY TODAY NAMED J. MICHAEL LUTTIG, FORMER U.S. FEDERAL JUDGE AND GENERAL COUNSEL FOR THE BOEING CO., TO SERVE AS COUNSELOR AND SPECIAL ADVISOR TO THE COMPANY AND ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. JUDGE LUTTIG WILL ADVISE THE COMPANY AND ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS ON TAX MATTERS, WITH PARTICULAR FOCUS ON THE COMPANY'S ONGOING LITIGATION WITH THE U.S. INTERNAL REVENUE SERVICE.
