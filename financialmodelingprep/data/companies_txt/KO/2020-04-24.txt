Coca-Cola Says May Be Required To Record Significant Impairment Charges Due To Effects Of COVID-19 Pandemic. COCA-COLA, UNDER RISK FACTORS IN 10-Q FILING, SAYS MAY BE REQUIRED TO RECORD SIGNIFICANT IMPAIRMENT CHARGES DUE TO EFFECTS OF COVID-19 PANDEMIC.COCA-COLA SAYS MAY BE REQUIRED TO WRITE OFF OBSOLETE INVENTORY, UNAMORTIZED BALANCES OF FUNDING PROVIDED TO CUSTOMERS THAT CLOSE DUE TO PANDEMIC.
