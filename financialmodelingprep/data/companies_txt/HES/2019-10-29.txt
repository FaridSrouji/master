Hess Corporation Reports Production Results for the Third Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2019; Provides Production Guidance for 2019. Hess Corporation reported production results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, net production was 312,000 boepd against 297,000 boepd a year ago. For nine months, net production was 301,000 boepd against 273,000 boepd a year ago. The company provided production guidance for 2019. Net production guidance, excluding Libya, increased to approximately 285,000 boepd, up from the previous guidance range of 275,000 boepd to 280,000 boepd; Bakken net production guidance increased to approximately 150,000 boepd, up from the previous guidance range of 140,000 boepd to 145,000 boepd.
