Hess Corp Announces 2020 Exploration & Production Capital, Exploratory Budget Of $3 Bln. HESS CORP - 2020 EXPLORATION & PRODUCTION CAPITAL AND EXPLORATORY BUDGET OF $3.0 BILLION.HESS CORP - MORE THAN 80% OF 2020 EXPLORATION & PRODUCTION CAPITAL AND EXPLORATORY BUDGET WILL BE ALLOCATED TO INVESTMENTS IN GUYANA AND BAKKEN.HESS CORP - NET PRODUCTION IS FORECAST TO AVERAGE BETWEEN 330,000 AND 335,000 BARRELS OF OIL EQUIVALENT PER DAY IN 2020, EXCLUDING LIBYA.HESS CORP - BAKKEN NET PRODUCTION IS FORECAST TO AVERAGE APPROXIMATELY 180,000 BARRELS OF OIL EQUIVALENT PER DAY IN 2020.HESS CORP - IN BAKKEN, WE PLAN TO MAINTAIN A SIX RIG PROGRAM THROUGH 2020.HESS CORP - OFFSHORE GUYANA FOCUS IN 2020 WILL BE ON LIZA PHASE 2 DEVELOPMENT, AMONG OTHERS.HESS - TO CONTINUE INVESTING IN ACTIVE EXPLORATION & APPRAISAL PROGRAM IN GUYANA ON BOTH STABROEK & KAIETEUR BLOCKS AND IN DEEPWATER GULF OF MEXICO.HESS - 2020 E&P CAPEX BUDGET ALLOCATED AS: $1.69 BILLION FOR PRODUCTION, $860 MILLION FOR OFFSHORE GUYANA DEVELOPMENTS, $450 MILLION FOR EXPLORATION & APPRAISAL ACTIVITIES.HESS CORP - 2020 E&P CAPEX BUDGET ALLOCATED AS: $1.73 BILLION FOR UNITED STATES AND $1.27 BILLION FOR INTERNATIONAL E&P.
