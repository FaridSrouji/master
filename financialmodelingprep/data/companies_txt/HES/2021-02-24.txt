Hess Corporation Donates $2 Million to Houston's Winter Storm Relief Efforts. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HESS CORPORATION (NYSE: HES) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS DONATING $1 MILLION TO THE HOUSTON HARRIS COUNTY 2021 WINTER STORM RELIEF FUND AND $1 MILLION TO THE HOUSTON FOOD BANK FOLLOWING THE SEVERE WINTER STORM THAT HAS SIGNIFICANTLY IMPACTED COMMUNITIES ALREADY SUFFERING FROM THE ECONOMIC EFFECTS OF THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
