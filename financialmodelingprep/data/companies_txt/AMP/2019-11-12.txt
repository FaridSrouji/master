Ameriprise Financial Launches New Credit Cards with Compelling Value and Benefits for Clients. Ameriprise Financial, Inc. announced the launch of a new line of credit cards designed to bring value and benefits to clients. The Ameriprise Visa Signature and Premier Visa Signature Cards will provide a competitive, broad rewards program including cash back redemption rates of up to 1.875%, as well as travel, lifestyle and retail redemption options. Additional features include, up to 1.5 reward points earned per $1 spent on eligible purchases, no reward points cap, travel credits, enrollment bonuses and other features. Clients will be able to view credit card balances while logged into their Ameriprise accounts, enabling them to see more of their financial picture in one place.
