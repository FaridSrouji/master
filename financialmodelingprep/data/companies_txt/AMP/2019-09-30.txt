Amfam, Inc. completed its acquisition of IDS Property Casualty Insurance Company from Ameriprise Financial, Inc. (NYSE:AMP).. Amfam, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to acquire IDS Property Casualty Insurance Company from Ameriprise Financial, Inc. (NYSE:AMP) for $1.1 billion on April 1, 2019. The purchase price is subject to certain post-closing financial adjustments. IDS Property Casualty Insurance Company will become a subsidiary within the American Family Insurance group and operate under its existing name until a new name is identified and approved by regulators. All IDS Property employees along with it’s headquarter in De Pere, Wisconsin will be retained by American Family Insurance, parent company of Amfam. Current IDS Property customers will retain their policies with no change in coverage or service. The transaction is subject to regulatory approval and satisfaction of other customary closing conditions. The transaction expected to close in the second half of 2019. George Matsuzaka, Carlos Marque and Daniel Schleifman of Credit Suisse Securities (USA) LLC acted as financial advisors and Todd freed, Patrick Lewis and Christopher Ulery of Skadden, Arps, Slate, Meagher & Flom LLP acted as legal advisors to Ameriprise in the transaction. Anne E. Ross, Thomas R. Hrdlick and Maureen C. Easton of Foley & Lardner acted as legal advisors and Seth Bair, Peter Houston, Dan Bank and Elizabeth Durkin of Keefe, Bruyette, & Woods, Inc. acted as financial advisors to American Family insurance company.
Amfam, Inc. completed its acquisition of IDS Property Casualty Insurance Company from Ameriprise Financial, Inc. (NYSE:AMP) on October 1, 2019. After a payment to an affinity partner by Ameriprise, the net proceeds were approximately $1.035 billion, which is subject to certain post-closing financial adjustments. Jessie Stauffacher succeeds Tom Ealy as IDS president and chief executive officer. Tom Ealy is retiring from IDS but will continue as a consultant through year end. Sharena Ali was appointed to the new position of chief operating officer at IDS.
