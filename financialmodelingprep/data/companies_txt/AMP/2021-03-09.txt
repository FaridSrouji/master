Ninepoint Partners adds Convertible Securities Fund to Product Lineup. TORONTO, MARCH 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NINEPOINT PARTNERS LP, ONE OF CANADA'S LEADING ALTERNATIVE ASSET MANAGERS, IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THE LAUNCH OF THE NINEPOINT CONVERTIBLE SECURITIES FUND. THE FUND WILL OFFER CANADIAN INVESTORS A UNIQUE SOURCE OF INCOME AND POTENTIAL FOR CAPITAL APPRECIATION.
