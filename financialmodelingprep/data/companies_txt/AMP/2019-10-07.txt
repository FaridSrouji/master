Ameriprise Financial, Inc. Announces Board Appointments. Ameriprise Financial, Inc. announced that Mark Burton and Peter Bazirgan have joined the firm’s employee channel with a combined $320 million in assets under management. Mark Burton, Financial Advisor and Managing Director, manages approximately $152 million in assets under management. He joined Ameriprise from Wells Fargo, where he spent the last 16 of his 20 years as an advisor. Burton formed a team with his branch manager Todd Lucius. The team, called The Burton Lucius Financial Consulting Group, includes his wife, Norma Burton, who serves as a client service associate, and Sandy Long, who is a registered client service associate, both of whom also joined from Wells Fargo. Peter Bazirgan, Financial Advisor and Vice President, manages approximately $168 million in assets under management. He joined Ameriprise after serving his entire 22-year career at Merrill Lynch.
