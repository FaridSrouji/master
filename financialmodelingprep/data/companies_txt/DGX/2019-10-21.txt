Quest Diagnostics Incorporated Revises Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019. Quest Diagnostics Incorporated revised earnings guidance for the year 2019. For the year, the company expected net revenues of approximately $7.72 billion and reported diluted EPS from continuing operations to be in the range of $5.48 to $5.53 compared to previously expect net revenues to be in the range of $7.60 billion to $7.75 billion and reported diluted EPS from continuing operations of Greater than $5.29.
