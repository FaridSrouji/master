Quest Diagnostics Completes Acquisition of Mercy's Outreach Laboratory Services Business. SECAUCUS, N.J. AND ST. LOUIS, JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE:DGX) AND MERCY TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT QUEST HAS COMPLETED ITS PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF THE OUTREACH LABORATORY SERVICES BUSINESS OF MERCY IN AN ALL-CASH ASSET TRANSACTION.
