Ginkgo Bioworks Teams with Quest Diagnostics to Provide COVID-19 Testing for K-12 Schools Nationwide. BOSTON, MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  GINKGO BIOWORKS ("GINKGO") TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION WITH QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE:DGX) TO PROVIDE COVID-19 KINDERGARTEN THROUGH SENIOR (K-12) SCHOOL TESTING SERVICES TO SUPPORT CLASSROOM LEARNING NATIONWIDE. THE SERVICES ARE BASED ON RECOMMENDATIONS PROVIDED BY THE ROCKEFELLER FOUNDATION IN ITS K-12 NATIONAL TESTING ACTION PROGRAM : CONNECTING SCHOOLS WITH THE NATION'S LEADING TESTING COMPANIES TO SAFELY REOPEN, WHICH PROVIDES A TESTING ROADMAP FOR SCHOOLS AND CITES BOTH QUEST AND GINKGO FOR THEIR COVID-19 TESTING EXPERTISE.
