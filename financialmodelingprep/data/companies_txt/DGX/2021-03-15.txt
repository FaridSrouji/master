Zora Biosciences Licenses Ceramide Technology to Quest Diagnostics for Cardiovascular Mortality Prediction Test. SECAUCUS, N.J. AND HELSINKI, MARCH 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ZORA BIOSCIENCES OY, A CLINICAL DIAGNOSTICS COMPANY WHOSE MISSION IS TO IDENTIFY ROBUST MARKERS OF DISEASE AND DEVELOP THEM INTO HIGH-THROUGHPUT, HEALTH-ECONOMIC VALUE-ADDED CLINICAL METHODS, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SIGNED A NON-EXCLUSIVE LICENSE AGREEMENT WITH QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD LEADER IN DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, FOR ZORA'S PATENTED CERAMIDE-ANALYSIS TECHNOLOGY.
