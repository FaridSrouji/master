Quest Diagnostics Says Delay in Reporting COVID-19 Public Health Data in Florida. QUEST DIAGNOSTICS MEDIA STATEMENT ABOUT COVID-19 TESTING.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS SAYS DUE TO TECHNICAL ISSUE, REPORTING OF SUBSET OF PUBLIC HEALTH COVID-19 TEST DATA, TO FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH WAS DELAYED.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS SAYS APOLOGIZES FOR THIS MATTER AND REGRETS THE CHALLENGE IT POSES FOR PUBLIC HEALTH AUTHORITIES IN FLORIDA.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS SAYS TECHNICAL ISSUE HAS SINCE BEEN RESOLVED.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS SAYS THE ISSUE DID NOT AFFECT OR DELAY REPORTING OF TEST RESULTS TO PROVIDERS AND PATIENTS.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS SAYS REMAIN OPEN TO WORKING WITH STATE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH TO PROVIDE TESTING THAT MEETS NEEDS REQUIRED FOR PATIENT CARE.
