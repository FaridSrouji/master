Quest Diagnostics - Adj EPS For Q2 Of 2020 Could Be In Range Of Breakeven To Slightly Profitable. QUEST DIAGNOSTICS INC - BELIEVES THAT ITS ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE FOR Q2 OF 2020 COULD BE IN RANGE OF BREAKEVEN TO SLIGHTLY PROFITABLE.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS - SINCE CO REPORTED RESULTS FOR Q1, HAS CONTINUED TO EXPERIENCE MATERIAL DECLINE IN ITS TESTING VOLUMES AS A RESULT OF PANDEMIC.QUEST DIAGNOSTICS- TESTING VOLUMES IN BASE BUSINESS (EXCLUDING COVID-19 MOLECULAR, ANTIBODY TESTING), HAVE RECOVERED FASTER THAN ANTICIPATED ON APR 22.
