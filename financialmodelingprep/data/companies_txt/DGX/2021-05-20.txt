Quest Diagnostics Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend. SECAUCUS, N.J., MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- QUEST DIAGNOSTICS (NYSE: DGX), THE WORLD'S LEADING PROVIDER OF DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION SERVICES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.62 PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON JULY 21, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD OF QUEST DIAGNOSTICS COMMON STOCK ON JULY 7, 2021.
