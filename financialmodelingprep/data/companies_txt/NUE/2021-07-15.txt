Nucor Corporation Will Reduce Greenhouse Gas Emissions Intensity of Steel Mills to 77% Less Than Global Steelmaking Average. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JULY 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY COMPREHENSIVE GREENHOUSE GAS (GHG) EMISSIONS REDUCTION STRATEGIES THAT WILL LOWER ITS GHG EMISSIONS INTENSITY OF STEEL MILLS TO 77% LESS THAN TODAY'S GLOBAL AVERAGE.
