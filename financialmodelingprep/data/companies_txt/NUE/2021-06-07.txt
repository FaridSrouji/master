Nucor to Acquire Insulated Metal Panels Business from Cornerstone Building Brands. CHARLOTTE, N.C., JUNE 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS SIGNED A DEFINITIVE PURCHASE AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE CORNERSTONE BUILDING BRANDS' INSULATED METAL PANELS (IMP) BUSINESS FOR A CASH PURCHASE PRICE OF $1 BILLION, OR APPROXIMATELY 10X PRE-PANDEMIC EBITDA INCLUSIVE OF EXPECTED SYNERGIES.
