Nucor to Upgrade Engineered Bar Mill in Nebraska. CHARLOTTE, N.C., MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NUCOR CORPORATION (NYSE: NUE) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A PROJECT TO UPGRADE THE COMPANY'S ENGINEERED BAR MILL IN NEBRASKA TO BETTER SERVE THE AUTOMOTIVE MARKET AND CONTINUE TO MEET ITS CUSTOMERS' NEEDS FOR THE HIGHEST QUALITY PRODUCTS.
