McDonald's Announces Quarterly Cash Dividend. CHICAGO, MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, MCDONALD'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $1.29 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK PAYABLE ON JUNE 15, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 1, 2021.
