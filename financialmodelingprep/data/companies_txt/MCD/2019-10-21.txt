McDonald's Corporation (NYSE:MCD) completed the acquisition of Apprente, Inc.. McDonald's Corporation (NYSE:MCD) entered into an agreement to acquire Apprente, Inc. on September 10, 2019. Upon completion, Apprente's team and their technology will form McD Tech Labs. Kris Withrow, Idan Nester and David Hayes of Fenwick & West LLP acted as legal advisor to Apprente, Inc.
McDonald's Corporation (NYSE:MCD) completed the acquisition of Apprente, Inc. on October 22, 2019.
