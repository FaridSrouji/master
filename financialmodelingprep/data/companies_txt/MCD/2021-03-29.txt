McDonald's Names Desiree Ralls-Morrison as General Counsel and Corporate Secretary. CHICAGO, MARCH 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MCDONALD'S CORPORATION (NYSE: MCD) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT DESIREE RALLS-MORRISON HAS BEEN NAMED THE COMPANY'S GENERAL COUNSEL AND CORPORATE SECRETARY, OVERSEEING GLOBAL LEGAL OPERATIONS AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE. RALLS-MORRISON WILL START ON APRIL 26, 2021 AND WILL BE BASED AT THE COMPANY'S CHICAGO HEADQUARTERS IN THE WEST LOOP.
