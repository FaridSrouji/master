Rockley Selects Synopsys for Silicon Photonics Design Solutions. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF., MARCH 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ: SNPS) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ROCKLEY PHOTONICS, A LEADING GLOBAL SUPPLIER OF INTEGRATED OPTICAL CHIPS AND MODULES, HAS ADOPTED SYNOPSYS SOLUTIONS TO ACCELERATE THE DESIGN AND VERIFICATION OF SILICON PHOTONICS FOR SENSING AND DATACOM APPLICATIONS.
