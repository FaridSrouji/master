Synopsys ZeBu Server 4 Adopted by Xsight Labs for Intelligent Networking Switch Processor. MOUNTAIN VIEW, CALIF., MAY 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SYNOPSYS, INC. (NASDAQ:  SNPS ) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT XSIGHT LABS HAS ADOPTED SYNOPSYS ZEBU® SERVER 4 EMULATION SOLUTION FOR VALIDATION OF ITS X1 INTELLIGENT NETWORKING SWITCH PROCESSOR.
