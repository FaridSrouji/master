Willis Towers Watson Public Limited Company Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Willis Towers Watson Public Limited Company announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 1,989 million compared to USD 1,859 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 107 million compared to USD 17 million a year ago. Net income was USD 75 million compared to USD 44 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.58 compared to USD 0.34 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.58 compared to USD 0.33 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 6,349 million compared to USD 6,141 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 642 million compared to USD 339 million a year ago. Net income was USD 500 million compared to USD 317 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.86 compared to USD 2.4 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.84 compared to USD 2.39 a year ago.
