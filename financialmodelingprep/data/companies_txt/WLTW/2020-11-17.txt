Willis Towers Watson launches Tech, Media and Telecom Differentiated Broking Solutions. ARLINGTON, VA., NOV. 17, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WILLIS TOWERS WATSON (NASDAQ: WLTW), A LEADING GLOBAL ADVISORY, BROKING AND SOLUTIONS COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LAUNCH OF ITS TECH, MEDIA AND TELECOM DIFFERENTIATED BROKING SOLUTIONS (TMT DBS). THIS SOLUTION ADDS TO THE ROSTER OF EXISTING DBS PROGRAMS OF GENERAL INDUSTRY DBS, FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS DBS AND REAL ESTATE DBS.
