Alexandria Real Estate Equities, Inc. Announces Pricing Of Upsized Public Offering Of 7,000,000 Shares Of Common Stock. PASADENA, CALIF., JUNE 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ALEXANDRIA REAL ESTATE EQUITIES, INC. ("ALEXANDRIA" OR THE "COMPANY") (NYSE: ARE) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF ITS UPSIZED PUBLIC OFFERING OF 7,000,000 SHARES OF THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK AT A PRICE OF $184.00 PER SHARE IN CONNECTION WITH THE FORWARD SALE AGREEMENTS DESCRIBED BELOW.
