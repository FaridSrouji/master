World Travel & Tourism Council, Carnival Corporation and Hilton Present the 2021 Global Scientific Summit on COVID-19. LONDON, MARCH 31, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE WORLD TRAVEL & TOURISM COUNCIL (WTTC) – TOGETHER WITH THE WORLD'S LARGEST CRUISE COMPANY, CARNIVAL CORPORATION  & PLC (NYSE/LSE: CCL; NYSE: CUK), AND THE LEADING GLOBAL HOSPITALITY COMPANY HILTON WORLDWIDE HOLDINGS INC. (NYSE: HLT) – TODAY ANNOUNCED THE 2021 GLOBAL SCIENTIFIC SUMMIT ON COVID-19. THE FREE LIVE VIRTUAL FORUM, WHICH IS OPEN TO THE PUBLIC, WILL TAKE PLACE FROM 10:00 AM TO 1:30 PM (EDT) [3:00 PM TO 6:30 PM GMT] [4:00 PM TO 7:30 PM CEST] ON THURSDAY, APRIL 15, 2021.
