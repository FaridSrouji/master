Canopy by Hilton Brings Lifestyle Brand to Hangzhou Offering a Refreshing Urban Oasis Where Business, Leisure and Nature Meet. Hilton announced the opening of Canopy by Hilton Hangzhou Jinsha Lake on Nov. 13, a 10-storey, 179-room hotel that provides energizing, positive stays for business travelers and cultured vacationers. The waterfront hotel is conveniently located in the heart of Qiantang New Area of Hangzhou East, offering a unique urban resort experience coupled with the city's best views of Jinsha lake. Canopy by Hilton Hangzhou Jinsha Lake marks the brand's twelfth property and is owned by Hangzhou Economic & Technological Development Asset Management Group and managed by Hilton. Canopy by Hilton Hangzhou Jinsha Lake has been carefully designed to incorporate the unique art culture of Hangzhou. Upon arrival at the hotel lobby, Canopy Central, guests will be greeted by a white ceramic centerpiece floating above the lobby inspired by the Osmanthus, the city's flower, which is traditionally planted in front of local homes and known as a "golden welcome" to guests. The 3D-grafted decorative wall panels along the hotel corridors feature continuously changing illustrations of aquatic plants and marsh birds, immersing guests in the nature of Hangzhou. At Canopy by Hilton, local flavors are a central feature in all restaurant menus. The hotel's cafe,Geli, meaning "This is the place" in Hangzhou dialect, offers a seasonal menu that guests can savor while enjoying beautiful lake views. At night, the space transforms into a social bar featuring evening tastings of local teas, craft cocktails, micro-brews and a comprehensive selection of wines. Guests can also enjoy a selection of healthy refreshments and a local ice jelly dessert throughout the day at the cafe lounge. With insider tips from friendly hotel team members called Enthusiasts, guests can discover popular neighborhood destinations such as Longhu Paradise Walk for a taste of local flavors. Canopy by Hilton Hangzhou Jinsha Lake offers a total of 179 spacious guest rooms, more than half of which come with panoramic lake views. Also available are 25 pairs of connecting rooms, perfect for families. To ensure a comfortable sleep experience, each room is equipped with a Serta mattress that is complemented with a ZoneActive™ 5-Zone Foam Topper for the right amount of support and Serta Cool Balance® Technology for optimal temperature control. Other room amenities include Canopy's signature "uncloseted" closet, a 55-inch TV, Nespresso® machine, generous workspace, and complimentary Wi-Fi. Suites offer even more space and privacy, plus thoughtful extras such as robes and Canopy socks. Guests also have access to a 24-hour fitness center equipped with a comprehensive range of TechnoGym® cardio and strength equipment, where guests can enjoy a good workout overlooking Jinsha Lake. For guests who prefer exercising in the outdoors, the vibrant Jinsha Lake Park features a jogging track. Guests can also hop on a Canopy bike to explore the local neighborhood with ease. The Enthusiasts are also on hand to connect guests with neighborhood partners who provide outdoor activities. Featuring five function rooms with a total of 324 square meters of indoor meeting space, Canopy by Hilton Hangzhou Jinsha Lake is an ideal venue for small scale business meetings, weddings or other intimate occasions. All function rooms provide natural daylight with stunning lake views. The hotel also offers a spacious outdoor venue by the lakefront beach, suitable for social gatherings or events. At Canopy by Hilton, guests can enjoy locally inspired experiences with recommendations from Enthusiasts. With their local know-how, Enthusiasts are always happy to connect guests with the best of the neighborhood has to offer, whether it's a customized silk factory tour with a silk craft lesson, touring the plants of famous local enterprises or discovering a hidden gem in the neighborhood.
