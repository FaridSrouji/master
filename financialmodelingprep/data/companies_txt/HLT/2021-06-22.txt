Sands Fenwick Inc. Recognized with 2020 Hilton Legacy Award for Fenwick Shores, Tapestry Collection by Hilton. FENWICK ISLAND, DEL., JUNE 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SANDS FENWICK INC. HAS BEEN RECOGNIZED WITH A 2020 HILTON LEGACY AWARD FOR NEW BUILD OF THE YEAR FOR FENWICK SHORES, TAPESTRY COLLECTION BY HILTON.
