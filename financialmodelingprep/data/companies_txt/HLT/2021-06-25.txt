Resorts World Las Vegas Officially Debuts as First Ground-Up Resort Built on Las Vegas Strip in Over a Decade. LAS VEGAS, JUNE 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  RESORTS WORLD LAS VEGAS , THE FIRST RESORT BUILT ON THE LAS VEGAS STRIP IN MORE THAN A DECADE, OFFICIALLY SWUNG OPEN ITS DOORS TO THE PUBLIC AT 11 P.M. ON THURSDAY, JUNE 24.
