FOX News Media to Launch Ad-Supported Streaming Weather Service FOX Weather In 2021. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FOX NEWS MEDIA WILL LAUNCH A NEW, ADVERTISING SUPPORTED, STREAMING WEATHER SERVICE ENTITLED FOX WEATHER IN 3Q 2021, ANNOUNCED SUZANNE SCOTT, CEO OF FOX NEWS MEDIA. SHARRI BERG, CURRENTLY THE CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER OF NEWS & OPERATIONS FOR FOX TELEVISION STATIONS (FTS), WILL TAKE ON THE ADDITIONAL ROLE OF PRESIDENT OF FOX WEATHER, REPORTING TO SCOTT, AND WILL RELINQUISH HER ROLE AS THE EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF NEWS OPERATIONS FOR FOX NEWS CHANNEL (FNC).
