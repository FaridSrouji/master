FOX News Channel to Launch New Weekend Programs in Early June. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FOX NEWS CHANNEL (FNC) WILL LAUNCH A NEW WEEKEND SLATE BEGINNING ON JUNE 5-6 NAMING TREY GOWDY TO HOST A 7 PM/ET SUNDAY EVENING PROGRAM, DAN BONGINO TO HOST A PRIMETIME SATURDAY SHOW AT 10 PM/ET, AND ADDING THE BIG SATURDAY AND THE BIG SUNDAY SHOW (5-6 PM/ET) TO THE SCHEDULE PERMANENTLY. ON SUNDAY EVENINGS, FORMER SOUTH CAROLINA CONGRESSMAN AND FNC CONTRIBUTOR TREY GOWDY WILL HEADLINE A NEW LIVE PROGRAM AT 7 PM/ET STARTING ON JUNE 6TH. GOWDY WILL UTILIZE HIS SIGNATURE
