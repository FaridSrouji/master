UnitedHealth Group Donates $30 Million to Increase Health Care Access for Underserved Communities Across Colorado. GRAND JUNCTION, COLO.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--ROCKY MOUNTAIN HEALTH PLANS, A UNITEDHEALTH GROUP COMPANY, IS DONATING $30 MILLION TO INCREASE ACCESS TO HEALTH CARE AND SUPPORT COMMUNITIES ON THE WESTERN SLOPE AND ACROSS COLORADO. THE DONATION INCLUDES A $25 MILLION CONTRIBUTION TO THE ROCKY MOUNTAIN HEALTH FOUNDATION TO FUND STEM EDUCATION AMONG YOUNG WOMEN AND PEOPLE OF COLOR AS WELL AS OTHER PROGRAMS ACROSS THE WESTERN SLOPE.
