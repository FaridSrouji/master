Ohio Department of Medicaid Chooses UnitedHealthcare to Serve Individuals Covered by Medicaid. COLUMBUS, OHIO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE OHIO DEPARTMENT OF MEDICAID, WHICH IS PARTNERING WITH MANAGED CARE HEALTH PLANS TO IMPROVE THE HEALTH AND WELLNESS OF INDIVIDUALS, SELECTED UNITEDHEALTHCARE COMMUNITY PLAN OF OHIO AS ONE OF SIX ORGANIZATIONS TO ADMINISTER ITS REIMAGINED MEDICAID PROGRAM, EFFECTIVE EARLY 2022. “WE ARE HONORED TO CONTINUE OUR PARTNERSHIP WITH THE STATE OF OHIO TO ENSURE INDIVIDUALS COVERED BY MEDICAID HAVE ACCESS TO HIGH QUALITY, COORDINATED AND SUSTAINABLE HEALTH CARE,” SAID
