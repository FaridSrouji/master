UnitedHealth Group Reports Third Quarter 2019 Results. UNITEDHEALTH GROUP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2019 RESULTS.SEES FY 2019 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $14.90 TO $15.00.SEES FY 2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE $14.15 TO $14.25.QTRLY NET EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $3.67, ADJUSTED NET EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $3.88.QTRLY TOTAL REVENUES OF $60.4 BILLION GREW BY 7% OR $3.8 BILLION YEAR-OVER-YEAR.QTRLY ADJUSTED NET EARNINGS PER SHARE WERE $3.88.140 BASIS POINT YEAR-OVER-YEAR INCREASE IN MEDICAL CARE RATIO TO 82.4 PERCENT IN QUARTER.Q3 MEDICAL COST TRENDS REMAINED IN LINE WITH EXPECTATIONS.FY2019 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $14.83 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.OPTUM Q3 2019 REVENUES GREW BY 13.3 PERCENT OR $3.4 BILLION YEAR-OVER-YEAR TO $28.8 BILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $3.75, REVENUE VIEW $59.79 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
UnitedHealth Group Incorporated Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year of Fiscal 2019. UnitedHealth Group Incorporated revised earnings guidance for the full year of fiscal 2019. Based on strong third quarter 2019 results and its positive view for the rest of the year, the company increased its full year net earnings outlook to $14.15 to $14.25 per share increasing $0.15 per share at the mid-point.
