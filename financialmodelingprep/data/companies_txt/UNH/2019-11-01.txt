UnitedHealth Group Announces Executive Changes. Patricia Lewis, the top HR officer at defense contractor Lockheed Martin, has been named executive vice president and chief human resources officer at UnitedHealth Group. Lewis is moving to UnitedHealth Group after more than eight years at Lockheed Martin and more than a decade at IBM. Lewis replaces Ellen Wilson, who will retire in the coming months, according to a company spokesman. Wilson has held the top HR job at UnitedHealth Group since 2013. Like Wilson, Lewis will serve among the company's top executives in UnitedHealth Group's office of the chief executive.
