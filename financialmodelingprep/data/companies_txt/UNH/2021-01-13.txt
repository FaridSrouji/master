CHANGE HEALTHCARE INVESTOR ALERT by the Former Attorney General of Louisiana: Kahn Swick & Foti, LLC Investigates Adequacy of Price and Process in Proposed Sale of Change Healthcare - CHNG. NEW ORLEANS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA CHARLES C. FOTI, JR., ESQ. AND THE LAW FIRM OF KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC (“KSF”) ARE INVESTIGATING THE PROPOSED SALE OF CHANGE HEALTHCARE (NASDAQGS: CHNG) TO UNITEDHEALTH GROUP (NYSE: UNH). UNDER THE TERMS OF THE PROPOSED TRANSACTION, SHAREHOLDERS OF CHANGE WILL RECEIVE ONLY $25.75 IN CASH FOR EACH SHARE OF CHANGE THAT THEY OWN.
