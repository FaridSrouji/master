UnitedHealth Group Board Authorizes Payment of Quarterly Dividend. MINNETONKA, Minn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--The UnitedHealth Group (NYSE: UNH) board of directors has authorized payment of a cash dividend of $1.25 per share, to be paid on December 15, 2020, to all shareholders of record of UnitedHealth Group common stock as of the close of business on December 7, 2020.
