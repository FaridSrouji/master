UnitedHealthcare Expands Access to Virtual Care, Including a New Virtual Primary Care Offering. MINNETONKA, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITEDHEALTHCARE HAS EXPANDED THE AVAILABILITY OF VIRTUAL CARE WITH LOCAL PHYSICIANS FOR MEMBERS ENROLLED IN EMPLOYER-SPONSORED PLANS, AND ALSO INTRODUCED A NEW VIRTUAL PRIMARY CARE SERVICE DESIGNED TO ENABLE ACCESS TO MEDICAL SERVICES IN A SIMPLER AND MORE CONVENIENT WAY.
