Idexx Laboratories, Inc. Updates Earnings Guidance for Full Year 2019; Provides Preliminary Earnings Guidance for 2020. IDEXX Laboratories, Inc. announced earnings guidance for full year 2019. For the year, the company is updating its revenue outlook to $2,395 million - $2,405 million. At mid-point, the updated revenue outlook is $5 million above company's previous guidance reflecting projected organic revenue growth at the higher end of company's previous guidance range. The company also updated its EPS outlook to $4.72 - $4.78, reflecting a decrease of $0.12 per share at mid-point, incorporating a projected $0.18 negative impact from CEO transition charges in the fourth quarter of 2019 and a negative $0.02 per share impact related to updated foreign exchange assumptions.For the year 2020, the company reported revenue guidance range of $2,610 million - $2,650 million and EPS guidance range of $5.30 - $5.46.
