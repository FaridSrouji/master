IDEXX Laboratories to Release 2021 Second Quarter Financial Results. WESTBROOK, MAINE, JUNE 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  IDEXX LABORATORIES, INC. (NASDAQ: IDXX), A GLOBAL LEADER IN VETERINARY DIAGNOSTICS, VETERINARY PRACTICE SOFTWARE AND WATER MICROBIOLOGY TESTING, HAS SCHEDULED THE RELEASE OF ITS 2021 SECOND QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 30, 2021 BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS. THE COMPANY WILL CONDUCT AN ANALYST CONFERENCE CALL BEGINNING AT 8:30 A.M.
