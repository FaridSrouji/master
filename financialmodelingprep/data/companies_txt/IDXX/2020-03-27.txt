Idexx Laboratories Enters Into Second Amendment To Multicurrency Note Purchase & Private Shelf Agreement. IDEXX LABORATORIES - ON MARCH 23, CO ENTERED INTO SECOND AMENDMENT TO MULTICURRENCY NOTE PURCHASE & PRIVATE SHELF AGREEMENT.IDEXX LABORATORIES - AMENDS TO INCREASE SHELF NOTES FACILITY SIZE FROM $150 MILLION TO $300 MILLION.IDEXX LABORATORIES - AMENDS EXTEND SHELF NOTES FACILITY ISSUANCE PERIOD TO DEC 20, 2022.
