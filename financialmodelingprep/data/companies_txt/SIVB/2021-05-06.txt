SVB Financial Group Announces Pricing of $1 Billion Preferred Stock Depositary Shares Offering and $500 Million 2.100% Senior Notes Offering. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MAY 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ("SVB") (NASDAQ: SIVB) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE PRICING OF AN UNDERWRITTEN OFFERING OF 1,000,000 DEPOSITARY SHARES, EACH REPRESENTING A 1/100TH INTEREST IN A SHARE OF ITS SERIES C NON-CUMULATIVE PERPETUAL PREFERRED STOCK, LIQUIDATION AMOUNT $100,000 PER SHARE (EQUIVALENT TO $1,000 PER DEPOSITARY SHARE) AT AN AGGREGATE OFFERING PRICE OF $1 BILLION.
