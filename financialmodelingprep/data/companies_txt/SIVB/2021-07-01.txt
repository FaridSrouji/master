SVB Financial Group Completes Acquisition of Boston Private. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., JULY 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ("SVB") (NASDAQ: SIVB), THE PARENT OF SILICON VALLEY BANK, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF BOSTON PRIVATE FINANCIAL HOLDINGS, INC. ("BOSTON PRIVATE"), THE PARENT COMPANY OF BOSTON PRIVATE BANK & TRUST COMPANY ("BOSTON PRIVATE BANK"), A LEADING PROVIDER OF INTEGRATED WEALTH MANAGEMENT, TRUST AND BANKING SERVICES TO INDIVIDUALS, FAMILIES, BUSINESSES AND NONPROFITS.
