SVB Financial Group Announces Pricing of Offering of Common Stock. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., MARCH 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ("SVB") (NASDAQ: SIVB), ANNOUNCED TODAY THE PRICING OF ITS UNDERWRITTEN PUBLIC OFFERING OF 2,000,000 SHARES OF ITS COMMON STOCK AT A PRICE TO THE PUBLIC OF $500.00 PER SHARE FOR EXPECTED GROSS PROCEEDS OF APPROXIMATELY $1,000,000,000 BEFORE UNDERWRITING DISCOUNTS AND COMMISSIONS AND OFFERING EXPENSES.
