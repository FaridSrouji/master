SVB Financial Group Reiterates That It Will Not Increase Purchase Price for Boston Private. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., APRIL 30, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ("SVB") (NASDAQ: SIVB) REMAINS FULLY COMMITTED TO THE MERGER WITH BOSTON PRIVATE FINANCIAL HOLDINGS, INC.
