SVB Financial Group CONFIRMS Announced Purchase Price for Boston Private is 'Best and Final'. SANTA CLARA, CALIF., APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ON APRIL 27, 2021 BOSTON PRIVATE FINANCIAL HOLDINGS, INC. ("BOSTON PRIVATE") (NASDAQ: BPFH) ADJOURNED ITS SPECIAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS UNTIL MAY 4, 2021 TO SOLICIT ADDITIONAL VOTES IN SUPPORT OF THE MERGER BETWEEN BOSTON PRIVATE AND SVB FINANCIAL GROUP ("SVB") (NASDAQ: SIVB).
