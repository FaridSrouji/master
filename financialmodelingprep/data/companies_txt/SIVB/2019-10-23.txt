Svb Financial Group Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. SVB Financial Group announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced net interest income was USD 520.644 million compared to USD 493.222 million a year ago. Net income was USD 267.281 million compared to USD 274.817 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 5.19 compared to USD 5.16 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 5.15 compared to USD 5.1 a year ago. For the nine months, net interest income was USD 1,562.933 million compared to USD 1,379.528 million a year ago. Net income was USD 874.000 million compared to USD 707.576 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 16.8 compared to USD 13.33 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 16.67 compared to USD 13.15 a year ago.
