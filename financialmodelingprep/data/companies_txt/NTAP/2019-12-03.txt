NetApp and ENS-Inc Secure California Data Center Modernization Contract. NetApp announced that Enterprise Networking Solutions, Inc. has been awarded an exclusive three-year contract by the State of California Department of General Services (DGS) to deliver NetApp’s data center modernization and hybrid cloud infrastructure solutions to the State of California. For this contract, ENS-Inc serves as the sole provider of a complete catalog of all NetApp® enterprise technology to the State of California, including data center equipment and associated data center hardware (for example, servers, storage, converged and hyperconverged systems, on-campus networking, and fabric).
