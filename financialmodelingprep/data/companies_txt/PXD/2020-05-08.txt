Pioneer Natural Resources Board Approves 20% Cut In Annual Base Salary Of Company's CEO. PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES -  BOARD APPROVED A 20% DECREASE IN ANNUAL BASE SALARY OF SCOTT D. SHEFFIELD, COMPANY'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER.PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES CO - 15% DECREASE IN ANNUAL BASE SALARIES OF COMPANY'S OTHER EXECUTIVE OFFICERS.PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES CO - BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF COMPANY APPROVED A 20% DECREASE IN DIRECTORS' CASH BASE RETAINER FEE.PIONEER NATURAL RESOURCES CO - 20% REDUCTION IN ADDITIONAL CASH RETAINER FEES PAYABLE TO CHAIRMAN OF BOARD AND CHAIRMAN OF AUDIT COMMITTEE.
