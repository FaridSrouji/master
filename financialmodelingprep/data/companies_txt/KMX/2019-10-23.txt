CarMax, Inc. Announces Executive Changes. CarMax, Inc. announced, as part of a long-term planning process, its CFO transition. Enrique Mayor-Mora, who has served as CarMax’s treasurer since 2016, will be promoted to senior vice president and CFO. Tom Reedy, who has served as CFO since 2010 and as executive vice president since 2012, will become executive vice president of finance. Mayor-Mora will continue to report to Reedy. The Company noted that Mayor-Mora will assume responsibilities for Accounting, Tax, Treasury, Expansion Planning and Analysis, Financial Planning and Internal Audit functions. Reedy will continue to oversee the CarMax Auto Finance, Financial Services and Products, Investor Relations, Store Delivery and Support Systems, and Real Estate teams.
