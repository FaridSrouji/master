Carmax Says As Of End Of July, Will No Longer Have Any Associates On Furlough. CARMAX INC - AS OF END OF JULY, CO WILL NO LONGER HAVE ANY ASSOCIATES ON FURLOUGH.CARMAX INC - ON JULY 24, BOARD APPROVED RETURN OF CEO BILL NASH'S BASE SALARY TO PREVIOUS LEVEL, EFFECTIVE JULY 31.CARMAX INC - BOARD ALSO APPROVED RESUMPTION OF BOARD MEMBER CASH RETAINER, EFFECTIVE AUGUST 1.CARMAX INC - BASE SALARIES OF EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENTS, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENTS & VICE PRESIDENTS HAVE ALL RETURNED TO PRE-REDUCTION LEVELS.
