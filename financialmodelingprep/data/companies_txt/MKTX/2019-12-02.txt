MarketAxess Holdings Inc. Announces Operating Results for the Month of November 2019. MarketAxess Holdings Inc. announced operating results for the month of November 2019. For the month, announced total monthly trading volume for November 2019 of $460.5 billion, consisting of $81.7 billion in U.S. high-grade volume, $78.8 billion in other credit volume, and $300.0 billion in rates volume.
