MarketAxess Adds New Efficiency and Cost-Saving Enhancements to Portfolio Trading Capabilities. NEW YORK, JUNE 23, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A SERIES OF NEW ENHANCEMENTS FOR CLIENTS USING PORTFOLIO TRADING ON THE MARKETAXESS PLATFORM TO BETTER INTEGRATE WITH A FULL SUITE OF TRADING SOLUTIONS.
