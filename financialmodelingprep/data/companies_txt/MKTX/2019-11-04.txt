MarketAxess Holdings Inc. Announces Monthly Operating Results for Oct. 2019. MarketAxess Holdings Inc. announced monthly operating results for Oct. 2019. The company announced total monthly trading volume for October 2019 of $182.6 billion, consisting of $91.2 billion in U.S. high-grade volume, $87.1 billion in other credit volume, and $4.3 billion in liquid products volume.
