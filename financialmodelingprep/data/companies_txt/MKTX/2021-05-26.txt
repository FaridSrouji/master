MarketAxess to Participate in the Deutsche Bank Global Financial Services and Fintech Information Services Conference. NEW YORK, MAY 26, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARKETAXESS HOLDINGS INC. (NASDAQ: MKTX), THE OPERATOR OF A LEADING ELECTRONIC TRADING PLATFORM FOR FIXED-INCOME SECURITIES, AND THE PROVIDER OF MARKET DATA AND POST-TRADE SERVICES FOR THE GLOBAL FIXED-INCOME MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CHRIS CONCANNON, PRESIDENT AND COO, AND TONY DELISE, CFO, ARE SCHEDULED TO SPEAK AT THE DEUTSCHE BANK GLOBAL FINANCIAL SERVICES AND FINTECH INFORMATION SERVICES CONFERENCE AT 11:50 A.M. EDT ON JUNE 2, 2021.
