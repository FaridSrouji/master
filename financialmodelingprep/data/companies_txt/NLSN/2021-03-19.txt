Women 50+ Are Underrepresented On Top TV Programs. NEW YORK, MARCH 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TO COMMEMORATE WOMEN'S HISTORY MONTH, NIELSEN (NYSE: NLSN) TODAY RELEASED "SHATTERING STEREOTYPES: HOW TODAY'S WOMEN OVER 50 ARE REDEFINING WHAT'S POSSIBLE ON-SCREEN, AT WORK AND AT HOME", PART OF THE NIELSEN DIVERSE INTELLIGENCE SERIES. USING CUSTOM ANALYSIS FROM THE RECENTLY-LAUNCHED GRACENOTE INCLUSION ANALYTICS, THE REPORT UNCOVERS THAT WOMEN OVER THE AGE OF 50 ARE UNDERREPRESENTED ON TOP TV PROGRAMS AND WHEN THEY ARE SEEN, THEY OFTEN PLAY STEREOTYPICAL ROLES.
