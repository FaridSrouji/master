Nexstar Media Group And Nielsen Enter Into Multi-Year Measurement Services Agreement. NEXSTAR MEDIA GROUP AND NIELSEN ENTER INTO MULTI-YEAR MEASUREMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT.NEXSTAR MEDIA GROUP - NIELSEN WILL PROVIDE ITS SCARBOROUGH QUALITATIVE SERVICE.NEXSTAR MEDIA GROUP - NIELSEN WILL BE PROVIDING SUPPLEMENTARY SUPPORT SERVICES SUCH AS RHIZA, GRABIX TO NEXSTAR'S LOCAL MARKETS.NEXSTAR MEDIA GROUP INC - NIELSEN TO DELIVER RATING SERVICES FOR NEXSTAR'S 197 LOCAL TELEVISION STATIONS.
