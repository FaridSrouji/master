US Consumer Confidence Up Sharply Again in April. NEW YORK, APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE CONFERENCE BOARD CONSUMER CONFIDENCE INDEX ® ROSE SHARPLY AGAIN IN APRIL, FOLLOWING A SUBSTANTIAL GAIN IN MARCH. THE INDEX NOW STANDS AT 121.7 (1985=100), UP FROM 109.0 IN MARCH.
