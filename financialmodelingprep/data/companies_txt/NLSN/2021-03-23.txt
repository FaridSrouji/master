Sandra Sims-Williams Appointed Nielsen's Chief Diversity Officer. NEW YORK, MARCH 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, NIELSEN (NYSE: NLSN) ANNOUNCED THAT SANDRA SIMS-WILLIAMS HAS BEEN APPOINTED CHIEF DIVERSITY OFFICER FOR NIELSEN. SHE WILL TAKE OVER FROM CEO DAVID KENNY, WHO HAS HELD THE CDO TITLE IN ADDITION TO CEO, SINCE FEBRUARY 2019.
