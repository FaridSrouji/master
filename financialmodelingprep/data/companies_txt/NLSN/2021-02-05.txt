Nielsen Declares Quarterly Dividend. NEW YORK, FEB. 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NIELSEN HOLDINGS PLC (NYSE: NLSN) DECLARED A QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.06 PER SHARE OF NIELSEN'S COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON MARCH 18, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON MARCH 4, 2021....
