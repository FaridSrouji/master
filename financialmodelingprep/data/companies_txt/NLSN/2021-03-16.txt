Spanish Broadcasting System Signs Multi-Year Agreement With Nielsen Audio. NEW YORK, MARCH 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY, NIELSEN (NYSE: NLSN) ANNOUNCED THAT SPANISH BROADCASTING SYSTEM (SBS) HAS ENTERED INTO A LONG-TERM AGREEMENT FOR NIELSEN AUDIO SERVICES. THIS MULTI-YEAR RENEWAL INCLUDES A CONTINUATION OF THEIR AUDIO AUDIENCE MEASUREMENT LICENSES ACROSS ALL FIVE PPM MARKETS AND THE DIARY MARKET OF PUERTO RICO.
