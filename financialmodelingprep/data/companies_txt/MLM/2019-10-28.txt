Martin Marietta Materials Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Martin Marietta Materials Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 1,420.246 million compared to USD 1,219.640 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 345.263 million compared to USD 240.662 million a year ago. Net income was USD 248.573 million compared to USD 180.221 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.97 compared to USD 2.86 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.96 compared to USD 2.85 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 3,638.668 million compared to USD 3,224.047 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 700.365 million compared to USD 543.696 million a year ago. Net income was USD 480.901 million compared to USD 375.621 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 7.67 compared to USD 5.95 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 7.65 compared to USD 5.93 a year ago.
