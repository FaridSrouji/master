Martin Marietta Appoints New Member to Board of Directors. RALEIGH, N.C., NOV. 09, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARTIN MARIETTA MATERIALS, INC. (NYSE:MLM) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE APPOINTMENT OF ANTHONY R. FOXX AS AN INDEPENDENT MEMBER TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS, EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 6, 2020. WITH MR. FOXX'S APPOINTMENT, THE MARTIN MARIETTA BOARD IS COMPRISED OF 11 DIRECTORS, 10 OF WHOM ARE INDEPENDENT AND SIX OF WHOM HAVE JOINED SINCE 2016. MR. FOXX WILL SERVE ON MARTIN MARIETTA'S FINANCE COMMITTEE.
