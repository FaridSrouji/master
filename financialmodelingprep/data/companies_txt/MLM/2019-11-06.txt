Martin Marietta Materials, Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on December 31, 2019. Martin Marietta Materials, Inc. announced that its Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.55 per share on the Company’s outstanding common stock. This dividend, which represents a cash dividend of $2.20 per share on an annualized basis, is payable on December 31, 2019, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 2, 2019.
