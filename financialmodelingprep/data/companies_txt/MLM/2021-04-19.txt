Martin Marietta Announces First-Quarter 2021 Earnings Conference Call. RALEIGH, N.C., APRIL 19, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARTIN MARIETTA MATERIALS, INC. (NYSE:MLM) (“MARTIN MARIETTA” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT WILL PROVIDE AN ONLINE WEB SIMULCAST OF ITS FIRST-QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL ON TUESDAY, MAY 4, 2021. THE COMPANY WILL RELEASE RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021, THAT MORNING BEFORE THE MARKET OPENS.
