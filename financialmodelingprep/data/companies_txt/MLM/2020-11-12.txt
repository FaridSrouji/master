Martin Marietta Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend. RALEIGH, N.C., NOV. 12, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MARTIN MARIETTA MATERIALS, INC. (NYSE: MLM) (“MARTIN MARIETTA” OR THE “COMPANY”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.57 PER SHARE ON THE COMPANY'S OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK. THIS DIVIDEND, WHICH REPRESENTS A CASH DIVIDEND OF $2.28 PER SHARE ON AN ANNUALIZED BASIS, IS PAYABLE DECEMBER 31, 2020, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON DECEMBER 1, 2020.
