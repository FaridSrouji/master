Domino's Pizza Announces Recapitalization Transaction. ANN ARBOR, MICH., APRIL 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINO'S PIZZA, INC. (NYSE: DPZ), THE LARGEST PIZZA COMPANY IN THE WORLD BASED ON RETAIL SALES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT CERTAIN OF ITS SUBSIDIARIES INTEND TO COMPLETE A RECAPITALIZATION TRANSACTION, WHICH WILL INCLUDE THE REFINANCING OF A PORTION OF THEIR OUTSTANDING SECURITIZATION DEBT WITH A NEW SERIES OF SECURITIZED DEBT.
