Domino's Announces Retirement EVP And CFO Jeffrey Lawrence. EVP & CFO JEFFREY LAWRENCE ANNOUNCES RETIREMENT FROM DOMINO'S.DOMINO'S PIZZA INC - LAWRENCE HAS AGREED TO CONTINUE SERVING AS CFO UNTIL COMPANY IDENTIFIES HIS SUCCESSOR.DOMINO'S PIZZA - ONCE SUCCESSOR IS NAMED, LAWRENCE WILL STEP DOWN FROM ROLE, WILL SERVE AS ADVISOR TO DOMINO'S CFO RITCH ALLISON UNTIL END OF 2020.
