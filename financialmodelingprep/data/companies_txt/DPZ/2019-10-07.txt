Domino's Pizza, Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 27, 2019. On October 4, 2019, Domino's Pizza, Inc.’s Board of Directors declared a $0.65 per share quarterly dividend for shareholders of record as of December 13, 2019 to be paid on December 27, 2019.
