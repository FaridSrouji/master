Schlumberger Limited Announces CFO Succession. SCHLUMBERGER LIMITED ANNOUNCES CFO SUCCESSION.SCHLUMBERGER NV - SIMON AYAT, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL STEP DOWN.SCHLUMBERGER NV - AYAT WILL BE REPLACED BY STEPHANE BIGUET AS COMPANY'S EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER.SCHLUMBERGER NV - SIMON AYAT WILL REMAIN WITH SCHLUMBERGER AS SENIOR STRATEGIC ADVISOR TO COMPANY'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER FOR A PERIOD OF TWO YEARS..
