PepsiCo Announces 2030 Goal to Scale Regenerative Farming Practices Across 7 Million Acres, Equivalent to Entire Agricultural Footprint. PURCHASE, N.Y., APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PEPSICO, INC. (NASDAQ:PEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED A NEW, IMPACT-DRIVEN POSITIVE AGRICULTURE AMBITION, ANCHORED BY A GOAL TO SPREAD REGENERATIVE FARMING PRACTICES ACROSS 7 MILLION ACRES, APPROXIMATELY EQUAL TO ITS ENTIRE AGRICULTURAL FOOTPRINT.
