Brisk Launches New Zero Sugar Lemon Iced Tea, First National Innovation for the Brand in Over Five Years. PURCHASE, N.Y., MARCH 22, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ --  BRISK , THE POWERHOUSE ICED TEA BRAND KNOWN FOR ITS THIRST-QUENCHING FLAVORS, IS UNVEILING ITS FIRST NATIONAL INNOVATION IN OVER FIVE YEARS: BRISK ZERO SUGAR LEMON ICED TEA.
