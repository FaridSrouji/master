PepsiCo Says Effective June 1, Co Terminated $3.75 Billion 364-Day Credit Agreement, Dated As Of June 3, 2019. PEPSICO INC - EFFECTIVE JUNE 1, CO TERMINATED $3.75 BILLION 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT, DATED AS OF JUNE 3, 2019.PEPSICO - ON JUNE 1, ENTERED INTO A NEW $3.75 BILLION 364-DAY UNSECURED REVOLVING CREDIT AGREEMENT.PEPSICO INC - 2020 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT ENABLES CO AND ITS BORROWING SUBSIDIARIES TO BORROW UP TO $3.75 BILLION IN U.S. DOLLARS AND/OR EUROS.PEPSICO INC - THERE WERE NO OUTSTANDING BORROWINGS UNDER 2019 364-DAY CREDIT AGREEMENT AT TIME OF ITS TERMINATION.
Pepsico Appoints Wern-Yuen Tan As CEO Of Asia Pacific, Australia, New Zealand And China Business. PEPSICO APPOINTS WERN-YUEN TAN AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF ASIA PACIFIC, AUSTRALIA, NEW ZEALAND AND CHINA BUSINESS.PEPSICO -  TAN WILL BEGIN HIS NEW ROLE ON JUNE 15, 2020.
