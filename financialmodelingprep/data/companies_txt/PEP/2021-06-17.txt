Alyssa Thompson And Bryce Boneau Named 2020-21 Gatorade® National Soccer Players Of The Year. CHICAGO, JUNE 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN ITS 36TH YEAR OF HONORING THE NATION'S MOST ELITE HIGH SCHOOL ATHLETES, GATORADE TODAY ANNOUNCED ALYSSA THOMPSON OF HARVARD-WESTLAKE HIGH SCHOOL IN LOS ANGELES, CALIF. AND BRYCE BONEAU OF KELLER HIGH SCHOOL (KELLER, TEXAS) ARE THE 2020-21 GATORADE NATIONAL SOCCER PLAYERS OF THE YEAR.
