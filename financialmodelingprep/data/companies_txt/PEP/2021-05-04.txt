PepsiCo Declares Quarterly Dividend. PURCHASE, N.Y., MAY 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PEPSICO, INC. (NASDAQ: PEP) TODAY DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.075 PER SHARE OF PEPSICO COMMON STOCK, A 5 PERCENT INCREASE VERSUS THE COMPARABLE YEAR-EARLIER PERIOD.
