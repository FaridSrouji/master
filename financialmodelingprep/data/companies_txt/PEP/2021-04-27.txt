PepsiCo Commits $1 Million To Improve Access To Soccer In Underserved Communities In The U.S.. PURCHASE, N.Y., APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PEPSICO, INC. (NASDAQ:PEP) TODAY ANNOUNCED "TEAM OF CHAMPIONS," A NATIONAL PURPOSE PLATFORM BUILDING UPON ITS GLOBAL UEFA CHAMPIONS LEAGUE PARTNERSHIP TO PROVIDE THE GIFT OF THE GAME TO UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES.
