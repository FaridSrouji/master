Henry Schein, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 28, 2019. Henry Schein, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 28, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 2,508.767 million compared to USD 2,355.565 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 187.198 million compared to USD 123.269 million a year ago. Net income was USD 140.557 million compared to USD 121.478 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.92 compared to USD 0.6 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 0.91 compared to USD 0.59 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.96 compared to USD 0.8 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.95 compared to USD 0.79 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 7,316.862 million compared to USD 6,945.047 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 521.927 million compared to USD 442.617 million a year ago. Net income was USD 364.872 million compared to USD 402.908 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 2.49 compared to USD 2.05 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 2.47 compared to USD 2.03 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.46 compared to USD 2.63 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.43 compared to USD 2.62 a year ago.
