Mohawk Industries Q1 Earnings Per Share $1.66 Excluding Items. MOHAWK INDUSTRIES REPORTS Q1 RESULTS.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.66 EXCLUDING ITEMS.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.54.Q1 SALES $2.3 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $2.29 BILLION.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.66 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.AT END OF APRIL, WITH MOST PEOPLE SHELTERING IN PLACE ACROSS WORLD, OUR SALES RATE IS ABOUT 35% BELOW PRIOR YEAR.UNABLE TO PROVIDE EPS GUIDANCE FOR Q2, AND WE ANTICIPATE AN OPERATING LOSS IN PERIOD DUE TO IMPACT OF COVID-19.BALANCE SHEET IS STRONG WITH SUBSTANTIAL LIQUIDITY OF $1.3 BILLION TO MANAGE THROUGH THIS CRISIS.REDUCING CAPITAL EXPENDITURES, CUTTING NON-ESSENTIAL EXPENSES AND PUTTING OUR STOCK PURCHASES ON HOLD.LOWERING OUR COSTS BY IMPLEMENTING LAYOFFS AND FURLOUGHS, USING GOVERNMENT ASSISTANCE WHERE AVAILABLE.
