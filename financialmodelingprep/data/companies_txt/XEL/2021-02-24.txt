Xcel Energy Announces 2030 Clean Energy Plan to Reduce Carbon Emissions 85%. DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--XCEL ENERGY – COLORADO TODAY ANNOUNCED THE DETAILS OF ITS UPCOMING CLEAN ENERGY PLAN TODAY THAT WILL DELIVER ITS CUSTOMERS AN ESTIMATED 85% REDUCTION IN CARBON DIOXIDE EMISSIONS FROM 2005 LEVELS BY 2030.
