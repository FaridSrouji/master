Xcel Energy Inc. Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019; Provides Earnings Guidance for the Year 2020. Xcel Energy Inc. revised earnings guidance for the full year 2019 and provided earnings guidance for the year 2020. The company expected earnings to the upper half of 2019 guidance range. the company narrows its 2019 EPS guidance range to $2.60 to $2.65 from $2.55 to $2.65.The company have issued 2020 earnings guidance of $2.73 to $2.83 per share, which is consistent with 5-7% growth objective.
