Xcel Energy Announces Redemption of Outstanding 4.70% Senior Notes, Series Due May 15, 2020. Xcel Energy Inc. announced that it has submitted a redemption notice to the trustee to redeem all of its outstanding 4.70% Senior Notes, Series due May 15, 2020 (Notes) on December 23, 2019, (Redemption Date). The redemption price is the outstanding principal amount of the Notes, plus accrued and unpaid interest to the Redemption Date. The aggregate principal amount of Notes currently outstanding is $550,000,000. The company issued new debt to fund the redemption of the Notes.
