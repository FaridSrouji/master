Consumers Energy and Attorney General Dana Nessel: Tens of Millions of Dollars Available to Pay Winter Bills. JACKSON, MICH., MARCH 15, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MICHIGAN ATTORNEY GENERAL DANA NESSEL AND CONSUMERS ENERGY ARE LAUNCHING A PUBLIC CAMPAIGN TODAY TO ENSURE MICHIGAN RESIDENTS TAKE ADVANTAGE OF TENS OF MILLIONS IN FEDERAL, STATE AND LOCAL DOLLARS THAT WILL HELP HOUSEHOLDS AND SMALL BUSINESSES PAY WINTER HEATING BILLS.
