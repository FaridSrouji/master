Cms Energy Corp. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. CMS Energy Corp. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 1,546 million compared to USD 1,599 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 351 million compared to USD 294 million a year ago. Net income was USD 207 million compared to USD 169 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.73 compared to USD 0.6 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.73 compared to USD 0.59 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 5,050 million compared to USD 5,044 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 928 million compared to USD 912 million a year ago. Net income was USD 513 million compared to USD 549 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.81 compared to USD 1.95 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.81 compared to USD 1.94 a year ago.
