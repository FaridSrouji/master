Forbes Names Consumers Energy as No. 1 Utility Company for Workplace Diversity in U.S.. JACKSON, MICH., APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FORBES HAS NAMED CONSUMERS ENERGY AS THE TOP UTILITY COMPANY IN THE COUNTRY AND SECOND OVERALL IN MICHIGAN IN ITS LATEST RANKING OF "AMERICA'S BEST EMPLOYERS FOR DIVERSITY," RECOGNIZING CONSUMERS ENERGY'S MAJOR EFFORT TO BE A LEADING COMPANY FOR DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION.
