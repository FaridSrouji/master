Ronald J. Tanski Joins CMS Energy and Consumers Energy Boards of Directors and Compensation and Human Resources and Finance Committees. Ronald J. Tanski has been elected to CMS Energy's board of directors, effective November 8, 2019. Tanski served as president and chief executive officer of National Fuel Gas Company ("National Fuel") from March 2013, through his retirement on July 1, 2019. Tanski joined National Fuel as an attorney in 1979 and served in positions of increased responsibility during his career. Tanski continues to serve as a director of National Fuel. He is also joining the Board of Directors of Consumers Energy, the principal subsidiary of CMS Energy. Tanski has been appointed to serve on the Compensation and Human Resources and Finance Committees of the Boards.
