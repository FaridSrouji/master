Spring Signals Start to Excavation Projects; Gov. Whitmer Declares April Safe Digging Month in Michigan. JACKSON, MICH., APRIL 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AFTER MORE THAN A YEAR OF DEALING WITH THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC, MANY MICHIGANDERS ARE MORE THAN READY TO UNDERTAKE OUTDOOR DIGGING PROJECTS LIKE LANDSCAPING AND OTHER HOME IMPROVEMENTS.
