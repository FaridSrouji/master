CMS Energy Announces Patti Poppe Stepping Down as CEO, Garrick Rochow Named President, CEO. JACKSON, MICH., NOV. 18, 2020 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CMS ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT PATTI POPPE IS LEAVING THE COMPANY, EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 1, 2020, TO TAKE THE POSITION AS CEO AT PG&E CORPORATION IN CALIFORNIA. GARRICK ROCHOW, CURRENT EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT OF OPERATIONS, WILL SUCCEED POPPE...
