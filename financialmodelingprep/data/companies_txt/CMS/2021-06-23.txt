Consumers Energy Announces Plan to End Coal Use by 2025; Lead Michigan's Clean Energy Transformation. JACKSON, MICH., JUNE 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY TODAY ANNOUNCED A SWEEPING PROPOSAL TO STOP USING COAL AS A FUEL SOURCE FOR ELECTRICITY BY 2025 —15 YEARS FASTER THAN CURRENTLY PLANNED.
