Consumers Energy Plans More Clean Energy for Michigan from Solar Project in Calhoun County. JACKSON, MICH., MARCH 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONSUMERS ENERGY ANNOUNCED TODAY AN AGREEMENT TO PURCHASE 140 MEGAWATTS OF CLEAN, RENEWABLE ENERGY – ENOUGH TO POWER ABOUT 70,000 RESIDENTIAL CUSTOMERS -- FROM THE CALHOUN SOLAR CENTER.
