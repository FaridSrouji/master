CMS Energy Declares Quarterly Dividend on Common Stock, Payable Nov. 27, 2019. The Board of Directors of CMS Energy has declared a quarterly dividend on the company's common stock. The dividend for the common stock is 38.25 cents per share. It is payable Nov. 27, 2019, to shareholders of record on Nov. 1, 2019.
