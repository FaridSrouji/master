Chief Collision Technology Introduces First Mobile 3D Hail Damage Scanning System. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., APRIL 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CHIEF COLLISION TECHNOLOGY, PART OF VEHICLE SERVICE GROUP (VSG) AND DOVER (NYSE: DOV), HAS INTRODUCED CONSTELLATION, THE COLLISION INDUSTRY'S FIRST MOBILE 3D HAIL DAMAGE SCANNING SYSTEM.
