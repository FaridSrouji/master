Dover Fueling Solutions Partners with EdgePetrol. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., MARCH 29, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER (NYSE: DOV) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS ENTERED INTO A COMMERCIAL PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT WITH, AND ACQUIRED A MINORITY INTEREST IN, EDGEPETROL LIMITED ("EDGEPETROL"), AN INNOVATIVE PROVIDER OF CLOUD-BASED SOFTWARE FOR REVENUE AND OPERATING RESULTS MANAGEMENT TO THE RETAIL FUELING INDUSTRY.
