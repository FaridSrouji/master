SWEP Receives AHRI Certification Performance Award. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., JUNE 2, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SWEP, PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), HAS RECEIVED THE AIR-CONDITIONING, HEATING, AND REFRIGERATION INSTITUTE'S (AHRI) CERTIFICATION PERFORMANCE AWARD RECOGNIZING SWEP'S TEST SUCCESS RATES FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR.
