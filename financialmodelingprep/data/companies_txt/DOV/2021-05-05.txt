Wilden Releases New Equalizer Surge Dampeners - Integrated SD Series (ISD) Available in Metal. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., MAY 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WILDEN, PART OF PSG, A DOVER (NYSE: DOV) COMPANY, AND A WORLDWIDE LEADER IN SPECIALTY PUMPS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE RELEASE OF ITS NEW EQUALIZER® SURGE DAMPENERS – INTEGRATED SD SERIES (ISD) BOLTED METAL OPTION.
