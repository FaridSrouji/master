Dover Declares Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., FEB. 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV) TODAY DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.495 (FORTY-NINE AND ONE-HALF CENTS) PER SHARE, PAYABLE ON MARCH 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF FEBRUARY 26, 2021.
