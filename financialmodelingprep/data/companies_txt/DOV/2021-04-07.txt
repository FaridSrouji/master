City of Columbus, Georgia Moving to Automated Trash Collection with the Help of Environmental Solutions Group. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., APRIL 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ENVIRONMENTAL SOLUTIONS GROUP (ESG), PART OF DOVER (NYSE: DOV), TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE CITY OF COLUMBUS, GEORGIA IS IN THE PROCESS OF STREAMLINING ITS REFUSE COLLECTION PROGRAMS AND IS LOOKING TO ESG'S HEIL® FOR NEW DURAPACK® PYTHON® AUTOMATED SIDE LOADERS TO HELP INCREASE EFFICIENCIES RELATED TO THE CITY'S RECYCLING PROGRAM.
