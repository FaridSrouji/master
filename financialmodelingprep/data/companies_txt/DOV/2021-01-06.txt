Dover Announces Acquisition of Innovative Control Systems, Inc., a Leading Provider of Vehicle Wash Site Management Solutions. DOWNERS GROVE, ILL., JAN. 6, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOVER (NYSE: DOV) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE ACQUISITION OF INNOVATIVE CONTROL SYSTEMS, INC. ("ICS"), A LEADING PROVIDER OF CAR WASH CONTROLLERS, PAYMENT TERMINALS, POINT-OF-SALE AND WASH SITE MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS....
