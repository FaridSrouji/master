PerkinElmer Agrees to Acquire Viral Vector Gene Delivery Leader SIRION Biotech. WALTHAM, MASS.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PERKINELMER HAS ENTERED AN AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE SIRION BIOTECH GMBH, A PROVIDER OF VIRAL VECTOR-BASED TECHNOLOGIES FOR CELL AND GENE THERAPIES.
