KTL Solutions Customer Becomes First Authorized C3PAO. FREDERICK, MD., JUNE 14, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- KTL SOLUTIONS IS PROUD TO ANNOUNCE THAT OUR CUSTOMER REDSPIN IS THE FIRST ORGANIZATION TO PASS THE DOD'S CYBERSECURITY MATURITY MODEL CERTIFICATION (CMMC) LEVEL 3 CERTIFICATION AS A CANDIDATE CMMC THIRD PARTY ASSESSOR ORGANIZATION (C3PAO).
