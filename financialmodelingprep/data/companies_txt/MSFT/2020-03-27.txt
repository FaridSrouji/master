Microsoft To Divest Its Shareholding In AnyVision. JOINT STATEMENT BY MICROSOFT & ANYVISION – ANYVISION AUDIT.MICROSOFT - FORMER U.S. ATTORNEY GENERAL ERIC HOLDER AND HIS TEAM AT COVINGTON & BURLING HAVE COMPLETED THEIR AUDIT OF ANYVISION.MICROSOFT - EVIDENCE DEMONSTRATED THAT ANYVISION’S TECHNOLOGY HAS NOT PREVIOUSLY & DOES NOT CURRENTLY POWER MASS SURVEILLANCE PROGRAM IN WEST BANK.MICROSOFT - COVINGTON & BURLING COULD NOT SUBSTANTIATE A BREACH OF THE MICROSOFT GLOBAL FINANCE PORTFOLIO COMPANY PLEDGE ON FACIAL RECOGNITION.MICROSOFT - CO AND ANYVISION AGREED THAT IT IS IN THE BEST INTEREST OF BOTH ENTERPRISES FOR CO TO DIVEST ITS SHAREHOLDING IN ANYVISION.MICROSOFT - FOR CO, ANYVISION AUDIT PROCESS REINFORCED CHALLENGES OF BEING A MINORITY INVESTOR IN A COMPANY THAT SELLS SENSITIVE TECHNOLOGY.MICROSOFT SAYS ITS FOCUS HAS SHIFTED TO COMMERCIAL RELATIONSHIPS THAT AFFORD IT GREATER OVERSIGHT, CONTROL OVER THE USE OF SENSITIVE TECHNOLOGIES.
