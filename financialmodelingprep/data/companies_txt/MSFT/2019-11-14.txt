Microsoft And Salesforce Expand Strategic Partnership To Accelerate Customer Success. MICROSOFT AND SALESFORCE EXPAND STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP TO ACCELERATE CUSTOMER SUCCESS.MICROSOFT - SALESFORCE NAMES MICROSOFT AZURE AS ITS PUBLIC CLOUD PROVIDER FOR SALESFORCE MARKETING CLOUD.MICROSOFT - SALESFORCE WILL ALSO BUILD A NEW INTEGRATION THAT CONNECTS SALESFORCE'S SALES CLOUD AND SERVICE CLOUD WITH MICROSOFT TEAMS.
