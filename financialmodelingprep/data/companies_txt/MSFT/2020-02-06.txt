RIB Signs 2020-2022 Follow-Up Agreement With Microsoft. SIGNS 2020 - 2022 FOLLOW-UP AGREEMENT WITH MICROSOFT TO CO-SELL MTWO TO TOP 1000 GLOBAL CLIENTS IN 2020 RIB SOFTWARE SE (RIB) SIGNS A PHASE-II-CONTRACT (NO. 4 / 2020).IN 2020, TARGET IS TO SIGN OVER 30 LARGE DEALS AND TO INCREASE NUMBER TO OVER 100 IN 2021.RIB'S AND MICROSOFT'S GLOBAL SALES TEAMS WILL CONTINUE TO CO-SELL MTWO SOLUTION FOR NEXT 3 YEARS TO SELECTED, TOP 1000 CLIENTS IN USA, ASIA/PAC AND EMEA.
