Progressive Insurance Announces Retirement Of CMO, Jeff Charney. MAYFIELD VILLAGE, OHIO, MARCH 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- (NYSE: PGR) – PROGRESSIVE INSURANCE TODAY ANNOUNCED JEFF CHARNEY'S INTENTION TO RETIRE AS CHIEF MARKETING OFFICER OF PROGRESSIVE, A POSITION HE HAS HELD SINCE 2010. THE EARLY ANNOUNCEMENT OF CHARNEY'S RETIREMENT WAS MADE TO ALLOW FOR AMPLE TIME TO CONDUCT AN EXTERNAL SEARCH FOR HIS SUCCESSOR.
