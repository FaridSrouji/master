Progressive Announces Quarterly Common Share Dividend. MAYFIELD VILLAGE, OHIO, MARCH 05, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE PROGRESSIVE CORPORATION (NYSE:PGR) TODAY DECLARED A $0.10 PER COMMON SHARE DIVIDEND, PAYABLE APRIL 15, 2021, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON APRIL 7, 2021 (EX-DIVIDEND DATE OF APRIL 6, 2021).
