TCH and Jack Henry to Bring Real-Time Payments to Minority Communities. NEW YORK, MAY 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN AN EFFORT TO BRING THE BENEFITS OF REAL-TIME PAYMENTS TO ALL AMERICANS, ESPECIALLY THOSE IN COMMUNITIES SERVED BY MINORITY-OWNED DEPOSITORY INSTITUTIONS (MDIS), THE CLEARING HOUSE AND JACK HENRY & ASSOCIATES ARE JOINTLY COVERING THE COSTS OF THE ONBOARDING FEES FOR MDIS CONNECTING TO THE RTP® NETWORK, THE REAL-TIME PAYMENTS NETWORK OPERATED BY THE CLEARING HOUSE, USING JHA PAYCENTER™, JACK HENRY'S PROPRIETARY PAYMENTS HUB. THE FDIC DEFINES MDIS AS ANY FEDERALLY INSURED DEPOSITORY INSTITUTION WITH 51 PERCENT OR MORE OF THE VOTING STOCK OWNED BY MINORITY INDIVIDUALS.
