Jack Henry & Associates Announces Regular Quarterly Dividend And Adds Five Million Shares To Stock Repurchase Authorization. MONETT, MO., MAY 17, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- JACK HENRY & ASSOCIATES, INC.
