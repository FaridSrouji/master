Mesa Air Group's Unit Entered Into 21st Amendment To Code Share & Revenue Sharing Agreement With American Airlines. MESA AIR GROUP INC - ON JUNE 11, CO'S UNIT ENTERED INTO TWENTY-FIRST AMENDMENT TO CODE SHARE & REVENUE SHARING AGREEMENT WITH AMERICAN AIRLINES.MESA AIR GROUP - AMENDMENTS TO AMERICAN CPA INCLUDE PERMANENT WITHDRAWAL OF 2 AIRCRAFT FROM THE AMERICAN CPA, EFFECTIVE JUNE 15.MESA AIR GROUP - AMENDMENTS ALSO INCLUDE ADDITION OF UTILIZATION-BASED CREDITS, ENTITLING AMERICAN TO PAYMENT CREDITS FROM APRIL 1 TO SEPT 30.
