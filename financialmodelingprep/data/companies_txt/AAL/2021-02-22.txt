American Airlines Names Adriane M. Brown to Its Board of Directors. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, FEB. 22, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AMERICAN AIRLINES GROUP INC. (NASDAQ: AAL) TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ELECTION OF ADRIANE M. BROWN, 62, TO ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS. BROWN WILL ALSO SERVE ON THE COMPANY'S AUDIT AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE AND PUBLIC RESPONSIBILITY COMMITTEES.
