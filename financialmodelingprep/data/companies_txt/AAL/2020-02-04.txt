American Airlines And Barzil's GOL Announce Codeshare Agreement. PRESS RELEASE - AMERICAN AIRLINES AND GOL ANNOUNCE CODESHARE AGREEMENT TO OFFER MORE DAILY SERVICE BETWEEN SOUTH AMERICA AND THE U.S. THAN ANY OTHER AIRLINE PARTNERSHIP.AMERICAN AIRLINES GROUP INC - AMERICAN WILL ALSO INCREASE SERVICE FROM MIAMI (MIA) TO RIO DE JANEIRO (GIG), ADDING A SECOND DAILY FLIGHT THIS WINTER.AMERICAN AIRLINES - PLANS TO PROVIDE MORE OPPORTUNITIES TO BRING CUSTOMERS TO LATIN AMERICA THROUGH MIAMI BY ADDING 12 FLIGHTS FROM SIX U.S. CITIES.
