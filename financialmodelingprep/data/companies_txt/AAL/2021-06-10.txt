American Airlines Invests in the Future of Urban Air Mobility. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, JUNE 10, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AS PART OF ITS ONGOING COMMITMENT TO ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY, AMERICAN AIRLINES TODAY ANNOUNCED IT WILL INVEST IN VERTICAL AEROSPACE, A LEADING UK-HEADQUARTERED ENGINEERING AND AERONAUTICAL BUSINESS DEVELOPING ELECTRIC VERTICAL TAKEOFF AND LANDING (EVTOL) AIRCRAFT. WITH THE INVESTMENT, AMERICAN IS DEMONSTRATING ITS FOCUS ON EMERGING TECHNOLOGIES TO REDUCE CARBON EMISSIONS AND INVESTING IN INNOVATIVE WAYS THAT COULD IMPROVE THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY.
