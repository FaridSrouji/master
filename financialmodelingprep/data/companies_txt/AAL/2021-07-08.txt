American Airlines Group Announces Webcast of Second-Quarter 2021 Financial Results. FORT WORTH, TEXAS, JULY 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- AMERICAN AIRLINES GROUP (NASDAQ: AAL) WILL WEBCAST A LIVE AUDIO FEED OF ITS SECOND-QUARTER 2021 FINANCIAL RESULTS CONFERENCE CALL WITH FINANCIAL ANALYSTS AND JOURNALISTS ON THURSDAY, JULY 22, AT 7:30 A.M. CT.
