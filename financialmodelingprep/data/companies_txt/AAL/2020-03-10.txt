American Airlines Says Demand Environment Remains Very Fluid Due To Covid-19, Suspended Q1 And FY 2020 Guidance. AMERICAN AIRLINES - DEMAND ENVIRONMENT REMAINS VERY FLUID DUE TO COVID-19, SUSPENDED Q1 AND FY 2020 GUIDANCE.AMERICAN AIRLINES -  DECLINE IN FUEL PRICE ALONE IS EXPECTED TO DRIVE ABOUT $3 BILLION IN 2020 COST SAVINGS VERSUS PREVIOUS GUIDANCE.AMERICAN AIRLINES - CAPACITY GROWTH IN 2020 WILL BE LOW-COST AND EFFICIENT.AMERICAN AIRLINES - COVID-19 HAS CREATED UNCERTAINTY, BUT AMERICAN IS WELL-POSITIONED TO MANAGE THROUGH IT.AMERICAN AIRLINES - HAVE OVER $7.31 BILLION IN LIQUIDITY AS OF MARCH 9 2020.AMERICAN AIRLINES - HAVE NO LARGE NEAR-TERM MATURITIES UNTIL 2022.
