Copart, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the First Quarter Ended October 31, 2019. Copart, Inc. announced earnings results for the first quarter ended October 31, 2019. For the first quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 554.424 million compared to USD 461.368 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 205.391 million compared to USD 151.440 million a year ago. Net income was USD 218.180 million compared to USD 114.083 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.94 compared to USD 0.49 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.91 compared to USD 0.47 a year ago.
