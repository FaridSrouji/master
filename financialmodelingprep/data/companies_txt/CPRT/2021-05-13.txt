Copart Deutschland GmbH Opens Mannheim Location. DALLAS, MAY 13, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COPART INC. (NASDAQ:CPRT), A GLOBAL ONLINE AUTO AUCTION COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED THE ADDITION OF A LOCATION IN BRUCHMÜHLBACH-MIESAU NEAR MANNHEIM IN GERMANY. "WE'RE EXCITED TO INCREASE OUR INVESTMENT IN GERMANY TO ACCOMMODATE FUTURE GROWTH," SAID KAI SIERSLEBEN, MANAGING DIRECTOR OF COPART DEUTSCHLAND GMBH.
