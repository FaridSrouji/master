Copart Opens New Location in Bismarck, North Dakota. DALLAS, JUNE 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- COPART INC. (NASDAQ: CPRT), A GLOBAL ONLINE AUTO AUCTION COMPANY, ANNOUNCED A NEW LOCATION IN BISMARCK, NORTH DAKOTA. "WE'RE EXCITED TO SERVE OUR CUSTOMERS IN NORTH DAKOTA FROM OUR NEW LOCATION IN BISMARCK," SAID GLENN SHIPMAN, GENERAL MANAGER OF COPART BISMARCK.
