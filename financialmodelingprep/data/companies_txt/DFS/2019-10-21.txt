Tranche Update on Discover Financial Services (NYSE:DFS)'s Equity Buyback Plan announced on July 18, 2019.. From July 18, 2019 to September 30, 2019, the company has repurchased 5,100,000 shares, representing 1.6% for $419 million. With this, the company has completed the repurchase of 5,100,000 shares, representing 1.6% for $419 million under the buyback announced on July 18, 2019.
