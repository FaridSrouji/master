Zimmer Biomet Expands Role for Ivan Tornos, Appointing Him to New Chief Operating Officer Post. WARSAW, IND., MARCH 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ZIMMER BIOMET HOLDINGS, INC. (NYSE AND SIX: ZBH), A GLOBAL LEADER IN MUSCULOSKELETAL HEALTHCARE, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IVAN TORNOS HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO THE NEWLY-CREATED POSITION OF CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER.
