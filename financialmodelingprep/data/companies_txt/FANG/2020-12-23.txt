QEP INVESTOR ALERT By the Former Attorney General of Louisiana: Kahn Swick & Foti, LLC Investigates Adequacy of Price and Process in Proposed Sale of QEP Resources - QEP. NEW ORLEANS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA CHARLES C. FOTI, JR., ESQ. AND THE LAW FIRM OF KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC (“KSF”) ARE INVESTIGATING THE PROPOSED SALE OF QEP RESOURCES (NYSE: QEP) TO DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC. (NASDAQ: FANG). UNDER THE TERMS OF THE PROPOSED TRANSACTION, SHAREHOLDERS OF QEP WILL RECEIVE ONLY 0.05 SHARES OF DIAMONDBACK FOR EACH SHARE OF QEP THAT THEY OWN.
