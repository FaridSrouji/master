Diamondback Energy, Inc. Announces Resignation of Michael L. Hollis as President, Chief Operating Officer and a Director. Diamondback Energy, Inc. announced that Michael L. Hollis has resigned from his positions as President, Chief Operating Officer and a director effective immediately. Mr. Hollis’ resignation was due to personal reasons and not because of a disagreement on any matter relating to Diamondback’s operations, policies or practices. Daniel N. Wesson, who has been with Diamondback since February 2012, will continue to oversee Diamondback's operations in his current position as Senior Vice President of Operations.
