Viper Energy Partners Provides Third Quarter Production And Acquisition Update. VIPER ENERGY PARTNERS LP, A SUBSIDIARY OF DIAMONDBACK ENERGY, INC., PROVIDES THIRD QUARTER 2019 PRODUCTION AND ACQUISITION UPDATE.VIPER ENERGY PARTNERS LP - Q3 2019 PRODUCTION OF 21,265 BOE/D (64% OIL), AN INCREASE OF 9% QUARTER OVER QUARTER.VIPER ENERGY PARTNERS LP - DURING Q3 OF 2019, VIPER ACQUIRED 1,272 NET ROYALTY ACRES FOR AN AGGREGATE PURCHASE PRICE OF APPROXIMATELY $193.6 MILLION.VIPER ENERGY PARTNERS LP - SIGNIFICANT PORTION OF DIAMONDBACK'S EXPECTED 2020 COMPLETIONS WILL BE WELLS ON VIPER'S ACREAGE.
