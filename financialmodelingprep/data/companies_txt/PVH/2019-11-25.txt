PVH Corp. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter of 2019; Revises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year of 2019. PVH Corp. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter of 2019. The company currently projects that fourth quarter 2019 earnings per share on a GAAP basis will be in a range of $1.56 to $1.58 compared to $2.09 in the prior year period. Revenue in the fourth quarter of 2019 is projected to be flat (increase approximately 2% on a constant currency basis) compared to the prior year period.The company currently projects that 2019 earnings per share on a GAAP basis will be in a range of $8.04 to $8.06 compared from $7.95 to $8.05 previously projected. Revenue in 2019 is projected to increase approximately 1% (increase approximately 4% on a constant currency basis) as compared to 2018. EPS outlook includes a negative impact of $0.35 per share related to foreign currency translation, in line with previous guidance.
