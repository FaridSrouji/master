PVH Corp. Names Trish Donnelly to New Role as Chief Executive Officer of PVH Americas. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #APPAREL--PVH CORP. [NYSE:PVH] ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT TRISH DONNELLY, FORMER CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF URBAN OUTFITTERS GROUP, WILL JOIN THE COMPANY AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF PVH AMERICAS. THE ADDITION OF THIS NEW ROLE TO THE LEADERSHIP TEAM IS AN IMPORTANT STEP TOWARD ACHIEVING THE COMPANY'S GLOBAL PRIORITY TO BUILD UPON PVH'S CORE STRENGTHS AND CONNECT THEM MORE CLOSELY WITH WHERE THE CONSUMER IS GOING.
