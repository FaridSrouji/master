Philip Morris International (PMI) Announces New Three-Year Share Repurchase Program of up to $7 Billion, With Target Spending of $5 to $7 Billion; Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend of $1.20 Per Share. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--REGULATORY NEWS: THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (NYSE:PM) TODAY AUTHORIZED A NEW SHARE REPURCHASE PROGRAM OF UP TO $7 BILLION, WITH TARGET SPENDING OF $5 TO $7 BILLION OVER A THREE-YEAR PERIOD EXPECTED TO COMMENCE AFTER THE COMPANY'S SECOND-QUARTER 2021 EARNINGS CALL.
