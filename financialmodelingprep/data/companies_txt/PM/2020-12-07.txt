Philip Morris International Announces U.S. Food and Drug Administration Authorization of IQOS 3 for Sale in the United States. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) TODAY CONFIRMED THAT IQOS 3, PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL'S ELECTRICALLY HEATED TOBACCO SYSTEM, IS APPROPRIATE FOR THE PROTECTION OF PUBLIC HEALTH AND HAS AUTHORIZED IT FOR SALE IN THE UNITED STATES. FDA'S DECISION FOLLOWS AN ASSESSMENT OF A PREMARKET TOBACCO PRODUCT APPLICATION (PMTA) FILED WITH THE AGENCY IN MARCH 2020.
