Philip Morris International's Latest Scientific Update Examines How Cutting-Edge Research Is Opening New Areas of Business. LAUSANNE, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (PMI) (NYSE: PM) TODAY RELEASES THE LATEST ISSUE OF ITS SCIENTIFIC UPDATE, A PUBLICATION DEVOTED TO RESEARCH EFFORTS TO DEVELOP AND ASSESS A RANGE OF SMOKE-FREE ALTERNATIVES TO CIGARETTES, AS WELL AS PRODUCTS BEYOND NICOTINE. IN THIS ISSUE, PMI REVIEWS MILESTONES FROM 2020, EXAMINES ADVANCES IN MULTIPLE FIELDS OF RESEARCH ARISING FROM ITS SMOKE-FREE PRODUCT ASSESSMENTS, AND DISCUSSES THE COMPANY'S NEW RESEARCH INITIATIVES.
