PMI's ESG Performance Recognized by the S&P Dow Jones Indices. LAUSANNE, SWITZERLAND--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (PMI) (NYSE: PM) HAS BEEN INCLUDED FOR THE FIRST TIME IN THE DOW JONES SUSTAINABILITY INDEX (DJSI) NORTH AMERICA, RECOGNIZING ITS EXEMPLARY SUSTAINABILITY PERFORMANCE. MOREOVER, FOR THE THIRD YEAR IN A ROW, PMI LEADS THE INDUSTRY IN DJSI'S INNOVATION MANAGEMENT CATEGORY, WHICH ASSESSES COMPANIES' RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT SPENDING, PRODUCT INNOVATIONS, AND PORTFOLIO OF TOBACCO ALTERNATIVES AND REDUCED-RISK PRODUCTS.
