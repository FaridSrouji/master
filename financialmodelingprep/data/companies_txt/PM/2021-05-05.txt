Philip Morris International Inc. Holds 2021 Virtual Annual Meeting of Shareholders: Appoints André Calantzopoulos as Executive Chairman of the Board and Jacek Olczak as Chief Executive Officer. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--REGULATORY NEWS: PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL INC. (PMI) (NYSE: PM) IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE THAT JACEK OLCZAK WAS APPOINTED CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER FOLLOWING THE COMPANY'S 2021 ANNUAL SHAREHOLDERS MEETING EARLIER TODAY. MR. OLCZAK—MOST RECENTLY THE COMPANY'S CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER—WAS ALSO ELECTED TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ANDRÉ CALANTZOPOULOS, WHO SERVED AS PMI'S CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER FROM 2013, WAS APPOINTED EXECUTIVE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD PRIOR TO THE MEETING. MR
