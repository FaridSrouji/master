U.S. Bancorp Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. U.S. Bancorp announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced net interest income was USD 3,281 million compared to USD 3,251 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,908 million compared to USD 1,815 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.16 compared to USD 1.06 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.15 compared to USD 1.06 a year ago. For the nine months, net interest income was USD 9,845 million compared to USD 9,616 million a year ago. Net income was USD 5,428 million compared to USD 5,240 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.26 compared to USD 3.05 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.25 compared to USD 3.04 a year ago.
