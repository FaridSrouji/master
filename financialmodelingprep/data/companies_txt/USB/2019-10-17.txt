U.S. Bank Hires Peter Gordon as Head of Emerging Payments Product and Strategy. U.S. Bank hired real-time payments industry veteran Peter Gordon to lead emerging payments efforts across the company. In this new role, Gordon will oversee the strategy and product development of emerging payments products and services at U.S. Bank. Gordon has deep experience in banking and payments and most recently served as chief revenue officer at PayFi. Gordon reports to White and is a part of the U.S. Bank digital leadership team.
