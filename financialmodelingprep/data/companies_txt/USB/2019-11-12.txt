U.S. Bancorp Receives Approval From Federal Reserve Board Of Governors. U.S. BANCORP RECEIVES APPROVAL FROM FEDERAL RESERVE BOARD OF GOVERNORS FOR ADDITIONAL CAPITAL DISTRIBUTION.U.S. BANCORP - APPROVED AN AUTHORIZATION, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, TO REPURCHASE UP TO $2.5 BILLION OF ITS OUTSTANDING COMMON STOCK.U.S. BANCORP - STOCK MAY BE REPURCHASED IN OPEN MARKET OR IN PRIVATELY NEGOTIATED TRANSACTIONS THROUGH JUNE 30, 2020.U.S. BANCORP - ACQUIRED SHARES TO BE HELD AS TREASURY SHARES, MAY BE REISSUED FOR VARIOUS CORPORATE PURPOSES.
U.S. Bancorp announces an Increase in Equity Buyback.. On November 12, 2019, the company announced an increase in its equity buyback plan. The company increased its authorization by $2,500 million, thereby bringing the total authorization to $5,500 million.
