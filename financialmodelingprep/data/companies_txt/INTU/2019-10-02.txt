Intuit Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the First-Quarter and Full-Year Fiscal 2020. Intuit Inc. provided earnings guidance for the first-quarter and full-year fiscal 2020. For the first quarter, the company expects revenue of $1.105 billion to $1.125 billion, growth of 9% to 11%, operating loss of $40 million to $50 million and loss per share of $0.02 to $0.04. For the year, the company expects revenue of $7.440 billion to $7.540 billion, growth of 10% to 11%, operating income of $2.065 billion to $2.115 billion, growth of 11% to 14% and diluted earnings per share of $6.35 to $6.45, growth of 8% to 10%.
