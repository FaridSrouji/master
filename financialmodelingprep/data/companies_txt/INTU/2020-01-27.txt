Intuit Recommends Shareholders Reject Mini-Tender Offer By TRC Capital Investment Corp. INTUIT RECOMMENDS SHAREHOLDERS REJECT MINI-TENDER OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT CORPORATION.INTUIT- NOTIFIED OF UNSOLICITED MINI-TENDER OFFER BY TRC CAPITAL INVESTMENT TO BUY UP TO 400,000 SHARES OF INTUIT STOCK AT $270.00 PER SHARE IN CASH.DOES NOT ENDORSE TRC'S OFFER AND RECOMMENDS THAT INTUIT SHAREHOLDERS REJECT OFFER.INTUIT RECOMMENDS THAT SHAREHOLDERS WHO HAVE NOT RESPONDED TO TRC'S OFFER TAKE NO ACTION.
