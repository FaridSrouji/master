Starbucks Announces Chief Financial Officer Succession. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STARBUCKS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: SBUX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT AFTER A MORE THAN 35-YEAR CAREER IN FINANCE, PATRICK GRISMER HAS DECIDED TO RETIRE FROM HIS POSITION AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (CFO) OF THE COMPANY EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 1, 2021. RACHEL RUGGERI, SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT OF FINANCE, AMERICAS AND A 16-YEAR STARBUCKS PARTNER, WILL SUCCEED GRISMER AS EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CFO, EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 1, 2021.
