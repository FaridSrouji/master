Starbucks Reports Q4 And Full Year Fiscal 2019 Results. STARBUCKS REPORTS Q4 AND FULL YEAR FISCAL 2019 RESULTS.Q4 NON-GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.70.Q4 GAAP EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.67.Q4 REVENUE $6.7 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $6.68 BILLION.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.70 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.ACTIVE STARBUCKS REWARDS MEMBERSHIP IN U.S. UP 15% YEAR-OVER-YEAR TO 17.6 MILLION IN QUARTER.ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND INCREASE OF 14% TO $0.41 PER SHARE.INTRODUCES FISCAL 2020 OUTLOOK REAFFIRMING LONG-TERM OPERATING GROWTH MODEL.Q4 COMPARABLE STORE SALES UP 5% GLOBALLY.SEES  2020 GAAP EPS IN RANGE OF $2.84 TO $2.89.SEES 2020 NON-GAAP EPS IN RANGE OF $3.00 TO $3.05.IN Q4 REALIGNED ITS OPERATING SEGMENT REPORTING STRUCTURE.5% COMP GROWTH IN CHINA DURING QUARTER.SEES 2020 CAPITAL EXPENDITURES OF APPROXIMATELY $1.8 BILLION.NO IMPACT TO CONSOLIDATED NET REVENUES, CONSOLIDATED OPERATING INCOME, OR NET EARNINGS PER SHARE AS A RESULT OF CHANGES.SEES 2020 CONSOLIDATED GAAP REVENUE GROWTH OF 6% TO 8%.SEES 2020 GLOBAL COMPARABLE STORE SALES GROWTH OF 3% TO 4%.SEES APPROXIMATELY 2,000 NET NEW STARBUCKS STORES GLOBALLY IN 2020.FY2020 EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $3.08, REVENUE VIEW $28.47 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
