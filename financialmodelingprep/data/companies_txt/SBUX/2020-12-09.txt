Starbucks Announces the Appointment of Mellody Hobson as Non-Executive Chair of the Board. SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--STARBUCKS CORPORATION (NASDAQ: SBUX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT MELLODY HOBSON WILL SERVE AS THE COMPANY'S NEXT NON-EXECUTIVE CHAIR OF ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS STARTING IN MARCH 2021. HOBSON, WHO FIRST JOINED THE BOARD AS AN INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR IN 2005 AND WAS APPOINTED AS THE BOARD'S INDEPENDENT VICE CHAIR IN 2018, WILL SUCCEED MYRON (MIKE) E. ULLMAN, III.
