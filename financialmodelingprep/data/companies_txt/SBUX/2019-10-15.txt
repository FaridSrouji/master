Starbucks Opens First Store in Grand Turk. Starbucks Corp. opened its first store in Grand Turk, the capital island of Turks & Caicos islands, on the Grand Turk Cruise Port. The coffee giant has entered into a deal with Caribbean Coffee Traders Ltd., a Caribbean joint venture led by Margaritaville Caribbean Group, to open the store. The islands of Turks & Caicos is Starbucks' 81st market globally, and 20th market in the Latin America and Caribbean region. The store features a cafe design unique to the island. Its pink exterior keeps the authentic Caribbean feel of the café, while the indoor palette was kept light to keep the tropical theme. The store offers Starbucks signature beverages and food, including ethically sourced, 100% Arabica coffee. The offerings include coffees from around the world, such as the Asia-Pacific and Latin America regions. Last year, while opening one of its stores in Jamaica, Starbucks was reported to be planning new stores in Turks & Caicos and Cayman. In August this year, the chain opened a store in Cayman at Camana Bay, which is the first of four coffee shops it plans to open in Grand Cayman over the next three years. In mid-September, the company announced its plan to open the world's Starbucks Reserve Roastery on November 15 on Chicago's Magnificent Mile. The Chicago roastery is the last and of the chain's grand coffee stores. Starbucks had opened its first store outside of Seattle in Chicago, its birthplace, nearly 30 years ago.
