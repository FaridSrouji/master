Waste Management, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Waste Management, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 3,967 million compared to USD 3,822 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 734 million compared to USD 699 million a year ago. Net income was USD 495 million compared to USD 499 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.17 compared to USD 1.16 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.16 compared to USD 1.16 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 11,609 million compared to USD 11,072 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,051 million compared to USD 2,022 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,223 million compared to USD 1,394 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.88 compared to USD 3.24 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.86 compared to USD 3.22 a year ago.
