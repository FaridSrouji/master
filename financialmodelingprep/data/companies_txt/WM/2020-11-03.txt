Waste Management Announces the Redemption of Senior Notes Due In 2021. HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Waste Management (NYSE: WM) announced today that it will redeem the entire outstanding principal amount of its 4.60% Senior Notes due 2021 (the “Notes”). The redemption date for the Notes is December 3, 2020 (the “Redemption Date”). The aggregate principal amount of the Notes outstanding is $400 million.
