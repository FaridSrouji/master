Fox Corporation Hires Brian Nick as Chief Communications Officer & Executive Vice President. NEW YORK AND LOS ANGELES, JULY 7, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOX CORPORATION (NASDAQ: FOXA, FOX) (THE "COMPANY" OR "FOX") TODAY ANNOUNCED THE COMPANY'S NEW HEAD OF COMMUNICATIONS.  BRIAN NICK HAS BEEN NAMED EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER.
