Baker Hughes Wins Major Contracts from Petrobras for Flexible Pipes Across Five Offshore Fields. HOUSTON & RIO DE JANEIRO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BAKER HUGHES ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS BEEN AWARDED TWO FLEXIBLE PIPE CONTRACTS BY PETROBRAS IN THE SECOND QUARTER OF 2021. THE FIRST CONTRACT COVERS UP TO 96 KILOMETERS OF FLEXIBLE PIPE FOR THE SAPINHOÁ AND TUPI FIELDS AND THE SECOND CONTRACT COVERS UP TO 226 KILOMETERS OF FLEXIBLE PIPE FOR THE MARLIM 2 AND ITAPU FIELDS.
