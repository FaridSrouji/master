Unit Of Baker Hughes Co, Intends To Change Name To Baker Hughes Holdings. BAKER HUGHES - BAKER HUGHES, A GE CO, LLC, UNIT OF BAKER HUGHES CO, INTENDS TO CHANGE NAME TO BAKER HUGHES HOLDINGS LLC, EFFECTIVE APRIL 15.
Baker Hughes Announces March 2020 Rig Counts. BAKER HUGHES ANNOUNCES MARCH 2020 RIG COUNTS.BAKER HUGHES CO - BAKER HUGHES INTERNATIONAL RIG COUNT FOR MARCH 2020 WAS 1,059, DOWN 26 FROM 1,085 COUNTED IN FEBRUARY 2020.BAKER HUGHES CO - INTERNATIONAL OFFSHORE RIG COUNT FOR MARCH 2020 WAS 244, DOWN 1 FROM 245 COUNTED IN FEBRUARY 2020.BAKER HUGHES CO - AVERAGE U.S. RIG COUNT FOR MARCH 2020 WAS 772, DOWN 19 FROM 791 COUNTED IN FEBRUARY 2020.BAKER HUGHES CO - AVERAGE CANADIAN RIG COUNT FOR MARCH 2020 WAS 133, DOWN 116 FROM 249 COUNTED IN FEBRUARY 2020.BAKER HUGHES CO - WORLDWIDE RIG COUNT FOR MARCH 2020 WAS 1,964, DOWN 161 FROM 2,125 COUNTED IN FEBRUARY 2020.
