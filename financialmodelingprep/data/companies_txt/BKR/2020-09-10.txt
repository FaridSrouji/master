Pelican Energy Announces Agreement To Buy Unit Of Baker Hughes. PELICAN ENERGY PARTNERS ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO ACQUIRE THE SURFACE PRESSURE CONTROL FLOW BUSINESS UNIT OF BAKER HUGHES.PELICAN ENERGY PARTNERS - BAKER HUGHES WILL RETAIN SURFACE PRESSURE CONTROL PROJECTS BUSINESS AFTER DEAL.PELICAN ENERGY PARTNERS - AGREEMENT TO PURCHASE ASSETS OF SURFACE PRESSURE CONTROL FLOW BUSINESS UNIT OF OILFIELD EQUIPMENT SEGMENT OF BAKER HUGHES.PELICAN ENERGY PARTNERS - CO  HAS SIGNED A BINDING AGREEMENT TO PURCHASE SPC FLOW BUSINESS UNIT, WHICH IS EXPECTED TO CLOSE IN Q4 OF 2020.
