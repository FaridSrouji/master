Baker Hughes Reports May 2020 Rig Counts. BAKER HUGHES ANNOUNCES MAY 2020 RIG COUNTS.BAKER HUGHES CO - INTERNATIONAL RIG COUNT FOR MAY 2020 WAS 805 DOWN 110 FROM 915 COUNTED IN APRIL 2020.BAKER HUGHES CO - INTERNATIONAL OFFSHORE RIG COUNT FOR MAY 2020 WAS 195, DOWN 33 FROM 228 COUNTED IN APRIL 2020.BAKER HUGHES CO - AVERAGE U.S. RIG COUNT FOR MAY 2020 WAS 348, DOWN 218 FROM 566 COUNTED IN APRIL 2020.BAKER HUGHES CO - AVERAGE CANADIAN RIG COUNT FOR MAY 2020 WAS 23, DOWN 10 FROM 33 COUNTED IN APRIL 2020.BAKER HUGHES CO - WORLDWIDE RIG COUNT FOR MAY 2020 WAS 1,176, DOWN 338 FROM 1,514 COUNTED IN APRIL 2020.
