the Charles Schwab Corporation Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. The Charles Schwab Corporation announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 2,711 million compared to USD 2,579 million a year ago. Net income was USD 951 million compared to USD 923 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.7 compared to USD 0.66 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.7 compared to USD 0.65 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 8,115 million compared to USD 7,463 million a year ago. Net income was USD 2,852 million compared to USD 2,572 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.06 compared to USD 1.81 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.05 compared to USD 1.79 a year ago.
