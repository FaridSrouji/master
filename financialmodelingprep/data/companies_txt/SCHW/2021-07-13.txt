INVESTOR ALERT: Kirby McInerney LLP Announces an Investigation of Shareholder Claims Against Charles Schwab Corp.. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)---- $SCHW--THE LAW FIRM OF KIRBY MCINERNEY LLP IS INVESTIGATING POTENTIAL CLAIMS AGAINST CHARLES SCHWAB CORPORATION (“CHARLES SCHWAB” OR THE “COMPANY”) (NYSE: SCHW). THE INVESTIGATION CONCERNS WHETHER SCHWAB HAS VIOLATED THE FEDERAL SECURITIES LAWS AND/OR ENGAGED IN OTHER UNLAWFUL BUSINESS PRACTICES. ON JULY 2, 2021, CHARLES SCHWAB SAID IT WILL TAKE A $200 MILLION CHARGE IN THE SECOND QUARTER RELATED TO A U.S. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION PROBE OF ITS ROBO-ADVISER PLATFORM
