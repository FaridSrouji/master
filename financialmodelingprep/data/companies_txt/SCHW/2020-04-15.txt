Charles Schwab Q1 Earnings Per Share $0.58. SCHWAB REPORTS FIRST QUARTER EARNINGS PER SHARE OF $.58, INCLUDING $.04 PER SHARE OF ACQUISITION AND PANDEMIC RESPONSE EXPENSES.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.58.Q1 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.62 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.QTRLY NET REVENUES $2,617 MILLION VERSUS $2,723 MILLION.CHARLES SCHWAB - Q1 RESULTS INCLUDE ACQUISITION-RELATED EXPENSES AS WELL AS EXPENSES TOTALING ABOUT $27 MILLION RELATING TO COVID-19.AT QUARTER-END, TOTAL CLIENT ASSETS WERE $3.50 TRILLION VERSUS $3.59 TRILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.TOTAL ACTIVE BROKERAGE ACCOUNTS AT QUARTER END TO 12.7 MILLION, UP 8% FROM MARCH 2019.DURING QUARTER HANDLED OVER 190 MILLION MORE CLIENT INTERACTIONS ACROSS WEB, MOBILE, CHAT, AND MESSAGING VERSUS A YEAR AGO.CLIENTS OPENED A RECORD 609,000 NEW BROKERAGE ACCOUNTS IN QUARTER – OVER 280,000 IN MARCH ALONE.CHARLES SCHWAB - TOTAL ACTIVE BROKERAGE ACCOUNTS AT QUARTER END OF 12.7 MILLION, UP 8% FROM MARCH 2019.Q1 REVENUE VIEW $2.61 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.
