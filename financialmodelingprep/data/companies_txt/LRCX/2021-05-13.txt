Lam Research Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividend. FREMONT, CALIF., MAY 13, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LAM RESEARCH CORPORATION (NASDAQ: LRCX) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS APPROVED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.30 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK. THE DIVIDEND PAYMENT WILL BE MADE JULY 7, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 16, 2021. FUTURE DIVIDEND PAYMENTS ARE SUBJECT TO REVIEW AND APPROVAL BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
