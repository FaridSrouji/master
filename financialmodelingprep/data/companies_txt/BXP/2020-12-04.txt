Boston Properties Earns Top ESG Rating in 2020 GRESB Assessment. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BOSTON PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: BXP), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY-TRADED DEVELOPER, OWNER AND MANAGER OF CLASS A OFFICE PROPERTIES IN THE UNITED STATES, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS EARNED A TOP ESG RATING IN THE 2020 GLOBAL REAL ESTATE SUSTAINABILITY BENCHMARK (GRESB®) ASSESSMENT. BXP EARNED A NINTH CONSECUTIVE “GREEN STAR” RECOGNITION AND THE HIGHEST GRESB 5-STAR RATING, AS WELL AS AN “A” DISCLOSURE SCORE.
