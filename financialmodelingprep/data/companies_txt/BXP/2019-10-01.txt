Boston Properties And Canada Pension Plan Investment Board Announce Joint Venture To Develop Class A Office Campus In San Jose, California. BOSTON PROPERTIES AND CANADA PENSION PLAN INVESTMENT BOARD ANNOUNCE JOINT VENTURE TO DEVELOP CLASS A OFFICE CAMPUS IN SAN JOSE, CALIFORNIA.BOSTON PROPERTIES - CPPIB WILL HAVE A 45% OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN PLATFORM 16 JOINT VENTURE.BOSTON PROPERTIES - WILL RETAIN REMAINING 55% OWNERSHIP STAKE IN JV AND PROVIDE CUSTOMARY DEVELOPMENT, PROPERTY MANAGEMENT AND LEASING SERVICES.BOSTON PROPERTIES INC - BOSTON PROPERTIES HAS SECURED APPROVALS AND ENTITLEMENTS FOR DEVELOPMENT OF PLATFORM 16.BOSTON PROPERTIES INC - CONSTRUCTION FOR DEVELOPMENT OF PLATFORM 16 COULD COMMENCE IN NEXT SIX MONTHS.
