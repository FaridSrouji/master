Boston Properties Announces Redemption of 4.125% Senior Notes Due 2021. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BOSTON PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: BXP), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY-TRADED DEVELOPER, OWNER AND MANAGER OF CLASS A OFFICE PROPERTIES IN THE UNITED STATES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS OPERATING PARTNERSHIP, BOSTON PROPERTIES LIMITED PARTNERSHIP, ISSUED A NOTICE TO REDEEM IN FULL THE $850.0 MILLION IN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF ITS 4.125% SENIOR NOTES DUE 2021 (THE “NOTES”) AT A REDEMPTION PRICE EQUAL TO PAR PLUS ACCRUED AND UNPAID INTEREST TO THE REDEMPTION DATE.
