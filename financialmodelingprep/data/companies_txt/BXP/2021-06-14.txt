Boston Properties Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend. BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--BOSTON PROPERTIES, INC. (NYSE: BXP), THE LARGEST PUBLICLY TRADED DEVELOPER, OWNER, AND MANAGER OF CLASS A OFFICE PROPERTIES IN THE UNITED STATES, ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A REGULAR QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND OF $0.98 PER SHARE OF COMMON STOCK FOR THE PERIOD APRIL 1, 2021 TO JUNE 30, 2021 PAYABLE ON JULY 30, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AS OF THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON JUNE 30, 2021.
