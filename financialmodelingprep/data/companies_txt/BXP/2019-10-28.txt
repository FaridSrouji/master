Boston Properties Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Boston Properties Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 743.553 million compared to USD 686.284 million a year ago. Net income was USD 110.396 million compared to USD 121.743 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.7 compared to USD 0.77 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 2,203.061 million compared to USD 2,011.919 million a year ago. Net income was USD 378.075 million compared to USD 431.710 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 2.4 compared to USD 2.74 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 2.39 compared to USD 2.74 a year ago.
