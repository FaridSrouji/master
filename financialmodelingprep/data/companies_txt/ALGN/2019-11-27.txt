U.S. ITC Affirms ALJ Finding That 3Shape Infringes 3 Align Technology Patents But Declines To Find Section 337 Violation. ALIGN TECHNOLOGY - INTERNATIONAL TRADE COMMISSION AFFIRMS ALJ FINDING 3SHAPE INFRINGES 3 OF CO'S PATENTS BUT DECLINES TO FIND SECTION 337 VIOLATION.ALIGN TECHNOLOGY - CO REVIEWING U.S. ITC DECISION TO DECLINE TO FIND A VIOLATION OF SECTION 337 AND WILL CONSIDER APPROPRIATE RESPONSE.
