Align Technology, Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter 2019. Align Technology, Inc. provided earnings guidance for the fourth quarter 2019. For the quarter, net revenues are expected to be in the range of $640 million to $650 million, up approximately 20% to 22% over the same period a year ago. Invisalign case shipments are expected to be in the range of 400 thousand to 407 thousand, up approximately 20% to 22% over the same period a year ago. Diluted EPS is expected to be in the range of $1.35 to $1.42.
