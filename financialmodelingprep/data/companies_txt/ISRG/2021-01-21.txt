Intuitive Announces Fourth Quarter Earnings. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., JAN. 21, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- INTUITIVE (THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: ISRG), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER IN MINIMALLY INVASIVE CARE AND THE PIONEER OF ROBOTIC-ASSISTED SURGERY, TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2020. REPORTED REVENUE AND PROCEDURE RESULTS ARE CONSISTENT WITH THE COMPANY'S PRESS RELEASE ISSUED ON JANUARY 13, 2021.
