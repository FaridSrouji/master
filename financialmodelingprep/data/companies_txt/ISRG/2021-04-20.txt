Intuitive Announces First Quarter Earnings. SUNNYVALE, CALIF., APRIL 20, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- INTUITIVE (THE “COMPANY”) (NASDAQ: ISRG), A GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY LEADER IN MINIMALLY INVASIVE CARE AND THE PIONEER OF ROBOTIC-ASSISTED SURGERY, TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED MARCH 31, 2021.
