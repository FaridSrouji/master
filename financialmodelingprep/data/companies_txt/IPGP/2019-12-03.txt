IPG Photonics Corporation Announces Retirement of Henry E. Gauthier from Board of Directors. IPG Photonics Corporation announced that Henry E. Gauthier, who has served on Board of Directors since 2006, informed the Company on September 6, 2019 that he has decided to retire from the Board and will not seek re-election at the 2020 annual meeting of stockholders. His retirement will be effective on the date of the 2020 annual meeting of stockholders. Mr. Gauthier joined the Company's Board in 2006.
