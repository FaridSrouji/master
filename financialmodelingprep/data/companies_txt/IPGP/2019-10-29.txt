Ipg Photonics Announces Third Quarter 2019 Financial Results. IPG PHOTONICS ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2019 FINANCIAL RESULTS.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE $1.07.Q3 REVENUE $329 MILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $336.9 MILLION.Q3 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $1.21 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES Q4 2019 REVENUE $270 MILLION TO $300 MILLION.SEES Q4 EARNINGS PER DILUTED SHARE IN RANGE OF $0.55 TO $0.95.IN QUARTER, DESPITE CHALLENGING MACROECONOMIC BACKDROP & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE IN CHINA, WE DEMONSTRATED GOOD PROGRESS.IN RESPONSE TO CURRENT BUSINESS ENVIRONMENT, INITIATED EXPENSE REDUCTION INITIATIVES DURING QUARTER & ASSESS COST STRUCTURE.
