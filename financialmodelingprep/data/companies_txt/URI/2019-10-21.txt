United Rentals Announces Full Redemption Of 4.625% Senior Secured Notes Due 2023. UNITED RENTALS ANNOUNCES FULL REDEMPTION OF 4.625% SENIOR SECURED NOTES DUE 2023.UNITED RENTALS- CO'S UNIT UNITED RENTALS (NORTH AMERICA) HAS GIVEN NOTICE OF INTENTION TO REDEEM ALL OUTSTANDING $1 BILLION SENIOR NOTES ON NOV. 20.UNITED RENTALS - NOTES WILL BE REDEEMED AT A REDEMPTION PRICE OF 102.313% OF PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF NOTES.
