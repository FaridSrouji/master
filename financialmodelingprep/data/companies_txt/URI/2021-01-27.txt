(NYSE: URI) TODAY ANNOUNCED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER 2020 AND REPORTED ITS FULL YEAR RESULTS ON FORM 10-K1. FOURTH QUARTER 2020 HIGHLIGHTS TOTAL REVENUE OF $2.279 BILLION, INCLUDING RENTAL REVENUE2 OF $1.854 BILLION.
