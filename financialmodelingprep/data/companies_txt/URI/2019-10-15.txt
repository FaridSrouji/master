United Rentals, Inc. Revises Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019; Reports Asset Impairment Charge for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. United Rentals, Inc. revised earnings guidance for the year 2019. For the year, the company expects total revenue in the range of $9.25 billion to $9.35 billion against the prior outlook of $9.15 billion to $9.45 billion.For the third quarter ended September 30, 2019, the company reported asset impairment charge per share of $0.02.
