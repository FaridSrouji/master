United Rentals Completes the Acquisition of General Finance Corporation. STAMFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--UNITED RENTALS, INC. (NYSE: URI) (“UNITED RENTALS”) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS COMPLETED THE PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED ACQUISITION OF GENERAL FINANCE CORPORATION (NASDAQ: GFN) (“GENERAL FINANCE”). MATTHEW FLANNERY, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF UNITED RENTALS, SAID, “WE'RE DELIGHTED TO WELCOME OUR NEW CUSTOMERS AND TEAM MEMBERS TO UNITED RENTALS. THIS IS AN ACQUISITION WITH STRONG STRATEGIC AND FINANCIAL MERITS, TIMED TO SERVE THE INCREASING DEMAND IN OUR END MARKETS.
