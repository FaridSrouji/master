Servicenow, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. ServiceNow, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 885.833 million compared to USD 673.097 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 56.324 million compared to USD 9.568 million a year ago. Net income was USD 40.598 million compared to USD 8.405 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.22 compared to USD 0.05 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.21 compared to USD 0.04 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 2,508.663 million compared to USD 1,893.375 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 13.611 million compared to operating loss of USD 42.704 million a year ago. Net income was USD 27.974 million compared to net loss of USD 33.719 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 0.15 compared to basic loss per share of USD 0.19 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 0.14 compared to diluted loss per share of USD 0.19 a year ago.
