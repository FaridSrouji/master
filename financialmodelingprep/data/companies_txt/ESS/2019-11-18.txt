(NYSE:ESS) acquired Velo Fremont apartments and Ray apartments in Seattle from Greystar Real Estate Partners, LLC for approximately $130 million on November 19, 2019. As reported, Essex Property Trust bought Velo Fremont Apartments at 3625 Woodland Park Ave. N. for $71.5 million, and Ray Apartments at 3636 Stone Way N. and 3651 Interlake Ave. N. for $61.5 million.
Essex Property Trust, Inc. (NYSE:ESS) completed the acquisition of Velo Fremont apartments and Ray apartments in Seattle from Greystar Real Estate Partners, LLC on November 19, 2019.
