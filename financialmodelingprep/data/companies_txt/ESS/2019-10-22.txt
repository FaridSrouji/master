Essex Property Trust, Inc. Provides Earnings Results for the Third Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2019; Revises Earnings Guidance for 2019. Essex Property Trust, Inc. provided earnings results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2019. For the quarter, the company reported net income per diluted share of $1.51 compared to $1.22 in the third quarter of 2018. Net income available to common stockholders was $99,335,000 as compared to $80,975,000 for the same period last year. For the nine months, the company reported net income available to common stockholders was $310,468,000 as compared to $272,333,000 for the same period last year. Net income per diluted share of $4.71 compared to $4.12 a year ago.For the full year, the company revised increased net income per diluted share guidance range to $6.04 to $6.14.
