West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc. Raises Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc. raised earnings guidance for the full year 2019. The company is raising its full-year 2019 net sales guidance to a new range of between $1.815 billion and $1.825 billion, compared to the prior guidance range of between $1.810 billion and $1.825 billion. Organic sales growth is expected to be approximately 8%. Net sales guidance includes a headwind of $54 million for the full-year 2019 based on current foreign exchange rates, compared to prior guidance of a full-year negative impact of $42 million.
