ResMed Announces Participation in the Goldman Sachs 42nd Annual Global Healthcare Conference. SAN DIEGO, JUNE 02, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RESMED (NYSE: RMD, ASX: RMD) TODAY ANNOUNCED ROB DOUGLAS, PRESIDENT AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER, WILL PARTICIPATE IN A VIRTUAL FIRESIDE CHAT AT THE GOLDMAN SACHS 42ND ANNUAL GLOBAL HEALTHCARE CONFERENCE ON TUESDAY, JUNE 8, 2021, BEGINNING AT APPROXIMATELY 1:20 P.M. (EASTERN DAYLIGHT TIME) VIA VIDEO WEBCAST.
