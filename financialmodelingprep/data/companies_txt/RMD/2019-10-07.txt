ResMed Inc., Annual General Meeting, Nov 21, 2019. ResMed Inc., Annual General Meeting, Nov 21, 2019, at 10:00 Pacific Standard Time. Location: corporate office located at 9001 Spectrum Center Boulevard San Diego California United States Agenda: To elect three directors, each to serve until 2022 annual meeting and until their successors are elected and qualified; to ratify selection of KPMG LLP as independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2020; to approve, on an advisory basis, the compensation paid to named executive officers; and to transact other business that may properly come before the meeting.
