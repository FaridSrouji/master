ResMed Announces Participation in the 23rd Annual Macquarie Australia Conference. SAN DIEGO, APRIL 27, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RESMED (NYSE: RMD, ASX: RMD) TODAY ANNOUNCED BRETT SANDERCOCK, CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER, WILL PRESENT VIRTUALLY AT THE MACQUARIE AUSTRALIA CONFERENCE ON WEDNESDAY, MAY 5, 2021, BEGINNING AT APPROXIMATELY 8:45 A.M. (AUSTRALIAN EASTERN STANDARD TIME) VIA VIDEO WEBCAST.
