ResMed Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend for the Period Ended September 30, 2019, Payable on December 12, 2019. ResMed Inc. announced that board of directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of USD 0.03900000 per share for the period ended September 30, 2019. The dividend will have a record date of November 7, 2019, payable on December 12, 2019. Ex-date is November 6, 2019.
