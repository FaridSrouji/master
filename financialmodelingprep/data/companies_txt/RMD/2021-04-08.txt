ResMed to Report Third Quarter Fiscal 2021 Earnings on April 29, 2021. SAN DIEGO, APRIL 08, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- RESMED (NYSE: RMD, ASX: RMD) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT PLANS TO RELEASE FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL YEAR 2021 ON THURSDAY, APRIL 29, 2021, AFTER THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE CLOSES. FOLLOWING THE RELEASE, RESMED MANAGEMENT WILL HOST A WEBCAST TO DISCUSS THESE RESULTS. OTHER FORWARD-LOOKING AND MATERIAL INFORMATION MAY ALSO BE DISCUSSED DURING THIS WEBCAST.
