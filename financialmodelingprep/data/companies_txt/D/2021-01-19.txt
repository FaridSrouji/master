Dominion Energy Acquires Solar Energy Project in Ohio from Invenergy. RICHMOND, VA., JAN. 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY (NYSE: D) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ONE OF ITS SUBSIDIARIES HAS ACQUIRED THE 150-MEGAWATT (AC) HARDIN SOLAR GENERATING FACILITY, WHICH RECENTLY ENTERED SERVICE IN HARDIN COUNTY, OHIO, FROM CHICAGO-BASED INVENERGY.
