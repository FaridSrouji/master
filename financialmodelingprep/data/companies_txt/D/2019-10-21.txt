Dominion Energy Announces Equity Recapitalization of Cove Point. DOMINION ENERGY ANNOUNCES EQUITY RECAPITALIZATION OF COVE POINT.DOMINION ENERGY INC - AGREEMENT WITH BROOKFIELD SUPER-CORE INFRASTRUCTURE PARTNERS.DOMINION ENERGY INC - COMPANY WILL TRANSFER A 25 PERCENT NON-CONTROLLING EQUITY INTEREST IN COVE POINT TO BROOKFIELD.DOMINION ENERGY INC - DEAL FOR CASH CONSIDERATION OF JUST OVER $2 BILLION, EXCLUDING WORKING CAPITAL.
Brookfield Super-Core Infrastructure Partners L.P. managed by Brookfield Asset Management Inc. (TSX:BAM.A) (Brookfield) entered into an recapitalization agreement to acquire 25% stake in Dominion Energy Cove Point LNG, LP from Dominion Energy, Inc. (NYSE:D) for approximately $2 billion.. Brookfield Super-Core Infrastructure Partners L.P. managed by Brookfield Asset Management Inc. (TSX:BAM.A) (Brookfield) entered into an recapitalization agreement to acquire 25% stake in Dominion Energy Cove Point LNG, LP from Dominion Energy, Inc. (NYSE:D) for approximately $2 billion on October 21, 2019. Brookfield will make the payment in cash which excludes working capital. The transaction is expected to be completed by the end of 2019. The proceeds are expected to be used for general corporate purposes including significantly reducing the annual common equity financing described at the Dominion's investor day in March 2019. Jay Horine, David Harkin, Andrew Castaldo, Alex Berezner, Jared Hendrick and Eric Anderson of J.P. Morgan acted as the financial advisors and Joanne Katsantonis, Jay Hughes and Colin Bumby of McGuireWoods LLP acted as the legal advisors to Dominion Energy. Doug Bacon, Allan Kirk, Roald Nashi, Rustin Brown, Brian Greene, Brooksany Barrowes, Nic Gladd, David Wheat and Alexandra Farmer of Kirkland & Ellis LLP acted as the legal advisors to Brookfield.
