Dominion Energy Declares Quarterly Dividend Payable on Dec. 20, 2019. The board of directors of Dominion Energy has declared a quarterly dividend of 91.75 cents per share of common stock. Dividends are payable on Dec. 20, 2019, to shareholders of record at the close of business Dec. 6, 2019.
