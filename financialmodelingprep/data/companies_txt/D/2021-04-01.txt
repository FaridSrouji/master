Dominion Energy Offers $1 Million in Critical Community Needs Grants. RICHMOND, VA., APRIL 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DOMINION ENERGY IS INVITING NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS ACROSS THE COMPANY'S FOOTPRINT TO APPLY FOR GRANTS OF UP TO $25,000 EACH TO MEET CRITICAL NEEDS IN COMMUNITIES.
