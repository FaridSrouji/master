SKF Recognized by General Motors as a 2020 Supplier of the Year. LANSDALE, PA., JULY 12, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SKF, A LEADING GLOBAL MANUFACTURER OF BEARINGS, SEALS, AND LUBRICATION, HAS BEEN NAMED A GM SUPPLIER OF THE YEAR BY GENERAL MOTORS FOR 2020.
