GM and LG Energy Solution Investing $2.3 Billion in 2nd Ultium Cells Manufacturing Plant in U.S.. NASHVILLE, TENN., APRIL 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- 2.8 MILLION SQUARE-FOOT FACILITY IN SPRING HILL, TENNESSEE, WILL CREATE 1,300 NEW MANUFACTURING JOBS NEW PLANT WILL SIGNIFICANTLY INCREASE GM'S ABILITY TO LEAD IN THE PRODUCTION OF BATTERIES AT SCALE LEADING ULTIUM BATTERY TECHNOLOGY IS AT THE HEART OF GM'S EV STRATEGY ULTIUM CELLS LLC, A JOINT VENTURE OF LG ENERGY SOLUTION AND GENERAL MOTORS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A MORE THAN $2.3 BILLION INVESTMENT TO BUILD ITS SECOND BATTERY CELL MANUFACTURING PLANT IN THE UNITED STATES.
