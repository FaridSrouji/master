GM, Isuzu Announce $175 Mln Investment To Build New Diesel Engine Components Plant In Brookville, Ohio. GENERAL MOTORS - CO, ISUZU ANNOUNCED A $175 MILLION INVESTMENT THROUGH ITS DMAX JV TO BUILD AN ALL-NEW, DIESEL ENGINE COMPONENTS PLANT IN BROOKVILLE, OHIO.GENERAL MOTORS CO - CONSTRUCTION ON NEW BROOKVILLE, OHIO FACILITY WILL BE COMPLETED BY END OF 2020.
