GM Appoints Dane Parker As Chief Sustainability Officer. GENERAL MOTORS CO - HAS APPOINTED DANE PARKER AS THE COMPANY’S CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER, EFFECTIVE FEB. 1, 2020.GM - PARKER, CURRENTLY VICE PRESIDENT OF SUSTAINABLE WORKPLACES, EXPANDS CURRENT ROLE WITH NEWLY-CREATED POSITION AS CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER.GM - NEW POSITION OF CHIEF SUSTAINABILITY OFFICER CREATED TO "ACCELERATE COMPANY’S PLANS FOR A ZERO EMISSIONS FUTURE".
