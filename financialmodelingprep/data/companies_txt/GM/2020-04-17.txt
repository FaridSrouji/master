General Motors Enters Into 364-Day Revolving Credit Agreement With JPMorgan Chase Bank. GENERAL MOTORS CO - ON APRIL 14, CO ENTERED INTO A 364-DAY REVOLVING CREDIT AGREEMENT WITH JPMORGAN CHASE BANK, N.A..GENERAL MOTORS CO - GM HAS ALLOCATED FACILITY FOR EXCLUSIVE USE BY GENERAL MOTORS FINANCIAL COMPANY.GENERAL MOTORS CO - FACILITY IS UNSECURED, PROVIDES AVAILABLE BORROWING CAPACITY OF $1.95 BILLION, AND MATURES ON APRIL 13, 2021.GENERAL MOTORS CO - FACILITY ALLOWS FOR BORROWING IN U.S. DOLLARS ONLY..GENERAL MOTORS CO - GM HAS GUARANTEED OBLIGATIONS OF SUBSIDIARY BORROWERS UNDER FACILITY..GENERAL MOTORS CO - FACILITY REQUIRES THAT GM MAINTAIN AT LEAST $4.0 BILLION IN GLOBAL LIQUIDITY AND AT LEAST $2.0 BILLION IN U.S. LIQUIDITY.
