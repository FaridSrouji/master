General Motors Co - Expects Covid-19 Will Materially Impact Results Of Operations During Remainder Of 2020. GENERAL MOTORS CO SAYS AS RESULT OF COVID-19, ESTIMATE IMPACT ON EBIT-ADJUSTED OF ABOUT $1.4 BILLION IN THREE MONTHS ENDED MARCH 31, 2020.GENERAL MOTORS CO - EXPECTS COVID-19 WILL MATERIALLY IMPACT RESULTS OF OPERATIONS DURING REMAINDER OF 2020.GENERAL MOTORS CO SAYS ON TRACK FOR TRANSFORMATION ACTIVITIES TO DRIVE BETWEEN $5.5 BILLION AND $6.0 BILLION OF ANNUAL CASH SAVINGS BY END OF 2020.GENERAL MOTORS CO SAYS ON TRACK TO REDUCE FY 2020 CAPITAL EXPENDITURES TO ABOUT $7.0 BILLION FROM ABOUT $8.5 BILLION ON NORMALIZED RUN-RATE BASIS.
General Motors Targeting To Restart Majority Of Manufacturing Operations On May 18 In U.S. And Canada. GENERAL MOTORS CO - TARGETING TO RESTART THE MAJORITY OF MANUFACTURING OPERATIONS ON MAY 18 IN THE U.S. AND CANADA.GENERAL MOTORS CO - QTRLY GM SALES IN THE U.S. DECLINED ABOUT 7 PERCENT, DRIVEN BY THE EFECTS OF THE PANDEMIC..GENERAL MOTORS CO - RECORDED TOTAL AFTER TAX CASH AND NON-CASH CHARGES OF $0.7 BILLION IN THE FIRST QUARTER RELATED TO INTERNATIONAL RESTRUCTURING.GENERAL MOTORS CO - CONSIDERABLE PLANNING UNDERWAY TO RESTART OPERATIONS IN NORTH AMERICA.GENERAL MOTORS CO QTRLY GM FINANCIAL EBIT-ADJUSTED $0.2 BILLION VERSUS POSITIVE EBIT-ADJUSTED OF $0.4 BILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.GENERAL MOTORS CO - GM FINANCIAL IS OFFERING SOLUTIONS FOR CUSTOMERS, INCLUDING DEFERRING PAYMENTS AND WAIVING LATE FEES.GENERAL MOTORS - ENDED QUARTER WITH STRONG $33.4 BILLION IN AUTOMOTIVE LIQUIDITY, INCL. ABOUT $16 BILLION DRAWDOWN FROM REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITIES.GENERAL MOTORS CO QTRLY GM CRUISE EBIT-ADJUSTED NEGATIVE $0.2 BILLION VERSUS NEGATIVE EBIT-ADJUSTED OF $0.2 BILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.GENERAL MOTORS CO -  IN CHINA, FOLLOWING STRONGEST SALES IMPACT IN FEBRUARY, INDUSTRY STARTED TO PICK UP IN MARCH, NARROWING MONTHLY SALES DECLINE.GENERAL MOTORS CO - PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT WORK ON COMPANY’S FUTURE EV AND AV PORTFOLIOS IS PROGRESSING AT A RAPID PACE.GENERAL MOTORS CO - QTRLY GM INTERNATIONAL EBIT-ADJUSTED NEGATIVE $0.6 BILLION VERSUS $0.0 BILLION REPORTED LAST YEAR.GENERAL MOTORS CO - QTRLY TOTAL WORLDWIDE VEHICLE SALES 1,457,000 VERSUS 1,878,000.
GM says Expect To Contribute $0.1 bln To U.S. Non-Qualified Pension Plans Over Remainder Of 2020. GM - EXPECT TO CONTRIBUTE $0.1B TO U.S. NON-QUALIFIED PENSION PLANS AND APPROXIMATELY $0.4B TO NON-US PENSION PLANS OVER REMAINDER OF 2020.GM - EXPECT NO SIGNIFICANT MANDATORY CONTRIBUTIONS TO U.S. QUALIFIED PENSION PLANS OVER NEXT FIVE YEARS.GENERAL MOTORS CO - QTRLY U.S. DEALER INVENTORY DOWN ON A YOY BASIS AS A RESULT OF LOWER PRODUCTION.GM - CONTINUE TO EXPECT DIVIDENDS TO BE PAID FROM CHINA OPERATIONS BETWEEN Q2 TO Q4.GM - RESTRICTED FROM REPURCHASING SHARES UNTIL CO NO LONGER HAS OUTSTANDING BORROWINGS UNDER CREDIT FACILITIES, EXCEPT FOR 3-YEAR $3.0B FACILITY.GM SAYS RESTRICTED FROM PAYING DIVIDENDS ON COMMON SHARES IF BORROWINGS UNDER FACILITIES EXCEED $5.0B, EXCEPT FOR 3-YEAR $3.0B FACILITY - PRESENTATION.
