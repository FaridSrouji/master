Ultium Cells LLC and Li-Cycle Collaborate to Expand Recycling in North America. DETROIT, MAY 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- ULTIUM CELLS LLC, A JOINT VENTURE BETWEEN GENERAL MOTORS AND LG ENERGY SOLUTION, ANNOUNCED AN AGREEMENT TODAY WITH LI-CYCLE TO RECYCLE UP TO 100 PERCENT OF THE MATERIAL SCRAP FROM BATTERY CELL MANUFACTURING. THE NEW RECYCLING PROCESS WILL ALLOW ULTIUM CELLS TO RECYCLE BATTERY MATERIALS, INCLUDING COBALT, NICKEL, LITHIUM, GRAPHITE, COPPER, MANGANESE AND ALUMINUM.
