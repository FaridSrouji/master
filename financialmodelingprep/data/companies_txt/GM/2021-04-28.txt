To Put 'Everybody In' an Electric Vehicle, GM Introduces Ultium Charge 360. DETROIT, APRIL 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- TODAY GENERAL MOTORS INTRODUCED ULTIUM CHARGE 360, A HOLISTIC CHARGING APPROACH THAT INTEGRATES CHARGING NETWORKS, GM VEHICLE MOBILE APPS1, AND OTHER PRODUCTS AND SERVICES TO SIMPLIFY THE OVERALL CHARGING EXPERIENCE FOR GM ELECTRIC VEHICLE OWNERS. ULTIUM CHARGE 360 BUILDS ON GM'S EXISTING CHARGING EFFORTS TO PROVIDE GM EV OWNERS MORE CONFIDENCE AND CONVENIENCE WHEN IT COMES TO EV CHARGING.
