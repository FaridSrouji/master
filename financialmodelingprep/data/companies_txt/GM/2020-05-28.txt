GM Says Restart Of Vehicle Production In North America Has Gone Smoothly. GM SAYS RESTART OF VEHICLE PRODUCTION AT CO'S COMPONENT AND ASSEMBLY PLANTS IN NORTH AMERICA HAS GONE SMOOTHLY.GM SAYS IT IS NOW IN A POSITION TO INCREASE PRODUCTION TO MEET STRENGTHENING CUSTOMER DEMAND AND STRONG DEALER DEMAND.GM SAYS STARTING JUNE. 1, THREE CROSSOVER ASSEMBLY PLANTS IN U.S. & CANADA WILL BE OPERATING TWO PRODUCTION SHIFTS.GM SAYS STARTING JUNE. 1, THREE U.S. ASSEMBLY PLANTS BUILDING MID- AND FULL-SIZE PICKUPS WILL MOVE FROM ONE- TO THREE-SHIFT OPERATIONS.GM SAYS FIVE OTHER U.S. ASSEMBLY PLANTS WILL OPERATE ONE PRODUCTION SHIFT.
