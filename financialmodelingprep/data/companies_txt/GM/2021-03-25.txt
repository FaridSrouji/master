Meg Whitman and Mark Tatum Join GM's Board of Directors. DETROIT , MARCH 25, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- MEG WHITMAN, A TECHNOLOGY LEADER AND FORMER HEAD OF HEWLETT PACKARD ENTERPRISE, AND MARK TATUM, DEPUTY COMMISSIONER AND CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER OF THE NATIONAL BASKETBALL ASSOCIATION, ARE JOINING THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GENERAL MOTORS CO. (NYSE: GM) EFFECTIVE TODAY, THE COMPANY ANNOUNCED.    WITH THE ELECTION OF WHITMAN, 64, AND TATUM, 51, GM NOW HAS 13 DIRECTORS.
