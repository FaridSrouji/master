Fitch Says Automotive Bankruptcies Remain Rare. Oct 10  - General Motors Co <GM.N>::FITCH SAYS HEALTHY PASSENGER VEHICLE SALES, SECTOR PROFITABILITY HAVE RESULTED IN FEW RECENT BANKRUPTCIES & LACK OF DISTRESS IN U.S. AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRY.FITCH SAYS WOULD NOT BE SURPRISING TO SEE SMALLER SUPPLIERS BECOME LIQUIDITY CONSTRAINED IF ON-GOING UAW STRIKE AT GENERAL MOTORS DRAGS ON.
