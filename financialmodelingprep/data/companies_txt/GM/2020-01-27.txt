GM says Detroit-Hamtramck To Be First Assembly Plant 100% Devoted To Electric Vehicles. DETROIT-HAMTRAMCK TO BE GM’S FIRST ASSEMBLY PLANT 100 PERCENT DEVOTED TO ELECTRIC VEHICLES.GENERAL MOTORS CO - ANNOUNCES $2.2 BILLION INVESTMENT AT DETROIT- HAMTRAMCK ASSEMBLY PLANT TO PRODUCE VARIETY OF ALL-ELECTRIC TRUCKS, SUVS.GENERAL MOTORS CO - GM’S FIRST ALL-ELECTRIC TRUCK WILL BE A PICKUP WITH PRODUCTION SCHEDULED TO BEGIN IN LATE 2021.GENERAL MOTORS - WILL ALSO INVEST ADDITIONAL $800 MILLION IN SUPPLIER TOOLING, OTHER PROJECTS RELATED TO LAUNCH OF NEW ELECTRIC TRUCKS.
