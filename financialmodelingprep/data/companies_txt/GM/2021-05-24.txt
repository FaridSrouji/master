BetterInvesting™ Magazine Selects The Hershey Co. As "Growth" Stock and General Motors Co. As "Undervalued" For August 2021 Issue. TROY, MICH., MAY 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE EDITORIAL ADVISORY AND SECURITIES REVIEW COMMITTEE OF BETTERINVESTING MAGAZINE TODAY ANNOUNCED THE HERSHEY CO. (NYSE: HSY) AS ITS "STOCK TO STUDY" AND GENERAL MOTORS CO. (NYSE: GM) IS ITS "UNDERVALUED STOCK" IN THE AUGUST 2021 ISSUE FOR INVESTORS' INFORMATIONAL AND EDUCATIONAL USE.
