3M Board Declares Quarterly Dividend. ST. PAUL, MINN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE 3M BOARD OF DIRECTORS (NYSE:MMM) TODAY DECLARED A DIVIDEND ON THE COMPANY'S COMMON STOCK OF $1.47 PER SHARE FOR THE FOURTH QUARTER OF 2020. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE DECEMBER 12, 2020, TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD AT THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON NOVEMBER 20, 2020. 3M HAS PAID DIVIDENDS TO ITS SHAREHOLDERS WITHOUT INTERRUPTION FOR MORE THAN 100 YEARS. AS OF SEPTEMBER 30, 2020, 3M HAD 576,821,878 COMMON SHARES OUTSTANDING AND 72,692 SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD. ABOUT 3M AT
