3M Corporation Settles with the Department of Justice for $9 Million Regarding the Allegedly Defective Earplugs. 3M Corporation has come under fire recently for their Combat Arms brand earplugs, Version 2 (CAEv2). The company is being accused of falsifying testing documents and knowingly manufacturing and distributing substandard earplugs to the U.S. military. 3M has settled with the Department of Justice for $9 million regarding the allegedly defective earplugs. An MDL was created in early 2019 in the Northern District of Florida, and is still in the very early stages. No bellwether trials have been scheduled yet, and additional cases are still being added to the docket. It's possible that thousands of military personnel may have been affected.
