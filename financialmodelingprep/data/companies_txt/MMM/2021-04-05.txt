New 3M silicone adhesive helps improve medical devices intended for people with fragile skin. ST. PAUL, MINN., APRIL 5, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- AS THE NUMBER OF PEOPLE WITH CHRONIC DISEASES AND THE SIZE OF THE AGING POPULATION CONTINUE TO GROW, MEDICAL DEVICE DESIGN ENGINEERS NEED ADHESIVE SOLUTIONS THAT ARE BOTH GENTLE AND SECURE TO HELP SUPPORT DEVICE COMPLIANCE BY END USERS.
