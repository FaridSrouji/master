3M Launches Analytics Platform that Integrates Social and Clinical Risk Data to Improve Population Health. SALT LAKE CITY, APRIL 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- 3M HEALTH INFORMATION SYSTEMS HAS INTRODUCED A NEW TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM THAT ALLOWS HEALTH CARE PROVIDERS AND PAYERS TO PRIORITIZE CARE AND ALLOCATE RESOURCES FOR HIGH-RISK INDIVIDUALS AND PATIENT POPULATIONS. 3M™ SOCIAL DETERMINANTS OF HEALTH ANALYTICS (3M SDOH) COMBINES CLINICAL, SOCIAL AND POPULATION HEALTH DATA TO CREATE A COMPLETE PICTURE OF PATIENT HEALTH.
