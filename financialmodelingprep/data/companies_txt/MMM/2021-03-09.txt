Cority to Collaborate With 3M to Accelerate the Future of Connected EHS Management and Worker Safety. TORONTO, MARCH 09, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CORITY, THE GLOBAL ENTERPRISE ENVIRONMENTAL, HEALTH, AND SAFETY (EHS) SOFTWARE PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED A COLLABORATION WITH 3M'S PERSONAL SAFETY DIVISION IN THE AREA OF CONNECTED SAFETY TECHNOLOGIES.
