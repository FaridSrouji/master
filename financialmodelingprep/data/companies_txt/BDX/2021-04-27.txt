BD Board Declares Dividend. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., APRIL 27, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX) HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.83 PER COMMON SHARE, PAYABLE ON JUNE 30, 2021 TO HOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 9, 2021.
