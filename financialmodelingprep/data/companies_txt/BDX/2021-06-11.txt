USA Swimming Partners with BD to Provide COVID-19 Screening for the Olympic Trials and Other Competitive Swimming Events. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., JUNE 11, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- USA SWIMMING, THE NATIONAL GOVERNING BODY FOR THE SPORT OF SWIMMING IN THE UNITED STATES, AND BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, HAVE PARTNERED TO PROVIDE COVID-19 TESTING AT USA SWIMMING EVENTS.
