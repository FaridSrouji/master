BD Announces FDA 510(k) Submission for BD Alaris™ System. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., APRIL 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS SUBMITTED A 510(K) PREMARKET NOTIFICATION TO THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR THE BD ALARIS™ SYSTEM, THE MOST WIDELY USED INFUSION PUMP IN ACUTE CARE HOSPITALS ACROSS THE UNITED STATES.
