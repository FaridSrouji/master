USATF Partners With BD To Provide Rapid COVID-19 Testing To Enhance Athlete Safety At Track & Field Events. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., APRIL 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- USA TRACK & FIELD (USATF) AND BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, HAVE PARTNERED TO PROVIDE RAPID COVID-19 TESTING FOR USATF ATHLETES AT JOURNEY TO GOLD MEETS TO HELP GET USATF PROFESSIONAL ATHLETES BACK TO COMPETITION SAFELY THROUGH THE USE OF THE BD VERITOR™ PLUS SYSTEM.
