Becton Dickinson And Co - Provides Update On Two Previously Announced Voluntary Recalls Related To Ambulatory Infusion Pumps And Sets. BECTON DICKINSON AND CO - PROVIDES UPDATE ON TWO PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED VOLUNTARY RECALLS RELATED TO AMBULATORY INFUSION PUMPS AND SETS.BECTON DICKINSON AND CO - BOTH PREVIOUSLY ANNOUNCED RECALLS HAVE NOW BEEN DESIGNATED AS CLASS I RECALLS BY FDA.BECTON DICKINSON AND CO - TO SUSPEND DISTRIBUTION OF BODYGUARD INFUSION PUMP SYSTEM & REMOVE ALL EXISTING PRODUCTS FROM U.S. MARKET.BECTON DICKINSON AND CO - RECALL AND MARKET REMOVAL AFFECT ABOUT 28,400 DEVICES.
