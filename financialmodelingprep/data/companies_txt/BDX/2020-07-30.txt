BD Announces U.S. Govt Investment To Support Scale Up Manufacturing Of Covid-19 Diagnosic Tests. BD ANNOUNCES $24 MILLION U.S. GOVERNMENT INVESTMENT TO SUPPORT SCALE UP OF U.S. MANUFACTURING OF COVID-19 DIAGNOSIC TESTS.BECTON DICKINSON AND CO - CAPITAL INVESTMENTS WILL ENABLE U.S. MANUFACTURING OF 8 MILLION BD VERITOR™ BRAND SARS-COV-2 TESTS PER MONTH.BECTON DICKINSON AND CO - INVESTMENTS WILL ENABLE GLOBAL PRODUCTION OF MORE THAN 12 MILLION TEST KITS PER MONTH BY END OF FEBRUARY 2021.
