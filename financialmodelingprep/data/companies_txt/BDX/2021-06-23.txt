BD Announces Expanded 510(k) Clearance for At-Home System for Ascites. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., JUNE 23, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY) (NYSE: BDX), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED 510(K) CLEARANCE FROM THE U.S. FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA) FOR THE PERITX™ PERITONEAL CATHETER SYSTEM FOR THE DRAINAGE OF SYMPTOMATIC, RECURRENT NON-MALIGNANT ASCITES.
