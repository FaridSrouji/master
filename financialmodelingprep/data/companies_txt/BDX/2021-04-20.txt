BD Announces the Voluntary Recall of Specified Lots of ChloraPrep™ Hi-Lite Orange™ 26 mL Applicator in the United States and U.S. Territories Due to Defective Applicator. FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J., APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- BD (BECTON, DICKINSON AND COMPANY), A LEADING GLOBAL MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY COMPANY, IS VOLUNTARILY RECALLING SPECIFIED LOTS OF THE CHLORAPREP™ HI-LITE ORANGE™ 26 ML APPLICATOR (2% W/W CHLORHEXIDINE GLUCONATE (CHG) AND 70% V/V ISOPROPYL ALCOHOL (IPA)) TO THE USER LEVEL DUE TO A DEFECTIVE APPLICATOR.
