Becton, Dickinson and Company Declares Quarterly Common Share Dividend, Payable on December 31, 2019; Declares 6.125% Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock Dividend, Series A, Payable on February 1, 2020. The Board of Directors of Becton, Dickinson and Company has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.79 cents per common share, an increase of 2.6% from the previous quarter. The dividend will be payable on December 31, 2019 to holders of record on December 10, 2019. In addition, the Board of Directors has declared a quarterly dividend of $15.3125 per share on the 6.125% mandatory convertible preferred stock, Series A, payable on February 1, 2020 to holders of record on January 15, 2020.
