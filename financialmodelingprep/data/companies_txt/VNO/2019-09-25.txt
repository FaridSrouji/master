Lincoln Property Company and Cara Real Estate acquired 3040 M Street, NW in Washington, DC from Vornado Realty Trust (NYSE:VNO) for $49.8 million on September 18, 2019. Lincoln Property Company and Cara Real Estate paid about $1,907 per square foot for 44,000-square-foot building. Eastdil Secured represented Vornado Realty Trust in the transaction.
Lincoln Property Company and Cara Real Estate completed the acquisition of 3040 M Street, NW in Washington, DC from Vornado Realty Trust (NYSE:VNO) on September 18, 2019.
