Vornado Completes $525 Million Refinancing of One Park Avenue. NEW YORK, FEB. 26, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VORNADO REALTY TRUST (NYSE: VNO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS 55% OWNED JOINT VENTURE HAS COMPLETED A $525 MILLION REFINANCING OF ONE PARK AVENUE, A 943,000 SQUARE FOOT MANHATTAN OFFICE BUILDING. THE INTEREST ONLY LOAN CARRIES A RATE OF LIBOR PLUS 1.107% (CURRENTLY 1.222%) AND MATURES IN FEBRUARY 2026, AS FULLY EXTENDED.
