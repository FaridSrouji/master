Vornado Realty Trust Declares a Regular Quarterly Dividend, Payable on November 18, 2019. Vornado Realty Trust announced that its Board of Trustees has declared a regular quarterly dividend of $0.66 per share payable on November 18, 2019 to common shareholders of record on November 4, 2019.
