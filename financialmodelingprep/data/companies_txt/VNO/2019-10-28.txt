Vornado Realty Trust Announces Third Quarter Non-GAAP FFO Per Share Of $1.46. VORNADO ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2019 FINANCIAL RESULTS.Q3 NON-GAAP FFO PER SHARE $1.46.QTRLY SAME STORE NOI AT SHARE UP 0.9%.QTRLY SAME STORE NOI AT SHARE ON CASH BASIS UP 1%.
Vornado Realty Trust Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Vornado Realty Trust announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 465.961 million compared to USD 542.048 million a year ago. Net income was USD 335.438 million compared to USD 203.179 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.69 compared to USD 1 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 1,463.732 million compared to USD 1,620.303 million a year ago. Net income was USD 2,942.187 million compared to USD 336.927 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 15.22 compared to USD 1.5 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 15.2 compared to USD 1.49 a year ago.
