Vornado Realty Trust Says As Result Of Ongoing COVID-19 Impact, CEO Steven Roth, Waived 50% Of Annual Base Salary For Remainder Of 2020, Effective April 1. VORNADO REALTY TRUST - AS RESULT OF ONGOING COVID19 IMPACT, CEO STEVEN ROTH, WAIVED 50% OF ANNUAL BASE SALARY FOR REMAINDER OF 2020, EFFECTIVE APRIL 1.VORNADO REALTY TRUST - IN ADDITION, PRESIDENT MICHAEL FRANCO, CFO JOSEPH MACNOW, HAVE EACH WAIVED 30% OF RESPECTIVE ANNUAL BASE SALARIES FOR REMAINDER OF 2020.VORNADO REALTY TRUST - GLEN WEISS, CO-HEAD OF REAL ESTATE, HAIM CHERA, EVP – HEAD OF RETAIL, WAIVED 15% OF THEIR RESPECTIVE BASE SALARIES FOR REMAINDER OF 2020.
