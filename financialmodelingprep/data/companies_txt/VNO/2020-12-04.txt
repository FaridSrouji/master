Vornado Announces Dividend On Series N Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Shares. NEW YORK, DEC. 04, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VORNADO REALTY TRUST (NYSE:VNO) ANNOUNCED TODAY A DIVIDEND ON ITS RECENTLY ISSUED SERIES N CUMULATIVE REDEEMABLE PREFERRED SHARES FOR THE PARTIAL PERIOD OF NOVEMBER 24, 2020 (SETTLEMENT DATE) THROUGH DECEMBER 31, 2020 OF $.1348958 PER SHARE.
