Vornado Completes $350 Million Refinancing of 909 Third Avenue. NEW YORK, MARCH 29, 2021 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VORNADO REALTY TRUST (NYSE: VNO) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT IT HAS COMPLETED A $350 MILLION REFINANCING OF 909 THIRD AVENUE, A 1.4 MILLION SQUARE FOOT MANHATTAN OFFICE BUILDING. THE INTEREST ONLY LOAN BEARS A FIXED RATE OF 3.23% AND MATURES IN APRIL 2031.
