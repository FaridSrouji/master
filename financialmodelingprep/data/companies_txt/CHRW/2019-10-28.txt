CH Robinson Worldwide Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2019. CH Robinson Worldwide Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 3,856.132 million compared to USD 4,291.900 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 201.085 million compared to USD 245.973 million a year ago. Net income was USD 146.894 million compared to USD 175.895 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.08 compared to USD 1.27 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.07 compared to USD 1.25 a year ago. For the nine months, total revenue was USD 11,516.182 million compared to USD 12,493.264 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 653.170 million compared to USD 656.566 million a year ago. Net income was USD 477.862 million compared to USD 477.355 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.48 compared to USD 3.42 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.45 compared to USD 3.39 a year ago.
