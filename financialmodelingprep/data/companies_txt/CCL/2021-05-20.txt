Holland America Line Announces Plans to Restart Cruising to Alaska from Seattle in July 2021. SEATTLE, MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- FOLLOWING PASSAGE OF THE ALASKA TOURISM RESTORATION ACT, WORK WITH ALASKA GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS, AND RECENT GUIDANCE FROM THE U.S. CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION (CDC), HOLLAND AMERICA LINE IS ANNOUNCING PLANS TO RESTART CRUISING TO ALASKA IN JULY ROUNDTRIP FROM SEATTLE, WASHINGTON, ABOARD NIEUW AMSTERDAM. BOOKINGS ARE OPEN FOR THE 10 SATURDAY DEPARTURES THAT WILL OPERATE FROM JULY 24 THROUGH OCT. 2.
Princess Cruises Plans to Resume Cruising in United States with Alaska Sailings Departing Seattle in July 2021.
