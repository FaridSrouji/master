Carnival Cruise Line, Federal Maritime Commission, Galveston Officials Participate in Cruise Restart Rally at Port of Galveston. GALVESTON, TEXAS, MAY 3, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN A UNIFIED SHOWING OF SUPPORT TO ENCOURAGE THE RESUMPTION OF CRUISING IN THE U.S., CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE JOINED MEMBERS OF THE FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION, GALVESTON CITY AND PORT OFFICIALS AND LOCAL BUSINESSES AT THE PORT OF GALVESTON TO HIGHLIGHT THE ECONOMIC IMPACT OF CRUISING IN GALVESTON AND THROUGHOUT TEXAS. THE PORT OF GALVESTON AT THE GALVESTON WHARVES IS THE FOURTH MOST POPULAR CRUISE HOMEPORT IN NORTH AMERICA AND THE ONLY CRUISE PORT IN TEXAS.
