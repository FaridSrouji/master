Carnival Says 4 More N.American Brands Temporarily Pause Fleet Cruise Ops. REG-CARNIVAL PLC: CARNIVAL CORPORATION ANNOUNCEMENT.CARNIVAL - FOUR ADDITIONAL CARNIVAL CORPORATION NORTH AMERICAN BRANDS ANNOUNCE MONTH-LONG TEMPORARY PAUSE IN FLEET CRUISE OPERATIONS.CARNIVAL - PAUSE IN CRUISE OPERATIONS IS BEING MADE OUT OF AN ABUNDANCE OF CAUTION TO SUPPORT GLOBAL EFFORT TO CONTAIN SPREAD OF COVID-19 CORONAVIRUS.CARNIVAL - INDIVIDUALLY, EACH BRAND IS COMMUNICATING WITH GUESTS TO PROVIDE DIRECTION REGARDING DISRUPTION TO THEIR CRUISE VACATIONS.CARNIVAL - DATE OF RESUMED CRUISE OPERATIONS ALSO WILL BE COMMUNICATED BY EACH RESPECTIVE BRAND AND AVAILABLE ON THEIR WEBSITES.CARNIVAL - COMPANY AND ITS BRANDS ARE ALSO NOTIFYING CREW MEMBERS, TRAVEL PROFESSIONALS, SUPPLIERS AND OTHER STAKEHOLDERS.CARNIVAL - ANNOUNCED THAT FOUR ADDITIONAL NORTH AMERICAN CRUISE LINE BRANDS WILL TAKE A VOLUNTARY MONTH-LONG PAUSE, SUSPENDING NEW CRUISE VOYAGES.
Carnival Sees Results Of Operations For FY Ending Nov 30 To Result In A Net Loss. CARNIVAL - : UPDATE ON DEBT FUNDING AND OTHER MATTERS.CARNIVAL - CORPORATION IMPLEMENTED A TEMPORARY PAUSE OF ITS GLOBAL FLEET CRUISE OPERATIONS ACROSS ALL BRANDS.CARNIVAL - EXPECT RESULTS OF OPERATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR ENDING NOVEMBER 30, 2020 TO RESULT IN A NET LOSS.CARNIVAL - ONGOING EFFECTS OF COVID-19 ON OPERATIONS AND GLOBAL BOOKINGS WILL HAVE MATERIAL NEGATIVE IMPACT ON ITS FINANCIAL RESULTS AND LIQUIDITY.CARNIVAL - TAKING ADDITIONAL ACTIONS TO IMPROVE ITS LIQUIDITY, INCLUDING CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND EXPENSE REDUCTIONS, AND PURSUING ADDITIONAL FINANCING.CARNIVAL - IS CURRENTLY UNABLE TO PROVIDE AN EARNINGS FORECAST.CARNIVAL - ON MARCH 13 CO PROVIDED NOTICE TO LENDERS TO BORROW ABOUT $3 BILLION UNDER FACILITY AGREEMENT FOR A PERIOD OF SIX MONTHS.
