Carnival Cruise Line Extends Pause From U.S. Through May 2021. MIAMI, FEB. 24, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE HAS NOTIFIED BOOKED GUESTS AND TRAVEL ADVISORS THAT ITS PAUSE IN OPERATIONS FROM U.S. PORTS HAS BEEN EXTENDED THROUGH MAY 31, 2021. A DATE FOR THE RETURN OF GUEST CRUISING OPERATIONS FROM U.S. PORTS HAS NOT YET BEEN DETERMINED....
