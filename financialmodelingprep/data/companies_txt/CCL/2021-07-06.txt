Carnival Cruise Line Returns To Guest Operations From PortMiami, Bolstering Local Economic Impact. MIAMI, JULY 4, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE KICKED OFF ITS FIRST CRUISE FROM IN ALMOST 16 MONTHS FROM PORTMIAMI, THE CRUISE CAPITAL OF THE WORLD, TODAY WITH THE DEPARTURE OF CARNIVAL HORIZON, PROVIDING A SIGNIFICANT BOOST TO THE LOCAL ECONOMY AND THE TENS OF THOUSANDS OF JOBS IN SOUTH FLORIDA THAT ARE SUPPORTED BY THE CRUISE INDUSTRY. CARNIVAL'S RESUMPTION OF SERVICE IN MIAMI PROVIDES GUESTS WITH A MUCH-ANTICIPATED VACATION AND IS A FURTHER BOOST TO THE ECONOMY BOTH LOCALLY AND THROUGHOUT THE STATE.
