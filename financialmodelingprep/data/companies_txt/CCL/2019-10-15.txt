Carnival Corporation & PLC Declares Quarterly Dividend. CARNIVAL CORPORATION & PLC DECLARES QUARTERLY DIVIDEND.CARNIVAL CORPORATION & PLC - ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.50 PER SHARE.
Carnival Corporation & plc Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 13, 2019. Carnival Corporation & plc announced that it has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.50 per share. The company's boards of directors approved a record date for the quarterly dividend of November 22, 2019, and a payment date of December 13, 2019.
