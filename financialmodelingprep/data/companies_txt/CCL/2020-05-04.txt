Carnival Cruise Line Announces Plan To Phase-In Service. CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE ANNOUNCES PLAN TO PHASE-IN SERVICE.CARNIVAL CORP - ALL NORTH AMERICAN CRUISES FROM JUNE 27 TO JULY 31 WILL BE CANCELLED..CARNIVAL CORP - PAUSE IN SERVICE EXTENDED, IN COMBINATION WITH GRADUAL SCHEDULE FROM SELECT HOMEPORTS.CARNIVAL CORP - PLAN TO PHASE IN A RESUMPTION IN NORTH AMERICAN SERVICE THIS SUMMER, BEGINNING ON AUGUST 1.CARNIVAL CORP - PAUSE IN OPERATIONS WILL BE EXTENDED IN ALL OTHER NORTH AMERICAN AND AUSTRALIAN MARKETS THROUGH AUGUST 31..CARNIVAL CORP - ALL CARNIVAL SPIRIT ALASKAN CRUISES FROM SEATTLE WILL BE CANCELLED.CARNIVAL CORP - IMPACTED GUESTS WILL HAVE OPTIONS INCLUDING COMBINED FUTURE CRUISE CREDIT (FCC) AND ONBOARD CREDIT (OBC) PACKAGE, OR A FULL REFUND.
