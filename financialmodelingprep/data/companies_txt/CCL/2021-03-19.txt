Princess Cruises Reveals Details of All Inclusive UK 'Summer Seacations' on MedallionClass® Ships. SOUTHAMPTON, ENGLAND, MARCH 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PRINCESS CRUISES HAS REVEALED DETAILS OF ITS SERIES OF SHORT BREAKS AND WEEK-LONG CRUISES ABOARD MEDALLIONCLASS SHIPS, REGAL PRINCESS AND SKY PRINCESS, SCHEDULED TO SAIL FROM SOUTHAMPTON AROUND THE UK COAST FROM LATE SUMMER. THE ALL-INCLUSIVE 'SUMMER SEACATIONS' WILL GO ON SALE ON WEDNESDAY, MARCH 24 AT 8 A.M.
