Carnival Cruise Line Goes Global To Select Godmother For Mardi Gras, Naming Miss Dominican Republic During MISS UNIVERSE Broadcast. MIAMI, MAY 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- WITH A GLOBAL TELEVISION AUDIENCE ESTIMATED AT 500 MILLION LOOKING ON, CARNIVAL CRUISE LINE PRESIDENT CHRISTINE DUFFY PRESENTED MISS DOMINICAN REPUBLIC KIMBERLY JIMÉNEZ WITH THE "SPIRIT OF CARNIVAL" AWARD FOR EMBODYING THE COMPANY'S VALUES OF FUN, FRIENDSHIP, DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION DURING THE MISS UNIVERSE BROADCAST, AND BESTOWED THE HONOR OF SERVING AS GODMOTHER TO THE LINE'S NEWEST AND MOST INNOVATIVE SHIP, MARDI GRAS , SET TO DEBUT FROM PORT CANAVERAL, FLA., LATER THIS YEAR.
