S&P Global Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 11, 2019. The Board of Directors of S&P Global has approved a regular quarterly cash dividend on the Corporation's common stock. The dividend of $0.57 is payable on December 11, 2019, to shareholders of record on November 26, 2019. The annualized dividend rate is $2.28 per share.
