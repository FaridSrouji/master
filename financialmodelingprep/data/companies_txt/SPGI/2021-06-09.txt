S&P Global Platts Global Energy Awards Now Accepting Nominations. NEW YORK, JUNE 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- NOMINATIONS ARE NOW BEING ACCEPTED FOR THE 2021 S&P GLOBAL PLATTS GLOBAL ENERGY AWARDS , WHICH RECOGNIZE CORPORATE AND INDIVIDUAL ACHIEVEMENTS IN NINETEEN AWARD CATEGORIES. THE AWARDS PROGRAM, OFTEN DESCRIBED AS "THE OSCARS OF ENERGY," IN ACCORDANCE WITH EASED COVID-19 RESTRICTIONS IN THE US WILL BE HELD IN-PERSON THIS YEAR AT CIPRIANI WALL STREET, NEW YORK, NY.
