S&P Global Platts Opens Further Consultation on Brent Benchmarks Transition. LONDON, MARCH 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL PLATTS ("PLATTS"), THE LEADING INDEPENDENT PROVIDER OF INFORMATION AND BENCHMARK PRICES FOR THE COMMODITIES AND ENERGY MARKETS, ANNOUNCED THAT IT IS OPENING A FURTHER CONSULTATION ON THE TRANSITION DATED BRENT AND ITS RELATED COMPONENTS. THIS REFLECTS EXTENSIVE FEEDBACK FOLLOWING ITS ANNOUNCEMENT ON 22 FEBRUARY THAT THE BASKET WOULD REFLECT WTI MIDLAND AND BE AMENDED TO A CIF DELIVERED ROTTERDAM BASIS FROM JUNE 2022 LOADINGS.
