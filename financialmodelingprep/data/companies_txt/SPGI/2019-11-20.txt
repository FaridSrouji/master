S&P Global Inc. (NYSE:SPGI) agreed to acquire ESG Ratings and ESG Benchmarking Businesses from RobecoSAM.. S&P Global Inc. (NYSE:SPGI) agreed to acquire ESG Ratings and ESG Benchmarking Businesses from RobecoSAM on November 21, 2019. The ESG Ratings Business is comprised of two units: one administering the SAM CSA for the purpose of issuing ESG Ratings and a second that provides in-depth reports to companies seeking to understand their performance relative to their peers. Following acquisition, Robeco and RobecoSAM will continue to have access to the CSA data for use in investment strategies and will provide advice on the CSA Methodology. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions. It is expected to close in the first quarter of 2020.
