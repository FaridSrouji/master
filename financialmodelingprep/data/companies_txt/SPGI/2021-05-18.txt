S&P/Experian Consumer Credit Default Indices Show Drop In Composite Rate In April 2021. NEW YORK, MAY 18, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES AND EXPERIAN RELEASED TODAY DATA THROUGH APRIL 2021 FOR THE S&P/EXPERIAN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULT INDICES. THE INDICES REPRESENT A COMPREHENSIVE MEASURE OF CHANGES IN CONSUMER CREDIT DEFAULTS AND SHOW THAT THE COMPOSITE RATE WAS FOUR BASIS POINTS LOWER AT 0.50%.
