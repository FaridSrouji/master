International Energy Agency Selects S&P Global Platts for LNG Pricing. NEW YORK, LONDON, AND SINGAPORE, MAY 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL PLATTS ("PLATTS"), THE LEADING INDEPENDENT PROVIDER OF INFORMATION, ANALYTICS AND BENCHMARK PRICES FOR THE COMMODITIES AND ENERGY MARKETS, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT THE INTERNATIONAL ENERGY AGENCY (IEA) WILL USE ITS LIQUIFIED NATURAL GAS (LNG) SERVICE THAT PROVIDES LNG PRICES AS WELL AS DATA ON LNG SHIPPING AND LNG PLANT OUTAGES. THE IEA IS AN AUTONOMOUS INTERGOVERNMENTAL ORGANISATION ESTABLISHED IN THE FRAMEWORK OF THE ORGANISATION FOR ECONOMIC CO-OPERATION AND DEVELOPMENT (OECD) IN 1974.
