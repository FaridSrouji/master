S&P Global Inc. Provides Earnings Guidance for the Full Year 2019. S&P Global Inc. provided earnings guidance for the full year 2019. For the full year, the company's 2019 GAAP diluted EPS guidance is increased from a range of $8.20 to $8.35 to a new range of $8.56 to $8.66 due to gains on recent divestitures, increased revenue and operating profit margin, and lower corporate unallocated expense.
