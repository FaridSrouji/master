DT Midstream Set to Join S&P MidCap 400. NEW YORK, JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- DT MIDSTREAM INC. (NYSE: DTM) WILL REPLACE PRA HEALTH SCIENCES INC. (NASD: PRAH) IN THE S&P MIDCAP 400 EFFECTIVE PRIOR TO THE OPEN ON FRIDAY, JULY 2. ICON PLC (NASD: ICLR) IS ACQUIRING PRA HEALTH SCIENCES IN A DEAL EXPECTED TO BE COMPLETED SOON PENDING FINAL CLOSING CONDITIONS.
S&P Dow Jones Indices Wins Environmental Finance's ESG Index Provider of the Year Award. LONDON, JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P DOW JONES INDICES ("S&P DJI"), THE WORLD'S LEADING INDEX PROVIDER, TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED AS ENVIRONMENTAL FINANCE'S ESG INDEX PROVIDER OF THE YEAR IN ITS ANNUAL SUSTAINABLE INVESTMENT AWARDS. ENVIRONMENTAL FINANCE IS RECOGNIZED AS A PREMIER SOURCE OF NEWS, INFORMATION AND ANALYSIS ON SUSTAINABLE FINANCE.
