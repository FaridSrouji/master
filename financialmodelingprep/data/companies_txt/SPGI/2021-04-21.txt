S&P Global Ratings Provides Full Alignment Opinion On Incheon Airport's Green, Social, And Sustainability Bond Framework. SINGAPORE, APRIL 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- S&P GLOBAL RATINGS SAID TODAY THAT INCHEON INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT CORP.'S (IIAC) GREEN, SOCIAL AND SUSTAINABILITY BOND FRAMEWORK IS FULLY ALIGNED WITH THE FOUR COMPONENTS OF THE GREEN BOND PRINCIPLES (GBP) AND THE FOUR COMPONENTS OF THE SOCIAL BOND PRINCIPLES (SBP), COLLECTIVELY REFERRED TO THE SUSTAINABILITY BOND GUIDELINES (SBG). THE FRAMEWORK ALIGNMENT OPINION REPORT IS AVAILABLE HERE.
