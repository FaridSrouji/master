Travelers Named a 2021 Military Friendly® Company. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS BEEN NAMED A 2021 MILITARY FRIENDLY® COMPANY BY VIQTORY, PUBLISHER OF G.I. JOBS. THE DESIGNATION IS GIVEN TO ORGANIZATIONS THAT HAVE ROBUST PROGRAMS IN PLACE TO SUPPORT THE MILITARY COMMUNITY. “WE HAVE A LONGSTANDING COMMITMENT TO ASSISTING VETERANS AND THEIR FAMILIES, AND IT'S AN HONOR TO BE RECOGNIZED FOR OUR EFFORTS,” SAID DIANE KURTZMAN, EXECUTIVE VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF HUMAN RESOURCES
