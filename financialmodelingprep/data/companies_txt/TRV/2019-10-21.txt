The Travelers Companies, Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on September 30, 2019, Payable on December 31, 2019. The Travelers Companies, Inc. declared a quarterly dividend of $0.82 per share. The dividend is payable on December 31, 2019, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 10, 2019.
