Travelers Institute Kicks Off 2021 Programming. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TRAVELERS INSTITUTE, THE PUBLIC POLICY DIVISION OF THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV), TODAY ANNOUNCED THE LINEUP OF ITS 2021 EDUCATIONAL WEBINARS, WHICH WILL FOCUS ON CRITICAL ISSUES FACING CUSTOMERS AND BUSINESSES. TOPICS WILL INCLUDE THE ROLLOUT OF COVID-19 VACCINES, HIRING AND WORKFORCE STRATEGIES, THE FUTURE OF INSURING AUTONOMOUS VEHICLES AND GEOPOLITICAL HOT SPOTS. THIS WILL CONTINUE THE TRAVELERS INSTITUTE'S “WEDNESDAYS WITH WOODWARD” VIRTUAL
