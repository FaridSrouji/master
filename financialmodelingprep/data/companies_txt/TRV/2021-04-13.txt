Travelers Institute to Host Dr. Ashish Jha for a Discussion on the Global Pandemic. HARTFORD, CONN.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE TRAVELERS INSTITUTE, THE PUBLIC POLICY DIVISION OF THE TRAVELERS COMPANIES, INC. (NYSE: TRV), WILL HOST ASHISH K. JHA, M.D., MPH, DEAN, BROWN UNIVERSITY SCHOOL OF PUBLIC HEALTH, FOR A WEBINAR TITLED “A HOPEFUL SPRING: VACCINE SUCCESSES AND OUTLOOK” ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 14. DR. JHA WILL SHARE HIS PERSPECTIVE ON THE GLOBAL COVID-19 PANDEMIC, INCLUDING AN UPDATE ON VACCINE DISTRIBUTION IN THE U.S., VACCINE HESITANCY IN THE POPULATION AND THE LATEST VIRUS VARIAN
