Bio-Rad Laboratories, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Bio-Rad Laboratories, Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 560.633 million compared to USD 545.138 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 57.460 million compared to USD 36.275 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 258.816 million compared to net income of USD 269.326 million a year ago. Basic loss per share was USD 8.68 compared to basic earnings per share of USD 9.02 a year ago. Diluted loss per share was USD 8.68 compared to diluted earnings per share of USD 8.89 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 1,687.231 million compared to USD 1,672.568 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 170.456 million compared to USD 123.713 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,205.189 million compared to USD 1,194.143 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 40.42 compared to USD 40.04 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 39.97 compared to USD 39.5 a year ago.
