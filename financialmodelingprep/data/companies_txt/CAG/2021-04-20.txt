Gardein Kicks Off Plant-Based Double Take Challenge On National Look Alike Day. CHICAGO, APRIL 20, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- IN CELEBRATION OF NATIONAL LOOK ALIKE DAY, GARDEIN, A BRAND OF CONAGRA BRANDS, INC. (NYSE: CAG), IS CHALLENGING CONSUMERS TO DO A DOUBLE TAKE AND TURN THEIR FAVORITE MEATY MEALS MEATLESS. THE POTASH TWINS, BEST KNOWN AS THE FOOD NETWORK'S TAKEOUT TWINS, HAVE JOINED GARDEIN TO ASK BUDDING CHEFS TO SWAP OUT THE MEAT IN THEIR FAVORITE DISHES AS PART OF THE "PLANT-BASED DOUBLE TAKE CHALLENGE.
