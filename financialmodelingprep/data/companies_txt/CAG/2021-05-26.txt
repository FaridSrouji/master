Conagra Brands Forms Multi-Year Partnerships with Thurgood Marshall College Fund and Hispanic Scholarship Fund. CHICAGO, MAY 26, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS (NYSE: CAG) ANNOUNCED TODAY NEW PARTNERSHIPS WITH THE THURGOOD MARSHALL COLLEGE FUND (TMCF), THE NATION'S LARGEST ORGANIZATION EXCLUSIVELY REPRESENTING THE BLACK COLLEGE COMMUNITY, AND THE HISPANIC SCHOLARSHIP FUND (HSF), ONE OF THE NATION'S LEADING NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS SUPPORTING HIGHER EDUCATION, TO SUPPORT BLACK AND BROWN STUDENTS. THE PARTNERSHIPS WILL HELP ENSURE STUDENTS RECEIVE EQUITABLE ACCESS TO QUALITY HIGHER EDUCATION OPPORTUNITIES AND REINFORCE CONAGRA'S COMMITMENT TO SUPPORTING THE COMMUNITIES WHERE ITS EMPLOYEES LIVE AND WORK.
