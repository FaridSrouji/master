Conagra Brands Accelerates Transition to 100% Cage-Free Eggs. CHICAGO, JUNE 28, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS (NYSE: CAG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THE COMPANY'S PLANS TO ACCELERATE ITS TRANSITION TO 100% CAGE-FREE EGGS, CONVERTING 100% OF ITS EGG USAGE TO CAGE-FREE BY THE END OF FISCAL YEAR 2024, ONE YEAR EARLIER THAN ITS ORIGINAL COMMITMENT TO DIRECTLY SOURCE 100% CAGE-FREE EGGS BY 2025. CONAGRA PLANS TO CONVERT APPROXIMATELY 20% OF ITS EGG USAGE TO CAGE-FREE BY THE END OF FISCAL YEAR 2021; APPROXIMATELY 45–50% TO CAGE-FREE BY THE END OF FISCAL YEAR 2022; APPROXIMATELY 60–70% TO CAGE-FREE BY THE END OF FISCAL YEAR 2023; AND DIRECTLY SOURCE 100% CAGE-FREE EGGS BY THE END OF FISCAL YEAR 2024.
