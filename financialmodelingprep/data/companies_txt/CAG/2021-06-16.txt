Conagra Brands Recognized As One Of The 50 Most Community-Minded Companies In The United States For The Third Consecutive Year. CHICAGO, JUNE 16, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CONAGRA BRANDS (NYSE: CAG) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT FOR THE THIRD CONSECUTIVE YEAR, IT HAS BEEN NAMED AN HONOREE OF THE CIVIC 50, WHICH RECOGNIZES THE 50 MOST COMMUNITY-MINDED COMPANIES IN THE NATION. AN INITIATIVE BY POINTS OF LIGHT, THE WORLD'S LARGEST ORGANIZATION DEDICATED TO VOLUNTEER SERVICE, THE CIVIC 50 PROVIDES A NATIONAL STANDARD FOR SUPERIOR CORPORATE CITIZENSHIP AND SHOWCASES HOW COMPANIES CAN USE THEIR TIME, SKILLS, AND RESOURCES TO IMPACT THEIR COMMUNITIES.
