Paccar Inc Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. PACCAR Inc. announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 6,004.2 million compared to USD 5,416.9 million a year ago. Net income was USD 607.9 million compared to USD 545.3 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.75 compared to USD 1.55 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 18,408.8 million compared to USD 16,205.9 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,856.6 million compared to USD 1,617 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 5.35 compared to USD 4.6 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 5.34 compared to USD 4.59 a year ago.
