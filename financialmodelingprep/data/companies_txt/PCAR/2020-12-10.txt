PACCAR Financial Corp. Transferring the Listing of Debt Securities to Nasdaq. BELLEVUE, WASH.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--PACCAR FINANCIAL CORP. (PFC) WILL BE DELISTING ITS 1.650% SENIOR NOTES DUE AUGUST 11, 2021 FROM THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE AND LISTING ITS 1.650% SENIOR NOTES DUE AUGUST 11, 2021 ON THE NASDAQ BOND EXCHANGE EFFECTIVE DECEMBER 21, 2020 AFTER MARKET CLOSE. THE 1.650% SENIOR NOTES DUE AUGUST 11, 2021 ARE EXPECTED TO BEGIN TRADING AS NASDAQ-LISTED SECURITIES ON DECEMBER 22, 2020.
