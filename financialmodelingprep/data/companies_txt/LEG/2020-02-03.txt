Leggett And Platt Reports Fourth Quarter And Full Year 2019 Results. LEGGETT & PLATT REPORTS 4Q AND FULL YEAR 2019 RESULTS.Q4 ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.68.Q4 SALES $1.14 BILLION VERSUS REFINITIV IBES ESTIMATE OF $1.16 BILLION.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE ESTIMATE $0.67 -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q4 EARNINGS PER SHARE $0.64.REPORTED Q4 2019 SALES $1.14 BILLION.REPORTED Q4 2019 SALES UP 9 PERCENT.SEES 2020 EPS OF $2.40-$2.60.SEES 2020 SALES OF $4.7 BILLION - $4.9 BILLION.FY EARNINGS PER SHARE VIEW $2.56, REVENUE VIEW $4.77 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.Q4 REVENUE VIEW $1.16 BILLION -- REFINITIV IBES DATA.SEES 2020 CAPITAL EXPENDITURES $160 MILLION.SEES 2020 ORGANIC SALES ARE EXPECTED TO BE -2% TO +2%.LEGGETT & PLATT - IN 2020 EXPECT MID-SINGLE-DIGIT VOLUME GROWTH FROM AUTOMOTIVE, U.S. SPRING, ECS, AEROSPACE, GEO COMPONENTS AND WORK FURNITURE.QTRLY ORGANIC SALES WERE DOWN 4%.
