Leggett & Platt, Incorporated Announces Sales and Earnings Guidance for the Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2019; Announces Impairment Charges for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Leggett & Platt, Incorporated announced sales and earnings guidance for the fourth quarter and full year 2019. For the quarter, sales are expected to be between $1.1 billion-$1.2 billion. EPS is expected to be $.57-$.72,including approximately $.02 per share of restructuring-related costs. For the year, the company's sales are expected to be between $4.7 billion-$4.8 billion against prior sales guidance of $4.7 billion-$4.85 billion. EPS is expected to be $2.40-$2.55 against prior EPS guidance of $2.30-$2.50.For the third quarter, the company reported impairments of $1.4 million against $0.1 million for the same quarter last year.
