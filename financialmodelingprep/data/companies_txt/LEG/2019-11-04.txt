Leggett & Platt, Incorporated Announces Dividend for the Fourth Quarter, Payable on January 15, 2020; Elects Mary Campbell as Director. Leggett & Platt's Board of Directors announced a dividend of $0.40 per share for the fourth quarter, an increase of $0.02 per share or 5.3% versus the fourth quarter of 2018. The dividend will be paid on January 15, 2020 to shareholders of record on December 13, 2019. In addition, Leggett & Platt announced the election of Mary Campbell to its Board of Directors effective immediately. Ms. Campbell, 52, serves as Chief Merchandising Officer of Qurate Retail Group and Chief Commerce Officer of QVC U.S.Ms. Campbell currently serves as a director of the Accessories Council.
