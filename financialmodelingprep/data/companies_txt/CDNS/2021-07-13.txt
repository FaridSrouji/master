Cadence and UMC Collaborate on 22ULP/ULL Reference Flow Certification for Advanced Consumer, 5G and Automotive Designs. SAN JOSE, CALIF. & HSINCHU, TAIWAN,--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CADENCE AND UMC ANNOUNCED THAT THE CADENCE DIGITAL FULL FLOW HAS BEEN OPTIMIZED AND CERTIFIED FOR THE UMC 22ULP/ULL PROCESS TECHNOLOGIES.
