Cadence To Seek Investments. Cadence Design Systems, Inc. (NasdaqGS:CDNS) will look for investment opportunities. Cadence Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer John Wall said, "We are raising our outlook for revenue, operating margin, earnings and cash from operations for the year while we continue to invest in proliferation opportunities with market-shaping customers".
