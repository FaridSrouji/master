Carrier Global Corporation Appoints Beth Wozniak to its Board of Directors. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA., JUNE 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE: CARR) TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT BETH WOZNIAK, CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND DIRECTOR OF NVENT ELECTRIC PLC, HAS BEEN APPOINTED TO CARRIER'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS.
Carrier Board of Directors Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA., JUNE 9, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE: CARR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.12 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF CARRIER COMMON STOCK.
