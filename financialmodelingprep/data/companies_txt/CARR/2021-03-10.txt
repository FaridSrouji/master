Carrier OptiClean Now Available for Online Purchase. THE WEBSITE MAKES THE AWARD-WINNING OPTICLEANTM AIR SCRUBBER AND ASSOCIATED ACCESSORIES AVAILABLE FOR DIRECT PURCHASE.
