Carrier Board of Directors Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend. PALM BEACH GARDENS, FLA., APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER GLOBAL CORPORATION (NYSE:CARR) ANNOUNCED TODAY THAT ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $0.12 PER OUTSTANDING SHARE OF CARRIER COMMON STOCK.
Carrier Agrees to Acquire Guangdong Giwee Group, a China-based HVAC Manufacturer. CHARLOTTE, N.C., APRIL 19, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- CARRIER TODAY ANNOUNCED THAT IT HAS SIGNED AGREEMENTS TO ACQUIRE GUANGDONG GIWEE GROUP (GIWEE GROUP) AND ITS SUBSIDIARIES, INCLUDING GUANGDONG CHIGO HEATING & VENTILATION EQUIPMENT CO., LTD.
