CHS Obtains Exclusive Rights To Corteva Agriscience Confectionary Sunflower Breeding Program In North America. CHS OBTAINS EXCLUSIVE RIGHTS TO CORTEVA AGRISCIENCE CONFECTIONARY SUNFLOWER BREEDING PROGRAM IN NORTH AMERICA.CHS - FOR 2020 GROWING SEASON, CORTEVA AGRISCIENCE CONOIL HYBRID SUNFLOWER SEEDS WILL BE MARKETED DIRECTLY THROUGH CHS SUNFLOWER & SELECT CHS LOCATIONS.CHS - BEGINNING IN 2021, CHS WILL ADD FULL PORTFOLIO OF CORTEVA AGRISCIENCE CONFECTIONARY SUNFLOWER SEED TO ROYAL HYBRID LINE UP.
