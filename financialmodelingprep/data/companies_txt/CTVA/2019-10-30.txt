Corteva, Inc. Revises Earnings Guidance for the Year 2019. Corteva, Inc. revised earnings guidance for the year 2019. The Company affirmed 2019 guidance for net sales. The Company revised its full-year operating EPS range, now expected to be between $1.20 and $1.26 per share. Using the mid-point, this represents a $0.04 improvement over the mid-point of the prior guidance.
