Corteva, Inc. Announces Retirement of Neal Gutterson as Senior Vice President and Chief Technology Officer. On October 8, 2019, Mr. Neal Gutterson, Ph.D., Senior Vice President and Chief Technology Officer indicated his intent to retire from Corteva, Inc. in the second half of 2020. He has agreed to remain with the Company until his successor has been selected and for a period of time thereafter to assure a smooth transition. The Company has engaged an executive recruitment firm to identify a replacement.
