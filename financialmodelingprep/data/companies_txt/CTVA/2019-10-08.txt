Corteva, Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend, Payable on December 18, 2019. Corteva, Inc. announced its Board of Directors has authorized a common stock dividend of 13 cents per share, payable December 18, 2019, to the Company's shareholders of record on November 29, 2019. This marks the second continuous cash dividend to be paid by the company since becoming an independent public company on June 1, 2019.
