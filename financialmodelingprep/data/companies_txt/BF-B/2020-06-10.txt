Brown-Forman Says Marshall Farrer Named President Of Brown-Forman Europe & Thomas Hinrichs Was Named President Of Brown-Forman Emerging International. BROWN-FORMAN -  MARSHALL FARRER NAMED PRESIDENT OF BROWN-FORMAN EUROPE & THOMAS HINRICHS WAS NAMED PRESIDENT OF BROWN-FORMAN EMERGING INTERNATIONAL.BROWN-FORMAN - ANNOUNCED CREATION OF BROWN-FORMAN EUROPE & BROWN-FORMAN EMERGING INTERNATIONAL.BROWN-FORMAN - FARRER WILL JOIN EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP TEAM (ELT) AND HINRICHS WILL CONTINUE TO SERVE ON ELT.
