Soho House Limited announced that it has received £78 million in funding from Raycliff Capital, Simon Property Group, Inc.. Soho House Limited announced that it has received £78 million in funding on October 28, 2019. The company issued common shares in the transaction. The transaction included participation from new investors Raycliff Capital and Simon Property Group, Inc. (NYSE:SPG). The investors acquired 5% stake in the company through this transaction. The transaction brings the post money valuation of the company to £1,600 million.
