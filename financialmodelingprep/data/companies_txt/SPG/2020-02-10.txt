Simon Property Group To Acquire Taubman Centers. SIMON PROPERTY GROUP TO ACQUIRE TAUBMAN CENTERS, INC..TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - TAUBMAN SHAREHOLDERS TO RECEIVE $52.50 PER SHARE IN CASH.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - TRANSACTION EXPECTED TO BE IMMEDIATELY ACCRETIVE TO SIMON'S FUNDS FROM OPERATIONS.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - SIMON EXPECTS TO FUND TOTAL REQUIRED CASH CONSIDERATION OF APPROXIMATELY $3.6 BILLION WITH EXISTING LIQUIDITY.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - TAUBMAN FAMILY WILL SELL ABOUT ONE-THIRD OF ITS OWNERSHIP INTEREST AT TRANSACTION PRICE AND REMAIN A 20% PARTNER IN TRG.TAUBMAN CENTERS - DEAL UNANIMOUSLY RECOMMENDED BY A SPECIAL COMMITTEE OF INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS OF CO AND APPROVED UNANIMOUSLY BY BOARDS OF BOTH COS.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - SIMON, THROUGH ITS OPERATING PARTNERSHIP, SIMON PROPERTY GROUP, L.P., WILL ACQUIRE ALL OF TAUBMAN COMMON STOCK.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - SIMON WILL ACQUIRE AN 80% OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN TAUBMAN REALTY GROUP LIMITED PARTNERSHIP.TAUBMAN CENTERS - DEAL EXPECTED TO BE AT LEAST 3% ACCRETIVE TO SIMON'S FFO PER SHARE ON ANNUALIZED BASIS, BEGINNING IMMEDIATELY UPON DEAL CONSUMMATION.TAUBMAN CENTERS - TAUBMAN FAMILY, WHICH REPRESENTS ABOUT 29% OF OUTSTANDING TAUBMAN VOTING STOCK, HAS AGREED TO VOTE IN FAVOR OF DEAL.
