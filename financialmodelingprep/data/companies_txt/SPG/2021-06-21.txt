Simon Property Group Announces 7.7 Percent Increase in Second Quarter 2021 Dividend. INDIANAPOLIS, JUNE 21, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- SIMON, A GLOBAL LEADER IN THE OWNERSHIP OF PREMIER SHOPPING, DINING, ENTERTAINMENT AND MIXED-USE DESTINATIONS, TODAY ANNOUNCED A 7.7% INCREASE IN ITS COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND FOR THE SECOND QUARTER 2021.  SIMON'S BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A $1.40 PER COMMON SHARE DIVIDEND, PAYABLE IN CASH, FOR THE SECOND QUARTER 2021.
