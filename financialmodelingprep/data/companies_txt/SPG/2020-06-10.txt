Taubman Says To "Vigorously Contest" Simon's Purported Termination Of Deal. TAUBMAN CENTERS CONFIRMS RECEIPT OF NOTICE FROM SIMON PROPERTY GROUP PURPORTING TO TERMINATE MERGER AGREEMENT.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - BELIEVES THAT SIMON'S PURPORTED TERMINATION OF MERGER AGREEMENT IS INVALID AND WITHOUT MERIT.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - INTENDS TO PURSUE ITS REMEDIES TO ENFORCE ITS CONTRACTUAL RIGHTS UNDER MERGER AGREEMENT.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - TO "VIGOROUSLY CONTEST" SIMON'S PURPORTED TERMINATION AND LEGAL CLAIMS.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - INTENDS TO PURSUE REMEDIES UNDER AGREEMENT, INCLUDING RIGHT TO MONETARY DAMAGES.TAUBMAN CENTERS INC - SPECIAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS REMAINS SCHEDULED FOR JUNE 25, 2020.
Simon Property Group Terminates Taubman Agreement. SIMON PROPERTY GROUP TERMINATES TAUBMAN AGREEMENT.SIMON PROPERTY GROUP INC - FILED ACTION IN CIRCUIT COURT FOR JUDICIAL CIRCUIT OF OAKLAND COUNTY, MICHIGAN AGAINST TAUBMAN CENTERS.SIMON PROPERTIES - TERMINATION OF MERGER AGREEMENT IS BASED ON TWO SEPARATE AND INDEPENDENT GROUNDS.SIMON PROPERTY- COVID-19 PANDEMIC HAS HAD UNIQUELY MATERIAL, DISPROPORTIONATE EFFECT ON TAUBMAN VERSUS OTHER PARTICIPANTS IN RETAIL REAL ESTATE INDUSTRY.SIMON PROPERTY- IN WAKE OF PANDEMIC, TAUBMAN BREACHED OBLIGATIONS, WHICH ARE CONDITIONS TO CLOSING, RELATING TO OPERATION OF BUSINESS.SIMON PROPERTY- TAUBMAN "FAILED TO TAKE STEPS TO MITIGATE IMPACT OF PANDEMIC AS OTHERS IN INDUSTRY HAVE".
