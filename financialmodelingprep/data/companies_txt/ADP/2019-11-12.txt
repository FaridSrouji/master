Automatic Data Processing, Inc. Approves Quarterly Cash Dividend, Payable on January 1, 2020. The board of directors of Automatic Data Processing, Inc. approved a $0.12 increase in the quarterly cash dividend to an annual rate of $3.64 per share. The new quarterly dividend rate of $0.91 per share will be distributed on January 1, 2020 to shareholders of record on December 13, 2019.
