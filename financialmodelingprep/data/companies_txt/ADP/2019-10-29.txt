Automatic Data Processing, Inc. Reports Earnings Results for the First Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Automatic Data Processing, Inc. announced earnings results for the first quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the first quarter, the company announced total revenue was USD 3,495.7 million compared to USD 3,310.3 million a year ago. Net income was USD 582.4 million compared to USD 505.4 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.35 compared to USD 1.16 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.34 compared to USD 1.15 a year ago.
