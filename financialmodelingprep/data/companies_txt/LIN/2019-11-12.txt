Linde plc Reports Earnings Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2019. Linde plc announced earnings results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2019. For the third quarter, the company announced sales was USD 7,000 million compared to USD 3,008 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 1,000 million compared to USD 669 million a year ago. Net income was USD 735 million compared to USD 461 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 1.35 compared to USD 1.6 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 1.36 compared to USD 1.6 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 1.34 compared to USD 1.58 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 1.35 compared to USD 1.58 a year ago. For the nine months, sales was USD 21,148 million compared to USD 9,035 million a year ago. Operating income was USD 2,278 million compared to USD 2,011 million a year ago. Net income was USD 1,774 million compared to USD 1,403 million a year ago. Basic earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 3.09 compared to USD 4.87 a year ago. Basic earnings per share was USD 3.27 compared to USD 4.87 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share from continuing operations was USD 3.07 compared to USD 4.82 a year ago. Diluted earnings per share was USD 3.25 compared to USD 4.82 a year ago.
