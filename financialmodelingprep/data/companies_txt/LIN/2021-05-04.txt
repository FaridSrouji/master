Linde Awarded Best-in-Class for Sustainability Disclosure Practices. LINDE AWARDED BEST-IN-CLASS FOR SUSTAINABILITY DISCLOSURE PRACTICES GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / MAY 4, 2021 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS RECEIVED THE BEST POSSIBLE RATING FROM ISS ENVIRONMENT QUALITYSCORE IN SUSTAINABILITY DISCLOSURE PRACTICES. ISS QUALITYSCORE HELPS INVESTORS MONITOR THE ENVIRONMENTAL RISKS IN THEIR PORTFOLIO COMPANIES.
