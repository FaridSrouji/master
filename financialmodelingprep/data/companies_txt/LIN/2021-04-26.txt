Linde plc: Linde Declares Dividend in Second Quarter 2021. GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / APRIL 26, 2021 / LINDE PLC (NYSE:LIN); (FWB:LIN) ANNOUNCED ITS BOARD OF DIRECTORS HAS DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND OF $1.06 PER SHARE. THE DIVIDEND IS PAYABLE ON JUNE 17, 2021 TO SHAREHOLDERS OF RECORD ON JUNE 3, 2021.
