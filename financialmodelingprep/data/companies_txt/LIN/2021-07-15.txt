Linde Starts Up New Liquid Hydrogen Plant in Texas. GUILDFORD, UK / ACCESSWIRE / JULY 15, 2021 / LINDE (NYSE:LIN)(FWB:LIN) TODAY ANNOUNCED IT HAS STARTED UP ITS FIFTH LIQUID HYDROGEN PLANT IN THE U.S., REINFORCING THE COMPANY'S ROBUST SUPPLY NETWORK OF PLANTS IN CALIFORNIA, ALABAMA, INDIANA AND NEW YORK. THE NEW PLANT IN LA PORTE, TEXAS, WILL SUPPLY OVER 30 TONS PER DAY OF HIGH-PURITY LIQUID HYDROGEN TO MEET GROWING DEMAND FROM LINDE'S CUSTOMERS.
