Linde Starts Up Air Separation Units in Eastern China Chemical Park. Linde plc announced that it started up two plants to supply oxygen and nitrogen to Taixing Jinyan Chemical Technology Co. Ltd. to support the production of ethylene oxide. The plants, with total combined production capacity of 29000 Nm3/h, will also supply gases to other customers in Taixing Economic Development Zone, ranked sixth amongst China's top chemical parks.
