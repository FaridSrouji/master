Linde Signs MoU With China Power To Develop Green Hydrogen Energy In China. LINDE SIGNS MOU WITH CHINA POWER TO DEVELOP GREEN HYDROGEN ENERGY IN CHINA.BOTH COMPANIES WILL COLLABORATE ON A VARIETY OF GREEN HYDROGEN INITIATIVES, INCLUDING HYDROGEN TECHNOLOGY RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT, AND IMPLEMENTATION OF GREEN HYDROGEN MOBILITY SOLUTIONS DURING CHINA'S INAUGURAL HOSTING OF 2022 WINTER OLYMPICS.
