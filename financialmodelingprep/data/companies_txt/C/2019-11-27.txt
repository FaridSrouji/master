Citigroup Inc Says It Made A One-Time Incentive Award To President Of Citi And CEO Of Global Consumer Banking. CITIGROUP INC - CO MADE A ONE-TIME INCENTIVE AWARD TO JANE FRASER, PRESIDENT OF CITI AND CEO OF GLOBAL CONSUMER BANKING.CITIGROUP - $12.5 MILLION AWARD TO JANE FRASER IS SCHEDULED TO VEST AND BE SETTLED IN FOUR EQUAL INSTALLMENTS.CITIGROUP - AWARD TERMS ARE MORE RESTRICTIVE THAN TERMS OF DEFERRED PORTIONS OF CITI'S REGULAR INCENTIVE AWARDS DELIVERED TO SENIOR EXECUTIVES.
