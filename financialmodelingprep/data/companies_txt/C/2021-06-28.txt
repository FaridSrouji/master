Citi Appointed as Depositary Bank for Tremor International Ltd's ADR Programme. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITI, ACTING THROUGH CITIBANK N.A., HAS BEEN APPOINTED BY TREMOR INTERNATIONAL LTD (“TREMOR”), A GLOBAL LEADER IN ADVERTISING TECHNOLOGIES, TO ACT AS DEPOSITARY BANK FOR ITS AMERICAN DEPOSITARY RECEIPT (“ADR”) PROGRAMME. TREMOR'S AMERICAN DEPOSITARY SHARES (“ADSS”) TRADE ON THE NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKET UNDER THE SYMBOL “[TRMR]”. EACH ADS REPRESENTS TWO ORDINARY SHARES OF THE COMPANY.
