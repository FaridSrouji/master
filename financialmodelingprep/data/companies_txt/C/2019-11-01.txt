Citigroup Inc Says Overall Impact Of CECL Methodology Is Estimated To Be An About 20–30% Increase In Expected Credit Loss Reserves. CITIGROUP INC SAYS OVERALL IMPACT OF CECL METHODOLOGY IS ESTIMATED TO BE AN ABOUT 20–30% INCREASE IN EXPECTED CREDIT LOSS RESERVES - SEC FILING.CITIGROUP-WHEN TRANSITIONING TO CECL ON JAN 1, 2020, EXPECTS TO BUILD RESERVES FOR CONSUMER EXPOSURES WHILE RELEASING RESERVES RELATED TO CORPORATE EXPOSURES.CITIGROUP INC - INCREASE IN RESERVES WOULD BE REFLECTED AS A DECREASE TO OPENING RETAINED EARNINGS, NET OF INCOME TAXES, AT JANUARY 1, 2020.
