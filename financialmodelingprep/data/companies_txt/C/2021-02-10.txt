Citi Appointed as Depositary Bank for Immunocore Holdings plc's ADR Programme. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITI, ACTING THROUGH CITIBANK N.A., HAS BEEN APPOINTED BY IMMUNOCORE HOLDINGS PLC (“IMMUNOCORE”), A LATE-STAGE BIOTECHNOLOGY COMPANY, TO ACT AS DEPOSITARY BANK FOR ITS AMERICAN DEPOSITARY RECEIPT (“ADR”) PROGRAMME. IMMUNOCORE'S AMERICAN DEPOSITARY SHARES (“ADS”) TRADE ON THE NASDAQ GLOBAL MARKET UNDER THE SYMBOL “IMCR”. EACH ADS REPRESENTS ONE ORDINARY SHARE OF THE COMPANY.
