DEADLINE ALERT: Bragar Eagel & Squire, P.C. Reminds Investors That a Class Action Lawsuit Has Been Filed Against Citigroup, Inc. and Encourages Investors to Contact the Firm. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- #CITIGROUP--BRAGAR EAGEL & SQUIRE, P.C., A NATIONALLY RECOGNIZED SHAREHOLDER RIGHTS LAW FIRM, REMINDS INVESTORS THAT A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT HAS BEEN FILED IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE SOUTHERN DISTRICT OF NEW YORK ON BEHALF OF INVESTORS THAT PURCHASED CITIGROUP, INC. (NYSE: C) COMMON STOCK BETWEEN JANUARY 15, 2016 AND OCTOBER 12, 2020 (THE “CLASS PERIOD”). INVESTORS HAVE UNTIL DECEMBER 29, 2020 TO APPLY TO THE COURT TO BE APPOINTED AS LEAD PLAINTIFF IN THE LAWS
