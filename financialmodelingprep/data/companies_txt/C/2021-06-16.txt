Citi Exclusively Distributes Innovative Adjusted Returns Indices on Single Stocks. LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITI HAS PARTNERED WITH STOXX QONTIGO TO DEVELOP A SERIES OF SINGLE STOCK LINKED INDICES IN AN EFFORT TO GENERATE A MORE EFFICIENT STRUCTURED PRODUCT UNIVERSE FOR FINANCIAL INTERMEDIARIES AND END INVESTORS. THE SINGLE STOCK DECREMENT INDICES ARE ADJUSTED RETURNS INDICES WHERE EACH INDEX REFERENCES ONE OF 23 BLUE-CHIP SINGLE STOCK NAMES.
