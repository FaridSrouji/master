CITIGROUP INVESTIGATION UPDATE BY FORMER LOUISIANA ATTORNEY GENERAL: Kahn Swick & Foti, LLC Continues to Investigate the Officers and Directors of Citigroup Inc. - C. NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA--(NEWSFILE CORP. - NOVEMBER 17, 2020) - FORMER ATTORNEY GENERAL OF LOUISIANA, CHARLES C. FOTI, JR., ESQ., A PARTNER AT THE LAW FIRM OF KAHN SWICK & FOTI, LLC ("KSF"), ANNOUNCES THAT KSF CONTINUES ITS INVESTIGATION INTO CITIGROUP INC. (NYSE: C). ON AUGUST 12, 2020, IT WAS REVEALED THAT THE COMPANY HAD INADVERTENTLY WIRED $900 MILLION OF ITS OWN MONEY TO VARIOUS LENDERS OF REVLON INC., SEEMINGLY INTENDED AS AN INTEREST PAYMENT...
