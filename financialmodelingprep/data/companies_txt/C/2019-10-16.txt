Citigroup Inc Names Peter Babej as New Asia Pacific Chief Executive Officer. Citigroup Inc. has named Peter Babej, global head of financial institutions group, as its new Asia Pacific chief executive officer. Babej had joined the bank in 2010. He is currently based in New York and is to relocate to Hong Kong.
