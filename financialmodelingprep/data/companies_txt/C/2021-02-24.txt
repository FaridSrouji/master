Citi Grows Nordic Custody Business Through Strategic Referral Arrangement With Nordea. STOCKHOLM--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CITIGROUP INC. (NYSE: C) CITI AND NORDEA HAVE FINALIZED AN AGREEMENT WHERE NORDEA WILL RECOMMEND ITS EXISTING CLIENTS APPOINT CITI AS THEIR NEW PROVIDER OF SUB-CUSTODY SERVICES IN THE REGION. THIS FOLLOWS A DECISION BY NORDEA TO EXIT THE NORDIC SUB-CUSTODY BUSINESS. “CITI IS PLEASED TO ENTER INTO THIS STRATEGIC REFERRAL ARRANGEMENT WITH NORDEA, A MOVE THAT AFFIRMS OUR COMMITMENT TO GROW AND INVEST IN OUR SUB CUSTODY NETWORK,” SAID OKAN PEKIN, GLOBAL HEAD OF SECURITIE
