Huntington Launches Standby Cash(SM) To Give Eligible Customers An Instant Line Of Credit For Unexpected Expenses. COLUMBUS, OHIO, JUNE 1, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- PLANNING FOR THE UNEXPECTED JUST GOT EASIER WITH HUNTINGTON'S (NASDAQ: HBAN) FIRST DIGITAL-ONLY PRODUCT, STANDBY CASH. TRUE TO ITS COMMITMENT TO LOOKING OUT FOR PEOPLE, HUNTINGTON HAS LAUNCHED STANDBY CASH, A LINE OF CREDIT GIVING ELIGIBLE CUSTOMERS IMMEDIATE ACCESS UP TO $1,000 WITH NO INTEREST OR FEES IF CUSTOMERS SIGN UP FOR AUTOMATIC PAYMENTS (OTHERWISE, A 1% MONTHLY INTEREST CHARGE, 12% APR, APPLIES TO OUTSTANDING BALANCES).
