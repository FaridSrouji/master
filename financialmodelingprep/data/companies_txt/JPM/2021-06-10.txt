Women Presidents' Organization and JPMorgan Chase Commercial Banking Seek Nominations for the 50 Fastest Growing Women-Owned and/or Led Companies of 2021. NEW YORK, JUNE 10, 2021 /PRNEWSWIRE/ -- THE WOMEN PRESIDENTS' ORGANIZATION (WPO) AND JPMORGAN CHASE COMMERCIAL BANKING ARE SEEKING NOMINEES FOR THE 50 FASTEST GROWING WOMEN-OWNED AND/OR LED COMPANIESTM RANKING. FOR THE 14TH YEAR WPO, A PEER ADVISORY GROUP FOR MILLION-DOLLAR PLUS WOMEN-LED COMPANIES, WILL HONOR THE WOMEN WHO ARE LEADING SOME OF THE MOST SUCCESSFUL BUSINESSES AROUND THE GLOBE.
