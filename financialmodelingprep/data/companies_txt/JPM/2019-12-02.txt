JPMorgan Chase & Co. Slows Pace for Local Branch Openings Plans to Have 17 by Year End. JPMorgan Chase & Co. opened two more local branches in late November but has curbed the number of locations it planned to have up and running by the end of the year from 24 to 17. Chase has five more branch openings planned for this year: On Dec. 3 at Canterbury Road & Old York Road in Abington and the following four on Dec. 17: Cecil B. Moore Ave. & 12th St. in North Philadelphia, Ark Road and Route 38, Mount Laurel, N.J., 8301 Henry Ave. in the Andorra section of Philadelphia and 901 Sumneytown Pike in Spring House. The bank has also received approval from the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency to open 23 additional locations: Two in South Jersey, five in Delaware, 10 in the Pennsylvania suburbs and six in Philadelphia. A spokeswoman said the bank has plans to open six locations in the first quarter of 2020 but that dates were fluid. Chase has also promised that 20%, or 10, of its new branches will be in low-to-moderate income neighborhoods. It appears to be well on its way to meeting that threshold, with existing locations in Camden, Upper Darby and Grays Ferry, the one coming in North Philadelphia and others in the works in Kensington, Port Richmond, West Philadelphia and Island Avenue near Philadelphia International Airport.
