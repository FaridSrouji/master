JPM Shareholder Alert: Bronstein, Gewirtz & Grossman, LLC Notifies JPMorgan Chase & Co. Shareholders of Class Action and Lead Plaintiff Deadline: December 23, 2020. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Bronstein, Gewirtz & Grossman, LLC notifies investors that a class action lawsuit has been filed against JPMorgan Chase & Co. ("JP Morgan" or "the Company") (NYSE: JPM) and certain of its officers, on behalf of shareholders who purchased or otherwise acquired JPMorgan securities between February 23, 2016 and September 23, 2020, both dates inclusive (the "Class Period"). Such investors are encouraged to join this case by visiting the firm's site: www.bgandg.com
