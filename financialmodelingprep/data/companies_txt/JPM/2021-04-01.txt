Survey from Chase Reveals That Two-Thirds of Consumers Have Forgotten About At Least One Recurring Payment In The Last Year. WILMINGTON, DEL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TODAY, CHASE RELEASED THE FINDINGS FROM A NEW STUDY AROUND CONSUMER HABITS RELATED TO RECURRING PAYMENTS. THE RESULTS INDICATE THAT CONSUMERS INCREASINGLY RELY ON RECURRING CREDIT CARD PAYMENTS, CARE ABOUT KNOWING WHERE THEIR CREDIT CARDS ARE DIGITALLY STORED, AND REMAIN FOCUSED ON GETTING A BETTER HANDLE ON THEIR FINANCES.
