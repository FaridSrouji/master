JPMorgan Chase & Co. Plans to Open A Branch in Washington County, A Regulatory Filing Revealed. JPMorgan Chase & Co. plans to open a branch in Washington County, a regulatory filing revealed. Chase's Donaldson Crossroads branch would be located at the intersection of Washington Road and East McMurray Road, according to the filing with the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency. So far, it has confirmed seven sites in the region, the first of which, in One Oxford Centre in downtown Pittsburgh, is to open in late October. But, like Bank of America Corp., the other big newcomer to the region's consumer banking ranks, Chase's activity thus far has chiefly centered on Pittsburgh. Neither has said how many branches are planned for southwestern Pennsylvania. BofA expects to have 12 open by the end of November. Based in New York, Chase is the nation's large bank according to assets, which are $2.7 trillion, and it ranks second to Wells Fargo & Co. for the most branches with 5,193.
