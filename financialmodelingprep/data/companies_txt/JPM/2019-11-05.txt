Jpmorgan Chase & Co. Opens Second Pittsburgh Branch Within Two Weeks. JPMorgan Chase & Co. has opened its second Pittsburgh branch within two weeks. The opening for the Shadyside office, located at the intersection of South Aiken and Walnut Street. So far, 10 branches have received regulatory approval and Chase said it plans about 25 for southwestern Pennsylvania but has not provided specifics on the time frame for the rollout. The first to debut was at One Oxford Centre in downtown Pittsburgh late last month.
