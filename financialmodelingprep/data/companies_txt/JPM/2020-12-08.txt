JPMorgan Chase Declares Common Stock Dividend. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. (NYSE: JPM) (“JPMORGAN CHASE” OR THE “FIRM”) DECLARED A QUARTERLY DIVIDEND ON THE OUTSTANDING SHARES OF THE COMMON STOCK OF JPMORGAN CHASE. INFORMATION CAN BE FOUND ON THE FIRM'S INVESTOR RELATIONS WEBSITE AT JPMORGANCHASE.COM/PRESS-RELEASES. JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. (NYSE: JPM) IS A LEADING GLOBAL FINANCIAL SERVICES FIRM WITH ASSETS OF $3.2 TRILLION AND OPERATIONS WORLDWIDE. THE FIRM IS A LEADER IN INVESTMENT BANKING
