CFTC Orders JPMorgan To Pay Record $920 Mln For Spoofing And Manipulation. CFTC ORDERS JPMORGAN TO PAY RECORD $920 MILLION FOR SPOOFING AND MANIPULATION.CFTC SAYS ORDER FINDS THAT JPM’S ILLEGAL TRADING SIGNIFICANTLY BENEFITED JPM AND HARMED OTHER MARKET PARTICIPANTS.CFTC SAYS CFTC ORDER WILL RECOGNIZE, OFFSET ANY RESTITUTION AND DISGORGEMENT PAYMENTS MADE TO THE DOJ AND THE SEC BY JPM.
