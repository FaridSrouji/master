Despite a Difficult Year, Consumers Are Financially Optimistic, See Improvements in Spending, Saving. NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--NEARLY HALF OF U.S. CONSUMERS THINK THAT THEIR FINANCES WILL BE BETTER OFF IN THE NEXT YEAR, AS NEARLY A THIRD SAW IMPROVEMENTS IN THEIR SPENDING HABITS AND SAVINGS, ACCORDING TO A NEW SURVEY FROM CHASE. OPTIMISM IS SLIGHTLY HIGHER AMONG BLACK (54%) AND LATINX (49%) RESPONDENTS AS MANY HAVE TAKEN ACTION TO IMPROVE THEIR CREDIT, SPENDING, OR EARN A SECOND INCOME.
