Michele Lawrence to Move Out of the Role as Lead Jpmorgan Chase & Co.'s Retail Expansion into Pennsylvania, Delaware and South Jersey; to Expand to 24 Branches in Philadelphia Region by the End of the Year. A little more than a year after being hired to lead JPMorgan Chase & Co.'s retail expansion into Pennsylvania,Delaware and South Jersey, the bank said Michele Lawrence is transitioning out of that role. The bank has moved quickly in the Philadelphia region, opening seven branches already and that number is slatedto expand to 24 by the end of the year. The bank has identified almost 40 branch locations, according torecords with the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency.
