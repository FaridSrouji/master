JPM LOOMING DEADLINE: Zhang Investor Law Alerts Investors of Deadline in Securities Class Action Lawsuit Against  JPMorgan Chase & Co. – JPM. NEW YORK, DEC. 11, 2020 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ZHANG INVESTOR LAW ANNOUNCES A CLASS ACTION LAWSUIT ON BEHALF OF SHAREHOLDERS WHO BOUGHT SHARES OF JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. (NYSE:JPM) BETWEEN FEBRUARY 23, 2016 AND SEPTEMBER 23, 2020, INCLUSIVE (THE "CLASS PERIOD").
