HP Sends Letter to Xerox. On November 24, 2019, HP Inc. announced that it has sent a letter to Xerox Holdings Corporation in response to Xerox’s November 21, 2019 letter. The Company reiterated that it rejects Xerox’s proposal as it undervalues the Company and additionally, it is conditional and uncertain. The Company stated that in particular, there continues to be uncertainty regarding Xerox’s ability to raise the cash portion of the proposed consideration and concerns regarding the prudence of the resulting outsized debt burden on the value of the combined company’s stock even if the financing were obtained and consequently, the proposal does not constitute a basis for due diligence or negotiation. In addition, the Company stated that the Board of Directors is committed to serving the best interests of the Company shareholders, not Xerox and its shareholders and the Company has numerous opportunities to create value for its’ shareholders on a standalone basis. In addition, the Company stated that it will not let aggressive tactics or hostile gestures distract it from its responsibility to pursue the most value-creating path.

Blackstone Real Estate Income Trust Completes Acquisition of Bellagio Real Estate from MGM Resorts International. Blackstone Real Estate Income Trust (BREIT) and MGM Resorts International reported the closing of the previously announced 95%/5% BREIT-led joint venture with MGM Resorts to acquire the real estate assets of the Bellagio for $4.25 billion in a sale-leaseback transaction. As part of the transaction, MGM Resorts has leased the property from the joint venture and continues to manage, operate and be responsible for all aspects of the property on a day-to-day basis. The transaction was announced on October 15.
