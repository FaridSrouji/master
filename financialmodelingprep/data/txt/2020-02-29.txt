Delta Air Lines Responds To Coronavirus (Covid-19) Outbreak. DELTA AIR LINES INC. - UPDATED: DELTA RESPONDS TO CORONAVIRUS (COVID-19) OUTBREAK.DELTA AIR LINES INC- ADJUSTED TRAVEL SCHEDULES, WAIVED CHANGE FEES FOR AFFECTED DESTINATIONS AND ARE WORKING WITH CUSTOMERS TO ADJUST TRAVEL PLANS.DELTA AIR LINES INC- DELTA SERVICE BETWEEN U.S. AND SHANGHAI AND BEIJING HAS BEEN SUSPENDED UNTIL APRIL 30.DELTA AIR LINES- CONSULTED WITH CDC, WHO, OTHER HEALTH ORGANIZATIONS TO ENSURE TO FOLLOW GUIDANCE ON HEALTH PRECAUTIONS RELATED TO CORONAVIRUS.
