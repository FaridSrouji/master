THE SITE IS LOCATED DIRECTLY ADJACENT TO DENBURY'S NEJD PIPELINE IN MISSISSIPPI, APPROXIMATELY 35 MILES SOUTH OF THE COMPANY'S JACKSON DOME FIELD. DENBURY IS PLANNING TO UTILIZE THE SITE TO PERMANENTLY SEQUESTER INDUSTRIAL CO2 IN SECURE UNDERGROUND GEOLOGIC FORMATIONS.
