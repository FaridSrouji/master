CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER AQUIFER STORAGE AND RECOVERY PROGRAM CAPTURES NEAR HISTORIC LEVELS OF WINTER RAIN. PACIFIC GROVE, CALIF.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER‘S AQUIFER STORAGE AND RECOVERY (ASR) PROGRAM CAPTURED EXCESS WINTER RAINWATER FROM THE CARMEL RIVER AND INJECTED 1,656.42 ACRE FEET OF WATER INTO THE SEASIDE BASIN THIS WINTER. THE STORED WATER WILL HELP ALLEVIATE WATER NEEDS DURING FUTURE DRY PERIODS WITHOUT RAIN AND WILL REDUCE FUTURE RELIANCE ON THE CARMEL RIVER AND SEASIDE BASIN. OF THE 182-DAY AQUIFER STORAGE AND RECOVERY SEASON, CALIFORNIA AMERICAN WATER SUCCESSFULLY INJECT.
