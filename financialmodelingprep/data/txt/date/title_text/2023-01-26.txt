THE STORAGE TECHNOLOGY WILL ALLOW XCEL ENERGY TO INTEGRATE MORE LOW-COST, RENEWABLE ENERGY INTO ITS SYSTEM AND MAINTAIN RELIABILITY AS IT RETIRES THE COAL PLANTS IN THE COMING YEARS AND TRANSITIONS TO A HIGHLY RENEWABLE FUTURE.XCEL ENERGY 2022 YEAR END EARNINGS REPORT.
