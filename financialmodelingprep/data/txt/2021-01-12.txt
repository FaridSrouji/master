THIS REPRESENTS A TOTAL EQUITY VALUE OF APPROXIMATELY $3.6 BILLION AND A TOTAL ENTERPRISE VALUE OF APPROXIMATELY $4.6 BILLION, INCLUDING CANTEL'S NET DEBT AND CONVERTIBLE NOTES.  THE AGREEMENT HAS BEEN UNANIMOUSLY APPROVED BY THE BOARDS OF DIRECTORS OF BOTH COMPANIES.
