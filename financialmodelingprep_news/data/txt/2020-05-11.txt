Walt Disney Company (The) -- Moody's assigns A2 to The Walt Disney Company's proposed notes offering. Moody's Investors Service, ("Moody's") assigned an A2 rating to The Walt Disney Company's (Disney) proposed senior unsecured notes offering.  The offering will include multiple tranches maturing in 2026, 2028, 2031, 2040, 2051, and 2060 and they will be pari passu with the company's existing senior unsecured indebtedness and will be guaranteed by TWDC Enterprises 18 Corp., a subsidiary of Disney.
